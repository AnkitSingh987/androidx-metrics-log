Once upon a time, there was a little shrimp who lived in the sea. The shrimp was very alert and always watched out for his friends. One day, the shrimp saw a big fish coming near his friends. He wanted to help them, so he thought of a plan. The shrimp decided to send a message to a big whale who lived far away. He asked the whale to come and scare the big fish away. The shrimp waited and waited for the whale to come. Suddenly, the big fish swam closer to the shrimp's friends. Just when the big fish was about to eat the shrimp's friends, the whale came and scared the big fish away. The shrimp's friends were safe, and they were so happy. The shrimp learned that being alert and asking for help can save the day. And they all lived happily ever after.

The sun shone down, making the day warm and bright. Johnny and his parents went for a walk in the woods. "What was that noise?" asked Johnny. His parents exchanged a look, something Johnny noticed. "It's probably nothing," said his father. "Let's keep going." They continued walking, but soon came across something strange. It was a huge, spotted animal with a long tail. Johnny could tell it was no ordinary animal. It was a leopard. Johnny shivered as the leopard moved closer and growled. He knew it was not happy to see them there. His father quickly grabbed him as the leopard came closer. Johnny's mother yelled, but the leopard just kept getting noisier and noisier. Just then, two dogs ran out of the woods. The leopard quickly ran away, frightened by all the noise. Johnny sighed with relief. His father patted him on the back and said, "See, I told you it was nothing."

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to make things. One day, he wanted to make something with fruit. So, he went to the store to buy some fruit. At the store, Tim saw many fruits. He wanted to be organized, so he picked one apple, one banana, and one orange. He was happy with his fruit choices. He went home to make a fruit salad. Tim started to make the fruit salad. He cut the apple, the banana, and the orange. But Tim was not careful. He cut his finger with the knife. Tim cried and his fruit salad was not finished. His mom came to help him, but the day did not end well for Tim.

Once upon a time, in a wide, wide yard, there was a big sack. The sack was full of toys. A little boy named Tim saw the sack and wanted to play with the toys. Tim opened the sack and started to play with the toys. He was very happy. One day, while Tim was playing, the sack began to shake. Tim was scared. Then, something unexpected happened. Steam came out of the sack. The steam grew bigger and bigger. Tim did not know what to do. Suddenly, the steam turned into a big, friendly dragon. The dragon said, "Thank you, Tim, for playing with my toys. Now, I want to play too!" Tim and the dragon played with the toys together. They had lots of fun and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a famous factory. In this factory, they made toys for all the good boys and girls. The factory was big and colorful, and it was always busy making toys. One day, a little toy bear was left behind in the factory. He was sad because all his friends were gone. He wanted to find his friends, but he didn't know how. So, he decided to remain in the factory and wait for them to come back. Time went by, and the little toy bear became friends with the other toys in the factory. They played together and had lots of fun. The little toy bear was happy again. He knew that even though his old friends were gone, he had made new friends in the famous factory. And they would always be there for him.

Lily and Sam were brother and sister. They lived in a big house with their mom and dad. They were wealthy, which means they had a lot of things and money. They had many toys, books, clothes and games. But they were not happy. They always fought and yelled at each other. They did not share or help or say sorry. One day, their mom and dad had to go away for work. They asked their aunt to watch Lily and Sam. Their aunt was kind and fun, but she had a rule. She said, "You have to get along and be nice to each other, or you will lose your toys." Lily and Sam did not listen. They still fought and yelled at each other. They did not share or help or say sorry. Their aunt was sad and angry. She said, "That's it. You have broken my rule. I will take away your toys and put them in a box. You will not get them back until you learn to be good." Lily and Sam were shocked and scared. They did not want to lose their toys. They begged their aunt to give them back. But their aunt said, "No. You have to earn them back. You have to show me that you can be kind and respectful to each other. You have to restore our trust, which means you have to make us believe you again." Lily and Sam did not know what to do. They looked at each other and felt sorry. They realized they had been mean and selfish. They decided to try to be better. They said sorry to each other and to their aunt. They started to share and help and say nice things. They played together and had fun. They made their aunt happy and proud. Their aunt saw that they had changed. She said, "You have done a good job. You have restored our trust. You have been good brother and sister. You can have your toys back. But remember, toys are not the most important thing. The most important thing is love and family." Lily and Sam hugged their aunt and thanked her. They hugged each other and smiled. They were happy. They had their toys back, but they also had something more. They had learned to be good and get along. They had restored their love.
