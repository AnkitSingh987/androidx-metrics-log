Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play ball with his friends. One day, he found a ball in the park. Spot wanted to play a game and get a good score. Spot's friend, a big cat named Mittens, saw the ball and wanted to play too. Mittens was very good at shaking things. They decided to play a game where they had to shake the ball and score points. The winner would be the one with the highest score. They played the game and had lots of fun. Spot was very fast, but Mittens was very good at shaking the ball. In the end, Mittens won the game, and Spot was happy for his friend. They both felt safe playing together and knew that they would always be there for each other, no matter who won or lost the game.

Once upon a time, there was a regular car with a motor. The car lived in a big town. The car liked to go fast and make a lot of noise. People in the town did not like the car because it was too loud. They would ignore the car when it went by. One day, the car met a little bike. The bike said, "Hello, car! Why do people ignore you?" The car said, "I don't know. I think it's because I have a loud motor." The bike said, "Maybe you should try to be more quiet." The car thought about what the bike said. The next day, the car tried to be more quiet. The car went slow and did not make a lot of noise. The people in the town started to like the car. They did not ignore the car anymore. The car was happy that it had a new friend, the bike. The car and the bike would go on rides together. They would talk and laugh. The car learned that it was better to be quiet and have friends than to be loud and be ignored.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who wanted to explore the world around her. Every day she would go outside, and examine everything she could find. One day, while she was exploring, the little girl decided to examine a delicate table she saw in a nearby garden. As she looked closer, the little girl noticed it was made from a shiny, glass like material. Suddenly, a voice behind the little girl shouted, "What do you think you are doing?" The little girl turned around, and to her surprise, saw a man standing behind her. The little girl was scared, but said, "I was just examining the table." The man replied, "You can't touch that. That table is delicate and is made from a special material." The little girl said, "I'm sorry. I didn't mean to do anything wrong." The man looked at her and smiled, "I know you were just exploring, but you still can't touch the table." The little girl thanked the man, and the two went their separate ways, the little girl once again exploring the world around her.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They walked on a smooth path, holding hands. Tim liked the park because it was full of fun things to do. He could play on the swings, slide down the big slide, and run around with his friends. At the park, Tim saw a little bird. The bird was singing a pretty song. Tim felt happy and at peace when he listened to the bird. He sat down on the grass and watched the bird sing. After a while, Tim's mom said it was time to go home. They walked back on the smooth path, holding hands. Tim said goodbye to the park and the little bird. He knew he would come back to the park soon to play and feel peace again.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man who loved to study. He had a big tower full of books. Every day, he would climb up the tower and read the books. He was very happy. One day, a little girl came to his tower. She looked up and said, "Hi, can I study with you?" The elderly man looked down and said, "Sure, come on up!" The little girl climbed up the tower and they began to study together. As they were studying, something unexpected happened. The tower began to shake and fall down. The elderly man and the little girl tried to hold the books, but it was too late. The tower fell down and all the books were gone. They were very sad and could not study anymore.
