Lila and Tom were playing with their toy rockets in the backyard. They pretended they were flying to different planets in the universe. Lila liked the red planet, because it was hot and bright. Tom liked the blue planet, because it was cold and dark. "Let's go to the red planet, Lila," Tom said. "It has a big volcano that can burn anything." "No, Tom, I don't want to go there. It's too scary. The volcano can burn us too," Lila said. "Let's go to the blue planet. It has a nice lake that we can swim in." "But the blue planet is boring, Lila. The lake is frozen and nothing lives there," Tom said. "The red planet is fun and exciting. We can see the lava and the rocks." Lila and Tom argued for a while, until they heard a loud noise. They looked up and saw a real rocket flying in the sky. It was shiny and white, and it had a big flame behind it. They were amazed and curious. "Wow, look at that, Lila. It's a real rocket. Maybe it's going to the red planet or the blue planet," Tom said. "Or maybe it's going to another planet we don't know, Tom. Maybe it's going to a planet with rainbows and flowers," Lila said. They watched the rocket until it disappeared in the clouds. They wondered who was inside it and where it was going. They felt lucky that they could see it. "Maybe someday we can go on a real rocket, Lila. Maybe we can explore the universe and see all the planets," Tom said. "Maybe, Tom. But for now, we can play with our toy rockets and imagine," Lila said. They smiled and hugged each other. They decided to stop fighting and share their planets. They played happily until their mom called them for dinner.

One day, a boy named Tim found a magic cup. This cup was very special. When Tim put something hot in the cup, the hot thing would vanish. Tim showed his friend, Jane. "Look at my magic cup!" he said. "When I put hot things in it, they go away!" Jane was very excited. "Let's try it!" she said. They put hot water in the cup. The hot water vanished! Tim and Jane laughed and clapped their hands. Tim had an idea. "Let's put my hot soup in the cup!" he said. Jane agreed. They put the hot soup in the magic cup. The hot soup vanished too! But then, the magic cup became very hot. Tim and Jane were sad. The magic cup was too hot to touch. They could not play with it anymore. The magic cup stayed hot forever, and they never got to use it again.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy barber named Tom. Tom liked to cut hair, but sometimes he made mistakes. One day, a little boy named Tim came to Tom's shop for a haircut. "Hi, Tom," said Tim. "I want a haircut, but please don't make it look funny." Tom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will do my best." But while cutting Tim's hair, Tom made a mistake and cut too much hair off. Tim looked in the mirror and laughed. That night, Tom had a dream. In his dream, he was the best barber in the world. Everyone loved their haircuts, and no one ever laughed at his mistakes. Tom woke up and remembered his dream. He decided to practice more, so he could be a better barber. As time went by, Tom became better at cutting hair. Tim came back to the shop and got another haircut. This time, Tom did a great job. Tim was happy, and Tom was proud.

Once upon a time, there was a unique musician named Tom. He could play many songs on his little guitar. Tom loved to make people happy with his music. One day, Tom saw a girl named Sue. Sue was very sad and began to weep. Tom wanted to help her feel better. So, he played a happy song on his guitar for her. "Please don't weep, Sue," Tom said. "Listen to my song and let it make you smile." Sue stopped weeping and started to listen to Tom's music. Soon, she was smiling and laughing. Tom was happy that he could help Sue feel better with his unique music. They became good friends and played together every day.
