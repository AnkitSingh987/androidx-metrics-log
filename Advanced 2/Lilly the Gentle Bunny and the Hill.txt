Once upon a time, there was a gentle bunny named Lilly. Lilly loved to play in the winter snow. She would hop and spring all over the place. One day, Lilly met a big bear named Ben. Ben was sad because he couldn't play in the snow like Lilly. "Hi Ben," said Lilly. "Why are you sad?" Ben looked at Lilly and said, "I'm too big to spring like you. I want to play in the snow too." Lilly thought for a moment and then had a great idea. Lilly and Ben went to the big hill. Lilly said, "Ben, you can slide down the hill in the snow!" Ben looked surprised but excited. He sat down and slid down the hill, laughing all the way. From that day on, Lilly and Ben played together all winter long. They learned that even though they were different, they could still have fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a small, light bug named Bob. Bob lived in a big structure made of sticks. One day, he met another bug named Sue. Sue was sad. "Why are you sad, Sue?" asked Bob. Sue said, "I can't accept that I am not good at making structures like you, Bob." Bob had an idea. "Let's work together to make a big structure!" Sue felt happy and they started to build. As they built the structure, a big wind came and blew it away! But Bob and Sue didn't give up. They built an even better structure. In the end, Sue learned to accept her skills and they became best friends.

Lila has a dog named Spot. Spot is a loyal dog. He loves Lila and follows her everywhere. He likes to play with her and make her happy. One day, Lila and Spot go to the park. They see a big tree with a hole in it. Lila is curious. She wants to see what is inside the hole. She tells Spot to wait for her. She goes to the tree and puts her hand in the hole. Spot barks. He is worried. He does not want Lila to get hurt. He runs to the tree and tries to pull Lila away. But Lila does not listen. She feels something in the hole. She pulls it out. It is a bone! Lila is excited. She thinks the bone is a treasure. She shows it to Spot. Spot is happy too. He thinks the bone is a treat. He wags his tail and licks Lila's face. Lila and Spot share the bone. They are both happy. They have found a new toy and a new friend. They go home together. They are a loyal team.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. He had a big mine where his cars would dig and find shiny rocks. Tim liked to relax and watch his cars work in the mine. One day, Tim found a long, flexible stick. He thought it would be fun to use it to help his cars dig in the mine. So, he put the stick in the mine and started to dig. Suddenly, the mine started to shake! Tim was scared. Out of the mine came a big, friendly dragon! The dragon said, "Thank you for helping me get out with your flexible stick!" Tim was very happy to have a new friend. They played and relaxed together. The moral of the story is to always be kind and helpful, because you never know who you might meet.

Once upon a time, a cute little boy named Timmy loved to play with his toy missile. He would run around the yard, holding it and making "whoosh" sounds. One sunny day, Timmy was playing with his missile when he heard his mommy call him. "Timmy, come here and listen, please!" said Mommy. Timmy ran to her and listened carefully. Mommy told him that they were going to the park to play. Timmy was very excited and wanted to bring his missile with him. At the park, Timmy played with his missile, running and laughing. Suddenly, he saw a cute little squirrel holding something shiny. Timmy looked closer and saw that the squirrel had his missile! He couldn't believe it! The squirrel had taken his missile when he wasn't looking. "Hey, Mr. Squirrel, can I have my missile back, please?" Timmy asked. The squirrel looked at Timmy, then handed him the missile with a little smile. Timmy thanked the squirrel, and they played together for the rest of the day. From that day on, Timmy and the squirrel were the best of friends, and they always played with the missile together.
