Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big dog named Max. Lucy wanted to play with Max, but her mom said, "First, you must pass a test." The test was difficult for Lucy. She had to pick up all her toys and put them away. Lucy tried very hard, and soon all her toys were put away. Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Lucy! Now you can play with Max." Lucy was so happy! She ran outside and called, "Max! Come play!" Max barked and wagged his tail. They played all day long because Lucy's mom allowed her to play after passing the test. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a gloomy sunflower. It was sad because it was not big and bright like the other sunflowers. One day, the sunflower decided it wanted to become happy and strong. The sunflower tried to grow taller by reaching for the sun. It drank water and tried to smile. But, it still felt gloomy. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl saw the sunflower and gave it a hug. The hug made the sunflower feel loved and special. Suddenly, the sunflower started to grow bigger and brighter. It was not gloomy anymore. The sunflower and the little girl became best friends, and they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom and a little mouse named Jerry. They lived in a big house with many nice things. The house had a lot of luxury, like soft beds and yummy food. Tom and Jerry liked to play all day. One day, Tom found a sour apple in the kitchen. He tasted it and made a funny face. Jerry saw this and laughed. "Tom, why did you eat the sour apple?" he asked. Tom said, "I thought it would be sweet, but it was not!" Tom wanted to play a game with Jerry. He said, "Let's play catch! If I catch you, you must surrender." Jerry agreed and they started to play. They ran all around the house, but Tom could not catch Jerry. They had so much fun playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sally. Sally liked to wear unique clothes. One day, she wore a dress with lots of colors. It was very pretty. Sally went to her friend Tom's house. Tom saw her dress and said, "Wow! I like your dress. It is unique!" Sally smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. I like it too!" At Tom's house, they had some bread with jam. The jam was sweet and yummy. They had a fun day together, and Sally's unique dress made them both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little cat. She lived in a small house with a red door. The cat was too scared to go out and play with the other animals. One day, a kind boy saw the shy cat. He wanted to help her not be scared. The boy had an idea. He would feed the cat every day, and maybe she would not be so shy anymore. The boy fed the cat every day, and the cat started to trust him. Soon, the cat was not shy anymore. She played with the other animals and made many friends. The moral of the story is that kindness can help others overcome their fears.

Jimmy loved the seasons. Winter was his favorite because he could play in the snow. One day, Jimmy asked his mom, "I want a snowman." His mom said, "That's a lot of work, Jimmy! We need to buy an expensive hat and a scarf for him." Jimmy looked very sad. His mom then said, "Don't worry, when winter comes again we can get these things and make him together, ok?" Jimmy smiled. He thought it was a good plan, and said, "Ok, I want to make the best snowman ever!" He couldn't wait for winter to come!

Anna and Ben are playing with their toys in the garden. They have many animals, like a lion, a giraffe, a monkey and a zebra. Anna likes the zebra best. She says it is pretty and has stripes. Ben wants to make the zebra do tricks. He says it is fun and funny. He puts the zebra on a bike and pushes it down the hill. The zebra falls off the bike and lands in a bush. It is hurt and scared. It cannot move. It is helpless. Anna sees what Ben did and runs to the zebra. She is angry and sad. She says to Ben, "That is not nice! You hurt the zebra! It is not a toy, it is a friend! You should say sorry and help it!" Ben feels bad and sorry. He says to Anna, "I am sorry, Anna. I did not mean to hurt the zebra. I was just playing. I will help it." He goes to the bush and picks up the zebra. He hugs it and says, "I am sorry, zebra. You are a good friend. I will not make you do tricks again." Anna smiles and says, "Thank you, Ben. You are a good brother. I am glad you are sorry and help the zebra. The zebra is happy too. It likes you and me. We can all play together nicely." She hugs Ben and the zebra. They go back to the garden and play with the other animals. They have fun and are kind.
