One day, a boy named Tim went to school. He had a big red ball. His teach was very nice. She wanted to play a game. The game was to drop the ball and catch it. Tim was happy to play. Tim tried to play the game. He dropped the ball, but it was hard to catch. The ball went far away. The teach said, "Try again, Tim!" Tim tried many times. He did not give up. Then, something funny happened. A small dog came and picked up the ball. The dog was very fast. It dropped the ball right into Tim's hands. Tim was so happy. The teach clapped and said, "Good job, Tim and dog!" Now, Tim and the dog were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a blue vest. The blue vest lived in a big box with other clothes. One day, the blue vest wanted to go on an adventure. It jumped out of the box and started to walk around the house. On the way, the blue vest met a nice cat. The cat said, "Hello, blue vest! I want to go on an adventure too!" So, the blue vest and the cat walked together. They went to the kitchen and saw a big cake. The cat said, "Let's eat the cake!" They ate and ate until they could not eat anymore. Just when they thought they would finish the cake, something unexpected happened. The cake started to grow! It grew bigger and bigger. The blue vest and the cat were surprised. Then, the cake said, "Hello, friends! I am a magic cake. Thank you for eating me. Now, I can talk and play with you!" The blue vest, the cat, and the magic cake became best friends and had fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, in a big town, there was a colorful house. In this house, lived a happy family. The family loved to sing and dance. They would sing very loud. They wanted to go to the opera one day. One day, the family went to the opera. They were so excited. They wore their best clothes. They got in their car and went "zoom" down the street. They were going very fast. They could not wait to get there. At the opera, they heard beautiful music. They saw many people singing and dancing. The family was so happy. They clapped and cheered. They knew they would come back to the opera again. And when they went home, they went "zoom" down the street, singing and dancing all the way.

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play outside on the lawn. One sunny day, Fluffy went outside to play with his friends. Fluffy saw his friend, a little bird named Chirpy, splashing in a puddle. Fluffy wanted to splash too! So, he ran to the puddle and jumped right in. The water went splash, and Fluffy and Chirpy laughed. After they played in the puddle, Fluffy and Chirpy sat on the lawn. They looked at the sky and saw big white clouds. They were tired but happy. They had so much fun splashing and playing on the lawn.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a pet cat named Kitty. They were best friends and played together all day. One day, Tim's dad said they had to sell Kitty because they did not have enough money to buy food. Tim was very sad. A man came to buy Kitty. The man was harsh and did not seem kind. Tim did not want to sell Kitty to him. Tim said, "Please, Dad, do not sell Kitty to this man. He is not nice." Tim's dad listened to him and did not sell Kitty to the harsh man. Instead, they found a way to make more money by selling things they did not need. They were able to keep Kitty and buy food. Tim and Kitty were happy together again. The moral of the story is to listen to your heart and find a good solution to a problem.

Lily and Max were very excited. Today was their mom's birthday and they wanted to make her happy. They decided to decorate the kitchen with paper and crayons. They drew flowers, hearts, stars and balloons. They also wrote "Happy Birthday Mom" in big letters. They asked their dad to help them bake a cake. They mixed flour, sugar, eggs and butter in a big bowl. They poured the batter into a pan and put it in the oven. They waited for the cake to bake and then took it out. It smelled yummy. They let it cool down and then put some frosting and sprinkles on top. They also added some candles. They hid the cake under a cloth and waited for their mom to come home. When they heard the door open, they ran to the kitchen and shouted "Surprise!" Their mom was very surprised and happy. She hugged them and thanked them for the beautiful decoration and the delicious cake. She said it was the best treat ever. She asked them to help her blow out the candles. They counted to three and blew hard. But something unusual happened. The candles did not go out. They kept burning and sparkling. Lily and Max were confused. They tried to blow again, but the candles still did not go out. They looked at their dad, who was smiling and holding a box. He said, "These are special candles. They are magic. They do not go out until you make a wish. I bought them for your mom, because she is also special and magic. Do you want to help her make a wish?" Lily and Max nodded and smiled. They thought it was a fun and cool idea. They held their mom's hands and closed their eyes. They made a wish together. They wished for their mom to be happy and healthy and to have a wonderful birthday. Then they opened their eyes and blew one more time. The candles finally went out. They all clapped and cheered. They cut the cake and shared it. They had a great time. They were a happy family.
