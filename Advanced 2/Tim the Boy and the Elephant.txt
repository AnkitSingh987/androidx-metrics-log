Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to run and play in the park. One day, Tim saw a filthy folder on the ground. It was dirty and had mud all over it. He picked it up and opened it. Inside, there were many colorful pictures of animals. Tim ran to his mom and showed her the folder. "Look, Mom! I found a folder with animals!" he said. Mom looked at the folder and said, "It's very dirty, Tim. Let's clean it up." So they went home and cleaned the filthy folder. After it was clean, Tim and his mom looked at the pictures together. They saw a big elephant, a tall giraffe, and a fast cheetah. Tim loved the animals and the folder. He took it with him everywhere he went. And every time he looked at the pictures, he would remember the day he found it while running in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a mummy and a daddy with their baby. They were very excited to go to the mall. When they arrived, they saw that the mall was brilliantly lit up. The baby was so excited that he jumped up and down. Mummy said, "Let's stand here and look at all the things in the mall!" Daddy said, "Yes, that sounds like a great idea!" The baby was so happy to to stand in the mall. He got to see all sorts of colourful toys and sweet treats. He wanted to explore everywhere and so he started to run around. Daddy said, "Be careful, you don't want to get lost!" Mummy said, "Yes, stand still and look around." So the baby stood still and carefully looked around. He saw so many wonderful things and couldn't believe his eyes. Daddy saw this and said, "That's brilliant! You've seen so much already!" Mummy said, "Yes, now it's time to come back home!". The baby said goodbye to the mall and waved until he couldn't see it anymore. Everyone was so happy and excited to have gone to the mall. They couldn't wait to go again!

Peter and his dad were walking down the street when they heard a noise. It was loud and sounded like a car. They looked around and saw a big, wide car. "Wow!" said Peter. "That car is so cool!" "That must be a race car," said Dad. Peter wanted to get closer to the car, so they walked up to it. The windows were rolled down, and they could see the driver inside. He was wearing a white helmet and waved at them. "Where's he going?" asked Peter. "He's going to win a race," said Dad. Peter was so excited. He wished he could join the race and win too. They stayed and watched until the car disappeared in the distance. Peter knew one day he would be just like the car driver and win a race too.

One day, a boy named Tim got a new toy. It was a laser. It was a fun toy that made a bright light. Tim played with the laser all day. He showed it to his friends and they liked it too. But Tim did not take good care of his laser. He left it outside in the rain. The laser got wet and spoiled. It did not work anymore. Tim was very sad. Tim learned that he should take care of his toys. He told his mom about the spoiled laser. She said, "Next time, you should rest your toy in a safe place." Tim said, "I will do that, Mom." From then on, Tim took good care of his toys, and they did not get spoiled.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man. He had lots of money and things, but he didn't feel happy. One day, the man decided to try something different. He found a big slide and decided to slide down it. He loved it so much! The feeling of the wind against his face and the joy of being able to move so fast. After that, the man decided to slide down the slide every day. He would wave to his neighbors as he went by. One day, the man decided to try a different way down. He started to slide down a mountain path full of caves and shadows. It was a scary path but he kept going. Soon he could see a beautiful castle full of gold and jewels in the distance. The man arrived at the castle and was so amazed by the riches. He had never seen anything like it before. The man decided to move into the castle and live happily ever after. From then on, the slide was the only way he would go.
