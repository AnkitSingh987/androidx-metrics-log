Once upon a time, there was a thin panda named Pandy. Pandy lived in a big forest with many trees. He had a lot of friends who loved to play with him all day long. One day, Pandy and his friends were playing hide and seek. Pandy found a big box to hide in. He went inside the box and waited for his friends to find him. After a while, Pandy heard his friend Bunny say, "Where is Pandy? I can't find him anywhere!" Pandy called out from the box, "I'm in here, Bunny!" Bunny went to the box and tried to remove the lid to let Pandy out. But the lid was stuck, and Bunny was not strong enough to open it. Bunny called their friend, Elephant, to help. Elephant came and used his strong trunk to remove the lid from the box. Pandy was free and happy to be out of the box. All the friends cheered and clapped. They continued playing hide and seek, and Pandy found a new hiding spot that was not as tricky as the box.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went to the park to meet his friend, Sam. The sun was shining, and the day was dry and warm. Tim was happy and excited to play with Sam. They liked to play with their toy cars and run around in the grass. As Tim and Sam played, they saw a big tree. They wanted to climb it and see how high they could go. As they climbed higher and higher, they felt very brave. They were having a lot of fun and felt like they had success. Suddenly, they heard a soft voice. "Hello!" it said. Tim and Sam looked around, surprised. They saw a talking bird sitting on a branch. The bird was friendly and wanted to play with the boys. They all played together, and it was the most fun day they ever had. The unexpected friend made their day even better.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy bear who lived in the woods. He was always happy and friendly, but he was all alone. One day, a beautiful fox came along. She was friendly and sweet, too. They both wanted to be together, so they got married. The sleepy bear and the fox knew they wanted to protect their marriage forever. The bear and the fox worked hard to take care of each other, laughing and playing together. They were very happy together in the woods. The bear and the fox taught us that happiness comes when we work together, care for each other, and protect our marriages. The sleepy bear and the fox lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He wanted to wear his favorite red hat to school. But his big sister, Sue, said she wanted to wear it too. They both loved the red hat very much. Tim felt sad and did not know what to do. One day, their mom said there would be a fun race at the park. The prize was a big, shiny star. Tim and Sue both wanted to win the prize. They knew they had to be fast and strong. So, they made a plan to help each other win the race. The day of the race came, and Tim and Sue worked together. They ran very fast, and they were winning. But in the end, a little dog jumped in and won the race. The little dog got the big, shiny star. Tim and Sue did not win the prize, but they learned that working together was the best prize of all. And they both got to wear the red hat.

Once there was a tall man. He was very proud of his trophy. He kept it on a shelf so he could show it off to everyone who came to his house. One day he decided to go to the store to get some things and he left his trophy on the shelf. But when he came back, he saw that something had happened! His trophy had been spoiled. He was very sad. He looked everywhere and tried to figure out who had spoiled it. Then, he saw his cat. It was in the corner with a guilty look on its face. The cat had spoiled the man’s trophy! The man was very angry with the cat, but he couldn’t stay mad for long. He picked up the cat and hugged it tightly. He was sad about his trophy, but he knew that it was just an accident. He forgave the cat and promised he would get it a new trophy.
