Once there was a little boy called Tom. He was only three years old and he loved exploring. One day he decided to go for a walk in a nearby street. It was a nice, lively street with lots of people. Tom thought it looked interesting, so he walked further and further along it. Suddenly, he stopped in front of a shop window. It was full of bright, shiny things and he wanted to peek inside. He stepped closer, but then he heard someone calling his name. It was his mum, who had been looking for him! Tom's mum called, "Tom, what are you doing? It's time to go home now!" Tom replied, "Oh, I just wanted to look around, I was having so much fun." "Well, okay" said his mum, "but we need to part now." Tom smiled as they said goodbye and waved to the street. He loved the lively street; he wanted to come back soon!

Once upon a time, there was a big barrel in a small town. The barrel was very hard and strong. Many people in the town liked to play with the barrel. They would roll it up and down the street, laughing and having fun. One day, a little girl named Mia found the barrel. She decided to roll it up a big hill, but it was very hard work. When she finally got it to the top, she let it go. The barrel rolled down the hill very fast. Mia ran after it, but she could not catch it. The barrel was gone! Mia was very sad, but she did not give up. She looked all over town for the barrel. Suddenly, the barrel returned! It rolled back to her, all by itself. Mia was very surprised and happy. She hugged the barrel and promised never to let it go again. The barrel and Mia became the best of friends and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy plate that she loved very much. She played with her plate every day. One day, it started to rain. The rain was gentle and soft. Lily looked out the window and saw the rain falling. She wanted to play outside, but she didn't want her plate to get wet. Lily put on her raincoat and boots. She went outside with her plate. She held the plate up and let the gentle rain fall on it. Lily was happy to play in the rain with her toy plate. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia went outside to play. She saw an enormous tree with many pretty leaves. Mia wanted to admire the tree up close, so she walked closer to it. As she got closer, she saw a big pile of dust under the tree. Mia wanted to touch the dust, but her mom had told her not to play with dust. Mia was sad because she wanted to play with the dust and the tree. Mia thought for a moment and then got an idea. She ran inside and got a broom. She came back outside and swept the dust away from the tree. Now, Mia could admire the enormous tree without touching the dust. She was happy and played under the tree all day.

Once upon a time, there was a kind frog named Sam. Sam lived in a big pond with many other animals. Sam liked to help his friends and make them happy. One day, Sam saw a little girl named Lily sitting by the pond. She looked sad. Sam hopped over and asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily said, "I lost my wonder toy. It was my favorite toy, and now it's gone." Sam wanted to help Lily find her toy. Sam and Lily looked all around the pond. They looked under rocks and in the tall grass. Finally, they found the wonder toy behind a big tree. Lily was so happy! She gave Sam a big kiss on his cheek and said, "Thank you, Sam! You are the kindest frog I know." Sam felt happy too, because he had helped his new friend.
