Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was three and she loved going on adventures. One cold winter day, Sue decided to go for a walk. She put on her warm coat, her hat and gloves and went out the door. Sue walked through the icy snow, until she reached the side of the road. A truck was stopped and the driver was loading something. Sue went up to the truck and said, "What are you loading, mister?" The driver said, "I'm loading some wood, little one. It's going to stay warm inside." Sue smiled and said, "I wish I can stay warm too." The driver smiled back and said, "Get inside the truck, little one. I'll keep it warm with the heater." So Sue climbed inside and the driver turned on the heater. It was so warm and cozy inside. Sue smiled and thanked the driver. The driver finished loading and drove Sue home. The icy wind was blowing, but Sue was safe and warm inside. When they arrived at Sue's house, the driver said goodbye and waved. Sue waved goodbye and went inside. She had a wonderful adventure and was glad she stayed warm and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside with his friends. One day, while playing in the mud, Tom got very dirty. His clothes and hands were full of dirt. Tom's mom saw him and said, "Tom, you must wash your hands and change your clothes." Tom went inside to wash his hands and change his clothes. After he was clean, his mom gave him a gift. It was a big, red ball. Tom was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Mom!" His mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tom. Remember to always be clean and say thank you when someone gives you something." Tom learned that day that being clean and saying thank you were important. He promised to always wash his hands and say thank you when someone gave him a gift. And he played with his big, red ball every day, always remembering to be clean and thankful.

Once there was a little girl who loved playing with her eraser. It was pink and it made her smile when she used it to draw. One day, the little girl was having fun outside when suddenly the sun appeared from behind the clouds. It was hot! The little girl ran inside the house, but then something weird happened. She looked at her eraser and noticed it was starting to melt! The little girl was so surprised and frightened, she looked out of the window and saw the sun was still shining brightly. ‘It’s so hot!’ the little girl yelled. The little girl's mother heard her and ran to her side. ‘Ah, the heat can sometimes make things melt. Let's put that eraser in the fridge,’ said mom. The little girl put the eraser in the fridge and ran outside to play. Now she was more careful when the sun was out, and kept her eraser away from the heat.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lily who really loved to zoom. She zoomed around the garden, around the house, and around town. Everywhere she went, everyone would smile and cheer as she zoomed by. One day, Lily zoomed to the farm, where she stopped to watch some wheat grow. It was so tall and so beautiful. She wished she could have some, so she started to pick the ripe wheat. But before she could get too far, Farmer Tom came running up. "What are you doing, Lily?" he asked. "This wheat isn't for you. You can't take it." Lily pouted. She thought the wheat was so spoiled, just ripe for the picking! She started to pick some more, despite Farmer Tom's protests. He then grabbed her hand. "Lily, don't do that," he said. "This wheat is not yours. It belongs to the farm. If you want some wheat, I can give you some. Just don't take the wheat that's not yours." Lily nodded, understanding Farmer Tom's words. From then on, Lily always asked before she zoomed away with anything. She knew it was wrong to take things that didn't belong to her.
