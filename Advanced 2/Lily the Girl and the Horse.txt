One day, a little girl named Lily found a magazine on the floor. She picked it up and looked at the pictures. She saw a big, stubborn horse that would not move. Lily thought the horse was funny. Lily showed the magazine to her friend, Tom. They tried to guess what the horse was thinking. "Maybe he wants a snack," said Lily. "Or maybe he wants to play," said Tom. They laughed and made up more funny ideas. At the end of the day, Lily and Tom put the magazine away. They were happy they had so much fun with a stubborn horse in a magazine. They knew they could always guess and make up stories together.

One day, a gifted bird named Lily loved to soar high in the sky. She was the best at flying. All her bird friends looked up to her. Lily was very happy. One morning, Lily met a little bird who could not fly. The little bird said, "Lily, can you help me fly? I want to soar like you." Lily thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I can help you." Lily and the little bird practiced flying every day. One day, the little bird could finally fly. Lily was very proud. The little bird said, "Thank you, Lily! Now I can soar too!" And they flew together, happy and free. But one day, Lily could not fly. She was very sad. The little bird saw Lily and said, "Don't worry, I will help you now." The little bird helped Lily get better at flying again. The moral of the story is that when you help others, they will help you too.

Once upon a time, there was a hero. This hero was very light and could jump high. The hero had a sharp stick to help people. One day, the hero saw a cat stuck in a tree. The hero used the sharp stick to make a ladder. The hero climbed up the tree and saved the cat. The cat was happy and the hero felt good. Everyone called the hero a true hero. The hero went home and took a nap, ready for more adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a big gorilla named Gogo. Gogo was very sad and miserable. He had no friends to play with in the jungle. One day, Gogo saw a little bird. The bird was trying to guess what was wrong with Gogo. The bird asked Gogo, "Why are you so sad?" Gogo said, "I have no friends to play with." The bird thought for a moment and said, "I will be your friend, Gogo!" Gogo was very happy to have a new friend. They played together all day long. From that day on, Gogo was not miserable anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy tutor named Mr. Bear. He was big and kind. He liked to help little animals learn new things. One day, Mr. Bear met a small bird named Tiny. Tiny wanted to learn how to rise high in the sky. "Can you help me rise, Mr. Bear?" Tiny asked. Mr. Bear smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you, Tiny." Mr. Bear and Tiny practiced every day. Tiny flapped her wings and tried to rise. Soon, Tiny could rise high in the sky. She was so happy. Mr. Bear was proud of her, too. They both laughed and played together, and Tiny became the best rising bird in the forest.

Once upon a time there was a little reindeer who wanted to go for a swim. Reindeer like to swim but it is not always very safe in the wild. The little reindeer asked his mother, "Can I go for a swim? I want to be pink like the water." His mother answered, "No! It's too dangerous. Stay on dry land". But the little reindeer didn't listen, he was too eager to jump in the water and turn pink. So without his mom's permission, he jumped in the water. But the water was very deep. He went under and he couldn't swim back up. He tried and tried but the water was too strong and he was too weak. Soon, the little reindeer had drowned and he was gone forever. Moral of the story: Always listen to your mother's advice, even when it seems hard at the time. It's better to be safe than sorry!
