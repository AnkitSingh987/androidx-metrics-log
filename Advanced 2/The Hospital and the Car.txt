Once upon a time, there was a big hospital. Inside the hospital, there were many doctors who helped people feel better. One day, a little boy named Timmy came to the hospital with his mom. Timmy had a toy car that was broken. He was very sad. In the hospital, Timmy saw a girl named Lucy. Lucy had a new toy car that was not broken. Timmy felt envious of Lucy's toy car. He wanted to play with a toy car too. Timmy's mom saw that he was sad and had an idea. Timmy's mom talked to a doctor at the hospital. The doctor helped Timmy replace his broken toy car with a new one. Now, Timmy had a new toy car just like Lucy. Timmy and Lucy played together with their toy cars and became good friends. They were both very happy in the hospital.

Once upon a time, in a big nation, there was a kind man named Tom. Tom was very good at helping people. He liked to write letters to his friends. One day, Tom met a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily said, "I want to write a letter to my friend, but I don't know how." Tom smiled and said, "I can help you write a letter. Let's do it together." Lily was happy. They sat down and wrote a good letter to Lily's friend. From that day on, Lily and Tom became good friends. They wrote many letters to people in their nation. Everyone was happy and loved their letters. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, a mom made some pasta. It was perfect pasta. The mom was happy. She called her little girl, Lily. "Lily, come eat the perfect pasta!" she said. Lily came to the table. She saw the pasta. It was not normal pasta. The pasta started to glow! It was a soft, pretty glow. Lily was so surprised. "Mom, the pasta is glowing!" she said. Mom looked at the glowing pasta. She smiled. "This is magic pasta," she said. "If you eat it, you will have a perfect day!" Lily ate the glowing pasta. She had the most perfect day ever.

Tom and Sam were brothers who liked to play with toys. Tom had a new toy that he got for his birthday. It was a laser that made bright lights and sounds. Sam wanted to play with it too, but Tom did not want to share. One day, Tom went to the park with his mom and left his laser at home. Sam saw it on the shelf and took it. He thought he could sell it to his friend Ben, who liked lasers too. He put it in his backpack and went to Ben's house. Ben was happy to see the laser and gave Sam some money for it. Sam felt good and went to buy some candy. But when he opened the backpack, he saw that the laser was not there. He had taken the wrong toy. He had taken Tom's rough bear that he loved very much. Sam was scared and ran back to Ben's house. He hoped that Ben did not notice the mistake. But when he got there, he saw that Ben was angry and crying. He had tried to play with the bear, but it was too rough and scratched his face. He threw the bear at Sam and wanted his money back. Sam felt bad and sorry. He gave Ben his money and took the bear. He ran home and put the bear back on the shelf. He hoped that Tom did not find out. He wished he had not tried to sell his brother's toy. He learned that sharing was better than selling.

Once upon a time, there was a man who had a very special job. His job was to restore old things. One day, he was called to restore a big black ladder. When he arrived, he saw the ladder and said to himself, "Wow, this ladder is very old and black. It needs some work." The man then went to look for the tools he needed. When he was ready to begin restoring the ladder, he heard a young voice: "Can I help you?" The man turned around and saw a little boy. "Yes, you can help me," said the man. Together, they worked hard to restore the old black ladder. After some time, it began to look brand new. The man thanked the little boy for all his help. The little boy smiled and said, "This ladder looks really nice! I'm happy to help restore it." The man then said goodbye and left with the restored black ladder. And they all lived happily ever after.
