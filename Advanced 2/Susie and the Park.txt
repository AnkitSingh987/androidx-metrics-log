Once upon a time there was a little girl called Susie. She loved playing outside but particularly loved going to the park to gaze at all the trees as they sprung back to life after the cold winter. Each day Susie would take a basket with her to the park and fill it with treasure she found, like pinecones, stones and leaves. One day she found an onion growing from the ground which she was very excited about. She took it home to show her mum, who was so grateful for Susie’s discovery. “Let’s make some onion soup for lunch,” said Mum. As Susie was stirring the pot of soup, Mum said to her “Being kind and thoughtful are always important, and it is even better when you give something of value”. Susie felt very grateful that she had found the onion and smiled with delight. She shared the soup they made with her family and they all enjoyed the taste. The moral of the story is that being kind and thoughtful can have surprising results, and by being generous you will always be grateful.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fun event. All the kids were very happy. They played games, ate yummy food, and laughed a lot. One little girl, named Mia, saw her friend Tom. Tom was very alert and always ready to help. Mia said to Tom, "Tom, can you bring me a big red ball?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, Mia. I will bring it to you." Tom ran fast and found a big red ball. He brought it to Mia. Mia was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" At the event, Mia and Tom played with the big red ball. They threw it high in the sky and caught it. They had lots of fun. Soon, it was time to go home. Mia and Tom said goodbye to each other. They knew they would see each other again at the next fun event.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the hospital with her mom. Lucy saw a big yellow truck outside the hospital. She liked the yellow truck very much. Inside the hospital, Lucy saw a boy named Tim. Tim was sad. Lucy asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I am sad because I fell down and hurt my leg. I feel embarrass." Lucy wanted to make Tim feel better. She told Tim about the big yellow truck outside. Tim smiled and said, "I like yellow trucks too!" They both laughed and felt happy. The hospital was not so scary after all.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the sun. The cat was dry and warm. The dog said, "Come, cat! Let's play and have fun!" The cat said, "Yes, let's play, but I want to stay dry." The dog was happy and they played together in peace. In the sky, dark clouds came. The cat saw them and felt scared. The dog saw the cat's face and said, "Don't worry, cat. We can still play and have fun. I will keep you dry." The cat felt better and they kept playing, but the dark clouds came closer. Then, something unexpected happened. The dark clouds went away, and the sun came back out. The cat and the dog were surprised and happy. They played all day in the sun and stayed dry. The cat said, "Thank you, dog, for keeping me dry and happy." They played in peace and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his equipment. He had many useful toys like balls, cars, and blocks. One day, while playing, Tim found a piece of paper. Tim's friend, Sara, came over to play. "Look, Sara," said Tim, "I found this paper. What can we do with it?" Sara thought for a moment and said, "Let's tear it and make something fun!" So, they began to tear the paper into small pieces. With the torn paper, Tim and Sara made a big, fun mess. They threw the pieces into the air and let them fall like rain. They laughed and played all day. In the end, they learned that even a simple piece of paper could be a useful toy.
