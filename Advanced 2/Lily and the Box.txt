Lily liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Anna. Anna had a blue dress and a yellow hat. Lily liked to dress Anna in different clothes. She had a big box of clothes for her dolls. One day, Lily found a new dress in the box. It was red and shiny, with flowers and ribbons. Lily thought it was very pretty. She wanted to try it on Anna. But when she put the dress on Anna, it was too big. It did not fit. Lily was sad. She wanted Anna to wear the new dress. She looked in the box again. Maybe there was another dress that would fit Anna. She saw a brown dress. It looked old and plain. It had no flowers or ribbons. Lily did not like it very much. But she decided to try it on Anna. Maybe it would fit. She put the brown dress on Anna. It fit perfectly. Lily was surprised. She looked at Anna more closely. She saw that the brown dress had small patterns on it. They looked like stars and moons and animals. They were very beautiful. Lily realized that the brown dress was not old and plain. It was ancient and special. It was a surprise. Lily smiled. She liked the brown dress now. She thought Anna looked very nice in it. She hugged Anna and said, "You are my best doll. I love you." Anna smiled back. She was happy too. She liked the brown dress too. She liked Lily more.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a stream with clear water. Tom was very thirsty, so he went to drink from the stream. A little bird named Sue saw Tom and said, "Tom, you must behave near the stream. Do not drink too much, or you will get sick." Tom did not listen to Sue and drank a lot of water. Suddenly, the water in the stream started to move fast. Tom was scared. He jumped back and said, "Sue, help me!" Sue flew down and helped Tom get away from the stream. They became good friends and Tom learned to behave.

One day, a big red ball was on a porch. It liked to bounce high in the sky. The ball was happy when it bounced. It made the kids happy too. A small green ball saw the big red ball. The small green ball felt jealous. It wanted to bounce high too. The small green ball said, "I want to bounce like you. Can you help me?" The big red ball smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's bounce together and have fun!" So, the big red ball and the small green ball bounced high together. They both made the kids happy. The small green ball was not jealous anymore. It was happy to bounce with its new friend. The big red ball and the small green ball became best friends. They loved to bounce and play on the porch every day.

Once upon a time, there was a small creek. Many animals lived near the creek. One day, a new animal came to the creek. His name was Timmy the Turtle. Timmy was very competitive. He liked to race with his friends. The other animals saw Timmy and said, "Welcome, Timmy! Do you want to play with us?" Timmy said, "Yes, I want to play. Let's have a race!" The animals were excited. They knew Timmy was fast, but they wanted to try. The race began. Timmy was very fast, but his friend, Bobby the Bunny, was faster. Timmy did not win the race. He was sad. Bobby said, "Don't be sad, Timmy. We can race again another day." The next day, Timmy practiced racing. He wanted to be faster. He raced with Bobby again. This time, Timmy won the race. All the animals cheered for Timmy. They were happy to have a new friend at the creek.

Once there was a girl named Anna. She loved playing with her toy model car. It was her favorite. One day, when Anna was playing with her model car, a weird bug ran in front of her. She wanted to pick it up, but it bit her! Anna was scared and jumped back. She quickly ran away from the bug. She felt a bit sad. Anna decided to go and find her mommy. She told her all about the weird bug that bit her. Her mommy was very concerned and quickly took her to the doctor. The doctor examined Anna and checked to make sure there was no infection from the bite. Thankfully, the doctor said Anna was all healthy. Anna was so relieved. She knew that she had to be careful when playing and she should never touch things that were weird and strange again. From then on, she stuck to playing with her model car and happily stayed away from anything that seemed suspicious.
