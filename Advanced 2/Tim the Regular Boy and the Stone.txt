Once upon a time, there was a regular boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and dog. Every day, Tim would play with his dog in the yard. One sunny day, Tim found a magic stone. The stone would blink like a star. Tim showed the stone to his mom. "What is this, Mom?" he asked. Mom looked at the stone and said, "This is a magic stone, Tim. It can make a print of anything you want." Tim was happy. He held the stone and said, "I want a print of my dog." The stone blinked, and there was a print of his dog. "Wow!" said Tim. He showed the print to his dad. Dad smiled and said, "This is a great print, Tim. You found something very special." Tim and his dog played with the magic stone all day. They made prints of many things. Tim loved his magic stone and knew he would have fun with it forever.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little girl named Ava. She loved to eat avocado every day. One day, Ava's mom told her, "We have no more avocado at home." Ava was sad. Ava and her mom went to the store to buy more avocado. They saw a big green avocado on the shelf. Ava's mom disagreed and said, "This avocado is too big." But Ava wanted the big avocado, so her mom bought it. When they got home, Ava opened the big avocado. Surprise! Inside the avocado was a small, cute bird. The bird became Ava's new friend, and they ate avocado together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big room filled with toys. The toys were very organized. They were in nice rows, and the room was clean. In the room, there was a toy monkey with long arms. The monkey loved to clap its hands and make music. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily came into the room. She saw the monkey with the long arms. Lily picked up the monkey and started to play with it. She made the monkey clap its hands. The sound was fun and made Lily very happy. Lily played with the monkey all day. She made it clap its hands and dance to the music. The other toys watched and enjoyed the show. At the end of the day, Lily put the monkey back in its organized spot. She gave it a hug and said goodnight. The monkey was very happy to have a friend like Lily.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a thief. The thief liked to pinch things from other people. One day, he saw a big, wet ball in a park. The ball was so wet because it had rained the day before. The thief thought it would be fun to take the wet ball. A little boy named Tim saw the thief trying to take the wet ball. Tim said, "Hey, that's my ball! Please don't take it!" The thief felt bad and decided to give the ball back to Tim. Tim and the thief became friends. They played with the wet ball together. The thief learned that it was more fun to play with others than to pinch their things. From that day on, the thief stopped being a thief and became a good friend to everyone in the town.

Once upon a time, there was a panda named Pandy. Pandy was a very noisy panda. He loved to play and have fun with his friends in the forest. One day, he met a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was very small, but she liked to sing. "Hi Chirpy," said Pandy. "Do you want to have fun with me?" "Yes, Pandy," said Chirpy. "I like to sing. Can we sing together?" So Pandy and Chirpy sang a song. They were very noisy, but they had a lot of fun. They sang and played all day long. All the other animals in the forest could hear them. At the end of the day, Pandy and Chirpy were very tired. They said goodbye to each other and went to their homes. Pandy went to sleep in his cozy bamboo house, and Chirpy went to sleep in her little nest. They both had a very happy day.
