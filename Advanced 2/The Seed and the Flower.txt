Once there was a tiny seed. It was planted in the ground. One day, there was an icy wind. It blew and blew. Suddenly, the seed bloomed and the ground around it shook and trembled. It was an earthquake! As the earthquake stopped, the seed was now a big and bright flower. Everyone was so happy to see it. It was so colorful and so beautiful. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a tiny, adorable bug named Ben. Ben loved to crawl all over the forest floor, looking for yummy leaves to eat. Every day, he felt the urge to explore new places and make new friends. One sunny day, while Ben was crawling, he met a nice bird named Bella. Bella was sad because she lost her way home. Ben had an idea. He told Bella to follow him, and they started to crawl together. As they crawled, they saw a big, twisty path. Ben and Bella decided to follow it. To their surprise, they found Bella's home at the end of the path! Bella was so happy, she hugged Ben. They learned that helping each other is the best way to solve problems. From that day on, Ben and Bella were the best of friends, and they always helped others in need.

Once there were two babies, a girl and a boy. They were playmates, but were very different from each other. The little boy was fierce, strong and brave, while the little girl was gentle and kind. One day they both saw an amazing car in a car show and decided to buy it. But they had to separate their money to do so. The girl offered her savings to the boy, because she was kind. "No, I can share mine," the boy said, but the girl replied, "It's OK, I have enough. Let's share!" The boy thought for a moment and said, "OK. Thank you!" They then bought their car and it was the best! The moral of this story is that sharing is caring. Being kind even to those who are different from us is always the best thing to do.

One day, Mama and her three-year old daughter walked into the kitchen. Mama said, "Let's prepare a yummy meal together." The daughter said, "Yes!" She pulled out her bright green wallet and said, "I've got some money in here!" Mama smiled, "That's great, but what do you need that for?" The daughter hummed, "I want to buy something special at the store. Maybe broccoli!" Mama smiled, "That's a great idea! Now let's get started preparing our meal." The daughter carefully put her wallet back in her pocket and helped Mama with the meal. Finally, the delicious meal was ready to eat. They both sat down and enjoyed it together. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to match things. One day, he went to the park with his mom. The park had a beautiful view. Tim saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was adorable. Tim wanted to play with Lily. He asked her, "Do you want to play match with me?" Lily said, "Yes, I would love to play match!" They started to match toys, like cars and dolls. They were having a lot of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all their toys away! Tim and Lily were sad. But then, they saw that the wind had matched all their toys in a big tree. They laughed and said, "The wind helped us match our toys!" And they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tom. He liked to spend time with his friends. One day, Tom and his friend Sue went to play near a big tree. As they played, they heard a loud crack. "What was that?" asked Sue. "I don't know," said Tom. They looked around and saw a big egg near the tree. The egg had a small crack in it. Tom and Sue were very surprised. They watched the egg as the crack got bigger and bigger. Suddenly, a cute baby bird came out of the egg. But it was not a normal bird. It could talk! "Hello, I am a magic bird!" said the baby bird. Tom and Sue were so happy to have a new magic friend. They spent lots of time playing and having fun together.
