One day, a little girl named Sue was playing in her garden. She saw a pretty flower that was sad and dry. Sue was anxious and wanted to help the flower. She ran to her mom and said, "Mom, the flower is sad and dry. What can I do?" Her mom smiled and said, "Sue, the flower needs water. Can you give it some water?" Sue was happy to help. She took a small cup and filled it with water. She ran back to the garden and gave the flower a drink. The flower became happy and not dry anymore. Sue felt proud and knew she could help her flowers grow.

Jimmy was excited. Today he was going to put his new football. He had been waiting for this moment for weeks. He put on his jersey and grabbed the football. His mom organized the backyard so Jimmy would have a lot of room to play. He ran outside and put the football down in the middle. He wanted to kick it, but he wasn't tall enough. So instead he threw it up in the air and caught it with both hands. Then he rolled it to the side and picked it up again. He practiced throwing and catching his football over and over. After a while he started to get tired, so he put the football back in his room. He was happy that he got to use his football and he knew that he would play with it a lot. He was so organized!

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who was very unique. He had a blue head, a red body, and green wings. All the other birds in the forest were just one color. The little bird was sad because he was different. One day, the unique bird decided to speak to the other birds. He flew to each bird and said, "Hi, I am a unique bird. Can we be friends?" The other birds would look at him and say, "You are different, but we can be friends." Soon, all the birds in the forest were friends with the unique bird. They played games, sang songs, and flew around together. The unique bird was happy because he had many friends who liked him for who he was.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion was very strong and lived in a big forest. All the animals were scared of the lion. They all did what the lion said because he was so big. One day, a small mouse came to the lion. The mouse was very small and not strong, but the mouse was not scared. The lion told the mouse to go away, but the mouse did not listen. The mouse did not resist the lion. The lion was very surprised. The lion and the mouse became friends. The mouse was very obedient and listened to the lion. The lion was happy to have a friend. All the animals in the forest saw this and learned that being kind and obedient is more important than being big and strong.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to draw with her crayons. She had a big box with many colors. Her favorite crayon was the red one. She drew big flowers, happy suns, and funny animals with her crayons. One day, Sue went to play with her friend Tom. They played with toys, ran in the park, and had fun. Then, Sue wanted to show Tom her drawings. She opened her crayon box, but her red crayon was gone! Sue looked everywhere, but she could not find it. She was sad and started to scream. Tom heard Sue scream and came to help her. He saw her crayon box and the missing red crayon. Tom had a little red crayon in his pocket. He gave it to Sue, and she was very happy. They both drew many pictures with their crayons and had a great day together.

One day, a smelly cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a symbol on it. The symbol was a big, red heart. Tom was very curious about the symbol. Tom met a friend, a dog named Max. Max said, "Hi Tom! What are you looking at?" Tom said, "I see a big, red heart on the tree." Max looked at the tree and said, "Oh, that is a nice symbol!" Tom and Max decided to rub the symbol on the tree. They thought it might be magic. They rubbed and rubbed, but nothing happened. They laughed and played near the tree with the big, red heart. Even though the symbol was not magic, Tom and Max had a fun day together. They learned that sometimes, it is not about finding magic, but about having fun with friends. And so, the smelly cat and his friend, the dog, went home happy.
