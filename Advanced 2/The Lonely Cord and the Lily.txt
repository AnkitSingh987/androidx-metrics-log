Once upon a time, there was a lonely cord. The cord lay on the floor in a big room. It was sad because it had no friends to play with. One day, a little girl named Lily came into the room. She saw the cord and said, "Hi, cord! Do you want to be my friend?" The cord felt happy and nodded its head. Lily picked up the cord and they played all day. They jumped, danced, and laughed together. The cord was not lonely anymore, and it had a best friend in Lily. They were happy and had fun every day.

One day, a little cat named Tom was in the garden. He saw a long vine hanging from a tree. Tom was scared, but he wanted to play with the vine. So, he took a deep breath and jumped to grab the vine. As Tom held onto the vine, it began to speed. It moved fast, and Tom was going up and down in the air. He was scared but also having fun. Tom's friend, a little dog named Max, saw him and said, "Tom, you are so brave! I want to try too." Max jumped onto the vine with Tom, and they both went up and down, speeding through the air. Suddenly, the vine stopped moving. They looked up and saw a big, friendly bird who was moving the vine. The bird said, "I wanted to play with you. I hope I didn't scare you." Tom and Max were surprised but happy. They all played together, taking turns on the speeding vine. And from that day on, they were no longer scared of the vine, and they had a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time there was a car. She liked to go places, but she had a problem. She had no fuel! The car was very sad and she worried that she would never get to go anywhere ever again. Suddenly, a magical creature appeared. He had a creative solution. "I can make you some special fuel with my magic," he said. The car was very excited and asked him, "What do I need?" The creature smiled and replied, "Bring me some sugar and I will make the fuel!" The car quickly drove away to get the sugar and soon returned. The creature mixed the sugar with his magic and created the fuel! The car thanked the creature, filled up her tank and happily drove off into the sunset. She could now go again, and it was all thanks to the creature's creative magic!

Once upon a time, there was a big sunflower. It lived in a field with many other flowers. Every day, the sunflower would see a girl pass by. The girl was very careful not to step on any flowers. One day, the girl saw the sunflower and wanted to pick it. She thought it was the most beautiful flower she had ever seen. The sunflower did not want to be picked, but it could not run away. The girl got closer and closer to the sunflower. Just as she was about to pick the sunflower, a big wind came. It blew the girl far away. The sunflower was sad because it wanted to be friends with the girl, but now she was gone.

One day, a thin bunny named Tim went to play with his friends. He liked to hop a lot. He hopped to the park where his friends were waiting. They all wanted to play a game, but they could not agree on which game to play. "Let's play tag!" said Tim. His friend, a small bird, did not like that idea. "No, I want to play hide and seek," said the bird. They argued and could not find the truth about the best game to play. An old, wise turtle saw them arguing and came to help. "Why are you fighting?" asked the turtle. Tim and the bird told him about their problem. The turtle said, "You both want to have fun, so why not play both games? You can take turns." Tim and the bird agreed to play tag first, and then hide and seek. They all had a great time and learned that it is better to share and take turns. The truth is, playing together is more fun when everyone is happy.
