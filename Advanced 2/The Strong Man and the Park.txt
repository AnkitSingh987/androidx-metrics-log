One day, a strong man went for a walk in the park. He saw a thief take a bag from a lady. The strong man said, "Stop! Give the bag back!" The thief did not listen and ran away. The strong man ran after the thief. He was very fast. The thief had to pause to catch his breath. The strong man said, "You are bad! Give the bag back now!" The thief shook his head and ran away again. The strong man tried to catch the thief, but he was too tired. The thief got away with the lady's bag. The strong man was sad. The lady was sad too. The thief was happy, but he was still a bad person.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Molly. She loved looking up at the big moon in the night sky. Every night, she would sit on her balcony and admire the beautiful moon. One night, something bad happened. There was a scary storm and the wind was blowing so hard! The trees on Molly’s street were trebling, and Molly was scared. Molly saw her mum, and ran to her. She said, “Mummy, I’m frightened! Will the moon stay safe?” Molly’s mum smiled and said, “Of course, the moon will stay safe. Don’t worry, this storm will pass soon.” Molly felt better after hearing her mum’s words. She hugged her mum and said, “The moon is so beautiful. I’m glad it’s safe!” Molly and her mum looked up at the sky and admired the moon. The storm had calmed down, and the moon was shining brightly. Molly smiled and said, “I won’t be scared of bad storms again.”

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved. It was red and shiny. He played with it all day. One day, Tim saw an expensive, big toy car in the store. He wanted it so much. Tim asked his mom, "Can I have the big car, please?" His mom said, "No, Tim. It is too expensive. You have a toy car at home." Tim was sad but he knew his mom was right. Later, Tim was playing outside with his red car. He put the car on the side of the road. He forgot about it and went to play with his friends. When he came back, his car was gone! Tim felt regret for leaving his car alone. Just then, Tim saw his little sister, Sue, holding his car. She said, "I found your car, Tim. I knew you would be sad if you lost it." Tim was so happy and hugged Sue. He didn't need the expensive car anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big, green lawn. On the lawn, there was a kind and compassionate dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends on the lawn. They would run, jump, and have lots of fun together. One sunny day, Spot and his friends were playing on the lawn. They were having a great time. But then, Spot started to sneeze. He sneezed once, twice, and then three times. His friends stopped playing and looked at him. They were worried about Spot. Spot's compassionate friends wanted to help him. They brought him some water to drink. They also found a soft, cozy blanket for him to rest on. Spot felt better after resting for a while. Soon, he stopped sneezing and was ready to play again. Spot and his friends played happily on the lawn for the rest of the day.
