One sunny day, Sam was walking outside. Suddenly he heard a harsh whistle. He looked up and saw a bird scatter in the sky. Sam smiled and the bird flew across the sky. Sam followed the bird and after a few minutes he arrived at a big field with lots of flowers. Sam smiled when he looked at the beautiful colors of the flowers. The bird had flown over to a tree and was looking down at Sam. Sam waved his hand and the bird started to whistle again. Then suddenly the bird flew away, scattering the seeds from the tree. Sam watched the bird until it disappeared in the sky. He smiled and smiled. He had seen a beautiful bird and it had been whistling its beautiful song.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little pink bug. The bug was very happy because it could do something special. It could perform a dance. One day, the pink bug wanted to show its dance to its friends. So, it called all its friends to come and watch. The friends were very excited to see the pink bug perform. The pink bug started to dance. It moved its legs and wings in a fun way. All the friends clapped and cheered. They loved the pink bug's dance. The pink bug was so happy that it performed the dance again and again. And they all had a great time in the big garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She was pale with big, blue eyes. One day, Papa decided to take Jane for a walk. They started at the park. In the park, Jane saw a big tree with lots of apples on it. She asked Papa if she could have one. "Yes, you can have one," Papa said. Jane smiled and she lifted the apple off the tree. It was heavy, so Papa helped her hold it. Then they went home. Jane wanted to make some juice with the apple. So, Papa helped her cut the apple into small pieces, and they put the pieces in a blender. Jane was excited, and she pushed the button to make the juice. When the juice was ready, Jane lifted her cup, and she was so happy because it was so yummy!

Lily and Tom were at the zoo with their mom. They saw many animals, like lions, monkeys, and giraffes. But their favorite was the panda. The panda was big and furry, with black and white spots. It ate bamboo and rolled on the grass. "Mom, can we go closer to the panda?" Lily asked. "Sure, honey. But be quiet and gentle. Pandas are shy and need their space," mom said. They walked to the fence and watched the panda. It looked at them with its black eyes and made a soft noise. "Hello, panda. What is your name?" Tom said. The panda did not answer. It just blinked and chewed. "I wonder what the panda is thinking," Lily said. "Maybe it is thinking about its home in the forest. Or maybe it is thinking about its friends. Or maybe it is thinking about us," Tom said. "I wish we could talk to the panda and be its friends," Lily said. "Me too. But we can still show it we love it. Let's wave and smile," Tom said. They waved and smiled at the panda. The panda waved back with its paw and smiled too. Lily and Tom felt happy. They loved the panda and the panda loved them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a friend named Tim. They liked to play and laugh together. One day, they found a big box. They did not know what was inside the box. They both wanted to open it. Amy said, "Let's celebrate and open the box!" Tim was scared. He felt ashamed because he was not brave like Amy. But Amy put her hand on Tim's neck and said, "Don't worry, we can open it together!" They opened the box and found many toys inside. They played with the toys all day. Tim was not scared anymore. They were both very happy. They learned that it is good to be brave and help each other.
