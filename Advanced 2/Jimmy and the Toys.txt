Jimmy was feeling bored. He had played all of his toys, and nothing seemed fun to him. He grabbed the newspaper from the kitchen table. It was a dull color and had a lot of words that he didn't understand. Jimmy decided to shake the newspaper and see what happened. Suddenly, all the words jumped off the page and scattered around the kitchen! Jimmy started to laugh. He chased the words around, but it was hard to catch them. Eventually, Jimmy stopped running. He slowly realized that he had found something new to do. He started to fold the paper and play with it instead. He was having such a great time that he didn't want it to end. Jimmy was so proud of himself. He had taken a dull-looking newspaper and found a new way to have fun!

Once upon a time, there was a modest town. In this town, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big problem. She would suffer from sneezes every day. Her mom and dad tried to help her, but they did not know what to do. One day, a wise old lady came to the town. She heard about Amy's sneezes. The old lady had a magic powder that could help. She went to Amy's house and said, "I have something that can help you. This powder will make your sneezes go away." Amy's mom and dad said, "Thank you, wise lady." They gave the powder to Amy. She took the powder and her sneezes went away. Amy and her family were very happy. The town people thanked the wise old lady for helping Amy. From that day on, Amy never suffered from sneezes again.

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there lived a small bird named Bobo. Bobo had many friends. One day, the sun was very hot. Bobo and his friend, Lulu the monkey, were very thirsty. Bobo said, "Lulu, let's find water to drink." Lulu agreed, and they went to look for water in the clear jungle. They saw a tall tree with big leaves. They thought, maybe there is water on the leaves. Lulu climbed the tree and saw water on the leaves. He called Bobo, "Bobo, I found water!" Lulu used his hands to pour the water from the leaves into Bobo's mouth. They drank the water and felt happy. Bobo and Lulu played in the jungle for the rest of the day. They were glad they found water to drink. From that day on, they always knew where to find water in the jungle.

One day, a little bird sat on a hanger. The hanger was in a big tree. The bird was happy and sang a pretty song. The sun was warm and the sky was blue. A rude cat came to the tree. The cat wanted to catch the bird. The bird saw the cat and was scared. The bird tried to warn its friends. "Watch out for the cat!" the bird said. The bird's friends heard the warning. They all flew away from the tree. The rude cat could not catch them. The birds were safe and happy. They thanked the little bird for the warning.

One day, a wide turkey named Tom was walking in the yard. He saw a big tree with many apples. Tom wanted to eat the apples, but he did not know how to get them. He looked up and down, and then he had an idea. Tom decided to jump high to get the apples. He tried and tried, but he could not jump high enough. Then, a little bird saw Tom and wanted to help. The bird flew up and picked an apple for Tom. They both were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The tree started to shake, and more apples fell down. The bird and Tom were surprised. They looked up and saw a big wind. The wind was blowing the apples down. Tom and the bird laughed and ate all the apples together.

One day, Jimmy and his Mommy went to the store. Jimmy saw a new pair of shoes, they were very colourful. Mommy said to Jimmy, "if you are obedient, you can have this pair of shoes". Jimmy was so happy and he smiled. He tried on the shoes and they were perfect. Jimmy said, "Mommy I can win this pair of shoes!" But Mommy just shook her head and said, "No, I'm sorry Jimmy, you can't win the shoes". Jimmy was so sad and he started to cry. But Mommy wouldn't change her mind and they left the store without the shoes.
