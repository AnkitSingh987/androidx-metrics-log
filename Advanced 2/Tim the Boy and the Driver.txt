Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One day, he found a big toy bus with a driver inside. Tim was very happy and wanted to play with the bus and the driver. Tim put the driver in the bus and started to play. But, the driver would not sit still. He kept falling out of the bus. Tim felt sad and said, "Please, driver, stay in the bus." But the driver kept falling out. Tim tried to fix the problem. He put the driver back in the bus and held him in place with a small piece of tape. Now, the driver stayed in the bus just fine. Tim and the driver had lots of fun playing together. They went on many adventures with the bus, driving all around Tim's room. In the end, Tim and the driver became good friends, and they had a great time playing together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick bathtub. It was in a small house. The bathtub was very happy because it was always full of water and bubbles. Every day, a little girl named Lucy would come to play in the bathtub. She loved to splash and make a lot of fun. One day, Lucy brought a new friend to play with her in the bathtub. It was a cute rubber duck. The thick bathtub was excited to accept the new friend. The duck floated on the water, and Lucy and the duck played together. They laughed and splashed in the bubbles. After a while, it was time for Lucy and the rubber duck to get out of the bathtub. They said goodbye to the thick bathtub and promised to come back and play again soon. The bathtub was happy to have new friends and couldn't wait for the next fun bath time.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk with her mom. They wanted to observe the animals in the park. Amy was very excited to see the ducks, squirrels, and birds. As they walked, Amy saw a small cat. The cat was under a big tree. The cat looked nervous because it was hiding from a dog. Amy wanted to help the cat feel safe. Amy's mom told her to be calm and quiet. They walked slowly to the cat. The dog went away, and the cat was not nervous anymore. The cat came out from under the tree and let Amy pet it. Amy was happy that she could help the cat.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite top. This top was very boring. It was all white with no colors. Tim's friends had tops with many colors, but not Tim. One day, Tim saw a box of colors. He had an idea! He wanted to match the colors of his friends' tops. So, he took the colors and made his top very colorful. He was so happy and could not wait to show his friends. When Tim showed his friends, they were all surprised. They thought his top was the most fun and pretty top they had ever seen! They all wanted to make their tops colorful too. The moral of the story is even if something seems boring, with a little work, it can become very special.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. One day, Tim saw an unknown plate on the table. It was new and had a pretty picture on it. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, where did we get this plate?" His mom said, "We got it from the store. It is for your lunch today." Tim was very happy because he liked the new plate. He wanted to use it every day. At lunch, Tim showed the plate to his dad. "Dad, look at my new plate!" Tim said. His dad smiled and said, "It's a nice plate, Tim. I hope you enjoy your lunch on it." Tim ate all his food and felt happy with his new plate.
