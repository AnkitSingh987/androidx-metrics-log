Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a kitchen. In the kitchen, Mom made a big cake. It was a yummy cake. She put the cake on the table to cool down. A little cat lived in the house too. The cat saw the yummy cake. The cat wanted to taste the cake. So, the cat jumped on the table to have a bite. But, surprise! The cake was not a cake! It was a big hat that looked like a cake. The cat did not taste the yummy cake. The cat tasted the hat. The cat was sad. Mom laughed and said, "Don't worry, little cat. I will make a real cake for you to taste." And they all lived happily ever after.

Heidi was a three year old girl with bright eyes. She had a lot of toys to play with that day. She was playing in her room with a toy farm and pretending that the toy pigs were real. Suddenly, she heard a noise outside her window. Heidi stopped playing and walked over to the window to see what was happening. Outside, she saw a long vine dripping with water. It was so green and wet! Heidi had an idea. She opened the window, put her arm out, and started to wrap the vine around her arm. She pretended it was a snake. It was slimy and wiggled in her hand. She smiled and laughed with delight. Suddenly, Heidi heard her mom calling her name. She quickly backed away from the window and said, "Oops, I better go." She put the vine back outside, ran back to her toys, and started to play again.

Once upon a time, a panda named Boo lived in a big forest. Boo loved to chew on green leaves all day long. He had many friends who played with him, but one day, a new panda named Mimi came to the forest. Mimi was very good at climbing trees and finding the best leaves. All the other friends looked at Mimi and felt envious. They wanted to climb trees and find the best leaves too. Poor Boo felt sad because he could not climb trees like Mimi. One day, while Mimi was up in a tree, something unexpected happened. Mimi got stuck and could not get down. Boo saw Mimi and wanted to help. He had an idea! Boo started to chew on the tree. He chewed and chewed until the tree fell down gently. Mimi was safe and not stuck anymore! All the friends cheered for Boo. They were not envious anymore. They knew that everyone was good at something. From that day on, Boo and Mimi became best friends, and they all lived happily in the big forest.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big hoop. She wanted to play with it. But her friend Tom was not available to play. Sue felt sad. She wanted to play with her hoop and her friend. Sue saw a boy named Jim. She asked, "Do you want to play with my hoop?" Jim said, "No, I want to shut my eyes and take a nap." Sue felt sad again. She really wanted to play with someone. Then, Sue saw her friend Tom. He was done with his work and could play now. Sue was happy. They both played with the hoop together. They laughed and had fun. The conflict was over, and Sue was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a soft bunny named Bob. Bob loved to draw in his notebook. One day, he met a big bear named Ben. Ben saw the notebook and wanted to draw too. "Can I draw in your notebook?" asked Ben. Bob said, "Yes, but please be careful." Ben tried to draw, but his big paws made it hard. He got angry and accidentally destroyed the notebook. Bob was sad. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird named Bella flew down and saw the destroyed notebook. "Don't worry," she said. "I can fix it!" Bella used her tiny beak to put the notebook back together. Bob and Ben were so happy, they all drew together and became best friends.
