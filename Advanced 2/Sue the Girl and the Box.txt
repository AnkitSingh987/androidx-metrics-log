One day, a little girl named Sue was playing with her toy cars. She loved to make them go fast and make fun motions. But today, she was feeling a bit bored. She wanted to add something new to her playtime. Sue's mom saw her looking sad and asked, "Sue, why are you so bored?" Sue said, "I want to add something to my playtime, but I don't know what." Her mom thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea! I'll be right back." Sue's mom left the room and returned with a big box. Sue's eyes lit up with excitement. Her mom opened the box, and inside were colorful tracks for her toy cars. "Now you can add these tracks to make your playtime more fun," her mom said with a smile. Sue quickly started to build the tracks, making twists and turns for her cars to drive on. As she played, she found that her playtime was no longer boring. The tracks added new motion and fun to her toy cars. From that day on, Sue loved to play with her toy cars and the colorful tracks. She learned that adding something new can make playtime even more exciting. And her mom was always there to help her find new ways to have fun.

Once there was a little girl called Emma. She was three years old. Everyday, she would struggle to reach the top of the slide in the park by herself. One day, Emma was in the park with her mum. She tried to climb the slide again. But this time she struggled more than before. Mum said, "It looks too hard. Here, let me help you." Emma stopped struggling and mum took her hand. Together, they climbed up the slide. Emma smiled and said, "Thank you, Mum!" At the top of the slide, Emma found a big pot of creamy ice-cream. "Yummy," she said, "It looks so good!" Mum asked, "Do you want to try it?" Emma smiled and replied, "Yes please!" She opened the pot and carefully scooped out one big scoop of ice cream. Emma tasted it and said, "Mmmm, that's so good!" Mum laughed and they enjoyed their creamy treat together.

One day, a boy named Tom found a baseball. It was light and easy to hold. He wanted to play with it. Tom called his friend, Sue. "Sue, do you want to play with my new baseball?" he asked. Sue said, "Yes! Let's play!" They went to the park to play catch. Tom threw the baseball and Sue tried to catch it. But she missed. The baseball went into a big puddle. It got all wet and started to soak. Tom and Sue were sad. They thought the baseball was ruined. Just then, a frog jumped out of the puddle. The frog had the baseball in its mouth. It gave the ball back to Tom and Sue. They were so happy! The baseball was not ruined. It was just wet. They thanked the frog and played with the baseball again.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little flower. The flower was worried. It wanted to bloom and be pretty like the other flowers. One day, the flower saw a brick. The brick was strong and tough. The flower asked the brick, "Can you help me? I want to bloom like the other flowers." The brick said, "Yes, I will help you." The brick stayed with the flower. They became good friends. The flower felt happy and not worried anymore. Soon, the flower started to bloom. It was the prettiest flower in the garden. The flower and the brick were very happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle kangaroo named Kiki. Kiki loved to play with her friends in the big park. One day, Kiki wanted to have a party. So, she decided to invite all her friends to play and have fun together. When Kiki gave the invites to her friends, a big, mean bird named Benny saw them. Benny felt left out because he didn't get an invite. He was very sad and angry. Benny thought, "Why didn't Kiki invite me to her party?" So, he decided to take all the invites away from Kiki's friends. Kiki saw what Benny did and felt bad. She didn't want anyone to feel left out. Kiki hopped over to Benny and said, "I'm sorry, Benny. I didn't know you wanted to come to my party. You can come too!" Benny felt happy and gave the invites back to Kiki's friends. On the day of the party, all the animals were there, including Benny. They all played games, ate yummy treats, and had lots of fun. Kiki was happy that she invited Benny, and they all became good friends. From that day on, Kiki always made sure to invite everyone to her parties.
