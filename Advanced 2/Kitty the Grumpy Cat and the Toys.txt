Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Kitty. She did not like to share her toys with others. One day, she found a box with many colors of paint. Kitty wanted to paint her house with these colors, but she did not want to share the paint with her friends. Kitty started to paint her house with the colors. She was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the paint all over her house. The colors mixed together and made a big mess. Kitty was sad and grumpy. Then, her friends came to help her. They showed Kitty how to share the colors and paint together. They painted the house with beautiful colors. Kitty learned that sharing can be fun and make things better. She was not grumpy anymore and became friends with everyone.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing in her room. She saw her mom wash a big sheet. The sheet was dirty and needed to be clean. Amy wanted to help her mom, so she asked, "Can I help you wash the sheet, Mom?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Amy. You can help me. Let's go to the empty tub and wash the sheet together." They went to the tub and filled it with water and soap. Amy and her mom put the sheet in the tub and started to wash it. They washed and washed until the sheet was clean. Amy felt happy that she could help her mom. They took the sheet out of the tub and hung it up to dry. Amy's mom gave her a big hug and said, "Thank you for helping me, Amy. You did a great job washing the sheet."

Once upon a time, there was a little train. The train loved to go fast. It would go up and down the tracks all day. One day, the train saw some bitter fruit on the tracks. The train did not like bitter fruit, so it went around them. As the train went around the bitter fruit, it saw a big hill. The train was tired and needed to rest. But the train wanted to see what was on the other side of the hill. So, the train went up the hill slowly, and then it stopped to rest at the top. While the train was resting, it saw some steam coming from the other side of the hill. The train was curious, so it went down the hill to see what the steam was. When the train got to the steam, it saw a big party with all its train friends! They had planned a surprise party for the little train. The train was so happy and had lots of fun with its friends.

Once upon a time, there was a duck who wanted balance. Every week, he tried to practice balancing. He started with balancing on one foot, then he tried crossing his feet. But he couldn't do it, so he was very sad. One day, as he was walking along, he saw a bright yellow flower. He smiled and he decided to practice his balancing on the yellow petals. He spread his wings and balanced for a few seconds before he wobbled and fell over. But it didn't stop the duck, he kept trying every week. Finally, one morning, he tried and he stayed up! He outstretched his wings, held his head high, and balanced. He was so happy he did a little dance before hopping away. The little duck practices balancing every week, but he never forgets to stop and appreciate the bright yellow flowers.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful park, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play on the swing and sit on the bench. Every day, Lily would run to the park with her mom and have lots of fun. One sunny day, Lily went to the park, and the swing was empty. She was very happy and started to swing high in the sky. Her mom sat on the bench, watching Lily and smiling. The birds sang, and the sun shone brightly. But then, a big wind came and pushed Lily too high on the swing. She got scared and fell off. Lily cried, and her mom ran to help her. They went home sad, and Lily had a big boo-boo on her knee. From that day on, Lily was scared to play on the swing, and the park was not as much fun as before.
