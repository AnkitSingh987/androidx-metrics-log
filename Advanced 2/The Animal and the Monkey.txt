Once upon a time, in a big, green forest, there was a small animal. This animal was a little monkey. The monkey liked to hang from the trees and play all day. One day, the monkey saw a big, tight rope high up in the trees. The monkey thought, "Wow! I want to play on that rope!" So, the monkey climbed up the tree and started to hang on the tight rope. As the monkey played on the tight rope, many other animals came to watch. They all clapped and cheered for the monkey. The monkey was very happy and proud. From that day on, all the animals in the forest became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a daughter who loved to play outside. One day, she felt very hungry, so she went to her mom and said, "Mom, I am hungry. Can I have some food, please?" Her mom said, "Of course, my dear daughter! Let's settle down and eat some yummy food together." So, they went to the kitchen and her mom made a big sandwich for Lily. Lily was very happy and ate the big sandwich. After eating, Lily was not hungry anymore. She said, "Thank you, Mom! That was very yummy!" Her mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, my dear daughter. Now, go and play outside with your friends." And Lily went outside to play, feeling happy and full.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big board. She liked to draw on it. One day, she felt anxious. She did not know what to draw. Lily asked her friend, Tom. He said, "I recommend you draw a big sun! It will make you happy!" So, Lily drew a big sun on the board. It was bright and yellow. Lily felt better. She was not anxious anymore. She and Tom played with the board all day. They drew many things and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy rabbit. He hopped around the meadow searching for something delicious. Suddenly, he spotted a big, juicy carrot! The rabbit hopped closer to the carrot and said: "Yum! I'm going to eat this!" But then a bee buzzed by and said, "Stop! That's my carrot!" The rabbit said, "I'm sorry, but it looks so delicious. I'm sure you can find something else to eat on the other side." So the bee flew off to the other side of the meadow. The rabbit gave a smile, and then happily nibbled away at the carrot. The end.

One day, a girl named Amy went for a walk. She saw a big cone on the ground. Amy felt glad and picked up the cone. She wanted to show her friend, Tim. Amy walked to Tim's house and knocked on the door. Tim came out and said, "Hi, Amy! What do you have?" Amy showed Tim the cone and said, "Look, I found a big cone!" Tim was glad too, and they both decided to play with the cone. They played with the cone all day, but then it started to rain. The rain made the cone wet and it broke into little pieces. Amy and Tim were sad. They could not play with the cone anymore. They both went home and the day ended with a bad ending.

One day, a girl named Lily went on a trip with her mom. They wanted to collect white rocks. Lily loved white rocks because they looked like snow. She put on her hat and shoes and went outside with her mom. On their trip, they saw a big white rock. Lily said, "Look, Mom! A big white rock!" Her mom said, "Yes, it is very big and white. Let's collect it!" But when they tried to pick it up, it was very heavy. They could not take it home. Then, something unexpected happened. The big white rock started to move! It was not a rock, it was a big white turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, I am not a rock. I am a turtle." Lily and her mom were surprised. They did not collect the big white turtle, but they made a new friend.
