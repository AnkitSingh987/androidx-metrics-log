Once upon a time, in a faraway place, a little boy named Tim lived with his mom and dad. In the winter, the place was very icy and cold. Tim loved to play outside in the snow. One day, while Tim was making a snowman, he met a new friend named Sue. Sue was also playing in the snow. They both said "hi" to each other and started to play together. They had so much fun that they didn't feel the icy cold. As they played, Tim's hands started to feel very cold. He told Sue, "My hands feel icy." Sue said, "Let's go inside and get warm." So, they went to Tim's house and his mom gave them hot cocoa. They felt warm and cozy. Tim and Sue became best friends and played together every day. They loved their icy place and had many fun adventures together. And they always remembered to go inside and get warm when their hands felt icy.

One day, a fish named Bob wanted to swim to the office. He felt guilty because he was late. He told his friend Sam, "I am late for office. I must swim fast." On the way, Bob saw a big shark. He was scared. The shark said, "You can't pass. I am hungry." Bob thought fast. He saw a big pile of food near the shark. Bob said, "Look! There is food. You can eat it." The shark looked at the food and smiled. He let Bob pass. This was the twist. The shark was not bad, he was just hungry. Bob swam fast to the office. He was not late after all. His boss said, "Good job, Bob!" Bob was happy. He learned that helping others can make things better.

One day, a messy little girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. Lucy saw a big oyster in a tank. The oyster had a shiny shell. Lucy wanted to take the oyster home, but her mom said, "No, we can't take the oyster home. It needs to stay in the water." Lucy was sad, but she listened to her mom. They went home, and Lucy cleaned her room. When her room was no longer messy, Lucy found a pretty stone under her bed. She showed her mom the stone and said, "Look, Mom! I found a shiny stone like the oyster had!" Her mom looked at the stone and said, "Lucy, this is not just a stone. This is a pearl! It must have come from an oyster like the one at the store!" They both were very surprised and happy. Lucy learned that if she keeps her room clean, she can find special things. The moral of the story is that good things can happen when you keep your space clean and listen to your mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to study the big tree in his yard. Every day, he would look at the tree and feel happy. One sunny day, Tim was feeling nervous. He saw something in the tree that he had never seen before. It was a big, round thing. Tim was scared, but he wanted to know what it was. He walked up to the tree and looked closer. Suddenly, the big, round thing moved! Tim jumped back. But then, he saw that it was just a friendly squirrel. The squirrel had been hiding behind a big nut. Tim laughed and was not nervous anymore. He and the squirrel became good friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a small garden full of delicate plants. There was a bird, and a bee, and a little rabbit. All the different creatures lived in peace, sharing the garden's soil. One day, the rabbit was hopping around and looking for something to eat. He saw something bright and shiny in the soil, and he decided to go have a closer look. The rabbit said, "What is this? What is it doing here?" The bee flew up and said, "It is a delicate flower, my friend. I think it needs our help to survive!" The rabbit and the bee decided to share their soil, and soon the flower was happy and healthy. The bunny and the bee smiled and danced around the garden in joy. They were happy to share and to make the garden beautiful.
