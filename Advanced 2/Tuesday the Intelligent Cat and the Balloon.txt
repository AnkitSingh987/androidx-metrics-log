Once upon a time, there was an intelligent cat named Tuesday. Tuesday loved to play with his ball and jump around in the sun. Every day, he would play with his friends, the birds and the bugs. One day, Tuesday saw something shiny in the sky. He stared at it for a long time. He didn't know what it was. He called his friends, the birds and the bugs, to look at the shiny thing with him. They all stared at it, too. Then, the shiny thing started to move! It came down from the sky and landed in front of them. It was a big, shiny balloon! Tuesday and his friends were so happy. They played with the balloon all day long, jumping and flying around with it. And that is how Tuesday and his friends found a new toy to play with.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to her old grandpa's farm. She saw many animals and plants. She liked the farm a lot. Grandpa loved to grow oats in his big field. Lucy wanted to feed the animals. She asked her grandpa, "What should I give them?" Grandpa said, "I suggest you give them some oats. They love oats very much." So, Lucy took some oats from the big field. She fed the animals and they were very happy. Lucy and her grandpa spent the whole day playing and laughing on the farm.

Once upon a time, there was a big house. It was very old. The house had a lot of history. A nice family lived in the house. The family wanted to make the house look new again. They wanted to restore the house. The family worked together to fix the house. The mom, dad, and kids all helped. They were independent. They did not need help from others. They painted the walls, fixed the roof, and cleaned the yard. When they finished, the house looked new and pretty. The family was happy. They were proud of their work. They had restored the house's history. The family lived in the house for a long time. They loved their independent life.

One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his grandma. She lived in a small, warm house with lots of flowers outside. Tim liked to visit grandma because she always had fun things to do and yummy food to eat. When Tim got to grandma's house, she said, "Hello, Tim! Are you hungry? I made spaghetti for lunch!" Tim's eyes grew big with excitement. He loved spaghetti, especially grandma's spaghetti. It was the best. Tim and grandma sat down to eat the warm spaghetti. They talked and laughed as they ate. Tim told grandma about his day at school, and grandma told Tim a funny story about her cat. They were very happy together.

Sam and Mia were playing in the park. They liked to ride their bikes on the paths and go down the slides. They wore helmets to keep their heads safe. They were lively and happy. "Look, Mia, a big hill!" Sam said. "Let's go up and see what's on the other side." "OK, Sam, but be careful!" Mia said. "The hill is steep and bumpy." They pedaled hard and reached the top of the hill. They saw a pond with ducks and a bridge. They also saw a sign that said "No bikes allowed". "Wow, this is pretty!" Mia said. "But we can't go on the bridge with our bikes. What should we do?" "Let's settle our bikes here and walk across the bridge," Sam said. "We can come back for them later." They got off their bikes and left them by the sign. They took off their helmets and held them in their hands. They walked across the bridge and fed the ducks some bread. They had fun watching the ducks swim and quack. After a while, they decided to go back to their bikes. They put on their helmets and got on their bikes. They rode down the hill and back to the park. They were lively and happy.

One day, a little girl named Amy got a new toy. It was a funny looking toy that could poke things. Amy was very happy with her new toy. She went to the kitchen to find something to poke. She saw the big fridge and thought it would be fun to poke it. Amy said to her toy, "Let's poke the fridge!" She poked the fridge with her toy, and something unexpected happened. The fridge door opened, and out came a talking cat! The cat said, "Hi Amy, I am a magic cat. Thank you for poking the fridge and letting me out." Amy was very surprised to see a talking cat. The cat said, "I can do magic for you, Amy. What do you want?" Amy thought for a moment and said, "I want a big ice cream!" The magic cat waved its tail, and a big ice cream appeared. Amy and the cat enjoyed the ice cream together, and they became best friends.
