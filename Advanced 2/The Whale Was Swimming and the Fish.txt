One day, a big whale was swimming in the sea. He saw a tasty fish and wanted to eat it. The fish saw the whale and was scared. The fish said, "Please, do not eat me. I can read a nice story to you." The whale thought about it and said, "Okay, read me a story." The fish read a story about a little fish and a big shark. The little fish was very smart and got away from the shark. The whale liked the story. But the whale was still very hungry. He said, "Thank you for the story, but I am still hungry." Then, the whale ate the tasty fish. The fish was sad, and the whale swam away.

Once upon a time, there was a boat that liked to sail. She was a big boat and she liked to glide through the water. The boat sailed all the time, day and night, in rain and in sunshine. One day, there was a big storm at sea. The boat was tossed around, tossed and turned in the big waves and it was a lot of fun. But after a while, it got tired so the boat pulled into a harbour to rest. When the boat stopped, it noticed the water was sour. It made a funny face, like it tasted something yukky. The boat decided to go somewhere else to sail. It glided away, out of the harbour and into the sunshine. There was no sour taste in the water and the boat was happy. It sailed around, in and out of the waves, for a long time. The boat sailed until it reached a beautiful bay, with lots of seagulls and a tiny beach. Finally, it had found the perfect place to take a break from sailing. The boat rested and sailed, sailed and rested, until it was time to sail away again.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to write a letter to her friend Tom. She was glad because she had not talked to Tom in a long time. Lily took a pen and a piece of paper and started to write. As she was writing, she thought of a question to ask Tom. She asked, "What is your favorite toy?" Lily was excited to hear what Tom would say. She finished writing her letter and put it in an envelope. Lily went to her mom and said, "Mom, can you help me send this letter to Tom?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Lily. I am glad you wrote to Tom. Let's go to the mailbox together." They walked to the mailbox and sent the letter. Lily was happy and could not wait for Tom's answer.

Lila and Ben are at the toy store with their mom. They see a big doll house that they like. It has many rooms and furniture and dolls. They want to play with it. "Mom, can we have the doll house?" Lila asks. Mom looks at the price tag. It is very high. She shakes her head. "No, sweeties, the doll house is too expensive. We cannot buy it today." Lila and Ben feel sad. They really want the doll house. "Please, mom, please!" Ben begs. Mom sighs. She loves her children, but she cannot afford the doll house. "Listen, Lila and Ben. I know you like the doll house, but we have to be patient. Maybe we can save some money and buy it later. Or maybe you can ask Santa for it for Christmas. But we cannot get it now. Do you understand?" Lila and Ben nod. They understand, but they are still disappointed. "Okay, mom. We will be patient. But can we at least look at it for a little while?" Lila asks. Mom smiles. She knows they are good children. "Sure, you can look at it for a little while. But then we have to go home, okay?" "Okay, mom. Thank you!" Lila and Ben say. They run to the doll house and tell each other stories about the dolls and their lives. They pretend they are the dolls and have fun. They forget about the price for a moment and enjoy the toy. Mom watches them and feels happy. She hopes they will get the doll house someday.
