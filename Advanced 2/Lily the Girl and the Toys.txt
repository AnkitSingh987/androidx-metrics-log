Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a favorite game she liked to play with her toys. She would match the toys that looked the same. Lily was very good at this game. One day, Lily found a fork in the kitchen. She thought it was a funny toy. She wanted to find another fork to match it. Lily looked all around the house, but she could not find another fork. Lily's big brother, Tom, saw her looking sad. He asked her what was wrong. Lily told him she wanted to match the fork. Tom was a competitive boy, so he made a game. He hid forks all around the house for Lily to find and match. Lily had so much fun finding and matching the forks. She was happy, and Tom was happy to help his little sister.

Grandpa and Grandma wanted to send an intelligent march. So they asked the little girl to join them. She was so excited! They all drove to the park, put on their hats and lined up. Grandpa said it was time to send the march. The little girl knew it was important to make it special. Grandma said to be smart, and the little girl knew exactly what to do. Everyone stepped forward in rhythm, arms swinging side to side. Grandpa clapped and cheered. Grandma smiled and waved her arms at the end. The little girl was so proud. She ran back and forth, happy to have helped send an intelligent march.

Tim and Lily love to play with their toy cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They like to zoom them on the floor, on the table, and on the sofa. Sometimes they make noises like vroom, beep, and honk. One day, they decided to have a race with their cars. They chose a long hallway as their track. Tim picked a red car and Lily picked a blue car. They lined up their cars at one end of the hall and counted to three. "Ready, set, go!" they shouted and pushed their cars as hard as they could. The cars zoomed along the hall, faster and faster. Tim and Lily ran after them, cheering and laughing. "Go, red car, go!" Tim said. "Go, blue car, go!" Lily said. They reached the other end of the hall and looked at their cars. The blue car was slightly ahead of the red car. Lily was very happy and jumped up and down. "I won, I won!" she said. Tim was a little sad, but he smiled and said, "Good job, Lily. You are very fast." They hugged each other and decided to have another race. They were very enthusiastic and had a lot of fun. After playing for a while, they felt hungry and thirsty. They went to the kitchen and asked their mom for a snack. Their mom gave them some yogurt and water. They thanked their mom and sat down to eat. They liked yogurt very much. It was creamy and sweet. They ate it with spoons and made funny faces. They also drank some water to quench their thirst. They felt full and happy. They decided to play with their cars some more. They zoomed them on the floor, on the table, and on the sofa. They had a wonderful day.

Once upon a time there were two brothers, Sam and John. Sam had a pet rabbit that he loved very much. Each day he took care of the bunny and made sure it was very healthy and happy. One day, Sam wanted to make a special dinner for his rabbit and he decided to make a stew. He put some meat into the pot and filled it with water and let it soak for two hours. When it was done cooking, he stirred the stew and found it was perfect. He called his brother, John, to come into the kitchen and see what he had made. John asked if he could try the stew. Sam said yes, but reminded John to always remember to be kind to animals, even if they can't talk. John agreed and thanked his brother for the important lesson. They both enjoyed the stew and knew they had made a perfect dinner, thanks to Sam's hard work. And they all lived happily ever after!
