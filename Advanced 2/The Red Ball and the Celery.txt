Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. The ball could talk and loved to play. One day, the ball saw a tall celery. The celery was very green and tall. The red ball said, "Hi, celery! Can I climb you?" The celery said, "Yes, red ball, you can climb me. But be careful, I am very tall." The red ball started to climb the tall celery. It climbed and climbed until it was very high. The red ball looked down and saw all its friends. They were playing and having fun. The red ball said to the celery, "Thank you for letting me climb you, tall celery. I can see all my friends from up here." The celery said, "You're welcome, red ball. Have fun with your friends." The red ball climbed down the tall celery and went to play with its friends. They all had a great time, and the red ball was happy it had climbed the tall celery.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to bake with her mom. They would make yummy cakes and cookies. Sue was very happy when she baked. One sunny day, Sue and her mom went to the park. Sue brought her net to catch butterflies. She was very anxious because she had never caught a butterfly before. She ran around the park with her net, trying to catch one. After a while, Sue caught a beautiful butterfly in her net. She showed it to her mom, who was very proud of her. Sue let the butterfly go, and it flew away into the sky. They went home and baked a cake to celebrate Sue's big catch.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. They had a big jar of jelly with them. Lucy loved to eat jelly because it was sweet and wiggly. Her mom was always reliable and made sure to bring her favorite snack. At the park, Lucy saw her friend Tom. They wanted to play catch, but they did not have a ball. Lucy had an idea! She asked her mom if they could use the jelly to play. Her mom said yes and gave them a spoon each. Lucy and Tom took turns to throw the jelly to each other. They laughed as the jelly wiggled in the air. Sometimes, the jelly fell on the ground, but they did not mind. They just picked it up and threw it again. They had so much fun playing with the jelly. At the end of the day, they went home with happy smiles and sticky hands.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug who loved to climb. Every day, the bug would try to climb high up a big tree. The sun was always shining, making the bug happy. But, the bug would always struggle to reach the top. One day, the bug saw a tall flower. The bug thought it would be easier to climb the flower than the tree. The sun was shining high in the sky. The bug started to climb the flower, but it was still a struggle. The bug tried and tried, but it could not reach the top of the flower. It started to get very tired. The sun was going down and the bug was still struggling. At last, the bug fell down and could not climb anymore. The bug was sad and did not try to climb again.

Once upon a time there was an onion who wanted to grow. He looked around to find a spot in the meadow where he could put down roots. As he looked, he noticed something sad. He saw a little girl alone in a corner, with her face in her hands. The onion felt guilty. He wanted to help the girl, but he wasn't sure how. Suddenly, he had an idea! He dug deep into the ground and took a deep breath. The onion began to grow bigger and bigger, until his head reached the girl's toes. The onion smiled and said, "Hello! Would you like to play?" The girl looked up and laughed. She had forgotten that she wasn't alone. The two of them played together in the meadow for the rest of the day, until the sun went down. The onion was so happy that he had grown that day - not just because he had gotten larger, but because he had helped the little girl smile.
