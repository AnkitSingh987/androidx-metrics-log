Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Sam. They liked to play together every day. One day, Tim saw a pretty flame on a candle. He wanted to show it to Sam. Tim went to his mom and asked if he could borrow the flame. His mom said, "No, it is not safe." But Tim did not listen. He took the flame and went to find Sam. When he found Sam, he showed him the flame. But Tim was not careful, and the flame made a tree angry. The angry tree started to burn, and the fire grew big. Tim and Sam were scared. They called for help, and the village people came to put out the fire. The fire was gone, and the tree was safe. Tim learned that he should always listen to his mom. The moral of the story is to listen to the people who care about you because they know what is best for you.

Once upon a time, in a big farm, there was a large bull named Ben. Ben was very big and strong. He lived with his friends, the cows, and the farmer who took care of them all. One day, a little bird named Billy came to the farm. Billy was small and fast, but he liked to tease the animals. He saw Ben and said, "You are so big, but I bet you can't catch me!" Ben was not happy, and he wanted to show Billy that he could catch him. The chase began. Ben tried to catch Billy, but Billy was too fast. Ben was getting tired, and the cows were watching. They said, "Ben, you don't need to catch Billy. We know you are strong and fast." Ben thought about it and decided that they were right. In the end, Ben and Billy became friends. They learned that it was better to be kind to each other and play together. And they all lived happily on the big farm.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to get a lemon from the store. Mia felt very important because she was going to do a big job. Mia walked to the store and saw a man selling lemons. She said, "Please, can I have one lemon?" The man gave her a lemon and Mia went back home. On the way, she saw a boy who was sad. Mia asked, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I am thirsty and I have no drink." Mia thought for a moment and then she had an idea. She gave the boy her lemon and said, "Here, you can have my lemon. Squeeze it into water and you will have a nice drink." The boy smiled and thanked her. Mia felt happy that she could help someone. When she got home, she told her mom about the boy and the lemon. Her mom was proud of her for being kind and sharing. The moral of the story is that it is important to help others when you can, even if it means giving up something you have.

Once upon a time, there was a big ranch. On the ranch, there were many animals. The animals were happy because they had a dependable farmer. The farmer took care of them and gave them food every day. One day, the farmer had to change something on the ranch. He built a new fence for the animals. The animals were excited because they thought it was a new place to play. But, they did not know that the new fence was for a new animal. The next day, the farmer brought a new animal to the ranch. It was a big, strong horse. The other animals were scared at first because they did not know if the horse would be nice. But the horse was very friendly and wanted to play with them. The animals learned that new things can be fun, and they all became good friends with the horse.
