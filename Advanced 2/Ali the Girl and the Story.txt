Title: Ali the Girl and the Story

Once upon a time there was a girl named Ali. She was 4 years old and loved to play. Every night when Ali was getting ready for bed, her mom would tell her a silly story. The stories were always so fun and made Ali laugh. One day, she had a nightmare. It made her so scared that she started to cry. Her mom came running into the room to comfort her. She gave her a hug and told her that sometimes bad dreams happen, but they don't need to be so scary. To help Ali, her mom allowed her to have a nightlight in her room. Ali felt a bit better after that. She was so happy and thanked her mom for allowing her to have a nightlight. Together they looked for the silliest nightlight Ali could find. When they found the perfect one, she couldn't wait to turn it on! From that night on, Ali was no longer scared of nightmares.

Max and his mom went to the airport. Max was excited to go on a trip. He saw planes taking off and landing. Max wanted his own plane! Max's mom took him to the toy store. She said he could pick out something special. Max found a toy plane. When he got it home, he pretended to fly it like a real plane. Max became a successful pilot. Max kept flying his plane all around the house. Everywhere he went, he pretended to own the airport. He liked being in charge! He beamed with pride when he saw his plane in the sky. When Max's mom asked him if he enjoyed being a pilot, he smiled and said yes. Max was a successful young pilot with his own airport.

Sara and Ben like to play with puzzles. They have many puzzles with different pictures. Sara likes puzzles with animals. Ben likes puzzles with cars. They share their puzzles and help each other. One day, Sara and Ben find a new puzzle in the toy box. It has a picture of a big red drink. Sara and Ben think it looks yummy. They want to drink it. They take the puzzle to the kitchen and put it on the table. Sara and Ben start to take the puzzle apart. They want to find the pieces that make the drink. They see a piece with a straw. They see a piece with a lid. They see a piece with a label. They do not read the label. They do not know what it says. Sara and Ben put the pieces together. They make the drink. They are happy. They take the lid off. They see the red liquid. They think it is juice. They take the straw and put it in their mouths. They drink. But the drink is not juice. It is something different. It is something bad. It is something that makes Sara and Ben sick. It is something that grown-ups use to clean things. It is something that has a poison sign on the label. Sara and Ben do not see the sign. They do not know they are drinking poison. Sara and Ben start to feel bad. They start to cough. They start to cry. They drop the puzzle. They call for mom and dad. But mom and dad are not home. They are at work. Sara and Ben are alone. They are scared. They are hurt. They need help. But no one hears them. No one comes. Sara and Ben drink the poison. Sara and Ben die. The end.

Once upon a time, there were two animals, a dog and a rabbit. Both animals were very gifted and could do wonderful things. The dog was crafty and could run very fast. It had strong legs and paws. The rabbit was also quick and gifted. It had long ears that helped it listen and sharp claws that gave it speed. One day, the dog and the rabbit decided to race each other to find out which one was the fastest. They lined up at the starting line and the dog barked, "Ready, steady, go!" The rabbit hopped off at tremendous speed while the dog galloped after it with its powerful paws. The rabbit hopped faster and hopped further, but the dog was still quickly catching up. Finally, the dog and the rabbit reached the finish line at the same time. They both looked at each other in amazement. They had both run fast and shared a wonderful gift. They smiled and panted, their tongues lolling in exhaustion.
