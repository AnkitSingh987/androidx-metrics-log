Once upon a time, there was a clumsy little girl. She loved to play games on her shiny computer screen. One day, she thought it would be fun to try and jump on top of it. So, she took a deep breath and hopped up, but her jump was much too high. She crashed straight into the screen with a loud bang and everyone heard it. The girl felt very scared and unsure of what to do. She just shrugged and looked around. The screen was broken and the girl was sad. Her parents did not have enough money to get a new screen, so the girl was without her favorite computer game. The end.

One day, a polite boy named Tim went to the park. He saw many kids playing. Tim wanted to play too, but he was a little shy. He sat on a bench and observed the kids having fun. Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was eating ice-cream. Tim loved ice-cream too. He walked to Sue and said, "Hi, I like your ice-cream. Can we be friends?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, we can be friends. Would you like some ice-cream too?" Tim was happy and they both ate ice-cream together. After that, they played with the other kids in the park. Tim was not shy anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tom. He loved to draw and design things. One day, he made a plan to design a big moon. He wanted to show it to all his friends. Tom worked very hard on his moon. He used crayons and paper to make it. But when he finished, it looked terrible. Tom was sad. He did not want to show his friends the terrible moon. He wanted to make it better. So, Tom tried again. He worked harder and harder. This time, the moon looked great. Tom was happy. He showed his friends the new moon he designed. They all loved it, and they played together with the moon. Tom learned that if he keeps trying, he can make things better.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a very old tunnel. The ancient tunnel was big and dark. All the kids in the town knew about it, but they were scared to go inside. One sunny day, a brave little boy named Tim decided to explore the tunnel. As he walked through the dark tunnel, he saw a little girl crying. He asked her, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I am lost and I don't recognize this place." Tim held her hand and said, "Don't worry, we will find our way out together." They walked and walked until they finally saw the light at the end of the tunnel. They were so happy to be outside again. The little girl hugged Tim and said, "Thank you for helping me." Tim smiled and said, "We should always help each other when we are in trouble." From that day on, Tim and the little girl became best friends, and they always remembered the moral value of helping others.

One day, a group of children gathered in the schoolroom to learn. They sat on the wooden benches in front of the old blackboard. There were two children, Fred and Sue. Fred was always very selfish. He always wanted to be the first one to answer the teacher's questions. But today, Sue was determined to be the first one. She quickly raised her hand and the teacher called on her. She proudly gave the right answer to the tricky question. The other children applauded, but Fred was angry. He felt jealous of Sue's success and vowed to get his revenge. The teacher continued with the lessons, but Fred did not pay attention. He had made a plan. He asked to go to the bathroom, but as soon as he left the classroom, he opened the window and snuck outside to the playground. When Fred came back to the classroom, he had a surprise. He was carrying a huge bucket of sand. He dumped it on the floor in front of the blackboard. The teacher was angry at Fred's selfish act. But before he could scold Fred, Sue had already leapt into action. She grabbed a rag and wiped away all the sand. The teacher was impressed by Sue's kindness. Fred just sat in the corner, embarrassed. Then he realised that he had been too selfish. From then on, Fred and Sue became close friends.
