Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very sad and miserable. He had no friends to play with. One day, he saw a group of kids playing together in the park. They were having a race to win a big, shiny trophy. Tim wanted to join them, so he went up to the group and asked, "Can I join your race, please?" The kids looked at him and smiled. "Of course, you can join us!" they said. Tim was very happy and excited. The race started and all the kids ran as fast as they could. Tim ran his fastest, too. At the end of the race, Tim came in second place. Even though he didn't win the trophy, he was not miserable anymore. He had new friends to play with, and they all had fun together.

One day, a little pink bunny went to the forest. The bunny wanted to find berries to eat. The sun was hot, and the bunny felt sleepy. The bunny let out a big yawn. The bunny saw a big berry bush. It had red berries and blue berries. The bunny was happy and started to eat the berries. The bunny ate and ate until its belly was full. Then, something unexpected happened. The bunny turned into a big pink bird! The bird was surprised but happy. Now, the bird could fly high in the sky and find even more berries to eat. And that's what the bird did, every day, flying and eating berries.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Kitty. Kitty was very good at jumping and playing. One day, Kitty found a ball with a label on it. The label said, "If you lose this ball, you will lose a friend." Kitty played with the ball every day, being very careful not to lose it. She would always put it in a safe place when she was done playing. Her friends would come and play with her, and they all loved the ball too. One day, Kitty was playing with the ball and it rolled into a bush. She looked and looked, but she could not find it. She was very sad because she did not want to lose a friend. Her friends saw her looking and they all came to help her find the ball. They looked and looked, and finally, they found the ball! Kitty was so happy. She learned that when you have good friends, they will always help you when you need it. And from that day on, Kitty and her friends played together every day, and they never lost the ball again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a wide slide and a tall stand. He wanted to play on them, but he did not know how. A girl named Sue saw Tim and came to help. She said, "I can explain how to play on the slide and stand." Tim was happy and listened to Sue. Sue explained, "First, you climb the stand, then you sit on the slide and go down." Tim climbed the stand and sat on the slide. But when he slid down, a big, soft pillow was at the bottom! Tim laughed and said, "That was fun and unexpected!" Sue laughed too, and they played together all day.

One day, a little cat named Lily was playing near a big wall. She saw a mouse named Timmy on the other side. Lily wanted to play with Timmy, so she called out, "Hi, Timmy! Can I play with you?" Timmy was frightened, but he wanted a friend. So, he said, "Okay, Lily. But we must be careful. This wall is not safe." As they played, Lily got very dirty. She knew it was time to bathe. She told Timmy, "I need to go home and get clean. Let's play again tomorrow." The next day, Lily came back to the wall to play with Timmy. But when she got there, she saw that the wall had fallen down! Timmy was hurt under the big wall. Lily was very sad and cried. She wished they had listened to Timmy about the wall not being safe. Now, she couldn't play with her new friend anymore.
