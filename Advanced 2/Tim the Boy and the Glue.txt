One day, a little boy named Tim had a glue bottle. He loved to use the glue for his art. He would tell his mom and dad about the pretty pictures he made with the glue. They were always happy to see his art. One sunny day, Tim wanted to make a big picture with his glue. He went outside and found a large piece of paper. He put the glue on the paper and made a beautiful picture with it. But then, something unexpected happened. A strong wind came and blew the paper away. Tim felt terrible. Tim went to tell his mom and dad about the lost picture. They said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will help you find it." They looked everywhere for the picture. Then, they found it stuck to a tree! The glue had saved the picture from flying away. Tim and his parents laughed and were happy that the glue helped them find the picture.

One day, a little helicopter was flying in the sky. The helicopter was feeling poor because it had a broken wing. It could not fly as high as the other helicopters. The little helicopter was very sad. Then, the little helicopter met a big bird. The bird said, "Why are you so sad, little helicopter?" The little helicopter said, "I have a broken wing and I can't fly high." The big bird felt sorry for the poor helicopter. The big bird had an idea. It said, "I can help you. I will hold your broken wing, and we can fly high together!" The little helicopter was happy. They flew high in the sky together. The little helicopter learned that when friends help each other, anything is possible.

Once upon a time, there was a great big cabinet. It was so big that it could hold all the toys in the world. The cabinet had a door with a zip. The zip was very special because it could open and close the cabinet. One day, a little boy named Tim found the cabinet while he was playing outside. He saw the zip and pulled it down. The cabinet opened, and Tim saw all the toys inside. He was so happy that he jumped up and down. Tim played with the toys all day long. When it was time to go home, he zipped the cabinet closed and said goodbye to the toys. Tim knew that he could come back and play with the toys any time he wanted. And every time he did, he felt great.

Once upon a time, there was a happy boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy axe. One day, he went to the park with his mom and his toy axe. He played and played, and had so much fun. While playing, Tom found a big box. It was very heavy. He wondered what could be inside. Tom tried to weigh it with his toy axe, but it was too heavy. He asked his mom to help him open the box. When they opened the box, they found a big cake! It was a surprise from Tom's dad. Tom and his mom were so happy. They all ate the yummy cake and played with the toy axe all day long.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play near the water. She saw a small island in the middle. Mia wanted to go to the new island. She got in a boat and rowed to the island. When Mia got to the island, she met a little boy named Tim. They played near the water and took turns to splash each other. They laughed and had fun together. "Let's find new friends on the island," said Mia. Tim agreed, and they looked around. They found a cute dog and a funny bird. All four of them played, splashed, and had a great time on the new island.

Once upon a time, there was a humble bird. The bird loved to whistle. It whistled all day long. The bird lived in a tree with a lemon on it. The lemon was big and yellow. One day, the bird saw a boy. The boy looked sad. The bird wanted to help. It flew down and gave the boy the big, yellow lemon. The boy smiled and said thank you. From that day on, the boy and the bird were friends. They played together and the bird whistled happy songs. The boy learned to whistle too. And they lived happily ever after.
