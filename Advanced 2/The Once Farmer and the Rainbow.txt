There was once a farmer who owned a big wheat field. One hot summer day, the farmer was out in the field and saw smoke rising in the sky. He quickly ran to the wheat field and found it burning. The fire was so loud and the farmer was worried it would damage the entire field. He called for help but nobody was around. He grabbed a bucket and started to run from the lake to the wheat field. He ran back and forth, bucket after bucket, trying to put out the fire. Finally, after what seemed like hours, the fire was out. The farmer was exhausted but relieved. He had saved his wheat field. The farmers thanked all the brave people who had helped him. Then, he looked up and saw a large rainbow stretching across the sky. He smiled and thought that the rainbow was a sign of good luck from the heavens.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion was rude to other animals. He would growl and scare them away. The animals did not like the lion. One day, a little bird came to the lion. The bird was lost and scared. The lion wanted to protect the bird. This was unexpected, because the lion was always rude. The other animals saw the lion being nice. They were surprised. The lion learned that being kind was better than being rude. From that day on, the lion changed and was nice to everyone. The animals became friends with the lion, and they all lived happily together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the garden. She saw a big cauliflower. It was ready to pick. She was very happy. She wanted to use it for dinner. Lucy ran to her mom and showed her the cauliflower. "Mom, look! The cauliflower is ready!" she said. Her mom smiled and said, "Great job, Lucy! Let's use it tonight." They cooked the cauliflower together. They made a yummy dish. Lucy and her mom shared it with the family. Everyone loved it. They were all very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty monster named Bob. Bob was big and green. He lived in a dark cave. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the cave. Tim was not scared of Bob. He said, "Hello, monster! My name is Tim. Will you be my friend?" Bob was surprised. He had never had a friend before. Bob and Tim played together every day. They had so much fun. Bob was happy to have a friend. And Tim was happy to have a mighty monster friend. They were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion was a leader. He was proud of his family. They lived in a nice place with lots of trees and grass. The lion had many friends, like the monkey, the elephant, and the giraffe. They all played together and had fun. One day, a war started between the animals. The lion did not like the war. He wanted everyone to be friends again. He thought of a plan to make the war stop. The lion talked to the monkey, the elephant, and the giraffe. He encouraged them to help him stop the war. The lion and his friends went to the other animals. They all talked and talked. Then, something unexpected happened. The animals all agreed to stop the war! They were all friends again. They played and had fun together. The proud lion was very happy.

Tim and Lily like to play in the garden. They look for bugs and flowers and rocks. One day, they find a lizard under a big leaf. The lizard is green and brown and has a long tail. "Wow, look at the lizard!" Tim says. "He is so interesting. Can we keep him?" Lily shakes her head. "No, we can't keep him. He is not a pet. He needs to live in the garden. He eats bugs and drinks water. He likes the sun and the shade." Tim nods. He understands. But he still wants to play with the lizard. He has an idea. He runs to the house and comes back with a plate and a cup. He puts some bread and juice on the plate and some water in the cup. He puts them on the ground near the lizard. "Look, Lily, I can serve the lizard. Maybe he is hungry and thirsty. Maybe he will be our friend." Lily smiles. She thinks Tim is funny and kind. She sits down next to him and watches the lizard. The lizard looks at the plate and the cup. He is curious. He crawls closer and sniffs the bread and the juice. He does not like them. He turns away and goes to the water. He licks the water with his tongue. He likes the water. He drinks some more. Tim and Lily clap their hands. They are happy. The lizard drank their water. They think he likes them. They talk to him softly and gently. They tell him their names and ask him his name. They do not know if he can hear them or understand them. But they do not care. They like the lizard. They think he is interesting. They hope he will stay in the garden and play with them again.
