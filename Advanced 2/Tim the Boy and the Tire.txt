One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big tire hanging from a tree. He wanted to swing on the tire, but a rude girl named Sue was on it. Sue did not want to share the tire with Tim. Tim was sad. Tim's mom told him, "You can succeed if you try and be nice." So Tim went to Sue and said, "Please, can I swing on the tire too?" Sue thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, we can take turns." Tim and Sue took turns on the tire swing. They both had fun and became good friends. Tim learned that being nice can help you succeed, even when someone is rude.

Once there was a small mouse who wanted to catch some food. The mouse started to move around, trying to spot some food. The mouse saw some tasty morsels but they were too far away. The mouse had an idea. It decided to hide in a dark corner, and wait for something tasty to pass by. The mouse waited patiently, its small eyes watching for any motion. Suddenly, the mouse saw something move. It was a tiny fly that had been buzzing around the room. Quickly, the mouse pounced on the fly, trying to catch it. The fly buzzed through the air, but the mouse was too fast. It quickly caught the fly and began to eat it. The mouse was happy that it had been able to catch its food. From then on, the mouse kept an eye out for any motion that could lead to a tasty meal.

One day, a girl named Sue found a cold, old book in her room. She wanted to read it, but she saw the word "secret" on the cover. Sue thought it was a secret book, so she did not tell her mom or dad. Sue read the secret book when mom and dad were not looking. She liked the book, but she felt bad for not telling her mom and dad. Sue wanted to be a good girl, so she decided to tell her mom and dad about the secret book. When Sue told her mom and dad, they were happy that she told them. They said it was okay to read the secret book. Sue felt good because she did the right thing. The moral of the story is to always be honest with your mom and dad.

Once upon a time, there was a jellyfish who was very popular. Everywhere he went, lots of people said hello to him. One day, he decided to go for a swim in the ocean. But as he was swimming, he had a big surprise! He bumped into a lot of other jellyfish. They had come from all over the ocean to meet him. He felt so happy and smiled as he swam around with them. Soon, he was having the time of his life! He was the most popular jellyfish in the sea and this was the best day ever!

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat pasta every day. He would sit on his chair and eat the yummy pasta his owner made for him. One sunny day, Tom saw a little bird outside his window. The bird was hungry and looking for food. Tom had an idea. He wanted to share his pasta with the bird. Tom went to his owner and asked for more pasta. Tom's owner made more pasta and put it in a bowl. Tom carried the bowl outside and sat it down. The little bird saw the pasta and flew down. The bird ate the pasta and was very happy. Tom felt good about sharing his pasta. From that day on, Tom and the bird became good friends, and they loved to sit and eat pasta together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw pictures. He was very gifted at drawing. Every day, he would try to draw something new. One day, he drew a big, round sun. It was a very nice picture. The next day, Tim went to draw another picture. But when he looked at the paper, he saw that the sun he drew was gone! Tim was sad and confused. He thought maybe his mom or dad took it. So, he asked them, but they didn't know where the sun went either. Tim decided to try again and draw another sun. But as soon as he finished, the sun jumped off the paper and turned into a real sun in the sky! Tim was so surprised and happy. From that day on, whenever Tim drew a picture, it would come to life. Tim knew he was a very gifted boy and he kept trying to draw more and more beautiful things.
