Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Tim's mom told him about a big race. The race was for kids to see who was the fastest. Tim wanted to win. The day before the race, Tim's mom reminded him to be a good sport. She said, "It's fine to want to win, but it's more important to have fun and be nice to your friends." Tim nodded and promised to remember. On the day of the race, Tim and his friends lined up to start. They all ran as fast as they could. Tim saw his friend, Ben, fall down. Tim stopped running to help Ben up. They finished the race together. Tim didn't win, but he felt fine because he helped his friend. The moral of the story is that being kind and helping others is more important than winning.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very serious dog. He liked to play with his ball and eat treats. One day, Spot found a big box in the house. The box was closed shut. Spot was very curious about what was inside the box. Spot wanted to open the box, but he was not strong enough. He tried to push it, pull it, and even bite it. But the box would not open. Spot decided to ask his friend, the cat, for help. The cat was very smart and knew how to open things. The cat tried to open the box with her claws, but it was still shut. Spot and the cat were very sad. They wanted to know what was inside the box. They tried one more time to open it. This time, they pushed the box down the stairs. The box opened, but it was not a treat inside. It was a big mess of paint. The paint spilled all over the floor and made the house very dirty. Spot and the cat were in big trouble.

Once upon a time, there was a funny girl named Lily. She loved to play with her makeup. She had lots of colors to put on her face. One day, Lily was playing with her makeup near a tree. She saw a big red ball up in the tree. Lily wanted to get the ball, so she tried to climb the tree. As Lily climbed, she started to fall. She was scared, but she landed on a soft pile of leaves. The makeup was all over her face, and she looked very funny. Lily laughed and went back to playing with her makeup.

One day, Tim found a map. The map was very big and brilliant. It showed a way to a special treasure. Tim wanted to own the treasure. He showed the map to his friend, Sam. "Look at this map, Sam!" Tim said. "Let's find the treasure and own it together." Sam and Tim went on a long walk. They followed the map to find the treasure. They walked up hills and through the woods. They were very tired, but they did not stop. They wanted to find the treasure. At last, they found the place on the map. But, there was no treasure. Instead, they found a big, beautiful tree. The tree had a note on it. The note said, "The real treasure is friendship." Tim and Sam smiled. They learned that having a good friend was better than owning any treasure. They were very happy to have each other. From that day on, they took care of the big, beautiful tree together.

Once, there were two brothers who loved to explore. One day, they came across a lime tree in a wooded area. When they saw it, they were so curious that they stared at it for a long time. The older brother said, "Let's try the limes. They must be delicious!" The younger brother hesitated and said, "I don't know. They look a bit disgusting." But he finally agreed, and they both plucked a lime and tried it. Unfortunately, their curiosity was unwise. The limes were bitter and sour and the two brothers couldn't stand it. The older brother shouted and said, "It was a bad idea to try this!" The younger brother sadly nodded and said, "We should be careful next time. It's not always a good idea to be curious." The brothers learned from this experience and went home, determined to listen to their instincts and be more cautious in the future.
