Once upon a time, there was a big park near a little girl's house. The girl's name was Amy. Amy loved to play at the park every day. She liked to do exercise, like running and jumping. Amy had a friend, a nosy little dog named Max. Max liked to play at the park too. One day, Amy and Max went to the park to play. They saw a new boy sitting on the grass. He looked sad. Amy went to him and said, "Hi, I'm Amy. Do you want to play with us?" The boy looked up and said, "I'm Tim. I don't know how to play here." Amy smiled and said, "We can show you! Let's play catch!" So, Amy, Tim, and the nosy dog Max played catch at the park. They had fun and did lots of exercise. After that, they all became good friends. They played at the park together every day.

One day, Tim and his mom went to the store. They wanted to buy some butter. Tim saw his friend, Sam, with his mom too. Sam had a big, red ball. Tim felt envious of Sam's ball. "Mom, can I have a ball too?" asked Tim. His mom said, "We can't spend our money on a ball today. We need to buy butter and other things." Tim was sad but he knew his mom was right. They bought the butter and went home. When they got home, Tim's mom had a surprise for him. She took out a big, blue ball from a bag. Tim was so happy! "Thank you, Mom!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "I knew you wanted a ball, so I bought it for you before we went to the store." Now, Tim was not envious of Sam's ball anymore. He had his own ball to play with, and it was even bigger and better. They both played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a land of different animals, there lived a kind dog named Max. One sunny day, Max wanted to introduce his new friend, a tiny bird named Bella, to his other friends. They all lived in a big forest with many trees and flowers. As they walked, Max asked Bella her opinion on the best place to play. Bella said she loved to play near the river, where she could fly and sing. Max agreed and took her to meet his friends by the river. They were excited to see Max and his new friend. When they reached the river, something unexpected happened. Bella started to grow bigger and bigger! She turned into a big, beautiful bird with colorful feathers. Max and his friends were surprised, but they still played together happily. They learned that even if friends are different, they can still have fun and share their opinions. And that is how Max and Bella became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tiny. Tiny loved to play and jump all day long. One day, Tiny saw a shiny thing high up on a shelf. Tiny wanted to reach it, but it was too high. Tiny's dad saw Tiny looking at the shiny thing and said, "Tiny, don't try to reach that. It is a razor and it is not safe for you." The next day, Tiny found a tight string. Tiny thought the tight string could help reach the shiny thing. Tiny tied the string to a toy and threw it up to the shelf. The toy hit the shiny thing and it fell down. Tiny was so happy to have the shiny thing. But then, Tiny's dad came in and saw the razor on the floor. He quickly picked it up and put it away in a safe place. He told Tiny, "I'm glad you didn't get hurt, but remember, some things are not safe for little cats to play with." Tiny learned a big lesson that day and never tried to reach for the razor again.

One day, a pumpkin went for a walk. It needed to find some friends. It passed by some animals, but they all looked the same. Then it saw a boy clapping. The pumpkin thought he might be different and ventured closer. The boy saw the pumpkin and smiled. He invited the pumpkin to play a game - clapping to the count of three. The pumpkin was excited and the boy started to count. On the last number, they both clapped at the same time! The boy was so pleased and gave the pumpkin a big hug. The pumpkin had finally found a friend. They played clapping games together every day until the pumpkin was big and ready to be picked. The little pumpkin had a different kind of friendship. And although it was sad to let go, it also seemed just perfect - like the best kind of ending. The pumpkin smiled and clapped in the air one last time.
