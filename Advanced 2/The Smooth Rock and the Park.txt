Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock. The rock lived in a big park. The rock had many friends. One day, the rock met a new friend. The new friend was a small bug. The bug said, "Hi, I am a bug. I like to bite. I will not bite you, rock. You are my friend." The rock was happy to have a new friend who would not bite. One night, the rock and the bug saw a firework in the sky. The firework was big and bright. It made the rock and the bug happy. They watched the firework together. They were very good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big bunny with incredible big ears. Bunny loved to fold his ears like paper. One day, Bunny met a little bird who flew down from a tree. "Wow, Bunny! Your ears are so big!" said the little bird. Bunny smiled and said, "Yes, I can fold them too!" So, Bunny showed the bird how he could fold his ears. They played together all day, folding Bunny's ears into different shapes. As the sun went down, Bunny and the bird were very tired. They decided to go home. On the way, they saw a big hole in the ground. Bunny didn't see it because his ears were still folded. He fell into the hole and couldn't get out. The little bird tried to help, but he was too small. Bunny was sad and stuck in the hole. The little bird flew away to find help, but nobody came. Bunny stayed in the hole, and his incredible ears were folded forever.

Once upon a time there were two brothers who were very different. One brother was naughty and the other was kind. One day, the naughty brother said to his kind brother "Let's fight!" and the kind brother said "no, let's not fight". But the naughty brother kept saying "Let's fight!" and the kind brother said again "no, let's not fight" The naughty brother got angry and said "I'm going to eat, and you can't have any." The kind brother was very ashamed and felt bad and said "ok, I don't want to fight, I never wanted to fight". The kind brother was very hungry but he was too ashamed to ask for food. Suddenly, the naughty brother saw how sad his kind brother was, and felt sorry for him. He said, "Here, take some of my food - let's eat together!" The brothers sat down and enjoyed their meal together. They never fought again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw her shadow on the ground. It was a sunny day. Lily thought her shadow was different. It was bigger than her! She wanted to play with her shadow. Lily said, "Shadow, will you play with me?" Her shadow didn't talk, but it followed her everywhere. They played in the park. They went on the swings and the slide. Lily's shadow followed her up and down, and side to side. At the end of the day, Lily went home. She told her mom about her new friend, the shadow. Her mom smiled and said, "Your shadow will always be with you on sunny days. It likes to follow you and play." Lily was happy to have a special friend that would always be there.

One day, a boy named Tim went outside to play. He wore his helmet because he wanted to ride his bike. He saw his friend, Sue, who was also wearing a helmet. Tim said, "Hi!" and Sue replied, "Hi! Let's ride bikes together!" They rode their bikes up and down the street. After a while, Tim thought it was boring. He wanted to do something fun and new. Sue had an idea. She said, "Let's go to the park and play on the swings!" When they got to the park, they saw a big, shiny box. Tim and Sue were very curious. They opened the box and found a magic helmet inside! They put the magic helmet on and suddenly, they could fly! Tim and Sue flew high in the sky, laughing and playing. They were so happy they found the magic helmet. From that day on, they always had fun adventures together. And they never thought riding bikes was boring again.
