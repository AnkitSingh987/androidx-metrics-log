One day, a girl named Sally found a comb on the floor. She picked it up and looked at it. It was a pretty comb with a picture of a sun on it. Sally wanted to keep the comb, so she put it in her pocket. Sally went to her friend Tom's house. It was a hot day, so they played inside. Tom saw the comb in Sally's pocket and asked, "What's that?" Sally showed him the comb and said, "I found it. It's mine now." Tom wanted to play with the comb too. They took turns combing each other's hair. After they were done, Sally put the comb back in her pocket. Then, they went outside to play. Before they left, Tom's mom said, "Lock the door, please." Tom locked the door, and they had fun playing outside all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy pistol that he loved to play with every day. One day, his friend Sam came over to play. Sam saw Tim's pistol and felt envious. He wanted to play with it too. Sam said, "Tim, can I play with your pistol too?" Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, but you have to replace it if you break it." Sam agreed and they started to play together. As they played, they started to argue about who got to use the pistol. The conflict made them both sad. They decided to take turns playing with the pistol, so they could both have fun. In the end, Tim and Sam learned to share and became even better friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. She had a regular bracelet. Sue loved her bracelet very much. One day, she went for a walk and saw a big tree. She began to wonder what was behind the tree. Sue went behind the tree and saw a small door. She opened the door and found a room full of toys. Sue played with the toys and had a lot of fun. She forgot about her bracelet and left it in the room. When Sue went home, she noticed her bracelet was gone. She felt sad and went back to the tree. But when she opened the door, her bracelet was not there. Instead, there was a big, shiny, new bracelet. Sue was so happy and couldn't believe it. She put on the new bracelet and went home, smiling all the way.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tower in a big forest. In the tower, there was a small bird named Bobo. Bobo was frightened to fly out of the tower. He would remain inside his home all day. One day, a friendly squirrel named Squeaky came to the tower. Squeaky said, "Hi Bobo! Why do you stay in the tower? Come, play with me outside!" Bobo was scared, but he wanted to try. Bobo took a deep breath and flew out of the tower. He was still frightened, but he was also happy to play with his new friend. From that day on, Bobo and Squeaky played together every day, and Bobo was no longer scared to leave the tower.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Zoom. Zoom loved to race around and sort his toy friends. One day, Zoom felt upset because he had no one to play with. Zoom went to the park to find a friend. He saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to race around it. Zoom raced around the tree, going very fast. He felt so happy and forgot he was upset. But then, Zoom raced too fast and hit the tree. He broke and could not move. Zoom was very upset again. His toy friends came to help him, but they could not fix him. The moral of the story is to be careful and not go too fast, or bad things can happen.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big earthquake. People were scared. They did not know what to do. A kind man named Tom wanted to help. He had a plan to release big balloons into the sky. The balloons would make everyone feel fine. Tom released the balloons and people looked up. They saw the pretty colors and felt fine. The earthquake was over and everyone was happy. The town was safe again, thanks to Tom and his balloons.
