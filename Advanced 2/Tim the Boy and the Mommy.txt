Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to wonder about the world around him. One day, he went for a walk with his mommy. They saw many cars and buses, and Tim wondered about the traffic. "Why are there so many cars, mommy?" Tim asked. His mommy smiled and said, "People need to go to different places, Tim. They use cars and buses to do that." Tim felt grateful that his mommy knew so much. As they walked, Tim saw a big truck stuck in the traffic. He felt sad for the truck driver. Tim said, "I wish I could help the truck move." His mommy hugged him and said, "That's very kind of you, Tim. But sometimes, we just have to be patient and wait." Tim and his mommy continued their walk, and Tim kept wondering about the world. He was grateful for his mommy, who always had the answers to his questions. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a calendar. The calendar was very nice, but sometimes it could be a little rude. It hung on the wall in a big room where many people came to play and have fun. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the room with her friends. They wanted to sing a song together. They were happy and excited. But when they started to sing, the calendar interrupted them. It said, "Stop! I don't like that song. It's too loud." Lucy and her friends were sad. They didn't want to be rude, but they wanted to sing. So, they tried to sing very softly. The calendar listened and then it smiled. It said, "That's better. Now I can enjoy your song too." From that day on, Lucy and her friends always sang softly in the room with the calendar. They learned that being nice to others, even a rude calendar, can make everyone happy. And the calendar learned to be nicer too, because it loved to hear the children sing.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant sun in the sky. It was very bright and made everyone happy. One day, the sun saw a big rock and decided to play with it. The sun used its heat to split the rock into two pieces. Inside the rock, there was a new texture. It was very soft and fun to touch. The sun was very happy with its new friend, the soft rock. The sun and the soft rock played together every day. They made shapes and patterns with the soft texture. Everyone in the world loved the beautiful things the sun and the soft rock made. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Lily were playing with blocks in the living room. They liked to build towers and houses with the blocks. Tom had a lot of yellow blocks, and Lily had a lot of blue blocks. They shared some of their blocks, but they also wanted to keep some for themselves. "Look at my tower!" Tom said. "It is very tall and yellow. It is the best tower ever!" Lily looked at Tom's tower. She thought it was nice, but she also wanted some yellow blocks. She had an idea. She waited until Tom was not looking, and then she took some of his yellow blocks and added them to her house. "Hey!" Tom said, when he saw what Lily did. "Those are my yellow blocks! Give them back!" "No!" Lily said. "I need them for my house. It is very pretty and blue and yellow. It is the best house ever!" Tom was angry. He did not like that Lily took his blocks. He wanted to get them back. He ran to Lily's house and pushed it with his hands. The house fell down and the blocks scattered on the floor. "Tom!" Lily said, crying. "You broke my house! That was not nice!" Tom felt bad. He saw that Lily was sad and he did not want to make her cry. He realized that he was not nice either. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I should not have pushed your house. That was not nice. Can we share the blocks and play together?" Lily stopped crying. She looked at Tom and saw that he was sorry. She said, "It's okay, Tom. I'm sorry too. I should not have taken your blocks. That was not nice. Yes, we can share the blocks and play together." Tom and Lily hugged each other. They picked up the blocks and started to build a new tower and a new house. They used yellow and blue blocks, and some other colors too. They had fun and they were happy. They learned to be nice and to share.
