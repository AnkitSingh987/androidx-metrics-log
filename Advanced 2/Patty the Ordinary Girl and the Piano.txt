Once upon a time, there was an ordinary girl named Patty. One day, Patty was playing with her friends when she realized she had an interest in music. She asked her mom if she could learn how to play the piano. Hearing this, her mom was so excited, knowing how special the piano was, so they immediately went out to buy a nice one. But once they arrived home and Patty was ready to start her lessons, something strange happened. The piano only played sad and angry songs. Patty tried again and again, but no matter what she did, the piano kept playing the same sad notes. Her friends were scared and stayed away. The more Patty played, the more frustrated she became. Suddenly, she realized it was not the piano's fault, but her own. Patty had been feeling sad and angry and so it was those emotions that were coming out in her playing. Confused and scared, Patty asked her mom, "What is wrong with me?". Her mom tried to reassure her, but Patty left the room in tears. From that day forward, Patty stayed away from the piano. She was too scared and sad to face it again. The end.

One day, a smart dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a big puddle on the ground. Spot wanted to jump in the puddle, but he didn't want to get wet. "Snap!" said Spot, as he had an idea. He found a long stick and put it in the puddle. He watched the water move around the stick. Spot was happy he could play with the puddle without getting wet. Just then, a little girl named Sue came by. She saw Spot playing with the stick in the puddle. "Hi, Spot!" said Sue. "Can I play too?" Spot wagged his tail and they both played with the puddle using sticks. They had a fun day and stayed dry.

Once upon a time, there was a brave girl called Lucy. She loved to explore, and one day she went off into the woods by herself. In the woods, Lucy stumbled across an old spear. She scooped it up and admired it. It was shiny and sparkly. Just then, a bossy old witch appeared. She shouted at Lucy to drop the spear. But Lucy refused. She wasn't scared of the witch. The witch stamped her foot and tried to grab the spear from Lucy. But Lucy was too fast for her, and she kept out of reach. The witch was angry, but Lucy stayed brave and determined. She would not drop the spear. Finally, the witch gave up and scuttled off into the woods. Lucy had won! She tucked the spear into her pocket and ran home, proud of her adventure.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a clear bucket in his yard. He was very happy. He wanted to show his friend, Sue. He ran to her house and said, "Look, Sue! I found a clear bucket!" Sue looked at the bucket and said, "Wow! That's a nice bucket, Tim! What can we do with it?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Let's go to the beach and fill it with sand and shells!" At the beach, Tim and Sue filled the clear bucket with sand and pretty shells. They said, "This is so much fun!" They played all day and made a big sand castle with their bucket. When the sun went down, they said goodbye to the beach and went home with their clear bucket full of sand and shells.

Once upon a time, a girl named Lucy loved to bake. One day, she wanted to make a delicate cake for her friend's birthday. She put on her apron and started to mix all the ingredients. While she was baking, her brother Tom came into the kitchen. He was holding a film. He said, "Lucy, can we watch this film together after you finish baking?" Lucy smiled and said, "Yes, Tom. We can watch the film when the cake is done." When the cake was ready, Lucy and Tom sat down to watch the film. They laughed and enjoyed the movie. After the film, they took the delicate cake to their friend's house. Everyone loved the cake and had a great time at the party. Lucy and Tom were happy they could make their friend's birthday special.
