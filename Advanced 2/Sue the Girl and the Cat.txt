Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with dough. She would make fun shapes like a cat, a dog, and a sun. Sue was very slow when she played with dough because she wanted her shapes to look just right. One day, Sue went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy more dough. Sue picked out her favorite colors: red, blue, and yellow. When they were ready to leave, Sue's mom had to pay for the dough. Sue watched as her mom gave the nice lady at the store some money. On their way home, Sue was so excited to play with her new dough. She thought about all the fun shapes she would make. Sue knew that being slow and careful would make her dough shapes look the best. And her mom was always happy to see her play and have fun.

Once there was a little boy called Jack. He was playing in the park with his dog, Max. But all of a sudden, Jack started to worry. He had forgotten to bring the shampoo! Without it, he and Max couldn't have their regular bath! Jack started running home as fast as he could. He ran and ran until he could see his house in the distance. He ran even faster and soon he was home. He rushed to the bathroom and got the shampoo. He quickly grabbed a towel and stuffed it into his bag. Jack then ran back to the park with his shampoo. Max was waiting for him, and he was so happy to see Jack. Jack gave Max a regular bath and they both felt much better. Jack was glad he had listened to his worry and gone home to get the shampoo. From now on, he always made sure he brought shampoo with him.

Once upon a time, in a small house, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue lived with their mom. They loved to eat sweet things, and sugar was their favorite. One day, their mom baked a big cake and put a lot of sugar on top. Tim and Sue were excited to eat the cake. But there was only one big piece left. They both wanted it. Their mom said, "You need to share the cake, and be flexible with each other." So, they decided to share the cake and be good friends. Tim and Sue cut the cake into two pieces. They were happy to share and be flexible. They ate the cake together, laughing and smiling. They knew sharing made them both happy. And from that day on, they always shared their sugar treats with each other.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was an ancient tree. The tree was very big and very old. It had many branches and leaves. The tree was special because it gave paper to the people in the village. The paper was used to draw, write and play. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the tree. She wanted to get some paper to draw a picture for her mom. Lily saw a squirrel resting on a branch of the tree. The squirrel was tired from running and jumping all day. Lily thought that the squirrel needed more rest, so she decided not to take any paper from the tree that day. The next day, Lily went back to the tree. The squirrel was gone, and she took some paper to draw her picture. She showed the picture to her mom, who was very happy. Lily learned that sometimes it is good to wait and let others rest. She also learned to care for the ancient tree and the animals that lived in it. This made her a kind and thoughtful girl in her village.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a big elephant. The elephant had a long trunk. He liked to enjoy playing with his trunk. He would pick up leaves and small sticks with it. All the other animals would watch him play. One day, a little green frog saw the elephant playing. The frog was jealous of the elephant's trunk. He wished he had a long trunk too. The frog went to the elephant and asked if he could try playing with the trunk. The elephant said yes, and they tried to think of a way for the frog to have a trunk. Suddenly, a big bird flew down with a long piece of grass. The bird put the grass on the frog's face like a trunk. The frog was so happy and started to enjoy playing with his new trunk. All the animals laughed and had fun together.
