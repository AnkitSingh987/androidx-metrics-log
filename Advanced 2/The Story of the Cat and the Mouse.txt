Once upon a time, there was a big, scary cat. The cat lived in a house made of steel. Every day, the cat would walk around the steel house and look for something fun to do. One day, a little mouse came to the house. The mouse was not scared of the big, scary cat. The mouse said, "Hello, big cat! Do you want to play with me?" The big, scary cat did not reply. The mouse waited and tried again, "Please, let's play together!" Then, something unexpected happened. The big, scary cat began to cry. The mouse was confused. The cat said, "I'm sorry I did not reply. I am not really scary. I am just very sad because no one wants to play with me." The mouse felt bad for the cat and said, "I will play with you!" From that day on, the big, scary cat and the little mouse became the best of friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a helmet. The helmet was big and shiny. Tim was so happy. He put the helmet on his head. It was heavy. Tim thought it must weigh a lot. He walked around with the helmet on, feeling like a strong hero. Tim saw a tree with tasty red apples. He wanted to eat one. He tried to reach up, but the helmet was too heavy. Tim felt sad. He took off the helmet and put it on the ground. Now, he could reach the apples. Tim picked a tasty apple and took a big bite. It was so good! He shared the apple with his friends. They all laughed and played, with the helmet next to them. They took turns wearing the helmet and pretending to be heroes.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate girl named Lily. Lily liked to help her mom and dad with chores around the house. One day, Lily saw water dripping from the faucet in the kitchen. She wanted to help, so she tried to close the faucet. Lily tried and tried to close the faucet, but it was very hard. She did not give up. She asked her mom for help. Her mom showed her how to turn the faucet the right way to close it. Lily tried again and finally, the water stopped dripping. Lily felt happy that she could help her mom and dad. They were proud of her for being so compassionate and not giving up. From that day on, Lily always helped her parents close the faucet whenever they needed her. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a kind queen who lived in a big castle. She had many friends who loved her. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the castle. She wanted to meet the queen. The queen saw Lucy and said, "Hello, little girl. What is your name?" Lucy said, "My name is Lucy, and I want to be your friend." The queen smiled and said, "I would love to be your friend too, Lucy." One day, Lucy did something bad. She broke a pretty vase in the castle. The queen was sad, but she did not want to hurt Lucy. So, she did not punish her. Instead, the queen said, "Lucy, we all make mistakes. Just say sorry and be more careful next time." Lucy said sorry and hugged the queen. They stayed friends and lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tom and a girl named Sue were playing with their toys. They had many toys, but they did not like to share. So they decided to separate their toys. Tom put all his toys on one side of the room, and Sue put her toys on the other side. They felt happy that they did not have to share. Tom's mom saw them playing and said, "Why don't you play together? It's more fun that way!" But Tom and Sue did not want to listen. They wanted to keep their toys separate. Tom's mom had an idea. She brought a big measure and said, "Let's see who has more toys. The one with more toys has to share with the other." Tom and Sue agreed to the game. They started to put their toys in the measure. But suddenly, the measure started to shake! The toys were jumping out of the measure and began to dance around the room. Tom and Sue were very surprised and a little uncomfortable. "Wow! Our toys are alive!" said Tom. "This is amazing!" said Sue. They looked at each other and smiled. From that day on, Tom and Sue decided to share their toys and play together. They learned that sharing can be fun and bring unexpected surprises.
