Once there was a little girl called Mia. She was playing in the garden, exploring and having lots of fun. Mia looked around and noticed the soil was soft, cold and icy. It felt strange on her feet so she started jumping up and down to make the soil even colder. Mia then looked around the garden to find more fun things to do. She noticed a tree with beautiful shiny leaves and decided to climb up and have a look. She felt the cold icy breeze on her face and heard the leaves rustling. She looked around and enjoyed the view at the top before carefully climbing down the tree. After that, Mia decided to look for some hidden treasures in the soil. She dug with her hands and uncovered some wonderful things - a tiny silver toy car, some stones and an old rusty button. Mia was so happy that she had found these treasures and she carefully took them home. From then on, Mia made sure that she always looked for buried treasures in the icy soil of the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a mouse called Chip. Chip was very brave and liked to explore. One day, he ventured into a weird, green wood. The wood looked scary but Chip decided to explore anyway. He soon got lost and started to suffer. He was so scared and couldn't find the way home. Suddenly, he heard a voice. It was a big, friendly owl. The owl asked Chip what he was doing so far away from home. When Chip told the owl that he was lost, the owl smiled and told him that he would help. The owl showed Chip the way home, pointing out all the landmarks along the way. Chip was so happy to be back home and thanked the owl for its help. The owl nodded and said, "Don't ever go into a strange, scary woods again. Even brave mice can suffer when they are lost." The moral of this story is that even brave mice can get into trouble if they explore unknown, scary places. Always be careful and stay away from dangerous places.

One day, a girl named Lily wore her pretty pale skirt. She loved to turn and spin in it. Lily went to the park to play with her friends. At the park, Lily saw her friend, Tim. Tim said, "I like your skirt, Lily!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim! I like to turn in it." They played and laughed together. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Lily's skirt up. Lily was sad and felt shy. Tim helped her and said, "Don't worry, Lily. We all have accidents." All the kids learned to be kind and help each other when things go wrong.

One day, a thin bird was in the sky. The bird could soar very high. It liked to fly near the sun. It had a big smile on its face. The bird was very happy. The bird saw a big tree. It wanted to sit in the tree. The bird went down to the tree. But, something unexpected happened. The tree was not a tree. It was a big green hat on a tall man. The tall man did not like the bird. He tried to catch the bird. The bird was scared. It tried to soar high again. But, the tall man was too fast. He caught the bird. The bird was sad. It could not fly in the sky anymore. The tall man put the bird in a small cage. The bird's face was not happy now. The bird missed the sky and the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw with his pencil. He had a big box of pencils in all colors. Tim was a healthy boy who liked to play outside with his friends. One day, Tim could not find his favorite pencil. He looked everywhere in his room, but he could not find it. Tim was very sad. He did not want to draw with any other pencil. His mom told him, "Maybe it's outside where you play with your friends." The next day, Tim went outside to look for his pencil. He asked his friends if they had seen it. His friend, Lily, said she found a pencil on the ground yesterday. She gave it to Tim, and it was his favorite pencil! Tim was so happy to have it back. He knew that he should keep his pencils in a safe place from now on. From that day on, Tim always put his pencils back in the box after he was done drawing. He did not want to lose his favorite pencil again. Tim and his friends played outside and drew pictures with their pencils every day. They all lived happily ever after.
