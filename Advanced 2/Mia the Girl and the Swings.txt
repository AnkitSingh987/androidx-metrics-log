Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a big, red bow in her hair. Mia loved her bow very much. It was not expensive, but it was very special to her. One day, Mia went to the park with her mommy. She was playing with her friends. They were pushing each other on the swings. Mia was having so much fun. But then, her bow fell off her head while she was playing. Mia was very sad. She looked and looked for her bow. She asked her friends to help her find it. They all pushed the swings away and looked under them. Finally, they found the bow under the slide. Mia was so happy to have her bow back. She thanked her friends and they all played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor named Tom. Tom was a good sailor, but he was embarrassed because he could not pray. Every night, he would watch his friends pray before they went to sleep. Tom wanted to learn how to pray, but he did not know how. One day, Tom asked his friend, Sam. "Sam, can you teach me how to pray?" Sam smiled and said, "Of course, Tom! It is easy. Just close your eyes and talk to the sky." Tom did as Sam said, but he still felt embarrassed. He was scared that he was not doing it right. The next night, Tom tried again. He closed his eyes and talked to the sky. This time, he felt better. He was not embarrassed anymore. Tom had learned how to pray, thanks to his friend Sam. Now, Tom could pray with his friends every night before they went to sleep.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Maria who didn't like to sleep. Maria stayed up late every night, making it difficult for her to focus in school the next day. One day Maria's mom gave her an idea. She said, "Maria, why don't you try putting some oil in a diffuser and see if it helps you sleep?" So, Maria tried it out. The smell of the oil made Maria feel sleepy and relaxed. She went to sleep earlier every night and woke up feeling organized and alert in the morning. Maria learned an important lesson - it's important to get enough sleep every night so you can do your best during the day.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called James. One day his aunt came to visit James and his family. James was so excited to see her! His aunt brought a bag of treats with her to give to James. James opened the bag, but instead of treats he found small pieces of paper. He looked up at his aunt and asked, "What are these?" But his aunt just looked away and said nothing. James was confused, but then his mum explained to him that these were special messages, and his aunt was scattering them into the sky. His mum further explained, “Your aunt is sending out a special message and calling for help.” James looked back at his aunt, who was now smiling and looked proud. He knew that his aunt was brave and didn't feel afraid, even though she felt sometimes quite ignorant. “Let's go and scatter the messages together!” his aunt said. “Yes!” James exclaimed, and the two joined hands and scattered the messages until all of them flew away.

One day, a little baby was playing with her toys. She had a big ball, a small doll, and a toy car. She was very happy. The sun was shining, and the baby was laughing. Suddenly, a bitter wind blew through the open window. The baby's toys flew away! The baby was very sad. She cried and cried. Then, a nice cat came in through the open window. The cat saw the baby crying and wanted to help. The cat ran outside and found the baby's toys. The cat brought the toys back to the baby one by one. The baby stopped crying and smiled at the cat. She said, "Thank you, cat!" The cat meowed happily. And from that day on, the baby and the cat were the best of friends.
