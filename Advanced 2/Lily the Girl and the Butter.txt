One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to help her mom in the kitchen. She saw her mom spreading butter on a plate. Lily thought it looked fun and wanted to try it too. "Mom, can I help spread the butter?" Lily asked. Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Lily! Here is your own plate and butter." Lily was so happy and started to spread the butter on her plate. She was persistent, even when it was hard to spread. After a while, Lily's mom looked at her plate and saw that she had done a great job. "Wow, Lily! You did it!" her mom said. Lily felt proud and knew that she could do anything if she was persistent.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Jenny. Jenny was very proud of an invitation she had. She had been invited to her friend Sue's birthday party. Jenny was so excited that she wanted to attach the invitation to her coat so she wouldn't lose it. Jenny's mom cut off a piece of string from her sewing box. Jenny held out her coat, and her mom helped her attach the invitation with the string. Jenny was even more proud of her coat now! The day of the party came, and Jenny and her mom drove to Sue's house. When Jenny went inside she saw lots of presents and many people. Jenny was happy to show her invitation to everyone. All her friends were very proud of Jenny's invitation. The party was a great success. Before Jenny left, she gave Sue a big hug and thanked her for the invitation. Jenny was glad she had remembered to attach it to her coat!

Once upon a time, there was a big ancient tree. The tree had many leaves. One day, a little boy found a leaf that had a print on it. The print was of a happy face. The boy liked the print on the leaf. In the same forest, there was a thief. The thief liked to take things from others. He saw the boy with the leaf and he wanted it too. The thief took the leaf from the boy when he was not looking. The boy was sad when he saw that the leaf was gone. He looked for the thief and found him with the leaf. The boy asked the thief to give the leaf back. The thief felt bad and gave the leaf back to the boy. From that day, the thief learned not to take things from others. The boy and the thief became friends and played together under the ancient tree.

Once upon a time, there was a famous bean. This bean was not like other beans. It could bounce very high. It lived in a small house with a big family. The family loved the famous bean. One day, the famous bean went outside to play. It bounced and bounced all around the yard. The famous bean was so happy. It loved to bounce and play with its family. The family watched the famous bean bounce and they all laughed. They clapped their hands and cheered. The famous bean felt so special. And so, the famous bean and the family lived happily ever after, always playing and bouncing together.

Once upon a time, in a large house, there lived a cat named Wink. Wink was a very big cat with a long tail. He loved to play with his little friends, the mice, in the house. One day, Wink saw his mouse friend, Tiny, looking sad. "Why are you sad, Tiny?" asked Wink. Tiny said, "I want to go outside and play, but my mom says I must stay inside today." Wink thought for a moment and said, "Let's play inside then! We can have lots of fun here too." So, Wink and Tiny played hide and seek, and they had a great time. They laughed and played all day long. In the end, Tiny was happy that he stayed inside with his friend Wink. And Wink was glad to see his friend happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside in her big yard. One day, she found a small tree on one side of the yard. The tree looked sad and not ready to grow big and strong. Lily's mom told her that the tree needed help. The tree's leaves were starting to rot. Lily wanted to help the tree, so she asked her mom what to do. Her mom said, "We need to give the tree lots of water, good dirt, and lots of love." Every day, Lily took care of the tree. She gave it water, put good dirt around it, and told the tree she loved it. Slowly, the tree started to look better. The leaves stopped rotting and the tree grew big and strong. Lily was so happy and proud. The moral of the story is that when we take care of things and give them love, they can grow strong and healthy.
