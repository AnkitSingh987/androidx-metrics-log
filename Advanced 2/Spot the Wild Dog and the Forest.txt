Once upon a time, there was a wild dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play all day. He had many friends in the forest. They would play fun games together. One day, Spot and his friends decided to have a race. They all stood in a row, ready to run as fast as they could. Spot was excited and wanted to win the race. His friends were excited too. The race started, and all the animals ran as fast as they could. Spot ran and ran until he was very tired. But he did not give up. Finally, Spot reached the end and finished the race. He was so happy and proud. All his friends cheered for him, and they all had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly sheep. The sheep lived on a farm with other animals. The sheep liked to play with his friends. One day, a big dog came to the farm. The dog was not nice. The dog said, "I will kick all the animals!" The elderly sheep was scared. He went to his friends and told them, "The big dog wants to kick us!" The animals were sad and scared. They tried to hide from the dog. But the dog found them. The dog kicked the elderly sheep and his friends. The animals were hurt and sad. The big dog left the farm, and the animals never forgot the bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Pete. He had a special sack, made of soft cloth and adorned with bold stars. One Sunday, Pete and his mum went to church. Pete was excited to go, but he was also scared. Church was a very big, noisy place and he was worried it might be difficult. On the way to church, Pete's mum stopped at the park. They took out the sack and filled it with pinecones, leaves and other things. Mum wrapped it tightly and told Pete to carry it to church. When Pete and his mum arrived at church, Pete was amazed. He couldn't believe his eyes. Everything was much bigger than he could have ever imagined. As Pete and his mum were about to enter the church, Pete's mum stopped and said: “This sack is special, Pete. I think it will surprise everybody inside.” Pete was excited and stepped forward with the sack. When he got to the front of the church, Pete opened the sack and out popped a giant, colourful balloon. Everyone in the church cheered and clapped in excitement. Pete smiled. He was proud of his sack and his courage to bring it to church, even though it was difficult.

Once upon a time, there was a shy boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toy gun. He would shoot at the trees and pretend he was a big hero. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big box under the slide. "What's that?" he asked. He opened the box and found treasure! There were shiny coins and pretty necklaces inside. Tim was so happy. He took the treasure home and showed his mom. She was proud of her shy hero. They played with the treasure and shot at trees together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a swan. The swan was very organized. Every day, the swan would look around the pond to find things to clean. One day, the swan saw a big mess near the water. The swan went to look at the mess. The swan cleaned up the mess and made the pond look nice again. All the other animals were happy. They liked the clean pond. They said, "Thank you, swan!" The swan was happy too. The swan liked to help and make things look nice.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to design. She would draw pictures all day. One day, she decided to design a waffle house. Lily worked hard to make her waffle house. She made the walls with waffles. She made the roof with waffles too. She wanted her waffle house to be safe for her and her friends. When the waffle house was done, Lily invited her friends to play. They all felt safe in the waffle house. They ate waffles and played games. They had a lot of fun in the waffle house that Lily designed.
