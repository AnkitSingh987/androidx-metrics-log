Once upon a time, there was a little bug. The little bug liked to crawl. One day, the little bug wanted to go to a big place called university. The bug wanted to learn new things. So, the little bug started to crawl to the university. On the way to the university, the little bug met a sour fruit. The sour fruit was sad because it was not sweet. The little bug told the sour fruit, "You can come with me to the university. We can learn how to be sweet together." The sour fruit was happy and went with the little bug. When the little bug and the sour fruit got to the university, they learned many things. They learned how to be sweet and how to be kind. The little bug and the sour fruit became best friends. They crawled back home, happy and sweet, and shared their new knowledge with all their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was fearful of lightning. Every time it rained, he would hide under his bed. One day, his mom told him, "Tim, you need to accept that lightning is just a part of nature. It won't hurt you if you're inside the house." Tim listened to his mom but still felt scared. One night, there was a big storm. Tim saw the lightning outside his window and felt very scared. But he remembered his mom's words and took a deep breath. He told himself, "I can accept the lightning." And just like that, Tim felt a little less scared.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Velvet. She had soft fur and big eyes. Velvet liked to ask many questions. She was a very serious cat. One day, Velvet met a big dog named Max. Velvet wanted to know why Max was so big. She asked Max, "Why are you so big?" Max did not answer. Velvet was sad. She wanted to know the answer. The next day, Velvet saw Max again. This time, Max was carrying a big bone. Velvet asked, "Why do you have a big bone?" Max smiled and answered, "I have a big bone because I am a big dog." Velvet was happy because she finally got her answer. From that day on, Velvet and Max were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Spot. Spot was very good at doing tricks. He could sit, roll over, and even fetch a ball. Spot loved to play with his friends at the park. One day, Spot saw a treat on the ground. He wanted to eat it, but he knew it was not his. Spot decided to tear a piece of the treat and share it with his friends. They all took turns eating a piece. Spot and his friends were very happy to share the treat. They played together all day long. Spot learned that sharing with his friends was a very good thing to do. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy and his mom went to the mall. They wanted to buy some toys and clothes. The mall was very big and had lots of shops. In the mall, the boy saw a twisty toy. He liked it a lot. He asked his mom if he could have it. His mom said yes, and they bought the twisty toy. On their way home, the boy played with his twisty toy. He was very happy. But then, he saw an angry dog. The dog barked loudly. The boy was scared, but his mom held his hand. They walked away from the angry dog and went home. The boy was safe and played with his twisty toy all day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw with his red pencil. One day, he found a big eraser on the floor. He thought it was magic because it was so big and shiny. Tim went to his friend, Sue, and said, "Look what I found! Let's start drawing with my red pencil and use this big eraser if we make a mistake." Sue was excited and they both started drawing together. They drew houses, trees, and animals. When they made a mistake, they used the big eraser to fix it. After a while, they noticed that their drawings looked better when they worked together. Tim and Sue learned that it was good to help each other and share. They also learned that it was okay to make mistakes because they could always use the big eraser to fix them. The moral of the story is that sharing and helping each other can make things better and more fun.
