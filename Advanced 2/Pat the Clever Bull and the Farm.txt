Once upon a time, there lived a clever bull named Pat. He lived on a quiet farm and every day, he liked to bring something from the farm to the other animals. One day, Pat decided to bring something special. He stood in the field, thinking hard about what to bring. He thought, and he thought and he thought some more. Suddenly, Pat had an idea - he would bring an apple! The next day, Pat woke up early and he prepared to bring the apple to the farm. As he walked, he heard a noise. It was a wild apple hunt with lots of animals running around looking for apples. All the animals were very happy when Pat brought them the apple. Pat was so happy and he felt pleased with himself. Everyone cheered him and praised him for being so clever. From then on, Pat brought the other animals on the farm the most wonderful things. He was a great friend and everyone loved him very much.

Once upon a time, in a small town near the sea, there lived a restless boy named Tim. Tim loved the sea and wanted to sail on it. One day, he went to the market with his dad to sell fruits. At the market, they met a sailor named Sam. Sam had a big boat. Tim asked Sam, "Can I sail with you?" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, you can join me on my boat." Tim was very happy. He helped his dad sell fruits and then went sailing with Sam. They sailed all day and Tim was no longer restless. He loved sailing and made a new friend.

Jimmy was walking in the park one day. He looked around and he saw an igloo. He wanted to know more about it so he decided to observe it. He went closer to the igloo and he heard voices from inside. Jimmy looked inside and he saw three kids. They were playing and they were laughing. Jimmy felt a bit ashamed because he didn't have anyone to play with. Suddenly, one of the kids noticed Jimmy. He invited him inside the igloo. Jimmy was so happy to join the kids and he started to play with them. After a long and happy game, one of the kids said something foolish. He said he could do whatever he wanted to do. All the other kids laughed at the foolish thing. Jimmy looked at them and he said: "Friends, I think it is very important to know how to play and how to laugh, but it's even more important to know when to be wise and wise words are not foolish - they are wise!" The kids thought about his words and they all agreed. From that day on, they all made sure to think carefully before saying foolish words.

Once upon a time, there was a big monster. The monster liked to destroy things. He broke trees and houses. People were scared of the monster. One day, a little girl saw the monster. She was not scared. She said, "Why do you destroy things, monster?" The monster looked at her and said, "I don't know. I just do it." The little girl thought for a moment. "Why don't you try to be good?" she asked. The monster thought about it. He decided to try. The monster started to help people. He fixed the houses and trees he broke. Everyone was surprised. They thought the monster was bad, but now he was good. The monster and the little girl became friends. They played together and everyone was happy.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big rain cloud in the sky. They are sad. They do not want to get wet. But then, the sun comes out. It shines on the rain. Anna and Ben see something beautiful. It is a rainbow. It has many colors. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple. They smile. They like the rainbow. They want to touch the rainbow. They run to the hill. They raise their arms. They try to reach the rainbow. But it is too far. They cannot touch it. They are disappointed. Then, they hear a voice. It is their mom. She calls them. She says it is time to go home. She has a surprise for them. They wonder what it is. They run to their mom. She hugs them. She gives them a cookie. It has rainbow sprinkles. They are happy. They thank their mom. They eat the cookie. It is sweet and crunchy. They think it is attractive. They share it with each other. They are good friends. They love the rainbow and the cookie.
