One day, a cool cat named Tom went to the park. He saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max had a big red ball. Tom wanted to play with the ball too. Tom said, "Hey Max, let's trade toys. I have a fun blue car. You can play with it if I can play with your ball." Max thought it was a good idea, so they traded toys. They played together and had lots of fun. But then, something happened. A big wind came and took Max's ball up into a tree. Max was sad. Tom had an idea. He put his blue car under the tree and climbed on it. He reached up and got the ball. Max was happy again. They learned that trading and helping each other made their day more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty town with lots of attractive houses. In the middle of the town, there was a big square. The square had trees, flowers, and a little playground where kids loved to play. One day, a nice family came to the town to settle in a new house. They had a little girl named Lily. Lily was very happy to see the square and the playground. Lily made new friends in the square. They played on the swings, climbed the trees, and had fun. Every day, Lily and her friends enjoyed the pretty square. And they all lived happily in their attractive town.

Once upon a time there was a rich man. He wore fancy clothes and had a house with all the best things money could buy. One day the man heard a rumbling in the sky. Rain was coming! He tied a rope from his balcony to a nearby tree so he could hang his expensive coat outside. But when the rain had finished, he saw something amazing! Where his coat had been hung, a beautiful rainbow had appeared! The man was very happy and he called out to his neighbours. "Come, come! Look at this rainbow - it is so beautiful and so mysterious! Nobody knows how it got there!" The neighbours were amazed. They all looked at the rainbow, marvelling at its beauty. "Do you know what the rainbow means?" one neighbour asked. "I think it means we should share what we have in this world. If each of us is generous, we bring a little bit of beauty to the world," the man replied. The neighbours all agreed, they said they would try to be generous and kind to others. So the next time it rains, remember the rainbow and be kind. You never know what beauty it might bring.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the farm with his mom. They saw many animals like cows, pigs, and chickens. Tim also saw a tall plant with yellow things on it. His mom said, "That is corn, Tim. It is a useful food for us and the animals." Tim wanted to whistle like his dad, so he tried to whistle at the corn. A small bird heard him and came to sit on the corn. The bird said, "Hello, I am Bob. Can you help me find more corn to eat?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you." They walked and found more corn. Tim whistled, and more birds came to eat the corn. But then, a big, angry farmer saw them. He shouted, "Stop! You are taking all my corn!" The farmer chased Tim, his mom, and the birds away from the farm. Tim was sad that the farmer was mad at him. He learned that taking corn without asking is not good. Tim promised to be more careful and not take things that were not his.

Once upon a time, there was an island far away. On this island lived a boy named Jack. One day, Jack saw a big boat. He decided to spray it with water from his hose. The water was cold and it felt so good. The boat belonged to a giant. After some time, the giant came back and saw Jack playing with his boat. He used his long arms to grab Jack and took him away to an unknown place. Jack cried for help, but nobody came. He screamed for his parents, but still nobody came. Jack was never seen again and nobody knows what happened to him. That is why parents always tell their kids not to spray too much water and to be careful of giants who want to take them away.
