One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with a lot of fruits. Tim wanted to pick the fruits, but they were too high. He started to complain, "I can't reach the fruits. It's not fair." A friendly bird heard Tim and said, "Don't be sad, little boy. I can help you get the fruits." The bird flew up and picked the fruits that were available. The bird gave the fruits to Tim, and he was happy. Tim thanked the bird, and they became friends. They played together in the park all day. Tim learned that life is good when you have friends to help you.

Once upon a time, there was a boy who loved to paint. He was always so careful and made sure his artwork was perfect. One day, he wanted to try something new and decided to paint without caring how it turned out. He got out his paint and brush and started painting. He just mixed the colors and splattered them on the paper. When he finished, he looked at his work, but it was a mess. He felt sad, but he accepted it. He decided to show his painting to his mom. When she saw it, she smiled and told him she loved it. She said that it showed how creative and brave he was. The boy felt happy and proud. From that day on, the boy accepted that both careful and careless paintings were good. He was comfortable trying new things and making mistakes. He finally had the courage and confidence to paint however he liked.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy raven. He liked to sit and watch the other birds rush around. They would fly fast and play games, but the lazy raven just sat and watched. One day, the lazy raven saw a big, red ball. The other birds were rushing to get the ball, but the lazy raven just sat and watched. He thought the ball looked fun, but he was too lazy to join the game. Then, a little boy came and took the ball away. The other birds were sad, but the lazy raven had an idea. He decided to stop being lazy and rush to get the ball back. The lazy raven flew fast, grabbed the ball, and brought it back to the other birds. They all played happily, and the lazy raven learned that it was fun to rush and play with his friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, tidy sandbox. Tim was very happy and started to play in the sandbox. He built a tall tower with the sand. He said, "This is mine!" A little girl named Sue came to the sandbox. She saw Tim's tower and said, "I want to play too!" Tim was not happy. He did not want to share. He said, "No, this is mine!" Sue was sad, but she did not leave. She sat down and started to build her own tower. As they played, a big wind came and blew both towers down. Tim and Sue were very surprised. They looked at each other and then they laughed. Tim said, "We can build a new tower together!" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They built a new tower, bigger and better than before. And from that day on, they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy some food for dinner. Lily saw a long green thing called celery. Lily wanted to examine it, so she picked it up. But she was messy and dropped it on the floor. Lily's mom told her to be careful. They put the celery in the cart and went home. Lily helped her mom cook dinner. They made a big yummy soup with the celery. Lily was so excited to eat the soup. When they sat down to eat, Lily took a big bite of the soup. But she found something unexpected in her bowl. It was a toy ring! Lily was so happy and surprised. The messy celery had a secret gift inside. From that day on, Lily loved eating celery soup.
