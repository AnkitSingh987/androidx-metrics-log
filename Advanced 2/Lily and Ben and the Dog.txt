Lily and Ben were friends. They liked to play in the park near the big green building. The big green building was a university. Lily and Ben did not know what a university was, but they liked the flowers and trees around it. One day, they saw a big dog in the park. The dog was barking and running towards them. Lily and Ben were scared. They ran to the big green building and tried to find a door to go inside. "Help! Help!" Lily shouted. "There is a big dog!" But no one heard them. The big green building was empty. It was a holiday and all the people who worked and learned there were gone. The big dog caught up with them. It jumped on Lily and bit her arm. Lily screamed and cried. Ben tried to protect her. He picked up a stick and hit the dog. But the dog was stronger and angrier. It bit Ben too. Lily and Ben fell to the ground. They were bleeding and hurting. The dog was still barking and biting. No one came to help them. They were alone and afraid. The end.

Once upon a time there was a mother and her chubby little boy. His name was Jonny. One day, Jonny was playing in the park and saw another little boy. Jonny went up to him and asked if he wanted to play. The little boy agreed and the two of them started to play together. They had so much fun! But then something strange happened. They disagreed about who had the biggest toy. Jonny said it was his, but the other little boy said it was his. They argued and argued until Jonny had a question. He asked, "Why can't we both have the biggest toy?" The other little boy said, "That sounds like a good idea. I can have the big blue toy and you can have the big red one." Jonny thought this was a great solution. They laughed, hugged, and went back to playing together.

One day, a little jellyfish named Bubbles lived in the sea. He was a mighty jellyfish who could zoom very fast. Bubbles loved to play with his friends, the fish and the turtles. One day, while Bubbles was zooming around, he met a new friend, a crab named Pinchy. Pinchy was sad because he could not zoom fast like Bubbles. Bubbles wanted to help Pinchy and said, "Let's play a game. I will zoom slow and you can try to catch me." Pinchy was happy to play with Bubbles. They played the game and laughed a lot. Bubbles did not zoom too fast, so Pinchy could catch him. They played all day and became very good friends. From that day on, Bubbles and Pinchy played together every day. They had lots of fun and many more friends joined them. The sea was full of happy animals who loved to play with the mighty jellyfish who could zoom.

Once upon a time, there was a big blue sea. In the sea, there was a small boat with a sail. The boat liked to tease the fish that swam by. The fish would get jealous of the boat's sail because they could not have one. One day, the boat saw a big fish with a sail on its back. The boat was very surprised! The boat wanted to tease the big fish, but it could not. The big fish was too fast and too far away. Then, something unexpected happened. The big fish came closer, and the boat saw that it was not a fish at all! It was a boat just like him, but with a fish painted on its sail. The two boats became friends and sailed happily together in the big blue sea.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to move around and play with her friends. She lived in a small house with her family, who loved her very much. One day, Fluffy felt sick. She did not want to move or play with her friends. Her family was worried, so they took her to the doctor. The doctor looked at Fluffy and said she needed a pill to feel better. Fluffy's family gave her the pill, and she took it. After a little while, Fluffy started to feel better. She began to move and play with her friends again. Her family was happy to see her feeling better, and they all lived happily ever after.
