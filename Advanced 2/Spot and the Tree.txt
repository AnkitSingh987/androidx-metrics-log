Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a long tail that wagged when he was happy. In the same house, there was a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was envious of Spot's tail because her tail was short and not wavy. One day, Spot and Fluffy were playing in the yard. Fluffy was mad at Spot because he had a longer tail. So, she thought of a plan to punish Spot. Fluffy hid behind a tree and waited for Spot to come by. As Spot walked by the tree, Fluffy jumped out to scare him. But something unexpected happened! Instead of getting scared, Spot started to wag his tail and laugh. Fluffy was surprised and started to laugh too. They both played together and became best friends. From that day on, Fluffy was no longer envious of Spot's tail, and they never tried to punish each other again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who wanted to go swim. She put on her dress and raced out to the beach. There, the girl saw something very impressive. She saw a big wave that she really wanted to go swim with. She couldn’t wait to get in the water. But before she could, the wave got bigger and bigger. The girl was so excited that she forgot to be careful. She kept swimming closer and closer to the wave. Suddenly, the wave was too strong for her. She was pulled too deep into the water. The next thing she knew, the wave pulled her dress away and she couldn’t breathe. She was too far down and it was too late. The little girl never made it out of the water. It was a bad ending for the girl and she was never seen again.

John was very excited to go to the restaurant with his family. He loved the feeling of being in the busy place, with all the noise and light. But when he got his food, he was very surprised. His food looked and smelled sour! He frowned, not wanting to speak. His mom asked him if he liked the food, but John just shook his head. He was too embarrassed to say what he really thought. His dad was also disappointed, because he wanted John to enjoy the meal. John's mom had an idea; she suggested that John go back to the kitchen and ask them to make him something else. John agreed, and soon the chef came out with freshly cooked food. He was relieved to see it didn't smell sour. Excitedly, he spoke to the chef, thanking him for the new meal. John was so happy to finally have good food, and he shouted with joy. His parents smiled, glad that John was able to have something he enjoyed. From now on, they all decided to be more careful when they chose restaurants.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play with toy soldiers and pretend they are in a war. They have two teams: red and blue. Tom is the leader of the red team and Sam is the leader of the blue team. One day, they are playing in the backyard. They have a big box that they use as a fort. Tom says to Sam, "My team is better than your team. We have more soldiers and bigger guns. We will win the war!" Sam says, "No, my team is better than your team. We have faster cars and smarter plans. We will win the war!" They start to fight with their toy soldiers. They throw them at each other and make loud noises. They knock over the box and make a mess. Mom hears them and comes outside. She sees the toy soldiers everywhere and the box on the ground. She is angry. She says, "What are you doing? This is not how you play with your toys. This is disgusting! You are making a war, not a game. You need to improve your behavior and be nice to each other. Now, pick up your toys and say sorry to each other." Tom and Sam feel bad. They know Mom is right. They say sorry to each other and hug. They pick up their toys and put them back in the box. They decide to play something else. They say, "We don't want to make a war anymore. We want to make a peace."
