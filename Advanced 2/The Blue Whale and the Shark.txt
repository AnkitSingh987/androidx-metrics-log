Once upon a time there was a big blue whale. He was swimming in the deep blue sea. But, one day he came across a fearful shark. The shark said in a loud voice, "Hey whale, why don't you go away?" The whale wanted to ignore the shark, but he was too scared. He decided to stay and try to be calm. The shark came closer and said, "Get out of here whale, right now!" The whale tried to ignore the shark again, but he was too scared. He started to shake and cry. Suddenly, another big creature arrived. It was a seagull. The seagull said, "Why are you so scared whale? Don't be afraid. I will protect you!" The whale was so relieved and thankful. With the seagull's help, he was able to ignore the shark and swim away safely. The whale was happy to be in the safety of the deep blue sea. He learned that it is ok to be scared, but he should never ignore his feelings. The whale was grateful to the seagull for protecting him and never forgot the lesson of courage he learned that day.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy dog named Max. Max loved to dig holes in the ground. One sunny day, Max found a big iron bone. He was very happy and excited. Max wanted to keep his iron bone safe. So, he decided to bury it in the ground. He dug a deep hole with his paws and put the iron bone inside. Then, he covered it up with dirt. Max was proud of his work. Every day, Max would visit his buried iron bone. He would dig it up, play with it, and then bury it again. Max and his iron bone lived happily ever after. The end.

Jill had a sister. Her name was Kim. Jill and Kim were very different. Kim was playful and Jill always stayed inside. Jill hate when Kim played. She thought it was too noisy. One day, Kim asked Jill to play with her. Jill didn't want to. She just wanted to stay inside. But Kim said "Please play with me!". So Jill went outside. They had lots of fun! They played tag and jumped on the trampoline. Before too long, Jill was having a lot of fun too! Jill's sister was so playful! It made Jill happy to be part of the fun. From then on, she always wanted to play with Kim.

Once upon a time, there was a big armchair in a small room. The armchair was very safe and soft. A little girl named Mia loved to sit on the armchair and play with her toys. One day, Mia found a big bowl and a spoon. She thought it would be fun to mix things together. She looked around the room and found many things to mix. Mia put her toys, some dirt, and a little water in the bowl. She mixed and mixed until it was all messy. Mia was so happy with her mix, she wanted to show her mom. She carried the bowl to the armchair and tried to climb up. But the bowl was too heavy and she fell. The messy mix spilled all over the safe armchair. Mia started to cry. The armchair was not safe and clean anymore. Now, it was dirty and had a bad smell.

Once upon a time, there was a small, reliable dog named Spot. He lived in a tiny house with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to play outside in the sun. One day, while playing, Lily found a pretty pebble. She showed it to Spot and asked, "Do you like my pebble?" Spot looked at the pebble and wagged his tail. He replied, "Yes, it's very nice!" Lily and Spot decided to take the pebble home. They put it in a special box to keep it safe. Every day, they would look at the pebble and remember their fun day outside. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small, weak cat named Kitty. She loved to wear a red hat on her head. One day, she found a big piano in the park. She wanted to play it, but she was too weak to push the keys. Kitty had a friend, a strong dog named Doggy. Doggy saw Kitty was sad and asked her why. Kitty told him she wanted to play the piano, but she was too weak. Doggy had an idea. He said, "Let's play the piano together!" Kitty was happy and agreed. Kitty sat on Doggy's back while he pushed the keys. They played beautiful music together. The people in the park stopped and listened. They clapped and cheered. Kitty and Doggy were so proud. They learned that when you help your friends, you can do anything.
