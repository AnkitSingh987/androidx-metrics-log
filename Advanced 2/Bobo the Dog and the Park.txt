Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Bobo. Bobo had a red ribbon that he loved to tie around his neck. One day, Bobo heard about a big meeting in the park. All the animals were going to be there. Bobo was excited, but a little scared too. Bobo put on his red ribbon and went to the meeting. When he got there, he saw many animals. There were big animals and small animals. Bobo felt scared, but he wanted to make new friends. So, he took a deep breath and went to say "hello" to the other animals. The other animals saw Bobo's red ribbon and thought it was very pretty. They all wanted to be friends with Bobo. Soon, Bobo was not scared anymore. He played with his new friends all day long. They had so much fun at the meeting. And from that day on, Bobo was never scared to make new friends again.

One day, a little girl named Mia was at home. She felt bored and wanted to watch her favorite show on TV. So, she went to the living room and sat on the comfortable couch. Mia picked up the remote and pressed the button to start the TV. Mia's mom came into the room and saw her watching TV. She said, "Mia, did you finish your snack?" Mia nodded and said, "Yes, Mom. I ate all my fruit." Her mom smiled and said, "Good job! Now you can watch your show." Mia was happy to watch her show. She loved to sing and dance along with the songs. When the show was over, Mia turned off the TV. She felt so happy and comfortable in her home. She knew that later, she could watch another show and have more fun.

One day, a lively bunny named Ben was hopping around the garden. He loved to eat all kinds of food. But there was one thing he did not like. Ben hated celery. Ben's friend, a little bird named Lily, saw him hopping around. "Why do you look so sad, Ben?" asked Lily. "I don't like celery," said Ben. "It's not yummy like other foods." Lily thought for a moment. "Why don't you try to eat it with something else? Maybe then you will like it." Ben thought that was a good idea. He tried eating the celery with some peanut butter. To his surprise, it was yummy! Now, Ben and Lily liked to eat celery together. They found out that trying new things can be fun. And Ben was a happy bunny once again.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She had a little red ball that she liked to play with. One day, she went to the park with her friends to play with the ball. While they played, a small boy named Tim came to them. He had an opinion. He said, "I think we should play catch with the ball." So they played catch, and they had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Lily and her friends were very happy. They liked Tim's opinion and they all became good friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. One day, he saw a big hook on a tree. He thought it would be fun to kick his ball at the hook. So, he kicked the ball very hard. The ball hit the hook and it fell down. The hook hit a bird's nest on the tree. The baby birds in the nest started to cry. Tim felt very guilty. He knew he did something wrong. He said, "I'm sorry, baby birds. I didn't mean to hurt you." Tim's mom came outside and saw what happened. She told Tim, "It's important to be careful when we play. We don't want to hurt others." Tim learned his lesson. He never kicked his ball at the hook again. From that day on, he played more carefully and always thought about how his actions could affect others.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tiny. Tiny loved to run and play in the yard. One day, Tiny saw a shiny ornament hanging on a tree. The ornament was round and pretty. Tiny wanted to play with it, but the tree was too tall for him to reach. Tiny had a friend, a poor little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy lived in the tree with the shiny ornament. Tiny asked Chirpy to help him get the ornament. Chirpy was kind and wanted to help, so he flew up and brought the ornament down for Tiny. Tiny was so happy to play with the ornament, but he forgot to be gentle. He bit the ornament and it broke into pieces. Tiny and Chirpy were both sad. They learned that they should be careful with things that are not theirs. They promised to be more careful next time and to share their toys. And from that day on, Tiny and Chirpy became better friends and always took care of each other's things.
