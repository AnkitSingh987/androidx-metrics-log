Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very bored. He wanted to play, but he had no friends to play with. One day, Tim found a ladder in his yard. He thought, "I can play on the ladder!" Tim climbed the ladder and saw a bird. The bird said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to play with me?" Tim was very happy. He played with the bird all day. But then, Tim did something bad. He jumped on the ladder too hard and it broke! Tim was sad. The bird said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can fix the ladder." They worked together and fixed the ladder. Tim and the bird were friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was an attractive elephant. The elephant had a big, long nose and large ears. He lived in a big forest with many other animals. One day, the elephant wanted to mail a letter to his friend, the lion. He walked to the mail place with his letter. On the way, he met a little bird. The bird said, "Hello, Mr. Elephant! What are you doing?" The elephant said, "I am going to mail a letter to my friend, the lion." But the bird was not happy. The bird wanted the elephant to play with him. The bird said, "No, Mr. Elephant! You cannot mail the letter. You must play with me first." The elephant did not want to play. He wanted to mail his letter. So, the bird got angry and took the letter away from the elephant. The elephant tried to get his letter back, but the bird flew high up in the sky. The elephant was very sad. He could not mail his letter to his friend, the lion. The bad bird won, and the elephant went home with no letter to mail.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a lucky pan in his house. He was very happy. He wanted to make a big cake with the pan. So, he asked his mom to help him. They went to the kitchen and started to make the cake. While making the cake, Tim saw a big bag. The bag had a tear. He looked inside and saw many colorful things. His mom said, "These are for the cake." Tim was very excited. He wanted to put all the colorful things on the cake. When the cake was ready, Tim and his mom put the colorful things on it. The cake looked very nice. They shared the cake with their friends. Everyone loved the cake. Tim was happy that he found the lucky pan.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big garden with many plants. In the garden, there was a very special root. This root was intelligent and could talk. Amy liked to visit the root every day. One sunny day, Amy went to the garden and asked the root, "What will make me laugh today?" The root smiled and said, "You will find a funny surprise in your house today. But first, you need to help your mom clean up." Amy was excited and went to help her mom. After they finished cleaning, Amy found a big box in her room. She opened the box and found a funny hat inside. She put on the hat and started to laugh. Her mom came in and laughed too. The intelligent root was right, and Amy had a very happy day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was playing in her room with her toys. She was very happy. Lily saw a big bowl on the table. It was full of yummy candy. Lily wanted the candy, but she could not reach the bowl. She felt frustrated. Lily had an idea. She got a chair, stood on it, and reached for the bowl. She was so happy. But then, she saw her mom come in. Lily was scared. Her mom saw the bowl and had a surprise for Lily. Lily's mom said, "I was going to give you the candy, but you must learn to ask for help when you need it." Lily felt bad. She said sorry to her mom. Her mom gave her some candy and said, "Next time, ask for help, and don't try to do everything alone." Lily learned a good lesson that day.
