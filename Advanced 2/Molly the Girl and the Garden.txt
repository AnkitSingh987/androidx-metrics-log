Once there was a little girl named Molly. She was three years old and had lots of nice clothes. She pulled on a big dress and went off to play in the garden. In the garden was a mineral that Molly had never seen before. It was big and sparkly and she wanted to keep it. Molly picked it up and carefully put it in her pocket, but all of a sudden she heard her mum calling. Molly quickly put the mineral back and ran inside. Her mum said it was time for dinner, but Molly was really scared. She was worried her mum would know she had taken the mineral, but luckily she didn't say anything. Molly was relieved. She thanked her lucky stars, and gave the mineral a secret goodbye. She knew her mum would understand and let her go back to the garden to keep the big mineral another day.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She wore her favorite blouse with red and blue flowers. Amy saw her friend, Tom, on the high slide. She wanted to play too. But, she was scared of the high slide. Amy said, "Tom, I want to play, but I am scared of the high slide." Tom thought for a moment and said, "Let's play a guessing game. If you guess right, you can choose another game to play." Amy agreed and they started to play. Tom said, "Guess what I am thinking. It is something we can eat." Amy thought about it and said, "Is it an apple?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, you guessed right!" So, Amy and Tom played a different game together. They had lots of fun and Amy forgot all about being scared of the high slide.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a toy called Symbol. Symbol was a small, round, and colorful ball. Tom loved to play with Symbol all day. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came to play with him. But Tom was being selfish and did not want to share Symbol with Sam. Sam said, "Please, can I play with Symbol too?" Tom said, "No, it's mine!" They both got very sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A magic fairy appeared and said, "If you don't share, I will take Symbol away!" Tom was scared and thought about what the fairy said. He did not want to lose Symbol. So, he said to Sam, "I'm sorry for being selfish. Let's play together!" Sam smiled and said, "Thank you for sharing, Tom! I forgive you." They both played with Symbol and had a lot of fun. From that day on, Tom learned to share his toys and not be selfish. The magic fairy was happy and never took Symbol away. The moral value of this story is that sharing is caring, and we should not be selfish with our friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue was in her room. It was very hot. Sue did not like the hot day. She started to complain. "Mommy, it's too hot!" she said. Mommy came into Sue's room with a hanger. "Sue, let's hang up your pretty dress. It will help you feel cooler," said Mommy. Sue took the hanger and hung her dress. A little later, Sue's brother Tim came into her room. He wanted to play with her toys. Sue did not want to share. "No, Tim! These are my toys!" she said. Tim got sad and left the room. Sue felt bad for making Tim sad. She went to find him. "Tim, I'm sorry. Let's play together," she said. Tim smiled, and they played together. The hot day was not so bad when they had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Mimi. Mimi loved to play with her ball all day. She played with it for a whole year. But one day, Mimi saw a big dog and got scared. She quit playing with her ball and ran away. Mimi's mommy saw that Mimi was sad. She asked Mimi, "Why did you quit playing with your ball?" Mimi said, "A big dog scared me." Mommy told Mimi not to worry because she had a plan. The next day, Mimi and her mommy went to the park. They saw the big dog again. But this time, Mimi's mommy brought a little dog named Toto. Toto was not scared of the big dog. He played with the big dog and they became friends. Now Mimi was not scared anymore. She started playing with her ball again. Mimi, Toto, and the big dog played together every day. They had lots of fun and Mimi never quit playing with her ball again.
