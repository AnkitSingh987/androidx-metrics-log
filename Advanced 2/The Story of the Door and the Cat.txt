Once upon a time in a small house, there was a nice little girl who loved tidying her closet. Every morning, she carefully put her toy cars and stuffed animals back in their place and made sure the closet was just perfect. One day, while she was picking something off the ground and putting it away, she heard a gentle whisper coming from inside the closet. She was scared at first, but then the whispering became more friendly. Curious, the little girl slowly opened the door, and to her surprise, she saw two small animals talking to each other in the corner. One of the animals, a small white cat, said to the other one, a small brown squirrel, “Let’s meet here every day. This tidy closet is the perfect place to talk quietly.” The little girl smiled when she heard them. She couldn’t believe that she had discovered such a special secret. And from that day on, every morning she greeted the two animals before tidying her closet.

Once upon a time, there was a frog named Tim. Tim had rough skin and lived in a small pond. One day, Tim saw a big red ball near the pond. He thought it was a new friend to play with. "Hi, red friend!" said Tim. "Do you want to play with me?" But the red ball did not say anything. Tim was sad. He wanted to play with his new friend. So, he decided to push the red ball into the pond. When the red ball touched the water, something unexpected happened. The water in the pond started to increase! It got bigger and bigger. Tim was scared and jumped out of the pond. As the water stopped growing, Tim looked at the red ball and said, "You are not my friend. You made the pond too big!" But then, Tim saw that with the bigger pond, more frogs came to play. Tim was happy again and made many new friends.

One day, a big cat and a little dog went to the park. They wanted to gather flowers for a dance. The big cat was very serious about finding the best flowers. "Little dog," said the big cat, "we need the best flowers for our dance. Help me find them." The little dog wagged his tail and said, "Yes, big cat! Let's find the best flowers!" They looked and looked, and found many pretty flowers. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the flowers away. The big cat and little dog were sad. "Don't worry, big cat," said the little dog. "We can still have our dance." The big cat thought about it and said, "You're right, little dog. We don't need flowers to have fun." So, the big cat and the little dog danced and played in the park. They had so much fun, they forgot all about the flowers.

One day, a boy named Tim went with his mom to the grocery store. They needed to buy food for the house. Tim liked going to the grocery store because he could help his mom pick out the food. He felt like a big boy when he helped his mom. At the grocery store, Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam was there with his dad. Tim and Sam started to joke and play in the store. They were having fun, but they made a big mess. Food was all over the floor. Tim's mom and Sam's dad were not happy. They told the boys, "You need to tidy up this mess." Tim and Sam felt bad for making a mess. They said sorry to their mom and dad. Then, they worked together to clean up the food. When they were done, the store was tidy again. Their mom and dad were happy, and they all went home with their groceries.

There was a girl named Lily. She liked to pour water into cups. One day, she found a pretty frame. The frame had a scary face on it. Lily was not scared. She thought it was funny. Lily had an idea. She took the frame to her friend Tim. Tim did not like scary things. Lily wanted to see if Tim would be scared. She showed him the frame. Tim looked at the frame. He did not look scared. He laughed! Lily was surprised. Tim said, "I have the same frame at home!" They both laughed and played with their scary frames together.
