Once upon a time, there was a very smart bee. This bee lived in a big hive with lots of other bees. The bee was so intelligent that it knew how to unlock the hive door. One day, a little boy found the hive. He saw the smart bee and wanted to play with it. The bee did not want to play, but the boy did not listen. The intelligent bee tried to unlock the hive door to hide inside. But the little boy was too fast. He opened the hive before the bee could unlock it. All the bees flew out and they were very angry. They stung the little boy and it hurt a lot. The boy cried and ran away. The smart bee and the other bees went back into their hive, but they were sad because they did not want to hurt the little boy.

One day, a friendly dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big tree and went to play near it. Max saw some smoke coming from behind the tree. He was very curious and wanted to see what it was. Max walked to the smoke and saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was poking a small fire with a stick. Max said, "Hi, I am Max. What are you doing?" Tim replied, "Hi Max! I am making a fire to cook my food. But the fire is not big enough." Max had an idea. He ran and found a big stick. Max and Tim poked the fire together. The fire grew bigger and bigger. Suddenly, it started to rain! The rain put out the fire and made the smoke go away. Max and Tim laughed and played in the rain together. They were happy that they became friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a kind girl named Lily. She had a pretty bow in her hair. Lily liked to help people and make them happy. One day, Lily saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared. Lily wanted to help the cat. She leaned against the tree and tried to reach the cat. But the cat was too high up for her to reach. A tall man named Tom saw Lily trying to help the cat. He wanted to help too. Tom leaned against the tree and lifted Lily up. Now, Lily could reach the cat. She took the cat in her arms and Tom put her down. The cat was happy and safe. Lily and Tom were happy too, because they helped the cat together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who really liked to explore. She was brave and always eager to try new things. One day she decided to explore the woods behind her house. As she was walking, she saw a big, clear lake. She was so excited to see it that she ran over and stuck her legs in the water. But then, something behind her scared her! She quickly looked around and saw a big, scary bear! The girl was so scared that she ran as fast as she could away from the bear. She didn't even take the time to take her legs out of the lake! She ran and ran until she was safe and back at her home. The little girl was so glad that she was safe. She decided to always be extra careful exploring the forest so she wouldn't get scared like that again.

Once there was a cute little squirrel. He lived on the edge of a big forest. Everyone in the forest was obedient to the old owl. But the squirrel disagreed. He wanted to be free and explore the world. So one day he decided to leave the forest. He set out on an exciting adventure. He scampered across fields and climbed tall trees. Everywhere he went, the obedient animals stared in awe. But the squirrel was happy. He was free to do what he wanted. He soon reached the edge of another forest. He was scared to enter, but he bravely stepped forward. Suddenly, he saw an old owl that reminded him of home. The squirrel took a deep breath and stepped closer. He and the owl disagreed on some things, but they were also friends. The squirrel decided he would stay in the new forest. He realized he was both brave and obedient. He waved goodbye to the owl and scampered away. From then on, he was happy and content in his new forest home.
