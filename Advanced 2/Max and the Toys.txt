Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was a selfish dog. He did not like to share his toys or his food with any of his friends. One day, Max met a little bird named Lucy. Lucy was very hungry. At noon, Max was eating his food. Lucy asked Max, "Can you please feed me? I am very hungry." Max looked at Lucy and thought for a moment. He decided to share his food with Lucy, even though he was selfish. Max and Lucy ate together. They became good friends. From that day on, Max learned to share with his friends. He was not selfish anymore. Lucy and Max played and ate together every day, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a cute dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and act silly. One day, Spot went for a walk with his friend, a little girl named Lily. As they walked, they found a big cake on the ground. It was a pretty cake with pink and blue icing. Spot and Lily were very happy to see the cake. Spot and Lily wanted to eat the cake, but first, they decided to act like it was a birthday party. Spot wore a funny hat and Lily sang a birthday song. Then, they shared the cake and had a fun time.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful swan. The swan liked to talk to all the animals in the pond. One day, a big duck came to the pond. The duck did not like to share the pond with the swan. The swan tried to talk to the duck. But the duck did not want to talk. The duck wanted the swan to go away. The swan was sad, but it did not want to leave the pond. The other animals saw the swan was sad. They wanted to help the swan. So, they all talked to the duck. They told the duck that the pond was big enough for everyone. The duck listened and started to think. The duck decided to share the pond with the swan. The swan was happy, and they all lived together in the pond.

Once upon a time there was a little mouse who was very upset. It was always late for work, so the other animals made fun of the little mouse. One day, when the little mouse was walking to work, it saw a trap. The little mouse knew it had to be careful, so it said to itself, "I must sit still, or I'll get caught in the trap." But the little mouse was so upset about the other animals making fun of it that it started running away. Just a few steps away from the trap, it stopped and looked back. It had barely missed the trap. Very happy and relieved, it sat down to catch its breath. Then, suddenly, the little mouse heard a voice. It was the raccoon who had set the trap. "Hey little mouse," said the raccoon, "Don't be so upset. Everyone makes mistakes once in a while. Next time, be more careful and sit still near the trap." The little mouse thanked the raccoon and went on its way, feeling much better. From then on, the little mouse sat still whenever it came close to a trap. And it never again made mistakes.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big hill. Tim asked his mom, "What is that?" His mom said, "That is a volcano, Tim." Tim did not know what a volcano was, but he thought it was fun to say. They walked closer to the volcano. Tim saw a bird and said, "Hello, bird! Do you know what can happen with a volcano?" The bird looked at him and said, "I am sorry, Tim. I am ignorant. I do not know what can happen with a volcano." Tim's mom heard him talk to the bird. She said, "Tim, a volcano can get very hot and make fire. But do not worry, this volcano is safe." Tim felt happy to know that the volcano was safe. He said thank you to his mom and the bird. They went home and told dad about their adventure with the volcano.
