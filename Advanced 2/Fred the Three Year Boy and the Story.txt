Once there was a three year old boy named Fred. He was excited for his birthday because he knew he would be getting lots of presents. On the morning of his birthday, Fred's older brother, Jack, began to tease him. Jack said that Fred's presents were going to be small, but Fred knew that his mother had bought him something enormous. Fred was very angry but he didn't want to fight with his brother. He decided he would just ignore Jack and be patient. When it was time to open his presents, Fred was amazed. His mother had bought him an enormous toy truck! Jack was so jealous that he started to cry. He said to Fred, "I'm sorry I teased you. I guess it was wrong of me to be mean before your birthday." Fred was very understanding and said, "It's ok. But you should always try to be kind, even before special days like birthdays." Then he happily played with his enormous toy truck. The moral of the story is it is always better to be kind and think before you act, especially during special occasions.

Once upon a time, there was a large dog named Max. Max lived on a street with his best friend, a little cat named Luna. They loved to play together and share their toys. One sunny day, Max and Luna found a big red ball. "Let's share the ball and play!" said Max. "Yes, let's play together!" said Luna. They took turns rolling the ball down the street, laughing and having fun. As they played, more friends joined them. A bird, a squirrel, and even a frog came to play with the big red ball. They all shared and took turns, making sure everyone had fun. At the end of the day, they were all happy and tired. Max and Luna knew that sharing made their street a great place to live.

Once upon a time, there was a little cabin in the woods. In the cabin lived a girl named Lily and her loyal dog, Max. They loved to play together all day long. One day, while playing outside, they saw a big bear. The bear looked at them and said, "Go away! This is my home." Lily was scared, but Max was brave. He barked at the bear and said, "No, this is our home. We won't ignore you, but you can't make us leave." The bear was angry, but Max was strong and loyal. He protected Lily and their cabin. In the end, the bear left, and Lily and Max were safe. They went back to their cabin and played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Lily. She had a pet bunny named Hop. Lily and Hop loved to play together in their big backyard. One day, they found a garden with lots of yummy food. In the garden, there was a long green thing called celery. Lily picked it up and showed it to Hop. Hop took a bite and loved it! They both ate the celery and played some more. After playing, Hop became very dirty. He was filthy! Lily knew she had to clean him up. She took Hop to the house and gave him a bath. After the bath, Hop was all clean. Lily was so happy, she gave Hop a big kiss. They both smiled and went to sleep, ready for more fun the next day.

Once there was a little boy named Timmy. He was three years old and wanted to go to the park to play. So one day his mom said, "okay Timmy, let's go to the park!" When they got there Timmy was so excited. He ran and played with his friends. He climbed on the slide and dig in the dirt. It was such a happy day. Then it started to rain and they had to go home. On their way home it started to rain even harder. Timmy started to cry because he didn't want to leave the park. His mom said, "let's save our tears for when we really need them. Is there something we can do to make the park better for everyone?” Timmy thought and then he had an idea. He said, “Can we raise money to buy a new slide for the park? That would make all the children happy!” His mom smiled and said yes. And so Timmy and his mom started a fundraiser to buy a new slide for the park. And when it was over the park was much more fun and the children were very happy. The moral of the story is to lift each other up, and with the right effort we can make big changes together.
