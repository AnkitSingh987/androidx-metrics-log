Once upon a time there was a little boy called Bobby. Every day he would go for a walk with his mom. One day Bobby came across a friendly wise old owl. "Hello Bobby, I'm so glad to meet you," the owl said. Bobby smiled. He liked this kind old owl. "Where do you live?" asked Bobby. The owl replied, "I do not have a home like most people. I spend my days flying around looking for wisdom. I'm always trying to learn more." Bobby enjoyed listening to the owl. Before Bobby said goodbye the owl said one last thing. "Bobby, if you keep your eyes and ears open, you might find your own wisdom. Do you understand?". Bobby nodded and ran off with his Mom. From that day Bobby was always paying more attention on his walks. He looked around, listened to the birds and sniffed around for interesting smells. One day his Mom was so shocked to find that Bobby had stopped in a meadow and spotted a wise old fox. The fox had seen Bobby and invited him to come closer. They talked and even ate together. Bobby had found the wisdom he was looking for. He had become friends with the fox.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben was very hungry, so he went on a search for food. He looked high and low, but he could not find any available food. Ben saw his friend, Tommy the turtle, and asked him, "Tommy, can you help me find food?" Tommy said, "Yes, Ben! I know where to find some yummy berries." So, they went together to look for the berries. When they found the berry bush, Ben was so happy. He ordered Tommy to pick some berries for him too. They both ate the berries and were very full. From that day on, Ben and Tommy always helped each other find food.

Once there was a boy named Sam. He was very jealous because his brother had a shiny new spear. One day, Sam saw his brother playing with his spear in the garden and he asked to have a go. His brother said no, so Sam became even more jealous. "Why can't I hold it?" Sam asked. His brother smiled and said, "Because you can't lift it." Sam tried to lift the spear, but it was too heavy. He was very unhappy. Then Sam had an idea. He ran off and found some big rocks. He put them on either side of the spear so he could lift it up. But when he was lifting the spear, something terrible happened. The rocks were too heavy and the spear fell onto his foot and broke his toe. Sam was in a lot of pain and he started to cry. He was so angry and sad at the same time. His brother was sad too and he said, "Sometimes being jealous can be bad!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big dog named Max. Max was deaf, but he was a very good dog. Lily loved Max very much. One day, Lily and Max were playing in the park. Lily fell down and hurt her knee. It was very painful. Lily started to cry. Max knew that Lily was sad, so he came over and licked her knee to make her feel better. That night, Lily went to bed and said a prayer. She prayed for her knee to get better and for Max to always be happy. The next morning, Lily's knee was all better, and Max was wagging his tail. They were both very happy.

One day, a charming little dog named Spot went for a walk. He was very happy because the sun was shining and the birds were singing. Spot loved to play outside on days like this. As Spot walked, he saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad. Spot went to her and asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily looked at Spot and said, "I lost my ball. I can't find it anywhere." Spot wanted to help Lily. They looked all around for the ball. Suddenly, Spot saw the ball behind a bush. He was so happy that he wagged his tail. He said, "Lily, look! I found your ball!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Spot!" From that day on, Spot and Lily became the best of friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. And every time something good would happen, they would remember the charming day they met under the big tree.
