One morning, a little boy named Tim woke up. He felt scared because he had a bad dream. He did not want to be alone, so he went to find his mom. When he found his mom, she was in the kitchen. Tim said, "Mom, I had a bad dream and I am scared." His mom gave him a big hug and said, "It's okay, Tim. I am here for you." Tim's mom told him a story to help him remember happy things. The story was about a big, friendly bear who loved to play with all the animals in the forest. Tim started to feel better. He was not scared anymore. He knew that his mom would always be there to help him feel safe.

The purple thick mud was squishy and fun. Sally loved it. She was three years old and loved to move through the mud. She could feel the big, fat squish of the mud between her toes. She took big steps and moved through the mud. It made funny, squelchy sounds. With every move she made, the thick purple mud squelched, squeaked and squelched. And the mud was so thick she could pretend she was a mermaid, swimming through the sea! Sally had so much fun moving through the mud, she didn't want to leave. After what seemed like all day she said goodbye to the mud, and she moved away from the thick purple mud.

Once there was a light boat that sailed on a river. One day, the boat stopped because it had no more gas. The boat's owner had to decide what to do. He looked around and saw a bright sun in the sky so he decided to get out, push the boat and let the sun be his gas. He was surprised by how light his boat was when he pushed it and, as he arrived ashore, he thought about what he learned. He realized that although it is sometimes hard to use other alternative energies instead of gas, it's worth a try because it may help to save energy and make things lighter. From then on, he decided to use the sun whenever he needs to push his boat.

Once upon a time, there was a little seal who lived near the sea. The seal liked to play with his friends in the water. One day, the seal saw a big fish. The fish looked bitter and sad. The seal wanted to help the fish. So, he decided to take the fish to his friends. The seal and the fish swam together. They found the seal's friends, and they all played together. The fish started to feel happy and not bitter anymore. The seal learned that when you take the time to help others, you can make them happy. The fish also learned that having friends can make you feel better. They all played together every day, and they were never bitter again.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very happy. One day, Lily went to the park to play. She loved to skip and jump in the park. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. As Lily skipped around, she saw something shiny on the ground. It was a small box. Lily picked it up and opened it. Inside, she found a pretty treasure! It was a shiny, gold coin. Lily was very happy to find the treasure. Lily skipped all the way home to show her mom and dad the treasure she found. They were so happy and proud of her. They put the shiny gold coin in a special place in their house. From that day on, Lily always remembered her happy treasure hunt in the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. He saw a lot of sand and water. Tim loved to play in the sand, so he started to build a big sand castle. He used a bucket to raise the sand up and make it tall. As Tim was playing, a mysterious girl came over. She had a big smile and said, "Hi! My name is Lily. Can I help you with your sand castle?" Tim was happy to have a new friend and said, "Yes, please!" Tim and Lily worked together to make the sand castle even bigger. They raised the sand higher and higher. When they were done, they were very proud of their work. They played all day at the beach and had a lot of fun.
