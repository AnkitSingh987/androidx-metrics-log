Once upon a time, there was a farmer. He had a cute little dog. The dog liked to play and run. The farmer and the dog were good friends. One day, the farmer and the dog went for a walk. They looked at the big sky, the green grass, and the tall trees. The dog saw a butterfly and chased it. The farmer laughed and clapped his hands. At the end of the day, the farmer and the dog went back home. They were happy and tired. The farmer gave the dog a big hug. They both went to sleep, ready for another fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play ball with his friends. They played a game where they tried to score points. Tim always wanted to succeed in the game, but sometimes he did not. One day, Tim's friend Sam said, "I know a secret to win the game. If we spoil the other team's fun, we can score more points." Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, let's try it." During the game, Tim and Sam started to spoil the other team's fun. They laughed when the other team missed the ball. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. The other team started to play better and scored more points. Tim and Sam were sad because they did not succeed. After the game, Tim's mom said, "You should always be kind to others. If you spoil their fun, you will not succeed." Tim learned that being kind is more important than winning. From then on, he played the game with a good heart and had more fun with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tall mole named Max. Every morning, he woke up, and he was ready to go for a ride. Max loved riding in the morning, so he always asked his mom for a ride. One day, Max asked his mom if he could ride for a little longer. His mom said “Yes, if you can get back before lunch time”. Max got very excited, and he waved goodbye to his mom as he left. He kept riding and riding, even after lunch time had passed. Suddenly, Max heard a loud noise coming from a nearby tree. When he looked up, he saw a tall eagle perched on a branch. Max stopped giggling and got worried. He had heard stories about eagles stealing small animals for lunch. He gulped, and started to run home. Max finally got home, and his mom was there waiting for him. She said, “Max, I was worried about you! I'm so glad you are safe.” Max smiled, happy to be home with his mom. He learned his lesson and will never stay out late again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was an orange cat. The cat's name was Peanut. Peanut loved to play with his ball all day long. He would run, jump, and roll with his ball in the house. One day, Peanut was playing with his ball when he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was walking with her mom. Peanut wanted to play with Lily, so he took his ball and ran to her. "Stop, Peanut!" said Lily's mom. "We can't play now. We have to go home." Peanut felt sad, but he knew he had to listen. So, he stopped and watched them walk away. Later, Lily came back to play with Peanut. They had so much fun playing with the ball. They laughed and played until the sun went down. From that day on, Peanut and Lily became best friends. They played together every day, and Peanut was always happy when Lily came to visit.

Once upon a time there was a delicate little bird. She sang beautiful songs, but today she felt sad. No one wanted to listen to her. The little bird was flying through the sky when a bolt of lightning struck her. She felt strange and her feathers began to tingle. She wobbled a little and then suddenly she could sing more beautiful songs than ever before. "What a delicate sound!" said a little girl who was walking in the park. The little bird flew down, perched on the girl's shoulder, and sang her a song. The little girl smiled, and the two of them had a wonderful time. From then on, whenever the bird was feeling sad, the little girl would come and listen to her songs.
