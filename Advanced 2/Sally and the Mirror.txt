One morning, Sally woke up. She stretched out her arms and yawned. She wondered what she should do today. She got out of bed and felt around her mouth. She looked in the mirror and saw that one of her teeth was wobbly! She wiggled it back and forth. She went to brush her teeth and the toothpaste tasted dry. She brushed her teeth slowly and thought about the wobbly tooth. When she finished brushing, Sally ran to her parents' room. She showed them her tooth and said that it felt dry. Her parents smiled and said that soon the tooth would be loose enough for her to pull out! Sally went outside to play as she waited for her tooth to come out. Later, when she came back in, the wobbly tooth was gone! Sally smiled at her mom and dad, who congratulated her and gave her a reward for being so brave. Sally was very happy and excited about having a grown up tooth!

Once upon a time, there was a monkey named Momo. Momo loved to eat bananas. One day, Momo found a big, yellow banana on the ground. He was very happy. Momo picked up the banana and started to eat it. But as he ate, he started to feel dizzy. Momo did not know what was happening. He looked around and saw a little girl with a spray bottle in her hand. The little girl had sprayed something on the banana. Momo said, "Why did you spray my banana?" The little girl said, "I am sorry, Momo. I did not know it was your banana. I was just playing with my spray bottle." Momo and the little girl became friends. They played together and had fun. Momo learned to be careful with his bananas, and the little girl learned not to spray things that were not hers. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Bella. Bella was the owner of an amazing toy. The toy was a pretty doll and it was safe. One day, Bella wanted to show her doll to her best friend Sophie. So, she sent the doll away in the mail. She was so excited for Sophie to receive the package! Sophie was very excited when the package arrived. She happily opened the box, and was delighted to find the safe doll inside. She thanked Bella for sending it to her. Bella was very happy that her friend had received the package safely. From then on, the two girls would often play with the doll together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy a fish. Tim was very excited. He looked at all the fish that were available. There were big fish and small fish, red fish and blue fish. Tim's mom said, "Pick one fish you like." Tim picked a small, green fish. He named it Bubbles. They took Bubbles home and put it in a fishbowl. Tim looked at Bubbles and said, "Hi, Bubbles! You are my new friend." Bubbles did not say anything back, but Tim knew Bubbles was happy. One day, Tim went to feed Bubbles. But Bubbles was not in the fishbowl! Tim was sad and looked everywhere for Bubbles. Then, he heard a small voice say, "Hi, Tim!" Tim looked down and saw Bubbles on the floor. Bubbles could talk! Tim was very surprised. He picked up Bubbles and put it back in the fishbowl. They were best friends forever.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a mint plant in her garden. She was very happy and wanted to share it with her friends. She was very careful when she picked the mint leaves. Mia put the mint leaves in a bag and went to find her friends. Mia found her friend Tom playing with his toy cars. "Tom, look what I found!" said Mia. "I have mint leaves from my garden. Let's share them with our friends!" Tom was excited and agreed to help Mia. But when they went to find their friends, they saw a big dog in their way. The dog wanted the mint leaves too! Mia and Tom were scared. Mia had an idea. "Let's change our path and go around the dog," she whispered to Tom. They were very careful and walked around the dog. The dog did not see them. Mia and Tom found their friends and shared the mint leaves with them. Everyone was happy and thanked Mia for her careful thinking.
