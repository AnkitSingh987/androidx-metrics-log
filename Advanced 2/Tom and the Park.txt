One day, Tom and his dog, Spot, went to play in the park. They found a big box that had a picture of a rocket on it. Tom was very excited. He wanted to play with the rocket, but he was scared that someone would take it away from him. So, he decided to bury it in the sand. The next day, Tom and Spot went back to the park. They saw that the sand was moved, and the rocket was gone. Tom was very sad, and Spot barked loudly. They looked around the park, but they could not find the rocket. Then, Tom saw a little girl playing with a rocket just like the one he buried. He went to her and told her that it was his rocket. The little girl was sorry and gave it back to Tom. He was very happy to have his rocket back. From that day on, Tom and the little girl became good friends and played with the rocket together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some food because they were hungry. Tim saw a big black dog outside the store. The dog looked hungry too. Tim told his mom, "Mom, the dog is hungry. Can we give it some food?" His mom said, "Okay, let's buy some dog food for the black dog." They went inside the store and got some food for themselves and the dog. Tim was excited to help the hungry dog. After they paid for the food, they went outside to give the dog some food. But the black dog was not there anymore! Tim was sad, but then he saw the dog walking with a man. The man said, "Thank you for finding my dog! I was looking for him everywhere." Tim was happy that the dog had a home and was not hungry anymore.

One day, a busy bee named Buzz was flying around. He saw his friend, Froggy the frog, sitting by a wet pond. Buzz flew to Froggy and said, "Hi Froggy! Do you want to play with me?" Froggy looked at Buzz and said, "I miss playing with you, but I am too wet right now. I just jumped out of the pond. Let me dry off first, then we can play." Buzz waited for Froggy to dry. They talked and laughed while Froggy got dry. When Froggy was all dry, they played fun games together. They were very happy to be with each other again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was scared to fly. All his friends could fly high and see the pretty view from the sky. Tim felt ashamed because he could not fly like them. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, you can fly too. Just try and you will see the nice view." Tim was scared, but he wanted to try. He flapped his wings and started to fly a little bit. Tim kept trying, and soon he was flying high in the sky. He saw the beautiful view of the trees and the river. Tim was not ashamed anymore. He was happy because he could fly and see the pretty view like his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow cat named Sunny. Sunny had a label on her collar that said her name. She lived in a big house with her friend, a little girl named Lisa. They loved to play together every day. One day, Sunny started to suffer from a bad tummy ache. She cried and cried, and Lisa heard her. Lisa asked, "Sunny, what's wrong?" Sunny could not tell her, but she kept crying. Lisa knew she had to help her friend. Lisa took Sunny to a nice man who could make her feel better. The man looked at the label on Sunny's collar and said, "Hello, Sunny. I will help you." He gave Sunny some medicine, and soon she was feeling much better. Lisa and Sunny went back home, happy to play together again.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there was a kind man named Sam. Sam liked to supply food to his friends. Today, Sam was going to visit his best friend, Tom. Sam walked to Tom's house with a big bag of yummy treats. He knocked on the door and said, "Tom, I have a surprise for you today!" Tom opened the door and smiled. "Wow, Sam! Thank you for the treats!" Together, Sam and Tom sat down to eat. They laughed and talked, enjoying their time together. The ordinary day became special because of their friendship and the yummy food Sam supplied. And they lived happily ever after.
