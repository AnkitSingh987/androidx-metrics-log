Once upon a time there was a little boy named Joe. Joe was very curious and loved exploring the world around him. One day he was walking in the park when he saw a shiny new box. Joe was so curious about what was inside the box so he opened it. Inside were two yummy pastries! Joe was so excited to try the new treats. He quickly grabbed one and took a big bite. It was so delicious that he wanted to have the other one too. So Joe took the other pastry and carefully wrapped it in a napkin. He kept it safe in his pocket and went off to explore the rest of the park. Joe had a wonderful day, with a yummy treat in his pocket for later.

Once there was a little girl named Emily. She had a toy car and loved to play with it. One day, her mom told her that she had to remove it and clean it up. Emily didn't want to remove it but she had to because her mom said so. She cleaned it up and put it away. When she went back to play again, she saw that there was a spark coming from the car. Emily thought it was exciting and wanted to show her mom. She ran to get her mom but her mom wasn't happy. She was mad and said to Emily in a very rude voice, "You didn't clean up the car properly, and now it's broken!" Emily was sad. She tried to fix the car but she couldn't. In the end, the toy car was broken and Emily couldn't play with it anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big, bossy cat named Max. Max liked to play with a loop that he found in the house. One day, Max saw a new little dog in the yard. Max wanted to introduce the dog to his loop. Max took the loop to the little dog. The dog was happy and wagged his tail. Max and the dog played with the loop all day. They ran and jumped and had fun with the loop. At the end of the day, Max was not bossy anymore. He and the little dog were friends. They shared the loop and played together every day. They were happy and always had fun.

One day, a little dancer named Lily wanted to improve her dance. She practiced every day in her room. She liked to spin and jump. Lily was a very good dancer, but she wanted to be the best. One day, while Lily was dancing, a harmless cat named Tom came into her room. Tom wanted to play with Lily, but Lily was too busy dancing. Tom felt sad because he wanted to have fun with his friend. He said, "Lily, can we play together?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "Tom, I need to improve my dance, but I don't want you to be sad. How about we dance together?" Tom agreed, and they started to dance. They had lots of fun, and Lily learned that she could improve her dance while having fun with her friend.

One day, a mommy and her three-year-old daughter were walking through the park and heard a jolly sound coming from a gigantic tree. Mommy smiled. "That's music, my love!" They squeezed between two branches and saw a man, polishing his guitar. "What are you doing, sir?" the little girl asked. "Why, I'm making it shine," he said. He winked at the girl and said, "Just like your eyes!" He then showed her how the thick cloth felt smooth against the guitar's wood. He wiped each string and polished all the spots that needed it. Mommy and daughter clapped as the man sang and played. They both watched, amazed at how the guitar shone and glimmered. The little girl thanked the man and they happily skipped away as he continued to play.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Max. Max liked to play with his ball and help his friends. One day, Max was playing with his ball when he heard a tiny voice. "Help, help!" said the little voice. Max looked around and saw a small bird on the ground. The bird had a hurt wing and could not fly. Max wanted to help the bird, so he gently picked it up with his mouth and carried it to his owner, a kind girl named Lily. Lily saw the bird and realized it needed help. She took the bird inside and put a bandage on its wing. Max sat in silence, watching Lily help the bird. After a few days, the bird's wing was all better. "Thank you, Max and Lily!" said the bird. "You were so helpful!" Max and Lily smiled, happy to have helped their new friend. From that day on, Max, Lily, and the bird were the best of friends, playing and helping each other whenever they could.
