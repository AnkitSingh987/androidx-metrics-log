Lily loved to sing. She sang all the time, even when she was not supposed to. She sang in the car, in the bath, in the bed, and in the kitchen. She sang loud and happy songs that made her feel good. One day, Lily saw a shiny ornament on the tree. It was red and round and had a star on it. She wanted to touch it and see how it felt. She reached for it with her hand, but it was too high. She got a chair and climbed on it. She stretched her arm and grabbed the ornament. But the ornament was not what she thought. It was not shiny and round and starry. It was sticky and squishy and smelly. It was a rotten apple that someone had put on the tree as a joke. Lily screamed and dropped the apple. It splattered on the floor and made a mess. Lily felt disgusting. She ran to the sink and washed her hand. She cried and cried. She did not want to sing anymore. She wanted to forget the bad apple. Mommy heard Lily and came to see what was wrong. She saw the apple and the chair and the ornament. She understood what had happened. She hugged Lily and wiped her tears. She told her it was not her fault. She told her it was a silly prank. She told her she was brave and smart and sweet. Lily felt better. She smiled and hugged Mommy back. She thanked her for being nice. She asked her if she could sing a song. Mommy said yes. She said she loved to hear her sing. She said she had a beautiful voice. Lily sang a song. She sang a song about Mommy and love and happiness. She sang loud and happy songs that made her feel good. She forgot the bad apple. She remembered the shiny ornament. She was glad she had Mommy. She was glad she could sing.

Once there was a brave girl called Amy. She wanted to go on an adventure. One day, Amy heard a voice. "Join me and explore," it said. Amy was excited to explore and see new sights. Amy looked around but couldn't see where the voice was coming from. Suddenly she spotted a small bird, flapping its wings. "Who are you?" asked Amy. "I'm a river raven," said the bird. "Let's join together and explore." Amy was brave. She nodded and hopped on the bird's back. They flew and flew until they saw a beautiful sight. It was a lake with many different types of birds, fish and plants. Amy was amazed. She had never seen anything like it before. She thanked the river raven for taking her on this journey. Amy had had a wonderful adventure and she couldn't wait to join the river raven for another one.

Once upon a time, there was a pink robot named Rosie. Rosie lived in a big toy box with other toys. She had many friends, but her best friend was a little boy named Timmy. Timmy and Rosie liked to play games and have fun. One day, while they were playing, Rosie's arm fell off. Timmy looked at Rosie and said, "Oh no, Rosie! Your arm!" Rosie started to panic. She was scared and didn't know what to do. Timmy was also scared, but he knew he had to help his friend. Timmy looked around the room and found a toy tool box. He picked up a toy screwdriver and fixed Rosie's arm. Rosie was so happy she could play again. She said, "Thank you, Timmy! You fixed me!" They played happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a big stadium with his family. The stadium was full of people. They were all there to see a fun game. Tim was so excited to be there. He knew something special would happen today. In the game, there were two teams. One team had a tough player named Max. Everyone liked Max because he played very well. Tim liked Max too and hoped to see him do something great. Max looked at Tim and said, "Watch me, I will do my best!" The game started, and everyone was happy. They clapped for their favorite players. Tim clapped very loud for Max. Then, Max did something amazing! He scored the winning point for his team. Everyone in the stadium clapped and cheered. Tim was so proud of Max and knew that he would never forget this special day at the stadium.
