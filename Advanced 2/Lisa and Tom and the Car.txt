Lisa and Tom were playing with their toy cars in the park. They liked to make them zoom and crash and honk. They had a lot of fun, but they also made a lot of noise. A cop was walking by the park. He heard the noise and came to see what was going on. He saw Lisa and Tom and their toy cars. He smiled and said hello. "Hi, cop!" Lisa and Tom said. They liked cops. They wore blue and had badges and helped people. The cop asked them if they wanted to see his car. Lisa and Tom nodded excitedly. They followed him to his car. It was big and shiny and had lights and a siren. The cop opened the door and let them sit inside. He showed them the buttons and the radio and the steering wheel. He let them turn on the lights and the siren. Lisa and Tom laughed and clapped. They felt like real cops. The cop told them to be careful with his car. He said it was delicate and needed to be treated well. He said he used it to do his job and help people. Lisa and Tom nodded. They understood. They thanked the cop for letting them see his car. They said they liked him and his car. They said they wanted to be cops when they grew up. The cop smiled and hugged them. He said they were very sweet and polite. He said he was glad to meet them. He said he hoped they would keep playing and having fun. Lisa and Tom got out of the car. They waved goodbye to the cop. They went back to their toy cars. They played some more, but they were quieter and gentler. They remembered the cop and his delicate car. They felt happy and proud.

Molly was restless. She wanted to explore the earth. She said to her mommy, "Please, let me explore the earth!" Her mom shook her head and said, "No, Molly. It's not safe to go outside right now." Molly was disappointed. She begged, "Please, mommy! I just want to explore the earth a little. I promise I won't go far." But her mom gently said, "No, Molly. Maybe another day." Molly was very sad. She felt like she couldn't explore the earth and have any fun. She asked her mom, "When can I go explore the earth?" Her mom paused, then said, "Let's think of another way for you to explore the earth without leaving the house. That way, your safety is always my top priority." Molly smiled and hugged her mom. She was so happy to have a way to explore the earth without leaving the house. Together, Molly and her mom explored the earth from their home. Molly was delighted with what she learned, and couldn't wait to explore the earth even more.

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Tom. Tom was always busy and loved working on his saw. Every day, he heard the saw make funny noises as he worked on his projects. One day, Tom heard a different noise. He stopped working and looked around. He heard a buzzing sound and it was getting louder! Soon Tom saw a bus coming around the corner. It was very busy and the driver was busy waving at Tom. Tom waved back and the bus drove away. He was happy that he had seen the bus today. From now on he could keep a lookout to see more buses!

Once upon a time there was a girl named Katie. She was three years old and loved to explore. One day, she saw a big desk in her bedroom. It made her so excited! Katie ran to the desk and began to explore. Everything was so shiny and light! Katie ran her fingers along the surface of the desk and looked around the room. She spotted a big, light button on the side of the desk and pushed it. Katie was so surprised when the top of the desk opened! She ran over to the open desk and looked inside. Suddenly, there was a bright light and music began to play. Katie was so excited by the surprise that she began to laugh and dance. Every day from then on, Katie would play with the desk and explore what else might come out of it.
