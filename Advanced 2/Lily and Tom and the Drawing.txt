Lily and Tom were twins. They liked to play with toys and books and crayons. But today it was raining and they could not go outside. They felt bored. "Mom, we have nothing to do!" Lily said. "Mom, can we watch TV?" Tom said. Mom was busy in the kitchen. She said, "No, TV is not good for you. Why don't you seat on the floor and draw something? I have some paper and pencils in the drawer." Lily and Tom went to the drawer and took out the paper and pencils. They seat on the floor and started to draw. Lily drew a flower. Tom drew a car. They showed each other their drawings and smiled. "Look, Mom, I drew a flower!" Lily said. "Look, Mom, I drew a car!" Tom said. Mom came to see their drawings. She said, "Wow, you are very good at drawing! I am proud of you. You see, you can have fun even when it is raining. You just need to use your imagination and creativity." Lily and Tom hugged Mom. They said, "Thank you, Mom. You are right. Drawing is fun. We are not bored anymore." Moral: Drawing is a fun and creative activity that can help you overcome boredom.

One day, a big cat named Tom found a magic ring. The ring had a spark in it. When Tom put the ring on, he could shrink. Tom was very happy. He played a game with his friend, a small mouse named Jerry. Tom said, "Look, Jerry! I can be small like you!" Jerry laughed and said, "That's fun, Tom!" Tom was careless with the ring. He did not know that the ring could only shrink him three times. He used it two times already. He wanted to show his mom, so he used it one more time. But this time, he did not grow back. Tom was very sad. Jerry tried to help Tom find a way to grow back. They looked for a magic ring that could make Tom big again. But they could not find one. Tom was small forever. His mom was sad, and Jerry was sad too. Tom learned that he should not be careless with magic things.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a gloomy room. In this room, there was a radio. The radio was old and did not work well. One day, a little girl named Amy walked into the room. She saw the radio and said, "I want to listen to music!" Amy tried to push the button on the radio, but it did not work. She pushed it again and again, but still, no music came out. Amy's big brother, Tom, heard her trying to play the radio. He came into the room and said, "Amy, that radio is broken. Let's go play outside." But Amy did not want to give up. She pushed the button one more time. Suddenly, the radio fell off the table and broke into pieces. Now, there was no music and the radio was gone forever.

John went to the park with his family. His mom gave him a tray to play with. He spun around and around with it. He was so successful that he made other children at the park join him. All the friends spun around in a circle with their trays and it was really fun. Everyone was happy and John was the most successful spinner! He kept spinning and spinning until it was time to go home. Everyone said goodbye and wished each other a good day. John went home with a big smile on his face, thinking about how much fun he had spinning with his tray.

Lily and Ben liked to collect sunflowers. They walked in the field and picked the big yellow flowers. They put them in a basket and smiled. "Look, Ben, we have so many sunflowers!" Lily said. "They are so pretty and bright!" "Yes, Lily, they are!" Ben said. "We are rich with sunflowers!" They wanted to show their sunflowers to their mom and dad. They ran to their house with the basket. But when they got there, they saw a big fire. The fire was burning their house and their mom and dad. "Mom! Dad!" Lily and Ben cried. They dropped the basket and ran to the fire. But it was too hot and too late. They could not save their mom and dad. The fire ate their house and their sunflowers. Lily and Ben were left alone and sad. They had no home, no family, and no sunflowers. They were not rich anymore. They were poor and lonely.
