Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She loved to eat jelly. One day, her mommy made a big jar of yummy jelly. Sally was very happy. She wanted to share the jelly with her friends at school. Her mommy said, "That's a fair idea!" The next day, Sally woke up late. She was in a hurry to go to school. She forgot to take the jelly with her. When she got to school, her friends were sad. They were looking forward to the jelly. Sally felt bad. She knew she had to make it right. After school, Sally went home and told her mommy what happened. Her mommy helped her make more jelly. The next day, Sally remembered to take the jelly to school. Her friends were so happy. They all enjoyed the jelly together. Sally learned that it's important not to forget things, especially when it comes to sharing with friends.

Once upon a time, there was a furry bunny. The bunny loved to hop all day long. One day, the bunny met a clown. The clown was big and funny. He had a red nose and big shoes. The furry bunny said, "Hi, clown! I like to hop. Do you like to hop too?" The clown laughed and said, "Yes, I like to hop! Let's hop together!" So, the furry bunny and the clown hopped up and down. They hopped in the park, they hopped by the pond, and they hopped through the town. Everyone saw them and smiled. The furry bunny and the clown were very happy. They hopped all day long and became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house with big windows, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to look out of her modern window and watch the birds come and go. She would wave to them and smile as they flew by. One sunny day, as Lily was looking out her window, she saw a little bird with a bright red chest. The bird came close to the window and looked at Lily. Lily waved and said, "Hello, little bird! Come and play with me!" The bird seemed to understand and flew away, only to come back with some of its friends. Lily was so happy to have new friends come to her window. She would sing and dance with the birds every day. They would come back to visit her and share their songs. And every time Lily looked out her modern window, she knew her friends would always come back to play.

One day, a big, powerful lion named Leo was walking in the jungle. He saw a little bird, named Bree, stuck in a tree. Bree was very sad and scared. Leo wanted to help Bree, so he said, "Don't worry, Bree. I will help you get out." Leo tried to reach Bree with his big paws, but the tree was too tall. Then, Bree had an idea. She mentioned, "Maybe you can shake the tree, and I can fly out." Leo thought it was a good idea. He shook the tree with all his strength, and Bree was free! Bree was so happy that she wanted to give Leo a reward. She said, "Thank you, Leo! I want to give you a special reward." Bree flew away and came back with a beautiful, shiny stone. Leo took the stone, and something unexpected happened. He turned into a colorful, powerful bird! Now, Leo and Bree could fly together and be friends forever.

One day, a brown dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a flower by the road. Max thought it was pretty. He wanted to give it to his friend, a little girl named Amy. Max walked to Amy's house and said, "Amy, I want to lend you this flower. It is for you." Amy smiled and said, "Thank you, Max. I love it!" She put the flower in a cup with water. The next day, Max came to see Amy and the flower. But the flower was not there. Max was sad. He asked, "Amy, where is the flower I gave you?" Amy said, "Max, something funny happened. The flower turned into a butterfly and flew away!" Max and Amy laughed. They were happy because they saw the pretty butterfly fly. They knew it was once their flower.
