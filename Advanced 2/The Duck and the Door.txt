Once there was a duck who wanted to play. She was so eager to find something fun to do so she decided to explore the big structure by the river. She waddled around, looking high and low but found nothing. Just when she was about to give up, she noticed a small door tucked away in the corner. Interested, the duck decided to take a short peek through the door. What she found amazed her. Inside was an incredible playground with all sorts of fun things to explore. The duck was so excited that she quickly jumped inside. But as soon as she did, she heard a loud click. She had been locked in! The duck started to panic until she saw a key hanging on one of the walls in the playground. She flew over and grabbed the key, unlocking the door so she could escape. The duck was so relieved and happy that she quickly ran back to the playground so she could start having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty orange. It was the biggest orange in the world! All the animals wanted to go and visit the orange. So all the animals went together on a journey. First, they had to cross a big river. The orangutan was brave and led the way, but it was too hard. Then they heard a voice. It was a mighty bird. The bird said it could help them get across the river! The bird flew closer and closer and opened its wings. All the animals climbed on, and the bird flew them across the river to the mighty orange. They all cheered when they reached the mighty orange. They had never seen anything so big and so bright before! They hugged and thanked the bird, then they all went back home. The end.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play in the garden. They have a hose that sprays water. They also have a big box of clothes. They like to wear different clothes and pretend to be someone else. One day, Sara finds a dress in the box. It is pink and shiny. It has many beads and flowers. Sara thinks it is very pretty. She wants to wear it. She puts it on and looks at herself in the mirror. She feels like a princess. "Look, Ben, look at me!" she says. "I am a princess. Do you like my dress?" Ben looks at Sara. He sees the dress. He thinks it is very expensive. He remembers that it belongs to their mom. Their mom only wears it for special occasions. She told them not to touch it. "Uh-oh, Sara, that is mom's dress!" he says. "You should not wear it. It is expensive. You might get it dirty or tear it. Mom will be angry." Sara does not listen to Ben. She likes the dress too much. She wants to play with it. She runs outside with the dress. She sees the hose. She has an idea. She thinks it will be fun to spray water on the dress. She thinks it will make it sparkle more. "Come on, Ben, come and play with me!" she says. "Let's spray water on the dress. It will be fun." Ben follows Sara outside. He sees her holding the hose. He feels scared. He knows that water will ruin the dress. He tries to stop her. "No, Sara, no, don't do that!" he says. "You will spoil the dress. It will get wet and stained. Mom will be very angry. Please, take it off. Put it back in the box." But Sara does not listen to Ben. She turns on the hose. She sprays water on the dress. She laughs and twirls. She thinks it is fun. She does not see the dress getting wet and stained. She does not hear Ben shouting. She does not notice mom coming home. She only sees the water and the dress. The end.

John walked into the shop. He had come to buy something special. He wiped his sweaty hands and looked around. He had saved up a lot of money. He wanted to buy something he’d never forget. Suddenly, he saw something in the corner. It was a shiny new bike! John couldn't stop smiling. He wiped his eyes and paid for the bike. He was so grateful! He said goodbye to the shopkeeper and walked outside. John couldn't wait to show his new bike to his friends. He got on and started to pedal away. Suddenly, he stopped. In front of him, John saw a beautiful pond. He had always wanted to see it. John was so surprised. He had no idea that the pond was so close by! He was so grateful that he had bought the bike — now he could explore places he’d never been before. He wiped his eyes again and started on his way.
