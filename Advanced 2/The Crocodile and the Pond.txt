Once upon a time, there was a big crocodile who lived in a hidden pond. The crocodile was very lonely because no one came to visit him. He wanted to make friends, but he didn't know how to speak to them. One day, a little bird flew near the pond and saw the crocodile. The bird said, "Hi, Mr. Crocodile! I didn't know you lived here. Do you want to be friends?" The crocodile was so happy that someone talked to him. He tried to speak, but all that came out was a big, loud roar. The little bird was scared and flew away. The crocodile was very sad that he scared away his new friend. But then, something unexpected happened. A wise old turtle came out from the hidden pond and said, "Don't worry, Mr. Crocodile. I can teach you how to speak softly and make friends." The crocodile was very excited and listened to the turtle. With the turtle's help, the crocodile learned to speak softly and made many friends in the forest. From that day on, the crocodile was never lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had long hair. She had a comb to brush her hair. One day, Sue could not find her comb. She was sad because she did not know where it was. She missed her comb. Sue asked her mom for help. Her mom did not know where the comb was, but she said, "Maybe it is in your room." Sue did not know what her mom meant. Sue was ignorant about what her mom was trying to tell her. Sue went to her room and looked for her comb. She found it under her bed. She was happy and said, "I found it!" Sue brushed her hair with her comb. She learned that sometimes things are hiding in her room. From that day on, Sue always looked in her room when she missed something.

One day, a fast cat named Tom went to the kitchen. He saw a stove and jumped on it. Tom felt very warm on the stove. He looked around and saw a big cake. Tom wanted to eat the cake, but he knew it was not his. Tom's friend, a small mouse named Jerry, came into the kitchen. He saw Tom near the cake. Jerry said, "Tom, do not eat the cake! It will vanish, and then we will be in trouble." Tom did not want to be in trouble, so he listened to Jerry. Later, a big party happened in the house. Tom and Jerry hid and watched from a safe place. At the party, everyone ate the cake until it was all gone. Tom and Jerry smiled at each other, happy that they did not make the cake vanish. They knew they did the right thing.

One day, a nice cat named Kitty wandered outside. She saw snow on the ground. Kitty liked to play in the snow. She jumped and rolled in the cold, white snow. She felt happy. Kitty saw a little bird on a tree. The bird was cold. Kitty said, "Hi bird, do you want to play in the snow with me?" The bird said, "Yes, Kitty, I want to play too!" Kitty and the bird played in the snow together. They had fun making snowballs and running around. After playing, they went inside to get warm. Kitty and the bird became good friends. They liked to wander and play together every day.

One day, a little cat named Kitty saw a big sign in the park. The sign had a picture of a lost dog on it. Kitty felt sad for the lost dog and wanted to help. She asked her friend, Birdy, if he had seen the dog. "I saw the dog near the big tree," Birdy replied. Kitty and Birdy went to the big tree to look for the dog. They saw the dog looking helpless under the tree. The dog was scared and cold. Kitty and Birdy wanted to help the dog find its home. Kitty and Birdy asked their friends for help. Soon, many animals came to help the dog. They all worked together to find the dog's home. At last, they found the dog's family. The dog and the family were so happy to be together again. The animals felt good for helping the dog. They learned that when they work together, they can do big things. The moral of the story is that helping others can make you feel happy and good inside.
