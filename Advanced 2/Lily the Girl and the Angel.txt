One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She was very bored. "This is a boring day," she said. She looked around her room and saw her water spray bottle. She had an idea. Lily took the spray bottle and went outside. She saw a butterfly and tried to spray it. The butterfly flew away, but something amazing happened. Where the water touched the ground, an angel appeared. The angel was very pretty and had big wings. "Hello, Lily," said the angel. "I am here to make your day fun." Lily and the angel played all day. They played games and had a lot of fun. The angel showed Lily how to make pretty pictures in the sky with the spray bottle. At the end of the day, the angel said, "I have to go now, but I will come back if you need me." Lily was sad, but she knew the angel would come back when she was bored again. And from that day on, Lily was never bored.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a thin blanket. The blanket had a nice texture. It was soft and warm. Lucy loved the blanket very much. She took it everywhere she went. One day, while Lucy was playing with her friends, she saw a sad boy named Tim. Tim was crying because he was cold. Lucy wanted to comfort Tim. She said, "Don't cry, Tim. I have a nice blanket we can share." Lucy and Tim sat down and shared the thin blanket. They felt warm and happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The blanket started to grow! It became bigger and bigger until it was big enough for all their friends to share. Lucy, Tim, and all their friends sat under the big blanket. They were all warm and happy. They thanked Lucy for sharing her special blanket with them. And from that day on, they all played together and shared the big, warm blanket.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tim. Tim lived near a big river. One day, Tim saw a raft by the river. The raft was small and made of wood. Tim wanted to play on the raft, so he jumped on it. As Tim played on the raft, it started to move. The raft went down the river very fast. Tim was scared and wanted to escape. He meowed loudly for help. A kind boy heard Tim's meow and saw him on the raft. The boy got a long stick and reached out to Tim. Tim held on to the stick and the boy pulled him back to the land. Tim was happy to escape the raft and thanked the kind boy. From that day on, Tim and the boy became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Spot. He always behaved very well. One day, Spot went to a big party. It was a marriage party for his friends, Tom the cat and Sally the squirrel. At the party, Spot saw a big cake. He wanted to eat it, but he knew he had to behave. He said to himself, "I will wait until it is time to eat the cake." Tom and Sally were very happy at their marriage. They said "I do" and everyone clapped. Then, it was time to eat the cake. Spot was so excited! Spot waited his turn and got a big piece of cake. He said, "Thank you for the cake, Tom and Sally." They smiled and said, "You're welcome, Spot. You are a very clever dog for behaving so well at our marriage." Everyone had a great time at the party, and Spot was happy he behaved well. He knew that being clever and behaving made his friends happy too.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a long line of kids waiting to play on the slide. Lily wanted to play too, but the line was too long. She felt sad and did not know what to do. Lily saw a boy named Tom standing near the line. She walked over to him and asked, "Why is the line so long?" Tom said, "A big kid named Max is not letting anyone play until he goes on the slide ten times." Lily felt mad, but she had an idea. Lily walked up to Max with a mild smile on her face. She said, "Max, can you please let us all play on the slide too?" Max saw Lily's smile and felt bad. He said, "Okay, I will let you all play." The long line of kids cheered and smiled. Now, everyone could play on the slide and have fun.
