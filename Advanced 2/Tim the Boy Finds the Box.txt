Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw pictures. He had a big box of colors and a lot of paper. Every day, he would sit and draw pictures of things he liked. He drew pictures of his mom, his dad, his dog, and even the sun and the moon. One day, Tim decided to draw a deep hole. He started at the top of the paper and drew a line all the way down to the bottom. Then, he made the hole look very deep by adding more lines. He was very proud of his deep hole picture. Tim showed his deep hole picture to his mom and dad. They liked it too. They hung the picture on the wall for everyone to see. Tim was so happy. And that is where the story ends. Tim kept drawing more pictures and his deep hole picture stayed on the wall for a long time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy pistol. He would run fast with it and pretend to be a hero. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play with his pistol. While Tim was playing, he saw a big rock. Tim thought it would be fun to hide behind the rock and jump out fast to surprise his friends. So, he hid behind the rock and waited for his friends to come. As Tim waited, something unexpected happened. The big rock started to move! It was not a rock at all, but a giant turtle! The turtle was very friendly and wanted to play too. Tim and the turtle played together all day, running fast and playing with the toy pistol. They became best friends and had lots of fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a big sunflower named Sunny. Sunny lived in a garden with many friends. Sunny was an obedient sunflower who liked to help others. One day, Sunny's friend, a little girl named Lily, wanted to bake a cake. She asked Sunny, "Can you help me bake a cake?" Sunny said, "Yes, I can help you!" So, they went to the kitchen to bake the cake together. They mixed the cake with eggs and flour. Then, they put it in the oven to bake. When the cake was ready, they took it out and shared it with all their friends in the garden. Everyone was happy and thanked Sunny and Lily for the yummy cake.

Once upon a time, there was a seal who lived near the sea. The seal loved to play with his friends. They would throw a big ball to each other and have lots of fun. One day, the seal found a delicious fish. He was so happy and wanted to share it with his friends. So, he called them over to see the fish. They all thought it looked very yummy. Together, they played a game. They would throw the fish to each other, just like the ball. After the game, they all sat down and ate the delicious fish. They were full and happy. From that day on, the seal and his friends always shared their food and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot lived in a house with a big fence. One day, Spot saw a cat outside the fence. The cat was very glad and was playing with a ball. Spot said, "Hi, cat! Can I play with you?" The cat said, "Yes, Spot! But you have to come out of the fence." Spot tried to get out, but the fence was too high. Spot was sad. Then, a big bird came by and saw Spot. The bird said, "I can help you, Spot!" The bird picked up Spot and carried him over the fence. Spot was so happy to be with the cat. They played with the ball all day. Spot was glad to accept help from his new friend, the bird.

Once there was a girl in the sea who wanted to find a dolphin. She had wanted one for a very long time. Every day she went out and shouted, "Dolphin! Where are you?" But she never heard an answer. One day, when she was out searching for a dolphin, she heard a mysterious noise. It was loud, like a shout. She followed the sound and it got louder and louder until she saw something amazing. It was a beautiful dolphin! The girl was so excited. She shouted with delight. The dolphin saw her and started to swim closer. It was so happy to see her. They played together in the sea all day long. The girl was very happy to finally have her dolphins.
