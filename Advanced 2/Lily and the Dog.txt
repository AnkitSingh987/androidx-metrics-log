One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. The park was big and open. Lily saw green grass and pretty flowers. She wanted to perform a dance for her mom. Lily danced on the grass. She was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog ran up to her. The dog wanted to play. Lily was scared at first, but then she had an idea. Lily started to dance again. The big dog danced too! They both danced on the grass. Lily's mom watched and laughed. The big dog was not so scary after all. They all had fun at the park.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw her friend Tom playing with a twisty toy. Lucy felt confused because she had never seen a toy like that before. "Hi, Tom! What is that twisty toy?" Lucy asked. Tom smiled and said, "Hi, Lucy! This is my new toy. You twist it like this and it makes a funny shape!" Lucy wanted to try it, so Tom gave her the toy. She twisted it and made a big circle. Then, she twisted it again and made a small square. Tom and Lucy laughed and played with the twisty toy all day. At the end of the day, they were tired but happy. Lucy said, "Thank you for showing me your twisty toy, Tom. I had so much fun!" Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lucy. I'm glad we are friends!" They both went home, excited to play together again soon.

Once there was a little girl called Emma. She was only three years old, and loved to play outside. One day, Emma was walking in the park when she stumbled over a penny. She looked at it and saw that it was shiny and new. She felt so happy and put it into her pocket. Suddenly, Emma felt a sharp pain in her foot. She looked down and saw that she had hurt her toe. She started to cry and stumbled back home. When she arrived, her mom was so worried. She hugged Emma and asked what had happened. Emma said that she had stumbled in the park and hurt her toe. Her mom kissed her hurt toe and put a big bandage on it. Then her mom gave Emma the penny and said it was to help make her feel better. Emma was so happy to have the penny and felt a lot better.

Once upon a time, there was a nice cat. The cat had a pipe. The pipe was very big. The cat liked to play with the pipe. The cat would roll the pipe all around the yard. One day, the cat met a dog. The dog did not know about the pipe. The cat showed the dog the pipe. The dog thought the pipe was fun. The dog and the cat played with the pipe together. The cat and the dog became good friends. They played with the pipe every day. They knew the pipe was fun. The cat and the dog were very happy. And they played with the pipe forever.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Jane and Jack. Jane invited Jack to her house to play. When Jack arrived, Jane showed him her new drum. Jane asked, "Do you want to play the drum with me?" Jack said, "No! Drums are too loud and make too much noise!" Jane was surprised. She said, "What if we can play the drum in a way that is not too loud?" Jack thought about it for a moment and said, "Okay." Jane and Jack decided to play the drum together in a healthy way. They used two drum sticks and hit the drum gently. Jane and Jack had so much fun playing the drum together. They even made up a song. At the end of their play date, Jack thanked Jane for inviting him to her house. They said goodbye, looking forward to playing the drum again soon.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was eager to explore the world. Every day she went out to search for the most interesting things she could find. Today she arrived to a spooky forest, full of tall trees and dark shadows. Suddenly she saw it: a skeleton! She was scared at first, but then she realised she wanted to take it home and show it to her family. She was very eager to do so, so she put all her strength into it and lifted the skeleton up. The skeleton was so light! It felt like it was made of paper. She was surprised and very happy. She whispered to the skeleton: "You can come with me". The girl immediately forgot about the fear and the shadows of the spooky forest. She walked happily towards home, with the skeleton in her hands.
