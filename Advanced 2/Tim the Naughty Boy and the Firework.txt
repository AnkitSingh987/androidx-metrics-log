Once upon a time, there was a naughty boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with firework. One day, Tim found a big firework in his dad's room. He wanted to hold it and play with it. Tim took the firework outside. He wanted to see it go up in the sky. Tim held the firework tight and lit it. But, something unexpected happened. The firework did not go up. It stayed on the ground and made a big mess. Tim was sad and scared. He thought he broke the firework. But then, his dad came out and smiled. He said, "Tim, that was not a firework. It was a smoke bomb for bugs!" Tim laughed, and they cleaned up the mess together.

One day, a girl named Mia went for a jog. She liked to run by the river where she could see the boats with big sails. Mia loved to watch the boats go by, some fast and some slow. She wanted to be on a boat one day. As Mia jogged, she saw a slow boat with a big sail. It was not moving at all. Mia thought the boat needed help. She ran to the boat and climbed in. She tried to make the boat go, but it did not move. Then, something unexpected happened! The boat began to move on its own, very fast! Mia was surprised and happy. The boat took her to a secret island with lots of fun games and yummy treats. Mia played all day and had so much fun. When it was time to go home, the boat took her back. Mia could not wait to jog by the river again and see the slow boat with the big sail.

Once upon a time, there was a little crab named Timmy. Timmy lived near the shore with his family. He was a very selfish crab and did not like to share his toys with his brothers and sisters. One day, Timmy was playing with his favorite toy near the shore. His sister, Sally, asked, "Can I play with your toy, please?" Timmy said, "No, it's mine!" and ran away. Timmy played and played, not watching where he was going. He went too far and soon he could not see the shore. Timmy was lost and scared. He cried, "Help! I want to go home!" A kind fish heard Timmy's cries and swam to him. The fish said, "I can help you find your way back to the shore, but you must promise to share your toys with your brothers and sisters." Timmy nodded and said, "I promise." The kind fish led Timmy back to the shore. When they got there, Timmy hugged his family and said, "I'm sorry for being selfish. I will share my toys from now on." And Timmy kept his promise, sharing his toys and playing happily with his brothers and sisters.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. His mom said, "Tim, be careful!" Tim started to climb the tree, but he saw a silly bird on a branch. The bird stared at Tim and made a loud whistle sound. Tim said, "Hello, silly bird!" The bird whistled again and stared at Tim. Tim tried to whistle like the bird, but he couldn't do it. He felt sad and wanted to show his mom the bird. So he called out, "Mom, look at the silly bird!" But his mom was talking to another mom and didn't hear him. Tim wanted to get closer to the bird, so he climbed higher. He reached out for the bird, but the branch broke. Tim fell down from the tree and started to cry. His mom ran to him and said, "Oh no, Tim! Are you okay?" Tim was hurt and couldn't stop crying. The silly bird flew away, still whistling. Tim's mom took him home, and they never saw the silly bird again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy. She was three years old and very adventurous. She loved to explore the world around her and learn something new each day. One day, Lucy's mom took her to a park near her house. While Lucy was exploring, she came across a small pond with a shower nearby. At the shower, Lucy saw a beautiful bird. She was so excited and started talking to the bird. The bird said, "Would you like to come to my party this evening? I know you would have a very rich experience there". Lucy was excited and said she would love to come to the party. She went home and told her mom about it. Her mom said that she could go but to come back home before it got late. When the night came, Lucy went to the party. As soon as she arrived, she was so overwhelmed with love. At the party, everyone was laughing, dancing and having a great time. All of a sudden, it started to rain and everyone starting running in and out of the shower. Lucy was amazed to see so many people gathered and having such a rich experience while they protected themselves from the rain. At the end of the party, everyone said their goodbyes and thanked Lucy for joining them. Lucy had the best time and thanked her mom for letting her go. She was so happy and couldn't stop talking about the party.
