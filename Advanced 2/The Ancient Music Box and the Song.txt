Once upon a time, there was an ancient music box. It was very old and special. The music box could record sounds. One day, it heard a beautiful song. The song was from an opera. The opera was about a kind king and a happy queen. The music box wanted to record the opera. It opened its tiny door and listened. It heard the high voice of the queen and the deep voice of the king. The music box was very happy. It saved the opera inside itself. Now, every time someone opened the music box, they could hear the opera. The beautiful voices of the king and queen made everyone smile. The ancient music box was very proud to share the happy opera with others. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a thoughtful girl who loved to help her mom and dad. One day, her mom asked her to clean her room. Lily said, "Yes, mom!" and went to her room. In her room, Lily found her favorite teddy bear under the bed. She remembered that she had put it there last night. She also found her red ball, her blue car, and her green frog. Lily put everything where it belonged and her room was clean. The next day, Lily's mom had a surprise for her. She took Lily to the park and gave her a big, new toy. Lily was so happy! She remembered how she had cleaned her room and knew that her mom was happy too. And from that day on, Lily always made sure to keep her room clean.

One day, a big dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a very small cat. The cat was sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, little cat?" The little cat said, "My friend is in jail. I want to pick flowers to make her feel great, but I can't reach them." Max wanted to help the cat. He picked the flowers for the cat. The cat was happy. Max and the cat went to the jail to give the flowers to the cat's friend. When they got there, they saw that the jail was open! The friend was not in the jail anymore. She was hiding behind a tree. She jumped out and yelled, "Surprise!" The cat and Max were so happy. They all played together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was an ordinary grape. This grape lived on a big tree with many other grapes. They all were happy and liked to play together. One day, the grape met a new friend. The new friend was a tiny bug. The grape said, "Hi, I am a grape! Who are you?" The tiny bug said, "Hi, I am a bug! I like to play too!" The grape and the bug became best friends. One sunny day, something unexpected happened. The grape and the bug were playing when a big bird came. The big bird tried to eat the grape! But the tiny bug was brave and said, "Stop! Don't eat my friend!" The big bird was surprised and flew away. The grape and the bug were safe and happy. They knew they would always be best friends.

Once there was a little boy and his mommy. The little boy really loved broccoli. Every day he would demand broccoli from his mommy. One day the little boy asked his mommy to get him broccoli. His mommy said they were all out, but then she found a white broccoli in the back of the fridge. The little boy was so excited that he jumped and shouted, "Mommy, look! White broccoli!" His mommy said, "Yes, my sweet, a white broccoli. What do you want to do with it?" The little boy said, "Let's eat it!" So his mommy cooked it and they shared it together. The little boy was so happy. He said, "Mommy, I'm so glad we had white broccoli for dinner."

Once upon a time, there was a big raven. The raven was elderly and wise. He lived in a tree near a farm. The raven had many friends on the farm. They liked to talk and play together. One day, the raven saw an apple on the ground. The apple was not good. It was rot. The raven called his friend, the cat. The cat came and looked at the apple. The raven said, "Look, cat, the apple is not good. It is rot. We should not eat it." The cat agreed and said, "Yes, raven, you are right. We should not eat the rot apple." They told their friends about the rot apple. All the animals knew not to eat it. The elderly raven and the cat were happy. They had helped their friends. They played and talked with their friends. They all lived happily on the farm.
