One day, a huge dog found a case. The case was big and red. The dog was very happy. He wanted to open the case, but he could not do it alone. So, he went to find a friend to help him. The huge dog met a small cat. The dog said, "Let's unite and open the case!" The cat agreed and they both tried to open the case. They pushed and pulled, but the case did not open. Then, a bird came and said, "I can help you open the case!" The dog and the cat were happy. They all worked together to open the case. But when they opened it, they found nothing inside. The dog, the cat, and the bird were sad because they thought they would find something great inside the case.

One day, a big frog lived in a swamp. The frog liked to do silly things. He liked to jump high and clap his hands. All the other animals in the swamp would laugh at the silly frog. One day, a small bird came to the swamp. The bird did not like the silly frog. The bird said, "Stop clapping your hands. It is too loud!" The frog was sad. He did not want to make the bird mad. The frog had an idea. He asked the bird to clap with him. The bird tried it and liked it. They both clapped their hands together. The other animals in the swamp joined them too. The swamp was now a happy place where everyone clapped and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a dirty plane. It was very old and had not been used for a long time. The plane lived in a big field with other old planes. They all wanted to fly in the sky, but they couldn't because they were too dirty and broken. One day, a kind man came to the field. He saw the dirty plane and felt sad. He wanted to help the plane fly again. So, he started to work. He cleaned the plane, fixed its wings, and made sure everything was good. The plane started to look better and better. Finally, the day came when the plane was ready to fly. The man and the plane went up into the sky, and they had so much fun. The other old planes saw them and felt happy. They knew that one day, someone would come and work on them too. And then, they would all fly together in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a big oak tree. The tree had many leaves. The leaves liked to play games. They were very competitive. One day, the leaves decided to have a race. They wanted to see who could complete the race first. They all lined up on the big oak tree. The wind blew, and the leaves started the race. Some leaves went fast, and some leaves went slow. The wind helped the leaves move. In the end, one leaf won the race. All the leaves were happy and had fun. The big oak tree was proud of its leaves.

Sammy was a happy bunny. He loved to hop around in his backyard. He was very comfortable with his family and they were always very kind to him. One day, Sammy's mommy sat him down and told him something very important. She said, "Sammy, I want you to go and see the teacher." Sammy was very confused. He had never been to a teacher before. He asked mommy, "Who is the teacher? What do they teach?" Mommy looked sad and said, "I'm sorry Sammy, but the teacher teaches naughty lessons. No more hopping around for you!" Sammy felt so sad. He had never been taught to be naughty before. He would miss hopping around in his yard. The next day, Sammy went to the teacher, feeling very scared. He was not happy at all. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. He was very happy to see all the animals. They saw big elephants, funny monkeys, and tall giraffes. Tim loved the zoo. In a corner of the zoo, Tim saw a sad monkey. He asked his mom, "Why is the monkey sad?" His mom said, "The monkey is sad because there is no food. The zoo needs to supply more food for the monkey." Tim wanted to help the sad monkey. He asked his mom if they could give the monkey some food. His mom said yes, and they bought bananas for the monkey. Tim gave the bananas to the monkey, and the monkey was happy. The zoo saw this and promised to supply more food for all the animals. Tim and his mom went home with big smiles, knowing they made the animals at the zoo happy.
