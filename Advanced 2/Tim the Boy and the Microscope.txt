Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy called a microscope. He loved to look at things with it. It made small things look big. One day, a naughty girl named Sue saw Tim's microscope. She wanted it very much. So, she took it when Tim was not looking. She had stolen it! Tim was sad when he could not find his microscope. Tim was a persistent boy. He looked and looked for his microscope. He found Sue playing with it. Tim told Sue it was not nice to steal. Sue felt bad and gave the microscope back to Tim. They became friends and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a nice cat named Sam. Sam liked to help his friends. One day, Sam saw his friend, the bird, with a lot of mail. "Can I help you with the mail?" Sam asked the bird. The bird said, "Yes, please! Thank you, Sam!" Sam and the bird took the mail to all their friends. They gave a letter to the dog, the fish, and the frog. All their friends said, "Thank you for the mail!" At the end of the day, Sam and the bird were tired but happy. They had helped their friends and had a fun day. Sam loved to help and be nice to his friends.

The evening was lovely and the sun was setting. A 3 year old child named Oliver was walking in the park, whistling happily as he went. Suddenly, Oliver noticed something creative: a large tree with a hole in it was filled with colourful ribbons. “Wow, this is so beautiful!” Oliver said excitedly. He ran over to the tree and started to take out the ribbons. Just then, a man came over and yelled, “Hey! Put those ribbons back! This is not for you to play with!" Oliver was scared. He started to cry and ran away, leaving the ribbons behind. The man sighed and shook his head. He wished he had been more creative and gentle when talking to Oliver. Now he had made the evening turn bad.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some ice-cream. Tim saw a big, red ice-cream box. It was cheap, so his mom said, "Yes, we can buy it." When they got home, Tim opened the box. He saw lots of ice-cream inside. He was very happy. He took a big spoon and started to eat it. But then, his sister, Sue, came into the room. She saw Tim eating the ice-cream. "Can I have some too?" Sue asked. Tim said, "Yes, but don't spoil it. We have to share." Sue took a small spoon and started to eat the ice-cream too. They both enjoyed the sweet, cold treat together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very hard worker. He had a toy car that he loved a lot. It was his own special car. Every day, he would play with his car and make it go fast. One day, Tim saw his mom talking on the phone. She looked worried. She said there was an emergency. Tim didn't know what that meant, but he knew it was not good. He wanted to help, but he didn't know how. Later, Tim saw his mom looking for something. She needed to go to the emergency place, but she couldn't find her keys. Tim had an idea! He got his toy car and put the keys on top. He pushed the car to his mom. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim! You saved the day with your own car!" Tim felt happy that he could help in his own way.

Mama and Father were getting ready to take their three year old daughter Ruby to a festival. Ruby loved festivals, and she was so excited. She asked them what she was allowed to do at the festival and they told her that there would be lots of things to do. On the day of the festival, they drive to the park and Ruby notices that it is a very special day. Father said, “This is a competitive festival, so let’s go and be careful!” They looked around and saw lots of children running, laughing and having fun. Suddenly, Ruby sees a girl her age with a winning trophy in her hands and thinks that she could be competitive too. She is eager to enter the festival and join in the competition. Mama and Father both smile and say, “You can enter the competition if you want. We know you will do well!” At the end of the day, Ruby is standing in the middle of the crowd with her trophy in her hands. She had won the festival andfelt like she belonged! Everyone was cheer and proud of Ruby's success. She knew that she had done something special that day.
