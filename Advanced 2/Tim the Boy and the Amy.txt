Once upon a time, there was a shy little boy named Tim. Tim was going to a new classroom to learn and make friends. He was very shy and scared to talk to the other kids. One day, Tim was playing with a ball in the classroom. He threw the ball up high and it started to fall. It fell right on a girl's head. The girl's name was Amy. Amy looked at Tim and said, "Hey, that's not nice!" Tim felt so bad and said, "I'm so sorry, Amy. I didn't mean to hit you. I was just playing with the ball." Amy smiled and said, "It's okay. Do you want to play together?" Tim was so happy and said, "Yes, please!" From that day on, Tim and Amy became best friends. They played together every day and Tim wasn't shy anymore. He learned that making friends was fun and easy. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a polite little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Spot. Lily liked to play with her toys and listen to stories her mom told her. One sunny day, Lily went to the park to play. She saw a sad boy sitting on a bench. Lily walked over and said, "Hi, I am Lily. Why are you sad?" The boy looked up and said, "I lost my toy car. I hope I can find it." Lily wanted to help the boy. They looked all around the park for the toy car. They looked under the slides, by the swings, and even in the sandbox. Finally, they found the toy car under a big tree. The boy was so happy, and he said, "Thank you, Lily, for helping me find my car." From that day on, Lily and the boy became good friends. They played together at the park every day. Lily learned that being polite and helping others can make a big difference.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree with many branches. On one branch, there was a bird named Bob. Bob liked to talk to his friends. One day, his friend, a squirrel named Sue, came to visit. Sue said, "Bob, I saw something far away. I want to tell you about it." Bob was very excited. He asked, "What did you see, Sue?" Sue mentioned, "I saw a big, colorful thing in the distant sky. I think it is coming closer." They both looked at the sky and waited. Suddenly, the big, colorful thing was right above them! It was a hot air balloon. The people in the balloon said, "Hello, Bob and Sue! We were lost, but now we found our way. Thank you for helping us!" Bob and Sue were very happy to help their new friends. And that is how Bob and Sue made friends with the people in the hot air balloon.

Once there was a wealthy man. He was very greedy and wanted all of the magic in the land. He had found a way to make all the magic in the world his. One day, a small fairy came up to the man and said, "Please, don't take all of the magic away. It makes the world special!" But the man laughed and waved his hand. He said, "No! All of the magic is mine!" The fairy was very upset and flew away. She knew this man was very foolish and would regret his actions. The wealthy man kept taking all of the magic until there was none left. He was happy to have all the power, but the land was empty and cold without the magic. Everyone was sad and the man felt guilty. The fairy was right - the man did regret his actions. All the magic was gone and it was too late to get it back. The man had lost all of his wealth, power, and the magic that made the world special.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little girl named Lily. Lily loved to discover new things. She would go on walks and look at the world around her. One day, she found a big, red ball. She played with it all day and had lots of fun. The next day, Lily went to a place where people give talks called a lecture. Her mom and dad were there too. They listened to a man talk about animals. Lily liked the animals, especially the big, fluffy dog. After the lecture, Lily went home and played with her red ball again. She felt happy because she discovered new things about animals and had fun with her ball. She knew she was a gifted little girl who could learn and play at the same time.
