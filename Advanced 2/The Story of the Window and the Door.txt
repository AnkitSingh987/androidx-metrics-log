One day, a little girl was playing at home. Suddenly, she heard a loud noise outside. She was very curious, so she went to the window to see what was happening. Outside she saw a big animal. It was very big, and the girl was afraid. She walked slowly towards the door, but the animal saw her. It started to move closer and closer to her. The girl was scared, so she started to run. She ran as fast as she could, but the animal was still following her. Suddenly, she remembered something her mother had told her. She had said to be careful and to always "warn" something if she ever saw a big animal. So, the girl started to dance. She danced slow and steady, and the animal stopped. The little girl was happy and relieved, until the animal started to move closer again. It grabbed her before she had a chance to run away. The little girl was never seen again. Her mother always warned her to be careful, but she didn't listen.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. She was a very happy and kind girl in her small town. But one day, she found out something not nice. Sometimes when Emma went to play with her friends, they said bad words. These were words that Emma didn’t understand, but they were rude. She felt sad because her friends were using rude words and not being nice. The next day, Emma decided to learn a different way. She wanted to learn how to be nice to her friends. She went to the library and read a book about behaving in a kind way. One day, she was walking in the park and saw a white bird in a tree. Emma knew the bird was sad, so she talked to it kindly. And that day, Emma learned something special - it's important to be kind to everyone and not be rude. That's how Emma learned to be nice. From then on, she always remembered to be gentle and kind to all her friends. She was a happy girl - and being nice made her even happier.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Max. He liked to play all day long. One day, Max decided to take a walk. As he was walking, he found a bunch of junk. Max wanted to look closer, so he started pushing the junk. He was having so much fun. Suddenly, he heard a voice saying, "Be careful, Max! That stuff is fragile!" Max looked around and saw a old man standing nearby. Max didn't know who he was, but the old man had kind eyes. Max said, "Sorry sir! What should I do?" The old man smiled and said, "Just take things slow and gentle. Don't push it so hard!" Max nodded and remembered the old man's advice. He started pushing the junk very carefully with his tiny hands. And he had so much fun!

Lily and Tom are twins. They share a bedroom. They have two beds, one blue and one pink. Lily likes the blue bed and Tom likes the pink bed. But sometimes they want to change. One night, Lily says to Tom, "Can I sleep in your bed? I want to try the pink bed." Tom says, "OK, but only for tonight. You have to give it back tomorrow." They change beds and say good night. They close their eyes and try to sleep. But Lily cannot sleep. The pink bed is too soft and too warm. She misses her blue bed. She feels sad. She whispers to Tom, "Tom, can we change back? I don't like the pink bed. I want my blue bed." Tom cannot sleep either. The blue bed is too hard and too cold. He misses his pink bed. He feels sad too. He whispers to Lily, "Lily, yes, let's change back. I don't like the blue bed. I want my pink bed." They get up and change beds again. They say sorry and hug each other. They go back to their own beds and smile. They feel happy and cozy. They fall asleep in their own beds. The bedroom is quiet and dark. Lily and Tom dream of nice things. They love their beds and they love each other.
