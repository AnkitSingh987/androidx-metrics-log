Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a silly dog named Bobo. Bobo loved to play outside in the wind. He would run and jump and have lots of fun. One day, Bobo met a big cat named Tilly. Tilly was not very nice. She said, "Bobo, you are so silly. Why do you play in the wind? You should bow to me, the big cat." Bobo was sad, but he wanted to be friends with Tilly, so he bowed to her. Later, Bobo's friend, a little bird named Kiki, saw what happened. Kiki said, "Bobo, don't be sad. You don't have to bow to Tilly. You are a good dog, and it's okay to be silly and play in the wind." Bobo learned that it's important to be yourself and not let others make you feel bad for being who you are.

One day, a little yellow bird flew around the world. The bird hoped to find a friend. The bird met a big blue fish in the sea. "Hi, fish! Do you want to be my friend?" the bird asked. The fish looked at the bird and said, "Yes, I would like that." The bird and the fish played all day. They had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The sky turned dark and it started to rain. The bird was sad because it could not fly in the rain. "Don't worry, bird," said the fish. "I have an idea." The fish jumped high and made a big splash. The water went up and made a big umbrella for the bird. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, fish! You are a great friend." They played under the water umbrella until the rain stopped. The sun came out, and the yellow bird and the blue fish were friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a small mouse. The mouse was very obedient. One day, the mouse wanted to perform a dance for his animal friends. He practiced and practiced his dance moves. He was very excited to show his friends. One day, a big cat came to the place where the mouse lived. All the animals were scared of the cat. The cat wanted to eat the mouse. The mouse was sad because he could not perform his dance for his friends. Then, the mouse had an idea. He told the cat, "I can perform a dance for you, and if you like it, please do not eat me." The cat agreed. The mouse danced and danced. The cat liked the dance so much that he let the mouse go. The mouse was very happy and could finally perform his dance for his friends, who were all very proud of him.
