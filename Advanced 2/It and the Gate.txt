It was a gloomy day. Amber was walking in the park with her mom. "Mommy, what is that?" asked Amber, pointing at a bottle of medicine. "That is medicine" said her mom. "It helps people to feel better if they don't feel well." "Can I try it?" Amber asked. "No, sweetheart. That medicine is for the people who need it. You don't need it." Amber smiled and said "okay". They came to a rusty old gate and her mom said "Let's close this gate. It's not safe to leave it open." Amber helped her mom close the gate and soon they were back to their walk. The gloomy day had ended and Amber was happy to be with her mom.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Amy. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to mind the soup while she went to the store. Amy was very happy to help. While Amy was watching the soup, she thought it needed something. She saw the salt on the table and thought it would be good to put some in the soup. She took the salt and put a little in the soup. The soup was not dry anymore. When her mom came back, she tasted the soup and smiled. She said, "Amy, you did a great job minding the soup and adding the salt. It is not dry at all. Thank you for helping me in the kitchen." Amy felt very happy and proud that she could help her mom.

One day, a girl and a boy went on a picnic. They had a big basket full of food. They wanted to find a hidden spot to eat. They walked and walked until they found a nice place under a big tree. The girl said, "I feel happy to eat here!" The boy nodded and said, "Me too!" They started to eat their yummy food. They had sandwiches, fruits, and juice. They laughed and talked while they ate. But then, dark clouds came and it started to rain. The girl and the boy felt sad. Their picnic was ruined. The rain made everything wet, and they had to go back home. They wished they could have had more fun at their picnic.

Once upon a time, there was a crazy pilot named Tim. He loved to fly his big red plane. One sunny day, Tim decided to go on an adventure. As Tim flew his plane, he saw a big lake below. He thought it would be fun to splash water with his plane. So, he flew down close to the lake and made a big splash! The water went everywhere, and Tim laughed. A little duck saw Tim and said, "Hey, crazy pilot! That was a big splash! Can you do it again?" Tim smiled and said, "Sure, little duck!" He flew his plane down again and made another big splash. The duck laughed and played in the water. Tim and the duck had a fun day splashing and playing together.

Billy was sad. He had been playing outside, but then he realized that his favorite toy was missing. He was sure he had left it on the porch, but it was nowhere to be seen. He asked his neighbor if they had seen it, but they said no. He asked everyone he could think of, but no one had seen his toy. Billy was worried. He searched high and low, but still couldn't find it. He went back to his neighbor, asking if they had seen where it might have gone. His neighbor suddenly smiled and said that they had seen someone hiding it in the garden! Billy was pleased. He thanked his neighbor for their help and ran off to look for it. After a few minutes of searching, he found his toy under some branches, hidden away. Now he had it back, and all was available again!

One day, a big man named Tom wanted to design a new elevator. He was very good at making things. He liked to make things go up and down. Tom drew a big box with buttons on a paper. He was happy with his design. Tom started to build the elevator. He used many colors to make it look nice. He put buttons inside the box. The buttons made the elevator go up and down. When the elevator was done, Tom wanted to show it to his friends. Tom's friends came to see the elevator. They were very excited. But, they were also impatient. They wanted to go up and down in the elevator right away. Tom said, "Wait, I'll show you how it works." They all went inside the elevator and pushed the buttons. The elevator went up and down, and they all had fun.
