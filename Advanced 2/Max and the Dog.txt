One day, a big, hairy dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a car with a driver inside. The driver was looking at a sign. The sign had a picture of a dog on it. The driver looked confused. Max went to the driver and said, "Hello, I am Max. Can I help you?" The driver said, "Hi Max, I am looking for the dog park. Can you show me where it is?" Max nodded and said, "Yes, I can help. Follow me." Max and the driver walked to the dog park together. When they got there, the driver said, "Thank you, Max. You are a good dog." Max wagged his tail and said, "You're welcome. Have fun at the dog park!"

Once upon a time, there were two caterpillars named Tom and Jerry. Tom and Jerry loved to crawl around and explore nature. One day, Tom and Jerry decided to pass through a meadow full of beautiful flowers. They were awed by the bright colors and gentle breeze. While passing through the meadow, the caterpillars saw a beautiful butterfly fluttering gracefully in the air. Tom asked Jerry: "Where did that beautiful butterfly come from?". Jerry replied "I don't know, but I'm sure she's gone through something special". Tom and Jerry spent the rest of the day exploring the meadow, until it was time to go home. That night, as Tom and Jerry were asleep, an amazing thing happened. A miracle! Tom and Jerry were beginning to transform into two beautiful butterflies with bright wings and colourful patterns. Tom happily exclaimed: "We passed through the meadow to become beautiful butterflies!". Jerry replied with a smile "Yes we did, and we will pass through many more meadows in our lifetime".

Once upon a time, there was a big gorilla. He lived in a green forest. The gorilla was very brave. One day, the gorilla saw a little bird. The bird did not have a home. The brave gorilla wanted to help the bird. The gorilla and the bird looked for a new home. They found a big tree. The bird was happy to have a home. The gorilla was happy too. They became good friends.

Once there was a boy named Bob. Bob was three years old and he loved to talk. One day, Bob saw his mom with a mop. She was cleaning the floor. Bob asked his mom, "What are you doing?" His mom smiled and said, "I'm cleaning the floor with the mop." Bob wanted to help and he asked, "Can I help you?" His mom said, "Sure!" Bob tried his best to help but he felt helpless. He couldn't get the mop to do what he wanted it to do. His mom realized he was getting frustrated, so she said, "It's okay Bob, why don't you sit over there and watch while I finish up." So Bob watched his mom clean the floor and he spoke to her while she worked. He asked her questions and they had a nice conversation while she moped up the mess. When she finished, his mom said, "Thanks for your help, Bob. You were a big help!" They both smiled and Bob was glad that he was able to help even if it wasn't with the mop.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a wet day, but Tim didn't care. He put on his boots and jumped in the puddles. "Splish, splash!" he laughed. In the park, Tim saw a toy train. It was red and blue. He wanted to play with it. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the train, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to get it wet." Tim played with the train and made it go fast. He was having so much fun. But then, he accidentally kicked the train into a puddle. The train got all wet. Tim was sad. His mom came over and saw the wet train. She said, "It's okay, Tim. We can dry it off." They took the train home and dried it with a towel. The train was good as new. Tim learned to be more careful when he played outside on wet days.
