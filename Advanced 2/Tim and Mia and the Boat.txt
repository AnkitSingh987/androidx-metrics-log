Tim and Mia like to play with their toy boat and pretend they are pirates. They have a big box that they use as a treasure chest. They fill it with shiny things and hide it under a blanket. One day, they decide to sail to a new island. They put their boat in the bathtub and turn on the water. They make waves with their hands and say "Arrr!" and "Yo-ho-ho!" They have fun rocking the boat and splashing each other. But then, Tim sees something scary. There is a spider on the wall, near the faucet. Tim does not like spiders. He screams and lets go of the boat. The boat tips over and the water spills out. The treasure chest falls out of the boat and opens. All the shiny things scatter on the floor. Mia sees Tim is scared and wet. She is a helpful pirate. She turns off the water and gets a towel. She wraps Tim in the towel and hugs him. She says "Don't worry, Tim. It's just a spider. It can't hurt you. I will get rid of it." She picks up a plastic cup and traps the spider inside. She takes it to the window and lets it go outside. She says "Bye-bye, spider. Go find a new home." She comes back to Tim and smiles. She says "There. The spider is gone. Are you okay, Tim?" Tim nods and smiles back. He says "Thank you, Mia. You are a good pirate. And a good sister." They hug and laugh. They pick up their shiny things and put them back in the treasure chest. They say "We are the best pirates ever!" They dry their boat and put it back in their room. They decide to play something else. They have many adventures together. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a colorful bird. The bird liked to talk to all the animals and people in the village. The bird was very happy and everyone loved the bird. One day, the colorful bird met a little girl. The girl was sad because she lost her toy. The bird wanted to help her. So, the bird talked to all the animals in the village to find the toy. After talking to many animals, the colorful bird found the toy. The little girl was very happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, the bird and the girl became best friends. They played and talked every day in the village.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play outside with her ball. One day, while playing, her ball went into a big, dark cave. Amy was scared, but she wanted her ball back. As Amy went into the cave, she saw a big monster. The monster looked at Amy and said, "Hi! I have your ball. If you give me something yummy, I will give it back to you." Amy thought about what she could give the monster. Amy went home and asked her mom for a yummy treat. Her mom gave her a big, juicy apple. Amy took the apple back to the monster in the cave. The monster was happy and gave Amy her ball back. From that day on, Amy and the monster were friends, and they played together all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that could shoot water. He loved to play with it in his bathtub. Every day, he would fill the bathtub with water and get ready to play. One sunny day, Tim found the perfect spot in the bathtub for his toy. He put the toy on the edge of the bathtub and started to shoot water at his rubber duck. The duck went round and round in the water, making Tim laugh. After a while, Tim's mom came in and saw the fun he was having. She smiled and said, "Tim, you found the perfect place to play with your toy!" Tim smiled back and continued to shoot water at his duck, having lots of fun in his bathtub.
