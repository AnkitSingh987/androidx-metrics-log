Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She had a white dress. One day, she went to play outside in the sun. While playing, she saw a white cat. The girl let the cat come near her. They played together and had fun. The girl and the cat became good friends. One day, the white cat jumped up high and turned into a pretty bird. The girl was so surprised! Now, the girl and the bird played together every day. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys and take the elevator in his house. The elevator was big and smooth. It went up and down, and Tim liked to ride it every day. One day, Tim wanted to take his toys with him in the elevator. He put them all in a big bag and went to the elevator. He pushed the button, and the elevator door opened. Tim and his toys went inside, and he pushed the button to go up. The elevator went up, but it did not stop. It went up and up, and Tim started to feel scared. He pushed the buttons, but the elevator did not stop. Tim and his toys were stuck in the elevator, and they could not get out. The elevator went up and up, and Tim cried. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim did not like Tuesdays. He thought Tuesdays were boring. He wanted to find a way to make Tuesdays more fun. One Tuesday, Tim found a magic hat. He put the hat on his head and wished for Tuesdays to become fun. Suddenly, the hat began to glow and everything around Tim started to change. Now, every Tuesday, Tim's toys would come to life and play with him. His toy car would race around the room, his stuffed bear would dance, and his toy robot would tell funny jokes. Tim was so happy that Tuesdays were no longer boring, all thanks to his magic hat.

One day, a friendly dog named Max went for a walk in the park. He saw a little boy named Tim playing with a toy pistol. Max went to Tim and wagged his tail. "Hi, dog! Do you want to play with me?" Tim asked. Max barked happily and nodded his head. Tim and Max played with the toy pistol. They took turns pretending to shoot at the trees. Max's owner, Sarah, came to the park to find Max. She saw Tim and Max playing together. "Max, you should care for the little boy," Sarah said. Max barked and wagged his tail more. Sarah and Tim became friends too. They all played together in the park every day. Tim, Max, and Sarah cared for each other and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was small girl named Daisy. Daisy was very kind and always thought of others before herself. One day, Daisy wanted to make a special cake for her family. She carefully mixed the ingredients, but when she poured them into the pan she noticed she forgot to buy butter. Daisy was sad and asked her mum for help. Her mum had a plan. She said,” Instead of buying butter, why don't you share what you have at home with others?” Daisy smiled, she knew her mum was right. Daisy shared some of her food with the neighbours and as a thank you, they gave Daisy some butter. Daisy was excited to bake her special cake. She added the butter and the cake tasted delicious. Daisy smiled and said,”That's what happens when we share and belong.”

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy club. One day, Tim's mom told him to polish his toy club because it was dirty. Tim was sad because he wanted to play, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. Tim took a dry cloth and started to clean his toy club. He rubbed and rubbed until it was shiny and clean. When he was done, he showed his mom. She smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! Now you can play with your clean toy club." Tim went outside to play with his friends. They all had their own toy clubs too. They laughed and played with their clean toy clubs all day long. They were so happy and proud of their shiny toys. And Tim learned that it was good to take care of his things.
