Henry was a helpful three year old boy. He loved to jump and he never ran out of energy. Every day he would run around the park and jump up and down as if he was a frog. One day he found an old novel lying on the grass. It had some colourful pictures on the cover and he thought it looked interesting. He couldn't wait to open and read it. He showed it to his mum and declared, "Look, mummy! I've found a novel!" His Mum smiled and said, "That's wonderful, Henry. Let's sit down and read it together." So together they sat on the grass, and Henry opened the book for the first time. The novel was full of stories and Henry soon forgot about his jumping and started reading instead. At the end of the day, he had read half of the novel and his Mum was so proud of him. "That was so helpful, Henry. You should be proud of yourself," she said. Henry smiled and said, "Mummy, can I jump again tomorrow?" His mum laughed and said, "Yes, of course. You can jump as much as you like!"

Once upon a time, there was a little pig. The pig lived on a farm with many other animals. One day, the pig went for a walk to see what he could find. As the pig walked, he saw a beautiful butterfly. The butterfly was red, blue, and yellow. The pig liked the butterfly very much. He wanted to be friends with it. So, the pig followed the butterfly as it flew around the farm. The butterfly led the pig to a big tree. Under the tree, the pig discovered something new. It was a big, shiny rock. The pig was very happy to find the rock. He showed it to all his friends on the farm. They all liked the beautiful rock too. And from that day on, the pig and the butterfly were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a clean little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and look at things through his microscope. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play with his friends. As they were playing, they found some pretty rocks and leaves. They decided to scatter them on the ground to make a beautiful picture. Tim said, "Look at all the colors! Let's put them in a big circle." His friend, Lily, agreed and said, "Yes, that's a great idea! Let's make the best picture ever." So, they scattered the rocks and leaves in a big circle. When they were done, Tim took his microscope and looked at the pretty rocks and leaves up close. He saw all the tiny details that made them special. Tim and Lily were very happy with their beautiful picture and had a fun day playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a calm island. It was a nice place with many trees and animals. The sun was always shining, and the water was clear and blue. People liked to go there to have fun and play. One day, a big family went to the island to park their car and have a picnic. They brought yummy food and a big ball to play with. The kids laughed and played, and the grown-ups talked and ate. Everyone was happy and having a good time. As the day went on, the family played by the water and enjoyed the calm island. They saw pretty birds and cute squirrels. When it was time to go home, they packed up their things and said goodbye to the island. They left with big smiles and happy memories.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a light bug on a leaf. Tim wanted to look at the bug, so he went closer. He saw the bug had tiny legs and wings. Tim asked his mom, "Can I touch the bug?" His mom said, "No, Tim. The bug might be scared. Let's just look at it." Tim and his mom examined the bug together. They watched it move its legs and wings. Just then, Tim heard a loud noise. It was an alarm from a big truck. The bug got scared and flew away. Tim was sad, but his mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can find more bugs next time." Tim smiled and went home with his mom.
