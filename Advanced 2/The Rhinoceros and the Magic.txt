Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a rhinoceros. His name was Remy. Remy was a big and strong animal. He liked to play with his friends. His favorite game was to throw a big ball. One day, Remy saw a bitter fruit on the ground. He thought it would be fun to throw it. So, he picked it up with his big mouth and threw it far away. The bitter fruit flew high in the sky. But then, something unexpected happened. The bitter fruit did not fall back down. It just stayed up in the sky. Remy and his friends looked up, amazed. They didn't know that the fruit was really a magic fruit. From that day on, Remy and his friends always had a fun story to tell about the day Remy threw the magic bitter fruit.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She was very enthusiastic about helping her mom clean the house. Amy wanted to fold her clothes and put them on the shelf. She thought it would make her mom happy. One day, Amy was folding her clothes when her little brother, Tim, walked in. He said, "I want to help too!" Amy was happy to have help. Together, they folded the clothes and put them on the shelf. As they were finishing, their cat, Fluffy, jumped on the shelf! The clothes fell off the shelf and onto the floor. Amy and Tim were surprised. They laughed and said, "Oh, Fluffy!" They learned that even when things don't go as planned, it's important to keep a good attitude and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a family of four who were on a walk in the woods. Suddenly, they found a yellow gem. Daddy asked, "Shall we open it?" The family said yes, so Daddy took a stick and opened it. When they opened the gem, a magical yellow light shined at them. It filled the forest with a beautiful yellow light. The family was amazed and started to play with the light. Mama said, "Let's send a wish up to the sky with this beautiful yellow gem." So the family all made a wish, and the yellow light started to shine brighter and brighter. Then, it flew up to the sky and spread all over the world! The family laughed and hugged each other. They had a beautiful day with the yellow gem.

Once upon a time, there was a big wood. In the wood, there was a nice girl named Lily. Lily was a healthy girl who liked to play and run in the wood. One day, Lily saw a little bird. The bird was sad. "Why are you sad?" Lily asked. The bird said, "I want to fly, but my mom won't allow me." Lily wanted to help the bird. She thought of a plan. She said, "Let's show your mom that you can fly well. Then, she will allow you to fly." They went to the bird's mom. Lily said, "Look, your baby can fly well!" The bird flew up and down. The mom bird was happy. But, something unexpected happened. The little bird changed into a fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you, Lily. I was a fairy who had to be a bird until someone helped me. Now, I can be a fairy again." Lily was happy she could help her new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big pond with many fish. They were fine fish, and they liked to swim all day. One day, a girl named Lily came to the pond. She saw the fine fish and wanted to bring them food. Lily went home and asked her mom for some food to bring to the fish. Her mom gave her some bread crumbs. Lily was very happy and said "thank you" to her mom. She took the bread crumbs and went back to the pond. When she got to the pond, Lily threw the bread crumbs in the water. The fine fish came up and ate the bread crumbs. They were very happy that Lily brought them food. Every day, Lily would bring food to the fish, and they all became good friends.
