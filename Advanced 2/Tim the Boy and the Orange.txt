Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had an orange flute that he loved to play. One day, Tim's mom asked him to deliver a cake to his grandma's house. As Tim walked to grandma's house, he played his orange flute. A big, mean dog heard the music and came running. The dog said, "Give me your flute, or I will eat your cake!" Tim didn't want to lose his flute or the cake, so he said, "No, go away!" The dog got angry and jumped on Tim. The cake fell on the ground and broke into pieces. Tim cried and went back home. His mom was sad, and the orange flute was lost. The cake was never delivered, and everyone had a bad day.

Mum and Dad went to the market. They wanted to buy some fruit. Mum held a big bag and Dad had a basket. They were looking around and they found lots of different fruits to choose from. Mum picked up some apples and bananas. Dad thought they were too boring so he decided to buy something else. He chose some strange looking fruit. It looked terrible, but he didn't care. Mum and Dad went back home with all the fruit. They set the table for dinner and put the fruit out for everyone to eat. They all tasted it and it was so delicious! Everyone was so happy and ate lots.

The sun glittered in the sky as two children approached a doorknob. The doorknob had a beautiful green hue, shimmering in the bright light. The older child, Susie, let out a gasp. "We can open the door," she said. "Let's try it." The younger child, Tom, smiled. He looked at the knob and felt something special. He grabbed hold, and as his fingers touched it, he felt a bitter coldness. Susie's eye lit up. "That knob is special, Tom. See how all the lines are pointing in the same direction? It just might yield something special if you keep trying." Tom nodded eagerly, and tried the knob again. This time, the knob moved and the door opened! Behind it, Tom found a wonderful surprise. A happy smile spread across his face, and he turned to Susie with delight. "You were right Susie," he said. "The knob yielded something special after all!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to have a big party with all his friends. He told his mom, "I want a party, please!" His mom said, "Okay, we can have a party." Tim and his mom started to plan the party. They made a list of friends to add. They also added cake, games, and fun. But Tim had a friend who lived in a distant house, far away. His name was Sam. Tim really wanted Sam to come to his party. On the day of the party, all of Tim's friends came. They played games, ate cake, and had a lot of fun. But Sam was not there. Tim was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. Sam came running in, with a big smile on his face! He said, "I'm sorry I'm late, Tim! I had to walk a long way to get here. But I'm here now!" Tim was so happy, and the party was even better with all his friends there.

Once upon a time, there was a nice town. In this town, there was a shop where people could buy things. One day, a big, strong man went to the shop. He wanted to buy a weapon to keep his family safe. The man saw a long, thin weapon. It was very pretty and shiny. He thought it was a graceful weapon. He bought the weapon and took it home with him. The man showed the weapon to his family. They all liked it very much. The man was happy that he could keep his family safe with the graceful weapon he bought. They all lived happily ever after.

One day, a bunny named Tim was very hungry. He wanted to find some food. Tim put on his red sweater and went outside. The sun was shining and the flowers were pretty. Tim saw his friend Max, the squirrel. Max said, "Tim, I found a big tree with lots of nuts. Let's go!" Tim was happy and they both went to the tree. Tim and Max ate the nuts and played together. They jumped and ran around the tree. Suddenly, Tim saw something shiny in the grass. He went closer and found a key. "Max, look! A key!" said Tim. Max was surprised. They decided to find what the key opened. They looked and looked, and then they found a small door in the tree. Tim used the key to open the door. Inside the door, they found a room filled with toys and candy. Tim and Max were so happy. They played with the toys and ate the candy. They had a great day and a big surprise.
