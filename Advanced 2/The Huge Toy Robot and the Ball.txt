Once upon a time, there was a huge toy robot. It had big eyes and a big smile. The robot needed a battery to have fun and play. One day, a little boy found the robot in his room. He put a battery in the robot to make it work. The robot came to life and was very happy. The little boy and the robot played all day long. They enjoyed playing with the ball and running around the house. The robot was so big that it could carry the little boy on its back. They laughed and had lots of fun. The little boy loved his huge robot friend, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a fair sunny day, a little cat named Kitty went for a walk. Kitty saw a sharp rock and a big tree. Under the tree, there was a shadow. Kitty thought, "This is a nice spot to rest." Kitty sat in the shadow and heard a sound. It was a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy flew down and said, "Hi Kitty, do you like the shadow?" Kitty smiled and said, "Yes, Chirpy, it is cool and nice." They played together in the shadow all day. They were very happy. At the end of the day, they said goodbye and went to their homes. Kitty and Chirpy became best friends and played in the shadow every fair day.

Once upon a time, there was a pink bunny with bright blue eyes. She really liked Sundays, because on Sundays she could perform special tricks. Once, on a Sunday, the pink bunny wanted to do something extra special. She hopped around the house until she found something blue. It was a blue hat! The bunny put on the blue hat and everyone who saw the bunny thought that she looked very nice. All of a sudden, the bunny had an idea. She could perform a special magic trick with the blue hat. The bunny pulled out a red daisy and told the people watching to cover their eyes. Then, she waved the blue hat and said a special magic word. When she said the word, the petals of the daisy changed to bright blue. Everyone was amazed! The blue hat was magical, and the bunny had performed a special trick. The bunny and everyone else had a lovely time on that Sunday.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable man named Tom. He liked to sell things. One day, he found a big shark at the beach. Tom thought it would be fun to sell the shark. So, he took the shark to the town. In the town, Tom met a little boy named Sam. Sam saw the shark and said, "Wow! What a big shark! Can I buy it?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, you can buy the shark. It is a good shark." Sam took the shark home, but the shark was not happy. The shark wanted to go back to the sea. So, the shark did something unexpected. It jumped out of Sam's hands and ran back to the sea. Sam and Tom were very surprised, but they were happy the shark was back where it belonged.

Sara and Tom were hungry. They wanted to eat some bread and jam. They went to the kitchen and looked for the jam jar. But they could not find it. "Where is the jam?" Sara asked. "I don't know," Tom said. "Maybe Mom took it." They looked in the fridge, in the cupboard, and under the table. But they still could not find the jam. "Let's ask Mom," Sara said. They ran to the living room, where Mom was reading a book. "Mom, where is the jam?" Sara questioned. Mom looked at them and smiled. "I have a surprise for you," she said. "I made some original jam with the berries we picked yesterday. Do you want to try it?" Sara and Tom nodded. They loved berries. Mom took them to the kitchen and showed them a big jar of red jam. It smelled sweet and fruity. "Wow, it looks yummy!" Tom said. "Thank you, Mom!" Sara said. They took some bread and spread the jam on it. They took big bites and smiled. The original jam was delicious. Sara and Tom were happy. They hugged Mom and thanked her again.
