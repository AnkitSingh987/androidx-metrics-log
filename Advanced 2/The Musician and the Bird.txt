Once upon a time, there was a musician who played the guitar. He was very good, but he wanted to improve. He practiced every day to become incredible. One day, a little bird came to listen to him play. The bird said, "I like your music, but I think we can make it even better!" The musician was surprised. He had never talked to a bird before. "How can we improve my music?" he asked. The bird had a magic voice. When it sang with the musician, they made the most incredible music together! People from all around came to listen to them. The musician and the bird became the best of friends, and they played music for everyone to enjoy.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play and talk a lot. But today they have to go to the doctor with Mom. The doctor will check their ears and noses. Mom says they have to be polite and quiet in the doctor's office. She says they have to seat on the chairs and wait for their turn. She says they can look at the books and toys, but not make noise. Anna and Ben try to do what Mom says. They seat on the chairs and pick a book each. They look at the pictures and whisper to each other. They see other kids and moms and dads in the office. Some of them look sick or sad. Some of them smile or say hello. The nurse comes and calls their names. She says they can follow her to the doctor's room. Anna and Ben hold Mom's hand and walk with the nurse. They see the doctor sitting at a desk. He has a white coat and a stethoscope. He smiles and says, "Hello, Anna and Ben. I'm Dr. Lee. How are you today?" Anna and Ben say, "Hello, Dr. Lee. We are good, thank you." They are polite and not scared. They know the doctor will help them and be nice to them. They listen to the doctor's questions and answer them. They let the doctor look at their ears and noses. They say, "Ahh" and "Eee" when the doctor asks them. They are brave and quiet. The doctor says, "Good job, Anna and Ben. You have healthy ears and noses. You are very polite and cooperative. I'm proud of you." He gives them each a sticker and a lollipop. He says, "You can go back to the waiting room and have your lollipop. Then you can go home and play. Have a nice day." Anna and Ben say, "Thank you, Dr. Lee. Have a nice day, too." They hug Mom and the nurse. They go back to the waiting room and have their lollipop. They are happy and proud. They seat on the chairs and enjoy the silence. They wait for Mom to finish talking to the receptionist. They are ready to go home and play.

One day, a charming little cat named Kitty was walking in the woods. She saw a big chest under a tree. Kitty was very curious, so she went to look at the chest. It was locked, but she found a small key nearby. She used the key to open the chest. Inside the chest, Kitty found a small, sad bird. The bird began to cry. "Why are you crying?" asked Kitty. The bird said, "I am lost and I miss my family." Kitty wanted to help the bird. She had an idea! "Let's look for your family together," she said. The bird stopped crying and they started to search for the bird's family. As they looked, Kitty found out that the bird could talk to other animals too! Finally, they found the bird's family. The bird was so happy that it started to sing a beautiful song. All the animals in the woods came to listen. The bird thanked Kitty for her help and they became best friends. From that day on, Kitty and the bird went on many fun adventures together, and they always helped each other when they needed it.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat ice cream. One day, he saw a big ice cream cone, but he could not reach it. Tim felt helpless. He wanted the cone so much. Tim saw his big sister, Sue, and asked her for help. Sue tried to explain to Tim that the big cone was just a picture on a sign. It was not a real ice cream cone. But Tim did not understand. He still wanted the cone. The next day, Sue took Tim to the ice cream shop. She bought him a real ice cream cone, just like the one in the picture. Tim was so happy. Now he understood what Sue was trying to explain. Tim and Sue enjoyed their ice cream cones together, and they lived happily ever after.
