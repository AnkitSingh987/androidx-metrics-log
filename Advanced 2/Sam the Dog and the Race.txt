Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Sam. Sam loved to run and play with his friends. He was very competitive, always wanting to win. One day, Sam and his friends decided to have a race. They wanted to see who could run the fastest. Sam was excited and wanted to succeed. He practiced running every day, so he could be the best. On the day of the race, Sam and his friends lined up. They were ready to run. When the race started, there was some smoke from a little fire. But Sam kept running. He ran faster and faster. In the end, Sam won the race! He succeeded and was very happy.

Jimmy and his mom were playing in the backyard. He saw a big pile of junk in the corner of the yard and he asked his mom what it was. His mom said to him, "That's a pile of junk. It's very old and not useful anymore." Jimmy wanted to make something out of the junk, but he was too little to do it himself. So, his mom said, "Let's pretend we can make something out of the junk. This way, we can play together!" Jimmy smiled. Together, the two of them started to build. Jimmy picked up little pieces of junk and arranged them into a little house. His mom made a pretend door for the house. Then, Jimmy put a tiny rock in the doorway so no one could come in. The two of them laughed and kept pretending as they kept building with the junk, until it was time for dinner.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tom. Tom was miserable because he did not know the way home. He wanted to learn how to find his way back, but he didn't know how. One day, Tom met a big dog named Max. Max said, "I can help you learn the way home, but first, we must play a game." Tom was scared of Max, but he really wanted to learn, so he agreed to play the game. Tom and Max played together and had lots of fun. After the game, Max showed Tom the way home. Tom was happy and not miserable anymore. The unexpected thing was that Tom and Max became best friends, even though cats and dogs usually don't get along. And now, Tom knew the way home!

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a little boy named Tim. He had a big, red boot. One day, he met a small, sad bird with a hurt wing. "Help me, please," said the bird. "I can't fly." Tim had an idea. He gave the bird his big, red boot to live in. The bird was very happy. Days went by, and the bird felt better. The bird said, "Thank you, Tim, for your help." The bird flew away, but it came back with a gift. It gave Tim a beautiful, shiny stone. Tim learned that when you give, you get good things back.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to swim. When she went to the beach, she played in the water all day long. One day, when she was swimming, she saw something interesting: a big tub of yogurt floating in the water! It was so tempting and she couldn't help but take it out of the water. The little girl ate the yogurt with glee. But suddenly, she felt very guilty because the yogurt was not hers. She had taken it without asking first, and she knew it wasn't available for her to take. The little girl was very sad. She cried and cried until she found a solution: she would leave the beach and find another big tub of yogurt and bring it back to the beach. She searched high and low until she found another tub of yogurt and brought it back to the beach to replace the one she had taken. The little girl felt relieved, and she was now happy to keep swimming and playing in the water.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Sophie. Sophie had a friend named Frank. Frank was very weird. He always wore strange clothes and acted strange too. One day, Frank was walking in the woods when he saw something stuck in a tree. Frank could see it was a kite, but he couldn't reach it. So Frank called Sophie to help him. When Sophie arrived, she saw that Frank was right - the kite was stuck in a tree. She said “How can we rescue it?” Frank and Sophie looked around, and then Frank had an idea. He fetched a ladder and placed it against the tree. Sophie climbed the ladder, pur and gently pulled the kite free. Frank and Sophie were happy they had rescued the kite. They laughed as they watched it soar in the sky. The end.
