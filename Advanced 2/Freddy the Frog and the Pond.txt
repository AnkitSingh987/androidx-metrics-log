Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Freddy. Freddy loved to jump all day long. One day, Freddy saw a big icy pond. He wanted to jump on it and play. Freddy met a new friend, a duck named Daisy. Daisy said, "Freddy, be careful! The pond is icy and you might slip." Freddy listened to Daisy and decided not to jump on the icy pond. Instead, they played near the pond and had lots of fun. Freddy and Daisy became best friends. They shared news about their day and laughed together. They were happy that they could play safely and have fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fireman. He was a very fearful fireman. He was scared of fire, but he wanted to help people. He had a big red hat and a big red truck. The fireman loved his job, but he was always scared. One day, the fireman got a call. "Help! There is a fire!" said the voice on the phone. The fireman jumped in his big red truck and drove very fast to the fire. He was scared, but he wanted to help. He got to the house and saw the fire. It was very big and very hot. The fireman started to put out the fire with water. He was doing a good job. But then, he heard a little girl cry. She was inside the house! The fireman was very scared, but he knew he had to save her. He went inside the house and found the little girl. He picked her up and tried to leave the house. But the fireman forgot the way out! They were lost in the house with the fire. The fire got bigger and bigger, and the fireman and the little girl could not find their way out. The fireman was too scared and forgot the way out, and they could not escape. The house burned down with them inside, and it was a very sad day for the town.

Once upon a time, there was a big whale named Wally. Wally lived in the deep blue sea. He liked to swim and play with his friends, the fish and the turtles. One day, Wally wanted to be independent and go on a big adventure. He swam far away from his friends and family. Wally felt happy and free, but he soon got lost. Wally did not know what to do. He felt scared and sad. Wally knew he needed help to find his way back home. He asked some new sea friends for help. They showed him the way back to his family. Wally learned that it is okay to be independent, but sometimes you need help from others. And that's the moral value of the story: it's good to be independent, but it's also important to ask for help when you need it.

One day, Joe was playing the piano. He wanted to write a song, so he sat down at the piano and started playing. He thought about how grateful he was for the beautiful lyrics of the song. Then, Joe saw his little sister Gigi standing next to him. She said, "Is that the song you're writing?" He nodded and said, "Yes, it is. I'm writing a special song. Do you want to help me write it?" Gigi smiled excitedly and nodded. She said, "Yes, I do!" So, Joe and Gigi wrote a song together. As they sang the song, they both felt very grateful to have each other. They knew that this special song was something they would always remember.

One day, a wise old cat saw a little bird. The bird was hungry. The cat wanted to help the bird. So, the cat said, "I will feed you, little bird." The bird saw the cat's motion and was happy. The cat gave the bird some food. They became friends. They played and laughed together. The bird said, "Thank you, wise cat, for helping me." But one day, the cat was very hungry too. There was no food for the cat. The cat looked at the bird. The cat thought, "I am so hungry." The cat ate the bird. The bird was gone. The cat was not wise anymore.
