One day, a boy named Tim had a great idea. He wanted to make a joke on Wednesday. He told his friend, Sam, about his plan. Tim said, "I will make a joke, and everyone will laugh." On Wednesday, Tim went to school. He was very excited. He told Sam, "I will tell my joke now." Tim stood up and told his joke. But no one laughed. They did not understand the joke. Tim felt sad. Sam tried to help Tim. He said, "Let's try another joke." But Tim was too sad. He did not want to joke anymore. That day, Tim learned that not everyone will always laugh at his jokes.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a toy skeleton named Mr. Bones. Tom loved Mr. Bones because he was a reliable friend. They played together every day. One day, Tom and Mr. Bones went to the park. Tom said, "I feel very happy, Mr. Bones!" Mr. Bones nodded his head. They played on the swings and the slide. They had so much fun. As the sun set, Tom and Mr. Bones went back home. Tom hugged Mr. Bones and said, "Thank you for being my friend, Mr. Bones. You are the best!" Mr. Bones smiled, and they both went to sleep.

Once upon a time, there were two friends - a rabbit and a fox. They liked to spend time together and have fun. One day, the fox said to the rabbit, "Let's go and explore the underground!" The rabbit was excited and the two of them set off together. When they reached the underground, the rabbit and fox were amazed by what they saw. They saw all sorts of useful things, like tunnels and burrows, which were perfect for hiding. The two friends had a great time exploring the underground. After a while, they both decided that it was time to leave. Just before they were about to go, the rabbit stopped and said to the fox, "Let's meet here tomorrow and explore the underground again!" The fox thought this was a great idea and the two of them agreed to meet there the next day. The fox and the rabbit had lots of fun exploring the underground. And, with the useful things they found, they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a new toy. The toy could provide clay. Tim loved to play with clay. One day, Tim took his toy to the park. He made a big ball with the clay. Tim's friends came to see. They liked the clay too. They all played with the clay. At the end of the day, Tim and his friends made many clay things. They made a dog, a cat, and a house. Tim was happy. His new toy made everyone smile.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. They brought some equipment to play with, like a ball and a jump rope. Lucy was very excited to play. At the park, Lucy met a boy named Tim. Tim was very good at jumping rope. He was flexible and could jump high. Lucy wanted to learn how to jump rope too. Tim said, "I can teach you, but first we need to tie the rope." Lucy and Tim tied the jump rope to a tree. They practiced jumping and had lots of fun. Lucy's mom watched and clapped for them. After a while, Lucy got better at jumping rope. She was happy that she made a new friend and learned something new.

Once upon a time, there was a patient alligator named Ally. Ally lived in a big pond. She had many friends like the fish, the frogs, and the turtles. Ally loved to play with her friends all day long. One day, Ally and her friends wanted to have a party. They needed to prepare for the party. Ally asked her friends, "What do we need for the party?" The fish said, "We need food!" The frogs said, "We need music!" The turtles said, "We need games!" So, Ally and her friends got ready for the party. They worked together to find food, make music, and set up games. Ally was very patient as she waited for everything to be ready. Finally, the party was all set. The day of the party came, and all the animals in the pond had a great time. They ate, danced, and played games. Ally was so happy to see her friends having fun. She knew that being patient and preparing for the party was worth it.
