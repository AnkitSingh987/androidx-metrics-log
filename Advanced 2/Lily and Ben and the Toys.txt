Lily and Ben were playing pirates in the garden. They had a big chest full of toys and pretended it was their treasure. They took turns to guard it from the enemy. "Arrr, I'm the captain of this ship, and this is my chest!" Ben said, holding a wooden sword. "No, I'm the captain, and this is my chest!" Lily said, holding a plastic hook. They both wanted to be the captain and have the chest. They started to fight over it, pulling and pushing. The chest was old and weak. It broke in half and all the toys fell out. "Oh no, look what we did!" Lily said, feeling sad. "We broke our chest, and our treasure!" Ben said, feeling sorry. They looked at each other and felt bad. They did not want to fight anymore. They wanted to fix their chest and play together. "Maybe we can fix it with some tape and glue," Lily said, having an idea. "Maybe we can share the chest and the toys, and both be captains," Ben said, being wise. They agreed and hugged. They went to find some tape and glue and fixed their chest. They put their toys back in and closed it. They decided to play pirates together and have fun. "Arrr, we are the best pirate team, and this is our chest!" Lily and Ben said, smiling.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy box in his room. He loved to play with his toys all day. One day, Tim wanted to see if all his toys could fit in the toy box. Tim took all his toys out of the box. There were cars, dolls, and even a wild lion toy. He put the toys back in the box one by one. He pushed and pushed, trying to make everything fit. Finally, all the toys fit in the box! Tim was very happy. That night, Tim's mom made a yummy dinner. They had chicken, rice, and green beans. Tim told his mom about fitting all his toys in the box. His mom was proud of him. After dinner, Tim went to his room and played with his toys until bedtime.

The little girl was very weak, but she had a big dream. She wanted to be a dancer! Every day the little girl watched the dancers on TV, and she could feel the rhythm in her heart. The little girl's mum saw how much she liked dancing, so she asked her, "Do you want to learn how to dance?" The little girl was so excited and she said, "Yes, please!" So her mum took her to a special dance class. The little girl was taught how to move her body to make different kinds of shapes and how to listen to the music. At the end of the class, the little girl was so happy that she asked, "Can I eat some chocolate now?" Her mum smiled and said, "Yes, you can eat some chocolate to give you energy. Dancing makes you very tired!" So the little girl ate a bit of chocolate, and then she was ready to try dancing again. She practiced until she was very tired, but with each practice she became less weak and more confident. She knew one day she would be a great dancer!

Once upon a time, there was a chubby little dog named Max. Max loved to wear his red cape when he played outside. Every morning, Max would wake up early to see the beautiful sunrise. One morning, Max met a little bird named Lily. Lily said, "Hi Max! I like your cape. Can I wear it too?" Max said, "Sure Lily, we can share my cape!" So, Max and Lily took turns wearing the red cape as they played together. As the sun began to set, Max and Lily said goodbye to each other. "I had fun playing with you today," said Max. "Me too," said Lily. "Let's play together again tomorrow at sunrise!" And so, Max and Lily became the best of friends, sharing the red cape and playing together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to play in her room all day. One day, she saw a cobweb in the corner of her room. It was big and dusty. Sue wanted her room to be clean and ready for her friends to come over. So, she asked her mom for help. Her mom came and wiped away the cobweb. Sue was very happy. Later, Sue accidentally spilled juice on the floor. She was scared her mom would be mad. But her mom said, "I forgive you, accidents happen." Sue felt better and they cleaned up the mess together.
