Once upon a time, there was a tall skeleton named Mr. Bones. He lived in a small house in the woods. Mr. Bones had many friends, but his best friend was a little girl named Sally. One day, Sally came to visit Mr. Bones. She saw that his house was very messy. "Mr. Bones, let's sort your things and make your house clean," said Sally. Mr. Bones agreed, and they started to sort all the things in his house. They put toys in one box, books in another box, and clothes in another box. Soon, Mr. Bones' house was clean and neat. After they finished sorting, Mr. Bones and Sally sat down to rest. "Thank you, Sally," said Mr. Bones. "Now my house is clean and nice." Sally smiled and said, "You're welcome, Mr. Bones. It's good to help friends and keep our homes clean." And from that day on, Mr. Bones always kept his house clean and neat.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Ann. One day, her mom asked her to pick up some groceries. She was very excited to help out, so she asked what she had to get. Her mom said she needed spaghetti. Ann shrugged, she knew where to find it. When Ann went to the store it was very busy. She was a bit overwhelmed and didn't know what to do. The aisles filled with food made it seem very difficult. After a while, Ann found the spaghetti. She was so proud of herself for finding it. On her way home, Ann started to get very hungry. She thought about how yummy the spaghetti would taste. She couldn't wait to get home and eat it. When Ann and her mom got home, she helped make the spaghetti. When dinner was finally ready, Ann and her family all sat around the table. She had a big smile on her face as they ate the delicious spaghetti. They all enjoyed the meal and it was a wonderful end to their day.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Rosie and Simon. Rosie was very silly and always made Simon laugh. Every day, Simon would look for Rosie so they could play together. One day, Rosie presented Simon with a gift. Simon didn't know what it was, but Rosie explained it was a symbol of her love for him. Simon thought it was powerful and felt very special. Rosie asked Simon if he wanted to play a game. Simon said yes, and Rosie told him to display the gift in different places around the room. Every time he did, Simon could feel a wave of love coming from Rosie. Simon had never experienced anything so powerful before. Every day, Rosie and Simon would play the game. It brought them so much joy and laughter. Whenever they were feeling sad, they would remember the love they shared. The End!

Once upon a time, there was an ordinary spider. It lived in a small house. The spider had many friends. One day, the spider found a big apple. It was very happy. But the apple started to rot. The spider did not like the bad smell. The spider and its friends worked together. They moved the bad apple far away. Then, they played and had fun. The ordinary spider was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a blue seed. The blue seed wanted to grow big and tall. It lived in a small garden with many other seeds. The blue seed had a dream to grow into a big blue tree. One day, a little girl came to the garden. She saw the blue seed and thought it was special. The little girl put the blue seed in the ground. She gave it water and sun. The blue seed was happy. As days went by, the blue seed started to grow. It grew into a big blue tree. The little girl loved to play near the tree. She would stamp her feet and dance around the tree. The blue tree and the little girl were very happy together.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. One was called Jack, and the other was called Tom. Jack and Tom loved to play together. They shared a toy car, and made lots of noise! One day, they were playing with their car and it got stuck in the mud. Jack started to worry, but Tom said: "Don't worry, Jack. We'll find a way to help!" Jack smiled, and together they looked for things to help free their car. They found a big stick, and put it in the mud in front of the car. They then pushed, and the car came out! When they had finished, Tom looked at Jack and said: "Sometimes, a loud and noisy way can help us solve our problems." Jack nodded and smiled. What Tom had said was true. Through working together, they had found a way to help, and it was loud!
