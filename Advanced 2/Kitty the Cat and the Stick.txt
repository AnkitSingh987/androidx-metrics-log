Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play games with her friends. One day, she saw her friends playing a new game. Kitty wanted to play too, but she didn't understand the game. Kitty asked her friend, Doggy, "How do you play this game?" Doggy said, "It's easy, just jump over the stick and run back. But you have to be fast!" Kitty tried to play, but she was not fast. She was too chubby to jump over the stick. Kitty kept trying to play the game, but she couldn't. She felt sad and cried. Her friends told her, "Kitty, you need to eat less and move more. Then you will be fast and can play the game." But Kitty didn't listen. She kept eating and not moving. She never learned to play the game and was always sad. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends when they try to help you. If Kitty had listened, she would have been able to play the game and be happy.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a small flexible snake named Sammy. Sammy loved to play with his friends, but he was afraid to climb up high in the trees. One day, Sammy's best friend, a little bird named Billy, said, "Sammy, come up and see the view from the tree! It's so pretty!" Sammy was scared but wanted to be brave, so he climbed up the tree very slowly. As he climbed higher and higher, he could see more and more of the forest. When he reached the top, he saw the beautiful view. The sky was blue, and the trees were green and tall. Sammy was so happy that he forgot to be scared. But then, Sammy looked down and saw how high he was. He started to weep because he was scared to climb down. Billy saw Sammy weep and said, "Don't worry, Sammy! I will help you." Billy held onto Sammy's tail, and they both climbed down the tree together. When they reached the ground, Sammy was so happy and thanked Billy for helping him. From that day on, Sammy was no longer scared of heights, and he loved to see the view from the trees with his friend Billy.

Once upon a time, a mommy and a daddy went for a walk in the park. The mommy stopped and said, “Listen and I will tell you a story.” The daddy looked around and said, “This looks like a great place for a story. Where does it start?” The mommy said, “This is the history of a very obedient prince. He always listens to his parents.” The mommy continued, “The prince was such a good boy. He was so obedient that he was given a very special gift!” The daddy looked curious, “What did he get?” The mommy smiled and said, “You’ll have to wait and see!” At the end of the story, the dad found out that the prince was given a special crown as a reward for being so obedient. The king and queen were very proud of him!

Once upon a time, there was a boy who wanted to climb a tall tree. He wanted to get to the top and see the world. No one believed he could make it to the top, so the boy was determined to prove them wrong. He tried and tried, but no matter how hard he tried, he couldn't make it. He started to get tired and his stomach felt funny. His head was all spinning and he was feeling dizzy! Finally the boy decided to surrender. He stopped trying and decided to come down. He climbed down slowly and carefully, until he was safely back on the ground. He felt relieved, but he also felt sad because he hadn't managed to get to the top. But the boy soon realised that even though he hadn't reached the top, he had still achieved something very special. He was brave for trying, and that made him proud.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had many toys, but his favorite toy was a big red truck. One day, Tim's big red truck got stuck under a big file of books. Tim tried to get his truck, but it was too hard. He started to complain. "Mommy, my truck is stuck!" he cried. His mommy came to help him. "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you," she said. Together, they moved the books and got the truck out. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, mommy!" Tim played with his big red truck all day and never complained again.
