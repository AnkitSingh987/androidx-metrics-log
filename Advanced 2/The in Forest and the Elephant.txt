Once, a big elephant with a long trunk lived in a forest. He had many friends. One day, he saw a small hole in a tree. He wanted to see what was inside the hole. He tried to put his trunk in the hole, but it did not fit. His trunk was too big for the small hole. The elephant asked his friend, the bird, to help him. The bird could see inside the hole because she was small. She flew near the hole and looked inside. She saw a small, shiny stone. The bird picked up the stone with her beak and gave it to the elephant. The elephant was happy to see the shiny stone. He thanked his friend, the bird. The bird was happy to help her friend. They played together in the forest, and the elephant kept the shiny stone with him always.

Once upon a time, Aaron and his mommy took a walk in the park. Aaron saw something far away. He pointed to it and asked his mommy, "What's that?" His mommy smiled and said, "That's a fence. Let's go see it!" So, Aaron and his mommy went to the fence. It was so long that Aaron had to stretch his neck to see the top. Aaron thought the fence looked strange. He asked his mommy, "Why is the fence so tall?" His mommy said, "It's to keep something hidden away from peoples' eyes." Aaron asked, "What is hidden?" His mommy smiled and said, "You'll see soon enough." And they did! Behind the fence was a beautiful garden with colorful flowers. Aaron's eyes went big with delight! He asked, "Can I go in?" His mommy grinned and said, "Of course!" So, Aaron went inside the garden and found that it was full of wonders. He explored it for hours, and he never wanted to leave. The end.

Once upon a time there were two friends, John and Tina. They were playing together and having lots of fun. John said to Tina, "Would you like a nice, fair couch?" Tina was so excited! She said, "Yes please! Can I have it?" John said, "Of course! I will give it to you." So John gave Tina a fair couch. It was so soft and comfortable. Tina hugged it and said, "Thank you, John! I love it!" John was so glad that Tina liked the couch. They kept playing together and had lots more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a man. This man was very careless. He would always drop things and not pick them up. One day, he was walking in the park and he dropped his hat. A little girl saw him drop his hat. The little girl said, "Hey, man! You dropped your hat!" The careless man did not hear her. He kept walking away. The girl picked up the hat and ran after the man. She wanted to give it back to him. The girl caught up to the man and said, "Here is your hat. You dropped it." The man looked at the girl and said, "Thank you." From that day on, the man was not careless anymore. He would always listen when someone talked to him.

Max and Sarah were both very curious. On Sunday morning, Sarah asked Max, "Where are you going?". Max replied, "I'm going to the park. Do you want to come?" Sarah answered, "Yes! I would love to go to the park." Max grabbed his duffle bag and the two of them quickly ran outside. When they arrived at the park, they both spoke excitedly as they saw all the exciting things they could do. They ran to the swings and had fun playing. After that, they decided to go to the slide. They had such a wonderful time, they wanted to stay at the park all day. On their way home, Max and Sarah both spoke about the wonderful day they had. They agreed that Sunday was the best day for a park adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play and find things in his big house. One day, Tim went to the old room upstairs to look for something new to play with. In the old room, he saw a big, old box. Tim opened the box and found an ancient uniform. It was very old and had many colors. Tim put on the uniform and felt very happy. He played in the old room all day, pretending to be a brave hero. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's toy bear, Mr. Cuddles, began to talk! "Tim," said Mr. Cuddles, "that uniform is magic. It makes your toys come to life!" Tim was very surprised, but he loved his new talking friend. They played and talked all day, and Tim's life was never the same again.
