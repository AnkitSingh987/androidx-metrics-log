Once upon a time there were two friends, Max and Muffy. They were best friends who liked to play together. One day, Max had an idea. He said to Muffy, "Let's make a game!" Muffy thought this was a fair idea, so they set out to make a game. First, they looked for some paper and a pencil. Then Muffy took a sheet of paper and Max used his pencil to draw a pattern. Then Max asked Muffy if they could make a printed copy. Muffy said yes and she found the printer. She asked her dad if they could use it and he permitted it. The two friends printed their pattern and cut it into four pieces. Then Max and Muffy took turns trying to join the pieces together in the same pattern they had printed. At first it was tricky but they worked together and eventually they managed to do it. They were very happy with their game and said it was fair, no one had won or lost. That's how Max and Muffy created a game of their own!

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy shampoo to wash their hair. Lily saw a big, heavy bottle of shampoo on the shelf. She asked her mom, "Can we get this one, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can get that one. It is heavy, but you can help me carry it." Lily was happy and picked up the heavy bottle of shampoo. They went to the cashier and bought the shampoo. When they got home, Lily took a bath. As she washed her hair with the new shampoo, she started to sing a fun song. Her mom heard her singing and smiled. The heavy shampoo bottle made Lily's hair clean and soft, and her singing made bath time fun for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a friend named Tom. They liked to play and laugh together. One day, they found an unusual thing in the garden. It was a bulb. They did not know what it was, but they thought it was funny. Lily and Tom took the bulb to Lily's mom. She told them it was a flower bulb. They could plant it in the ground and watch it grow. Lily and Tom were very happy. They dug a hole and put the bulb in it. Then, they covered it with dirt and gave it some water. Every day, they went to the garden to look at the bulb. They were very excited to see it grow. One day, a beautiful flower grew from the bulb. It was big and colorful. Lily and Tom laughed and clapped their hands. They were so proud of their unusual flower. They knew that they had done a good job.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy train. One day, he got a new toy called a subway. The subway was big and fun to play with. One day, Tim took his subway outside to play. It started to rain, and the subway got wet. Tim was sad, but he knew he had to bring his subway back inside to make it dry. As Tim played with his subway more and more, he wanted to increase his fun. So, he asked his mom for more toy trains to play with. His mom said yes, and Tim was very happy. Now, Tim could have even more fun with his subway and toy trains.

One day, a big truck had to deliver a very big box. The truck driver knew he had to be careful because the box was dangerous. He had to deliver the box on Tuesday. As the truck driver was driving, he saw a little girl on the road. He stopped the truck and asked, "Little girl, why are you here all alone?" The little girl said, "I am lost. Can you help me find my mom?" The truck driver wanted to help the little girl, but he also had to deliver the dangerous box. Then, something unexpected happened. The little girl said, "I know where your dangerous box needs to go. I can show you the way if you help me find my mom." The truck driver and the little girl worked together. They found the little girl's mom and delivered the dangerous box safely. They were both very happy and became good friends.
