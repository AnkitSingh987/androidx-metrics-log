One Sunday, a little boy named Tim was very tired. He had played all day with his toys and friends. His mom saw him and said, "Tim, it's time for a nap." Tim did not want to nap, but his mom was right. He went to his room and lay down on his bed. As he closed his eyes, he saw a big, round moon in his dream. The moon was smiling at him. In his dream, Tim could fly. He flew up to the moon and said, "Hello, Moon!" The moon smiled and pointed at the stars. Tim looked and saw his friends playing. They were all having fun. Tim joined them and played until he was not tired anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat named Max. Max loved to play in the sun all day. One day, Max was very hungry. He wanted to find a sandwich to eat. Max went outside and looked around. He looked under the big tree and in the tall grass. He could not find a sandwich. Max felt sad. But then, Max heard something. It was a little bird singing in the tree. Max asked the bird, "Have you seen a sandwich?" The bird said, "Yes, I saw a sandwich in the house." Max was happy. He went inside the house and found the sandwich on the table. Max ate the sandwich and felt full. From that day on, Max and the little bird were good friends. They played together every day. Max was not silly anymore. He knew that if he needed help, he could always ask his friend, the little bird.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Bob. Bob loved to swim all day long in his pond. One day, Bob saw a bookshelf near the pond. The bookshelf was very attractive and had many colorful books. Bob's friend, Tim the turtle, came by and saw the bookshelf too. Tim said, "Wow, that's a pretty bookshelf! Let's look at the books together!" So, Bob and Tim swam to the bookshelf and looked at the books. They saw a book about sharing and read it together. After reading the book, Bob and Tim learned that sharing is good. They decided to share their food and toys with their friends in the pond. All the animals were happy and played together. The moral of the story is that sharing makes everyone happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a photo in her room. In the photo, she saw a small bird with a hurt wing. Sue felt sad for the bird and wanted to help it heal. She showed the photo to her mom and asked if they could find the bird. Sue and her mom went to the park to look for the bird. They found it near a tree, still with a hurt wing. Sue's mom told her to be careful and not to be careless, so they could help the bird heal. They took the bird home and took care of it, giving it food and water. After some days, the bird's wing was better. Sue and her mom took the bird back to the park and let it fly away. Sue felt happy that she could help the bird heal. She learned that being careful and kind is important to help others.

Jill was so excited - she was going to the big stadium! She rushed around the house getting her things together. She wanted to be there early. When Jill arrived at the stadium she saw a very kind man. He was holding lots of tickets. She asked him for a ticket, but he shook his head. Jill was so disappointed. She had been looking forward to going to the stadium all day. She thought that the man would understand and be kind. But he wasn't. He said no tickets were left. Jill had to hurry home. She was sad and a little scared. It wasn't a very kind ending for her day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to pour water into cups. She had many different cups in many different colors. Every day, she would pour water into the cups and wait for her friends to come and drink. One sunny day, Mia poured water into her different cups and went outside to wait for her friends. She saw a big, pretty butterfly flying near the cups. The butterfly landed on one of the cups and started to drink the water. Mia was very happy to see the butterfly enjoying the water. But then, something unexpected happened. The butterfly started to change and grow bigger. It turned into a beautiful fairy! The fairy smiled at Mia and said, "Thank you for the water, Mia. I was a butterfly, but your kindness turned me back into a fairy. As a thank you, I will grant you one wish." Mia thought for a moment and wished for more different cups to share water with her friends and all the creatures in her garden. The fairy waved her wand, and Mia's wish came true. From that day on, Mia and her friends enjoyed water in their different cups, and they all lived happily ever after.
