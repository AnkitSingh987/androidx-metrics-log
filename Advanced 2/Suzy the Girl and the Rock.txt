Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Suzy. She was 3 years old, and filled with curiosity about the world around her. One day, Suzy went to the playground with her Mom and Dad. Suzy saw the big slide and ran towards it. She wanted to go to the top, but it was a bit too high. "Can I climb up?" Suzy asked her Mom. "No, sweetheart," said her Mom. "You have to wait until I can help you." Suzy was a bit sad. Then she spotted something else. It was a big, smooth rock. "I want to go to the top of this," said Suzy. "No, Suzy," said Dad. He kneeled down to Suzy's level and said "Suzy, it's not safe to climb up the rock. You might slip and fall." Suzy was quiet for a moment. Then she looked up at Dad and said "Can I pretend I'm going to the top?" Dad smiled. "Sure you can," he said. Suzy lay on her stomach, pretending to climb up the rock. She giggled and laughed, pretending she was reaching the top. She had so much fun, she almost forgot that she couldn't really go to the top.

Once upon a time, in a small ordinary town, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play with her friends and explore the town. One sunny day, she found a tiny lost kitten in the park. The kitten was scared and hungry. Lily wanted to help the kitten find its way home. She took the kitten's paw and began to lead it through the town. They walked past the big tree, the red house, and the blue store. The kitten started to feel safe with Lily. Finally, they found the kitten's home. The kitten's mommy and daddy were so happy to see their little one. They thanked Lily for being so kind and helpful. From that day on, Lily and the kitten became the best of friends, and they played together in the ordinary town every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Ben. He loved to explore and discover new things. One day, he found a secret box under his bed. He opened the box, and saw a tiny, little key inside. Ben got so excited. He looked around his bedroom and quickly found something to unlock the key. It was a small door to what Ben believed was a magical world. He grabbed the key, ran to the door and opened it. Once inside, Ben noticed that he was getting smaller and smaller. His legs were shrinking. His arms were shrinking. He was getting really scared. He started to cry and shouted "Help me!" Suddenly, he heard someone calling his name. It was his mom. She came running to him and asked, "What's wrong Ben? You look miserable". Ben told his mom that he had found a secret box with a key and opened a door. His mom smiled. "Don't worry Ben. It's okay. Your shrinking is a good thing. And it can be your secret. Nobody else needs to know about it." Ben nodded and hugged his mom. He was so relieved. From then on, he never told anyone else about his special shrinking secret.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was a good dog who liked to help his friends. One day, Max saw a little bird on the ground. The bird was not flying and looked very sad. Max knew he needed to support the bird to make it happy again. Max took the bird to a safe place away from the busy road. He found a small box that looked like a jail, but he made it nice and cozy for the bird. He put soft grass inside and made sure the bird was comfortable. Max then went to find some food for the bird. He found worms and bugs, which the little bird loved to eat. After a few days, the bird started to feel better. It was not dead anymore! The bird was so happy that Max had helped and supported it. The bird thanked Max and flew away to join its family in the sky. Max felt proud of himself for helping his new friend. And from that day on, Max and the bird became the best of friends.
