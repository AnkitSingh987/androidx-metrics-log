Once upon a time, there was a bossy lawyer named Tom. He loved to attach papers together. He would go to the park and attach papers all day long. He liked to tell people what to do. One day, Tom met a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing with her toy car. Tom wanted to attach papers to Sue's toy car. Sue did not want that. Tom was sad but he still tried to make her agree. Tom kept trying to attach papers to the toy car. He did not see the big hole in the ground. Tom fell into the hole because he was too bossy. Sue was safe with her toy car. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a proud goat. The goat liked to play with his friends. They played in the sun and had lots of fun. One day, the goat saw a big rock. He wanted to jump on it. He tried and tried, but he could not get on the rock. The goat started to cry. Then, a bird came and said, "Do not cry, goat. I will help you." The bird picked up the goat and put him on the rock. The goat was happy and stopped crying. But, the rock was not a rock! It was a big turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, goat. Let's be friends." The goat and the turtle played with the bird, and they all had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a secret box that she loved very much. Inside the box, she kept her most useful things. Every day, she would polish the box to make it shiny and pretty. One day, Lucy's friend, Tom, came to play. He saw the shiny box and wanted to know what was inside. Lucy told him it was a secret and that he could not look. Tom was sad, but he played with Lucy all day. He could not stop thinking about the secret box. The next day, Lucy and Tom played outside. It started to rain, and they got very wet. Lucy had an idea. She ran inside and got her secret box. She opened it and took out two useful things: a big umbrella and a warm towel. Tom was so happy! They both stayed dry and warm, thanks to Lucy's secret box.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Max lived in a small house. Max was always alert and liked to help his family. One day, Max's family set a new bathroom in the house. Max was very excited about it. Max wanted to help with the new bathroom. He saw his dad put a big, soft rug on the floor. Max picked up a small towel with his mouth and set it on the rug. His dad smiled and said, "Good job, Max!" Every day, Max would check the bathroom to make sure it was clean and safe for his family. He felt very proud to help. Max's family loved their new bathroom, and they were very happy to have such an alert little helper.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to watch television every day. One day, while he was watching his favorite show, the television made a loud noise and turned off. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. His mom came into the room and saw the broken television. "Oh no, Tim," she said. "The television is very fragile. We must be careful with it." Tim felt bad and asked his mom, "What can we do now?" Mom had a surprise for Tim. She took him to the store, and they bought a new television. Tim was so happy and excited. When they got home, they set up the new television and watched a fun movie together. From that day on, Tim was always careful with the fragile television.

Once upon a time, a charming little boy named Tim found a penny. He believed that the penny was magic. Tim was very happy and showed the penny to his friends. They all played with the penny and believed it was magic too. One day, while playing with the penny, Tim dropped it. The penny rolled away and went into a small hole. Tim and his friends were very sad. They tried to get the penny back but they could not reach it. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse came out of the hole with the penny. The mouse gave the penny back to Tim. Tim and his friends were very surprised and happy. They thanked the mouse and played with the penny again. From that day on, they believed that the mouse was magic too.
