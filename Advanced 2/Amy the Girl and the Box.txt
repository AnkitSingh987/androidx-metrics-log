Title: Amy the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a toy box full of fun toys. One day, she found a new toy. It was a triangle. She loved playing with it all day. Amy had a friend named Ben. Ben saw Amy playing with the triangle. He felt jealous. He wanted to play with the triangle too. But he was too shy to ask Amy if he could play with it. Amy saw Ben looking sad. She asked him, "Do you want to play with my triangle?" Ben said yes. Amy was kind and said, "I will lend it to you." Ben was so happy. They both played with the triangle together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a magic stick, which she called her "staff". Lily loved her staff and was always very careful with it. She took it with her everywhere she went. One day, Lily went to the park with her staff. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. So, she used her staff to help her. She put the staff on the tree and climbed up, up, up. When she got to the top, she could see the whole park. After playing in the park, Lily went home with her staff. She was happy and tired. She put her staff next to her bed and went to sleep. Lily had a good day with her magic staff, and she knew they would have many more fun days together.

The sun shined bright in the sky and the birds sang outside the window. Little Jack was very sad. He had been crying for hours and rubbing his eyes that were now swollen and red. Mommy saw Jack and knelt down beside him. She put her arms around him, hugged him tight and said, "Don't weep my sweet boy, tell me what's wrong?" Jack sniffed and said, "I'm tired and no one will come and give me a nap." Mommy smiled and said, "Well I'm here now, so I will give you a nap and we can go on an adventure afterwards!" Jack was excited and he held his mommy's hand tight as they went up to his room. They tucked him in under the warm quilt and kissed his forehead. Soon Jack was fast asleep in a dream world full of magic and adventure. Mommy watched over him with a smile and a loving heart, happy to see that her son was now feeling much better.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved cocoa. Every day, he would drink a big cup of cocoa after playing outside. One day, Tim fell down and hurt his knee. He was very sad and ashamed. His mom saw him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. Your knee will heal soon." She gave him a cup of cocoa to make him feel better. The next day, Tim went outside to play again. He saw a big dog and wanted to share his cocoa with the dog. But the dog didn't like cocoa! The dog got mad and chased Tim. Tim ran away, but he dropped his cup of cocoa. He was very sad and his knee still hurt.

Little Wes was walking through the garden looking for something fun. Suddenly, he spotted a big muffin sitting on the grass. His eyes lit up. He quickly ran over to it and took a big bite. It was delicious! Little Wes was so happy that he started running around the garden, even faster than before. He was having so much fun, he didn't even notice that he had been running away from something. Suddenly, he heard a loud laugh coming from behind him. He stopped running and slowly turned around. There, standing in front of him, was an evil witch. She had a loud, funny laugh, and she was looking very mischievous. Little Wes knew he was in trouble. He was about to escape when the witch pointed her finger at him. Suddenly, he felt a powerful force pull him towards the witch. However, Little Wes was brave and determined. He closed his eyes tightly, and with a big burst of energy he was gone—escaping the clutches of the evil witch.
