Title: Anna and Ben and the Car

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys. They have many cars and trucks and buses. They like to match them by color and size. One day, they find a new toy in their room. It is a yellow taxi. It has four wheels and a sign on top. It is shiny and pretty. "Wow, look at this!" Anna says. "It is a taxi. I like taxis. They go fast and beep beep." "Me too!" Ben says. "Can I play with it?" "Sure, you can play with it. But we have to match it with another toy. Which toy do you think matches the taxi?" Anna asks. Ben looks around. He sees a red fire truck, a blue police car, a green tractor, and a white ambulance. He thinks hard. "I know!" he says. "The police car matches the taxi. They both have signs on top. And they both help people." "You are right!" Anna says. "The police car and the taxi are a good match. They are both good toys. Let's play with them together." Anna and Ben smile. They make the taxi and the police car drive around the room. They pretend they are drivers and passengers. They have fun with their new toy and their old toy. They are happy twins.

Once there was a naughty girl named Mary. Mary always wanted things her way and she never listened to her parents. One day, Mary's parents decided to get her a cup of coffee. Mary was happy, but she wanted it to be really spicy. Her parents said no, but Mary spoiled and started to cry. Finally, her parents agreed to make her the spicy coffee. So they made it for her and handed it to her. Mary tasted the coffee and shouted, "It's too spicy!" and started to cry. Her parents tried to talk to her, but Mary was very naughty. She wouldn't listen to them, so in the end, she couldn't drink the coffee. Mary got really angry and had to leave the room. Her parents were very disappointed with her behaviour and decided that she had been spoiled too much. Moral of the story: Don't spoil or bad things will happen!

Once there was a charming girl named Lola. She loved to look at patterns in nature and in books. One day, she was looking at a pattern on her fence when her mom came over. She said it was time to go home. Lola felt embarrassed. She quickly grabbed her things and went inside. That night, Lola decided to use the pattern as an idea for a project she was working on. She cut out the pattern from the fence, drew it on paper and added her own charm. The next day, she proudly showed off her creation to her mom. Her mom praised her efforts and they both shared a laugh. Lola was no longer embarrassed and was happy to have made something beautiful. She remembered to always have confidence in her abilities, even when things seem daunting.

One day, a nice girl named Lucy went to collect rocks. She loved to look for rocks with pretty colors. While she was looking, she met a boy named Tim. "Hi, I'm Tim!" he said. "What are you doing?" "I'm collecting rocks," Lucy said. "Do you want to help me?" Tim and Lucy looked for rocks together. They found a big rock that had a paper under it. The paper had a law written on it. The law said, "Whoever finds this paper will get a big surprise!" They were very excited. They showed the paper to their mom and dad. Their parents said, "Let's see what the big surprise is!" Suddenly, a big truck came down the street. It stopped in front of their house. The truck was full of ice cream! The driver said, "You found the paper! This ice cream is all for you!" Lucy and Tim were so happy. They shared the ice cream with all their friends. It was the best day ever!

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play with his friends. One day, they had a race to see who was the fastest. Spot was very excited. During the race, Spot tripped and fell. He was hurt and frightened. His friend, a kind cat named Fluffy, came to help him. "Don't worry, Spot," said Fluffy. "I will help you heal." Fluffy stayed with Spot until he felt better. Slowly, Spot's hurt went away and he was not frightened anymore. Spot and Fluffy played together and became the best of friends. They knew that if one of them got hurt again, the other would be there to help them heal. And they lived happily ever after.
