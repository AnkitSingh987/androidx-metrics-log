Once upon a time, there was a sweet girl named Lily. She had a little sister named Emma. They loved to play together in their big, green yard. One sunny day, Lily and Emma were playing with their toys. They saw a pretty, red bird up in a tree. The bird was singing a happy song. Lily and Emma wanted to be friends with the bird. Lily found a long stick and tried to reach the bird. But the stick was too short. So, Emma had an idea. She said, "Let's snap this stick and make it longer!" They snapped the stick and made it longer. Then, they reached the bird and it came down to play with them. The bird was very happy to be friends with Lily and Emma. They played all day long. When the sun went down, the bird flew back to its tree. Lily and Emma went inside their house, excited to play with their new friend again the next day.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play with cars. They had many cars of different colors and sizes, but their favorite was a big red car that their dad gave them. The big red car was expensive and shiny, and it could make loud noises and go very fast. One day, Lily and Ben decided to race their big red car with their other cars. They made a track with books and pillows and lined up their cars at the start. Lily said, "I will drive the big red car, and you can drive the blue car." Ben said, "No, I want to drive the big red car, it is better than the blue car." Lily and Ben started to argue and pull the big red car from each other. "Stop it, you two!" their mom said, coming into the room. "You have to share your toys and take turns. If you can't play nicely, I will take the big red car away." Lily and Ben did not listen to their mom and kept fighting over the big red car. Their mom shook her head and said, "Fine, then you can't play with it at all." She took the big red car and put it on a high shelf, out of their reach. Lily and Ben felt sad and angry. They did not have their favorite car anymore, and they did not have anyone to play with. They looked at their other cars, but they did not seem as fun as the big red car. They wished they had listened to their mom and shared the big red car. After a while, Lily said, "I'm sorry, Ben. I was selfish and mean. I should have let you drive the big red car sometimes." Ben said, "I'm sorry, too, Lily. I was greedy and rude. I should have let you drive the big red car sometimes." They hugged and said, "We love you, brother/sister." They went to their mom and said, "We're sorry, mom. We learned our lesson. Can we have the big red car back, please?" Their mom smiled and said, "I'm glad you apologized and made up. Yes, you can have the big red car back, but only if you promise to share it and take turns." Lily and Ben nodded and said, "We promise, mom." Their mom gave them the big red car and said, "Remember, it's not how expensive or shiny a toy is that makes it fun, it's how you play with it and who you play with." Lily and Ben thanked their mom and went back to their track. They drove the big red car together, and sometimes they switched with the blue car or the yellow car or the green car. They had a lot of fun and laughed a lot. They learned that sharing is caring and that playing nicely is better than fighting.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Sammy. She was very fair, with curly blonde hair. One day, she was out for a walk with her mommy and saw another family. They were throwing a party and it looked like a lot of fun. Sammy asked her mommy, “Can I play too?” Her mommy smiled and said, “Yes, of course, sweetheart.” So Sammy ran over and joined the family. They were celebrating a very special day - the couple had just gotten married. The bride gave Sammy a plate with spaghetti. “I'm so happy you got to marry Tony!” Sammy said with a smile. The bride laughed, “Thank you, Sammy. It was a fair thing to do.” Sammy smiled and gobbled up the spaghetti. The three of them laughed and celebrated the fair marriage.
