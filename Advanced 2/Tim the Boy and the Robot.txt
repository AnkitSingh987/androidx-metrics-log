One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his friend, the robot. The robot was big and had a lot of buttons. The robot was also very dependable. It always helped Tim when he needed it. Tim and the robot played with a ball. They threw the ball to each other and laughed. The robot was very good at catching the ball. Tim was happy to have a friend like the robot. After they played, Tim and the robot ate some snacks. They had cookies and juice. Tim said thank you to the robot for being a good friend. The robot smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim." They were very happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tim. Tim loved to balance on things. He would balance on chairs, tables, and even his toys. One day, Tim saw a big rock and thought it would be fun to balance on it. Tim took a trip to the rock with his friends, a dog named Sam and a bird named Sue. They all wanted to see if they could balance on the rock too. They played a fair game to see who would go first. Tim won the game, so he climbed up to balance on the rock. As Tim balanced on the rock, he saw a shiny ball far away. He wanted to play with it, but he knew he had to be careful not to fall. Tim slowly moved his paws and reached for the ball. He got it! Tim was so happy that he could balance and get the ball. His friends clapped for him, and they all took turns balancing on the rock and playing with the shiny ball.

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to eat some hot soup. She went to the kitchen and saw a big, red bowl on the table. Mia was very hungry and couldn't wait to eat. Mia's mom said, "Be careful, Mia. The soup is very hot, and the bowl might break." But Mia didn't listen. She wanted to eat the soup right away. She picked up the bowl and started to walk to her chair. As Mia walked, the hot soup made the bowl very hot too. Mia felt the heat and got scared. She dropped the bowl, and it broke on the floor. The hot soup spilled everywhere. Mia felt sad that she didn't listen to her mom. Mia's mom helped her clean up the mess. They found a new bowl and put more soup in it. This time, Mia listened to her mom and waited for the soup to cool down. She ate her soup slowly and carefully, so she wouldn't break the bowl again.

One day, a little girl named Sue was feeling miserable. She was sad because her favorite rag doll had a big tear. Sue wanted to cheer up, so she decided to cook something yummy with her mom. "Mom, can we cook cookies?" Sue asked. Her mom said, "Yes, let's cook cookies together!" They put on their aprons and started to mix the cookie dough. Sue felt a little better as they cooked. Suddenly, while they were cooking, the rag doll started to move! It jumped off the chair and onto the table. Sue and her mom were very surprised. The rag doll picked up a spoon and started to help cook the cookies. They all laughed and had fun cooking together. In the end, Sue's day wasn't miserable anymore, and she had a yummy treat to share with her rag doll.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a small camera that she loved very much. She would take pictures of everything she saw. One day, she went to the park with her mom and dad. She was very happy and grateful to be there. While at the park, Amy saw a big tree with a hole in it. She thought, "I wonder if my camera will fit in the hole." She walked up to the tree and put her camera in the hole. To her surprise, it fit perfectly! She took a picture of the inside of the tree. Then, something unexpected happened. When Amy pulled her camera out of the hole, there was a new picture on it. The picture was not of the inside of the tree, but of a secret party with animals! They were dancing and playing games. Amy showed the picture to her mom and dad, and they all laughed. They never knew that the tree was hiding such a fun secret.
