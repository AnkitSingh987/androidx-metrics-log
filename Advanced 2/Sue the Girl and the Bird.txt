Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to spend time with her dad in his work room. One day, Sue saw a small, fragile bird outside the window. The bird had a hurt wing and could not fly. Sue wanted to help the bird, so she asked her dad what to do. Her dad said, "We can make a safe home for the bird." So they got some wood and nails to build a little house. Sue was very happy to help her dad. They worked hard and made a nice house for the bird. They put it outside and the bird went in to rest. But then, something unexpected happened! The bird started to change. It grew bigger and bigger, and its hurt wing got better. The bird was not a fragile little bird anymore. It was a big, strong bird! Sue and her dad were very surprised. The bird flew away and Sue waved goodbye. Sue was happy she could help the bird, and she knew it would be okay now.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a powerful dog named Max. Max had a big chain around his neck. He was very strong and could run very fast. One day, Max saw two toys in the park. One was a red ball, and the other was a blue bone. Max had to choose which toy he wanted to play with. He thought very hard about it. In the end, Max chose the red ball. He played with the ball all day and had lots of fun. His powerful legs made him run very fast, and his chain jingled as he ran. Max was very happy with his choice.

Once upon a time, there was a little pet dog named Spot. Spot was a happy dog, but sometimes he was impatient. He wanted to play all day long and did not like to wait. One day, Spot saw a big ball in the yard. He wanted to play with it, but it was stuck under a tree. Spot tried to get the ball, but he could not reach it. He started to suffer because he wanted the ball so bad. Just then, a little bird flew down and pushed the ball out for Spot. The bird and Spot became friends and played together all day. The unexpected thing was that the bird was also impatient, but they had fun together. Spot learned that sometimes, good things happen when you wait.

One day, a curious little bunny went for a walk. He saw a cop standing near a big tree. The cop was tall and had a big hat. The bunny hopped closer to see what the cop was doing. The cop was folding a piece of paper. He made it into a little boat. The bunny watched the cop put the boat in a puddle. The boat floated on the water. The bunny wanted to fold a boat too. The cop gave the bunny a piece of paper. The bunny tried to fold the paper, but it was hard. The cop helped the bunny and they made a little boat together. The bunny was happy and they played with their boats all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Toto. Toto loved to whistle and eat meat. He lived in a small house with his friend, a big cat named Milo. They played together all day long. One day, Toto found a big piece of meat on the ground. Toto was very happy and whistled a happy song. He wanted to share the meat with Milo. But, when they started to eat the meat, it tasted terrible. They did not like it at all. Milo and Toto didn't know that the meat was bad. They got sick from eating the terrible meat. They felt very bad and could not play anymore. They learned to be careful with what they eat.

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow armchair. It was very happy and loved to be sat on by people. The armchair lived in a small house with a nice family. The family had a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily was playing with her toys in the living room. She looked at the yellow armchair and said, "I want to marry you, armchair!" The armchair felt very special and happy. Lily put a pretty dress on the armchair and pretended they were getting married. After their pretend wedding, Lily and the yellow armchair sat together and played with toys. They had so much fun and laughed a lot. The yellow armchair loved being Lily's friend and they lived happily ever after.
