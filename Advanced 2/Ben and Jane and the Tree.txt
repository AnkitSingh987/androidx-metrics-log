Once upon a time, there were two friends – Ben and Jane. They were each playing in the park on a sunny day. Jane saw Ben pinch a flower and gasped. She asked him why he had done it. Ben said he had done it because it was so yummy. Jane said that flowers were not for pinching, they were for touching. She then showed him a special patch of grass to touch. Ben was not happy. He wanted to pinch something yummy. Jane thought for a moment and then remembered the apple tree. She asked Ben if he wanted to pinch the apples. They both scurried to the tree and began to pick the apples. Once the apples were ripe, Ben and Jane carefully pinched them off the tree. Then they bit into the yummy apples. All was forgiven and Ben and Jane were happy. They sat beneath the tree and laughed in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a big shower that lived in the sky. The shower had many friends, like the sun and the moon. They all liked to play games and have fun together. One day, the sun and the moon had a quarrel. They both wanted to be the best at a game they were playing. They were very competitive. The sun said, "I am the best!" The moon said, "No, I am the best!" The shower saw the quarrel and wanted to help. So, the shower spoke to the sun and the moon. "Why do you quarrel? We can all be good at the game. Let's play together and have fun!" The sun and the moon listened to the shower and stopped their quarrel. From that day on, the sun, the moon, and the shower played together happily. They learned that being friends was more important than being the best. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a man. The man was very careless. He had a big balloon. He loved his balloon so much. One day, the man wanted his balloon to increase. He blew air into the balloon. The balloon got bigger and bigger. The man was happy to see his balloon grow. But the careless man did not stop. He kept blowing air into the balloon. The balloon got too big and it popped. The man was sad because his balloon was gone. He learned to be more careful next time.

Once there was a little bee who wanted to design something beautiful. She took a piece of paper and drew a line. The bee liked what she had created and decided to make it even more beautiful. She drew circles and wavy lines. It was a very funny design. Suddenly, the bee heard her mom's voice. "Don't forget to help your friends!" her mom called. The bee was confused. She wasn't sure how to help her friends with the design she had made. But then the bee thought of something. She took the paper, and tore it in two equal halves. She gave one of the halves to her friend. The bee and her friend had so much fun working together to design something beautiful! They made a wonderful, colorful design by working together. The bee learnt an important lesson that day: working together with your friends always makes something much more beautiful and enjoyable than working alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to collect smooth rocks. He would find them near the river and put them in a big bag. The rocks were shiny and pretty. One day, Tim found a tiny jail that was made just for bugs. He thought it would be fun to put his smooth rocks inside the jail. He put one rock in, then two rocks, then three rocks. The bugs watched as he filled the jail with rocks. When the jail was full of smooth rocks, the bugs decided to play a game. They climbed on the rocks and played hide and seek. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He was happy that his smooth rocks made the bugs have fun.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, filthy kayak. It was by the water. Tim wanted to make it clean. He got a pail of water and a rag. He washed the kayak until it was shiny. Tim was happy. He put on a life vest and got in the kayak. He used a long stick to push the kayak in the water. Tim went very fast. He liked to speed in the water. The wind blew through his hair. As Tim played in the kayak, he saw a duck. The duck was also playing in the water. Tim and the duck had fun together. They raced each other. Tim loved his new kayak and his new friend, the duck.
