Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a small flower. The flower wanted to bloom and be the top flower in the garden. The flower was very competitive with the other flowers. One sunny day, the flower started to bloom. It grew bigger and bigger. The other flowers saw the flower and wanted to be the top flower too. They all tried to bloom and be the best. In the end, all the flowers in the garden bloomed and looked beautiful. The small flower was happy because it was now a big flower at the top. The garden was full of colors and all the flowers were happy.

Sara loves to sing. She sings all day long. She sings when she wakes up, when she eats, when she plays, and when she goes to bed. She sings happy songs, sad songs, silly songs, and loud songs. She sings with her toys, with her mom, with her dad, and with her cat. One day, Sara goes to the park with her mom. She sees a big slide, a swing, a sandbox, and a fountain. She runs to the slide and slides down. Whee! She runs to the swing and swings high. Wow! She runs to the sandbox and makes a castle. Yay! She runs to the fountain and sees a bird. The bird is small and brown. It has a red spot on its chest. It sits on the edge of the fountain and sings. It sings a beautiful song. Sara stops and listens. She likes the bird's song. She walks slowly to the bird and stares at it. She wants to sing with the bird. "Hello, bird," Sara says. "I like your song. Can I sing with you?" The bird looks at Sara and tilts its head. It sings again. Sara smiles and sings too. She sings a song that sounds like the bird's song. The bird and Sara sing together. They sing a beautiful song. Sara's mom watches and smiles. She thinks Sara and the bird are very cute. She takes a picture of them with her phone. She says, "Sara, you are a good singer. And you have a new friend." Sara nods and says, "Yes, mom. I have a new friend. The bird is my friend. We sing a beautiful song."

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. He had a bright collar with his name on it. He was very happy. Every day, the little dog would run around in the sunshine. He would bark and play with all his new friends. But one day, he didn't know where he was. He was scared, and he had no way of getting home. He tried to find someone who could help him, but no one seemed to know. Eventually, the little dog grew very tired. He curled up under a tree to take a nap. In the morning, the sun was bright, but the little dog was gone. He had been taken away by someone who wanted him, but he didn't know where he had gone. And so, the little dog was never seen again, and he was never able to find his way back home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lila who loved playing and exploring. One day, she came across a big shiny box and she wanted it so badly. But when she opened the box, she found a battery inside. She knew it was dangerous and she felt scared. She ran to her mom and said, "Mommy, I have the battery!" Her mom said, "Lila, it is not safe to play with it. We have to put it away." Lila was sad and she silently put the battery back in the box and promised never to touch it again. But she was still curious about the sparkly battery and she wanted to know why it wasn't safe. So Lila asked her mom, "Mommy, why do we have to forgive the battery?" Her mom said, "Because it has powers that we need to be careful with. But if we forgive it, we can keep it safe and use it for special things." Lila understood and promised to always forgive the battery and keep it safe. She was so happy to learn something new and to be able to keep it safe! The End.
