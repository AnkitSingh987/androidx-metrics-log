Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy blocks. He would build a big stack of blocks, making a tall tower. Tim was very happy when he built his tower. One day, Tim saw his friend Sue playing with her toy blocks. Sue's tower was even bigger than Tim's. Tim felt envious. He wanted to build a tower just like Sue's. So, Tim asked Sue to help him make his tower bigger. Sue showed Tim how to replace some of the blocks in his stack to make it stronger. They worked together and soon, Tim's tower was bigger than ever. Tim was no longer envious, and both friends were very happy with their tall towers. They played together all day, building and sharing their blocks.

John went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He tried, but the tree was too tall. He was frustrated. John kept walking until he found a small tree. He was so excited! He ran to it and tried to climb it. He succeeded and was able to reach the top of the tree. He smiled and looked around from the treetop. John enjoyed his view for a bit, but he then remembered that he had to return home. He quickly climbed down the tree and ran back home. When John got back home, his mom was happy. He had had an excellent adventure.

One day, Mommy and Daddy took Sally to play at the pool. It was very big and blue, and sparkly. Sally was so excited, she couldn't wait to get her toes wet. But when she got there, she noticed something disgusting in the water. A big mean fish was swimming around, teasing all the other little fishes. Sally wanted the fish to go away. She splashed and kicked and yelled, but the fish didn't move. Instead, it kept teasing the other fishes, and it made Sally feel mad and sore. Suddenly, Sally had an idea. She ran back to the car and grabbed her old smelly socks. She threw them into the pool and the big mean fish swam away. Everyone was so happy, and Sally was proud of herself. Now she could play in the pool without anyone teasing her!

Mia was playing in the living room when suddenly, her mom put a big pill on the table. "Mia," her mom said, "This pill will make you feel better. Can you take it?" Mia looked at the pill and frowned. "It's too big," she said. "You can take it slowly," her mom said. "Here, let me show you how." Mia's mom displayed how to swallow the pill. She put the pill in her mouth, followed by a glass of water. She showed Mia how to move the pill around her mouth before finally swallowing it. Mia watched as her mom did it a few more times, until she was sure she had understood how to take the pill. "Okay," Mia said. "I understand. I can take it slowly like you showed me." Mia's mom smiled and gave her a hug. "Good job, Mia," she said. "You'll feel better soon."

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside, especially when it was hot. One day, while playing in his yard, he saw something strange. It was a big, colorful ice block. Tim thought it was beautiful and wanted to keep it. As the hot sun shined on the ice block, it started to melt. Tim was sad but then he remembered that he could put it in the freezer. He ran inside and put the ice block in the freezer to stop it from melting. Tim felt happy that he saved the ice block from the heat. The next day, Tim opened the freezer to look at the ice block. But instead of the ice block, there was a big, friendly bear! The bear was not a threat, but it was a surprise. Tim and the bear became best friends and played together all day. The ice block was a magic gift that brought Tim a new friend.
