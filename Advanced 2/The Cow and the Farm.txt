Once upon a time, there was a cow. The cow lived on a farm with her friends. They played together every day. The cow was big and had a happy smile. One day, the cow was ready to play with her friends. But her friends were not there. She looked around and started to frown. The cow was sad because she could not find her friends. She walked around the farm to look for them. She found her friends hiding behind a big tree. They were playing hide and seek! The cow was happy again. She joined her friends in the game, and they all played together. The cow's frown turned into a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive igloo. In this igloo, there lived a happy boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball all day long. He would kick the ball high up in the sky and watch it come back down. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play with his ball near the igloo. He kicked the ball harder and harder each time. Tim was having so much fun that he didn't notice that the ball was getting closer and closer to the igloo. Then, with one big kick, the ball hit the igloo very hard. The igloo started to crack and break. Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. He tried to fix the igloo, but it was too late. The igloo fell apart and Tim was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Max. Max was always happy and loved to play. One day, Max found a big, red ball in the park. He played with the ball all day long. Later, Max was very hungry. He went home to eat his food. His food was in a big, blue bowl. Max ate all of his food very fast because it was so yummy. After eating, Max saw a new friend outside. It was a little, yellow bird. Max said, "Welcome, bird!" They played together and became best friends. Max and the bird were happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there were two friends called Sarah and Amy. Sarah was 3 years old and loved to play in the park. She was always wearing a yellow dress. One day she was smelling a yellow flower and her friend Amy said, "What are you doing, Sarah?" Sarah replied, "I'm smelling this yellow flower. It smells really nice, like perfume." Amy smiled and said, "I care about this flower too. Let's take care of it together." The two friends watered the flower, talked to it and sang it a song. They gave it lots of love and made sure it was safe. From that day on, Sarah and Amy always took care of things together. They shared the same perfume and wore yellow things on special occasions.

Once upon a time, there was a humble cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her toy, a soft wrap. She would toss it, catch it, and sleep with it. Kitty was always good, so she was never punished. One day, Kitty's wrap was gone. She looked everywhere, but couldn't find it. Kitty was very sad. She thought her mom punished her and took her wrap away. Kitty didn't know why she was punished. But then, something unexpected happened. Kitty's mom brought a new wrap, even bigger and softer than the old one. Kitty was so happy! Her mom didn't punish her; she just wanted to give her a better toy. Kitty played with her new wrap all day and knew that her mom loved her very much.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little girl named Lily. One day, she was playing in the park when she fell down and hurt her knee. It was not very bad, but she needed a bandage to make it feel better. Lily's mom came to help her. "Oh no, Lily! Let's clean your knee and put a bandage on it," she said. Lily's mom took a clean cloth and gently wiped her knee. Then, she set a bandage on Lily's knee to cover the scratch. Lily felt much better with the bandage on her knee. The next day, Lily went back to the park to play. She saw her friend Tim and showed him her bandage. Tim said, "Wow, Lily! Your bandage looks nice and clean. I'm glad you're feeling better!" They played together all day, and Lily was very happy.
