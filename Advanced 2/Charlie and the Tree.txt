Charlie was a little boy who loved playing in his garden. He was always looking for adventure. One day, he found a stamp in his garden and was excited. He picked it up and ran to show his mum. His mum smiled and said “That’s a very special stamp! With this, you can do wonderful things.” Charlie wanted to find out what he could do with the stamp so he went outside again. He wanted to use the stamp on a big tree but it was hard to reach. He kept trying until his mum came and encouraged him. Charlie tried harder and eventually he managed to use the stamp on the tree. Unfortunately, the stamp made a big mark on the tree and Charlie knew he had done wrong. His mum told him it was not right to mark things without asking and she reminded him that he should always respect other people's belongings. Charlie never marked things without asking again so he could avoid bad endings.

Once upon a time there was a family living in a cozy house. They kept a lot of lumber around in a pile near their house. One day, the Dad wanted to move the lumber to their shed, but the pile was so tight! He tried and tried to move it, but it wouldn't budge. The Dad kept shaking his head. He was sure he could move the lumber if he just had some help. His two sons, who were 3 years old, noticed the Dad looking a little frustrated. The Dad asked the two boys to help move the lumber. They started to work together and sure enough the lumber started to loosen. The Dad looked surprised, but he kept the boys working. It was not long before the lumber was all in the shed. The Dad smiled in relief. The lumber was finally moved, and the shed was so much tidier. The Dad praised the two boys for their hard work. They had all worked together to keep the lumber tight and get it safely into the shed. From then on, the family kept the lumber in the shed and never had any trouble moving it.

One day, a cat and a dog went to the market. They had a cart to put things in. The cat was soft and the dog had sharp teeth. They were good friends. At the market, they saw a big, soft bed. The cat said, "Let's buy this bed for our home." The dog agreed and they put the bed in the cart. They were very happy. As they walked home, a big wind came. The wind blew the soft bed out of the cart. The cat and the dog were sad. Then, the dog had an idea. He said, "I will use my sharp teeth to hold the bed!" The dog bit the bed and held it tight. They walked home with the bed in the dog's mouth. The cat and the dog were happy again.

One day, a big dog named Max and a little cat named Lily were playing in the park. They found a stack of colorful blocks. Max and Lily wanted to see who could make the tallest stack. Max said, "I will push my stack to make it taller." Lily said, "I will push my stack too." They both pushed their stacks and they grew taller and taller. Max and Lily laughed and clapped their paws. Then, an old deaf rabbit named Mr. Hops came to the park. He could not hear Max and Lily, but he saw their big stacks. Mr. Hops smiled and pushed his stack too. All three friends had fun playing with the stacks.

Riley and Mommy were going on a vacation. It was very exciting! As they drove, Mommy told Riley to take a look at the fragile flowers growing alongside the road. "Look!" said Mommy. "Aren't those flowers so pretty?" Riley smiled. She examined the flowers carefully. They were so delicate! Soon it was time for them to continue on their vacation. Riley was sad to leave the fragile flowers behind. But Mommy promised there would be many more things to examine on their journey. Riley couldn't wait!
