Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy sword. He liked to play with it every day. One day, a big cat came to Tim's house. The cat said, "I dare you to find the most useful thing in your house." Tim thought hard. He looked at his toy sword and said, "I think my sword is very useful." The cat laughed and said, "Show me how it is useful." Tim picked up his sword and played with it. He swung it in the air and pretended to fight. The cat watched and smiled. In the end, the cat agreed that Tim's sword was useful. They both played with the sword and had a fun day. The cat became Tim's friend, and they had many more adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to sing and dance all day. One day, Tim's mom took him to see an opera. At the opera, there were many people singing and dancing on stage. Tim was so happy to watch them. In the opera, there was an ugly witch. The witch had a big nose and messy hair. She was mean to everyone in the story. She would punish them if they did not listen to her. Tim did not like the ugly witch because she was not nice. At the end of the opera, the ugly witch learned to be kind. She stopped punishing people and became friends with everyone. Tim was happy that the witch changed. He learned that being nice is better than being mean.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big, smooth toy car that she loved to play with. She would push the car across the floor and watch it go fast. One day, while playing with her car, she heard a tiny voice say, "Hello!" Sue looked around and saw a little mouse on the floor. The mouse said, "I believe I can help you make your car go faster!" Sue was surprised but wanted to see what the mouse could do. The mouse climbed into the car and started to turn a tiny wheel inside. Suddenly, the car went very, very fast! It zoomed around the room and made Sue laugh. The mouse and Sue became good friends, and they played with the smooth toy car every day.

It was a wild day. Angus was in his backyard, playing with a page. He was running around with it, shuffling it in the air. He was having so much fun. Suddenly, he heard a noise coming from his house. He stopped and listened. He heard a voice, it was familiar. It was his mom, she was complaining. Angus was worried. He had no idea what his mom was complaining about. Angus went inside and found his mom upset. She was complaining about how things were falling apart at home. Angus felt scared, he didn't understand what was going on. His mom explained, they were going to have to move soon. Angus was sad. He was going to have to leave the backyard, the page and all his friends. But he was also excited, he asked his mom where was he going to move to. She didn't say, insisting he would soon find out. And sure enough, that same wild day, Angus moved to an entirely new house, with a wild backyard, more pages and lots more friends!

Once upon a time, there was a creative dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and get dirty. After playing, Spot's mom would always bathe him to make him clean again. One day, Spot's mom was busy with an operation. She was fixing a toy for Spot's little sister. Spot wanted to help, but he didn't know how. So, he decided to play outside and wait for his mom. While Spot was playing, he found a big puddle of mud. He jumped and splashed in it, getting very dirty. When he went back home, he saw his mom was still busy. Spot had an idea. He would bathe himself! He turned on the water and jumped in the tub. He washed and scrubbed until he was clean again. Spot's mom was so surprised and happy that Spot could bathe himself. From that day on, Spot always helped his mom by bathing himself after playing.
