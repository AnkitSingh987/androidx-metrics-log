Once upon a time, there lived a dragon. It was cute with bright red scales and wide, friendly eyes. One day, the dragon wanted to eat something sweet. He flew until he reached a village, where he saw some children playing. "I want candy!" said the dragon. The children were so surprised when they saw the dragon. They ran away, scared. But one little girl stayed. She walked right up to the dragon and said, "You don't need to take candy from people without asking. It's not very nice!" The dragon felt ashamed. He thanked the little girl kindly and flew away. The moral of this story is that it's important to be nice and to always ask before taking something that doesn't belong to you.

Once upon a time, there was a man who would deliver things. One day, he had to deliver a flexible pipe. The pipe was long and could bend. The man put the pipe in his big truck and drove away. On the way, he saw a big hill. He thought it would be fun to go down the hill with the pipe. So, he took the flexible pipe out of the truck and sat on it. He slid down the hill very fast. Whee! But, guess what? At the bottom of the hill, there was a big hole. The man and the pipe fell into the hole. The flexible pipe bent and saved the man from the fall. The man was happy and said, "Thank you, pipe!" Then, he finished his job and delivered the pipe.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tim. Tim liked to eat a lot. One day, he went to a big party. At the party, he saw a big cake. He wanted to eat the cake, but he needed a napkin. Tim asked a little girl, "Can you give me a napkin, please?" The little girl gave Tim a napkin. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you!" As Tim was about to eat the cake, something unexpected happened. The cake started to talk! The cake said, "Please don't eat me, Tim!" Tim was very surprised. He did not eat the cake. Instead, he became friends with the cake. And with the help of the cake, Tim learned to eat less and gain new friends at the party.

One day, a big, red car arrived at the hospital. Inside the car was a girl named Lily and her mom. They were going to see Lily's new baby brother. Lily was very excited to meet him. When they got to the hospital, Lily saw many people in white coats. They all looked busy. Lily's mom took her hand and they walked to a room. In the room, Lily saw her dad holding a tiny baby. He looked so unique and special. "Meet your baby brother, Lily," said her dad with a big smile. Lily looked at her baby brother and said, "Hi, I am your big sister. We will have lots of fun together." And from that day on, Lily and her baby brother became the best of friends.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to make dough with his mom. They mixed flour and water to make the dough. Tim was very happy because he loved making dough with his mom. But then, Tim saw a big, scary bug on the floor. He was scared and did not want to make dough anymore. His mom saw the bug too and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will make the bug go away." Tim's mom took the bug outside and Tim felt better. After the scary bug was gone, Tim and his mom went back to making dough. They had a little delay because of the bug, but they still had fun. They made yummy bread with the dough and ate it together. Tim was happy and not scared anymore.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play in her backyard. It was a dry, sunny day, and she was very excited. She saw a big tree with a hole in it. Lucy went closer to look at the hole. Inside the tree hole, she found a small bag of cocoa. She thought it was a serious gift from the tree. She ran back to her house and told her mom about it. "Mom, look what I found!" Lucy said. "It's cocoa from the tree." Her mom looked at the cocoa and said, "Wow, Lucy! That's very special. Let's make some hot cocoa together." They went to the kitchen and made hot cocoa. They both enjoyed the yummy drink and had a fun day together.
