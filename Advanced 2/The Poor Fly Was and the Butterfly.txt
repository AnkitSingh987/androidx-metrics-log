One day, a poor little fly was sad. It could not fly well. It wanted to improve and fly high like the other flies. The poor little fly asked its friend, the bee, for help. "Please help me improve my flying," said the poor little fly. The bee thought for a moment and said, "OK, I will help you." The bee took the poor little fly to a magic flower. The bee told the fly to drink the flower juice. The fly drank it, and something unexpected happened. The poor little fly turned into a beautiful butterfly! Now, the butterfly could fly high and fast. It was so happy and thanked the bee for its help. The butterfly and the bee became best friends and flew together every day.

Jimmy was standing next to the pond. It was a big pond with lots of blue water. The sun was shining and Jimmy felt happy. He watched the water ripple beneath him. Suddenly, Jimmy felt uncomfortable. He looked around and saw a big frog. The frog was staring at him! Jimmy was scared and he wanted to run away. But the frog said, "Come here. I need you to help me." So Jimmy stepped closer. The frog said, "I need you to jump into the pond so I can get home." Jimmy was scared and said no. Then the frog got really mad. The frog said, "If you don't help me, I'll make you stand here forever!" Jimmy was very scared. So, he had to jump into the pond. Jimmy was very uncomfortable, but he jumped anyway. He stayed in the pond until dark, and then the frog let him go. Jimmy never wanted to stand near the pond again.

Once upon a time, there was a nice man named Tom. Tom liked to sell red apples. He had a big apple tree in his yard. Every day, he picked apples and put them in a basket. He was very generous and gave some apples to his friends. One day, a little girl named Sue came to Tom's house. She had a question for him. "Can I have an apple, please?" she asked. Tom smiled and said, "Of course, you can have an apple." He gave her a big, red apple from his basket. Sue was very happy. She took the apple and said, "Thank you, Tom!" She went home and showed the apple to her mom. Her mom was happy too. They ate the apple together and it was very sweet. From that day on, Sue and Tom became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a cute, adorable dog named Max. They loved to play together every day. One day, Lily and Max went to visit her dad at his work. Her dad worked in a big room called a lab. In the lab, there were many fun things to see and touch. Lily and Max were very excited. While they were playing, Max saw a big, round ball. He ran to the ball and started to play with it. But the ball made a loud noise, and Max got scared. He started to scream. Lily heard Max scream and ran to him. She gave him a big hug, and Max felt better. They both learned to be careful in the lab, and they had a fun day with Lily's dad.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a friendly bear named Ben. Ben loved to eat honey. Every day, he would go to the beehive and ask the bees for some honey. The bees liked Ben because he was always kind to them. One day, Ben wanted to make a special treat for his friends. He decided to make honey sandwiches. Ben went to the beehive and asked the bees for some honey. The bees were happy to give Ben some honey for his sandwiches. Ben took the honey home and started to make the sandwiches. He added honey to the bread and made many sandwiches for his friends. When his friends came over, they all enjoyed the delicious honey sandwiches. They thanked Ben for being such a good friend and making the tasty treat.
