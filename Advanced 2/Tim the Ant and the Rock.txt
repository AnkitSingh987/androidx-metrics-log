Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a tiny ant named Tim. Tim had a special power. He could use his power to lift big things. This made Tim feel impressive. One day, Tim met a big bear named Ben. Ben was sad because a big rock was on his foot. Ben said, "Help me, Tim! Can you use your power to move the rock?" Tim knew he could help Ben with his impressive power. Tim used his power to lift the big rock off Ben's foot. Ben was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! Your power is impressive!" They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a smart raven. He had many friends in the forest. One day, the smart raven saw a small bird shiver in the cold wind. "Hi, little bird," the smart raven said. "Why do you shiver?" The small bird replied, "I am cold and lost. Can you help me find my family?" The smart raven thought for a moment. "I have an idea," he said. "We can ask my friends for help. They know the forest well." So, the smart raven and the small bird went to find help. They met a kind squirrel who knew where the small bird's family was. The squirrel led them to a warm nest in a tall tree. The small bird was happy to see his family again. The smart raven smiled and said, "I knew my friends could help us." From that day on, the small bird and the smart raven became good friends. They played together every day, and the small bird never felt cold or lost again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was very excited for bed time. She went to her bedroom, which was full of colorful toys, dolls, and stuffed animals. The little girl skipped around the room, picking up different items to play with. She paused when she saw a bright, new ball. It was so colorful! She skipped to the ball, picked it up, and hugged it close. The little girl was so excited that she danced around and said to herself, "I can play catch in the morning, yay!" She put the ball down, gave it a kiss, and skipped over to her bed. She snuggled into her blankets, feeling very happy and content. The little girl said a prayer before going to sleep, and then closed her eyes. She was dreaming of playing catch with her colorful ball the next morning.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there were many animals. There were cows, pigs, and chickens. They lived in different places on the farm. A big fence separated them. One day, a little cow named Daisy felt restless. She wanted to play with the pigs and chickens. She looked at the fence and thought about how to get to the other side. Daisy tried to jump over the fence, but she was too small. She asked her friend, a big horse named Max, for help. Max was strong and could jump high. Max jumped over the fence and helped Daisy get to the other side. Now, Daisy could play with the pigs and chickens. They had a fun day together. When the sun went down, Max helped Daisy get back over the fence. Daisy was happy and went to sleep, dreaming of her new friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing near a big water. He saw a box and wanted to open it. He tried and tried, but it would not open. He felt troubled because he wanted to see what was inside the box. "Help me open it," Tim said to his friend, Sue. Sue came over and tried to open the box too. They both pushed and pushed, but it still would not open. They were both troubled and did not know what to do. Just then, a big frog jumped out of the water and onto the box. The box opened all by itself! Tim and Sue were very surprised. Inside the box, they found many fun toys to play with. They were so happy that the frog helped them open the box, and they played together all day long.
