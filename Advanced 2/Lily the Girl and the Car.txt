Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a sister named Rose. They liked to play together. One day, they went to the park to play. Lily wanted to use her new toy car, but she did not know how to use it. Rose was ignorant about how to use it too. While they were trying to play with the toy car, a big dog came to them. The dog picked up the toy car in its mouth. Lily and Rose were scared. They did not know what to do. They thought the dog would eat their toy car. But then, something unexpected happened. The big dog put the toy car on the ground and pushed it with its nose. The toy car started to move! The dog was showing them how to use the toy car. Lily and Rose were so happy. They played with the dog and their toy car all day. The big dog was not scary after all. It was their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a proud cat named Tom. Tom liked to play outside. One day, he saw a big trap in the yard. Tom wanted to look at the trap. As he walked close to it, he did not see a banana peel on the ground. Tom stepped on the peel and began to slip. He slid all the way to the trap. Luckily, Tom stopped right before the trap. He was very happy that he did not get caught. Tom learned to be careful when playing outside. And he was still a proud cat.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Grace. Grace was very graceful when she flew in the sky. She loved to spread her wings and feel the wind. One day, while flying, Grace saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she had lost her toy. Grace wanted to help Lily. So, Grace prayed for a way to find the toy and make Lily happy again. Grace found the toy near a big tree. She picked it up and flew back to Lily. When Lily saw her toy, she was very happy. Grace and Lily became best friends and played together every day. And from that day on, they always looked at the sky and remembered how praying helped them.

Once upon a time there was a witch. She was very happy and fine. She lived in her house by the forest. One day she decided to take a walk through the woods. She began to wander and pretty soon she found a little path. She followed this path and it took her deeper and deeper in the forest. Suddenly she heard a noise and stopped. What was that noise? She wondered. She kept walking until she saw a little bunny! The bunny hopped away and the witch decided to follow it. The bunny stopped by a special tree and looked around. Then it pointed at a wall of bright blue flowers. The witch smiled and said, "These are so beautiful!" She decided to take two of the flowers. Holding them in her hand she said, "Thank you for showing me these special flowers." She turned to leave when the bunny smiled or rather nodded its head. The witch waved goodbye and went back through the forest. When she arrived at her home the witch looked at the flowers. She thought they were so pretty and fine. They made her feel happy and grateful.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Jack. He wanted to get somewhere very quickly so he ran as fast as he could. He was in such a hurry to get to his destination he was even running faster than the steam. He decided to take a shortcut through the woods, but when he got there, he noticed a big, harsh, grizzly bear. The bear was blocking his way and Jack knew he had to get around quickly. Jack scrambled around to the side of the bear and kept running as fast as he could. He was running all the way until he got to his destination. He made it just in time, the steam was still in the air. He had looked the grizzly bear in the face but he had made it safely. Jack was so relieved he had hurried safely.
