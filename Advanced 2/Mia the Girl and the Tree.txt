Once upon a time, in a wide green park, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside and enjoy the sun. One day, she saw a big tree with something hanging from it. She thought, "I want to see what that is!" The next day, Mia went to the tree and saw that it had round, green things hanging from it. She picked one and took a bite. It was very sour! Mia made a funny face and laughed. She thought, "I will come back tomorrow and try again." Mia went back to the tree every day, and each day the green things got a little less sour. One day, they were not sour at all. They were sweet and yummy! Mia enjoyed them and shared them with her friends. She was happy that she kept trying the green things, even when they were sour at first.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Sue liked to play with her toys and her dog, Buddy. She also had a neighbor who lived next door. Her neighbor was an old lady who had a big garden with pretty flowers. One day, Sue saw her neighbor was sad. The old lady told Sue that her favorite flower was hurt and needed to heal. Sue wanted to help her neighbor and make her happy again. She thought of a plan to fix the flower. Sue went to the store with her mom to buy a cheap gift for her neighbor. She picked out a small bag of special food for the flower. When they got back home, Sue gave the gift to her neighbor. The old lady was so happy and thanked Sue. They put the special food on the flower and watched it heal. Soon, the flower was pretty and healthy again. Sue and her neighbor were happy, and they spent the day playing in the garden together.

Once upon a time, there was a dark, scary cave. The cave was so dark that it made everyone who walked in feel scared. One day, a brave boy decided to go into the cave and explore. When he got inside, he heard a loud scream. He was so scared that he ran away and hid. He waited for a few minutes before he decided to go back and try to find out what was making the sound. The brave boy went deeper into the cave and eventually he saw what was making the sound. It was a strange creature with a big, ugly brain. Its eyes were full of fear and it screamed when it saw the boy. The brave boy was so scared that he ran out of the cave. But the creature screamed louder and chased him out. The boy was so scared that he tripped and fell. The creature caught up to him and ate his brain. The brave boy died and never made it out of the dark cave.

Once upon a time, there was a fox. The fox was rare because it had a red tail. The fox lived in a big forest. One day, the fox was very thirsty. It wanted to drink some water. So, it went to look for a river in the forest. The fox found a river and drank the water. It was so happy! Now the fox was not thirsty anymore. The fox went back to its home and played with its friends.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a gorilla. He was a very big and friendly gorilla. His name was Bobo. Bobo liked to play with the children who came to visit him. He was a generous gorilla, always sharing his food with his friends. One day, Bobo heard a whistle. It was a little boy named Tim. Tim was whistling a happy song. Bobo liked the song very much. He wanted to learn how to whistle too. So, Tim taught Bobo how to whistle by blowing air through his lips. Bobo practiced and practiced. Soon, he could whistle the happy song too. The other animals in the zoo heard Bobo whistle and they liked it. They all started to whistle with Bobo. The zoo became a happy place, full of whistling animals. The end.
