Once upon a time, there was a great hippo. Every day, the hippo would smile and splash around in the big pond. The other animals in the forest would come and watch, and they would smile, too. One day, a small rabbit came to the pond. He hopped up, and he asked the hippo, "What are you doing?" The hippo smiled and said, "I'm splashing around and having fun!" The rabbit said, "That's great!" and the hippo smiled even bigger. The two friends splashed around in the pond until the sun went down, then they said goodbye. The hippo smiled and said, "I had a great time!" "Me too!" said the rabbit, and they smiled and waved as each of them hopped away.

Once there was a little girl. Her name was Susie and she was three years old. Susie was playing in the garden one day when she saw something shiny in the grass. She went to see what it was and it was a hoop! It was made of silver and it was so pretty that Susie wanted to take it home. Susie picked up the hoop and held it in her hands. She was so lucky to find such a nice hoop! Susie decided to take the hoop home and play with it. She took it to her backyard and started to mind her hoop. She would spin it around and around and around. She hopped and skipped after the hoop, laughing and smiling as she watched it spin. Susie played with her hoop for hours and she enjoyed every moment. She felt so lucky to have found such a wonderful toy, and she will cherish it forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a small, safe nest on top of a big rock. Every day, Tim would fly around and play with his friends. At night, he would come back to his nest and rest. One day, while Tim was out playing, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew his nest off the rock. When Tim came back to rest, he saw his nest on the ground. He was very sad and didn't know what to do. But then, a friendly squirrel named Sam came along. Sam saw Tim's problem and had an idea. He helped Tim put his nest back on the rock, but this time, they used strong sticks to hold it in place. Tim was so happy! Now his nest was safe again, and he could rest on his rock without worry. And from that day on, Tim and Sam became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Max. One day, Max's dad asked him to help with the housework. His dad said, "Max, please clean the kitchen." Max was so happy to help and happily agreed. He carefully put on his apron and got to work. It was hard work but he enjoyed it. He took out a sponge and washed the counter until it was shiny and clean. Max was so proud of himself for doing such a good job. Next, his dad said, "Max, can you go to the store and get some beef?" Max put on his shoes, grabbed his basket and marched off to the store. At the store, Max was amazed by all the beautiful vegetables. Then he saw the beef and was even more amazed. He was so excited he grabbed the biggest piece of beef he could find. With pride, Max brought the beef back home. As soon as he walked through the door, his dad smiled and said, "Well done, Max! You did a great job." Max was so happy and proud of himself!

The girl kept saying nice things to people, and kept wondering what the result would be. Her friends kept doing nice things for her in return. She was so happy. She was so glad to be surrounded by friendly people. The girl continued to do nice things and wonder what the result would be. Each time, she was pleasantly surprised with the result. She had a wonderful time with her friendly friends.
