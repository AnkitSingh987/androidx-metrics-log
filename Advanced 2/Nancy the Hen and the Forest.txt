Once upon a time, there was a friendly little hen named Nancy. One day, she was walking through the forest looking for berries to eat. Suddenly, she stepped in some mud and became very wet. Nancy was very unhappy and she began to cry. Then, a kind owl flew over and said, “Don’t cry, little hen. I can help you.” She looked up and saw a big, friendly feather. The Owl said, “Let’s use this feather to dry you off.” She agreed and the owl carefully used the feather to dry her. Once Nancy was all dry, she said thank you to the owl and ran off through the forest, looking for more berries. The owl smiled and watched her go.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big gate. The gate was in front of a big house. Tim thought the house looked expensive. He wanted to see what was behind the gate. Tim's mom saw him looking at the gate. She urged him to stay away. She told him it was not safe to go near the big house. Tim felt sad, but he listened to his mom. Later, Tim and his mom went to the park. They played games and had fun. Tim forgot about the big gate and the expensive house. He was happy to spend time with his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little raven. The raven liked to spring high into the sky. He had a big open nest in a tall tree. One day, he saw something shiny on the ground. It was a key. The raven picked it up with his beak and took it to his nest. In the woods, there was a small house with a locked door. The animals knew about the house but could not open the door. They tried to push and pull, but the door stayed shut. The raven saw the animals and remembered the key in his nest. The raven flew back to his nest and got the key. He brought it to the animals at the small house. They used the key to open the door. Inside, they found a room full of yummy food and warm beds. The raven and his friends were very happy. They ate, played, and slept in the house. The little raven had helped them find a cozy home.

Once there was a playful girl named Ann. She loved to play outside with her friends, but one day she went for a very special adventure. Ann went to a field full of beautiful flowers. While she was looking around, she saw an amazing sign. The sign said "Kiss the flowers!" Ann was so excited, she ran to the flowers and kissed each one! As she kissed the flowers, they all began to bloom! Ann was so happy and she felt so special. She had never seen anything like this before. As Ann left the field, she decided that she would keep the magical sign in her pocket, so she could always remember her special adventure.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree with perfect fruit. Tim wanted to eat the fruit, but it was too high. He had an idea. He would cut a stick to reach the fruit. Tim found a stick and cut it with a small knife. With the stick, Tim reached the fruit and pulled it down. He took a bite and it was very yummy. Tim was happy. He walked more and found a nice place with water and trees. It was an oasis. Tim wanted to share the fruit with his friends. Tim ran back to his friends and told them about the perfect fruit and the oasis. They all went together to the oasis. They used the stick to get more fruit. They played and laughed by the water. It was a great day for Tim and his friends.

There once was a wild bunny who loved to splash in the mud. One sunny day, the bunny hopped over to a big puddle of mud. He jumped in with a big splash! The mud went all over him, and he giggled with joy. As the wild bunny played, he saw a little girl named Lily. She was sad because she couldn't find her favorite toy, a red radish. The bunny hopped over to her and asked, "Why are you sad?" Lily told him about her lost radish. The wild bunny wanted to help, so they looked for the radish together. They searched high and low, and finally found it next to a big tree. Lily was so happy, she hugged the bunny and said, "Thank you!" They both went back to the mud puddle and splashed together, laughing and having fun.
