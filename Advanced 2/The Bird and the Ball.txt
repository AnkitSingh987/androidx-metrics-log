Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to fly. The bird had orange wings and liked to jog on the ground. One day, while the bird was jogging, it saw a big tree. The bird decided to fly up to the top of the tree. When it got to the top, it saw a big orange ball. The bird thought the ball was a new friend. It tried to say hello, but the ball did not talk back. Then, something unexpected happened! The orange ball began to float up into the sky. The bird was surprised and tried to follow it. The orange ball was not a friend, but a big balloon. The bird and the balloon flew together, and they had a fun day in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She was 3 years olds and very stubborn. Even though her mum wanted her to eat her broccoli, Lucy didn't like it at all. "Lucy, you have to eat your broccoli," said her mum. But Lucy just shook her head and said, "No! I don't love broccoli." Her mum smiled and said, "It's ok if you don't love broccoli, but at least give it a try." So Lucy reluctantly took a bite of the broccoli. But she still didn't like it. When she had finished eating all her broccoli, she got a big hug from her mum. "I love you even when you're stubborn," her mum said. Lucy smiled. She knew that her mum loved her very much, even when she was stubborn.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy called Microscope. Microscope was a very smart toy. It could help Tom see little things that he could not see with his eyes. Tom loved to play with Microscope every day. One day, Tom and Microscope went for a walk. They saw a big tree with a bird on it. Tom said, "Microscope, can you help me see the bird up close?" Microscope said, "Yes, Tom! I can help you." So, Tom took Microscope and looked at the bird. The bird was very pretty and had many colors. Tom was very happy to see the bird so close. While they were looking at the bird, a cat came by. The cat was very alert and saw the bird too. Tom said, "Oh no, Microscope! The cat wants to take the bird! What should we do?" Microscope said, "Tom, we must scare the cat away to save the bird!" So, Tom and Microscope made a loud noise. The cat ran away, and the bird was safe. Tom and Microscope were very happy that they saved the bird. They went back home and played together, knowing they did a good thing.

One day, a cute little bird found a bottle. The bird wanted to attach the bottle to his nest. He tried to fly with the bottle, but it was too heavy. The bird felt sad because he could not carry the bottle. Then, a big strong bird saw the cute little bird. The big bird wanted to help. He tried to attach the bottle to the nest too. But the bottle was still too heavy for both birds. They were not happy. Finally, they had an idea. They asked their friends to help. All the birds worked together to attach the bottle to the nest. They were very happy and proud. The cute little bird's nest was now the best nest in the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little ball named Bounce. Bounce was very intelligent. He loved to bounce all day long. One day, Bounce was bouncing around the house when he saw a big, white thing. It was a toilet! Bounce had never seen a toilet before. Bounce wanted to see what was inside the toilet, so he bounced higher and higher. But, he could not see inside. Bounce felt sad. He wanted to know what was in there. He thought and thought about how he could look inside. Then, Bounce had an idea. He found a big book and put it next to the toilet. Bounce bounced on the book, and then he could see inside the toilet. He was so happy! Bounce learned that being intelligent helped him solve problems. And he continued to bounce around the house, exploring new things.
