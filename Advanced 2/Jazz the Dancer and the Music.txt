Once upon a time, there was a dancer named Jazz. Jazz was very flexible, so she could do amazing moves and tricks. She wanted to finish her dance and show it to everyone. One day, Jazz had a big idea: what if she danced to some lively and fun music? She went to find some jazz music and played it out loud. Jazz started to move her body to the music, spinning and twirling around the room. When Jazz finished her dance, everyone was amazed by how flexible she was. They clapped and cheered, and they all wanted Jazz to dance again. Jazz knew that she had found something special with her jazz music and her dance. And she would always finish her dance with a twirl.

Once, there was a very fair cricket. She hopped around all over the place, trying to understand the world around her. Everywhere she went, she saw so many things and she wanted to learn about them all. One bright and sunny day, she saw a big tree. It had branches and leaves and was very tall. She wanted to understand what the tree was, so she hopped up close and hopped on a branch. She looked around and saw a bird in the tree. The bird was hopping from branch to branch, singing a song. It was happy and the cricket wanted to understand why. The cricket watched for a very long time and eventually she figured out that the bird was happy because the day was fair. She was so glad to understand that happiness can come from a fair day!

Once upon a time, there was a museum. In the museum, there were many old things. Some things were dead. One day, a little girl went to the museum with her mom and dad. In the museum, the girl saw a big dead fish. She wanted to save the fish. She asked her mom and dad if they could save the fish. But her mom and dad said no, the fish was dead and they could not save it. The little girl was sad. She tried to save the dead fish by herself. She touched the fish and it broke. The museum people were mad. The girl learned that she should listen to her mom and dad.

Once upon a time, there was a person who felt very miserable. They sat under a big tree and began to cry. A friendly dog walked by and saw the person crying. The dog asked, "Why are you crying, person?" The person said, "I am sad because I have no friends." The dog wagged its tail and said, "I will be your friend!" The person stopped crying and smiled. They played together all day and were happy. From that day on, the person was not miserable anymore because they had a new friend in the dog.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside, but the weather was not always nice. Some days, it was too hot or too cold. But Spot did not let this stop him from having fun. One day, the sun was shining, and the weather was just right. Spot decided to set up a fun game for his friends. He found a big ball and set it in the middle of the yard. He called his friends, and they all came running to play. They played with the ball all day long. They would run, jump, and laugh together. The weather stayed nice, and they had a great time. Spot was so happy that he could make his friends smile. And from that day on, they knew they could always depend on Spot to have fun, no matter what the weather was like.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to ride his bike. He liked to ride with style. He put on his cool hat and big shoes. He was ready to go. As he rode down the street, he saw his friend Sam. Sam had a sour face. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, Sam?" Sam said, "I lost my toy. I can't find it." Tim wanted to help his friend. Tim and Sam looked for the toy. They looked under the tree, behind the big rock, and in the tall grass. At last, they found the toy near the flowers. Sam was happy again. They both rode their bikes with style and a big smile.
