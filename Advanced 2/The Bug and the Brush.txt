Once upon a time, there was a little bug. The little bug liked to crawl all day. One day, the little bug found a fine brush. The bug thought, "This brush is so nice, I want to keep it." The little bug crawled back to show its friends the fine brush. But the friends did not like the brush. They said, "That brush is not for bugs. It is for the big people." The little bug was sad, but it wanted to be a good bug. So, the little bug crawled to the big people's house. It gave the fine brush back to the big people. The big people were very happy. They gave the little bug a big hug. The little bug learned that it is good to give things back. And the little bug and its friends lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Amy and Bonnie. They loved playing together. One day, Amy locked away a potato in her bedroom. Bonnie asked why, and Amy said, "so nobody can eat it!" Bonnie was very shy, so she did not know how to ask Amy for a piece of the potato. She just stood there, thinking, until finally she said "Can I please have a piece of the potato?". Amy smiled and said, "Yes, of course you can!". Bonnie was so happy and thanked Amy for sharing. Then they both enjoyed the potato together and ate it all up. The end.

Once upon a time there were two boys named Christopher and Leo. They were very adventurous and loved to explore. One day, they decided to explore the beach near their house. As they were walking, Christopher slipped and fell over. He was putting his hand out in front of him and he felt something round and hard. He looked down and saw a little pill. Christopher showed Leo the pill and asked him, "What is this?" Leo looked at it and said, "I don't know - but let's not touch it." Christopher nodded in agreement. The boys decided it would be best to leave the pill and go home. They had learnt an important lesson - when you're out exploring, it's best to be careful and know what you're doing. The most adventurous things in life can be risky, and it's always important to be aware of what you pick up.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big, light balloon in the sky. It was so pretty! Tim wanted to touch the balloon, but it was too high up. Tim had an idea! He would shoot a little ball into the sky to hit the balloon. He ran inside and found a ball. Then, he went back outside and threw the ball as hard as he could. The ball went up, up, up into the sky. It hit the light balloon and made it come down. Tim was so happy! He played with the balloon all day long. The sky was filled with joy, and Tim had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely sailor. He had a big boat. He liked to sail on the sea. One day, he saw a little island. The sailor thought, "I want to go there." When the sailor got to the island, he saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a small box. The sailor said, "What is in the box?" He opened the box and found a pretty hat. The hat had a note that said, "Wear me to celebrate." The sailor put on the hat and started to dance. He was not lonely anymore. He felt happy. The sailor knew that the hat was special. He danced all day and all night. The sailor was never lonely again.

It was Amy's birthday, which made her very happy. She was with her mom and dad, who had something special planned. They told Amy that it was time to split up, and that she was going to have lots of fun. Amy had an easy time making her way through the trees. Her mom and dad were hiding for her in different places. As she moved through the dark, she heard something calling her name. She quickly followed the sound and found her mom and dad together, holding a big surprise for her. "Happy Birthday!" they said. Amy was so excited. She jumped up and down and hugged her parents. She didn't know it was going to be this fun. "Thank you Mom, thank you Dad!" Amy said, with a big smile. She had a wonderful birthday, and splitting up was worth it.
