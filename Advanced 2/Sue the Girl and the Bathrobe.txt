One day, a little girl named Sue found a big, soft bathrobe in her room. She was very glad because it was cold outside. Sue put on the bathrobe and went to play with her toys. She liked how warm and cozy it felt. While playing, Sue heard a noise from the bathrobe. She was scared at first, but then she realized it was a tiny kitten hiding in the pocket. The kitten was cold and needed a warm place to stay. Sue was happy to share her bathrobe with the kitten. Sue thought she was the one who found the bathrobe, but it was the kitten who found Sue. The kitten was the one who brought the bathrobe to Sue's room. They became best friends and played together every day. Sue was glad that something unexpected happened because it brought her a new friend.

Tom and Mia liked to play in the sand near the water. They made castles and bridges and tunnels with their buckets and shovels. Sometimes they saw fish and crabs and shells in the water. They were careful not to go too far from the shore, because their mom and dad told them to be safe. One day, they saw a big fin in the water. It was gray and sharp and moved fast. They heard their mom and dad shout, "Shark! Shark! Get out of the water!" Tom and Mia were scared. They dropped their buckets and shovels and ran to their mom and dad. They hugged them tight and looked at the water. The shark was still there. It was a terrible shark. It had big teeth and a mean eye. It bit a fish and made a splash. It swam around and looked for more food. Tom and Mia did not like the shark. They wanted it to go away. Their mom and dad told them to remain calm and quiet. They said the shark would not come to the shore. They said they would wait until the shark left. They said they would be okay. Tom and Mia nodded and listened. They remained calm and quiet. They hoped the shark would leave soon. They did not want to play in the water anymore. They wanted to play in the sand again.

Once upon a time, there was a lucky little girl named Lily. She had a pretty ornament that she loved very much. The ornament was shiny and round, and she played with it all the time. One day, Lily lost her ornament while playing outside. She was very sad and did not know what to do. She called her mommy and daddy to help her look for it. They looked all around the yard, but they could not find the ornament. Just when they were about to give up, Lily's dog, Spot, came running with the ornament in his mouth. He heard Lily call and wanted to help. Lily was so happy to have her ornament back. She gave Spot a big hug and knew that they were both very lucky to have each other.

Once upon a time there was a shy bug that lived in a refrigerator. Every day the bug would crawl around and look for something to eat. One day, the bug heard a loud noise coming from the refrigerator door. When the door opened, a large hand reached in and grabbed the bug. The bug was scared, but it was too shy to say anything. The hand placed the bug in a glass jar and snapped the lid shut. The bug tried its best to escape, but the lid was too tight. It looked around and saw that it was all alone in the jar. It felt so scared and lonely. The bug kept trying to escape until it heard someone say "Shh, be quiet. We have to separate you from the other bugs." The bug got even more scared, realizing that it was now completely alone and would never get back to the refrigerator. The bad ending tells that the poor bug was never able to get home, doomed to be stuck in the jar for eternity.

Tim and Lily were best friends. They liked to play with toys and pretend they were in space. One day, they found a big box in the garage. It had many shiny things inside. They were very happy. "Look, Lily, this is a rocket!" Tim said, holding a metal cone. "We can go to the universe with it!" "Wow, Tim, that's so cool!" Lily said, picking up a plastic helmet. "Can I wear this? It looks like an astronaut!" "Yes, you can, Lily. And I have this, it's a laser gun!" Tim said, showing a red flashlight. "We can fight the bad aliens!" They put on their space things and climbed into the box. They closed the lid and made noises like a rocket. They imagined they were flying to the stars. But they did not know that the box was not a toy. It was a trap. A bad man had put it there to catch kids. He had used the shiny things to make them think it was a game. He was watching them from a window. He had a fake smile. When he saw them go into the box, he locked it with a big padlock. He laughed and said, "Gotcha, you silly kids! You are not going to the universe, you are going to my basement! You will never see the sun again!" Tim and Lily heard his voice and felt scared. They tried to open the box, but it was too hard. They shouted for help, but no one heard them. They cried and hugged each other. They wished they had never played with the box. They wished they had stayed with their mom and dad. They wished they had known it was all fake.
