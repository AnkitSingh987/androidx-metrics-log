Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Mark. Mark was a rich boy, but he was not happy. He had many toys, but he wanted a friend to play with. One day, Mark met a girl named Sue. Sue was not rich, but she was happy. Mark asked Sue, "How are you so happy?" Sue said, "I pray every day, and I have friends to play with." Mark wanted to be happy like Sue, so he decided to pray too. Mark prayed every day, but he still felt sad. He did not have any friends to play with. One day, while Mark was playing alone, a big storm came. The storm was very bad, and it broke all of Mark's toys. Now Mark was not rich, and he was still sad.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy had a special book. This book was her diary. It was important to her because she wrote about her day and drew pictures in it. One day, Lucy could not find her diary. She looked and looked but it was not in her room. Lucy asked her friend, Tom, to help her find it. Tom was good at finding things. They decided to examine the house together. They looked in the living room, the kitchen, and the garden. Finally, they found the diary under Lucy's bed. Lucy was so happy and thanked Tom for helping her. They sat down and drew pictures in the diary together. And they lived happily ever after.

But, the fat cat did not look where it was crawling. The fat cat crawled into the street. A big car came and the fat cat got hurt. The ambulance could not help the fat cat. The fat cat was sad, and it was a bad ending.

Once upon a time there was a boy named George. He was a happy little boy who liked to explore. One day he decided to go exploring underground. He grabbed his torch, and zipped on his coat. George started walking down the dark underground tunnel, looking for something exciting. It was very dark and spooky, but he didn't mind. Suddenly he heard a buzzing sound. He followed the sound until he saw a little bee. It was zipping around in circles, up and down the tunnel walls! George smiled and watched the bee for a while. Then the bee flew off and he continued his exploring. He soon came to a regular door in the tunnel wall. He opened it and found a secret room! George was so happy! He explored the room and found lots of special treasures. Then he zipped back home, excited to tell his story.
