Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. She was three years old and had long, golden hair. It was a cold winter day and Sarah was outside in the snow. She was wearing a pretty, red coat, but still she was shivering! She looked up at the sky and wished for a bit of luxury. Suddenly, an angry man appeared. He had a big bag filled with blankets and food. He ordered Sarah to take the bag home. Sarah was surprised, but very happy at the same time. She jumped up and down and thanked the man enthusiastically. Then she carefully carried the bag of luxury home. That night, Sarah tucked herself in with a warm blanket and ate her delicious dinner. She never felt so warm and happy before! She thanked the man again in her mind and smiled before falling asleep.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there lived a small rabbit named Remy. Remy had a huge friend named Ellie. Ellie was an elephant. They liked to play and have fun together. They were best friends. One day, Remy found a box. "Look, Ellie!" said Remy. "I found a box. Let's mail it to our friend, Max the mouse." Ellie smiled and said, "Yes, let's do it!" So, they took the box to the mail place. They gave the box to the mailman. The mailman said, "Thank you, I will send this to Max." After a few days, Max got the box. Max was so happy. He called Remy and Ellie. "Thank you for the box, friends! I love it!" Max said. Remy and Ellie were happy too. They all laughed and played together. And they were the best of friends, forever and ever.

One day, a kind girl named Lily was playing with her friend Tom. They were having fun, but they could not decide what to do next. Lily had an idea. She said, "I suggest we watch a movie on the screen!" Tom agreed, and they went inside the house. As they watched the movie, they started to argue. Lily wanted to watch a funny movie, but Tom wanted to watch a scary one. They could not agree, and their voices got louder and louder. Lily's mom heard them and came into the room. Lily's mom said, "You both need to be kind to each other and take turns. Today, you can watch a funny movie, and tomorrow, you can watch a scary one." Lily and Tom agreed, and they learned that it is important to be kind and take turns.

Tommy did not like pants. He liked to wear his blue shorts every day, even when it was cold. Mommy said he had to wear pants today, because they were going to Grandma's house. Grandma liked to see Tommy in pants. "Tommy, put on your pants, please," Mommy said. She held a pair of green pants with a dinosaur on them. Tommy liked dinosaurs, but he did not like pants. "No, Mommy, no pants!" Tommy said. He ran away from Mommy and hid under his bed. He hugged his blue shorts. "Tommy, come out of there. Don't be silly. You can't wear shorts in the winter. You will be cold and sick," Mommy said. She tried to pull Tommy out, but he refused. He kicked and screamed. "Mommy, I want shorts! I want shorts!" Tommy said. Mommy sighed. She was tired of fighting with Tommy. She had an idea. She went to the closet and found another pair of pants. They were red and had a fire truck on them. Tommy loved fire trucks. "Tommy, look what I found. These are your fire truck pants. They are very cool and warm. Do you want to wear them?" Mommy said. She showed Tommy the pants. Tommy peeked out from under the bed. He saw the fire truck pants. He liked them. He crawled out and took them from Mommy. "Fire truck pants! Fire truck pants!" Tommy said. He put them on and smiled. He felt like a firefighter. "Good boy, Tommy. You look very handsome in your fire truck pants. Now let's go to Grandma's house. She will be happy to see you," Mommy said. She gave Tommy a hug and a kiss. Tommy and Mommy went to Grandma's house. Grandma was happy to see them. She gave Tommy a cookie and a toy car. She said he looked very nice in his fire truck pants. Tommy was happy. He forgot about his blue shorts. He liked his fire truck pants. He did not refuse to wear them anymore.
