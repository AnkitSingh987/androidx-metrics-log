Once upon a time there was a little girl called Emma. One day, Emma found an oat in her garden. It was so sweet! She remembered how her mummy used to give her sweet oat snacks whenever she was feeling a little bored. She wanted to do the same for her friends. So Emma took the oat and went to see her friends. When she saw them, she exclaimed 'Look at the sweet oat I found!' Her friends were so excited to see Emma's sweet oat and they asked her, 'What should we do with it?' Emma thought carefully and then answered, 'Let's make oat cookies!'. So together they made oat cookies with the sweet oat that Emma had found. While they were making the oat cookies, Emma said, 'I'll never forget the sweet oat I found today!' The oat cookies tasted delicious and all of Emma's friends remembered how they had made the cookies together with the sweet oat that Emma had found!

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Gia. She was only three-years-old and loved exploring the world around her. One day Gia was walking in the forest and she found a purple skull lying at the foot of a big tree. She bent down and whispered softly, “What’s this?” Suddenly, a small voice came from inside the skull. “I am an old spirit and this is a special purple skull. It is very important to always remember to do what’s right and to be kind to all those around you.” Gia smiled and said, “I will be kind, I promise!” The spirit smiled and said, “Then you will be full of joy and kindness for the rest of your days.” Gia learned an important lesson that day. Being kind and doing the right thing will always make her feel happy.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to study the big world around him. He loved to learn about new things. One day, Buddy found a whip on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and took it to his friend, the cat. The cat showed Buddy how to play with the whip. They had lots of fun together. The sun went down and it was time for Buddy and the cat to go home. They said goodbye and Buddy took the whip home with him. He was very happy. From that day on, Buddy and the cat played with the whip and studied the world together.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a very old tree. Near the tree, a little sunflower grew. The sunflower loved the sun and wanted to be tall like the tree. The tree was ancient and wise, but it did not like the sunflower. One day, the sunflower asked the tree, "Can you help me grow tall like you?" The tree did not want to help the sunflower. It said, "No, I will not help you. You are too small and not like me." The sunflower was sad, but it did not give up. The sunflower tried to grow tall by itself. It drank water and reached for the sun. The other flowers in the garden saw the sunflower and wanted to help. They gave the sunflower more water and cheered it on. The sunflower grew taller and taller. The tree saw the sunflower and was surprised. It learned that even small things can grow big and strong with help from friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red vest that he loved to wear. One sunny, mild day, Tim and his mom went to the park to play. At the park, Tim saw a big slide. He was so excited! He took off his red vest and left it on the bench. Tim ran to the slide and went up and down many times. After a while, Tim felt tired and went back to the bench. But his red vest was gone! Tim looked around the park, but he could not find it. He felt very sad. Tim and his mom went home without the red vest.
