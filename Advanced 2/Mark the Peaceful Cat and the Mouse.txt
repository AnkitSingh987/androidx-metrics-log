Once upon a time, there was a peaceful cat named Mark. Mark was a very happy cat who loved to drink tea. Every day, he would sit under a big tree and have his tea time. One day, Mark's tea was gone. He looked around and saw a little mouse. The mouse was drinking his tea! Mark was sad, but he had an idea. He would make more tea and share it with the mouse. So, Mark and the mouse sat under the big tree and drank tea together. They were very happy and peaceful. From that day on, Mark and the mouse were best friends, and they always shared their tea.

Lily liked to watch her mom dance. Mom was graceful and moved like a bird. Lily wanted to dance like mom, but she was too small. Mom said she had to wait and learn. One day, mom had to go to work. She left Lily with dad. Dad was busy on his phone. He did not want to play with Lily. Lily was bored and sad. She saw mom's phone on the table. It was shiny and pretty. Lily wanted to see it. Lily took mom's phone and ran to her room. She tried to turn it on, but she did not know how. She pushed all the buttons, but nothing happened. She got angry and threw the phone on the floor. The phone broke into pieces. Lily heard a loud noise. Dad came to Lily's room. He saw the broken phone and got very mad. He yelled at Lily and said she was naughty and stupid. He did not explain why the phone was important. He did not hug Lily or say sorry. He just took her to the corner and told her to stay there. Lily cried and cried. She did not understand what she did wrong. She missed mom and wanted to dance with her. She felt alone and scared. She wished dad would explain and be nice. She wished mom would come back and make everything better. But mom did not come back. And dad did not explain. And Lily stayed in the corner. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a green cat named Tom. Tom was not like other cats. He had a friend who was a skeleton. They liked to play together. One day, they went to a party. At the party, there was a big cake. Tom wanted to eat the cake, but the skeleton said no. The skeleton did not eat. Tom was sad, but he did not eat the cake. He did not want his friend to feel bad. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came to the party. The dog wanted to eat the cake too. But the dog did not want to share. Tom and the skeleton had an idea. They asked the dog to play a game. If the dog lost, he would share the cake. The dog agreed. They played the game, and the dog lost. The dog was sad, but he shared the cake with everyone. Tom, the skeleton, and all their friends were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a humble mailbox. It stood by the road, waiting for the mail to come. The mailbox was red and small, but it was happy. One day, a little girl named Lucy walked by the mailbox. She looked at it and said, "I admire you, mailbox. You are so pretty and strong." The mailbox felt happy and proud. From that day on, Lucy and the mailbox became friends. Lucy would visit the mailbox every day and tell it stories. They were both very happy to have each other. And the humble mailbox knew it was loved.

Once upon a time, there was a big house. In the house, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to scatter her toys all over the floor. She had dolls, cars, and blocks. Amy's favorite place to play was upstairs. One day, Amy's mom said, "Amy, you need to pick up your toys. They are not useful when they are scattered all over the floor." Amy looked around and saw that her mom was right. She decided to pick up her toys and put them away. As Amy picked up her toys, she found her favorite doll, Lucy. Amy said, "Lucy, let's play upstairs where it's clean now." Lucy and Amy went upstairs and played together. They had so much fun, and Amy learned that her toys were more useful when they were not scattered all over the floor.
