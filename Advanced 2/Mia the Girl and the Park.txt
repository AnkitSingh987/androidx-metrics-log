One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She loved to swing on the swings. As she swung back and forth, she saw a shiny crystal on the ground. Mia got off the swing and picked up the crystal. It was so pretty! Mia saw a big boy named Tom near the slide. He looked fearful. Mia walked up to him and asked, "Why are you scared, Tom?" Tom said, "I lost my crystal and I'm scared I won't find it." Mia smiled and showed him the crystal she found. "Is this your crystal?" she asked. Tom's face lit up with joy. "Yes, that's it! Thank you, Mia!" They became good friends and played together at the park every day.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a chubby bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to play in the maze made of tall green bushes. Every day, he would hop and jump all around the maze, looking for yummy carrots to eat. One sunny day, as Bobo was playing in the maze, he met a small ant named Andy. Andy was trying to lift a big leaf, but it was too heavy for him. "Help me, please," Andy said to Bobo. "I need to take this leaf to my home." Bobo wanted to help his new friend. He used his strong legs and fluffy tail to lift the big leaf. Together, they carried the leaf through the maze, laughing and talking along the way. They became very good friends. When they reached Andy's home, all the ants cheered and clapped. They were so happy to see the big leaf. Andy thanked Bobo for his help, and they promised to play together in the maze every day. From that day on, Bobo and Andy were the best of friends, and they had many fun adventures in the garden together.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful boat. The boat liked to sail on the water. It went fast when the wind blew. One day, the boat went under a big bridge. The bridge was so tall! The boat liked to sail under the bridge. It was fun and exciting. The beautiful boat sailed all day. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. The boat was happy, and everyone who saw it smiled too. And they all had a wonderful time.

One day, a funny comet came from the sky. It was big and shiny. It wanted to play with the kids on Earth. The funny comet saw it was going to rain. It was sad because it wanted to play outside. But the funny comet had an idea. The funny comet made the rain stop. Then, it played with the kids. They all had fun and laughed. The funny comet was happy to have new friends.

One fly was buzzing around. It had been around for a long time. Suddenly, it came across an enormous, old well. The fly flew closer, and looked down into the well with wonder. It touched the edge of the well with one of its wings. Then it heard a voice from inside the well. "Hello?" said the voice. The fly was so surprised! It had never spoken to anyone before. "Who is that?" asked the fly. "I'm down here in the bottom of the well," said the voice. "It's so enormous, I can't get out!" The fly thought of a plan. It touched the edge of the well again and a rope came down from above. The fly flew up and touched the rope to pull the voice out. When the voice reached the top, it was a friendly frog! The frog said, "Thank you! You helped me escape the well." The fly was so happy that it had made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty garden with many flowers. Her favorite flower was a red rose. She loved to water the rose and watch it grow. One day, Lily's friend Tom came over to play. They were running and laughing in the garden. But, Tom accidentally stepped on the red rose. Lily was very sad and Tom felt sorry. He said, "I am sorry, Lily. I did not mean to hurt your rose." Tom knew he needed to help fix the rose. He got some water and a stick. They put the stick next to the rose and tied the rose to the stick. Lily watered the rose every day. Soon, the rose was standing tall and pretty again. Lily and Tom were happy and they played in the garden together.
