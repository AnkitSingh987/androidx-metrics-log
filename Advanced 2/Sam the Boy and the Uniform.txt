Once upon a time, there was a boy named Sam. Sam went to school every day. He wore a uniform. One day, Sam felt embarrassed because his uniform was dirty. Sam's friend, Tim, saw Sam and said, "Why are you embarrassed, Sam?" Sam said, "My uniform is dirty, and I don't want people to laugh at me." Tim said, "Don't worry, let's study together and forget about your uniform." Sam and Tim studied together all day. They tried to forget about Sam's dirty uniform. But when they went outside to play, all the kids saw Sam's uniform and laughed at him. Sam felt very sad and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat. The cat was fearful of going to the other side of the street. One day, a wise old dog saw the cat and wanted to help. The dog said, "Don't be fearful, little cat. I can show you how to change and be brave." The little cat listened and learned from the dog. They walked together to the other side of the street. The little cat was happy and not fearful anymore. The cat and the dog became good friends. The moral of the story is that we can change and be brave with the help of our friends.

One day, a soft cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a long arrow on the ground. Tom picked up the arrow and started to play with it. He liked how it felt in his paws. Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max asked, "What is that, Tom?" Tom said, "It's an arrow! I found it on the ground. Let's play with it!" Max agreed and they started to run and jump with the arrow. As they played, they stretched the arrow by pulling it. Suddenly, the arrow made a loud sound and a big ball came out of it! Tom and Max were very surprised. They had never seen anything like it before. They played with the big ball and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tom and Max always played with the arrow and the big ball, and they became the best of friends.

Alice was excited to visit the park. She ran to her room, grabbed her favourite shirt and put it on. She ran back to the living room and found her parents. "Are you ready?" asked her father. Alice shouted "Yes!" with a big smile on her face. Alice, her parents, and her big brother climbed into the car. "We're here!" her dad said as they reached the park. Alice was so excited! Alice and her brother ran to the swings. "Let's swing together!" Alice said to her brother. They both jumped onto the swing and went higher and higher. They laughed and giggled until they couldn't swing anymore. Alice and her family stayed at the park until the sun set. It was time to go home. They all waved goodbye to the park and said they would visit again. Alice was already excited to come back!

One day, a little squirrel went to play in the park. It was a mild day, and the sun was shining. The squirrel was very happy. The squirrel saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As it went up, it saw a little bird. The bird said, "Hello, squirrel! Be careful not to fall!" The squirrel smiled and said, "I will be careful, bird!" But then, the squirrel slipped and started to fall. The bird flew down to help the squirrel. The bird said, "Hold on to my wings!" The squirrel held on tight, and the bird flew them both down to the ground. The squirrel said, "Thank you, bird! You saved me!" The bird and the squirrel became good friends and played together in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She wanted to play with her toys but the floor was so messy. Instead of cleaning up, Sarah had an idea. She ran to her mom and said, "Mommy, can I put the toys away in the cupboard?" Her mom said, "No, Sarah. Cleaning up is a very important lesson for life. When you clean up, it will make your room so much nicer." Sarah thought and thought about her mom's words, and then she started to pick up all the toys. She cleaned until the floor was nice and clean. Once she was done, Sarah looked around the room with a big smile and said,"Mommy, look what I did! It looks so much nicer now." Her mom smiled back and said, "See, Sarah. It's important to clean up so that everything is tidy and organized. That's the best way to stay organized and healthy." Sarah thought about her mom's words and said,"I'm so glad I cleaned up. Now I can enjoy playing with all my toys." Moral: Cleaning up is important to stay organized, tidy and healthy.
