Once upon a time, there was a big wood. In the wood, there were many animals. They all belonged to a group. The group was very organized. They liked to help each other. One day, a new animal came to the wood. The new animal did not belong to the group. The group did not want to help the new animal. They said, "You do not belong here. Go away." The new animal was sad. It went away from the wood. The sky became dark. The wind blew hard. A big storm came. The organized group of animals was scared. They did not know what to do. The new animal knew how to be safe in a storm. But it was not in the wood. The group of animals did not have help. The storm was very bad. The wood was not safe for the animals. The animals were sad and scared because they did not let the new animal belong.

One day, a swan named Sam was swimming in a pond. Sam was a silly swan who loved to play with his friends. He saw a frog named Fred and said, "Hi, Fred! Do you want to play?" Fred said, "Yes, Sam! But first, help me release this ball. It is stuck in the mud." Sam used his beak to help Fred get the ball out of the mud. They were very happy and started to play. As they played, a big wind came and took the ball high up in the sky. Sam and Fred were sad because they thought they lost the ball. But then, a bird named Bella flew down with the ball in her beak. "I found your ball!" she said. Sam, Fred, and Bella played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in an ancient forest, there was a big rock. This rock had special minerals in it. All the animals in the forest liked the rock. One day, a little bird and a squirrel found the rock at the same time. They both wanted the minerals. The bird said, "I saw it first, it's mine!" The squirrel said, "No, I saw it first, it's mine!" They did not want to share. An old wise owl saw them fight. The owl said, "Why don't you both share the rock? You can both have the minerals." The bird and the squirrel thought about it. They decided to share the rock and became friends. The moral of the story is that sharing is better than fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a soft bunny named Bob. Bob lived in a big forest with many trees and vines. He liked to play with his friends, the birds and the squirrels. One day, Bob found a big vine in his way. He could not go past it to play with his friends. Bob was sad. He wanted to remove the vine so he could play with his friends again. Bob asked his friends for help. The birds and the squirrels came to help Bob. Together, they all worked to remove the vine. They pulled and pulled until the vine was gone. Now Bob could play with his friends again. They were all very happy.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lisa. She was very busy running around and playing with her toys. One day, her mommy called her to come inside because it was time to go on a trip. Lisa hopped in the car and was so excited to go! When they reached their destination, Lisa's mommy said, "We're going to stay here for a holiday. See, there are so many places to explore!" Lisa smiled and ran around exploring her new surroundings. Every day, Lisa would play and adventure in the fields around her. But in the evenings, she would sit quietly with her mommy and speak about the day. Her mommy would tell her all about the birds, the trees and the flowers. It was such a busy and fun holiday for Lisa, but soon it was time to drive back home. Lisa was sad to go but happy to have spent her special time away.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jill. She was three years old and loved to make things clean. One day, she was playing with some math toys. Jill really enjoyed math and thought it was attractive. She worked for hours to arrange the numbers and patterns. Jill's mom saw her daughter working and said, "Do you like math, sweetheart?" Jill smiled and said, "Yes, I like cleaning up the numbers and patterns." Jill's mom smiled back at her daughter and said, "That's so great. Keep up the hard work!" So, Jill kept cleaning and arranging the numbers and patterns. She was very proud of herself and happy that she made something attractive and helpful.
