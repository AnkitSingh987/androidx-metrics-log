Lily was sad. She had a bad day at school. Some kids were mean to her and took her crayons. She wanted to cry, but she did not. She was a big girl. When she came home, she saw her mom in the kitchen. Her mom was making a pie. It smelled good. Lily loved pie. She ran to her mom and hugged her. "Mom, can I have some pie?" she asked. "Of course, sweetie. But you have to wait a little. The pie is still hot. Why don't you tell me about your day?" her mom said. Lily told her mom everything. She told her how the kids were mean and how she felt sad. Her mom listened and nodded. She comforted Lily with kind words and a kiss. "I'm sorry you had a bad day, Lily. But you know what? You are a brave and smart girl. And I'm very proud of you. You don't need to worry about those kids. They are spoiled. They don't know how to share and be nice. You are better than them. And you have me. I love you very much. And I have a surprise for you." her mom said. "What is it, mom?" Lily asked. Her mom smiled and opened the oven. She took out the pie. It was golden and crispy. She cut a big slice and put it on a plate. She added some whipped cream and sprinkles. She gave it to Lily. "Here you go, Lily. A special pie for a special girl. Enjoy!" her mom said. Lily's eyes sparkled. She took the plate and thanked her mom. She took a bite of the pie. It was warm and sweet and yummy. She felt happy. She forgot about her bad day. She had the best mom and the best pie in the world.

Once upon a time, there was a robot that was very foolish. It lived in a big house with a family of five. Every day the robot did silly things and behaved very badly. The family became quite concerned about their robot. One day, the mother sat the robot down for a talk. She looked at it and said, "Robot, why do you behave like this? You must be more careful and act nicely." The robot was very sorry and promised to be better. But soon after, the robot began to misbehave again. It ran around the house and caused a big mess. The father of the family became very frustrated and yelled at the robot. "Robot, you must stop this foolish behavior," he said. "It's very naughty. If you can't behave properly, we will have to get rid of you." The robot looked very sad and promised to try harder. Ever since then, the robot has been very well behaved. It follows all of the rules, and helps around the house. All of the family are very proud of their robot and love it very much!

Once upon a time, there was a sad reindeer named Remy. Remy was sad because he had no friends to play with. One day, Remy decided to fill a big bag with toys and share them with other animals in the forest. As Remy walked through the forest, he met a big bear named Benny. Remy gave Benny a toy from his bag. Benny was very happy and wanted to play with Remy. They played and laughed together. Remy was not sad anymore. But then, something unexpected happened! Benny was not a bear, but a reindeer like Remy! Benny had been wearing a bear costume all along. Remy was so surprised and happy to find a reindeer friend. Now, Remy and Benny played together every day and were never sad again.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a long walk. He saw the big, round moon in the sky. Tim wanted to talk to the moon, so he shouted, "Hello, Moon!" The moon heard Tim and said, "Hello, Tim! What can I tell you today?" Tim was very happy that the moon could talk to him. He asked, "Moon, can you tell me a story?" The moon smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Once upon a time, there was a little dog who loved to play. One night, the dog looked up and saw me, the moon. The dog barked and I said hello. We became good friends and every night, the dog would come out to play and talk with me." Tim loved the story and said, "Thank you, Moon! I want to be your friend too!" The moon was very happy and from that day on, Tim and the moon were the best of friends.
