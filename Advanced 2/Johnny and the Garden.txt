One day, in the backyard, there were two friends playing. One was Johnny and the other was Jilly. They were playing in the garden and Jilly was looking at the plants while Johnny was doing somersaults. Suddenly, Jilly saw something by her feet. It was a seed and she said, "Let's join this seed and plant it. Then we'll see what grows!" Johnny was excited, so the two friends joined their hands together and put the seed in the ground. They waited for the seed to grow and everyday they watered it and made sure it got enough sun. One day, the two friends were exploring the garden and saw something growing from the ground. It was a little bitter plant! They were so happy that the seed had finally grown! The moral of the story is that if we do things together and work together, anything is possible!

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some icy treats to eat. Tim liked the cold and sweet ice cream. His mom liked the icy fruit bars. They walked down the store aisle, looking at all the treats. While they were looking, Tim saw a big, green frog. The frog looked at Tim and nodded. Tim was very surprised. He asked the frog, "Can you really nod?" The frog nodded again. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, the frog can nod!" Mom came to see the frog. But the frog did not nod for her. She said, "Tim, frogs can't nod. It's just a toy." Tim was sad. But then, when mom looked away, the frog nodded at Tim again! Tim knew the frog was special. He smiled and nodded back.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in an ordinary house with his mom and dad. One day, Tim saw a big pole in his yard. He did not know what it was for, but he wanted to play with it. Tim asked his mom if he could play with the pole. His mom said, "You may play with the pole, but be careful." Tim was very happy and went outside to play. He used the pole to make a tent with a big blanket. While Tim was playing, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and sat on the pole. Tim was very surprised! He asked the bird, "Do you want to play with me?" The bird nodded its head and they played together all day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the doctor with his mom. The doctor wanted to see what was inside Tim's body. He used a special thing called an x-ray. The x-ray could show the doctor the unknown parts inside Tim's body. When the doctor looked at the x-ray, he saw something appear on the picture. It was a small toy car! Tim had swallowed the car when he was playing. The doctor told Tim's mom that the car was in his tummy. Tim's mom was happy to know what was inside Tim's tummy. She thanked the doctor for finding the unknown toy car. The doctor told Tim to be careful when he played with his toys. Tim promised to be more careful and not swallow any more toys.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy had many friends, but he loved to play with his best friend, Tommy the turtle. They played all day and had a lot of fun. One day, Remy and Tommy decided to play a game. They wanted to see who could get the best score. Remy was very fast and Tommy was very slow. They knew that Remy would win the race, but Tommy had an idea. He told Remy that if he could accept that he was slow, he could still try to be successful. Remy thought about it and agreed. They made a plan and started the game. Remy ran as fast as he could, while Tommy took his time. Tommy knew that he would not win, but he was happy to try. In the end, Remy won the race and got the best score. But, Tommy was successful too, because he did not give up and he accepted that he was slow. Remy and Tommy were both very happy and they learned that it is important to accept who you are and always try your best.
