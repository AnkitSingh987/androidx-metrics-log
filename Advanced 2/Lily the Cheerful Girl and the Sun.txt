One day, a cheerful girl named Lily wanted to wear her pretty dress. She put it on and went outside to play. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Lily felt happy in her dress. Lily saw a small tree with red fruits. She picked one and took a bite. The taste was sweet and yummy. She liked the fruit a lot. Lily picked more fruits and put them in her dress pocket. Lily went back home and showed her mom the fruits. Mom was happy and said, "Let's make a fruit salad!" They made a tasty salad and shared it with the whole family. Everyone loved the taste and smiled. Lily felt proud and joyful.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to study animals in books and pictures. They want to see a real tiger someday. They ask their mom and dad to take them to the zoo. At the zoo, they see many animals. They see monkeys, elephants, giraffes, and bears. They are happy and excited. They run and point and ask questions. They look for the tiger. They find the tiger in a big cage. The tiger is orange and black and has sharp teeth and claws. The tiger is lying on the ground and sleeping. Ben and Mia are a little disappointed. They want to see the tiger move and roar. They wait and watch. They are quiet and alert. They hope the tiger will wake up. They whisper to each other. "Look, Mia, the tiger is opening his eyes!" Ben says. "Wow, Ben, he is so big and beautiful!" Mia says. The tiger yawns and stretches. He gets up and walks around the cage. He looks at Ben and Mia and the other people. He makes a loud roar. Ben and Mia clap and cheer. They are not afraid. They are amazed. The tiger stops and stares at them. He seems curious and friendly. He comes closer and rubs his head against the bars. Ben and Mia reach out and touch his fur. It is soft and warm. The tiger purrs and licks their hands. Ben and Mia giggle and smile. They feel a connection with the tiger. They have studied the tiger in books and pictures, but now they have seen and touched a real tiger. They have learned something new and wonderful. They are happy and grateful. They say goodbye to the tiger and thank him for the experience. They wave and walk away. They will always remember the tiger.

Tom and Lily were going to visit their grandma. They were very happy, because grandma always gave them hugs and kisses and told them stories. Mommy told them to pack their bags with some clothes and toys. She also packed some food for the road, like sandwiches, apples and cookies. Tom and Lily each had a small bag, but Tom wanted to take more toys than Lily. He put his teddy bear, his car, his ball, his dinosaur and his puzzle in his bag. He also took some of his sister's cookies and hid them under his toys. Lily only took her doll, her book and her crayons. She saw Tom taking her cookies, but she did not say anything. She thought maybe he was hungry. When they got in the car, Mommy checked their bags. She saw that Tom's bag was very heavy and Lily's bag was very light. She asked Tom why he packed so many things. Tom said he wanted to play with them at grandma's house. Mommy said he did not need so many toys, because grandma had some toys too. She also said he should not take his sister's cookies, because that was not nice. She took some of his toys and cookies out of his bag and gave them back to Lily. She told Tom to say sorry to Lily. Tom felt bad and said sorry. Lily smiled and said it was okay. She gave him a hug and shared one of her cookies with him. Mommy said they were good children and she was proud of them. She said they should always be kind and share with each other, because that is what family does. She said they would have a lot of fun at grandma's house, and maybe get some more cookies if they behaved well. Tom and Lily nodded and said they loved Mommy and each other. They ate their food and sang songs in the car. They were very excited to see grandma. They learned that packing less is better, and that sharing is caring.
