Once upon a time, there was a red dragon. The red dragon lived in a big cave. The red dragon liked to clap his hands and dance. One day, the red dragon went to the park. He saw kids playing and clapping their hands. The red dragon was happy and wanted to play too. He clapped his hands and started to dance. But the kids were scared of the red dragon. They ran away and left the park. The red dragon was sad and went back to his cave. He clapped his hands, but he was all alone. The red dragon cried because he had no friends to play with.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a big race. The winner would get a prize. A little boy named Tim wanted to win the prize. Tim saw a dangerous path that could make him win faster. His friend, Sam, said, "Don't go there, Tim! It's dangerous. I want to prevent you from getting hurt." Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, Sam. I will listen to you." They both ran in the race together, and even though they didn't win the prize, they were safe and happy. They learned that being safe was more important than winning a prize.

Once upon a time, there was a wide tree. A stubborn bird lived in the tree. The bird loved to sing when it did not rain. One day, it rained a lot. The bird could not sing. The bird was sad. While it rained, a big frog came to the tree. The frog liked the rain. The frog sang a happy song. The stubborn bird listened to the frog's song. The bird wanted to sing too. The bird and the frog sang together in the rain. Their song was loud and happy. They became good friends. The stubborn bird learned to sing even when it rained. The bird was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a brown and white ostrich who loved running. Everywhere it went, it ran and ran, sometimes a little too fast. One day, the ostrich was running through the forest when it came across a tidy little house. What a surprise! The ostrich ran to the door, but it couldn't open it. "Who is there?" a voice called from inside. The ostrich was so surprised, it began to shake. "It's me," the ostrich croaked. "I'm just running around." The voice laughed. "Well, why don't you come in and have some lunch with me," it said. The ostrich was so happy, it ran inside and found a nice, tidy kitchen. It enjoyed a big feast with the voice, who turned out to be a friendly old woman. They ate and chatted until it grew late, and then the ostrich ran back into the forest. From then on, whenever the ostrich was feeling lonely, it would come back to the tidy little house for some lunch and a chat.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly bug. The bug had a hard body. It lived in a small garden with many pretty flowers. The bug was not happy because it was alone, and it wanted to make friends. One day, the ugly bug met a little ant. The ant was carrying a big piece of food. The ugly bug said, "Hello, little ant. Can we share your food?" The little ant looked at the ugly bug and said, "No, you are too big and ugly. I don't want to share with you." The ugly bug felt very sad and went away. It saw a group of butterflies playing near the pretty flowers. The ugly bug went to the butterflies and asked, "Can I play with you?" The butterflies looked at the ugly bug and said, "No, you are too big and ugly. We don't want to play with you." The ugly bug was very sad and lonely. It went to the edge of the garden and cried. No one wanted to be its friend or share with it because it was big and ugly. The ugly bug stayed alone and never found any friends. And so, the ugly bug lived a sad life in the garden, never knowing the joy of sharing and friendship.
