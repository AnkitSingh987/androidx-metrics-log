Once upon a time there was a boy named Max. Max loved to match things. He always had a big pile of colorful vests, which he loved to match up together! One day Max took a bag of vests to the park. He found a big rock in the park that was very rough. He thought it would make a perfect seat. He spread his vests around the rock and began to match them up. Soon, he had a rainbow of vests all around him! Max was so proud of himself. Suddenly, a park ranger came over and said, "That looks great! Can I take a picture of your masterpiece?" Max smiled and said, "Yes!" So, the ranger took a picture. From that day on, all of Max's friends visited the park to see the colorful and unique vest match that he had created!

Once upon a time, there was a big, dependable truck. The truck had a job to carry oil from one place to another. The truck liked its job and did it every day. One day, the truck was driving on a bumpy road. The oil inside the truck was shaking a lot. The truck wanted the oil to settle, so it drove slowly and carefully. Finally, the truck reached the place where the oil needed to go. The truck was happy that the oil was safe. Everyone knew that the dependable truck could always do its job well.

One day, a little bird named Tim was flying around. He saw a beautiful chamber in a big tree. Tim wanted to settle in the chamber. But there was a problem. A big bird named Sam was already living in the chamber. Tim was envious of Sam. Tim flew to Sam and said, "I want to live in the chamber, too." Sam did not want to share the chamber with Tim. They did not know what to do. So, they decided to ask their friend, Cat, for help. Cat listened to Tim and Sam. She thought for a moment and said, "Why don't you both share the chamber? You can take turns living in it." Tim and Sam liked the idea. They settled in the chamber and lived happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. He lived in a big pond with his family. Fin was a frightened fish because he was scared of the big rocks in the pond. One day, Fin met a new friend, a turtle named Tim. Tim was not scared of the big rocks. Fin said, "I am frightened of the big rocks. Can you help me?" Tim said, "Yes, I can help you. I will push the big rocks away." Tim and Fin worked together to push the big rocks. They pushed and pushed until the rocks moved. Fin was no longer frightened of the big rocks. Fin said, "Thank you, Tim. You are a good friend." Tim said, "You're welcome, Fin. We are brave together." And they swam happily in the pond.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Jill. Jack was very proud and always wanted everything to go his way. Jill was a humble girl, who listened to others and was always willing to help. One day, Jack and Jill went for a walk in the countryside. As they wandered, they came across a big fence. Jack wanted to climb over it but Jill was afraid. Jack: “Come on! We can climb over it and explore what’s on the other side.” Jill: “No, I’m scared. I don’t want us to get hurt.” Jack: “Don’t be silly, let’s go!” Jack started to climb the fence, but he suddenly slipped and fell. He panicked and started to cry. Jill ran over to him and helped him up. She dried his tears and hugged him. Jack was embarrassed but he was also very thankful for Jill’s help. From that day onward, Jack was more humble, which meant he was able to think about the consequences of his actions. The moral of the story is to think about what could happen before you do something, and to be humble and listen to others.

Once upon a time, there was a sad star named Twinkle. Twinkle was sad because she was not as bright as the other stars. She wished she could increase her shine so everyone could see her in the sky. One day, Twinkle met a wise old star named Sparkle. "Why are you sad, Twinkle?" asked Sparkle. "I want to increase my shine," said Twinkle, "but I don't know how." Sparkle smiled and said, "I can help you, but you must be very brave." Twinkle agreed, and Sparkle took her to a secret place where there were magic rocks. "If you touch one of these rocks, your shine will increase," said Sparkle. Twinkle was scared, but she touched a rock. Suddenly, her shine grew brighter and brighter! But then, something unexpected happened. Twinkle's shine became so bright that she turned into a shooting star! She zoomed across the sky, leaving a trail of light behind her. All the other stars and people on Earth saw her and cheered. From that day on, Twinkle was no longer a sad star. She had become the most special star in the sky, and she was very happy.
