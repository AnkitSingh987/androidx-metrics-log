One day, a little ball named Bouncy was feeling sad. Bouncy was ashamed because he could not bounce as high as the other balls. His friend, Teddy the bear, saw him sitting in the corner and asked, "Why are you sad, Bouncy?" Bouncy replied, "I can't bounce high like the other balls. I feel bad." Teddy wanted to help his friend, so he thought of a plan. Teddy went to the doctor and asked for some special medicine. The doctor gave Teddy the medicine and said, "Give this to Bouncy. It will help him bounce high." Teddy thanked the doctor and went back to Bouncy. Teddy gave the medicine to Bouncy and said, "Take this, Bouncy. It will help you bounce high like the other balls." Bouncy took the medicine and soon, he could bounce very high. Bouncy was so happy and thanked Teddy for helping him. Now, Bouncy was not ashamed anymore. He could bounce high and play with the other balls. Bouncy and Teddy became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a boy named Tim found a wallet on the ground. He was very excited. The wallet was big and brown. Tim wanted to find the person who lost the wallet. He thought the person must be sad. Tim saw the sun shine on a man's face. The man looked sad. Tim walked to the man and asked, "Is this your wallet?" The man looked at the wallet and said, "Yes, that is mine! Thank you, Tim!" But the man was not a good man. He took the wallet and ran away fast. Tim was sad. He wanted to help, but the man was not nice. Tim learned that not everyone is good.

Once upon a time there was a small boy called Chris. Chris was only three years old and he had a coat. It was a rough coat: the most rough coat he had ever seen. One day Chris was walking along and he caught a glimpse of something behind a bush. He was curious, so he peeked. He saw a small rabbit! The rabbit quickly ran away, but it had left something behind. Chris was excited and he rushed to pick it up. When he picked it up, he felt something soft and furry. He held it up to his face to see what it was. It was a beautiful, fluffy, white coat. He quickly put it on and he found that it fit perfectly! He ran home, wearing his new rough coat, and was very happy.

Anna and Ben are playing with their mom's makeup. They like to put lipstick, eyeshadow and blush on their faces. They think they look funny and pretty. "Look at me, Anna!" Ben says. "I have red lips and blue eyes!" "You look silly, Ben!" Anna says. "I have pink cheeks and purple lips!" They laugh and make faces at each other in the mirror. Mom comes into the room and sees them. She is not happy. She has a gray face and a frown. "What are you doing with my makeup?" Mom asks. "You made a big mess!" "Sorry, Mom," Anna and Ben say. "We were just playing." Mom sighs and shakes her head. She takes a wet cloth and wipes their faces. "You need to reverse this, kids," Mom says. "Makeup is not for playing. It is for grown-ups." Anna and Ben nod and say sorry again. They help Mom clean the makeup and put it away. Mom hugs them and smiles. "I love you, Anna and Ben," Mom says. "But next time, please ask me before you use my things." "We love you too, Mom," Anna and Ben say. "And we will ask you next time." They kiss Mom and go to play with their toys. They decide to play with cars and dolls instead of makeup. They think they are more fun and less messy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was adventurous and loved to learn new things. One day, she decided to learn about doctors. She asked her mom to tell her a story about doctors. Her mom told her a story about a kind doctor who helped people feel better. The doctor would listen to their hearts, look in their ears, and give them medicine. Lucy thought that was amazing. She wanted to learn how to be a doctor too. So, Lucy and her mom played doctor with her toys. They took turns being the doctor and the patient. Lucy learned how to make her toys feel better. She loved being a doctor and helping others.
