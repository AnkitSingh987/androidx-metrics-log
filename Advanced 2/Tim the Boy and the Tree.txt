Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, Tim saw a big tree with a root sticking out of the ground. Tim wanted to touch the root, but his mom told him not to. She said it was rude to touch things that did not belong to him. Tim did not listen to his mom. He touched the root when she was not looking. Tim felt happy, but he knew he did something wrong. He knew he should not have touched the root. He felt bad for being rude. Later, Tim told his mom he was sorry for touching the root. His mom was happy he said sorry. She told him it is important to listen and be kind. Tim learned that being rude is not nice, and he should always listen to his mom. From that day on, Tim was a good boy and never touched things that did not belong to him.

Once upon a time, there was a kind old lady who loved to paint. Every day, her favourite activity was to use her pastel colours to create beautiful, bright pictures. One day, a little boy came to her door. When the kind old lady opened the door, she saw that the boy had a sad face. She asked him what was wrong, and the boy said he was hungry. Her compassionate heart filled with love, and the kind old lady smiled kindly at the little boy. The kind old lady offered the boy a delicious lunch and he ate it with joy. Afterwards, the boy told the kind old lady that he hated painting and asked why she spent so much time doing it. She smiled and said "because it makes me happy". But the little boy continued to hate painting and said bad things about the beautiful pictures the kind old lady painted. He said that he didn't like the pastel colours and she should paint with only one colour. The kind old lady was so sad that she closed her door, and never opened it again. She stopped painting and instead just stared at the pictures she had created previously. But her beloved pastel colours were never seen again.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Max. Max could do many things that other dogs could not do. He could think and talk like people. Max loved to help his friends in the town. One day, Max saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and said, "Help me, Max! I cannot get down!" Max knew this was an emergency. He thought of a plan to help the cat. Max asked his friend, a big bird, to fly up and help the cat down. The bird helped the cat, and the cat was safe. After the rescue, the cat thanked Max and the bird. Max felt happy that he could think and help his friends. Everyone in the town knew that Max was a gifted dog who could solve any problem. They loved Max, and Max loved them too.

Once upon a time, in an ancient house, there lived a boy named Tom and his cat named Cut. They loved to play and have fun together. One day, Tom and Cut found a big box in the house. The box was very old and dusty. Tom said to Cut, "Let's open the box and see what's inside!" They opened the box and found many colorful blocks. Tom had an idea, "Let's add the blocks and make a big tower!" So, they started to add the blocks one by one, and the tower grew taller and taller. As they added the last block, something unexpected happened. The tower started to shake and suddenly, it turned into a big, friendly giant! The giant said, "Thank you for building me! Now, let's all play together!" Tom, Cut, and the giant played and had lots of fun for the rest of the day.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to play with his ball. One day, Tim saw an unknown big box near his house. He was very excited. Tim ran to the box and saw a note on it. The note said, "Rise to the top and get the prize." Tim wanted the prize, so he tried to climb the box. He tried and tried, but it was too high. Then, Tim had an idea. He got his ball and threw it at the box. The box opened and a big, shiny prize came out. Tim was very happy and showed the prize to his friends. They all played together with the prize and had lots of fun.
