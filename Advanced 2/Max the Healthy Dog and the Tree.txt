One day, a healthy dog named Max was playing near his house. He loved to run and jump in the sun. Max had a big, strong ball to play with. He was very happy. Max saw a little girl named Lily. She was sad. Max wanted to help her. He went to her and said, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" Max nodded and wagged his tail. They looked and looked for the toy. Max saw it near a big tree. He ran to get it, but he was going too fast. Max bumped into the tree and heard a loud "CRACK!" Max was scared. He had break a branch. But Lily was happy. She had her toy back. She hugged Max and said, "Thank you, Max. You are a good friend." Max wagged his tail and felt happy again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a green bird in a tree. The bird had a pretty bracelet in its beak. Lily wanted to be friends with the bird. Lily had an idea. She found a big leaf and started to fold it. She made it look like a bracelet too. She showed the green bird her new leaf bracelet. The green bird liked the leaf bracelet. It flew down and gave Lily the pretty bracelet. Lily and the green bird became good friends. They played together and showed each other their bracelets.

Tom was an adventurous boy. He liked to explore new places and try new things. One day, he found a big bag of popcorn in the kitchen. He wondered what it would taste like. He opened the bag and put some popcorn in his mouth. It was crunchy and salty and yummy. He ate more and more popcorn. He felt happy and full. But then, he saw something on the bag. It was a word he did not know. It said "expired". He asked his mom what it meant. She said it meant the popcorn was old and bad. It could make him sick. Tom felt scared and sad. He did not want to be sick. He spit out the popcorn he had in his mouth. He threw the bag in the trash. He said sorry to his mom. She said it was okay. She gave him a hug and a glass of water. She told him that sometimes, food can rot. It means it gets spoiled and yucky. She said he should always check the date on the bag before he eats something. She said he was a good boy for being adventurous, but he should also be careful. Tom learned his lesson. He decided to be more careful next time. He still liked to explore new places and try new things, but he also liked to be healthy and safe. He thanked his mom for teaching him. He said he loved her. She said she loved him too. They smiled and hugged.

Janie and Mommy were in the garden and Janie looked so happy. She saw some red flowers and anxiously asked, "Mommy, can I pick the flowers?" Mommy replied, "Sure Janie, but afterwards, why don't you make something out of them?" Janie smiled and ran around the garden and found a big piece of cardboard. Mommy told Janie to flatten the cardboard on the grass. Then Janie carefully placed each of the red flowers on the cardboard. Mommy said, "Now all you need to do is wait!" Janie didn't understand, but she waited anyway. After a while, the red flowers started to bloom and they looked beautiful! Janie was so happy and she said, "Look Mommy, I made all this myself!" Mommy smiled, wiped a tear from her eye and hugged Janie tight. Janie felt so proud of her handiwork and she walked around the garden telling all her favourite flowers how she made them bloom.

Mike and his mom were taking a walk in the park. Mike was happily hopping and skipping. He felt so free. His mom stopped walking and noticed something on the ground. She picked it up and said to Mike, "Look, it's a cane! It must belong to somebody." Mike was curious and asked, "What do you do with a cane?" His mom replied, "Some people need a cane to help them walk." They decided to find the owner. As they continued their walk, they could see somebody in the distance. At a closer look, the person had a cane! Mike and his mom were so happy and rushed to help. After a few minutes of conversation, they learned that the person had lost his cane and was so grateful to have it back. He thanked Mike and his mom, and they were very pleased to help. Mike smiled and felt happy that he had helped somebody. He could now understand the importance of a cane. He knew that in the future, he too would be grateful if he ever needed a cane.
