One day, a little boy named Tim went to his friend's house to play. They had a lot of fun playing with toys and running around the house. Tim saw some big furniture and felt helpless because he could not move it. Then, his friend's mom came in and said, "Kids, please be careful and do not play near the furniture. It is very big and heavy. You could get hurt." Tim and his friend listened to her and moved away from the furniture. As they were playing, Tim's friend suddenly pointed at the window. "Look, a bird is trying to come inside!" They watched as the bird flew inside the house. The bird was scared and didn't know how to get out. Tim and his friend felt bad for the bird, so they opened the door and helped the bird fly back outside. They were happy that they could help the bird. The moral of the story is to always be careful and listen to adults, and to help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog. This frog lived near a river. The river was big and had many fish. The frog liked to jump and play with the fish. One day, the frog saw an angry bird. The bird was not happy. The bird said, "Stop playing with the fish!" The frog did not want to make the bird angry. So, the frog said, "Okay, I will not play with the fish." The next day, the frog went back to the river. The frog saw the fish and wanted to play. The frog remembered what the angry bird said. The frog did not want to make the bird angry again. So, the frog did not play with the fish. The frog just sat by the river and watched the fish swim. The frog did not want to repeat the same mistake.

Once upon a time, there was a funny clown. He had a big red nose and big shoes. He lived in a small house. The clown was very good at making people laugh. Everyone loved him because he was brilliant at his job. One day, the clown went to a big party. There were many kids at the party. They all wanted to see the clown do funny tricks. The clown made funny faces, walked in a silly way, and made the kids laugh a lot. They were very happy. After the party, the clown went back to his small house. He was very tired. He took off his big shoes and his red nose. The clown lay down in his bed to rest. He closed his eyes and started to settle down. Soon, the clown was fast asleep, dreaming of the next fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a little sister named Emma. Lily loved to decorate their room. She wanted to make it look modern. One day, Lily and Emma were putting up new things on the wall. They were having lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came through the window and blew all the things off the wall! Lily and Emma were surprised. The sisters did not give up. They put the things back up on the wall. This time, they made sure the window was closed. When they were done, the room looked very modern. Lily and Emma were happy. They learned that sometimes, unexpected things happen, but they can still make things work.

Once upon a time, a little girl was playing in her room. She loved to look at all the furniture. One particular piece was her favorite because it was very thick. Suddenly, she heard a loud sound coming from outside. She opened the window to take a look, but she saw a big storm rolling in. The little girl's mom came in and said, "We need to go now! Grab your things and let's go. It's not safe here." The little girl was sad to leave her favorite piece of furniture behind, but she didn't want to get hurt. So she grabbed her things and ran outside with her mom. But it was too late. As soon as they were outside, the storm hit with a loud crash. The wind was so strong that it blew furniture into the air. The little girl cried out, but it was too late. The thick furniture smashed against the side of their house. The little girl fought back tears as she followed her mom to safety. From that day on, she remembered to always stay warned of bad weather.
