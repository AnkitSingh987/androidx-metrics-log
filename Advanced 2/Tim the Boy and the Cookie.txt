Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, Tim was playing with his toy car. He was so happy. But then, the toy car did not move. It was broken. Tim was sad. He did not know how to fix it. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Don't be sad, Tim. I have a treat for you." Tim's eyes got big. He loved treats. Tim's mom gave him a big, yummy cookie. Tim was happy again. He forgot about his broken toy car. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's little sister, Sue, walked in. She was holding Tim's toy car. Sue had fixed it! Tim was so surprised. He thought his toy car was broken forever. Tim learned that he was ignorant about his sister's skills. Tim and Sue played with the toy car together and shared the yummy cookie. They were both very happy.

Once upon a time there was a girl who wanted to be the best in everything she did. She was very competitive. One day, the girl got a special gift. It was a wooden box with a button on top. She was very excited to take a look. She opened the box and saw something strange. Suddenly, the box started to stand on its own! She jumped up and down. Inside the box was a special toy. Even though it was small, it could do lots of incredible things. The girl was amazed at all the fun activities she could do with her new toy. The girl was super happy with her new gift. From then on, she trained really hard to be the best and she was so excited to show everyone her awesome gift.

One night, there was a lonely bear in the woods. He looked up at the stars and sighed. He was longing for a friend. All of a sudden, he heard a sound. It was coming from the other side of the woods. He decided to spread his legs and walk to see what was there. As the bear got closer, he saw a line of animals. They were all chatting to each other - even the rabbits and birds. The bear was so happy! He walked up to the line of animals and explained that he was lonely. He wanted to join them. The animals welcomed him with open arms. They spread themselves out to make some room for the bear. Now the bear has new friends and he's never lonely again. They meet every night in the woods to chat and play.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird named Bobo. He loved to fly and sing. One day, Bobo saw a tap in the park. The tap was new and shiny. Bobo wanted to play with the tap. Bobo flew down and sat on the tap. He said, "Hello, tap! Let's be friends!" The tap did not say anything. Bobo tried again, "Do you want to play with me?" But the tap still did not say anything. Bobo felt sad. Bobo decided to lie down on the grass near the tap. He looked up at the sky and saw a big cloud. The cloud looked like a giant bird. Bobo smiled and said, "I may not have a friend in the tap, but I have a friend in the sky!" And with that, Bobo flew up to the sky and played with the cloud, feeling happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a very important letter that he needed to mail. Tim waited for a whole week to mail it because he was busy playing with his toys. One sunny day, Tim saw that the mail truck was available. He ran outside with his letter and gave it to the mailman. The mailman smiled and took the letter. Tim was happy that he finally mailed the letter. But, oh no! Tim forgot to write the name and address on the letter. The mailman could not send it. Tim felt sad, but then he remembered that he had another letter ready. He ran inside, got the other letter, and gave it to the mailman. This time, it had the name and address on it. Tim was happy again, and he learned to always check his letters before mailing them.
