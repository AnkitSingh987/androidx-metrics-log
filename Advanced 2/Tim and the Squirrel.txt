Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a large brush. He loved to use it to paint pretty pictures. One day, he took his brush to the park to paint a big tree. As he was painting, a friendly squirrel came up to him. "Hi, Tim!" said the squirrel. "Can I borrow your large brush to clean my home? I will return it when I am done." Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, but please be careful with it." The squirrel took the large brush and went to clean his home. Tim waited and played in the park. After some time, the squirrel returned with the brush. "Thank you, Tim! Your large brush helped me clean my home very well!" Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, Mr. Squirrel! I'm glad it helped you." From that day on, Tim and the squirrel became good friends. They shared the large brush and painted many beautiful pictures together. And they always remembered to return it to each other when they were done.

One day, a boy named Tim and his friend Sue went to play a game. They wanted to see who could get the best score. Tim was very good at the game, but Sue was incredible. "Wow, Sue, you are so good!" said Tim. "I can't believe it!" Sue was happy, but she did not like to hear Tim complain. "It's okay, Tim. We can play again and you can try to get a better score." Just then, something unexpected happened. A big dog ran into their game and took the ball! Tim and Sue were very surprised. The dog wanted to play too, and he was even better at the game than Sue! "Wow, the dog is incredible!" said Tim. "Now we can all play together and have fun!" So Tim, Sue, and their new dog friend played the game and had a great time. No one complained, and they all tried to get the best score.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Spot. Spot loved to go for walks. Every day, he would walk with his friend, a little girl named Sally. One sunny day, while they were on their walk, they saw a boy crying. Sally asked the boy, "Why are you crying?" The boy said, "I lost my toy car." Sally and Spot decided to help the boy find his toy car. They looked all around, under the trees and behind the bushes. Finally, Spot found the toy car. He picked it up with his mouth and gave it to the boy. The boy was very happy and thanked Spot and Sally. They all became good friends and went on walks together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was deaf, but she could feel things very well. She had a big dream to play with other kids and have fun. One sunny day, Amy went to the park. She saw a boy named Tom. Tom was playing with a ball. Amy wanted to play too, so she walked up to Tom and tapped him on the shoulder. Tom looked at her and said, "Hi! Do you want to play with me?" Amy nodded her head and smiled. She couldn't hear Tom, but she could feel the ball when he threw it to her. They played catch and had a great time. Tom and Amy became good friends, and they always played together at the park. Amy's dream came true. She was very happy to have a friend like Tom who understood her. They played together every day, and Amy felt that her world was full of fun and love.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful plant. It was very special because it could glow at night. The plant lived in a big garden with many other plants and flowers. One day, a little girl named Lily found the glowing plant. She was so happy to see it. She wanted to show her friends the powerful plant, so they could see it glow too. At night, Lily and her friends went to the garden. They saw the powerful plant start to glow. Everyone was so excited and they all clapped. The glowing plant made them all smile and they played in the garden until bedtime.
