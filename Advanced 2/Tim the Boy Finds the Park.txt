One day, a nice boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a shiny medal on the ground. He picked it up and looked at it. It was very pretty. Tim had a big question in his mind. Whose medal was this? Tim walked around the park with the medal. He asked his friends if they lost a medal. But no one said it was theirs. Tim felt sad because he wanted to find the owner. He kept asking people in the park his question. Finally, Tim found a girl who was crying. He asked her the question about the medal. The girl's face lit up and she said it was hers. She was so happy to have her medal back. Tim felt good for helping her. They became friends and played together in the park.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to jump. She jumped everywhere! She even jumped to the top of a tall palm tree. It was so high, but she was brave! Once she was at the top of the palm tree she saw how long the world was. She could see far away, and it was so beautiful. She even saw a big rainbow stretching across the sky. She felt so happy! The little girl loved sitting in the palm tree and watching the world go by. But soon she was ready to jump down. With a big jump, she made it safely back to the ground. She was grinning from ear to ear and feeling so proud. From that day on, the little girl never forgot how long and beautiful the world can be. She knew she could climb tall palm trees and take a look. She jumped for joy!

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree in a small park. The tree had a big heart. It loved to help others. One hot day, a little boy named Tim came to the park. He was very thirsty. He wanted to drink some water, but he could not find any. The big, thick tree saw Tim and felt sad. It wanted to help Tim. So, the tree used its big heart to think of a way. It shook its branches and leaves. Then, something unexpected happened. Water began to fall from the leaves like rain. Tim was very happy. He opened his mouth and started to drink the water. He was not thirsty anymore. He thanked the big, thick tree with the big heart. From that day on, Tim and the tree became best friends. They played together in the park every day, and the tree always gave Tim water when he was thirsty.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved his owner, a kind lady who wore a pretty blouse. Every day, they played together in the park. One day, the lady threw a ball for Spot to catch. Spot ran fast to get the ball. When he got the ball, he returned to the lady. She was so happy and clapped her hands. Spot and the lady played fetch many times. They had a lot of fun. Spot always returned the ball to the lady in the pretty blouse. They were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play dress up and make her own fashion. Lucy had many pretty dresses and fun hats. One day, Lucy saw a big box in her room. She was a little scared, but she trusted her mom and dad. Lucy opened the box and found a new dress inside. It was the most beautiful dress she had ever seen! She put it on and started to dance around. Then, something unexpected happened. The dress began to talk to her! The dress said, "I am a magic dress, and I can make your dreams come true!" Lucy was surprised, but she trusted the dress. They played together and had the best day ever. And from that day on, Lucy and her magic dress were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a reindeer named Remy. Remy was not like other reindeer. He could not fly. This made Remy sad. One day, Remy met a big bird named Benny. Benny said, "I can help you get up in the sky. It is easy." Remy was excited. He wanted to fly with Benny. Benny told Remy, "To fly, you must run fast and jump high." Remy practiced running and jumping every day. He got better and better. Benny watched Remy and said, "Soon, you will fly." One day, Remy ran very fast and jumped very high. He thought he would fly. But instead, Remy did something unexpected. He jumped so high that he landed on a cloud. Remy was surprised. He did not know he could do that. From the cloud, Remy could see everything. He saw his friends down below. Remy called to Benny, "I did not fly, but I am high up in the sky." Benny smiled and said, "You found a new way to be up high. That is even better." Remy was happy. He did not need to fly like other reindeer. He could jump on clouds. Remy knew he was special. And from that day on, Remy and Benny played together in the sky, jumping from cloud to cloud.
