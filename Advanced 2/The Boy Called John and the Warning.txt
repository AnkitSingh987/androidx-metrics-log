Once there was a boy called John. John was quite popular and he liked thinking about his days at school. One day, he was walking home from school, thinking about the day, when he noticed a sheet of paper lying on the ground. He reached for it and picked it up, thinking about what it could be. He was excited as he opened it but was disappointed when he saw what was written, it was a warning! Someone was warning him to stop being so popular, or else. He thought about that for a while before deciding to ignore the warning. The next day when he went to school, he found out that he was no longer popular, everyone was avoiding him and nobody wanted to talk with him. He had difficulty understanding why and thought about it a lot. He was sad and confused, he couldn't understand why everyone had become so cold to him. He then realised that the warning he had seen on the sheet of paper must have been right, and that he should have taken it seriously.

One day, Tom and Sally went for a walk. They saw a big puddle on the ground. Tom wanted to pass the puddle, but Sally was scared. Tom said, "Don't worry, I will help you pass the puddle." Sally felt better and they held hands to pass the puddle. But as they tried to pass, Sally slipped and fell into the puddle. She was frustrated and started to cry. Just then, a friendly frog jumped out of the puddle. The frog said, "Hi, I'm Fred the Frog. I can help you pass the puddle too!" Sally stopped crying and smiled. With Fred's help, Tom, Sally, and Fred all passed the puddle together. They became good friends and had a fun day playing in the park.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Sam and Jason. Sam was very excited as it was his turn to choose what they would do. Sam chose to go to the park and play on the swings. Jason was not so keen on this idea. He wanted to go to a different park and play on the see-saw instead. Sam and Jason began to argue. "No, I chose the swings!" said Sam. Jason was not happy. He shouted, "I don't want to go to the swings. I hate them! Can't we go to the other park?" Suddenly, they heard a tap noise coming from behind them. Looking around they saw an old man standing there. He told them they should choose wisely how they spend their time. The man said, "Arguing over something so small is terrible. It will only lead to more conflict and upset." Sam and Jason thought about what the man said. Finally, they decided to go to the other park and share the see-saw together. It was a much better decision and they both enjoyed the ride.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Bobo. Bobo loved to play with his red ball. One day, he saw a big brick house. He was very alert and wanted to see what was inside. Bobo went into the house and found a big room with lots of toys. He played with the toys and had lots of fun. But then, he saw a shadow on the wall. It looked like a big cat. Bobo was scared, but he wanted to be brave. Bobo walked towards the shadow and realized it was just a toy cat. He was happy and not scared anymore. He played with the toy cat and his red ball. From that day on, Bobo knew that sometimes things look scary, but they might not be.

Anna and Ben were twins. They liked to play with their toys. They had a baby doll, a car, a ball and a brush. One day, they found a bottle of polish in the kitchen. It was shiny and red. "Let's polish our toys!" Anna said. "They will look new and pretty!" "OK!" Ben agreed. "I will polish the car and the ball. You polish the baby and the brush." They took the polish and their toys to their room. They opened the bottle and poured some polish on their toys. They rubbed and rubbed with the brush. The toys became very shiny and slippery. "Look, Ben!" Anna said. "The baby is so shiny! She looks like a real infant!" Ben looked at the baby doll. It was covered with polish. Its eyes were closed. Its mouth was open. It did not move. "Anna, the baby is dead!" Ben cried. "You killed the baby with the polish!" Anna was scared. She dropped the baby doll. She ran to Ben and hugged him. "I'm sorry, Ben!" Anna sobbed. "I didn't mean to kill the baby! I wanted to make her pretty!" Ben hugged Anna back. He was sad and angry. "It's OK, Anna," Ben said. "It was an accident. But we should not play with polish. It is bad for toys. And for babies. And for us." They threw away the polish and the brush. They washed their hands and their toys. They put the baby doll in a box. They said sorry to the baby. They hoped the baby would forgive them. They never played with polish again.
