Title: Amy the Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play outside in the sun. One day, she found a box. The box was locked. Amy wanted to unlock the box to see what was inside. Amy asked her honest friend, Tom, for help. Tom said, "Be careful, Amy. There might be poison inside the box." Amy was scared, but she still wanted to unlock the box. She knew Tom was an honest friend and he would help her. Tom and Amy found a key to unlock the box. They opened it very slowly. Inside the box, there was no poison. It was a pretty toy! Tom and Amy were happy. They played with the toy and had fun. They knew that being honest and careful helped them unlock the box safely.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree in a small park. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw the tree and wanted to give it a hug. Tim said, "Mom, can I give the tree a hug?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim, you can give the tree a hug." So, Tim went to the tree and gave it a big hug. But the tree was upset because it did not like hugs. The upset tree started to shake and drop its leaves. Tim got scared and ran to his mom. The tree kept shaking and all the leaves fell down. The park became messy and everyone was upset. Now, the park was not fun anymore. The people in the park had to go home because of the upset tree. Tim learned that sometimes it is better not to give hugs to things that don't like them.

One day, a bald queen was in her big home. She was sad because she had no hair. She would look in the mirror and hope for hair to grow. She tried to put on a hat, but it did not make her happy. "Hello, queen," said a small bug who lived in the home. "Why are you sad?" "I want hair on my head," said the queen. "But I am bald and it makes me feel bad." The bug had an idea. He went to his bug friends and they all worked together. They found a lot of pretty flowers and leaves. The bugs used the flowers and leaves to make a beautiful crown for the queen. The queen put the crown on her head and looked in the mirror. She smiled and felt happy. The bald queen did not need hair anymore, because she had the most beautiful crown in the world. The queen and the bugs became good friends, and they all lived happily in the big home.

One day, a big crocodile went to a pond. The crocodile was very enormous. He saw many fish in the pond. He wanted to eat the fish. He thought, "I will count how many fish there are." The crocodile started to count. "One fish, two fish, three fish," he said. He was very happy. He saw a little bird near the pond. The bird was watching the crocodile count the fish. "Hello, bird," said the crocodile. "I am counting the fish. I want to eat them." The bird was scared but said, "Okay, crocodile. But you can't eat all the fish. You can eat only one." The crocodile agreed. He ate one fish. The bird was happy. The crocodile was happy too. They both played near the pond. The enormous crocodile learned to share and be a good friend.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a very intelligent dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to cook pies for all his friends. One day, he decided to make a big pie for everyone to share. Buddy invited his best friend, a little bird named Chirpy, to help him cook the pie. Chirpy was very excited and said, "Yes, I want to help you cook the pie, Buddy!" They worked together to make the pie. Buddy mixed the ingredients and Chirpy helped to put the pie in the oven. When the pie was ready, Buddy and Chirpy took it out of the oven. It smelled so good! All their friends came over to eat the pie. They all said, "Thank you, Buddy and Chirpy, for the yummy pie!" Buddy and Chirpy were very happy to share their pie with their friends. And from that day on, they cooked pies together and shared them with everyone.
