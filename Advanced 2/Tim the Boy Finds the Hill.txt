Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to run and rush all day. Tim was very healthy and strong. One day, Tim saw a big hill with ice on it. He wanted to play on the ice. Tim told his mom about the ice hill. She said, "Be careful, Tim. Ice can be very slippery." Tim said, "Okay, mom. I will be careful." He put on his warm clothes and went outside to play on the ice hill. At the ice hill, Tim remembered what his mom said. He did not rush on the ice. He walked very slow and careful. He had a lot of fun playing on the ice hill. In the end, Tim went home safe and happy. His mom was proud of him for being careful on the ice.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely duck named Ducky. Ducky lived near a pond with no other ducks around. One day, he found a telephone near his pond. Ducky did not know what to do with it. A little boy named Timmy saw Ducky with the telephone. Timmy said, "Hi Ducky, do you need help with the telephone?" Ducky nodded. Timmy showed Ducky how to grab the telephone and talk into it. Ducky and Timmy became good friends. They would talk on the telephone every day. Soon, other ducks came to the pond, and Ducky was not lonely anymore. The moral of the story is that talking to others can help you make friends and not feel lonely.

Once upon a time, there was a big sun in the sky. It was very bright and happy. The sun loved to play with all the animals in the forest. They would run and jump together all day long. One day, a little bird felt guilty. He had taken a key from his friend, the rabbit. The key could lock and unlock a special door. The bird wanted to play a trick on his friends, so he locked the door and hid the key. The animals could not find the key and were sad. They could not play with the sun anymore. The little bird saw his friends were sad and felt very guilty. He knew he had to fix it. So, the little bird gave the key back to the rabbit. The rabbit unlocked the door, and all the animals were happy again. They could play with the sun once more. The little bird promised never to take the key again, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a toy phone in her room. She was very excited. She wanted to make pretend calls with her new phone. Lily picked up the phone and started to talk. "Hello, is this my friend Tom?" Lily said into the phone. Tom was her best friend. They liked to play together. "Let's meet at the park and play," she added. Lily went to the park with her phone. She saw Tom and ran to him. "Tom, I have a new phone! We can make pretend calls!" Lily said, showing her phone to Tom. Tom was excited too. They played with the phone all day, making pretend calls to their friends and family. They were very happy and had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, Lily and Tom went home, excited to play with the phone again tomorrow.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to write a poem. He asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you supply me with words for a poem?" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Let's think of some words together." They sat down and thought of words. Tim tried to write a poem, but it was terrible. He showed his mom, and she said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can make it better." Just then, their cat, Fluffy, jumped on the table. Fluffy walked on the paper and made paw prints. Tim looked at the paw prints and had an idea. He used the paw prints to make a new poem. It was not terrible anymore. Tim and his mom laughed and clapped. Fluffy had helped make the poem better.
