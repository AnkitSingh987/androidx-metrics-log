Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a big house with a long hall. Mia loved to play in the hall with her toys. One day, Mia saw an incredible, shiny toy car in the hall. She was so excited! Mia wanted to play with the car right away. So, she started to rush to the car as fast as her little legs could go. As Mia played with her incredible toy car, she raced it down the hall. She laughed and had so much fun. Mia and her toy car had a great day in the hall, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a humble little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to clap his hands and dance. One day, while playing outside, he found a shiny mineral. It was round and smooth, and he thought it was very special. Tim ran inside to show his mom the shiny mineral. "Mom, look what I found!" he said. His mom smiled and clapped her hands too. "Wow, Tim! That's a pretty mineral you found," she said. They both danced and clapped together, happy to have found such a special thing. The next day, Tim took the shiny mineral to school to show his friends. They all clapped and danced just like Tim and his mom. Tim felt very happy to share his special find with his friends. And from that day on, whenever Tim and his friends found something special, they would always clap, dance, and share their happiness with each other.

Molly was having a hot day. Everywhere she went, it was so hot that she felt like she was melting. Everywhere, that is, except her refrigerator. Molly was very curious about her refrigerator. She loved the way it hummed, and the way it always made a cool breeze. She wanted to know more about it. So, one day, Molly decided to try to solve the mystery. She opened the refrigerator door and was amazed by? a surprise inside. There, sparkling in the light, was an ice cream sundae! Molly squealed with joy. She grabbed the sundae and enjoyed it in one bite. From that day onward, Molly was never too hot. Whenever the heat was too much, she would just grab a sundae from the refrigerator and cool off.

One day, a big dog and a little cat were playing. They were friends. The dog was very hard and strong. The cat was soft and small. They liked to play with a red ball. The dog would throw the ball, and the cat would try to catch it. They played all day. Then, the ball went too far. The cat could not see it. The dog could not see it. They both sighed. They were sad. They looked for the ball, but it was gone. They did not know what to do. A bird saw their sad faces. The bird knew where the ball was. The bird gave them a wink and flew away. The dog and the cat followed the bird. The bird took them to the ball. They were happy again. They played with the ball and had more fun.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a journal on the floor. The journal was very pretty. It had a big sun and flowers on it. Lucy wanted to carry the journal home. She thought it was a special book. On her way, Lucy met her friend, Tom. Tom saw the journal and said, "That's my journal! I lost it!" Lucy felt guilty because she wanted to keep the journal for herself. She gave the journal back to Tom. Tom was happy to have his journal back. Lucy and Tom sat under a tree. They opened the journal and drew pictures together. They drew a big house, a cat, and a dog. They had lots of fun playing with the journal. Lucy was happy to share the journal with her friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He was a normal boy who liked to play with his toys and run around outside. One day, Tom was playing with his ball and accidentally broke a window. His mom came out and saw the broken window. "Tom, did you break the window?" asked Mom. Tom knew he had to use his brain and tell the truth. "Yes, Mom, I did," he said sadly. Mom looked at Tom and said, "I'm glad you told the truth, but I have to punish you for breaking the window." Tom's punishment was to help Mom clean up the broken glass and not play with his ball for the rest of the day. He was sad, but he knew it was important to be careful and listen to his mom. After the punishment, Tom played with his other toys and learned to be more careful with his ball.
