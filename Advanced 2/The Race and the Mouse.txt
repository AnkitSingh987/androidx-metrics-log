Once upon a time, there was a big race. All the animals were there. They wanted to win. The race was very long, but they were not scared. They wanted to be the best. Before the race, a small mouse and a big elephant talked. The mouse said, "You are too big and slow. I will win." The elephant said, "You are too small and stupid. I will win." They both laughed and got ready for the race. The race started. The mouse ran very fast. The elephant was not as fast, but he did not give up. He used his big body to push through the crowd. The other animals could not keep up. At the end of the race, the mouse and the elephant were very close. The mouse tried to run faster, but he was too tired. The elephant pushed and pushed, and he won the race. The mouse was sad, but the elephant said, "It is okay. We both tried our best. Next time, we can race together." They became friends and learned that sometimes, being big or small does not matter. It is about trying your best and having fun.

Molly and Jake were walking in the park. "Look," shouted Jake and pointed at a big blue animal. "What is it?" asked Molly. "It's a zebra," replied Jake. "It zips around the park all day." Molly was amazed. She had never seen a zebra before. She wanted to get closer. So, she started to run. But the zebra was fast and it zigzagged around the grass, zipping away. Molly and Jake giggled. "Maybe we can try again tomorrow," said Molly. "Let's go home now." When they got home, Molly and Jake saw something blue outside their window. "What's that?" asked Molly. Then, the blue animal zipped past the window. "That was a hummingbird!" exclaimed Jake. "It's so fast! I wonder where it's going." Molly and Jake smiled. They had seen two amazing blue animals in one day. They couldn't believe it!

Once upon a time, there was a lady who lived in a small house. She had a big garden with many pretty flowers. One day, she saw a disgusting bug in her garden. She did not like the bug because it was eating her flowers. The lady wanted to make the bug go away. The lady had a friend named Tom. She told Tom, "I have a disgusting bug in my garden. Can you help me?" Tom said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's make a surprise for the bug." They made a plan to surprise the bug and make it go away. The next day, the lady and Tom hid behind a big tree in the garden. When the disgusting bug came to eat the flowers, they jumped out and yelled, "Surprise!" The bug was so scared that it ran away and never came back. The lady was happy that her flowers were safe, and she thanked Tom for his help.

Once upon a time, there was a big race in the town. All the animals wanted to come and run in the race. There was a big dog, a small cat, and a weak little bird. They all wanted to win the race. On the day of the race, the sun was shining and the sky was blue. The big dog, the small cat, and the weak little bird lined up to start the race. The town people came to watch and cheer. They all thought the big dog would win. As the race began, the big dog and the small cat ran very fast. The weak little bird tried to fly, but she was too weak. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and picked up the weak little bird. It took her high in the sky, and she could see the whole race from up there. The big dog and the small cat were so surprised by the wind that they stopped running. They looked up at the weak little bird flying high in the sky. The weak little bird flew over the big dog and the small cat and crossed the finish line first. The town people cheered for the weak little bird. They learned that sometimes, even the weak can win a race. And they all lived happily ever after.
