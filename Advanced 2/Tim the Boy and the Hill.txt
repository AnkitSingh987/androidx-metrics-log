Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car. It was a modern car. It was red and shiny. Tim liked to play with his toy car all day. One day, Tim wanted to make his car go up a hill. The hill was big. Tim's car was small. The car started to struggle. It went up a little, then it went down. Tim tried again and again, but the car could not go up the hill. Tim felt sad. Tim's friend, Sam, saw Tim and came to help. Sam had a big toy truck. Sam said, "Let's put your car on my truck. My truck can go up the hill." Tim put his car on Sam's truck. Together, they went up the hill. At the top, they counted to three and let the car go. It went down the hill very fast. Tim and Sam laughed and clapped. They had zero problems now. They played together all day.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to find something fun. The sun was light and bright in the sky. Tim was happy. Tim saw a prune on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to his friend, Sam. "Look, I find a prune!" said Tim. Sam looked at the prune and said, "Let's eat it!" But the prune was old and not good. They both ate it and felt sick. Tim and Sam were sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a daddy and daughter who loved each other very much. They did everything together and were original, always inventing fun new games and activities. One day, the dad and daughter decided to meet a new friend, who would come to visit them in their home. They were so excited! The day of the big meetup came and the dad was happy to see their new pal. But the daughter was worried; their friend wasn't like them. He wasn't original or kind like her dad. Suddenly, the dad began to change - his original way of thinking, the way he smiled at his daughter, all shifted. He was not kind to her any longer. The daughter was very sad and didn't understand why her daddy was different now. But it was too late; the damage had been done. The dad and daughter would never be the same again.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful land, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat dessert. His favorite dessert was a big, sweet, red apple. One day, Tim saw a beautiful butterfly. He wanted to catch it, but his mom said, "No, Tim, it's time for dessert. Come eat your apple." But Tim decided to ignore his mom and chase the butterfly instead. Tim chased the butterfly all around the beautiful land. He was so happy. But soon, Tim was tired and hungry. He went back to his mom and said, "I'm sorry for ignoring you. Can I have my dessert now?" His mom smiled and gave him his apple. Tim learned that he should listen to his mom and not ignore her when she tells him something important.

Joey was a very honest three-year-old. He always tried to do the right thing and be good. One day he was playing in the backyard with his plastic toys. Suddenly he heard a noise. He saw two men mixing something in a big barrel. Joey went over to the men and asked what they were doing. One of the men said it was none of his business. Joey said it was his business because it was his backyard. The men were angry and tried to take Joey away. Joey screamed and tried to run away. He was too fast and he ran inside the house. The men chased him around the house until they finally caught him. The men put Joey in the barrel and mixed him in with whatever they were mixing. Joey was never seen again and the men got away. Joey was never able to tell anyone else what had happened to him. He was gone forever because of his honest curiosity.
