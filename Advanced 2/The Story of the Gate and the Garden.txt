Once upon a time, there was a big, harsh gate. It was very old and would shake when the wind blew. Behind the gate, there was a pretty garden with many colorful flowers and green trees. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily was playing near the gate. She saw a small, cute bird stuck in the gate. Lily wanted to help the bird, so she tried to shake the gate. The gate shook a lot, and the bird was free! But then, something unexpected happened. When the gate shook, it opened! Lily was so surprised. She went inside the garden and found a hidden toy box. The toy box was full of fun toys and games. From that day on, Lily and her friends played in the secret garden and had lots of fun. The harsh gate wasn't so bad after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, they found a big club. They thought it was very serious. They wanted to find out what the club was for. Tim and his friends took the club to a big puddle. They had an idea. "Let's soak the club in the water," said Tim. His friends agreed. They put the club in the puddle and watched it get wet. They laughed and splashed in the water too. After the club was all wet, they took it out of the puddle. They tried to use the club for different games. They tried to hit a ball with it, but it was too heavy. They tried to use it as a stick to play in the mud, but it was too big. In the end, they decided to leave the club by the puddle and just play in the water. They had lots of fun, and they never forgot the day they found the big, serious club.

One day, a brave little girl was playing in the field near her home. She loved the feeling of the fresh spring air on her skin. Suddenly, a small butterfly landed on her arm. She thought it was the most beautiful thing in the world! The little girl leaned in close to the butterfly and whispered, "What do you want?" The butterfly flew up to the little girl's ear and whispered back, "Sleep, my child." The little girl thought for a moment, then remembered that during springtime, butterflies need to rest in order to make a new home for themselves. The little girl smiled and said, "I understand! I'll make sure you get a good rest so you can be brave and find a new home." With that, the little girl grinned and waved goodbye to the butterfly, and it flew away. The brave little girl watched until the butterfly had gone, and then she went back home to have a good night's sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Daisy. She was very brave and wanted to see the world. One day, Daisy decided to go for a walk in the countryside near her home. As she walked, Daisy saw a big, high mill. Its round walls reached up to the sky. Daisy was so curious that she wanted to get closer to the mill. "Can I go inside the mill, please?" Daisy asked her mom. "It's too dangerous," replied her mom. But Daisy was determined to explore. She showed her mom she could be brave and soon they were both inside the mill. Daisy saw something amazing inside. To her surprise, it was a whole new world with a magic doorway that led to bright stars and strange creatures. Daisy's mom smiled and said, "I knew you had it in you to show how brave you can be." From then on, Daisy and her mom spent lots of time together exploring the magical world inside the high mill.

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing in her room. She saw a big cushion on her bed. Sue wanted to fold the cushion to make a small bed for her toy bear. But Sue was careless and she dropped the cushion on the floor. Sue's mommy came into the room and saw the cushion on the floor. She said, "Sue, why is the cushion on the floor?" Sue looked sad and said, "I wanted to fold it for my bear, but I was careless and it fell." Mommy smiled and said, "That's okay, Sue. Let me show you how to fold the cushion." Sue and her mommy folded the cushion together. Now, Sue's toy bear had a small bed to sleep on. Sue was happy and she thanked her mommy for helping her.
