Once upon a time, there was a big cat. The cat was very fierce. The cat had big ears. One day, the cat went for a walk in the woods. He saw a small box on the ground. The box was wrapped with a big ribbon. The fierce cat was very curious. He wanted to know what was inside the box. He used his sharp claws to open the ribbon. The box opened, and out came a tiny mouse. The cat was very surprised. The mouse looked at the fierce cat and said, "Thank you for opening the box!" The cat looked at the mouse and then at his big ears. He thought about being fierce, but then he decided to be nice. The cat and the mouse became best friends, and they played together in the woods every day.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball, run fast, and eat yummy food. One day, Max went to the big park with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. At the park, they saw a big, round melon on the ground. Max and Lily didn't know what it was, so they just gave a shrug. They thought it might be fun to play with, so Max pushed the melon with his nose, and it rolled around the grass. Lily laughed and clapped her hands. Max and Lily played with the melon all day long. They had a lot of fun and were very happy. When it was time to go home, they left the melon at the park for other kids and dogs to play with. Max and Lily knew they had a mighty good time, and they couldn't wait to come back to the park again.

One day, a boy and his mom went to the store. They wanted to buy a game. The boy was very happy. The store was big and had many toys. The boy saw a game with an icy picture on it. He liked it a lot. The boy and his mom took the game home. They opened the box and started to play. But something unexpected happened. The pieces of the game were icy and cold! The boy and his mom were very surprised. Then, the icy pieces started to move by themselves! The boy and his mom were scared at first. But then, they saw that the pieces were making a fun new game. They played the new game and had a lot of fun. The icy game was magic!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play pirate in his room. He had a toy sword, a toy hat, and a toy ship. He would pretend to sail the seas and look for treasure. One day, Tim's mom came into his room. She said, "Tim, I need to remind you that playing pirate can be dangerous. Please be careful with your toy sword." Tim nodded and said, "Okay, Mom. I will be careful." The next day, Tim was playing pirate again. He remembered what his mom said, so he was very careful with his toy sword. As he was playing, he found a secret treasure map under his bed. He followed the map and found a big treasure chest in the backyard. Tim was so excited! He opened the chest and found lots of toy gold and jewels. He ran inside to show his mom his new treasure. His mom was happy that he was careful and found the treasure. From that day on, Tim always remembered to be careful when playing pirate. And every time he found new treasure, he would share it with his friends and family.

Once upon a time there was a chubby bunny called Jack. Jack loved hopping and playing hide and seek. He always hid behind the cushions in the living room. One day, Jack was playing hide and seek. He hopped to the living room and hid behind the cushions. But then he heard a loud sound. Jack said, "What was that?" He was so curious. Then he heard a voice. "We have to shut all the windows and doors. It's raining outside," said Mommy Bunny. Jack was disappointed that he couldn't continue playing. He heard Mommy Bunny shut all the windows and doors in the house. Jack hopped over to her and said, "Can I help you shut the windows and doors, Mommy?" Mommy Bunny smiled at him. "That's so kind of you, Jack. But why don't you go hop around the garden some more and I will finish up here," she said. So Jack hopped away, happy that he could still play even if the doors and windows were shut.
