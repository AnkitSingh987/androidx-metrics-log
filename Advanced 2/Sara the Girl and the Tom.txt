Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Sara. Sara loved to run and play with her friends. One day, there was a big race in the town. Sara wanted to win a medal, so she decided to try her best. On the day of the race, Sara lined up with the other kids. Her friend Tom said, "Sara, you are foolish to try. You are too small to win." But Sara didn't listen to Tom. She knew she could do it if she tried hard. The race began, and Sara ran as fast as she could. She passed many kids, even Tom. At the end of the race, Sara won the medal! She was so happy. She showed her medal to Tom and said, "See, I told you I could do it if I tried." Tom smiled and said, "You were right, Sara. I was foolish to doubt you."

One day, a little girl named Lily and her friend, a silly cat named Tom, went on a big adventure. They wanted to find a hidden treasure. They walked and walked until they found a big tree. Lily said, "Let's look under the tree for the treasure!" Tom agreed, and they started to dig. As they dug, they found a big box. They were very excited, but the box was very dirty. Tom said, "We need to clean it!" Lily and Tom took the box to a small pond nearby. They put the box in the water to soak. After a while, the dirt washed away, and they could open the box. Inside the box, there was a shiny treasure! They were so happy they found it. But then, a mean dog named Max came and wanted the treasure for himself. He growled at Lily and Tom. Lily said, "No, Max, this is our treasure. We found it together!" Max was sad, but he knew it was true. In the end, Lily, Tom, and Max decided to share the treasure and be friends. They played together all day, and they all learned that sharing is better than fighting.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was three years old and loved to look in shops. Everything she saw looked so amazing! One day the little girl was walking by a shop window and saw something she wanted. She asked her mom if she could go inside. But her mom said no and kept walking. The little girl looked so sad. The little girl felt like her mom was ignoring her. She tried and tried to get her mom to come into the shop with her. But no matter what she said, her mom kept walking by. The little girl's mom thought it was too much to take her into the shop. So she kept on walking and ignored her daughter's plea. The little girl was so sad but she kept looking in the shop windows. She would stop and smile every time she saw something she liked. She knew that one day she would get to go inside and get something amazing!

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a polite oyster named Ollie. Ollie had many friends, like fish and crabs. They would play and enjoy the sea together. One day, Ollie met a big fish named Finn. Finn said, "Hello, Ollie! Do you want to play with me?" Ollie was happy and said, "Yes, Finn! Let's enjoy the sea together." They swam and played all day. But, Finn was not a nice fish. He wanted to eat Ollie. So, when Ollie was tired, Finn opened his big mouth and ate Ollie. Ollie's friends were sad, and they learned to be careful with new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Bob. He was only three years old. One day, he was playing in the park with his plastic toy when he noticed a frog sitting near a pond. The frog was dry, so Bob wanted to help it. Bob poked at the frog carefully with his plastic toy, thinking he could help it to the water. But the frog was scared and suddenly jumped away! Bob chased the frog until it hid under a rock. Bob was sad that the frog was so scared of him. He realized he had made a mistake and should have left the frog alone. When he got home, Bob told his mom what had happened. His mom said, "Remember, Bob, to respect all animals, even if it looks like you can help them. Let nature take its course." Bob was sad but he learned an important lesson. From then on, Bob made sure that he left all animals alone and only enjoyed watching them from a distance.
