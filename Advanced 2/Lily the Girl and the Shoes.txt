One day, a little girl named Lily went to the busy ocean with her mom. There were many people playing in the water and on the sand. Lily wore her favorite shoes with laces that she needed to tie. Lily saw a friendly crab near the water. The crab said, "Hi, Lily! Can you help me tie my shell? It's coming loose." Lily bent down and tied the crab's shell just like she tied her shoes. After helping the crab, Lily played in the ocean with her mom. They splashed and laughed together. The crab waved goodbye as they left. Lily had a fun day at the busy ocean, and she was happy she could help her new crab friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to be organized. She had a place for all her toys. One day, Fluffy's friend, Bunny, came to play. Bunny did not know how to be organized. He took out all the toys and made a big mess. Fluffy did not like the mess. She started to feel sad. She wanted her toys to be organized again. Fluffy told Bunny, "I like my toys in their place. Can you help me put them back?" Bunny looked at Fluffy and saw that she was sad. Bunny wanted to make Fluffy feel happy. So, he helped her put all the toys back in their place. They worked together and soon, everything was organized again. Fluffy felt happy and thanked Bunny for helping her. From that day on, Bunny learned to be more organized when he played with Fluffy. They had lots of fun together and became best friends.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the bakery with her mom. They saw many tasty treats, but Lucy's favorite was a sweet pastry. Her mom said, "Lucy, you can have one pastry." Lucy was very excited. She saw a big pastry that she wanted to eat. But she saw a little mouse near the pastry. The mouse was deaf, so it did not hear Lucy coming. Lucy tried to tell the mouse to go away, but it did not hear her. Lucy had an idea. She walked up to the mouse and gave it a little pinch on its tail. The mouse felt the pinch and ran away, scared. Lucy took the pastry and said, "Thank you, little mouse, for letting me have the pastry." She and her mom went home, happy with their sweet treat.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old man. He liked to go to the market. At the market, he would buy food for his family. One day, he saw a big box at the market. He did not know what was inside. The wise old man decided to pray. He wanted to know what was in the box. He closed his eyes and said, "Please show me what is inside the box." When he opened his eyes, the box was open. Inside the box, there was a big, shiny ball. The wise old man was happy. He took the ball home. But when he got home, the ball was not shiny anymore. It was a big, round cake! The wise old man was surprised. He shared the cake with his family. They all laughed and enjoyed the cake together.

Once upon a time, there was a large hero named Big Tom. He lived in a small town where everyone liked him. Big Tom was always happy to help others. One day, a mean dog came to the town. The dog was loud and scared everyone. The people in the town did not know what to do. They asked Big Tom for help. Big Tom said, "Don't worry, I will make the dog go away." Big Tom went to the dog and said, "You are not welcome here. Please leave." The dog did not want to go. Big Tom and the dog had a big fight. In the end, Big Tom won. The people in the town were very happy. They said, "Thank you, Big Tom! You are our hero!" And from that day on, the town was safe and everyone lived happily ever after.
