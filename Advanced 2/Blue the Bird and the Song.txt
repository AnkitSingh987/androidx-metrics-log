Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Blue. Blue loved to sing. Every morning, he would wake up and sing a happy song. His song made everyone feel good. One day, Blue felt weak. He could not sing his happy song. Blue was sad. He wanted to make everyone feel good again. Blue had a friend, a big tree named Wonder. Wonder saw that Blue was sad and wanted to help. Wonder told Blue to rest on his branches. Blue listened to Wonder and rested for a little while. After some time, Blue started to feel better. He was no longer weak. Blue was so happy that he began to sing his happy song again. Everyone felt good, and Blue and Wonder were happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a thin toy car that he loved to play with every day. One day, he took his toy car to school. At school, the teacher gave the children a test. The test was about sharing. Tim was good at sharing, so he finished the test very fast. After the test, Tim saw a new friend named Sam. Sam did not have a toy to play with. Tim decided to share his thin toy car with Sam. They played together and had lots of fun. Tim was happy because he made a new friend by sharing.

One day, a calm cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a tap near a big tree. The tap had water coming out of it. Tom was thirsty, so he went to the tap to drink some water. When Tom went to the tap, he saw a little bird. The bird was also thirsty. Tom and the bird took turns drinking water from the tap. They became good friends. After they drank water, Tom and the bird went to play in the park. They had a lot of fun. They played until the sun went down. Then, they said goodbye and went to their homes.

But the help came too late. The earthquake was too big and too strong. The town was very broken. The people were very sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive magnet. It was very strong and could pick up many things. One day, the magnet went on a fun trip to find new things to pick up. On the trip, the magnet saw a big group of little rocks. The magnet thought it would be fun to pick them up. So, it went close and tried to pick them up. But, something unexpected happened. The rocks did not stick to the magnet. The magnet was very sad. The magnet wanted to settle down and rest. It went to a quiet place to think. Then, a big wind came and blew the magnet away. The magnet was lost and could not find its way back home. The magnet was very sad and never found its way back. The end.
