Once upon a time, a warm little comet named Twinkly lived in the sky. Twinkly was very kind and always minded her friends. She loved to play with her friends, the stars, and the moon. They would dance and twirl in the sky, making the night bright and shiny. One day, a new star named Sparkly joined them. Sparkly was shy and did not know how to play with the others. Twinkly minded Sparkly and wanted to help. She went to Sparkly and asked, "Do you want to play with us?" Sparkly nodded and smiled. Twinkly showed Sparkly how to dance and twirl, just like the other stars and the moon. Soon, Sparkly was having fun and playing with everyone. All the stars, the moon, and Twinkly were very happy. They learned that when you mind others and help them, everyone can have fun together. The moral of the story is to always be kind and mind others, so everyone can have fun and be happy.

Once there was a heavy flower. It was so big and delicate that Daisy wanted to test it. She touched it ever so gently, but it started to droop and became very sad. Daisy was scared and tried to make it better, but it was too late. The flower had died. Daisy was so sad. She cried and cried, feeling so guilty for killing the flower. She could never have a flower so heavy and beautiful again. The end.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim and his family wanted to go on a vacation. They had to choose where to go. Tim wanted to go to the beach, but his sister, Sue, wanted to go to the mountains. Their mom and dad said they could only pick one place. Tim and Sue thought about it. They both wanted to have fun on their vacation. They talked about the wide beach with lots of sand and water to play in. They also talked about the tall mountains with trees to climb and rocks to find. In the end, Tim and Sue decided to choose the beach. They knew they would both have fun playing in the wide sand and swimming in the water. Their mom and dad were happy too. They all went on their vacation and had a great time.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. The cat found a pin on the ground. The dog said, "Be careful with that pin, it is sharp!" The cat did not listen and played with the pin. While they were playing, a big, rude bird came and took the pin from the cat. The cat and the dog were sad. They paused and thought about how to get the pin back. The cat had an idea. She told the dog to bark very loud at the bird. The dog barked, and the bird got scared. The bird dropped the pin, and it fell into a hat. The cat and the dog were happy and played with the hat instead. The bird learned not to be rude and became friends with the cat and the dog.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a popular ice cream shop. Every day, many kids would go there to order their favorite ice cream. One day, a little boy named Timmy went to the shop with his mom. At the shop, Timmy saw many kids ordering ice cream. Timmy had a question. He asked his mom, "Mom, what ice cream should I order?" His mom said, "You can order any ice cream you like, Timmy." Timmy looked around and saw a little girl drop her ice cream. She was sad. Timmy decided to give her his ice cream. The girl said, "Thank you, Timmy!" and they both smiled. The moral of the story is to be kind and share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate cat named Mark. Mark loved to play with yarn. One day, he found a big ball of yarn in the park. He was very happy and started to play with it. While Mark was playing, a little dog came over. The dog said, "Can I play with the yarn, too?" Mark thought about it and decided to share the yarn with the dog. They played together and had lots of fun. After they played, the dog said, "Thank you for sharing the yarn, Mark. You are very kind." Mark felt happy that he shared the yarn. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can make you and them happy.
