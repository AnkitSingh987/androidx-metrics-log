Once there was a little girl. Her name was Lucy. She was very curious and sometimes she was a bit careless. One day, Lucy wanted to explore a lake near her house. She loved being outdoors. So, Lucy went to the lake and it was big and dark. She was scared at first, but then she heard a splash. It was so loud! She was not sure what made the noise, but she wanted to find out. So, Lucy went closer to the lake and this time she heard two splashes. She was so excited! She saw two ducks swimming in the lake and they were making big splashes. Lucy laughed and she was so happy. But then Lucy got too close to the lake and she accidentally stepped into the water. She was very surprised and she got quickly out of the water. Lucy giggled and looked back at the lake. She had learned her lesson: never be too careless near the water.

One day, a boy named Tim found a rare toy gun in his room. He was very happy. He picked up the gun and started to play with it. He stepped around the room, pretending to be a hero. Tim went outside to show his friend Sam the rare gun. "Look, Sam!" said Tim. "I found a rare toy gun!" Sam was excited too. They both played with the gun, taking turns to step like heroes. As they played, they saw a big dog. The dog was nice and wanted to play too. Tim and Sam stepped back and let the dog join them. They all played with the rare toy gun and had a fun day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big rock and thought, "Is this the earth?" Tim was happy to find something new. He ran to his friend, Sue, and said, "Sue, I found the earth! It is a big rock." Sue looked at the rock and smiled. "No, Tim, the earth is what we stand on. It is like a big ball," Sue said. Tim realized Sue was right. He was happy to learn something new. They played on the earth and laughed. They were happy to be friends and learn together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to sing songs. One day, she heard a modern song on the radio and wanted to learn it. She asked her mommy, "Can I learn this new song, please?" Her mommy said, "Of course, but first help me gather the toys in your room." So Lily helped her mommy gather all the toys and put them away. After they finished, her mommy taught her the new song. They sang the modern song together and had a lot of fun. One day, Lily and her mommy went to the park. Lily saw a sad boy sitting alone. She went up to him and said, "Hi, I am Lily. Do you want to sing a song with me?" The boy smiled and said, "Yes, I would like that." They sang the modern song together and became good friends. The moral of the story is that when we help others and share our happiness, we can make new friends and spread joy.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn bird, named Pebbles. Pebbles was always looking for something special and shiny. One day, she found a rare mineral, but it was very hard to get to. As she flew closer, she heard voices. "It's mine, don't touch it!" said a voice. "No, it's mine!" said another. Pebbles realized that two rabbits were arguing over the mineral. Not wanting to ignore them, she decided to join in. "Can I have the mineral?" asked Pebbles. The rabbits laughed at her and said, "No, this is ours!" Pebbles was too stubborn to ignore them. She tried again and said, "Please, I'd really like it." The rabbits looked at each other and then handed her the mineral. They had no idea how special it was and were happy to share. Pebbles was so excited. She thanked the rabbits and flew away, holding the mineral tightly in her beak. From then on, she would always remind herself not to ignore anyone—even if they were small.
