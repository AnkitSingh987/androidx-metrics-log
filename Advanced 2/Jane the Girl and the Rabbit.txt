Once upon a time there was a young girl named Jane. She had a purple toy which she absolutely loved. It was a pink rabbit. One day Jane decided to play with her rabbit. She put it on the ground, said "bonjour" and moved away. But the rabbit stayed in its place and did not move at all. Jane then sat in front of the rabbit and said, "Come on rabbit! Let's dance!" But the rabbit just stayed still and didn't dance. That's when Jane's mom came over and said, "Jane, you need to separate yourself from the toy. You need to keep a distance from the rabbit. Let's try dancing to some music." Jane and her mom started singing and dancing to a spicy song. Jane noticed the rabbit began to move to the beat! It hopped and swayed until the song ended. Jane was so amazed and excited. She thanked her mom and hugged her bunny tightly. She knew that from that day forward, she and her bunny would always separate and reunite with a dance.

Once there was a boy. He wanted to buy something special. His mom said he could have whatever he wanted. So, he went to the nearest shop to buy. He saw a cliff and he wanted it. He asked his mom to buy the cliff for him. She said it was too expensive. The little boy was very sad. He didn't want anything else. He just wanted the cliff. So, he asked his grandma if she could buy it for him. She said it was a clean cliff, so she bought it for him. The boy was so happy. He took the cliff home and began to play. But after a few days, the cliff became dirty. It was no longer clean. The boy was disappointed and couldn't play with it anymore. He wished he never bought the cliff!

One day, a little dog named Tim was playing in the park. He was running and jumping, having lots of fun. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim was very happy. Suddenly, the ground started to tremble. Tim felt scared. It was an earthquake! He ran to his friend, a big cat named Sam. "Sam, Sam! The ground is shaking!" cried Tim. Sam looked around and said, "Don't worry, Tim. Let's hide under this big tree until it stops." After a while, the earthquake stopped. Tim and Sam came out from under the tree. They were both very hungry after their big adventure. They found some tasty treats on the ground that had fallen from the tree during the earthquake. They ate the treats and felt much better. "Thank you for helping me, Sam," said Tim. "You're a good friend." Sam smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. We're always here to help each other." They played in the park for the rest of the day, happy that the earthquake was over.

Once upon a time, there was a king who wanted a new crown. He asked his friend to design a special one for him. The friend was very good at making things. He made a crown that was different from all the others. It was big, shiny, and had many colors. One day, the king wore his new crown to a party. All the people at the party liked it very much. They said it was the best crown they had ever seen. But then, something unexpected happened. The crown started to change its shape! The king was very surprised. His friend, who made the crown, was smiling. He said, "I made the crown different on purpose. It can change its shape to make you happy." The king laughed and clapped his hands. He loved his new crown even more. Now, he had a crown that was not only different but also fun to wear.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little car named Tim. Tim loved to drive on the road with all his car friends. They would go fast and slow, and play in the traffic. One sunny day, Tim and his friends were playing near a big hill. They were having so much fun. But suddenly, Tim started to roll down the hill very fast. His friends were scared and called out to him, but Tim could not stop. As Tim rolled down the hill, he saw a magic button. He pressed it and, poof, he vanished! His friends could not see him anymore. Tim was now invisible! He went back up the hill to his friends, and they were so surprised. They could hear Tim but could not see him. Tim pressed the magic button again, and he was visible once more. His friends were so happy to see him again. From that day on, they all played together, knowing that Tim had a special secret. And they all lived happily ever after, driving and playing in the traffic, with Tim's magic button always ready to help them when they needed it.
