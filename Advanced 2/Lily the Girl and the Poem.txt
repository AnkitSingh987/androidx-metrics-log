One day, a little girl named Lily was very excited. She wanted to go to the park with her mom. Lily had a poem she wanted to share with her friends at the park. She knew they would like it too. Lily and her mom walked to the park. Lily saw her friends playing. She ran to them and said, "I have a poem to share with you!" Her friends stopped playing and listened. Lily started to say her poem, but she forgot the words. She felt sad. Lily's mom saw her feeling sad. She went to Lily and whispered the poem in her ear. Lily smiled and felt excited again. She said the poem to her friends, and they all clapped and cheered. Lily was happy that she could share her poem with her friends, and they all had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little engine. This engine loved to go fast. One day, it was very confused. It didn't know why it couldn't go fast anymore. The engine had a friend, a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to help the engine go fast again. So, he asked his mom what to do. His mom said, "You need to feed the engine." Tim found some fuel and started to feed the engine. The engine was very happy. It could go fast again. Tim and the engine went on many fun rides together. And they were never confused again.

One day, a big, noisy bird woke up very early. The bird lived in a tree near a little boy's house. The bird liked to sing when the sun came up. The boy liked to hear the bird sing, but not too early. The noisy bird saw the sunrise and started to sing. The little boy woke up and looked outside. He saw the bird and the pretty sunrise. The boy thought, "I want to see the sunrise too." So, he put on his shoes and went outside. The boy asked the noisy bird, "Can you let me see the sunrise with you?" The bird nodded and they both watched the sunrise together. They had a fun time and became good friends. From that day on, the boy and the noisy bird would watch the sunrise together every morning.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was feeling sleepy one day. He did not want to play with his toys or go outside. He just wanted to sleep. His mom saw that Tim was not feeling well. She gave him a pill to help him feel better. The pill was small and easy for Tim to take. Tim took the pill and hoped he would feel better soon. Later, Tim started to feel better. He did not complain anymore. He was not sleepy now. He went outside to play with his friends and had a lot of fun. Tim was happy that the pill helped him feel better.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet farm. On the farm, there was a big bull named Bob. Bob liked to eat grass and play all day. One day, a girl named Lily came to the farm. She wanted to serve food to the animals. She had apples for the horses, corn for the chickens, and grass for the bull. Lily saw Bob and said, "Hi, Bob! I have grass for you." Bob was happy and ate the grass. He said, "Thank you, Lily!" They became friends and played together on the quiet farm. Every day, Lily would come to serve food to the animals and play with Bob. They had lots of fun together.

Once there was a little boy who really wanted to help his family. So one day he decided to go out and earn some money. He found a job at the market selling ties. It was a very cold day and a lot of people were wearing coats and hats to keep warm. THE LITTLE BOY worked hard, and he sold a lot of ties. At the end of the day he was very happy because he earned some money for his family. But then he realized he was missing one of his ties. He had given it away to a customer who hadn't had enough money to pay for it. The little boy was so sad. He wanted to help his family, but he had given away the money they needed. Then he remembered something his mother had said: "Everyone should pay for what they get". The little boy learnt an important lesson that day: it was very important to always pay for things that he used. That way everyone was happy, and he could help his family at the same time.
