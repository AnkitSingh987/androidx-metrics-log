Lily was a little girl who liked to play dress-up. She had many hats and scarves and shoes in her big box. One day, she found a shiny mirror in the box. She picked it up and looked at it. She saw her face and smiled. "Hello, Lily!" she said to the mirror. "Do you want to play with me?" The mirror did not say anything. It only showed Lily's face. Lily moved the mirror around and saw different things behind her. She saw her bed, her toys, her window, and her door. She wondered what else the mirror could show her. She walked to the door and opened it. She saw the hall and the stairs. She went down the stairs and saw the living room and the kitchen. She saw her mom and dad and her baby brother. She waved the mirror at them. "Look, Mom, look, Dad, look, baby!" she said. "I have a mirror! It shows me everything!" Her mom and dad smiled and said hello. Her baby brother giggled and reached for the mirror. Lily did not want to share. She ran away with the mirror. She went to the garden and saw the flowers and the trees and the birds. She saw a pond with water and fish. She went to the pond and looked at the mirror. She saw the water and the fish and her face. She leaned closer and closer to the mirror. She wanted to see more. But the mirror was slippery and Lily lost her grip. The mirror fell into the pond and sank. Lily could not see it anymore. She only saw the water and the fish. She felt sad and scared. She had lost her mirror. She cried and cried. She ran back to the house and told her mom and dad what happened. They hugged her and said it was okay. They said they would buy her a new mirror. They said they loved her. Lily felt better. She said she was sorry. She said she loved them too. She learned a lesson. She learned that the mirror was not as deep as the pond. She learned that the mirror was not as important as her family. She learned to be careful and to share. She was a good girl.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a white cup in her kitchen. She wanted to pour water into the cup to make a pretty pattern. So, she turned on the tap and filled the cup with water. Lily saw the water make a nice pattern in the cup. She was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The pattern in the cup started to move and change. Lily was very surprised. The pattern turned into a tiny white fish. The fish looked at Lily and said, "Thank you for pouring water into the cup. Now, I can swim and play!" Lily and the fish became good friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Tom. Tom loved to wake up early every morning. He would put on his red hat and big boots. Tom was very good at his job. One day, Tom met a dog named Max. Max was a big, brown dog with a wagging tail. Max was jealous because he wanted to be a fireman too. He wanted to wear a red hat and big boots like Tom. Tom saw that Max was sad and had an idea. He found a small red hat and little boots for Max. Now, Max could be a fireman too. Every morning, Tom and Max would wake up early and go to work together. They were a great team and the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy rock that lived on the moon. It loved to look at the Earth and see all the pretty lights. Every night, the rock would sit and watch the Earth, feeling happy and loved. One day, the rock saw a little boy on Earth who also loved to look at the moon. The boy would look up at the moon every night and wave to the rock. The rock wanted to wave back, but it was too heavy to move. Then, something unexpected happened. A bright, shiny star came to the rock and said, "I can help you wave to the little boy!" The star touched the rock, and it became light and fluffy. The rock was so happy, it jumped and waved to the boy. The boy saw the rock waving and laughed with joy. From that day on, the boy and the rock became best friends, waving to each other every night.
