One day, a boy named Tom saw an unusual pie on the table. He knew it was special because it was big and had a smiley face on it. His mom said, "Tom, this pie is for the party tonight. Please don't spoil it." Later, Tom's friend, Sam, came over to play. They played with toys and had lots of fun. But Tom kept thinking about the pie. He wanted to show Sam the special pie. So, he took Sam to the table and said, "Look at this unusual pie! But we can't spoil it. It's for the party." Sam looked at the pie and smiled. They both knew they shouldn't touch it. But then, Tom's toy car rolled near the pie. As Tom tried to grab the car, he accidentally bumped the table. The pie wobbled, but it didn't fall. Tom and Sam were so relieved! That night, at the party, everyone loved the unusual pie. Tom and Sam were happy they didn't spoil it. They learned to be careful around special things and enjoyed the party with their friends and family.

Once upon a time there was a flower who lived in the garden. One day, the flower wanted to explore the world outside the garden. The flower said, “Wrap me up, I want to go outside!”. The garden said, “No, flower. It is too dangerous for you. You might get hurt.” But the flower didn't listen and ordered the garden to wrap her up. The garden had no choice but to obey. So garden carefully wrapped up the flower in a soft wrap and said, “Be careful, flower. Don't leave the garden.” The flower nodded and said farewell. Soon, the flower found herself in a vast world full of beauty. But, the flower soon discovered that the outside world was full of hurtful things too. The flower realised that it had made a mistake. It hurriedly ran back to the garden and thanked it for trying to keep her safe. From that day onwards, the flower stayed in the garden and explored the world through its stories.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful bird. The bird had big, colorful wings. It liked to fly high in the sky. One day, it saw a shiny wire. The wire was long and went from one tree to another tree. The bird thought it was a fun place to sit. The bird spread its wings and flew to the wire. It sat on the wire and looked around. It was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened! The wire started to move! The bird was scared and didn't know what to do. The wire was not a wire at all! It was a long, friendly snake! The snake said, "Hello, beautiful bird! I am sorry I scared you. I just wanted to play." The bird and the snake became friends. They played together every day, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max liked to play in the big yard near the corn field. One day, Max saw a big box near the house. He knew it was mail. Max ran to his friend, the little girl named Lily. "Lily, look! Mail!" said Max. Lily was happy and ran to the box with Max. They both wondered what was inside the box. The next day, Lily opened the box. Inside was a big, fun toy for Max and Lily to play with. They played with the toy all day in the yard near the corn field. The mail brought them a fun surprise, just like Max thought it would.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. The tree lived in a big forest. In the forest, there were many animals. One day, a little bird hurt its wing. The bird could not fly. The bird was sad. A kind squirrel saw the bird. The squirrel wanted to help the bird. The squirrel gave the bird a massage. The massage made the bird feel better. The squirrel and the bird became good friends. The other animals saw the squirrel help the bird. They all started to help each other too. They became a big, happy family. One day, they all worked together to make the tall tree even taller. When the tree was very tall, the animals were very happy. They all started to applaud. The moral of the story is that helping each other makes everyone happy.
