One day, a big bear and a little rabbit were in the kitchen. They wanted to bake a cake. The big bear held a bowl and the little rabbit held a pin. The big bear said, "Let's stir the cake mix with the pin!" The little rabbit nodded and started to stir the mix with the pin. The big bear helped too. The little rabbit said, "This is fun!" After the cake was done, they saw a dead bug on the floor. The big bear and the little rabbit were sad for the bug. They put the bug outside and went back to eat the cake. They were happy to have made the cake together.

Jimmy was walking across the field with his dad. All around them birds were chirping. All of a sudden, Jimmy said, "What is that, Dad?" His dad smiled and said, "That's a kangaroo. It's hopping around the field." Jimmy asked, "What is a kangaroo?" His dad replied, "It's a special animal that can use its tail to jump around!" Jimmy's eyes widened. Just then, the kangaroo hopped over to them. Jimmy's dad said, "This kangaroo wants to tell us a secret. Shall we hear it?" Jimmy nodded, excited. The kangaroo revealed, "I am stupid!" Jimmy and his dad laughed. Then the kangaroo hopped away! Jimmy and his dad started walking again. Jimmy smiled and said, "That was a funny kangaroo!" His dad agreed with a smile.

Once upon a time, there was a guitar. The guitar was unknown to the people in the town. One day, a boy found the guitar. He did not know what it was. The boy wanted to play with it. He tried to throw the guitar like a ball. But it did not work. It was not a ball. The guitar made a pretty sound when it hit the ground. The boy showed the guitar to his friends. They all liked the pretty sound. They did not throw it anymore. They played with the guitar and made music. The unknown guitar made the kids happy.

Once there was a little girl named Mia. She was three years old and it was the most perfect day. The sun was shining and Mia was so excited for her surprise. Mia looked outside and saw something sitting on the porch. She ran to the door and opened it. She found a big box and wondered what was inside it. Mia smiled and asked, “What is it?” Her mom revealed, “It's a surprise!” Mia opened the box, and what she found was the most perfect present. It was a jumping castle! Mia was so happy - she had never seen anything so fun! She exclaimed,” Yay!” Mia shared the joy with all of her friends that day. They played all day in the jumping castle and had the best time ever. It was a perfect day for Mia and her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a shy prince named Tim. He lived in a big castle with his mom and dad. Tim was scared to talk to other kids, so he played alone. One day, a girl named Amy came to the castle. Amy was very good at talking and playing. Tim wanted to be friends with her, but he was too shy. So, he decided to listen to Amy talk and play with other kids. As Tim listened, he learned how to talk and play like Amy. He became less shy and started to make friends. Tim and Amy became best friends and played together every day. The moral of the story is that sometimes, we can learn from others by listening to them.

Once upon a time, there was a little ball named Bounce. Bounce was a new ball, and it loved to bounce all day long. It would bounce high, and it would bounce low. Bounce was very happy. One day, Bounce went to the grocery store with its mom. It saw many yummy things, like apples, bananas, and cookies. Bounce wanted to bounce in the grocery store, but its mom said, "No, Bounce. You must stay still." Bounce tried to stay still, but it was very hard. It wanted to bounce so much! Then, Bounce saw a big cake. It looked so soft and sweet. Bounce couldn't help it – it bounced right onto the cake! The cake went splat, and Bounce went flying. Bounce was sad, but its mom gave it a hug. They cleaned up the mess and went home. Bounce learned to only bounce when it was okay.
