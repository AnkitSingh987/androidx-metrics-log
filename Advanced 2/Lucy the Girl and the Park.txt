One day, a girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a big swing and wanted to play. Lucy ran to the swing and started to swing high in the sky. She felt happy and free as she swung back and forth. In the park, Lucy saw a boy with a violin. The boy played a nice song on his violin. Lucy stopped swinging to listen to the music. The boy's song made her want to dance. Lucy asked the boy if the violin was fit for her. The boy said yes and let her try to play. Lucy played the violin and the boy danced. They had so much fun playing and dancing together at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toy models. She had a model of a dog, a cat, and a bird. The dog model was her favorite, but one day, she could not find it. Lily was very sad because she missed her dog model. Lily looked all around her room for her dog model. She asked her deaf cat, Tippy, if he had seen it. Tippy could not hear her, but he wanted to help. Tippy looked under the bed, behind the door, and in the toy box. But they still could not find the dog model. Finally, Lily's mom came into the room. She saw how sad Lily was and wanted to help. She looked in the closet and found the dog model hiding behind a shoe. Lily was so happy to have her dog model back. She thanked her mom and Tippy for helping her. From that day on, Lily made sure to keep her models in a safe place, so she would never miss them again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the beach with her mom and dad. She wore her fancy pink dress and her big sun hat. She loved to play in the sand and feel it between her toes. Lily wanted to search for pretty shells in the sand. She looked under her feet and found a big, shiny shell. She was so happy and showed it to her mom and dad. They smiled and clapped their hands. But then, something unexpected happened. The shiny shell started to wiggle! Lily put it down on the sand and it opened up. Inside was a little crab! The crab waved its claws and danced around. Lily and her family laughed and had so much fun with their new crab friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little otter named Ollie. Ollie lived in a river with his family. They liked to swim and play all day. One day, Ollie saw something terrible. A big, scary monster was near the river. Ollie was very scared. The monster had big eyes that would blink very fast. Ollie didn't know what to do. He swam back to his family and told them about the monster. They all decided to talk to the monster and see if it was nice or mean. Ollie's mom said, "Hello, monster. Are you nice or mean?" The monster blinked and said, "I am not mean. I am just big and scary looking. I want to be friends." Ollie and his family were happy to hear that. They all became friends with the monster and played together in the river. From that day on, they were not scared of the terrible monster anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy was an intelligent bird who lived in a big tree. One day, Billy saw a shiny wire on the ground. He thought it would be fun to play with the wire. Billy picked up the wire with his beak and started to play. He met his friend, Tommy the squirrel. "Look at my shiny wire!" said Billy. Tommy wanted to play too, but he was scared of the wire. "No, thank you!" said Tommy. Billy didn't listen to Tommy and kept playing with the wire. Suddenly, he got stuck in the wire and couldn't move. He screamed for help, but nobody came. Billy was sad and scared. In the end, he couldn't get out of the wire and had a bad day.
