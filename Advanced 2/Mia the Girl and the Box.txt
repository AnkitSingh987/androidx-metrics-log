Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was going on a big trip with her family. Mia's mom told her, "We need our passports to go on the trip." Mia saw the passports on the table. One day, Mia wanted to help her mom. She thought, "I will remove the passports from the table and put them in a safe place." So, she took the passports and hid them in her toy box. On the day of the trip, Mia's mom asked, "Mia, have you seen our passports?" Mia said, "Yes, Mom. I put them in a fine place!" She showed her mom the toy box. Mia's mom was happy and said, "Thank you, Mia. You are a good helper." But when they got to the airport, they found out that Mia had removed the wrong papers! The family was sad, but they learned a lesson. Next time, they would make sure to check everything before leaving home. The moral of the story is: always double check your things before going on a big trip.

One day, a little girl named Sue was walking to school. It was a good Wednesday morning. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Sue was happy. On her way, she saw a sign. The sign had a picture of a cat and a dog. Sue liked cats and dogs. She wanted to see them. But the sign also had an arrow pointing to a big house. Sue did not know if she should go there. She was scared. Sue asked her friend Tom, "Should we go see the cats and dogs?" Tom said, "No, we will be late for school." Sue was sad, but she knew Tom was right. They went to school and told their friends about the sign. After school, they all went to see the cats and dogs together. They had fun and felt brave because they were all together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a gifted bird named Bella. Bella was special because she could sing very pretty songs. All the other animals loved to listen to her sing. One day, Bella found a little twig on the ground. She decided to build a nest with it. She asked her friend, Bunny, "Do you want to help me build a nest?" Bunny said, "Yes, I would love to help you, Bella!" Together, Bella and Bunny worked hard to make the nest. They put lots of twigs and leaves in it to make it cozy. When they were done, they were very tired. They looked at the nest and were happy with their work. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew the nest away. Bella and Bunny were sad. Bella said, "I surrender. We can't make the nest again." But Bunny said, "Don't give up, Bella! We can do it together." So Bella and Bunny built a new nest. This time, they made it stronger. They were very proud of their work. Bella learned that even when things are hard, it is important to never surrender.

Once upon a time, there was a restless year. It shrank and got smaller day by day. Every morning it was a little bit shorter than the day before. This made the year feel worried. One night, it waited until the stars shone brightly in the sky. When the time was right, it began to shrink even more. Smaller and smaller it got until it was tiny, like a little speck. But the year had a plan. It flew up, up into the night sky. As it flew it grew bigger and bigger. By the end of the night, it had grown back to its full size again! The year felt so relieved, and it was happy to be big again. From then on it made sure that it remained the same size, day after day, so that it was never restless again.

Once upon a time, there was a little shrimp. The shrimp lived in a big sea with thick, wavy water. The shrimp had many friends, like fish and crabs. One day, the sun came out and shined very bright. The sun was so hot that it started to burn the water. The shrimp and its friends felt too hot and wanted to find a cooler place to live. The shrimp and its friends swam and swam until they found a nice, cool spot under a big rock. They were very happy in their new home, and they didn't feel the burn from the hot sun anymore. They played and had fun together, and they lived happily ever after.
