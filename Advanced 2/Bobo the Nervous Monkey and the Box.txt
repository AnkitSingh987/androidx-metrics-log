Once upon a time, there was a nervous monkey named Bobo. Bobo lived in a big tree. He liked to play with his friends in the sun. One day, Bobo was playing hide and seek with his friends. He hid in a big box. But the box was not just a box, it was a magic box! When Bobo went inside, the box took him far away to a new place. In the new place, Bobo met other monkeys who were also nervous. They told Bobo that the magic box brought them there too. They all wanted to go back home. Bobo had an idea. He told everyone to hold hands and wish to go back home. They closed their eyes and wished very hard. Suddenly, the magic box opened again! All the nervous monkeys went inside the box, and it took them back to their big tree. At the end, Bobo and his friends were happy to be home. They played in the sun all day, and they were not nervous anymore.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Janey who went for a wander in the woods. She explored high and low, until she heard a voice. It was gentle and kind and Janey couldn't resist it. She followed the voice and eventually discovered a beautiful little fairy. The fairy said "Hello Janey. Are you ready for an adventure?" Janey nodded with a smile. She was so excited. The fairy took Janey around the woods and showed her special places. Janey was so proud of all the places she had seen. Before leaving, the fairy said "I'm so pleased you came on this adventure with me. Oh, I almost forgot something - here, take this stone. It will bring you luck!" Janey thanked the fairy for her wonderful adventure and held the stone close to her heart. She was so proud of the magical time she had spent with the fairy and looked forward to more adventures to come.

Once upon a time, there was a modest boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat cereal every morning. One day, he found a big box of cereal in the kitchen. Tim went to his friend, Sam, and said, "Look at my big box of cereal! Let's share it." Sam was happy and they both ate the cereal together. After they ate, Tim had an idea. "Let's bury the empty box in the yard and see what happens," he said. So, Tim and Sam dug a hole and buried the empty cereal box. After a few days, they went to check on it. To their surprise, they found a beautiful flower growing from the spot where they buried the box. Tim and Sam learned that sharing and being modest can lead to beautiful things.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to her classroom. Sue was a tough girl who liked to play with her friends. In her classroom, she had a big red ball that she loved to bounce around with her friends. One day, Sue's friend Tom came to visit. Tom said, "Let's play with the big red ball!" Sue and Tom tried to bounce the ball, but it would not rise. They tried and tried, but the ball just stayed on the ground. Sue and Tom were sad, but they did not give up. They went to their teacher and asked for help. The teacher showed them how to pump air into the ball. With the teacher's help, the ball began to rise. Sue and Tom were happy and played with the big red ball all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite toy, a big red ball. One day, he could not find his ball. He looked everywhere, but it was not clear where it was. Tim was very sad. Tim's big sister, Sue, knew where the ball was. She had put it in the closet. She wanted to play a trick on Tim. Tim went to Sue and said, "Give me my ball!" He was very mad. Sue said, "No, you have to find it!" Tim looked and looked, but he could not find the ball. He was very tired. He went to Sue and said, "Please, tell me where my ball is." Sue laughed and said, "It is in the closet!" Tim went to the closet and found his ball. But when Tim opened the closet, all the things inside fell on him. It hurt a lot. Tim cried and his ball rolled away. Sue felt bad for playing the trick on Tim. The ball was lost again, and Tim was hurt. This was a bad day for both Tim and Sue.
