Once upon a time, there was a harmless house. One day, the house wanted to do something fun. So, it began to bounce! The house went higher and higher and it jumped so much that it reached the sky. As the house enjoyed its bouncy fun, a little girl saw it and stopped in her tracks. She had never seen such a thing, and she asked the house, "What are you doing?" The house replied, "I am bouncing! Would you like to join me?" So, the little girl laughed and bounced with the house. They smiled and played until the sun went away. The little girl said goodbye to the harmless house and ran off to her own house. But, the little girl and the house agreed to play again soon. The End.

Once upon a time, in a fine spring day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They loved to play together under the warm sun. The park was full of pretty flowers and big trees. Tim and Spot liked to run and jump around. While they were playing, Tim saw a small bird on the grass. He stopped and stared at it. The bird stared back at Tim. "Hello, bird," said Tim. "Do you want to play with us?" The bird chirped and hopped closer to Tim and Spot. Tim, Spot, and the bird played all day in the park. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. As the sun began to set, Tim said, "We should go home now. Bye, bird!" The bird chirped and flew away. Tim and Spot walked home, happy after a fun day in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play outside in the sunshine. One day, she saw a pretty butterfly in her yard. The butterfly was eager to fly and play. Amy said to the butterfly, "Can I join you and play too?" The butterfly nodded and they began to play together. They ran and jumped in the warm sunshine. They were very happy. As they played, a friendly dog came by. He saw Amy and the butterfly and wanted to join in the fun. "Can I play with you?" he asked. Amy and the butterfly said, "Yes, come play with us!" So, they all played together in the sunshine until it was time for dinner.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy was playing in her backyard. She was very happy and enjoyed playing with her toys. She had built a big tower of blocks, and was feeling a bit dizzy from spinning around in circles. Suddenly, Lucy noticed that it was getting late. She realized that it was time for her nap. She was so tired that she couldn't even play anymore, so she started walking towards her house. On the way there, she stopped to take one last look at her tower and imagined how tall it could be. She said goodbye to it and headed inside. When Lucy got to her room, she plopped down on her bed and fell asleep. She had many lovely dreams and slept until it was time to wake up. The next morning, Lucy got up feeling refreshed and happy. She went outside to find her tower still standing, even though she had been so dizzy the day before. She beamed with happiness and set about playing until it was time for her next nap.

Once upon a time, there was a girl who loved the flame. She looked at the flame every day and it made her happy. One day, it started to rain. The rain wet the flame, so the girl couldn't look at it any more. She felt lonely and missed the flame. She wanted to see the flame again. The next day, the rain stopped and the sun came out. The sun dried the flame and the girl was so happy. She ran to look at the flame and gave it a big hug. She smiled and said, "I missed you, flame!"

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big blue sea. One day, while swimming, Fin found a shiny diamond on the sea floor. He picked it up and showed it to his friend, Sam the Seahorse. "Wow, Fin! That is a very pretty diamond," said Sam. "Let's go show it to our friends!" So Fin and Sam swam to show the diamond to their friends. As they swam, a big, scary shark saw the diamond and wanted it for himself. He chased Fin and Sam, who felt helpless and scared. But then, something unexpected happened. A friendly dolphin named Del came to help Fin and Sam. "Hey, Mr. Shark! Leave my friends alone!" said Del. The shark was afraid of the big dolphin and swam away. Fin, Sam, and Del were so happy that they were safe. They swam together and showed the diamond to all their friends. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.
