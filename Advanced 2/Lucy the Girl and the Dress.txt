Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived with her mom, dad, and her aunt. Her aunt was famous for making pretty dresses. One day, Lucy wanted to wear a dress like her aunt's. Lucy asked her aunt, "Can you make me a pretty dress too?" Her aunt smiled and said, "Of course, Lucy! Let's make a dress together." They worked all day, and finally, the dress was done. It was the most beautiful dress Lucy had ever seen. The next day, Lucy put on her new dress and went outside to play. As she was playing, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew her dress up into a tree! Lucy was sad, but her aunt said, "Don't worry, Lucy. We can make another dress together." And they did. From that day on, Lucy and her aunt made many more pretty dresses, and they became famous dress makers together.

Once there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved swimming in the pool and he did it every day. He would jump in and splash around with the biggest smile. One day, Tom wanted to show his friends a different view, so he took them to the deep end of the pool. As they got closer, they all became a bit scared. But Tom was brave and he showed them the beautiful view he had been looking at. "It's so nice here," Tom said. He splashed around and showed them some of his swimming tricks. They were all having so much fun, they didn't even realise how scared they had been. They all laughed as they splashed around together, enjoying the beautiful view.

One day, a group of kids went to play outside. They had a big, tight tent. They put the tent in the yard. They all went inside the tent to play games. They were very happy. A little dog named Spot came to the tent. He wanted to play too. One of the kids, named Tim, said, "No, Spot! You can't play in our tent!" Tim started to tease Spot. Spot felt sad and went away. Then, something unexpected happened. The tent started to shake! The kids were scared. They came out of the tent and saw that Spot had found a big, friendly bear. The bear was shaking the tent because he wanted to play too. The kids were surprised and said, "Wow, Spot! You found a new friend for us!" They all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat and play all day. One day, Tim's mom told him she had a big surprise. She said, "I will reveal it at dinner time." Tim was very excited and could not wait. At dinner time, Tim saw a big pot on the table. "What's in the pot, Mom?" asked Tim. "You will see," she said with a smile. Tim was so curious. He thought it might be his favorite food, but he was not sure. When Tim's mom opened the pot, Tim saw something green and hairy. It was broccoli! Tim did not like broccoli at all. But then, his mom said, "I made it special for you, Tim. Try it!" Tim tried the hairy broccoli, and it was yummy! He was happy that his mom revealed the surprise, even if it was not his favorite food. Now, Tim likes broccoli too.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue wanted to buy a kitten. She saved her money and went to the pet shop with her mom. They looked at many kittens, but Sue wanted a brave kitten. She looked and looked until she found the perfect one. Sue and her mom took the brave kitten home. They played with the kitten all day. They had so much fun and the kitten was very happy. Sue named her new kitten Fluffy because it had soft fur. One day, while Sue and Fluffy were playing, something unexpected happened. Fluffy jumped up and caught a bug in the air. Sue was so surprised! She didn't know that her brave kitten could catch bugs too. From that day on, Sue and Fluffy played together and caught bugs, and they were the best of friends.
