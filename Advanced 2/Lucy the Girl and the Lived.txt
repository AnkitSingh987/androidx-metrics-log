Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lucy loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Lucy saw thick smoke coming from a big house. She told her mom and dad, and they called for help. The firemen came and put out the fire. Everyone was safe and happy. Later, Lucy met a boy named Tom who lived in the big house. They became best friends and played together all the time. When they grew up, Lucy and Tom decided to marry. They had a beautiful wedding, and all their friends and family came to celebrate. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a small cage in a big house. He liked to look around and learn new things. One day, Tim saw a toy car outside his cage. He wanted to play with it, but he couldn't reach it. Tim asked his friend, the cat named Sam, to help him. Sam was very nice and said, "I can borrow the toy car for you, Tim." So, Sam went to the toy car and brought it back to Tim's cage. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" Tim and Sam played with the toy car all day. They had lots of fun together. When it was time for bed, Sam put the toy car back where it belonged. Tim was still curious about the world, but he knew he had a good friend in Sam who would help him explore.

Once upon a time, there was a restless goat. Every day, she wanted to go and explore. So one day, she decided that she would travel. The goat packed her bag and said goodbye to her friends. Then she started her journey. She walked for miles and miles, seeing the world outside of her home for the first time. Eventually, she found herself in a new land. It was a beautiful place filled with new things to explore. The goat was so excited. She spent her days running around and eating the delicious grass of the meadows. But soon, the goat became restless once again. She wanted to keep travelling, to explore even more of the world. So one morning, she packed her bag and said goodbye to her new friends, ready to keep travelling. And she traveled forever, never settling down and never getting tired of experiencing the world around her.

Once upon a time, there was a clever prince. He lived in a big castle with his mom and dad. The prince had many friends, but he liked to play with his best friend, a small cat. One day, the prince and the cat found a big tree. They wanted to climb it, but they did not know how. They started to discuss what to do. The cat said, "I can climb the tree first, then help you up." The prince agreed, and the cat climbed the tree. The prince tried to climb the tree too, but he could not. The cat tried to help, but it was too hard. The prince was sad, and the cat was sad too. They decided to go back to the castle and ask for help. The prince's mom and dad showed them how to climb the tree. They were happy, and they played in the tree all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bobo. He wanted to learn how to hop high. But every time he tried, he felt helpless. He could not hop high at all. One day, Bobo met a big bunny named Bubu. Bubu was good at hopping high. Bubu said, "I can help you, Bobo!" So, they went to the park to start hopping. With Bubu's help, Bobo tried and tried again. At first, he was still helpless. But Bubu did not give up on him. After many tries, Bobo began to hop higher and higher. In the end, Bobo succeeded in hopping high. Bobo was very happy, and he thanked Bubu for being a good friend.

Once upon a time, there was an obedient dog named Max. Max loved to help his friends. One day, Max saw his friend, a little bird, trying to make a nest. The bird needed support to finish her nest. Max had an idea! He found a brush and picked it up with his mouth. Max used the brush to help the bird build her nest. They worked together, and the bird was very happy. In the end, the bird's nest was strong and cozy. Max felt proud that he could support his friend. The bird thanked Max for being such an obedient and helpful friend. And they lived happily ever after.
