Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Lily. Lily lived in a small house near the woods. One day, Lily went to the woods to find a log to sit on. In the woods, Lily saw a big log. But there was a sad squirrel on the log. The squirrel said, "I regret taking nuts from my friend. Now, I have no friend to play with." Lily felt bad for the squirrel. She said, "I will help you say sorry to your friend. Then, you can play together again." The squirrel smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily." Lily and the squirrel went to find the squirrel's friend. They said sorry and gave back the nuts. The two squirrels were happy and played together again. Lily was happy she could help them.

Sam and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of toys, because their parents were rich and bought them whatever they wanted. They liked to make a lot of noise and mess, and they did not care about anyone else. Their grandma was in the kitchen, making a cake for their birthday. She was very old and tired, and she needed to rest. She asked Sam and Lily to be quiet and not disturb her, but they did not listen. They kept shouting and throwing their toys around. "Grandma, we want cake!" Sam yelled. "Not now, dear. The cake is not ready yet. You have to wait." Grandma said. "But we are hungry and bored!" Lily whined. "Then why don't you play something else? Something nice and calm. Like a puzzle or a book." Grandma suggested. "No, we don't like those. We like loud and fun toys. Like this!" Sam said, and he pressed a button on his robot. The robot made a loud beep and started to walk around. The robot walked towards the kitchen, where Grandma had left the cake on the table. It bumped into the table and made the cake fall off. The cake landed on the floor with a splat, and the robot stepped on it. "Oh no! My cake!" Grandma cried. Sam and Lily ran to the kitchen and saw the mess. They saw Grandma's sad face and the ruined cake. They felt bad for what they had done. "Sorry, Grandma. We did not mean to disturb you." Sam said. "Sorry, Grandma. We did not mean to tip the cake." Lily said. Grandma sighed and hugged them. She was not angry, but she was disappointed. "It's okay, my dears. I know you did not do it on purpose. But you have to learn to be more careful and respectful. Being rich does not mean you can do whatever you want. You have to think about others and their feelings. Do you understand?" Grandma said. Sam and Lily nodded. They understood. They said they were sorry again and helped Grandma clean up the mess. They decided to play something else, something nice and calm. Like a puzzle or a book. And they waited for Grandma to make another cake.

Once upon a time, there was a robot. The robot liked to hang things on the wall. He was very good at it. The robot was also very persistent. He never gave up when he tried to hang something. One day, the robot found a big, pretty picture. He wanted to hang it on the wall for everyone to see. But when he tried to hang the picture, it was too heavy. The robot tried and tried, but he could not hang it up. Just then, a little girl came into the room. She saw the robot trying to hang the picture. The little girl had an idea. She helped the robot by holding the picture while he hung it up. They worked together, and the picture stayed on the wall. The robot and the little girl were happy. They did not expect to work together, but they made a great team.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy food for dinner. Tim saw a big red thing on the shelf. He asked his mom, "What is that?" His mom said, "That is a tomato." Tim did not know what a tomato was. He said, "I think I want to try it." His mom let him have one. They went home and his mom cut the tomato for him. Tim took a big bite. The tomato was wet and slimy in his mouth. Tim made a face and said, "Yuck! This is disgusting!" He did not like the tomato at all. From that day on, Tim knew he did not like tomatoes. But he was happy he tried something new.
