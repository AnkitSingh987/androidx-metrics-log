Momma and Baby were walking through the park. Baby looked around and saw a photo on a bench. “Momma, what is it?” asked Baby. Momma answered “It’s a photo Baby. Someone has taken a picture that they want to remember. Let’s take a closer look.” Baby walked up to the bench and could see an old wise man in the picture. The man had a kind smile and Baby liked him. “Momma, why does he look happy?” asked Baby. Momma replied, “The wise man is happy because he has surrendered his worries. He believes that if he gives up his troubles, the world will be better. That’s why he looks so happy.” Baby thought about what Momma said. He thought it was very wise.

Once upon a time, in a jolly little town, there was a big gear. This gear was very important, as it helped the town work well. Every day, the gear would turn and make the town run. One day, a little cat came to the town. The cat was very sleepy. It saw the big gear and thought it would be a nice place to sleep. So, the cat curled up and went to sleep on the gear. As the cat slept, the gear could not turn. The town stopped working. The people in the town were very sad. They tried to wake the cat, but it would not wake up. The town stayed still, and the people were not jolly anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was an ordinary boy who loved to play with his toys. One day, he saw a cat in his yard. The cat was very hungry and looked sad. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he went to his mom and whispered, "Mom, can I give the cat some milk?" His mom said, "Yes, you can help the cat. Let's give her some milk." So Tim and his mom went outside with a bowl of milk. They put the milk on the ground and the cat drank it up. The cat was very happy and thanked Tim. "Meow, thank you, Tim," the cat said. Tim felt happy too, because he helped a new friend.

One day, a little duck named Ducky had an idea. He wanted to bathe in the big pond. He was very excited to splash and play in the water with his friends. Ducky loved the pond because it was the most fun place in the whole world. Ducky and his friends went to the pond to bathe. They splashed and played in the water all day. They had so much fun! But then, Ducky saw a broken toy boat near the pond. It was sad and lonely. Ducky had an idea to make the toy boat happy again. Ducky and his friends worked together to fix the broken toy boat. They used sticks and leaves to make it look new again. Then, they put the toy boat in the pond and watched it float. The toy boat was so happy to be in the water again. Ducky and his friends were very proud of their idea and they all played together in the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a spoiled girl who always wanted her way. One day, she was playing with her toys when she saw her friend Timmy with a new toy car. Lily wanted that car too. Lily went to her mom and said, "Mommy, I want Timmy's car. Can I have it, please?" Her mom knew that Lily was spoiled and often got into trouble when she didn't get what she wanted. So, she decided to teach Lily a lesson. Mom said, "Lily, you can request to play with Timmy's car, but you can't have it. You need to learn to share and not cause trouble." Lily didn't like this answer, but she went to Timmy anyway. "Timmy, can I play with your car, please?" Lily asked. Timmy was a kind boy and said, "Sure, Lily, we can play together." They played with the car and had a lot of fun. Lily learned that sharing was better than always getting what she wanted.
