Once upon a time, there was a big billboard in a small town. It had a picture of a happy cat on it. The people of the town loved the cat and always smiled when they saw it. One day, a little girl named Lucy was walking with her mom. Lucy looked up and saw the billboard. She felt anxious because the cat looked sad. "Mom, the cat is sad. Can we save it?" she asked. Her mom looked at the billboard and said, "Don't worry, Lucy. The cat is just a picture. It's not real." Lucy wanted to make the cat happy again. She went home and drew a big smile on a piece of paper. She showed it to her mom and said, "Look, I made the cat happy!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Lucy. You saved the cat by making it happy again." Lucy felt proud and knew she could always help others, even if they were just pictures on a billboard.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little hut, there lived a kind rabbit. The rabbit had many friends who would come and play with him. They had fun all day long, running, jumping, and laughing. One day, the rabbit saw a big bird. The bird looked sad. The rabbit wanted to help the bird. He asked the bird, "Do you want to play with me and my friends in my cheerful hut?" The bird said, "Yes, I want to play with you." The rabbit, the bird, and all their friends played together in the cheerful hut. They had so much fun that the bird forgot he was sad. From that day on, the bird was always happy, and they all played together in the cheerful hut.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to eat pizza for lunch. He asked his mom, "Can we order pizza today?" His mom said, "Yes, we can order pizza." They called the pizza place and asked for a big, harmless pizza with cheese and tomato. They waited for the pizza to come to their house. Tim was very happy and excited. When the pizza came, they opened the box and saw a big surprise. The pizza was not a normal pizza. It was a pizza with a big smiley face made of cheese and tomato! They laughed and enjoyed the funny pizza together.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. A little red bird lived in the tree. The bird had a friend, a tiny squirrel. They liked to play together. One day, the squirrel said to the bird, "I found a nail. Let's play with it." The bird said, "Okay, but we must be careful. It is sharp." The bird and the squirrel played with the nail. They made a little game. They took turns hiding the nail, and then finding it. The bird hid the nail high in the tree. The squirrel hid the nail low in the grass. As they played, the bird said, "This game is fun. But it reminds me to be careful with the nail." The squirrel agreed, "Yes, we must be safe when we play." And so, the bird and the squirrel played safely and happily with the nail. They were always careful, and they had lots of fun together.

Once, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pet dog named Buddy. They liked to play together in the park. One day, when Lily was playing with Buddy, she saw an old man sitting on a bench. The old man was very wise. The old man saw Lily and Buddy playing and called out to them. He told Lily that he knew a lot and wanted to share his wisdom with her. Lily was very happy to learn from him. She listened carefully to everything the old man said. One day, while they were talking, Buddy started to run away. Lily yelled for Buddy to come back. The old man told Lily to be grateful for her dog and to always take care of him. Lily thanked the old man for his wisdom and promised to be a good friend to Buddy.

Once upon a time, there was a modest dog named Max. Max lived on a small planet with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to play and follow each other around the planet. One day, while they were playing, a big bird came to their planet. The bird was loud and scared Max and Lily. The bird wanted to play too, but it was too big and rough. Max and Lily didn't want to play with the bird because they were scared. Max and Lily tried to hide from the bird, but it kept following them. They decided to be brave and talk to the bird. They told the bird that it needed to be gentle if it wanted to play with them. The bird listened and became a good friend. They all played together and had fun on their small planet.
