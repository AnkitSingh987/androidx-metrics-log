Once upon a time, there was a long log by the river. A little boy named Tim went to play near the log every day. He liked to wonder what was under the log. He thought maybe there were bugs or maybe a frog. One sunny day, Tim went to the long log again. He heard a small sound from under the log. Tim was so excited! He wanted to know what was making the sound. He thought it was a bug or a frog, but he was wrong. Tim moved the long log and saw a tiny kitten. The kitten was scared and hungry. Tim felt so happy to find the kitten. He took the kitten home and gave it food and love. Now Tim and the kitten play near the log every day.

Once upon a time, in a jolly little town, there lived a boy named Tim. He liked to watch his mom sew pretty clothes for his toys. One day, his mom was using a needle to make a dress for Tim's favorite doll, Molly. "Mom, can I watch you sew?" asked Tim. "Yes, Tim, you can watch," said his mom with a smile. Tim sat down next to his mom and watched her sew the dress. He saw how the needle went in and out of the fabric, making the dress look nice. As he watched, Tim's mom told him a story about a magical needle that could sew anything in the world. Tim's eyes grew big as he listened. He imagined all the fun things he could make with the magical needle. When the dress was done, Tim's mom put it on Molly. Tim clapped his hands and said, "Thank you, Mom!"

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The park was very wide and had many things to play with. Tim loved to run, jump, and touch everything he saw. His mom told him, "Tim, be careful not to touch anything terrible." Tim played with his friends and had a lot of fun. But then, he saw a big, dark box in the corner of the park. Tim was curious and wanted to touch it. His friend Sally said, "I don't know, Tim. Your mom said not to touch anything terrible." Tim thought about what his mom said, but he couldn't help it. He touched the box, and suddenly, it opened! Out came a big, friendly dog. The dog was not terrible at all. Tim and Sally laughed and played with the dog. Tim learned that sometimes, things are not as terrible as they seem.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who wanted a bag. Whenever she saw someone with a bag, she would sigh in envy. One day, while she was playing in the park, she saw an old man with a beautiful bag. She wanted the bag so badly, so she sneaked up on the old man and stole it. The little girl ran away with the bag and was very excited. She ran until she reached a quiet corner and looked inside the bag. Suddenly she felt very bad. The bag contained the old man's lunch and a picture of his family. The little girl realized her mistake and ran back to the park. But the old man was gone. She sighed sadly and returned home, feeling very guilty. The moral of this story is that it's wrong to take something that doesn't belong to you, even if you really want it. Greed can make us do bad things, so it's important to be honest and respect other people's property.

Once there was a puppy. He was very shy. One day he was walking down the street when a big boy came up to him. The big boy wanted to play a game. He said to the puppy, “Let’s twist around!” The puppy didn’t want to twist around but the boy was very big and strong. He kept twisting the puppy until the puppy started to cry. The boy finally stopped, but the puppy was very hurt. The poor puppy had to go to the doctor and his owner was very sad. The puppy was so scared that he never wanted to twist again. The bad ending was that the puppy was always too shy to play with other dogs.
