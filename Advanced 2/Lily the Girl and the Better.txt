Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big, cool toy bear. She loved to play with her bear every day. One day, Lily felt sick. Her mommy gave her some medicine to make her feel better. Lily looked at her bear and said, "Bear, do you want some medicine too?" The bear did not say anything. Lily thought that maybe her bear felt sick too. So, she gave her bear a little bit of her medicine. The next day, Lily felt better. She asked her bear, "Do you feel better too, Bear?" The bear did not say anything, but Lily thought the bear gave a little shrug. Lily was happy that her bear felt better too. They played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a colorful forest, there lived a little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to act and put on shows for his animal friends. One day, Ben decided to make a big event for everyone in the forest. Ben hopped to his friend, the squirrel, and said, "I am making a big event! I will act and make everyone happy! Will you help me?" The squirrel nodded and said, "Yes, Ben! I will help you!" Together, Ben and the squirrel made a stage out of sticks and leaves. They invited all their friends to watch the show. The birds, the deer, and even the ants came to see Ben act. On the day of the event, the forest was full of happy animals. Ben put on a colorful hat and started to act. He made funny faces and danced around. All the animals laughed and clapped. After the show, the animals thanked Ben and the squirrel for making such a fun event. The colorful forest was filled with joy and laughter. And from that day on, Ben and his friends put on more shows for everyone to enjoy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big tree with a hole in it. Tim was curious, so he looked inside the hole. He found a treasure box! Tim was very excited. Tim tried to open the box, but it was closed with a zip. He pulled the zip and opened the box. Inside, he found many colorful toys and a big, soft teddy bear. Tim was very happy. As Tim was playing with his new toys, he heard a noise. It was a small, frightened bird that fell from its nest. Tim picked up the bird and put it back in its nest. The bird was safe and happy. Tim felt great because he had found treasure and helped a friend.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a young boy named Tim. Tim had a toy cat that he loved to play with all day. One day, when Tim was playing with his toy cat, it vanished. Tim looked everywhere, but he could not find it. He felt sad and sat down on his knee. Then, Tim heard a soft meow. He looked up and saw a real cat standing in front of him. The cat was just like his toy cat. Tim was so happy and started to play with the real cat. They played all day and had lots of fun. But the next day, the real cat vanished too. Tim was sad again. He went to his room and found his toy cat on his bed. It turned out that his mom had found the toy cat and put it back for him. Tim was happy to have his toy cat back, and sometimes, he still played with the real cat when it came to visit.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Lucy. She was three years old and very naughty. Her parents had bought her a beautiful paint set, with lots of bright colours, but she only used it to draw on the walls. Her parents were very angry. "You must not paint on the walls!" they told her. But Lucy did not listen. Her parents tried to show Lucy the right way to paint. "You must hold the brush by the handle!" they said. But Lucy was too busy to listen. One day, Lucy was painting on the walls again but this time, she suddenly felt guilty. She stopped and looked around her. She had made a mess. Lucy now knew what she had to do. She got her paint set and started to paint on a piece of paper instead. This time she held the brush properly, by the handle. It's important to listen to your parents and use things in the right way. Lucy now knew this and was never naughty with her paint set again.
