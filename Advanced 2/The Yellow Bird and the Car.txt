Once upon a time, there was a yellow bird. The yellow bird had a pretty egg. The egg was in a nest high up in a tree. One day, the yellow bird went to find food. While the bird was away, a big wind came. The wind made the egg roll out of the nest. The egg went down the tree and onto the ground. A car saw the egg and stopped. The car wanted to help the yellow bird. The car picked up the egg and zoomed to the tree. The yellow bird came back and saw the car with the egg. The bird was very happy. The car put the egg back in the nest. The yellow bird said thank you to the car. They became friends and lived happily ever after.

Once there was a foolish rabbit who always wanted to eat carrots. One day he decided he wanted to finish all the carrots in the garden. He hopped around and ate as many as he could. Soon, he was so full that he could hardly move. He felt so silly. The rabbit knew he should have eaten just a few carrots, like he usually did. Suddenly, he heard a loud voice shout: "If you eat too much in one go, you won't have any carrots for later!" The rabbit looked around but couldn't see where the voice came from. Then he realized it was his own voice of wisdom, reminding him to be careful. Ever since then, the rabbit has been careful not to eat too many carrots in one go. He has learned that it can be foolish to finish everything!

Once upon a time, there was a little, reliable dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They were always together. One sunny day, they went to the park. Spot saw a long strip of grass and insisted on playing fetch. Tim threw a ball, and Spot ran after it. They played and laughed for a long time. As the sun began to set, it was time to go home. Tim and Spot were tired but happy. They knew they could always count on each other for fun and friendship. And from that day on, they played fetch on that strip of grass every chance they got.

Jill was an enthusiastic young girl who liked spending her days outside. One day, she was walking near a pond in her garden when she saw something strange. Suddenly, a frog hopped out from the pond! Jill smiled and said "Hi frog! What's the problem?" The frog made a croaked noise that sounded like it was saying "Help me!" Jill thought hard and then she knew what to do. She said to the frog: "I can solve your problem frog! Follow me." Jill took the frog to a bucket of water nearby. She placed the frog gently inside. The frog blinked its eyes in thanks and then hopped away. Jill was very pleased with herself. She felt quite proud, but she was also a little embarrassed that she had been talking to a frog. She hurried back home and promised herself to never tell anyone about her adventure with the frog!

Once there was a penguin. He was very cheerful, and he smiled all the time. One day, he went for a walk. He seemed happy, and kept on smiling. But then, something happened. He looked up, and he saw a big soar in the sky. He felt scared. He started to run. He thought that the soar would get him. He ran to the ocean. He wanted to get away from the soar. But when he got to the ocean, it seemed very dark and cold. He didn't like it. He looked up, and the soar was still there. He started to cry. He wished he had never gone for a walk. Then, the soar swooped down and it flew away with the penguin. The end.

Ben and Mia liked to play with their toy automobiles. They had many different colors and sizes of automobiles. They made noises and pretended to drive them on the floor. One day, they saw a big, shiny automobile parked outside their house. It was red and had four doors. They wondered who it belonged to. "Let's go see the big automobile!" Ben said to Mia. "OK, but we have to be careful. Mommy said not to touch other people's things," Mia said. They put on their shoes and ran outside. They looked at the big automobile with curiosity. It had a silver key in the ignition. "Wow, look at the key! Maybe we can make the automobile go!" Ben said. He reached for the key, but Mia stopped him. "No, Ben, don't touch the key! That's not our automobile. We could get in trouble," Mia said. But Ben did not listen. He grabbed the key and turned it. The automobile made a loud noise and started to move. "Ben, stop! What are you doing?" Mia shouted. Ben was scared. He did not know how to stop the automobile. He pushed and pulled on the wheel, but the automobile did not stop. It went faster and faster. "Help! Help!" Ben and Mia cried. The automobile went out of the driveway and onto the street. It bumped into a trash can and a mailbox. It scared a dog and a cat. It made people scream and honk. The automobile was out of control. It disappeared around the corner. Ben and Mia did not know what to do. They hoped the automobile would stop soon. They hoped no one would get hurt. They hoped Mommy would not be angry. They wished they had never touched the key. They wished they had stayed inside and played with their toy automobiles. They wished they had listened to Mia.
