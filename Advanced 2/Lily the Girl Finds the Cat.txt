Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a big, fierce dog named Max. Max was a very good dog, and he loved to play with Lily. They would run and jump and have lots of fun together. One day, while they were playing, Max found a cut on his paw. Lily saw the cut and wanted to help Max feel better. Lily said, "Max, you have a cut on your paw. Let me help you." Max looked at Lily with his big, sad eyes, and he let her take care of his cut. Lily went inside the house and found a band-aid. She put it on Max's paw, and he felt much better. Suddenly, a big, fluffy cat appeared in their yard. Max and Lily were very surprised to see the cat. Max, being a fierce dog, wanted to chase the cat away. But Lily said, "Max, don't chase the cat. Let's be friends with the cat." Max listened to Lily, and they all became friends. They played together every day, and they all lived happily ever after.

One morning, a little girl named Sue woke up. She was very stubborn. Sue did not want to attach her shoes by herself. She asked her mom for help. "Mom, can you attach my shoes?" Sue said. Her mom smiled and said, "You can do it, Sue. I know you can!" Sue tried and tried, but she was still stubborn. Her mom told her a story about a brave girl who could attach her shoes all by herself. Sue wanted to be like that girl. So, Sue tried again. This time, she was able to attach her shoes! She felt so happy and proud. Sue's mom gave her a big hug and said, "See? I knew you could do it!" From that morning on, Sue was not stubborn about attaching her shoes anymore.

Once upon a time there was an expensive wide house. There was a family who had lots of fun living there. One day the family decided to take a trip. Dad said he would take all the special things with him that they wouldn't want to leave behind. When it was time to go the family packed all the things and took them away. As the family said goodbye to their wide house, they were sad to leave. Everything looked very different once they drove away. The family asked someone to look after their wide house, but they forgot to ask how much the person wanted to be paid. When they returned, the person had taken away all of their expensive furniture. All that remained was an empty, expensive house.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to collect rocks. One day, he went to a big pit to find more rocks. The pit was very deep, and Tom was excited to see what he could find. Tom met another boy named Sam at the pit. Sam said, "I want to be successful at collecting rocks too!" So, Tom and Sam started to look for rocks together. They found many pretty rocks and put them in their bags. Suddenly, they heard a noise. It was a big dog! The dog had a shiny rock in its mouth. The dog put the rock down and wagged its tail. Tom and Sam were surprised! They never thought they would find a dog at the pit. The dog wanted to help them collect rocks. Tom, Sam, and the dog became good friends and were very successful at collecting rocks together.

Once upon a time, there was a humble bunny. The bunny loved to teach others. One day, the bunny found a squash in the garden. The bunny wanted to teach his friends how to grow squash. He showed them how to plant the seeds and water them. They all worked together and waited for the squash to grow. But one day, a big bird came to the garden. The bird saw the squash and wanted to eat it. The bird took the squash away. The bunny and his friends were sad. They learned that sometimes things don't end well.
