Once upon a time, there was a kind doctor who liked to supply medicine to all the sick people in the town. He had a big bag full of medicine. The people were always grateful when he made them feel better. One day, the doctor went to a house where a little girl was sick. He wanted to supply her with the right medicine to make her feel better. The girl's mom was very grateful and said thank you. But, the doctor made a mistake. He gave the little girl the wrong medicine. The girl did not feel better. In fact, she felt worse. The mom was sad and the doctor felt very bad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a charming shrimp. This shrimp lived in a big sea with many friends. They loved to play together all day long. One day, the shrimp heard a big fish say that something bad was coming. The shrimp did not know what it meant, but it made him feel scared. The charming shrimp went to tell his friends about what he heard. They all felt scared too. They knew they had to do something to be safe. The shrimp had an idea. He said, "We must unite and stay close to each other. Then, we can be strong and safe together." The friends listened to the charming shrimp and decided to unite. They stayed close and played together, just like before. When the big fish came back, it saw how strong the friends were united. It could not hurt them now. The charming shrimp and his friends were happy and safe, and they played together forever.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with easy steps to climb. Tim wanted to climb the tree, so he asked his mom if he could. His mom said yes, but to be careful. As Tim climbed the tree, he saw a bird with a red hat. The bird looked at Tim and said, "Give me your arm." Tim was surprised that the bird could talk. The bird said again, "Give me your arm," but this time it said "please." Tim didn't know what to do, so he gave the bird his arm. The bird took Tim's arm and put it on its head. The red hat was gone! Tim's arm was now a hat. Tim was happy because he didn't need his arm to climb down. He went down the tree with his new hat-arm. His mom was very surprised and they both laughed.

Once upon a time, there were two owls, Big Owly and Little Owly. Big Owly was big and strong, but Little Owly was small and gentle. "Let's shoot the feather!" said Big Owly. So Big Owly and Little Owly went out to collect some feathers. Little Owly found a gentle feather and decided to shoot it. Big Owly was tired of waiting, "You're too slow. Let me shoot it instead," he said. But Little Owly was determined to shoot the feather himself. So they argued back and forth until finally, Big Owly said: "If you want to shoot the feather, then you should do it. You know you can do it!" And so, with a little bit of courage, Little Owly went ahead and shot the feather. It felt so good to do it himself. The moral of the story is that everyone should be allowed to do things their way, even if it takes a little bit of extra effort. Doing things yourself can be a great feeling!

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a fierce bull. The bull had big, sharp horns. He liked to quarrel with the other animals. One day, he met a little rabbit. The rabbit was scared of the bull's horns. The bull wanted to quarrel with the rabbit, but the rabbit had a plan. The rabbit said, "Let's have a race. If I win, you must stop being fierce and quarreling with others." The bull agreed, thinking he would win. They started the race. The rabbit ran fast, and the bull ran fast too. But the rabbit was too quick for the bull. In the end, the rabbit won the race. The bull was sad, but he remembered the promise he made. From that day on, the bull stopped being fierce and quarreling with others. He became a kind and friendly bull. The other animals liked him now. The moral of the story is: it is better to be kind and friendly than to be fierce and quarrel with others.
