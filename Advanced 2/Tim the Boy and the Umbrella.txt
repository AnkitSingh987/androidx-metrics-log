Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big red umbrella. He loved to play with it in the rain. One day, it was raining very hard. Tim took his umbrella and went outside to play. Tim was playing when he saw a small cat. The cat was wet and cold. "Help me, please!" said the cat. Tim wanted to help the cat, but he did not know how. He thought and thought, and then he had an idea. Tim opened his big red umbrella and said, "Come, cat! Escape the rain with me!" The cat jumped under the umbrella. They ran very fast to Tim's house. The rain could not get them. When they got to the house, the cat was warm and dry. The cat said, "Thank you, Tim, for saving me from the rain!" Tim was happy he could help the cat. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max had thick fur and big paws. He lived in a small house with his friend, a little boy named Tim. One day, Max and Tim went for a walk in the park. Max felt happy to be outside. He ran and played with his big paws. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. They played catch with a red ball. After a while, Max got tired. He lay down on the soft grass. Tim sat down next to him and said, "Max, I feel happy when I am with you." Max wagged his tail and licked Tim's face. They both smiled and went home, feeling very happy.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a big empty bag in her room. She thought, "I can use this bag to celebrate my friend's birthday!" So, Mia took the bag and went to the store to buy some fun things for the party. She got balloons, candy, and a small toy for her friend. At the party, Mia's friend, Tom, saw the bag and asked, "What's in the bag, Mia?" She smiled and said, "Surprise! I got you some fun things to celebrate your birthday!" Tom was very happy and opened the bag. But, something unexpected happened. Instead of the balloons, candy, and toy, there were only old socks in the bag! Mia was very confused, and so was Tom. Then, Mia's little brother came running into the room, laughing. He said, "I switched the bags! I thought it would be a funny joke!" Mia and Tom laughed too. They knew it was just a silly trick. They found the real bag with the fun things, and had a great time celebrating Tom's birthday.

Mimi was an obedient girl who lived with her mummy and daddy. She was three and loved to watch the television. One day, Mimi asked her mummy if she could watch the television while they had dinner. Mummy said, "No, Mimi. Mummy and Daddy need to eat dinner and you need to manage without the television." Mimi was sad. She thought, "I will manage better if I had the television." Then she asked, "Please, Mummy, can I watch some television while I manage my dinner?" Mummy smiled and said, "Yes, you can, but you must manage to only watch for a few minutes." Mimi was happy and she managed to eat her dinner while watching her favourite show. From then on, she was an obedient girl who managed to look after herself and watch the television at the same time.

One day, a big black bird was flying high in the sky. It was very hot, and the bird was thirsty. It saw a small oasis far away. The bird thought, "I can get water there and fill my beak!" When the bird got to the oasis, it saw a little girl. The girl was also thirsty. She said, "Hello, bird! Can you help me fill my cup with water?" The bird nodded and used its beak to fill the girl's cup. Just then, something unexpected happened. The water in the oasis turned into sweet juice! The girl and the bird looked at each other and smiled. They drank the juice and felt happy. They had found a magic oasis that made their hot day much better.
