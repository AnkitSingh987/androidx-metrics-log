Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, red ball. He loved to play with his ball every day. One day, he left his ball on the top of the hill. Tim was sad because the ball was important to him. Tim asked his mom for help. They walked to the top of the hill together. They looked for the ball everywhere. Tim was worried that he would never find his ball again. Finally, they found the ball under a big tree. Tim was very happy. He hugged his mom and thanked her for helping him. From that day on, Tim always remembered to put his important ball away when he was done playing with it.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys and make a big mess. He had a big bin where he kept all his toys. One day, Tim's mom asked him to care for his toys and put them in the bin when he was done playing. Tim did not listen to his mom. He thought it was silly to care for his toys and put them in the bin. He left his toys all over the floor and went outside to play. While he was gone, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all his toys away. When Tim came back, he saw that his toys were gone. He was very sad. Tim's mom told him that if he had cared for his toys and put them in the bin, they would not have been blown away. Tim learned that it is important to care for his things and not be silly. From that day on, he always put his toys in the bin when he was done playing.
