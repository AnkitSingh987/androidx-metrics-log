Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Every morning, her dad would set an alarm so they could wake up in time. Despite the alarm, Lucy would struggle to remain awake. One morning, her dad came in and saw how tired she was. He said, "Why don't we try a regular wake-up time? We'll set the alarm for the same time every morning, that way you won't be so tired." Lucy said, "OK, Daddy. That sounds good." From that day on, every morning, their alarm would go off at the same time. Lucy would wake up and feel much more rested because she was getting a regular, good night's sleep. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cow named Daisy. Daisy lived on a big farm with her friends. She liked to eat grass and play in the sun. One day, Daisy was very sleepy. She wanted to take a nap under a tree. Her friend, a little bird, saw her and asked, "Daisy, why are you so sleepy?" Daisy replied, "I don't understand why, but I feel so tired today." The little bird thought for a moment and said, "Maybe you need to rest for a bit. Let's find a nice, soft spot for you to sleep." Together, Daisy and the little bird found a cozy spot under a big tree. Daisy laid down, closed her eyes, and fell fast asleep. The little bird watched over her as she slept. When Daisy woke up, she felt much better and thanked her friend for helping her. From that day on, Daisy and the little bird were the best of friends.

Lily and Tom were at the park with Mom. They saw a big fountain with water that went up and down. Lily and Tom liked the fountain. They wanted to touch the water. Mom said, "Be careful, the water is cold. And don't get wet." Lily and Tom nodded. They went closer to the fountain. They saw a cute duck swimming in the water. The duck had yellow feathers and an orange beak. Lily and Tom smiled. They wanted to pet the duck. They reached out their hands to the duck. But the duck was shy. It swam away from them. Lily and Tom followed the duck. They did not see the water that went up and down. Splash! The water came down on Lily and Tom. They got wet. They felt cold. They cried. Mom heard them cry. She ran to them. She hugged them. She said, "Oh, no, you are wet. Let's go home and change. And have some hot chocolate." Lily and Tom nodded. They still liked the duck. But they did not like the water. They wanted to go home and melt.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went on a big boat. They wanted to find a new island. The island was unknown. No one had seen it before. Tim asked Max, "Do you think we will find the island?" Max did not know, so he just shrugged. They kept going on the boat, looking for the island. After a long time, they saw land. They found the unknown island! Tim and Max were so happy. The island was full of fun things to play with. They knew they would have a great adventure.

The family had a big plan on Wednesday. They were going to visit the wealthy aunt. Everyone was so excited. They talked about what clothes to wear and what gifts to bring. On the morning of Wednesday, the family hopped into the car and drove for a long time. Finally, they reached the wealthy aunt's house. When the family arrived, the wealthy aunt was so happy to see them. She talked about how much she missed them. Then they gave her the gifts they had brought. She was really pleased. The family stayed with her for the whole day, talking and laughing together. They ate together, played together and had a great time. When it was time to go, the family said goodbye to the wealthy aunt. She was sad to see them go, yet happy they had all spent the day together.
