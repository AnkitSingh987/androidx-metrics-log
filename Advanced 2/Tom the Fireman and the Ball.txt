Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Tom. Tom was very careful when he did his job. He loved to help people and discover new things. One day, Tom went to the park to play. He saw a big red ball and wanted to discover what was inside. He tried to be careful, but the ball rolled away and went into the street. Tom was sad and wanted his ball back. Tom ran to get the ball, but he forgot to look both ways before crossing the street. A big car came and hit Tom. The careful fireman was hurt very badly. He could not help people or discover new things anymore. Everyone was very sad because Tom was not careful this time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to study. He had a big book with pictures of things. One day, he saw a picture of a bulb. It was a very original bulb. It was big and bright. Tim wanted to find this bulb. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can I find the original bulb?" His mom said, "No, Tim. It is too far away. You might get lost." But Tim did not listen. He went out to look for the original bulb. Tim walked and walked. He saw a big tree. He thought the original bulb might be there. He climbed the tree. He looked around. But he did not see the bulb. Then, he saw a big hole in the ground. He thought the original bulb might be there. He went into the hole. In the hole, it was dark. Tim was scared. He heard a voice. It said, "I have the original bulb. You can have it if you want." Tim said, "Yes, please." The voice gave Tim the bulb. But the bulb was not bright. It was dark and scary. Tim tried to go back home. But he was lost. He had the original bulb, but it was not what he wanted. He was sad and scared. He wished he had listened to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful football. It was red, blue, and yellow. The football had many friends who liked to play with it. One day, two boys wanted to play with the colorful football at the same time. They started to fight over it. They pulled the football this way and that way. The football was sad because it didn't like to see its friends fight. Then, a big girl came and saw the boys fighting. She told them to stop and share the colorful football. The boys listened and started to play together. The football was happy again, and everyone had fun.

One day, a little dog named Max was playing in the park. He saw a pretty pattern on the ground. It was made of colorful stones. Max wanted to show his friend, a big cat named Sam. Max called, "Sam, come see this!" Sam came running over to Max. He saw the pattern too. "Wow, that's nice!" Sam said. But Sam wanted the pattern all for himself. He was being selfish. He told Max, "I want to keep it. You can't look at it anymore." Max was sad, but then something unexpected happened. A bird flew down from the sky and landed on the pattern. The bird looked at Max and Sam. Then, it started to move the colorful stones. The pattern changed! Sam was surprised and didn't want the pattern anymore. Max and Sam looked at the new pattern together. They both learned that sharing is better than being selfish. And they played happily in the park for the rest of the day.

One day, a bird named Bob had a big feather. Bob was very sleepy. He saw his friend, the dog named Max. Max wanted to perform a trick. "Look, Max!" said Bob. "I have a big feather. I am very sleepy. Can you perform a trick?" Max said, "Yes, Bob. I can jump high and catch the feather." Max jumped high to catch the feather. But something unexpected happened. The feather flew away! Max and Bob were sad. They looked for the feather and found it on a tree. Max jumped high again and got the feather. They were happy and went home to play. Bob was not sleepy anymore.
