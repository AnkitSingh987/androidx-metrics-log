Once upon a time, there was a delicate little fish named Fin. Fin had a big lung, and he loved to swim fast. One day, he saw a race. Many fish were there to swim and try to win. Fin wanted to join, but he was scared. Fin's mom told him, "You have a big lung, and you are fast. You can do it!" Fin felt better and decided to try. The race started, and Fin swam as fast as he could. He used his big lung to help him swim faster. As the race went on, Fin was close to the finish. He thought of his mom's words and swam even faster. With one last push, Fin crossed the finish line. He had won the race! All the other fish cheered for him. Fin was so happy, and he knew his big lung had helped him win.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing outside with his old toy car. He liked to pretend it could go very fast, like lightning. As he played, he started to wonder about how fast lightning really was. One day, a big storm came to Tim's town. Tim saw the lightning in the sky. It was so bright and fast! He wondered if his old toy car could ever go as fast as the lightning. He wanted to race with the lightning, but he knew it was not safe to play outside during a storm. After the storm was over, Tim went outside to play again. He looked at his old toy car and smiled. Even if it could not go as fast as the lightning, he still loved it. Tim knew that he could always wonder about the lightning while playing with his old toy car. And that made him very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and keep them organized. Lily had a big box where all her toys fit nicely. One day, Lily hurt her knee when she fell down. She was sad and didn't want to play. Lily's mom saw her sad face and had an idea. She told Lily to sit down and watch. Mom took all the toys out of the box and put a soft pillow in it. Then, she told Lily to put her hurt knee on the pillow. Lily did as her mom said, and her knee felt better. But now, Lily's toys did not have a place to be organized. Just then, Lily's dad came home with a surprise. He had a new, bigger box for Lily's toys! Lily was so happy. She put all her toys in the new box, and they all fit perfectly. Now, Lily could keep her toys organized and take care of her knee too.
