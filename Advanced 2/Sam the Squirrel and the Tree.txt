Once upon a time, in a big green tree, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam was a clumsy squirrel. He loved to play with his friends in the forest, but sometimes he would fall or bump into things. One day, Sam found a big nut on the ground. He was so happy! He wanted to take the nut to his home and save it for later. But Sam was clumsy, and he kept dropping the nut. His friends saw him and laughed, but Sam did not give up. Sam tried very hard to stay near the nut and not drop it. He walked very slowly and carefully, holding the nut tight in his little paws. Finally, Sam made it to his home in the tree with the nut. He was so proud of himself! From that day on, Sam learned that even if he was clumsy, he could still do things if he tried hard and stayed focused.

Once upon a time there was a little elephant named Earl. Earl was very happy, he loved to explore the world and drink from the river. One day he wanted to drink something fancy, so he went to the river and looked for something special. He found a trunk! He curled his trunk around it and started to drink. Suddenly, another elephant showed up. He was very angry and he said, "That's MY trunk, you shouldn't drink from it!" Earl was very scared, so he let go of the trunk and stepped away. He didn't want to make the other elephant mad. At first, Earl was very sad, he wanted to have something fancy to drink. But then, he realized that it was better to respect the other elephant and share what he found. That's the moral of this story: it's better to share and respect than to take what isn't yours.

Once upon a time there was a small boy called Jack who was very adventurous. One day, he decided to explore the forest nearby. As he was wandering, he heard a strange noise. He stopped and looked around, but he couldn't see anything. Suddenly, out of nowhere, a big rabbit appeared. "Hi there," said the rabbit. "Would you like to join me on an adventure?" Jack's eyes lit up. "Yes! What sort of adventure?" He asked. "Come and follow me," said the rabbit. The rabbit and Jack went down a path until they reached a small door. "What's behind this door?" Jack asked. "Inside is a secret room," said the rabbit. "There is a box of colourful tapes. We can make something special with them!" Jack was so excited. They opened the door and ran inside. The rabbit found the tape and they started creating different shapes. Jack was happy, it was so much fun! After a while, they had created lots of adventurous things. When the adventure was over, Jack said goodbye to the rabbit and went home feeling very happy. He was so glad that the rabbit had appeared!

Once upon a time, there was a big house. In this house lived a happy family. There was a mommy, a daddy, and a little girl named Amy. Amy had a pet fish named Bubbles. Bubbles liked to dive in his fishbowl. One sunny day, the family went for a walk. When they came back, they saw a terrible mess in the house. Everything was on the floor! They were very sad. They did not know who made the mess. Then, they saw Bubbles diving in his fishbowl. But wait! Bubbles was not a fish! Bubbles was a tiny magical dragon! The family was very surprised. Bubbles changed back into a fish and the house was clean again. The family was happy and they all lived together with their magical pet.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with a trunk that was very wide and brown. They wanted to climb the tree and see the birds and the sky. "Can we climb the tree, Mom?" Ben asked. Mom looked at the tree and shook her head. "No, Ben. The tree is too high and the trunk is too rough. You might fall and hurt yourself. And the birds might not like it if you disturb them." Ben and Lily felt sad. They liked to climb and explore. They looked around and saw a big hill with a light green grass. They had an idea. "Can we roll down the hill, Mom?" Lily asked. Mom looked at the hill and nodded. "Yes, Lily. The hill is not too steep and the grass is soft. You can roll down the hill and have fun. But be careful and don't go too far." Ben and Lily smiled. They ran to the hill and lay down on the grass. They held hands and started to roll. They felt the wind and the sun on their faces. They laughed and shouted. "Look, Mom, we are like balls!" Ben said. "Yes, you are, and you are very fast!" Mom said. Ben and Lily rolled down the hill until they reached the bottom. They stood up and brushed off the grass. They felt dizzy and happy. "Can we do it again, Mom?" Lily asked. Mom smiled and permitted them. "Yes, you can. But only one more time. Then we have to go home and have lunch." Ben and Lily hugged Mom and thanked her. They ran to the hill and rolled down again. They had a lot of fun in the park.
