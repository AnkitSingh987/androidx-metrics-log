Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. Lily has a big doll that can talk. Ben has a small car that can move. They share their toys and have fun. One day, Lily and Ben receive a letter from their grandma. Grandma lives far away, on the other side of the sea. She sends them a gift for their birthday. Lily and Ben are happy and excited. They open the letter and find a card and a box. The card says: "Happy birthday, Lily and Ben! I love you very much. I hope you like your gift. It is something special for you. Love, Grandma." Lily and Ben open the box and see a fluffy teddy bear. The teddy bear has a red bow and a heart on its chest. It also has a button on its ear. Lily and Ben hug the teddy bear and thank Grandma. "Let's see what the teddy bear can do," Lily says. She presses the button on its ear. The teddy bear says: "Hello, I am your new friend. I can sing and tell stories. Do you want to hear one?" Lily and Ben nod and smile. They listen to the teddy bear's voice. But Ben notices something strange. He says: "Lily, the teddy bear only talks in one ear. The other ear is quiet. Why is that?" Lily looks at the teddy bear and sees a small tag on its other ear. She reads it and says: "Oh, Ben, the teddy bear is deaf in one ear. That means it cannot hear from that side. But it can still talk and sing and tell stories. It is still a nice teddy bear." Ben nods and says: "You are right, Lily. The teddy bear is still a nice teddy bear. And it is a special gift from Grandma. She knows we love teddy bears. And we love her too." Lily and Ben hug the teddy bear again and say: "Thank you, Grandma. We love you and your gift. You are the best Grandma ever."

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Bessie. Bessie loved to be creative. She lived on a farm with her best friend, a bird named Tweety. One day, Bessie and Tweety were talking. Bessie said, "I want to record my moo and your tweet. We can make a fun song together!" Tweety agreed, and they started to make their song. Bessie mooed loudly, and Tweety tweeted sweetly. They sang and danced, having lots of fun. When they finished, they played their song for all their friends on the farm. The other animals loved their song. They clapped and cheered for Bessie and Tweety. From that day on, Bessie and Tweety were known as the best singers on the farm. And every time they ate their favorite food, beef, they would remember the fun they had making their song together. Bessie and Tweety were happy, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim found a penny on the ground. He was very happy. He wanted to buy a toy with his new penny. The toy was a modern car that could go very fast. Tim loved the car because it was shiny and red. Tim went to the store with his penny. He showed the store man the penny and said, "I want to buy the red car." The store man looked at the penny and said, "This penny is not enough. You need more pennies to buy the car." Tim was sad. He went home and told his mom about the modern car. He said, "I want the red car, but I need more pennies." His mom heard him and said, "You can do chores to earn more pennies." Tim worked hard and did many chores. He soon had enough pennies to buy the car. Tim went back to the store with his pennies. The store man counted the pennies and said, "Now you have enough pennies to buy the red car." Tim was very happy. He took the modern car home and played with it all day.

Once upon a time, there was a unique little girl named Mia. She loved to visit different places with her mom and dad. One day, Mia had a big dream. She wanted to visit the universe! Mia told her mom and dad about her dream. "I want to visit the universe!" she said. "Can we go there?" Her mom and dad smiled at her. "That's a big dream, Mia," her dad said. "But we can try to learn about the universe together." So, Mia, her mom, and her dad went to a big place with many books. They found a book about the universe and looked at the pictures together. They saw stars, the moon, and other unique things. Mia was very happy. As they went home, Mia's mom said, "Now you know that the universe is very big and special. It's important to keep learning and dreaming. That way, you can visit many unique places, even if it's just in your mind." And so, Mia learned that dreams can take you to unique places, even if you can't really visit them. She also learned that learning and dreaming are important. And from that day on, Mia never stopped dreaming about the universe and other special places.
