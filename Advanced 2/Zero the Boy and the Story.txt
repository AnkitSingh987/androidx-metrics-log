Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Zero. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, it was very cold and the wind was blowing. Zero's mom told him to stay inside, but he didn't listen. Zero went outside to play with his friends. They played a game called "freeze". When someone said "freeze", everyone had to stop and not move. Zero was very good at this game. He was always alert and could hear when his friends said "freeze". As they played, the wind got stronger and it got colder. Zero started to feel very cold, but he didn't want to stop playing. His friends went home, but Zero stayed outside. Soon, he was so cold that he couldn't move. His mom found him and took him inside. Zero got very sick and couldn't play outside for a long time. The moral of the story is to always listen to your mom and stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to study his big book of animals. One day, he heard a mysterious noise outside his window. Tim was curious and wanted to find out what was making the noise. He went outside and saw a big, friendly dog. The dog said, "Woof! Woof!" Tim asked the dog, "Did you make that noise?" The dog wagged its tail and barked again, "Woof! Woof!" Tim knew that the dog was the one making the mysterious noise. Tim and the dog became good friends. They played together every day and Tim continued to study his big book of animals. He even learned more about dogs. Tim and the dog were very happy and loved to make noise together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to learn new words. He had a big book with many words. It was called a dictionary. Tim would read the dictionary every day. One cold day, Tim found a small box. The box could record his voice. Tim thought it was fun. He wanted to record all the words he knew from the dictionary. So, he opened the dictionary and started to say the words out loud. The box recorded his voice. When Tim finished, he played the recording. He heard all the words he said. Tim was happy. Now, he could listen to the words anytime. Tim took the box and the dictionary to his mom. She was proud of him. They listened to the words together and Tim learned even more words.

Once upon a time, there was a big building. This building was very brilliant. It was a place where people would go to dance and have fun. One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the building with her mom and dad. They wanted to see the people dance. Lucy was very excited. She loved to watch dancing. When they got to the building, they saw many people dancing. The music was loud and happy. Lucy started to dance too. She jumped and twirled with a big smile on her face. Lucy's mom and dad danced with her. They all laughed and had a great time. The brilliant building was filled with joy and dancing. As the sun went down, it was time to go home. Lucy, her mom, and her dad left the building, still smiling. They knew they would come back to the brilliant building to dance again soon.

Once upon a time, a little baby named Tim was playing in the park. He had a red ball that he loved very much. Tim liked to throw the ball and watch it bounce. One day, he threw the ball too far and it rolled away. Tim was sad and wanted to find his ball. Tim started to search for his red ball. He looked under the slide and behind the swings. He even asked his friend, "Have you seen my red ball?" His friend said, "No, but let's look together." So, they both searched for the little red ball. Finally, they found the red ball near a big tree. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you for helping me!" They both played with the red ball and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tim always made sure to keep his little red ball close by.
