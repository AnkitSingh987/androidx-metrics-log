Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a beautiful dress that she loved very much. One day, she wanted to wear her beautiful dress to play outside with her friends. "Mom, can I wear my beautiful dress to play outside?" asked Lily. Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to get it dirty." While playing with her friends, Lily saw a sad girl sitting alone. She went up to her and asked, "Why are you sad?" The sad girl said, "I don't have a pretty dress like yours to wear." Lily thought for a moment and decided to do something unexpected. She took off her beautiful dress and gave it to the sad girl. Now, both girls were happy and they spent the whole day playing together. Lily learned that sharing with others can make everyone happy.

One day, a little bunny named Ben went to the woods. He saw a big tree with a pear on it. The pear was green and round. Ben wanted to eat the pear, so he tried to jump and grab it. But the pear was too high up! Ben had an idea. He saw a big rock near the tree. He decided to rush and push the rock under the tree. With all his strength, he pushed and pushed. The rock moved slowly, but finally, it was under the tree. Ben jumped on the rock and reached for the pear. This time, he got it! He took a big bite and found out it was bitter. He made a funny face, but he was still happy. He had worked hard to get the pear, and now he could share it with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball and drink tea. One day, Spot saw a cat with a cup of tea. The cat looked happy. Spot wanted the tea, so he tried to steal it. He ran fast and took the tea from the cat. The cat was sad. But, something unexpected happened. The tea was not tea. It was a magic drink that made Spot small like a cat! Spot was scared and wanted to be big again. He went to the cat and said sorry. The cat forgave Spot and they became friends. The cat knew how to make Spot big again. They found more magic drink and Spot drank it. Spot was big and hairy again. They played together and had real tea. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful cat named Tim. Tim liked to help his friends. One day, Tim saw a little bird shivering in the cold. The bird was sitting on a cord that was hanging from a tree. Tim asked the bird, "Are you cold, birdie?" The little bird replied, "Yes, I am cold. I wish I had something to cover me." Tim thought for a moment and then ran to his house. He found a warm, soft cloth. Tim carried the cloth in his mouth and went back to the bird on the cord. Tim said, "Here, birdie. You can use this cloth to cover you and keep you warm." The little bird was very happy and thanked Tim. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a monkey. He was very scared of the dark. Every night, he would run and find a place to hide and cover himself. He would cover himself with a big blanket so that he would stay safe and warm. One night, while the monkey was walking through the forest, he saw something scary. He saw a giant spider crawling near a tree. He was so frightened that he jumped and ran away. He searched for a place to hide and cover himself. He found a cave and crawled inside. He was safe, but he was still scared. Then he saw a big hole in the wall and he could see outside. He saw the scary spider again, so he quickly covered himself with a big blanket. The monkey stayed in the cave until morning. When the sun rose, the scary spider was gone. The monkey was happy and relieved. He came out from under his blanket and looked around. He was safe and he knew that he could cover himself with a blanket if he ever got scared again.
