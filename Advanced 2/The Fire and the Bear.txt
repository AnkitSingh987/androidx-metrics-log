Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small fire. The fire started to burn. The smoke went up, up, up into the sky. The animals in the forest saw the fire and were scared. A figure, who was a big and strong bear, saw the fire too. He had a clear idea to help the animals. He told the animals, "Do not worry, I will put out the fire." The animals were happy to hear that. The bear went to the river and took water in his big mouth. He went back to the fire and spit the water on it. He did this many times, and the fire went out. The animals were very happy and thanked the bear. From that day on, the bear was a hero in the forest.

Once there was a girl. She was the daughter of two people who worked very hard. Everyday they would leave the house and go to work so they could make money. The daughter usually stayed at home and played with her toys. She had one toy that she loved more than the others. It was a pink bear. She always carried it around with her. One day the daughter's parents had to go away for work. They told her to stay at home, to be good and to look after her pink bear. After a while, the daughter heard a noise coming from outside. She couldn't see anyone, but she thought that someone might be stealing her pink bear. She grabbed her bear, ran outside, but it was too late. The thief had already disappeared. The daughter was sad and she cried. She never saw the thief or her pink bear again. Her parents tried to buy her another one, but it was not the same. She never experienced the same joy as when she had that pink bear.

Once upon a time, there was a horse named Bob. Bob was a mysterious horse. He lived in a big field with lots of grass. Bob had a secret: he could increase his size. When he did this, he grew very big! One day, a little girl named Sue came to play with Bob. Sue loved horses and wanted to ride Bob. But Bob was too big to ride! So, Bob showed Sue his secret. He increased his size and became smaller. Now, Sue could ride him. Together, Sue and Bob had lots of fun. They ran around the field and played with the other horses. Sue loved her mysterious friend, Bob. She would visit him every day, and they would play together. And every time Sue came, Bob would increase his size to be just right for her to ride. They were the best of friends.

Dad was very happy. He wanted to give his three year old daughter a surprise. He said he was going to take her to the park. She was excited and wanted to go right away. On the way there, Daddy said, "Maybe we will see something very scary tonight - but don't worry. I will be right beside you." But his daughter couldn't help being a little scared. Once they arrived at the park, Daddy said, "Look, up in the sky - it's the moon!" Daddy pointed to the big, round moon in the night sky. His daughter was amazed at how bright and beautiful it was. The surprise had worked! Daddy had gone to the park to show his daughter the moon. She was no longer scared - only filled with wonder.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside. One day, he saw a poor bird with a hurt wing. Tom wanted to help the bird. Tom took the bird to his camp. He made a soft bed for the bird to rest. Tom said, "Don't worry, bird. I will take care of you." He used a big leaf to cover the bird and keep it warm. Days went by, and the bird got better. One day, the bird said, "Thank you, Tom, for helping me. I can fly now!" Tom was happy and said, "Goodbye, bird! Have fun flying!" The bird flew away, and Tom went back to his camp.
