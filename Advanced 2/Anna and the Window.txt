Title: Anna and the Window

Anna is a young girl who likes to see new things. She has a network of friends who live in different places. Sometimes, she talks to them on a screen. She can see their faces and hear their voices. One day, Anna sees a big truck outside her house. The truck has a lot of wires and boxes. Anna is curious. She asks her mom, "What is that?" Her mom says, "That is a network truck. They are fixing the wires that help us talk to our friends on the screen. Sometimes, the wires get broken or old, and they need to be replaced." Anna says, "Oh, I see. Can I watch them work?" Her mom says, "Sure, but you have to stay away from the truck and the wires. They can be dangerous. You can watch from the window." Anna nods and runs to the window. She sees the network workers climb up a pole and connect the wires. She sees them use tools and helmets. She thinks they are very brave and smart. She waves to them and smiles. They wave back and smile too. Anna is happy. She likes her network of friends and the network workers. She hopes they can fix the wires soon, so she can talk to her friends on the screen again. She thinks the network is a wonderful thing.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was a happy boy who liked to play with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends decided to go on an adventure. They wanted to find a treasure in the west part of the town. As they walked to the west, they saw a big sign. The sign said, "Do not go this way. It is not safe." But Tim and his friends wanted to find the treasure, so they ignored the sign and kept going. They walked and walked until they found a big hole in the ground. Tim's friends were scared, but Tim wanted the treasure. He jumped into the hole, but it was too deep. Tim could not get out, and his friends could not help him. In the end, Tim was stuck in the hole, and his friends went home sad.

Once upon a time, there lived an icy girl named Lily. She loved to play in the snow and make snowmen. One day, while Lily was playing in the snow, she had an urge to make a snowman that was extra special. She began to strike the snow together to form a big snowman. "Woah," she exclaimed. Just then, her friend Davey came running up to her. He saw the giant snowman and was amazed. "Wow, Lily!" Davey said. "That's the biggest snowman I've ever seen!" Lily smiled proudly. She had a feeling she accomplished something special. Together, they started to make some icy decorations for the snowman. They both worked really hard and soon the snowman was finished. It was so beautiful, and it sparkled in the sunlight. "We did it!" Davey said. Lily grinned. She was so happy with their creation. She knew it was the best snowman she ever made.

Today, Bob went for a ride in the car. His dad was driving and Bob felt very excited. He was looking out of the window and seeing the trees, the sky and the birds in the air. Bob had asked his dad to go for a haircut. As they drove to the barber's shop, Bob kept asking his dad 'Are we nearly there?' But dad said, 'We're almost there.' When they arrived, the barber was very enthusiastic. He smiled and asked Bob what kind of haircut that he would like. Bob said he wanted a big and tall haircut. So the barber trimmed Bob's hair, made it look neat and smart. When Bob was finished, his dad was very proud of him. Bob felt proud too. His dad drove them home and Bob was left with an extra skip in his step.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fat cat named Bobo. Bobo liked to create things with his toys. His favorite toy was a big, red ambulance. He would play with it every day. One day, Bobo saw a little bird fall down from a tree. The bird was hurt and needed help. Bobo remembered his ambulance toy and had an idea. He ran back to his house to get it. Bobo used his ambulance toy to help the little bird. He put the bird on the toy and pushed it all the way to the bird's home. The bird's family was very happy to see Bobo and the little bird. They thanked Bobo for his help. From that day on, Bobo and the birds were the best of friends.
