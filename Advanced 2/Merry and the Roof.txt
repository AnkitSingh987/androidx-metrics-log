She shouted out, "Look at the roof! What a cool shape!" Her mom walked over with a smile, "Yes, it sure is!" Merry couldn't stop looking at it. She asked, "Mommy, can I touch it?" Mommy answered, "I don't think so, sweetheart. The roof is very high up and we don't want you to fall." Merry thought for a moment and said, "It still looks so interesting. Maybe I can shout up to it." She then closed her eyes and shouted as loud as she could, "Hello rooft! Hello rooft!" Her mom laughed and clapped her hands. "You're so gifted, my little one!"

Once upon a time, there was a very messy animal. His burrow was filled with bits of dirt and sticks, and it was always a mess. One day, he decided he wanted to tidy up his burrow and make it nice and neat. He spoke to the animal who lived next door and asked for help. "I need supplies for my burrow," the messy animal said. The animal neighbour said, "I can help! I have lots of supplies that I can supply you with!" The messy animal was so happy and thanked the neighbour animal. With the help of his neighbour, the messy animal was able to tidy up his burrow and make it sparkling clean. The messy animal was so pleased with his clean burrow and thanked his neighbour once again. From then on, the messy animal made sure to keep his burrow nice and clean, as he didn't want it to get messy again!

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to create a gift for his mom. He was excited to make her happy. He found some paper, colors, and glue. Tim started to make a pretty card with a big heart on it. Tim showed his friend, Sue, the card he made. Sue said, "That's a nice gift, but you spelled 'Mom' wrong." Tim looked at the card and saw he wrote 'Mum'. He felt embarrassed. Sue helped Tim fix the mistake on the card. Tim gave the gift to his mom. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim! I love it!" Tim felt happy and proud. He knew that even if he made a mistake, his mom would still love his gift.

Once upon a time, there was a big jug. The jug was full of yummy juice. The yummy juice was red and sweet. The jug lived in a small house with a happy family. One day, the sun was very hot. The family wanted to drink the yummy juice from the jug. But they saw that the jug was open. They did not want bugs to get in the juice. So, they had to cover the jug with a lid. The family was very happy. They could drink the yummy juice from the jug without any bugs. They laughed and played outside. The sun was still hot, but the yummy juice from the jug made them feel cool and happy. And they always remembered to cover the jug.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and make different shapes with them. One day, she saw a big box in her room. She was very curious about what was inside the box. Lily opened the box and found many shapes inside. She was so happy to see them. She started to play with the shapes and made a big mess in her room. Her mom came in and said, "Lily, you made a big mess. You must clean it up or I will have to punish you." Lily didn't want to be punished, so she started to clean up the shapes. As she was cleaning, she found a special shape that she had never seen before. It was a star shape! She showed it to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a star shape!" Her mom was surprised and said, "Wow, Lily! I didn't know we had that shape. You did a good job cleaning up. Let's play with the shapes together." They played with the shapes and had a lot of fun. The star shape was Lily's favorite, and she was happy that she found it while cleaning up.
