Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to visit her Aunt May's house. Aunt May had a big orange cat named Max. Max was very soft and liked to play with Lily. One sunny day, Lily and Aunt May sat under a big tree in the yard. They saw Max chasing butterflies. Lily and Aunt May started to discuss Max's games. They both laughed as Max tried to catch his own tail. Later, they all went inside to eat some yummy snacks. Aunt May made orange juice for Lily and herself. They continued to discuss Max's funny games. Lily, Aunt May, and Max had a very fun day together.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who wanted to go to the museum. He had heard wonderful stories about the museum and was so excited to finally go to this amazing place. On the way to the museum, the little boy stopped to chat with his mom. "Mom, can we spend lots of time at the museum?" he asked. His mom smiled and said, "Of course, honey. We can spend all morning there if you'd like!" The little boy felt so happy hearing this. Finally, the family arrived at the museum. The little boy was surprised by all the amazing things he saw! There were big dinosaurs, colorful paintings, and cool robots. He and his family spent the morning looking around, trying to see as much as possible. They had such a great time that they didn't want to leave. But when it was time to go, the little boy knew he would come back again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a very long garden in her backyard. Lily loved to grow things in her garden. One day, she found a new plant. It was a special herb. In the morning, Lily would wake up early to take care of her herb. She would give it water and talk to it. "Grow big and strong, little herb," she would say. One day, a tiny bug came to visit the herb. The bug said, "Hello, I like your herb. Can I live here too?" Lily thought about it and said, "Yes, you can stay with my herb. But you must help me take care of it." The bug agreed and they became good friends. Every day, they would wake up and take care of the herb together. And the herb grew big and strong, just like Lily wanted.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a small toy navy boat. It was blue and pretty. She was very happy and played with it all day. She would put the boat in the water and watch it float away. Then, she would say "come back, boat!" and the boat would come back to her. She liked to repeat this game many times. Amy was very grateful for her new toy. She loved her navy boat and took it everywhere with her. She played with it in the bath, in the pool, and even in the rain. She liked to show her friends her boat and they would play together. One day, Amy's friend Tom wanted to play with the navy boat too. Amy was happy to share her toy. They played together and took turns making the boat float away and come back. They repeated the game many times and laughed a lot. Amy and Tom were very grateful for their fun day with the navy boat.

One day, a cute little dog named Max went for a walk. He wanted to go up the big hill. Max saw his friend, a small bird named Lily. Lily asked, "Max, do you want to play with me?" Max said, "Yes, but first I want to go up the big hill." Lily said, "Okay, I will go with you." So, Max and Lily went up the big hill together. They walked and walked, and soon they could see the top of the hill. Max was very excited. When they got to the top of the hill, Max and Lily saw a big tree. Max said, "I think this is the end of our walk, Lily. Now we can play!" They played by the big tree all day. Max was happy he went up the big hill with his friend Lily.
