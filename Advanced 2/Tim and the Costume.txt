Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim wanted to dress up in a costume. He asked his mom, "Can I have a costume?" His mom said, "Yes, you can have a costume." Tim was so happy. He put on his costume and went outside to play. He saw his friend, Sue, who was also wearing a costume. They were having so much fun together. But then, something unexpected happened. The sky became dark and it started to rain. Their costumes got wet and spoiled. Tim and Sue were sad. They went back home and told their moms about the spoiled costumes. Their moms said, "Don't worry, we can fix them." They worked together and made the costumes look like new again. Tim and Sue learned that even when something bad happens, good can still come out of it.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted bird who loved to sing. Every day, the bird would sing a beautiful song that made everyone happy. The bird had many friends who loved to listen to her song. One day, the bird's friend, a little cat, got stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and called for help. The gifted bird knew she had to rescue her friend. So, she flew up to the tree and used her strong beak to help the cat climb down. After the rescue, the cat was very thankful to the bird. They were even better friends now. To thank the bird, the cat learned a new song to sing with her. From that day on, they sang their songs together and made everyone in the forest happy.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a pink lion. The pink lion was not like other lions. He was kind and had a big heart. He liked to play with his friends, the little birds and the small rabbits. They loved him very much. One day, the pink lion found a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird had a stitch in its wing. The pink lion wanted to help the bird. He tried to fix the stitch with his big paws. But, his paws were too big and clumsy. The pink lion roared and roared, trying to call for help. But no one came to help them. The little bird was sad, and the pink lion was sad too. They could not play together anymore. The little bird flew away with its hurt wing, and the pink lion was left all alone in the big forest.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little sunflower. The sunflower was very small but wanted to grow big and strong. Every day, the sunflower tried to grow taller and taller. It wanted to have thick, green leaves and a big, yellow face. One day, the sunflower met a kind bee. The bee said, "I can help you increase and grow bigger!" The bee went to find some water and brought it back to the sunflower. The sunflower drank the water and felt stronger. The bee also told the sunflower to stand tall and face the sun. As the days went by, the sunflower listened to the bee's advice. It drank water, faced the sun, and soon, it started to grow. The sunflower's leaves became thick and green, and its face turned big and yellow. The sunflower was very happy and thanked the bee for its help. They became good friends and lived happily in the big garden.

Tommy was so restless. He wanted to do something fun, so he asked his mom. She said he could take out the kite. He was so excited! Tommy ran outside and his mom gave him the kite. He took off the string and then he held it high in the sky. Tommy's mom watched him with a smile and said, "Now you can fly it!" Tommy smiled and he ran around, holding onto the string. The kite was so high! He let it go, and it flew higher and higher. Tommy watched it for a few moments before he felt something tugging on the string. He looked down to see the kite had gotten caught on a branch. He said to his mom, "What can I do?" His mom said, "Well, if you want to get it down, you have to remove it from the branch. Can you do that?" Tommy nodded and carefully pulled the kite off the branch. He was so happy that it was down from the sky. He ran around the yard, holding his kite high in the air.
