Title: Amy the Girl and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her mom's makeup. One day, her mom told her that a new makeup set would arrive soon. Amy was very excited and could not wait. The next day, Amy saw a big box by the door. She knew the new makeup had arrived. She opened the box and found lots of colors. There was also a small bottle with a bitter smell. Amy did not know what it was, but she thought it might be important. Amy played with the makeup all day. She put on red lips, blue eyes, and pink cheeks. Then, she remembered the small bottle. She opened it and put some on her face. Suddenly, her face felt funny. Amy called her mom, who said the bitter bottle was for cleaning, not for playing. They both laughed and cleaned Amy's face. From that day on, Amy was more careful with her makeup.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy a pool for their backyard. Tim saw a big, expensive pool and said, "Mom, I want that one!" But his mom said, "No, Tim. That pool is too expensive. Let's find another one." Tim and his mom looked at many pools. They saw small pools, big pools, and round pools. Tim could not decide which pool he wanted. He looked at his mom and asked, "Mom, can you help me choose a pool?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. Let's pick a pool that is not too expensive and just the right size for our backyard." They found a nice pool that was not too big or too small. It was also not too expensive. Tim was very happy with the pool they picked. When they got home, Tim and his mom set up the new pool in their backyard. Tim was so excited to play in the water. He put on his swimsuit and jumped into the pool. Tim had a great day playing in his new pool and was glad they found the perfect one.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to play outside with her friends. One day, Sue found an unusual pebble. It was round and shiny. She showed it to her friends and they all thought it was very pretty. Sue wondered what the pebble might taste like. She put it in her mouth and gave it a little lick. "Yuck!" she said. The pebble did not taste good at all. It tasted like dirt and made her mouth feel funny. Sue's friends laughed and told her not to taste pebbles. Sue learned that day that even if something looks interesting, it doesn't mean it will taste good. She decided to keep the unusual pebble as a special treasure and never put it in her mouth again. Sue and her friends went back to playing, happy to have a new story to tell.

One day, Toby was sad because outside it was raining. He asked his mom what they could do and she told him that they could go to the garden to pick carrots. Toby was delighted and they put on their coats, grabbed an umbrella and headed out. When they arrived at the garden, Toby was so excited to find lots of delicious carrots. He couldn't believe how many were there! He picked up one and said, "Mom, look how yummy it is!" His mom agreed, laughing "Yes, Toby, this is a really delicious carrot!" As they went home, stopping in the rain to pick up more carrots, Toby had a feeling that something special was about to happen. When they got back home, his mom said there was one last surprise for him. She put the carrots in a pot and cooked them with some other ingredients. The smell was so amazing, Toby couldn't wait to see what it was. When the food was ready, his mom served him a big bowl of carrot soup. He was so happy and couldn't believe it - rain had turned into the most delicious carrot soup!

Once upon a time, there was a pretty crab. He lived near the sea. He had many friends. They played together every day. One day, the pretty crab started to worry. He thought his friends did not like him. He felt sad. He did not play with them anymore. The pretty crab's friends missed him. They looked for him. They found him hiding. They tried to make him happy. But the pretty crab did not listen. He stayed sad and alone. In the end, the pretty crab's friends stopped trying. They went away. The pretty crab was all alone. He was very sad. The moral of the story is: Do not worry too much about what others think. It can make you lose your friends.
