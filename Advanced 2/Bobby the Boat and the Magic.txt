Once upon a time, in a small harbor, there lived a little boat named Bobby. Bobby was a happy boat, but sometimes he felt embarrassed because he was smaller than the other boats. One sunny day, Bobby saw a big boat coming into the harbor. The big boat looked strong and fast. Bobby wanted to be friends with the big boat, but he was scared. He thought the big boat might not like him because he was so small. Bobby decided to do a magic trick to impress the big boat. He thought if he could vanish, the big boat would want to be his friend. So Bobby closed his eyes and wished very hard to vanish. When he opened his eyes, he was still there. Bobby felt sad and even more embarrassed. But then, the big boat saw Bobby and came over to say hello. The big boat didn't care that Bobby was small. They became friends and played together in the harbor every day. Bobby learned that he didn't need to vanish to make friends. All he had to do was be himself.

Once upon a time, a pale cat named Tim went on a tour. Tim liked to hide and play all day. He went to the park, the zoo, and the beach. Tim had so much fun on his tour. One day, Tim went to hide in a big box. He thought it would be a great place to play. But when he jumped in, something unexpected happened. The box started to move! Tim was scared but also excited. The box took Tim to a new place. It was a land full of big, yummy treats and fun toys to play with. Tim was so happy. He found out that the box was a magic box. Now, Tim could go on tours to new places whenever he wanted. And he always had the best hiding spots.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, Spot was very naughty. He took the ball and ran away from his home. Spot kept running and running. He did not look where he was going. His head bumped into a big tree. "Ouch!" yelped Spot. He felt sad and wanted to go back home. Spot walked and walked until he found his home. His family was so happy to see him. They gave Spot a big hug. From that day on, Spot promised to never be naughty again and always stay close to his family.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little crab. He lived near the sea and loved to play all day. The crab had many friends who lived with him on the beach. One day, the crab found a new friend. The new friend was a bird who liked to clap. The bird clapped his wings and made a fun sound. The crab thought it was funny and wanted to clap too. The crab and the bird played together every day. They clapped and danced on the beach. The curious crab was very happy to have a new friend who could clap. They were the best of friends and had many fun days together.

One day, a little bunny named Tim was very hungry. He went to the garden to find a carrot. Tim saw a big, orange carrot and wanted to eat it. But the carrot was very popular with all the animals. A big bird named Sam saw Tim and said, "Tim, can you promise to share the carrot with me? I am hungry too." Tim thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I promise to share the carrot with you. We can be friends." Tim and Sam tried to pull the carrot out of the ground. But they were not strong enough. More animals came to help. They all wanted a piece of the popular carrot. They all promised to share the carrot. Finally, they pulled the carrot out of the ground. But there were too many animals, and the carrot broke into tiny pieces. No one got enough to eat. All the animals were still hungry and sad. They wished they had found more carrots to share.
