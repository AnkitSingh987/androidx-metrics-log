One day, a little dog named Spot wanted to go for a jog. He looked up at the wide sky and saw big, white clouds. He was very happy. Spot asked his friend, the cat named Whiskers, "Do you want to jog with me?" Whiskers looked at the sky too. He saw dark clouds far away. Whiskers said, "Yes, but we must be back before the rain comes." So, Spot and Whiskers jogged together. They jogged through the park and by the pond. They saw ducks, frogs, and other friends. They jogged and jogged, having lots of fun. But then, the sky got darker. The wind started to blow. Spot and Whiskers remembered the dark clouds they saw before. They knew it was time to go home. They jogged back home as fast as they could. Just as they got inside, the rain started to fall. Spot and Whiskers were happy they made it back in time. They looked out the window and said, "We had a fun jog today. Let's do it again soon!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Primrose. She lay in the sun, happily playing with a ball of yarn. A mysterious figure approached her, wearing a long cloak and a tall hat. “Good afternoon,” said the figure. Primrose was afraid and stayed very still. “Who… who are you?” she asked. The figure smiled and said, “I am your guardian angel. Do you know what that means?” Primrose shook her head. “No…” “A guardian angel is someone that looks out for you,” the figure explained. “Whenever you are in danger, I will be there to help.” Primrose was comforted, and she thanked the figure for watching over her. She asked why the figure was so mysterious. The figure chuckled, “A guardian angel has to be mysterious to their charge, because then you never know when they might appear.” After the angel departed, Primrose lay in the sun and thought about the conversation. She felt surprised, and also grateful that she had someone looking after her. The moral of the story is that everyone has a guardian angel looking out for them, even if they can't always see it. It's important to remember to stay safe, and always have faith that someone is looking out for you

One day, a big dog named Max was very tired. He wanted to take a nap. He found a great spot under a big tree. Max was so happy to rest there. He closed his eyes and fell asleep. While Max was napping, a small cat named Kitty walked by. She saw Max and thought he was in jail! Kitty wanted to help Max get out. She had a plan to save him. Kitty found a big stick and tried to open the jail. But, the stick hit Max's nose and woke him up! Max saw Kitty and was not mad. He told her, "I am not in jail, I was just taking a nap!" They both laughed and became good friends. From that day on, they napped together under the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Sam. He was three years old and loved to stay with his mom and dad. One day, Sam asked his mom and dad if he could have a lemon. His mom and dad smiled and took him to the store. They went to a modern store and saw lots of different kinds of lemons! Sam picked up a big yellow lemon. His mom said, "Good choice! Let's bring it home and make some lemonade." Sam stayed for a few minutes and then hopped into the car with his mom and dad. Back at home, Sam and his parents made a tasty lemonade with their lemon. They all enjoyed a drink together in the backyard. From that day on, Sam always wanted a lemon!

Once upon a time, there was a warm little house where a goose lived. The goose had a friend named Tim. They liked to play together in the yard. One day, they found a big red ball. "Let's play with the ball," said Tim. The goose agreed, and they started to play. They would squeeze the ball and watch it bounce. They laughed and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball bounced too high and got stuck in a tree. Tim and the goose looked up, worried. They didn't know how to get the ball down. Tim had an idea. He asked the goose to help him climb the tree. The goose used its strong wings to lift Tim up. "Thank you, goose!" said Tim, as he reached the ball. The goose smiled, happy to help. They played with the ball again and had even more fun than before. And from that day on, they knew they could always count on each other.
