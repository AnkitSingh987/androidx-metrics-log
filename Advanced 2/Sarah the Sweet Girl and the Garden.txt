Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Sarah. She liked to peek around her garden to see what she could find. One day, Sarah was peeking around her garden when she suddenly saw something sparkly. It was a beautiful fairy! She was wearing a pretty pink dress and twirling her wand in her hand. Sarah was so excited that she wanted to talk to the fairy. But the fairy had a very serious look on her face and Sarah knew she must have been busy. So Sarah quietly walked over to her and waited patiently. Finally, the fairy noticed Sarah and asked her why she was there. Sarah smiled and said that she just wanted to know if the fairy was okay. The fairy smiled back and said she was happy to have Sarah watching over her. Sarah and the fairy talked for a while and became good friends. They still meet up in the garden every day to have fun and play. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a magic box. One day, she opened the box and something important appeared. It was a dress with pretty colors. Lily was so happy. She wanted to wear the dress to show her friends. Lily put on the dress and went outside to play. She saw her friends and they liked her new clothes. They played and had fun all day. When it was time to go home, Lily saw that her dress was dirty. She was sad and worried. Lily went back to her magic box and put the dress inside. She closed her eyes and wished for the dress to be clean again. When she opened the box, her dress was not there. Instead, there were new clothes. Lily learned that her magic box could make new clothes appear, but only when she needed them the most.

One day, a girl named Lily went to school. Her teacher, Miss Jane, had a big, clean blackboard in the front of the room. Lily loved to watch Miss Jane write on the blackboard with her white chalk. Today, Miss Jane had a big box in the room. "What is in the box?" asked Lily. Miss Jane said, "Let's unpack the box and see!" They opened the box and found many toys inside. There were cars, dolls, and blocks. Lily and her friends were so happy! They played with the toys all day. At the end of the day, Lily helped Miss Jane put the toys back in the box. They both said, "We had a fun day!" Miss Jane gave Lily a big hug, and Lily went home with a big smile on her face.

One day, a big helicopter came to a small town. The helicopter was very modern. It had many buttons and lights. The people in the town were excited to see it. A man named Tom wanted to teach the people how to use the helicopter. But some people did not want to learn. They were scared of the big, modern helicopter. This made Tom sad. Tom thought of a plan. He asked the children to help him. The children loved the helicopter and wanted to learn. They showed their parents how fun it was. Soon, everyone in the town wanted to learn. Tom was happy to teach them all.

Once upon a time, a big, round ball lived in an empty box. The ball was sad because it wanted to play with friends. One day, a small, round ball came to the box. The big ball was happy and said, "Let's play!" The two round balls played and had fun together. They jumped and rolled around the empty box. They were very happy to have each other. The big ball said, "I want to fill the box with more friends." The big ball went to find more friends to fill the box. It found a very big, heavy rock. The big ball said, "Come play with us in the box!" The rock agreed and followed the big ball to the empty box. The rock jumped into the box and tried to play with the round balls. But the rock was too heavy and squished the small round ball. The big ball was very sad and cried. The box was not a fun place anymore.
