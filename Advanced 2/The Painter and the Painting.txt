Once upon a time, there was a painter who loved to paint. He was very eager to show his friends his new painting. He called his friend, the rabbit, to come and see. "Rabbit, come look at my painting!" said the painter. Rabbit hopped over and saw the painting. "Wow, I enjoy your painting," said Rabbit. The painter was very happy. But then, a big wind came and blew the painting away. The painter and Rabbit tried to catch it, but they couldn't. The painting was lost, and they were sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a famous pasta maker. Every day, he would wake up early to make the freshest and yummiest pasta in the village. He was very proud of his craft and he loved when people would tell him how delicious his pasta was. One day, the pasta maker heard a voice outside his cottage. It was a young boy who was asking for some free pasta. The pasta maker, who was scared of being taken advantage of, kindly told the boy that he couldn't give him any pasta. The little boy was very sad and began to cry. The pasta maker felt bad for the boy and decided to give him a small bowl of pasta for free. He said, "Here, take this bowl of pasta my friend. I make it with love, so try to enjoy it." The little boy was so grateful and smiled with delight as he tucked into the delicious pasta. The famous pasta maker had saved the day! Everyone in the village was so proud of him and they all promised to tell stories of his kind heart to all of the other children.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom lived in a big office with many people. He liked to play and sleep all day long. He had many friends in the office, and they loved him too. One day, Tom saw a big ball on the floor. He thought it was a toy and wanted to play with it. So, he ran fast and hit the ball with his paw. "Oh no!" said a man in the office. "Tom, you break the ball!" The ball was not a toy, but a very important thing for the office. Tom felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know. I thought it was a toy." The people in the office were not angry with Tom. They knew it was an accident. Then, something unexpected happened. Inside the broken ball, they found a treasure map! The people in the office were very excited. They decided to find the treasure together with Tom. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved the ocean. She would play in the water and build sand castles. One day, Mia found a pretty shell. The shell was shiny and bright. Mia hoped to find more shells like it. Mia's friend, Tom, wanted to help. Tom found a shell, too. But his shell was fake. It was made of plastic. Mia and Tom did not like the fake shell. They wanted real shells from the ocean. Mia and Tom looked for more shells. They found many real shells. They were happy. They put the real shells in a big bucket. Mia and Tom hoped to come back to the ocean soon. They loved the ocean and the real shells they found.

Once upon a time, there was a little rat. The rat had a very dirty body. He wanted to bathe to be clean again. The rat looked for a place where he could find water. The rat found a small pond. The water in the pond was icy. The rat was scared to bathe in the icy water. But he wanted to be clean, so he jumped in. The rat splashed and played in the icy water. He felt cold, but he was happy to be clean. After the rat was done, he ran back to his warm home. The rat felt good and clean, and he was happy that he had found the icy pond to bathe in.
