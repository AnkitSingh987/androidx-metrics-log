Once upon a time, there was a little gray shrimp. The shrimp lived in the sea with his friends. They liked to play and swim all day. One day, the shrimp and his friends met a big fish. The big fish said, "I have a secret. Do you want to know?" The little shrimp and his friends nodded. They were very excited. The big fish took them to a hidden place. The big fish moved a big rock to reveal a beautiful, shiny treasure. The little shrimp and his friends were surprised and happy. The big fish said, "We can all share this treasure and be friends forever." And they all played and swam happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a kind dog named Max. Max lived in a little house with a big fireplace. He loved to sit by the fireplace and feel warm. One day, Max felt very tired. He wanted to take a nap by the fireplace. A little bird named Lucy flew into Max's house. Lucy was cold and shivering. She saw Max by the fireplace and asked, "Can you lend me some warmth, please?" Max smiled and said, "Of course, come sit by the fireplace with me!" Lucy was very happy and they both sat by the fireplace together. As they sat by the fireplace, Max started to fall asleep. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. Max woke up and saw a big cat outside. The cat was trying to get inside the house to catch Lucy! Max quickly jumped up and closed the door. The cat went away, and Lucy was safe. Lucy thanked Max for saving her. The moral of the story is to always help others, even when you are tired.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big hammer. He thought it was impressive. Tim wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. "Look, Sam!" Tim said. "I found this big hammer. It's so cool!" Sam looked at the hammer and thought it was impressive too. They decided to use the hammer to print pictures on paper. They put paint on the hammer and hit the paper with it. The paint made fun shapes on the paper. Tim and Sam showed their printed pictures to their moms. Their moms were very happy and proud. They hung the pictures on the wall. Tim and Sam felt good about their impressive hammer art.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He was going to play a game with his friends. They wanted to win and feel victory. Tim's mom tried to explain how to play the game. They felt victory and were so excited. They all cheered and hugged. Tim thanked his mom for explaining how to play the game. They all went home happy.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run all day. One day, Spot went into a big, dark house. In the house, he met a ghost. The ghost was sad and lonely. Spot said to the ghost, "Why are you sad?" The ghost said, "I have no friends. No one wants to play with me." Spot felt bad for the ghost and wanted to help him gain friends. Spot and the ghost played together. They had so much fun. Soon, other kids saw them playing and joined in. The ghost was happy to have friends. Spot was happy too, because he helped the ghost. They all played together every day, and the ghost was never lonely again.

One day, a popular cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a hole in the ground. Tom was very curious about the hole. He wanted to know what was inside. Tom called his friend, a dog named Max. "Max, come look at this hole!" Max came running to see the hole. They both decided to stir the dirt around the hole. They wanted to make the hole bigger so they could see inside. As they stirred the dirt, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly bear popped out of the hole! The bear said, "Hello, Tom and Max! Thank you for making my hole bigger. Now I can come out and play with you!" Tom, Max, and the bear became good friends and played together every day.
