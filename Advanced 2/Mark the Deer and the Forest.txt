Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a deer named Mark. Mark had a big horn on his head. He was very fast and alert. He had many friends in the forest. One day, Mark and his friends were playing near the river. They saw a small, lost bunny. The bunny was very scared. Mark and his friends decided to help the bunny find its way home. Mark used his big horn to make marks on the ground. This way, they could find their way back. They walked and walked until they found the bunny's home. The bunny's family was very happy to see them. They thanked Mark and his friends for their help. Mark and his friends learned that it is good to help others. They felt happy and proud. From that day on, they always tried to help anyone in need. And the forest became a better place for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a big family. They were very generous. They liked to share toys and food with each other. One day, they decided to go on a vacation. They wanted to see new places and have fun together. On the vacation, the family went to a beach. They played in the sand and swam in the sea. The kids made sand castles and laughed a lot. The sun was shining and the water was warm. They were very happy. But then, the kids wanted to play separate games. One kid said, "I want to play catch." The other kid said, "I want to build more sand castles." So, the family decided to let the kids play their own games. They knew that it was okay to be separate sometimes. They still loved each other very much. The family had a great vacation and made many happy memories together.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Sally. She lived in a small town and had no friends. She was lonely and wanted someone to play with. One day, Sally was walking down the street when she noticed a kitty by her feet! She reached out her hand and the kitty let her pick it up. Sally was so happy and held the kitty in her arms. The kitty looked at Sally with a big wink and then it jumped away. Sally had gained a new friend! She smiled and took the kitty home. She was so excited to have a new friend. The kitty stayed with Sally and they played together every day. They were so happy and even kissed each other good night every night. After a few weeks, the kitty moved away, but Sally was always happy because she had gained a friend after all. Now Sally has a normal life. She plays with her friends and the kitty is still her best friend. Whenever Sally and the kitty meet, the kitty gives a wink, telling Sally that it still loves her. They have a very special friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a unique cat. This cat was very big and had a lot of fur. Everyone in the town liked the cat. They would always say "welcome" when the cat came to play. One day, the cat went to a little girl's house. The girl liked to rub the cat's fur. She thought it was very soft. The cat liked it too. They played together all day long. But then, something bad happened. The cat got sick. The girl tried to help the cat, but she could not. The cat was very sad. The girl was sad too. The cat had to go away and never came back.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent dog named Max. Max had a jeep. One day, Max wanted to go on a trip. So, Max said, "I need to pack my things." Max put his toys, food, and bed in the jeep. Then, he saw his friend, a little bird named Tim. Tim said, "I want to come with you, Max!" Max was happy to have a friend on his trip. As they drove, Max and Tim saw a big box on the road. "What's that?" asked Tim. Max stopped the jeep and they looked inside. They found a treasure chest full of treats! Max and Tim were so happy. They shared the treats and had a great trip together.
