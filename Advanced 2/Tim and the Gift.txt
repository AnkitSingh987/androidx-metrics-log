Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to visit his grandpa. His grandpa lived in a big house with a red motor. Tim loved his grandpa's motor very much. He was always excited to see it. One day, Tim's grandpa said, "Tim, I want to give you a special gift. I will let you ride in my red motor." Tim was so happy! He said, "Thank you, grandpa! I am grateful for your gift." Grandpa and Tim went for a ride in the red motor. They went up and down hills and through the town. They saw many people and animals. Tim was very happy and grateful for his grandpa's gift. They had a fun day together.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a wide bottle. It was a very pretty bottle. Tim wanted to open it, but the top was stuck. He tried to pull it, but it would not open. Tim was sad. Tim asked his friend, Sally, to help him. Sally was strong. She tried to pull the top off the wide bottle, but it did not open. Sally and Tim were both sad. They wanted to see what was inside the bottle. Then, they had an idea. They asked their big dog, Max, to help them. Max used his strong teeth and pulled the top off the wide bottle. Inside, they found a beautiful toy. Tim, Sally, and Max were all happy. They played with the toy all day.

Once upon a time, there was a fancy otter. All day it liked to scatter around and have fun. One day, it thought of something fancy - to find something to scatter. So, the otter started looking around. Everywhere it looked, there was nothing that it wanted to scatter. It started feeling sad, until it stumbled upon an old box tucked away in the corner of the woods. Excited, the otter opened the box and found all kinds of shiny things inside. The otter scattered all the shiny things around the forest. It had lots of fun, but it eventually decided it was time to go home. But as soon as it stepped away, the otter realised it had forgotten something. It had left the box of shiny things. The otter was pleased to find the next day that the box of shiny things hadn't gone anywhere. And from then on it would scatter the shiny things around the woods, bringing lots of fun to the forest.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a lot of fun playing on the swings and slides. Tim was very happy. After playing, they went to the ice-cream shop. Tim's mom bought him a big, fine ice-cream cone. But when they walked outside, Tim tripped and dropped his ice-cream on the ground. He was very sad and started to cry. His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, I forgive you." She bought him another ice-cream cone and they sat on a bench, enjoying the sweet treat together. Tim felt much better and was happy again.

One day, Bill and Jane were in the park. Bill was so excited because he was going to skip with his dad. He saw his dad walking toward him with a nut in his hand. "Look Jane," said Bill, "Dad brought a nut. I'm going to try and skip it!" Bill's dad smiled and handed him the nut. Bill carefully picked it up and said, "Okay, here I go!" He threw the nut with all his strength and started to skip around it. He skipped over it then around it then back over it again. Jane was clapping and cheering Bill on every time he skipped it. Bill's dad smiled as he watched him. "Good job Bill!" he said. Bill was so excited he kept skipping around the nut until it was time to go home. As they walked away, Bill and Jane were talking about how much fun they had.

Mum and Bob were in the kitchen. Bob was only three years old. He asked: "Mum, what are you doing?" Mum answered: "I am making some powder to eat." Bob was surprised. He had never seen powder like this before. He asked: "What kind of powder?" Mum said: "This is a modern powder. It’s going to be really delicious!" Bob smiled. He wanted to see the powder. He asked: "Can I try it?" Mum said: "Yes, you can try some. Come here and taste it." Bob tasted the powder. It was so yummy! He said: "Yum, this is great!" Mum smiled and said: "Yes, I’m glad you like it. Now, let’s eat!" So Bob and Mum ate the modern powder. They both enjoyed it very much!
