Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Ella. Ella had a long trunk. She lived in a modern house with her friend, a little bird named Billy. One day, Billy liked to tease Ella. He flew around her trunk and said, "Catch me if you can!" Ella laughed and tried to catch Billy with her trunk, but he was too fast. Ella said, "Billy, you are very fast, but I will catch you one day!" Billy laughed and said, "We will see, Ella!" They played and had fun all day in their modern house. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a dream. In his dream, he saw a big, shiny motorcycle. He wanted to ride it, but it looked very silly. The motorcycle had a big smile and funny eyes. Tim asked the motorcycle, "Can I ride you?" The motorcycle said, "Yes, but only if you can catch me!" The motorcycle started to go very fast. Tim ran and ran, trying to catch it. He was getting tired, but he did not want to give up. Finally, Tim caught the silly motorcycle. He climbed on and they went for a fun ride together. They laughed and played all day. Tim was very happy that he did not give up. When he woke up, he told his mom and dad about his silly dream. They all laughed and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had long hair. She liked to play outside. One day, she went to the park. At the park, she saw a swing. Sue loved to swing. Sue sat on the swing and began to move. She went up and down, up and down. She swung high in the sky. Sue felt happy. She swung for a long time. Then, Sue got hungry. Sue went home to her mom. Her mom was in the kitchen. She saw a stove. Sue's mom made food on the stove. They ate together. Sue told her mom about the swing. They both smiled. Sue had a great day.

One day, there was a cube that was very normal. It was nice and smooth and had no sharp parts. But then, a curious three-year-old came along. She was determined to make the cube look different, so she picked up a sharp pencil. She took the pencil and started to make one side sharper. She kept going until the cube was nice and pointy on one side. She looked at her work and smiled. Then she put the cube down, hopped off, and never looked back.

Lilly was a three-year-old girl who loved animals. One morning she woke up feeling excited. She had dreamed of a modern ostrich! She bounced out of bed and asked her mom if they could go to the zoo. Her mom smiled, and said, "Yes, Lilly, let's go find the modern ostrich!" So, off they went to the zoo. When they arrived, Lilly saw the ostrich, and she was amazed. The ostrich had big, beautiful wings and long, strong legs. She was tall and modern looking. Lilly wanted to get closer, so she asked a zoo keeper for help. The zoo keeper laughed and said, "Yes, my dear. Let's go say hello to the modern ostrich." And Lilly did. She got to pet the ostrich and give it a big hug. The ostrich felt so soft and gentle. Lilly was so happy she couldn't stop smiling. She thanked the zoo keeper, and her mom, and waved goodbye to the modern ostrich as they left the zoo. From then on, Lilly always dreamed of visiting the zoo again and saying hello to the modern ostrich.

Once upon a time, there was a big engine named Tom. Tom was a compassionate engine who loved to help his friends. He lived in a town with many other engines, big and small. One day, Tom saw his friend, a little engine named Tim, trying to raise a heavy box. Tim was struggling and looked very sad. Tom wanted to help, so he went to Tim and said, "Let me help you raise the box, Tim." Tim was happy that Tom wanted to help him. Together, they raised the heavy box and put it in its place. Tim said, "Thank you, Tom. You are a very compassionate engine." From that day on, Tom and Tim became best friends. They always helped each other and played together. And they lived happily ever after.
