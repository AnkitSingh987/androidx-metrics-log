Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play outside. One day, he found a heavy hose in the garden. Tim wanted to play with the hose, but it was too heavy for him to carry. He asked his friend, Sam, to help him. "Sam, can you help me carry this heavy hose?" Tim said. Sam agreed to help Tim. Together, they carried the heavy hose. But, they started to argue about who got to hold the hose first. Tim said, "I found it, so I should hold it first!" Sam said, "But I am helping you, so I should hold it first!" They both wanted to play with the hose, but they couldn't agree. At last, they decided to share the hose and take turns. They both got to play and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to organize her toys. She had a big toy box filled with all her favorite things. One day, Amy found a drum in the toy box. It was a brilliant red color. Amy picked up the drum and started to play. She tapped it softly at first, then louder and louder. Her friends heard the drum and came running to see what was going on. They all wanted to play with the brilliant drum too. Amy and her friends took turns playing the drum. They organized a small parade and marched around the room. They were so happy playing with the brilliant drum, and they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, in a cold place, there was a penguin named Pippin. Pippin was a compassionate penguin who liked to help others. He lived with his family and friends in a big group. One day, Pippin saw a small baby penguin who was lost. The baby penguin cried, "I can't find my mom!" Pippin wanted to help, so he said, "Don't worry, I will help you find your mom." Pippin and the baby penguin walked and walked. They asked other penguins if they knew where the baby's mom was. Finally, they found the baby's mom. She was so happy to see her baby. She said, "Thank you, Pippin, for helping my baby." After that day, Pippin was known as a helper. Penguins would order him to help them, and he always did. Pippin was a true friend to everyone in his group.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy monkey named Momo. Momo loved to roll and play in the jungle. One day, Momo found a big, yellow banana. Momo said, "Wow, a banana! I want to eat it!" But when Momo tried to pick up the banana, he was too clumsy. He slipped and rolled down the hill. As Momo rolled, he saw his friend, Toto the turtle. Toto said, "Momo, why are you rolling?" Momo said, "I found a banana, but I am too clumsy to pick it up." Toto smiled and said, "Let me help you." Toto picked up the banana and gave it to Momo. They both sat down and shared the yummy banana. Momo was happy to have a good friend like Toto.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big sea. He liked to play with his friends near the shore. One day, Fin saw a new friend. This new friend was big and had a shell. Fin said, "Welcome, new friend! Let's play!" The big friend with the shell was not easy to play with. It was difficult for Fin to swim fast with the big friend. Fin was sad. He wanted to play with the big friend, but it was too hard. Then, something unexpected happened. The big friend with the shell opened up and said, "Hi, I'm Shelly! I can play too!" Shelly was not just one big friend, but two friends inside a shell! Shelly was really two little friends named Shell and Lee. Fin was so happy. Now, they could all play together near the shore. They swam and played all day long. And they all became the best of friends.
