Once upon a time there was a girl who loved music. She wanted to learn how to play the violin, so she asked her mummy and daddy if she could have one. They said yes and soon the girl had a new violin of her own. Every day the girl practiced playing the violin. She tried to be as fast as she could so she could be the best she could be. One day Mama and Daddy had a surprise. "We have something special to tell you," Mama said. "We are getting married!" The girl was excited for Mama and Daddy, but she was worried about the violin. "Will I still have time to practice now that you are getting married?" she asked. Mama and Daddy looked at each other. "Of course!" they both said together. The girl smiled and hugged her parents. Now she had even more of a reason to practice even faster!

Once there was a girl named Lily. She had a dress that she loved very much. It was an original dress with pretty colors. One day, she wanted to wear the dress to a party. But her little brother, Tom, took the dress and hid it. Lily was sad and asked Tom, "Where is my dress? I want to wear it to the party." Tom just smiled and did not say anything. Lily looked everywhere for her dress. She was very upset. Then, Tom saw how sad Lily was and felt bad. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I hid your dress. Here it is." Lily was happy to have her dress back. At the party, everyone said her dress was beautiful. Lily learned to forgive her brother, and Tom learned not to take things that were not his. They both learned that love and being kind is more important than a dress.

One day, Tom and Sue went to play outside. They saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The sun was in the east and the sky was clear. Tom said, "Let's climb the tree and see what we can find!" As they climbed the tree, they found a big nest with pretty eggs. They were excited and wanted to see what was inside. Sue said, "We should wait and see what comes out!" They sat in the tree and waited. After a while, they heard a sound. The eggs began to crack! But instead of baby birds, out came baby dragons! Tom and Sue were very surprised. They became friends with the baby dragons and played all day.

Once there was a girl who loved lemons! Everywhere she went, she would collect lemons to take home with her. One day she had a lot of black lemons and was very excited. She took them home and decided to fix them. First, she took one of the black lemons and cut it into thin slices. Then, she put the slices in a bowl and added a little water. Next, she stirred it until it was nicely mixed. Finally, she tasted it. The lemonade was delicious! She couldn't believe it was so easy to make. She was so happy, she ran to tell her mom the good news. Her mom was thrilled and they both sat down to enjoy the lemonade. It was so tasty and refreshing. From that day on, the girl always fixed lemonade when she had black lemons.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to paint. She would paint all day with her paint and brush. She would paint the sun, the trees, and her toys. Lily was very persistent. She would not stop until her painting was perfect. One day, Lily's mom said they needed to buy more paint. Lily was very excited. They went to the store and looked at all the different colors. Lily picked her favorite colors: red, blue, yellow, and green. When they got home, Lily started to paint right away. She painted a big, bright rainbow. Lily was so happy with her new paint. And she was proud to be persistent, because her rainbow was the most beautiful one she had ever painted.

Anna and Ben were friends who liked to play games. Sometimes they played with cards, sometimes they played with blocks, and sometimes they played with dolls. They had fun together, but they also wanted to win. When one of them won, they would say "I have victory!" and the other one would feel sad. One day, they met a new girl in the park. Her name was Cara, and she had a big smile. She asked Anna and Ben if she could play with them. They said yes, and they decided to play with cards. They taught Cara how to play, and they started the game. Cara was very good at playing cards. She knew how to match the colors and the numbers, and she had a lot of luck. She won the first round, and the second round, and the third round. She said "I have victory!" and she was happy. But Anna and Ben were not happy. They felt angry and jealous. They did not want to play with Cara anymore. They wanted to win too. They started to say mean things to Cara. They said she was cheating, and she was not a good friend, and she should go away. Cara felt hurt and confused. She did not cheat, and she wanted to be a good friend, and she did not want to go away. She liked playing with Anna and Ben, and she wanted them to like her too. She tried to be calm and nice. She said "I'm sorry, I did not mean to make you feel bad. I just like playing cards. I don't care about victory. I care about having fun. Can we still be friends?" She looked at Anna and Ben with her big eyes, and she hoped they would say yes. Anna and Ben felt ashamed. They realized they were being rude and selfish. They did not want to lose Cara as a friend. They said "We're sorry, too. We were not nice to you. You did not do anything wrong. You are a good friend. We want to have fun with you. Can you forgive us?" They looked at Cara with their small smiles, and they hoped she would say yes. Cara was happy to hear their words. She said "Of course I can forgive you. You are my friends. I'm glad we can play together. Can I introduce you to my other games? I have blocks and dolls, too. Maybe you will like them." She took out her toys from her bag, and she showed them to Anna and Ben. Anna and Ben were curious and excited. They said "Yes, please. We would love to see your other games. Maybe we can learn something new. Maybe we can have more fun. Maybe we can share victory." They took Cara's hand, and they followed her to a shady spot under a tree. They played with blocks and dolls, and they had a lot of fun. They learned how to build and pretend, and they helped each other. They did not care who won or lost. They cared about being friends. They were calm and happy. The moral of the story is: Winning is not everything. Friendship is more important. Be kind and respectful to others, and they will be kind and respectful to you. Share and enjoy what you have, and you will have more.
