One day, a fat cat saw a box. The cat liked the box. The cat wanted to sit in the box. But the box was too small for the fat cat. The cat was sad. A little dog saw the fat cat. The dog wanted to help the cat. The dog had an idea. The dog pushed the box. The box moved and hit a wall. The box broke. Now, the box was big. The fat cat could sit in the box. The cat was happy. The cat said, "Thank you, dog." The dog was happy too. They became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little candle who lived in a clean house. The candle loved to shine and make the room bright at night. But the candle was sad because it was too small and its light was not strong enough. One day, a big candle saw the little candle and said, "Don't be sad, little candle. I will encourage you to shine brighter." The little candle was happy to have a friend who believed in it. Together, the big candle and the little candle practiced shining their lights. Every day, the little candle's light grew stronger and brighter. Soon, the little candle was able to light up the whole room. The big candle was proud of the little candle, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl. Her name was Anna and she was three years old. Anna had two friends, Sam and Ben. They all lived in a big, happy house. One day, Sam and Ben wanted to show Anna something special. They had found a turn that could spin around very fast and make lots of colours. Anna was very excited! She wanted the turn all to herself. Sam and Ben tried to show her how to play with the turn together. But Anna did not want to share. She liked the turn so much that she became jealous. Ben and Sam felt sad. They wanted Anna to know that it was important to value things that other people have too. The friends agreed that when they all played with the turn, they would take turns. Anna understood what they meant and was happy that she could share the turn with them. The friends were so glad to be together, sharing the turn and playing together. They had learnt a valuable lesson - that it is important to share and value what we have together.

A little girl named Anna was playing in the park one day. Suddenly, she felt a pinch on her arm. She quickly looked to see where it came from and saw a big dog nearby. Anna scrunched up her face and said, "No biting!" The dog just sat there and smiled at her. Suddenly, Anna heard her mother calling from the other side of the park. Anna ran over to her mother and noticed she was holding a thermometer. Her mother said, "Anna, I need you to hold the thermometer for me." Anna nodded her head and grabbed the thermometer. Suddenly, the big dog came running through the park, heading right for Anna. Anna felt scared and tried to back away, but the dog got closer and closer. "No!" yelled Anna. She quickly grabbed her thermometer and held it up in front of her. The dog stopped in his tracks and looked modestly away. Anna's mother smiled and said, "Good thing you had that thermometer to protect you." Anna agreed, and she and her mother laughed and hugged. Anna learned that day that it pays to be careful and well-prepared when out in the park.

Once upon a time, there were twin boys named Tim and Tom. They liked to play together in their big white house. One sunny day, they went outside to play with their white ball. Tim and Tom loved to throw the ball to each other. They laughed and smiled as they played. The ball went up and down, back and forth between them. They were very happy. Then, their mom called them inside for lunch. The twins sat down at the table and ate their yummy food. After they finished, they went back outside to play with their white ball some more. They played until the sun went down and it was time for bed. Tim and Tom hugged each other goodnight, excited to play again the next day.
