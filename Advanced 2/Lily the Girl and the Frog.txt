One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her number toys. She liked to count and learn. Her mom always said that counting would give her wisdom. Lily wanted to be smart, so she played with her number toys every day. Lily's friend, Tim, came over to play. When he saw the number toys, he asked, "What are these for?" Lily said, "They help me learn and be smart. My mom says they give me wisdom." Tim wanted to be smart too, so they played with the number toys together. As they played, the sky became dark and it started to rain. The rain was so clear and clean. Suddenly, a big frog jumped into their room through the open window! The frog said, "I like your number toys. They make me feel wise, too." Lily and Tim were surprised, but they let the frog play with them. They all learned and had fun together with their number toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little panda who lived in a big forest. The panda was very tough and liked to play and run around all day. One day, the tough panda found a pretty red ball. The panda loved the ball so much, he wanted to hold it all the time. In the forest, there was a big mean lion. The lion saw the tough panda holding the red ball and wanted it too. The lion was very big and scary, and the panda was very small. The lion growled and said, "Give me the red ball, or else!" The tough panda didn't want to give the red ball to the big mean lion. He loved the ball and wanted to keep it. But the lion was much bigger and stronger than the panda. In the end, the lion took the red ball away from the panda. The panda was very sad and cried. The big mean lion didn't care and walked away with the red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily and her dog, Max, loved to go for a walk every day. They liked to feel the warm sun on their faces as they walked and played. One day, Lily and Max were getting ready for their walk. But, Lily's mom said, "Lily, we have to delay the walk. It's time for lunch." Lily was sad, but she knew that they could go for their walk after they ate. After lunch, Lily and Max finally went for their warm walk. They saw their friend, Tom, and his cat, Whiskers. Tom said, "Hi, Lily! Do you want to play with us?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes!" So, Lily, Max, Tom, and Whiskers all played together in the warm sun, happy that the walk was only delayed, not stopped.

Daisy and Mommy had a secret. They were excited to bake something special. Daisy couldn't wait to find out what the secret was. Mommy put Daisy's apron on her and gave her a big bowl full of flour and other baking ingredients. Daisy and Mommy mixed, stirred and added the secret ingredients. They were having so much fun! Finally, it was time to bake. Daisy and Mommy put the mix in the oven and the house started to smell delicious. Daisy was so excited, she couldn't wait to find out what the special secret was. Mommy opened the oven and Daisy saw a yummy looking cake. "It's a special chocolate cake," said Mommy, "just for you, Daisy." Daisy was so happy and shouted, "yay!" Daisy and Mommy enjoyed the delicious cake. Daisy couldn't believe they had made it together. She was excited to have a secret baking adventure with Mommy. It was the best day ever!

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom and dad. He had a big net to catch small fish. Tim was so happy to play near the water. He saw a small fish and tried to catch it with his net. As he played, he saw a man he did not know. The unknown man had a big boat. He waved at Tim and said "Hi!" Tim waved back and said "Hi!" too. Tim's mom and dad saw the unknown man and waved to him as well. Tim and his parents played at the beach all day. They caught many small fish with the net. They waved to the unknown man every time they saw him. When it was time to go home, Tim waved goodbye to the man and the beach. He was excited to come back and play again.
