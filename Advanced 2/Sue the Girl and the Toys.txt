Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She was an independent girl who liked to play alone. One day, she saw a big door. Sue wanted to see what was inside, so she decided to enter. When she went in, she found a room full of toys. There were dolls, cars, and even a big slide. Sue was so happy! But then, she saw a sign on the wall. The sign said, "Law: You must share toys with others." Sue didn't want to share. She wanted to play alone. But then, a little boy came into the room. He was sad because he had no one to play with. Sue thought about the law and decided to share her toys with the boy. They played together, and Sue found out that playing with others can be fun too.

Once upon a time, there was a useful blanket. It was very special because it could reverse. One day, a little boy named Tim found the blanket in his room. He was very happy because it was cold outside and he needed a warm blanket. Tim went to the park with the blanket. He played on the swings, the slide, and the seesaw. But then, something unexpected happened. The blanket started to reverse all by itself. It became a big, soft pillow. Tim was surprised but he still liked his new pillow. He took it home and showed it to his mom. She was also amazed by the magical pillow. They decided to keep it as their secret. That night, as Tim slept with his new pillow, it reversed back into a warm blanket. He was so cozy and happy. Every day, the blanket and pillow would switch, and Tim loved his new useful friend. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a brown scooter in his yard. He was very happy. Tim wanted to ride the scooter fast. He had a plan. Tim's plan was to go down the big hill by his house. He thought it would be fun to ride the scooter very fast. Tim got on the brown scooter and started to go down the hill. But Tim did not know that the scooter was broken. The wheels were not good. Tim fell off the scooter and got hurt. The plan was not good. The brown scooter was not fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big bull named Bob. He lived on a farm with many other animals. Bob was not like the other bulls. He was very delicate and gentle. He liked to play with the butterflies and smell the pretty flowers. One day, a little girl named Sally came to the farm. She saw Bob and wanted to play with him. She said, "Bob, can I request to play with you?" Bob was happy to have a new friend and nodded his big head. As they were playing, something unexpected happened. A strong wind blew through the farm, lifting Sally up into the air! Bob, being a delicate bull, was scared but knew he had to help his friend. He ran as fast as he could and caught Sally just in time. From that day on, Sally and Bob were the best of friends, and everyone knew that even a delicate bull could be a hero.

One day, a little girl named Amy was eager to go outside and play. She put on her shoes and ran to the door. But her mom stopped her and said, "Wait! Your shoes are dirty. You need to wipe them first." Amy got a cloth and wiped her shoes. She was happy to see them clean. Then, she went outside to play with her friends. They ran and jumped in the grass, having lots of fun. Suddenly, a big dog came up to them. The dog was friendly, but it had dirty paws. The dog jumped on Amy, and her shoes got dirty again. Amy was sad, but then she had an idea. She said to her friends, "Let's teach the dog how to wipe its paws!" And they all laughed and played together, teaching the dog to wipe its paws.
