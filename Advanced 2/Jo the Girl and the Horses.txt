Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jo. Jo was three years old and she wanted to go to the stable to visit the horses. She put on her favorite pink dress and asked her mom if she could go outside and play. Mom said yes, so Jo went to the stable. When Jo got there, she was so excited to see all the horses. They were all so big! She was a bit fearful, but she wanted to pet the horses and have an adventure. "Hi horses!" said Jo. The horses came close to Jo and she pet them. She was so happy to be in the stable with the horses. She sat down and the horses stood there and greeted her. Jo was glad to be with them. After a few minutes of petting the horses, Jo looked around the stable. It had a lot of hay in it and Jo thought it looked really cool. She was sad to go home but happy that she had made some new friends. Jo said goodbye to the horses and gave them one last pat before she turned to go home. She knew she would be back to the stable soon to play with her new friends.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there lived a selfish flower. This flower did not like to share. It wanted all the sunlight and water for itself. The other flowers in the garden were sad because they needed sunlight and water too. One day, a new plant came to the garden. It was a mint plant. The mint plant was kind and friendly. It liked to share its space with the other flowers. The mint plant helped the other flowers to bloom and grow. The selfish flower saw how happy the other flowers were with the mint plant. It started to feel lonely. Soon, the selfish flower learned to share its sunlight and water with the other flowers. They all bloomed together, making the garden beautiful and full of life. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can bring happiness to everyone.

One day, a soft cat named Sam went out to play. The sun was shining very bright. Sam saw a long tape on the ground. He thought it was a toy. Sam said, "I want to play with the tape!" He picked it up with his paw. The tape was sticky. Sam did not know that the tape was not a toy. He played with it more. Then, something bad happened. The tape got stuck to Sam's tail. He tried to run, but he could not. The tape was too strong. Sam was sad and scared. He said, "I wish I did not play with the tape."

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Fin. Fin lived in a reef with his friends. They liked to play and swim all day. One day, a big boat came near the reef. The boat had a lot of food to supply to the fish. The fish were happy, but Fin was scared. He did not know what the boat wanted. Then, something unexpected happened. The boat dropped the food in the water, and it was all their favorite food! Fin and his friends were not scared anymore. They ate the food and played with the boat. The boat was their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with her best friend, a girl named Anna. They liked to play and have fun together all day. One day, Anna and Kitty were in the kitchen. Anna wanted to make a sandwich with butter. Kitty saw the butter and thought it looked tasty. She whispered to Anna, "Can I have some butter, please?" Anna smiled and gave Kitty a little bit of butter to try. Kitty loved the taste of the butter! They both laughed and played with the butter, making their sandwiches. After they ate, they went outside to play in the sun. Anna and Kitty were very happy and had a great day together.

Once upon a time, in a small kitchen, there was a little muffin. The muffin was very sad because he felt foolish. He did not know what to do. One day, a happy cookie came to visit him. "Hello, muffin! Why are you so sad?" the cookie asked. The muffin looked at the cookie and said, "I feel foolish. I don't know how to be a good muffin." The cookie smiled and said, "I can tell you how to be a good muffin. Just be sweet and yummy!" The muffin listened to what the cookie told him. He tried to be sweet and yummy. Soon, he felt happy and not foolish anymore. He thanked the cookie for helping him. From that day on, the muffin and the cookie became best friends. They played and laughed together in the kitchen, and they were always sweet and yummy.
