Once upon a time there was a little boy called Tom. He was three years old and he loved to clean. Every day he would look for something to clean around the house. Today he found a very special invitation. It said that his mum and dad were throwing a party for him and all his friends. Tom was very excited but he also knew that he had to clean his room first. Tom got to work tidying his toys and his bed and scrubbing the floor until it was spotless. When he was finished, his room was so clean. He was so proud that he ran to give his mum and dad a big hug. At the party Tom and his friends ate lots of healthy food like fruit, salads and sandwiches. They were having so much fun that Tom forgot all about cleaning the house. After the party was over, Tom and his mum and dad cleaned up all the plates and cups to get ready for the next day. Tom had a very healthy and happy day – and all because he wanted to clean his room and made sure it was perfect for his invitation. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a small boat named Bobby. Bobby lived in a harbor with many other boats. Bobby loved to touch the water and play with his friends. One day, Bobby saw a sour fruit floating in the water. It was a lemon. Bobby was curious and wanted to touch the lemon. But an older boat named Wise Willy told Bobby not to touch it. Wise Willy said that sour things can make you feel bad. Bobby didn't listen to Wise Willy and touched the sour lemon. Bobby's face turned funny and he felt bad. Bobby learned that he should listen to older boats who know more. From that day on, Bobby always listened to Wise Willy and never touched sour things again.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little boy named Jack. He lived near a beautiful beach and every day he wanted to go and play there. His mum said that he couldn't go to the beach – it was too dangerous. Jack was sad but he understood. One day, the sun was shining and it looked like a perfect day for the beach. Jack asked his mum again and she said no. But Jack was brave. He wanted to go to the beach. He knew that his mum wanted to stop him to prevent him from getting hurt. Jack thought and thought until he came up with a plan. He packed a lunch for him and his mum and asked if she would come to the beach with him. His mum paused for a moment and then smiled. The mum and son had a wonderful day at the beach. Jack was so brave and happy that his mum had said yes. From then on, he was able to go to the beach with her and have lots of fun.

One day, Mum looked at the floor in Max's room. It was cold and hard, and she thought he needed a rug for his room to be more comfortable. Mum took Max to the shops to look for a rug. She asked him, “What colour do you want, Max?” Max thought for a moment, then exclaimed “Green!” with delight. So they looked through the rugs and eventually found the perfect one – it was a nice shade of green. Max was so excited to have his new rug that he helped to carry it all the way home. When they got back, he helped Mum lay it out on the floor in his room. Mum said, “I think this rug looks great in here – and I'm sure you'll be nice and cosy on it!” Max smiled and agreed – he really did need a green rug.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a serious dog who liked to help his friends. One day, Max went to a meeting with his animal friends. At the meeting, Max said, "We need to serve food to all our friends." His friends agreed and they all went to find food. Max found apples, his friend Cat found milk, and Bird found seeds. They all brought the food back to the meeting. Max served the apples, Cat served the milk, and Bird served the seeds. All the animal friends enjoyed the food and had a fun time at the meeting. They were all happy that Max helped them.
