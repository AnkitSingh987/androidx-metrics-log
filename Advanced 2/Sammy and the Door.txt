Sammy was so grateful when his mommy and daddy showed him the surprise. They took him to the bathroom and opened the door to a huge room with shiny white tiles. There was a large shower in the corner with silver knobs on the wall beside it. Sammy was so excited he clapped his hands! The parents showed Sammy how to turn the knobs and make the shower spray out water from the nozzle. Sammy squealed with joy every time the water came out. After a few minutes, the parents taught Sammy how to turn the knobs off and step into the shower. Sammy loved feeling the warm water spraying over his body. He played with the water for a few minutes before his parents showed him how to use the soap and shampoo. It felt so good to stand beneath the shower and get all cleaned up. When the shower was finished, Sammy thanked his parents for showing him how to use it. He was so grateful for being able to have a shower!

One day, a little boy named Tim went with his mom to the mailbox. They wanted to send a letter to Tim's friend, Sam. Tim was very excited to send his first mail. When they got to the mailbox, Tim's mom said, "Okay, Tim, put the letter in the mailbox." Tim put the letter in, but it got stuck. "Oh no," said Tim, "I'm sorry, Mom." Tim's mom smiled and said, "It's okay. Let's reverse the letter and try again." So, Tim took the letter out, turned it around, and put it back in the mailbox. This time, it went in easily. Tim was so happy to send the mail. He thanked his mom for helping him. They walked home together, holding hands, and Tim couldn't wait to hear back from his friend Sam.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a troubled toy. The toy was hurt and could not play. Tim wanted to heal the toy, so he went to find help. Tim saw a drain near his house. He thought that the water from the drain could heal his toy. So, he put the toy in the drain. But, something unexpected happened. The water did not heal the toy. Instead, it made the toy grow bigger! Now, the toy was not troubled anymore. It was big and strong. Tim and the toy played together all day. They were very happy. And that's how the drain helped Tim's toy in a way he never thought it would.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his bike all day long. One day, while riding his bike, he saw a big monster. The monster had long arms and long legs. Tim was scared, but the monster said, "Don't be scared. I am a nice monster. Will you be my friend?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Okay, we can be friends." Together, they played and had fun. Tim showed the monster how to ride a bike, and the monster showed Tim how to reach high things with his long arms. They were happy and became best friends.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the kitchen. She saw a harmless bug on the floor. Lucy wanted to grab the bug and show her mom. "Mom! Look at the bug!" said Lucy. Her mom said, "Be careful, Lucy. It might be a trick." The next day, Lucy went back to the kitchen. The harmless bug was still there. Lucy thought about what her mom said. She did not grab the bug this time. "I will wait and see," she said to herself. On the third day, Lucy saw the harmless bug again. This time, it was on a yummy cake. Lucy knew it was a trick now. She did not grab the bug. Instead, she told her mom. "Mom, the bug is on the cake!" Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Lucy! You were smart not to grab it. Now we can eat the cake together." And they did.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She was a lively girl who loved to play and have fun. One day, Amy was playing in the park with her parent when something unexpected happened. A big bird came down from the sky and started to speak! The bird said, "Hello, I am a magic bird. I can grant you one wish." Amy and her parent were very surprised. They never saw a bird that could speak before. Amy thought for a moment and said, "I wish for a fun day with my parent." The magic bird nodded and flew away. Suddenly, Amy's parent turned into a big, bouncy ball! Amy laughed and played with the bouncy ball all day. It was the most fun day she ever had. In the end, the magic bird turned her parent back into a person, and they went home happy.
