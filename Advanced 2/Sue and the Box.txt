One day, a little girl named Sue decided to wander outside. She walked and walked until she saw her neighbor, Mr. Brown. Mr. Brown was very organized. He always had his toys and tools in the right place. Sue liked to watch him work in his yard. Sue saw Mr. Brown put a big box in his yard. She thought it was a new toy for her to play with. She ran to the box and opened it. But, it was not a toy. It was a big, soft bed for Mr. Brown's dog, Spot. Sue was sad. She thought the box was for her. But then, Mr. Brown saw her and smiled. He took out a small box from his pocket. Inside the box was a pretty doll for Sue. She was very happy and played with her new doll all day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a hairy dog named Bobo. Bobo was a good dog who loved to play and help his family. One day, Bobo saw smoke coming from the kitchen. He knew that smoke was not good, so he ran to his family. Bobo barked, "Woof, woof!" and his family came to see what was wrong. They saw the smoke and quickly put out the fire. They were very thankful to Bobo for helping them. They gave him a big hug and rubbed his hairy back. The family learned that they should always be careful in the kitchen. Bobo taught them that it is important to help each other and pay attention to things around them. And from that day on, they always made sure to keep an eye on the stove when they cooked.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to play with her friends, the animals. One day, Lily saw a shiny rock on the ground. It was a diamond! The diamond was enormous and very pretty. Lily wanted to show her friends the diamond. She found her friend, Mr. Bunny. Mr. Bunny liked to tease Lily by hiding from her. He saw the enormous diamond and said, "Wow! That is so big and shiny!" Lily and Mr. Bunny went to show more friends. They found Miss Squirrel and Mr. Bird. They all looked at the enormous diamond and thought it was very pretty. They played with the diamond and teased each other by hiding it. They had a lot of fun with their new shiny toy.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy. She was three years old and loved playing with her toys. One day Lucy's Mommy gave her a big green dictionary. Lucy was so excited and she couldn't help but lift it up to look at it. "Mommy, what is this?" asked Lucy. "It's a dictionary," replied Mommy. "It has all kinds of words in it. You can learn a lot!" So, Lucy opened up the dictionary and started looking through it. Every time she found a new word, she would lift it up and say it out loud. She was so excited and couldn't wait to learn more! Soon, Lucy had learned so many different words and was even reading stories by herself. Mommy was so proud of her little girl and gave her a big hug. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a green journal that she loved very much. Lucy would hold her journal every day and draw pretty pictures in it. One day, she drew a big sun in her journal, but she didn't know that the sun would become very important later. Lucy went to the park with her green journal. She found a nice spot under a tree to sit and draw. As she was drawing, she saw a little boy who looked sad. Lucy thought that maybe if she showed him her big sun drawing, it would make him happy. Lucy walked over to the little boy with her green journal. She held it out to him and showed him the big sun drawing. The little boy smiled and started to feel happy again. They both sat under the tree and drew pictures together. The big sun drawing that Lucy made had made a new friend for her, just like she had hoped.
