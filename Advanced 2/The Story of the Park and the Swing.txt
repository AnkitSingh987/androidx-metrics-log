Once upon a time, there was a girl. She liked to go to the park and swing. One day, when she went to the park, the price of the swing had changed. It was much more now and this made the girl very upset. She sat on the swing and cried. Her friends all came over and saw she was sad. They wanted to make her feel better, so they took out their pocket money and all together it was enough to pay for the swing. The girl was so happy and thanked her friends. She jumped up and down on the swing. For the rest of that day, she was having so much fun swinging and playing with her friends.

Lily and Max were twins who shared a room and a bed. They liked to sleep on the big mattress that was soft and comfortable. But sometimes they fought over who got more space or who kicked who in the night. One day, their mom said she had a surprise for them. She took them to a store that had many beds and mattresses. She said they could each pick one for their room. Lily and Max were excited and ran around the store. They tried different beds and mattresses, but they could not agree on which one they liked. Lily liked a bed that was pink and had flowers on it. She said it was pretty and cozy. Max liked a bed that was blue and had cars on it. He said it was cool and fast. They both wanted their own bed, but they did not want to share a room with the other bed. They started to argue and cry. They said they did not like the other bed and they did not want to be near it. Their mom was sad and disappointed. She said they were being selfish and rude. She said they should be happy and grateful that they had a chance to get new beds and mattresses. She said they should think about how to fit both beds in their room and how to get along with each other. Lily and Max felt sorry and ashamed. They realized they were being mean and silly. They hugged their mom and said they were sorry. They said they loved each other and they did not mind sharing a room. They said they would try to find beds and mattresses that fit their room and their tastes. They said they would be respectful and kind to each other. Their mom smiled and hugged them back. She said she was proud of them and she loved them too. She said they had learned a valuable lesson about sharing and caring. She said they could still pick their own beds and mattresses, but they had to work together and compromise. She said they would have a new and comfortable room soon. Lily and Max nodded and smiled. They thanked their mom and went to look for their new beds and mattresses. They were happy and excited again.

Once upon a time, there was a mill in a gloomy forest. The mill was big and made a loud sound. Many animals lived near the mill. They were happy and played all day. One day, a little duck lost her way. She could not find her home. The duck was sad and scared. She walked and walked until she saw the mill. The mill was big, but it was also gloomy. The duck went inside to look for help. Inside the mill, she met a kind frog. The frog saw the sad duck and wanted to help her. They both went outside and looked for the duck's home. After some time, they found her home near a pond. The duck was happy to be home again. The frog and the duck became best friends, and they played together every day near the mill.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dog named Bobo. Bobo had a hurt heel. He was a fearful dog, always scared of the other animals. Because of his hurt heel, he could not run and play with them. One day, a big cat named Kitty came to the town. Kitty seemed very strong and fast. All the other animals wanted to be friends with Kitty, but Bobo was scared. He thought Kitty would not like him because of his hurt heel. Bobo's friend, a bird named Chirpy, told him to be brave and talk to Kitty. Chirpy said, "Don't be scared, Bobo. You are a good dog. Kitty will like you." So, Bobo went to Kitty and said "hello". To his surprise, Kitty was very nice to him. They became friends and played together. The moral of the story is: Don't be scared to make new friends, even if you feel different. Everyone has something special to share.
