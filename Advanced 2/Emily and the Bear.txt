Once upon a time there was a strong girl called Emily. She lived in a big house far away, on a hill. One day, Emily and her mum went for a walk in the snow. Emily's mum was wearing a warm coat and Emily was wearing her red hat and boots. Suddenly, a big bear appeared. Emily's Mum was scared and ran away. Emily stood still and started to cry. The bear said to Emily, "Don't cry. I will not hurt you". Emily replied, "I am scared". The bear said, "Don't be scared. I will rescue you". The bear scooped Emily up in its big arms and carried her back to Emily's house. Emily was so happy and thanked the bear for rescuing her. The end.

Once upon a time, there were two twin girls, named Jane and June. They liked to play together, but sometimes they would quarrel. One day, they found a big, red ball in the park. They both wanted the ball, but neither wanted to share. They started to quarrel and say, "It's mine!" A small, talking bird was watching them. The bird saw how selfish they were being. The bird flew down and said, "Stop your quarrel! I will help you learn to share." The twins looked at the bird, surprised that it could talk. The bird took the ball and flew high into the sky. The bird dropped the ball, and it bounced very high. The twins watched the ball bounce, and they forgot about their quarrel. They started to laugh and play together, trying to catch the ball. They learned to share and not be selfish. The twins and the talking bird became good friends, and they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch films with his mommy. One day, they watched a film about a big, friendly dog. Tim was very happy and smiled a lot. In the film, the dog met a small, frightened cat. The cat said, "Please don't hurt me, big dog!" The dog smiled and said, "I won't hurt you, little cat. I want to be your friend." The cat smiled back, and they became best friends. One day, while they were playing, they found a magic ball. When they touched it, something unexpected happened: the dog became small, and the cat became big! They both laughed and smiled, because now they could play even better together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there lived a mighty man. He loved to have pizza for dinner every night. But one night, he had a strange feeling in his heart. He felt like he was doing something he shouldn't. That night he chose not to have pizza. Instead, he had fruit and veggies. At the time it seemed like the right choice, but now he regretted it. He wished he could go back in time and make the right choice. He wished he had his favorite pizza that night. But alas, it was too late - the night was gone. He never forgot his feeling of regret. He never ate a dinner without pizza again. When his friends asked him why, he just said it was because he was mighty!

Sara and Ben were playing in the garden. They saw a big red strawberry on a green plant. Sara wanted to pick it and eat it. "Can I have the strawberry?" Sara asked Ben. Ben shook his head. "No, it is mine. I saw it first." Sara felt sad. She liked strawberries very much. She looked at the strawberry. It was shiny and juicy. She wanted to taste it. "Please, Ben, can we share it?" Sara begged. Ben thought for a moment. He liked Sara. She was his friend. He did not want to make her sad. But he also liked strawberries. He wanted to eat it all. "Okay, we can share it. But be careful. The strawberry is delicate. It can break easily." Ben said. Sara smiled. She was happy. She thanked Ben. They carefully picked the strawberry. They broke it in half. They each took a bite. The strawberry was sweet and yummy. They enjoyed it. They looked at each other. They were both happy. They hugged. They did not mind sharing. Sharing was good. They decided to look for more strawberries together. They had fun in the garden.
