Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Blinky. Blinky had a big wing and a small wing. Blinky liked to blink a lot. One day, Blinky saw a popular bird named Poppy. Poppy had many friends. Blinky wanted to be friends with Poppy. Blinky said, "Hi! Can we be friends?" Poppy looked at Blinky's wings and then said, "Sure! We can be friends." Blinky and Poppy played together every day. They flew high and low. Blinky's big wing and small wing did not stop him. Blinky blinked and smiled. Blinky and Poppy became best friends. And Blinky was very happy.

Once upon a time, in a friendly little town, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to skip and play with her friends. They would skip all day long, laughing and having fun together. One day, Sue met a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that Sue really liked. It was a bright, shiny ball. Sue wanted to play with it, but she didn't have one. So Sue asked Tim, "Can we trade toys? I have a pretty doll that I think you will like." Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, let's trade." They traded toys and were very happy. Now Sue and Tim could play together with their new toys. They skipped and laughed all day long. Their friendly town was filled with joy and fun. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once there was a kind tank. It was filled with all sorts of fun things. There was sand, water, and lots of other cool items. One day, the tank wanted to mix everything together. It didn't know how, so it asked its friends for help. They tried shaking it and stirring it, but nothing worked. Finally, one of the friends had an idea. They decided to add some water to the tank and mix everything together. They stirred and stirred until everything was mixed up. And that's how the tank turned all its different things into one big mix!

Mama was cooking dinner. The pan was full of steaming, hot rice. "It looks so good!" Annie said to her mama. "It sure does," Mama said, but she had a worried look on her face. "What's wrong Mama?" Annie asked. Mama sighed. "I'm worried that the rice is too difficult for you to eat," she said. "You don't have many teeth yet." Annie thought for a minute. Then she had an idea. "I know! The rice fairy can come and help me!" Mama smiled. She was relieved that there was a solution. "Alright then," she said. "Let's ask the rice fairy to come." Annie clapped her hands. Suddenly, there was a glittering light and the rice fairy flew into the room. "Thank you for coming!" Annie exclaimed. The rice fairy smiled and waved her wand. Slowly, the grains of rice became softer and easier to eat. Annie smiled in delight. "Dinner is ready!" Mama said. Annie and Mama both enjoyed the rice. It was just right - not too difficult to eat. From then on, they always asked the rice fairy to come help them with dinner!

One day, a little gray cat named Mimi went for a walk. Mimi saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. But Mimi was scared. She looked at the tree and said, "I want to go up, but I am too small." A big dog named Max came by. He saw Mimi and the tree. Max wanted to help. He said, "I can help you, Mimi. I will offer you my back. You can jump on me and then go up the tree." Mimi was happy and said, "Thank you, Max!" Mimi jumped on Max's back and then onto the tree. She climbed and found a pocket in the tree. It was a perfect place for her to sit. Mimi was so happy she could climb the tree. She said, "Thank you, Max! You are a good friend!" Max smiled and said, "You're welcome, Mimi!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. They had a pretty garden with lots of flowers and trees. Lucy loved playing in the garden every day. One day, Lucy found a small, ordinary pebble on the ground. She thought it looked a little sad and plain. So, Lucy decided she wanted to decorate the pebble to make it special. She picked up the pebble and took it inside her house. Lucy used her colorful paints to decorate the pebble. She painted it with red, blue, and yellow, making it look very pretty. When she was done, she showed her mom and dad, who both smiled and clapped. They thought the pebble was beautiful now. Lucy took the decorated pebble and placed it in the garden, making it even more lovely. And they all lived happily ever after.
