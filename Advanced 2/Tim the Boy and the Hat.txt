One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw an old man sitting on a bench. The old man looked sad and dirty. Tim walked up to him and asked, "Why are you sad?" The old man said, "I lost my hat. It was my favorite hat." Tim wanted to help the old man. He looked around the park and saw a big tree. Tim thought maybe the hat was in the tree. He climbed the tree and saw something in the branches. Tim pinched the hat from the tree and climbed down. He gave the hat to the old man. The old man smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim felt happy that he could help the old man. He learned that helping others can make you feel good too.

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big tree with many leaves. They ran to the tree and looked for bugs and birds. "Look, Ben, a mushroom!" Lila said. She pointed to a small, brown mushroom under the tree. It had white spots on it. "Can I mark it?" Ben asked. He had a red crayon in his hand. He liked to mark things with his crayon. "No, Ben, don't mark it!" Lila said. "Mushrooms are not for marking. They are for growing. Maybe a fairy lives in it." "A fairy?" Ben said. He looked at the mushroom with wonder. He wanted to see a fairy. "Yes, a fairy," Lila said. "Maybe she is sleeping in the mushroom. Maybe she will come out if we are quiet and nice." Lila and Ben sat down next to the mushroom. They were quiet and nice. They waited for the fairy to come out. They felt the cold wind on their faces. They shivered. "Maybe the fairy is cold too," Lila said. "Maybe we can give her something to keep her warm." Lila took off her scarf and wrapped it around the mushroom. Ben took off his hat and put it on top of the mushroom. They smiled at each other. "Maybe the fairy will like our gifts," Ben said. "Maybe she will," Lila said. They hugged each other and watched the mushroom. They hoped to see the fairy. They did not know that the mushroom was not a fairy house, but a toadstool. And they did not see the toad that lived in it, who was very confused by the scarf and the hat.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. Sarah was very careful, and she loved exploring new places. One day, Sarah went for a walk and discovered a secret garden. In the middle of the garden was a beautiful crystal. Sarah was so excited and reached out to pick up the crystal. Suddenly, a voice shouted out, "Don't touch that crystal!" Sarah was startled and looked around. It was an old wizard! The wizard said, "You must be very careful with the crystal. If you touch it, I must demand something from you." Sarah was confused and asked, "What do you demand?" The wizard replied, "A kind deed." Sarah vowed to be very careful with the crystal and do something kind for the wizard. While saying goodbye, the wizard said, "I'll be nearby if you need me." Then he smiled and walked away. Sarah was so happy and continued to explore the garden. She was careful with the crystal and did something kind for the wizard. What an adventure!

Once upon a time there was a smart girl called Dawn. One day, Dawn worked really hard to create something special for her mum and dad. She wanted to make them something really special for their anniversary. Dawn made a surprise for them and put it into a white envelope. She licked the envelope and sealed it with a big kiss. Then, she put it into the post box and she mailed the special surprise. When Dawn's mum and dad opened the envelope, they were so happy with the gift. It was a picture of Dawn with her mummy and daddy. Dawn was so happy that her surprise had made her mum and dad so happy. She was a very smart girl indeed.
