Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Max. Max loved to jog in the park every day. One sunny day, Max met a little girl named Lily. Lily said, "Max, can you teach me how to jog like you?" Max wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, I can teach you!" Max and Lily jogged in the park together. They jogged up and down the hills. They jogged around the trees. Lily was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Max! Now I can jog too!" Max and Lily became best friends. They jogged and played together every day. And Lily became a clever jogger, just like Max.

A little rabbit joined the race. The rabbit was very fast too. Tim and the rabbit raced to the finish line. It was a close race. In the end, the rabbit won by a little bit. Tim was not sad. Once upon a time there was a smart kid called Tommy. Tommy was three years old and he loved playing outside. Tommy was walking one day when he heard a noise which sounded like something had dropped. He looked around and saw something twinkling in the grass. It was a box and when Tommy opened it something beautiful came out, it was peace. Tommy was so excited to find peace. He sat down and stared at it in awe. Suddenly, his mommy came running over. She asked him why he was sitting there. Tommy showed her the peace and said, “Look, mommy. I found peace!” His mommy smiled and said, “That’s wonderful Tommy! Let’s take it home and show daddy, he will be very happy.” So Tommy and his mommy took the peace home, to show daddy. Daddy was very smart and he was very proud of Tommy when he saw the peace. They all hugged and then daddy said, “Let’s keep the peace safe and share it with everyone.” Tommy and his family kept the peace safe and shared it with all their friends and family. The End

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to run fast and play outside. One day, Spot's mom said it was time to go for a walk. She went to search for the leash. Spot was so happy to go for a walk. While they were walking, Spot saw a big ball. He wanted to play with it. Spot ran fast to get the ball. But, he forgot he was on the leash. The leash pulled him back, and he fell down. Spot was sad. Then, a little girl came to Spot. She said, "I saw you fall. Are you okay?" Spot wagged his tail. The little girl picked up the ball and played with Spot. They had a lot of fun. The plot twist was that Spot made a new friend. The moral value of the story is to always be kind and help others.
