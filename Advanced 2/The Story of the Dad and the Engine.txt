The engine was loud. It made a lot of noise. Little Jake wanted to see it. He wanted to get close. So Jake crept up to the engine. He was careful not to make any noise. He stared at the huge and loud machine. He was very curious. Suddenly, a loud shout came from behind him. It was an angry shout! It was his dad! His dad told him not to go near the engine. He said it was dangerous. Jake was scared. He looked up to his dad. His dad was very angry. He was scowling at him. But he was also very sad. Jake looked back at the engine. He didn't want to understand why his dad was angry. He just wanted to stare and imagine what it was like to be near it.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a cool, big tree. He wanted to bring his mom to see it too. So, he took her hand and walked with her to the tree. When they got to the tree, Tim had a question. He asked his mom, "Why is the tree so big?" His mom smiled and said, "The tree is very old, so it had a lot of time to grow big and strong." Tim was happy to learn about the cool, big tree. He thanked his mom for answering his question. They played under the tree all day, and Tim loved the park even more.

Once upon a time, there was a taxi. The taxi was yellow and big. The taxi driver was not smart. People called him a stupid driver. He did not like that. One day, a man got in the taxi. He wanted to go far. The stupid driver did not know the way. He tried to add the numbers on the houses. He wanted to find the right house. The man saw the driver was not smart. He told the driver to stop. Then, the man got out of the taxi. The man was a teacher. He wanted to help the driver. So, he gave the driver a book. The book could make the driver smart. The driver read the book. He became smart. The next day, he was not a stupid driver anymore. He knew how to add and find the right house. The teacher and the driver became good friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Mary and Jane. Mary was eager to show Jane a special place. She said to Jane, "Let's hurry to the park, it's so much fun there!" Jane was excited, so they ran off to the park. But when they got there, they saw two boys in the middle of a fight. Mary was scared, but Jane was eager to help. She shouted at the boys to stop, but they ignored her. Mary grabbed Jane's hand, and they tried to run away, but one of the boys followed them. Mary got really scared then, and said to Jane, "We need to hurry!" Jane and Mary were running as fast as they could, but the boy gained on them. Eventually he caught Mary and held her back, while Jane ran away. The two friends never saw each other again. Mary was never found, and Jane was sad for the rest of her life.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom was very friendly and always said "welcome" to everyone he met. Tom loved to play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Tom and his friends decided to have a test. They wanted to see who could jump the highest. All the animals in the town came to watch and cheer for their friends. The birds, dogs, and rabbits all tried their best to jump high. In the end, it was Tom who won the test. He jumped so high that everyone was amazed. Tom felt very happy, but he also knew that winning was not the most important thing. The most important thing was that all the animals had fun and played together. The moral of the story is that it's not always about winning, but about having fun and being kind to others.
