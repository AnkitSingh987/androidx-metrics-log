Once upon a time, there was a clever little bird named Blue. Blue lived in a big tree near a small house. Every year, the tree grew tall and had many leaves. One day, Blue saw a shiny thing high up in the tree. Blue wanted to reach it. So, Blue flew up and up, trying to get the shiny thing. But it was too high. Blue did not give up. Blue had an idea. Blue got help from friends. The friends stood on top of each other. Now, Blue could reach the shiny thing. Blue was happy and thanked the friends. The clever little bird and the friends played together all year.

One day, a little girl named Jenny was out on a walk with her mom. As they went, Jenny saw something very interesting. She said to her mom, "Look Mom! A land!" Mom replied, "I see it. This must be a special land. What do you think we should do?" "Let's collect it," Jenny said excitedly. So Jenny and her mom started collecting the land. They picked up different rocks, leaves and twigs. Suddenly, a nosy squirrel approached them. He said, "Meow! What are you doing?" Jenny's mom laughed. "We're just collecting this land," she answered. The squirrel got very excited. He then promised them, "I will help too!" And so, he did. When they were done, Jenny and her mom thanked the squirrel. They packed their land in bags, and went back home with a big smile on their faces.

Once upon a time, there was a modest seal named Sam. Sam lived in a big sea with many fish friends. One day, a little fish named Fin asked Sam if he could play with him. "Can you permit me to play with you, Sam?" asked Fin. Sam said, "Yes, Fin! Let's play together." While playing, they found a big shell. The shell opened, and a magic crab came out. The crab said, "I will give you one wish, Sam and Fin." Sam and Fin were very happy. They wished to be the best friends forever. The magic crab made their wish come true. Sam and Fin learned that it was good to share and permit others to play with them. They became the best friends in the big sea, and all the other fish wanted to be their friends too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a toy bunny named Hop. They were best friends and played together every day. Lily loved Hop with all her body. One day, Lily and Hop went to the park with Lily's mom. They had so much fun playing on the swings and slides. But when it was time to go home, Lily forgot Hop at the park. She didn't remember until bedtime. Lily was very upset. Lily's mom knew how much Hop meant to her. So, the next day, they went back to the park. There, on the swing, was Hop waiting for Lily. Lily was so happy to see Hop again. She hugged Hop tight and promised to always remember him.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys and go outside. One day, she found a cut on her finger. It hurt a little, but she was brave. Lily showed her mom the cut. Her mom said, "Oh no, let's leave the house and go to the store. We need to buy a band-aid to make it all better." So, Lily and her mom left the house and went to the store. At the store, they found a box of band-aids with pretty colors. Lily picked a pink one for her cut. Her mom put the band-aid on Lily's finger, and it felt better. They went back to their quiet home, and Lily played with her toys again, happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a tired little girl. She had been walking in the hot sun for so long and her feet were sore. All around her was an endless desert. Just when she thought she couldn't walk any more, something surprising revealed itself; a beautiful oasis! She could hardly believe her eyes. The oasis was an amazing sight; it was so full of life with trees and colorful flowers. She sat down and the cool water of the oasis felt amazingly refreshing. Suddenly, the little girl heard a voice. It was coming from the oasis and it said, "Welcome! I'm so glad you've made it here. Would you like to stay here? I have a special deal for you; it's very cheap!" The little girl was so excited that she almost jumped up and down. She had found a place to stay and it was so cheap! She said, "Yes please!" The oasis revealed its secret; it was suddenly full of life, birds, animals and the most beautiful plants she had ever seen. The little girl was very happy in her new home. She had found the perfect oasis to stay in, and it was even cheap!
