Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a big book with many pictures. They study the book together. They learn about animals, plants, and places. One day, they see a picture of a war. They see people with guns and tanks and planes. They see fire and smoke and blood. They do not like the picture. They feel sad and scared. "What is war?" Lily asks Ben. "War is when people fight and hurt each other," Ben says. "War is bad and mean." "Why do people do war?" Lily asks. "I don't know," Ben says. "Maybe they are angry or greedy or afraid. Maybe they do not know how to share or talk or be kind." "Can we stop war?" Lily asks. "I hope so," Ben says. "Maybe we can help by being nice and friendly and helpful. Maybe we can teach others to do the same." They hug each other and close the book. They decide to play something else. They run and jump and swing and slide. They laugh and sing and have fun. They feel happy and dizzy. They forget about war. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a red thief. He was very naughty and wanted everything for himself. He was selfish and would never share. One day, the thief had a dream. In the dream, he saw a world without selfishness. Everyone was kind and generous. The thief didn't understand his dream and continued to be selfish. But for some reason, he could never catch enough things for himself. The thief had a second dream. This one showed him being kind and generous instead. When he was kind and generous, he was able to catch much more than when he was selfish and never shared. Finally, the thief understood his dreams. He learned the power of kindness and generosity and was never selfish again. His kindness and generosity brought him more joy than selfishness ever did.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to chew on things. One day, Max found a case in the yard. The case was tight and hard to open, but Max wanted to see what was inside. Max chewed and chewed on the case. He tried to open it with his teeth. His friend, a little bird named Lily, saw Max and asked, "What are you doing, Max?" Max replied, "I am trying to open this tight case, Lily! I want to see what is inside." Lily helped Max by pecking at the case with her beak. Together, they opened the case. Inside the case, they found a ball. Max and Lily were very happy. They played with the ball all day long. They learned that working together made things easier and more fun. And from that day on, Max and Lily were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Amy. She was three years old. She liked having adventures in the woods near her home. One day, Amy decided to take a new path down to the river. The ground was rough, so she had to be careful as she stepped. But she didn't know that the path was dangerous. Suddenly, an animal jumped in front of her. It was a fox with a sharp pointy nose. The fox wanted to scare Amy. His brain was telling him it was the best way to get the food he wanted. Amy was scared, so she ran away. She thought she was safe, but the fox was quicker and followed her. He kept right behind her, getting closer and closer. Amy didn't reach home in time. The fox got ahold of her and hurt her badly. He was too sharp and too quick, and Amy didn't escape. The bad ending made Amy's family very sad. They missed her and nothing could make them feel better.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw some clay on the ground. Tim was very happy. He picked up the clay and started to make a ball. A girl named Sue came to the park too. She saw Tim playing with the clay. Sue asked, "Can I play with the clay too?" Tim said, "Yes, we can make a big ball together." They tried to make the ball, but it was not easy. The clay was sticky and hard to shape. Tim and Sue felt frustrated. They tried and tried, but the ball did not look good. Then, a big dog came to the park. The dog saw the clay ball and thought it was a toy. The dog picked up the ball in its mouth and ran away. Tim and Sue looked at each other and laughed. They were not frustrated anymore. They played together in the park and had a fun day.
