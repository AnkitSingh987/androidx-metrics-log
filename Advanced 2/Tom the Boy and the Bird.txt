Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was very miserable. He did not have any friends to play with. One day, Tom went to the park. He saw a light in the sky. The light was very bright and pretty. Tom walked to the light and saw a little girl. The little girl said, "Hi, my name is Sue. I put the light in the sky to find a friend. Do you want to be my friend?" Tom said, "Yes, I want to be your friend!" Tom and Sue played together. They had a lot of fun. Then, the light in the sky changed. It was not bright and pretty anymore. It was dark and scary. Tom and Sue were scared. They did not know what to do. Suddenly, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird said, "I am sorry, I made the light dark and scary. I wanted to find friends too." Tom and Sue said, "You can be our friend!" The bird was happy, and the light became bright and pretty again. From that day on, Tom, Sue, and the big bird were the best of friends. They played together every day, and they were never miserable again.

Once upon a time, in a huge swamp, there lived a big frog and a little frog. They were not friends because the big frog always took the little frog's food. One day, the little frog said, "Please, let's settle this. I don't want to fight." The big frog laughed and said, "No, I like being mean to you. I don't want to settle anything." The little frog felt sad and didn't know what to do. One day, a big bird saw the frogs in the swamp. The bird was very hungry. It saw the big frog being mean to the little frog. The bird decided to eat the big frog because it was mean. The bird swooped down and took the big frog away. The little frog was now alone in the swamp, but he was safe.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max and a small cat named Luna. They both lived in a fancy house with a nice family. Max and Luna liked to play, but sometimes they would fight over toys. One day, Max and Luna had a big fight over a toy. Max said, "This is my toy! I will win!" Luna said, "No, I will win!" They fought and fought, but neither of them could win. They were both very tired. Just then, a little mouse came into the room. The mouse picked up the toy and ran away with it. Max and Luna stopped fighting and looked at each other. They both laughed and said, "We should not fight. Let's be friends and share our toys." From that day on, Max and Luna never fought again, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Lucy. She loved to climb trees. One day, while climbing, she saw a boy named Max. Max was sad because he had a tough job to do. He needed to help with a big marriage party, but he did not know how. Lucy had an idea. She climbed down the tree and went to Max. She said, "Let's make the marriage fun!" They worked together to make the party nice. They put up pretty lights and made yummy food. Everyone was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the party things away! Lucy and Max were scared, but they did not give up. They found new things to make the marriage fun again. In the end, everyone had a great time. The moral of the story is that when things get tough, we should work together and never give up.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy was a very fit rabbit who loved to run and play all day. One day, Remy went to a lecture. At the lecture, there were many animals who wanted to learn how to run fast like Remy. During the lecture, Remy talked about how he managed to run so fast. He told the animals to eat good food, sleep well, and practice running every day. All the animals listened carefully to Remy's words. They all wanted to be fit and fast like him. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, slow turtle named Tilly walked into the lecture. All the animals laughed because they did not think a turtle could be fast. But Remy decided to help Tilly. To everyone's surprise, Tilly managed to become very fast too! All the animals learned that anyone can be fit and fast if they try hard and never give up.
