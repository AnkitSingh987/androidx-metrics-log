Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a restless little fox. He liked to run and play all day. The fox had many friends, but he also liked to tease them. One sunny day, the little fox was playing with his friend, a brown bear. They were having fun, but the fox started to tease the bear. The bear got sad and walked away. The little fox felt bad for teasing his friend. The next day, the little fox tried to find the bear to say sorry. But he could not find him anywhere. The little fox was very sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and took the fox up in the sky. The bird had seen the fox tease the bear and wanted to teach him a lesson. The fox was scared and promised to be nice to his friends from now on. The bird let the fox go, and he ran to find the bear to say sorry. They became best friends again, and the fox never teased anyone again.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a small colorful bird named Bella. Bella had a best friend, a fluffy bunny with soft fur named Benny. They liked to play together every day. One day, Bella saw Benny weep under a tree. "Why are you sad, Benny?" Bella asked. Benny said, "I lost my favorite toy, and I can't find it." Bella wanted to help her friend, so they went to look for the toy together. They looked behind trees, under rocks, and in the tall grass. Finally, they found the toy near the river. Benny was so happy that he hugged his toy and stopped weeping. Bella and Benny played together and had a fun day. They knew that they would always help each other when they were sad.

Once upon a time, there was a messy horse. The horse lived on a farm with many other animals. The horse loved to play in the mud and get dirty. It made him very happy. One day, a little girl came to visit the farm. She saw the messy horse and gazed at him. The horse gazed back at the little girl. They both smiled at each other. The little girl wanted to help the horse get clean. She got a bucket of water and a brush. The little girl and the horse worked together to clean the mud off. The messy horse was now a clean horse. They became best friends and played together every day.

The sun was shining so brightly and it was a perfect day for a picnic. Mama and Max had set off on a walk and were now at the park. Mama spread out a picnic blanket and she took out sandwiches, snacks and a book. "Max, why don't you sit here and look around while I read this book," said Mama. Max looked around and saw a big tank filled with colorful fish. He could see them swimming around and he was excited to watch. Mama looked up and saw her son watching the fish. She smiled and said, "Max, why don't you go over to the tank and take a closer look at the fish? They look really yummy." Max was excited that he could get closer to the fish and he ran to the tank. He put his hands up to the glass and watched the fish swimming around. They looked so beautiful and he even tried to say hello to them. Mama read a few chapters in the book and then she looked up to see Max still standing in front of the tank. She smiled, picked up the picnic blanket and called to Max, "Time to go. Let's go home and have some yummy snacks." Max said goodbye to the fish and waved goodbye to them. He followed Mama back home and they both enjoyed the snacks mama had prepared. It was a wonderful day and Max had enjoyed his special time at the park.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sarah. She was 3 years old, and she was looking for her cat. She went into the bathroom and it seemed darker than usual. She looked around and saw something smelly. Was it her cat? She said, “Meow!” But there was no answer. Suddenly she heard rustling noises! She looked around but she didn’t see anything. She asked, “Who’s there?” Then she heard a quiet voice saying, “It’s me!” She looked in the corner and saw a big, smelly rat. She screamed and ran out of the bathroom. The rat seemed quite scared and kept apologizing for being in the bathroom. Sarah said she was sorry for being so scared, and the rat told her it was ok. Then Sarah asked if the rat had seen her cat, and he shook his head. She thanked him and ran off to look for her cat. The End.
