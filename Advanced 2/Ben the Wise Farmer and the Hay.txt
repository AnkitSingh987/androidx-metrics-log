Once upon a time, there was a wise old farmer named Ben. Ben had a lot of hay on his farm. One day he was out in his hayfield, pinch by pinch gathering hay for his animals. Suddenly, he heard a small voice coming from nearby. Ben stopped and looked around. He saw a little 3 year old girl standing in the hay. Ben smiled and said, "What are you doing here, young lady?" The little girl replied, "I want to pinch hay like you." Ben looked down at her and said, "Well, that is a wise idea. Here, why don't you help me?" So, the little girl and Ben gathered hay together, pinch by pinch. Soon, the hay was gathered and ready to go. The little girl thanked Ben for the help and ran off, feeling wiser and more adventurous than ever.

Molly and her mommy went to the store. Molly saw a big tomato. Mommy said "Would you like to hold it?" Molly said yes and she held it. It was so big, Molly couldn't even wrap her arms around it. But Molly was so happy and smiled because she was holding the big tomato. Mommy took them home and brought out a plate. She put the tomato on the plate and Molly was so excited. Mommy cut it and said "Look at it. Isn't it beautiful?" Molly looked and said "Yes!" Molly and her mommy ate the big tomato together. Yum! Then Molly said "Mommy, can we go get more tomatoes?" Mommy said "Maybe next time".

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a pretty rose. Sue wanted to have the rose, so she picked it up. The rose was red and smelled nice. Sue was very happy to have the rose. As Sue walked, she met a boy named Tom. Tom saw the rose and said, "I want that rose! Give it to me!" Sue thought Tom was rude, but she didn't want to be mean. So, she said, "Okay, you can have the rose." Tom took the rose and smiled. But then, something unexpected happened! The rose started to talk! The rose said, "Thank you for being nice, Sue. I don't like rude people like Tom." Sue and Tom were very surprised. They never heard a talking rose before! From that day, Tom learned to be nice and not rude. Sue and Tom became good friends and played together every day.

Once there was a boy named Jeff. Jeff loved to jump. Every day he would jump and laugh in the backyard. One day, Jeff's dad brought home a new guitar. Jeff was so excited! He ran to the guitar and started to jump up and down. His dad said, "Jeff, be careful with the guitar. If you break it, you'll be very upset." Jeff didn't listen. He kept jumping and eventually he broke the string on the guitar. Jeff was very upset but he eventually learned his lesson. He put the guitar away and said he would never jump near it again. From then on, Jeff knew it was important to listen and be careful. He learned that if he didn't listen, it could make him very upset.

One day there were two friends, Jane and David. They wanted to go fly a kite. Jane found a kite in her closet. It was flexible and blue. She and David went to the park to fly it. When they got there, the wind was strong. They put the kite up in the air and it started to move. It was having so much fun. Suddenly, the wind got stronger. The kite split in two pieces. Jane and David were sad. But then they decided to go find some string and tape. They used the string and tape to fix the kite. Now it was flexible and strong! They took the kite back to the park and flew it. It was fun and they laughed and smiled.

Once there was a little girl named May. May was a curious person who liked to explore. She found an old mill and wanted to see what was inside. "Wait here," May warned her mom. May went in the mill by herself. Inside the mill was something shiny. May was excited and wanted to attach it to her backpack. But as soon as she touched the object, a loud siren started to sound. May was scared and tried to run out the mill, but the door was locked. May's mom heard the siren and came to help. But the police were already there. They saw May had taken the object from the mill and it was not good. May was taken to jail and never able to go back home.
