Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat mints. One day, Lily saw a new, unknown mint at the store. She asked her mom if she could have it. Her mom said, "No, not today." Lily was sad and began to cry. She really wanted the mint. A kind old lady saw her and asked, "Why are you crying, dear?" Lily told her about the unknown mint. The old lady said, "I will give you the mint if you promise to share it with your friends." Lily agreed and got the mint. She went to her friends and shared it with them. They all enjoyed the mint together. From that day, Lily learned that sharing with friends is better than having something all to yourself.

One day, a little girl woke up with a smile. She immediately rushed to her closet and got her most graceful clothes. She quickly put on her beautiful dress, which made her look like a princess. "Mommy, look at me!!" the little girl said, twirling around. Her mother looked at her daughter's graceful outfit and said, "You look stunning!". Suddenly, the doorbell rang, and the little girl's eyes twinkled. She knew exactly who it was. She ran to the door and opened it. On the other side, a handsome prince was standing there with a smile. "Good morning, Princess!", said the Prince. The little girl was so happy to see the prince, she broke into a graceful dance around him. The prince laughed and said, "That was so graceful! Can I have a dance?" The little girl nodded and giggled, and they started to dance around the room. In the end, the prince and the little girl said goodbye, with a promise to meet again soon. The little girl skipped back into her house, with a big, happy smile.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a brilliant brain. He loved to think and learn new things. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tim wanted to help Sue. He used his brilliant brain to think of where the toy could be. Tim thought it might be under a big tree. He took Sue to the tree and they found the toy. Sue was very happy. Tim and Sue became good friends. They played together every day. Tim always offered to help Sue when she needed it. They had lots of fun using their brains to learn and play.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a little bird named Polly. Polly had a small nest. She was very intelligent. Every day, she would polish her nest to make it clean and shiny. One day, Polly went to find some food. She left her nest for a short time. When she came back, she saw something unexpected. Her nest was gone! Polly looked around and saw another bird with her nest. Polly was sad and asked the other bird, "Why did you take my nest?" The other bird said, "I thought it was mine. I am sorry." Polly was not angry. She said, "Let's be friends and make a new nest together." So, Polly and her new friend worked together to build a big, beautiful nest. They polished it every day. They became best friends and lived happily in their shiny nest. The other birds in the tree saw how happy they were and learned that sharing and being kind was the best way to live.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. The sky was dull and gray. They saw a big puddle from the rain last night. Tim said, "I think there was a flood here, Spot!" As they walked, Tim asked his dog, "Do you like the rain, Spot?" Spot barked as his answer. Tim smiled and knew that Spot liked the rain. They walked and played near the puddle, splashing water on each other. Soon, the rain started to fall again. The dull sky had told them it would rain. Tim and Spot ran home, laughing and splashing in the puddles all the way. They were happy they had their fun walk before the big rain came back.
