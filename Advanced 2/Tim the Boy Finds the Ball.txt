One day, a little boy named Tim went to the port with his mom. At the port, they saw big boats and many people. Tim was very excited to see the boats. While they were walking, Tim saw a ball. He wanted to play, so he kicked the ball very hard. The ball flew far away and hit a big, loud bird. The bird got frightened and flew away, making a loud noise. Tim's mom said, "Oh no, Tim! You scared the bird! Next time, be careful when you kick the ball." Tim felt sad for scaring the bird, but he promised to be more careful next time. They continued to walk and enjoy their day at the port.

Once upon a time, there was a mouse called Milly. She was very special because she loved to build. Every day, Milly would find things to build with and use her clever hands. One day, she decided to build a house for herself and all her friends. Milly gathered up some sticks and twigs and started to build the walls. Then she used her small, sharp teeth to stitch them together to make them fit. Milly kept building until the walls were very hard and strong. She was so happy with her work and it made her the happiest mouse ever! She invited all her friends over to share her new house. They all played together and had so much fun. Every day, they were thankful to Milly for building such a cozy and safe home.

Jimmy was a young boy. One day he was playing in the park when he saw a swing. Jimmy had a great idea! He wanted to try and hang on the swing for as long as possible. So he asked his friend, "Will you help me make my idea happen?" His friend agreed, and they put it into action. But then Jimmy's friend had a stupid idea. She said, "Let's hang from the branches!" Jimmy thought it was a bad idea. He said, "No! That's too dangerous!" But his friend was too excited to listen and started to hang from the branches. Just then the branches snapped and his friend fell to the ground. Jimmy was horrified and ran to help his friend. She was badly hurt but thankfully not seriously. The moral of this story is to always listen to others, but also to think carefully before you act. It is important to weigh up all the risks and benefits of any new idea to make sure it will not do more harm than good.

Once upon a time, there was a weird cat named Tuesday. Tuesday was not like other cats. He did not want to play with toys or chase birds. Tuesday liked to wear a big hat and dance in the rain. One day, Tuesday met a dog named Sam. Sam was sad because he did not have a friend to play with. Tuesday wanted to help Sam. "I want to be your friend," said Tuesday. Sam smiled and they played together all day. Every day, Tuesday and Sam played together. They had so much fun. All the other animals thought they were weird, but they did not care. They were happy to be friends and play together. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a big dog named Max and a small cat named Lily lived in a house. They were good friends. Max was jealous of Lily because she could jump high and disappear into small places. Max had an idea to play a game with Lily. Max said, "Let's play hide and seek, Lily!" Lily agreed and they started the game. Lily was very good at hiding. She would jump high and disappear into small places. Max looked and looked, but he could not find her. He was sad. Then, Max had another idea. He said, "Lily, let's play a new game. We can race to the tree!" Lily agreed and they started the race. Max ran fast and won the race. Lily was happy for Max. They played games all day and had lots of fun.

One day, a curious little cat named Lucy saw a pool. She had never seen a pool before. She wanted to know what it was. Lucy walked closer to the pool to take a look. Lucy saw the water in the pool. She wanted to try to touch it. She put her paw in the water. It was wet and cold. Lucy jumped back, but she still wanted to know more about the pool. Lucy saw a ball near the pool. She had an idea. She pushed the ball into the pool. The ball floated on the water. Lucy was happy. She had fun playing with the ball and the pool.
