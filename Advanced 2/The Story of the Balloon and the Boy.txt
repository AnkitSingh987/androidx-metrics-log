Once upon a time, there was a big, boring party. A man wore a nice suit to the party. He did not like the party, but he had to be there. He saw a little boy sitting alone. The man thought the little boy looked sad. The man wanted to make the little boy happy. He had an idea! He went to the little boy and offered him a big, red balloon. The little boy smiled and took the balloon. The man was happy he could help. But then, something unexpected happened! The big, red balloon started to lift the little boy into the air! The man in the nice suit grabbed the little boy's hand. Together, they flew away from the boring party and had a fun adventure in the sky.

Once, there was a big wall in a small town. The wall was very tall and strong. People in the town liked the wall because it helped prevent bad things from coming in. The wall kept everyone safe and happy. One day, a loud noise came from the other side of the wall. It made the people in the town scared. They did not know what the loud noise was. They wanted to find out and make sure everyone was still safe. A brave girl named Lily decided to climb the wall. She wanted to see what made the loud noise. When she got to the top, she saw a big drum. The wind was making the drum go boom! Lily told everyone in the town that they were safe. The people were happy and thanked the wall for preventing bad things from coming in.

Once upon a time, there was an attractive insect. It had bright colors, like yellow and green, and it flew around happily. One day, it flew into a big, metal thing. The insect had never seen anything like it before. It tried to get out, but the metal thing wouldn't open. It was locked. The insect was so scared! But then, another insect came and showed it how to unlock the metal thing. The attractive insect was so happy! It said goodbye and flew away.

Once upon a time, in a small town, winter was very harsh. Snow was all around. Many animals did not like it. But a kind rabbit named Tim and a tiny bird named Sue were friends. They played in the snow and had fun. One day, while they were playing, they saw a big ball of snow. They did not know what it was. Tim and Sue wanted to pass it to each other. They pushed and pushed. The big ball of snow rolled away. As the big snowball rolled, it got bigger and bigger. Tim and Sue were scared. But then, something unexpected happened. The snowball hit a tree, and all the snow fell off. Under the snow, there was a big, warm cave. Tim, Sue, and their animal friends could stay there in the harsh winter. They were all happy and warm.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, he got very dirty. His fur was full of mud and dirt. Spot's owner, a little girl named Lily, saw him and said, "Oh no, Spot! You are so dirty!" Lily took Spot to the porch. She filled a big tub with water to soak Spot. Lily said, "Spot, it is time to get clean!" She put Spot in the water and began to wash him. Spot liked the water and wagged his tail. After a while, Spot was all clean again. Lily took him out of the water and dried him with a towel. They went back inside the house. Spot was happy to be clean and Lily was happy to have her clean dog back. They played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird. The bird loved to eat yogurt. One day, the bird saw a big bowl of yogurt on the ground. The bird flew down to eat the yogurt. But as it ate, it started to feel funny. The bird tried to fly away, but it could not. It was too full of yogurt. Then, the bird began to fall. It fell and fell until it hit the ground. The bird was very sad. It could not fly anymore because it ate too much yogurt. The bird learned not to eat too much next time.
