Once upon a time, there was a fat caterpillar who loved to cook. One day, he invited his friend to join him for dinner. His friend said: “What are we going to have?” “I’m cooking something special,” said the fat caterpillar. “It’s a surprise!” So they waited and waited until dinner was ready. Then, the fat caterpillar opened the oven. “Look!” he said. “I made a big cake!” His friend gasped. “It looks so delicious!” The fat caterpillar smiled and said: “Let’s eat it together!” They had a wonderful dinner, and the big cake was the best part. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to paint. She had many colors of paint to make pretty pictures. One day, Sue was in a hurry to finish her picture. She wanted to show it to her mom. But she was frustrated. The paint was not going where she wanted it to go. Sue's face turned red. Sue's mom saw her and said, "Don't worry, Sue. Take your time and have fun." Sue smiled and tried again. This time, she painted slowly and carefully. Soon, her picture was done. Sue was happy, and her mom was proud.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. They were called Ali and Bob. Ali was tall and Bob was short. One day, Ali thought of a great idea. He said to Bob, "Let's unite in a photo!" Ali took out a camera and the two of them stood close together, smiling for the photo. When the photo was taken, Ali showed it to Bob. They both loved it and smiled at how tall they looked in the photo. From then on, Ali and Bob were even better friends. They united in the photo and nothing could stand in their way.

Once upon a time there was a brother and sister who lived together in a small house. The brother was called Jack and the sister was called Jill. Jack and Jill loved to play together in the garden and Jack would often bow down to Jill as she was older. This made Jill feel special. One day, Jack's parents had to travel away, leaving Jack feeling very lonely. Jill noticed this and wanted to help her brother. Jill decided to bow to Jack, as he had done to her. Jack looked up in surprise and said "Thank you, sister!" He was so glad that he was no longer alone. From then on, whenever Jack felt lonely, Jill would bow to him and he remembered that he was never alone because he had his loving sister by his side.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. They were normal friends who liked to mix things up. The cat's name was Kitty and the dog's name was Doggy. They liked to play with a ball and run around. "Hey, Doggy, I have some news!" said Kitty. "There is a big box in the house. I think it has toys for us!" Doggy got very excited and they both ran inside to see the box. When they got to the box, they saw a big, mean cat sitting on it. The mean cat did not want to share the toys. Kitty and Doggy were sad and did not know what to do. They wanted to play with the new toys. Kitty said, "Let's mix things up and try to get the toys when the mean cat is not looking!" Doggy agreed and they waited for the right time. When the mean cat went to eat, Kitty and Doggy opened the box and found new balls to play with. They played with their new toys all day and had lots of fun. The mean cat saw them playing and decided to join them. They all became friends and shared the toys. Now, they all play together and have lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug. The bug loved to crawl on the ground. It liked the motion of crawling. One day, the bug went outside. The sun was very bright and it felt harsh on its back. The bug wanted to find a cool place to crawl. As it crawled, it found a big tree. The bug thought the tree would be a good place to crawl. It started to crawl up the tree. The tree was not harsh like the sun. The bug was happy. But then, something unexpected happened! The bug found a big, colorful thing on the tree. The colorful thing started to move! The bug was scared. But, the colorful thing was just a butterfly! The butterfly was nice and they became friends. The bug and the butterfly played together on the tree, and they were both very happy.
