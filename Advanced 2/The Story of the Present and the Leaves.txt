Once upon a time there was a shy girl. She was always helping her friends but never wanted to take credit for it. One day, the girl's friends wanted to give her something for being so kind. They decided to give her a present that was light and small. The girl was happy, but she was a bit worried too. Even though the present wasn't heavy, she still wanted to give something back in return. The girl decided to make something special from sticks, stones and leaves. She even put a little weight in the middle. When she gave it to her friends, they were surprised at how thoughtful and sweet she was. They all hugged her and thanked her. The shy girl smiled fron ear to ear and was happy that her friends cared. The end.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play pretend. One day, they decided to be kings and queens of a big castle. They used pillows and blankets to make a throne and a crown. They also found a big mattress in the basement and dragged it to the living room. "This is our royal bed," Anna said. "Only we can sleep on it." They put the mattress on the floor and climbed on it. They pretended to have a feast and a ball with their stuffed animals. They had a lot of fun. But then, they heard their mom coming home. She opened the door and saw the mess they had made. "Anna! Ben! What are you doing?" she asked. The twins jumped off the mattress and ran to hug her. They were happy to see her, but also a little scared. "We're sorry, mom," Ben said. "We were playing kings and queens." Mom looked at the pillows, the blankets, the mattress, and the toys. She was not happy. "You made a big mess," she said. "You have to clean it up. And you can't use the mattress for playing. It's for sleeping." She told them to kneel down and pick up the things they had scattered. She helped them fold the blankets and put the pillows back. She also helped them carry the mattress back to the basement. "Next time, you can play pretend with something else," she said. "Something more ordinary." Anna and Ben nodded. They were sorry for making mom angry. They loved her very much. They finished cleaning up and gave mom a big hug and a kiss. Then they went to their room and played with their books and crayons. They still liked to play pretend, but they also liked to be good.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Harold and Nathan. They were always unhappy because they never knew when their next adventure would be. One morning they decided to gather up their things and go on their biggest adventure yet! They set out to explore the ugly hills and came across a mysterious creature sleeping in the grass. It was an ugly wake, and the friends were scared. They knew they had to be brave and find out what the wake was up to! So, they gathered courage, tiptoed their way closer, and carefully looked at the creature. It was a giant tortoise! The tortoise saw them and let out a giant yawn. He was so happy to see new friends that he followed them back home! Harold and Nathan were so happy that they had made a new friend. From then on, they would gather things to take on new adventures every day and take their new friend the tortoise along with them.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was fearful of the dark. One night, Tim heard a noise under his bed. He was very scared. Tim called his mom. "Mom, I am scared. Can you come?" His mom came to his room. She looked under the bed. "Look, Tim, it's just a button!" she said. Tim seemed to feel better. From that night on, Tim kept the button by his bed. When he felt scared, he would hold the button tight. It made him feel safe. And Tim was not fearful of the dark anymore.
