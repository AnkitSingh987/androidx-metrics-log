One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She found a pretty pebble on the ground. It was shiny and smooth. Lily liked the pebble a lot. She wanted to store it in her pocket. Lily met a boy named Tim. He saw the pebble and asked, "What is that?" Lily said, "It's a pretty pebble. I found it on the ground." Tim looked at the pebble and said, "I never saw a pebble like that before. I didn't know they could be so pretty." Lily and Tim played with the pebble. They didn't know many things about pebbles, but they liked to learn. They were not ignorant anymore because they learned about the pretty pebble. They both stored the pebble in their pocket and went home, excited to show their families the pretty pebble they found.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in his room with all his toys. His room had a lot of furniture like a bed, a chair, and a table. One day, his mom told him to mind his toys and keep his room neat and clean. Tim wanted to be a healthy and strong boy, so he listened to his mom. But when Tim played with his toys, he sometimes forgot to mind them. He left his toys on the furniture and on the floor. This made his mom very sad. She told Tim that if he didn't mind his toys, they might get lost or broken. Tim didn't want that to happen, so he tried harder to remember. One day, Tim was playing with his favorite toy car. He left it on the chair and went to eat his healthy snack. When he came back, he couldn't find his toy car. Tim was very sad and looked everywhere for it. He finally found it under the chair, and it was not broken. Tim was so happy! From that day on, he always minded his toys and kept his room neat and clean. His mom was proud of him, and Tim grew up to be a healthy and strong boy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys. One day, he found a big box of equipment in his room. The equipment was delicate, so Tim had to be careful. Tim saw a toy car in the box. He wanted to play with it. His mom came into the room and said, "Tim, be gentle with the equipment. It can break if you're not careful." Tim listened to his mom and played with the car gently. Later, Tim's friend, Sam, came over. They played with the equipment together. They were careful and had fun. Then, something happened. Sam dropped a toy, but it didn't break. Tim and Sam learned that being careful with delicate things is important. They were happy they listened to Tim's mom.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little girl named Amy. She loved to play outside in her big yard. One sunny day, Amy found a small pebble that was very pretty. She wanted to keep it forever. Amy put the pebble in her pocket and went to play with her friends. They played tag and hide-and-seek. Amy was having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. When Amy reached into her pocket, the pebble was gone! She started to weep because she lost her pretty pebble. Amy's friends saw her weep and wanted to help. They all looked for the pebble together. Then, Amy's friend Tim found a big, shiny rock. It was even prettier than the pebble! Amy stopped weeping and smiled. She now had a new, shiny rock to keep forever. The day ended with all the friends playing together, happy and cheerful.

One day, a big elephant with a long trunk saw a fancy hat. The hat was on the ground, near a tree. The elephant wanted to wear the hat and have fun. The elephant put the fancy hat on his head. He started to dance and sing. "Look at me, I can dance with my fancy hat!" he said. The other animals saw the elephant and laughed. A little bird saw the elephant and said, "You look so funny with that fancy hat! Can I dance with you?" The elephant smiled and said, "Of course! Let's dance together!" So, the elephant and the bird danced and had lots of fun.
