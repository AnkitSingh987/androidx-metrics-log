Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy rock. The rock could talk. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the rock. He saw a sign that said, "Listen to the rock." Tim asked the rock, "What do you want to say?" The rock said, "I am very heavy, and I need help to move." Tim tried to push the heavy rock, but it was too hard. So, he asked his friend, Sam, to help. Together, they pushed the rock. The rock was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim and Sam." From that day on, Tim and Sam always listened to signs and helped others when they could. And the heavy rock became their good friend.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the doctor with her mom. Mia was wearing a red dress. At the doctor's office, Mia saw a big machine. The doctor told her it was an x-ray machine. Mia was curious and wanted to discover what the x-ray machine could do. The doctor said it could see inside her body. Mia was excited and a little scared. But she was brave and let the doctor take an x-ray of her. When the doctor looked at the x-ray, he saw something strange. Mia had swallowed her favorite red toy! The doctor told Mia and her mom that the toy was safe and would come out soon. Mia and her mom were surprised, but happy that everything was okay.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful girl named Sue. Sue loved to draw on big pages. One day, she found a magic crayon. She was very happy and excited. Sue started to draw a big sun on the page. But something strange happened. The sun began to shrink! It became very small. Sue was surprised and curious. She tried to draw a big tree. But the tree also started to shrink! Sue knew the magic crayon was special. She used it to make many small things. Sue and the magic crayon were very helpful to her friends. They all had fun and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a polite little girl named Mia. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, dark clouds came and it started to rain. Mia was sad because she could not play outside anymore. Mia's mom saw her sad face and had an idea. She gave Mia a big umbrella and said, "Let's go for a walk in the rain!" Mia smiled and put on her rain boots. They went outside and walked in the rain. Mia felt the rain on her face and laughed. As they walked, Mia saw a big puddle. She pointed at it and said, "Look, Mom!" Then, Mia jumped into the puddle and splashed water everywhere. They both laughed and played in the rain until the sun came out again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a sister named Emma. Lucy and Emma loved to play outside in the warm sun. They liked to run and jump in the soft green grass. One day, Lucy asked Emma a question. "Emma, do you like the warm sun?" Emma answered, "Yes, Lucy! I love the warm sun. It makes me happy and it feels nice on my skin." Lucy and Emma played outside all day. They laughed and had fun together. When it was time to go inside, they were tired but happy. They knew they would play again in the warm sun the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside and watch the sunset. One day, she saw a pretty bird with a hurt wing. Lily felt sad for the bird and wanted to help it. Lily decided to hold the bird gently in her hands. She was very careful, and the bird did not feel scared. Lily took the bird to her mom, who helped her fix the bird's wing. They used a soft cloth to wrap around the wing so it could heal. As the days went by, the bird got better and better. Lily was grateful that she could help her new friend. The bird was grateful too and sang a happy song for Lily every day. Soon, the bird could fly again, and it flew off into the sunset. Lily learned that it is important to be kind and help others when they are hurt. And every time she saw a sunset, she remembered her bird friend and the good she had done.
