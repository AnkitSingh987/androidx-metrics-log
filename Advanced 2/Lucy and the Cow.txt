Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy went to the store. She was looking for a cow. "Do you have a cow to buy?" Lucy asked the storekeeper. The storekeeper smiled. "Yes, I have a very noisy cow," he said. "She is perfect!" Lucy and the storekeeper drove to the farm to get the cow. When they arrived, Lucy saw a big, noisy cow. She was so excited! The storekeeper smiled again. "Would you like to buy the cow?" he asked. Lucy nodded. She was so happy to have the noisy cow. She thanked the storekeeper and she took the cow home. Lucy and the noisy cow had a lot of fun together. They played in the fields and they went on long walks. Lucy loved her noisy cow very much!

Once upon a time, there was a pretty goose. This goose lived near a big tree with many other animals. They were all good friends and liked to play together. One day, the pretty goose and her friends wanted to find a new place to play. They walked and walked until they found a big lake. The pretty goose was very happy because she loved to swim. But her friends could not swim like her. The pretty goose had an idea to unite her friends. She found a big leaf and asked her friends to sit on it. Then, she pushed the leaf into the water and swam with them. All the animals had fun in the lake together. They were happy that the pretty goose helped them unite and play at the lake.

Once upon a time, there was a star named Twinkle. Twinkle lived high up in the sky. Every night, Twinkle would shine brightly for all to see. One day, Twinkle saw a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to feed the birds in her yard. Twinkle felt jealous. Twinkle wanted to play with Lily too. So, Twinkle came down from the sky to talk to Lily. "Hi, Lily!" Twinkle said. "Can I play with you?" Lily looked up and saw Twinkle. She was happy to see the star. "Yes, Twinkle! You can help me feed the birds," Lily said. Twinkle and Lily spent the day feeding the birds together. They had lots of fun. When it was time for Twinkle to go back to the sky, Lily said, "Bye, Twinkle! Come play with me again soon!" Twinkle smiled and went back to the sky. Now, Twinkle was not jealous anymore. Twinkle had a new friend in Lily, and they played together whenever they could.

One day, a boy named Tim found a long rope. He wanted to play with it. He saw a big tree and thought, "I can use the rope to swing!" Tim tied the rope to the tree and started to swing. He was having so much fun. Then, his friend Sue came and wanted to play too. Tim said, "Sue, come swing with me!" As Sue started to swing, something unexpected happened. The rope began to split. Tim and Sue got scared. They didn't want to fall. Just then, a big bird flew down and caught the rope with its beak. It held the rope until Tim and Sue got off safely. They were so happy and thanked the bird. They learned that the rope was dead and couldn't be used anymore. But they were glad they were safe and had a new friend, the big bird.

One day, a little girl named Lily was troubled. Her toy car was broken and she didn't know how to repair it. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Let's fix the car together." Lily and her mom worked together to repair the toy car. They put the wheels back on and made sure it could go fast. When they were done, Lily was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Mom!" That night, after dinner, Lily's mom made a special dessert. It was a big ice cream sundae with lots of yummy things on top. Lily and her mom ate the dessert together, and they talked about how much fun they had fixing the toy car.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful robot. The robot had many friends who would admire him. One day, the robot met a little girl named Lucy. "Hi, robot!" said Lucy. "I like your colors. Can you play with me?" The robot nodded and they played together. They played with toys, ran around, and laughed a lot. The robot and Lucy were very happy. But then, it started to rain. The colorful robot was not good in the rain. The rain made the robot stop working. Lucy was sad and cried. The robot could not play anymore. The end.
