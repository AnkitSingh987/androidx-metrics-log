Lily was a popular girl in her class. She had many friends who liked to play with her. She was good at sharing, singing and drawing. She always smiled and said nice things. One day, Lily felt a pain in her tummy. She wanted to go to the toilet, but she did not want to miss the story time. She thought she could wait until the story was over. She tried to listen to the teacher, but the pain got worse and worse. She raised her hand and said, "Teacher, I need to go to the toilet, please." The teacher nodded and said, "OK, Lily, you can go. But hurry back, we are almost done." Lily got up and ran to the door. She hoped she could make it in time. But as she reached the door, she felt something wet and warm on her legs. She looked down and saw that she had peed in her pants. She felt ashamed and scared. She did not know what to do. She started to cry. The other kids saw what happened and laughed at her. They pointed and said, "Lily peed in her pants! Lily peed in her pants!" Lily felt more ashamed and scared. She wished she had gone to the toilet sooner. She wished she could disappear.

One day, a young boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big cone on the ground. Tim wanted to play with the cone. He picked it up and said, "I will strike the cone and see what happens." Tim's friend, Sue, came to the park too. She saw Tim with the cone. "What are you doing with the cone?" she asked. Tim said, "I will strike it and see what happens." Sue wanted to watch. Tim hit the cone with his hand. The cone fell over. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew out from under the cone. Tim and Sue were very surprised. They did not know the bird was there. The bird said, "Thank you for helping me get out." Tim and Sue were happy they could help the bird. They played together in the park all day.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. On this tree, there was a jar. The jar seemed very high up. A little boy saw the jar and wanted to get it down. The boy tried to climb the tree, but he was not tall enough. He felt sad. Then, a big bird came and saw the boy. The bird wanted to help the boy get the jar. The bird flew up and got the jar. The boy was happy. But when the bird gave the jar to the boy, something unexpected happened. The jar was not a jar at all! It was a hat! The boy laughed and put the hat on his head. He thanked the bird, and they became good friends.

Micky was a brave and curious little boy. One day, he found a big mop in the corner of his living room. He decided to give it a try, so he split the mop into two pieces. But suddenly it started to move and he heard a voice coming from it! Micky was very scared, but he followed the sound. It was coming from a dead butterfly! He had never seen one before and he wanted to know why it wasn't flying around like the other butterflies he had seen. Micky asked the butterfly why it was dead and the butterfly said, "I split my wings while flying and I couldn't fly anymore." Micky was very sad to hear this and he kindly asked the butterfly if he could use his mop to help it move around. The butterfly said, "Yes! That would be great! Thank you!" Micky was so happy to be able to help. He split the mop into two pieces and carefully lifted the butterfly in between them. Every day after that, Micky and the butterfly became friends and they would spend time together.

Once upon a time, there were two friends who liked to decorate. One day, they decided to decorate the nearby meadow. The first friend said: "Let's make the meadow beautiful!" The second friend replied: "Let's put up some decorations!" The first friend said: "We can hang up some ribbons!" The second friend said: "Yes, and we can make it sparkly!" The two friends worked together to make the meadow beautiful. They hung ribbons, glitter, and flowers. Suddenly, they saw a grumpy sheep watching them. The grumpy sheep said: "What are you two doing?" The first friend said: "We are decorating the meadow!" The sheep seemed to like their work and the two friends were happy. In the end, the meadow was decorated and everyone was happy!
