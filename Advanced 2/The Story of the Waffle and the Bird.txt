Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a kind, old man. He loved to make waffles for breakfast every morning. The waffles were yummy and made everyone happy. One sunny day, the old man made a big waffle. He put it on a plate to cool down. But a cheeky bird saw the waffle and wanted it. The bird flew down and took the waffle away. The old man was sad. He wanted to release the waffle from the bird. He thought and thought. Then he had an idea. He made more waffles and put them outside. The bird saw the waffles and let the big waffle go. The old man got his big waffle back and everyone was happy again.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She wore her favorite skirt. It was red and had pretty flowers on it. Amy was very happy. As Amy walked, she saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was impatient. He wanted to play with his toy car, but it was stuck in a tree. Amy wanted to help Tim. Amy said, "I see your toy car. Let's try to get it down." Tim smiled, and they both tried to reach the car. They jumped and jumped, but they couldn't get it. Then, Amy had an idea. She took off her skirt and threw it into the air. The skirt caught the toy car, and it fell down. Tim was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Amy!" They played with the toy car and had a fun day together.

Alice was a very proud girl. She loved father and mother very much, and they loved her too! Every day, Alice would wake up, whistle with glee, and eat breakfast. She especially loved broccoli! One day, her mummy said to her "Come Alice! Let's go to the park and play!" Alice was so excited and she whistled happily as she ran outside. At the park, Alice was so proud of herself. She ran and skipped and jumped, and she even managed to climb the slippery slide all by herself. Everyone clapped and cheered, and Alice felt like the proudest girl in the world! Alice smiled at her mummy and said "Can we come back again tomorrow?". Her mummy nodded and whistled in agreement. It was the best day for Alice and she went to sleep with a big, proud smile on her face.

Mommy said it was time to clean the house. She walked to the machine and opened it up. Inside were all the nice and clean things. She said they needed to be put away. "But why?" asked the little girl. Mommy smiled and said, "It's a good idea to keep things tidy. That way it's clear and easy to find what we want." The little girl agreed and went around the house picking up things. She put them carefully in the machine and she closed the door. The machine was humming and shaking. Suddenly, a noise came from the machine and it started to smoke. "Oh no! Close the door, quickly!" shouted Mommy. The little girl ran to the doorway and closed the door. But it was too late. They could hear a loud bang from inside the machine and smoke filled the room. The little girl didn't understand what just happened. Mommy looked very sad. She said, "The machine is broken and all the things are gone." The little girl was very sad. And she was sadder still when Mommy said it was time to start cleaning all over again.

One day, a little bunny named Sam went to the forest. He wanted to find some berries to eat. He saw a big bush with red berries. Sam thought they looked yummy. As he was eating the berries, he heard a tiny voice. It was an adorable bird named Tina. Tina said, "Sam, you need to escape! A big, mean fox is coming!" Sam got scared and ran away very fast. Sam and Tina escaped and found a safe place to hide. They became best friends and played together every day. They also found more berries to eat and shared them with each other.
