Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max lived in a big lab. In the lab, there were many things to see and do. Max liked to play with his ball and run around. One day, Max met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a big dog, but he was very kind. Max and Sam played together in the lab. They played with the ball and ran around all day long. As they played, Max said, "Sam, mind the bitter taste!" Sam tasted the bitter thing and made a funny face. They both laughed and played some more. Max and Sam became best friends in the lab. They played together every day, and they were very happy.

Once there was a little girl. She wanted to explore her house. She decided to stand up and start walking. When she reached the top of the stairs, she saw an attic. She opened the door to the attic and peeked inside. She saw lots of books and boxes. She was excited to explore the attic. The girl stood at the bottom of the stairs, but she was too small to reach the first step. She whispered softly and was gentle trying her best. Then, she felt a gentle push from behind, and she started to stand up. She made it all the way up to the attic door. The girl went inside the attic. She opened the boxes and found a snow globe. She shook it, and the white snowflakes flew all around. Then, she put it back and started to look for something else. She found a gentle little mouse and started to play with it. The girl was so happy, exploring the attic. She played with the mouse and opened all the boxes. After a while, she said goodbye to the mouse, and started walking back home. She was so proud of herself for standing up and exploring the attic.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red rug. This rug was in the middle of the room. A cat and a dog lived in the house with the rug. The cat was very silly. The dog was very nice. One day, the cat and the dog both wanted to sit on the rug at the same time. The cat said, "I want to sit on the rug!" The dog said, "No, I want to sit on the rug!" They did not know what to do. They thought and thought. Then, they decide to share the rug. The cat sat on one side, and the dog sat on the other side. They were both happy. The cat and the dog learned to share and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a nice little girl that loved sailing. Every day she would get out her little boat and sail around the lake near her house. One sunny afternoon, the little girl was sailing when she saw something strange at the far side of the lake - a bookshelf! She thought it was strange because bookshelves usually belong indoors. She happily sailed up to it and began to explore. Unfortunately, when she got closer, she saw that someone was already there. It was a grumpy old man who was guarding the bookshelf. She asked him politely if she could explore it, but the man shouted at her and said she had to leave. The little girl was worried and decided to talk to her parents. Her parents told her that it was a kind thing to do to share the bookshelf with the old man. So, the next day, the little girl took a nice blanket, some books and some snacks with her when she went sailing. When she got to the bookshelf, she smiled at the grumpy old man and told him she had brought something for him too. The old man was so surprised he softened and let her explore the bookshelf. They even enjoyed the snacks together. From that day onwards, the little girl and the grumpy old man shared the bookshelf and sailed around the lake together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to run very fast. He was the fastest boy in his town. One day, he met a girl named Sue. Sue was also very fast. They decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. Tim and Sue started the race. They ran as fast as they could. Tim was in front, but Sue was right behind him. They did not quit. They wanted to win the race. They passed a big tree, a small house, and a little dog. The dog barked at them as they ran by. As Tim and Sue got closer to the finish line, they saw a man with a trade. The man was selling ice cream. Tim and Sue both wanted ice cream, so they stopped running. They decided to quit the race and have ice cream instead. They traded their fast running for a cold, sweet treat. Tim and Sue became good friends and enjoyed their ice cream together.
