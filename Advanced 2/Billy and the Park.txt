Billy was walking in the park on a Sunday morning. He was wearing a white shirt and when he saw a large, black dog, he trembled with fear. "Mommy, the doggy looks scary," he said. Billy's mother, who was also walking with him, smiled. "It's ok, Billy. He's friendly, I promise," she said. Billy wasn't sure, so he reached out and carefully touched the doggy's back. To his surprise, the doggy licked his hand and wagged his tail. Billy smiled. He felt so brave now that he was no longer trembling. He looked at his mom and said, "Let's keep walking, Mommy!" They both smiled and went on their merry way, enjoying the beautiful Sunday morning together.

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Tom. Tom liked to help people and make them feel safe. One day, Tom saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and wanted to come down. Tom said, "Don't worry, kitty. I will help you." He put up his big ladder and climbed up. He got the cat and carried it down. The cat was happy and safe now. Tom's friend, Sam, saw what happened. Sam said, "Good job, Tom! You are a great fireman." Tom felt happy to complete his task. Now, the cat and the people were safe and Tom was proud to be a fireman.

One day, a playful cat named Kitty went to a big tree. Kitty loved to climb trees and play with her friends. She saw a little bird sitting on a branch and wanted to play with it. So, Kitty requested, "Little bird, can we play together?" The little bird looked at Kitty and said, "Okay, but we must be careful not to get into trouble." Kitty agreed, and they started playing. They played hide and seek, and Kitty climbed up and down the tree while the bird flew around. As they were playing, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the little bird away from the tree. Kitty was worried and wanted to help her friend. She quickly climbed down the tree and found the little bird on the ground. Kitty was happy to see her friend was okay, but then she noticed something strange. The little bird had changed colors! It was now a beautiful, bright rainbow bird. The bird said, "Thank you for helping me, Kitty. I am a magic bird, and now that you have saved me, I can grant you one wish." Kitty thought for a moment and then requested, "I wish that we can all play together without getting into trouble." The magic bird smiled and granted Kitty's wish. From that day on, all the animals in the forest could play together without any trouble.

Once upon a time, there was a duck who lived in a pond. He was a graceful swimmer and a happy duck. He was the symbol of joy in the pond. One day, the duck found something rot in the pond. He was curious, so he picked it up and ate it. It was so sweet. The duck didn't stop there. He kept on eating the roted stuff. The other animals in the pond were worried about the duck. They warned him that if he kept on eating rotted stuff, he would get sick. But the duck did not listen. He kept finding more rotted stuff and kept eating it. One day, the duck was so sick he couldn't swim. He was too weak to move. The duck knew he should have listened to the other animals in the pond. The bad ending of the story is that the duck died from eating the rotted stuff. The moral of the story is to always heed advice and warnings from others, even when it seems tempting not to.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big park. Many kids loved to play near the tree. One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were playing near the tree. They wanted to know how tall the tree was. So, they decided to measure it. Tim said, "Sue, let's use my long rope to measure the tree." Sue agreed, and they started to wrap the rope around the tree. They tried to reach the top, but the tree was very tall. They could not reach the top. A big wind came and pushed the tall tree. The tree fell down on Tim and Sue's rope. The rope broke, and they could not measure the tree anymore. Tim and Sue were sad because they could not find out how tall the tree was.
