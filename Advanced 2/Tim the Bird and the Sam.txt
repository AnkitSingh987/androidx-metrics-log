Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a bird named Tim. Tim loved to travel. He would fly high up in the sky and visit many places. One day, while flying, Tim saw a wire. He wanted to rest, so he sat on the wire. On the wire, Tim met another bird named Sam. Sam looked sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, Sam?" Sam said, "My friend is dead. I miss him a lot." Tim wanted to help Sam feel better. So, Tim asked Sam to travel with him. They flew to many places together and had a lot of fun. Sam started to feel happy again. They became best friends and always sat on the wire together, sharing stories of their travels.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a fast rabbit named Remy. Remy had a friend, a blue bird named Bella. They liked to play together every day. One sunny day, Remy and Bella were playing near the river. Remy said, "Bella, I want to jump over the river. Can you help me?" Bella said, "Yes, Remy. Trust me, I will help you." Bella held Remy's hand and they jumped together. They made it to the other side of the river. Remy was very happy and hugged Bella. "Thank you, Bella!" Remy said. From that day on, Remy and Bella became best friends and always trusted each other.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely boy named Tim. Tim had a red scooter. He loved to ride his scooter up and down the street. One day, Tim saw a girl with a blue scooter. Her name was Sue. Tim and Sue became friends and rode their scooters together. One day, Tim and Sue were hungry. They wanted to cook something yummy. They went to Tim's house and asked his mom for help. Tim's mom said, "Let's cook a big pizza!" Tim and Sue were very happy. They put cheese, sauce, and toppings on the pizza. After the pizza was cooked, Tim and Sue sat down to eat. They shared the pizza with their families. Everyone loved the pizza. Tim and Sue were not lonely anymore. They had fun riding scooters and cooking together. They were best friends.

The clown wanted to leave the park. He was feeling guilty. He had stayed too long. He tried to sneak away, but he couldn't. Two children saw him. They screamed when they saw his face. The clown felt even guiltier. Then, a big man came over. He put his arm around the clown. The big man said it was OK to leave. The clown was thankful. He waved goodbye to the children and the big man. Then the clown left the park, feeling happy and not guilty anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ann. She had a pretty dress with a big, original bow on it. One day, the bow came off and Ann was very sad. Ann went to her mom and said, "Mom, can you help me? My bow is off my dress." Her mom looked at the bow and saw that the thread was broken. She knew she had to show Ann how to fix it. Mom took a new thread and showed Ann how to sew the bow back on the dress. Ann tried her best, but it was hard. She was getting upset. Then, mom said, "Don't worry, Ann. I will help you." Together, they sewed the bow back on the dress. Ann was so happy and proud of her work. The dress was as beautiful as before, and Ann learned a new skill from her mom. They hugged each other and smiled, knowing that they could fix anything together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a funny face that made everyone laugh. Lily liked to play with her friends and make them happy. One day, Lily saw her friend Sam frown. She wanted to make Sam happy again. So, she made her funny face and danced around. Sam started to laugh and his frown went away. Lily and Sam played together all day. They had so much fun that they forgot about the frown. They learned that being funny and happy can make a sad friend feel better.
