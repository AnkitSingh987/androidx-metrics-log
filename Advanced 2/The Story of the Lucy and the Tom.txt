Once upon a time, there was a big school called a university. Many people went there to learn. One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to go see the university. When Lucy got there, she saw a man named Tom. Tom was nice and helped people learn. Tom saw that Lucy needed help. He kneeled down to talk to her. Lucy asked if she could learn at the university. Tom said the university was for big people, but there were other places for little kids like Lucy. Lucy was happy to know there were places available for her to learn. She thanked Tom and went to find a school just right for her.

One day, a girl named Sue went to play by the deep lake. She saw ice on the water. Sue wanted to mix the ice and water to see what would happen. She took a big stick and started to mix the ice and water. A frog jumped out from the ice and said, "Hi! I am Fred the frog. Thank you for mixing the ice and water." Sue was very surprised. She did not know frogs could talk. Sue said, "Hi, Fred! I am happy to help you." Fred the frog told Sue that he was stuck in the ice for a long time. When Sue mixed the ice and water, she set Fred free. Sue and Fred became good friends. They played by the deep lake every day.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a chubby bunny named Binky. Binky loved to hop and play all day. One day, Binky saw a big ball. He wanted to touch it, but it was too far away. Binky had a friend named Tommy the turtle. Tommy was very smart. Tommy told Binky, "I can help you touch the ball. I have a plan." Binky was excited to hear Tommy's plan. Tommy whispered the plan to Binky. The next day, Binky and Tommy went to the big ball. Tommy had a long stick. He told Binky to shoot the stick at the ball. Binky was scared, but he trusted Tommy. Binky took a deep breath and shot the stick at the ball. The stick hit the ball, and the ball rolled closer to Binky. Binky was so happy! He could finally touch the big ball. Binky and Tommy laughed and played with the ball all day. They were the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a big mattress in her room. She was very happy and jumped on it. It was fun and she felt cheerful. She played on it all day long. Lily's mom came into the room and saw the mattress. "Lily, did you say thank you for the mattress?" Mom asked. Lily thought for a moment and then said, "No, I forgot. I will say thank you now." Lily went to her dad and said, "Thank you for the mattress, Dad. It is so fun!" Her dad smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I'm glad you like it." From that day on, Lily always remembered to say thank you when she got something new. And she played on the mattress every day, always feeling cheerful and happy.

One day, a girl named Amy found a telephone in the park. She did not know who it belonged to. She wanted to find the owner because it was the fair thing to do. She asked her friend, Tom, to help her. They went to the park and asked people, "Is this your telephone?" A man said, "No, but thank you for asking." A woman said, "No, it is not mine, but good luck finding the owner." Suddenly, a dog ran up to Amy and Tom. The dog had a note on its collar. The note said, "I belong to the person who lost the telephone." Amy and Tom were surprised! They never thought a dog could help them find the owner. They followed the dog to a little boy. The boy was so happy to have his telephone back. He said, "Thank you for finding my telephone and my dog!" Amy and Tom were happy they could help.
