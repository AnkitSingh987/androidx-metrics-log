One day, a little boy named Tim saw a pale raven. The raven was sad. It lost its way and could not find its family. Tim wanted to help the raven. He gave it some food and water. The raven was happy and said "thank you" to Tim. Tim and the raven walked in the woods. They looked for the raven's family. They saw many trees and rocks. They walked and walked, but could not find the family. Tim felt sad for the raven. Then, something unexpected happened. The raven started to change! It grew big and turned into a man! The man was a magic friend. He said "thank you" to Tim for helping him. The magic man showed Tim the way back home. Tim was happy and thanked the magic man. They became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a box with a lock. Inside the box, she kept her favorite toy, a small teddy bear. Amy loved her teddy bear very much. One day, Amy's friend Tom came over to play. Tom saw the box with the lock and wanted to see what was inside. Amy did not want to show Tom her teddy bear because it was very special to her. Tom was sad, but he had a plan. Tom thought he could send a letter to Amy's mom and ask her for the key to the lock. He wrote a letter and gave it to Amy's mom. She read the letter and gave Tom the key. Tom was successful in getting the key. He opened the lock and took the teddy bear out of the box. When Amy saw that Tom had her teddy bear, she was very sad and angry. She asked her mom why she gave Tom the key. Her mom said she thought it would be okay. Amy cried and cried because her special teddy bear was not a secret anymore. This story has a bad ending because Amy lost her secret and was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a hungry little boy named Tim. He had a big bag with him. Tim wanted to eat, so he sat under a tree to unpack his bag. As he was unpacking his bag, a sneaky thief saw him. The thief was hungry too. He wanted to take Tim's food. So, he came up with a plan. The thief dressed up like a funny clown. He made Tim laugh and play. While Tim was not looking, the thief took some food from the bag. Tim saw the thief and said, "Hey, that's mine!" The thief felt bad and gave the food back. Tim was kind and shared his food with the hungry thief. They ate together and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lively dog named Spot. Spot loved to carry a red ball in his mouth. Today, Spot wanted to play with his friend, Sam the cat. Spot went to Sam's house with his red ball. He saw Sam sitting on a big rock. Spot thought, "I will hide the ball and see if Sam can find it." So, Spot hid the ball behind a tree. Sam saw Spot hide the ball and thought, "I will surprise Spot and carry the ball back to him." Sam quietly went behind the tree, picked up the ball, and carried it back to Spot. Spot was very happy to see Sam with the ball. They played together all day, carrying the ball and having lots of fun. Spot and Sam were the best of friends, and they loved their lively game of carry the ball.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a kind doctor. The doctor was very patient. Every day, he helped people who were sick. He had many regular visitors who came to see him. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the doctor. She was feeling sick. She asked the doctor, "Can you help me, please?" The doctor answered, "Of course, Lucy! Let me see what is wrong." The doctor checked Lucy and gave her some medicine. Lucy said, "Thank you, doctor!" The doctor answered, "You're welcome! Now, go home and rest." Lucy went home and soon felt better. She was very happy that the patient doctor helped her.
