One day, a polite cat named Tom went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tom saw a big tree and decided to hang from a branch. He was having so much fun. Tom saw a little dog named Max. Max was looking for a shelter to hide from the rain that was coming. Tom asked, "Do you want to play with me, Max?" Max said, "I need to find a shelter first. The rain is coming soon." Tom thought and said, "I know! We can use the big tree as a shelter!" Max was happy and they both played under the tree. Soon, the rain came, but they were safe and dry under the tree. They had found the perfect shelter and became best friends.

Jimmy was feeling very gloomy. He sat in his bedroom, looking sadly at the window. Outside the world seemed so peaceful and calm. He wished he could join in the fun. Just then, he heard a voice calling his name. It was Grandma! She was standing outside his bedroom window, holding a big book. She was calling out to him, "Jimmy! Come look at the book I brought you!" Jimmy didn't hesitate. He ran to the window and opened it. Grandma handed him the book, and his eyes lit up. It was a book full of exciting pictures and stories! He thanked Grandma, and she smiled. Together, they read the book for the rest of the day. The gloomy feelings were forgotten, and Jimmy was filled with joy.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a rich man named Tom. He lived in a big hut. Tom was not happy. He would frown all day. One day, a little boy named Tim saw Tom frown. Tim asked, "Why are you sad?" Tom said, "I have a big hut, but I am not happy." Tim had an idea. He told Tom, "Let's play and laugh together." Tom and Tim played all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Tom stopped frowning and became happy. From that day on, Tom learned that playing with friends made him happy. He shared his big hut with everyone in the village. Tom and Tim became best friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a weak little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree near a tall pole. He liked to sing, but he could not fly very high. One day, Tim saw a big cat near the pole. The cat wanted to nap in the warm sun. Tim was scared. He wanted to fly to the top of the pole, but he was too weak. Then, a little bug came and told Tim a secret. The bug said, "You can be strong if you try hard and don't give up." Tim tried very hard and flew to the top of the pole. The cat woke up and saw Tim. The cat tried to climb the pole but it was too slippery. The cat fell down and ran away. Tim learned that even a weak bird can be strong if they try hard and never give up.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a toy model of a cat. She loved her cat model very much. Every day, she played with her cat model and had lots of fun. One day, Amy saw a big, delicious cake on the table. She wanted to eat the cake, but it was too high for her to reach. So, she had an idea. She decided to stretch her arms up high like a tall tree. Amy stretched and stretched, and finally, she could touch the cake. She took a piece and shared it with her cat model. They both enjoyed the delicious cake together. And from that day on, Amy knew that if she stretched, she could reach anything she wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a big wardrobe in a peaceful room. The wardrobe was full of beautiful clothes. One day, a little girl named Lily arrived at the room. She saw the wardrobe and wanted to wear the pretty clothes. Lily opened the wardrobe and said, "Wow, these clothes are so nice!" She put on a pretty dress and felt very happy. Her friend, Tom, came into the room and saw Lily. Tom said, "Lily, those clothes are not yours. You should ask before you take something." Lily felt sad and took off the dress. She put it back in the wardrobe. She learned that it is important to ask before taking something that does not belong to her. The room stayed peaceful and Lily and Tom played together happily.
