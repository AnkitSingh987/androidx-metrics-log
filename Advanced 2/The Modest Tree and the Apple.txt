Once upon a time, there was a modest tree. The tree had a red apple. One day, a boy came to the tree. He wanted to eat the apple. The boy tried to get the apple, but it was too high. A little bird saw the boy. The bird wanted to help. The bird flew up and tried to start pushing the apple down. The boy was very happy. He thought the apple would fall soon. But something unexpected happened. The apple started to grow bigger and bigger. The boy and the bird were very surprised. The apple was now too big for them to eat. They decided to share the big apple with their friends. And they all had a fun day eating the big apple.

Once upon a time there was a deer. He was very ordinary, but he was happy. Every day he would lock himself in the barn. The barn was very cozy, and he liked it there. One day, the deer heard some loud noises coming from the window. He opened the barn door and peeked out. He saw an ordinary little boy! The boy was looking in the window at the deer. The deer thought the boy was friendly, so he said hello. The boy was so excited to see the deer that he ran away! The deer chased him, and eventually the deer caught up. He wanted to be friends with the boy. So, the deer asked if he could lock the door. The boy agreed and helped the deer lock the door. The deer was so happy. Finally he had a friend who he could lock in the barn with him. They spent hours in the barn, playing together and enjoying each other's company. The deer and the boy were best friends. They were both very happy every time they locked themselves in the barn together.

Once upon a time, there was a big race. Many animals came to run. A fast rabbit and a slow turtle were in the race too. The rabbit was very impatient. He wanted to win the race right away. The turtle was slow but he did not give up. The race started and the rabbit ran very fast. The turtle walked slow but kept going. The rabbit saw a cliff and thought he could jump over it. But he was scared to jump. The rabbit stopped and thought of what to do. The turtle came and saw the rabbit by the cliff. The turtle had an idea. He walked around the cliff and kept going. The rabbit saw the turtle and decided to follow him. They both finished the race together. The animals learned that being impatient is not always good and that it is better to work together.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet pumpkin named Pumpy. Pumpy lived in a big garden with many other pumpkins. Every day, Pumpy would wake up when the sun came out and play with his friends. One day, Pumpy met a little girl named Lily. Lily saw the sweet pumpkin and said, "Hello, Pumpy! Do you want to be my friend?" Pumpy was happy and nodded. They played together in the garden and had lots of fun. As the sun started to go away, Lily knew it was time to go home. She gave Pumpy a big hug and said, "Goodbye, Pumpy! I will come back tomorrow to play with you again." Pumpy smiled and went to sleep, excited to see his new friend the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was going on a big train with his mom. He was so excited! They were going to visit his grandma, who lived in a distant place. Before they got on the train, Tim's mom gave him a ticket. She said, "Tim, this is your ticket. You need to keep it safe and remain with me on the train. Can you do that?" Tim nodded and held the ticket tight in his hand. As they rode the train, Tim looked out the window and saw many things. He saw cows, trees, and big fields. Tim was happy to be on the train with his mom, and he remained close to her the whole time. When they reached grandma's house, Tim still had his ticket. He had kept it safe, just like his mom asked.
