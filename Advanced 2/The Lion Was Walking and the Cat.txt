One day, a big lion was walking in a distant land. He saw a small cat near a pass. The cat was sad because she could not go to the other side. The lion went to the cat and asked, "Why are you sad, little cat?" The cat said, "I want to go to the other side, but I am scared." The lion said, "I will help you. I will roar and scare away anything that is in our way." The lion roared very loud, and they walked to the pass. The cat was happy and said, "Thank you, big lion!" The lion said, "You're welcome, little cat. Always help others when they need it." The moral of the story is to be kind and help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a popular island. Many people liked to visit the island. It was a happy place with lots of sun and sand. The island had pretty birds and friendly animals. One day, a naughty bird decided to steal. The bird took a shiny toy from a little girl. The little girl was sad, and everyone on the island was upset. They wanted the bird to give the toy back. A kind boy saw the sad girl and decided to help. He talked to the bird and asked it to give the toy back. The bird felt bad for what it did and returned the toy to the girl. Everyone on the island was happy again, and the bird promised not to steal anymore. The island stayed a popular and happy place for all to visit.

Once upon a time, there was a family of four who loved to go on picnics. On this day they decided to go to the park near noon. All the family members rushed to get ready and loaded the car with a large picnic basket. As they drove off, dad asked, “Does anyone have the scissors?” Mum replied, “No, I think we left them at home. I’ll have to cut the sandwiches with my knife.” Dad then said, “That’s alright. It will be hard to cut them, but I’m sure we’ll manage”. When they got to the park at noon, Mum used her knife to cut the sandwiches into small pieces and the family enjoyed their picnic together. Everyone was so happy that they agreed to go on a picnic more often!

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. She was only 3 years old, but she knew what it meant to be brave and trust. One day Jane and her dad decided to go for a walk in the woods. Everything seemed peaceful until they heard a grumpy growling coming from the forest. Jane wasn't scared, she knew that her dad would keep her safe, so she trusted him. Her dad wrapped his arm around her and said, "Don't worry Jane. It's just a grumpy bear." Jane was surprised and asked, "Are you sure Dad?", but she trusted him and stayed calm. Dad and Jane slowly stepped away from the grumpy bear and continued their walk in the forest. No matter what happened, Jane knew that she could trust her dad to keep her safe.

One day, a grumpy leopard was walking in the woods. He was very sad because no one wanted to play with him. The other animals were scared of him. The leopard saw a little bird and asked, "Will you play with me?" The bird looked at the leopard and said, "Yes, I will play with you, but only if you promise not to be grumpy." The leopard agreed, and they started to play. They played hide and seek, and the bird was very good at hiding. The leopard was happy and started to clap. He was not grumpy anymore. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird turned into a big, friendly lion! The lion said, "I was the bird all along. I wanted to see if you could be nice and not grumpy. Now we can be friends forever." The leopard was so happy, and they played together every day.

One day, a fat king was very bored. He had a big crown, but no friends to play with. He called his servant and said, "Bring me someone to make me happy." The servant nodded and ran away. He looked for someone who could make the king happy. He saw a clown, a singer, a dancer, and a painter. But he thought they were too silly, too loud, too fast, and too messy. He wanted to find someone who was smart and good. He walked and walked until he came to a small house. He knocked on the door and a girl opened it. She had a bright smile and a kind face. The servant asked, "Can you make the king happy?" The girl shrugged and said, "I don't know. What does he like?" The servant said, "He likes nothing. He is very bored and lonely. He has a big crown, but no friends." The girl said, "That is sad. Maybe I can try to make him happy. But I have nothing to give him. Only this flower that I picked from the garden." The servant said, "That is enough. Come with me." They went to the palace and met the king. The king looked at the girl and said, "Who are you? What can you do for me?" The girl said, "I am just a girl. I have nothing to do for you. Only this flower that I picked from the garden. It is for you." The king took the flower and smelled it. He felt a warm feeling in his heart. He smiled and said, "Thank you. This is the most beautiful thing I have ever seen. What is your name?" The girl said, "My name is Rose." The king said, "Rose, you have made me happy. Will you be my friend?" Rose said, "Yes, I will be your friend." The king and Rose became friends. They played and talked and laughed together. The king learned to enjoy the simple things in life. He shared his crown with Rose and gave her a hug. He was not bored and lonely anymore. He was happy and grateful. The moral of the story is: Happiness is not found in things, but in people. A friend is more precious than a crown. A flower is more beautiful than gold.
