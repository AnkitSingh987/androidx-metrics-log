Once upon a time, there was a warm and sunny day. A little girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw a big pile of dust on the ground. She did not like the dust. Lily wanted to make the dust go away. Lily went inside to find her mom. She told her mom about the dust. Her mom gave her a big broom. She said, "Sweep the dust and it will disappear." Lily was happy. She went outside to sweep the dust. Lily started to sweep the dust with the big broom. She worked very hard. Soon, the dust began to disappear. Lily was so happy! The dust was gone, and her yard was clean. She played outside all day long.

Once there was a little girl called Amy. She was three years old and loved to play in the park. Every day, she would take her teddy bear with her and stand near the old mill. One day, Amy noticed something was wrong. The mill was silent. She was very upset. She looked at her teddy bear and hugged him tight. Suddenly, she heard voices coming from inside the mill. Someone was crying. Amy was confused. She didn't know what to do. Amy decided to stand up and take a peek inside the mill. When she looked inside, she saw a little kitten. It was scared and had nowhere to go. Amy wanted to help. She reached out and scooped it up in her arms. The little kitten felt safe and the crying stopped. Amy was so happy that she could help the kitten. From then on, Amy visited the mill every day with her teddy bear. She made sure the little kitten was safe and happy.

One day, a big plane was in the sky. The plane was incredible. It could go very fast. Inside the plane, there were many people. They were happy. The plane also had many things. It included toys, food and beds. The plane went up and down. It was fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The plane turned into a big bird. The bird was very big and nice. The people were surprised. They did not know what to do. The big bird took the people to a new place. The place was beautiful. There were many trees and flowers. The people were happy again. They played with the big bird. In the end, they all became good friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. They saw a small, calm dog sitting by a tree. Sue wanted to hug the dog, but she was scared. Her mom said, "It's okay, the dog is calm and nice." Sue slowly walked to the dog and gave it a gentle hug. The dog wagged its tail and licked Sue's face. Sue laughed and hugged the dog again. They were both very happy. The result of Sue's hug was a new friend. Sue, her mom, and the calm dog played in the park all day. They had lots of fun together. Sue learned that giving hugs can make new friends and make everyone happy.

It was a peaceful Saturday afternoon when Mama and little Jim were spendng some time together. Jim liked spending time with his Mama, especially when she made his favorite cocoa. Mama always made the cocoa extra special with mini marshmallows. They were enjoying their cocoa together when Jim heard his Mama laugh. She looked at him and said, "Do you want to hear a joke?" Jim smiled and nodded his head. Mama said, "What did the cocoa say to the marshmallow?" Jim smiled and thought and thought, but he didn't know the answer. Mama said, "It said 'Hey Marshmallow, it sure is nice and cozy in here!'" Jim laughed and laughed. He loved when Mama told jokes. He said, "Again! Again!" Mama laughed and said, "Ok! One more." Mama smiled and said, "What did the marshmallow say to the cocoa?" Jim thought, but again, he couldn't think of an answer. Mama said with a smile, "It said 'Thanks for making me feel so warm and fuzzy!.'" Jim laughed so much his belly hurt. He was so happy when his Mama joked around with him.
