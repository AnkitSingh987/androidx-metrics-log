Once upon a time, there was a curious little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day long. One day, Spot saw a big plate of yummy food on the table. He wanted to eat it all, but he knew it was not his food. Spot tried to stop thinking about the food, but it was so hard for him. He wanted to be a good dog, but the food smelled so good. He thought about what his mom would say if he ate the food. She would be sad and mad at him. In the end, Spot decided to be a good dog and not eat the food on the plate. He went to play outside instead. When his mom saw that he did not eat the food, she was so happy and proud. She gave him a big hug and a special treat just for him. Spot learned that it is important to do the right thing, even when it is hard.

Once upon a time, there was an independent butterfly. This butterfly liked to fly and play all by itself. It did not need any help from its friends. The butterfly was very happy. One day, the butterfly wanted to number the flowers in the garden. It started to count the flowers as it flew around. It counted one, two, three, four, and so on. The butterfly was having a lot of fun. But then, the sky turned dark and it started to rain. The independent butterfly tried to find a place to hide, but it did not know where to go. It got very wet and cold. The butterfly was sad because it did not have any friends to help it. In the end, the butterfly was all alone in the rain.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot lived in a big house with a nice family. They loved to play in the yard together. One sunny day, Spot found a message on the ground. The message was from a cat named Whiskers. Whiskers wanted to play with water. Spot loved water, so he was very happy. Spot and Whiskers played with a hose that could spray water. They sprayed water on the grass, the flowers, and even on each other. They laughed and had lots of fun. The end.

Molly was tired. She had been running all day, but she never stopped hoping. She wanted to race as fast as she could. She looked down at her little legs and wiggled her toes. She smiled when she saw how worn out her shoes were. The heels were squished and the thread was pulling away. Molly kept hoping. She kept running. Her little legs were getting tired, but she kept going. Suddenly, she heard a snap. It was her worn out heel! She stopped to take a look and saw that it was broken. Molly wished she had new shoes so she could run and race again. She kissed her shoes and put them in her pocket. Molly kept hoping that one day her shoes would be fixed and she could run again. Until then, she would keep dreaming of being the fastest runner.

Once upon a time, there was a little pony. The pony lived in a big farm with many friends. They played and ran in the green grass every day. One day, the pony fell down and got hurt. The pony was sad and ashamed because it could not play with its friends. It wanted to heal and be happy again. A kind lady saw the pony and helped it. She put a soft bandage on the pony's leg to help it heal. After a few days, the pony felt better and could play again. The pony was no longer ashamed and was very happy. All the friends were happy too.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to bake a cake. She put on her apron and got all the things she needed. She mixed the flour, eggs, and sugar in a big bowl. It was fun and messy. Sue was excited to eat her yummy cake. While the cake was baking, a smelly stranger came to the door. The stranger was a big, friendly dog. He smelled the cake baking and wanted some too. Sue liked the dog and wanted to be his friend. Sue gave the smelly stranger a bath to make him clean. They played together while they waited for the cake to be ready. When the cake was done, Sue and the dog shared the cake. They were both very happy and became best friends.
