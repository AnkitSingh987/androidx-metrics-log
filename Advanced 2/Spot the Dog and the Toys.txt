Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toys. He had a big stack of toys in his room. Every day, he would play with them and make them into a big tower. Spot was very good at it. One day, Spot's friend, a cat named Fluffy, came to play. They were having so much fun, but then something unexpected happened. Spot tried to make the tower, but it was very hard. He tried and tried, but he couldn't do it. Spot felt embarrass because he wanted to show Fluffy how good he was at making the tower. Then, Fluffy had an idea. She said, "Let's work together, Spot!" So, Spot and Fluffy worked together to make the tower. And guess what? They made the biggest and best tower ever! Spot was so happy, and he wasn't embarrass anymore. They played all day and had lots of fun. From that day on, Spot and Fluffy always helped each other and had the best time together.

Once upon a time there was a busy farm. On this farm there were lots of animals that needed to be taken care of. Every day was busy for the farmer, who would pass from animal to animal to make sure everyone was happy and healthy. One day, the farmer had to go to the market. He wanted to buy some food for the farm's animals. He asked his little son to help take care of the animals while he was gone. The farmer's little boy was very happy to help. He quickly passed from one animal to the next, feeding them and making sure they were all content. When his father returned from the market, he saw how hard his son had been working and praised him for it. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he went on a trip with his family to a place where it was very cold. There, he saw a mighty tree. The tree was so big, and Tim was so small. Tim saw a squirrel on the tree. The squirrel said, "Brrr, I am cold. I wish I could freeze nuts for the winter." Tim thought it was funny that the squirrel wanted to freeze nuts. Tim's mom called him, "Tim, come here! Let's get warm clothes on you." Tim put on his warm clothes and went back outside to play. He looked at the mighty tree and thought about the squirrel who wanted to freeze nuts. Tim smiled and continued his trip with his family.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On the farm, there was hay. The hay was wet. It was wet because it rained. The animals did not like wet hay. A helper came to the farm. The helper wanted to make the hay not wet. The helper wanted to restore the hay. The animals were happy. They wanted the helper to restore the hay. The helper worked hard. The sun came out. The sun helped the helper. The hay became not wet. The helper restored the hay. The animals were very happy. They liked the not wet hay. And they all lived happily on the farm.

Once upon a time, there was a little pupil named Tim. Tim was troubled because he was too small to reach the top of the tree to get his favorite toy. His toy was stuck in the tree, and he wanted it back. One day, Tim met a tall and kind giraffe named Gigi. Gigi saw that Tim was troubled and wanted to help him. Gigi told Tim that she could stretch her long neck to reach the top of the tree. Tim was so happy that Gigi could help him. Gigi stretched her long neck up, up, up to the top of the tree. She got Tim's toy back for him. Tim was so thankful to Gigi for helping him. From that day on, Tim and Gigi became the best of friends.

Once upon a time there lived a dragon. It had big claws, a sparkly scale and it could roar very loud. One day it felt very hot and wanted to go for a swim. It flew away and soon stopped above a lake. The water looked inviting, so the dragon dove in. It swam round and round, enjoying the cool water. Suddenly, it heard a loud roar. It got scared and flew away, quickly. As it flew away it noticed that the hot sun had been the one roaring - it was just too hot. Now the dragon goes to the lake every day, but it makes sure to stay in the shade so its sparkly scales don't get too hot.
