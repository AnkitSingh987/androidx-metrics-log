One day, a little fish named Finny was swimming in the sea. Finny loved to swim and play with his friends. They would watch the big fish swim by and have fun in the water. Finny saw a crab named Crusty walking by. "Hi, Crusty!" said Finny. "Do you want to swim with us?" Crusty looked at Finny and said, "I can't swim, but I can watch you and your friends." As they played, Finny found a spicy red pepper floating in the water. He showed it to Crusty. "Look what I found!" said Finny. "What is it?" asked Crusty. "It's a spicy pepper," Finny replied. Finny and Crusty decided to give the spicy pepper to their friend, Octo the octopus. Octo liked to cook and make tasty food for his friends. "Thank you, Finny and Crusty," said Octo. "I will make a yummy meal for all of us!" That night, all the sea friends enjoyed a delicious dinner made by Octo. They laughed and had fun together, thankful for the spicy pepper and their wonderful friendship.

One day, a kind girl named Lily went to the big store with her mom. They needed to go up to the very top floor. To get there, they had to use the elevator. Lily was excited, as she loved riding the elevator. When they got inside the elevator, Lily saw a small boy with a red ball. The boy threw the ball up in the air and tried to catch it. But he missed, and the ball rolled out of the elevator. The boy was sad. Lily said, "Don't be sad. I will help you catch your ball." The kind girl and the boy went out of the elevator and chased the ball. They both reached out and caught it together. The boy smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily!" They both went back to the elevator and rode it all the way to the top floor, happy to have made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. The mouse loved to swing on a tree. Every day, the mouse would go to the tree and swing. One day, the mouse saw a tasty treat on the ground. The mouse wanted to eat the treat. The mouse got off the swing and went to the treat. But, oh no! The treat was not good. The mouse felt bad after eating it. The mouse could not swing on the tree anymore. The mouse was sad.

Once upon a time, there was a big cook named Bob. Bob lived in a nation with many people. Some people were wealthy, and some people were not. One day, Bob decided to cook food for everyone in the nation. He wanted to show that sharing is good. He cooked a lot of yummy food, like rice, bread, and fruit. The people in the nation came to eat Bob's food. The wealthy people and the not wealthy people all ate together. They were happy and shared the food. They learned that sharing is good and makes everyone happy. The end.

One day, a little dog named Max was playing in the park. He felt itchy and saw a tiny flea on his fur. Max was embarrassed because he did not want his friends to see the flea. Max met his best friend, a cat named Lily. "Lily, I have a flea and I feel embarrassed," said Max. Lily smiled and said, "Don't worry, Max. I will help you get rid of the flea. We all have fleas sometimes, it's okay." Max and Lily went to find a special soap that would make the flea go away. When they found it, Max took a bath with the soap. The flea was gone! Max was very happy and thanked Lily. He learned to value their friendship even more because Lily helped him when he was embarrassed.

Once there was a little red bear who lived in a wrap. He was so small and loved to play in the sun. One day, he started to feel a little tickle in his nose. He thought it was funny, but then he sneezed! He sneezed so hard that he flew out of the wrap and into the sky. The little red bear thought it was an adventure at first. He flew higher and higher until he eventually saw a storm brewing in the distance. He flew closer and saw lightning, thunder and dark clouds. Suddenly, he felt scared. "It's time to go home," he thought. But he was too late. The storm was getting closer and it was too strong for the little bear to fight. He couldn't get back to his wrap. The little red bear was stuck! He sneezed and admitted his defeat. Eventually, the storm passed, but he was never able to return home. The little red bear was never seen again.
