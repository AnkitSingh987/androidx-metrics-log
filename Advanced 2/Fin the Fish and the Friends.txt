Once upon a time, there was a fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the water all day. He was a very generous fish and liked to share his food with his friends. One day, Fin and his friends went to a quiet place in the water. They wanted to play and swim together. They were so happy to be in the quiet place because they could hear each other better. As they swam and played, Fin found a big pile of food. He remembered that he was a generous fish, so he shared the food with all his friends. They all ate and had a great time. They swam and played in the quiet place until it was time to go home. Fin and his friends were very happy and they all said "Thank you, Fin!"

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. But they also liked to explore. They saw a big gate with a sign. The sign said "Keep Out". It was a mysterious sign. "Let's go in," said Tom. He was curious and brave. "No, we can't," said Lily. She was careful and scared. "The sign says no. We have to mind the sign. It is an order." Tom did not mind the sign. He did not care about the order. He wanted to see what was behind the gate. He opened the gate and went in. Lily followed him. She did not want to be alone. Behind the gate, they saw a big house. It was old and dark. It had many windows and doors. Some were broken. Some were locked. It looked spooky. "Wow, this is cool," said Tom. He ran to the house. He wanted to look inside. "Wait, Tom, this is not cool," said Lily. She stayed by the gate. She wanted to go back. "This is dangerous. We should not be here. Someone might see us. Someone might be angry." Tom did not listen to Lily. He did not think it was dangerous. He thought it was fun. He found a door that was open. He went inside. Lily followed him. She did not want to lose him. Inside the house, they saw many things. They saw old furniture, dusty books, broken toys, and strange pictures. They also saw cobwebs, mice, and spiders. It was dirty and scary. "Look, Lily, this is awesome," said Tom. He picked up a toy car. He liked cars. "Tom, this is not awesome," said Lily. She dropped a book. She did not like spiders. "This is creepy. We should leave. Now." Tom did not want to leave. He wanted to see more. He went to another room. Lily followed him. She did not want to stay alone. In the other room, they saw a big closet. It was closed. It had a lock. It also had a sign. The sign said "Do Not Open". It was another mysterious sign. "Let's open it," said Tom. He was excited and naughty. "No, we can't," said Lily. She was nervous and good. "The sign says no. We have to mind the sign. It is another order." Tom did not mind the sign. He did not care about the order. He wanted to see what was inside the closet. He found a key. He opened the lock. He opened the closet. Lily closed her eyes. She did not want to see. Inside the closet, they saw a big surprise. They saw a man. He was old and angry. He was the owner of the house. He did not like visitors. He did not like children. He did not like Tom and Lily. "Who are you? What are you doing here? How did you get in?" he shouted. He was loud and scary. Tom and Lily were shocked and sorry. They did not know what to say. They did not know what to do. They wished they had minded the signs. They wished they had followed the orders. They wished they had never come.

Once upon a time there was a man with a big mustache. He loved to wash his mustache every day before he went out. The mustache was very fluffy and bouncy and made him look very successful. One day the man went out without washing his mustache. Everyone he passed by laughed and made fun of him. He felt embarrassed and sad. The man decided that he would never go out again without washing his mustache. He remembered how important it was to keep himself looking clean. The man learned a very important lesson that day. He learned that success means taking care of the small details, like washing his mustache. From then on, he was proud of himself for taking the time to wash his mustache every day.
