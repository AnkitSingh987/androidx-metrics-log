The sun was shining and a little boy was playing in the park. He ran around, stopped, and then laughed. He saw a bird and said a funny word. Suddenly, he spotted something that made him smile - it was a balloon. He ran towards it and stopped in front of it. He thought it was a fun thing, but didn't know what it was. He stood there looking at it and smiled. He reached out and grabbed the balloon and it flew up into the sky. The little boy looked up at it in surprise, and smiled even bigger. He watched the balloon travel higher and higher until it was out of sight. He thought about what it was, but the word was unknown to him - he cheered and smiled. He turned around and skipped away, thinking about the balloon and its unknown word. He could still feel the same smile on his face.

Once upon a time, in a big green yard, there was a little ant. The little ant had a long way to walk to find food. One day, the ant saw a big red ball. The ant wanted to play with the ball, but it knew it had to find food first. The ant thought, "I will work hard now and play later." The little ant walked and walked, and it found a big pile of food. The ant worked hard to take the food back to its home. The ant was tired, but it did not pause. It knew that if it worked hard now, it could play later. After a long time, the ant took all the food back to its home. It was very happy. The ant went back to the big red ball and played all day. The other ants saw the little ant play and wanted to play too. They learned that if they work hard first, they can have fun later. And they all played together, happy and full.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bug named Zip. Zip was a calm bug who liked to play in the sun. He had many friends in the garden. One day, Zip and his friends went to the park. They played on the swings and slides. Zip loved to zip up and down the slide. His friends laughed and clapped their hands as they watched him. As the sun went down, Zip and his friends said goodbye. They all went back to their homes in the garden. Zip felt happy and calm as he lay down to sleep, dreaming of more fun days with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to listen to music. One day, Tim felt sad because his favorite toy broke. He decided to listen to music to feel better. Tim went to his friend, Sam. Sam liked to cut paper into shapes. Tim asked Sam, "Can you cut a shape for me?" Sam said, "Yes, I can cut a heart for you!" Tim was happy because he liked hearts. As they were cutting the heart, they listened to music. The music made Tim feel less sad. When the heart was cut, Tim said, "Thank you, Sam!" Sam smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim!" They played together and listened to more music. Tim was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a huge motor with lots of buttons and switches. Every day, the motor whirred, buzzing and chugging along. One day a little girl called Sally who was three years old went for a walk in the garden. She saw the huge motor and wanted to know what it was. Sally said, “Mommy, what is this?” Her mommy replied, “It’s a huge motor. It makes things work. Don’t worry, Sally. You don’t need to be scared.” Sally had been worried about the motor, so she was relieved. She skipped away, happy that things weren’t as scary as she had thought. A few weeks later, Sally was walking in the garden again, but this time, she noticed that the motor wasn’t moving. She ran up to her mommy, worried again and asked, “Mommy, why isn’t the motor running?” “Well, it’s broken. I’ll need to get somebody to fix it. Don’t worry, though. Someone will be able to fix it soon,” her mommy explained. And sure enough, when Sally went for another walk in the garden a few days later, the huge motor was buzzing and running again
