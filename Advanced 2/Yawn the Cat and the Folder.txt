One day, a cat named Yawn saw a flexible folder in the park. Yawn was a very sleepy cat. He liked to yawn all the time. The folder was big and could bend easily. Yawn was curious about the folder. Yawn went to the folder and said, "Hi, folder! Can I play with you?" The folder said, "Yes, Yawn! I can bend and be fun to play with." Yawn and the folder played together. They had a lot of fun. After playing, Yawn said, "Thank you, folder! I am sleepy now." The folder said, "You're welcome, Yawn! Sleep well!" Yawn yawned and went to sleep. The flexible folder stayed with Yawn, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a skull. The skull was very happy and cheerful. He was looking for someone to provide him with company and adventure. One day, the skull spotted a young girl walking in the woods. He called out to her, and asked her to help him find some fun. The girl was delighted to help, and agreed that she would provide him with a great adventure. She took the skull around the forest, and along the way she showed him all of the wonderful things it had to offer. The skull was amazed at all the beauty, and followed her wherever she went. Finally they arrived at a large lake, and the skull asked the girl what they should do. She said they should build a sandcastle and so they got to work. The girl and the skull worked together to build the perfect sandcastle. When they were finished, the girl and the skull sat together and watched the sunset. The sky was pink and orange, and they were both cheerful. They hugged and said goodbye, and the girl promised to come back and visit the skull soon.

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat named Tom. Tom liked to rub his head on everything. He would rub his head on the table, the chair, and even on people's legs. One day, Tom found a shiny coin on the floor. He did not know its value, but he thought it was pretty. So, Tom picked up the coin with his mouth and took it to his favorite spot. He rubbed his head on the coin, and it made a funny sound. Tom's friend, a little girl named Sue, saw him with the shiny coin. She knew its value and told Tom that it was worth a lot. Tom didn't care about the value, but he was happy that Sue liked his coin too. They played with the coin and laughed together, having a fun and silly day.

Once there was a girl named Lucy. She loved the color pink. One day, she found a fragile pink paper on the ground. She did not know what to do with it. Lucy took the pink paper home. Her mom said, "We can fold it to make something fun!" They folded the paper together. Lucy was excited to see what they would make. At last, they finished folding the pink paper. It was a beautiful pink butterfly! Lucy was so happy. She put the fragile pink butterfly on her shelf. Now, she could see it every day.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big wardrobe in his room. It was light blue, like the sky. Tim wanted to make it more fun. He decided to paint it with many colors. Tim's friend, Sue, came over to help him. They took out their paint and brushes. Tim painted red, and Sue painted yellow. They laughed and talked while they painted. "This is fun!" said Tim. "Yes, it is!" agreed Sue. When they finished, the wardrobe was very pretty. It had red, yellow, green, and purple. Tim and Sue were very happy with their work. They knew the wardrobe was now the best part of the room.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to share her toys with her friends. She always had a big smile on her face when she shared. One day, Amy found a book with a poem inside. The poem was about a bad cat who liked to steal things. Amy thought the poem was funny, so she wanted to share it with her friends. Amy took the book to her friend's house. They all sat in a circle and listened as Amy read the poem out loud. When Amy finished, everyone laughed and clapped. They thought the bad cat in the poem was silly. Amy was happy that she could share the funny poem with her friends.
