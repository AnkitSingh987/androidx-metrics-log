Lily and Max were twins who loved to play together. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big red car that they could ride in the garden. They took turns to drive the car and to sit in the back. One day, they were playing with the car when they saw their mom come out of the house with a tray. On the tray, there were two cups of juice and some cookies. Lily and Max were very happy, because they were thirsty and hungry. "Juice! Juice!" they yelled, and ran to their mom. They each took a cup of juice and a cookie, and thanked their mom. They were about to go back to the car, when they saw something else on the tray. It was a small, fluffy, white kitten. It had blue eyes and a pink nose. It was adorable. Lily and Max gasped, and dropped their juice and cookies. They ran to the tray again, and reached for the kitten. "Kitty! Kitty!" they yelled, and tried to pick it up. But the kitten was scared, and scratched their hands. Lily and Max cried, and let go of the kitten. The kitten jumped off the tray, and ran away. Their mom came to them, and hugged them. She said she was sorry, and that the kitten was a surprise for them. She said she would find the kitten, and bring it back. She said they had to be gentle with the kitten, and not yell at it. Lily and Max nodded, and said they were sorry too. They said they loved the kitten, and wanted to be its friends. They said they would wait for the kitten, and share their juice and cookies with it. They smiled, and hugged their mom. They hoped the kitten would come back soon.

Once upon a time, two friends called Joe and Emma were walking in the park together. Joe was carrying a basket. "What do you have in the basket, Joe?" asked Emma. Joe smiled and said, "Let's pause and find out!" He opened the basket and there were two avocados. They were different sizes. "These both look delicious!" said Emma, who was very hungry. Joe smiled again and asked, "Do you want to have one?" Emma nodded eagerly and so Joe gave her an avocado. They both enjoyed the tasty snack and smiled with joy. The two friends continued to walk in the park, full and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a polite little bear named Bobby. Bobby loved to eat blueberries. One day, he went to the store to buy some blueberries for his mom. At the store, Bobby met a big bear named Grumpy. Grumpy also wanted blueberries, but there was only one box left. "I want that box!" said Grumpy. Bobby felt sad but he remembered to be polite. "Can we share the blueberries?" asked Bobby. Grumpy thought about it and said, "Okay, we can share." They both took the blueberries home and shared them with their families. In the end, they became good friends. The moral of the story is that being polite and sharing can make everyone happy.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big board. He wanted to make an impressive picture. He took his colors and started to draw. Tim was very happy with his picture. Then, a girl named Amy came. She saw Tim's picture and said, "Wow, that's impressive! Can I help you complete the picture?" Tim said, "Yes, let's complete it together!" They both started to draw on the board. As they were drawing, a big wind came. The wind was so strong that it blew the board away. They could not complete the picture. Tim and Amy were very sad. They did not know that the wind would take the board away.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to school. He was very fit and loved to play with his friends. In class, they were learning about math. Tim did not like math very much. During the lesson, Tim started to stare at the window. He saw a big bird outside. He asked his friend, "Do you see the big bird?" His friend looked and said, "Yes, it's so big!" After school, Tim and his friends went to play near the big tree. Tim wanted to climb the tree to see the big bird. His friends told him not to, but he did not listen. He climbed higher and higher. Suddenly, he fell down from the tree and hurt his leg. Tim cried and his friends ran to get help. The bad ending made Tim very sad, and he wished he had listened to his friends.
