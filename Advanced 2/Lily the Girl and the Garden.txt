Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a small garden where she liked to grow things. One day, she decided to grow some broccoli. She took a seed and put it in the ground. Then, she gave it some water. After a few days, a tiny green plant came out of the ground. The plant was not pretty. It was ugly. Lily looked at the plant and said, "Why are you so ugly, little broccoli?" The little broccoli did not say anything. It just kept growing. One day, Lily's mom came to look at the garden. She saw the ugly broccoli and said, "Lily, this is the best broccoli I have ever seen! We can eat this for dinner tonight!" Lily was so happy that her ugly broccoli was good. She learned that even if something looks ugly, it can still be very good.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She lived in a big house with her wealthy family. Lily had many toys, but her favorite was a small case filled with pretty dresses for her dolls. One sunny day, Lily played with her dolls in the garden. She dressed them in their prettiest dresses from the case. Then, she decided to have a wedding for her dolls. She picked two dolls, named Daisy and Tom, to marry each other. Lily said, "Daisy, do you want to marry Tom?" Daisy, the doll, said in a tiny voice, "Yes, I do." Then, Lily asked Tom, "Do you want to marry Daisy?" Tom, the doll, said, "Yes, I do." Lily was very happy. She clapped her hands and sang a happy song. She loved playing with her dolls and their case of pretty dresses. And that day, Daisy and Tom were married in the sunny garden, surrounded by all their doll friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big red boot in her yard. She was upset because she could not find the other boot. She asked her mom, "Mom, can you help me find the other boot?" Her mom said, "Let's look together." As they were looking for the other boot, they saw a small cat stuck in a tree. The cat was very upset and scared. Sue wanted to save the cat, so she put the big red boot on her hand and reached up to grab the cat. The cat jumped into the boot and Sue pulled it down from the tree. The cat was so happy to be saved that it jumped out of the boot and ran away. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat came back with the other big red boot in its mouth! Sue and her mom were so surprised. They thanked the cat, and Sue now had a pair of big red boots to wear.

Once upon a time, there was a big pine tree. It was very tall and green. All the other trees were envious of the big pine tree. They wanted to be tall and green too. One day, a little bird came to the big pine tree. The little bird said, "Guess what? I know a secret." The big pine tree was excited. It wanted to know the secret. The little bird told the big pine tree that there was a magic seed. If the other trees ate the seed, they would become tall and green too. The big pine tree was happy for the other trees. But then, something unexpected happened. The big pine tree started to shrink! It became smaller and smaller. The other trees grew tall and green. At first, the big pine tree was sad. But then it realized that now all the trees were tall and green, just like it. The big pine tree was happy again. It didn't need to be the tallest and greenest tree anymore. All the trees could be tall and green together.

One day, a strong frog named Fred lived near a big hose. He loved to jump all day long. He would jump on the hose and play with his friends. The hose was very long and could reach far away. One morning, Fred's friend, a little bird, told him, "I heard there is a big party at the end of the hose today. Let's go!" Fred was excited and wanted to jump all the way to the party. So, he and his friend started their journey, jumping and flying along the hose. As they got closer to the party, they saw other animals playing and having fun. Fred jumped higher and higher, feeling happy. Finally, they reached the end of the hose, and there was a big pool for all the animals to play in. The pool was the big surprise! Fred and his friend had a great time at the party, playing in the water and making new friends. The long hose had led them to a fun day they would never forget.
