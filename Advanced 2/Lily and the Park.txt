Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to go outside and play. One day, she went to the park with her mom. At the park, Lily saw a big, friendly dog. The dog's name was Max. Lily and Max got to meet each other. They had a lot of fun playing together. Lily's mom told her that Max was a very intelligent dog. Max could do many tricks. He could sit, lay down, and even roll over. Lily thought Max was amazing. She wanted to teach Max a new trick. She called this trick "exercise." Together, Lily and Max did the exercise. They jumped up and down, ran around, and even danced. They were both very happy. They played at the park all day long. Lily and Max became best friends. From that day on, they always met at the park to play and do their special exercise.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. He wanted to eat something yummy. Tim saw a big yellow fruit on a tree. He thought it would be tasty. Tim asked his mom, "Can I eat the yellow fruit, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can eat the fruit. It is a banana. It is good for you." Tim took the yellow banana from the tree. He peeled it and took a big bite. Tim enjoyed the sweet taste of the banana. He was not hungry anymore. He shared the rest of the banana with his mom. They both enjoyed the yellow banana together.

Once upon a time, there was a curious goose. The goose liked to walk around and look at things. One day, the goose saw a big box. It was a surprise box. The goose wanted to know what was inside. The goose walked up to the box and tried to open it. The box was very hard to open. The goose tried and tried, but it could not open the box. The goose was very sad. Then, a big wind came and blew the box away. The goose tried to follow the box, but it was too fast. The goose could not find the box again. The curious goose never knew what was inside the surprise box, and it was a bad ending for the goose.

One day, a wet cat named Tim went outside. The sky was dark and full of clouds. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he climbed, he felt happy and excited. Then, Tim saw a bird named Sue sitting in the tree. Sue was sad because her nest was messy. Tim wanted to help, so he said, "Let's organize your nest, Sue!" Sue nodded and they started to fix the nest together. While they were working, lightning flashed in the sky. Tim and Sue were scared, but they stayed in the tree. They finished organizing the nest and felt proud. The storm went away, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a new toy club. He loved to play with his club all day. One day, Tim took his club to the park to play. While at the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue liked Tim's new club and wanted to play too. Tim said, "Let's play together!" They played and had fun with the club all day. When it was time to go home, Sue was sad. Tim said, "Don't be sad, we can play again tomorrow!" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play with the new club tomorrow!" And they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Kitty. She lived in a small house with her best friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to play together all day long. One day, Kitty and Lily woke up very early, just before sunrise. They looked out the window and saw the sky turning pink and orange. "Wow, look at the pretty sunrise!" said Lily. Kitty meowed in agreement, her furry tail wagging with excitement. After they watched the sunrise, Lily had an idea. "Let's store our toys outside and play there today!" she said. Kitty meowed happily and helped Lily carry their toys outside. They played and laughed under the warm sun until it was time to go inside for dinner. The end.
