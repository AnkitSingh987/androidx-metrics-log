Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was an eager boy who loved lollipops. One day, Tim's mom told him that he could have a lollipop after dinner. Tim was very happy and could not wait for dinner to be over. During dinner, Tim could not stop thinking about the lollipop. He knew it would be a big, sweet, and yummy treat. Tim ate his dinner very fast, so he could have his lollipop soon. His mom saw how eager he was and smiled. After dinner, Tim's mom gave him the lollipop. It was big and colorful. Tim was so happy that he danced around the room. He knew that the lollipop would be the best treat ever. And it was! Tim enjoyed every bit of it, just as he had imagined.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Pat and Sam. Pat and Sam were so excited to discover a new land! They were sure it would be full of fun! They packed their things and set out on their journey. As they crossed the sea, they could see a beautiful land in the distance. When they finally arrived, they were so amazed. It was a magical place, with trees, grass and a big sun. Pat and Sam couldn't wait to explore the land. Together they climbed up a big hill and discovered a kind fairytale castle. When they stepped inside, they couldn't believe their eyes. There were animals everywhere and a kind princess who welcomed them with a big hug. Pat and Sam spent the whole day playing in the magical land. They caught fireflies, made daisy chains and shared stories. When it was time to go, they promised to come back soon. Pat and Sam had discovered a magical land and made a new friend.

One day, Tom went to the zoo with his mom. They saw many animals like big lions, tall giraffes, and funny monkeys. Tom was very happy. At the zoo, Tom saw a man with a serious face. The man was trying to sort the animals' food. He looked sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad?" The man said, "I can't sort the food right. The animals will be hungry." Tom wanted to help. He said, "I can help you sort the food." So, Tom and the man worked together to sort the food. They gave the right food to each animal. The animals were happy and not hungry anymore. The man smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom!" And Tom felt good because he helped the man and the animals at the zoo.

Anna and Ben were going to bed. They did not want to sleep. They wanted to play more. "Can we read one more book, Mom?" Anna asked. "No, it is late. You need to sleep," Mom said. "Can we watch one more show, Dad?" Ben asked. "No, it is dark. You need to rest," Dad said. Anna and Ben were sad. They did not listen to Mom and Dad. They waited until they left. Then they turned on the light and the TV. They saw a show about a pink owl. The owl was funny. It made jokes and flew around. Anna and Ben laughed and clapped. They did not hear a noise outside. It was a real owl. The owl was hungry. It saw the light and the TV. It smelled Anna and Ben. The owl flew to the window. It broke the glass. It came inside. It saw Anna and Ben. It was not funny. It was angry. "Who are you?" the owl asked. "We are Anna and Ben. We are watching a show about a pink owl," Anna and Ben said. "You are not watching a show. You are delaying your sleep. You are being naughty. You are my dinner," the owl said. The owl opened its beak. It grabbed Anna and Ben. It ate them. Mom and Dad came back. They saw the broken window. They saw the owl. They saw the bones. They screamed. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lulu. She was three years old and very happy. One day, Lulu went to the market with her mommy. She saw lots of things, but did not know what to buy. When they passed by a luxury toy, Lulu asked her mommy if she could have it. Her mommy said no because it was very expensive and they did not have enough money. Lulu started to disagree and they argued back and forth. Lulu's mommy wanted to take her home but Lulu was scared. She pleaded with her mommy to stay at the market for a little longer. Her mommy relented and agreed to buy Lulu a toy. When Lulu saw it, she was so excited! She thanked her mommy for being so kind. From then on, Lulu learned to always be agreeable when she disagreed with her mommy.
