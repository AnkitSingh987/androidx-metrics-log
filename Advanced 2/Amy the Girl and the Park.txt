Title: Amy the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in an ordinary house with her mommy and daddy. Amy had a favorite toy named Mr. Bunny. She took Mr. Bunny everywhere she went. One day, Amy and Mr. Bunny went to the park to play. At the park, Amy met a new friend named Tim. They played together on the swings, and they had a lot of fun. Tim said, "Amy, I like your bunny!" Amy smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim! I like your toy car!" They played until it was time to go home. When Amy got home, she realized she did not have Mr. Bunny. She was sad and told her mommy, "I miss Mr. Bunny!" Her mommy said, "Let's go back to the park and find him." But when they got to the park, Mr. Bunny was not there. Instead, they found a magic switch on the ground. Amy picked it up and pressed the switch. Suddenly, Mr. Bunny appeared! Amy was so happy to have her friend back. From that day on, Amy knew that she lived in an extraordinary house with a magic switch that could help her find her lost toys.

Sara and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to gather flowers and leaves and make pretend spells. They had a big pot and a wooden spoon. They put the flowers and leaves in the pot and stirred them with the spoon. They said funny words like "abracadabra" and "hocus pocus". But Tom, the boy next door, did not like their game. He thought it was dull and silly. He wanted to play with his ball and his bike. He came over to the fence and shouted at Sara and Ben. "Stop playing with that rubbish! You are not real witches or wizards. You are just babies. Come and play with me. I have a cool ball and a fast bike. They are much more fun than your stupid pot and spoon." Sara and Ben felt sad and angry. They liked their game. They did not want to play with Tom's ball and bike. They thought they were boring and loud. They told Tom to go away and leave them alone. But Tom did not listen. He climbed over the fence and ran to their pot. He kicked it over and spilled their flowers and leaves. He laughed and said, "There, I broke your spell. Now you have to play with me." Sara and Ben cried. They were very upset. They had worked hard to gather their flowers and leaves. They had made a beautiful spell. They did not like Tom. He was mean and rude. They wished he would disappear. Then, something strange happened. A cloud of smoke came out of the pot. It covered Tom and made him cough. He tried to run away, but he tripped and fell. He looked at himself and saw that he had turned into a frog. He croaked and hopped, but he could not change back. Sara and Ben stopped crying. They looked at each other and smiled. They realized that their spell had worked after all. They had turned Tom into a frog. They felt happy and proud. They picked up their pot and spoon and went to find more flowers and leaves. They decided to make more spells and have more fun. They did not care about Tom's ball and bike. They left him in the garden, hopping and croaking. They hoped he would learn his lesson.

Once upon a time there was a beautiful princess. She loved playing outside and looking up at the stars in the sky. One night while she was playing, she stumbled across something magical. It was a tiny mint that was glowing bright. The princess was so surprised to see a glowing mint that she wanted to pick it up and keep it for herself. She carefully bent over and picked it up. But then she heard a loud snap and the glowing mint suddenly broke into two pieces. The princess was so sad that her mint was broken, but just then a tiny light began to glow from the broken mint itself. She was surprised to see that a tiny fairy had been living inside the mint. The fairy thanked the princess for setting him free, and as a thank you, he cast a spell. The spell made it so that every time the princess looked up at the night sky, she would see a glowing mint in the stars. The princess smiled and thanked the fairy, happy that her broken mint still made the sky glow.
