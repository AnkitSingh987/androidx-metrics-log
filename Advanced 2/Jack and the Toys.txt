Once upon a time there were two little boys. One was called Jack and the other Thomas. Jack really wanted to add an aeroplane to his collection of toys, but Thomas was very eager to add a dinosaur. Both boys wanted their own way and they argued. They argued and argued until their parents had to step in. Their parents said that they could both add one toy each. Jack was so happy that he got to add an aeroplane to his collection of toys. Thomas was just as happy because he was able to add a dinosaur too. Both little boys were excited and hugged each other in delight!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a very gifted girl. She loved to play with her toys and have fun with her friends. One sunny day, Lily and her friend Sam were playing outside. They found a big box. "What is this?" Sam asked. "I don't know," Lily said, "Let's open it!" They opened the box and found many fun toys inside. They played with the toys all day long. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, Lily and Sam were very happy. They learned that they could have fun and share their toys with each other. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to her friend Tom's house. They were playing with toys in Tom's messy room. Sue saw a tray with many colors on it. She wanted to play with the tray, so she asked Tom, "Can I play with the tray?" Tom said, "Yes, but we need paper to use the tray." They found some paper and started to sign on it with the colors from the tray. They had a lot of fun making pretty signs. Suddenly, the tray fell on the floor and all the colors mixed together. Sue and Tom were sad because they thought the colors were ruined. But then, they saw that the mixed colors made a new, beautiful color. They were so happy and continued to sign with the new color.

Once upon a time, there was a popular teacher named Miss Bee. She loved to help her little pupils learn new things. One day, she told her class, "Today, we will examine some rocks." All the kids were excited. They went outside and started looking at the rocks. One pupil, named Timmy, found a big, shiny rock. He showed it to Miss Bee and said, "Look what I found!" Miss Bee looked at the rock and was very surprised. She knew that it was not a normal rock. It was a magic rock! She told the class, "This rock can make wishes come true. Let's all make a wish together." The pupils wished for a big, fun playground. Suddenly, a beautiful playground appeared in front of them. The kids were so happy, and they played all day long. Miss Bee smiled, knowing that her class had learned something new and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little town. In the town lived a boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toy car. His toy car was red and shiny. One day, Tim was playing with his toy car near a big wreck. The wreck was old and rusty. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim. Don't break your toy car." Tim said, "Okay, Mom. I will be careful." But Tim was not careful. He played too close to the wreck, and his toy car got stuck. Tim tried to get his toy car out, but he broke it. Tim was sad. His mom said, "I told you to be careful, Tim." Tim learned that he should always listen to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to talk a lot. One day, she went to the park with her mom. Lily saw a big, open space to play in. She saw many kids playing and talking to each other. Lily wanted to play with them, but they were talking in a different language. She did not understand their language. Lily felt sad and sat down on a bench. Then, a little boy came up to her. He talked in the same language as Lily! They both smiled and started to play together. The other kids saw them and wanted to play too. They all tried to talk in the same language. Soon, they were all playing together and having fun. The park became a happy place where everyone could talk and play, no matter what language they spoke.
