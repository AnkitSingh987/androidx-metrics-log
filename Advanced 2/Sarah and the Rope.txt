Once upon a time, there were two best friends. Sarah and Jack were always together, so when Sarah came over with her new fake rope, of course Jack wanted to try it out. "Let's play with the rope, Jack!" said Sarah excitedly. "Great idea!" said Jack. So they both took hold of the rope and began to spin around and around. The fake rope flew up in the air and around and around they went, laughing and having so much fun. But soon, the fun was over and the two friends needed to go home. But they vowed to play with the fake rope another day. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl called Lucy. She lived in a big village with lots of people. One day, Lucy was playing in the park when she saw a stone on the ground. She wanted to take the stone home with her, but she wasn't sure if she could. She decided to ask her mum if it was alright. When she asked her mum, her mum said no. She said it was not nice to take things that did not belong to her. But Lucy was very creative and she wanted to find a way to have the stone. So, at bedtime she asked her mum if she could keep the stone if she promised not to take anything else that didn't belong to her. After much thought, her mum said yes and allowed Lucy to take the stone home. So Lucy was very happy and kept her stone safe. From then on, she was always very careful to not take things that didn't belong to her.

Once there was a mole who loved to run around in the dark forest. He stumbled around looking for things to eat when it was dark outside. One day he was running around and he stumbled right into a big hole! He looked around and realized that he was in a dark hole and he was all alone. The mole was so scared! He tried to find his way out, but it was so dark that he couldn't see a thing! He stumbled around and around in the darkness until finally he saw a tiny bit of light coming through. He crawled towards the light until he finally saw the bright sunshine again! He was so happy, he ran around the forest until he got back to his home. The mole was so excited to tell his friends about his adventure in the dark forest. Even though he was scared, he was happy that he made it back safely.

Once there was a big girl who had a large hat. She liked to wear her hat because it made her feel special. One day, the girl wanted to show off her hat to the world. So she put on her hat and went for a walk. As she kept walking, she noticed other kids were looking at her hat. They commented how large and special it was. The girl was so proud of her hat. She even started to run with her hat on. But, as she was running, she suddenly tripped over and her large hat flew off her head. She quickly stood up, but the hat was gone. She looked all around and screamed, but it was too far away for her to find it. The girl was so sad and did not have her special hat anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bob. Bob had a fine suit that he loved to wear. One day, he put on his suit and went for a walk in the woods. As he walked, he saw a little bird. The bird was sad and crying. Bob wanted to comfort the bird, so he sat down next to it. "Why are you sad, little bird?" he asked. The bird told Bob that it was lost and couldn't find its way home. Bob knew the woods very well, so he decided to help the bird. They walked together, and soon, they found the bird's home. The bird was so happy and thanked Bob for his help. From that day on, they became best friends.
