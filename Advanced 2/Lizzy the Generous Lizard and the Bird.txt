Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a generous lizard named Lizzy. Lizzy loved to share her food with all her friends. One day, while she was walking, she met a new animal. It was a small, scared bird. "Hello," said Lizzy. "I am Lizzy the Lizard. You are welcome to be my friend." The bird smiled and said, "Thank you, Lizzy. I am Benny the Bird." Lizzy and Benny played together all day. They ran, they jumped, and they laughed. But soon, it was time to eat. Lizzy had a big pile of food to share with Benny. They ate and ate until they were full. The next day, a big storm came to the forest. The wind was strong and the trees shook. Lizzy was scared, but then she saw Benny. Benny could fly high in the sky, and he saw a safe place for them to hide. "Follow me, Lizzy!" Benny called. Lizzy followed Benny, and they found a safe cave to hide from the storm. When the storm was over, they went back to their homes. They were happy and thankful for each other's help. The moral of the story is: when you are generous and welcome others, they will help you in your time of need.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy bear living in the woods. One day he was searching for food. He spotted a branch in a tree filled with delicious green berries so he decided to take a closer look. He jumped up and tried to reach the branch but it was out of reach. He was about to give up when he heard a voice nearby. "Hey, lazy bear," the voice said, "why don't you use this stick to help you reach the branch?" The bear was so relieved. He had found a friend! He grabbed the stick and used it to successfully grab the branch. He ate as many of the yummy berries as he could and thanked his new friend before heading back into the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a bad tornado. It came to a town where many people lived. The tornado was very big and strong. It made the wind blow very fast. The people in the town were scared. One day, a little girl named Sara was playing outside. She saw the bad tornado coming. She knew it did not belong in her town. Sara was brave and wanted to help her friends and family. Sara told everyone to go inside their homes. She knew they would be safe there. The bad tornado came and went away. The town was safe again. Everyone was happy and thanked Sara. They knew she helped them and that the bad tornado did not belong there.

Once upon a time, there was a black car. The car went to the mechanic. The mechanic said, "I will help you, car." The mechanic filled the car with gas. The car was happy. The car said, "Thank you, mechanic." But then, something unexpected happened! The car turned into a big, black bird! The bird flew away and said, "Thank you, mechanic, for filling me with gas. Now I can fly!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her ball. It was a round ball. One day, Sue decided to play with her ball outside. As Sue played, she saw a big box. It was empty. Sue thought it was a good idea to put her round ball in the empty box. So, she did. Later, when Sue went to get her round ball from the empty box, she found a big, round, bouncy ball inside! Sue was so happy and surprised. She played with her new big, round ball all day.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to mail a letter to her friend. She put on her coat because it was cold outside. As she walked to the mailbox, she saw a big pile of ash near a tree. Lily didn't know what it was, but she felt that it was important. Lily mailed her letter and went back home. She told her mom about the ash. Her mom said it was from a fire a few days ago. Lily was sad for the tree and wanted to help. She thought of a plan to fix the problem. The next day, Lily and her mom went to the store. They bought a new tree and some dirt. They went back to the big pile of ash and planted the new tree. The tree was happy and grew big and strong. Lily felt proud that she could help the tree and make the world a little better.
