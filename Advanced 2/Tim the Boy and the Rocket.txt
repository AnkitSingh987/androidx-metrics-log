One day, a boy named Tim found a big box. He wanted to unpack the box. Inside, he found a toy rocket. Tim was very happy. He showed the rocket to his friend, Sue. Sue said, "Wow, that's a cool rocket!" Tim and Sue played with the rocket all day. They made the rocket fly high in the sky. They had so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The rocket started to smell spicy. Tim and Sue looked at each other. "What is happening?" asked Sue. The rocket opened up, and out came a little spicy pepper. They laughed and played with the spicy pepper and the rocket.

One day, a big red truck was on the road. The truck was shiny and loud. A bald man was driving the truck. He was happy and liked to honk the horn. The truck had a big job to do. The bald man saw a little boy on the side of the road. The boy was pointing at the truck. He was excited and wanted to see the truck up close. The bald man stopped the truck and said, "Hi, do you like my big red truck?" The little boy said, "Yes, I love trucks! Can I see inside?" The bald man smiled and let the boy look inside the truck. The boy was so happy. They talked about trucks and honked the horn together. After a while, the boy said, "Thank you!" and went home. The bald man waved goodbye and drove away in his big red truck.

Once upon a time, there was a big room. In the room, there was a piano. A girl named Lily liked to play the piano. One day, Lily saw a boy enter the room. The boy looked at the piano too. Lily thought, "Maybe he wants to play with me." Lily asked the boy, "Do you want to play the piano with me?" The boy said, "Yes, I do." They played the piano together. They had fun. The boy said, "I have never played a piano before. Thank you for letting me play with you." Lily was happy because the boy was honest. Lily and the boy played the piano every day. They became good friends. They learned that being honest is important. When you are honest, you make good friends. And that is how Lily and the boy lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big blackboard in a small room. The blackboard was very kind and compassionate. It loved to help the kids learn to draw and write. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the room. He saw the blackboard and wanted to draw on it. Tim had a friend named Dare. Dare was a little bird who liked to play with Tim. Dare saw the blackboard and wanted to play too. But Dare didn't know how to draw. So, the compassionate blackboard showed Dare how to draw a nice picture. Tim and Dare were very happy. But then, a big wind came and blew the door open. The wind was very strong. It blew Tim, Dare, and the blackboard all around the room. The blackboard tried to help Tim and Dare, but it couldn't. The wind blew them all away, and they never saw each other again. And that was the sad end of Tim, Dare, and the compassionate blackboard.

Once upon a time, there was a small, cute animal. He was walking in the forest, looking for something to do. Suddenly, he heard a noise coming from behind a tight bush. He asked, "Who's there?" A small voice responded, "It is me." The animal walked closer and saw a small, blue bird. The bird took a deep breath and said, "I am so hungry and I'm stuck! Can you help me?" The animal was very happy to help. First, he pushed the bush apart very tightly until the bird was free. Then he gave the bird some food. The bird thanked the animal and said, "I am so happy that this happened. Now I can eat and fly away." The animal smiled and waved as the bird flew away. Then, he went back to searching for something exciting to happen.
