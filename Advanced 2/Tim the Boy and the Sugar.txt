Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat sugar. He would eat sugar all day. His mom would tell him, "Tim, eating too much sugar is bad for you." But Tim did not listen. One day, Tim saw a big jar of sugar on the table. He wanted to eat it, but the jar was too high for him. He tried to stand on his toes, but he still could not reach it. Then, he had an idea. He stood on a chair to get the sugar. But the chair was not strong, and it broke. Tim fell down and started to cry. Tim's mom came and hugged him. She said, "Tim, remember that eating too much sugar is bad for you. And standing on a weak chair is not safe." Tim learned his lesson. From that day on, he did not eat too much sugar and always listened to his mom. The moral of the story is to listen to the advice of those who care about you and to not do things that can hurt you.

Once upon a time there was a mild day. Jack and his mum were walking together. Suddenly a big museum appeared in front of them. Jack was excited. They went inside and saw lots of amazing things. Jack kept running around, exploring all the different things. His mum remained close. She watched Jack with a big smile on her face. After a while they had to leave. But Jack remained happy and excited to remember his museum trip.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Sue loved to play outside in the snow. One day, it was very icy and cold, but Sue still wanted to play. Sue put on her warm coat and boots. She went outside and saw a big lock on the gate. Sue was sad because she could not open it. She went back inside and asked her dad for help. Her dad came outside and opened the lock for her. Sue said, "Thank you, dad!" Now Sue could play in the icy snow. She made a snowman and had lots of fun. When she was done, she went back inside to get warm. Sue thanked her mom for the hot cocoa. She was happy and loved her family very much.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a smart girl named Amy. She loved to play at the park. Her favorite thing to do was to swing high in the sky. One sunny day, Amy went to the park with her mom. While Amy played on the swing, her mom sat on a bench nearby. Her mom had a cup of coffee to drink. Amy liked to watch her mom drink coffee, but she was too little to try it. As Amy swung high, she saw a bird in the sky. The bird flew near her and said, "You are a smart girl, Amy!" Amy smiled and kept swinging. She had a fun day at the park with her mom. They went home happy, and Amy's mom let her smell the coffee.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived an elderly lady. She loved to play hide and seek with the children. Every season, she would gather them in her big yard to play. One sunny day, while they were playing, the elderly lady decided to hide in a new spot. She found a big bush and hid behind it. The children looked and looked, but they could not find her. As the elderly lady waited for the children to find her, she heard a soft voice. It was a little bird! The bird said, "I saw you hide! Can I play too?" The elderly lady was surprised but happy. She said, "Of course, little bird!" The bird flew up high and hid in a tree. The children finally found the elderly lady and were very surprised to see the little bird with her. From that day on, the bird played hide and seek with the elderly lady and the children every season, and they all had lots of fun together.
