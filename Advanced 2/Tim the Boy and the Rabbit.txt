Once there was a little boy named Tim. He was only three years old. He had a pet rabbit named Fluffy. Tim loved Fluffy very much. One day, they were playing in the garden. Fluffy started running around and became a bit wild. He started to bite Tim. "Ow!" cried Tim. He ran inside the house and closed the door behind him. Mummy was upset when she saw what happened. "Tim, why did Fluffy bite you?" she asked. Tim tried to explain but mummy didn't believe him. "I'm sorry, Tim," she said, "but this is a serious matter. You'll have to get a lecture from Daddy." Daddy was very stern when he heard the news. "No more wild rabbit bites!" he said. He talked and talked about the importance of being careful with animals. After the lecture was finished, Tim promised Daddy he would try to be more careful around Fluffy. Mummy and Daddy were proud of Tim. They hugged him, and they all went outside to play. Fluffy and Tim had lots of fun together, but they made sure to stay away from wild rabbit bites.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a thin vendor. He sold yummy treats like ice cream and cookies. He had a little cart that he pushed around the town. One day, a big dog came to the vendor's cart. The dog wanted to eat all the treats! The vendor said, "No, dog! These treats are for the kids." But the dog did not listen. It tried to take the treats from the cart. Just then, a brave girl saw what was happening. She wanted to rescue the vendor and his treats. She picked up a stick and waved it at the big dog. "Shoo, dog! Go away!" she said. The dog saw the stick and ran away. The vendor was so happy that the girl had saved his treats. He thanked her and gave her a big ice cream cone. The girl felt proud that she had rescued the vendor, and they both enjoyed the yummy treats together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw a lot of different candy. He wanted to buy candy, but his mom said, "No, Tim. We need to buy healthy food." Tim was sad, but he had an idea. He took a candy and hid it in his pocket. When they got home, Tim went to his room and ate the candy. But then, he felt very thirsty. He needed to drink water. Tim went to the kitchen to drink water, but his mom saw him. She asked, "Why are you so thirsty?" Tim told her about the candy. His mom was not happy, but she said, "Next time, ask me first. Let's drink water together." Tim learned his lesson and they drank water together.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to hop on the path near his home. Every day, he would put on his little shoes and go outside to play. One day, while Bobo was hopping on the path, he found a pretty flower. Bobo thought it would be a nice gift for his mom. So, he put the flower in his bag and continued hopping. When Bobo got home, he gave the pretty flower to his mom. She was so happy and gave him a gentle hug. They both smiled and went inside to eat yummy carrots together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to fold paper into fun shapes. One day, Lily wanted to fold a big paper into a pretty bird. She tried and tried, but she could not make the bird. Lily's face was red and she felt frustrated. She asked her mom for help. Her mom tried to show her how to fold the paper, but Lily still could not make the bird. Lily was very sad. Lily did not want to fold paper anymore. She put the paper away and went to her room. She did not make the pretty bird and was still sad. The end.
