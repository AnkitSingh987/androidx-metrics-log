Once upon a time, there was a smelly goat. The goat lived on a farm and liked to roam around. One day, the goat became disturbed when it saw something strange. It was a man carrying a price tag! The goat had never seen this before, so it began to follow the man closely. As the goat followed, the man became more and more disturbed. The man kept looking around, and the goat hopped along beside him. They traveled further and further into the forest, the man becoming more and more nervous. Finally, after a long walk, the goat and the man stopped in a clearing. The goat watched the man closely as he placed the price tag on a tree. Then, the man quickly backed away. The goat was confused. What was the smelly price tag for? The goat looked at the price tag and sniffed it. Suddenly, the goat realized-- the smelly price tag was bait for animals!

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to watch a video. He asked his mom, "Can I watch a video, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can watch one video." Tim was very happy. He settled on the floor in front of the TV. He picked a fun video about a dog and a cat playing together. The dog and cat were friends. They played with a ball and ran in the park. After the video, Tim wanted to watch more. But his mom said, "No, it's time to go outside and play." Tim was sad, but he knew he could watch another video another day. So, he went outside and played with his friends, just like the dog and cat in the video.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy shield that he loved to play with. One day, he saw something unusual on the ground. It was a small, green ball. Tim picked it up and decided to play with it. As he played with the ball, he gave it a big squeeze. Suddenly, a tiny frog jumped out of the ball. "Ribbit!" said the frog. "Thank you for helping me. I was stuck inside that ball." Tim was surprised but happy to have a new friend. The frog told Tim that he had a magic power. He could make Tim's shield grow big or small. Tim asked the frog to make his shield big. The frog touched the shield, and it grew so big that it covered Tim's whole body. Tim and the frog played with the shield all day, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a big lion. The lion was very hungry and wanted to eat lunch. He looked for food and found a small rabbit. The lion said, "I will eat you for lunch, little rabbit!" The rabbit was scared but had a smart idea. "Wait!" said the rabbit. "I know where you can find a bigger lunch. Follow me!" The lion agreed and followed the rabbit. They walked and walked until they found a big cave. The rabbit said, "In there is a big lunch for you!" The lion, very excited, went into the cave. But inside the cave was a big bear! The bear roared loudly at the lion. The lion was so scared that he ran away. The rabbit had tricked the lion and saved himself. The rabbit went back home, happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a switch on the wall. In the room, there was a little boy named Tim and his cat, Mimi. They played all day and had fun. Tim was a fine boy, and Mimi was a fine cat. They were best friends. One day, Tim remembered that he never used the switch on the wall. So, he decided to see what would happen if he turned it on. Tim went to the wall and turned the switch. Suddenly, the room changed into a fun playground with slides and swings. Tim and Mimi were very happy and played all day. But then, something unexpected happened. When Tim turned the switch again, Mimi changed into a big, friendly bear! Tim was surprised but not scared. The bear was still his friend Mimi. They played together in the playground and had even more fun. In the end, Tim and Mimi learned that the switch was magic and could make their days more exciting.
