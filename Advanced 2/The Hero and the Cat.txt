Once upon a time, there was a hero. The hero had long arms. He liked to help people. One day, the hero saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared. The hero tried to help the cat. He used his long arms to reach the cat. But the cat was too high. The hero did not quit. He asked his friends to help. They made a tall ladder. The hero climbed the ladder. He saved the cat. Everyone was happy. The hero and his friends went home. They knew they could help when they worked together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a favorite toy, a small doll named Tina. Tina wore a pretty top and always had a big smile on her face. Lily loved to hug Tina because she was soft and reliable. One day, while playing outside, Lily saw a big dog. The dog looked sad and lost. Lily wanted to help the dog, so she brought Tina with her. As they got closer, the dog saw Tina and thought she was a toy to play with. The dog took Tina in its mouth and ran away. Lily was very sad and missed her doll. Later, the dog came back with Tina. The dog felt bad for taking Tina away from Lily. The dog had learned that taking things from others was not nice. Lily hugged Tina and the dog, happy to have her doll back. The dog now knew the importance of sharing and being kind to others. From that day on, the dog became Lily's reliable friend, and they all played together happily.

Toby was very upset. Every day, he would watch his friends on the playground. They were always so much better at running, jumping and climbing. Toby wished he could be like them, but he didn't know how. One day, Toby's mommy told him not to worry. She said that if Toby practiced and worked hard, he could improve and be just as good at running, jumping and climbing. Toby was hopeful. So, Toby practiced every day and every week. With each passing day he felt better and better. After a lot of time and hard work, Toby was running, jumping and climbing on the playground just like his friends! Toby was so happy! He was not upset anymore and he was proud that he had worked hard and improved. Toby's mommy was proud of him, too! Toby showed to everyone that nothing is impossible if you are determined, and put in the time.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy cars. He loved them so much. He had a fast red car, a big blue truck, and a shiny yellow bus. Tim was very enthusiastic when he played with his cars. He liked to make them go with great speed. Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. Sue saw Tim's red car and thought it was very cool. She asked Tim, "Can you lend me your red car to play with?" Tim said, "Yes, you can play with my red car." They played together, making the red car and the blue truck race with lots of speed. After a while, it was time for Sue to go home. She gave the red car back to Tim and said, "Thank you for lending me your car. I had so much fun playing with it." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome. We can play with the cars again tomorrow." They both were very happy and couldn't wait to play together again.

One day, a little cat named Loop was very eager to play. Loop loved to run and jump all day. Loop's best friend, a dog named Spot, liked to play too. They were very happy together. Loop and Spot saw a big ball. They both wanted to play with it. Loop said, "I want to play with the ball!" Spot said, "No, I want to play with the ball!" They both tried to grab the ball at the same time. The ball rolled away very fast. Loop and Spot looked at each other and said, "Let's come together to get the ball!" They ran after the ball as fast as they could. They both jumped and caught the ball. Loop and Spot laughed and played with the big ball together. They were happy to share and have fun.
