Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was upset because the year was about to end. She was sad because she had so much fun this year, and she didn't want it to end. One day, Lily went for a walk with her mom and dad. They saw a big tree with many leaves. The leaves were all colors: red, orange, and yellow. Lily's mom told her that when the leaves fall, it means the year is ending. Lily felt sad, but her mom said, "Don't worry, a new year will start soon!" Lily thought about all the fun things she could do in the new year. She could play with her friends, go to the park, and eat ice cream. She started to feel less upset. When the year ended, Lily was ready for a new year full of fun and adventures. And she knew that even when a year ends, there will always be a new one to enjoy.

Once upon a time, in an ancient sea, there was a little oyster. The oyster liked to swing with the waves. It was fun to move back and forth with the water. One day, while swinging, the oyster saw a big, shiny stone. The oyster wanted the stone. It thought the stone would make it happy. The oyster took the stone, but soon it felt sad. The stone was too big and heavy. The oyster could not swing with the waves anymore. The oyster learned that taking things we do not need can make us unhappy.

One day, a little girl named Lisa was playing outside. She saw dark clouds in the sky and felt a little scared. Lisa was curious about what would happen next. She asked her mom, "Will there be lightning?" Her mom looked at the sky and said, "Maybe, but don't worry. We can go inside if it starts to rain." Lisa felt better and continued to play. Soon, it started to rain, and Lisa saw a flash of lightning. She quickly ran inside with her mom. They sat by the window and watched the rain fall. Lisa asked her mom, "Why does lightning happen?" Her mom just shrugged and said, "I'm not sure, but it's fun to watch, isn't it?" Lisa smiled and nodded, enjoying the storm from the safety of her home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a long stick called a staff. He liked to poke things with his staff. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. It was difficult for him to poke the top of the tree with his staff. So, he poked the bottom instead. A squirrel ran out of the tree and looked at Tim. "Hi, squirrel!" Tim said. The squirrel looked at Tim's staff and said, "Please don't poke my tree! It's my home." Tim felt sad and said, "Sorry, squirrel." They became friends and played together in the park.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fancy house. In this house, there was a big family. They had a cat named Hurricane. Hurricane was a very playful cat who loved to run and jump all day. One day, Hurricane was very tired from playing. He wanted to sleep. He looked for a soft and warm place to rest. He found a big, cozy bed in one of the rooms. Hurricane jumped on the bed and curled up into a little ball. As Hurricane began to sleep, the family came into the room. They saw Hurricane sleeping on the bed and thought it was very cute. They decided to let him sleep there. When Hurricane woke up, he felt very happy and loved. From that day on, the fancy house became Hurricane's favorite place to sleep.

One day, a brilliant sun rose in the sky. A small ball named Billy saw the sun and wanted to roll outside. He knew that he needed to use his muscle to move, so he tried very hard. Billy rolled and rolled, and soon he was outside in the warm sun. As he rolled, he met a big ball named Bob. Bob said, "Hi, I like to roll too! Let's roll together." So, Billy and Bob rolled and rolled, having fun in the sun. They knew that they were going to have a great day. After a while, the sun began to set. Billy and Bob were tired, but they had used their muscle well. They said goodbye and rolled back to their homes, knowing that they would have many more brilliant days together.
