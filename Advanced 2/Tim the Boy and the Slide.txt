Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. He felt lucky because he had a big park near his house. In the park, there was a tall slide that Tim liked to climb and slide down. One sunny day, Tim went to the park to play on the slide. He climbed up the slide and got ready to slide down. But just as he was about to slide, he saw a big, fluffy dog sitting at the bottom of the slide. Tim did not want to slide down and hit the dog, so he stayed at the top of the slide and waited. The dog looked up at Tim, and to Tim's surprise, it started to climb the slide too! Tim was so happy to have a new friend who liked to climb and slide as much as he did. They played on the slide together all day, and Tim felt even luckier than before.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw an elderly lady sitting on a bench. She had a big hat and a kind smile. Tim's mom wanted to introduce him to the nice lady. They walked over to her and said, "Hi, I'm Tim and this is my mom." The elderly lady smiled and said, "Hello, Tim and mom. My name is Mrs. Brown. I like to come to the park and feed the birds." She had some bread in her hand and threw a piece to the birds. Tim liked watching the birds eat the bread. Suddenly, Tim saw a big red ball near a tree. He pointed to it and said, "Look, a ball!" He ran to get it, but when he picked it up, a little squirrel jumped out from behind the tree. Tim was surprised, and so was the squirrel. They both looked at each other, then laughed. Tim gave the ball a little roll, and the squirrel chased it. From that day on, Tim, his mom, and Mrs. Brown would meet at the park and play with their new squirrel friend.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They were walking outside and Jill saw something shiny. She quickly ran to it and grabbed the handle. "What is it?" asked Jack. "It's a jar and it smells bitter," said Jill. Jack warned his friend, "Let go of the handle, Jill. It could be dangerous!" Jill didn't listen and kept a tight grip on the handle. Suddenly, a loud noise erupted from the jar and both Jill and Jack were scared. Jack took the jar away from Jill and told her. "We should be careful before we grab something. You never know what it can do." The two friends went back home, and learnt an important lesson that day - never grab something until you know it is safe.

Molly was a little girl. She was packing her bag. She was very frightened. She had to go on a boat. "Are you ready to go?" asked mommy. Molly nodded. She hugged her mommy, and looked at the big boat. The boat was huge, and it was floating on the water. There were many rows of seats. Molly picked her seat, and put her bag on the floor. Then, the boat started moving. Molly was very frightened. She held onto her mommy's hand. As they came closer to their destination, Molly could hear the waves splashing against the boat. Finally, they arrived. Molly was so glad she had arrived safely. She was no longer frightened. She was ready to explore the new place.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a toy bear named Buzzy. They were best friends and did everything together. Buzzy belonged to Lily, and Lily belonged to Buzzy. They loved each other very much. One day, they went to the park to play. They played on the swings, the slide, and the seesaw. They laughed and had fun. But then, Lily saw a group of kids playing together. She wanted to join them, but she was embarrassed because she thought they might not like Buzzy. Lily took a deep breath and walked up to the kids with Buzzy. The kids smiled and welcomed them to play. They all played together, and Buzzy made many new friends. They had so much fun, and there was peace in the park. In the end, Lily wasn't embarrassed anymore because she knew that Buzzy belonged with her and her new friends.
