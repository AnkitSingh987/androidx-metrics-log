Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, strong lion named Leo. Leo liked to roar very loud. He lived near a road where a big, yellow bus passed by every day. One day, the bus got stuck in the mud. The wheels could not move, and the people inside were sad. They needed help. Leo saw the bus and wanted to help the people. Leo used his strong legs and big paws to push the bus out of the mud. He roared loudly to show he was strong. The people in the bus were so happy and thanked Leo for his help. From that day on, Leo learned that being strong is good, but using his strength to help others is even better. And the people in the town loved and respected Leo, the strong and helpful lion.

Once there was a girl named Sofia. Sofia was three years old. She had a mattress that she slept on every night. One day, Sofia noticed that there was a big tear in her mattress! She was very worried. So Sofia's parents went out and found a man who could repair her mattress. He was very skilled at repairing things. Sofia watched as he fixed the big rip. He used some glue and some tape to make it better. When the man finished, the mattress was good as new. Sofia was very happy. She was so excited that she jumped on the mattress and enjoyed a good night's sleep. Sofia's friends were very envious. They wanted mattresses like Sofia's. But Sofia knew that her mattress was extra special because she watched somebody repair it.

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a little bird. This bird had a delicate voice. She loved to sing all day long. The bird's songs made everyone happy. The city was full of joy when she sang. One day, the bird flew to a park in the city. She saw a little girl who was sad. The bird wanted to help the little girl. So, she started to sing a beautiful song. As the bird sang, the girl's face lit up with a big smile. The delicate song made her very happy. The bird was happy too, because she could help the girl. From that day on, the bird sang in the city every day, spreading happiness and joy to all.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to draw and paint. Tim was very creative. One day, he saw a big trophy in his dad's room. He wanted to win a trophy too. Tim thought of a fun idea. He would have a drawing contest with his friends. He asked his mom to help him make a trophy. They used a cup, some paper, and shiny stickers. The trophy was ready for the contest. Tim and his friends sat outside to draw. They drew pictures of their favorite things. Tim drew a big sun, and his friend drew a funny cat. They all had a great time. In the end, everyone got to hold the trophy. They were all winners.

One day, Tom had a very special tool. It was big and yellow, and Tom was so proud of it. Every time he used it, it would glow brilliantly. Everyone was amazed how intelligent Tom was with his tool. Tom was always eager to use his tool and show it off. One day, Tom decided to use his tool. But as he was playing, something went wrong. The tool’s glow quickly changed to a deep, menacing red. Everyone ran away in fear, including Tom. He was so scared that he quickly ran away too and never came back. But something even worse happened. Whenever the tool would glow red, a rain of bad things would come afterwards. People were terrified of the tool, and bitterly blamed Tom for it. Tom never returned and his beloved tool was left behind. People avoided the tool, scared of its deep red glow. Unfortunately, Tom and his tool were never seen again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a modern fountain. The fountain was in the middle of a big park. Kids loved to play near the fountain. They liked to watch the water go up and down. One sunny day, two friends, Tim and Sue, went to the park to play. They saw the fountain and ran to it. Tim said, "Let's splash in the water!" But Sue said, "No, I don't want to get wet." They did not agree. Tim and Sue sat by the fountain and thought. Tim said, "Let's throw rocks in the water." Sue liked the idea. They found small rocks and threw them in. They laughed and played. They had so much fun at the modern fountain, even when they disagreed.
