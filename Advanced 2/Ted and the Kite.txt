Ted was a dependable three year old. Everywhere he went, people noticed him and smiled. One day, Ted was walking in his front yard when he stopped to notice the world around him. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise. Ted became scared and didn't know what to do. He started to cry. Luckily, his mom heard him and came out to investigate. She noticed Ted's frightened face and asked him what was wrong. Ted pointed up and said, "Noise." His mom looked up and saw a kite soaring in the sky. She explained to Ted that it was just the kite and nothing to be scared of. Ted was a little relieved, but he was still scared. His mom gave him a big hug and said, "Don't be afraid, the world is dependable. You can trust that nothing bad will happen to you." Ted smiled and calmed down. He hugged his mom tightly, feeling happy and safe. From then on, he was no longer scared of the noise in his world.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red cat. He liked to sit in the sun all day. One day, he saw a little bug near a cobweb. The bug wanted to play with the cat, but he was scared of the cobweb. The cat, being foolish, tried to help the bug. He jumped up and tried to break the cobweb. But the cobweb was too strong, and the cat got stuck in it too! A little girl saw the cat and the bug in the cobweb. She laughed and said, "Silly cat and bug! You should be more careful!" She helped them get out of the cobweb, and they all became good friends. They played together every day, and never played near the cobwebs again.

Once, there was a very small boy named Mike. He was only three years old and he wanted to explore the world. He often pinched his mom and dad. One day, Mike decided he was going to find an adventure. He came across a really big truck that said "Emergency". Mike was very interested and he wanted to open the door to see what was inside. Suddenly, his mom and dad came around the corner. They said, "Mike, this truck is very important. You cannot go inside!" Mike hoped he could look, but his mom and dad said no. They told him, "Sometimes emergencies need protection from curious people like you!" His mom and dad smiled, but Mike was a bit sad. He wanted to have an adventure. Mike was quiet for a little while and then he said, "Maybe one day I can help if there is an emergency! That would be fun and important, right?" His mom and dad smiled and gave him a hug.

Lily was sad. She missed her mom and dad. They went away for work and left her with Grandma. Grandma was nice, but Lily wanted her mom and dad. She lay in bed and looked at the ceiling. The ceiling had stars and moons and planets. Her dad painted them for her. He said they were like a big map of the sky. Lily liked the ceiling, but she wished her dad was here to tell her stories about the stars and moons and planets. She heard a knock on the door. It was Grandma. She came in with a tray. On the tray there was a glass of milk, a cookie and a book. "Hello, Lily," Grandma said. "How are you feeling?" "I'm sad, Grandma," Lily said. "I miss mom and dad." "I know, Lily," Grandma said. "I miss them too. But they will be back soon. They love you very much. And they are working hard to make the world a better place." Grandma put the tray on the nightstand. She sat on the bed and hugged Lily. "Do you want me to read you a story?" Grandma asked. Lily nodded. She liked stories. Grandma picked up the book. It was about a brave princess who went on an adventure. Grandma read the story with a soft voice. Lily listened and looked at the pictures. She felt a little better. She was grateful for Grandma. Grandma was kind and funny and smart. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Grandma. You are the best." Grandma smiled and said, "You are welcome, Lily. You are the best too." They finished the story and said good night. Grandma turned off the light and kissed Lily on the forehead. Lily closed her eyes and thought of mom and dad. She hoped they were safe and happy. She hoped they would come back soon. She looked at the ceiling and saw the stars and moons and planets. She felt a warm feeling in her heart. She knew they were watching over her. She fell asleep and dreamed of the brave princess.
