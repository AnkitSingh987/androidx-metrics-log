Once upon a time, there was a fisherman named Tom. He had a wide boat to catch many fish. One sunny day, Tom went to the sea to fish. He sat in his wide boat and waited for the fish to come. "Hello, Mr. Fisherman!" said a big fish, jumping out of the water. Tom was very surprised. He didn't know that fish could talk. The big fish looked at Tom and asked, "Why do you catch my friends?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "I catch fish to eat and sell at the market. But I don't want to make you sad." The big fish smiled and said, "I have an idea. I will teach you a special sign. When you do this sign, many fish will come to you." The big fish taught Tom the special sign. Tom did the sign, and many fish came to his wide boat. He only took a few fish to eat and let the others go. From that day on, Tom and the big fish became good friends. The moral of the story is to be kind to others, even if they are different from you. And when you are kind, good things can happen, like making new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to dance. She would dance all day long. But there was a problem. Sue was not very good at dancing. She would fall and get hurt. Sue felt sad. One day, Sue met a big, tough dog named Max. Max could dance very well. Sue asked Max to help her gain better dance moves. Max said yes. They danced together every day. Sue started to dance better and better. Max was a great teacher. One day, Sue and Max had a dance show. Everyone came to watch. Sue and Max danced together. They were so good! Everyone clapped and cheered. Sue was so happy. She had gained better dance moves. The moral of the story is: with help from friends, we can learn and grow.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sara. She was very excited because she was about to watch her favorite cartoon. She ran to the TV and said, "I want to open the TV so I can watch my cartoon!" As soon as she said that, her mother came in the room and said, "Good idea, sweetie! But first we have to find the cartoon you want to watch." So, Sara and her mother searched together until they found the cartoon Sara wanted. Sara was so excited, she ran to the TV and said, "Open the TV now mummy!" Her mother smiled and said, "Yes, let's turn on the TV." So they opened the TV and as soon as it was open Sara watched her cartoon and laughed and smiled the whole time. It was a great day for Sara and everyone was happy. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Mia's mother was very tired. She told Mia, "Please play inside today, I need to rest." Mia played inside, but she missed playing outside with her friends. She saw a small bird outside her window. The bird seemed to want Mia to follow it. Mia opened the door and the bird lead her outside. The bird took Mia to a beautiful garden. There, Mia found her friends playing with the bird's family. Mia's mother woke up and saw Mia was gone. She went outside and found Mia in the garden. Mia said, "Look, Mom! The bird lead me here to play with my friends!" Mia's mother smiled and they all played together in the garden.

Once upon a time, in a big green garden, there was a smart ant named Andy. He loved to enjoy the warm sun and play with his friends. Andy had many friends like bees, butterflies, and other ants. They all liked to have fun together. One day, Andy met a new friend named Betty the bee. Betty said, "Hi Andy! Let's play together and enjoy the sun." Andy replied, "Yes, I would love to play with you, Betty!" They played all day, running and laughing in the garden. At the end of the day, Andy and Betty were very happy. They had enjoyed their time together and became best friends. From that day on, the smart ant and his new friend, Betty the bee, played together every day in the big green garden. They knew they would always have lots of fun and enjoy their time together.
