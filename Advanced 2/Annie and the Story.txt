Title: Annie and the Story

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Annie. Annie was three years old and excited to visit the fair. There was a spectacular statue which made the fair even more beautiful. The little girl asked her mum, "Please can I touch the statue?" Mum said, "Yes, sweetheart, you can touch it." Annie reached out for the statue, but just before her finger touched it, she stopped. She looked back at her mum and said, "Mum, is it ok if I touch it? I don't want to do something I'm not allowed to." Mum smiled and said, "Well done, Annie. That is a very kind thing to do. This statue is very special and it belongs to everyone. By showing respect, we are showing how fair we can be to everyone." Annie agreed and never lied again. She learned to always be respectful and treat everyone treat everyone fairly. The moral of the story is that it is important to be honest, kind and respectful to others.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. The bear loved to hug. Every day, he would go out in the sunshine and give hugs. The bear felt very calm when he hugged his friends. All the animals in the forest liked to hug the bear too. One day, the bear went to hug his friend, the rabbit. But the rabbit was not there. The bear looked everywhere for the rabbit. He felt sad because he could not find his friend. The sunshine was not making him feel calm today. Then, the bear heard a noise. He looked up and saw the rabbit in a tree! The rabbit had learned to climb. The bear was very surprised. The rabbit climbed down from the tree and gave the bear a big hug. Now, the bear felt calm and happy in the sunshine again.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful hotel. In the hotel, there was a big room where all the kids could play. The kids loved to play with their toys and have fun. One day, a new family came to the hotel. They had a little girl named Lily. Lily was very shy and did not want to play with the other kids. The kids tried to let Lily join them, but she was too scared. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came into the room. The dog went to Lily and let her pet him. Lily smiled and started to play with the dog. The other kids saw this and joined in. They all played together and had a great time. And from that day on, Lily was not shy anymore and made lots of new friends at the peaceful hotel.

One day, a tall tree was in the park. It was very big and strong. It had many friends who lived around it. The tall tree liked to talk to its friends every day. One day, the tall tree saw a long line of ants. The ants were walking in the park. The tall tree wanted to talk to the ants, too. It asked the ants, "Where are you going?" The ants said, "We are going to find food. We will come back soon." The tall tree waited for the ants to come back. It knew they would be back with food. Soon, the ants came back with lots of food. The tall tree was happy to see the ants again. It talked to the ants and they shared their food with the tall tree's friends. The tall tree was very happy to have new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate clown named Bobo. Bobo loved to study and learn new things. He lived in a small house in a big town. One day, Bobo went to the park to play. He saw a little girl named Lily who was sad. She had lost her toy. Bobo wanted to help her. He said, "Don't cry, Lily. I will find your toy." Bobo and Lily looked for the toy together. They looked under the trees and behind the bushes. Finally, they found the toy near the swings. Lily was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Bobo!" From that day on, Bobo and Lily became good friends. They played together every day and studied new things. And they lived happily ever after.
