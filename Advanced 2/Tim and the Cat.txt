One day, a little boy named Tim found a magic pencil. It was a very fast pencil. When Tim drew with it, the pictures would come to life. Tim wanted to have a friend to play with. So, he drew a cat. The cat jumped out of the paper and said, "Hi, I am Cat!" Tim and Cat wanted to play. They decided to run outside. They ran very fast. They ran to the park and played on the swings. They laughed and had fun. "You run fast, Tim!" said Cat. "You run fast too, Cat!" Tim said. When the sun started to go down, Tim and Cat went back home. Tim used the magic pencil to draw a bed for Cat. Cat jumped on the bed and said, "Thank you, Tim." They were both very happy. Tim knew that with his magic pencil, he would never be lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to write. She would write all day. One day, she wanted to write a story about a crazy cat. But, she could not find her pen. So, she started a search. Lily looked everywhere for her pen. She looked under her bed, in her toy box, and in the kitchen. But she could not find it. She felt sad. She really wanted to write her crazy cat story. Then, something unexpected happened. Lily's dog, Max, came running in with her pen in his mouth. He had found it! Lily was so happy. She hugged Max and started to write her story. But, as she wrote, the crazy cat in her story came to life! It jumped out of the paper and played with Lily and Max. They all had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy who loved to read books. He had lots of books about the universe, which he read every day. He wanted to know all about the stars, planets and asteroids. One day, he was in the park and saw an old man sitting on a bench. He went up to the old man and said “What book are you reading?” The old man looked up and smiled. “I am reading about the universe. It’s very dependable.” The boy was very excited. He asked the old man if he could read the book too. “Of course,” said the old man. “If you are careful, you can borrow it and return it when you’re done. The universe is an exciting place to learn about!” So the boy thanked the old man and borrowed the book. He read it every day in the park, learning about the universe. He was very happy and always looked forward to his visits to the park.

One day, a little dog went for a walk. The sun was warm and the sky was blue. The dog was happy to be outside. The dog saw a big tree and wanted to sit under it. When he got there, he saw a stranger sitting there too. The stranger was a big, fluffy cat. The dog was scared, but the cat looked nice. The dog and the cat sat under the tree together. They talked and played all day. They became good friends. The warm sun made them feel happy. And they were not strangers anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a light blue automobile named Bobby. Bobby loved to match things. He liked to find things that were the same color as him. One day, Bobby saw a light blue ball. He said, "Hi, Ball! We match! We are both light blue!" The ball said, "Yes, Bobby! We do match! Let's be friends!" So, Bobby and the ball became friends. Bobby and the ball played together every day. They liked to find other things that were light blue. They found a light blue bird, a light blue hat, and a light blue cat. They all became friends and had fun playing together. And they all matched!

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to complete a big puzzle. He was very independent and wanted to do it all by himself. He had a cooler full of snacks to eat while he worked on the puzzle. Tim's friend, Sue, came over and saw him with the puzzle. She asked, "Can I help you with the puzzle?" Tim thought about it and said, "No, I want to do it all by myself." Sue was sad but understood that Tim wanted to be independent. After a while, Tim got tired and hungry. He opened the cooler and shared his snacks with Sue. They laughed and talked. Tim realized that sometimes it's okay to ask for help. Together, they completed the puzzle and had a great time.
