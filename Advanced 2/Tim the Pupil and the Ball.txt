Once upon a time, there was a little pupil named Tim. Tim was a good boy, but sometimes he would get angry. He would want things that he could not have. One day, Tim saw a big red ball in the school yard. He wanted the ball so much, but it was not his. The next day, Tim saw the big red ball again. He knew it was not his, but he still wanted it. He thought about taking the ball, but he knew that would make the other kids angry. So, Tim walked away and played with his own toys. A few days later, Tim found a small blue ball in his bag. He did not know where it came from, but he was happy to have it. He played with the blue ball all day and forgot about the big red ball. The plot twist was that the blue ball was a gift from his teacher, who saw how much Tim wanted the red ball. The teacher wanted to make Tim happy, and it worked. Tim learned that sometimes, good things come when you wait and do not take what is not yours.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to help his mom in the kitchen. His mom asked him to order some food for dinner. Tim was excited to help and said "Yes, mommy!" Tim ordered the food and waited for it to come. When the food arrived, it was heavy. Tim tried to carry the heavy food inside, but it was hard. He asked his mom for help, and they carried it together. Tim was proud that he could help his mom with the heavy food. After dinner, Tim's mom gave him a reward for being a good helper. The reward was a big, shiny star sticker. Tim was so happy and put the sticker on his shirt. He showed everyone his reward and told them how he helped his mom. Everyone was proud of Tim.

Mama was in the kitchen cooking dinner. She was using the big spoon to stir the food. Suddenly, little Timmy came into the kitchen. He was staring at the spoon. He was very curious about it. Mama gave Timmy a gentle smile, but he didn't smile back. He grabbed the spoon from her hand and ran away. Mama called after him, but he wasn't listening. She started to get angry. Mama chased after Timmy. She wasn't very fast and soon he was just a distant figure in the hallway. Mama was frustrated and she slapped the floor. At the sound of the slap, Timmy stopped. Mama could see from the kitchen that Timmy had dropped the spoon and he was standing still in the hallway. Mama walked up to Timmy and said, "Don't take things without asking, Timmy. If you have questions, just ask me. Everything is easier that way!" Timmy nodded, looking understanding. Mama and Timmy hugged and went back to the kitchen. Mama put the spoon away until the next time they needed it.

Once upon a time, a fine man had an axe. He used it to cut wood. The man was very happy with his axe. One day, the man left his axe outside. It rained a lot, and the axe got wet. The rain started to spoil the axe. The man was sad. The next day, the man saw ants on the axe. The ants used the spoiled axe to make a new home. The man was happy again because his axe helped the ants. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy cars and go on adventures with his mommy and daddy. One day, they decided to go on a trip to the city. Tim was very excited! In the city, they saw a big hole in the ground. It was a subway! Tim's mommy said, "We have to be careful here. It can be dangerous." They held hands and walked slowly. They saw a big sign that said "yield". Tim's daddy told him, "Yield means we have to stop and look around to be safe." They went down the stairs to the subway. It was dark and loud. Tim was a little scared, but he held his mommy's hand tight. The subway came and they got on. Tim looked out the window and saw the city go by very fast. He was happy and couldn't wait for their next adventure.
