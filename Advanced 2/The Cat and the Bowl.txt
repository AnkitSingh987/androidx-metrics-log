Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little cat. The cat loved cream. One day, the cat saw a big bowl of cream in the kitchen. The cat was very happy. The cat wanted to get the cream. But there was a big dog sleeping near the bowl. The cat was scared of the dog. The cat thought, "How can I get the cream?" The cat had an idea. The cat found a toy and threw it far away. The dog woke up and ran to get the toy. The cat was not scared anymore. The cat got the cream and drank it all. The cat was very happy.

One day it was raining outside. A little boy wanted to have some fun in the rain, but he did not have any rain equipment. He saw a huge tube in his yard and he got a great idea. He asked his mom for some extra umbrellas, rain boots and rain coats. He gathered all the materials and put together a tube that was big enough to slip inside and slide down! The little boy was so excited! He put on the raincoat and boots, and grabbed the umbrellas. He placed the umbrellas over the tube and started sliding down. He had so much fun! As more and more kids noticed how much fun the little boy was having, they started to come over and wanted to join in. Soon the yard became a competition of who could stay on the tube the longest! The little boy was the winner every time! He was very competitive and proud of his skills! The little boy enjoyed the rain and playing with his friends. Even on a rainy day, he could still have lots of fun!

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to play all day long. One day, Spot found a tunnel in the park. He was very excited. Spot ran into the tunnel and shouted, "Hello!" His voice repeated, "Hello, hello, hello!" Spot thought it was fun. He ran in and out of the tunnel, barking and listening to his voice repeat. A little girl named Sue saw Spot playing. She walked up to the tunnel and said, "Hi, doggy!" Spot barked back, "Woof, woof!" They played together, laughing and listening to their voices repeat in the tunnel. They had a great day at the park.

Once there was a little girl called Louise. She had a secret that was hidden in her chimney. She looked into it but couldn't understand what it was. One day her mother asked her to clean the chimney. Louise was scared; she knew her secret would be revealed. She tried her best to avoid it but her mother was determined. Finally, Louise had to do it and as she looked into the chimney, her secret was revealed. It was a big bad mouse! Louise screamed and tried to run away but the mouse chased her. She was so scared that she ran out of the house. She was never able to go back and get her secret back. Louise had learned a valuable lesson – it's better to keep secrets hidden. But it had been too late.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing with her toy blocks. She wanted to build a big tower. Mia was very alert and watched the tower grow bigger and bigger. She was so excited! Mia's mommy came into the room and saw Mia's tower. "Wow, Mia! Your tower is so big!" she said. "Can you add more blocks to make it even bigger?" Mia nodded and used her finger to pick up a block. She put it on top of the tower. Just then, Mia's little brother, Max, crawled into the room. He saw the tower and wanted to play too. Mia saw Max coming and knew she had to protect her tower. She quickly picked up her toy car and gave it to Max. "Here, Max! Play with the car," she said. Max was happy with the car and left the tower alone. Mia smiled and added more blocks to her tower. It was the biggest tower she had ever built!
