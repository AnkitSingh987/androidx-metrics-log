Once upon a time, in a big palace, there lived a king and a queen. They had a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily found some paint and decided to paint the palace walls. Lily painted the walls with red, blue, and green. She was very happy. But when the king and queen saw the walls, they were not happy. They said, "Lily, you made a mess on the palace walls!" Lily felt sorry. She knew she did something wrong. She said, "I am sorry, Mom and Dad. I will clean the walls." Lily cleaned the walls with soap and water. The king and queen forgave her, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a girl who loved snacks went out with her mom. The mom said "We are going to get you a snack". The girl was so excited! She looked in her mom's wallet, but her mom said "This is not enough money. We need to go somewhere else". They went to the store and the girl saw so many delicious snacks. She wanted to get them all! Her mom said, "It looks like those snacks are too expensive. Let's look somewhere else". They went to the next store and the mom said "Here we can pay". The girl was so happy! She chose a few snacks that looked yummy and delicious. The girl was so excited to go home and try her new snacks. She paid with her mom's money and they went home. She couldn't wait to eat the snack and it was delicious!

One day, a girl named Lily went to the market with her mom. They wanted to buy some food for their dinner. They saw many things like apples, bananas, and carrots. Lily's mom picked up some apples and said, "Let's buy these!" On their way home, they walked by a river. The river was very pretty, and there were ducks swimming in it. Lily loved to watch the ducks play in the water. She asked her mom if they could stop and look at the ducks for a while. Her mom said, "Yes, we can watch the ducks for a little bit." After they watched the ducks, they went home and made dinner with the food they bought. They had apples, bananas, and carrots to eat. Lily was grateful for the food and the fun time watching the ducks at the river. She said, "Thank you, Mom, for the food and the fun day!" Her mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I'm happy you had a good day."

Once upon a time there was a lady. She was very polite and kind. One day she went out for a walk. As she walked, she saw a little door. She was so curious that she decided to enter. When she stepped through the door, she found herself in a beautiful room. In the middle of the room there was a table with a needle and thread. The lady picked up the needle and thread and began to make a stitch. She worked hard making each stitch carefully and neatly. When she was done, she had made a beautiful blanket with many stitches. She smiled and said, “What a polite job!” The lady was so pleased, she gave the blanket away to a little baby. The baby was so happy and hugged the blanket tightly. From that day onward, the lady was known as the “polite stitcher”.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to wander around the park. The park was very big and had lots of trees and flowers. Buddy loved to sniff the flowers and chase the birds. One day, Buddy was very busy at the park. He ran and played with his friends. They played fetch and chased their tails. After playing, Buddy was very hungry. He wanted a snack. Buddy's mom had a yummy snack in her bag. It was a tasty bone! Buddy wagged his tail and ate his snack. He was very happy. After his snack, Buddy was ready to play again. He wandered back to his friends and played until the sun went down.
