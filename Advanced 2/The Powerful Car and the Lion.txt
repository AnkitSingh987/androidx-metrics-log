One day, a powerful car went to the zoo. The car could talk and make friends. At the zoo, the car met a big lion. The lion said, "Hello, car! Let's play together." The car was happy and they played all day. The car wanted to show the lion how it could reverse. The lion thought it was fun. They played and laughed. Then, the car had an idea. "Let's play reverse with all the animals in the zoo!" said the car. The car and the lion went to see the other animals. They taught them how to reverse too. All the animals played together and had fun. The zoo was now a place where everyone could reverse and play. And the powerful car was the happiest of all.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was very weak and small because she was only three years old. One day, Lily heard about a big victory that her family was going to have. Lily wanted to join in the celebration, but because she was so young and weak, she couldn't help much. So she decided to stir a big pot of soup that was on the stove. When Lily was stirring the soup, she saw some clouds of steam and thought they looked like beautiful bubbles. She kept stirring and stirring, until finally the soup boiled over. Everyone was so mad at Lily. Her mom said, "Lily! That was not a very smart thing to do! You could have hurt yourself." The moral of the story is to be careful with things that can be dangerous, even if you are trying to help somebody. Even though Lily was trying her best to join the celebration, she ended up making a big mess instead.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a great toy. It was a big, red rubber ball. He loved to play with it every day. One day, Tim took his ball to the park. At the park, Tim met a new friend named Sue. Sue saw Tim's ball and said, "Wow! That's a great ball! Can I hold it?" Tim said, "Yes, you can hold my ball." Sue held the ball and they both played with it, bouncing it up and down. Soon, it was time to go home. Tim and Sue said goodbye to each other. "I had fun playing with your ball," Sue said. Tim smiled and said, "I had fun too! Let's play again soon." They both went home, happy to have made a new friend and excited to play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a big blackboard in a small room. The blackboard had many colors on it. One day, a wild bird flew into the room. The bird was very pretty and had many colors too. It saw the blackboard and blinked its eyes. The bird said, "Hello, blackboard! You have many colors like me. Can we be friends?" The blackboard did not talk, but the bird felt happy because it had a new friend. The bird blinked its eyes again and started to sing a nice song. Every day, the wild bird came to the room and sang to the blackboard. It also used its beak to draw pretty pictures on the blackboard. The bird and the blackboard became best friends. They were always happy when they were together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Sam. Sam had a rubber ball that he loved to play with every day. One day, Sam felt nervous because he could not find his rubber ball. He looked everywhere in his room, but it was not there. Sam's mom saw that he was sad and asked him what was wrong. Sam told her that he lost his rubber ball. His mom said, "Let's pray and ask for help to find it." Sam and his mom closed their eyes and prayed together. After they prayed, Sam's mom had an idea. She looked under the sofa and found the rubber ball! Sam was so happy and hugged his mom. He was not nervous anymore. Sam played with his rubber ball all day and remembered to always pray when he needed help.
