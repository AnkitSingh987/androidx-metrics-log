Once upon a time, there was a kind little girl named Sarah. She was playing in her backyard when she saw a sparkly island across the sea. Sarah was curious and wanted to explore, so she built a boat out of sticks, leaves and magic. When she got to the island, it was full of treasures, but also lots of scary things. Sarah was brave and kept exploring even when things got tough. When she finally got to the center of the island, she met a scary monster. The monster was mean and wanted to hurt her, but Sarah was brave and resisted! She didn't give up, even when the monster got bigger and bigger. Eventually the monster went away and Sarah saved the day. Sarah was very proud of herself, and she celebrated her victory by giving the island a big hug. Sarah was so relieved and happy, she wanted to show everyone how kind and brave she had been. So Sarah hopped back into her boat, and sailed home with her heart full of happy memories.

One day, a dog named Max went for a walk with his boy, Tim. Max was on a thin leash. They liked to walk in the park and see the birds and flowers. Max was a very fast dog. While walking, Max saw a cat. He wanted to chase the cat. Max said, "Tim, can I chase the cat? Please?" Tim said, "No, Max, you are on a leash. You cannot chase the cat." Max was sad but he listened to Tim. As they walked, Max saw a ball. He wanted to play with the ball. Tim said, "Okay, Max, you can play with the ball." Tim took Max off the thin leash. Max ran with speed to get the ball. He was so happy! They played until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a small flower who lived in a big garden. The flower was sad because it could not bloom. Every day, the sun would shine, but the flower stayed small and closed. One day, a wise old tree saw the sad flower. The tree gave a lecture to the flower about how to grow and bloom. The tree's voice was harsh, but the flower listened carefully. The tree said, "You must be brave and strong to bloom." The flower tried very hard to be brave and strong. Slowly, it started to grow bigger and bigger. One day, a big storm came to the garden. The flower was scared, but it remembered the tree's lecture. It tried to be brave and strong like the tree said. But the storm was too strong for the small flower. The wind blew and the rain fell hard. The flower could not hold on any longer. It fell to the ground and did not bloom. The tree was sad, but it knew that sometimes, even the bravest flowers cannot beat the storm.

Once upon a time, there was a pig named Pete. He was a big and fat pig. Pete loved to roll around in the mud. His skin was rough and he liked to dig in the dirt. One day, Pete was walking in the woods when he saw something soft. He went closer and he felt it was a blanket. He put it on his back and it was warm and cosy. Pete kept walking around, feeling happy and warm in his blanket. But then he hit a rock and the blanket got a hole. He felt sad and cold without his blanket. Pete went back to his house. He saw his mom and he went to her, feeling sad. His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry Pete, I have something for you!". She gave him a big, soft blanket with stripes. Pete was so happy and he felt warm and comfy under it.

One day, Mom and Baby Joe went to the beach. The ocean was very calm and the tide was low. Baby Joe saw a long stick in the sand and he wanted to take it home. Mom said, "That stick is too big to take home. But why don't you pick up some shells instead?" So Baby Joe gathered some pretty shells. Suddenly, the tide changed and got higher. Baby Joe was scared because the water was coming closer. He asked Mom, "Why is the tide coming up?" Mom said, "Tides come and go. They move in and out all day long. That's why it's called the tide. It will go back down soon, so don't worry." Baby Joe smiled and started picking up more shells. After a long time, the tide went back down and Baby Joe was proud of his shell collection. He had a lot of shells to take home and show his friends.
