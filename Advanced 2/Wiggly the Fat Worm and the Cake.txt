Once upon a time, in a small garden, there lived a fat worm named Wiggly. Wiggly loved to eat and play with his friends. One sunny day, Wiggly was very hungry, so he went to find some food. As Wiggly crawled through the garden, he met his friend, Birdy the bird. Birdy was busy baking a cake. Wiggly asked, "Can I help you bake the cake?" Birdy said, "Yes, Wiggly! We can bake it together." Wiggly and Birdy worked together to bake the cake. They mixed and stirred and poured. When the cake was done, they shared it with all their friends in the garden. Everyone loved the cake and had a fun time. Wiggly was happy he could help and was not hungry anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived with her parent in a small, light house. Mia loved to help her parent with many things. One day, her parent was very busy and needed help with the house chores. Mia said, "I can help you, parent!" Her parent smiled and asked her to pick up her toys and put them away. Mia was happy to help and started picking up her toys. While she was cleaning, she found her favorite toy, a small red ball. Mia wanted to play, but she remembered her parent needed her help. After Mia finished helping her parent, they sat down for a rest. Mia's parent said, "Thank you for your help, Mia. You did a great job!" Mia felt proud and happy that she could help. She learned that helping others is important and can make you feel good inside. The moral of the story is: Helping others can make you feel happy and proud.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a big, old tree with many holes. They wanted to see what was inside the holes. "Let's examine the holes!" Lily said. "Maybe we can find some animals or treasures." "OK!" Tom agreed. They ran to the tree and looked at the holes. Lily put her eye close to one hole. She saw something shiny and round. She reached her hand inside and pulled out a necklace. It had a blue stone that sparkled in the sun. "Wow! Look what I found!" Lily exclaimed. "It's so pretty!" Tom put his eye close to another hole. He saw something furry and brown. He reached his hand inside and touched it. It moved and made a noise. "Yikes! It's a squirrel!" Tom shouted. He quickly pulled his hand out and ran away from the hole. The squirrel peeked out and chattered at him. Lily laughed and ran after Tom. She put the necklace around her neck and showed it to him. "Look, Tom, I have a treasure!" she said. "Do you want to share it with me?" Tom nodded and smiled. He was not scared of the squirrel anymore. He was happy that Lily found a treasure and wanted to share it with him. They hugged and continued to play in the park. They wondered what else they could examine and find.

One day, a pale cat named Mark went for a walk. He saw a small twig on the ground. He picked it up and played with it. Mark was very happy with his twig. But then, a big dog came and wanted the twig too. The big dog barked at Mark and tried to take the twig. Mark was scared, but he did not want to lose his twig. They both pulled on the twig, but it did not break. Mark and the big dog were very strong. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny ant came and took the twig away from Mark and the big dog. They were both so surprised! The tiny ant was stronger than they thought. Mark and the big dog stopped fighting and became friends. They learned that even small things can be strong.

Once upon a time, there was a big war between two groups of ants. One group was red and the other was black. They wanted to see who could build the biggest and most successful ant hill. The red ants worked very hard. They tried to succeed by carrying little pieces of dirt and leaves. The black ants worked hard too. They wanted to be successful by finding the best food to make their ant hill strong. In the end, both the red ants and the black ants were successful. They built big ant hills and found lots of food. They stopped fighting and became friends. And they all lived happily ever after.
