One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They were buying food for dinner. Lily saw celery and said, "I hate celery, Mommy!" Her mom just smiled and put the celery in the cart. At home, Lily was worried. She did not want to eat the celery for dinner. Her mom knew Lily did not like celery, so she had an idea. She chopped up the celery and mixed it with some yummy cheese. When it was time for dinner, Lily's mom said, "Try this, Lily. I made something special for you." Lily tried a bite and said, "Wow, Mommy! This is good! What is it?" Her mom told her it was celery with cheese. Lily was surprised that she liked the celery. She learned that sometimes trying new things can be fun. Now, Lily was not worried about eating celery anymore.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big grape tree. The grapes are purple and shiny. Sara and Tom want to eat some grapes. "Let's climb the tree and get some grapes," Sara says. "OK, but be careful," Tom says. They climb the tree and reach for the grapes. But the tree is not happy. The tree does not like children who take its grapes. The tree shakes its branches and makes a loud noise. "Ow, ow, ow!" Sara and Tom cry. The branches hit them and hurt them. They fall from the tree and land on the ground. "Help, help, help!" Sara and Tom shout. They are scared and sad. They have bumps and bruises all over. But no one hears them. No one comes to help them. They are alone in the park. The sun goes down and it gets dark and cold. Sara and Tom hug each other and cry. They wish they never saw the big grape tree. They wish they never tried to take its grapes. They wish they were home with their mommies and daddies. But they are not. They are stuck in the park with the angry tree. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She was happy, carefree and very curious. One day, Lucy decided to take a walk. She walked for a long time until she came across a river. At first, Lucy was scared, but when she saw how cool and sparkly the river was, she was filled with joy. Lucy jumped into the river and started to play. She was having so much fun that she didn't realise how deep she had gone. Suddenly, Lucy realised she was in trouble and started to get scared. But then, just in time, a friendly man came to help her. He was careful and kind, and he helped Lucy back to the riverbank. Lucy was relieved to be safe and realised that she had been careless. Lucy thanked the man and promised herself to always be more careful around rivers. She waved goodbye and went home, with a big smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth bell. This bell lived in a big tree. Every day, it would ring and make a loud sound. All the animals in the forest would come and dance when they heard the bell. One day, a little boy came to the forest. He heard the bell ring and wanted to find it. He walked and walked until he saw the big tree. The little boy wanted to ring the smooth bell too. He climbed the tree and touched the bell. But he did not know that the bell had a big brain. The bell did not like to be touched. It became very angry. The bell rang so loud that the little boy fell from the tree. He got hurt and cried. The animals in the forest were sad and did not dance anymore.

Once there was a little girl named Lucy who loved to eat. One day she had to have an operation. She was very scared because it sounded dull and boring. When she got to the hospital she was given yummy foods to eat. She wasn't as scared anymore and she ate them all up. After the operation she felt better and stronger. She was excited to do fun things with her friends. Lucy was very happy and she was never scared of operations again.
