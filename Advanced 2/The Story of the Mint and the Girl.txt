Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was so happy! She loved to dance and play in the backyard. One day she found a magical mint. It was the most fancy mint she'd ever seen! It was so sparkly. The little girl touched the mint. Suddenly, the mint started to glow. It was so bright! The little girl was so excited. She jumped around with joy and showed her family the magical mint. Everyone was amazed. They couldn't believe it was real. The little girl kept the mint forever and it glowed every night. She felt very special being the only one with such a fancy mint!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat tasty treats. One day, Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a tasty cake and asked his mom, "Can I have that cake, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can have the cake, but you must remain next to me while we shop." Tim was so happy and promised to stay by her side. As they walked, Tim saw a shiny razor on a high shelf. He asked his mom, "What is that shiny thing?" His mom replied, "That is a razor. It is not for little boys like you." Tim nodded and stayed close to his mom. They bought the tasty cake and went home. Tim learned to always listen to his mom and remain safe.

One day, a little girl named Annie found a mysterious box in her room. She opened the box and saw a video inside. She was very curious and wanted to know what was on the video. Annie asked her mom, "What is this video about?" Her mom did not answer. She asked her dad, "Do you know what this video is?" Her dad did not answer too. Annie was sad that no one could tell her about the mysterious video. Annie decided to watch the video by herself. She put the video in the TV and pressed play. She saw a funny show with dancing animals. Annie laughed and clapped her hands. She was happy to find out the secret of the mysterious video.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was very sad. She was so sad that she started to cry. Her tears rolled down her cheeks as she wept and wept. She was weeping so loudly that her mom came to check on her. Her mom saw that she was upset and asked her why she was so sad. The little girl said she couldn’t find the zipper for her coat. Her mom smiled and said it was ok. She took the little girl's hand and they went on a search together. In the end, they found it under the bed! The little girl hugged her mom and thanked her. Now that she found the zipper, she was happy again. No longer was she sad and noisy, but she was now calm and quiet. The little girl smiled and went to play outside.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man who lived all by himself. Every day he would kneel in a small patch of grass at the edge of the forest. He would close his eyes and tap the ground three times with his finger. Everyone in the forest wanted to know why he did this every day, but they were too afraid to ask. One day, a small three year old girl, full of courage and curiosity, decided to carefully creep up to the man and ask why he did this every day. The man, startled to see the girl, jumped up and was surprised to hear such brave words coming from someone so small. The man smiled and said “When I tap on the ground, I am sending my love and good wishes to all the animals who live in the forest and asking them to stay safe. That is why I kneel here every day.” The little girl was amazed and smiled and thanked the man for his kindness. The two of them soon became friends and from that day on the man was never alone in his patch of grass. The girl would come to join him and sometimes they would kneel and tap the ground together.
