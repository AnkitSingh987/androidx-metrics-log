One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. It was a cold day, but Sue wore her warm coat. Sue saw a big purple ball near a tree. She picked it up and wanted to play with it. A boy named Tim came over and said, "That's my ball!" Sue looked at Tim and said, "Can we play together?" Tim thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, let's play!" They played with the purple ball and had lots of fun. As they played, the sun went down and it got colder. Sue and Tim decided to go home. They walked together, holding the purple ball. When they got to their houses, Sue said, "I had fun today. Let's play again tomorrow!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's do that!" The next day, Sue and Tim played with the purple ball again. They learned that sharing and playing together was more fun than playing alone. And they became best friends, always ready to share and help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely big machine. The machine could cook yummy food. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the machine. He wanted to make a tasty cake for his mommy's birthday. Tim said, "Machine, please make a cake." The machine started to work. It mixed flour, sugar, and eggs. Tim watched the machine make the cake. He was very happy. But, the machine made a mistake. It cooked the cake for too long. The cake began to burn! Tim smelled the smoke and said, "Oh no, the cake is burning!" He turned off the machine and took out the burnt cake. Tim was sad, but he didn't give up. He asked the machine to make another cake. This time, the machine made a perfect cake. Tim and his mommy had a lovely birthday party with the yummy cake.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Lily. She had a garden where she grew many herbs. She loved her garden very much. One day, Lily saw a big weed in her garden. She knew she had to remove it to keep her herbs healthy. She pulled and pulled, but the weed was very strong. She needed help. Lily asked her friend, Tom, for help. "Tom, can you help me remove this big weed?" Tom came over and said, "Yes, I can help you, Lily!" Together, they pulled and pulled. The weed came out! Lily was very happy. She gave Tom some nice herbs from her garden to say thank you. They both smiled and went back to their homes, knowing they had done a good job.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Jane. She had a magical wheel that she could use to make amazing things. One day, Jane decided to take her wheel and cook something special. She wanted to make something that her friends would be envious of. Jane gathered all her smells and ingredients and set to work with her wheel. When she had finished the recipe, she took it out of the oven and presented it to her friends. "Wow", they said. "That looks delicious! It looks like something even a professional cook would make." Jane smiled. She had worked hard and her friends were envious!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big red ball. One day, she went on a trip to the park with her mom. Lily was so happy to play with her ball. At the park, Lily saw a poor little bird with a hurt wing. She went close to the bird and whispered, "Hi, bird. I will help you." Lily's mom saw her talking to the bird and came to help too. Together, they took care of the bird and made it feel better. The bird whispered, "Thank you, Lily and mom." After that, they all played with the big red ball and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat called a leopard. The leopard had many spots on his body. He liked to play in the sun and observe things around him. One day, the leopard met a little bird. The bird was very useful to the leopard. The bird would fly high and tell the leopard where to find food. They became very good friends and played together every day. As they played and observed the world together, the leopard and the bird learned many things. The leopard learned how to be fast and strong, and the bird learned how to be smart and brave. They were very happy and lived together for a long time.
