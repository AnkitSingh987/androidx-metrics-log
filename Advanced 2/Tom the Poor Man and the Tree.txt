Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a poor man named Tom. Tom had a little house and a big tree in his yard. He wanted to cut the tree with his axe, but the tree was too strong. One day, Tom met a wise old lady. She told him a secret to make his axe stronger. Tom was excited and went home to try it. He didn't know if he could manage to cut the tree, but he wanted to try. Tom used the secret the old lady told him and his axe became very strong. He went to the tree and started to cut it. With his strong axe, Tom managed to cut the tree down. He was so happy and thanked the wise old lady for her help.

Once upon a time, there was a fearful little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of many things, but he was most scared of the dark. Every night, he would hide under his blanket, hoping the dark would go away. One day, Tim found a toy motor in his toy box. He thought it was very cool. He played with it all day, making it go fast and slow. Tim loved his new toy motor so much that he forgot to be scared. That night, Tim had an idea. He took his toy motor to bed with him. He poured some water into a cup and put the motor in the water. The motor made a soft sound that made Tim feel safe. With the help of his toy motor, Tim was no longer fearful of the dark.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a long, pretty dress. She liked to play outside with her friends. One day, she found a small, colorful fan on the ground. She picked it up and started to wave it around. As Lily played with the fan, a cute little cat came to her. The cat liked the fan too. It tried to follow the fan as Lily moved it back and forth. Lily giggled and said, "Let's play together, little cat!" Lily and the cat played with the fan all day long. They ran around the yard, and the cat would follow the fan wherever it went. They had so much fun. When it was time to go inside, Lily said goodbye to her new friend. The cat went back to its home, and Lily went back to hers. They both had a very happy day.

Once there was a little girl called Mia. She loved to go for walks in the woods. Today she was out looking for adventure. As she walked, she saw a shiny knob on the ground. She bent down to pick it up and gave it a big smile. Mia continued walking until she got to a big tree. The tree had a big knob at the top. Mia was so excited, she started to climb. As she made her way to the top, the knob twinkled in the sun. She finally reached it and wanted to turn it. Mia was so excited, she didn't even notice how quiet it was. But when she turned the knob, something amazing happened. Suddenly, music filled the air and birds flew around singing. Mia laughed and danced, she had never heard such beautiful music before. When the music stopped, Mia put the knob back and thanked the tree. Then she picked up her shiny knob and walked home with a big smile.

One day, Mommy and her little girl were walking in the park. The little girl was busy observing the world around her. Mommy held her little girl's hand as they passed by the playground. The little girl heard a noisy sound coming from the playground. Curious, she asked Mommy what was making the loud noise. Mommy told her that it was the children playing and having fun on the slides and swings. The little girl wanted to play too. Mommy told her that they could come back to play later if they were both feeling up to it. The little girl smiled, happy to know that she might get to go to the playground later. They continued on, and soon the little girl heard the same noisy sound that she heard at the playground. She looked around and saw a pond with lots of ducks in it! The ducks were all quacking and swimming around in the water, making a lot of noise. The little girl was surprised and excited to see the ducks. Mommy told her to observe the ducks and then they could come back to feed the ducks later. The little girl clapped her hands, delighted to know that she would get to feed the ducks. She was also looking forward to coming back to the playground!
