Once upon a time there was a little girl who was very shy. She lived in a big farm with lots of animals and acres of land to wander. One day, she decided to wander around the farm and see what her family had been up to. When she reached the barn she saw a sheep that looked very scared. She hesitated and was quite shy but she eventually went up to it, speaking softly. The sheep said, "What is your name?" The little girl looked around, surprised. She told the sheep her name and asked how it knew. The sheep said, "I can hear it in your voice. You seem very shy." The two of them were friends from that day forward. The little girl often liked to wander around the farm and look for her sheep friend. Whenever she found it, she would happily give it a big hug and play together.

Once upon a time, there was a weird little dog named Spot. Spot had a very big nose. One day, Spot found a long string. He wanted to play with it. Spot had an idea. He thought, "I will tie the string to my nose." Spot tied the string to his big nose. He ran around the yard, playing and having fun. But soon, he saw a big tree. Spot had another idea. He thought, "I will tie the string to the tree, too." Spot tied the string to the tree and his big nose. Spot was very happy with his game. He ran around the tree, laughing and barking. But then, he saw a squirrel. Spot wanted to chase the squirrel. He forgot about the string tied to his nose and the tree. Spot ran after the squirrel but the string pulled him back. He felt a little tug on his nose. Spot was confused. He looked back and saw the string tied to the tree. He remembered his game. Spot laughed and untied the string from his nose and the tree. From that day on, Spot was more careful with his games. He still played with the string, but never tied it to his nose again. Spot learned that sometimes, even weird games can be fun, but you have to be careful.

Once there was a girl named Martha. She was three years old. One day, she went to a special place with her mommy and daddy. They saw an image on the wall. It was of a beautiful tree. Martha wanted to get a closer look. Her mommy said she could come near and examine the image. Martha walked over to the image and looked closely at it. She saw all the colors and the way the branches looked like they were dancing in the wind. It was amazing! But then Martha realized that the image was actually a painting of a real tree. She felt very ignorant because she had not noticed it before. Martha never forgot this moment. After that day she made sure to examine everything closely to see if there was more than what first met the eye. She never wanted to be ignorant again!

Once upon a time, in a green world, there lived a big dog named Spot. Spot loved to chew on things. He would chew on toys, bones, and even sticks. One day, Spot saw a little bird named Tweet. Tweet was sad because she lost her favorite toy. Spot wanted to help Tweet find her toy. They looked everywhere in the green world. Spot and Tweet had a hard time finding the toy. This was their big problem. Finally, Spot found the toy under a big tree. He was so happy to help Tweet. But, Spot wanted to chew the toy too. Tweet said, "Please don't chew my toy, Spot." Spot thought about it and decided not to chew the toy. Spot and Tweet were both happy. They played together in the green world and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom liked to relax in the sun. One day, he found a big hill with a nice view. He could see many things from the top of the hill. Tom saw a big tree, a little house, and a blue lake. He liked this view a lot. Tom felt very happy and sleepy. He laid down on the soft grass and closed his eyes. Soon, Tom fell asleep on the hill. He had a nice dream about playing with his friends. When Tom woke up, he felt good and not sleepy anymore. He went back to his little house, ready to play.
