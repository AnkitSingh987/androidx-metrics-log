Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She was not happy because she thought she was ugly. Lily had a friend named Tom. Tom was a doctor who did operations. One day, Lily told Tom, "I want to marry, but I think I am too ugly." Tom said, "I can do an operation to make you pretty." Lily was happy and said yes. Tom did the operation on Lily. But something went wrong. After the operation, Lily looked in the mirror and saw that she was now more ugly than before. She cried and cried. Tom was sad too. Lily did not marry, and she stayed unhappy forever.

Once there was a little girl who had a special job. Every day, she had to go to the garden and yield the best flowers. She knew if the flowers were their most impressive, she would get a special symbol. The little girl took her job seriously and made sure no flower went unyielded. She spend hours in the garden and soon she was rewarded with a symbol. The symbol was oval and it had a royal blue color. It was very impressive! The little girl had never seen anything like it. She kept the symbol safe and put it atop her bookshelf. And every day, she returned to the garden to yield her impressive flowers.

Once upon a time, there were two twin cats named Tom and Tim. They lived in a small house with their best friend, a little girl named Sara. Sara loved her twin cats very much, and they loved her too. They were always together, playing and having fun. One sunny day, Sara wanted to play a new game. She called Tom and Tim and said, "Let's play a game where we take turns giving orders. I'll go first, then Tom, and then Tim." The twin cats agreed, and they started playing the game. Sara ordered Tom to jump, and he did. Tom ordered Tim to spin, and he did. Tim ordered Sara to clap her hands, and she did. As they played, they learned to listen and follow each other's orders. They also learned how important it was to be dependable. They knew they could trust each other to do what they were asked. This made their friendship even stronger. The moral of the story is that being dependable and listening to others can make friendships stronger.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Lily. Lily loved to relax under the big tree in her yard. One day, she saw a pretty bow on the ground. She thought it would look nice on her. Lily's friend, a small dog named Max, came by and saw the bow. "Can I have the bow, please?" Max asked. But Lily was selfish and said, "No, it's mine!" Later, Lily saw Max looking sad. She thought about how she had been selfish. She wanted to make Max happy, so she gave him the bow. Max was so happy, and they played together all day. From that day on, Lily learned to share and not be selfish.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gifted cat named Tom. Tom was very good at jumping and running. One day, Tom saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As Tom started to climb, he saw a little bird on a branch. The bird was crying. "Why are you crying?" Tom asked. The bird said, "I can't fly, and my family is on the other side of the cross." Tom wanted to help the bird, so he had an idea. Tom told the bird to climb on his back. He stretched his legs and jumped over the cross. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" The bird's family was happy too. From that day on, Tom and the bird became best friends. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a big frog named Bob. Bob had a very big stomach. He lived near a distant pond. One day, Bob saw a little bug. The little bug was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked Bob. The little bug said, "I am sad because I can't find my friend. We were playing, but we got apart." Bob wanted to help the little bug. They looked for the bug's friend near the pond. They looked and looked but could not find the friend. As they were looking, a big bird came. The big bird saw Bob and the little bug. The big bird was very hungry. The big bird swooped down and ate Bob and the little bug. They were gone. The little bug's friend was still lost and now both of them were gone too.
