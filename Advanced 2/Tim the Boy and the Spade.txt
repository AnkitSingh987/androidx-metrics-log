One day, a little boy named Tim found a spade in his yard. It was a small spade, just right for him. He felt very independent and decided to dig a hole. As he dug, his friend Sara came over. "What are you doing?" she asked. Tim said, "I am digging a hole with my spade. Do you want to help?" Sara thought for a moment and said, "No, I want to play with my doll." Tim was sad but decided to ignore Sara and keep digging. He dug and dug until the hole was big enough to sit in. He was very proud of his work. Sara saw how much fun Tim was having and decided to join him. She brought her doll and they both sat in the hole, playing together. Tim was happy that he had his spade and a friend to share his fun.

Once, there was a patient rabbit. He was very careful, springing here and there to avoid getting hurt. He loved to look for fresh vegetables to eat, but he knew he had to be careful. One day, he came to a tall hedgerow. He hopped to the top and looked down into a beautiful garden. He spotted carrots and lettuce, but something was in his way. A giant door! "What do I do now?" said Patient Rabbit. Just then, he spotted a little mouse. "Excuse me, little Mouse," said the Rabbit. "I'm wondering if you could help me get into this garden." Little Mouse smiled and said, "No worries! With a good spring, you can do it!" The Patient Rabbit thought to himself, took a deep breath, and sprang at the door. He landed on the other side with a thud! He shouted with glee, "Thank you, Little Mouse!" He gathered some carrots and lettuce and hopped away carefully. Patient Rabbit enjoyed his delicious treat, and never forgot the day he went on a careful spring into the garden.

Once upon a time, a little green frog named Freddy lived in a pond. One day, Freddy decided to wander away from his pond and explore the big world. He hopped and hopped until he found a big, strange thing. The big thing was a machine. It was a big, red machine with wheels. Freddy was curious and wanted to see what the machine could do. He hopped onto the machine and looked around. Suddenly, the machine started to move! It went fast, and Freddy held on tight. Freddy was scared, but then the machine stopped. He looked around and saw that he was back at his pond. The machine had taken him home. Freddy was happy to be back with his friends and family. He knew that he would always remember his big adventure with the big, red machine.

Once, there was a clumsy cat named Tom. He liked to play with a red tube. One day, Tom's friend, a small bird named Sue, came to visit him. Sue saw the red tube and wanted to play too. They decided to prepare a fun game with the tube. "Let's roll the tube down the hill!" said Sue. Tom agreed, and they both pushed the tube to the top of the hill. They were excited to watch the tube roll down. As they let go of the tube, something unexpected happened. The tube did not roll down the hill! Instead, it started going up into the sky! Tom and Sue looked at each other, surprised. They did not know how this happened, but they laughed and enjoyed watching the tube fly away. In the end, Tom and Sue had a great day together. They learned that sometimes, when things don't go as planned, it can still be fun. The clumsy cat and the little bird stayed friends, and they always remembered the day the red tube flew away.

Sara was sad. She missed her mom. Mom went to work and Sara stayed with Nana. Nana was nice, but Sara wanted Mom. Sara cried on the sofa. She hugged her teddy bear. Nana came and sat next to her. She put her arm around Sara. "Don't cry, Sara. Mom loves you. She will come back soon. She left you a note. Do you want to see it?" Nana said. Sara nodded. She wiped her tears. Nana gave her a paper. Sara looked at it. It had a heart and some words. Sara could not read the words, but she knew they were from Mom. "Mom says: I love you, Sara. You are my sweet girl. Be good for Nana. I will be home at five. Kisses and hugs, Mom." Nana read the note for Sara. Sara smiled. She felt better. She hugged the note and the teddy bear. She kissed Nana. "Thank you, Nana. You are nice. You comfort me. I love you and Mom." Sara said. "I love you too, Sara. You are a brave girl. I'm glad you are happy. Do you want to play a game?" Nana said. Sara nodded. She got up from the sofa. She held Nana's hand. They went to the playroom. Sara forgot her sadness. She had fun with Nana. She waited for Mom.
