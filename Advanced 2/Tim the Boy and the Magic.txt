Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his parent. Tim loved to play outside with his friends, but sometimes he felt scared when it got dark. One day, Tim's parent gave him a magic key. They said it could unlock anything that was scary. Tim was very happy and excited. He put the key in his pocket and went outside to play with his friends. As it got dark, Tim and his friends saw a big, scary shadow. Tim remembered the magic key in his pocket. He took it out and said, "Unlock the scary!" The shadow went away, and everyone was happy. From then on, Tim was never scared again.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved Wednesdays because he got to play with his best friend, Sam. They liked to run, jump, and laugh together. One Wednesday, Tim and Sam found a big hill. "Let's race to the top!" said Tim. "Okay!" said Sam. They started to run up the hill, but it was very hard. Tim was tired, but he wanted to succeed. Tim said, "I can do it!" He tried and tried, and finally, he reached the top of the hill. Sam was there too. They were both happy that they succeeded. From then on, every Wednesday, they raced up the hill and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a bean. It was attached high up on the wall in a difficult spot. The bean wanted to be very active and get down to the floor. But it was impossible. It couldn't reach up to be free. One day a small girl came by and saw the bean. She wanted to help the bean. She held out her hand and said "I can do it!". Then she carefully started to pull the bean off the wall. It was very difficult, but with a lot of tugging, the bean finally came loose. The girl was so happy that she had helped the bean. She immediately put the bean in her pocket, so that she would never forget it. From that moment, the bean and the girl were always together. They had a lot of fun and shared a lot of adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a mild cat named Mimi. Mimi liked to climb a ladder to reach the top of a tree. She loved to sit on the branches and watch the birds fly by. One day, Mimi saw a little bird named Billy who didn't know how to fly. Mimi wanted to help him. She decided to teach Billy how to fly. Mimi and Billy became good friends. Every day, Mimi would climb the ladder with Billy on her back. They would sit on the tree branch and Mimi would show Billy how to flap his wings. Soon, Billy learned how to fly. Mimi and Billy were very happy and played together in the sky.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little white bunny. The bunny had many friends. One day, the bunny wanted to send a symbol to his friend, the squirrel. The bunny drew a big nut on a piece of paper. The nut was the symbol for "hello" in the forest. The bunny said to the bird, "Can you please take this paper to the squirrel?" The bird took the paper and flew to the squirrel's tree. The squirrel saw the paper with the nut symbol and smiled. "Thank you, bird!" said the squirrel. "I will send a symbol back to the bunny." The squirrel drew a big carrot on a piece of paper. The carrot was the symbol for "hello" in the forest too. The bird took the paper and flew back to the white bunny. The bunny saw the carrot symbol and was very happy. The bunny and the squirrel became even better friends. They always sent symbols to each other to say hello. And they lived happily in the big forest.

One day, a boy named Tim went with his dad to play golf. Tim's dad showed him how to hit the ball. Tim tried to wave the golf stick, but it was too big for him. His dad laughed and said, "You need a smaller stick, Tim!" Tim found a smaller stick and tried again. This time, he hit the ball and it went far away. Tim and his dad walked to find the ball. They saw a messy dog with the ball in its mouth. The dog looked at them and wagged its tail. "Hey dog, can we have our ball back?" Tim asked. The dog dropped the ball and ran away. But when Tim picked up the ball, it was not a golf ball. It was an egg! Tim and his dad looked at each other and laughed. They went back to play golf with their own ball, and left the egg where they found it.
