Once upon a time there was a young girl called Marian. She found a unique wrap one day. She was very excited and took the wrap home. When she got home she wanted to show it to her family. But her family were too busy to look. So, Marian thought of a plan. She wanted to sack them. She put the wrap around her family. She thought it was funny and funny. But it wasn't. It was a mean thing to do. Her family were so angry! They yelled at Marian for being so mean and took the wrap away. Marian was sad. She had been so excited about the wrap and had wanted to share it with her family. But she had not thought about how her family would feel. This story shows us that no matter how excited we are about something, we should always think about how it might affect others. We should be kind and considerate.

Once upon a time, there was a small hill where all the animals liked to play. One day, a little bird named Bluey wanted to dance on the hill. Bluey was very good at dancing and all her friends loved to watch her. As Bluey danced on the hill, she saw a big, useful stick. She had an idea. "I can use this stick in my dance!" she said to her friends. Bluey danced and danced with the stick, and her friends clapped and cheered for her. Suddenly, a strong wind blew the stick out of Bluey's wings. The stick rolled down the hill and stopped a big rock from falling on a small bug. The bug looked up and said, "Thank you, Bluey! Your dance saved my life!" Bluey felt happy and proud. The moral of the story is that sometimes, having fun and being yourself can also help others in unexpected ways.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ashley. She was very impatient. One day, she was walking in the park when she noticed something shiny. It was a tiny bulb, stuck in the ground. She wanted to see what it was so she got closer and reached out to touch it. Suddenly, she heard a voice. "Don't touch that!" It was her mom. Ashley was surprised, but she obediently stepped away from the bulb. Suddenly, her mom said, "Now Ashley, I want you to push the bulb like this." She demonstrated how to press it. Ashley was excited and quickly followed her mother's instructions. She put both her hands on the bulb, closed her eyes and pushed. The bulb lit up with a bright white light. Ashley shouted with joy! She had turned on the light. Her mom smiled. Ashley had used her patience and followed her mom's instructions to discover the wonderful light. She had proved that being patient can pay off!

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Whiskers. Whiskers loved to play and run all day. One day, Whiskers saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. As he started to climb, he heard a whisper, "Hey, watch out!" It was a small bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was scared that Whiskers might fall. Whiskers said, "I will be careful, Chirpy!" They both smiled and became friends. They played together every day. One sunny day, Whiskers and Chirpy went on a trip. They walked and played in the park. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, Whiskers and Chirpy went home. They were happy to have each other as friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a bike with pedals. He liked to ride his bike fast. One day, he saw a big hill. Tim wanted to go up the hill. He tried to pedal, but it was hard. Tim was persistent, he did not give up. As Tim went up the hill, he saw a cat. The cat seemed sad. Tim wanted to help the cat. He got off his bike and walked to the cat. The cat looked at Tim and then ran away. Tim was surprised. He thought the cat needed help. Tim followed the cat. The cat led Tim to a big tree. At the bottom of the tree, there was a little kitten. The kitten was stuck in a hole. Tim helped the kitten get out of the hole. The cat and the kitten were happy. Tim was happy too. He learned that sometimes, unexpected things can happen when you are persistent.
