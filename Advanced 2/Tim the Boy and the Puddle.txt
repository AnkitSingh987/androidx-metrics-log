Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the water. One sunny day, he went to the park with his mom. They found a big, old puddle. Tim looked at his mom and asked, "Can I splash in the puddle, please?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can splash in the puddle, but be careful." Tim was so happy. He jumped and splashed in the puddle. The water went up and down. Tim laughed and splashed some more. As Tim played, he found a small, green plant. It was a mint plant. Tim picked it up and smelled it. "Mmm, it smells good," he said. He showed the mint to his mom. She agreed that it smelled nice. Tim and his mom took the mint home and planted it in their garden. They had lots of fun playing and splashing in the puddle that day.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Tom. Tom was old and loved to play in the yard. One day, while Tom was playing, he saw something move behind a big tree. Tom said, "Who's there? I see you peek!" A little bird came out from behind the tree. The bird was very shy and scared. "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to scare you," said the bird. Tom and the bird became friends. They played together every day. Tom was not so clumsy anymore, and the bird was not so shy. And they lived happily ever after.

Sammy was walking outside when he saw a fly. He watched it fly around the sky and he smiled. The fly was so clean and sparkly that it twinkled in the sunlight. Sammy looked around and saw a tree. He walked over to it and saw a little girl, Daisy, sitting beneath it. Daisy looked at Sammy and said, "Hello! What are you doing?" Sammy smiled and said, "I am watching a fly fly around in the sky." Then he paused and said, "Isn't it pretty?" Daisy nodded her head and said, "Yes, it is very clean and sparkly." The fly had been blinking in the sunlight and Sammy thought it was really beautiful. He asked Daisy, "Do you want to blink too?" Daisy laughed and said, "Yes, I would love to." So Sammy and Daisy blinked in the sunlight and watched the fly twinkle and shine. Sammy was so happy to feel the sun on his face and to watch the fly's clean wings flutter by. Together they blinked and smiled until the fly flew away.

One evening, a little red car named Zoomy loved to zoom around the town. Zoomy was very good at playing hide and seek, because he could find hidden places to hide. He had lots of friends who loved to play with him. One day, Zoomy was playing with his friend, a blue car named Blu. They were playing hide and seek. Zoomy said, "Blu, you count to ten and I will go hide!" Blu closed his eyes and started counting, "One, two, three..." Zoomy zoomed away to find a hidden spot. He found a big bush and hid behind it. Blu finished counting and said, "Ready or not, here I come!" Blu looked and looked, but he could not find Zoomy. Finally, he found Zoomy behind the bush. Blu said, "Zoomy, you are so good at hiding!" Zoomy and Blu laughed and went back to play again. They played hide and seek all evening, taking turns hiding and seeking. They had so much fun and loved playing together. Zoomy and Blu were the best of friends and always had a great time when they played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who wanted to have an adventure. She went to the beach and hung her towel on the beach chair. She looked around and saw a jellyfish in the water. The girl started talking to the jellyfish. “Hi there, jellyfish. Do you want to be friends?” The jellyfish bobbed up and down in the water. “Yes, let’s be friends,” the jellyfish said. They started playing in the water but the little girl got bored quickly. “This is boring, let’s go do something else,” the girl said. So they went to the beach and hung their towels on the tree. Then they started making a sandcastle together. They had so much fun, and the sandcastle was so big and beautiful. The little girl said goodbye to her new friend, the jellyfish. She went home feeling so happy and full of joy.
