Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy made of steel. The toy was strong and shiny. Tim loved to play with his toy every day. But one day, Tim thought his toy was boring. He wanted a new toy to play with. Tim went to his mom and asked her for a new toy. His mom said they could not buy a new toy right now. Tim felt sad. His mom saw that he was sad and wanted to comfort him. She gave Tim a big hug and said they could make a fun game with his steel toy. Together, they made a new game with the steel toy. They would hide the toy and then find it. Tim and his mom played the game all day. Tim was happy again. He learned that even if a toy seems boring, you can still have fun with it when you play with someone you love.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy hunter. He liked to walk in the woods. He had a big hat and a long stick. He looked for food to eat. One day, the hunter saw a big tree. The tree had a long branch. The hunter wanted to balance on the branch. He climbed up and tried to walk on it. The hunter was very good at balancing. He walked back and forth on the branch. He was happy and laughed. Then, he climbed down and went home. The healthy hunter had a fun day in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a lucky room. In the lucky room lived a mommy and her little girl. Every day, the mommy would pour the little girl's cereal into a bowl for breakfast. Then, the mommy would pour a delicious glass of milk to go along with it. After breakfast, the mommy would pour bubbles into the bathtub. The little girl would have so much fun playing in the bubbles. Sometimes, she would even pretend like she was in a bubble land. When the little girl was done playing in the bubbles, she and her mommy would pour water on the plants in the backyard. Then, the lucky little girl and her mommy would drink cold water and watch the sky change colors. The lucky little girl was so happy to live in her lucky room.

One day, a cat named Tom was very hungry. He went to the kitchen and saw a can of tuna on the table. Tom was very happy. He jumped on the table and tried to open the can. But he was too small and weak. Tom saw a big, fearful dog named Max outside. Tom had an idea. He opened the door and said, "Max, can you help me open this can of tuna?" Max nodded and came inside the house. Max used his strong teeth to open the can of tuna. Tom was very excited. He started to rub his paws together, ready to eat the tuna. But Max was very hungry too. He looked at the tuna and then at Tom. Max said, "I helped you open the can, so I should eat the tuna." Tom was scared but he didn't want to share the tuna. Max growled and ate all the tuna in one big bite. Tom was sad and hungry. He learned that sometimes, asking for help can lead to a bad ending.
