Jimmy was a careless little boy. One day, he was in the woods and he stayed longer than he should have. Suddenly, Jimmy felt a sharp pain and he saw that he had been bitten! He started to cry and then he heard his mom's voice. He ran to her and hugged her tightly. She told him that he had been careless and that he needs to stay away from the woods. Jimmy promised to never be careless again and he stayed close to his mom. He never forgot the lesson he learned that day.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Ben went for a walk in the park. Ben saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. While he was sitting, he heard a sound. "Tweet, tweet!" It was a little bird. Ben looked up and stared at the bird. The bird stared back at Ben. Then, the bird said, "Why are you staring at me?" Ben felt ashamed. He said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to stare. I just heard your sound and wanted to see where it came from." The little bird smiled and said, "It's okay. My name is Lily. Do you want to be friends?" Ben wagged his tail and said, "Yes, I'd love to be friends!" From that day on, Ben and Lily played together in the park every day, and they were never ashamed to stare at each other again.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a bookcase. The bookcase was full of books for kids to read. One day, Tom and Sue went to the room to pick a book. Tom saw a book he liked at the top of the bookcase. He wanted to get it, but he was too small. Tom had an idea. He tried to lean on the bookcase to reach the book. But the bookcase started to shake. Sue saw this and said, "Tom, be careful! The bookcase might fall!" Tom felt embarrassed. He knew he should not lean on the bookcase. Sue helped Tom by finding a small chair. Tom stood on the chair and got the book. They both sat down and read the book together. The moral of the story is to always ask for help when you need it. It's better to be safe than sorry.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house near the woods. The man loved his wife very much, and they were happy together. One night, the man saw a glow in the woods. He was fearful, but he wanted to find out what it was. So, he went into the woods to look. The glow was coming from a big, shiny rock. The man touched the rock, and something unexpected happened. He turned into a big, strong lion! The lion went back to his house to show his wife. At first, she was scared, but then she saw that it was her husband. She hugged the lion and they were happy. The next day, the lion went back to the shiny rock and turned back into the man. From that day on, they knew that they could face anything together, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Sally. She was always so curious and loved exploring. She was on a long walk and she came across a strange cave. Sally was brave, so she decided to go inside. Inside the dark cave, Sally came across a big box. She opened it and found lots of shiny gold coins. With a huge smile, Sally said, "Wow! All this gold!" Suddenly, something strange happened. All the gold coins became difficult to touch. They were very hot! Sally was scared and a little worried. At that moment, a voice in the cave said, "If you want the gold, you gotta be brave!" Sally was confused, so she asked, "Who said that?" In answer, a big dragon appeared. "If you want to keep the gold," he said, "you'll have to find your courage. It won't be easy, but if you try hard, you can do it!" Sally was scared, but she was also determined. She took a deep breath, then said to the dragon, "I know I can do it!" The dragon smiled and the gold coins became cool to touch. Sally picked up the gold and ran home, feeling proud of her courage!
