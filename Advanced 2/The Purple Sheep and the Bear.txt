One day, there was a little purple sheep. His name was Pete. He wanted to know what the world was like. So he decided to go on a journey. Pete skipped through the meadows and over the hills. Everywhere he went, he looked for knowledge. Finally, Pete came across a huge mountain. He had never seen anything like this before and he wanted to know what was up top. So he started to climb. The mountain was very slippery and Pete found it hard to stand up. After a long time trying, he finally made it to the top. But then, Pete ran into a huge brown bear! The bear said, "What are you doing here? You should not be here!" Pete screamed and tried to escape. But the bear was too fast and before the little purple sheep knew it, the bear had caught him and was carrying him away. Pete was never seen again!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a favorite notebook. She loved to draw in it every day. She would draw the sun, trees, and her family. Her notebook was very special to her. One day, Lucy's friend, Timmy, came over to play. Timmy saw the notebook and wanted to draw too. But Lucy was selfish and did not want to share her notebook. Timmy was sad and said, "Please, can I draw too?" Lucy thought for a moment and realized that sharing is good. She decided to let Timmy use her notebook. They took turns drawing pictures together. They drew a big house with a happy family inside. They were having so much fun drawing together. In the end, Lucy and Timmy were very happy. They learned that sharing is important and makes everyone happy. They promised to always share and be good friends forever. And Lucy's notebook had even more beautiful drawings in it because she shared it with her friend.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tim. Tim liked to watch the birds outside his window. One day, Tim saw a big dog named Max doing exercise in the yard. Tim wanted to play with Max, so he opened the door and went outside. Max saw Tim and said, "Hi, Tim! Want to do exercise with me?" Tim said, "Yes, let's play!" They started to run and jump together. But then, a big bird saw Tim and Max playing. The bird was angry because it thought they were too loud. The bird flew down and took Tim away. Max tried to save Tim, but he was too slow. Tim was never seen again, and Max was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys in his room. One day, he found a cool, twisty toy on the floor. He did not know what it was, but he liked it a lot. He played with it all day and put it on his wall when he went to bed. The next day, Tim went to play with his twisty toy, but it was not on his wall. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. He was sad and told his mom. His mom said, "Maybe it will come back later." That night, Tim went to bed and heard a noise. He saw his twisty toy on the wall again! But this time, it was moving and twisting by itself! Tim was so surprised and happy. He played with the twisty toy and it danced with him all night long. From that day on, Tim and his twisty toy were best friends. They played and danced together every day. And every night, the twisty toy would go back on the wall, waiting for more fun the next day. Tim was so happy he found the cool, twisty toy that could dance.

Sara and Ben are twins who like to play with paper. They can fold paper into many shapes, like birds, boats and hats. They have a lot of fun making paper crafts. One day, they find a big book of paper crafts in their room. The book has many pictures of different things they can fold. They are very excited and want to try them all. They start to fold a cell, which is a small box with many sides. They follow the steps in the book and make a cell with red paper. They think it looks very pretty and want to show it to their mom. They run to the kitchen, where their mom is cooking. They see a big pot on the stove, with steam coming out. They think it is water and want to put their cell in it. They say, "Mom, look what we made! It is a cell! Can we put it in the water?" Their mom turns around and sees them holding the paper cell. She says, "No, no, no! That is not water, that is soup! It is very spicy and hot! It will burn your cell and your fingers! You should not touch the pot, it is dangerous!" She takes the cell from them and puts it on the table. She hugs them and says, "I am glad you are safe. I am sorry your cell is ruined. But you can make another one later. You are very good at folding paper. But you should always ask before you touch something you do not know. You should be careful and listen to your mom. Do you understand?" Sara and Ben nod and say, "Yes, mom. We understand. We are sorry. We will be careful and listen to you. We love you." Their mom smiles and says, "I love you too. Now, do you want to help me make some bread? You can fold the dough and make shapes with it. It is not spicy and hot, it is soft and yummy." Sara and Ben say, "Yes, mom. We want to help you. We like to fold dough and make shapes. It is fun and tasty." They wash their hands and go to the counter, where their mom gives them some dough. They start to fold it into different shapes, like stars, hearts and flowers. They have a lot of fun making bread crafts. They learn that folding can be fun, but they should also be careful and listen to their mom.
