Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Max. Max loved to help people. One day, he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad. She was hungry and wanted to eat. Max had an idea. He found a menu from a nearby food place. He showed it to Lily. "Look, Lily! Food!" Lily looked at the menu and smiled. She saw yummy things to eat. Max and Lily went to the food place. Lily said, "I want a sandwich, please." The person at the food place gave her a sandwich. Lily was happy. She thanked Max for being helpful. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a cow called Mary. Mary was an original cow, she was never like the other cows. Every day, Mary would roam the fields and roar at the other cows outrageously. All the other cows were very scared of Mary and her loud roaring. One day, Mary saw a small mouse scampering around the fields. She thought it would be fun to scare it. So she chased the small mouse and started roaring very loudly. The mouse was so scared that it ran away as fast as it could. Mary thought this was very funny and laughed. But what she didn't know was that she was scaring away all the other cows too. The other cows were so scared of Mary that they ran away and never returned to the field. From then on, Mary was always alone. The moral of the story is that our actions can cause unexpected consequences - so be careful what you do.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely girl named Lily. She liked to admire the pretty flowers in her garden. She played with her dog, named Spot, every day. They were very happy. One day, Lily and Spot found a well near their garden. They had never seen it before. They looked inside and saw water. Lily and Spot wanted to see what was in the well. They were very curious. Lily and Spot went to get a big rope. They tied it to a tree and put it in the well. Lily and Spot climbed down the rope. When they got to the bottom, they found a secret garden! It was full of beautiful flowers and animals. Lily and Spot played in the secret garden all day. The well was magical, and they had a fun time.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the library with his mom. He saw a big black book on a shelf. Tim wanted to look at the black book, but he was too small to reach it. He asked his mom to help him, but she was busy looking for her own books. Tim saw a chair near the shelf. He thought he could use it to get the black book. He tried to move the chair, but it was too heavy. Tim felt sad because he couldn't get the book he wanted. Then, Tim remembered that his mom told him to ask for help when he needs it. He found a library helper and asked her to get the black book for him. The helper was very nice and got the book for Tim. Tim was happy and thanked the helper. He learned that it's important to ask for help when he can't do something on his own.

One day, a big bear named Ben was walking in the woods. He saw a club laying on the ground. Ben picked up the club and kept walking. He was looking for food to eat. Ben was a very hungry bear. Ben saw a little rabbit named Rita hopping by. "Hello, Rita!" said Ben. Rita looked at the club in Ben's paw and felt scared. "Why are you angry, Ben?" she asked. Ben looked at the club and said, "I'm not angry, I just found this club on the ground." Just then, a big wind came and blew the club out of Ben's paw. It flew in the air and landed on a big beehive. The bees came out and started chasing Ben and Rita. They ran and ran until they found a cave to hide in. The bees couldn't find them. Ben and Rita laughed and became friends. They learned that sometimes, things that look angry can be fun too. They played in the cave and shared their food. From that day on, they were best friends and always helped each other in the woods.
