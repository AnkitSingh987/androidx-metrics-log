One day, a little cat named Tom found a big box. Inside the box, there were many things to sort. Tom saw long rods, short rods, and many ugly toys. He wanted to make the box look nice. Tom started to sort the things in the box. He put all the long rods in one place and all the short rods in another place. He put the ugly toys in a pile by themselves. But while Tom was sorting, a big dog named Max came by. Max saw the ugly toys and wanted to play with them. He took the toys and ran away. Tom was sad because he wanted the box to look nice. He chased Max and asked him to give the toys back. Max saw that Tom was sad and felt bad. He gave the toys back to Tom. Together, they sorted the box and made it look nice. Tom and Max became friends and played with the toys all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named John who lived by the sea. One day, John went for a walk by the beach and discovered a big, old wreck. It was a wooden boat and had seen much better days. John was surprised to see the wreck and was curious to know what was inside. He walked closer and closer, until he heard voices. John called out, "Hello, who's there?". A friendly voice answered back, "It's me! I'm a survivor from the wreck. I'm getting fit again and soon I'll be able to go home!" John was so excited to discover a survivor and he invited him home with him. John and the survivor gladly accepted the invitation. John's parents were so happy to have a new friend living in their home and they all had loads of fun together. The end!

One day, a little bee was flying around. The bee saw an elderly lady sitting on a bench. The lady looked very sad. The bee wanted to help and make her happy. The elderly lady's hat flew away in the wind. The bee wanted to save the hat for her. The bee flew fast to catch the hat. But the hat went higher and higher. The bee tried very hard to save the hat. But the wind was too strong. The hat went far away. The bee could not save the hat. The elderly lady was still sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big dream. She wanted to find a fair copper coin. She looked for it every day, but she could not find it. One day, Lily met a nice boy named Tim. Tim said to Lily, "I can help you find the fair copper coin. I know where it is!" Lily was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim! Let's find it together!" Lily and Tim went to a big tree. Under the tree, they found the fair copper coin. Lily was so happy that her dream came true. She said, "We did it, Tim! We found the fair copper coin!" They both smiled and played together all day.

Once upon a time there was a layland and a bear. They were best friends. One day Layland and the bear went to the park. Layland decided to hang on the swings while the bear found a thermometer. He was curious and wanted to see what it was. Layland started feeling uncomfortable as the bear played with the thermometer. She asked what he was doing. The bear said he was learning how to take readings. Layland said, "That's not safe!" The bear thought for a moment and realized that taking readings could be dangerous. He carefully hung the thermometer back on the tree. The two friends then decided to go play something else. The moral of the story is that it is important to be careful and think twice before doing something risky. It's not wise to play with something if we don't know how to use it safely.

Janine had a yellow balloon. She really wanted to make it pink. She took it outside to the garden and hoped and hoped it would turn pink. But it did not. Janine kept trying, but nothing worked. The yellow balloon stayed yellow even though she hoped it would turn pink. Janine was very sad. She was so sad she started to cry. She hugged the balloon and held it close, but it still remained yellow. Janine was so sad she had to go inside and she never saw the yellow balloon again. She hoped for a pink balloon but it was not to be.
