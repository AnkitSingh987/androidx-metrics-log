Once upon a time, there was a nice park. In the park, there was a big tree. An elderly lady liked to sit under the tree. She loved to watch kids play. One day, a little boy came to the park. He saw the elderly lady and said, "Hi! Do you like to play?" The lady smiled and said, "I like to watch, but I can't play like you." The little boy thought for a moment. Then he said, "I can stand on one foot! Can you do that too?" The elderly lady tried to stand on one foot. She wobbled a bit but did it! They both laughed and clapped. From that day on, the little boy and the elderly lady became friends. They had fun at the park together. The boy would play, and the lady would watch and sometimes try new things. They loved their time at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Stella who was ready to go for a walk. She put on her shoes and said goodbye to her mom. As she walked, Stella approached a big hill. She was scared that she wouldn't be able to pass over it. But her mom had said that if she was ready, she could do anything. So she said to herself, "I'm ready!" Stella started to walk up the hill. She climbed and climbed, and the hill just kept getting bigger. She was starting to get tired, but she kept going. Finally, she reached the top. She was so proud of herself! But then, Stella noticed something: the other side of the hill was very steep. She hesitated, but suddenly remembered what her mom said. She was ready, she could do it! So Stella started to run down the hill. At first, it was so much fun! But then, Stella started to fall faster and faster. She held on tight and closed her eyes. At last, she reached the bottom of the hill. She opened her eyes and was so happy to see that she had made it! Stella realized that she could do anything if she was ready. And from that day on, she was ready for anything.

Once there was a helpful little girl. One day she was given an egg. The egg was special because when you unpacked it, something interesting was inside. The little girl couldn't wait. She ran over to a table and carefully unpacked the egg. Suddenly, her brother walked in. He looked curiously at the egg on the table. "What is that?" He asked. The little girl was excited to show him. She carefully lifted the lid and the two of them peeked inside. Inside was a colourful toy car. The little girl and her brother were delighted. "Cool," her brother exclaimed. They were both so excited to play with the new toy. The little girl was glad that she was so helpful and unpacked the egg to find the surprise inside.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends named Tim and John. They were both very happy to be together. One day, Tim was playing with a whip and was having lots of fun. "Hey John," said Tim, "Let's share this whip. We can have even more fun together!" John agreed and happily grabbed the whip. Together the two boys played with the whip, running around, laughing and having a wonderful time. But then, something strange happened. John was whacking the whip harder than Tim was and it was starting to hurt a bit. Tim said, "John, please be more gentle with the whip. Hard whacks can really hurt!" John was sorry and nodded his head. The two best friends hugged each other and shared the whip more softly. They smiled and both felt very happy to be together. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate loop. The loop was very pretty and lived in a big tree. The loop loved to play with the wind and dance in the sun. One day, the loop saw a big bee. The bee was very big and very strong. The loop wanted to be friends with the bee. The loop said, "Bee, let's be friends and play together!" The bee said, "Yes, let's be friends." The loop and the bee played together all day. They were very happy. But then, the wind came and blew the loop away. The loop was not strong like the bee. The loop broke and was gone forever. The bee was very sad. The moral of the story is: be careful who you play with, because some friends may not be as strong as you.
