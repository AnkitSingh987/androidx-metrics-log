Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a unique boy. He had the power to care for all the animals. The boy loved his power and used it every day. He helped the animals when they were sick, and they loved him too. One day, the boy met a big dog. The dog was sad and needed help. The boy used his power to care for the dog. The dog became happy and strong. The boy and the dog played and had fun all day. But then, the sky turned dark and a big storm came. The boy and the dog were scared. They tried to find a safe place, but the wind was too strong. The storm took away the boy's power to care. The boy and the dog were very sad. They could not help the animals anymore. The unique boy lost his power, and the animals were not happy ever again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a perfect toy house. The house had a door with a frame. Sue loved to play with her toy house every day. One day, Sue's friend, Tim, came over to play. "Can I play with your toy house?" asked Tim. "Yes, you can!" said Sue. They played together and had lots of fun. As they played, Sue showed Tim how to open and close the door with the frame. They took turns opening and closing the door. They laughed and played until it was time for Tim to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy old man. He had a special buckle that was very valuable. He kept the buckle away and always hid it. One day, a thief heard about the old man's wealth. He thought of a plan to steal the old man's buckle. He found where the old man had hidden it and began to take it. But the old man saw what was happening. He shouted and tried to catch the thief, but it was too late. The thief had already taken the buckle and escaped. The old man was sad when he realized his special buckle was gone. He had been so careful to hide and protect it, but now it was gone. He never saw it again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Daisy. Daisy was only three years old, but she was full of magic and imagination. One day, Daisy went out to peek at the fairy. The fairy was so small, you could barely see it! Daisy gasped in delight. She decided she wanted to make a tasty treat for the fairy, so she grabbed some best berries she could find. She carefully placed the berries in a bowl and took it back to the fairy. Daisy saw the fairy had been watching her, so she took a small peek. The fairy smiled and danced with joy when she saw the bowl of tasty berries! She waved goodbye to Daisy and flew away. Daisy was so happy to have made a sweet treat for the fairy. She closed her eyes and imagined she was a fairy herself, flying away with the fairy with a bowl of yummy berries!

One day, a big bird and a small bird lived in a tree. The big bird could fly very high and see the sunrise. The small bird could not fly high. The small bird was envious of the big bird. The small bird wanted to see the sunrise too. So, the big bird thought of a way to help. The big bird would provide a ride for the small bird. The small bird was very happy. The next day, the big bird took the small bird up high. They saw the sunrise together. The small bird was happy and not envious anymore. The big bird and the small bird became good friends. They lived happily in the tree.

Once upon a time there was a tiny kitten that had a secret. She was really scared because she thought no one would understand it. One day she met a nice little girl. She was only three years old and it made the kitten feel safe. So she wanted to show her secret to the little girl. The kitten opened her paw and in it was a small hurt ball. The little girl put out her hand and removed it. When she saw the ball her eyes grew big. She knew it was a secret and she wanted to keep it safe. The little girl held the secret tight and said she would never let it be hurt again. From that day on, the kitten felt happy and free. And she knew that the little girl would keep her secret safe.
