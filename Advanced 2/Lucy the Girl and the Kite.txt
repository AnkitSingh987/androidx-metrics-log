Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, it was very hot, and Lucy wanted to find some shade. She looked around and saw a big tree. The tree had many leaves that made a nice shade under it. Lucy was happy because she could play under the tree and not get too hot. Lucy had a kite that she wanted to raise in the air. She was very patient, waiting for the wind to blow just right. When the wind blew, Lucy ran and let her kite go up, up, up into the sky. The kite danced in the air, and Lucy laughed with joy. After a while, Lucy got tired and sat down in the shade of the tree. She watched her kite fly high above her. She felt happy and cool under the tree. Lucy knew that she could always find shade and have fun on hot days. And she learned that being patient helped her raise her kite high in the sky.

Lily liked to read books. She had many books in her room. She had books about animals, books about colors, books about shapes, and books about stories. She liked to look at the pictures and say the words. One day, Lily found a new book on her shelf. It was a big book with a green cover. It had a picture of a snake on it. Lily was curious. She opened the book and saw more pictures of snakes. Some snakes were long, some snakes were short, some snakes were red, some snakes were black. Lily thought they were pretty. She turned the page and saw a big word. It said "dangerous". Lily did not know what it meant. She asked her mom. Her mom looked at the book and said, "This book is not for you, Lily. It is for grown-ups. Some snakes are dangerous. They can bite and hurt you. You should not touch them." Lily was scared. She did not like dangerous things. She closed the book and put it back on the shelf. She did not want to see it again. She picked another book. It was a book about kittens. Kittens were harmless. They could not bite or hurt you. They were soft and cute. They liked to play and cuddle. Lily smiled. She liked kittens. She opened the book and saw a picture of a kitten with a bow. It looked like her kitten, Daisy. Daisy was her best friend. She always slept on her bed and played with her toys. Lily loved Daisy. She reminded her of happy things. She hugged the book and said, "This is my favorite book. It is for me."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. One day, she saw a big black bird. It was a raven. The raven looked weird, but Lily liked it. Lily wanted to be friends with the raven. She gave it some food, and the raven ate it. They played together in the yard. Lily started to trust the raven, even though it was weird. The raven stayed with Lily every day. They became best friends. Lily's mommy and daddy saw that the raven was nice, and they trusted it too. And they all lived happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl who wanted to explore the world. She decided to march on an exciting adventure. With a skip in her step, she walked until she saw a big, blue sky. She spread her arms out wide and smiled. The little girl kept marching and saw lots of different things. She saw big trees, colorful flowers and a pond with frogs. But then the sky started to look boring. All the little girl saw were clouds, but there was nothing exciting to explore. Heartbroken, the little girl stopped. But then a bright idea popped into her head. She started to march in a circle around the boring sky. She smiled, for she was now creating a new adventure! The little girl marched around and around, until she felt happy and exhausted. The little girl smiled, filled with content. She had explored the sky in her own adventurous way.
