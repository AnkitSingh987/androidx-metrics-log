Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to match things. She had a big box full of different things. She would take them out and try to find two things that looked the same. One day, Sue found a new thing. It was small and harmless. She thought it was a bug. Sue wanted to find another one to match it. She looked and looked in her box, but she couldn't find one. Sue was sad. She wanted to match the harmless bug. Then, her mom came in and saw the bug. She told Sue it was a toy bug. Sue was happy again. She put the toy bug with her other toy bugs. Now, Sue had a new match. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily loved cookies. She saw a big jar of cookies on the kitchen table. Mommy said, "No cookies before dinner, Lily. They are for later." Lily nodded, but she still wanted a cookie. When Mommy went to the living room, Lily tiptoed to the kitchen. She climbed on a chair and reached for the jar. She grabbed a cookie and smiled. She was about to take a bite when she heard Mommy's voice. "Lily, what are you doing?" Mommy asked, coming back to the kitchen. She saw Lily with the cookie in her hand. She looked angry. "Lily, you are naughty. You did not listen to me. You have to wait for dinner. Give me the cookie, now." Lily felt sad and scared. She did not want to give up the cookie. She wanted to eat it. She said, "No, Mommy. It is my cookie. I grabbed it." Mommy shook her head. She said, "No, Lily. It is not your cookie. You grabbed it without asking. That is wrong. You have to share and be polite. If you eat the cookie now, you will spoil your appetite. And you will have a tummy ache. And you will have no dessert. Is that what you want?" Lily thought for a moment. She did not want to spoil her appetite. She did not want to have a tummy ache. She did not want to have no dessert. She wanted to be good and make Mommy happy. She said, "Sorry, Mommy. I was naughty. Here is the cookie. I will wait for dinner." Mommy smiled. She said, "Thank you, Lily. I am proud of you. You are a good girl. You can have a cookie after dinner, if you eat your vegetables. OK?" Lily nodded. She said, "OK, Mommy. I love you. And I love cookies." Mommy hugged her. She said, "I love you too, Lily. And I love cookies too. But not too many. Come on, let's go to the living room. Daddy will be home soon."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to eat avocado. One day, he found a big, green avocado in the kitchen. He was very happy and wanted to eat it all by himself. Tom's big sister, Sue, saw him with the avocado. She said, "Tom, that avocado is spoiled. We cannot eat it." Tom did not want to believe her. He disagreed and said, "No, it is a good avocado. I want to eat it." Sue took the avocado and cut it open. Inside, it was brown and yucky. Tom saw the spoiled avocado and knew Sue was right. He was sad, but Sue said, "Don't worry, Tom. We can go to the store and buy a new avocado." Tom smiled, and they went to the store together to find a fresh, yummy avocado to share.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. One day, Tim saw a mysterious ladder in his backyard. It was big and tall, reaching up to the sky. Tim was very curious about the ladder. Tim decided to start climbing the ladder. He climbed and climbed, higher and higher. He saw many things along the way, like birds and clouds. Tim was very excited and happy to be on this adventure. At the top of the ladder, Tim found a magical land with lots of fun things to do. He played with new friends and had a great time. When it was time to go home, Tim climbed back down the ladder. He was happy to tell his mom and dad about his mysterious ladder adventure.
