Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play a lot. They had many toys and games, but they also liked to make up their own fun. Sometimes they were playful and kind, but sometimes they were naughty and mean. One day, their mom and dad told them they were going on a vacation. They were very happy and excited. They packed their bags and got in the car. They drove for a long time, and then they arrived at a big house near the beach. It was their grandma's house, and she was waiting for them with a big hug and a smile. Tom and Lily loved their grandma, but they also wanted to explore the house and the beach. They ran around, looking for things to do. They found a closet full of old clothes and hats, and they tried them on. They found a basket full of books and puzzles, and they read and solved them. They found a garden full of flowers and butterflies, and they chased and picked them. They had so much fun, they forgot to say hello to their grandma, or to help her with anything. Their grandma was sad and lonely. She wanted to spend time with her grandchildren, and to tell them stories and teach them things. But they were too busy playing, and they ignored her. She felt like they did not care about her, or about being polite and grateful. One day, it rained a lot, and Tom and Lily could not go outside. They were bored and restless. They looked for something to do, but they had already played with everything in the house. They started to fight and argue, and to make a mess. Their mom and dad were angry and disappointed. They told them to stop being selfish and rude, and to go and apologize to their grandma. Tom and Lily felt ashamed and sorry. They realized they had been wrong, and they had hurt their grandma's feelings. They went to her room, and knocked on the door. They said they were sorry, and they asked her to forgive them. They said they wanted to talk to her, and to listen to her stories and learn from her. Their grandma was surprised and happy. She opened the door, and hugged them. She said she loved them, and she forgave them. She said she was glad they had come to see her, and she invited them to sit with her. She told them stories about her life, and about their mom and dad when they were little. She taught them songs and games, and gave them cookies and milk. They had a wonderful time, and they laughed and smiled. Tom and Lily learned a lesson. They learned that playing is good, but not when it makes someone feel bad. They learned that saying hello and thank you is important, and that being kind and respectful is better than being naughty and mean. They learned that their grandma was a special person, and that they should cherish her and her wisdom. They learned that they should not forget the people who love them, and who they love. They learned that vacation is not only about having fun, but also about being with family.

Sam and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. They make sand castles, swing on the swings, and slide on the slides. They have fun every day. But one week, it rains a lot. Sam and Tom cannot go to the park. They are sad and bored. They stay at home and watch TV. They do not play or talk. They are lazy. One day, Sam's mom says, "Enough! You need to do something. Why don't you make a craft with these things?" She gives them paper, glue, scissors, and crayons. Sam and Tom look at the things. They do not know what to make. They shrug and say, "OK." They start to cut and glue and color. They make a big paper rocket. They make a paper moon and stars. They make paper aliens and astronauts. They use their imagination. They have fun. They show their craft to Sam's mom. She smiles and says, "Wow! You made a wonderful space adventure. I'm proud of you. Do you want to play with it?" Sam and Tom nod and say, "Yes!" They take their craft to the living room. They pretend to fly the rocket to the moon. They meet the aliens and the astronauts. They talk and laugh. They are happy. They are not lazy anymore. They are creative.
