Once upon a time, there was a bear who was very poor. He was always looking for things that might make him happy. One day, he decided to go on a walk to try to find something special. As he walked, he noticed something shiny on the ground. He picked it up, and it was a bag full of lots of different things: shiny stones, colorful flowers and big buttons. He was very excited! He knew he should sort through the bag to make sure he had all the special things. So he put the stones together, the flowers together and the buttons together. He had fun doing this, and he was amazed when he was finished. The bear was so excited he took the bag and ran home to show his friends. When he arrived, they were all very happy! His friends thought the things he found were so special. The poor bear was happy. Not only did he find something special, but he also had fun while sorting through the bag.

Once upon a time, there was a big park with lots of trees, a slide, and swings. Every day, many kids came to play there. They laughed and made noise while playing. One day, a new kid came to the park. He was very quiet and did not talk to anyone. The other kids thought he was stupid because he did not make noise like them. They did not play with him. The new kid felt sad and sat under a tree. Suddenly, the sky turned dark, and it started to rain. All the kids ran to find a place to hide from the rain. The new kid, who was already under the tree, stayed dry. The other kids saw this and realized they were wrong to call him stupid. They asked if they could join him under the tree, and he said yes. They all became friends, and from that day on, they played together in the park, making noise and having fun. And they learned that sometimes, being quiet is not so bad after all.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to build a tower with his toy blocks. He took out his big box of blocks and a board to build on. Tim thought it would be interesting to see how tall he could make his tower. As Tim built his tower, it got taller and taller. He was very happy with his tower. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into his room and landed on the tower. The tower shook and wobbled. Tim was scared that the tower would fall, but the bird had an idea. The bird helped Tim build the tower even taller. With the bird's help, the tower was stronger and did not wobble. Tim and the bird were very proud of their interesting tower.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball. One day, Max saw his ball at the top of the stair. Max wanted to get his ball, but he was scared to go up the stair. Max thought about how much he loved his ball. He knew he had to be brave to get it back. Max took a deep breath and started to go up the stair. But Max was not careful, and he fell down. Max felt sad and he suffered a little hurt on his leg. Max's friend, a kind bird, saw Max's trouble. The bird wanted to help Max. The bird flew up the stair, picked up Max's ball, and brought it back down to him. Max was so happy to have his ball back! He thanked the bird and they played together all day. Max learned to be more careful on the stair, and he never suffered a fall again.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Max. Max liked to play all day with his friends. They would run, jump, and have lots of fun together. One day, Max and his friends found a big box. They decided to put all their toys in the box. They put balls, cars, and dolls in the box. Max and his friends were very happy to see all their toys in one place. When night came, Max and his friends were tired from playing. They said goodnight to each other and went to their homes. Max was happy to have such a fun day with his friends. He curled up in his bed and fell asleep, dreaming of more jolly days to come.
