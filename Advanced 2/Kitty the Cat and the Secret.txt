Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play near the big bookcase in her house. One day, she wanted to help her mom clean the house. Kitty decided to polish the bookcase to make it shine. As Kitty started to polish, she saw a small door behind the books. She was very curious and opened the door. Inside, she found a secret room full of toys and treats! Kitty was so happy and excited. Kitty played in the secret room all day long. When her mom came home, she showed her the secret room. Her mom was surprised too! They both laughed and played together in the secret room, and the bookcase became their favorite place in the house.

There once was a little pupil named Tim. Tim liked to listen to his teacher. One day, Tim's teacher wore a fancy hat. Tim asked, "Why do you wear a fancy hat today?" The teacher said, "It is a special day. We will have a surprise." All the kids were excited for the surprise. They played and waited. Then, a big box came into the room. The box moved and made noise. The kids could not believe it. They thought it was a big toy. But, when the teacher opened the box, it was not a toy. It was a big, scary monster! The monster roared and scared all the kids. The teacher was sad. The surprise was not fun. The kids did not like the bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor. He had a big boat. The sailor was important. He wore a blue hat and a big smile. He loved to sail on the water. One day, the sailor found a box. The box had a lock. The sailor needed to unlock the box. He looked for a key. The key was shiny and small. The sailor found the key. He unlocked the box. Inside the box, there were toys. The sailor was happy. He shared the toys with his friends. They played together and had fun. The end.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Tim. He was three years old and he loved to play outside. One day, Tim was playing in his garden when he saw something strange. His wheelbarrow was full of heavy oil. Tim was curious and he asked his dad, "What is this heavy oil in my wheelbarrow?" His dad laughed and said, "It's motor oil. We use it to keep the car running smoothly." Tim was excited and asked, "Can I spoil it?" His dad smiled and said, "No, son, you can't spoil the oil. We need it for the car. But why don't you help me clean up the wheelbarrow?" Tim gladly agreed and soon the wheelbarrow was almost as good as new. The end.

Once upon a time there was a clean house. Inside it there was a family. Every day they would have a meal together. They would all sit around the table and share the meal. The mum would have tea, the dad would have juice and the little one would have milk. They would laugh and talk as they ate their meal. After the meal was finished, everyone would help to clean up. The dad would wash the cups and plates, the mum would dry them and the little one would put them away. When everything was clean and tidy, the family would have a hug and be very happy. They were very happy that their meal was done and their house was clean. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her dog, Max. One day, Lily and Max went to the park to play ball. They were having so much fun. At the park, Lily saw a stranger. The stranger had a big bag of tasty treats. He said, "Hi, Lily! Do you want some tasty treats for you and Max?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "Yes, please!" The stranger gave Lily and Max some treats. Suddenly, Max saw a squirrel and ran after it. Lily tried to catch Max, but he was too fast. The stranger said, "I can help you find Max!" Lily and the stranger went to look for Max, but they couldn't find him. Max had escaped, and Lily was very sad. The stranger took Lily's hand and led her away from the park. Lily never saw Max again, and she learned to be careful with strangers.
