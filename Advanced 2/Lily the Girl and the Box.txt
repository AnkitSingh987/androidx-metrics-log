Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside. One day, Lily saw an attractive box. It was a package. It was big and had pretty colors. Lily said to her mom, "Mom, can I open the package?" Her mom said, "Yes, but pause and think first. It may not be ours." Lily thought for a moment and then decided to open it anyway. When she opened the package, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog jumped out! The dog was a gift for Lily from her dad. Lily was so happy and played with her new dog all day.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside with her friends. One day, she saw a beautiful rainbow in the sky. Lily had an idea. She wanted to invite all her friends to see the rainbow. She ran to her friends' houses and said, "Come see the rainbow! It is so pretty!" Her friends were very excited and came with her. Together, they all looked at the rainbow and smiled. They were so happy to see the pretty colors in the sky. They played and laughed under the rainbow until it was time to go home. And from that day on, whenever they saw a rainbow, they remembered the fun day they had together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. His name was James and he was three years old. James loved to build things. One day, he was outside building with some blocks. All of a sudden, he noticed a cherry tree nearby. He wanted to build something with the cherries! He asked his mom, "Can I pick some cherries and build with them?" His mom smiled and said, "Sure, but be careful. It's cold outside!" So, James carefully picked some cherries and found a spot back on the lawn. He used the cherries to build a big cherry tower. When he was finished, he was very proud. James beamed and said, "Look, mom, I built a cherry tower!" His mom smiled and said, "That's fantastic! It looks great, James!"

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Olive. She liked to play with her toys and send letters to her friends. One day, Olive saw a big, dark cloud in the sky. She felt scared but didn't know why. Olive had a new friend who lived far away. She wanted to send him a letter. She wrote the letter and put it in a box with a toy. Olive's mom told her to be careful, but Olive didn't listen. She ran outside to mail the box. The dark cloud got bigger and closer. Olive didn't see it and kept running. She tripped and fell, and the box opened. The letter and toy flew away in the wind. Olive was very sad and cried. She learned that she should have listened to her mom and been more careful.

One day, a cat and a dog were walking in the park. They saw a nice strip of grass to play on. The cat said to the dog, "Let's play here!" The dog agreed, and they started to play. As they played, they saw a big box on the nice strip of grass. They stopped playing to discuss what was inside the box. The cat said, "Maybe there are toys in the box!" The dog said, "Or maybe there are treats!" The cat and the dog decided to open the box to find out what was inside. They were very excited, thinking about the toys or treats they might find. But when they opened the box, they found something they didn't expect. It was a big, colorful ball! The cat and the dog looked at each other and smiled. They knew they were going to have even more fun playing with the ball on the nice strip of grass. They played with the ball all day, and it was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Elly. She had a long trunk that she used to pick up things. Elly lived in a big forest with her friend, a small bird named Billy. One sunny day, Billy was very frustrated. He could not find his favorite toy. He said to Elly, "I can't find my toy, can you help me look for it?" Elly said, "Yes, I can use my trunk to help find your toy." They looked and looked, but they could not find the toy. Then, Elly had an idea. She used her trunk to disturb the leaves on the ground. As she moved the leaves, they found the toy hiding under them! Billy was so happy to find his toy. He said, "Thank you, Elly, for helping me with your trunk!" They played together all day long, and they were the best of friends.
