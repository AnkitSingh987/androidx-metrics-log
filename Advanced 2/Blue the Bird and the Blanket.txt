Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Blue. Blue lived in a small cage in a cold room. Blue was not happy because he wanted to fly and play outside. One day, a kind girl named Lily came to visit Blue. She saw that Blue was sad in his cold cage. Lily had an idea to make Blue happy. She put a warm blanket on the cage to make it less cold. Blue felt better, but he still wanted to go outside. Lily opened the cage door and let Blue fly outside. Blue was so happy to be free! He flew around and played in the warm sun. At the end of the day, Blue flew back to Lily. She put him back in his cage, but now it was not cold anymore. Blue and Lily became best friends and they played together every day.

Once there was a little boy who was very hungry. He walked into the kitchen to find something to eat. But the kitchen was very hot and all of the food was gone. "Mommy, why is it so hot in here?" asked the little boy. "I'm sorry, honey" Mommy replied, "but the stove is broken." The little boy was so disappointed. He couldn't eat and he was feeling sleepy. He went to bed without dinner, feeling sad and hungry. But sleep wouldn't come. The little boy tossed and turned in the hot room all night, never falling asleep. In the morning, he was still exhausted and grumpy. He had a terrible day at school and was so cranky that he couldn't even play with his friends. The little boy never found out that the kitchen was so hot. And he never got to have dinner.

Once upon a time, there was a small, enthusiastic boy named Tim. Tim loved to supply his friends with toys and treats. One day, he found a big box of gum in his room. He was very excited and thought about how he could share the gum with all his friends at school. The next day, Tim took the gum to school. He told his friends, "I have a surprise for you all later!" His friends were excited too. They could not wait to see what Tim had for them. They knew that Tim always had something fun to share. At lunchtime, Tim opened the box and gave gum to all his friends. They were so happy! They chewed the gum and made big bubbles. Suddenly, one of Tim's friends blew a bubble so big that it popped and covered his face! Everyone laughed and had a great time. Tim felt proud that he could supply his friends with such a fun treat.

One day, a brown dog named Mark went for a walk. He saw a bike with a pedal. Mark thought the pedal looked fun. He wanted to try it. "I will learn to ride the bike," Mark said. Mark tried to get on the bike. He put his paw on the pedal. "This is hard," Mark said. He tried again and again. He did not give up. Soon, Mark could ride the bike. He was so happy. He rode the bike all day. "I did it!" Mark said. "Now I can ride the bike with the pedal." And he did, every day.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to bake cookies with her mommy. One day, Lily said, "Mommy, I want to make cookies today!" Mommy smiled and said, "That's a great idea, Lily! Let's get the sugar and start baking." They went to the kitchen and started making yummy cookies. Lily was very happy to mix the sugar and help her mommy. After they baked the cookies, they shared them with their friends. Everyone loved the cookies and said, "Thank you, Lily and Mommy! These cookies are so good!" Lily felt proud and wanted to bake cookies again soon. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went out to play. He saw a big igloo in the snow. It was very organized. Tim was very happy to find it. Tim said, "Hello, who lives in this igloo?" A small penguin came out and replied, "Hi, I am Pip the Penguin. This is my home." Tim and Pip became good friends. One day, they found a big box. They opened it and saw many balloons. They played with the balloons and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The balloons lifted the igloo into the sky! Tim and Pip were surprised but they laughed and enjoyed their new flying home.
