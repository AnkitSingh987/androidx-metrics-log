Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride the subway with his mom. It was big and fast, and he liked to watch the world go by. One day, Tim and his mom went on the subway. They sat down and Tim looked out the window. He saw a big, slow turtle trying to reach a green leaf. The turtle tried and tried, but it was too slow. Tim wanted to help the turtle. He asked his mom if they could get off the subway and help the turtle. His mom said yes, so they got off and walked to the turtle. Tim picked up the leaf and gave it to the turtle. The turtle was happy and thanked Tim. Then, Tim and his mom went back on the subway and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Zigzag. Zigzag loved to run and jump all day. One sunny day, Zigzag went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Zigzag saw a big dog. The dog was very thirsty and wanted to drink some water. But Zigzag was playing near the water bowl, and the dog was scared to go near him. The dog said, "Zigzag, can I please drink some water? I am very thirsty." Zigzag wanted to be a good friend, so he stopped playing and let the dog drink the water. The dog was very happy and thanked Zigzag. After that, Zigzag and the dog became best friends and played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with his friends in the park. They would run, jump, and chase each other all day long. Fluffy was very happy with his friends. One day, Fluffy and his friends decided to play a new game. They wanted to split into two teams. One team had to catch the other team. Fluffy and his friends were very excited to play this game. Fluffy was the leader of one team, and his best friend, a dog named Spot, was the leader of the other team. They played the game all day, and everyone had lots of fun. In the end, both teams were very tired but happy. They all agreed that it was the best game they had ever played.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run around. One day, while playing, he saw a big cobweb in a corner of the yard. Spot wanted to play with the cobweb, but his friend, the small cat named Kitty, said, "No, Spot! Don't play with the cobweb. It's not good." But Spot did not listen and refused to stop. Spot touched the cobweb and, all of a sudden, a big spider came out. The spider was not happy that Spot touched its home. The spider bit Spot, and he felt very bad. Spot learned that he should have listened to Kitty.

Once upon a time there was a big pot which was heavy. It was very important and it needed to be protected. So every day, a big strong man would carry the pot and keep it safe. One hot day, the man didn't have the energy to carry the heavy pot, so he asked for help. A small 3-year-old girl saw the man struggling, so she offered to help. He was very happy! The girl put her tiny hands around the pot and carried it everywhere. She was so brave and so strong! She would go and protect the important pot wherever it needed to be. Everyone was so proud of the girl, and thanked her for helping to protect the special pot. The man said he was so glad that the girl was there to help. From then on, the girl always smiled when she thought of the pot. She knew she had done an important job helping to protect it.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to swim in the creek near her home. Every day, she would splash and play in the water with her friends. One day, Ducky saw a new friend by the creek. It was a tiny fish who looked very hungry. Ducky thought, "I will feed the little fish!" She found some crumbs on the ground and put them in the water for the fish to eat. The fish was very happy and thanked Ducky for the food. They became good friends and played together in the creek every day. Ducky learned that helping others can make new friends and bring joy to everyone.
