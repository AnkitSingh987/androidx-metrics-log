Once upon a time, in a small house, a boy named Tim found an ashtray. He did not recognize what it was. It was round and had a funny smell. He thought it was a toy, so he played with it. He showed it to his mom, but she was busy cooking spicy food for dinner. One day, Tim put the ashtray on the table. He saw a small, red box near it. The box had a picture of fire on it. Tim was curious, so he opened the box. Inside, he found small, wooden sticks. He played with them too, not knowing what they were. Later, when mom was not looking, Tim took one stick and rubbed it on the box like he saw in a cartoon. Suddenly, the stick caught fire! Tim was scared and dropped it on the table. The fire touched the ashtray and started a big fire in the house. The spicy food burned, and the house was filled with smoke. Tim and his mom got out, but they were very sad because their house was gone. From that day, Tim learned not to play with things he did not recognize.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a brother named Sam. They lived in a small house with their mom and dad. Tom felt lonely because Sam always played with his friends and not with him. One day, Tom saw a big box in the living room. He asked his mom, "What is in the box?" His mom said, "It is a supply of toys for you and your brother to play together." Tom was very happy. He ran to his brother and said, "Sam, we have new toys to play with!" Sam smiled and said, "Let's play together, Tom!" They opened the box and found many fun toys. They played together all day and were not lonely anymore. Tom and Sam became the best of friends and always shared their toys with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big pond. One day, Fin saw a big pick on the ground. He felt nervous. "What is this?" he asked his friend, Sam the frog. Sam said, "I don't know, but let's swim away!" As they swam away, they heard a loud noise. They stopped and looked back. The big pick was gone! They felt scared but curious. A friendly bird flew down and said, "Don't be scared! I took the big pick to build my nest." Fin and Sam felt happy and safe. They swam and played together, and they were not nervous anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little nosy cat. The cat loved to stretch and play all day. One day, the cat found a tiny box. The box was only an inch big. The nosy cat was very curious about the box. The cat tried to stretch its paw to open the box. The box opened and something unexpected happened. Out came a tiny mouse! The cat was very surprised to see the mouse. The mouse was also surprised to see the cat. But instead of being scared, the mouse smiled. The cat and the mouse became friends and played together. They had lots of fun and the nosy cat was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, a lawyer lived in a small town. He was very dull and did not have many friends. One day, he received a gift from a faraway place. He was very happy and excited to see what was inside the box. The lawyer opened the box and found a talking bird. The bird said, "Hello, I am your new friend!" The lawyer was surprised but happy to have a new friend. They talked and played all day. But one day, the talking bird flew away. The lawyer was very sad and cried. He looked for the bird everywhere, but he could not find it. In the end, the lawyer was alone again, and he missed his friend very much.

One day, a little girl was in her room. She was wearing a pale blue skirt and her shoes made tapping noises as she stepped around. Suddenly, she heard a voice. "Hello," said the voice. Startled, the little girl looked around but she couldn't see anyone. "Where are you?" she asked. "I'm here," said the voice. The little girl stepped closer and noticed a tiny mouse peeking out from beneath her skirt. The mouse said, "Your skirt is so pale and lovely. Can I borrow it for a few minutes?" The little girl smiled and said, "Of course you can. What would you like to do with it?" The mouse said, "I want to use it to make a cozy bed for myself." The little girl stepped back and watched as the mouse carefully made a beautiful bed right under her skirt. When the mouse was finished, it stood up, bowed, and said, "Thank you for your kindness. I'll never forget it!"
