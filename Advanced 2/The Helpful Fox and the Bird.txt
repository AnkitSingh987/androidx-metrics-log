Once upon a time, there was a helpful fox. He lived in a big forest with many friends. The helpful fox liked to help all his friends when they needed it. One day, a little bird lost her nest. She was very sad. The helpful fox wanted to make her happy. So, he put the little bird on his back and went to find her nest. They looked all around the forest. They found the nest high up in a tree. The helpful fox put the little bird back in her nest. The little bird was so happy. They became the best of friends. And from that day on, the helpful fox and the little bird played together in the big forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten named Fluffy. Fluffy was very anxious because she lost her toy ball. She looked everywhere for it, but she couldn't find it. Fluffy started to cry. Her friend, Tommy the dog, saw her crying and asked her what was wrong. Fluffy told him that she lost her toy ball. Tommy wanted to help her find it. They decided to count all the places where the ball could be. First, they looked under the bed. One. Then, they looked behind the couch. Two. Next, they looked in the garden. Three. Finally, they found the toy ball in the kitchen. Four! Fluffy was so happy, and she thanked Tommy for helping her. From that day on, Fluffy and Tommy became the best of friends. They played together every day, and Fluffy was never anxious again. And whenever they lost something, they would count the places to look, just like they did with the toy ball.

One day, a big cat named Tom was playing in the kitchen. He saw a sink with deep water. Tom was very curious and wanted to touch the water. He did not know that the water could make things shrink. Tom jumped into the sink and splashed in the water. He felt funny and looked down. He saw that he was now a small cat! Tom was scared because he did not want to be small. He heard his friend, a dog named Max, coming into the kitchen. Max saw the small cat in the sink and did not know it was Tom. Max helped the small cat out of the sink and gave him a big hug. Tom felt happy and safe with Max. The water in the sink dried up, and Tom grew back to his big size. Max was surprised but very happy that his friend was back. They played together all day, and Tom never went near the sink again.

Once there were two friends, Mit and Kit. They were always together and had a very special knob which they shared. The knob was made of smooth, round stone and was a reminder of the special bond they shared. One day Mit and Kit decided to go on an adventure in the forest. They were alert and careful as they walked through the trees, searching for an exciting place to explore. Suddenly, Kit stopped and pointed to a big rock. "Look Mit! Let's go up there. We can see the whole forest from the top!" Mit smiled and said, "Let's do it together!" They united and scrambled up the rock. The view from the top was beautiful. They looked at each other, smiling, and held the knob up high. With the knob, they remembered that when they are united, nothing can stand in their way.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very adventurous. He loved to play with his toys and explore the world around him. One day, Tim found a big screw on the ground. He decided to twist it and see what would happen. As he twisted the screw, something unexpected happened. A big door opened in the ground! Tim was surprised and excited. Tim went inside the door and found a magical land full of fun and games. He played all day and made lots of new friends. When it was time to go home, Tim twisted the screw again, and the door closed. He couldn't wait to tell his family about his big adventure.
