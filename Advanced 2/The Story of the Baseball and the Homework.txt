Once there was a boy who loved baseball. He was very stubborn about it, he felt that he could do anything! One day, his mom told him to do his homework instead of playing baseball. He refused, saying "No, I wanna do baseball!" His mom said, "You have to do your homework first. It’s important." The boy was very stubborn. He shook his head and said, "No, I wanna do baseball first!" His mom sighed and said, "Okay, but only if you promise to do your homework after." The boy nodded, smiled, and ran outside to play baseball. He played for hours, he was so happy! When he finally went inside, he did his homework without any arguments. He was happy because he got to do what he wanted, but he also learned that it was important to do his homework too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch cartoons on TV. One day, he found a magic remote that could make the cartoons come to life. This made Tim very happy, but he soon got spoiled. He watched cartoons all day and did not play with his friends. One day, the magic remote stopped working. The cartoons went away and Tim was sad. He tried to fix the remote, but it did not improve. Tim's mom told him that maybe it was time to play outside with his friends. Tim did not want to, but he had no choice. As Tim played outside, he found a new magic remote. This time, it did not bring the cartoons to life. Instead, it made Tim's friends laugh and have fun. Tim learned that being spoiled was not good and that playing with friends was more fun than watching cartoons all day. The moral of the story is that too much of a good thing can be bad, and it's important to spend time with friends.

Once upon a time, there was a proud boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy arrow. One day, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, he saw a big tree. He wanted to serve his arrow to the tree. Tim said, "Mom, can I throw my arrow at the tree?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful, Tim." So Tim threw his arrow at the tree. The arrow hit the tree and fell down. Tim was very happy. Tim's friend, Sam, came to the park too. Sam saw Tim's arrow and said, "Wow, Tim! You did a good job!" Tim was proud and said, "Thank you, Sam. Do you want to play with me?" Sam said, "Yes, let's play together!" And they played with the arrow all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a stable full of animals. There were horses, goats, cows and many more. But the animals were all miserable. They disagreed and quarreled with each other all the time. One day, a 3-year-old boy named Peter came to the stable. He asked the animals why they were quarreling. One of the horses replied, "It's because none of us are happy. We are all very miserable." Peter thought for a moment and then said, "You must all be nice to each other and be happy!" All the animals nodded and agreed that would make them happy. From then on, the animals in the stable were always friendly to each other. They were happy and content. And Peter always came back to visit them and share his wisdom.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big dog. The big dog looked at Tim and said, "I want food!" Tim was scared, but he wanted to help the dog. Tim ran home to get some food. He looked in the kitchen and found a big bowl. He put dog food in the bowl and made it ready. He took the bowl to the park for the big dog. When Tim got back to the park, he saw the big dog was not alone. There was a little dog too! The big dog and little dog were now friends. They both said, "Thank you!" and ate the food. Tim was happy to help and made two new friends.
