Once upon a time, there was a big billboard. On the billboard, there was a sad face. Every day, people walked by and saw the sad face. They wondered why the face was sad. One day, a little girl named Lily looked at the sad face. She wanted to make the face happy. Lily closed her eyes and started to imagine. She imagined a big, bright sun and lots of happy faces. As Lily imagined, the sad face on the billboard started to change. It became a happy face! Everyone who walked by now saw the happy face and smiled. Lily knew that imagining good things could make people happy. And from that day on, the billboard with the happy face made everyone feel good.

Once upon a time, there was a little ball named Bounce. Bounce lived in a small, dull room with no toys or friends. One day, Bounce saw a big, fancy room through a window. It was full of luxury, with lots of toys and fun things. Bounce wanted to go there and play with the other toys. Bounce decided to roll out of the dull room and find the luxury room. On the way, Bounce met a toy car named Zoom. Bounce said, "Hi, Zoom! Can you help me find the big, fancy room?" Zoom replied, "Sure, Bounce! Let's go together." Bounce and Zoom rolled and bounced together until they found the luxury room. Inside, they saw lots of toys and had so much fun playing with them. Bounce liked the new room and was happy to have a friend like Zoom. They played together every day, and Bounce was never lonely again.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Daisy and Jack who were very happy together. Every day they played together in the park, laughing and having fun. One day, they decided to play tag. Jack was so fast and he couldn't be caught. Daisy started to get jealous and she wanted to get revenge. She sharped a stick and started chasing Jack with it. Jack was so scared and he ran away. But Daisy was too fast for him and she hit Jack with the stick making him cry. Daisy shouted, "I have won victory!" She thought she won the game and she was happy with herself. But Jack was really hurt and he ran away crying. Daisy realised that she had made a mistake and hoped that Jack was okay. But Jack was too upset and he never came back. Daisy was alone in the park and she never won a victory again.

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow sun. It was very useful because it gave light and warmth to all the animals and plants. In a small forest, two friends, a rabbit and a squirrel, lived happily. One day, the rabbit and the squirrel found a big yellow fruit. The rabbit said, "This fruit is mine, I saw it first!" The squirrel said, "No, it is mine, I touched it first!" They started to disagree about who the fruit belonged to. As they argued, a wise old owl heard them. The owl came and said, "Why don't you share the fruit? It is big enough for both of you." The rabbit and the squirrel agreed. They both ate the yellow fruit and were very happy. From that day on, they always shared and never disagreed again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy. She was 3 years old and lived in a big city. One day, Lucy's mom needed to pay for the house. She had to go to the store to do this and it took a long time. When she finally got home, Lucy saw a big leak on the ceiling. Water was dripping down and it was making a big mess. Lucy's mom was not happy. She said to Lucy, "We need to pay to get this fixed. Are you comfortable with that?" Lucy nodded her head yes and smiled. She was happy that her mom was going to make the house comfortable again!

Anna and Ben were eager to go to the park. They liked to play on the swings, the slide and the sandbox. They also liked to look at the flowers and the bugs. "Look, Anna, a bee!" Ben said, pointing to a yellow and black insect on a purple flower. "It is making honey!" Anna was curious. She wanted to see the bee up close. She walked slowly to the flower and leaned in. "Be careful, Anna!" Ben warned. "The bee might sting you!" Anna did not listen. She trusted the bee. She thought the bee was friendly and pretty. She reached out her hand to touch the bee. But the bee did not like that. The bee felt scared and angry. The bee buzzed loudly and flew away from the flower. Then the bee turned around and stung Anna on her finger. "Ow!" Anna cried. She pulled back her hand and saw a red bump on her finger. It hurt a lot. She ran to Ben and showed him her finger. "I told you, Anna!" Ben said. "The bee can sting you! You should not touch the bee!" Anna felt sorry. She realized she made a mistake. She should have listened to Ben. He knew more about bees than she did. "I'm sorry, Ben," Anna said. "You were right. I should not have touched the bee. Can you help me?" Ben nodded. He was a good brother. He took Anna's hand and led her to their mom. Their mom was sitting on a bench nearby. She had a first aid kit in her bag. "Mom, Anna got stung by a bee!" Ben said. "Oh, no!" their mom said. She looked at Anna's finger and saw the red bump. She took out a band-aid and some cream. She put the cream on the bump and covered it with the band-aid. "There, there, Anna," their mom said. "It will be okay. The cream will make it feel better. The bee was just scared. It did not mean to hurt you." Anna felt a little better. She thanked her mom and hugged her. She also hugged Ben and said sorry again. "It's okay, Anna," Ben said. "I forgive you. Next time, let's just watch the bees from far away, okay?" Anna nodded. She learned her lesson. She decided to be more careful and listen to Ben. She still liked the bees, but she did not want to touch them anymore. She and Ben went back to play in the park. They had fun and forgot about the bee sting.
