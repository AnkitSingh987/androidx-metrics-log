Once upon a time, there was a poor dog named Spot. Spot liked to wander around the big park. One day, Spot saw a big well in the park. He had never seen it before. He was very curious about the well. Every day, Spot would wander near the well and look inside. He could not see the bottom of the well. He wondered what was down there. Spot wanted to find out, but he was too scared to get too close. One sunny day, Spot saw a little boy playing with a ball near the well. The ball rolled and fell into the well. The little boy was sad. Spot knew he had to help. He was brave and went close to the well. Spot barked and barked. Soon, a big man came and got the ball out of the well. The little boy was happy, and Spot was proud. Spot had faced his fear and helped a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a line of ants. They liked to march all day long. They were very dependable. Every day, they would march to find food and bring it back to their home. They were very good at their job. One sunny day, the ants were marching as usual. They were in a long line, going from their home to a big picnic. They were going to get food for their family. But, something unexpected happened. A big raindrop fell from the sky and landed right in front of the line. The ants didn't know what to do. Then, a brave ant decided to march around the raindrop. The other ants followed him. They made a new line and kept marching to the picnic. The ants were happy because they found a way to keep going. They learned that even when something unexpected happens, they could still be dependable and find a way to finish their job.

One day, a big group of friends went on a safari. They saw many animals like lions, elephants, and monkeys. They were very happy to see all the animals and learn their names. They saw a big rock and decided to sit on it to rest. They started to rock back and forth, laughing and having fun. The animals came close to watch them play. The friends felt like they were part of the animal family. After playing, they felt hungry. They had a picnic with spicy food. The monkeys smelled the spicy food and came to try it. The monkeys liked the spicy food, and they all became friends. The friends and the animals had a great day together on the safari.

Once upon a time, there was an attractive dog named Bobo. Bobo lived in a small house with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They loved playing together and going on adventures. One day, they found a big switch near the park. They didn't know what it did, but they wanted to find out. Bobo said to Tim, "Let's rescue the switch and see what happens!" Tim agreed and they both pushed the switch together. Suddenly, the ground started to shake and they heard a loud noise. They were scared, but they wanted to know what was happening. As they looked around, they saw a big hole in the ground. The hole was getting bigger and bigger. They tried to run away, but it was too late. The hole swallowed them up, and they were never seen again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was an obedient squirrel. He lived in a big tree in a park. He liked to play with his friends, the birds and the bugs. One day, the squirrel saw a big, red ball near the tree. He thought it was a nut, so he tried to steal it. But when he touched the ball, it started to roll away. The squirrel was surprised and ran after the ball. As the squirrel chased the ball, he met a dog who was playing with the ball. The dog was not mad that the squirrel tried to steal the ball. Instead, they became friends and played together. The squirrel learned that it was more fun to share and make new friends than to steal.
