Once upon a time, in a big blue sea, there was a big red whale named Wally. Wally was not like the other whales. He was red, and he did not behave well. He would splash water on the other sea animals and laugh. The other sea animals did not like this. One day, a small fish named Finny swam up to Wally and said, "Wally, please behave. We do not like when you splash water on us." Wally laughed and splashed water on Finny. Finny was sad and swam away. Later, Wally saw that all the sea animals were playing together, but they did not want to play with him. Wally felt sad and lonely. He thought about what Finny said and decided to behave. He swam to the sea animals and said, "I am sorry. I will behave now." The sea animals smiled and let Wally play with them. From that day on, Wally behaved well and had many friends. The moral of the story is that if you behave well, others will want to be your friend.

Once upon a time there were two best friends who were always together. One day they were playing in the park and they found a crystal. The boys were very excited and they wanted to take the crystal home. They were both worried that someone else might try to take it. The crystal was very shiny and sparkly. The boys decided to put it in a tidy box so that it would not get dirty or broken. The boys looked after the crystal and made sure it was safe. But the crystal needed something special. One day, the boys started to cry. The crystal listened to their cries and a magical thought came to their minds. They needed a wish! A wish was made and the crystal glowed with a glowing light. Suddenly, a beautiful rainbow appeared in the sky and the boys could not believe it! The crystal magic had given them the wish that they needed and from that day on the boys were never sad again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved the color purple. She had a purple dress, purple shoes, and even a purple bed. But what Lily wanted more than anything was a purple pony. One day, Lily went for a walk with her mom. They walked past a big, locked gate. Behind the gate, Lily saw a beautiful purple pony! She was so happy and wanted to play with the pony. Lily asked her mom if they could unlock the gate. Her mom found the key and unlocked it. Lily went inside and played with the purple pony. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, Lily and her mom said goodbye to the pony and went home. Lily was so happy she got to play with a purple pony.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had long, soft ears and loved to dance. One day, Spot was dancing in the park when he saw his friend, Kitty. "Hi, Kitty!" said Spot. "Do you want to dance with me?" Kitty smiled and said, "Yes, Spot! I love to dance too!" So, Spot and Kitty danced together in the park. They danced and danced until they were tired. Just then, they saw a big tree with a treat hanging from it. "Look, Kitty! A treat!" said Spot. "Let's get it!" Spot and Kitty worked together to get the treat. They were so happy to share the treat after their long dance. From that day on, Spot and Kitty were the best of friends and loved to dance together in the park.

Lily and Max were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. Lily wore a pink apron with flowers on it. Max wore a blue apron with stars on it. "Let's make a cake!" Lily said. "We need flour, eggs, milk, and sugar." "OK!" Max said. "I will get the flour. You get the eggs." They opened the cupboard and took out the things they needed. They put them on the table. They also found a big bowl and a wooden spoon. "Ready, set, go!" Lily said. "Let's shoot the flour into the bowl!" She grabbed the bag of flour and squeezed it hard. A cloud of white dust came out of the bag and flew into the air. It covered the table, the floor, and Lily's face. "Wow!" Max said. "That was fun! Let me try!" He took the bag of flour from Lily and squeezed it harder. Another cloud of white dust came out of the bag and flew into the air. It covered the chairs, the window, and Max's face. They laughed and laughed. They looked like snowmen. "Hey, what are you doing?" Mom said. She came into the kitchen and saw the mess. She was not happy. She wore a regular apron with no flowers or stars on it. "Sorry, Mom," Lily and Max said. "We were making a cake." "A cake?" Mom said. "This is not how you make a cake. This is how you make a disaster. Come on, let's clean up this mess. And then we can make a real cake together. OK?" "OK, Mom," Lily and Max said. They took off their aprons and helped Mom wipe the flour off everything. They learned a new word: shoot. They also learned that shooting flour was not a good idea.
