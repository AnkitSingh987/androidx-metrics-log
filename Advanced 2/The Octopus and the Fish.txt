One day, a funny octopus lived in the sea. The octopus had many friends. They liked to play and laugh together. The octopus was very good at making everyone happy. One day, the octopus found a toy. The toy was broken. The octopus wanted to help. The octopus said, "I can replace the broken toy with a new one." The fish said, "Thank you, octopus. You are a good friend." The funny octopus went to find a new toy. It found a big, colorful ball. The octopus brought the ball to the fish. The fish was very happy. They played with the ball all day. The octopus and the fish had a lot of fun.

Sara and Tom are going to visit their grandma. They need to pack their bags with clothes and toys. Sara likes to pack her purple dress and her doll. Tom likes to pack his cars and his ball. "Are you ready, Sara?" Tom asks. He is waiting by the door with his bag. "Yes, I am ready, Tom," Sara says. She zips her bag and picks it up. "Let's go!" They go outside and see their mom and dad putting the bags in the car. The car is big and red. It has a roof that can open and close. "Can we open the roof, mom?" Sara asks. She likes to see the sky and the birds when they drive. "Sure, we can open the roof, Sara," mom says. She pushes a button and the roof slides back. "But we have to close it if it rains, okay?" "Okay, mom," Sara says. She climbs into the car and sits next to Tom. They buckle their seat belts and smile. "Are you excited to see grandma?" dad asks. He starts the car and drives away. "Yes, we are excited, dad," Sara and Tom say together. They look out the window and see the trees and the houses. They wonder what grandma has for them. Maybe some cookies or a story. They can't wait to get there.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved cheese more than anything else in the world. One day, she found a big, yellow piece of cheese in the kitchen. Mimi was so happy, she wanted to share the cheese with her best friend, Buzzy the bee. Mimi went to Buzzy's house and said, "Buzzy, I want to introduce you to my favorite food, cheese!" Buzzy looked at the cheese and thought it was a stupid idea. "I only like flowers," Buzzy said. But Mimi was a good friend and asked Buzzy to try just a small bite. Buzzy tried the cheese and found out that he liked it too! From that day on, Mimi and Buzzy enjoyed eating cheese together and they stayed best friends forever.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big, fierce dog. The dog had sharp teeth and a loud bark. Lily was scared, but she wanted to look at the dog. Lily said, "Hi, dog. You look fierce. Are you nice?" The dog stopped barking and wagged its tail. Lily knew the dog was nice, even though it looked fierce. Just then, an alarm went off. The loud noise scared the dog. Lily said, "Don't worry, dog. It's just an alarm." The dog felt better and they became friends. They played together all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in her garden. One day, she saw a big sunflower. It was so tall and pretty. Lily was very happy to discover it. But there was a problem. A naughty bird came and wanted to eat the sunflower. Lily did not want the bird to eat her pretty sunflower. So, she thought of a plan to stop the bird. Lily found some food that birds like. She put the food far away from the sunflower. The bird saw the food and flew to it. The bird ate the food and did not eat the sunflower. Lily was so happy that her plan worked. And the sunflower was safe and grew even taller.
