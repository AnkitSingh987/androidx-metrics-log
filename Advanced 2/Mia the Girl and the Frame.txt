Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to paint pretty pictures. One day, she found a big, dull frame in her room. She wanted to make it beautiful. Mia took her paint and brush and started to paint the frame. She painted it with bright colors. But she forgot to put something under the frame. The paint began to soak into the table. Mia was sad. Mama came and saw the mess. She was not mad. She said, "Mia, next time put something under the frame." Mia learned that it is important to be careful when we do things. And the frame became very pretty.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the doctor with his mom. Tim had a silly toy that he loved to play with. It was a funny-looking toy with a big nose and big ears. Tim took his silly toy everywhere he went. At the doctor's office, the doctor told Tim that he needed to get an x-ray. Tim was scared, but his mom said, "Don't worry, it will be quick and easy." Tim held his silly toy tight and went into the x-ray room. The doctor took a picture of Tim's insides with the x-ray machine. When the x-ray was complete, Tim and his mom went back to the doctor. The doctor looked at the x-ray and said, "Everything looks good, Tim!" Tim was happy and relieved. He thanked the doctor and went home with his mom and his silly toy, feeling brave and proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim did not like avocado. He would say, "I hate avocado!" every time his mom tried to give him some. One day, his mom thought of a fun idea to make Tim like avocado. In the morning, Tim's mom made a noisy toy out of avocado. It was a funny, green toy that made a loud sound when Tim played with it. Tim's mom said, "Look, Tim! This is a special avocado toy. It's noisy and fun to play with!" Tim looked at the noisy avocado toy and started to play with it. He laughed and said, "I like the noisy avocado toy!" Tim's mom smiled and asked, "Do you want to try eating some avocado now?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Okay, I will try it." Tim took a small bite of the avocado and found out that he liked it! He said, "Yum! I don't hate avocado anymore." Tim's mom was happy that he finally liked avocado. From that day on, Tim loved to eat avocado and play with his noisy avocado toy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a lot of junk on the ground, and it made him feel miserable. He wanted to play, but the park was not clean. Tim said, "Mom, can we pick up the junk and make the park nice again?" As they started to clean up, a funny clown came to the park. The clown saw Tim and his mom picking up the junk and wanted to help. The clown said, "I can make you laugh with a joke while we clean up!" Tim smiled and said, "Okay!" The clown told a joke that made Tim and his mom laugh a lot. As they laughed, something unexpected happened. All the junk in the park started to float away! The clown had magic jokes that could make things better. Tim, his mom, and the clown cleaned up the park together, and everyone was happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with many branches. Tim wanted to climb the tree. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim. Don't go too high." Tim started to climb the tree. He saw a wild squirrel on a branch. The squirrel said, "Hello, Tim. I need help. My friend, the mechanic, is stuck in the tree." Tim asked, "Where is your friend?" The squirrel pointed to a high branch. Tim climbed higher and saw the mechanic squirrel. The mechanic squirrel was scared. Tim helped the mechanic squirrel climb down. They all became good friends. Tim's mom was proud of him.
