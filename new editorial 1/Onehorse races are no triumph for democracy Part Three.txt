Title: Onehorse races are no triumph for democracy Part Three

One-horse races are no triumph for democracy

Neither the "pathbreaking" constitutional reform of One Nation, One Election nor the idea of an Opposition-mukt Bharat is aimed at fostering competition, and that does not augur well. There will be little thrill left in a one-horse race. That it strikes at the very roots of the democratic character of the nation may be a far greater concern.

By Ashok Lavasa Former Election Commissioner of India and former Union Finance Secretary.
