Title: Health data must drive action not just Part Three

Health data must drive action, not just headlines

The next time survey findings are released, the real question should not be what the numbers show. The real question should be: what will change in programmes in a month's time, in six months and in a year? Alongside this, India definitely needs more and timely health data. However, what we need even more is accountability.

Dr. Chandrakant Lahariya is a practising physician in preventive and cardio-metabolic medicine and specialist in health policy. He has worked with the World Health Organization (WHO) and the UN system for nearly 18 years
