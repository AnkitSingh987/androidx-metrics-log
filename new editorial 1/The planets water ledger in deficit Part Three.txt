Title: The planets water ledger in deficit Part Three

The planet's water ledger in deficit

Fix cropping patterns to hydrology: Align minimum support prices, procurement policies and crop insurance with local water availability; discourage water-intensive crops in semi-arid basins and promote millets, pulses and climate-resilient varieties. Scale circular water systems in cities: Make wastewater reuse, rainwater harvesting and storm-water capture mandatory for large urban developments and industrial clusters; treat treated wastewater as a core supply source, not an afterthought. Protect natural water infrastructure: Restore wetlands, floodplains and groundwater recharge zones; legally secure environmental flows in rivers to maintain ecological resilience and long-term water security.

By Priyanka Vadrevu The writer is Fellow  -  Climate Co-Adaptation at PlanAdapt and Independent Researcher (Water Resources Management, Climate Change & Disaster Risk Resilience)
