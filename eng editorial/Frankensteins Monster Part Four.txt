Title: Frankensteins Monster Part Four

Frankenstein's Monster

But the popularity of the "Buy Canadian" campaign - an expression of economic nationalism triggered by Trump's threats, including making Canada the 51st state of the US  -  has already shown that many Canadians support their government's challenge to the US president. Carney will now have to convince them that the costs of confrontation are worth paying. For the Canadian PM, the showdown with Trump is the clearest test yet of his thesis that middle powers must stand up to great powers and unite.

Indeed, he has been busy securing deals in Asia and Europe, a paradigm shift away from Canada's economic dependence on the US so far. It is too early to say whether these new partnerships have the substance to mitigate the pain that a breakdown in US-Canada relations will cause. But Carney is doing something rather rare in the Trump era: A middle power is saying no to a superpower.
