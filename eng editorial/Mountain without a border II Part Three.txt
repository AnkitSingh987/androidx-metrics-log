Title: Mountain without a border II Part Three

Mountain without a border ~ II

None of this asks the three governments to trust one another. It asks only that they act on a fact they already share and mostly ignore. The frontier we have just taught to speak of armies still has nothing to say about ice, and the ice is the likelier killer. The convoy will always be needed; grief will always find work.

But a region that can keep a hotline open against a war it dreads can surely keep one open against a flood it knows is coming ~ and the honest measure of whether we have understood this August at all will be whether, when the next mountain lets go, the warning crosses the border before the water does. By T KANNAN (The writer is a practising Chartered Accountant. He can be reached at kannan@cakt.in)
