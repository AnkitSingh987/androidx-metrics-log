Title: Can AI really fix Americas trillion debt Part Three

Can AI really fix America's $39 trillion debt problem?

It also depends on how US policymakers respond to it. Will they raise taxes on corporations? Or will they cut back on income support for workers displaced by AI? So yeah, AI isn't some magical money tree. It will probably make the US economy stronger and give the government more breathing room. But it won't magically wipe out a $39 trillion debt.

At the end of the day, tough decisions about taxes and government spending will still have to be made. AI may buy policymakers some time and make those choices a little easier, but it can't make them disappear.
