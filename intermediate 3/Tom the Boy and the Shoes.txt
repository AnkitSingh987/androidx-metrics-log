Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tom. Tom was very generous. He loved to share his toys and snacks with his friends. One day, Tom found a big print on the ground. It looked like a big foot. Tom was curious and wanted to know who made it. Tom followed the print and found a big monster. The monster was sad because it would accidentally destroy things with its big feet. Tom wanted to help the monster. He thought of a plan to make the monster stop destroying things. Tom gave the monster soft shoes to wear. The shoes made the monster's feet gentle. The monster was happy and did not destroy things anymore. Tom and the monster became friends. The moral of the story is to always help others and be kind.

Once upon a time there was a fierce storm. The wind was blowing so strong it made the trees spin. A log was spinning in the wind. The log spun and spun, getting faster and faster. Along came a brave boy who wanted to help the log. He held onto the log tightly and tried to slow it down. But the wind was too fierce! The boy was getting tired but he would not give up. He kept holding onto the log and slowly it began to spin slower and slower and then it stopped. The boy gave a big smile. He had done it! He had stopped the log from spinning in the fierce wind.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Grace. Grace liked to play in the mud. She would get muddy all the time. Each day, her mom would get a mop and clean up the mess. One day, Grace decided to find a way to prevent her mom from having to clean up the mud. She thought and thought, and then she had an idea. She decided to find a fancy way to play in the mud. Grace found it. She found a way to make fancy patterns in the mud. They looked like stars and hearts and pretty designs. Her mom was so surprised and pleased when she saw it. From then on, her mom would take out the mop less and less, as Grace continued to make fancy mud designs.

Lily and Ben like to spend time with Grandma. Grandma has a big house with many things to see and touch. Lily and Ben like to look at the vase on the table. The vase is blue and shiny and has flowers on it. Grandma says the vase is very old and was a gift from Grandpa. One day, Lily and Ben are playing in the living room. They see the vase on the table. They want to smell the flowers. They go to the table and reach for the vase. But the vase is too heavy and slippery. It falls from their hands and breaks on the floor. The flowers and water spill everywhere. The vase is empty and broken. Lily and Ben are scared and sad. They cry and say sorry. Grandma hears the noise and comes to the living room. She sees the mess and the broken vase. She is angry and sad too. She says the vase was very special and can't be fixed. She tells Lily and Ben to go to their room. Lily and Ben go to their room. They feel bad and wish they didn't touch the vase. They hug each other and say they love each other. They hope Grandma will forgive them and love them too. They wait for Grandma to come and talk to them.

Once upon a time, in a friendly little town, there lived a cat named Tom. Tom had many friends in the town. One day, Tom wanted to make a yummy meal for all his friends. He thought and thought about what to make. Tom decided to take some sauce and mix it with noodles. He thought his friends would love it. He went to the store and got the sauce and noodles. On his way home, he met his friend, Dog. Dog wanted to help Tom make the meal. Together, Tom and Dog made the sauce and noodles. They were very happy. They invited all their friends to eat the yummy meal. Everyone loved it and had a great time. Tom and Dog were proud of their cooking and happy to see their friends enjoy it.
