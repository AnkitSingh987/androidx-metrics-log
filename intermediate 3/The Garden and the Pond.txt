Category: Intermediate
Title: The Garden and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a garden. In this garden, there were many ordinary things. There were flowers, trees, and a little pond. The garden was a fun place to play. One sunny day, a bird named Bobby came to the garden. Bobby liked to soar high in the sky. He saw a little girl named Lily playing near the pond. Lily saw Bobby and said, "Hi, bird! Can you soar high?" Bobby said, "Yes, I can!" He soared high up into the sky. Lily clapped and laughed. Bobby felt happy. They played together in the garden all day long. And from that day on, the garden became an even more special place for both Bobby and Lily.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful dancer named Lily. She loved to dance and make people happy. Every day, she danced and played with her friends in the park. One day, while Lily was dancing, a new kid came to the park. He looked sad and didn't recognize anyone. Lily stopped dancing and walked over to the new kid. She said, "Hi, I'm Lily. Do you want to play with us?" The new kid smiled and said, "Yes, please." As they played, something unexpected happened. The new kid started to dance better than anyone else! Lily and her friends were amazed. They learned that it's good to help others and make new friends. The more friends they had, the more fun they could have together. And that made everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a mysterious man. He had a big bag of candy. He gave candy to all the kids. The kids liked the candy a lot. One day, a little girl named Lucy met the mysterious man. He gave her some candy too. Lucy was happy. She wanted to trust the mysterious man because he gave her candy. Lucy told her mom about the mysterious man and the candy. Her mom said it was okay to enjoy the candy, but always be careful. Lucy learned to trust her mom and still enjoy the candy from the mysterious man. And they all lived happily ever after.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big hill. Sara wants to climb the hill. Ben is scared. "Come on, Ben. Let's test our boots. They are new and strong. They can help us climb the hill," Sara says. "No, Sara. The hill is too high and steep. We might fall and hurt ourselves. I don't want to test our boots. I want to stay here," Ben says. Sara is brave. She does not listen to Ben. She puts on her boots and starts to climb the hill. She feels happy and proud. She thinks she can reach the top. Ben is not brave. He stays on the ground. He watches Sara climb the hill. He feels sad and worried. He thinks Sara might get in trouble. Suddenly, Sara slips and falls. She screams and cries. Her boot is stuck in a hole. She can't get up. She is scared. "Help, Ben! Help me! My boot is stuck! I can't move!" Sara shouts. Ben hears Sara. He forgets his fear. He runs to help Sara. He puts on his boots and climbs the hill. He reaches Sara and hugs her. "Don't worry, Sara. I'm here. I'll help you. You are brave, but you are also silly. You should not climb the hill alone. It is dangerous," Ben says. He pulls Sara's boot out of the hole. He helps her stand up. He checks if she is hurt. She has some scratches and bruises, but nothing serious. "Thank you, Ben. You are brave, too. You came to help me. I'm sorry I did not listen to you. You are right. We should not climb the hill alone. It is dangerous," Sara says. They hug each other. They are happy and relieved. They decide to go back to their home. They hold hands and walk down the hill. They still have their boots on. They did not test their boots, but they tested their friendship. They learned a lesson. They are brave, but they are also smart.
