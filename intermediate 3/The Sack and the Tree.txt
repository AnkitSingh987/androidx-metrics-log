Category: Intermediate
Title: The Sack and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big sack. The sack had a funny nose on it. The nose was very attractive. It made people smile when they saw it. One day, the sack went for a walk. It met a little boy. The boy saw the sack's attractive nose and laughed. He wanted to play with the sack. So, they played together all day. They ran, they jumped, and they had a lot of fun. After a long day, the sack and the boy were tired. They sat under a tree and rested. The boy hugged the sack with the attractive nose. They were happy to be friends. From that day on, they played together every day and had many adventures.

---

One day, on Tuesday, a little girl named Mia went for a walk with her mom. They wanted to find a special bug that could glow. Mia felt safe with her mom by her side as they walked. They looked under rocks and behind trees. Then, they found a little bug that was glowing! It was so pretty and made Mia very happy. They watched the bug glow and it made them feel warm and safe. Mia and her mom went home and told dad about the glowing bug they found. They all felt happy and safe together. Every Tuesday, they would go and look for more glowing bugs, making it their special day.

Once upon a time, there was a busy little girl named Lily. She loved to draw and color. One day, her mommy gave her a big box. Lily was very excited to see what was inside. Lily started to unpack the box. Inside, she found many pencils of different colors. There were red, blue, yellow, and green pencils. She was so happy to see them all. Lily decided to draw a beautiful picture with her new pencils. She drew a big sun, a blue sky, and green grass. She showed her mommy the picture, and her mommy was very proud. They hung the picture on the wall, and Lily felt very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty frog named Fred. Fred liked to jump and play all day. He lived in a big pond with his friends. One day, Fred saw his friend Tim the turtle. Tim was sad because he could not jump like Fred. Fred wanted to help Tim. He said, "Tim, I will encourage you. You can do it!" Tim tried to jump. At first, he could not do it. But Fred kept encouraging him. Soon, Tim was able to jump a little bit. They both laughed and played together. And they were very happy.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big, red ball. She liked to play with her ball outside. One day, Mia saw a weak, little bird. The bird was cold and hungry. Mia wanted to help the bird. Mia went inside her house to find something to wrap the bird in. She found a soft, warm cloth. Mia carefully wrapped the bird in the cloth. She also found a small fork and some bread. Mia used the fork to give the bird little pieces of bread to eat. Soon, the bird felt better. The bird was warm and not hungry anymore. Mia was happy to help the bird. She learned that it is good to help others when they need it. The bird flew away, and Mia went back to play with her ball. But now, she always looked out for other friends who might need her help.

---

One day, a boy named Tim found a phone. He liked to play with the phone. But the phone did not work. Tim was sad. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Stand up, Tim. Let's fix the phone." They tried to fix the phone together. But the phone was still broken. Tim felt dizzy because he was so sad. Then, Tim's dad came home. He saw Tim and his mom with the broken phone. Tim's dad knew how to fix it. He fixed the phone and it worked. Tim was happy and not dizzy anymore. They all played with the phone together.
