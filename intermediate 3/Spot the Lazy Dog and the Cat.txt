Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Lazy Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time in a small house, there lived a lazy dog named Spot. Spot did not like to play or run. He liked to sleep all day. Spot had a little bed that he loved, but he was growing big. One day, he could not fit in his little bed anymore. Spot's friend, a cat named Whiskers, told him about a big, soft bed in the old house down the road. Spot and Whiskers went to find the big bed. The old house was dark and cold. They saw a small flame in the room where the big bed was. Spot was so happy to see the big bed. He jumped on it and it was very soft. But, the flame from the room was getting bigger and bigger. Spot and Whiskers did not see the flame grow because they were having too much fun. The flame got too big and the old house started to burn. Spot and Whiskers tried to run away, but it was too late. The fire was all around them. They should have been careful and not played near the flame. The moral of the story is to always be careful and not play near fire. Being lazy can lead to bad things happening, like Spot not fitting in his bed and going to the old house.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy found a magic key. This key could unlock a special door. Behind the door was a world full of fun and luxury. Lucy was very excited to find the door and use the key. In the special world, Lucy saw a big, crazy party. There were balloons, candy, and games. The animals from the forest were there too. They were all dancing and playing. Lucy joined the party and played with the animals. Lucy had a great time at the crazy party. She ate lots of candy and played many games. When it was time to go home, Lucy used the magic key to unlock the door again. She said goodbye to her new friends and went back to her world. Lucy always remembered the fun and luxury she had in the special world.

One day, a big dog and a small cat had a quarrel near the gate. The dog said, "This is my gate. You cannot come in." The cat said, "No, this is my gate. I want to come in." The dog was very big, and the cat was very small. The cat was scared, but it wanted to go through the gate. The dog did not let the cat go in. They were both very angry. Suddenly, a nice bird came and said, "Stop! This gate is for everyone. You can both come in." The dog and the cat were surprised. They stopped fighting and went through the gate together. They became friends and were not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a clumsy boy named Tim. Tim had a flute that he loved to play. Every day, he would play his flute and make music. One day, Tim put his flute on a high shelf. He was too small to reach it. He tried to jump, but he could not get the flute. Tim was sad. He wanted to play his flute and make music. Tim's friend, Sam, came to visit. Sam saw Tim looking sad. "Why are you sad?" Sam asked. "I can't reach my flute," Tim said. Sam was taller than Tim. Sam reached up and got the flute for Tim. Tim was happy. He played his flute and made music. Tim and Sam danced and laughed. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a barber named Tom. Tom had a shop where he would cut hair. He had many friends who came to him for haircuts. One day, a new barber named Sam opened a shop next door. Tom was jealous because Sam's shop was big and shiny. One day, a little boy named Tim came to Tom's shop for a haircut. Tom was very happy to see Tim. Tom said, "Hi Tim! How do you want your hair cut today?" Tim said, "I want a cool haircut, please!" Tom started to cut Tim's hair, but he was thinking about Sam's shop and how jealous he was. While Tom was thinking about Sam, he accidentally cut too much of Tim's hair off. Tim's hair was too short! Tom was sad because he did not want to break Tim's heart. He said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I made a mistake." Tim said, "It's okay, Tom. I still like my haircut." Tom felt better and learned that it's important to focus on his own work and not be jealous of others.
