Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bald Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a bald bird named Bob. Bob had no feathers on his head, but he had a very long neck. He liked to play with his friends, the little rabbits, and the small mice. One day, Bob saw a big cat coming into the forest. The cat was looking for food. Bob wanted to help his friends, so he went to warn them. He said, "Watch out, friends! Big cat is coming!" The rabbits and mice heard Bob's warning and quickly hid in their homes. The big cat looked around but couldn't find any food. He saw Bob and said, "Why did you warn them, bald bird?" Bob replied, "Because they are my friends, and I want to keep them safe." The big cat left the forest, and Bob's friends came out to thank him. They were all happy and safe, thanks to Bob's long neck and big voice.

Once upon a time, there was a gray raven. The raven liked to go and play with his friends. One day, he saw a big, red ball in the sky. The raven thought it would be fun to go and play with the ball. The raven told his friend, "I will go play with the big, red ball in the sky." His friend said, "No, don't go! It is not a ball. It is hot and will hurt you." But the raven did not listen. The gray raven flew up and up to the big, red ball. He got closer and closer. But it was not a ball. It was the hot sun. The raven got too close and got hurt. He fell down and could not play with his friends anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to step on things. He would step on toys, bugs, and even his sister's dolls. One day, Tim saw a distant hill, and he wanted to step on it too. Tim took a rag with him and started to walk to the hill. The rag was his favorite, and it made him feel safe. As he walked, he saw many things to step on. He stepped on flowers, leaves, and even a small ant hill. But he didn't know that stepping on things could sometimes be bad. When Tim finally reached the distant hill, he felt very happy. He stepped on the hill, and then he saw something he had never seen before. It was a big snake! The snake was angry because Tim had stepped on its home. It bit Tim, and he dropped his rag. Tim was very sad and hurt. Tim learned that stepping on things can be bad. He should be more careful and kind to the things around him. The moral of the story is to treat everything with care and respect, because you never know when something might bite you back.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a big jar of candy. He loved his candy very much. Every day, he would eat some candy from the jar. His friends would come and ask if they could have some too, but Tim would say "no". He wanted all the candy for himself. One day, a new boy came to play. His name was Sam. Sam saw Tim's jar of candy and asked if he could have some too. Tim said "no" again. Sam was sad but he did not fight. Instead, he went home and brought back his own jar of candy. Sam shared his candy with all the friends. They were very happy. Tim saw how happy everyone was with Sam. He felt bad for not sharing his candy. He decided to share his candy too. All the friends were even happier now. Tim learned that it is good to share with others. Sharing makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy old man. He lived in a small house near the train tracks. Every day, he would watch the trains go by, blowing steam from their big engines. The man liked the steam, but the noise made him grumpy. One day, the grumpy man decided to buy a train ticket. He wanted to see where the steam trains went. He put on his hat and walked to the train station. The man bought a ticket and waited for the steam train to come. When the train arrived, the grumpy man got on and found a seat. As the train went down the tracks, he looked out the window and saw beautiful fields and trees. The steam train took him on a fun adventure, and the grumpy man smiled. He learned that sometimes, even grumpy people can have a good time.
