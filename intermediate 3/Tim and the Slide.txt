Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Slide

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play at the park. One day, Tim saw a big slide that he had never seen before. He was very excited to go on it. Tim went closer to the slide and noticed a smelly smell. He did not recognize where the smell was coming from. He looked around and saw a big, smelly sock next to the slide. Tim thought, "Maybe the sock will be important later." Tim climbed up the slide and slid down. Whee! He had so much fun. But when he reached the bottom, he saw that the smelly sock had moved. Now it was right in front of the slide! Tim remembered the sock from before and knew what to do. Tim picked up the smelly sock and threw it in the trash. The smell went away, and Tim could play on the slide without the smelly smell. The other kids at the park were happy too. Tim had saved the day, all because he recognized the smelly sock from before.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a magic tape. She was very excited and ready to play with it. Lily thought, "What if I use this tape on my big teddy bear? Maybe it will shrink and become small like my other toys." So, she put the tape on her teddy bear. Suddenly, the teddy bear started to shrink! It became as small as her other toys. Lily was very happy. Then, Lily heard a tiny voice. It was the teddy bear! It said, "Thank you, Lily! Now I can play with the other toys too." Lily and the teddy bear played together all day long. They had lots of fun with the magic tape. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a vendor named Tom. He sold fruits in a small town. Every day, he would yell, "Fruits available! Come get your fruits!" One sunny day, a little girl named Sally heard Tom yell. She wanted to buy some fruits for her mom. Sally walked to Tom's fruit stand and saw many colorful fruits. She picked a big red apple and a bunch of sweet grapes. Tom was happy that Sally came to buy his fruits. He thanked her with a big smile. Sally took the fruits home to her mom. Her mom was so happy and proud of her. They ate the fruits together, and they were the best fruits they ever had.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a hive. Inside the hive, there were many bees. One bee was named Buzz. Buzz was a gifted bee. He could make honey faster than any other bee. One day, Buzz met a big bear named Bear. Bear was very hungry. Buzz wanted to help Bear. Buzz said, "I can give you honey, Bear. I make the best honey." Bear was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Buzz. I love honey." Buzz went back to the hive to get honey for Bear. He worked very hard and squeezed the honey out. He filled a jar with honey. Buzz took the jar to Bear. Bear was very excited. He opened the jar and started to eat the honey. But then, something unexpected happened. The honey was not sweet. It was very spicy! Bear's mouth was on fire. He cried, "Buzz, this honey is too hot for me!" Buzz was confused. He didn't know how the honey became spicy. Bear was sad and hungry. He went away to look for food. Buzz felt very bad. He didn't know what went wrong. The other bees laughed at Buzz. They had played a trick on him and changed the honey. Now, Buzz was not the gifted bee anymore.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to throw a ball. He asked his mom, "Can I throw the ball, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can." Tim threw the ball high in the air. Suddenly, a zoom car came by very fast. The ball went into the zoom car's open window! Tim and his mom were surprised. The zoom car stopped, and a nice man got out. He said, "Is this your ball?" Tim said, "Yes, it is!" The man gave the ball back to Tim, and they all laughed. Tim and his mom had a fun day at the park.
