Title: The Parrot and the Tree

Once upon a time, there lived a parrot in a tall treetop. Everyday he would jump, skip and balance around the tree, going from one branch to the next. One day, the parrot decided he was feeling a bit tired, so he settled into a cozy nest of leaves. He closed his eyes and cuddled up next to his feathers, feeling very sleepy. Just then, a small voice called up to the parrot. It was a three year old girl, looking up in wonder at the parrot's nest. The parrot opened one eye and said, "Hello little one, why are you here?" The girl replied, "I wanted to learn how to balance as good as you. Can you show me?" The parrot laughed and hopped down from his nest. He showed the girl how to take slow steps and stay steady in one place, just like him. He stretched his wings wide and said, "Now it's your turn, be sure to take small steps and balance." The little girl carefully balanced and before she knew it, she was jumping from one branch to the next. She felt so proud of herself! The parrot and the girl both laughed and cheered at the top of their lungs. Then, the parrot said his goodbyes and flew off to his nest to take a much needed nap. Once there was a little girl who wanted to go for a walk. She stepped out of her house onto the porch. It seemed to go on forever and as she walked, it seemed like the porch went deeper and deeper. The girl liked this deep porch because it made her feel like she was exploring a whole new world. As she walked, the little girl saw all kinds of things that she had never seen before. There were butterflies, birds, and flowers of all different shapes and colors. It was so exciting! Finally, the little girl came to the end of the porch. At the end, she saw a big, deep tree with a swing tied to a low branch. She was so happy, she hopped on the swing and pushed herself as hard as she could. She felt like she was flying! The little girl spent the rest of the day happily swinging and exploring the deep porch. Eventually, the sun began to set and she knew it was time to go back home. But this was a day she would never forget!

One day, a boy and his dad went to buy some land. They were very glad to have a new place to play and grow things. The boy said, "Dad, I am so happy we can buy this land!" On the way to the land, they saw a big, big hole. They did not know what it was for. The boy asked, "Dad, what is that big hole for?" Dad said, "I don't know, let's go and look." When they got close to the hole, they found a big, friendly dog. The dog was digging the hole to hide a toy. The boy and dad were surprised! They did not know a dog lived on their new land. The boy said, "Dad, can we keep the dog?" Dad said, "Yes, we can." And they all lived happily on their new land with their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little reindeer. This reindeer felt nervous. He wanted to make new friends, but he was too shy. One day, the reindeer saw a big tree. He gazed at the tree and saw a little bird sitting on a branch. The reindeer said, "Hi, bird. I am nervous. I want to be your friend, but I am shy." The bird smiled and said, "Hi, reindeer. I am shy too. But we can be friends. Let's play together." The reindeer felt happy. He and the bird played all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. The reindeer was not nervous anymore. He had a new friend. From that day on, the reindeer and the bird were best friends. They were not shy or nervous anymore. They played together every day and had lots of fun.
