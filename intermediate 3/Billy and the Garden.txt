Category: Intermediate
Title: Billy and the Garden

Billy was three years old. He was playing in the garden with his mum, when he spotted a pebble. It was different to any other pebble he'd seen before. It was bright blue, and it sparkled in the sunlight. He stared at it in wonder. “What is it, Mum?” he asked. Mum looked troubled. “Oh, that's a special pebble,” she replied softly. “Can I have it?” he asked, reaching out. She smiled sadly and shook her head. “No, my darling,” she said. “It's not for us to keep. It belongs to the sky.” Billy looked up at the sky, wondering why it needed a pebble. He kept staring, wondering what the pebble meant.

Sara likes to make cards for her friends. She uses paper, crayons, stickers and glue. She writes "I love you" and draws hearts and flowers. She feels happy when she makes cards. One day, Sara has a lot of cards. She wants to give them to her friends at school. She puts them in her backpack and goes to the bus. She feels excited to share her cards. At school, Sara gives a card to each of her friends. They smile and hug her. They say "thank you" and "you are so generous". They like Sara's cards very much. They give her some cards too. Sara feels proud and grateful. Sara has one card left. She sees a new girl in her class. The new girl looks sad and lonely. She has no cards. Sara walks to her and says "hi". She gives her the last card. The new girl looks surprised and happy. She says "thank you" and "you are so kind". She gives Sara a big hug. Sara feels warm and friendly. Sara and the new girl become friends. They play and talk and laugh. They make more cards together. They feel joy and love.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot loved to help his friends. One day, a little bird needed help. The bird lost her word. This word was very special to her. Spot said, "I will find your word." Spot looked and looked for the word. He managed to find it under a big tree. The bird was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The word changed. The bird was sad again. Spot said, "Don't worry, I will help you." Spot and the bird worked together. They managed to change the word back to the right one. The bird was happy again. Spot was a good friend. The moral of the story is: always help your friends, and good things will happen.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous cat named Max. Max loved to play with his friends on Sunday. One Sunday, Max woke up late and missed playing with his friends. Max was sad. He looked for his friends everywhere. Max looked in the park, at the big tree, and near the pond. But Max could not find his friends. Max felt very lonely. Then, Max heard a sound near the big tree. He saw his friends playing a game. Max ran to them and they all played together. Max was happy that he did not miss his friends on Sunday anymore.

Once there was a rabbit and a duck who loved to play together in the hay. One day, the rabbit was running and hopping so quickly he got too close to the edge of the deep lake and lost his balance. The rabbit slipped into the lake and started struggling to stay afloat. The duck, seeing what had happened, quickly flew down and tried to help. "Hold onto my wings so I can help pull you up," he said. But the rabbit was too scared. "I'm too scared," said the rabbit. "I can't!" The duck kept encouraging the rabbit, saying: "Don't be scared, I'm here. I won't let you lose." With a little courage, the rabbit grabbed onto the duck's wings and he was able to make it out of the lake safely. The moral of the story is that having courage and help from others can help us in times of trouble. It is important to face our fears and not get scared when we find ourselves in challenging situations.
