Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Lisa went to the park with her mom. They saw a big ice cream truck. Lisa was very happy. She wanted to get a cone. Her mom said, "Yes, we can include a cone in our park fun today." Lisa was excited but a little worried. She did not know which flavor to pick. There were so many! Her mom said, "Don't be worried, just pick the one you like most." Lisa thought and thought. Then she said, "I want the pink one, please!" The ice cream man gave Lisa a big pink cone. She was not worried anymore. Lisa and her mom sat on a park bench. They enjoyed the sunny day and their ice cream cones. Lisa was very happy and thought it was the best day ever.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very impatient. He wanted to go to the park and enjoy playing with his friends. He put on his shoes and his belt, and ran to his mom to ask if he could go. At the park, Tim and his friends played on the swings, the slide, and the seesaw. They had lots of fun, but then something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came running up to them. The dog had a belt in its mouth. Tim and his friends were surprised. They looked around to see if someone was looking for the dog. Just then, a girl came running up. She was so happy to see her dog and thanked Tim and his friends for finding him. The dog had taken her belt when she wasn't looking! They all laughed and played together, enjoying the rest of the day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a pilot named Tom. Tom flew a big plane in the sky. The plane was very smooth when it went up and down. One day, Tom met a little girl named Lisa. Lisa asked Tom, "What do you do?" Tom said, "I am a pilot. I fly a big plane." Lisa wanted to fly too, so Tom recommended that she try a toy plane first. Lisa got a toy plane and played with it all day. She loved her toy plane and had so much fun. Soon, Lisa wanted to be a pilot just like Tom when she grew up.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom was very hungry. He saw a big pizza on the table. Tom wanted to eat the pizza all by himself. But he knew he had to split the pizza with his friend, a little dog named Max. Tom called Max and said, "Max, I have a big pizza. Let's split it and eat it together!" Max was happy and ran to Tom's house. When they were about to eat the pizza, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew in and took the pizza away! Tom and Max were very sad. They looked at each other and said, "We need to get our pizza back!" So, they worked together to make a plan. They were not lazy anymore. They chased the big bird and got the pizza back. Tom and Max split the pizza and ate it together, happy to have their pizza back and be good friends.

One day, a happy boy named Tim went to play hockey with his friends. They all went to the park and put on their hockey shoes. Tim was very excited to play with his friends. When the game started, Tim tried to grab the ball. He said, "I want to score a goal!" His friend, Sam, also wanted to grab the ball. They both ran very fast to get it. They laughed and had a lot of fun. At the end of the game, Tim scored a goal. He was very happy. He said, "I love playing hockey with my friends!" They all smiled and agreed. They could not wait to play again.

Once there was a pig who was very stupid. He did not think before he acted. One day, he was walking through the forest when he saw a big, juicy apple. His mouth started to water. "That apple looks so good," the pig thought. Without thinking twice, the pig took a big bite. But the apple turned out to be a trap! A trap set by a little boy waiting nearby. "Haha!" the boy laughed. "You bit my apple!" The pig was very embarrassed. He tried to explain, but all he said was, "I'm sorry, I was stupid." The boy shook his head and said, "You should always think before you bite!" Then he smiled and picked up his apple. They both went on their way.
