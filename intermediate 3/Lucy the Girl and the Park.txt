Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was troubled because she had no friends. She wanted to play and have fun, but no one wanted to play with her. One day, Lucy went to the park and saw a group of kids doing yoga. They were bending and stretching, and they looked like they were having a lot of fun. Lucy wanted to join them, so she walked over and asked if she could play too. The kids said yes, and they all started to do yoga together. As they did yoga, they began to unite and become friends. Lucy was not troubled anymore because she had found new friends to play with. They all had fun doing yoga, and they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to play in the water. One day, he went to the pond and saw a new thing. It was a long and thin thing called tape. The tape was on the ground, near the pond. Ducky was curious and wanted to play with the tape. He walked up to it and said, "Hello, tape! Can you play with me?" The tape didn't talk, but Ducky didn't mind. He picked up the tape with his beak and carried it to the pond. Ducky jumped into the water and started to splash. He splashed water on the tape and made it wet. Suddenly, a big, noisy truck came to the pond. The truck driver saw Ducky and the tape. He said, "Hey, Ducky! That tape should not be in the water. It can make the pond dirty." Ducky listened and said, "Oh, I didn't know. I'm sorry." The truck driver smiled and took the tape away. Ducky continued to splash and play in the clean water, happy that he had learned something new.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red bird. The bird had a hurt wing. A kind girl named Lily wanted to help the bird. She was a generous girl who always shared her toys with her friends. Lily knew she could heal the bird's wing. One day, when Lily was walking in the forest, she found a shiny jewel. It was a magic jewel that could fix things. Lily took the jewel and went to the big, red bird. She touched the bird's hurt wing with the jewel, and the wing started to heal. The big, red bird was very happy. It could fly again! The bird thanked Lily and gave her a hug. Lily felt good because she helped her new friend. She knew that being generous and kind was the best way to be.

One day, a girl named Sally went to her friend Tom's house. They wanted to play with their toys. Sally had a big, round ball and Tom had a small, fast car. They were happy to play together because they were reliable friends. While they were playing, Sally noticed a big frame on the wall. It had a picture of a funny cat in it. She pointed at the frame and said, "Look, Tom! A funny cat!" Tom looked at the frame and laughed. They both liked the funny cat. Suddenly, the frame began to shake. The funny cat jumped out of the frame and into their room! Sally and Tom were very surprised. They didn't know that the cat could do that. The cat played with them and their toys. They all had a fun time together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved candy so much. He felt very lucky because his mom gave him candy every day. Tim was a happy boy. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. But his mom said, "No, Tim, you can't climb the tree. It's too high. It can prevent you from falling and getting hurt." Tim was sad but he listened to his mom. Then, something unexpected happened. A squirrel came down from the tree. The squirrel gave Tim a piece of candy. Tim was very surprised and happy. He shared the candy with his mom. They both felt lucky and had a great day at the park.
