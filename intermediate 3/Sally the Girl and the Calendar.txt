Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Calendar

One day, a little girl named Sally found a calendar on the floor. She looked at the pretty pictures and sighed. She wanted to be popular like the people in the pictures. Sally went to her friend Tom and said, "Look at this calendar! I want to be popular like them." Tom looked at the calendar and sighed too. He wanted to be popular as well. They decided to do what the popular people in the calendar did. They tried to wear the same clothes and do the same things. But they didn't feel happy. They only felt sad. In the end, Sally and Tom were not popular. They were still sad and sighed a lot. The calendar did not make them happy.

One day, a little squirrel was in the park. He saw a big tree with lots of nuts. The squirrel was very fast. He ran up the tree to get the nuts. "Hello, squirrel!" said a bird on a branch. "Do you want to play with me?" The squirrel wanted to play, but he also wanted the nuts. So he said, "I will play with you after I remove all the nuts from the tree." The squirrel was very fast at removing the nuts. He put them in a pile on the ground. But when he came back to the tree, the nuts were gone! "Oh no!" cried the squirrel. "Where are my nuts?" The bird laughed and said, "I hid your nuts while you were up in the tree! I wanted to play a game with you." The squirrel was surprised, but he smiled. They both played a game to find the nuts together. And they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bean. This bean was very rare. It was red and shiny. The bean lived in a big garden with many other beans. One day, the bean met a small bug. The bug was sad. The bean asked, "Why are you sad, bug?" The bug said, "I am lost. I cannot find my home." The bean wanted to help the bug. They started to look for the bug's home together. As they looked, they had to pause. They saw a big bird in the sky. The bird was looking for food. The bean and the bug hid under a leaf. The bird went away. They were safe. The bean and the bug found the bug's home. They were happy and became good friends.

Once upon a time there was a young princess. She was very kind and obedient. Everywhere she went, people smiled at her. The princess wanted very much to find her true love. She was sure she would find him if she looked hard enough. So every day she set off to explore her kingdom, searching for her prince. One day, as she was wandering near the edge of the kingdom, she saw a handsome prince in the distance. The princess was so happy she couldn't speak! She ran towards the prince, hoping he was the one she had been searching for. The prince smiled at the princess. "Hello there, Princess," he said. "Would you like to go on an adventure with me?" The princess nodded, full of hope. "Yes, I would!" she said, as she took the prince's hand. And so the princess and the prince lived happily ever after. All because the princess never gave up hope.

One sunny day, a boy named Tim went to the river with his dad. They wanted to play with their raft. They put the raft in the water and were very careful. They didn't want the raft to go away. As they played, the water started to increase. Tim's dad said, "Tim, be careful! The water is getting higher!" Tim listened to his dad and held the raft tightly. They both stayed close to the shore. Soon, the water stopped increasing. Tim and his dad were safe and happy. They learned that being careful is important. They had a fun day with their raft and went home with big smiles.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a wide path in the woods. He saw a small bulb on the ground. He picked it up and saw that it was very pretty. Tim decided to follow the wide path to see where it went. As he walked, he met a big bear. The bear looked at the bulb and said, "That is a nice bulb. Can I have it?" Tim said, "No, I found it. It is mine." The bear got angry and wanted the bulb even more. Tim ran away, but the bear followed him. Tim was scared, and he dropped the bulb. The bear picked it up and was happy. Tim was sad because he lost his pretty bulb and had to go home without it.
