Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and the Train

Sam liked to play with his toy train. He had a big track that went around his room. He made the train go fast and slow, and he made noises like a real train. He wondered how a real train worked. He wanted to see one up close. One day, his dad said they could go to the train station. Sam was very happy. He put on his coat and hat and got in the car. He saw many cars and trucks on the road, but he only looked for trains. He asked his dad many questions about trains. They got to the train station and saw a big train. It was made of iron and it had a powerful engine. It was shiny and loud. Sam's dad bought tickets and they got on the train. Sam found a window seat and looked outside. He saw trees and houses and fields go by very fast. He felt the train move and shake. He heard the whistle blow. He smiled and clapped his hands. He thought the train was the best thing ever. He was glad he wondered about trains.

Once upon a time, there was a very smart cat named Tom. Tom had a big brain and was very reliable. He always helped his friends when they needed him. Tom could lie down and think very hard to find good answers to problems. One day, Tom's best friend, a dog named Max, lost his favorite toy. Max was very sad and asked Tom for help. Tom lied down and thought hard with his big brain. He came up with a plan to find the toy. Tom and Max looked high and low for the toy. They looked in the park and by the big tree. They looked near the pond and behind the bushes. Just when they were about to give up, they found the toy in a place they never expected. It was in Max's dog house all along! They both laughed and were so happy that Tom's big brain and reliable help saved the day.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little girl named Lily. She loved to skip and play with her friends. One day, Lily's mom asked her to go to the grocery store with her. At the grocery store, Lily saw her friend Tom. Tom said, "Hi Lily, do you want to skip with me?" Lily felt shy, but she wanted to play with Tom. So, she said, "Yes, let's skip together!" They skipped up and down the grocery store aisles, laughing and having fun. When it was time to go home, Lily's mom said, "Thank you for helping me at the grocery store, Lily." Lily smiled and said, "I had fun, Mom!" From that day on, Lily was not shy at the grocery store anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious team. They went to the store together with their shopping list. When they arrived, they got some puzzled looks. "What are you all doing here?" asked the cashier. The team was surprised, so the leader stepped forward. "We're here to store some stuff," he said. The cashier shook her head. "We don't store things like that here. You need to leave." The team was sad, and the leader tried to think of a way to convince the cashier. But nothing useful came to mind. Suddenly one of the team members had an idea. "We could help you!" she said. The cashier smiled. "That's very kind of you. Maybe you could start by stocking the shelves?" The team was overjoyed and quickly set to work. When they were done, the cashier was impressed. "I'm sure glad you decided to help. Now you may store your things here." The team was relieved, and the mysterious team left with their shopping list fulfilled.

Once upon a time, there was a big garden. In the garden, there were many pretty flowers and yummy fruits. A nice man lived near the garden. He loved to raise the flowers and fruits. He thought it was easy and fun. One day, a mean rabbit came to the garden. The rabbit liked to eat the flowers and fruits. The nice man was sad. He did not want the rabbit to eat his garden. The nice man tried to stop the rabbit. But the rabbit was too fast. The rabbit ate all the flowers and fruits. The garden was gone. The nice man was very sad. The end.
