Category: Intermediate
Title: Mark the Mysterious Cat and the Dog

One day, a mysterious cat named Mark went to yoga class. He saw other animals there too. There was a dog, a bird, and a frog. They all wanted to learn yoga. Mark was excited but also a little scared. He had never done yoga before. The yoga teacher, a wise old owl, said, "Today, we will learn a new pose. It is called the 'tree pose'." Mark tried to do the tree pose, but he fell down. The dog laughed at him and said, "You can't do yoga, Mark! You're not good at it!" Mark felt sad and wanted to go home. But the bird and frog came to him and said, "Don't listen to the dog, Mark. We will help you." They practiced the tree pose together. Slowly, Mark got better at it. The next day, Mark, the bird, and the frog showed the owl their tree pose. The owl was happy and said, "Great job, Mark! You did it!" The dog was surprised and stopped laughing at Mark. They all became friends and went to yoga class together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she saw a high closet in her room. She wanted to see what was inside. Lily tried to open the closet, but it was too high for her. She felt sad. Her mommy saw her and came to comfort her. "Don't worry, Lily," said her mommy. "I will help you." Mommy opened the closet for Lily. Inside, she found a big, soft teddy bear. Lily hugged the teddy bear and felt happy. From that day on, Lily and her teddy bear played together all the time.

One day, a cat named Jazz went for a walk. Jazz was an independent cat who liked to go on adventures by herself. She walked and blinked her eyes at the bright sun. On her walk, Jazz met a big dog. The dog said, "Hi, I'm Max. Who are you?" Jazz said, "I'm Jazz. I like to walk alone." Max said, "Can I walk with you?" Jazz thought for a moment and said, "Okay, but only for a little bit." As they walked, Jazz saw a big tree. She wanted to climb it, but she was scared. Max said, "I can help you." He gave her a push and Jazz climbed the tree. When she got to the top, she saw a big surprise. There was a big party with lots of cats and dogs playing together. Jazz was happy she had Max to help her and they both had fun at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a little lamp. The lamp was sad because it could not hear. It was deaf. The lamp had many friends who encouraged it to be happy. They would say, "You are a bright light in our lives!" One day, the lamp and its friends went on a walk. They saw a big box. They did not know what was inside. They were curious. The friends encouraged the lamp to open the box. They knew the lamp was brave. The lamp opened the box. Inside was a magic hat! The lamp put on the hat. Something unexpected happened. The lamp could hear! It was not deaf anymore. The lamp and its friends were so happy. They cheered and danced. The magic hat made the lamp's dream come true.

One day, a boy named Tom got a new bicycle. He was very happy. He wanted to ride it all day. But his mom said, "Tom, you need to eat lunch first." Tom did not want to wait. He thought it was stupid to eat lunch. He wanted to ride his bicycle now. But he could not resist his mom's words. So he ate his lunch very fast. After lunch, Tom rode his bicycle. He saw his friend, Sue. Sue said, "Wow, your bicycle is cool!" They rode their bicycles together and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce tiger named Tom. Tom loved to paint. One day, he wanted to paint a picture of his home, the jungle. So, he took his paints and started to paint. While Tom was painting, he saw a little bird named Bella. Bella was sad because she lost her passport. A passport is a special book that helps you go to new places. Bella wanted to find her passport to fly and see her friends. Tom said, "Don't be sad, Bella. I will help you find your passport." They looked high and low, under the trees and in the bushes. Finally, they found Bella's passport near the river. Bella was very happy and thanked Tom. As a thank you, Bella helped Tom paint his jungle picture. They painted trees, flowers, and animals together. When they finished, the painting was very beautiful. From that day on, Tom and Bella were the best of friends. They played, painted, and had many adventures in the jungle. And they always helped each other when they needed it.
