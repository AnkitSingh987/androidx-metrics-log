Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Hairy Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Max. Max loved to dig in the yard. One sunny day, Max found a big bone while digging. He was very happy. Max saw his friend, a cat named Lucy. "Look, I found a bone!" Max said. Lucy wanted to play with the bone too, but Max did not want to share. Lucy was sad and went away. Later, Max felt bad for not sharing with Lucy. He went to find her and said, "I am sorry, Lucy. Please forgive me. Let's share the bone and play together." Lucy forgave Max, and they played happily with the bone all day long.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent worm. This worm was very smart and could do many things. One day, the worm was playing in the dirt when it found a big rock. The worm had an idea. It decided to rub the rock. As the worm rubbed the rock, something unexpected happened. The rock began to change. It became soft and warm, and it started to move! To the worm's surprise, the rock was not a rock at all. It was a baby bird! The bird had been hiding under the dirt. The worm and the baby bird became friends, and they played together every day. The intelligent worm had found a new friend by doing something very simple, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run all day. One day, Spot saw a big cat. The cat was very cool and calm. Spot wanted to be friends with the cat. Spot said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me?" The cat looked at Spot and said, "Okay, let's play. But first, I need to find some meat to eat." So, Spot and the cat went to look for some meat. Spot and the cat rushed to the store. They found some meat and shared it. After they ate, they played and had fun all day. In the end, Spot and the cat became best friends.

One day, a kind dog named Max went to the park. He saw a big tree and a small tree. Max was very happy. He wanted to play with his friends. He saw a big steak on the ground. He wanted to share it with his friends. Max had a great idea. He would add more friends to his play time. He called all his friends to the park. They all came running. They were excited to see the steak. They all wanted to eat it. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down from the sky. The bird took the steak and flew away. Max and his friends were sad. They did not get to eat the steak. But they still had fun playing together. In the end, they were happy because they were all together.

One day, Milly was feeling very anxious. She heard a strange sound coming from the phone. She ran to her mom to ask what it was. Her mom said it was just a phone call. Milly felt a little bit better and decided to go and take a bath. She gathered her favorite toys and climbed into the bathtub. The warm water felt so good on her skin. She had so much fun playing and splashing around. After a while, Milly remembered that she hadn't answered the phone. She felt very anxious again. She stepped out of the bath with her toys and ran back to her mom. She asked her mom if she could answer the phone. Her mom said yes and started drying her off with a towel while she answered the call. Milly was feeling much less anxious after taking her bath and having the chance to answer the phone. She could finally relax and enjoy her day.

John was a 3 year old who enjoyed sports. He liked to play soccer, basketball and run around the playground. One day, John's mom and dad took him to a new sport. He was very nervous. He had never done this sport before and didn't know what it would be like. John looked around and saw some older kids playing and having lots of fun. He smiled and his nervousness faded away. John took a deep breath and jumped right in. He had a lot of fun playing the new sport and couldn't wait to go back and play it again.
