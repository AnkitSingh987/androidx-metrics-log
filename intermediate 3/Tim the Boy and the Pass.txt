Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pass

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was adventurous and loved to play outside. One sunny day, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big ball. He wanted to pass the ball with someone. He looked around and saw a girl named Sue. Tim asked, "Do you want to pass the ball with me?" Sue said, "Yes, I love to pass the ball!" They passed the ball back and forth, laughing and having fun. Tim's mom watched them with a smile in her eye. Tim and Sue became good friends and played together every day.

One day, Danny saw an old blackboard outside on the sidewalk. "What's that?" he asked his mom. "It's a blackboard," she replied. "It's very cheap, so we can buy it!" Danny was so excited. He took a deep breath and sighed. Inside their house, his mom set up the blackboard, and Danny ran to it. He opened up his box of chalk and happily scribbled on the blackboard. Suddenly, his sister Emily came into the room. "What are you doing?" she asked. Danny showed her the board. "Mommy bought me a blackboard and it was so cheap!" Emily smiled. She hopped up and down, reaching for the chalk. "Let's draw together," she said. Danny smiled and passed Emily a piece of blue chalk. "Okay!" he said. For the rest of the afternoon, the two of them drew pictures and wrote words on the board. They worked until their mom said it was time for dinner. Danny and Emily smiled and hugged each other goodbye. "Let's draw on the blackboard tomorrow," said Emily. Danny sighed happily, and the two of them ran off to the kitchen.

Once upon a time, there was a red bird named Bob. Bob liked to attach things to his nest. He wanted his nest to have style. One day, Bob found a red ribbon. He thought it was pretty. He attached the red ribbon to his nest. Now, his nest had more style. Bob was happy with his red ribbon. His friends liked the style too. They all played together in the nest with the red ribbon. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a patient pirate named Pete. Pete was an old pirate who sailed around the world in his boat. One day, he sailed to a nearby island and decided to explore. He marched along the beach, looking for something exciting. All of a sudden, Pete heard a voice. "Stop! Don't move!" Pete turned around to see a small pirate standing in front of him. "Who are you?" asked the small pirate. "I'm Pete the patient pirate," replied Pete. "How can I help you?" "I'm looking for a reward," said the small pirate. "Do you have one for me?" Pete thought for a moment. Then he decided to help the brave pirate. "Yes," said Pete. "You can have my hat. It's not much, but it should make a nice reward!" The small pirate thanked Pete and then ran off with the hat. Pete smiled and then marched back to his ship. And he sailed off into the sunset, happy to have helped the brave small pirate.

Lily and Max were twins who loved dessert. They liked cake, ice cream, cookies, and candy. But their favorite dessert was pie. They liked apple pie, cherry pie, and pumpkin pie. But their most favorite pie was blueberry pie. One day, their mom made a large blueberry pie for them. She put it on the table and said, "This is for you, Lily and Max. But you have to wait until after dinner. Don't touch it until then, okay?" Lily and Max nodded, but they looked at the pie with big eyes. It smelled so good and looked so yummy. They wanted to eat it right away. After their mom left the kitchen, Lily whispered to Max, "Let's have a little bite. Just a little one. Mom won't notice." Max shook his head. "No, Lily. Mom said to wait. We have to listen to her." But Lily did not listen to Max. She took a fork and cut a small piece of the pie. She put it in her mouth and smiled. "Mmm, it's so good. Try some, Max." Max looked at the pie and saw a hole in it. He felt panic. He knew their mom would be angry. He said, "Lily, what did you do? You made a hole in the pie. Mom will see it and know we ate some. We're in trouble." Lily looked at the pie and saw the hole too. She felt panic too. She said, "Oh no, Max. You're right. What can we do?" They thought hard, but they could not think of anything. They heard their mom coming back to the kitchen. They hid under the table and hoped she would not see them or the pie. But they knew they could not hide for long. They wished they had listened to their mom and waited for dessert.
