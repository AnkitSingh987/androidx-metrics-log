Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bright Sun and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It was a happy day. A little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved to play on the swings and slide. Tim saw a big red ball on the ground. He wanted to play with it. Tim used his hands to push the ball. The ball rolled fast and far. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He ran after the ball and pushed it again. After playing for a long time, Tim felt tired. His mom said it was time for a nap. Tim laid down under a big tree. The tree's leaves made a nice shade. Tim closed his eyes and took a nap. He dreamed of playing with the big red ball again.

Tom and Lily are playing with their toy guns in the park. They like to pretend they are spies and shoot at the bad guys. They run and hide behind the trees and the swings. "Bang! Bang! I got you, Lily!" Tom says, pointing his gun at his sister. "No, you did not! You missed me!" Lily says, shooting back at him. "Yes, I did! You are lying!" Tom says, getting angry. Lily sticks out her tongue at him. "No, you are lying! You are not honest!" Tom and Lily start to argue. They do not hear their mom calling them. She is holding a big bag with a zip. She has a surprise for them. "Tom! Lily! Come here, please!" Mom says, louder. Tom and Lily stop fighting and run to their mom. They wonder what is in the bag. "Look what I have for you!" Mom says, opening the zip. She takes out two ice creams, one for each of them. "Wow! Thank you, Mom!" Tom and Lily say, smiling. They hug their mom and take their ice creams. They forget about their toy guns and their argument. They lick their ice creams and enjoy the sun. Mom is happy to see them happy. She loves her honest and sweet children.

Once upon a time, there was a funny bunny. He lived near a big mountain. The bunny liked to hop and play all day. One day, the funny bunny met a kind squirrel. The squirrel liked to serve food to his friends. He had many yummy nuts and berries. The funny bunny and the squirrel became good friends. They played together on the mountain. They laughed and had so much fun. The funny bunny and the squirrel were very happy. They were the best friends that lived on the mountain.

Once upon a time, there was a boat named Tom. Tom sailed across a great port to a far away land. Along the way he was faced with difficult storms and rough waters, but never once lost hope that he would reach his destination. Tom soon thought he was close, but he was wrong. The more he sailed, the more difficult it became to reach the place he wanted to go. He eventually became so tired, he had to tie his boat to a nearby dock and rest. Out of nowhere, a kind old man appeared. "What's wrong my boy?" He asked. Tom told the old man about his problem. The old man smiled and said, "I may have a solution for you. You see, there is a secret port nearby that not many know about. I will tell you how to get there and it will be much easier for you to reach your destination. But beware, the port has been known to rot the wood on boats." Tom thanked the old man and headed off toward the secret port. His journey was difficult, but with the old man's help, he was able to reach his destination. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to write. He wrote about everything he saw. One day, Tim went to a park with his mom. The park had a beautiful landscape. There were green trees, colorful flowers, and a big blue sky. Tim saw a modern art statue in the park. It was big and shiny. Tim wanted to write about the statue. He asked his mom, "Can I write about the statue, Mom?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. You can write about the statue." So, Tim sat on a bench with his mom. He wrote about the modern statue, the pretty landscape, and the fun day at the park. Tim was happy because he could write about all the things he saw and loved.
