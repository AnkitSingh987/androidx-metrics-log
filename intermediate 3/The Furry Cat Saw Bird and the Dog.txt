Title: The Furry Cat Saw Bird and the Dog

One day, a furry cat saw a bird in a tree. The cat wanted to play with the bird, but the bird was scared. The cat said, "Please come down, bird. I just want to play." The bird did not want to play with the cat, but it did want to be nice. So, the bird came down from the tree. The cat and the bird played together. They played hide and seek. They played tag. They played catch. The cat was happy to play with the bird. The bird was happy too, but it was also tired. The bird said, "I need to rest now, cat. Thank you for playing with me." The next day, the cat wanted to play with the bird again. The cat called out, "Bird, come play with me!" But the bird did not come. The cat felt sad. Then, the cat saw a new friend. It was a dog! The cat was scared of the dog at first. But the dog said, "Don't be scared, cat. I want to play too!" The cat, the bird, and the dog all played together. They had lots of fun. They learned that it is good to be kind and make new friends. They also learned that even if someone is different, they can still be a good friend. And they all lived happily together, playing and having fun every day.

Alison and her mom were out at the park one day. Alison saw a big tree, and she wanted to jump up and touch it. “Alison,” Mom said, “Don’t jump so high, you might get hurt.” But Alison kept jumping higher and higher until she couldn’t reach the tree. Alison was worried as she started to cry. Mom looked up, and saw a hanger up in the tree. She had an idea. Mom asked her to stand still, and she held the hanger out. Alison jumped, and managed to grab the hanger. She was so proud of herself! Afterwards Mom said, “You can jump and play, Alison, but be careful. You don’t want to get hurt.” Alison nodded and said, “OK Mom, I won’t jump so high next time.” The moral of the story is that it’s alright to play and jump, but it’s important to be careful and be aware of your surroundings.

Once upon a time there lived a gray neighbor. She lived alone in her quiet house. One day, a big storm came. It was very loud, and the neighbor didn't understand what was going on. Suddenly, the neighbor heard a banging on the window. She looked outside and saw a little 3-year old girl. The girl was scared and was pounding on the window for help. The neighbor felt sorry for her, so she opened the door and let her in. The little girl said she was lost. The neighbor tried to help her and asked her where her home was. The girl didn't understand what the neighbor was saying. The neighbor was feeling frustrated and wasn't sure what to do. Then, she had an idea. She opened up her map, and then helped the little girl recognize the pattern of her home. Once the girl saw the map, she knew right away where her house was. She waved goodbye and thanked the neighbor for her help. The neighbor felt relieved and watched the little girl go home until she reached the front door.

One day there was a brave boy who wanted to go on an adventure. He grabbed a map, then his friends all crowded around him. He smiled and said, "Let's go!" So they all rushed out in the dark to start their adventure. The night was dark, but they could see where they were going because of the map. Soon enough, they found some woods, and everybody got excited. They ran through the trees, crunching on leaves and twigs. After a while, they came to a clearing and stopped to catch their breath. A path was ahead of them, and so they rushed towards it. The path led them to a field illuminated by the bright, full moon above them. In the field, they all lay down, looking up at the stars and admiring the night sky. It was a beautiful adventure and they all felt very happy. Then they rushed back home, grateful for the map that had helped guide them on their journey.
