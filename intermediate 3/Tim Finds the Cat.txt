Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim Finds the Cat

One day, Tim and his dog, Max, went for a walk. They liked to watch cars and people on the street. Max was a reliable dog and always stayed close to Tim. They were best friends. As they walked, they saw a cat. Max wanted to chase the cat, but Tim said, "No, Max! We must be nice to the cat." Max listened to Tim, but the cat was scared and climbed a tree. The cat was stuck and couldn't get down. Tim and Max wanted to help the cat. They found a tall man who helped the cat get down from the tree. The cat was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim and Max!" The moral of the story is to always be kind to others, even if they are scared.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit who wondered what the world was like. One day he decided to go outside to have a look. As he stepped out of his house, he was in for a shock! There was a big tree in the garden and around it, there was rainbow coloured grass! He had never seen anything like it before! It was so unusual. The rabbit was very excited. He hopped around the garden, exploring the rainbow grass with his friends. Every corner he turned, there was something new and unexpected. He couldn't believe it! The rabbit grew curious and he wanted to find out why the grass was this way. He asked his mum if she knew why it looked so colourful. She explained that it was because of the rain and the sunshine that come together to make the grass this way. The rabbit couldn't believe it and felt so amazed. He thanked his mum and jumped back into the garden to explore some more. He continued to wonder what else he could find in the garden. The world was a truly incredible place!

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went for a walk. Tim loved his dog very much. They saw a big, thick blue ball in the park. Tim said, "Look, Max! A big blue ball! Let's play with it!" Tim kicked the ball and Max ran after it. They played and played until they were tired. As they sat down to rest, a girl named Sue came up to them. She said, "Hi, I am Sue. That is my ball. I love that ball too!" Just then, the ball started to move on its own! Tim, Max, and Sue were surprised. They watched as the ball grew bigger and bigger. It was not a ball, but a big blue balloon! The wind blew the balloon away, and they all laughed. In the end, Tim, Max, and Sue became good friends. They played together every day, and they all loved the big blue balloon that brought them together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big parade. Many people came to see it. They were very excited. In the parade, there was an ancient car. It was very old but still worked. A little boy named Tim saw the car and thought it was cool. Tim wanted to be in the parade too. He had a little red bike. He thought it was fast. So, he decided to join the parade. He said to his mom, "Mom, I want to be in the parade with my bike!" His mom said, "Okay, but be careful and don't rush." The parade started, and Tim was very happy. He was riding his bike with the other people. But Tim wanted to be in front of the parade. He thought that if he rushed, he could be the first one. So, he started to rush. He went very fast, but then he fell off his bike. He was sad and hurt. A kind man in the ancient car saw Tim and stopped. He helped Tim up and said, "Little boy, remember not to rush. It's not good to be too fast." Tim learned his lesson. He knew that rushing is not always good. The moral of the story is that we should not rush in life. It's better to be slow and steady.
