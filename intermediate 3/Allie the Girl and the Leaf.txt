Category: Intermediate
Title: Allie the Girl and the Leaf

Once there was a little girl named Allie. One day, she went for a walk in the forest. As she was walking, she noticed a big leaf, lying on the ground. She walked closer to it and found something hidden underneath the leaf. Allie was very curious, so she picked up the leaf to see what it was. Allie was surprised to see that it was an ant! It had two big eyes and it was blinking at her. Allie was scared, so she quickly put the leaf back. As soon as she did, the ant ran away and hid again. Allie was relieved and she started to walk away, happy that the ant was not hurt. Suddenly, she saw the ant again! This time it was standing on a tree branch, watching her. Allie smiled and waved at it. The ant started to walk away, but it kept blinking at Allie. Allie smiled back, waved goodbye and then ran home.

One day, a strong ant named Tim went on a walk. He wanted to provide food for his family. Tim saw a big cookie and said, "This is a good start!" As Tim tried to lift the cookie, a friendly bird named Sue came by. "Can I help you?" asked Sue. Tim said, "Yes, please!" So, Sue helped Tim carry the cookie. But then, something unexpected happened! The cookie broke into many pieces. Tim and Sue were sad. But they saw other little bugs come to eat the cookie pieces. Tim and Sue smiled. They made new friends and still provided food for their families.

One day, a little bird with a smooth wing went to the park. The bird saw a big ball. The bird wanted to play with the ball. The bird said, "Ball, let's bounce and have fun!" The ball did not say anything. The bird pushed the ball with its smooth wing. The ball started to bounce. The bird was very happy. They bounced and played all day long. The bird said, "Ball, you are my best friend." Then, something unexpected happened. The ball hit a rock and popped! The bird was very sad. But, a little girl came and said, "Don't be sad, bird. I can fix your ball." The little girl put a band-aid on the ball. The ball could bounce again! The bird and the ball were so happy. They played together every day after that.

Once there was a puppy named Max. Every day Max played in the park. He would run, roll and jump. One day, Max was in the park with his best friend, a little girl named Allie. She was holding a leash. Allie had a big smile on her face, she was so happy. Max said to Allie: “What is that?” Allie replied: “It’s a leash. It’s so I can keep you close by when we go for a walk. Do you want to go?” Max sighed in delight. He was so excited. Allie put the leash on Max and they went out for a walk. Max ran with Allie, happy and free. They went to meet Allie’s mom, they laughed and they all hugged. Max and Allie had a wonderful day. They all snuggled together and sighed contentedly as the sun set.

Once upon a time, in a tidy kitchen, there was a big bowl of pasta. The pasta was very yummy and colorful. The pasta was waiting to be eaten by a happy family. One day, a little girl named Lily came into the kitchen. She saw the pasta and thought it looked so good. She wanted to eat it, but she did not know if it belonged to her. Lily asked her mommy, "Does this pasta belong to me?" Her mommy smiled and said, "Yes, the pasta belongs to you and your brother Tom." Lily was so happy, and she shared the pasta with Tom. They both enjoyed the tasty pasta and had a great time. From that day on, they always shared their food and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a sheep named Woolly. Woolly had lots of wool that needed a wash. One day, Woolly met a kind duck named Quacky. Quacky said, "I can help you wash your wool, Woolly!" As they walked to the pond, Woolly's wool felt tight. They got to the pond and Quacky helped wash Woolly's wool. They splashed and played in the water. Woolly's wool got clean and soft. But then, something unexpected happened. Woolly's wool started to change colors! It turned red, blue, and green. Woolly and Quacky looked at each other and laughed. They had so much fun that day, and Woolly's wool stayed colorful forever.
