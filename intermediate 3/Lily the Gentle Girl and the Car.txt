Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Gentle Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gentle girl named Lily. She loved to help her friends and family. One day, Lily had a dream that she could fix anything that was broken. The next day, Lily's friend Tim came to her with a broken toy car. Lily remembered her dream and thought she could fix it. She tried very hard, and to her surprise, the toy car was fixed! Tim was so happy, and Lily felt good about helping her friend. As the days went by, Lily helped many people in the town by fixing their broken things. But one day, she tried to fix a toy that was too hard for her. She felt sad and thought she had lost her special power. Then, her mom told her that it's okay to ask for help sometimes. Lily learned that even if you can't fix everything, it's important to try your best and ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Ben. Ben loved bananas. One day, he went to the store to buy some bananas. He saw a big bunch of bananas that were just right. Ben was very happy. "Hello, Mr. Store Man," said Ben. "I want to buy these bananas, please." "Sure, Ben," said Mr. Store Man. "But I must tell you, these bananas can shrink things. You must be careful with them." Ben took the bananas home. He wanted to see if they could really shrink things. He put a banana next to his big chair. Suddenly, the chair began to shrink! It became so small that Ben could not sit on it anymore. "Oh no!" said Ben. "I must be careful with these bananas." From that day on, Ben was very careful with his magic bananas. He only used them to shrink things that were too big. And he always remembered to share his bananas with his friends.

One day, a girl named Lily and her mom went to a restaurant. They wanted to eat some food. The restaurant had a deep pool near it. Lily liked to look at the pool. At the restaurant, Lily's mom said, "Lily, stay close to me." Lily said, "Okay, mom." They ate their food and had a good time. But then, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the deep pool! It went into the restaurant. Everyone was surprised. The fish said, "I am hungry too!" So, Lily and her mom shared their food with the fish. They all became friends and had a fun day at the restaurant.

Once upon a time, there was a little chicken named Chirpy. Chirpy was an ordinary chicken who lived on a farm. He had many friends, but he was bored. He wanted to do something new and fun. One day, Chirpy saw a big tree near the farm. He thought it would be fun to climb the tree. So, he told his friends about his plan. They all thought it was a good idea and wanted to see Chirpy climb the tree. Chirpy was excited, but a little scared too. The next day, Chirpy was ready to climb the tree. All his friends gathered around to watch. Chirpy took a deep breath and started to climb. He climbed higher and higher. When he reached the top, he looked down and saw all his friends cheering for him. Chirpy was so happy that he did something new and fun. From that day on, Chirpy was not just an ordinary chicken anymore.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big slide. Lila wants to go on the slide, but Ben is scared. "Come on, Ben, it is fun!" Lila says. "You are strong, you can do it!" Ben shakes his head. "No, no, no, it is too high. I do not like it." Lila thinks for a moment. She has an idea. She runs to her mom and asks her to bring a thing. Her mom gives her a soft toy. It is a bear. Lila goes back to Ben. She shows him the bear. "Look, Ben, this is my bear. His name is Bobo. He likes to go on the slide. He is not scared. He is strong, like you. Do you want to hold him?" Ben looks at the bear. He likes bears. He nods. He takes the bear from Lila. He feels a little better. "OK, Lila, I will try. But you go first. I will follow you." Lila smiles. She climbs up the slide. She sits down and slides down. She laughs. She waits for Ben. Ben holds the bear tight. He climbs up the slide. He sits down and slides down. He is a little scared, but he does it. He laughs too. He is happy. "Wow, Ben, you did it! You are strong!" Lila says. "Thank you, Lila. And thank you, Bobo. You helped me. You are strong too!" Ben says. They hug each other and the bear. They play on the slide again and again. They have fun. They are friends.
