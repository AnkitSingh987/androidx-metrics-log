Title: Tom and Mia and the Hill

Tom and Mia were playing in the garden. They liked to look at the bugs and flowers. They saw a big hill of dirt with many ants. Tom wanted to see the ants up close. He picked up a stick and poked the hill. "Tom, don't do that!" Mia said. "The ants will be angry. They can bite you. It is dangerous." But Tom did not listen. He poked the hill harder. He made a big hole. The ants came out of the hole. They were very angry. They ran towards Tom. They bit his hand and his leg. Tom cried out loud. He dropped the stick and ran away. Mia ran after him. She helped him wash his bites. She put a band-aid on his hand and his leg. She hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Tom. The ants hurt you. You should not poke their home. They live there. They do not like it when you part their home." Tom nodded. He said, "I'm sorry, Mia. You were right. The ants are dangerous. I will not poke their home again. I will be nice to the ants. And to you."

Once upon a time, in a jolly little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom and dad. One day, Tim's dad asked him to load the car with boxes. Tim was very excited to help his dad. As Tim was loading the boxes, he saw his friend, Sam. Sam asked, "What are you doing, Tim?" Tim replied, "I am helping my dad load the car with boxes. We are going to give them to people who need them." Sam thought that was a great idea and wanted to help too. Tim and Sam worked together to load the car. When they were done, they had to wait for Tim's dad to come and drive them. While they waited, they talked about how good it felt to help others. In the end, Tim and Sam learned that helping others made them feel happy and proud. And that was the moral of the story: helping others is a good thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a modest theater in a small town. The theater had a magic show for kids. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the theater with his mom. Tim was very excited to see the magic show. During the show, the magician said, "Now, I will make something disappear!" He took a big red ball and covered it with a cloth. Tim watched closely. The magician said, "One, two, three!" and the red ball was gone. Tim clapped his hands and laughed. After the show, Tim asked his mom, "Mom, how did the ball disappear?" His mom smiled and said, "It's magic, Tim. The magician knows how to make things disappear!" Tim was very happy and couldn't wait to tell his friends about the magic show at the modest theater.

Once upon a time, there was a tired island. It had been loved, but recently neglected and it looked a bit sad. One day, two friends Sam and Martha were out walking together. Sam looked sad. "Why are you so tired Sam?" asked Martha, "What's wrong?" Sam sighed and said, "I think someone might be spoiling the island. I want to go and help it!" Martha thought for a moment then replied, "Let's go! That sounds like a great plan!" and so off they went. When they arrived at the island, it was much worse than they expected. The island was covered in rubbish and weeds. But Sam and Martha weren't giving up! They started to work hard, picking up the rubbish and clearing the weeds. It took a few days hard work, but slowly and surely the island started to look better. Finally, after a few weeks of hard work, the island was restored to its former glory. Sam and Martha were both very tired, but they were also very proud of their work. The End.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl named Sarah. She was three years old and very curious. One day, Sarah noticed a loud insect buzzing around her house. She wanted to solve the problem and get rid of the insect. “Oh no!” she said. “Mommy, help me!” Mommy smiled and said, “I know how to solve this Sarah. Don’t worry.” Mommy took Sarah outside and the two of them found a big stick. With one swift movement, Mommy slammed the stick on the ground and the loud insect was gone. Sarah was very happy. She had solved the problem on her own. She looked up and smiled at her mom. “Thanks Mommy!”
