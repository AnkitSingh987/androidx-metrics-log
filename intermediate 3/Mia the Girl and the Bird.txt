Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play at the park. Her favorite thing to do was to go on the slide. One sunny day, Mia went to the park with her mommy. While playing, Mia saw a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird was patient and let Mia and her mommy help. Mia's mommy said, "We will help the bird heal." They took the bird home and gave it food and water. After a few days, the bird's wing healed. Mia and her mommy took the bird back to the park. They said goodbye to the bird and watched it fly away. Mia felt happy as she played on the slide, knowing she and her mommy had helped their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had a big horse. The man loved his horse very much. One day, he wanted to give his horse a gift. So, he went to find a surprise for his horse. The wealthy man found a big, soft blanket. He thought his horse would love it. The man took the blanket to his horse. He wanted to wrap it around the horse to keep it warm. The horse saw the blanket and was very happy. But, as the man started to wrap the blanket around the horse, something unexpected happened. The horse gave a big sneeze! The sneeze was so big that it blew the blanket away. The man and the horse looked at each other and laughed. They knew they would remember this funny surprise for a long time.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful house. In the house, there was a big bathroom. The bathroom had a big tub and a small sink. The walls were blue, and the floor was white. Everyone loved the beautiful bathroom. One day, a little boy named Tim went into the bathroom to wash his hands. He turned on the water and got his hands all wet. Then, he put soap on his hands and rubbed them together. He made lots of bubbles! Tim loved the bubbles and laughed. When Tim was done, he turned off the water and dried his hands. He looked around the beautiful bathroom and smiled. It was time to go play outside with his friends. Tim left the bathroom and ran to the door. The fun in the bathroom had come to an end.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Spot. Spot had a flea friend named Tiny. They lived in a big yard with green grass and pretty flowers. Spot and Tiny would play all day and enjoy the sun. One day, Spot felt sad. He missed his other dog friends who lived far away. Tiny saw that Spot was sad and wanted to help. Tiny had an idea to make Spot happy again. Tiny jumped on Spot's back and tickled him. This made Spot laugh and feel better. Spot and Tiny played more games, like catch and hide-and-seek. They enjoyed their time together, and Spot was not sad anymore. They were the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was feeling dull one day because it was raining outside. She could not go out and play with her friends. So, Mia decided to help her mom in the kitchen. Mia's mom was making soup for lunch. She asked Mia, "Can you help me stir the soup?" Mia was happy to help. She took a big spoon and managed to stir the soup very well. Mia's mom was proud of her. Just then, Mia's dad came into the kitchen. "Wow, this soup smells good!" he said. Mia smiled and told her dad, "I helped mom make the soup!" Her dad gave her a big hug and they all sat down to enjoy the yummy soup together. The rain outside did not seem so dull anymore.

Mia was a 3 year old girl and she loved the outdoors. One day she asked her mom, "Can I go outside?" Her mom said, "Sure, just make sure you stay in the backyard". So Mia went outside and she saw a big gray landscape. The ground was gray and there were no bright colors. Mia sat down and started playing in the dirt. She noticed a big gray rock lying in the grass. Mia ran up to the rock and said, "Mommy, look! I found a rock!" Her mom laughed and said, "That's wonderful!". Mia laid down next to the rock, put her head on it and said, "This is like my own little bed". Her mom smiled and said, "That's cute, sweetheart!" Mia felt so happy lying there next to the rock that she fell asleep! When she woke up, the sun was starting to set. Mia and her mom went inside together. Mia had such a wonderful time exploring the gray landscape in her backyard.
