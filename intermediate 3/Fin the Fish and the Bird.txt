Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin liked to swim and play with his friends. They all lived in a big pond. Fin was always alert and watched out for danger. One day, a big bird saw the fish. The bird was hungry and wanted to eat them. The bird said, "I will grab you, little fish, and eat you up!" Fin heard the bird and told his friends, "Swim away, friends! There is a big bird who wants to grab us!" Fin and his friends swam fast and hid under the rocks. The bird could not find them. The bird was sad and flew away. The fish were safe because Fin was alert. The moral of the story is: always be alert and watch out for your friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a warm mineral while he was playing in the sand. He didn't know what it was, but he thought it was very special. He showed it to his friend, Sally. "Look, Sally! I found a warm mineral!" said Tim. "Let's clean it and see what happens!" Sally and Tim cleaned the warm mineral with water and a cloth. They were very excited to see what would happen next. But as they cleaned it, the warm mineral started to get very hot. "Ouch! It's too hot!" cried Sally, dropping the mineral on the ground. The warm mineral got hotter and hotter until it was too hot to touch. Tim and Sally were sad because they could not play with their special find anymore. The warm mineral had turned into a hot rock that was no fun to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little ball named Bounce. Bounce loved to jump up and down all day long. He lived in a big room with many books. One day, he saw a big book called a "novel" and thought it looked fun to jump on. Bounce went up to the novel and started to jump on it. The novel was very tough, so Bounce jumped higher and higher. He was having so much fun! Bounce did not know that the novel had a secret inside. The secret was that it could make things come to life. After a while, Bounce jumped so high that he touched the secret part of the novel. Suddenly, all the books in the room came to life! The books started to dance and play with Bounce. They all had a big party and played together all day long. In the end, Bounce and the books became best friends. They would play together every day and have lots of fun. Bounce was very happy that he found the secret of the novel. And from that day on, the room was always filled with joy and laughter.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big swing. They want to swing on it. "Can I swing first?" Anna asks. "No, I want to swing first!" Ben says. They both run to the swing. They push and pull. They are not happy. A lady sees them. She is attractive. She has a nice dress and a big hat. She smiles at them. "Hello, children. Why are you fighting?" she asks. "We both want to swing," Anna says. "Swinging is fun, but sharing is better," the lady says. "Why don't you take turns? You can swing for a little while, and then let the other one swing. That way, you can both enjoy the swing and be friends." Anna and Ben think about it. They nod. "OK, we can share," Ben says. "Thank you, lady," Anna says. The lady helps them get on the swing. She pushes them gently. They feel the wind and the sun. They laugh and sing. They are happy. They swing and share.

Once upon a time, in a big yellow house, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily had a soft, warm blanket that she loved very much. She took her blanket everywhere she went. One day, Lily and her blanket went to the park. They saw a big tree with yellow leaves. Lily thought it would be fun to play hide and seek with her blanket. She hid behind the tree, and her blanket hid under a bush. Lily started to count, "One, two, three!" Then she shouted, "Ready or not, here I come!" She looked around and saw something peek out from under the bush. "I see you, blanket!" Lily laughed as she picked up her blanket. "Let's play again," said Lily. This time, the blanket hid behind the tree, and Lily hid under the bush. They played hide and seek all day, laughing and having fun. And when it was time to go home, Lily and her blanket walked back to the big yellow house, hand in hand.
