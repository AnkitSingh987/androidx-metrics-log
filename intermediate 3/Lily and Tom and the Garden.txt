Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Garden

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to play in the garden. One day, they saw a shiny crystal hanging from a tree. It was a gift from their grandma, who loved them very much. Lily and Tom wanted to touch the crystal, but their mom said no. She said the crystal was very special and fragile, and they could only look at it from far away. She said they could enjoy the crystal by seeing how it sparkled in the sun. But Lily and Tom were naughty. They waited until their mom was busy in the kitchen, and then they ran to the tree. They climbed on a stool and reached for the crystal. They wanted to feel how smooth and cold it was. But as they pulled the crystal, they broke the string that held it. The crystal fell to the ground and shattered into many pieces. Lily and Tom were scared and sad. They knew they had done something wrong. Their mom heard the noise and came to the garden. She saw the broken crystal and the twins' guilty faces. She was very angry and disappointed. She said they had disobeyed her and ruined their grandma's gift. She said they had to go to their room and think about what they had done. Lily and Tom felt very sorry. They wished they had listened to their mom and enjoyed the crystal from far away. They learned that being naughty can have bad consequences, and that they should respect their mom and their grandma. They hoped they could find a way to make up for their mistake.

Once upon a time, there was a nice teacher. She had a big blackboard in her classroom. Every day, the teacher wrote on the blackboard for the kids to see and learn. One day, the teacher found a big fruit. She wanted to share it with the kids. So, she took a knife and split the fruit into many parts. The kids were happy to eat the fruit. But, something unexpected happened. When the teacher split the fruit, a little bird came out. The kids were surprised and laughed. The little bird flew around the room and then went outside. The kids clapped and cheered. They had a fun day with the nice teacher and the little bird.

Lily liked to go to the park with her mom. She liked to see the ducks and the swans in the pond. She liked to feed them bread and watch them swim. One day, she saw a blue swan in the pond. She had never seen a blue swan before. She thought it was very pretty and special. She wanted to touch it and examine it. She asked her mom if she could go closer to the blue swan. Her mom said no, it was too far and too dangerous. She said the blue swan might bite or scratch her. She said Lily should stay with her and enjoy the other swans. But Lily did not listen to her mom. She waited until her mom was talking to another mom. Then she ran to the edge of the pond. She reached out her hand to the blue swan. But the blue swan was not a real swan. It was a plastic swan that someone had painted blue and left in the pond. It had a sharp metal wire sticking out of its neck. When Lily touched it, the wire cut her hand. She screamed and cried. Blood dripped from her hand. Her mom heard her scream and ran to her. She saw the blue swan and the wire and the blood. She was very angry and scared. She picked up Lily and wrapped her hand with a cloth. She took her to the car and drove to the hospital. Lily learned a hard lesson that day. She learned to listen to her mom and not to touch things that she did not know. She learned that not all swans were nice and that blue swans were not real. She learned that some people were mean and did bad things. She was very sad and sorry.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was adventurous and loved to play outside. One day, she wanted to dress up like a princess and have a tea party in her garden. Sue's big brother, Tom, was sitting on a seat, reading a book. Sue asked, "Tom, can you help me dress up like a princess?" Tom didn't want to stop reading, but he saw Sue's sad face and said, "Okay, let's do it." They found a pretty dress and a shiny crown. But when they went to the garden, it started to rain. Sue was sad, and Tom felt bad. So, they moved the tea party inside. They put a seat for Sue, a seat for Tom, and a seat for their teddy bear. They all had a great time at the tea party, and Sue was happy again.
