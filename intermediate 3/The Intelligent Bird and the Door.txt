Category: Intermediate
Title: The Intelligent Bird and the Door

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent bird who lived in a small cage. The bird loved to sing and talk to its friend, the mailman. Every day, the mailman would bring letters to the bird's house and talk to the bird. One day, the mailman did not come. The bird was sad and did not sing. But then, the bird had an idea! It opened the cage door and flew away to find the mailman. The bird flew high and low, looking for its friend. The bird found the mailman at another house. The mailman was surprised to see the bird. He said, "I thought you were in your cage!" The bird told the mailman that it missed him and wanted to help deliver the mail. The mailman agreed, and together, they delivered the mail to all the houses. The bird was happy, and from that day on, it was free to fly and help the mailman every day.

One day, a small red truck was driving down the road. The truck liked to help people. It saw a little boy with a ball. The ball was stuck in a tree. The boy was sad. The small truck said, "I can help you get your ball." The boy was happy. The truck pushed the tree with its strong wheels. The ball fell down. The boy picked it up and said, "Thank you, truck!" The boy was so happy that he started to clap. The small truck was happy too. They played with the ball together. The boy and the truck became good friends. They had a fun day.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Mark. Mark was very eager to see the big parade in his village. He had never seen a parade before, so he was excited to see all the sights. On the day of the parade, Mark woke up early and ran to his mom. "Mom, can we go to the parade now?" he asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mark, let's go see the sights!" They walked to the main street where the parade would pass. As they waited, Mark saw many sights. He saw big balloons, people in funny hats, and even a dog with a painted face. When the parade started, Mark clapped and danced with joy. He was so happy to finally see the parade with his mom, and they had a great day together.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a hose in the yard. It was a big, long hose. Tim wanted to play with the hose. He asked his mom, "Mom, can I please play with the hose?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. But be careful and don't make a big mess." Tim was very happy. He turned on the water and the hose was open. Water came out fast. He played and played. He made a big puddle in the yard. Tim saw something in the puddle. It was a frog! The frog jumped out and said, "Thank you, Tim, for making me a new home!" Tim was very surprised. He did not know frogs could talk. The frog said, "Please, can I stay in your yard?" Tim said, "Yes, you can stay. But you must be nice to my mom and dad." The frog agreed and they became good friends. And that is how Tim met his new friend, the talking frog.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a pale cat sitting on the grass. Tim wanted to pet the cat, but the cat did not want to stay. So, Tim sat down on his knee and waited. After a while, the cat came to Tim. It sat on his knee and let him pet it. Tim was very happy. They played together all day long. But then, something strange happened. The pale cat started to grow! It became very big and turned into a big, friendly dog. Tim was surprised but happy to have a new friend. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

One day, a little bird named Tim was feeling adventurous. He wanted to find a big oak tree to build his nest. Tim flew around and saw a very big oak tree. He was happy and started to lift sticks to build his nest. As Tim was lifting sticks, he saw a squirrel named Sam. Sam was also building a nest in the big oak tree. Tim and Sam became friends and helped each other lift sticks for their nests. They were excited to live in the big oak tree together. But one day, something unexpected happened. The big oak tree started to shake and lift up from the ground. It was not an oak tree, but a big green giant! Tim and Sam were scared at first, but then the giant smiled and said, "I am a friendly giant, and I will take you on an adventure!" Tim, Sam, and the giant became best friends and went on many adventures together.
