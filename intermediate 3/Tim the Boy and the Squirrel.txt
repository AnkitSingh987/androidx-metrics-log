Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was troubled because he lost his boot. He looked everywhere for it, but he could not find it. Tim was very sad. One day, Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree and thought his boot might be there. He started to strike the tree with a stick. "Boot, come out!" he said. But the boot did not come out. Then, a kind squirrel came to Tim. "Why are you troubled?" the squirrel asked. Tim told the squirrel about his lost boot. The squirrel said, "I saw a boot near the slide. Let's go look there!" Tim and the squirrel went to the slide, and there was Tim's boot! Tim was so happy, and he thanked the kind squirrel. They played together in the park all day.

One day, a boy named Tom found a video. He was very proud of his video. Tom wanted to show his video to his best friend, Sam. So, he ran to Sam's house to share his new treasure. "Look, Sam! I found a video!" Tom said. Sam was excited to see the video too. They went inside Sam's house and put the video in the player. As they watched the video, they saw a big monster that could destroy things. The monster on the video was big and strong. It destroyed a toy car and a toy house. Tom and Sam were scared, but they also thought the monster was cool. They wished they could be as strong as the monster. After the video was over, Tom and Sam went outside to play. They pretended to be big, strong monsters. They tried to destroy toy cars and toy houses, but they were not as strong as the monster in the video. They laughed and had fun pretending to be monsters, and they were proud of their new game.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and eat lollipops. One day, she heard a loud noise outside her house. Lily was scared, but she wanted to see what made the noise. Lily went outside and saw a big, angry dog. The dog had her lollipop in its mouth. Lily frowned because she was sad and scared. She wanted her lollipop back, but she was too scared to go near the big, angry dog. Lily called her mom to help her. Her mom tried to get the lollipop back, but the angry dog wouldn't let go. Lily's mom told her they couldn't get the lollipop back. Lily was very sad because she lost her lollipop and the big, angry dog took it away.

Once upon a time, there was a funny little fish named Fin. Fin loved to play pretend with his friends in the sea. One day, he found a shiny scale on the ocean floor. He was very excited and wanted to show it to his friends. Fin swam to his friend, Sally the seahorse, and said, "Look, Sally! I found a pretty scale. Let's play pretend with it. I will be a king, and you can be a queen." Sally smiled and agreed to play with Fin. They played pretend all day long, having lots of fun. They swam around the sea, telling all their friends about their new game. All the other sea animals laughed and joined in, too. At the end of the day, Fin and Sally were happy and tired. They knew they had the best day ever, playing pretend with their shiny scale.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. They sat on a bench near a big tree. Amy had a book she wanted to read. She had not read it yet. While Amy was reading, a boy named Tim came to the park too. He was very rude. He took Amy's book and ran away with it. Amy was sad and told her mom what happened. Amy's mom talked to Tim's mom about the rude boy. Tim's mom made him say sorry and give the book back. Amy could read her book now. She was happy and they all played together at the park.
