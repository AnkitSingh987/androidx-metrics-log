Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob and the Box

One sunny day, a happy bunny named Bob went for a hop. He liked to hop on Tuesdays. As he hopped, he saw his friend, the cat named Tom. "Hi Tom!" said Bob. "Do you want to hop with me on this Tuesday?" Tom said, "Yes, I want to hop too!" So, they hopped together. As they hopped, they saw a big box. They did not know what was in it. They opened the box, and balloons came out! They were so surprised! Now, they had a fun balloon party on their Tuesday hop.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was restless. He wanted to go on a big adventure. He said to his mom, "I want to find something new!" One day, Tim found an egg. The egg was big and pretty. Tim felt happy to find the egg. He told his mom, "Look what I found, Mom!" His mom smiled and said, "That's a nice egg, Tim." Tim and his mom took care of the egg. They kept it warm and safe. Soon, the egg started to crack. A cute baby bird came out. Tim felt excited to have a new friend. They played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn rat named Tom. Tom liked to run, jump, and play all day long. But he never wanted to relax. One day, Tom's mom said, "Tom, you need to relax for a little bit." Tom did not listen. He kept running and jumping around. Tom got very tired. His legs hurt, and he was sleepy. Finally, Tom listened to his mom. He laid down and began to relax. Tom felt much better after he relaxed. From that day on, he knew it was important to relax sometimes.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Tom. Tom liked to walk around the park every day. One day, Tom saw a small cat named Tim. Tim looked sad because he had a task to do. He had to find his toy, but he could not do it alone. Tom wanted to help Tim with his task. Tom asked, "Do you want help to find your toy?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" So, they both walked around the park to look for the toy. They looked under trees, behind bushes, and in the grass. Tom and Tim were very good at working together. Finally, they found the toy near the pond. There was a foolish duck sitting on it. The duck thought the toy was an egg! Tom and Tim laughed, and the duck realized its mistake. The duck said, "I was foolish, but now I know." Tom and Tim learned that working together made their task easier. They became good friends and walked together in the park every day. The moral of the story is to help others and not be foolish like the duck.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Molly. She loved playing outside in the garden. On this day she went out to the garden and saw a big leaf. Molly was so excited and she shouted out "I want that leaf!" She ran over to the leaf and picked it up. She blew on it and saw that it flew away. Molly laughed so much and giggled and ran after the leaf. While she was running, she noticed the garden was very messy. There were leaves everywhere and the grass was all over the path. Molly screamed with joy and started to jump and hop around. She laughed and laughed and couldn't stop. Just then a little bird came down from a tree and flew away with the leaf. Molly asked the bird "Where is my leaf?" The bird just smiled and flew away again. Molly was so sad and started to cry with tears in her eyes. Then the bird came back with the leaf in its mouth and gave it to Molly. Molly was so happy and laughed and thanked the bird. She hugged the leaf and said goodbye before running back inside to show her mum her leaf.

Once upon a time, in a faraway nation, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to discover new things. One day, he found a big, useful stick. He was so happy and wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. Tim ran to Sam's house and said, "Look, Sam! I found a big, useful stick!" Sam looked at the stick and said, "Wow, Tim! That is a great stick! What can we do with it?" Tim and Sam thought and thought. Then, they decided to use the stick to help others. They used the stick to pick up trash, reach things that were high up, and help their friends. Everyone in the nation was happy because of the useful stick. In the end, Tim and Sam learned that it's good to discover new things and use them to help others. The moral of the story is that when you find something useful, you can share it with others and make everyone happy.
