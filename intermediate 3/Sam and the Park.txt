Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and the Park

Sam had the best day. He was playing in the park and organizing a game with his friends. Suddenly, he noticed something off in the distance. It was a reef! Sam was so excited he couldn't keep still. He was jumping up and down, spinning in circles and feeling a bit dizzy. Sam's friends saw his excitement and asked, "What is it, Sam?" Sam replied, "It's a reef. Let's go see it!" The friends felt just as excited as Sam and they all ran towards the reef. When they arrived, they were amazed at how beautiful it was. There were brightly colored fish and lots of coral. They played for hours and had an amazing time. When it was time to leave, Sam and his friends had to organize their things. Sam said, "We have to be sure to clean up to leave the reef just like we found it." The friends all agreed and, as they walked away, they looked back one more time to take in the beauty and mystery of the reef.

Once upon a time, there was a tiny kitten. She was a pretty little thing with bright green eyes. She was so happy to explore the world around her. One day, the kitten went outside and saw a wonderful glowing light. She hopped closer to see it. It was so warm and comforting. She felt like she could stay there all night. Suddenly, a man came out of his house and saw the little kitten. He sighed and said, "You should be ashamed of yourself! Don't stay near that glowing light." The kitten was so scared. She ran away and hid behind a tree. She was so embarrassed. From behind the tree, the kitten watched the man go back inside his house. When the man was gone, the kitten was left alone with the glowing light. She didn't want to get in trouble again, so she stayed hidden and watched the light until it disappeared. The little kitten had learned her lesson. When she found a new glowing light, she knew it was best to keep her distance.

One day, Mama and Baby were out for a walk. They looked up at the sky, which was a bright yellow colour. Papa and Baby both said, "Wow!" Suddenly, Mama stopped. Baby looked up and saw a yellow stitch lying on the ground. Mama said, "Look Baby, don't you think it's time to drop that stitch back where it belongs? Baby understood and carefully picked up the stitch. Papa, who saw what was happening, said, "Good job Baby, that's very generous. Always remember to look after things and put them where they belong." Mama and Baby continued on their walk and felt happy for having done the right thing.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty bear. He lived in the big woods. He had many friends. One day, the mighty bear met a little boy. The little boy said, "Mr. Bear, can you bring me a big red ball?" The bear said, "Yes, I can bring you a big red ball." The mighty bear went to find a big red ball. He looked and looked, but he could not find one. The bear was sad. He did not want to let the little boy down. The mighty bear went back to the little boy. He said, "I am sorry, I could not bring you a big red ball." The little boy was sad too, and he went home. The mighty bear felt bad and went back to the woods.

Ben is a bunny who likes to hop. He hops in the grass, he hops in the mud, he hops in the snow. He hops all day long, and he is very happy. One day, he meets a fox who wears a red vest. The fox says, "Hello, little bunny. You hop very well. Do you want to hop with me?" Ben thinks the fox is nice, and he likes his vest. He says, "Yes, I want to hop with you. Where do you hop?" The fox says, "I hop in the woods, where there are many trees and flowers. Come with me, and I will show you." Ben follows the fox, and they hop in the woods. But the fox is not nice, he is mean. He wants to eat Ben, and he waits for the right moment to catch him. But Ben is not silly, he is wise. He sees the fox's sharp teeth and his hungry eyes. He remembers what his mom told him, "Never trust a fox, they are not your friends." Ben says, "I have to go now, my mom is waiting for me. Bye, fox." The fox says, "Wait, don't go. Hop with me a little more. It is fun." But Ben does not listen, he hops away as fast as he can. He hops back to the grass, where he is safe. He is glad he escaped the fox, and he learns a lesson. He says, "I will never hop with a fox again. I will only hop with my friends."
