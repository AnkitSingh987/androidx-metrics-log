Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Caterpillar and the Car

One day, a little caterpillar named Tim was walking in the garden. He saw a big, expensive toy car. Tim wanted to play with the car, but he was too small. Tim asked his friend, the ant, "Can you help me play with the car?" The ant said, "Yes, I can help you, but we need to be careful." So, they went to the car and tried to play with it. But as they were playing, Tim slipped and fell. The car started to move fast and hit a tree. The car was broken, and Tim and the ant were sad. They learned that they should ask before playing with things that are not theirs. Tim and the ant said sorry to the owner of the car and promised to be more careful next time. The owner forgave them, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Spot. Spot liked to play near the fireplace. He liked to feel warm and cozy. One day, Spot saw something new near the fireplace. It was a big puzzle. Spot wanted to complete the puzzle. Spot started to put the puzzle pieces together. He was having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Spot heard a strange noise. It came from behind the fireplace. Spot was scared, but he wanted to find out what it was. Spot went behind the fireplace and saw a little bird. The bird was scared too. Spot helped the bird find its way back outside. The bird was so happy, it gave Spot a special puzzle piece. Spot went back to the puzzle and put the special piece in. The puzzle was now complete. Spot and the bird became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play with her friends, the animals. They would run, jump, and laugh together all day long. Mia could remember all their names and the fun games they played. One sunny day, Mia and her friends found a big, old book. The book was full of stories from long, long ago. Mia's friend, the wise owl, said, "This is a book of history." They all sat down to read the stories together. As they read, the sky turned dark and the wind started to blow. Suddenly, a magic fairy appeared! She was clear like a ghost. The fairy said, "Hello, Mia! I am here to give you a special gift. You can now talk to the animals in their own languages!" Mia was so happy and surprised. From that day on, Mia and her animal friends had even more fun playing and talking in the big green forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play on his street. One day, he found a filthy toy car on the ground. He thought it was a fun toy, so he picked it up to play with it. Tim wanted to display his new toy to his friends. He called them to come see the car. His friends came and looked at the filthy toy car. They did not like it because it was dirty. They told Tim to put it back. Tim was sad, but he did not listen to his friends. He kept the filthy toy car and played with it. His hands got very dirty. His mom saw his dirty hands and was not happy. She took the toy car away and made Tim wash his hands. Tim was sad because he lost his toy car and had a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. One day, he went to the park to play. He saw a big tree and wanted to kick his ball near it. Tim was excited to play. As he kicked his ball, it hit a cup that was under the tree. Tim felt frightened because he did not want to make a mess. He went to pick up the cup and saw that it was full of pretty stones. Tim was surprised and happy to find them. Tim showed the stones to his mom. She told him that the stones belonged to someone else. Tim felt sad but decided to find the owner. He found a little girl who had lost the stones. She was so happy to get them back. Tim learned that it is important to return things that do not belong to you.
