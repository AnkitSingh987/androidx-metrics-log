Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bird

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She liked to skip and sing. As she skipped, she saw a big tree with red fruit. She thought, "I want to taste that fruit. It looks yummy." Lily picked a fruit and was about to take a bite when a bird said, "Stop! That fruit is not safe. It will make you sick." Lily was scared and put the fruit down. She thanked the bird and they became friends. Together, Lily and the bird skipped and sang. They told others about the not safe fruit. Everyone was happy and safe. The unexpected thing was that Lily made a new friend. The moral of the story is to listen to others and share what you know.

Lily and Ben were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big car with shiny wheels and a pretty lady inside. The lady smiled at them and said, "Hello, children. Do you want to see my car? It is very nice and has many luxury things." Lily and Ben were curious. They wanted to see the luxury things. They said, "Yes, please. Can we see your car?" The lady said, "Of course. Come with me. But don't tell anyone. It is a secret." Lily and Ben followed the lady to her car. They saw a big TV, a soft bed, and a lot of toys. They were amazed. The lady said, "Do you like it? You can play with anything you want. But first, let me give you some candy. It is very sweet and yummy." Lily and Ben loved candy. They said, "Thank you, lady. You are very nice." They took the candy and ate it. It was very sweet and yummy. But soon, they felt sleepy. They wanted to lie down on the bed. The lady said, "Go ahead. You can sleep here. I will watch over you." Lily and Ben fell asleep on the bed. They did not know that the candy was bad. It made them sleep and not wake up. They did not know that the lady was bad. She wanted to take them away and sell them. They did not know that they would never see their parents or the park again. They did not know that their playtime was over. They did not know that this was the end.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. Lucy saw a big jar with a label on it. She wanted to see what was inside the jar, but she struggled to open it. Lucy was frightened that she would break the jar. Lucy asked her mom, "Mom, can you help me open the jar?" Her mom came over and opened the jar for her. Inside the jar, there were lots of colorful candies. Lucy was so happy! "Thank you, Mom!" Lucy said with a big smile. They took some candies and put them in a bag. Then, they went home to share the candies with Lucy's little brother. They all enjoyed the sweet treats and had a great day.

Max was so excited as he watched his brand new car zooming around. Every time he saw it his eyes lit up and he smiled wide. He wanted to do something special for the car, so he rushed outside with a big hug. Max hugged his car and said, “You are the best thing ever!” But his enthusiasm was quickly dashed when the car zoomed away. Max watched it speed away in disbelief. He had no idea where his car was going. Max tried to chase his car, but it had already gone far away. He was so frustrated. Suddenly, Max had an idea. He just needed to be more enthusiastic and run faster. He zoomed as fast as he could, but the car was too far away. Max was heartbroken. Unfortunately, his enthusiasm wasn’t enough to catch up to his car.

One day, a cute dog went for a jog. He liked to run and feel the wind on his face. As he jogged, he saw a little boy near a tree. "Hi," said the dog. "Do you want to jog with me?" The little boy smiled and said, "Yes, I like to jog too." So, the cute dog and the little boy jogged together. They felt happy and had fun. After their jog, they sat under the tree and laughed. They were now good friends.
