Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Foolish Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a foolish dog named Bobo. Bobo loved to jog all around the land. One day, while jogging, he met a big cat. "Hi, I am Bobo. I like to jog!" said Bobo to the cat. The cat looked at him and said, "I am Cat. I like to nap. Jogging is for foolish dogs." Bobo didn't care. He kept jogging around the land. He jogged so far that he got lost. Bobo was very sad and scared. He couldn't find his way back home. Bobo tried to ask for help, but no one could understand him. He was all alone in the big land. In the end, Bobo never found his way back home. He wished he had listened to the big cat and not been so foolish.

Ben and Mia were hungry. They wanted to eat something sweet. They saw a big jar of cream in the fridge. "Can we have some cream, Mom?" Ben asked. "Only a little bit, and you have to share," Mom said. "And you have to eat some fruit too." Ben and Mia nodded. They took the jar of cream and two spoons. They also took some bananas and apples. They went to the table and sat down. They opened the jar of cream and dipped their spoons in it. It was white and thick and yummy. They put some cream in their mouths and smiled. "This is so good!" Mia said. "I want more!" Ben said. He turned the jar upside down and shook it. He hoped more cream would come out. But nothing happened. The jar was empty. "Hey, that's not fair!" Mia said. "You ate too much cream!" She grabbed the jar from Ben and looked inside. She saw a little bit of cream stuck to the bottom. She tried to scrape it with her spoon, but it was too hard to reach. "Mom, can we have more cream, please?" Mia asked. "No, you had enough," Mom said. "You have to eat some fruit now. Cream is not good for you if you eat too much. It can make your tummy hurt." Ben and Mia made a face. They did not want to eat fruit. They wanted more cream. But they knew Mom was right. They put the jar back in the fridge and picked up their fruit. They peeled the bananas and bit the apples. They were still hungry, but they felt better. They decided to save some cream for tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a big airport. In the airport, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a nice boy. He liked to help people. One day, Tim saw an old man. The old man looked sad. He had a rough bag. The bag was very old and heavy. Tim went to the old man. He said, "I can help you. I can give you a new bag." The old man was happy. Tim and the old man went to a shop. They bought a new bag. The old man put his things in the new bag. The old man was very happy. Then, something strange happened. The old man was not old anymore. He was a magic man! The magic man said, "Thank you, Tim. You are very kind. I will give you a gift." The magic man gave Tim a small toy plane. The toy plane could fly very fast. Tim was very happy. He learned that being kind and helping others is good.

Emily was feeling very sad. She hadn't been feeling well and the medicine made her stomach hurt. Everyone had been scurrying around her trying to give her comfort but nothing seemed to be helping. Then, one day, something magical happened. Grandma arrived with a present. Emily was so excited. Her eyes lit up when she saw it was a special teddy bear. Grandma said she had brought it to comfort Emily when she didn't feel good. Little did Emily know that the magical teddy bear also had a secret. When Emily squeezed it tight, a special kind of medicine filled up her body. "See," said Grandma, "it's all filled up with special medicine for you." At first it was hard to believe but soon enough the special medicine started to take effect. Emily could feel her sadness floating away. Each time she needed a little extra comfort, she held the bear close and it filled her with a warmth and happiness she had never felt before. Grandma had brought Emily the most special present ever.
