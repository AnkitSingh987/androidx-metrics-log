Category: Intermediate
Title: The Enormous Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was an enormous tree. A little bird lived in the tree and loved to count the leaves every day! The bird had a silly idea - to use a napkin to count each and every leaf. The bird flew to the ground and picked up a napkin. He flew back up to the tree and started counting. But when he tried to count them all, the napkin just couldn’t fit all the leaves! The bird was frustrated and flew away from the tree, "This is impossible! How will I ever get to count all these leaves?" Just then, an old wise owl appeared from behind the tree. "Listen, my young friend," said the old owl, "you can’t count everything in the world, some things can’t be counted. So enjoy the beauty of the leaves and don’t try to count them all. Life’s too short to count everything!" The bird was enlightened by the owl’s wise words and flew away with a smile on his face. He enjoyed the beauty of the leaves without even trying to count them. The moral of the story is that while counting is important, we should not forget to appreciate the beauty of things that cannot be counted.

Once upon a time, in a normal little town, there lived a kind cat named Tom. Tom was a good cat who followed the law. He played with his friends and helped his family. One day, Tom heard a whisper. "Psst, Tom," said the whisper. Tom looked around and saw a tiny mouse. The mouse looked scared. "Can you help me?" asked the mouse. Tom was surprised. Cats and mice were not friends in this town. But Tom wanted to help. Tom and the mouse became friends. They played together and whispered secrets to each other. The other cats and mice saw them and were surprised too. Soon, all the cats and mice in the town became friends. The town was happier than ever before. And it all started with a little whisper.

Once there was a boy named Jack. He lived in a house with his mom and dad. One day, Jack's mom and dad told him that he would have to separate from them to go to a new place. Jack was scared, but his mom and dad said it would be fun. So, Jack took a rod and rode in his dad's car. When he got to the new place, he met a bossy woman. She told Jack about the rules and fun things he could do. Jack was excited, but he also missed his mom and dad. The next day, Jack went to the new place and did a lot of fun things. He read books, played games and sang songs. Jack made a lot of friends. At the end of the day, Jack waved goodbye to all of his new friends and got back in the car. He was excited for the next time he would be able to separate from his mom and dad and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little girl named Lucy. She had a friend, a talking cactus named Spike. They lived in a small house with a pretty garden. One day, Lucy said to Spike, "Let's play a game! I will say a word and you will repeat it." Spike agreed and they started to play the game. Lucy said, "Cat," and Spike repeated, "Cat." They laughed and played the game for a long time. As they played, Lucy's mom came outside. She saw them having fun and said, "It's time for lunch, Lucy. Come inside and wash your hands." Lucy said goodbye to Spike and went inside. After lunch, Lucy and Spike played more fun games together. They were the best of friends.

Lily likes to exercise. She likes to run and jump and play. She feels strong and happy when she exercises. She wants to invite her friend Ben to exercise with her. She calls Ben on the phone. "Hi, Ben. Do you want to exercise with me?" she asks. Ben is weak. He does not like to exercise. He likes to watch TV and eat snacks. He feels tired and bored when he exercises. He does not want to exercise with Lily. "No, thank you, Lily. I do not want to exercise. I want to watch TV and eat snacks," he says. Lily is sad. She likes Ben, but she does not like to watch TV and eat snacks. She thinks they are not good for her. She wants to have fun and be healthy. "Please, Ben. Exercise is good for you. It makes you strong and happy. It is fun to exercise with a friend. Please, come with me," she says. Ben thinks for a moment. He likes Lily, too. He does not want to make her sad. He wants to be a good friend. He decides to try to exercise with Lily. "Okay, Lily. I will exercise with you. But only for a little bit. Then we can watch TV and eat snacks," he says. Lily is happy. She thanks Ben and tells him to meet her at the park. She hopes Ben will like to exercise with her. She hopes Ben will feel strong and happy, too. She thinks exercise is good for them.
