Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Woods

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the woods. She saw a small cabin appear. The cabin was made of wood and had a red door. Mia was very happy to see it. A kind lady lived in the cabin. The lady said, "Hello! My name is Sue. Would you like to come in?" Mia said, "Yes, please!" She went inside and saw a warm fire. Mia felt grateful for the warm fire because it was cold outside. Mia and Sue had a fun time in the cabin. They played games and had a yummy snack. When it was time for Mia to go home, she said, "Thank you, Sue! I had a great time." Sue said, "You're welcome! Come back soon!" Mia walked back to her house, feeling happy and grateful for her new friend.

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to go on a safari. She put on her hat and went outside. She saw her old friend, Mr. Elephant, and said, "Let's go on a safari together!" As they walked, Mia started to skip. Mr. Elephant tried to skip too, but he was too big and slow. He felt sad that he could not skip like Mia. Mia saw Mr. Elephant was sad and stopped skipping. Mia said, "Don't be sad, Mr. Elephant. We can walk together and still have fun on our safari." Mr. Elephant smiled and they walked together, looking at all the animals. They had a great time on their safari, and Mr. Elephant was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play in the water and splash. She also loved to put on lotion when she was done playing. One day, Sue found a bottle of lotion that was spoiled. It was old and smelled bad. She didn't know what to do with it. Sue had an idea. She thought she could play a trick on her friend, Tom. She gave the spoiled lotion to Tom and said, "Here, put this on after you splash in the water." Tom did not know the lotion was spoiled. He put it on and said, "Yuck! This smells so bad!" Sue laughed and laughed. But then, she saw that Tom was sad. Sue felt bad for playing a trick on her friend. She said sorry and gave Tom her good lotion. They both put on the good lotion and played in the water together. From that day on, Sue learned that it is not nice to play tricks on friends. The moral of the story is to always be kind to others and not play mean tricks.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his small cart. He would push it all around his house and fill it with his favorite toys. One sunny day, Tim was playing outside with his cart when a big dog came running up to him. The dog wanted to play too, but he was too rough. He knocked over Tim's cart and all his toys fell out. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Tim's mom saw what happened and came to help. She picked up the toys and put them back in the small cart. Tim felt better and decided to share his cart with the big dog. They played together and became good friends. Tim learned that even when bad things happen, it can turn into something good.

Lily likes to learn new things. She likes to read books, play games, and draw pictures. But sometimes she needs help with hard words or numbers. That is why she has a tutor. A tutor is a person who helps you learn. Lily's tutor is called Sam. He is very nice and funny. He comes to Lily's house every week and brings a big bag of toys and books. They sit on the floor and play and learn together. One day, Sam brought a new toy. It was a puzzle. It had many pieces that you had to fit together to make a picture. The picture was of a big, green dinosaur. Lily loves dinosaurs. She thinks they are incredible. Lily and Sam started to work on the puzzle. They looked for pieces that matched the colors and shapes. They found the head, the tail, the legs, and the body. But they could not find the last piece. It was the eye of the dinosaur. They looked everywhere. They looked under the sofa, behind the curtains, in the bag, and on the table. But they could not find the eye. Lily was sad. She wanted to finish the puzzle and see the dinosaur. Then, she had an idea. She looked at Sam. He was wearing glasses. She asked him if she could try them on. He said yes. She put the glasses on and looked around. Everything looked bigger and clearer. She saw something shiny on the floor. It was the eye of the dinosaur! She picked it up and ran to the puzzle. She put the eye in the right place. The puzzle was complete. The dinosaur looked amazing. Lily was happy. She hugged Sam and thanked him. He smiled and said he was proud of her. They both agreed that the puzzle was incredible.
