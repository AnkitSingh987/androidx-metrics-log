Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Vase

One day, a pretty girl named Lily found a broken vase. She was sad because the vase was very pretty. She wanted to heal the vase and make it whole again. Lily asked her friend Tom for help. Tom said, "I can help you heal the vase. We can use glue to put the pieces back together." So, they started to glue the broken pieces. As they worked, their friend Sam came by. Sam wanted to play, but Lily and Tom were busy fixing the vase. Sam was sad and said, "I want to play too!" Lily told Sam, "We can all play after we heal the vase. You can help us too!" So, Sam helped Lily and Tom fix the pretty vase. When they finished, they all played together and were very happy.

Fred and his mom were in the garden. Fred was pushing his toy car. He suddenly saw something racing by. He shouted with surprise, "Look, mom! It's a hose! It's zooming!" His mom replied, "Yes, Fred, it's an honest hose that is zooming by us. Let's watch carefully and see where it goes." The hose zoomed around the corner and out of sight. Fred and his mom waited a few moments, and then continued playing in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very interesting boy. He liked to play with his toys and run in the park. One day, Tim found a big well in the park. He looked into the well and saw his toy ball at the bottom. Tim was sad. He wanted his toy ball back. But he didn't know how to get it. He tried to put a stick in the well, but the stick was not long enough. Tim felt bad and didn't know what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. He remembered he had a long rope at home. He ran back to his house and found the rope. He did not forget the rope this time. Tim tied the rope to a big rock and put it in the well. The rock pulled the toy ball up. Tim was happy to have his toy ball back.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the store with her mom. She saw a big lollipop and asked, "Mom, can I have the lollipop, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful with it." Amy held her lollipop and walked outside. She saw a dog with a big bow on its neck. The dog looked at Amy and the lollipop. Amy said, "Hello, dog! Do you like my lollipop?" The dog jumped and took the lollipop from Amy's hand. Amy was sad, but then the dog did something unexpected. The dog gave the lollipop back to Amy and bowed. Amy laughed and said, "Thank you, dog! You are a good friend." From that day on, Amy and the dog with the bow were best friends. They played together and shared the lollipop, always being careful not to drop it. And the dog always bowed to say thank you.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max loved to run and play in the park. One day, Max found a loop. He thought it was a fun toy to play with. He picked it up with his mouth and started to run around. Max met a cat named Lily. Lily saw Max running with the loop and said, "Max, why are you running with that stupid loop? You should play with a ball instead!" Max didn't want to listen to Lily. He loved his loop and didn't think it was stupid. So, he kept running and playing with it. Later, Max saw a little boy who was sad. The boy had lost his loop. Max knew it was the right thing to do, so he gave the loop back to the boy. The boy was very happy and thanked Max. Lily saw this and realized she was wrong. She said, "Max, I'm sorry for calling your loop stupid. You did a good thing by helping the boy." Max and Lily became friends and played together in the park. They learned that it's important to be kind and not call things stupid because they might be special to someone else.
