Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Hair

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had long hair that she loved to play with. She was very gifted and could do many things. One day, Lucy's mom told her that they were going to get her hair cut. Lucy started to worry. She didn't want to lose her long hair. But her mom said it would be good for her. The next day, they went to the hair place. Lucy was still worried, but then she saw a girl with short hair. The girl looked happy and pretty. So Lucy let them cut her hair. After the haircut, Lucy felt happy too. Her hair looked nice and she didn't worry anymore.

Once upon a time there was a popular pink bear. His name was Fluffy and he was always happy. One day, however, Fluffy began to frown. He didn't know why he felt so sad, but he couldn't cheer up. He asked his friends if they could help. One of his friends said, "Maybe you need to take a break." So Fluffy did. He took a nap and soon he began to feel better. When he woke up, he was smiling. He felt so happy that he wanted to share his joy with his friends. He came up with an idea to make everyone laugh. He started dancing and he was very good at it! Soon all of his friends were laughing and smiling. Fluffy was so excited that he jumped up and down. Everyone was so happy. It was a pink, happy day!

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to help his family. He was a very useful dog. He would fetch the paper, carry bags, and even help set the table for dinner. One day, Spot's family had a big dinner with friends. They asked Spot to help by bringing a big bowl of food to the table. Spot was a bit scared because it was heavy, but he knew his family trusted him to do it. Spot carried the big bowl to the table, being very careful not to drop it. When he got to the table, everyone clapped and cheered for Spot. They were so happy that he could help with dinner. Spot was proud to be a useful dog that his family could trust.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a normal boy who liked to play outside. One day, Tim saw a ladder near his house. He wanted to climb it and see what was up there. As Tim climbed the ladder, he saw a toy gun on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Wow, I can shoot with this!" He played with the toy gun and pretended to shoot at the sky. "Pew, pew!" he said, laughing and having fun. Just then, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. "Hi, Tim! Can I play too?" asked Sam. Tim said, "Sure, let's shoot together!" They both played with the toy gun, climbing up and down the ladder, and having a great time. They were happy to be normal kids, playing outside and enjoying their day.

Once upon a time, there was a daughter named Lily. She liked to play outside in the open. One sunny day, Lily asked her mom if she could play outside. Her mom said yes, but to be back before dark. Lily went to the big, open field near her house. She saw her friends, Tom and Sue, playing with a ball. They asked Lily to play with them. Lily was very happy to play with her friends. They played and played until the sun went down. Lily remembered her mom's words and said goodbye to her friends. She ran back to her house with a big smile on her face. Her mom was waiting for her with a warm hug. Lily had a fun day playing in the open with her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy, a laser. His best friend, a boy named Tim, would always make the laser move, and Spot would chase it. They had so much fun together. Tim would always say, "Spot, you're the best dog ever!" One day, Tim and Spot were playing outside when a big, scary cat came into the yard. The cat wanted the laser, but Spot didn't want to give it up. Tim had an idea. He told Spot, "We can trick the cat, but you have to trust me." Tim made the laser move far away from the cat. The cat chased the laser, but it was too fast for the cat to catch. The cat got tired and stopped chasing the laser. Tim said to the cat, "You can't catch the laser. You should give up and go away." The cat didn't want to keep trying, so it surrendered and left the yard. Spot was so happy that he barked and wagged his tail. He knew that Tim was very smart and would always help him. From that day on, Spot and Tim played with the laser every day, and they were always happy together.
