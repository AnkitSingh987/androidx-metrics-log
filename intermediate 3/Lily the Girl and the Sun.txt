Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Sun

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to make a nice picture. She looked for her scissors to cut paper. But she could not find them. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "The scissors are not available. Let's use our hands to tear the paper." Lily felt sad but her mom encouraged her. They started to tear the paper into shapes. They made a sun, a house, and a tree. They glued the shapes on a big paper to make a pretty picture. Lily was happy. Then, something unexpected happened. Lily's cat jumped on the table and knocked over the glue. The glue spilled all over the picture. But Lily and her mom laughed. They said it looked like it was raining on their picture. They added blue paper for rain and made a fun, rainy day picture.

One day, a girl named Mary was playing with her violin. She was very proud of it and loved to stretch out the strings and make music. Suddenly, a kind old man appeared. He asked Mary if he could see her violin. But Mary didn't like to share and she said no. The old man was very sad but he still smiled at Mary. He asked her if he could stretch the violin strings for her. Mary was a bit unsure but eventually said yes. Mary watched carefully as the kindly old man gently stretch each string until it sounded perfect. Mary was so pleased and thanked the old man for his kindness. Then they both made music together and it sounded beautiful.

Once upon a time there was a big bear who liked to do yoga. One day he decided to go to a shop to get his yoga mat repaired. He asked the shopkeeper, "Can you please repair my yoga mat?" The shopkeeper smiled and said, "Yes, I can repair it for you." The bear was very happy and thanked the shopkeeper. He waited beside the shop while the shopkeeper repaired his mat. After a while the shopkeeper came out with the bear's repaired yoga mat, but it was much bigger than before. The bear was very surprised and asked the shopkeeper, "How come my yoga mat is now bigger?" The shopkeeper replied, "That's the only way to repair it!" The bear looked very sad as he realized that he couldn't use the big yoga mat for yoga. He took his mat and left the shop with a heavy heart. Unfortunately, the bear's yoga mat was ruined and he could no longer do yoga. The End.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a bald man. He had a pen that he loved very much. One day, he lost his pen and felt very sad. He asked his friend, "Have you seen my pen?" His friend said, "No, but I will help you look for it." They looked and looked, but they could not find the pen. The bald man was still sad. Then, a little girl came up to him and asked, "Why are you sad?" The bald man said, "I lost my pen." The little girl said, "I found a pen. Is this your pen?" The bald man was very happy. He said, "Yes, that is my pen! Thank you!" The bald man and his friend learned that when you ask for help, good things can happen. They also learned to be kind to others, just like the little girl. This made them very happy.

Tom is a smart boy. He likes to learn new things. He has a black cat named Max. Max is his best friend. They play and cuddle every day. One day, Tom finds a book in his room. It has many pictures and words. Tom wants to read the book. He opens it and looks at the pages. He sees a picture of a bird. He points at it and says, "Bird!" Max jumps on the bed and looks at the book too. He sees the bird and says, "Meow!" Tom is surprised. He looks at Max and says, "Did you speak? Did you say meow?" Max nods and says, "Yes, I did. I can speak. I am a smart cat. I learned from you." Tom is happy. He hugs Max and says, "Wow, you are amazing! You are a smart cat. I love you, Max." Max purrs and says, "I love you too, Tom. You are a smart boy. Can you read me the book?" Tom smiles and says, "Yes, I can. Let's read the book together." They read the book and learn new things. They have fun and are happy. They are smart and love each other.
