Category: Intermediate
Title: White the Cat and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named White. White loved to nap. Every day, White would nap in the warm sun. But one day, the sky was dark. White could not find a sunny spot to nap in. White looked around and saw a big, dark cave. White thought, "Maybe I can nap in the cave." So, White went into the cave to find a cozy spot. Inside, it was very dark, but White found a soft place to nap. White took a long nap in the dark cave. When White woke up, the sky was not dark anymore. The sun was shining bright outside the cave. White was happy to see the sun again and went outside to nap in the warm sun. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On the farm, there was a fat pig. The pig was very happy. He liked to play in the mud and eat a lot. He had many friends like the cow, the duck, and the sheep. They all played together every day. One day, the pig started to wonder. He wondered what was outside the farm. He wanted to see new things and meet new friends. So, the pig decided to go on an adventure. He said goodbye to his friends and left the farm. As the pig walked, he saw a big, shiny thing. It was a big, round ball. The pig thought it was a new friend. He went up to the ball and said, "Hello, I am a fat pig from the farm. What is your name?" The ball did not say anything. The pig was sad. But then, the ball started to roll. The pig was very happy. He had a new friend to play with. They rolled and played together all day. The pig did not wonder about the farm anymore. He was happy with his new friend, the big, shiny ball.

Sara and Lily were best friends. They liked to play with dolls and dress up. One day, Sara got a new necklace from her grandma. It had a shiny jewel on it. Sara was very happy and showed it to Lily. "Wow, your necklace is so pretty!" Lily said. "Can I try it on?" "Sure, you can try it on. But be careful, it is very special to me," Sara said. She let Lily wear the necklace for a while. Lily liked the necklace so much that she did not want to give it back. She thought it made her look more beautiful. She was envious of Sara. She decided to keep the necklace for herself. "Sara, I have to go home now. Bye!" Lily said. She ran away with the necklace. Sara was sad and angry. She did not understand why Lily took her necklace. She felt like she lost her friend and her gift. The next day, Lily came to Sara's house. She looked sorry and ashamed. She had the necklace in her hand. "Sara, I am very sorry. I did a bad thing. I took your necklace because I wanted it. But I was wrong. It is yours and you love it. I was not a good friend. Can you forgive me?" Lily said. Sara looked at Lily. She saw that Lily was sincere and regretful. She remembered that Lily was her best friend. She decided to forgive her. "Lily, I forgive you. I am glad you brought back my necklace. But you know what? You don't need a jewel to be beautiful. You are beautiful because you are kind and smart and fun. I love you for who you are, not for what you have. That is what friends do," Sara said. Lily hugged Sara. She felt happy and relieved. She learned a lesson. She realized that jewels are not as important as friendship. She thanked Sara for being a good friend. The moral of the story is: Don't be envious of what others have. Be grateful for what you have. Friendship is more valuable than jewels.

One day, a blue tank went for a ride. It saw a big hill and wanted to go up. The blue tank tried to push up the hill, but it was too steep. The tank felt sad. A little red car came by and saw the blue tank. The little red car said, "Hi, blue tank! Why are you sad?" The blue tank said, "I want to go up the hill, but I can't push myself up." The little red car wanted to help. It said, "Let's try together!" They both pushed and pushed. With the help of the little red car, the blue tank made it up the hill. They were so happy and became good friends. They played together all day long.
