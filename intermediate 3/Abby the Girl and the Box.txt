Category: Intermediate
Title: Abby the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Abby. She was three years old. One day, Abby wanted to build something special. She grabbed some old cardboard and made a big box out of it. Next, Abby imagined what could be inside the box. She wanted it to be filled with treasures. She started to imagine what it could be. Suddenly, Abby found out that the walls of the cardboard box were too flimsy. She tried to push it together but it kept falling apart. She was getting frustrated. Nearby, Abby's mom saw her struggling. “Why don't you ask for help," she said. "I'm sure together we can make it better." Abby smiled. She asked her mom to help her find some tape and some scissors. Together, they worked to make the box stronger. After a few tries, the box became sturdier and Abby was excited to explore what was inside!

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Max. Max loved to ride on the couch in his house. One day, Max saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she could not find her toy. Max said, "I can help you find your toy, Lily!" So, Max and Lily looked all around the house. They looked under the bed, behind the chair, and even inside the big box. But they could not find Lily's toy anywhere. Finally, Max had an idea. He asked Lily to ride on the couch with him. They both jumped onto the couch and started to bounce. Suddenly, Lily's toy popped out from under the couch cushions! Lily was so happy, and she hugged Max. From that day on, Max and Lily were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big dream. He wanted to meet the white moon. Every night, he looked up at the sky and saw the moon shining bright. He told his mom, "One day, I will meet the moon." One day, Tim found a big box. He had an idea. He would make a rocket to fly to the moon. He worked hard on his rocket. His mom saw him and asked, "What are you doing, Tim?" He said, "I am making a rocket to meet the moon." His mom smiled and said, "I believe in you, Tim." Finally, the rocket was ready. Tim climbed inside and counted, "One, two, three!" The rocket flew up into the sky. It went higher and higher until Tim met the white moon. The moon was happy to meet Tim too. They talked and played all night. Then, Tim went back home in his rocket. His mom was so proud of him. Tim's dream came true.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Bop. Bop loved to play and hop around all day. One day, Bop found a big wreck. It was an old car that had been left in the forest. Bop was very curious and wanted to see what was inside. Bop hopped closer to the wreck and saw something sour. It was a big, sour lemon! Bop didn't like sour things, so he hopped away from the wreck. But as he hopped away, he heard a loud noise. It was a big, scary bear! Bop knew he had to escape from the bear. Bop hopped as fast as he could to escape the bear. He remembered the wreck and had an idea. Bop led the bear to the wreck and threw the sour lemon at him. The bear didn't like sour things either and ran away. Bop had escaped the bear, and he was safe. From that day on, Bop knew that even sour things could help him when he needed it.

One day, a furry cat named Tom found a newspaper. He wanted to gain a friend. So, Tom walked to the park with the newspaper. At the park, Tom met a dog named Max. Tom said, "Hi, I am Tom. I have a newspaper. Do you want to be my friend?" Max said, "Yes, I want to be your friend." They played with the newspaper, but then a big wind came. The wind took the newspaper up, up, up into the sky. Tom and Max were sad. They thought they lost the newspaper. But then, a bird brought the newspaper back to them. They were so happy and played all day.
