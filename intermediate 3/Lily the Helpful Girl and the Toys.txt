Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Helpful Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a helpful girl named Lily. She liked to help her mom and dad with chores. One day, her mom asked her to clean her room. Lily was happy to help. Lily started to clean her room. She picked up her toys and put them in the closet. She found a toy that did not belong to her. It was her brother's toy. Lily thought, "I should give it back to him." So, she went to her brother's room and said, "Please, here is your toy. I found it in my room." Her brother was happy and said, "Thank you, Lily!" He gave her a big hug. Lily felt good because she was helpful and kind. The moral of the story is: always be helpful and kind to others.

One day, a brave boy named Tom went to the park. He saw a big scale on the ground. He wanted to see how much he weighed. Tom stood on the scale and looked down. The numbers on the scale went up and down. He was very excited. Suddenly, a loud shout came from behind a tree. It was a little girl named Sue. She was scared of the scale. Tom wanted to help Sue feel brave too. He told her that the scale was fun and not scary. Sue slowly walked over to the scale and stood on it. When Sue stood on the scale, something strange happened. The scale started to move and it turned into a big slide! Tom and Sue laughed as they slid down the slide together. They had so much fun that they forgot all about the scale. They played on the slide all day and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big stack of yummy food. The food was on a big table. Many animals came to eat the food. They were all happy and excited. But there was a problem. A little bug wanted to eat the food too. The other animals did not want to share. They thought the bug was too small. The bug was very sad. The bug had an idea. It found a bitter leaf and put it on the stack. The animals did not like the bitter leaf. They did not want to eat it. The bug ate the bitter leaf and smiled. The other animals saw that the bug was brave. They let the bug eat with them, and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his rope toy. One day, he saw a big tree with a rope hanging down. Spot was curious and wanted to see what was up there. Spot asked his friend, Cat, "Can you help me raise the rope?" Cat said, "Yes, let's do it together!" So, Spot and Cat pulled the rope up and up. They were very strong. As they raised the rope, they saw a fun treehouse at the top of the tree. Spot and Cat climbed up and played all day. They were happy they found the treehouse and had lots of fun with their new rope toy.

Once upon a time, there was a big ostrich named Ollie. Ollie lived on a rich farm with many animals. He had lots of friends like cows, pigs, and chickens. They played together every day. One day, Ollie saw a big box near the farm. It was a gift for him! Ollie was very happy to receive the gift. He opened the box and found a big, shiny ball inside. Ollie loved his new ball. Ollie showed his new ball to all his friends on the farm. They played with the ball all day long. Ollie and his friends had so much fun. They were all very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to imagine things. She would close her eyes and think of fun places to play. One day, Lucy imagined a deep hole in her backyard. She wanted to see how deep it could go. Lucy found a stick and started to dig the deep hole. As she dug, she found a small box. Lucy was very excited. She opened the box and saw a magic lamp. She gave it a rub, and a genie came out. The genie said he would give Lucy one wish. Lucy thought about it and decided to wish for a big, beautiful garden for her family and friends to enjoy. The genie made her wish come true, and the deep hole was now a big, beautiful garden. Lucy learned that when you imagine good things and work hard, you can make them happen. And that was the moral value of the story: always use your imagination for good and work hard to make your dreams come true.
