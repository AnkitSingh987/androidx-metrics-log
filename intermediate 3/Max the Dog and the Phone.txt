Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Phone

One day, a big dog named Max found a phone on the floor. The phone was important because it belonged to a girl named Lily. Max knew he had to give the phone back to Lily. He picked it up with his mouth and went to find her. Max found Lily in the park. She was playing with her friends. Max was scared to go near her because he was very shy. He started to tremble. Lily saw Max and noticed her phone in his mouth. She ran to him and said, "Max, is that my phone? Thank you for finding it!" Max was happy to help Lily. He gave her the phone and they became good friends. They played in the park all day. The unexpected thing was that Max was not shy anymore. He learned that being brave and helping others can make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lucky cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a big backyard. He loved to play and run in the backyard every day. One day, Tom saw a pretty bird in a tree. He wanted to be friends with the bird. Tom set a trap with food to catch the bird. He thought the bird would like the food and they could play together. But the bird did not want to be friends with Tom. The bird flew away and never came back. Tom felt sad and lonely. He wished he had not set the trap. Now, Tom did not feel lucky anymore.

Once upon a time there was a seal that lived in the ocean. He was a very adventurous seal and loved to explore. One day he decided to go on a journey. As he swam far and wide, he saw all sorts of creatures and plants growing. The seal said to himself "This is amazing! I'm going to keep exploring to see what else I can find." Soon, he reached a beach. He saw little crabs scurrying around and went to take a closer look. But the crab said sternly in a rough voice "What are you doing here? This beach is too small for you to be comfortable." But the seal was not scared. He replied, " I'm looking for something adventurous! I promise I won't stay too long." The crab thought for a moment before softening and said, " Well, if you must, you can stay for a little while." And so, the seal explored the beach for some time before returning to his home. He had learnt a lot about his surroundings and was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little theater in a small town. In this theater, there was an incredible lion who could roar very loud. The lion loved to put on shows for all the children in the town. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to watch the lion's show. She was very excited to see the lion roar. When the lion came on stage, he roared so loud that everyone clapped and cheered. Lucy was so happy, she stood up and shouted, "You are the best lion ever!" The lion smiled and said, "Thank you, Lucy! I love to make everyone happy with my roar. I hope you come back to the theater to watch my show again soon!" Lucy promised she would, and she and the lion became great friends. From that day on, Lucy always came to the theater to watch the incredible lion's shows, and they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a big owl. The owl lived in a tree. The tree was in a park. The owl loved to mix things. One day, a little boy came to the park. He had a cheap toy. The toy was a car. The owl saw the toy and wanted to play. The owl said "Can I play with your toy car?" The little boy said "Yes, you can play with my toy car." The owl and the little boy played with the toy car. They had fun. They mixed dirt and leaves to make a road for the car. They laughed and played all day. The owl and the little boy became good friends.
