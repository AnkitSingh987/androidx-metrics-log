Category: Intermediate
Title: The Clever Rabbit and the Frog

Once upon a time there was a clever rabbit. He loved to jump! Everywhere he went he hopped and skipped. One day, he was hopping around and he saw a start at the top of a hill. The clever rabbit was so excited, he could not wait to jump on it! He hopped and hopped until he reached the start. He jumped on it and he felt so happy! Then he jumped off it and rolled down the hill, back to the bottom again! He was so full of energy that he kept jumping and hopping around the start. He had so much fun that he didn't even stop when his friend, Mrs Frog, came by to visit. Mrs Frog was so surprised to see him that she too started jumping and hopping around with the rabbit! They both laughed and had so much fun around the start! It made the rabbit even more clever! The rabbit and Mrs Frog kept jumping and hopping around the start until the sun set. They said goodbye and the clever rabbit hopped off into the night, happy and full of energy.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue went to the park. The sun was shining, and it was a calm day. They saw a big fruit tree with many fruits on it. They wanted to eat the fruits, so they picked some. Tim said, "Let's split the fruits so we can both eat them." Sue agreed, and they started to split the fruits. They were happy to share the fruits with each other. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, angry bird came down from the sky. It did not like that Tim and Sue took the fruits from the tree. The bird took all the fruits from them and flew away. Tim and Sue were sad, and they went home with no fruits to eat. The day did not end well for them.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was a cute frog. The cute frog had many friends. They liked to play and jump all day long. One day, the cute frog saw something shiny and sharp near the pond. The cute frog did not know what it was, but he thought it was very interesting. The cute frog told his friends about the sharp thing. They all wanted to see it too. They jumped and hopped to the shiny, sharp thing. They were all very curious. The cute frog said, "We should be careful. This sharp thing could hurt us." The next day, the cute frog and his friends played near the sharp thing again. They were very careful not to touch it. Then, a big bird flew down and tried to catch one of the cute frog's friends. The cute frog quickly jumped and pushed the sharp thing towards the big bird. The big bird got scared and flew away. The cute frog and his friends were safe, and they were very happy. They thanked the cute frog for being brave and smart. From that day on, they knew that the sharp thing could protect them. They still played near the pond, but they always remembered to be careful around the sharp thing.

Anna and Ben were at the zoo with their mom. They saw many animals, like lions, monkeys, and giraffes. They were very happy and excited. Then they came to the elephant house. There was a big elephant inside. He had a long nose, big ears, and a gray skin. He looked very sad. He did not move or make any sound. "Why is he so upset, mom?" Anna asked. Mom said, "He is lonely. He misses his family and friends. They live far away, in Africa. He wants to go back to them, but he can't." Anna and Ben felt sorry for the elephant. They wanted to cheer him up. They waved at him and said, "Hello, elephant! We are your friends. We like you. You are very big and strong and smart." The elephant heard them. He lifted his head and looked at them. He saw their smiling faces and kind eyes. He felt a little bit better. He opened his mouth and yawned. He made a loud noise, like a trumpet. Anna and Ben laughed. They clapped their hands and said, "Wow, elephant! You have a very loud voice. You are very funny. Can you do it again?" The elephant nodded. He yawned again and again. He made more noises. He flapped his ears and swung his nose. He started to move and play. Anna and Ben were happy. They had made a new friend. They said, "Bye, elephant! We will come back to see you soon. We hope you will be happy too." The elephant smiled. He said, "Bye, Anna and Ben! Thank you for being my friends. You made me happy too."
