Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Loyal Dog and the Cat

One day, a loyal dog named Spot was playing outside. He liked to run and jump in the grass. Spot's best friend was a little boy named Tim. Tim and Spot did everything together. "Spot, come here!" Tim would call, and Spot would run to him. They would play catch with a ball and have a lot of fun. One day, while they were playing, they saw something in the bushes. They went to take a look. When they looked in the bushes, they found a little cat. The cat was scared at first, but then it started to play with Tim and Spot. They all had fun together. "Now we have a new friend!" said Tim, and they all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a heavy swan. It would dance up and down all day. The swan did this to make itself feel happy. One day, a little girl came to the lake and saw the heavy swan dancing. She was so happy to watch the swan dance. She started to clap her hands and did a little dance too! The swan came closer to the little girl and danced around her. The girl was so happy that she started to run around with the swan. They both had so much fun, running and dancing together. The little girl said goodbye to the heavy swan and ran off home. The swan carried on dancing and was happy for the rest of the day.

One day, there was a little boy walking through the park. He saw a large potato in the grass and he was envious. He wanted to get the potato, so he ran toward it. When he got close to it, he saw a rabbit hop in first. The rabbit started to eat the potato, and the little boy was very unhappy. He was so envious of the rabbit! Suddenly, an old man walked up to the boy. He said, "Why don't you enter that old tree over there and find some potatoes?" The little boy was excited and followed the old man. They went over to the tree and knocked on it. Suddenly a lot of potatoes fell out! They were all different sizes and colours. The little boy was so happy. He ran around gathering up all the potatoes. The old man laughed and said, "Good job, now you can have some potatoes of your own too." The little boy was so happy and thanked the old man. He went on his way with a bag of potatoes, and never looked back with envious eyes.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little bunny named Tim. Tim loved to play in the grass. He would hop and jump all day long. But Tim was too shy to make friends with the other animals. One sunny day, Tim saw a big, round ball in the grass. He stared at it, wondering what it was. The ball was red and shiny. Tim wanted to play with it, but he didn't know how. Just then, a friendly bird flew by and saw Tim staring at the ball. The bird showed Tim how to push the ball with his nose. They played together and had lots of fun. Tim was happy he found a friend and was not shy anymore.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a bird named Grace. Grace was a very pretty bird. She had big wings and she could soar high in the sky. She was very graceful when she flew. All the other birds liked to watch Grace fly. One day, a big bird came to the forest. The big bird told all the other birds about a new law. The law said that all the birds had to fly low, not high. The big bird said that if they did not follow the law, something bad would happen. Grace did not like this law, but the other birds said they had to listen to the big bird. Grace tried to fly low, but she did not like it. She wanted to soar high in the sky like before. One day, she decided to fly high again. She started to soar, and all the other birds watched. But then, the big bird saw Grace. The big bird got very mad. He came to Grace and took her away. The other birds were sad, and they knew they had to follow the law. They never saw Grace again.
