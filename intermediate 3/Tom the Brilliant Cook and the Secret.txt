Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Brilliant Cook and the Secret

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant cook named Tom. Tom wanted to improve his pizza. He thought of a plan. He would add a new topping to make it better. Tom went to the store and found a special topping. It was a secret. He was very excited. He put the topping on the pizza and cooked it. The smell was very good. When the pizza was done, Tom called his friends to try it. They took a bite and something unexpected happened. The pizza made them dance and laugh! They had never had so much fun eating pizza before. Tom's plan worked, and his pizza was the best in town.

Once upon a time there was a family that lived in a big city. The little boy wanted to explore the city and asked his parents if he could wander around with them. His parents agreed and they grabbed an empty tube and went out. The boy was curious and asked a lot of questions as they walked around. They went down big roads, walked though small parks and looked at tall buildings. After a while, the little boy got sleepy. His parents brought him to a playground and he climbed on the tube. He rocked back and forth and soon fell asleep. The parents watched over him as he dreamed happily and eventually it was time to go home. The boy said goodbye to the playground tired but content.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to throw a ball. He played with his ball every day. One day, he saw a new ball in the store. It was a modern ball. It was very pretty. Tom asked his mom for the modern ball. She said yes. Tom was very happy. He took the ball home to play. He threw the ball up in the air. It was so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. When Tom threw the ball very high, it turned into light! The light was bright and beautiful. Tom was surprised. He had a magic ball. Now, Tom could play with his magic ball and make pretty lights in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a rag doll that she loved very much. The rag doll was her best friend. They played together every day. One day, Sue saw a big tree with a mighty branch. She wanted to climb it with her rag doll. Sue tried and tried, but she could not climb the tree. She was sad. Then, Sue's big brother Tom came to help. He held her hand and they tried again. This time, Sue and her rag doll succeed. They climbed the tree and sat on the mighty branch. They were so happy and played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big frog named Tom. Tom lived in a small pond. He had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, Tom saw a spoiled apple near the pond. He did not like the spoiled apple because it was not good to eat. Tom had an idea. He thought if he could kiss the spoiled apple, it might turn into something nice. He hopped closer to the apple and gave it a big kiss. After the kiss, the apple did not turn into anything else. It was still a spoiled apple. Tom felt sad, but his friends came to cheer him up. They all played together and had fun. Tom learned that it's okay if things don't always go the way you want them to. The most important thing is to have good friends and be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a modest cat named Jazz. Jazz loved to watch birds fly in the sky. One day, Jazz saw a big, pretty bird on a tree. Jazz said, "Hello, bird. I like to watch you fly. Can we be friends?" The bird looked at Jazz and said, "Yes, we can be friends. But you must not try to catch me, okay?" Jazz and the bird played together every day. They were very happy. But one day, Jazz got too excited and tried to catch the bird. The bird was scared and flew away. Jazz was sad because he lost his friend.
