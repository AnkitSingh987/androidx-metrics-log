Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Scared

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. Every night, when it was time for bed, he would hide under his covers and shake. His body felt cold and he did not like it. One day, Tim's mom had an idea. She told Tim to test his bravery. She said, "Tim, let's see if you can stay in your room without being scared." Tim wanted to try, but he was still scared. Tim's mom gave him a big hug and said, "You can do it, Tim!" So, Tim took a deep breath and went to his room. He closed his eyes and counted to ten. When he opened his eyes, he saw that the dark was not so scary after all. Tim was brave and he was not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to study. She would sit at her desk and look at her books every day. One day, as she was studying, she heard a bird outside her window. The bird was singing a pretty song. Lily opened her window and asked the bird, "Why are you singing so happily?" The bird replied, "I am singing because I am flying to the east. My friends are there, and I miss them." Lily felt a little jealous because she wanted to go to the east too. So, Lily and the bird made a plan. The next day, they would both go to the east. Lily was excited and studied her map to find the way. The next morning, Lily and the bird started their journey. They walked, and they sang, and they had a lot of fun. When they reached the east, Lily met the bird's friends and they all played together. Lily was happy she had made new friends and learned that studying can take you to wonderful places.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely doctor. He had no friends, but he liked to study. Every day, he would go to his big room and study about people's insides. One day, he found a special picture called an x-ray. The x-ray showed the insides of a person. The doctor was very happy because he could help people feel better by looking at the x-ray. Soon, many people came to the doctor to get their x-rays. The doctor was not lonely anymore. He had many friends and they all liked him because he made them feel better. The doctor was happy, and he loved to study x-rays every day.

Lily and Tom were bored. They wanted to do something fun. They saw a big bottle of shampoo in the bathroom. They had an idea. "Let's bake a cake with shampoo!" Lily said. "OK!" Tom said. They took the shampoo and some other things from the kitchen. They mixed them in a big bowl. They put the bowl in the oven. They waited for the cake to be ready. But the cake was not good. It was bad. It smelled bad. It looked bad. It made a lot of smoke. The smoke made the fire alarm beep. Mom and Dad came running. They saw the mess. They saw the bad cake. They were angry. "What did you do?" Mom asked. "We baked a cake with shampoo!" Lily and Tom said. "That was a bad idea!" Dad said. "Shampoo is not for baking. Shampoo is for washing your hair. You could have started a fire. You could have hurt yourselves. You need to be careful." Lily and Tom felt sorry. They said sorry to Mom and Dad. They helped to clean up the mess. They learned their lesson. They never baked with shampoo again.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. This rock lived in a beautiful park. Many kids came to play near the rock every day. One sunny day, a little bird came to the rock. The bird said, "Hello, big rock! I can soar high in the sky. Can you soar too?" The rock replied, "No, little bird. I cannot soar like you. I can only stay on the ground." The little bird felt sad for the rock. It thought of a plan to help the rock. The bird asked its friends, a squirrel and a bunny, to help. They all worked together to make the rock feel special. The squirrel and the bunny painted the rock with pretty colors. The little bird sang a happy song. The big rock felt very happy and thanked its new friends. From that day on, the big rock became a special place for all the kids to play. Even though it could not soar, it was loved by everyone.
