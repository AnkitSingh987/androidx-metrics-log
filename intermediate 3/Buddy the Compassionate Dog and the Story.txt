Category: Intermediate
Title: Buddy the Compassionate Dog and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to help people. One day, Buddy saw a boy named Tim trying to tie his shoe. Tim was sad because he could not tie it. Buddy went to Tim and showed him how to tie the shoe. Buddy used his teeth to hold the nail and helped Tim tie his shoe. Tim was very happy and thanked Buddy. From that day on, Tim and Buddy became best friends. They played together and helped each other. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient tree in a big park. The tree had a secret veil hidden in its trunk. Many kids liked to play near the tree, but they did not know about the secret veil. One sunny day, a little girl named Mia and her friend Tom went to the park to play. They decided to play hide and seek near the ancient tree. Tom said, "Mia, I will search for you. You go hide!" Mia smiled and ran to find a hiding place. Mia hid behind the ancient tree and found the secret veil. She put it on and something magical happened! She became invisible. Tom searched and searched, but he could not find Mia. Then Mia took off the veil and appeared in front of Tom. They both laughed and played with the magical veil all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely pirate. He had no friends on his big ship. One day, he saw a pretty box. He wanted to remove the box from the sand. He thought it had treasure inside. He took the box to his ship. He was very excited. He opened the box and found a small cat inside. The cat was scared, but the pirate was kind. The cat started to like the pirate. Now, the pirate was not lonely anymore. He and the cat were best friends. They found more treasure together. They were happy and had lots of fun. The pirate learned that friends are the best treasure.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to bake. One day, she found a magic recipe. Sue was very happy. Sue decided to make something different. She put on her apron and started to bake. She mixed all the ingredients in a big bowl. When she was done, she put the mix in the oven. After a while, the magic cake was ready. Sue shared the cake with her friends. They all loved the different magic cake. Sue was very proud.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy. She liked to explore and try new things. One day, Lucy wanted to find a new way home from the park. She looked around, but didn't know which way to go. This made her feel a bit sad. Then she saw a friendly dog nearby and thought she could ask it for help. She said, "Excuse me, do you know the way home?" The dog could not answer her with words, but it started to wag its tail. Lucy guessed it wanted to show her the way. The dog started walking and Lucy followed it. The dog was very helpful. After a while, they made it home. Lucy was very happy. She thanked the dog and played with it for a bit before they both had to go. The end.

Lily liked to drive her toy car. She had a red car with four wheels and a horn. She drove it around the house and made vroom-vroom noises. She liked to pretend she was going to the park or the store or the zoo. One day, she saw a new toy in the store. It was a bean. It was a big, soft, green bean with a smiley face and arms and legs. It looked very funny and cute. Lily wanted the bean. She asked her mom, "Can I have the bean, please?" Her mom looked at the bean and said, "That is a popular toy, Lily. Many kids want the bean. But you already have a lot of toys. Do you really need the bean?" Lily thought for a moment. She did have a lot of toys. She had her car, and her doll, and her blocks, and her books, and her teddy. But she liked the bean. She said, "Yes, I need the bean. The bean is my friend. I can drive the bean in my car. We can go to the park or the store or the zoo. Please, mom, please." Her mom smiled and said, "Okay, Lily. You can have the bean. But you have to share your toys with other kids, okay? And you have to take good care of the bean. It is a special toy." Lily was very happy. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you, mom. I will share my toys and take care of the bean. I love you, mom. And I love the bean." She took the bean and put it in her car. She drove it around the store and made vroom-vroom noises. She and the bean were very happy. They were the best friends.
