Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Pillow

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved soft things like her teddy bear and warm blankets. One day, Lucy saw a big, soft pillow in her room. She was so happy! Lucy's mommy gave her a chocolate treat. Lucy trusted her mommy, so she knew it would be yummy. She took a small bite of the chocolate and it was so good. Lucy ate the whole chocolate treat and her tummy was happy. Lucy took her soft pillow and her teddy bear to her bed. She felt safe and warm. She knew she could trust her mommy to keep her safe. Lucy closed her eyes and went to sleep, dreaming of more soft things and yummy chocolate treats.

Once upon a time there was a girl who had a special oyster. She was very happy to have her oyster and she wanted to make sure it was safe. So she locked the oyster in a box. The box was very strong. Nothing could get into it, not even air. But the girl wanted to keep her oyster extra safe, so she even locked the lock on the box with a very strong key. The girl was happy that she had done such a good job keeping her oyster safe. She showd it to all her friends and told them how useful this special, locked box was. The girl kept her oyster safe and sound for a long, long time. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat had a big anchor. One day, the boat went to the sea. The sea was full of water and fish. The fish were very happy. A big fish saw the boat. The big fish was graceful. It liked to dance in the water. The big fish wanted to play with the boat. The big fish swam fast and jumped high in the air. The boat liked the big fish. The sky became dark. The wind started to blow. The boat needed to stop. The boat dropped the anchor. The anchor went down, down, down into the water. The anchor hit the bottom of the sea. The boat was safe. The big fish stayed with the boat. They were friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a funny little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside and ask questions about everything. One day, Tim saw a big tent in his yard. He was very excited to see what was inside. Tim ran to his mom and asked, "Mommy, what is the big tent for?" His mom smiled and said, "It's for a special surprise. You'll find out soon!" Tim couldn't wait to see the surprise. Later that day, Tim and his friends went inside the tent. They found lots of toys, games, and yummy snacks. They played and laughed all day long. The tent was the best surprise ever!

Once upon a time, there was a sad spider. The spider was sad because it had no friends to play with. It would try to talk to other bugs, but they were scared of the spider. One day, the spider saw a small bug. The spider said, "Hi, do you want to be my friend?" The bug was not scared and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend!" The spider was so happy that it had a new friend to play with. After a long day of playing, the spider and the bug were very tired. They found a soft leaf to sleep on. The spider was not sad anymore because it had a friend to sleep with. They slept all night and had many more fun days together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a large pile of clay near the sandbox. He was very excited and wanted to play with it. Tim took some of the clay and started to make a big ball. He rolled it and rolled it, and it got bigger and bigger. Tim was very happy with his large clay ball. Then, Tim saw a little girl named Sue. She was playing with clay too. They decided to play together and make a big clay castle. They had a lot of fun and made new friends.
