Category: Intermediate
Title: The Shark and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big shark. This shark was not like other sharks. He was a harmless shark. He did not want to hurt anyone. He just wanted to play with his fish friends. But the fish were scared of the shark because he was so big. One day, the harmless shark saw a small fish who was not scared. The fish said, "I want to be big like you!" The shark wanted to help the fish. He told the fish to eat more food to increase in size. The fish listened to the shark and ate more and more food. After some time, the fish grew bigger and bigger. But then, something unexpected happened. The fish turned into a shark! Now there were two harmless sharks in the sea. They were both happy and played together all day long. The other fish saw that the new shark was not scary, and they all became friends.

Tim and Lily were best friends. They liked to play together every day. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big red car. They pretended to be famous drivers and raced around the yard. One day, they saw a new boy in the street. He had a shiny blue bike. He rode very fast and made loud noises. Tim and Lily wanted to play with him, but he did not stop. "Who is he?" Tim asked. "I don't know. Maybe he is a famous bike rider," Lily said. "Let's go and talk to him," Tim said. They ran after the boy, but he was too fast. They could not catch him. "Hey, wait for us!" Tim shouted. "Can we play with you?" Lily asked. The boy stopped and looked at them. He had a big smile. "Sure, you can play with me. My name is Ben. What are your names?" he said. "I'm Tim and this is Lily. We like your bike. It is very cool," Tim said. "Thank you. I like your car. It is very fun," Ben said. "Do you want to race with us?" Lily asked. "Yes, I do. But you have to believe in yourself. You can be as fast as me if you try hard," Ben said. "OK, we will believe in ourselves. Let's race!" Tim said. They lined up their bike and car at the start. They counted to three and zoomed away. They had a great race. They laughed and cheered. They became friends. They played together every day. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat yogurt. One day, Tim saw his friend, Jane, eating yogurt too. Tim wanted to have fun, so he said, "Let's see who can eat yogurt faster!" Jane liked the idea, and they started their competitive yogurt race. They both ate and ate, trying to be the fastest. Tim was winning, but then he dropped his yogurt on the floor. Tim started to weep because he was sad about his yogurt. Jane stopped eating and looked at Tim. She said, "Don't cry, Tim. We can share my yogurt." Tim stopped weeping and smiled at Jane. They both ate Jane's yogurt together and had a lot of fun. From that day on, Tim and Jane became best friends who loved to eat yogurt together. They learned that sharing was more fun than competing. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Grace. Grace saw a newspaper on the table that her mom had been reading. She picked it up and displayed it to her mom proudly. "Mommy, look What I found!" Grace said. Her mom smiled. "It's a newspaper, sweetheart. Where did you find it?" "I found it on the table," Grace said. But suddenly, Grace dropped the paper and started to cry. Her mom saw that she was hurt by something she read in the newspaper. "What's wrong, my baby?" her mom asked as she hugged her. "Mrs. Bossini is in the newspaper," Grace said sadly. Her mom quickly looked through the paper until she found an article about the special lady. Mrs. Bossini was hurt in a car accident last week. "Oh dear," her mom said, feeling hurt for Mrs. Bossini. She turned to Grace and said, "Remember, sweetheart, there's nothing more powerful than love and the power of healing." Grace hugged her mom tightly and smiled. With her mom's love, she knew that Mrs. Bossini would be OK soon.
