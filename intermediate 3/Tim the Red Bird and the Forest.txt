Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Red Bird and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little red bird named Tim. Tim was a different bird because he had a very long beak. Every day, Tim would fly around the forest and look for food. One day, while he was flying, he saw a big tree with a hole in it. Tim thought, "I wonder what is inside the hole." Tim flew to the hole and looked inside. He found a little blue egg. "Hello, egg," said Tim. "Where is your mom?" The egg did not say anything. Tim felt sad for the egg and decided to help it. He picked up the egg with his long beak and flew away to find the egg's mom. As Tim was flying, he saw a big bird with a long neck. "Excuse me," said Tim. "Is this your egg?" The big bird looked at Tim and the egg. "No, that is not my egg," said the big bird. "But I know where the egg's mom is." Tim was happy and asked the big bird to show him the way. They flew together to a tall tree where the egg's mom was waiting. When they reached the tree, the egg's mom saw her egg and was very happy. "Thank you for returning my egg," she said to Tim. "You are a good bird." Tim felt proud and said goodbye to the egg and its mom. From that day on, Tim and the big bird became best friends. And the forest was filled with wonder and joy.

Once upon a time there was a very troubled little girl. Her name was Lizzie and she had a lot of problems understanding things. One day, her parents took her to see a patient doctor. Lizzie was worried about what the doctor was going to say - but the doctor was kind. Doctor: "Hi Lizzie, how are you today?" Lizzie: "I don't understand." Doctor: "It's okay, I'm here to help. I'm going to help you understand things better." The doctor explained things to Lizzie slowly and in simple words. He was very patient and soon Lizzie was able to understand. The doctor smiled and said, "Now you understand - that's great!" Lizzie was so happy and the doctor was really proud of her. After that, things got much better for Lizzie - she was no longer troubled and she was able to understand things much more easily.

Once upon a time, there was a little caterpillar named Cathy. Cathy lived in a big green tree. She had many friends. One day, Cathy met a new friend, a tiny ant named Andy. Andy was very small but he was ready to gain new friends. "Hi!" said Andy. "Do you want to be my friend?" Cathy smiled and said, "Yes! Let's be friends!" Cathy and Andy played together all day. They had so much fun. They were very happy to gain a new friend. And that is how Cathy the caterpillar and Andy the ant became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw. One day, he could not find any paper. Tim felt sad. He wanted to draw a big sun with a happy face. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, I need paper. Can you provide paper for me?" Mom looked at him and said, "Yes, Tim. I will provide paper for you." She gave Tim a big stack of paper. Tim was very happy. Tim started to draw. It was difficult at first, but Tim did not give up. He drew a big sun with a happy face. He showed his mom the drawing. Mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! I am proud of you." Tim felt happy and proud. From that day on, Tim kept drawing and never gave up, even when it was difficult.

One day, a little girl named Sue found an envelope on the floor. She felt an urge to open it. She tried and tried, but she felt frustrated because it was hard to open. She ran to her mom for help. Her mom opened the envelope for her. Inside, there was a note. Sue could not read, so her mom read it to her. The note said, "Surprise! You get a fun toy!" Sue was very happy and excited. But then, something unexpected happened. Sue's mom turned the note around and saw more words. The words said, "Just kidding! No toy for you!" Sue was sad, but then she and her mom laughed. They knew it was just a silly joke.
