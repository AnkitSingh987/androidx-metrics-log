Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Rock

Once upon a time, in a small camp, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to collect rocks. One day, while she was collecting rocks, she found a mysterious big rock. It was shiny and had many colors. Lucy was very happy and excited. Lucy showed the mysterious rock to her friend, Max. Max said, "Wow, that is a cool rock. Where did you find it?" Lucy told him where she found the rock, and Max wanted to see if he could find one too. Max and Lucy went back to the spot where Lucy found the mysterious rock. They started to collect more rocks, but then something unexpected happened. The big rock started to glow and shake. Max and Lucy were very surprised. The mysterious rock opened up, and a tiny fairy came out. She said, "Thank you for finding my home. I was lost, but now I'm safe." Lucy and Max couldn't believe their eyes. They had found a fairy! From that day on, Lucy and Max were friends with the fairy. They continued to collect rocks together, and the fairy showed them more magical things in the camp. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Amy. One day, Amy's mom asked her to pack a bag for a trip. Amy was very happy to go on a trip. She put her clothes, toys, and a big mug in the bag. The mug was her favorite because it had a picture of a cat on it. On the way to their trip, they stopped at a park. Amy wanted to have a drink from her cat mug. But when she opened her bag, the mug was gone! She was very sad and did not know what to do. Amy and her mom looked everywhere in the park for the lost mug. Then, a kind squirrel came up to Amy. The squirrel had found her cat mug! The squirrel gave the mug back to Amy. She was very happy and thanked the squirrel. The trip became even more fun because of the unexpected help from the kind squirrel. And Amy never forgot to pack her favorite mug again.

One day, a cop named Ben went for a walk. He was a careful cop. He liked to keep the town safe. As he walked, he saw a cat up in a tree. The cat was scared and did not know how to get down. Ben said, "Don't worry, little cat. I will help you." He climbed the tree very carefully. The cat was happy to see Ben. But then, Ben had to sneeze. "Achoo!" he sneezed loudly. The cat was scared by the sneeze and jumped to Ben. Ben held the cat tight and climbed down the tree. The cat was safe now. Ben said, "Next time, be more careful, little cat." The cat meowed and went on its way. Ben was happy that he could help.

One sunny day, Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park to have some fun. Tim loved to run and play with Spot. They played catch with a red ball. Tim threw the ball far away, and Spot ran fast to catch it. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. "More, more!" Tim demanded. Spot wagged his tail and looked guilty, as he had lost the ball. Tim and Spot looked around the park to find the ball. They walked near the big tree and saw a little girl, Sue, holding the red ball. "Can we have our ball back, please?" Tim asked. Sue smiled and said, "Yes, but can I play with you too?" Tim and Spot agreed, and they all played catch together. They had lots of fun, and no one felt guilty anymore. The sun shone brightly, and it was a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a pale girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily was a kind girl with a big spirit. She loved to play with her toys and hold her teddy bear. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a little boy who was sad. Lily asked the boy, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." Lily wanted to help the boy. She looked around the park and found the toy under a tree. She picked it up and held it tight. She gave the toy back to the boy and said, "Here is your toy!" The boy smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily!" Lily and the boy played together at the park. They held hands and ran around, laughing and having fun. The boy's spirit was happy again, all thanks to Lily's kindness. From that day on, Lily and the boy became best friends.
