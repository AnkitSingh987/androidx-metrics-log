Category: Intermediate
Title: Dare the Bunny and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Dare. He had very big ears. Dare loved to listen to the sounds around him with his big ears. One day, Dare was hopping in the forest when he saw a sad bird. The bird was crying. Dare asked, "Why are you sad, bird?" The bird said, "I lost my nest and can't find it." Dare wanted to help the sad bird. He used his big ears to listen for the sound of the bird's nest. Dare hopped around the forest, listening very carefully. Soon, he heard a sound that he thought might be the bird's nest. Dare followed the sound and found the bird's nest high up in a tree. The sad bird was so happy to see her nest! She thanked Dare for helping her. From that day on, Dare and the bird were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Blink. Blink lived in a wood with many trees. He had a lot of friends, like the birds, the bees, and the ants. One day, Blink wanted to play a game with his friends. He thought of an easy game they could all play together. The game was called "Blink and Seek". Blink would close his eyes and count, while his friends would hide. When Blink finished counting, he would open his eyes and look for his friends. He found the birds in the trees, the bees near the flowers, and the ants under a rock. They all laughed and played the easy game until the sun went down. Then, they said goodnight and went to their homes in the wood. Blink and his friends had a very fun day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to his friend's house. His friend, Sara, had a big room with a long curtain. Tim and Sara liked to play hide and seek. They would hide behind the curtain and try to find each other. Tim asked Sara, "Can we play hide and seek again?" Sara said, "Yes, let's play!" So they started the game. Tim hid behind the curtain first. Sara looked for him and found him quickly. Then, it was Sara's turn to hide. Sara hid behind the curtain, but Tim could not find her. He looked and looked. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise. The curtain fell down, and Sara was hurt. Tim ran to her and asked, "Are you okay?" Sara said, "Yes, I am fine. But the curtain scared me." They told Sara's mom about the curtain. She fixed it and made sure it was safe. After that, Tim and Sara played a different game. They learned to be more careful when playing near the curtain.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, brown dictionary. He wanted to paint the dictionary with his bright colors. He took out his paint and brush and started to paint the dictionary. Tim's friend, Sue, came over and saw what he was doing. She said, "Tim, why are you painting the dictionary? We need it to learn new words!" Tim looked at her and said, "But I want to make it pretty with my colors." Sue thought for a moment and then said, "Why don't we paint a nice picture on a paper and put it on the dictionary? That way, it will be pretty and we can still use it to learn new words." Tim liked the idea and they both painted a beautiful picture together. They put the picture on the dictionary, and it looked very pretty. Tim and Sue were both happy and they learned new words from the brown dictionary.

One day, a boy named Tom went to play by a big structure. The structure was made of blocks. Tom liked to play there every regular day. Tom saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a cup. She started to pour water on the structure. Tom got mad. "Stop pouring water on my structure!" he said. Sue did not stop. Tom had an idea. He went to get his own cup. He poured water on the structure too. The structure got bigger and bigger. It turned into a big pool. Tom and Sue were surprised. Tom and Sue played in the pool. They became friends. They learned that playing together was more fun.
