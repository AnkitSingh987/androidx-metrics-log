Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Puddle

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk with her mom. The sky was dark, and Lily thought it might rain. Her mom said, "Lily, let's bring an umbrella just in case." As they walked, Lily saw her friend, Tim. Tim had a big, red ball. He threw the ball high in the sky, and it started to fall. Lily ran to catch it, but she was not successful. The ball fell into a big puddle, and Lily got wet. Just then, it started to rain. Lily's mom opened the umbrella, and they all stood under it. Tim said, "Thank you for bringing the umbrella!" They all laughed and stayed dry, even though the ball was still wet.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to go for a walk with his dog, Spot. They loved to walk in the park and play together. One day, Tim and Spot were walking when Spot started to walk slow. Tim did not understand why Spot was walking slow. He looked at Spot's paw and saw that it was hurt. Tim felt sad for his friend. Tim took Spot home and put a bandage on the hurt paw. Spot felt better and wagged his tail. Tim understood that he needed to be careful with Spot's paw. They waited until Spot's paw was not hurt anymore, and then they went for a walk in the park again.

Once upon a time, in a small farm, there was a loud hen. The loud hen had many eggs. She liked to scatter her eggs in the soft grass. She was very happy when she saw her eggs. One day, a little boy came to the farm. He saw the loud hen and her eggs. The little boy wanted to help the loud hen. He picked up the eggs and put them in a safe place. The loud hen was so happy. She clucked and danced around the little boy. They became good friends. The little boy came to the farm every day to help the loud hen with her eggs. They played and laughed together, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to imagine things. One day, he found a cane in his room. It was a big cane. Tim thought it was magic. Tim went to his friend, Sam. "Look what I found!" said Tim. "I think it is magic!" Sam looked at the cane and said, "Let's try it!" So, they went outside to play with the cane. They played with the cane all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The cane broke! Tim felt very sorry. He said to Sam, "I am sorry. The cane was not magic." They were sad and went home.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a brave little rabbit. The rabbit had a special power. When he would snap his fingers, he could make things grow. He loved to help his friends in the forest by making their food grow big and fresh. One day, the rabbit saw a tiny, sad plant. He wanted to help the plant grow, so he snapped his fingers. But, instead of growing, the plant got smaller! The rabbit was very confused. He tried to snap his fingers again, but the plant got even smaller. The rabbit felt sad because he could not help the plant. Then, a wise old owl came to the rabbit. The owl told the rabbit that sometimes, it is okay to ask for help. The rabbit listened and asked his friends to help the plant grow. They all worked together and soon, the plant was big and strong. The rabbit learned that even with his power, it is good to ask for help.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She felt miserable because it was raining outside. Lucy wanted to play in the sunny park. Lucy's mom saw her sad face and said, "Let's make a yummy snack to cheer you up!" Lucy smiled and they went to the kitchen. They took out bread and a jar of berry jam. Mom helped Lucy spread the jam on the bread. "Yummy," said Lucy, "I love berry jam!" They ate their snack together, and Lucy felt happy. Later, the sun came out, and Lucy went to play in the park. She saw her friend Tom and told him about the snack. "I want to try that too!" said Tom. Lucy and Tom played all day, and they were not miserable anymore.
