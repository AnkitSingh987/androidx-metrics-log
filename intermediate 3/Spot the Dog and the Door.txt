Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a hurt leg. It made him feel uncomfortable when he tried to run and play with his friends. Spot was sad because he wanted to play with his friends all day. One day, Spot saw a big door. He heard his friends playing on the other side. Spot wanted to play too, so he pushed the door with his good leg. The door shut behind him. Spot was happy to see his friends. "Hi, Spot!" said his friend, Bunny. "We missed you! Are you okay?" Spot nodded and said, "My leg hurts, but I want to play." Bunny and the other friends were careful to play gently with Spot, so he didn't feel uncomfortable. They all had a great time playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a mommy who wanted to make a pie. She wanted it to be special, so she measured out the ingredients very carefully. She put the pie in the oven and it cooked until it was golden and yummy-smelling. Meanwhile, the mommy and her baby went outside to play. It was cold, so the baby put on his warm coat. When the pie was done, the mommy and baby came back inside. The pie was hot and yummy. The mommy took it out of the oven, and the baby said, "Is it for me?" The mommy smiled and said, "Yes, it is for you!". The baby was so excited that he clapped his hands. The mommy put the pie on the table, and said, "Let's measure the pie before we eat it." Together, the mommy and baby measured the pie. It was round, and just the right size. Then they ate the pie, and it was so yummy and sweet. The mommy and baby ate the pie until it was all gone. The mommy and baby were both so happy and full of yummy pie. They hugged and said, "Yum yum- the yummiest pie ever!"

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a kind and humble girl named Lily. She had a pretty sweater that she loved to wear. It was soft, warm, and had a big heart on the front. One day, Lily saw a friend named Sam who looked cold. She wanted to help him. So, she took off her pretty sweater and said, "Here, Sam, I give you my sweater. It will keep you warm." Sam smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily, for the nice sweater. I feel warm and happy now." They played together all day, and Lily felt happy too, because she had helped her friend. From that day on, Lily and Sam were the best of friends. They learned that giving can bring joy and warmth, just like a pretty sweater.

One day, a little red ball was rolling down the street. It went past a big tree and a small dog. The red ball was looking for a friend to play with. Soon, the red ball saw a little girl named Sue. Sue saw the red ball and said, "Hi, red ball! Do you want to play with me?" The red ball was happy and said, "Yes, let's play!" So, Sue and the red ball played together. They had a lot of fun. But then, Sue had to go home. She told the red ball, "I have to go now, but I will come back tomorrow to play." The red ball said, "I will wait here for you." The next day, Sue came back and saw the red ball waiting by the big tree. She was so happy to see her friend again. Sue ran up to the red ball and gave it a big kick. They played together all day long, and the red ball was very happy to have a friend like Sue.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a mysterious box under a big tree. Mia was very curious. She wanted to know what was inside the box. She opened the box and found a beautiful peach. The peach was big and looked very yummy. Mia was so happy. She decided to skip all the way home to show her mom the peach. She skipped and skipped, holding the peach in her hands. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Mia felt so lucky to find the peach. When Mia got home, she showed the peach to her mom. Her mom was surprised and happy too. They decided to share the peach for dinner. They ate the peach and it was the sweetest peach they ever had. Mia and her mom smiled and knew that it was a very special day.
