Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fast Rabbit and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a fast rabbit. He liked to run and play with his friends. One day, he found a shiny knife in the woods. He picked it up and showed it to his friend, the squirrel. "Look what I found!" said the rabbit. "I can cut things now!" The squirrel said, "Be careful! A knife can hurt you or others. We should value being safe and kind." One day, while playing, the rabbit tripped and the knife flew out of his paws. It landed in a tree, high up. The rabbit and squirrel were sad. They thought they lost the knife forever. Suddenly, a friendly bird picked up the knife with its beak and brought it down to them. The rabbit and squirrel thanked the bird. They learned that it is important to value friends and be careful with things that can hurt others.

Once upon a time there was a girl. She loved sticks. She would take them with her everywhere she went. She wondered what it would be like to find the perfect stick one day. One day, the girl was walking through the forest and she spotted the most perfect stick she had ever seen. It was so straight and just the right size! She couldn't believe her luck and quickly picked it up. The girl took the stick back home with her and played with it all day long. She liked it so much that she decided it would never leave her side. Little did the girl know, her stick had magical powers. Every time the girl hugged or kissed it, it made her happy. She felt so special and content within her heart. But one day the girl went out and the stick became lost. Everywhere she went, the girl searched, but sadly, the stick was nowhere to be found. The girl was so sad. She missed her perfect stick so much and wished that she had never let it out of her sight. Fortunately, one day she spotted it in a bush by the side of the road. She hugged it tightly and never let it go again. She was so happy to have it back.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny who loved to hop. One day, the bunny went on a wild adventure. He hopped and hopped all day long. He took big steps and small steps, hopping so far away from his home. As the bunny hopped, he saw a big, wild forest. He thought it would be fun to hop and step inside. The bunny hopped into the wild forest, and the trees grew tall and dark. But soon, the little bunny was lost. He hopped and hopped, trying to find his way back home. The wild forest was too big and dark for him. He could not find his way out. The little bunny was sad and scared, and he never found his way back home.

One day, a clown came to town. He had a big red nose and funny shoes. The clown wanted to make everyone laugh. He went to the park to find kids to play with. At the park, the clown saw a little girl named Amy. Amy was sad. The clown wanted to make Amy happy. He said, "Look at me, Amy! I can do easy tricks!" The clown made a funny face, and Amy started to laugh. Just then, a big wind came and blew the clown's hat away. The clown was sad now. Amy wanted to help. She said, "Don't worry, clown! I can make easy tricks too!" Amy did a silly dance, and the clown started to laugh. They both laughed and played together, and they were happy again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small blue bird. The blue bird loved to speak to his friends. Every day, he would fly around and talk to all the animals in the forest. One day, the blue bird found a small stream. The stream was very pretty, with clear water and lots of fish. The blue bird liked to sit by the stream and speak to the fish. The fish would jump up and down, and the blue bird would laugh. The blue bird and the fish became very good friends. They would play together by the stream every day. The blue bird was very happy, and so were the fish. And they all lived happily ever after.
