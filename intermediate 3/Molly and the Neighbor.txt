Category: Intermediate
Title: Molly and the Neighbor

Molly wanted to buy candy at the store, but she didn't have any money. She had a penny, but it was her last one. She got so excited when she found a shiny penny on the ground. Then, she saw her neighbor. "I'm so lucky!" Molly said. The neighbor was confused. He asked her why she was so lucky. Molly explained that she was lucky because she found a penny. Then she could buy candy. The neighbor felt bad for Molly. He gave her five pennies. Molly was so happy. She hugged the neighbor and thanked him. But then the neighbor tried to take the shiny penny from Molly. Molly got mad. She slapped the neighbor's hand. The neighbor was surprised and confused. Molly had a determined look on her face. "This penny is mine!" she said. The neighbor smiled and nodded. He handed Molly all of the pennies. Molly celebrated by going to the store to buy her favorite candy.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty car. It was a bright red car with four sparkly wheels and two happy eyes. The car was so strong and fast that it could race around the world. One day, the mighty car was so hungry that it started to chew on leaves and twigs. After a while, it had chewed so much, its stomach started to hurt. "Oh dear, what can I do?!" thought the car. Suddenly, it had an idea. It raced around its garden, searching for something to eat. It soon spotted a big, juicy apple. The car quickly guided itself over to it and started to chew. The apple was so tasty, it made its hunger go away. "That was mighty delicious!" said the car, feeling much happier. The car continued to chew on everything it could find. From apples to leaves and twigs, it ate them all! Its tummy was nice and full.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys and books. One day, they find a razor in the bathroom. They do not know what it is for. "Look, Sam, what is this?" Tom asks, holding the razor. "I don't know, Tom. Maybe it is a toy. Let me see." Sam says, reaching for the razor. "No, it is mine. I found it first." Tom says, pulling the razor away. They start to fight over the razor. They do not see their mom coming. "Boys, what are you doing with that razor? That is not a toy. That is for shaving. It is very sharp and dangerous. You could hurt yourself or each other. Give it to me right now." Mom says, taking the razor from them. "Sorry, mom. We did not know. We were curious." Tom and Sam say, looking down. Mom hugs them and says, "It is okay to be curious, but you have to be careful. Some things are not for children. If you want to play with something, you can ask me or dad first. Now, why don't you go to your room and print some pictures with your crayons? That is a fun and safe activity." "Okay, mom. We will do that. Thank you." Tom and Sam say, smiling. They go to their room and print some pictures with their crayons. They print a house, a car, a dog, and a flower. They are happy and proud of their work. They show their mom and dad their pictures. Mom and dad praise them and hang their pictures on the fridge. Tom and Sam hug their mom and dad and say, "We love you." Mom and dad say, "We love you too." They are a happy family.

One day, Bob was playing with his trumpet. It made a loud and sweet noise. His little sister, Jill, wanted to join in and asked Bob if she could play with him. "No way," said Bob. "This is my trumpet - you can't play it!" Jill was sad. She didn't have a trumpet, so she couldn't have any fun with Bob. But then Jill had an idea. She asked her brother if she could pretend to have a trumpet. "Oh, OK," said Bob. So Jill and Bob started to play pretend. Jill pretended to blow into her pretend trumpet and made sweet noises like Bob. But then something bad happened. Bob pushed Jill and her pretend trumpet flew across the room. Jill started to cry. Bob felt terrible. He had spoiled their game of pretend and made his little sister cry.
