Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Robot

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy did not like to do her tasks. She would always say, "I hate tasks!" One day, her mom gave her a modern toy to play with. The toy was a robot that could help her with her tasks. Amy was very happy to have the robot, and she played with it all day. The robot helped her with her tasks, and she did not have to do them herself. But one day, something unexpected happened. The robot stopped working! Amy was sad and did not know what to do. Amy's mom saw her sad face and said, "Amy, sometimes we have to do things we do not like. It is a part of life." Amy thought about it and decided to try doing her tasks without the robot. To her surprise, she found that she was good at doing her tasks! She learned that it is important to try things even if we think we will not like them. And that was the day Amy learned the value of doing her tasks.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable little ant named Andy. Andy lived in a big tree with his family. He had a job to find food for everyone. One day, Andy saw a cobweb near the tree. He thought it was pretty. Andy wanted to show the cobweb to his friends. He told them to hurry because he found something nice. When they saw the cobweb, they were scared. They thought it was a trap from a big spider. But then, a tiny, friendly spider came out. The spider said he made the cobweb for fun. The ants and the spider became friends. They played together every day. The ants learned that not all spiders were scary.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball all day long. One day, while playing, he saw a note on the ground. The note had a picture of a big, red ball. Spot wanted that ball very much. Spot showed the note to his friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue said, "I want to play with the big, red ball too!" They decided to look for the ball together. They walked and walked, but they could not find the big, red ball. Finally, they saw the big, red ball in a tree. Spot and Sue both wanted to play with it, but they didn't know how to get it down. Spot had an idea. He said, "Sue, help me push this big rock. We can make the ball fall!" They pushed the rock together, and the big, red ball fell down from the tree. Spot and Sue were so happy. They played with the big, red ball all day long, and they shared it like good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Max. He was three years old and the best three year old ever! Max always loved to go to the theater and watch the show, and he was so excited when his parents took him to see a new show. But as soon as they stepped into the theater. Max heard a loud shout. It was coming from the stage! He ran up to the front and saw a really crazy man dancing around and shouting. Max's mom and dad told him that the show was over and it was time to go. But Max didn't want to leave. He wanted to stay and watch the show. He started shouting as loud as he could. His parents got so mad and dragged him away from the theater, quickly. Now, Max was all alone with no show and no theater, so he started to cry. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a famous tree. This tree was very big and very old. It had many branches and on these branches, there were many fruits hanging. The fruits were red, yellow and green. Everyone loved the famous tree and its fruits. One sunny day, a little girl named Sally went to the famous tree. She looked up and saw a big red fruit hanging from a branch. Sally wanted the fruit, but it was too high for her to reach. She jumped and tried to grab it, but she couldn't. Sally asked her friend Tom for help. Tom came and said, "Let's work together to get the fruit!" Sally smiled and nodded. Tom lifted Sally up on his shoulders, and now she could reach the fruit. Sally picked the fruit from the tree and they both laughed. They shared the tasty fruit and felt happy. From that day on, Sally and Tom were the best of friends.
