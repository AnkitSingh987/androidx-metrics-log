Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Smart Dog and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Max. Max loved to play and have fun. One day, while playing in the park, Max found a big wreck. The wreck was a big pile of toys and balls. Max had an idea. He decided to collect all the toys and balls from the wreck. He picked up each toy and ball with his mouth and put them in a big pile. Max was very happy with his new collection. Soon, all the kids in the park saw Max's collection. They came to play with Max and his toys. Everyone had a great time, and Max made many new friends. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy whip that he loved to play with. One day, Tim's whip became very dirty. He wanted to clean it, but he didn't know how. So, he asked his mom for help. His mom said, "I will help you clean the whip, Tim." Together, they went to the sink and started to wash the dirty whip. But something unexpected happened! The whip started to wiggle and move in the water. Tim and his mom were very surprised. They didn't know that the whip could do that. Then, the whip turned into a little snake! Tim and his mom were scared at first, but the snake was friendly. The snake said, "Thank you for cleaning me. I was a whip, but now I am a snake again!" Tim and his mom were happy that they could help the snake. From that day on, Tim had a new friend, the snake, and they played together all the time.

One day, a black cat walked into a big room. The cat had a long neck. It saw a paper on the floor. The cat wanted to fold the paper. So, it used its paws to fold the paper. It made a pretty bird with the paper. The cat was very happy. Then, a little girl came into the room. She saw the black cat and the pretty paper bird. The girl was happy too. She picked up the bird and played with it. The cat watched her and felt good. But then, something unexpected happened. The pretty paper bird started to move. It jumped from the girl's hand and flew around the room. The girl and the cat were very surprised. They did not know that the paper bird could fly. They watched the bird fly and laughed. The black cat, the little girl, and the paper bird all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a car named Tim. Tim wanted to become a good taxi. He wanted to help people go to places they needed to be. One day, Tim met a big yellow taxi named Tom. Tom was a good taxi too. Tom told Tim, "You can become a good taxi if you work hard and help people." Tim listened to Tom and worked very hard. He helped people go to the store, the park, and the doctor. Soon, Tim became a good taxi just like Tom. Everyone loved Tim the taxi. They knew he would always help them get where they needed to go. Tim was very happy to be a good taxi and help people every day.

Max was feeling bored. He wanted something to do that was fun. Then he had an idea. He would have a matching game! Max grabbed some cards with different shapes on them and laid them out on the floor. He looked at them and said,"I'm ready for my match game!" Max soon realized that some shapes matched and some didn't. After a few minutes, he had successfully matched all of the shapes. Max was so excited that he had been successful. He knew he would not get bored if he could match the shapes again. Then he thought of something else he could match. Max ran to the kitchen and grabbed some salt shakers. They each had two different pictures on them. He thought to himself,"I can match these pictures too!" So Max started to match the pictures on the salt shakers. After a few minutes, he had successfully matched them all. Max had lots of fun playing his matching game. He was happy that he had been so successful. With a big smile on his face, he said,"That was fun!"
