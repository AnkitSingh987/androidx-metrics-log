Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was a very adventurous girl. She loved to play outside and make new friends. One day, Mia heard about a big party happening in the park. She was very excited and wanted to go. On the way to the party, Mia saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play, but Mia was scared. She did not know what to do. The dog barked and Mia wanted to get away. She decided to be brave and face the dog. Mia walked up to the dog and said, "Hi, I want to go to the party. Can you please let me go?" The dog stopped barking and wagged its tail. It was a friendly dog! Mia and the dog became friends and went to the party together. They had lots of fun and Mia learned that sometimes being adventurous means facing your fears.

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to sleep a lot. One day, while Fluffy was sleeping, a little bird came to visit. The bird was very persistent and wanted to play with Fluffy. The bird said, "Wake up, Fluffy! Let's play!" Fluffy opened her eyes and saw the bird. She was not happy to be woken up from her sleep. But the bird was so persistent, Fluffy thought, "Maybe I should play with the bird for a bit." So, Fluffy and the bird played together all day. As the day went on, Fluffy started to get tired. She remembered how much she liked to sleep. The bird saw that Fluffy was tired and said, "Fluffy, you can go to sleep now. I will let you rest." Fluffy was so happy to hear this. She thanked the bird and went back to sleep. From that day on, Fluffy and the bird became the best of friends, and they always played together when Fluffy was not sleeping.

One day, a boy named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big pole in the park. Tom wanted to climb the pole. He tried and tried, but he could not climb it. An honest dog named Ben saw Tom. Ben said, "Tom, I can help you climb the pole." Tom was happy. He did not know that dogs could talk. Ben told Tom to hold on to his tail. Tom held Ben's tail and Ben helped him climb the pole. When they were at the top, something strange happened. The pole started to move! It was not a pole, but a tall giraffe! The giraffe said, "Hello, I am Gigi!" Tom and Ben were surprised. They had a fun time with their new friend Gigi.

Once upon a time, there was a big ranch. On the ranch, there were many animals. There were cows, horses, and pigs. They all played together and had fun. One day, a sour lemon came to the ranch. The lemon wanted to play with the animals too. But the animals did not like the sour lemon. They thought it was too sour to play with them. The sour lemon felt sad. It wanted to find a friend. So, the lemon decided to mail a letter. The letter asked for a friend to come and play. Soon, a sweet orange came to the ranch. The sweet orange and the sour lemon became good friends. They played with the animals every day, and everyone was happy.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to pack a bag for a trip. She put her toys, clothes, and a big ball of dough in the bag. Sue loved to play with dough and make fun shapes. On the way, Sue met a naughty dog named Max. Max said, "Can I play with your dough, please?" Sue said, "Yes, but only if you are nice." Max started to play with the dough, but he was naughty and made a mess. Sue was sad and said, "Max, you were naughty and now my dough is gone." Max felt bad and said, "I am sorry, Sue. I will help you find more dough." They looked everywhere, and finally found a big, new ball of dough under a tree. It was a surprise gift from Max's mom. Sue and Max learned that being nice and saying sorry can make things better. They played with the new dough and had lots of fun together.
