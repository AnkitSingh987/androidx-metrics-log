Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Fireman and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Tom. Tom had a big red truck and a friendly dog. The dog was very obedient. He always listened to Tom. One day, Tom and his dog went to put out a fire. They saw a little girl who needed help. The obedient dog went to the girl and Tom put out the fire. The girl was so happy to be safe. After the fire was out, the girl wanted to own a dog just like Tom's. She asked her mom and dad for an obedient dog. They said yes, and the girl got a dog. The girl, her dog, and Tom the fireman became best friends. They all lived happily ever after.

One day, a jolly dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big lab with a lot of fun things inside. Max wanted to see what was in there, so he went to the door and asked, "Can I come in and play?" A kind man opened the door and said, "Yes, Max! You can come in and play with all the fun things in the lab." Max was so happy and he went inside to play. Max saw a big red ball and ran to play with it. But when he touched the ball, something unexpected happened. The ball turned into a big cake! Max was surprised and looked at the kind man. The man laughed and said, "Surprise, Max! This is a magic lab, and you can turn things into anything you want!" Max had a great time playing in the magic lab and turning things into fun toys and treats.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to play in the park with his friends. One day, he saw a big, shiny stone on the ground. Tom picked it up and showed it to his friends. His friends said, "Tom, that stone is loud! We don't like it." But Tom liked the stone a lot and did not want to let it go. He said, "No, I want to keep it." His friends did not like the loud stone, but Tom insisted on keeping it. Tom played with the stone every day. It was so loud that his friends did not want to play with him anymore. They went to play far away from Tom and his loud stone. Tom was sad and alone. He wished he had listened to his friends and let go of the loud stone.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a helpful little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and help the people in the village. One day, while Spot was playing in the park, he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her toy. Spot wanted to help Lily, so he started to look for her toy. He looked under the trees and behind the bushes. Then, Spot discovered something shiny near the pond. It was Lily's toy! The toy was in the water. Spot jumped into the water and got the toy for Lily. Lily was so happy to see her toy again. She thanked Spot for being so helpful. From that day on, Spot and Lily became the best of friends. They played together every day and always helped each other. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big house with a tall chimney. Inside the house lived a little boy named Tim and his mom. Tim was a happy boy who liked to play and gather toys in his room. One day, Tim noticed a smelly smell coming from the chimney. "Mom, what is that smell?" he asked. His mom sniffed the air and said, "Something is stuck in the chimney, Tim. We need to get it out." Tim and his mom tried to reach the smelly thing in the chimney, but they couldn't. They were sad and didn't know what to do. Then, they heard a knock on the door. It was a friendly man who knew how to clean chimneys. The man climbed up and got the smelly thing out of the chimney. It was a wet, dirty sock! Tim and his mom thanked the man and were happy again. The house smelled nice, and they could enjoy the warm fire in the chimney.
