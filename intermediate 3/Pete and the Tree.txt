Category: Intermediate
Title: Pete and the Tree

Pete was mighty tired from a long day of playing. He wandered away from his home, looking for one last adventure. "Pete!" his mother called, "Where are you? It's time to go home!" But Pete had forgotten all about home! He wandered further and further away, until he came across a giant tree. Pete thought the tree was mighty awesome and climbed up to the top. Suddenly, he heard his mother's voice again. She had followed his tracks and found him in his tree! "Time to get down, Pete. It's way past your bedtime!" Pete was relieved and slid down the tree. He followed his mother home, happy that he wouldn't forget home ever again!

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a tiger named Tom. Tom was a reliable friend to all the animals. He was big and strong, but also kind and gentle. One day, Tom saw a ball stuck in a tree. His friend, a little bird, asked for help. "Tom, can you help me get my ball?" the bird said. Tom wanted to help his friend. He tried to roll on the ground to shake the tree, but the ball didn't fall. Tom didn't give up. He rolled and rolled, faster and faster, until the tree shook so much that the ball fell down. The little bird was so happy. "Thank you, Tom! You are the best friend!" said the bird. And they played with the ball together, rolling it all around the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little puppy named Spot. Spot was a cool puppy with a lot of energy. He loved to run, jump, and play with his friends. One day, Spot wanted to improve his jumping. So, he practiced and practiced. He jumped over a small stick, then a bigger stick, and finally, he jumped over a big log. Spot was very happy that he improved his jumping. He showed his cool jumps to all his friends. They all clapped and cheered for him. And from that day on, Spot was known as the best jumper in the land.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow bear. The bear was so happy that he decided to take a dip in the stream and soak himself. As he soaked himself in the cool water, he said: "Ahhh... this feels so nice!" Just then, a little frog hopped over to him and said: "Why hello there, bear! What are you doing?" The bear replied: "I'm taking a soak in the stream. The water is so refreshing!" The frog replied: "I want to take a soak too! Can I join you?" The bear smiled and said: "Of course you can! Let's go together!" So the yellow bear and the little frog took a nice and refreshing soak in the stream. They both came out feeling relaxed and happy!

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play with blocks. They wanted to build a big tower with all the blocks they had. They worked hard and put one block on top of another, until the tower was taller than them. "Wow, look at our tower!" Lily said. "It is so big and strong!" "Yes, it is!" Tom said. "Let's give it a massage to make it happy!" They rubbed their hands on the tower, gently and softly. They thought the tower liked it. But they did not see that the tower was wet. It had rained before, and the blocks were slippery. Suddenly, the tower wobbled and fell down. It made a loud noise and broke into many pieces. Some of the pieces hit Lily and Tom and hurt them. "Ow, ow, ow!" Lily and Tom cried. "Our tower is gone! And we are hurt!" They ran to their mommies, who hugged them and kissed their boo-boos. They were sad and angry. They did not want to play with blocks anymore. They wished they had never built the tower. They wished they had never given it a massage. They wished it had not been wet.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in his big tent. One day, he went to play in his tent with his toys. But, Tim was so clumsy, he would often forget things. This day, Tim forgot to close the tent door. While he was playing, a funny little dog came into the tent. The dog was playful and wanted to be Tim's friend. Tim was so happy to have a new friend to play with in his tent. As they played, the dog found a toy that Tim had lost a long time ago. Tim was very surprised and happy! The funny little dog had helped him find his lost toy. From that day on, Tim and the dog played in the tent every day, and Tim tried not to forget to close the tent door.
