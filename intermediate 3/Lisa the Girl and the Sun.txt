Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa the Girl and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Lisa found a big pink folder in her room. She did not know what to use it for. Lisa asked her mom, "Mom, what can I use this pink folder for?" Her mom said, "You can use it to keep your drawings safe." Lisa loved to draw. She had many drawings of the sun, her family, and her cat, Tom. The next day, she went to the park with her friends. They all had fun playing and drawing together. Lisa had a feeling that her pink folder would be very important today. At the park, the wind started to blow very strong. Lisa's drawings started to fly away! She was sad and scared. But then she remembered her pink folder. Lisa quickly put all her drawings in the folder. The wind could not take them away now. Lisa's friends were amazed. They said, "Wow, Lisa! Your pink folder saved your drawings!" Lisa smiled and knew that her pink folder was very special. From that day on, she always used her pink folder to keep her drawings safe.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived an honest girl named Sue. Sue was always nice to her friends and never lied. One day, Sue felt the ground shake. She knew it was an earthquake, but it was a small one, so she did not worry. The next day, Sue went to play with her friends. She tried to mention the earthquake to them, but they did not understand. They just laughed and continued to play. Sue felt sad that her friends did not believe her, but she kept playing with them. A few days later, a big earthquake came to the town. The ground shook and the houses fell down. Sue and her friends were very scared. They remembered what Sue had mentioned about the small earthquake, but it was too late. The town was broken, and everyone was sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, orange ball. He loved to play with his ball every day. One day, while playing with his ball, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue had no toys to play with. "Can I play with your ball?" Sue asked Tim. Tim thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, we can share my orange ball." Tim and Sue played with the orange ball all day. They took turns and shared the ball. They became very good friends. At the end of the day, they were both very happy. They knew they would have lots of fun playing together with the orange ball. And from that day on, Tim and Sue always shared their toys and had great fun.

Lily liked to play with her toy gun. She liked to shoot at the birds and the trees and the cars. She pretended she was a brave cowboy like in the movies. She wore a hat and a belt and a badge. One day, she went with her mom to the barber shop. Her mom wanted to cut her hair. Lily did not like the barber. He had a big scissors and a loud machine. He looked scary. Lily wanted to go home. "Hello, little lady," the barber said. "Are you ready for a nice haircut?" "No!" Lily said. She hid behind her mom. She did not want the barber to touch her hair. "Don't be afraid," her mom said. "The barber is very dependable. He will make you look pretty. And he has a surprise for you." Lily did not believe her mom. She did not want any surprise. She held her toy gun tightly. The barber smiled. He took out a box from under his chair. He opened it and showed Lily what was inside. It was a toy horse. It was brown and soft and had a saddle and a mane. "Wow!" Lily said. She forgot about the barber and the haircut. She loved horses. She wanted to play with the toy horse. "This is for you," the barber said. "If you let me cut your hair, you can have it. Do you like it?" "Yes!" Lily said. She ran to the barber and hugged him. She gave him her toy gun. "You can have this. You are a nice barber." The barber laughed. He took the toy gun and put it on his belt. He lifted Lily on his chair. He cut her hair gently and quickly. He gave her a mirror and a ribbon. "You look beautiful," he said. Lily smiled. She looked at her new haircut. She liked it. She thanked the barber. She took her toy horse and hugged it. "Can we come back?" she asked her mom. "Sure," her mom said. "The barber is very dependable. And he has more surprises." Lily was happy. She liked the barber shop. She liked the barber. She liked her toy horse. She did not need her toy gun anymore. She was a brave cowboy. And she had a new friend.
