Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Cake

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to bake a dessert. She asked her mom, "Can we make a cake, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's bake a cake together!" They went to the kitchen and put on their aprons. Lily's apron was gray, and her mom's apron was pink. They mixed the cake ingredients in a big bowl. Lily liked to stir the mix with a big spoon. Her mom helped her pour the mix into a cake pan. They put the cake in the oven to bake. While they waited, Lily and her mom danced and sang in the kitchen. When the cake was ready, they took it out of the oven. It smelled so good! They let the cake cool, then put frosting on it. Lily and her mom enjoyed their delicious dessert together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because his toy was broken. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, my toy is broken. Can you fix it?" His mom looked at the toy and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I can't fix it. But I have an idea! Let's make some popcorn and watch a movie together instead." Tim liked this idea. They went to the kitchen and put the popcorn in the big pot. Tim's mom let him strike the pot to make the popcorn jump. Pop, pop, pop! The popcorn sounded like little fireworks. They sat down to watch the movie, and Tim forgot all about his broken toy. He was happy to eat popcorn and spend time with his mom. From that day on, whenever Tim felt sad, he and his mom would make popcorn and watch a movie together. And every time, it made him feel better.

Once upon a time, there was a little gray dog named Max. Max loved to run and play with his friends in the park. One day, Max saw a big motor in the park. The motor was making a loud noise, and Max wanted to see what it was. Max ran to the motor and saw it was a big gray car. The car was very fast and made Max want to run faster. Max and the car had a race. The car went vroom, vroom, and Max went woof, woof as they raced down the road. In the end, Max and the gray car became good friends. They played and raced together every day. Max was happy to have a new friend who loved to run as much as he did. And they lived happily ever after.

Once there lived a little girl called Lucy. She was three years old and very happy. Every day she played with her toys and laughed until it was time for bed. One day Lucy's mommy said it was time for breakfast. When Lucy walked into the kitchen, she saw a special surprise. On the table was a tube of her favourite cereal, with a charming smiley face on it. Lucy was so excited that she couldn't wait to try it. She opened the tube and poured some cereal into her bowl. She added some delicious milk and stirred it around. Lucy tasted it and smiled. It was just as good as it looked. She enjoyed every bite and was so happy. After breakfast, Lucy's mommy told her to put the tube away. Lucy put it back in the cupboard, happy to have served her special breakfast. She knew she would be back for more soon.

Once upon a time, there was a prince. He lived in a big, big house. The prince had many toys. One day, he found a box. The box was closed. The prince wanted to open the box. The prince tried to open the box. He pulled and pulled. The box did not open. The prince was sad. Then, he had an idea. He got a key. The key was fake, but it looked like a real key. The prince put the fake key in the box. The prince turned the fake key. The box opened! Inside the box, there were more toys. The prince was very happy. He played with the new toys all day. He knew that the fake key helped him open the box. And they all lived happily ever after.
