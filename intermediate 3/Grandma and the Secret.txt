Category: Intermediate
Title: Grandma and the Secret

Grandma was sitting in the living room when she heard little Sarah running down the hall towards her. "Grandma, let's race!" Sarah said excitedly. Grandma laughed. "Sarah, where do you want to race? Around the block?" "No, let's race to the calendar in my room," Sarah said. Grandma smiled. "That sounds like fun! I'll count to three, and then we'll race." Grandma counted and then they raced across the hall. Sarah won! "You're so fast!" Grandma said. Sarah beamed. She pointed to the calendar on the wall. It was a beautiful blue color. "Look, I won the race and now I can put something special on the calendar today," Sarah said. Grandma nodded and laughed. "You certainly can! What are you going to put on the calendar?" Sarah thought for a moment. Then her eyes lit up in excitement. "That's a secret!" she said.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat. The cat liked to play with a birdcage. Inside the birdcage, there was a little bird. The cat and the bird were friends. One day, the cat wanted to play with the bird, but the birdcage door was stuck. The cat tried to open it, but it could not. The cat was sad. It wanted to help the bird. Then, the cat had an idea. It found a paper and used its claws to tear it. The cat put the torn paper under the birdcage door. With a push, the door opened. The bird was free, and they played together. The cat and the bird were happy.

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the pool with their toys. One day, they decided to go on a journey under the water. They put on their goggles and held their breath. "Are you ready, Ben?" Lily asked. "Yes, I am, Lily. You are a dependable friend. You will not let go of my hand, right?" Ben said. "Of course not, Ben. I will always hold your hand. Let's dive together!" Lily said. They dived into the water and saw many colorful fish and plants. They felt like they were in a different world. They smiled at each other and waved to the fish. They had so much fun. But soon, they needed to breathe again. They looked up and saw the sun shining through the water. They let go of each other's hand and swam to the surface. They took a big breath and laughed. "That was an amazing journey, Lily. Thank you for diving with me," Ben said. "Thank you, too, Ben. You are a dependable friend. Let's do it again sometime," Lily said. They hugged and splashed each other. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a fancy prize. It shined bright and was very special. A little girl wanted to win it more than anything else in the world. So, the little girl wished upon a star. The star said she could have the prize if she tasted something very special. The little girl was so excited and wondered what it would be. The star told her to taste the most delicious ice cream in the whole world! The little girl was so excited and tried the ice cream. It was amazing and tasted so yummy! After she tasted the ice cream, the star gave her the fancy prize. The little girl was so happy and hugged the prize close to her. She had what she wished for!

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, distant hill with steam coming from the top. Tim was curious and wanted to see what was making the steam. He walked closer and closer to the hill. As he got near the steam, he saw a small, hungry bird. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he tried to feed it. But, the bird did not want to eat. It was scared of the steam. Tim tried to stop the steam, but he could not. The steam got bigger and bigger. The bird flew away, still hungry and scared. Tim felt very sad, because he could not help the bird or stop the steam. The day did not end well for Tim and the bird.
