Category: Intermediate
Title: The Green Rocket and the Moon

Once upon a time, there was a green rocket. It could fly high up in the sky. It liked to shine bright like the sun. One day, the green rocket went up, up, up. It wanted to touch the moon. But, the rocket saw something big and round. It was not the moon! The big round thing was a giant ball. The green rocket played with the ball. They had fun together. The rocket did not need the moon. It was happy with its new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very intelligent. He liked to play with his toys and look for things in his house. One day, Tim found a big cupboard in his room. He had never seen it before. Tim opened the cupboard and saw many toys inside. He started to play with them. Then, something unexpected happened. The toys began to talk! They said, "Surrender your toys to us, and we will be your friends." Tim thought about it and decided to surrender his toys to the talking toys. But then, the talking toys became his best friends. They all played together every day, and Tim was very happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a funny bird named Bob. Bob lived in a big tree with his friend, a small ant named Tim. They liked to play and talk together. One day, Bob saw a bright light in the sky. He said to Tim, "Look! It's a comet!" Tim looked up and began to panic. "Oh no, what if it comes close to our tree?" Tim asked. Bob tried to calm Tim down, saying, "Don't worry, it's far away." They watched the comet together, and soon Tim stopped panicking. The comet was very pretty and made them both happy. They laughed and played, knowing that their tree was safe. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Lily. Lily loved to play with her soft sheet. She would hide under the sheet and pretend she was invisible. Lily felt safe and happy under her sheet. One sunny day, Lily went to the store with her mom. They needed to pay for some food. Lily saw a toy she liked and asked her mom if she could have it. Her mom said, "If you want the toy, you have to pay for it." Lily was shy, but she wanted the toy very much. She took a deep breath and went to the store lady. Lily gave her the money to pay for the toy. The store lady smiled and said, "Good job, Lily!" Lily felt proud and happy. She went home and played with her new toy under her favorite sheet.

Once upon a time, there was a gray boat. The boat had its own port near the sea. The port was a fun place to be. One day, the gray boat saw a big fish. The boat wanted to play with the fish. They played all day in the sea. At the end of the day, the gray boat went back to its port. The boat was happy to have a new friend. They played together every day at the port.

Once upon a time there was a hungry man who wanted dinner. He searched and searched, but all he could find was a long stick. He decided to make dinner with it. He looked for something else, but all he could find was an old rock. Then he had an idea! He used the stick and the rock to make a fire. With the fire, he thought he could make dinner. He turned the stick into the fire and it started to burn. He waited and waited, but dinner never came. Soon the fire became too big and the man could not turn it off. His dinner was never made and he was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck. The duck lived near a lake. The lake was very big and blue. The duck liked to swim in the lake every day. One day, the duck saw a poor fish. The fish was sad because he was stuck in a net. The fish asked the duck for help. The duck wanted to help the fish. So the duck used its beak to open the net. The duck let the fish go free. The fish was very happy. The fish and the duck became good friends. They played together in the lake every day. And they lived happily ever after.
