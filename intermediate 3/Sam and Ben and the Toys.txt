Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and Ben and the Toys

Sam and Ben are brothers. They like to play with their toys in their room. One day, Sam finds a wallet on the floor. It is brown and has a zipper. He opens it and sees some money and cards inside. "Wow, look what I found!" Sam says to Ben. "This is a wallet. It has money and cards. Money can buy things. Cards can do magic. This is a powerful wallet!" Ben is curious. He wants to see the wallet too. He tries to take it from Sam, but Sam holds it tight. "No, this is mine!" Sam says. "I found it first. You can't have it!" "Yes, I can!" Ben says. "We can share it. It is not fair that you have it all. Give it to me!" They start to pull and tug on the wallet. They are angry and loud. They do not hear their mom coming to their room. "Boys, what is going on here?" their mom asks. She sees the wallet in their hands. She is surprised and worried. "Where did you get this wallet? This is not yours. This is dad's wallet. He lost it this morning. He was very sad. He needs it back." She takes the wallet from them. She tries to explain to them that the wallet belongs to their dad, and that they should not take things that are not theirs. She tells them that money and cards are not toys, and that they are not powerful. She says that they should be sorry and say sorry to their dad. Sam and Ben feel bad. They did not know that the wallet was their dad's. They did not mean to make him sad. They listen to their mom and say sorry to her. They say that they will not take things that are not theirs again. They hug their mom and say that they love her. Their mom smiles and hugs them back. She says that she loves them too. She says that they can play with their toys, but they have to be nice and share. She says that she will give the wallet back to their dad, and that he will be happy. She says that they are good boys, and that they can learn from their mistake. She says that they can ask her or their dad if they have any questions, and that they will explain to them. She says that they are proud of them.

Once upon a time, there was a happy family. Mama and Papa, and two children Jack and Jill. One day, a tornado came into town. Everyone was scared when they saw it. Mama said to Papa: "What should we do?" Papa said: "We will unite and protect our family." Mama was very happy to hear that. She said: "Lets unite. Let's all grab hands." They all joined hands and huddled together. The winds of the tornado were blowing around them, but they stayed calm and united. When the tornado passed, the family was relieved. Jack and Jill were happy to have stayed together during the scary moment. Mama and Papa smiled. They knew that when they unite, they can brave any storm.

Once upon a time, there lived a wise princess. Every day, she would collect twigs, pebbles and leaves. One day, she came across a magical rock. She brought it over to a group of children and said, "Look what I have collected!" The children were so excited. They asked the princess, "What can the rock do?" The princess replied, "This rock is magical. Whoever holds it can make a wish!" The children gathered around the princess and waited for her to hand the rock over to each one of them. They all wished for different things. Once everyone was done wishing, the princess took the rock and said, "I'm glad you all made your wishes. Now it's time to go home and collect more things!"

Once upon a time there was a powerful lawyer. Every day he helped lots of people. He could do lots of hard things with words and make everyone happy. One day, a little boy was sad. He wanted to help but he didn't know how. He asked the lawyer for help. The powerful lawyer smiled as he knew just what to do. He helped the little boy write a letter. The lawyer wrote lots of words, but he explained them all in a way that the little boy could understand. The lawyer used his powerful words to help the little boy. In the end, the lawyer and the little boy were both happy.
