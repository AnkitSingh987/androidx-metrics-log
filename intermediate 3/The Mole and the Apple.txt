Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mole and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a mole who was very intelligent. One day he was walking in a field and he spotted something interesting. So he went up to it to take a closer look. It was a small apple. He picked it up and was about to take a bite of it when he heard a voice. "Don't eat it! That apple is mine!" The mole turned around and saw a little boy. "I'm sorry," the mole said. "I didn't know it was yours." The little boy smiled. "That's okay," he said. "Just don't bite it." The mole smiled too and put the apple down. "Thank you for being so intelligent," the little boy said. The mole nodded and hopped off into the nearby woods. And from then on, the mole was very careful before he took a bite of something.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to bake a cake. She asked her mom, "Can we bake a cake today?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can bake a cake. But first, we need the key to the big cupboard." Sue looked everywhere for the key. She looked under the bed, on the table, and in her toy box. Finally, she found the key in her mom's bag. "I found the key!" Sue said, and she gave it to her mom. Her mom opened the big cupboard and took out all the things they needed to bake the cake. They mixed the cake stuff and put it in the oven. When the cake was ready, they shared it with their family. Everyone said it was the best cake ever. Sue was happy that she could help her mom bake the cake.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big red wagon. He wanted to make something fun with it. Tim thought and thought. He had an idea, but he needed help. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to help him. They worked together to make the wagon enormous. They used big boxes and lots of tape. They painted it with bright colors. They were very excited. The next day, Tim and Sam showed their enormous wagon to all their friends. They all got in and had a big ride. They laughed and had so much fun. The enormous wagon was the best thing they ever made.

Once there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a peaceful town with her mom and dad. Lily loved to play with her toys and run in the park with her friends. One day, her mom asked her to help with something new. "Can you help me mail this letter, Lily?" her mom asked. "We need to put it in the big blue box down the street." Lily said, "Yes, mommy!" She was very excited to help. She took the letter and walked with her mom to the big blue box. They talked and laughed on their way there. When they got to the big blue box, Lily's mom lifted her up so she could put the letter inside. Lily felt proud and happy that she could help her mom. They walked back home together, and Lily couldn't wait to tell her dad about her big adventure.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. She saw a big tree and wanted to gather pretty leaves. She found red, yellow, and green leaves. Lucy was very happy. "Look, Mom!" Lucy said, holding up her leaves. "I want to keep them in a bottle." Her mom gave her a bottle, and Lucy put the leaves inside. She was proud of her leaf collection. Later, Lucy saw a small bug near the tree. She was scared, but she wanted to be brave like her big sister. "Hello, bug," she said, stepping closer. The bug didn't move, so Lucy wasn't scared anymore. She went home with her mom and her bottle of leaves, feeling very brave.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia did not like going to the market with her mom. She would always say, "Mom, I hate the market!" Her mom would smile and say, "But Mia, we need to buy food for our home." One sunny day, Mia and her mom went to the market. The sun was very harsh, but Mia was happy because she had her big hat. At the market, they saw a big, red ball. Mia's eyes grew wide and she asked her mom, "Can I have the ball?" Her mom said, "If you are good at the market, I will buy you the ball." Mia was very good at the market. She helped her mom pick out fruits and vegetables. Suddenly, a big wind came and took Mia's big hat away! Mia was sad and started to cry. Then, something unexpected happened. The big, red ball flew up into the air and blocked the harsh sun. Mia stopped crying and smiled. She said, "Mom, I don't hate the market anymore!" Her mom smiled and said, "I'm glad, Mia. Now we can go home and play with your new ball."
