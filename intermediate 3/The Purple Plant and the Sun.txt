Category: Intermediate
Title: The Purple Plant and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a purple plant. The plant lived in a big garden with lots of other plants. They all liked to play together in the sun. One day, the purple plant felt very tired. It decided to take a nap under a big tree. While it was napping, a big wind came and blew the purple plant far, far away. When the purple plant woke up, it was in a new place. It saw a big blue plant and a big green plant. The purple plant was scared at first, but the blue and green plants were nice. They all became friends and played together in the sun. And that's how the purple plant found a new home with new friends.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent dog named Max. Max loved to search for things. He would search for sticks, balls, and even newspapers. One day, Max saw a newspaper on the ground. He picked it up and took it to his owner, Sam. Sam said, "Good job, Max! You found a newspaper." Max was very happy. He wagged his tail and smiled at Sam. Little did they know, this newspaper had a secret. The next day, Max and Sam played in the park. Max searched for more newspapers. He found one and brought it to Sam. Sam looked at the newspaper and saw a big surprise. The newspaper had a picture of Max on it! "Max, you're in the newspaper!" Sam said. Max wagged his tail even more. He was so proud of himself. The newspaper said that Max was the best searching dog in town. Max and Sam were very happy and played in the park all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max had interesting spots on his skin. He liked to play outside with his friend, a little girl named Lily. One day, Max and Lily were playing catch with a ball. Max would run and catch the ball in his mouth. Lily would laugh and clap her hands. Suddenly, Max saw a butterfly and wanted to chase it. He said to Lily, "Wait, let's pause." Lily looked at the butterfly and smiled. She said, "Okay, let's watch it together." They paused and watched as the butterfly flew around them. It was a fun and special moment for Max and Lily. After the butterfly flew away, they continued playing catch and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a dancer named Lily. Lily loved to dance all day long. One day, Lily had a broken leg. She was very sad because she could not dance. Lily's friend, Tom the cat, saw her and said, "Don't be sad, Lily. I will help you." Tom had an idea. He told Lily to follow him. They went to the park to find a way to fix her broken leg. At the park, they met a wise old owl. Tom asked the owl, "Can you help Lily? Her leg is broken." The owl thought for a moment and said, "I know what to do. Follow me." The owl led them to a magic tree. The tree had special leaves that could make Lily's leg better. Lily, Tom, and the owl took the leaves and put them on Lily's broken leg. Soon, her leg was all better! Lily was so happy. She danced and danced, and Tom and the owl danced with her. They all had a great time, and Lily never forgot her friends who helped her.

Once upon a time, there was a little bee named Buzz. Buzz was a selfish bee. He did not like to share his flowers with the other bees. One day, Buzz was flying around the garden. He saw a big, pretty flower. He wanted it all to himself. Buzz said, "This is my flower! No one can have it!" Another bee, named Bree, heard Buzz. Bree felt sad because she wanted to share the flower. Bree said, "Buzz, we should share the flower. It will be more fun!" Buzz thought about it and decided to pause. He looked at Bree and said, "Okay, let's share the flower." They both shared the flower and had a great time. Buzz learned that sharing is better than being selfish.
