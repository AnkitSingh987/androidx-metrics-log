Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Park

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big lake with ducks and boats. Tim wanted to go on a boat ride. His mom said, "Okay, let's go on a boat ride!" They found a boat with many rows. Tim and his mom sat in the boat. Tim's mom showed him how to pull the long stick to move the boat. They started to row the boat together. As they rowed, the ducks followed them. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. The wind started to blow, and the air got a little bitter. Tim's mom said, "It's getting cold, let's go back." They rowed back to the shore. Tim pulled the boat onto the land. They said goodbye to the ducks and went home to have warm soup.

Once upon a time, in a fair and happy land, there was a little bunny named Lily. Lily loved to hop and play in the warm spring sun. She had many friends, like the birds and the bugs, who also loved the spring. They would play all day long. One day, Lily was playing near a big tree. She heard a small whisper in her ear. It was a tiny bug who lived in the tree. The bug told her that there was a secret place where they could play and have more fun. Lily was excited and wanted to see this secret place. Lily and the bug went to the secret place. It was a beautiful garden with many flowers and butterflies. But then, something unexpected happened. The garden was not a secret place for fun, but a magical place where Lily and her friends could talk like people! They all laughed and talked, and it was the best day ever. The secret garden became their favorite place to play, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a rare mole. He lived in a hole in the ground. The mole was very shy and did not have many friends. He was not like the other moles. He was special. One day, the rare mole went out to play. He saw a big tree. He wanted to touch the tree. So, he touched the tree with his little paw. The tree was very rough and hard. As the rare mole touched the tree, he saw a little bird. The bird was stuck in the tree. The rare mole wanted to help the bird. He climbed the tree and helped the bird get free. The bird was very happy. The rare mole and the bird became good friends. They played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very intelligent. He loved to learn and play with his friends. One day, Tim went to the park with his friends to play. At the park, Tim saw some ice on the ground. He was very careful because he knew ice could be slippery. Tim's friend, Sam, said, "Look, Tim! The ice is sharp. Be careful!" Tim listened to Sam and did not touch the sharp ice. Tim and Sam played together all day. They had lots of fun at the park. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Thank you, Sam, for telling me about the sharp ice. You are a good friend." Sam smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad we could play together safely today." And they went home, happy and safe.

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a daddy. They had a little girl and she was very proud. Her mommy and daddy were proud of her too. One day, the daddy took his staff and went for a walk. He walked for a long time until he saw a waterfall. It was so big and beautiful. He felt very proud. The daddy decided to take a closer look. He carefully stepped over the rocks on the ground until he was at the edge of the waterfall. He was so surprised by how lovely it was. He gave the waterfall a big kiss! When the daddy came back home, the little girl was so happy to see him. She gave him a big hug and said “I am so proud of you!” Then, the daddy gave the little girl a big kiss and they were both very happy.
