Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to grow plants in his backyard. One day, he found a big seed and wanted to see if it would grow. He dug a hole, put the seed in, and covered it with dirt. Tim asked his mom, "Will it grow?" His mom replied, "We will have to wait and see. It's like a test." Tim watered the seed every day, and soon, a tiny plant came out of the ground. One day, Tim saw that the plant was not looking good. It had bad, brown leaves. Tim felt sad. He asked his dad, "What can I do?" His dad said, "Give it more water and love." Tim did just that, and soon, the plant grew big and strong with green leaves. Tim was so happy he passed the test and helped his plant grow.

Pete was walking through the park. He was feeling a bit fearful and wasn't sure why. He stopped walking and paused. He looked around at the trees and spotted a mouse. Pete gasped and stepped away. Jimmy, Pete's friend, saw him and ran over. Jimmy: "What's wrong Pete?" Pete: "I saw a mouse!" Jimmy: "Oh don't be fearful, Pete. Come and have some cheese with me." Pete: "OK!" Jimmy and Pete sat down on the grass and shared some cheese. They laughed and talked until dusk. Afterwards, Pete felt much better and his fear had gone.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Emma and Alex. They were very excited and wanted to spend the day together. So, they headed down to the beach. Before long, they spotted a whale out in the ocean. It was big and pink! The sight of the whale made them so happy. Emma shouted, “Can we spend the day playing with the whale?” Alex replied, “Of course! Let's go!” So, the two of them raced across the beach and hopped into the water. They splashed and played with the whale all day. At the end, they said goodbye to the pink whale and gave it a big hug. They couldn't wait to come back and spend another day together with the whale!

One day, a baby was playing in the park. The baby saw a big tree and wanted to play near it. The baby's mom was sitting on a bench and thought the park was boring. She called out to the baby, "Come back here, baby! There is nothing fun by the tree." The baby did not listen and went to the tree. The baby noticed something shiny on the ground. The baby picked it up and showed it to mom. "Look, mom!" said the baby, "I found something fun!" Mom was surprised and walked over to the baby. She looked at the shiny thing and saw it was a key. They used the key to open a hidden door in the tree. Inside the tree, they found a room full of toys and games. The baby and mom played together and had a lot of fun. From that day on, the park was not boring for them anymore.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She saw a busy street with many cars. Amy wanted to cross the street to see a big structure on the other side. The structure was a tall building with many colors. Amy saw her friend, Ben, standing next to her. "Ben, let's hold hands and cross the street together!" said Amy. Ben smiled and they squeezed each other's hands tightly. They looked both ways and started to walk. As they were walking, something unexpected happened. The big structure started to move! It was not a building, but a giant robot! Amy and Ben were surprised and laughed. They decided to play with the robot and have a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a kind daughter who loved her mom and dad very much. She always helped them with little things, like picking up her toys and giving them hugs. One day, Lily saw a tiny bug on the ground. She wanted to become friends with the bug, so she put her hand down and let it crawl on her. The bug tickled her hand and made her giggle. She knew she had a new friend. Lily took the bug home and showed her mom and dad. They were happy that she was kind to the little bug. They all played together and had a fun day. At night, Lily said goodnight to her mom, dad, and her new bug friend. She knew that being kind made the world a better place.
