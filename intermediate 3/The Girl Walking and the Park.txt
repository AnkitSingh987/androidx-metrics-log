Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Walking and the Park

One day there was a little girl walking in the park. She was walking by a hedge, when suddenly two boys popped out from behind it. They started to tease the little girl and made her feel scared, so she started to cry. The little girl's mother then came over and saw the boys teasing her daughter. She asked them: "Why are you teasing my daughter? What will she learn from this?" The boys then looked down in shame and said, "We were just being envious of her because she seemed to be so happy." The little girl's mother then said, "You will never find happiness by teasing other people and making them feel bad. How do you think teasing makes the other person feel?" The boys replied, "It just makes them feel like their not good enough." The little girl's mother smiled and said, "That's right. You can learn to be happy and make others happy by being kind, not by being envious and teasing them. That's a much better way to live your life." The boys felt embarrassed and learned their lesson. From then on, they stopped teasing and started to be kind to one another. They realised that happiness comes from being kind to each other, and not by being envious and teasing.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He had a bag with chalk in it. Tim was very alert as he walked. He saw a big tree and thought it would be a good place to draw. Tim took out his chalk and began to draw a big sun. A girl named Sue saw Tim and said, "What are you doing?" Tim said, "I am drawing with chalk. Want to play?" Sue said, "Yes!" Tim gave Sue some chalk and they drew a big house under the sun. They were having lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A small dog came running and took Tim's bag. Tim and Sue were surprised. They ran after the dog, but it was very fast. The dog went behind a bush and Tim and Sue heard a zip sound. They looked behind the bush and saw that the dog had zipped the bag closed. The dog wagged its tail and gave the bag back to Tim. Tim, Sue, and the dog laughed and played together for the rest of the day.

One day, a little girl named Sue went on a trip with her mom and dad. They had big luggage with them. Sue was very excited to go on the trip. As they walked, Sue saw a banana peel on the ground. "Mom, look! A banana peel!" she said. Her mom replied, "Be careful, Sue. You might slip on it." Later, Sue's dad got angry because he couldn't find the right way. Sue had an idea. "Let's go back to the banana peel, Dad. Maybe we missed the right way there." They went back to the banana peel and found the right way. Sue's dad was not angry anymore. They all laughed and continued on their trip, being careful not to slip on the banana peel.

Once upon a time, there was a good little pupil named Tim. Tim loved to learn and play with his friends at school. Every day, he would wake up, eat his breakfast, and get ready for school. One day, Tim learned how to tie his shoes. He was so proud of himself. He showed his mom and dad, and they clapped their hands. They said, "Good job, Tim! You are a good pupil!" At school, Tim showed his friends how to tie their shoes too. They all learned how to do it, and they were happy. They played and ran around the playground without tripping on their shoelaces. Tim felt good because he was a good pupil and a good friend.

One day, a shy cat named Tim went to the park. He wanted to take his cooler with him. Inside the cooler, he had yummy food to eat. He was very happy to spend the day at the park. While at the park, Tim saw a big dog. The dog was very friendly. Tim was scared, but the dog just wanted to play. They played together and had a lot of fun. Tim was not shy anymore. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog opened Tim's cooler and took out the food. But instead of eating it, the dog shared the food with Tim! They both ate and became good friends. From that day on, Tim was not shy anymore, and he always took his cooler to the park to share with his new friend.
