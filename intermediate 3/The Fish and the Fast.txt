Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fish and the Fast

Once upon a time, there was an old fish who lived in the sea, near the shore. One day, he met a young fish who was swimming fast. The old fish said, "Hi! Why are you swimming so fast?" The young fish said, "I want to quit being slow. I want to be fast like the big fish in the sea." The old fish smiled and said, "You can be fast, but first, you must learn from the old fish like me. Do not quit trying, and you will be fast one day." The young fish listened to the old fish and practiced swimming every day. He did not quit, and he became fast like the big fish in the sea. The old fish was proud of the young fish, and they swam together near the shore, happy and fast.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play games with his friends. One day, they had a big race to see who was the fastest. Tim ran very fast and won the race. He was so happy! Tim got a trophy for winning the race. It was a little trophy, but it meant a lot to him. He took it home and showed it to his mom and dad. They were very proud of him. Tim put the trophy on his shelf in his room. One day, it was very hot outside. Tim was thirsty, so he got a cup of water. He went to his room and saw his trophy. He had an idea. He poured the water into the trophy and pretended it was a big cup. Tim drank the water from his trophy and felt very happy. He loved his little trophy even more now.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her toy car. She loved to make it go fast and take turns. Her mom told her, "Lily, it's time to clean up your toys." Lily did not want to stop playing, so she asked, "Mom, please allow me to play for a little bit more?" Her mom said, "Okay, but only for a few more minutes." Lily was happy and kept playing with her toy car. She made it go fast and take many turns. But after a while, she thought it was boring to play alone. Suddenly, her toy car started to talk! It said, "Hi, Lily! I can play with you!" Lily was very surprised but also excited. She and her toy car had so much fun together. They raced around the room and took turns trying new tricks. In the end, Lily was happy that her toy car could talk, and they played together all the time.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Dave and Allie. Allie was very clumsy and Dave was very good at sports. They both liked playing baseball, so they decided to mail a baseball back and forth. One day Dave sent Allie the baseball in the mail. Allie received it and wanted to send it back to Dave. But when she tried to mail it, she was so clumsy that the baseball fell out of her hands and rolled away before she could mail it. Allie started to cry. But then Dave came over to Allie's house, and looked at her sadly. He opened his bag and Allie saw that he had another baseball in there. Dave said, “I got you another one, don't worry!” Allie smiled and thanked Dave. They decided to keep playing with their two baseballs together, and never to mail them apart again.

Lily and Ben are in school. They like to draw on the blackboard with chalk. They make shapes and animals and letters. Lily draws a big sun. Ben draws a small cat. "Look at my cat!" Ben says. "It is cute and fluffy." "I like your cat," Lily says. "But my sun is brighter and warmer." They smile and draw more things on the blackboard. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is the teacher. She is angry. She sees the blackboard full of chalk. She does not like it. "Who did this?" she asks. "Who made this mess?" Lily and Ben are scared. They do not want to get in trouble. They look at each other. They do not know what to say. The teacher walks to the blackboard. She picks up an eraser. She wants to erase the chalk. But she drops the eraser. It falls on the floor. It makes a big dust cloud. Lily and Ben cough. They cover their mouths. They cannot see anything. The teacher is also coughing. She cannot see either. She is looking for the eraser. Lily and Ben see a chance. They run to the door. They want to escape. But then, Lily stops. She feels sorry for the teacher. She thinks the teacher is sad and lonely. She wants to help her. She turns around. She goes back to the blackboard. She finds the eraser. She gives it to the teacher. "Here you go," she says. "I am sorry for the chalk. It was me and Ben. We were just having fun." The teacher is surprised. She takes the eraser. She looks at Lily. She sees her brave face. She smiles. "Thank you, Lily," she says. "That was very kind of you. And very brave. I am not angry anymore. I understand. You and Ben can draw on the blackboard. But only sometimes. And only with permission. OK?" Lily nods. She is happy. She feels proud. She calls Ben. He comes back. He is also sorry. He says sorry to the teacher. The teacher hugs them. She says it is OK. She says they are good kids. She says they can draw one more thing on the blackboard. Together. Lily and Ben smile. They pick up the chalk. They draw a big heart. They write their names inside. They show it to the teacher. The teacher likes it. She says it is beautiful. She says they are her best students. She says she loves them. Lily and Ben love her too. They are happy. They are friends. They are brave.
