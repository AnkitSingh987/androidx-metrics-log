Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Bus Driver and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a bus driver named Tom. Tom was a popular driver because he was always nice to everyone. He would wave and say "Hello!" when he saw people. The kids on the bus liked Tom a lot. One day, Tom was driving the bus, and he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was lost and didn't know where to go. Tom stopped the bus and asked, "Can you guess where you need to go, Lily?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "I need to go to my grandma's house, but I don't know where it is." Tom wanted to help Lily find her grandma's house. He asked the kids on the bus if they knew where her grandma lived. One boy said, "I know! Her grandma lives by the big tree!" Tom smiled and drove the bus to the big tree. Lily was so happy when she saw her grandma waiting for her. She waved goodbye to Tom and the kids on the bus as she ran to give her grandma a big hug.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy top that she loved to play with every day. The top was very colorful and useful, as it helped Lily learn about colors and how to share with her friends. One day, Lily's top got very dirty. Her mom said, "Lily, we need to bathe your top to make it clean again." Lily agreed and helped her mom fill a small tub with water and soap. They gently washed the top, and it became clean and shiny again. After they were done, Lily's mom said, "Now your top is clean and ready to play with again!" Lily was happy and thanked her mom. She played with her top all day, and remembered to keep it clean so it would always be useful and fun.

Lily likes to dress up. She has many clothes in her big box. She can be a princess, a pirate, a doctor, or anything she wants. She likes to wear hats and shoes and scarves and gloves. One day, she finds a gray coat in her box. It is very soft and warm. It has buttons and pockets and a hood. Lily puts on the coat and looks in the mirror. She thinks she looks like a bear. She goes to the door and tries to open it. She wants to go outside and play. But the door is locked. She cannot turn the handle. She is sad and angry. She knocks on the door and shouts. "Mommy, mommy, let me out! I want to play!" Mommy hears Lily and comes to the door. She sees Lily in the gray coat and smiles. "Hello, my little bear. Where are you going?" "I want to go outside and play. But the door is locked. I cannot open it." Mommy unlocks the door and opens it. She hugs Lily and kisses her. "You can go outside and play, but you have to wear something else. The gray coat is too big and too hot for you. It is daddy's coat. He wears it when it is cold and snowy." Lily looks at the coat and feels silly. She takes it off and gives it to mommy. "Sorry, mommy. I did not know. Can I wear this pink dress instead?" Mommy nods and helps Lily to dress. She gives her a sun hat and a flower. "Yes, you can wear the pink dress. It is very pretty and perfect for a sunny day. You look like a princess." Lily smiles and thanks mommy. She runs outside and plays. She is happy and proud. She likes to dress up.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a kind man named Tom. Tom had a staff that he used to walk. His staff was big and original. It was special because it was made by his grandpa. One day, Tom was walking with his staff in the woods. He saw a little bird who couldn't fly. Tom paused and said, "Little bird, why can't you fly?" The bird replied, "I hurt my wing and need help." Tom used his staff to lift the bird and carried it back to his home. At home, Tom took care of the bird until its wing was better. The bird was grateful and said, "Thank you, Tom, for helping me." Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome, little bird. Now you can fly again." The bird flew away happily, and Tom went back to walking with his original staff.
