Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Lily and her dog, Max, went for a walk. They were both very tired because they had been playing all day. They sat under a big tree to rest. Lily looked at Max and said, "Max, let's discuss our day." Max barked and wagged his tail. Lily said, "We played with the ball, and we ran in the park. It was so much fun!" Max barked and nodded his head. They both felt happy in their heart. Soon, it was time to go home. Lily and Max walked back to their house. They were still tired, but they had a good day. They talked and played, and their hearts were full of joy.

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. Fin was a naughty fish who liked to play tricks on his friends. He would hide behind rocks and jump out to scare them. His friends did not like this, but Fin thought it was funny. One day, Fin saw his friend Sam swimming by. Fin decided to play a trick on Sam. He hid behind a rock and waited. When Sam swam close, Fin jumped out and yelled, "Boo!" Sam was so scared that he started to panic. He swam away very fast, yelling, "Help, help!" Fin felt bad for scaring Sam so much. He swam after him and said, "I'm sorry, Sam. I didn't mean to scare you that much. I won't be naughty anymore." Sam was still scared, but he forgave Fin. From that day on, Fin stopped playing tricks on his friends and they all swam happily together in the sea.

One day, Tom and Sue went to the park. They saw a big bean plant. Tom said, "Look, Sue! A bean plant!" Sue shrugged and said, "I don't know what that is." They walked closer to the bean plant. It was cold outside, so they wore big coats. Tom touched the bean plant. Suddenly, the bean plant started to shake. "What is happening?" Sue asked, scared. Out of the bean plant came a talking bird! The bird said, "Hello, Tom and Sue! I am a magic bird. I live in the bean plant." Tom and Sue were very surprised. They had never seen a talking bird before. The bird became their new friend, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a fake toy cat that she loved very much. She named her toy cat, Luna. Mia and Luna went on many fun adventures together, and they were always happy. One day, Mia saw a real cat outside her house. The real cat looked just like Luna! Mia went outside and said, "Hi, kitty! Do you want to play with me and Luna?" The real cat looked at Mia and Luna, and then it meowed happily. The real cat wanted to play with them! Mia, Luna, and the real cat played together all day. They chased each other, played with a ball, and had a tea party. Mia was so happy that her fake cat, Luna, and the real cat became friends. They all united and had the best day ever. From that day on, Mia, Luna, and the real cat were always together, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a strong girl named Lily. She loved to watch movies with her friends. One day, she found a movie about a princess who needed help. Lily and her friends decided to watch it together. As they watched the movie, they saw the princess trying to improve her castle. She wanted it to be the best castle in the land. Lily and her friends cheered for the princess as she worked hard to make her castle better. In the end, the princess made her castle strong and beautiful. Lily and her friends clapped and laughed. They were so happy to see the princess improve her castle. They all agreed that it was a great movie to watch.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily was a selfish girl who did not like to share her toys with her friends. One day, her mom told Lily that they were going to celebrate her birthday soon. Lily was excited and said, "Mom, can I have a big cake for my birthday?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Lily, we will have a big cake for you to share with all your friends." Before the party, Lily's mom hid the cake in the cupboard so that it would be a surprise. Lily was curious and wanted to see the cake. She opened the cupboard and saw the big cake. She thought, "I don't want to share my cake with my friends. I want it all for myself." At the party, Lily's mom brought out the cake, and all the kids were excited. But Lily said, "No, this is my cake! I won't share it!" Her friends were sad and didn't want to play with her anymore. Lily's mom talked to her and said, "Lily, it is not nice to be selfish. Sharing makes everyone happy." Lily thought about it and decided to share her cake with her friends. They all enjoyed the cake and had a great time at the party. From that day on, Lily learned to share and became a better friend.
