Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Lily and the Cookie

Ben and Lily are twins. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books. They also have a big bed. Sometimes they nap on the bed. One day, they wake up from their nap. They are hungry. They smell something tasty. It is cookies. Mom is baking cookies in the kitchen. They want to eat some cookies. They crawl out of the bed. They crawl to the door. They crawl down the hall. They crawl to the kitchen. They see Mom. They see the cookies. They smile. "Hi, Ben and Lily," Mom says. "Did you have a good nap?" "Yes, Mom," Ben and Lily say. "Can we have some cookies?" "Of course, my loves," Mom says. "But first, wash your hands. And then, sit on the chairs. No crawling on the floor." Ben and Lily nod. They wash their hands. They sit on the chairs. Mom gives them each a cookie. They bite the cookie. It is warm and sweet and tasty. They say, "Thank you, Mom." "You're welcome, Ben and Lily," Mom says. "I love you." "I love you, Mom," Ben and Lily say. They hug Mom. They eat their cookies. They are happy.

One day, a big dog and a little cat were playing outside. The big dog said, "I have a big brain, so I can run fast and catch the ball." The little cat said, "I have a small brain, but I can still try." They both saw a ball far away. The big dog said, "Let's hurry and get the ball!" They both ran as fast as they could. The big dog was sure he would win because of his big brain. But as they got close to the ball, a little bird came down and took the ball away. The big dog and little cat were both surprised. The little bird said, "I have a small brain, but I am fast too!" The big dog and little cat learned that having a big brain doesn't always make you the best at everything. They all played together and had lots of fun.

One day, a painter was very hungry. He went to the store to buy some sweet food. He saw a sweet cake and bought it. He was so happy to eat it. On his way home, he met a little girl. She was sad and hungry. The painter said, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I am hungry and have no food." The painter wanted to help. He gave her his sweet cake. The little girl was very happy and said, "Thank you!" The painter went back to his work, but something unexpected happened. The little girl came back with a big plate of cookies. She said, "I want to feed you too!" The painter was very surprised and happy. They both ate the cookies and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was an interesting oven. It was not like the other ovens. This oven could talk and walk! It lived in a small house with a nice family. The family loved to cook and bake with the oven. One day, the oven wanted to help the family complete a big meal. The family was having a party with lots of friends coming over. The oven knew it was his time to shine. So, it walked around the kitchen and helped the family cook. The family was very happy with the interesting oven. It helped them complete the meal in no time. The friends at the party loved the food that the oven helped make. Everyone had a great time, and the oven felt proud. From that day on, the oven was always there to help the family with their cooking.

Once upon a time, there was a polite cat named Lily. Lily loved to eat her food with sauce. Every day, she would ask her mom for sauce to put on her food. Her mom would smile and give her the sauce she loved so much. One day, Lily's sauce disappeared. She looked everywhere for it, but she could not find it. Lily was very sad and did not want to eat her food without her favorite sauce. Lily asked her friend, a friendly dog named Max, to help her find the sauce. They looked all around the house and the garden. Finally, they found the sauce under a big tree. Lily was so happy, and she thanked Max for helping her. They both enjoyed their food with the sauce and had a great day.
