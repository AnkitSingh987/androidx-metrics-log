Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Blanket

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little girl named Lily. She was an honest girl who loved to help others. One day, it rained a lot and everything was wet outside. Lily saw a small cat shivering in the cold rain. Lily went to the cat and said, "Little cat, you are all wet. Let me help you." The cat looked at Lily with big, sad eyes. Lily picked up the cat and took it to her house to get warm and dry. Inside her house, Lily gave the cat a warm blanket and some food. The cat was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lily, for being so honest and kind. You are a true friend." From that day on, Lily and the cat were best friends and they always helped each other.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park. He saw two toys, a big ball and a small car. Tom had to choose one to play with. He thought the big ball was easy to play with, but the small car looked fun too. Tom asked his friend, Sam, "Which one should I choose?" Sam said, "I like the big ball. It's easy to kick and throw, but the small car is fun to push and pull. You choose, Tom." Tom picked up the big ball. But, oh no! The ball was not a ball. It was a heavy weight! Tom could not lift it. Sam and Tom were surprised. They tried to lift the weight together, but it was too heavy. A girl named Lily came over. She said, "I can help you." With a magic wand, she turned the heavy weight back into the big ball. Tom, Sam, and Lily played with the big ball and the small car. They had fun and became friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, shiny diamond. It was very pretty. Lily loved her diamond and took it everywhere. One day, Lily went to the park to play. She met a boy named Jack. Jack saw the diamond and said, "Wow! That's a big diamond! Can I see it?" Lily was frightened because she didn't know Jack. But Jack smiled and said, "Don't worry, I won't take it. I just want to look at it." Lily let Jack see the diamond. They played together in the park and had lots of fun. After playing, they were tired. They sat under a tree to rest. Lily put her diamond on the grass next to her. When it was time to go home, Lily forgot her diamond. The next day, Lily went back to the park to find her diamond. She was very sad because she couldn't find it. Then, Jack came to the park and saw Lily. He said, "Lily, don't be sad. I found your diamond yesterday and kept it safe for you." He gave the diamond back to Lily. Lily was very happy and thanked Jack. They became best friends and played together every day. And Lily never forgot her diamond again.

Once upon a time, in a jolly little town, there lived a kind dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friends and help others. One day, while Max was playing near the river, he saw a small beef stuck in the mud. The beef was sad and scared. Max knew he had to rescue the beef. He jumped into the mud and pulled the beef out with his strong teeth. Max and the beef became best friends. They played together every day, and the beef was very thankful to Max for saving him. The other animals in the town saw how Max and the beef were always happy and kind to each other. They learned that being helpful and caring for others can bring happiness and friendship. And that was the moral of the story.

John saw a pencil in the desk. He was excited! He quickly grabbed the pencil and asked his mom, "Can I bring it to show my friends?" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can bring it. I think your friends will think it's cool." John smiled, grabbed the pencil and raced outside. He showed his friends the pencil and they cheered. Everyone said it was cool! John was so happy that he had such a cool pencil. He kept it close to him the whole day. When night time came, he finally had to put the pencil back in his desk. But he had such a great day with his cool pencil!
