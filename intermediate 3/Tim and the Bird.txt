Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with clay. He made many things with the clay. He made a cat, a dog, and a big sun. Tim was very happy. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue was having a difficult time making a clay bird. Tim said, "Hi, Sue! Can I help you?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, please! I want to make a bird, but it is difficult." Tim showed Sue how to make a bird with the clay. They rolled the clay, and then they peeked at the bird they made. The bird looked very nice. Sue was very happy. Tim and Sue played with the clay bird all day. They had a lot of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in the park. He saw an elderly man sitting on a bench. The man had a stick with a sharp tip. Tim was curious and asked the man, "Why do you have that stick?" The man said, "I use it to walk and to keep me safe. The sharp tip helps me feel the ground better." Tim thought about it and wanted to try the stick too. Tim took the stick and started to walk with it. Suddenly, a big dog came running at him. Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. He held the stick out in front of him, and the dog stopped. It was the elderly man's dog! The man laughed and said, "You're brave, Tim. My dog just wanted to play with you." Tim was happy and gave the stick back to the man. They all played together in the park for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Sean. He was very playful and loved playing outside in the sun. One day, Sean’s dad gave him a very colourful cooler box. “What’s this?” Sean asked. “It’s a special cooler box,” Dad said. “It’s for when we go for picnics! We can use it to pack our lunch and drinks.” Sean smiled. He was so excited to use his new cooler box. He grabbed some sandwiches, drinks and snacks and put them in the cooler box. Dad was happy to see Sean so excited and he said, “Let’s invite your friends over and go on a picnic together. We can all have some fun!” Sean was overjoyed. He quickly told all his friends and they were very excited. All the children had lots of fun in the park and they made sure to look after the cooler box so they could use it again next time. Moral of the story: Inviting friends over to have fun is more fun than playing alone.

Once upon a time there was a little squirrel called Sarah. Sarah was very shy and always stayed in her tree. One day, Sarah decided to go out to explore. She saw a big lumber pile, and climbed to the top. It was exciting, but also scary being so high. Suddenly, Sarah saw another squirrel. She was scared and very shy, so she was about to run away. But the other squirrel said, “Hey, don’t be scared. I am your friend.” Sarah slowly nodded. The two squirrels got to know each other and made friends. Sarah was very happy that she had been brave enough to try something new. The moral of the story is that it is okay to try something new and you never know who you might meet!

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim and a girl named Kim. They were brother and sister. They loved to play with their toys, but they were very stubborn. They always wanted the same toy at the same time. One day, they both wanted to play with their favorite toy car. Tim said, "I want to play with the car!" Kim said, "No, I want to play with the car!" They started to fight over the toy car. They pulled and pulled, but neither would let go. Mom came into the room and saw them fighting. She said, "Stop fighting! You must learn to share and take turns." Tim and Kim didn't listen and kept fighting. Mom took the toy car away and said, "If you can't share, then neither of you can play with the car." Tim and Kim felt sad. They didn't want to lose their favorite toy. So, they decided to try again. This time, they took turns and shared the toy car. They played together and had lots of fun. They learned that sharing and taking turns made them both happy. From that day on, Tim and Kim tried not to repeat their stubborn ways. They learned that sharing and taking turns was better than fighting. And they lived happily ever after.
