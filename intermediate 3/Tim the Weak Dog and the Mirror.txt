Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Weak Dog and the Mirror

Once upon a time, there was a weak little dog named Tim. He liked to look at himself in the big mirror in his home. One day, Tim was going to play with his friends, but he saw the mirror and wanted to look at himself first. This made him delay his playtime. As Tim looked in the mirror, something unexpected happened. His reflection started to talk to him! "Hello, Tim!" said the reflection. Tim was very surprised and a little scared. He had never seen a talking mirror before. The talking mirror told Tim that it was lonely and wanted to play too. Tim felt bad for the mirror and decided to invite it to play with his friends. When they all played together, the mirror turned out to be very fun and silly. Tim and the mirror became best friends, and they never felt weak or lonely again.

On Wednesday, Ben and Lily went to the park with Mom. They liked to play on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. They also liked to run and chase each other on the grass. One time, Ben was running after Lily and he stumbled on a rock. He fell down and hurt his knee. He cried and Mom came to help him. She gave him a hug and a band-aid. She said, "It's okay, Ben. You are very brave and powerful. You can get up and play again." Lily felt sorry for Ben. She said, "I'm sorry, Ben. I didn't mean to make you stumble. Do you want to play with me?" Ben nodded and smiled. He said, "Yes, Lily. You are my best friend. Let's play together." They held hands and went to the sandbox. They made a big castle with a moat and a bridge. They had fun and forgot about the rock and the band-aid. They were happy on Wednesday.

One day, a little bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a beautiful tree with many branches. The bird wanted to make a home in the tree. The bird started to grab twigs from the ground. It took the twigs up to the tree and put them on a branch. The bird did this many times to make a big nest. When the nest was done, the bird was very happy. It had a new home in the beautiful tree. The bird sang a happy song and lived in the tree with its family.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big cave. Tim said, "I want to discover what is in the cave." Tim went into the cave. Inside, he found a big bear. The bear was eating something yummy. The bear said, "Hello, do you want to eat with me?" Tim was scared at first, but then he saw the bear was nice. They ate yummy food together. The bear said, "I found this yummy food in the cave. I did not know it was here." Then, a girl named Sue came into the cave. She said, "I lost my yummy food. Did you see it?" Tim and the bear looked at each other. They discovered that they were eating Sue's food! They all laughed and decided to share the yummy food together. From that day on, they were all good friends.

One day, a big festival was in town. All the kids and their families went to have fun. There were games, food, and music for everyone to enjoy. At the festival, there was a big tree. On the tree, there was a dead leaf. A little girl named Sally saw the leaf and stopped to admire it. She thought it was pretty, even if it was not green like the other leaves. Sally showed the leaf to her friends. They all liked it too. They played with the leaf and had lots of fun at the festival. At the end of the day, Sally took the leaf home to remember the fun time they had.

Ben and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and plant seeds. They wanted to grow flowers and vegetables. They watered their seeds every day and waited for them to grow. One day, they saw something green poking out of the soil. "Look, Lily, our seeds are growing!" Ben said. He ran to see what it was. He pulled it out and saw a carrot. It was big and orange and crunchy. He was very happy. He showed it to Lily. Lily was happy too. She wanted to find a carrot as well. She dug in another spot and found something green too. She pulled it out and saw a vegetable. But it was not a carrot. It was brown and soft and smelly. It had holes and worms in it. It was rotten. She was very sad. She threw it away and cried. Ben saw Lily crying and went to hug her. "What's wrong, Lily?" he asked. He saw the rotten vegetable and made a face. "Ew, that's gross. Where did that come from?" Lily sniffed and said, "I don't know. I wanted a carrot like yours, but I got this. It's a bad vegetable. It's rotten. It can't grow anymore. It's helpless." Ben felt sorry for Lily and the rotten vegetable. He said, "Maybe it was sick or old or forgot to drink water. Maybe it didn't have enough sun or space or friends. Maybe it was lonely or scared or sad. Maybe it needed some help." Lily looked at Ben and smiled. She said, "You're very kind, Ben. You always try to help. Maybe we can help the other vegetables too. Maybe we can make them happy and healthy and not rotten." Ben nodded and said, "Yes, let's do that. Let's take care of our garden and our vegetables. Let's give them water and sun and space and friends. Let's make them smile and grow and not rot." They hugged and went back to their garden. They watered their seeds and plants and vegetables. They talked to them and sang to them and played with them. They hoped they would grow flowers and vegetables. They hoped they would not rot. They were happy.
