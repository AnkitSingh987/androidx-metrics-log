Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Stubborn Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little boy named Tom. He loved to watch his favorite cartoon on TV. One day, his mom asked him to try something new. "Tom, let's go outside and play. You might like it," she said. Tom didn't want to go outside. He wanted to watch his cartoon. But his mom said, "You can watch your cartoon later. Let's have some fun outside first." Tom was not happy, but he went outside with his mom. They played in the park and Tom tried to climb a tree. At first, he couldn't do it. But he kept trying and trying. Finally, he was able to climb the tree. Tom was very happy and proud of himself. He learned that trying new things can be fun. Later, they went back home and Tom watched his cartoon, feeling happy about his day.

One day, a sleepy cat named Tom found a cable. Tom was very curious about the cable. He wanted to play with it. So, he picked it up with his mouth and ran around the house. Tom saw his friend, a bird named Sue. "Look, Sue! I found a cable!" Tom said. Sue was excited too. She wanted to play with the cable as well. They decided to play a game with the cable. Tom and Sue started to scatter the cable all over the floor. They had so much fun running and scattering the cable. After a while, they got tired and sat down. "That was fun!" said Sue. Tom agreed, and they both took a nap, feeling very sleepy.

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl who loved grills. She often asked her mom to bring home a special kind of grill, but her mom always said she had to delay it. But one day her mom brought home a special kind of grill! When the girl saw it, she was so happy and excited. She started to use the grill right away and she shared it with her friends. The friends were very happy, too. They all had so much fun together. The kind girl was very proud of her special kind of grill!

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing outside. The sun was going down and it was almost night time. Mia saw the sky was turning black. She knew it was time to go home. Mia's mom said, "Mia, it is night time. It is time for bed." Mia did not want to go to bed. She wanted to play more. But she listened to her mom and went to her room. That night, something funny happened. Mia heard a noise outside her window. She looked out and saw a big, black cat. The cat said, "Hello, Mia! I can talk!" Mia was very surprised. She had never met a talking cat before. They became friends and played all night. In the morning, the cat went back to being a normal cat. Mia knew it was their secret and she could not wait for the next night to play with her new friend again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play games with his friends. They were very competitive. They played hide and seek, tag, and many other games. Tim always wanted to win, so he thought of a new game to play. He called it "disappear". One day, Tim found a toy weapon in his room. It was a magic wand that could make things disappear. He thought it would be fun to use the wand in his new game. Tim and his friends went to the park to play "disappear". Tim hid the magic wand behind his back so no one could see it. When it was Tim's turn to make someone disappear, he waved the magic wand at his friend. Suddenly, his friend was gone! Tim and the other kids were very surprised. They didn't know what to do. Tim felt bad and wanted his friend to come back. He waved the magic wand again, and his friend reappeared. From that day on, Tim and his friends decided not to play "disappear" anymore. They learned that winning was not as important as having fun and being together.
