Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Monkey

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to pretend and was always thinking up new ideas. One day, she decided to pretend to be a hero! Lucy grabbed a cape and put it on. Then she grabbed her monkey toy and pretended it was her sidekick. They both had a great style and were ready to save the day! The two were off to find danger. They soon came across a deep, dark cave. It looked awfully dangerous, but that didn’t stop Lucy and her sidekick monkey! They headed inside and saw a big, scary monster! Lucy knew she had to act quick, so she used her trusty pretend powers to fight the monster away. After a while, they had defeated the monster and brought peace back to the cave. Lucy and her sidekick had become real heroes! They'd saved the day with their pretend powers and style.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was very intelligent. He loved to play with his dog, Max. They liked to throw a ball in the park. One day, Tim and Max saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she had a test at school. "I don't know if I can do it," Sue said. Tim wanted to help Sue. "Max and I can help you study for your test," Tim told her. They studied together and played with Max. Sue felt better and did well on her test. Tim was happy he could help Sue. The moral of the story is to help others when they need it.

One day, a dog named Spot had a hurt heel. He could not run and play like the other dogs. Spot felt sad and asked his friend, a bird named Tweet, for help. Tweet said, "I will pray for your heel to get better." Spot was happy and thanked Tweet. They both prayed together for Spot's heel to heal. The next day, a wild cat came to their yard. The cat wanted to fight Spot. Spot was scared but he remembered that he and Tweet prayed for his heel. He felt strong and stood up to the wild cat. Spot said, "You can't hurt me, wild cat!" The wild cat saw that Spot was brave and decided to leave. From that day on, Spot's heel got better and he could run and play again. Spot and Tweet learned that praying can help you feel strong and brave when facing problems.

One day, a girl named Mia was in her room. She had a big sheet. Mia liked to play with the sheet. She liked to make a tent with it. It was a mild day, so Mia was happy. Mia's brother, Tom, came into the room. "I want the sheet," he said. Mia did not want to give the sheet to Tom. "No, it is mine," she said. Tom was mad. He wanted to play with the sheet too. Mia and Tom did not know what to do. They both wanted to play with the sheet. Then, their mom came into the room. "Why not play together?" she asked. Mia and Tom thought that was a good idea. They made a big tent with the sheet and played together. They were both happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little orange helicopter. It had a job to do. The helicopter had to collect things for people in the town. It was a very helpful helicopter. One day, the orange helicopter went to the park. It saw a little girl crying. She had lost her toy. The helicopter wanted to help. So, it flew up high and looked for the toy. It found the toy in a tree and collected it. The little girl was very happy. She thanked the orange helicopter. The helicopter felt good for helping. It flew away to find more people to help in the town. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, round candle. She loved her candle very much. One day, Lily's mommy said, "Lily, we need to finish cleaning your room." Lily looked at her candle and said, "Mommy, can I keep my candle?" Mommy smiled and said, "Yes, you can keep your candle." Later, Lily and her mommy were done cleaning. Lily's room looked nice, not terrible anymore. She put her candle on the table. She was very happy.
