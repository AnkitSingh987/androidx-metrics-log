Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Magic

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big tent with a sign that said "Display of Magic". They wanted to see what was inside. They paid a coin and entered the tent. It was dark and spooky. They saw a woman with a black hat and a long nose. She was a witch! "Hello, children," the witch said. "Do you want to see some magic?" Tom and Lily nodded. They were curious and scared. The witch took out a wand and waved it. She said some strange words. Then, she pointed at Tom and Lily. "Poof!" A cloud of smoke appeared. When it cleared, Tom and Lily looked at each other. They gasped. Tom had turned into a frog. Lily had turned into a mouse. They were both pale and small. "Ha ha ha!" The witch laughed. "That is my magic display. You are my new pets now. Come with me!" The witch grabbed Tom and Lily and put them in a cage. She walked out of the tent with them. Tom and Lily cried. They wanted to be children again. They hoped someone would help them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a stubborn aunt who did not like to be disturbed. Lily loved to play with her toys and make a lot of noise. One day, Lily was playing with her toys and making a lot of noise. Her aunt was trying to sleep, but Lily's noise disturbed her. Aunt got up from her bed and said, "Lily, please play quietly. I need to sleep." Lily did not want to disturb her aunt, so she played quietly with her toys. Her aunt went back to sleep, and Lily learned that it is important to be quiet when someone needs to rest.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in the living room. Sara has a doll and Ben has an automobile. They pretend that the doll and the automobile are friends and go on adventures. One day, they hear a loud noise outside. They run to the window and see a big truck. The truck is carrying many automobiles. Sara and Ben are very excited. They want to see the automobiles up close. They put on their shoes and jackets and open the door. They run to the street. They do not see their mom or dad. They do not see the cars or the people. They only see the truck and the automobiles. "Wow, look at all the automobiles!" Sara says. "They are so shiny and colorful!" "Can we touch them?" Ben asks. "Can we play with them?" They run to the truck. They try to climb on it. They want to reach the automobiles. They do not hear the horn. They do not see the driver. They are in danger. "Stop! Stop!" a voice shouts. It is their mom. She sees them from the window. She is very scared. She runs to the street. She grabs Sara and Ben. She pulls them away from the truck. "Sara, Ben, what are you doing?" she says. "You can't run to the street like that! You can't play with the automobiles on the truck! They are not toys! They are for the people who buy them! You could have been hurt!" Sara and Ben are shocked. They realize that they did something bad. They realize that they made their mom angry and worried. They feel sorry. "We're sorry, mom," they say. "We didn't know. We just wanted to see the automobiles." Mom hugs them. She is glad that they are safe. She is not angry anymore. She is just relieved. "I know, sweeties," she says. "But you have to be careful. You have to listen to me and dad. You have to stay away from the street and the trucks. They are dangerous. You have to be wise and follow the rules." Sara and Ben nod. They understand. They promise to be wise and follow the rules. They go back to the living room. They play with their doll and their automobile. They are happy. They love their mom.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big house and lots of toys. One day, Tom wanted to have a fun party for all his friends. Tom's mom said, "Don't forget the popcorn!" This remind Tom that he needed to buy popcorn for the party. So, he went to the store and got a big bag of popcorn for everyone to eat. At the party, all of Tom's friends had lots of fun. They played games, ate popcorn, and laughed a lot. In the end, everyone was happy and Tom felt good about sharing his toys and popcorn with his friends.
