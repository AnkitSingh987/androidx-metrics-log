Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tall Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time there was a tall tree. It was a very big tree that climbed really high into the sky. One day, a little girl dropped her stamp. She had been collecting stamps from around the world and this was her favourite one. The stamp had fallen from the top of the tall tree, where the girl had been collecting leaves. The girl was sad. She wanted her stamp back so she started to climb up the tree. But it was too high for her, and she started to feel scared. Suddenly, a big bird flew down from the sky. It grabbed the stamp from inside the tree and flew away with it. The little girl was very sad, but then she had an idea. She ran home and got some treats. Then, she returned to the tree and scattered them around it. After a while, the bird came back. It saw the treats and stopped to eat them. This gave the little girl a chance to grab her precious stamp back. The little girl was so happy to get her stamp back. She thanked the bird, and she never forgot that day.

Once upon a time, there was a nice dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to serve food to his friends. He had a big party with all his friends. They played games and ate yummy food. Buddy was very happy. But then, an ugly cat named Grumpy came to the party. Grumpy was not happy. He wanted to play a trick on Buddy. So Grumpy put a yucky bug in Buddy's food. Buddy did not know about the yucky bug. When Buddy ate the food, he saw the yucky bug. It was not good. Buddy and his friends were sad. But then, they all worked together to make new food. The result was a big, yummy cake! Buddy and his friends ate the cake and had fun. Grumpy saw them and learned that being nice is better than being mean.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because it was a gloomy day. He looked out the window and saw big, dark clouds. Tim wanted to play outside, but he could not because of the rain. Tim had a toy car that could reverse. He played with his car in his room. He made the car go forward and then reverse. Tim liked to watch the car move. He thought it was fun. He wished he could go outside and play with his car in the sun. Then, something unexpected happened. The rain stopped, and the sun came out. Tim was so happy! He ran outside with his toy car. But as he played, the car got stuck in the mud. Tim tried to make the car reverse out of the mud, but it would not move. The toy car was broken. Tim was sad again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sophie. Sophie was three and she loved to wear lots of lovely clothes. Every day she would find something new to put on and look fabulous. One day Sophie's mum bought her a beautiful present. It was a new dress and it was so lovely that it made her smile. She couldn't wait to put it on and show it off to her friends. The next day Sophie put on her new dress and she looked amazing. Her mum said "Well done Sophie, you look beautiful." But when Sophie went to school, none of the other children said nice things. They started to laugh at her and call her names. Sophie felt sad and embarrassed, she wished that she had never put on her new dress. In the end, Sophie felt so upset that she never wanted to wear her lovely dress again.

Once upon a time, there was a big bookcase. The bookcase was full of books. The books were friends. They lived together in the big bookcase. One day, a small book said, "I don't like being at the bottom. It's dark here." The small book was sad. The other books heard the small book complain. A big book on the top shelf said, "Don't worry, small book. We can help you." The big book had an idea. The books worked together to move the small book to the top shelf. Now, the small book was happy. It could see the sun. All the books in the big bookcase were friends, and they were happy too.
