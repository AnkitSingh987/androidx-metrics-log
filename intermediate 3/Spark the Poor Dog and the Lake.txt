Category: Intermediate
Title: Spark the Poor Dog and the Lake

Once upon a time, there was a poor little dog named Spark. He was always wet because he lived near a big lake. Every day, he tried to stay dry, but he always ended up wet again. Spark was sad because he wanted to play with the other dogs, but they didn't like how wet he was. One day, Spark met a kind girl named Lily. She saw that Spark was wet and felt bad for him. Lily took Spark to her home and dried him off with a big towel. Spark was so happy that he wagged his tail and jumped around. Now that he was dry, Spark went to play with the other dogs. They liked him now that he was dry and became his friends. Spark learned that it's important to help others when they need it, just like Lily helped him. And the other dogs learned not to judge someone just because they are different.

Lila liked to help her mom in the garden. She watered the plants and pulled out the weeds. She was very happy when she saw the radishes growing under the ground. They were red and round and crunchy. One day, her mom said, "Lila, you have been a very good helper. You can receive one radish as a reward. You can pick any one you like." Lila was very excited. She ran to the garden and looked for the biggest radish she could find. She saw one that was very big and shiny. She reached for it, but then she stopped. She remembered that her mom had planted that radish for her dad. Her dad loved radishes and he worked very hard. Lila wanted to be honest and not take what was not hers. She looked for another radish that was smaller, but still nice. She found one that was red and round and crunchy. She pulled it out and washed it in the water. She ran back to her mom and showed her the radish. Her mom smiled and hugged her. She said, "Lila, you are a very honest and kind girl. You did not take the biggest radish, but the one that was right for you. I am very proud of you. You can eat your radish and share it with your dad if you like." Lila nodded and smiled. She bit into her radish and felt very happy. She loved her mom and her dad and her garden.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play with his friends. They all made a big, messy circle in the sand. Tim's mom saw the messy circle and said, "You need to clean that up!" Tim and his friends just nod and keep playing. A big wind came and blew the messy circle away. Tim's mom was mad. She said, "I told you to clean it up!" Tim's friend, Sue, said, "We nod, but we did not do it. The wind did it." Tim's mom was surprised. She did not know the wind could do that. Tim and his friends learned that they should listen to their mom and clean up when they are told. They also learned that sometimes, unexpected things can happen. So, they all helped to clean up the messy circle and make the sand look nice again.

Once upon a time, in a small place, there lived a strong ant. The ant had a heavy rock that he wanted to move to the other side of his home. He tried and tried, but the rock was too heavy for him to move alone. He felt sad and didn't know what to do. One day, a friendly bee saw the ant trying to move the heavy rock. The bee asked if he could help the ant. The ant was very happy and said yes. Together, they pushed and pushed, and the heavy rock began to move. It was still hard work, but with the help of his new friend, the ant could move the rock. In the end, the ant and the bee moved the heavy rock to the other side of the ant's home. They were both very happy and proud of what they had done. The ant thanked the bee for his help, and they became good friends. From that day on, they always helped each other when they needed it. The moral of the story is that we can do big things when we work together with our friends.
