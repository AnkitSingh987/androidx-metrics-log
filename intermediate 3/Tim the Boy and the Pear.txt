Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pear

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat pears. One day, he found an unknown pear tree in his yard. He was very happy to see it. The tree had many big, sweet pears. Tim wanted to eat them all. Before Tim could eat the pears, he had to wash them. Tim took a big pear from the tree and went inside. He turned on the water and washed the pear. The water was cold and made the pear shiny and clean. Tim was excited to eat the clean pear. Tim took a big bite of the pear. It was the best pear he had ever eaten. He shared the pear with his mom and dad. They all loved the unknown pear tree in their yard. And from that day on, they washed and ate pears every day, always happy and full.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to dig and bury his toys in the yard. One day, Max got a new toy. It was a small ball that looked like a planet. Max was very happy. Max took his new planet toy outside to play. He dug a hole and put the toy in it. Then, he covered it with dirt. Max was proud of his work. But when he tried to dig it up again, he couldn't find it. Max felt frustrated. Max kept digging and digging, trying to find his planet toy. His friends, a cat and a bird, came to help him. Together, they searched the whole yard. Finally, they found the planet toy under a big tree. Max was happy, and they all played together with the toy.

Once upon a time, in a cold winter, there was a tough bird named Billy. Billy loved to fly high in the sky and dive into the water. He had many friends who played with him every day. One day, Billy saw a big tree with no leaves. He thought it was a good place to dive from. He flew up and up, and then he dove down fast into the water. His friends clapped and cheered for him. Billy was very happy. He loved diving and playing with his friends in the winter. Even though it was cold, Billy was a tough bird and had lots of fun. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat and play all day. One sunny day, Tom's friend, a little girl named Sue, had a great idea. Sue wanted to have a picnic with Tom in the park. Tom was very excited and wagged his tail. Sue and Tom went to the park with a big basket. They found a nice spot under a big tree. Sue took out yummy food from the basket. They had sandwiches, apples, and cookies. Tom's favorite was the cookies. They ate and laughed together. After the picnic, Sue had a surprise for Tom. She gave him a gift. Tom was happy to receive the gift. Inside the gift was a new toy for Tom to play with. They played with the toy all day long. Tom and Sue had the best day ever.

Once upon a time there was a soldier who was very brave. He had a long hat and a long coat and was very proud. One day he went for a walk and suddenly he lost his hat. He looked around for a long time but he couldn't find it. He was very sad but he kept looking and eventually he found it. He was so happy! He put it back on his head and kept walking. But soon he realized he had lost something else: his long coat! He looked and looked, but he couldn't find it. Finally, he had to go home without his coat. But he still kept his head high, because he was a brave soldier.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a friendly oak tree. The friendly oak tree had many friends. It had bird friends, squirrel friends, and bug friends. They all lived happily together. One day, a little bird came to the friendly oak tree. The little bird was sad and started to cry. The oak tree asked the little bird, "Why are you crying, little bird?" The little bird said, "I lost my family, and I don't know where they are." The friendly oak tree wanted to help the little bird. It asked all its friends in the forest to help find the little bird's family. The squirrel friends, the bug friends, and the other bird friends all looked for the little bird's family. Finally, the squirrel friends found the little bird's family. They were in another tree not too far away. The little bird was so happy that it stopped crying. The friendly oak tree and all its friends celebrated together. From that day on, the little bird and its family lived with the friendly oak tree. They all became good friends and lived happily ever after in the big forest.
