Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to look for pretty things in the woods. One day, she found a big, shiny jewel. It made her smile. Lily wanted to show her mom the jewel. But on her way home, she met a sad bunny. The bunny was upset because he lost his home. Lily wanted to help the bunny. She thought about the jewel and had an idea. Lily gave the jewel to the bunny. The bunny was so happy! Then, something unexpected happened. The bunny touched the jewel and turned into a big, friendly bear! The bear smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily!" Now, Lily and the bear are best friends, and they play in the woods every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to splash in the water. Every day, Ducky would go to the pond and play with his friends. One day, Ducky saw a big bowl of cream by the pond. The cream looked very yummy. Ducky wanted to taste it. So, he dipped his beak into the cream and took a big sip. It was so good! After playing and drinking the cream, Ducky felt very comfortable. He went to his soft nest near the pond and took a nap. Ducky had a great day full of fun and yummy treats.

One day, a little girl named Mia went outside to play. It was a hot and dry day. Mia wanted to play a game. She saw her friends Tom and Sue. They were playing a game with matches. Mia wanted to play too. Tom and Sue told Mia the game was to make a fire. They wanted to see who could make the biggest fire. Mia knew this was not a good idea. It was too hot and dry. The fire could get too big and be hard to stop. Mia told Tom and Sue to stop. She said they could play a different game. They could match the colors of the flowers in the garden. Tom and Sue liked this idea. They all played the new game and had fun. Mia was happy she stopped the fire game. She knew it was not safe to play with matches on a hot and dry day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, serious dog named Max. Max loved to display his tricks to his friends. One sunny day, Max saw a little cat named Lucy. Max wanted to show Lucy how good he was at doing tricks. "Lucy, look at me!" Max said. Max did a big jump, and then he did a fast spin. Lucy watched Max and clapped her paws. Max was so happy that he did even more tricks. Max thought he was a big success. But then, something unexpected happened. Lucy said, "Max, I can do tricks too!" Lucy did a high jump, and then she did a long flip. Max was very surprised. He didn't know that little cats could do tricks too. Max and Lucy became good friends and loved to display their tricks together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was restless and always wanted to do something fun. One day, Mia had a great idea. She wanted to design her own kitchen. She thought it would be a fun thing to do. Mia took her crayons and paper and started to draw. She drew a big oven, a fridge, and a table with chairs. She was very happy with her design. Mia showed it to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I designed a kitchen!" Her mom smiled and said, "Wow, Mia! That's a great kitchen! I love your design." Mia felt very proud of her work. She put her design on the fridge so everyone could see it. From that day on, Mia loved to design more things. She designed clothes, houses, and even a playground. Everyone in her family loved to see what Mia would create next. And Mia was no longer restless, because she found something fun to do.

One day, a little dog named Max went to the park with his mom. Max was an obedient dog and always listened to his mom. They walked by the pond and saw a big splash. Max was curious and wanted to find out what made the splash. Max and his mom walked around the pond and saw a small mint plant. The mint plant was talking and said, "I made the big splash!" Max and his mom were very surprised. They didn't know that mint plants could talk or make splashes. The mint plant told Max and his mom that it was magic. It could make splashes and do other fun things. Max and his mom became friends with the magic mint plant. They had lots of fun playing and making splashes in the pond together.
