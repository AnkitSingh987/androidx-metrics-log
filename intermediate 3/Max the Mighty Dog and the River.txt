Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Mighty Dog and the River

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to play near the river. One sunny day, Max saw a red kayak. Max wanted to ride the kayak, but he did not know how. A friendly duck named Ducky saw Max. Ducky said, "Hi Max! Do you want to ride the kayak?" Max barked, "Yes, Ducky! But I don't know how." Ducky smiled and said, "I can help you, Max!" Ducky hopped into the kayak and Max followed. Max and Ducky had fun riding the kayak down the river. They saw fish, frogs, and other friends. When they were done, Max and Ducky shut the kayak door. Max said, "Thank you, Ducky! I had so much fun!" Ducky smiled and said, "You're welcome, Max! Let's play again soon!" Max and Ducky became best friends and played together every day.

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they found a big tree with a rope. They wanted to swing on the rope and have fun. "Look, Lily, a rope!" Tom said. "Let's hang on it and go up and down." "OK, Tom, but be careful. It looks old and cheap. It might break," Lily said. They took turns to hang on the rope and swing. They laughed and shouted. They felt happy and free. But then, something bad happened. The rope snapped and Tom fell to the ground. He hurt his arm and cried. Lily ran to him and hugged him. "Tom, are you OK? I'm sorry, I told you the rope was cheap," Lily said. "It's OK, Lily, it's not your fault. But it hurts a lot. And it's cold. Can we go home?" Tom said. "Yes, Tom, let's go home. Mom will make you feel better. And maybe she will buy us a new rope. A strong and good one," Lily said. They walked home, holding hands. They learned a lesson. Cheap things are not always good. And hanging on a rope can be dangerous. But they still loved each other. And they still liked to play outside.

Once upon a time, there was a mouse living in a big house. One day, the mouse decided to explore the house and find some new places. The mouse opened a door and saw a big wheel. He was so excited and asked his friends to come see it. So they all opened the door and gathered around the wheel. They even jumped on it and had a lot of fun together. After a while, they noticed that the wheel was getting slow and dull. The mouse said, “We should keep playing. How boring it would be if this wheel stops working.” But the others disagreed and everyone left the room. The mouse was still stubborn, so he kept playing with the wheel by himself. Suddenly, the wheel became too dull and it stopped working. The mouse cried and said, “We should have listened to our friends.” The story has a moral lesson that we must be careful with our decisions and listen to our trusted friends. If not, we might find ourselves in trouble.

Once upon a time, there was a kind princess. She lived in a big castle with her family. The princess had a mild smile that made everyone feel happy. One day, the princess met a little girl in the garden. The little girl was sad. The princess asked, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I lost my toy." The princess wanted to help her new friend. They looked for the toy together. The princess found the toy under a bush. The little girl was very happy. The princess said, "Always remember to look around and ask for help when you need it." The little girl thanked the princess and promised to remind herself of the princess's words. From that day on, the little girl always asked for help when she needed it. She also helped others when they needed it too. The princess and the little girl became best friends. They reminded each other to be kind and helpful to everyone they met. The moral of the story is to always help others and ask for help when you need it.
