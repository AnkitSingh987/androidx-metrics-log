Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tall Pole and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a tall pole. It was a very hot day, but the wind was mild. The pole was in the middle of a park where kids liked to play. One day, a little bird came to the pole. The bird was sad and began to complain. It said, "I am so tired, and I need a place to rest." The pole heard the bird and wanted to help. The pole said to the bird, "You can sit on top of me, and rest for a while." The bird was happy and thanked the pole. It flew up and sat on the pole. The bird felt better and soon flew away, feeling good. The pole was happy it could help the bird.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jake and Sally, who loved to play together every day. One day, Jake had a letter he wanted to show Sally. He said, "Look at this letter!" Sally was very excited to see it and asked, "What's in the letter?" Jake smiled and said, "It's a rough letter from my grandma." Sally wanted to see it, so Jake showed it to her. Inside the letter was a picture of a big green tree with a hundred colorful birds sitting in it. The letter told them to attach something special to the tree and when they did, the birds would bring good luck. So, they decided to attach some colorful ribbons they had around the tree. Then they stood back and watched in amazement as the tree gradually filled up with birds of all colors and sizes. The moral of the story is that when you attach something special, like love and kindness, good luck will follow.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a fake newspaper on the floor. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a newspaper!" he said. His mom looked at the newspaper and knew it was fake. She said, "Tim, this is a fake newspaper. It is not real. Let's go to the store and find a real one." So, Tim and his mom went to the store. They saw a man enter with a big stack of newspapers. Tim asked the man, "Are those real newspapers?" The man smiled and said, "Yes, these are real newspapers. You can have one." Tim took the real newspaper and thanked the man. Tim and his mom went home with the real newspaper. Tim was happy to learn the difference between a fake and a real newspaper. They sat down together and read the real newspaper, and Tim felt very grown up.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play outside with his dog, Max. One sunny day, they went to the park to play. Tim threw a ball for Max to catch. Max ran fast, but he got dizzy after a minute. Tim saw Max spinning around and around. Tim laughed and said, "Silly Max, you are so dizzy!" Max stopped spinning and wagged his tail. He barked, "Woof! Woof!" Tim knew that meant Max was happy. They played and laughed together for the rest of the day.

Lisa and Ben are twins. They like to play pretend. Today they want to make a theater show for Mom and Dad. They use a big box, some paper, and crayons to make the stage and the props. They draw a sun, a tree, a house, and a car. Lisa says, "I will be the princess. You will be the prince. We will go on a trip in the car. But first, we need to dry our hair. It is wet from the bath." Ben says, "OK. I will get the hair dryer. You get the crowns and the dresses. We will look very pretty and handsome." They dry their hair with the hair dryer. It makes a loud noise. They put on the crowns and the dresses. They are ready for the show. They go to the box and peek out. They see Mom and Dad sitting on the couch. They smile and clap. Lisa and Ben say, "Welcome to our theater show! We hope you like it!" They start the show. They pretend to drive the car to different places. They see the sun, the tree, the house, and other things. They sing and dance and tell jokes. Mom and Dad laugh and cheer. At the end of the show, they bow and say, "Thank you for watching! We love you!" Mom and Dad say, "That was a wonderful show! You are very talented and creative! We are so proud of you!" They hug and kiss Lisa and Ben. They give them some cookies and milk. They are delicious. Lisa and Ben are happy. They say, "This was the best day ever!"
