Category: Intermediate
Title: Julia the Girl and the Basket

Once upon a time there was a girl named Julia. She loved playing dress up and one day she wanted to play with her mom's makeup. She opened the wide drawer in her mom's bathroom. Julia was so excited to see all the pretty things, but her mom said she had to stay away from it. So Julia had to stay away from the pretty makeup, but she was still so curious. Sometime later, Julia's mom gave her some of her old makeup so that Julia could play with it. She put the wide brush in her pocket and the colorful makeup in her basket. Julia was so excited and went outside to put on some of the makeup. She put on the wide rainbow on her cheeks and sparkly stars on her nose. She twirled around and looked in the mirror, feeling like a princess. When it was time to go to bed, Julia's mom helped her wash off all of the makeup. Julia said she had so much fun, and she thanked her mom for letting her stay with the makeup. Now Julia can keep all of her makeup in her own basket and take out whenever she wants to play.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had an incredible trumpet. He loved to play it all day long. One day, Tim went to the park to play his trumpet for his friends. As Tim played his trumpet, a girl named Sue came up to him. "Wow, your trumpet sounds so good!" she said. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue! I love playing it for everyone." They were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and pushed Tim's trumpet out of his hands. The trumpet flew high into the sky. Tim and Sue looked up and saw a big bird catch the trumpet. The bird flew away with the trumpet. Tim and Sue were sad, but they decided to be friends and play together without the trumpet.

Lily was very happy. It was her birthday. She had a smooth pink dress and a big cake. She invited her friends to play and eat. But Tom was not nice. He wanted the cake for himself. He pushed Lily and took the knife. He cut a big piece of cake and put it on his plate. "Stop!" Lily cried. "That is not fair! You have to share the cake with everyone!" Tom did not listen. He laughed and ate the cake. He made a mess on his face and his shirt. Lily was very sad. She ran to her mom and hugged her. "Mom, Tom is mean. He took my cake and did not share." Mom was angry. She went to Tom and took his plate. She said, "Tom, you are very rude. You have to say sorry to Lily and give her some cake. Or you have to go home." Tom was scared. He did not want to go home. He wanted to play and eat more cake. He said, "Sorry, Lily. I was wrong. Can I have some cake, please?" Lily was kind. She said, "OK, Tom. You can have some cake. But you have to share with everyone. And you have to be nice." Tom nodded. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend. Happy birthday." Lily smiled. She said, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend, too. Let's play and have fun." They hugged and played. They shared the cake and the toys. They had a happy birthday.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to taste new foods. One day, her mom gave her a hard candy to try. Amy put the candy in her mouth and it tasted very sweet. Amy's friend, Tom, came over to play. They decided to watch a film together about a funny dog. The dog in the film made them laugh a lot. While they were watching the film, Amy shared her hard candy with Tom. "Wow, this candy tastes so good!" said Tom. "Thank you for sharing, Amy!" Amy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tom!" They enjoyed the sweet candy and the funny film together, and they had a great time.
