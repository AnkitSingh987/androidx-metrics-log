Category: Intermediate
Title: The Popular Bird and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a popular bird who could whistle very well. This bird lived by a little door. Every day, the bird would sit on a tree branch and whistle a happy song. The kids who lived near the door loved to hear the bird's song. They would come to the door and listen to the bird. One day, the bird saw a big box by the door. The bird was curious and wanted to see what was inside. So, the bird flew down to the box and started to whistle. The kids heard the bird's song and came to the door to see what was going on. When the kids opened the box, they found a big cake inside! They were so happy and surprised. They shared the cake with the bird, and they all enjoyed a fun party by the door. The bird's whistle had brought them a big surprise, and they all had a great day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a secret. She knew a shop that had the best toys. Lily told her friends, but they did not believe her. She wanted to show them the shop to reveal the secret. One day, Lily and her friends went for a walk. They walked and walked until they saw the shop. The shop was big and bright. Lily's friends were so happy. They knew Lily was reliable. Lily and her friends went into the shop. They played with many toys. They had so much fun. Lily's secret was revealed. Now, all her friends knew she was right about the shop. They all went home with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a cube that he loved to play with all day. The cube was very tough and strong. One day, Tim saw another cube that was bigger and better. He wanted to gain that big cube, so he took it from a girl named Sue. Sue was sad and cried because her cube was gone. Tim played with the big cube and was happy for a while. But then, the big cube broke because it was not tough like his old cube. Tim was sad and now had no cube to play with. He learned that taking things from others is wrong and it can make you lose what you have.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a theater. The theater was very interesting. Every day, many people went to the theater to watch fun shows. One day, a man wanted to sell tickets to the theater. He met a friend and said, "Hello! I sell tickets for the interesting theater. Would you like to buy one?" His friend said, "Yes! I love the theater. Thank you for the ticket!" The man and his friend went to the theater together. They watched a show about sharing and being kind. After the show, they both felt happy. They learned that being kind and sharing with others made them feel good inside. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with others.

Once upon a time, there were two little girls who loved to play. One day they were playing in the park when they found a big blue box. The two were excited, so they decided to open it. Inside the blue box were all kinds of things. There were big scissors that could cut. There were little cups with different tastes inside. The little girls were so excited. The first thing they did was cut different shapes with the scissors. Then, they tasted each cup. One of them was sweet and one was sour. The girls had so much fun and it was the best day ever! They played until it was time to go home. They hugged the blue box and said goodbye, but they knew they would come back soon.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a regular bucket that he loved to play with. He would fill it with sand, water, and toys. One day, Tim's bucket disappeared. Tim looked everywhere for his bucket. He asked his mom, "Mom, have you seen my bucket?" His mom said, "No, I have not seen your bucket, Tim. Keep looking." Tim searched in the house and in the yard. He was sad because he could not find his bucket. Then, he saw his dog, Spot. Spot was hiding under a big tree. Tim walked up to Spot and found his bucket! It was under the tree with Spot. Tim was so happy that he hugged Spot and said, "Thank you, Spot, for finding my bucket." Tim played with his bucket and Spot for the rest of the day.
