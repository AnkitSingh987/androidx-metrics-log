Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Fat Squirrel and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a fat squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to eat nuts. He had a big tree full of nuts near his home. One day, Sam saw a little bird who needed help. The little bird was hungry and wanted a nut to eat. Sam wanted to help the bird. He climbed the big tree and picked a nut for the bird. The bird was very happy and said "thank you" to Sam. From that day on, Sam and the little bird became good friends. They played together and helped each other find food. Sam learned that helping others made him very happy. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a soft cat named Lily. Lily liked to sign her name on the wall with her tail. She had her own style, and it was very pretty. One day, Lily met a dog named Max. Max said, "Lily, let's play!" Lily said, "Okay, but first, look at my sign on the wall!" Max looked at the sign and said, "Wow, nice style!" They played all day long. But then, a big rain came. It washed away Lily's sign on the wall. Lily was very sad. Max tried to make her feel better, but Lily just cried. The pretty sign was gone forever.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a beautiful sunset in the sky. The colors were so fine and pretty. Tim wanted to touch the sunset, but he didn't know how. Tim saw a big tree and thought, "Maybe I can touch the sunset if I climb the tree." He started to climb, but he struggled. He felt sad and asked a bird, "How can I touch the sunset?" The bird said, "You can't touch the sunset, but you can enjoy it from here. Sometimes, it's better to look at the pretty things from far away." Tim sat down and watched the sunset. He learned that some things are best enjoyed from a distance. And that was okay.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Tom and Sue. They lived next to each other, with a big fence between their homes. Tom had a lot of toys, but Sue had only a few. Sue always looked at Tom's toys and wished she could play with them too. One day, Tom's mom told him to share his toys with Sue. She said it was a good thing to do. Tom thought about it and decided to share his original toy car with Sue. He brought the toy car to the fence and gave it to Sue. Sue was very happy and thanked Tom. Soon, Tom and Sue played together every day. They learned that sharing made them both happy. They became the best of friends, and the fence between their homes was no longer a barrier. The moral of the story is that sharing brings happiness and friendship.

Anna was miserable. She wanted to fly her kite, but it was raining. She looked out the window and saw gray clouds and wet grass. She sighed and hugged her teddy bear. She wished the sun would come out and make the flowers bloom. She loved to see the colors and smell the sweet air. She remembered how she and her mom had planted some seeds in the garden last week. She wondered if they would grow into beautiful flowers. She heard a knock on the door. It was her friend Ben. He had a big smile and a surprise for her. He showed her his new kite. It was bright and shiny, with a long tail and a happy face. He said he had made it himself, with some paper and sticks and string. "Let's go fly it!" he said. "The rain has stopped and there is a nice breeze." Anna jumped up and put on her shoes and coat. She grabbed her own kite, which was blue and green, with a star and a moon. She and Ben ran outside, to the park near their house. They found a big open space, where they could run and fly their kites. They laughed and shouted as they watched their kites soar and dip in the sky. They felt the wind on their faces and the sun on their backs. Anna looked around and saw something else. The flowers in the garden had bloomed. They were red and yellow and pink and purple. They smelled wonderful. Anna felt happy. She thanked Ben for his surprise and his friendship. She forgot about being miserable. She had a wonderful day.
