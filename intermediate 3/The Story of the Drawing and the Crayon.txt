Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Drawing and the Crayon

Once upon a time, there was a modern house with a big upstairs. In the house lived a happy family. The family had a mom, a dad, and a little boy named Timmy. One day, Timmy found a big piece of paper. He wanted to make a drawing for his mom and dad. So, he took a crayon and started to draw. But, oh no! Timmy made a mistake and he began to tear the paper. Timmy felt sad and ran upstairs to his room. His mom and dad saw the torn paper and knew Timmy was upset. They went upstairs to Timmy's room and gave him a hug. They told him that it's okay to make mistakes and they can always get more paper. Timmy felt better and went back to drawing. This time, he made a beautiful picture for his mom and dad. They put it on the fridge and Timmy felt proud. The family was happy and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big store. In the store, there were many toys. Tim, a small boy, loved to go to the store with his mom. One day, Tim saw a dangerous toy at the tip of a shelf. He wanted to play with it. "Mom, can I have that toy?" Tim asked. "No, Tim," said his mom. "That toy is dangerous and not for you." Tim was sad but he knew his mom was right. He picked a safe toy instead. Tim and his mom went home, and he played with his new toy all day.

Lily had a red spot on her arm. It was itchy and sore. She scratched it with her nails, but it did not help. It made it worse. "Ow, ow, ow!" she cried. "Mommy, my arm hurts!" Mommy came to see what was wrong. She looked at Lily's arm and frowned. "You have a rash, Lily. Maybe you touched something you are allergic to. Don't scratch it, or it will get infected. I have some cream that will make it better." Mommy took a tube of cream from the medicine cabinet. She squeezed some on her finger and rubbed it gently on Lily's arm. The cream was cold and smooth. It made Lily feel a little better. "There, that should help. The cream will stop the itch and heal the rash. But you have to be quiet and still for a while, so the cream can work. Do you want to read a book or watch a show?" Lily nodded. She wanted to watch a show. Mommy turned on the TV and found a show that Lily liked. It was about animals and colors. Lily snuggled on the couch with Mommy and watched the show. She forgot about her arm and the rash. She was quiet and happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his red bike up and down the street. One sunny day, while riding his bike, Tim saw a small, helpless bug on the ground. Tim got off his bike and said, "Oh no, little bug! You need help." He ran inside his house to find a jar. Tim put the bug inside the jar and carried it back outside. Tim showed the jar to his friend, Sally. Sally said, "Wow, Tim! You saved the bug!" Tim smiled, happy that he could help the bug. Together, they let the bug go free in the grass. After that, they rode their bikes and played until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, in a wide green field, there lived a little pony named Pippin. Pippin loved to run and play with his friends. They would chase each other, jump over small hills, and sit under the big tree to rest. One day, Pippin saw a big rock near the tree. He thought it would be fun to jump over it. But his friend, a wise old pony named Gramps, told him to be careful. Pippin didn't listen and tried to jump over the rock. He fell and hurt his leg. Pippin felt sad and couldn't play with his friends. Gramps came to sit with him under the tree. He told Pippin that it's important to be careful and listen to others. Pippin learned his lesson and promised to be more careful next time. Soon, his leg got better, and he played with his friends again. And from that day on, Pippin always listened to Gramps and stayed safe.
