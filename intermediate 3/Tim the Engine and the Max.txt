Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Engine and the Max

Once upon a time, there was a little engine named Tim. Tim loved to put things in his cart and take them to the other side of the town. One day, Tim was feeling very healthy and strong. He wanted to help everyone he could. Tim met a big engine named Max. Max was not very nice. He told Tim that he was too small to help. This made Tim feel sad. But Tim knew he was healthy and strong, so he decided to show Max that he could help too. Tim put lots of things in his cart, more than he had ever put before. He pulled the cart across the town, even though it was hard. Max saw what Tim did and was surprised. He said sorry to Tim and they both helped the town together. In the end, Tim and Max became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat with soft fur. The cat liked to lie down in the sun. The sun made its fur warm and cozy. One day, a little bird came to the cat. The bird's feathers were dull. The bird said, "Hello, cat. Can I lie down with you?" The cat said, "Yes, come lie down with me." The bird and the cat lied down together in the sun. The bird's dull feathers became bright and pretty. They were happy friends, and they loved to lie down together in the sun.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a fast octopus. He loved to race with his friends. He would always win because he was very fast. All the sea animals liked to watch the octopus race. One day, the fast octopus found a big mess on the sea floor. He thought it would be fun to wipe the mess. He called his friends and said, "Let's see who can wipe the mess fastest!" All his friends came to help. They all wiped very fast, but the fast octopus wiped the fastest. He was proud of winning again. But when they were done, they saw that the mess was not good to wipe. It was bad for the sea. The fast octopus and his friends got sick from the bad mess. The sea animals were sad. They learned that they should not play with things that can make them sick. The moral of the story is to be careful with what you play with and always think before you act.

One day, a little sheep named Lucy went to search for some wool. Lucy had very delicate wool that was soft and warm. She wanted to find more wool to make a big, cozy bed for her and her friends. As Lucy searched, she met a big, wise owl. The owl said, "Lucy, why are you looking for more wool? You already have enough on your body." Lucy thought about it and said, "But I want to make a big bed for me and my friends." The wise owl smiled and said, "Lucy, you can share your wool with your friends. When you share, you show love and care." Lucy thought about this and decided to share her delicate wool with her friends. They all worked together to make a big, cozy bed. In the end, Lucy and her friends had a warm place to sleep. They learned that sharing is caring, and when you work together, you can make something great. And so, they all lived happily, knowing that they had each other to keep warm and safe.

One day, a little fish named Fin was sad. He sighed because it was difficult for him to find friends. Other fish were scared of him because he looked like a baby shark. Fin met a big fish named Bubbles. Bubbles said, "Hi, I am Bubbles! You look sad, why do you sigh?" Fin told Bubbles that he was sad because it was difficult to find friends. Bubbles said, "I will be your friend, Fin!" Fin and Bubbles played and had fun. They swam fast, jumped high, and laughed a lot. The other fish saw that Fin was not a mean shark. They all wanted to be friends with Fin and Bubbles. Now, Fin was happy and did not sigh anymore.
