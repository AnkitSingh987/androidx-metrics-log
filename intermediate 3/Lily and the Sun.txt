Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a box with a big jewel inside. The jewel was very pretty and shiny. Lily liked to look at it every day. One day, Lily wanted to raise the jewel up high to see how it looked in the sun. She was ready to show her friends. She went outside and held the jewel up to the sky. The sun made the jewel sparkle and shine even more. Lily's friends saw the pretty jewel and came to look. They all liked the jewel too. They played together and took turns raising the jewel up high. It was a fun day for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a park. In the park, there were many kids playing. They liked to play in the sand, go on the swings, and slide down the slides. The park was a fun place for all the kids. But, the park was also a bit dirty. One day, a little boy named Tim found a pinchy bug in the dirty sand. Tim thought it was funny to pinch his friends with the bug. He pinched his friend, Sara, and she cried. Tim laughed and pinched another friend, Max. Max cried too. Tim's friends did not want to play with him anymore. They were sad and scared of the pinchy bug. Tim felt bad and put the bug back in the dirty sand. But, now Tim was all alone in the park. He had no friends to play with. Tim sat on the swing and cried.

Once upon a time, there was an old closet. The closet was in a small room. The room had a big window. The closet had many toys inside. The toys were happy in the closet. One day, it rained a lot. The rain came in through the window. The rain started to soak the room. The toys in the closet got wet. The toys were sad and scared. A nice girl named Lily lived in the house. Lily saw the rain and the wet toys. She wanted to help them. Lily closed the window. She got a big towel. Lily dried the toys and the closet. The toys were happy again.

---

One day, a big tiger was walking in the forest. The tiger was deaf, so he could not hear anything. He liked to look at the trees and the flowers. He was very happy. A little girl was also walking in the forest. She saw the tiger and was scared. She wanted to go home. The little girl tried to run away, but the tiger saw her. The tiger did not know she was scared. The little girl had an idea. She picked up a big stick and hit it on the ground. The tiger could not hear, but he could feel the ground shake. The tiger was scared too, and he ran away. The little girl was happy. She went home and told her mom about the big tiger. They were both glad that she was safe.

Once upon a time, two friends, Will and Lily, were playing near a pond. Will was very eager to jump into the pond. "Let's jump in the water," Will said. "No, let's not," Lily said. But Will didn't listen. He jumped in the pond. Suddenly, Will couldn't see anything. He quickly imagined what had happened. "I think I'm under the water," Will said, panicking. Lily quickly tried to help Will. She jumped into the pond and grabbed onto Will's arm. But it was too late. Will had already been under the water for too long. Lily pulled Will out of the water, but Will didn't move. Will's friends were sad and scared. Will did not survive the accident. Everyone was sad, and they wished they had heeded Lily's warnings.

Once upon a time, there was a big alligator. He lived in a pond. The alligator was white. His name was Al. Al was a happy alligator. One day, Al wanted to relax. He went to the soft grass near the pond. He lay down on the grass. The sun was warm and bright. Al felt good. Soon, Al saw a little bird. The bird was blue. The bird sang a pretty song. Al liked the song. The bird and Al became friends. They relaxed together in the sun.
