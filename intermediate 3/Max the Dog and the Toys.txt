Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a troubled dog because he would always stumble when he tried to run. One day, Max saw a big stand with lots of toys in the park. He wanted to go and play with the toys, but he was scared that he would stumble again. Max met a new friend, a kind bird named Bella. Bella saw Max looking at the stand and said, "Hi Max, do you want to play with the toys? I can help you walk to the stand." Max was happy and said, "Yes, please help me, Bella." So, Bella helped Max walk to the stand without stumbling. When they reached the stand, something unexpected happened. Max saw a pair of special shoes that would help him not to stumble anymore. Max put on the shoes, and he could run and play without any trouble. Max and Bella played with the toys and had lots of fun. From that day on, Max was no longer a troubled dog, and he and Bella were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog. His name was Ring. He always enjoyed playing in the park, running around and chasing his friends. One day, he was playing when he noticed something strange on the ground. He bent down and saw it was a bottle of oil. He couldn't understand why it was there, but he picked it up and started sniffing it. He decided to take it home with him. When he got home, Ring asked his owner if he could play with the oil. His owner laughed and said "What do you have in mind?" Ring thought for a moment and said, "I want to use it to make my fur shiny and smooth!" His owner smiled and said "That's a good idea. Just be careful not to get too much oil in one place." Ring nodded and then went to the bathroom. He opened the oil bottle and started rubbing it on his fur. He smiled as he felt his fur become softer. He kept rubbing until it was all perfectly shiny. Ring stepped out of the bathroom, feeling proud of himself. His owner smiled and said "You look great Ring! I can see you've done a good job." Ring smiled and said "Thank you!" From then on, Ring was the smartest and shiniest dog in the park!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy bear named Ben. Ben was a bit broken because Lily played with him all the time. One day, Lily's mom told her it was time to do laundry. Lily said, "Mom, can Ben come too?" Her mom looked at broken Ben and said, "I recommend we wash Ben too. He is very dirty." So, Lily and her mom put all the clothes and Ben in the laundry. They watched as the laundry machine went round and round. When the laundry was done, they took out the clothes and Ben. Lily was happy to see that Ben was clean and not so broken anymore. She gave him a big hug and said, "Now we can play again!" From that day on, Lily and Ben had more fun and stayed clean together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy axe. He liked to play with his axe. One day, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a toy too. It was a ball. Tim asked Sue, "Can I play with your ball?" Sue said, "Yes, if I can play with your axe." They played together and had fun. After playing, they felt thirsty. Tim's mom gave them a drink. But the drink was bitter. Tim and Sue did not like it. They made a funny face. Tim's mom laughed and gave them sweet juice instead. They drank the juice and felt happy. Then, they played some more until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. She wanted to mix something special. She took a bowl and put some ingredients inside. Then she added a pinch of magic dust. But when Amy tried to mix it, she couldn't. No matter how hard she tried, it just wouldn't mix. Amy was sad. She didn't know why it wouldn't mix. Then, out of the blue, a friendly mist appeared. "If you want it to mix, you need to be ready," the mist said. Amy nodded. She put her hands on the bowl and said, "I'm ready." Immediately, the ingredients started to swirl. They began to mix on their own and soon there was a magical mixture. Amy was so happy that she danced around in circles. She was relieved that the mist had helped her mix her special ingredients. The end!
