Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Toma and the Present

Once upon a time there lived two friends, Tom and Toma. Tom was fat and Toma was small. Every day they would go play together outside in the park. One day, Toma had an idea. He called out to Tom and said, “I have a present for you.” Tom asked, “What is it?” Toma smiled and said, “It’s a surprise. I’ll show you when we go to the park tomorrow.” The next day, Tom and Toma went to the park. Toma said, “Come on, let’s go over there. That’s where my present is.” When they got there, Tom saw a group of children clapping in a circle. Toma said, “Look, this is your present.” He took Tom’s hand and helped him join the circle. The children all smiled, and soon the whole circle was clapping and singing together. Tom clapped with them and smiled. He couldn’t believe he had been so surprised. He said, “This is the best present ever!” The two friends clapped together for the rest of the day, and Tom knew that this was one of the best moments of his life.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who had a drum. He felt so excited to use it, but he was frustrated when nothing happened. He looked around, but no one could help him. Suddenly, the drum began to glow a bright light. He stopped feeling so frustrated and he began to smile. The drum was magical and things were happening! Every time he played with the drum, it glowed bright colors. The little boy was so happy to share his drum with all his friends. They all had so much fun. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the warm sun. One day, she found a small, blue cup in the grass. Lily wanted to open the cup to see what was inside. She tried and tried, but the cup was stuck. She asked her big brother, Tom, to help her open the cup. Tom was very strong. He opened the cup with ease. Inside the cup, they found a tiny, yellow ball. Lily and Tom played with the ball and the cup all day long, and they were very happy.

One day, a girl named Mia found a smooth stone on the ground. She picked it up and showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look what I found!" she said. Tom looked at the stone and just shrugged. "I don't know what to do with it," he said. Mia had an idea. She took a long thread from her dress and tied it around the stone. "Now we can play with it," she said. Mia and Tom played with the stone and thread all day. They took turns pulling it and chasing after it. They had so much fun! At the end of the day, they were both tired but happy. They learned that even simple things can be fun when you use your imagination.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk with her mom. They saw a tree with small green olives. Mia was tired, but she wanted to try an olive. Her mom picked one and gave it to her. Mia took a bite and made a funny face. "Yuck!" Mia said, "This olive is not good!" She started to weep because she was sad and tired. Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't cry, Mia. Let's go home and rest." As they walked back, a small bird flew down and landed on Mia's shoulder. It looked at the olive in Mia's hand and chirped. Mia stopped weeping and gave the olive to the bird. The bird ate it and smiled. "Thank you, Mia!" the bird said, surprising both Mia and her mom. "I love olives! You made me very happy." Mia and her mom smiled, happy that something unexpected had happened. They went home, feeling less tired and ready to share their story with the rest of the family.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely building in a big town. No one lived in the building. It was very sad. One day, a little bird flew to the building. The bird saw how lonely the building was. The bird said, "Don't be sad, building. I will encourage you. I will make a nest here and live with you." The building was happy to have a friend. The little bird made a nest and lived with the building. They talked and laughed together. Soon, more birds came to the building. They made nests and lived there too. The building was not lonely anymore. It was full of friends and laughter. The little bird had encouraged the building to be happy.
