Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Ben. Ben was very obedient. Every day, he would listen to his mom and dad. They loved Ben a lot. One morning, a thick fog came to their town. Ben could not see well. He went outside to play, but he got lost in the fog. Ben was scared. He did not know what to do. Then, Ben heard a sound. He listened very carefully. It was a little cat! The cat was also lost in the fog. Ben and the cat became friends. They helped each other find their way home. The fog went away, and they were not scared anymore. They were happy to be home, and their families were happy too.

Once upon a time there was a tall knight who wanted to join a parade. He got dressed in his armour and got on his horse. As he trotted down the street, people cheered. He waved to them and shouted "hello". When he got to the parade, he tried to join the parade but the other knights were too tall. He felt sad but he was determined and decided he had to try harder. He called to the other knights, "Please, let me join you." One knight said, "If you can jump higher than us, you can join us!" The tall knight was determined to try, so he guided his horse to the edge of the parade. He put his heels to his horse's sides and and with a yell, he and his horse flew off the ground. The knights watched in amazement as the tall knight jumped higher than them all. They clapped and welcomed him to the parade. The knight smiled and jumped down from his horse. He had done it - he had tried and it had paid off. He was finally part of the parade.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to act like a big bear. He would growl and stomp around his home. One day, Tim saw a big blueberry bush in his yard. He wanted to eat the blueberries, but they were up high. Tim tried to act like a tall tree to reach the blueberries. He stood on his toes and stretched his arms high. But he was still too short. He felt sad and wanted to find a way to get the blueberries. Then, Tim had an idea. He would act like a big, dry rock. He got on the ground and curled up tight. He waited and waited. Soon, a bird came to eat the blueberries. When the bird saw Tim, it got scared and dropped a blueberry. Tim caught it and ate it. He was so happy to eat the yummy blueberry.

Once upon a time, there lived three little bears. Papa Bear was old and kind. Mama Bear was warm and gentle. And Baby Bear loved honey. Every day, the three little bears would share honey with each other. One day, Baby Bear asked his Papa and Mama, "Can we share more honey today?'" Papa Bear laughed and said, "Yes, of course, Baby Bear! We always share our honey with each other." Then Mama Bear smiled and added, "Let's make some special honey today. We can use Grandma Bear's old recipe." So, that's what they did. Papa Bear, Mama Bear and Baby Bear all worked together to make the special honey. And when they were done, they shared the sweet honey with each other. The three little bears were soon full of honey and content smiles. And from that day on, they shared honey with each other every day.

One day, a chicken went to play in the yard. The chicken liked to dig and bury things. She saw a big, fair rock and thought it would be fun to bury it. The chicken met a dog and said, "I want to bury this rock. Can you help me?" The dog wagged his tail and said, "Yes! Let's do it together." They both started to dig a hole for the rock. After they buried the rock, they heard a sound. They looked up and saw a cat. The cat said, "I saw you bury the rock. Can I play too?" The chicken and the dog said, "Yes! Let's all play together." And they all had a fun day playing in the yard.
