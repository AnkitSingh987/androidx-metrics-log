Title: It and the Stick

It was a beautiful day and a proud Wilson was outside in the sun. With a stick, Wilson stirred a bucket full of water. He was humming a happy tune. Suddenly, Wilson heard a noise. It was a motor coming from a nearby car! "Can I help with that motor?" asked Wilson. The car driver smiled and said, "That's okay little buddy, I can take care of it. Thank you for offering to help though!" Wilson thanked the driver and continued stirring his bucket of water. Suddenly, he had an idea! "I know! I can use the motor to make my bucket spins!" he exclaimed. The driver smiled and said, "You are a smart kid Wilson. Let's try it out and see what happens." So Wilson attached the motor to a stick and put it in the bucket. Then he switched on the motor and the bucket began to stir. Wilson was so proud! He felt like he had made a small invention.

Once upon a time, there was a girl. Her name was Lenna. She loved playing with her little toys. One day, Lenna's dad came home and said he had to leave his job. This made Lenna sad, but her dad said it was okay. Lenna's daddy said he was off to find a new job that was more lovely than his old job. He said he was going to find something that would make him happy. Lenna was sad to see her daddy go, but she was also very happy for him. She decided she wanted to help. The next day, Lenna helped her dad look for a lovely new job. They looked all over town and finally found one he liked. Lenna was so proud of her dad that he had a new job. She was so happy for him and gave him a big hug when he went off to start working at his new job. The end.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. Amy saw a row of black birds on the fence. She wanted to play with them. "Mom, can I play with the birds?" Amy asked. Her mom said, "Yes, but be gentle." Amy walked up to the birds slowly. She didn't want to scare them away. But when she got close, the birds got scared and they scattered. They flew up into the sky. Amy was sad. She wanted to play with the birds. Her mom told her, "Don't worry, we can come back another day and try again." Amy smiled and they went home. The next day, they came back to the park, and Amy tried again to play with the black birds.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play hide and seek with his friends. One day, Tim wanted to play with gear too. He found a big gear in his dad's work room. Tim thought it was crazy to play with gear, but he wanted to try. Tim took the gear outside to play. He hid the gear behind a tree. Then, he called his friends to come and find it. His friends looked and looked, but they could not find the gear. Tim laughed and laughed because he was good at hiding. But then, something bad happened. Tim's dad came outside and saw the gear behind the tree. He was very angry because the gear was not a toy. Tim's dad said, "No more playing with gear!" Tim felt sad and his friends went home. Tim learned that he should not play with things that are not toys.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big room with many toys. She loved to play with her toys all day. One day, Amy's mom told her that they would go to the store to buy a new toy. Amy was very happy and excited. She knew that the store was a fun place to find new toys. At the store, Amy's mom said, "Before we look for a new toy, we need to buy some shampoo." They went to the shampoo aisle and picked a bottle with a picture of a happy, smiling sun. Amy thought that the sun looked very friendly. She could not wait to use the shampoo at bath time. That night, Amy's mom helped her enter the bathtub. Amy's mom said, "Let's try the new shampoo!" Amy was very excited. The shampoo made her hair feel soft and smell nice. It was easy to wash out, too. After the bath, Amy felt so clean and happy. She knew that the friendly sun on the shampoo bottle had made her bath time even more fun. And the best part was, when she was all clean, she got to play with her new toy from the store.
