Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Wealthy Man and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Bob. Bob had many sunglasses. He loved to print pictures of his sunglasses and show them to his friends. One day, Bob met a new friend named Tom. Bob said to Tom, "Look at my sunglasses! I print pictures of them. Do you want to see?" Tom said, "Yes, I want to see your sunglasses." Bob showed Tom the pictures of his sunglasses. Tom liked the pictures very much. One day, Bob and Tom went to the park. They wore their sunglasses. But it was a very windy day. The wind blew very hard. Bob's sunglasses fell off his face. They broke into pieces. Tom's sunglasses fell off too. They broke into pieces as well. Bob and Tom were very sad. They could not wear their sunglasses anymore. The end.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big cardboard box. She thought it was great. She wanted to do something fun with it. Sue had an idea, but she needed to wait until the next day. The next day, Sue woke up very early. She was very excited for her idea. She ran to the cardboard box and started to work on it. She cut and colored the box. It took all day, but she was happy. At last, Sue was done with her great cardboard box. She made a little house with a door and windows. She invited her friends to come and play. They all had so much fun in the great cardboard house. And Sue was glad she woke up early to make it.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bobo. Bobo had a powerful hug that could make anyone feel happy. One day, a little bird named Tweetie came to Bobo, looking very sad. Bobo asked Tweetie, "Why are you sad?" Tweetie tried to explain, but it was hard for her. She said, "I can't find my mom. I miss her a lot." Bobo wanted to help Tweetie feel better. Bobo gave Tweetie a powerful hug, and it made her smile. They looked for Tweetie's mom together. When they found her, Tweetie and her mom hugged too. They were so happy, and they thanked Bobo for his help. From that day on, they were all good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to go for a walk with his dog, Max. They would walk in the park, in the woods, and even in their own backyard. Tim and Max were best friends and did everything together. One day, Tim and Max went for a walk in the woods. The wind was cold and bitter. They walked and walked until they found a big tree. Tim gazed at the tree, and Max started to bark at it. They heard a strange noise coming from the tree. Tim was scared, but Max was brave and barked louder. Suddenly, a big, mean wolf jumped out of the tree. Max tried to protect Tim, but the wolf was too strong. Tim and Max ran away as fast as they could, but the wolf chased them. In the end, the wolf caught Max, and Tim had to go home without his best friend. Tim was very sad and missed Max every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cat named Tom. Tom loved to work in the garden with his mom. One day, while they were working, Tom found a small jar in the dirt. He was very excited and showed it to his mom. Tom and his mom cleaned the jar and put it on the table. They wondered what they could put inside the jar. Tom had an idea. He went outside and picked some pretty flowers from the garden. He put the flowers in the jar and showed it to his mom. She was very happy. The next day, Tom and his mom found something unexpected. The flowers in the jar had turned into beautiful butterflies! They opened the jar, and the butterflies flew all around the room. Tom and his mom laughed and clapped as they watched the butterflies. They knew that they had found a magical jar, and it made their day very special.
