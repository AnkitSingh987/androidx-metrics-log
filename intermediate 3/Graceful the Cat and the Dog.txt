Category: Intermediate
Title: Graceful the Cat and the Dog

One day, a cat named Graceful went for a walk. She saw a big tree with a cup of yogurt on the ground. She was very hungry, so she ate the yogurt. Graceful felt happy and full. As Graceful walked, she saw a dog. The dog was shivering because it was cold. Graceful felt bad for the dog. She wanted to help the dog feel warm. So, she went to find a warm place for the dog. Graceful found a small house. She thought it was empty, but when she opened the door, she saw the dog's family inside. They were all eating yogurt too! The dog was not cold, he was just excited to see his family. Graceful was happy to help the dog find his family, and they all ate yogurt together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. It was a cold day, so he wore his warm hat and coat. As he walked, he saw a small red hammer on the ground. Tim picked up the hammer and decided to take it home. When Tim got home, he showed the hammer to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a hammer!" he said. His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! What do you want to do with it?" Tim thought for a moment and replied, "I want to build something!" So, Tim and his mom spent the day building with the hammer. They made a little wooden house for Tim's toys. When they finished, Tim felt happy and proud of what they had built. The cold day became a warm memory as they laughed and played together.

One day, there was a race. Two little birds wanted to see who was the fastest flyer. They flew up high and raced against each other with their wings moving really fast. The birds flew past tall trees and furry animals. They fluttered through the sky while they raced to see who was the quickest. Suddenly, they spotted a big shade tree with lots of branches. The birds flew up to the tree and landed on the branches. It was cool and underneath was nice and quiet. The birds looked around and noticed the ground was full of spicy red berries. They gobbled them up and felt refreshed. The birds were happy to have found such a nice shady spot. After taking a rest, they flew off again, racing each other to the next exciting adventure.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He wanted to help his mom with a task. His mom was making a spicy soup for dinner. Tim liked to help his mom cook. "Mom, can I help you?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, you can help me cut the vegetables." They cut carrots and potatoes together. Tim was very happy to help his mom with the task. While they were cooking, they heard a noise. It was Tim's cat, Max. Max wanted to try the spicy soup too. Tim's mom said, "No, Max! Spicy soup is not for cats." Tim and his mom laughed. They finished making the soup and had a nice dinner together.

One day, a little bird with a big wing was flying in the sky. The bird looked down and saw a boy. The boy saw the bird and said, "Wow, I admire your big wing!" The bird was happy to hear this. The boy asked the bird, "Can you teach me how to fly with my arms like wings?" The bird said, "I can try, but you may not be able to fly like me. You can try to flap your arms like my wings." The boy tried to flap his arms, but he could not fly. He was sad, but the bird said, "It's okay. You are not a bird, you are a boy. You do not know how to fly, but that is not bad. You can do many things I cannot do." The boy felt better, knowing that it was not bad to be ignorant about flying. They both became friends and played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big cardboard box. It was in her room. Sue was very happy. She wanted to play with the box but was a little careless. Her mom said, "Be careful with the box, Sue." Sue did not listen to her mom. She played with the box all day. She put her toys in the box. She even made the box into a small house. But, she was still careless. The box started to look sad. The next day, Sue found her box on the floor. It was broken. Sue was very sad. She remembered what her mom said. She wished she had been more careful with her cardboard box.
