Category: Intermediate
Title: The Strong Model and the Sun

Once upon a time there was a strong model. Every day the model worked hard building a big tall wall. Every day the model got a little closer to finishing the wall. One day, the wall was almost finish. All that was left was a tiny little piece. The model worked even harder to get the last piece in place. With one last push, the wall was complete! The model smiled and raised its arms in celebration. It was so proud of all the hard work it had done. Its strong muscles glistened and the sun shone off them. The model was happy that it had been able to finish the wall. Each day it would look at the wall with pride and happiness. It had done such a good job!

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sarah. All she wanted to do was win a coat. Every day she worked hard to try to win one. She played hide and seek with her friends, ran around outside and asked kind people every day if they had a coat she could have. One day, Sarah was so tired. She had played and searched, but she still didn't have a coat. She lay down on the grass and looked up at the sky. Suddenly, a kind old lady walked up to Sarah. She said she had the most beautiful coat and she thought Sarah should have it. Sarah was so excited! She proudly put on the coat and thanked the kind old lady over and over. She had won her very own coat. Sarah was so happy she gave the kind old lady a big hug and ran home, her coat flying out behind her. From that day on, every time Sarah got tired of playing, she would wear her coat and remember how she worked hard to win it.

"Look at this!" Mama said. She was holding a big glass jar with a green label on it. "It's so sparkly," said Mia, her three year old daughter. Mama smiled. "It's a jar of mints," she said. She took the lid off and Mia looked inside. "It's so expensive!" she whispered. Mama laughed. "Yes, it is," she said. "But it tastes delicious too!" Mia nodded. She reached in and scooped out a mint. She put it in her mouth and smiled. Mama smiled and gave her a hug. "You're lucky to get such an expensive treat," she said. Mia hugged her back and whispered, "Yes, I know."

One day, a light, fast bug and a big, slow turtle had a race. The bug said, "I will win!" The turtle said, "I will try my best." They started to run. The bug was very fast. He saw the turtle far behind him. The bug thought, "I can rest now. I will still win." The bug took a nap under a tree. While the bug was sleeping, the turtle kept going. He did not recognize the sleeping bug. The turtle passed the bug and got to the finish line first. The turtle had a big victory. The bug woke up and saw the turtle. He said, "You won! I did not see that coming."

One day, a boy named Tim found a fake gun in his toy box. He was very happy and started to play with it. He ran around the house making "bang bang" sounds. His little sister, Sally, saw him and wanted to play too. Tim said to Sally, "You can play with me, but you have to find your own fake gun." Sally looked in her toy box and found a fake gun too. They both ran around the house, laughing and playing. After a while, they got tired and decided to return the fake guns to the toy box. They put them away and went to eat some snacks. Tim and Sally had so much fun playing with the fake guns, and they couldn't wait to play again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He liked to pretend he was a bunny. Tom would hop around the yard and eat carrots. One day, Tom found a poor, sad bunny in his yard. The bunny was hungry and tired. Tom said, "Hi, bunny! I like to pretend I am a bunny too. Let's eat carrots together!" The bunny looked at Tom and nodded. Tom and the bunny ate lots of carrots. They had so much fun together, hopping and eating. But then, a big, mean dog came into the yard. The dog saw Tom and the bunny. The dog wanted to chase them. Tom and the bunny tried to run away, but the dog was too fast. The dog caught the poor bunny. Tom was very sad. He could not pretend to be a bunny anymore.
