Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dog

One day, a little boy named Tim found a teaspoon in the kitchen. He was very happy and decided to show it to his friend, Sam. He went to Sam's house and knocked on the door. "Look, Sam! I found a teaspoon!" said Tim. Sam looked at the teaspoon and said, "Wow! That's a nice teaspoon!" They were both grateful for the new toy to play with. They played with the teaspoon all day, pretending it was a little boat. Later, they saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play with them, but Tim and Sam were scared. They decided to ignore the dog and keep playing with the teaspoon. The dog barked, but they just kept playing. In the end, the dog went away, and Tim and Sam were happy. They played with the teaspoon until it was time to go home. They were very grateful for their fun day together.

Once upon a time there was a small girl who liked to swing. Every day she would go to the park and swing as high as she could. She felt like she could almost fly! One day, her mom said it was too dangerous to swing so high. She told the little girl to wait until she could find help to make sure she was safe. But the girl didn't want to wait, so she decided to go by herself. Suddenly, she heard a loud crack. The swing had snapped and the girl was thrown through the air. She hit the ground, but luckily, she was alright! Her mom ran over and said, "Now do you see why it's important to wait for help when you're doing something dangerous?" The little girl nodded and promised to always wait for help when doing something dangerous. And from then on, she was always sure to ask for help before she swung. The moral of the story is: when you're doing something dangerous, it's important to wait for help before you try it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved summer. She liked the bright sun, the green grass, and the orange flowers. She played outside every day with her friends. One hot summer day, Lily found a big orange ball in her yard. She was very happy. She wanted to use the ball to play with her friends. They played catch, kicked the ball, and had lots of fun. As the sun began to set, Lily's mom called her inside. Lily said goodbye to her friends and picked up the orange ball. She knew she would use it again to play with her friends on another sunny summer day.

Once upon a time, there was a very hairy man. He lived in a small house. The hairy man had a friend who was a barber. One day, the hairy man went to the barber. He wanted a haircut. The barber was happy to help. He took out his scissors and started to cut the hairy man's hair. But the hairy man did not like the haircut. He began to yell. The barber felt sad. The hairy man left the barber's shop, still yelling. And they were not friends anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a thick fur cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play outside in the sun. One day, while playing, Fluffy saw a little bird stuck in a tree. The bird was scared and couldn't fly. Fluffy wanted to help the bird. He climbed up the tree and said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will rescue you." The bird looked at Fluffy and said, "Thank you, kind cat." Fluffy used his thick fur to keep the bird warm. He helped the bird out of the tree and onto the ground. The bird was so happy to be free. Fluffy and the bird became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy gems. One day, his friend Sam came over to play. They both wanted to play with the same gem. This made them very sad. Tim's mom saw them and wanted to help. She suggested, "Why don't you take turns playing with the gem? That way, you can both be happy." Tim and Sam thought this was a good idea. They decided to try it. First, Tim played with the gem, then Sam played with it. They kept taking turns and had lots of fun. Their plan was successful! They were both very happy and played together all day.
