Title: Sally the Girl and the Frog

One day, a little girl named Sally was playing with her toy horse in the bathroom. Her mom was nearby, getting ready to give Sally a bath. Sally's mom had a big bottle of shampoo to wash Sally's hair. Sally was a little anxious because she didn't like getting her hair wet. Sally's mom said, "Don't worry, Sally. The shampoo will make your hair nice and clean." Sally tried to be brave and nodded her head. As her mom opened the shampoo bottle, something unexpected happened. A tiny, friendly frog jumped out of the bottle! Sally and her mom were very surprised. The frog said, "Hello! I'm Freddy the Frog. I was stuck in that shampoo bottle, but now I'm free. Thank you for releasing me!" Sally and her mom laughed. They were happy to help Freddy. From that day on, Sally wasn't anxious about taking a bath anymore. She knew Freddy the Frog would be there to make it fun.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little girl and a big, bad wolf. The little girl loved to ride her bike in the forest. One day, the big, bad wolf saw the little girl riding her bike and wanted to ride it too. But the wolf was very selfish and did not want to share the bike with the little girl. The little girl saw the wolf and was scared. She ran away and left her bike behind. The big, bad wolf got on the bike and started to ride it. But the wolf did not know how to ride a bike well. The wolf fell off the bike and broke it. The little girl was sad because her bike was broken. The big, bad wolf felt bad too. The wolf learned that being selfish is not good. The little girl and the wolf did not become friends, and the little girl never got her bike back. The moral of the story is to not be selfish and to share with others.

Once upon a time there lived a dog named Spot. Spot always wanted to go on an adventure. One day Spot decided to go off and explore. Spot trotted through the forest and soon came across a big bone. Spot's eyes lit up and he snatched the bone up with his teeth. Just then, a big bear appeared who saw Spot with his bone. The bear said, "My bone! Give it back to me!" Spot nervously looked back, not sure what to do. Suddenly, he had an idea. Spot quickly ran off with the bone. The bear was surprised by Spot's adventurous spirit. He smiled and shouted, "You can keep the bone, little one! It's all yours." Spot, who was very happy, barked back his thanks with a big woof! From then on, Spot and the bear became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fast car. The car loved to speed down the road. It was a very successful car. It won many races. The car was red and shiny. One day, the car had to take a letter to the post. It was a very important letter. The car was excited to go fast and help. The car said, "I will be very fast and take the letter to the post!" The car sped down the road with the letter. It went so fast, it was like a red flash. The car reached the post and gave the letter. The car felt happy and successful. It had done a good job. The car went back home and told all its friends about the fun day at the post.

Once, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to hold her toy rocket. She wanted to go to space one day. Mia would play with her rocket all day long. One day, Mia took her rocket to the park. She was so happy. But then, a big wind came and blew her rocket away. Mia was sad and ashamed. She did not want to lose her rocket. Mia looked and looked for her rocket. She found it under a big tree. Mia was so happy to hold her rocket again. She promised to take better care of it. Now, Mia and her rocket are ready to go to space together.
