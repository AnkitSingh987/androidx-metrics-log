Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Lily and the Cap

Tim and Lily wanted to decorate a cap for their dad. He liked to wear caps when he went outside. They found a big cap in his closet and took it to their room. They had some stickers, crayons, and glitter to make the cap pretty. "Let's put a star sticker here," Lily said, pointing to the front of the cap. "OK, and I'll draw a heart with a crayon here," Tim said, scribbling on the side of the cap. They had fun decorating the cap. They put more stickers, crayons, and glitter on it. They made a big mess on the floor, but they did not care. They finished the cap and ran to show their dad. They hoped he would like it and wear it. "Dad, dad, look what we made for you!" they shouted, holding the cap. Their dad looked at the cap and frowned. He did not like it at all. He saw that it was his favorite cap, the one he got from his friend. He was very angry. "What did you do to my cap?" he yelled. "You ruined it! You spoiled it! How could you do this?" He snatched the cap from their hands and threw it in the trash. He scolded them and sent them to their room. He did not hug them or say he loved them. Tim and Lily felt very sad. They cried in their room. They did not understand why their dad was so mad. They only wanted to make him happy. They wished they had not decorated the cap.

Once upon a time, Noel and James wanted to have a treat together. So, Noel decided to invite James to his house. But when James came, he was doing something naughty! He was running inside, playing with Noel's toys without asking and making a big mess. Noel was angry, but he didn't want to get into an argument. So, he kindly told James that he needed to be more respectful. James felt sorry for his behavior, and he apologized to Noel. Then, they had some treats together, and they both learned a valuable lesson; it's important to be kind and respect each other!

One day, a cheerful bird found a feather on the ground. The bird picked up the feather with its beak. It wanted to take the feather back to its nest. The bird was very happy. As the bird flew, it started to sneeze. The feather was tickling its nose! The bird sneezed and sneezed. Each time, the feather flew away. The bird chased the feather and picked it up again. At last, the bird got the feather to its nest. The bird was so happy and stopped sneezing. Now, the bird had a new feather for its nest. And the bird was very, very cheerful.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys, but he did not like to clean up. His room was always a big mess. One day, his mom came into the room and saw all the waste on the floor. She was not happy. "Tim, please clean up your room. It is difficult to walk in here," said his mom. Tim did not want to clean up, but he said "okay" and started to pick up his toys. As Tim was cleaning, he found a small toy car that he had not seen in a long time. He was very happy and played with it for a while. Then, he heard a loud noise. He looked up and saw a big monster! The monster said, "I am the Waste Monster! I love messes, and your room is perfect for me to live in. I will stay here and make it even messier!" Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. He tried to clean up more, but the Waste Monster made it very difficult. Tim's room became messier and messier, and he could not stop the monster. In the end, his room was so messy that he could not find any of his toys, and he was very sad.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the museum with his mom. They saw many things like big animals, old toys, and pretty rocks. Tim liked the museum a lot. While walking, Tim heard a bitter bird. The bird was sad because it lost its friend. Tim decided to listen to the bird and help it find its friend. Tim looked around the museum and found the bird's friend near the old toys. The two birds were happy to see each other. Tim felt good for helping them and enjoyed the rest of the day at the museum with his mom.
