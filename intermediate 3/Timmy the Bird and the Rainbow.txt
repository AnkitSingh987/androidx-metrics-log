Category: Intermediate
Title: Timmy the Bird and the Rainbow

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy liked to keep his nest very organized. He would always look for the best and strongest branches to build his nest. One day, he saw a big branch that was perfect for his nest. Timmy told his friend, Sally the squirrel, about the big branch he saw. Sally was excited for Timmy and wanted to help him. They both went to see the branch together. As they looked at the branch, they knew something special was going to happen. The next day, Timmy and Sally worked hard to build the nest on the big branch. When they finished, they saw a beautiful rainbow in the sky. They knew that finding the big branch and working together made their day special. The rainbow was like a gift for their hard work and friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a pink room. She loved her room very much. In her room, she liked to play with her toys and watch her fish swim. One day, Mia's brother Max came into her room. Max wanted to watch the fish too. But Mia did not want to share. She wanted to watch the fish all by herself. Max was sad and left the room. Mia's mom saw Max looking sad. She talked to Mia and told her that sharing is good. Mia thought about it and decided to let Max watch the fish with her. Max was happy, and they both watched the fish together in the pink room. From that day on, Mia and Max became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a mom and her daughter. They went to the park to play. The daughter was a bit naughty. She liked to run and jump, but sometimes she did not listen to her mom. At the park, the mom told her daughter to stay close. The naughty daughter did not listen. She ran far away from her mom. The mom called her daughter, but she did not come back. The mom was sad and scared. She looked for her daughter everywhere. But she could not find her. The naughty daughter was lost. The mom cried, and the daughter cried too. The end.

Once upon a time there were two friends, a girl and a boy. They loved to spend time together. One day, the girl and boy started walking along the beach. It was a lovely warm day with a bright sun in the sky. They saw lots of lovely things on their beach adventure. Pretty shells, sandcastles and a big reef in the sea. As they looked out to the sea, they were amazed by the colors of the reef - reds, blues and yellows. The reef was also home to lots of sea creatures. The girl and boy loved to watch them swim around. They watched the sea creatures for hours and soon the sun started to go down. The girl and boy said goodbye to the reef, happy to have a lovely day out. They knew that no matter where they went, they would always be very good friends.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. Sue had a small purse that she loved. She thought it was very useful. In the purse, she kept her toy car, her favorite candy, and a pretty red pen. Sue's mom told her to resist playing with her purse until they got home. At the store, Sue saw a big toy bear. She wanted the bear so much, but her mom said no. Sue was sad, but she tried to resist the urge to cry. They bought some food and went back home. When they got home, Sue opened her purse to play with her toy car. But instead of her toy car, she found the big toy bear! Sue's mom had put it in her purse when she was not looking. Sue was so happy and hugged her mom. They both laughed and played with the bear all day.

Lily and Ben are brother and sister. They like to play with their toys in the living room. One day, they decide to have a tea party with their stuffed animals. They put a blanket on the floor and set up their cups and plates. "Would you like some tea, Mr. Bear?" Lily asks, pretending to pour from a teapot. "Yes, please, Lily. You are a very good hostess," Ben says, making his bear talk. They giggle and sip their pretend tea. They are having a lot of fun. But then, they hear a knock on the door. It is their mom, coming home from work. She opens the door and sees them playing. "Hello, my sweeties. What are you doing?" she asks, smiling. Lily and Ben look at each other. They feel embarrassed. They think mom will laugh at them for playing tea party. "We are... we are..." Lily stammers. "We are having a meeting," Ben says quickly. "A meeting? About what?" mom asks, curious. "About... about... important things," Ben says, trying to sound serious. "Like what?" mom asks, playing along. "Like... like... how to be nice to each other," Lily says, joining in. Mom nods and smiles. She is proud of her children. "That sounds like a very good meeting. Can I join you?" she asks. Lily and Ben look at each other again. They decide to let mom join their tea party. They think she will not laugh at them. They think she will like their tea. "Sure, mom. You can join us. We have enough cups and plates," Lily says, handing her a cup. "Thank you, Lily. You are very kind," mom says, taking the cup. They sit on the blanket and have their tea party. They talk and laugh and hug. They are happy. They are not embarrassed anymore. They are a family.
