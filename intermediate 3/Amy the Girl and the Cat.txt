Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy was an honest girl who always told the truth. She had a cat named Whiskers, who could speak like people. They were best friends and played together every day. One day, Amy and Whiskers were playing in the kitchen. They saw a yummy dessert on the table. It was a big, sweet cake with colorful icing. Amy wanted to eat the dessert, but she knew it was for her mom's birthday party later. Whiskers, the speaking cat, told Amy, "We should not eat the dessert. It is not ours." Amy knew Whiskers was right. She was an honest girl and did not want to make her mom sad. So, they played with their toys instead and waited for the party. At the party, everyone enjoyed the dessert together, and Amy felt happy that she had been honest.

Once upon a time, there was a little pink bunny named Daisy. Daisy loved to hop and play in the big green field. One day, she saw a big tree with a hole in it. "I wonder what is in there?" Daisy thought. Daisy hopped closer to the tree and heard a small voice from inside the hole. "Hello," said the voice. "I am a little bird. Can you help me? I am stuck." Daisy thought for a moment. She knew she needed to trust the little bird. "I will help you," Daisy said. She reached into the hole and carefully pulled the little bird out. The bird was so happy to be free. "Thank you, Daisy," said the bird. "You are a good friend." From that day on, Daisy and the little bird were best friends. They played together in the big green field every day. And Daisy learned that trusting her new friend was the best thing she could have done.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his dog, Spot. One day, they were playing in the park when Tim saw a trap. He was worried that his dog might get hurt. Tim said to Spot, "Look, a trap! Stay away!" Tim pointed at the trap so Spot could see it too. Spot looked at the trap and barked. He understood that he should not go near the trap. A park worker saw Tim and Spot near the trap. He came over and said, "Good job, Tim! You kept Spot safe." The worker took the trap away. Tim and Spot were happy and continued to play in the park.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a tiny flea. This flea had a rough time finding a place to live. He wanted a warm and cozy home, but he was too small for anyone to see him. One day, the flea saw a big, fluffy dog. The dog looked warm and cozy, so the flea jumped on the dog's back. The dog didn't notice the flea because he was so small. The flea was happy to have a new home. But, the dog's owner saw the flea and wanted him to vanish. The owner tried to catch the flea, but the flea was too fast. The flea jumped and jumped until he vanished into the grass. The flea was sad, but he knew he had to find a new home. And so, the little flea kept looking for a warm and cozy place to live.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a bookshelf in his room. The bookshelf was not big, it was regular. Tom liked to display his toys on the bookshelf. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the bookshelf and said, "Wow, I like your bookshelf with your toys on it." Tom said, "Thank you, I like to display my toys on it." Tom and Sam played with the toys from the bookshelf. They played for a long time. But then, Tom and Sam got into a fight. They were not careful, and they knocked over the bookshelf. The bookshelf broke, and the toys fell on the floor. Tom and Sam were sad. They could not fix the bookshelf. The day had a bad ending.
