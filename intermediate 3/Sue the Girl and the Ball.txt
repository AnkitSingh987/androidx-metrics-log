Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a small green ball that she held tight in her hand. Her palm would get all sweaty, but she loved her ball so much. One day, Sue's mom told her a story. She said that if you believe in something, it can come true. Sue wanted to believe that her ball could fly. She closed her eyes and wished very hard. Sue opened her eyes and threw her ball up in the air. It didn't fly, but it bounced high. She was happy to see it bounce and knew that believing made it bounce higher. Sue played with her ball all day, believing in its magic.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a delicate toy named Bunny. Bunny was very special to Amy. They played together all day long. One day, Amy and Bunny played so much that they felt very tired. They needed to rest. So, Amy took Bunny to her room and they lay down on the bed. They closed their eyes and rested together. It was nice and quiet. After they woke up, Amy thought it was time to put Bunny away. She opened the closet and gently placed Bunny inside. Now Bunny was safe and cozy in the closet. Amy knew that Bunny would be there when she wanted to play again. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small home, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a seed. The seed was unusual. It was big and shiny. Sue wanted to see what would grow from the seed. She asked her mom, "Can I plant the seed?" Her mom said, "Yes, Sue. Let's plant the seed in our garden." So, they went outside and planted the seed. They gave it water and sun. They waited for the seed to grow. Sue was very excited. Days went by, and the seed started to grow. It grew bigger and bigger. One day, Sue saw a big, green plant. The plant had something on it. It was a big, red fruit. Sue was so happy. She picked the fruit and shared it with her family. They all enjoyed the unusual fruit from the seed. And they all knew that Sue's seed was very special.

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to play all day with a long line. She would jump and run, trying to catch the line. One day, Kitty saw another cat playing with her line. She did not want to share her line. She tried to take it back, but the other cat did not let her. They both pulled the line, but no one would give up. At last, Kitty decided to surrender. She let the other cat have the line. To her surprise, the other cat wanted to play together. So, they both had fun and played with the line all day. Kitty learned that sharing can be fun too.

Once upon a time there was a little brown rabbit called Sarah who lived in the woods. One morning she hopped up to see what the day would bring, when all of a sudden she spoted a flea who was trying to steal an apple from a nearby tree. The flea was struggling so Sarah decided to help. Sarah hopped up to the tree and said to the flea, "What are you doing?" The flea replied, "I'm trying to steal an apple from the tree, but I can't reach it." Sarah smiled and told the flea that she could help. She stood on her hind legs and stretched out her arm - with a 'plop' the apple fell into her paw! The flea was so grateful and thanked Sarah for her help. Sarah smiled back and replied, "It's OK, that's what friends are for!"

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Jill. One day, Jack had the idea that he wanted to climb up a big hill. Jill was scared, but Jack said it would be fine. So, Jack started to go up the hill. But then he lost his footing and started to fall. Jill rushed to try and help him, but it was too late. Jack fell on his stomach and was hurt. Jill ran home to get help and soon Jack was feeling better. He learned that it is important to be very careful and listen to advice. Jack and Jill were both fine at the end of the day and they learned that it is good to be safe.
