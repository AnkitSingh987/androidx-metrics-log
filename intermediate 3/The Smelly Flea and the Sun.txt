Category: Intermediate
Title: The Smelly Flea and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a smelly flea. He loved to lean against a wall and feel the sun on his back. Every day, he would try to reach different parts of the wall but could never quite get there. One day, he decided to jump as high as he could. He leaned back, ready to go. But, he hadn't accounted for the wind that came from behind him, which pushed him much higher than he had expected. He flew high up above the wall and passed over a smelly pond with fish swimming in it. Finally, he came to land on a strong tree branch. From there, he could see everything, from the wall to the smelly pond. He leaned back, feeling the sun on his back, and smiled. He had finally made it!

One day, a little cat named Lucy saw a big wall. The wall was in her way. She wanted to go to the park on the other side. Lucy was an adorable cat with soft fur. Lucy tried to solve her problem. She thought and thought. She tried to jump over the wall, but it was too high. She tried to dig under the wall, but the ground was too hard. Finally, Lucy had an idea. She found a big box near the wall. She pushed the box to the wall and jumped on it. Now, she could jump over the wall! Lucy was happy and went to play in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a poor cat named Kitty. Kitty was always very clean. She would clean her fur every day. Kitty had a friend named Birdy. Birdy would wake up early every morning and sing a song. One day, Kitty was cleaning her fur when she saw Birdy fly by. Birdy looked sad. Kitty asked, "Why are you sad, Birdy?" Birdy said, "I can't wake up early anymore. I can't sing my song." Kitty wanted to help her friend. The next morning, Kitty had an idea. She would clean Birdy's nest for her. While cleaning, Kitty found a big rock in the nest. Kitty took the rock out and the next day, Birdy could wake up early again. Birdy was so happy, she sang her song and Kitty danced along. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a fearful little boy named Tim. He was scared to go to school because he had to wear a uniform. The uniform was big and itchy, and he didn't like it at all. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, let's sort your clothes and find the best uniform for you." So, they looked at all the uniforms and found one that was soft and just the right size. The next day, Tim wore his new uniform to school. He was still a little scared, but when he got there, he saw his friend, Sam. Sam said, "I like your uniform, Tim!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you! I like yours too!" Now, Tim wasn't so fearful anymore, and he was happy to go to school in his uniform.

Once upon a time, there was a unique frog. This frog was not like other frogs. It was very big and could talk. One day, the frog met a little boy named Tim. Tim was very surprised to see a talking frog. "Hi Tim!" said the frog. "I need your help. Can you send me back to my pond? I am lost." "Yes, I can help you," said Tim. So, Tim and the frog went on a walk to find the pond. They walked and walked, but they could not find it. Tim was sad, and the frog was sad too. Then, something unexpected happened. They found a magic rock. The rock could talk too! The rock said, "I can help you find the pond, but first, you must do something for me. Make me laugh!" Tim and the frog tried to make the rock laugh. They danced and sang, and the rock laughed so hard it shook. The rock was happy and showed them the way to the pond. Tim and the frog were happy too. They found the pond, and the frog went home.
