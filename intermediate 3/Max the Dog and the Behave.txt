Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Behave

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to have fun and play with his friends. He liked to run, jump, and bark. Max was a very glad dog. One day, Max's mom told him to behave. She said, "Max, you need to be a good dog and behave when we go to the park. You can have fun, but you must listen to me." Max shook his tail and said, "Yes, Mom, I will behave." At the park, Max played with his friends. They had lots of fun. Max remembered to behave, and his mom was very glad. They went home, and Max got a treat for being such a good dog.

---

One day, a large dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a cross on the ground. Max was curious, so he went to look at it. "Hi, I am Bob the bird," said a bird sitting on a tree. "Do you want to play with me?" Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, I want to play with you. What is this cross?" Bob the bird replied, "This cross is where I work. I put seeds here to grow plants. It helps me find my way back home." Max was excited and wanted to help Bob. Max and Bob worked hard all day. They put seeds on the cross and waited for them to grow. Soon, they saw little plants coming out of the ground. They were very happy and proud of their work. At the end of the day, Max said, "Bye, Bob! I had fun working with you." Bob replied, "Bye, Max! I hope we can work together again soon." And they went their separate ways, hoping to meet again.

Once upon a time, there was a popular girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty silver key. The key could lock and unlock a magic door. One day, Sue found the magic door and used her silver key. Inside the door, Sue met a talking cat named Max. Max said, "Hi, Sue! Can you help me? I am locked in this room." Sue wanted to help Max, so she used her silver key to unlock the door. Max was happy and said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue and Max became best friends. They played together every day. They locked and unlocked the magic door to go on fun adventures. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, tough lion. He lived in a dark, scary forest and usually kept to himself. One day, the lion was so hungry he could hear his tummy rumbling. He decided to go hunting and he soon spotted a delicious looking rabbit. The lion took a big step forward, but just then the rabbit screamed, "You need a whip!". Suddenly, an old man appeared carrying a whip and he said, "Yes, if you want to catch that rabbit, you need this whip!". The lion was so scared he shivered and ran away. He knew he'd never be able to catch the rabbit without the whip. The old man smiled and walked back home and the lion never bothered that rabbit ever again.

Once upon a time there was a silly sun who liked to make all the things in the world laugh. He was very happy and always cheered up the animals and plants. One day, the sun was feeling lonely, so he started to weep. His tears were so big that the whole world started to cry too. All the animals, birds, and plants were feeling sad too. Luckily, there was a little bird who was brave enough to come down and talk to the sun. She asked, “Why do you cry?” The sun said, “I am lonely and I have no one to play with.” The bird knew just what to do. She promised to be the sun’s friend and play with him whenever he was feeling sad. The sun was so happy to have a friend that he stopped weeping and they both flew up into the sky together. From then on, when the sun was feeling silly, he would fly down, and the bird and the sun would play together in the meadow. The End.
