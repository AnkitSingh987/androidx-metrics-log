Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Clock

One day, a brave boy named Tim saw a big clock. The clock was locked in a box. Tim wanted to unlock the box to see the clock better. He asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Tim said, "Sam, can you help me unlock this box? I want to see the big clock inside." Sam nodded and said, "Yes, Tim. Let's find the key to unlock it." They looked and looked for the key. Then, they found it under a rock. They were so happy! Tim put the key in the lock and turned it. The box opened, but there was no clock inside. Instead, they found a big, shiny toy car. Tim and Sam were surprised, but they were also very happy. They played with the toy car all day and forgot about the big clock. The clock was hiding somewhere else, waiting for another brave kid to find it.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to bake a cake. She put on her apron and went to the kitchen. She had a big bowl and a spoon. Lily put the cake mix, eggs, and milk in the bowl. She stirred and stirred until it was all mixed up. She poured the mix into a pan and asked her mom to put it in the hot oven. Lily had to wait for the cake to bake. She thought waiting was boring. So, she went outside to play. She saw a drain in the yard. Lily liked to watch water go down the drain when it rained. But today, it was not raining. After a while, Lily's mom called her inside. The cake was done baking! Lily was so happy. She and her mom put sweet frosting on the cake. They shared the yummy cake with the whole family. Lily was very proud of her cake, and she forgot all about the boring drain.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a normal day, but she liked to imagine she was a mail carrier. She would take her toy letters and give them to her family and friends. One day, Amy put on her mail carrier hat and went to give her toy mail. She gave her mom a letter, her dad a letter, and her baby brother a letter too. Everyone was happy to get their toy mail. In the end, Amy felt like a real mail carrier. She knew that her normal days could be fun if she used her imagination. Amy learned that she could be anything she wanted if she just imagined it.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia loved to eat toast. She liked her toast pale and soft. Every morning, Mia's mom would make her a plate of pale toast with butter. One day, Mia saw her mom put butter on the toast. She could not resist it. She wanted to try putting butter on the toast too. Mia asked her mom, "Can I put butter on the toast?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can try, but be careful." Mia tried to put butter on the toast. At first, it was hard. But then, she got better at it. Mia was very happy. She showed her mom the toast with butter. Her mom was proud of her. They both ate the pale toast together and it was very yummy.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run all day long. One day, while Spot was playing outside, he felt an itch on his back. He tried to scratch it, but he couldn't reach it. Spot had an idea. He saw a tree and decided to stand next to it. He rubbed his back against the tree to scratch the itch. The itch went away and Spot felt much better. Now that the itch was gone, Spot went back to being a lively dog. He played with his friends and had a great day. Spot was happy that the tree helped him, and he knew where to go if he ever had an itch again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Ben. Ben liked to run and play in the park. One sunny day, Ben went to the park with his best friend, a girl named Sue. In the park, they saw a rail. Ben wanted to jump over the rail. He ran very fast, but he did not jump high enough. Ben stumbled and fell down. He felt sad because he could not jump over the rail. Sue saw Ben was sad and gave him a big hug. She said, "It's okay, Ben. We can try again." Ben and Sue tried to jump over the rail together, and this time they did it! Ben was not sad anymore. They played and laughed in the park all day long.
