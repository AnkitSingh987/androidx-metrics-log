Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Lily and the Butterfly

Ben and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to look for insects and see what they did. They saw ants, bees, ladybugs and spiders. They were careful not to touch them or hurt them. "Look, Ben, I found a caterpillar!" Lily said, holding a green and fuzzy insect in her hand. "It is so soft and cute!" "Let me see!" Ben said, reaching for the caterpillar. "Wow, it is! Can we keep it as a pet?" Lily nodded. "Yes, let's. We can make a home for it in a jar. Maybe it will turn into a butterfly!" They ran inside the house and found an empty jar. They put some leaves and grass in it and made a hole in the lid. They gently placed the caterpillar inside and closed the jar. They put the jar on the windowsill and watched the caterpillar. They gave it a name: Fluffy. The next day, they went to check on Fluffy. But they were shocked by what they saw. Fluffy was not green and fuzzy anymore. He was brown and hard. He looked like a stick. "What happened to Fluffy?" Lily cried. "He is not Fluffy anymore!" Ben frowned. "Maybe he is sick. Or maybe he is sleeping. Maybe he will wake up and be Fluffy again." They decided to wait and see. They hoped Fluffy would be okay. The day after, they went to check on Fluffy again. But they were even more shocked by what they saw. Fluffy was not brown and hard anymore. He was not Fluffy at all. He was a butterfly. A beautiful butterfly with orange and black wings. But the butterfly was not happy. He was trapped in the jar. He wanted to fly and be free. He tried to push the lid, but it was too tight. He tried to flap his wings, but it was too hot. He felt his wings start to melt. "Help me! Help me!" the butterfly cried. But Ben and Lily could not hear him. They only saw the butterfly moving in the jar. "Look, Lily, Fluffy turned into a butterfly!" Ben said, smiling. "He is so pretty!" Lily smiled too. "He is! But he looks sad. Maybe he wants to go outside." Ben nodded. "Maybe you are right. Let's open the jar and let him go." They opened the jar and lifted the lid. The butterfly flew out of the jar and landed on Lily's hand. He looked at them with his big eyes. "Thank you, thank you!" the butterfly said. But Ben and Lily could not understand him. They only heard a buzz. "You are welcome, Fluffy!" Lily said, stroking his wings. "We are sorry we kept you in the jar. We did not know you would change." The butterfly nodded. He was glad they were sorry. He was glad they let him go. He was not mad at them. He knew they were just curious. He flew away from Lily's hand and into the air. He felt the breeze and the sun. He felt happy and free. He waved his wings at Ben and Lily. "Goodbye, goodbye!" he said. But Ben and Lily could not see him. They only saw a flash of orange and black. "Goodbye, Fluffy!" Ben and Lily said, waving back. "We hope you have a good life!" They watched the butterfly fly away and disappear. They felt sad and happy at the same time. They learned something new. They learned that caterpillars turn into butterflies. They learned that insects can change. They learned that sometimes it is better to let things go.

Once upon a time, there was a great big tank. It lived in a big yard with lots of toys. The tank loved to play with its friends, the car and the truck. They had so much fun together, rolling around and making lots of noise. One sunny day, the tank and its friends were playing a game of tag. The tank was very fast, and it was hard for the car and the truck to catch it. They all laughed and had a good time. Suddenly, the car said, "Stop! I see something new!" The tank and the truck stopped and looked at what the car saw. It was a big, shiny ball. They all went to the ball and started to play with it. The tank pushed the ball with its big wheels, and the car and the truck chased after it. They played for hours and hours until it was time to go to bed. The great big tank, the car, and the truck were all very tired, but they were happy. They knew they would have more fun tomorrow with their new ball.
