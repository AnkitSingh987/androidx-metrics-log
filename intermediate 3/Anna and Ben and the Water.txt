Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Water

Anna and Ben were adventurous twins who liked to play with their toys. They had a lot of toys, but their favorite was a water gun that could shoot water very far. They liked to shoot water at each other, at the plants, and at the birds. But they knew they had to be careful not to shoot water at the things in the house, because their mom and dad would be angry. One day, they were playing with their water gun in the backyard, when they saw a big, shiny toilet in the corner. It was a new toilet that their dad had bought, but he had not installed it yet. Anna and Ben were curious about the toilet, and they wanted to see how it worked. They ran to the toilet, and Anna lifted the lid. She saw a lot of water inside, and she had an idea. "Let's shoot water at the toilet!" she said to Ben. "Maybe it will make a funny sound!" Ben thought it was a good idea, too. He aimed his water gun at the toilet, and pulled the trigger. He shot water at the toilet, and waited for the sound. But nothing happened. He shot water again, and again, and again. Still nothing. "Maybe it's broken," he said to Anna. "Maybe we need to shoot more water," she said. They both shot water at the toilet, until their water gun was empty. They looked at the toilet, and saw that it was full of water. They wondered what would happen if they flushed it. "Let's flush it!" Anna said. "OK!" Ben said. They pushed the handle, and the toilet flushed. But instead of going down, the water came up. It came up very fast, and very high. It splashed all over Anna and Ben, and all over the backyard. It made a loud noise, and a big mess. Anna and Ben were wet, and shocked, and scared. They heard their mom and dad coming out of the house. They looked at them, and saw that they were angry. "What did you do?" their mom asked. "We shot water at the toilet," Anna said. "Why did you do that?" their dad asked. "We wanted to hear a funny sound," Ben said. They realized that they had done something bad. They felt sorry, and ashamed. "We're sorry, mom and dad," they said. Their mom and dad sighed, and hugged them. "We're glad you're OK, but you have to be more careful," their mom said. "You can't shoot water at things that are not meant for water. You could have broken the toilet, or hurt yourself, or someone else." Their dad said, "You have to respect the things that we have, and use them properly. You can't just play with them, without thinking of the consequences." Anna and Ben nodded, and said, "We understand, mom and dad. We won't do it again." Their mom and dad said, "We love you, but you have to learn from your mistakes. Now, let's clean up this mess, and then you can play with your water gun again. But only in the right places, and with the right things." Anna and Ben said, "OK, mom and dad. Thank you." They helped their mom and dad clean up the mess, and then they played with their water gun again. But this time, they only shot water at each other, at the plants, and at the birds. And they had fun, without making trouble. They learned their lesson, and they were happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a scooter in his yard. He was confused. He did not know what it was. He asked his mom to show him how to use it. His mom showed him how to ride the scooter. Tim put one foot on the scooter and pushed with his other foot. He went very fast. Tim was happy. Tim showed his friends how to ride the scooter too. They all had fun. They rode the scooter up and down the street. They laughed and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to plan things. He was very independent. One day, he planned to wear his favorite vest to the park. At the park, Tom played with his friends. They played catch and ran around. Tom's vest was very special. It was red and blue and very soft. Suddenly, a dog came running to them. The dog was big and friendly. The dog liked Tom's vest too. The dog jumped on Tom and took his vest! Tom was sad, but the dog just wanted to play. So, Tom and his friends played with the dog and the vest. In the end, Tom shared his vest with the dog, and they all became good friends.
