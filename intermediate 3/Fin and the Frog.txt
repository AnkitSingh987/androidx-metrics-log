Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin and the Frog

Once upon a time in a big pond, there were two little fish named Fin and Gill. They were best friends and loved to play in the water. One day, they saw a big frog sitting on a rock. The frog had an itch on his back. Fin and Gill swam close to the frog and said, "Hello, Mr. Frog! We see you have an itch. Can we help you?" The intelligent frog thought for a moment and said, "Yes, please. If you can splash water on my back, it will help my itch go away." Fin and Gill swam fast in the water and made a big splash. The water hit the frog's back, and his itch went away. The frog was very happy and said, "Thank you, Fin and Gill! You are very kind and smart fish. As a thank you, I will teach you a new game to play in the water." The frog taught Fin and Gill a fun game called "Jump and Splash." They played the game all day long and had a great time. From that day on, the two little fish and the big frog became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite stick. He loved to play with it in the yard. One day, the stick broke. Tim felt very sad. He started to weep. Tim's mom saw him crying. She asked, "Why are you weeping, Tim?" Tim said, "My stick is broken. I feel so poor without it." His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, we can fix it." Tim's mom got some tape. She put the broken parts of the stick together. The stick was not broken anymore. Tim was happy again. He played with his stick and did not weep anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten named Luna. Luna was very calm and loved to play with her toys. She liked to chase balls and climb trees. Luna was a happy kitten. One day, Luna found a small seed in the garden. She dug a hole and put the seed in the ground. Luna was excited to see what would grow from the seed. Every day, she would check on the seed and give it water. After a few days, the seed started to grow. Luna was very happy to see the small plant. As the days went by, the plant grew bigger and bigger. Luna would sit next to the plant and watch it grow. The plant made Luna feel calm and happy. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to school. At school, they had a quiz. The quiz was about animals. Tim loved animals, so he was happy. He knew all the answers. After the quiz, Tim's friend, Sam, said, "I don't like quizzes. They are disgusting." Tim said, "I like quizzes. They are fun." Sam thought for a moment and said, "Maybe I will like quizzes if I reverse how I think about them." The next day, Sam tried to reverse his thoughts about quizzes. He thought, "Quizzes are fun, not disgusting." The teacher gave them another quiz. This time, Sam liked the quiz. He told Tim, "You were right. Quizzes can be fun if I reverse how I think about them." Tim and Sam became better friends and liked quizzes together.

Once upon a time, a young boy named Ben was walking through the park. He wanted to get back home before it got dark. He was in a hurry when he heard a strange—but somehow interesting—noise. Ben stopped to see what the sound was. Then he saw an old man seeming to be stuck in a tree. “Help! I’m stuck,” said the old man. Ben was first scared, but then he remembered living by his mom's advice that you should always lend a helping hand. ”I can help you!” said Ben. He ran and found a big enough branch to help the old man down. But just then, it started to rain heavily. “Hurry, let's go to my house,” said the old man. “We can get shelter there." Once they reached the old man's house, Ben found out it was a nightmare! There were spiders, ghosts, and a bat at the door! The old man looked sad and said, “Thank you for helping me, Ben. You were brave and kind, even in a difficult situation. You were kind even with a stranger. That's why kindness and helping is always the best way, whatever the situation!" Ben knew he had just had a very interesting adventure and learned a very valuable lesson.
