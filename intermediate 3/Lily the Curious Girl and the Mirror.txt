Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Curious Girl and the Mirror

Once upon a time, there was a curious little girl named Lily. She liked to explore and learn new things. One day, she saw her mom putting on makeup. Lily thought it was pretty and wanted to try it too. Lily asked her mom, "Can I borrow your makeup, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "Sure, but let me help you." They sat down together, and her mom showed her how to put on the makeup. Lily felt happy and excited. After they finished, Lily looked in the mirror and saw her face with the makeup. She loved how it looked and felt special. From that day on, Lily and her mom would sometimes have fun putting on makeup together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved ice-cream. Her favorite ice-cream was the delicious chocolate flavor. One day, she went to the ice-cream shop with her mom. At the ice-cream shop, Lily saw a magic ice-cream cone. It could shrink things! Lily asked the ice-cream man, "Can I have that magic ice-cream cone, please?" The ice-cream man said, "Yes, but be careful with it!" Lily and her mom went home. Lily wanted to try the magic ice-cream cone. She pointed it at her toy and said, "Shrink!" The toy became very small. Lily was so happy. She played with her tiny toy and ate her delicious ice-cream.

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a soft, cuddly bunny. The bunny loved to play with his friends, the dog and the cat. They played all day, running and jumping in the city park. One day, the bunny saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with it, but he did not ask his friends to play with him. The bunny kicked the ball very hard, and it went far away. He felt regret because he did not share with his friends. The bunny, the dog, and the cat looked for the ball together. They found it under a tree. They all played with the ball and had lots of fun. The bunny learned to share and never felt regret again.

One day, a man and his wife went for a walk. They wandered in the woods, looking at the trees and the birds. The man saw a big, filthy puddle. He thought it would be fun to jump in it. The man said, "Look at this puddle! I want to jump in it!" The wife said, "No, don't do that! You will get very dirty!" But the man did not listen. He jumped in the puddle and got his clothes all filthy. The wife was not happy. She told the man, "Now we have to go home and clean your clothes." They walked back home, and the man had to wash his dirty clothes. He learned to listen to his wife and not jump in filthy puddles.

One day, a playful cat named Bob went for a walk. Bob liked to step on the soft grass and chase the light bugs. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. It was a beautiful day. As Bob walked, he saw a big dog named Sam. Sam was very friendly. "Hi, Bob!" said Sam. "Do you want to play with me?" Bob was scared of big dogs, but he wanted to be brave. "Yes, Sam, let's play!" said Bob. They played and played, and then they saw a light in the sky. It was not the sun. It was something else! The light came closer and closer. It was a bird with a bright light on its head! The bird said, "Hello, friends! I am here to light up the night!" Bob and Sam were surprised, but they were happy to have a new friend. From that day on, they all played together under the light of their new bird friend.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little dog named Max. Max loved to play in the park with his friends. One day, Max saw a new friend in the park. The new friend was a little cat named Luna. Max went up to Luna and said, "Welcome to the park! Do you want to play?" Luna looked at Max and said, "Yes, I want to play. But I don't know how to play your games." Max smiled and said, "I will show you. Follow me!" Max showed Luna how to play all the games in the park. They ran to the point where they could see the whole park. They played hide and seek, tag, and chase. Max and Luna had so much fun. They became the best of friends and played together every day.
