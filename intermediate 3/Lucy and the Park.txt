Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy and the Park

One day, Lucy was walking in the park when she noticed a thick fog. She thought it was interesting, but then she noticed that everyone was walking around looking unhappy. Lucy felt ashamed because she didn't understand what was going on. Suddenly, she heard a loud cheer. Everyone around the park started to cheer and wave their arms in the air. Lucy was confused but then she saw something coming through the fog. It was a bright pink balloon! Everyone was so happy that the fog had gone away. Lucy was no longer ashamed and she joined in with the cheering. Lucy was glad that the fog had lifted and that everyone had something to smile about.

One day, a big board sat in the park. It was a sunny day, and many kids came to play. A boy named Tim saw the board and wanted to play with it. He ran to the board and started to draw on it. A girl named Sue came up to the board and wanted to draw too. But Tim was rude and yelled, "No! This is my board!" Sue felt sad and walked away. The other kids saw Tim being rude and did not want to play with him. Later, a dog came to the park. The dog saw the board and ran to it. The dog jumped on the board and knocked it over. Tim was shocked and yelled, "Oh no! My board!" Sue saw what happened and came to help Tim. Together, they picked up the board and started to draw. They both learned that it is more fun to share and be kind.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely parrot. It was so pretty and it sang so beautifully. Everywhere it flew, it made the people happy. One day, the parrot flew too close to the window. Suddenly, a hand came out and slapped the parrot! The parrot was so scared. It flew away as fast as it could. The parrot flew over a forest and through the meadow until it was far away from the window and the hand. Now it was happy and safe again. It flew to a lovely tree with many different kinds of colourful birds. There it found a home and some friends. And it was happy there, until the end of its days.

Once upon a time, there was a polite little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big sea with his friends. One day, Fin saw a big, round thing in the water. He did not know what it was, but he was very curious. Fin told his friends about the big, round thing. They all wanted to see it too. They swam and swam until they found it. Fin and his friends looked at it and wondered what it could be. Fin had an idea. He would poke it to see what happens. Fin swam up to the big, round thing and gave it a gentle poke. Suddenly, it opened up, and out came a lot of pretty, shiny things. Fin and his friends were so happy! They played with the shiny things all day long. And that is how Fin and his friends found a fun new toy in the big sea.

Once upon a time, there was a big river. It was very pretty. In the river, there lived a fish. The fish was not like other fish. It could glow in the dark. At night, the fish would light up like a star. One day, a fierce dog came to the river. The dog wanted to catch the fish. The dog said, "I will eat you, fish!" The fish was scared. But it had a smart idea. The fish said, "Wait, dog! If you eat me, you will not see me glow. I can light up the dark. Let me show you." The dog agreed. The fish started to glow. The dog was amazed. The dog said, "You are so pretty! I will not eat you. Let's be friends." And so, the fish and the dog became best friends. They played together every day by the river.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy food for dinner. Mia was very glad because she loved going to the store. At the store, Mia saw many things. She saw fruits, vegetables, and toys. Then, she saw pasta. Mia loved pasta! She looked at her mom and asked if they could get some. Her mom said yes, and Mia was so happy. They took the pasta home and cooked it for dinner. Mia looked at her plate and smiled. She was glad to eat her favorite food. After dinner, Mia gave her mom a big hug and thanked her for the yummy pasta.
