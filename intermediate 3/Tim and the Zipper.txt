Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Zipper

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a zipper. The zipper was shiny and pretty. Tim liked the zipper a lot. He wanted to show it to his friend, Lily. Tim went to Lily's house to show her the zipper. "Look, Lily! I found a zipper!" said Tim. Lily looked at the zipper and thought it was very nice. They played with the zipper all day. They pretended it was a fierce snake that could bite. They laughed and had fun. Later, they decided to bury the zipper in the sandbox so they could find it again the next day. They dug a hole and put the zipper inside. They covered it with sand and made a big X to mark the spot. The next day, Tim and Lily found the zipper again and continued to play with their new toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big barrel in his yard. He liked to play with the barrel every day. But one day, Tim was feeling naughty. He thought it would be fun to push the barrel down the hill. Tim pushed the barrel down the hill, but it went too fast! It bumped into a tree and broke. Tim was sad. He wanted to improve his barrel and make it better. So, he asked his dad for help. Together, Tim and his dad fixed the barrel. They made it stronger and better than before. Tim promised to be good and not be naughty with the barrel again. Now, Tim and his barrel were happy and played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk in the park. She saw a grumpy old man sitting on a bench. The man looked sad. Amy thought she could make him happy by finding something special. Amy walked around the park and discovered a shiny rock with a symbol on it. The symbol looked like a smiley face. She thought the grumpy man would like it. So, she picked up the rock and went back to the man. "Look what I found!" said Amy, showing the rock to the grumpy man. The man looked at the rock and the smiley face symbol. He smiled for the first time in a long time. "Thank you, little girl," he said. "You made me happy." And from that day on, the grumpy old man was never grumpy again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a distant tree with a big red ball stuck in its branches. Tim wanted to get the ball down, but it was too high. Tim's mom had an idea. She took out her wallet and found a long string. She tied the string to the ball and said, "Now, we can pull the ball down together!" Tim and his mom pulled and pulled until the ball was free. After playing with the ball, Tim thanked his mom. She smiled and said, "Remember, when we work together, we can do anything!" Tim learned that helping each other is important and can make things easier.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big park with a lot of kids playing. Tim felt good because he liked to play outside. His mom said, "Let's go do some exercise and have fun!" In the park, they saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she had no one to play with. Tim's mom said, "Tim, go talk to Sue and ask her to play with you." Tim was shy, but he really wanted to make a new friend. He walked up to Sue and said, "Hi, I'm Tim. Do you want to play with me?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you!" They played together all day, running and jumping. They had so much fun doing exercise. Tim's mom watched them and knew she made a good choice by telling Tim to talk to Sue. At the end of the day, Tim and Sue were very tired but happy. They said goodbye to each other and promised to meet again at the park soon. Tim learned that talking to new friends can make a good day even better. And his mom was proud of him for being brave and making a new friend.
