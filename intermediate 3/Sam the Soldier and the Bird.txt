Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Soldier and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a shy soldier named Sam. Sam was not like the other soldiers. He did not like to talk or play with them. Sam liked to be by himself. One day, Sam went to the woods to gather sticks. He liked to make things with the sticks. As he was gathering them, he saw a little bird. The bird was hurt and could not fly. Sam felt sad for the bird. Sam took the bird home and took care of it. Every day, he gave the bird food and water. Soon, the bird got better and could fly again. The bird and Sam became best friends. They played together every day, and Sam was not shy anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big peach tree. The tree was full of big, yummy peaches. Tim wanted to pick a peach to eat. He looked up and saw a peach that was just right. But he was too small to reach it. A foolish bird was sitting on a branch near the peach. The bird looked at Tim and said, "Blink your eyes, and I will help you get the peach." Tim blinked his eyes, and the bird flew down and picked the peach. The bird gave the peach to Tim, and they both smiled. Just then, a big wind came and blew the peach right out of Tim's hand. The peach rolled and rolled until it hit a big rock. The peach broke open, and inside was a shiny gold coin! Tim and the bird were very surprised. They shared the coin and bought more yummy peaches to eat. They became best friends and had lots of fun adventures together.

Once there was a boy and a girl. They were brother and sister. One day the brother and sister went on vacation to the beach. The brother was playing in the sand with his bucket and shovel. Suddenly he saw a big fish in the ocean. He wanted to get it and ran into the water. But the water was too deep and soon the brother got scared. He started to scream. The sister heard his screaming and ran to help him. She saw that he was in trouble and quickly pulled him out of the ocean. The brother was so happy and thanked his sister for saving him. They both felt guilty for not listening to their parents. They stayed at the beach until sunset and then they went home. After that day, the brother and sister always listened to their parents and had a great vacation together.

Once upon a time, there was a cat with a long tail. The cat was very foolish. One day, the cat played near a tree. The cat's tail got stuck in the tree. The cat was sad and started to cry. A dog heard the cat cry. The dog came to the tree and saw the cat's tail stuck. The dog wanted to help the cat. The dog tried to think of a plan to save the cat. The dog pulled the cat's tail very gently. The cat cried more, but the tail came out of the tree. The cat was happy and stopped crying. The cat and the dog became best friends. The cat learned not to play near the tree anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She was very shy, and didn't like to talk to strangers. One day, she was playing in the garden when a TV appeared. It was glowing very brightly and she wondered what it was. Suddenly she heard a voice coming from the TV. “Hello! I’m the magical TV! I can shrink you and take you anywhere!” Amy was so excited. “Will I be able to see the world?” she asked. “Yes, you will. I can shrink you and take you anywhere!” said the TV. Amy was so excited that she said yes and the TV began to shrink her until she was very small. She fell through a tiny door in the TV and was whisked away. Amy was so amazed at all the different places she saw as the TV shrank her and took her around the world. When it was time to go home, the TV shrunk her back to her normal size and put her back in the garden. From that day on, Amy was no longer shy. Whenever she wanted to explore the world, she just had to ask the magical TV and off she went!
