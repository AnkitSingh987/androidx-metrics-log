Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Fast Cat and the Stick

One day, a fast cat named Tim saw some food on the ground. The food was near a big tree. Tim was very hungry and wanted to eat the food. But, he felt something was not right. Tim asked his friend, a bird named Sue, "Sue, do you see that food? Is it safe to eat?" Sue looked at the food and said, "I don't know, Tim. Be careful!" Tim went closer to the food. He saw something sharp next to the food. It was a big, sharp stick! Tim knew that the stick could hurt him. He remembered Sue's words and decided not to eat the food. In the end, Tim was happy he did not eat the food. He found more food later that was safe to eat. Tim thanked Sue for helping him. They played together and had a fun day.

One day, a boy named Tim had a cold. He did not like it. Tim wanted to prevent more colds. His mom told him to use a brush to clean his hands. Tim thought this was a good idea. Tim took his brush everywhere. He brushed his hands all the time. One day, he went to the park. He played with his friends. When it was time to eat, Tim took out his brush. He started to clean his hands. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the brush away. Tim was sad. He thought he would get another cold. But his mom said, "It's okay, Tim. Just wash your hands with water." Tim did that and he did not get a cold again.

One day, a chubby cat named Tom saw a pretty pattern on a shoe. The pattern had many colors. Tom liked the pattern very much. He wanted to learn how to tie the shoe. Tom asked his friend, a bird named Sue, to help him. Sue was good at tying shoes. Sue showed Tom how to tie the shoe with the pretty pattern. Tom tried very hard to tie the shoe, but his paws were too big. Just when Tom was sad, a little bug came by. The bug said, "I can help you tie the shoe!" Tom was surprised. He did not think a little bug could help. But the bug was very good at tying shoes. The bug tied the shoe with the pretty pattern for Tom. Tom was very happy and thanked the little bug. Now, Tom and the bug were friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to stare at things. One day, she saw a pretty cabinet. It was very attractive. The cabinet was big and colorful. She wanted to look at it all day. Lily went to the cabinet and opened it. Inside, she found many toys. There were dolls, cars, and balls. Lily was very happy. She played with the toys all day long. In the end, Lily put all the toys back in the cabinet. She closed the door and smiled. The attractive cabinet was her new favorite thing. She knew she would stare at it every day and play with the toys inside.

Once upon a time, there was a playful boy named Tim. He liked to run, jump, and play outside. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom and dad. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He heard a loud sound from the tree. A colorful parrot appeared! The parrot said, "Hello!" Tim was very happy. He played with the parrot and they became good friends. Then, something unexpected happened. The parrot started to change. It grew bigger and bigger. The parrot was not a parrot at all! It was a kind man dressed as a parrot. The man was a magician who loved to make kids smile. Tim and the magician played and laughed together. It was a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time there lived a very hairy bear. He enjoyed his days snuggling up on the grass underneath the big tree in the forest. He never had to worry about where his next meal would come from. One day the bear came to a clearing and noticed something odd. There was a large pile of fuel. The fuel was beginning to rot. It made the bear's nose wrinkle up with disgust. The bear found a nearby squirrel and asked, “What is wrong with the fuel?” The squirrel explained, “The fuel is rotting. We are all sad because that means there won't be any more fuel to help us keep warm.” The bear thought for a moment and then said, “I have an idea. I can use my furry coat to keep us warm. I'm very hairy!” And sure enough, the bear's soft fur kept everyone in the forest warm and cozy until the fuel was replaced. From then on, the bear was known as the Hairy Hero of the Forest!
