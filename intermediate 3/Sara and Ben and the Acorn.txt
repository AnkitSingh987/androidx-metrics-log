Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Acorn

Sara and Ben were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big tree with many acorns on the ground. Sara picked up an acorn and said, "Look, Ben, this is a nice acorn. Do you want one?" Ben nodded and took an acorn too. He said, "Thank you, Sara. These acorns are fun. We can pretend they are hats or balls or food." They played with the acorns for a while, making different shapes and sounds with them. Then, they heard a distant voice calling them. It was Sara's mom, who came to pick them up. "Sara, Ben, it's time to go home. Say goodbye to each other and come here," Sara's mom said. Sara and Ben hugged each other and said, "Bye, friend. See you soon." Sara said, "Ben, do you want to come to my house tomorrow? We can play with my toys and have a snack." Ben smiled and said, "Yes, Sara, I want to come. Thank you for inviting me. That sounds fun." Sara's mom said, "That's very nice of you, Sara. Ben, you are welcome to come. I will talk to your mom and arrange a time." Sara and Ben waved to each other as they walked away with their moms. They were happy to have each other as friends and to share their acorns.

Once upon a time, there was a black cat. The cat liked to take long walks and explore new places. One day, the cat wanted to find an exciting story to listen to. So, the cat went to the nearest house and requested a story. The people who lived in the house wanted to help the cat, and they said "Yes!" to the cat's request. But because the cat was black, the people were suspicious of the cat and decided to scare it away instead. The people yelled loudly and threw a big rock at the cat. The rock hit the wall and made a loud crash. The cat was very scared and ran away as fast as it could. Unfortunately, the cat was never able to find that special story it had requested. The cat felt very sad, but still kept looking for new stories.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a fragile toy. He loved to play with it all the time. One day, Tim's friend, Sally, came to play with him. They played with the toy together. Sally said, "Can you give me the toy? I want to play with it too." Tim gave the toy to Sally, but she dropped it. The toy broke into pieces. Tim was sad and a little bit mad at Sally. That night, Tim had a nightmare. In his dream, the toy came back to life and was mad at him. But then, the toy said, "I am not mad at you, Tim. I am here to tell you that friends are more important than toys." Tim woke up and felt better. He knew that even though his toy was broken, he still had his friend Sally.

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in his backyard. One day, he found a big jar while digging in the dirt. Tim called his friend, Sue, and said, "Look, I found a jar in the ground! Let's bury it again and make a treasure map!" Sue agreed, and they buried the jar together. After they buried the jar, Tim and Sue drew a map to remember where it was. They shared the map with their friends, and everyone had fun looking for the treasure. The children in the charming town learned to work together and share, making them all very happy.

John was feeling nervous as he stared at the sauce. It was his mom's special sauce and he knew that he wasn't supposed to touch it. But, it smelled so good! He could not help himself. He reached out to take some of the sauce and tasted it. John's mom soon discovered what he had done. She was very angry and began to shout. John was so scared. He just wanted to make his mom happy, but instead he had made her mad. He didn't know what to do, so he just shrugged. His mom was still mad and he started to cry. She told him that he was not allowed to touch her sauce again and stormed off. John was now all alone in the kitchen feeling very upset. He hadn't meant to make his mom mad. He just wanted some of her special sauce.
