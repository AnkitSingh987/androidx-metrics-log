Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Box

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tom. He had a fancy green bike. Tom loved to ride his bike around the town. One day, he saw an old woman with a big box. The old woman looked tired and sad. Tom went to the old woman and said, "Can I help you? I can deliver the box with my fancy green bike." The old woman smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. That would be very nice." So, Tom put the big box on his fancy green bike and rode it to the old woman's house. When he got there, the old woman was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Tom. You are very kind." Tom felt good that he had helped the old woman. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it.

Once upon a time there was a boy named John. He loved the ocean and all the animals that lived there. One day, John wanted to go swimming in the ocean. He asked his mom to take him. His mom explained that it was not safe to swim alone and she needed to stay on shore. She told him that an anchor would keep their boat safe. John listened very carefully, and his mom was so proud of how obedient he was. He put on his life vest and followed his mom onto the boat. Once the anchor was dropped, they both put on their snorkeling gear and jumped into the water. John was so excited to explore the reef and see all the beautiful fish. After a fun day in the ocean, they returned safely to shore. John was thankful that his mom had explained how an anchor kept them safe while they enjoyed the day. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to stay in the park and play with her friends. Every day, they would run and jump around the trees. Fluffy's friends always told her to be careful and stay away from the big road near the park. One day, Fluffy saw a big, shiny ball on the other side of the big road. She wanted to play with it, but she remembered her friends' order to stay away from the road. Fluffy felt sad, but she knew it was important to listen to her friends. Just then, a kind boy saw Fluffy looking at the ball. He picked up the ball and brought it to Fluffy. They played together in the park, and Fluffy was so happy. She was glad she had listened to her friends and stayed away from the big road. In the end, Fluffy and the boy became best friends, and they played together every day.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with a flexible twig. The cat said, "I bet I can ring the twig and make it move!" The dog said, "No, you can't. It's too high!" The cat tried to jump and ring the twig, but it was too high. The dog laughed and said, "I told you, you can't do it!" The cat felt sad and wanted to prove the dog wrong. The cat thought and thought, and then had a great idea. The cat found a big ball in the park. The cat said, "I will use this ball to hit the twig and make it ring!" The dog said, "You can try, but I don't think it will work." The cat rolled the ball and hit the twig. The twig rang and moved! But then, something unexpected happened. The flexible twig bounced the ball back and it hit the dog! The cat laughed and said, "I did it! I rang the twig and you got hit by the ball!" The dog was surprised but laughed too. They both had fun and played together in the park all day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big hill with lots of pebbles. He wanted to play with them. Tim was very alert and careful as he picked up the pebbles. "Mom, can I pack these pebbles in my bag?" Tim asked. His mom looked at him and smiled. "Sure, Tim. But only a few, okay?" she said. Tim was happy and packed five pebbles into his bag. As they walked home, Tim showed his mom the pebbles he had packed. They were all different colors: red, blue, green, yellow, and white. Tim and his mom talked about the pebbles and had a fun day at the park.
