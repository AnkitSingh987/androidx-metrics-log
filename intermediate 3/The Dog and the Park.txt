Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. The dog was very harmless. He liked to play with the kids in the park. They would run, jump, and laugh together. The dog was always happy to see his friends. One sunny day, the dog and the kids went to the park to enjoy their playtime. They played with a big red ball. The dog would chase the ball and bring it back to the kids. They were all having a lot of fun. As the sun began to set, it was time for the dog and the kids to go home. They were tired but happy. The dog knew he would see his friends again soon. They waved goodbye and went to their homes, looking forward to the next day of fun.

Jack was a very curious three-year-old boy. He loved to explore and often went to the museum with his mom and dad. Today, they were going to the museum again. When they arrived, Jack asked, "What will we see here?" His mom replied, "Well, it's Spring, so we'll see lots of colorful flowers and plants." Jack was very excited and ran inside the museum. Once inside, Jack started to explore. He went into a room with a very big door. Jack tried to open it, but it wouldn't open. He began to tug and pull at the door, but it was too heavy. Suddenly, the door sprang open and Jack fell into a dark and musty room. He was scared and tried to find the way out. After a few minutes, he got lost and couldn't find his way out. Jack was never seen again. His family was very sad and always curious about what happened to Jack.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Dizzy. Dizzy had a hurt leg. He could not run or play with his friends. Dizzy was sad. One day, Dizzy met a kind bird who told him, "Believe in yourself, and your leg will get better." Dizzy did not know if he could believe that, but he tried. Every day, Dizzy believed more and more. Soon, his leg felt better. Dizzy was so happy! He could run and play with his friends again. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Lily. She liked to serve food to her friends. One day, she met a dizzy bird who could not fly well. The bird was sad and needed help. Lily wanted to make the bird happy. She thought of a fun idea. She asked the bird, "Do you want to play with a spirit?" The bird said yes. Lily took the bird to her secret place in the woods where they found a friendly spirit. The spirit saw the dizzy bird and used its magic to help the bird fly better. The bird was very happy and thanked the spirit. Lily, the bird, and the spirit played all day long. From that day on, they became best friends and had many more fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy was a quick rabbit who loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Remy and his friends decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. "Ready, set, go!" said Remy, and they all started to run. Remy was very fast, but his friend Timmy the turtle was not. Timmy was very patient and did not worry about being slow. As they ran, Remy looked back and saw that Timmy was far behind. Remy stopped running and went back to Timmy. "Don't worry, Timmy," said Remy. "We can finish the race together." Timmy smiled and said, "Thank you, Remy. I am patient, and I know I can finish the race with your help." So Remy and Timmy walked together, and they both finished the race as friends.

Sara and Ben were bored. They had nothing to do in their room. They looked at their toys, but they did not want to play with them. They looked at their books, but they did not want to read them. They looked at their window, but they did not see anything fun outside. "Let's go to the attic," Ben said. "Maybe we can find something interesting there." "But we are not allowed to go there," Sara said. "Mom and Dad said it is dangerous." "Who cares?" Ben said. "They are not here. They went to the store. Come on, it will be fun." Ben took Sara's hand and led her to the door. He had a key in his pocket. He had found it under the couch. He thought it was the key to the attic. He put the key in the lock and turned it. The door opened with a creak. "Wow, look at all this stuff!" Ben said. He ran inside and started to explore. He saw boxes, bags, clothes, books, toys, and many other things. He picked up a hat and put it on his head. He looked like a pirate. Sara followed him slowly. She was scared. She did not like the attic. It was dark, dusty, and spooky. She heard noises and saw shadows. She wished they were back in their room. "Ben, can we go now?" Sara said. "I don't like it here." "Wait a minute," Ben said. "I want to see what's in that chest." He walked to a big wooden chest in the corner. He lifted the lid and peeked inside. He saw something shiny and round. He reached for it. "Ben, don't touch that!" Sara said. "It might be dangerous." But it was too late. Ben grabbed the shiny thing and pulled it out. It was a bell. A loud bell. A very loud bell. The bell rang and rang and rang. It made a terrible noise. It hurt their ears. It scared them. "Help! Help!" Sara and Ben screamed. They dropped the bell and ran to the door. They wanted to get out of the attic. They wanted to go back to their room. But the door was locked. The key was inside the chest. They had no way to open it. They banged on the door and cried. They hoped Mom and Dad would come back soon. They hoped they would hear them and save them. They learned their lesson. They never went to the attic again. They never used the key again. They never said they were bored again.
