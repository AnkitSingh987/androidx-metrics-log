Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tall Tree and the Shout

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. It wanted to shout out, but it couldn't, because of its weight. One day, a little girl came walking by and saw the tree. She said to it, "I know you want to shout! I can help you shout if you want!" The tree replied, "Yes please! I need to shout!" So the little girl put her hands on the trunk of the tree and gave it a big push. Suddenly, the tree shouted so loud that it could be heard all through the village! The little girl ran home with a big smile on her face and told everyone about the tall tree she had helped to shout. And from that day on, the tree shouted loudly whenever the little girl visited it.

Once upon a time, there was a warm pool in a big park. Many kids liked to play in the pool on hot days. The pool was a happy place where kids could swim, splash, and have fun. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily came to the pool with her mom. Lily had a big toy that she wanted to play with in the pool. The toy could mix water and make it spin around. Lily was very excited to try it out. Lily put the toy in the pool and started to mix the water. The water spun around and around, making a big whirlpool. All the kids laughed and played in the spinning water. They had so much fun with Lily's toy. The warm pool was the best place to be on that sunny day.

Once upon a time, there was a big race. Many animals came to run in the race. There was a fast rabbit, a strong bear, and an unusual snail. The snail was not like the others. He was very slow, but he wanted to try. Before the race, the rabbit said, "You are too slow, snail. You cannot win." But the snail did not listen. He wanted to fill his heart with joy and try his best. The race began, and all the animals ran as fast as they could. The rabbit was in the lead, but he got tired. He thought, "I am so far ahead. I can take a short nap." The rabbit fell asleep under a tree. The snail kept going, even though he was very slow. He passed the sleeping rabbit and won the race. The other animals were surprised, but they cheered for the unusual snail. The rabbit learned that it is important to always try your best and never give up.

One day, a clean bird was in a tree. The bird saw a big box. The bird was sad. It wanted to send the box to a friend. The bird did not know how to send the box. It was too big for the bird to carry. A nice cat came to help the bird. The cat said, "I can send the box for you." The bird was happy. The cat and the bird tried to send the box together. They pushed and pulled the big box. Then, something unexpected happened. The big box opened! Inside the box were many toys. The cat and the bird were surprised. They did not need to send the box now. They played with the toys and had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a pink bunny named Lily. She loved to hop and play all day with her friends. One day, Lily heard about a big race where the winner would get a prize. The day of the race, Lily and her friends lined up to start. "Are you ready?" asked Lily. "Yes!" answered her friends. They ran as fast as they could, and in the end, Lily won the race. She was very happy and proud. The prize was a soft, pink pillow. That night, Lily took her new pillow and went to sleep. She had the best sleep ever because of her special prize. Lily knew that hard work and fun could bring good things.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a small cabin in the woods with his mom and dad. The cabin was made of big orange logs. Tim loved the color orange, so he was very happy to be there. He knew they were going to have a fun time. In the cabin, there was a big bed for mom and dad, and a little bed for Tim. At night, Tim heard a sound outside. He was scared, but he did not want to embarrass himself by telling his mom and dad. So, he stayed quiet and tried to sleep. The next day, Tim and his family went for a walk in the woods. They saw the thing that made the sound last night. It was a cute, orange fox! Tim was not scared anymore. He was happy to see the fox and knew he should not have been embarrassed to tell his mom and dad. They all laughed and played with the fox, and had a great day together.
