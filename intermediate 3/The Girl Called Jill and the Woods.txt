Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Jill and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a young girl called Jill. Every day, Jill would take a walk in the woods around her house. One day, while walking, Jill noticed a sign that said 'No Trespassing'. Jill was confused and wondered what she could do. Just then, she heard a noise and saw a man walking towards her. He said, “I recommend that you turn around and go back the way you came. These woods are dangerous!” Jill was a bit scared but decided to listen to the man and turned away. As she left, she noticed a path on the other side of the sign. When Jill peeked down the path, she saw an empty house. Inside the house were broken windows and empty furniture. Jill realized that the man was trying to warn her away from danger and she was so glad to have listened to his advice. Moral of the story: It is important to listen to advice when it is given. Doing so could help keep you out of trouble.

One day, a girl named Sue found a telephone. She was very happy because she could call her friends. She picked up the telephone and called her best friend, Tom. "Hi, Tom! I have a telephone now. Let's talk and have fun!" Sue said. Tom was not happy. He did not want to part with his toy car. He wanted to play with it all day. But Sue wanted to talk on the telephone. This made them both sad. They did not know what to do. Then, Sue had an idea. "Tom, we can both be happy! You can play with your toy car and I can talk on the telephone. We can be comfortable and have fun together!" Tom agreed and they both played happily. Sue talked on the telephone and Tom played with his toy car. They were both very comfortable and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a king. The king liked to play games. He was very competitive. He wanted to win all the games. One day, the king played a game with his friends. They had to stir a big pot. The king stirred very fast. He wanted to win. But something unexpected happened. The pot fell over! The king was sad. He thought he would lose. But then, his friends started to laugh. They all had fun. The king learned that it was okay not to win. He was happy playing with his friends.

One day, a big, red bird named Bob wanted to travel far away. Bob was very excited to see new places. He told his friend, a small, blue bird named Tim, "I want to travel far, far away!" Tim thought it was a foolish idea, but he wanted to help his friend. So, they went on their journey together. They flew over tall trees, wide rivers, and green fields. They saw many new things and met new friends along the way. At the end of the day, Bob and Tim found a beautiful place to rest. They were very tired but happy. Bob said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me on this journey." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, Bob. It wasn't so foolish after all." They slept under the stars, excited for the next day of their adventure.

There once was a clever dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the grass. One day, Spot saw a little bird on the ground. The bird looked sad. Spot asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I can't find my mom. I need help." Spot wanted to care for the bird and help it find its mom. They looked around the grass and talked to other animals. Finally, they found the bird's mom. The mom said, "Thank you, Spot, for caring for my baby." Spot felt happy, and they all played in the grass together.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a big figure there. It was a big, friendly dog. The dog came up to Mia and said, "Hi! I am Max. Do you want to play?" Mia said, "Yes, I want to play. But I am scared of big dogs." Max felt sad, because he wanted to play with Mia. He thought of a way to make her feel better. Max said, "I am a nice dog. I will be careful not to scare you." Mia thought about it and decided to give Max a chance. They played together and had lots of fun. Mia was not scared of big dogs anymore. She and Max became good friends.
