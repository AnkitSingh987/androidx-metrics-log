Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ball of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very intelligent. He loved to play outside in the sunshine. One day, while playing, he met a new friend named Sam. Sam was sad because he didn't have any toys to play with. Tim thought for a moment and said, "I can lend you my ball, so we can play together!" Sam's face lit up with a big smile. They played with the ball all day long, laughing and having fun in the sunshine. As they played, a small bird flew down and took the ball away! Tim and Sam were very surprised. They looked at each other and decided to work together to get the ball back. They followed the bird and found their ball in a nest with baby birds. The baby birds were using the ball to keep warm. Tim and Sam decided to leave the ball with the baby birds. They learned that sometimes it's good to share and help others, even if it means giving up something you love. And from that day on, Tim and Sam became best friends, always playing and sharing in the sunshine.

Once upon a time, there was a modest town. In the town, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to read. One day, she found a big book called a novel. Lily wanted to read the novel, but it was too hard for her. Lily asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "You can poke the words with your finger to help you read." Lily tried poking the words, and it helped her read the novel better. She read the novel every day and learned many new words. One day, Lily shared the novel with her friend, Tim. Tim was happy to read the novel too. He also poked the words to help him read. They both learned that sharing and helping each other is good. The moral of the story is that we should always help and share with our friends.

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Mark. Mark loved to play in the mud. Every day, he would run and jump in the mud with his friends. One day, Mark and his friends found a big mud puddle. They had so much fun playing in it. They splashed and made mud pies. Mark's friends said, "Mark, you are the best at playing in the mud!" After playing, Mark and his friends were very dirty. They had mud all over them. They went to the river to wash off the mud. The water was cold, but they had fun splashing each other. When they were clean, Mark and his friends went home. They told everyone about the big mud puddle they found. Everyone wanted to play in the mud with Mark. He was a famous mud-playing dog, and he loved it.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a pale leaf on the ground. He picked it up and noticed a tiny insect on the leaf. The insect was green and had six legs. "Hello, little insect," Tim said. "What are you doing on this leaf?" The insect looked up and said, "I am eating. This leaf is my food." Tim smiled and put the leaf back on the ground. He watched the insect eat for a little bit. Then, he saw another insect on a nearby flower. This insect was yellow and had wings. "Hi, yellow insect," Tim said. "What are you doing on that flower?" The yellow insect replied, "I am drinking. The flower has sweet juice for me." Tim was happy to learn about the insects. He went home and told his mom about the green and yellow insects he met. His mom was proud of him for being kind to the insects and learning about their lives.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple cow named Mimi. Mimi lived on a farm with her friends. They liked to eat hay and play all day. One day, Mimi saw a big pile of hay. She was so happy and told her friend, the little pig, "Look, I found a lot of hay! Let's eat it!" The little pig said, "Wait, Mimi. We should not eat all the hay now. We need to save some for later. If we eat it all, we will spoil it." Mimi did not listen to the little pig. She ate and ate until all the hay was gone. The next day, Mimi and her friends were hungry, but there was no more hay. Mimi felt sad and said, "I am sorry, friends. I should have listened to the little pig. I spoiled the hay." From that day on, Mimi learned to share and save things for later. She became a better friend and the farm was a happy place.
