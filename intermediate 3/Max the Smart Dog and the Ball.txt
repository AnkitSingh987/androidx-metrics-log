Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Smart Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Max. Max loved to play in the yard. One sunny day, Max saw a big ball in the yard. He barked and wagged his tail with joy. Max ran to the ball and leaned on it. He pushed it with his nose and rolled it around the yard. Max's friend, a little girl named Lily, saw him playing. She came outside and said, "Max, you are so smart! Let's play together!" Max and Lily played with the ball in the yard all day. They laughed and had lots of fun. When the sun went down, they went inside the house, tired and happy. Max and Lily were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat. The cat was very hungry. It wanted to eat some food. It looked around and saw a pan. The cat went to the pan. It wanted to cook some food. But the cat did not know how to cook. It tried to cook but made a mess. Then, a nice dog came. The dog saw the furry cat and the pan. The dog wanted to help. The dog cooked food in the pan. The cat and the dog ate together. They were happy and full.

Once upon a time, there was a little orange cat. The cat lived in a big house with a nice family. One day, the cat saw a big, scary dog outside. The cat was very scared. The cat wanted to hide, so he tried to vanish. He closed his eyes and wished to disappear. But when he opened his eyes, he was still there. The dog saw the cat and started to bark. The cat ran away fast and found a safe place to hide. The family heard the noise and came to help. They took the cat to the veterinarian to make sure he was okay. The veterinarian said the cat was fine, and the family took him home. The cat was happy to be safe again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Lily. She had a wild cat named Mittens. Lily and Mittens liked to play outside. One day, it was very cold. Lily's mom said, "Lily, you need to wear mittens to keep your hands warm." Lily put on her mittens and went outside to play. Mittens the cat saw a big box. "Let's play with the box," said Lily. They played and played. Then, Lily had a fun idea. "I will wrap Mittens in the box like a gift!" She wrapped the box with Mittens inside. Lily's mom called her inside. "Time to eat," she said. Lily went in, but she forgot about Mittens in the box. Mittens was scared but then found a small hole to get out. When Lily came back outside, she saw the empty box and got scared. "Where is Mittens?" she cried. Then, Mittens came out from behind a tree. Lily was so happy to see her cat. The moral of the story is to always remember to take care of your friends and not leave them behind.

Once upon a time, there was a bear. He was a funny-looking bear and his fur was very rough. He lived in a cave far away from people. One day, the bear felt lonely, so he decided to explore the world. As the bear was walking, he spotted a little girl. The bear looked at her and then said, "Hello!" The little girl was very scared, but then she saw the bear's arm. It was big and strong. She suddenly felt brave and said "Hello!" back to the bear. The bear smiled and said, "Let's be friends!" He then roared so loud that the little girl started to giggle. From that day on, the bear and the little girl were the best of friends. They explored the forest together, playing and laughing.

Once upon a time, there was a funny woman who loved to make people laugh. She lived in a small house with her cat. The cat was very playful and liked to chase its tail. One sunny day, the funny woman felt the urge to go outside and play. She put on her hat and went to the park. There, she saw many children playing with their toys. The funny woman started to make funny faces and dance around. The children laughed and clapped their hands. The funny woman felt happy that she could make the children laugh. She urged them to join her in the fun. They all danced and played together until the sun went down. Then, they all went back to their homes with big smiles on their faces. The funny woman and her cat went back to their small house, feeling happy and tired from a day full of fun.
