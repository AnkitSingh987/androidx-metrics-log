Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Tom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play with her toy bear named Tom. One day, Mia and Tom had a tea party. They sat on the floor with a small table and tiny cups. Mia said, "Tom, would you like some tea?" Tom nodded his head. Mia poured some tea into Tom's cup. Tom took a sip and said, "Yummy! This tea is so good!" After the tea party, Mia saw that Tom was dirty. She took a soft cloth and began to rub Tom's fur. "Don't worry, Tom. I will make you clean again," Mia said. Tom smiled as Mia rubbed his fur gently. Soon, Tom was clean and soft again. Mia hugged Tom and said, "Now you are nice and clean! Let's have another tea party tomorrow." Tom nodded happily, and they played together all day long.

Tom and Lily were playing in the backyard. They had a big box that they pretended was a spaceship. They took turns being the pilot and the co-pilot. "Tom, it's my turn to be the pilot now," Lily said. "You have been the pilot for a long time." "No, I want to be the pilot more," Tom said. "You can be the co-pilot again." "But that's not fair," Lily said. "We have to share the box. And the co-pilot seat is uncomfortable. It has a hole in it." Tom did not listen to Lily. He stayed in the pilot seat and made spaceship noises. Lily was angry. She got out of the box and walked away. "Fine, then I will play something else," she said. She saw a ball on the grass. She picked it up and threw it at the box. The ball hit the box hard and made a loud sound. Tom was scared. He jumped out of the box and looked at Lily. "Why did you do that?" he asked. "You broke our spaceship." "I didn't break it," Lily said. "I just wanted to pay you back for being mean to me. You didn't share the box with me." Tom felt sorry. He realized he was wrong. He went to Lily and gave her a hug. "I'm sorry, Lily," he said. "You are right. We have to share the box. Do you want to be the pilot now?" Lily smiled. She forgave Tom. She took his hand and they went back to the box. "OK, I will be the pilot now," she said. "But you can be the pilot again later. And we can fix the hole in the co-pilot seat." They got into the box and resumed their spaceship adventure. They were happy and had fun. They learned to share and be nice to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Wednesday. Wednesday was a very good dog. One day, Wednesday was playing with a ball. The ball was big and red. Wednesday loved the ball very much. While playing, Wednesday's paw started to hurt. Wednesday did not like this. Wednesday wanted help to feel better. Wednesday went to find a friend. The friend was a kind cat. The cat saw that Wednesday was not happy. Wednesday said, "Please, help my paw. It hurt." The cat wanted to help Wednesday. The cat licked Wednesday's paw to make it feel better. Soon, Wednesday's paw did not hurt anymore. Wednesday was very happy and said, "Thank you, cat!" From that day on, Wednesday and the cat were best friends. They played together every day. They took care of each other and always said "please" and "thank you." And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim was a very good boy, but one day, he met a rude bird. The bird would fly around and take things from the other animals. Tim did not like this at all. One day, the wind was very strong. The rude bird was flying around, and the wind blew it into a tree. The bird got stuck and cried for help. Tim heard the bird and said, "I will save you, but you must promise not to be rude anymore." The bird agreed, and Tim helped the bird get out of the tree. The bird said, "Thank you, Tim. I will not be rude anymore." After that day, the bird was very nice to all the other animals, and they all became good friends. And they all lived happily ever after.
