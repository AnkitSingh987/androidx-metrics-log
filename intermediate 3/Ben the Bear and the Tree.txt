Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Bear and the Tree

One day, a bear named Ben went to find honey. He loved honey a lot. He saw a bee and asked, "Where can I find honey?" The bee said, "Follow me, I will show you the way." They found a big tree with honey in it. Ben was very happy. He took some honey and started to eat. A squirrel named Sam was watching. Sam seemed jealous of Ben. Sam wanted honey too, but he was too small to get it. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the tree down. Now, Sam could reach the honey too. Sam and Ben shared the honey and became friends. They were not jealous anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was an obedient dog. He always did what his owner, Tim, told him to do. They lived in a small house with a big yard. One day, Tim said to Spot, "Spot, we need to wash your chain. It is dirty." Tim took the chain and a bucket of water. They went outside to wash the chain. Spot watched as Tim put the chain in the water and scrubbed it clean. After the chain was clean, Tim said, "Good job, Spot! Now you have a clean chain to play with." Spot wagged his tail and barked happily. They played in the yard with the clean chain all day long.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a letter on the ground. She picked it up and saw it had a pretty stamp. Sue wanted to know what the letter said. She took the letter to her mom who was in the kitchen. "Mom, can you read this?" asked Sue, giving the letter to her mom. Her mom read the letter out loud. "Oh no, this letter is for Mr. Smith. His cat is hurt and needs help." Sue felt sad for the hurt cat. She knew what she had to do. She put on her shoes and went to Mr. Smith's house. When she got there, she saw Mr. Smith looking worried. "Mr. Smith, I found this letter for you," Sue said, giving him the letter. Mr. Smith read the letter and smiled. "Thank you, Sue. Now I can help my hurt cat." Sue walked back home, happy that she could help Mr. Smith and his hurt cat. She lay in her bed that night, feeling proud of what she did.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boring door with a shiny knob. Behind the door, there was a big room full of toys. Every day, a little girl named Lily played with her toys in the room. One day, while playing, Lily saw the shiny knob on the boring door start to wiggle. She stopped playing and watched. What will happen, she thought. The door opened, and a funny looking cat walked in. The cat wore a big hat and had a big smile. Lily and the funny cat played with the toys all day. They laughed and had fun. The boring door and shiny knob were not boring anymore. They became the door to fun and games. And from that day on, every time Lily opened the door, something fun would happen.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Buddy. Buddy was very hungry. He went to the kitchen and saw a big steak on the table. He wanted to eat the steak, but he knew it was not for him. Buddy was not foolish. Buddy's friend, a little cat named Mimi, came into the kitchen. Mimi saw the steak too. She said, "Buddy, let's eat the steak!" Buddy paused and thought about what to do. Buddy said, "No, Mimi, we cannot eat the steak. It is not for us." Mimi was sad, but she knew Buddy was right. They went outside to play together, and they forgot about the steak. Later, their owner gave them a yummy treat for being good.

Once upon a time, there was a weak monkey named Momo. Momo lived with his friends in the big tree. One day, they all felt very hot. They wanted to drink water, but they could not find any. Momo saw a big cloud in the sky. He thought, "Maybe the cloud has water." He climbed up and up, until he reached the cloud. Momo asked the cloud, "Can you pour some water for my friends? They are very hot and thirsty." The cloud said, "Yes, I can help, but you must promise to be strong and help your friends too." Momo promised to be strong and help his friends. The cloud poured water, and it rained. The monkeys drank the water and felt happy. From that day on, Momo became a strong monkey. He always helped his friends when they needed him. The other monkeys learned from Momo, and they all helped each other too. They learned that when they worked together, they could do anything.
