Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Story

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim wanted to play hockey. He was very excited but also a little impatient. He wanted to be the best hockey player right away. One day, Tim was playing hockey with his friend, Sam. Sam was good at hockey, but Tim was not. Tim got sad and said, "I can't play hockey. I'm not good like you." Sam smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can practice together. I will encourage you." They practiced every day. Sometimes, Tim felt like giving up, but Sam always encouraged him. They had a lot of fun playing hockey together. After some time, Tim became better at hockey. One day, they played a game with other kids. Tim scored a goal, and everyone cheered for him. Tim was so happy, and he thanked Sam for encouraging him. Sam said, "See, Tim? You can do it if you try and don't give up." The moral of the story is that with patience, practice, and encouragement from friends, we can achieve our goals.

One day, a little bird named Tim was very anxious. He did not know how to whistle. All his friends could whistle, but not Tim. He felt sad and wanted to learn. Tim went to his friend, the wise old owl. "Help me whistle, please," he said. The wise old owl took Tim to a small hut in the woods. In the hut, there was a big mirror. "Look in the mirror and try to whistle," said the wise old owl. Tim looked in the mirror and tried to whistle. At first, he could not do it. But he kept trying. Soon, Tim could whistle a little bit. He was so happy! The wise old owl told him, "Keep practicing and you will get better." Tim thanked the wise old owl and went back to his friends. Now, Tim could whistle with all his friends and they played happily together.

One day, a big, impressive dog saw a billboard. The billboard had a picture of a big cup with a drink. The dog felt very thirsty. He wanted to find the drink in the picture. The dog walked and walked, looking for the drink. He asked a small cat, "Do you know where I can find the drink in the billboard?" The cat said, "I don't know, but maybe the bird can help you." The dog went to the bird and asked, "Do you know where I can find the drink in the billboard?" The bird said, "Yes, I saw it near the big tree." The dog went to the big tree and found the drink. The dog was very happy to find the drink. He drank it all and felt much better. Now, he was not thirsty anymore. The dog, the cat, and the bird all played together near the big tree. They were happy to be friends.

Tommy was a little elephant who loved to play with his friends. He liked to swing his trunk and spray water on them. He also liked to make loud noises with his trunk and ears. He was very good at making noises. One day, he heard that there was a big show in the jungle. All the animals were going to show their talents. Tommy wanted to join the show. He thought he could make the best noises of all. He practiced his noises every day until he was ready. The day of the show came and Tommy was very excited. He went to the stage and waited for his turn. He saw his friends and waved his trunk at them. They waved back and smiled. He saw the other animals do their acts. He saw a monkey swing from a vine, a bird sing a song, a snake slither in a circle, and a lion roar. The animals all applauded for each other. Then it was Tommy's turn. He walked to the center of the stage and lifted his trunk. He was about to make his first noise when he saw something that made him stop. He saw a big mouse running across the stage. Tommy was afraid of mice. He forgot his noises and ran away. He tripped over his trunk and fell down. He felt miserable. He heard the animals laugh at him. He wanted to hide. But then he heard another sound. He heard his friends clap their trunks for him. They were not laughing at him. They were cheering for him. They liked his act. They thought it was funny and cute. They ran to the stage and helped him up. They hugged him and told him he did a great job. Tommy felt happy. He realized he had made a new noise. He had made a squeak. He squeaked again and made his friends laugh. The animals all applauded for him. He bowed his trunk and smiled. He was not afraid of mice anymore. He was proud of his noises. He had fun at the show.
