Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Soft Bunny and the Blanket

Once upon a time, there was a soft little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to play in the wild with his friends. One day, he heard his mommy say that there was a big surprise for him. Bobo was so excited, but he had to wait. He decided to play in the wild with his friends while he waited. Bobo liked to listen to the birds sing and the leaves rustle in the wind. He played hide and seek with his friends, and they had so much fun. After a long day of playing, Bobo went back home. His mommy had a big, soft blanket for him. Bobo was so happy and remembered that his mommy said there was a surprise for him earlier. He snuggled in his new soft blanket and felt so warm and cozy. And that's how Bobo got his big surprise after a fun day in the wild.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play hockey. He played it every day with his friends. They had so much fun. One day, it was very cold and bitter outside. Tim still wanted to play hockey. He put on his warm clothes and went outside. His friends were there too. They were ready to play. They played hockey all day long. They laughed and had a good time. When they were done, they went inside to warm up. Tim and his friends loved playing hockey, even on bitter days.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy magnet. He loved to play with his magnet all day. It was his favorite toy. He liked to make things stick to the magnet. One day, Tim found a big box. The box was fit for his toys. He put all his toys in the box, but he kept his magnet. Tim wanted to show his magnet to his friend, Sue. Sue lived next door. Tim went to Sue's house. Sue saw the magnet and clapped her hands. She was very happy. They played with the magnet together. They clapped and laughed all day. Tim and Sue had so much fun with the magnet.

One day, a chubby cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little girl named Sally. Sally was looking at the sky. She said, "I love the stars in the universe!" Tom wanted to see the stars too. He urged Sally, "Help me see the stars, please!" Sally smiled and said, "Okay, let's get you up high!" They found a big hill and climbed up. Tom was excited. When they got to the top, something unexpected happened. Tom looked at the sky and saw that the stars were not stars, but little twinkling lights. Sally laughed and said, "Silly Tom, those are not stars, they are fireflies!" Tom was surprised, but he loved the fireflies just as much as the stars.

Once upon a time, there was a brave dog named Max. Max loved to play outside with his leash. He would run and jump all day long. His best friend was a little girl named Emma. Emma loved Max very much. One day, Max and Emma went for a walk in the park. Max was so happy to be outside with his leash. Emma held the leash tight so Max would not run away. They saw many other dogs and kids playing in the park. But then, a big, mean dog came up to Max and Emma. The mean dog was not on a leash. Max wanted to be brave and protect Emma. He barked and tried to scare the mean dog away. But the mean dog did not go away. Instead, the mean dog bit Max. Max was hurt and could not play anymore. Emma was sad and cried. From that day on, Max and Emma always stayed away from the park.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Spot was very impatient. He wanted to surprise his best friend, a cat named Fluffy. Spot thought and thought about what to do for the surprise. Then, he had a big idea. Spot found some paint and made a print of his paw on a piece of paper. He was very happy with his print. But, when he tried to give it to Fluffy, the paint was still wet. The paint got all over Fluffy's fur, making her look funny and colorful. Fluffy was not happy about the paint on her fur. She was upset with Spot. But Spot said he was sorry and helped Fluffy clean up. Fluffy saw the print Spot made and knew he just wanted to surprise her. In the end, they both laughed and played together, happy to be friends again.
