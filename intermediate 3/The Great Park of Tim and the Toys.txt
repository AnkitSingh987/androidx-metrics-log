Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Park of Tim and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a restless boy who always liked to run and play. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He took his favorite bag with him to carry his toys. At the park, Tim saw another boy playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play with the ball too. He asked the boy if he could play, but the boy said no. Tim felt sad and embarrassed. He went back to his mom and told her what happened. Tim's mom told him that it's okay to feel embarrassed, but it's important to be kind to others. She said, "Next time, ask the boy if he wants to play with your toys too." Tim learned that sharing and being kind to others is a good thing. From that day on, Tim always shared his toys with other kids at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who wanted to go sailing. He had never gone sailing, but he thought it would be fun. On a sunny day, he found a boat and he climbed inside of it. He didn't know how to ride it, but he was brave and he did his best. He pushed the boat away and it started to sail. He had a big smile on his face as the wind blew in his hair. He felt so free! But soon he started to get bored. He looked around, but there was nothing interesting to see. He shouted: "Is there anybody there?" Suddenly he heard a voice from the water: "Yes, there is! This is Captain Jack!" The Captain pulled up beside the boat and said: "Hey little one, why don't you join us? We're sailing to fun adventures." The little boy was so happy to have some company and went with the Captain. He had so much fun that day that he never felt bored again!

Anna and Ben were bored. They had no toys to play with. Mom was busy with laundry. She had a big basket of clothes. She said, "Do not touch the laundry. It is clean and I need to fold it." But Anna and Ben did not listen. They saw the laundry as fun. They took some clothes out of the basket. They put them on their heads and bodies. They pretended to be animals and monsters. They made loud noises and ran around the house. Mom heard the noises and came to see. She was not happy. She saw the laundry on the floor and on the kids. She said, "What are you doing? You made a mess. You need to put the laundry back in the basket. Now!" Anna and Ben felt sorry. They did not want to make Mom angry. They said, "We are sorry, Mom. We were just playing. We wanted to decorate ourselves with the laundry. We thought it was fun." Mom sighed. She said, "I know you were just playing. But laundry is not for decorating. It is for wearing and keeping warm. You can decorate with other things, like stickers or crayons. But you need to ask me first. And you need to clean up after yourself. OK?" Anna and Ben nodded. They said, "OK, Mom. We will do that. We will help you with the laundry. We will put it back in the basket. And we will say sorry to the clothes. They are small and soft. They do not like to be on the floor." Mom smiled. She said, "Thank you, Anna and Ben. You are good kids. I love you. Now, let's finish the laundry and then we can have a snack. How does that sound?" Anna and Ben smiled too. They said, "That sounds good, Mom. We love you too. And we love the laundry. But only when it is in the basket or on us."

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to collect things. He would collect leaves, sticks, and rocks. But his favorite thing to collect was radishes. He thought radishes were very tasty. One day, Remy found a very fragile radish. It was so fragile that it could break if he was not careful. Remy wanted to show his friend, Lilly the squirrel, his special radish. He hopped over to Lilly's tree and said, "Look, Lilly! I found a fragile radish! Isn't it pretty?" Lilly looked at the fragile radish and said, "Yes, it is very pretty! But be careful, Remy. It might break." Remy nodded and held the radish very gently. They both looked at the radish and smiled. Remy was happy to share his special find with his friend. And from that day on, they both collected fragile radishes together.
