Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Bird

One day, a girl named Sue found a soft thing. It was velvet. Sue loved it. She showed it to her friend, Tim. "Guess what I found, Tim!" Sue said. "It's very soft and mild." "What is it?" Tim asked. "It's velvet!" Sue said. They played with the velvet all day. They made a cape, a hat, and a small bag. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the velvet away. Sue and Tim were sad. But then, a bird flew down with the velvet in its beak. The bird gave the velvet back to Sue and Tim. They were very happy and said "thank you" to the bird. The bird became their new friend, and they played with the velvet together.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends called Mia and Dash. Mia was a bit shy and Dash was very curious. They always played together after school. One day, Mia had a special surprise for Dash - she had a new game to teach him. The game was called Smelly Shapes. The game was about matching different shapes and colours with different smells. Dash was so excited! Mia showed Dash a smelly square. "What does this smell like?" she asked. Dash smelled it carefully and smiled. "I think it smells like bananas!" he said. Mia nodded. "That's right! Now it's your turn to teach a smelly game!" Dash thought carefully, then came up with an idea. "How about a smelly circle?" he said. Mia smiled and said she was ready. Dash sniffed the circle. "Mmm it smells like apples," he said. Mia was right - it smelt like apples! Dash and Mia played the smelly game for hours. They had so much fun learning about different shapes and smells together. Finally, it was time to go home. Mia hugged Dash and said goodbye. "Let's play again soon!" she said. "I can't wait." Dash smiled and the two friends headed home, happy and full of smelly shapes and new game ideas.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a box of pastel crayons. She was very happy because she loved to draw. She ran to her mommy and said, "Look, mommy! I found pastels!" Her mommy smiled and said, "That's great, Lily! Let's draw together." They went to the open garden and sat down to draw. Lily drew a big sun and her mommy drew a pretty flower. They had so much fun drawing with the pastels. After a while, they decided to play a new game. "Let's bury the pastels in the sand and find them like treasure!" said Lily. Her mommy agreed and they buried the pastels in the sand. They took turns finding the pastels and laughed as they dug them up. They played until the sun went down and then went inside to eat dinner.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy called Magnet Man. Magnet Man was not an ordinary toy. He could stick to things made of metal. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. He saw Magnet Man and liked him a lot. "Can I have Magnet Man?" Sam asked Tim. But Tim said, "No, he is my favorite toy." Sam wanted Magnet Man so much that he decided to steal him. He took Magnet Man and ran home. When Tim found out, he was very sad. He told his mom, and she said, "We must teach Sam that stealing is wrong." Tim's mom went to Sam's house and talked to his mom. Sam's mom told Sam to give Magnet Man back and say sorry. Sam gave Magnet Man back to Tim and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I should not have taken your toy." The moral of the story is that stealing is wrong, and we should always ask before taking something that belongs to someone else.

Once upon a time, there was a big, generous fish named Fin. Fin lived in a tank with his friends. They were all very happy. One day, a little girl named Lily came to visit the tank. She saw Fin and said, "Hi, fish! I like your tank. It's so pretty!" Fin was very happy to hear that. Later, Fin wanted to play with his friends. But, as he swam fast, he started to slip. "Oh no!" he said. His friends came to help him. They all laughed and played together. And they all lived happily ever after.
