Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Cat and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a funny cat named Bob. Bob loved to make jokes. He had a friend named Sam who was a dog. They liked to play together in the park. One day, Bob wanted to prepare a special joke for Sam. He thought hard about what would make Sam laugh. He decided to put a toy mouse on Sam's head. Bob knew Sam would be surprised and laugh. When they met at the park, Bob said, "Hey Sam, look up!" Sam looked up and saw the toy mouse on his head. He laughed and said, "Bob, you are so funny! That was a good joke!" They both laughed and played together all day.

One day, a quiet pilot named Sam went to fly his big plane. He loved to show people how to fly. Sam saw a little girl named Lily near the plane. She was sad because she was lost. Sam asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily said, "I can't find my mom." Sam wanted to help Lily. He said, "I can show you how to find your mom. Let's look for her together." Sam and Lily looked for her mom. They asked people if they saw her. Soon, they found Lily's mom. Lily was happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" Sam was happy too. He knew that helping others was good. The moral of the story is that it's important to help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, a little bird lived in a big tree. The bird had a long neck and liked to fly around. One day, the bird was careless and flew into a bush. The bush had a tasty treat inside. The bird saw a big red fruit in the bush. The fruit looked so good, so the bird wanted to taste it. The bird used its long neck to reach the fruit. It took a big bite and it was very yummy. The bird was so happy it found the tasty fruit. It was more careful when flying after that. The bird told its friends about the fruit and they all tasted it too. They all loved it and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big high hill. On top of the hill, there was a small house. In the house, there lived a kind man named Tom and his little dog, Spot. Tom made things with iron. He would strike the iron to make it into pretty shapes. One day, Tom saw a sad boy named Tim. Tim was crying because he lost his toy. Tom wanted to help Tim feel better. So, he said, "Don't cry, Tim. I will make you a new toy with my iron." Tom took Tim to his house on the high hill. Tom showed Tim how he could strike the iron to make it into a toy. Tim watched as Tom made a beautiful toy horse for him. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom! You are very kind." Tom and Tim became good friends. They played with the toy horse and Spot on the high hill every day. And they learned that helping others can make you feel happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to explore. One day, she walked far away from home, until she came across a big, unknown forest. In the forest, she found a jacket. She picked up the jacket and looked around, wondering who it belonged to. She couldn't find anyone and she wanted to make sure it was safe, so she decided to take it home with her. When she arrived home, she showed the jacket to her parents. Her mom recognized it right away. She said “This looks like your uncle’s jacket.” The little girl was so excited! She couldn’t believe that she had found her uncle’s jacket in the forest. She was so happy to recognize something so familiar in such an unknown place.

One day, a little girl named Lily and her mom went to the store. They wanted to buy popcorn. Lily loved popcorn. It was delicious! At home, Lily's mom put the popcorn in a big pot. She said, "Lily, can you add some oil?" Lily added the oil. They waited for the popcorn to pop. When the popcorn was all popped, Lily and her mom sat down to eat. They shared the popcorn and talked. "Yum! This is delicious!" said Lily. Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, it is!" They were very happy eating their popcorn together.
