Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Lazy Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day and never wanted to play with the other animals. One sunny day, Tom saw a line of ants walking by his house. They were carrying food to their home. Tom watched the ants and thought, "I wish I could be like them, working together and not being lazy." Just then, a little bird named Sally flew down and said, "Hi Tom, why don't you come play with us?" Tom looked at Sally and said, "I'm too lazy to play, but I want to be like the ants in the line. They work together and don't quarrel." Sally smiled and said, "You can be like the ants too, Tom. Just try not to be lazy and join us in our games. We can all play together and not quarrel." So, Tom decided to join Sally and the other animals in their games. They all played happily together, just like the ants in the line, and they never had a quarrel again.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a big slide and wanted to play on it. She was very happy. But, she also felt a little hungry. She told her mom, "Mom, I want to play on the slide, but I am hungry." Her mom replied, "Okay, Lucy. Let's have a snack first, and then you can play on the slide." Lucy ate her snack and felt better. Her mom said, "Now you can play on the slide." Lucy ran to the slide and climbed up. She looked down and saw her friend, Tim. Tim was also hungry. Lucy remembered her snack and said, "Tim, I have a snack for you!" She gave Tim her extra snack. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lucy!" Now, Lucy and Tim played on the slide together. They had lots of fun. And because they had their snacks, they were not hungry anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly fish. This fish had a long spear on its head. It lived in a blue pond with many other fish. One day, the fish saw that the pond was very messy. It wanted to help its friends. So, the ugly fish used its spear to sort things in the pond. It pushed the green plants to one side and the small rocks to the other side. The other fish were so happy. They clapped their fins and said, "Thank you, ugly fish!" The ugly fish felt good inside. It learned that even if it was ugly, it could still help others and be a good friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a delicate toy cat that she took with her everywhere. One day, Lily and her cat went outside to play in the sun. While playing, Lily saw a big sheet hanging on a line. She wanted to make a tent for her and her cat. So, she took the sheet and put it over a chair. Lily and her cat went inside the tent to have fun. As Lily was playing, she did not see a toy on the floor. She stepped on it and started to slip. But her cat was there to catch her. Lily hugged her cat and they continued to play in their tent. They had a great day together.

Anna was a shy girl who liked to draw and write notes. She had a big box of crayons and many papers. She did not like to talk to other kids or play with them. She only liked to be alone with her notes. One day, a new boy came to her class. His name was Ben. He was loud and mean. He liked to tease and pull other kids' hair. He saw Anna sitting by herself with her notes. He thought she was weird and boring. He wanted to make fun of her. He walked up to her and said, "Hey, what are you doing with those papers? Are they your secret love letters? Let me see them!" He reached out his hand and tried to grab her notes. Anna was scared and angry. She did not want him to touch her notes. They were her special things. She said, "No, go away! Leave me alone!" She held her notes close to her chest and turned away from him. But Ben did not listen. He was stronger and faster than Anna. He pulled hard on her notes and ripped them out of her hands. He laughed and said, "Ha ha, look at these! They are just silly drawings and words. You can't even spell right. You are such a baby!" He threw her notes in the air and watched them fall to the ground. He stomped on them and tore them apart. He did not care that he was hurting Anna's feelings. He only cared about himself. Anna felt a big pain in her heart. She saw her notes, her precious notes, destroyed by Ben. She felt like crying. She felt like running away. She felt like she had nothing left. She looked at Ben with hate and fear. She wished he would go away. She wished he would never come back. She wished he would disappear. But he did not. He stayed in her class. He kept on being loud and mean. He kept on teasing and pulling other kids' hair. He kept on making Anna's life miserable. Anna never drew or wrote notes again. She never smiled or laughed again. She never made any friends or had any fun again. She only felt sad and lonely and angry. She hated Ben. She hated school. She hated herself.
