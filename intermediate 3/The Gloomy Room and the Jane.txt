Category: Intermediate
Title: The Gloomy Room and the Jane

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a gloomy room. The room had old furniture that no one liked. One day, a little girl named Jane wanted to make the room nice and bright. Jane thought of a plan. She wanted to pour paint on the furniture to make it pretty. She picked up a can of yellow paint and started to pour it on a chair. But, she poured too much and it made a big mess on the floor. Her mom saw the mess and was not happy. Jane felt sad but wanted to fix it. She cleaned up the paint and asked her mom to help her. Together, they painted the furniture and made the room bright and happy. Now, everyone loved the room and Jane was proud of her work.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a plan to find a hidden treasure in her backyard. Mia knew that if she found the treasure, she would get a big reward. One sunny day, Mia started to dig in the yard. She dug and dug, but she couldn't find the treasure. Suddenly, she saw something shiny under a big rock. Mia thought she found the hidden treasure and got very excited. But when she picked up the shiny thing, it was not the treasure she was looking for. It was a key! Mia was surprised but she didn't give up. She used the key to open a little door in her treehouse. Inside, she found the hidden treasure and got her reward - a big box of her favorite toys!

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a wild cat. This cat loved to play with colors. One day, he found a big box of colors in a house. He was very happy and wanted to enter the house to play with the colors. The wild cat entered the house and saw many colors. He played with the red, blue, and yellow colors. He made a big mess in the house. The people who lived in the house came back and saw the cat and the mess he made. They were very sad. The wild cat saw the sad people and felt bad. He wanted to make them happy again. So, he used the colors to make a beautiful picture on the wall. The people saw the picture and smiled. They loved the cat's art. The wild cat learned that it is good to make others happy. From that day, he always tried to make people happy with his colors.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty rabbit named Bob. Bob liked to play tricks on his friends. One day, he set a trap to scare his friend, the bird. Bob hid behind a bush and waited for the bird to come. When the bird came near the trap, Bob jumped out and said, "Boo!" The bird was very scared and flew away quickly. Bob laughed and thought it was funny. Later, the bird came back with a big bear. The bear said, "Why did you scare my friend?" Bob felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, I was just being naughty. I won't do it again." The bear and the bird forgave Bob, and they all played together happily.

Once upon a time, there was a little square. The square was red and obedient. It always did what it was told. One day, the square went for a walk. It was a sunny day and the sky was blue. As the square walked, it met a big circle. The circle was green and not obedient. The circle said, "Hey, square! Let's play a game." The square thought about it and said, "Okay, but we must be careful." They played and played, but the circle was not careful. The circle did not listen to the square. The sky started to get dark and gray. The square said, "We should go home now. It looks like rain." But the circle did not listen. It kept playing and playing. Then, the rain came down hard. The square tried to go home, but it was too late. The rain washed the square away, and it was never seen again. The circle was sad and learned to be more obedient.
