Category: Intermediate
Title: Joe and Chuck and the Cat

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, a penguin named Joe and a cat named Chuck. They lived together in a very warm and cozy house with big windows. One day, Chuck was looking out of the window and saw something very high in the sky. "What is that Joe?!" he asked his friend. Joe came closer and said, "It looks like a big bird - it's a high flying plane!" But Chuck was not so sure, he was a bit scared by the high flying plane. "Let's go and take a closer look," Joe said. But to Chuck's dismay, he could not fly in the sky and he was so sad. Joe told him, "Don't worry, I'll try to find a way to get us up there to see that plane." So they set off and searched for something that could help them get high up in the sky and navigate the plane. At first, it didn't seem successful, but then they found some balloons! Chuck got so excited and quickly said, "Let's use the balloons and fly up to the plane." Joe nodded and agreed. At last, they reached the plane and had a great look. It was exciting and fun! But then it was time to end their journey and sadly they both floated down back to their cozy house.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy who wanted to go on a yacht. He asked his parents for permission, but they said it wasn't available. He was very sad. The boy was determined. He marched all around the house looking for a way to get a yacht. He looked in cabinets, under beds and everywhere else. Nothing was available. Then the boy remembered his friend's boat. He marched down to the lake and asked his friend if he could use his boat. His friend said it wasn't available. The boy was so disappointed. Just then, a big, beautiful yacht sailed into the lake. The boy marched over to the yacht and asked the man who owned it if he could take it for a ride. The man said yes! The boy hopped on the yacht and sailed away. He had a wonderful time and made many memories. He was so happy that he was able to march his way to his adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a fearful boy. He was scared of many things. One hot day, Tim and his mom went to the store. At the store, Tim saw a big dog. He was scared of dogs. The dog said, "Woof! Woof!" Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, I'm scared of the dog!" His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. The dog is nice. He won't hurt you." Tim felt better. They finished shopping and went home. From that day on, Tim was not scared of dogs anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a unique restaurant. It was a special place where people went to eat. One day, a little boy named Tim and his mom went to the restaurant. Inside the restaurant, they saw many tables with numbers on them. Tim's mom said, "Let's sit at table number three." They sat down and looked at the food. Tim wanted to eat pizza. A nice man came to their table and asked, "What would you like to eat?" Tim said, "I want pizza, please." The man smiled and went to get the pizza. When the pizza came, Tim and his mom shared it. They were very happy. Tim learned that sharing food with others can make everyone happy.

One day, a little pink bear went for a walk in the woods. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. The pink bear was very happy. Soon, the pink bear met a big brown bear. The brown bear said, "Hello, little pink bear! What are you doing today?" The pink bear said, "I am going for a walk to see my friend, the rabbit." The two bears walked and talked until they found the rabbit's home. The rabbit was taking a nap in the soft green grass. The pink bear and the brown bear sat down next to the rabbit and waited for him to wake up. When the rabbit woke up, he saw the two bears and smiled. "Hello, friends!" he said. "Would you like to play with me?" The pink bear and the brown bear nodded, and they all played together in the woods. After a day full of fun and laughter, the pink bear, the brown bear, and the rabbit were tired. They all decided to take a nap together under a big tree. The sun was setting, and the woods were quiet. As they napped, they all dreamt of more fun days together.
