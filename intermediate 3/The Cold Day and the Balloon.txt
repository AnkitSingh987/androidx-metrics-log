Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cold Day and the Balloon

Once upon a time, there was a cold day. The wind blew and made everyone feel very cold. A little boy named Tim wanted to send a message to his friend, Tom. Tim had a great idea! He would attach the message to a balloon and let it fly to Tom's house. Tim wrote the message on a piece of paper. He said, "Hi Tom! Let's play inside today. It is too cold outside. Love, Tim." Then, Tim found a big balloon. He used a string to attach the message to the balloon. He went outside and let the balloon go up, up, up into the sky. The balloon with the message flew and flew until it reached Tom's house. Tom saw the balloon and grabbed it. He read the message and smiled. Tom ran inside to tell his mom about the message from Tim. They both agreed it was a good idea to play inside because it was too cold outside.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big, white dog named Spot. They liked to play in the yard together. One sunny day, Tim found a hose in the yard. He had a fun idea. Tim said to Spot, "Let's play a joke on Dad!" Spot wagged his tail and barked. Tim turned on the hose and filled a bucket with water. They hid behind a tree, waiting for Dad to come outside. They knew Dad would be out soon to water the plants. Soon, Dad came outside with his watering can. As he walked by the tree, Tim jumped out and threw the water from the bucket at him. "Surprise!" Tim yelled. Dad laughed and said, "You got me, Tim! That was a good joke!" Spot barked happily, and they all played with the hose together.

One day, a boy named Tim had a toy that could print. He was very glad to have it. He liked to print fun things like dogs, cats, and big balls. But one day, Tim's toy did not print. He was sad and did not know what to do. Tim went to his mom for help. She looked at the toy and saw that it had a small cut on its lip. She told Tim that the toy needed to be fixed before it could print again. Tim was worried, but his mom said it would be okay. Tim and his mom went to the toy fixer. The toy fixer looked at the toy and said he could fix it. He put a band on the toy's lip and then the toy could print again. Tim was so glad! He thanked the toy fixer and went home to print more fun things.

One day, Sarah and Mommy decided to go on a walk. Sarah wasn't sure where they were going, and all around was a blur of green and blue. Sarah asked Mommy where they were going, and Mommy said, "We are going to listen to the birds, and find some peace." Sarah thought this sounded nice, and so she followed Mommy down the path. Soon the woods opened up, and Sarah could hear the sound of birds singing. It sounded like a chorus of joy! Mommy and Sarah sat on a log and Mommy said, "This is the place where we can gain a little peace." Sarah thought about this and then asked Mommy a question: "What is peace?" Mommy smiled and said, "Peace is a very nice word. It means contentment and joy, like the kind of feeling you have after a long car ride or a nice walk. It is something special to be able to find peace, and that is something everyone should try to do." Sarah listened carefully, trying to understand what Mommy had said. And as they rested on the log, Sarah could feel peace slowly creeping over her. She realized that it was something special to gain peace.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little dog named Max. Max lived in a small cave near a big tree. He loved to play with his ball all day. One sunny day, Max saw a big stick near his cave. He thought it would be fun to play with it. Max picked up the stick with his mouth and started to run around. He was so happy. Max wanted to see how far he could throw the stick. He ran and ran, then he struck the stick on the ground. The stick flew high in the sky and landed far away. Max felt very proud of himself. He was a clever little dog who loved his cave and playing with sticks.
