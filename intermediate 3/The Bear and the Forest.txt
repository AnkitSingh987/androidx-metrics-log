Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bear and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a cheerful forest, there lived a big bear. The bear loved to play and have fun with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One sunny day, the bear and his friends decided to have a picnic. They all sat in a circle and shared their yummy food. The bear was very hungry, so he ordered a big sandwich with his favorite things inside. After they ate, the bear and his friends played games and sang songs. The cheerful bear was so happy to be with his friends in the beautiful forest. They played until the sun went down, and then they all went home to sleep.

Once upon a time, in a charming little house, lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to eat fruit. She had a big fruit bowl in her kitchen. One day, she found a giant fruit at the store. It was so big, it did not fit in her fruit bowl! Lily tried to make the fruit fit in the bowl. She pushed and pushed, but it just would not go in. "Oh no!" said Lily, "What will I do with this big fruit?" Her friend Tom came over to play. He saw the big fruit and said, "Wow! That is a big fruit! Let's eat it together!" So, Lily and Tom ate the giant fruit. It was yummy and sweet. The big fruit was now gone, and Lily's fruit bowl was empty again. Lily and Tom laughed and played. They were happy that they solved the problem together.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. He lived in a garage. The garage was his home. It was small, but Tom liked it. One day, Tom's friend, a dog named Max, came to visit him. Max said, "Tom, you should relax. You look tired." Tom agreed and they both sat down in the garage. As they sat and relaxed, they talked and laughed. They had a fun time together. Tom was happy that Max came to visit him in his garage. And that's how they spent their day, being happy and relaxed.

Once upon a time, there was a generous cat named Tom. Tom liked to spend time outside and play with his friends. One day, a big storm came to the town. Tom was scared of the storm, so he went to his friend, a small mouse named Jerry. "Jerry, can I stay with you during the storm?" Tom asked. Jerry was kind, so he said, "Yes, Tom, come in." But the storm was very strong. The wind blew and blew. The rain fell hard. Tom and Jerry were very scared. The storm broke their home. They had no place to stay. Tom and Jerry were sad because of the bad storm.

Once upon a time there was a bee. The bee was very brave. The bee wanted to join the hive. So he flew to the hive and knocked on the door. He asked to join the hive and the bees at the hive said yes. The bees at the hive were very kind. But then something bad happened. A bad bee came to the hive and said the brave bee could not join. The brave bee was really sad. He was very sad because he wanted to join the hive so badly. But the kind bees at the hive did not let the bad bee make the brave bee sad. They were very brave, just like the brave bee. The kind bees protected the brave bee and said he could join the hive. So the brave bee was so happy. He joined the hive and he was very happy. The bees celebrated and they were all very happy. The brave bee had found his home.

Once there was a little girl named Sarah. She was always very thoughtful and never did anything bad. One day she was walking through a field of flowers with her mom. In the sky above them, Sarah could count five birds flying in a circle. They were so beautiful! Then, something very strange happened. A boot came flying down from the sky and landed right in front of Sarah. It was so strange that the little girl couldn’t believe it. She reached down and picked it up and was about to show her mom, but then she noticed that the boot was filled with money. Sarah was surprised and not sure what to do. She thought it was too much money to keep and that it might not be hers. But she was also very tempted to take it. She started to think of what she could do with the money and all the things she could buy. But in the end, Sarah decided it was not right to take the money without knowing where it came from. She put the boot back down on the ground and told her mom what had happened. The mom was very proud of her daughter’s decision and they both went home. But sadly, the money was never found, and the person who had lost it never got it back.
