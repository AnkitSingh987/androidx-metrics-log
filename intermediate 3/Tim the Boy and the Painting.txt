Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Painting

One day, a boy named Tim found a big sheet. He wanted to paint on it. He took his paint and brush and started to paint. He painted a big sun, a blue sky, and green grass. He was very happy with his painting. Tim saw a distant hill in his painting. He thought it would be fun to have a friend there. So, he painted a little girl on the hill. Suddenly, the little girl came to life! She jumped out of the painting and said, "Hi, I'm Lily!" Tim and Lily played and painted together. They painted more hills, trees, and flowers on the sheet. They had a great time and became best friends. From that day on, Tim and Lily always painted together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a brown coat. The coat was warm and cosy and belonged to a small boy. One day the boy's mum said to him, "Time to go outside and play". The boy picked up his brown coat and went outside. On the way he met his friend, Dave. Dave said "Where did you get your coat?" The boy replied, "It's my coat - it belongs to me". Dave said, "It sure looks nice and cosy. It must keep you nice and warm". The boy smiled and said, "Yes, it's my favourite. I love my brown coat". Then the two friends ran off to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane had a pretty dress that she loved to wear. One sunny day, Jane wanted to go to the park with her mom. At the park, Jane saw a seat. She wanted to sit on it and watch the birds. But when she sat down, she heard a terrible sound. Jane looked down and saw that her dress had ripped. Jane was sad about her dress, but her mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, we can fix it." They went home and her mom sewed the dress. Jane was happy again and knew that her mom could fix anything.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was a healthy dog who loved to run and play all day. One day, Max found a small ball in the park. He thought it was a toy, so he picked it up with his mouth and brought it home. The next day, Max started to feel sick. He did not want to eat or play. His owner, a little girl named Amy, was very worried. She told her mom, and they decided to take Max to the veterinarian. The veterinarian was a nice lady who helped animals feel better. At the veterinarian's office, the nice lady checked Max and found the ball in his mouth. She gently took the ball out and revealed that it was not a toy, but a ball of yucky stuff that made Max sick. Once the ball was out, Max started to feel better. He was soon back to being a healthy and happy dog. Amy was very happy too, and they both learned to be careful with what they find in the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a huge gray rock in the middle of the park. Tim thought there might be a hidden treasure behind the rock. So, he went to search for it. As Tim was searching, he met a girl named Sue. Sue was also searching for the treasure. They decided to search together. Tim said, "Let's look behind the huge gray rock!" Sue agreed, "Okay, let's go!" Tim and Sue searched and searched, but they couldn't find any treasure. They were sad, but then Tim's mom came over with a big surprise. She had found a box of cookies and shared them with Tim and Sue. The two friends were happy again, even though they didn't find any treasure. They enjoyed their cookies and played together at the park all day.

Once upon a time there was a fat fox. The fox was very happy but he was often lonely. One day, the fox decided he wanted some friends. He searched the meadow and found a rabbit, a hedgehog and a squirrel. The fox invited his new friends to a picnic. He brought some juicy berries and tastey nuts. The others were so happy! They all ate together and had lots of fun. Suddenly, the rabbit said, "Look everyone! A big, fat bee!". The bee was buzzing around and not paying attention to them. The fox said, "Let's ignore the bee and keep playing". But the bee just kept buzzing and buzzing. The fox tried to ignore the bee but it was too loud and annoying. So in the end he had to shoo it away. The other animals said goodbye to the fox and thanked him for the tasty picnic. From that day on, the fox always had lots of friends to play and picnic with.
