Category: Intermediate
Title: The Warm Classroom and the Kids

Once upon a time, there was a warm classroom. In this classroom, kids would come and learn how to design. They would draw and color with their friends. The teacher was nice and always helped them. The kids were happy and loved their classroom. One day, a new kid came to the classroom. His name was Tim. Tim wanted to design like the other kids. But his drawings were different. The other kids laughed at him. Tim felt sad and didn't want to design anymore. Then, something unexpected happened. The teacher showed the kids that Tim's drawings were special. They were not bad, just different. The kids learned that it's okay to be different. They all became friends and designed together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She had a diary where she drew pictures of her day. Lily loved to share her diary with her friends. One sunny day, Lily went to the park. She saw her friend, Tom, near a big tree. Tom was sad because he lost his toy car. Lily wanted to help Tom find his toy car. "Let's look for your car together," she said. Lily and Tom looked high and low. They leaned down to look under the slides and leaned over to look in the bushes. Finally, they found the toy car near the swings. Tom was so happy, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, in a small camp, there lived a kind cat and a happy dog. The cat and dog were best friends. They played together all day long. The camp was their home, and they loved it very much. One day, the cat found a tight ball of yarn. The cat loved to play with the yarn. The dog saw the cat playing and wanted to join the fun. So, they played together with the tight ball of yarn, running and jumping around the camp. As the sun went down, the cat and dog were tired from playing all day. They sat close to each other and whispered secrets. They whispered about their fun day and the tight ball of yarn. Then, they went to sleep, dreaming of more adventures in their camp.

Jim and Jane were excited to visit their grandma's house. She always baked them treats, let them pet her dog, and take them to special places. As they walked in, the smell of baked cookies filled the air and the chatter of people could be heard. Grandma was having a party! Jim and Jane looked around and noticed everyone was dancing. Grandma saw them and said, "Come here, you two! Let’s join in the fun!". Jim and Jane started to dance and soon everyone else was joining in. The nosy neighbor, who was always peeking out of the window, saw the party and decided to come join too! Grandma welcomed her with open arms and said, "You are always welcome here no matter what." Everyone started to dance again and had so much fun. Jim and Jane were thrilled that they got to visit Grandma's house and join the dance party!

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy bunny named Bob. Bob had big eyes that could blink. Sue loved Bob very much. One day, Sue found a magic wand. She waved the wand and said, "I wish Bob could talk to me!" Suddenly, Bob could talk. "Hi, Sue!" said Bob. Sue was so happy. But Sue's friend, Tim, was jealous. Tim wanted a talking toy too. Tim took the magic wand when Sue was not looking. He waved it and said, "I wish my toy could talk too!" But he didn't know how to use the wand right. Instead of making his toy talk, it made all the toys stop talking. Sue was sad. She said, "Please give me back the wand, Tim." Tim felt bad and gave the wand back to Sue. Sue tried to fix what Tim did. She waved the wand and said, "I wish all the toys could talk again!" And just like that, the toys could talk. Bob blinked his big eyes and said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue and Tim learned to share the magic wand and played with their talking toys together.
