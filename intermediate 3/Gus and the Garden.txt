Category: Intermediate
Title: Gus and the Garden

Gus was playing in the garden. He had lots of cool toys. He was playing with his telephone and when his mom called his name, he ran to the kitchen. In the kitchen Gus saw a funny thing. There was smoke coming from the oven and a loud noise. Mama was on the phone, telling somebody that the oven was burning. She opened the oven and there were flames inside. They were very high and bright. Mama called the fire department and soon a big fire truck arrived. The firemen hooked the hose up to the fire hydrant and sprayed water all over the kitchen. The flames went out and the smoke cleared. Mama hugged Gus and said it was all cool now. The firemen checked the kitchen and thanked Mama. Then they all left. Gus was happy it was now cool in his home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived with his mom and dad in a small house. Tim loved to play with his toys, but he didn't like to clean up after himself. His mom and dad always told him, "Tim, you need to listen and keep your things clean." One day, Tim's mom made a yummy cake. She put the cake on the table and told Tim not to touch it until after dinner. Tim wanted the cake so much, but he remembered he needed to listen to his mom. So, he played with his toys and waited for dinner. When it was time for dinner, Tim's mom asked him to clean the table. Tim listened and cleaned the table very well. After dinner, Tim's mom gave him a big piece of cake. Tim was so happy that he listened to his mom and dad. He learned that when he listens and keeps things clean, good things happen.

One day, a little fish was swimming in the water. The fish saw a big twist in the water. The fish wanted to swim through the twist. It looked fun, but it also looked difficult. The fish tried to swim through the twist. It was very difficult. The fish tried again and again. Each time, the fish got a little better at swimming through the twist. Finally, the fish swam through the twist. It was not difficult anymore. The fish felt happy and proud. The fish had fun swimming through the twist all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little blue bird. The bird liked to sing and play all day. One day, the bird saw a big stage in the park. The bird was very excited. The bird went to the stage and lay down on it. It looked up at the sky and saw the big, bright sun. The bird felt very happy on the stage. Soon, other animals came to the stage. They all sang and danced together. The blue bird was the star of the show. Everyone clapped and laughed. The blue bird and its new friends had a great time on the stage.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Max. Max loved to live in luxury. He had a big, soft bed and lots of toys. Max liked to tell all his friends what to do. One day, Max met a dog named Sam. Sam was very nice, but Max wanted to be the boss. Max said, "Sam, you must give me your toy." Sam was sad, but he gave Max his toy. Max insisted, "Now, you must carry me to my big, soft bed." Sam did not want to, but Max was very bossy. So, Sam carried Max to his bed. Max was happy, but Sam was not. Soon, Max learned that being bossy was not nice. Max gave Sam his toy back and they became good friends.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. They liked to play together and they were very comfortable with each other. The cat had a nice look and the dog was big and strong. They were best friends. While they were playing, a big bird came and took the cat's toy. The cat was very sad and said, "Dog, please help me get my toy back." The dog wanted to help his friend, so they went to find the bird. They found the bird in a tree. The dog said, "Please give the toy back to my friend, Cat." The bird did not want to surrender the toy, but then something unexpected happened. The bird saw how much the cat and the dog loved each other and felt bad for taking the toy. So, the bird gave the toy back to the cat and they all became friends.
