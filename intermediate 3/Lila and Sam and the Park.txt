Category: Intermediate
Title: Lila and Sam and the Park

Lila and Sam were best friends. They liked to play in the park and look at the sky. One day, they saw something shiny and fast in the sky. It was a rocket! "Wow, look at that!" Sam said. "It is a rocket. It goes to space." "I want to see space," Lila said. "Maybe there are stars and planets and aliens." "Maybe we can meet an alien," Sam said. "That would be fun and rare. Not many people meet aliens." They ran after the rocket, hoping to get closer. They followed it to a big field, where it landed. They saw a door open and a green creature come out. It had four eyes, two arms and two legs. It looked friendly. "Hello, little ones," the creature said. "I am Zeebo. I come from Zork. I am here to explore your planet. Do you want to be my friends?" Lila and Sam were surprised and curious. They nodded and smiled. "Yes, we want to be your friends," Lila said. "We are Lila and Sam. We come from Earth. This is our planet." "Nice to meet you, Lila and Sam," Zeebo said. "You are very brave and kind. Do you want to see my rocket? It is very cool and fast." "Yes, please," Sam said. "We love rockets. They are rare and amazing." Zeebo took them by the hand and led them to his rocket. He showed them the buttons, the lights and the windows. He told them stories about his home and his travels. Lila and Sam listened and asked questions. They had a lot of fun and learned a lot. They made a new friend from space. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to ride his bike. One day, Tim's mom saw him ride without a helmet. She did not want him to get hurt. So, she told him to stop and come inside the house. Tim's mom said, "You need a helmet to ride your bike. It will keep your head safe." Tim did not have a helmet, so his mom went to the store to order one for him. Tim was sad because he could not ride his bike until the helmet came. A few days later, the helmet arrived. Tim's mom gave it to him and said, "Now you can ride your bike and not worry about getting hurt." Tim put on the helmet and went outside. He rode his bike all day long and felt very happy and safe.

One day, a cheerful girl named Lily was playing with her dolls. She loved her dolls very much. She liked to sort them by size, color, and what they wore. Today, she was sorting them by color. As Lily was sorting her dolls, one of her dolls, named Daisy, started to talk. "Hi, Lily!" said Daisy. Lily was very surprised. She had never heard a doll talk before. "Hello, Daisy!" said Lily. They talked and played together. But then, something unexpected happened. Daisy told Lily she was a magic doll. Daisy said, "I can make your other dolls talk too!" Lily was so excited. Daisy waved her hands, and all the dolls started talking. Lily and her dolls had a big, fun party together.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the port with his dad. They wanted to make a big boat. They had wood, nails, and paint to make it. Tom was very excited to make the boat with his dad. As they started to make the boat, a bird came and said, "Hello, Tom! What are you doing?" Tom replied, "I am making a boat with my dad." The bird said, "Can I help?" Tom said, "Yes, you can help us paint the boat." When they finished the boat, they all wanted to eat. Tom's mom gave them a fruit that looked like an orange, but it was not sweet. It was very bitter! They all made a funny face when they tasted it. Suddenly, the bird said, "I know what to do!" The bird flew away and came back with sweet fruits. They all ate the sweet fruits, and they were not bitter anymore. Tom, his dad, and the bird were all happy, and they had a great day at the port.
