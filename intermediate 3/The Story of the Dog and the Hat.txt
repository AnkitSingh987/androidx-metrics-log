Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Hat

Once upon a time, in a small town, winter came. The cold wind blew, and snow fell on the ground. A little girl named Lily and her dog, Max, loved winter. They liked to play outside and watch the snowflakes fall from the sky. One day, Lily and Max went outside to play. They built a big snowman with a big hat and a long scarf. Lily said to Max, "Look, Max! Building a snowman is easy and fun!" Max barked happily and wagged his tail. As Lily and Max played, they saw their friend Tom come outside too. Tom was excited to see the snowman. He asked, "Can I play with you?" Lily and Max smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They all played in the snow and watched the snowflakes fall until it was time to go inside for hot cocoa.

Once upon a time there was a poppy. It was a beautiful flower with red petals. One day the poppy wanted to speak. But it couldn't because it had no mouth. It was very impatient. The poppy asked the wind to help it speak. So the wind blew and blew and blew. Eventually, it blew hard enough that the poppy was able to speak! The poppy was so surprised and happy that it could speak. It said "hello!" to all the other flowers. The flowers were so surprised to hear the poppy speak. The poppy was so happy that it could speak. From that day on, it could speak to all the other flowers. The poppy was no longer impatient.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved going to the store with her mom. At the store, they would buy food and toys. Lily liked to look at all the things in the store. One day, Lily saw a toy that could record her voice. She was so happy! She asked her mom if she could have it. Her mom said yes, and they took it home. Lily loved to record herself singing and play it back for her mom and dad to hear. But one day, the toy stopped working. Lily was upset. Her mom and dad tried to fix it, but they couldn't. So, they went back to the store to get a new one. The store had more toys that could record voices. Lily picked a new one, and they took it home. Now, Lily was happy again, and she could record her songs for everyone to hear.

Once upon a time, there was a little hill. On this hill, there lived an obedient dog named Spot. Spot loved to dance and play all day long. One sunny day, Spot saw a big butterfly. He wanted to dance with it. So, he ran and jumped to catch it. The butterfly went up and down, and Spot danced along, trying to touch it. But as Spot danced, he did not see the big hole in the ground. He fell into the hole and could not get out. Spot was sad and scared. The butterfly flew away, and Spot was alone in the hole.

Max and his mommy were at the store. She was picking out some fun things, such as a bag of colorful pins. Max was so excited. "Can I have one?" Max asked. "Number one," his mommy said, handing him the pin. Max had so much fun looking at all the different colors of the pins in the bag. He put the pin on his shirt and spun around in circles. "It's noisy," he said. "Yes it is!" his mommy replied, laughing. Max smiled and put the pin back into the bag. He carefully counted all the pins to make sure he got the right number. "All done," he said proudly. "Good job!" his mommy said with a kiss. They left the store with a big smile on both of their faces. From then on, Max always counted his pins to make sure he got the right number.

Once upon a time, there was a big brown bear. He had big brown eyes. He liked to rock on his chair. One day, he found a small brown box. He thought, "I will open it later." The brown bear went to play with his friends. They played ball and ran around. The brown bear had fun. He forgot about the small brown box. The sun went down, and it was time to go home. At home, the brown bear remembered the small brown box. He opened it and found a tiny brown rock. The rock had a picture of an eye on it. The brown bear put the rock on his chair. When he rocked on his chair, the rock made him feel happy. The brown bear knew that the small brown box had a special gift for him.
