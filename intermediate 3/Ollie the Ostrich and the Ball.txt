Category: Intermediate
Title: Ollie the Ostrich and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big ostrich named Ollie. Ollie had a lot of friends. They played in the sun all day long. Ollie liked to run very fast. His long legs made it easy for him to win races. One day, Ollie and his friends wanted to play a new game. They decided to play "find the clear ball". Ollie's friend, Ellie the elephant, said, "I will hide the clear ball, and you all must find it." Ollie and his friends were very excited to play the game. Ellie hid the clear ball, and all the animals started to look for it. Ollie looked under the trees, behind the rocks, and in the tall grass. Finally, Ollie found the clear ball near the river. "I found it!" Ollie shouted. All his friends were very happy. Ollie received a big hug from Ellie. They all played with the clear ball and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a nice boy named Will. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Will loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Will and his friends were playing near a big tree. Will's friend, Tom, said, "Look, I found a stick. Let's burn it!" Will and his friends were very excited. They gathered around the stick and watched as Tom tried to burn it. But Will's mom saw them and came running. "Stop!" she said. "Burning can hurt you. Let's find a safe game to play." Will and his friends listened to his mom. They put the stick down and went to play a nice game of catch. They learned that burning was not a good idea and promised to always play safe games.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived an intelligent little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to hop around and play with his friends in nature. One sunny day, Remy met a big bear named Benny. "Hi, Benny!" Remy said. "Do you want to play with me in the forest?" Benny looked at Remy and smiled. "Yes, Remy! I would love to play with you. But I am scared of the loud sounds in the forest." Remy saw that Benny was trembling, so he thought of a plan to help his new friend. "Benny, I will hold your hand and we will be brave together." Benny and Remy played in the forest all day. They laughed and had fun, not scared of the sounds anymore. Benny learned that with a friend like Remy, there was no need to tremble. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy phone that she loved to play with. One day, she saw a real phone on the table. Sue wanted to play with the real phone, but her mom said no. Sue was sad, but she knew her toy phone was still fun to play with. Later, Sue saw her mom go to a distant room in the house. Sue thought this was her chance to play with the real phone. She picked it up and started to play, but she dropped it by accident. The phone broke and Sue was very scared. She knew she did something wrong. Sue went to her mom and told her what happened. Her mom was not happy, but she said, "I forgive you, Sue. You made a mistake, but it's important to tell the truth." Sue felt better and learned that it's important to listen to her mom and not touch things she shouldn't. From that day on, Sue only played with her toy phone and never tried to touch the real phone again.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree with many olives. The tree was in a distant place where no one could see it. The tree was all alone. One day, a boy named Tom found the tree. He saw the olives and wanted to eat them. Tom picked some olives and ate them. They were very tasty. Tom was happy. Tom wanted to share the olives with his friends. He took some olives and went to his friends. They all ate the olives together. They were all happy and shared the olives with everyone. The tree was happy too because it was not alone anymore.
