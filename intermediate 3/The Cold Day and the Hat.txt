Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cold Day and the Hat

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a cold day. Everyone stayed inside their homes because it was too cold to play outside. A little girl named Mia was feeling bored. She wanted to have fun, but there was nothing to do. Mia had an idea. She thought, "Maybe I can celebrate something today!" She decided to celebrate a "Cold Day Party". Mia put on her warm hat, scarf, and gloves. She told her mom and dad about her party idea. They loved it and helped her make yummy snacks. Mia invited her friends to her "Cold Day Party". They all wore their warm clothes and played fun games inside. They ate the snacks and danced to music. The cold day was not boring anymore. Everyone had a great time and they all celebrated together.

---

One day, a boy named Tom was playing in his room. He had a toy fork that he loved very much. He liked to pretend it was a magic fork that could do anything. Tom was always happy when he played with his fork. One morning, Tom woke up and found that his fork was gone. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. Tom felt very sad and bitter. He did not want to play without his magic fork. He yelled for his mom to help him look for it. Mom came into Tom's room and saw him crying. She asked him what was wrong. Tom told her about his missing fork. Mom smiled and said, "I have a surprise for you!" She went to the kitchen and came back with a new, shiny fork. It was even bigger than his old one. Tom was so happy! He hugged his mom and thanked her. Now, he could play with his new magic fork and have even more fun.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim found a toy that could shoot soft balls. He wanted to play with his friends, but they were not available. So, he decided to play with his dog, Max. Tim said to Max, "Let's play a game! I will shoot the ball and you catch it, okay?" Max wagged his tail and barked, showing he was ready to play. Tim started to shoot the balls, and Max ran fast to catch them. They were having so much fun! After a while, Tim's friends came to his house. They saw Tim and Max playing and wanted to join. Tim had a surprise idea. He told his friends, "Let's all hide and shoot the balls at Max. When he catches them, we will come out and say surprise!" They all agreed and hid around the yard. When Max caught the last ball, they all jumped out and shouted, "Surprise!" Max was so happy to see everyone and wagged his tail even more. They all laughed and played together for the rest of the day.

---

One day, a boy named Tom had a ball. He wanted to play with his friend, Sue. They liked to pass the ball to each other. Tom passed the ball to Sue, but he was weak. The ball did not go very far. Sue saw a zip on the ball. She thought it was fun to open the zip. Inside the ball, they found a toy cat. The cat was very soft and nice. Tom and Sue liked the cat a lot. Tom and Sue played with the cat and the ball. They passed the ball and the cat to each other. They had a fun day. They were happy and laughed a lot. Tom and Sue liked to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a strong girl named Lily. She loved to play and have fun. One day, she found a big closet in her room. She opened the closet and saw many toys inside. Lily was very happy and said, "Welcome, toys!" Lily played with the toys all day long. She had so much fun with them. But one day, when she opened the closet, the toys were not there. She was very sad and wondered where they went. Then, Lily heard a noise coming from the closet. She looked inside and saw a small door. She opened the door and found a secret room! Inside the room were all her toys, and they could talk! The toys said, "Welcome, Lily!" She was so surprised and happy. They all played together and had lots of fun in the secret room. And from that day on, Lily and her toys had many more adventures together.
