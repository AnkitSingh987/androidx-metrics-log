Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy and the Spoon

Once upon a time, there was a nice, polite girl named Lucy. One day Lucy was playing with her spoon in the garden when she heard some rustling. Lucy looked around and saw a big, scary monster! She was so scared that she dropped her spoon and began to cry. The monster had seen Lucy crying, so it spoke in a friendly voice. "Please don't be scared, Lucy," the monster said. "I'm not here to hurt you. I just wanted to say hello and ask if you wanted to play." Lucy, feeling a bit brave, wiped away her tears and said, "Yes, I would like to play. But only if you promise to be polite like me." The monster smiled and said, "Of course, I promise to be very polite." So Lucy and the monster played together with her spoon, and they had so much fun! Lucy was no longer scared and they both became fast friends.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to visit her aunt. Her aunt lived in a big, red house. Sally was very happy to see her aunt. They played with toys and ate yummy food. Sally loved her aunt very much. While they were playing, Sally saw a big ball. She wanted to play with it. Sally threw the ball very hard. But, she did not see her aunt standing there. The ball hit her aunt and it hurt. Sally's aunt said, "Ouch! That hurt, Sally!" Sally felt bad. She said, "I am sorry, aunt. I did not mean to hurt you." Her aunt hugged her and said, "It's okay, Sally. I know you did not mean to hurt me." Then, something unexpected happened. The ball that hurt Sally's aunt began to talk! It said, "I regret that I hurt your aunt, Sally. I am a magic ball and I can make things better." So, the magic ball made Sally's aunt feel better, and they all played together happily.

Once upon a time, there was a lawyer. He was very honest. He helped people who had a problem or something to do. One day there was a big problem and lots of people asked the lawyer for help. The lawyer worked very hard and helped everyone with their problem. When the lawyer finished helping everyone, the people clapped for him. They clapped and clapped to show how much they loved him. The lawyer was so happy that he smiled and clapped too. He was so happy that he danced around and clapped his hands. Finally, everyone was clapping and laughing and the lawyer was very happy. He was so lucky to have honest friends and to help everyone with their problems.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a phone. The phone was a creative toy for Tim. He liked to play with it. He would talk to his friends and family on the phone. They would laugh and have fun. Tim wanted to supply a gift to his friend, Sam. He thought of giving him a phone too. Tim asked his mom for help. She went to the store and bought a phone for Sam. Tim gave the phone to Sam. Sam was very happy. Now, they both had creative phones to play with. They talked and played games on their phones. They had lots of fun together.

Ben and Lily are twins. They are three years old. They love to play with toys and books. Tomorrow is their birthday. They want to give each other a present. Ben finds a big red car in his toy box. He likes the car, but he thinks Lily will like it more. He decides to give it to her as a present. He looks for something to wrap the car with. He sees a blue blanket on his bed. He takes the blanket and wraps the car with it. He ties a knot with the blanket. He puts the present under his bed. Lily finds a soft teddy bear in her toy box. She likes the bear, but she thinks Ben will like it more. She decides to give it to him as a present. She looks for something to wrap the bear with. She sees a pink scarf on her dresser. She takes the scarf and wraps the bear with it. She makes a bow with the scarf. She puts the present in her closet. The next morning, Ben and Lily wake up. They are very excited. They run to each other's rooms. They see the presents. They smile and hug. "Happy birthday, Ben!" Lily says. She gives him the present. "I made this for you. I hope you like it." "Happy birthday, Lily!" Ben says. He gives her the present. "I made this for you. I hope you like it." They open the presents. They see the car and the bear. They are very happy. They thank each other. "Wow, Lily, this car is awesome! Thank you so much!" Ben says. He makes the car zoom on the floor. "Wow, Ben, this bear is so cute! Thank you so much!" Lily says. She cuddles the bear. They play with their new toys. They are very happy. They are the best twins ever.
