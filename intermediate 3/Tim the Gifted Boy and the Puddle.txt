Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Gifted Boy and the Puddle

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a gifted boy named Tim. Tim had a special power. He could separate things with his hands. One day, Tim went for a walk in the park. While walking, Tim saw a big puddle on the path. He knew that he could help. Tim used his gifted hands to separate the water in the puddle. The water moved to the sides, and the path was dry. Tim's friends saw what he did and clapped their hands. They were happy that Tim used his gift to help. From that day on, Tim used his power to help his friends and make the village a better place.

Once upon a time, there was a big barrel in a small town. The barrel was open, and it was full of toys. All the kids in the town loved to play with the toys from the barrel. One day, a little boy named Timmy went to the barrel to find a toy. He saw a funny-looking toy that could dance and clap. He picked it up and said, "Wow, this toy can applaud! I want to show my friends." Timmy took the toy to his friends, and they all watched it dance and applaud. They laughed and clapped along with the toy. "This toy is so much fun!" said Timmy's friend, Lucy. Everyone in the town loved the applauding toy, and they all played with it together. The open barrel brought happiness and fun to the town, and the kids always had something to smile about. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a favorite doll named Daisy. One day, Daisy's arm got a big hole in it. Lily was very sad and asked her mommy for help. "Mommy, can you please fix Daisy's arm?" Lily asked. Her mommy looked at the hole and said, "I can use a stitch to fix Daisy's arm, but I need some thread. Let's see if we have any available." They looked everywhere in the house for thread, but they could not find any. Lily's mommy said, "I'm sorry, Lily. We don't have any thread available right now. We'll have to go to the store tomorrow." Lily did not want to wait. She went to her big brother, Tom. "Tom, can you fix Daisy's arm?" she asked. Tom looked at Daisy and said, "No, I refuse to fix it. I don't know how to do a stitch." Lily was sad, but she knew she had to wait. The next day, her mommy took her to the store to buy thread. They found the perfect color and went home. Lily's mommy fixed Daisy's arm with a stitch, and Daisy was as good as new. Lily was so happy, and she thanked her mommy for helping her.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was going to a party. Lucy was very excited. She put on her favorite dress and her shiny shoes. At the party, Lucy saw her friend Tim. Tim had a big, red balloon. Lucy felt jealous. She wanted a big, red balloon too. Lucy said, "Tim, can I have your balloon?" Tim said, "No, Lucy. This is my balloon. You can get your own." Lucy felt sad and went to find a balloon. She found a big, blue one. As she was playing with it, the balloon popped! Lucy felt embarrass. Everyone looked at her. But then, Tim came over and gave her his red balloon. "Here, Lucy. You can have my balloon now." Lucy smiled and thanked Tim. They played together for the rest of the party. Lucy learned that it is okay to feel jealous, but it is better to share and be kind to her friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his dog, Max. They would run and jump in the big, green park. One hot day, Tim and Max were playing when they saw a man. The man said, "I lost my cat. Can you help me find her?" Tim and Max were alert and wanted to help. They looked high and low for the cat. Finally, Max heard a soft "meow" from a tree. He barked and Tim looked up. There was the cat! Tim called the man over, and he climbed the tree to get his cat. The man was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim and Max!" Tim and Max were proud to help. They went home, tired but happy. They had finished their big job for the day.
