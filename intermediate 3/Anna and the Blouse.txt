Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Blouse

Anna likes to play dress-up with her mom's clothes. She likes to get her mom's blouse and put it on. The blouse is big and soft and has many colors. Anna feels like a princess when she wears the blouse. One day, Anna's mom is going out with her friends. She wants to wear her blouse, but she cannot find it. She looks everywhere, but the blouse is not there. She is sad and angry. She asks Anna, "Did you take my blouse?" Anna hears her mom and feels scared. She knows she did something bad. She is hiding in her room with the blouse. She does not want to give it back. She loves the blouse too much. But then she thinks about her mom. She remembers how her mom hugs her and reads her stories and makes her cookies. She loves her mom more than the blouse. She decides to be a good girl and say sorry. She goes to her mom and says, "Mom, I'm sorry. I took your blouse. I wanted to play dress-up. Here is your blouse. Please don't be mad at me." Her mom sees Anna and the blouse. She is not mad anymore. She is grateful that Anna told the truth and gave back the blouse. She says, "Thank you, Anna. I'm glad you gave me my blouse. I know you like it, but it is mine. You can play dress-up with your own clothes. I love you, Anna." She hugs Anna and kisses her. Anna feels happy and proud. She says, "I love you too, mom. You are the best mom ever."

Once upon a time, there was a big, enormous tree. Under the tree, a small boy named Tom found a stick. Tom thought the stick was a fun weapon to play with. Tom saw his friend, Sam, and showed him the weapon. Sam did not know what it was, so he just shrugged. They decided to play together with the stick. As they played, they found more sticks and had a big stick fight. They laughed and had fun under the enormous tree. In the end, they were happy and tired. They went home to rest and have a snack. They could not wait to play again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day long. He did not like to play or run around. One day, Tom woke up and saw a big mess on the floor. He knew he had to clean it up. Tom looked around for something to help clean the mess. He found a big brush. He used the brush to sweep the mess away. The brush was very good at cleaning. It made the job easy for lazy Tom. After Tom cleaned the mess, he felt hungry. He went to his friend, the dog named Max. Max was good at supplying food. Max gave Tom some yummy fish to eat. Tom was very happy. From that day on, Tom and Max were best friends, and they helped each other when needed.

Once upon a time there was a girl who wanted some groceries. She was a little worried, as she had not picked groceries before. She thought it would be hard to do. So, she asked her mom for help. Mom said, "Let's go together and pick the groceries!" The girl was so excited. She quickly jumped in the car and they drove off. When they got to the grocery store, mom showed the girl how to look for fruits and vegetables. Then, they picked some apples, oranges, and potatoes. The girl was so proud that she had helped pick the groceries. She said, "Mom, I'm not worried anymore. I can pick groceries now!" Mom smiled and said, "That's great! I knew you could do it!" The girl smiled and hugged her mom, thankful that she wasn't worried anymore.

Tom and Anna were excited to go to the library with their mom. They loved to look at the books on the shelves and find new stories to read. Mom told them to be quiet and respectful in the library, and to put the books back where they found them. Tom and Anna nodded and ran to the children's section. They saw many colorful books with pictures and words. Tom picked up a book about dinosaurs and Anna picked up a book about fairies. They sat on a big seat and opened their books. "Wow, look at this T-rex!" Tom said in a loud voice. "He is so big and scary!" "Shh, Tom, you have to be quiet," Anna said. "Look at this fairy! She is so pretty and sparkly!" Tom and Anna forgot to be quiet and started to talk louder and louder. They also forgot to put their books back on the shelves and left them on the seat or on the floor. Mom heard them and came to the children's section. She saw the mess they made and the angry looks from the other people in the library. She was not happy. "Tom and Anna, what are you doing?" she asked. "You are being very rude and noisy. You are not respecting the books or the library. You have to apologize and clean up your mess." Tom and Anna felt ashamed and sorry. They said sorry to the librarian and the other people. They picked up the books and put them back on the shelves. They learned their lesson and promised to be quiet and respectful in the library from then on. Mom hugged them and said she was proud of them for admitting their mistake and fixing it. They still loved the library, but they knew how to behave better.
