Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Dark

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was scared of the dark. One night, Max heard a noise outside. He did not know what to do. So, he just gave a little shrug. Max went outside to see what the noise was. He saw a big bone on the ground. Max was very happy to find the bone. He picked it up with his mouth and wagged his tail. Max took the bone back to his little house. He was not scared of the dark anymore. Max had a big smile on his face. He knew that he was brave. From then on, Max played outside even when it was dark.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a woman named Sue. Sue was adventurous and loved to explore new places. One day, she saw a big hill and decided to climb it. As Sue climbed the hill, she met a little bird. The bird said, "Hi, Sue! Be careful up there. I recognize something special at the top." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, little bird. I will be careful." Sue reached the top of the hill and saw a big, beautiful tree. She remembered what the bird said and looked around. Under the tree, she found a shiny treasure box. Sue was so happy that she listened to the bird's advice. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a unique bunny who loved to hop. The bunny had a best friend named Tom. Tom liked to play hockey. They both lived in a small, happy town. One sunny day, the bunny and Tom went to play hockey in the park. They were having so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the hockey ball far away. They could not see where it went. The bunny and Tom hopped and hopped to find the ball. They looked under trees and behind bushes. Then, they found the ball in a big hole. But it was not just one ball, there were many balls! All the town's lost balls were in the hole. The bunny and Tom were very happy. They hopped back to the park and played hockey with all the balls.

One day, a boy named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big rock. It was not an ordinary rock. It was a wonder rock. Tom was very happy to find it. Tom took the wonder rock home. He put it in his room. Every day, Tom would talk to the wonder rock. He would tell the rock his secrets. The wonder rock never complained. One day, Tom came home and the wonder rock was gone. He was very sad. He looked for the wonder rock everywhere. Then, Tom found the wonder rock in the kitchen. The wonder rock had turned into a big, yummy cake! Tom was very surprised and happy. The wonder rock was not just a rock. It was a magic cake rock.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went with his dad to the park. They brought some fun equipment to play with, like a ball and a toy car. Tim was very happy and excited to play with his dad. While playing, Tim saw a silly bug on the ground. He decided to examine it closely. He looked at its tiny legs and its funny wings. Just then, the bug flew away and landed on Tim's toy car! Tim was surprised and laughed. He thought the bug wanted to play with the car too. But then, the bug picked up the car with its tiny legs and flew away! Tim and his dad couldn't believe it. They ran after the bug, trying to get the car back. In the end, the bug dropped the toy car and flew away. Tim and his dad were happy to have the car back. They went back to playing, but they were always careful to keep an eye on any silly bugs.

Once upon a time, there was a big octopus. He lived in the deep blue sea. He had long arms that were rough. One day, he met a small fish. "Hi, I am octopus. What do you weigh?" he asked the fish. The fish did not know what "weigh" meant. Octopus saw the fish was confused. "Let me show you," said octopus. He took the fish in his arms and lifted it up. "You are light," he told the fish. The fish smiled and thanked the octopus. From that day on, octopus and the fish were friends. They played together in the sea. They were happy and had lots of fun.
