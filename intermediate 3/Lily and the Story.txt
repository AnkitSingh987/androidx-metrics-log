Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Story

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to learn about fashion. She would look at her mom's clothes and try to make her own pretty dresses. One day, Lily found a beautiful, shiny dress in her mom's closet. She wanted to wear it and show her friends. But her mom told her, "This dress is dangerous, Lily. The shiny parts are very sharp and can hurt you." Lily did not listen to her mom. She put on the dangerous dress and went outside to play. Suddenly, she tripped and fell. The sharp parts of the dress hurt her arm. She cried and ran back to her mom. Her mom hugged her and said, "I told you the dress was dangerous. You need to listen and learn from me." Lily learned her lesson. From that day on, she always listened to her mom and never wore dangerous clothes again. And so, the moral of the story is to always listen to your parents, because they know what is best for you.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little mouse named Mimi. Mimi lived in a small house made of cheese. She loved to bake yummy treats for her friends. One day, Mimi decided to bake a big cake for her friend, Kitty. She mixed the cake with her tiny paws and put it in the oven. While the cake was baking, Mimi danced around her house, excited to share her treat. When the cake was ready, Mimi knocked on Kitty's door. "Hi, Kitty! I baked a cake for you!" Mimi said with a big smile. Kitty was so happy and said, "Thank you, Mimi! Let's eat it together!" They both ate the cake and laughed. From that day on, Mimi wasn't shy anymore, and she loved to bake treats for all her friends.

One day, a boy named Tim was playing in the park. He saw a big red ambulance. It had a loud sound. Tim was very curious. Tim went to the ambulance and saw a nice man. The man said, "Hello, I am an ambulance driver. I help people who are hurt." Tim thought that was very fair. Tim said, "Can I see inside?" The man said, "Yes, you can." Tim looked inside and saw many things. He learned how the ambulance helps people. Tim was happy and said thank you to the man.

Once upon a time, there was a playful puppy named Spot. Spot loved to run and jump in the park. One sunny day, Spot was playing near a big tree. Spot saw a little girl named Lily. Lily gazed at Spot with a big smile. She said, "Hi, puppy! Do you want to play with me?" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, let's play!" They had fun playing together. They threw a ball and laughed. Spot and Lily became best friends. And they played happily ever after.

One day, a big red bus was going down the road. The bus had many kids inside. They were going to the park to play. The kids were very happy and excited. They could not wait to get to the park and play on the slide. At the park, a little boy named Tim was anxious. He was scared to go on the big slide. His friend, Sue, said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you. We can go on the slide together." Tim smiled and felt better. They both climbed up the steps to the slide. When they got to the top of the slide, they saw a big, funny dog. The dog was wearing a hat and sunglasses. The dog said, "Hello, kids! I am here to slide with you!" Tim and Sue were very surprised. They never saw a talking dog before. They all slid down the slide together, laughing and having fun. The kids on the bus saw them and joined in. It was the best day at the park ever.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friends. One day, Max saw a towel by the door. He wanted to play with it. Max picked up the towel with his mouth. He held the towel tight. Max took the towel outside. He saw his friend, a little bird named Tim. Max asked Tim if he wanted to play with the towel. Tim said yes. They played a game of tug and pull. They had so much fun with the towel. After a while, Max and Tim were tired. They sat under a big tree. Max held the towel close. He was happy to have a new toy and a good friend. Max and Tim played with the towel every day, and they were very happy.
