Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in his big yard. One day, he saw a bush with pretty red berries. His mom told him, "Tim, be serious. Do not touch the bush. It is not safe." Tim was a good boy, so he did not touch the bush. But he wanted to lift something. He saw a big rock near the bush. Tim thought, "I will lift this rock and show my mom how strong I am." Tim tried to lift the rock. It was very heavy, but he did not give up. He tried and tried, and finally, the rock moved. Under the rock, Tim found a small shiny key. He showed the key to his mom, and she was very happy. She said, "Tim, this is the key to a secret box. I was looking for it for a long time. Thank you for finding it." Tim was proud that he could help his mom. He learned that being serious and listening to his mom was important. And he also learned that he was strong enough to lift the big rock.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very anxious because he saw a big cat in the yard. He wanted to protect his home, but he was too scared to go outside. Max said to his friend, a bird named Sam, "I suffer because I am not strong. I wish I had the power to chase the cat away." Sam thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea. Let's work together." So, Max and Sam made a plan. When the cat came back, Max barked very loud while Sam flew around the cat's head. The cat got scared and ran away. Max and Sam were so happy that they had the power to protect their home together.

The day was long and sunny. A little girl named Emma wanted to take a nap. Her mom said, "No, not yet Emma. It’s time to go pick pumpkins! You can take a nap later.” So Emma and her mom went to the pumpkin patch. Emma found the perfect pumpkin. It was round and thick. “This pumpkin is perfect for our house!" Emma said. Emma and her mom took the pumpkin home. After they put it in the house, Emma was ready for a nap. "Mom, can I please take a nap now?" "Yes, Emma. You can take a nap now. Just rest your eyes and take a peaceful nap." So Emma closed her eyes and fell into a deep, thick pumpkin-scented nap.

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Bessie. Bessie lived on a big farm with many friends. Bessie liked to eat grass and play in the sun. One day, Bessie met a new friend. The new friend was a little girl named Mia. Mia said, "Welcome to our farm, Bessie!" Bessie was happy to have a new friend. Mia's mom cooked some beef for dinner. Bessie watched Mia eat her food. Mia said, "This beef is perfect!" Bessie was glad that Mia liked her dinner. They both played and had fun together on the farm.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On that farm, there was an enormous pig named Pinky. Pinky loved to eat lots and lots of food. All the other animals on the farm knew that Pinky was very big and loved to eat. One day, a small bird named Chirpy came to visit the farm. Chirpy saw Pinky and said, "You are so big! I want to be big like you." Pinky smiled and said, "If you want to be big like me, you have to eat lots of food." Chirpy wanted to be big, so he asked Pinky, "Can you give me some of your food?" Pinky thought for a moment and said, "Okay, but you have to do something for me first. You have to find me a big, yummy treat." Chirpy flew all around the farm, looking for a big treat for Pinky. He found an enormous apple and brought it back to Pinky. Pinky was very happy and said, "Thank you, Chirpy! Now you can have some of my food." Chirpy ate some of Pinky's food and started to grow bigger and bigger. All the other animals on the farm were amazed at how big Chirpy had become. From that day on, Chirpy and Pinky were the best of friends, and they always shared their food with each other.
