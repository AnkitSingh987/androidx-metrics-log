Category: Intermediate
Title: The Empty Triangle and the Car

Once upon a time there was an empty triangle. It liked to move all around the room. One day, it moved too much and bumped into a toy car. The car started to cry and the triangle felt bad. It didn't mean to make the car sad. So the triangle started to think of a way to make the car happy again. After some time, it came up with an idea. It moved over to the car and used its triangle shape to help build a block tower. The car had lots of fun helping the triangle build this tower. When they were finished, the triangle and car looked at their tower together. They were so proud of themselves. The triangle was glad it could make the car happy again. They were friends forever.

Once upon a time there was a brave little boy called Jack. He was three years old and loved exploring his village. One day he decided to go on an adventure. He grabbed his shield and put it on his back. He wanted it to protect him from the harsh sun. As Jack walked, he could feel the sun start to melt the shield. He realised he was in trouble and started to run. Suddenly, he saw a friendly face. "Hello! Can you help me?" he asked. The face smiled and said: "I can help you. Come with me." The friendly face led Jack to a cool, shady tree. Jack thanked her for her help, and sat in the shade for some rest. He was happy because his shield was safe from the harsh sun. From that day on, Jack was able to explore the village safely, with the help of his shield!

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful day on Tuesday. On this day, a girl named Sue and a boy named Tim wanted to play. They went to the park to have fun. At the park, they saw a big tree. Sue said, "Let's measure the tree!" Tim agreed and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They used their hands to measure the tree. They counted one, two, three, four, and five. The tree was five hands tall. After measuring the tree, they played together all day. They laughed and had fun. The beautiful Tuesday was a happy day for Sue and Tim. They went home tired but smiling, excited for more adventures together.

One day, a blue bird named Bob was trying to balance on a thin branch. He was very good at it. He loved to show his friends how he could balance on one leg. His friend, Cat, came to watch. "Look, Cat! I can balance on one leg!" said Bob. Cat clapped her paws and cheered for Bob. They were having so much fun. Then, a big wind came and blew Bob off the branch. He fell into a bush. Bob was scared, but he was okay. He took a big breath with his lungs and flew back up to the branch. He tried to balance again, but this time, he held on tight with both legs. Bob and Cat laughed and played together, happy that they were safe and still having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly man who lived in a small house. One day, he found a leak in his roof. He was sad because his house was wet. He needed to replace the old roof to make his house dry again. So, he went to his friend, the jolly man, for help. The jolly man said, "I will help you replace your roof and fix the leak!" They worked together all day to fix the roof. They were happy because they were helping each other. But then, something unexpected happened. The jolly man found a big box in the roof. He opened the box and found a lot of toys inside. They were so surprised! They shared the toys with all the kids in the town. The kids were very happy. The jolly man and his friend learned that when you help others, good things happen. They were very happy because they made the kids happy, and their house was dry again. And they lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is, when you help others, you will be happy too.
