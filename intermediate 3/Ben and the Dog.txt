Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and the Dog

Ben likes to draw pictures with his crayons. He has a folder where he keeps his best pictures. He is very proud of his folder. One day, he goes to play with his friend Sam. Sam has many toys and games. Ben wants to show Sam his folder, but Sam is not interested. He says, "Your pictures are boring. Let's play with my cars instead." Ben feels sad, but he agrees to play with Sam. He puts his folder on the floor, next to Sam's bed. They play with the cars for a while, but then Sam's mom calls them for lunch. Ben and Sam go to the kitchen. They leave their toys and Ben's folder in Sam's room. They do not know that Sam's dog, Spot, is hiding under the bed. Spot is hungry and curious. He smells something on the floor. He comes out from under the bed and sees Ben's folder. He thinks it is a snack. He takes the folder in his mouth and starts to chew it. He rips the folder and the pictures inside. He makes a big mess. He does not like the taste of the folder and the crayons. He spits them out and runs away. When Ben and Sam come back from lunch, they see the mess in Sam's room. Ben sees his folder and his pictures. They are torn and dirty. He feels very angry and sad. He says, "Look what your dog did! He took my folder and ruined my pictures! They were my best pictures! I worked very hard on them!" Sam sees the mess and feels ashamed. He knows his dog is naughty and he should have put him in his cage. He says, "I'm sorry, Ben. I didn't know Spot was here. He likes to take things and chew them. He is a bad dog. I will help you clean up and make new pictures." Ben is still angry and sad, but he sees that Sam is sorry. He says, "OK, Sam. Thank you. But next time, please be careful with my folder. It is very important to me." Sam nods and says, "I will, Ben. I'm sorry again. You are a good friend." They hug and start to clean up. They decide to make new pictures together. They use Sam's crayons and paper. They draw cars and dogs and flowers. They have fun and forget about the mess. They put their new pictures in a new folder. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to have fun and be adventurous. One day, Tim's mom told him they were going on a holiday. Tim was very excited. On the holiday, Tim saw a big wheel. He wanted to ride it. He asked his mom, "Can I ride the big wheel?" His mom said, "Yes, you can." Tim was so happy. Tim got on the big wheel and it started to turn. He went up and down, round and round. He laughed and had so much fun. Tim's holiday was the best. He loved being adventurous with his mom.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there lived a white otter. The white otter had many friends. They liked to play and swim together. One day, the white otter and his friends were playing near the water. The white otter saw a big fish and wanted to catch it. He swam very fast to get the fish. But he did not see his friend, the duck, who was in his way. The white otter bumped into the duck, and the duck fell into the water. The duck was sad and said, "You hurt me, white otter." The white otter felt bad and said, "I am sorry, duck. I did not see you. Please forgive me." The duck looked at his friend and said, "I forgive you, white otter. Let's be careful next time." After that, the white otter and the duck played more carefully. They had lots of fun together. All the animals in the pond were happy and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy boy who loved to draw with his pencil. One day, the pencil broke and he got very angry and frustrated. His mum came to him, smiled and said calmly "You should forgive your pencil!" The little boy thought for a moment and said, "How can I forgive it?" His mum said, "It's easy. Just say the words 'I forgive you'. Those words are very special and powerful." The little boy smiled, closed his eyes and said, "I forgive you." He suddenly noticed that his pencil felt lighter. When he opened his eyes, he noticed it was one piece again. The little boy thought in wonder and looked at his mum. She winked and said, "Forgiveness is a beautiful thing. When you forgive someone or something, you are the one that benefits the most." The little boy smiled, hugged his mum and said, "Yes, I understand now." The moral of the story is that forgiveness is a powerful gift that brings peace and joy. When we forgive, we create a safe and loving environment that can only lead to good things.
