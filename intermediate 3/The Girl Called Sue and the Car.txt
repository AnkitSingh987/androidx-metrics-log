Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Sue and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Sue. She had long hair and she was very impatient. She wanted everything to happen quickly. One day she was in a hurry, but she had to wait for her mum to finish talking. Sue was getting very impatient and she stamped her foot in anger. Then she started combing her hair, but because she was in a hurry, she was very rough with the comb. She wanted to hurry and go out to play. However, her mum said she had to wait. Sue was getting so impatient she started to cry. But her mum said that if she was patient, she would be able to go sooner! So, Sue waited and eventually her mum was ready to go. Just as they got to the door, they heard a loud noise. A car had broken down on the other side of the road. This meant that Sue and her mum had to delay going out to play. But Sue was a brave girl and eventually the car was fixed. Sue was finally able to go outside and play!

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. The ball was a big, red circle. He would run and jump and catch the ball all day long. One day, Spot's ball got very dirty. It had mud and dirt all over it. Spot's mom said, "Spot, you need to clean your ball." Spot wanted to clean his ball, but he didn't know how. Spot's mom showed him how to clean the ball. They took a bucket of water and a soft cloth. Spot dipped the cloth in the water and rubbed it on the ball. He cleaned the ball until it was shiny and red again. Spot was happy to have his clean ball back, and he played with it all day long.

One day, a big dog and a little cat were in the park. The dog was very slow and the cat was very fast. They liked to play together. The dog said, "Let's play a game. I will try to catch you, but you have to wait for me to nod before you run." The cat liked this idea and said, "Okay, I will wait for your nod." The dog nodded and the cat ran very fast. The slow dog tried to catch the cat, but he was too slow. They both laughed and had a lot of fun. After playing for a while, the dog and the cat were very tired. They sat under a tree and took a long rest. They were happy to be friends and play together.

Once upon a time, there was a big bee hive. The bees were very busy making honey. One day, a little girl named Lily saw the hive. She wanted to look at the bees, but she was scared. Lily's mom saw her looking at the hive. She said, "Lily, do you want to see the bees?" Lily nodded her head. So, her mom took a fake bee and put it on display for Lily to see. Lily looked at the fake bee and smiled. She said, "Mom, I like the bee, but I want to see the real bees too." Her mom held her hand and they went close to the hive. They watched the bees work together to make honey. Lily was happy and not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little white bunny. The bunny liked to cook. One day, the bunny wanted to cook something new. The bunny saw a pot with steam coming out. The steam was hot and made the bunny's nose wiggle. The bunny hopped closer to see what was inside the pot. Inside the pot, there were yummy carrots. The bunny put the carrots on a plate and ate them all up. The white bunny was very happy and full.

Once upon a time, there was a little bee. The bee was very careless. One day, the bee went for a walk. As the bee walked, it saw a big tree. The bee wanted to go up the tree. It flew up and up. The bee was happy. But, the careless bee did not see a spider web. The bee got stuck! A nice bug came and helped the bee. The bee said "thank you" and went back to its walk, being more careful.
