Category: Intermediate
Title: The Light Bird Found Violin and the Cat

One day, a light bird found a violin. The bird wanted to play it, but it was too big. So, the bird asked a cat for help. "Can you help me balance the violin?" the bird asked. The cat said, "Yes, I will help you." The cat held the violin and the bird started to play. They made beautiful music together. All of the animals came to listen. They all danced and had fun. The bird and the cat were happy to be friends. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the violin away. The bird and the cat were sad. But the wind brought something new. It was a small violin, just right for the bird. The bird played the small violin, and the cat danced. The animals came back and they all had fun again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a charming deer. The deer had many friends who liked to play with her. One day, the deer met a little bird who was lost. "Hi, I am lost," said the little bird. "Can you help me find my way home?" The charming deer wanted to help her new friend. She thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I know where your home is. It reminds me of my own home. Let's go!" The deer and the little bird walked together through the forest. They saw many pretty flowers and trees. The little bird was very happy to have met the charming deer. Finally, they reached the little bird's home. "Thank you for helping me," said the little bird. "You are a very good friend." The charming deer smiled and said, "You're welcome! I am happy to help. Now, let's play together!" And they played and laughed in the forest, becoming the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big bull named Bob. Bob lived in a small farm with many animals. One day, Bob saw a disgusting mud puddle. He thought it would be fun to play in it. As Bob played in the mud, a little bird came by and whispered, "Bob, you should not play in the mud. It is dirty and not good for you." But Bob did not listen to the bird. He said, "I like the mud. It is fun to play in." Bob played in the mud all day and got very dirty. When the sun went down, he went back to the farm. The other animals saw him and said, "Bob, you are so dirty! You cannot come in." Bob was sad and had to sleep outside. He was cold and lonely all night.

Tommy loved to watch the mechanic work on the cars. He liked the big tools and the loud noises. He wanted to help the mechanic, but he was too small. One day, the mechanic gave Tommy a spray bottle. He said, "You can spray water on the cars to make them shiny. But be careful, don't spray the engine or the wires. They don't like water." Tommy was very happy. He took the spray bottle and went to the cars. He sprayed water on the doors, the windows, the wheels, and the lights. He made the cars shiny and wet. But then, he saw a red car with a hood that was open. He was curious. He wanted to see what was inside. He walked to the red car and looked at the engine. He saw many wires and tubes and metal parts. He thought they looked dirty. He decided to spray water on them to make them clean. He pressed the spray bottle and water came out. He sprayed water on the engine and the wires. He heard a hiss and a spark. He smelled something bad. He saw smoke and fire. The mechanic saw what Tommy did. He was very alert. He ran to the red car and grabbed a fire extinguisher. He sprayed foam on the fire and put it out. He was angry and scared. He shouted at Tommy, "What did you do? You sprayed water on the engine! You could have burned the car and yourself! You were very naughty and dangerous!" Tommy was very sorry. He dropped the spray bottle and started to cry. He said, "I'm sorry, I'm sorry, I didn't know. I wanted to help you. I wanted to make the car clean." The mechanic sighed. He was still angry, but he felt sorry for Tommy too. He said, "I know you wanted to help, but you have to listen to me. You can't spray water on the engine or the wires. They are very sensitive and can catch fire. You have to be careful and follow the rules." He hugged Tommy and wiped his tears. He said, "It's okay, don't cry. The fire is out and the car is not too damaged. I can fix it. But you have to promise me, never spray water on the engine or the wires again. Do you understand?" Tommy nodded. He said, "I understand. I promise. I'm sorry." The mechanic smiled. He said, "Good. Now, let's go and have some ice cream. You can still help me with the cars, but only with the spray bottle and the shiny parts. Deal?" Tommy smiled too. He said, "Deal." He took the mechanic's hand and they walked to the ice cream shop. He learned his lesson and never sprayed water on the engine or the wires again. He became a good helper and a good friend to the mechanic.
