Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Film

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to watch films with her mom and dad. One day, they found a new film to watch together. Amy's mom put the film on, but it was a bad film. The pictures were not nice, and the sounds were not fun. Amy did not like it. She felt sad because she wanted to watch a good film. Amy told her mom and dad, "I don't like this film. It's bad." Her mom and dad agreed. They stopped the bad film and found a better one. The new film made Amy happy. They all watched it together and had a great time.

Mia was walking through the park with her dad when she stumbled on the grass. She held onto her dad tightly and looked down at her shoe. She saw a sparkly object on the ground and wanted to pick it up. Her dad told her it was beautiful so Mia eagerly picked it up and held onto it tightly. Dad said that it was nice but they had to keep walking as they had places to go. Mia was so excited to keep this sparkly thing that she decided to find the perfect place to store it away. She decided to carefully put it in her pocket, so she wouldn't forget it. When they got home, Mia ran to her room and opened her drawer to take out the sparkly object. Dad followed her and asked how much it would cost. Mia felt guilty because she didn't know the price. Dad said that it was okay and she could keep it. She was glad she could keep it, but she never forgot the feeling of being guilty of not knowing the price.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful shop. It was full of toys, balloons, and candy. Kids loved to go to the shop to see all the fun things. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the shop with her mom. She heard a funny noise coming from a box. She opened the box and found a cute, little puppy inside. The puppy barked and wagged its tail. Lily loved the puppy and asked her mom if she could keep it. Her mom said yes, and they took the puppy home. The puppy was so happy to have a new friend. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to ride her red bike. She rode her bike every day. It was easy for her, and she had so much fun. One day, Lily met a boy named Tim. Tim wanted to ride a bike too. But he didn't know how. Lily said, "I can help you, Tim. Riding a bike is easy." Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Lily." Lily and Tim practiced riding bikes together. Soon, Tim could ride a bike too. They both had fun riding their bikes. They became good friends and rode their bikes every day. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big hill with icy snow on it. Tim wanted to play in the snow. He put on his warm coat and gloves. Then, he started to walk up the hill. As Tim walked, he saw a little bird. The bird was cold and could not fly. Tim wanted to help the bird. He took off his glove and let the bird sit on his finger. Tim decided to carry the bird back to his house to get warm. When Tim got home, he opened the door and the bird flew off his finger. But the bird did not fly away. Instead, the bird turned into a magic fairy! The fairy thanked Tim for his help and gave him a big surprise. Tim's icy snow hill turned into a fun snow slide. Tim and the fairy played on the slide all day.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big, round ball in her yard. She was very happy and wanted to share it with her friend, Tom. She went to Tom's house and showed him the round ball. Tom frowned because he thought the ball was his. "Give me my ball back!" Tom said. Sue frowned too, but she gave it back to him. They both felt sad and did not want to play together anymore. Later, they found out that the round ball was not Tom's ball, but a rare, magic ball that could make them happy. They decided to share the ball and play together. They learned that sharing can make everyone happy and it is better to be friends than to fight.
