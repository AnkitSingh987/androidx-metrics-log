Category: Intermediate
Title: Daisy the Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Daisy. Daisy wanted to make a surprise for her mom. She found a rare pan in the cupboard and shouted for joy. The mom was surprised when she heard the shout. Daisy started to put ingredients in the pan. But then her mom said "No, don't put that in there!". Daisy didn't understand why not. Her mom said that the ingredients were not right and they would not taste good when cooked. Daisy got upset and started to shout and cry. Mom tried to explain about the cooking ingredients but Daisy wouldn't listen. Finally, Mom said that if Daisy found the right ingredients, she could make a special surprise for her. Daisy was so happy and ran to find the ingredients. After a long search, she got the rare ingredients and ran back to the pan. Mom was so happy with Daisy's efforts and said "Well done!". Daisy put the ingredients inside the pan and the wonderful smell filled the room. All were surprised and happy. Everyone shouted with joy as they tasted the special surprise made by Daisy!

Once upon a time, there was a mighty boy. His name was Jack. He loved to play and explore. One day, Jack was looking for a way to make his mommy happy. He thought and thought and finally had an idea. He would kiss her! So Jack went looking for his mommy. He found her in the kitchen baking a cake. "Mommy," he said, "I want to kiss you!" His mommy smiled and leaned down so Jack could reach her. He kissed her on the cheek and said, "I love you, Mommy." His mommy hugged him tight and said, "I love you too, Jack. That was a mighty nice way to make me happy."

Once upon a time, there was a little ranch. On the ranch, there was a cow, a pig, and a sheep. They liked to play together. One day, they were playing a game. The cow would clap, and the pig and sheep would jump. The pig said, "Let's see who can jump the highest when the cow claps!" The sheep agreed. They were both alert and ready to jump. The cow clapped her hooves, and the pig and sheep jumped as high as they could. The cow clapped again, and the pig and sheep jumped even higher. They were having so much fun. The sun was shining, and the friends were happy. At the end of the day, they all went back to their homes on the ranch. They couldn't wait to play again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run very fast. One day, Tim saw a big cup. He wanted to take the cup to his mom. Tim said to his friend, "Let me take the cup to my mom." His friend said, "Okay, but be careful. Don't run too fast." Tim was happy and started to run with the cup. Tim did not listen to his friend. He ran very fast. He tripped and fell. The cup broke into many pieces. Tim was sad, and his mom was not happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her rubber ball. The ball was red and blue and would bounce very high. One day, Lily lost her rubber ball in the park. She was very sad. She looked and looked for her ball, but she could not find it. Her mommy and daddy helped her look, but they could not find the ball either. Then, a kind man who worked at the park came to help. He had a big supply of rubber balls. He gave Lily a new ball just like her old one. Lily was so happy and thanked the man. She played with her new rubber ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to hop around and play in the sun. One day, Bobo found a weak tree with a big hole in it. He decided to store his toys in the hole. In the hole, Bobo found a big root. He thought it was a fun toy to play with. So, he took the root out of the hole and put his toys inside. He was very happy to have a new place to store his toys. Every day, Bobo played with the root and his toys. He had so much fun! His friends, the birds and squirrels, also liked the weak tree and the root. They all played together and had a great time. And they all lived happily ever after.
