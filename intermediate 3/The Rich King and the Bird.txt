Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rich King and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there lived a rich king. The king loved the warm season. He liked to play outside with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, a little bird came to the king's window. "Help me, please," said the bird. "I need to escape from a big, mean cat." The king wanted to help the bird. So, he opened the window and let the bird come inside. The king and his friends hid the bird in a small box. They played a game to keep the cat away. The cat tried to find the bird but could not. The bird was safe, and the cat went away. "Thank you for helping me escape," said the bird. The king was happy to help. The bird flew away, and the king and his friends went back to playing in the warm season. They knew they had done a good thing by helping the bird.

Lily and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They saw a big bowl of fruit on the table. Lily wanted to try an orange. She picked one up and peeled it. She broke it into pieces and gave one to Ben. "Here, try this. It is sweet and juicy," she said. Ben took a bite of the orange. He made a funny face and spit it out. He did not like it at all. "Yuck! This is sour and bitter. I don't like it," he said. Lily was surprised. She tasted her piece of orange. It was not sour or bitter. It was sweet and juicy. She liked it very much. "Why do you say it is sour and bitter? It is not. It is sweet and juicy. I like it," she said. Ben shook his head. He did not want to try it again. "No, it is sour and bitter. You have a different tongue than me. I like apples better. They are crunchy and sweet," he said. Lily shrugged. She did not understand why Ben did not like the orange. She liked apples too, but she also liked oranges. She decided to eat the rest of her orange by herself. "Okay, you can have an apple. I will have the orange. We have different tongues, but we are still friends," she said. Ben smiled. He picked an apple from the bowl and bit into it. He liked it very much. "Yes, we are still friends. We can like different things. That is okay," he said. They finished their fruit and went back to playing. They were happy and full.

Once upon a time, in a small, tidy house, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia had a red ball that she loved to play with every day. She would throw it, bounce it, and kick it all around her house. One sunny day, Mia left her ball outside to dry after playing in the water. That night, when she went to bed, she had a nightmare. In her bad dream, her red ball was lost and she could not find it anywhere. Mia woke up feeling very sad. The next morning, Mia ran outside to see if her red ball was still there. To her joy, she found it right where she left it, all dry and ready to play. Mia hugged her ball and promised to never leave it outside again. From that day on, Mia and her red ball were always together, and she never had another nightmare about losing it.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Buddy. Buddy had a very long nose. He loved to play and always stayed calm. One day, Buddy went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Buddy saw a little girl with a red ball. The little girl threw the ball, and Buddy ran to get it. But something unexpected happened! When Buddy tried to pick up the ball, it got stuck on his long nose. Buddy tried to get the ball off his nose, but it would not come off. His friends laughed at him, but they did not punish him. They knew it was not his fault. They all tried to help Buddy get the ball off his nose. Then, a kind old lady came up to Buddy. She gently touched the ball, and it magically came off his nose! The old lady was a fairy! She smiled at Buddy and his friends. They all thanked her and played happily ever after.
