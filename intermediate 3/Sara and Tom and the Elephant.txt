Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Tom and the Elephant

Sara and Tom like to play with clay. They make animals and people and cars with clay. Sara makes a big elephant with a long nose and big ears. Tom makes a small mouse with a long tail and small ears. Tom wants to play a trick on Sara. He takes a piece of clay and makes it look like a cheese. He puts it near Sara's elephant. He thinks Sara will think it is real cheese and try to eat it. But the clay cheese is bitter and not yummy. Sara sees the cheese and thinks it is for her elephant. She picks it up and puts it near the elephant's mouth. She says, "Here, elephant, have some cheese. You must be hungry." She does not know it is clay cheese. The elephant tries to eat the cheese, but it does not like it. It spits it out and makes a loud noise. Sara is surprised and scared. She drops the clay cheese and covers her ears. She says, "What is wrong, elephant? Why are you angry?" Tom laughs and says, "It is not cheese, Sara. It is clay. I made it to fool you. It is bitter and not yummy. You should not give it to the elephant." He thinks he is funny, but Sara does not. Sara is sad and angry. She says, "That is not nice, Tom. You are mean. You hurt the elephant and me. You should say sorry and finish your mouse." She picks up her elephant and hugs it. She does not want to play with Tom anymore.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. They saw a new nurse pass by. The nurse had a big bag with her. Sue and her mom said "hello" to the nurse. The nurse smiled and said "hello" back. They all played on the swings and had fun. Then, the nurse opened her big bag. Sue thought she was going to find band-aids or medicine. But, something unexpected happened. The nurse pulled out a big, red ball! Sue and her mom were very surprised. The nurse said, "I like to have fun too! Let's play!" Sue, her mom, and the nurse played with the ball. They laughed and had a great time. The new nurse was not just good at helping people, but also good at making friends. Sue and her mom were very happy to meet her. They all promised to play together again soon.

Lily and Ben were playing in the yard with their toys. They liked to make pretend stories with their dolls and cars. They had a colorful house made of blocks and a big hill made of dirt. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise in the sky. They looked up and saw dark clouds and flashes of light. They heard a boom and felt a drop of water on their noses. "It's a storm!" Lily said. "We have to go inside!" "But what about our toys?" Ben asked. "They will get wet and dirty!" Lily thought for a moment. She had an idea. She ran to the shed and got a big blanket. She came back and raised the blanket over their toys. She asked Ben to help her hold the corners. "Look, we made a tent!" Lily said. "Now our toys are safe and dry!" Ben smiled. He liked Lily's idea. They crawled under the blanket and hugged their toys. They listened to the storm and made more pretend stories. They were not afraid. They were happy.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big tree with interesting leaves. The leaves were many colors. Lily liked the tree a lot. Lily saw a small bird on the tree. The bird said, "I am hungry. Can you supply me some food?" Lily nodded and looked around. She found some bread on the ground. She gave the bread to the bird. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Lily!" The bird flew away. Lily felt good. She played at the park all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was very happy and he loved to play. He loved playing with animals, running around in the park and playing hide and seek. One day, Tom had an idea. He wanted to make a wish and see if it would come true. He closed his eyes, made a wish and waited. Nothing happened. He waited, but nothing changed. Tom was so sad and felt a little bit bitter that nothing magical had happened. Then, one day, he was playing in the park when he tripped over a special rock. He got up and found a secret box with many colourful jewels inside. He opened the box and realized that his wish had come true! Tom was excited and happy that his wish had really come true. Since then, Tom always played with the box full of jewels, and would often think back to the wish he had made. He was no longer bitter and learned that magic can still happen if you believe in it.
