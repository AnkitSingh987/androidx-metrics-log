Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Clever Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Spot. Spot had a black coat and loved to play with his friends. One day, Spot's family was going on a trip, and he wanted to go with them. Spot knew he had to pack his toys and food for the trip. Spot said to his friend, Cat, "I need to pack my things for the trip. Can you help me?" Cat nodded and said, "Yes, I can help you, Spot." Together, they packed Spot's toys and food into a big bag. The bag was black, just like Spot's coat. They were very happy to be working together. Finally, everything was packed and ready for the trip. Spot and Cat high-fived each other. Spot's family was very proud of him for being so clever and packing his things all by himself. Spot said goodbye to Cat and went on the trip with his family. They had a great time and Spot couldn't wait to tell Cat all about it when he got back.

Once upon a time, there was a big box of colors. The colors liked to play and have fun. One day, a new color came to join them. The new color was called Tasty Orange. Tasty Orange was very happy to join the other colors. They all played together and made beautiful pictures. The sun was shining, and the colors were having lots of fun. After playing, the colors got hungry. Tasty Orange shared its tasty treats with all the colors. They all ate and laughed together. The colors were happy that Tasty Orange joined them, and they all became good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a symbol on a big rock. The symbol was like a smiley face. It reminded Tim of his friend, Sam. Tim wanted to show Sam the symbol. Tim ran to Sam's house and said, "Sam, I found a cool symbol on a rock. Let's go see it!" Sam was sorry he was busy. He said, "I can't go now, but I'll go with you later." Tim was sad but said okay. Later, Sam and Tim went to the big rock. But the symbol was gone! They were both surprised. Then they saw a little girl with a chalk in her hand. She said, "I drew the smiley face. I'm sorry it's gone now. I'll draw it again for you." Tim and Sam smiled, happy to see the symbol again.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play with fire. They had a box of matches that they hid under their bed. One day, when their mom was out, they decided to light some matches and watch the flames. "Look, Anna, how pretty the flame is!" Ben said, holding a match in his hand. "Yes, Ben, it is very pretty. But be careful, don't burn yourself or the bed," Anna said, holding another match. They lit more matches and threw them on the floor. They laughed and clapped as they saw the flames grow bigger and brighter. "Anna, I want to light more matches. Give me some," Ben said, reaching for the box. "No, Ben, these are mine. You already had enough. Go away," Anna said, pulling the box away. "Anna, don't be selfish. Share with me. It's not fair," Ben said, trying to grab the box. They started to fight over the box, pulling and pushing each other. They did not care about the flames that were spreading around them. They did not hear the smoke alarm that was beeping. They did not see the fire that was reaching their bed. "Anna, Ben, what are you doing? Stop it! There's a fire!" their mom shouted, running into the room. She saw her children fighting over a box of matches, surrounded by flames. She was scared and angry. She tried to reach them, but it was too late. The fire was too big and too hot. She could not save them. She could only cry and scream.

There were two friends, Jack and Jill. They were both very organized. They wanted to make some money, so they decided to sell soap. They put all the soap in a box and it looked very neat. They went out and sold all the soap. Everyone was happy they could buy the soap and it made Jack and Jill very proud. At the end of the day, they had lots of money. Jack and Jill were very happy. They had worked so hard and sold all their soap. Jack and Jill had money for lots of new things. They were proud of their work and had lots of fun!
