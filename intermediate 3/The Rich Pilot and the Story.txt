Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rich Pilot and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a rich pilot. He had a big plane. He loved to fly high in the sky. One day, a little boy came to the pilot. The boy said, "Can I fly with you?" The pilot said, "Yes, you can come with me." So, the boy and the pilot went up in the sky together. While they were flying, the boy saw a poor man on the ground. The boy said, "Why don't we help him?" The pilot thought about it and said, "You are right. Let's help him." They landed the plane and gave the poor man some food and clothes. The poor man was very happy. The pilot learned that it is good to help others. He and the boy became good friends. They flew in the sky and helped many more people together. And they lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is: It is important to help others and be kind.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a playful cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his ball all day long. He would jump and run in the yard, making everyone laugh. One day, Tom saw a big jar of salt in the kitchen. He wanted to take it and play with it. So, Tom jumped up and knocked the jar down. The salt spilled all over the floor. Tom had fun playing with the salt, but soon his mom came in and saw the mess. She was not happy. She told Tom to help clean up the salt. Tom learned that he should only play with his toys, not the things in the kitchen.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big pile of leaves and decided to jump in it. They skipped to the pile and took turns throwing leaves in the air and laughing. But when Lily landed on the ground, she felt something hard under the leaves. She moved some leaves away and saw a skull. It was white and had no eyes or nose. It looked scary and made Lily scream. "Help! Help! There is a skull here!" Lily cried. Tom heard Lily and ran to her. He saw the skull and was scared too. He wondered who it belonged to and why it was in the park. "Maybe it was a bad person who did bad things and got punished," Tom said. "Or maybe it was a good person who got lost and nobody found them," Lily said. They felt sad and sorry for the skull. They decided to bury it under the leaves and say a prayer for it. "Goodbye, skull. We hope you are in a better place now. We are sorry you were alone and forgotten. We will be good and kind to everyone, so we don't end up like you," they said. They held hands and skipped away from the pile. They learned that life is precious and that they should be grateful for what they have. They also learned that they should not judge people by their looks, but by their hearts.

Once upon a time, there was a young lion who lived in a big forest. He had a nice home, with a lot of furniture. All the animals in the forest were scared of the lion because he liked to roar. One day, the young lion was playing with some animal friends. They were running around and playing tag. Suddenly, the lion took a deep breath and he started to roar. It was so loud! All the animals ran away, scared of the lion's roar. After the lion was done roaring, he was feeling lonely. He looked at all the empty furniture in his house and he wished he had someone to play with. Suddenly he heard a small voice asking if he wanted to play. It was a young rabbit who wasn't scared of the lion's roar. The lion and the rabbit became very good friends. They played tag and hide-and-seek together. The lion didn't roar anymore, because he was so happy to have a friend. The young lion and the rabbit lived happily ever after.
