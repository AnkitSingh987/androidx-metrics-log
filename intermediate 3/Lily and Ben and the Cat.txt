Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Cat

Lily and Ben were playing with crayons and paper. They liked to draw animals and flowers and cars. Lily drew a big purple cat with green eyes. Ben drew a yellow truck with red wheels. "Look at my cat!" Lily said. "She is so pretty and soft." "Look at my truck!" Ben said. "It is so fast and strong." They showed each other their drawings and smiled. They were proud of their work. But then, Lily saw something on her paper. It was brown and sticky and smelled bad. It was dog poop! Lily's dog, Max, had pooped on her paper when she was not looking. "Yuck!" Lily cried. "Max pooped on my paper! It is disgusting!" She dropped the paper and ran to the bathroom. She grabbed some toilet paper and tried to wipe the poop off her hands. But it was too much. She needed help. "Mommy!" Lily shouted. "Mommy, help me!" Mommy heard Lily and came to the bathroom. She saw the poop on Lily's hands and on the paper. She felt sorry for Lily. "Oh, Lily, I'm sorry," Mommy said. "Max is a naughty dog. He should not poop on your paper. Come here, I will help you." Mommy took Lily to the sink and washed her hands with soap and water. She threw the paper in the trash. She gave Lily a hug and a kiss. "It's okay, Lily," Mommy said. "You can draw another cat. I'm sure it will be even prettier than the first one." Lily nodded and smiled. She felt better. She thanked Mommy and went back to the playroom. She picked up a new paper and a new crayon. She drew a big purple cat with green eyes. She showed it to Ben. "Look at my cat!" Lily said. "She is so pretty and soft." "Wow, Lily, your cat is amazing!" Ben said. "Can I draw a truck with you?" "Sure, Ben," Lily said. "Let's draw together." They drew trucks and cats and had fun. They forgot about the poop and the paper. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Jenny. Jenny wanted to make something. She had an idea in her head and it made her smile. Jenny went to the kitchen and found some paper. She put the paper on the table and took some scissors. Then she started to make her idea. Carefully and slowly, she started cutting the paper. After she was done, she showed it to her mom. "Look, Mom! I made this!" Jenny said proudly. Her mom smiled and said, "That's beautiful, my darling. You are so talented!" Jenny was happy that her mom liked it. She had made something special, all by herself.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Molly. Every day Molly would go outside to play with her friends. As she was walking, one day she noticed a card lying on the ground. She quickly picked it up and saw that it had a picture of a very tasty cake on it. Molly couldn't believe her luck. She showed her friends the card and they were so excited. "Let's go to the bakery and get some cake!" said one of her friends. Molly and her friends started walking, chattering excitedly about the cake they were going to get. When they arrived at the bakery, Molly and her friends saw the most delicious cake they had ever seen. Molly couldn't wait to take a bite. "How did you know that this bakery existed?" asked one of her friends. Molly smiled and held up the card she had found earlier. "I noticed the card on the ground, and the picture on it looked so tasty!" The friends were amazed that Molly had been so lucky to find the card and get them some cake. They all enjoyed the tasty treat together. Molly never forgot her lucky find and always looked for special cards on the ground. She never knew what treats she might find!

One day, a little boy named Tim found an unknown ball. It was big and round. He did not know it was a basketball. He wanted to play with it, but did not know how. Tim saw his friend Sue and called her, "Sue, come see this ball I found!" Sue came and looked at the ball. She said, "Tim, that's a basketball! You need to try and throw it in a hoop to play." Tim and Sue tried to play with the basketball, but they could not find a hoop. They looked around and saw a big tree. They tried to throw the basketball in the tree, but it was too high. They asked a tall man for help. The tall man said, "I can help you, but you need to find a real hoop." Tim and Sue looked around and found a real hoop. They tried to play with the basketball and had lots of fun. They were happy they learned a new game.
