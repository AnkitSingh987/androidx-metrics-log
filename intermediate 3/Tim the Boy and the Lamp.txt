Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Lamp

Once upon a time, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a lamp that he loved very much. The lamp was fine and bright. Tim took the lamp with him everywhere he went. One day, Tim was walking with his lamp when he met a girl named Sue. "Hi, I'm Sue!" said the girl. "What's that?" she asked, pointing at Tim's lamp. "This is my fine lamp," said Tim. "It makes everything bright." Sue wanted to see the lamp work, so they waited until it was dark. But the dark did not come. They had to delay their fun. They played with other toys and ate snacks, but they were not happy. Finally, it was dark. Tim turned on the lamp, but it was not bright. The lamp was broken. Tim and Sue were sad. They could not play with the fine lamp anymore. The end.

One day, a three year old boy went to the beach with his parents. He saw the ocean and it looked so blue and beautiful. He exclaimed, "Wow!", and started to run towards it. His parents were worried because the water was deep, but the boy was brave and didn't hesitate. The warm sunshine was shining down, and the waves were gentle. Everything around was so peaceful, so the boy started to explore the shore. He saw all the little stones, the shells and some seaweed. The seaweed was especially fun because it was crunchy and a little bit salty. Suddenly, the boy saw something that caught his eye. He ran towards it and found a shiny blue rock in the sand. He enjoyed feeling the sharp edges against his fingers. He shouted to his parents, "Look! I found a blue rock like the ocean!" His parents smiled and said, "That's great! You found a special treasure." "Yes," the boy said, beaming. "This is my special blue treasure from the ocean." He kept his treasure closely and took it home. From then on, it was his most treasured possession.

Once upon a time, in a big, big forest, there lived a little bunny. The little bunny loved to look at the sky and listen to the rhythm of the rain. One day, the little bunny saw a big, big tree, and he wanted to climb it. As the little bunny climbed the big tree, he heard a sound. He looked up and saw a big, big bird. The big bird was sad because he could not find his way home. The little bunny wanted to help the big bird, so they both looked for the bird's home together. They looked high and low, following the rhythm of the rain. Then, something unexpected happened. The big bird found out that the big tree was his home all along! The little bunny and the big bird became best friends. The moral of the story is always to help others, even when it is hard.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim liked to help his mom and dad in the garden. One day, his mom gave him a rake. Tim was very happy. The next day, Tim went to the garden with his rake. He saw a big pile of leaves. Tim knew what to do. He started to rake the leaves. As he was raking, he saw something shiny under the leaves. Tim was very curious. Tim used his rake to stir the leaves. He found a small, shiny box. He opened the box and found a beautiful toy car. Tim was so happy! His hard work in the garden had led him to a wonderful surprise. And from that day on, Tim loved to help even more in the garden with his rake.

One day, a curious cat went for a walk. The cat saw a big tree. The tree had a saw on it. The cat wanted to play with the saw, but it was too high. A tall man came to the tree. He wanted the saw too. The man said, "I will pay you if you help me get the saw." The cat jumped on the man's back and climbed up to the saw. The cat got the saw for the man. The man was happy and paid the cat. The cat was happy too. They both played with the saw and had a fun day.
