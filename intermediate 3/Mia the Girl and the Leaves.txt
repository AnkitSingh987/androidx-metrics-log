Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a soft toy cat named Kitty. One day, Mia and Kitty went on a journey to find pretty things to decorate their room. As they walked, they saw a big tree. Mia said, "Look, Kitty! Let's get some leaves to decorate our room!" They picked up some leaves and put them in a bag. Then, they saw some pretty flowers. Mia said, "Kitty, these flowers will make our room pretty too!" So, they picked some flowers and put them in the bag. When they got home, Mia and Kitty decorated their room with the leaves and flowers. They were very happy with their journey. Mia said, "Kitty, our room is so pretty now!" Kitty meowed happily, and they both went to sleep in their beautiful room.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big bag with her. Lucy wanted to unpack her bag to find her snack. She was very hungry. Inside the bag, she found a sandwich with butter. Lucy liked butter a lot. She also found a cute toy cat. She loved her toy cat and played with it. Suddenly, a big dog came running. Lucy was frightened. The dog was loud and fast. But the dog just wanted to play. Lucy and the dog played together. They became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a mill. The mill was a bit unusual. It was very big and had lots of colors. Many people liked to visit the mill. One day, a little boy went to the mill with his mom and dad. When they got to the mill, the little boy saw a big, red door. He wanted to see what was behind it. He opened the door and went in. Inside, he found a room full of toys! The little boy was very happy. He played with the toys and forgot about his mom and dad. After a while, the little boy started to cry. He missed his mom and dad. He didn't know how to get back to them. Just then, the unusual mill heard the little boy cry. The mill wanted to help him. The mill started to shake and twist. The big, red door opened, and the little boy saw his mom and dad. They were right there, waiting for him! The little boy ran to them, and they all hugged. The mill had helped the little boy find his way back to his mom and dad. From that day on, the little boy knew that the unusual mill was a very special place.

Once upon a time, there was a pig who lived on a farm. This pig was very wealthy because he had a lot of food to eat. He was a big, happy pig. One day, a little bird came to the pig's farm. The bird was hungry and asked the pig, "Can I have some food, please?" But the wealthy pig did not want to share. He said, "No, this is my food!" The bird flew away, sad and hungry. Later, the pig saw the bird again. The bird had found food and was now happy. The pig thought about how he had not shared his food. He felt bad. The next time a friend asked for help, the pig decided to share. He learned that sharing with others is important and makes everyone happy.

One day a bald man named Bob went to the store. He was looking for something special. He found a large pan and put it in his shopping cart. At home, he talked to his 3-year-old daughter, Mary. “What should I do with this pan, Mary?” Bob asked her. “Let’s make some cookies!” Mary said excitedly. So Bob and Mary spent the day baking cookies. When night came, Bob had an idea. “Let’s put all the cookies we made into the pan,” he said. Mary took the pan and filled it with cookies. The next day Bob put the pan on the windowsill. He called the neighbors together and said, “Come and get some cookies!” Everyone was so excited and they thanked Bob for making their day. They all enjoyed the cookies they shared. Bob smiled. He knew he had put the pan to good use and that it was a great idea.
