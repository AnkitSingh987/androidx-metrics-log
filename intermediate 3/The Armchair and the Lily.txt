Category: Intermediate
Title: The Armchair and the Lily

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a small armchair. The armchair was very plain and it wanted to be pretty. It saw other chairs in the house looking nice and it was sad. One day, a girl named Lily saw the small armchair looking sad. She wanted to help it. Lily had an idea! She would decorate the armchair to make it pretty and happy. Lily found some colorful cloth and ribbons. She used them to decorate the small armchair. Now the armchair was pretty and happy. It was not sad anymore. Lily and the armchair became best friends and they had fun together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Suzy. She loved to visit her friend, Timmy, who lived by the beach. One sunny day, Suzy and Timmy went for a walk by the water. They saw many pretty things, like shells and pebbles. Suzy found a very special pebble. It was filthy, but she liked it. "Look, Timmy," she said, "I found a pebble!" Timmy looked at the pebble and said, "It's dirty. Let's clean it in the water." They washed the pebble in the water, and it became shiny and bright. Suzy and Timmy learned that day that even if something looks dirty or not pretty, it can still be special when you give it love and care. They played with the clean pebble and had a very fun day at the beach. They knew they would always remember the lesson they learned from the little pebble.

Lily and Max were twins who liked to play outside. One night, they saw many stars in the sky. They wanted to catch a star and keep it as a pet. "Let's go to the hill and find a star," Max said. "OK, but we have to be alert. Mom and Dad will punish us if they find out we are gone," Lily said. They put on their coats and shoes and sneaked out of the house. They ran to the hill and looked at the stars. They saw one that was very bright and big. "That's the one we want," Lily said. They reached out their hands and tried to grab the star. But the star was too far and too hot. They could not touch it. "Ow, ow, ow!" they cried. They heard a loud voice behind them. "What are you doing here?" It was their dad. He had followed them to the hill. He was angry and worried. "We wanted to catch a star, Dad," Max said. "A star? You can't catch a star. Stars are not pets. They are balls of fire in the sky. They are very dangerous. And you are very naughty. You left the house without telling us. You could have gotten hurt or lost. You are in big trouble," Dad said. He took them by the hand and led them back to the house. He put them in their beds and turned off the lights. "You are punished. No more playing outside for a week. And no more star hunting. Go to sleep and think about what you did," Dad said. Lily and Max felt sorry and sad. They wished they had listened to their mom and dad. They learned their lesson. Stars are not for catching. They are for watching.

There was a hot summer day. The sun shone brightly in the sky. It was a perfect day for going to the park. Two siblings, Jack and Jill, promised their mom they would go out and play. The pair ran out the door to the park. The sky was blue. As they played, they heard loud rumbles in the sky. A storm was coming. Jack and Jill promised each other that they would make it home before it started raining. They quickly ran home as fast as they could. The sky was dark now, with clouds everywhere. They were running so fast, but the raindrops started to fall. They tried to hurry. Finally, they reached home. They promised their mom they would be careful, and they were. Mom hugged them tightly, glad they had made it home safe. The storm passed, and the sun slowly came out again. They were thankful they had kept their promise.
