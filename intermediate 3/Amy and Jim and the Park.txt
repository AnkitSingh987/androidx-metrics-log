Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy and Jim and the Park

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Amy and Jim. They were playing in the park and dreaming of what the world had to offer, when suddenly they saw a volcano. "Let's go and meet the volcano!" said Amy. So they ran over to the volcano, and it was very bossy. "Go away!" it said. "This is my mountain, and you can't come here!" Amy and Jim were not scared though. They walked over to the volcano and asked it to be their friend. The volcano stepped forward, and said, "I will be your friend, but only if you promise to be my bossy friends." Amy and Jim looked at each other and smiled, and said, "Yes, we promise that!" From that moment on, Amy and Jim visited the volcano every day. They had lots of fun playing in the shadow of the volcano. It seemed that the bossy volcano was very happy to have them as friends. The end.

Mama and Bobby were playing in the backyard. Bobby was playing with his baseball. He threw it and caught it with his mitt. He was feeling so lively. After a while, Mama and Bobby left the backyard. Mama told Bobby to bring the baseball with him. But Bobby forgot and left it behind. Mama got angry and said, "Bobby, why didn't you listen to me and bring the baseball? That's wrong!" Bobby said, "I'm sorry, Mama." But Mama couldn't forgive Bobby. Mama tried to stay calm. She said, "Bobby go back to the backyard and pick up the baseball. Maybe I can forgive you." But Bobby was so upset that he ran away from home. Mama was still angry and she couldn't forgive Bobby. The story had a bad ending because Bobby and Mama could not forgive each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play outside in the sand. One day, he found a small box. He thought it was a normal box, but it was not. Tim wanted to bury the box in the sand. But his friend, Sam, did not want him to do that. Sam wanted to keep the box. They did not know what to do. They both wanted the box. Tim and Sam thought and thought. Then they had an idea. They would take turns with the box. Sam could keep the box today, and Tim could bury it tomorrow. They both liked this plan. They played with the box and had fun. The end.

Sam and Mia liked to play music. They had a band with their friends. Sam played the drum, Mia played the guitar, Ben played the piano and Lily sang. They practiced every day in Sam's garage. They wanted to be the best band ever. One day, they had a big show at the park. They were very excited. They packed their instruments and went to the park. But when they got there, they saw dark clouds in the sky. "Oh no," said Mia. "It looks like it will rain soon. What will we do?" "Don't worry," said Sam. "Maybe it will not rain. Maybe it will be normal. Let's set up our instruments and get ready to play." They did what Sam said. They put their instruments on the stage and checked the sound. They saw some people coming to watch them. They smiled and waved. But then, they heard a loud boom. It was thunder. And then, they felt a drop. It was rain. And then, more drops. And more. And more. It was raining hard. The people ran away. The instruments got wet. The band got wet. They were sad and scared. "Quick," said Ben. "Let's get our instruments and go to the garage. Maybe we can save them." They did what Ben said. They grabbed their instruments and ran to the garage. They dried them with towels and hoped they would still work. They were not sure. "Maybe we can play another day," said Lily. "Maybe the rain will stop. Maybe the people will come back. Maybe we can still be the best band ever." They did not know. They hoped. They waited. They played. They sang. They were a band.
