Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Dog

Anna loved to dance. She danced in the morning, she danced in the afternoon, she danced at night. She danced with her dolls, she danced with her dog, she danced with her mom and dad. She danced when she was happy, she danced when she was sad, she danced when she was bored. But there was one place where Anna could not dance. She could not dance at school. Her teacher said that dancing was not allowed in the classroom. She said that dancing was too loud, too messy, too silly. She said that Anna had to sit still, be quiet, and learn. Anna did not like that. She thought that dancing was fun, beautiful, and smart. One day, Anna had a great idea. She decided to dance in her head. She closed her eyes and imagined that she was in a big hall with music and lights and flowers. She imagined that she was wearing a pretty dress and shiny shoes. She imagined that she was spinning and jumping and twirling. She felt happy and free and alive. But then she heard a voice. It was her teacher. She was angry. She said that Anna was not paying attention. She said that Anna was being rude and lazy and naughty. She said that Anna had to open her eyes and do her work. Anna felt sad and scared and trapped. She wished that her teacher would allow her to dance. She wished that her teacher would see that dancing was not difficult, but easy. She wished that her teacher would join her in her dance and feel happy and free and alive. She wished that her teacher would love to dance as much as she did.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a little beetle named Buzzy. Buzzy had a big family, but he felt like he didn't belong. He was a spicy beetle, and he liked to do things that the other beetles didn't like to do. Buzzy loved to dance and play, but his family only liked to eat leaves and sleep. One day, Buzzy met a new friend named Slinky the worm. Slinky was also spicy and loved to dance and play. They had so much fun together, and Buzzy felt like he finally belonged. But soon, they saw dark clouds in the sky. A big storm was coming, and they knew they had to find a safe place to hide. Buzzy and Slinky went to Buzzy's family and warned them about the storm. At first, the other beetles didn't want to listen to the spicy beetle and his worm friend. But then, the rain started to fall, and the wind began to blow. Buzzy's family realized they needed to find a safe place too. Together, they all found a big, strong tree to hide under. The storm was very scary, but Buzzy and Slinky kept everyone calm by dancing and playing. When the storm was over, Buzzy's family thanked him and Slinky for saving them. They realized that even though Buzzy was spicy, he still belonged with them. From that day on, Buzzy's family learned to love his spicy ways. They all danced and played together, and they were very happy. Buzzy knew that he belonged with his family, and they loved him just the way he was.

Once upon a time, there was a restless little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys all day. One day, he found a big box in his room. Inside the box was a shiny trumpet. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to play the trumpet, but he did not know how. He tried to blow into it, but he only made a tiny sound. Tim did not give up. He tried again and again. Soon, he made a loud sound that made his dog jump. Tim was so happy that he jumped too. He jumped so high that he hit a paper on the wall. The paper started to tear. Tim felt sad, but his mom came and fixed it. They played the trumpet together and had a fun day.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball to each other and run after it. Sometimes they shouted "Catch!" or "Here!" when they threw the ball. One time, Ben kicked the ball too hard and it flew over the fence. They could not see where it went. They ran to the fence and looked for the ball. They saw a big road with many cars and trucks. They heard loud noises and saw bright lights. "Where is our ball?" Lily asked. "I don't know. Maybe it is on the other side of the road," Ben said. "Can we go and get it?" Lily asked. "No, we can't. The road is too dangerous. We have to wait for Mom or Dad to come and help us," Ben said. They sat on the grass and waited. They felt sad and bored. They wished they had their ball back. They looked at the road and saw a distant figure. It was their Dad. He was walking towards them with something in his hand. It was their ball. He had seen it on the sidewalk and picked it up. "Dad, Dad, you found our ball!" Ben and Lily shouted. They ran to him and hugged him. "I did. I'm sorry you lost it. Are you okay?" Dad asked. "We're okay. We waited for you. We didn't go on the road," Ben said. "That's good. You were very smart and safe. Come on, let's go home. We can play with the ball later," Dad said. Ben and Lily nodded. They were happy to have their ball and their Dad. They held his hand and walked with him. They did not look at the road anymore. They looked at the sky and the trees and the birds. They smiled and talked and laughed.
