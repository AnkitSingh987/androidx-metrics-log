Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob and the Mechanic

Little Bob loved cars. Every day he would go sit in his driveway and look up and down the street. He would watch the cars go by and imagine what it would be like to drive one. One day, Bob was extra excited to see an old, dusty truck coming down the street. He ran out in the street and waved his hands at the truck. The truck stopped and out came an old mechanic. Bob was so excited he could hardly contain himself. The mechanic said, “Hi there, son. You must love cars, eh?” Bob nodded enthusiastically. The mechanic looked pleased and said, “I’ll tell you what. I need someone to help me wrap up this old truck so I can take it to the shop. Would you like to help me?” “Yes!” Bob said, jumping up and down. The mechanic smiled and handed Bob a roll of tape. The mechanic and Bob worked together to wrap up the truck. After they finished, the mechanic stood up, smiled, and said he was proud. “Looks great,” he said. Bob knew that he was proud of their work. He felt so happy that he could help the mechanic with his truck. From then on, Bob knew he wanted to be a mechanic when he grew up.

Once upon a time, there was a big, organized room. In the room, there was a soft rug. On the rug, there were blocks. A little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue loved to play with the blocks. They wanted to build a big tower with the blocks. One day, Tim said, "Let's build the biggest tower ever!" Sue agreed and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They started to build the tower on the rug. They put one block on top of the other. The tower grew taller and taller. Suddenly, their cat, Fluffy, jumped on the rug. Fluffy knocked the tower down! Tim and Sue were sad. But then, Fluffy did something unexpected. Fluffy started to help them build the tower! Tim and Sue laughed and said, "Thank you, Fluffy!" Together, they built the biggest tower ever.

One morning, a big bird named Bob wanted to perform a fun show for his friends. He was very good at flying high in the sky. He told all his friends to come and watch him fly. They were all very excited to see Bob perform. Bob started to fly high, and his friends clapped and cheered. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Bob far away from his friends. Bob was scared, but he knew he had to be brave and find his way back. Bob flew and flew, trying to find his friends. Then, he saw a big tree. He thought, "Maybe I can see my friends from the top of the tree." He flew to the top, and there they were! Bob was so happy to see his friends again. He flew back to them, and they all laughed and played together. The unexpected wind made the day even more fun and exciting for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very creative. She loved to draw, paint, and make things with her hands. One hot day, Mia saw her friend, Tom, sitting under a tree. Tom looked very hot and tired. Mia wanted to help him. She had an idea! Mia took her big fan and went to Tom. She said, "Tom, you look hot. I can offer you my fan to help you feel better." Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, Mia! That is very kind of you." They sat under the tree, and Mia used her fan to make a cool breeze for both of them. They felt much better. The moral of the story is: always offer help to your friends when they need it.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a big box in her room. She was very happy and wanted to show it to her friend, Tom. Tom came over to play with the box. They both tried to catch the box, but it was too big and heavy. Amy said, "Let's put our toys in the box!" Tom agreed, and they began to fill the box with their toys. But then, they saw a spoiled apple in the box. "Yuck!" said Tom, "We need to take this out!" Amy didn't like the spoiled apple either, so they decided to clean the box. After cleaning the box, they continued to play and fill it with their toys. They had so much fun catching toys and putting them in the box. They played together all day and forgot about the spoiled apple. The big box became their favorite toy, and they played with it every day.
