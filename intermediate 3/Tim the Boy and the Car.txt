Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy car. He would make it zoom all around the house. He had so much fun watching it go fast. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sue, playing a violin. The music was so pretty. Tim felt envious. He wanted to play the violin too. But he did not know how to play it. Tim asked his mom if he could learn to play the violin. His mom said, "Yes, you can learn!" Tim was so happy. He learned to play the violin, and he and Sue played music together. They had fun, and Tim still loved to make his toy car zoom.

One sunny day, a little girl decided to go camping with her family. She was very excited, as she had never camped before. Her family put up a big, wide tent for her to sleep in, with bright colorful walls and many layers of soft blankets. She thought it was very attractive. That night, she laid down in the tent and let the stars above twinkle in her eyes before she fell asleep. She slept very well and the next morning, she woke up feeling refreshed and happy. With the sun peeking through the blanket- covered windows of the tent, the little girl opened her eyes and smiled. "Good morning!" said her father, poking his head through the tent opening. "Are you ready for a day of camping fun?" The little girl was very excited. "Yes!" she said. The father smiled and winked. "Let's go explore then!" And so, the little girl and her father spent the day exploring nature together, having a wonderful time.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to whistle all day long. He had a long tail and bright feathers. Tim lived in a big tree near a small house. One day, Tim saw a bowl of cereal through the window of the small house. He was very hungry. He flew to the window and looked at the cereal. The window was open, so Tim went inside to eat the cereal. As Tim ate the cereal, a little girl named Amy came into the room. She saw Tim and smiled. Amy liked Tim's long tail and happy whistle. From that day on, Tim and Amy became best friends. They played and whistled together every day.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play together. One day, Sara has a gift for Ben. She puts it in a smooth paper with stars. She is happy to see Ben. She goes to his house and knocks on the door. Ben opens the door and sees Sara. He smiles and says, "Hi, Sara. What do you have?" Sara says, "I have a gift for you, Ben. It is your birthday today. Happy birthday!" Ben says, "Thank you, Sara. You are very nice. Can I open it?" Sara says, "Yes, you can. I hope you like it." Ben opens the gift and sees a toy car. It is red and shiny. He likes it very much. He says, "Wow, Sara. This is a great gift. I love it. Can we play with it?" Sara says, "Yes, we can. Let's go to the park and race it." They go to the park and have fun with the toy car. They laugh and wave to other kids. They are very happy. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy sword that was light. He liked to play with his sword all day. Tim would run and jump with his sword, pretending to be a hero. One day, Tim saw a big cat stuck in a tree. He wanted to help the cat. Tim thought he could use his light sword to reach the cat. He tried and tried, but the sword was too short. Tim felt sad and wanted to quit. Just then, a tiny bird flew by and saw Tim. The bird wanted to help too. The bird flew up to the cat and the cat grabbed onto the bird's legs. The bird flew down with the cat, and they were safe. Tim was so happy that they all worked together. The bird had saved the day, and Tim learned that sometimes, even the smallest friends can make a big difference.
