Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Furry Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Max. Max loved to play outside all day. He liked to run, jump, and bark. Max was a happy dog. One day, Max was playing outside when he saw a beautiful sunset. The sky was red, orange, and pink. Max was very happy to see the sunset. But then, something unexpected happened. The ground began to tremble! Max was scared and did not know what to do. Max ran back to his house, but the door was closed. He barked and barked, and finally, the door opened. It was not his owner, but a big, furry cat! The cat said, "Do not worry, Max. The ground was just trembling because of a big truck passing by." Max was happy to know it was just a truck and not something scary. From that day on, Max and the big, furry cat became best friends.

One day, a boy named Tim had a tutor. The tutor came to Tim's house every day. They did regular things like read and write. Tim liked his tutor a lot. One day, the tutor said, "Today, we will do something new. We will include a fun game." Tim was very happy. They played the game and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The tutor turned into a big, friendly bear! Tim was very surprised. The bear said, "I am still your tutor, but now we can have even more fun!" Tim and his bear tutor had the best time ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. It was a beautiful day. As Lily walked, she saw an enormous tree. The tree was very tall and had many leaves. Lily wanted to meet the tree, so she walked closer. When she got to the tree, she saw a friendly face. It was Mr. Season, the tree's friend! Mr. Season told Lily that he could change the tree's leaves. In the hot sun, the leaves were green. In the cold, they turned red and yellow. Lily loved the tree and Mr. Season. They all played together until the sun went down, and Lily went home. From that day on, Lily visited the tree and Mr. Season every day, and they all became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play sports. His favorite sport was to kick a ball. One day, Tom was playing with his ball in the house. Tom's mom saw him and said, "Tom, do not play with the ball in the house. You can break things." Tom did not listen to his mom. He kept playing with the ball in the house. He kicked the ball very hard, and it broke a big vase. His mom was not happy. Tom's mom said, "Tom, I told you not to play with the ball in the house. Now, you broke the vase. I have to punish you." Tom felt poor. He said, "I am sorry, mom. I will not play with the ball in the house again." Tom's mom hugged him and said, "I forgive you, but you have to be more careful next time."

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there lived a bossy ant named Andy. He liked to tell all the other ants what to do. One day, Andy saw a big, sweet cookie on a table. He wanted to steal it for his ant friends. Andy said to his friends, "Let's take the cookie! We can eat it together!" So, the ants worked as a team to carry the cookie. They moved it inch by inch, taking it to their home. As they carried the cookie, a little girl saw them. She was sad to see the ants steal her cookie. But when she saw how hard they worked, she smiled. She said, "You can have the cookie, ants. Just promise to share it with everyone." Andy and the ants promised, and they all enjoyed the cookie together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a pond with many fish in it. Tim loved to watch the fish swim and play. "Mom, can I play with the fish?" Tim asked. His mom said, "No, Tim. Fish live in the water, and we can't go in there. We can watch them, but we can't play with them." Just then, a foolish duck jumped into the pond to play with the fish. The fish got scared and swam away. The duck felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, fish. I just wanted to play." The fish came back and said, "It's okay, duck. We can play together." So the duck, the fish, and Tim all played together at the park. And they had lots of fun.
