Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fisherman and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman. He liked to catch fish in the big blue sea. One sunny day, he went to the sea with his little red boat. While he was fishing, a big fish jumped out of the water. The fisherman was very surprised. The big fish then slapped the fisherman with its tail. The fisherman felt embarrassed because the fish was so strong. The fisherman went home and told his friends about the big fish. They all laughed and had fun. The fisherman was not embarrassed anymore. He was happy to have a funny story to share with his friends. And they all went fishing together the next day.

Sara likes pumpkins. She likes to look at them, touch them, and smell them. She likes to make faces on them with her mom and dad. She likes to put a light inside them and see them glow at night. One day, Sara goes to the farm with her mom and dad. She sees many pumpkins in a big field. She runs to them and picks one. It is big and round and orange. She hugs it and says, "This is my pumpkin!" She wants to bring it home. She tries to lift it, but it is too heavy. She asks her mom and dad to help her. They say, "OK, Sara, we will help you. But first, we have to pay for the pumpkin. And then, we have to put it in the car." Sara nods. She follows her mom and dad to the farmer. The farmer smiles and says, "That is a fine pumpkin, Sara. You have a good eye. How much do you think it is worth?" Sara thinks. She does not know how to count very well. She holds up three fingers and says, "Three!" The farmer laughs and says, "Three what? Three dollars? Three cents? Three hugs?" Sara says, "Three hugs!" The farmer says, "That is a fair deal, Sara. You can have the pumpkin for three hugs. But you have to hug me, your mom, and your dad." Sara agrees. She hugs the farmer, her mom, and her dad. They all hug her back. They say, "We love you, Sara. You are a sweet girl." They pay for the pumpkin and put it in the car. Sara sits next to it and holds its stem. She is happy. She says, "Thank you, farmer. Thank you, mom. Thank you, dad. You are the best. I can't wait to make a face on my pumpkin." The end.

Once upon a time, there was a poor crab who lived in the ocean. One day, the crab was walking around looking for food when he heard a voice. "Hello, poor Crab!" said the voice. "Hi! Who are you?" asked the crab. "I'm an octopus," replied the octopus. "Why don't you have some of my food? I have plenty to share." The crab was delighted and thanked the octopus for its generosity. As he ate, he learned an important lesson. "Giving to those in need is more important than having," said the octopus. The crab understood the lesson and knew that if he wanted to be healthy and happy, he should always be generous to others. From then on, the crab shared his food with those who needed it. The moral of the story is that it is better to give than to have.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big plane in the sky. He was very happy and excited. He wanted to show his mom the plane. But when he looked back, the plane had vanished. Tim felt anxious and sad. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mommy, I saw a big plane in the sky, but now it's gone!" His mom looked at him and smiled. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. Planes fly very fast. It must have flown away. Maybe we will see another plane soon." Tim and his mom sat outside and watched the sky. They saw birds and clouds, but no planes. After a while, Tim saw another plane. He pointed to the sky and said, "Look, Mommy! Another plane!" They both smiled and watched the plane until it vanished again. Tim was no longer anxious, and he knew that planes just fly away sometimes.
