Category: Intermediate
Title: The Hairy Monster and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a big universe, there lived a hairy monster. The monster lived in a cave and liked to hold things. He held sticks, stones, and even his own feet. One day, the hairy monster found a shiny ball. The ball was very pretty, and it made the monster happy. He decided to hold the ball all day and all night. He loved the ball so much that he never wanted to let it go. But one day, the ball started to get smaller and smaller. The hairy monster was sad and didn't know what to do. He held the ball tighter and tighter, but it kept getting smaller. In the end, the ball disappeared, and the monster was very sad. He cried and cried, but the ball never came back.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a new toy car. He loved to play with his toy car every day. One day, his toy car broke. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom said she would replace the toy car. She got him a new toy car. Tim was happy again. He played with his new toy car all the time. One night, Tim had a bad dream. In his nightmare, a big monster came and took his new toy car away. Tim woke up scared and crying. He looked for his toy car, but it was gone. Tim's mom could not find the toy car to replace it. Tim was very sad and missed his toy car.

Timmy was on a special adventure. He was walking down the street to get a treat. Timmy was feeling excited! But suddenly he ran into a bossy girl. She said, "I don't like you! Go away!" Timmy felt sad and he started to yawn. But then his big sister came and gave the bossy girl a big frown. She said, "Leave my little brother alone!" The bossy girl ran away and Timmy was happy again. He ran down the street and got his treat. It was ice cream and it tasted delicious. He smiled as he ate it. Timmy had a great day, despite a bumpy start. He was thankful for his big sister, who was always there for him. He yawned one last time, content and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Tim saw a big, red strawberry on a bush. He wanted to eat it, but it was too high for him to reach. Tim felt grumpy and sad. Tim's friend, a small bird, saw him and wanted to help. So, the bird flew up to the strawberry and tried to pull it off the bush. But the strawberry did not come off. The bird tried and tried, but it was too hard. Then, a big, strong bear came along. The bear saw the grumpy boy and the small bird trying to get the strawberry. The bear wanted to help, too. So, the bear stood up tall on his back legs and picked the strawberry off the bush. The bear gave the strawberry to Tim and the bird. Tim and the bird were so happy and not grumpy anymore. They learned that if they try and work together, they can do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle bird named Blue. Blue lived in a big tree near a pretty lake. Blue loved to sing happy songs every day. One day, Blue saw a sad boy sitting by the lake. Blue flew down and asked, "Why are you sad?" The boy looked up and said, "I lost my toy in the lake." Blue wanted to help, so he had an idea to change into a big bird. As a big bird, Blue flew over the lake and picked up the toy with his strong feet. He brought the toy back to the boy. The boy was so happy and said, "Thank you, Blue!" Blue changed back into his gentle self and sang a happy song. From that day on, Blue and the boy became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tutor named Tom. Tom was very silly. He liked to make kids laugh. He taught them how to fit shapes into the right holes. One day, Tom brought a big box to class. The box had many shapes like a circle, a square, and a triangle. The kids were very excited to play with the shapes. Tom showed them how to fit the shapes into the right holes on the box. The kids tried to fit the shapes, but they were being silly like Tom. They tried to fit the circle into the square hole and the triangle into the circle hole. Tom laughed and showed them the right way again. The kids learned and had fun. They loved their silly tutor, Tom.
