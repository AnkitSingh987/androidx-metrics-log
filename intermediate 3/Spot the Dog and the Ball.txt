Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a happy dog named Spot. Spot loved to visit his friend, a big cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a luxury house with many toys and a big yard to play in. Spot and Fluffy would play together all day, and they were the best of friends. One sunny day, as Spot went to visit Fluffy, he saw a new, original toy in the yard. It was a big, red ball with a long string. Spot was very excited and wanted to play with it. He grabbed the ball with his mouth and started to run. But something unexpected happened! As Spot ran, the string on the ball got longer and longer. Suddenly, Fluffy jumped out from behind a bush and caught the other end of the string. They both laughed and played with their new toy. Spot and Fluffy learned that sharing their toys made their friendship even stronger, and they had even more fun together. And that was the best day ever!

Once upon a time, there was a little dinosaur named Dino. Dino had a favorite toy. It was a fake dinosaur. He took it everywhere he went. One day, Dino and his friend, Bunny, were playing in the park. Bunny wanted to play with the fake dinosaur too. But Dino did not want to share. They both got mad at each other. Bunny said, "Please forgive me, Dino. I will give your toy back." Dino thought for a moment and said, "I forgive you, Bunny. We can both play with the fake dinosaur." They both smiled and played happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved to play with boards. One day, he went to the park with his dad and set up his favorite board on a bench. He was having so much fun on it, that he didn't hear his dad shouting at him to come home. The dad came up to the boy, grabbed the board and threw it on the ground. The boy started crying. "Why did you do that, dad?" he said through his tears. His dad said, "You were being stupid. I asked you to come home and you ignored me!" The boy replied, "I'm sorry, dad, I didn't mean to be stupid. Please forgive me." His dad hugged him and said, "It's okay. I forgive you." Then he picked up the board and gave it back to the boy. The little boy and his dad went back home and started playing with the board together. They laughed and had fun until it was time for them to go to sleep. The boy thanked his dad for forgiving him and hugged his dad goodnight.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to walk in the park with her dog, Max. One sunny day, they went for a walk and saw a big pile of junk. Sue was curious and wanted to see what was in the junk. As they got closer, they heard a terrible sound. Max started to bark, and Sue was scared. Suddenly, a small, dirty cat jumped out of the junk. The cat looked sad and said, "Meow, I am so hungry." Sue felt bad for the cat and wanted to help. She said, "Don't be sad, little cat. I will find food for you." Sue and Max looked around and found some yummy food for the cat. The cat was very happy and thanked Sue and Max. From that day on, Sue, Max, and the cat became best friends. They always went to the park together and had lots of fun. And they never saw the terrible junk pile again.

Once upon a time, there was a mechanic. He liked to fix things. He had a big shop where he worked. It was dark in the shop, but he had a bright light to see. One day, he found a broken toy car. It was very old and dirty. The mechanic wanted to make it new again. He wanted to restore it. So, he started to work on the toy car. He cleaned the car and fixed the wheels. He painted it a bright color. The toy car looked new and shiny. The mechanic was happy with his work. He took the car out of the dark shop and gave it to a little child. The child was very happy and played with the car all day.
