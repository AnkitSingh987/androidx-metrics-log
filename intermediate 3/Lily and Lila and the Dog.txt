Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Lila and the Dog

Lily and Lila are twins. They look the same and like the same things. They have a lot of dresses and shoes and hats. They like to wear them and play dress-up. One day, they find a big box in the attic. It has many old and funny clothes. They want to try them on and have fun. They take out a long coat, a big hat, a pair of glasses and a fake beard. They think they look very silly. They decide to wear the old clothes and go downstairs. They want to surprise their mom and dad and make them laugh. They walk slowly and pretend to be old and grumpy. They say hello in a deep voice. But mom and dad are not there. They are outside in the garden. Lily and Lila do not see them. They only see a big dog. The dog is not their dog. It is a stranger dog. It is big and loud and scary. The dog sees Lily and Lila. It does not like their old clothes. It thinks they are foolish. It barks and growls and runs towards them. Lily and Lila are afraid. They scream and drop their clothes. They run back to the attic. They learn a lesson. They should not wear old and funny clothes outside. They should only wear them inside and with mom and dad. They hug each other and say sorry. They are happy to be twins. They are happy to be themselves.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to read books. He had a big bookcase in his room with many books. One day, Tim's mom told him, "Do not read the big red book at the top of the bookcase. It is not for you." But Tim felt curious and guilty. When his mom was not looking, he climbed up the bookcase and got the big red book. He opened it and started to read. As he read, something unexpected happened. The words in the book started to move and dance on the page! Tim was so surprised and scared. He closed the book and put it back on the bookcase. He promised never to read the big red book again. From that day on, Tim only read the books his mom said were okay. And sometimes, he would look at the big red book and remember the dancing words.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow ball. The yellow ball loved to bounce all day long. It would bounce up and down, and side to side. The yellow ball had a lot of fun bouncing all around. One day, the yellow ball saw a lamp. The lamp was shiny and pretty. The yellow ball wanted to bounce near the lamp. So, it started to bounce closer and closer to the lamp. The yellow ball bounced too close to the lamp. It hit the lamp and the lamp fell down. The lamp broke into many pieces. The yellow ball was very sad. It could not bounce near the pretty lamp anymore.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man. He loved to play with his big, round basketball. Every day, he went outside to bounce and throw it. One sunny day, while he was playing, a new friend appeared. It was a little dog! The dog loved to chase the basketball too. They had so much fun playing together. After playing, the elderly man and the little dog were tired. They sat under a tree and shared a snack. From that day on, they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Tim. Tim was good at giving massages. He liked to help his friends feel better. One day, Tim saw a sad dog named Max. Max had a big frown on his face. Tim wanted to provide a massage to make Max feel better. Tim walked up to Max and said, "Hi Max, can I give you a massage?" Max looked at Tim and said, "No, I don't want a massage. I am sad because I lost my toy." Tim felt bad for Max and wanted to help him find his toy. Tim and Max looked all over for the toy. They looked under the trees and behind the rocks. At last, they found the toy near a big bush. Max was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Tim!" Then, Max let Tim give him a massage. They became good friends and played together every day.
