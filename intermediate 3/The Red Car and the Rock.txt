Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Car and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little red car who loved to go fast. Every day, the car would race down the street and all the other cars would say, "Wow, you are so fast!" The little red car was very proud of how fast it could go. One day, the little red car heard a voice. The voice said, "Be careful, little red car. If you always go too fast, you may suffer." The little red car didn't listen to the voice and kept going fast, thinking nothing bad would happen. But one day, the little red car went too fast and hit a big rock. The car suffered and broke down. The little red car remembered the voice and wished it had been more careful. From that day on, the little red car learned to be careful and not always go too fast.

Sara liked to help her mom in the garden. She liked to dig and water and pull out weeds. She also liked to pick the carrots that grew under the ground. They were orange and crunchy and sweet. One day, Sara saw a small white rabbit hopping near the fence. She was curious and wanted to make friends with it. She ran to the garden and picked a big carrot. She broke it in half and held one piece in her hand. She walked slowly to the rabbit and said, "Hello, bunny. Do you want a carrot?" The rabbit looked at Sara and twitched its nose. It smelled the carrot and came closer. It took a bite and nibbled happily. Sara smiled and stroked its soft fur. She said, "You are very cute and delicate. I like you. What is your name?" The rabbit did not answer, but it licked Sara's hand and cuddled with her. Sara felt happy and warm. She said, "I will call you Fluffy. Do you want to be my friend?" The rabbit nodded and snuggled with Sara. Sara said, "I recommend that you come back tomorrow. I will bring you more carrots and we can play together. OK?" The rabbit agreed and hopped away. Sara waved goodbye and ran to her mom. She told her about Fluffy and how they shared a carrot. Her mom hugged her and said, "That sounds wonderful, Sara. You are very kind and generous. I am proud of you."

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. He lived in a deep, blue sea. Fin loved to move fast and play with his friends. One day, Fin found a big book with many pages. He wanted to read it, but the pages were hard to move. Fin asked his friend, the crab, to help him. Together, they moved the pages and read the fun stories inside. As they moved the pages, Fin and the crab learned about new places and animals. They had a great time reading and playing together in the deep, blue sea. And from that day on, they always moved the pages to read new stories and share them with their friends.

One day, a raven and a cat saw a perfect, big cookie. They both wanted the cookie very much. The raven said, "I saw the cookie first, so it is mine." The cat said, "No, I saw it first, so I should have it." The raven and the cat started to fight. They pushed and yelled at each other. They both wanted the cookie so much that they did not see a little girl coming. The little girl saw the raven and the cat fighting. She said, "Stop fighting! You can both have the cookie." The raven and the cat stopped fighting. They looked at the little girl and then at the cookie. They decided to share the cookie. The raven and the cat became friends and ate the perfect cookie together. They learned that sharing is better than fighting.

One day, a charming little bird flew to a tree. The bird saw a prune on the tree. The bird wanted to eat the prune, but it was too high. The charming bird tried to fly up, but it was a struggle. The charming bird did not give up. It tried again and again, but it still could not reach the prune. The bird was sad, but it did not stop trying. It wanted the prune very much. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and shook the tree. The prune fell down to the ground. The charming bird was happy and ate the prune. The bird learned that even when it is hard, do not give up.
