Category: Intermediate
Title: The Duck and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little duck. The duck was very patient. One day, it started to rain. The duck saw a small bird who was getting wet. The duck wanted to help the bird. The duck said, "Come here, little bird. I will cover you with my wing." The bird came to the duck, and the duck covered the bird with its wing. They both stayed dry under the duck's wing. After the rain stopped, the bird said, "Thank you, duck, for being so patient and kind." They became good friends. The moral of the story is to always be patient and kind, and help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter named Tom. Tom liked to wake up early and watch the sun rise. One day, while he was out, he saw a big, red bird. Tom wanted to catch the bird, but it was too fast for him. He felt embarrassed that he could not catch it. Tom asked his friend, Sam, for help. "Sam, can you help me catch the big, red bird?" Sam said, "Yes, I will help you." They made a plan to catch the bird together. They would wake up early, when the sun started to rise, and find the bird. The next day, Tom and Sam woke up early and went to find the big, red bird. They saw it near a tree. They tried to catch it, but the bird was too fast again. Tom and Sam felt embarrassed, but they did not give up. They made a new plan. They would make a trap for the bird. They put some food in the trap and waited for the bird to come. When the bird came to eat the food, they caught it! Tom and Sam were very happy. They were not embarrassed anymore. They had caught the big, red bird together. They learned that working together can help solve problems. And from that day, they became the best of friends.

One day, a thoughtful cat named Tom went for a walk. He found a big sink in the park. Tom was very curious and wanted to see what was inside the sink. Tom jumped into the sink and saw a little fish. The fish looked sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad, little fish?" The fish said, "I want to settle in a big pond, but I don't know how to get there." Tom thought for a moment and said, "I can help you!" He carefully picked up the fish with his paw and carried him to a big pond nearby. The fish was very happy and thanked Tom. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a magic bottle that she found in her room. The bottle was very special. It could make things disappear when she said the magic word, "poof!" One day, Lily was playing in her room. She saw an uncomfortable chair that she did not like. It was old and not fun to sit on. She decided to use her magic bottle to make the chair disappear. She pointed the bottle at the chair and said, "poof!" The chair disappeared, and Lily was very happy. Lily loved her magic bottle. She used it to make all the uncomfortable things in her house disappear. Her room became the most fun place to play. Everyone wanted to play in Lily's room because it was so nice. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, unusual tree. This tree had a ladder. The ladder was very long and went all the way up to the top of the tree. Many kids liked to climb the ladder and play in the tree. One day, a mean boy came to the tree. He wanted the ladder for himself. So, he decided to steal it. He took the ladder away from the tree and hid it in his home. The other kids were very sad. They wanted to play in the tree, but they could not climb it without the ladder. The kids made a plan to get the ladder back. They went to the mean boy's home and tried to take the ladder. But, the mean boy saw them and got very angry. He chased them away and broke the ladder into pieces. Now, no one could play in the tree again. The kids were very sad, and the tree was lonely without them.
