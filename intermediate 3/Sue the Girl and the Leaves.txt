Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Leaves

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to draw and write. One day, Sue made a pretty picture for her friend Tim who lived far away. She wanted to send it to him. Sue asked her mom, "Can I send my picture to Tim?" Her mom said, "Yes, we need a stamp to send it." They went to the store and bought a stamp. Sue put the stamp on her picture and put it in the mailbox. The next day, it was very dry outside. The sun was shining and the wind blew the leaves. Sue's picture flew to Tim's house. Tim was happy to see the picture from Sue. He smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue!"

One day, a little girl named Lily had a secret. She wanted to tell her best friend, Tom. Lily trusted Tom because he was always nice and kind. She went to Tom and whispered her secret in his ear. Tom smiled and said he would keep her secret safe. In the park, there was a nosy squirrel. The squirrel heard Lily and Tom talking about the secret. The squirrel wanted to know the secret too. So, he followed Lily and Tom everywhere they went. But Lily and Tom were careful and never talked about the secret when the nosy squirrel was around. One day, the nosy squirrel tried to trick Tom into telling the secret. But Tom was smart and knew not to trust the nosy squirrel. Tom told Lily about the squirrel's trick, and they laughed together. Lily was happy that she could trust Tom to keep her secret safe. And the nosy squirrel never found out the secret.

---

One day, a busy bee named Bobby was flying around. He saw a razor on the ground and wanted to help move it. The razor was sharp, but Bobby was brave. He knew he had to be careful. Bobby asked his friend, a strong ant named Andy, "Please help me move the razor." Andy said, "Okay, let's work together." They picked up the razor with their little legs and carried it far away from their friends. When they were done, all their friends were happy and safe. Bobby and Andy were proud of their hard work. They knew that even small friends can do big things when they help each other.

Sara likes to help her mom in the garden. She likes to water the flowers and dig in the dirt. But she does not like the prunes. Prunes are weird fruits that grow on a big tree. They are wrinkly and brown and sour. One day, mom tells Sara to pick some prunes from the tree. Sara does not want to, but she nods and says okay. She climbs on a ladder and reaches for the prunes. But they are too high and too hard to pull. She tries and tries, but she cannot get any. Then she sees a bird on the tree. The bird is small and blue and has a long beak. It looks at Sara and makes a funny noise. Sara thinks the bird is cute and wants to be friends. She says hello and smiles. The bird nods and smiles back. Then it pecks a prune and tosses it to Sara. Sara catches the prune and says thank you. The bird nods and pecks another prune and tosses it to Sara. Sara catches the prune and says thank you again. The bird nods and pecks more prunes and tosses them to Sara. Sara catches all the prunes and puts them in a basket. She says thank you one more time. The bird nods and flies away. Sara is happy and proud. She climbs down the ladder and brings the basket to mom. Mom is surprised and happy too. She says Sara did a great job and gives her a hug. She says they can make some prune jam and some prune cake. Sara thinks that sounds yummy. She says maybe prunes are not so weird after all. She says maybe the bird likes prunes too. Mom nods and says maybe. They go inside and make some prune jam and some prune cake. They share some with the bird. The bird nods and says yum. They all smile and nod. They are all friends.
