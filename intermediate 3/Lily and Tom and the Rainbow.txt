Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Rainbow

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. There is a big bridge in the park. The bridge goes over a river. Lily and Tom like to run on the bridge and look at the water. One day, Lily and Tom see a rainbow in the sky. The rainbow is very pretty. It has many colors. Lily and Tom wonder what is at the end of the rainbow. They think maybe there is a pot of gold or a fairy or a unicorn. Lily and Tom decide to follow the rainbow. They run on the bridge and try to get closer to the rainbow. But the rainbow is very far away. They run and run, but the rainbow does not get closer. They start to feel tired and sad. Then, they see a man on the bridge. The man is nice. He has a big smile and a hat. He says hello to Lily and Tom. He asks them what they are doing. Lily and Tom tell him they are looking for the end of the rainbow. The man laughs. He says the end of the rainbow is not a real place. He says the rainbow is just a trick of the light and the water. He says there is no pot of gold or fairy or unicorn at the end of the rainbow. Lily and Tom feel confused. They do not understand. They want to believe in the rainbow. They want to find something magical. They ask the man if he is sure. The man nods. He says he is sure. He says he is a scientist. He says he knows a lot about rainbows and how they work. He says he can show them a book with pictures and words that explain rainbows. Lily and Tom look at each other. They do not know what to do. They like the man. He is nice. He is a scientist. He has a book. But they also like the rainbow. It is pretty. It is a mystery. It makes them happy. Lily and Tom think for a moment. Then, they smile. They say thank you to the man. They say they will look at his book later. But they say they still want to believe in the rainbow. They say they still want to play and imagine and dream. The man smiles back. He says he understands. He says he likes to play and imagine and dream too. He says he believes in the rainbow in his own way. He says he is happy to see Lily and Tom having fun. Lily and Tom hug the man. They say goodbye to him. They run on the bridge again. They look at the rainbow again. They feel happy again. They believe in the rainbow. They believe in themselves. They believe in their friendship.

One day, a pretty bird lay an egg in a big tree. The tree was near a nice house. In the house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue saw the bird in the tree and wanted to watch the egg. Sue went to her mom and said, "Mom, can I watch the bird and the egg?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful and do not touch the egg." Sue sat under the tree and watched the bird and the egg. She liked the pretty bird. She thought it was very attractive. She watched the egg every day. One day, the egg cracked and a cute baby bird came out. Sue was very happy to see the baby bird.

Once upon a time there was a lazy bear. He loved to sleep all day, and he never liked to do any work. One day, the bear entered a dark and scary forest. The forest was full of tall and scary trees with thick, gnarly lumber. He was so scared he didn't know where to go. Suddenly, he heard someone talking in the distance. It was a bee who said, "Don't be scared, bear. Follow this path and you'll find what you need." The bear followed the bee's instruction and, soon enough, he found an abandoned lumber mill. He was so excited! He entered the mill and found it full of cut and chopped lumber. He was so happy he danced around the mill! He took a few pieces of lumber and went home, thinking of all the new things he could do with the lumber he had found. The end.
