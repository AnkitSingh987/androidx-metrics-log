Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Puzzle

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved very much. It was a fragile car, and he had to be careful when he played with it. One day, Tim's mom told him that they were going to the store to buy a gift for his friend's birthday. At the store, Tim saw a big puzzle. It looked fun to complete. He told his mom, "I want to get this for my friend!" His mom said, "Great idea, Tim! Your friend will love it." When they got home, Tim wanted to play with his fragile car before wrapping the gift. But he forgot to put the puzzle away. While playing, his car zoomed and hit the puzzle box. The box opened, and all the pieces fell out. Tim was sad and worried. He knew he had to complete the puzzle before giving it to his friend. So, Tim and his mom worked together to finish the puzzle. In the end, Tim learned to be more careful and put things away. His friend loved the gift, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little brown rabbit. The rabbit was very fast and liked to run. One day, he found a club where many animals played together. The rabbit thought it was fun and wanted to join the club. The rabbit went to the club and met a big bear. The bear said, "Hello, little rabbit! Do you want to play with us?" The rabbit nodded and said, "Yes! I want to play with you and have fun!" So, the rabbit joined the club and played with the bear and other animals. One day, the rabbit and his friends played a game called "Escape." The rabbit was very good at this game because he could run very fast. When his friends tried to catch him, he always escaped. The rabbit was very happy and loved playing with his friends at the club.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob liked to hop and play in the sunny field. He had a lot of friends, but his best friend was a rare bird named Tim. Tim was blue and yellow. One day, Bob and Tim were playing when they saw a big carrot. They both wanted the carrot, but there was only one. Bob and Tim started to frown because they didn't want to share. Then, a wise old turtle came by and saw them frown. He told them to break the carrot in half, so they could both have some. Bob and Tim did what the turtle said, and they were happy again. They ate the carrot and played all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside with her dog, Buddy. They would run and jump in the big green yard. One day, they saw a big white cloud coming from the west. Mia and Buddy were very excited, so they ran to the cloud. The cloud was very cold and made everything freeze. Mia and Buddy were not scared. They knew they could find a way to make things warm again. Mia looked for all the available things to make a big fire. She found wood and dry leaves. Mia and Buddy made a big fire, and the ice started to melt. The sun came out, and everything was warm again. Mia and Buddy were happy, and they played in the yard all day.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom did not like to play or run. He just liked to sleep all day. One day, Tom saw a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was very fast and loved to play. Jerry wanted to be friends with Tom. He offered Tom a game of tag. But Tom was too lazy to play. Jerry tried to teach Tom a spell to make him fast, but Tom did not want to learn. Jerry was sad because he wanted a friend to play with. One day, a big dog came to their home. The dog wanted to catch both Tom and Jerry. Jerry used the spell to run very fast and got away. But Tom was too lazy to run. The big dog caught Tom and took him away. Now, Jerry was all alone and had no friend to play with.
