Category: Intermediate
Title: The Knight and the Giant

Once upon a time, there was a brave knight who had a spear. He held the spear high and charged into battle. He was so courageous that no one else was willing to stand beside him. Suddenly, a giant appeared. The knight was very frightened. He tried to use his spear to fight against the giant, but the giant was too powerful. The giant roared and the ground shook. The knight regretted his decision and ran away. "Stop knight!" The giant yelled. "Why did you come here? I could have easily crushed you!" The knight stopped and looked up. He was too afraid to answer. The giant sighed. He put the spear back in the knight's hand and said, "Be brave, but not reckless. This spear is too powerful for a foolish heart to handle." The knight nodded, thankful for the giant's wisdom. He never forgot the lesson. From then on, he fought every battle without regret.

One day, a boy named Tim found a basketball. He was very happy. He wanted to play with it. Tim took the basketball to the park. There were many kids at the park. A big dog saw the basketball. The dog was alert. It wanted to play too. The dog ran fast to Tim. Tim was scared, but he did not run away. He knew what to do. Tim started to stir the basketball on the ground. The dog saw this and wanted to join. The dog and Tim played together. They had a lot of fun. Tim was not scared anymore.

One day, a little mosquito named Mo was flying around. Mo was a modest mosquito. He did not like to make a lot of noise or bother anyone. Mo wanted to find a friend to play with. Mo saw a big frog named Fred sitting by the water. Mo flew up to Fred and said, "Hi, Fred! Can I play with you?" Fred looked at Mo and said, "Okay, Mo. I will let you play with me. Let's play catch with this ball." Mo was very happy. Mo and Fred played catch with the ball. They laughed and had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Mo said, "Thank you, Fred, for letting me play with you. You are a good friend." Fred smiled and said, "You are a good friend too, Mo. Let's play again tomorrow." Mo flew away, happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day and play with junk. He had a big box of junk in his room. Tom's favorite thing to do was poke the junk with his paw. One sunny day, Tom was poking his junk when he found a pretty ball. The ball was shiny and made a funny noise when Tom poked it. Tom was very happy and played with the ball all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball started to grow bigger and bigger! Tom was scared, but he poked the ball one more time. The big ball turned into a friendly dog named Max. Tom was surprised but happy. Now Tom and Max play together every day, and Tom is not lazy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little boy named Tim. He loved to ask questions and learn new things. One day, he found a shiny coin on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "What is this?" he asked. "That is money," she said. "We use it to buy things." Tim thought about what he could buy with his money. He decided to go to the store with his mom. At the store, he saw a toy he really wanted. It was a toy car that made fun noises. He asked his mom if he could buy it with his money. She looked at the price and said, "I believe you have enough money for that toy car." Tim was so happy! He gave the money to the store man and took the toy car home. He played with it all day and all night. He learned that if he saved his money, he could buy more fun things. And he always remembered to be curious and ask questions, because that's how he found his first money.
