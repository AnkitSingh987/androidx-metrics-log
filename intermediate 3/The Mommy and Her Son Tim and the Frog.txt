Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mommy and Her Son Tim and the Frog

Once upon a time, in a unique little house, there lived a mommy and her son Tim. Every day, they would play, eat, and bathe together. Tim loved bath time and getting a big hug from his mommy after. One day, while Tim was bathing, he saw a small, friendly frog. The frog said, "Hi Tim! Can I have a hug?" Tim was surprised but happy. He gave the frog a big hug. After the hug, the frog turned into a beautiful fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you, Tim! Your hug broke the spell on me. I'm free now!" Tim and his mommy were so happy. They played with the fairy all day and became best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big red ball in the park. Tim was very happy to play with it. He would throw it high in the air and catch it. He would also kick it and watch it roll. Tim liked to take turns playing with the ball. On his way home, Tim had to cross a bridge. While he was on the bridge, he thought it would be fun to drop the ball and watch it bounce. But when he dropped the ball, it did not bounce. Instead, the ball rolled off the bridge and fell into the water below. Tim was very sad. He wished he had not dropped the ball. He was grateful for the fun he had with it, but now the ball was gone. Tim went home with a heavy heart, knowing he had lost his new friend, the big red ball.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his dad. They had a big net with them. They wanted to catch some bugs. Tim was very excited. He had never caught bugs before. As they walked, Tim saw a pretty butterfly. He wanted to catch it. "Daddy, can I catch the butterfly?" he asked. His dad said, "Yes, but be gentle. It is very delicate." Tim tried to catch the butterfly in the net. He prayed that the butterfly would be available to catch. Just then, the butterfly flew into the net. Tim was so happy! He carefully picked up the butterfly and looked at it. "Wow, it's so beautiful!" he said. His dad smiled and said, "Yes, it is. Now, let's let it go." Tim opened the net and the butterfly flew away. He was happy to have caught it, but even happier to let it go. Tim and his dad continued their bug-catching adventure, and they had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a small green forest, there lived a cute little snake named Sam. Sam liked to play with his friends, the birds, the bugs, and the frogs. They would play hide and seek, and Sam would always win because he was very good at hiding. One day, Sam saw a big red ball in the grass. He wanted to play with it, but it belonged to his friend, the bunny. So, Sam went to the bunny's house and asked, "Can I borrow your big red ball, please?" The bunny said, "Of course, Sam! We can play together." Sam and the bunny played with the big red ball all day long. They laughed and had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Sam said, "Thank you for letting me borrow your ball, bunny." The bunny smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sam. We can play again tomorrow." And from that day on, Sam and the bunny became best friends. They shared their toys and played together every day in the small green forest. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a bossy ant named Tim wanted to find a big spoon. He thought it would make him feel strong. He told his ant friends to march with him to find the spoon. As they marched, they saw a big hill. Tim said, "The spoon must be on the other side!" They marched up the hill, but they were very tired. Tim was still bossy and told them to keep going. When they got to the top, they saw a big spoon. But they were so tired, they could not pick it up. Tim's friends were sad and did not want to play with him anymore. Tim was sad too, because he learned being bossy was not good.
