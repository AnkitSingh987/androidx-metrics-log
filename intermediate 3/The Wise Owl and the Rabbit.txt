Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wise Owl and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, in a small cabin, there lived a wise old owl. The owl knew many things and liked to help his friends. One day, a little lost rabbit came to the cabin. The rabbit was sad and scared. The wise old owl saw the rabbit and wanted to help. He asked the rabbit, "Why are you sad?" The rabbit said, "I am lost and I can't find my way home." The owl thought for a moment and then had an idea. The owl told the rabbit, "I will help you find your way home." He flew up high and looked around. He saw the rabbit's home far away. The owl then pointed with his wing and said, "Go that way, little rabbit. That is where your home is." The rabbit was happy and thanked the wise old owl. The rabbit hopped away, following the way the owl pointed, and soon found his way back home.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the park. She saw a big tree with many colorful leaves. Sally wanted to choose the best leaf to take home. Sally looked at the leaves and found a terrible leaf. It was brown and dry. She said, "No, I don't want this leaf." Then she saw a pretty leaf that was green and yellow. She used her finger to touch it and said, "This leaf is nice!" Sally took the pretty leaf home and showed her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Sally! You chose a beautiful leaf!" Sally was happy and put the leaf on the table to show everyone.

Once upon a time there was a delivery man. He carried many boxes of incredible items. One sunny morning he made a delivery to the beach. When he arrived the two palm trees were swaying in the breeze. He stacked the boxes at their feet. The delivery man smiled. “Where do these go?” he asked. A voice came back. “Just put them underneath the palms,” it said. The delivery man was stunned. He looked around but could not see who had spoken. He shrugged and did as he was told. Suddenly a tiny mermaid emerged from the waves. “Thanks for the delivery,” she said. The delivery man was speechless. He knew that the items he had delivered were incredible. He had been chosen to deliver them to the mermaid - a very special honour indeed. “You’re welcome,” he finally managed to say. He smiled, collected his things and made his way back to the delivery centre. What an incredible day it had been!

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play together in the park, and share their toys and snacks. One day, they decided to go on an adventure in the woods behind their houses. They took a flashlight, a map, and a backpack with some water and cookies. They walked and walked, and saw many things. They saw birds and bugs and flowers and trees. They gazed at the sky and the clouds and the sun. They felt happy and excited. They followed the map to a big rock, where they found a cave. They wanted to see what was inside. "Let's go in, Lily!" Ben said. "Maybe we'll find treasure or a secret tunnel!" "OK, Ben, but be careful!" Lily said. "It's dark and scary in there!" They turned on the flashlight and entered the cave. They saw rocks and dirt and shadows. They heard noises and echoes and drips. They felt cold and nervous. They walked deeper and deeper, until they reached a dead end. "Oh no, Lily!" Ben said. "We're lost! We can't find the way out!" "Don't worry, Ben!" Lily said. "We have the flashlight and the map! We'll find the way out!" They tried to retrace their steps, but they got more confused. They turned left and right and left and right, but they couldn't see the entrance. They started to panic. They felt sad and scared. They hugged each other and cried. "Help! Help!" they shouted. "Is anyone there? Can anyone hear us?" They shouted and shouted, but no one answered. They felt hopeless and alone. They turned off the flashlight to save the battery. They sat on the floor and waited. Suddenly, they saw a light. A bright light. A familiar light. It was their flashlight! Someone had found it and turned it on! "Lily! Ben!" they heard a voice. A familiar voice. It was their mom! "Mom!" they screamed. "We're here! We're here!" They ran towards the light and the voice. They saw their mom's face. A happy face. A relieved face. She hugged them and kissed them and said: "I'm so glad I found you! I was so worried! How did you get in here?" "We were on an adventure, mom!" Ben said. "We wanted to see the cave, but we got lost!" "We're sorry, mom!" Lily said. "We were scared and sad!" "It's OK, my loves!" mom said. "I'm not mad. I'm just happy you're safe! Come on, let's go home!" They followed mom out of the cave. They saw the sun and the sky and the trees. They felt warm and calm and grateful. They smiled and laughed and said: "Thank you, mom! You're the best!" They held mom's hand and walked home. They learned a lesson. They never went to the cave again. They still played together in the park, and shared their toys and snacks. They were still best friends. They were happy.
