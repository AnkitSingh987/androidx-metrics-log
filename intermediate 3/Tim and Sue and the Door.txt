Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Sue and the Door

Tim and Sue were twins who liked to wander around the big house. They wanted to see everything and touch everything. They were very nosy. One day, there was a big storm outside. The sky was dark and loud. Tim and Sue heard their mom say, "Stay inside, kids. It's not safe to go out. There is lightning and thunder." But Tim and Sue did not listen. They wanted to see the lightning. They sneaked out of their room and went to the back door. They opened it and ran outside. They saw a big flash of light in the sky. It was bright and scary. They heard a loud boom. It was loud and scary. They felt a shock. It was painful and scary. They fell to the ground and did not move. They did not see or hear anything anymore. They were gone. Their mom came out and saw them. She screamed and cried. She was sad and angry. She wished they had listened to her. She wished they had stayed inside. She wished they were still alive.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a creek. A little girl named Lily liked to gather rocks near the creek. One day, Lily saw a big, tough frog sitting by the water. The frog looked sad. Lily asked the frog, "Why are you sad?" The frog said, "I am too tough, and no one wants to be my friend." Lily felt bad for the frog and wanted to help. Lily had an idea. She told the frog, "Let's gather rocks together and make a nice place for everyone to sit by the creek." They worked together and made a beautiful spot. Soon, other kids came to play and sit by the creek. They saw how kind the tough frog was and wanted to be his friend too. The frog was happy, and so was Lily. The moral of the story is that even if you look tough, being kind and helpful can make you many friends.

Once upon a time, there was a hungry boy named Tim. He loved to eat, and his favorite food was bread with jam. One day, Tim found a new book, called a novel, in his room. The novel was about a magic jam that could talk. In the story, Tim met the magic jam, and it said, "Hello, Tim! I can spread myself on your bread. Would you like that?" Tim was very happy and said, "Yes, please!" The magic jam jumped onto Tim's bread and spread all over it. The bread with the magic jam tasted so good, Tim could not stop eating. Tim shared his magic jam with his friends, and they all loved it. They were all happy and full. The magic jam made Tim and his friends smile and laugh. Tim knew that the magic jam was special and that he should share it with everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to use her modern red toy car. She would play with it all day long. One day, Sue's mom told her that they were going to the store to buy a big, round melon. Sue was very happy and wanted to take her car with her. At the store, Sue saw the big melon and got very excited. She thought about how she could use her car to carry the melon home. Sue's mom smiled and said it was a great idea. They put the melon on the car and started to go home. As they walked, the melon rolled off the car and onto the ground. Sue was sad, but her mom said they could try again. This time, they used a big bag to hold the melon on the car. It worked! Sue and her mom took the melon home and had a fun day eating it together.

One day, a baby was in her white bed. She started to cry very loud. Her mom and dad came to see what was wrong. They tried to make her stop crying, but they did not know how. Then, a big white bird came to the window. It heard the baby cry and wanted to help. The bird flew in and gave the baby a soft hug. The baby stopped crying and looked at the bird. The mom and dad were very surprised. The big white bird stayed with the baby. They became good friends. The baby did not cry anymore. The mom and dad were very happy. They all lived together and had fun every day.
