Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to eat delicious food. One day, she saw her mom in the kitchen making a cake. Lucy's mom had a big frown on her face. She was trying to measure the sugar to put in the cake. Lucy asked her mom, "Why are you frowning?" Her mom said, "I can't find the right measure for the sugar. The cake won't be delicious without it." Lucy wanted to help her mom. She looked around the kitchen and found a small cup. "Here, Mom! Use this!" she said. Her mom smiled and used the cup to measure the sugar. They mixed the cake together and put it in the oven. When the cake was done, Lucy and her mom ate a big piece. It was so delicious! Lucy's mom was very happy and proud of her. From that day on, they always baked together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful garden, there lived a little onion. The onion was very sad because he wanted to play with the other plants, but he could not move. One day, a big and strong ant came by and saw the sad onion. "Hello, onion! Why are you so sad?" asked the ant. "I want to play, but I cannot move," the onion said with a sigh. The ant thought for a moment and had an idea. "I can help you!" said the ant. "I will manage to carry you to the other plants so you can play with them." The onion was very happy. The ant managed to carry the onion to the other plants, and they all played together. From that day on, the onion was not sad anymore, and he had many friends in the beautiful garden.

One day, a little boy named Tim was walking with his mom. They were going to the park to play. Tim was very happy. He saw a big dog and it reminded him of his dog at home. The dog was nice and wanted to play with Tim. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. Tim was frightened. He did not know what the noise was. His mom told him not to worry. She said it was just a car. But then, they saw a police car coming down the street. The police car stopped near them. The police man got out of the car. Tim thought he was in trouble. But the police man smiled and gave Tim a toy police car. The police man said, "I saw you were frightened. I just wanted to make you feel better." Tim was so happy and thanked the police man. They all went to the park together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, in a big, green forest, there lived a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to play with his friends and find yummy nuts to eat. Every day, he would run up and down the trees, looking for the best nuts he could find. One day, Sam found a very special nut. It was shiny and big. Sam was so happy! He decided to take the nut home and show it to his mommy. As he carried the nut, he noticed that it started to increase in size. It became bigger and bigger, and Sam could hardly carry it. When Sam showed the nut to his mommy, she was amazed. She said, "Sam, this nut is so big and yummy! Let's share it with our friends." So, Sam and his mommy invited all their friends to come and eat the big nut. Everyone had a great time, and they all agreed that it was the yummiest nut they had ever tasted.

One day, Tom and Sue went to play. They saw a big, thick tree with a switch on it. Tom said, "What does the switch do?" Sue said, "I don't know. Let's try it!" Tom pushed the switch. Suddenly, the tree started to talk! The tree said, "Hello, I am Mr. Tree. Thank you for turning me on!" Tom and Sue were very surprised. They did not know that trees could talk. Mr. Tree told them a secret. He said, "I can make yummy treats!" He shook his branches, and treats fell down. Tom and Sue were so happy. They ate the treats and played with Mr. Tree all day.
