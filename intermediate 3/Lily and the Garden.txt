Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Garden

Lily liked to help her mom in the garden. She watered the plants and pulled out the weeds. She saw a big white flower in the ground. She asked her mom, "What is this?" Her mom smiled and said, "That is a cauliflower. It is a vegetable that we can eat. It grows from a seed and it increases in size as it gets more sun and water." Lily was curious. She wanted to taste the cauliflower. She asked her mom, "Can we pick it and cook it?" Her mom nodded and said, "Yes, we can. But first, we have to share some with our neighbor, Mr. Lee. He is very kind and he gave us some tomatoes last week. He likes cauliflower too." Lily agreed. She thought sharing was nice. She helped her mom cut the cauliflower and put it in a basket. They walked to Mr. Lee's house and knocked on the door. Mr. Lee opened the door and smiled when he saw them. "Hello, Lily and mom. What a lovely surprise. What do you have in the basket?" he asked. Lily said, "We have a cauliflower from our garden. We want to give you some because you are kind and you gave us tomatoes." Mr. Lee thanked them and said, "That is very generous of you. I love cauliflower. It is one of my favorite vegetables. Can I give you something in return?" Lily and her mom said, "No, thank you. We have enough. We just want to make you happy." Mr. Lee hugged them and said, "You have made me very happy. You are very kind too. Thank you for being my friends." Lily and her mom said goodbye and went back to their house. They cooked the cauliflower and ate it for dinner. It was delicious. Lily felt proud and happy. She had learned something new and she had done something good. She had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play games with his friends. They would keep playing games all day. Their favorite game was catch. They would throw a ball and run to catch it. One day, Tim found a big, shiny rock. He showed it to his friends. They thought it was very pretty. They decided to use the rock in their game. They would take turns hiding the rock and then finding it. They called this game "Find the Rich Rock". Tim and his friends played the "Find the Rich Rock" game every day. They were very happy to have a new game to play. They would keep playing until the sun went down. And every day, they would find the rich rock and feel very special.

Once there was a little girl who was very curious. She wanted to find out what was on the other side of a big wall. She was determined to find out and kept asking her mom. But her mom kept saying, "No". The little girl was getting more and more frustrated. She started to insist that she wanted to explore. She was getting quite rude, so her mom said, "If you want to explore so badly, why don't you jump over the wall?" The little girl was thrilled and started to prepare herself. She lifted up her foot, determined to jump. But she stopped when she saw her little toe poking out of her shoe. She said to her mom, "Mommy, I can't jump the wall, my toe will get hurt!" Her mom smiled and said, "If you insist on exploring the other side of the wall, why don't you get a ladder instead? That way your toe will stay safe. The little girl was very happy and quickly got a ladder. She placed it against the wall and started her exploration. She never forgot to be careful, so her little toe was safe!

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car he loved very much. One day, he saw his friend, Sam, looking sad. Tim wanted to help Sam feel happy. Tim had an idea. He would lend his toy car to Sam. Tim knew that Sam liked cars too. He thought that playing with the car would make Sam happy. So, Tim gave his toy car to Sam and said, "You can play with my car today." Sam's face lit up with joy. He played with the toy car all day and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Sam gave the car back to Tim and said, "Thank you for lending me your car. You are a very thoughtful friend." Tim felt happy that his plan worked, and they both played with the toy car together.
