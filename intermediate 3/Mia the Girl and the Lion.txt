Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Lion

Once upon a time, in a small and humble house, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to eat jam on her bread every day. It was her favorite food. One day, a big lion came to her house. The lion was very hungry and wanted to eat Mia's jam. The lion roared loudly, and Mia was scared. She did not want to give her jam to the lion, but she also did not want the lion to be angry. Mia had an idea. She gave the lion some of her jam and said, "Please, Mr. Lion, do not roar so loud. We can share the jam, and we can be friends." The lion liked the jam and agreed to be Mia's friend. From that day on, Mia and the lion shared the jam and were friends. The lion never roared loudly again. The moral of the story is that sharing can make friends, even with those who may seem scary at first.

There was a gloomy day outside. The sky was gray and the clouds were dark. Inside, there was a house with a family inside. Suddenly, the fire alarm went off. The family was scared and they started to run. All of a sudden, a fireman appeared with a siren. He had on a hat and a red coat. He was carrying a hose and a ladder. He was ready to put the fire out! The fireman blinked and sprayed some water on the fire. The fire quickly diminshed and the house was soon safe and sound. The family was so happy that the fireman saved the day! They hugged the friendly fireman and thanked him for his help. The fireman winked and smiled. He waved goodbye before he jumped back into his truck with the sirens blaring. The family sighed with relief and they were happy that the gloomy day was over.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Tim. Tim loved to crawl all day long. He would crawl on the green grass, on the brown dirt, and on the big rocks. One day, Tim was very tired. He had crawled all day long and it was now night. The sky was dark and Tim could see the shiny stars. Tim knew it was time to go to sleep. Tim found a nice, soft leaf to sleep on. He curled up on the leaf and closed his eyes. As Tim slept, he dreamed of crawling on the green grass, the brown dirt, and the big rocks. Tim was very happy, and he couldn't wait for the next day to crawl and play again.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a bottle. Inside the bottle, there was a special thing that could make bubbles. Lily wanted to play with the bubbles. She took the top off the bottle and started to blow. Out came big, round bubbles that floated in the air. Lily was very happy. She blew more and more bubbles. She wanted to see them fly high in the sky. She blew harder and harder, and the bubbles went higher and higher. Then, Lily saw a little bird stuck in a tree. The bird was sad because it could not fly away. Lily had an idea. She blew a big bubble near the bird. The bird hopped onto the bubble, and Lily let it go. The bubble carried the bird up and out of the tree. The bird was free! It could fly again. The bird was very grateful to Lily for helping it. Lily felt happy that she could help the bird. From that day on, Lily and the bird were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was nervous because she lost her toy. Mia looked everywhere for it, but she could not find it. She felt very sad. One day, Mia saw a big raven in her yard. The raven had her toy in its beak! Mia said, "Please, Mr. Raven, can I have my toy back?" The raven looked at her and dropped the toy on the ground. Mia was so happy! Mia smiled and said, "Thank you, Mr. Raven!" The raven smiled back and flew away. Mia was not nervous anymore. She played with her toy all day, and she knew that the raven was her friend.
