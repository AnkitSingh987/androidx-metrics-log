Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Orange

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big orange ball near the water. Tim wanted to play with the ball, so he ran to it. He was very happy. As Tim got closer, he saw the ball move! He stopped and looked. The orange ball was not a ball at all. It was a big, friendly dog with orange fur. The dog wagged its tail and looked at Tim. Tim was not scared. He walked up to the dog and gave it a hug. The dog licked Tim's face, and they played together near the water. They had lots of fun. And from that day on, Tim and the orange dog were best friends.

Jimmy and his mom were sitting on the grass, watching the sunset. It was a beautiful, lively evening. Jimmy looked up at his mom with wide eyes. "Mom," he said, "Look! The sunset's so pretty." His mom smiled and nodded. "Yes it is, Jimmy. Sunsets are one of the best things to watch!" Suddenly a butterfly flew by. Jimmy watched it flutter in the air. "Look, Mom!" he shouted. "A butterfly!" His mom chuckled. "Yes, it's lovely, isn't it? Watching animals can be fun too." Jimmy couldn't believe his eyes. He was filled with happiness. He smiled at his mom and laughed. They stayed a little longer, watching the sun slowly go to sleep. When it was dark, they said goodbye to the sunset and walked back home, still feeling the lively atmosphere of the evening.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Buddy. Buddy loved dinner time. He would eat and eat until his belly was full. One day, his family made a very big dinner with lots of good food. Buddy was very happy. During dinner, Buddy saw a deep bowl of yummy soup on the table. He wanted to taste it, but he could not reach it. Buddy jumped and jumped, but he could not get the soup. Then, he had an idea. He would use his tail to get the soup. Buddy swung his tail and tried to grab the deep bowl of soup. But, oh no! He made a mistake. The bowl of soup dropped on the floor. The soup went everywhere. Buddy felt sad, but his family just laughed. They cleaned up the mess and gave Buddy a big hug. They knew it was just an accident.

One day, a little girl named Mia was walking in the woods. She saw a small hut. Mia was confused because she had never seen a hut in the woods before. She went close to the hut to see what was inside. As Mia got close to the hut, she heard a voice. "Hello! Who are you?" said the voice. Mia looked around and saw a talking frog. "I am Mia," she said. "I live in the house near the woods. Who are you?" The frog said, "I am Fred. I live in this hut." Fred the frog told Mia that he was once a prince, but a witch turned him into a frog. He said he needed a friend to help him become a prince again. Mia wanted to help Fred. She gave him a big hug, and suddenly, something unexpected happened. Fred the frog turned back into a prince! Mia was so happy she could help her new friend. They both went back to the hut and closed the door. From that day on, Mia and Prince Fred were the best of friends, and they had many more adventures together in the woods.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a nice bedroom with lots of toys. One day, he saw a big bug in his room. The bug was harsh, and Tim was scared. Tim wanted the bug to go away, so he tried to observe it. He saw the bug liked to eat his toys. Tim was sad because he did not want his toys to be eaten. He thought of a plan to stop the bug. Tim got a big box and put some old toys inside. He put the box near the bug and waited. The bug went into the box to eat the toys. Tim quickly closed the box and took it outside. The bug was gone, and Tim was happy. His bedroom was safe again, and he played with his toys all day.
