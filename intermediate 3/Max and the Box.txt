Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Box

One day, a dog named Max went to a lab. In the lab, there were many light things that Max could see. He saw a big box and thought, "I can pack my toys in this box." Max said to his friend, a cat named Sam, "Let's pack our toys in the box and take them outside to play." Sam said, "Okay!" They both started to pack their toys in the box. As they were packing, a big wind came and blew the box away. Max and Sam looked at each other and said, "Oh no! Our toys are gone!" They felt sad. But then, they saw the box in a tree. They climbed the tree, got the box, and played with their toys. They were happy again.

One day, a girl named Lucy found a colorful toy in her room. It was a microscope. She could use it to see things very close up. Lucy loved her new toy and played with it all day. Lucy wanted to tell her friend Tom about her microscope. She went to his house and showed him the toy. Tom was very happy to see it. They played with the microscope and looked at many things close up. They saw colorful things like flowers and leaves. Then, something unexpected happened. When they looked at a tiny bug under the microscope, they saw that it was not a bug at all. It was a little fairy! The fairy talked to Lucy and Tom. They were very surprised. The fairy told them that she had been hiding as a bug to find a new friend. Now, Lucy and Tom had a new fairy friend to play with.

Once there was a dog named Spot. He was a very strong dog with a lot of power. Spot wanted to do something special, so he decided to complete a tightrope challenge. So Spot got ready and he put on his backpack and climbed up the rope. The rope was very tight and he had to use all of his power to keep his balance. He kept walking to the other side while thinking about how he was going to complete the challenge. Suddenly, Spot slipped and he began to fall! He was so close to completing the challenge. But just then, Spot remembered his power and was able to use it to grab the rope tight again. He was now able to complete the challenge, and he was so proud of himself!

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play outside. One day, he saw a big puddle. Max jumped in the puddle and got all wet. He was so happy! Max heard a whistle. It was his friend, Tim the bird. Tim was flying in the sky. Tim saw Max playing in the puddle, so he flew down to play too. They splashed and played together. After a while, they found a fake toy in the puddle. It was a toy fish! Max and Tim laughed and played with the toy fish. They had so much fun in the puddle. They played until the sun went down and it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, in a far away land, there was a light temple. The temple was very pretty and shiny. Many people liked to visit the temple to see the light. One day, a little girl named Amy went to the temple. She saw a big problem. The light was gone! The temple was dark and not shiny anymore. Amy wanted to help. She wanted to solve the problem. Amy looked around and saw a big box. She thought the light was inside. She opened the box, but it was empty. Then, Amy looked up and saw the light in the sky! It was the sun! The sun was hiding behind a big cloud. The cloud moved away, and the light came back to the temple. The temple was shiny again, and everyone was happy.

One day, a young boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. In the tree, there was a small bird. The bird was not happy. The bird started to complain. "Chirp, chirp! I can't find my food," said the bird. "I am so hungry." Tim wanted to help the bird. He looked around the park and saw an olive on the ground. He picked up the olive and gave it to the bird. The bird was so happy. "Thank you, Tim!" said the bird. "Now I am not hungry anymore." Tim felt good. He helped the bird, and now the bird was happy. Tim and his mom went home, and they told dad about their day at the park.
