Category: Intermediate
Title: The Woman and the Dog

One day, a woman went to the store. She wanted to buy a hairy rug for her house. She saw many rugs in the store, but she did not know which one to pick. She saw a girl and asked, "Can you help me measure the rug?" The girl said, "Yes, I can help you." They measured the rug together. It was big enough for the woman's house. But when they were done, the rug started to move! It was not a rug at all. It was a big, hairy dog! The woman and the girl laughed. The woman said, "I will not buy a rug today, but I will take the dog home." And they all lived happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a little clock who lived in a big house. The clock was very sad because it had a terrible sound. Every time it went "tick-tock", it sounded like a loud scream. The people in the house did not like the clock's noise. One day, a kind mouse came to the clock and whispered, "Don't worry, little clock. I will help you fix your terrible sound." The mouse worked all day and all night. The clock started to feel better and hoped that its noise would be nice now. The next day, the people in the house listened to the clock. Instead of a terrible sound, the clock went "tick-tock" with a soft whisper. The people were so happy, and they loved the clock. The clock and the mouse became best friends and lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Lily was playing in her room. She had a lot of fun toys and pretty things. She also had a big box of fine paper in many colors. Lily liked to draw and make things with her paper. Lily's friend, Tim, came to play with her. He saw her fine paper and said, "Can I have some too?" Lily said, "Yes, we can share!" They both started to draw and make things with the paper. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened! A small wind blew through the open window and took all the fine paper away. Lily and Tim were sad. They looked outside and saw a big tree with all their paper stuck in it. They smiled and said, "Let's climb the tree and get our paper back!" So, they did, and they had more fun than ever before.

One day, a girl named Lily found a book. The book was a novel. Lily liked to read, so she was happy. She wanted to put the novel in her bag, but her bag was full of toys. So, she took out some toys and put the novel in the bag. Lily went to the park to read her novel. It was a sunny day, but the wind felt icy. Lily was cold, but she wanted to read her book. She put on her warm coat and sat on a bench. She opened the novel and began to read. As she read the novel, Lily forgot about the icy wind. She was lost in the story. Soon, her mom called her to go home. Lily put the novel back in her bag and left the park. She couldn't wait to read more of her novel at home.

Lily and Sam went to the zoo with Mom and Dad. They saw many animals, like lions, monkeys, and elephants. Lily liked the lions best. She wanted to hear them roar. Sam liked the monkeys best. He wanted to see them swing. They came to a big gate. It had a sign that said "Do not open". Lily and Sam were curious. They wondered what was behind the gate. They asked Mom and Dad, but they said it was a secret. Lily and Sam waited until Mom and Dad were looking at the map. They ran to the gate and opened it. They saw a hidden path that led to a small house. They followed the path and knocked on the door. "Who is there?" a voice said from inside. "Lily and Sam," they said. The door opened and they saw a man with a big beard and a hat. He smiled and said, "Hello, Lily and Sam. Welcome to my house. I am the zookeeper. Do you want to see something special?" Lily and Sam nodded. They were excited. The zookeeper took them inside and showed them a big cage. Inside the cage, there was a baby panda. It was black and white and fluffy. It was playing with a ball. "This is Ping," the zookeeper said. "He is the newest animal in the zoo. He is very rare and precious. Only a few people know about him. He is my friend. Do you want to be his friends too?" Lily and Sam said yes. They went closer to the cage and said hello to Ping. Ping looked at them and made a cute noise. He rolled the ball to them. Lily and Sam rolled it back. They played with Ping for a while. They had fun. The zookeeper said, "You are very lucky to see Ping. He is a special gift from China. He will soon join the other pandas in the big enclosure. But for now, he needs to stay here with me. He is still very young and shy. He needs a lot of care and love. You can come and visit him anytime, but you have to keep him a secret. Can you do that?" Lily and Sam said yes. They promised to keep Ping a secret. They thanked the zookeeper and said goodbye to Ping. They hugged him and gave him a kiss. They went back to the gate and closed it. They ran to Mom and Dad and pretended nothing happened. They had a big secret. They had a new friend. They were happy.
