Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong dog named Max. Max had big muscles. He loved to spend his days running and playing with his friends. One sunny day, Max met a little cat named Lily. Lily was very hungry. Max said, "Lily, would you like to share my food? It is very delicious." Lily said, "Yes, please! Thank you, Max." Max and Lily spent the day playing and eating together. They had lots of fun. Max used his big muscles to help Lily climb trees. They became best friends and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a funny bunny named Bob. Bob loved to paint. One day, he wanted to paint the four seasons: spring, summer, fall, and winter. Bob started to paint spring with pretty flowers and green grass. Next, he painted summer with a big, bright sun and kids playing. Then, he painted fall with orange leaves falling from trees. Finally, he painted winter with white snow and kids making snowmen. As Bob finished his painting, something unexpected happened. The painting came to life! The seasons jumped out of the painting and started to play together. Flowers danced with snowflakes, and leaves played with the sun. Bob laughed and joined the fun. He realized that every season can be friends, just like in his painting.

Once upon a time, there was a little engine who loved to skip. One day, the little engine went to the park to play. He skipped and skipped all day long. He was so happy when he skipped. In the park, the little engine saw a big, disgusting mess. The mess was made by a naughty dog. The little engine did not like the mess. He wanted the park to be clean and pretty. So, the little engine skipped to his friend, the kind duck. The duck helped the little engine clean the disgusting mess. They worked together and made the park clean again. The little engine was happy and thanked the duck. They both skipped and played together in the clean park.

Once upon a time, there lived a brave little girl. Her name was Luca. She was a happy girl who liked to explore the world. One day, when she was out near the woods, Luca noticed a sparkly light out of the corner of her eye. She was curious and she wanted to take a closer look. She walked towards the shining light and soon she arrived at a big lake. Luca was mesmerized by the lake. The lake was so beautiful and sparkly. It looked like a dream. Suddenly, Luca heard a loud voice. It said, "Do not go near the lake!". Luca was scared, but she trusted her bravery. She turned around to see who had said the threat. It was Luca's best friend - Mikey. Mikey was always reliable and he wanted to make sure that Luca was safe. So he had warned her. Luca thanked him for looking out for her. She hugged him and then they both went home. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big whale. The whale loved to bounce in the water. One day, the whale met a little fish. The fish was very persistent. It wanted to learn how to bounce too. "Can you teach me to bounce?" asked the little fish. The whale smiled and said, "Of course, I can teach you. Just watch me and try to do the same." The little fish watched the whale bounce up and down in the water. It tried to do the same, but it was not easy. The fish kept trying, and it did not give up. The whale cheered for the little fish. Finally, the little fish could bounce just like the whale. The whale and the fish were so happy. They bounced together all day long. And they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy bear named Mr. Bear. Mr. Bear was very old and dead. Tim loved Mr. Bear a lot, even if he was dead. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, we need to talk about Mr. Bear." Tim looked at his mom and asked, "Why, Mom?" His mom mentioned, "The truth is, Mr. Bear is too old and dead. We need to get you a new toy." Tim felt sad, but he knew his mom was telling the truth. They went to the toy store and bought a new, alive bear. Tim named his new bear Mr. Bear Jr. Tim loved Mr. Bear Jr., but he always remembered his old, dead Mr. Bear.
