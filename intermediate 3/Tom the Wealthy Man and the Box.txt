Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Wealthy Man and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He lived in a big place with many rooms. Tom had a dog named Max. Max liked to play with Tom and find things. One day, Max said to Tom, "Let's go outside and find something new!" So Tom and Max went outside to play. They looked around and Max found a big box. Tom was very excited to see what was inside the box. Tom opened the box and found a magic hat. He put the hat on Max's head. Suddenly, Max could talk like a person! Max said, "Wow, I can talk now! Let's go find more fun things!" Tom and Max had a great day finding new things and talking together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wise old owl. The owl liked to match sounds he heard in the forest. He was very good at it. One day, a naughty squirrel came to the forest. He wanted to play a trick on the wise owl. The squirrel made a strange sound. The wise owl tried to match the sound, but he could not. The squirrel laughed and said, "You are not wise! You cannot match my sound!" The wise owl felt sad and wanted to find a way to match the squirrel's sound. The wise owl practiced and practiced. He listened to all the sounds in the forest. One day, he heard the squirrel make the strange sound again. This time, the wise owl was ready. He matched the sound perfectly. The squirrel was surprised and said, "You are a wise owl after all!" The wise owl smiled and the two became friends. They played together, matching sounds in the forest, and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little fox named Foxy. Foxy loved to play in the forest with his friends, Bunny and Squirrel. They had lots of fun running, jumping, and playing hide and seek. One day, Foxy, Bunny, and Squirrel saw a big pile of waste in their forest. They were sad because the waste made their forest dirty and not nice to play in. Foxy had an idea. He said, "Let's clean up the waste, please. We can make our forest pretty again." Bunny and Squirrel agreed, and they all started to pick up the waste. They put the waste in a big bag. When they were done, the forest was clean and pretty again. Foxy, Bunny, and Squirrel were very happy. "Thank you for helping," said Foxy. "Now we can play in our clean forest again." Bunny and Squirrel smiled and said, "You're welcome, Foxy. You are very clever for thinking of this idea." And they all played happily ever after.

Jack was very excited. He had just seen a new goose! He watched as the goose waddled around and said, “Hi Goose!” The goose didn’t answer. It just kept waddling. Jack was sad at first, but then he had a great idea. He got a stick and said to the Goose, “Come, Goose! Let's go and play.” The Goose looked at him, and then it lay down on the grass. Jack smiled, grabbed the stick and said, “Yay! Now we can play.” They played a game of tag and had a lot of fun. When it was time to go, Jack said goodbye to the new Goose and gave it a big hug.

Once upon a time, there was a little dolphin named Dolly. Dolly lived in the big blue sea with her family. She loved to swim and play with her friends all day long. One day, Dolly had an important job to do. She had to find a special shell for her mom's birthday. Dolly started to worry because she didn't know where to look. She swam and swam, looking under rocks and in the sand. Finally, Dolly found the perfect shell. It was shiny and pretty. She swam back home as fast as she could. When she gave the shell to her mom, her mom was so happy. Dolly felt proud and didn't worry anymore. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little dog named Max found a big bone. Max was very happy. He wanted to take the bone to his home. But the bone was very big, and Max was very small. Max had to struggle to move the bone. Max was worried. He did not want to lose his big bone. He tried to push the bone. He tried to pull the bone. But the bone did not move. Max was sad. He wanted to keep the big bone. Then, Max saw his friend, a big dog named Sam. Sam wanted to help Max. Sam pushed the bone with his nose. Max pulled the bone with his mouth. Together, they moved the big bone to Max's home. Max was very happy. He shared the big bone with Sam.
