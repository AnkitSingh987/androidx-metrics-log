Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a very lucky dog. He had many friends and a big yard to play in. One day, Max went to search for a surprise for his friends. He wanted to make them happy. Max looked and looked, but he could not find a surprise. He was sad. Then, he saw a big box in the yard. Max was excited! He thought it was the surprise he had been searching for. He opened the box, but it was empty. Max was sad again. But then, Max had an idea. He filled the box with his favorite toys and treats. He took the box to his friends and they were so happy! Max learned that sometimes, the best surprise is one that you make yourself. And that made Max feel very lucky.

One day, a big rock sat in the sun. It was a nice day. The rock felt safe and warm. A little bird flew down and sat on the rock. The bird had a small piece of tape in its beak. The rock and the bird were friends. The bird put the tape on the rock. They both laughed. The rock and the bird played a game. They put the tape on each other. They had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the tape away. The rock and the bird were sad. They looked for the tape but could not find it. Then, they saw a big tree. The tree had the tape! The wind put the tape on the tree. The rock and the bird were happy again. They played with the tape and felt safe with their new friend, the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a zebra who lived in a meadow. He was always very bored in the meadow and wanted something exciting to happen. One day, the zebra decided to go for a walk and explore. While he was walking, he soon heard a voice. It was coming from a tree! He quickly stopped and stood still, listening to the voice. It said, “Hello zebra, I can help you make your meadow less boring.” The zebra was curious, so he stepped closer and spoke to the tree. The tree said, “If you come with me, I can take you to a place that is filled with fun and excitement.” The zebra couldn’t believe it - this was just what he wanted! Without hesitation, the zebra followed the tree. The tree brought the zebra to a beautiful and magical place. There, the zebra found many other zebras, each having their own fun and exciting adventure. The zebra finally realized he didn’t have to have a boring life in the meadow - all he had to do was follow the tree!

Lila and Tom were playing in the garden. They had a big lime tree with many green fruits. Lila liked to pick them and smell them. Tom liked to throw them and watch them bounce. "Look, Lila, I have a lime!" Tom said, holding a round lime in his hand. "What are you going to do with it?" Lila asked, curious. "I'm going to shoot it!" Tom said, pretending his hand was a gun. He pointed his hand at Lila and made a loud noise. "Bang! Bang!" Lila ducked and laughed. She picked up a lime and threw it back at Tom. "You missed me!" Tom dodged the lime and ran towards Lila. He grabbed another lime and tried to shoot it at her. But he squeezed it too hard and the juice squirted in his face. "Yuck!" Tom cried, spitting and rubbing his eyes. "The lime is disgusting! It's sour and bitter!" Lila giggled and ran to help him. She took a napkin and wiped his face. "It's okay, Tom. The lime is not for shooting. It's for eating. But you have to peel it first." She showed him how to peel the lime and break it into pieces. She gave him a piece and took one for herself. "Here, try it. It's sweet and juicy." Tom hesitated, but he trusted Lila. He bit the piece and smiled. "You're right, Lila. The lime is good. It's not disgusting at all." They shared the lime and enjoyed the taste. They decided to pick more limes and make a lemonade. They had fun playing in the garden with the limes.
