Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Clumsy Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run around in the park. One day, Spot found something new on the ground. It was green and round. Spot did not know what it was, but he picked it up with his mouth. Spot took the green thing home to show his friend, Kitty. Kitty knew what it was! She said, "That is an avocado, Spot!" Kitty told Spot that people like to eat avocados, but they must chew it well first. Spot wanted to try it too. Spot and Kitty sat down to eat the avocado together. They took turns to chew it. It was soft and yummy. They both liked it a lot. From that day on, Spot and Kitty would look for avocados in the park and enjoy them together. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a lovely cat named Lily wanted to go on an adventure. She put on her hat and walked out of her house. As she walked, she saw her friend, a dog named Max. Max was holding a paper with the word "quiz" on it. "Hi, Max! What is that?" asked Lily. Max looked at the paper and said, "This is a quiz! We have to find things and answer questions. Do you want to play with me?" Lily thought it would be fun, so she said, "Yes, let's go!" Lily and Max went around the park, looking for things and answering questions on the quiz. They found a big tree, a red ball, and a small rock. They had a lot of fun playing the quiz game together. At the end of the day, they were both happy and tired. They went back to their homes and told their families about their lovely day.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a small patch of pretty flowers. She was very happy and wanted to pick some for her mom. Lily was careful not to step on the flowers. She stood on the grass next to the patch. A tiny bug saw Lily and said, "Hello! I live here with my family. We love these flowers too." Lily smiled and said, "Hi, little bug! I want to pick some flowers for my mom, but I will be careful not to hurt your home." The tiny bug thanked Lily and said, "You are a good friend. Let's pick the flowers together." So, Lily and the tiny bug picked some flowers for Lily's mom. They were very careful and stood on the grass. Lily's mom loved the flowers and gave Lily a big hug.

Once upon a time there was a little bird who wanted to soar in the sky like an eagle. The little bird wanted to try and fly but it did not feel brave enough. But then the little bird found a tank filled with a hot air. The bird jumped in the tank and it was a bit messy. The bird looked around the tank and said: "This is not so bad! I feel brave enough to try and fly". So the bird started flapping its wings and it soon began soaring in the sky. The little bird flew through the clouds and felt so happy and free. The little bird flew around for hours and hours, until at last it was time to come back down to the ground. The little bird landed back in the tank, all safe and sound. "That was awesome!" the little bird said with a big smile on its face. And ever since then, the little bird soars in the sky whenever it can.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Ben. Ben liked to enjoy the sun in the forest. He had a friend, a little bird named Tina. Tina would teach Ben how to sing. They were very happy. One day, Ben and Tina found a big box. They did not know what was in it. They were very excited. They opened the box and found many hats inside. They tried on the hats and had a lot of fun. Then, a little girl named Lisa came to the forest. She saw Ben and Tina with her hats. Lisa was not mad. She was grateful. She said, "Thank you for finding my hats! Now, we can all play together!" And they all enjoyed the day in the forest.
