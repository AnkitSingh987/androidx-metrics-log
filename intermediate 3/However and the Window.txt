Category: Intermediate
Title: However and the Window

One day, a young boy wanted to go outside and play. However, it was wet outside, so the boy was sad. He looked out of the window, searching for a way to gain some fun. Suddenly, the boy had an idea. He put on his rain boots and grabbed his yellow raincoat. The boy opened the door and jumped outside into the rain! The boy laughed as the water touched his skin. He ran around, playing in the rain with a big smile on his face. He splashed around the puddles, feeling the wetness on his face. The young boy was having so much fun playing in the rain. Soon, he was completely soaked, but the boy didn't mind because he had gained some fun!

Once upon a time, there was a shy little otter. She was very modest, and she kept to herself every day. She would often play alone in the lake, or explore the shore. One day, while exploring the shore, she met a kind lady. The lady asked her, "Hello there little otter. Can I give you something?" The otter was a bit shy, so she shook her head no. But the lady told her, "Don't worry, I have something for you. Come closer little one and I'll give it to you." With that, the otter hesitantly placed a paw in the lady's outstretched hand. The lady smiled and said, "Here, this is for you!" She held out a shiny, golden coin. "This is to show that kindness is always better than being shy." The otter smiled and thanked the lady, then put the coin in her pocket. From then on, she stayed modest but wasn't afraid to be brave and kind.

Lily and Tom were brother and sister. They liked to play outside in the sun. One day, they saw a creek near their house. It had clear water and shiny rocks. They wanted to go closer and touch it. "Mom, can we go to the creek?" Lily asked. "No, you can't. The creek is dangerous. It can be very powerful and fast. You might fall in and get hurt. You have to behave and stay away from it." Mom said. But Lily and Tom did not listen. They waited until Mom was busy and ran to the creek. They thought it was fun and pretty. They threw rocks and sticks in the water. They tried to catch fish and frogs. "Look, I found a big rock. I can jump on it." Tom said. He jumped on the rock, but it was slippery. He lost his balance and fell into the creek. The water was cold and strong. It pulled him away from the shore. He screamed and cried. "Lily, help me! I can't swim!" Tom said. Lily was scared and sad. She wanted to help Tom, but she did not know how. She ran to Mom and told her what happened. "Mom, Tom fell in the creek. He is in trouble. Please help him." Lily said. Mom was angry and worried. She ran to the creek with Lily. But it was too late. Tom was gone. The creek had taken him away. Mom and Lily cried and hugged. They wished they had behaved and stayed away from the creek.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy pistol that he loved to play with. He would run around the yard, holding the pistol tight in his hand. He would pretend to be a hero, saving the day. One sunny day, Tim was playing with his pistol when his mom called him. "Tim, it's time for lunch!" she said. Tim didn't want to stop playing, so he thought of a way to delay going inside. He told his mom, "I need to find my toy first!" He pretended to look for his pistol, even though it was right in his hand. His mom knew what he was doing, but she let him have his fun for a little while. After a few minutes, she called him again. "Tim, come inside now," she said. Tim knew he couldn't delay any longer. He held his pistol tight and ran inside to eat lunch with his mom. After lunch, he went back outside to play with his toy pistol and be a hero once more.
