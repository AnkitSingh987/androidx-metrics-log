Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pink Bike and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a pink bike. A little boy named Tim wanted to ride the bike. He asked his mom if he could ride it. His mom said, "Yes, but don't go too far." Tim started to ride the pink bike. He saw a big temple. It was pretty and tall. Tim wanted to ride the bike around the temple. He forgot what his mom said. Tim kept riding the bike around the temple. He went too far and got lost. He was sad and scared. A kind man found Tim and took him back to his mom. Tim learned to always listen to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was restless. He liked to kick a red ball all day. One day, Tim saw a big dog. The dog was running fast. Tim wanted to play with the dog. Tim kicked the red ball to the dog. The dog did not like it. The dog barked loud at Tim. Tim was scared. He did not want the dog to be mad. He wanted to make the dog happy. Tim had an idea. He gave the dog a treat. The dog liked the treat. The dog stopped barking. Tim and the dog became friends. They played together every day. And they were happy.

Once upon a time, in a big town, there was a huge stadium. The stadium was very old and needed some repair. The people in the town loved the stadium because they played games there. They wanted to make it bright and new again. One day, a kind man named Tom came to the town. He saw the stadium and knew he could help. Tom was very good at fixing things. The people in the town were very happy and asked Tom to repair the stadium. Tom said yes and started to work. Tom worked very hard to fix the stadium. He painted it with bright colors and made it look new again. The people in the town were so happy. They thanked Tom and had a big party at the stadium. Everyone played games and had fun. The stadium was bright and beautiful, just like they wanted.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, John and Joe. John was wild and liked to do all kinds of fun things. One day, Joe fell and hurt himself very badly. John saw that Joe was very hurt and he quickly ran to get an ambulance. The ambulance was white and it had a sharp siren. It was very loud and John had to cover his ears! When the ambulance arrived, the doctors jumped out and they quickly helped Joe. The doctors put Joe on a stretcher and put him in the ambulance. Then they drove away with their sharp siren. John was so glad he got help for Joe. They both went home and Joe was feeling better soon. John told everyone about his wild adventure and everyone was so happy for Joe.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round ball. This ball was very heavy. It had a lot of weight. The ball was always alert and liked to watch the other toys play. One day, the ball saw a magic wand. The ball knew the wand could do something special. The ball wanted to play with the other toys, but it was too big and heavy. The ball wished to shrink and be smaller. The magic wand heard the ball's wish. The wand worked its magic and the ball began to shrink. The ball became smaller and smaller. Now, the ball was not heavy anymore. The ball was so happy! The other toys saw the small ball and invited it to play. The ball was excited and joined them. They all played together and had so much fun. From that day on, the ball was always alert and thankful for the magic wand. The ball played with the other toys every day, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Bob. Bob was a tough fish. He liked to swim very fast in the water. He showed off his speed to all his friends. One day, Bob saw a pretty shell at the bottom of the water. He wanted to get it for his friend, Sally. So, Bob took a deep breath and decided to dive down to get it. Bob dived deep down into the water. He swam fast with all his speed. He picked up the pretty shell and swam back up. Sally was so happy with her gift. Bob felt great that he could help his friend. They swam together, and they were the best of friends.
