Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his best friend, a cat named Luna. They played all day long, running and jumping in the sun. Max was happy when he played with Luna. One day, Max saw Luna playing with a new friend, a bird named Sky. Max felt jealous because he wanted to play with Luna too. Max went to his house and did not play with Luna and Sky. He waited for dinner time and thought about what to do. When dinner time came, Max had an idea. He invited Luna and Sky to eat dinner with him. They all ate dinner together and had so much fun. After dinner, they all played together, and Max was not jealous anymore. The unexpected thing was that Max, Luna, and Sky became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a kind boy named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little bird who couldn't fly. Tom felt sad for the bird. He wanted to help the bird see the world. So, Tom said, "Let me help you, little bird." Tom picked up the bird and put it on his shoulder. They walked together and saw pretty flowers, big trees, and a blue sky. The bird was very happy. Tom was a compassionate friend to the bird. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the bird off Tom's shoulder. But the bird didn't fall! It started to fly! The bird said, "Thank you, Tom, for showing me the world. Now I can fly and see it on my own!" Tom was happy and proud of his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little mouse named Tim. Tim lived in a house with a big cat named Sam. Tim always tried to stay quiet so Sam would not find him. One day, Tim found a magic measure stick. He did not know it was magic yet. Tim was playing with the measure stick when Sam came into the room. "Who is there?" asked Sam. Tim was scared and wished he could disappear. Suddenly, he did! The magic measure stick made him disappear. Sam looked around, but he could not find Tim. Tim was so happy he could disappear. He knew he could use the magic measure stick to be safe from Sam. From that day on, Tim could play and explore the house without being scared of Sam. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and jump all day. One day, Spot found a red cap on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to show his friends. Spot found his friend, a little girl named Lisa. He showed her the red cap. Lisa said, "Wow, Spot! That's a nice cap. Can I wear it?" Spot wagged his tail and let Lisa put the cap on her head. They played together in the sun. After playing for a long time, Spot and Lisa were very tired. They sat under a big tree to rest. Lisa took off the red cap and put it on Spot's head. They both smiled and fell asleep, dreaming of more fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was eager to wash his hands in the sink. He believed that if he washed his hands well, a fun surprise would happen. One day, as Tim washed his hands, he saw a little fish in the sink! He was so happy and surprised. He played with the fish and they became friends. Tim named the fish Fin. But then, Tim found out that Fin was not a real fish. Fin was a toy fish that his mom put in the sink for him. Tim was sad at first, but then he was happy. He still had a new toy friend to play with. And he knew that if he believed, fun surprises could happen.

Tony was a three year old boy who loved pushing his toy automobile around. One day when Tony went outside with his toy car, he saw a real automobile parked on the street. It was bright red with shiny wheels and Tony wanted to get in and explore. He ran up to it and started blinking his eyes, waiting for the car's door to open. Just then, Tony heard Ms. Smith from next door call out to him. She said, "Hey, Tony! Would you like to try my special spicy cookies?" Tony smiled, excited to have a treat. He nodded his head yes and then followed Ms. Smith into her kitchen. Tony ate the cookies and they were the best he'd ever tasted. He loved how they were so spicy. Tony thanked Ms. Smith and then returned to the street. He wished he could be in the red automobile, but he knew that it couldn't happen. He continued playing with his toy car, happy to have tasted such a delicious and spicy treat.
