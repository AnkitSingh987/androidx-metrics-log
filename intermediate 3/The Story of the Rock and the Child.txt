Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Rock and the Child

Once upon a time, there was a child. The child had a toy. The toy was not pretty. It was ugly. But the child loved the ugly toy very much. One day, the child found a sharp rock. The child wanted to make the ugly toy pretty. So, the child used the sharp rock to draw on the toy. The child drew a big smile on the toy's face. Now, the toy had a big smile. The child was happy. The ugly toy was not ugly anymore. The child and the toy played all day. They were best friends.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sally had a birthday party. Her mom wanted to make it special. So, she ordered a loud clown to come and play with Sally and her friends. When the clown came, he had a big red nose, funny hair, and big shoes. He made everyone laugh with his loud honks and silly dances. The kids clapped and cheered for the clown. At the end of the party, the clown made balloon animals for everyone. Sally and her friends said "thank you" to the clown. They all agreed that it was the best birthday party ever.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a nosy cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to watch everything that happened. One day, Kitty saw a big bicycle with a red bow. She knew it was for someone's birthday. Kitty wanted to know who it was for, so she asked her friend, Doggy. "Doggy, do you know whose bicycle that is?" Kitty asked. Doggy shook his head and said, "I don't know, Kitty. But we can find out!" They decided to look for clues. As they walked, they saw a big box with colorful paper. Kitty thought it was a good place to hide and watch. She told Doggy to wrap her in the colorful paper, so no one would see her. Doggy wrapped Kitty in the paper, and she hid in the box. Soon, a little girl named Lucy came by. She saw the bicycle and the box. "Wow! A bicycle for my birthday!" Lucy said. She opened the box and found Kitty inside. Kitty jumped out and said, "Surprise! Happy birthday, Lucy!" Lucy laughed and said, "Thank you, Kitty! Let's ride the bicycle together!" From that day on, Kitty and Lucy were the best of friends. They had many fun adventures on the bicycle, and Kitty learned that sometimes it's better to be a friend than to be nosy.

Sara and Ben are best friends. They like to play pretend a lot. One day, they pretend to have a marriage. Sara wears a white dress and a flower crown. Ben wears a black suit and a tie. They hold hands and walk to the garden. "Ben, do you want to be my husband?" Sara asks. "Yes, Sara, I want to be your husband," Ben says. They smile and kiss each other on the cheek. They are very happy. But then, a tall boy comes to the garden. He is Tom, Sara's big brother. He sees Sara and Ben and gets angry. "Hey, what are you doing?" Tom shouts. "You can't have a marriage. You are too little. And you are not a real couple. You are just friends." Sara and Ben feel scared and sad. They let go of each other's hands and run away. They hide behind a tree and cry. "Tom is mean," Sara says. "He doesn't understand. We are just playing pretend. We love each other." "I know, Sara," Ben says. "He is just jealous. He doesn't have a friend like you. Don't worry. We can still have our marriage. We just need to find a new place." They hug each other and wipe their tears. They look around and see a big tent. They run to the tent and go inside. They find a cake and some balloons. They think it is a gift for them. "Wow, this is perfect," Ben says. "This is our new home. We can have our marriage here. And no one can bother us." "Yes, Ben, this is perfect," Sara says. "This is our happy ending. We can have our marriage here. And we can eat the cake and play with the balloons." They smile and kiss each other on the cheek again. They are very happy. They don't know that the tent belongs to Tom. He made it for his birthday party. He is going to be very mad when he finds out. But Sara and Ben don't care. They are too busy playing pretend. They are the happiest couple in the world.
