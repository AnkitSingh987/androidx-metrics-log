Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Song

Once upon a time, there was a big, crazy bird. The bird loved to leave its nest every sunrise. It would fly high in the sky and sing happy songs. One day, the bird woke up and saw the sunrise. It was time to leave the nest. But today, something unexpected happened. The bird found a little, lost kitten on the ground. The kitten was scared and alone. The crazy bird decided to help the kitten find its way home. They looked for the kitten's home together, and the bird sang happy songs to make the kitten feel better. The kitten started to sing too, and they made a beautiful song together. Suddenly, they found the kitten's home. The kitten's family was so happy to see them. They thanked the crazy bird for helping. From that day on, the bird and the kitten were the best of friends. They would leave their homes every sunrise and sing their beautiful song for everyone to hear.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the snow. They make a snowman. The snowman is tall and has a hat and a carrot nose. Lily and Ben are happy. But then a big dog comes. The dog is hungry. He sees the carrot nose. He runs to the snowman and bites the nose. The snowman falls down. Lily and Ben are sad. The dog is sorry. He wants to be friends. He jokes with Lily and Ben. He makes funny sounds and wags his tail. He brings them a stick and a ball. Lily and Ben laugh. They play with the dog. They throw the stick and the ball. The dog runs and catches them. He is fast and strong. They make a new snowman. They use ice for the nose. The dog cannot bite the ice. The snowman is safe. Lily and Ben are happy again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sally. She loved ponies and dreamed of having one of her own. Every day she would visit the farm where the ponies were kept and study them. One day she learned that a new pony had arrived. Sally couldn't wait to see it, so she raced over to the farm. The pony was so pretty with its clean golden fur. Sally started to stroke the pony and whisper soft words to it. Suddenly, the pony spoke to her. "Hello little girl. What is your name?" "My name is Sally," she replied. "Would you like to be my best friend and take me home, Sally?" asked the pony. Sally's eyes lit up, and she couldn't believe it was true. She hugged the pony tightly and said yes. With a smile on her face, Sally hopped onto the pony's back and rode home. She was so happy to have a new friend, and she promised to her pony she would always take care of it and keep it clean.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play together. But they did not like to share their toys. One day, they found a magic wand in the attic. It was shiny and had a star on the end. "I want it!" Tom said. "No, I want it!" Lily said. They both grabbed the wand and pulled hard. The wand broke in two pieces. They were very sad and angry. "You broke it! I hate you!" Tom shouted. "No, you broke it! I hate you more!" Lily shouted. They threw the pieces on the floor and ran to their rooms. The next day, they saw the wand on the table. It was whole again. They were very surprised and curious. "Maybe it is a magic wand!" Tom said. "Maybe it can do magic things!" Lily said. They decided to try it out. They took turns to wave the wand and say magic words. The wand made their toys come to life, their clothes change colors, and their bed fly in the air. They had a lot of fun and laughed a lot. They also learned to share the wand and say sorry to each other. They realized that they did not hate each other. They loved each other. They were twins and they were tough. They hugged and thanked the magic wand.
