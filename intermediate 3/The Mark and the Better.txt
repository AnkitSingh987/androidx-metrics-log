Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mark and the Better

Once upon a time there was a mark. The mark was fair and it made everything better. Everybody liked the mark. But one day something unexpected happened. The mark started to increase and it kept growing bigger and bigger. It was amazing! Everyone was so happy because the mark was making everything even better. The mark kept increasing until it was the biggest mark anyone had ever seen. All the people cheered when they saw it. Everyone thought it was so fair. And so the mark stayed big and it kept making everything better. Everyone smiled and all was right in the world. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a gift under his bed. It was a small, quiet toy cat. Tim was very happy with his new gift. He played with the cat all day and all night. The next day, Tim took the cat to the park. He saw his friend, Sue. "Look at my gift!" Tim said to Sue. "It is a quiet cat!" Sue liked the cat too. They played with the cat and had a lot of fun. But then, it was time for Tim to go home. "Can the cat stay with me?" asked Sue. "No, the cat must remain with me," said Tim. Sue was sad, but she understood. Tim and the quiet cat went home together. They remained best friends and played every day. And sometimes, Sue came over to play with the quiet cat too.

One day, a shrimp was walking very slow. The shrimp saw a big wrap. The shrimp was hungry, so it went to the wrap. The shrimp said, "Hi wrap! Can I eat you?" The wrap said, "No, shrimp! I am not for you to eat!" The shrimp was sad. But then, the wrap opened up and there was a toy inside! The shrimp was happy. It played with the toy and forgot about being hungry. The shrimp and the wrap became best friends.

One day, a lovely cat named Bella went for a walk. She saw a bench and wanted to sit on it. So, she jumped up onto the bench. Bella was very happy sitting there. A little boy named Tim saw Bella on the bench. He said, "Hi, lovely cat! Can I sit with you?" Bella said, "Yes, you can sit with me. I like new friends." So, Tim sat on the bench with Bella. As they were talking, a big wind came. It made Tim slip off the bench. Bella was surprised! She jumped down to help Tim. They both laughed and became best friends. They played together all day and had lots of fun.

One day, a little cat named Tim went outside to play. It started to rain. The rain was persistent. Tim did not like getting wet, but he wanted to play. Tim saw a big tree and ran to it. He used his strong legs to climb the tree. The tree kept him dry from the rain. Tim felt happy and safe in the tree. While Tim was in the tree, he saw a little bird. The bird had a hurt leg. Tim wanted to help the bird. He picked up the bird with his mouth and climbed down the tree. Tim took the bird inside his warm and dry house. The little bird was thankful for Tim's help. They became good friends and played together even when it stopped raining.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Sarah. She was three years old. She was very healthy and had lots of friends. One day Sarah and her friends went to the park. Sarah lost her balloon while they were playing and she couldn't find it. She was sad and felt like crying. But then she found something else - a big green button! Sarah excitedly picked up the button and showed it to her friends. She decided to keep it. Later that day, Sarah put the button in her pocket and went home. But when Sarah arrived home, she could not find the button. She had forgotten it somewhere. Sarah almost cried but then she remembered that she was very healthy and strong, and didn't need to cry. She was sure she'd find the button again one day.
