Category: Intermediate
Title: The Brown Vehicle and the Girl

Once upon a time, in a big town, there was a brown vehicle. This vehicle was very sad. It did not know where it belonged. It saw many other vehicles, but they were not brown like it. One day, the brown vehicle saw a little girl. The little girl was looking for something. She was very sad too. The brown vehicle wanted to help the little girl. The vehicle thought maybe they could belong together. The brown vehicle went to the little girl. The girl was very happy. She said, "I was looking for you! You are my brown vehicle!" The brown vehicle was happy too. Now, they both belonged together. They went for a ride and had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the beach with her mom and dad. They played in the sand and splashed in the water. Mia liked to feel the wet sand on her feet. She saw a small oyster in the sand. Mia picked it up and showed it to her mom and dad. Her mom said, "Let's put the oyster in the sun to dry." They put the oyster on a big rock and watched it. Mia was very alert, looking at the oyster all the time. She didn't want it to get lost or taken by a bird. After a while, the oyster was dry. Mia picked it up and put it in her pocket. She was very happy to have a new friend. They went home and Mia showed the oyster to her toys. They all had a fun day at the beach.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big dog with a ball in its mouth. Tim wanted to play with the dog and the ball. But his mom said, "No, Tim. That dog might be dangerous." Tim did not listen to his mom. He went to the dog and took the ball from its mouth. The dog did not like that. It jumped on Tim and bit his waist. Tim cried out loud. Mom came running and took Tim away from the dog. She said, "I told you the dog was dangerous, Tim." Tim learned his lesson. He knew he should listen to mom next time. And the twist was that the dog was not dangerous at all. It just wanted to play with Tim, but Tim scared it when he took the ball.

Once upon a time there was a tired goose called Molly. She was a grey goose and was tired because she had been walking all day. One day, Molly decided to go for a walk in the meadow. She was looking for something to eat and for somewhere nice to rest. Suddenly, Molly heard another goose calling out to her. It was a white goose and it said: "Hello Molly! Are you tired? I am too!". Molly looked at the white goose and said: "Yes I am tired. I have been walking for a long time." The white goose replied: "Why not stay with me? We can take turns resting and eating. That way we will both become less tired." Molly thought about it and smiled. This was a nice idea. She said: "Yes, let's do that!" So, Molly and the white goose became friends. They ate and rested together, and soon they were both happy and not so tired anymore. They were a perfect pair!

Jack, a three year old, was very excited. He was going for a ride in the van. He was bouncing up and down as his mom opened the door. "Do you want to bounce around in there, Jack?" asked his mom. Jack nodded enthusiastically. "Yes!" he said with a big smile. His mom smiled back. "Okay, but don't bounce too much, okay?" Jack nodded and hopped into the van. He bounced around for a few minutes, but his mom reminded him a few times to keep his bouncing normal. They drove around the city together, and Jack enjoyed the ride. He looked out the window, admiring the sights and the buildings, and he would occasionally bounce in his seat to get comfortable. When they got back home, Jack thanked his mom and they both laughed. "That was so much fun, Jack!" said his mom as she hugged him. Jack nodded and smiled. He had a wonderful time bouncing around in the van.
