Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Magic

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and chase each other. They also liked to look for bugs and flowers and rocks. They had a lot of fun. One day, they found a big circle made of metal. It was shiny and had a hole in the middle. They wondered what it was and what it could do. They tried to roll it, but it was too heavy. They tried to wear it, but it was too big. They tried to sing to it, but it did not answer. "Maybe it is a magic circle," Lily said. "Maybe it can unlock something special." "How can we unlock it?" Ben asked. "Maybe we need a key," Lily said. "Or a code. Or a wish." They looked around for a key, but they did not find one. They tried to guess a code, but they did not know any. They closed their eyes and made a wish, but nothing happened. They were about to give up, when they saw a graceful bird flying in the sky. It was white and had long wings and a long tail. It looked like a swan. It flew over the circle and dropped something shiny in the hole. Lily and Ben ran to the circle and picked up the shiny thing. It was a key. It had a feather on it. They put the key in the hole and turned it. The circle made a click sound and opened. Inside, there was a treasure. It was a necklace with a swan pendant. It sparkled in the sun. "Wow!" Lily and Ben said. "It is a magic circle. And a magic bird. And a magic necklace." They put the necklace around Lily's neck and thanked the bird. The bird nodded and flew away. Lily and Ben hugged and smiled. They had a new adventure and a new friend. They were very happy.

Anna was a big girl. She did not wear diapers anymore. She used the toilet like Mommy and Daddy. But sometimes she forgot. Sometimes she was too busy playing or reading or watching TV. Then she felt wet and dirty. She felt miserable. One day, Anna was playing with her dolls. She made them talk and sing and dance. She did not hear Mommy call her. She did not feel her tummy hurt. She did not go to the toilet. She had an accident. She cried and cried. Mommy came and saw Anna. She was not angry. She was not sad. She was gentle. She hugged Anna and said, "It's OK, my love. Accidents happen. I understand. You are still learning. You are still a big girl. Let's go and change your clothes and clean you up. Then we can play some more." Anna felt better. She said sorry to Mommy. She said thank you to Mommy. She went to the toilet with Mommy. She changed her clothes and washed her hands. She smiled and hugged Mommy. She said, "I love you, Mommy. You are the best." Mommy smiled and hugged Anna. She said, "I love you, too, Anna. You are the best." They went back to play with the dolls. They had fun. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly boy named Tim. Tim was always happy and loved to play with his toys. One day, he found a big book with many words. Tim wanted to understand the words in the book, but they were too hard for his little brain. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, can you help me understand this book?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Let's read it together." So, they sat down and started to read the big book. As they read, Tim's brain began to understand the words. As they read more and more, Tim became even more jolly. He was so happy to understand the big book with his mom's help. They laughed and had a great time reading together. And from that day on, Tim's brain grew bigger and he could understand even more words.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to jog. He jogged every day. One day, it was very dry outside. Spot knew that when it was dry, he needed to drink water. But he forgot to drink water before his jog. As Spot jogged, he got very thirsty. He saw a big tree and thought there might be water there. But there was no water. Spot was sad. He knew he needed to find water to prevent feeling bad. Spot saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a water bottle. Spot barked and Sue understood. Sue gave Spot some water. Spot drank the water and felt much better. Now, Spot knew he should always drink water before his jog. And he and Sue jogged together every day.
