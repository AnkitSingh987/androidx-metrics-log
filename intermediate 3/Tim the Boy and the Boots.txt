Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Boots

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a pair of big, reliable boots. Tim loved his boots. One day, it rained a lot. Tim put on his boots and went outside to play. Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam did not have boots. Sam said, "Can I try your boots, Tim?" Tim said, "No, they are my boots." Sam was sad, but Tim did not care. He played in the water and his boots kept his feet dry. Then, a big wind came. It blew Tim's boots away. Tim tried to catch them, but he could not. His feet got wet and cold. Sam did not help Tim. Tim was sad. He lost his boots and his friend.

One day, a dog named Max was playing outside. The sky was dark and a storm was coming. Max heard a loud boom and felt scared. He saw a little bird in a tree. The bird was also scared. Max wanted to help the bird. Max called out, "Bird, come down! I will rescue you from the storm!" The bird looked at Max and said, "Thank you, Max! But I can't fly in this rough wind." Max thought hard and had an idea. He found a big leaf and held it up. The bird jumped onto the leaf. Max and the bird went inside Max's house. They were safe from the storm. The bird said, "I have a secret. I am a magic bird!" The bird waved its wings and a yummy treat appeared. Max and the bird shared the treat and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite toy, a rubber ball. He loved to play with it every day. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sally. Sally was wearing a pretty dress. Tim wanted to play with his rubber ball, but it was filthy. "Hi, Sally!" said Tim. "Do you want to play with my rubber ball?" Sally looked at the filthy ball and said, "No, thank you, Tim. I don't want to get my pretty dress dirty." Tim felt sad. He went home and asked his mom to help him clean the rubber ball. His mom showed him how to wash it with soap and water. Soon, the ball was clean and shiny. The next day, Tim saw Sally again. "Look, Sally! My rubber ball is clean now. Do you want to play?" Sally smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! Let's play together!" The moral of the story is: If you take care of your things and keep them clean, others will be happy to share and play with you.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to her garden. She saw a big cauliflower. But this cauliflower was not white like others. It was pink! Lily could not believe her eyes. She called her mom to see the pink cauliflower. Lily's mom came outside and looked at the pink cauliflower. She was surprised too. "How did this happen?" she asked. Lily thought for a moment. She said, "Maybe I can imagine it white again." So Lily closed her eyes and imagined the cauliflower was white. When Lily opened her eyes, the cauliflower was still pink. But something unexpected happened. All the other vegetables in the garden turned pink too! Now, they had a garden full of pink vegetables. Lily and her mom laughed and decided to make a big salad with their pretty pink vegetables.

One day in the dark forest, a little bunny started to explore. Suddenly, she spotted a shiny nail on the ground. She began to hop closer to investigate the nail. Before she could get to it, a mean fox appeared out of nowhere and grabbed the nail. He ran away with it quickly. But the bunny wasn't ready to give up that easily. She began to chase after him. She chased him for what seemed like forever until finally, the bunny was able to catch up to him. She scratched the fox's tail with her long nails and the fox yelped in pain, dropping the nail. Quickly, the bunny grabbed the nail and ran away with it. Finally, the bunny was safe and could keep the nail for herself. She was so proud of herself for keeping the nail!
