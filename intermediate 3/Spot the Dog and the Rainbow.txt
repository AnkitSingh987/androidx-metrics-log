Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Rainbow

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the sun. One day, a thick fog came and covered the whole town. Spot was scared because he could not see his home. Spot heard a voice say, "Don't be scared, I can help you." It was a big dog named Max. Max was envious of Spot because he was small and cute. But Max wanted to rescue Spot and be a good friend. They started to walk together in the fog, trying to find Spot's home. As they walked, the fog started to lift. Suddenly, they saw a big, beautiful rainbow in the sky. The rainbow led them right to Spot's home. Spot was so happy that he hugged Max and said, "Thank you for rescuing me!" Max felt happy too because he made a new friend. From that day on, Max was not envious anymore, and they played together all the time.

Robbie was a little boy. He wasn't used to the dark. He was frightened. One night he couldn't sleep. As he lay in his bed he heard a noise. He leaned over to listen. It was coming from outside. Robbie was scared but he wanted to find out what was making the noise. He slowly opened his bedroom window. He couldn't see anything, but he knew something was there. He held his breath and jumped out of bed. He leaned out of the window to look around. Just then the morning sun came out from behind the clouds. Robbie saw it. It was a big beautiful bird. Its wings were spread wide. Robbie was no longer frightened. He just watched the bird until it flew away. Then he smiled and jumped back into bed. Robbie finally felt ready to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to find new things. One day, he went for a walk to a distant place. He wanted to find something new to teach his friends. On his walk, Tim found a big, red ball. He was very happy. He thought, "I can teach my friends how to play with this ball!" But as he picked up the ball, something unexpected happened. The red ball started to talk! It said, "Hello, I am a magic ball. I can teach you new games!" Tim was very surprised. Now, Tim and the magic ball play and teach each other new games every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a birthday party to celebrate with his friends. He was very happy and excited. At the party, Tim saw a big box. Inside the box, there were many tools. Tim thought the tools looked fun to play with. But his mom said, "No, Tim. The tools are dangerous. Do not touch them." Tim did not listen to his mom. He wanted to play with the tools. So, when no one was looking, he took a tool from the box. He started to play with it, but it was very dangerous. Tim got hurt by the tool. His mom heard him cry and came running. She was very sad and scared. Tim's birthday party was not fun anymore. Everyone went home, and Tim learned that he should listen to his mom when she says something is dangerous.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He had a big bag with toys to play with. Tim was very happy. The sun was shining, but it was a little cold outside. Tim wore his warm coat. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue wanted to play with Tim's toys, so they played together. They had so much fun with the toys from the bag. But then, Tim's hat fell off his head. His hair was messy, and Sue laughed at him. Tim felt embarrass. He didn't like when people laughed at him. Tim's mom saw that he was sad. She told Tim not to worry about his hair. She said that everyone has messy hair sometimes. Tim felt better. He put his hat back on and continued playing with Sue. They played until it was time to go home. Tim learned that it's okay to be embarrass sometimes, and it's more important to have fun with friends.
