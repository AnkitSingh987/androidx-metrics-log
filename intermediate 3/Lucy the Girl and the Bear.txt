Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to attach things to her toys. One day, she found a pastry in the kitchen. It was very soft and smelled good. Lucy thought it would be fun to attach the pastry to her teddy bear. So, she put the pastry on the bear's head like a hat. Her teddy bear looked very comfortable with the pastry on its head. Lucy's mom saw what she did and said, "Lucy, that's a funny hat for your bear!" Lucy laughed and said, "Yes, Mommy, the bear likes it too!" They both laughed and enjoyed the silly pastry hat on the teddy bear.

One day, a big dog and a small cat were playing in a wide field. They were very happy. The big dog said, "Let's join the birds and play with them!" The small cat agreed and they both ran to the birds. As they played with the birds, a hurt bee came near them. The bee was very sad. The big dog asked, "Why are you sad, little bee?" The hurt bee said, "I can't fly with my friends because my wing is hurt." The big dog and the small cat wanted to help the hurt bee. They had an idea. They found a big leaf and put the hurt bee on it. Then, they ran very fast with the leaf so the hurt bee could feel like it was flying. The hurt bee was very happy and thanked the big dog and the small cat. They all played together in the wide field until the sun went down. From that day on, the big dog, the small cat, the birds, and the hurt bee were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a slow turtle named Tim. Tim loved to race with his friend, a fast rabbit named Rob. One day, they decided to have a big race to see who would win a shiny medal. Before the race, Rob said, "I am so fast, I will win for sure!" Tim just smiled and said, "We will see, my friend." They both started to run. Rob was very fast, and he went far ahead of Tim. But then, Rob saw some yummy grass and stopped to take a bite. He thought, "I have lots of time. I can eat and still win." Tim kept going, slow and steady. He did not stop to eat or rest. He just kept moving. Soon, he passed Rob, who was still eating. By the time Rob saw Tim, it was too late. Tim won the race and got the shiny medal. Rob felt sad but learned a lesson. He said, "You were right, Tim. Being slow and steady is better than being fast and stopping." The moral of the story is: Slow and steady wins the race.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy butterfly. The butterfly liked to dare other animals in the forest. One day, the butterfly dared a big cat to climb a tall tree. "Can you climb to the top?" asked the butterfly. The big cat said, "I can do it!" The cat started to climb the tree, but it was scared. The cat looked down and saw the nosy butterfly laughing. The cat got mad at the butterfly. "Why do you dare me?" asked the cat. The butterfly said, "I just want to have fun!" The cat did not like this. The cat and the butterfly talked about their feelings. In the end, the butterfly learned not to be nosy and dare others. The cat and the butterfly became friends. They played together in the forest and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a land of many seasons, there lived a little girl named Jane. Jane was an independent girl who loved to play outside. She liked to lead her friends on fun adventures in the sun, rain, and snow. One day, during a warm season, Jane and her friends went to the park. Jane led them to a big tree with lots of leaves. They played hide and seek, and had lots of fun. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. As the seasons changed, Jane continued to lead her friends on new adventures. They played in the leaves during the fall, built snowmen in the winter, and picked flowers in the spring. Jane and her friends were happy, and they loved exploring the world together.
