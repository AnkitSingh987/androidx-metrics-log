Category: Intermediate
Title: The Warm and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a warm, friendly sun. The sun liked to spread its light all over the land. It made the grass, trees, and flowers grow big and strong. The sun also made the people happy when they played outside. One day, a little boy found a watch on the ground. The watch was shiny and had a big smile. The boy put the watch on his wrist and ran to show his friends. They all liked the watch and wanted to play with it. The warm sun watched them play with the watch. It spread its light on the children, making them laugh and have fun. The sun was happy to see the children enjoy their day. And the little boy knew he had found a special watch that would always remind him of that warm, sunny day.

Once there was a boy called Tom who lived in a big house by the beach. One day, Tom leaned out of the big window of his house and looked towards the sea. He waved at the waves and the birds flying by. All of a sudden, they stopped mid-flight and a deep silence filled the air. It was wet outside and Tom was wet too. Tom frowned and said, "Why is it so quiet? Where did all the birds go? "Don't worry," said his mum. She was standing behind him watching. "Sometimes it gets too wet and the birds fly away. They'll come back soon." Tom sighed and leaned out of the window again. He smiled as he heard the birds come back and sing their happy tunes.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to read books. In her room, she had a big bookcase full of colorful books. Lucy was very grateful for her bookcase because it held all her favorite stories. One day, Lucy's friend, Tom, came over to play. "Come, let's read a book!" Lucy said. Tom looked at the bookcase and picked a book. They sat down and read the book together, laughing and having fun. But then, Lucy had an idea. "Let's climb the bookcase!" she said. Tom was scared, but Lucy said it would be fun. So, they started climbing. The bookcase shook and wobbled. Suddenly, the bookcase fell down with a loud crash. Books were everywhere, and Lucy and Tom were scared and sad. They knew they should not have climbed the bookcase.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with shapes. He had a big box full of different shapes like circles, squares, and triangles. One day, Tim saw a magic door in his room. He decided to enter the door. Inside, he found a world full of shapes! There were big shapes, small shapes, and even shapes that could talk. "Hello, Tim!" said a friendly square shape. "Welcome to our world of shapes! Do you want to play with us?" "Yes, I would love to play!" Tim replied with a big smile. So, Tim and the friendly square played with all the shapes. They had lots of fun and laughed together. After a while, it was time for Tim to go back home. "Goodbye, new friends!" said Tim. "I will come back to play again soon." And with that, Tim left the world of shapes and went back to his room. He couldn't wait to tell his mom and dad about his amazing adventure.

One day, a funny bird with big wings was flying in the sky. The bird was very happy and liked to play with other birds. They would fly up and down, and all around. They had a lot of fun together. One day, the funny bird saw a big fruit on a tree. The bird wanted to eat the fruit, but it was too big. So, the bird decided to split the fruit into two parts. It used its beak to split the fruit, and it was very easy. The funny bird shared the split fruit with its friends. They all ate the fruit and had a great time. After they finished eating, they played more games with their wings. The funny bird and its friends were very happy and had a wonderful day.
