Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, in a beautiful forest, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play with her friends, the animals. One day, while playing, Mia found a big axe near a tree. She didn't know what it was for, but she thought it was interesting. Mia showed the axe to her friend, Mr. Squirrel. "Look what I found!" said Mia. Mr. Squirrel looked at the axe and said, "Be careful, Mia. It can be dangerous." Mia listened to her friend and put the axe away in a safe place. Later, Mia and Mr. Squirrel found a door in the forest. The door was locked. Mia remembered the axe she found earlier. She used the axe to break the lock and open the door. Inside, they found a beautiful garden full of flowers and fruits. Mia and Mr. Squirrel played and ate in the garden all day, happy that they found such a wonderful place.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim had a broken wing and could not fly with his friends. He was sad and stayed in his nest all day. One sunny day, Tim saw a big slide in the park. He had an idea! Tim hopped and hopped until he reached the top of the slide. He found a soft feather there. Tim put the feather on his broken wing and smiled. Tim slid down the slide, fast and fun! He felt like he was flying with his friends. Tim played on the slide all day, laughing and happy. Now, Tim knew that even with a broken wing, he could still have fun.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to a park with his mom and dad. They wanted to have a fun day outside. Tim saw a big grill where his dad was making yummy food. Tim was very happy. While Tim was playing, he saw a little girl who looked sad. Tim wanted to help her feel better. He walked up to her and gave her a big hug. The little girl smiled and hugged him back. They became good friends and played together all day. Later, Tim's mom called them to come eat the food from the grill. Tim and the little girl ran to the grill and saw the yummy food. But Tim tripped and fell, making the food fall on the ground. Tim felt embarrassed, but his mom and dad hugged him and said it was okay. They all laughed and enjoyed the rest of their day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He had a little sister named Sue. They lived in a small house with their mom and dad. Tim and Sue liked to play together, but sometimes they would quarrel. One morning, Tim and Sue's mom made toast for breakfast. Tim wanted the bigger piece of toast, but Sue wanted it too. They started to quarrel about who should get the bigger toast. Their mom heard them quarrel and came into the room. She said, "Tim and Sue, please stop. We can break the toast in half, and both of you can have a piece." Tim and Sue agreed, and they stopped quarreling. They ate their toast and then went outside to play together happily.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a sad boy named Tom. He was always miserable because he could not find his toy axe. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. One day, while he was walking, he saw a big frog with his toy axe. "Please, Mr. Frog, can I have my toy axe back?" Tom asked. The big frog looked at him and said, "I will give you your axe, but first, you have to give me a kiss." Tom did not want to kiss the frog, but he wanted his toy axe back. So, he closed his eyes and gave the frog a little kiss. Suddenly, the frog turned into a kind fairy! "Thank you for your kiss, Tom," said the fairy. "Now, here is your toy axe." Tom was so happy to have his toy axe back. From that day on, he was not miserable anymore. He played with his toy axe and made many friends in the village.
