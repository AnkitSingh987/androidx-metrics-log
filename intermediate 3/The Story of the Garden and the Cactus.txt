Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Garden and the Cactus

Once there was a little boy. He had a long cactus. One day he wanted to get on top of the cactus. So he went to ask his mum for help. Mum: What do you want? Boy: Help me with the cactus. Mum: Okay, but don't lie on it. Boy: I won't. So the boy and his mum went outside to the cactus. His mum helped him stand on top of the cactus. He wasn't allowed to lie down but he could stand. He had a great view from the top of the cactus. But then he looked down and saw how high he was. He was scared! He wanted to lie down on top of the cactus. But he remembered what his mum had said. So he stayed standing and looked out over the garden. He had an amazing view and he was so happy!

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a big piano. A little girl named Mia loved to play the piano. One day, Mia's friend Tom came to visit. "Let's play the piano together," Mia said. Tom was honest and said, "I don't know how to play." Mia had an idea. She said, "I can teach you, Tom!" She suggested they start with a simple song. Tom liked the idea and sat down next to Mia. Mia showed Tom how to play the piano. They played a fun song together. They were so happy and laughed a lot. From that day on, Mia and Tom played the piano together every time they met. They loved making music and having fun.

Once upon a time there was a seal named Sean. He lived in the sea and had many friends there. One day Sean decided to part from his friends and go explore the world above the water. Everyone waved goodbye to him from the shore. Sean was very excited to explore the world, but he was nervous too. He swam for a long time and eventually made it to the shore. When he arrived, Sean was surprised by how terrible the world looked. He decided to go back into the sea and stay with his friends. When Sean got back to the shore, his friends were all disappointed. They wanted him to stay and explore the world. But Sean was too scared. He said, “The world is too terrible. I think it’s best that I stay here in the sea.” Even though his friends were sad, they understood why Sean wanted to stay in the sea. After all, it was his home, and it was much safer than the world above the water. So they all agreed to stick together and explore the world together. Sean and his friends swam out into the world and even though it was still terrible, it was much better when they did it together. They partied, explored and had a wonderful time. In the end, they all agreed that nothing was as wonderful as friendship — not even the world above the water.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the river with his mom. They wanted to have a fun day. Tim saw a fish in the river. The fish was hurt. Tim felt bad for the fish. "Mom, can we help the fish?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, we can help the fish. Let's be careful not to hurt it more." Tim and his mom took the fish out of the river. They put it in a big bowl with water. The fish started to feel better. Tim was happy that he could help the fish. After a few days, the fish was not hurt anymore. Tim and his mom went back to the river. They said goodbye to the fish and put it back in the river. The fish swam away, happy and healthy. Tim and his mom were glad they could help.

One day, a busy bee was flying around. He saw a big, red flower. He went to touch it. When he touched it, it began to talk! "Hello, Mr. Bee," said the flower. "You made me feel nice with your touch. Can you do it again?" The bee was very surprised. He had never met a talking flower before. He touched the flower again. The flower smiled and said, "Thank you, Mr. Bee!" Just then, a little girl saw the bee and the flower. She was so happy to see them talking. She started to clap her hands. The bee and the flower looked at her, and she said, "I want to applaud you both for being such good friends!" The bee and the flower were happy that the little girl liked their friendship. They all became friends and played together every day. And the little girl always applauded their fun times.
