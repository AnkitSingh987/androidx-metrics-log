Category: Intermediate
Title: The Furry Cat and the Cobweb

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat. The cat liked to play outside and explore. One day, the cat found an interesting thing - a cobweb! The cobweb was so big that the cat could part it with its paws. The cat did just that and felt the cobweb's softness. The cat was so happy that it started to play in the cobweb. It jumped, wiggled and rolled around in it. Suddenly, the cat heard a noise and it was time to go home. The cat sadly parted the cobweb and ran back home. But, the cat had fun and it knew it would be back for more fun soon. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big, fluffy dog named Max. Max was a good and obedient dog. He always listened to Lucy. One day, Lucy and Max went to the park to play. They saw a big, red ball. Lucy wanted to touch the ball, but Max knew it was not theirs. Max barked at Lucy to let her know not to touch it. Lucy listened to Max and did not touch the ball. They went home and Lucy's mom told her to shut the door. Max was happy and wagged his tail because Lucy was obedient too. They both had a fun day and went to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a big, reliable dog named Spot. Spot liked to read books. He had a big red book that he loved very much. Spot knew many things from his book. One day, while Spot was reading his book, a little cat named Whiskers came by. Whiskers wanted to play with Spot, but Spot did not want to stop reading his book. Whiskers was sad and mad. He wanted to have fun with Spot. Whiskers had an idea. He took Spot's book and ran away with it. Spot saw Whiskers take his book and ran after him. They ran and ran until they were both very tired. Spot caught up to Whiskers and got his book back. They both knew they were wrong. Spot said he was sorry for not playing with Whiskers. Whiskers said he was sorry for taking Spot's book. They both promised to be better friends. From that day on, Spot and Whiskers played together and read books together. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house near the big tree. He had no toys to play with, so he felt very sad. One day, Spot saw a barrel near the big tree. He thought it would be fun to play with the barrel. But the barrel was too heavy for Spot to move. So, he asked his friend, a strong horse named Max, for help. Max was very kind and wanted to help Spot. He said, "I will lend you my strength to move the barrel." Together, they pushed the barrel and it rolled around. Spot was so happy to have a new toy to play with. He thanked Max and they played together all day long.

Lily was a chubby girl who liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite one was a woman doll with long hair and a blue dress. She called her Wonder Woman, because she saw her on TV and liked how she helped people. One day, Lily was playing with Wonder Woman in her room, when she heard a knock on the door. It was her mom, who came to check on her. "Hi, Lily, what are you doing?" her mom asked. "I'm playing with Wonder Woman, mom. She is very strong and brave," Lily said. "That's nice, honey. Do you know what Wonder Woman does?" her mom asked. Lily thought for a moment. She wondered what Wonder Woman did when she was not on TV. Did she have a home? Did she have friends? Did she eat ice cream? "I don't know, mom. What does she do?" Lily asked. "Well, Wonder Woman is a hero, Lily. She fights bad people and helps good people. She also has a special plane that can fly very fast and a magic rope that can make people tell the truth. She is very smart and kind," her mom explained. "Wow, mom, that's amazing. I want to be like Wonder Woman when I grow up," Lily said. Her mom smiled and hugged her. "You can be anything you want, Lily. You are already wonderful, just like Wonder Woman," she said.
