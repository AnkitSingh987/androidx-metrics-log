Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toast

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very pretty with long hair. One day, she wanted to make some toast for breakfast. So, she asked her mom to help her. Lily's mom showed her how to make toast. They put the bread in the toaster and waited. When the toast was ready, Lily was very happy. But, she was sad too. She felt regret because she didn't say "thank you" to her mom. Lily knew it was important to say "thank you". She went to her mom and gave her a big hug. She said, "Thank you for helping me make toast, Mommy!" Her mom smiled and hugged her back. From that day on, Lily always remembered to say "thank you" when someone helped her. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely plane. The plane lived in a big field. The plane had no friends. It was very sad. One day, a little bird came to the plane. The bird said, "I believe you can be my friend." The plane was happy to have a friend. The bird and the plane played together every day. They were not lonely anymore. They were very happy. And they were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a small country, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to run, jump, and play all day long. He had many friends, like the cat, the bird, and the frog. One sunny day, Spot and his friends were playing in the park. They found a big, round ball. "Let's play with the ball!" said Spot. His friends agreed, and they started to play a fun game of catch. After playing for a while, Spot had an idea. "Let's bury the ball and find it later!" he said. His friends thought it was a great idea. They dug a hole, put the ball in it, and covered it with dirt. A few days later, Spot and his friends came back to the park. "Let's find the ball we buried!" said Spot. They all started to dig and dig. Finally, they found the ball and played with it again. From that day on, Spot and his friends loved to bury things and find them later. They had many fun adventures together in their little country. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big hole in the ground. Lily wanted to see what was inside. Ben was scared. "Come on, Ben. Let's go look. Maybe there is something fun in there," Lily said. She tried to encourage Ben to follow her. "No, Lily. It could be a trap. Maybe there is a bad animal or a mean person in there. We should stay away," Ben said. He did not want to go near the hole. Lily did not listen to Ben. She ran to the hole and looked inside. She saw something shiny and reached for it. But then she heard a loud snap. She screamed. She was caught in a trap. It was a metal cage with a bait inside. Someone had set it up to catch animals. Ben heard Lily scream and ran to help her. He saw her in the trap and felt sorry for her. He also felt angry at the person who did this. He looked around and saw a stick. He used the stick to lift the cage and free Lily. Lily was hurt and scared. She hugged Ben and thanked him. "Are you okay, Lily? That was a very bad trap. You should have listened to me," Ben said. He was glad he saved Lily. "I'm sorry, Ben. You were right. I was silly and curious. I should have stayed away. Thank you for helping me," Lily said. She was embarrassed and sorry. She learned her lesson. They ran back to their parents and told them what happened. Their parents were shocked and angry. They called the park ranger and reported the trap. They also checked Lily's wound and put a bandage on it. They praised Ben for being brave and smart. They hugged Lily and Ben and told them they loved them. Lily and Ben felt better. They decided to play somewhere else. They also decided to listen to each other more. They were good friends.
