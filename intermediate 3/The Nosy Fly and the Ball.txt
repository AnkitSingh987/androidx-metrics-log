Category: Intermediate
Title: The Nosy Fly and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little fly. The fly loved to buzz and look at everything. One day, the fly saw a big red ball in the park. The fly thought, "I want to play with that ball!" The next day, the fly went back to the park. The fly saw a girl playing with the big red ball. The girl would throw the ball up and then catch it. The nosy fly wanted to play too. The fly buzzed close to the girl and the ball, waiting for a chance to play. Finally, the girl got tired and dropped the ball. The nosy fly was so happy! The fly buzzed around the big red ball and played with it all day. The girl saw the fly having fun and smiled. From that day on, the girl and the nosy fly played with the big red ball together.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to a shop with her mom. They wanted to buy some coffee. Lily saw a brilliant red cup. She said, "Mom, I want this cup for my coffee!" While they were walking home, Lily tripped and fell. Her knee hurt and she cried. Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, it will heal soon." The next day, Lily woke up and saw her knee was better. She was so happy! She took the brilliant red cup and filled it with water. But, surprise! The cup turned into a big, funny hat! Lily laughed and said, "This is not a cup, it's a hat!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a magic mirror in her room. The mirror could change things when Lily looked at it. She liked to play with the mirror and change her toys into other toys. One day, Lily felt confused because her favorite toy was gone. Lily went to the magic mirror to find her toy. She thought the mirror could change and bring her toy back. But the mirror did not change her toy. Lily was sad and confused. She did not know what to do. Lily tried to change the mirror, but it did not work. She was very sad and cried. The mirror broke and her favorite toy was never found. Lily learned that sometimes, things do not go the way we want them to.

One day, a bald man went to the park to play. He liked to play with a big red ball. He threw the ball high in the sky and caught it. He was happy. Then, a big dog came into sight. The dog wanted to play too. The dog took the ball from the bald man. The man was sad. He wanted his ball back. The bald man had a plan. He found a stick and threw it far away. The dog ran after the stick. The man got his ball back. The dog came back and they played together. Now, they were both happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple car. The car loved to drive fast. But one day, the car got stuck in traffic. It could not move. The purple car was sad. Then, a nice man came to fix the traffic. He saw the purple car and wanted to help. The nice man waved his hands and told the cars to move. The traffic started to move again. The purple car was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The purple car turned into a purple bus! The purple bus could carry many kids. The kids were happy to ride the bus. The purple bus loved its new job. And they all lived happily ever after.

Karen and Johnny were playing outside when all of a sudden, they heard a strange sound. Karen said to Johnny, “Do you hear that? What do you think it is?” Johnny, who was feeling brave, said, “Let’s go find it.” The two of them then ventured over to a patch of grass and saw something very impressive. There was a blue car with black stripes that was making the noise. Karen and Johnny both noticed that the car was making the noise because of the gas being pinched. They saw the gas being pinched by the car’s wheel. Karen asked, “I wonder who it belongs to.” Johnny replied, “It must belong to a really special person.” The two of then ran around the car and saw that there was nobody inside. Karen asked, “How do cars even work?” Johnny replied, “I don’t know, but it’s really impressive!” The two of them admired the car for a few moments, before running off to play a new game.
