Category: Intermediate
Title: Jim and the Bird

Jim was an old man who was always very sad. One day, he saw a bright blue bird in the sky. He wanted it to be his friend. So, he folded his arms and wished for the bird to come and sit on his shoulder. To his surprise, the blue bird came to him and sat on his shoulder. Jim was so happy that he decided to give the bird some of his food. But when the bird ate the food, it flew away and never came back. Jim was so sad that he started crying. He could never forget the blue bird and wished it would come back. He tried to call it back so many times, but it never came. Eventually, Jim realised that he would never see the blue bird again.

Once upon a time, in a big red cave, there lived a little boy named Tim and a small rabbit named Hop. They were best friends and played together every day. The cave was their favorite place to play. One sunny day, Tim and Hop decided to have a race. "I bet I can win!" said Tim with a big smile. "Let's see who is faster!" Hop agreed and they started the race. They ran and ran, laughing as they went. As they reached the end of the race, Hop hopped a little faster and won the race. Tim was not sad, he clapped and said, "Good job, Hop! You are so fast!" They both laughed and went back to their red cave to rest. They were very happy to have each other as friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to collect things. One day, Mia went to the wide garden with her mom. In the garden, there were many pretty flowers and yummy fruits. Mia saw some red strawberries. "Mom, can I collect the strawberries?" she asked. "Yes, Mia. Let's collect them together," said her mom. So, Mia and her mom picked the strawberries and put them in a basket. After they collected all the strawberries, they went home. Mia and her mom washed the strawberries and ate them. They were so sweet and tasty. Mia was happy that she got to collect and eat strawberries with her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a big statue in a park. The statue was of a brave man. Every day, kids would come to the park to play and look at the statue. One day, a little boy named Tim was very enthusiastic to see the statue. He ran to the park with his mom. As Tim was playing near the statue, he saw a small cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and crying. Tim wanted to help the cat. He said, "Mom, we have to rescue the cat!" His mom nodded and they tried to think of a way to save the cat. Suddenly, the big statue came to life! The brave man from the statue stepped down and climbed the tree. He gently picked up the cat and brought it down to Tim. Everyone in the park was surprised and happy. The brave man went back to being a statue, and Tim knew that the statue was not just a statue, but a real hero.

Once upon a time, there was a little puppy. He liked to play hide and seek with his friend, a small boy. One day, they played in the big park. The puppy ran to find a hidden spot. The small boy started to look for the puppy. He looked behind trees and under benches. Then, he saw a big box. The box was close to a tree. The small boy said, "I found you, puppy!" But when the small boy opened the box, it was not the puppy. It was a big, mean cat! The cat jumped out and chased the small boy. The small boy was very scared. He did not find the puppy that day.

Once there was a folder. The folder was strong because it was made from tough, thick cardboard. The folder was bright red and every morning when the sun shone in through the window it looked like it was made from fire! The folder was a special folder because it wasn't just any old folder. It was Max's folder. Max was a very clever 3 year old who used it to carry his papers and things to and from school. Max was so very proud of his strong red folder. He used it everyday and took very good care of it. He kept it safe and clean inside his backpack when he wasn't using it. That's why the folder stayed strong and bright red - because Max loved it and took very good care of it.
