Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Crayon

One day, a girl named Lucy wanted to dress her toy bear. She looked for clothes that would fit him. It was difficult to find the right size, but she finally found a small shirt and pants. She dressed her bear and felt very happy. Lucy then decided to draw a picture of her bear with her crayons. She picked up a blue crayon and started to draw. As she drew, her bear said, "Hello, Lucy!" Lucy was surprised and asked, "Bear, can you talk?" The bear nodded and said, "Yes, I can talk when I wear these clothes!" Lucy and her talking bear became best friends. They played and talked every day. They had lots of fun together and shared many adventures. And it all started when Lucy dressed her bear in those special clothes.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He wore a red sweater because it was cold. Tim saw his friend, Sue, playing with a ball. He wanted to play too. "Can I play too?" Tim asked Sue. Sue was bad and did not want to share. She said, "No, go away!" Tim felt sad and walked away. Later, Tim saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play with Sue's ball. Sue tried to ignore the dog, but the dog took the ball and ran away. Sue was sad and cold. Tim saw Sue and said, "You can wear my red sweater and we can play together." Sue said, "Thank you." They became friends and played all day.

Once upon a time, in a land full of sunshine, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very hungry boy who loved to play outside with his friends. One day, he found a big sack in his yard. Tim was curious about the sack, so he opened it. Inside, he found lots of yummy treats! Tim wanted to eat all the treats by himself, but he thought about his friends who were also hungry. So, he decided to share the treats with them. Tim and his friends enjoyed the treats from the sack together. They were all very happy and full. The sunshine smiled down on them as they played and shared. Tim learned that it was more fun to share with his friends than to keep everything for himself. The moral of the story is that sharing makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy dog named Spot. Spot liked to sleep all day and play with his toy ball. One day, Spot saw his friend, a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy had a bandage on his wing. Spot felt sad for Chirpy and wanted to help. Spot thought of a plan to make Chirpy feel better. He went to find his toy ball. He knew that Chirpy loved to play with the ball too. Spot found the ball and showed it to Chirpy. Chirpy's eyes lit up with joy. They started to play together, and Chirpy forgot about his hurt wing for a while. As they played, the bandage on Chirpy's wing came loose. Spot saw this and knew what to do. He took the loose part of the bandage in his mouth and pulled it off. Chirpy was so happy that he could fly again. Spot and Chirpy played together all day, and Spot was not lazy anymore. They became the best of friends, and they always helped each other when they needed it.

Lily and Sam liked to play with sand. They had a big bucket and a small bucket. They also had a shovel and a rake. They went to the park with Mom and Dad. There was a lot of sand in the park. Lily and Sam wanted to make a castle with sand. They used the big bucket to fill with sand. They turned the bucket over and lifted it up. They made a big hill of sand. They used the small bucket to make more hills of sand. They put the hills around the big hill. They used the shovel and the rake to make the hills look like walls and towers. They made a castle with sand. Lily and Sam wanted to measure their castle. They used their feet to measure. They walked around the castle and counted their steps. They said, "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten." They measured ten steps around the castle. They were happy with their castle. Mom and Dad came to see their castle. They said, "Wow, what a big and beautiful castle! You did a great job!" They took a picture of Lily and Sam with their castle. Lily and Sam smiled and hugged. They were happy with their castle and their Mom and Dad. They had a lot of fun with sand.
