Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Oyster

One day, a girl named Sue went to the beach with her mom. They saw a dull oyster on the sand. Sue picked it up and said, "Mom, can I test if there's a pearl inside this oyster?" Her mom said, "Okay, but be careful." Sue tried to open the oyster, but it was very hard. She asked her mom for help. Her mom tried to open it too, but it was still too hard. They tried and tried, but the oyster would not open. A big wave came and took the oyster away. Sue was sad and cried. She never found out if there was a pearl inside the oyster.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a pet cat named Fluffy. They loved to play together. Tim's room was always tidy because he liked to keep his toys neat. One day, Fluffy went outside to play. She saw a big tree and climbed up very high. Fluffy looked down and saw she was too high to get back down. She was scared and started to shout for help. Tim heard Fluffy shout and ran outside. He saw Fluffy up in the tree and called for his dad. Dad got a long ladder and helped Fluffy get down. Tim was so happy to have his pet safe. They went back inside and played in Tim's tidy room.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong dog named Max. Max loved to dig and pull things out of the earth. One sunny day, Max was digging in the garden when he found a big, shiny rock. Max thought the rock was important, so he pulled it out of the earth and ran to show his friend, Lily the cat. "Look, Lily!" said Max. "I found a big, shiny rock!" Lily looked at the rock and said, "Wow, Max! That is a very important rock. Let's take it to our human friend, Tim, and show him." So Max and Lily ran to Tim and showed him the important rock. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Max and Lily! This is a very special rock. Let's keep it safe in our home." Max and Lily were proud that they found something so important, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ruby. She loved to watch all kinds of things. One day, she wanted to watch a pepper. She quickly ran to the garden, but when she got there the pepper was broken. Ruby was very sad. She ran to her mom and said, "Mom! The pepper is broken!" Her mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Ruby. I have another pepper." Ruby was relieved and said, "Yay! Now I can watch it!" So, Ruby and her mom went back to the garden. They found a big, juicy pepper and Ruby watched it carefully. She looked at its shape, color, and size. She watched it for a long time. Finally, she said, "Mom, I'm done watching the pepper!" Her mom laughed and said, "That's nice, sweetheart. Do you want to pick it and eat it now?" Ruby smiled and said, "Yes!" And so, she and her mom picked the pepper and enjoyed it together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dark Sunday. The sky was full of big gray clouds. A little girl named Lucy was sad because she couldn't play outside. Her mom told her that they could bake cookies together to make the day fun. Lucy and her mom got all the things they needed to make cookies. They put them on the table. But they saw that they had no eggs. Lucy's mom said they needed eggs to make cookies. She told Lucy, "Don't worry, I will go to the store and supply the eggs we need." While Lucy's mom was at the store, the dark clouds went away. The sun came out and it was a bright Sunday. Lucy was happy because she could play outside now. When her mom came back, they made cookies and played in the sun. The dark Sunday turned into a happy day.
