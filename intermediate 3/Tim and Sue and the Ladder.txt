Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Sue and the Ladder

Once upon a time, there was a tall ladder in a park. A little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue wanted to climb the ladder at the same time. Tim was rude and did not want to wait for Sue. He said, "I will climb the ladder first!" Sue was sad but she let Tim climb the ladder first. When Tim reached the top, he was scared to come down. He called for help. Sue saw Tim was scared and she wanted to help him. She said, "Don't worry, Tim! I will help you come down." Sue climbed the ladder and helped Tim come down. Tim was grateful to Sue and he learned that being rude is not good. He said, "Thank you, Sue. I'm sorry for being rude. I will not do it again." They became good friends and played together in the park. The moral of the story is to be kind and patient with others.

Once upon a time there was a noisy peacock. He spent his days walking around in the garden and shouting. "Hey! Look at me! I'm so beautiful!" he squawked. One day, a curious little girl decided she wanted to paint the peacock. She found some paints and set out to make him look even more beautiful. She painted his feathers in all kinds of beautiful colors, like peach and pink and red. When the little girl finished, the peacock was so amazed with how colorful he looked that he hopped up and down with joy! Instead of making lots of noise, he kept surprisingly quiet. The little girl knew she had taught the peacock a valuable lesson - when you look beautiful, you don't always have to be noisy to show it off. She had also taught herself something too - that no matter how small or quiet you are, it's always good to do something nice to make others happy!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play dress-up. She had a big box of costumes. She liked to be a princess, a pirate, and a bunny. One day, Sue put on her favorite costume. She was a very honest girl. She never lied to her mom or dad. She wanted to show her costume to her friends. So, she went outside to play. As she played, a magic bunny appeared. The bunny said, "Sue, I like your costume!" Sue was so happy. She played with the bunny all day. At the end of the day, the bunny went home. Sue knew she had a new friend.

One day, a funny dog saw a big aeroplane in the sky. The dog was very happy and wanted to show his friends. He ran to find his friends, a cat and a bird. The dog pointed to the sky and said, "Look! Big aeroplane!" The cat and the bird looked up and saw the aeroplane too. They all laughed and clapped their hands. They loved watching the aeroplane fly high in the sky. They played a game to see who could point to the aeroplane the fastest. The dog won, but they all had fun. They played until the sun went down, and the aeroplane was gone. The funny dog, cat, and bird went home, excited to play again tomorrow.

Tom and Lily are playing with their toys in the garden. Tom has a big truck with four wheels. Lily has a small doll with a dress. Tom likes to make his truck go fast and loud. Lily likes to make her doll talk and dance. One day, Tom sees Lily's doll and wants to play with it. He thinks it is pretty and soft. He grabs it from Lily and runs away. Lily is angry and sad. She shouts at Tom and chases him. She wants her doll back. Tom runs to the shed and hides behind the door. He holds the doll in his mouth and bites it. He thinks it is funny and tasty. He does not hear Lily coming. Lily sees the truck outside the shed. She has an idea. She takes one wheel from the truck and goes inside. Tom is surprised when he sees Lily. He drops the doll and tries to get the wheel. Lily is quick and throws the wheel at Tom. The wheel hits Tom on the head and makes him cry. Lily grabs her doll and runs out. She is happy and proud. Tom is hurt and sorry. He looks at his truck and sees it has only three wheels. He feels sad and silly. He wishes he did not bite Lily's doll. He goes out and says sorry to Lily. He asks her to be his friend again. Lily is kind and forgives him. She gives him back his wheel and they hug. They decide to share their toys and play nicely. They are happy and friends.
