Category: Intermediate
Title: The Swing and the Park

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was a swing. This swing was different from the others because it was very high. A little girl named Lily saw the swing and wanted to play on it. Lily tried to climb on the swing but it was too high. She struggled to reach it. Her friend Tim saw her and said, "Lily, be careful! That swing is too high for you." But Lily didn't listen and kept trying to climb on the swing. Finally, Lily managed to get on the swing. She started swinging very high. Tim was scared and said, "Lily, please stop! It's not safe!" Lily didn't listen and kept swinging higher and higher. Then, she fell off the swing and got hurt. Tim was sad and wished Lily had listened to him.

Tom and Lily are going to the airport with Mom and Dad. They are very happy because they will fly to Grandma's house. They have cheap tickets, so they can save money for ice cream and toys. "Look, Tom, there are so many planes!" Lily says, pointing at the sky. "Wow, they are big and loud!" Tom says, jumping up and down. "Come on, kids, we have to jog to the gate. Our plane is leaving soon," Mom says, holding their hands. "Okay, Mom, we will jog fast!" Tom and Lily say, and they start to run with Mom and Dad. They jog past many people and bags and shops. They see signs and numbers and colors. They hear noises and voices and music. "Here we are, gate 12. Let's show our tickets to the lady," Dad says, stopping at a desk. "Hello, lady, we are going to Grandma's house!" Lily says, smiling. "Hello, Lily, that's very nice. Here are your boarding passes. Have a nice trip!" the lady says, giving them cards. "Thank you, lady!" Tom says, taking his card. They follow the lady to a door. They go through the door and walk to a big plane. They climb up the stairs and find their seats. They buckle their belts and look out the window. "Are you ready, kids?" Mom asks, hugging them. "Yes, Mom, we are ready!" Tom and Lily say, excited. The plane starts to move and then goes up in the air. Tom and Lily wave goodbye to the airport and hello to the clouds. They are going to Grandma's house!

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty dress. It was a modern dress with many colors. She liked to wear it when she went out to play. One sunny day, Sue went to the park. She wanted to meet new friends. She played on the slide and swings. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, nice dog came up to her. The dog wanted to play too. Sue and the dog played together all day. They had so much fun. But then, the dog's owner came. The owner was Sue's mom! Sue did not know her mom got a new dog. Now, Sue had a new friend and a new pet. They all went home, happy and tired.

One day, a boy named Tim found a rake in his yard. He liked to play with the rake, because it helped him clean up leaves. Tim saw a squirrel in a tree and wanted to play with it. He picked up a small stone and tried to shoot it near the squirrel so it would come down. "Please don't shoot stones at me!" the squirrel said, looking scared. Tim felt sorry for scaring the squirrel. "I'm sorry," Tim said. "I just wanted to play with you." The squirrel came down from the tree and said, "It's okay, but we should be kind to each other. Let's play together without hurting anyone." Tim agreed, and they played together in the yard with the rake and leaves. They had a lot of fun, and Tim learned that it's important to be kind to others.

Once upon a time, there was a careful fisherman. He liked to bring fish home for dinner. He had a little red boat and a big net. One sunny day, the careful fisherman went to catch fish. He put his net in the water and waited. But instead of fish, he caught a big, shiny box. The fisherman was surprised! He opened the box and found a magic fish inside. The magic fish said, "I will bring you lots of fish if you let me go!" The careful fisherman let the magic fish go. From that day on, he always had lots of fish to bring home. And they all lived happily ever after.
