Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys on the floor. One day, Tim found a broken toy car. He wanted to fix it so he could slide it on the floor with his other toys. Tim asked his mom, "Can you help me fix the broken toy car?" His mom said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I don't know how to fix it. Maybe we can find a new one later." Tim was sad, but he still wanted to play. He tried to slide the broken toy car on the floor, but it didn't move like the other cars. It was too broken. Tim got very sad and cried. His mom came and hugged him, but she couldn't fix the broken toy car. Tim had to play with his other toys, and the broken toy car stayed broken. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a group of animals. They were very competitive. They liked to play games and see who was the best. One day, they decided to have a big race. The animals lined up at the start. The rabbit said, "Ready, set, go!" and they all ran as fast as they could. The rabbit was in the lead, but he saw a big hill up ahead. He knew he could not climb the hill, so he ordered the other animals to stop. The other animals were confused, but they stopped. They all looked at the hill and wondered what to do. Then, a small ant came up to them and said, "I can help you climb the hill. Just follow me." The animals followed the ant up the hill, and they were all able to finish the race. In the end, the animals learned that sometimes it is better to work together than to be competitive. They thanked the ant for helping them and became friends. From that day on, they always helped each other and had fun in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a strong ant named Andy. Andy was getting ready to prepare for a big party. He made a sign that said, "Party Here!" so all his friends would know where to go. The day of the party, Andy put the sign up in the grass. He was so excited to see his friends. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew very hard and took the sign away! Andy was sad, but he had an idea. He asked his strong friend, Benny the Beetle, to help him find the sign. They looked and looked, and finally found the sign stuck in a tree. Benny used his strong legs to push the sign out of the tree. The party was saved, and all the friends had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a tight small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy gun. He would shoot at the trees in his yard. His favorite tree to shoot at was the big pine tree. One day, while Tim was playing, a squirrel came by. The squirrel was running up and down the pine tree. Tim thought it would be fun to shoot at the squirrel. But the squirrel did not like this game. The squirrel felt scared and angry. The squirrel decided to get back at Tim. It jumped down from the pine tree and ran into Tim's house. The squirrel made a big mess in the tight small house. When Tim's mom came home, she saw the mess and was very upset. She took away Tim's toy gun, and Tim was sad. The end.

One day, a nice girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to turn around it. As she walked, she found a small box. Sue was very happy and said, "Wow! What is this?" Sue opened the box and saw a pretty dress. She put it on and said, "I look nice! This is fashion!" Sue wanted to show her mom, so she ran back home. On the way, she met her friend, Tom. Tom looked at Sue and said, "Sue, you are a fashion star!" But then, something unexpected happened. The dress started to turn colors! First, it turned red, then blue, and then green. Sue and Tom were very surprised. They didn't know what to do. They decided to take the dress off and put it back in the box. They learned that sometimes, things can be fun and strange at the same time.
