Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Kite

One day, Tom had a different kite. He wanted to provide fun for his friends. So, he went to the park with his kite. He saw his friends there too. They were all happy to see Tom's different kite. Tom and his friends played with the kite. They took turns flying it. The kite went up and up in the sky. They were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The kite changed colors! It went from red to blue, then to green. The friends were surprised and excited. They didn't know that Tom's different kite could do that. They all laughed and played more. The different kite made their day even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, it rained a lot. The rain made a lot of mud in Tim's yard. Tim was very excite to play in the mud. Tim put on his boots and went outside. He saw a gentle dog near the mud. The dog wagged its tail and barked. Tim said, "Hi, dog! Do you want to play in the mud with me?" The dog barked again and jumped into the mud. Tim and the dog played in the mud all day. They laughed and splashed. They made mud pies and threw them at each other. At the end of the day, they were both very dirty, but they had so much fun.

Sam likes to create things with his blocks. He builds towers, bridges, and cars. He has many blocks of different colors and shapes. He likes to show his mom and dad what he creates. One day, Sam wants to create a big castle. He uses all his blocks to make the walls, the towers, and the gate. He is very proud of his castle. He wants to play with it and pretend he is a king. But when he tries to move his castle, it is too heavy. He pushes and pulls, but it does not move. He tries harder, but he feels a pain in his arm. He cries out loud. He has hurt his arm. His mom hears him and comes to see what is wrong. She sees his castle and his arm. She hugs him and says, "You created a wonderful castle, Sam. But it is too big to move. You need to exercise your arm gently, not force it. That will make it feel better." She helps him take apart his castle and put his blocks away. She gives him some ice for his arm and a kiss. She says, "Next time, you can create a smaller castle, or ask for help to move it. Then you will not hurt yourself." Sam nods and says, "OK, mom. I love you. And I love to create things with my blocks."

One day, a big red ambulance came to the zoo. It was there to help a sick elephant. The elephant was very sad and needed help. A careless monkey was playing near the elephant. The monkey jumped and laughed. The elephant said, "Please be careful, monkey. I do not feel well." Two doctors came to weigh the elephant. They wanted to know how big he was. The elephant stood on a big scale. The doctors said, "You are very heavy, but we can help you." The doctors gave the elephant some medicine. Soon, the elephant felt better. The monkey was not careless anymore. They all played together, and the ambulance went away.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a bedroom with lots of toys. He liked to play with his toys and make a big mess. One day, Tim's mom told him to clean his bedroom. Tim was not happy about this. He didn't know why his mom wanted him to clean his room. He was ignorant about how important it was to keep his room clean. Tim started to clean his bedroom. He picked up his toys and put them away. But then, he found a big piece of paper. Tim thought it would be fun to tear the paper into little pieces. So he did. He tore the paper into many small pieces and threw them all over his bedroom floor. Now his room was even messier than before! Tim's mom came into the bedroom and saw the torn paper everywhere. She was not happy with Tim. She told him that he needed to clean his room again, and this time, he had to pick up all the torn paper too. Tim was sad, but he knew he had to do it. So he picked up all the torn paper and put his toys away. In the end, Tim learned that it was important to keep his room clean and not make a bigger mess.
