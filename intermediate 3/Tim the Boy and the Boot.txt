Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Boot

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to learn new things. One day, he saw a big boot in his room. He wanted to learn how to put the boot on by himself. Tim tried to put the boot on, but it was hard. He felt embarrassed because he could not do it. His mom saw him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom." Together, they learned how to put the boot on. Tim was happy and not embarrassed anymore. Now, he could put his boot on all by himself. Tim and his mom were very proud of what they learned.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant named Andy. Andy was an independent ant. He liked to do things all by himself. He lived in an underground home with his family. One day, Andy found a big hill. He wanted to push a ball to the top of the hill. Andy tried to push the ball, but it was too big. He tried and tried, but the ball would not move. Andy's friends saw him trying to push the ball. They came to help him. Together, they all pushed the ball up the hill. Andy learned that sometimes it is okay to ask for help. And they all played happily on the hill with the ball.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to eat yummy food. One day, Lucy found a big, yummy cake on the table. She wanted to eat it all by herself. She looked around and saw no one. So, she took a big bite with her thumb and said, "Yummy! This is the best cake ever!" Just then, Lucy heard a tiny voice. It was a little ant who said, "Please do not eat our cake! We worked hard to make it." Lucy was very surprised. She did not know that the cake was made by the ants. The ants were scared that Lucy would eat their cake. They wanted to escape with the cake. Lucy felt bad for the ants. She said, "I am sorry, little ants. I did not know this was your cake. I will not eat it anymore." The ants were very happy. They thanked Lucy and quickly took their cake away to escape. Lucy learned a lesson that day. She learned that she should not take things that do not belong to her. And the ants learned that sometimes, big people can be kind too. Everyone was happy, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a friendly dog named Max. Max had a big, leather ball that he loved to play with. He would run and jump with the ball all day long. One sunny day, Max met a little girl named Lucy. Lucy saw Max's leather ball and wanted to play with it too. Max thought about it and decided to share his ball with Lucy. They took turns throwing and catching the ball, laughing and having fun together. "Thank you for sharing your ball with me, Max!" Lucy said with a big smile. Max wagged his tail and barked happily. From that day on, Max and Lucy became the best of friends, always playing and sharing their toys with each other. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, black dog named Max. Max lived with a nice man who went to work every day. Max liked to bring things to the nice man when he came home from work. One day, Max found a black ball in the yard. He was very happy and wanted to show the nice man. Max waited all day for the nice man to come home from work. When the nice man came home, Max ran to him with the black ball in his mouth. Max said, "Look what I found!" The nice man smiled and said, "Good job, Max! Let's play with the black ball." Max and the nice man played with the black ball all evening. They had lots of fun, and Max was very happy that he could bring the black ball to the nice man.
