Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Boy and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Sam. Sam loved to spend time at the gym with his mom and dad. They would play games, run, and jump. Sam was very happy when they went to the gym. One day, Sam saw a naughty dog at the gym. The dog was running and barking. Sam's mom said, "Sam, don't go near the naughty dog." But Sam wanted to play with the dog. He thought it would be fun. Sam went to the dog and said, "Hello, dog. Let's play!" The naughty dog stopped barking and started to play with Sam. They ran and jumped together. Sam's mom and dad watched them play and smiled. The naughty dog was not naughty anymore. Sam and the dog became good friends and they all had fun at the gym.

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman. He liked to catch fish on perfect, sunny days. One day, he went to the river to catch some fish. He saw a big fish and tried to catch it, but the big fish swam away. The fisherman was sad. He tried and tried to catch the big fish, but it was too fast. He felt like he would never catch it. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and delayed the big fish by splashing water on it. The fisherman saw his chance. He threw his net and caught the big fish. He was so happy! The big bird helped him catch the big fish. They became good friends and went fishing together every perfect day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a modern house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Max. One day, Lily found a loose tooth in her mouth. She was very excited because she knew the Tooth Fairy would come to visit her. That night, Lily had an idea. She took a string and attached it to her tooth. Then, she tied the other end of the string to Max's collar. "Max, you can help me," she said. Max wagged his tail and smiled at Lily. When Lily's mom came into the room, she saw the string attached to Max and Lily's tooth. "Oh, Lily, we don't need to do that," her mom said, laughing. She gently helped Lily take the string off her tooth and Max's collar. Lily's tooth fell out on its own a few days later, and the Tooth Fairy left her a shiny coin under her pillow.

Tom and Lily were playing in the yard when they saw dark clouds in the sky. They heard a loud noise like a big train. It was a hurricane! "Come inside, quick!" Mom shouted from the door. "The hurricane is coming!" Tom and Lily ran to the house. They were scared. The wind was very strong and it made things fly in the air. They saw a tree fall down and a car move by itself. Mom took them to the basement. She gave them blankets and toys. She said they had to stay there until the hurricane was over. "Will we be okay, Mom?" Lily asked. "Yes, we will be okay," Mom said. "We just have to kneel and pray and wait for the storm to pass." Tom and Lily knelt with Mom and held her hands. They prayed for the hurricane to go away and for everyone to be safe. They heard the noise get quieter and quieter. After a long time, Mom said they could go upstairs and see what happened. They went to the living room and looked out the window. The sky was blue and the sun was shining. The yard was messy, but the house was still standing. "Look, Mom, look!" Tom pointed to the street. "There is a chubby dog!" They saw a chubby dog with brown fur and a red collar. He was wagging his tail and looking for someone. "Maybe he lost his home in the hurricane," Lily said. "Can we keep him, Mom?" Mom smiled and said they could. She said they would call him Lucky, because he was lucky to survive the hurricane. Tom and Lily were happy. They hugged Lucky and gave him some food and water. They decided to share their toys with him. They thanked God for keeping them safe and for sending them a new friend. They hoped the hurricane would never come back.
