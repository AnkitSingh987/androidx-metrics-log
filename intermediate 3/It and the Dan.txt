Category: Intermediate
Title: It and the Dan

It was a sunny day. Dan was playing in the backyard. He saw a spark hovering in the air. What was it? Dan asked his mom, "Mommy, what is that?" His mom replied, "That is a firefly, Dan. A spark that brings joy." Dan smiled and asked, "Can I catch it?" His mom nodded and watched as he reached his arm out. He caught it carefully and the spark beamed brightly in his hands. He whispered, "I'm reliable. I will take good care of it." The firefly seemed happy to be in Dan's hands. Dan smiled and ran around the yard, chasing after the spark as it flew around. He laughed as the spark moved from one hand to the other. His mom watched him play and smiled at the happy scene.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Stretch. Stretch loved to keep his toys organized. He liked to play with his toys and then put them back in their place. Stretch was a good cat. One day, Stretch found a new toy. It was a big, red ball. He played with it all day long. But Stretch started to feel tired. He took a deep breath with his lungs and decided to take a nap. When Stretch woke up, he saw that his toys were no longer organized. The big, red ball had moved all of his toys while he slept! Stretch was sad, but he learned that sometimes things don't always stay the same. So, Stretch put his toys back in their place and played with the big, red ball again.

One day there was a reliable gorilla. He wanted to give something special to his friend. He thought and thought but nothing came to mind. Suddenly, he had an idea! He decided to give his friend a surprise party. He gathered all of his friends together and they decorated a room with lots of balloons and streamers. Once the decorations were finished they were ready to surprise their friend. When the gorilla's friend came in, she was so excited! She was delighted with the surprise. Everyone laughed and danced and shared stories. It was a wonderful day for everyone, thanks to the reliable gorilla's giving heart.

Lily liked to help her mom with the laundry. She liked to put the clothes in the big machine and watch them spin. She liked to smell the soap and the softener. She liked to fold the warm clothes and put them in the basket. One day, her mom was very busy. She had to cook and clean and talk on the phone. She told Lily to play with her toys and not to touch the laundry. But Lily was bored. She wanted to help. She saw a bottle on the shelf. It had a picture of a flower on it. It looked like soap. Lily took the bottle and went to the laundry room. She opened the bottle and saw a button. She pushed the button and a mist came out. It smelled nice. Lily thought it was like perfume. She sprayed the mist on the clothes in the machine. She sprayed it on the clothes in the basket. She sprayed it on the floor and the walls. She sprayed it on herself. She felt happy. But the bottle was not soap. It was bleach. It made the clothes white and ruined them. It made the floor and the walls white and stained them. It made Lily's skin and eyes burn and hurt. Lily started to cry. Her mom heard her cry and ran to the laundry room. She saw the mess and the bottle and Lily. She was very angry and very scared. She yelled at Lily and took her to the bathroom. She washed her face and hands and put cream on them. She called the doctor and the dad. She said Lily was very naughty and very lucky. She said Lily could have been blind or dead. Lily felt very sad and very sorry. She wished she had listened to her mom and not touched the laundry.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was restless and could not sleep. Her mom gave her a small pill to help her sleep. Lily took the pill and went to bed. In her dream, Lily met a snowman. The snowman said, "I am very hot and I will melt if I don't find shade." Lily wanted to help the snowman, so they looked for a big tree to stand under. They found a big tree, but the sun was still too strong. The snowman started to melt. Lily tried to save the snowman, but she could not. The snowman melted away and Lily was sad. She woke up from her dream and cried, because the snowman was gone.
