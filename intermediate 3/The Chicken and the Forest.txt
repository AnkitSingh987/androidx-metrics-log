Category: Intermediate
Title: The Chicken and the Forest

Once upon a time there was a chicken. The chicken was modern and brave. One day the chicken was worrying about being on its own. The chicken worried about what might happen if it ever got lost. It couldn’t think of a way to help itself if something bad happened. But then one morning, the chicken saw something. It saw a bright and shiny light coming from the forest. Curious, the chicken went to investigate. When it reached the light, the chicken realized it was a big machine. Inside was a big button. The chicken pushed the button and the machine said, “I will help protect you if you’re ever in trouble.” The chicken was so happy! From then on, the chicken was never worried. It knew that the machine was always looking out for it. The moral of the story is: it’s important to be brave and prepared in case something bad happens. With a little creativity, you can find ways to help yourself!

Tim and Kim are twins. They like to walk with Mom and Dad in the park. They see many things: trees, flowers, birds, dogs, and people. They have fun and learn new words. One day, they walk to a big pond. They see ducks and geese in the water. They want to feed them bread. Mom and Dad give them some bread and tell them to be careful and gentle. Tim and Kim walk to the edge of the pond. They break the bread into small pieces and throw them to the ducks and geese. The ducks and geese come closer and make noises. Tim and Kim laugh and clap their hands. But then, Kim sees a big goose with a long neck and a black head. She thinks it is funny and wants to touch it. She walks closer and reaches out her hand. The goose does not like it. It snaps at Kim and bites her finger. Kim cries and drops the bread. The goose eats the bread and swims away. Mom and Dad run to Kim and hug her. They check her finger and put a band-aid on it. They tell her that she should not touch the animals without asking. They say that the animals may be scared or hurt by people. They say that she should respect the animals and their space. Kim says she is sorry and that she did not know. She says that her finger hurts and that her stomach is hungry. Mom and Dad say that they are sorry too and that they love her. They say that they are ready to go home and have lunch. They say that they will come back to the park another day and watch the animals from a distance. Tim and Kim hold Mom and Dad's hands and walk back to the car. They say goodbye to the ducks and geese and thank them for the show. They learn a lesson: to be kind and careful with the animals. They also learn a new word: goose.

Lily was a big girl now. She did not sleep in a crib anymore. She had a bed with a pink blanket and a teddy bear. But sometimes, she missed her crib. It was cozy and safe. One night, she heard a noise outside her window. It sounded like a big cat. She was scared. She wanted to go to her crib, but it was in the basement. She grabbed her teddy bear and ran to the door. "Mommy! Daddy! There is a scary cat outside!" she cried. Her parents came to her room. They looked out the window. They saw a black cat on the fence. It was not very big. It was just meowing. "That is not a scary cat, Lily. That is a friendly cat. He is looking for a home. Maybe he is hungry or cold," her mommy said. "Can we keep him?" Lily asked. Her daddy smiled. "Maybe. But first, we have to cut his nails. They are too long and sharp. He might scratch the furniture or hurt himself." He went to the kitchen and got a pair of scissors. He came back to Lily's room. He opened the window and called the cat. "Here, kitty, kitty. Come here. We have some milk and tuna for you." The cat jumped off the fence and came to the window. He was curious and hungry. He let Lily's daddy pick him up and hold him. He did not like the scissors, but he did not bite or scratch. He was a good cat. Lily's daddy cut his nails carefully. He gave him some milk and tuna in a bowl. He put him on Lily's bed. "Look, Lily. He likes you. He is purring. Do you want to pet him?" he asked. Lily nodded. She reached out her hand and touched the cat's fur. It was soft and warm. The cat rubbed his head against her hand. He licked her fingers. Lily smiled. She was not scared anymore. She liked the cat. She hugged him and her teddy bear. "Can we name him Crib?" she asked. Her parents laughed. "Sure, Lily. That is a cute name. Crib is your new friend. You can sleep with him tonight. But tomorrow, we have to take him to the vet and see if he is healthy and has a chip." Lily nodded. She did not know what a vet or a chip was, but she trusted her parents. She snuggled with Crib and her teddy bear. She closed her eyes and fell asleep. She dreamed of pink blankets and cozy cribs.
