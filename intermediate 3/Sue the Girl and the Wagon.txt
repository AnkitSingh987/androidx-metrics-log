Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Wagon

One day, a little girl named Sue found a red wagon. She was very happy. Sue wanted to play with her new wagon. She put her favorite toy, a small teddy bear, in the wagon. "Let's go for a ride, Teddy!" she said. As Sue pulled the wagon, it started to rock back and forth. Sue was worried. She did not want Teddy to fall out. Sue thought of a plan. She put a soft blanket in the wagon to help Teddy stay safe. Sue and Teddy went to the park. They saw a big hill. Sue wanted to go down the hill with the wagon. "Hold on tight, Teddy!" she said. As they went down the hill, the wagon started to rock again. But this time, it turned into a real rocking horse! Sue and Teddy were so surprised and happy. They had a fun day at the park with their magical wagon.

On this particular day, as she was out in the park, Jane heard something moving in the grass. She bent down and poked the grass with her finger, and out jumped a tiny green frog. Jane squealed with delight and scooped up the little frog. She said, "Hello there! What's your name?" The frog croaked in response. Jane poked the frog gently in its back and said, "Okay, then I'll just call you Froggy!" The frog seemed to understand and nodded. The two became fast friends and they spent the whole day together playing and laughing. Jane and Froggy ran around, tickling the tall grass and chasing dragonflies. Once upon a time, there was a furry cat. The cat was black. The cat liked to sort things. One day, the cat went to a big box. The box was full of toys. The cat wanted to sort the toys. The black cat met a dog. The dog was big. The dog said, "Can I help you sort the toys?" The cat said, "Yes, you can help me." The dog and the cat started to sort the toys together. They were happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came. The wind blew all the toys away. The cat and the dog were sad. They could not sort the toys anymore. The toys were gone. The cat and the dog went home.

Once upon a time, in a small, humble town, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to go up a hill and look at the pretty view. She saw trees, flowers, and her little town. One sunny day, Lily went up the hill to see the view. On her way, she saw a big rock. She wanted to climb it. But when she tried to climb, her foot did not stay on the rock. Her foot did a slip, and she fell down. Lily was sad, but then she saw something new. When she did the slip, she found a secret path. She went on the path and found a beautiful garden. The garden was full of colors and fun. Lily was very happy. The slip was not bad, because it showed her a new, pretty place.

One day, a jet flew high in the sky. It was a big, fast jet. The jet made a loud noise as it went by. A thin cloud was near the jet. The jet flew through the cloud. "Look, Mom!" said a little girl. "The jet made the cloud scatter!" The little girl was happy to see the cloud scatter. The thin cloud was now all around the sky. Then, something unexpected happened. The jet turned into a bird! The bird was big and pretty. It flew down to the little girl. "Thank you for watching me fly," said the bird. The little girl and the bird became friends. They played together all day.
