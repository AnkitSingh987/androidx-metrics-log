Category: Intermediate
Title: Billy the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Billy. Billy loved to fly high in the sky. One day, while flying, Billy saw a big tree. He thought it would be a good place to take a nap. So, he flew down and sat on a branch. While Billy was napping, a little girl named Sally walked by the tree. She looked up and saw Billy. "Hi bird!" she said, "Do you want to play with me?" Billy woke up from his nap and looked at Sally. He was grateful for the new friend. Billy and Sally played together all day. But then, something unexpected happened. Billy found out that he could not fly anymore. Sally helped him by giving him a big hug. Suddenly, Billy could fly again! They both laughed and played together, happy to have each other as friends.

Sara and Tom were twins. They liked to match their clothes. They had red hats, blue shirts, and yellow shoes. They wore them when they went on a tour with their mom and dad. The tour was to see the zoo. Sara and Tom loved the zoo. They saw lions, monkeys, and elephants. They made noises like the animals. They had fun. But one man on the tour was grumpy. He did not like the noise. He did not like the animals. He did not like the twins. He said, "Shh! Be quiet! Stop matching! You look silly!" Sara and Tom felt sad. They liked matching. They liked the animals. They liked the noise. They did not like the grumpy man. Their mom and dad saw them. They hugged them. They said, "Don't worry. You are not silly. You are cute. We like matching, too. We like the animals, too. We like the noise, too. We do not like the grumpy man, either. He is rude." Sara and Tom smiled. They felt happy. They matched their clothes. They saw the zoo. They made noises like the animals. They had fun. They ignored the grumpy man. He was alone. He was sad. He missed the fun.

One day, a girl named Amy went to her grandpa. She asked, "Grandpa, can you tell me a story?" Grandpa smiled and said, "Yes, I will tell you a story about a big war." In this war, there were two sides. One side had many people, and the other side had unknown friends. The unknown friends were very good at hiding. They would help their side win the war. One day, a boy from the side with many people met an unknown friend. The boy said, "We should not fight. Let's be friends." The unknown friend agreed, and they told their sides to stop the war. The two sides stopped fighting and became friends. They learned that they were not so different after all. From then on, they lived in peace and helped each other. And that was the end of the story.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big, round eye. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. But she started to worry because she could not find them. "Where are all my friends?" asked Lucy. A rude boy named Tom came up to her and said, "I don't know where your friends are, but I will play with you." Lucy did not like Tom because he was rude, but she did not want to play alone. As they played, something unexpected happened. Lucy's eye grew bigger and bigger. Tom saw this and started to worry too. He said, "I am sorry for being rude, Lucy. Please let me help you." They looked for Lucy's friends together, and they found them hiding behind a tree. They all laughed and played together. From that day on, Tom was not rude anymore, and Lucy's eye went back to normal size.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a modest flute. It was not big and shiny, but she liked it. She wanted to play it and make pretty sounds. Lily did not know how to play the flute, but she could pretend. Lily pretended to play the flute. She made fun sounds with her mouth. Her dog, Spot, liked the sounds too. Spot danced and jumped as Lily played her pretend flute. They had so much fun together. Lily and Spot played pretend flute all day. They made up songs and danced. They were happy and tired when the sun went down. Lily put the modest flute by her bed, ready for more fun tomorrow.
