Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Necklace and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl playing at the park. As she ran around, she saw an old man sitting under a tree. He smiled and said, "Come, little girl, come sit with me. I have something to show you." The little girl went and sat next to the old man. He softly pulled something out of his pocket, and held it up in the air. It was a small box. The old man smiled and said, "Open it, little girl." With a curious smile, the girl opened the box. Inside was a bright, shining necklace. The girl gasped in delight. The old man said, "This necklace will give you a special power: when you close your eyes and wink, anything you wish for can come true. But be careful with this power, and use it for good." The little girl thanked the old man, and put the necklace around her neck. She ran off, full of joy and excitement. The next day, the little girl did as the old man had said. She closed her eyes, winked, and imagined a world full of peace. She opened her eyes, and smiled - a world of peace was just where she was. The moral of the story is that happy things happen when you use your imagination and wish for good things.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park to play. He saw a big net on the ground. He wanted to spend time playing with it. Tom picked up the net and ran around, trying to catch things. He was having a lot of fun. Tom saw a little bird on a tree. He wanted to catch the bird with the net. Tom was careless and did not think about how the bird would feel. He ran to the tree and tried to catch the bird. The bird was scared and flew away. Tom's friend, Sally, saw what he did. Sally said, "Tom, you should not be careless with the net. The bird was scared. You should be kind to animals." Tom felt sad that he scared the bird. He knew Sally was right. He decided to be more careful with the net. From that day on, Tom spent his time playing with the net in a kind way. He did not try to catch any more animals. Tom learned that it is important to be careful and kind to all living things.

Sara and Tom are twins. They like to play outside with their toys. They have a big brown automobile that they can sit in and pretend to drive. They also have a ball, a kite, and a teddy bear. One day, they see a big balloon in the sky. It is red and yellow and blue. It is very pretty. They want to see it closer. They run to their brown automobile and get in. "Let's go to the balloon!" Sara says. "OK!" Tom says. He turns the wheel and makes a vroom-vroom sound. They drive their brown automobile across the grass. They go past the flowers, the trees, and the pond. They see the balloon getting bigger and bigger. It is very high. They stop near a fence. They get out of their brown automobile and look up. The balloon is right above them. They can see the people in the basket. They wave and shout. "Hello!" Sara says. "Hello!" Tom says. The people in the basket wave and shout back. "Hello!" they say. "Do you like our balloon?" "Yes!" Sara and Tom say. "It is very pretty!" "Thank you!" the people say. "Do you want to come with us?" Sara and Tom look at each other. They are excited. They want to go in the balloon. They nod and smile. "Yes!" they say. The people in the basket lower a rope. Sara and Tom grab it and hold on. The people pull them up. They climb into the basket. They hug their teddy bear. The balloon rises higher and higher. Sara and Tom see their brown automobile, their toys, and their house getting smaller and smaller. They see the clouds, the birds, and the sun. They feel the wind and the warmth. They are very happy. They go on a wonderful adventure in the sky.
