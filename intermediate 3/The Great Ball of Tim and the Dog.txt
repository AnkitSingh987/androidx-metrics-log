Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to question everything. One day, he saw a big, red ball in the park. It looked perfect to him. Tim wanted to play with the ball, but he didn't know who it belonged to. Tim asked his mom if he could play with the ball. His mom said, "I don't know who it belongs to, so we must be careful." Tim was very happy to play with the ball. He kicked it with his toe and laughed as it bounced around. Suddenly, a big dog came running and grabbed the ball with its teeth. Tim was scared and cried. The dog ran away with the ball and Tim couldn't play with it anymore. Tim's mom hugged him and said, "Sometimes, things don't go the way we want them to." Tim was sad, but he learned a lesson about being careful with things that don't belong to him.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a soft toy car. She loved the car and played with it all day. She drove the car around her room, making car sounds. Lucy's mom saw her playing and smiled. Lucy said to her mom, "I want to drive a real car like daddy!" Her mom laughed and said, "You need a license to drive a real car, sweetie. You are too little for that now." The next day, Lucy was playing with her car when she found a small card on the floor. It was a license! She couldn't believe it. She ran to show her mom. "Look, mom! I found a license! Can I drive now?" she asked. Her mom looked at the card and said, "This is a toy license, but we can pretend." Lucy insisted on trying to drive the real car. Her mom agreed to play along. They went outside to the car, and Lucy sat in the driver's seat. Suddenly, the car started moving! But it was just Lucy's dad pushing the car from behind. Lucy and her mom laughed and had fun pretending to drive.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a thin cat named Whiskers. They lived in a small house with a big tree in the yard. One day, Amy found a gift under the tree. It was a box with a big red bow on top. She opened the box and found a yummy cake inside. The cake was for her and Whiskers to share. Amy and Whiskers sat down to taste the cake. The cake was sweet and soft. They ate the whole cake and felt very happy. They played together all day and knew that they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a nice little town, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to organize her toys. One day, she built a toy volcano with her blocks. "Wow, Lily! Your volcano is so nice!" said her friend, Tom. They both liked to play with the volcano. They made it go "boom" and pretended to run away from it. One day, Lily and Tom had a great idea. They wanted to organize a volcano race. They asked their friends to join them. "Come play with us! We will have a volcano race!" said Lily and Tom. All their friends came, and they had a fun day racing around the nice volcano. They laughed and played until it was time to go home. Everyone said, "Thank you, Lily and Tom, for organizing the nice volcano race!"

One day, a big hippo named Bob was walking by the water. He saw a pretty rock and wanted to get it. But the ground was wet and muddy. "I have to be careful," he thought. Bob tried to be graceful like a cat. He walked slowly, step by step. He did not want to slip in the mud. He got closer and closer to the pretty rock. At last, Bob reached the pretty rock. But then, he slipped in the mud and fell down. He was not graceful like a cat. But he got the pretty rock and was happy. He learned that even big hippos can slip in the mud, but it's okay.
