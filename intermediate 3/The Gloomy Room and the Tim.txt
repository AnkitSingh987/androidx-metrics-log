Category: Intermediate
Title: The Gloomy Room and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a gloomy room. In this room, there was a big hook on the wall. A little boy named Tim lived in this house. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, can you help me number my dresses? I want to know how many I have." Tim was happy to help his mom. He went to the gloomy room with the big hook. Tim and his mom put the dresses on the big hook, one by one. As they put each dress on the hook, they said the numbers out loud. "One, two, three, four, five," they counted together. When they were done, Tim's mom said, "Thank you, Tim. Now I know I have five dresses!" Tim felt proud that he could help his mom. From that day on, the gloomy room with the big hook didn't seem so gloomy anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars and trucks. One day, Tim saw a big, dangerous bug in the kitchen. He was scared but wanted to be brave. Tim had an idea. He took his toy truck and a cup from the table. He struggled to put the cup on the bug. "I can do it!" he said to himself. The bug tried to run away, but Tim was fast. He caught the bug under the cup. Tim felt proud. "Mom, look!" Tim called out. His mom came into the kitchen and saw the dangerous bug under the cup. "Good job, Tim!" she said. "You were very brave." She picked up the cup and let the bug go outside. Tim and his mom were happy that the bug was gone, and they knew that Tim could be brave when he needed to be.

Once upon a time, there was a famous gem. It was beautiful, with sparkles of many colors. Everyone wanted to have it, but the gem changed its shape according to the person who owned it. One day, a little girl named Lucy found the gem and decided to keep it. She looked at the gem, and was so amazed that it had changed its shape. "Wow! How does it change?" Lucy asked the gem. The gem answered, "I can change if you wish for it. Close your eyes, and think of something you really want". Lucy did just that, and when she opened her eyes, the gem had turned into a ring. She was so excited to wear her new gem! Every day, Lucy would show her gem off to all of the kids in her school. They were all very impressed and wanted one too. After that, the gem became very famous throughout the town. Everyone soon wanted one, and they all wanted to find out how it could change. And that's how the famous gem changed the little girl's life forever.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a big fireplace. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, loved to sit by the fireplace to stay warm. One day, Tim's mom told him not to play with the fireplace. But Tim didn't listen. When his mom went to the store, Tim and Spot played near the fireplace. Tim felt guilty because he knew he shouldn't be playing there. Suddenly, they knocked over a log, and the fireplace broke. Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. Tim's dad came home and saw the broken fireplace. He wasn't happy, but he knew how to fix it. Tim helped his dad fix the fireplace and promised never to play near it again. Tim learned that it's important to listen to his mom and dad because they know what's best for him.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a job. His job was to shoot a ball into a hoop. He loved his job very much. Every day, he would go outside and play with his ball. One day, Tom met a new friend named Sam. Sam was deaf. He could not hear, but he could see and talk. Tom and Sam started to play with the ball together. They would throw the ball to each other and try to shoot it into the hoop. "Nice shot!" Tom said to Sam. Sam smiled and gave a thumbs up. They played all day long. Tom and Sam became the best of friends. They learned that even if someone is different, like being deaf, they can still have fun together. And they did, every day, shooting the ball into the hoop and laughing together.
