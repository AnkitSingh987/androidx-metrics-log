Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Clever Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a clever girl named Lily. She had a pretty bracelet that she loved very much. Lily wore her bracelet every day. One day, while playing, Lily saw a lost cat. She wanted to care for the cat and take it home. But she was scared that her mom would not let her keep the cat. So, she thought of a plan. Lily took off her bracelet and put it on the cat. She hoped her mom would see the cat and the pretty bracelet and let her keep the cat. But the cat ran away with the bracelet. Lily was very sad. She lost her pretty bracelet and the cat. The moral of the story is: do not use things you love to try to get what you want. It might not work, and you could lose something important.

Once upon a time, there was a prince. He was very intelligent. The prince had a big, red ball that he loved to play with. One day, the ball rolled far away and he had to find it. The prince went on a walk to look for his ball. He saw a little dog with a zip on its back. The dog was friendly and wanted to help the prince find the ball. The prince thought the dog was cute, so he said yes. Together, the prince and the dog found the big, red ball. They played with the ball and had lots of fun. The prince was happy that he had a new friend. They went back to the castle and played all day.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She had many toys, but she did not want to play with any of them. Mia felt envious of her friend Lily, who had a new toy that she did not have. She sat on her bed and said, "I wish I had a new toy like Lily." Suddenly, a kind spirit appeared in Mia's room. The spirit seemed to be friendly and asked Mia, "Why are you sad?" Mia told the spirit about her friend's new toy and how she wanted one too. The spirit smiled and said, "I can help you with that." The spirit waved its hand, and a big box appeared in Mia's room. Mia was very excited and opened the box. Inside the box was the same toy that Lily had! Mia was very happy and thanked the spirit. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy started to talk! It said, "I am not just a toy, I am a magical toy. I can make your other toys come to life." Mia was surprised but excited. She asked the magical toy to make her other toys come to life. The magical toy waved its hand, and all of Mia's toys started to move and talk. Now, Mia was not envious of Lily anymore. She had her own magical toy and her other toys had come to life. Mia played with her toys all day and had lots of fun. And the kind spirit watched from afar, happy to have helped Mia find joy in her own toys.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her favorite cord. She would jump and run with it all day long. One sunny day, Kitty met a little boy named Tim. Tim saw Kitty playing with her cord and asked, "Can I play too?" Kitty meowed and nodded her head. Tim was very happy and started to play with Kitty and her cord. They played together all day long. Kitty would chase the cord as Tim swung it around. They laughed and had so much fun. When the sun started to set, Tim had to go home. He waved goodbye to Kitty and said, "I had fun playing with you, Kitty. Let's play again soon!" Kitty meowed happily, and they both knew they had found a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to watch films on TV. His favorite film was about a big, fit dog who could run very fast and jump very high. One day, Tim saw a film that he wanted to watch. But the film was on the top shelf, and Tim was too small to reach it. He tried to jump, but he could not get it. He felt sad. Tim had an idea. He got a chair and stood on it. Now he was tall enough to reach the film. He took the film, put it in the TV, and watched it with a big smile on his face.
