Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Toys

One day, a little girl named Amy found a needle on the floor. She picked it up and saw it was very brilliant. It shined like the sun. Amy wanted to show her mom the needle, so she went to find her. In the kitchen, Amy found her mom. "Look, Mom! I found a needle!" she said. Her mom was very happy. "That's a special needle, Amy. It can make your toys bow to you. Do you want to try?" Amy was excited and said, "Yes, please!" Amy went to her room with the needle and her mom. She pointed the needle at her teddy bear and said, "Bow!" Suddenly, the teddy bear bowed to her! Amy was so amazed. But then, something unexpected happened. All the other toys in her room started to bow too! They all wanted to be friends with the brilliant needle. Amy and her mom laughed and played with the toys all day long.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a restless squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to run and jump from tree to tree. One day, while playing, Sam found a shiny acorn. He picked it up and showed it to his friends. Sam's friends were excited and wanted the acorn too. They started to fight over it. They were not happy friends anymore. Then, a wise old owl saw them fighting. The owl told them to stop and unite. He said, "If you work together, you can find many more acorns in the forest." Sam and his friends listened to the wise owl. They stopped fighting and started to work together. They found many more acorns and shared them with each other. They were happy friends again. The moral of the story is that working together and sharing is better than fighting over something.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to play catch with her friends. One day, Sue wore a big bow in her hair. She was very happy. Sue and her friends played catch in the park. But then, a big wind came. It blew Sue's bow off her head. Sue was sad. She wanted to find her bow. Sue and her friends looked for the bow. They looked high and low. At last, they found the bow in a tree. Sue was happy again. They all played catch and had fun.

One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sam. They liked to play with loops. They made crazy loops with string. They laughed and had fun. While they played, a big wind came. It took their loops away. They were sad. They did not know what to do. They looked at each other. Then, a bird came. The bird had their loops! The bird was their friend now. They played with the bird and the loops. They had more fun than before. The bird was the best friend they could have. They were happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gloomy day. The sky was full of dark clouds. In this town, there was a girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she wanted to play outside, but it was too gloomy. Lily had an idea. She had a magic book that could order the sun to come out. She opened the book and said, "Magic book, please order the sun to come out and make the day bright." Suddenly, the clouds went away, and the sun came out. But then, something unexpected happened. Instead of just one sun, there were two suns in the sky! The magic book made a mistake. The town was now very bright and warm. Everyone was happy and played outside all day. In the end, the gloomy day turned into a fun and sunny day for everyone.

John was a friendly little boy. He loved to wear white clothes. One day, he saw a very white bird. He wanted to be friends with it, so he put on his white clothes and went outside. He walked towards the bird and said "Hello! I'm friendly. Can we be friends?" The bird chirped in response and flew down to John. John was very excited. From that day on, the two were good friends. They spent every day playing and laughing together. John was so happy that he had a friendly white bird as a friend.
