Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Puppies and the Owner

Once upon a time, two puppies were standing in the middle of a big meadow. They were friends and were having a great time. One of the puppies barked, calling out to the jolly owner. The other pup followed, and soon the owner arrived. She was so happy to see them. The puppies jumped around and made the owner laugh. They ran and played until they both felt tired. The jolly owner said it was time to go home, so the puppies followed her. Along the way, they walked, sniffing and exploring. When they reached the house, the puppies were happy. They both ran inside, looking for their favourite blankets. The owner watched the puppies and smiled. They were so happy and it was so much fun watching them. When they settled down, the owner gave them each a hug and said goodbye. The puppies lay down to take a nap, they were content. Until the next time they were called, they were happy and jolly.

Once upon a time, there was a smart child named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys and learn new things. He had many friends who liked to play with him too. One day, Tim went to the park with his friends. They played on the swings, slid down the slide, and ran around in the grass. Tim felt very happy and excited to be with his friends. As the day went on, Tim started to feel tired. He said goodbye to his friends and went back home. Tim's mom gave him a big hug and told him how smart he was for knowing when to rest. Tim felt loved and proud of himself.

---

Tim and Sam were brothers who liked to play in the park. They ran, jumped, slid and swung on the swings. They had a lot of fun. One day, they saw a big cobweb on a tree. It was shiny and sticky. Tim wanted to touch it, but Sam said no. He said it was dirty and scary. He said there might be a wild spider in it. Tim did not listen to Sam. He reached out his hand to the cobweb. He wanted to feel it. But as soon as he touched it, he felt a sharp pain. He screamed and pulled back his hand. There was a red spot on his finger. A wild spider had bitten him. Sam ran to Tim and hugged him. He said he was sorry and he should have listened to Sam. He said they should go home and tell mom. Tim nodded and cried. He said he was sorry too and he should have listened to Sam. He said he did not like cobwebs or wild spiders. They held hands and walked home. They told mom what happened. Mom cleaned Tim's finger and put a band-aid on it. She said it would be okay. She said they should be careful in the park and not touch things they did not know. Tim and Sam said they understood. They said they loved mom and each other. They hugged and said they would still play in the park, but not with cobwebs or wild spiders.

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play together. One day, they found a video camera in Lily's room. They decided to make a video of themselves doing funny things, like making faces, dancing and singing. They had a lot of fun and laughed a lot. They did not know that Lily's big brother, Ben, was watching them from the door. He thought they were silly and dull. He wanted to embarrass them. He took the video camera when they were not looking and ran to the living room. He turned on the TV and played the video for everyone to see. He said, "Look at these two clowns! They are so dull and stupid!" Tom and Lily heard Ben's voice and ran to the living room. They saw their video on the TV and felt very embarrassed. They saw their parents, their grandparents and their cousins watching them and laughing. They felt their faces turn red and hot. They ran back to Lily's room and hid under the bed. They did not want to talk to anyone. They felt sad and angry. They wished they had never made the video. They wished Ben had never seen it. They wished they could erase it.
