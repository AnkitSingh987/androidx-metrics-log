Title: Molly and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a dependable mommy and her little three-year old daughter named Molly. Every day, Molly's mommy would make a delicious breakfast for her. Today when Molly came down to the kitchen, she saw a big breakfast waiting for her on the table. Molly: "Mommy, what did you make for me today?" Mommy: "I made french toast. I know it's your favorite!" Molly: "Yay! I love french toast!" Mommy: "Now let's enjoy it together. We can talk about the new season that's coming up soon. Molly: "Ooh, what season is it?" Mommy: "It's spring! All the flowers and trees will bloom and it will be very nice outside. You can go out for many walks with me and we can hunt for critters like spiders and butterflies." Molly: "Yay! I love spring and walkies with mommy too." Molly and her mommy enjoyed the food and talked about spring, which is her mommy's dependable favorite season.

One day, a gentle cat named Kitty went to the park. She took a basket with her. In the basket, she had her toys and food. She was very happy. Kitty loved to play in the park. While playing, Kitty saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play too. Kitty started to panic. She did not know if the dog was friendly. Kitty ran away and hid behind a tree. The dog looked sad. Kitty thought about it and decided to be brave. She came out from behind the tree and played with the dog. They became good friends. Kitty learned that it is good to be brave and not panic when you meet new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom was a flexible cat. He could bend and stretch in many ways. One day, Tom went to the park to play. At the park, Tom saw a bird named Sue. Sue was eating a snack. The snack was a yummy worm. Tom wanted to be friends with Sue. So, Tom walked up to Sue and said, "Hi, can we speak?" Sue looked at Tom and said, "Yes, we can speak. Do you want some of my snack?" Tom said, "No, thank you. I just want to play." Sue and Tom played together all day. They were very happy and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the kitchen. One day, he saw a sign that said "Do not touch the hot stove." Tim was frightened, but he wanted to see what would happen if he touched it. Tim thought for a moment and then touched the hot stove. It hurt a lot and he cried. His mom came running and saw what happened. She was very sad and told Tim that he should have listened to the sign. The moral of the story is to always listen to signs and warnings. They are there to keep us safe. If Tim had listened, he would not have been hurt.

One day, a cat and a dog went for a walk. They saw an open door. The cat said, "Let's go in!" The dog said, "Okay!" They went in and saw a big room. In the room, there was a drain on the floor. They did not know what it was for. The cat and the dog played in the room. They had fun running and jumping. The cat saw a rope hanging from the ceiling. The cat said, "I want to hang on the rope!" The dog said, "Be careful!" The cat jumped and hung on the rope. Suddenly, water started to come into the room. The water went down the drain. The cat and the dog were scared. They remembered the drain they saw before. They ran to the drain and watched as the water went down. The room was safe again. The cat and the dog learned that the drain was there to help them. They were happy and went back to playing.

Once upon a time, there was a little dolphin named Dolly. Dolly lived in the cold, blue sea with her family. She loved to play and swim with her friends all day long. One day, Dolly met a little fish named Finny. Finny was sad because he did not know how to swim fast. Dolly wanted to help Finny. She said, "Don't be sad, Finny. I can show you how to swim fast. Just follow me and do what I do." Finny was happy and said, "Thank you, Dolly!" They swam together, and Dolly showed Finny how to move his tail fast. Soon, Finny was able to swim fast just like Dolly. They played and swam together, having lots of fun in the cold sea. Dolly and Finny became best friends. They knew they could always help each other when they needed it. And they lived happily ever after, swimming and playing in the cold, blue sea.
