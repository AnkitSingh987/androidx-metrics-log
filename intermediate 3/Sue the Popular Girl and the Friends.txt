Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Popular Girl and the Friends

Once upon a time, there was a popular girl named Sue. Sue had many friends who liked to play with her. One day, Sue and her friends decided to play a game. They wanted to see who could run the fastest. Sue and her friends went to a big rug in the playroom. They stood at one end of the rug and got ready to race. They said, "Ready, set, go!" and started to run. Sue ran very fast, and so did her friends. Sue and her friends ran and ran, and soon, Sue was in front. She ran faster and faster, and then she won the race! All her friends clapped and cheered. They were very happy for Sue. Sue felt proud and happy too. From that day on, Sue and her friends played the running game many times, and they always had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved ice cream cones. One day, her mom gave her a fake cone. It was a toy, but Lucy did not know that. Lucy tried to eat the fake cone, but it was hard and not yummy. She was sad and cried. Her mom came and wiped her tears. She said, "Don't worry, I have a surprise for you." The next day, Lucy's mom took her to the ice cream shop. Lucy saw real cones with yummy ice cream. She was so happy! She picked her favorite flavor and ate it all up. Then Lucy's mom wiped her face, and they both smiled.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had an original toy. It was a small guitar. She loved her guitar very much. She played with it every day. One day, Sue was sad. Her friend Tom was sick. Sue wanted to help Tom feel better. She thought about her guitar. Sue had an idea. She would play her guitar to comfort Tom. Sue went to Tom's house. She played her guitar for him. Tom smiled and started to feel better. Sue was happy she could help her friend. They played with the guitar together. From that day on, Sue and Tom were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with rocks. One day, he found a shiny rock. The shiny rock was a mineral. Tim showed the mineral to his friend, Sue. Sue was angry because she did not have a shiny rock. She decided to look for one too. Tim and Sue looked for shiny rocks together. They found more minerals and were happy. They played with the shiny rocks all day and were not angry anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Amy who was very naughty. She liked to do things that she knew she shouldn't, like swinging on the railings outside her house. One day, her mum called her in and said, "Amy, why are you so naughty today? You know you shouldn't swing on the railings". Amy just smiled and said, "But mum, it's so much fun to swing on the railings. I want to do it every day". Her mum sighed and said, "I'm sorry Amy, but you can't do it. It's too dangerous". Amy started to cry and said, "But mum, I just want to swing". Her mum hugged her and said, "I know you do, sweetheart. How about I take you to the park tomorrow, and you can swing on the swings there?". Amy smiled and said, "Yes please!" and with that, her naughty activity was banned forever.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty monkey called Molly. Molly was up to no good and she loved to get into trouble. One day, Molly's mommy wanted to teach her a lesson. She brought out a card and showed it to Molly. "This is your naughty card," said Mommy. She explained that Molly would have to earn five stars on her card before she could go out and play. Molly was not happy. She stomped her feet and frowned. But Mommy was firm – she had to learn to be good. So Molly went to work, trying to get her five stars. When she was naughty, Mommy took away a star. But when she was good, she earned a star. In the end, Molly was able to get her five stars on her card. She was so proud to show it to Mommy and be able to go out and play. The End.
