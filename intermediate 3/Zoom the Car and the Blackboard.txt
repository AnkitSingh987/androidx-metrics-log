Category: Intermediate
Title: Zoom the Car and the Blackboard

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Zoom. Zoom was a sad car because he did not have any friends to play with. He would drive around the town all alone, looking for someone to be his friend. One day, Zoom saw a big blackboard near the school. The blackboard looked lonely too. Zoom had an idea! He drove up to the blackboard and said, "Hi, I am Zoom! Do you want to be my friend?" The blackboard smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to be your friend!" From that day on, Zoom and the blackboard were best friends. They would play together every day. Zoom would drive around the blackboard, making funny shapes and drawings with his wheels. The children at the school loved to watch them play. Zoom was not sad anymore, and he was very happy to have a friend like the blackboard. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a small girl thought about waving to her friends, but she was a little too shy. She frowned and decided not to do it. The next day, when the girl came back to the same place, she saw a lot of other little kids there. She noticed they were all waving to each other and she felt brave enough to join in. She waved and smiled, and the other kids waved back. The girl felt great! Seeing that it was easy to make new friends when you just wave to them. The girl was so happy that she waved to everyone she saw every day afterwards. Now the girl has more friends than ever before. Every time she waves to them, they wave back and everyone smiles. She loves it and knows it's because she wasn't too small to make a difference in the world by just waving.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little bird named Whistle. Whistle lived in a big tree with his family. He loved to fly and play with his friends, but he was too shy to talk to them. One day, Whistle saw a big switch on the ground. He was curious and asked his mom, "What is this?" His mom said, "That is a switch. It can turn things on and off." Whistle wanted to know what the switch could do, so he picked it up with his beak. Whistle flew around with the switch and saw his friends playing. He wanted to join them but was too shy to say hello. He thought, "Maybe the switch can help me." He flipped the switch, and suddenly, he could whistle a beautiful song. His friends heard the song and came over to play with Whistle. They all had a great time, and Whistle was not shy anymore.

One day, a calm cat named Tim and a dog named Max were playing near a big tree. They saw a pretty frame hanging on the tree. The frame had a picture of a fast car. Tim and Max liked the car and wanted to see it speed. Max said, "Let's find the car and watch it speed!" Tim agreed, and they began to look for the car. They walked and walked, but they could not find the car. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. They looked up and saw the car from the frame! It was speeding in the sky! Tim and Max were very surprised. They never thought they would see a car speed in the sky. Tim and Max laughed and said, "Wow! That was unexpected!" They watched the car speed away and knew they would never forget this day.

Once upon a time, there was a fearful little cat named Tim. Tim was scared of everything. One day, Tim saw a big tree and wanted to reach the top. He thought that if he could do it, he would not be so scared anymore. Tim started to climb the tree. He went up and up, higher and higher. Suddenly, Tim saw a big bird. The bird had a lung in its beak. Tim was scared, but he wanted to help the bird. So, he reached out and took the lung from the bird. The bird was not mad at Tim. Instead, the bird was happy that Tim helped it. The bird and Tim became friends. They played together in the tree. Tim was not scared anymore. He learned that being brave can bring good things.
