Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ice

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to go to the mall with his mom. They would walk around and look at all the fun things. One day, Tim saw a yummy ice cream shop at the mall. He asked his mom, "Can I have some ice cream?" His mom answered, "Yes, you can have some ice cream, Tim." Tim was very happy. Tim and his mom went to the ice cream shop. Tim got a big, yummy ice cream cone. They sat down and ate their ice cream together. It was a fun day at the mall for Tim and his mom.

Once upon a time, on a far away purple planet, there lived a big fish named Bob. Bob liked to splash in the water all day long. He had many friends who loved to play with him. One day, Bob met a new friend named Tim. Tim was a small fish with big eyes. They played together and splashed in the water. "Splash, splash!" said Bob. "This is fun!" said Tim. They laughed and played all day. But soon, the sky turned dark and the wind began to blow. The water started to move very fast. Bob and Tim were scared. They tried to swim away, but the water was too strong. They got pulled into a big whirlpool and couldn't get out. In the end, Bob and Tim were lost in the whirlpool, and their friends never saw them again.

Once upon a time there was a loyal dog called Jess. Jess was a loyal dog who wanted to be supported by her owner, Mrs. Meadows. Every day, Mrs. Meadows would give Jess a special treat and the two of them would go on a nice walk. One day, Mrs. Meadows was having lunch outside and she didn't have a napkin. "Oh no", she said. Suddenly, Jess came running up to her with a napkin in her mouth. Mrs. Meadows was surprised and said, "You are so loyal Jess. Thank you for being so supportive!" She gave Jess a big hug and thanked her for being so kind. From then on, whenever Mrs. Meadows was outside and needed something, Jess was there to help. Whether it was getting a napkin, going for a walk, or just needing some loving support, Jess was always there for Mrs. Meadows and was loyal to her no matter what. And they all lived happily ever after!

Once upon a time, there was a smart little girl named Amy. She loved to decorate her room with pretty things. One day, she found a hammer in her dad's toolbox. Amy thought, "I can use this hammer to hang up my new pictures!" Amy started to hammer a nail into the wall. But as she hit the nail, something unexpected happened! The wall opened up like a door. Amy could not believe her eyes. She peeked inside and saw a secret room. It was full of toys and games! Amy decided to keep the secret room a secret. She would play in the room when no one was watching. And when she was done playing, she would close the secret door. Amy felt very smart for finding the secret room. From that day on, she had the best time playing and decorating her new secret room.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and jump all day. One day, Spot saw a napkin on the floor. He thought it was a toy, so he picked it up and ran around with it. While Spot was playing, he heard a funny sound. He stopped and looked around. He saw a big box that was moving! Spot was scared, but he wanted to help. He used the napkin to pull the box and out came a little cat! The little cat was named Fluffy. Spot and Fluffy became best friends. They played and had fun together. And Spot learned that he could gain a new friend by being brave and kind.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a busy man named Tim. He had a job to deliver things to people's homes. One day, he had to deliver a big grill to a house. When Tim got to the house, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing outside. Tim said, "Hi Sue, I am here to deliver this grill for your family." Sue said, "Thank you, Tim. My mom and dad will be happy." Tim put the grill in the backyard. But he was so busy, he forgot to check if it was safe. Later, Sue's mom and dad tried to cook food on the grill. The grill was not safe and it broke. The food fell on the ground and everyone was sad. Tim learned that he should not be too busy and should always check if things are safe.
