Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Mia and the Gum

Tim and Mia liked to build things with gum. They would chew many pieces of gum and stick them together to make shapes. Sometimes they made animals, sometimes they made cars, sometimes they made houses. One day, they decided to build a big gum tower. They chewed and chewed and stuck and stuck until they had a tall and sticky tower. They were very proud of their tower and wanted to show it to their mom. "Mom, look what we made!" they shouted as they ran to the kitchen. Mom came to see their tower and smiled. She was happy that they were having fun, but she also knew that gum was not good for their teeth. "That's a very nice tower, Tim and Mia," she said. "But you know that gum is not good for your teeth, right? You should not chew so much gum, or your teeth will get sick." Tim and Mia looked at each other. They did not want their teeth to get sick. They liked their teeth. They used their teeth to eat and talk and smile. "But mom, gum is harmless," Tim said. "It does not hurt anyone. It is just sweet and soft." Mom shook her head. She explained to them that gum had sugar and chemicals that could damage their teeth. She told them that they should only chew gum sometimes, and not for too long. She also told them that they should brush their teeth after chewing gum, and drink water to wash away the sugar. Tim and Mia listened to mom. They understood that she was right. They wanted to take care of their teeth. They agreed to chew less gum and brush their teeth more. They hugged mom and thanked her for teaching them. They also asked her if they could keep their gum tower. Mom laughed and said yes, as long as they did not chew it again. She said they could put their gum tower in their room and use it as a decoration. Tim and Mia were happy. They still liked to build things with gum, but they also learned to be careful with their teeth. They took their gum tower to their room and admired it. They thought it was the best gum tower ever.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a funny dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with shapes. One day, Spot found a big box full of shapes in the park. Spot saw a little girl named Lisa near the box. "Hi, Lisa!" Spot said. "Do you want to play with shapes with me?" Lisa smiled and said, "Yes, Spot! I love shapes too!" They played with the shapes for a long time. They made a big tower with the shapes. Then, they paused for a moment to look at their tower. "Wow!" said Lisa. "Our tower is so tall and pretty!" Spot barked happily, agreeing with Lisa. They continued to play until the sun went down, and they had a great day together.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with water. He would splash in the puddles after it rained. Tim had a toy police car he liked to play with too. He would make the police car drive through the puddles and splash water everywhere. One day, Tim saw a big puddle near the road. He wanted to make a big splash with his police car. He ran to the puddle and threw the car into the water. The police car made a big splash, and Tim laughed. But then, the police car floated away in the water. Tim tried to reach for it, but it was too far. He was sad because he lost his favorite toy. The young boy learned not to play near the road and to be more careful with his toys.

Once upon a time, in a hidden place, there was a tiny cell. The cell was very lonely, because it was so small that nobody could see it. It lived all by itself in a big, big world. One day, a kind bird flew by and saw the little cell. The bird felt sad for the lonely cell and wanted to help. So, the bird went to tell all its friends about the hidden cell. The bird said, "I found a tiny cell that needs friends. Let's go and meet it!" The bird and its friends went to the hidden place where the cell lived. They all said hello to the cell and played with it. The cell was very happy to have friends. From that day on, the cell was never lonely again.
