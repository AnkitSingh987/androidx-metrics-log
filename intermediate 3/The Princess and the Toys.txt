Category: Intermediate
Title: The Princess and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a shy princess. She lived in a big castle with her mom and dad, the king and queen. The princess loved to play with her toys in her room, but she was too shy to play with the other kids in the town. One sunny day, the princess went outside to play in the garden. She found a big tub of water. She wanted to play with her toys in the water, so she put them in the tub. The toys started to soak in the water. The princess was very happy to see her toys swim and float. As the princess played, a little girl from the town saw her. The little girl asked if she could play too. The shy princess was scared at first, but then she said yes. They both played with the soaked toys in the water and had lots of fun. The princess was not so shy anymore, and she made a new friend that day.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to go outside and play. She put on her pink shoes and her yellow hat and asked her mom if she could go. Her mom said yes, but told her to stay in the garden and not to wander too far. Lily went to the garden and saw many pretty flowers. She liked the red ones and the blue ones and the white ones. She picked some of them and made a necklace for herself. She felt happy and smiled. Then she saw a big flower that was purple and pink. It was the most beautiful flower she had ever seen. She wanted to touch it and smell it. She forgot what her mom said and wandered to the flower. She reached out her hand and touched the petals. They were soft and mild. But then she heard a loud buzz. A bee came out of the flower and flew around her head. It was angry that she disturbed its home. It stung her on her nose. It hurt a lot. She cried and ran back to her mom. Her mom saw her and hugged her. She put some ice on her nose and gave her a kiss. She told her that she was sorry that she got stung, but that she should not wander away from the garden. She said that the flower was not hers to touch, but the bee's. She said that she should be careful and respectful of nature. Lily nodded and said that she was sorry too. She learned her lesson and did not wander again. She still liked the flowers, but she looked at them from a distance. She was happy and safe in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a big red car. The car lived in a garage. The garage was next to a small house. The car was very happy there. One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to help her dad. Her dad needed to feed the car. Lily went to the garage with her dad. They saw the enormous car. Lily was so excited! Lily's dad showed her how to feed the car. They put the food in the car's mouth. The car was very happy. Lily and her dad went back to the house. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a friend named Sue. They liked to play together every day. One day, they found a big rock with a point. They were frightened because they thought it was a monster. They did not want the monster to hurt them. Tom said to Sue, "We should support each other and be brave." Sue agreed, and they held hands. They walked up to the big rock and saw that it was not a monster at all. It was just a big rock with a point. They were not frightened anymore. They laughed and played around the big rock. They knew that they could support each other and be brave together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a cheerful boy named Tim. He loved to watch the colorful fish swim in the clear water near his house. One day, while Tim was watching the fish, he saw a shiny tube in the water. Tim wanted to get the tube, but he knew it was not safe to go in the water alone. He ran to his mom and asked for help. His mom came with him and helped him get the tube from the water. Tim was very happy and said thank you to his mom. Tim learned that it is important to ask for help when we need it. He knew that his mom would always be there for him. From that day on, Tim always asked for help when he needed it, and he stayed safe and happy.
