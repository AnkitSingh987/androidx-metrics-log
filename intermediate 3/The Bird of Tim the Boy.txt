Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They went to the park near their home. It was a foggy day, so it was difficult to see very far. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim. It's hard to see today." While they walked, Tim saw a small bird in the fog. The bird was sad because it could not find its family. Tim wanted to help the bird. He told his mom, "I want to help the bird find its family." They looked and looked for the bird's family. The fog began to go away, and it was not so difficult to see. Finally, they found the bird's family in a tree. The bird was very happy to see its family again. Tim and his mom were happy too, because they helped the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a tough auto. People all around town marveled at how strong it was. But the auto had one problem - it was too noisy! The auto's owners warned it sternly not to make such loud noises. They said if it disobeyed again then it would be punished. But the auto didn't listen. It blared its horn and revved its engine without a care. The auto's owners were so angry they decided to punish it. They drove the auto out to a distant, quiet spot. When they got there, the owners opened the hood and let out a loud surprise. The auto hadn't expected that its punishment would be a new and shiny muffler. But it was glad to be quiet again. From that day on, it finally drove with respect, and people no longer complained about the noise. The auto had learned from the tough punishment that it must obey its owners.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a mirror that she loved very much. Lucy was a patient girl, always waiting her turn to play with her friends. One day, Lucy and her friends played a game where they had to gain points by finding things. They looked for toys, snacks, and pretty rocks. Lucy wanted to win, so she tried very hard to find lots of things. Then, something unexpected happened! When Lucy looked in her mirror, she saw a hidden toy behind her. She turned around, and there it was! Lucy won the game because the mirror helped her find the last toy. From that day on, Lucy knew her mirror was very special.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the garden. One day, they see some red berries on a bush. They want to eat them. "Look, Ben, berries!" Anna says. "They are so red and shiny. Can we have some?" "I don't know, Anna. Maybe they are not good for us. Maybe they are sour or bitter. Or maybe they have bugs on them." Ben says. Anna picks a berry and smells it. It smells sweet and fresh. She shows it to Ben. "It smells good, Ben. And it looks clean. I think we can eat it." Anna says. Ben is not sure. He looks at the berry. He sees a tiny spot of dirt on it. "Wait, Anna. We should wash the berries first. Dirt is not good for us. It can make us sick. Come, let's go to the tap and wash them." Ben says. Anna agrees. She puts the berry back on the bush. She and Ben pick some more berries. They are careful not to drop them. They run to the tap and wash the berries. The water is cold and clear. The berries are wet and shiny. They look even more yummy. Anna and Ben sit on the grass and eat the berries. They are sweet and juicy. They make their mouths red. They laugh and giggle. They are happy and lively. They share the berries and enjoy them. They are good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim had a large hockey game. He was very excited. Tim got ready and ran to the field with his friends. They all wanted to win the game. At the game, Tim's team played very well. But, Tim forgot his hockey stick at home. "Oh no!" he cried. His friend, Sam, said, "Don't worry, Tim. You can share my stick." They took turns using Sam's stick, but it was hard to play like that. Then, something unexpected happened. A dog ran onto the field with a hockey stick in its mouth. The dog dropped the stick in front of Tim. Tim picked it up and said, "Thank you, dog!" With the new stick, Tim's team played even better and won the game. They were all very happy.
