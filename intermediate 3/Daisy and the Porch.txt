Category: Intermediate
Title: Daisy and the Porch

One day, Daisy and her parents were playing outside. Daisy could see the porch from their backyard. She liked the porch because it was soft. Daisy hopped over to the porch and stood on it. "Do you want to take a walk around the porch?" asked her dad. Daisy nodded excitedly. As Daisy and her parents walked around the porch, Daisy hopped from one soft spot to another. She loved feeling the softness beneath her feet. She looked up and saw the clouds in the sky. She waved to the birds that flew around chirping. At the end of their walk, Daisy ran back to the porch and hopped around it. "Let's go hop again tomorrow!" she said, smiling. Her parents agreed, and together they went back inside.

Once upon a time, there was a big fire in a small town. The fire was rare because it was blue. The people in the town were scared and did not know what to do. They needed help to put the fire out. A helpful lady named Sue wanted to organize the people. She told everyone to get water from the river. They all took buckets and ran to the river. They filled the buckets with water and ran back to the fire. The people threw the water on the fire. The blue fire got smaller and smaller. Soon, the fire was all gone. The town was safe again, and the people were happy. They thanked Sue for helping them organize and put out the rare fire.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toy cars and trucks in the living room. They liked to pretend they were drivers and workers, and they made noises like honk, beep, and vroom. Tom had a big yellow truck that could load and unload things. Lily had a red car that was very fast and shiny. "Look, Lily, I can load these blocks on my truck," Tom said, as he picked up some wooden blocks and put them on the back of his truck. "Now I can take them to the building site and make a house." "That's cool, Tom, but I can go faster than you," Lily said, as she zoomed her car around the carpet. "I can race you to the other side of the room. Ready, set, go!" Tom and Lily started to drive their toys as fast as they could, but they did not see that their mom had left her purse on the floor. Lily's car bumped into the purse and made it fall over. Some things came out of the purse, including a shiny badge and a whistle. "Wow, what's this?" Lily asked, as she picked up the badge. "It looks like a star." "That's mom's badge, Lily. She is a police," Tom said. "She helps people and catches bad guys. She wears the badge on her shirt and the whistle on her neck." "Can I try it?" Lily asked, as she put the badge on her shirt and the whistle in her mouth. She blew the whistle and made a loud sound. "Wee-woo, wee-woo, I'm a police too!" Tom and Lily laughed and played with the badge and the whistle, pretending they were police. They did not hear their mom coming back from the kitchen. She saw them with her things and smiled. "Hey, you two, what are you doing with my stuff?" she asked, as she picked up her purse and put the things back in. "Are you having fun?" "Yes, mom, we are playing police," Tom said. "You are a good police, mom. We want to be like you." "Thank you, Tom. That's very sweet of you," mom said. "But you know, being a police is not always easy. Sometimes it can be hard and scary. That's why I need you and Lily to be good and safe when I'm not home." "We will, mom. We love you," Lily said. "Can we give you a hug?" "Of course, you can," mom said, as she hugged them both. "You are my best helpers and my favorite people. You make me feel comfortable and happy."

Once there was a young girl, named Lucy. She loved to dress up and play pretend. One day, Lucy heard that a magical place called the Fun Castle was opened nearby. She was so excited! She wanted to wear her princess dress and go straight away. "Let's go to the Fun Castle, Mummy," Lucy said excitedly. "Sure," said Mum. When they arrived at the castle, Lucy saw how clear the sky was and how magical the castle looked. She couldn't wait to explore it! Inside the castle, she found lots of games, rides and costumes to wear. She wore her princess dress and laughed with joy. She had so much fun! When it was time to go home, Lucy was sad they had to leave. "Let's come back to the Fun Castle soon," said Mum. Lucy smiled and held Mum's hand tightly. "Yes, let's come back here again!"
