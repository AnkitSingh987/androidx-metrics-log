Title: Lily and Max and the Toys

Lily and Max were friends. They liked to play with dolls and cars. One day, Lily's mom said they could have pizza for lunch. Lily was happy. She loved pizza. "Can Max come too?" Lily asked her mom. "Sure, honey. I'll call his mom and ask," her mom said. Lily ran to Max and told him the good news. Max was happy too. He loved pizza. "Let's go to your house and wait for the pizza," Max said. They went to Lily's house and played in her room. Lily had a lot of toys. She had a big dollhouse, a train set, a teddy bear, and a puzzle. Max had fewer toys. He had a car, a ball, and a book. He looked at Lily's toys and felt jealous. "Lily, can I play with your dollhouse?" Max asked. "No, Max. That's mine. You can play with the car," Lily said. "But I always play with the car. I want to play with something else," Max said. "Too bad. The dollhouse is for girls. You are a boy," Lily said. Max was angry. He didn't like Lily's words. He wanted to play with the dollhouse. He thought it was unfair. He grabbed the dollhouse and tried to take it. Lily grabbed it too and pulled it back. They both tugged and shouted. "Stop it, Max! Let go of my dollhouse!" Lily yelled. "No, Lily! I want to play with it!" Max yelled. They pulled harder and harder. The dollhouse broke. The dolls and furniture fell on the floor. Lily and Max stopped and looked at the mess. They both felt sad. Lily's mom heard the noise and came to the room. She saw the broken dollhouse and the crying children. "What happened here?" she asked. "Lily and Max broke my dollhouse!" Lily said. "No, Lily broke it! She wouldn't let me play with it!" Max said. Lily's mom was angry. She reminded them that they were friends and they should share and be nice. She said they couldn't have pizza anymore. She said Max had to go home and Lily had to stay in her room. Lily and Max felt sorry. They said they were sorry. But it was too late. The pizza was gone. The dollhouse was gone. The friendship was gone.

One day, a cat and a dog went to play in the wild. They liked to move and run around. They played for many minutes, having lots of fun. Suddenly, they saw a big box. The box started to move! The cat and the dog were scared. They hid behind a tree and watched the box. The box opened, and out came a big, wild bird! The cat and the dog were not scared anymore. They became friends with the wild bird, and they all played together. The cat, the dog, and the wild bird had a great time in the wild.

Once there was a big castle. One day, a monster came to the castle and wanted to destroy it. But he was patient and waited for the perfect view. After some time, he found what he was looking for and started to destroy the castle. The people living in the castle became scared and were running around. But no matter how much the monster destroyed, the castle kept standing. Suddenly, a brave knight arrived. He saw the monster and was determined to protect the castle. He used his sword and shield and kept the monster away. He was so patient and confident that eventually the monster left. The people were so relieved. The castle was saved and they were safe again. The knight had been very brave and patient, and the people were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Sam. Sam loved to cook. One day, he wanted to make a big cake. He was very excited. But Sam did not know how to cook a cake. He felt frustrated. Sam had a friend, a cat named Tina. Tina was good at exercise. She could jump and run very fast. Sam asked Tina to help him cook the cake. Tina said yes, and they started to cook together. They mixed and poured and baked the cake. It smelled yummy. But then, something unexpected happened. The cake grew too big and broke the oven! Sam and Tina were sad. They could not eat the cake. The cake was a big mess. The end.
