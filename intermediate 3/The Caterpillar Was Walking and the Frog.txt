Title: The Caterpillar Was Walking and the Frog

One day, a caterpillar was walking in a field. He stepped on a big rock and suddenly he slipped and fell into the water! The caterpillar struggled in the water trying to get back on the rock. Suddenly, a frog put his head out of the water. He smiled at the caterpillar and said, "I can help you!" The caterpillar was scared but he said, "Yes, please!" The frog stepped onto the rock. He said, "Climb on my back and I'll help you get out of there." The caterpillar hesitated but then he said, "Okay," and hopped on the frog's back. The frog slowly stepped out of the water and flopped onto the grass. The caterpillar said thankfully, "Thank you so much!" But then the frog said in a scornful voice, "You're stupid! You shouldn't have fallen into the water in the first place!" The caterpillar was surprised and angry. He said, "That's not nice! You should never call someone stupid!" The frog was surprised by the caterpillar’s response. He said, "You're right. I'm sorry. I won't do it again." The caterpillar smiled and said, "It's okay. You're forgiven." He stepped away from the frog and went on his way.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Mary who had lots of dolls. She loved to play with them all day long, but when she would get too frustrated, she would always ask her mum for help. One day Mary was playing with her dolls and she started to get angry when things weren't going her way. She looked up to her mum and said, "Mummy, I'm so frustrated. Can you help me?" Mum said, "Yes, sweetheart, I can help you. But first I want you to wait. Can you wait one minute and then I'll help you?" Mary said, "Yes, Mummy, I can wait." She waited patiently while her mum helped her. Then when they were finished, Mum said "Mary, it's very important to forgive. Even when it's hard, it's important to forgive." Mary thought about her mum's words and smiled. From then on she always tried to forgive and not get too frustrated when things didn't go her way. She remembered to wait and to forgive when she was having a hard day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Sue. Sue had a black cat named Max. They loved to play in the yard. One sunny day, Sue found a rake in the yard. She used the rake to gather leaves. Max watched her and started to dance on the leaves. Sue laughed and said, "Max, you are so funny!" Sue and Max played with the leaves all day. They danced and jumped until the sun went down. Then, they went inside the house, tired but happy.

Once upon a time, there was a bee who wanted to find a place to rest. She looked and looked, but all the places she found seemed too loud and busy. Just as she was about to give up and go away, she noticed a fine tree with a few red leaves still hanging. The bee was pleased and flew up to the tree. "Hello!" she said with a buzz. "Good day, bee," said the tree. "Where are you going?" The bee explained her plight, and the tree smiled and said, "You can stay here, if you wish. I won't be needing this spot for a while." The bee was delighted and thanked the tree for the kindness. From then on, the bee rested in the tree every day. The two were the best of friends, and the bee's days seemed to end just fine. One day, the bee thanked the tree again for the kindness and said goodbye. She flew off, and the tree soon realized that it was only right that a friend should help out another in need. This story teaches us that it's important to be kind and lend a helping hand to those in need. We should never pass up a chance to help make someone else's day end just fine.

Once upon a time, there was a kid named Tim. Tim liked to paint. He had a lot of fun when he painted. One day, Tim wanted to paint something good. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He would paint a big sun in the sky. The sun would be happy and bright. Tim got his paint and brush. He painted the big sun with a big smile. It was very good. Tim was happy. He showed his painting to his mom and dad. They thought it was good too. Tim felt proud of his good painting.
