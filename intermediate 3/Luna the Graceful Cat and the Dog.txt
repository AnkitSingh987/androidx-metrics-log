Category: Intermediate
Title: Luna the Graceful Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Luna. Luna loved to play with her soft, fluffy towel. She would roll it up, jump on it, and poke it with her paw. One sunny day, Luna was playing with her towel in the yard. She poked it very hard, and it rolled away from her. Luna chased after it, but the towel rolled faster and faster. Suddenly, the towel stopped rolling and stood up on its own! Luna was very surprised. The towel was not just a towel, but a friendly dog named Max. Max had been hiding inside the towel all along. Luna and Max became best friends and played together every day.

Lily and her mom went to the store to buy meat for dinner. Lily liked meat a lot, especially when it was rare and juicy. She saw a big red steak in the fridge and pointed to it. "Can we buy this one, mom?" she asked. Mom looked at the steak and shook her head. "No, sweetie, that one is too expensive. We can buy this one instead." She picked up a smaller, brown steak that looked dry and tough. Lily frowned and pouted. She did not want the brown steak. She wanted the red steak. She thought of a plan to get it. When mom was not looking, Lily sneaked to the fridge and grabbed the red steak. She hid it under her coat and ran to the checkout. She hoped mom would not notice. But mom did notice. She saw the big lump under Lily's coat and felt something wet and cold. She pulled out the red steak and gasped. "Lily, what did you do?" she scolded. "You can't take things without paying. That's stealing. And this steak is dripping blood all over your coat. It's not cooked. It's raw. You can't eat raw meat. It can make you sick." Lily felt ashamed and scared. She did not know stealing was bad. She did not know raw meat was bad. She just wanted the red steak. She started to cry. "I'm sorry, mom. I'm sorry. I just wanted the rare meat. Please don't be mad at me." Mom sighed and hugged Lily. She was not mad, but she was disappointed. She explained to Lily why stealing and raw meat were wrong. She gave the red steak back to the store and paid for the brown steak. She told Lily she would try to make it tender and tasty for dinner. Lily said she was sorry again and promised to be good. She learned her lesson. She decided she did not like rare meat anymore. She liked cooked meat better.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to play. She had many toys, but she could not choose which one to play with. She saw her scissors and thought, "I can cut pretty shapes with these!" Lucy's mom saw her with the scissors and said, "Lucy, be careful with those scissors. They are sharp and can hurt you." But Lucy didn't listen. She wanted to cut shapes from her favorite book. The book was old and spoiled, but Lucy loved it. As Lucy started to cut, something unexpected happened. The scissors turned into a magic wand! Lucy was so surprised. She waved the wand and all her toys started to dance. Lucy laughed and said, "I'm glad I chose the scissors!"

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat. The cat loved to sleep on a big, soft chair. Every day, the cat would jump on the chair and take a long nap. One day, a little girl came to visit the cat. She saw the cat sleeping on the chair. The little girl wanted to play with the cat, but the cat was too lazy. The girl tried to wake the cat up, but the cat just rolled over and went back to sleep. The little girl had an idea. She leaned down and gave the cat a gentle kiss on the head. The cat opened its eyes and looked at the girl. The cat was happy and got up from the chair. They played together all day long, and the cat was not lazy anymore.
