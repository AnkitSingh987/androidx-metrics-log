Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Baseball

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. One day, they decided to play baseball. Tim had never played baseball before, but he wanted to try. Tim's friend gave him a powerful hit, but Tim missed the ball. He felt sad and said, "I hate this game!" But his friends encouraged him to try again. They told him, "You can do it, Tim!" So Tim tried again. This time, he hit the ball very far. All his friends cheered for him. Tim felt happy and didn't hate baseball anymore. He learned that with practice, he could become better at the game. And they all played baseball happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a small, weak cube on the floor. It had four sides and was blue. Tim wanted to play with the cube, but it was too weak to hold. So, he asked his friend, Sam, to join him and help fix the cube. "Sam, can you join me and help me fix this weak cube?" Tim asked. Sam said, "Yes, I will join you and help!" Together, they used glue and tape to make the cube strong. When the cube was fixed, Tim and Sam played with it all day. They had lots of fun and were happy that they could fix the weak cube.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big toe that was always getting hurt. One day, Tim felt sad and ashamed because his toe was hurt again. He did not want to play with his friends because of his hurt toe. Tim's mom saw that he was sad and wanted to help. She went to the store to supply some things to make Tim feel better. She got a big, soft bandage for his toe and a yummy ice cream treat to make him smile. When Tim's mom put the bandage on his toe, he felt much better. He was not ashamed anymore. Tim ate his ice cream treat and went outside to play with his friends. They all had fun together, and Tim's toe did not hurt anymore.

Once upon a time, a boy and a girl were looking for something to play. The boy saw a chimney outside and asked his friend if she wanted to play there. The girl was excited and said yes. So the two ran outside and arrived at the chimney. It was big and tall. The girl was scared at first, but the boy said that it would be a fun game to see who could climb to the top. He said they could see the whole world if they could get up there. The girl wanted to go first, so the boy let her take a try. She put her foot on the first brick of the chimney and started climbing up bit-by-bit. The boy was watching her, making sure nothing bad happened. Finally, she reached the top, and the boy started cheering. The girl looked around and noticed there was a window available. She opened it and crawled inside. The boy was curious and asked her what the inside of the chimney was like. The girl said it was dark but that it was an amazing view from up there. The boy was so excited that he wanted to join her. He also started to climb, but he was a bit slower as he had to take more breaks than the girl. Finally, he also reached the top and opened the window to join his friend. The two of them were so happy that they could now play together in the chimney.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to throw a ball. He had a big, wide yard to play in. Tim also had a dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and catch the ball when Tim threw it. One day, Tim and Spot were playing in the yard. Tim threw the ball far away. Spot ran fast to get it. But, when Spot found the ball, he saw a cut in the ball. Spot was sad because he could not play with the cut ball. Then, a little bird flew down to Spot. The bird saw the cut ball and had an idea. The bird picked up the ball and flew high up in the sky. The bird dropped the ball, and as it fell, the cut closed. The ball was fixed! Spot was happy, and Tim was surprised. They both played with the ball again, and they became friends with the little bird.
