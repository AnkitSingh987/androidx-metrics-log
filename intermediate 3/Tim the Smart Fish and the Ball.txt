Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Smart Fish and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a smart fish named Tim. He lived in a small tank in a big room. Tim was a happy fish who loved to swim around and play with his toys. One day, a new fish named Sam came to live in the tank. Sam was a little shy and did not talk to Tim. Tim wanted to be friends with Sam, but Sam would ignore him. This made Tim sad, but he did not give up. Tim thought of a fun game to play with Sam. He took a ball and pushed it to Sam. Sam saw the ball and started to play with it too. Soon, Sam and Tim were playing together and having lots of fun. They became best friends and never ignored each other again.

Once upon a time, there was a big jug. It was sad because it was empty. It wanted to be full and happy. One day, the jug had an idea. It wanted to fit a lot of things inside it. So it went on a hunt to find things to fit inside. The jug found some small toys and put them inside. It felt a little better, but not very happy yet. Next, the jug found some soft socks and put them inside too. The jug was getting full, but it was still sad. It wanted something more to fit inside. Then, the jug saw a big ball. It wanted to fit the ball inside, but it was too big. The jug was very sad. But then, something unexpected happened! The ball started to get smaller and smaller. It was a magic ball! The ball became small enough to fit inside the jug. The jug was now full and very happy. It was not sad anymore, and it had a magic ball to play with.

One day, a jolly sun was shining in the sky. It was very hot and bright. The sun loved to play and make everyone warm and happy. But sometimes, the sun played too much and things would start to burn. The grass and the trees would get very hot and dry. Then, the rain came to help. The rain was cool and gentle. It fell from the sky and gave water to the grass, the trees, and the flowers. The rain loved to make everything wet and help them grow. The rain and the sun were good friends. When the sun would burn too much, the rain would come and make everything better. They worked together to keep the world happy and healthy. The sun would shine and the rain would fall, and everyone was happy.

One day, a polite dog named Max went to the park. He saw a big sand box and wanted to play. Max jumped in the sand and started to dig. He found a big red ball under the sand. Max was very happy. Max saw a little girl named Lily. She was crying. Max asked, "Why are you sad?" Lily said, "I lost my red ball." Max wanted to help her. He thought he could solve her problem. Max showed Lily the red ball he found in the sand. Lily was very happy and said, "Thank you, Max!" But then, another boy named Tim came. He said, "That's my red ball!" Max was confused. Who did the ball belong to? Max had an idea. He said, "Let's all play with the red ball together!" Lily and Tim liked the idea. They all played nicely in the sand with the red ball. Max solved the problem and made two new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her white folder. She would change the folder into many shapes. Fluffy was very happy. One day, Fluffy went to the park with her white folder. She saw a big tree and wanted to change her folder into a hat. She put the folder on the ground and started to change it. While Fluffy was changing her folder, a big wind came. The wind blew the white folder away. Fluffy ran and ran to catch her folder, but it was too fast. The white folder was gone. Fluffy was very sad. She went home without her white folder. She could not change it into shapes anymore. The end.
