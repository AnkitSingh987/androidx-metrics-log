Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Stick

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big tree with rough bark. Tim wanted to climb the tree. He put his foot on a low branch and started to go up. As Tim climbed higher, he heard a small voice say, "Help me, please!" He looked around and saw a tiny bird stuck in a hole. The bird said, "I can't get out. My wing is hurt from a fall. Can you help me?" Tim wanted to help the bird, but he was scared of falling too. Tim thought for a moment and had an idea. He found a long stick and used it to lock the hole. This made the hole bigger. The bird was able to get out and thanked Tim. They were both happy and became friends. The bird's wing got better, and they played together every day.

One day, a girl named Lucy went for a jog in the park. She wore her mom's apron because she thought it was useful. Lucy liked to jog and play in the park. In the park, Lucy met a boy named Tom. Tom saw Lucy's apron and said, "Why do you wear an apron to jog?" Lucy said, "It is useful to hold my toys and snacks." Tom thought that was a good idea. As they jogged, a big wind came and blew Lucy's apron. The wind made the apron fly like a kite. Lucy and Tom were surprised! They laughed and played with the apron-kite. They had a fun day at the park.

Lily was a little girl who liked to play in the garden. She had a red ball that she loved to bounce and throw. One day, she saw a fox in the garden. The fox was hungry and looking for food. He saw Lily's red ball and thought it was a big apple. He wanted to eat it. The fox ran to the ball and grabbed it with his mouth. He tried to bite it, but it was hard and rubbery. He was confused. This was not a good apple. He did not like it. He wanted to spit it out, but he was afraid that Lily would see him and be angry. Lily saw the fox with her ball and was angry. She shouted at the fox and ran to him. She wanted her ball back. The fox was scared and ran away. He dropped the ball and hid behind a bush. He hoped that Lily would not find him. Lily picked up her ball and hugged it. She was happy to have it back. She looked around for the fox, but she could not see him. She wondered why he stole her ball. She did not understand. She decided to go inside and tell her mom what happened. She hoped that the fox would find some real food and not steal again.

Once upon a time, there was a big gray tower. The tower was very tall and strong. Many people liked to come and see the tower. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the gray tower with his mom. They wanted to spend the day together. Tim was very happy because he loved to play near the tower. Tim and his mom played all day. They ran, laughed, and had fun. The gray tower watched them with a big smile. As the sun went down, Tim and his mom went home. They were very tired but happy because they had a great day at the gray tower.

Once upon a time, there was a black cat named Tom. Tom liked to play outside. One day, Tom saw an egg in the grass. Tom stared at the egg. He did not know what it was. Tom touched the egg with his paw. It was cold and smooth. Tom looked after the egg. One day, the egg cracked open. A little bird came out. Tom and the bird became good friends. They played together every day.

The sun was shining, the birds were singing, and the sky was a beautiful blue. On this day, Timmy was feeling adventurous and wanted to find something exciting. He found a hole in the ground and started to explore. He walked along the dirt path and soon he discovered a gray cave. Timmy had a feeling of curiosity and wanted to see what was inside. He stepped in. It was quite peaceful, and Timmy was happy to find something exciting like this! Suddenly a loud roar was heard from inside. Timmy froze in fear. He was scared and wanted to turn back but he was too scared to move. A giant monster stepped out from the shadows and exclaimed: "You are trespassing! You must leave!" Timmy was terrified and ran away as fast as he could. He never returned to that peaceful gray cave again.
