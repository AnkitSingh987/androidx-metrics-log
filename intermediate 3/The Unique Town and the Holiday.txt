Category: Intermediate
Title: The Unique Town and the Holiday

Once upon a time, there was a unique town. In this town, people loved to welcome others. They were kind and friendly. They liked to have fun and play. One day, they decided to have a big holiday. On the holiday, everyone in the town came together. They wore funny hats and played games. They laughed and shared yummy food. The children ran and played all day long. They welcomed new friends to join them. At the end of the holiday, everyone was happy and tired. They said goodbye to their new friends. They knew that they would always remember this unique holiday. They looked forward to welcoming more friends next year.

---

One day, a creative little mouse named Max was looking for cheese. He went to the kitchen and saw a big piece of cheese on the table. Max was very happy and wanted to eat it all. As Max tried to reach the cheese, something unexpected happened. A big cat named Tom came into the kitchen. Max was scared, but he had a smart idea. He gave Tom a little piece of cheese and Tom liked it a lot. Then, Max and Tom became good friends. They shared the cheese and played together all day. They both learned that sharing and being kind was the best way to make friends. And that's how a little mouse and a big cat became best buddies.

---

One day, Mary asked her mom to buy a carpet from the store. Her mom said yes, and off they went. At the store, there was a very polite man selling carpets. He showed them many different carpets. Mary picked the one she liked the most. The polite man sold the carpet to Mary's mom, who thanked him for his help. Mary and her mom thanked the polite man and went home with the new carpet. Mary's mom put the new carpet in the living room. Mary was so happy to have her own carpet. She laid down and played on it all day. The End.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He loved to play on the ground, making mud pies. He was very good at it. He would cook them in the sun until they were dry. Tim felt happy when he played with mud. Tim saw a little girl named Sue. Sue looked anxious. She was trying to cook her own mud pies, but they kept falling apart. Tim went to her and said, "Do you need help?" Sue nodded and said, "Yes, please." Tim showed Sue how to make the mud pies. They played together, cooking their mud pies on the ground. Soon, Sue's mud pies were just as good as Tim's. They both felt proud and happy.

---

Lily liked to bake with her mom. They made cakes and pies and cookies. Lily liked to eat them too. She liked sweet things. One day, Lily saw a can of tuna in the kitchen. She did not know what it was. She thought it was something to bake. She opened the can and poured the tuna into a bowl. She added some flour and sugar and eggs. She mixed it all up. She put it in a pan and put it in the oven. Mom came home and smelled something bad. She went to the kitchen and saw Lily's tuna cake. She was very grumpy. She said, "Lily, what did you do? This is not a cake. This is tuna. Tuna is for cats, not for baking. You made a big mess. You wasted good food. You have to clean it up." Lily was sad. She did not mean to make mom grumpy. She wanted to make a cake. She thought tuna was a cake. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I wanted to bake with you. I wanted to make you happy. I did not know tuna was for cats. I thought it was a cake." Mom said, "Lily, I love you, but you have to learn. You can't bake with anything you find. You have to ask me first. You have to follow a recipe. You have to be careful. Baking is fun, but it is also hard. You have to wait until you are older. Now, help me clean this up. And no more tuna cakes, ever." Lily helped mom clean the kitchen. She threw away the tuna cake. She felt bad. She did not like tuna anymore. She did not like baking anymore. She wished she had never seen the can of tuna. She wished she had made a real cake. She wished mom was not grumpy. She wished she had a happy ending.
