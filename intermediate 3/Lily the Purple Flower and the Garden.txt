Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Purple Flower and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a pretty purple flower named Lily. Lily lived with other flowers, but she was the only purple one. Lily liked to watch the birds and the bugs in the garden. One day, a little bug came to visit Lily. The bug had wings and could zoom very fast. Lily liked the bug and they became friends. They played together every day, and the bug would zoom around Lily, making her laugh. One sunny day, the bug told Lily he had to go away. Lily was sad, but the bug promised to come back. Lily waited and waited, and one day, the bug came back. They were so happy to see each other again. From that day on, they played together every day, and the bug would always zoom around the pretty purple flower named Lily.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big pile of leaves and wanted to jump in it. But they needed a stick to poke the leaves and make sure there were no bugs or sharp things inside. "Look, there is a twig!" Lily said, pointing to a thin branch on the ground. "Let's use it to poke the leaves." "OK," Ben said, and picked up the twig. He walked towards the pile of leaves, but then he saw something shiny in the grass. It was a red ball. Ben liked red balls. He dropped the twig and ran to get the ball. "Ben, wait!" Lily called. "We need the twig to poke the leaves!" But Ben did not listen. He grabbed the ball and threw it in the air. He laughed and ran after it. He did not see the big rock in his way. He tripped over the rock and fell down. He scraped his knee and started to cry. Lily ran to help him. She saw his knee was bleeding. She felt sorry for him. She took off her scarf and wrapped it around his knee. She hugged him and said, "It's OK, Ben. It will get better soon." "Thank you, Lily," Ben said, sniffling. "You are a good friend. I'm sorry I did not listen to you. I should have used the twig to poke the leaves. Then I would not have hurt myself." Lily smiled and said, "That's OK, Ben. Next time, you can suggest what we do, and I will listen to you. But we have to be careful and look where we are going. And we have to share our things. Then we can have more fun." Ben nodded and said, "You are right, Lily. Sharing is caring. And being careful is smart. Can we still play with the ball and the leaves?" Lily said, "Sure, Ben. But first, let's find the twig and poke the leaves. Then we can jump in them and play with the ball. And maybe we can find some more friends to join us." Ben said, "That sounds like a great idea, Lily. Let's go!" They held hands and ran to the pile of leaves. They found the twig and poked the leaves. They did not find any bugs or sharp things. They jumped in the leaves and laughed. They played with the ball and the twig. They saw some other kids and invited them to play. They had a lot of fun. They learned to listen, to share, and to be careful. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a playful girl named Susie. She loved to mix things together to see what happened. One day, as she was playing in the backyard, she spotted a pile of colorful hair ribbons. She couldn’t resist the temptation and decided to mix things together. She grabbed one of her ribbons and tied it to a bright yellow one. Then she made a pretty bow and placed it on top of her head. She felt like a princess! Just then, her mother came up behind her. “What are you doing, Susie?” she asked with a smile. Susie grinned. “I’m mixing my hair ribbons to make a pretty bow!” “That’s very creative!” her mother said. “Go ahead and show me what else you can mix up!” So Susie kept mixing, making braids and ponytails with her ribbons and all kinds of playful hairstyles. By the time she finished, her hair was full of colorful and creative designs. Susie beamed with pride. She was so glad her mother had encouraged her to mix things up and be creative.
