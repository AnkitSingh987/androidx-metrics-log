Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ball and the Apple

Once upon a time, in a small yard, there lived a happy ball. The ball loved to bounce all day long. It would bounce up and down, and side to side. The yard was its favorite place to play. One sunny day, the ball met a new friend. It was a delicious apple from a tree in the yard. The apple was round and red, just like the ball. They both liked to roll and play together in the yard. As they played, the ball taught the apple how to bounce. The apple tried to bounce, but it was not easy at first. After some time, the apple learned to bounce too. The ball and the apple became best friends, and they bounced together in the yard every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. It was a very tough barn. Inside the barn, there were many animals. There were cows, pigs, and chickens. They all lived happily together in the barn. One sunny day, the animals found a big pile of ice outside the barn. They were very curious about it. They wanted to play with the ice, but they didn't know how to move it. Then, the sun came out and started to melt the ice. The animals watched as the ice became smaller and smaller. As the ice melted, it made a little pool of water. The animals were very happy. They played in the water all day long. They splashed and had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, they went back into the tough red barn, tired but happy.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow bird named Bob. Bob loved to sing all day. One day, he had a big idea. He wanted to record his song for all his friends to hear. So, he found a big stick and started to sing his song. Bob's friend, Cat, heard him sing. Cat was very hungry. Cat thought, "If I catch Bob, he will be a tasty meal." So, Cat hid behind a tree and waited for Bob to finish his song. Cat wanted to jump and catch Bob when he was done. But, when Bob finished his song, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the stick far away. Bob flew away with the stick. Cat was so surprised! He could not catch Bob now. Bob's song saved him from being a tasty meal. And now, all the animals could hear Bob's song on the big stick.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some food. Tim saw some yogurt and asked his mom if they could get it. His mom said, "Yes, but we need to get a gift for your friend's birthday too." At the store, Tim saw a toy snake. He thought it would be a fun gift for his friend. The toy snake was fake, but it looked real. Tim's mom said, "That's a great idea! Let's wrap it when we get home." When they got home, Tim and his mom wrapped the fake snake in pretty paper. Tim was excited to give the gift to his friend. But, he also wanted to play a joke. He put the wrapped snake in the fridge next to the yogurt. The next day, Tim's mom went to the fridge to get yogurt. She saw the wrapped gift and screamed, "What's that?" Tim laughed and told her it was just the fake snake. They all laughed, and Tim knew his friend would love the gift and the funny story.

Once upon a time, in a very green land, there lived four friends. They were called Tim, Tom, Joe and Jake. One day, the sky got very dark and there was a rumble. Tim and Tom looked at each other and said, “What was that sound?” Suddenly, a big tornado came. They were all very scared. Tim and Tom ran to Joe and Jake and said “Let’s go hide!” Joe and Jake were both very hungry, so instead of running away, they stayed in the fields to pick apples. Tim and Tom looked at Joe and Jake and said “Let’s go! The tornado is coming!” But Joe and Jake said “We have to eat something first. We can’t run away with empty stomachs!” So they all ran away together, while they were still picking apples. They, luckily, got away before the tornado came. This story teaches us that it is important to stay safe, and not let hunger get in the way of our safety.
