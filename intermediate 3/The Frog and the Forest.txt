Category: Intermediate
Title: The Frog and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a frog who liked to help. This frog was very competitive. He always wanted to be the best helper in the forest. All his friends knew that he liked to help and be the best. One day, the frog saw a big race happening in the forest. All the animals were there to see who was the fastest. The frog wanted to help, so he went to the race. He saw a little bug who was sad because he was too slow to race. The frog said, "I will help you win the race." The bug was happy and they started to race together. The other animals laughed at the frog and the bug. They did not think they could win. But the frog and the bug worked together and helped each other. They were very fast and they won the race! All the animals were surprised. The frog learned that helping others and working together is more important than being the best. And they all lived happily ever after.

Max and his little sister, Lucy, were playing hide and seek. Max went to look for Lucy. He found her hiding behind the big tree in the garden. Max said, "Found you!" Lucy smiled and giggled. She said, "You'll never find my surprise!" Max was curious and asked, "What surprise?". Lucy said, "Hang on. I'm going to get it". She ran off toward the house. Max waited and watched. Suddenly, he saw Lucy come out of the house with a big box in her hands. She hung the box from the tree. Max was so excited. Lucy said, "I hung a surprise for you on the tree. Go and see what's in it". Max was so excited! He ran to the tree and opened the box. Inside, there was a bad present, but it was still fun. Lucy had hung a ball and a bat in the tree! Max was so happy and thanked Lucy. They played together the whole afternoon.

One day, a big dog named Max and a small cat named Lily were playing in the park. They found a big, rich treat on the ground. Max wanted to chew the treat, but so did Lily. They both looked at the treat and then back at each other. "I want to chew it!" said Max. "Let's trade. I will give you my ball if you let me chew the treat." Lily thought about it for a moment and then said, "No, I want to chew it too! We need to find a way to share." Max and Lily were not happy. They both wanted the treat but could not agree on how to share it. They decided to ask their friend, a wise old owl named Oliver, for help. Oliver listened to their problem and thought for a moment. Oliver said, "Why don't you break the treat in half? That way, you can both chew it and be happy." Max and Lily liked the idea. They broke the treat in half and each chewed on their piece. They both smiled and played together in the park, happy that they found a way to share.

Tom is a big boy who likes to play with his computer. He has many games and pictures on his computer. He can make the computer do what he wants by pressing the buttons. He likes to play with his computer in the morning, when the sun rises and makes the sky bright. One morning, Tom wakes up and sees the sun rising. He jumps out of his bed and runs to his computer. He wants to play his favorite game, where he can make cars and planes and animals. He reaches for his computer, but it is too heavy for him. He tries to lift it, but it falls down and makes a loud noise. "Tom, what are you doing?" his mom asks, coming into his room. She sees the computer on the floor and Tom looking sad. She picks up the computer and puts it back on the table. She checks if it is broken, but it is not. She hugs Tom and says, "It's okay, honey. The computer is too heavy for you to lift. You have to be careful with it. Why don't you ask me or dad to help you when you want to play with it?" Tom nods and says, "I'm sorry, mom. I just wanted to play with the computer. I like it so much. Can I play with it now?" Mom smiles and says, "Yes, you can. But first, let's have some breakfast. You need to eat something to make you strong and happy. Then we can play with the computer together. How does that sound?" Tom smiles and says, "That sounds good, mom. Thank you. I love you." Mom says, "I love you too, Tom. Come on, let's go to the kitchen." She holds Tom's hand and they walk to the kitchen, where dad is waiting for them with some toast and juice. Tom is happy and excited to play with his computer later. He thinks his computer is the best thing in the world.
