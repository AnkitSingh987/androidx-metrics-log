Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to draw and design things. One day, she found a big box of shapes. They were all different: some were round, some were square, and some were like long lines. Lily had an idea. She wanted to make a big picture with all the shapes. So, she started to put the shapes together. She used the round shapes for the sun, the square shapes for a house, and the long lines for the trees. Lily was very happy with her picture. When she was done, Lily showed her picture to her mom and dad. They were so proud of her. They said, "Wow, Lily! You did a great job with your shapes!" They put her picture on the fridge for everyone to see. Lily felt very proud of her design, and she knew that she wanted to keep making beautiful things with her shapes.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went to play outside in the icy cold. He put on his coat, hat, and gloves. He was so excited to play in the snow. Tim saw a big hill and wanted to sled down it. He thought it seemed safe. So, he climbed up and got ready to slide. Just then, a friendly squirrel said, "Wait! Don't go yet!" Tim stopped and looked at the squirrel. The squirrel said, "There's a big hole at the bottom of the hill. You can't see it from here. It's not safe to sled there." Tim was surprised, but he thanked the squirrel. They found a new hill to sled down together. They had lots of fun in the snow and became great friends.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play in the park. One day, Spot saw a little bridge. He wanted to cross the bridge to play on the other side. But there was a problem. A big, mean cat sat on the bridge. The cat would not let Spot cross. Spot was sad. He wanted to play with his friends on the other side of the bridge. Spot had an idea. He would make the cat laugh and become friends. Spot danced and made funny faces. The cat laughed and became friends with Spot. They both crossed the bridge together. From that day on, Spot and the cat played together every day. The moral of the story is that being kind can make friends out of enemies.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there lived a little car named Cara. Cara loved to drive around and help her friends. One day, she noticed that she was low on fuel. She needed to find a fuel station to fill up her tank. As Cara drove through the town, she saw her friend, Tim the truck, by the side of the road. Tim looked sad. Cara stopped and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim replied, "I have a flat tire and I can't drive. I need to replace it, but I don't know how." Cara knew how to replace a tire, so she decided to help her friend. She found a new tire and helped Tim put it on. Tim was very happy, and they both went to the fuel station together. Cara filled up her tank and they drove off, happy to be on the road again.

Once upon a time, there was a little lion named Leo. He lived in a big forest with his family. Leo loved to eat tasty food every day. He always wanted more and more. One day, Leo saw a big cake on a table. He wanted to eat it, but his mommy said, "Leo, mind your manners, and don't be greedy." Leo didn't listen and tried to take the whole cake for himself. But the cake fell on the ground, and it was ruined. Leo felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, mommy. I should have minded my manners." His mommy hugged him and said, "It's okay, Leo. We can make another tasty cake together. Remember, it's important to share and be kind to others." From that day on, Leo learned to share and be a good lion.
