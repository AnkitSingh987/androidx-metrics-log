Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a bit clumsy, but she wanted to learn how to balance better. So, she asked her mom for help. "Mom, can you help me balance?" she said. Her mom thought of a fun test for Lily. "Let's play a game, Lily. I will put a book on your head. You have to walk across the room without it falling off." Lily liked the idea and put the book on her head. Lily started to walk very slowly. She was careful not to let the book fall. It was a little hard, but she did it! Her mom was so proud of her. "Good job, Lily! You did the test and learned to balance better!" Lily smiled and knew she could do anything if she tried.

Once upon a time, there was a strong seal named Sam. Sam lived in the big blue sea. He loved to swim and play with his friends all day. One day, Sam and his friends decided to have a race. They wanted to see who could swim the fastest. They all got ready, and then they started to swim as fast as they could. Sam was very strong, so he swam very fast. He was so fast, he won the race! After the race, Sam and his friends were very tired. They wanted to rest. So, they found a nice, warm rock to settle on. They all lay down on the rock and took a long nap. When they woke up, they felt much better and were ready to play again.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful boy named Tim. He loved to play with his flashlight. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom and dad. He took his flashlight with him. As they walked, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to attach his flashlight to the tree. He thought it would be fun to see the light on the tree. But when he tried to attach it, the flashlight fell and broke. Tim was sad. But then, something unexpected happened. A little girl came up to Tim and gave him a new flashlight. She said, "I saw your flashlight break, so I want to give you mine." Tim was so happy! The little girl was very helpful, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to tease his friend, a big cat named Luna. They liked to play together and run around. One day, Max and Luna were playing near a seat. Max said, "Luna, come sit on the seat with me!" Luna jumped on the seat, but Max moved away. Max laughed and said, "You can't sit with me!" Luna felt hurt. Later, Max saw Luna sitting alone. He felt bad for teasing her. Max went to Luna and said, "I'm sorry for teasing you. Let's sit on the seat together." Luna smiled, and they both sat on the seat, happy to be friends again.

Once upon a time, in a calm wood, there lived a little frog. The frog loved to jump all day long. He would jump from one leaf to another, playing with his friends. One sunny day, the frog saw a big, red ball in the wood. He wanted to play with it. So, he jumped on the ball and it started to roll. The frog was very happy and laughed as he rolled and jumped. After a while, the ball stopped near a big tree. The frog's friends saw him and came to play too. They all jumped and rolled with the big, red ball. The calm wood was filled with joy and laughter as the little animals played together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to relax under the big tree in her yard. One day, Sue saw something shiny near the tree. She felt excited and went to see what it was. Sue found a big box of chocolate. She felt very proud and happy. She wanted to eat the chocolate. But when she opened the box, there was no chocolate inside! Sue felt sad and confused. Suddenly, a tiny mouse jumped out of the box. The mouse had eaten all the chocolate! Sue laughed and played with the mouse. They became friends and relaxed under the big tree together. And that's how Sue found a new friend in the most unexpected way.
