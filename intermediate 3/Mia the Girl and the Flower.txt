Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was very obedient and always listened to her mom and dad. Mia liked to play outside, pick flowers, and watch her favorite show on the screen. One sunny day, Mia went outside to pick some flowers for her mom. She saw a big, red flower and a small, yellow flower. Mia picked the big, red flower and the small, yellow flower. She put them in a pretty vase and showed them to her mom. Her mom was so happy to see the flowers. She gave Mia a big hug and said, "Thank you, Mia! You are such an obedient girl." Then, they sat down together and watched Mia's favorite show on the screen. They laughed and had a fun time together.

Once there was a powerful king. He decided to invite all the people in his kingdom to come to his castle for a big party. Everyone was excited and started getting ready for the event. One of the people was a small 3 year old kid. He was very eager to go to the castle, so one day he decided to visit the king. The kid brought the king a beautiful picture he had made himself. When the kid arrived at the castle, the king was not that happy to see him. He said to the kid: "You don't have an invitation. You can't come to the party." The kid tried to explain that he was just an little excited boy who had made a beautiful picture to give to the king, but nothing could sway the king's mind. The kid sadly returned home with his picture. He felt very disappointed and sad, but nothing could change the king's opinion. The kid knew his invitation had been lost and he could never attend the powerful king's grand party.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. Tom has a new bicycle. It is red and shiny. Lily does not have a bicycle. She has a doll. One day, Tom rides his bicycle to the park. He sees Lily sitting on a bench with her doll. He wants to show off his bicycle. He rides around Lily and says, "Look at me! Look at my bicycle! It is fast and cool. You don't have a bicycle. You only have a doll. Dolls are boring. Bicycles are fun." Lily feels sad and angry. She does not like Tom's words. She thinks Tom is mean. She says, "Stop it, Tom! Stop teasing me! I don't care about your bicycle. I love my doll. She is pretty and soft. You are rude and loud. Go away!" Tom does not stop. He is stubborn. He thinks he is better than Lily. He rides closer to Lily and says, "No, you go away! This is my park. This is my bicycle. You can't play here. You can't touch my bicycle. You are silly and weak." Lily has had enough. She stands up and grabs Tom's bicycle. She pushes it away from him. She says, "No, you are silly and weak! This is not your park. This is our park. You can't bully me. You can't keep your bicycle. I will take it from you." Tom is shocked and scared. He does not expect Lily to do that. He runs after Lily and tries to get his bicycle back. He says, "Hey, give me back my bicycle! That is mine! You are mean and bad!" Lily and Tom fight over the bicycle. They pull and push and shout. They do not see the big dog coming. The big dog likes bicycles. He sees Tom's bicycle and thinks it is a toy. He runs to the bicycle and bites it. He makes a loud noise. He breaks the bicycle. Lily and Tom stop fighting. They look at the big dog and the broken bicycle. They feel sorry and scared. They say, "Oh no! Oh no! What have we done? What will we do?" The big dog runs away with the bicycle. Lily and Tom cry. They hug each other. They say, "I'm sorry. I'm sorry. I was wrong. You are my friend. I don't want to fight with you." They go home and tell their parents what happened. Their parents are not happy. They say, "You should not tease each other. You should not fight over things. You should share and care. You should be kind and gentle." Lily and Tom learn their lesson. They promise to be good friends. They promise to respect each other. They promise to never tease again.
