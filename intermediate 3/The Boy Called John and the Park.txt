Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called John and the Park

Once upon a time there was a boy called John. He was three years old and liked to play. One day, John was playing at the park. As he was playing he saw something behind a tree, it was a cloth! It was the most beautiful colour John had ever seen. He was so excited and very surprised. He ran to the cloth, picked it up and shook it out to have a closer look. Then he noticed something else. The cloth had eyes, ears and a mouth! It was a surprise cloth monster! John was so happy to meet him. He asked the cloth monster what his name was and the cloth monster said his name was Fred. They became friends instantly. But John's parents were very upset, they were shouting and running towards him. John was scared and the cloth monster was scared too. But then, John's parents laughed. They said they were only surprised to see Fred and were not mad at all. John and Fred hugged and laughed. They both were so happy and had lots of fun playing together. The End.

Mum and Dad were walking in the park. The sun was warm, and the grass was soft. Mum said to Dad, "Let's sit down and take a break." They sat down together on the blanket. Dad held a big knot in his hands. Mum said, "What are you holding?" Dad smiled. "Let's make a game! Who can make the biggest knot?" Mum and Dad laughed and held the knot together. They laughed and played until they had to go home. Mum carried the warm knot in her hands. They were both tired but they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock near a small house. Under the rock lived a little bug named Tim. Tim was a mild bug who liked to play with his friend, Sam the ant. One sunny day, Tim and Sam were playing near the rock. They liked to play hide and seek. Tim said, "I will hide and you find me, Sam!" Sam said, "Okay, Tim!" Tim hid under a leaf while Sam looked for him. Sam looked and looked but could not find Tim. Then, Sam saw a small part of Tim under the leaf. Sam said, "I found you, Tim!" Tim smiled and said, "You are good at this game, Sam!" They played all day under the big rock and had lots of fun.

Once there was a girl called Lucy. She was three years old and could not hear anything. Lucy's mum wanted her to learn the value of being helpful. So one day, her mum took her to the train station. When they reached the station, Lucy asked What is this place? Her mum said It's a train station, where people can buy tickets to go places. Then Lucy's mum pointed to a man in front of the ticket office. He seemed very deaf and busy. Lucy's mum said Let's help the man. She asked Lucy to go and push the door of the ticket office. Lucy ran over to the door and pushed it open. The man was so thankful and smiled at Lucy. He gave her a ticket and thanked her. Mum said See, by helping others, you can make someone smile. Lucy was happy too, as she kept the ticket as a reminder of the good deed she had done.

Mum and dad were in the bedroom. Mum said to dad, "Look, it's a very modern bedroom now." Dad nodded and pointed to the window. Mum smiled and said, "Yes, that's our new view." They were both excited. Dad said, "Let's show Mia." So they all went to Mia's bedroom. She was so excited. Mia pointed to the bed and shouted, "Wow, it looks so comfy!" She then ran to the dresser and said, "It's so shiny!" Mum laughed and said, "Yes, the bedroom is very modern." Dad smiled and said, "Let's explore the rest of the house." So they all went out of Mia's bedroom and started exploring the rest of the house. Mia loved it!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to dance. Every day, Lily would put on her favorite music and dance around her room. She felt so excited when she danced. One day, Lily saw a butterfly while she was dancing. The butterfly also loved to dance. It flew around Lily, moving its wings up and down. Lily giggled and tried to copy the butterfly's dance. Lily and the butterfly danced together all day. When the sun started to go down, Lily was tired. She knew it was time to stop dancing. She told the butterfly, "I surrender. I need to rest now." The butterfly understood and flew away. Lily went to bed with a big smile on her face, excited for the next day of dancing.
