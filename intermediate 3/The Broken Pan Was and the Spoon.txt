Category: Intermediate
Title: The Broken Pan Was and the Spoon

One day, a broken pan was sad. It had a big crack and could not hold anything. A spoon saw the pan and said, "Do not worry, I will help you!" The spoon went to find some friends to unite and fix the broken pan. The spoon found a cup, a plate, and a fork. They all wanted to help the broken pan. They talked and thought of how to fix the pan. The cup said, "I have an idea! Let's use some glue to close the crack." They found some glue and worked together to fix the broken pan. They put glue on the crack and held it until it was dry. The pan was very happy and said, "Thank you, friends! You united to help me!" But then, something unexpected happened. The glue did not hold and the crack opened again. The pan was sad, but the spoon said, "Do not worry, we will find another way to fix you!" They all united again and thought of a new plan to help their friend, the broken pan.

Once upon a time, in a big farm, there was a big bull. The bull was very organized. Every day, he would eat, play, and sleep at the same time. All the other animals liked the bull because he was so nice. One day, the farmer left the farm door open. The bull saw the open door and thought, "I should close the door so no one gets lost." He walked to the door and pushed it with his big head. The door closed, and all the animals were safe. The other animals were happy that the bull closed the door. They learned that being organized and helpful is good. From that day, all the animals tried to be more like the bull. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It made the day happy. A girl named Amy loved to paint. She had many pastel colors. She liked to welcome her friends to paint with her. One day, Amy and her friends were painting outside. They saw a big, bright cloud in the sky. They thought it was strange, but they kept painting. They painted the cloud with their pastel colors. Then, something unexpected happened. The bright cloud came down to them. It was not a cloud, but a big, soft, pastel bird! The bird said, "Thank you for painting me. You made me feel welcome." Amy and her friends were so surprised and happy. They painted with the big pastel bird all day long.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with boats in the pond. They have two boats, one is red and one is blue. Lily likes the red boat and Ben likes the blue boat. One day, they go to the pond and see only one boat. It is the red boat. Ben says, "Where is the blue boat?" Lily says, "I don't know. Maybe someone took it." Ben says, "No, you took it. You want both boats. You are bad." Lily says, "No, I didn't take it. You are mean. You don't share." They start to yell at each other. They are very angry. A duck swims by and sees them. The duck says, "Quack, quack, why are you so loud? You are scaring the fish." Lily and Ben stop yelling and look at the duck. The duck says, "What is the problem?" Lily and Ben tell the duck about the boats. The duck says, "Oh, I see. The blue boat is over there, behind the reeds. I moved it because it was in my way. I'm sorry, I didn't know it was yours." Lily and Ben say, "Oh, thank you, duck. We are sorry, too. We should not fight over the boats. We should share and be nice." The duck says, "You're welcome. Quack, quack, have fun." Lily and Ben smile and hug. They take their boats and play together in the pond. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big bottle of perfume that her mom gave her. Lily loved to put the perfume on every day. Her best friend, Tim, did not like the smell of the perfume. Tim would say, "I hate that smell, Lily!" One day, Lily and Tim were playing in the park. Lily put on her perfume before they left the house. When they got to the park, a serious-looking dog came over to them. The dog did not like the smell of Lily's perfume. It started to bark and growl at Lily. Tim said, "Lily, I think the dog hates your perfume too!" Lily felt sad that her friend and the dog did not like her perfume. She decided that she would not wear the perfume when she played with Tim or went to the park. She learned that sometimes, it is important to think about how others feel. The moral of the story is to be considerate of others' feelings and preferences.
