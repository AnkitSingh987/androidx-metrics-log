Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Bag

A small boy named Tim held a bag in his hands. He rolled it around the living room. It made a quiet sound as it rolled across the carpet. Tim was excited as he rolled the bag fast and far. He quickly ran after it and rolled it some more. He was having lots of fun. Suddenly the bag stopped rolling and Tim realized it had reached the corner of the room. He slowly crept towards it, carefully looking around to make sure no one was watching. Tim gently grabbed the bag, looked around one more time, and then rolled it quickly in the opposite direction. He was careful to be very quiet this time, in case his mom was nearby. Tim rolled and ran after the bag until it was time for lunch. He was exhausted, but he smiled as he placed the bag back in its place. He had a lot of fun rolling it around the room.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the pond with his mom. They saw many ducks swimming in the water. Tim had a bag of bread to feed the ducks. He was very happy. "Zip your coat, Tim," said Mom. "It is a bit cold today." Tim zipped his coat and held his mom's hand. They walked to the edge of the pond. Tim threw the bread to the ducks. The ducks quacked and ate the bread. Tim saw a duck trying to eat a hard rock. "No, duck! Don't eat the hard rock!" Tim shouted. The duck looked at Tim and dropped the rock. It swam to Tim and ate the bread he threw. Tim and his mom laughed and went home after a fun day at the pond.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a generous man named Tom. Tom liked to help people. He lived near the woods. Every day, he would pass the woods on his way to the market. One day, while Tom was walking, he heard a small voice. It was a little bird. The bird said, "Help me, please." Tom saw that the bird was stuck in the wood. He gently moved the wood and set the bird free. The bird said, "Thank you, Tom. You are very kind." Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome, little bird. Be careful next time." Then, Tom and the bird said goodbye. Tom continued to pass the woods every day, always ready to help his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fearful cat. The cat was scared of everything. One day, the cat found a big, strong network. The network was made of many strings. The cat had an idea. The cat decided to wear the network like a cape. It thought the network would make it feel brave. So, the cat put the network on its back and tied it around its neck. With the network cape, the fearful cat felt strong. It was not scared anymore. The cat played and had fun with its friends. The cat learned that being brave can help you have fun.

One day, a mysterious thief came to the town. He liked to eat a lot. He would take food from people's houses when they were not looking. The people in the town were sad and did not know what to do. One day, a little girl named Lily saw the thief. She said, "Hey, why do you take our food? We are hungry too!" The thief looked at her and felt bad. He did not know that he was making people sad. The thief said, "I am sorry, I did not know. I will not take your food anymore." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you!" The mysterious thief learned his lesson and never took food from the town again. The people were happy and could eat their food without worry.

Once upon a time, there was a little train named Tim. Tim loved to feel the warm steam puffing from his engine. One day, Tim was chugging along the tracks when he saw a soft, fluffy bunny. "Hello, Bunny!" said Tim. "Do you like the steam from my engine?" Bunny hopped closer and felt the warm steam. "Yes, Tim! It feels nice and soft like my fur," said Bunny. Tim and Bunny became best friends. They played together every day. Tim would puff soft steam for Bunny to feel, and Bunny would hop around happily. They were always there for each other, and they lived happily ever after.
