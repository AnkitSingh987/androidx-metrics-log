Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Bug

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing with her toy. It was a fun toy that could spin. Mia loved to watch it spin round and round. She would spin it fast and slow, and it made her very happy. Mia's mom called her from the other room. "Mia, come here!" she said. Mia left her spinning toy and went to her mom. Her mom showed her a small cell. Inside the cell was a fierce bug. Mia looked at the bug and was a little scared. She had never seen a bug like that before. Mia's mom said, "Don't worry, Mia. This bug is not mean. It just looks fierce. Watch what it can do!" Mia's mom opened the cell and the bug came out. To Mia's surprise, the bug started to spin just like her toy! Mia laughed and clapped her hands. She was not scared of the bug anymore. Now, Mia and the spinning bug were friends, and they had lots of fun spinning together.

One day, a boy and his mom went to the mall. The mall was big and had many shops. They wanted to buy a toy car for the boy. The boy was very happy. After they bought the toy car, they went to the park. It was a mild day, not too hot and not too cold. The boy played with his toy car, while his mom sat on a bench. Suddenly, a big dog came and took the toy car. The boy tried to get it back, but the dog ran away. The boy was very sad. His mom hugged him, but his toy car was gone. The day at the park ended badly.

Once upon a time, there was a child playing outside on a bright day. She had a spade and was busy digging, making big piles of dirt. She stopped and looked up, pointing to something in the distance. "Look, what is that?" asked the child. Her mom paused and then replied, "It looks like a big tree, maybe we can go take a closer look at it later." The child was excited, but moments later they heard a loud sound that made them jump. It was a strange creature walking towards them. The child knew it was too big and loud to be a friend. She was scared, but her mom told her not to worry. The creature kept coming closer and it was getting harder to keep distance. Suddenly, the child's mom picked up the spade and faced the creature with it. The creature paused and then slowly turned around, never bothering them again. The child was so relieved, and hugged her mom for keeping them safe. The two of them went back to playing, even closer and with a lot more care. They both were relieved and happy the day had ended well.

Lily loved to cook with her mom. They made cakes and pies and cookies and bread. Lily liked to mix and stir and pour and taste. She wanted to cook something special for her dad, who worked very hard. Mom said they could make a luxury dish for him. "What is luxury?" Lily asked. "Something very nice and fancy and yummy," Mom said. "Like this big fish we have. We can cook it with lemon and butter and herbs. It will smell and look and taste amazing." Lily helped Mom wash and dry the fish. It was huge, bigger than Lily's arm. Mom cut some lemon and butter and herbs and put them inside the fish. Then she wrapped the fish in foil and put it in the oven. While the fish was cooking, Lily and Mom made a salad and some rice. They also set the table with plates and forks and knives and napkins. Lily made a card for Dad that said, "I love you, Dad. Enjoy your luxury fish." When Dad came home, he smelled the fish and smiled. He hugged Lily and Mom and thanked them for the surprise. He opened the foil and saw the fish. It was golden and juicy and fragrant. He cut a piece and put it in his mouth. He closed his eyes and said, "Mmm, this is the best fish I ever had. You are both amazing cooks. This is a luxury meal." Lily and Mom smiled and clapped. They were happy to make Dad happy. They ate the fish and the salad and the rice. They saved some cake for dessert. They had a wonderful dinner together. Lily thought, "This is what luxury means. To cook and eat and love with my family."
