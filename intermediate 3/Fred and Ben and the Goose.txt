Category: Intermediate
Title: Fred and Ben and the Goose

Once upon a time there were two brothers, Fred and Ben. They were very competitive and loved to compete in games. One day, their father asked them to compete in a game to see who could send a goose the farthest. The brothers eagerly agreed to the challenge and set off on a mission. Ben had a great plan to make sure his goose flew the farthest. He said, “I'm going to take my goose and give it a running start before I send it off!” Fred wasn't quite sure what a running start was, but he quickly agreed. Both brothers sent their geese off and the competition began. Ben's goose flew the furthest, so he won the challenge and was very proud. Fred was happy too, but he wanted to try and compete with Ben again soon.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved to play on the swing. He would sit on the swing and his mom would push him. Tim felt strong when he swung high in the sky. While Tim was on the swing, he saw something behind a big tree. He wanted to know what it was, so he told his mom to stop pushing. Tim got off the swing and walked to the tree. He took a peek and saw a big, strong dog. Tim was scared at first, but then the dog smiled at him. The dog was not mean, it was a nice dog! Tim and the dog became friends and played on the swing together. The dog would push Tim on the swing, and Tim would laugh and have fun. The unexpected friend made Tim's day at the park even better.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to prepare food for her friends. One day, she thought of making a new cake. She was very excited. Sue saw a sign near her house that said, "Cake Contest." She knew she wanted to join. So, she went home and started to make her new cake. She mixed and stirred, and soon the cake was ready. On the day of the contest, Sue carried her cake to the park. Her friend, Tom, saw her and said, "Wow, Sue! Your cake looks yummy!" Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. I hope I win." At the contest, many people tried Sue's cake. They all liked it very much. In the end, Sue won a prize for her new cake. She was very happy and shared her cake with all her friends.

One day, a cool cat named Sam went to the grocery store. He wanted to buy some food for his friends. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Sam felt happy and excited. On his way to the grocery store, Sam saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it. Sam kicked the ball and it rolled away very fast. He ran after the ball, but he forgot to stop at the grocery store. Sam's friends were waiting for him at home. They were hungry and tired. The sun went down and the sky turned dark. Sam never got the food for his friends. He was sad that he didn't stop at the grocery store. The cool cat learned that he should not forget important things.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane who loved to walk. She enjoyed walking down the street and feeling the wind blow in her hair. Every day, Jane would go for a walk in the park, taking in all the sights and sounds. One day, Jane found an ugly pillow lying in the grass. She picked it up and decided to take it home with her. As she walked, she talked to the pillow. "What a funny looking pillow you are," Jane said. When Jane made it home, she put the pillow on her bed and tucked it in amongst her other pillows. Jane was so excited to have a new pillow that she hugged it and said goodnight. As the days went on, Jane and her ugly pillow became very good friends, and they would go for a walk in the park together every day.
