Category: Intermediate
Title: Mum and Dad and the Jack

Mum and Dad took little Jack to see a wild dancer. Jack looked at the dancer with surprise and clapped his hands. "Look at the dancer," said Dad. "She looks so wild!" Little Jack was fascinated by the dancer and he couldn't take his eyes off her. The dancer moved gracefully across the stage and she made a big jump and twisted her body in all directions. Little Jack watched in awe. At the end of the show, the wild dancer completed her dance with a big bow. Everyone in the audience clapped and Little Jack clapped the loudest. "Did you like the dance Little Jack?" asked Dad. Little Jack nodded his head enthusiastically. "Yes, I like it! She is wild!" he said.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion who liked to roar very loud. One day, he found a pretty dress in the distant forest. He thought it would be fun to wear the dress and see what would happen. The lion put on the dress and walked around the forest. All the other animals saw him and laughed. They had never seen a lion in a dress before. The lion felt silly, but he didn't care. He liked the dress and how it made him feel. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl saw the lion in the dress and thought he was a big cat. She was not scared of him at all. The lion was surprised and happy. He made a new friend and they played together all day. The lion learned that it was okay to be different and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a sad animal. This animal was a little bird. The little bird was sad because it could not fly. It tried and tried, but it could not fly like the other birds. The other birds would fly high in the sky, but the little bird would just fall. One day, the little bird met a big and strong bird. The big bird told the little bird that it would help it fly. The little bird was very happy. They tried and tried, but the little bird still could not fly. The big bird had to go away. The little bird was very sad. It tried to fly one more time, but it fell to the ground. The little bird did not finish learning how to fly. The little bird stayed sad and could not fly with the other birds.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. Every night, when it was time to sleep, he felt scared. His mom knew what to do. She gave him a small candle. The candle was not scary. It gave a soft, warm light. One night, there was a big storm outside. Tim heard the loud wind and rain. He felt very scared. But then, he remembered his candle. He lit the candle and saw its warm light. It made him feel better. The candle's light comforted him. After the storm, Tim was not scared of the dark anymore. He learned that sometimes, a little light can make scary things go away. The moral of the story is to always look for the light in dark times.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot was a very good dog who loved to help. He had a big purple ball that he played with every day. Spot would run and jump with his purple ball, and everyone in the town loved to watch him play. One day, Spot was playing with his purple ball when a little girl named Sally came up to him. "Can you help me, Spot?" she asked. "I lost my toy and I can't find it." Spot wagged his tail and nodded. He wanted to help Sally find her toy. Spot and Sally walked around the town, looking for the lost toy. They looked by the trees, near the pond, and even inside the store. But they couldn't find the toy anywhere. Just when they were about to give up, Spot saw something shiny under the big purple ball. He moved the ball and found Sally's toy! It was hiding under his ball the whole time. Sally was so happy and thanked Spot for his help. From that day on, Spot and Sally became the best of friends and played together every day.
