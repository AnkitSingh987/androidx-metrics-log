Category: Intermediate
Title: Snow the White Cat and the Park

One day, a white cat named Snow found a toy pistol in the park. Snow did not know who the toy pistol belonged to. Snow wanted to find the owner, so she asked her friend, a dog named Spot. "Spot, do you know who this toy pistol belongs to?" Snow asked. "No, but let's go ask our friend Bird," replied Spot. As they walked to find Bird, they saw a little boy crying. The little boy was looking for his toy pistol. Snow and Spot went to the boy and showed him the toy pistol. "Is this yours?" Snow asked the boy. "Yes, it is! Thank you so much!" the boy said, smiling. Snow and Spot were happy that they could help the boy find his toy pistol. They all played together in the park and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a pink cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to eat soup. One day, she went to the store to buy some soup. At the store, Kitty saw a big dog named Doggy. Doggy wanted the last can of soup, too. Kitty and Doggy started to fight. They both said, "I want the soup!" A nice lady came and said, "Stop fighting, Kitty and Doggy! I have more soup at my house." Kitty and Doggy stopped fighting and followed the nice lady. They went to her house and ate soup together. They became good friends and never fought again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. His name was Tim. Tim had a dog. The dog's name was Sam. Tim and Sam liked to play outside. One day, it was very cold. Tim and Sam went to find a warm hat. They looked in the house. They looked in the yard. They could not find a hat. Tim was sad. Sam was sad too. Then, Sam had an idea. He ran to the door. Tim opened the door. Sam ran outside. He came back with a hat. Tim was happy. He put on the hat. They played outside all day. The moral of the story is to never give up.

Once upon a time there was a red bird named Joe. Joe was very happy and sang a song all day long. One day, Joe was warned by a wise owl. The wise owl said, “Math is coming and it's very bad!” Joe was scared and ran away. Joe ran and ran until he came to a red castle. He thought this would be the perfect place to hide, so he stepped inside. But as soon as Joe stepped inside the castle, math appeared. Joe was so scared he didn't know what to do. Then, the wise owl flew in with a big book. The wise owl said, “Don’t be scared. This book is your math lesson. I was trying to warn you that you needed to learn math!” Joe was relieved and the wise owl taught him how to do math. Joe learned his math lessons and was no longer scared of math. He continued to fly around and sang his songs, always grateful for the wise owl's warning.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her toys. Her favorite toy was a big, red hospital. She played with it every day. One day, Amy's teddy bear felt weak. She knew that her bear needed help. So, she took the bear to her hospital. Amy played doctor and made her bear feel better. After a while, the teddy bear was not weak anymore. Amy was so happy that her bear was better. They played together all day long. And from that day on, Amy and her teddy bear always had fun at the hospital.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely needle who lived in a big sewing box. The needle had no friends because it was very sharp, and all the other things in the box were afraid of getting hurt. One day, a small, friendly thread came to live in the box, and the lonely needle was very happy. The needle said to the thread, "Will you be my friend? We can make pretty things together." The thread was scared but wanted to help, so it said, "Okay, but you must be very careful not to hurt me." The needle promised to be gentle, and they started to make a beautiful dress. But the needle had a secret. It was not very clean. It had been hiding in the dirt before it came to live in the sewing box. As the needle and thread worked together, the dirty needle made the thread sick. The thread became weak and broke. The needle was very sad and cried, "I'm so sorry, my friend. I should have cleaned myself before we played together." The thread said, "It's too late now. I am very sick and cannot play anymore." The needle was very sorry for what it had done, but it could not fix its mistake. The thread was too sick to be friends with the needle, and the needle stayed lonely forever.
