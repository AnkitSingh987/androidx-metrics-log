Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Door

Once upon a time, in a lively little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, it started to get very cold. Tim felt the cold air on his face and saw his breath in the air. He knew it was going to freeze tonight. Tim had a small, blue door in his room. Behind the door was a magical open space where he could play with his toys. When it was time to sleep, Tim would close the door and dream of his fun day. That night, Tim had an idea. He opened the small, blue door and saw the magical open space. He thought, "I will bring the freeze inside!" He opened his window and let the cold air in. The open space started to freeze, and Tim was so happy to see the ice and snow. In the morning, Tim woke up and saw the frozen open space. He put on his warm clothes and went to play. He slid on the ice, made a snowman, and had a snowball fight with his toys. Tim had the best day ever in his lively little town. When the day was done, Tim closed the small, blue door and went to sleep. He knew that the next time it was going to freeze, he could have another fun day in his magical open space. And so, Tim and his lively little town lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Tim. Tim loved to swim all day in the big blue sea. One day, Tim found a soft pillow floating on the water. He was very happy because he could rest his head on the pillow after swimming. But Tim was worried. He didn't know if he could keep the pillow safe in the sea. He swam around, asking his fish friends for help. His friend, the crab, said, "Don't worry, Tim! I will help you keep your pillow safe." Tim and the crab swam together, holding the pillow tight. They found a cozy spot under a big rock to keep the pillow safe. Now, Tim could swim and play all day, and when he was tired, he could rest his head on his soft pillow. Tim was not worried anymore, and he was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful goose. The goose lived on a farm with many friends. One day, the goose saw a little boy crying near the pond. The goose went to the boy and asked, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my toy in the pond." The helpful goose wanted to make the boy happy again. So, the goose said, "I will find your toy for you." The goose started to look for the toy in the pond. It swam and looked under the water. Soon, the goose found the toy and gave it back to the boy. The boy was very happy and said, "Thank you, helpful goose!" They became good friends and played together every day.

Ben and Lily were bored. They had no toys to play with. Their mom was busy in the kitchen. She said they could not go outside because it was raining. "Let's play march!" Ben said. He liked to pretend he was a soldier. He found a stick and a hat. He gave Lily a scarf and a spoon. "Okay, how do we play?" Lily asked. "We march around the room and say 'left, right, left, right'," Ben said. He showed her how to hold the stick and the spoon. "And we pretend we have a vehicle. A big, strong vehicle that can go anywhere." "What kind of vehicle?" Lily said. "Um, a truck. A dull, gray truck. Like the one dad has," Ben said. "Okay, let's go!" Lily said. She followed Ben around the room. They marched and said 'left, right, left, right'. They made noises like a truck. 'Vroom, vroom, beep, beep'. Their mom heard them and smiled. She was glad they were having fun. She finished making lunch and called them. "Ben, Lily, come and eat!" she said. They stopped marching and ran to the kitchen. They were hungry and happy. They told their mom about their game. "That sounds like a fun game," she said. "But you know what? I think your truck is not dull. I think it is shiny and colorful. Like a rainbow." Ben and Lily looked at each other and laughed. They liked their mom's idea. They decided to play march again after lunch. But this time, they would have a rainbow truck.
