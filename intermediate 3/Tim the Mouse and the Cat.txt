Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Mouse and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Tim. Tim was very quiet and liked to relax in his little home. One day, Tim went out to find some food. While Tim was looking for food, he saw a big piece of cheese. He was very happy but did not know it was a trap. Tim ran to the cheese and tried to take it. Suddenly, the trap closed on Tim's tail. Tim was scared and cried for help. A big cat named Sam heard him and came to see what was wrong. Sam saw the trap and helped Tim get free. Tim was very thankful and they became friends. From that day on, they both played together and had fun.

John and his mom were walking to the park on a beautiful day. John was excited to go and kept saying, "More they try, more they go." His mom was confused, but smiled and said, "Yes, I think that's right. We'll try our best to get there!" It was a long walk, but luckily the sun was shining and the flowers were in full bloom. John looked around at all the sights, and then he spotted something in the grass - a bee! "Look, Mom!" he said. "A bee!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, that's a bee. Let's see where it's going!" The bee flew for what felt like forever, until it finally stopped at a tree. Right at the top of the tree was a beehive! "It's full of honey," said John. "That bee is going to try and get some honey!" His mom nodded, "Yes, honey comes from bee hives." John and his mom kept walking, until they finally arrived at the park. They sat down on a bench and watched the bees as they tried to get honey from the hives. John was amazed at how hard they tried, and when they finally got some honey, he clapped excitedly. "That was such a long try," said John, his eyes wide with wonder.

Once upon a time, a little bird named Bobby wanted to fly high in the sky. He loved to feel the warm sun on his wings. One day, while Bobby was sitting on a dry tree branch, he saw a big bird flying towards him. "Hello, big bird!" said Bobby. "I want to fly high like you!" The big bird smiled and said, "Hello, little bird! I can help you fly high. Just flap your wings and follow me!" Bobby flapped his wings and started to fly. He followed the big bird as they flew higher and higher. They flew so high that they could touch the sun. Bobby felt happy and free. After a while, they came back down to the dry tree. Bobby thanked the big bird for helping him fly high. They became good friends and flew together every day, enjoying the warm sun and the wide sky.

One day, a busy ant named Tim was marching to find food. He saw a big, tall sunflower. Tim thought, "Wow! That sunflower looks yummy. I want to eat it." Tim climbed up the sunflower. When he got to the top, he met a friendly bee named Bella. Bella said, "Hi, I am Bella. What are you doing here?" Tim said, "I want to eat this sunflower." Bella laughed and said, "You can't eat the whole sunflower, but I can show you something better." Bella took Tim to a hidden part of the sunflower. There, they found lots of sweet honey. Tim tasted the honey and said, "This is better than the sunflower! Thank you, Bella." They both became good friends and shared the honey with their friends. From that day on, Tim and Bella always looked for new yummy things together.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy car. He felt like he needed to use the toilet, but he was scared. He did not dare to go alone. So, he asked his big sister, Sue, to go with him. "Please, Sue, come with me to the toilet. I am scared," Tim said. Sue smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will go with you. It is safe." When they got to the toilet, they saw something unexpected. There was a big, green frog sitting on the toilet seat! Tim and Sue were surprised. They did not know what to do. Sue said, "Let's tell mom about the frog." They ran to find their mom and told her about the frog on the toilet. Mom laughed and said, "That is very funny! Let's go see the frog together." Mom, Tim, and Sue went to the toilet, and the frog was still there. Mom gently picked up the frog and took it outside. "Now the toilet is safe for you to use, Tim," she said. Tim was happy and not scared anymore. He used the toilet, and from that day on, he was not scared to go alone.
