Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Painting

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was restless and always liked to play. One day, Mia found a big bottle of ink in her room. She was very happy and thought of a fun game to play with the ink. Mia went to her friend Tom and said, "Tom, I have a fun game! Let's bring this ink and play with it outside!" Tom was excited and agreed to play with Mia. They both ran outside with the big bottle of ink, ready to have fun. As they played with the ink, they made a big mess on the ground. Suddenly, it started to rain. The rain washed away all the ink, making a beautiful painting on the ground. Mia and Tom were very happy to see their art. They knew that the ink they brought outside made a special memory for them.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy ball. The ball liked to roll all day long. It would roll down the road and say hello to all the animals. The animals liked the ball because it was fun to play with. One day, the ball saw a fashion show. The animals were wearing pretty clothes. The ball wanted to join the fashion show, but it was just a big, heavy ball. It did not have any clothes to wear. The ball was very sad. A kind squirrel saw the ball and had an idea. The squirrel found some leaves and sticks and helped the ball make a pretty dress. The ball joined the fashion show and all the animals clapped. The ball was very happy. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others. When you help others, you make them happy, and you will be happy too.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the ocean with his mom. Tim loved the water and the sand. He played and splashed all day. Tim saw a big, blue bottle on the beach. He picked it up and opened it. A strange, purple cloud came out of the bottle. The cloud spoke, "I am a magic cloud! I can shrink things. Tell me what you want to shrink, and I will do it." Tim thought for a moment and said, "Shrink the ocean! I want to see all the fish." The cloud agreed and started to shrink the ocean. As the ocean shrank, the fish had no place to swim. The fish were sad and scared. Tim felt guilty. He knew he made a mistake. The ocean was almost gone, and the fish were not happy. Tim asked the cloud to make the ocean big again, but the cloud said, "I can only shrink things, not make them big." The ocean was now too small for the fish to live. Tim cried as he watched the fish struggle. His mom hugged him and said, "We must learn from our mistakes and be more careful next time." Tim felt very sad and guilty for what he had done. He wished he never found the magic cloud.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Spot. Spot had a basket that he loved to carry around with him. One day, Spot's mom asked him to take the basket to his friend, Cat. Spot walked and walked until he found Cat. He said, "I have a basket for you." Cat looked at the basket and saw that it was empty. "What is it for?" Cat asked. Spot did not know, but he felt that it was important. "I don't know, but we should keep it," Spot replied. Later, they played near a tree. They saw a bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. Cat said, "We can help the bird!" Spot remembered the basket. They put the bird in the basket and took it to a safe place. The bird got better and thanked Spot and Cat. Spot was happy that he kept the basket.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there lived a girl named Sally. She loved to play with her friends at the park every day. One day, Sally had to choose what to wear before going to the park. At noon, Sally met her friends at the park. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. They played on the swings, slide, and seesaw. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. It was a very fun day. When it was time to go home, Sally and her friends said goodbye. They promised to play at the park again tomorrow. Sally went home, had dinner, and went to bed. She dreamed of the busy park and all the fun she would have with her friends the next day.
