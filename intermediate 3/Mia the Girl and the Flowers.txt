Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Flowers

One day, a little girl named Mia got an invitation. It was for a fun party. Mia was so happy. She wanted to dress in her best clothes. She put on a pretty dress with flowers on it. At the party, Mia met a nosy boy named Tom. Tom asked, "What is in your bag?" Mia said, "It is a surprise for later." Tom wanted to know, but Mia did not tell him. Tom was not happy. He wanted to find out the surprise. Tom tried to take Mia's bag when she was not looking. But, Mia saw him. She said, "No, Tom! It is a surprise!" Just then, the bag opened. Out came a big, funny clown! Everyone laughed. Tom was sorry for being nosy. Mia forgave him, and they all had fun at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a long pen. The pen lived in a box. One day, the pen wanted to go outside and play. The pen tried to unlock the box. It pushed and pushed, but the box did not open. Then, something unexpected happened. A big hand came and opened the box for the pen! The pen was so happy to be outside. It played and played all day long. But then, the big hand came back. The big hand was not a friend. It was a big dog! The dog wanted to eat the pen. The pen was scared and ran back to the box. The box was safe and the pen was happy to be home.

One day, a furry cat named Kitty went for a walk. She saw a big tree with small round things on it. She thought they were prunes. Kitty wanted to eat them, so she climbed the tree. When Kitty got to the top, she saw a big bird. The bird said, "Hi, Kitty! What are you doing up here?" Kitty said, "I want to eat the prunes!" The bird laughed and said, "These are not prunes, they are apples!" Kitty was sad but the bird had an idea. "I will show you where the prunes are!" The bird flew away and Kitty followed. They went to a little bush. The bird moved the leaves to reveal the prunes. Kitty was very happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" They both ate the prunes and became good friends. From that day on, they always played and ate prunes together.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted boy named Tim who had a big dream. He wanted to lock away all the bad things in the world. One day, he found a magic box that could do just that. Tim went around and said, "I can lock away all the bad things. Let me help you!" The people were happy and gave him their bad things to lock away. He put them all in the magic box. But then, something unexpected happened. The box shook and opened up. All the bad things came out and made a big mess. Tim was sad and the people were sad too. In the end, Tim learned that he couldn't lock away all the bad things, but he could try to make the world a better place by being kind and helpful.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He wanted to find something fun to play with. He saw a big, purple ball. Tim kicked the ball and it went far away. He ran after it to get it back. As Tim got closer to the ball, he saw something big and furry. It was a gorilla! The gorilla was holding Tim's purple ball. Tim was scared at first, but then he saw that the gorilla was smiling. The gorilla wanted to play with the ball too! Tim and the gorilla played together with the purple ball. They laughed and had lots of fun. After playing, the gorilla gave Tim a big hug. Then, the gorilla walked back into the forest. Tim was so happy he got to play with a new friend. He couldn't wait to tell his mom about the fun day he had with the purple ball and the gorilla.

Once upon a time, there was a little tiger cub who got lost in the forest. It was getting dark, and the tiger cub was very scared. Suddenly, the cub heard a pleasant voice. "Don't be afraid - I'm here to help you," the voice said. The cub looked up and saw a kind old elephant who had come to the rescue. "I'm sorry I scared you," the elephant said, smiling. He handed the cub a jar full of honey. "Here, have some honey to help you feel better." The tiger cub gratefully ate the honey and thanked the elephant, who smiled and said, "You're welcome! I'm glad I could help." The tiger cub happily made it home that night, safe and sound. He was thankful for the elephant's rescue.
