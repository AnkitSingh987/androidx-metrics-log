Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia and Sam and the Fish

Mia and Sam liked to go to the zoo with their mom. They loved to see the animals and learn their names. Their favorite animal was the otter. The otter was lively and playful. It swam and slid and splashed in the water. One day, they went to the zoo and saw a new sign. It said: "Do not feed the otter. It is sick." Mia and Sam felt sad for the otter. They wanted to make it happy. They saw a man selling fish near the otter's pool. They asked their mom for some money. She gave them some coins and told them to spend them wisely. Mia and Sam ran to the man and bought some fish. They thought they were being kind. They threw the fish into the otter's pool. The otter saw the fish and got excited. It ate the fish quickly. It did not know the fish were bad for it. Soon, the otter started to feel sick. It coughed and wheezed and lay on the ground. Mia and Sam saw the otter and got scared. They ran to their mom and told her what they did. Their mom was angry and worried. She called the zookeeper and told him what happened. The zookeeper came and checked the otter. He shook his head and said: "You should not have fed the otter. It is allergic to fish. It might not survive." Mia and Sam felt sorry and guilty. They cried and said: "We are sorry, Mr. Zookeeper. We did not know. We wanted to help the otter." The zookeeper said: "I know, but you should have followed the sign. Now the otter might die because of you." Mia and Sam learned a hard lesson. They wished they could take back what they did. They hoped the otter would get better. But it was too late. The otter did not make it. It died that night. Mia and Sam were very sad. They never forgot the otter. They never fed the animals again.

Once upon a time, there was a young dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, while he was playing, the ball went far, far away. Spot looked and looked for his ball, but he could not find it. He was worried. Spot asked his friends, the cat and the bird, to help him find his ball. They looked high and low, near and far. They looked under the tree and behind the bush. But the ball was still gone. It seemed to vanish! Finally, the young dog, the cat, and the bird found the ball. It was under a big rock. They were all very happy. Spot was not worried anymore. They played with the ball all day long. And they knew they could always find it, even if it tried to vanish again.

One day, a big dog named Sam and a little cat named Mia were playing in the house. They were running and jumping on the floor. They were having a lot of fun. The sun was shining through the window, making the room warm and bright. Suddenly, Mia said, "Stop, Sam! I smell something bitter!" Sam stopped and sniffed the air too. They both smelled the bitter smell coming from the kitchen. They ran to the kitchen to find out what was making the smell. When they got to the kitchen, they saw a big cake on the table. But the cake was not sweet, it was bitter! The cake was made of lemons, not sugar. Sam and Mia laughed and said, "This is a funny cake!" They decided to eat the cake anyway and had a great time.

Sam was walking in the garden, being very thoughtful. He wanted to pick something nice for his Mom. He saw a radish and thought it would be perfect! He pinched himself and said, "Go for it!" Sam carefully digged the radish and took it home. He was very excited and ran inside to show it to his Mom. When his Mom saw the radish, her eyes lit up, but then she frowned. "I'm sorry, Sam, but I really need carrots for dinner." Sam was disappointed, but he knew the radish would still be useful. So he said, “It’s okay, Mom! I will go pick some carrots!” And with that, Sam went out to the garden and got some carrots for his Mom. He was very thoughtful, and his Mom was delighted.
