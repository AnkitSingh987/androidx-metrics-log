Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Sausage

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play, run, and eat. One day, Spot found a delicate sausage on the ground. It was a very yummy sausage. Spot wanted to eat it all up. Spot picked up the sausage and ran to his favorite spot to lie down. He was so happy to eat his yummy sausage. But then, he saw his friend, Kitty. Kitty looked hungry too. Spot thought about it and decided to share his delicate sausage with Kitty. They both ate the sausage together and were very happy. After eating, they played and had lots of fun. Spot and Kitty were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a red taxi. The red taxi liked to help people get to where they needed to go. One day, the red taxi was driving down the street when it saw a little girl named Lucy. Lucy said, "Taxi, can you help me get to my friend's house?" The red taxi said, "Yes, I can help you. Get in, and I will take you there." Lucy got in the red taxi, and they drove down the street. As they drove, Lucy and the red taxi talked and laughed. They had a lot of fun. Soon, they got to her friend's house. Lucy said, "Thank you, red taxi, for helping me get here." The red taxi smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lucy. Have fun with your friend!" And with that, the red taxi drove away, ready to help someone else get where they needed to go.

One day, a little white cat named Snowy was very excited. She wanted to play with her friend, Brownie the dog. Snowy went to Brownie's house and said, "Let's act like we are in a big race!" Brownie agreed, and they both started to run. But then, they saw a big tree in their way. Snowy was scared, but Brownie said, "Don't worry, we can jump over it!" They jumped over the tree and kept running. Soon, they saw a big hill. Snowy was even more scared now. "What if we fall?" she asked. Brownie smiled and said, "We can do it! Just be brave!" So, they climbed the big hill and made it to the top. Snowy was so happy that she had faced her fears. She thanked Brownie for helping her and they both agreed that it was the best race ever.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Jane. Jane was three years old and she had an apple tree in her garden. She would go to the tree every day and pick an apple. One day, Jane went to the tree to pick an apple but it was gone. She looked around and noticed something weird. All around her, there were apple cores laying on the ground. Jane was very surprised. She asked her mom: "Mom! Where is my apple?" Her mom replied: "There must have been a weird creature that took your apple! Don't worry though, you can pick another one tomorrow". Jane nodded and said: "I miss my apple". Her mom hugged her and said: "I know it's hard to miss something you love, but it's also a good opportunity to try to find another one". So Jane went out the next day and found another apple. She was very happy and never missed the old one again!

One day, a little boy named Tim found a dry cord in his toy box. He did not know what it was for. So, he decided to ask his mom. "Mom, what is this?" Tim showed her the cord. "Oh, that is a cord to pull your toy car," she said. Tim was very happy to find out what the cord was for. Tim went outside to play with his toy car and the cord. He pulled the cord and the car went very fast. Suddenly, a small dog came running and took the cord in its mouth. Tim was surprised and didn't know what to do. The dog ran away with the cord, but then it stopped and looked at Tim. The dog wanted to play too! So, Tim and the dog played together with the cord and the toy car. They had a lot of fun and became good friends.
