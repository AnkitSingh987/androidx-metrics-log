Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Balloon

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the station with his mom. They wanted to take a train to visit grandma. At the station, Tim saw a big red balloon. He wanted it so much, but his mom said "No, not today." Tim felt sad and a little guilty. He knew he should listen to his mom, but he really wanted the balloon. So, he tried to get the balloon by himself. He jumped and jumped, but the balloon was too high. Tim started to struggle. A nice man saw Tim struggle and said, "Do you need help?" Tim looked at the man and nodded. The man got the balloon for Tim. Tim said, "Thank you!" and ran back to his mom. When his mom saw the balloon, she smiled and said, "I see you got your balloon. Let's go visit grandma now." Tim was happy and they took the train to grandma's house.

Once upon a time, in a small home, there was a bright ornament. It was very pretty and shiny. It lived on a tree with many other ornaments. They all liked to play together. But the bright ornament always wanted to be the best. One day, a strong wind blew through the home. The tree shook and the ornaments fell off. The bright ornament did not want to fall. It tried to remain on the tree, but it fell too. The other ornaments were sad, but the bright ornament was very sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl found the bright ornament on the floor. She picked it up and put it back on the tree. The bright ornament was happy again. It learned that it did not need to be the best. All the ornaments were special in their own way. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man who lived in a big house. He had a fast car and loved to drive it. One day, he was driving around the town when he saw a small boy walking in his shadow. "Why are you walking in the shade?" asked the rich man. The boy said, "If I am in the sun, it is too hot for me, so I am walking in my shadow to keep me cool." The rich man thought about this and decided to give the boy a ride in his car. He said, "Come with me, I will drive you home." The boy happily hopped into the car and the rich man drove him home. The moral of the story is that helping others can make us feel rich, even if we don't have much money. It's important to share kindness and be generous.

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing in her room. She had a toy car that she loved. It was a fast car that she liked to push around the room. At night, she would put the car next to her bed and go to sleep. One night, Sue heard a noise coming from her toy car. She got up and saw the car talking to her. "Sue, I need your help," said the car. "My home is very far away and I need to go back. Can you help me?" Sue was surprised but wanted to help her toy car. She got on the car and it started to go very fast. As they were going, they saw a big fire. The fire was burning brightly. Sue and the car stopped to help put out the fire. They found a big bucket of water and threw it on the fire. The fire was gone and everyone was safe. The toy car thanked Sue for her help and said, "You are very brave, Sue. Now, I can go back to my home." Sue was sad to say goodbye but knew her toy car needed to go. She waved goodbye as the car went away. The next morning, Sue woke up and saw her toy car next to her bed. She was happy to see it again. It was a dream! She hugged her toy car and knew they would always be together.
