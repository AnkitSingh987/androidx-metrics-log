Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was an adventurous girl who loved to play and explore. One day, she went into her mom's room to look for fun things to do. In the room, Lily saw lots of furniture. She saw a big bed, a tall chair, and a small table. She wanted to play with them all. So, she started to jump on the bed, sit on the chair, and touch the table. While playing, Lily saw a ball. She wanted to strike the ball with her hand. She hit the ball very hard, and it flew across the room. The ball hit the small table, and the table fell down. The table broke, and Lily was sad. Her mom came in and saw the mess. Lily got in trouble, and she was not allowed to play in her mom's room anymore.

One day, a little bird named Tim was in his nest. Tim was a fragile bird and he would worry a lot. He had a big idea to fly high in the sky. He told his mom, "I want to fly high like you." His mom said, "But you are still small, Tim. You need to grow more before you can fly high." Tim was sad but he still wanted to try. He jumped out of the nest and started to flap his wings. He was scared but he tried his best. As Tim flew, he saw a big tree. He did not see it before. He was so happy with his new idea. He flew to the tree and found many new friends there. Tim learned that even if he was fragile and worried, he could still have big ideas and make new friends. The unexpected thing was that he could fly higher than he thought.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big toy box in her room. One day, she found a small key inside the box. Amy was very excited and wanted to find out what the key could open. Amy went to the kitchen and saw her mom pour milk into a cup. She asked her mom if the key could open the milk. Her mom laughed and said, "No, Amy, keys are for doors and locks, not for milk." Amy felt a little uncomfortable because she didn't know that. Amy went around the house trying the key in different doors and locks. Finally, she found a tiny door in the corner of her room. The key fit perfectly! She opened the door and found a secret room filled with toys and games. Amy was so happy she found the right place for the key. She played in the secret room all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a pale, round face and big, blue eyes. Mia loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Mia saw a small, lost kitten. The kitten was scared and hungry. Mia wanted to help it. She said, "Don't be afraid, I will show you where to find food." Mia took a step and the kitten followed her. They walked together to Mia's house. Mia's mom gave the kitten some milk and food. The kitten was very happy. Mia and the kitten became best friends. They played together every day. The kitten was not lost and scared anymore. And Mia was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play in his yard with his big ball. One day, he saw an enormous box near the tap. He did not know what was inside the box. Tim went to his mom to explain what he saw. His mom was very curious too. They both went outside to see the box. They tried to open it, but it was locked. Tim and his mom were sad, but they kept trying. Then, something unexpected happened. The box opened on its own, and out came a big, friendly dog! They were all very happy and surprised. The dog became Tim's best friend, and they played together every day.
