Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Barn and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. In the barn, there were many animals. There were cows, pigs, and chickens. The animals were happy and loved to play. One day, a little bird came to the barn. The bird saw a big mud puddle near the barn. The bird thought it would be fun to dive into the puddle. So, the bird dove in and got very dirty. The bird was covered in mud and looked disgusting. A pig saw the bird and said, "You look funny! You should clean up!" The bird agreed and flew to a pond to wash off the mud. The pig and the other animals laughed and continued to play. The bird felt better after washing off the mud and joined the animals in the barn. They all played together and had a great time.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a hoop. The hoop was not like any other hoop. It had a soft glow that made her eyes light up. Lily thought it was the best hoop she had ever seen. She wanted to show her friends the glowing hoop. Lily took the hoop to the park. She saw her friends playing there. They saw the hoop and thought it was very special too. Lily's friends took turns playing with the glowing hoop. They felt rich with joy and happiness. As the sun began to set, Lily and her friends played with the glowing hoop one last time. They watched as the hoop's glow grew brighter in the dark. They knew they had found something truly special. With smiles on their faces, they said goodbye and went home, excited to play with the glowing hoop again soon.

One day, a delicate little bird named Lily lived on an island. She had many friends. They liked to play and sing. Lily was happy on her island. One sunny day, Lily and her friends saw a big group of ants. The ants were marching. "Where are you going?" asked Lily. "We are going to find food," said the ants. Lily and her friends decided to follow the ants and help them. As they marched, they saw a big hill. The ants started to climb, but they could not do it. Lily had an idea. She and her friends picked up the ants and flew them to the top of the hill. The ants found a lot of food there. They were so happy and said, "Thank you, Lily and friends!" Now, the ants and the birds were friends too.

One day, Grandma was walking in the park. She saw a little girl playing with a note. Grandma asked the girl why she was playing with it. The girl smiled and said that it was a special note that she had made for her mom. Grandma was glad. But then, grandma saw the little girl trying to quit playing and hide the note behind her back. Grandma asked her why she wanted to quit playing. The little girl said that she was scared. She was afraid her mom would be mad if she found out what she had done. Grandma was worried. Grandma told the girl that it was ok to make the note, but to fill it with nice things. Grandma helped the girl write down all the love and care she felt for her mom and then she gave the little girl a hug. The little girl was glad. Grandma said goodbye and the little girl ran off to show her mom the note. Grandma smiled, knowing that the little girl had found out that it was ok to show her mom her love.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, Amy's mom asked her to count the cups in the cupboard. "Amy, can you count the cups in the cupboard for me, please?" she asked. Amy opened the cupboard and started counting. "One, two, three, four, five!" she said excitedly. Amy's mom was very happy and gave her a big hug. "Good job, Amy!" she said. One day, Amy saw a poor boy outside her house. He looked sad and hungry. Amy remembered the cups in the cupboard and thought of an idea. She asked her mom if they could give the boy some food. Her mom agreed, and they filled a cup with yummy soup for the boy. The boy was very thankful and smiled. Amy felt good helping someone in need. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others when you can.
