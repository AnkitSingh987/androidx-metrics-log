Category: Intermediate
Title: Sarah the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. One day she saw a big and shiny bear in the woods. She wanted to follow the bear, so she took off her shoes and started walking barefoot. Sarah kept walking further and further. Soon, she was so deep into the woods that she couldn't find her way back home. She was scared and began to cry when she saw the bear again. But this time, it wasn't the big and shiny bear she was looking for. Instead, she saw a small and weak toe sticking out of the ground! Sarah was so excited and asked the toe: "Can you lead me home?" The toe was surprised and answered: "I wish I could little one, but I'm too weak. Maybe you should look for a stronger animal. Look for a horse, or maybe even a deer!" After a few more minutes of searching, Sarah found a deer that was very strong and proud. She thanked the little toe and followed the deer all the way home. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a best friend named Tim. They loved to play together. One day, they found a big hill. The hill had two sides. One side was sunny and had pretty flowers. The other side was dark and had scary trees. Sue said, "I recommend we go to the sunny side. It is perfect for playing." But Tim wanted to be brave. He said, "No, let's go to the dark side. It will be fun." Sue did not want to go to the dark side. She was scared. But she did not want Tim to be alone. So, they went to the dark side together. As they played, it got darker and darker. They could not see well. They got lost. They tried to find their way back, but they could not. Sue and Tim were very sad. They wished they had stayed on the sunny side.

Once upon a time, in a little house, lived a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom had soft fur, and the texture was like a warm blanket. He liked to sleep all day and play all night. Tom had a friend, a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily asked Tom, "Can you lend me your toy, please?" Tom looked at his toy and then at Lily. He thought for a moment, and then said, "Okay, you can play with my toy." Lily was happy and played with the toy all day. Tom was still grumpy, but he liked seeing Lily happy. The next day, Lily gave Tom's toy back to him. She said, "Thank you for lending me your toy, Tom." Tom was not grumpy anymore. He learned that sharing can make others happy. From that day on, Tom and Lily shared their toys and played together every day. And they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to build things with her toy blocks. One day, she decided to build a big house for her dolls. She was very happy. Sue's mommy was in the kitchen making a pie. Sue smelled the pie and it was very yummy. She wanted to eat the pie, but mommy said she had to wait until after dinner. Sue went outside to play in the dirt. She got very filthy from playing. When she came inside, mommy said, "Sue, you are so filthy! You need to wash your hands before you can eat the pie." Sue washed her hands and then she got to eat the yummy pie.

Once upon a time, there was a round ball named Rollie. Rollie loved to roll all day long. He rolled up hills and down hills, and he always had a big smile on his face. One sunny day, Rollie saw his friends playing a game. The game was called "Win the Race." Rollie wanted to play and win too! So, he joined the game and started to roll as fast as he could. Rollie rolled so fast that he won the race! All of his friends clapped and cheered for him. But then, Rollie felt a little guilty. He knew that he was good at rolling, and that made the game easy for him. Rollie decided to help his friends learn how to roll faster too. They all practiced together, and soon everyone could roll fast. Now, they could all have fun playing the game and trying to win together.
