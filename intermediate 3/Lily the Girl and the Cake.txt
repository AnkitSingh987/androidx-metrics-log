Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with dough. One day, she wanted to make a big cake with her dough. She knew she would need a lot of dough to make her cake. So, she asked her mom for help. Lily's mom helped her make more dough. They mixed and rolled the dough together. But, when they tried to put the dough in the cake pan, it broke. The dough was broken. Lily was sad. Her mom said, "Don't worry, Lily. We can fix the broken dough." They rolled the dough again and put it in the pan very carefully. This time, the dough did not break. Lily was happy. She and her mom made a big cake with the dough. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, a lively little boat named Bob wanted to sail. But Bob was in a big field, not in the water. He looked around and saw many pretty flowers and green trees, but no water. One day, a friendly bird named Tim came to the field. Tim saw Bob and asked, "Why are you in the field, boat? You should be in the water." Bob said, "I want to sail, but I don't know where the water is." Tim wanted to help Bob. "I will find the water for you," he said. Tim flew high in the sky and saw a big blue lake not far away. He came back to Bob and said, "I found the water! Follow me!" So, Bob and Tim went to the lake together. When they got there, Bob was so happy. He could finally sail on the water. Bob and Tim became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big squash in a small garden. The squash was very open to making friends with all the other plants. One day, a man came to work in the garden. He saw the big squash and thought it was very nice. The squash wanted to be friends with the man, too. But the man did not talk to the squash. He just worked and worked in the garden. The squash was sad because it wanted to be friends with the man. One day, the squash saw the man was tired from all his work. So, the squash did something unexpected. It jumped up and gave the man a big hug. The man was surprised and laughed. From that day on, the man and the squash were best friends. They worked and played in the garden together, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a clever dog named Zip. Zip loved to play and gather his toys in the yard. Every day, he would run outside and find his favorite ball, bone, and stuffed animal. One sunny day, Zip was playing with his toys when he saw a little girl named Amy. Amy was sad because she lost her toy rabbit. Zip wanted to help Amy, so he began to gather all the toys he could find in the yard. Zip searched and searched until he found the toy rabbit hiding under a bush. He picked it up and brought it to Amy. She was so happy to see her rabbit again! Amy thanked Zip and gave him a big hug. From that day on, Zip and Amy became the best of friends, playing and gathering toys together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a strong boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and measure things with his ruler. One day, Tim found a little bug in his room. He wanted to help the bug escape to the outside. So, Tim opened the window and tried to let the bug out. But the bug did not want to go. It liked Tim's room and wanted to stay and play. Tim was sad because he wanted the bug to be free. He tried one more time to let the bug out the window, but it still did not want to leave. The bug stayed in Tim's room and they could not play together. The end.
