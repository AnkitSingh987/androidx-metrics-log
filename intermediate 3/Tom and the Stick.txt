Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Stick

One day, Tom the cat and Sam the dog were playing. Tom was very hungry. He saw a yummy yogurt on the table. Tom wanted to eat the yogurt. Tom said to Sam, "I want to eat the yummy yogurt. Can you help me?" Sam said, "Yes, I can help you." They tried to get the yogurt, but it was too high. Tom had an idea. He found a sharp stick. He said, "I will use this sharp stick to get the yogurt." But when Tom used the sharp stick, the yogurt fell on the floor. The yogurt was all gone. Tom and Sam were sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to bake. One day, Sue wanted to bake a cake. She put on her apron and went to the kitchen. Sue's mom saw her and said, "Sue, it's cold outside. Make sure to close the door." Sue closed the door and started to bake her cake. She mixed the cake mix, eggs, and milk in a big bowl. Sue was very excited. But Sue's hands were filthy. When Sue put the cake in the oven, her mom saw her dirty hands. "Sue, you need to wash your hands before you bake," her mom said. Sue washed her hands and finished baking her cake. The cake was warm and yummy, and Sue and her mom enjoyed it together.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park. It was a normal day. He saw a dog and said, "Hi, dog! Do you want to play?" The dog wagged its tail, and they played for a minute. Tom enjoyed playing with the dog. Then, they saw a big box. "What is in the box?" Tom asked the dog. The dog barked, and they went to look. They opened the box, and inside was a big, colorful ball. Tom and the dog played with the ball. But then, the ball started to fly! Tom and the dog were very surprised. They chased the ball, and it took them on a fun adventure. In the end, they found out the ball was a magic ball. Tom and the dog had a great day, and they knew they would always remember their magic adventure.

Once upon a time, in a land far, far away, there lived an old man. He was a very wise and kind man and he loved to take long walks in the warm sunshine. One day, the old man was out for his daily walk when he spotted something strange in the dirt. He knelt down to take a closer look and discovered it was a hand. The hand was very old and looked like it had been there for a long time. The old man was very curious and he decided to take the old hand along with him on his walk. When he got home, he asked his grandson if he knew what the hand was. The grandson looked confused and said, "I don't think so. It's very old and looks like it might rot soon." "Yes, you are right," the old man replied. "And that is why I wanted to show it to you. Let this be a reminder to you that nothing lasts forever. And if you don't use the time you have wisely, it might rot away before you know it." The old man's grandson nodded, understanding the lesson he was trying to teach. However, despite the warning, he kept the old hand with him for days, taking it for walks around the countryside. One fateful day, tragedy struck. While the grandson was out walking with the old hand, a sudden rainstorm hit and caused the hand to rot away completely

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved boats. Every day, he would go to the pond with his toy boat. He was very enthusiastic about playing with his boat. One day, while Tim was playing, he saw a big boat on the pond. Tim started to wonder who the boat belonged to. He looked around but saw no one. He decided to go closer and look at the big boat. As Tim got near the big boat, he saw a friendly frog sitting on it. The frog said, "Hi, I am Fred the Frog, and this is my boat!" Tim was surprised and happy to meet Fred. From that day on, Tim and Fred played together with their boats on the pond, and they became the best of friends.
