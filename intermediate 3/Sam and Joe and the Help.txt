Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and Joe and the Help

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Sam and Joe. Sam was a very kind and helpful person. He would always help his friends when they needed it. One day, Sam's friend Joe asked him for help. Joe had a lot of laundry to do but he was very tired and he needed a hand. Sam was happy to help out, so he prepared the laundry for him. But, Joe wasn't happy with the way Sam prepared the laundry. He did not like the way the clothes were folded. He asked Sam why he had not folded the clothes in a 'regular' way. Sam was a bit confused and said he didn't know what Joe meant. Joe was a bit angry but explained the 'regular' way to fold the clothes. Sam quickly understood, apologized, and went back to work. The lesson here is that we should never be too proud to ask questions. We might even learn something new. Everybody can make mistakes, and rather than being angry, we should explain what we really mean in a friendly way and help the other person learn.

One day, on a long Wednesday, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved to play on the swings and slide. He was so happy to be there. While Tim was playing, a big dog came running into the park. The dog was very fast and it embarrassed Tim when it knocked him over. Tim's face turned red and he felt sad. But then, something unexpected happened. The big dog came back to Tim and started to lick his face. Tim started to laugh and felt happy again. The dog was not mean, it just wanted to play! Tim and the big dog became best friends and played together all day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a long bird with colorful wings. The long bird lived in a small cage near a big tree. The long bird was sad because it wanted to fly high in the sky. One day, a little girl named Mia found the long bird in the cage. Mia felt sad for the bird and wanted to help it. She opened the cage and the long bird flew out. The long bird was very happy. Mia and the long bird became good friends. The long bird would fly high in the sky and come back to Mia. When Mia was sad, the long bird would comfort her with its soft and colorful wings. They played and laughed together in the big forest.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a plan to pick fruit from a big tree in his yard. He was very happy to eat the fruit. One day, while Tim was picking fruit, a big bird came and took the fruit from the tree. Tim was upset. He did not know what to do. He wanted the fruit back. Tim thought and thought. Then he had a new plan. He put some fruit on the ground and hid behind the tree. The big bird came down to eat the fruit. Tim jumped out and scared the bird away. The bird flew away and never took Tim's fruit again. Tim was happy and shared the fruit with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little horse named Horsie. Horsie lived in a stable with his family. It was a nice stable, but sometimes Horsie felt uncomfortable. He wanted to go outside and play with his friends. One day, Horsie saw a big, red ball stuck in a tree. He really wanted to play with it, but he couldn't reach it. So, he asked his friend, Birdie, to help him. Birdie tried to pull the ball out of the tree, but it was too hard. Horsie felt sad, and he didn't know what to do. Then, Horsie had an idea. He asked all of his friends to come and help. They all worked together to pull the ball out of the tree. With everyone's help, they got the ball out, and they all played together. Horsie learned that when you work together, you can do anything.
