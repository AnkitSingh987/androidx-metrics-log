Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack the Kid and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little kid named Jack. He loved to play outside in the garden with his best friend, a big green dragon. One day, they were playing together when Jack found a rare prune hidden in a bush. He quickly grabbed the prune and started to laugh. "Hey, give me that prune," the dragon said. But Jack didn't want to share it, so he shook his head and ran away. The dragon chased him and tried to get the prune, but Jack kept running. All of a sudden, the dragon pounced and slapped Jack on the back. Jack was so sad. He burst into tears and ran away to the other side of the garden. He felt bad that the dragon had slapped him, but he was also glad he still had the prune. He hugged the prune close and vowed never to share it.

One day, a funny bird with a big wing went to play with his friends. He seemed very happy. The bird saw his friend, the cat, and said, "Let's play a game!" The cat said, "Okay, but I see a big tree. I think something will happen there soon." They played near the tree, not knowing what was going to happen. Later, a big wind came and blew the funny bird's big wing. The bird flew high into the sky and landed on the big tree. The cat climbed up to help the bird. They both laughed and said, "We knew something would happen at the big tree!"

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tom. Tom did not like to eat his broccoli. Every day, his mom would say, "Tom, you need to eat your broccoli." But Tom would just shake his head and say, "No, Mommy, I don't like it." One day, Tom saw a tiny bug on his plate. The bug was studying the broccoli. Tom watched the bug and said, "Why do you like broccoli, little bug?" The bug looked at Tom and said, "Because it makes me strong and helps me grow." Tom thought about what the bug said. The next day, Tom's mom put broccoli on his plate again. Tom remembered the bug and decided to give it a try. He took a bite and said, "Mommy, I will eat my broccoli now." From that day on, Tom ate his broccoli every day. He grew big and strong, just like the little bug said. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a stable. In the stable, there were five horses. The horses liked to play games. They were competitive. They would run and see who was the fastest. One day, a new horse came to the stable. The new horse was very big. The other horses were scared. They did not want to play with the new horse. So, they locked the stable door. The new horse could not get in. But something unexpected happened. The new horse pushed the stable door, and it opened! The other horses were surprised. The new horse was not mean. He just wanted to play with them. They all played together and had fun. In the end, they were all friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue put on her bathrobe after her bath. She felt nice and dry. Sue went to the living room and saw her mom. Her mom said, "Sue, can you help me gather your toys, please?" Sue nodded and started to pick up her toys. As she was gathering them, she found a big, red ball. She rolled the ball across the floor. Suddenly, the ball hit the wall and made a loud noise. Sue's dog, Spot, came running into the room. Spot saw the red ball and started to play with it. He picked it up in his mouth and ran around the room. Then, something unexpected happened. Spot jumped on the couch and the ball fell out of his mouth. The ball rolled under Sue's bathrobe. Sue laughed and said, "Silly Spot, you found a new place to hide the ball!" She picked up the ball and gave it back to Spot. They played together for the rest of the day, and Sue forgot all about gathering her toys.
