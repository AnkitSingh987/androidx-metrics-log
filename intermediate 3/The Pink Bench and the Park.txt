Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pink Bench and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a pink bench in a park. The pink bench was very pretty. It was near a big tree and a slide. Every day, kids would come to the park to play. One sunny day, a little girl named Sally came to the park. She saw the pink bench and wanted to sit on it. But she was too small to climb up. She asked her big brother Tom for help. "Tom, can you support me to sit on the pink bench?" Sally said. Tom smiled and said, "Of course, Sally!" Tom supported Sally to climb up the bench. They both sat on the pink bench and watched the other kids play. They had a fun time at the park. The pink bench was happy to have friends sit on it. From that day on, Sally and Tom always visited the pink bench when they went to the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved the color orange. She had an orange dress, orange shoes, and even an orange hat. One day, Sue went for a long walk in the park. While walking, Sue found a big package. The package was orange too! She was very happy. Sue wanted to open the package, but she was tired from her long walk. So, she decided to sit under a tree and rest. After Sue had rested, she opened the orange package. Inside, she found a soft, orange teddy bear! Sue loved her new friend. She hugged the bear and took it home. From that day on, Sue and her orange teddy bear were always together, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a messy dog on the side of the road. The dog was very dirty and had mud all over him. Sue felt bad for the dog and wanted to help him. Sue took the dog home with her. She filled a big tub with water and bubbles. Sue and the dog had a fun time playing in the water. They splashed and laughed. The messy dog was now clean and happy. After the bath, Sue and the dog went to her room. She saw that her toys were all over the floor. Sue knew she had to clean up. She put all her toys in a big box and closed it. The dog sat by her side as she did this. Now, Sue's room was clean and she had a new friend.

Once upon a time, there lived a wide family. Every day they went out together to explore the world. One day, they spotted a strangely shaped tree. They all gathered around it, and were very excited to find that it was very special! The tree had a language of its own, and it spoke to them in a strange language. They asked the tree what it wanted, but it simply said nothing. The family tried to think of a way to get the tree to tell them what it wanted. Suddenly, one of the littlest children had an idea. She hugged the tree and said, “Please tree, can you tell us what you want? We want to help you!” To her surprise, the tree did yield! It thanked her for her kindness and said that it just wanted to be taken care of. The family was so happy to learn that the tree just wanted to be loved. They happily agreed to do whatever they could to help the tree, and from then on the wide family took care of the special tree every day.

One sunny day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He had a new toy. It was a kite. The kite was big and colorful. Tim wanted to play with it outside. Tim went to the park with his dad. The wind was blowing just right. Tim held the kite string and ran. The kite went up, up, up in the sky. Tim was very happy. His dad was enthusiastic too. They both laughed and played. After a while, Tim saw an apple on the ground. The apple was old and not good to eat. It had started to rot. Tim's dad said, "Don't touch it." Tim listened and went back to flying his kite. They had a fun day at the park.
