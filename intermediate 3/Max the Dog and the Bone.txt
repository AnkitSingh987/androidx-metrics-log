Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Bone

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max loved to play and find bones. One day, Max saw a big, brilliant bone in the yard. He thought it was the best bone ever. Max wanted to show his friend, a cat named Luna, his brilliant bone. But when Max went to find Luna, the bone was gone! Max was sad and didn't know where the bone went. He thought very hard about where he last saw it. Then, Max saw Luna playing with a toy that looked like his brilliant bone! Max was happy because he found his bone, but then he saw it was just a toy. Luna gave Max the toy, and they played together all day. Max thought it was even better than a real bone.

One day, a boy named Tim found a fancy toy in his room. It was a toy that could drink. Tim was so happy and played with the toy all day. But then, his sister Sue saw the toy and wanted to play with it too. "Can I play with the fancy toy?" asked Sue. Tim didn't want to share and said, "No, it's mine!" Sue was sad and ran away. Tim felt bad for not sharing, but he still wanted to play with his toy. Later, Tim was playing with the toy when something unexpected happened. The toy stopped drinking! Tim tried to make it drink again, but it wouldn't work. He felt sad and thought maybe if he shared the toy with Sue, it would start drinking again. Tim found Sue and said, "I'm sorry I didn't share. You can play with the fancy toy now." Sue was so happy and started playing with the toy. Suddenly, the toy started to drink again! Tim and Sue played together and had lots of fun. The moral of the story is that sharing is important and can make everyone happy.

Tom and Mia were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a set of furniture for their dolls. They had a sofa, a table, a bed, and a chair. The furniture was pale, like the color of milk. One day, Tom and Mia were playing with their furniture in their room. They set the sofa and the table in the middle of the floor, and the bed and the chair near the window. They pretended that their dolls were having a tea party. "Would you like some tea, Mrs. Doll?" Mia asked, holding a tiny cup. "Yes, please, Mr. Doll," Tom answered, making a sipping sound. They were having fun, until their mom came in. She saw the furniture on the floor and frowned. "Tom and Mia, what are you doing?" she asked. "You know you have to put your toys away when you are done playing. You can't leave them on the floor. Someone could trip and get hurt." Tom and Mia looked at each other. They knew their mom was right, but they didn't want to stop playing. "Can we play a little more, mom?" Mia asked. "Please, mom?" Tom added. Their mom shook her head. "No, you have to put your toys away now. It's time for lunch. And you have to learn to be responsible and tidy. That's a good habit to have." Tom and Mia sighed. They knew their mom loved them and wanted them to be good. They picked up their furniture and put it back in the box. They said sorry to their mom and gave her a hug. "Thank you, Tom and Mia," their mom said. "I'm proud of you for listening and doing the right thing. Now, let's go have some lunch. I made your favorite, macaroni and cheese." Tom and Mia smiled. They were hungry and happy. They learned that playing was fun, but putting away their toys was important too. They decided to do that every time they finished playing. They loved their mom and their toys, and their pale furniture set.

Sammy was three and loved stamps. He had a collection of all kinds of bright, colourful stamps and he was very proud of them. One day Sammy took his box of stamps to show his friends. They all had a lot of fun looking at them and talking about them. They each chose a few of their favourites and Sammy was so happy. Sammy's dad was watching from a distance, smiling. He couldn't help but feel proud of his strong, hardworking son. Suddenly the kids started discussing a very important topic - which stamp was the best? "This one!" said Sammy, holding up a bright green stamp. His friends all nodded in agreement. "The green stamp is the best!" The kids laughed and played with their stamps for a while longer before finally saying goodbye. Sammy was thrilled to know that his green stamp was the best one!
