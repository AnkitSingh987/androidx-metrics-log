Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pumpkin and the Gate

Once upon a time, there was a big pumpkin in a small garden. It was unknown who put the pumpkin there. A little boy named Tim saw the pumpkin and wanted to take it home. Tim asked his mom, "Can I take the pumpkin home?" His mom said, "You can take it, but you must shut the gate after." Tim was happy and promised to shut the gate. Tim took the pumpkin home and shut the gate like his mom said. The next day, the pumpkin grew bigger and bigger. Tim was very happy because the pumpkin was now big enough to make a lot of pumpkin pie.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was only three years old and he loved to explore. One day, Tom went for a walk in a big, black forest. As Tom was exploring, he came across a big, black door. He tried to open it, but he found that it was locked. Tom looked around and he saw a staff lying on the ground. He picked it up and tried to use it to open the door. The door opened and Tom saw that there was a secret room inside! Tom stepped inside and asked, "What is this place?" Suddenly, a voice answered, "This is my special room, and it's just for me!" Tom said, "Can I come back and explore it with you?" The voice responded, "Yes, but you must always remember to lock the door behind you." So, Tom did as he was told and every time he visited the special room, he always made sure to lock the door when he was done.

One day, a small cat named Tom was walking in the park. He saw a big wall and wanted to climb it. But Tom was too small and felt helpless. He thought, "I need to study how to climb the wall." The next day, Tom went to the park again. He saw a big dog named Max. Max was good at climbing walls. Tom asked Max, "Can you help me study how to climb the wall?" Max said, "Yes, I can help you." Tom and Max studied how to climb the wall together. After some time, Tom became very good at climbing. One day, they saw a ball stuck on top of the wall. Tom used his new skills to climb the wall and get the ball. Max was happy, and they played with the ball together. Tom was no longer helpless, and they became best friends.

Once there was a boy called Jack. He was only three years old. One day, his Mum and Dad asked him to leave. He was so sad and he said "Why do I have to go?" His Mum said "We are sorry Jack, but you need to go now." Jack was very upset and he started to cry. His Dad saw how sad Jack was, so he gave him a special wrap. It was made of special fabric and had bright colours. Jack liked it and hugged it tight. His Mum said "This is a special goodbye wrap. Whenever you miss us, just hug it." Jack hugged it and smiled. He felt a little better knowing he had a special wrap. Just before Jack left, his Dad said, "I'm sorry Jack, but it's time to go now." Jack hugged his wrap, waved goodbye and left. He was still very sad about leaving. But he knew if he ever missed his parents, he could just hug his special wrap and it would make him feel better.

One day, a poor girl named Lily wanted to buy a pretty velvet dress. She saw the dress in a shop and it was her favorite color, red. But Lily did not have enough money. She was sad and went to her friend Tom for help. "Tom, I want to buy a red velvet dress, but I don't have enough money. What can I do?" Lily asked. Tom thought for a moment and said, "Let's do some work for our neighbors. We can help them and earn some money." Lily and Tom worked hard. They helped Mrs. Brown with her garden and Mr. Smith with his dog. Soon, they had enough money for the dress. Lily was so happy and thanked Tom for his help. They went to the shop and bought the red velvet dress. Lily wore it with a big smile, and they played together all day.
