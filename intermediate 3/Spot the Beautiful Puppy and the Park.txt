Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Beautiful Puppy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful puppy named Spot. Spot had a big friend named Tom. Tom and Spot liked to play in the park. They ran and jumped all day long. One day, Spot met a mean cat named Max. Max did not like Spot. Max said, "I hate puppies!" Spot was sad, but he did not let Max make him cry. He knew he was a good puppy. Tom saw Spot and Max. He told Max, "You should not hate. It is not nice." Max felt bad and said sorry to Spot. Then, they all played together in the park. From that day on, Max learned to be nice and not hate. They all became good friends and had lots of fun.

One day, a humble bird named Bob wanted to soar high in the sky. He saw a trail that led to a big hill. Bob thought if he went up the hill, he could fly very high. Bob met a dog named Max on the trail. Max said, "I want to go up the hill too!" So, they went up the hill together. They were happy to be friends. When they got to the top of the hill, Bob tried to soar high. But he fell down and could not fly. Max tried to help, but he could not. They both felt very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy called Mike. Mike was a microphone. Lily liked to sing with Mike. She sang all day and all night. She was a little crazy when she sang. Her friends liked to listen to her. One day, Lily met a new friend named Tim. She wanted to introduce Mike to Tim. Lily said, "Tim, this is Mike. Mike is my microphone. I like to sing with Mike." Tim said, "I like to sing too. Can I sing with you and Mike?" Lily and Tim sang together with Mike. They sang so loud and crazy. They did not listen to their mom and dad. Their mom and dad said, "Please stop singing so loud." But Lily and Tim did not listen. They sang louder and crazier. Then, something bad happened. The microphone, Mike, broke. Lily and Tim could not sing anymore. They were very sad. They learned that they should have listened to their mom and dad. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents, and not be too crazy.

One day, a boy named Tim was watching his favorite cartoon on TV. The sun was shining and it was very hot outside. Tim's mom told him to go play outside, but Tim did not want to stop watching his cartoon. While Tim was watching, his big sister Sue came into the room. Sue wanted to watch her show, but Tim did not want to pass the TV remote. They started to argue and fight over the remote. Their mom heard the noise and came into the room. She told Tim and Sue to stop fighting and go outside to play together. Tim and Sue went outside and had fun in the hot sun. They forgot about the TV and learned to share and play together.

One day, a clever girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a shiny jewelry box near a big tree. Lily was very happy to find it. She wanted to show her friends the beautiful jewelry inside. Lily ran to her friends and waved to them. She showed them the pretty jewelry from the box. They all wanted to wear it and play with it. They put on the necklaces, bracelets, and rings. They felt like princesses and princes. But then, a mean witch saw them playing with the jewelry. She wanted it back because it was hers. The witch waved her hands and took the jewelry from the kids. She also turned Lily and her friends into frogs. They tried to wave for help, but no one could understand them. They stayed as frogs and could not play with the jewelry again.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Max. Max loved to play in the yard. One day, Max saw a green herb in the yard. He did not mind the herb. Max thought the herb was fun to play with. Max played with the herb all day. He ran, jumped, and rolled in the yard. The herb made Max happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The herb began to grow bigger and bigger. Max was not scared. He thought the big herb was even more fun to play with. Max played with the big herb all day. At night, the herb turned into a big tree. Max was very surprised. Now, Max had a new friend to play with, the big tree. And they played happily ever after.
