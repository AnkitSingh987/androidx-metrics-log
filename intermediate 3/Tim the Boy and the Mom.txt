Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mom

One day, a boy named Tim found a zipper on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a zipper!" he said. His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! Let's see what it can do." Tim pulled the zipper and, all of a sudden, he was very fast! He ran around the yard, laughing and playing. His mom watched him and said, "Wow, Tim! You are so fast now!" But then, Tim realized he could not stop running. He was scared and called out to his mom, "Help, Mom! I can't stop!" His mom thought fast and grabbed the zipper from him. She pulled it again, and Tim stopped running. Tim and his mom were surprised but happy that everything was okay. They decided to put the zipper away, so no one else would have trouble with it. From that day on, Tim knew that some things are better left alone.

Once upon a time, there was a gray scooter. The scooter lived in a big, red house. It loved to play outside with its friends, the bike, and the ball. One day, the gray scooter saw a branch. The scooter had an idea. "I want to hang from that branch!" it said to the bike and the ball. The bike and the ball thought it was a fun idea too. They all wanted to try it. The gray scooter tried first. It went very fast and jumped up to the branch. It was able to hang there for a little bit. "Look at me!" said the scooter. The bike and the ball clapped and cheered. Next, the bike tried to hang. The bike was too heavy, but it still tried its best. The ball tried too, but it just bounced off the branch. They all laughed and had fun. At the end of the day, the gray scooter, the bike, and the ball went back to their big, red house. They were happy because they had a fun day playing together.

Once upon a time, in a far away planet, there lived a loyal dog named Max. Max was a good dog who loved to play and run. He was always happy to be with his friends. One day, Max saw a big ball in the sky. He thought it was a toy and wanted to catch it. Max jumped and jumped, but he could not reach the ball. Max felt sad and decided to lie down under a tree. He closed his eyes and started to dream. While Max was lying there, something unexpected happened. The big ball in the sky came down to Max! It was not a toy, but a new friend from another planet. Max and his new friend played and had fun all day. The new friend showed Max how to jump high and touch the sky. Max was happy and knew that he had found a true friend.

Once upon a time there was a long, dark night. It was a special night, and the sky was full of stars. The stars were so bright that even the animals in the forest were happy, stretching their arms and legs to feel the moonlight. But then something strange happened. A gloomy grey cloud rolled in from the east. It was like a big, grey pill, pulling in the light from the stars and leaving a blanket of darkness all around. The animals in the forest were sad and tired, so one by one they curled up under the trees and went to sleep, dreaming of a brighter day. But as all hope seemed lost, they suddenly heard a loud stretch coming from the sky. The cloud had moved away and the starlight returned! The animals all cheered, stretching and jumping with joy. This night was special, and it was the stretch of sunshine that made it so. The stars shone bright and the animals in the forest were happy once again.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there lived a nice crocodile. The crocodile liked to play with his friends, the fish and the birds. They all had fun together in the sun. One day, a little bird asked the crocodile, "Please, can you help me find my friend? He is lost in the pond." The nice crocodile said, "Yes, I will help you. Let's go find your friend." They looked and looked in the pond. The nice crocodile saw the little bird's friend stuck in some plants. The crocodile swam over and helped the friend get free. The little bird said, "Thank you, nice crocodile, for helping me find my friend." The nice crocodile, the little bird, and the friend all played together in the pond. They were very happy to be together again. From that day on, they all became best friends and had many more fun days in the sun.
