Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Lucy. She loved to visit her grandma. Lucy's grandma lived in a small house with a big garden. Lucy liked to play in the garden with her toys. One day, Lucy went to visit her grandma. Grandma was very happy to see her. She said, "Lucy, I have a surprise for you." Lucy was excited and wanted to know what the surprise was. Grandma went to the kitchen and came back with a big box of chocolate. Lucy's eyes were wide with joy. She said, "Thank you, Grandma!" They both sat down and ate the chocolate together. It was a very happy day for Lucy and her kind grandma.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor named Sam. Sam loved to sail on the open sea. He had a big boat with a big sail. Sam was happy when he was on his boat. One day, Sam met a little bird. The bird was lost. Sam asked the bird, "Do you want to sail with me?" The bird said, "Yes, please! I need help to find my way home." So, Sam and the bird sailed together on the open sea. As they sailed, they talked and laughed. They saw fish and big whales. They felt the wind blow on their faces. Soon, they found the bird's home. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" Sam smiled and said, "You're welcome! Let's sail again soon."

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a gloomy house. It looked old and sad. Tim wanted to make the house happy. Tim found some copper in his pocket. He had an idea. He could use the copper to make the house pretty. Tim started to pull on the copper. It became long and thin. He put the long copper on the gloomy house. The house looked happy now. Tim was happy too. They both smiled and had a fun day.

One day, a thoughtful leopard was walking in the forest. He wanted to make a new friend. He saw a little bird in a tree. The leopard said, "Hi, bird! Will you be my friend?" The bird looked at the leopard and said, "Yes, I will be your friend, leopard!" They played together all day. They ran, they jumped, and they laughed. At the end of the day, the leopard and the bird were very happy. They had made a new friend. The sun went down and it was time for the bird to go to sleep. The leopard said, "Good night, bird. See you tomorrow!" The bird said, "Good night, leopard. I had fun today!" The thoughtful leopard and the little bird were now the best of friends. They played together every day and were always happy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Love. She was always so happy and loved by everyone. One day, she went to the park with her mom. At the park, they saw a small pond covered in ice. Love loved the ice because it sparkled in the sun. Love's mom quickly recognized the original beauty of the ice. She exclaimed, "Look at this ice, Love! It's so pretty, isn't it?" Love smiled and said, "Yes, mommy. It is so sparkly and original!" She touched the ice and laughed with joy. What Love didn't know was that her mom had a surprise hidden in the ice. Underneath it was a beautiful, glittering necklace with a shining diamond in the center! Love's mom quickly pulled out the necklace, and Love's eyes lit up with joy. She was amazed at its beauty and realized it was her very own necklace. Love felt so special and hugged her mom tight. She was so thankful to recognize this precious and original gift. She thought to herself, "I am so lucky to have a mom like mine!"

One day, a boy named Tim went to play at the park. He saw a big slide and wanted to go on it. He climbed up and slid down fast. He liked the slide and went on it many times. The sun was light and bright in the sky. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue wanted to go on the slide too, but she was scared. Tim said, "Don't worry, I will help you." They climbed up together and slid down. Sue was happy and thanked Tim. After playing, Tim and Sue felt hungry. They went to Tim's house for a snack. Tim's mom gave them bread and butter. But there was a problem! The butter was too hard to spread on the bread. Tim and Sue tried to spread it, but it was not easy. They asked Tim's mom for help. She put the butter in the sun to make it soft. Soon, the butter was easy to spread, and they ate their snack happily.
