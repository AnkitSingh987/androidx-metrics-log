Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man and the Park

Once upon a time, there was an old man who was counting his money in the park. He had a large pile of coins on his lap and was carefully counting each one. Suddenly, a terrible thunderstorm started and the old man’s money blew away in the wind. Frightened, he started to cry. A three year old girl, who was playing in the park, saw him and ran to his side. “Don’t worry, I can count it all for you,” said the girl. The old man was very grateful. He held out his palm and she filled it with coins. As the girl started counting, the old man began to smile. She counted the coins out loud, very carefully, until the last one was counted. “You’re terrific!” exclaimed the old man. The girl smiled proudly and said, “You’re welcome. Anytime you need help counting, I’m here for you!”

One day, a boy named Tim went to play golf with his dad. Tim was very happy because he felt comfortable in his new clothes. They went to a big field with a lot of grass. While playing golf, Tim saw a big rock. He did not see it and he stumbled on it. Tim fell down and started to cry. His dad came running to help him. Dad picked up Tim and gave him a big hug. Tim stopped crying and felt better. They went back to playing golf and had a fun day. Tim was careful not to stumble again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to climb on things. One day, Max saw a big TV in the living room. He thought it would be fun to climb on the TV. So, Max ran and jumped onto the TV. But, the TV began to shake and wobble. Max was scared. Max's friend, a little cat named Luna, saw what was happening. She said, "Max, you shouldn't climb on the TV! It's not safe." Max listened to Luna and jumped off the TV. He was happy to be safe on the ground. Later, Max and Luna found a delicious apple. They shared the apple and enjoyed it together. Max learned that it's better to share and have fun with friends, instead of climbing on things that aren't safe. And they both learned that good things can happen when you listen to your friends.

One sunny day, a little bee was very tired. He needed a nap. He flew back to his hive to rest. His bee friends were making honey. "Buzz, buzz," said the bee. "I am so tired. I need a nap." "Buzz, buzz," said his friend. "Poor little bee, take a nap in the hive." The little bee went inside the hive and lay down. He closed his eyes and took a nap. He slept for a while and felt better. When he woke up, he was not tired anymore. He said thank you to his friend and went back to work, making honey with his friends. They all worked happily together.

Mama sent Tommy to the closet. Tommy was a very obedient little boy and he knew exactly what Mama wanted him to do. He put on his coat, closed the door behind him and walked into the closet. Inside, Tommy found a surprise. Mama had sent him to the closet so he could find a secret box. Inside the box was a toy car! Tommy was so excited that he ran back out to show Mama his new car. Mama was so proud of Tommy's obedience and she gave him a big hug. He had remembered to do exactly what she asked. Tommy was a happy little boy now that he had a new toy car.

One day, it started to rain. The rain made the ground wet and smelly. Tim, a small boy, looked out the window. He liked the sight of rain falling on the grass. Tim put on his rain boots and rain coat. He went outside to play. He jumped in the puddles and laughed. The rain felt nice on his face. After a while, the rain stopped. The sun came out, and the smelly ground started to dry. Tim went back inside. He was happy he got to play in the rain.
