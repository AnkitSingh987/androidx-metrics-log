Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Fireman and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Tim. Tim was very important because he helped people when they needed him. One day, Tim was walking down the street when he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because her cat was stuck in a tree. Tim said, "Don't worry, Lily. I will help you. We need to get your cat down from the tree." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. You are very important to me." Tim climbed the tree and carefully picked up the cat. He brought the cat down to the ground and gave it to Lily. Lily was so happy that she hugged Tim. Tim felt good because he was an important fireman who helped people when they needed him.

Once upon a time, there was a little goat. The goat was lost and felt helpless. It was sad and wanted to find its way back home. One day, a kind bird saw the goat. The bird wanted to help the goat. So, they both started to search for the goat's home together. They looked and looked, but they could not find it. Then, they met a big dog. The dog was strong and knew the way. The dog, the bird, and the goat all went to search together. They walked and walked until they found the goat's home. The goat was so happy to be back home and thanked the bird and the dog for their help. They all became good friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a child. She was very happy because today was the day she was going to learn how to swim! The child's family brought her to the pool. The water was so shiny and there were red swimmers everywhere. The child felt so excited and couldn't wait to start. The child's mom and dad said, "The most important rule is to always listen to your swimming teacher." The child nodded her head. The swimming teacher said, "Let's get into the water. Come on everyone, it's time to swim!" The child jumped into the pool with the other swimmers. Everyone was having so much fun swimming around. The child was picking up lots of skills, like kicking with her feet and moving her arms. At the end of the day, the child said goodbye to her swimming teacher and smiled. She was so proud of herself for learning how to swim!

Once upon a time, in a little town, there lived a restless boy named Tim. Tim loved to play all day. One day, Tim found a mask in his room. The mask was big and funny. Tim put the mask on and went outside to play. In the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw the mask and laughed. "That mask is so funny!" she said. Tim smiled and said, "I promise, if you play with me, I will let you wear the mask too." Sue agreed, and they played together all day. As they played, a little dog came running up to them. The dog saw the mask and got scared. The dog barked and ran away. Tim and Sue were surprised. They took off the mask and saw that it was not a funny mask, but a scary mask. They laughed and promised to be more careful next time.

Lily and Tom were playing in the jungle. They liked to swing on the vines and make noises like monkeys. They saw many animals and plants, but they were not afraid. They had each other and their big friend, Rafa. Rafa was a gorilla who lived in the jungle. He was very strong and kind. He liked to play with Lily and Tom, and he taught them many things. He showed them how to find fruits and nuts, how to make a nest, and how to avoid danger. One day, they heard a loud roar. It was a lion who was hunting for food. He saw Lily and Tom and ran towards them. Lily and Tom were scared. They did not know what to do. They looked for Rafa, but he was far away. "Help, Rafa, help!" they shouted. Rafa heard them and quickly swung on the vines. He reached them just in time and scared the lion away. He hugged Lily and Tom and made sure they were okay. "Thank you, Rafa, you saved us!" they said. "You're welcome, my little friends. You are brave and smart, but you have to be careful. The jungle can be dangerous. Come, let's go to our nest and rest. You are safe with me." he said. They followed Rafa to his nest, which was made of leaves and branches. They snuggled together and felt warm and cozy. They closed their eyes and fell asleep. They dreamed of more adventures in the jungle.
