Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy and Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a boy and a girl who were best friends. One day, the two were walking in the park and spotted something rare. "What is that?" the boy asked and pointed. The girl gasped. "It's a band!" The boy leaned in closer. "It looks so stretchy!" The girl nodded. "I've never seen one like this before. It looks so rare!" The boy smiled. "Let's stretch it out and give it a try." The two kids stretched out the band and the girl jumped in. She gave the band a few test bounces. "Wow!" the boy said. "That was awesome!" The girl smiled. "Let's keep it and come back here every day to see if we can find something else!" And so, each day the two kids went to the park and stretched out the band, and each day they found another rare and exciting thing. They found happiness and fun stretching the band out each day in the park.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big book full of pages. Lily loved to read and imagine fun adventures. Her mom was always reliable to read her a bedtime story. One day, Lily met a new friend named Tim. Tim was shy and did not like to talk much. Lily wanted to help Tim feel happy. She said, "Tim, let's imagine a fun adventure together. We can look at the pages in my book and think of a great story." Tim looked at the pages with Lily. They imagined they were in a big forest, looking for a lost treasure. They met a wise owl who said, "To find the treasure, you must be kind and help each other." Lily and Tim worked together and found the treasure. They were so happy! From that day on, Tim felt more confident and talked more. He and Lily became best friends. They learned that being kind and helping each other made them feel happy. The wise owl was right, and they never forgot the lesson they learned on their adventure.

One day, a cat named Tom was very excite. He found some dough in the kitchen. He wanted to play with it. Tom felt a little guilty, but he still played with the dough. While Tom was playing, a big dog named Max came in. Max saw the dough and wanted to play too. They both played with the dough and made a big mess. They were having so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The dough started to grow and grow! It became so big that it filled the whole room. Tom and Max were very surprised. They didn't know that the dough was magic. In the end, they had even more fun playing with the big dough.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a bookcase. The bookcase was very tall and full of books. In the room, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim loved to read books and sit in his comfortable chair. One day, Tim saw a new book on the top shelf of the bookcase. He wanted to read it so bad. But he was too small to reach it. He tried and tried, but he could not get the book. Tim felt sad and embarrassed because he could not get the book by himself. Tim thought of an idea. He would use a chair to stand on and reach the book. But when he tried to get the book, the chair fell and the bookcase came down too. Tim and the books were all on the floor. He was not hurt, but he was very sad. The room was a big mess, and Tim felt even more embarrassed.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to ride her bike outside. One day, she saw a big, fearful spider on the ceiling of her room. She was scared and ran to tell her mom. "Mommy, Mommy!" cried Lily. "There is a big, fearful spider on my ceiling!" Mommy came to look, but she was scared too. They didn't know what to do. Then, Lily had an idea. "I will ride my bike and chase the spider away!" But when she tried to ride her bike in the room, she crashed into the wall. The spider fell on Lily and bit her. She cried and cried. The spider went away, but Lily was sad and hurt. The end.
