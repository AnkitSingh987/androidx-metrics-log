Category: Intermediate
Title: Billy the Bird and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small bird named Billy. Billy had a weak voice and could not speak very loud. He felt sad because his friends could not hear him when they played together. One day, while Billy was walking in the forest, he saw a big tree with a special pick on it. The pick was shiny and pretty. Billy thought that maybe the pick could help him speak louder. Billy took the pick and tried to speak. To his surprise, his voice was now loud and clear! His friends could hear him, and they all played happily together. From that day on, Billy was no longer weak, and he could speak with everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a calm boy named Tim. Tim had a mask that he loved very much. It was his favorite toy. One day, Tim's mom told him, "Tim, your mask is dirty. You need to polish it." Tim took a soft cloth and began to polish his mask. He made it clean and shiny. He was very happy. One day, Tim went to the park with his polished mask. He met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Wow, Tim! Your mask is so shiny! Can I try it on?" Tim said, "Yes, Sue! You can try it on." Sue put on the mask and they both played together, having lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a wide slide that was very big. Lily was scared to go on the slide, but her mom encouraged her to try. "Come on, Lily," her mom said. "You can do it! I'll be right here to catch you." Lily took a deep breath and climbed up the slide. She sat at the top and then slid down, laughing as she went. Her mom caught her at the bottom, and they both smiled. After playing at the park, they went home and Lily's mom gave her a treat. It was a sugar cookie with sprinkles on top. "Good job being brave at the park," her mom said. Lily felt very proud and happy. She knew she could be brave with her mom by her side.

Once upon a time, there was a little horse named Daisy. Daisy lived on a farm with her friends. She loved to run and play with them. One day, they were playing a game of push. They would take turns pushing each other and see who could push the hardest. During the game, Daisy pushed her friend Tom a little too hard. Tom fell down and started to cry. Daisy felt very bad and ashamed. She didn't mean to hurt Tom. She just wanted to play and have fun. Daisy said sorry to Tom and helped him stand up. Then, something unexpected happened. Tom stopped crying and started to laugh. He told Daisy that he was not really hurt. He was just pretending to cry to make Daisy feel better. Daisy was surprised and happy to hear this. She laughed too, and they continued to play their push game together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, green dragon. This dragon was very nice and lived in a park. The park had lots of trees, flowers, and a big pond. The dragon loved to play in the park all day. One day, a crazy bird came to the park. The bird was very loud and fast. The bird wanted to play with the dragon. The dragon and the crazy bird played together. They ran, they jumped, and they splashed in the pond. They had so much fun. After playing all day, the dragon and the crazy bird were very tired. They sat under a big tree and looked at the pretty flowers. They were very happy to be friends. From that day on, the dragon and the crazy bird played together in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very restless. He always wanted to play and run around. One day, Tim's mom gave him a shiny coin. She told him not to spoil it. Tim took the coin and went outside to play. He played with his friends and showed them the coin. They all liked it very much. But Tim was restless, and while playing, he dropped the coin in the mud. The coin was not shiny anymore. It was dirty and spoiled. Tim felt sad. He went back home and told his mom what happened. His mom hugged him and said it was okay. She helped him clean the coin until it was shiny again. Tim learned to be more careful with his things, and he was not as restless as before.
