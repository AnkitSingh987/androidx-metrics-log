Title: Tom and Lily and the Says

Tom and Lily are playing with their toys. Tom has a big tank that can make noise and move. Lily has a doll with long hair and a dress. Tom likes to pretend that his tank is in a war and shoots at things. Lily likes to pretend that her doll is a princess and goes to parties. One day, Tom gets bored of his tank and wants to play with Lily's doll. He asks Lily if he can have her doll for a while. Lily says no, because she loves her doll and does not want to share. Tom gets angry and says that he will take her doll anyway. He grabs the doll from Lily and runs away. Lily cries and chases after Tom. She wants her doll back. She catches up with Tom and tries to pull the doll from his hands. They both tug and pull, but Tom is stronger. He pushes Lily away and laughs. He says that the doll is his now and he can do whatever he wants with it. He takes a pair of scissors and cuts the doll's hair. He says that the doll looks ugly now and throws it on the floor. Lily is very sad and angry. She picks up her doll and hugs it. She says that Tom is a bad boy and a liar. She says that he is not honest and not nice. She says that she does not want to play with him anymore. She runs to her mom and tells her what Tom did. Mom is very upset and disappointed. She goes to Tom and scolds him. She says that he should not take things that are not his and he should not hurt other people's feelings. She says that he should apologize to Lily and say sorry. She says that he should be honest and nice if he wants to have friends. Tom feels ashamed and sorry. He realizes that he was wrong and mean. He goes to Lily and gives her back her doll. He says that he is sorry and that he will not do it again. He says that he likes her and her doll and that he wants to be her friend. He asks her if she can forgive him. Lily is still sad, but she sees that Tom is honest and sorry. She says that she forgives him and that she will be his friend. She says that she likes him and his tank and that they can play together. She asks him if he can help her fix her doll's hair. Tom says yes and smiles. He says that he has some glue and some yarn that they can use. He says that they can make the doll's hair look pretty again. He says that they can also play with his tank and make it go to nice places. He says that they can have fun and be happy. They hug and go to play. Mom is proud and happy. She says that they are good kids and that she loves them. She says that honesty and kindness are very important and that they should always remember them. She says that she is glad that they are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant sun in the sky. It was big and bright. It made everyone happy. One day, the sun saw a little boy playing outside. The little boy was having fun, but he was feeling hot. The sun wanted to help the little boy. So, it moved behind a cloud for a while. The little boy felt better and continued to play. He ran and jumped and laughed. But then, he had a fall. He was sad and cried a little. A kind bird saw the little boy and flew down to him. The bird mentioned to the little boy that the sun was trying to help him. The little boy smiled and thanked the sun. The sun came out from behind the cloud and shined brilliantly again. The little boy and the bird played happily under the warm sun.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful cat named Lucy. Lucy loved to play and have fun. One day, Lucy was walking in the park and saw a big plate of spaghetti. Lucy was very hungry and wanted to eat the spaghetti. A little boy named Tim saw Lucy looking at the spaghetti. Tim said, "Hi, Lucy! Do you want to join me and eat this spaghetti?" Lucy meowed happily and nodded her head. Tim and Lucy sat down together and started eating the spaghetti. As they ate, Lucy and Tim laughed and talked. They became very good friends. From that day on, they always played and ate spaghetti together. They were happy to have each other as friends.
