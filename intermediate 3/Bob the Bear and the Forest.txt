Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bear and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a little bear named Bob. Bob liked to take long walks in the forest. One day Bob was walking in the forest and he came across a spring. The water was sparkling and it glittered in the sun. Suddenly, Bob felt dizzy. He started to tremble - his furry little body shaking from head to paw. He didn't know what to do. He stood completely still as he tried to shake off the dizziness. After a few moments, he was feeling better and he kept walking. Bob was careful now, he knew that spring could have strange effects on animals and he wanted to be careful.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Catch. Catch loved to jump high in the air. One day, Catch saw a big jar of ink on a high shelf. He wanted to see what was inside. Catch jumped high to reach the jar of ink. He knocked it down, and the ink spilled all over the floor. Catch was so surprised! The ink was very dark and made a big mess. Catch's friend, a dog named Spot, came to help clean up the ink. They worked together to make the floor clean again. Catch learned to be careful when jumping high near things that can spill.

Jill was playing with her toys on the beach. Suddenly she heard a loud noise coming from the sea. She looked out and saw an ugly octopus. Jill was scared and started to cry. "Jill, what's wrong?" asked her mom. "I heard a loud noise and saw an ugly octopus," replied Jill. "It's ok, it just means you have to listen and be careful," said her mom. Jill nodded and walked slowly to the sea. The octopus was still there. It was huge and very ugly. Jill wanted to scream, but she didn't. She bravely walked closer to the octopus and listened. The octopus said, "You don't have to be afraid of me. I won't hurt you." Jill felt relieved. She smiled and said, "Okay! I won't be afraid anymore." The octopus thanked her and said goodbye. Jill waved back and ran back to the beach to play with her toys.

Once upon a time there was a funny little girl called Daisy. She loved to laugh and play. One day she wanted to go outside, but it was very hot. Daisy said, "It's so hot I can hardly rise!" So she went inside and opened the fridge. Inside the fridge was a bottle of wine. Daisy was so excited, she said, “Weird! What is this?” She grabbed the bottle and opened it. She didn't know what it was, but she decided to drink it anyway. Daisy took a sip and she started to rise, up and up she went until she reached the ceiling! Now Daisy had a funny, new way of getting around the house. Whenever she wanted to go somewhere, all she had to do was take a sip of wine. The end.

Lila and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to look for bugs and flowers. They saw a big jar on the tip of a table. It was full of fireflies. The fireflies made the jar glow with light. "Wow, look at that!" Lila said. "Can we open it?" Ben shook his head. "No, we can't. Mom said it belongs to Mr. Lee. He is very nosy. He will be mad if we touch his jar." But Lila was curious. She wanted to see the fireflies up close. She climbed on a chair and reached for the jar. Ben tried to stop her, but it was too late. Lila knocked the jar off the table. It fell on the ground and broke. The fireflies flew out of the jar and into the air. "Oh no!" Ben said. "What have you done?" Lila felt sorry. She did not mean to break the jar. She saw Mr. Lee coming out of his house. He looked angry. He saw the broken jar and the fireflies. "Who did this?" he shouted. "Who messed with my fireflies?" Lila and Ben ran away. They hid behind a bush. They hoped Mr. Lee would not find them. They learned their lesson. They should not be nosy and touch things that are not theirs.
