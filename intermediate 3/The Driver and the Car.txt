Category: Intermediate
Title: The Driver and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a driver who had a very nice car. The car was attractive and everyone liked it. The driver liked to use the car to go on trips with his friends. One day, the driver and his friends went to the park. They played and had a lot of fun. They were very happy. But when they were ready to go home, something unexpected happened. The attractive car was gone! They were very sad and didn't know what to do. Then, they saw a little girl with a toy car. It looked just like the attractive car! The girl said, "I used magic to make your car small!" The driver and his friends laughed. They were not sad anymore. They played with the toy car and had even more fun. The little girl showed them how to use magic to make the car big again, and they all went home happy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sally. Sally liked to sell things. She had a little shop where she sold toys, balloons, and candy. One day, her friend Billy came to the shop. Sally had a big surprise for him. "Hi, Billy!" said Sally. "I have a surprise for you!" Billy was excited. He loved surprises. Sally gave him a big, round, spinning toy. Billy played with the toy and started to feel dizzy. He laughed and said, "Sally, this toy makes me dizzy!" Sally laughed too. She said, "I knew you would like it! It's a fun toy to play with when you're feeling happy." Billy thanked Sally for the surprise and they played with the toy together. They had a great time, even when they felt dizzy. And they were the best of friends, always sharing surprises and fun times at Sally's shop.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to eat blueberries. One day, she found a big basket of blueberries in her kitchen. Sue was very happy. Before she could eat them, her mom said, "Sue, you need to wash the blueberries first." So, Sue took the blueberries to the sink and washed them. She washed them until they were clean and shiny. After washing the blueberries, Sue found a broken toy on the floor. She was sad because it was her favorite toy. But then, her mom came and fixed the broken toy. Sue was happy again. She ate her clean blueberries and played with her fixed toy all day long. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide and wanted to play on it. He climbed up the stairs and sat down at the top. "I'm going to slide down now!" he shouted to his mom. As he slid down, he saw a terrible print on the slide. It looked like a big scary face. Tim was scared and didn't want to slide anymore. He told his mom about the print. "Mom, there's a terrible print on the slide. I don't want to slide anymore." His mom looked at the slide and smiled. "Tim, that's not a terrible print. It's just a picture someone drew with chalk. It will wash away with the rain." Tim felt better and slid down the slide again. As he slid, something unexpected happened. The print started to change. It turned into a big, happy smiley face. Tim was so happy to see the smiley face. He laughed and said, "Look, mom! The terrible print turned into a happy face!" Tim and his mom smiled and played on the slide all day.

Tom was a boy who liked to play with his dad's things. One day, he found his dad's razor and shaving cream in the bathroom. He wanted to try them, so he put some cream on his face and used the razor to make a mustache. He looked in the mirror and laughed. He thought he looked like a grown-up. He ran to the living room, where his mom and dad were watching TV. He wanted to show them his mustache. He said, "Look at me, I have a mustache like dad!" He waved his hand and smiled. But his mom and dad did not smile. They looked at him with big eyes and open mouths. They saw that Tom had cut his face with the razor and there was blood on his mustache. They were scared and angry. They said, "Tom, what have you done? That is very dangerous and naughty! You could have hurt yourself badly! Go to the bathroom and wash your face right now!" Tom felt ashamed. He did not mean to make his mom and dad upset. He did not know that the razor was sharp and could hurt him. He just wanted to have fun and look like his dad. He said, "I'm sorry, mom and dad. I won't do it again." He ran to the bathroom and washed his face. He cried and wished he had not played with his dad's things.
