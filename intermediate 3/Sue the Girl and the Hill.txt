Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Hill

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to climb a big hill. On top of the hill, there was a pretty rose. Sue wanted that rose. Sue asked her mom, "Can I climb the hill to get the rose?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Sue was happy and started to climb the hill. It was hard, but she kept going. When Sue reached the top, she saw that the rose was not original. It was a fake rose! Sue was sad. But then, she saw a real rose near the fake one. She took the real rose and went back to her mom. Sue learned that sometimes, things are not what they seem.

One day, a little girl named Daisy went to the park with her mom. Daisy loved to play on the swings and slide. She also liked to pick flowers. Today, she found a very pretty, strong flower that she wanted to take home. Daisy's mom said, "No, we can't take the flower home. It needs to stay in the park." But Daisy insisted. She really wanted the flower. She thought it was special. Daisy and her mom talked about it. They decided that Daisy could look at the strong flower every time they came to the park. That way, the flower could stay in the park, and Daisy could still enjoy it.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a silly cable. It was all twisty and bendy. Tim thought it was a toy, so he played with it. He made it into shapes like a snake and a circle. It was so much fun! Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. She saw the silly cable and asked, "What are you playing with, Tim?" Tim was happy to answer, "It's a fun toy I found! We can play with it together." Sue was excited and they played with the silly cable all day. Then, something unexpected happened. The silly cable started to wiggle and move all by itself! Tim and Sue were very surprised. The silly cable wasn't a toy after all. It was a friendly snake! They laughed and played with their new snake friend, having more fun than ever before.

Once upon a time, there was an old grill. The old grill lived in a big park. Many people came to the park to play and have fun. One day, a small boy named Tim came to the park with his mom. Tim saw the old grill and said, "Mom, can we use the grill to cook food?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. We can cook yummy food on the grill." They put some food on the grill to cook. The food smelled very good. While the food cooked, Tim saw a butterfly. The butterfly was stuck in a net. Tim wanted to help the butterfly. He said, "Mom, I want to release the butterfly." His mom said, "Good job, Tim. Let's release the butterfly together." They opened the net and the butterfly flew away. Tim and his mom were very happy. They went back to the old grill, ate their yummy food, and had a great day at the park.

One day, a little cat named Kitty felt very tired. She let out a big yawn. Kitty was hungry too. She wanted to find some food to eat. So, she went to the kitchen to look for something yummy. In the kitchen, Kitty found a menu. She was confused. She did not know what a menu was. But she saw pictures of food on it. There was a picture of fish, a picture of milk, and a picture of a cookie. Kitty liked all of these things! Kitty pointed to the fish on the menu. She wanted to eat fish. She meowed and meowed until her friend, a big dog named Buddy, came to help. Buddy saw Kitty pointing at the menu. He went to the fridge and got some fish for Kitty. They both ate and were happy. Kitty did not feel tired or hungry anymore.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a big, shiny knob on a door. She was very proud of her find. She wanted to show it to her best friend, Tom. Tom came over and looked at the knob. He thought it was very cool. "Let's discuss what we can do with this knob," said Lucy. Tom thought for a moment and then said, "We can make a secret room with this special door." Lucy liked that idea a lot. They both worked together to make a secret room. Inside the secret room, they found many fun toys. They played with the toys and had a great time. When it was time to go home, they closed the door with the big shiny knob. They felt very proud of their secret room and couldn't wait to play there again.
