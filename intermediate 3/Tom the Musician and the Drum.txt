Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Musician and the Drum

Once upon a time, there was a musician named Tom. Tom loved to play music on his drum. He lived in a small house near a thick forest. One day, it started to rain. The rain made a nice sound on the roof of Tom's house. Tom thought the rain sounded like music. He decided to play his drum along with the rain sound. So, he took his drum and went outside. Tom met a little girl named Sally. Sally heard the music and came to see who was playing. Sally said, "I like your music! Can I play with you?" Tom said, "Yes! Let's play together in the rain!" So, Tom and Sally played music and danced in the rain. They had a lot of fun and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her fancy toy blocks. The blocks had numbers on them. Lucy liked to stack them up and make tall towers. One day, Lucy's friend Tim came over to play. "Can I add some blocks to your tower?" Tim asked. Lucy said, "Yes, let's make it the biggest tower ever!" They took turns adding blocks with numbers on them. As they added more and more blocks, the tower grew taller and taller. Lucy and Tim were so happy to see their fancy tower with all the numbers. They counted the numbers together, and laughed as they played.

Once upon a time, there was a shy boy named Bob. Bob didn't like to talk to anyone and he always kept to himself. One day, Bob was feeling sick and he went to the doctor. The doctor said, "Bob, I'm going to give you some medicine to make you feel better." Bob was very scared of the medicine. He started to cry and said, "No, I don't want the medicine!" The doctor smiled and said, "Don't worry, it won't hurt. It will help you get better." Bob was still nervous, but he tried the medicine and it made him feel much better. From that day on, Bob was less shy and he started talking to people more.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to bathe in the pond near his house. Every day, he would play in the water with his friends. They would splash and swim, having lots of fun. One day, Ducky saw a new friend by the pond. He was very alert and asked a question, "Hi, do you want to play with us in the water?" The new friend was a cat named Kitty. Kitty said, "I don't like water, but I can watch you play." Ducky and his friends played and splashed in the water, while Kitty watched. After a while, Kitty saw that Ducky and his friends were having so much fun. Kitty felt brave and decided to try the water. Slowly, Kitty stepped into the pond and found that it was not so bad. Soon, Kitty was playing and splashing with Ducky and his friends. The moral of the story is to be brave and try new things, even if they seem scary at first.

Once upon a time, there was a great frog. The frog lived in a pond with many other frogs. This frog was very big and green. All the other frogs liked the great frog. One day, the great frog decided to go on a walk. The other frogs wanted to go too. So, they all started to follow the great frog. They hopped and jumped all around the pond. As they followed the great frog, they saw many fun things. They saw fish, birds, and bugs. The frogs had a great day following their big, green friend. At the end of the day, they all went back to the pond, happy and tired.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a bit confused one day. He found a small cell in his room. It was round and shiny. Tim did not know what it was. He wanted to show his mom. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, look! I found this in my room. What is it?" His mom looked at the shiny cell and smiled. "Oh, Tim," she said, "that is a button from your shirt! It must have fallen off when you were playing." Tim wanted to fix his shirt. His mom said, "I will cut some thread and sew the button back on for you." Tim watched as his mom cut the thread and sewed the button back onto his shirt. He was so happy that his shirt was fixed. From that day on, Tim was not confused about the shiny cell anymore. He knew it was a button. And whenever a button fell off, he knew his mom could fix it with a cut thread and a needle. Tim and his mom lived happily ever after.
