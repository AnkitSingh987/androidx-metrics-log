Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Curious Dog and the Jail

Once upon a time, there was a curious little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Spot saw a big building. It was a jail. Spot had never seen a jail before. Spot wanted to know what was inside the jail. He heard a man inside. The man was giving an order. The man said, "Stay in your room!" Spot was scared, but he was also curious. Spot went closer to the jail. He peeked inside and saw people in rooms. They looked sad. Spot knew he should go home. He did not want to be in jail. Spot ran back to his friends and played all day. He was happy to be free.

Once upon a time, in a big harbor, there was a little boat named Bobby. Bobby was an obedient boat who always did what he was told. He loved to learn new things from the other boats in the harbor. One day, Bobby heard the older boats talking about a big storm coming. The older boats told Bobby to stay near the harbor and be safe. Bobby listened and stayed close, even though he wanted to go out and play. The storm came and the wind blew hard. But Bobby was safe because he stayed near the harbor. When the storm was gone, the older boats were happy that Bobby had learned to be obedient and stay safe. Bobby was proud of himself for listening and learning.

Once upon a time, there was a cold day. A boy named Tim and a girl named Sue wanted to play outside. They put on their coats and went out to have fun. Tim had a case full of toys. They wanted to play together, but they needed to separate the toys first. Tim said, "Sue, let's separate the toys from the case." Sue said, "Okay, Tim!" They took the toys out of the case and put them into two piles. One pile had cars and the other pile had dolls. Tim played with the cars and Sue played with the dolls. But then, the sky became dark and it started to rain. Tim and Sue were sad. They quickly put the toys back into the case and ran inside. They were cold and wet. Their toys were wet too. They could not play with them anymore. The day had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there were two friends who loved to play together. They were both very excited one day when they discovered a big, shiny cable. It was long and sparkly and it seemed like it was asking them to play with it. The friends split the cable in two and decided to have a race. One of they said "On your mark, get set, zip!" and then both of them started to race. But soon the other friend realised that his cable was much shorter than the first friend's. The first friend said "You cheated! You made your cable shorter so you could win faster!" He then said, "That's so selfish!" The other friend was very sorry and said "I'm sorry. I made the wrong decision. Let's both use the same cable and race again." The friends both smiled and agreed. They had a fair race and everyone had lots of fun. From then on, they always played fairly.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big heart and loved to help others. One day, Mia went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a toy for her little brother. At the store, Mia saw a pretty doll. She said to her mom, "Mom, I want to buy this doll with my own money." Her mom smiled and said, "Okay, Mia. You can pay for the doll." Mia was happy because she had some money available from helping her mom at home. She gave the money to the store lady and took the doll home. Mia's little brother saw the doll and said, "Wow, Mia! Thank you for the toy!" Mia felt the love from her brother and was glad she could make him happy. That night, Mia and her brother played with the doll and had lots of fun. They knew that sharing and caring was the best way to show love to each other.
