Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. One day, while they were playing, Tim found a big, healthy tree. The tree had many branches and leaves. Tim thought it was the perfect tree to climb. Tim's friend, Sam, said, "I value this tree too. Let's climb it together!" So, they started to climb the tree. They were so happy and excited. But, as they climbed higher, they saw something strange. There was a big cut on the tree trunk. Tim and Sam were curious about the cut. They thought, "Who could have made this cut?" Then, suddenly, a squirrel popped out of the cut! The squirrel looked at them and said, "Hello! I made this cut to hide my nuts. Do you want to play with me?" Tim and Sam were surprised to see a talking squirrel. But they were happy to make a new friend. So, they played with the squirrel all day long. From that day on, the healthy tree with the big cut became their favorite place to play. And they all lived happily ever after.

On Tuesday, Lily and Ben went to the park with Mom. They played on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. They had a lot of fun and made new friends. Mom gave them juice and cookies for a snack. "Mom, can we stay longer?" Lily asked. "Sorry, sweetie, we have to go home soon. It's almost time for your nap," Mom said. "But we're not sleepy," Ben said. Mom smiled and said, "You will be, after all this playing. And you know what? When you nap, you can dream. Dreaming is like making up stories in your head. You can dream about anything you want." "Really?" Lily and Ben said. "Yes, really. You can dream about flying, or being a superhero, or going to the moon, or anything else. Dreaming is fun and good for you. It helps you grow and learn and be wise," Mom said. "What does wise mean?" Lily asked. "Wise means you know a lot of things, and you make good choices, and you are kind and helpful to others," Mom said. "I want to be wise," Ben said. "Me too," Lily said. "Then let's go home and nap and dream," Mom said. Lily and Ben agreed. They said goodbye to their friends and followed Mom to the car. They were happy and excited to dream. They wondered what they would dream about. Maybe they would dream about each other. Maybe they would dream about Mom. Maybe they would dream about something new and amazing. They closed their eyes and drifted into sleep. They began to dream.

Once upon a time, there was a mommy and her two little daughters, Emma and Mia. One day, Mia was exploring in the kitchen and she saw a bottle. She asked her mom: "What's this mommy?" Her mom said: "It's a bottle. We can get juice out of it." Mia got very excited. She said: "I wanna get juice!" So her mommy got out some juice. But then, Emma said: "How about I organize it? We can have a really fun time!" Mia and Emma got busy and started organizing the bottles. They made three piles: juice, water and milk. Once they were done, Emma said: "Let's get some juice!" Mia and Emma clinked their bottles together and made a toast. They said: "Let's get organized today!" The girls laughed and spent their day having a lot of fun. They made sure to always stay organized.

Once upon a time, there was a crazy train. It had a big engine that went chug-chug. The train loved to go fast. One day, the train was going so fast, it missed a stop. The people on the train were sad. They wanted to get off the train, but they could not. The train went faster and faster. It missed more stops. The people were very scared. The train could not stop. At last, the train went too fast and fell off the tracks. The train was broken, and the people were not happy.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim wanted to build. He had many blocks in his room. Tim liked the color orange, so he used many orange blocks. He started to build a high tower with the blocks. Tim's cat, Fluffy, saw the high tower. Fluffy was curious and wanted to play too. Fluffy jumped and hit the orange tower. The tower fell down. Tim was sad, but then he had a new idea. Tim decided to build again, but this time, he built a big house for Fluffy. Fluffy loved the new house. Tim was happy because he could build and play with Fluffy at the same time. They both had fun together.
