Title: Tim and the Doctor

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the doctor with his mom. The doctor was amazing and very kind. He would always make Tim feel better when he was sick. Tim liked going to the doctor. While they were waiting, Tim's mom said, "Tim, please repeat after me. I will be brave at the doctor's today." Tim repeated, "I will be brave at the doctor's today." They said it a few more times, and Tim felt ready to see the doctor. When it was Tim's turn, the doctor came in with a big smile. But then, something unexpected happened. The doctor slipped on a toy car and fell down! Tim and his mom were surprised. Tim looked at the doctor and said, "Doctor, please repeat after me. I will be careful at the doctor's today." The doctor laughed and repeated, "I will be careful at the doctor's today." They all laughed and had an amazing day at the doctor's office.

One day, a playful dog named Max went outside to play. He saw a big box on the earth. Max was curious and wanted to see what was inside the box. He started to unpack the box with his paws. Inside the box, Max found a little cat. The cat was scared and said, "Who are you?" Max smiled and said, "I am Max, a playful dog. I want to be your friend." The cat was not sure if she wanted to be friends with Max, because she thought dogs were mean. Max tried to show the cat that he was a good friend. They played with a ball and had fun. But then, Max accidentally stepped on the cat's tail. The cat was sad and cried, "Ouch! That hurt!" Max felt bad and said, "I am sorry. I did not mean to hurt you." The cat forgave Max and they continued to play together. They learned that even though they were different, they could still be friends. From that day on, Max and the cat were the best of friends and always played together.

Once upon a time, there was a king who had a big, shiny crown. He loved his crown and wore it every day. One day, the king needed to load his wagon with food for a party. He asked his friend, the knight, to help him. "Can you help me load the wagon?" the king asked the knight. The knight said, "Yes, I will help you." Together, they put the food into the wagon. They put apples, bananas, and cookies inside. The king and the knight worked very hard. They were successful! The wagon was full, and they were ready for the party. The king put on his crown and smiled. "Thank you for helping me," he said. The knight smiled back and said, "You're welcome, my king." They went to the party and had lots of fun with their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy parrot. He lived in a big tree. The parrot liked to sleep all day. One day, the parrot heard a noise. He opened his eyes and saw a little girl. The girl said, "Hi, parrot! I want to find my ball. Can you help me?" The lazy parrot did not want to help, but he liked the girl. So, he said, "Okay, I will help you." The parrot and the girl looked for the ball. They looked in the grass. They looked by the tree. At last, they found the ball near a bush. The girl was happy. She said, "Thank you, parrot!" The lazy parrot smiled and went back to sleep.

Once upon a time there was a brave little girl called Mary. Mary was troubled. Every day she would look up at the sun, thinking about how she could help her troubled town. One sunny morning, Mary went for a walk and found a beautiful yellow flower. She looked closely at the flower and then bowed. She was so happy that she bent all the way down, almost touching the ground. A tall man approached and said, "Good morning, Mary. What are you doing?" Mary looked up, smiled and said, "I was bowing to the sun. I'm wishing for an end to all this troubledness in our town." The man smiled and thanked Mary for her brave wish. As Mary watched the tall man walk away, she continued to bow to the sun. She knew that her wish might come true.
