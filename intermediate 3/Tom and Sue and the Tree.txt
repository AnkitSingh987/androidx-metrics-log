Title: Tom and Sue and the Tree

One day, Tom and Sue went to play outside. The sunshine was bright and warm. They saw a big tree with yummy red apples. Tom said, "Look, Sue! Yummy apples! Let's eat some." Sue said, "Yes, let's eat!" They picked some apples and sat under the tree. They took big bites and smiled. The apples were so yummy! Just then, a funny thing happened. A tiny squirrel came down from the tree. It looked at Tom and Sue and said, "Hello, friends! Thank you for finding my yummy apples. Can I eat with you?" Tom and Sue were surprised, but they smiled and said, "Of course, little squirrel! Let's all eat yummy apples together!" And they all had a fun time eating apples under the warm sunshine.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a harmless dog who loved to play with his ball. He lived with a boy named Tim. Tim and Spot were best friends. One day, Tim's family decided to celebrate his birthday. They had a big party with balloons and treats for everyone. Spot was very excited to join in the fun. He played with the balloons and ate some treats too. At the end of the day, Tim and Spot were very happy. They had a great time celebrating with their friends and family. They knew that they would always be best friends, and they looked forward to many more celebrations and treats together.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly lion. The lion lived in a big, green forest. He liked to play with his friends, the happy monkey and the funny bird. They liked to run, jump, and laugh together. One hot day, the lion was very thirsty. He wanted to drink some water. So, the lion, the monkey, and the bird went to look for a river. They walked and walked until they found a small, blue river. The jolly lion drank the cool water. He felt so much better! The monkey and the bird also drank from the river. After they drank, they played by the river all day long. They were all very happy.

Lily wanted to see the stars on her ceiling. She had a lamp that made them shine at night, but it was too high for her. She asked her big brother Sam to help her. "Sam, can you turn on my star lamp, please?" she said. Sam was busy with his homework, but he was a dependable brother. He put down his pencil and went to Lily's room. "Sure, Lily. Where is your lamp?" he said. Lily pointed to the shelf near the ceiling. "There, Sam. It is too high for me. I cannot reach it." Sam smiled and lifted Lily up. She reached for the lamp and turned it on. The ceiling was filled with bright stars. "Wow, thank you, Sam. You are the best brother ever!" Lily said. "You're welcome, Lily. You are the best sister ever!" Sam said. They hugged and looked at the stars together. Lily felt happy and safe with her dependable brother.

One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sam. They wanted to play hockey together. They were very happy to see each other. "Hi, Tim! Let's play hockey!" said Sam. They took their hockey sticks and went to the park. The sun was high in the sky, and it was a beautiful day. They played and laughed together. After playing, they saw a girl who was sad. She had lost her hockey ball. Tim and Sam looked for it and found it under a bush. They gave it back to the girl, who smiled and said, "Thank you!" The boys felt good about helping the girl. They learned that it is nice to help others when they need it. And they played hockey together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a red car. The red car lived in a garage. The garage was big and nice. The red car liked to go fast. One day, the red car saw a big ball in the road. The red car did not want to hit the ball. The red car wanted to prevent the ball from getting hurt. The red car stopped fast. The red car told the ball to be careful. The ball said thank you. The red car and the ball became friends. They played together in the garage. They were happy.
