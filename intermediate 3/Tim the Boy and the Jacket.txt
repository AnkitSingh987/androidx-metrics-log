Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Jacket

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a jacket that he loved very much. It was a gift from his mom. One day, Tim went to a show where people perform. He wore his jacket to the show. At the show, Tim saw a man who looked miserable. The man was cold and sad. Tim thought about his jacket and how warm it made him feel. He wanted to help the man, so he gave him his jacket. The man was happy, and Tim felt good about helping him. But when Tim went home, he felt cold and sad without his jacket. He missed it very much. The next day, he tried to find the man to get his jacket back, but the man was gone. Tim never saw his jacket again. The moral of the story is to think about the long-term effects of your actions before you do something.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. Amy wore her favorite yellow dress. She loved to play in the park with her friends. Amy's mom had a camera to take photos of Amy as she played. While playing, a nice dog came up to Amy. The dog was big and yellow too. Amy asked her mom, "Can I play with the yellow dog?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Amy played with the dog and they had fun. Amy's mom took a photo of Amy and the dog playing together. Then, something unexpected happened. The yellow dog found a wallet on the ground. Amy gave the wallet to her mom. They looked inside and found the owner's name. Amy and her mom took the wallet to the owner's house. The owner was so happy and thanked Amy for being honest. The moral of the story is to always do the right thing, even when no one is watching.

Once upon a time, little Sarah was looking out of her window. She saw a bright shining light and it was the most beautiful sight she had ever seen. She got so excited that she couldn't help but run outside to see what it was. When she got outside, standing in the light was her wonderful Uncle. She had not seen him in a long time and she was so happy. She started to jump around with excitement and ran up to him, giving him a big hug. Uncle asked Sarah, "Do you like the light, sweetheart?" and Sarah replied, "Yes, Uncle! I love it!" "Let's go shine it together!" said Uncle with a smile. So they did. Together they shined the light all around the house, and it made the world sparkle and shine. Little Sarah and her Uncle had the best time and were so excited to be sharing it. They had tons of fun and were even shining into the night sky. When it was time to go to bed, Sarah was so happy that she could send her Uncle of with a warm hug and lots of love in her heart.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they find a big box of labels. The labels have different words and pictures on them. "Look, Lily, we can use these labels to teach each other new things!" Tom says. He picks up a label that says "apple" and has a red fruit on it. He sticks it on a real apple on the table. "This is an apple. It is red and sweet. Can you say apple?" "Apple!" Lily repeats. She smiles and claps her hands. She picks up another label that says "dog" and has a brown animal on it. She sticks it on a stuffed dog on the floor. "This is a dog. It is brown and furry. It can bark and run. Can you say dog?" "Dog!" Tom repeats. He nods and hugs the stuffed dog. He likes dogs. He picks up another label that says "intelligent" and has a star on it. He sticks it on Lily's shirt. "This is intelligent. It means you are smart and learn fast. You are intelligent, Lily. Can you say intelligent?" Lily looks at the label and frowns. She does not know what it means. She tries to say it, but it is hard. "In-tel-li-gent?" She stutters. Tom laughs and says, "Yes, that's right. You are intelligent, Lily. And so am I. We are both intelligent, because we like to teach and learn new things. That is a good thing to be. Can you say good?" "Good!" Lily says. She smiles and sticks the label on Tom's shirt. "You are good, Tom. And so am I. We are both good, because we are kind and helpful. That is a good thing to be. Can you say kind?" "Kind!" Tom says. He smiles and gives Lily a high-five. "You are kind, Lily. And so am I. We are both kind, because we share and care. That is a good thing to be. Can you say share?" "Share!" Lily says. She looks at the box of labels and says, "Let's share these labels with our other friends. Maybe they want to learn new things too. Do you want to share, Tom?" "Yes, I want to share, Lily. Sharing is fun and fair. Let's go and find our other friends. Maybe they have some labels for us too. Come on, Lily, let's go!" Tom and Lily take the box of labels and run to the playground. They find their other friends and show them the labels. They teach and learn new things together. They are intelligent, good and kind. They are happy.
