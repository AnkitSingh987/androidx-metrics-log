Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Car

Tom likes to play with his toy cars. He has many cars of different colors and sizes. He likes to make them go fast and make noises. Sometimes he makes them crash and flip over. One day, he finds a new car in his toy box. It is red and shiny and has a button on the roof. Tom wonders what the button does. He pushes it and the car makes a loud beep. Then it starts to move by itself. Tom is surprised and happy. He follows the car as it zooms around the room. The car goes under the table, around the chair, and over the rug. It is very smart and avoids hitting anything. Tom thinks it is the best car ever. He wants to see how far it can go. He takes a measure from his dad's toolbox and puts it on the floor. He makes the car go past the measure and counts the numbers. The car goes very far. But then, the car reaches the wall. It cannot go any further. Tom expects it to stop or turn around. But it does not. Instead, it pushes the button on the roof again. The car makes another loud beep. Then it starts to reverse. It goes backwards very fast. Tom is shocked and scared. He tries to catch the car, but it is too quick. The car reverses past the measure, over the rug, around the chair, and under the table. It does not avoid anything. It hits the table leg and makes it shake. It hits the chair and makes it fall. It hits the rug and makes it slide. It makes a lot of noise and mess. Tom is worried and sad. He does not like the car anymore. He wants it to stop. He hears his mom coming. She is alert and angry. She sees the car and the mess. She sees Tom and the measure. She is not happy. She takes the car and the measure away. She tells Tom to clean up and to be careful. She tells him to never play with that car again. Tom is sorry and sad. He wishes he never pushed the button. He wishes he never found the car. He wishes he played with his other cars instead. He learns a lesson. He does not like to reverse.

One day, a fine reindeer named Bob was playing in the mud. He had so much fun, but he got very dirty. Bob knew he needed to wash before going home. As he walked to the river to wash, he saw a big frog. The frog said, "Hi Bob! You are very dirty. Do you need help to wash?" Bob said, "Yes, please help me, frog." The frog jumped into the water and splashed Bob. But instead of washing the mud off, the frog's splash made Bob even more dirty! The frog said, "Oh no, I am sorry Bob!" They both laughed and decided to wash together. Now they were both clean and happy.

One day, a boy named Tim found some clay. The clay was soft and flexible. Tim liked to play with the clay. He made a toy with the clay. The toy was a little cat. Tim loved the cat very much. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. They played with the clay cat. But they played too hard. The clay cat broke. Tim was very sad. Sam was sorry. He said he would help Tim repair the cat. Sam and Tim worked together. They used more clay to fix the cat. They made the cat stronger. The cat was now even better than before. Tim and Sam were happy. They learned that when they work together, they can fix anything.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a mighty tree. The tree was very big and tall. It had many green leaves. The tree was very good at making oxygen for all the animals to breathe. One day, a little bird came to the tree and asked, "Tree, can I sit on your branch?" The tree answered, "Yes, little bird. You can sit on my branch and sing your pretty song." The little bird was very happy and sang a beautiful song. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. The mighty tree started to talk! It said, "Thank you, little bird, for your song. It made me feel so happy that I can now talk!" The little bird was very surprised and said, "Wow, tree! I didn't know you could talk!" The tree and the little bird became very good friends. They talked and laughed together every day, and the tree kept making oxygen for all the animals in the forest.
