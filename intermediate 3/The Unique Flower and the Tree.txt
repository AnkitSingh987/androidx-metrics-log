Category: Intermediate
Title: The Unique Flower and the Tree

Once there was a unique flower. It was very pretty. The flower wanted to grow tall and strong. It tried very hard every day. The flower wanted to succeed. One day, the flower saw a big tree. The tree was very tall. The flower wanted to be as tall as the tree. The flower tried harder to grow. It was still not as tall as the tree. Then, something unexpected happened. The tree bent down to the flower. The tree said, "You are unique and pretty. You do not need to be tall like me." The flower was happy. It knew it could succeed by being itself.

John and his mom were walking through the forest. Suddenly, John saw something. "Look, mom!" he yelled and pointed to the ground. His mom looked down and discovered a big cob of corn. John was so excited. He had never seen a cob of corn before, especially a black one like this. "Wow, mom," John said. "Can we take it home?" John's mom smiled and said, "Yes, of course!" John bent down and carefully picked up the cob of corn. He held it tight in his hands and they began walking back home. On their way, John noticed a crow flying in the sky. "Mom," he said. "Look at the black crow in the sky!" John's mom smiled and said, "Yes, I see it! Let's get home and share our special discovery in the forest with the family.

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy jet. It was old and dirty. He took it home to play with it. Tim loved to fly the jet around his room, making it zoom through the air. As Tim played, he heard a small voice. "Hello," said the voice. Tim looked around and saw that the old jet was talking to him! "Hello," Tim replied, surprised. "How can you talk?" The jet told Tim that it was magic. They became friends and played together every day. One day, as they played, the jet began to glow. Suddenly, the old jet turned into a shiny new jet! Tim and the jet were very happy and continued to have fun and play together.

One day, a girl named Sue went for a walk. It was a wet day, and she wore a raincoat to stay dry. Sue walked by the park and saw a pretty veil stuck in a tree. She wanted to get it down. Sue tried to push the tree, but it was too big. A boy named Tom saw her and asked, "What are you doing?" Sue said, "I want to get the veil, but I can't reach it." Tom thought of an idea. Tom found a long stick and said, "Let's use this to push the veil down." Sue liked the idea. They both pushed the stick and the veil fell down. Sue was happy and thanked Tom. They played together in the park, and Sue wore her new veil.

Once upon a time, there was a lively cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her toys and run around the house. She was always happy and full of energy. One day, Kitty's owner, a kind girl named Sally, had to take an exam at school. The exam was very important to her, and she wanted to do well. Sally studied hard every day, and as she learned more, her confidence increased. On the day of the exam, Kitty stayed by Sally's side to cheer her up. When Sally came home, she was very happy because she did well on her exam. Kitty was proud of her friend and they played together all day long. They were both very happy and had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Mia looked up at the sky. She saw a small planet far away. Mia sighed and said, "Oh, I want to go there." Mia met a kind bird named Bob. "Can you take me to the planet?" she asked. Bob nodded and said, "Yes, let's go!" They flew high up in the sky, closer to the planet. When they got there, they saw a poor, sad dog. The dog looked at Mia and said, "I am lonely. Will you be my friend?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, we can be friends. Let's all play together!" And they played and laughed on the little planet, happy to have new friends.
