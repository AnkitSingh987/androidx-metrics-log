Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Wagon

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went for a walk. They saw a red wagon near their house. Tim said to Max, "Let's play with the wagon!" Max wagged his tail and barked, happy to play. As they played, a girl named Lily came by. She saw the red wagon and felt embarrassed. "That's my wagon," she said. Tim felt bad and wanted to make her feel better. He suggested, "Let's all play together!" They all played with the wagon, having fun. Suddenly, the wagon started to move on its own! It went faster and faster. Tim, Max, and Lily were surprised. They chased the wagon, laughing and having a great time. In the end, they caught the wagon and found out it was a magic wagon. It could move on its own and make everyone happy. They all became good friends and played with the magic wagon every day.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, pretty marble. He was very excited. He showed it to his friend, Sam. Sam loved the marble too. He asked Tim, "Can you lend me your marble to play with?" Tim thought about it and said, "Yes, I can lend it to you, but you have to give it back when you are done." Sam agreed, and they played with the marble all day. They had lots of fun rolling it, and they even shared it with their other friends. At the end of the day, Sam gave the marble back to Tim. They both learned that sharing and lending can make everyone happy. The friends knew that when they shared, they could have more fun together. And that is how Tim, Sam, and their friends learned the joy of sharing.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the yard. They have a big sandbox and many toys. They also have a watering can and a bucket. One day, they decide to make a sand castle. They fill the bucket with sand and water. They pour the wet sand on the ground. They pat it with their hands. They make a tower and a wall. "Look, Lily, our castle is so big!" Ben says. "Yes, Ben, it is very nice. But it needs something more," Lily says. "What does it need?" Ben asks. Lily thinks for a moment. She sees a flower in the grass. She picks it and puts it on top of the tower. "It needs a flag!" she says. Ben smiles. He thinks the flower is adorable. He picks another one and puts it on the wall. "Now it has two flags!" he says. Lily and Ben are happy. They hug each other. They admire their sand castle. They think it is the best castle ever.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a pet bird named Blue. One day, Blue flew away from the house and got stuck in a tree. Blue was scared and cried for help. Lily heard Blue and saw him on a high branch. "Don't worry, Blue! I will rescue you!" she said. But Lily was too small to reach the branch. She needed help to save her friend. A boy named Tom heard Lily and came to help. Tom and Lily found a long stick. They used the stick to get Blue down from the branch. Blue was very happy to be safe again. Lily and Tom became good friends, and they all played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little house. In the house, there was a stove. The stove was very good at cooking food. It made the house warm and cozy. The people who lived in the house loved the stove very much. One day, a big wind came and blew the house away. The stove was sad because it did not have a home anymore. It wanted to change and find a new home. The stove went on a long walk to look for a new place to live. After a long walk, the stove found a new house. The people in the new house were very happy to have the stove. The stove changed their home and made it warm and cozy. The stove was happy again, and everyone lived happily ever after.
