Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fierce Dragon and the Lake

Once upon a time there lived a fierce dragon. Every day the dragon flew through the sky and made the animals tremble with fear. One day, the dragon spotted a small house by a lake. Inside the house was a boy and his mother. The dragon flew down to the house. "Mother, help me!" said the boy. The mother smiled and said, "I will help you. I know a poem." She began to recite her poem and the dragon suddenly became calm. The boy and his mother were safe! The dragon flew away and the boy was so happy he hugged his mother. "Thank you for saving us," said the boy. The mother smiled and said, "Anytime!"

Lily loved her furry teddy bear. She named him Glow, because he had a shiny button on his chest that made him glow in the dark. She always hugged him at night and told him stories. One day, Lily saw a beautiful rose in the garden. It was red and smelled nice. She wanted to pick it and give it to Glow. She thought he would like it. She reached for the rose, but she did not see the thorns. Ouch! The rose pricked her finger and made it bleed. Lily cried and dropped the rose. "Are you okay, Lily?" her mom asked, hearing her cry. She ran to the garden and saw Lily holding her finger. She saw the rose on the ground. "Oh, you tried to pick the rose, but it hurt you. I'm sorry, sweetie. Come, let me clean your finger and put a band-aid on it." Lily's mom took her inside and washed her finger. She put a band-aid on it and kissed it. "There, all better. You know, roses are very pretty, but they also have thorns. They are like nature's way of saying, 'look, but don't touch'. You have to be careful with them." Lily nodded. She felt better, but she still wanted to give Glow a rose. She looked around and saw a paper rose on the table. It was pink and had glitter on it. It was from her art class. She smiled and took it. She ran to her room and found Glow on her bed. She gave him the paper rose and hugged him. "I'm sorry, Glow. I wanted to give you a real rose, but it hurt me. But I found this paper rose for you. It's pretty and it doesn't have thorns. Do you like it?" Glow's button flashed and he made a soft sound. Lily knew he liked it. She put the paper rose next to his ear and snuggled with him. She whispered, "You're my best friend, Glow. I love you." Glow glowed brighter and warmer. He whispered back, "I love you too, Lily." They fell asleep together, happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little desk. The desk was nervous because it wanted to be loved. The desk sat in a big room with other desks, chairs, and toys. The nervous desk wished that a child would come and play with it. One day, a little girl named Lily came into the room. She saw the nervous desk and smiled. She walked over and said, "Hi, desk! I want to play with you!" The desk was so happy. Lily drew pictures and wrote her name on the desk. They had so much fun together. The desk was not nervous anymore. It felt loved by Lily. Every day, Lily would come and play with the desk. They became the best of friends. The desk was very happy because it was no longer nervous and it was loved.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to collect things. She would collect rocks, leaves, and even bugs. Sue's mom and dad would tell her about history. She liked to hear stories about the past. One day, Sue went to the park to collect more things. She found a big, old tree. Sue was a little fearful because the tree was so big. But she saw something shiny up in the tree. Sue wanted to see what it was. Sue climbed the tree to get the shiny thing. When she got to the top, she found a box. Inside the box, there was a map. The map showed her the way to a hidden treasure. Sue was so excited! She thought she was going to find gold and jewels. But when Sue followed the map, she found something unexpected. The treasure was not gold or jewels. It was a box full of history books! Sue was happy. She could learn more about the past and share the stories with her friends. From that day on, Sue was not fearful of big trees or new things. She knew that sometimes, the best treasures were not shiny or pretty. They were the things that taught her about the world and its history.
