Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Park

Once there were two friends who were walking in the park. They were having a lot of fun, playing together and running around. Then one of them had a plan to capture a bird. He thought it would be a nice pet. The friend went to the tree and looked for a bird to catch. He saw one sitting on a branch and rising up with a big splash. He pulled out a cage and carefully put it on the ground. His friend tried to talk him out of it, but he didn’t listen. The bird flew away, but the friend still went ahead with his plan. He was rude to his friend and ignored his advice. He kept walking and looking for a bird to catch. After a few minutes, he found one in a bush. He quickly opened the cage and grabbed the bird. The bird was scared and flew away. The friend was so angry and frustrated. He yelled at the bird and his friend, then he started to cry. He had learned a lesson that day, that being rude and not listening to your friends doesn’t get you far.

Once upon a time, there were two little fishes. One was named John, and the other was named Kate. They loved to swim in the pond every day. One day, they saw an old rusty cage in the middle of the pond. They swam closer to investigate. Suddenly, a bossy fish appeared. He said, "Get away from my cage!" John and Kate were scared. They started to swim away as fast as they could. John said to Kate, "We should never go near that bossy fish again. Let's just swim around the pond and have fun!" From then on, John and Kate could be seen swimming, playing, and having lots of fun around the pond. They never went near the bossy fish's cage again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big slide. He wanted to slide on it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I slide, please?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Be careful and have fun!" Tim was so happy. He went to the slide and slid down. He went up and down the slide many times. After playing, Tim and his mom went home. Mom made a messy meat sandwich for Tim. Tim ate the sandwich and said, "Yummy! Thank you, Mom!" Tim had a fun day at the park and a tasty meal.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved the color purple. One day, he went to the doctor with his mom. The doctor said, "Tim, you need an x-ray." Tim was scared, but his mom held his hand. They went into a big room. There was a big seat in the middle. The doctor said, "Please, seat on the big chair, Tim." Tim sat down, and the doctor took the x-ray. When the x-ray was done, Tim saw the picture. It looked like a purple skeleton. Tim was so happy. He said, "Mom, look! I have a purple skeleton!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. You are very special." They went home and Tim showed everyone his purple x-ray.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy cars. One day, his mom told him not to play near the big road. She did not permit him to go there because it was not safe. Tim wanted to play with his cars on the big road, but he remembered what his mom said. He asked his friend, Sam, for his opinion. Sam said, "I think it's okay to play there, just be careful." Tim thought about it, but he decided to listen to his mom. One day, Tim was playing with his cars near the big road. He was not being careful and a car almost hit him. His mom saw what happened and was very sad. She told Tim that he should always listen to her and not be careless. Tim was very sorry and promised to be more careful, but the accident left him with a bad injury.
