Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Slow Turtle and the Lion

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there was a slow turtle named Tim. Tim had many friends, like the fast rabbit and the big elephant. They all liked to play and have fun together. One day, while they were playing, they saw a mean lion coming towards them. The fast rabbit said, "We must protect each other from the mean lion!" Tim, the slow turtle, knew he had to help his friends. But he was worried because he was not fast like the rabbit or big like the elephant. The big elephant said, "Tim, you can protect us too, even if you are slow." So, they all made a plan to keep the mean lion away. The fast rabbit would run around and make the lion tired. The big elephant would use his big body to push the lion away. Tim, the slow turtle, had a very important job. He would use his strong shell to protect his friends when the lion tried to get them. The mean lion tried and tried, but he could not get past Tim's strong shell. In the end, the mean lion gave up and went away. All the friends were safe, and they were very proud of Tim, the slow turtle, for helping to protect them. They all played and had fun together in the big jungle, knowing that they could always count on each other.

Tom was a lazy man. He did not like to work or help his wife. He liked to sleep and watch TV all day. His wife was very sad and angry. She had to do all the work at home and take care of their baby. One day, his wife said to him, "Tom, you are too lazy. You need to do something useful. Go to the store and buy some milk and bread for us. And hurry up, we have no food left." Tom did not want to go, but his wife was very loud and scary. He got up and put on his shoes. He took some money and walked to the door. He was not careful and he stumbled on a toy. He fell down and hurt his knee. He cried out loud. His wife heard him and came to see. She saw him on the floor and the toy. She was not sorry for him. She said, "Tom, you are so clumsy. You can't even walk without stumbling. How can you go to the store? You are useless. Get up and go now, or I will be more angry." Tom was afraid of his wife. He got up and limped to the store. He bought some milk and bread and came back. He gave them to his wife and said, "Here, I did what you asked. Can I watch TV now?" His wife took the milk and bread and said, "No, you can't. You have to help me with the baby and the dishes and the laundry. You have to learn to be more helpful and less lazy. Or I will not be your wife anymore." Tom was very unhappy. He wished he had a nicer wife and a better life. He wished he had not stumbled on the toy. He wished he had stayed in bed.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to eat jam on her toast. One day, she found a jar of jam in the kitchen. It was delicate and pretty. Amy's mom saw her holding the jar. "Amy, be careful with that jar of jam," she said. "It is delicate and might break." Amy wanted to know how heavy the jar was. "Mom, can we weigh the jam?" she asked. Her mom agreed and they put the jar on a scale. The jam was not too heavy, but Amy still needed help to carry it. Together, they made toast and put the jam on top. Amy and her mom enjoyed their snack, happy that they had been careful with the delicate jar of jam. And from that day on, Amy always asked for help when carrying something delicate.

Mommy and Daddy went to the store. They wanted to buy a toy. Daddy said they should look for something that was cheap. Mommy and Daddy found a toy. It was a circle. Daddy weighed the toy in his hands. He thought it was not too heavy. Mommy and Daddy bought the toy. They were happy that it was so cheap. When they got home, Mommy and Daddy gave the toy to their son, Sammy. He was very happy. He couldn't wait to play with it. Sammy smiled and gave Mommy and Daddy a big hug.
