Category: Intermediate
Title: The Unique Crocodile and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big river, there lived a unique crocodile. He was very friendly and loved to play with his friends. One day, his best friend, a little bird, lost her favorite toy. She was very sad. The crocodile said, "I will help you search for your toy!" They looked under the rocks, behind the trees, and in the water. They could not find the toy anywhere. The little bird started to cry. Then, the unique crocodile had an idea. "Let's ask the other animals for help!" They asked the fish, the frog, and the turtle. The turtle said, "I saw a toy near the big tree!" The crocodile and the little bird went to the big tree, and there they found the toy! The little bird was so happy, and she hugged her best friend, the crocodile. The crocodile and the little bird learned that working together and asking for help can solve problems. They played happily ever after, always helping each other when they needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a big bull. The bull lived on a farm. The bull was very strong and liked to play in the mud. One day, the farmer wanted the bull to change. He wanted the bull to be clean and obedient. So, the farmer gave the bull a bath and talked to him gently. Soon, the bull became obedient. He listened to the farmer and did what he was told. The farmer and the bull were very happy together. They played and worked on the farm every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once there was a small girl named Katie who loved to create with her toys. She was reliable, always finishing her tasks. One day, her mom asked her to create something new with all of her toys. Katie was so excited! She went right to work, arranging and sorting through her toys to find the best pieces to use. After a while, she finished her task and showed it to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, “That’s so amazing! You were so reliable and did a great job.” Katie was overjoyed. She was proud of her work and grateful for her mom's encouragement. From then on, she was always reliable when creating something.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day and play with his toy model car. He never wanted to help his friends or do any work. One day, Tom's friends asked him to help them build a big sand castle. Tom said no and went to play with his model car instead. While playing, Tom saw a big dog coming towards him. He got scared and tried to run away. But the big dog was too fast, and Tom had to pause to catch his breath. The big dog caught up to Tom and took his model car away. Tom was very sad and wished he had helped his friends build the sand castle instead. If he had helped them, he would still have his model car and be safe with his friends. But now, Tom was alone, and he had lost his favorite toy.

Tim and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were lions and chase each other. They would roar and run and jump on the grass. "Roar! I'm the king of the jungle!" Tim said. "No, I'm the queen of the jungle!" Lily said. They laughed and roared some more. But then they saw a weird thing. It was big and round and shiny. It had wheels and a door and a window. It looked like a car, but not like any car they had seen before. "What is that?" Tim asked. "I don't know. Let's go and see." Lily said. They walked towards the weird thing. They were curious and brave. They wanted to touch it and look inside. But as they got closer, they heard a loud noise. It was not a roar. It was a beep. The weird thing was beeping and flashing. It scared Tim and Lily. "Run!" Tim said. They turned around and ran away. They ran back to their mom and dad, who were sitting on a bench. They hugged them and told them what they saw. "It's okay, sweeties. It was just a spaceship. Someone was playing a prank. It's not real." Mom said. "A spaceship? What is a spaceship?" Lily asked. "It's a thing that goes to the sky and the stars. But it's very rare and weird. You don't have to worry about it." Dad said. Tim and Lily felt better. They still liked to pretend they were lions, but they also liked to pretend they were astronauts. They would roar and beep and fly to the moon. They had fun and they had big hearts.
