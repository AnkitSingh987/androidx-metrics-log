Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Ball

Lily was a little girl who loved playing with her friends. One day, her mom told her that she was going to have a football match against her friends. Lily was very excited! On the day of the match, Lily went to the playground. Her friends were already there and they all said hello. They started playing football, running around and having lots of fun. Lily was about to take a shot when her mom yelled from the sidelines: "Lily! Don't forget to pray!" Lily stopped and put her hands together and said a little prayer: "Dear God, please help us to have a fair and fun match. Amen." After her prayer, Lily took the shot. The ball went right into the goal! Everyone cheered and congratulated her. After the match, Lily's mom took everyone to get some delicious ice-creams. Lily was so happy and excited. She ate her ice-cream with her friends and thought about how much fun she had. It was the best day ever!

One day, a boy named Tim saw an ordinary aeroplane. The aeroplane was big and blue. It could fly high in the sky. Tim wanted to fly in the aeroplane, but he did not know how. He asked his mom, "Can we go on the aeroplane?" His mom said, "We can go, but we need to value our things first. We must pack our bags and be ready." Tim and his mom packed their bags. They went to the big aeroplane. They were so excited. But when they got there, something unexpected happened. The big blue aeroplane turned into a big red car! Tim and his mom were very surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. They said, "Let's go on a ride in the big red car instead!" So they went on a fun ride in the big red car, and they still valued their things and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a flexible snake named Sammy. Sammy liked to play with his friends in the big green park. One day, Sammy met a new friend. This new friend had a big head and was very tall. "Hi, I am Sammy," said the flexible snake. "What is your name?" "Hello, Sammy. My name is Tommy," said the tall friend with the big head. "I am a giraffe. Do you want to play with me?" Sammy was very happy to play with Tommy. They played together all day. They did not recognize that the sun was going down. The sky turned dark, and it was time for them to go home. As they said goodbye, Sammy and Tommy knew they would play together again soon. They had so much fun that day. And now Sammy had a new friend with a big head who was very tall.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lilly. She had a fancy dress with pretty flowers on it. Lilly loved to dance and twirl in her fancy dress. One day, Lilly went to the park with her mom. She saw a big tree and wanted to keep a memory of it. So, Lilly picked up a leaf from the ground and put it in her pocket. When they got home, Lilly showed her mom the leaf. Her mom smiled and said, "That's a nice memory to keep, Lilly." And they put the leaf in a special box so Lilly could always remember her fun day at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to search for his toy car. He looked under his bed, but it was difficult to see. He asked his mom, "Mom, can you help me find my toy car?" His mom came into the room and said, "Let's look together, Tim." They searched all around the room, but the toy car was still missing. Tim felt sad and bit his lip. Finally, Tim's mom found the toy car hiding behind a big pillow. She said, "Here it is, Tim! Your toy car was hiding from us!" Tim felt happy and smiled. He thanked his mom and played with his toy car all day.

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw many leaves on the ground. The leaves were red and yellow and brown. They were pretty. "Let's choose some leaves and make a picture," Lila said. "OK," Ben said. "I like this leaf. It is big and red." He picked up the leaf and showed it to Lila. "I like this leaf. It is small and yellow," Lila said. She picked up the leaf and showed it to Ben. They chose more leaves and put them on a paper. They made a picture of a tree and a sun and a bird. They were happy with their picture. They saw a girl sitting on a bench. She was sad. She had no leaves and no paper. "Who is she?" Ben asked. "I don't know. Maybe she is new here," Lila said. "Let's be kind and share our picture with her," Ben said. "OK," Lila said. They walked to the girl and smiled. "Hi, we are Lila and Ben. We made this picture with leaves. Do you want to have it?" Ben asked. The girl looked up and saw the picture. She saw the tree and the sun and the bird. She liked the picture. "Thank you, Lila and Ben. You are very kind. I like your picture. It is beautiful," the girl said. She took the picture and smiled. "What is your name?" Lila asked. "My name is Mia. I just moved here. I have no friends," Mia said. "You have friends now. We can play together," Lila said. "Really? That would be nice," Mia said. They became friends and played in the park. They had fun. They chose more leaves and made more pictures. They were happy.
