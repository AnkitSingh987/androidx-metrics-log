Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Box

One day, a little girl named Mia found a long, dry string in her toy box. She wanted to play with it, so she took it outside. As she walked, she saw her friend Tim. Tim was sad because he had no one to play with. Mia had an idea. She asked Tim, "Do you want to play with me and my string?" Tim said, "Yes! What can we do with the string?" Mia thought for a moment and said, "We can skip with it!" So, they held the string and began to skip. They skipped and laughed, having lots of fun together. Other kids saw them and wanted to play too. Soon, there was a long line of kids skipping with the string. After playing, they all sat down and shared their snacks. They talked and laughed. Mia felt happy because she made her friend Tim happy by sharing her string. The kids learned that sharing and playing together can make everyone happy. And that was the day they all learned the joy of sharing.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was very lonely. She was looking for a friend, but couldn't find one. One day, the little girl went out for a jog. While she was running, she saw another little girl jogging too. The little girl was very excited. She sort-of knew the other girl from school. She thought to herself, "maybe she can be my friend!". She ran up to the other girl and asked her if she wanted to be her friend. The other girl said yes! The two girls were so happy to finally have each other to play with. They jogged together every day and had lots of fun. Whenever either one of them felt lonely, the other one was always there to cheer them up. They became best friends and always watched out for each other. This was the best thing that had ever happened to the little girl and she was so grateful to have a friend to sort-of rely on.

Once upon a time, there was a weak boy named Tim. He had a big sack and a spoon. Tim loved to play with his spoon and sack all day long. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's sack and asked, "What's in your sack, Tim?" Tim replied, "I have my spoon in my sack. I love my spoon. Do you want to play with my spoon and sack, Sue?" Sue said, "Yes, I want to play with you, Tim." Together, Tim and Sue played with the spoon and sack. They filled the sack with soft dirt and used the spoon to dig. They had lots of fun playing with the spoon and sack. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once, there was a rich man who lived in a big house. He had a garden with many pretty flowers. In the middle of the garden, there was a statue of a happy dog. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim saw the statue while he was walking by the garden. He liked the happy dog statue very much. He wanted to touch it, but the garden had a big gate. Tim could not go inside. The rich man saw Tim and walked to him. "Do you like my happy dog statue?" he asked. Tim said, "Yes, I do. Can I please touch it?" The rich man smiled and said, "Of course! Please come in." Tim went inside the garden and touched the happy dog statue. It felt cold and smooth. He was very happy. The rich man and Tim became good friends, and Tim could visit the happy dog statue whenever he wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named, Lucy. She was always so lonely. She had no one to play with. Every day when she would get dressed in the morning, she would put on her bright red boots. She didn't like having to go outside, when she was all alone. One day, she decided to go outside and try to find someone to play with. She looked near and far, but there was no one around. Suddenly, she heard a voice. It was a little boy. He was sitting all by himself in the park. She smiled and asked, "Do you want to play?" The little boy's eyes lit up and he said, "YES!" The two of them played together all afternoon, until the sun began to set. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye. From then on, Lucy was no longer lonely. Whenever she would get dressed in the morning, she'd make sure to put on her bright red boots so she could go find her new friend.
