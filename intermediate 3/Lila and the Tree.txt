Category: Intermediate
Title: Lila and the Tree

Lila is a girl who likes to measure things. She has a long string and a small ruler. She uses them to measure her toys, her books, and her bed. She likes to know how big and how small they are. One day, she wants to measure her cat, Toto. She thinks Toto is very soft and very cute. She takes her string and her ruler and goes to find Toto. She sees him sleeping on the sofa. She tries to wrap the string around him, but he wakes up and jumps away. "Meow!" Toto says. He does not like to be measured. He runs to the kitchen and hides under the table. Lila follows him. She crawls under the table and tries to catch him. But Toto is too fast and too smart. He runs to the window and jumps out. He lands on the grass and runs to the tree. Lila runs after him. She wants to measure him very much. She reaches the tree and looks up. She sees Toto sitting on a branch. He looks down at her and licks his paw. "Please, Toto, let me measure you," Lila says. "I just want to know how long and how wide you are." Toto shakes his head. He does not want to be measured. He likes to be free and wild. He jumps to another branch and then to another. He goes higher and higher until Lila cannot see him anymore. Lila feels sad. She sits on the grass and hugs her string and her ruler. She wishes Toto would come back and let her measure him. She thinks he is the best cat in the world. But then she hears a noise. She looks up and sees Toto coming down the tree. He jumps to the ground and walks to her. He rubs his head against her leg and purrs. Lila smiles. She is happy to see Toto. She puts her string and her ruler away. She does not need to measure him anymore. She knows he is the perfect size for her. She picks him up and cuddles him. He is rough and soft at the same time. "I love you, Toto," Lila says. "I love you, too, Lila," Toto says. He does not mind being cuddled. He thinks Lila is the best girl in the world.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. One day, he played with his car near a door with a knob. Tim was not careful and the car went under the door. "Oh no!" Tim said. "I miss my car. How do I get it back?" He tried to open the door, but the knob was too high for him. He felt stupid for losing his car. Tim asked his big sister, Lily, for help. "Lily, can you help me get my car? It's under the door and I can't reach the knob." Lily smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you." She opened the door with the knob and got the car for Tim. Tim was happy to have his car back and thanked his sister. From that day on, Tim was more careful when he played with his car. He didn't want to feel stupid again and miss his car. And he always knew that his sister Lily would help him when he needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty crystal. It was shaped like a big diamond and sparkled in the sun. The crystal belonged to a little girl named Sally. Every day, Sally played with her crystal outside in the garden. But one day, a naughty dog came out and started barking at Sally. He wanted the crystal and tried to grab it, but Sally was too quick. She picked the crystal up and ran away. The naughty dog chased after Sally, but Sally was very obedient. As soon as her mom told her to drop the crystal, she did. The dog picked it up and ran away. Sally was very sad and started to cry. She missed her pretty crystal so much! Luckily, her mom was very kind and bought Sally a new crystal. Sally was so happy and she promised to obey her mom from now on.
