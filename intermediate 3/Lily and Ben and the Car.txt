Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Car

Lily and Ben like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. Some cars have motors that make them go fast. Some cars are flexible and can bend and twist. One day, Lily and Ben find a big box in the garage. They open the box and see a big car inside. It is red and shiny and has a motor. Lily and Ben are very happy. They want to play with the big car. They push the big car out of the box and onto the floor. They try to turn on the motor, but nothing happens. They look at the big car and see that it has no wheels. It only has four metal sticks. Lily and Ben are sad. How can they play with the big car without wheels? Lily has an idea. She runs to her room and gets her toy wheels. She has four wheels that are flexible and can fit on the metal sticks. She brings them to Ben and says, "Let's use these wheels. They are flexible and can support the big car." Ben likes the idea. He helps Lily put the wheels on the metal sticks. They push the big car again and turn on the motor. The big car starts to move. It makes a loud noise and goes fast. Lily and Ben laugh and cheer. They play with the big car all day. They are very happy. They have made the big car work with their flexible wheels. They have supported each other with their ideas. They are good friends.

One day, a small bird was zipping through the sky in search of her friends. She sang her song while flying, and when she looked down, she saw a whistle lying in the grass. Curious, she swooped down and picked it up in her beak, and then flew up to a branch in a tree. The bird clutched the whistle in her talons and observed it. It looked rather harmless, and she wondered what it could be for. Suddenly, she heard another bird call from a nearby tree. She looked over and saw one of her friends, and he said, "Let's see what happens when you blow the whistle!" Excited, the bird hopped down from the branch and put the whistle to her beak. She puffed her tiny chest and then blew into the whistle, creating a gentle sound that echoed in the air. From behind her, the bird heard a reply. It was another call from another bird, and soon one by one, all of her friends showed up! They all gathered around, curious to hear the gentle sound again. The bird happily obliged, and each time she blew the whistle, more and more birds came to join the joyful reunion. The harmless whistle had brought them all together!

One day, a boy named Tim had a birthday. He was very happy. He wanted to have a big party with his friends. He told his mom, "I want a big party with cake and games!" On the day of the party, it was very wet outside. It rained and rained. Tim was sad because he wanted to play outside with his friends. But then, his mom said, "Don't worry, we can play inside!" During the party, Tim's dad played a game with them. He tried to make a funny face, but he fell down. Everyone laughed. Tim's dad felt embarrass. He said, "Oops! I am okay!" Then, something unexpected happened. The door opened and a big, funny clown walked in. All the kids were surprised and happy. The clown made them laugh and forget about the wet day. Tim had the best birthday ever.

Once upon a time, there was a lively bird named Bob. Bob lived in a big tree in a beautiful forest. He loved to sing and play all day. Bob was very wise and always tried to protect his friends in the forest. One day, a small mouse named Tim came to Bob for help. "Bob, please help me. A big cat is trying to catch me!" Tim said. Bob knew that he had to protect his friend Tim. He thought about his wisdom and came up with a good plan. Bob told Tim, "Don't worry, Tim. I will protect you. Let's hide you in a safe place." Bob found a small hole in the tree for Tim to hide in. Then, they waited for the big cat to come. When the big cat came near the tree, Bob started to sing loudly. The big cat got scared and ran away. Bob and Tim were safe, and they played happily together in the forest.
