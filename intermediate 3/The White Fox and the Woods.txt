Category: Intermediate
Title: The White Fox and the Woods

One day, a white fox went for a walk in the woods. The sun was warm and the sky was blue. The fox saw a big tree with a long branch. The fox said, "I want to play on that branch." So, the fox jumped up and hung on the branch. The branch started to bend and make a sound. The fox thought, "That sound is not good." Then, the branch went "snap!" The white fox fell down, but he was okay. He said, "Next time, I will be more careful." And the fox walked away, happy to play another day.

One day, a little girl named Amy wanted to go outside and play with her toys. She had a lot of fun stuff to play with like a ball, a doll, and a toy car. But her mom said, "Amy, we have to delay playtime. We need to clean your room first." Amy felt frustrated because she really wanted to play. She looked at her room and saw all the stuff on the floor. She knew she had to clean it up before she could go outside. So, Amy started to put her toys away, one by one. After a while, Amy's mom came back and saw her daughter cleaning her room. She said, "Good job, Amy! You can go outside and play now." Amy was happy that she could finally play with her toys. She ran outside and had lots of fun with her friends.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in a big yard. The cat saw a tree with a bird on it. The cat wanted to catch the bird. The dog saw the cat and wanted to help. The cat tried to climb the tree, but it was too hard. The dog had an idea. The dog found some butter in the yard. The dog put the butter on the tree. The cat tried to climb again, but it was too slippery. The dog felt sad and shouted loud, "I am sorry, cat!" The cat shouted back, "It's okay, dog!" They both laughed and played together in the yard. The bird flew away, and they were still happy friends.

Once upon a time, in a big green garden, there lived a tiny ant. The ant had lots of friends and they loved to play together. They would run, jump, and climb all day long. The sun was always shining and the garden was their favorite place to be. One day, the tiny ant saw a huge flower. It was bigger than any flower he had ever seen. The ant was excited and wanted to show his friends the huge flower. They all gathered around it and looked up at the big, colorful petals. Just then, a big cloud appeared in the sky and it started to rain. The raindrops fell on the huge flower and made it spray water all around. The tiny ant and his friends laughed and danced in the water. They had so much fun playing in the spray from the huge flower. And from that day on, the tiny ant and his friends loved the rain just as much as the sunshine.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Amy liked to wander around her house. One day, she found a big closet in her room. She had never seen it before. Amy was very excited to see what was inside. Amy opened the closet and saw many pretty dresses, big hats, and funny shoes. She wanted to play dress-up. Amy put on a long dress, a big hat, and some funny shoes. She looked in the mirror and laughed. She felt a little embarrassed, but it was fun. Amy showed her mom and dad her new look. They laughed and clapped. They were so happy to see her having fun. They all played dress-up together and had a great time. At the end of the day, Amy was tired but very happy. She knew she would always remember the fun day when she wandered into the big closet.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia went to the woods to play. In the woods, she saw a weird bird. The bird was an owl. Mia wanted to introduce the owl to her friends. She took the owl to her friends. Her friends were scared of the weird owl. They ran away. Mia was sad. She let the owl go back to the woods. Mia went home alone. She did not have her friends anymore. This was a bad ending.
