Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Car

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in the park. It was a warm and sunny spring day. Tim was very happy because he loved to play outside. He was running, jumping, and laughing with his friends. Tim had a small toy car that he liked to play with. But Tim was sometimes impatient and didn't like to wait for things. While playing with his friends, he put the toy car down and went to play on the swings. After a while, he forgot about the toy car. When it was time to go home, Tim looked for his toy car but he couldn't find it. He had lost his toy car. Tim felt sad and cried a little. His mom hugged him and said they would look for the toy car together. They walked around the park and soon found the toy car near the swings. Tim was happy again and learned to be more careful with his toys.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient tree in a big park. It was very old and wise. One day, a little girl named Mia found a pretty mug under the tree. The mug was blue and shiny. Mia picked up the mug and said, "Wow, this is a nice mug! I want to show it to my friends." But when she looked around, she saw a tiny squirrel. The squirrel looked at Mia and said, "Please, put the mug back. It is magic and can vanish things!" Mia didn't listen to the squirrel. She took the mug to her friends. They all played with the mug, but then, one of them dropped it. The mug broke into pieces, and suddenly, all of Mia's friends vanished. Mia was very sad and cried, "I should have listened to the squirrel!" She went back to the ancient tree and asked the squirrel for help. But the squirrel said, "I'm sorry, I cannot help you. The magic is gone." So Mia learned to listen to others, but she never saw her friends again. The ancient tree and the broken mug stayed in the park, reminding her of the day when her friends vanished.

Once there was a curious little boy. He liked to explore and learn about the world around him. One day he was in his backyard and he saw a lawn. He thought it looked fun and he wanted to see what it felt like. So he started running as fast as he could across the lawn. But then he slipped and fell down. He got a big scrape and started to cry. He was glad when his mom came to pick him up and take him inside the house. She helped him feel better and cleaned his scrape with a cool cloth. She then said to the boy, "Next time you should be more careful when you play. You should think before you act, so you don't regret it later." The little boy listened to his mom and was more careful after that. He found other fun ways to explore the world around him that were safe and didn't make him feel regret.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. He saw a dolphin in the water. The dolphin was very close to the shore. Tim wanted to play with the dolphin, so he ran into the water. The dolphin said, "Hi Tim! Do you want to climb on my back for a ride?" Tim was very happy and climbed on the dolphin's back. They swam together in the water and had lots of fun. But then, Tim fell off the dolphin's back. He felt sad and ashamed. The dolphin said, "Don't be sad, Tim. We all fall sometimes. Let's try again." Tim climbed back on the dolphin's back and they continued to play. They both had a great day at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a girl who loved to spend time with her family. They often went to the park together to play on the swings and have a picnic. One day, the girl, her mum, dad and brother all went to the park and spent a lot of time playing together. Soon, they all got thirsty and found a wide pond. They saw a sign next to it that said 'No Drinking'! The family were sad because they were so thirsty. The girl's dad opened his bag and pulled out four large bottles of water. He smiled and said, "Let's all have a drink!" The family were so happy and opened the bottles. They all drank the water and felt refreshed. After that, they spent the rest of the day playing together in the park. The end.
