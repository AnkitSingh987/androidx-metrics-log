Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Tube

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy tube that he loved to play with all day. He would roll it, throw it, and even talk to it. Tom thought his tube was the best toy ever. One day, a naughty boy named Sam saw Tom's tube. Sam wanted the tube for himself, so he decided to steal it. When Tom was not looking, Sam took the tube and ran away. Tom looked around and said, "Where is my tube?" Tom saw Sam with his tube and ran to get it back. Sam laughed and said, "This tube is mine now!" Tom was sad and told Sam, "That's not nice! You should not steal!" Sam felt bad and gave the tube back to Tom. Tom looked at the tube and saw that it was dirty and disgusting. He said, "Eww! My tube is yucky now!" Tom and Sam decided to clean the tube together. They washed it until it was shiny and clean. After that, they both played with the tube and became friends. They learned that stealing is wrong and sharing is better.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin was enthusiastic and loved to dare himself to do new things. One day, he had an idea. He wanted to swim close to the shore where the big rocks were. Fin swam closer and closer to the shore. He saw the big rocks and felt very brave. The sun was shining, and the water was warm. Fin dared himself to touch the big rocks with his tail. Fin touched the big rocks and felt proud. He swam back to his friends and told them about his adventure. They all wanted to dare themselves too. Together, they swam to the shore and had a fun day near the big rocks.

One day, a little boy named Tim was in his house. He saw a big armchair in the living room. Tim felt very excite to sit on it. He called his friend, Sam, to come over. "Sam, come see the big armchair!" Tim said. Sam came and they both looked at the chair. They wanted to play on it. They took turns sitting in the armchair. It was so big and fun! But then, they both tried to sit on it at the same time. The chair broke and they fell down. They were both sad and cried. The big armchair was not fun anymore.

One day, a big pig and a little pig went to play outside. They saw a big mud puddle. The big pig said, "Let's get in the mud!" The little pig said, "No, I want to be tidy." The big pig jumped in the mud and got very dirty. The little pig went to find a toy. He saw a girl with a big ball. The little pig asked, "Can you give me the ball?" The girl said, "Yes, you can have it." The little pig took the ball back to the big pig. He said, "Look, I have a ball!" The big pig was so excited that he jumped out of the mud. They played with the ball and had lots of fun. But then, the ball went into the mud! The little pig was sad, but the big pig said, "Don't worry, I will get it!" The big pig got the ball, and they both became very dirty. They laughed and played in the mud together.

Once there was a man who was very enthusiastic to explore nature. He decided to go on an adventure and named it 'The Great Nature Hunt'. He set off and soon climbed a tall mountain. But he was not careful and slipped and fell. The fall hurt him badly and he couldn't get up. The moral of this story is that when we want to explore something new, it is important to be very careful. We mustn't be too enthusiastic or it can lead to a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was an adventurous monkey named Bobo. Bobo loved to eat bananas. One day, he saw a big banana on a high tree. He climbed the tree and picked the banana. Bobo was very happy. Bobo wanted to share the banana with his friends. He saw a fire near the tree. He thought, "I will burn the banana a little to make it warm and tasty." But Bobo did not know how to use fire. When Bobo put the banana near the fire, it burned too much. The banana turned black and smelled bad. Bobo was sad. He thought his friends would not like the burned banana. But something unexpected happened! Bobo's friends came and saw the burned banana. They thought it looked fun and different. They all tried a piece of the burned banana and liked it! Bobo was happy that his friends enjoyed the new banana treat. They all laughed and played together, and Bobo learned that sometimes, a surprise can be a good thing.
