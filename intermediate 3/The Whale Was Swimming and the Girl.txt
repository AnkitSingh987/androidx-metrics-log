Category: Intermediate
Title: The Whale Was Swimming and the Girl

One day, a big whale was swimming in the sea. He saw a little girl standing near the water. The girl looked sad. The whale wanted to help her. "Hello, little girl," said the whale. "Why are you sad?" "I lost my toy," said the girl. "It is in the water, and I can't get it." The whale wanted to make the girl glad. He swam away and looked for the toy. The girl saw the whale swim away and hoped he would find her toy. After a while, the whale came back. He had the toy in his mouth. The girl was so glad! She said, "Thank you, Mr. Whale!" The whale was happy he could help the girl. They both had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a big engine named Tom. He lived in a high place with many trees. Tom liked to listen to the birds sing every day. One day, Tom heard a little bird say, "I know a secret. A big surprise is coming soon." Tom was very excited. He told all his friends about the big surprise. They all listened carefully to the little bird. The bird said, "When the sun goes down, look up at the sky. You will see something very special." The sun went down, and all the animals looked up at the sky. They saw something they had never seen before. The moon was big and bright, and it looked like a big, round ball. The animals were so happy to see the moon. They thanked the little bird for the wonderful surprise.

Lily liked to play with her dolls in her room. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Anna. Anna had long hair, a pretty dress and an expensive necklace. Lily always put the necklace on Anna when she played with her. One day, Lily was playing with Anna on her bed. She lay Anna on the pillow and pretended to brush her hair. She was about to put the necklace on Anna, when she saw something on the bed. It was a spider. A big, black spider with eight legs. Lily was scared of spiders. She screamed and ran away from the bed. "Mommy, mommy, there is a spider on my bed!" Lily cried. Mommy heard Lily and came to her room. She saw the spider on the bed and picked it up with a tissue. She threw the spider out of the window. "Don't worry, Lily, the spider is gone now. It won't hurt you or your dolls," Mommy said. Lily hugged Mommy and thanked her. She went back to her bed and looked for Anna. She saw Anna on the pillow, but the necklace was missing. She searched the bed, but she could not find the necklace. "Mommy, where is Anna's necklace? It was expensive and shiny. I liked it very much," Lily said. Mommy looked at the bed and saw a small hole in the sheet. She lifted the sheet and saw the necklace under the bed. The spider had taken the necklace and dragged it under the bed. "Look, Lily, I found the necklace. The spider must have liked it too. It was shiny and sparkly. But it is yours, not the spider's. Here, you can have it back," Mommy said. She gave the necklace to Lily and helped her put it on Anna. Lily smiled and hugged Anna. "Thank you, Mommy. You are the best. I love you and Anna and the necklace. But I don't love spiders. They are scary and naughty. They can't have my things," Lily said. Mommy laughed and kissed Lily. "You are welcome, Lily. I love you too. And you are right, spiders can't have your things. But they are not so bad. They help us by eating bugs and making webs. Maybe we can learn more about them someday. But for now, let's play with your dolls," Mommy said. They lay on the bed and played with the dolls. Lily was happy and forgot about the spider. She had fun with Mommy and Anna and the necklace. She hoped the spider would not come back.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a red scooter. He loved to ride it up and down the street. One day, while he was playing, a big boy named Max came by. Max liked to tease Tim. He said, "Your scooter is not cool." Tim felt sad. He went home and told his mom about Max. His mom gave him a sour apple to eat. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. Just be nice to Max and maybe he will stop teasing you." The next day, Tim saw Max again. Tim said, "Hi, Max! Do you want to try my scooter?" Max looked surprised. He tried the scooter and had fun. Max stopped teasing Tim, and they became friends.
