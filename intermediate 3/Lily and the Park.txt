Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Park

Once upon a time, in a modest little house, there lived a kind mom and her little girl, Lily. They loved to play, talk, and laugh together. Their house was always full of peace and joy. One sunny day, they went for a walk in the park. Lily saw a big tree with many branches. She had an idea! "Mom, let's play snap the branch!" said Lily. "Okay, but be careful," said her mom. Lily and her mom took turns trying to snap the small branches. They were having so much fun! At the end of the day, they went back to their modest little house, happy and tired. The peace and joy in their home grew even more, as they shared their fun day together.

---

One day, a cat named Tom found a key. Tom was a good cat. He wanted to unlock something. He looked around and saw a big box. Tom thought, "Maybe the key can unlock the box!" Tom put the key in the box and turned it. The box unlocked! Inside the box, Tom found a suit. The suit was big and red. Tom put on the suit and looked at himself. He felt very silly. Tom walked around the garden. His friend, a dog named Max, saw him. Max said, "Tom, that suit looks bad on you!" Tom laughed and said, "I know, but it is fun to wear!" They both laughed and played together all day.

---

One day, a big dog and a small cat had a race. They wanted to see who was faster. They were both very excited. The dog's name was Max and the cat's name was Luna. They had a big, yummy steak waiting for the winner. Max and Luna started the race. They ran very fast. Max was in front, but Luna was not far behind. They raced through the park and past the big tree. They were both very happy and having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, frightened bird flew down from the tree. The bird saw the steak and wanted it too. The bird picked up the steak and flew away with it. Max and Luna stopped running. They were both very surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. They did not care about the race anymore. They had fun and made a new friend, the big bird. Now, they all shared the yummy steak together.

---

Once there was a dancer named Lily. She loved to dance all day. One day, she saw a big tree and wanted to dance under it. She started to dance and sing, "I love to dance, dance, dance!" A little bird in the tree saw Lily dance. The bird was envious. It wanted to dance too. The bird said, "I want to dance like you, Lily! Can you teach me?" Lily said, "Of course! Let's dance together!" They danced and danced under the tree. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to grow bigger and bigger! Soon, the bird was as big as Lily. Now, they could dance together even better. They danced and laughed, happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big house. The house had many stairs. One day, it was very cold, and the stairs became icy. A little girl named Lucy lived in the house. She wanted to go down the stairs to play with her toys. But she saw the icy stairs and knew she had to be careful. Lucy thought of a fun game to include her friends. Lucy called her friends, Tom and Sue, to come and play. They all put on their warm clothes and went to the icy stairs. They took turns sliding down the stairs, laughing and having fun. They played all day and included everyone. The icy stairs made their day very special.

---

Once there was a boy named Nick. Nick was 3 years old. One day, Nick and his dad went to the park. It was a cool day and the park was really nice. Nick and his dad were playing on the swings. Nick loved the swings and he used them a lot. Nick's dad said, "Nick do you want to go under the slide?" Nick laughed and said, "Yes Dad, that sounds cool!" So they went under the slide and Nick had a lot of fun. He laughed and he used his imagination to pretend he was on a big adventure. When Nick was finished, he and his dad went home. Nick had a great day with his dad and he was happy.
