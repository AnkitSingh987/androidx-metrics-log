Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Bench

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their toy dinosaurs. They liked to pretend that they were big and scary like the dinosaurs and roar at each other. Sometimes they would hide behind the trees and bushes and jump out to surprise each other. One day, they saw a new boy sitting on a bench with his mom. He had a big red hat and a small blue car. Tom and Lily thought he looked funny and wanted to make him laugh. They decided to roar at him and see what he would do. They crept up behind the bench and waited for the right moment. Then they both jumped out and roared as loud as they could. "ROAR! ROAR! ROAR!" they shouted. The new boy was very scared. He dropped his car and started to cry. His mom hugged him and tried to calm him down. She looked at Tom and Lily with a sad and angry face. Tom and Lily felt ashamed. They did not mean to make the new boy cry. They thought he would laugh and play with them. They realized that they had been very rude and mean. They walked up to the bench and said sorry to the new boy and his mom. They picked up his car and gave it back to him. They asked him his name and if he wanted to play with them. The new boy stopped crying and looked at them. He saw that they were not scary, but friendly. He smiled and said his name was Ben. He nodded and said he wanted to play with them. Tom and Lily smiled back and winked at him. They invited him to join their dinosaur game. They showed him their toy dinosaurs and taught him how to roar. They also taught him how to hide and jump and laugh. Ben had fun playing with Tom and Lily. He forgot about his fear and his tears. He made new friends and learned a new game. Tom and Lily learned a lesson. They learned that roaring can be fun, but not when it hurts someone. They learned that being kind and polite is better than being rude and mean. They learned that making friends is more fun than making fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because her room was very messy. She wanted to play with her toys, but she could not find them in all the mess. One day, her friend Tom came over to play. Tom said, "Lily, why are you sad?" Lily told him about her messy room. Tom had an idea. "Let's organize your room together! It will be fun, and then you can find your toys!" Lily and Tom worked together to clean up the room. They found a spark in the corner, and it made them laugh. They put all the toys in their right places. Soon, the room was clean and organized. Lily was not sad anymore. She said, "Thank you, Tom, for helping me organize my room! Now we can play with my toys!" Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily! Let's have fun!" And so, Lily and Tom played happily in the clean and organized room. They had lots of fun, and Lily was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a very stubborn girl. She was so stubborn that if anyone ever said something she didn't like, she would just walk away. She never wanted to listen. One day, she was standing in front of a big mirror. She wanted to know what she looked like, but she had never seen a mirror before. She said, "Who is that girl in the mirror?" But no matter what the girl said to the mirror, it never answered. She was getting more and more frustrated. Suddenly, her mom came in the room. She said, "That's you sweetie! That's what you look like. You can know it by looking in the mirror." The girl was so happy. She couldn't believe it. From that day on, she wasn't so stubborn anymore. She started to listen to everyone and see what they had to say.
