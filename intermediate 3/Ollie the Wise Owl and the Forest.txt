Category: Intermediate
Title: Ollie the Wise Owl and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a wise old owl named Ollie. Ollie loved to help his friends. He would remind them to eat, play, and sleep. He was very good at helping. One day, Ollie found a menu. It was a list of yummy foods. He wanted to share it with his friends, so he planned a big party. Ollie invited all his friends - the birds, the bugs, and the animals. They were all very excited. At the party, Ollie showed everyone the menu. But, there was a problem. The menu was not for food. It was for a game! Ollie and his friends laughed and laughed. They played the game all day long and had so much fun. In the end, the party was even better than they thought it would be.

One day, a man with a big mustache came to the park. He looked different from the other people. He had a big hat and a long coat. He walked up to a little boy playing with his toy car. "Hi," said the man with the mustache. "Do you want to play with me?" The little boy looked at the man and then shrugged. He didn't know what to say. So, they started to play together. As they played, the man's big hat fell off. Under the hat, there was a big, funny wig! The little boy laughed and laughed. The man with the mustache laughed too. "I like to dress up and make people smile," he said. The little boy and the man with the mustache had a great time playing and laughing together.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a bad dream. He called it a "nightmare." In the nightmare, there was a big monster with a serious face. The monster was not happy and it scared Tim. Tim woke up and told his mom about the nightmare. "Mom, I had a nightmare. A big monster with a serious face was in it!" His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, it was just a dream." That night, Tim went to bed and saw the monster again. But this time, Tim decided to look closer. He saw that the monster was not serious at all. The monster was just sad because it had no friends. Tim said, "Do you want to be my friend?" The monster smiled and said, "Yes, please!" And they became friends. From that day on, Tim and the monster played together in his dreams. They had lots of fun, and the nightmares went away. Tim learned that sometimes, things that seem scary just need a closer look and a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant sun. It was very bright and made everyone happy. The sun lived in the sky, on one side of the world. One day, the sun wanted to sort its toys. It had many toys, like balls and cars. The sun liked to play with them, but they were all mixed up. So, the sun started to sort them by color and size. While the sun was sorting its toys, it forgot to shine. The world became dark and cold. People were sad and missed the sun's light. They did not know what to do. The sun was so busy sorting its toys, that it did not notice the people were sad. It kept sorting and sorting. The world stayed dark, and the people were not happy anymore. In the end, the sun never stopped sorting its toys. The world stayed dark, and the people were very sad. They wished the brilliant sun would come back and make them happy again. But it never did.

Once upon a time, there was a big carpet. It was a magic carpet. It could fly high in the sky. A little girl named Lily loved the carpet. She was eager to fly on it every day. One day, Lily told her friend Tom about the magic carpet. Tom did not believe her. He said, "No way! Carpets can't fly." Lily said, "Come with me. I will show you." Tom went with Lily to see the carpet. When they saw the carpet, Tom was amazed. The carpet started to fly with Lily and Tom on it. They laughed and had fun in the sky. Tom said, "I believe you now, Lily. This carpet is magic!" They flew on the magic carpet every day and had lots of fun together.
