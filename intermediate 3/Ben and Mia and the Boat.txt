Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Mia and the Boat

Ben and Mia were twins who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big pond with many ducks and boats. They wanted to go on a boat and feed the ducks. They asked their mom if they could. "OK, but only for a little while. And be careful, the boats are cheap and old. They might break or leak," their mom said. Ben and Mia ran to the boat man and gave him some coins. He gave them a row boat and two paddles. They got in the boat and started to row. They had fun splashing the water and throwing bread to the ducks. But soon, they felt the boat was wet and heavy. They looked down and saw water coming in through a hole. They got scared and tried to row back to the shore. But the boat was too slow and the water was too high. "Help! Help!" they shouted. "Our boat is sinking!" Their mom heard them and ran to the pond. She saw them in the middle of the water, holding on to the boat. She called the boat man and asked him to help. But he was angry and said it was their fault for breaking his cheap boat. He said he would not help them unless they paid him more coins. Their mom did not have more coins. She did not know what to do. She watched as the boat sank lower and lower, and Ben and Mia cried louder and louder. She wished she had never let them go on the boat. She wished she had listened to her gut feeling that something bad would happen. She wished she could save them, but it was too late.

Once upon a time, there was a gray monkey. He lived in the treetops, climbing from tree to tree. One day, he wanted to find food, so he started to swing through the branches. Suddenly, he noticed a big, shiny red fruit. He grabbed it with a smile! But then the monkey heard a loud noise. It was thunder! Instantly, the skies darkened and a big lightning bolt struck the tree near him. The poor little monkey was scared, so he quickly grabbed the red fruit and ran. The lightning made the monkey run so fast! But then it started to rain. The ground was slippery, making it hard for the monkey to run away. Thankfully, he saw a cave close by. He ran inside and smiled, safe from the storm. The gray monkey stayed inside the cave until the storm was over. Then he ventured out and started his journey back home. He was happy to have the juicy red fruit, but most of all, he was glad to be safe after the strike of lightning!

Once upon a time, there was a little fairy named Lily. Lily had a magic wand that could increase things. One day, Lily was very hungry. She wanted to eat a big apple. Lily saw a small apple on a tree. She said, "I will use my wand to make the apple big!" She waved her wand at the apple. The apple got bigger and bigger. Lily was so happy. Lily's friend, Tom the squirrel, saw the big apple. He was hungry too. Tom asked, "Can I have some apple, please?" Lily said, "Yes, we can share!" They ate the big apple together and were not hungry anymore.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a big lion. The lion liked to roar very loud. Every time he roared, all the animals would run and hide. The lion was glad because he felt strong and brave. One day, the lion saw a big shadow on the ground. He thought it was another big lion. He roared at the shadow, but the shadow did not move. The lion was scared. He thought the shadow would roar back, but it did not. The lion felt small and not brave. A little mouse saw the lion and the shadow. The mouse knew the shadow was just a tree. The mouse told the lion, "Do not be scared. The shadow is not another lion. It is just a tree." The lion was happy to know the truth. He thanked the mouse and they became friends. The lion learned that even the small and weak can be wise and helpful.
