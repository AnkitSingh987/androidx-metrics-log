Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Basket

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. One sunny day, Lily went to the park to play. She had a big, fine basket with her. Inside the basket, she had toys and yummy snacks. While playing, Lily saw a new friend. His name was Max. Lily wanted to introduce Max to her basket of fun. She said, "Hi Max, I am Lily. This is my basket. It has toys and snacks. Do you want to play with me?" Max was very happy. He said, "Yes, Lily! I want to play with you and your fine basket." So, Lily and Max played all day long. They shared the toys and ate the yummy snacks from the basket. They had a great time at the park. And that is how Lily and Max became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a big room with lots of toys. He also had a shelf in his room where he kept his favorite books. One day, Tim's mom saw that the shelf was very messy. She suggested, "Tim, let's clean the shelf and make it look nice." Tim agreed and they started to clean the shelf together. While they were cleaning, Tim found a small toy. It was a deaf dog named Spot. Spot could not hear anything, but he was still a good friend to Tim. They played together and had a lot of fun. And from that day on, Tim always kept his shelf clean and neat.

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. On one side of the hill, there was a little boy named Tim. On the other side, there was a little girl named Sue. They both liked to play on the hill, but they did not know each other. One day, Tim saw that the hill had split. There was a dangerous hole in the middle of the hill. Tim was scared and said, "Oh no! The hill has a big hole! I must be careful when I play." Sue also saw the hole and said, "This hole is dangerous! I will tell my friends to be careful when they play on the hill." Tim and Sue met at the hole and became friends. They played together on the hill, but always stayed away from the dangerous hole. They had lots of fun and were always safe.

One day, a little girl named Amy had a new toy. It was a fun tape with many colors. She was very happy. Amy wanted to show her friend, Tim, her new toy. "Look, Tim! I have a new tape," she said. Tim saw the tape and nodded. "Wow, Amy! That is a nice tape," Tim said. They played with the tape and made fun shapes. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the tape away. Amy and Tim were sad. They looked for the tape, but could not find it. A nice bird saw the tape and picked it up. The bird flew to Amy and Tim. "Is this your tape?" the bird asked. Amy and Tim nodded, "Yes, it is!" They were very happy to have their tape back. The bird helped them make new shapes with the tape.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. Mia loved to eat pasta. It was her favorite food. One day, Mia decided she wanted to order pasta from the big high chair at the table. Mia climbed up into the high chair and said, "Mommy, I want to order pasta for dinner tonight, please!" Mommy smiled and said, "Okay, Mia. We will have pasta for dinner tonight." That night, Mia sat in her high chair, and her mommy brought a big plate of pasta to the table. Mia was so happy. She ate all of her pasta and said, "Thank you, Mommy, for the yummy pasta!" Mommy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Mia. I'm glad you enjoyed it." From that day on, Mia always ordered pasta from her high chair. It was her special place to eat her favorite food. And every time she ate pasta, she felt very happy and loved.
