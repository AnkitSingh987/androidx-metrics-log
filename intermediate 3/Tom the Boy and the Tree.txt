Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Tree

One day, a little boy named Tom found a small thing on the ground. It was dry and brown. He did not know what the thing was, so he took it to his friend, Sue. "Look at this thing I found!" Tom said to Sue. "Do you know what it is?" Sue looked at the thing and said, "I don't know. Let's ask Mom." When they asked Mom, she told them it was a seed. "If you plant it in the ground and give it water, it will grow into a big tree," Mom said. Tom and Sue were excited to plant the seed. But they started to fight over who would get to plant it. Sue wanted to plant it in her garden, but Tom wanted to plant it in his. They both started to cry. Mom saw them crying and said, "Why don't you plant the seed together in the middle of both your gardens? That way, you can both watch it grow." Tom and Sue liked that idea. They stopped crying, planted the seed, and watched it grow into a big, beautiful tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was hungry and wanted a sandwich. He asked his mom for a sandwich. His mom made him a yummy sandwich with bread, cheese, and ham. Tim's sister, Sue, saw the sandwich and wanted it too. Tim and Sue started to quarrel. They both wanted the sandwich and did not want to share. Their mom felt helpless, not knowing how to make them stop fighting. As they quarreled, a big bird flew by and saw the sandwich. The bird swooped down and took the sandwich away. Tim and Sue were sad, and they had no sandwich to eat. They learned that fighting can lead to a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion was very adorable. He lived in a big forest with many trees. All the animals in the forest liked the lion. They would play and have fun together. One day, the lion saw something strange. He could not believe his eyes. It was a big box. The lion and his friends went to look at the box. They were very curious. They wanted to know what was inside. The lion and his friends opened the box. They were very surprised. Inside the box, there was a tiny lion. The tiny lion was also adorable. The big lion and the tiny lion became best friends. They played and had fun together with all the other animals in the forest. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk down the road. He saw a ball on the ground. Tim thought it was amazing. He picked up the ball and wanted to throw it. Tim saw his friend, Sue, on the other side of the road. He yelled, "Sue, look at this amazing ball! I will throw it to you!" Sue got ready to catch the ball. Tim threw the ball as hard as he could. But, something unexpected happened! A big bird flew down from the sky and caught the ball in its beak. The bird flew away with the amazing ball. Tim and Sue were very surprised. They laughed and said, "Wow, that bird really liked the amazing ball!"

Once upon a time, there was a prince who lived in a big castle. Every day, the prince would wake up and put on his ordinary clothes. He loved to play with his friends in the forest. One day, the prince found a secret path in the forest. He told his friends, "I will go down this path tomorrow." His friends were very excited and couldn't wait to see what the prince would find. The next day, the prince woke up early and went down the secret path. After a long walk, he found a beautiful garden with a big tree. He climbed the tree and saw a shiny key hanging from a branch. He took the key and went back to the castle. The prince and his friends used the key to open a hidden door in the castle, and inside they found a room full of toys and games. They played all day and had so much fun. From that day on, the prince and his friends had a secret place to play and have adventures together.
