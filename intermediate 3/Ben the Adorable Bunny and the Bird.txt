Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Adorable Bunny and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little adorable bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop and play in the big green field. The field was full of peace and quiet. Ben had many friends like birds, bugs, and other animals. One sunny day, Ben was hopping and playing with his friends. A new friend, a big bird, flew to the field. The big bird seemed nice and said, "Hello, I am Bill. Can I play with you?" Ben and his friends said, "Yes, come play with us!" While they were playing, the sky got dark and it started to rain. All the friends ran to find a dry place. Ben and Bill found a big tree to hide under. But then, the rain stopped and a big rainbow appeared in the sky. Bill said, "I can fly up and touch the rainbow!" Ben and his friends were amazed. They all became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play in her garden. One day, she saw a big squash growing. She was very happy. Lucy wanted to show her friends the big squash. She asked her friends to come to her garden. When they came, she said, "Welcome to my garden!" Her friends saw the squash and were amazed. Lucy's mom cooked the squash for dinner. They all sat down to eat. The squash was very yummy. Lucy and her friends smiled and laughed. They had a great time eating the yummy squash.

Once upon a time, there was a woman. She was very dirty because she worked in the mud all day. She had a big, friendly dog. The dog was also dirty from playing in the mud with the woman. One day, the woman and the dog went for a walk. They looked at the sky and saw pretty birds. They looked at the trees and saw green leaves. They looked at the ground and saw many flowers. They liked to look at all the things around them. When they went back home, the woman and the dog were very dirty. They looked at each other and laughed. They took a bath together and became clean. Now, they were happy and clean, ready to play in the mud again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy had a big, red folder. She loved to put her drawings in it. One day, she noticed her folder was gone. She looked all around her room, but she could not find it. Lucy was very sad. Lucy went to her mom and said, "Mom, I can't find my folder. Can you help me?" Her mom was busy, but she said, "Lucy, I saw your dog, Max, with something red in his mouth. Maybe he took your folder." Lucy went outside to look for Max. She saw him laying under a tree. He had her folder! But it was not the same. It was all wet and full of holes. Lucy was sad, but then she noticed something inside the folder. There was a new drawing in the folder. It was a picture of her and Max. Lucy's deaf friend, Sam, had drawn it for her. Sam had given the drawing to Max to bring to Lucy, but Max had played with the folder instead. Lucy laughed and hugged Max. She was happy to have a new drawing for her folder.

Once upon a time, there was a generous girl named Mia. She loved to help everyone in her town. One day, a boy named Tim came to her. He was sad. "Can you help me? My lock is broken," Tim said. Mia smiled and said, "Don't worry, I will replace it for you!" Mia went to her house and found a new lock. She took it to Tim's house and replaced the broken lock. Tim was very happy. "Thank you, Mia! You are so generous," Tim said. Mia smiled and said, "I am always here to help my friends!" They played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tim and Mia became best friends.

One day, a smart cat named Tom went for a wander. He saw a big red balloon tied to a tree. Tom was very curious about the balloon. Tom asked his friend, Sam the dog, "What is that big red thing?" Sam said, "That is a balloon. It can fly high in the sky if you let it go." Tom wanted to see the balloon fly. So, Tom jumped and grabbed the balloon with his paws. But something unexpected happened! The balloon started to lift Tom high into the sky. Sam barked, "Hold on, Tom!" Tom was scared, but he held on tight. Sam ran to get help from their friend, Liz the bird. Liz flew up and helped Tom let go of the balloon safely. They all laughed and were happy that their adventure ended well. Tom learned that balloons can be very surprising!
