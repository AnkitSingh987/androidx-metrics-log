Category: Intermediate
Title: Emily and the Shoes

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Emily. One day Emily wanted to go exploring, so she put on her most comfortable shoes, and set off on an adventure. As she walked along the beach, Emily peeked into the rockpools. She saw all kinds of creatures, including some oysters! She decided to take a closer look and crept carefully towards them. When she peeped into the rockpool, she saw the oysters, their shiny shells sparkling in the sunshine. Emily felt excited and wanted to learn more. She peered at them for a while, before settling down on the sandy beach. It was very comfortable lying there, and Emily stayed and watched the oysters, until the sun started to set and it was time to go home. Emily had had a wonderful day, discovering lots of new things. She felt very proud that she had been so brave and explored the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a very hairy dog named Max. Max loved to play and run in the park. One day, Max found a big pot of soup in the park. The soup smelled very good. Max wanted to eat the soup, but it was too hot. He tried to wait for the soup to cool down, but he was very hungry. Max had an idea. He would blow on the soup to make it cool faster. Max blew on the soup, and it started to cool down. He blew and blew until the soup was just right. Max was so happy that he succeeded in cooling the soup. Now, he could eat the tasty soup. Max ate all the soup and felt very full. It was a good day for Max, the hairy dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very careless. One day, he went to the park with his mom. In the park, Tim saw a cactus. He wanted to touch it, but his mom said, "No, Tim! It will hurt you." Tim did not listen to his mom. He touched the cactus and it hurt him. He cried and his mom punished him for not listening. Tim was sad, but he learned a lesson. The next day, Tim went to the park again. This time, he saw a big, green balloon near the cactus. It was a surprise from his mom to make him happy. Tim was very careful not to touch the cactus again. He played with the balloon and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a wise old fox. The fox had many friends, but he also had one big problem. A mean wolf wanted to catch him. One day, the fox went to his friend, the rabbit. He said, "Help me! The mean wolf is coming!" The rabbit thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. "Let's play a game. We will hide, and the wolf will try to find us. If he can't, he will go away." The fox and the rabbit hid behind a big tree. The mean wolf came and started to look for them. He looked and looked, but he couldn't find them. Then, the fox and the rabbit did something unexpected. They vanished! The wolf was very surprised. The mean wolf was angry. He said, "You tricked me! Now, I will be even more mean!" The wise old fox and the rabbit were sad. They thought their plan would work, but it didn't. The mean wolf was still mean, and now he wanted to catch them even more. In the end, the wise old fox and the rabbit had to run away. They left their home in the big forest and went to live somewhere else. The mean wolf stayed in the forest, and he was still mean. The fox and the rabbit were sad, but they knew they had to be brave and find a new home.

One day, a gifted dog named Tim found a chain in the park. Tim liked the chain very much and wanted to show it to his friend, a cat named Sam. Tim went to Sam's house and said, "Look at my chain, Sam!" Sam looked at the chain and said, "I want the chain too!" Tim did not want to give the chain to Sam. They both wanted the chain for themselves. This made them feel sad and mad. Tim insisted, "The chain is mine! I found it first!" Sam did not agree. They decided to ask their friend, a wise old owl named Mr. Hoot, to help them solve the problem. Mr. Hoot listened to Tim and Sam's story. He thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea! You both can share the chain. One day, Tim can have it, and the next day, Sam can have it." Tim and Sam thought this was a good idea. They were happy to share the chain and be friends again.
