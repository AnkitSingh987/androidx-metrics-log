Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Grumpy Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom did not like to bathe. He would run and hide when it was time for a bath. Tom's friend, a happy dog named Max, loved to play in the water. Max always had fun when he played. One day, Max asked Tom to join him for a bath. Tom was scared, but Max said, "Don't worry, it's fun!" So Tom decided to try it. They played in the water, splashing and laughing. Tom found out that bathing could be fun too. After their bath, Tom was not grumpy anymore. He learned that trying new things can be fun and make you happy. From that day on, Tom and Max always had fun together, and Tom was never grumpy again. The moral of the story is to not be afraid to try new things, because they might make you happy.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a polite deer. This deer had a special trick. He could bow very low to say hello to his friends. One day, he met a big bird with one lung. The bird was sad because she could not fly very high. The polite deer wanted to help the bird. He had an idea. He bowed low and let the bird stand on his back. Then, he ran very fast and jumped high into the air. The bird flapped her wings and, to her surprise, she started to fly higher and higher. As they flew together, the polite deer and the bird with one lung became best friends. They played and laughed all day long. And from that day on, the bird could fly high and the deer could bow even lower. They lived happily ever after in the big forest.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Jane. Jane had a pet dog called Lily, and they both cared for each other very much. One day, Jane went outside and saw a lime tree in the garden. It had limes that were green and long. Jane was very excited and picked one of the limes from the tree. When she went back inside her house, she asked her mom if they could share the lime together. "Of course, we can," said her mom. So they both took a piece of the lime. The flavour was sour, but it was delicious. Both Jane and her mom enjoyed it a lot. After they were done, Jane ran back outside to give the rest of the lime to Lily. But when Lily saw the lime, she just sniffed it and walked away. Jane was worried and asked her mom why Lily wasn’t interested in the lime. "Maybe she doesn't like limes," said her mom. Jane was sad and went back outside to play with Lily. Jane was surprised when Lily ran back to her and gave her a big lick. That made Jane feel much better, because she realized that Lily cared for her more than anything else.

One day, a little bunny named Tom was playing in the yard. He saw a big hole and felt dizzy. He sat down and said, "I need help." Tom's friend, a bird named Sue, heard his request. She flew to him and asked, "What's wrong, Tom?" Tom told her about the hole and feeling dizzy. Sue said, "Let's ask Mr. Owl for help." They went to Mr. Owl, who was very wise. They told him about the hole and Tom's dizziness. Mr. Owl thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea. Let's go back to the hole." When they got to the hole, Mr. Owl looked inside. He saw a shiny object. Mr. Owl took it out and showed them. It was a magic stone! The stone was making Tom dizzy. They thanked Mr. Owl for his help and played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a smart skeleton. The smart skeleton loved to play with his friends. They played all day and had lots of fun. One day, the smart skeleton saw a big hill. He had an idea! He told his friends, "Let's roll down the hill together!" All his friends thought it was a great idea. They all lined up at the top of the hill and got ready to roll. They rolled down the hill, laughing and having fun. When they reached the bottom, they all helped each other up. The smart skeleton said, "See, when we work together and help each other, we can do anything!" His friends agreed, and they all learned the value of teamwork and friendship.
