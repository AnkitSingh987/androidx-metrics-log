Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Zipper

One day, a little boy named Tim found a magic zipper. The zipper was shiny and had a special power. Tim was excited to see what the magic zipper could do. Tim showed the zipper to his friend, Sally. "Look what I found, Sally! It's a magic zipper!" said Tim. Sally was amazed and asked, "What does it do?" Tim replied, "Let's find out!" They both held the zipper and wished for a big, spicy pizza. Suddenly, a big, spicy pizza appeared in front of them. It was hot and smelled yummy. Tim and Sally were so happy that they started to eat the pizza. They laughed and enjoyed their tasty treat. Later, Tim and Sally wanted to remember their fun day with the magic zipper. They drew a picture of the zipper and the spicy pizza. They put the picture on the wall so they could always see it and remember their special day.

Once there was a girl called Leah who had lots of pieces in her room. Some were toys, some were colouring pencils, and some were from puzzles. One day, Leah was playing with all her pieces when Mum came in and said it was time to tidy up. Leah saw all the pieces lying around and didn't know how to tidy them up. Then Mum showed her how to take each piece, start small and put them in piles. This way, it would be easier for Leah to pick up and put away. After she finished, Leah's room was so peaceful. Mum said to Leah that when she tidied up, it gave her a sense of calm and control, so she should try and tidy up often. Leah realised that if she tidied up, it could make her feel peaceful and in control. From then on, Leah remembered to tidy up each day and she kept her room peaceful.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to read. Every day, he would read many books. His life was full of fun and joy. One day, while reading, Tim heard a small voice. "I am so hungry," said the voice. Tim looked around and saw a tiny bird. The bird was very hungry, and it could not find any food. Tim wanted to help the bird. He looked for food, but he could not find any. The bird started to cry. "Please help me," said the bird. Tim felt sad for the bird, but he did not give up. He asked his mom for help. She gave him some bread. Tim took the bread and gave it to the hungry bird. The bird ate the bread and felt happy. In the end, Tim and the bird became good friends. They would read together and play every day. Tim learned that helping others can make life even better.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to play with her cards. One day, it was very dry outside, so she decided to play with her cards under a big tree. As Lily played, a friendly squirrel came by. The squirrel said, "Hi, what are you doing?" Lily smiled and said, "I am playing with my cards. Do you want to play too?" The squirrel nodded, so Lily showed him how to play. They took turns to reveal the cards. Each time they revealed a card, they laughed and had fun. They played together until the sun went down, and they became good friends.

One day, Tom found a big box. He wanted to put his toys in the box. He tried to fit all his toys inside, but they were too many. Tom felt sad. Tom's mom saw him and asked, "What's wrong, Tom?" Tom said, "My toys don't fit in the box." His mom had an idea. She gave Tom a long tape. "Let's use this to make the box bigger," she said. Together, they used the tape to make the box bigger. Tom felt a little embarrassed that he didn't think of it before. Now, all his toys fit in the box. Tom was happy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" They both smiled and played with the toys.
