Category: Intermediate
Title: The in Forest and the Bird

Once upon a time, a little lizard lived in a big forest. The lizard had many friends, like the bird, the ant, and the frog. They all liked to play together and have fun. One day, the lizard wanted to have a big party for all of his friends. He knew he needed to prepare for the party. He went to the ant to ask for help. The ant said, "I can help you find yummy food for the party!" The lizard was happy and thanked the ant. The lizard's party was very successful. All of his friends came and had a great time. They ate the yummy food the ant found, played fun games, and danced together. The lizard was so happy to see his friends having fun. And from that day on, the lizard and his friends had even more parties in the big forest.

Tina was a young girl who had a special secret. Every week she would store up her pocket money and use it to buy something special. One Monday morning, Tina decided to go to the store and see what she could get with her week's pocket money. After looking at lots of toys and treats, she finally chose a pretty rubber duck. When she got home she showed it to her mum who smiled. Tina hugged the duck tightly and played with it every day. Every week she would save up her pocket money and look forward to her next trip to the store. Over time, the rubber duck became Tina's special friend. It went wherever she went and she was so proud of it. Life was much more fun when it was around. Tina loved going to the store each week to see what new treasures she could find.

One day, a little deer was walking in the forest. The deer was looking for food. It was very hungry. The deer saw some big trees with green leaves. The deer tried to eat the leaves, but they were too high up. The deer was sad. Then, a tall giraffe came to help the deer. The giraffe could reach the high leaves. The giraffe took some leaves and gave them to the deer. The deer was very happy. The deer said, "Thank you, giraffe!" But the giraffe said, "I am not a giraffe, I am a deer like you!" The deer was very surprised. The other deer was just standing on a big rock. The deer was happy to meet a new friend. They both ate the leaves together.

One day, a large cat and a small dog were playing in a wide field. They were very good friends. The large cat was very strong, and the small dog was very fast. They liked to play games together. The large cat said, "Let's decide who is the best at jumping." The small dog agreed, and they started to jump. The large cat jumped high, but the small dog jumped higher. The large cat was sad. The small dog said, "Don't be sad, large cat. We can both be good at different things. I am fast, and you are strong. We can help each other and be a great team." The large cat smiled, and they continued to play together. The moral of the story is that everyone is good at different things, and we should help each other and work together.

Tom and Anna like to play knights and princesses. Tom has a big shield made of cardboard. Anna has a shiny crown made of paper. They pretend to fight dragons and save each other. One day, they hear a loud roar from the garden. They run outside and see a big dog. The dog is barking and jumping. Tom and Anna are scared. They think the dog is a dragon. "Help, help!" Anna cries. "The dragon wants to eat me!" "Don't worry, princess!" Tom says. "I will protect you with my shield!" Tom holds his shield in front of him and Anna. He tries to look brave. The dog runs towards them and sniffs the shield. It licks the shield and wags its tail. "Hello, dragon!" Tom says. "Are you friendly?" The dog barks again. It sounds like an answer. "Maybe he is not a dragon," Anna says. "Maybe he is a dog." "A dog?" Tom says. "But he is so big and loud!" The dog nudges the shield with his nose. He wants to play. "Maybe he is a gentle dog," Anna says. "Maybe he wants to be our friend." Tom and Anna lower their shield and pet the dog. The dog licks their faces and rolls on the grass. "See, he is a gentle dog," Anna says. "He is not a dragon at all." Tom and Anna laugh and hug the dog. They decide to name him Sparky. They play with him until their mom calls them for lunch. They are happy. They have a new friend.
