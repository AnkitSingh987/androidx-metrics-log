Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Spoon

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim was troubled because he could not eat his rice. He would struggle to use his spoon and the rice would fall off. One day, Tim's mom gave him a fork instead of a spoon. Tim was happy and thought he could eat his rice now. But, when Tim tried to eat with the fork, the rice still fell off. He was sad and didn't know what to do. Tim's little sister, Sue, saw him struggle and she had an idea. She ran to the kitchen and came back with something that made Tim smile. Sue brought Tim a pair of chopsticks. Tim had never used chopsticks before, but he was excited to try. To his surprise, Tim could eat the rice with the chopsticks! He was so happy and ate all his rice. Tim's mom and Sue were very proud of him. From that day on, Tim always ate his rice with chopsticks, and he was never troubled again.

Once there was a boy who was very successful. He had nice clothes and lots of friends. One day, the boy was swimming when he ripped his pants. He was so embarrassed that he started to cry. All of his friends laughed at him and he ran away. The boy was so sad and embarrassed he couldn't stop crying. He went back home and tried to hide his tears, but his mom saw them. She said she was sorry, but her son said he was a failure and he would never be successful again. He felt so hopeless and scared, he kept crying all day. Eventually, his mom came over and hugged him tightly. She said it was ok and that he could still be successful, but it didn't seem to make a difference. The boy felt like he would never succeed in life. The boy went to bed that night feeling like a failure. He knew he would never be successful again and continued to cry.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a muffin on a table. The muffin looked tasty. Sue wanted to eat the muffin, but she did not know whose muffin it was. A bird flew down and said, "Hi Sue! I serve tasty muffins to my friends. Do you want to be my friend?" Sue was happy and said, "Yes! I want to be your friend and eat the tasty muffin." But then, a big dog came and ate the muffin very fast. Sue and the bird were sad. The dog said, "I'm sorry, I was hungry." Sue had an idea. She said, "Let's all be friends and share the next muffin!" The bird, Sue, and the dog were happy and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong man. He was looking for a special crystal. He knew that it was very important, but he didn't know where to find it. So he set off on a journey to find the crystal. He searched high and low, but it was nowhere to be seen! He was starting to get worried, so he kept searching. Finally he saw it! It was a large, shimmering crystal. He was so excited! He ran towards it, but then he heard a loud voice. It said "You must struggle to get the crystal. Can you do it?" The man looked around, but he could not see anyone. He was a bit scared, but he decided he was brave enough to try. So he worked really hard, pushing and pulling, until the crystal came loose. He picked it up and felt really proud. He hugged it close and his heart was filled with joy. As he walked away, he could hear a soft laugh. It was the voice from before! He thanked it for helping him, before setting off home. When he arrived, he saw a bright light coming from his crystal. He looked closer and saw a wish written inside. He smiled and said "Thank you!"

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a hairy man named Tom. He had a big spear that he used to catch fish. Tom loved to eat fish, and he was very good at catching them. One day, Tom went to the river to catch some fish. He saw a little boy named Tim sitting by the water. Tim looked sad. "Why are you sad?" asked Tom. "I want to catch fish too, but I don't have a spear," said Tim. Tom wanted to help Tim, so he let him borrow his spear. Together, Tom and Tim caught many fish. They were both very happy. But when they went back to the village, they saw that the fish they caught had been spoiled. The sun was too hot, and the fish went bad. Tom and Tim were sad, but they learned a lesson: next time, they would eat the fish right away, so they wouldn't spoil.
