Category: Intermediate
Title: A Tale of the Rabbit

Once upon a time a bear and a rabbit were best friends. They loved being together and were always happy. One day, the rabbit became very sad. The bear saw how sad the rabbit was and wanted to help. He asked the rabbit what was wrong. The rabbit said he was too big for his favourite coat and he couldn't wear it anymore. The bear had a great idea. He said that he could make the coat fit again. He told the rabbit to close his eyes and make a wish with all his love. The bear used his magic to make the coat shrink. When the rabbit opened his eyes, he saw that his coat was the perfect size. He hugged the bear and thanked him. The rabbit was so happy that he could wear his favourite coat again. The bear and rabbit stayed best friends for life. They shared their love with each other, and their coat fit perfectly.

Once upon a time, there was a human named Tom. Tom was a good man. He liked to polish his shoes. One day, Tom found a big red ball in his yard. Tom played with the ball. He saw a little girl named Amy. Amy wanted to play too. Tom said, "Let's play with the ball. We can have fun." Amy was happy. They played and played. Then, the ball went into the road. Tom ran to get it. A big car came. The car hit Tom. Tom was hurt. Amy was sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a girl who wanted to have a party. She asked her mom for a party, and her mom said yes. The girl was so excited, she started to plan her party. She wanted it to be very special. She invited lots of friends and bought decorations for the party. But then something terrible happened. It started to rain very hard on the day of the party. The rain spoiled her plans and her party! Everyone had to stay inside, and the party was ruined. The girl was very sad and cried. But then her mom said, "Let's have a party inside instead. We can still have fun!" So they did, and the little girl had a great time.

One day, a pale cat named Lily was playing in the park. She saw a big ball stuck in a tree. Lily wanted to help, so she tried to lift the ball with her paws. But she was too small to reach it. A kind owl named Mr. Hoot came by and saw Lily. He asked, "What is wrong, Lily?" Lily said, "The ball is stuck, and I can't lift it." Mr. Hoot gave Lily a wink and said, "I will help you." Mr. Hoot flew up to the tree and used his wings to lift the ball. The ball fell down, and Lily was happy. She thanked Mr. Hoot, and they played with the ball together.

Once upon a time, there was a great big ranch. On this ranch, there were many animals. There were cows, horses, and sheep. They all played and lived together. One day, the animals wanted to play a game. They decided to pass a ball to each other. The cows passed the ball to the horses. The horses passed the ball to the sheep. The sheep passed the ball back to the cows. They all had fun playing together. As they played, they learned to share and be kind to each other. This made the ranch a happy place for all the animals. The moral of the story is that when we share and are kind, we can all have fun and be happy.

Once upon a time, in a big, big forest, a group of animals decided to gather. They wanted to have a fun day together. The animals were all friends, and they loved to play games and share their life with each other. The animals decided to have an organized race. The race started, and all the animals ran as fast as they could. The rabbit was in the lead, and the turtle was far behind. The rabbit said, "I'm so fast, I can take a rest and still win!" So the rabbit sat down under a tree and closed his eyes. While the rabbit slept, the turtle kept moving. The turtle was slow, but he did not stop. The other animals cheered for the turtle. When the rabbit woke up, he saw the turtle was almost at the finish line. The rabbit could not believe it! He ran as fast as he could, but the turtle won the race. The animals clapped and said, "Good job, turtle! You showed us that being slow and steady can win the race!"
