Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Basket and the Forest

A mommy and her little three year old son were walking through a forest. The son was looking around and admiring all the beauty around him. He asked his mommy what all the trees and plants were and why they were so big and colorful. The mommy explained that all the plants had a lot of energy, like lungs fill with air. "Wow!" the little boy said, "I want to feel that energy too!" His mommy smiled, and reached into her pocket. She pulled out a basket of colorful little stones, each one special and powerful. She told her son that every time he needed some energy, he should hold one of the stones in his hand, and imagine it filling his lungs and body with power. The little boy smiled and took the basket of stones. He wanted to show his friends right away, so he ran off to find them. His mommy looked after him and felt a little envious. She wished she could have filled her lungs with energy too.

One day, a boy named Tim was playing in his yard. He liked to yell very loud. His neighbor, an old lady, was very weak. She did not like when Tim yelled. "Tim, please stop yelling," the old lady said. "I am weak and it hurts my ears." But Tim did not listen. He kept yelling and playing. The old lady became very sad. She tried to talk to Tim's mom, but Tim's mom was not home. The old lady went back to her house and cried. Tim saw the old lady cry and felt bad. He stopped yelling and went to say sorry. But it was too late. The old lady moved away the next day. The moral of the story is to be kind and listen to others.

One day, a boy named Tim found a microscope in his room. He did not know what it was. He thought it was a toy. Tim wanted to see if it was a fun toy. Tim showed the microscope to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Let's taste it!" They both tasted the microscope. It was smelly and tasted bad. They did not like it. They thought it was a bad toy. Then, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw the boys with the microscope. She said, "Oh no! That is not a toy. It is for looking at tiny things." Tim and Sam were sad. They had broken the microscope by tasting it. Now, they could not use it to look at tiny things.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Tim. Tim was always scared, and he would often tremble when he heard a loud noise. One day, Tim met a big dog named Max. Max had a harsh bark, and it made Tim tremble even more. Max said, "Don't be scared, little cat. I am your friend." Tim was surprised because he thought all big dogs were mean. Max told Tim that he knew a magic spell that could make Tim brave and not tremble anymore. Tim wanted to try the spell, so Max said the magic words and waved his paw. Suddenly, Tim felt different. He didn't tremble when Max barked or when they heard a loud noise. Tim was happy and thanked Max for helping him. They became best friends and played together every day. And from that day on, Tim was a brave little cat who didn't tremble anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat delicious ice cream. One day, she went to the park with her mom to play. While playing, Lily saw a big ice cream truck. She ran to it and got a big ice cream cone. But, she forgot her toy near the pole. So, she ran back to get it. When Lily came back to the ice cream truck, her ice cream was gone! A naughty dog had eaten it. Lily was sad. Her mom bought her another ice cream cone, and they played happily in the park.

Bob had a blouse he loved. It was high and blue. One day it was windy and the blouse blew away over the fence. Bob tried to reach it but it was too high. Bob ran around the fence but he could not get to his blouse. He started to cry. He was so sad. He knew he could not get his blouse back. Bob kept on crying until it was dark. He was still sad and disappointed. He had lost his blouse and it made him feel very bad.
