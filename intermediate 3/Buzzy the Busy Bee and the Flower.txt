Category: Intermediate
Title: Buzzy the Busy Bee and the Flower

One day, a busy bee named Buzzy was flying through the sky. Buzzy saw a big cloud that looked like a flower. Buzzy said, "Hello, cloud! You look like a flower. I want to find a flower to get some food." The cloud heard Buzzy and wanted to help. The cloud said, "Hello, Buzzy! I am not a flower, but I can help you find one. Follow me!" The cloud moved through the sky, and Buzzy followed. As they flew, Buzzy saw a big, pretty flower on the ground. Buzzy was very happy and said, "Thank you, cloud! I recognize this flower. It has the best food!" But when Buzzy went to get food from the flower, it was not a flower at all. It was a butterfly! The butterfly said, "Hello, Buzzy! I am not a flower, but I can help you find one too." Buzzy, the cloud, and the butterfly all laughed and became friends. They flew through the sky together, looking for the perfect flower for Buzzy to get some food.

Once upon a time, in a green land, there was a helpful frog. The frog lived near a small stream. Every day, the frog would jump and play near the water. One day, the frog saw a big bird in the sky. The bird was flying very fast. The frog thought the bird might miss the stream, so he called out, "Bird, come here! There's a nice stream to play in!" The bird heard the frog and flew down. But, surprise! The bird was not a bird. It was a butterfly! The butterfly had big wings that looked like a bird from far away. The helpful frog and the butterfly became good friends. They played near the stream every day and never missed a moment of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a cat called Tigger. Every day, she would curl up in her bed and dream of adventures. One day, she decided to explore the house. As Tigger walked by, she noticed an open door and went inside. The room was full of fluffy pillows, and Tigger felt like it belonged to her. She jumped into the bed and started to purr. Suddenly, a voice said, "What are you doing in my bed?" Tigger looked up and saw a little girl. She felt a bit guilty and said, "Sorry, I thought this bed belonged to me." The little girl smiled and said, "It's ok, Tigger. I think this bed belongs to you too." She patted Tigger's head, and Tigger purred even louder. From then on, the two were best friends - and the bed was always a cozy place for them to rest.

One day, a cheerful girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a pretty flower called a poppy. The poppy was red and bright. Lily wanted to take the poppy home, but she did not know how to raise it. Lily asked her mom, "How do I raise a poppy?" Her mom said, "You need to give it water, sun, and love." Lily was happy to learn how to take care of the poppy. Each day, Lily gave the poppy water, put it in the sun, and told it she loved it. The poppy grew big and strong. Lily and the poppy were happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a spoiled boy who always wanted more toys. One day, Tim's mom tried to explain that he had enough toys, but he didn't listen. One day, Tim was playing in his room when he saw a shiny toy in the shower. He wanted it so badly, but his mom said no. Tim was very mad and decided to take the toy without asking. As Tim played with the toy, it broke into pieces. He tried to fix it, but he couldn't. Tim was very sad and cried. His mom came and saw the broken toy. She was upset that Tim took the toy without asking. Tim learned that being spoiled and not listening can lead to bad things.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spark. Spark was a useful dog. He liked to help people. One day, Spark saw a big ball. He wanted to play with it. So, he ran to the ball and tried to bite it. "Ouch!" said Spark. The ball was not soft. It was hard. Spark's teeth hurt. A little girl saw Spark and came to him. She said, "Don't bite that ball, Spark! It is not for biting." Spark was sad. He wanted to play. The little girl had an idea. She said, "Let's play with my soft ball." Spark was happy again. They played with the soft ball. Spark did not bite the hard ball anymore. But one day, Spark saw the hard ball again. He forgot what the little girl said. He bit the hard ball very hard. "Ouch!" said Spark. His teeth broke. Spark was very sad. He could not help people anymore. He learned that he should listen to his friends.
