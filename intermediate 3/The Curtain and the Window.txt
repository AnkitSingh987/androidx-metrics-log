Category: Intermediate
Title: The Curtain and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a little curtain. The curtain was restless. It wanted to go outside and play. But it could not. It had to hang by the window all day long. One day, the wind blew very strong. The curtain thought, "Now is my chance!" It blew and blew, and the curtain got loose. It went outside to play. The curtain played with the wind all day long. It had so much fun! But when the sun went down, the curtain started to miss its window. It went back home and hung by the window again. From that day on, the curtain was happy to hang by the window and watch the world outside.

Once upon a time there was a little boy. He was playing in the park when he saw a big, red brick. The little boy wanted to push it. He saw an old man sitting gamily on a nearby park bench. He asked the old man, "Can I push the brick?" The old man smiled. "Yes, you can push the brick. But, be careful, it's very heavy." The little boy was scared, but he wanted to push the brick. He took a deep breath and gave the brick a big push. Nothing happened. He pushed again, a little harder this time. Finally, the brick moved! The little boy was so happy, but he was also a little scared. He decided the best thing to do was to take a break and push the brick again later. So he ran off to play on the swings. The little boy never did push the big, red brick again. But he found out later that the brick had been covering up a secret trap door. Who knows what he might have found if he had kept pushing!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in his backyard. One sunny day, he saw a lizard. The lizard was green and fast. Tim wanted to grab the lizard and show it to his mom. Tim tried to grab the lizard, but it ran away. Tim felt frustrated. He tried again and again, but the lizard was too fast. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. He got a cup and put it near the lizard. The lizard went into the cup. Tim grabbed the cup and showed the lizard to his mom. She smiled and Tim felt happy. They let the lizard go back to the yard. Tim learned that sometimes, you need a new way to do things.

Once upon a time, there was an old man who had a cart. He used his cart to carry things from one place to another. One day, he was walking down the road when he saw a little boy crying. The old man asked the boy, "Why are you crying?" The boy said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." The old man felt sad for the boy and wanted to help him. The old man kneeled down and looked under his cart. He saw the toy! He gave it to the boy and said, "Here is your toy!" The boy stopped crying and said, "Thank you!" The old man and the boy became friends and they walked down the road together, both happy.

One day, a big dog named Spot found an enormous umbrella in the park. Spot loved to play with new things, so he took the umbrella home. But when Spot tried to play with the umbrella, it was too big for him. Spot started to complain, "This umbrella is too big for me to play with!" Spot's friend, a small cat named Fluffy, heard him complain. Fluffy had an idea to help Spot. "Let's trade toys!" said Fluffy. Fluffy gave Spot a small ball to play with, and Spot gave Fluffy the enormous umbrella. They were both very happy with their new toys. But then, something unexpected happened. It started to rain! Spot and Fluffy didn't want to get wet. Fluffy opened the enormous umbrella, and they both sat under it to stay dry. The big umbrella was perfect for both of them! They laughed and played under the umbrella until the rain stopped.
