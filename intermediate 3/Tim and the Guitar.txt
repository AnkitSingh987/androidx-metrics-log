Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Guitar

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a guitar. He loved to play it all day. One day, he saw a big tree. He thought it was a good place to play his guitar. Tim played his guitar under the tree. He was happy. But then, he heard a loud sigh. Tim looked around, but he did not see anything. He was a little scared, but he kept playing. The next day, Tim went back to the tree. He played his guitar again. The sigh came back, louder this time. Tim was very scared now. He ran away and left his guitar under the tree. Tim told his mom about the sigh. She did not believe him. She told him to go get his guitar. Tim went back to the tree, but his guitar was gone. He felt very bad and sad. The end.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful land, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy arrow. One day, he decided to go to the park to play with his friends. When Tim got to the park, he saw his friend Sue. Sue was sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, Sue?" Sue said, "I lost my toy ball." Tim wanted to help Sue find her toy ball. As they looked for the ball, they saw a big dog. The dog had the ball in its mouth! The dog saw Tim's arrow and wanted to play. Tim threw the arrow, and the dog ran to get it. The dog dropped the ball and brought back the arrow. Sue was happy to have her ball back, and they all played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to sing and play with her toys. One day, she found a big bowl in the kitchen. Lily had an idea! She decided to put her toys in the bowl and make them sing too. Lily had a fake toy cat that she liked a lot. She put the fake cat in the bowl with the other toys. She started to sing a fun song, and the toys in the bowl danced and sang with her. Lily was so happy, she knew something special was going to happen. As Lily sang louder, the fake cat began to change. It became a real cat! The cat jumped out of the bowl and danced with Lily. They sang and played together all day long. From that day on, Lily and her new cat friend would always sing and dance together.

Once upon a time, there was a loud train. The train loved to turn on the tracks. It went fast and made a lot of noise. The train was big and strong. It could pull many things. The train needed coal to go. The coal made the train go fast. The train went to the coal yard to get more. The train was happy when it had coal. It could go to many places. One day, the train went around a turn. It saw a big hill. The train went up the hill. It was hard, but the train made it. At the top, the train saw a beautiful view. The train was very happy it could turn and go up the hill.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful butterfly. The butterfly loved to fly and play. One day, the butterfly saw a long road. The butterfly wanted to see what was on the other side of the road. As the butterfly flew over the road, a big frog appeared. The frog said, "Hello, beautiful butterfly! Where are you going?" The butterfly replied, "I want to see what is on the other side of the road." The frog and the butterfly hopped and flew together. But when they got to the other side, they found a big surprise. Instead of more flowers and trees, there was a big party with lots of animals! The animals were dancing and playing. The frog and the butterfly joined the fun and made many new friends.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a messy raven. His name was Bobo. Bobo liked to play with his friends, but his nest was always messy. One day, Bobo's friend, a small bird named Lulu, came to play. "Hi Bobo, can I come in?" Lulu asked. Bobo looked at his messy nest and felt sad. He knew Lulu didn't like messes. But Bobo had an idea. "Wait here, Lulu," he said. Bobo went to find his other friend, a big bear named Bubu. Bubu was good at cleaning. Bobo asked Bubu for help. "Bubu, can you help me clean my nest? I want Lulu to play with me." Bubu smiled and said, "Yes, I will help you." Bubu and Bobo cleaned the nest. Then, Bobo went back to Lulu and said, "Now you can come in, Lulu." Lulu was happy to see the clean nest. But then, something unexpected happened. A loud sound came from the tree. It was a big wind! The wind blew the nest and made it messy again. Lulu, Bobo, and Bubu looked at each other and laughed. They knew they could clean it again together. And they all played happily in the messy nest.
