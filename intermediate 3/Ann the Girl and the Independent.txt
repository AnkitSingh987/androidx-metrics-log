Category: Intermediate
Title: Ann the Girl and the Independent

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ann. Ann was a very independent girl. She loved to play and have fun all by herself. One day, she saw a shiny ball that seemed to glow. She picked it up and decided to play with it. Ann threw the glowing ball up in the sky and watched it fly high. She laughed and clapped her hands. She played catch with the ball all day long. The ball made her feel happy and strong. The next day, Ann took the ball to her friends. They played a game to see who could throw the ball the highest. Ann threw the ball higher than anyone else. She got a big win! Ann was so proud, and her friends cheered for her. From that day on, the little girl and her glowing ball were always together, having fun and being independent.

Once upon a time, there was a big, pale cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to celebrate with his friends. One day, Fluffy and his friends decided to have a color party. They all wore their favorite colors and played fun games. During the party, Fluffy's friend, Bunny, said, "Let's play a game with colors!" All the friends thought it was a great idea. They took turns saying their favorite color and why they liked it. Fluffy said, "I like blue because it's the color of the sky!" Suddenly, something unexpected happened. A rainbow appeared in the sky with all the colors they were talking about! Fluffy and his friends were so surprised and happy. They all looked at the rainbow and said, "Wow! The sky is celebrating with us!" The rainbow made their color party even more special. Fluffy and his friends had the best time ever. From that day on, they always remembered the magical rainbow that joined their celebration. And they knew that the sky loved colors just as much as they did.

One day, a modest cat named Sam wanted to play golf. He liked to hit the small ball and watch it roll. Sam had a friend, a dog named Max. Max wanted to play too, but he did not know how to play golf. Sam tried to teach Max how to play. Max tried to hit the ball, but he could not. He got mad and wanted to quit. Sam did not want Max to quit. He knew Max could learn if he tried more. Sam told Max to try again. This time, Max hit the ball! It rolled far away. Max was happy he did not quit. Sam and Max played golf all day and had lots of fun. They learned that trying is better than quitting.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toy jewelry. She had a necklace, a bracelet, and a ring. One day, her mom told her, "Lily, if you are obedient and do your chores, I will pay you with a new toy." Lily was excited and did all her chores. She cleaned her room, put away her toys, and helped her mom in the garden. After she finished, her mom said, "Good job, Lily! You were very obedient. Now, let's go to the store and pay for your new toy." At the store, Lily saw a beautiful jewelry box. She wanted it so much! But then, she saw a little boy crying. The boy said, "I lost my toy and I don't have money to pay for a new one." Lily felt sad for the boy. She thought for a moment and then said, "Here, take my money. You can pay for a new toy." The boy was so happy and thanked Lily. Then, something unexpected happened. The store owner saw what Lily did and said, "That was very kind, Lily. I have a gift for you." He gave Lily the jewelry box she wanted. Lily was so happy and learned that being kind and sharing can bring happiness to everyone.

Sara and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were grown-ups and cook and clean. Sara put some fake food on a plate and gave it to Ben. "Here you go, honey. Eat your dinner," she said. Ben pretended to eat the food and smiled. "Mmm, this is yummy. Thank you, Sara. You are a good cook," he said. He put the plate in the sink. "Now we have to wash the dishes," he said. Sara nodded. "Yes, we do. But we don't have a dishwasher. We have to wash them by hand," she said. She turned on the faucet and got some soap. She started to scrub the plate with a sponge. Ben watched her and had an idea. "Hey, Sara. I know how we can get a dishwasher. We can buy one!" he said. Sara looked at him and frowned. "Buy one? How can we buy one? We don't have any money," she said. Ben smiled and pointed to the couch. "Yes, we do. Look, there are some coins under the cushions. We can use them to buy a dishwasher," he said. Sara followed him to the couch and saw the coins. They were shiny and round. She picked one up and looked at it. "But these are fake coins. They are not real money. We can't buy anything with them," she said. Ben shrugged. "They look real to me. Maybe the store will not notice. Come on, let's go and buy a dishwasher. It will be fun," he said. Sara was not sure, but she liked the idea of having a dishwasher. She put the fake coins in her pocket and took Ben's hand. "Okay, let's go. But we have to be careful. We don't want to get in trouble," she said. They put on their coats and shoes and went outside. They walked to the store and looked for a dishwasher. They saw one that was big and white and had many buttons. They liked it. They went to the cashier and gave him the fake coins. "We want to buy this dishwasher, please," they said.
