Category: Intermediate
Title: The Popular Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a popular boy. He was a youth. He liked to play with his friends. They all had fun together. They liked to run, jump, and laugh. One day, they played a game. They had to find a ball. The ball was hidden. They looked and looked. The popular boy found the ball. But, the ball did not look right. It was not round. It was not bouncy. It was not a ball. The popular boy did not yield. He kept looking. He found the real ball. It was round and bouncy. His friends were happy. They all played together. The popular boy was a hero. And they all had fun again.

One day, a girl named Amy went for a walk. She loved to skip and play. She saw a plastic bag on the ground. Inside the bag, there was a big, red apple. Amy thought the apple looked tasty. Amy picked up the bag and skipped back home. She wanted to eat the tasty apple. She showed it to her mom. Her mom said, "Wait, let's wash the apple first." After they washed the apple, Amy took a big bite. But the apple was not tasty at all! It was made of plastic too. Amy and her mom laughed and laughed. They put the plastic apple on a shelf to remember their funny story.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to measure things. He measured his toys, his bed, and even his dog. Tim's dad had a big job. He worked with power. Tim thought his dad's job was not dull, it was fun! One day, Tim's dad took him to work. Tim saw big machines that made power. Tim's dad told him that power made the lights turn on. Tim wanted to help his dad. He asked if he could measure the machines. His dad said yes, and Tim was very happy. Tim measured the big machines. He found out they were very big! His dad was proud of him. They went home and told Tim's mom about their day. She was happy too. Now, Tim knew that measuring things and making power could be fun. He thought his dad's job was the best job ever.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little girl named Maddie. She was only three years old and was very fond of books. One day, Maddie found a special book in the park. She was so excited that she took it home with her. Once she opened it, she noticed that something magical was inside. With each page she turned, the book began to shrink. The book got smaller and smaller until it had shrunk to the size of a walnut! Maddie couldn't believe her eyes. She was about to show her parents her incredible discovery when, suddenly, the book grew back to its normal size. She quickly told her parents what had happened, but they just couldn't believe it. Maddie's father then said: "Come on, Maddie! A shrinking book? That's impossible!" Maddie smiled at her dad and replied: "But Daddy, I saw it! The book really shrank!" The father smiled back, shook his head and said: "Okay, Maddie. If you say so, I believe you." From that day on, Maddie kept her little book close to her at all times. She loved it dearly, and she was sure it was full of magical surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to take trips with her family. One day, her mom said, "Lily, we are going on a trip to the park!" Lily was happy and excited. Before the trip, Lily's mom reminded her to wear her comfortable shoes. Lily put on her shoes and felt good. She was ready to walk and play at the park. At the park, Lily played on the swings, slide, and with her ball. She had so much fun! When it was time to go home, Lily's mom reminded her to say goodbye to the park. Lily said, "Goodbye park, see you soon!" And they went home after a fun trip.

One day, a little boy named Tim was hungry. He wanted to eat a sandwich. So, he went to the kitchen and made a big, yummy sandwich. Tim was happy. Then, a poor little dog came to the door. The dog was hungry too. Tim wanted to help, so he called his mom. "Mom, can I give the dog my sandwich?" he asked. His mom said yes. Tim gave his sandwich to the dog. The dog ate it very fast. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog turned into a big, friendly bear! Tim was surprised, but he was not scared. The bear gave Tim a big hug and a new sandwich. They became best friends.
