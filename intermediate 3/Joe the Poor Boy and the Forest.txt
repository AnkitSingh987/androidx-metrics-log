Category: Intermediate
Title: Joe the Poor Boy and the Forest

Once upon a time there was a poor boy named Joe. He lived in a small hut in the country. Every day, Joe would rub his old board with a cloth. He did this to make the board look shiny and new. He rubbed and rubbed until the board was clean. Every day Joe would carry the board with him on his journey. He brought it to the forest, under the bridge, and even when he went swimming in the lake. One day, Joe arrived at a large village. The villagers had never seen Joe's shiny board and asked if he wanted to sell it. Joe said no. He said the board was very special and he would not sell it. The villagers thought that was very kind. As a reward for his kindness, they gave Joe a big bag of food for his poor family. Joe was so happy to see the good food. Joe and his family celebrated with a feast that night. He never forgot the kindness the villagers had shown him and he kept his shiny board close by his side.

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman. He was alert and always watching the sky. On this day he went out to sea in his boat to find some fish to eat. He cast his net and waited. But he waited and waited and still nothing came. Then suddenly he spotted something! "Ahoy there!" said the fisherman, "What's that I see?" "It is a big fish!" said the fish with a smile on its face. The fisherman was so happy! He took the fish and brought it back to shore. He cooked the fish and ate it with his family. Everyone enjoyed it very much. The fisherman went back out to sea and he was even more alert every time he went out. But he also looked happy, as he knew that he could find fish to eat whenever he wanted.

One day, a cop named Tim was walking down the street. He saw a little boy named Sam who was sad. Sam had a dull toy car that did not work well. The cop wanted to help Sam feel better. Cop Tim said, "Hi Sam! Why are you sad?" Sam looked up and said, "My toy car is dull and does not go fast." The cop thought for a moment and had an idea. He took out his own toy car from his pocket. Cop Tim said, "Let's play together and repeat the race until your car goes fast." Sam smiled and they started to race their toy cars. They played and repeated the race many times. Slowly, Sam's dull toy car started to go faster. Sam was happy and said, "Thank you, Cop Tim! My car is going fast now!" The cop smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sam. I'm glad I could help make your toy car go fast." They both laughed and continued to play with their toy cars until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Bree. Bree lived in a regular cage. She wanted to escape the cage and fly in the sky. One day, a big wind came and blew the cage door open. Bree was happy and thought she could escape. But she saw her friend, a small bug named Bug, was in trouble. Bug was stuck in a spider web. Bree decided to help Bug first before she escaped. She flew to the web and saved Bug. They both went back to the cage, and Bree closed the door. Bree learned that helping friends is more important than escaping the cage. And she felt happier than ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Spark, who was a tiny, shiny bug. They liked to play together in the park. One day, Tim was very frustrated. He couldn't find his toy car. He sat down and started to cry. Spark saw Tim and flew over to him. Spark whispered, "Don't cry, Tim. I will help you find your toy car." Tim and Spark looked everywhere in the park. They asked their friends, "Have you seen Tim's toy car?" All their friends said, "No, we haven't seen it." Finally, they found the toy car under a big tree. Tim was so happy! He hugged Spark and said, "Thank you for helping me find my toy car." Tim and Spark played together all day, and they were the best of friends.
