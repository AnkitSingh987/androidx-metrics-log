Category: Intermediate
Title: The Modest Town and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a modest town. In this town, people liked to trade. They traded toys, food, and clothes. Everyone was happy and kind. One day, a tired man came to the town. He had walked a long way and needed to rest. The people in the town saw the man and wanted to help him. They gave him a soft bed to sleep on and some yummy food to eat. After the man had a good rest, he thanked the people for their kindness. He decided to stay in the town and trade with them. He traded his strong hands to help build houses and fix things. The people loved the man, and they all lived happily in their modest town.

Once upon a time, there was a little orange cat named Tim. Tim liked to play in the quiet park near his home. One sunny day, Tim saw a big orange ball and wanted to play with it. Tim kicked the ball very hard and it went flying into the air. Oh no! The ball hit a little girl who was playing nearby. She started to cry. Tim felt very bad for what he did. Tim went to the little girl and said, "I am sorry, can you forgive me?" The little girl looked at Tim and saw that he was sorry. She smiled and said, "Yes, I forgive you." Then, they both played with the orange ball together, and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a strong man named Tom. He loved to decorate his house with many things. One day, he found a big box of junk. He was so happy! He thought he could use the junk to make his house look more fun. Tom started to put the junk on the walls and the floor. It looked very nice. But then, something unexpected happened. The junk started to move! The junk was not just old things, but they were also magic things. The magic things did not like being used as decorations. The magic things started to talk to Tom. They said, "Please do not use us as decorations. We want to be free and have fun too!" Tom was surprised, but he understood. He took the magic things off the walls and the floor. He let them go free and have fun. The magic things were very happy. From that day on, Tom learned to be kind to all things, even junk. The moral of the story is to treat everything with respect and kindness, because you never know what might be special.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a red cap that he loved. He wore the cap every day. One day, a fierce wind blew his cap away. Tim was very sad. Tim went to his mom and told her about his cap. His mom told him to pray. So, Tim closed his eyes and prayed. He asked for help to find his cap. The next day, Tim went outside. He saw his cap on a tree. Tim was very happy. He thanked God for helping him find his cap. From that day, Tim always prayed and wore his cap with a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was a loud bird named Bob. Bob liked to sing all day. One day, Bob saw a little insect on a leaf. The insect was wet from the rain. Bob wanted to help the insect. He used his wings to fan the insect. Bob tried to make the insect dry. The insect was happy and started to dance. Bob and the insect became good friends. They played together every day. The loud bird and the little insect had lots of fun in the sun. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a dancer who lived in the forest. She was an honest dancer and she always wanted to join the party. One day, the dancer saw a little girl in the forest. Her name was Sarah, and she was only three years old. Sarah said to the dancer, "Can I join you?" The dancer smiled and said, "Of course! I would love it if you join me. What can you do?" Sarah proudly said, "I can dance!" The dancer told her that it was a great skill and asked her to join in. So Sarah and the dancer started to do a dance together. They did twirls and jumps and had a lot of fun. The honest dancer was so happy to have a new friend. Sarah loved it too! And from that day on, Sarah and the honest dancer danced together whenever they saw each other in the forest.
