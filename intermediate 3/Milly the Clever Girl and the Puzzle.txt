Category: Intermediate
Title: Milly the Clever Girl and the Puzzle

Once upon a time there was a very clever little girl named Milly. She loved to play with puzzles in her room. One day she was working on an icy puzzle and she couldn't seem to settle it. It was very difficult! Milly's mom came in and asked, “What’s wrong, sweetheart?” Milly replied, “My icy puzzle won’t settle!” Milly’s mom smiled and said, “I’ll help you. Let’s see if we can figure it out together.” They began looking through the icy puzzle pieces and soon enough, Milly and her mom started to see a pattern. They worked together and placed all of the pieces in the right spot. In no time, the icy puzzle was settled! Milly was so proud of herself and smiled. “We did it Mom!”

Anna liked to play with her mother's lotion. She liked how it smelled and how it made her skin soft. Sometimes, she would sneak into her mother's room and take the lotion bottle to her own room. She would put some lotion on her hands, her arms, her legs, and even her hair. She felt very graceful and pretty. One day, her mother noticed that her lotion was almost gone. She asked Anna, "Did you take my lotion, Anna?" Anna felt scared. She knew she did something wrong. She nodded and said, "Yes, mommy. I'm sorry. I like your lotion." Her mother was not angry, but she was not happy either. She said, "Anna, I know you like my lotion, but you can't take it without asking me. It is not nice to take things that are not yours. And you don't need so much lotion. It is not good for your hair. You have to return it to me, okay?" Anna felt sorry. She said, "Okay, mommy. I will return it to you. I won't take it again. Can I have some lotion for myself?" Her mother smiled and said, "Yes, you can have some lotion for yourself. But only a little bit. And you have to ask me first. Come on, let's go to the store and buy you some lotion. You can choose the one you like." Anna was happy. She hugged her mother and said, "Thank you, mommy. I love you. You are very graceful and pretty, too."

Once upon a time, in a wide green park, there lived a little girl named Mia and a small cat named Tom. They were best friends and loved to play together every day. One sunny day, Mia said to Tom, "I want to play a new game, but I don't know what to play." Tom thought for a moment and then suggested, "Let's play hide and seek!" Mia loved the idea and they started to play. Mia closed her eyes and counted to ten while Tom ran to hide. When Mia opened her eyes, she looked for Tom in the park. She found him hiding behind a big tree. They both laughed and took turns hiding and finding each other. They played all day long and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Mia said, "I love playing with you, Tom!" And Tom replied, "I love playing with you too, Mia!"

Once upon a time, there was a big furry cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a small house with a nice family. The house had a warm fireplace where Fluffy liked to sleep. One day, Fluffy was near the fireplace when he noticed something strange. There was a small bug walking on the floor. Fluffy was very curious about the bug and decided to follow it. The bug led Fluffy to a big tree outside the house. Fluffy climbed the tree and saw many more bugs. They were all playing together. Fluffy had a fun time watching the bugs and playing in the tree. When it was time to go home, Fluffy went back to the warm fireplace and fell asleep, dreaming about the new friends he had made.

Ben has a trumpet. He likes to blow it loud and make music. He is playful and happy. He plays with his trumpet in the park with his mom and dad. But there is a dog in the park too. The dog is big and brown. He does not like the trumpet. He thinks it is noisy and scary. He runs to Ben and tries to bite his trumpet. Ben is scared and sad. He does not want the dog to bite his trumpet. He cries and hugs his mom. His mom tells him it is okay. She says the dog is just curious and does not know better. The dog's owner comes and says sorry. He says his dog is playful and friendly, but sometimes he does not listen. He gives Ben a treat and a pat. He tells him his dog likes to play fetch and tug. Ben smiles and wipes his tears. He says he likes to play too. He shares his treat with the dog and throws a stick for him. The dog chases the stick and brings it back. He wags his tail and licks Ben's face. Ben laughs and blows his trumpet again. The dog barks and runs around. They play together in the park. They are playful and happy.
