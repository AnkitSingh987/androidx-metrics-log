Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Furry Cat and the Dog

One day, a furry cat named Tom went to the subway. He wanted to give his friend a toy. His friend was a little dog named Max. The furry cat walked very fast to get to the subway. When Tom got to the subway, he saw Max. Tom said, "Hi Max! I have a toy for you!" Max was very happy. He wagged his tail and said, "Thank you, Tom! I love toys!" Tom and Max played with the toy on the subway. They laughed and had lots of fun. The furry cat and the little dog were best friends. They loved to give each other toys and play together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a favorite shoe that he loved very much. The shoe was old and had a hole in it. Tim's mom wanted to throw it away, but Tim didn't want to let it go. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw the old shoe and said, "Why do you keep that old shoe?" Tim replied, "I love this shoe. It's my favorite." Sue didn't know why Tim loved the shoe so much, but she wanted to be his friend. Tim and Sue played together every day. They had lots of fun, and Sue learned to love the old shoe too. One day, Sue gave Tim a big hug and said, "I'm happy we're friends." Tim hugged her back and said, "Me too. And I'm glad you like my shoe." They laughed and continued to play, happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tweety. He had an orange beak and orange wings, and he always liked to guess what was around him. One day he was flying near a big tree. He saw a bright orange thing moving inside one of the tree's leaves. He used his beak to peep inside and suprised himself when he saw that it was a bright orange caterpillar. Tweety couldn't guess what it was and he was scared. The caterpillar seemed to be stuck in the tree, so Tweety decided to help him. He flew around the tree, looking for a way to help, but he couldn't find any. He was feeling sad, when he noticed a thin orange string coming out of the caterpillar's mouth. He followed the string, up the tree and down. He guessed it was a way to get out of the tree. Tweety flew on top of the tree and with his beak, he untied the string from a branch. Then he flew back down, and the orange string floated with him. The caterpillar came out of the tree too, and soon Tweety and the caterpillar were back on the ground. Tweety had helped the caterpillar and he felt very happy. He said goodbye to the caterpillar and they both flew away, into the bright orange sky.

Once upon a time, there was a warm, sunny day. A little boy named Tim went to play outside. He liked to dig in the dirt with his toy shovel. One day, while he was digging, he found a treasure! It was a shiny, pretty box. Tim wanted to change the treasure into something even more fun. So, he took the box to his mommy. She helped him open it. Inside, they found many colorful stones. Tim was very happy with his new treasure. He decided to share his treasure with his friends. They all played together with the colorful stones. The warm sun shined down on them as they laughed and played. The treasure changed their day into a fun adventure. And they all had a great time.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and laugh. They saw some flowers and bees and butterflies. They felt the sun on their skin. It was very hot. "Let's go inside and get some water," Lily said. "I'm thirsty." "Me too," Ben said. They ran to the door and opened it. But they saw something that made them stop. There was a big cake on the table. It had candles and frosting and sprinkles. It smelled yummy. "Wow!" Lily said. "What is this?" "It's a surprise!" Mommy said. She came out of the kitchen with a smile. "It's your birthday, Lily! Happy birthday, my sweet girl!" Lily gasped. She had forgotten it was her birthday. She felt happy and excited. She hugged Mommy and said, "Thank you, Mommy! I love you!" "I love you too, Lily," Mommy said. "And I love you, Ben. You helped me make the cake. Do you want to sing happy birthday to Lily?" "Yes, Mommy!" Ben said. He loved his sister very much. He wanted to make her happy. He took her hand and said, "Come on, Lily. Let's blow the candles and make a wish." Lily and Ben went to the table. They sang happy birthday with Mommy. They blew the candles and made a wish. They cut the cake and ate some. It was delicious. They felt full and warm and peaceful. They had a wonderful birthday surprise.
