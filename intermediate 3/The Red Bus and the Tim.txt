Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Bus and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a big red bus. The bus liked to help people go to different places. One day, a little girl named Amy needed to find her lost toy. So, she went on the bus to search for it. On the bus, Amy met a rude boy named Tim. Tim was always loud and did not listen to others. He wanted to find the toy first and keep it for himself. Amy did not like how Tim acted, but she still wanted to search for her toy. As they looked around, they found the toy under a seat. Amy was so happy! Tim felt bad for being rude and said sorry. They both learned that it is better to be kind and helpful. From that day on, Tim was not rude anymore, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a green bird. The bird liked to whistle all day. One day, the bird found a soft cushion on the ground. It was a nice place to rest. The bird said to his friend, the cat, "I found a cushion! It is soft and nice. Come see!" The cat went with the bird to see the cushion. They both sat on it and felt happy. But, the cushion was not just a cushion. It was a big turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, I am a turtle, not a cushion!" The bird and the cat were surprised. They said sorry to the turtle. The turtle smiled and said, "It's okay. Just remember to not sit on others without asking." From that day, the bird and the cat always asked before sitting on something new. They learned to be kind and respect others.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a helmet that he loved to wear. He wore it all the time, even to bed. One day, he lost his helmet and he was very sad. He looked for it everywhere, but he could not find it. Tim had a friend named Max. Max saw that Tim was sad and wanted to help. Max asked Tim, "Why are you sad?" Tim said, "I lost my helmet and I can't find it." Max wanted to make his friend happy, so he said, "Let's look for it together!" Tim and Max looked for the helmet all day. They looked in the house, in the yard, and in the park. Finally, they found the helmet under a big tree. Tim was so happy that he could not speak. He put on his helmet and smiled. Tim thanked Max for helping him find his helmet. From that day on, Tim and Max were the best of friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to play. He went to his room to find his favorite toy. Tim looked all around but could not find it. He saw a hanger on the floor and picked it up to examine it. As Tim looked at the hanger, he heard a small voice say, "Hello!" Tim looked around and saw a tiny bug on the hanger. The bug said, "I am Bug, can you help me find my home?" Tim was surprised but wanted to help his new friend. Tim and Bug went outside to search for Bug's home. They looked under rocks, in the grass, and on trees. Then, something unexpected happened. The hanger began to glow and turned into a magic wand! Tim waved the wand, and Bug's home appeared. Bug was so happy and thanked Tim. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there lived two sisters. One of them was called Mellie and the other one was called Tilly. Mellie and Tilly wanted to make a cake for the family. Tilly had a bag of dough which she wanted to use for the cake but Mellie had other ideas. Mellie said to Tilly, “Let’s use the dough to make something more fun than a cake; I’ve got a crazy idea!” Tilly replied “What? A crazy idea? What is it?” Mellie said, “Let’s make our own train with the dough. We can use it to play a game.” Tilly was excited, “Yes! That’s a great idea! Let’s make our own train!” So they rolled out the dough, shaped it into a train and placed it on the table. They then used the dough to make station stops and crazy tunnels. Before they knew it, the sisters owned an incredible train made from dough.
