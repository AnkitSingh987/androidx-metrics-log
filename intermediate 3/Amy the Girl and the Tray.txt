Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Tray

One day, a little girl named Amy had an important job to do. She had to put all her toys away in a tray. She wanted the room to be clean for her mom. Amy started to put her toys in the tray, but there were too many toys. Amy's friend, Tim, came over and saw the tray. He said, "Amy, I can help you fit all the toys in the tray." Amy was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They started to put the toys in the tray together. Tim had a good idea. He put the big toys on the bottom and the small toys on the top. Soon, all the toys fit in the tray. Amy was so happy! Her mom came in and saw the clean room. She said, "Good job, Amy and Tim!" They both felt proud of their important job.

Lily was a popular girl in her class. She had many friends who liked to play with her. She was good at drawing and singing. She always had a folder with her pictures and songs. One day, Lily brought her folder to school. She wanted to show her friends her new picture of a rainbow. She put her folder on her desk and went to the bathroom. While she was away, Ben saw her folder. He was curious. He wanted to look inside. He did not ask Lily. He opened her folder and took out her picture. He did not like it. He thought it was boring. He crumpled it and threw it in the trash. When Lily came back, she saw her folder was open. She looked inside and saw her picture was gone. She was sad. She asked her friends, "Who took my picture?" They did not know. They helped her look for it. They found it in the trash. It was wrinkled and dirty. Lily was angry. She cried, "Who did this to my picture?" Ben heard her. He felt bad. He knew he did a bad thing. He went to Lily and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I took your picture. I did not like it. I threw it away. I was wrong. Please forgive me." Lily looked at him. She saw he was sorry. She said, "Thank you for telling me, Ben. I forgive you. But please don't do that again. It's not nice. My picture is important to me. It's my work. You should ask before you look at someone's folder." Ben nodded. He said, "You're right, Lily. I'm sorry. I won't do that again. Can I help you fix your picture?" Lily smiled. She said, "Yes, you can. Let's go to the art room. Maybe we can make a new picture together." They went to the art room. They found some paper and crayons. They made a new picture of a rainbow. They put it in Lily's folder. They became friends. They learned to respect each other's work. They shared their pictures and songs with their other friends. They had fun.

Once upon a time there was an ordinary pirate. He owned his own ship, and he liked to sail around the seas looking for adventure. One day, he spotted a small island in the distance. He decided to sail close and take a look. He was sure he had never seen the island before and he was intrigued. As he got closer to the island, he noticed something strange. There was a voice coming from the island calling out for help. He decided to investigate and rowed his small dinghy to the shore. As he arrived, he noticed that the voice was coming from a little three year old boy. The little boy's ship had gotten stuck on the island and he was very scared. The pirate smiled kindly, and offered to help. The pirate got out of his dinghy and tied it to the shore. He walked over to the little boy and said, “Let’s get your ship back in the water so that you can go home.” The little boy was so relieved and they both worked together to free the ship. After they had done, the pirate smiled and said, “Sometimes asking for help is the normal thing to do. You don’t have to try and do everything by yourself.” The little boy thanked the pirate for his kindness and set off for home, safe and sound.
