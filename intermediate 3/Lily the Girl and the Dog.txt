Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a pretty bracelet. She loved her bracelet very much. One day, Lily saw a scary big dog. The dog was sad because it was lost. Lily wanted to help the dog. She knew that she could deliver it back to its home. Lily took the dog to its house. The dog's family was very happy. They said "thank you" to Lily. They gave her a new bracelet because she was so nice. Lily learned that helping others is good. Even if something is scary, we can still be brave and help. And when we help, we can make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved the color pink. One day, Sue went outside to play. She saw a big, rough rock. The rock was not pink, but she wanted it to be. So, she found some pink paint and painted the rock. The next day, Sue went outside to see her pink rock. But, the rock was not there! Sue was very sad. She looked all around but could not find it. Then, she saw something strange. Her pink rock was now in the sky! It began to rise higher and higher. Sue was very surprised. She did not know that her rock could fly. The pink rock went up and up until it was very high. Then, the rock came back down to Sue. She was so happy to have her pink rock back. From that day on, Sue and her flying pink rock had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat oats for breakfast. She also liked to play games with her friends. Sue was very good at games and always wanted to win. She was competitive. One day, Sue's mom wanted to teach her how to make oats for breakfast. Sue was very excited. She watched her mom cook the oats and listened carefully to the instructions. Sue's mom told her that there was a special secret to making the best oats. The next day, Sue wanted to make oats for her friends. She remembered the special secret her mom taught her. Sue invited her friends over for breakfast. They all ate the oats and played games. Sue won some games, but she also let her friends win too. Sue's friends loved the oats she made. They asked her to teach them how to make the special oats. Sue was happy to share her secret with her friends. They all learned how to make the best oats and enjoyed playing games together. Sue learned that sharing and teaching others was more fun than always trying to win.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They played in the park every day. One day, while playing, Lily saw a pretty image on the ground. It was a drawing of a butterfly. "Look, Spot!" she said, "What a nice picture!" Spot wagged his tail and barked. He liked the image too. But then, it started to rain. The rain washed the image away. Lily was sad. Spot wanted to make her happy. So, he took a stick and drew a new butterfly in the mud. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Spot! You're the best friend ever!" And they both remained happy and played in the park every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods with his dad. They saw a white bunny hopping around. Tim was very excited and said, "Dad, look! A white bunny!" "Yes, Tim, that is a white bunny," his dad replied. "Let's follow it and see where it goes." As they followed the white bunny, they saw a big tree. The tree had a hole in it. Tim asked his dad, "Why does the tree have a hole?" His dad said, "Someone must have used a saw to make the hole. Maybe they wanted to make a home for the bunny." Tim and his dad went back home, happy that they saw a white bunny and a tree with a hole. They had a fun day in the woods together.
