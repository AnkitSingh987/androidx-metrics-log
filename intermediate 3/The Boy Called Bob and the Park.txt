Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Bob and the Park

Once there was a little boy called Bob. He was only three years old but already liked to explore the world. Every day he liked to go out and discover new and amazing things. One day he decided to go to the park. He had his favorite jacket with him. When he arrived at the park, he was amazed! He saw lots and lots of colorful flowers. He started running through the park, enjoying the wonderful day. Suddenly, he stumbled. He fell in the middle of all the flowers. His jacket was all covered in dirt and mud. Bob was very sad and confused. He started crying and didn't know what to do. Then a kind lady came over to him. She smiled kindly at him and said: "Don't worry Bob, everything will be alright." Bob calmed down and he was able to stand up again. The kind lady gave him a handkerchief to clean his jacket. Afterwards, he thanked the kind lady with a big smile. Bob had learned a wonderful lesson that day: always be kind and helpful to others. He was so proud of himself and smiled all the way home.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate little bunny named Bella. Bella loved to play and jump in the forest with her friends. One day, she found a big flower that made her want to sneeze. "Oh no!" said Bella, "I don't want to sneeze and make trouble." But the sneeze was too strong, and Bella sneezed very loud. The sneeze was so loud that it scared her friends away. Bella was sad and wanted her friends to come back. She said, "I'm sorry for the loud sneeze. I didn't mean to scare you." Her friends heard her and came back. They all hugged Bella and said, "It's okay, Bella. We know you didn't mean to make trouble." From that day on, Bella and her friends played together and had lots of fun. They knew that even if they made mistakes, they could always say sorry and be friends again. And Bella learned to be careful of big flowers that made her want to sneeze.

Once upon a time, in a big desert, there was a pretty oasis. In this oasis, there lived a loyal dog named Max. Max liked to wear a red collar. He was always happy and loved to play with his friends. One sunny day, Max and his friends went to play near the oasis. They ran, jumped, and played in the cool water. Max wore his red collar, and it made him feel special. After a fun day, Max and his friends went back to their homes. They were tired but happy. Max's red collar was wet, but he didn't mind. He knew that he was a loyal dog and his family loved him. And every time he wore his red collar, he remembered the fun day at the oasis.

One day, a big, heavy dog named Max was playing in the park. He saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her ball. Max wanted to help her find it. So, they went looking for the ball together. As they were looking, they saw a big man with a mean face. He had Lily's ball! Max felt a threat from the man. He wanted to protect Lily. Max said, "Give the ball back!" The mean man just laughed and said, "No, it's mine now!" Max knew what to do. He kneeled down and picked up a stick. Then, he threw the stick far away. The mean man thought Max wanted to play, so he let go of the ball to get the stick. But plot twist! Max didn't want to play. He grabbed the ball and ran back to Lily. Lily was so happy to have her ball back. But then, something unexpected happened. The mean man came back with the stick, and he was very angry. He took the ball from Lily and Max and said, "You tricked me! Now, you can't have the ball!" The mean man left with the ball, and Lily and Max were sad. They didn't get the ball back, and the day ended badly. But they knew they were brave and tried their best. They hoped they would find another ball to play with soon.
