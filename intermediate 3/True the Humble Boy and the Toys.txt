Category: Intermediate
Title: True the Humble Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a humble boy named True. True liked to play and create things with his toys. He had a lot of fun making new friends with his toys. One day, True wanted to create something big. He asked his mom for help. His mom said, "Let's create a big tower with your blocks!" True was very happy and started to build the tower with his mom. After they built the tower, True felt proud of what they created. He showed his tower to his friends and they all wanted to play with it. True was a humble boy and shared his tower with everyone. They all had a great time playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom loved to relax all day. He had a big, soft armchair that he would sit in. Every day, Tom would look out the window and watch the birds fly by. He wished he could catch one, but he was too relaxed to try. One day, Tom saw a little bird sitting near his armchair. The bird looked so yummy. Tom knew this was his chance. He slowly got up from his armchair and tried to sneak up on the bird. He was very quiet, but the bird saw him coming. The bird flew away, and Tom was sad. Tom went back to his armchair and thought about what happened. He knew he needed to be faster if he wanted to catch a bird. So, every day, Tom practiced running and jumping. He got better and better. One day, Tom saw another bird near his armchair. This time, he was ready. He ran fast and jumped high. Tom caught the bird! He was so happy. From that day on, Tom knew that even a chubby cat could catch a bird if he practiced enough.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys all day. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As Tim climbed the tree, he discovered something new. He found a small model car hiding in the tree. The car was red and shiny. Tim was very happy and excited to find the model car. Tim played with the model car and pretended it was a mighty race car. He raced the car around the park and had lots of fun. His new car was very fast and strong. Tim had a great day at the park with his new toy.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a mighty lion named Leo. Leo was the king of the forest. All the animals liked him because he was very kind. One day, a little bird named Chirpy came to Leo. Chirpy was sad because he could not fly yet. Chirpy said, "Leo, can you help me? I want to fly high in the sky, but I can't do it yet." Leo smiled and said, "Don't worry, Chirpy. I will help you succeed." The next day, Leo and Chirpy went to the highest hill in the forest. Leo told Chirpy to jump off the hill and try to fly. Chirpy was scared, but he trusted Leo. He jumped off the hill, and something unexpected happened. Chirpy did not fly, but he started to bounce! He bounced higher and higher, and soon he was bouncing as high as the trees. All the animals in the forest came to see Chirpy bounce. They were amazed and clapped for him. Chirpy was happy that he could bounce so high. He said, "Thank you, Leo! I may not be able to fly yet, but I can bounce, and that is just as fun!" From that day on, Chirpy became the best bouncer in the forest. And even though he could not fly yet, he knew that with the help of his friend Leo, he would succeed one day.

One day, a soft cat named Fluffy felt very excite. She wanted to play with her ball. She looked for it but could not find it. Fluffy was sad. Then, Fluffy saw a thief take her ball. The thief was a big dog. Fluffy was not scared. She wanted her ball back. Fluffy ran to the dog and said, "Give me my ball!" The dog was nice and gave the ball back. Fluffy was happy and they played together.
