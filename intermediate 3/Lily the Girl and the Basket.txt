Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Basket

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big black dog named Max. They loved to play outside in the sun. One day, they went to a farm to pick strawberries. Lily saw a big strawberry bush. She said, "Max, help me load the basket with strawberries!" Max wagged his tail and helped Lily. They picked many red strawberries and put them in the basket. After they filled the basket, Lily sat down and shared the strawberries with Max. "Yummy!" said Lily. Max barked happily. They had a great day picking strawberries together.

---

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. It was a dark and cloudy day. She saw her friend Tim playing with a ball. Lily had a card in her hand. She wanted to give it to Tim. "Hi Tim!" said Lily. "I have a card for you!" Tim looked at the card and smiled. They both enjoyed playing with the card, even though it was dark outside. Suddenly, the card flew up into the sky! They were surprised. The card turned into a big, bright star. The sky was not dark anymore. They laughed and played under the new star all day.

---

Once upon a time, in a large palace, there lived a king and queen. The palace was very big and had many rooms. One day, the king said to the queen, "Let's go for a walk outside." So, the king and queen walked in the garden. They saw pretty flowers and green trees. They also saw a little cat. The queen said, "Look, a cute cat!" The cat walked with them and played in the grass. After their walk, they went back to the palace. They were happy and tired. The king and queen sat on their big chairs and the cat sat near them. From that day, the cat lived in the palace and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. This mouse was weak and lived in a tiny hole in a big house. The mouse was sad because his hole was broken. One day, the mouse decided to repair his hole. He looked for things to help him. He found some sticks and leaves. The mouse worked very hard to fix his hole. After some time, the mouse repaired his hole. He was so happy! The little weak mouse now had a nice and safe home. The mouse was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball that loved to bounce. It would bounce all day and all night. One day, the ball met a small stitch. The stitch was wild and jumped around a lot. The ball and the stitch became good friends. They played together every day. They would bounce and jump, having lots of fun. But one day, the stitch had an idea. It wanted to see how high they could bounce together. The ball and the stitch started to bounce higher and higher. They went so high that they could see the whole world below them. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The wild stitch got caught on a big bird flying by. The bird took the stitch far, far away. The big red ball was very sad. It tried to bounce high enough to find the stitch, but it could not. The ball and the stitch never saw each other again. And the big red ball never bounced as high or as happy as it did with the wild stitch.

Teddy and Sarah were playing in the park together. Teddy was trying to build a tower but it was very difficult. Every time he tried, his blocks would crash down. Teddy began to feel frustrated. “I can’t do it!” he shouted. Sarah smiled at him. “It’s ok Teddy. Here, let me show you,” she said. Sarah put the blocks in a very quiet way, one next to the other. She put one block on top of the other. Soon Teddy was able to see a big tower in front of him. “Wow!” Teddy said. “It looks great! How did you do that?” Sarah said, “I just stored one block on top of the other! That’s how you do it.” Teddy smiled. With Sarah’s help, he was able to make lots of towers now. He was so proud of himself. The friends had lots of fun playing with the blocks and they stored many towers. They had a great time together.
