Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Family

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a unique dog because he had a big spot on his back. He liked to run and play with his family in the park. One day, Spot's family went to the park to play. Spot was so excited, he ran ahead of everyone. When he stopped, he looked around and could not find his family. Spot was sad and scared. Spot started to look for his family. He looked behind trees and under benches. Then, he heard a sound. It was his family calling his name! Spot ran to them and they all hugged. Spot was happy to find his family and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Mr. Brown. He had a big farm with a lot of animals. One day, Mr. Brown saw a big root in his field. He wanted to pull it out, so he asked his friend, Mr. Green, for help. Mr. Brown said, "Mr. Green, can you help me pull out this big root?" Mr. Green looked at the root and said, "Yes, I will help you." They both tried to pull the root, but it was very hard. Then, Mr. Brown had an idea. He said, "Let's guess how long it will take to pull out the root." Mr. Green said, "I think it will take ten minutes." Mr. Brown said, "I think it will take five minutes." They both pulled and pulled, and after five minutes, the root came out! They were very happy and went to eat some cake.

Mike and Emma were at the store. They wanted to buy some treats for their special picnic. Mike saw a sour candy he liked. He asked the store man, “Can I have one of those?” The store man smiled and said, “Yes, sure. It will be my pleasure to deliver it to you.” When Emma saw the sour candy, she said, “No! I don’t want to eat something so sour! I don’t like it.” Mike thought for a moment, then asked the store man, “Do you have something that’s not so sour?” The store man said, “I have some sweet candy over here. Would you like to try that instead?” Mike smiled and said, “Yes, please! I love sweet candy.” The store man delivered the candy to Mike and Emma. They paid for it, then went outside to enjoy their treats with a smile on their faces.

Once upon a time, there was a nation. This nation was very strong. It had many people. The people were happy and they liked to help each other. In this nation, there was a king. The king was kind and he loved his people. He wanted his people to be happy and strong too. So, he made sure everyone had food, and a place to live, and friends to play with. One day, a big storm came to the nation. The wind blew and the rain fell. The people were scared, but they knew they were strong. They helped each other and worked together to fix what the storm broke. The king was proud of his people, and the nation was strong and happy once again.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Bob and Rob. Bob was a very clever bear and Rob a mischievous fox. Rob was always up to something and Bob was always trying to stop him. One day they were walking through the forest when Rob spotted a buckle. Rob said to Bob, "Let's take it and have some fun!" Bob said, "No, we shouldn't do that, it will destroy it." But Rob was determined and he went take it anyway. Bob tried to stop him but it was too late. Rob took the buckle and ran away. Bob warned Rob that the buckle belonged to someone else and they could get into trouble. Rob replied, "Don't worry, Bob. I'm too clever to get caught!" But Bob knew better and he was sure that Rob would soon learn his lesson. Sure enough, when Rob returned with the buckle, it was broken. Rob had learnt that being too clever can often be destructive.
