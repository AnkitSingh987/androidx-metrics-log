Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Mom

One day, a girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. Lily saw a big, pretty purse. She wanted it very much. Her mom said, "No, we can't buy it today." Lily was sad but she listened to her mom. On their way home, Lily found a small, shiny coin on the ground. She picked it up and put it in her pocket. She thought, "Maybe I can save money to buy the purse." She started to look for more coins and put them in a jar. She was serious about saving money. One day, Lily's mom saw the jar full of coins. She asked Lily, "What is this for?" Lily said, "I am saving for the purse." Her mom smiled and said, "I have a surprise for you." She took out the big, pretty purse from a bag and gave it to Lily. She had bought it for her birthday! Lily was so happy. She said, "Thank you, mom!" and shut the purse tight to keep her coins safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a fragile toy brush. She loved to brush her doll's hair every day. The brush made her doll's hair look very pretty. One night, Amy had a dream. In the dream, she and her doll were in a beautiful garden. They played with the brush and made each other's hair look nice. They laughed and had so much fun together. When Amy woke up, she was happy. She told her mom about the dream. Her mom smiled and gave her a big hug. Amy knew that she and her doll would always be best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little house with a red roof. In the house, there lived a girl named Sally and her dog, Spot. They liked to play together. One day, Sally found a big box. She had a brilliant idea. "I will wrap the box like a present!" she said. So, she got some paper and wrapped the box. Spot watched her and wagged his tail. Sally and Spot went outside to play with the wrapped box. They put it under a tree. Then, they heard a noise from the box. "What's that?" Sally asked. Spot barked. They opened the box and a little kitten jumped out! "Oh, what a surprise!" said Sally. They all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was a happy girl who loved to play. One day, her mom told her that they were going to visit her grandma in a week. Amy was very excited because she loved her grandma. During the week, Amy remembered to mind her mom and dad. She was a good girl and helped with chores around the house. She picked up her toys and put them away. She was very helpful, and her mom and dad were proud of her. Finally, the week was over, and it was time to visit grandma. Amy and her family got in the car and drove to grandma's house. When they arrived, grandma was waiting for them with a big smile. They all had a great day filled with hugs, laughter, and fun. Amy was so happy to see her grandma and couldn't wait to visit her again.

Once there was a mom and a dad who loved to share things with each other. They also loved to share things with their little girl. One day, the dad took the little girl to the park. At the park, there was a big gate. The dad opened the gate and held the little girl's hand as they walked in. Inside the park, the little girl was so excited that she wanted to share everything she saw. But the mom wasn't there. The dad suddenly felt very upset. He wanted to share the park with his little girl and the mom together. He wished the mom had come too. The dad and the little girl kept exploring the park and having fun. After a while, the dad started to feel happier. They shared this special time in the park, just the two of them. When they were ready to go home, the dad closed the gate and they walked away happy.
