Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to play outside and be independent. One day, she went for a walk on a trail in the woods. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. As Mia walked, she saw a big rabbit. The rabbit said, "Hi Mia! Do you want to play with me?" Mia was happy to have a new friend, so she said, "Yes, Mr. Rabbit! Let's play!" While they were playing, Mia saw a shiny thing on the ground. She went to grab it, but the shiny thing moved! It was a magic wand! Mia picked it up, and it made her and Mr. Rabbit fly in the sky. They had a fun day together, and Mia learned that the unexpected can be magical.

Amy was a bossy little girl who lived on a farm. Every day she would go for a walk by the river and look for things to buy. She loved to stop and talk to Mama on the way. One day, Amy was walking with Mama and they stopped to look at some pretty flowers. Suddenly, Amy spotted a toy boat on the other side of the river. She pointed at it and said, "Mama, buy!" Mama looked across the river at the toy boat and said, "No, we can't reach it from here. We would need to cross the river to get it." But Amy was very bossy and had already made up her mind about the toy boat. She stamped her foot and said, "Buy!". Mama smiled and said, "Okay, let's cross the river and buy the toy boat!" They went across the river, bought the toy boat, and then went home. Since then, Amy was very happy to take her toy boat to the river every day and play in it with Mama.

One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his grandma. She lived in a small house near the park. Tim was happy to see her, but he saw that grandma was feeling miserable. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, grandma?" Grandma said, "I am sad because I miss my friends. They moved away and I can't visit them." Tim wanted to help his grandma feel better. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's go to the park and make new friends!" Grandma smiled and they went to the park. They met lots of nice people and had fun. Grandma was not miserable anymore.

One day, a little girl named Sally was playing in the park. She was skipping around the grass and laughing. Suddenly, Sally's mom called out to her, "Sally, it's almost time for the movie!" Sally was so excited that she started to skip even faster. She ran inside and found her dad waiting for her. He said, "Let's go, it's time for the movie!" Sally and her dad hopped into the car. Soon, they arrived at the theater and found their seats. The lights were bright and the popcorn was warm and dry. Sally couldn't wait to start watching the movie. Just as the movie was beginning, Sally's dad leaned over and whispered, "Sally, enjoy the movie, and remember to skip past the scary parts!" Sally smiled and nodded as the movie started to play.

Once upon a time, there was a long belt. It was so long that it could go around a big tree. One day, the belt met a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to imagine things. He had many fun ideas in his head. "Hello, long belt!" said Tim. "Can I play with you?" The belt nodded and said, "Yes, Tim, let's play together!" Tim and the belt had a lot of fun. They played a game where they imagined they were a train. Tim put the belt on the ground and pretended to be the train driver. "Choo choo!" he said as he walked along the belt. Then, they played a game where the belt was a snake. Tim held one end of the belt and moved it like a snake. "Hiss!" said the belt, and they both laughed. At the end of the day, Tim and the long belt were tired. They lay down under the big tree and looked at the sky. Tim said, "Thank you, belt, for playing with me. You are my friend." The belt smiled and said, "You are my friend too, Tim. Let's play again tomorrow!"
