Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a shy little cat named Kitty. Kitty was always hungry but too scared to ask for food. One day, Kitty saw a big bowl of food across the yard. She wanted to eat it, but was too shy to go near it. Kitty blinked her eyes and thought of a plan. She saw a ball near her and decided to push it towards the food. Maybe if the ball hit the food, it would spill and she could eat it without anyone knowing. Kitty pushed the ball, but it went the wrong way. She felt sad. Kitty didn't give up. She tried again and again, each time getting closer to the food. Finally, the ball hit the bowl and the food spilled on the ground. Kitty was so happy! She ate the food and wasn't hungry anymore. From that day on, Kitty learned that she could do anything if she tried hard enough.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the library with her mom. Sue liked the library because it had many books. She wanted to find a book about a mighty hero. Sue asked her mom, "Can you help me find a book?" Sue and her mom looked for a book with a mighty hero. They found a big book with a picture of a strong man on the cover. Sue was very happy. She said, "I want to read this book!" But when Sue opened the book, she saw that the mighty hero was not a strong man. The hero was a tiny ant! The ant was very smart and could use things to save its friends. Sue was surprised but she liked the story. She learned that even small things can be mighty too.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a weird square. She did not know what it was. She wanted to show it to her friends. So, she took it to the park. At the park, Mia saw her friend Tom. Tom was sad because he lost his toy. Mia thought the weird square could make Tom happy. She said, "Look, Tom! I found this weird square. Let's hang it on the tree and play with it." Tom looked at the square and said, "That's not my toy, but it's fun to play with." They hung the weird square on the tree and played with it. But then, another kid named Sam came and said, "That's my square! Give it back!" Mia and Tom did not want to give the square back. They liked playing with it. But they knew it was not nice to keep something that was not theirs. So, they gave the square back to Sam. Sam was happy and said, "Thank you! You can play with it too!" And they all played with the weird square together.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw a big landscape with lots of trees, flowers, and a big hill. Tim was nervous because he had never been there before. Tim said to Spot, "I'm nervous. Let's split up and look around." So Tim went one way and Spot went the other. They looked at all the pretty things in the landscape. After a while, they found each other again. Tim said, "I'm not nervous anymore! This place is fun!" They played together in the landscape until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a dragon who lived in a mountain. He was a big dragon and the people thought he was very scary. One day, the dragon was feeling very sorry because he had done something bad. He wanted to make things better but he didn't know how. The dragon thought and thought until he suddenly had an idea. He decided to reverse everything he had done! He flew around the mountain and reversed all of the bad things he had done. When the dragon was finished, the people were so happy! They thanked the dragon and said he was not scary at all. The dragon was very happy and he went back to his mountain. The end.
