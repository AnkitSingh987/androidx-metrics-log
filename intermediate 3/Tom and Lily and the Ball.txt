Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Ball

Tom and Lily liked to play in the garden. They had a big ball and a small hoop. They tried to throw the ball into the hoop. Sometimes they did it, and sometimes they missed. One day, they saw a hole in the ground. They looked inside and saw a mole. The mole was brown and furry. It had a long nose and small eyes. It was digging in the dirt. "Hello, mole!" said Tom. "Do you want to play with us?" The mole did not answer. It kept digging. It made more holes in the garden. Lily did not like the holes. She said, "The mole is making a mess. It is ruining the flowers. We have to prevent it from digging." "How can we do that?" asked Tom. Lily had an idea. She said, "We can use the ball and the hoop. We can make a high wall around the mole. Then it cannot dig anymore." Tom liked the idea. He said, "Let's do it!" They took the ball and the hoop and put them around the mole. They made a high wall. The mole could not see or dig. It was trapped. The mole was scared and angry. It tried to push the ball and the hoop away. But they were too big and heavy. It could not move them. Tom and Lily were happy. They said, "We prevented the mole from digging. We saved the garden. We are smart and brave." They hugged each other and went to tell their mom. They did not see the mole escape. It found a gap in the wall and ran away. It did not want to play with them again. It wanted to dig in peace.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an adorable pet cat named Fluffy. They loved to play together all day long. One day, Tim found a big box in his room. He had an idea to make a fun game for Fluffy. Tim took some colorful tape and started to make a maze on the floor. He used the tape to make lines and turns. When the maze was ready, Tim called Fluffy. "Fluffy, come here! Let's play a game!" Fluffy came running and looked at the maze. Tim put a small ball at the start of the maze. Fluffy was excited and started to push the ball with her nose. She followed the tape lines, turning left and right. Tim cheered, "Go Fluffy, you can do it!" Fluffy pushed the ball all the way to the end of the maze. Tim clapped and hugged Fluffy. They had so much fun playing the maze game together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a chubby little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his favorite toy, a big round gear. One sunny day, Max went outside to play with his gear in the park. As Max rolled the gear around, he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily saw Max and his gear, and she wanted to play too. "Can I play with you?" asked Lily. Max wagged his tail happily and said, "Yes, let's play together!" Max and Lily took turns rolling the gear and running after it. They laughed and played all day long. When it was time for Lily to go home, Max decided to follow her. They walked together, talking and laughing, until they reached Lily's house. "Thank you for playing with me, Max," said Lily, giving Max a big hug. Max wagged his tail and said, "You're welcome! Let's play again tomorrow!" From that day on, Max and Lily became the best of friends. They played with the gear every day and always had lots of fun together. And Max was always happy to follow Lily home and spend time with his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a sock. He was very lonely and often wished he had friends to play with. One night, he heard a noise outside. He was scared and jumped into the corner. But then he heard a voice. It said, "Hello, Sock! Where are you?". He was still scared, but he saw a little mouse scurrying around. The mouse was hungry and looking for food. So, Sock shared his food with the mouse, and they became friends. From then on, they played together every night and Sock was no longer lonely. The end.
