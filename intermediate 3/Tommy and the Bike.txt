Category: Intermediate
Title: Tommy and the Bike

Little Tommy was so excited, today was the day he was going to learn how to ride a bike! He put on his helmet and clapped his hands with joy. "Today is going to be great!" he shouted. His mom and dad smiled and helped him get on the bike. His dad showed him the pedals and said, "See the pedal, Tommy? You put your feet on them and balance." Tommy carefully put his feet on the edge of the pedals. He started to wobble and his dad grabbed his shoulders to steady him. "It's okay, just balance. You can do it!" Tommy concentrated and finally he was balanced! He felt so great and his mom praised him, saying, "You did it, Tommy! See, you can do it!" With big smiles on their faces, they all watched Tommy pedal forward on his bike. He was so proud of himself and his parents were proud of him, too. And that's how Little Tommy learned how to ride a bike. He had a great day and he was so excited to do it all again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to discover new things. He walked and walked until he found a smelly cave. Tim thought it would be fun to go inside the cave. In the cave, Tim saw a big face on the wall. The face had a big nose and a big mouth. Tim said, "Hello, Mr. Face! Why are you so smelly?" The face did not say anything. Tim was sad that the face did not talk to him. Tim decided to leave the cave. But when he tried to go out, he could not find the way. He was lost. Tim started to cry. He was scared and did not know what to do. The smelly cave was dark and not fun anymore. Tim was stuck in the cave, and it was a bad ending for him.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little town, there lived a cop. The cop liked to keep everything neat and clean. He would say, "I insist on a tidy town!" Every day, he would walk around and make sure everything was in its place. One day, while the cop was walking, he saw a big mess by the park. The cop was not happy. He said, "I insist we clean this up!" So, the cop and the people in the town worked together to make the park tidy again. They picked up all the trash and put it in the trash can. Just when they thought the park was tidy, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the trash out of the trash can! The cop and the people were surprised. They had worked so hard, but now the park was messy again. This time, the cop had a new idea. He said, "Let's put a lid on the trash can!" So, they put a lid on the trash can, and the wind couldn't blow the trash out anymore. The cop and the people were happy, and the town stayed tidy.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She wanted to discover new things. Mia saw a big tree and a small dog. She played with the dog for a while. Then, Mia saw a cop. The cop was bossy. He told Mia to go home. Mia was sad, but she listened to the cop. She walked back to her house. When Mia got home, she found a surprise. The small dog was at her house! The bossy cop had brought the dog to her house to play. Mia was happy and played with the dog all day.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her toy kitchen. She was stirring a big pot of soup. "I'm ready to cook for everyone," she said to her teddy bear. Her mom heard her and smiled. "Lily, do you want to help me cook dinner tonight?" she asked. Lily was excited and said, "Yes, Mommy!" Lily's mom had a big pot on the stove. She let Lily stir the soup. They cooked together and had fun. When it was time to eat, Lily's dad came home from work. He worked at a hospital. He was very tired, but he was happy to see Lily and her mom cooking. "Lily, did you make this?" her dad asked, taking a bite of soup. "Yes, Daddy! I stirred the soup and made it with Mommy," she said proudly. Her dad smiled and said, "It's very good, Lily. Thank you for helping Mommy cook dinner." They all enjoyed their meal, and Lily felt proud of her hard work.
