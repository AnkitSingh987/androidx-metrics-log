Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Coat

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom was very good at finding things. One day, he found a big coat. The coat was so big that it could cover Tom and his friends. Tom was very happy about his new coat. Tom took the coat to his friends. He showed them the coat and they all tried it on. They all had fun playing with the coat. They played hide and seek with the coat. One friend would hide under the coat and the others would try to find them. One day, it was very cold outside. Tom's friends asked him if they could use the coat to keep warm. Tom did not know what to do. He just shrugged and said, "Okay." They all shared the coat and stayed warm together. They were very thankful for the coat and for their friend Tom.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a shy little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to play near the tall pine trees. One sunny day, Ben saw a fun slide near the pine trees. He hopped closer to the slide, excited to play. As Ben was about to slide, he saw a tiny squirrel named Sue. Sue was also shy, but she wanted to play too. Ben asked Sue, "Do you want to slide with me?" Sue nodded her head and smiled. They both climbed up the slide and went down together, laughing and having fun. Suddenly, while they were playing, a big gust of wind blew through the forest. The wind pushed the slide, and it started to move! Ben and Sue held on tight as the slide took them on a wild ride through the forest. They were scared at first, but then they started to enjoy the adventure. In the end, the wind stopped, and the slide came to a stop near a beautiful rainbow. Ben and Sue looked at each other and laughed, happy to have had such an unexpected and fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a big ship. The ship was not new. It was old and cheap. It had a big sail and a small flag. The ship went on the water. One day, the ship saw a little boat. The boat was lost. The ship wanted to help the boat. The ship recognized the boat. The boat was a friend. The ship and the boat went together. They played in the water. They were happy. The cheap ship and the little boat were best friends. They went on many fun trips.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty dress that she loved very much. Her mom told her it was an important dress because it was a gift from her grandma. One sunny day, Lily went to the park with her mom. She wore her important dress. At the park, she saw her friend, Tim. Tim said, "Wow, your dress is so pretty!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim! My grandma gave it to me." As they played, the sky turned dark, and it started to rain. Lily and Tim ran to find shelter under a big tree. Lily was worried about her important dress getting wet. Tim took off his jacket and gave it to Lily to cover her dress. Lily said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." And they waited under the tree until the rain stopped, and they could play again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a good dog and always tried to behave. One day, while playing in the park, he saw a big, scary cat. The cat had a mean look on its face and made Spot feel very scared. Spot went to his friend, a kind bunny named Floppy. He told Floppy about the scary cat. Floppy said, "Don't worry, Spot. I know how to make you feel better. I will give you a massage." So Floppy used his soft paws to gently rub Spot's back. It felt really nice and made Spot forget about the scary cat. The next day, Spot and Floppy went back to the park to play. They saw the scary cat again. But this time, Spot wasn't scared. He remembered how good the massage felt and knew that his friend Floppy would always be there to help him feel better. So, Spot and Floppy played happily in the park, not afraid of the scary cat anymore.
