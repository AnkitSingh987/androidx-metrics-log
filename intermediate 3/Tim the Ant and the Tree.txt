Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Ant and the Tree

One day, a little ant named Tim was very excited. He wanted to jog to the big tree. The big tree had sweet food for ants. Tim told his friend Sam, "Let's jog to the big tree!" Sam said, "Okay, but we must be careful. I heard there is a big bird that likes to eat ants." Tim and Sam started to jog to the big tree. They were happy and excited. They saw the big tree and the sweet food. They forgot about the big bird. Then, the big bird came. It saw Tim and Sam. The big bird was hungry. It ate Tim and Sam. The ants did not get to eat the sweet food.

Once upon a time, there was a little lion named Leo. He lived in a big jungle with his family. Leo had a special gift: he could roar very loud. He would roar to say hello to his friends and to play games with them. One day, Leo found a house in the jungle. Inside the house, there was a lot of furniture. There were chairs, tables, and beds. Leo was so excited! He wanted to play with the furniture. But he had to be careful not to break anything. Leo invited his friends to the house. They all played together and had a lot of fun. They were very careful with the furniture. When it was time to go home, Leo roared a big goodbye to his friends. They all went back to their homes in the jungle, happy and tired from a fun day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Molly. She was only three years old, but she had a very big dream. She wanted to eat a delicious waffle. One day, she asked her mom if she could have a waffle. Her mom smiled and said, "Let me see what we have in the kitchen." Molly followed her mom into the kitchen and saw a big, heavy box sitting on the counter. She asked her mom, "What’s in the box?" Her mom opened the box and revealed delicious, golden waffles. Molly's eyes lit up and she couldn't believe it. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you for making my dream come true!" Molly and her mom enjoyed the waffles together and Molly had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy had a big egg in his nest. He did not know what was inside the egg. He was very excited to find out. One day, a big wind came. It blew the egg out of the nest. "Oh no!" said Billy. He flew down to find his egg. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. Billy was very sad. Just then, a kind turtle named Timmy found the egg. He knew it was Billy's egg. Timmy called out, "Billy, I found your egg!" Billy was so grateful to Timmy. He said, "Thank you, Timmy!" Together, they put the egg back in the nest. Soon, the egg cracked open, and a baby bird came out. Billy was so happy, and he knew he had a new friend in Timmy.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and little brother, Tim. Lily loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, while helping her mom cook, Lily saw a pretty cup on the shelf. Lily wanted to see the cup up close, so she tried to reach it. But she was too small, and the cup fell down. The cup broke into pieces, and Lily felt sad. Her mom said not to worry, they could heal the cup with glue. Lily and her mom worked together to fix the cup. When they were done, the cup looked like new. Lily's mom put it back on the shelf, but this time she put it on a lower shelf so Lily could see it. The next day, Lily's little brother Tim saw the pretty cup on the shelf. He wanted to touch it too, but he was too small. He tried to reach it, but the cup fell down again. This time, it didn't break. Lily and Tim were so surprised! The cup had changed into a soft, unbreakable cup. The glue they used to heal the cup had made it special. Now, Lily and Tim could both enjoy the pretty cup without worrying about breaking it again.
