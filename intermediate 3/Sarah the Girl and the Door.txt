Category: Intermediate
Title: Sarah the Girl and the Door

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sarah. She loved to explore the world around her and learn new things every day. Today Sarah decided to explore her mum's room. She opened the door and saw drawers full of special perfumes and colourful make-up. When Sarah came closer she could smell the perfume. "Wow, this smells nice!" she said. She wanted to try it, but her mum said: "No, Sarah! Be careful, those perfumes are not healthy for you". Sarah nodded and said: "Ok, I will stop". But then she saw a small bottle that said "Healthy Perfume". She looked at her mum and smiled: "Can I use this one? It says healthy - can I try it?" Her mum smiled and said: "Yes, this one is safe. But only a tiny bit!" Sarah nodded happily and sprayed a little bit of the healthy perfume. The smell was so good. Sarah smiled and said: "Thank you mummy! I love your healthy perfume". Her mum nodded and said: "You're welcome honey". And that was the end of the little girl's adventure in her mum's room.

Once there was a little girl and she was very sad. Everywhere she went, she felt the sadness in her heart. Every night, she would sit and weep and weep until she fell asleep. One day, her auntie came to visit. She had something special for the little girl, a beautiful veil! It was white and sparkly and the little girl loved it. She covered her head with it and it made her feel so happy. But not for long. Suddenly, she heard loud voices coming from outside. It was her relatives, all arguing and shouting. They were so loud and it made the little girl sad again. Even with the veil on, she couldn't stop the tears. She didn't want her auntie to see her weep, so she ran away. She ran until she found a quiet spot in the garden. She was out of sight, but she still heard the loud voices. That's when the veil worked its magic. It filled her heart with calm and with peace. Since then, the little girl has never stopped wearing her magical veil. When the loud voices come, it's like they can't make it past the veil!

Sara liked to draw with ink. She had a big jar of ink and a thin stick to poke on paper. She made many pictures of animals, flowers and stars. She was very happy. One day, her brother Tom came into her room. He saw the jar of ink and the stick. He wanted to play with them. He did not ask Sara. He took the jar and the stick and ran away. Sara was very angry. She ran after Tom and shouted, "Give me back my ink and stick! They are mine!" Tom did not listen. He laughed and ran faster. He ran into the kitchen, where Mom was cooking. He did not see the pot of soup on the stove. He bumped into it and spilled the soup. The soup was very hot. It burned his hand. He dropped the jar and the stick. The ink splashed everywhere. It stained the floor, the walls and the curtains. It was a big mess. Mom was very mad. She scolded Tom and told him to clean up the ink. She took him to the sink and put cold water on his hand. She said, "You are very naughty. You should not take things that are not yours. You should say sorry to Sara and help her with her pictures." Tom felt very sorry. He said sorry to Mom and Sara. He helped Sara clean up the ink. He gave her back her jar and stick. He said, "I will not take your ink and stick again. They are fine for drawing. You are very good at drawing. Can I draw with you?" Sara smiled and said, "Yes, you can draw with me. But you have to ask me first. And you have to be careful with the ink and the stick. They can make a big mess." They hugged and went to Sara's room. They drew together with the ink and the stick. They made many pictures of animals, flowers and stars. They were very happy.
