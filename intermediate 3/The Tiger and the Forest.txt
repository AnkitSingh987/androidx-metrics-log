Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tiger and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a big tiger. The tiger lived in a green forest. He liked to run, jump, and play with his friends. The tiger was very patient when he played games with his friends. One day, the tiger and his friends wanted to do a race. They all lined up to start. The tiger took a deep breath and waited. When it was time to go, the tiger ran as fast as he could. His friends ran fast too. The tiger was patient and did not give up. He kept running and running. In the end, the tiger won the race. All his friends clapped and cheered for him. The tiger was happy and proud. He knew that being patient helped him win the race.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had a big house and many toys. He liked to play with his toys all day. One day, he saw a small girl outside. She was sad because she had no toys to play with. The wealthy man wanted to help the girl. He gave her a toy from his hand. The girl was very happy. She played with the toy all day. She did not feel sad anymore. One day, the girl saw a big ball rolling to the street. She wanted to prevent the ball from going too far. She ran and stopped the ball with her hand. The wealthy man was proud of the girl. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a unique little town, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to twist and turn when she danced. She danced all day and all night, and everyone in the town clapped and smiled. One day, Sue started to worry. She thought that maybe she was not good at dancing. She felt sad and did not want to dance anymore. Her friends saw her worry and wanted to help. Her friends came up with a plan. They all got together and made a big dance party just for Sue. They all twisted and turned, and Sue saw how much fun they had. She joined in and danced with her friends. Sue was happy again, and her worry went away. The unique little town was full of joy and dancing once more.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby little boy named Tim. He loved to play and pretend to be a big hero. Tim had a big book of history that his mommy read to him every night. The stories of brave knights and big castles made him happy. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mommy. He took his toy sword and shield to pretend to be a brave knight. As he was playing, he saw a little girl crying. He went to her and asked, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I lost my toy bunny." Tim decided to help her. He pretended to be a brave knight on a big quest. They looked everywhere for the toy bunny. Then, they saw a squirrel holding the bunny in its mouth! The squirrel ran up a tree, and the little girl cried even more. But then, something unexpected happened. The squirrel dropped the toy bunny from the tree. Tim caught it with his shield and gave it back to the little girl. She stopped crying and hugged her toy bunny. She said, "Thank you, brave knight!" From that day on, Tim and the little girl became best friends. They loved to play and pretend together. And Tim learned that even chubby little boys can be heroes, just like the knights in his history book.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to her friend Tom's house to play. They loved to joke and laugh together. Tom showed Amy his new toy, a sweet little doll with a pretty dress. Amy liked it very much. "Let's have a tea party!" said Tom. They took out their toy cups and plates. Tom filled a glass with water and pretended it was tea. They laughed and joked as they drank their pretend tea. Suddenly, Amy's hand bumped the glass and it fell to the floor. Crash! The glass broke into many pieces. Amy felt sad and scared. Tom's mom came into the room and saw the broken glass. She was not angry, but she told them, "Be careful when you play, my dear children." Amy and Tom learned that they should be more careful when playing with things that can break. They promised to take care of their toys and be more careful next time. The moral of the story is to be careful and responsible when playing.
