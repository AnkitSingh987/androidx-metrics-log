Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because summer was here. Summer was amazing because he could play outside all day. One day, Tim and his friend Sam were playing in the park. They were having so much fun that they did not want to stop. Then, Tim's mom called him. "Tim, it's time to go home!" she said. Tim did not want to go, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. As they walked home, Tim realized he forgot his toy car at the park. He was very sad. His mom saw his sad face and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim told her about the toy car. His mom said, "Don't worry, we'll go back and find it tomorrow." The next day, Tim and his mom went back to the park. They looked everywhere for the toy car, and finally, they found it! Tim was so happy, and he promised to never forget his toys again. The rest of the summer was amazing, and Tim and Sam played together every day.

One day, a kind man named Tom saw a hurt bird. The bird had a spike in its wing. Tom wanted to help the bird, so he said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will repair your wing." Tom took the spike out of the bird's wing. The bird said, "Thank you, Tom. I feel better now." Tom was happy to help the bird. They became good friends and played together every day. One day, the bird told Tom a secret. "Tom, I am not just a bird. I am a magic bird!" The bird changed into a big, beautiful bird with many colors. Tom was surprised and happy. The magic bird took Tom for a ride in the sky, and they had more fun than ever before.

Once upon a time, there was a famous band. They played music and sang songs. They had many friends who loved to listen to them. One day, the band went to a big park to play for all the kids. As they played, a little cat came to the park. The cat stared at the band. The band stopped playing and stared back at the cat. The kids started to laugh. They thought it was funny that the cat and the band were staring at each other. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat started to sing! The band was so surprised. They never knew a cat could sing. The band played music and the cat sang along. Everyone clapped and danced. The cat became a part of the famous band, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small island. On the island, there was a big tree. One day, a baby bird fell from the tree. The baby bird was helpless. It could not fly. A kind boy saw the baby bird. He wanted to help it. He waved to his friend. His friend came and saw the baby bird too. They both thought of a plan to help the baby bird go back to its nest. The boy and his friend found a long stick. They thought they could lift the baby bird with the stick. But when they tried, something unexpected happened. The baby bird grew big wings! It was not a baby bird anymore. It was a big, beautiful bird. The big bird flew back to its nest in the tree. The boy and his friend were very happy. They waved goodbye to the big bird. The big bird waved its wings back at them. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Tom were playing with their toy cars in the living room. They liked to make them zoom and crash and honk. But they did not like to clean up their mess. They left the cars all over the floor and the sofa and the table. "Time to tidy up, kids!" Mom called from the kitchen. "Dinner is ready soon!" Lily and Tom groaned. They did not want to stop playing. They wanted to keep having fun. "Can we play a little more, Mom?" Lily asked. "No, you can't. You have to tidy up now. It's not fair to leave your toys everywhere. Someone could trip and get hurt. And it's not nice to be messy. You have to respect your home and your things." Mom said. Lily and Tom knew Mom was right, but they still felt lazy. They decided to play a trick on Mom. They took their toy cars and put them in reverse. They made them go backwards and hide under the couch and the chairs and the pillows. They thought Mom would not see them and they could play with them later. "Look, Mom, we tidied up!" Tom said, pretending to be proud. Mom came to the living room and looked around. She saw that the floor was clear, but she also saw something strange. She saw little wheels and bumpers and headlights poking out from under the furniture. She realized what Lily and Tom had done. "That's not tidy, that's sneaky!" Mom said, frowning. "You did not put your cars away, you just reversed them and hid them. That's not how you tidy up. That's how you make more trouble. You have to take your cars out and put them in the toy box. And you have to apologize for lying to me." Lily and Tom felt ashamed. They knew Mom was angry and disappointed. They did not like to make Mom feel that way. They took their cars out and put them in the toy box. They said sorry to Mom and gave her a hug. "We're sorry, Mom. We won't reverse our cars and hide them again. We'll tidy up properly next time." Lily and Tom said. Mom smiled and hugged them back. She was glad they learned their lesson. "That's good, kids. I'm proud of you for being honest and responsible. Now you can have dinner and maybe play a little more later. But remember, always tidy up after yourself. It's the right thing to do." Mom said. The end. Moral value: The story teaches the value of honesty, responsibility and respect. It shows that lying and hiding things is not a good way to avoid chores, and that it can hurt the feelings of others. It also shows that tidying up is important for safety and order, and that it can make the home and the toys more enjoyable.
