Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Smart Cat and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tim. Tim loved to sit on a big hill and look at the view. He could see trees, birds, and people playing. One day, Tim saw a sack near the hill. He was curious and wanted to see what was inside. So, he used his paw to open the sack. Inside, he found a lot of colorful balls. Tim took one ball out and said, "Wow, look at this ball! It's so pretty!" He played with the ball all day long, rolling it up and down the hill. Other animals saw Tim having fun and joined him too. They all played together and enjoyed the beautiful view from the hill.

Once upon a time, there was a wild cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with strings and tie them into knots. One day, Tom found a big string in the bathroom. He wanted to play with it and tie it around the tub. While Tom was playing, his friend, a little mouse named Jerry, came to see him. Jerry saw the big string and thought it would be fun to play with, too. They played together and tied the string around the bathroom. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. It was Tom's mom, who was looking for the big string. She needed it to hang clothes outside. Tom and Jerry were scared. They quickly untied the string and gave it to Tom's mom. Tom's mom was happy to get the string back. She thanked Tom and Jerry for their help. They learned that it is important to ask before taking things that do not belong to them. From that day on, Tom and Jerry always asked before playing with something new.

One day, a girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a swing and ran to play on it. The swing was icy, but Mia did not know. Mia sat on the swing and said, "Brrr! This swing is cold!" A boy named Tim came to the park too. He saw Mia on the icy swing and said, "Mia, be careful! The swing is icy!" Mia and Tim played on the swings together, going up and down, up and down. All of a sudden, a big, warm sun came out from behind a cloud. The sun saw the icy swing and said, "I can help!" The sun shined and shined, and the icy swing became warm. Mia and Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, sun!" They kept playing on the swing, happy and warm.

Once upon a time, there was a polite bear. He was very quiet and he liked to keep to himself. One day, he decided to go for a walk. He walked through the woods until he reached a lake. He walked to the edge and was so excited to see the water. He wanted to try something new, so he leaned over and dropped a pebble into the lake. As it made a 'plop', the water rippled and the bear smiled. He dropped two more pebbles into the lake, and the ripples got bigger. He enjoyed watching them but then realized it was getting late. He waved goodbye and walked away, being sure to be extra polite. As he wandered back to his home in the woods, he could feel the quiet calm of the night.

Once upon a time, in a very tidy town, there was a little school. In this school, there were many kids who loved to play and learn. They had a very nice teacher named Miss Jane. One day, a little boy named Timmy wanted to play with a toy car. He saw that his friend, Billy, had the toy car he wanted. Timmy went to Billy and said, "Can I please play with the toy car?" This was Timmy's request. Billy smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with the toy car." After playing with the toy car, Timmy and Billy decided to help keep their school tidy. They picked up all the toys and put them away. Miss Jane saw what they were doing and said, "Thank you for keeping our school tidy, Timmy and Billy. You are very good helpers." Timmy and Billy smiled, happy to help their teacher and their school.
