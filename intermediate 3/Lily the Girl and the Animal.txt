Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Animal

Once there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to bake with her mom. One day, she decided to go for a walk in nature. She wanted to find new things to bake with. As Lily walked, she met an unknown animal. The animal was big and furry. "Hello," said Lily. "Do you want to bake with me?" The animal nodded and smiled. They went back to Lily's house and started to bake. But the animal was not nice. It made a big mess and broke things. Lily was sad. In the end, the animal left, and Lily had to clean up the mess all by herself.

Once upon a time, there were two brothers who were best friends. One day, their mom told them to go for a track in the park. They happily agreed and ran off to find an exciting track. But then, something strange happened! The brothers started to behave badly. They started to run and shout and didn't listen to their mom. Not only that, but they even started pushing each other around. Their mom was very disappointed in them and she told them to go home if they couldn't behave better. But then, she noticed a cheap old track in the park nearby and suggested they try it out. The brothers were so excited! They forgot all about misbehaving and just strode down to the track, ready to give it a try. From then on, the brothers started behaving much better and even went to the park almost every day to play on the track. And they even started to pay attention to their mom - she was very proud of them! The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big castle. In the castle, there lived a nice king and a kind queen. They had a little girl. The little girl loved to play in the castle. She felt safe and happy there. One day, the little girl found a small cat. The cat was scared and tried to slap her with its paw. The little girl was sad. She wanted to be friends with the cat. So, she gave the cat some food and a soft bed. The cat felt safe too. After some days, the cat and the little girl became best friends. They played together in the castle. The cat never tried to slap her again. The little girl learned that if you are kind to others, they will be kind to you too. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, Tom and Sue were playing with shapes. They had a box full of different shapes like circles, squares, and triangles. They liked to build things with them. Tom was good at making towers, and Sue liked to make houses. As they were playing, a little bird flew in through the window. The bird was ignorant and didn't know how to find its way back outside. Tom and Sue wanted to help the bird, so they hurried to make a plan. Tom said, "Let's use our shapes to make a path for the bird to follow." Sue agreed, and they started to put the shapes on the floor. The bird saw the path and began to follow it. Just as it was about to reach the window, the shapes turned into real houses and towers! Tom and Sue were surprised, but they were happy that their shapes helped the bird find its way out. They learned that even simple things can make a big difference.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim was very proud because he was now a big boy and could ride the big slide. At the park, Tim saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she could not find a seat on the swings. Tim said, "Don't be sad, Sue. I will find you a seat." Tim looked around and saw an empty seat on the swings. But, there was a big puddle in front of it. Tim knew he had to delay Sue from sitting on the swing until he could find a way to help her. Tim thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He found a big stick and pushed the puddle away from the swing. Sue was so happy that she could now sit on the swing without getting wet. She said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Tim felt very proud that he could help Sue. They played at the park all day, and Tim knew he would always be there to help his friends.
