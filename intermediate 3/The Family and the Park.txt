Category: Intermediate
Title: The Family and the Park

Once upon a time there was a family that went on a picnic in the park. It was a beautiful day. The family was so excited to explore the park and have fun. The mom provided a pink picnic basket full of yummy snacks and drinks. The little girl was so delighted that she wanted to share with the birds that were around. Right when the family was finishing with their picnic, some sunshines appeared. They were so bright and colorful. One of them even had a pink bow! The family went closer to take a better look. To their surprise, the sunshines had come to join them for their picnic. They shared everything the family had. It was so much fun! That day, the family learned that with a little bit of love, anything is possible. Even a picnic with sunshines!

---

One day, a girl named Lucy wanted to go on an adventure. She had a map to help her find her way. The map showed her where to go. It had a big red "X" on it. Lucy knew that the "X" was where she would find something fun. Lucy's friend, Tom, wanted to help her. He said, "I will encourage you on your adventure!" So, they started to walk together. They looked at the map and followed the path. They saw trees, flowers, and birds. They were very happy. At last, they found the big red "X". There was a big box with a ribbon on it. They opened the box and found many toys inside. They played with the toys all day. Lucy and Tom were successful in their adventure. They had lots of fun and were very proud of themselves.

---

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big red truck. The truck has a name. It is a fire truck. The fire truck makes a loud noise. It goes to help people. Tom and Sue run after the fire truck. They want to see what it does. They see a house with smoke. The smoke is black and bad. The fire truck stops near the house. The fire truck has a hose. The hose has water. The water is good. The fire truck has people. The people have a name. They are firefighters. The firefighters wear red hats and yellow coats. The firefighters use the hose to spray water on the house. The water makes the smoke go away. The water also makes the heat go away. The heat is from the fire. The fire is red and bad. Tom and Sue watch the firefighters. They think they are brave and kind. They want to be like them. They clap and cheer for the firefighters. The firefighters see Tom and Sue. They smile and wave at them. They give them stickers. The stickers have a name. They are fire badges. Tom and Sue are happy. They say thank you to the firefighters. They put the stickers on their shirts. They pretend to be firefighters. They go back to the park. They play with the big red truck. They have fun.

Anna and Ben were playing in the living room. They liked to pretend they were superheroes and fight bad guys. Anna had a cape and Ben had a mask. They ran around and made loud noises. Suddenly, they heard a scream from the kitchen. It was their mom. She sounded frightened. Anna and Ben stopped playing and ran to the kitchen. They saw their mom holding a phone and looking scared. "Mom, what's wrong?" Anna asked. "Someone called me and said they have your dad. They want money or they will hurt him. I don't know what to do." Mom said. Anna and Ben felt scared too. They loved their dad and didn't want him to be hurt. They hugged their mom and tried to make her feel better. "Mom, don't worry. We will help you. We are superheroes, remember?" Ben said. "Yes, we are. We will find dad and save him. We are brave and strong." Anna said. They looked at the phone and saw a number. They decided to call it and talk to the bad guys. They hoped they could trick them and find out where their dad was. They picked up the phone and pressed the number. "Hello, who is this?" a mean voice said. "Hello, we are Anna and Ben. We are superheroes. We know you have our dad. You better let him go or we will come and get you." Anna said. The bad guy laughed. He thought Anna and Ben were silly and cute. He didn't think they were superheroes. He didn't think they could do anything. "Really? You are superheroes? That's funny. How are you going to save your dad? Do you have any powers?" the bad guy said. "Yes, we do. We have super speed, super strength, and super smarts. We can find you and stop you. We know where you are. You are in the old house by the park. We are coming for you." Ben said. The bad guy was surprised. He didn't know how Anna and Ben knew where he was. He was scared. He looked out the window and saw two kids in capes and masks running towards the house. He dropped the phone and ran away. Anna and Ben reached the house and saw their dad tied up in a chair. They untied him and hugged him. He was happy and proud of them. He thanked them for saving him. "Wow, you are amazing. You are superheroes. How did you do it?" Dad asked. "We used our powers and our phone. We called the bad guy and tricked him. We told him we knew where he was and we scared him. He ran away and left you here." Anna said. "That's incredible. You are very brave and smart. I love you so much." Dad said. They went back to the kitchen and told their mom what happened. She was relieved and happy. She hugged them and kissed them. She said they were the best superheroes ever. They smiled and agreed. They were a superhero family.
