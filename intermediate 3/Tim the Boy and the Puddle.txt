Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Puddle

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very anxious about going to school. He was scared that he would not make any friends. One day, Tim's mom gave him a big hug and told him that he would make friends soon. At school, Tim saw a big puddle of water. He decided to jump in it and soak his feet. Tim thought it was fun and started to laugh. A girl named Lily saw Tim and wanted to join him. They both jumped in the puddle and soaked their feet together. Just then, they heard a loud noise. It was a big truck that had stopped nearby. The truck driver got out and told them that he was here to fix the gas leak in the school. Tim and Lily felt brave and wanted to help. They found out that the gas leak was under the puddle they were playing in. They had saved the school by finding the gas leak. Tim and Lily became best friends and were not anxious about school anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a modest girl named Ann. She liked to mix colors to paint pretty pictures. Ann had a small rag she used to clean her hands when she was done. One day, Ann found a big, white wall. She wanted to mix her colors and paint the wall to make it beautiful. So, she took her rag and her colors, and she began to paint. Ann mixed red, blue, and yellow. The wall became a bright and happy place. Ann was very proud of her work. Her rag was very messy, but her heart was full of joy. Everyone loved Ann's beautiful wall, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy who was three years old. She was an independent young girl, who liked to do things on her own. One day, when she was too scared to go to school, her parents encouraged her and bought her a new uniform. Lucy was so excited to put it on, it was pink and lovely. The next morning, she was ready to face school. Her mummy encouraged her to say hello to all her friends and teachers when she was there. At school, Lucy felt independent and strong. She showed her uniform to all her friends and they all thought she was really smart. The teacher encouraged Lucy to do her best and she soon found out that she had made the right decision to go to school. She felt so proud and independent and it was all thanks to her parents who encouraged her and bought her the uniform.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big red ball. "This is mine now," said Tim. He played with the ball all day. He had so much fun. Tim heard a girl cry. "Why are you sad?" he asked. The girl said, "That ball is mine. I lost it. I am poor and cannot buy a new one." Tim felt bad. He wanted to make the girl happy. Tim said, "You can have the ball back." The girl stopped crying. She took the ball and ran away. Tim was sad. He missed the ball. But then, the girl came back with a big mean dog. The dog took Tim's toy car. The girl and the dog left, and Tim was left with nothing.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a gifted mole. This mole could dig very fast. He made long tunnels under the ground. His friends, the birds, the bugs, and the rabbits, all liked to play in these tunnels. One day, the mole saw a big hole in the garden. He thought, "I can fill this hole with dirt and make it safe for my friends to play." So, he started to dig and dig, filling the hole with dirt. Soon, the hole was full and safe for everyone. The animals in the garden were very happy. They thanked the mole for his hard work. The mole felt good because he helped his friends. The moral of the story is: when you use your gifts to help others, you make everyone happy.
