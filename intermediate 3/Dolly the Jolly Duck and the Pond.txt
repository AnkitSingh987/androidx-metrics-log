Category: Intermediate
Title: Dolly the Jolly Duck and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a jolly duck named Dolly. She lived near a pond. Dolly loved to splash in the water all day long. She would play with her friends, the fish and the frogs. One day, Dolly found a magazine near the pond. The magazine had big, bright pictures. Dolly liked the colors and the pictures. She showed her friends the magazine, and they looked at it together. Dolly and her friends had a fun day with the magazine. They took turns looking at the pictures and splashing in the water. They were all very happy and had a jolly time. And from that day on, they always played together at the pond.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. Lily had a fancy doll with a pink dress and long hair. Ben had a truck with big wheels and a loud horn. They were having fun until Lily saw Ben's string. "Can I have your string, Ben?" Lily asked. "I want to make a necklace for my doll." "No, it's my string," Ben said. "I need it for my truck. It's a tow rope." "Please, Ben, just a little bit," Lily begged. "Your truck has enough rope. My doll needs a necklace. She will look so pretty." "No, Lily, go away," Ben said. He pulled the string away from Lily and wrapped it around his truck. "This is my string and you can't have it." Lily was angry. She wanted the string very much. She tried to grab it from Ben, but he was stronger. He pushed her away and made a rude noise. Lily felt like crying. She looked around and saw a pair of scissors on the table. She had an idea. She ran to the table and took the scissors. She came back to Ben and said, "If you don't share your string, I will cut it." Ben was scared. He liked his string and he didn't want it to be cut. He tried to resist, but Lily was fast. She snipped the string with the scissors and ran away with a piece of it. She laughed and said, "Now I have some string and you don't." Ben was very sad. He looked at his truck and saw the broken string. He felt like crying too. He wished he had shared his string with Lily. He said, "Lily, you are mean. You broke my string. I don't want to play with you anymore."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat cereal every day. Her favorite cereal was purple and very yummy. One morning, Lily woke up and saw something strange. The sun was shining very bright, but the sky was purple too! Lily went outside to play. She saw her friend Tom. Tom was eating the same purple cereal. They were happy to eat it together. But then, something unexpected happened. The purple cereal began to shine, just like the sun. Lily and Tom looked at each other and smiled. They knew that their purple cereal was magic. It made the sky purple and shiny. They ate more and more cereal, and the sky became more beautiful. They played all day under the purple, shining sky. And from that day on, Lily and Tom's mornings were always full of magic and fun.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a big secret. This secret was very important. He did not want anyone to know about it. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. They became friends. Sue asked Tim, "Do you have any secrets?" Tim said, "Yes, but I can't tell you. It's important." Sue was sad, but she did not ask again. Later, Tim had to sneeze. He sneezed so hard that his secret came out. The secret was that he had a magic toy. The toy could do anything. But when the secret came out, the toy stopped working. Now, Tim and Sue could not play with the magic toy. They were sad, and Tim wished he had kept his secret.

One day, a big tree was in the way. Dad needed to cut it down. He got his axe, ready to chop. But the tree did not want to be cut down. The tree began to shake, trying to scare Dad away. Dad said, "Don't be scared, tree. I need to make room for a new garden." The tree kept shaking, not wanting to be cut down. Dad was sad, but he knew he had to do it. Then, a little bird came and said, "Please don't cut the tree. It is my home." Dad thought for a moment. He decided to let the tree stay. He found a new place for the garden. The tree, Dad, and the little bird were all happy.
