Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in his pond. He would swim all day and play with his friends. Fin was a very happy fish. One day, while swimming, Fin saw a big mushroom near the pond. The mushroom was soft and comfortable. Fin liked the mushroom so much that he wanted to tell his friends about it. Fin and his friends went to see the mushroom. They all sat on the comfortable mushroom and had a fun time. After playing, they went back to swim in the pond. Fin was so happy to share his new find with his friends. They all had a great day together.

One day, a big colorful bird flew into a house. In the house, there was a soft armchair. The bird sat on the armchair. The bird liked the armchair because it was warm and cozy. In the house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim saw the bird on the armchair. He said, "Hello, bird! Why are you in my house?" The bird said, "I need help. I need to supply food for my family. Can you help me find food?" Tim and the bird went outside to find food. They looked and looked, but they could not find any food. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird said, "I have an idea! I can use my colorful feathers to make people happy. They will give us food in return." The bird danced and showed its colorful feathers to people. The people were happy and gave Tim and the bird food. They took the food back to the bird's family. The bird thanked Tim for his help. From that day on, Tim and the bird were best friends.

Once upon a time there was a lazy player. He did not like to do anything. All day he would sleep and not move. One day his friends came to talk to him. They wanted to discuss something. But the lazy player was too sleepy to join in. His friends tried to talk to him but the lazy player just kept sleeping. He did not want to discuss anything. So, the friends went away, but they promised to come back and discuss it another time. The lazy player just snored and kept on sleeping.

Sara and Ben are playing in the park. They like to run and catch each other. Sara is faster than Ben, but Ben is smarter. He knows how to hide and surprise Sara. One day, they see a big slide in the park. Sara wants to try it, but Ben is scared. He thinks it is too high and too fast. Sara says, "Don't be scared, Ben. It is fun. I will go first and show you." Sara climbs up the ladder and sits on the slide. She smiles at Ben and says, "Ready, set, go!" She pushes herself down the slide. She feels the wind in her hair and the sun on her face. She is happy. But then, she sees something at the end of the slide. It is a big dog. It is barking and jumping. It wants to catch Sara. Sara is scared. She screams, "Help, Ben, help!" Ben hears Sara and runs to the slide. He sees the dog and the sight makes him brave. He picks up a stick and waves it at the dog. He shouts, "Go away, dog, go away!" The dog is scared of Ben and the stick. It runs away. Sara slides down and hugs Ben. She says, "Thank you, Ben, thank you. You saved me. You are not scared. You are brave." Ben smiles and says, "You are welcome, Sara, you are welcome. You are brave too. You tried the slide. Maybe I will try it too. But first, let's play catch." They run and catch each other. They are happy.

One day, a girl named Lily went to a party. She wore pretty makeup on her face. At the party, she saw her friend, Tom. They were happy to see each other. Tom said, "Let's dance, Lily!" Lily said, "Yes, I love to dance!" They danced and danced. The music was peaceful and soft. Everyone at the party was having fun. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew Lily's makeup away. Her face was clean now. Lily was sad and surprised. Tom saw her and said, "Don't worry, Lily. You look pretty even without makeup. Let's keep dancing!" They danced and danced until the party was over. Lily learned that she didn't need makeup to have fun and feel pretty.
