Title: Anna and Ben and the Castle

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play with clay. They had many colors of clay, but their favorite was gray. They said gray clay was the best because it had a soft texture and could make any shape. One day, they decided to make a big castle with gray clay. They worked hard and used all their gray clay to build the walls, the towers, and the gate. They were very proud of their castle and put it on the table to dry. But then, they saw that their little brother, Sam, had taken some of their gray clay and made a ball. He was bouncing the ball on the floor and laughing. "Hey, that's our clay!" Anna shouted. "Give it back!" "No, it's mine!" Sam said. "I like the ball!" Ben ran to Sam and tried to grab the ball, but Sam was faster and ran away. Anna and Ben chased him around the house, but they could not catch him. They were angry and sad. Mom saw what was happening and called them to the living room. She asked them why they were fighting over the clay. "Because he took our clay!" Anna and Ben said. "But you have so much clay!" Mom said. "Can't you share with your brother?" "No, we can't!" Anna and Ben said. "We need all the gray clay for our castle!" Mom looked at their castle and smiled. She said it was very beautiful and creative, but she also said that clay was meant to be played with, not to be kept on the table. She told them a story about how she and her sister used to play with clay when they were little. They made different things every day and had fun. They also shared their clay with their friends and learned new ideas. She said that playing with clay was more fun when you share and learn from others. She asked them if they wanted to try that with Sam. She said that maybe they could teach him how to make a castle, or learn how to make a ball. She said that sharing and learning would make them happy and smart. Anna and Ben thought about it and agreed. They said they were sorry to Sam and asked him if he wanted to play with them. Sam was happy and said yes. They went to the table and started to play with the gray clay. They made a bigger castle, a ball, and some other things. They had fun and laughed. They learned that sharing and learning is better than fighting and settling. They also learned that gray clay can make many things, not just one. They thanked Mom for her story and her advice. Mom hugged them and said she was proud of them. She said that gray clay was the best because it had a soft texture and could make any shape, but also because it could make them happy and smart.

Once upon a time, there was a big ostrich named Ollie. Ollie lived in a nice place with many friends. They liked to play together every day. Ollie's friends were a mix of animals, and they all had fun together. Their games always include running, jumping, and laughing. One day, Ollie was upset. He did not want to play with his friends. His best friend, a small bunny named Bella, asked him, "Ollie, why are you upset?" Ollie looked at her and said, "I am too big to play hide and seek. I can't find a good place to hide." Bella thought for a moment and said, "We can play a new game that is fun for everyone. Let's play tag!" All the friends agreed and started to play. Ollie was not upset anymore. He had fun playing tag with his friends. From that day on, Ollie and his friends played many games that include everyone, big or small.

Once upon a time, there was an old chamber in a big house. In this chamber, there were many toys. A little girl named Amy lived in the house. She loved to play with her toys in the chamber. One day, Amy couldn't find her favorite toy, a small red car. She looked everywhere, but she couldn't find it. She asked her big brother, Tom, "Did you take my red car?" Tom said, "No, I didn't take it. Maybe it's in the old chamber." Amy went to the old chamber and saw a big mess. All her toys were all over the floor. She was sad and didn't know what to do. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Let's clean the chamber together and maybe we will find your red car." Amy and her mom cleaned the chamber and put all the toys in their right places. Finally, they found the small red car under a big teddy bear. Amy was so happy and she thanked her mom. From that day on, Amy always kept the old chamber clean and tidy.
