Category: Intermediate
Title: The Modest Pond and the Fish

Once upon a time, in a little town, there was a modest pond. In this pond, there lived a small fish named Fin. Fin was a very happy fish, and he loved to play with his friends. One day, Fin saw a big rock near the pond. He wanted to jump over it, but it was too high. So, Fin decided to try and try again. He swam back and forth, trying to jump higher each time. After many tries, Fin finally jumped over the rock. He was so happy and proud of himself. All his friends cheered for him. From that day on, Fin knew that if he tried hard, he could do anything he wanted.

Lily and Tom were playing in the living room. They liked to look at the pictures on the wall. There were pictures of their family, their pets, and their friends. Lily saw a picture of a big cake with candles. She pointed to it and asked Tom, "What is this?" Tom looked at the picture and said, "That is a birthday cake. It was for Mommy's birthday. We had a party and ate cake and ice cream. It was yummy." Lily smiled and said, "I like cake and ice cream. Can we have some now?" Tom shook his head and said, "No, we can't. Mommy said we have to wait until after dinner. She said cake and ice cream are treats, not snacks." Lily frowned and said, "But I'm hungry. And I want cake and ice cream. They look so good in the picture." Tom said, "Lily, don't be silly. You can't eat the picture. It's not real. It's just a frame with paper inside. You have to wait for Mommy to make a real cake and ice cream." Lily didn't listen to Tom. She was very curious and wanted to taste the picture. She climbed on the couch and reached for the frame. She pulled it off the wall and tried to bite it. But the frame was hard and sharp. It hurt her mouth and made her bleed. She dropped the frame and started to cry. Tom was scared and ran to get Mommy. Mommy came and saw what Lily had done. She was angry and sad. She said, "Lily, what have you done? You broke the frame and hurt yourself. That was a very bad thing to do. You should have listened to Tom. He was right. You can't eat the picture. It's not available. It's only for looking, not for touching. Now you have to go to your room and think about what you did. And no cake and ice cream for you today." Lily felt sorry and ashamed. She wished she had listened to Tom. She wished she had waited for Mommy. She wished she had not been so curious and greedy. She went to her room and cried some more. She learned a hard lesson that day. She learned that some things are not available, and that some questions are better left unanswered.

Once upon a time, there was a pirate. The pirate was old. He had a big boat. The boat was on the sea. The old pirate loved his boat. One day, the old pirate found a toy. The toy was soft. The pirate liked to squeeze the toy. When he squeezed the toy, it made a funny noise. The old pirate laughed. The old pirate and the toy went on many adventures. They found treasure and met new friends. The pirate loved to squeeze the toy when he was happy. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy rabbit named Bob. He loved to hop around the garden and tease his friends. One day, Bob saw his friend Sally the squirrel eating a salad. Bob hopped over to Sally and said, "I can eat a salad faster than you!" Sally looked up and said, "No, you can't!" So, they decided to have a race to see who could eat their salad the fastest. Bob and Sally started eating their salads very fast. All their friends watched and cheered. In the end, Sally finished her salad first. Bob laughed and said, "You win, Sally!" They all played together happily after that.
