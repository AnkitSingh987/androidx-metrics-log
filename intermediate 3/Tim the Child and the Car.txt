Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Child and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a child named Tim. Tim had a broken toy car. He was very sad about it. One day, he went outside to play in the rain. He jumped in puddles and got all wet. Then, the rain stopped and the sun came out. It was time for Tim to go inside and get dry. As Tim walked inside, he met his friend, Sue. Sue saw the broken toy car in Tim's hand. She asked, "Why is your toy car broken?" Tim said, "I don't know. It just broke one day. I am sad." Sue wanted to help Tim feel better. Sue had an idea. She took Tim's broken toy car and fixed it with some glue. She told Tim, "Now, let's wait for the glue to dry." They waited and played with other toys. Soon, the glue was dry, and the toy car was fixed. Tim was so happy, and he thanked Sue. They played with the fixed toy car together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely crayon. The lonely crayon lived in a big box with many other crayons. They were all friends, but sometimes the lonely crayon would forget how to play with them. One day, the lonely crayon wanted to draw a pretty picture. The lonely crayon tried to remember how to draw, but it could not. The lonely crayon felt sad and asked the other crayons for help. The other crayons were kind and helped the lonely crayon remember how to draw. They all drew a big, beautiful picture together. The lonely crayon was happy and never felt lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Amy. She had lots of friends who liked to play together. They had a big network of friends who shared toys and helped each other. One day, Amy met a new boy named Tim. Tim wanted to join the network of friends, but he was shy. He asked Amy, "Can I play with you and your friends?" Amy said, "Yes, you can. But you must be nice and share your toys." Tim was happy to accept the rules. But when they started playing, Tim didn't want to share his toys. The other kids in the network were sad and didn't want to play with Tim anymore. Amy went to Tim and said, "You must share your toys if you want to play with us." Tim thought about it and decided to share his toys with the other kids. Soon, everyone was happy again and Tim became a part of the network. They all played together, shared their toys, and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his bike. His bike had two big pedals. Tim would push the pedals to make the bike go fast. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue did not have a bike. Tim wanted to help Sue. He said, "I will provide you with a bike so we can ride together." Sue was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim!" They both rode their bikes and had lots of fun. They were jolly friends who loved to pedal their bikes together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl named Lily. She loved to go to the park with her mom and dad. One day, they went to the park and saw a big pond. Lily saw something white and pretty in the pond. It was a swan! The swan was swimming in the water. Lily wanted to say hi to the swan. She walked up to the pond and stared at the swan. The swan stared back at her. They looked at each other for a long time. Lily smiled at the swan, and the swan swam closer to her. Lily's mom and dad were happy to see her make a new friend. They took a picture of Lily and the swan. Lily said bye to the swan, and they went home. Lily told all her friends about the brave swan she met at the park. She could not wait to go back and see the swan again.
