Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Doctor

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big party at his house. He wanted to invite all his friends. He also wanted to invite a doctor. Tim thought it would be fun to have a doctor at the party. The doctor came to the party. He was very nice. He wore a white coat and had a big smile. The doctor made the party easy for Tim. He helped Tim's friends when they fell down or got hurt. The doctor made everyone feel better. At the end of the party, Tim and his friends were very happy. They thanked the doctor for coming. The doctor was happy to help. He said, "I had fun at the party too!" Tim knew that inviting the doctor was a good idea.

One day, Tom and his friend, Sam, went to the park. They liked to play there. Tom had a toy sword. He loved his sword. Sam liked to spin around. He got dizzy when he spun. Tom said, "Let's play knights with my sword!" Sam said, "OK, but first let me spin!" Sam spun around and around. He got very dizzy. Tom laughed at Sam. Tom swung his sword in the air. Sam stopped spinning and took the sword. They played knights together. They had a lot of fun at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play outside. One day, he saw a big wall made of bricks. He wanted to see what was on the other side of the wall. Spot tried to climb the wall. He put his paws on the bricks and pushed up. He climbed higher and higher. But he got tired. Spot was not used to climbing so high. Spot was very tired, but he did not give up. He took a rest and then tried again. This time, he climbed all the way to the top of the wall. Spot was happy he did not give up. On the other side of the wall, he found a big park to play in.

One day, a brown dog named Max found a big tank. Max was very happy. He wanted to show his friend, a cat named Bella. Max said, "Bella, look at this big tank I found!" Bella looked at the tank and said, "Wow, Max! That is a big tank. What can we do with it?" Max thought for a moment and said, "Let's pour water in the tank and play in it!" So Max and Bella started to pour water into the tank. They were very excited to play. But as they poured the water, something unexpected happened. The tank began to shake and make funny noises. Suddenly, the tank turned into a big friendly elephant! Max and Bella were very surprised. The elephant said, "Thank you for pouring water on me. I was stuck as a tank, but your water helped me change back." Max and Bella were happy to help. They played with the elephant all day, and they all became best friends.

One day, a white cat named Fluffy went to her friend, a brown dog named Buddy. Fluffy wanted to borrow Buddy's red ball to play with. So, she asked, "Buddy, can I borrow your red ball, please?" Buddy thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, Fluffy, you can borrow my red ball. But don't be foolish with it. Bring it back when you are done playing." Fluffy was happy and took the red ball to play. She had lots of fun jumping and rolling with the ball. After some time, Fluffy saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. She thought it would be fun to take the red ball up the tree. But she forgot what Buddy said about being foolish. Fluffy tried to climb the tree with the red ball, but it was too hard. The red ball fell down and rolled away. Fluffy felt sad because she lost Buddy's red ball. She went back to Buddy and told him what happened. Buddy was not happy, but he forgave Fluffy. They both went to look for the red ball together. They found it near a bush and promised to play together and not be foolish again.
