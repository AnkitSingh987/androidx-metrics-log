Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Flower

One day, a little girl named Lily was eager to go outside. She wanted to see the flowers. She put on her shoes and went to the garden. In the garden, she saw a big flower. The flower was very pretty. Lily said, "Hello, flower! You are so pretty. Can I touch you?" The flower said, "Yes, Lily. You can touch me, but be gentle." Lily touched the flower and it started to melt. The flower turned into a puddle of colors. Lily was sad and said, "I'm sorry, flower. I didn't mean to melt you." The puddle of colors started to move. It turned into a beautiful butterfly. The butterfly said, "Don't be sad, Lily. I am still here. Now, I can fly and see the world." Lily was happy again. She made a new friend, the butterfly. They played together in the garden every day. And the butterfly always remembered being a flower.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. They saw a big tree with lots of cherries on it. Lucy wanted to pick some cherries to take home. Her mom said, "We can pick the cherries that are available, but be careful not to slip!" Lucy and her mom started picking the cherries. Lucy saw a big red cherry on a low branch. She reached for it, but her foot slipped on the grass. "Oh no!" she cried. Her mom came to help her up. "Are you okay?" her mom asked. Lucy nodded and they both laughed. After they picked many cherries, Lucy and her mom went home. They washed the cherries and shared them with the family. Lucy told her dad and brother about her day and how she slipped at the park. They all laughed and enjoyed the sweet cherries together.

Once upon a time, there was a pale dog named Spot. Spot had a job to help his friend, a farmer named Tom. Spot's job was to watch the farm animals and make sure they did not run away. One day, Spot saw a big yellow ball in the sky. He thought it was a toy, so he started to jump and try to catch it. Tom saw Spot and said, "No, Spot! That is not a toy. That is the sun. It will not yield like your toys do." Spot felt sad that he could not play with the sun. He went back to his job and watched the animals. But then, something unexpected happened. A small ball fell from the sky! Spot was so happy. He ran and picked up the ball with his mouth. It was a yellow ball that looked like the sun, but it was a toy that would yield. Tom laughed and said, "Good job, Spot! You found a new toy that looks like the sun. Now you can play with it while you watch the animals." Spot wagged his tail and played with his new toy, happy that he could still do his job and have fun at the same time.

Once upon a time there lived a nosy little girl called Mandy. Mandy had a small brown desk in her bedroom where she kept all of her things. Mandy valued her desk because it gave her a place to store her precious treasures. One day in her bedroom Mandy heard her parents talking in the hallway. She was curious, so she crept to the door to have a closer look. Mandy's dad saw her and said "Mandy, why don't you go back to your room and play with your toys?" Mandy replied "But I value my desk more than anything, I want to stay here and keep listening!" Her dad laughed and said "Now, now, it's not polite to be so nosy. Go back to your desk and I will come talk to you soon." Mandy smiled and agreed, she understood it wasn't right to be too nosy. She skipped back to her desk and found a new treasure to add to her collection. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that he loved very much. It was a small red car. One day, the red car disappeared. Tim was very sad. Tim asked his mom and dad if they knew where his red car was. They did not know the truth. Tim looked everywhere in the house, but he could not find his red car. He asked his friend, Sam, if he had seen it. Sam said he did not know where it was. Tim never found his red car. He was not glad. He missed playing with it every day. Tim learned that sometimes things disappear and we never find them again. This made Tim very sad.
