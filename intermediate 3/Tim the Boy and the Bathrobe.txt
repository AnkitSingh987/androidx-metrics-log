Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bathrobe

One day, a little boy named Tim got a new bathrobe. It was soft and warm. The bathrobe had a big zip in the front. Tim was very happy with his new bathrobe. In the morning, Tim put on his bathrobe and zipped it up. He went to the kitchen to eat breakfast. His mom made him a delicious pancake. Tim ate the pancake and felt very cozy in his bathrobe. After breakfast, Tim played with his toys in the living room. He wore his bathrobe all day. At night, Tim took off his bathrobe and went to bed. He dreamed of more fun days in his soft, warm bathrobe.

Once upon a time, there was a bee whose name was Frank. Frank forgot all the flowers in the garden one week, so he was very sad. He decided to get help from his friend, Tom the Tiger, who lived in the jungle nearby. “Oh Tom, what should I do? I forgot to take all the flowers!” said Frank sadly. Tom thought for a second and said, “Don't worry, Frank. I have a plan. You should wait one more week and come back here. I will show you something special.” Frank was confused but grateful that his friend wanted to help. He agreed and waited an entire week. When he came back, he saw that Tom had replanted all the flowers! Frank was so happy that he hugged Tom and said “Thank you! I am so grateful!” From then on, Frank and Tom became best friends, and they promised to never forget each other.

Tom and Anna like to play in the park. They see a big structure made of wood and metal. It has slides, swings, ladders and bridges. They want to climb on it and have fun. But when they get closer, they see a sign that says "Do not enter. Dangerous." The structure is old and broken. Some parts are split and rusty. The wood is rotten and the metal is sharp. "Let's go somewhere else," Tom says. "This structure is not safe. It can hurt us." But Anna is curious. She wants to see what is inside. She says, "I don't care. I want to explore. It looks like a castle." She runs to the structure and tries to open a door. But the door is stuck. She pushes harder and harder. Suddenly, the door breaks and falls down. Anna falls with it. She screams. Tom hears her scream and runs to help her. He sees her lying on the ground, bleeding from her leg. He is scared and sad. He says, "Anna, why did you do that? I told you it was dangerous. Now you are hurt." He takes off his shirt and wraps it around her leg. He tries to stop the bleeding. He says, "Don't worry. I will get help. Stay here. Don't move." He runs to find a grown-up. He sees a lady with a phone. He says, "Please, help me. My friend is hurt. She fell from the structure. It is dangerous. Please, call an ambulance." The lady nods and calls an ambulance. She says, "Don't worry, little boy. You did the right thing. Your friend will be okay. The ambulance is coming soon." Tom hopes she is right. He hopes Anna will be okay. He hopes she will learn to listen and be careful. He hopes they can still play in the park, but not on the dangerous structure.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to cook with her mom. One day, they decided to cook a yummy cake. They mixed all the ingredients and put the cake in the oven. Sue was so excited! While the cake was cooking, Sue found an envelope on the table. It was from her friend, Tom. Sue opened the envelope and saw a drawing. It was an ugly drawing of a cat. Sue laughed and showed it to her mom. "Look, mom! Tom sent me this funny cat!" Sue said. Her mom laughed too. When the cake was ready, they took it out of the oven. It smelled so good! Sue and her mom enjoyed the cake together. They talked about the ugly cat drawing and laughed some more. Sue was happy she could cook and share a fun day with her mom.
