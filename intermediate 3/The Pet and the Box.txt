Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pet and the Box

Once there was a pet. His name was Spot. Spot always loved to measure things. He used a measuring tape to measure everything around the house. One day, Spot found something strange. He saw an old box in the corner of the room that he didn't recognize. He decided to measure it. When Spot measured the box, he noticed something strange. He could hear a noise coming from inside! Spot was very curious what it could be. Spot slowly opened the box. Inside he found a dead bird! Spot was very sad to see the poor bird. He thought to himself, "Why doesn't anyone care for the poor pet anymore?" Spot carefully closed the box and laid it down in the corner, so no other pet would be harmed. Spot had learned his lesson. He realized that he should be careful and only measure what he was allowed to.

One sunny day, a big tree stood tall and proud in the park. The tree loved to give shade to people who came to play. Many kids, moms, and dads would sit under the tree and have fun. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the tree. He said, "Hi, tree! Can I sit in your shade?" The tree was happy and said, "Yes, Tim! You can sit in my shade and play." Tim was very happy and played under the tree all day. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the tree's leaves away! The tree was sad because it could not give shade anymore. Tim saw this and said, "Don't worry, tree! I will pay you back for all the shade you gave me." Tim planted new leaves on the tree, and soon, the tree was proud and tall again. The tree and Tim became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a brilliant red jug. He loved his jug very much. He would play with it in the sand and fill it with water. Tim would pour the water on the flowers to help them grow. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. Sue saw the brilliant red jug and wanted to play with it too. She asked Tim, "Can I please play with your red jug?" But Tim did not want to share. He said, "No, you can't play with my jug." Sue was sad and asked again, "Please, can I play with it just for a little bit?" Tim thought about it and decided to share. He said, "Okay, you can play with my jug." They played together with the brilliant red jug, pouring water on the flowers and having fun. In the end, Tim was happy he shared his jug with Sue. They both had a great time playing together. From that day on, Tim learned that sharing his toys with friends made playing even more fun.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful forest, there was a very old tree. The old tree had many friends who lived with it. One day, a little bug came to the tree. The bug liked to crawl all over the tree. "Hello, old tree!" said the little bug. "I want to crawl up to your top and see the whole forest." "Hello, little bug!" said the old tree. "You can crawl as high as you want. Be careful and have fun." The little bug started to crawl up the tree. It saw many other friends on the way. They all said hello to the bug. The bug crawled higher and higher until it reached the top. From the top, the little bug saw the whole peaceful forest. It was so beautiful. The little bug thanked the old tree for letting it crawl up so high. The old tree was happy to help the little bug see the forest. They all lived in the peaceful forest together, as friends.

The dog was hungry. He looked around and saw a rabbit. He wanted to bite it. The rabbit saw the dog and tried to run away. But the dog was too fast. He bit the rabbit and the rabbit yelped. The rabbit was weak and could not stand up. The rabbit was in pain. He felt scared and sad. The dog wanted to bite him again, but the rabbit didn't want to get hurt. So, he hopped away as fast as he could. But the dog was very fast and he quickly caught up with the rabbit. He bit the rabbit again and the rabbit yelped. The rabbit was too weak to stand up. The dog bit the rabbit one last time. The rabbit felt so much pain that he could cry, but he had no more strength. He looked at the dog with anger and fear in his eyes. Then, he closed his eyes and did not move. The rabbit felt sadness and pain. The dog had a bad ending.
