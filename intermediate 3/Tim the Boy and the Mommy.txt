Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mommy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch his favorite show on the TV. One day, he could not find the TV remote. He looked and looked, but it was not available. Tim's mommy saw him looking sad. She asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I can't find the remote to watch my show." Mommy smiled and said, "Let's look together." Tim felt happy and started to look with his mommy. They looked under the couch, on the table, and behind the chair. Then, Tim's mommy pointed at the shelf. "Look, Tim! There's the remote!" Tim saw the remote, grabbed it, and turned on the TV. Now, he could watch his favorite show. Tim and his mommy hugged and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big black dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends. His friends include a cat, a bird, and a fish. They all lived in a big house with a nice family. One day, Spot and his friends wanted to play a game. They could not agree on what game to play. The cat wanted to play with a ball, the bird wanted to fly, and the fish wanted to swim. Spot wanted to play fetch. This made them all very sad. The big black dog, Spot, thought hard about how they could all have fun together. Then, he had an idea! They could play a game that includes everyone's favorite thing. They played a game where they took turns. First, they played with the ball, then they watched the bird fly, and then they watched the fish swim. At the end, they all played fetch with Spot. They were all very happy and had a great day together.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house. In the house lived a nice cat and a happy dog. They liked to play together. One day, they wanted to gain a new friend. They saw a little fire outside. The fire was not too big, but it was alone. The cat and the dog thought the fire could be their new friend. But, the fire was not a good friend. The fire got too big and tried to hurt the house. The cat and the dog got water and put the fire out. They learned that not all things can be friends. They were happy with just each other.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim went on a safari with his dad. They wanted to observe animals like lions, elephants, and giraffes. They were very excited. On the safari, they saw a weak baby giraffe. The baby giraffe was all alone. "Dad, can we help the giraffe?" Tim asked. His dad said, "Yes, let's help it find its mom." As they walked with the weak baby giraffe, they heard a loud noise. It was the baby giraffe's mom! But, the mom was not a giraffe. She was a big elephant! The baby giraffe was happy to see its mom. Tim and his dad were very surprised, but happy they could help. They had a fun safari and went home with a great story to tell.

One day, a girl named Daisy went for a walk. She saw a weird bug. The bug was big and had many legs. Daisy liked the bug and wanted to be its friend. Daisy took the bug home. She wanted to bake a cake for the bug. She mixed flour, sugar, and eggs. Then, she put the mix in the oven. The cake smelled yummy. When the cake was done, Daisy and the bug ate it. They had a fun time eating the cake. Daisy and the bug became best friends. They played together every day.

One day, a jolly cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big box. The box was a mystery. Tom was very curious. "Who is in the box?" Tom called out. "Come out and play with me!" A little mouse named Jerry came out of the box. Tom was surprised but happy. They played and laughed all day. After a while, Tom and Jerry found another mystery box. "Let's call out together," said Tom. "Who is in the box?" they both called. Suddenly, a big dog named Spike jumped out of the box! Tom and Jerry were scared, but Spike just wanted to play too. They all became best friends and had lots of fun together.
