Title: Tim the Boy and the Guitar

One day, a boy named Tim found a guitar. It was in a distant place, far from his house. He picked it up and took it home. He wanted to play it, but he saw that the strings were loose. He thought of how to fix the guitar. Tim remembered his dad tying his shoes. He thought that maybe he could tie the strings on the guitar too. So, he tried to tie the strings like his dad tied his shoes. It took some time, but he did it. Finally, the strings were tight on the guitar. Tim was happy. He started to play the guitar. It made beautiful sounds. His mom and dad heard the music and came to see. They were so proud of Tim for fixing the guitar and playing it so well.

Tom and Lily were playing hide and seek in the park. Tom was the seeker and Lily was the hider. Lily ran to a big bush and hid behind it. She was very quiet and hoped Tom would not find her. Tom looked around and saw many places where Lily could hide. He saw a slide, a bench, a tree, and a pond. He decided to check the pond first. He walked to the pond and saw something green and wet. It was a swamp. Tom did not like swamps. They were smelly and dirty and had bugs and frogs. Tom heard a noise from the swamp. It was a loud croak. Tom was scared. He thought there was a big frog in the swamp. He ran away from the pond and shouted, "Lily, Lily, where are you?" Lily heard Tom's voice and peeked from behind the bush. She saw Tom running away from the swamp. She giggled and said, "I'm here, Tom. I'm behind the bush. You did not find me." Tom heard Lily's voice and turned around. He saw Lily smiling and waving from behind the bush. He ran to her and said, "Lily, you are a good hider. But I did not like the swamp. It was noisy and scary. There was a big frog in the swamp. It croaked at me." Lily laughed and said, "Tom, that was not a big frog. That was me. I made the noise to trick you. I revealed myself to you. Do you want to play again?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, I want to play again. But this time, I will be the hider and you will be the seeker. And no more swamps, OK?" Lily nodded and said, "OK, no more swamps. Let's play again. One, two, three, go!"

Once upon a time, there was a big crocodile. He lived in a pond. The crocodile was rare. He was green and had big teeth. He liked to play with his friends, but he did not like it when they disturb him. One day, the crocodile was taking a nap. He was very tired. His friends were playing near the pond. They were making a lot of noise. The crocodile was getting disturbed. He got up and went to tell his friends to be quiet. But when the crocodile got to his friends, he saw something he did not expect. There was a big party! All his friends were there. They had a cake and balloons. They were not trying to disturb him. They were trying to surprise him. It was his birthday! The crocodile was so happy. He had a great time at his party.
