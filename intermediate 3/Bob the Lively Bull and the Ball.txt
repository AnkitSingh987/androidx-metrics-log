Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Lively Bull and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a lively bull named Bob. He loved to run and play in the grass. One day, he saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the big bull. "Hi, Tim," said the lively bull. "Do you want to play with me?" "No, you are too big," said Tim. "I am scared you will bite me." Bob the bull wanted to show Tim that he was a good friend. He decided to play with a toy ball. But while playing, Bob accidentally bit the ball. The ball went "pop!" and air came out. Tim saw the ball and cried. He ran away from Bob the bull. The lively bull was sad because he wanted to be friends with Tim. But now, Tim was even more scared of Bob. And they never became friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a happy boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and run in the park. One day, when Tim was playing in the park, he found a wallet on the ground. He picked it up and saw that it had money inside. Tim knew he should give the wallet back to the person who lost it. But, he also wanted to keep the money to buy candy. Tim thought about it and decided to lie. He told his mom that he found the money on the ground, not in a wallet. His mom knew he was lying and said, "Tim, it is not good to lie. You should always tell the truth." Tim felt bad and told his mom about the wallet. They went to the park and found the person who lost the wallet. The person was so happy to get it back and thanked Tim. Tim learned that it is always better to tell the truth and be honest. He felt happy in his heart for doing the right thing. From that day on, Tim always told the truth and was a good boy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily lived in a small house with her mom and dad. It was cold outside, so Lily wore a warm coat. She liked to play with her friends, but they were not around today. One day, Lily met a little bird. The bird was sad and said, "I want peace. The other birds are too loud, and I can't sleep." Lily thought about how she could help the bird find peace. She had an idea and said, "Come with me, little bird. I know a place where it is quiet." Lily took the bird to a big tree in her yard. The tree had many leaves, and it was away from the other noisy birds. The little bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Lily. This is the peace I wanted." They became best friends, and the little bird slept well in the big tree. And from that day on, Lily and the bird played together every day, and they both enjoyed the peace they found together.

Once there was a pot of plant. Every day, the plant got some sunshine and lots of water. Tom, who was very bossy, decided he wanted the plant for his house. He asked the owners if he could take it. The owners said "no". But Tom was very bossy and he didn't listen to them. So he went ahead and removed the plant from the pot. Tom didn't know how to take care of a plant. He forgot to give it water and the plant got very thirsty. Its leaves started turning brown and it looked very sad. Tom tried to give it some water, but it was too late. The poor plant had died and there was nothing Tom could do. He felt very sad and cried a lot.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to move around his room. One day, he found a filthy strip of cloth on the floor. He didn't know where it came from, but he thought it was dirty and needed to be thrown away. Tim decided to use the strip to clean his toy car. As he started cleaning, he noticed that the car was changing colors! It was not just dirty, but it had been painted with a special paint that changed colors when it was cleaned. Tim was very happy with his new colorful car. He learned that sometimes, even if something looks dirty or bad, it can still have something good inside. So, he promised himself to always look for the good in things and not judge them by how they look.
