Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a pet cat named Tom. One day, Sue wanted to taste a new candy that her mom had bought for her. Sue said to Tom, "Let's go to the kitchen and taste the candy!" Tom meowed and followed Sue. They went to the kitchen and Sue opened the candy jar. She took out two candies, one for her and one for Tom. Sue closed the jar and gave a candy to Tom. As they ate the candies, they started to feel dizzy. Sue said, "Tom, I feel dizzy!" Tom meowed and sat down because he felt dizzy too. They sat down together and waited until they didn't feel dizzy anymore. After a while, they felt better and played together happily.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lily. She loved to do puzzles. Every day after school she would get out her puzzle box and finish the jigsaw. One day Lily decided to try a harder puzzle. She cleared a space on the table and tipped out the pieces. It was hard! Lily worked hard and eventually all the pieces joined together and made a beautiful picture. She was so proud. She put the puzzle in the sink and watched as it filled up with water. In a few minutes the jigsaw was finished. Lily was really proud she had completed the puzzle. She hugged the sink and was so happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play with her toy car. One day, her toy car stopped moving. Amy was sad. Amy's mom said, "Oh no! We need oil to make it go." They went to the store to buy oil. But the oil was disgusting. It was sticky and smelly. Amy did not like it. Amy's mom put the disgusting oil on the toy car. The car started to move again. Amy was happy. That night, Amy went to sleep with her toy car by her side.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion lived in a green place with many trees. The lion had a friend, a little bird. The bird liked to sing songs to the lion. One day, the lion was very tired. The lion said, "I want to sleep." The bird said, "Okay, I will sing you a song to help you sleep." The bird sang a soft song and the lion fell asleep under a tree. While the lion was sleeping, the bird saw a sour fruit. The bird thought, "I will give this to the lion when he wakes up." When the lion woke up, the bird gave him the sour fruit. The lion made a funny face because it was so sour, but he was happy to have a friend like the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play near the water and watch the surf. One day, the sky was dull and the water was not very bright, but Lucy still wanted to play. As Lucy played by the water, she saw a little fish. The fish was sad because it missed its family. Lucy wanted to help and comfort the fish. She picked up the fish and put it back into the water. With a big smile, the fish swam away to find its family. Lucy felt happy that she could help. She kept playing near the surf and enjoyed her day at the beach.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a hard tooth on the ground. She wanted to show it to her friend, Tim. Lily put the tooth on her head and tried to balance it as she walked. "Look, Tim!" Lily said. "I found a hard tooth and I can balance it on my head!" Tim tried to balance the tooth on his head too, but it fell down. He felt sad and said, "I can't do it, Lily. It's too hard for me." Lily thought for a moment and said, "Let's try together, Tim. Hold my hand and we can balance the tooth together." Tim held Lily's hand and they both balanced the hard tooth on their heads. They were happy and proud of what they could do together. And they learned that sometimes, it's easier to do things with a friend.
