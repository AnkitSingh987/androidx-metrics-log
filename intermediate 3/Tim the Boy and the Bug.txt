Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bug

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to sit and play with his toys in the hall. One day, he found a disgusting bug on the floor. Tim was scared and did not know what to do. He ran to his mom and said, "Mommy, there is a disgusting bug in the hall!" His mom went to look at the bug. She saw it and told Tim, "Don't worry, I will take care of it." Tim watched as his mom took the bug outside. When she came back, she said, "The bug is gone now. You can go back and sit in the hall to play." Tim was happy and went back to play with his toys. He was not scared anymore because his mom had helped him.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy lady named Jane. She lived in a small house near the park. Jane loved to go for walks and play with the birds and the squirrels. One sunny day, Jane went for a walk in the park. She saw a little girl crying under a tree. Jane went to the girl and asked, "Why are you sad?" The girl said, "I lost my toy." Jane wondered where the toy could be. Jane and the little girl looked for the toy together. They looked behind the trees and under the bushes. Finally, they found the toy near a pond. The little girl was so happy, she hugged Jane. From that day on, Jane and the little girl became good friends and played together in the park every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a purple house. In the house lived a girl named Lily and her dog, Max. They loved to play games and solve mysteries together. Lily and Max were very good friends. One day, they found a broken toy in their yard. It was a mystery how it got there. Lily and Max decided to fix the toy. They worked hard, and soon the toy was as good as new. They were very happy and proud of their work. Later, a little boy came by looking sad. He had lost his toy. Lily and Max showed him the toy they fixed. The boy was so happy to see his toy again. Lily and Max learned that helping others can make you feel good inside. And that was the best mystery they ever solved.

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Tom. Tom loved to taste new things. One day, Tom found a pencil on the floor. He thought, "I wonder what this tastes like." So, he put the pencil in his mouth. "Yuck!" said Tom. "This pencil does not taste good." He spat it out and looked around for something else to taste. Then, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was drawing a picture with her pencils. "Hi Tom!" said Sue. "Do you want to draw with me?" Tom meowed and sat down next to Sue. He watched as she drew a big, pretty rainbow. Tom tried to draw, too, but he could not hold the pencil with his paws. Sue saw Tom trying to draw and had an idea. She took a piece of paper and put some paint on it. Then, she dipped Tom's paw in the paint. Tom pressed his paw onto the paper, making a colorful paw print. "Good job, Tom!" said Sue. They both laughed and kept drawing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very sad and miserable. He lived in a small house with a big, colorful carpet. Tim loved to sit on the carpet and play with his toys. One day, it was very hot outside. The sun was shining so bright. Tim decided to go outside and play. He left his ice cream on the carpet. The ice cream started to melt. It made a big mess on the carpet. The carpet was not happy. The carpet wanted to be clean and pretty again. But then, something unexpected happened. The carpet started to move! It lifted off the ground and began to fly. Tim saw the flying carpet and jumped on it. The carpet took Tim high up in the sky. They flew over the trees and the houses. Tim was not miserable anymore. He was happy and excited. The carpet and Tim became the best of friends. And they lived happily ever after.
