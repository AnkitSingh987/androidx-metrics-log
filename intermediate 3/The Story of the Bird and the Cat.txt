Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Cat

One day, a little yellow bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a big tree and wanted to rest. So, the bird went to the tree and sat on a branch. The bird was happy. Then, something happened. A small cat saw the bird and wanted to play. The cat said, "Hello, bird! Let's play together!" But the bird was scared and flew away. The cat felt sad and ashamed because it scared the bird. The next day, the cat saw the bird again. This time, the cat said, "I'm sorry, bird. I didn't want to scare you. Can we be friends?" The bird smiled and said, "Yes, let's be friends!" From that day on, the cat and the yellow bird became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there was a well. The well had cold, icy water. Every day, the farmer would use a bucket to raise the water from the well. He gave the water to the animals and plants, so they could grow big and strong. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the farm. She saw the well and wanted to help the farmer. The farmer showed Lily how to raise the water from the icy well. Lily was very happy to help, and the farmer was happy too. Together, they raised the water from the well and gave it to the animals and plants. The farm became the best farm in the land. The plants grew tall, and the animals were healthy. The farmer and Lily were very proud of their hard work. They knew they had done a good job, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max had an owner named Tom. Max loved to show Tom his incredible tricks. One day, Max wanted to show Tom a new trick he learned. Max said, "Tom, look at me!" Max jumped very high and flipped in the air. Tom was so happy and clapped his hands. But then, another dog named Sam came. Sam wanted to show Tom that he could do better tricks. Sam said, "I can do that too!" Sam did the same trick as Max, but he jumped even higher. Max was sad because he thought Tom would like Sam more. But Tom said, "Max, you are still my best friend. I love both of your tricks!" Max and Sam became friends and they both showed Tom their incredible tricks. They all played together and had lots of fun. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little bunny named Ben went for a hop in the woods. He liked to hop and play with his friends. But this time, he saw something mysterious. It was big, and it had long arms. Ben was scared. Ben hopped closer to see what it was. The mysterious thing with long arms was a big tree! The tree was sad because it had no friends. Ben thought, "I should help the tree find friends." Ben hopped around and told his friends about the tree. They all hopped to the tree and played near it. The tree was happy to have friends now. Ben learned that even if something looks scary, it can be nice.

One day, a green bird named Bob lived in a tree. Bob loved to look at the sky. He looked at the sky every day. One day, he saw something big and scary in the sky. It was a hurricane coming to his tree. Bob was scared and went to find his friend, Cat. Bob said, "Cat, look! A big hurricane is coming. What do we do?" Cat said, "We need to hide in a safe place. Let's go to my house." So, Bob and Cat went to Cat's house to hide from the hurricane. But when they got there, something unexpected happened. The hurricane was not a hurricane at all! It was a big, green balloon. The wind blew the balloon away, and the sky was clear again. Bob and Cat laughed and said, "We were scared for nothing! Let's go play outside." And they played happily ever after.
