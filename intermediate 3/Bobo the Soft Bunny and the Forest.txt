Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Soft Bunny and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a soft bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to hop and play with his friends in the forest. One day, he saw his friend, a little bird named Chirpy, sitting on a branch with a sad face. "Chirpy, why are you sad?" asked Bobo. Chirpy looked at Bobo and said, "I hurt my wing. It's in pain, and I can't fly to my nest." Bobo wanted to help his friend. He thought for a moment and said, "I can lend you my soft bed to sleep in until your wing feels better." Chirpy was very happy and said, "Thank you, Bobo!" So, Chirpy slept in Bobo's soft bed until her wing felt better. They played together every day, and soon, Chirpy could fly again. She was so thankful for Bobo's help and they became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a wolf. The wolf was very graceful. He liked to run and play with his friends. One day, the wolf saw a big lake. The lake was blue and shiny. He wanted to dive into the lake. The wolf's mom told him, "Be careful when you dive. Look before you leap." The wolf did not listen. He was too excited to play in the water. He ran and jumped into the lake. But he did not see a big rock under the water. The wolf hit the rock and it hurt. He swam back to the land and cried. His mom came and hugged him. She said, "Remember, always look before you leap." The wolf learned his lesson. From that day on, he always looked before he dove into the water. And he told his friends to do the same.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to visit her Aunt May. Aunt May had a big kitchen and liked to mix things in bowls. Lucy liked to help her aunt in the kitchen. One day, Aunt May said, "Lucy, let's make a spicy soup!" Lucy was excited. They mixed tomatoes, onions, and peppers in a big pot. The soup smelled good. Lucy said, "I can't wait to eat it!" When the soup was done, Lucy and Aunt May sat down to eat. Lucy took a bite and said, "Wow, this is spicy!" They laughed and enjoyed their soup together. Lucy loved spending time with her Aunt May.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a red ball. She loved her ball very much. She played with her ball every day. One day, she saw her friend Tim display his toy car. Amy wanted to show her ball too. The next day, Amy and Tim had a plan. They wanted to display their toys together. But, when they played, Amy's ball hit Tim's toy car. Oh no! Tim's car had a little break. Tim was sad, and Amy was sorry. Amy thought and thought. She had an idea! She told Tim to wait and ran home. Amy came back with her dad. Her dad could fix Tim's car. Tim was happy! They played together again. This time, they were more careful. Amy learned to watch her toe when she kicked her red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to sniff and listen to everything around him. One day, he heard a tiny sound with his big ear. Buddy insisted on finding out what the sound was. He looked high and low, near and far. He asked his friends, the cat and the bird, if they knew what it was. The cat said, "I don't know," and the bird said, "I don't know either." Finally, Buddy found a little mouse hiding in a hole. The mouse said, "I made the sound! I was singing a song." Buddy was happy to find the mouse and they became friends. From that day on, Buddy, the cat, the bird, and the mouse would play together and sing songs all day long.

Once upon a time in a big universe, there was a little planet. On this planet, there lived a hungry boy named Tim. Tim was always looking for food to eat. One day, Tim saw a big, yummy cake on the table. He wanted to eat it, but he knew it was not his. Tim's friend, Sam, came over and said, "Tim, that cake is not yours. If you eat it, you will be punished." Tim thought about it and knew Sam was right. But Tim was so hungry, he could not resist. He ate the cake anyway. When his mom found out, she punished him by making him clean his room. Tim was sad but he learned his lesson. From that day on, he never took things that were not his.
