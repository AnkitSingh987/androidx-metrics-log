Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Drawing

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a notebook where she liked to draw and write. One day, she drew a terrible monster in her notebook. The monster had big eyes, sharp teeth, and long arms. Lily was scared of the monster, but she knew it was just a drawing. Lily showed her monster drawing to her friend, Tom. Tom thought the monster was funny and not scary at all. He wanted to make the monster even bigger, so they used the word "increase" to make it grow. Now, the monster was bigger and stronger in the notebook. But Lily and Tom were brave. They knew the monster was still just a drawing in the notebook. They laughed and played, and the terrible monster didn't scare them anymore. They drew funny hats on the monster and made it look silly. In the end, Lily and Tom had fun with their big monster friend.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a big gate in his yard. One day, he wanted to make the gate look nice. So, he thought of printing pretty pictures on it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I print on the gate, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but only if you use washable paint." Tim was very happy and went to find his paints. He printed a sun, a tree, and a house on the gate. It looked very nice. Tim's friends came over and saw the gate. They said, "Wow, Tim! Your gate looks so pretty!" Tim felt proud and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock in a distant land. The rock had a little door on it. No one knew what was behind the door. Many people tried to open it, but they could not. They did not know how to unlock it. One day, a little girl named Lucy found the rock. She saw the door and wanted to open it. Lucy tried to unlock the door with her toy key. To her surprise, the door opened! She was very happy. Inside the rock, Lucy found a pretty garden. There were many flowers, trees, and butterflies. Lucy played in the garden all day. When it was time to go home, she locked the door. Lucy promised to visit the secret garden again soon.

One day, a small, weak fish named Fin swam in the water. Fin had a friend named Big Scale. Big Scale was a big, strong fish. They liked to play together. Fin and Big Scale wanted to go to a fun fish party. But Fin was slow and weak. Big Scale said, "Fin, I will help you get strong. Then we can go to the party." They played and swam to get strong. But Fin was still weak and slow. The day of the party came. Fin and Big Scale started to swim to the party. But Fin was so slow, it made them delay. They swam and swam, but they did not get to the party in time. The party was over. Fin was sad because he was still weak and slow.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. The cat was very persistent. She wanted to find some food to eat. The dog wanted to play with the water hose. He liked to spray water on the grass. The cat said, "I am hungry. Let's find some food." The dog agreed and they both looked for food. They found a big box in the yard. They thought there must be food inside. The dog used his paws to open the box. But instead of food, they found a big toy that could spray water. The cat and the dog looked at each other and laughed. They played with the toy and sprayed water all over the yard. They had so much fun that they forgot they were hungry.

One day, a girl named Lucy wanted to print a big picture of her dog. She wanted to give it to her mom as a gift. She went to her dad and said, "Dad, can you help me print the picture?" Her dad said, "Of course, Lucy! Let's go to the computer and print it." They printed the picture, but it was hidden behind some papers on the desk. Lucy looked and looked, but she could not find it. She felt sad and asked her dad, "Where is the picture? I can't see it." Her dad looked around and saw the picture hidden behind the papers. He said, "Here it is, Lucy! It was hiding!" Lucy felt happy and said, "Thank you, Dad!" Then, they found some wrap to put around the picture. They wrapped it up and put a pretty bow on it. Now, Lucy was ready to give the gift to her mom. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, I have a surprise for you!" Her mom opened the wrap and saw the big picture of their dog. She smiled and said, "I love it, Lucy! Thank you so much!" Lucy felt proud and happy that her mom liked her gift.
