Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim wanted to use scissors to cut paper. He asked his mom, "Can I use the scissors?" His mom said, "I will permit you to use the scissors, but be careful." Tim was very happy. He cut the paper into shapes. He made a sun, a tree, and a big, healthy apple. Tim showed his mom the apple. His mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! That is a very healthy apple." Tim felt proud. He put the sun, tree, and healthy apple on the wall. Now, everyone could see his art. His mom hugged him and said, "I am glad I permitted you to use the scissors." Tim hugged his mom back and felt happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to draw and make prints with her hands. One sunny day, she went outside to play with her friend, Tom. Lily and Tom had a fun idea to make hand prints on the ground. They dipped their hands in paint and pressed them on the ground to make pretty prints. They were very happy and excited. But then, Lily accidentally dropped her paint pot. The paint splashed everywhere, and some got on Tom's shirt. Tom was upset, and his eyes filled with tears. Lily said sorry to Tom and helped him clean up the mess. After that, they continued making prints and had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a dark forest, there lived a kind bunny named Bob. Bob had a secret. He knew how to repair broken things. One day, while hopping through the forest, Bob saw his friend, the little bird, crying. "Why are you sad?" asked Bob. The little bird said, "My nest is broken, and I don't know how to fix it." Bob smiled and said, "Don't worry, I have a secret. I can repair your nest." The little bird was so happy. Bob fixed the nest, and it was as good as new. From that day on, Bob and the little bird were the best of friends. They played and laughed in the dark forest, and Bob always helped his friends when they needed him. The end.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. The cat saw a treat on the ground. The dog saw it too. They both wanted the treat. "Stop!" said the cat. "That treat is mine!" The dog did not want to be normal and share. He wanted the treat all for himself. The cat and the dog began to fight over the treat. In the end, they both pulled so hard on the treat that it broke into pieces. The cat and the dog were sad. They did not get to enjoy the treat. If they had shared, they would have been happy. But now, the treat was gone and they both felt bad.

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his stack of blocks. Every day, he would build a big tower with his blocks. One sunny day, Tom met a pretty cat named Sue. Sue was very nice and they became good friends. Tom wanted to marry Sue because he liked her a lot. But he was too shy to ask her. One day, Tom decided to show Sue his big tower of blocks. He hoped that she would like it and want to marry him. But, as he was showing Sue his tower, a strong wind blew and knocked it down. Tom was very sad. Sue saw how sad Tom was and decided to help him. Together, they built an even bigger and better tower. Tom was so happy that he found the courage to ask Sue to marry him. She said yes and they lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is that when we help each other, we can overcome our shyness and build something even better together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to collect things. Tim had many different things like rocks, leaves, and toys. One day, Tim wanted to collect something new. He thought about the weather outside. It was icy and cold. Tim put on his warm coat, hat, and gloves. He went outside to see what he could find. He saw a big pile of snow. Tim thought it would be fun to collect snow. He started to make snowballs. He made big ones and small ones. Tim had a great idea. He would make a snowman with all the snowballs he collected. Tim's friend, Lily, saw him from her window. She put on her coat and went outside too. "Hi Tim! What are you doing?" Lily asked. "I'm collecting snow to make a snowman," Tim said with a big smile. "Can I help you?" Lily asked. "Yes, let's make the snowman together!" Tim said. They both worked hard, and soon they had a big, beautiful snowman. Tim and Lily were very happy with their icy snowman. They played in the snow until it was time to go home.
