Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Money

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a modern house with his family. One day, Tim saw his mom put some money in a box. He wanted the money to buy a toy. Tim took the money and went to the store. He bought a toy and played with it. But soon, Tim started to cry. He knew taking the money was wrong. Tim decided to tell his mom the truth. Tim's mom was sad, but she hugged him. She told Tim that it's important to be honest and not take things that don't belong to us. Tim learned his lesson and promised never to do it again.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a red deer. This deer had a lot of friends. They liked to play and run all day. One day, the red deer saw some sweet food on the ground. It was hard to resist. The red deer ate the food, but it was not good. The deer felt sick. The deer's friends came and helped. They gave the red deer water and love. Soon, the red deer felt better. They all played and ran again, happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had many leaves. The tree was very good. It would provide shade for all the animals. The animals liked to sit under the tree when it was hot. One day, a little rabbit came to the tree. The rabbit was very eager to play. He wanted to play with the other animals under the tree. The rabbit said, "Hello! Can I play with you?" The other animals said, "Yes! Come play with us under the tree!" The rabbit was very happy. They all played together and had lots of fun. The tree was happy too, because it could provide shade for all its new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with puzzles. One day, she found a big puzzle with many pieces. She wanted to put it together, but it was very hard. Amy asked her friend, Tom, to help her. Tom was a reliable friend, always there when Amy needed him. Together, they tried to put the puzzle together. They worked and worked, but some pieces were still missing. Amy and Tom didn't want to surrender. They searched and searched until they found the last pieces. They put the puzzle together and were very happy. They learned that with a good friend, they could do anything.

---

One day, a zebra named Zara wanted to create a big sand castle. She asked her friend, a little bird named Bob, to help her. "Bob, can you help me create a sand castle?" Zara asked. Bob was not very nice. He was a bad bird. He didn't want to help Zara. "No, Zara. I don't want to help you," said Bob. Zara was sad, but she didn't give up. She tried to create the sand castle all by herself. Zara worked hard on her sand castle. She made it big and strong. Other animals saw her and wanted to help. A kind elephant and a happy monkey came to help Zara. Together, they made the best sand castle ever. Bob saw the castle and felt bad for not helping. Zara forgave him, and they all played together.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to enter the haircut shop. He had long hair and his mom said it was time to get it cut. Tim did not want to get a haircut. He was scared of the haircut shop. Inside the shop, there was a mild and nice man who gave haircuts. He saw that Tim was scared, so he tried to make Tim feel better. He gave Tim a toy car to play with. Tim started to feel a little better. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog entered the shop. The dog was very friendly and wanted to play with Tim. The dog made Tim laugh and forget about his fear. Tim got his haircut while playing with the dog. Now, Tim loves going to the haircut shop to see his new friend.
