Category: Intermediate
Title: George and the Tree

Little George was walking through the park. He stopped in front of a big tree and looked around. Suddenly he spotted something shiny in one of the branches. He was excited and knew he had to get it. George climbed up carefully and finally reached the shining object. He reached out and grabbed it. To his surprise, it was a page from a book, and it was yellow and crinkled around the edges. He climbed down from the tree and went to show his mum the page. "Look mum, I found a page. Can I keep it please?" his mum said yes, so he beamed with happiness. He wanted to read the page, but he couldn't as it was full of big words he didn't know. He was disappointed but was determined to find out what it said. He held the page close to his heart, and tried to be successful at reading it. But no matter how hard he tried, he couldn't understand any of the words. And so, in the end, he never found out the meaning of the page and never managed to be successful.

Once, there was a big race in the park. A rabbit and a turtle wanted to see who was faster. The rabbit said, "I am fast. I will win the race." The turtle said, "I am slow, but I will try." The race began. The rabbit ran fast, but he knew he needed to be patient. The turtle walked slow and steady. The rabbit saw a tree and thought, "I can rest here. I will still win." So, the rabbit took a nap under the tree. When the rabbit woke up, he saw the turtle near the finish line. The rabbit ran as fast as he could, but it was too late. The turtle won the race. The rabbit was sad because he lost. He learned that being too sure of himself and not patient enough can lead to a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a modest cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a big wall. He had many friends who would play with him. One day, Tom wanted to gain a new toy. He asked his friends to help him find one. They looked all around the house and the big wall, but they could not find a toy. Tom was sad. He tried to climb the big wall to look for a toy on the other side. But as he climbed, he fell down and got hurt. Tom did not find a new toy and now he was hurt too. This was a bad day for Tom.

Once upon a time, there was a kind clown named Bobo. Bobo lived in a small town. He loved to make people laugh and smile. Bobo had many friends because he was very nice. One sunny day, Bobo went to the park to play. He saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her toy. Bobo wanted to help her. He whispered to her, "Don't worry, I will help you find your toy." Bobo and Lily looked for the toy together. They looked under the trees and behind the bushes. Finally, they found the toy near the swings. Lily was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Bobo! You are the best friend ever!" After that, Bobo and Lily played together all day. They laughed and had so much fun. From that day on, they were the best of friends. And everyone in the town loved the kind clown named Bobo.

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman named Tom. He lived near a big lake. Every day, Tom went to the lake to catch fish. He liked to catch big fish and small fish. One cold day, Tom went to the lake, but the water was frozen. Tom said, "Oh no, I can't catch fish today!" He was very upset. Tom saw a little boy named Tim. Tim said, "Don't be upset, Tom. We can still have fun!" Tom and Tim decided to play on the ice. They had a fun time sliding and making snowballs. They laughed and played all day. In the end, Tom was not upset anymore. He learned that even when things don't go as planned, you can still find a way to have fun.
