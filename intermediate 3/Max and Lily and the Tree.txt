Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and Lily and the Tree

One day, a big dog named Max and a little cat named Lily went for a walk. Max had a cool style. He wore a red collar with his name on it. Lily had a pretty pink bow on her head. They were best friends. As they walked, they saw a big tree. Max said, "I want to climb the tree, but I am too big. Can you help me, Lily?" Lily said, "Yes, Max. I can climb the tree and you can trust me to help you." Lily climbed the tree and dropped a long rope for Max. Max held the rope and Lily helped him climb up. When they reached the top, they saw the whole world. They were so happy to be together. Max and Lily climbed down the tree and went back home. They knew they could trust each other and their friendship was very special. They had a great day and couldn't wait for their next adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat that loved to sail on the big sea. One sunny day, the boat saw a helpless fish with a hurt toe. The fish was sad and couldn't swim fast. The boat said, "I can help you, fish! Jump on me, and I will take you to your family." The fish jumped onto the boat, and they sailed away. As they sailed, the sky turned dark, and it started to rain. The rain made the fish's toe feel better. Then, something unexpected happened! The fish grew bigger and bigger. It was a magic rain! The big fish said, "Thank you, boat! Now, I can help you sail even faster!" And they sailed happily together forever.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a cat and a dog. The cat and the dog did not like to share. They always fought over their toys and food. One day, while they were fighting, a small mouse came into the house. "Stop!" said the mouse, looking alert. "You should share and be friends." The cat and the dog looked at the mouse and stopped fighting. They started to talk and listen to the mouse. The cat and the dog learned to share their toys and food. They became good friends with the mouse. They played together and had lots of fun in the little house. The cat, the dog, and the mouse were very happy. They learned that sharing is good and makes everyone happy.

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. Fin was a very good diver. He loved to dive deep in the water to see what he could find. Fin had a friend named Crabby. Crabby was a red crab who walked with a cane because one of his legs was hurt. Fin and Crabby saw a big, boring rock in the water. "Let's dive and see what's under the rock!" said Fin. Crabby agreed, and they both dived down to the rock. When they got to the rock, they saw something shiny under it. Fin and Crabby moved the rock and found a big treasure chest! They opened it and saw lots of shiny things inside. They were so happy and excited. The boring rock was not boring anymore! Fin and Crabby took the treasure back to their homes to show their friends and family.

Once upon a time, there was a unique car named Beep. Beep could do something no other car could do - it could reverse very fast! Beep loved to show off this special skill to his friends. One day, Beep was playing with his friend, a small bike named Zoom. Zoom said, "Beep, can you show me how you reverse fast again?" Beep smiled and said, "Of course, Zoom!" Beep went back, back, back very fast. Zoom was so happy to see Beep's special trick. But then, Beep's alarm went off. It was time for Beep to go home. Beep said to Zoom, "I have to go home now, but we can play again tomorrow!" Zoom nodded and said, "I can't wait, Beep! See you tomorrow!" And they both went back to their homes, excited to play together again.
