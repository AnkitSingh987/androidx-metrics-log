Category: Intermediate
Title: The Gray Pen and the Hello

Once there was a gray pen. It was a very special pen, because it had magical voice powers. Whenever someone picked it up, the pen would yell ‘hello!’ One day, a little three-year-old girl walked into the room and saw the gray pen on the floor. She picked it up and said ‘hello’. And then the pen yelled ‘hello!’ back at her! The little girl was so excited, she yelled back ‘hello!’ again. And so they kept yelling ‘hello!’ back and forth, over and over again. The little girl was so happy, she wanted to take the pen home with her. So she put it in her pocket and ran home with a smile on her face. The magical gray pen had a forever home.

One day, a smart cat named Tom set out to play with his friends. He took his favorite blanket with him. Tom loved his blanket very much. It was soft and warm. Tom and his friends played all day. They ran, jumped, and had fun. But then, they saw a big dog coming. The dog was not nice. He wanted to take Tom's blanket away. Tom and his friends were scared. Just when the dog was about to take the blanket, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird picked up the dog and flew away. Tom and his friends were so happy. They hugged the blanket and played some more. The smart cat and his friends had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a stubborn boy who always wanted to get his way. One day, he saw a big, shiny toy in the store. It was a luxury toy that he really, really wanted. Tim asked his mom, "Can I have that toy, please?" His mom said, "No, Tim. We can't buy that toy today." Tim was sad and angry. He had a plan. The next day, Tim went back to the store. He tried to take the toy without his mom knowing. But as he was running away, he heard a big "tear!" His shirt got caught on something and ripped. Tim knew he did something wrong. He felt bad about trying to take the toy. He went back to his mom and said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I won't be stubborn anymore." His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tim. We all make mistakes. Let's go home and fix your shirt." From that day on, Tim learned to be more patient and not be so stubborn. He knew that sometimes, you can't always get what you want. And that was okay.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, he found a big red button on the floor. He was very curious and wanted to press it. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can I press the button?" His mom said, "No, Tim. That button is sharp. You might hurt your finger." But Tim was persistent. He really wanted to press the button. Tim listened to his mom and did not press the button. He learned that it is important to listen to his mom because she knows what is best for him. And they lived happily ever after.

Once there was a boy named Tim. He wanted to go to the attic. The attic was up, up, up in his house. He had to climb a ladder to get there. It was a struggle for Tim because he was small. But he tried very hard. When Tim got to the attic, he saw a big box. He opened the box and found a hat. The hat was very old and big. Tim put the hat on his head. It was too big, but he was proud to wear it. Tim went down the ladder and showed his mom the hat. She smiled and said, "You look great!" Tim felt proud and happy. He played with the hat all day.

Once there was a little girl named Lola. She was three years old, and loved to explore. One day, while walking through the park, Lola saw something hard sticking out of the ground. She bent down to look closer and saw it was a bottle. She picked it up and opened it. To her surprise, she found lotion inside! Lola was so happy and couldn't believe her luck. She carefully put some of the lotion on her hands and rubbed them together. She liked the way it felt, smooth and soft. She decided to take the lotion home with her. When Lola got home, she told her mom all about finding the lotion. Her mom was so surprised, she asked her where she found it. Lola smiled, and then told her all about her adventure in the park. At bedtime, Lola put the lotion on her hands again and marveled at how soft it made them feel. She smiled, knowing she had made a great surprise discovery that day.
