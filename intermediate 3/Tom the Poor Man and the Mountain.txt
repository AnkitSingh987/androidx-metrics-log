Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Poor Man and the Mountain

Once upon a time there lived a poor little man named Tom. Tom was very trusting of the wind, believing it could take him to new and wonderful places. One day, Tom decided to trust the wind and it took him to the edge of a mountain. Tom peered over the edge and was amazed at what he saw. He decided to take one step forward to get a closer look. The wind began to blow and Tom started to slide down the mountain. He shouted, "Help me! Please, help me!" Tom's best friend, Joe, heard him and ran to the edge to help. The wind, however, had other plans. It blew harder, preventing Joe from grabbing Tom. Joe cried out, "I'm so sorry, Tom! I can't get to you!" Tom was scared, but then he heard the wind whisper, "You trusted me, now trust yourself." Tom got an idea and he spread his arms out wide and let the wind take him away. When Tom was found, he had landed safely in a nearby valley. Unfortunately, he was too late. He had forgotten to trust himself sooner and was now in a coma. Tom had trusted the wind, but it had betrayed him in the end.

Sara likes to make things with her blocks. She has many blocks of different colors and shapes. She can make a house, a tower, a bridge, or anything she wants. One day, she wants to make a big tower that reaches the sky. She puts one block on top of another, and another, and another. She is very happy with her tower. It is very tall and strong. But then, her brother Tom comes into the room. He sees Sara's tower and he wants to play with it. He runs to the tower and tries to take a piece. But the tower is too strong for him. He pulls harder and harder, but the tower does not fall. Sara sees Tom and she is angry. She says, "Stop! That is my tower! You cannot take a piece! Go away!" Tom does not listen. He pulls even harder. He thinks he can make the tower weak. But he is wrong. The tower is not weak. It is very strong. It does not fall. But Tom falls. He pulls so hard that he loses his balance. He falls on the floor and hurts his knee. He cries and cries. Sara feels sorry for Tom. She knows he is hurt. She goes to him and gives him a hug. She says, "I am sorry, Tom. Do you want to play with me? We can make another tower together. A bigger and stronger one." Tom stops crying. He nods and smiles. He says, "Yes, Sara. I want to play with you. Thank you." They go to the blocks and start to make a new tower. A tower for both of them. A tower that reaches the sky.

Anna liked to play in the garden with her dolls. She gave them names and dresses and made them talk to each other. She also liked to pick flowers and put them in a vase on the table. She thought flowers were pretty and smelled nice. One day, she saw a new flower in the garden. It was big and yellow and had many petals. She wondered what it was called. She ran to the house and asked her mom. "Mom, what is this flower?" she said, holding the flower in her hand. "That is a sunflower, Anna. It is a very special flower. It follows the sun and blooms in the summer. Do you like it?" her mom said. "I love it, mom. It is so bright and happy. Can I keep it?" Anna said. "Of course, you can, sweetie. But you have to take good care of it. It needs water and light to grow. Why don't you put it in a vase and find a nice spot for it?" her mom said. Anna nodded and ran to the kitchen. She filled a vase with water and put the sunflower in it. She looked around for a place to put the vase. She saw a cupboard near the window. She thought it was a good place. She climbed on a chair and opened the cupboard. She was surprised by what she saw. Inside the cupboard, there were many books and toys and pictures. They belonged to her big brother, Tom. He was very clever and liked to read and learn. He also liked to hide his things from Anna. He said she was too little and curious. Anna was curious. She wanted to see what was in the cupboard. She reached for a book. It had a picture of a dinosaur on the cover. She liked dinosaurs. She wanted to look at the book. She forgot about the vase and the sunflower. She pulled the book out of the cupboard. But the book was heavy and the vase was not stable. The vase tipped over and fell to the floor. The water spilled and the sunflower broke. Anna heard a loud crash and a splash. She gasped and dropped the book. She looked at the mess and felt scared and sad. She heard her mom and Tom coming. She knew they would be angry and disappointed. She wished she had not touched the cupboard. She wished she had listened to her mom. She wished she had taken care of the sunflower. She started to cry.
