Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Fat Cat and the Grass

Once upon a time, in a sunny summer, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play outside with his friends, the birds and the bugs. One day, Tom saw something hiding in the grass. He was very curious. Tom went closer and asked, "Who is hiding there? Please come out and play with me!" Slowly, a little mouse named Jerry came out of the grass. Jerry was scared, but Tom said, "Don't worry, I am a nice cat. We can be friends!" So, Tom and Jerry played together all summer. They ran, they jumped, and they laughed. They had so much fun. And when the summer was over, they revealed a big secret: even a fat cat and a little mouse can be the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tower. The tower was very light and pretty. It stood in a big park where kids liked to play. One day, a little girl named Lily and her friend Tom went to the park. They saw the tower and wanted to go inside. "Let's go in the tower," said Lily. Tom agreed, and they went inside. The tower had many steps to climb, but they were not scared. They climbed and climbed until they reached the top. At the top, they saw a big, bright light. It made the tower light up. "Wow, look at the light!" said Tom. Lily smiled and said, "It's so pretty!" They stayed at the top of the tower, looking at the light until it was time to go home. They went down the steps and left the tower, happy with their adventure.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a chubby snowman. He had a big smile on his face and a carrot for a nose. He loved to play with the kids in the town. They would laugh and dance around him all day. One sunny day, the snowman started to feel very hot. He looked at his friend, a little girl named Lily, and said, "Lily, I feel like I am going to melt! What can I do?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "Let's ask the wise old owl for help!" Lily and the snowman went to the wise old owl, who lived in a big tree. The owl said, "Snowman, you must find a place that is very cold to keep your spirit happy. Go to the top of the mountain where the snow never melts." So, Lily and the snowman went on a journey to the top of the mountain. When they got there, the snowman felt much better. He was no longer too hot, and he didn't melt. The snowman's spirit was happy, and he could play with Lily and the other kids forever.

Once upon a time, there was a lady who lived in a small house. She had a big dog named Spot. They loved to play outside in the sun. One day, the lady and Spot went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. In the park, they met a little boy. The boy was scared of Spot. He started to tremble when he saw the big dog. The lady told the boy, "Don't worry, Spot is a nice dog. He won't hurt you." The boy said, "But he is so big! I'm scared!" The lady was careful and told Spot to sit down. Spot sat down and wagged his tail. The boy saw that Spot was a friendly dog. The lady told the boy, "See? Spot is a good dog. You can pet him if you want." The boy slowly reached out his hand and touched Spot's soft fur. He smiled and said, "You're right, he is a nice dog!" From that day on, the boy and the lady became good friends. They played together with Spot in the park every day. The boy learned not to be scared of big dogs, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, modern city. In this city, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a dream. She wanted to rescue animals that needed help. She told her mom and dad about her dream every day. One day, Lily saw a small cat stuck in a tree. She knew this was her chance to rescue the cat, just like in her dream. Lily's dad helped her find a long stick. They used the stick to help the cat come down from the tree. The cat was very happy to be safe. Lily felt proud that she could rescue the cat, just like she always dreamed. She knew that she could help more animals in the future. From that day on, Lily and her dad would go out and rescue animals together. They became known as the best animal rescuers in the modern city.
