Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Painting

Lily was a gifted girl who loved to paint. She could make beautiful pictures with her crayons and brushes. She liked to paint the sky, because it had many colors and shapes. Sometimes she saw birds, clouds, stars, and even rainbows in the sky. One day, Lily wanted to paint the sunset. She asked her mom if she could go outside and watch the sky. Her mom said yes, but she had to wear her coat and hat, because it was cold. Lily put on her warm clothes and took her paper and crayons. She ran to the backyard and looked at the sky. The sky was very pretty. It had orange, pink, purple, and blue. Lily started to draw what she saw. She made a big sun, some fluffy clouds, and a few birds. She was very happy with her painting. She wanted to show it to her mom. But then, she felt very tired. She had been painting for a long time, and it was getting dark. She yawned and rubbed her eyes. She decided to go back inside and finish her painting later. She folded her paper and put it in her pocket. She walked to the door and knocked. Her mom opened the door and smiled. She saw Lily's sleepy face and her paper. She asked her what she had been doing. Lily showed her mom her painting and said, "I painted the sunset, mom. It was very beautiful. But I'm very sleepy now. Can I go to bed?" Her mom hugged her and said, "Of course, sweetie. You did a wonderful job. You are a gifted painter. I'm very proud of you. Let's go to your room and tuck you in." She took Lily's hand and led her to her bedroom. She helped her change into her pajamas and tucked her in. She kissed her goodnight and turned off the light. Lily closed her eyes and smiled. She dreamed of the sky and her painting. She was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play outside on the earth. He would run and jump and chase butterflies. One sunny day, Fluffy was very tired from playing. He decided to take a nap under a big tree. The tree had lots of leaves that made a nice shade for Fluffy. He curled up and closed his eyes. While Fluffy napped, a little bird came and sat on the tree branch. The bird sang a happy song. Fluffy woke up and saw the bird. They became friends and played together until the sun went down. Then Fluffy went back to his warm home, feeling happy and loved.

One day, a grumpy cat named Tom saw a muffin on a table. He wanted to eat it, but it was too high for him. He tried to jump, but he could not reach it. Tom was sad. Tom asked his friend, a bird named Sally, to help him. Sally said, "I can help you get the muffin!" She flew up and took the muffin from the table. Tom was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. Sally dropped the muffin, and it fell on the ground. It broke into pieces. Tom was sad again. Sally said, "I'm sorry, Tom." Tom looked at the broken muffin and had an idea. He said, "Let's tear the muffin into small pieces and share it." They both ate the muffin together and were happy.

One day, a big ugly bug and a little red car had a race. They wanted to see who was faster. The bug said, "I am big and strong. I will win the race!" The little red car said, "I am small and fast. I will win the race!" They started the race. The ugly bug ran fast. The little red car went faster. They raced down the road. The little red car was winning the race. The ugly bug was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The little red car stopped. There was mail on the road. The little red car picked up the mail. The ugly bug won the race. The little red car said, "It is okay. I helped get the mail. We both did a good job!"
