Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Flute

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a flute. She loved to play the flute. She played it every day. Sue was happy when she played the flute. One day, Sue saw a boy named Tom. Tom wanted to play the flute too. But Sue was selfish. She did not want to share the flute with Tom. Tom was sad. He wanted to play the flute too. Sue saw that Tom was sad. She thought about it. She did not want to be selfish. So, Sue gave the flute to Tom. Tom was happy. He played the flute. Sue was happy too. They played the flute together. And they both learned that sharing is good.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very enthusiastic about playing games with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends decided to play a game with a pipe. They wanted to see who could throw the pipe the farthest. Tim's friend, Sam, said, "I can throw the pipe very far!" Tim smiled and said, "I want to try too!" They took turns throwing the pipe. Sam threw the pipe far, but Tim threw it even farther. Tim was so happy, he jumped up and down. In the end, Tim won the game. He and his friends cheered and clapped. They had so much fun playing with the pipe. Tim was proud of his win and couldn't wait to play more games with his friends.

It was a nice day, perfect for a picnic. Max and his mum set out early, with a blanket and a basket full of treats tucked under their arms. As they walked, the sun grew brighter and the wind began to rush. "Mum, can I have a treat now?" asked Max. "Wait, Max. We're almost there," said his mum in a tight voice. Soon, they had reached the park and put down the blanket. Max ran around and watched the other children, who were running and playing games. His mum unpacked the treats, and pushed one forward. "Here you go," she said. Max grabbed it and bit into it. The treat was sweet and crunchy. He looked up with delight. "Hey, this is way better than the ones I normally get!" Little did he know that his mum had been rushing because she wanted to surprise him with his favourite treat. It was the perfect setup and payoff, and Max was so happy he couldn't stop smiling.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big job to do. He had to deliver a letter to his friend, Sam. Tim was proud because this was his first time doing this all by himself. To get to Sam's house, Tim had to go through a network of paths in the park. Tim was a little scared, but he knew he could do it. As he walked, he saw a squirrel. The squirrel said, "Hi Tim! Where are you going?" Tim replied, "I am going to Sam's house to deliver a letter." The squirrel said, "I can show you the way!" So, Tim and the squirrel walked together through the network of paths. They saw pretty flowers and tall trees. Finally, they reached Sam's house. Tim gave the letter to Sam, and they both smiled. Tim felt proud that he had delivered the letter all by himself. And he was happy that he made a new friend, the squirrel, along the way.

Ellie loved to look at the sun, so each morning she would gaze out the window. The sun was so bright and warm that Ellie felt it was her best friend. One day, when she was on the balcony, she noticed something unusual. In the distance, a beautiful white horse was in a garden, nibbling on some bushes. "Look at the horse!" screamed Ellie. Her father came out to take a look. He said, "That horse looks very spoiled, you can tell it gets lots of attention!" Ellie agreed, the sun was making his coat very shiny. The horse, noticing Ellie and her father, stopped eating and trotted over to the fence. Ellie smiled and held out her hands. The horse leaned into her gentle touch, and they shared a moment. Ellie and her father smiled happily. Everyone knew that Ellie had made a new friend.
