Category: Intermediate
Title: The Hotel and the Chair

Once upon a time, there was a big hotel. In the hotel, there was a tall chair that could stand by itself. The chair was very proud and said, "Look at me! I can stand all alone!" One day, a small chair came to the hotel. It could not stand by itself. The tall chair laughed at the small chair and said, "You can't stand like me! I am better than you!" The small chair felt sad and jealous of the tall chair. But then, something unexpected happened. A strong wind blew through the hotel and knocked the tall chair down. The small chair, even though it was jealous, helped the tall chair to stand again. The tall chair said, "Thank you, small chair. I am sorry I was mean to you." From that day on, the tall chair and the small chair were friends. They learned that being kind is more important than being the best at something. And they lived happily ever after in the big hotel.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his friends in the park. One day, Spot saw a boy named Tim. Tim was wearing a red shirt. Spot ran to Tim and said, "Let's play!" Tim was scared because he thought Spot was too big. Spot felt sad. He did not want Tim to be scared of him. He wanted to be friends with Tim. Spot had an idea. He found a stick and went back to Tim. Spot snapped the stick with his teeth to show Tim a trick. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He said, "Good job, Spot! Let's play together!" Spot and Tim played all day and became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a dependable girl named Lucy. She was kind and loved to help others. One day, she saw her friend Tom trying to perform a task in his garden. Tom was having a hard time. Lucy went to Tom and asked, "What are you doing, Tom?" Tom said, "I am trying to clean up these leaves, but it's hard." Lucy knew just what to do. She went to her house and got her rake. It was a big, strong rake that could help them clean up the leaves. Lucy and Tom worked together, using the rake to clean up all the leaves. They laughed and had fun while they worked. Soon, the garden was clean and beautiful. Tom was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lucy! You are a very dependable friend." They both smiled and knew they could always count on each other.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom had a small house with a leak in the roof. Every time it rained, water would drip inside. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came to visit. Sam saw the leak and said, "Tom, I suggest you fix that leak, or your house will be wet all the time." Tom agreed, and they both went to the store. They bought a big, strong piece of wood and some nails. Then they went back to Tom's house and fixed the roof together. Now, Tom's house was dry and cozy. From that day on, Tom and Sam were the best of friends. They always helped each other and had lots of fun together. And Tom's house never had a leak again.

One day, a little girl had an adventure. In her backyard, she noticed a spider and she decided to gather it. She was very nervous to pick it up, but she was brave and did it anyway. When she decided to take it home, she was stopped by her mom. Mom said that the spider was too big and she was worried that it would scare the cat. The little girl was sad, because she wanted to be friends with the spider. She asked her mom if she could bring the spider a snack and make friends with it. Mom said yes and the little girl was very happy. She quickly gathered some food and returned to the backyard. Unfortunately, when she returned, the spider was gone. She looked around, but it was nowhere to be found. The little girl was very nervous and sad, but finally decided she would look for the spider tomorrow. With that, she headed back inside, hoping to find it the next day.
