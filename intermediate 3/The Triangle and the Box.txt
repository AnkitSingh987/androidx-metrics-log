Category: Intermediate
Title: The Triangle and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a big triangle. This triangle had two friends, Circle and Square. They played together every day. Triangle was very happy. One day, Circle and Square had a fight. They did not want to play together anymore. Triangle was sad. He said, "Please don't separate, we are friends." But Circle and Square did not listen. Triangle was envious of other shapes that played together. He had an idea. Triangle found a big box. He called Circle and Square to come inside the box. When they were inside, something unexpected happened. Circle and Square were stuck to each other. They had to work together to get out of the box. They learned that they needed each other. They became friends again and played with Triangle happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a wise old owl. The owl had a family of baby owls who lived in a tree. The wise owl loved his family a lot and wanted to teach them good things. One day, the wise owl told his family, "Always value each other and be good friends." The baby owls listened and nodded. They played together and were happy. But they did not know that something unexpected would happen soon. One sunny day, a big wind came and blew the baby owls out of the tree. They were scared and cried for help. A kind squirrel heard them and helped the baby owls to find each other. The baby owls remembered what the wise owl said, and they valued each other more than ever. They thanked the squirrel and went back to their tree, where the wise owl hugged them tight.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big, fluffy dog named Max. Tim and Max loved to play outside in the sun. They liked to run, jump, and laugh together. One day, Tim and Max were playing hide and seek. Tim hid behind a tree and whispered, "Max, come find me!" Max looked around, sniffed the air, and found Tim. They laughed and played some more. As they played, Tim stubbed his toe on a rock. He felt frightened and began to cry. Max saw Tim's hurt toe and licked it gently. Tim felt better and gave Max a big hug. They went home, happy and tired, ready for bedtime.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a big twisty slide. Mia was very happy and wanted to play on the slide. She saw a boy with a big smile on his face. The boy said, "Hi, I am Tom. Do you want to play with me?" Mia said, "Yes, let's play on the twisty slide!" They both climbed up and slid down the twisty slide. They laughed and had fun. But then, they saw a big, dangerous dog near the slide. The dog looked angry and it scared Mia and Tom. Tom said, "We should run away from the dangerous dog!" They tried to run away, but the dog was too fast. The dog caught them and bit their legs. Mia and Tom cried and went home with boo-boos. They learned to be careful around dangerous dogs.

Once upon a time, there was a little bear. The bear was fair and kind to everyone in the woods. He had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, the bear found a big box. He wanted to wrap the box with pretty paper. He thought it would be a nice gift for his friend, the bunny. So, the bear went to find some paper to wrap the box. He found some paper and started to wrap the box. His friends helped him make it look nice. When they were done, they gave the box to the bunny. The bunny was so happy and thanked the fair bear for the gift. They all played together and had a fun day.

Jill was a big girl who liked to care for animals. She had a dog, a cat, and a fish. She gave them food, water, and hugs every day. She was happy with her pets. One day, she saw a bird on the ground. It was small and brown, and it had a hurt wing. It looked helpless and sad. Jill wanted to help the bird. She ran to her mom and asked for a box and a cloth. Mom gave her the things and told her to be gentle. Jill put the cloth in the box and made a soft bed for the bird. She picked up the bird carefully and put it in the box. She gave it some water and seeds. She named it Penny, because it had a shiny spot on its head. Penny was scared at first, but soon it felt better. It chirped and ate and drank. Jill watched it and smiled. She was glad she could care for Penny. She hoped Penny would get well soon and fly again. She was a good friend to Penny.
