Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Boy and the Box

Once there was a boy named Max. He was three years old and he loved to put things together. One day, he found a box with funny looking parts in it. He took out one piece and saw it had a knob on it. He thought maybe it would be fun to put it together. Max got some tools and began to put the piece together. He made sure each part fit together nice and snug. He put the knob on last and heard a clicking sound. Max was feeling proud of himself and wanted to see how fast the piece worked. He quickly put the piece on the floor and gave it a push. It began to move fast across the floor. Max was very happy. Max had put the piece together perfectly, and it worked just as he had hoped. He kept playing with it all day, until it was time for a nap. And he never forgot how fun it was to put the piece together with the knob.

One day, a panda named Bob was in the forest. He was a creative panda. He liked to paint and draw. He made many pretty pictures. His friends, the other animals, would admire his art. One day, a big storm came. The wind blew hard. The rain fell fast. Bob's art got wet and messy. Bob was sad. He did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. The rain made the colors on his art run and mix. It made new and pretty colors. Bob was happy. He saw that the storm made his art even more beautiful. All his friends still admired his creative work.

Once upon a time, there was a mailman named Joe. Every day he went to the post office and picked up his mail. Every day he went out into the neighborhood and delivered mail. One day Joe came to a tall tree. He saw something strange on the top of the tree. He decided to climb up and take a look. When Joe got to the top of the tree, he saw a little house. There was a little door and a window. Joe opened the door and a strange creature said "Hello!" Joe was surprised. He asked the creature "What are you?" The creature answered "I'm a hard egg. I'm getting ready to become a bird." Joe asked the creature how he could help. The creature said "I need some mail. Can you help me get some mail? I can't fly yet." Joe smiled and said "I can help. I'm the mailman! I'll do my best to get you some mail." Joe went out into the neighborhood and collected mail for the creature. Soon enough, the creature was ready to fly. Joe watched from the ground as it became a bird and flew away. Joe was happy to have helped the creature and was thankful for its mail.

One day, Peter and Mommy were in the living room. Peter was playing with his blocks. Mommy said, "Let's load up the car!" Peter was happy, he said "Yay!" When Mommy and Peter went outside, the car was very smelly. Mommy said, "Oh no, our car has a bad smell!" Peter didn't understand, he said "What, Mommy?" Mommy replied, "We can't drive this car, it's too smelly." But then, a big truck drove by. It was the best truck ever! Mommy said, "We'll drive this truck instead!" So, Peter and Mommy loaded up the blocks in the back of the truck. But then, the truck driver yelled, "Hey! What are you doing? Put the blocks back!" Mommy and Peter quickly unloaded the blocks and ran away. Peter was sad that they couldn't take the blocks, but at least they were safe. The truck driver was a bad guy, and Mommy and Peter were lucky that nothing bad happened.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big slide and wanted to ride it. So, he climbed up and slid down, laughing all the way. His mom smiled and clapped her hands. After playing on the slide, Tim saw a man with a big box. The man said, "Hello, would you like a lollipop?" Tim looked at his mom, and she said it was okay. He picked a red lollipop and said, "Thank you!" The man was grateful and smiled. Tim and his mom sat on a bench to eat the lollipop. It was sweet and yummy. They talked about the fun day at the park, and Tim was happy. He knew he had the best mom in the world.
