Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Match

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim wanted to play a match. He was very independent and liked to win. Tim wanted to play with his ball, but his big sister, Sue, took it. This made Tim sad and mad. Tim asked Sue to give the ball back, but Sue said no. She wanted to win too. They both wanted to play the match and win. So, they had an idea. They would play the match together, and the winner would get the ball. They played the match, running and kicking the ball. Tim tried his best, but Sue was older and faster. In the end, Sue won the match. Tim felt sad, but he knew he tried his best. He was still independent and would try again another day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a very independent girl who liked to do things by herself. One day, she found a baby bird on the ground. The infant bird was all alone and needed help. Lily decided to take care of the baby bird. She said, "I will help you, little bird, and we will be friends." She fed the bird, and they played together every day. As the bird grew, it learned to fly and became strong. One day, the bird told Lily, "I want to marry another bird and start a family." Lily knew that she had to let the bird go to be happy. She said, "I will miss you, but you need to follow your heart." The bird flew away and found a bird to marry. Lily was sad, but she knew she had done the right thing. The moral of the story is that sometimes we have to let go of the ones we love to help them be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy and he liked to behave. He always listened to his mom and dad. One day, Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked down a long road. The road was easy to walk on because it was flat and smooth. As they walked, Tim saw a big, red ball on the side of the road. He wanted to play with it. Tim's mom said, "Tim, you must behave and not pick up things from the road." Tim listened to his mom and kept walking. He felt sad but knew it was important to behave. At the end of the road, they found a park. Tim's mom had a surprise for him. She took out a new, blue ball from her bag. Tim was so happy! He played with the ball all day at the park. Tim learned that when he behaves, good things happen.

One day, a fast cat named Max met a slow turtle named Tim. Max and Tim were very different, but they liked to play together. They liked to guess things, like what color a flower was or how many leaves were on a tree. One day, Max and Tim met a new friend, a bird named Bella. Bella spoke a different language. Max and Tim did not understand her. They wanted to be friends, so they tried to guess what she was saying. Max said, "I guess Bella is saying 'hello'!" Tim said, "I guess Bella wants to play!" Max and Tim tried to talk to Bella, but they still did not understand her language. Bella saw that Max and Tim were trying very hard. She decided to teach them her language. Soon, Max, Tim, and Bella were all talking and playing together. They were very happy to be friends and learn new things from each other.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing outside. She saw her friend Tom kneeling by a tree. Tom had a bandage on his leg. He looked sad. Amy ran to him and asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "I fell and hurt my leg. My mom put this bandage on it, but I can't run and play now. I feel spoiled." Amy thought for a moment. She remembered her mom telling her that when she was hurt, a little rest would make her feel better. So, Amy told Tom, "Let's sit under the tree and rest for a while. Maybe you will feel better soon." They sat under the tree and talked. After some time, Tom tried to stand up. To his surprise, his leg felt better! He could walk without pain. Amy smiled and said, "See, I told you! A little rest can make you feel better." Tom was happy and thanked Amy. They held hands and went back to playing together.
