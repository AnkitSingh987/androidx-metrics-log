Category: Intermediate
Title: The Delicate Raven and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a delicate raven. She had a nest with her baby birds. The baby birds were hungry, so the delicate raven went to find food for them. One day, the delicate raven saw a big red fruit. She wanted to take it to her babies, but it was too close to a big dog. The big dog looked scary. The delicate raven was scared, but she wanted to feed her babies. The delicate raven waited for the big dog to sleep. When the big dog was asleep, she took the big red fruit and flew back to her nest. The baby birds were very happy and they ate the big red fruit. The delicate raven learned that being patient and careful can help her and her family stay safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a book that he loved to read every day. One day, Tim was reading his book when he saw a strange page. The page had a picture of a big, rude cat. The cat had a mean face and big teeth. Tim was not happy with the rude cat. He wanted the cat to be nice. Tim said, "Cat, you need to be nice. You can't be rude." But the cat did not change. The cat looked at Tim and said, "No, I will not be nice. I demand you to turn the page." Tim was scared, but he was also brave. He decided to turn the page and see what would happen. When he turned the page, he saw that the rude cat was now a small, nice cat. The cat said, "Thank you, Tim. I was only rude because I was so big and scary. Now that I am small, I can be nice." Tim was happy that the cat was nice now. They became good friends and had many fun adventures together. And from that day on, the cat was never rude again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little tree. The tree had rough bark and many colors on its leaves. It loved to stretch its branches high up in the sky. The little tree wanted to be the tallest tree in the forest. One day, a wise old tree saw the little tree stretching. The old tree told the little tree that it was good to stretch, but it should also help others. The little tree did not understand why it should help others. Soon, the little tree saw a small bird with a broken wing. The bird could not fly to its nest. The little tree decided to help the bird. It stretched its branches and let the bird hop onto them. The bird was happy and said thank you. The little tree felt good for helping the bird. It started to help more animals in the forest. The wise old tree saw this and was proud of the little tree. The little tree learned that helping others made it feel happy and tall inside. And so, the little tree with the rough bark and many colors on its leaves became the kindest tree in the forest. All the animals loved the little tree. The little tree learned that being kind and helping others was more important than being the tallest tree.

One day, a kind girl named Sue found a little mug. The mug was sad. Sue wanted to help the mug feel happy again. Sue asked the mug, "Why are you sad?" The mug did not know how to tell her. Sue thought of a plan. She would find a way to make the mug happy. Sue took the mug to her room. She put the mug next to her toys. The mug saw the toys and felt happy. Sue was happy too. The kind girl and the little mug played together every day.

Once upon a time, a little worm named Wiggly lived in a big garden. Wiggly was not a scary worm. Wiggly was a nice worm. He liked to play with his friends, the bugs and the birds. They all had fun in the sun. One day, Wiggly was playing with his best friend, a little bug named Buzz. They were playing a game of hide and seek. Wiggly found a big log to hide under. But when he hid under the log, he found a big, scary spider! "Help!" Wiggly cried. Buzz heard him and came to help. Buzz saw the scary spider and said, "Don't worry, Wiggly. We can split the spider's web and run away!" So, Buzz and Wiggly split the spider's web and ran away as fast as they could. The scary spider was sad but Wiggly and Buzz were safe. They played in the garden and had fun with their friends. Wiggly and Buzz were happy and they knew they would always help each other when they were in trouble.
