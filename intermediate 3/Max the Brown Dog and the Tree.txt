Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Brown Dog and the Tree

One day, a brown dog named Max went to play in the park. He saw a big open space and ran to it. There, he met a little girl named Lily. Lily said, "Hi, I'm Lily. What's your name?" Max barked, "Woof! I'm Max." Lily wanted to introduce Max to her friend, a cat named Bella. They walked to Bella's house. When they got there, they saw Bella on the roof! Bella was too scared to come down. Lily said, "Oh no, Bella! How did you get up there?" Max had an idea. He ran and found a big, strong tree. He pushed the tree until it fell down next to the house. Bella climbed down the tree to the ground. Bella said, "Thank you, Max!" And they all played together in the big open space.

Lily was a little girl who liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite one was Anna. Anna had long hair, a pretty dress, and a smile on her face. Lily valued Anna very much and always took her to bed with her. One night, Lily had a bad dream. She dreamed that a big monster came into her room and took Anna away. Lily tried to scream, but no sound came out. She tried to run, but her feet were stuck. She felt very scared and sad. She wanted Anna back. She woke up with a loud cry. Her mom and dad heard her and came to her room. They saw Lily crying and holding Anna tightly. They hugged her and asked her what was wrong. "I had a nightmare, mommy. A monster took Anna away. I was so afraid. I love Anna. She is my best friend." "Oh, honey, it was just a dream. No monster can take Anna away from you. She is safe with you. We love you and Anna too. You are very brave and kind to value your doll so much." They kissed her and tucked her in. They turned on a small lamp and sang her a lullaby. Lily felt better and calmed down. She snuggled with Anna and closed her eyes. She hoped to have a peaceful dream. She knew her mom and dad and Anna were always there for her.

Once upon a time, there was a bright room with a big carpet. In the room, there was a boy named Tim and his dog, named Spot. They loved to play on the carpet every day. One day, they were playing with a ball. Tim would throw the ball and Spot would run to get it. As they played, the ball went under the carpet. Tim and Spot looked at each other, surprised. They had never seen this happen before. They both went to the edge of the carpet to start lifting it up. They wanted to get the ball back. But when they lifted the carpet, they found a secret door under it! Tim and Spot looked at each other, excited. They opened the door and found a new, fun room to play in. They played in the secret room all day long. And from that day on, they had more fun than ever before.

One day, a tall tree and a small bush were talking in the sun. The tall tree said, "I love being tall. I can see so much from up here." The small bush nodded and said, "I wish I could see more too." The tall tree felt sad for the small bush. So, the tall tree had an idea. "I can help you see more," the tall tree said. The small bush nodded and said, "How can you help me?" The tall tree told the small bush to climb on its branches. The small bush climbed and climbed until it was very high. Now, the small bush could see far away. The small bush was happy and said, "Thank you, tall tree!" The tall tree nodded and smiled in the sun. They were happy friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Emma. She loved to organize her toys and her room. She liked to invite her friend Jane to play. One day, Emma was organizing her room and she wanted her friend Jane to come over to join her. She asked her mom to call Jane to come over and play. When Jane arrived, Emma was very excited to see her. She said to Jane with a smile, "Hi Jane! I'm organizing my room, do you want to help me?" Jane was very friendly and said, "Yes, I'd love to help you". So they both started to organize together. They put Emma's toys in their place, put the blankets in the wardrobe, and put the books in the shelf. When they finished organizing the room, they both felt very happy. Emma and Jane laughed and hugged. Emma said, "Thanks for your help. I really like having you as my friend". Jane replied, "Me too! It was so much fun organizing together." The two friends enjoyed their time organizing together. They promised to always be good and friendly to each other.
