Category: Intermediate
Title: Melinda and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Melinda. She was always very shy, so she stayed close to her mum and dad. One day, Melinda and her mum went to the park. They brought a big bin with them. Melinda was very curious about the bin, so she went over to it and started to examine it. She opened it and looked inside. She saw lots of interesting things like balls, bowls and books. She picked up each item carefully and looked them all over. After she finished examining the items, Melinda started to play catch with her mum. She was still a bit shy, but she had lots of fun. When it was time to go, they put all the things back in the bin and Melinda carried it back to the car. The next day, Melinda and her mum went back to the park. She was still a bit shy, but she was so excited to examine the bin again!

Once upon a time, in a far away land, there was a big race. All the animals came to play and have fun. The sun was shining, but the wind was icy. The animals knew they had to unite to stay warm. The rabbit said to the bear, "Let's all run fast and close in the race. That way, we can stay warm from the icy wind." The bear agreed and told all the other animals. They all got ready to run the race together. As the race began, the animals ran fast and close, just like the rabbit said. They felt warm even though the wind was icy. At the end of the race, they were all happy and warm. The animals learned that when they unite, they can beat the cold wind.

Tim and Mia were making a cake with Mom. They liked to help Mom in the kitchen. They had a big bowl, some eggs, some flour, some sugar, and some butter. Mom said they needed to stir everything well. "Can I stir, Mom?" Tim asked. "Me too, me too!" Mia said. "Okay, you can both stir, but be careful not to spill," Mom said. She gave them each a wooden spoon. Tim and Mia started to stir the bowl. They stirred fast and slow, up and down, left and right. "Look, Mom, I made a knot!" Tim said. He showed Mom his spoon. It had a big lump of dough stuck to it. Mom smiled. "That's a very nice knot, Tim, but we need to make the cake smooth and soft," she said. She helped Tim to untie the knot and put it back in the bowl. "I made a brown cake!" Mia said. She showed Mom her spoon. It had some chocolate on it. Mom had added some chocolate chips to the batter. Mom smiled. "That's a very yummy brown cake, Mia, but we need to bake it in the oven first," she said. She helped Mia to scrape the chocolate off her spoon and put it back in the bowl. Mom poured the batter into a cake pan and put it in the oven. She set the timer and said, "Now we wait for the cake to be ready. Do you want to help me clean up?" Tim and Mia nodded. They liked to help Mom in the kitchen. They washed their spoons and their hands. They wiped the table and the floor. They sang a song and danced a little. Ding! The timer rang. The cake was ready. Mom took it out of the oven and let it cool. She cut a slice for Tim and a slice for Mia. She put some whipped cream and some sprinkles on top. "Here you go, my little helpers. Enjoy your cake," Mom said. "Thank you, Mom. You are the best," Tim and Mia said. They ate their cake and smiled. It was delicious. They gave Mom a big hug and a kiss. They loved to help Mom in the kitchen.

Once upon a time there were two young girls, Tina and Betty. They were both very curious and wanted to explore the world. One day, they decided to design their own escape game. Betty ran to get some paper while Tina brought a few markers. They sat on the floor, drew shapes and and wrote words together. They got so excited that they shouted "Eureka!" "Let's test it," said Tina. So, they went to the backyard garden and played their game. They moved around and followed the simple instructions they had created. Suddenly, Tina ran back to the house. "I have a great idea!" said she. "Let's make an easier game for the little ones!" Fun and laughter echoed through the garden as they designed a simple game for their younger friends. In the end, all the young children had a great time playing the game.
