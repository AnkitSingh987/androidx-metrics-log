Title: Ben and the Pond

One day, Ben was outside. Ben took his jeep and drove to the pond. He wanted to splash in the pond. He parked his jeep and ran to the pond. When he got to the pond, Ben made big splashes. He was very cheerful. He laughed and splashed for a long time. After the splashing, he was tired. He drove his jeep home and hugged his mom. Ben was very happy and cheerful.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia was wearing her adorable new dress that her mom bought for her. She loved to dress up in cute fashion. At the park, Mia saw her friend, Tom. Tom was playing with a big red ball. Mia ran to Tom and said, "Hi Tom! Do you like my new dress? My mom got it for me." Tom looked at Mia's dress and said, "Yes, Mia! Your dress is very pretty. Let's play together." Mia and Tom played with the ball and had so much fun. They were getting thirsty, so they went to the water fountain to fill their cups. They drank the water and felt happy. Mia and Tom played in the park all day, wearing their cute fashion and having a great time. They loved being with each other and couldn't wait to play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a kind old man who wanted to give his grandchild a special gift. He was very thoughtful and he couldn't think of what to give her. On the day of his grandchild's birthday, the man had a surprise for her. He went to her door with a present in his hands. When she opened the door, the man gave her a rake. The little girl was confused. She asked the old man, "What is it for?" The old man replied, "You will receive many blessings if you use it wisely." The grandchild thanked him but had no idea what he meant. She thanked him, took the rake and gave him a hug. As the months went by, the little girl successfully used the rake to clean up the garden. Gradually, she started to receive all sorts of blessings - a bushel of fresh vegetables, beautiful flowers and a generous supply of firewood. At some point, the man visited her again, and he said, "Now you know what I meant when I gave you the rake. I knew you would receive many blessings if you used it wisely." The little girl was so thankful for her thoughtful present. From that moment on, she always raked the garden, proving that the old man had been right all along.

Once upon a time, in a far away planet, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play with her friends, the stars. The stars were very easy to talk to and they would always listen to Mia's stories. They would jump and dance in the sky as they played together. One day, Mia met a new friend named Mr. Moon. Mr. Moon was very big and round. Mia asked, "Do you want to play with me and the stars?" Mr. Moon smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you and your friends!" So, Mia, Mr. Moon, and the stars played together all night. They laughed and had so much fun. When it was time for Mia to go home, she gave Mr. Moon and the stars a big hug. Then, she gave each of her friends a kiss on the cheek. "Good night, friends," Mia said. "I will see you tomorrow!" From that day on, Mia, Mr. Moon, and the stars would play together every night. And every night, Mia would give each of her friends a kiss before going to bed. They were the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after on their beautiful planet.

Once there was a boy named John. He would measure things all the time. Measuring was his favorite thing to do. One day he went out measuring but he measuring was so high he got sent to jail! The jail was a scary place. John cried and asked why he was in jail. The guard said, "You measured too high. That's why you're here." John said, "But why do I have to stay here?" The guard said, "If you want to get out of jail you have to measure within the rules." John thought a lot but he still didn't understand why. So, he tried measuring a lot of different things. But he always kept it within the rules. After a while, the guard said, "You measure so well that I'm going to let you out of jail." John was so happy he ran home and measured with caution from then on. The end.
