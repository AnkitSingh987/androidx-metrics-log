Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Clever Spider and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a clever little spider named Sam. Sam lived in a big tree with a lot of cobwebs. Sam loved to watch his cobwebs grow bigger and stronger every day. One day, a big bird named Bob came to the tree. Bob saw Sam's cobwebs and said, "I want to eat you, little spider!" Sam was scared but he had a clever idea. He said, "Wait! If you let my cobwebs grow more, I can make a big, soft bed for you to sleep in." Bob thought about it and agreed. He said, "Okay, I will come back in three days. If you make a big, soft bed for me, I won't eat you." Sam was happy and started to work on the bed right away. Three days later, Bob came back to the tree. He saw the big, soft bed made of cobwebs that Sam had made for him. Bob was very happy and said, "Thank you, clever spider! I won't eat you. We can be friends." Sam was happy too, and they both lived together in the big tree, watching the cobwebs grow.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with a little girl named Sue. Sue and Kitty loved to play together all day. One sunny day, Kitty was playing with her favorite toy, a small ball. She chased the ball around the house, and it rolled into the bathroom. The ball went right into the toilet! Kitty tried to get the ball out, but her paw got all wet. She didn't like the water, so she ran away. Sue saw Kitty's wet paw and knew what happened. She went to the bathroom and found the ball in the toilet. Sue used a cup to get the ball out. Then, she let the ball soak in warm, soapy water to make it clean again. Kitty was so happy to have her ball back, and she played with Sue all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. It had many leaves. In the fall, the leaves turned red, yellow, and orange. The tree looked very pretty. The wind would blow, and the leaves would fall to the ground. One day, a small bird came to the tree. The bird was sad because it had no place to live. The tree wanted to help the bird. The tree suggested, "You can live in my branches." The bird was happy and said, "That is a fine idea!" So, the bird made a small nest in the tree. Every day, the bird would sing a happy song. The tree and the bird became good friends. They were happy together in the fall, with the fine leaves all around them. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big clock. The clock was very original. It could talk and weigh things. The clock lived in a small house with a boy named Tim. One day, Tim wanted to weigh his toy car. He asked the clock, "Can you weigh my car?" The clock said, "Yes, I can do that." The clock weighed the car and told Tim, "Your car is light." Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, clock!" They played together every day. Tim loved his original talking clock. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy box. A little boy named Tim found the box in his room. He did not know what was inside, so he wanted to open it. Tim tried to tear the box open with his small hands, but it was too hard. He asked his mom for help. She tore the box open for him. Inside the box, they found a toy thief. The toy thief was a funny man who liked to take things. Tim played with the toy thief all day. They had a lot of fun together. But Tim also learned that taking things without asking is not nice. At the end of the day, Tim put the toy thief back in the heavy box. He knew that it was just a toy, and that he should always ask before taking something. Tim and his mom were happy, and they lived happily ever after.
