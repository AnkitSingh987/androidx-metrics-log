Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Woods

Tim was wandering in the woods. As he was looking around, he was amazed at how messy the woods were. Suddenly, he heard a sound. It was a tiny voice calling his name. He followed the sound until he found a small little creature dressed in green. The creature said, "You were wandering the woods. May I join you?" Tim smiled and said, "Of course!" The creature showed Tim the hidden secrets of the woods. They rambled through the trees, playing hide and seek. They laughed and joked as they went. Tim soon realized that the mess of the woods was actually quite beautiful. The sky turned orange and Tim knew it was time to go home. He thanked the creature for showing him the beauty of the woods. The creature smiled and waved goodbye as Tim wandered off in the direction of home.

John had a big problem. His family was very poor, and he didn't have enough money to buy food for everyone. So he thought up a clever plan. He would get his friends to help him. He went to talk to them, and he was very excited. He told them that he wanted to deliver a threat to a rich man who lived in the village. He thought the man would give them some money for it. His friends were very scared, but eventually they agreed. So, John and his friends went to deliver the message. When they arrived, the rich man was outraged. He said that he wouldn't give them any money and that they should leave. John and his friends were disappointed, and they started to walk away. Suddenly, the man called out to them. He said that if they didn't leave right away, he would get his very expensive security guard to deliver a different kind of threat. John had no choice. He and his friends quickly ran away, never to return. They were so scared that they never told anyone what had happened. John was devastated. His plan had backfired, and he still didn't have enough money to feed his family. He had to learn the hard way that sometimes, even the best plans can have bad endings.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a humble man. He had a little house with a close door. The man was very kind and always helped others. One day, he found a big box. The box was full of gold! He was very happy. The humble man wanted to share the gold with his friends in the town. He took the gold and gave some to each person. They all said thank you and smiled. The humble man felt good. The town was full of happy people, all because of the humble man with the close door.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They were having a fun day, playing and laughing. Tim's mom had a big bag of popcorn for them to eat. She said, "Tim, don't eat too much popcorn or you won't fit in your car seat later!" Tim didn't listen to his mom. He loved popcorn so much that he ate the whole bag! His mom looked serious and said, "Tim, I told you not to eat all the popcorn. Now, let's see if you fit in your car seat." When it was time to go home, Tim tried to sit in his car seat. But he was too big! His mom said, "I told you, Tim. Now we have to walk home." Tim was sad, but he knew his mom was right. Next time, he would listen to her and not eat all the popcorn.

Tim and Lily were playing in the park with their sand toys. They liked to make castles and bridges and tunnels. They saw a pigeon on the grass. It was not moving. Tim went closer and touched it. It was cold and stiff. "Look, Lily, a pigeon!" Tim said. "It is sleeping." Lily came and looked at the pigeon. She shook her head. "No, Tim, it is not sleeping. It is dead. That means it will not wake up. It is sad." Tim felt sad too. He liked pigeons. They were soft and pretty. He wanted to do something nice for the pigeon. He had an idea. "Let's bury the pigeon, Lily. That means we put it under the sand. Then it can rest and be safe." Lily nodded. She thought it was a good idea. They dug a hole with their shovels and put the pigeon inside. They covered it with sand and put some flowers on top. They said goodbye to the pigeon. They were about to go back to their castles when they heard a loud noise. It was a big dog. It was running towards them. It saw the flowers and smelled the sand. It started to dig with its paws. It wanted to find the pigeon. Tim and Lily were scared and angry. They shouted at the dog. "Stop, dog, stop! That is our pigeon! You cannot have it! Go away!" The dog did not listen. It barked and dug faster. It found the pigeon and took it in its mouth. It ran away with the pigeon. Tim and Lily cried. They felt very sad and very angry. They had lost their pigeon. They hugged each other and went to find their mom. They told her what happened. She hugged them and said she was sorry. She said they were very kind to the pigeon. She said they could go and feed some other pigeons if they wanted. Tim and Lily nodded. They still liked pigeons. They hoped the dog would not hurt the pigeon. They hoped the pigeon would find peace.
