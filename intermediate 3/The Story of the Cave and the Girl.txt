Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cave and the Girl

One day a little girl decided to march around the field. She was so happy and she felt free as she marched. As she marched along, suddenly she felt something on her shoe. Looking down, she realized it was a magnet. Quickly she picked it up and kept marching. As she kept marching, she noticed an open cave. She got closer and realized the cave smelled funny. Intrigued, she went inside and looked around. Then she heard a loud noise. Suddenly, the cave started to close and the little girl was stuck inside! She started to cry, feeling scared and sad. After a while, someone heard her cries and was able to open the cave again. But the girl learned her lesson and never went into any strange places ever again!

One day, a fox was walking in the forest. He saw a big lake with clear water. He was thirsty, so he went to drink. But when he looked at his reflection, he saw that his fur was dirty and messy. "Oh no!" he said. "I need to clean myself. I want to look nice and shiny." He jumped into the water and started to scrub his fur with his paws. He splashed and played and had fun. He felt happy and fresh. But then he heard a voice behind him. It was a serious owl, who was sitting on a branch. "Fox, what are you doing?" the owl asked. "You are wasting time and water. You should be hunting and learning, not playing and cleaning. You are a wild animal, not a pet." The fox felt ashamed. He got out of the water and shook his fur. "I'm sorry, owl," he said. "I just wanted to look nice and shiny. I didn't mean to be silly." The owl looked at him with his big eyes. "Fox, you don't need to look nice and shiny. You need to be smart and strong. That's what makes you a good fox. And you are not silly. You are curious and playful. That's what makes you a happy fox. But you also need to balance your fun and your work. That's what makes you a wise fox." The fox nodded. He understood what the owl said. "Thank you, owl," he said. "You are a good friend. You teach me a lot. Can I ask you a question?" "Of course, fox," the owl said. "What do you want to know?" The fox smiled. "Can I clean myself again tomorrow?" he asked.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a brilliant idea. He wanted to play with his friends outside. Tim took his favorite blanket with him. The blanket was soft and warm. He loved his blanket very much. Tim and his friends played all day. They played catch, they played hide and seek, and they played tag. Tim's blanket was always with him. When he was tired, he sat on the blanket and rested for a while. At the end of the day, it was time to go home. Tim was very tired. He forgot his blanket outside. When he got home, he looked for his blanket but could not find it. Tim was very sad. He went to bed without his blanket, and he cried all night.

Once upon a time, there was a strong metal robot named Robby. Robby was a very helpful robot. He liked to help his friends improve their skills. One day, Robby met a little bird named Benny. Benny was sad because he could not fly very well. Robby wanted to help Benny improve his flying. They went to the park to practice together. Robby said, "Benny, flap your wings faster!" Benny tried and started to fly better. They practiced every day. Benny became a strong flyer. Now, Benny and Robby play together in the sky. They are the best of friends.

One day, Tom and Sam wanted to build a big fort. They were gifted at making things. They went to the east side of the park to find the best spot. Tom said, "I want to build here!" Sam said, "No, I want to build there!" They could not agree on where to build the fort. They felt sad and mad at each other. Then, a nice dog came and barked at them. Tom and Sam laughed. They decided to build the fort together in the middle of the park. They were happy and had fun playing in their new fort.
