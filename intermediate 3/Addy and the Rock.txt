Category: Intermediate
Title: Addy and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a big, smooth rock. On this rock, there lived a small bug named Addy. Addy loved to add things to her rock. One day, Addy found a pretty leaf. She added the leaf to her rock. Then, she found a small twig. Addy added the twig to her rock too. She was very happy with her rock. One day, Addy met a new friend named Lung. Lung was a small fish who lived in the water near the rock. Lung liked Addy's rock and wanted to help her add more things to it. Together, Addy and Lung found more leaves, twigs, and even a small flower. They added all of these things to the rock. The rock was now very pretty. Addy and Lung were very happy with their rock. They had many fun days playing on the smooth rock with all the things they added. And they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. He liked to sleep all day and play all night. One day, Tom saw a spade in the garden. It was big and shiny. He wanted to play with it. Tom went to his friend, a little mouse named Jerry. "Jerry, let's play a game with the spade!" said Tom. Jerry liked games, so he said, "Okay, Tom! Let's play a game called 'match the holes'." They started to play the game. Tom used the spade to dig holes in the ground. Jerry tried to match the holes by jumping into them. They played and played, and were having so much fun. Suddenly, while digging a hole, Tom found a big box. He opened it and saw many toys inside! Tom and Jerry were so happy. They played with the toys and forgot all about the spade and the game. From that day on, Tom wasn't lazy anymore. He loved playing with the toys and his friend Jerry.

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there was a lot of snow. The snow was white and soft. It covered the ground like a big, fluffy blanket. All the kids in the town loved to play in the snow. They would make snowmen, have snowball fights, and slide down hills on sleds. One morning, the sun began to rise, and the sky turned pink and orange. The kids woke up and saw the snow outside their windows. They put on their warm clothes and ran outside to play. The snow was so deep that they had to walk very carefully. They laughed and played all day long. As the sun started to set, the kids knew it was time to go home. They said goodbye to their friends and walked back to their warm houses. The charming little town was quiet again, with only the soft sound of snow falling. The kids went to bed, dreaming of more fun days in the snow.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. Her name was Lily and she was only three. One day, Lily's parents took her to the museum. It was very big and open. When they arrived, Lily was so excited. She loved looking at all the different things. But then suddenly, Lily started to weep. She was so sad! Everyone looked around, wondering why she was so upset. Lily's dad came over and asked what was wrong. She said the museum was too open. Everywhere she looked there were so many people. It made her feel very small and lonely. But Lily's dad hugged her tight and said, "It's ok. It's ok to be sad sometimes. We can stay here as long as you want, and I'll never leave your side." Lily smiled and stopped weeping. She felt safe with her dad beside her. From that day on, Lily loved the museum. She was never afraid to explore and have fun!

One day, a boy named Tim felt miserable. He had very long hair and needed a haircut. His hair was so long that it covered his eyes, and he couldn't see well. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Tim, it's time for a haircut!" Tim and his mom went to the haircut place. The haircut man said, "Hi Tim! Sit in the big chair, and I will give you a haircut." Tim sat in the big chair, and the haircut man pulled a cape around him. Tim felt better already. The haircut man started to snip, snip, snip. Tim's hair fell to the floor. When the haircut man was done, Tim's hair was short and neat. Tim looked in the mirror and smiled. He was not miserable anymore. He said, "Thank you, haircut man!" And they all went home happy.
