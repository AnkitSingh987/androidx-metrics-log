Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Pupil and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little pupil named Tim. Tim loved to collect rocks. He had a big box full of rocks in his room. One day, Tim went to the park to find more rocks. He saw a big, round rock near a tree. Tim was so happy. He said, "Wow! This rock is so big! I want to take it home." But when he tried to pick it up, he heard a loud noise. Tim got frightened. A small squirrel came out from behind the tree. The squirrel said, "Don't be scared! That was just me. I was hiding behind the tree." Tim smiled and said, "Oh, I was so frightened! But now I am happy to meet you." The squirrel and Tim became friends and collected rocks together.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. They saw a big hill with a lot of rocks. They wanted to climb it. "Can we go up there?" Tom asked Lily. "OK, but be careful. There are spikes on some rocks. They can hurt you." Lily said. They held hands and started to climb. They saw a lot of flowers, bugs and birds. They felt happy and brave. They reached the top of the hill. They saw a big lake with ducks and fish. They also saw a man with a boat. "Hello, kids. Do you want to ride my boat?" The man asked. "Yes, please!" Tom and Lily said. "OK, but you have to request nicely. Say please and thank you." The man said. "Please, can we ride your boat?" Tom and Lily requested. "Sure, hop in. But wear these vests. They will keep you safe and fine." The man said. He gave them vests and helped them into the boat. He rowed them around the lake. They saw more ducks, fish and frogs. They had a lot of fun. "Thank you for the ride, sir." Tom and Lily said. "You're welcome, kids. You are very polite and kind." The man said. He took them back to the shore. They waved goodbye and ran back to their mom. "Mom, we rode a boat!" Tom and Lily said. "Wow, that sounds exciting. Did you have a good time?" Mom asked. "Yes, we did. And we saw spikes, flowers, bugs, birds, ducks, fish and frogs." Tom and Lily said. "That's wonderful. You are very brave and curious." Mom said. She hugged them and gave them a snack. They ate and drank and told her more about their adventure. They felt fine and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Sally. Sally liked to play in her garden. One day, a ladder arrived in her garden. Sally was very excited because she had never seen one before. She ran straight outside to take a closer look. She touched the ladder and it felt very cold. Suddenly, Sally heard a really noisy noise coming from the top of the ladder. She was careful and looked up to see what was causing the noise. It was a little bird chirping away! Sally was so happy that she smiled from ear to ear. She climbed up the ladder to get closer to the bird. "Hello, little bird. What's your name?" asked Sally. The bird replied, "My name is Harry. Nice to meet you Sally!" Sally was very happy to have made a new friend! She and Harry played together in the garden until it was time for Sally to go inside. They said goodbye and waved happily to each other. From then on, every time the ladder arrived in Sally's garden, it was noisy as there were always birds waiting to say hello to Sally!

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She lived in a big castle. The princess had a problem. It was very hot in her room. She needed to solve this problem. The princess thought of a plan. She opened her window to let the cool air in. The hot room started to feel better. The princess was happy that her plan worked. Now, the princess could play with her toys and have fun. She solved her hot room problem. The princess lived happily in her cool room ever after.
