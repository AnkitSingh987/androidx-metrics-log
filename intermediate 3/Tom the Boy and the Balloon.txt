Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Balloon

One day, a boy named Tom went to a food place with his mom. They sat down and looked at the menu. Tom thought the menu was boring. It had only food he did not like. He told his mom, "I don't want to eat here. It's boring!" His mom said, "Let's wait a little more. Maybe we can find something you like." So they delayed and looked at the menu again. Tom was sad and did not want to eat the boring food. Just then, a big, funny clown walked in! The clown started to make balloon animals and funny faces. Tom laughed and clapped his hands. The clown made a special balloon for Tom. It was a big, red dog! Tom loved it. He told his mom, "This place is not boring anymore!" They stayed and ate their food, and Tom had a fun time.

Once upon a time, there was a nice lawyer named Tom. Tom liked to help people. One day, a little girl named Lisa came to him. She was sad because she lost her toy. Tom wanted to help Lisa find her toy. Tom asked Lisa, "Can you explain what your toy looks like?" Lisa said, "It's a small, soft bear with a red bow." Tom and Lisa looked everywhere for the toy. They looked in the park, in the shops, and under the trees. At last, they found the toy bear near a big tree. Lisa was very grateful to Tom. She gave him a big hug and said, "Thank you for helping me find my bear!" Tom smiled and was happy to help. From that day on, Lisa and Tom were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful girl. Her name was Ella and she loved to use her imagination! One day, Ella was in the hall when she had an idea. She decided to use her gracefulness to dance in the hall. Slowly, she began to glide around the hall just like a ballerina. As she danced, she spun and twirled in graceful circles. Ella felt like a princess and was so happy to be dancing. Her graceful performance made everyone in the hall smile. Pretty soon, everyone was clapping and cheering for Ella! She felt so proud to be able to use her gracefulness in such a fun way.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was always hungry. One day, he saw a big cookie on the table. Tim wanted to eat the cookie, but it was too high for him to reach. He tried to jump, but he was too small. Tim felt sad. Later, Tim's big sister, Sue, came into the room. She saw Tim looking at the cookie. Sue had an idea. She told Tim to wait and went away. Tim did not know what Sue was doing, but he hoped she could help. Soon, Sue came back with a chair. She put the chair next to the table. Tim climbed onto the chair and now he could reach the cookie. With his hand, he took the cookie and ate it. Tim was not hungry anymore. He was so happy, and he thanked Sue for her help. They both knew that, together, they could make anything appear.

One day, Max saw that his teacher had an exam. He was excited to see what the exam was about, but the teacher looked serious. “What’s the exam about?” asked Max. His teacher shook her head. “I’m sorry, Max. This exam is only for big kids. You can’t do it,” she replied. Max started to feel sad. “Please,” he begged, “I want to do the exam.” His teacher shook her head again. “No, Max. You’re not old enough. Go and play,” she said. Max was so upset that he started to cry. But the teacher was firm. No matter how hard he begged, she wouldn’t let him do the exam. He went to play, feeling sad and disappointed.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Tom and Tim. They loved to play together. One day, they found a big prize in the park. The prize was a big, red ball. Tom and Tim both wanted the ball. They started to quarrel. Tom said, "I saw it first!" Tim said, "But I touched it first!" They were jealous of each other. A wise, old bird saw them quarrel. The bird said, "Stop fighting! You can both play with the ball." Tom and Tim listened to the bird. They stopped being jealous and played together. They had lots of fun with the big, red ball.
