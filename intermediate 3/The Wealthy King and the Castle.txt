Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wealthy King and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy king. He had a big, shiny sword. The king lived in a huge castle. He liked to visit his friends. One day, the king went to visit his friend, the queen. The queen lived in a pretty castle, too. They played with the king's sword. They had a lot of fun. "Look at my sword!" said the king. "It's big and shiny!" The queen smiled and said, "Yes, it is a nice sword!" And they played together all day long. The king and the queen were very happy. They became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Lily saw the sun shining brightly in the sky. She wanted to join her friends and play in the sun. Lily went outside and saw her friend Tom. Tom was eating a tasty apple. Lily asked Tom, "Can I join you and eat a tasty apple too?" Tom said, "Yes, come and sit with me. We can eat tasty apples together in the sun." So, Lily and Tom sat under a big tree, eating their tasty apples and enjoying the warm sun. They laughed and talked, and had a great time. They were happy they could join each other on this sunny day.

One day, a little bird named Timmy was feeling naughty. He wanted to play a trick on his friend, Tommy the cat. Timmy knew that Tommy loved to whistle. So, he thought of a fun plan. Timmy went to a big drain near Tommy's house. He hid behind the drain and started to whistle. Tommy heard the whistle and thought it was another cat. He wanted to find the cat and make a new friend. Tommy looked and looked, but he could not find the cat. Then, he saw Timmy behind the drain. Timmy laughed and said, "I tricked you!" Tommy was not mad. He laughed too. They both played together near the drain, whistling and having fun. And from that day, they became best friends forever.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Sally. Sally was very bossy and she loved to take baths. Every day before lunch she would go to the bathroom, take off her cap and hop in the tub for a nice long bath. One day Sally was getting ready for her bath when her younger brother came in and tried to jump in the tub. Sally yelled at him and said, "No way! You can't bathe with me!" Her brother was disappointed, but he knew Sally was bossy and he respected her wishes. He went to the kitchen to find something else to do, leaving Sally to have a nice, peaceful bath. When she was finished, she dried off, put on her cap and stepped out of the bathroom with a satisfied smile on her face.

One day, a little frog named Tim hopped to an empty pond. He felt sad because there was no water for him to swim in. While he sat by the pond, he saw a big fish named Sam. Sam said, "I'm sad too. I can't swim in this empty pond." Tim had an idea. He told Sam, "Let's find water to fill the pond. Then, we can swim together." They looked for water, but they could not find any. Tim and Sam felt very sad. Then, a kind ladybug named Lily flew by and saw them. Lily said, "I know where to find water. Follow me!" So, Tim and Sam followed Lily to a big lake. They all worked together to bring water back to the empty pond. When the pond was full, Tim was so happy that he gave Lily a big kiss. They all swam and played in the pond, and they were not sad anymore.

One day, Peter found a wish. It looked all shiny and pretty, so he wanted to take a closer look. He picked it up and started to chew on it, but it tasted very bitter and he didn't like it. "Yucky!" said Peter, and he threw it on the ground. But then a big voice said, "Wait. That's my wish!" Peter turned around and saw a big man. He was mad that Peter had chewed his wish. Peter was scared and cried, "Please don't be mad! I'm sorry!" The man thought for a moment, and then said, "It's okay, but don’t chew on wishes. It's bad luck! Next time, don't do it." Peter was relieved, and he said thank you. The man smiled and disappeared. From then on, Peter never chewed on wishes again. He knew it was wrong and that it brought bad luck. He was careful with wishes and always treated them with respect.
