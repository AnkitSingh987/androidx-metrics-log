Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cloud and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was dull and gray. It liked to make rain for the flowers and trees. One day, the cloud forgot how to make rain. The flowers and trees were sad. They needed water to grow big and strong. A little girl saw the sad cloud. She wanted to help. The girl found a watering can and watered the flowers and trees. The cloud saw this and remembered how to make rain. The cloud made rain, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl. She was just 3 years old and very adventurous. One day she saw a big mountain. She decided to go climbing up its big and empty sides. She headed up the mountain, nodding as she walked along. She explored the empty sides, looking around in amazement. After a while, she became very tired and decided to take a break. But as she tried to rest, the mountain started to shake! It was an earthquake! She was terrified and tried to climb down, but it was too late. The mountain started to crumble, and she was buried beneath tonnes of rocks. The bad ending was that the brave little girl was never heard from again. She was gone forever.

Tom and Lily like to play with their kayak in the lake. They take turns to paddle and steer. They have fun and see many fish and birds. One day, they see a big duck on the water. They want to get closer and say hello. But the duck does not like them. It quacks loudly and flaps its wings. It splashes water at them and tries to bite their kayak. Tom and Lily are scared and angry. They paddle away from the duck. They shout at it. "Go away, duck! You are mean and rude! You cannot have our kayak!" The duck chases them for a while, but then it gives up. It swims back to its nest. It has eggs there. It wants to protect them from strangers. Tom and Lily realize why the duck was angry. They feel sorry for it. They paddle back to the shore. They decide to leave the duck alone. They say to each other. "Maybe the duck was just scared, like us. It loves its eggs, like we love our kayak." They hug and smile. They are still friends. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a purple bug. This bug was a mosquito. The mosquito had a friend, a little boy. The boy liked to play outside. The mosquito would fly and play with the boy. One day, the boy saw a big tree. The boy wanted to climb the tree. The mosquito wanted to encourage the boy. So, the mosquito said, "You can do it! Climb the tree!" The boy started to climb. He went up and up. But then, the boy fell down. He cried and cried. The mosquito felt sad. The boy was hurt. The purple mosquito could not help the boy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl who lived in a small cottage with her parents. One day, when the girl was playing outside, a witch arrived. She was tall and was wearing a fine dress. When the Witch saw the girl, she smiled. 'Hello there, little girl,' said the Witch. 'Would you like to make a wish?' The girl was very excited. 'Yes, please!' she said. The Witch whispered to her and the girl closed her eyes. Suddenly, it started to snow. Fluffy, white snowflakes filled the air. When she opened her eyes, the girl saw that everything around her had changed - it was all so beautiful. The Witch smiled again. 'Make sure to keep your wish a secret!' she said, before she flew away. The girl was so happy. She knew all her dreams had come true - everything was going to be fine.

Mia and Nathan were playing in the park when it started to get dark. Mia was worried. "We should go home now," said Nathan. Mia shook her head. "No, I want to play here." Nathan could see trouble ahead. "We shouldn't stay here. It's too dark." Mia thought for a moment. "OK, maybe you're right," she said. So they decided to go home. The night was so dark they had to keep a close eye on each other. But they eventually made it home safe and sound.
