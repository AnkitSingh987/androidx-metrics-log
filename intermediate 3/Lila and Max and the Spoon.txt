Category: Intermediate
Title: Lila and Max and the Spoon

Lila and Max were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They found an avocado on the counter and decided to use it for their salad. "Look, Max, this is an avocado. It is green and soft and good for you," Lila said, holding the fruit in her hand. "I don't like avocado. It is yucky and mushy and bad for me," Max said, making a face. Lila frowned. She wanted Max to try the avocado and like it. She thought of a way to make it fun. "Let's play a game, Max. I will cut the avocado in half and you can kick the seed. The seed is the big brown ball inside the avocado. It is hard and round and fun to kick. Maybe you will like avocado after you kick the seed," Lila said, smiling. Max thought for a moment. He liked to kick things. He liked balls. Maybe he would like the seed. "OK, Lila, let's play. But only if I can kick the seed first," Max said, nodding. Lila agreed. She took a knife and cut the avocado in half. She gave one half to Max and kept the other half for herself. She put the seed on the floor and told Max to kick it. Max ran to the seed and kicked it with his foot. The seed flew across the room and hit the wall. Max laughed. He liked to kick the seed. It was fun. Lila clapped. She was happy that Max liked the game. She hoped he would like the avocado too. "Good job, Max. You kicked the seed very far. Now, do you want to try the avocado? It is very tasty. You can put some salt and lemon on it and eat it with a spoon. It is like a green pudding," Lila said, holding her half of the avocado. Max looked at the avocado. He was curious. He liked pudding. He liked salt and lemon. Maybe he would like the avocado. "OK, Lila, I will try the avocado. But only if you try the seed. Maybe you will like the seed too. It is like a brown ball," Max said, giving his half of the avocado to Lila. Lila laughed. She knew the seed was not good to eat. But she wanted to be nice to Max. She took the seed and pretended to bite it. "Yum, yum, yum. The seed is very crunchy. Thank you, Max. You are a good chef," Lila said, making a funny face. Max smiled. He was glad that Lila liked the seed. He took a spoon and scooped some avocado. He put some salt and lemon on it and tasted it. "Mmm, mmm, mmm. The avocado is very creamy. Thank you, Lila. You are a good chef too," Max said, making a happy face. Lila and Max hugged. They were proud of their salad. They ate the avocado and kicked the seed. They had fun. Mom came into the kitchen and saw the mess. She saw the avocado peels, the salt and lemon, the knife and the spoon, and the seed on the wall. She was grumpy. "Who did this? What are you doing? This is not how you play with food. This is not how you make a salad. This is a disaster," Mom said, shaking her head. Lila and Max looked at each other. They were scared. They did not want to make Mom grumpy. They wanted to make her happy. "Sorry, Mom. We were playing. We were chefs. We made a salad. We used an avocado. We kicked the seed. We liked the avocado. We liked the seed. We had fun," Lila and Max said, in a small voice. Mom looked at Lila and Max. She saw their innocent faces. She saw their bright eyes. She saw their sweet smiles. She was not grumpy anymore. She was happy. "It's OK, Lila and Max. I'm sorry I was grumpy. I'm glad you had fun. I'm glad you liked the avocado. I'm glad you kicked the seed. You are good chefs. You are good kids. I love you," Mom said, hugging them. Lila and Max hugged Mom back. They were happy. They loved Mom. They loved avocado. They loved the seed. They loved each other. They were a happy family.
