Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Sam and the Garden

There was a boy called Sam. He was 3 years old and very happy. One day, he was running around the garden when suddenly he felt dizzy. He stopped and said, "Uh oh!" Sam fell to the ground and started to cry. His mum heard him and came out of the house. She saw Sam lying on the ground and said, "What's wrong, Sam?" Sam said, "I feel dizzy and my head hurts." His mum picked him up and said, "Let's get you inside. I'll get you a nice cold drink from the bottle." Sam smiled as his mum gave him the bottle. He felt much better after drinking the cold drink and even laughed as he finished the last sip. Sam thanked his mum and said, "No more running around for me until I'm feeling better!"

One day, Mommy was in the kitchen and something smelt funny! She wondered what it was. She asked her three-year-old daughter, "What did you do?" The little girl smiled and said, "I was spoiling myself in the bathtub!" Mommy said, "Oh, that sounds fun!" She peeked into the bathtub and saw that the little girl was surrounded by lots of bubbles and floating toys. Mommy said, "Wow! That looks like so much fun! You must have really enjoyed that." The little girl laughed and said, "Yes, it was a great time!" Mommy said, "It's time to get out of the bathtub now so we can go play outside and get some fresh air." The little girl said, "Ok, can we go play with the bubbles too?" Mommy said, "Of course! That sounds like a lot of fun! Let's go!" And so, the mother and daughter went outside and played with the bubbles, laughing and squealing in delight. It was one of the most fun days they had ever had!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named John. He had a stick made of wood. One day, he wanted to pretend he was a knight and play with his stick. So, he struck it against a tree. Unfortunately, the wood broke in two! John felt embarrassed. He didn't know what he should do. John's mom heard him and came running. With a sad smile, she said it was okay and gave him a new stick. John was happy again. He thanked his mom and they both smiled. John started playing and, this time, he was extra careful. He didn't want to break his new stick so he was very gentle. He made sure he only struck it lightly against the tree. John was very proud and he didn't feel embarrassed anymore.

One day, a girl named Lisa went for a walk with her dog, Spot. They liked to be creative and make up games to play. Today, they decided to play a game called "Trust Walk". In this game, Lisa closed her eyes and let Spot guide her. As they played, Lisa bumped into a big tree. "Ouch!" she said. A bird in the tree spoke, "Why don't you open your eyes?" Lisa was surprised. She did not know birds could talk. The bird said, "I can help you play your game better. Trust me." Lisa trusted the bird and opened her eyes. The bird showed her a secret path. They all walked on the path and found a beautiful garden with lots of pretty flowers. Spot and Lisa were very happy. They thanked the bird and played in the garden all day.

Tommy was only three. He was very nervous and he did not know what to do. He wanted to relax but he didn't know how. One day, mommy said "Tommy, I want you to lie down and take a nap." Tommy took his nap. When he woke up, he felt much better. He felt calmer than before, like he had been relaxing. The next day, mommy said "Tommy, why don't you have a nap again?". Tommy was nervous again. But this time, he felt like he knew what was coming. He lay down and continued to relax until he fell asleep. When Tommy woke up, he was surprised. He realised he had been feeling much more relaxed then before. He had learned how to relax! He was so glad he had taken his naps - it had helped him a lot.
