Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his big net. One day, he went to the park with his net. He wanted to catch something unknown. He did not know what that would be, but he wanted to try. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He threw his net up in the tree. But the tree did not yield. Tim tried again and again. Then, something unexpected happened. The tree shook and a big ball fell down. Tim was so happy! He thought he caught something unknown. But when he looked at the ball, he saw it was just a ball. He was a little sad. But then, the ball started to bounce by itself! Tim laughed and played with the magic ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty boy named Tim. He loved to play with his white toy car. One day, Tim thought it would be fun to polish his toy car to make it shine. As Tim was polishing his car, he saw a tiny white bug on the floor. The bug was very small, but it looked like it wanted to play too. Tim thought it would be fun to race the bug with his toy car. They started the race, but something unexpected happened. The tiny white bug began to grow bigger and bigger! Soon, it was as big as Tim's toy car. Tim was very surprised, but he kept racing. The white bug was very fast, and it won the race. Tim was not sad that he lost the race. Instead, he was happy to have a new friend. From that day on, Tim and the white bug played together every day. And they both learned that being naughty can sometimes lead to fun surprises.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim had a lot of pasta. Tim loved pasta. It was his favorite food. One day, Tim saw a flexible worm on the ground. The worm was wiggling and dancing. Tim said, "Hi, worm! Do you like pasta?" The worm said, "Yes, I love pasta too! Can I have some?" Tim gave the worm some pasta. The worm ate the pasta and started to wiggle and dance even more. They both laughed and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The worm turned into a magic fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you, Tim, for sharing your pasta with me. As a thank you, I will give you one wish." Tim thought about it and said, "I wish for more pasta!" The fairy waved her wand and suddenly, there was a big mountain of pasta! Tim and the fairy remained friends and shared the pasta together.

One day, a sweet dog named Lily went for a walk. She loved to lead her friends in the park. Today, she met a new friend named Tim. Tim was a small boy who liked to play. "Hi, I'm Tim. Do you want to play?" asked Tim. Lily wagged her tail and barked. They played fetch with a ball. They had so much fun. After playing, Tim was hungry. He went home and made a pizza. He shared it with Lily. They both liked the pizza. It was a good day for Lily and Tim.

Once upon a time there was a blue boot that loved to bounce. Every day the boot would bounce high in the sky and then back down to the ground again. One sunny day the boot came across a friendly rabbit. "Hi boot! Where are you going today?" asked the rabbit. The boot smiled and said, "I love to bounce so I'm just bouncing around!" The rabbit laughed and decided to join in the fun. Together they bounced all around the field, higher and higher. The boot felt so happy and free! But then suddenly, the ground started shaking and the boot and rabbit both got scared. An evil dragon had landed! With one swipe of its tail, the boot and rabbit were sent flying. The boot hit the ground hard and got a big bump. The blue boot was no longer able to bounce. The poor boot was now broken and the rabbit was never seen again.
