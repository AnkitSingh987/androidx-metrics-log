Category: Intermediate
Title: The Garden and the Plants

Once upon a time, there was a big garden. In the garden, many plants were troubled. They were not happy. The little boy, Tom, saw the plants and was sad too. One day, Tom saw a little bug. The bug said, "I can help your plants. But, you must find me good food." Tom thought hard. He knew the plants could rot if they did not get help. Tom found good food for the bug. The bug ate the food and then helped the plants. The plants grew big and strong. No more rot! Tom and the plants were happy. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to drink cocoa. One day, she found a rich cocoa in her kitchen. It was very yummy. Lily was very happy. Lily wanted to share her rich cocoa with her friend Tim. So, she put the cocoa in a cup and went to Tim's house. On the way, she tripped and the cocoa spilled. Lily was sad. But then, Lily had an idea. She would reverse back to her house and make more cocoa. She made more rich cocoa and went back to Tim's house. This time, she did not trip. Tim loved the cocoa too. They both were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, yellow sun in the sky. The sun was always happy and shining. One day, the sun was upset because it was raining, and it couldn't shine as bright. A little girl named Lucy saw the sun was upset. She wanted to make the sun happy again. Lucy said to the sun, "Don't be upset, sun. I will dance for you to make you smile." Lucy danced and danced under the rain. The sun saw Lucy's dance and started to feel happy again. The sun began to shine, and the rain stopped. The sun said, "Thank you, Lucy, for making me happy again." All the animals and people saw the sun shining and Lucy dancing. They were all happy too. They clapped their hands and said, "Yay, Lucy! You made the sun happy!" Everyone was so happy that they all started to dance and applaud with Lucy under the bright, happy sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was eating his lunch with his mom. He had a napkin in his hand to clean his face. Tim saw a big bug on the table. The bug had many legs and it made Tim feel scared. The bug blinked its eyes at Tim. Tim thought the bug was rude for blinking at him. He wanted the bug to go away. Tim waved his napkin at the bug, but the bug did not move. Tim's mom saw the bug too, and she told Tim not to be scared. Tim's mom used the napkin to pick up the bug. She took it outside and let it go. The bug was happy to be outside and Tim was happy the bug was gone. Tim learned that sometimes bugs can be rude, but it's best to let them go and not be scared.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly cat named Tom. He liked to play with his friend, a little girl named Sue. They loved to have fun in the kitchen. Sue liked to make sauce, and Tom liked to watch her. One day, Sue and Tom were in the kitchen making sauce. Sue said, "Tom, can you help me lock the door?" Tom nodded and went to lock the door. Now they could have fun without anyone coming in. While they made the sauce, they heard a noise. Sue asked, "Tom, did you lock the door?" Tom said, "Yes, I did." They looked around and saw a smelly sock on the floor. They laughed and knew it was just the sock that made the noise. They went back to making their sauce, and they had a great day together.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Spot. Spot loved to play at the park with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They would run and play all day, and they were very happy. One day, Lily's mom told her that they were moving to a new house far away. Lily was sad and asked her mom if Spot could come with them. Her mom said no, but she promised that they would visit Spot at the park sometimes. Lily and her mom moved away, and Spot waited at the park every day for them to come back. He was very sad and missed his friend. Days turned into weeks, and weeks turned into months, but Lily and her mom never came back to visit Spot. Spot was alone and sad at the park, and he never saw his best friend Lily again. The promise was broken, and the clever dog's heart was too.
