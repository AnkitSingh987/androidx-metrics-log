Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a happy dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play in the park. One day, Spot saw a big billboard with a picture of a bone on it. Spot wanted that bone very much. Spot went to his friend, a cat named Whiskers, and said, "Whiskers, I saw a big bone on a billboard. How can I get it?" Whiskers thought for a moment and said, "Spot, you must be nice and yield to others. Maybe someone will give you a bone." Spot listened to Whiskers and went to the park. He played with the other dogs and always yielded when they wanted a turn with the ball. He was very nice to everyone. One day, a little girl saw how happy and nice Spot was. She said, "Spot, you are such a good dog. I want to give you a gift." The little girl gave Spot a big, tasty bone just like the one on the billboard. Spot was so happy! He thanked the little girl and shared his bone with Whiskers. Spot learned that being nice and yielding to others can bring happiness and good things.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a fly. The fly was not happy. The fly said, "I want to chew on some food, but I can't find any good food!" Tim wanted to help the fly. He looked around the park. He saw a big apple tree. Tim picked a small piece of apple from the tree. He gave it to the fly. The fly was very happy. "Thank you, Tim!" the fly said. But then, a big bird came. The bird wanted to eat the fly. Tim was scared, but he wanted to help his new friend. Tim waved his arms and shouted, "Go away, big bird!" The bird was scared of Tim and flew away. The fly was safe. Tim and the fly were very happy. They had a good day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She wanted to marry a boy named Tom. Tom was fast, he had a lot of speed. But there was a problem. A big, mean dog did not want them to be together. One day, Lily said to Tom, "I want to marry you. But the big, mean dog is always in our way." Tom said, "I know. We must be persistent. We will find a way to be together." They tried to be together, but the big, mean dog was always there. They ran fast, but the dog was faster. They hid, but the dog found them. They were very sad. In the end, Lily and Tom could not be together. The big, mean dog was too strong and fast. They were very sad, but they knew they tried their best.

Once upon a time, in a small port, there was a playful cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his friends, the birds, and the fish. One day, Tom was playing with his bird friend, Tim. They were having so much fun. "Tom, I feel a sneeze coming!" said Tim. Tom did not know what a sneeze was. Tim told him, "When I sneeze, my feathers fly everywhere!" Tom thought it was funny and wanted to see it happen. The next day, Tom and Tim played again. Tim sneezed, just like he said he would. Feathers flew everywhere! Tom laughed and laughed. He was so happy to have a playful friend like Tim. And from that day on, they played together every day, sneezes and all.

Jimmy was always fascinated by his mom's incredible baking. Every night, he watched eagerly as she measured the right amount of ingredients, stirred and finally planted the cake in the oven. One night, Jimmy asked: "Mommy, can I help lift the cake out of the oven?" Mommy smiled and nodded. With trembling hands, Jimmy reached out to the oven. He grasped the tray and lifted the steaming cake. But, with the sudden movement, Jimmy lost his balance and the cake crashed on the floor! Jimmy's mom looked disappointed. "Oh no," she said. "We'll have to start over again now." Jimmy's heart sank. He felt so bad that he had ruined the beautiful cake. He looked at his mom and said, "I'm sorry, mommy." Mommy hugged him and said, "It's okay, Jimmy. Things like this happen." Jimmy knew he had made a mistake. He had ruined the incredible cake and his mom was disappointed.
