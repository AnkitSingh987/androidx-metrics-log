Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Rock

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big rock and Tim wanted to climb it. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim!" Tim started to climb the rock slowly. He was not scared, but he was not fast either. His mom watched him and clapped her hands when he reached the top. Then, Tim saw a big red car on the road near the park. The car went by with lots of speed. It was loud too! Tim's eyes got big as he watched the car zoom by. He wanted to go fast like the car, so he got an idea. Tim started to rock back and forth on the big rock. He rocked faster and faster, pretending he was the big red car. Suddenly, the rock started to move! It was not a rock at all, but a giant turtle! The turtle was happy to be free and took Tim on a ride around the park. Tim laughed as they went fast like the car, and his mom waved and cheered.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. On his walk, he saw a big house. It was a rich house with a big yard. Tim walked up to the house and saw a girl named Sue. "Hi," said Tim. "I see your rich house. Can I come in?" Sue said, "Yes, you can come in. But first, let's play outside!" They played and had fun. Then, they saw a big red button on the ground. It had a sign that said "start." Tim and Sue looked at each other and smiled. "Let's push the button!" said Sue. Tim pushed the button, and something unexpected happened. The rich house started to fly! Tim and Sue laughed as they flew around in the sky. They had a great day and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little girl named Lily. She loved to dream about faraway lands and the different languages people spoke there. One day, she told her mom about her dreams. "Mommy, I dream about people talking in a new language. I want to learn it too," said Lily. Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Lily! We can learn together." The next day, Lily and her mom started to learn the new language. They practiced every day and had lots of fun. They talked to each other in the new language, and Lily was very happy. She loved sharing her dreams with her mom and learning new things together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lucky red jug. He loved his jug very much. Every day, he would fill it with water and take it with him to play outside. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his jug. He saw his friends playing catch. Tim wanted to play too. He put his lucky jug down under a big tree and went to join his friends. They had lots of fun running and laughing. After a while, Tim felt thirsty. He ran back to the tree to get his lucky jug. But it was gone! Tim looked around and saw a squirrel holding his jug. The squirrel saw Tim and began to run. Tim ran after the squirrel, trying to get his jug back. The squirrel was fast, but Tim was faster. He caught the squirrel and took his lucky jug back. Tim was very happy to have his lucky red jug again.

One day, there was a man walking in the jungle. He was very brave, but he didn't know what was ahead. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise. He stopped and looked around. He could see a tiger in the distance. He was scared but knew he had to continue. The man warned the tiger not to come any closer. But the tiger didn't listen. As he got closer, the man saw the tiger's yellow eyes and sharp teeth. He was terrified. The man tried to run, but the tiger was too fast. The man was sure the tiger would eat him. He quickly looked around and noticed a dead tree branch nearby. He picked it up and started swinging it, trying to scare off the tiger. To the man's surprise, the tiger backed away. The man was relieved and kept swinging the branch, so the tiger wouldn't come back. The man was very lucky and made it safely out of the jungle. He learned an important lesson that day: always be prepared and stay on guard when you're in the jungle.
