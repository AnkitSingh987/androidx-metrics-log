Category: Intermediate
Title: The Woman and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a woman who lived in a small house. She was very fragile and needed help to do things. One day, she wanted to prepare a big meal for her friends. The woman called her friend, the dog, and said, "Dog, can you help me prepare a big meal for our friends?" The dog said, "Yes, I can help you." They started to prepare the meal together. As they were preparing the meal, the dog saw a cat outside. The dog ran out to chase the cat, leaving the fragile woman alone. The woman tried to do everything by herself, but she was too fragile. She fell and broke a plate. The meal was ruined, and her friends were sad. The moral of the story is: don't leave your friends when they need your help.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Daisy and Bob. Daisy was very selfish and always wanted things her way. Bob didn't like when Daisy was so selfish, but he was always kind to her. One day they decided to go to the park together. At the park they could see a dark cloud settling in the sky. Daisy said, "That dark cloud is scaring me. I want to go home now." But Bob said, "Let's not be so selfish. Let's stay and play for a little while. I'm sure the dark cloud won't stay for long." So Daisy and Bob stayed at the park and played together. Eventually the dark cloud disappeared, and the sun came out. Daisy and Bob had a lot of fun playing and laughing together. The next day the park was all sunny. Daisy and Bob went back to play again. But this time, a huge dark cloud was settling in the sky again. Daisy screamed and wanted to go home, but Bob said, "Let's just be brave and wait until it passes. I promise it won't stay long." Once again Daisy and Bob waited together until the dark cloud disappeared. Daisy was so glad they didn't give in to her selfishness the day before, or they would have missed out. From then on they both agreed not to be so selfish and to always be brave.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet town. In the town, there was a small house. In the house, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to rise early and play with his toys. One day, Tom found a nail on the floor. He didn't know what it was. He showed it to his friend, Sam. "What is this?" Tom asked. Sam didn't know either. They decided to play with the nail. Tom and Sam played with the nail all day. They didn't know it was sharp. When it was time to go home, Tom put the nail in his pocket. He forgot it was there. At home, Tom sat on his bed. He felt a sharp pain. The nail had poked him. Tom cried and cried. His mom came and saw the nail. She was sad too. The day had a bad ending.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to play with her toys. She asked her daddy, "Daddy, can we play with my toys?" Her daddy smiled and said, "Yes, Lucy, let's play with your toys." As they played, Lucy's toy said something rude. Lucy looked at her daddy and asked, "Daddy, why did my toy say something rude?" Her daddy thought for a moment and then he suggested, "Maybe the toy is just having a bad day, Lucy. Let's try to be kind to it." So, Lucy and her daddy kept playing with the toys. They were kind to the rude toy, and soon it started being nice too. They all had a fun day playing together, and Lucy learned that being kind can make others happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little witch. She lived in a small house in the woods. She had a big garden where she grew many things. The witch liked to grow flowers, fruits, and vegetables. She took care of her garden every day. One day, the witch found a seed. She thought, "I will plant this seed in my garden and see what grows." She dug a hole and put the seed in the ground. She gave it water and waited. The seed started to grow, but it grew very slow. The witch waited and waited for it to grow big. Finally, the plant was big enough to see what it was. It was a beautiful flower! The witch was so happy. She took care of the flower every day. The flower grew and grew, and it made the witch's garden even more pretty. The little witch and her slow-growing flower lived happily in their small house in the woods.
