Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Hen and the Pig

Once upon a time, in a small farm, there was a big red hen. The red hen laid an egg every day. She was very proud of her egg. One day, a rude pig came to the farm. The pig saw the egg and wanted to sit on it. The red hen said, "No, you cannot sit on my egg. You are too big and heavy." But the rude pig did not listen. He sat on the egg anyway. The red hen was very sad and worried. Then, something unexpected happened! The egg did not break. Instead, it became a big, strong egg. The rude pig was surprised and left the farm. The red hen was happy, and she learned that even when others are rude, she must stay strong and protect what is important to her.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They brought a cooler with yummy food inside. Lily was very happy to eat the food. The cooler was open, so Lily could see all the yummy things inside. Lily saw a big tree and wanted to play near it. While she played, a small bird flew down and landed on the cooler. The bird saw the open cooler and started to eat the food. Lily saw the bird and ran to her mom. Lily's mom got a cloth to wipe the cooler clean. She was not mad at the bird. She told Lily that the bird was hungry too. Lily and her mom shared their food with the bird. They all ate together and had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a nice little town, there was a mist that covered the sky. The mist was so thick that it made it hard for the people to see. Everyone was sad because they could not play outside. One day, a boy named Tim wanted to find out why the mist was there. He asked his mom, but she did not know. Tim went outside to look for the reason. He saw a big, mean dragon. The dragon was making the mist with its breath. The dragon did not want the people to be happy. Tim was brave and went up to the dragon. He told the dragon to stop making the mist. The dragon was surprised that Tim was not scared. The dragon said it would stop if Tim could make it laugh. Tim told the dragon a funny joke, and the dragon laughed. The dragon promised not to make the mist anymore. The people were happy, and they did not punish Tim for talking to the dragon. They all played outside and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in her original red dress. One day, she found a big hole in her backyard. Lily was very curious about the hole. Lily called her friend Tim and said, "Tim, come see the big hole!" Tim came running and looked at the hole. They both wondered what was inside. Tim had an idea. "Let's reverse a toy car into the hole!" he said. So Lily and Tim found a toy car and pushed it into the hole. The car went in and came back out with a note. The note said, "Thank you for your toy car! Love, the Hole." Lily and Tim laughed and played with their toy car for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very creative. She loved to draw and paint. One day, Mia found a big hook in her yard. She thought, "What can I do with this hook?" Mia had an idea. She took the hook and some string. She tied the string to the hook. Then, she hung the hook from a tree. She said, "Now I can swing on it!" Mia's friend, Tim, came over to play. He saw the hook and said, "Wow! What a fun swing!" They both swung on the hook and had a great time. After a while, they were tired. Mia said, "Let's rest under the tree." They sat down and rested, happy with their creative play.
