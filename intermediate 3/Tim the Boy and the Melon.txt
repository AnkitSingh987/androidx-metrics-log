Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Melon

One day, a little boy named Tim went with his mom to the store. They wanted to buy a great melon for a snack. Tim was very excited because he loved melons. They were big, round, and very tasty. Tim's mom said, "Let's go find the best melon!" When they got to the store, they saw many melons. Tim saw a big green melon and said, "This one looks great!" His mom agreed and they picked up the melon. Suddenly, Tim saw a little bug on the melon. He was scared and let out a scream. His mom said, "Don't worry, it's just a little bug. It won't hurt you." Tim felt better and they took the great melon home. They cut the melon into small pieces and put them in a big bowl. Tim and his mom sat down and ate the sweet melon together. They both loved it very much. The little bug from the store found a new home outside, and Tim was happy that he had a great melon to share with his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate little bird named Blue. Blue loved to help his friends solve problems. One day, Blue's friend, Cat, was sad. "Why are you sad, Cat?" Blue asked. "I want to find my favorite toy, but I can't," Cat said. Blue wanted to help, so he flew around to look for the toy. He saw a big cup of coffee on the table. Next to the coffee, he found Cat's toy. "Look, Cat! I found your toy!" Blue said. Cat jumped with joy and said, "Thank you, Blue! You solved my problem!" From that day on, Blue and Cat were the best of friends. They loved to play together and help each other. And they always stayed away from the coffee.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named John. John was very eager to learn new things. One day at his school, the children got to act out a famous story. Everyone was excited, especially John. John was given the role of a farmer. The teacher told him that he had to pick a vegetable from the garden. John was very eager to pick a vegetable. He wandered over to the garden and saw a beautiful carrot. John bent down and carefully grabbed the carrot. Suddenly, he heard his teacher shouting from behind. "John! Act like you're picking the carrot. Show us what you're doing!" John nodded and pretended to pick the carrot. He lifted it up and was so proud of himself. Everyone at school clapped and cheered for him. John had learned a new thing and was very eager to do it again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very impatient. He always wanted things right away. One day, Tim saw a big toy car in a store. He wanted it so much. But, the toy car had a high price. Tim asked his mom if he could have it, but she said no. Tim was sad, but he had an idea. He would stand by the store every day until he could buy the toy car. Every day, Tim stood there, waiting and waiting. He was so impatient that he did not want to wait for his mom to save money for the car. One day, while Tim was standing by the store, a big wind came. The wind blew Tim away! Tim was scared and cried for his mom. But she could not hear him. Tim was lost and could not find his way back home. Because he was too impatient to wait, Tim never got to play with the toy car or see his mom again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to see the sunrise every morning. But one day, she woke up late and missed it. Lily was sad. She went to her friend, a wise old owl. "Mr. Owl, I miss the sunrise. Can you help me see it again?" Lily asked. Mr. Owl thought for a moment and then had an idea. "I have a fake sunrise for you, Lily. It's not the same, but it's still pretty," Mr. Owl said. He showed her a big, round, yellow ball. Lily smiled and played with the fake sunrise. She knew it wasn't the real sunrise, but it made her happy until the next morning when she could see the real sunrise again.
