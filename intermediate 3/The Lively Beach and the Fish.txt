Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lively Beach and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a lively beach. On this beach, there were two friends, a crab and a fish. They liked to play and have fun in the sun. But one day, they had a quarrel. The crab wanted to build a sand castle, but the fish wanted to swim in the water. The crab and the fish were very sad. They did not want to play together anymore. So, the crab went to build his sand castle, and the fish went to swim in the water. They both felt lonely without their friend. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wave came and washed away the crab's sand castle. The fish saw this and felt bad for the crab. The fish swam over to the crab and said, "I am sorry, let's be friends again." The crab was happy and said, "Yes, let's play together." So, the crab and the fish became friends again and had lots of fun on the lively beach.

Once upon a time, there was a happy boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, Tim's mom gave him a big box. She said, "Tim, unpack this box and you will find something fun inside." Tim was very excited. He opened the box and found a long rope. He thought it was the best toy ever! Tim played with the rope all day. He jumped over it and tied it around things. But then, Tim had a bad idea. He tied the rope to a tree and tried to swing on it. The rope broke and Tim fell down. He was hurt and sad. Tim learned that he should be careful when playing with new toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane lived on a farm with her mom, dad, and dog, Spot. They grew wheat on their farm. Wheat is a plant that people use to make bread. Jane liked to help her family on the farm. One day, Jane and Spot found a rare, pretty stone while playing near the wheat. Jane was so happy. She showed the stone to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! Spot and I found a rare stone near the wheat. It's so pretty!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Jane! Let's perform a show with the stone!" Jane and her mom made a stage in the yard, and Jane and Spot performed a fun show for their family. They danced and played with the rare stone. Everyone had a good time, and they clapped for Jane and Spot's performance. From that day on, Jane and Spot always played with the rare stone near the wheat. They loved to perform shows for their family and friends. They were very happy, and everyone enjoyed their fun shows.

Once upon a time there was a bee called Buzz. He was very busy buzzing from flower to flower, looking for nectar and making honey. Every day Buzz was busy making more and more honey with his buzzy wings. One day, Buzz got a bright idea: he would sell his honey so he could buy something cool! After a day of buzzing back and forth, he had collected a jar full of golden syrup. He went to the market to find a buyer. On the way, he met a bear called Barry. Barry asked what was inside the jar and Buzz replied: "Honey, I'm going to sell it to get something cool!". Barry smiled and told him: "That's a great idea, but you won't be able to sell it unless you have a sign!" And with a wave of Barry's paw, he gave Buzz a little placard with "Honey for Sale!" written on it. So Buzz set off holding the placard above his head. He soon found a happy customer and sold the jar of honey. With the money, he bought a cool toy for himself, and with the rest he bought a special treat for Barry. From that day, Buzz and Barry were the best of friends, buzzing and playing together. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw a big, ugly bug. Tim said, "Ew! That bug is ugly!" Spot barked at the bug. They walked away from the bug, but then Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue said, "Hi Tim! I am going to the hospital to see my mom. She is a nurse there." Tim and Spot paused for a moment. They thought about going with Sue to the hospital. Tim said, "Okay, we will go with you!" They walked to the hospital together. At the hospital, they saw Sue's mom. She was helping people who were not feeling well. Tim, Sue, and Spot were happy to see her. They said goodbye to Sue's mom and went back home. They had a fun day together.
