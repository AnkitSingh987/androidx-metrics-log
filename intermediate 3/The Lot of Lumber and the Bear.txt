Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lot of Lumber and the Bear

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a lot of lumber. This lumber was from dead trees. Many animals lived near the lumber. One day, a little squirrel wanted to count the lumber. She started counting, "One, two, three..." Suddenly, she heard a loud sound. It was a big bear! The bear was looking for food. The squirrel was scared, but then something strange happened. The bear saw the lumber and started to count too! The squirrel and the bear became friends. They counted the lumber together and had fun. The forest was a happy place for all.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He was walking in the park with his parents. He saw something very loud in the sky. It was a big, colorful kite. The boy wanted to grab the kite so he ran towards it. His parents shouted for him to stop. But the boy was too excited. He kept running until he was right under the kite. He jumped up and tried to grab it. But it was too high up. He fell down and hurt himself. The little boy was sad. He had hurt his arm and the kite was too far away. His parents hugged him and took him home. The little boy never went to the park again because he was scared to try and grab the kite again.

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She lived in a big castle. She did not know many things. She was ignorant. One day, she went for a walk in the woods. She was very happy. In the woods, she saw a big box. The princess was excited. She opened the box. She thought it was a gift. Inside the box, there was a funny hat. She put it on her head. But the hat was magic! When she put it on, she became a frog. The princess was surprised. She did not know what to do. Then, a kind frog friend came and helped her. The frog friend showed her how to be a frog. The princess learned many new things. She was not ignorant anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom had a big hat that he loved to wear. One day, Tom lost his hat. He looked everywhere for it, but he could not find it. Tom was very sad. Tom's friend, a little bird named Sam, saw Tom looking sad. Sam asked, "Tom, why are you sad?" Tom said, "I lost my hat. I looked everywhere, but I can't find it." Sam wanted to help Tom find his hat. Sam told Tom, "Let's pray for help to find your hat." Tom and Sam prayed together. Suddenly, a strong wind blew, and Tom's hat flew right to him! Tom was so happy to have his hat back. He thanked Sam and they played together all day.

One day, a little bug went for a crawl. The bug was very small and slow. It crawled on the grass and saw many things. The bug saw a big tree, a red flower, and a small rock. The bug liked to crawl and explore. The bug met a daddy bug who was frustrated. The daddy bug said, "I can't find my baby bug. Can you help me?" The little bug wanted to help, so they crawled together to look for the baby bug. They crawled under the tree and behind the rock, but they couldn't find the baby bug. Then, they crawled near the red flower and saw the baby bug playing with a butterfly. The daddy bug was so happy and said, "Thank you for helping me!" The little bug was glad to help and they all crawled back home together.

Once upon a time, there was a cold day outside. A little girl named Lily went for a walk. She put on her warm coat and hat. Lily loved to observe things in the park. As she walked, Lily saw a flower. It was all alone in the cold. She said to the flower, "Hi, flower! Are you cold too?" The flower did not talk back, but Lily thought it looked sad. Lily decided to help the flower. She went home and got a warm blanket. She put the blanket around the flower. Now the flower was not cold anymore. Lily was happy she could help her new friend.
