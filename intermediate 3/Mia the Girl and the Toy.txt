Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Toy

One day, a girl named Mia went to the store with her mom to buy a toy. Mia saw a toy she liked and asked her mom, "Can I buy this toy, please?" Her mom looked at the toy and said, "Yes, you can buy it." When they got home, Mia's brother, Tim, saw the toy and wanted it too. He tried to take it from Mia, but she said, "No, this is my toy. I bought it with mom." Tim felt bitter because he didn't get a toy. At dinner, Mia saw Tim was sad. She gave him a wink and said, "You can play with my toy after dinner." Tim smiled and they both played with the toy together. The conflict was over, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a toy car. The toy car was very fast. It loved to race with other cars. But one day, it started to feel anxious. It was scared to become junk if it lost a race. One day, the toy car met a big truck. The big truck was slow but strong. The toy car asked the big truck, "Can you help me not become junk?" The big truck said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's be friends." The toy car and the big truck raced together. They were a great team. The toy car was not anxious anymore. It knew it would never become junk with the big truck by its side. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a generous cat named Silver. Silver loved to share her toys with her friends. She lived in a small house with her best friend, a dog named Spot. One day, Silver and Spot went to play outside. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. As they played, they saw something shiny on the ground. They went to look at it and saw it was a silver key. They did not know what it was for, but they took it with them. As they played, they found a small door in the ground. Silver and Spot paused and looked at the door. They tried the silver key, and it opened the door! Inside, they found a big room full of toys and treats. The room belonged to a kind squirrel who loved to share. The squirrel invited Silver and Spot to play with the toys and eat the treats. Silver, Spot, and the squirrel became good friends and shared everything together.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball and a small blue ball. They lived in a cold park. The big red ball liked to push the small blue ball all the time. The small blue ball did not like it. One day, the small blue ball said, "Stop! I do not want to push anymore!" The big red ball did not listen. They started to have a fight. The small blue ball tried to push back, but it was hard. A kind yellow ball saw the fight. The yellow ball came over and said, "Stop the fight! Let's be friends and play together!" The big red ball and the small blue ball stopped pushing. They all played together and had fun in the cold park.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to her grandma's house. Grandma was in the kitchen. She was getting ready to make a yummy cake. Sally saw her grandma's hands move in a mysterious way. She asked, "What are you doing, Grandma?" Grandma said, "I am preparing the cake, Sally. I am doing a special motion with my hands to make the cake taste good." Sally wanted to learn the motion too. So, she watched her grandma closely. Then, she tried to do the motion herself. It was not easy, but she kept trying. Finally, she did the motion just like her grandma. Together, they made the cake. It was very yummy. Sally was happy that she learned the special motion. She knew that she could do anything if she tried hard and never gave up. The moral of the story is to always try your best and never give up, even if something seems hard at first.
