Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Curtain

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a weird shadow behind the curtain. He was scared. He called his mom and said, "Mom, there is something behind the curtain. I am scared." Mom came to the room and looked behind the curtain. She saw that it was just a toy. She showed Tim the toy and said, "See, it is just your toy. Do not be scared." Tim felt better. He learned that sometimes things can seem scary, but they are not. He knew that he should not be scared of the weird shadow anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Amy. Amy was only three years old, so she loved playing hide and seek. One day, she saw an interesting bottle at home. She wanted to find out what was inside, so she grabbed it and opened it. Amy was very curious, so she took a sip from the bottle. Suddenly she started feeling really bad. Her mum quickly came and asked, "What did you drink?" Amy revealed it was a bottle of poison. Amy's mum said, "Amy, you should never drink anything unless you ask an adult first". Then she gave Amy something delicious to help her feel better. Amy understood her mum's advice and promised she would never do that again. The moral of the story is: Be careful, and always ask an adult before trying something new.

One day, a smart cat named Tom went out to play in the yard. He saw many toys and things to sort. He was very happy to play and sort all day. Tom found big toys and small toys. He put the big toys in one pile and the small toys in another pile. He sorted all the toys and felt proud of his work. But then, a big dog came into the yard. The big dog saw Tom and the toys. The dog took all the toys and ran away. Tom was sad and went back home. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a hot day. The sun was very bright, and everyone was sweaty. A little girl named Lily wanted to play outside, but she needed to find a way to stay cool. Lily had an idea! She would make a big fan to help her stay cool. She found some paper and a stick. She put the paper on the stick and managed to make a fan. Lily was very proud of her fan. She waved it to make a nice breeze. As Lily played outside, she saw her friend Tim. Tim was hot too. Lily had a kind heart, so she wrote a note for Tim. The note said, "I made a fan to stay cool. You can make one too!" Tim read the note and smiled. He made a fan just like Lily's. Now, they both could play outside and stay cool on the hot day. They were very happy.

Anna liked to paint with her spoon. She dipped it in the red paint and made lines on the paper. She dipped it in the blue paint and made dots. She dipped it in the yellow paint and made swirls. She was happy with her painting. She wanted to show it to her mom. She picked up the paper and ran to the kitchen. But she did not see the big dog that was sleeping on the floor. She tripped over the dog and fell down. The paper flew out of her hand and landed on the dog's face. The dog woke up and barked loudly. Anna was frightened. She screamed and cried. The dog was frightened too. He shook his head and tried to get the paper off. The paint smeared all over his fur. He looked funny. Mom heard the noise and came to the kitchen. She saw Anna and the dog and the paint. She was surprised. She hugged Anna and calmed her down. She petted the dog and cleaned him up. She looked at the paper and smiled. "Anna, you made a beautiful painting," she said. "But next time, please use a brush, not a spoon. And watch out for the dog, okay?" Anna nodded. She was sorry for the mess. She was glad that mom and the dog were not angry. She gave them both a kiss. She still liked to paint with her spoon, but she did it more carefully.
