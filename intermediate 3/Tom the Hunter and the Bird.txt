Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Hunter and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a hunter named Tom. He liked to bring food to his friends. One day, he went to the woods to find some food. He saw a big, red apple on a tree. Tom wanted to bring the apple to his friends. When Tom tried to get the apple, a bird came and took it away. This made Tom upset. He wanted to find the bird and get the apple back. So, he looked around the woods until he found the bird's nest. Tom saw the bird with the apple in its nest. He asked the bird to give the apple back. The bird was upset too because it needed the apple for its babies. Tom felt bad and let the bird keep the apple. He decided to find more apples to bring to his friends and the bird's family. In the end, Tom and the bird became friends, and they all shared the apples together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to collect things. She collected rocks, leaves, and even sticks. One day, Mia went to the park with her mom and dad. She wanted to find something new to collect. As Mia walked through the park, she saw a shiny rock on the ground. "Wow, what's this?" she asked her dad. Her dad told her, "That's a mineral, Mia. It's a special kind of rock." Mia thought the mineral was very fancy. She picked it up and put it in her pocket. Mia was so happy to have a fancy mineral in her collection. She showed it to her friends at school the next day. They all thought it was very pretty. From that day on, Mia loved to collect minerals and share them with her friends.

Once there was a little boy called Bob. Bob loved to explore and play, and one day he decided to go on an adventure. He packed his bag and ran out the door. Bob travelled through the forest and across the meadows until he came to a space. He peeked inside and saw a big machine. Bob was brave, so he went right inside and started to play with it. But it was a foolish thing to do! As Bob touched the machine, he accidentally hit a button. The machine began to whirr and rumble and then it exploded! It destroyed everything around it. Bob was very scared, but thankfully he was unharmed. He quickly ran away and vowed never to be so foolish again!

Once upon a time, there was a restless boy named Tim. He loved to run and play all day. Tim had a big toy skull that he liked a lot. It was his favorite toy. One day, Tim took his skull to the park. He put it on the ground and said, "Skull, let's start a race!" The skull didn't talk, but Tim pretended it said, "Okay, let's go!" Tim began to run as fast as he could. He looked back to see if the skull was running too, but it was still on the ground. Tim felt sad that his skull couldn't run with him. He picked it up and said, "Don't worry, skull. We can still have fun together!" Tim carried the skull and ran around the park, laughing and playing. They had a great day, and Tim's restless legs got tired. He went home with his skull and they both took a long nap.

Stanley was three years old and very independent. Every day he liked to go on a special track and explore. Stanley's aunt came to visit one day and asked him where he liked to go. He smiled and said, "I go on a track in the park". Stanley's aunt said, "That sounds like a great adventure. Let's go together. What do you like to see?" Stanley was so excited. He said, "Oh, lots of things. You can see the birds and squirrels, and I can climb the trees." Stanley and his aunt walked through the park. Stanley loved the freedom he had to explore and the independence to make choices. He ran around and looked at everything, and his aunt followed along closely. They soon reached the end of the track, and Stanley saw his favourite spot. He said, "This is where I like to go!" His aunt held his hand and asked, "What do you like to do here?" Stanley grinned up at her. "I like to look for bugs and watch the leaves dance in the wind," he said. His aunt smiled and said, "That sounds like a wonderful way to visit the park. Let's go explore!"
