Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She was three years old and every day she would go to the park with her mum. At the park, Amy and her mum would sit on the grass and have a picnic. One day, when the sun was shining brightly, Amy saw a cooler with cold water. Amy was very excited and asked her mum, "Can I take a bath in the cooler?" Her mum laughed and said, "Of course you can!" So Amy started to take off her clothes and stepped into the cooler. The cold water felt so nice and refreshing! She had a wonderful time splashing about in the water. When Amy was finished, she felt like a new person. The cold water had made her feel peaceful and calm. She said to her mum, "That was the best bath ever!"

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day and play all night. He had a best friend, a little boy named Tim. Tim and Tom were always together, and they loved to play with Tim's toys. One sunny day, Tim found a toy arrow in his room. He showed it to Tom and said, "Look, Tom! Let's play with this arrow!" Tom meowed and nodded his head. They went outside to play with the arrow. Tim threw the arrow high up in the sky, and Tom would try to catch it. After playing for a while, Tim had an idea. "Tom, let's measure how far the arrow can go!" Tom meowed and agreed. Tim threw the arrow as far as he could, and Tom ran after it. They measured the distance by counting Tom's steps. They played this game all day, and Tom wasn't lazy anymore. He loved running and playing with the arrow. From that day on, Tom and Tim played with the arrow every day. They measured the distance the arrow went, and Tom became faster and stronger. They were very happy, and their friendship grew even stronger. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a big black dog named Max found a sail. He thought, "I need to find a way to use this sail." So, Max took the sail to the river. At the river, Max met a duck named Lily. Lily asked, "What do you have, Max?" Max said, "I have a sail, but I need help to use it." Lily said, "I can help you, Max!" Together, they put the sail on a big log. Then, Max and Lily went on the log and used the sail to move. They had fun sailing on the river all day. And that is how Max and Lily became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a big and deep river lived two fish. One of them was called Blue and one was called Red. One day, Blue and Red were swimming together and Blue said: "Let's have some fun, let's splash each other!" Red laughed and both of them started to splash each other. Suddenly, they heard somebody shouting: "Stop! Blue and Red, I know what you are doing!" Blue and Red looked up and they saw a turtle swimming towards them. The turtle said: "Don't you see the chain in the river? It is here to protect you, if you don't watch out, the chain can hurt you!" Blue and Red, looked at the chain, it was light and silvery, so beautiful! From that day on, Blue and Red learnt their lesson and stayed away from the chain. They knew it was there to protect them. The moral of the story is: Always be careful and listen to the wise advice of your friends.

One day, a big, loud dog named Spot went out to play. He saw a lot of grass and started to run. Spot loved to run in the grass. It was so green and soft. Spot saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing with a ball. She said, "Spot, do you want to play with me?" Spot barked loud and happy. Sue said, "We can include you in our game!" Sue and Spot played with the ball in the grass. They had a lot of fun. Spot was very fast, and Sue was very happy. They played until the sun went down. Then, they went home to rest.
