Category: Intermediate
Title: Mimi the Mouse and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved cheese more than anything else. One day, she saw a big piece of cheese on the table. Mimi wanted to get the cheese, but she was confused about how to reach it. Mimi's friend, a kind bird named Benny, saw her looking at the cheese. Benny asked, "Mimi, do you want to learn how to get the cheese?" Mimi nodded her head and said, "Yes, please, Benny!" Benny taught Mimi how to climb up the chair and then the table. Mimi tried and tried until she finally reached the cheese. She was so happy and thanked Benny for helping her learn. Mimi and Benny shared the cheese and had a great time. From that day on, Mimi was never confused about getting cheese again.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a collar. It was a flexible collar. He loved to play and run with his collar on. One day, Spot saw a big door. The door was locked. Spot wanted to go inside. Spot asked his friend, Cat, "Can you help me open the door?" Cat said, "Yes, I can try!" Cat was very smart. She used her paw to unlock the door. The door opened, and Spot and Cat went inside. Inside, they found a big, fun room. There were toys and treats. Spot and Cat played all day. They were so happy. In the end, they went back home. Spot said, "Thank you, Cat, for helping me." Cat said, "You're welcome, Spot. We are good friends."

Once upon a time, in a big farm, there was a turkey named Tom. Tom was lazy. He did not like to work with the other animals. He only wanted to play and hide all day. One day, the farmer's wife needed help. She asked all the animals to find some food. Tom did not want to help. He wanted to hide and play. The other animals worked hard, but Tom was lazy. At the end of the day, the animals found a lot of food. They were very happy. Tom was hungry, but he did not help. The animals did not want to share their food with Tom. Tom was sad. He learned that if he was lazy and did not help, he would not get to share the good things. From that day on, Tom stopped being lazy. He helped the other animals on the farm and they shared their food with him. The moral of the story is that if you work hard and help others, you will be rewarded.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy horse named Kick. Kick loved to play with his friends in the big field. One day, Kick got an invitation to a big party. He was very happy and excited. Kick went to the party with his friends. They played games, ate yummy food, and laughed a lot. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the decorations away. The friends were sad and scared. But Kick had an idea. He used his strong legs to kick a big ball up in the sky. The ball hit a cloud and all the decorations came back. The friends were so happy and they continued the party. In the end, Kick was the hero of the party.

One day, a group of small bugs lived in a big tree. They were very happy. They had a huge leaf that they all cared for. They played on the leaf every day. But one day, a big bird saw the huge leaf. The bird wanted the leaf for her nest. She said, "I want that leaf!" The bugs did not want to give up their leaf. They said, "No, we care for this leaf. It is our home!" The bird was very mad. She tried to take the leaf away from the bugs. The bugs tried to stop her, but she was too big and strong. The bird took the huge leaf and flew away. The group of bugs was very sad. They lost their huge leaf that they cared for. They had no place to play and live. The bugs had to find a new home, but they missed their old leaf very much.
