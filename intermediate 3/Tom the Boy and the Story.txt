Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was an ordinary boy who loved to eat steak. One day, Tom's mom cooked a big, yummy steak for dinner. Tom's friend, Sam, came over to play. When it was time for dinner, Tom's mom put the steak on the table. Sam looked at the steak and said, "I don't understand. What is that?" Tom said, "This is a steak. It is very good!" Tom shared his steak with Sam. They both ate and were very happy. After dinner, they played some more. Tom learned that it was good to share with his friends. The moral of the story is that sharing makes everyone happy.

One day, a cool cat named Tom found a big red umbrella. He was very happy. Tom walked in the park with his new umbrella. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tom saw his friend, a little dog named Tim. Tim looked very sad. Tom asked, "Why do you weep, Tim?" Tim said, "I lost my toy ball. I am very sad." Tom wanted to help his friend. They looked for the toy ball together. They looked under the trees and behind the bushes. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew the red umbrella away. It flew up into the sky. Tom and Tim watched as the umbrella floated away. But, when it fell back down, it brought Tim's toy ball with it! Tim was so happy, and they both laughed. They played with the ball and forgot all about the umbrella.

---

One day, a little rat named Tom wanted to ride his toy car. It was a wet day outside, so he had to play inside his house. Tom loved to ride his toy car up and down the halls. He went fast and had lots of fun. Tom's friend, a cat named Sue, came to visit him. Sue saw Tom riding his toy car and said, "Can I ride with you, Tom?" Tom was happy to share his toy car with Sue. They both got on the car and started to ride together. As they rode down the hall, they laughed and had a great time. The wet day outside didn't bother them at all. Tom and Sue played together all day, taking turns riding the toy car. They were very happy to be friends and have fun inside on the wet day.

One morning, there was a painter who wanted to lay some paint on the wall. He was very weak and had a hard time doing it. Suddenly, a little 3 year old came over and offered to help him lay the paint. The painter was so happy that someone wanted to help him that he kept saying "Thank you! Thank you!" The painter and the 3 year old worked together for a few hours until the wall was painted. The painter was so pleased that he said to the 3 year old, "Your help was wonderful! I'll give you something for your help." The 3 year old was excited, so he asked for candy. But the painter refused, saying, "You can't have candy. It's bad for you." The little 3 year old was so disappointed that he started to cry. He felt so sad and weak. The painter patted him on the back, saying, "It's okay, I'll give you some money instead." But sadly, the painter never had a chance to give the 3 year old the money. In the morning, the little 3 year old tripped and hurt himself, and the painter never saw him again. The wall was painted, but the little 3 year old never got the reward he deserved.

Once upon a time, there was a little dish. The dish was very restless. It did not like to stay in one place for too long. One day, the dish went for a walk in the garden. In the garden, the dish saw a big, friendly bug. The bug liked to blink its eyes a lot. The dish and the bug became good friends. They played and laughed all day long. As the sun went down, the restless dish and the blinking bug were tired. They found a cozy spot under a big tree. The dish and the bug slept and had sweet dreams, ready for another fun day together.
