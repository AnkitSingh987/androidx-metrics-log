Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Max and the Proud

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Max. He was a very proud little boy and liked when people told him how smart he was. One day, Max wanted to try something new. He decided to climb on the furniture. He was very brave and he wanted to be high up off the ground. Max slowly climbed to the top of the furniture, higher and higher. He was so proud of himself and had a big smile on his face. Suddenly, he heard a loud voice yell out, "Max, get down from there!" His mom had seen him and was worried Max would get hurt. Max was scared and quickly climbed down from the furniture. His mom was very proud of him, she praised him and told him how brave he was. Max was so happy that she was proud of him and smiled from ear to ear. From that day on, Max was careful when climbing on furniture.

One day, a clean aeroplane was in the sky. The aeroplane went up and up. The aeroplane's pilot, Tom, talked to his friend, Sue, on the radio. "Hi, Sue! My aeroplane is going up!" said Tom. "Wow, Tom! Your aeroplane is so clean and nice!" said Sue. The aeroplane went higher and higher. Tom saw big birds and little clouds. He saw the sun and the moon. Tom and Sue talked more and more. "Tom, can your aeroplane go higher?" asked Sue. "Yes, Sue! I can increase how high I go!" said Tom. Tom made the aeroplane go up even more. He was happy to fly his clean aeroplane. Sue was happy to talk to Tom. They had a great day in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big fan in his room. He liked to watch it spin around and around. One day, the fan made a loud noise. Tim felt scared. He ran to his mom. "Mom, I am scared!" Tim said. "The fan is making a loud noise!" Mom remembered that the fan needed to be fixed. She gave Tim a hug and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will fix the fan for you." Mom fixed the fan, and it stopped making the loud noise. Tim was not scared anymore. He was happy that he could watch the fan spin around and around again. Tim and his mom smiled and hugged each other. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a happy family. Mommy, Daddy, and their little girl. They were so happy, they would always smile and laugh together. One day, the family went to a modern cafe. Daddy ordered coffee and Mommy asked for a slice of cake. The waitress brought out a plate with something the little girl had never seen before. It was a small plate of round things with color. "What are those?" the little girl asked curiously. "Salt," Daddy said, "It makes the food tasty." He couldn't help but to frown; he had forgotten to ask for his coffee without salt. Little girl looked confused but after she tried the salt, she smiled. She thought it was so yummy! Now Mommy and Daddy were happy too. The family left the cafe and went home. They shared a happy hug and laughed together. They had a lot of fun and that made them even happier.

Lily liked to play in the park with her ball. She liked to kick it and catch it and throw it. She liked to see how far it could go. One day, she kicked it very hard and it flew over the fence. She ran after it, but she could not see it. She was sad. She saw a man on the other side of the fence. He had a big hat and a long coat. He looked like a stranger. He had her ball in his hand. He smiled at her and said, "Hello, little girl. Do you want your ball back?" Lily was scared. She did not know the man. She did not like his smile. She wanted to run away, but she also wanted her ball. She said, "Yes, please. Give me my ball." The man said, "OK, but first you have to do something for me. You have to come with me to my car. I have something very unusual to show you. It is a surprise. You will like it." Lily felt a chill in her heart. She remembered what her mom had told her. She had told her to never go with strangers. She had told her that strangers could be bad and hurt her. She had told her to scream and run if a stranger tried to take her. Lily knew what to do. She shouted, "No! Leave me alone! Help! Help!" She turned around and ran back to the fence. She saw her mom and dad coming. They had heard her scream. They looked angry and worried. They ran to her and hugged her. They asked her what happened. Lily told them about the man and the ball and the car. She pointed to the man, who was still holding her ball. He looked scared and guilty. He tried to run away, but he was too late. A police officer came and caught him. He took him away and gave Lily her ball back. Lily was happy and relieved. She thanked the police officer and hugged her mom and dad. They told her she was very brave and smart. They told her they loved her. They told her to always be careful and never talk to strangers. They took her home and gave her a big cookie. She ate it and smiled. She set her ball on the shelf and went to bed. She had a good sleep. She dreamed of playing in the park with her ball. She dreamed of kicking it and catching it and throwing it. She dreamed of seeing how far it could go.
