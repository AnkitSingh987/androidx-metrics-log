Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Cat

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily loved to visit her grandma's house. Grandma had a big closet in her room. Lily liked to look in the closet and find fun things to play with. One day, Lily found a small, patient cat in the closet. The cat was very quiet and waited for Lily to find her. Lily was so happy to see the cat. She picked it up and gave it a hug. Lily and the patient cat played all day long. They had so much fun together. When it was time to go home, Lily said goodbye to the cat. She couldn't wait to visit Grandma's house again and play with her new friend.

Once upon a time, in a wild pond, there were many fish. One little fish, named Fin, had a big dream. He wanted to include all his friends in a fun race. One day, Fin told his friend, "Let's have a race. We can include all our friends!" His friend was very excited and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They told all the other fish about the race. They knew something fun was going to happen. The day of the race came. All the fish were ready. They lined up and waited for Fin to say, "Go!" When Fin said it, they all swam as fast as they could. The fish raced through the wild pond, splashing water everywhere. Everyone was having so much fun. At the end of the race, all the fish were happy. They thanked Fin for including them in the fun race. Fin smiled and said, "I'm glad we all had fun together in our wild pond." From that day on, the fish in the wild pond had many more races and fun times together. And they always remembered to include all their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a strong fireman named Tom. Tom had a big red truck. He liked to help people. One day, Tom saw a fire in a house. He drove to the house very fast. He put on his hat and said, "Don't worry, I will put out the fire!" Tom used water to make the fire disappear. The people in the house were happy. They said, "Thank you, Tom! You are a strong fireman!" Tom smiled and said, "I am happy to help." Then, he went back to his big red truck and drove away. The people waved goodbye to Tom, the strong fireman.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there lived a girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside and discover new things. One day, Lucy went for a walk and found a big, funny-looking rock. It was uncomfortable to touch, but she took it home anyway. "Mom, look what I found!" said Lucy, showing her mom the rock. Her mom looked at it and was very surprised. "Lucy, this isn't just a rock, it's a special egg!" she said. Lucy and her mom waited for the egg to hatch. One day, the egg cracked open and out came a little baby dragon! Lucy and the dragon became best friends and had many fun adventures together in their town. And the town was never the same again.

One day, a lively cat named Tom saw a football in the park. He thought it would be fun to play with it. He told his friend, a dog named Max, "Let's play football!" Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, let's play!" They started to play and had lots of fun. Tom would kick the football, and Max would rush to catch it. They played for a long time, and the sun started to go down. Tom said, "One more kick, and then we go home." Tom kicked the football high into the air. Max rushed to catch it, but he missed. The football rolled into the bushes. When they found it, they saw a big surprise. Behind the bushes was a hidden box with lots of toys! They were so happy and played with the new toys all day.

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Mark. Mark was a poor man, but he loved to help people. One day, Mark went to put out a big fire in a house. When he got there, he saw a little girl crying. "Help me, please!" she said. Mark wanted to help the girl, but the fire was too big and hot. He tried to use water, but it did not work. The fire kept getting bigger and bigger. In the end, Mark could not save the house or the little girl. He was very sad and cried. The other firemen tried to help, but they could not. Everyone was sad because of the bad ending.
