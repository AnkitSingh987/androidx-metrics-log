Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Rainbow

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. She saw a big globe that could spin. She ran to it and pushed it with her hands. She liked to see the different colors and shapes on the globe. "Mom, what is this?" she asked. "That is a globe, Mia. It shows all the places in the world. Each place is called a nation. Some nations are big, some are small. Some are hot, some are cold. Some are near, some are far," her mom explained. Mia wanted to learn more. She pointed to a red and white nation. "What is this one?" "That is Canada, Mia. That is where we live. It is a big and cold nation. We have snow and ice and bears and moose," her mom said. Mia smiled. She liked Canada. She pointed to a green and yellow nation. "What is this one?" "That is Brazil, Mia. That is a big and hot nation. They have rainforests and monkeys and parrots and soccer," her mom said. Mia was curious. She pointed to a small and pink nation. "What is this one?" Her mom laughed. "That is not a real nation, Mia. That is a sticker. Someone put it on the globe. It is an adorable sticker. It has a unicorn and a rainbow and a heart." Mia giggled. She liked the sticker. She thought it was pretty and fun. She wanted to put more stickers on the globe. She asked her mom if she could. "Sure, Mia. But only if you share with the other kids. Maybe they want to put stickers too. Maybe they want to spin the globe too. Maybe they want to learn about the nations too," her mom said. Mia nodded. She was a good girl. She liked to share and play and learn. She spun the globe and looked for more stickers. She hoped to find more friends and more nations. She was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite shirt. It was a red shirt with a big star on it. He felt important when he wore it. Tim wore his red shirt every day. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared. Tim wanted to help the cat. He said to himself, "I dare to help the cat because I have my important shirt on." Tim's mom helped him climb the tree. When Tim got close to the cat, he said, "Don't be scared, kitty. I will help you." Tim held the cat and climbed down the tree with his mom's help. The cat was happy to be safe. Tim felt proud and knew his important shirt helped him be brave.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sarah. She was only three years old and had the insistence of trying new things, especially when she saw something that looked interesting. One day, Sarah saw something extraordinary because it was so flexible. It was the way her cat seemed to lean around the corner of the kitchen wall. Sarah asked the cat, "How do you lean so much?" The cat replied, "It requires a lot of practice! But you can do it too if you are flexible." Sarah was very curious, so she asked the cat to show her how it was done. The cat showed her how to lean around the corner of the wall and Sarah tried to copy the cat. At first she found it hard, but soon she was able to lean in the right way. After that, Sarah kept practicing so she could get better and better each day. The end.

One day, a girl named Lucy wanted to go on a picnic. She asked her mom to help her pack a big basket with food. They put in sandwiches, apples, and cookies. Lucy was very happy. Before they left, Lucy's mom told her to wear a hat because it was sunny outside. Lucy put on her favorite hat with a big bow. She felt good wearing it. They walked to the park and found a nice spot under a tree. At the park, they saw a poor, little bird with no food. Lucy felt sad for the bird. She decided to share her picnic with it. The bird ate some crumbs from the cookies and was very happy. Lucy and her mom had a great time at the picnic, and they made a new friend too.
