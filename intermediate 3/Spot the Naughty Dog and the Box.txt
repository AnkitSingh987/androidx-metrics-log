Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Naughty Dog and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a naughty dog named Spot. Spot liked to play and run all day. One day, Spot found a box. The box was closed, but Spot wanted to see what was inside. So, Spot used his paws to open the box. Inside the box, Spot found a thermometer. He didn't know what it was, but it looked fun to play with. Spot took the thermometer out of the box and started to run around with it in his mouth. Spot's friend, a little girl named Sue, saw him playing with the thermometer. Sue knew it was not a toy, so she said, "Spot, you are being naughty! Give me the thermometer, please." Spot listened to Sue and gave her the thermometer. Sue put it back in the box and closed it. Then, she found a ball for Spot to play with instead. Spot was happy and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was an ugly bunny. This bunny had big ears and a funny tail. The other bunnies did not like to play with the ugly bunny. They would laugh at him and make him sad. One day, the ugly bunny found a big carrot. He wanted to share it with the other bunnies. But they still laughed at him and did not want the carrot. The ugly bunny felt very sad and went away. Then, a nice bunny came to the ugly bunny. She did not laugh at him. She liked the big carrot and wanted to share it. The two bunnies ate the carrot together and became friends. The other bunnies saw this and felt bad. They learned not to laugh at others and became friends with the ugly bunny too.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. He loved to swim in the pond near his home. One sunny day, Buddy saw a little girl named Lily by the pond. She had a big jar of jam with her. "Hi, Buddy!" said Lily. "Do you want some jam?" Buddy wagged his tail and barked. He liked jam a lot. Lily gave Buddy a little jam, and it was very yummy. Buddy and Lily played by the pond all day. They swam together and had fun. Buddy was so happy to have a new friend. From that day on, they were always together, and Buddy was a loyal friend to Lily.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree in the forest. A man with an axe came to the tree. He wanted to cut it down. The tree was very sad. It did not want to yield to the man with the axe. But the man did not listen. He kept trying to cut the tree down. One day, a little bird came and sat on the tree. The bird sang a sweet song to the tree. The tree felt happy, and it made the man's face sour. The man with the axe stopped cutting the tree. He listened to the bird's song and felt better. The tree and the man became friends.

There was a girl named Jane. She wanted something special for her birthday. So she asked her mom for a pink balloon. Her mom told her that no balloon shop was open. So Jane started to cry. Suddenly her mom had an idea. She took out a big bowl and a jar of butter from the kitchen. Then she took a bundle of strings from the drawer and made a big pink heart. After that, she attached the heart to the strings and said to Jane: "Here you go, a pink heart!" Jane was so excited that she hugged her mom. She smiled and said: "Can I attach the strings to the butter?" Her mom said: "Sure, but why are you attaching the strings to the butter?" Jane laughed and said: "So I can fly away on my pink heart for my birthday!"

Once upon a time, there was a big, mighty statue in the park. It was so big that all the kids liked to look at it and play near it. The statue was of a man who could cut things very well. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. Lily saw the mighty statue and wanted to play near it. She looked at the man who could cut and thought he looked nice. So, she started to play with her ball near the statue. But then, something unexpected happened! The ball hit the statue's foot, and the statue began to move! The mighty statue was not just a statue, but a real man who could cut! He smiled at Lily and started to play with her and the ball. Lily and the man who could cut had a lot of fun that day, and they became good friends.
