Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big, wide forest. In the forest, there were many animals who lived and played together. They were all friends and liked to have fun. One day, two animals, a cat and a dog, had a fight. The cat said, "I can run fast and jump high." The dog said, "I am strong and can bark loud." They both wanted to see who was the best. So, they started to fight. As they fought, their friends, a rabbit and a squirrel, saw them. The rabbit said, "We should not fight. We are friends." The squirrel agreed and they both went to stop the fight. The rabbit and squirrel held the cat and dog tight, so they could not move. Soon, the cat and dog stopped fighting and remembered they were friends. They all hugged and played together again in the wide forest.

---

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a soft, pretty bird in a tree. The bird said, "Hi, Lucy! I have a surprise for you!" Lucy was very excited. She asked, "What is the surprise?" The bird told her to close her eyes and count to ten. So, Lucy closed her eyes and counted, "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!" When Lucy opened her eyes, she saw a big pile of leaves on the ground. She was confused. "Is this the surprise?" she asked the bird. The bird said, "Yes! Jump in and see what happens!" Lucy jumped into the pile of leaves. To her surprise, she fell into a secret, fun playroom! The bird had made a special place just for her. Lucy and the bird played all day long, and they became the best of friends.

---

John and Sam were friends. Sam had a big nose and John always teased him about it. Today was a special day. They both wanted to compete in a race. John said, "You can't win the race! Your nose is too big!" Sam smiled. He said, "I can try! I can be competitive!" John thought for a moment. He knew that Sam was right. So they both started to run. Sam ran very fast. He was so sure that he would win. But then John put on a burst of speed and passed him. John won the race! Sam cheered and hugged him. He said, "You were so competitive!" John smiled and said, "Yes I was! We can race again next time!"

Once upon a time, there was a big frog. The frog was very sad because he was boring. No one wanted to play with him. He sat by the pond every day, all alone. One day, a little girl came to the pond. She saw the sad frog and wanted to help him. She knew a magic trick. She said, "If I kiss your lip, you will not be boring anymore!" The frog was scared, but he wanted to try. The little girl gave the frog a big kiss on his lip. Then, something unexpected happened. The frog turned into a beautiful butterfly! The butterfly was so happy and the little girl was happy too. They played together all day and became best friends.

---

One day, a grumpy cat named Tom went to the store to buy a bow. He wanted to look nice for his friend's birthday party. Tom walked to the store and saw many bows. There were big bows and small bows, red bows and blue bows. He picked a pretty yellow bow. At the party, Tom wore his new yellow bow. His friend, a dog named Max, said, "Wow, Tom! I like your bow!" Tom felt happy and not grumpy anymore. They played games and ate cake at the party. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl saw Tom's bow and thought it was a toy. She took the bow off Tom and started to play with it. Tom got grumpy again. But Max had an idea. He went to the girl and said, "Can we trade? I have a toy for you." The girl agreed and gave Tom his bow back. Tom and Max were happy again and continued to enjoy the party.
