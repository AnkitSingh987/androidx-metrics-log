Category: Intermediate
Title: The Scary Robber and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a scary robber who lived in a cave. Every day, he came out to steal rags from people’s homes. One day, the robber saw a charming doll in the window of a house. He wanted to steal the doll for himself. The robber knocked on the front door and a little girl opened it. She was only three years old. “What do you want?” she asked. The robber said, “I want your doll. Give it to me or I’ll be scared.” The little girl knew she shouldn't give away her cherished doll. So she said, “No!” The robber was angry and scared the little girl away. Then he took the rag doll and left. The moral of the story is that it's never right to take what belongs to someone else. No matter how scared you are, it's important to stand up for what's right.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a slow walk in the woods. He saw a big tree with a hole in it. Tim thought the hole could reveal something fun or interesting. He didn't know that there was trouble hiding inside. Tim put his hand in the hole to see what was inside. He felt something soft and furry. He got excited and pulled it out. It was a small, scared rabbit! The rabbit was in trouble because it had lost its way home. Tim knew he had to help the rabbit. He remembered seeing a rabbit hole near his house. He thought the rabbit might live there. Tim walked slowly back to his house, holding the rabbit gently. When they got to the rabbit hole, the rabbit hopped in happily. Tim felt good that he could help the rabbit and save it from trouble.

Once upon a time, there was a lively boy named Tim. He loved to play and run around all day. Tim had a big heart and always wanted to help others. One day, Tim saw a little bird trying to fly but it could not. The bird was sad and Tim wanted to prevent the bird from being sad. So, Tim found a stick and gently helped the bird back to its nest. The bird was happy and thanked Tim for his help. The next day, Tim found out that the bird was a magic bird! To thank Tim for his help, the magic bird gave him a special gift. Tim could now help and prevent bad things from happening to his friends. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others, because good things can come from it.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. The friends were Jack and Jill. Every day, Jack and Jill would jog together in the park. One day, Jack suggested a snack for after their jog. "Let's have some yogurt for a snack," Jack said. Jill agreed and said, "Yes, yogurt is dependable and very tasty." Jack and Jill went to the store and bought the yogurt. They jogged back to the park, each carrying a cup of yogurt. When they finished jogging, they enjoyed their yogurt. It was delicious. Jack and Jill vowed to jog together every day. They were happy to have such a dependable friend. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a hot day. A little girl named Sue felt very warm. She saw a fan in the room. Sue wanted the fan to make her cool. Sue asked her mom for the fan. "Mom, can I have the fan, please?" Her mom heard her request. She said, "Yes, you can use the fan, Sue." Sue was happy to hear that. Sue turned on the fan. The fan made a nice breeze. It made Sue feel cool and happy. Sue played with her toys and felt good. The fan helped Sue on the hot day.

Once upon a time, in a modern house, there lived a dog named Max. Max was a good dog who loved to protect his family. He always stayed near them and made sure they were safe. One day, Max heard a noise outside. He went to the door and saw a little cat. The cat looked cold and hungry. Max opened the door and said, "Hello, little cat. You can come in and be warm." The cat came in and they became friends. Max and the cat played together all day. They had so much fun. But then, the cat started to grow bigger and bigger! Max was surprised. The cat turned into a big lion! Max said, "Oh no! I must protect my family!" The lion smiled and said, "Don't worry, Max. I am a friendly lion. I just needed help to become big again. Thank you for being kind to me." Max and the lion stayed friends and they all lived happily ever after.
