Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to sail his little boat. One day, he woke up early to see the sunrise. He was very enthusiastic. He ran to the lake with his boat. At the lake, he met a girl named Sue. Sue also had a boat. "Let's sail together," said Tom. Sue agreed, and they both put their boats in the water. They had so much fun sailing under the pretty sunrise. As they sailed, they saw a duck stuck in some plants. Tom and Sue worked together to help the duck. The duck was so happy. The moral of the story is: it is good to help others and share fun times with friends.

Once there was a deer who wanted to go on an adventure. The deer went for a walk in the forest and found an open field. The deer was excited to explore and so it ran around the field. Suddenly, it noticed a large measuring tape. The deer had never seen anything like it before. The deer wanted to measure its long legs, so it grabbed the measuring tape. But then it realized it was stuck and couldn't get free. The deer tried pulling and tugging but it was no use. It was stuck! The deer was so scared but it didn't give up. It saw a butterfly land on a nearby flower and asked it for help. The butterfly said, "Sure! I can fly around and try to find an open window to get you out." The butterfly flew away and after a few minutes, it came back with an opened window. The deer was excited and hopped out through the window. It had finally escaped from the measuring tape, and the deer continued its adventure.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He had a red kite with him. The sky was clear and blue. Tim was very happy. He ran and let the kite fly high in the sky. But then, the wind blew very strong. The kite went far away from Tim. He started to wander and look for his kite. He was sad and scared. He did not know where his kite was. Tim saw a big tree. The red kite was stuck in the tree! He thought of a plan. Tim found a long stick and got the kite out of the tree. He was very happy. Tim knew that he should be more careful when flying his kite.

Once upon a time, there was a busy rat named Remy. Remy liked to play all day. He lived in a big park with many trees. He had many friends who liked to play too. One sunny day, Remy met a new friend named Lily. Lily was a little bird. Remy said, "Hi, Lily! Do you want to play with me?" Lily said, "Yes, Remy! I want to play too!" They were very happy to play together. Remy and Lily played many games. They ran, they jumped, and they laughed. They played hide and seek, and tag. The sun was going down, and it was time to go home. Remy said, "Bye, Lily! Let's play again tomorrow!" And they did.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big sign. The sign says: "Race for kids. Run fast and win a prize. Sign up here." Tom and Lily want to join the race. They run to the sign and write their names. They are very excited. The race starts soon. Tom and Lily line up with the other kids. They hear a loud whistle. They start to run. Tom runs very fast. He wants to win the prize. Lily runs fast too, but not as fast as Tom. Lily sees a small boy fall down. He cries and holds his knee. He is hurt. Lily stops running. She goes to the boy and asks: "Are you okay?" The boy shakes his head. He says: "No, it hurts. I can't run anymore." Lily feels sorry for the boy. She decides to be helpful. She says: "Don't worry. I will help you. Come on, let's go to the nurse. She will make you feel better." Lily takes the boy's hand and walks with him to the nurse. The nurse smiles and says: "Thank you, Lily. You are very kind and helpful. You did the right thing." Lily smiles too. She is happy to help the boy. She does not care about the race anymore. She thinks: "Being helpful is better than winning a prize."
