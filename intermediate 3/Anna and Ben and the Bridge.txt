Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Bridge

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play near the river. There was a big bridge over the river, where cars and buses went. Sometimes, Anna and Ben would go under the bridge and look at the water and the stones. One day, they saw a duck with her ducklings swimming in the river. They wanted to feed them some bread, so they ran to the bridge and climbed over the fence. But their mom had told them not to go on the bridge, because it was dangerous and naughty. "Anna, Ben, come back here!" their mom shouted from the other side of the fence. "You are not allowed to go on the bridge! You could fall or get hurt!" But Anna and Ben did not listen. They thought it was fun to be on the bridge. They threw some bread to the duck and her ducklings, and they quacked happily. "Look, mom, they like it!" Anna said. "Yes, they do, but you are being very naughty!" their mom said. "You don't understand how dangerous it is to be on the bridge! You have to come back now, or I will be very angry!" Anna and Ben felt sorry for making their mom angry. They did not want to be naughty. They wanted to be good. They climbed back over the fence and ran to their mom. They hugged her and said they were sorry. Their mom hugged them back and said she was glad they were safe. She said she loved them, but they had to listen to her and not go on the bridge again. She said they could feed the duck and her ducklings from the shore, where it was safe. Anna and Ben nodded and said they understood. They followed their mom to the shore and threw some more bread to the duck and her ducklings. They watched them swim and quack, and they smiled. They were happy to be good.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play in the bathroom. They had many toys that they could put in the water and make bubbles and noises. But sometimes they also liked to quarrel over who could use the faucet. One day, they were playing with their rubber ducks and boats, when Lily decided she wanted to make the water high. She turned the faucet all the way and the water started to fill the tub. Ben did not like that. He wanted to make the water low. He tried to turn the faucet the other way, but Lily pushed his hand away. "Stop it, Ben! I want high water!" Lily said. "No, I want low water!" Ben said. They began to pull and push each other, trying to get to the faucet. They did not notice that the water was getting too high and spilling over the tub. They also did not notice that their mom was coming to check on them. "Mommy, help! They are quarreling!" their baby sister, Mia, said from her crib in the next room. Mom heard Mia and hurried to the bathroom. She saw the mess and the twins fighting. She was very angry and scared. "Lily and Ben, stop it right now!" she shouted. "Look what you have done! You have made a flood in the bathroom! You are in big trouble!" Lily and Ben stopped and looked at their mom. They saw the water on the floor and the toys floating. They felt sorry and ashamed. "We are sorry, Mommy," they said. Mom turned off the faucet and drained the tub. She took the twins out of the bathroom and dried them off. She told them to go to their room and think about what they had done. She also told them to say sorry to each other and to Mia. Lily and Ben did as they were told. They hugged and said sorry to each other and to Mia. They learned their lesson and promised to share the faucet and not quarrel anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a busy hunter named Tom. Tom liked to look for animals in the big woods. One day, Tom saw a big, brown bear. "I will catch you, bear!" said Tom. Tom ran after the bear, but he did not see the big rock in his way. He fell down and hit his head. When he woke up, he saw that he was lost. "Oh no, where am I?" Tom said. Just then, a kind squirrel came up to Tom. "Why are you sad?" asked the squirrel. Tom told the squirrel that he was lost. The squirrel said, "I know these woods. I can help you find your way home." Tom was very happy. The squirrel and Tom walked together, and soon they found Tom's home. Tom said, "Thank you, squirrel. You are a good friend." And they lived happily ever after.
