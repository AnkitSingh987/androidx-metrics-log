Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack the Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time there was a boy named Jack. He was full of energy and was always restless. One day he was in the living room playing when he saw the shelf across the room. He had never noticed it before and wanted to find out what was on the shelf. He ran over to the shelf and tried to reach the top. He wasn't tall enough and became frustrated at not being able to see what was on the shelf. “Why don’t you march to your room and get a chair to stand on?” said his mother. Jack didn't want to march to his room. He kept trying to reach the shelf but he still couldn't get there. Suddenly, his mom marched into the room and carried him to his room. She put him onto the chair and he was able to see the top of the shelf. He was delighted to see all the toys on the shelf! When Jack looks back at that moment, he remembers feeling restless and frustrated, but also very happy for discovering what was on the shelf.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. They would run and play all day long. Sue's mom would always give them snacks to eat. Spot's favorite snack was celery. One day, they were playing outside when it started to rain. Sue and Spot had to go inside the house. They wanted to play more, but the rain made them delay their fun. Sue's mom gave them celery to eat while they waited for the rain to stop. Sue and Spot were very loyal to each other. They waited and waited. Finally, the rain stopped, and they could go outside to play again. They were so happy to play together, and they shared the celery as their special snack. They knew they would always be loyal friends, no matter what.

Once upon a time, in a large park, there was a big statue. The statue was of a kind man who loved to play with the kids. Near the statue, there was a red ball that kids loved to bounce. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. Lily saw the red ball and wanted to play. She ran to the ball and started to bounce it high in the air. She was very happy. As Lily played, she looked at the big statue and imagined it was a friend. She bounced the ball to the statue, and it seemed like the statue wanted to play too. Lily and the statue played all day, and when it was time to go home, she gave the statue a big hug.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very excited because she was going to see ice for the first time. Lily lived in a warm place, and she had never seen ice before. Her mom told her they would go to the ice rink today. When they got to the ice rink, Lily saw the ice and said, "Wow, it's so shiny!" Her mom held her hand and they walked onto the ice together. Lily was a little scared, but her mom was there to comfort her. "Don't worry, Lily. I'm here to help you," her mom said. As they skated on the ice, Lily began to feel more comfortable. She started to have lots of fun. She laughed and said, "I like ice! It's so slippery and fun!" Her mom smiled and they skated together until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely train. It went on a rail every day. The train had no friends. It felt very sad. One day, a little boy came to the rail. He saw the lonely train. The boy said, "Hello, train! Why are you sad?" The train said, "I have no friends. I am lonely." The boy felt bad for the train. The boy had an idea. He said, "I will take you to find friends!" The train was happy. They went on the rail to find friends. They met a big tree, a red car, and a cute dog. They all became friends. The train was not lonely anymore. They all lived happily together.
