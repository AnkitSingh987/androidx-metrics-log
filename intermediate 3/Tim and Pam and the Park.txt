Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Pam and the Park

Tim and Pam are twins. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big hole in the ground. They want to know what is in the hole. They find a cheap rope near the hole. They tie the rope to a tree. Tim says, "I will lift you with the rope. You can look in the hole." Pam says, "OK, but be careful." Tim holds the rope and Pam sits on the end. Tim pulls the rope and lifts Pam up. Pam goes up and up. She sees the sky and the birds. She feels happy. But then, the rope breaks. Pam falls down. She screams. Tim screams. Pam lands in the hole. She is scared and hurt. Tim runs to the hole. He sees Pam. He says, "I'm sorry, Pam. Are you OK?" Pam says, "No, Tim. I'm not OK. The hole is dark and cold. And I have a boo-boo on my leg." Tim cries. He says, "I will get help, Pam. Don't worry." He runs to find a grown-up. He hopes Pam will be OK. He wishes he did not use the cheap rope. He wishes he did not lift Pam. He wishes they did not play near the hole.

Once upon a time there was a helicopter. The helicopter loved to rock. It rocked up and down and side to side. One day the helicopter flew up too high. It hit a big cloud and got stuck. The cloud pushed the helicopter and it came crashing down. The helicopter was broken. The little boy who owned the helicopter was very sad. He put the broken pieces on the floor. Then he held his hands up high and began to rock. His rocking made the broken pieces move. He rocked and rocked and rocked. Suddenly, the broken pieces started to come together. The broken helicopter was as good as new! The little boy kept rocking and suddenly, the helicopter rose up from the floor. It was flying again! The boy kept rocking and the helicopter flew higher and higher. He was so happy that his helicopter was mended. The little boy and his wonderful helicopter had lots of fun rocking and flying together.

Once upon a time, there was a small town. In this town, there were many small houses. Each house had some furniture inside. There were chairs, tables, and beds. The people in the town liked their furniture. One day, a big wind came. It blew all the furniture out of the houses. The furniture was all mixed up. The people did not know what to do. They were sad and worried. A little girl named Lucy had an idea. She said, "Let's unite and find our furniture!" The people liked her idea. They all worked together to find their chairs, tables, and beds. Soon, all the furniture was back in the right houses. The people were happy again. They thanked Lucy for her idea. From that day on, the people in the small town were always ready to unite and help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Max. He liked to play outside in front of his porch. Every day, Max would twist and turn his body in all kinds of crazy ways. One day, while Max was playing and twisting on the porch, a harsh voice interrupted him. "Hey, little boy," the voice said, "stop twisting around like that and go in the house right now." Max looked up to see a tall man standing in front of him. He knew the man wouldn't let him play anymore, so he stopped twisting and slowly walked inside. As he walked away, Max whispered to himself, "It wasn't fair. Why did I have to stop twisting around on my porch? It was fun!"

Once upon a time there was a pink bear. He loved to stir things with his paws. One day, he saw a jar of honey on the counter. The bear's eyes sparkled and he began to stir the honey with his paws. It was so much fun, he stirred and stirred until the honey was all mixed together! He was so proud of himself because his honey was now the perfect texture and tasted so sweet. The pink bear smiled and went back to his nap.
