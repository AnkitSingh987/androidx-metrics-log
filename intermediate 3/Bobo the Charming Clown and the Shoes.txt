Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Charming Clown and the Shoes

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a charming clown named Bobo. Bobo loved to make people laugh and smile. He had a big red nose, funny hair, and big shoes. One day, Bobo wanted to attach balloons to his big shoes. He thought it would be fun to walk around with balloons on his shoes. So, he found some pretty balloons and tied them to his shoes. Bobo walked around town with his balloons. The people laughed and smiled when they saw him. The charming clown made everyone happy with his funny shoes and big red nose. And that is how Bobo the clown made his town a happier place.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big red ball near a tree. Tim wanted to play with the ball, so he asked his mom, "Can I play with the ball?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim started to play with the ball. He kicked it and it went very far. It was clear that Tim was having fun. But then, the ball went near the road. Tim's mom saw it and said, "Stop! Don't go near the road." Tim stopped and looked at his mom. She had an opinion about where to play. She said, "Let's play on the grass, not near the road." Tim agreed and they moved to a safer place. They played with the ball and had a fun day at the park.

The night was dark, but deep in the cave, a light started to glow. It was a clear light that shone deep in the cave. Sam the fox was curious and decided to explore. He ventured into the cave and saw the glowing light. He thought it was beautiful and was very surprised! Sam moved closer to the light and saw lots of sparkles. He waved his paw and the sparkles danced around him. He couldn't believe how magical the light was. He knew he had to keep it a secret and share with only his closest friends. Sam smiled and hugged the light before rushing off to his burrow with the knowledge of the secret cave and its beautiful glowing light.

One day, a little girl named Lily was very hungry. She told her mom, "Mom, I am hungry. Can we go to the restaurant?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's go." They went to the restaurant and sat at a big table. Lily saw a yummy cake on the top shelf. She wanted the cake, but she could not reach it. She asked her mom for help. Her mom reached up high and got the cake for Lily. They ate the cake together at the restaurant. Lily was not hungry anymore. They went home happy after their fun day eating cake.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day. Spot had a crazy, fun friend named Tim. Tim was a little boy with big, brown eyes. One day, Tim and Spot went to play in the park. Tim saw a big tree and said, "I will climb that tree!" Spot looked at the tree and just gave a little shrug. He could not climb trees because he was a dog. Tim started to climb the tree and Spot watched him from below. Tim climbed higher and higher. Suddenly, he slipped and fell. But, Spot was right there to catch him. Tim hugged Spot and said, "Thank you for saving me, Spot!" From that day on, they knew they would always look out for each other.

Once upon a time, there was a prince. The prince was kind and mild. He lived in a big castle with his mom and dad. They all loved flowers. One day, the prince went to the garden to see the flowers bloom. He saw red, blue, and yellow flowers. They were very pretty. The prince was very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the flowers away. The prince was sad. But then, he saw a tiny flower that did not blow away. It was the prettiest flower of all. The prince smiled and knew that everything would be okay.
