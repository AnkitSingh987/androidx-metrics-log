Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Police

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy police car. He loved his toy police car so much, he took it everywhere he went. One day, while playing outside, his toy police car got very dirty. Tim knew he had to wipe the dirt off to make his toy police car clean again. Tim went to his friend, Sally, and said, "Look at my toy police car, it's dirty. I need to wipe it clean." Sally was envious of Tim's toy police car because it was so cool. She said, "I want a toy police car too!" Just then, a real police car drove by and stopped. The police officer got out and saw Tim and Sally. He said, "I saw you two playing and I have a surprise for you!" The police officer opened the trunk of his police car and took out a brand new toy police car. He gave it to Sally and said, "Now you both have toy police cars to play with!" Tim and Sally were both very happy. They wiped their toy police cars clean and played together all day long. The real police officer smiled and drove away, happy to have made their day special.

One day, a small cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he climbed, he saw a mist in the sky. The mist was soft and wet. Tom was excited to see the mist. Tom wanted to fill a jar with the mist. He thought it was a fun idea. He climbed down the tree and found a jar. He tried to catch the mist in the jar, but it was not easy. Tom felt embarrassed because he could not do it. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew the mist away. Tom was sad, but he saw a rainbow in the sky. The rainbow was beautiful and made Tom happy. He forgot about the mist and enjoyed the rainbow instead.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a hot day. The sun was shining, and everyone was hot and sweaty. They wanted to find a way to cool down. One little boy named Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's all go swim in the big pond near the town!" Everyone thought that was a great idea. So, they put on their swim clothes and went to the pond. At the pond, they all jumped in the water and started to swim. They splashed and played, and soon they were not hot anymore. Everyone had so much fun swimming on the hot day. They all went home happy and cool.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Mary. Mary was shy and she liked to go to bed early. One night, Mary was in bed early. Suddenly, her mom called out, "Mary! It's time for dinner!" Mary was so shy she didn't want to go out. So she decided to lie! She said, "I'm not hungry Mom." But her Mom said,"You need to come to dinner. I made your favorite meal!" So Mary went out of her room and to dinner. She was still very shy, but she was happy to have her favorite meal. She ate the meal and then went back to bed. And from then on, she was not so shy. She ate dinner with her family every night.

One day, a girl named Sue had a pretty blouse. She loved to wear it all the time. Sue liked to play with her friends in the park. They would laugh, run, and have fun. The park was sometimes noisy, but they liked it. One day, Sue and her friends wanted to do something new. They saw a big hill and decided to climb it. Sue was wearing her pretty blouse. They started to climb the hill. It was hard, but they kept going up. When they got to the top of the hill, they saw a big, muddy puddle. Sue's friends jumped in the puddle and had fun. Sue did not want to get her pretty blouse dirty. She watched her friends play and felt sad. Sue learned that sometimes it is better to have fun with friends, even if it means getting dirty.
