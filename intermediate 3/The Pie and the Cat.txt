Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pie and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a pie. The pie was very yummy. A big cat liked the pie. The cat wanted to eat the pie all day. One day, the cat's friend, a little bird, came. The bird was dead. The cat was very sad. The cat wanted to provide food for the bird, but the bird could not eat. The cat ate the pie alone. The cat was still sad. The cat missed the bird. The pie did not make the cat happy. The cat cried, and the pie was all gone.

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a flexible snake named Sam. Sam loved to play with his friends, the birds, the rabbits, and the squirrels. They all had fun together in the forest. One day, Sam's friend, a little bird named Tim, fell from his nest. Tim was very sad and started to weep. He did not know how to fly back up to his home. Sam wanted to help his friend and make him happy again. Sam used his flexible body to make a long ladder. Tim climbed up the snake-ladder, and he was back in his nest. Tim stopped weeping and was very happy. All the friends in the wild forest cheered for Sam and Tim. They knew they could always count on each other.

One day, Tim went out to play in the snow. He wore his big coat, hat, and gloves, but he forgot his warm pants. He wore his normal pants. Tim made a snowman and had a snowball fight with his friends. It was a fun and icy day. While playing, Tim's pants got wet from the snow. They started to soak. Tim felt cold, but he kept playing because he was having so much fun. He didn't want to stop. Soon, Tim's mom called him inside. She saw his wet pants and knew he must be cold. She had warm pants ready for him to change into. Tim put on the warm pants and felt so much better. He learned that next time he plays in the icy snow, he should wear warm pants to stay cozy and dry.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a notebook. She wanted to use it to draw. Sue asked her mom, "Can I use this notebook to make pretty pictures?" Her mom said, "Yes, that is fair. You can use it." Sue was very happy. She started to draw a big sun, a house, and a happy family. As she was drawing, something unexpected happened. The pictures in the notebook started to move and talk! The sun said, "Hello, Sue! Thank you for drawing me!" Sue was very surprised but also excited. She talked to the sun and the happy family she drew. They all became good friends. From that day on, Sue and her new friends in the notebook had many fun adventures together.

Once there was a friendly dog named Max. Max was a very happy dog and always found something to do. All the people from the neighbourhood would pet him and invite him over for play time. One day Max decided that he wanted to go explore the woods. He asked his neighbour, Mrs. Johnson, if she could take him there. “Well, you’re a brave dog, Max! But it’s getting late. Maybe some other time?”, said Mrs. Johnson. Max nodded in agreement and ran around the garden with the children. The next morning, Mrs. Johnson had an urge to take Max to the woods. She prepared him a special picnic and promised he would have a lot of fun. “Are you ready to go, Max?”, asked Mrs. Johnson. Max barked and was very excited for the surprise. The woods were full of beautiful views and smells. Max was so happy that he ran around and chased the birds. After a few hours of playing, Mrs. Johnson decided it was time to go home. Max barked in agreement and promised to return to the woods soon.

Once upon a time, there was a funny dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run all day. One day, Spot saw a big structure. It looked like a house made of sticks. Spot met a little girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi Spot, do you want to play in my structure?" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, I want to play!" So, Spot and Sue played in the stick house together. But then, something unexpected happened. The stick house started to shake and fall down. Spot and Sue tried to run, but Spot's leg got hurt. He had to suffer because he could not play anymore. Spot and Sue were sad that their fun day had a bad ending.
