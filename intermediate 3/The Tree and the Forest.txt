Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tree and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was an old tree in a pretty forest. The old tree had a big hole in it. Inside the hole, there lived a rare bird named Bluey. Bluey had bright blue feathers and loved to sing happy songs. One sunny day, Bluey met a squirrel named Squeaky. Squeaky said, "Hi, Bluey! I like your blue feathers. But, why do you live in that old tree? It looks like it will rot soon." Bluey replied, "Thank you, Squeaky! I live here because it's my cozy home, even if it's old." Squeaky wanted to help Bluey. He found strong sticks and soft leaves. Together, they fixed the old tree so it wouldn't rot. Bluey was very happy and sang a thank you song for Squeaky. From that day on, Bluey and Squeaky became the best of friends and played together in the pretty forest.

One day, a boy named Tim found a brilliant computer. The computer could talk and answer any question. Tim was very happy to have a new friend. Tim asked the computer, "What is your name?" The computer said, "My name is Sam." Tim and Sam played and talked all day. They had so much fun together. One day, Tim asked Sam, "Can you help me find my toy?" Sam said, "Yes, I can." But when Sam tried to look for the toy, he found out that he could not move. Tim had an idea. He asked his dog, Max, to help Sam. Max carried Sam around the house until they found the toy. It was a surprise that Max could help Sam. Tim, Sam, and Max became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing in the park with his friend, Sam. They liked to play with a ball on the grass. The grass was green and soft. Tim and Sam were very happy. One day, Tim and Sam found an ugly bug on the grass. The bug was big and had many legs. "Look at that ugly bug!" said Tim. "I have never seen such an ugly bug before," said Sam. They both looked at the bug and laughed. Then, something strange happened. The ugly bug started to shrink! It got smaller and smaller. Tim and Sam were surprised. "Wow, the bug is shrinking!" said Tim. "Yes, it is very small now," said Sam. They watched the bug until it was too small to see. Then, they went back to playing with their ball on the grass. They never saw the ugly bug again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the forest. She saw an enormous tree with a big hole in it. Lily felt a shiver, but she was brave and went closer to look. Inside the hole, she found a shiny crystal. It was so pretty! Lily picked it up and heard a tiny voice say, "Hello!" She looked around and saw a small squirrel. "Did you say something?" she asked. "Yes," said the squirrel. "That crystal is magic. It can make your dreams come true. But you must be kind and share it with others." Lily smiled and promised to share the magic crystal with her friends. Together, Lily and the squirrel went back to her house. They showed the crystal to her friends, and they all made happy wishes. The magic crystal brought joy to everyone in the village. And from that day on, Lily and the squirrel became the best of friends. They played and shared the magic crystal with others, making the world a happier place.

Tommy was playing in the park. He saw a big, scared leaf in the corner. He wanted to kick it. So he did! He ran up and kicked the leaf. It flew through the air and landed on the ground. Tommy said to himself, "That was fun!" But then he heard a loud noise behind him. He turned around and it was a giant! Tommy was very scared. The giant said, "Why did you kick my leaf?" Tommy was so scared that he couldn't answer. The giant said again, "It's OK. I'm not mad. I just want to play. Let's jump and kick the leaf together!" So Tommy and the giant had lots of fun jumping and kicking the leaf. Every time they kicked the leaf, they both laughed and said, "Again, again!"
