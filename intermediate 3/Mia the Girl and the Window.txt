Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Window

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She heard a big noise outside. The noise was so loud it made her jump. "What is that noise?" she asked her mom. Her mom went to the window and looked out. "Oh, it's just a big truck," she said. "Don't be scared, Mia. It's not going to hurt you." Mia felt grateful that her mom was there to help her. She went back to playing with her toys. Suddenly, she heard the noise again. This time, she was not scared. She knew it was just the big truck. "Bye-bye, big truck!" Mia said as she waved at the window. "Thank you, Mommy, for not letting it scare me." "You're welcome, Mia," her mom said with a smile. "I'm always here to help you when you're scared."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy cars. Tim was very enthusiastic about his cars. He liked the black car the most because it was big and shiny. One day, Tim's mom said, "Let's add a new car to your collection." Tim was very happy. They went to the toy store and found a new black car. It was even bigger than the one he had at home. Tim and his mom bought the new black car and went back home. Tim played with his cars all day long. He was very enthusiastic about adding the new black car to his collection. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big toy car. It had a battery inside to make it go. The big toy car liked to go fast and play with other toy cars. One day, the big toy car met a pretty doll. The pretty doll liked the big toy car too. They played together and had a lot of fun. They wanted to be together all the time. So, the big toy car and the pretty doll decided to marry. They had a fun party with all their toy friends. They were very happy and played together every day. The big toy car and the pretty doll loved each other very much.

Once upon a time, in a small house with a porch, there was a colorful bird named Bob. Bob liked to sing and play. One day, Bob saw a big tree with a high branch. He wanted to fly up to the branch, but he was scared. Bob's friend, Cat, came by and said, "You can do it, Bob! Try and you will succeed!" Bob thought about it and decided to try. He took a deep breath and started to fly. As he got closer to the branch, he felt happy. He knew he could do it because Cat believed in him. Finally, Bob reached the high branch and looked down at his friend. "I did it, Cat! I succeeded!" he shouted. Cat smiled and said, "I knew you could do it, Bob!" From that day on, Bob was never scared to fly high again.

Once upon a time, there lived a brave little girl named Sarah. She always had a flexible mind and an adventurous spirit. One day, Sarah was out walking in the forest when she saw a big, grey wolf taking a nap. She knew she had to be careful, but she was brave and decided to try and make friends with him. Sarah took a few steps forward and said, "Hello, Wolf. Do you want to be my friend?" The Wolf was a little bit scared and didn't know what to do. He became scared and snapped at Sarah. She became scared too and quickly started to run away. Suddenly, the Wolf changed his mind. He realized Sarah was brave enough to come talk to him and he had made a mistake. The Wolf ran after Sarah and quickly caught up to her. He apologized for scaring her and said he wanted to be her friend. Sarah smiled and was very happy. Together, they ran through the forest, laughing and having a lot of fun. From then on, Sarah and her Wolf were the best of friends.
