Category: Intermediate
Title: Joy the Beautiful Girl and the Magic

Once upon a time there lived a beautiful girl named Joy. No one liked her very much because she was always getting so sick. One day she found a sparkly, magical bottle of medicine. She knew it was special so she refused to tell anyone, but she wanted to try it too. Joy pretended to still be sick and asked her parents for her usual medicine, but she also secretly took a sip of the magic medicine. It felt like a warm hug and made her heart sing. Suddenly she was full of energy and she couldn't help but laugh and dance with joy. Her parents were surprised to see her so happy and naturally asked her what had made the difference. In an instant, Joy knew that she had to escape, so she quickly made up an excuse and ran to the garden. She continued to sip the magic medicine until her energy was gone. When Joy returned, she smiled and hugged her parents. They were glad to see her feeling so well and healthy, and they were sure the old medicine just needed a bit longer. Little did they know it was the beautiful, magical medicine that had made Joy feel like she could dance around the world.

One day, a kid named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw a toy he really liked. He asked his mom, "Can I have the toy, please?" His mom said, "Okay, but you have to pay with your own money." Tim was so excited. He gave the man at the store his money and got the toy. He played with his new toy all day. He ran, jumped, and laughed. Soon, Tim was very tired. He sat down to rest. While Tim was resting, a dog came and took his toy! Tim was sad and surprised. He did not want to lose his new toy. Tim's mom said, "Don't worry. Let's find the dog and get your toy back." They looked and looked, and finally found the dog. The dog had a lot of puppies who were also playing with the toy. Tim saw how happy the puppies were, so he let them keep the toy. His mom was proud of him for being kind.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing with her toys. She saw a pretty angel toy on the shelf. Lucy wanted to play with the angel, but it was too high up. She tried to reach, but she couldn't. Lucy felt bad and started to panic. Lucy's mom saw her panic and asked, "Lucy, what's wrong?" Lucy pointed to the angel and said, "I want to play with the angel, but it's too high up." Her mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, I will help you get the angel." Her mom got the angel down for Lucy. Lucy was very happy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" She played with the angel all day long. Lucy was not bad and didn't panic anymore. She knew her mom would always be there to help her.

Once upon a time, there was a wise cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his friend, a little girl named Sue. One day, Sue was playing in the sun. She got very hot and her skin started to hurt. Sue said, "Tom, my skin hurts from the sun. What should I do?" Tom thought for a moment and then said, "You need to use lotion, Sue. It will help your skin feel better." Sue found the lotion and put it on her skin. She started to feel better. Tom showed Sue how to turn the bottle to close it. They played together all day, and Sue's skin did not hurt anymore. Tom was a very wise cat, and Sue was happy to have him as her friend.

One day, Ben and Lily went to the park with Mom. They brought some bread to feed the birds. They saw many birds in the park. Some were big, some were small, some were red, some were yellow, some were black, and some were blue. Ben and Lily liked the blue birds the most. They were very pretty and shiny. They wanted to make friends with them. They tore some bread into small pieces and threw them on the grass. The blue birds saw the bread and flew down to eat it. Ben and Lily ran to the blue birds and tried to touch them. But the blue birds were scared of Ben and Lily. They did not want to be touched. They flew away quickly and made loud noises. They scattered in the sky and hid in the trees. Ben and Lily were sad. They did not understand why the blue birds did not like them. They wanted to play with them. Mom came to Ben and Lily and hugged them. She told them that the blue birds were wild and shy. They did not like to be near people. They liked to be free and safe. Mom said that the best way to enjoy the blue birds was to watch them from far away. She said that the blue birds would come back if Ben and Lily were quiet and gentle. She said that the blue birds would like them if they respected them. Ben and Lily nodded and smiled. They sat on a bench with Mom and watched the blue birds. They saw them eat the bread, sing, and fly. They thought they were very beautiful and happy. They felt happy too.
