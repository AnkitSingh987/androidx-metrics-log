Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to relax on her soft velvet bed. One day, she saw a big and dangerous dog outside her house. The dog looked mean and scary. Kitty's mom said, "Kitty, don't go near the dangerous dog. It's not safe." But Kitty wanted to show she was brave. She went outside to talk to the dog. "Hi, dog. I am Kitty. Let's be friends," said Kitty. The dangerous dog did not want to be friends. He growled at Kitty and chased her. Kitty ran back to her house and hid under her velvet bed. She was very scared. The dog went away, but Kitty learned a lesson. She should have listened to her mom and stayed away from the dangerous dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to do incredible jumps with her jump rope. She practiced every day in her yard. Mia's best friend, Tom, would watch her and clap his hands. One sunny day, Mia and Tom were playing in the park. Mia showed Tom her incredible jump. Tom said, "Wow, Mia! You do that jump so well!" Mia smiled and did the jump again. They laughed and played together. Later, Mia's mom came to the park. She saw Mia's incredible jump and was very proud. She gave Mia a shiny medal. Mia showed her medal to Tom and said, "Look, I got a medal for my jump!" Tom said, "That's great, Mia! You deserve it!" They both felt very happy and continued to play together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a big, powerful dog. The dog was nice and liked to play with Tim. They played catch and had lots of fun. Then, a new kid came to the park. The new kid did not talk like Tim. He talked in a different language. Tim wanted to play with the new kid, but they did not understand each other. Tim felt sad and started to tremble. The powerful dog saw Tim was sad and had an idea. The dog went between Tim and the new kid and barked, "Woof, woof!" The dog showed them how to play a game without talking. Tim and the new kid played the game and had fun. They did not need words to be friends. The powerful dog helped them learn a new way to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a little puppy named Spot. Spot was upset because he lost his toy ball. He looked everywhere for it, but he could not find it. Spot saw his friend, Bird, and asked, "Bird, have you seen my ball?" Bird replied, "No, Spot, I have not. But let's look for it together!" Spot was happy to have help, so they both looked high and low for the ball. After a while, they found the ball under a big tree. Spot was so happy that he wagged his tail and jumped. Spot said, "Thank you, Bird!" Bird replied, "You're welcome, Spot!" They played with the ball together and had lots of fun. Spot was not upset anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle cat named Tom. Tom liked to polish his iron toy every day. He would take a soft cloth and make his toy shiny and clean. One day, while Tom was polishing his iron toy, he heard a voice. "Hello, can you help me?" it said. Tom looked around and saw a little bird. "I hurt my wing," said the bird. "Can you be gentle and help me?" Tom wanted to help, but he was scared. He had never talked to a bird before. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to grow bigger and bigger! It was a magic bird! "Don't be scared," said the big bird. "I am here to help you too." Tom and the magic bird became friends. They helped each other and played together every day. The gentle cat learned that sometimes, unexpected things can bring the best surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a little puppy named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends. One day, Spot saw something scary. It was a big box with a wrap on it. Spot did not know what it was. Spot went to his friend, Bear, and said, "I saw something scary. It's a big box with a wrap on it." Bear was brave, so he went with Spot to see the box. When they got there, Bear said, "Let's open it!" They opened the box and found a big, soft blanket inside. Spot and Bear laughed. It was not scary at all! They took the blanket and played with it. They wrapped themselves in it and had lots of fun. In the end, Spot and Bear learned that sometimes, things that seem scary are not scary at all. They just need to be brave and look closer. And that was the day Spot and Bear became best friends.
