Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Cheerful Frog and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful frog named Bob. Bob had many friends in the pond. One day, Bob saw a little fish with a hurt toe. The fish was very sad. Bob asked the fish, "What happened to your toe?" The fish said, "I hit it on a rock. It hurts a lot." Bob wanted to help the fish feel better. Bob went to his friend, the wise turtle. The turtle told him to give the fish a soft leaf to make the toe feel better. So, Bob gave the fish a soft leaf. The fish wrapped the leaf around his hurt toe, and it felt much better. The fish said, "Thank you, Bob! You are a good friend." They played together in the pond all day. Bob learned that helping friends is a kind thing to do.

One day, a girl named Amy wanted to paint an amazing picture. She took out her paint and brushes and started to paint. She was very happy as she painted a big sun, a blue sky, and green trees. But then, her little brother Tom came into the room. Tom wanted to paint too, but he was not good at it. He took a brush and started to paint on Amy's picture. Amy was sad and angry. She did not want Tom to ruin her amazing picture. Amy had an idea. She gave Tom his own paper to paint on. They both painted together, and had lots of fun. In the end, they both made amazing pictures. They showed their mom and dad, who were so proud. Then, they hung the pictures on the wall and went to sleep, excited to wake up and see them the next day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a happy girl named Lily. She had a big smile and loved to play with her friends. One day, Lily's mom told her that they were going to join a marriage party. Lily was excited and couldn't wait to go. At the marriage, Lily saw a beautiful bride in a pretty dress. She also saw a handsome man in a nice suit. They were getting married and everyone was happy. Lily was patient and waited for the fun to begin. Soon, it was time to dance and eat yummy food. Lily joined her friends and they all danced together. They laughed and had a great time. The bride and groom were happy too. It was a day that Lily would always remember.

Mia and her mommy arrived at the hotel, and Mia thought it was so adorable. She loved the bright colors and the friendly people who worked there. “Mommy, what are we doing here?” Mia asked. “We’re going to spend the night here, sweetheart.” Mia was excited about spending the night in a hotel- until the night came. As soon as darkness fell, Mia started feeling scared. Mommy stayed with her, holding her until she eventually drifted off to sleep. The next morning, Mia woke up feeling safe. Mommy was there with her, and they had a nice day exploring the hotel. They went to the lobby and saw art displays, and they even stopped in the little cafe for a snack. All the while, Mia felt safer and more secure knowing Mommy was there. “Mommy, I’m so glad you stayed with me, so I didn’t feel scared,” Mia said. Mommy smiled. “I’m glad too. I’m always here for you, no matter what.”

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big house and many toys. One day, he heard a knock on his door. It was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he had no toys to play with. "Please, can I play with your toys?" Tim asked Tom. Tom looked at the boy and thought for a moment. He loved his toys, but he also wanted to be nice. "Okay," Tom said, "you can play with one toy." Tim was very happy and picked a toy car to play with. As they played, Tom accidentally broke the toy car. Tim started to cry. "You broke my toy car!" he said. "I'm sorry, Tim," said Tom. "I'll fix it, please don't cry." Tom tried to fix the toy car, but he couldn't. So, he went to the tap and filled a big bucket with water. He poured the water on the toy car and it magically turned into a brand new car. Tim was so happy and thanked Tom. From that day on, they played together and shared their toys. And they both learned that sharing and being kind is more important than having many toys.
