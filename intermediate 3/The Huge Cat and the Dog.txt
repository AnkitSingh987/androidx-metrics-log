Category: Intermediate
Title: The Huge Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a huge cat. The cat had a big purse. One day, the cat saw a sack. The sack was on the ground. The cat wanted the sack. The cat put the sack in the purse. The cat was happy. The cat walked home with the purse. The cat did not know what was in the sack. At home, the cat opened the sack. Out came a big dog! The dog was not nice. The dog took the cat's purse. The cat was sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Will. Will loved to sing. Every day, he sat on a branch and sang his perfect song. One day, Will met a frog. The frog liked Will's song. They became good friends. The frog wanted to sing too. So, Will taught the frog how to sing the perfect song. They sang together every day. All the animals came to listen. They loved the perfect song. Will and the frog were very happy. And they sang their perfect song forever.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big box. The box had a sign that said "prize." Tim wanted the prize. But the box was up high. He could not reach it. Tim felt sad. Tim went to his friend, Ben. He asked Ben for help. Ben was tall. Ben could reach the box. Ben got the box down for Tim. They were excited to see the prize. When they opened the box, it was empty. No prize was inside. Tim and Ben felt sad. They asked their mom why the box was empty. Mom said, "The prize is the fun you have with friends." Tim and Ben smiled. They played together all day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a gorilla named Bobo. Bobo loved to stretch his long arms and legs every morning. He was a fast runner and liked to play with his friends. One sunny day, Bobo was playing with his friend, a little bird named Chirpy. They were racing through the forest. "I am very fast!" said Bobo. "You can't catch me!" Chirpy flew high in the sky and said, "I am faster than you, Bobo!" They laughed and played together. But, as they played, they heard a loud noise. It was a big tree falling down! Bobo and Chirpy were scared. Then, they saw a tiny ant stuck under the tree. Bobo used his strong arms to stretch and lift the tree. Chirpy flew down and helped the ant. The ant was safe! The ant said, "Thank you, Bobo and Chirpy!" They all became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a new friend named Tim. They liked to play together. One day, Sue wanted to invite Tim to her house. Sue asked her mom if she could make cocoa for them. Her mom said yes and made the best cocoa ever. Sue was very happy and ran to Tim's house to invite him. When Tim came over, they both drank the cocoa and played with Sue's toys. They laughed and had so much fun. Sue and Tim were happy to be new friends who shared cocoa together.

John was bald. He was okay with it, he thought it made him look special. One day, he was standing outside his door as it was open. He looked inside, and there was a machine. It was called a record player. What could it do? John had no idea, but he was curious. John stepped inside the house, and moved closer to the strange machine. He touched it. He pressed a button and suddenly, music began to play. It was beautiful and strange. After that, John pressed more buttons and different music filled the air. He liked it. From that day on, John was a record collector. He started collecting different kinds of records and sharing them with his friends. Every day, he explored all kinds of kinds of music and information. He told all his friends how he had found his love of records, right at his door. He discovered something even more special with the machine that he found that day.
