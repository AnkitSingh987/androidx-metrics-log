Category: Intermediate
Title: Billy the Amazing Goat and the Timmy

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there lived an amazing goat named Billy. Billy loved to play with his friends. One day, he saw a little boy named Timmy. Timmy was sad because he had no friends to play with. Billy wanted to help Timmy. Billy went to Timmy and said, "Hi, I am Billy. Do you want to play with me?" Timmy looked at Billy and smiled. They played all day long. They ran, jumped, and waved their hands in the air. They were very happy. One day, while they were playing, Billy saw another goat who was in trouble. The goat was stuck in a hole. Billy and Timmy helped the goat out of the hole. The goat said, "Thank you, Billy and Timmy!" From that day on, the three friends played together every day. They learned that helping others can make you happy and bring you more friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room with a big table in the middle. On the table, there was a toy that could spring up and down. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, liked to play with the toy. One day, Tim and Spot were playing with the spring toy on the table. They were having lots of fun. Spot would jump and try to catch the toy as it sprang up in the air. Tim would laugh and clap his hands. But then, something bad happened. Tim pushed the toy too hard and it sprang off the table. It broke into many pieces. Tim and Spot were very sad. They knew they could not play with the toy anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They saw a tap near a big tree. The tap was flexible and could move around. Tim and Spot played in the water that came out of the tap. They got very wet and had lots of fun. Tim found a small box and had an idea. He said, "Spot, let's bury the box under the tree and come back later to dig it up!" Spot barked happily, and they both started to dig a hole. They put the box in the hole and covered it with dirt. After a few days, Tim and Spot went back to the park to dig up the box. But when they dug up the box, they found something unexpected inside. "Wow, Spot! Look, there are new toys in the box!" Spot barked with joy. They didn't know who put the toys in the box, but they were very happy and played with the new toys all day.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Ben. He was only 3 years old. One day, he went outside and saw a big chamber. It looked so smooth and inviting. He wanted to explore it, so he walked up the stairs that led inside the chamber. Once he got to the top, Ben was so amazed. The walls were so smooth and shiny that he couldn't help but reach out and touch them. He kicked the walls with curiosity, and he heard a strange echoing sound inside. He nudged his mother, "Mom, what is this? What does this chamber do?" His mother smiled and replied, "This is an echo chamber! It echoes every sound you make. Try saying something, and you'll hear it come back to you!" Ben was so excited. He shouted, "Hi!" and heard his voice come back. Delighted, he kept shouting and kicking the walls. Every time he did, the walls seemed to respond with a smooth and pleasing echo. Ben was having so much fun that he spent the entire afternoon in the echo chamber. He loved the way his voice bounced off the walls and created a fun and playful noise. All too soon, it was time to leave. But, Ben could not forget the wonderful time he spent in the chamber, learning how to make his voice echo.

One day, a busy bee named Bob was flying around. He was looking for flowers to get food. Bob saw his friend, a little bird named Betty. Betty was sad because she was caught in a net. "Help me, Bob!" said Betty. "I am stuck in this net." Bob wanted to help his friend. He thought of a plan. He flew away and came back with a surprise. It was a stick! Bob used the stick to lift the net. Betty was free! "Thank you, Bob," said Betty. "You are a good friend." Bob and Betty played together for the rest of the day. They were very happy. The surprise stick saved the day!
