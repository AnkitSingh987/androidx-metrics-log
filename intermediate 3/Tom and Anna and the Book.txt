Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Anna and the Book

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they go to the library with their moms. The library has many books with pictures and words. Tom and Anna want to read the same book. It is a book about a big bear and a little mouse. "Can I have the book?" Tom asks Anna. "No, I want the book," Anna says. They both pull the book. The book rips. Tom and Anna are sad. Their moms are angry. "Tom and Anna, you have to share the book. Sharing is good. You can take turns to read the book," Tom's mom says. "Yes, sharing is good. You can also read the book together. You can tell each other the story of the bear and the mouse," Anna's mom says. Tom and Anna say sorry to each other. They say sorry to their moms. They say sorry to the librarian. They share the book. They read the book together. They tell each other the story of the bear and the mouse. They laugh and smile. They finish the book. They are happy. They are also tired. They give the book back to the librarian. They say thank you. They go home with their moms. They take a nap. They dream of the bear and the mouse.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty boy named Tim. He loved to watch films all day. One day, Tim's mom told him to finish his food before he could watch a new film. Tim was so excited to watch the film that he did not finish his food. He hid the food under the table and ran to watch the film. This was a bad thing to do. As Tim watched the film, he heard a loud noise. His mom found the food under the table. She was very sad and angry. Tim could not watch the film anymore. He learned that being naughty and not finishing his food had a bad ending.

One day, a cop named Tim wanted to decorate his car. He liked to be adventurous. He had many colors to use. He was very excited. Tim began to paint his car. But a little girl named Sue saw him. She said, "What are you doing, Mr. Cop?" Tim said, "I am making my car pretty." Sue wanted to help. But she was too small to reach the car. Sue was sad. She wanted to be part of the fun. Tim saw Sue's sad face. He had an idea. He said, "Sue, you can help me choose the colors." Sue was happy. They finished decorating the car together. It was the most adventurous car in town.

Once there was a boy named John who loved to play outside. One day, his mommy decided to teach him how to use the grill. She showed him the grill and told him to be creative. He was a little scared, but he trusted his mommy and decided to try it. So first, he put all the pieces of the grill together and then carefully placed the food on the top. He watched as his mommy taught him how to light the grill and he watched the flames slowly rise. He was amazed by all the different flavours that came from the food. John was so proud of what he had accomplished. He had learnt something new and he couldn't wait to show his dad. Sure enough, when his dad came home and saw the food on the grill, he was beyond impressed. He congratulated his son for his creative cooking. John had a big smile on his face as he waved goodbye to his mommy and daddy. He had taught himself something new that day and he felt very proud of himself!

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a unique nest. A bird family lived in the nest. The mom bird, dad bird, and baby birds were very happy. One day, mom bird went to spread food for the baby birds. She flew far away to find yummy bugs. While she was gone, the baby birds played in the nest. But then, something unexpected happened! A big wind came and moved the nest. The baby birds were scared. They looked around and saw they were in a new tree! Mom bird came back and saw the nest was gone. She was worried. But then, she looked up and saw the nest in the new tree. She flew up and hugged her baby birds. From that day on, the bird family loved their new tree. They made new friends and had lots of fun. And their unique nest was safe and sound.
