Category: Intermediate
Title: The Crab and the Fish

One day, a little crab went to the sea to test if he could swim. He was a brilliant crab, but he never swam before. The other sea animals watched him, and they all wanted to see if he could do it. As the crab tried to swim, a big fish came and laughed at him. The fish said, "You can't swim, little crab! You are too small!" The crab felt sad and wanted to give up. But then, a kind turtle came and told the crab, "Don't listen to the fish. You can do it if you try hard!" The crab listened to the kind turtle and tried again. This time, he swam very well! All the sea animals clapped and cheered for him. The big fish felt bad for being mean and said sorry to the crab. The crab forgave him, and they all became friends. The moral of the story is to never give up, even when others don't believe in you. And always be kind to others, because everyone can do brilliant things when they try.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy lived in a cage. He was scared to fly outside. One day, a girl named Sue came to see Timmy. Sue said, "Hi Timmy, don't be scared. I will help you fly outside." Timmy liked Sue and felt a little less scared. Sue opened the cage and Timmy flew outside. He was so happy! Timmy said, "Thank you, Sue!" Then, Timmy gave Sue a little kiss on her cheek. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a famous driver. His car could reverse faster than anyone else's. One day, a little girl asked him, "Can I see your car reverse? Please!" The driver smiled and said, "Yes, of course!" So the driver drove in reverse fast, and the little girl couldn't believe her eyes. She clapped her hands with joy. The driver laughed and said, "That was fantastic!" The little girl said, "You're the most famous driver in the world". The driver felt really happy. The driver gave the little girl a big hug and said, "Thank you for noticing me." From then on, the driver was known to be the best driver in the world.

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Sam. He was very adventurous and loved trying new things. His mummy supported him, so every week they did something new together. One day, Sam and his mummy went to a farm. Sam saw chickens and horses, he even petted some rabbits! He was so excited. Then it was time to go inside and pick their dinner. Mummy asked Sam, "What are you going to choose tonight?" Sam grinned and said, "I want a steak please!" Mummy was surprised. She said, "That's adventurous! Are you sure you want a steak?" Sam beamed and said, "Yes, I'm sure!" So mummy got him a steak and they ate it together. Sam was over the moon and said, "Thank you for supporting me Mummy!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a regular boy who loved to play outside. One day, he found a jar with a zip. He opened the jar and found a magic spread inside. Tim went to his friend, Sue. "Look what I found!" he said. "It's a magic spread!" Sue looked at the jar and said, "Wow, let's try it!" They took the spread and put it on their hands. Suddenly, they could run very fast and jump very high. Tim and Sue played all day with their new magic. They ran races and jumped over big rocks. The magic spread made them very happy. At the end of the day, they put the jar with the zip back in its secret spot. They knew they could come back and play with the magic spread anytime they wanted. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw snow on the ground and was very happy. Tim loved to play in the snow. He put on his warm coat, hat, and gloves. Tim was grateful for his warm clothes because they kept him warm in the cold. As Tim played in the snow, he saw a little bird on a tree. The bird looked cold and hungry. Tim stared at the bird for a moment. He wanted to help the bird feel warm and full. Tim went inside his house and found some bread. He broke the bread into little pieces and took them outside. He put the bread on the ground for the bird to eat. The bird flew down and ate the bread. The bird was grateful for the food and sang a happy song. Tim smiled and knew he had made a new friend.
