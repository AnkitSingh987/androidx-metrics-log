Category: Intermediate
Title: Leo the Lion and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a big lion named Leo. He lived in a green forest with many trees. Leo had a long shadow that followed him everywhere he went. He was a very creative lion and liked to play with his friends. One day, Leo and his friends decided to play a game. They wanted to see who could make the loudest roar. Leo's friend, a little bird named Bella, said, "Let's all roar and see who is the loudest!" All the animals got ready to roar. First, Bella tried to roar, but her roar was very small. Then, a monkey named Max tried to roar, but his roar was also small. Finally, it was Leo's turn. He took a deep breath and roared as loud as he could. His roar was so loud that even his shadow seemed to shake! All his friends clapped and cheered. They all agreed that Leo had the loudest roar in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a weird cat. The cat was black. The cat wanted to succeed in catching a big red ball. The black cat saw a little girl. The little girl had the big red ball. The cat said, "Can I play with the red ball?" The little girl said, "Yes, you can play with the red ball." The black cat tried to catch the red ball, but the ball was too big. The cat tried and tried, but he could not succeed. The black cat was sad. The little girl was sad too. The black cat did not have fun with the big red ball.

Once there was a little boy. He wanted to cook something very special. He opened a rare envelope and inside he found some special ingredients. He put them in a pot and started to cook. When it was finished, it smelled so good! The little boy was very excited to taste it. But when he took the first bite, it was too hot and made him cry! He had cooked something too spicy and he learned his lesson. He knew not to cook something so rare again.

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy lived in a hole under a big tree. He was very glad to have a warm and cozy home. One day, Remy saw a small bird crying near his hole. The bird was sad because it lost its mom. Remy wanted to help and comfort the bird. He asked the bird to stay in his hole until they found its mom. Remy and the bird looked for the mom together. They asked other animals if they saw her. After a while, they found the mom. The bird was so happy! The mom said thank you to Remy for being kind. Remy was glad he could help and comfort his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a busy helicopter. It flew high in the sky. The helicopter liked to help people. It was always going up and down, here and there. One day, the busy helicopter saw a little boy. The boy was running after his ball. The ball was going to roll into the street. The helicopter wanted to help the boy. So, it flew down fast to stop the ball. The helicopter stopped the ball just in time. The boy was happy and said, "Thank you, busy helicopter!" The helicopter flew back up into the sky, happy to help. It went on to help more people and have more fun.

Once upon a time, in a nice little town, there was a boy named Will. Will liked to stand by the big tree in his yard. Every day, he would stand there and watch the birds fly by. One sunny day, Will saw a big, shiny thing in the tree. He wanted to see what it was. He tried to climb the tree, but he was too small. Will was sad. Then, a friendly bird flew down and said, "I can help you." The bird picked up the shiny thing and gave it to Will. It was a magic stone! The stone made Will grow bigger, and now he could climb the tree. Will was happy, and he and the bird became best friends.
