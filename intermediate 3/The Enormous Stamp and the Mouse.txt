Category: Intermediate
Title: The Enormous Stamp and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a enormous stamp. It was so big that it filled the entire sky. One day, a small mouse wanted to eat the enormous stamp. He went right up to it and took a big bite! But it was so big that the mouse could only eat a tiny bit of it. So he kept eating and eating, until he had eaten the whole enormous stamp. The next day when the mouse went back to the sky, he was so excited to see what was there. And he was astonished! Instead of the enormous stamp, he found that the sky was filled with colorful butterflies that fluttered around happily. The mouse shared the sky with them and had lots of fun. The end.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful land, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big shield that she carried with her wherever she went. She loved her shield because it made her feel safe. One day, Mia was playing with her friend, Tom. Tom looked at Mia's shield and asked, "Why do you always carry that shield? Don't you worry it's too big?" Mia smiled and said, "No, I don't worry. It keeps me safe." Suddenly, a big wind came and blew the shield up into a tree! Mia and Tom were scared. But then, a kind bird flew down and picked up the shield with its beak. The bird gave the shield back to Mia. Mia and Tom were happy and didn't worry anymore. They thanked the bird and played together with the shield, feeling safe and peaceful.

Once upon a time, there was a big and ancient fish. This fish lived in the deep blue ocean. The ancient fish had a very special job. It had to pay the little fish with shiny stones for their help. One day, the ancient fish told the little fish that something big was going to happen. The little fish were excited and wanted to help the ancient fish. They knew that if they helped, they would get more shiny stones. The next day, the ancient fish asked the little fish to help move a big rock. The little fish worked hard, and they moved the rock. Under the rock, there were many shiny stones. The ancient fish was happy and paid the little fish with the shiny stones they found. The little fish were very happy too, because they got many shiny stones for their help. The ancient fish's secret was now known, and the little fish knew that when the ancient fish said something big was going to happen, it meant that they would get many shiny stones.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy fork. He loved his fork very much. It was a rare fork, not like the ones his friends had. Tim played with his fork every day. One day, Tim saw the sun rise. It was big and bright. He had an idea. He wanted his fork to rise too. So, Tim tied a string to his fork and let it go. The fork went up, up, up into the sky. Tim was happy to see his fork rise high. It was a fun day for Tim and his rare fork. They played until the sun went down. And when it was time to go home, Tim pulled the string and his fork came back to him. They had more fun days together.

One day, a big melon and a small melon were friends. They lived in a garden. The big melon was slow, but the small melon was fast. They liked to play and talk. One day, they had a race. The small melon said, "I will zip and win!" The big melon was sad because he was slow. But he wanted to try. So, they started to race. The small melon zipped fast. The big melon went slow. The small melon was almost at the end. But then, he stopped. He looked back and saw his friend. The big melon was very slow and tired. The small melon went back to help. They finished the race together. They were both happy.
