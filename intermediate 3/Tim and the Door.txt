Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Door

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Spike. Spike was a small, round ball with spikes on it. Tim loved to play with Spike every day. One day, Tim was playing with Spike outside when it rolled into the neighbor's yard. Tim was very upset. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, Spike is in the neighbor's yard. Can you help me get it back?" His mom replied, "Tim, you should go to the neighbor's house and request them to give Spike back to you." So Tim went to the neighbor's house and knocked on the door. When the neighbor opened the door, Tim said, "Hello, my toy Spike is in your yard. Can I please have it back?" The neighbor smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Let me get it for you." The neighbor got Spike and gave it back to Tim. Tim was very happy to have Spike back. He thanked the neighbor and went home. Tim's mom was proud of him for being brave and asking for help. The moral of the story is that it's important to ask for help when you need it and always be polite.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a thin pillow that she loved very much. Every night, she would hug her pillow tight and go to sleep. One day, she lost her pillow and felt very sad. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, have you seen my pillow?" Her mom said, "No, but let's look for it together." They looked all over the house, but they could not find the pillow. Lily started to cry, thinking she would never find her pillow again. But, the next day, something unexpected happened. Lily found a big, fluffy pillow under her bed. She looked at her mom and asked, "Is this mine?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, your thin pillow was hiding inside this big pillow all along." Lily was so happy to have her pillow back, and now it was even better than before.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved cookies very much. One day, she saw a big cookie jar on the top shelf in the kitchen. She wanted to reach it, but she was too small. Lucy asked her big brother, Tom, for help. "Tom, can you help me reach the cookie jar?" Tom said, "Sure, Lucy!" He lifted her up, and she got the cookie jar. They were both very happy. When they opened the jar, they found not cookies, but a note. The note said, "Surprise! Go look in the fridge!" Lucy and Tom went to the fridge and found a big plate of cookies. They were so grateful and enjoyed the cookies together.

Once upon a time there were two friends: Max and Jill. They always liked to play together. One day, Max found a knife in his room. It was metal with a sharp point and he thought it was very nice. He showed it to Jill. "Wow!" said Jill. "Where did you get that from?" Max said, "From my room." Jill said, "Be careful, Max. It's sharp. You could hurt yourself." Max agreed and put the knife away. They went to the park to play. After a few minutes Jill got tired and said, "I quit. It's time to go home." Max said, "No, it's not time to quit. Come on, let's keep playing." Jill smiled and said, "Okay, but let's be careful." So they continued to play until it was time to go home. Max and Jill were nice friends who always looked out for each other and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim liked to follow his big sister, Sue, everywhere she went. They would play games and have fun together. One sunny day, they went to the park to play. Tim found a stick on the ground and said, "Look, Sue! I have a weapon!" Sue laughed and said, "That's a nice weapon, Tim. Let's have a pretend fight!" They played with their pretend weapons, laughing and running around the park. After a while, they got tired and sat down under a big tree. Tim looked at Sue and said, "I love following you, Sue. We always have fun!" Sue smiled and hugged her little brother. "I love having you follow me too, Tim," she said. And they continued to have many more fun adventures together.
