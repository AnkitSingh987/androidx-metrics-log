Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Blanket and the Mother

The little boy was feeling upset. He threw himself onto the floor and started to cry. His mother came in and saw him. She went to him and hugged him tightly. She whispered in his ear: "It's ok, my dear, it will be alright." The little boy was soothed by his mother's soft words and gentle embrace. He slowly stopped crying and looked up at her with calm eyes. His mother nodded and smiled softly. She took out a pill and told her son "Take this pill. It will help you feel better." The little boy shook his head but his mother said: "It's ok, it will make you feel calm again." The little boy was scared but he knew that his mother wouldn't let anything bad happen to him, so he took the pill and let his mother cover him with a warm, gentle blanket. He closed his eyes and soon fell asleep. The pill had worked and soon the little boy was feeling much calmer. He opened his eyes and looked up with a smile to his mother. She smiled back, giving him a proud look.

Once upon a time there were a brother and sister. They were walking through the park when they saw a heavy label lying on the grass. The brother said, ‘Let’s take it home!’ But the sister was scared. She said, ‘I don’t want to. It might rot.’ The brother said, ‘It’s ok, I’ll look after it.’ The sister was still scared, but the brother was very eager. So the sister agreed to take it home. The brother was very happy and he took very good care of the label. He made sure that it didn’t get wet or rot. But one day, the label did get wet and it started to rot. The brother was very sad and the sister was angry. She said, ‘See, I told you it would rot!’ The brother was very disappointed but he learned a valuable lesson. He realised that sometimes, it’s better to listen to the advice of others and not be too eager.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily liked to enjoy playing with her toys. She had a doll that she loved very much. One day, Lily found a bottle of perfume. She did not know what it was because she was still little and did not know many things. Lily thought the perfume was something to play with. She opened the bottle and the smell was very nice. She put some on her doll because she wanted her doll to smell nice too. Lily did not know that perfume was for people, not for dolls. Lily's mom came into the room and saw the perfume on the doll. She told Lily that perfume was for people to smell nice, not for dolls. Lily felt a little silly but now she knew what perfume was for. She learned something new that day and was happy to know more.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big tunnel made of wood and metal. They wanted to go inside and see what was there. "Come on, Ben, let's go in the tunnel!" Lily said. Ben was scared. He did not like the tunnel. It was dark and weird. He did not know what was inside. "No, Lily, I don't want to go in the tunnel. It is too scary. Let's play somewhere else." Ben said. Lily was angry. She did not like Ben's complain. She wanted to have fun and explore the tunnel. "Don't be a baby, Ben. It is not scary. It is just a tunnel. Maybe we will find something cool inside. Please, come with me." Lily said. Ben did not want to make Lily angry. He wanted to be a good friend. But he was still scared. He did not want to go in the tunnel. "OK, Lily, I will go with you. But only for a little bit. And you have to hold my hand." Ben said. Lily smiled. She was happy that Ben agreed to go with her. She held his hand and they walked into the tunnel. They saw some lights and some noises. They saw some people and some cars. They saw some signs and some pictures. They saw a lot of things in the tunnel. Lily and Ben were amazed. They did not know that the tunnel was so big and so busy. They forgot to be scared. They had fun and learned a lot in the tunnel.
