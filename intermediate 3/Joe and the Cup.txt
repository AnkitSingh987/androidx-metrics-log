Category: Intermediate
Title: Joe and the Cup

Joe was a wealthy man. Every morning, he went to his favourite coffee shop to enjoy a cup of coffee. One day, he was in a rush and didn't realise his coffee was going to drop. As Joe walked out of the café, the coffee dropped down and spilled on the ground. Joe was so sad and started to cry since he couldn't afford to buy another coffee. Joe was really upset and started to walk away. He never been back to the coffee shop again and lost his wealth.

One day, a big whale was swimming in the deep blue sea. He saw a little fish who was sad. The fish said, "I am envious of you, whale. You are so big and strong. I wish I could be like you." The whale thought for a moment and said, "I recommend you to be happy with who you are. Big or small, we all have something special in us." The little fish swam with the whale and they played together. They had so much fun. Soon, the little fish forgot about being envious. He was happy just being himself. The moral of the story is to be happy with who you are and not to be envious of others.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl called Jane. One day Jane was feeling a bit sad. Her mummy asked, "What's wrong Jane?". Jane said, "I'm tired, I don't know what to do". Her mummy smiled and said, "Maybe a snack can make you feel better!" Jane was happy and said, "Yes, snacks are my favourite!". Mummy opened the cupboard and said, "Welcome to our snack shop Jane! Choose anything you like". Jane was so excited and said, "I want cake!". Mummy smiled and gave her some cake. Jane was so happy and said, "Thank you mummy!". After eating her cake, Jane's mummy said, "Let's do something fun". Jane asked, "What can we do?". Mummy said, "We can have a picnic, it's going to be so much fun!". Jane said, "Let's go, I'm so excited!". Mummy packed some sandwiches and fruit. She made everything flexiable for Jane's little hands. Then they walked to the park. When they arrived, Mummy said, "Welcome to our picnic!". Jane smiled and said, "Yay, let's have fun!" And they did!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim wanted to help his mom in the kitchen. His mom asked him to complete a task. She gave him a teaspoon and asked him to put sugar in the cup. Tim was very happy to help. He put the teaspoon in the sugar jar. But he saw a toy on the floor. He wanted to play with the toy. So, he left the teaspoon in the sugar jar and went to play. Later, mom came back to the kitchen. She saw the teaspoon in the sugar jar. She was not happy. She called Tim and asked him why he did not complete the task. Tim was embarrassed. He knew he should have finished the task. But now, it was too late. Mom made the cake without sugar, and the cake did not taste good. Everyone was sad, and Tim learned to complete his tasks before playing.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a restaurant. A boy named Tim and his mom went to eat there. They were very tired from playing all day. At the restaurant, Tim and his mom sat down. Tim was thirsty. He said, "Mom, I want to drink water." His mom asked the waiter for water. The waiter brought them water to drink. Tim drank his water very fast. He wanted more. But the waiter was busy. Tim got up to get water by himself. He was still tired and not careful. He slipped and fell. The water spilled all over him. Tim cried, and his mom came to help him. They went home sad because Tim was wet and the day did not end well.

Once upon a time, there was a little lizard. He was dry and lived in a dry place. One day, he went for a walk to find water. On his walk, he met a big frog. The frog said, "I know where water is. Follow me." The little lizard was happy and followed the frog. They walked and walked until they found water. The lizard was excited and said, "Thank you, frog! I am not dry now." But then, the frog gave the lizard a surprise. He ate the little lizard. The lizard was gone, and the frog was not nice.
