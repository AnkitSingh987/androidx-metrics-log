Category: Intermediate
Title: John and David and the Cat

There were two brave boys: John and David. Both boys wanted to rescue someone. John said, "Let's find something to rescue." Then he saw an arrow pointing to the forest. David said, "Let's go there and see what we can rescue." John and David followed the arrow and soon they reached a clearing. In the clearing, they saw a little cat stuck in a tree. John said, "That cat needs to be rescued!" David said, "Let's get it!" They got a ladder and placed it against the tree. Then, David climbed up the ladder and rescued the cat. The cat was so happy it purred. John said, "That was a good rescue!" David nodded and said, "Yes, it was a very good rescue!" Then, they all smiled.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to her desk. It was a long desk with lots of toys on it. She wanted to draw a picture, but she did not know how. Lily's mom saw her and said, "Why don't you try to draw a big sun? It is easy and fun!" Lily thought it was a good idea. So, she took a yellow crayon and tried to draw a big sun. She drew a big circle and then added long lines all around it. Lily was very happy with her sun. She showed it to her mom, who smiled and said, "Good job, Lily! You did it!" Lily felt proud of her drawing. She put it on the fridge for everyone to see. Now, every time Lily looks at her long desk, she remembers how she tried and made a beautiful sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big hose in his yard. Tim liked to play with the hose and help his mom water the plants. He was an obedient boy who always listened to his mom. One sunny day, Tim picked up the hose to water the plants. But as he started to water, he accidentally dropped the hose. The water splashed everywhere, and Tim got all wet! Tim's mom saw what happened and started to laugh. She picked up the hose and joined Tim in the water fun. They both laughed and played with the hose, getting wet and having a great time. The unexpected splash turned into a fun surprise for both Tim and his mom.

Once upon a time, in a small open house, a girl named Lily lived with her mom. Lily liked to help her mom with house work. One day, mom asked Lily to gather some thread from the store. Lily was happy to help. Lily walked to the store and found many colors of thread. She picked up red, blue, and yellow thread. She also saw a toy she liked. But, she remembered mom said to only get the thread. So, she left the toy and went back home. Lily gave the thread to her mom. Mom was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lily!" They used the thread to sew pretty clothes. Lily felt good helping her mom, and they had a fun day together.

One day, a little crab named Tim went for a walk by the sea. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. Tim liked to play in the sand and feel the water on his feet. The tide came in and out, making the beach wet and then dry again. As Tim walked, he saw a big, wide shell. He thought it would be fun to play with. He picked it up and ran to the water. He wanted to see if it could float. Tim put the shell in the water and watched as the tide carried it away. He felt sad that his new toy was gone. Just then, a friendly fish named Sam swam by. He saw Tim's sad face and asked, "Why are you so sad, Tim?" Tim told Sam about the wide shell and how the tide took it away. Sam wanted to help Tim feel better, so he said, "Let's go find your shell together!" Tim and Sam swam through the water, looking for the wide shell. They paused to ask other sea creatures if they had seen it. Finally, they found the shell stuck in some seaweed. Tim was so happy to have his toy back. He thanked Sam for helping him. From that day on, Tim and Sam were the best of friends. They played together on the beach and in the sea, always watching the tide and having fun.
