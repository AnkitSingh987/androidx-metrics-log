Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Anna and the Slide

Tim and Anna are friends. They like to play in the park. They run, jump, slide and swing. They have fun. But Tim is thirsty. He wants to drink some water. He sees a fountain near the slide. He runs to the fountain and drinks. Ahh, that is good. Anna is thirsty too. She follows Tim to the fountain. She waits for her turn. She is polite. She says, "Tim, can I drink some water too, please?" Tim says, "Sure, Anna. Here you go." He moves away from the fountain and lets Anna drink. Anna says, "Thank you, Tim. You are nice." But then Tim feels something. He needs to go to the toilet. He says, "Anna, I need to go to the toilet. Where is the toilet?" Anna says, "The toilet is over there, near the trees. You can see the sign. It says 'toilet'. Hurry, Tim. Don't wet your pants." Tim says, "Okay, Anna. I will be back soon." He runs to the toilet. He hopes it is not too late. Anna waits for Tim. She plays with a ball. She kicks it and catches it. She is happy. Tim comes back from the toilet. He is happy too. He says, "Anna, I made it. I did not wet my pants. I am proud." Anna says, "Good job, Tim. I am proud of you too. Do you want to play some more?" Tim says, "Yes, Anna. Let's play some more. You are a good friend." They hug and smile. They play some more. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big pastel box. It had many colors. A little girl named Lucy loved to take the pastels and draw. She made perfect pictures. She drew the sun, trees, and flowers. Lucy was very happy. One day, Lucy took her pastels outside to draw. She started to draw a big tree. But something unexpected happened. The wind blew very hard. The wind took her pastels away. Lucy was very sad. Lucy looked for her pastels. She saw a small dog. The dog had her pastels. The dog was drawing a perfect picture too. Lucy and the dog became friends. They drew perfect pictures together. Now, Lucy was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a clown named Bobo. Bobo was not very smart, but he was always happy and liked to make others laugh. One day, he wanted to measure how tall he was. He found a big stick and tried to measure himself. But, he was too silly and did it wrong. Bobo was sad because he thought he was very short. He didn't want to be a short clown. A little girl saw Bobo and wanted to help him. She told him, "You are not short, you are just measuring wrong. Let me help you." The little girl showed Bobo how to measure right. She put the stick next to him and marked how tall he was. Now, Bobo knew he was not short, and he was very happy. He thanked the little girl and gave her a big hug. From that day on, Bobo learned that it was okay to ask for help when he didn't know how to do something. The little girl and Bobo became good friends, and they always helped each other when they needed it. The moral of the story is that it's okay to ask for help, and we can learn from our mistakes.

One day, there was an alert butterfly flying through the sky. He zigzagged from place to place looking for something. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise. He looked down and saw a big, scary machine. He wanted to turn around, but it was too late. The machine was zooming closer and closer. He tried to fly away, but the machine was too fast. The butterfly got scared and started to cry. In the end, the machine gobbled up the butterfly and he was never seen again.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play and pretend. One day, he found a fork in the kitchen. Tim went to his friend, Sam. He said, "Let's pretend this fork is a magic wand!" Sam was excited and they started to play. They waved the fork and pretended to do magic. But then, Tim tripped and fell. The fork poked his arm and it hurt. Tim cried and Sam felt sad. They learned to be careful when playing with forks. The end.
