Category: Intermediate
Title: The Event and the Amy

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there was a big event. Everyone was excited. They all wanted to go to the event. A little girl named Amy asked her mom, "Can we go to the event, Mom?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can go, Amy. Let's get ready." At the event, there were lots of games and food. Amy met a boy named Tim. They played games together and had so much fun. They became good friends. Amy and Tim got tired. They sat down and ate yummy ice cream. They talked and laughed. They were very happy. At the end of the day, Amy and Tim said goodbye. They promised to play together again soon. The event was a fun day for everyone in the busy little town.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. She had an original toy. It was a big ball with a funny head on top. Amy loved to play with her toy every day. One day, Amy took her toy to the park. She played with it under the big tree. She threw the ball high in the sky and the head would laugh. Amy laughed too. Then, a boy named Tim saw Amy play. He asked, "Can I play with you?" Amy said yes. They played together and had so much fun. From that day on, Amy and Tim were best friends. And they always played with the original toy with the funny head.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who knew that he should always care. One day, his mommy told him to go find a film. He searched all around, but he couldn’t find a film. He was getting a bit dizzy from all the running. Suddenly, he saw it! There was a film in a nearby tree. The little boy carefully climbed up the tree and grabbed the film. He was so happy. He said “Mommy, I found the film!” And his mother said, “That’s great! I’m glad you cared enough to search for the film.” The little boy was so proud of himself. From that day on, he always remembered to care.

Once upon a time, there was a big, modern house. In this house, there was a girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to play with her toys and scatter them all around her room. One day, she found a small pin on the floor. She picked it up and looked at it. Lucy thought the pin was pretty, so she put it in her pocket. Later, her mom came into her room and saw the mess. She asked Lucy to clean up the toys. Lucy began to pick up her toys, but she forgot about the pin in her pocket. When Lucy's mom saw the pin, she told Lucy that it was not safe to have it in her pocket. Lucy learned that she should not only clean up her toys but also be careful with things that can hurt her. The moral of this story is to always be careful and clean up after playing.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big sack. One cold day, he wanted to move the sack to his friend's house. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, can I move my sack to my friend's house?" His mom said, "Yes, but wear your warm coat. It's very cold outside." So, Tim put on his warm coat and started to move his big sack. He walked and walked until he reached his friend's house. His friend was happy to see Tim and the big sack. Together, they opened the sack and found many toys inside. Tim and his friend played with the toys all day long. They had so much fun and forgot about the cold day outside. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "I'm glad I moved my sack to your house. We had a fun day!" His friend agreed and they promised to play together again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a creative dog who liked to joke. One day, Spot saw a cat and wanted to play. Spot went up to the cat and said, "Do you want to hear a joke?" The cat said, "Yes, I like jokes." Spot told the cat a funny joke and they both laughed. They played and joked all day. At the end of the day, Spot's owner came to find him. The owner was happy to see Spot and the cat playing. Spot told his owner the joke, and they all laughed together. And from that day on, Spot, the cat, and the owner were the best of friends.
