Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Ladder

Once upon a time, in a big country, there lived a happy girl named Lily. One day, Lily wanted to decorate her room. She loved to make things pretty, and she was very excited. Lily started to hang up pretty pictures on her walls. She used a big ladder to reach high up. But then, something unexpected happened! The ladder shook and Lily fell down. She felt hurt, and she cried. Lily's mom heard her cry and came to help. She hugged Lily and made her feel better. Then, Lily's mom had a fun idea. They could decorate the room together! They worked side by side, and soon, Lily's room was the prettiest in the country.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird named Tim. Tim loved to fly and sing in the big tree near his house. One sunny day, Tim saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sitting in her garden and having a tea party with her dolls. Tim was curious and wanted to join the party. But he had a question. "Can I join your tea party?" asked Tim. Sue smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! You can be my special guest." Tim was very happy and flew down to sit with Sue and her dolls. As they were having tea, a big wind came and blew Sue's favorite doll away. Sue was very sad and started to cry. Tim wanted to help, so he flew high and fast to catch the doll. He brought it back to Sue, who was now very happy. They continued their tea party, laughing and having fun. The moral of the story is that if you help others, you will make friends and be happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house near the sea. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. One day, a big storm came to the town. It was a hurricane! The wind blew strong and the rain fell hard. Tim's mom told him to stay inside the house. She set a table with toys and games for him to play. Tim played with the toys, but he wanted to see the hurricane. He went to the window and saw the trees dance in the wind. The sea was angry, with big waves crashing on the shore. Tim's mom saw the door was open. She closed it fast, so the rain and wind could not come in. Tim learned that hurricanes are strong and scary. He knew it was better to stay inside and play when a hurricane comes. Tim was safe and happy with his mom, and they played games until the storm went away.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little mill. The mill was near a big tree. The tree had small, pretty flowers that would bloom in the sun. The mill and the tree were friends. One day, the mill saw the tree's flowers were not blooming. The tree was sad. The mill wanted to help the tree. The mill thought and thought. It had an idea! The mill started to spin fast to make a strong wind. The wind blew the clouds away. The sun came out and shined on the tree. The small flowers started to bloom again. The tree was happy and thanked the mill. The mill and the tree were friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big red bus. The bus was very noisy. One day, the bus went on a trip to the park. Many kids and their moms and dads got on the bus. They were all very excited. On the bus, there was a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue. Tim had a big bag of yummy snacks. Sue looked at the snacks and said, "Can I have some, please?" Tim thought for a moment and decided to share his snacks with Sue. They both smiled and enjoyed the tasty treats together. At the park, Tim and Sue played and had lots of fun. They shared their toys and made new friends. The other kids saw how happy Tim and Sue were, and they started to share too. Everyone had a great time on the noisy bus trip to the park. When it was time to go home, all the kids and their moms and dads got back on the big red bus. They talked about the fun day and how sharing made it even better. The noisy bus took them all home, and they couldn't wait for their next trip. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can make everyone happy and create new friendships.
