Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Mighty Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to race with his friends. They would run in the park and have lots of fun. Max always wanted to include everyone in the race. One sunny day, Max and his friends were racing in the park. A small cat named Lily saw them and wanted to join. Max said, "Yes, you can be in the race too!" Lily was very happy. But during the race, Max and Lily both wanted to win. They ran faster and faster. Max and Lily did not look where they were going. They both fell into a big hole. They could not get out. Max and Lily were sad. They learned that winning was not the most important thing.

One day, a girl and her mom went to the store. The store was big and modern. They wanted to buy a toy for the girl. They looked at many toys, but the girl could not pick one. The girl saw a toy that seemed fun. It was a small car. The mom said, "We can buy this car." The girl was happy. They went to pay for the car. But when they opened the car box at home, they found a big surprise! Inside the box was not a car, but a pretty doll! The girl was very happy with her new doll. The mom and the girl laughed and played with the doll all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a suit that he loved very much. One day, he put on his suit and went to play outside. While playing, Tom saw a small, frightened bird on the ground. The bird was scared and could not fly. Tom wanted to care for the bird, so he talked to it. "Don't be scared, little bird. I will help you," said Tom. Tom took the bird to his mom. She showed him how to care for the bird. They gave it food and water. Soon, the bird was happy and strong. Tom and the bird became friends. They played together every day, and Tom always wore his suit.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He felt miserable because he was hungry. He saw a small house and knocked on the door. An old lady opened the door and said, "Hello, what do you want?" Tim said, "I am hungry, can I have some food?" The old lady gave him a plate of toast. Tim started to eat the toast, but something unexpected happened. The toast began to talk! The toast said, "Please don't eat me! I can help you feel better if you let me go." Tim was surprised but agreed. He put the talking toast back on the plate. The toast jumped up and down and then turned into a big, tasty cake. Tim was not miserable anymore. He thanked the old lady and the magic toast, and they all enjoyed the cake together.

"Ah, what a beautiful day," said John as he walked through the woods. The sun was shining and there was a light breeze blowing. Suddenly, John noticed a deer enter the clearing. He stopped to take a closer look. John watched in amazement as the deer frolicked around in the clearing. But then he noticed something strange. The deer had a smelly odor coming from it. John wrinkled his nose in disgust. John laughed and said, "Well, deer don't usually smell like that. What could have caused it?" He tried to get closer, but the deer ran away. John shrugged and continued on his way. As he walked away, he smiled and said, "That was definitely an interesting sighting."

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with a big family. In the living room, there was a warm fireplace. Spot liked to lay by the fireplace to keep cozy. One day, Spot found a light green ball near the fireplace. He picked it up with his mouth and started to play with it. He would toss it in the air and then bite it. Spot was having so much fun! Spot's little friend, Tim, came to play too. Tim said, "Spot, can I play with the ball too?" Spot wagged his tail and let Tim have a turn. They took turns throwing and biting the ball. They had a great time playing together by the fireplace.
