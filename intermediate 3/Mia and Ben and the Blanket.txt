Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia and Ben and the Blanket

Mia and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. Mia found a lime under a bush. She picked it up and showed it to Ben. "Look, Ben, a lime!" she said. "It is green and round and sour." Ben looked at the lime and made a face. He did not like sour things. He liked sweet things, like candy and cake. "Can I have a bite?" he asked. Mia nodded and gave him the lime. Ben took a small bite and spit it out. He did not like the lime at all. He made a funny noise and rubbed his tongue. "Yuck, yuck, yuck!" he said. "The lime is too sour! It hurts my mouth!" Mia laughed and took the lime back. She liked sour things. She took a big bite and smiled. "Yum, yum, yum!" she said. "The lime is so good! It makes my mouth happy!" Ben looked at Mia and yawned. He was tired from playing. He wanted to take a nap. "Mia, can we go inside?" he asked. "I am sleepy and thirsty. I want some water and a blanket." Mia looked at Ben and yawned too. She was also tired from playing. She wanted to go inside too. "Okay, Ben, let's go inside," she said. "But you are very brave for trying the lime. I am proud of you." They held hands and walked inside. They drank some water and cuddled with a blanket. They fell asleep and dreamed of limes and bugs.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he heard a sound coming from deep in the woods. He wanted to find out what it was. Tim walked into the woods and saw a small bird. The bird was sad because it could not fly. Tim made a promise to help the bird. He looked for a way to make the bird happy again. Tim found a big leaf and put the bird on it. He pushed the leaf and the bird started to fly. The bird was so happy and thanked Tim for his help. Tim felt good because he kept his promise. They both went back home and played together all day.

Ben and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig holes and make mud pies. They did not care if they got dirty. They had fun. "Look, I found a shiny thing!" Lily said. She held up a small metal box. It had a button and a light. The light was red and it blinked. "What is it?" Ben asked. He wanted to see. "I don't know. Maybe it has sugar inside. Let's open it!" Lily said. She pushed the button. The box made a loud noise. It was not sugar. It was a bomb. The bomb exploded. Ben and Lily were gone. The garden was on fire. The light stopped blinking.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to joke and laugh with her friends. One day, Lily found a magic wand in her toy box. She was very excited because she never saw it before. Lily wanted to show her friends the magic wand. She thought it looked expensive. They all gathered around and Lily waved the wand. Suddenly, a big, funny clown appeared! The clown made them all laugh with his jokes. Lily and her friends had so much fun with the clown. But then, something unexpected happened. When Lily waved the magic wand again, the clown turned into a cute little bunny! Everyone was so surprised. They laughed even more at the twist. Lily and her friends played with the bunny and continued to have a great day.

Once upon a time, there were two good friends named Tony and Sophie. Tony was very jealous of Sophie's beautiful birdcage. Every day, he asked if he could borrow it, but Sophie would always say no because it was very special to her. One night, Tony got a funny idea. He quietly sneaked into Sophie's house and stole the birdcage! Then, he quickly ran out of the house so nobody would catch him. The next morning, Sophie quickly noticed that her birdcage was missing. She was very sad. "Who stole my birdcage?" she asked Tony, who was looking very sneaky. Tony didn't say anything. He just stared at the floor, looking guilty. Sophie knew who took it. She was very angry and said, "You shouldn't be so jealous, Tony. You can't take something just because you want it." Tony knew she was right and he said sorry, then he quickly ran away.
