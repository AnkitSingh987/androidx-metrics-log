Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Seed

Once upon a time, a little seed lay in the soil. The seed was far away from other seeds. One day, the seed woke up and wanted to grow big and tall. But it was not easy because the seed was all alone. One sunny day, a big bird came by. The bird saw the seed and wanted to help. The bird dug a hole in the soil and put the seed in it. The seed was so happy that it started to grow. It grew and grew, and soon it became a big and strong plant. But then, something unexpected happened! The wind blew very hard, and it carried the plant far away to a new place. The plant was scared at first, but it soon saw many other plants around. The plant was not alone anymore. It made new friends and they all grew big and strong together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to wear a big red hat. One day, Spot met a little girl named Sue. Sue liked Spot's hat and wanted to play with him. "Hi Spot!" said Sue. "I like your hat. Can we play together?" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, let's play!" They played all day in the park. They ran, jumped, and laughed. It was a happy memory for both of them. At the end of the day, Sue gave Spot a hug and said, "Goodbye, Spot. I will remember our fun day together." And from that day on, Spot and Sue were the best of friends. They always had fun playing together, and Spot always wore his big red hat.

Ben liked to poke things. He poked his toys, his food, his dog, and sometimes his mom and dad. He liked to see what would happen when he poked. He poked hard and soft, big and small, loud and quiet things. One day, Ben went with his dad to the barber. The barber had a big chair, a sharp tool, and a funny cape. Ben was curious. He wanted to poke the barber. He waited until his dad was busy talking to the barber. Then he got up from his seat and walked to the barber. He reached out his hand and poked the barber's nose. The barber was surprised. He made a loud noise and dropped his tool. His dad and the other people in the shop looked at Ben. They were not happy. They said, "No, Ben, no! You don't poke the barber!" Ben felt bad. He did not mean to make the barber angry. He said, "Sorry, barber, sorry." The barber was kind. He smiled and said, "It's okay, Ben, but please don't poke me again. It hurts and it's dangerous. You can poke something else, like this." He gave Ben a soft toy to poke. Ben liked the toy. He said, "Thank you, barber, thank you." Ben learned a lesson. He learned that some things are not good to poke. He learned that the barber was unusual. He was not like his toys, his food, his dog, or his mom and dad. He was a person who had feelings and who could help him look nice. Ben decided to be nice to the barber too. He did not poke him anymore. He sat still and let him cut his hair. He said, "Bye, barber, bye." The barber said, "Bye, Ben, bye. See you next time."

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Pete. He was a very happy boy, who always had a smile on his face. One day, Pete asked his mom to invite his best friend over for a play date. His mom said yes, and Pete was so excited! He quickly ran to the living room and found a beautiful vase. He decided to use it to decorate the kitchen for his friend. He was very careful with it, and put it gently on the table in the middle of the room. When his friend, Tom, arrived, he was so impressed by how fast Pete had decorated the kitchen. Pete and Tom had a wonderful time playing, and ended with a hug goodbye. The lesson Pete learnt was that a little effort can go a long way - especially when you invite a friend over!
