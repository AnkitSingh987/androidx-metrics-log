Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Soap

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a very interesting toy. It was a soap. Tim liked to play with soap and imagine it could talk. One day, Tim was playing with his soap. He imagined the soap said, "Hi Tim! Do you want to play with me?" Tim said, "Yes, I want to play with you, soap!" They played together all day. But then, Tim got very tired. He wanted to take a nap. He put the soap on the table and went to sleep. When he woke up, the soap was gone. Tim was very sad. He looked everywhere but could not find his interesting soap. The soap was lost and Tim could not imagine and play with it anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful crane. The crane had many friends. They all liked to play together. One day, the crane and his friends played a game. They kicked a ball. The crane was very good at it. He kicked the ball high in the sky. The friends laughed and clapped. They had so much fun. The colorful crane was happy. They all played until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to collect shiny things. One day, he found a shiny faucet in his house. The faucet was very special and expensive. Tim wanted to show the faucet to his best friend, Lily. So, he picked it up and put it in his bag. He walked to Lily's house, excited to share his new treasure. When Tim showed the faucet to Lily, she was very happy. They played with the faucet all day long. In the end, Tim put the expensive faucet back in his house, and they promised to find more shiny things to collect together.

One day, a little girl and her mom went to the zoo. The little girl was so excited! She wondered what it would be like. At the zoo, the little girl saw all kinds of animals. She saw monkeys, giraffes, and even a big elephant! She wondered what the animals were like. The little girl also saw a rich man in the zoo. He was wearing a fancy outfit and had a fancy hat. She wondered what he was doing at the zoo. The little girl asked her mom, "Is he here to feed the animals?" Her mom laughed and said, "No, he's here to look at them. He's very rich, so he can afford to go to the zoo whenever he wants." The little girl was amazed! She wondered why the man was so rich.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lot of toys and equipment to play with. He loved to run and jump and have fun with his toys. One day, Tim was playing in his room. He saw his favorite toy, a big red ball. He wanted to kick the ball and make it fly high in the sky. But when Tim tried to kick the ball, he didn't see a toy car on the floor. Tim's foot went on the toy car and he started to slip. He tried to catch himself, but he fell down on the soft carpet. Tim felt a little sad and scared, but he was not hurt. Tim's mom came into the room and saw what happened. She picked up the toy car and gave Tim a big hug. She told him to be careful and watch where he steps. Tim learned to look before he kicks and to always put his equipment away when he is done playing.

Once upon a time, there was a humble daddy named Sam. He lived in a small house with his little girl, Lily. They were very happy together. One day, Sam and Lily went to the park to play. At the park, Sam and Lily saw a big tree. Sam said, "Let's try to stretch and touch the tree's leaves!" Lily was excited and tried to stretch her arms high, but she couldn't reach the leaves. Sam laughed and said, "Let me try!" He started to stretch his arms up to the sky. As Sam was stretching, a big wind came and blew him away. Lily cried and said, "Come back, daddy!" But Sam was too far away. He got stuck in the tree, and he couldn't get down. Lily was very sad that her daddy was gone.
