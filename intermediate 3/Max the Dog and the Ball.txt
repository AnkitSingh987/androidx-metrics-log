Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to play outside. One day, Max saw something different. It was a small, red ball. Max was very happy and wanted to play with the ball. Max played with the ball all day. But then, the ball rolled away and Max could not find it. Max started to worry. He looked and looked for the ball, but it was gone. Max began a big search to find his new toy. Max looked behind a tree, under a bush, and in a hole. Finally, Max found the ball near a big rock. He was so happy to have his ball back. Max played with the ball all day and never let it out of his sight. And Max never worried again.

One day, a girl named Sue and her dog, Spot, found a big stack of boxes. Sue said, "Wow, Spot! Look at this incredible stack!" They wanted to send the boxes to their friends. Sue and Spot put the boxes on a big truck. As they drove to their friends' house, they heard a funny sound. "What's that, Spot?" Sue asked. Spot barked and wagged his tail. When they got to their friends' house, they opened the boxes. Instead of toys, they found balloons! The balloons flew up into the sky. Sue, Spot, and their friends laughed and clapped. They had a fun day playing with the balloons.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to pour juice for her mom. She went to the kitchen and saw a big jug with a knob. Sue was an obedient girl, so she asked her mom if she could pour the juice. Her mom said, "Yes, you can pour the juice, Sue." Sue was happy and grabbed the knob. She started to pour juice into a cup. But then, something unexpected happened! The juice turned into a rainbow! Sue and her mom were so surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. They knew they would never forget the day the juice turned into a rainbow.

Once there was a perfect butterfly. He loved flying through the sky and finding new flowers to explore. One day, he flew up to a window to see what was inside. He was so excited when he saw a jar of butter inside the room! He wanted to use it, so he flew in. When he reached the jar, he tried to open it but he couldn't. No matter how hard he tried, it wouldn't open! He was very sad and scared. Suddenly, the door opened and a big person came in. The butterfly was so frightened that he flew out as fast as he could. He never went back, and he never got to use the butter. The end.

Once upon a time there was a child who wanted to learn all sorts of fun things. On Tuesday, the child was feeling very flexible. He wanted to try something new. So he asked his mom if he could learn how to ride a bike. His mom said, “Yes! That would be a great idea.” The child was so excited. He got his bicycle and wheeled it over to the park. When he got there, he found his dad waiting for him. His dad showed him how to balance on the bike and how to pedal. He also taught him how to use the brakes. The child practiced and practiced, until finally he could ride his bike all by himself! He felt so proud and happy. He had learned a new skill! The child kept practicing, and soon he was riding his bike around the park with ease. He had become very flexible, and learning how to ride a bike was one of the best Tuesdays he had ever had!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big rain. The rain made the town very wet and filthy. The people in the town did not like the filthy rain. They wanted the rain to stop. One day, a wise man came to the town. The wise man said he could order the rain to stop. The people were very happy. They asked the wise man to order the rain to stop right away. The wise man said he would, but he needed something first. He asked the people to give him all their toys. The people did not want to give away their toys, but they wanted the rain to stop. So, they gave the wise man all their toys. The wise man then ordered the rain to stop. The rain stopped, but the town was still filthy. The people were sad. They had no toys and the town was still filthy. The wise man left with all their toys. The moral of the story is to not give away what you love for something that may not make you happy.
