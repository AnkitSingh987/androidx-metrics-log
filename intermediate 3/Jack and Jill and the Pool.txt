Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and Jill and the Pool

One day, Jack and Jill went out to the pool. They were very excited and giggling in anticipation. Jack and Jill had big swimming plans. When they arrived at the pool, Jack wanted to jump in first. But Jill wanted to jump in first too! They ended up quarreling about it and feeling sad. Then Jack got a brilliant idea. He said, "Let's jump in together! We can both jump in the same time." Jill happily agreed. But when they both got to the edge of the pool, Jack was worried. He paused for a moment and looked a little bit scared. Jill was a bit worried too, but she grabbed Jack's hand, and together they jumped into the pool. They both screamed with joy as they jumped in!

Once upon a time, there was a little guy called Tom. Tom and his Mum went to the beach. It was hot, and Tom wanted to play in the sand. Tom remembered seeing a pebble under the hot sand. He scooped away some sand with his hands, and there was the pebble! Tom was so happy and put the pebble in his pocket. When Tom and his Mum got home, he took out the pebble and he remembered all the fun he had. Every night, Tom put the pebble next to his bed. He liked the way it sparkled in the light. One day, the pebble was gone. Tom had left it at the beach! Even though he was sad, he knew he had to go back and find it. Tom and his Mum went to the beach and looked for the pebble. After searching and searching, they found it! Tom was so happy and put the pebble in his pocket. He remembered all the fun they had, even though it was hot. Tom held the pebble tight, and vowed never to forget where it was again.

Mum and Dad were going on a picnic. They had a basket full of food to bring. They put the basket into the car and drove off. "Look up," said Dad. Mum and Dad looked up and saw a big white fluffy cloud in the sky. "That's a beautiful cloud," said Mum. "Let's take a picture," said Dad. So they stopped the car and took a picture of the cloud. The sky was so blue and the cloud was so white. When they arrived at the park, it was very lively. There were other families playing and a lot of birds singing. They spread their blanket and enjoyed the sunny day. Mum and Dad spent the day relaxing and eating their picnic. When it was time to go home, they looked up at the sky again and saw the same white fluffy cloud they had taken a picture of earlier. "I've had a lovely day," said Mum. "Me too," replied Dad.

Mommy was very excited -- she had something to show her little 3 year old. She carefully took out a bucket and then took out something from the bucket. "What is that?" asked the little girl. Mommy explained, "It's a very special creature. It's called a tide." The little 3 year old was so curious! She wanted to meet the tide. But when she tried to reach out and touch it, the tide seemed very shy. It quickly swam away. Mommy could see her little girl was sad that the tide had left so quickly. She reached out and hugged her daughter. "Don't worry," she said. "I'm sure the tide will come back soon. We just have to be patient and wait." The little girl smiled and nodded. She knew her mommy was right and she trusted her. In no time, the tide came back and the little girl was able to finally meet it.

Once upon a time, there was a big box in a room. A little girl named Amy found it. "What is in the box?" she asked her mom. Her mom said, "It is a surprise, but you must be safe when you open it." Amy was so excited. She opened the box, and a small dog jumped out. The dog was very happy to see Amy. It wagged its tail and gave her a small bow. "Wow!" said Amy. "A new friend!" Amy and the dog played all day. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog started to grow bigger and bigger! It was not a dog, but a big horse! Amy was surprised, but she knew she could still be safe and have fun with her new big friend.
