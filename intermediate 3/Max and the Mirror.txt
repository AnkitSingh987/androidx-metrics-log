Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Mirror

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Max. Max liked to play and run all day. He had lots of fun with his friends. One day, Max found a mirror. He looked into the mirror and saw another dog! Max was very surprised. He barked at the dog in the mirror, but the dog barked back. Max called his friends to see the mirror. They all looked at the mirror and saw themselves too. They were all surprised and laughed. They played with the mirror all day and had lots of fun.

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the kitchen. He liked to pretend he was a cook. One day, he found a magic pot in the kitchen. The pot could make any food he wanted. Tim wished for lots of food. He wanted to become rich. So, he shared the food with his friends. They were all very happy. They played and ate yummy food all day long. Soon, people heard about Tim and his magic pot. They came to see it in the kitchen. Tim shared his food with everyone. He became rich with love and friends. Tim and his friends were happy and full of joy.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy was nervous because he did not trust his wings. He was scared to fly high in the sky. One day, his mom told him a story about a beautiful rainbow that would make him brave. The next day, Timmy saw a big rainbow in the sky. He remembered his mom's story and thought that maybe the rainbow could help him trust his wings. The rainbow was very far away, but Timmy knew he had to try. He took a deep breath and started to fly. As Timmy got closer to the rainbow, he felt less nervous. He was flying higher and higher! When he reached the rainbow, he felt so brave. Now, Timmy trusted his wings and was not scared anymore. He knew that he could always remember the rainbow when he needed to be brave.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She had a big sheet that she liked to keep on her bed. Mia liked to play with the sheet and make it into a cape, a dress, or even a tent. She was having so much fun playing with the sheet. Mia's mom called from the kitchen, "Mia, can you come here and help me, please?" Mia put on her sheet like a cape and ran slow to her mom. She didn't want to trip and fall. When she got to the kitchen, her mom asked her to help set the table for lunch. As Mia was setting the table, she heard a noise coming from her room. "What was that?" she thought. She went back to her room to see what was going on. When she opened the door, she saw her sheet moving all by itself! "Hi Mia!" said the sheet. Mia was very surprised. She didn't know her sheet could talk! The sheet told her that it was a magic sheet and could do many fun things. Mia and the magic sheet became the best of friends and played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He was very enthusiastic and happy. He loved to see the trees, the flowers, and the animals. As he walked, he saw a big cobweb on a tree. He thought it was very pretty. Tim wanted to show his friend, the squirrel, the cobweb. He called out, "Squirrel, come see this pretty cobweb!" The squirrel came running and looked at the cobweb. "Wow!" said the squirrel. "That is a big cobweb!" They both admired the cobweb for a while. Then, Tim decided to push a stick through the cobweb to see what would happen. The cobweb broke and fell to the ground. Tim and the squirrel were sad to see the pretty cobweb go, but they knew that the spider would make a new one soon. They continued to walk through the woods, looking for more pretty things to see.
