Category: Intermediate
Title: Hoppy the Bunny and the Secret

Once upon a time, in an ancient forest, there was a little bunny named Hoppy. Hoppy loved to hop all day. One day, Hoppy found a big stamp on the ground. He was very curious about it. He thought, "I will hop on the stamp and see what happens!" Hoppy hopped on the stamp, but nothing happened. He was sad, but he kept hopping. He hopped all day and all night. He hopped so much that he got very tired. He thought, "I will try the stamp one more time before I go to sleep." Hoppy went back to the stamp and hopped on it again. This time, something amazing happened! The stamp began to glow and shake. Hoppy was very surprised and happy. The stamp made a big sound and opened a secret door in the forest. Hoppy hopped into the secret door and found a beautiful garden full of yummy food and new friends. Hoppy played with his new friends and ate yummy food. He was so happy that he found the stamp. From that day on, Hoppy and his friends would always hop on the stamp and have fun in the secret garden. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl who lived in a big tree. One day, a small mouse came to the wise owl for help. The mouse said, "Mr. Owl, I trust you. Can you help me find my way home?" The wise owl said, "Of course, little mouse. I can help you. First, we must go past the big gas cloud. It is smelly, but we can do it together." The mouse and the wise owl walked past the gas cloud. The mouse held his nose so he would not smell the bad gas. After they passed the gas cloud, the wise owl showed the little mouse his home. The mouse was so happy to see his family again. He thanked the wise owl for helping him. From that day on, the mouse and the wise owl became the best of friends.

Once there was a little boy who was eager to go jogging. He put on his favourite shoes, grabbed his favourite hat and ran out the door. His mom saw him and asked, "Where are you going, sweetheart?". The little boy replied, "I'm going jogging, mommy!". His mom said, "It's such a nice day outside, why don't you take a root while jogging around?". So the little boy eagerly ran outside to take a jog with a root in his hand. He ran around, playing with his root, until he got to an apple tree. He stopped to rest and looked up at the leaves and branches. After a few minutes his mom called out, "Are you ready to come back home? The little boy said yes and jogged back home. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Tim. Tim loved to jump all day long. One day, Tim saw a big red apple in a tree. The apple looked delicious. Tim wanted to eat it. Tim asked his friend, Cat, "Can you help me get the apple?" Cat said, "Yes, I can climb the tree and get it for you." Tim was very happy. Cat climbed the tree and got the apple. Tim jumped with joy. Tim took a big bite of the delicious apple. But, oh no! The apple was not good inside. It was rotten and yucky. Tim felt sick. He learned that not all things that look good are good for you.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play outside. One day, it started to rain. Amy wanted to go out and play, but she needed to dress in her rain clothes. Amy put on her smart red raincoat and her matching boots. She looked in the mirror and said, "I look good in my rain clothes!" She grabbed her umbrella and went outside. Amy splashed in the puddles and sang, "Rain, rain, go away, come again another day." She saw her friend Tom, who was also dressed for the rain. "Hi, Tom!" said Amy. "Let's play in the rain together!" So Amy and Tom played in the rain all day, and they had lots of fun.
