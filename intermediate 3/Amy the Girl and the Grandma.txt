Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Grandma

One day, a little girl named Amy went to her grandma's house. Grandma was making a tasty pie in the kitchen. Amy wanted to help, so she went to the kitchen and knelt down next to her grandma. "Can I help you, Grandma?" asked Amy. "Yes, dear. You can help me sew the cloth for the pie," said Grandma. Grandma gave Amy a needle and some thread. Amy tried her best to sew the cloth, but it was hard for her small hands. After a while, the pie was ready. Amy and her grandma sat at the table and ate the tasty pie together. They were very happy and enjoyed their time. And Amy learned that helping others can be fun and rewarding.

One day, a pink fish went to wash in the sea. The fish loved to play in the water. He liked to jump and swim with his friends. As the pink fish was washing, he saw a big wave coming. The wave was so big, it scared the pink fish. He swam fast to hide behind a rock. When the big wave came, it did something unexpected. It picked up the pink fish and put him on top of the rock. The pink fish was now safe and dry. He looked down and saw his friends in the water. They all laughed and clapped their fins. The pink fish had a fun day with the big wave.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat. The cat loved to polish his bowl every Wednesday. He was very good at it. One day, another cat saw him polishing his bowl. The other cat said, "Why do you polish your bowl every Wednesday?" The famous cat said, "I like it shiny and clean." The other cat wanted to be famous too. So, he tried to polish his bowl. But he was not good at it. He pushed too hard and the bowl broke. The other cat was sad. The moral of the story is, do not try to be like others. Be yourself and be happy with who you are.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to read books. One day, she found a big book with many pages. She was very happy. Sue started to read the book. She turned page after page. But then, something unexpected happened. When she turned one page, a big wind came and blew all the pages away. Sue was very sad. Sue tried to find all the pages, but she could not. She felt regret for turning the page too fast. The book was gone forever, and Sue could not read it anymore. The end.

Ben and Lily liked to play with snow. They made snowballs and snowmen and snow forts. One day, they had a big idea. They wanted to build a stadium with snow. They worked hard and used many snowballs. They made a big circle with snow walls. They made seats with snow and sticks. They made a field with snow and grass. They made goals with snow and cans. They were very proud of their stadium. They invited their friends to play soccer with them. They divided into two teams and wore hats of different colors. They had a lot of fun kicking the ball and scoring goals. But then, the sun came out and the snow started to melt. The stadium became icy and slippery. Ben and Lily and their friends fell down and slid on the ice. They laughed and giggled and had more fun. "Wow, this is the best stadium ever!" Ben said. "Yes, it is!" Lily agreed. "But we have to enjoy it while we can. Soon it will be gone." They hugged each other and played until the snow was gone. They were happy and tired. They said goodbye to their friends and went home. They dreamed of building another stadium someday.

Once upon a time, there was a red car. The red car was fast. The red car went zoom-zoom. One day, the red car met a cop. The cop said, "Stop! You go too fast!" The red car did not stop. The cop saw something hiding in the red car. The cop said, "Show me what you hide!" The red car did not want to reveal what was hiding. The red car went zoom-zoom again. The cop followed the red car. They went around and around. Then, the red car stopped. The cop said, "Now, you must show me what you hide!" The red car revealed a big, red ball. The cop took the red ball away. The red car was sad. The end.
