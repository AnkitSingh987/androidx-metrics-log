Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucas the Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time there was a kind boy named Lucas. He was always looking for ways to help people. One day he saw a strange lip stuck in a tree. Lucas wondered how it got there. He decided to send it back to its owner. Lucas asked around his town for help but no one knew who to send it to. He was about to give up when he met an old lady who told him a secret. She said that long ago a beautiful princess had owned the lip and had been really sad when she lost it. Lucas felt so happy that he could help the princess. He set out on a journey through the kingdom until he reached the palace. He spoke to the King and Queen and told them his story. The King and Queen were so amazed and kind that they invited Lucas to stay in the palace. Lucas was so happy that he could help the princess and be rewarded in this special way. He sent the lip back to the princess and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to create things with her hands. One day, she decided to make something tasty for her friends. Lily went to the kitchen and found some bread, cheese, and fruit. She put them all together and made a tasty snack. She was very proud of what she had created. Lily took the tasty snack to her friends, and they all loved it. They had a great time eating and playing together. From that day on, Lily knew that she could create anything she wanted with her hands and make her friends happy. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved cookies. One day, he saw a big, high jar full of cookies on the kitchen counter. Tim wanted to eat those cookies, but he was too small to reach the jar. Tim had an idea. He would gather all his toys and make a big pile. He put his teddy bear, his ball, and his blocks in a pile. He climbed on top of the pile to reach the high jar. But the pile was not high enough. Just then, Tim's big sister, Amy, came into the kitchen. She saw Tim trying to reach the cookies. "Do you need help?" she asked. Tim nodded, and Amy helped him get the cookie jar. They both sat down and enjoyed the yummy cookies together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a favorite toy named Stitch. Stitch was a small, soft, and nervous toy dog. Sue loved Stitch and always took him everywhere she went. One day, Sue and her family went to a big park. Sue played with Stitch near the trees. She was very happy. Sue's mom told her to value Stitch and be careful not to lose him. Sue said "Yes, mom!" and kept playing. But then, Sue saw a big red ball. She wanted to play with the ball too. She put Stitch down on the grass and ran to the ball. Sue played with the ball for a long time and forgot about Stitch. When it was time to go home, Sue's mom asked, "Where is Stitch?" Sue looked around and couldn't find him. She felt sad and cried. Stitch was lost, and Sue learned the importance of valuing her toys.

Once there was a girl. She was walking to the store. But on the way, she saw something strange! It was an ugly loop. The girl stopped and decided to stay and watch the loop go round and round. It went in a big circle and would never stop. It kept going in a loop. The girl stayed and watched the loop. She watched it spin and go round and round. It was so strange to her! It wasn't like anything she had seen before. The girl stayed until it got dark. She was so mesmerized by the ugly loop that she didn't want to leave. When it was time to go, the girl was sad to see the loop go. But when the girl came back to visit, the loop was still there making its ugly loop. The girl stayed and watched it go round and round. She was happy she could always come back and stay and watch the loop!
