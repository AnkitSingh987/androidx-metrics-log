Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Blocks

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks. They have many blocks of different colors and shapes. They can make houses, cars, animals and more with their blocks. One day, they decide to play a game. They want to see who can make the biggest tower with their blocks. They each have a pile of blocks to use. They start to build their towers on the floor. "Look at my tower, Lily!" Ben says. "It is very tall and strong. I have many numbers on my blocks. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!" "Wow, Ben, your tower is good," Lily says. "But look at my tower, too. It is very pretty and bright. I have many pink blocks on my tower. Pink is my favorite color. I also have some stars and hearts and flowers on my blocks." They keep building their towers, trying to make them higher and higher. They are having fun and laughing. But then, something bad happens. Ben wants to add one more block to his tower. He reaches for a block, but he moves too fast. He bumps into his tower and makes it fall. His blocks crash to the floor and make a loud noise. "Oh no, Ben, your tower!" Lily says. "It is broken. All your numbers are gone." Ben feels sad and angry. He looks at Lily's tower. It is still standing. It is still pink and pretty. He does not like it. He wants to break it, too. He runs to Lily's tower and pushes it hard. He makes it fall, too. Lily's blocks crash to the floor and make a louder noise. "Oh no, Lily, your tower!" Ben says. "It is broken, too. All your pink blocks are gone." Lily feels sad and angry, too. She looks at Ben. He has a mean smile on his face. She does not like him. She wants to hit him. She raises her hand and slaps him on the cheek. He cries out and slaps her back. They start to fight and scream. Their mom hears the noise and comes to see what is wrong. She sees the mess on the floor and the children hitting each other. She is very mad. She scolds them and makes them stop. She tells them to clean up the blocks and to say sorry to each other. She says they cannot play with blocks anymore today. She says they have to go to their rooms and think about what they did. Lily and Ben feel sorry and ashamed. They pick up the blocks and put them away. They say sorry to each other, but they do not mean it. They go to their rooms and cry. They do not want to be friends anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there were two little bears called Coco and Poco. They were very good friends and loved to play together. One day, Coco said to Poco, “Let’s bake a special igloo together!” Poco was very excited and ran to get their baking supplies. They quickly gathered what they needed, but when they tried to put the igloo together, it wouldn’t stick! Coco said, “Let’s try again. We can do it!” Poco shook his head. He said, “I don’t know how we’re ever going to make a successful igloo!” Coco thought for a while and then said, “Maybe we just have to be more careful when we put the pieces together.” So they tried again, more slowly this time. After a few tries, finally their igloo started to take shape. When they were finished, Poco looked at the igloo in amazement. “We did it!” he said, giving Coco a big hug. Together, Coco and Poco had made a successful igloo!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside. One day, it was very gloomy. The sky was gray, and it looked like it would rain. Tim wanted to play, but he was not sure if he should. Tim's mom said, "Tim, it is time to get your toys." Tim was happy. He knew that this meant he could play. He went outside and started to play with his toys. He played with his car, his ball, and his blocks. He was having so much fun. All of a sudden, it started to rain. Tim was sad, but he remembered what his mom said. It was time to get his toys. He picked up his car, his ball, and his blocks. He ran inside just before the rain got too heavy. Tim was happy that he listened to his mom. Now, he could play inside and stay dry.
