Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a happy dog named Spot. Spot liked to play all day. He played with his ball, he played with his friends, and he played in the grass. One day, when Spot was playing in the grass, he got an itch on his leg. He tried to scratch it, but the itch would not go away. Spot's friend, Tom the cat, saw Spot was not happy. Tom wanted to help Spot. Tom went to his house and got a book. Tom could read the book. The book told Tom how to help Spot with his itch. Tom read the book and found a way to make Spot's itch go away. He got some water and a soft cloth. He put the cloth in the water and then put the wet cloth on Spot's itch. Spot felt better right away. Spot was happy again, and he and Tom played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong man named Tom. Tom had a new job. He had to carry lumber. He liked his new job because he was very good at it. One day, Tom met a little bird. The bird said, "I want to help you carry lumber, Tom!" Tom laughed and said, "You are too small, little bird. But you can come with me and watch." The little bird was happy to go with Tom. The next day, Tom and the little bird went to carry lumber. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew all the lumber away. Tom was sad because he could not find the lumber. The little bird said, "Don't worry, Tom! I will help you find the lumber!" The little bird flew up high and saw the lumber. The bird showed Tom where the lumber was. Tom was very happy and thanked the little bird. From that day on, Tom and the little bird worked together to carry lumber.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. She had a smooth purple dress and always felt pretty when she wore it. One day, she was invited to a birthday party. She was so excited to go and show off her pretty dress. At the party, there were many other kids and they were having fun. Amy joined the game, but then one of the other kids bumped into her. His toy broke and purple paint spilled all over Amy's dress. She was so sad and it ruined her dress. The party ended and Amy had to go home. Her dress was covered in purple paint and she felt so bad. Amy never wanted to wear her smooth purple dress again.

One day, Emily was walking to the store to get some candy. She was so excited! But then she stumbled and dropped her penny on the ground. She started to worry. She picked it up and brushed off the dirt. "Daddy, I'm worried," said Emily to her father. He smiled and said, "Don't worry, Emily. Just be careful next time!" Emily started walking again but then she saw a clumsy dog chasing a butterfly. She was afraid that it would run after her! She started to worry even more. "Daddy, I'm worried. Will the dog get me?" Emily asked. Her father smiled and said, "No, Emily. Just stay away from the clumsy dog, ok?" Emily felt better and she continued walking to the store. Her father was so calming and she was very happy that he could keep her from worrying.

Once, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toys. One day, she wanted to use her toy oven to make a pretend cake. Sue found a piece of soft dough. She rolled it and put it in the toy oven. She waited and waited until the pretend cake was done. When it was time to take the cake out, it was hot! Sue was very happy. She shared the pretend cake with her friends. They all had a fun time playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat carrots. One day, he went to the garden to find a big, yummy carrot. He looked and looked, but he could not find any carrots. Bob was worried. Bob saw a big tree and thought, "Maybe the tree can help me find a carrot." So, he asked the tree, "Please, can you yield a carrot for me?" The tree said, "I am sorry, Bob, I cannot yield a carrot. I can only yield leaves." Bob was very sad. He went to his friend, the bird, and asked, "Can you help me find a carrot? I am very worried." The bird said, "I will try, Bob." The bird flew high and looked all around, but she could not find a carrot. Bob was so sad that he could not find a carrot. He went back to his home and cried. That day, Bob did not eat any carrots, and he went to bed hungry. The next day, he decided to plant a carrot seed, but he knew it would take a long time to grow. Bob was still worried, and he wished he had found a carrot in the garden.
