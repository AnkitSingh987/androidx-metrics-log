Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day. One day, Spot found a camera in the park. It was big and black. Spot picked it up with his mouth and ran home. Spot showed the camera to his friend, Tom the cat. Tom looked at the camera and said, "Wow, a camera! We can take pictures!" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, let's take pictures of our fun times!" They took pictures of themselves playing in the park, chasing butterflies, and rolling in the grass. But one day, they left the camera outside, and it rained. The camera got wet and started to rot. Spot and Tom were sad, but they learned to have fun without the camera, using their imaginations to remember their happy days.

---

One day, a little cat named Kitty wandered outside. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As she climbed, she saw a bird's nest up high. The nest was very tight and cozy. Kitty said, "Hello, bird! Can I see your nest?" The bird did not like Kitty being so close. It said, "No, Kitty! My nest is not for you. Please go away!" Kitty felt sad and climbed down the tree. She went back home to her mom. Her mom made her a cup of warm tea. Kitty told her mom about the bird and the tight nest. Her mom said, "Kitty, we must be kind to others. The bird's home is not for us to see. Let's enjoy our tea and let the bird be." Kitty understood and felt better. She sipped her tea and promised to be more kind to the bird and other animals.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very hot dog because he played outside all day. His mom saw him and said, "Spot, you need to bathe so you won't be hot anymore." Spot went inside the house and started to bathe. He splashed water all over and felt much better. But, Spot still felt a little hot. His mom gave him a small pill to help him feel better. "Take this pill, Spot. It will help you not feel hot." Spot took the pill and soon, he felt much better. He was not hot anymore. Spot said, "Thank you, Mom, for helping me feel better!" His mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Spot. Now you can play outside again, but remember to take breaks and drink water." Spot went back outside to play with his friends. He was happy that he was not hot anymore, and he had a great day playing with his friends. Spot always remembered to take breaks and drink water, so he never felt too hot again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy box with a lock. She loved her toys so much. One day, the wind was harsh and blew the key away. Sue was sad because she could not open the lock to play with her toys. Sue's friend, Tim, saw her cry. Tim wanted to help Sue. He had an idea to rescue the key. Tim got a big, strong kite. He thought the kite could help find the key. Sue and Tim went outside to fly the kite. The kite flew high and far. It was fun to watch. Then, the kite saw the key on a tree branch. The kite took the key and brought it back to Sue and Tim. Sue was so happy! She could open the lock and play with her toys again. Sue and Tim played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little boy named Bob. He had a big heart and loved to explore. Looking up at the sky one day, Bob was filled with excitement. The sky was a mild blue and looked so inviting! Bob started running towards the sky. He was so eager to see what was above! Soon, his feet started to get tired, so he had to stop. Sitting down beside a big tree, he looked up again. At that moment, Bob noticed a white plane flying above him and he shouted out, "What is that?" Just then, Bob heard a voice coming from the sky. It was a friendly voice that said, "That's a plane, Bob. It's a flying machine." Bob was so amazed and couldn't believe what he had heard. He waved to the sky and shouted back, "Bye bye plane!" From that day on, Bob looked up at the sky with amazement and wonder. He knew his new friend was watching over him, keeping him safe with a mild and loving sky. The End.
