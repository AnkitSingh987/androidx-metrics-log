Category: Intermediate
Title: Hippy the Clever Hippo and the Puzzle

Once upon a time, there was a clever hippo named Hippy. Hippy lived in a big river with his friends. They liked to play all day in the water. One day, Hippy had a big job to do. He had to complete a puzzle. The puzzle had many pieces, and it was hard for Hippy. But Hippy was a clever hippo, so he did not give up. Hippy worked and worked on the puzzle. He put the pieces together, one by one. After a long time, Hippy completed the puzzle. He was so happy and proud. All his friends cheered for him. Hippy knew that being clever helped him finish the puzzle.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was a great dog. He loved to play and run in the park. One day, Max found a big red ball. He loved to play with it. He would push it with his nose and chase it all day. One day, Max's ball got very dirty. It had mud and dirt all over it. Max did not want to spoil his ball. So, he took it to his mom. His mom helped him clean the ball. She gave it a good rub with a wet cloth. The ball was clean and shiny again. Max was very happy. He played with his great red ball every day. He learned to keep it clean and not spoil it. Max and his ball were the best of friends. They had lots of fun together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a unique kangaroo named Kiki. Kiki was different from the other kangaroos because she had a very big pocket. She loved to unpack things from her pocket and share them with her friends. One sunny day, Kiki met a little bird named Bobby. Bobby was sad because he had no toys to play with. Kiki said, "Don't worry, Bobby! Let me help you!" She reached into her big pocket and started to unpack some toys. First, she took out a red ball, then a blue car, and lastly a green teddy bear. Bobby's eyes lit up with joy. Bobby and Kiki played with the toys all day long. They laughed and had a lot of fun. The other animals at the zoo saw them playing and joined in too. Kiki was very happy that her unique pocket could bring so much joy to her friends. From that day on, Kiki and Bobby became best friends and always played together.

Mia smiled as she got into her comfort bed. She loved her bed so much as it was always so cozy and warm. She reached over to the nightstand and grabbed her favorite blanket. She said, "Mmmm this is so soft." Just then, Mia heard some strange noises coming from outside. She looked out the window, and saw it was raining hard. "Oh no," Mia said. "I better soak up some of this water before it gets inside." Mia put on her rain boots, grabbed an umbrella and ran outside. She used her little pink bucket and started collecting the rainwater. She was so busy that soon she got tired and had to take a break. Mia plopped down in the rain and laid in the cool, calm grass. The rain had eased up, and Mia was happy to see it was almost gone. She had soaked up all the extra water, so her house stayed safe and dry. Mia was proud of her work and was now ready for a nice, long nap.

One day, a cat named Tom found a mysterious toy. It was long and could stretch. Tom played with the toy all day. He liked to stretch it very far. But, Tom did not know that the toy could cause trouble. Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max wanted to play with the toy too. "Can I play with your toy?" asked Max. Tom said, "No, it is mine!" Max was sad. They both wanted the toy, so they started to fight. They pulled and stretched the toy. But, they stretched it too much. The toy broke into two pieces. Now, Tom and Max had nothing to play with. They were both very sad.
