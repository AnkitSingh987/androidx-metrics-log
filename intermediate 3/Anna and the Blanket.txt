Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Blanket

Anna liked to read novels. She had many novels in her room. She liked to read about brave people and big adventures. Sometimes, she read at night with a lamp. One night, she was reading a novel about a scary monster. The monster had big teeth and claws. It liked to chase people and make them scream. Anna felt fearful. She wanted to stop reading, but she also wanted to know what happened next. She heard a noise outside her window. She looked up and saw a shadow. It looked like the monster. She gasped and hid under her blanket. She was very scared. "Anna, are you okay?" It was her mom. She came to check on her. She opened the door and turned on the light. Anna peeked out from her blanket. She saw her mom and the shadow. It was not the monster. It was a tree branch. It moved in the wind. "Mom, I was reading a novel and I saw a shadow. I thought it was the monster," Anna said. Her mom smiled and hugged her. "It's okay, Anna. It was just a tree. There is no monster. Novels are fun, but sometimes they can make you fearful. You can raise your hand if you want me to read with you." Anna nodded and raised her hand. She felt better. She gave her mom the novel and snuggled with her. They read together until Anna fell asleep. She dreamed of happy things.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the lake. He saw a pretty kayak. The kayak was blue and peaceful. Tom wanted to go on the kayak. Tom asked his mom if he could go on the kayak. His mom said yes. Tom was happy. He started to load the kayak with his toys. He put a ball, a bear, and a boat in the kayak. Tom got into the peaceful kayak. He played with his toys in the water. He had a lot of fun. The sun was warm, and the water was cool. Tom loved the peaceful kayak.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree with many oranges. A little boy named Tim wanted to get an orange from the tree. He tried to reach it, but he was too small. One day, Tim met a big bird. The bird said, "I can help you get an orange. I can fly up and separate it from the tree." Tim was happy and said, "Yes, please help me!" The big bird flew up and separated an orange from the tall tree. The orange fell down, and Tim picked it up. He said, "Thank you, big bird!" The bird was happy to help, and they both enjoyed the sweet orange together.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Ben and Lara. They were both 3 years old. One day, they were playing together when suddenly they saw something they had never seen before. On the table there was a beautiful, shiny dress that sparkled in the sun. Lara, being very excited, said to Ben, "Let's dress up in the dress!" Ben thought for a moment and then replied, "But I don't even know what it is - I'm ignorant!" Lara, being wise beyond her years, explained to Ben, "Just put the dress on and you'll see! We can have lots of fun!" Giggling, the two friends dressed up in the dress. They loved the way the dress felt, and the way it looked. They danced happily around the table all day long, completely enchanted by the wonderful dress!

Lily and Ben are kids who like to play with blocks. They have many blocks of different colors, but they both love the yellow ones the most. One day, they decide to build a big tower with all the yellow blocks they can find. "Look, Lily, our tower is so tall and pretty!" Ben says, putting the last yellow block on top. "Yes, Ben, it is! But I want to play with the yellow blocks too. Can I have some?" Lily asks, reaching for the tower. "No, Lily, you can't! These are my yellow blocks. You can play with the other colors. Go away!" Ben says, pushing Lily's hand away. Lily feels sad and angry. She wants to play with the yellow blocks too. She thinks Ben is not nice. She decides to make him sorry. She kicks the tower with her foot, and all the yellow blocks fall down. "Hey, Lily, what did you do? You broke our tower! That's not fair!" Ben shouts, looking at the mess. "I don't care, Ben. You were mean to me. You didn't share the yellow blocks. Now you have none!" Lily shouts back, sticking out her tongue. The kids start to cry and yell at each other. They are not friends anymore. They end their playtime and go to their rooms. They feel lonely and sorry. They wish they had shared the yellow blocks. They wish they had been kind to each other. They wish they were friends again.
