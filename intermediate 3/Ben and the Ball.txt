Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and the Ball

Ben likes to play soccer with his friends. He is a good player. He can kick the ball far and fast. He can score many goals. His friends are happy when he plays with them. One day, Ben is sick. He has a fever and a cough. He has to stay in bed. He cannot go to school or to the park. He cannot play soccer with his friends. He is sad and bored. He misses his friends and the ball. His friends miss him too. They go to the park to play soccer, but it is not the same without Ben. They have one player less. They cannot score many goals. The other team is stronger. They lose the game. They are sad and angry. They wish Ben was there. Ben hears them from his window. He sees them playing soccer. He sees them losing. He wants to help them. He wants to play with them. He tries to get out of bed, but he is too weak. He falls on the floor. He hurts his head. He cries. His mom hears him. She runs to his room. She sees him on the floor. She sees the blood. She is scared. She calls the doctor. She hugs Ben. She tells him to stay still. She tells him he will be okay. But Ben is not okay. He is very sick. He has to go to the hospital. He has to stay there for a long time. He cannot play soccer anymore. He cannot see his friends anymore. He is alone. He is empty.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy man named Bob. Bob had a big luggage. He was going on a trip. He was not happy because his luggage was broken. One day, Bob met a nice lady named Sue. Sue knew how to repair things. Bob asked Sue to fix his luggage. Sue said, "I can help you, but you must be kind to others." Bob thought about it and said, "Okay, I will try." Sue fixed Bob's luggage. Bob was very happy. He started to be nice to everyone he met. People liked him now. Bob and Sue became good friends. They went on many trips together with the fixed luggage. And Bob was never grumpy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was very adventurous. She liked to play outside, climb trees, and look for bugs. One day, Amy found a small, shiny rock. She picked it up and took it home. In her room, Amy held the rock and made a wish. Suddenly, a kind spirit came out of the rock. The spirit said, "I promise to be your friend and help you on your adventures." Amy was very happy to have a new friend. Together, Amy and the spirit went on many fun adventures. They played in the park, went on a treasure hunt, and even found a secret cave. They promised to always be friends and have more adventures together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there lived a great lion. He lived in a green forest with his family. One sunny day, the lion had a problem. He wanted to find something to make his family happy. So he went for a walk and surprisingly, he heard a loud sound. It was a loud horn! The lion ran to the poor and he found a cow who was stuck in between some rocks. The lion said, "Hello cow! How can I help you?" The cow replied, "I'm stuck! I can't get out!" The lion said, "I'll help! I will complete the task." So he used all his strength and pulled the cow out of the rocks. The cow was so happy. She thanked the lion and said, "You are great! Thank you for rescuing me!" The lion smiled and said, "You are welcome." The lion was proud of himself. He had completed his task and the cow was safe!

Once there was a boy named Tom. Tom was very clever. He loved to play outside. One day he went to a park and he saw a motorcycle. He really wanted to ride it but his mom said no. Tom was very clever though, and he had an idea. He decided that if he asked the park’s workers they might include him in their motorcycle fun. So Tom asked one of the workers, and he said yes! Tom was so excited. They let him sit on the motorcycle and the worker showed him how to make it go. It was so much fun! When Tom got home he told his mom all about it. She was amazed at how clever he was. Tom was so proud that he had figured out how to include himself in the motorcycle fun!
