Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cacky

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily had a pet cactus named Cacky. Cacky was a spoiled cactus. Lily loved Cacky so much and always gave it the best spot in the room. Cacky loved to be near the big window where it could see the sun. One day, Lily wanted to take Cacky outside to play. "Cacky, let's go outside and play!" said Lily. So, Lily tried to carry Cacky. But Cacky was a cactus and had sharp parts. Lily had to be very careful not to get hurt. Lily carried Cacky to the yard. They had a fun day playing in the sun. Cacky loved being outside with Lily. When it was time to go back inside, Lily carefully carried Cacky back into the house. They were both happy and tired from their fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy box full of her favorite toys. Every day, she would play with her toys and have lots of fun. One day, Sue found a big, round ball. She thought it was very important and wanted to keep it safe. Sue tried to fit the ball into her toy box. It was too big! She asked her mom, "What should I do?" Her mom said, "You can try to find a new place for your important ball." So Sue looked all around her room for a place to put the ball. As Sue was looking, she tripped and had a small fall. The ball rolled away and bumped into her toy box. The toy box opened, and all the toys came out to play! The toys had a great time playing with the ball. Sue learned that it's not important to keep things all to yourself. Sharing with others can be more fun!

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. She had a big, red lollipop in her hand. Lucy liked the sweet taste of her lollipop. She saw her friend Tom playing near the slide. Lucy was happy to see him, so she waved her hand and called out, "Hi, Tom!" Tom saw Lucy and her lollipop. He wanted one too. He walked over to Lucy and asked, "Can I have your lollipop, please?" Lucy was gentle and kind, so she said, "Okay, you can have it." She gave her lollipop to Tom. Tom took the lollipop and said, "Thank you, Lucy!" But as he started to eat it, a big gust of wind blew the lollipop out of his hand. The lollipop fell on the ground and broke into many pieces. Tom and Lucy were sad because the lollipop was gone. They both learned that sometimes, even when you share, things don't always end well.

One day, a little boy named Tim felt anxious. He wanted to write a poem for his mom's birthday. But he did not know how to write one. He asked his friend, a small dog named Max, for help. "Max, can you help me write a poem?" Tim asked. Max wagged his tail and said, "I can't write, but I can bite. Maybe I can help you by biting the words into the paper." Tim thought it was a good idea, so they tried it. Max started to bite the paper, but it did not look like a poem. The paper was all torn and messy. Tim felt sad and said, "This is not a poem, Max. We need to find another way." Just then, Tim's big sister, Lily, came into the room. She saw the torn paper and asked what happened. Tim told her about the poem and Max's help. Lily smiled and said, "I can help you write a poem, Tim. Let's do it together." Tim felt happy and less anxious. They wrote a beautiful poem for their mom. Tim thanked Max and Lily for their help. In the end, their mom loved the poem and gave them all a big hug.

One day, a helpful crane was near a big slide. The crane liked to help kids play on the slide. It would lift them up and put them at the top, so they could slide down fast. A little boy saw the crane and wanted to play on the slide. The crane picked him up and put him at the top of the slide. The boy slid down fast and had fun. He wanted to go again and again. The crane was happy to help, but it was getting tired. It picked the boy up one more time. But this time, the crane was too tired and let the boy go too soon. The boy fell and got hurt. The crane was sad it could not help more.
