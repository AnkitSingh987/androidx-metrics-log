Category: Intermediate
Title: Sarah and Jack and the Quiz

Once upon a time, two friends, Sarah and Jack, were playing together. They were very playful, laughing and joking around. They had lots of fun, but then Jack had an idea - he wanted to pretend to be the teacher of a quiz. Sarah was not sure about this, but Jack was so excited that she couldn't say no. So Jack went off and made a quiz for Sarah. It was quite tricky and Sarah just couldn't seem to get it right. Jack kept making it harder, but Sarah just couldn't seem to get the answers. She was getting frustrated and a little bit angry, but she still kept trying. Finally, Jack realised that Sarah wasn't enjoying the quiz and he felt bad. He quickly made it easier for her and the two of them soon started having fun again, laughing and joking like before. The End.

Once upon a time, in a big palace, there lived a funny king. The king loved to laugh and play with his friends. One day, the king said to his friend, "I want to make the palace bigger. Let's increase the size of our home!" The king and his friend worked together to make the palace bigger. They added more rooms and a big garden. The king was very happy with his new, big palace. One day, while they were playing in the garden, they found a small door. The king and his friend opened the door and went inside. To their surprise, they found a room full of toys! The king said, "Wow! We didn't know this was here! This is the best surprise ever!" They played with the toys and had lots of fun in their new, big palace.

Once upon a time, there was a potato. The potato had a friend. The friend was a little bird. They liked to play together. They played in the sun and had fun. One day, the potato felt tired. The little bird saw this and said, "Potato, you need to rest." So, the potato lay down under a big tree. The little bird sang a song to help the potato rest. While the potato was resting, a boy came by. The boy saw the potato and thought it was stupid. He wanted to kick the potato. The little bird saw this and got scared. The bird flew to the boy and said, "Please don't hurt my friend." The boy saw the little bird and felt sorry. He said, "I'm sorry, little bird. I won't hurt your friend." The boy and the little bird became friends too. They all played together and had fun. And the potato was not stupid anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, scary tree. Tim was scared, but he wanted to play near the tree. He saw a little girl named Sue playing near the tree too. Tim went to Sue and asked, "Is the tree scary to you too?" Sue said, "Yes, it is scary. But I like to play here." While they were playing, dark clouds came. It started to rain. Tim and Sue ran to find a place to hide from the rain. They found a small shower near the park. They went inside to stay dry. As the rain got heavier, the shower got smaller. Tim and Sue were scared. They didn't know what to do. The scary tree was still outside, and they didn't want to go back out there. In the end, Tim and Sue stayed in the small shower. They were scared and wet. The rain didn't stop, and they couldn't go home. It was a bad day for Tim and Sue.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a nice girl named Lily. She loved to prepare food for her family. One day, she wanted to make a cake. She had a big, green pine tree in her yard. Lily had an idea to make a pine cake. Lily went outside and picked some green pine leaves. She put them in the cake. She mixed it all up and baked it. When the cake was done, she put it on a plate. The cake looked very yummy, but it was fake. It was not a real cake. Lily's family came to eat the cake. They took a bite and made a funny face. The cake tasted like pine! They all laughed and said, "This cake is so silly!" Lily felt happy that she made her family laugh. From that day on, they called her the "Pine Cake Girl."
