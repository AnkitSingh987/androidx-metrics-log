Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tall Tree and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in the woods. The tree was so tall that it could touch the sky. Many animals lived in the tree, and they were all happy. One day, a spirit came to the woods. The spirit was big and strong. The animals were scared and asked the spirit to stop. They did not want the spirit to hurt their tall tree. The spirit looked at the animals and smiled. The spirit said, "I will not hurt your tree. I am here to help you." The spirit touched the tree, and something unexpected happened. The tree grew even taller, and more animals could live in it. The animals were happy, and they thanked the spirit for helping them.

Johnny and Anne were best friends. They did everything together - running in the park, playing hide and seek, and even hugging and kissing. One morning, Anne went to the hospital. She was feeling very sick and had to stay there for a few days. Johnny was sad that his friend had to leave, but he knew she would come back. Every day, he visited Anne at the hospital. Johnny brought her flowers and green balloons. He would give her a big hug, and kiss her on the cheek. Finally, the day came when Anne was well and she could go home. Johnny was so happy to have his best friend back. They hugged and kissed. Johnny was glad that Anne was feeling better and they could have fun together again!

Once upon a time, there was an old house. In this house, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play with her toys and look in the mirror. She would make funny faces and laugh at herself. One day, Amy was playing with her toys when she heard a strange noise. It sounded like someone was weeping. She looked around and saw a little boy in the mirror. The boy was sad and weeping. Amy was scared, but she wanted to help the boy. Amy touched the mirror, and something unexpected happened. She went into the mirror and met the little boy. He was scared too, but they became friends. They played with toys and laughed together. After a while, Amy found a way to bring the boy out of the mirror. They were both happy and never wept again.

One day, a little dog named Max went to the park. He saw a big ball and wanted to play with it. Max tried to push the ball with his nose, but it was too big. He felt sad and sat down. A big dog named Sam saw Max and walked over. "Why are you sad?" asked Sam. Max said, "I want to play with the big ball, but I can't push it." Sam smiled and said, "I can help you!" Sam and Max tried to push the ball together. They were impatient because the ball still didn't move. But then, a little girl came over. She picked up the ball and threw it for them. The two dogs were very happy and ran after the ball. The little girl laughed and played with them all day.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big tree with red apples on it. They wanted to pick some apples to eat, but the tree was too high for them. "Let's ask the squirrel to help us," Lily said. "He can climb the tree and get some apples for us." "OK," Tom said. "But how do we talk to the squirrel?" Lily had an idea. She said, "We can dance for him. Maybe he will like our dance and give us some apples." They started to dance under the tree. They jumped and spun and clapped their hands. They made funny faces and noises. They had fun dancing. The squirrel watched them from a branch. He thought they were charming and funny. He liked their dance. He decided to share some apples with them. He picked some apples and threw them down to Lily and Tom. "Thank you, squirrel!" Lily and Tom said. They caught the apples and took a bite. They were sweet and juicy. They smiled and waved to the squirrel. The squirrel smiled and waved back. He was happy to make new friends. He joined them in their dance. They danced and ate apples until it was time to go home. They said goodbye and promised to come back soon. They had a wonderful day in the park.
