Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Scarf

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She wore a pretty scarf on her neck. It was a sunny day, so she gazed at the sky and saw a big, white cloud. In the park, Sue met a boy named Tim. Tim was rude and said, "I don't like your scarf." Sue felt sad but she said, "That's okay, I like my scarf." Then, they played a fun game of catch. Sue and Tim became friends. They gazed at the clouds together and found shapes in them. Sue showed Tim a cloud that looked like her scarf. Now, Tim liked her scarf too. They played in the park until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. One day, she went on a journey. She wanted to find something unusual. She walked and walked until she found a big, old tree. The tree was very different from the others. Under the tree, Amy saw a tiny, funny bug. The bug could dance and sing! Amy thought it was very unusual. She started to clap for the bug. Clap, clap, clap! She was applauding the bug's show. Amy and the bug became good friends. They had lots of fun on their journey together. They found more unusual things and met new friends. And every time they found something unusual, Amy would applaud and the bug would dance.

One day, a little duck named Ducky went to bathe in a pond. The water was cool and nice. Ducky loved to splash and play. As he was playing, he saw a big frog named Froggy. Froggy looked sad. "Hi Froggy, why are you sad?" asked Ducky. Froggy said, "I want to jump far, but I can't. I want to be incredible." Ducky wanted to help Froggy feel good. So, they made a plan. Ducky and Froggy practiced jumping together. They jumped and jumped. Then, something unexpected happened. Ducky found out he could fly! Ducky was so happy. He said, "Look, Froggy! We did it! We are a success!" Froggy was happy too. They were both incredible.

Once upon time, there was a little girl named Sally. She was very loyal and always put others before herself. One day she went to the park to play hockey with her friends. As she played, she heard a loud scream coming from the playground. She was scared, so she told her friends to stay put while she checked it out. She went to the playground and saw a small kitten that looked like it had been hurt. She knelt down and tried to soothe the cat by talking in a gentle voice. Suddenly the cat jumped up and screamed again. Then Sally knew that it was just scared, not hurt. She scooped up the kitten and brought it back to her friends. They all took turns petting the kitten until it calmed down and purred happily. Sally was so proud that she had been loyal to the kitten and helped it feel safe. She smiled with satisfaction at her good deed and then went back to playing hockey.

One day, a boy named Tim went on a holiday with his family. They went to a big fun park. Tim saw a big fake bear. He wanted to hug the bear. "Can I hug the bear, Mom?" Tim asked. Mom said, "Yes, Tim. You can hug the bear." Tim hugged the bear and was very happy. But something unexpected happened. The bear moved and talked! "Hello, I am Bob the bear!" said the bear. Tim was surprised. He thought the bear was fake. But Bob was a nice bear. They played and hugged all day at the fun park. Tim and Bob became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her mom's makeup. One day, she saw a high shelf in her room. She wanted to see what was on it. Lily asked her mom, "Can I open the box on the high shelf?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Lily climbed on a chair to reach the high shelf. She opened the box and found makeup inside. Lily was so happy. She took the makeup and started to play. She put on red lipstick and blue eyeshadow. When her mom saw her, she laughed. "You look so pretty!" her mom said. They both laughed and had a fun day playing with the makeup.
