Category: Intermediate
Title: Zoom the Red Car and the Biggie

One day, a little red car named Zoom was driving in the wild. Zoom loved to go fast. He had a lot of speed. As he was driving, he saw a big blue car named Biggie. Biggie was going slow. "Hi Biggie!" Zoom said, "Why are you going so slow?" Biggie looked at Zoom and said, "I am slow because I am big. I cannot go as fast as you." Zoom thought about it and realized that Biggie was right. Zoom was small and fast, but Biggie was big and slow. They were different, but that was okay. Zoom and Biggie became friends. They played together in the wild. Zoom would race ahead, and Biggie would follow slowly. They were happy to be friends, even if they were not the same.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl who was very brave. She had a knob on her door, and she loved to turn it. One day she heard a noise outside and wanted to find out what it was. She went outside and saw smoke in the distance. The smoke was coming from her neighbour's house. The little girl ran all the way there. When she got there, she saw that the fire was very big and scary. She started to cry. Suddenly her neighbour came running out of the house. He said to the little girl, "I'm so glad you're here. Can you help me turn the knob to turn on the water hose? We have to put out the fire." The little girl and her neighbour both grabbed onto the knob. It was hard and hairy but they managed to turn it. Suddenly the fire hose was on and the fire started to go out. The neighbour gave the little girl a big hug. He said, "Thank you for helping me put out the fire. You are so brave!" The little girl was very happy. She had had an adventure, and she learnt that she was brave enough to handle it.

Tim had a birdcage with a blue bird in it. He liked to watch the bird sing and fly in the cage. He never let the bird out of the cage, because he was afraid it would fly away. He also never cleaned the cage, because he was lazy. The cage was filthy, with old food and poop everywhere. One day, Tim's mom told him to clean the cage, or she would give the bird to someone else. Tim did not want to lose his bird, so he said he would clean the cage later. But he did not. He went to play with his toys instead. He forgot about the bird and the cage. The next day, Tim's mom came to his room and saw the filthy cage. She was very angry and sad. She saw that the bird was not moving or singing. She touched the bird and felt that it was cold and stiff. The bird was dead. It had died from the dirt and the sickness in the cage. Tim's mom took the cage and the bird away. She told Tim that he was a bad boy and that he could not have a bird anymore. She said he had to learn to take care of his pets, or he would have no pets at all. Tim cried and cried. He wanted to hug his bird and say sorry, but it was too late. He had lost his bird forever. He was very sorry and very sad.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fearful boy named Tim. Tim was scared of many things, but he loved the different seasons. In each season, he would try to keep a special thing to remember it by. One day, during the fall season, Tim found a big leaf. He wanted to keep the leaf to remember the fun he had. He put the leaf in his pocket and went to play outside. As he played, the wind blew the leaf out of his pocket, and he lost it. Tim was very sad and cried. He looked for the leaf, but he could not find it. The wind had taken it far away. Tim learned that sometimes, we cannot keep things we love, and not all stories have a happy ending.
