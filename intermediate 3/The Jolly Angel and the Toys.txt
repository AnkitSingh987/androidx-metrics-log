Category: Intermediate
Title: The Jolly Angel and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a jolly angel. Her job was to make sure that everyone was happy. One day, she had an idea. She wanted to use a magical wand to make the world even more jolly than it already was! The angel flew down and waved her wand. Suddenly, everything was more fun and exciting! Everywhere she went, people were smiling and laughing. The angel used her wand to make toys and sweets. The children were delighted. They loved playing with all the new toys. The angel was very happy with her work. The world was much more jolly now! All the children laughed and played, with the angel watching over them.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw a toy he really liked. It was a little red car. He wanted to buy it, so he asked his mom if he could have it. His mom said yes, but he needed to pay for it. Tim had a wallet with some money inside. He opened his wallet and saw that he had enough money to buy the little red car. He was very happy. Tim and his mom went to the lady at the store to pay for the toy. The lady at the store took the money from Tim's wallet and gave him the little red car. Tim said thank you and put his wallet back in his pocket. He was so excited to play with his new toy. Tim and his mom went home, and Tim played with his little red car all day.

---

Tom and Lily were playing cops and robbers in the backyard. Tom was the cop and Lily was the robber. Tom had a toy gun and Lily had a bag of candy. "Stop, robber! Give me the candy or go to jail!" Tom shouted. "No, never! You can't catch me!" Lily said, running away. Tom chased Lily around the trees and the swing. He was faster than Lily, who was chubby and got tired easily. He caught up with her and grabbed her arm. "Ha, I got you! Now you have to go to jail!" Tom said, pointing to the doghouse. Lily pouted and tried to pull away. "No, I don't want to go to jail! It's dark and scary and I want my candy!" Tom felt sorry for Lily. He didn't want to make her cry. He had an idea. "OK, how about this? You can keep your candy, but you have to share it with me. And you have to say sorry for being a robber. Then you don't have to go to jail. Deal?" Tom said. Lily thought for a moment. She liked candy, but she also liked Tom. He was her best friend. She nodded. "OK, deal. I'm sorry for being a robber. You're a good cop. Can we be friends again?" Lily said. Tom smiled and hugged Lily. "Of course we can be friends again. You're a good robber. Well, not really, but you know what I mean. Come on, let's eat some candy together. I'll encourage you to run faster next time." Tom said. They went to the porch and shared the candy. They were happy and forgot about jail. They decided to play something else later. Maybe pirates and mermaids. Or astronauts and aliens. Or dinosaurs and cavemen. They had many ideas and many fun games. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a clever fox. The fox had many friends, but he also had one big threat. The threat was a mean wolf who always wanted to catch the fox. One sunny day, the fox was playing near a pond when the mean wolf came. The wolf said, "I will catch you today, clever fox!" The fox thought fast and jumped into the pond to soak. The wolf did not like water, so he waited by the pond. The clever fox said, "Come in, the water is nice and warm!" But the wolf did not want to get wet. The fox stayed in the water, and the wolf got tired of waiting. The wolf left, and the fox was safe again. The clever fox had a big smile on his face, knowing he had tricked the mean wolf once more.
