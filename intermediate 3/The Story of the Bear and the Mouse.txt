Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy bear. The bear lived in the woods. One day, the bear had a big pain in his tummy. He did not know what to do. He guessed that he needed help. The bear went to see a doctor. The doctor said, "You need an operation." The bear was scared. He did not want an operation. The doctor tried to make the bear feel better. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse came out of the bear's pocket. The mouse said, "I am sorry. I was hiding in your pocket and I ate your food." The bear laughed. He did not need an operation after all. The bear and the mouse became friends and lived happily in the woods.

Lila and Ben were twins who liked to play in the woods. They had a big dog named Rex who always followed them. One day, they found a stream with clear water and shiny stones. They wanted to soak their feet and look for pretty stones. "Look, Ben, I found a red stone!" Lila said, holding up her treasure. "Wow, Lila, that's nice! I found a blue one!" Ben said, showing his. They were so happy that they did not hear the growl behind them. A tall, gray wolf was hiding in the bushes, watching them. He was hungry and wanted to eat them. Rex heard the growl and barked loudly. He ran to the twins and stood in front of them, ready to fight. The wolf saw Rex and was afraid. He knew Rex was bigger and stronger than him. He decided to run away and look for another meal. "Rex, you saved us!" Lila said, hugging the dog. "Thank you, Rex, you are a good dog!" Ben said, patting his head. They took their stones and ran back to their home. They told their mom what happened and showed her their stones. She was glad they were safe and praised Rex. She gave them cookies and milk and let them keep their stones. They were happy and proud of their adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a generous boy who loved to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. He brought his favorite toy, a big red ball. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. She was sad because she didn't have any toys to play with. Tim went over to Sue and said, "Do you want to play with my ball?" Sue was very happy and said, "Yes, thank you!" They played together, laughing and having fun. As they played, Tim started to run too fast and he stumbled. He fell down and hurt his knee. Sue helped Tim up and they walked to the exit of the park together. Tim's mom saw what happened and put a band-aid on his knee. Tim learned that even when you stumble, it's important to be kind and share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She loved to count her toys every day. One day, Lily went to the doctor with her mom. The doctor said she needed an x-ray. Lily was scared, but her mom said it would be fast. The x-ray machine was big and made a funny noise. Lily was brave and did what the doctor said. After the x-ray, Lily went home. She wanted to count her toys, but they were gone. Lily was very sad. The toys did not come back, and it was a bad day for Lily.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her cards. She had many cards with pictures of animals on them. One day, she found a new card with a picture of a big, noisy bird. Sue wanted to know what would happen if she showed the noisy bird card to her friends. She took the card to the park where her friends were playing. When Sue showed the card to her friends, they all laughed and made noisy bird sounds. As they played and made noises, a real noisy bird flew down from a tree. It looked just like the one on Sue's card! They all played together, laughing and making noises with the noisy bird. When it was time to go home, Sue put the card in her pocket, and the noisy bird flew back to its tree. Sue knew she would never forget the fun day that happened.
