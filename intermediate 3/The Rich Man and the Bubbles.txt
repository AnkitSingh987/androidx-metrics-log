Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rich Man and the Bubbles

Once upon a time, there was a rich man who lived in a big house. He liked to play with bubbles. Every day, he would go outside and blow many bubbles. The bubbles were big and shiny. He loved to watch them fly in the sky. One day, the rich man saw a little girl. She was trying to blow bubbles too. But her bubbles were small and not so pretty. The rich man wanted to help her. He gave her his special bubble wand. The girl was happy and said thank you. Together, they blew lots of big, shiny bubbles. They had fun and laughed. The rich man and the little girl became good friends. They liked to complete their bubble fun every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Milly. Milly had a very thick blanket to keep her warm at night. One day, Milly's mum told her that she had to go to the hospital for an operation. Milly was very scared and didn't want to go. Milly's mum said, "Milly, don't worry, it will be all right. You must receive this operation, it will help you. Come, let's go and see the doctor." Milly still wasn't sure and asked, "What will happen in the operation, mummy?" Mummy smiled and said, "Don't worry, it won't hurt. You will receive a small operation and then you will feel better. Now let's go!" So, Milly and her mummy went to the hospital and Milly received the operation. After the operation, Milly felt much better and was very happy. She even thanked the doctor with a hug! Milly returned home and snuggled up in her thick blanket. And she lived happily ever after!

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. It was a sunny day, but the grass was still wet from the rain. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He put his hands on the wet tree and started to climb. As Tim climbed higher, he saw a monkey sitting on a branch. The monkey looked at Tim and said, "Hi, I am Momo the monkey. Do you want to play with me?" Tim was happy to meet a new friend and said, "Yes, let's play together!" Tim and Momo played in the park all day. They jumped in the wet grass and splashed water on each other. They laughed and had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Goodbye, Momo. I hope we can play again soon!" Momo waved goodbye and climbed back up the tree. Tim went home with a big smile on his face, excited to tell his mom about his new friend.

One day, a little girl named Mia was feeling gloomy. She wanted to do something fun, but it was raining outside. Mia's mom saw her sad face and thought of a surprise to cheer her up. Mia's mom called her over and said, "Mia, do you want to learn how to make a stitch? We can make a fun toy together!" Mia's eyes lit up, and she said, "Yes, please!" They found some soft cloth and a needle. Mia's mom showed her how to make a stitch. They stitched and stitched until they made a cute little toy. Mia was so happy with her surprise, and her gloomy day turned into a fun one.

Tom and Anna like to play pretend. They have a stage in their room, made of chairs and a blanket. They dress up in funny clothes and act out stories. Sometimes they are pirates, sometimes they are animals, sometimes they are superheroes. One day, they decide to put on a show for their mom and dad. They make tickets and posters and invite them to watch. They are very excited and practice their lines and moves. Tom is a dragon and Anna is a princess. They have a sword and a crown and a toy fire. When mom and dad come to see the show, they sit on the floor and clap. Tom and Anna go on the stage and start their show. Tom roars and breathes fire. Anna screams and runs away. They chase each other around the stage and say their lines. But then, something bad happens. Tom trips over the blanket and falls down. He hurts his knee and starts to cry. Anna stops and runs to him. She hugs him and says, "Are you okay, Tom? I'm sorry you fell." Mom and dad get up and go to the stage. They help Tom up and check his knee. They say, "Don't worry, Tom. It's just a little scratch. You were very brave and did a great job. We loved your show. You and Anna are very good actors." Tom stops crying and smiles. He says, "Thank you, mom and dad. I'm glad you liked our show. Anna, you were a very good princess. Do you want to play some more?" Anna nods and says, "Yes, Tom. Let's play some more. But first, let's fix the stage. We don't want to trip again." They fix the stage and hug mom and dad. Then they go back to their show and display their skills. They have fun and make mom and dad laugh. They are happy and proud. They love to play pretend.
