Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy and the Bell

Once there was a very tough girl called Lucy. She loved playing outside so much and had lots of friends. One day, some of her friends had a secret to tell her. They gave her a note and asked her to read it. Lucy opened the note and found out that her favourite teacher, Miss Bell, was leaving the school. Lucy couldnt believe it and started to cry. She knew that her teacher was so kind and she would really miss her. Lucy's friends tried to console her but nothing could stop her tears. She was so sad and felt really lonely. When all hope seemed lost, Miss Bell came to the school to say goodbye. But when Lucy saw her, she burst into tears again and ran away. Miss Bell was so sad that she couldn't say goodbye to her best student. She waved sadly and walked away. And so, with a heavy heart, Lucy had to say goodbye to her favourite teacher. It was a tough moment indeed.

Once upon a time, there was a cute dog named True. True was a very good dog who loved to play all day. True had many friends in the park, and they would run and jump together. One day, True's owner gave him a big gift. True was so happy to receive the gift. He opened the gift and found a yummy bone inside. True took the bone to the park to show all his friends. True was so proud of his bone that he didn't see the big hole in the ground. He fell into the hole and couldn't get out. His friends tried to help, but they couldn't. True was sad and stuck in the hole, and his cute bone was left all alone.

One day, a big bear and a little duck went to a lake. They liked to play near the water. The lake was cold, but they did not care. They splashed and laughed all day. As the sun went down, the air got bitter. The little duck was cold. The big bear saw the little duck shiver. He wanted to help his friend. The big bear gave the little duck a big hug. The hug was warm and soft. The little duck felt better. They walked home together, happy and warm.

One day, Tom was eager to play with his new toy, a big computer. He was very happy because he could play fun games on it. He wanted to show his friend, Tim, how cool it was. Tim came over to play, and they both sat in front of the computer. Tom said, "Look, Tim! I can change the game on the computer. We can play many games." Tim was excited to see the different games. Tom pressed a button on the computer, and the game changed. They played a game with cars, and then they played a game with animals. After playing for a while, Tom's mom called them for a snack. They ate cookies and drank juice. They talked and laughed about the fun games they played on the computer. Tim said, "I want to come back and play more games with you, Tom!" Tom smiled and said, "We can play every day!"

Once upon a time, there was a noisy dog named Max. He loved to play and bark all day. Max had a big part in a play at the park. He was very excited. On the day of the play, Max's friend, a cat named Coco, came to watch. Coco brought a cup of cocoa to drink. While Max was on the stage, something unexpected happened. Coco spilled her cocoa! The cocoa made a big mess on the stage. Max was scared and did not know what to do. Then, Coco had an idea. She jumped on the stage and started to clean the cocoa with her tail. Everyone laughed and clapped. The play became even better with Coco on the stage. Max and Coco were now the stars of the show. They were very happy and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl. The owl had a plan. He wanted to help his friends in the forest win a medal. The wise owl told his friends, "Let's work together and do our best." They all agreed and began to practice for the big race. The animals ran and jumped every day. On the day of the race, the wise owl cheered for his friends. They all tried their best and won the race. The wise owl was so proud. The animals got a big medal and they all lived happily ever after.
