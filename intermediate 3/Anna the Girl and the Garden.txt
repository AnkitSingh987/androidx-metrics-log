Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a friendly girl named Anna. She loved to play and make new friends. One day, while playing in the garden, she found a tiny flea. The flea was lost and wanted to find a new home. Anna had an idea. She wanted to design a small house for the flea. She used sticks, leaves, and flowers to make the house. The flea was very happy with its new home. They played together every day and became best friends. The moral of the story is that even the smallest creatures can be our friends. We should be kind and help others when they need it.

One day, a cat named Tom found a tight ball of newspaper. He played with it all day, rolling it around the house. Tom loved his new toy. It was fun to chase and jump on. He showed it to his friend, a dog named Max. Max liked playing with the newspaper ball too. One sunny day, Tom and Max were playing with the newspaper ball in the yard. They pushed it too hard, and it rolled into the street. A big car was coming fast. Tom and Max were scared. They did not want their toy to be lost. Just then, a bird flew down from the sky. It picked up the newspaper ball in its beak and saved it from the car. The bird dropped the ball back in the yard. Tom and Max were so happy. They thanked the bird and kept playing with their toy. But the bird had left them a surprise. Inside the tight newspaper ball, there was a shiny coin. Tom and Max found it and knew they could use it to buy more fun toys. The bird had not only saved their toy but also gave them a special gift.

Once upon a time, there was a slow snail named Sam. Sam liked to move slowly all day. One day, Sam saw a big cobweb in the garden. In the cobweb, there was a small bug. The bug wanted to go away, but the cobweb was too strong. The bug tried and tried, but it could not go away. Sam wanted to help the bug. Sam thought and thought. Then, Sam had an idea. Sam told the bug to surrender and stop moving. The bug listened to Sam and stopped moving. The wind came and blew the cobweb away. The bug was free and happy. Sam and the bug became good friends.

Once upon a time, a unique little bird named Tim lived in a big tree. He had many friends in the forest. One day, Tim heard his friend, the wise old owl, say, "I have an exam tomorrow. I need to save my energy and rest." The next day, Tim saw that the forest was in trouble. A big, bad storm was coming. Tim knew he had to do something to save his home and friends. He thought about the wise old owl's exam and knew that he needed to help him too. Tim went to the wise old owl and said, "I will save your exam and our home!" He quickly gathered all his friends and they worked together to protect the forest. They built a wall to stop the wind and rain. The wise old owl was able to take his exam in peace. The storm passed and the forest was safe. The wise old owl said, "Thank you, Tim. You saved my exam and our home. You are a true hero!" Tim felt happy and proud that he could help his friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big blue tree in a small park. The tree was very different from the other trees. All the other trees were green, but this tree was blue. The blue tree felt very sad because it was so different. It didn't want to be blue anymore. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the park. He saw the blue tree and thought it was very cool. Tim decided to play near the tree. But some other kids came and started to laugh at Tim. They said, "Why are you playing near that weird blue tree?" Tim felt embarrassed and ran away. The blue tree saw Tim run away and felt bad. It didn't want Tim to be embarrassed because of it. So, the blue tree decided to do something special. The next day, when Tim came back to the park, the blue tree had beautiful blue flowers all over it. Tim and the other kids were amazed. They all played near the blue tree and had a great time. The blue tree was happy because it made Tim and the other kids happy too.
