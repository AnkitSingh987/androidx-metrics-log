Category: Intermediate
Title: The Igloo and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a cold, cold land, there was a big igloo. In the igloo lived a bald man named Bob. Bob liked to bow to everyone he met. He was very nice. One day, Bob went for a walk. He saw a big, round thing on the ground. It was very shiny. Bob did not know what it was. He bowed to the shiny thing and said "hello." Then, something unexpected happened. The shiny thing changed! It turned into a big, happy dog. The dog wagged its tail and licked Bob's face. Bob was very surprised. The dog was magic! Bob and the magic dog became best friends. They went back to the igloo and played all day. From that day on, Bob and the magic dog lived happily ever after.

Once, there was a girl named Sue. Sue was deaf, but she was very happy. She had a friend named Tom. Tom and Sue liked to play together. One day, Tom said, "Sue, when we grow up, let's have a marriage." Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Tom!" Tom and Sue grew up and they still wanted to have a marriage. But, Sue's deafness made it hard for her to talk to people. Tom had an idea. He said, "Sue, I will provide a special way for us to talk." Tom learned sign language. Now, they could talk even better. The day of the marriage came. Everyone was happy and excited. But, there was a surprise! When it was time for the marriage, a big, funny clown came out. The clown started to dance and make everyone laugh. Sue and Tom laughed too. They had a fun and happy marriage with their new clown friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his toy, a small rag. He would run around and shake the rag like crazy. One day, Max lost his rag. He looked everywhere for it, but he could not find it. Max was very sad. Max's friend, a bird named Sam, saw Max feeling sad. Sam asked, "Max, why are you so sad?" Max told Sam about his lost rag. Sam wanted to help Max. He urged Max to keep looking. "Don't worry, Max. We will find your rag," Sam said. Together, Max and Sam looked all around. They looked in the grass, under the trees, and behind the rocks. Finally, they found the rag near a big tree. Max was so happy! He knew that with Sam's help, they would find the rag. Max and Sam played with the rag for the rest of the day, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. He liked to roar very loud. One day, he met a nosy bird. The bird liked to ask many questions. The lion did not like this. The nosy bird had an aunt. The aunt was a wise old bird. She knew the lion did not like the questions. She told the nosy bird to be more quiet and listen. The nosy bird listened to her aunt. She stopped asking so many questions. The lion was happy. The nosy bird and the lion became good friends. They learned that it is good to listen and not always ask questions.

Once there lived a little boy who loved blue. Everywhere he went it seemed like blue was around him: blue birds, blue skies and blue rivers. One day he was walking along his favourite river when he saw something amazing. It was a giant blue bear standing in front of him! Immediately the little boy began to tremble with fear. "Don't worry," the bear said in a gentle voice. "I'm an amazing blue bear, but I mean you no harm. I just wanted to say hello." The little boy stared in disbelief at the gentle giant. He soon stopped trembling and said, "Hello, blue bear. I am so happy to meet you." The bear smiled and said, "It's nice to meet you too. We should be friends forever, don't you think?" The little boy smiled wide and said, "Yes! That would be amazing!" Together the little boy and the amazing blue bear began an unforgettable friendship.
