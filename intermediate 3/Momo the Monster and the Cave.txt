Category: Intermediate
Title: Momo the Monster and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a little monster named Momo. Momo lived in a cold cave with his family. Momo loved to play with his friends in the snow outside the cave. One day, Momo saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was cold and lost in the snow. Momo went to Lily and said, "Hi, I am Momo. You look cold. Do you need help?" Lily said, "Yes, I am cold and lost. Can you help me find my home?" Momo said, "Yes, I can help you." Momo and Lily walked through the snow to find her home. They talked and laughed as they walked. Soon, they found Lily's home. Lily was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Momo, for helping me." Momo said, "You're welcome. I am happy to help." They hugged and Momo went back to his cold cave, feeling warm inside because he helped his new friend.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a dull, gray rock by the water. He wanted to test how far he could throw the rock into the water. He picked up the rock and said, "I will throw you far, far away!" Tim threw the rock with all his might. But, the rock did not go far. Instead, it fell into the water with a big splash! Just then, a big fish jumped out of the water. The fish said, "Thank you for the rock! I needed a new friend to play with." Tim was very surprised. He did not know that fish could talk. He laughed and said, "You're welcome!" Tim and the fish became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a kind dog named Spot. Spot lived near a big bridge. He loved to play on the bridge. Every day Spot would remember to jump over the bridge. He'd have lots of fun. Then he would give sweet little barks for the other animals nearby to come and join him. Spot was so happy when he was playing on the bridge that he always remembered to be kind to everyone. He made sure no one was left out of the fun! One day, Spot remembered that his friend was feeling sad, so he took him to the bridge. Spot showed him how to play and be happy. His friend had lots of fun and was so happy again! Spot smiled, knowing he had been kind to his friend. He said goodbye and ran off to play again. Spot was the happiest dog when he was playing at the bridge.

Once upon a time, there was a lively dog named Max. Max loved to run and play all day. Max had a belt that he liked to wear. The belt was very special to Max because his best friend gave it to him. One day, Max saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her toy. Max wanted to help Lily find her toy. Max asked Lily, "Do you mind if I help you find your toy?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, please!" Max and Lily looked everywhere for the toy. Max used his belt to dig in the dirt. They looked under rocks and behind trees. Finally, Max found the toy under a big bush. Lily was so happy and thanked Max. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot liked to play in the big space outside his house. One day, Spot saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it. Spot ran to the ball and said, "Ball, let's play!" The ball didn't answer, but Spot didn't care. He pushed the ball with his nose and it rolled away. Spot chased the ball and it rolled faster and faster. All of a sudden, the ball stopped. Spot tried to stop too, but he was too clumsy. He bumped into the ball and it popped! Spot was sad, but then he saw something amazing. Inside the ball was a new toy! It was a soft, fluffy blanket. Spot picked it up with his mouth and ran back to his house. He used the blanket to cover his bed and snuggled in for a good night's sleep.
