Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Wealthy Man and the Pillow

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. Tom had a big house with many soft pillows. One day, Tom wanted to give a pillow to his friend, Sam. Tom asked a nice man named Bob to take the pillow to Sam. Bob said, "I will deliver the pillow for you." Tom was happy and gave Bob the pillow. Bob walked and walked until he found Sam. He gave Sam the pillow. Sam was very happy and said, "Thank you, Bob, for delivering the pillow!" They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. The mouse loved cheese very much. Every day, he would find cheese and eat it. The mouse was very happy. One day, the little mouse could not find any cheese. He looked and looked, but there was no cheese. The mouse felt helpless. He did not know what to do without cheese. The little mouse asked his friends for help. But they did not have any cheese either. The mouse was very sad. He kept looking for cheese, but he could not find any. In the end, the little mouse was still hungry and sad because he lost his cheese.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was very happy and loved to sleep. One day she woke up and saw a big hill in the distance. She wanted to climb it, so she ran and ran until she got to the top of the hill. At the top, she was so grateful to be able to see so far. But then, the hill started to shake and rumble. Oh no! It was an earthquake! The little girl tried to stay still, but the ground was shaking too hard. She got scared and fell back down the hill. When the earthquake was over, she was too hurt and tired to go back home. She lay down on the ground and tried to sleep, but all she could hear were loud noises in the distance. Sadly, she never made it home, and she had to sleep there forever.

Once upon a time there were two friends: Ben and Terry. Ben was a loyal dog and Terry was a wet cat. One day, they were walking together in the park. Ben wanted to play with a red ball, but it was too far away. Terry said: "Let me help you!" He used his wet paws and pushed the ball until it was near to Ben. Ben was very happy and said: "Thank you Terry, you are so loyal!" Terry gave a big purr. Then he said: "I'm glad I could help you to gain the ball!" Ben kept the ball and kept playing with it, while Terry watched him. They were both very happy.

One day, a big dog named Max went to the park. Max saw his friend, a little cat named Lily. They liked to play together. Max and Lily wanted to serve some food for their friends. Max said, "Let's serve food in one minute!" Lily agreed and they started to get the food ready. They put the food on a big plate. Their friends were happy and excited. But then, something unexpected happened! A big bird came and took the plate of food up in the sky. Max and Lily were sad. The bird came back and said, "I'm sorry, I was hungry too. Can I play with you?" Max and Lily said yes and they all played together in the park.

Tommy was a little mouse who lived in a big house with his family. He liked to explore the house and find new things to play with. One day, he found a shiny ball that rolled and bounced. He wanted to show it to his sister, Tina, who was in the kitchen. He ran across the hall, but he did not see the big cat that was sleeping on the sofa. The cat opened one eye and saw Tommy with the ball. He jumped off the sofa and chased Tommy, who was very scared. He dropped the ball and ran as fast as he could. He reached the kitchen and saw Tina, who was nibbling on a piece of cheese. He shouted, "Tina, help! The cat is after me!" Tina saw the cat and quickly hid behind a pot. She said, "Tommy, come here! We can hide together!" Tommy ran towards Tina, but the cat was faster. He pounced on Tommy and caught him with his paw. He lifted him up and said, "Ha! I got you, little mouse! You will be my snack today!" Tommy was very sad and afraid. He shivered in the cat's paw and said, "Please, don't eat me! I have a family and friends! I just wanted to play with the ball!" The cat was about to bite Tommy, when he heard a loud voice. It was the elderly lady who lived in the house. She saw the cat with Tommy and said, "Bad cat! Leave that mouse alone! He is not your toy! Drop him now!" The cat was surprised and scared. He dropped Tommy and ran away. The elderly lady picked up Tommy and said, "Poor little mouse! Are you hurt? Don't worry, I won't hurt you. I like mice. They are cute and smart. Come, I will take you to your family." She carried Tommy to the hole where his family lived. She put him down and said, "Here you go, little mouse. Be careful and stay away from the cat. He is very naughty and mean. Have a nice day!" Tommy was very happy and grateful. He thanked the elderly lady and hugged her. He said, "Thank you, kind lady! You saved my life! You are very nice and sweet. Have a nice day, too!" He ran inside the hole and found Tina, who was waiting for him. She was very happy to see him. She said, "Tommy, you are safe! I was so worried about you! Are you okay?" Tommy said, "I am okay, Tina. The cat almost ate me, but the lady saved me. She was very nice to me. She likes mice. She gave me back the ball. Do you want to play with it?" Tina said, "Yes, I do! But first, let's hug and say we love each other. You are my brother and my best friend. I am glad you are here with me." They hugged and said they loved each other. Then they played with the ball and had fun. They were happy and safe. They did not see the cat again. The end.
