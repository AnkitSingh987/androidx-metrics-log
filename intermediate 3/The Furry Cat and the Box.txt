Category: Intermediate
Title: The Furry Cat and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat. This cat loved to design. One day, the cat found a big box. The cat went under the box to design a new home. The furry cat worked hard. It made the box very pretty. The cat was happy with its new home under the box. Then, something unexpected happened. A small dog came to the box. The dog was also furry. The dog liked the cat's design. The cat and the dog became friends. They lived happily in the new home under the box. And they designed many more things together.

Once upon a time there were two friends. They lived in the same hall. They were both very young but felt very grown up. The two friends often shared things with one another. One day they started talking about what they had done that day. One of they said, "I went out to the park today. It was a lot of fun." The other friend said, "I'm afraid I couldn't go to the park today. It was too difficult for me." The first friend was sympathetic and said, "That's too bad. Maybe next time." Soon after, the two friends were walking home together. The other friend suddenly said, "It's so good to have a friend like you. You make life a lot easier." The first friend smiled and said, "It's nice to have a friend like you too. We both know that life can be difficult. So it's nice to be able to share our lives with each other."

Once upon a time, in a colorful house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to have tea parties with her stuffed animals. Today was a special day, so Lily wanted to celebrate with her friends. Lily set up a small table with colorful cups and plates. She filled the cups with tea and put cookies on the plates. She called her stuffed animals to the party. "Come, friends! Let's celebrate with tea and cookies!" said Lily. Her stuffed animals sat around the table. They all had a great time drinking tea and eating cookies. Lily laughed and talked to her friends. They all enjoyed the colorful tea party. And that's how Lily and her stuffed animals celebrated the special day.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat named Kitty. She had a happy face and loved to play. One day, Kitty saw a book on the floor. She wanted to learn how to read. Kitty went to her friend, Doggy. She said, "Doggy, can you help me read this book?" Doggy wagged his tail and said, "Yes, Kitty! I can help you read." They sat down together and began to read the book. As they read, Kitty's face became happier and happier. She liked learning new words. Doggy was proud of his friend. When they finished the book, they played together in the sun. Kitty was happy she could read and had a good friend like Doggy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside with his toy truck. One sunny day, Tom found a broken toy truck in the park. He wanted to fix it and make it new again. Tom took the broken truck home and asked his mom to help him fix it. His mom said, "Let's lift the truck up and see what we can do." They lifted the truck and found that it needed new wheels. Tom and his mom went to the store to buy new wheels for the broken truck. On their way home, they saw a field with oats growing. Tom asked his mom, "Can we take some oats to feed the birds?" His mom agreed, and they picked some oats. They took the oats and the new wheels home. Tom and his mom fixed the broken truck, and then they went outside to feed the birds with the oats. Tom was very happy, and he played with his trucks all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his rubber ball. The ball was light and easy to throw. Tim played with his ball every day. One day, it rained very hard. Tim was sad because he could not go outside to play with his ball. The rain stopped, but everything was wet. Tim waited for the sun to come out and dry the ground. When the ground was dry, Tim went outside to play with his rubber ball. He threw the ball high into the sky. The ball was so light, it flew very far. Tim laughed and had lots of fun playing with his ball.
