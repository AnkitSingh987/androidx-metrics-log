Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Garden

Sara and Ben are playing in the garden. They like to run and jump and laugh. They see many flowers and bugs and birds. They think the garden is beautiful. But then, they hear a buzz. A mosquito is flying near them. It wants to bite them and make them itch. Sara and Ben do not like mosquitoes. They are scared and angry. "Go away, mosquito!" Sara shouts. "You are not nice. You are not beautiful. You are mean and ugly." The mosquito does not listen. It flies closer and closer to Sara and Ben. It tries to land on their arms and legs and faces. Sara and Ben rush to the house. They want to be safe and warm. They want to find their mom and dad. They want to get rid of the mosquito. But the mosquito follows them. It is fast and sneaky. It slips through the door and into the house. "Help, help!" Sara and Ben cry. "There is a mosquito in the house. It wants to bite us. It wants to make us itch." Mom and Dad hear their cries. They come to the rescue. They see the mosquito and they swat it. They make it fall and stop buzzing. "Good job, Mom and Dad!" Sara and Ben cheer. "You saved us from the mosquito. You are brave and smart. You are nice and beautiful." Mom and Dad hug Sara and Ben. They are happy and proud. They say, "We love you, Sara and Ben. You are our beautiful children. You are safe and warm. You do not have to worry about the mosquito anymore."

Lily and Max were at the beach with their mom and dad. They liked to play in the sand and splash in the water. The sun was bright and the sky was clear. "Look, Lily, I made a big castle!" Max said, showing his sister his sand creation. "That's nice, Max, but mine is bigger and prettier!" Lily said, pointing to her own castle with shells and stones. "No, mine is better!" Max said, feeling angry. He ran over to Lily's castle and kicked it, making it fall apart. "Hey, stop that!" Lily shouted, trying to save her castle. Mom and dad heard them and came over. "What's going on here?" mom asked, looking at the mess. "Max ruined my castle!" Lily cried. "Why did you do that, Max?" dad asked, frowning. Max felt bad and embarrassed. He didn't know what to say. "I'm sorry, Lily. I was jealous of your castle. It was very pretty," he said, looking down. Lily felt sorry for him too. She knew he didn't mean to hurt her. "It's okay, Max. I forgive you. We can make a new castle together, if you want," she said, smiling. Max smiled back and nodded. "Thank you, Lily. You're a good sister. I'm sorry I was mean to you," he said, hugging her. Mom and dad were happy to see them make up. They helped them build a new castle, bigger and prettier than before. They had fun at the beach and forgot about their fight.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It made everything look happy. In a big forest, there were many trees. The trees had big, strong lumber. Many animals lived in the forest. One day, a little bird wanted to build a home. The bird asked a big bear for help. The bear was nice and wanted to help the bird. The bear said, "I suggest we use lumber from the trees to build your home." They worked together to build the bird's home. They used the lumber from the trees, and the home was strong. The bird was very happy. The bright sun shined on the new home, and the bird and bear were the best of friends.

Once there was a boy who was walking in the park. He was so happy because it was such a peaceful place. He stopped to look at a big billboard. Then he heard someone calling him. It was his friend, Jack. He wanted to show him something special. So they went to an old tree. Jack said he wanted to show the boy something very special. Then they both heard a noise coming from the tree. It sounded like a tiny chirp. When they looked closely they saw a bird in the tree. It was so tiny and the feathers were so soft. The bird was tweeting and the boy thought it was so peaceful. The two boys laughed and then went on their way. They were both so happy to have heard the bird tweeting in the tree.
