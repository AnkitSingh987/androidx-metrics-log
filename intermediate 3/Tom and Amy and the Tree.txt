Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Amy and the Tree

Tom and Amy were playing in the park. They liked to climb the big oak tree. It had many branches and leaves. They felt like birds in the sky. "Look, Amy, I can go higher than you!" Tom said. He moved to a thin branch near the top of the tree. He was not careful. He did not see the crack in the branch. "Tom, be careful! That branch is not strong!" Amy said. She was scared for Tom. She wanted him to come down. But Tom did not listen. He wanted to show off. He jumped on the branch. It made a loud snap. The branch broke. Tom fell down. He hit the ground with a thud. He did not get up. Amy screamed. She rushed down the tree. She ran to Tom. He was not moving. He was bleeding. He looked very hurt. Amy cried. She called for help. But no one was there. They were alone in the park. Amy wished they had not climbed the oak tree. She wished they had stayed on the ground. She wished Tom was okay. But he was not.

Once upon a time there were two adorable figures walking in the park. One figure was tall, the other was small. The small figure had a big smile and kept making jokes. The tall figure would smile and start to giggle. The small figure made a joke about a butterfly. The tall figure laughed and said, "Let's go fly a kite!" The small figure got very excited and said, "Yes! Let's do that!" The figures went to the kite field and saw the big blue sky. They played around with the kite until it flew up high. The figures laughed and joked with one another until it was time to go home. On the way back, the tall figure made a joke. The small figure laughed and said, "That was a funny one! But it's so nice out here, let's stay a bit longer." The tall figure thought for a moment before saying, "Let's go fly the kite again!" The small figure smiled and said, "Let's go!" The figures flew the kite again, and laughed and joked until the sun set and it was time to go home. As they walked home, the tall figure said, "That was so much fun! We should do it again soon!" The small figure smiled and said, "I was thinking the same thing! How adorable!"

One day, a clumsy dolphin was playing in the sea. He liked to jump and splash. He was very happy. But sometimes, he was not careful. The clumsy dolphin saw a big fish. He wanted to play with the fish. He swam fast to the fish and said, "Hi, big fish! Let's play together!" The big fish looked at the clumsy dolphin and said, "Okay, but be careful not to slap me with your tail." They played and had fun. But the clumsy dolphin forgot to be careful. He slapped the big fish with his tail. The big fish was sad and said, "Ouch! That hurt!" The clumsy dolphin felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, big fish. I will be more careful next time." The big fish forgave the clumsy dolphin, and they continued to play. The clumsy dolphin tried to be more careful, and they had a great time together.

Once upon a time, there was a small town with many happy animals. In this town, there was a law that everyone had to be patient and kind. If someone was not patient, they had to talk to the wise owl who would help them. One day, a little rabbit named Bobby was playing with his friends. Bobby was not very patient that day. He did not want to wait for his turn to play with the ball. He began to complain and cry. His friends did not like this and told him to remember the law. Bobby knew he had to be patient, so he went to see the wise owl for help. The owl listened to Bobby's story and told him a secret. The secret was that if Bobby could be patient for one whole day, he would find a big surprise. Bobby was excited and decided to try his best. The next day, Bobby was very patient. He waited for his turn to play and did not complain at all. At the end of the day, his friends took him to a big tree. Under the tree was a big box filled with toys! Bobby was so happy and thanked his friends for the surprise. From that day on, Bobby always remembered the law and was patient with everyone. He learned that being patient and kind made everyone happy, and the town was a better place because of it.
