Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rich Man and the Lunch

Once upon a time there was a rich man. He was very happy with his lunch every day. One day, he was walking to his lunch spot when something terrible happened! A big thunderstorm came and destroyed his lunch. The man was so sad. He had nothing to eat for lunch. He sat down in the rain and started to cry. Suddenly, a little girl appeared and handed him a delicious sandwich. She said, “This is for you. Have a nice lunch!” The man was so happy and thanked the girl. He ate his lunch and felt so happy. He wanted to do something nice for the girl in return, so he gave her a big bag of sweets. They both smiled and went their separate ways. The man continued on his journey and never forgot that day. He learned a lesson about being kind and helping others in need. He was very glad he could do something for the little girl that had been so nice to him.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cat. The cat liked to play outside. One day, Mom did not permit the cat to go out. The cat was sad. The cat looked out the window. He saw a big box on the side of the house. The cat wanted to see the box. He wished Mom would let him go out. Later, Mom opened the door. The cat ran to the box. The box moved! The cat was scared. Then, a little orange kitten came out. The cat was happy. They played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat had a big anchor. The anchor was perfect. The boat liked to go fast in the water. It was a sunny day, and the boat was very happy. One day, the boat saw a small hole. The boat wanted to go through the hole. The boat thought it would fit. The boat tried very hard to go through the hole. The boat did not fit. The boat got stuck in the hole. The anchor fell off the boat. The boat was very sad. The boat could not go fast anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy who always helped his mom and dad. One day, his mom asked him to deliver a box to the closet. Tim took the box and walked to the closet. On his way, he met his friend, the cat. The cat said, "Meow, what do you have there?" Tim replied, "I am delivering this box to the closet for my mom." They went to the closet together. Tim opened the door and put the box inside. The cat said, "Good job, Tim!" Tim felt happy and proud because he helped his mom. They both went to play outside, and they had a fun day.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the grocery with his mom. They needed to buy food for their home. Tim liked going to the grocery because he could help his mom pick out the food. One day, while they were at the grocery, Tim saw a big black dog. The dog looked very nice and was wagging its tail. Tim wanted to say hello to the dog. So, he asked his mom, "Can I say hi to the black dog?" His mom said, "Yes, but first let me introduce you to the dog's owner." Tim's mom walked with him to the dog's owner, a kind lady. The lady said, "This is my dog, Luna. You can pet her, she is very friendly." Tim gently pet Luna and they became friends. Tim was very happy to meet Luna at the grocery.

One day, Tom and Sue went to play outside. They saw a big gate. The gate was bright and pretty. They wanted to know what was behind the gate. Tom and Sue went to ask Mom. They said, "Mom, can you tell us about the gate?" Mom said it was a special gate. She told them that it led to a fun park. Tom and Sue were happy. They went through the gate and saw the fun park. They played all day and had a great time. The bright gate was their new favorite place.
