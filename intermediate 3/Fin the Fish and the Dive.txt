Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Dive

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Fin. Fin loved to dive deep in the water. One day, Fin joined a class to learn new ways to dive. Fin felt anxious on the first day of class. He looked at the other fish and wondered if they were better at diving. The teacher, a big fish named Mr. Splash, saw Fin and smiled. "Don't worry, Fin. We will all learn together," he said. As the days went by, Fin learned many new ways to dive. He practiced with his new friends in the class. Soon, Fin was not anxious anymore. He was happy and loved diving more than ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small, humble house with her mom and dad. They had a tiny room where Lily loved to play with her toys. One day, Lily found a magic wand in her toy box. She thought it was a toy and waved it around. Suddenly, she said, "I wish my room would shrink!" And just like that, her room began to shrink! The room became smaller and smaller until it was the size of a dollhouse. Lily was scared and called out to her mom, "Mommy, help! My room is tiny!" Her mom came running and saw the small room. She knew what to do. She took the wand from Lily and said, "I wish the room would grow back to normal." The room grew back to its normal size, and Lily learned to be careful with her wishes.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very kind boy. He loved to help his mom and dad. One day, Tim's mom told him to shut the door after he came inside. She said it was important to keep the house safe. At lunch time, Tim saw that there was only one apple available. He knew his mom and dad liked apples too. Tim thought for a moment, and then he cut the apple into three parts. He gave one part to his mom, one part to his dad, and kept one part for himself. They all enjoyed their lunch together. The next day, Tim's mom and dad were very happy. They told Tim that he was a good boy for sharing the apple and for shutting the door. They said that it was important to share and to help keep the house safe. Tim felt proud and knew that he had done the right thing. And so, Tim learned that sharing and caring for others made everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot was always running into things and making a mess. One day, Spot was playing in the yard when he lost his favorite ball. He felt sad and began his search for the ball. Spot looked everywhere for his ball. He looked under the big tree, in the tall grass, and behind the little house. Spot started to hate the search because he could not find his ball. This made him very sad and upset. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down from the sky with Spot's ball in its beak. The bird had seen Spot's search and wanted to help. Spot was so happy and thanked the little bird. From that day on, Spot and the little bird became the best of friends, and Spot learned that even a clumsy dog can have a happy ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little player named Tim. Tim loved to play with his big red ball. He was very enthusiastic when he played with it. One day, Tim saw a big door. He wanted to see what was behind it. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "How do I unlock the door?" Sam said, "You need a key." Tim looked around for a key. He found a small, shiny key under a rock. Tim was very happy. Tim used the key to unlock the door. Behind the door, he found a big, green field. Tim and Sam played with the big red ball in the field all day. They had so much fun. The end.
