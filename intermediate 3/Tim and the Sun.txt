Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Sun

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big yellow ball in his yard. He was very happy and wanted to play with it. Tim saw his friend, Sam, and called out to him. "Sam, come play with my new yellow ball!" Sam ran over, excited to play too. As they played, the sun got hot in the sky. The yellow ball started to feel hot too. Tim and Sam wanted to test how hot the ball could get. They left the ball in the sun and went to get some water to drink. When they came back, they saw the yellow ball was getting bigger and bigger. It was so hot that it was starting to grow! Tim and Sam were scared. They tried to push the ball away, but it was too big and hot for them to touch. Suddenly, the yellow ball popped! It made a very loud noise. Tim and Sam were sad because they could not play with the ball anymore. They learned not to leave things in the hot sun for too long.

One day, a boy named Tim found a magic rock. He showed it to his friend Sue. Sue said, "Wow, it's so shiny! What can it do?" Tim said, "I don't know, let's find out!" They took the rock to the park. When they got there, they saw an angry cat stuck in a tree. The cat was meowing very loud. Tim held the magic rock and said, "Magic rock, please help the cat!" Suddenly, the rock cut the tree branch. The cat fell down, but it was okay. The angry cat was now happy and ran away. Tim and Sue were very surprised. They knew the magic rock could help them do good things. They decided to keep the rock and help others with its magic power.

One day, a pirate named Tim found a rare map. The map showed the way to a big red box. Tim wanted the box. He thought it had fun toys inside. So, he got on his boat and went to find it. On the way, Tim met a fish named Fin. Fin said, "I can help you find the big red box, but you have to give me some food." Tim said, "Okay, I will give you food if you help me." So, Tim and Fin went to find the box together. They found the big red box under the water. Tim was very happy. But when he tried to open it, the box did not open. Fin looked at the box and saw a small stick. He used his tail to snap the stick. The box opened! But instead of toys, the box had more food for Fin. Tim was sad, but Fin was happy. They both learned that sometimes, what you find is not what you thought it would be.

Once upon a time, there was an elephant. He was very mischievous and always behaved in a very messy way. One day, he was walking through the forest and came across a bush with juicy-looking strawberries. The elephant couldn't resist and started eating them all! When the elephant heard a voice behind him, he was startled. It was a little girl, with a stern expression. She said, "Why are you eating all the strawberries? That's not very nice." The elephant was a bit embarrassed and replied nervously, "I'm sorry. I just couldn't help it. I shouldn't have behaved in such a messy way." The little girl smiled. "It's okay", she said, "But next time, remember to be more behaved." The elephant was thankful for the girl's kindness and thanked her before he carried on his journey. From that day on, the elephant always tried his best to be more behaved when walking through the forest.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there lived a silly cat named Tom. Tom loved to play outside, even when it was very cold. One day, the weather was so cold that everything started to freeze. Tom saw his friend, a small bird named Sam. Sam said, "Tom, it's too cold to play outside. We should go inside our homes." But Tom didn't listen. He thought it was fun to play in the cold and didn't want to stop. As the day went on, it got colder and colder. Tom started to feel very cold too. He wanted to go home, but he couldn't move. He had played in the cold for too long and now he was frozen. Sam tried to help Tom, but it was too late. Tom had not listened to his friend and now he was frozen outside.
