Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Park

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They had a picnic with their mom. Mom made them sandwiches with cheese and ham. Anna liked cheese and ham, but Ben did not. He wanted peanut butter and jelly. "Can I have your sandwich, Anna?" Ben asked. "No, this is mine. You have yours," Anna said. "But I don't like cheese and ham. They are yucky," Ben said. "Too bad. You should have told mom before," Anna said. Ben was angry. He grabbed Anna's sandwich and threw it on the ground. Anna was sad. She loved her sandwich. She started to cry. "Ben, that was not nice. You made Anna cry. You should say sorry and share your sandwich with her," mom said. Ben felt bad. He saw Anna's sandwich was dirty and broken. He knew he was wrong. "I'm sorry, Anna. I was mean. Can you forgive me?" Ben said. Anna looked at Ben. She saw he was sorry. She wiped her tears. "OK, I forgive you, Ben. But don't do that again. Sandwiches are fragile. They can break easily," Anna said. "Thank you, Anna. You are a good sister. Here, you can have half of my sandwich. I will try cheese and ham. Maybe they are not so yucky," Ben said. Anna smiled. She took half of Ben's sandwich. She gave him half of hers. They hugged and ate their sandwiches. Mom was happy. She was proud of her children. They learned to forgive and share.

Once upon a time, in a small house, a girl named Lily lived with her mom. They loved to bake yummy treats together. One day, they decided to make some bread using dough. Lily's mom showed her how to make the dough smooth. They mixed and mixed until it was just right. Then, they put the dough in the oven to bake. While they waited, they played a fun game of hide and seek. As they played, they could hear the oven timer go "beep beep!" The bread was ready! They took the bread out of the oven and let it cool. It smelled so good! Lily and her mom enjoyed the warm, tasty bread together. They were so happy they baked the bread using the smooth dough.

Once upon a time, there were two animals - a big, bright horn and a tiny, gentle mouse. The mouse had very soft fur, and the horn was very long and strong. The mouse and the horn lived happily in the woods. Every day, the mouse and the horn would run and play all day with the other animals in the woods. The mouse and the horn were best friends. The mouse and the horn shared a very special love for each other. Every day, the mouse and the horn shared kindness and lots of joy with each other. One day, the mouse and the horn saw a very bright light in the sky. It was so magical that the mouse and the horn knew they would love it forever. The mouse and the horn loved the light and they knew it was a special sign of their friendship. The mouse and the horn laughed and hugged each other and looked up at the light. They were so happy that they could share the beauty of the bright light with each other. They would always love each other and the bright light in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a modest little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Remy and his friends decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. The race began, and Remy ran as fast as he could. But, Remy's friend, Tina the turtle, was not fast. She was sad because she could not run as fast as the other animals. Remy wanted to help his friend. Remy had an idea. He went to find Tina and discovered her crying under a tree. Remy told Tina that they could race together. They would run and walk, and it would be fun. Tina smiled and agreed. Remy and Tina raced together, and they had a great time. They laughed and cheered as they crossed the finish line. In the end, they both won, because they had fun and helped each other. And that is the best kind of race.
