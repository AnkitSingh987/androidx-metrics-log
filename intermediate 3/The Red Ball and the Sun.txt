Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Ball and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. It was very round and shiny. The red ball liked to play with the sun. The sun was its best friend. They played together every day. One day, the red ball wanted to take the sun to the park. The sun was happy to go with the red ball. They played on the swings and the slide. They had so much fun together. After playing all day, the red ball and the sun were tired. They said goodbye to the park and went home. The red ball and the sun were very happy they had a fun day together. They knew they would play together again soon.

One day, a mule named Momo was walking in the forest. Momo was sad because he had a broken leg. He saw a big tree with yummy fruit. Momo wanted to eat the fruit, but he could not reach it. A bird named Bree saw Momo and said, "Why are you sad, Momo?" Momo told Bree about his broken leg and the fruit. Bree said, "I can help you get the fruit. I will fly up and make the tree yield the fruit for you." Bree flew up and pushed the fruit. The fruit fell down, and Momo was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The fruit turned into a magic fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for helping me. I was stuck in the fruit. I will fix your broken leg, Momo." Momo's leg was fixed, and he was very happy. He and Bree became best friends and played in the forest every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a thin jacket that he did not like. He would always say, "I hate this jacket!" His mom would tell him to wear it, but Tim did not want to. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a cold day, and his mom said, "Tim, wear your jacket." Tim said, "No, I hate my jacket!" But his mom made him wear it anyway. At the park, Tim played with his friend, Sam. Sam said, "I like your jacket, Tim!" Tim looked at his jacket and thought, "Maybe it is not so bad." From that day on, Tim did not hate his jacket anymore.

One day, a little mole went out to play. He was glad to see the sun and feel the warm grass. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. But the mole was too small to climb the tree. He felt sad and sat down. A bird saw the mole and flew down. The bird said, "Why are you sad, little mole?" The mole said, "I want to climb the tree, but I am too small." The bird thought for a moment and had an idea. The bird said, "I can help you! I will point to the easy places to climb." The mole was glad and said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird pointed to the low branches, and the mole started to climb. With the bird's help, the mole climbed the tree and felt very happy. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, attractive dog named Spot. He loved to play and eat. One day, Spot found some popcorn on the floor. He was very happy because popcorn was his favorite. While Spot was eating the popcorn, he started to feel a tickle in his nose. He tried to hold it in, but he couldn't. Spot let out a big sneeze! The popcorn went flying all over the room. Spot's owner saw the mess and laughed. She helped Spot clean up the popcorn. After that, Spot was more careful when he ate his favorite treat. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a cute baby named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. Lily loved to play with her toys and give them big hugs. One sunny day, Lily went to the park with her mommy. She saw a soft, fluffy teddy bear on the ground. Lily picked up the teddy bear and gave it a big squeeze. She felt happy and smiled. Lily took the teddy bear home and showed it to her daddy. Her daddy said, "Wow, that's a very cute teddy bear!" Lily loved her new teddy bear and gave it a squeeze every day. They became the best of friends and had many fun days together.
