Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Pot

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the theater with her mom. They were very excited to see a play. The theater was big and had many chairs. But when they sat down, they noticed something smelly. Lucy looked around and saw a man with a big pot. He was stirring something inside it. The smell was coming from the pot! Lucy and her mom did not like the smelly pot. They wanted to enjoy the play, but the smell was making it hard. Lucy's mom went to the man and asked him to stop stirring the smelly pot. The man was sad but understood. He stopped stirring and the smell went away. Lucy and her mom were happy and enjoyed the play. The man found a new place to stir his pot, where it did not bother anyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to watch films on his regular TV. One day, he found a new film to watch. It was about a funny dog that could shake its body very fast. Tim was excited to watch the film. As Tim watched the film, the funny dog said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to play with me?" Tim looked around, surprised. He didn't know that the dog could talk to him. "Yes, I want to play with you!" Tim said, smiling big. The funny dog jumped out of the TV and into Tim's room. They played and had fun, shaking and laughing together. Tim's mom came into the room and saw the funny dog. She was very surprised, too! But she laughed and joined them. They all played and shook together, making it the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little pink bunny. The bunny liked to hop and play all day. One day, the bunny found a big, round skull. The skull was from a very old animal that lived a long time ago. The bunny wanted to help the skull relax. So, the bunny took the skull to a soft, green grass spot. The bunny and the skull sat down on the grass. They looked at the blue sky and the white clouds. They felt the warm sun on their faces. The skull started to feel more relaxed. Soon, the bunny and the skull became good friends. They played together every day. And every day, they would go back to the soft, green grass spot to relax. The bunny and the skull were very happy, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to her room. She saw her big bookcase full of books. Mia loved to look at the pictures in the books. She liked the pink book the most. It had a pretty pink cover with a picture of a cat on it. Mia took the pink book from the bookcase to read. She sat down and began to look at the pictures. Mia observed the cat in the book. It was a happy cat that played with a ball of yarn. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat in the book jumped out of the page and into Mia's room! The cat was now real and not just a picture in the book. Mia was very surprised. She played with the cat and they had lots of fun. When it was time for bed, Mia put the cat back in the pink book. The cat went back to being a picture. Mia knew she had a magic book and she couldn't wait to see what would happen the next time she read it.

Anna and Ben were playing on the beach. They liked to make sand castles and look for shells. They saw some big waves and ran to the water. They splashed and laughed. "Look, Ben, a jellyfish!" Anna said, pointing to a shiny thing in the water. "It is pink and pretty. Can I touch it?" "No, Anna, don't touch it!" Ben said. "Jellyfish can hurt you. They have stingy things. They are not nice." Anna was curious. She wanted to see the jellyfish closer. She took a step forward. But Ben grabbed her hand and pulled her back. "Reverse, Anna, reverse!" Ben said. "We have to go away from the jellyfish. They are not our friends. They are hidden dangers." Anna listened to Ben. She reversed and ran back to the sand. She was scared of the jellyfish. She hugged Ben and thanked him. "Thank you, Ben, for saving me from the jellyfish. You are my best friend. You are brave and smart." "You are welcome, Anna. I am glad you are safe. You are my best friend too. You are sweet and funny." They smiled and hugged. They decided to play with their sand castles and shells instead. They had fun on the beach. They stayed away from the jellyfish.
