Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Frog

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big, clean house. It was so pretty. Tim wanted to see what was inside. He opened the door and went in. The house had a lot of light, and it made him happy. In the house, Tim found a big red ball. He played with it and had fun. Then, he heard a loud shout. It was a girl named Sue. She was mad because Tim was playing with her ball. Tim said sorry and they started to play together. As they played, they heard a strange noise. They looked around and saw a big, green frog. The frog could talk! It said, "I am a magic frog. I can make wishes come true." Tim and Sue were surprised. They wished to be best friends forever. The magic frog made their wish come true, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a scared pony. He was scared of everything, from the tall grass waving in the wind to the tall trees standing proudly in the forest. The pony was so fearful he never wanted to leave his meadow. One day, the pony heard a voice rush through the meadow. It said, "Come on pony, you can be brave. You can explore the world and not be scared any more." The pony thought hard before he took a step forward. He was still scared, but he made his feet move. He soon found that the world was grand and wonderful and fun! He was no longer scared but happy. The moral of the story is that no matter how scared you are, if you just take a small step forward, the world around you can be amazing. Don't be fearful of what is new and exciting. Rush forward and find out how much you can learn!

One day, a little yellow bird was sitting on a tree. The bird loved to sing songs. She would sing all day long. Her songs made everyone happy. A small girl named Lily heard the bird's song. She walked to the tree and looked up. "Hello, bird," Lily said. "I like your song. Can we talk?" "Hello, Lily," the bird said. "I am glad you like my song. We can talk. What do you want to talk about?" Lily thought for a moment. "Can you teach me your song?" she asked. The bird was happy to help. She sang her song again, and Lily listened. Then, Lily tried to sing the song too. They sang the song together, and it was a happy day.

Once upon a time, there was a big quarrel between two animals. One was a fierce lion, and the other was a big elephant. They were both very mad at each other. The lion said, "I am the king of the jungle. You cannot stay here." The elephant said, "No, I am the biggest animal. I will stay." They fought and fought. They used their strong legs and big palms to hit each other. In the end, they both got hurt very badly. They could not move anymore. They were both very sad because they could not be friends. And they never talked to each other again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. It was an icy day. Tim saw a big hill and thought, "I want to go down the hill!" So, he started to walk up the hill. At the top of the hill, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Wait, Tim! We must prevent going down the hill too fast." Tim wondered why. Sue showed Tim a big rock at the bottom of the hill. They did not want to hit the rock. Tim and Sue thought of a plan. They found a big stick and pushed the rock away. Then, they went down the hill together, laughing and having fun. They were happy they prevented the danger and could play safely.

Once upon a time, there was a little orange bug named Buggy. Buggy lived in a big green tree. He had many friends, but his best friend was a bird named Birdy. They loved to play together. One sunny day, Buggy asked Birdy, "Can we play in the tree today?" Birdy said, "I will ask my mom if she permits." Birdy's mom said yes, so Buggy and Birdy played all day in the tree. As they played, Buggy and Birdy saw other bugs and birds. They all played together and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Buggy and Birdy were very happy. They knew they would always be best friends.
