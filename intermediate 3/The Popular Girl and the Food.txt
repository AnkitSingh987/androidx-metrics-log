Category: Intermediate
Title: The Popular Girl and the Food

Once there was a popular little girl. She was very cute and everyone loved her. One day she wanted to make a special tasty lunch. She ran to the store and bought some yummy food, like cheese and tomato. When she got home, she looked for something to cook on. Then she saw the grill. She thought it would be perfect. She put the things she bought on it and turned it on. She waited and watched as the food began to sharpen. When it was time, she took the food off the grill. It was very tasty. Everyone liked it. Everyone said it was the best lunch they had ever had. Even her mom said it was the best food ever made. From then on, the little girl was known for her delicious, sharpplans.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. She felt proud when she got to the top. From up high, she saw a bird. The bird said, "Hello! Can you repeat after me?" Lily nodded her head. The bird sang a pretty song, and Lily tried to repeat it. She sang and sang, but the song did not sound the same. Lily felt sad. The bird saw her face and said, "Don't worry. Let's try again." They tried many times, but the song still did not sound the same. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Lily's hat off her head. The bird flew down and caught the hat. Lily felt wonder at what the bird did. The bird said, "You may not sing like me, but you can do something else. Be proud of what you can do!" Lily smiled and climbed down the tree. She was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys in his room. One day, he found a big brush under his bed. He was very happy because he could use the brush to clean his toys. Tim's friend, Sue, came to his house to play. Tim showed Sue his big brush. Sue wanted to join Tim and help him clean the toys. They both started to clean the toys with the big brush. They had a lot of fun doing this together. But then, Tim accidentally hit Sue with the brush. Sue felt sad and started to cry. Tim felt sorry that he hurt his friend. He said sorry to Sue and gave her a hug. Sue forgave Tim and they continued to play and clean the toys with the big brush. They were happy friends again.

Mira and Sami were thirsty and tired. They had walked a long way in the hot sand. They wanted to find water and shade. They saw a green spot far away. They hoped it was an oasis. "Look, Mira, maybe there is water there!" Sami said. "Let's go, Sami, maybe we can drink and rest there!" Mira said. They ran towards the green spot. They were happy when they got closer. They saw palm trees, grass, flowers and a pond. It was an oasis. They drank from the pond and ate some dates from the trees. They felt better. They saw some birds and animals near the pond. They were friendly and curious. They came to say hello to Mira and Sami. Mira and Sami liked them. They played with them. They saw a monkey, a camel, a parrot and a lizard. The monkey was clever. He could do tricks and make funny noises. He made Mira and Sami laugh. He showed them how to dance. He moved his arms and legs and swung his tail. He invited Mira and Sami to dance with him. "Come on, Mira, come on, Sami, dance with me!" the monkey said. "OK, monkey, we will dance with you!" Mira and Sami said. They danced with the monkey. They had fun. The other animals watched them and clapped. They liked the music the parrot made. He sang and whistled. He copied the monkey's noises. He said hello to Mira and Sami. "Hello, Mira, hello, Sami, welcome to the oasis!" the parrot said. "Hello, parrot, thank you for the music!" Mira and Sami said. They stayed at the oasis for a while. They enjoyed the water, the shade, the food and the friends. They thanked the animals for their kindness. They said goodbye to them. They continued their journey. They were happy. They remembered the oasis and the dance.
