Category: Intermediate
Title: Buzzy the Beetle and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a little garden, there was a tiny beetle named Buzzy. Buzzy was a compassionate beetle who loved to help his friends. One sunny day, Buzzy saw a little ant who was lost and scared. "Hi, little ant! I am Buzzy the beetle. Why are you sad?" asked Buzzy. The little ant replied, "I can't find my way home. I am lost!" Buzzy wanted to help the little ant, so he said, "Don't worry, follow me. I will help you find your home." So, the little ant followed Buzzy through the garden. They walked past pretty flowers and green leaves. Buzzy was a good leader, and the little ant felt safe with him. Soon, they found the ant's home. "Thank you, Buzzy! You are a true friend," said the little ant with a big smile. Buzzy was happy to help and said, "You're welcome! Remember, friends are always there to help each other." From that day on, Buzzy and the little ant became best friends, and they played together in the garden every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little white dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the mud. One sunny day, he went to the park with his friend, a brown dog named Buddy. Spot saw a big mud puddle and said, "Buddy, let's play in the mud!" Buddy was happy and jumped into the mud with Spot. They played and played until they were both very dirty. Spot wanted to go home, but he needed a towel to clean off the mud. He went to his friend, a big white cat named Fluffy, and asked, "Can I borrow a towel, please?" Fluffy said yes and gave Spot a towel. Spot and Buddy cleaned off the mud and went home, tired but happy.

One day, a tall man named Jack went to the park. He liked to watch the birds and play with the kids. Jack was very nice and everyone liked him. One day, Jack found a bottle with a word he didn't know. It said "poison." He asked his friend, "What is this?" His friend said, "It is bad. It can make you very sick." Jack was scared and wanted to keep everyone safe. Jack had an idea. He took the poison far away from the park, so no one could get sick. As he did this, a little bird flew by and dropped a shiny stone in Jack's hand. It was a gift for keeping everyone safe. The bird and Jack became best friends, and they played in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was not always nice. Sometimes, he was terrible. He would take toys from other kids and not say sorry. One day, Tim went to the park to play. At the park, Tim saw a girl with a pretty toy car. He wanted the car, so he took it from her. The girl was sad and cried. Tim's mom saw what happened and said, "Tim, that is terrible! Give the car back and say sorry." Tim felt bad and gave the car back to the girl. He said, "I'm sorry." The girl smiled and said, "Thank you." Tim learned to be nice and not be terrible. From that day on, Tim would turn into a good boy and share his toys with others.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his dog, Max. They liked to see new things and play in the park. On their way, they saw a cable on the ground. Tim was curious and wanted to see what it was for. They followed the cable and found a big, red button at the end. Tim was excited and wanted to press the button. He thought it would be fun. So, he pressed the button and suddenly, a big cake appeared! Tim and Max were very surprised. Tim felt sorry for pressing the button, but he and Max decided to share the cake with their friends. They invited everyone to the park and had a big party. In the end, the cable and the button made everyone happy, and they all had a great time.
