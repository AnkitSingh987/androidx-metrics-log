Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Hill

One day, a little bird named Tim wanted to sing. He flew to the top of a big hill. He felt fit and strong. On the hill, he met a kind frog named Sam. "Hi, Sam!" said Tim. "I want to sing a song. Will you sing with me?" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! I love to sing too!" Tim and Sam sang a beautiful song together. Other animals heard them and came to the hill. They all sang together. They were happy and had fun. At the end of the day, Tim and Sam learned that sharing and being kind to others makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a good queen. She lived in a big castle. The queen had many friends. She liked to help people. One day, the queen wanted to make a big party for all her friends. She asked her helpers to get yummy food and fun toys. The queen was very happy. At the party, everyone had a good time. They ate the yummy food and played with the fun toys. The queen's friends said "Thank you!" The good queen smiled and said, "I am happy you had fun!"

One day, there was a modest way. It was a very special way because it could test all sorts. A big brother and little sister decided to try it. First, the big brother tried it. He stepped onto the way and the way began to move. The big brother was amazed and he whooped with joy! Then it was the little sister's turn. She stepped onto the modest way, and it moved too! She was delighted and grinned from ear to ear. The brother and sister spent the rest of the day testing the way. They tried all sorts of tricky moves, and soon they were both giggling. The brother and sister had great fun testing the way. And when the sun went down, they agreed that it was the best way any brother and sister ever had!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved the color orange. She had an orange dress, an orange ball, and even an orange cat. One day, she saw a big orange toy in a store. She wanted it so much. Lily went to her mom and said, "Mom, can I have the orange toy? It is so pretty!" Her mom looked at the toy and said, "I don't know, Lily. I need to know the price first." They both went to the store man and asked, "How much is the orange toy?" The store man looked at them and said, "The price is very high. It is too much for you." Lily and her mom were sad. They did not have enough money to buy the orange toy. They went back home with heavy hearts. That night, Lily could not sleep. She was thinking about the orange toy all the time. She wished she could have it. The next day, Lily and her mom went back to the store, hoping the price had gone down. But the store man said, "I am sorry, the price is still too high." Lily and her mom went home again, feeling very sad. They knew they could not buy the orange toy. Lily had to learn that sometimes, we cannot have everything we want. And that was the bad ending of the story.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy tube. It was a fragile tube, so he had to be very careful. One sunny day, Tim took his tube outside to play. As Tim was playing, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird saw the tube and thought it looked tasty. The bird picked up the tube with its beak and started to eat it. Tim was sad to see his fragile tube being eaten by the big bird. But then, something unexpected happened. The big bird didn't like the taste of the tube. It spat the tube out and flew away. Tim was happy that his fragile tube was not eaten. He picked up his tube and went back to playing, grateful for the unexpected twist that saved his toy.
