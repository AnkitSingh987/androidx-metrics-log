Category: Intermediate
Title: The Blueberry and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a big blueberry. It was round and tasty. The blueberry lived in a green field with many friends. One day, it heard a loud noise. It was a car driving near the field. The car stopped and a small girl came out. She saw the big blueberry and wanted to take it home. So, she picked it up and put it in her car. The blueberry was happy to go with the girl. They drove to the girl's house. When they got there, the girl showed the blueberry her room. It was full of toys and colors. The girl and the blueberry played all day. They had lots of fun and became best friends.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Max lived in a small house. Max had a big lawn where he loved to play. He liked to run and jump on the grass every day. Max had a friend named Ben, who was a small bird. Ben lived in a tree near Max's lawn. One day, the sun was very harsh. Max wanted to play on the lawn, but the sun made the grass hot. Max's mom told him to mind the hot grass. She said, "Max, be careful. The sun is harsh today. Mind the hot grass when you play." Max and Ben still wanted to play on the lawn. They had an idea. They played under the tree where Ben lived. The tree gave them shade from the harsh sun. Max and Ben played all day. They had a lot of fun and they did not mind the hot grass.

Once upon a time, there were twin dogs named Tom and Tim. They liked to play and build with their toys. One day, they decided to build a big tower with their blocks. Tom and Tim were very noisy when they played, but they had so much fun together. While they were building, a little bird flew into their room. The bird was scared of the noisy twins and said, "Please stop being so loud! I can't find my way home." The twins wanted to help the bird but didn't know how. Then, Tom had an idea. They could build a big tower to help the bird see its home. The twins worked together, and this time, they were quiet. They built the tallest tower they could. The bird flew to the top and saw its home. The bird said, "Thank you, Tom and Tim!" The bird flew home, and the twins were happy they could help.

One day, a little girl named Sally was very excited. She woke up early and ran to her mom. "Mommy, let's go to the park today!" she said. Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can go after breakfast." At the park, Sally saw a big tree. She wanted to climb it. She dared herself to go up one branch. "Look, Mommy! I can climb!" she shouted. Her mom clapped and said, "Be careful, Sally!" Sally climbed higher and higher. She felt so excited that she dared herself to go even higher. But then, she heard her mom say, "That's high enough, Sally. Come back down now." Sally listened to her mom and climbed back down. They had a fun day at the park, and Sally was happy that she dared to climb the big tree.

Ringo the rabbit was hopping around in the meadow when he noticed something different. He squinted his eyes and saw that there was a lot of dust covering everything in the meadow. Ringo hopped closer and the dust made him sneeze. He scrubbed his nose and noticed that everything was very dirty. Ringo started to question where all the dust and dirt had come from. He hopped around looking for an answer. Suddenly, he saw a lion cub with a box of sparkles. Ringo asked the lion cub, "Where did the dust and dirt come from?" The lion cub smiled, "I was playing with my sparkles, and the wind blew them everywhere. Now everything is dirty." Ringo thought that was a good answer. He thanked the lion cub and hopped off to find an adventure elsewhere.
