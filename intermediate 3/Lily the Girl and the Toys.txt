Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys and dance. She was very graceful when she danced. Her mom would watch her and clap her hands. One day, Lily found her mom's makeup. She wanted to look pretty like her mom. So, she put some on her face. But she made a big mess. There was makeup all over her face and clothes. Lily's mom saw the mess and said, "Lily, you must remain here and let me clean you up." Lily's mom helped her wash her face and change her clothes. Then, Lily danced again, feeling happy and graceful.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to borrow a toy car from his friend Sam. Tim went to Sam's house and asked, "Can I borrow your toy car, please?" Sam said, "Yes, you can borrow it, but please be careful." While Tim was playing with the car outside, a thick cat came by. The cat saw the toy car and thought it was a real mouse. The cat tried to catch the car, but Tim was scared the cat would break it. Just then, a nice cop walked by and saw what was happening. The cop shooed the thick cat away and said to Tim, "Don't worry, the cat is gone now. You can play with the toy car safely." Tim thanked the cop and continued to play with the toy car. When it was time to go home, he returned the car to Sam and told him about the thick cat and the kind cop. They both laughed and were happy that the toy car was safe.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw a big, red strawberry. He wanted to eat it. His mom said he could have it, but they had to pay first. Tim was very happy. He held the strawberry tight. He wanted to wrap it up so it would not get dirty. He found a soft cloth and wrapped the strawberry in it. Tim was a persistent boy. He did not want to let go of his wrapped strawberry. But something unexpected happened. A dog ran into the store. The dog saw the wrapped strawberry and wanted it too. The dog jumped on Tim, grabbed the strawberry, and ran away. Tim was very sad. He lost his favorite treat, and the dog got it instead.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom had a big, soft carpet in his house. He loved to sit on the carpet and read books. One day, Tom saw the carpet move. He was very surprised. Tom looked closer and saw a little mouse under the carpet. The mouse was scared and wanted to hide. Tom felt bad for the little mouse. He decided to help the mouse find a new home. Tom and the mouse went outside. They looked for a new home for the mouse. Then, something unexpected happened. The little mouse found a small hat. The mouse gave the hat to Tom. Tom put the hat on his head, and his bald head was covered. Tom was very happy, and the mouse found a new home. They became good friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy old cat named Mr. Whiskers. He lived in a small house with a big yard. Mr. Whiskers liked to catch fast bugs in the yard. One sunny day, Mr. Whiskers saw a very fast bug. It had a lot of speed. He said, "I want to catch you, fast bug!" The fast bug said, "You can't catch me, grumpy cat!" Mr. Whiskers tried and tried to catch the fast bug, but it was too fast for him. Then, a little girl named Lily came to play in the yard. She saw Mr. Whiskers trying to catch the fast bug. Lily said, "Let me help you, Mr. Whiskers!" Together, they chased the fast bug. With Lily's help, Mr. Whiskers was able to catch the fast bug. They both laughed and played, and Mr. Whiskers was not grumpy anymore.
