Category: Intermediate
Title: Jim the Boy and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jim. Jim had an amazing garden filled with tasty vegetables and fruits. Every day Jim would go into his garden and pick the tasty vegetables. One day, while he was in the garden, a magical fairy appeared. She said to Jim, "I can help heal the world." Jim got very excited and asked the fairy how. The fairy said with a smile, "If you take these tasty vegetables to the people in the jail, they will know that you care. And when they know that the world is full of love, they will begin to heal." Jim was so excited. He collected all the tasty vegetables and before he knew it he was on his way to the jail. When he arrived, the prisoners were so happy to get the vegetables. One of them thanked Jim and said, "It's so nice to have people care about us. This is really going to help heal our hearts." Jim was so happy. He had made a difference and people were healing from their sadness. And that's how Jim helped heal the world with his tasty vegetables!

Once upon a time, there was a reliable dog named Spot. Spot loved to store his toys in a big box. He had many toys, like balls and bones. Spot was very good at keeping his toys safe. One day, Spot heard a loud alarm. It was coming from the big red fire truck. Spot knew he had to help. So, he grabbed his favorite toy, a soft, squeaky bone, and ran outside. Spot saw the fire truck near a tree. A little cat was stuck in the tree. Spot knew what to do. He gave the cat his squeaky bone. The cat played with the bone and came down from the tree. Everyone was happy, and Spot was a hero.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a fearful dog, and he was scared of many things. One day, Max's mom said, "Max, we need to go to the store to buy some wire." Max was scared, but he wanted to be brave. So Max went with his mom to the store. When they got there, Max saw a big dog. Max was scared, but he said, "Hi, big dog. My name is Max." The big dog was nice and said, "Hi, Max. My name is Buddy. Don't be scared. I am a nice dog." Max felt better and they became friends. Max and his mom bought the wire and went home. Max was happy that he had been brave and made a new friend.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play in the garden. They had a big tree house where they kept their toys and books. One day, they saw an enormous bird flying over their house. It had bright feathers and a long tail. They wanted to see it better, so they ran to the tree house and climbed up the ladder. "Wow, look at that bird!" Tom said. "It is so pretty and big!" "I wonder what it is," Lily said. "Maybe it is a parrot or a peacock." They watched the bird for a while, until it landed on a branch near their tree house. They could hear it making loud noises. "Hello, hello, hello!" the bird said. Tom and Lily were surprised. The bird could talk! "Hello, bird!" Tom said. "What is your name?" "My name is Polly," the bird said. "I am looking for a meeting." "A meeting?" Lily asked. "What is a meeting?" "A meeting is when you talk to other birds and discuss things," Polly said. "I am new here and I want to make friends." Tom and Lily thought that sounded fun. They liked to talk and discuss things too. "Can we join your meeting?" Tom asked. "Sorry, no," Polly said. "The meeting is only for birds. You are not birds, you are children." Tom and Lily felt sad. They wanted to be friends with Polly, but she did not want them. "That is not fair," Lily said. "We are nice and we like birds. Why can't we join your meeting?" "Because you are different," Polly said. "You have no feathers, no wings, no beak. You do not belong with us." Tom and Lily did not like that answer. They thought Polly was rude and mean. "We are not different, we are special," Tom said. "We have hair, hands, teeth. We can do many things that birds can't." "Like what?" Polly asked. "Like reading, writing, drawing, singing, dancing," Lily said. "Those are silly things," Polly said. "Birds can fly, sing, build nests, find food. Those are important things." Tom and Lily and Polly started to argue. They did not agree on anything. They raised their voices and hurt each other's feelings. Soon, they heard other birds coming. They were Polly's friends. They saw Tom and Lily and Polly in the tree house. "Who are they?" one bird asked. "They are children," Polly said. "They wanted to join our meeting, but I said no." "Good," another bird said. "We do not like children. They are noisy and messy and scary." The other birds agreed. They flew around the tree house and made angry sounds. They tried to scare Tom and Lily away. Tom and Lily were scared. They did not like the other birds. They wanted to go home. "Let's go, Lily," Tom said. "We do not want to be here." They grabbed their toys and books and ran down the ladder. They ran to their house and closed the door. They did not want to see Polly or the other birds again. They were not nice. They did not want to be friends. Tom and Lily hugged each other. They were glad they had each other. They were twins and they loved each other. They decided to read a book and draw a picture. They did not need a meeting. They had each other.
