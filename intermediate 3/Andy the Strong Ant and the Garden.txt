Category: Intermediate
Title: Andy the Strong Ant and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a strong ant named Andy. Andy lived in a big garden with many flowers. His favorite flower was a pretty daisy. One sunny day, Andy saw that the daisy was sad. "Why are you sad, Daisy?" he asked. Daisy said, "I worry that the wind will blow me away." Andy wanted to help his friend Daisy. He thought and thought, and then he had an idea. "I will ask my ant friends to help you," he said. Andy and his ant friends worked together. They found sticks and put them around Daisy. Now, Daisy was safe from the wind. Daisy was so happy and said, "Thank you, Andy and friends!" Andy felt good that he could help his friend. They played together in the garden, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was an obedient girl who always listened to her mom and dad. She had a pretty crystal that she loved very much. The crystal was shiny and bright, and it made her happy. One day, Lily's mom told her, "Lily, it's time to bathe." Lily said, "Okay, Mommy." She went to the bathroom to bathe. She took her favorite crystal with her and played with it while she was in the bathtub. The crystal sparkled in the water, and Lily felt so happy. As Lily was bathing, she heard her dad calling her. "Lily, come here, please!" Lily quickly finished her bath and wrapped herself in a towel. She held her crystal tightly in her hand and went to see her dad. Her dad asked her, "Did you have a good bath, Lily?" She smiled and said, "Yes, Daddy! I played with my crystal in the water!" Her dad smiled back and said, "That's great! Now, let's get you dressed and ready for bed." And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play with her toys and her dog, Spot. One day, Amy saw a big, pretty rock. She wanted to keep it all to herself. Amy was being selfish. Amy's mom saw her and said, "Amy, you should share the rock with your brother, Tim. It's not good to remain selfish." Amy thought about it and decided to share the rock with Tim. They both played with the rock together and had lots of fun. As they played, a tiny bug came and said, "Thank you for sharing the rock. I am a magic bug, and I will give you a wish." Amy and Tim wished to become big, strong humans when they grew up. The bug granted their wish, and they knew that sharing was good. They promised never to be selfish again.

Lily and Tom were playing with a hoop in the park. They liked to roll the hoop and run after it. Sometimes they threw the hoop in the air and tried to catch it. A big dog saw them and wanted to play too. He ran towards them and barked. Lily and Tom were scared of the dog. They did not know if he was friendly or mean. "Go away, dog!" Tom shouted. He picked up the hoop and threw it at the dog. The dog dodged the hoop and jumped on Tom. He bit Tom's arm and made him cry. Lily wanted to save Tom. She ran to him and tried to pull the dog away. But the dog was too strong and angry. He bit Lily's leg and made her cry too. No one heard their cries. No one came to help them. The dog did not stop biting them. Lily and Tom were hurt and sad. They wished they had not played with the hoop. They wished they had stayed home.

One day, a fat cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a long chain on the ground. Tom was very curious and wanted to play with it. Tom said, "I will pull the chain and see what happens!" He pulled the chain and found a big, soft bed at the end. Tom was very tired from his walk, so he decided to take a nap on the bed. While Tom was napping, a little mouse named Jerry came by. He saw the fat cat sleeping and said, "Wake up, Tom! Let's play together!" Tom woke up from his nap, and they both played with the chain and had a fun day.
