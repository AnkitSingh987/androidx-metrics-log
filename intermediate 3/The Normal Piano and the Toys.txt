Category: Intermediate
Title: The Normal Piano and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a normal piano. It was in a big room with lots of toys. The piano was sad because it did not belong with the toys. One day, a little girl named Amy came into the room. She saw the piano and said, "Hello, piano! Do you want to play with me?" The piano was happy and played a pretty song for Amy. Amy played with the piano every day. Soon, the piano felt like it belonged with the toys. It was not sad anymore. The piano and Amy were very happy together.

One day, a humble ball named Bounce went for a walk. Bounce loved to bounce up and down, all around the town. As Bounce was bouncing, he saw a little alarm clock named Alarm sitting on the side of the road. Alarm looked sad. "Hi, Alarm! Why are you sad?" Bounce asked. Alarm looked up and said, "I can't make any noise. I am too quiet. No one can hear me when it's time to wake up." Bounce wanted to help Alarm. He had an idea. "I can bounce you up and down! Then, people will see you and wake up," Bounce said with a smile. Alarm liked the idea and agreed to try it. The next morning, Bounce and Alarm went to a house. Bounce started to bounce Alarm up and down. People saw Alarm and woke up. They were so happy that they could wake up on time. Alarm was happy too, and thanked Bounce for his help. From that day on, Bounce and Alarm became the best of friends, helping people wake up every morning.

Once upon a time, there was a small town. In the town, there was a big drain. People did not like the drain. It was not clear. It was full of dirt and trash. The town was not happy. One day, a kind man came to the town. He saw the dirty drain. He wanted to help. The kind man started to remove the dirt and trash. He worked very hard. Soon, the drain was clear. The town was happy. The people in the town learned a lesson. They learned to keep the drain clear. They helped each other to remove the trash. The town was clean and happy. The moral of the story is to always help keep things clean and work together.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little lake. In the lake, there lived a fish named Fin. Fin had a friend, a frog named Fred. They liked to play and swim together. One day, Fin and Fred found a big, yummy cake near the lake. "Let's split the cake!" said Fin. "Yes, let's share!" agreed Fred. So, Fin and Fred split the cake into two parts. They each had a part to eat and were very happy. A big bird saw Fin and Fred sharing the cake. The bird felt happy too. He flew down and said, "You both are very kind to share the cake. I want to be your friend too!" So, Fin, Fred, and the big bird became good friends. They played together every day, and they always shared their food. The moral of the story is: Sharing makes everyone happy, and it helps you make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird named Sunny. Sunny lived in a big tree with his family. He loved to sing and play with his friends all day long. One day, Sunny saw a new bird in the tree. The new bird was shy and did not talk much. Sunny's friends did not want to play with the new bird. They said he was too quiet and ignored him. Sunny felt bad for the new bird and decided to be his friend. They spent a minute getting to know each other and soon became best friends. As time went on, Sunny and the new bird played together every day. The other birds in the tree saw how much fun they were having and decided to be friends with the new bird too. The moral of the story is to be kind to others, even if they are different, because everyone needs a friend.
