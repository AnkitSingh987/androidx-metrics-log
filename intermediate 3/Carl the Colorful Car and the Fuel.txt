Category: Intermediate
Title: Carl the Colorful Car and the Fuel

Once upon a time, there was a colorful car named Carl. Carl loved to drive around with his friends. One day, Carl noticed he was low on fuel. He needed to buy more fuel so he could keep playing with his friends. Carl went to the fuel store with his friend, Timmy the Truck. Timmy said, "Carl, let's buy fuel together!" Carl was happy to have his friend's help. They went inside the store and bought the fuel they needed. After buying the fuel, Carl and Timmy went back to play with their friends. They were so happy to have full tanks of fuel. Now they could drive and play all day long with their colorful friends. And they all had a great time together.

One day there was a little girl named Jessica. She loved playing with glue and making things. One day she asked her mommy if she could have some glue so she could make something. Her mommy said yes and gave her some glue. Jessica was very happy. She started to make something but then her brother came into the room. He saw the glue and was jealous. He asked his mommy if he could have some glue too. Mommy smiled and said, "Of course you can have some glue." She gave him some glue and he started to make something too. Jessica saw this and said, "That's not fair! Why did you give him glue too?" Mommy looked at her and replied, "I gave him glue because I love both of you equally." Jessica was happy to hear this and smiled. She was no longer jealous and kept making her project with the glue.

Once upon a time, there was a wide wood. In the wood, there lived a big bear and a little bunny. They were good friends and liked to play together. One day, they wanted to play a new game. They decided to race to the end of the wood. They both got ready and started to run. The bear was fast, but the bunny was faster. The bunny got to the end of the wood first and won the race. The bear was not sad, because they both had fun. They played more games and had a great day in the wide wood. The end.

Mommy and Daddy had a baby. She was so little and thin. Mommy wanted to hug her, but Daddy refused. "No!" he said. Mommy was confused. "But why?" she asked. "I don't want a baby," Daddy said. He was angry. Mommy was sad. She tried to make Daddy see the baby's cute little face, but he still refused. Finally, Mommy and the baby had to leave. Every day, Mommy looked back at Daddy, hoping he would change his mind. But he never did. The baby grew up without her loving Daddy. And Mommy was left sad and alone.

Once upon a time, there was a wide cat named Tom. Tom found a laser toy in the park. He liked to play with the laser. It made him very happy. Tom had a friend named Jerry. Jerry was a little mouse. One day, Tom said to Jerry, "Look at my laser! Let's play with it." Jerry liked the laser too. They played with the laser in the sun. They got very hot and tired. They needed to find a place to rest. Tom and Jerry found a dry tree near a river. They sat under the tree to relax. But, the tree was very old. While they were playing with the laser, the tree fell down. Tom and Jerry could not run away. The tree fell on them, and they got hurt. The laser broke too. They were very sad.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Tom and Sue. Tom had a desk and Sue wanted to sit on it with him. Tom trusted Sue and felt comfortable having her join him. They sat side by side, their feet dangling over the edge of the desk. They talked, laughed, and played all day. They shared stories, secrets, and dreams. At the end of the day, they each lay down. Tom trusted Sue to take care of his desk while he slept. They were both so comfortable and safe. The next morning, Tom and Sue woke up to find that the desk was still right where they left it. Tom was very happy to see that Sue had trusted him by taking care of the desk. He thanked her and they hugged. Tom and Sue will always be great friends, trusting each other and finding comfort in their friendship.
