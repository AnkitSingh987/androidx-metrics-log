Category: Intermediate
Title: Sandy and the Ceiling

Sandy was an inquisitive 3 year old. She was exploring her house one day, when she came upon a room she had never seen before. It had a mysterious ceiling that made her wonder what secrets it held. Curious, Sandy asked her mom, "Mommy, what is this place?" Mommy replied,"This is a special place, Sandy. We are going to examine the ceiling together. Does that sound exciting?" "Yes!" Sandy replied happily. Together they stepped into the room and looked around. The walls were grey, but the ceiling was painted a bright blue. Sandy looked up and noticed strange symbols on the ceiling that made her gasp. Mommy smiled and said, "Let's examine the ceiling together. Who knows what mysteries we might find!" Sandy and Mommy took turns looking up at the ceiling and pointing out the various shapes, figures and colours that emerged from the mysterious ceiling. Sandy felt excited and happy to explore the mystery above her head. After a few minutes, Mommy suggested that it was time to go. Sandy reluctantly agreed, but she vowed to come back soon and explore this mysterious ceiling some more.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play with his friends. They were playing with a ball in the park. Tim saw a big dog that looked scary. He was afraid of the dog and thought it was a threat. Tim's mom saw him feeling scared. She was a compassionate mom, so she went to talk to him. "Tim, what's wrong?" she asked. Tim said, "I want to play, but the big dog scares me." Mom said, "Don't worry, the dog is nice. He won't hurt you." She held Tim's hand and they went to the dog. The dog wagged its tail and was friendly. Tim felt better and played with his friends and the dog all day.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow fish. The fish lived in the big sea. The fish liked to swim and play with his friends. One day, the yellow fish saw a big thing in the sea. It was not a fish. It was not a boat. The yellow fish did not mind. He wanted to see what it was. The big thing was a big, yellow hat! The hat could swim and talk. The yellow fish was happy. Now, he had a new friend to play with in the sea. The hat and the fish swam and played all day.

Lucy loved to visit her aunt. Her aunt had a big house with a garden and many toys. Lucy liked to play with her aunt's dog, Spot. Spot was adorable. He had soft fur and a wet nose. He liked to lick Lucy's face and run after balls. One day, Lucy and her aunt went to the park. They saw many children and animals. Lucy and Spot ran and jumped on the grass. They made new friends. Lucy shared her snacks with a girl named Mia. Mia had a doll with a pink dress. Lucy liked the doll. Mia liked Spot. "Can I hold your doll?" Lucy asked Mia. "Sure, you can. She is very nice. Her name is Lily," Mia said. "Can I pet your dog?" Mia asked Lucy. "Sure, you can. He is very friendly. His name is Spot," Lucy said. Lucy and Mia played with the doll and the dog. They had fun. They laughed and hugged. They were happy. But soon, it was time to go home. Lucy and Mia were sad. They did not want to part. "Bye, Mia. I hope I see you again," Lucy said. "Bye, Lucy. I hope so too," Mia said. They gave each other the doll and the dog. They waved goodbye. Lucy and her aunt walked back to the car. Spot followed them. Lucy held the doll in her arms. She smiled. "Did you have a good time, Lucy?" her aunt asked. "Yes, I did. I made a new friend. And I have a new doll. Thank you, auntie," Lucy said. "You're welcome, Lucy. I'm glad you're happy. You're adorable," her aunt said. They hugged and kissed. They drove home. Lucy fell asleep with the doll and the dog. She dreamed of Mia and the park. She was happy.
