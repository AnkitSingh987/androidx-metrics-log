Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and the Dad

Ben likes to follow his dad. His dad is a famous cook. He makes yummy food for many people. Ben wants to be a cook too. One day, Ben follows his dad to the big kitchen. He sees many pots and pans and spoons and knives. He sees a big oven with a knob. He wants to turn the knob and see what happens. He waits until his dad is busy. Then he sneaks to the oven and turns the knob. He hears a loud hiss and sees a big flame. He gets scared and runs away. His dad sees the flame and turns off the oven. He is angry and worried. He finds Ben and hugs him. He says, "Ben, you should not touch the oven. It is very hot and dangerous. You could get hurt. You have to listen to me and stay away from the oven." Ben feels sorry and says, "I'm sorry, dad. I wanted to be a cook like you. I wanted to turn the knob and make food." His dad smiles and says, "I understand, Ben. But you are too little to cook with the oven. You have to learn first. You can help me with other things, like washing the vegetables or stirring the soup. But you have to promise me to never touch the oven again." Ben nods and says, "I promise, dad. I love you." His dad says, "I love you too, Ben. Come on, let's go and make some yummy food together."

Once upon a time, there was a huge dog named Max. Max liked to play in the sun. One day, Max saw a small thing on the ground. It was a thermometer. Max picked it up with his mouth and carried it to his mom. "Mom, what is this?" Max asked. His mom looked at the thermometer and said, "That's a thermometer, Max. It tells us if it's hot or cold outside." The next day, Max went outside to play again. He took the thermometer with him. Suddenly, the thermometer started to shrink! Max was surprised and didn't know what to do. Max ran to his mom with the tiny thermometer. "Mom, look! The thermometer got small!" he said. His mom laughed and said, "Oh, Max, that's because it's a special shrinking thermometer. It gets small when it's very hot outside. Now we know it's a hot day!" Max and his mom had a fun day playing in the sun with their special shrinking thermometer. And from that day on, Max always knew when it was a hot day, thanks to his new friend, the thermometer.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a baby. The baby liked to play with toys and laugh. One day, the baby's mom had a surprise for the baby. She said, "I will reveal something fun for you!" The baby was very excited. The mom showed the baby a big box. The baby opened the box and found a new toy inside. It was a funny toy that made silly faces. The baby laughed and played with the toy all day. But then, the toy started to be rude. It made a face that was not nice. The baby did not like this. The baby told the toy to stop being rude. The toy listened and started to be nice again. The baby and the toy became good friends and played happily together.

One day, a blue bird named Billy was flying around. He saw a shelf near a big tree. The shelf was full of little toys. Billy liked the toys and wanted to play with them. He called his friend, Sally the squirrel. "Look at the toys on the shelf!" Billy said to Sally. They played with the toys and had lots of fun. After playing, they wanted to mail a letter to their friend, Tommy the turtle. They wrote the letter and put it in a box. They went to the mail place and gave the box to the mail man. But, the mail man said, "This is not a letter. This is one of the toys from the shelf!" Billy and Sally were surprised. They had put the toy in the box by mistake. They laughed and went back to the shelf to get the letter. They mailed the letter and Tommy the turtle got it. They all played together and had a great day.
