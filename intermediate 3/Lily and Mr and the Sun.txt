Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Mr and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant sun in the sky. Under the sun, a little girl named Lily and her friend, a pastry named Mr. Puff, were playing. They were very happy. Mr. Puff could talk, and he was Lily's best friend. One day, Lily and Mr. Puff went to the park. They played on the swings and the slide. Then they sat down to rest. Mr. Puff said, "Lily, I want to remain your friend forever!" Lily smiled and said, "Me too, Mr. Puff! We will always be friends." But then, a big wind came. It blew Mr. Puff away from Lily. She tried to catch him, but he was too far. Lily was very sad. She looked for Mr. Puff, but could not find him. In the end, Lily went home without her friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to wander around the big park near his home. One day, while he was wandering, he saw a deep well. Max was scared to go near the well, but he wanted to see what was inside. He walked closer and closer, until he could see the water at the bottom. Max thought the well was very big and deep. Just then, Max's friend, a little bird, came to help him. The bird told Max not to be scared, and they both walked away from the well. Max was happy his friend was there to help him, and they played in the park all day.

Once upon a time, there was a brave boy named Tim. He loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he found a big folder in his yard. Tim was very curious about what was inside the folder. He opened the folder and found a magic wand. Tim waved the wand and said, "Freeze!" Suddenly, everything around him stopped moving. The birds in the sky stood still, and the leaves on the trees did not move. Tim saw his friend, Sue, and said, "Look what I can do!" He waved the wand again and said, "Unfreeze!" Everything started moving again. Sue was amazed and said, "Wow, Tim, you are so brave to use the magic wand!" They played with the wand all day, having lots of fun with their new magic power.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk with her mom. They saw a big storm coming. Lily's mom said, "That looks like a fierce hurricane! Let's go home." On their way home, they saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The wind was making the tree shake. The cat was scared. Lily said, "Mom, we have to help the cat! The hurricane is coming!" Lily's mom had an idea. She found a long stick and told Lily to squeeze it tight. Lily squeezed the stick and reached up to the cat. The cat grabbed the stick and climbed down. Just as the cat reached the ground, the storm stopped. The fierce hurricane was gone! It was just a little rainstorm. Lily, her mom, and the cat were happy and safe. They all went home and became the best of friends.

Once there was a person who had a bell. The bell was very useful. It was helpful for waking up, getting people's attention, and making noise when the person was happy. One day, the person decided to bury the bell. They dug a hole in their garden and carefully placed the bell inside. They filled the hole with dirt and covered it with a big rock. The bell was now safe and hidden away. The person felt sad to not have the bell anymore, but they had an idea! They got some paper and a marker, and drew a little picture of the bell. They hung it up so they could still see it and remember the bell. After that, the person always remembered the bell and how useful it was. They knew it was safe in the ground.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to pretend he was a wild lion. He would run and jump in the grass, making a big roar. His friends would laugh and play with him. One day, Tim and his friends went to the park. They saw a little dog. The little dog was wild and wanted to play. Tim pretended to be a lion and roared at the dog. The little dog jumped and barked, happy to play with Tim. Tim and his friends played with the little wild dog all day. They ran, jumped, and pretended to be wild animals. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye to their new friend. Tim was happy that he got to pretend to be a wild lion and make a new friend.
