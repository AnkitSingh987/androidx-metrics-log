Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Swing

Tom and Lily went to the park with Mom. They saw a large swing that looked fun. Tom ran to the swing and sat on it. He asked Mom to push him. "Push me, Mom, push me!" he said. Mom pushed Tom and he went high in the air. He laughed and felt happy. Lily wanted to swing too. She walked to the swing and said to Tom, "Let me swing, Tom, let me swing!" Tom did not want to share. He said, "No, Lily, this is my swing. Go find another one." Lily felt sad and angry. She said, "That is not nice, Tom. You have to share. Mom, tell Tom to share!" Mom looked at Tom and said, "Tom, you have to share with your sister. She wants to swing too. You can take turns." Tom shrugged. He did not care. He said, "No, Mom, I don't want to share. I like this swing. It is large and fun." Mom frowned. She said, "Tom, that is not how we behave. If you don't share, you have to get off the swing and go to time out." Tom did not like time out. He knew Mom was serious. He sighed and got off the swing. He said, "Fine, Lily, you can swing. But only for a little bit." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good brother." She sat on the swing and asked Mom to push her. Mom pushed Lily and she went high in the air. She laughed and felt happy. Tom watched Lily and felt sorry. He realized he was mean to her. He said, "Mom, can I swing with Lily? Can we swing together?" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tom, you can swing with Lily. That is a good idea. You can swing together and have fun." Tom and Lily swung together and had fun. They shared the large swing and were happy. Mom was proud of them. She hugged them and said, "I love you, Tom and Lily. You are good children."

Once upon a time, there was a graceful dog named Max. He had a colorful collar and he was always happy. Every day he would go out, sniff around and have lots of fun. One day, Max was running and he noticed a squirrel. He chased it but soon he realized it was too far away and he couldn't catch it. He needed to stop, so he decided to sit under a tree and rest. Later, he heard people talking and he quickly realized they were his owners. His owners were saying, "We can't take Max with us anymore. We need to leave him here." Max was sad, he didn't want to be left alone. He started barking but they didn't listen. The next thing Max knew he felt something cold on his neck and realized it was the collar being taken away. His owners left him alone under the tree, and Max never saw them again. He stayed there for a long time and he was never the same. He was no longer graceful, just sad.

Once upon a time, there was a small planet. On this planet lived a tiny alien named Bobo. Bobo was green and had big eyes. He liked to blink his eyes a lot. One day, Bobo met a new friend, a blue alien named Lulu. Bobo said, "Hi, Lulu! I blink a lot. Do you blink too?" Lulu felt ashamed, because she did not know how to blink. She said, "No, I don't know how to blink." Bobo wanted to help his new friend. He said, "I will teach you how to blink, Lulu!" They practiced blinking together. As they blinked, something unexpected happened. The planet started to change color! The more they blinked, the more colors the planet changed. First, it turned red, then yellow, and then blue. Bobo and Lulu were very happy. They learned that when they blinked together, they could make their planet beautiful with many colors.

John and his mom were walking in a park on a sunny day. John saw an elderly man throwing a football. He asked his mom if he could try it too. His mom said yes and John ran over to the elderly man. John said to the man, "Hi, can I pull the football?" The elderly man smiled and said, "Sure, come here and I'll show you how to do it!" He taught John how to pull the football and soon John was throwing it as far as he could. John was so happy and he thanked the elderly man. The elderly man said, "It was my pleasure, young man! I hope you keep playing with that football." John and his mom waved goodbye to the elderly man. John promised himself he would come back to the park the next day and throw the football again.
