Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Fat Cat and the Box

One day, a fat cat named Tom found a big box. The box had a laser toy inside. Tom was very happy. He liked to play with the laser toy. It made a red dot that he could chase. Tom's friend, a little dog named Max, came over. Max saw the laser toy and wanted to play too. "Can I play with the laser toy?" Max asked. Tom said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played with the laser toy all day. They would fill the room with laughs and barks as they chased the red dot. They had so much fun. Tom and Max were very tired, but they were happy. They loved playing with the laser toy together.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Max. Max loved to play with his toy, a long cable. He would run and jump with the cable in his mouth. Max and the cable were best friends. One day, Max saw a big cat. The cat wanted Max's cable. Max did not want to give his cable to the cat. They fought over the cable. Max and the cat pulled the cable from both ends. Then, something unexpected happened. The cable broke in half! Max and the cat were very sad. They both loved the cable, but now it was gone. The fight had made them lose their favorite toy. In the end, Max and the cat learned that fighting was not good. They should have shared the cable and played together. But now, the cable was gone, and Max and the cat were still sad. The story had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red jacket that he loved very much. One day, it was cold outside, so he put on his red jacket to go play. While Tim was playing, he saw a big puddle. He thought it would be fun to jump in it. But when he jumped, he did a foolish thing. He slipped in the puddle and fell down. His red jacket got all wet and muddy. Tim was sad that his jacket was dirty. He went home to tell his mom. His mom helped him clean his red jacket, and it was good as new. Tim learned to be more careful when playing near puddles, so he wouldn't slip again.

Once upon a time, there was a new gorilla. He loved to slide in the playground. Everyday he went to the playground and the children loved to watch him. One day, a little girl called Lily walked up to the gorilla and said, "Hello, can I join you?" The gorilla smiled and said, "Yes, let's slide together." They had so much fun sliding around together. When it got dark, the girl said goodbye and the gorilla said, "Goodbye Lily! See you tomorrow!" The next day, Lily came back to the playground with all her new friends. Everyone had fun sliding around with the gorilla. The gorilla said, "Now this is what I call a fun day!"

One day, a playful dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big, red strawberry on the ground. Max was very happy and wanted to eat it. But, the strawberry was broken. Max was sad. Max thought, "I can repair the strawberry!" He looked around the park and found a small stick. Max used the stick to put the strawberry back together. It was not perfect, but Max was proud. Now, the strawberry was fixed. Max ate the strawberry and it was very yummy. Max felt happy and continued his playful walk in the park. He knew that he could repair things and make them better.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a brown pet. The pet was a dog. The dog liked to play with a ball. One day, the dog's friend, a little boy, came to play. The boy would throw the ball, and the dog would run and catch it. They would repeat this game many times. The dog and the boy were very happy. After playing the game, the boy and the brown pet would sit by a tree. They would eat snacks and share them. They loved to spend time together. And they would play with the ball every day.
