Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to clean. Every day, the bird would fly around and help his friends clean their homes. One day, the little bird was very excited because he was going to help his best friend, a big bear, clean his cave. As the little bird and the big bear were cleaning the cave, they found a shiny box. They were very surprised and excited to see what was inside. The big bear opened the box, and they found a beautiful, magic hat. The hat could make them fly even higher and faster than before. The little bird and the big bear decided to use the magic hat to help clean their friends' homes even better. They flew around, helping everyone and making their homes very clean. All their friends were so happy and thankful. In the end, the little bird and the big bear learned that sharing and helping others can bring happiness to everyone.

---

Tim and Sam were best friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big bucket near the slide. They ran to the bucket and looked inside. "Wow, look at this!" Tim said. He pulled out a shiny red toy car. "This is mine!" "No, it's mine!" Sam said. He grabbed the car from Tim. "I saw it first!" They started to fight over the car. They pulled and pushed and shouted. They did not see the big dog that came to the bucket. The dog was hungry and angry. It saw the car and wanted it too. The dog barked and jumped on Tim and Sam. It bit them and took the car. Tim and Sam cried and ran away. They were hurt and scared. They lost the car and the bucket. They also lost their friendship. The dog was happy. It had a powerful car and a big bucket. It played with the car and the bucket. It did not care about Tim and Sam. It did not have any friends.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with a big yard. He loved to play with his red ball and run around in the grass. One sunny day, Spot found a cable in the yard. He did not know what it was for. He decided to ask his friend, Birdy the bird. Spot and Birdy liked to talk and discuss things together. Spot said, "Birdy, what is this cable for?" Birdy looked at the cable and said, "I think it is used to connect things." Spot and Birdy talked more about the cable and how it could be used. In the end, they decided to leave the cable alone and play with the red ball instead. They had a fun day playing together in the yard.

One day, a small cat named Mimi went to play in the big park. Mimi saw a box near the sink. She wanted to unpack the box to see what was inside. She thought it was a fun game. Mimi pulled and pulled on the box. The box opened and out came a big, shiny ball. Mimi was so happy to see the ball. She played with the ball, rolling it and jumping on it. But then, something unexpected happened. The big, shiny ball popped! Mimi was sad. She didn't want the ball to pop. She went home and told her mom about the ball. Mimi's mom said, "It's okay. We can find a new ball to play with." Mimi was still sad, but she knew her mom would help her find a new ball to play with next time.

One day, a girl named Daisy went for a walk. Daisy liked to pick flowers and play outside. She saw a door in a tree. The door had a lock on it. Daisy wanted to see what was inside. Daisy tried to open the lock, but it was difficult. She pushed and pulled, but the lock did not open. Daisy felt sad. She wanted to know what was behind the door. Then, a kind bird flew down and helped Daisy. The bird had a key in its beak. It put the key in the lock and turned it. The door opened! Daisy smiled and thanked the bird. Inside the tree, Daisy found a beautiful garden. She played there all day.
