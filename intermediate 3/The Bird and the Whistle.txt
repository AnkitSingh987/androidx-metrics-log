Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird and the Whistle

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to whistle. His name was Billy. Billy lived in a big tree in the middle of nature. He had many friends who lived there too. They all liked to play and have fun together. One day, the wind blew very hard. It was so strong that it shook the trees. Billy and his friends were scared. They knew they had to find a safe place to hide. Billy thought of a plan. He told his friends to follow him. Billy led his friends to a small cave. They all went inside and felt safe. The wind blew and blew, but they were not scared anymore. After a while, the wind stopped. They all went back to their homes in the trees. Billy was happy that his plan worked. His friends thanked him for being so smart and brave. From that day on, Billy was known as the hero of the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong bear named Ben. Ben lived in a green forest with many trees. Ben had a big heart and liked to help his friends. One day, Ben found a little bird with a broken wing under a bush. The little bird was sad and scared. Ben wanted to help the bird, so he picked it up gently and carried it to his warm cave. In the cave, Ben took care of the bird every day. He gave it food and water, and soon the bird's wing started to heal. The little bird grew strong and was able to fly again. The bird was so happy and thanked Ben for his help. Ben and the little bird became best friends. They learned that when you help others, you make the world a better place. And that is how the strong bear and the little bird lived happily ever after, always helping each other and their friends in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a boy and a girl who liked to play all day. One day they decided to build a really big house. The girl found some wood and the boy found some rocks. Together they built a house that was so big, that it reached up to the sunset! They were so proud of their house. They made it extra special with a big, bright red door. When they opened the door, it was so funny to see what was inside! Everything was upside down, but they tried their best to fix it. The sun started to go down, so the children decided it was time to go home. But when they turned around, their house was gone! They searched and searched, but it had gone. The children were so sad that the funny house had gone. They had worked so hard to complete their big house, but now it was gone! There was nothing they could do. The sunset was beautiful, but the ending of their adventure was not very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to clap his hands. One day, Tim found a big pile of junk in his yard. He was worried because he did not know where it came from. Tim looked at the junk and saw a broken toy car, a ripped book, and a torn shirt. Tim saw his friend, Sam, walking by. "Sam, look at this junk!" said Tim. Sam looked at the junk and said, "That's a lot of junk, Tim. I don't know where it came from either." They were both worried. Tim and Sam tried to clean up the junk, but it was too much for them. They clapped their hands to call for help, but nobody came. The junk stayed in the yard and made them sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big race in a small town. All the kids were very excited. They all wanted to win the big trophy. There was a little boy named Tim who wanted to win the trophy too. On the day of the race, all the kids lined up to run. The race started and they all ran very fast. Tim was running as fast as he could, but he was not in the lead. Suddenly, the kid in front dropped the big trophy. Tim saw the trophy on the ground and stopped to pick it up. As Tim picked up the trophy, he felt ashamed because he didn't win it. But then, something unexpected happened. All the kids who ran past the trophy came back to help Tim. They all decided that winning wasn't everything and that helping each other was more important. In the end, they all shared the trophy and became the best of friends.
