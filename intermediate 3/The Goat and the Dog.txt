Category: Intermediate
Title: The Goat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little goat. This goat lived on a farm with many friends. The goat was very fragile and needed to be careful when playing with others. One day, the goat went to play with his friends. They were having so much fun! But then, the goat started to panic. He was scared because he saw a big dog coming near him. The goat's friends saw him panic and came to help. They stood around the fragile goat to keep him safe. The big dog just wanted to play too, so they all played together. The goat was happy and not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Lucky. Lucky loved to fly high in the sky. He had a best friend named Cord. Cord was a long, thin worm who lived in the ground. One day, Lucky and Cord wanted to play a fun game. Lucky had an idea. He would pick up Cord with his beak and fly high in the sky. Then, he would let Cord go and Cord would fall back to the ground. Cord thought it was a fun game, so they started to play. Lucky picked up Cord and started to fly high. But something unexpected happened. As Lucky let Cord go, Cord did not fall back to the ground. Instead, Cord started to fly too! It turned out that Cord was a magic worm. Now, Lucky and Cord could fly together in the sky and have even more fun. They were both very happy and played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a small panda. The panda lived in a big forest. He had many friends. He loved to play with them every day. One day, a fire started in the forest. The fire began to burn the trees. The small panda and his friends were scared. They did not know what to do. They needed to find a safe place. The small panda was very brave. He led his friends away from the fire. They found a safe place near a big lake. The fire could not burn there. The small panda and his friends were happy and safe. They played by the lake every day after that.

One day, a big bird had an egg. The bird was very proud of her egg. She sat on it to keep it warm. She wanted a baby bird to come out soon. One day, a cat saw the egg. The cat said, "I want to eat the egg!" The big bird said, "No, you cannot eat my egg!" The cat tried to get the egg, but the bird stopped the cat. The cat was not happy. The cat tried again to get the egg. This time, the cat stumbled on a rock. The cat fell down and went away. The big bird was happy. She knew her egg was safe. The baby bird will come out soon.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small, adorable bunny. This bunny loved to play with his friends. They would jump, run, and laugh all day long. One day, while the bunny was playing, he heard a loud "snap" sound. He looked around and saw a broken twig on the ground. The bunny was curious and wanted to find out what made the sound. He hopped and hopped until he found a big tree. Behind the tree, he found a squirrel. The squirrel said, "I'm sorry, I broke the twig." The adorable bunny and the squirrel became friends and played together in the forest every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and her old cat, Dusty. Dusty was an old cat with gray fur like dust. Lily loved to play with Dusty all day long. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, it's time to learn something new today." Lily was excited to learn. She asked her mom, "What will I learn today?" Her mom smiled and said, "You'll see soon, my dear." Lily and her mom went to the park. There, Lily saw a big red ball. Her mom said, "Today, you will learn to play catch with Dusty." Lily was happy and picked up the ball. She threw the ball to Dusty, and Dusty ran to catch it. They played catch for a long time, and Lily learned how to throw the ball just right. As they played, the sun began to set. Lily's mom said, "It's time to go home now, Lily." Lily and Dusty were tired but happy. They had a fun day, and Lily had learned something new. She knew that tomorrow would bring another exciting day of learning and playing with her old cat, Dusty.
