Category: Intermediate
Title: Clara the Girl and the Flowers

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Clara. She was very happy and always wanted to try new things. One day Clara decided to explore her backyard. When she stepped outside she saw a beautiful lawn full of flowers. She was so excited, she ran over and welcomed the sunny grass. The brown grass looked so soft and Clara just wanted to stretch out and relax. She laid down and felt the warmth of the sun and the gentle breeze. Clara smiled and said hello to each of the flowers. She ran around the lawn and made up a game. She laughed and played until it was time to go inside. Clara said good bye and thanked the lawn for being so nice. She was glad to have spent the day with her new friend, the brown lawn.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a silly cat named Tom. Tom loved to play and make everyone laugh. He had a best friend, a dog named Max. They played together every day, but they liked Tuesdays the most. On Tuesdays, they went to the park and played on the swings. One Tuesday, Tom wanted to try something new. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Max was worried, but Tom said, "Don't worry, I can do it!" Tom started to climb the tree. He went up and up, higher than ever before. Max watched from below, hoping his friend would be safe. Suddenly, Tom slipped and fell from the tree. Max ran to comfort him, but Tom was hurt. They couldn't play anymore, and Tom had to go to the doctor. The doctor said Tom needed to rest for a long time. Tom and Max were both sad. They learned that sometimes being silly can lead to trouble, and it's important to be careful when trying new things.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Amy. She was three years old and liked to play a lot. Amy often played with her friends, but sometimes she was selfish and wanted things just for herself. One day, Amy wanted to play with her new toy, but her mom said she had to do the laundry first. Amy was very angry and frustrated. She didn't want to do the laundry. But then she saw something special through the window: it was a little tree, and it was growing bigger and bigger. Amy loved watching the tree grow and forgot about the laundry. She spent hours looking and smiling at it. Finally, Amy decided to help her mom instead of being selfish. She did the laundry together and it made her feel so proud. And every day, she looked out of the window, growing happier and happier as she watched her little tree grow bigger and bigger.

Once upon a time, there was a big castle. The castle was very wide. A little boy named Tim lived in the castle. He loved to run and play in the big, wide rooms. One day, Tim was playing with his ball. He was running and laughing. But then, he did not see a toy on the floor. Tim stumbled on the toy and fell down. He was sad, but he did not cry. Tim's mom came and helped him up. She gave him a big hug. Tim felt better. He learned to look where he was going. Now, Tim plays in the wide castle and does not stumble anymore.

Lily was very happy. She had a new toy. It was a scale. She could put things on it and see how heavy they were. She liked to bring her toys and books and fruits and see which ones were heavier and which ones were lighter. She wanted to show her new toy to her friend Ben. She called him on the phone and asked him to come over. Ben said yes. He liked to play with Lily. He said he would bring his new toy too. He said it was a surprise. Lily waited for Ben. She put her scale on the table. She put her teddy bear on one side and her doll on the other side. The scale tipped to the teddy bear side. The teddy bear was heavier than the doll. Lily smiled. She wondered what Ben's new toy was. Ben came to Lily's house. He had a big box in his hands. He said hello to Lily and put the box on the floor. He said he wanted to see Lily's new toy first. Lily showed him her scale. She asked him to guess which side was heavier. Ben looked at the teddy bear and the doll. He said the teddy bear was heavier. Lily said he was right. She clapped her hands. Then Lily asked Ben to show her his new toy. Ben opened the box. He took out a big snake. It was rubber and had spots and stripes. It looked very real. Lily screamed. She was scared of snakes. She ran away from the table. The snake fell on the scale. The scale broke. The snake was too heavy for the scale. Ben was sorry. He said he did not know Lily was scared of snakes. He said he liked snakes. He said he wanted to make Lily laugh. He said he was sorry he broke her new toy. Lily was sad. She liked her new toy. She did not like snakes. She did not laugh. She cried.
