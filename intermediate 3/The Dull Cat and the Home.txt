Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dull Cat and the Home

Once upon a time, there was a dull cat. This cat had soft fur. One day, the cat wanted to decorate her home. She found many pretty things to put in her home. The cat was happy with her new home. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the pretty things away. The cat was sad. But then, the cat saw her friends. They all came to help her. They found new pretty things to decorate her home. The cat was happy again. And her home was even prettier than before.

Sara likes to play with her toys in the bathtub. She has a yellow duck, a blue fish and a red boat. She makes them swim and splash in the water. She also has a sponge that she can squeeze and make bubbles. One day, Sara's mom fills the bathtub with water for her. She says, "Sara, it's time for your bath. The water is nice and hot. Be careful not to burn yourself." Sara runs to the bathroom and jumps into the bathtub. She says, "Yay, bath time! I love bath time!" She grabs her sponge and squeezes it hard. But the water is too hot and it hurts her hand. She drops the sponge and says, "Ow, ow, ow! Mom, the water is too hot! It hurts!" Sara's mom hears her cry and comes to the bathroom. She says, "Oh, Sara, I'm sorry. The water is too hot for you. Let me add some cold water to make it better." She turns on the faucet and mixes the water until it is warm and not hot. Sara feels the water and says, "Thank you, mom. The water is good now. It does not hurt." She picks up her sponge and squeezes it again. She makes bubbles and smiles. She says, "I like bubbles. They are fun and soft. Can I stay in the bathtub for a long time?" Sara's mom says, "Yes, you can stay for a little while. But not too long, or you will get wrinkly. And don't forget to wash your hair and your body with soap. Then you will be clean and smell nice." Sara says, "OK, mom. I will wash myself. But first, I want to play with my toys. They are my friends. They like the bubbles too." She puts her duck, her fish and her boat in the water and makes them talk to each other. She has a lot of fun in the bathtub.

One day, little Sally wanted to go on an adventure. She opened the door of her house and started walking. She looked around and noticed something green and white. It looked like a big cauliflower! Sally was very excited. She reached out to touch it and found that it was very smooth. She was so happy and ran home to tell her mom and dad about her discovery. Sally showed her mom and dad the cauliflower and they were so amazed and happy. They decided to put it into a salad for supper. At supper, they all ate the delicious salad and talked about Sally's adventure. It was a wonderful night and they all agreed to go out on more walks and look out for something special like this!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a witch. She had an icy heart and seemed very mean. She lived in a dark house that no one liked to go near. All the kids in the town were scared of her. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her ball. The ball went into the witch's yard. Lily was scared, but she wanted her ball back. She went to the witch's house and knocked on the door. The witch opened the door and said, "What do you want?" Lily was shaking, but she said, "I am sorry, my ball is in your yard. Can I please have it back?" The witch looked at her and then smiled. She got the ball and gave it to Lily. The witch said, "I am not mean, I just seem that way because I am lonely. Do you want to be my friend?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I will be your friend." From that day on, the witch was not icy anymore and became friends with all the kids in the town.
