Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sun

One day, a little boy named Tim went to school. In his classroom, there was a big blackboard. His teacher, Miss Kate, wrote on it with chalk. Tim liked to draw on the blackboard too. But today, he felt foolish because he did not know what to draw. Tim's friend, Sam, said, "Let's draw a big sun on the blackboard!" Tim thought it was a good idea. So, they started to draw the sun together. They drew a big circle and many lines around it to make it look like a sun. They were happy with their drawing. But then, something unexpected happened. The sun they drew on the blackboard began to yield light! The whole classroom became bright and warm. All the other kids were amazed. Miss Kate said, "Wow, Tim and Sam, you made a magic sun!" Tim and Sam felt very proud and not foolish anymore. They learned that working together can make special things happen.

One day, a boy named Tim found a book. The book was unknown to him. It was big and had many pages. He wanted to know what was inside. So, he opened the book and started to read. While reading, Tim saw a cat. The cat was fast and tried to catch a ball. Tim wanted to help the cat. He said, "Cat, let's catch the ball together!" The cat looked at Tim and nodded. They played and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat started to talk! The cat said, "Thank you, Tim. The unknown book you found made me talk. I am a magic cat!" Tim was very surprised but happy. They became best friends and had many more adventures together.

---

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max liked to go on walks with his owner, Tim. Tim would put a leash on Max, and they would walk together. One day, Tim had an idea. He wanted to print a picture of Max. Tim took out his camera and said, "Max, sit! I want to take a picture of you!" Max sat, and Tim took a picture. Tim printed the picture and showed it to Max. Max looked at the picture and wagged his tail. He was happy to see himself in the big picture. Tim said, "Max, you look great in this picture! Let's go for a walk now." Max barked happily, and they went for a walk with the leash.

---

One day, a little girl named Mia found a mint plant in her garden. She touched the green leaves and smelled them. They had a nice smell. Mia wanted to taste the mint, so she picked a leaf and put it in her mouth. It tasted bitter, and she made a funny face. Mia ran to her friend Tom and said, "Tom, I found a green plant. It smells good but tastes bitter. Try it!" Tom looked at the mint leaf and wanted to taste it too. He put it in his mouth and made a funny face like Mia. "Yuck!" he said. Mia and Tom had an idea. They decided to release the bitter taste by making a sweet drink with the mint. They picked more leaves and mixed them with water and sugar. They shared the sweet mint drink with their friends, and everyone loved it. Mia and Tom were happy that they could turn something bitter into something sweet.

Once upon a time, there was a hero. This hero was humble and kind. He lived in a small house near the woods. Every day, he would jump and play with his animal friends. The hero loved to help others and make them happy. One day, the hero saw a big bird stuck in a tree. The bird cried, "Help me, please!" The hero wanted to help the bird, so he jumped and jumped, trying to reach it. But no matter how hard he tried, he could not reach the bird. Then, something unexpected happened. A small mouse came out of the woods. The mouse said, "I can help you, hero." The hero was surprised but let the mouse try. The mouse climbed the tree and helped the big bird get free. The bird thanked the mouse and the hero. The hero learned that even the smallest friends can do big things. From that day on, the hero and the mouse became best friends. They continued to help others and make the world a better place.
