Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and Tom and the Duck

Sam and Tom are friends. They like to play with cars and boats. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big pond with many ducks and fish. "Can we drive our cars and sail our boats in the pond?" Sam asks his mom. "No, Sam. The pond is for the ducks and the fish. You can drive your cars and sail your boats on the grass or the sand," his mom says. Sam and Tom nod. They find a nice spot on the grass. They drive their cars and sail their boats. They have fun. But then, they see a duck that is stuck in a plastic bag. The duck is scared and sad. It cannot swim or fly. "Oh no! The duck needs help!" Tom says. "Let's be helpful and save the duck!" Sam says. They run to the pond. They use their boats to get closer to the duck. They are careful not to scare the duck. They pull the plastic bag off the duck. The duck is free. It quacks happily and swims away. "Yay! We did it!" Sam and Tom cheer. Their moms see what they did. They are proud of them. "Good job, Sam and Tom! You are very helpful and kind. You saved the duck," their moms say. Sam and Tom smile. They hug their moms. They are happy. The moral of the story is: Be helpful and kind to animals. They need our care and respect.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Fred and Lucy. They were best friends and did everything together. They loved going on adventures. One day, Fred and Lucy decided to go for a picnic in the park. They packed a basket full of tasty snacks and headed to the park. They laughed and smiled as they touched the soft flowers and tall trees. The best part was when Fred and Lucy shared a delicious yummy apple. It was so tasty that they couldn't help but smile. They were so happy that they hugged each other and laughed. The day was so much fun that they couldn't stop smiling. They touched the soft grass and played tag until the sun started to set. Then they said goodbye until the next time they went on an adventure.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl named Gina. Gina went on a walk in the woods and found a magical tree. She got an idea to take some of the tree's leaves to make a special hat. Gina carefully picked some of the leaves and put them in her hat. She was so pleased with her idea, so she quickly ran home to show her mom. When she arrived, her mom asked where she got the leaves and Gina told her about the magical tree. Gina's mom was so angry. She said that taking things from the woods without asking was naughty and she told her to return the leaves to the tree. Gina felt guilty, but she knew her mom was right, so she put the leaves back. Gina walked back to the tree, feeling sad. But when she arrived, she noticed a big smile on the tree's face. The tree seemed happy that she had returned the leaves. Gina smiled and thanked the tree, feeling much better. She realized the importance of asking first before taking things and promised to always remember her good idea.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to design something on his computer. He loved to draw and make fun things. But Tim was sometimes careless and didn't always pay attention to what he was doing. One day, while Tim was busy designing, his little sister Sue came in the room. She wanted to play with Tim, but he didn't want to stop his work. Sue got upset and started to cry. Tim got mad and was not careful. He spilled his juice on the computer. The computer stopped working, and Tim was very sad. He learned that he should have been more careful and listened to his sister. Tim and Sue worked together to fix the computer, and they became better friends. They learned that it's important to be careful and help each other.
