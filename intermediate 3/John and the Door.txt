Title: John and the Door

John was a happy little boy. He loved exploring the great big jungle near his house. Every day he would make an adventure of it. One day, John noticed something else in the jungle that he had never seen before. He ventured closer and saw an old wooden door. It looked like it had been there for a long time. The door was flexible, so John put his hand on it and it opened! He walked inside and saw a big treasure chest! John thought he was in for a major treat, so he opened the lid. But then he heard a loud noise. It was a strange clicking sound and he realized the treasure chest had shut! John was stuck! He tried and tried to open the chest, but it wouldn't budge. He felt sad, but he was also a bit curious. What was inside the chest? Fortunately, John had heard one of his older siblings mention something about a special tool that could open chests like this one. He promised himself he would find the tool and come back to the jungle later. And that's just what he did. The next day he found the special tool and returned to the jungle. He used the tool to open the chest, and inside he found a secret! John was so excited, he never expected to find something so precious in the middle of the jungle. Tom and Mia are playing in the park. They like to run and jump and slide. They see a big hill with flowers and grass. They want to go up the hill and see what is on the other side. "Come on, Mia, let's go!" Tom says. He runs towards the hill. "Wait for me, Tom!" Mia says. She follows him, but she is slower. She sees a butterfly with bright colors. She stops to look at it. She likes butterflies. Tom reaches the top of the hill. He looks around. He sees a pond with ducks and frogs. He sees a tree with a swing. He sees a house with a fence. He wants to explore more. "Mia, where are you?" Tom calls. He does not see Mia. He pauses. He is worried. He does not want to lose Mia. Mia hears Tom's voice. She looks up. She sees Tom on the hill. She waves to him. "Sorry, Tom, I saw a butterfly!" Mia says. She runs to catch up with him. "It's okay, Mia, I found something cool!" Tom says. He shows her the pond and the tree and the house. They are happy. They like spring. They like to play.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a weak boy named Tim. Tim was not strong like the other kids, but he was very kind. He had a friend named Sam. Sam was strong and liked to play games. One day, they were playing with a ball. Tim and Sam wanted to pass the ball to each other. They were having fun and laughing. Then, a big wind came and took the ball away. The ball went into the woods. Tim and Sam were sad, but they knew they had to find the ball. They walked into the woods and found the ball near a big tree. Tim told Sam the truth about being weak, but Sam did not care. They passed the ball again and went back home. From that day on, they learned that being kind and telling the truth is more important than being strong. And they were always good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a brave girl. She liked to write on her paper. One day, her brother said he did not want to play. The brave girl was sad. But then, she had an idea. She asked her brother if he wanted to write together. She showed her brother her paper and asked him to write something. Suddenly, the brother smiled. The brave girl was glad and they began to write. Together, they painted pictures, wrote stories and made funny shapes. Soon, the brother and sister were playing a fight game. They both laughed and ran around the room, pretending to fight each other. At the end, the brave girl was happy. Her brother was happy too. Together, they had learnt to write, laugh and be brave friends.
