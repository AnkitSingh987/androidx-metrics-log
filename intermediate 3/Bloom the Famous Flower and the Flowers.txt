Category: Intermediate
Title: Bloom the Famous Flower and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a famous flower named Bloom. Bloom lived in a big garden with many other flowers. They all liked to play and have fun together. One day, Bloom and his friends went on a vacation. They went to a beautiful park where there were many other flowers. Bloom and his friends played all day long. They played hide and seek, and they danced in the sun. But soon, Bloom noticed that some flowers in the park were not happy. They were sad because they did not have any friends. Bloom decided to be kind and make friends with the sad flowers. He talked to them and invited them to play with him and his friends. Soon, all the flowers in the park were happy and playing together. Bloom learned that being kind and making friends is a good thing to do. And that is the story of Bloom and his vacation. The moral of the story is to be kind and make friends with others.

Once upon a time, there was a wide temple. In the temple, there lived a kind man named Tom. Tom liked to help people. He was always happy and nice. One day, Tom had to serve food to all his friends. He cooked a big meal with lots of yummy things to eat. His friends came to the temple, and they were all very hungry. Tom served the food to his friends with a big smile. They all ate and had fun. After the meal, Tom's friends said thank you and went home. Tom was happy because he made his friends happy. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, Mama and her little girl went to the store. Mama pointed to a big box of pencils. The little girl was excited and she wanted to buy them all. Mama said, "That's an awfully big box, why don't you stretch your arms wide and see if you can fit all of the pencils in your arms?" The little girl stretched her arms wide and tried. She could barely fit a few. She said, "Mama, these pencils are so ugly, I don't want them!" Mama smiled and said, "Sometimes things that are ugly can still be useful. Why don't you pick out a few that you like?" The little girl pouted, but she picked out a few pencils. As they left the store, she smiled proudly at her pencils.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to read books to Tom every day. They had a lot of fun together. One day, Lily found a big box in her room. She saw a label on the box. Lily tried to read the label, but the words were too hard for her. She called Tom and said, "Tom, can you help me read this label?" Tom looked at the label and tried to read it too, but he was just a cat and could not read. Lily decided to ask her mom for help. Her mom read the label and said, "This box is full of new toys for you and Tom to play with!" Lily and Tom were so happy. They opened the box and played with their new toys all day long. And from that day on, Tom was not a grumpy cat anymore.

One day, a nosy little dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a big red ball in the park. Spot wanted to play with the ball, so he ran to it. He saw a hook near the ball, and he thought it was strange. Spot heard a girl named Amy say, "Hey, that's my ball!" Spot looked at her and wagged his tail. He wanted to be friends with Amy. Amy said, "I need to get my ball, but it is stuck on the hook. Can you help me, Spot?" Spot barked and jumped up to the ball. He used his nose to push the ball off the hook. The ball fell down and rolled away. Spot and Amy ran after the ball, and they laughed and played together. Just then, a big wind came and blew the ball high into the sky. Spot and Amy looked up and saw the ball turn into a big, shiny balloon. They were very surprised! The balloon floated away, but Spot and Amy didn't mind. They had fun together and became good friends. Everyone in the park saw what happened and started to clap. They all applauded Spot for being such a helpful dog. Spot and Amy smiled and played in the park all day long.
