Category: Intermediate
Title: The New Red Truck and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a new red truck. The truck liked to load things. One day, the truck saw a big box. The box had a picture of a fly on it. The truck wanted to know what was inside the box. The truck took the box to a park. It opened the box and found a toy fly. The toy fly was very special. It could fly high in the sky. The truck was very happy with the new toy. The truck played with the toy fly every day. It would load the toy fly on its back, drive to the park, and watch the toy fly high in the sky. The truck and the toy fly had lots of fun together. The end.

Once there was a little boy who lived in the east. He often looked at the west and promised that one day he would go there and explore. One day, his parents took him to the west. At first he was excited, but when he saw the big, sad mountains, he was scared. He didn't want to go any further. His parents promised him that they wouldn't leave him alone and that he would be safe. So he held their hands and they hiked together along the path. Each day they hiked further into the west and he started to explore. He met animals, saw beautiful flowers and the sun shone on him too. He realised that he wasn't scared anymore and he had kept his promise. Finally, after many days, they reached the end of the path. The little boy said goodbye to the west and looked forward to the adventures that would come next.

Tim and Sam were brothers who liked to play with cars. They had many cars of different colors and sizes. They made roads and bridges with blocks and books. They liked to make noises and pretend they were driving fast. One day, they found a new toy in their room. It was a big car that had a button on it. When they pressed the button, the car made a loud sound and a spark came out of the back. Tim and Sam were very excited. They wanted to try the new car on their roads. "Let me go first!" Tim said. He grabbed the car and put it on the floor. He pressed the button and the car zoomed away. He laughed and followed the car. "No, it's my turn!" Sam said. He ran after Tim and tried to take the car from him. He pulled the car and Tim pulled back. They both wanted the car. "Stop!" their mom said. She heard the noise and came to see what was going on. She saw the boys fighting over the car and the spark on the floor. She was worried. She took the car from them and put it on the shelf. "This car is not safe for you. It can make a fire. You have to yield and share your toys. Do you understand?" Tim and Sam nodded. They felt sorry. They did not want to make a fire or make their mom angry. They hugged their mom and said they were sorry. Then they went back to their other cars and played nicely. They learned to yield and share. They had fun with their cars and their mom.

Once upon a time, there was a barrel that was very popular. Everyone wanted to try it. So one day, a group of friends decided to measure the barrel. They used a measuring tape to see how big it was. But the barrel was too small. The friends were all very sad and they tried to measure it again. But it was still too small. The barrel was so popular that they decided to put it in a bigger barrel. When they measured it again, it was just right! Everyone in the group was so happy. They all celebrated, and now the barrel was even more popular! Everyone loved it even more, and they went and measured it time and time again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a huge house. In this house, there was a big room with a warm fireplace. The fireplace made the room feel nice and cozy. One day, a little dog named Spot came into the room. Spot saw the fireplace and felt happy. He sat down near the fireplace to feel warm. Spot liked the crackling sound of the wood burning. In the room, there was also a little girl named Lily. Lily saw Spot by the fireplace and clapped her hands. She was so happy to see Spot. Lily sat down next to Spot and they both enjoyed the warm fireplace together. They had a great day in the huge house.
