Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Coat

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside. One day, it was very icy, but Spot still wanted to play. He put on his warm coat and went out to have fun. Spot saw a big pit in the ground. He wanted to jump over it. Spot ran and tried to jump, but he slipped on the icy ground. He fell into the pit. Spot started to struggle to get out, but it was too hard. Then, a kind bird named Tweet flew by and saw Spot. Tweet asked, "Do you need help?" Spot said, "Yes, please!" Tweet flew down and helped Spot out of the pit. They both laughed and played together on the icy day.

One day, a brave dog named Max lived in a small house. Max loved to play with his ball and drink cocoa. His friend, a little bird named Lily, lived in a tree next to Max's house. One sunny day, Max and Lily were playing with the ball when it went into the deep forest. Max said, "I will go find the ball!" Lily said, "Be careful, Max!" Max went into the forest to look for the ball. The forest was very big, but Max was brave. As Max walked deeper into the forest, he saw a small cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and cried, "Help me, please!" Max knew he had to rescue the cat. Max climbed the tree and saved the cat. The cat said, "Thank you, Max!" Max, the cat, and Lily all went back to Max's house to drink cocoa and play together.

Once upon a time there was a strong dog named Matt. He was always happy his owners welcomed him into their home. One day, Matt was feeling unwell so his owners took him to the vet. The veterinarian was very kind and Matt felt much better after their visit. However, later that night Matt started feeling poorly again. His owners weren't sure what else to do and felt very worried about him. The next morning, the owners took Matt back to the vet. The vet said, "Welcome back. Let's see what we can do." The vet felt around Matt's stomach and said, "He needs a strong medicine to help him get better and he'll be just fine." Matt's owners were so relieved to hear the good news. They thanked the vet and took Matt home. After taking the medicine, Matt started feeling better. He was so happy to have his strength back and wagged his tail in excitement. The owners were relieved too, and welcomed Matt back to the family with lots of cuddles!

Once upon a time, there was a lovely harbor. In the harbor, there were many boats. One day, a boy named Tim went to the harbor. He wanted to create a boat of his own. Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "I want to create a boat too!" So, Tim and Sue worked together. They made a big, strong boat. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the water! The fish said, "Hello, I like your boat!" Tim and Sue were surprised, but they let the fish join them. They all had fun in their lovely boat at the harbor.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite belt. It was a blue belt with a big yellow buckle. He wore it every day. One day, Tim was playing with his friend, Sam. They were playing tug of war with the blue belt. Tim pulled one end, and Sam pulled the other end. They were both very stubborn and did not want to let go. Suddenly, they heard a loud rip. The belt had a tear in it! "Oh no!" cried Tim. "My belt is broken!" "Don't be sad," said Sam. "We can fix it!" So, Tim and Sam found some tape and put it on the tear. The belt was fixed and they were happy again. From that day on, Tim and Sam were more careful when playing with the blue belt.
