Category: Intermediate
Title: Ann the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ann. Ann loved to play with her toys. She had many toys, but her favorite was a big red ball. One day, Ann wanted to see how big her ball was. She had a measure that her mom gave her. Ann started to measure her ball. She was very happy because she was successful in finding out how big it was. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball started to grow bigger and bigger! Ann was very surprised. As the ball grew, it lifted Ann up in the air. She was scared at first, but then she saw her friends and they all laughed together. They played on the big ball all day long. In the end, the ball went back to its normal size, and Ann had a fun and special day with her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little cat named Kitty. She loved to play with her ball and her friends. One day, Kitty saw a big tree with a bird on the top. Kitty wanted to reach the bird and play with him. Kitty tried to climb the tree but she was too shy to ask for help. She looked around and saw her friend Dog. Dog saw Kitty and said, "Kitty, why are you sad?" Kitty replied, "I want to reach the top of the tree and play with the bird, but I am too shy to ask for help." Dog smiled and said, "I will help you, Kitty!" Dog helped Kitty climb the tree, and soon they reached the top. Kitty was so happy to play with the bird, and she thanked Dog for his help. From that day on, Kitty was not shy anymore, and she and Dog became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lazy thief. He liked to take things that did not belong to him. He did not like to work hard. One sunny day, the lazy thief saw a pretty dress hanging outside a house. He wanted to take the dress for himself. So, he went to the house and tried to take the dress. But as he pulled it, the dress began to tear. A little girl saw the thief and said, "Hey! That's my dress! You can't take it!" The lazy thief felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to tear your dress." The little girl was sad, but she forgave the thief. From that day on, the lazy thief decided to change. He promised to work hard and not take things that did not belong to him. And the little girl and the thief became friends, playing and laughing together in the small town.

Once upon a time, there was a little, adventurous lizard named Lizzy. She liked to mark her path with a stick as she walked around the big forest. One day, Lizzy found a big, shiny rock and marked it with her stick. The next day, Lizzy told her friend, Tim the turtle, about the shiny rock. Tim wanted to see it too. So, Lizzy took him to the big forest to find the rock she marked. They looked and looked, but they could not find the marked rock. Finally, they saw the mark Lizzy made with her stick. They followed the mark and found the shiny rock. Tim and Lizzy were so happy to find the rock together. They played by the rock all day and had lots of fun. From that day on, the marked rock became their favorite place to play in the big forest.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Max. Max loved to play with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They played in the sun and had lots of fun. One day, while they were playing, Lily saw a big blueberry bush. "Max, look at the big blueberry bush!" she said. Max wagged his tail and ran to the bush. They picked blueberries together and filled a basket. Suddenly, Lily saw a blueberry fall into a small hole. She tried to get it, but her hand got stuck. Lily was scared and cried, "Help, Max!" Max knew he had to save Lily. He pulled and pulled until Lily's hand was free. Lily hugged Max and said, "Thank you, Max! You saved me!" Max barked happily, and they went home with their blueberries. From that day on, they knew they would always be loyal friends.
