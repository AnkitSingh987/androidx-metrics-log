Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Coat

Anna loved to visit her grandpa, who worked in a lab. He showed her many things, like how to make bubbles, how to mix colors, and how to look at tiny things with a big glass. Anna liked to ask him many questions, and he always answered with a smile. One day, Anna went to the lab with her grandpa, and saw a new friend. It was a big, furry dog, with a white coat and a wagging tail. Grandpa said, "This is Max, he is an elderly lab. He helps me with my work, and he is very smart and gentle. Do you want to say hello to him?" Anna nodded, and walked slowly towards Max. She held out her hand, and Max sniffed it. Then he licked her fingers, and nuzzled her cheek. Anna giggled, and hugged him. She said, "Hi, Max, you are a good dog. I like you very much. Do you want to play with me?" Max barked, and wagged his tail harder. He followed Anna around the lab, and played with her. They chased balls, they hid under tables, and they shared snacks. Grandpa watched them, and smiled. He said, "You two are having fun, aren't you? I'm glad you like each other. Max is very lonely, he doesn't have many friends. He needs someone like you, Anna, to make him happy." Anna looked at grandpa, and said, "I'm happy too, grandpa. Max is my new friend. Can I come to the lab again, and play with him?" Grandpa nodded, and said, "Of course, Anna. You can come anytime you want. Max and I will be waiting for you." He hugged Anna, and Max licked them both. They were a happy family.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth stone. It lived by a river. One day, a little girl named Mia found the stone. She liked it very much. Mia put the stone on her shoulder and took it home. At home, Mia showed the stone to her mom. "Look, Mom! I found a smooth stone by the river. Can it stay with us?" she asked. Mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mia. The stone can stay with us." Mia was very happy. She and the stone played together every day. The stone stayed on her shoulder as they played. They became the best of friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside and discover new things. One day, he found a bean on the ground. It was a green bean, and he thought it was special. Tim showed the bean to his mom. "Mom, look what I found!" he said. His mom looked at the bean and said, "That's nice, Tim. But our garden is empty. Why don't we plant the bean and see what happens?" Together, they went to the garden and planted the bean. They watered it and took care of it every day. Soon, the bean grew into a big, tall plant. Tim was so happy to see his bean grow. He learned that if you take care of something, it can grow and become better. The moral of the story is: when you find something small, don't ignore it. With love and care, it can become something big and wonderful.

Once upon a time, there was a proud cat and a proud dog. They lived in a house with a big yard. The cat and dog did not like to share. They would always quarrel about who was the boss of the yard. One day, they saw a long cable in the yard. The cat wanted to play with the cable, but the dog wanted it too. They began to quarrel again. They pulled the cable from each end, trying to take it away from each other. Then, a little bird came and sat on the cable. The bird said, "Why do you quarrel all the time? You can play with the cable together and have fun." The cat and dog thought about it and decided to try. They played with the cable together and had a great time. From that day on, they learned to share and became good friends.
