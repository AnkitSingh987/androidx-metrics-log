Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pet potato. The potato was not a regular potato. It was a special potato because it could talk. Every day, Sue would feed her potato. She would give it water and a little bit of food. The potato would say, "Thank you, Sue!" and it would grow a little bit bigger. One day, Sue's potato grew so big that it could not fit in the house. Sue and her potato went outside to play. They had a lot of fun in the sun. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to decorate her room with her favorite color, blue. One day, she found an attractive blue ribbon and had an idea. Amy went to her friend, Tim, and said, "Look at my pretty blue ribbon! I want to decorate our tree outside with it. Will you help me?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Amy! Let's make the tree beautiful together." They worked together to tie the blue ribbon around the tree. Soon, other kids saw the tree and wanted to help too. They all brought their own pretty things to decorate the tree. In the end, the tree was very beautiful, and all the kids were happy. Amy and Tim learned that when you share your ideas and work together, you can create something even more beautiful. And they all played under the pretty tree, happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys in the bathtub. One day, while playing, his favorite toy, a little boat, started to sink. Tim felt sad and wanted to rescue the boat. Tim tried to save the boat quickly, but he couldn't. His mom told him to try slowly. So, Tim took a deep breath and moved his hand slow and steady. This time, he was able to rescue his boat from the water. Tim was happy to have his boat back. He learned that sometimes, being slow and careful can help you do things better. From that day on, Tim always remembered to be patient and take his time when he needed to do something important.

Anna and Ben were playing in the backyard. They saw a big box by the fence. It had a sticker that said "mail". They were curious. "Let's open it!" Ben said. "Maybe it's a present!" Anna said. They ran to the box and lifted the lid. Inside, they saw a shiny scale. It looked like a toy. They picked it up and pressed a button. The scale made a beep sound. "Wow, it's cool!" Ben said. "Let me see!" Anna said. They took turns holding the scale and pressing the button. They did not know that the scale was not a toy. It was a bomb. Someone had mailed it to their house to hurt them. Suddenly, the scale made a loud noise. It flashed and exploded. Anna and Ben screamed. The blast hurt them very badly. They fell to the ground. The box caught fire. Smoke filled the air. Their mom heard the noise and ran outside. She saw the fire and her children. She was shocked and scared. She called for help. She tried to save them. But it was too late. Anna and Ben were gone. The mail had killed them. The scale was not harmless. It was evil.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his dog, Spot. Tim loved to play outside with Spot. They would run and jump and have lots of fun. One day, Tim and Spot were playing on the side of their house. They were having a great time when they heard a loud noise. Tim didn't know what it was, but he wanted to find out. He told Spot to stay and went to see what was making the noise. As Tim walked around the house, he saw a big cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and shouting, "Meow! Meow!" Tim knew he had to help the cat. He was brave and climbed the tree to save the cat. When he got the cat down, the cat was happy and Tim felt proud. He went back to playing with Spot, knowing he did a good thing.
