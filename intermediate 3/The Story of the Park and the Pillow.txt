Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Park and the Pillow

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved bows. She had many different kinds of bows - some were big, some were small, and some even glittered. She loved them all. One day, the little girl decided to go outside and play with her bows. She chose a big, hard bow and ran off to the park. She had lots of fun tossing it around, twirling it in the air, and catching it with a big smile on her face. Suddenly, the little girl sneezed. "My bow!" she cried. But when she looked up, the hard bow was nowhere to be seen! She searched all over, but she couldn't find it. "Where did it go?" the little girl asked. But no one seemed to know. The little girl sadly went home without her hard bow. But the next day, when the little girl woke up, she saw something special - her hard bow was lying neatly next to her pillow! She smiled and enjoyed the rest of her day with her precious bow.

Lila and Ben were twins who liked to play outside. They had a big yard with many trees and flowers. One day, they decided to have an adventure and explore the east side of the yard, where they had never been before. They walked and walked for a long time, holding hands and looking around. They saw a big rock, a shiny pond, and a funny bird. They had fun talking to the bird and throwing pebbles in the pond. They did not remember to look at the sun or the clock. Soon, it was getting dark and cold. Lila and Ben started to feel scared and hungry. They did not know how to go back to their house. They wished they had remembered to tell their mom and dad where they were going. They hugged each other and cried. They hoped someone would find them. They heard a loud noise. It was their dog, Spot, who had followed their smell. Spot barked and wagged his tail. He was happy to see them. He licked their faces and led them to the west, where their house was. Lila and Ben ran after Spot, feeling relieved and thankful. They saw their mom and dad waiting for them at the door. They were worried and angry, but also glad and proud. They hugged and kissed Lila and Ben and took them inside. They gave them warm soup and bread and milk. They told them to never go too far without telling them. They also told them they were brave and smart to stay together and to trust Spot. Lila and Ben said they were sorry and they loved them. They also said they had a fun adventure, but they would remember to be careful next time. They ate their soup and bread and milk and went to bed. They dreamed of the east side of the yard, the big rock, the shiny pond, and the funny bird. They smiled in their sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little bird named Bobo. Bobo lived in a big tree near a tall wall. Every day, Bobo would sit on a branch and rock back and forth, watching the other birds play and sing. One day, a friendly bird named Lulu came to Bobo and asked, "Do you want to play with us?" Bobo was scared but wanted to try. So, he slowly left his branch and joined Lulu and the other birds near the wall. As they played, Bobo started to feel happy and not so shy anymore. He learned that making friends was fun and it made him feel good. The other birds liked Bobo, too. They all played together and had a great time. The moral of the story is to not be afraid to make friends and try new things, even if you feel shy. When you try, you might find that you have a lot of fun and make new friends.

One day, a bright sun was up in the sky. Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk near the pond. They saw a frog sitting on a rock. "Hello, frog!" said Tim. The frog looked at Tim and said, "Hello, Tim! Can you please help me? I need to wipe my face but I can't reach it." Tim was surprised that the frog could talk! He took a soft cloth and helped the frog wipe its face. The frog was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! Now I have a surprise for you!" Suddenly, the frog jumped into the pond and came out with a big, shiny toy car! Tim was so happy and thanked the frog. Tim and Spot played with the car all day, and they had lots of fun with their new talking frog friend.
