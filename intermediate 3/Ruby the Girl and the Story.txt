Title: Ruby the Girl and the Story

Once upon a time there was a young girl named Ruby. Ruby was nosy and always wanted to know what was going on around her. One day Ruby noticed that her mum was busy preparing something. She was curious, so she asked her mum what she was making. Her mum replied, “Hush now, I'm preparing something special for you. It's full of wisdom, so you have to wait until it's ready. But Ruby couldn't wait and she kept asking her mum questions. Finally her mum got very frustrated and told her to go outside and play. Ruby was upset, but she went outside. But then a bad thing happened. As she stepped outside, she slipped and fell into a deep hole. She screamed and screamed, but nobody heard her. She was stuck there forever! The moral of the story is this - be careful of nosy behaviour, because it can lead to bad things. It's better to be patient and wait for things to be prepared. Wisdom is often worth waiting for.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat liked to sail on the water. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. One day, the boat saw a duck. The duck was sad. The boat asked, "Why are you sad, duck?" The duck said, "I am sorry, but I lost my toy." The boat wanted to help the duck. They looked for the toy together. They sailed on the water, under the sun. They looked near and far. At last, they found the toy. The duck was happy. The boat was happy too. They sailed back home, under the bright sun and the blue sky. And they were friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a fancy cat named Sam. Sam liked to wear a big hat and a bow tie. He lived in a big house with his best friend, a dog named Max. One day, Sam and Max decided to play hide and seek. Max closed his eyes and counted to ten. Sam ran and hid behind a big tree. When Max was done counting, he went to find Sam. He peeked behind the tree and saw Sam's big hat. "I see you!" Max said. "Your fancy hat gave you away!" Sam laughed and said, "You got me! Now it's your turn to hide!" Max went to hide while Sam counted. When Sam was done counting, he looked for Max. He peeked behind a bush and saw Max's tail. "Found you!" Sam said. "Your tail was sticking out!" Max laughed and they played all day long. At the end of the day, they sat under the tree and talked about their fun day. Sam said, "We are good at hide and seek, but we need a judge to pick the best hider." Max agreed, "Next time, let's ask our friend, the wise owl, to be the judge!" They both smiled and went to sleep, dreaming of more fun days to come.

Lily has long hair. She likes to brush her hair and make braids. She has a doll with short hair. She wants to give her doll long hair too. She asks her mom, "Mom, can I give my doll some hair?" Mom says, "How will you do that, Lily?" Lily says, "I can cut some of my hair and glue it on her head." Mom says, "No, Lily, that is not a good idea. Your hair will not grow back fast. And glue is not good for your doll. She will be sticky and hairy." Lily says, "But I want her to have long hair like me." Mom says, "I have a better idea. Why don't we go to the toy store and buy a new doll with long hair? You can choose any doll you like. And you can keep your hair and your old doll too." Lily says, "Really? Can we do that, Mom?" Mom says, "Yes, we can. Come on, let's go." Lily is happy. She hugs her mom and her doll. She says, "Thank you, Mom. You are the best."

Once upon a time, there was a young girl called Joanna. She was three years old and loved to explore. Joanna was planning an exciting adventure when she noticed something strange on the ceiling. She saw the rarest butterfly she had ever seen and she gasped with delight. Joanna's Mum came into the room and asked her what she was looking at. Joanna said, “I am planning an adventure to catch the rare butterfly!” Joanna's Mum smiled and said, “That's so creative. I wish you luck!” Joanna grabbed her net and slowly climbed up to reach the ceiling. She carefully moved her net closer to the butterfly and with one swift movement, she caught it. Joanna was so happy that she had achieved her plan. She ran back to show her Mum her new discovery. Joanna's Mum said, “Well done! That was an amazing plan.” Joanna smiled and held up the butterfly proudly. She had made the rarest discovery of all and the adventure was finished.
