Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Man and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a man named Tom. Tom had a wife named Sue. They lived in a small house with their dog, Spot. Tom and Sue loved to play with Spot in their yard. One day, Sue had an idea. She said to Tom, "Let's make a fun game for Spot." Tom liked the idea and said, "Yes, let's be creative!" Tom and Sue thought and thought about what game to make. They decided to make a ball game. Tom and Sue found some old socks and put them together. Sue said, "Now we have a ball for Spot!" They went outside and threw the ball for Spot to catch. Spot loved the new game and they all played happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who had a big ladder. He loved to step on the ladder and look at his big house. One day, he saw a little bird on the ladder. The bird was sad and could not fly. The wealthy man wanted to help the bird, so he climbed up the ladder to reach it. As he stepped up the ladder, he saw the bird was not alone. There were many birds on the ladder, all of them sad and unable to fly. The wealthy man felt bad for the birds and decided to help them all. He carefully picked up each bird and put them in a safe place. Then, something unexpected happened. The birds started to sing and dance. They were not sad birds, but magic birds! The wealthy man was so surprised. The magic birds thanked the man for helping them and granted him a wish. The man wished for more friends, and the birds became his friends. From that day on, the wealthy man and the magic birds lived happily together, and he always remembered to share his ladder with his new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to learn and understand new things. One day, her mom told her a story about history. Lily listened very carefully. In the story, there was a big, clear lake. People who lived near the lake were very happy. They played in the water and had fun. Lily liked the story and wanted to know more about history. Lily asked her mom to tell her more stories about history. She wanted to understand everything. Her mom was happy to see Lily so interested. They spent the whole day talking and learning together. And Lily understood more and more each day.

Once upon a time, there was a fast pigeon named Pete. Pete loved to fly high in the sky. He liked to play with his friends. One day, Pete saw a soft, green spot to lie down. He was tired from flying fast. So, Pete lay down on the soft, green spot. He closed his eyes and took a nap. When Pete woke up, he saw his friends. They were all happy to see him. Pete got up and they all played together again. Pete was happy to be with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a blue cup. The cup lived in a kitchen near a sink. The cup had a friend, a small spoon. They liked to play together in the sink. One day, the cup asked the spoon, "Why am I blue?" The spoon tried to explain. "You are blue because you were made that way. Blue is a color like red or green." The cup was happy to know why it was blue. The blue cup and the spoon had lots of fun playing in the sink. They splashed in the water and made bubbles. They were always together and became the best of friends. And the blue cup was proud to be blue.

One day, a crab was walking on the sand. He saw a big pot. The crab was very serious. He wanted to know what was inside the pot. The crab said, "Hello, pot. What is inside of you?" The pot said, "I have water. I am very hot. Do you want to stir me?" The crab said, "Yes, I want to stir you." The crab put his claw in the pot to stir the water. But the water was too hot! The crab's claw got hurt. He was very sad. The crab learned that he should not touch hot things.
