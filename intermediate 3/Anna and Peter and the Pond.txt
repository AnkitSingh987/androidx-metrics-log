Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Peter and the Pond

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Anna and Peter. It was a bright, sunny day and they were playing in the park. Suddenly, Anna saw a swan by the pond. "Look, Peter!" Anna pointed to the swan. "What is it?" asked Peter in wonder. "It's a swan!", Anna said. "Let's go closer!" So they went over to the swan. It was very cold by the pond but the swan didn't seem to mind. Anna and Peter stared in awe. Suddenly, the swan started to change! Its white feathers began to turn black, its body shrank, and it legs started to grow. Soon, it had changed into a different animal. "What is it now?" asked Peter. "It's a duck!" said Anna with a smile. "Isn't it amazing how the swan can change?" Peter nodded. Then the two friends went off to look for more animals to discover.

Once upon a time, there was a sad alligator. He lived in a big pond with his friends. The sad alligator did not know how to count numbers, so he always felt left out when his friends played number games. One day, a kind and wise bird saw the sad alligator. The bird flew down and asked, "Why are you so sad, alligator?" The alligator told the bird about his problem with numbers. The kind bird decided to help the alligator learn numbers. They started with number one and went on from there. The alligator was very happy to learn numbers with the bird. Soon, he could count numbers like his friends. The alligator was not sad anymore. He played number games with his friends and had lots of fun. And the alligator and the bird became best friends forever.

Tom and Mia like to bake. They have a big book with many recipes. Today, they want to bake a cake for their mom. They take eggs, flour, sugar, butter, and milk from the fridge. They mix everything in a big bowl. They pour the batter in a round pan. They put the pan in the oven. While the cake is baking, they see a bird outside the window. The bird is yellow and blue. It sings a pretty song. Tom and Mia want to play with the bird. They go outside with a cage. They put some seeds in the cage. They wait for the bird to come. The bird sees the seeds. It flies to the cage. It hops inside. Tom and Mia are happy. They close the cage. They take the bird inside. They put the cage on the table. They talk to the bird. "Hello, bird. What is your name?" Tom asks. "Tweet, tweet. I am Sunny. I like to sing and fly." The bird answers. "You are a nice bird. We want to be your friends." Mia says. "Thank you. You are nice too. But I miss the sky and the trees." The bird says. Tom and Mia look at each other. They feel sorry for the bird. They hear a beep from the oven. The cake is ready. They take the cake out. It smells good. It has a golden crust. They put some icing and sprinkles on it. They write "Happy Mom's Day" with a tube. They look at the bird again. They have an idea. They open the cage. They let the bird out. The bird flaps its wings. It flies around the room. It lands on the cake. It pecks at the icing. It makes a mess. "Hey, bird. Stop that. You are ruining our cake." Tom says. "No, bird. Keep going. You are making our cake more fun." Mia says. Tom and Mia start to argue. They are competitive. They both want to have the best cake. They forget about the bird. The bird sees the window. It flies to the window. It pushes it open. It escapes. It sings a goodbye song. Tom and Mia stop arguing. They see the window. They see the bird. They see the cake. They feel sad. They feel silly. They hug each other. They say sorry. They clean the cake. They smile. They are still friends. They wait for their mom. They hope she will like their cake. They hope she will understand. They hope they will see the bird again.
