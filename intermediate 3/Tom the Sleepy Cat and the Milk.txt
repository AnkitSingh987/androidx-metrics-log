Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Sleepy Cat and the Milk

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom lived in a little house with a nice lady. One day, Tom saw a big mug on the table. The mug was full of yummy milk. Tom wanted to drink the milk, but the mug was too high for him. Tom thought and thought about how to gain the milk in the mug. He saw a chair near the table. Tom jumped on the chair and then onto the table. Now, he was close to the mug. Tom said, "Yay! I can drink the milk now!" He drank all the milk from the mug. It was very yummy. Tom was so happy that he could gain the milk. After drinking the milk, Tom felt even more sleepy. He went to his soft bed and slept all day long.

Lily loved cheese. She liked to eat cheese with bread, cheese with fruit, cheese with crackers. She liked all kinds of cheese: yellow cheese, white cheese, soft cheese, hard cheese. She always asked her mom for cheese when she was hungry. One day, Lily saw a big piece of cheese on the table. It looked very yummy. It was round and brown and had holes in it. Lily thought it was cheese for her. She was very excited. She ran to the table and grabbed the cheese. She took a big bite. But the cheese was not cheese. It was soap. Lily's mom had put it on the table to wash the dishes. Lily spit out the soap. It tasted very bad. It made her mouth hurt. It made her cry. She dropped the soap and ran to her mom. Lily's mom hugged her and gave her some water. She told her that the soap was not cheese. She told her that she should always ask before she ate something. She told her that she was sorry. She told her that she had some real cheese available in the fridge. Lily felt sad and sorry. She wished she had asked her mom first. She wished she had not eaten the soap. She wished she had not been so excited. She learned a lesson. She did not want to eat soap again. She wanted to eat real cheese.

Once upon a time, there was a funny cat. This cat was green. The cat liked to play with a ball. One day, the cat's mom did not permit the cat to play outside. The cat was sad. The cat wanted to play with the ball, but the mom said no. The cat tried to play inside, but it was not fun. The cat wanted to go outside and play with the ball in the sun. The cat did not listen to the mom. The cat went outside to play with the ball. But, the cat lost the ball. The cat was very sad and could not find the ball. The cat's mom was not happy. The cat learned to listen to mom next time.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Emily. She wanted to learn lots of new things, so every day her mum and dad would teach her something new. One day, they decided to teach her about trees. They showed her a dry trunk outside and explained that it came from a tree. Emily was excited and wanted to learn more, so they taught her that trees need water and sunlight to be healthy. Emily then started to look closely at the trunk. She noticed that there were small grooves in the wood and asked her parents what they were. Her mum and dad taught her that the grooves were made by animals living in the tree and that they helped them to climb up and down. Emily was fascinated by this new knowledge and ran off to find some more trees. The next day, Emily's parents took her to a nearby forest. They taught her about different trees and showed her how to tell them apart. Emily was amazed at all the things she had learned, and she spent the rest of the day playing around the trunks. There was so much to learn and Emily was determined to learn even more. Before long, she was an expert on trees and her parents were very proud. Emily's new found knowledge was just the beginning of her teaching adventures!
