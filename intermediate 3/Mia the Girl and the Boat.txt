Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to fold paper into fun shapes. One sunny day, Mia went outside to play with her friends. They played with a ball near a big tree. Mia saw a pretty leaf on the ground. She picked it up and decided to fold it. Mia folded the leaf into a tiny boat. She put the boat on her palm and showed it to her friends. They all thought it was very cool. Mia wanted to see if the boat could float on water. They found a little puddle and put the boat in it. The boat floated, and they were all happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A tiny frog jumped into the boat! The frog was terrible at steering the boat, and it went in circles. Mia and her friends laughed and laughed. The frog jumped out of the boat and hopped away. Mia and her friends had a fun and silly day, and they would never forget the frog who sailed their little leaf boat.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He liked to dream big. One day he dreamed of flying. He flew high in the sky, over tall buildings and far away places. He was weightless and free. But when he looked below him he saw a lot of waste. It was heavy and made the earth look sad. He knew that he had to do something to help. The little boy worked really hard to pick up all the waste and put it in the rubbish bin. He was tired but he felt happy that he had done something good. When he went to bed that night, he dreamed of flying again. But this time, he looked down and saw a clean and happy planet. He smiled and knew he had done the right thing.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play. One day, he saw a big stand in the park. It was very organized with many toys on it. Tim was very happy and went to the stand to look at the toys. A man at the stand said, "Hello, Tim! Do you want to play with the toys?" Tim was excited and said, "Yes, please!" The man showed Tim how to play with the toys. They had so much fun together. But then, something unexpected happened. The toys started to talk! One toy said, "Hello, Tim! We like to run and play too!" Tim was surprised but happy. He played with the talking toys all day long. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. Tim had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there were two siblings: Maisy and Alex. Maisy was very competitive and wanted to be the best. Alex was content with whatever place he ended up. One day, their mum asked them to separate the laundry. Maisy grabbed the iron quickly and began to press the clothes. Alex started to collect all the socks and pair them up. Maisy saw Alex working so slowly and said, "Why don't you do as much as I'm doing? I'll finish first if you keep going like that!" Alex simply replied, "It's okay. I'm just taking my time." Maisy couldn't understand why Alex wasn't as competitive as she was. In the end, they both finished sorting the laundry and got praised by their mum.

One day, a big bear named Bob wanted to bake a cake. He put on his hat and went to the store. He bought a big bag of flour, some eggs, and a cone. Bob wanted to make an enormous cake that he could share with all his friends. When Bob got home, he put all the things on the table. He mixed the flour and eggs in a big bowl. Then, he put the cone on top of the cake. "This will be the best cake ever!" said Bob. He put the cake in the oven and waited. Just then, a little mouse named Max came into the kitchen. He saw the big cake in the oven and wanted to help. Max tried to take the cake out of the oven, but it was too hot! The cake started to grow bigger and bigger. It got so enormous that it broke the oven door! "Oh no!" said Max. "What will we do now?" Bob and Max looked at the enormous cake on the floor. They were sad, but then they had an idea. They could still share the cake with their friends! So, Bob and Max invited all their friends to come and eat the enormous cake. Everyone was happy and they all had a great time.
