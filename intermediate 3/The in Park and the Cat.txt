Category: Intermediate
Title: The in Park and the Cat

Once upon a time, a cute rat lived in a park. The rat liked to play with his friends, the birds, and the bugs. They all had fun together in the park. One day, a big cat came to the park. The cat saw the cute rat and said, "Hello, little rat. Do you want to play with me?" The rat was scared, but he didn't want to be rude. So, he said, "Okay, we can play." The cat and the rat played in the park. But the cat was not nice. The cat chased the rat and caught him. The rat's friends tried to help, but they were too small. The cat ate the cute rat, and all the friends were sad. The moral of the story is: be careful who you trust.

Sara and Tom like to play with books. They have many books with pictures and words. Sara can read some words, but Tom cannot. He likes to look at the pictures and make sounds. One day, they find a big book in the attic. It has a lock on it. Sara tries to open the lock, but it is too hard. She asks Tom to help her. "Tom, can you open this lock? It is a big book. Maybe it has many pictures and words inside." Tom tries to open the lock, but he cannot. He shakes the book and hears something inside. He thinks it is a toy. "Maybe there is a toy in the book. I want to see the toy. Let me try again." He pulls the lock hard, and it breaks. The book opens, and a loud noise comes out. It is a horn. The horn makes a very loud sound. Sara and Tom cover their ears. They are scared. The horn stops, and they look at the book. It has only one page. The page is blank. There are no pictures and no words. Sara and Tom are confused. "Where is the toy? Where are the pictures and words? This is a bad book. It has nothing inside." They close the book and put the lock back. They do not want to play with the book anymore. They run away from the attic. They do not hear their mom calling them. They are deaf from the horn.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little bird named Billy. Billy had a nest on a strong branch, hidden high up in a tall tree. Billy loved his home, and he loved to sing. One sunny day, Billy saw a squirrel named Sally. Sally was looking for her lost nuts. "Can you help me find my nuts, Billy?" asked Sally. Billy nodded and said, "Let's guess where they are!" They looked under rocks, behind trees, and in the grass. Finally, Billy guessed, "Are your nuts hidden in that hole?" Sally looked in the hole and found her nuts! She was so happy. "Thank you, Billy!" said Sally. They became best friends and played together in the forest every day. And every night, Billy sang a happy song from his branch before going to sleep.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy had very long ears. One day, Remy was hopping around the forest when he heard a small cry. He stopped and listened. He heard the cry again and followed the sound. Remy found a little bird named Benny, who had a hurt wing. Benny had a fall from his nest. Remy said, "Don't worry, Benny. I will help you." Remy used his long ears to gently lift Benny back into his nest. Benny said, "Thank you, Remy!" From that day, Remy and Benny became best friends. They played together every day. All the animals in the forest saw their friendship and learned to help each other too. The moral of the story is: always help your friends when they need you, and be kind to everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot lived near an airport. He liked to watch the big planes fly up in the sky. One day, Spot saw a little bird who looked very sad. Spot asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird said, "I want to fly with the big planes, but I am too small. I surrender, I will never be like them." Spot had an idea. He found a big balloon and tied it to the bird. The balloon lifted the bird up high in the sky. The bird was so happy, he started to sing. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew the bird and the balloon far away from the airport. Spot didn't know what to do. He ran and ran, trying to catch up with his new friend. Finally, Spot found the bird in a big tree. The balloon had popped, and the bird was safe. They both laughed and went back home to the airport. From that day on, the bird knew that being small was okay, and Spot was always there to help.
