Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very alert and loved to play outside. One day, Max saw a big sandwich on the grass. He thought, "Wow! What a big sandwich! I want to eat it, but I am so tired from playing." Max decided to take a short nap before eating the sandwich. While Max was napping, a little bird came by and saw the sandwich too. The bird said, "I am hungry, but this sandwich is too big for me. I will share it with my friends." The little bird and her friends ate the sandwich together. When Max woke up from his nap, he saw the little bird and her friends eating the sandwich. Max was sad that he did not get to eat the sandwich, but he learned that it is better to share with friends than to eat alone. And from that day on, Max always shared his food with his new bird friends.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Blink. Blink had a special power. Every time he would blink, he could change how things look. One day, Blink went to the park to play with his friends. While playing with his friends, Blink saw a big tree. He thought it would be fun to change the tree's look. So, Blink blinked his eyes, and the tree turned into a big, colorful slide. All the animals at the park were so happy and excited to play on the new slide. But then, something unexpected happened. When Blink tried to blink again, nothing changed. Blink had lost his special power. He was sad, but his friends told him that he was still a smart cat. They all played together on the slide, and Blink learned that he didn't need his powers to have fun with his friends.

Lily and Ben like to play yoga. They have a big mat and some cards with pictures of yoga poses. They take turns to pick a card and show the pose to each other. They try to copy the pose and hold it for a while. They have fun and feel good. One day, Lily and Ben find a new card in the box. It has a picture of a person doing a sign with their hands. The sign looks like a letter O. Lily and Ben do not know what the sign means. They think it is a yoga pose. They want to try it. They sit on the mat and make the sign with their hands. They look at each other and smile. They think they are doing yoga. But they are not. The sign means "OK" in sign language. Sign language is a way of talking with your hands. Some people use sign language when they cannot hear or speak. A man sees Lily and Ben doing the sign. He thinks they are talking to him. He uses sign language too. He walks over to them and says hello with his hands. He asks them their names and how they are. He is friendly and kind. Lily and Ben do not understand the man. They do not know he is using sign language. They think he is doing yoga too. They try to copy his signs. They make funny shapes with their hands. They giggle and laugh. The man is confused and sad. He thinks Lily and Ben are making fun of him. He thinks they are rude and mean. He feels hurt and angry. He walks away and shakes his head. He does not want to talk to them anymore. Lily and Ben do not know they have upset the man. They think they have made a new friend. They are happy and proud. They do not know they have done something wrong. They do not know they have missed a chance to learn something new. They do not know they have hurt someone's feelings. They do not know they have a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a big house. In this house, there was a family with a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Lucy. Lucy liked to play upstairs in her room with her toys. She had many toys, but her favorite was a small red car. One day, Lucy was being careless while playing with her toys. She left a candle burning in her room. The candle was too close to her toys and the flame started to burn them. Lucy saw the fire and was scared. She ran downstairs to her mom and dad. They saw the fire upstairs and quickly put it out with water. They told Lucy to be more careful next time. Lucy learned her lesson and promised to never be careless with fire again.
