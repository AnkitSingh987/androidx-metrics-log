Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Sue and the Ball

One day, Tom and Sue found a big box of junk. It was very heavy. Tom said, "Let's lift it together!" Sue agreed, and they lifted the box to their playroom. Inside the box, they found many things. There was a perfect, shiny ball. Tom and Sue played with the ball. They were very happy. They also found a toy car and a doll. They played with these toys too. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball began to talk! The ball said, "Thank you for playing with me. I have been in the junk box for a long time." Tom and Sue were surprised, but they became friends with the talking ball. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to sing and dance. She had a toy microphone that she used all the time. Mia was restless and always wanted to play. One day, Mia was playing with her microphone near the bath. She wanted to pretend she was a star. She sang and danced while her mom got the bath ready. Mia was excited to bathe and sing at the same time. As Mia was singing, something unexpected happened. Her toy microphone fell into the bath! Mia was sad and thought her microphone was broken. But then, she saw it float on the water. It was a toy that could float! Mia was happy and enjoyed her bath while singing with her floating microphone.

Once there was a fast bunny. He was very proud of his image. He loved to race through the fields with his friends. He was very fast, so fast that all of his friends looked up to him. But one day, a big bad wolf came and wanted to catch the bunny. The bunny knew he had to be careful if he wanted to stay safe. He ran fast and tried to get away, but the wolf was too fast. Then suddenly, out of nowhere, there was a big bird! The bird flew in front of the bunny, and protected him. The wolf couldn't get past the big bird, so the bunny was safe. He thanked the bird for protecting him. The bunny was grateful, and remembered the image of the fast bird protecting him. He was thankful that the big bird had kept him safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little shrimp. This shrimp was not happy. He had a rough shell, and it made him feel sad. All the other shrimps had smooth shells. They would play and swim, but the little shrimp would just sit and weep. One day, a big fish came to the shrimp's home. The fish wanted to eat all the shrimps. The shrimps with smooth shells swam away fast. The little shrimp could not swim fast because of his rough shell. He was so scared. But then, something unexpected happened. The big fish tried to eat the little shrimp, but the rough shell was too hard. The fish did not like it and swam away. The little shrimp was safe! His rough shell saved him. Now, the little shrimp was happy and proud of his shell. He did not weep anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very generous boy. He liked to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tim saw a new girl in the park. Her name was Sue. Tim wanted to make Sue feel welcome. Tim said to Sue, "Hi, would you like to play with me?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, thank you!" They played together all day. Tim shared his toys with Sue, and they had lots of fun. Later, Tim wanted to climb a big tree. He tried and tried, but he could not do it. Sue said, "You can do it, Tim! Just keep trying!" Tim tried again, and this time he succeed. Tim and Sue were so happy. They played together every day after that, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys, but her most important toy was her doll, named Lily. One sunny day, Sue and Lily were playing in the park when they saw a big red ball. Sue said to Lily, "Let's play with the ball!" They played together, kicking the ball back and forth. But then, the ball rolled into the woods. Sue and Lily went to look for the ball, but they got lost. Sue started to cry, and Lily hugged her tight. As they walked through the woods, they saw a small flame. Sue and Lily knew that the flame could help them find their way back. They followed the flame, and it led them right back to the park. Sue and Lily were so happy, they hugged each other and said, "We will never forget this important adventure!"
