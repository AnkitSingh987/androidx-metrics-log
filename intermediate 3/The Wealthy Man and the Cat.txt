Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wealthy Man and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who lived in a big house. One day, it started to rain a lot. The rain made a big puddle near the man's house. A little cat was outside in the rain. The cat was cold and wet. The cat wanted to get away from the rain, but it could not. The cat meowed loudly for help. The wealthy man heard the cat's meows. He put on his coat and went outside. The man saw the little cat and wanted to help. The man rescued the cat from the rain. The cat was happy and warm inside the big house. The wealthy man and the cat became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fast little rabbit. The rabbit liked to run and play in the forest. One day, while running, the rabbit saw something new. It was a mushroom. The rabbit did not understand what it was. The rabbit asked his friend, the bird, about the mushroom. The bird told the rabbit that the mushroom was a kind of food. Some mushrooms were good to eat, but some were not. The rabbit wanted to learn more, so he could understand better. The rabbit and the bird went to ask the wise old owl. The owl knew a lot about mushrooms. He told the rabbit which mushrooms were good to eat and which were not. Now, the rabbit could understand the difference. He was happy to learn and share his new knowledge with his friends in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little bug named Benny. Benny loved to crawl all around the big garden. One day, Benny saw a big wheel. He wanted to play with it, but he was too small. Benny asked his friend, the ant, "Can you help me push the wheel?" The ant said, "Yes, let's do it together!" So Benny and the ant tried to push the wheel together. They pushed and pushed, but the wheel was still too heavy. Then, a big and strong beetle saw them. The beetle said, "I can help you!" With the beetle's help, Benny, the ant, and the beetle pushed the wheel together. They had so much fun playing with the wheel in the garden. They were all brave and happy friends.

One day, a painter was painting a big wall. He was very happy. He liked to clap when he was happy. So, he clapped his hands and sang a song. A little boy saw the painter. The boy said, "Why do you clap and sing?" The painter felt embarrassed. He said, "I clap and sing because I am happy when I paint." The boy wanted to paint too. The painter gave the boy a brush. The boy painted on the wall. But the boy was not good at painting. He made a big mess. The painter was not happy now. He said, "You made a mess. Now I have to paint again." The boy felt sad. The painter did not clap and sing anymore.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to dress up. She put on a pretty dress, fancy shoes, and a shiny hat. She looked in the mirror and smiled. Lucy felt like a princess. Lucy's mommy had a big, expensive card she got for her birthday. It had a picture of a beautiful cake on it. Lucy liked the card a lot. She wanted to show her friend, Tim. Lucy went to Tim's house and knocked on the door. Tim opened the door and said, "Wow, you look nice!" Lucy said, "Thank you! Look at this big card my mommy got. It is very special." Tim looked at the card and said, "It's so pretty!" They played dress up and looked at the card all day. They had lots of fun together. When it was time to go home, Lucy gave Tim a big hug and said, "I had a great day with you!"

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a big, round, red ball. Lily loved to play with her ball every day. It was her favorite toy, and she thought it was very special. One day, while Lily was playing with her ball, it split! The ball had a big hole in it, and she was very sad. Lily's mom saw her sad face and decided to give her a lecture. She told her, "Lily, it's okay. We can fix your ball or get a new one, but remember to be careful when you play." Lily listened to her mom's words and felt a little better. The next day, her mom took her to the store to find a new ball. They found one that looked just like her original ball. Lily was so happy to have a new ball to play with. From that day on, Lily was always careful when she played with her new ball. She remembered her mom's lecture and made sure not to let it split again. And they all lived happily ever after.
