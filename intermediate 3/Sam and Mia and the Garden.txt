Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and Mia and the Garden

Sam and Mia are playing in the garden. They like to dig with their shovels and find things in the dirt. They find worms, stones, and leaves. They also find roots. Roots are the parts of plants that grow under the ground. "Look, I found a big root!" Sam says, holding up a long and thick root. "It is from the tree over there." "Wow, that is a big root!" Mia says. "Can I see it?" Sam gives the root to Mia. She feels it with her hands. It is rough and hard. She tries to bend it, but it does not bend. She tries to break it, but it does not break. "Roots are strong," Mia says. "They help the plants stay in the ground." "Yes, they do," Sam says. "But we should not pull them out. They are not for us to play with. They are for the plants to live." Sam and Mia put the root back in the hole they dug. They cover it with dirt. They pat the dirt with their shovels. They make the ground smooth again. "Good job, Sam and Mia," their mom says, coming out of the house. "You are very careful with the plants. But you are very messy with yourselves. Look at your clothes and your hands. They are full of dirt." Sam and Mia look at each other. They giggle. They are messy, but they had fun. "Sorry, mom," they say. "We will wash up." They run to the hose and spray water on themselves. They wash off the dirt. They are clean, but they are wet. "Now you are wet," their mom says, smiling. "But you are happy. Come on, let's go inside and have a snack." Sam and Mia follow their mom. They are hungry. They had a good time playing in the garden. They learned about roots. They do not mind being messy or wet. They love to explore.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a famous bunny named Bobo. He loved to hop and play all day with his friends. Bobo was very good at running fast and jumping high. All the animals in the forest liked to watch him play. One day, while Bobo was playing, he saw a big, mean fox. The fox wanted to catch Bobo and his friends. Bobo knew he had to do something to save everyone. He had an idea, but he needed to rest his body first. So, Bobo found a safe place and took a short nap. When Bobo woke up, he felt strong and ready. He went to the fox and said, "I bet you can't catch me!" The fox laughed and started chasing Bobo. But Bobo was too fast and smart for the fox. He led the fox away from his friends and into a trap. The fox got stuck, and Bobo saved the day! All the animals in the forest were safe and happy, and they knew that their famous friend Bobo would always be there to help them.

Once upon a time, in a young garden, there lived a happy herb. This herb loved to organize parties for all his friends. His friends were all the other plants in the garden. One day, the herb decided to have a big party. He invited all his friends to come and have fun. They played games, ate yummy food, and danced in the sun. At the end of the party, everyone was very happy. They thanked the herb for organizing such a fun day. The young herb smiled and knew that he had made his friends very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She dreamt of a bright green world with open skies. In her dreams, she could play all day long without ever getting bored! She visited fields of flowers, rivers of glittering water and mountains of fluffy clouds. She even met a small friendly dragon. Every day she'd close her eyes and dream of wonderful things. She'd pretend she could fly above the green grass and visit far away places. On sunny days, she'd lay in the open field and turn her face up to the blue sky. One day, as she explored a new part of her green world, she discovered a magical doorway. She waved at the door and it opened before her. Inside was a secret garden filled with strange and wonderful creatures. Lucy dreamt of playing there ever after.
