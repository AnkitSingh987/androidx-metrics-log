Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Hairy Caterpillar and the Flower

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a hairy caterpillar named Bob. Bob was very hungry, so he ate many green leaves all day. One day, Bob saw a beautiful flower that was about to bloom. He was very excited to see the flower open up. Bob met a friendly bee named Bella. Bella said, "Hi Bob, would you like an invitation to the flower party? All the bugs will be there when the flower blooms!" Bob was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to go to the party!" The next day, the flower bloomed, and it was very pretty. Bob and Bella went to the flower party together. They saw many other bugs at the party, like ants, ladybugs, and butterflies. They all had a great time dancing and playing in the blooming flower. And Bob was not hungry anymore because he had so much fun with his new friends.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a rare rag in her room. She did not know where it came from. Mia showed the rag to her mom. Her mom said, "Wow, that is a rare and pretty rag! Let's mind it and keep it safe." Mia and her mom put the rare rag in a box. They put the box on a shelf. Mia's friend, Tim, came to play. Mia told Tim, "I have a rare rag. We must mind it and be careful." Tim said, "Okay, I will mind the rare rag too." They played with toys and had fun. They did not touch the rare rag. Mia and Tim were good at minding the rare rag. At the end of the day, Mia's mom told them, "You both did a great job minding the rare rag. Now, let's put it away and get ready for bed." Mia and Tim smiled, happy that they had minded the rare rag all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Bob. Bob loved to play with his toy loop. One day, Bob went to the park with his friend, a little cat named Tom. At the park, Bob and Tom saw a delicious cake on a table. They wanted to eat it. Tom said, "Let's eat the cake!" Bob nodded and wagged his tail. As they got closer to the cake, Bob felt a sneeze coming. He tried to hold it in, but he couldn't. "Achoo!" Bob sneezed. The sneeze blew the cake off the table! Bob and Tom laughed and went back to playing with the loop.

Once upon a time there was a poor princess who lived in a tiny castle. She was very sad because she had no friends to play with. One day, she decided to go for a walk in the woods. On her walk, she met an old man who gave her a smile and waved hello. The princess smiled back and decided to stop and talk. The old man asked her why she was so sad and the princess told him she had no friends. The old man thought for a moment and told the princess: "I know a ten-legged spider who would be happy to be your friend. He is looking for a new home near the river." The princess was curious, so she thanked the old man and took his advice. When the princess arrived by the side of the river, she saw the ten-legged spider and the two became fast friends. From then on, the princess was never lonely again. She and the spider played together in the woods every day, and they both lived happily ever after. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big event. All the kids were so excited. They wanted to have fun and play games. There was a crazy game called "Drop the Ball". Everyone wanted to play. A little girl named Sally went to the event with her mom. She saw the crazy game and said, "Mom, I want to play 'Drop the Ball'!" Her mom smiled and said, "Okay, let's go play!" Sally and her mom went to the game. The man at the game said, "To play, you need to drop the ball in the right hole. If you do, you win a prize!" Sally tried and tried, but she could not drop the ball in the right hole. She was sad. Her mom said, "Don't worry, Sally. You can try again later." Sally smiled and said, "Okay, Mom. I will try again later." They went to play other games and had a lot of fun at the event.
