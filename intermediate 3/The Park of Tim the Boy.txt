Category: Intermediate
Title: The Park of Tim the Boy

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park to collect rocks. He was very serious about his rock collection. He had big rocks, small rocks, and shiny rocks. Tim loved all his rocks. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was also collecting rocks. Tim said, "I like your rocks. Can I see them?" Sue showed Tim her rocks. They were different colors and shapes. Tim was surprised. Then, a big bird flew down and took Tim's favorite rock from his head. Tim and Sue were shocked. They ran after the bird, but it flew away. Tim was sad, but Sue said, "Don't worry, we can find more rocks together!" Tim smiled, and they both went to collect new rocks, happy to have a new friend.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run, jump and slide on the grass and the swings. They saw a big tree with many leaves and branches. They wanted to see what was inside. They went to the tree and looked for a hole. They found one and crawled inside. It was dark and quiet. They heard a strange sound. It was like a bird singing, but different. "Hello?" Lily said. "Who is there?" The sound stopped. Then they saw a flash of light. It was a fairy. She had wings, a dress and a flute. She smiled at them. "Hello, children," the fairy said. "I am Flora. I live in this tree. I play the flute to make music for the animals and the plants. Do you like my music?" Lily and Ben nodded. They were amazed. They had never seen a fairy before. They wanted to know more about her. "Can we play with you?" Ben asked. "Can we see the animals and the plants?" Flora nodded. She took their hands and led them out of the hole. They saw many wild things. They saw squirrels, rabbits, birds and flowers. They saw a stream, a bridge and a rainbow. Flora played the flute and the wild things danced and sang. Lily and Ben had fun. They played with Flora and the wild things. They learned many new things. They discovered a magic world in the park. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a unique lizard. This lizard was not like the others. It was very colorful and could change its colors like a rainbow. It lived in a big forest with many trees and animals. One day, the unique lizard met a little girl. The girl was very happy to see the lizard. She wanted to play with it. So, she picked it up and gave it a gentle squeeze. The lizard felt the love from the girl and changed its colors to match her dress. The girl and the unique lizard became best friends. They played together every day in the forest. The other animals saw how happy they were and wanted to be friends too. So, they all played together, making the forest a happy place for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy bear named Bob. Bob loved to eat grapes. One day, he found a big bunch of grapes in the woods. He was so happy! Bob wanted to eat all the grapes by himself. But then, he saw his friends coming. He didn't want to share his grapes. So, he decided to scatter them all around the woods. He thought his friends would not find them. But his friends were very smart. They found all the scattered grapes and started eating them. Bob felt bad for being grumpy and not sharing. He said sorry to his friends, and they all ate the grapes together. They had a fun day in the woods.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. The park had an ancient tree that was very big. Lucy liked to play near the tree. Suddenly, the sky turned dark and it started to rain. The rain made a flood in the park. Lucy and her mom ran to the ancient tree to stay dry. Under the tree, they saw a small bird. The bird was wet and hungry. Lucy said, "Mom, can we feed the bird?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's give it some food to eat." They gave the bird some bread and it ate it happily. The rain stopped, and Lucy, her mom, and the bird were all happy.
