Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Dog

One day, a big girl named Sue made a healthy pie. She put it on the table to cool down. Sue was very happy and proud of her pie. She wanted to share it with her friends. Sue went to find her friends, Tom and Ann. "I made a healthy pie! Come and eat with me," she said. Tom and Ann were very excited. They all ran to Sue's house to eat the pie. But when they got there, the pie was gone! They looked around and saw a little dog with pie on its face. The dog had eaten the pie! Sue, Tom, and Ann laughed. They were sad the pie was gone, but happy the dog liked it too.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max was very hungry, so he went to a little food place with his friend, a cat named Lily. At the food place, they looked at the menu on the wall. The menu had many yummy things to eat. Max and Lily could not decide what to eat. Max said, "I want to eat everything!" Lily said, "We can't eat everything, Max. We have to choose." They started to get upset with each other. They wanted different things from the menu. Then, a nice lady came to help them. She said, "Why don't you both choose one thing and then share?" Max and Lily thought this was a great idea. So, Max picked a big sandwich, and Lily picked some tasty fries. They were so happy to share their food. They loaded their plates with the yummy food and enjoyed eating together. From that day on, Max and Lily always shared their food and never fought again.

Once upon a time, in a humble little town, there was a big mystery. No one knew the number of apples on the big tree. The people wanted to know, but they could not count that high. One day, a little girl named Sally thought of a way to find the number. She asked her friends to help her. They began to count the apples on the tree. They tried their best, but they still could not find the number. Then, something unexpected happened. A kind bird flew down and whispered the number into Sally's ear. Sally was so happy! She told everyone in the town the number of apples on the tree. The people were very surprised and happy too. The mystery was solved, and they all thanked Sally and the kind bird.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small rabbit named Bob. He had a task to find a hidden carrot. The carrot was in a secret place. Bob was very excited to start looking for it. Bob hopped and hopped around the forest. He looked under the trees and behind the rocks. He saw a squirrel and asked, "Have you seen the hidden carrot?" The squirrel said, "No, but keep looking!" So, Bob kept hopping and looking. Finally, Bob found the hidden carrot under a big leaf. He was so happy to finish his task. He took the carrot home and shared it with his family. They all enjoyed the tasty carrot and were proud of Bob for finishing his task.

Once upon a time, there was a man named Tom. Tom liked to play with his big red ball. One day, he kicked the ball too hard and it went far away. Tom ran to get the ball. As he picked it up, he saw a hurt dog. The dog said, "Woof! My leg is hurt." Tom wanted to help the dog, so he kneeled down next to it. Tom said, "Don't worry, dog. I will help you." He took the dog to his home and gave it some food and water. The dog started to feel better. Tom and the dog became best friends and played with the big red ball every day.

One day, a big red bus arrives in town. The bus has a happy face on its front head. It loves to help people and is very generous. The bus waits for everyone to get on and takes them where they want to go. In the town, there is a little girl named Lily. She sees the red bus and wants to ride it. She asks her mom if she can go on the bus. Her mom says yes, and they both get on the bus. The bus is happy to take them to the park. At the park, Lily and her mom have fun. They play on the swings and slide. They see the red bus waiting for them. When it is time to go home, the bus takes them back to their house. They thank the generous bus for a fun day.
