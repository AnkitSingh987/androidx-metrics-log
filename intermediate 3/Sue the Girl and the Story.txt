Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Story

One day, a little girl named Sue went to a new place called Happy Land. She was very excited. In Happy Land, everyone was nice and always said "welcome" to new friends. Sue met a little boy named Tim. "Welcome to Happy Land!" said Tim. They played together all day. They laughed, ran, and had so much fun. But then, Sue fell down and hurt her knee. It was very painful. Tim saw Sue was hurt and he helped her. He said, "Don't worry, Sue. I will take care of you." Tim got a band-aid and put it on Sue's hurt knee. Sue felt better and said, "Thank you, Tim." They learned that in Happy Land, friends help each other when they are hurt. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help friends when they need it. That way, everyone can have fun and be happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play outside with his favorite toy, a long cord. He would run and jump and wag his tail while holding the cord in his mouth. One sunny day, Spot had a foolish idea. He thought it would be fun to bury his cord in the soft dirt. So, he dug a hole and put the cord in it. Then, he covered the cord with dirt and patted it down with his paws. Later, Spot wanted to play with his cord but he couldn't find it. He looked and looked but it was gone. Spot felt sad. He remembered that he had buried it and started to dig. Soon, he found his cord and was happy again. Spot learned not to bury his toys and played with his cord all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a tiny girl called Rose. Rose was only three years old and she loved going for walks in the park. One day, Rose was walking in the park with her mummy and she saw a big, helpful cart. Rose was surprised and shouted, "Wow! What is that!?" Her mummy replied, "That's a cart! It's very helpful, sweetie." Rose was so excited and she asked, "Can I push it?" Her mummy nodded and said, "Yes, you can push the cart." Rose was so happy and she began to push it around the park. She was so proud and she shouted, "Look mummy, I'm pushing it all by myself!" Her mummy smiled and said, "I'm so proud of you, Rose!" Rose was very happy and she pushed the cart around until it was time to go home. What a surprise the cart was! It was so helpful and Rose had so much fun with it.

Once upon a time, there was a rich fox. This fox loved to dress up in pretty clothes. Every day, the fox would wear a new outfit. The other animals in the forest would watch the fox and wish they could dress like him. One day, a little bird saw the fox and said, "Wow, you look so pretty! Can you help me dress up too?" The fox smiled and said, "Of course! I would love to help you." So the fox and the bird went to the fox's big closet filled with clothes. The fox picked out a small hat for the bird to wear. The bird tried on the hat and it fit just right. They both looked at each other and laughed. From that day on, the fox and the bird became best friends, and they loved to play dress up together.

Harry was a three year old who loved spending time on the beach. Every day, his parents would dress him in his navy blue swimsuit and take him to the beach. One day, as he was playing in the sand, he had a funny idea. He decided to vanish from his parents. So he ran straight into the ocean and started swimming away. His parents were horrified and started to look for him. As they shouted from the beach, he shouted back, "Don't worry, I'm here!" He finally swam back to shore and ran to his mummy and daddy who hugged and kissed him. His parents were relieved and Harry was very happy that he had such a funny adventure.
