Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia and the Cloud

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived on a big ranch with her mom, dad, and her dog, Buddy. Mia loved to gaze at the sky and watch the birds fly by. One day, she saw a big, dark cloud coming. She knew something was going to happen. The wind started to blow and the air became harsh. Mia's mom and dad told her to stay inside the house. Buddy was scared, so Mia hugged him tight. They stayed close and waited for the wind to stop. After some time, the wind stopped and the sun came out. Mia, her mom, and her dad went outside to see what had happened. The ranch was a mess, but they were all safe. Mia knew that when she saw the big, dark cloud, it was a sign that something was going to happen. They all worked together to fix the ranch and make it beautiful again.

Once there was a bald man with a big heart. He had a special surprise for his 3 year old daughter. He walked into his daughter's room and said, "Open your hand." His daughter was so curious and excited. She opened her hand and inside was a small box. She asked, "What is it?" In a soft voice, the bald man said, "Open it, and you will see." The little girl opened the box and saw a shiny yellow lollipop inside. "A lollipop!" she exclaimed. The bald man smiled. "Yes, it's a special lollipop just for you." The little girl was so happy. She gave the bald man a big hug and said, "Thank you." He hugged her back and said, "You're welcome."

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She saw a big tree with a sour apple on it. Mia wanted the apple, so she tried to grab it. But it was too high for her to reach. She jumped and jumped, but she could not get the apple. A kind duck saw Mia and asked, "Why are you sad?" Mia said, "I want the sour apple, but I can't grab it." The duck thought for a moment and had an idea. "I can help you," said the duck. Mia sat on the duck's back, and the duck lifted her up. Mia reached out her hand and grabbed the sour apple. She thanked the duck and shared the apple with her new friend. They both laughed and ate the sour apple together. From that day on, Mia and the duck were best friends, and they always helped each other when they needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Bob. Bob loved to perform tricks for his friends. One day, Bob wanted to show his friends a new fancy trick he learned. Bob went to his friend Cat and said, "Look at my new fancy trick!" Cat watched as Bob tried to perform his trick. But it was very cold outside, and Bob slipped on the ice. "Oh no!" said Cat. Bob felt sad because he could not perform his fancy trick. The cold made it too hard for him. His friends felt sad too. They all wished it was not so cold outside. And so, Bob could not show off his new trick, and everyone stayed sad.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She saw a big gate. The gate was fine and pretty. Mia wanted to see what was behind the gate. So, she opened it and went in. Inside the gate, there was a big garden. Mia saw a lot of pretty flowers. She also saw a small rabbit. The rabbit was running around. Mia wanted to play with the rabbit. She tried to catch it, but the rabbit was too fast. Then, something unexpected happened. A big butterfly appeared. The butterfly was very pretty. It had many colors. The rabbit stopped running and looked at the butterfly. The butterfly flew to the rabbit and landed on its nose. The rabbit was not scared. It looked happy. Mia was happy too. She did not want to catch the rabbit anymore. She just wanted to watch the rabbit and the butterfly play together.
