Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Fish and the Sam

One day, a little fish named Tim was swimming in the wet sea. He was having a lot of fun. Tim saw a big shark named Sam. Sam was not a nice shark. He liked to scare little fish like Tim. Tim was scared of Sam. He did not want to be near the big shark. Tim had to decide what to do. He thought and thought. Tim decided to hide from Sam. He swam very fast to a safe place. In the safe place, Tim found other little fish. They were hiding from Sam too. Together, they all decided to stand up to Sam. They swam back to Sam and said, "You can't scare us anymore!" Sam was surprised. He swam away and did not bother them again.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Amy. She loved to eat pasta. Amy would eat pasta every day. Her mom would make the best pasta in town. One morning, Amy did not want to wake up. She wanted to sleep all day. Her mom told her, "Amy, you need to wake up or you will miss the nice pasta for lunch." Amy did not listen and kept sleeping. When Amy woke up, it was too late. The nice pasta was all gone. She felt sad and hungry. Amy learned that she should listen to her mom and wake up on time. From that day on, Amy always woke up early and never missed her favorite pasta again.

Once upon a time there lived a bear who loved to explore. He was called an adventurous bear. Every day, he went to new places and met new friends. One day, Bear saw something shiny in the water. He wanted to investigate. He jumped into the water to take a closer look. Suddenly, Bear felt himself getting swept away by the current in the river. He shouted for help but no one could hear him. Just then, a brave fish swam up to Bear. Fish said, “Don’t worry. I will save you!” Bear was relieved to find a friend. Fish grabbed ahold of Bear and took him to shore. When they got there, Fish said, “Hold on tight! I’m going to give you a hug.” Bear was so happy that he was safe! They hugged and Bear thanked Fish for his bravery and kindness. From then on, Bear was careful whenever he went out exploring. He always made sure to be extra careful near water. Bear was an adventurous bear, but his friend Fish had likely saved him from danger one more time.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play a game. He saw a cheap toy gun at the store. "Mom, can I buy this?" he asked. His mom said yes, and they took it home. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to shoot the toy gun and win a trophy. He set up some cans in the yard. His friend, Sam, came over. "What are you doing?" asked Sam. Tim said, "I want to shoot the cans and get a trophy." Tim and Sam took turns shooting the cans. They both tried very hard. At the end, Tim hit all the cans. He was so happy! His mom gave him a trophy. Tim and Sam smiled and played more games.

Lila and Ben were very happy. They were going to the festival with Mom and Dad. The festival had many things to see and do. There were balloons, games, music, and food. Lila and Ben saw a big tent with a sign that said "Twist and Turn". They wanted to go inside and see what it was. Mom and Dad said yes and followed them. Inside the tent, there were many people who could twist their bodies in amazing ways. They could bend their arms and legs, touch their noses with their toes, and make funny shapes. Lila and Ben watched with wide eyes and clapped their hands. "Wow, they are so good at twisting!" Lila said. "Can we try, too?" Ben asked. Mom and Dad smiled and said they could. They found a mat on the floor and helped Lila and Ben lie down. Then they showed them how to twist their bodies gently and safely. Lila and Ben had fun trying to twist and turn. They laughed and giggled as they made different poses. They felt like they were part of the show. "Look, Mom, I can twist like a pretzel!" Lila said. "Look, Dad, I can twist like a snake!" Ben said. Mom and Dad praised them and took pictures. They were proud of their children. "You are very good at twisting, too!" Mom said. "But you are still our regular Lila and Ben, and we love you very much!" Dad said. Lila and Ben hugged Mom and Dad and thanked them for taking them to the festival. They had a wonderful day.
