Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fat Cat and the Tree

One day, a fat cat went to find a place to settle. He walked and walked until he found a big tree. The tree had a lot of picks on the ground. The fat cat thought it was a good place to settle. "Hello, tree," said the fat cat. "Can I settle here?" The tree did not say anything, but the fat cat knew it was okay. So, the fat cat picked up the picks and made a soft bed under the tree. He was very happy. Soon, a little bird came by. The bird saw the fat cat and said, "Hello, fat cat! Why are you here?" The fat cat said, "I found this nice tree and I want to settle here." The little bird was happy to have a new friend. They played and laughed together under the big tree.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a wallet on the floor. It was a very organized wallet with all the cards and money in their right places. Tim did not know whose wallet it was, so he decided to ask his mom for help. "Mom," Tim asked, "I found this wallet. What should I do with it?" His mom thought for a moment and said, "We should try to find the person who lost it. They must be very sad without it." Together, Tim and his mom went to the park where Tim found the wallet. They asked the people there if they lost a wallet. Finally, they met a man who said it was his. The man was so happy to see his wallet again. He thanked Tim and his mom for being so kind and helpful. In the end, Tim felt good about doing the right thing. He learned that it is important to help others when they are in need. And his mom was very proud of him for being such a caring and honest boy.

Once upon a time there was a cop who had an important job. His job was to protect everyone. He felt it was his duty to help keep everyone safe. One day, there was someone causing trouble in the town. He was very smelly and made lots of noise. The cop saw him and tried to catch him, but the smelly man ran away. Then the cop found a way to protect the town from the smelly man. He figured out a plan to keep the town safe. He organized a team of other cops and they watched the town to make sure the smelly man didn't get back in. They were able to protect the town from the smelly man. Everyone was happy and grateful to the cop for his hard work. The town was safe and sound and the cop was proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, red bucket. He loved his bucket very much. One day, Tim took his bucket to the park to play in the sand. At the park, Tim met a noisy bird. The bird liked to sing and talk. The bird said, "Hello, Tim! What do you have there?" Tim replied, "I have my big, red bucket. I will use it to play in the sand." Tim and the noisy bird played together in the sand. They filled the bucket with sand and made a big sand castle. They had so much fun, they wanted to increase the size of their sand castle. So, Tim and the noisy bird worked hard to make their sand castle bigger. They used the big, red bucket to carry more sand. The sand castle grew and grew. At the end of the day, Tim and the noisy bird looked at their huge sand castle. They were very proud of their work. Tim said, "Thank you, noisy bird, for helping me make this big sand castle!" The noisy bird replied, "You're welcome, Tim! I had fun playing with you and your big, red bucket!"

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Jane. She had a big body and loved to wear a pretty ring on her finger. Jane lived in a small house with her cat, Tom. One day, Jane lost her pretty ring. She looked everywhere for it. She looked in the house and in the yard. She asked Tom the cat if he had seen it, but he just meowed. Finally, Jane found her ring outside near a big tree. She was so happy! She put the ring back on her finger and danced around the tree with Tom the cat. They played all day and had lots of fun.
