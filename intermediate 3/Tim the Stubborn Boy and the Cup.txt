Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Stubborn Boy and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little boy named Tim. He did not like to help his mom with chores. One day, his mom asked him to help her measure the soap for the dishwasher. Tim did not want to do it, but his mom said it was important. So, Tim took the cup and put the soap in it. He measured the soap carefully and poured it into the dishwasher. Then, he and his mom put the dirty dishes in the dishwasher. They closed the door and turned it on. The dishwasher made a loud noise as it cleaned the dishes. After the dishwasher was done, Tim and his mom took out the clean dishes. They were so shiny and clean. Tim felt proud that he had helped his mom. From that day on, Tim was not so stubborn about helping with chores. He learned that helping his mom was fun and made him feel good.

Once upon a time there were two friends called Lucy and Jones. They were best friends and loved playing together. One day they were out at the beach. Lucy spotted a hook and wanted to take it home. So she decided to squeeze it really tightly to get a good grip. They started walking back with the hook. Along the way they met a lively seagull. It started jumping around and made them laugh. Soon they were talking and playing with it. They had a great time playing with the seagull. But when Lucy went to squeeze the hook again, it was gone! It had slipped out of her hands. The two friends searched everywhere. Eventually, Lucy spotted the hook and ran to pick it up. She gave it a big squeeze and hugged it tightly. From then on, Lucy and Jones went to the beach every day and played with the seagull. They were the best of friends!

One day, a cute little dog named Spot went to the park with his friend, a small cat named Fluffy. They played with a ball and ran around. They were very happy. Spot saw a big bucket near the sand. He told Fluffy, "Let's use the bucket to make a sand castle!" Fluffy said, "Yes, that sounds fun!" So, they started to build the castle together. They worked hard and made a big, pretty castle. A bossy bird flew by and saw the castle. The bird said, "I order you to give me the castle. It is mine now!" Spot and Fluffy were scared, but they had an idea. They kicked the ball at the castle, and it fell down. The bossy bird flew away, sad. Spot and Fluffy built a new sand castle, even bigger and prettier than before. They learned it is good to work together and stand up to bossy birds. The moral of the story is to work together and not let others take what is yours.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived on a big ranch with his mom and dad. They had a black dog named Spot. Tim loved to play with Spot every day. One day, Tim and Spot were playing outside. They saw a big black bird fly down and start to eat their food. Tim said, "No, bird! That is our food!" But the bird did not listen. It kept eating and eating. Tim had an idea. He called Spot over and said, "Spot, help me scare the bird away!" Spot barked and ran at the bird. The bird got scared and flew away. Tim and Spot were happy they saved their food. They went inside to tell mom and dad about their big adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to play with his cardboard toys. One day, he saw a big, healthy tree in the park. Tim wanted to make a new toy from the tree. Tim met a wise old man who told him, "Trees are healthy and good for us. We should not hurt them." Tim did not listen and took a small branch from the tree. The next day, he saw that the tree was not healthy anymore. Tim felt sad and asked the wise old man for help. The old man said, "Plant a new tree and take care of it." Tim did that, and soon, the new tree grew big and healthy. Tim learned that we must care for trees and not hurt them.
