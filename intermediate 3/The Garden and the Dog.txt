Category: Intermediate
Title: The Garden and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big garden. In the garden, there was a cat and a dog. They liked to play together. They were ready to play a game. The game was to catch a ball. The cat threw the ball to the dog. The dog tried to catch the ball, but it was too fast. The ball went far away. The dog ran after the ball. The cat waited in the garden. The dog found the ball near the road. He was happy to catch it. But when he tried to go back to the garden, a big car came. The dog was not careful. The car hit the dog. The cat was sad. The dog did not come back to play in the garden.

---

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big statue in the middle of the park. The statue was of a dog. It was an adorable dog with a big smile. Tim wanted to play with the statue. He went to touch it, but something unexpected happened. The statue started to move! It was not a statue at all, but a real dog! The dog had been very still, like a statue. Tim and the dog became best friends. They played together all day at the park. They ran and jumped and had so much fun. Tim was very happy that the statue was really a dog. They were friends forever.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a fair girl with a big heart. One day, she found a velvet toy bunny in the park. She picked it up and said, "You belong to me now." Lily took the velvet bunny home and played with it all day. But her friend Tim saw the bunny and said, "Hey, that bunny belongs to me! I lost it in the park!" Lily and Tim got mad at each other. They both wanted the bunny. Lily said, "No, I found it, so it is mine!" Tim said, "But it was mine first!" They could not agree, so they asked their moms for help. Their moms said they had to share the bunny. But Lily and Tim did not want to share. They pulled and pulled on the bunny. The velvet bunny ripped in half. Now, neither Lily nor Tim could play with the bunny. They were both very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big, hard ball made of leather. He loved to play with his ball every day. One day, he took his ball to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Tim and his friends played a game. They would wave their hands and try to catch the hard leather ball. They laughed and had lots of fun. The sun was shining, and it was a beautiful day. But then, something bad happened. Tim threw the hard leather ball very high, and it went too far. The ball went into the street, and a big truck came by. The truck ran over the ball and broke it. Tim and his friends were very sad. They could not play with the ball anymore.

---

Once upon a time, there was a nice dog named Buddy. Buddy was a very good dog. One day, Buddy saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was sad and scared. Buddy wanted to help the cat, but he did not know how. Buddy had an idea. He would go to the veterinarian to ask for help. The veterinarian was nice and smart. Buddy walked to the veterinarian's place and told them about the cat. The veterinarian said, "I will help you, Buddy. Let's go save the cat." Buddy and the veterinarian went to the tree. The veterinarian climbed the tree and got the cat down. Buddy was happy to see the cat safe. The cat was happy too. They both thanked the veterinarian for their help. Buddy and the cat became friends and they all played together. From that day on, Buddy learned to mind and help others when they need it.
