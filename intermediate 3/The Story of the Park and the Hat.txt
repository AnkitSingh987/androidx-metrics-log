Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Park and the Hat

One day, a funny person put on a show in the park. The person wore a big hat and a red nose. They made everyone laugh with their silly faces and tricks. The funny person could juggle and spin plates, too. The funny person said, "Look at me, I can stand on one foot!" The children laughed and tried to do it too. The person then made a funny face and said, "Can you make a face like this?" The children tried, and everyone laughed. At the end of the show, everyone was happy. They clapped their hands to say thank you. The funny person smiled and bowed. They said, "Thank you for coming to my show!" Everyone left the park with big smiles, and they all hoped the funny person would come back again soon.

Once upon a time, a loyal dog named Max lived in a small house with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They loved to play in the big yard and go for long walks. One day, while they were walking, Max found a shiny mineral on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and showed it to Tim. Tim was very happy and wanted to take the mineral home. But a big bird saw the mineral and wanted it too. The bird tried to take the mineral from Max, but Max did not let go. He was a loyal friend to Tim, and he knew Tim wanted the mineral. The bird and Max pulled and pulled, but Max would not let go. Tim helped Max, and together, they made the bird go away. Tim and Max went home with the shiny mineral. They were very happy, and they knew they would always be loyal friends. They played in the yard with the mineral, and they knew they could face any problem together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. She had a new brush and she was so excited to use it. She asked her Mom to help her put it on. Mom said, “Let’s get it just right.” Jane carefully placed the brush on her head. She tried to move it around, but it was too small. Mom said, “Let me help you.” She gently removed the brush and said, “This one isn’t fit for you. We need to find another one.” They went to the store and found a bigger brush. Jane carefully placed it on her head and it fit perfectly. She smiled and said, “I like this one! Can I keep it?” Mom smiled and said, “Yes, you may.” And they lived happily ever after with the new brush!

Once upon a time, there was a busy ant. This ant loved to load things onto his back. One day, he found a big mushroom. The ant wanted to take the mushroom to his home. On his way, he met a mean bug. The bug wanted the mushroom too. They both did not want to share. The ant and the bug began to fight for the mushroom. The fight was very loud. A big bird heard the noise. The bird was hungry. It saw the ant and the bug fighting. The bird swooped down and ate both the ant and the bug. The mushroom was left all alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was fearful of a big metal box in his room. One day, he wanted to look inside the box. He asked his mommy, "Can I open the box?" Mommy said, "Yes, but remember to be careful, Tim." Tim slowly opened the big metal box. He found a lot of toys inside! He was very happy and not fearful anymore. Tim played with the toys all day long. From that day on, he loved the big metal box.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a big, old toy box. He loved to play with all his toys. Yesterday, his mom did not permit him to play with his toys because he had to clean his room. Tim was sad but he cleaned his room. While cleaning, he found an old toy car under his bed. It was a toy car he forgot about. Tim was happy and wanted to play with it. He asked his mom if he could play with the old toy car. His mom smiled and said yes. The toy car was magic! When Tim played with the car, it grew big and he could ride in it. Tim and his mom went for a fun ride in the magic car.
