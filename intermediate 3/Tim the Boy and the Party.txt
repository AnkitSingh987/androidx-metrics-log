Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Party

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a slow walker. One day, he got an invitation to a party. He was very happy. Tim wanted to grab his favorite toy to take to the party. But when he went to his room, the toy was not there. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. Then, Tim saw his little sister holding the toy. She was playing with it! Tim was mad, but he had an idea. He gave his sister the invitation to the party. Now, they could both go to the party and play with the toy together. The party was fun, and Tim and his sister had a great time.

One day, a boy and a girl went to play by the river. They saw a pretty, fair kayak. The boy said, "Let's go for a ride!" The girl said, "Yes, let's go!" They got in the kayak and started to paddle. As they went down the river, they saw a big fish. The fish said, "Hello, can you show me where my home is?" The boy and the girl looked at each other and said, "We can try!" So they paddled down the river, looking for the fish's home. Suddenly, the fish jumped out of the water and turned into a big, friendly bird! The bird said, "Thank you for trying to help me. I was a bird all along, but I was stuck as a fish! You were so kind, and now I am free!" The boy and the girl were very surprised, but they were happy that they could help their new bird friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was three-years-old and she loved swimming in the big blue pool. Every day, she would laugh and splash around in the water. One day, the little girl got frustrated. She had been trying to swim all day, but it just seemed too hard. Suddenly, her mom came to the pool. "Come on sweetie," said her mom. "Let's give it a try!" But no matter how hard the little girl tried, she just couldn't swim. She felt more and more frustrated until at last, she started to cry. "Sorry, mom," she said sadly. But her mom just smiled. "Don't give up, sweetie," said her mom. "You will get there soon enough!" But it was too late. The little girl felt so frustrated that she never went to the pool again. She never laughed in the pool, never learned to swim and never enjoyed it again.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house near the woods. The man was very careful when he went to the woods to pick fruits. He didn't want to get lost. One day, while picking fruits, the man saw a big, round thing in the sky. He stared at it for a long time. The big, round thing stared back at him. The man was scared and ran back to his wife. "Look at the sky!" the man said to his wife. "There is a big, round thing staring at me!" The wife looked up at the sky and said, "That's just the moon. It's always there at night. Don't be scared." The man felt better and laughed. He was not scared of the moon anymore. From that day on, the man and his wife would stare at the moon together before going to bed. They were happy and lived a careful life.

Mommy and Andy were walking outside. As they passed by the office, Andy noticed the big building and stopped. He looked up at it, admiring the tall windows. "It's so tall, mommy!" Andy said in amazement. Mommy smiled and nodded. "It sure is big, isn't it?" As they walked closer to the office, they noticed two people arguing outside the door. "No way! That's not safe!" one man screamed. "But I want to go inside," the other man replied. Mommy grabbed Andy's hand and quickly stepped away. "We better go back, Andy," Mommy said. Andy was disappointed. He wanted to admire the office closer, but it was unsafe. Mommy was right that it was best for them to go home.
