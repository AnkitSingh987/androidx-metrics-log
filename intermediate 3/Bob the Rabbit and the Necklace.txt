Title: Bob the Rabbit and the Necklace

Once upon a time there was a little rabbit named Bob. Bob was very curious and always looking for new adventures. One day, he decided to explore the woods. As he hopped around, he found a shiny gem in the dirt. He thought to himself “this gem is so yummy!” and he kept it. Bob's friend, a curious cat named Pippin, spotted Bob holding the gem. Pippin said “What’s that you have there? Is it a yummy gem?” Bob smiled and said “Yes! I found it in the woods.” Suddenly, Pippin said “We should make a necklace with the gem so you never forget about it.” They both hopped and skipped around the woods, looking for materials to make the necklace. After a few minutes, they had made a necklace with the gem in the middle. Bob put the necklace around his neck so he would never forget about his yummy gem. The moral of the story is to never forget the most special things in life.

Once upon a time, there was a red car. The red car was very fast. It loved to race with other cars. One day, the red car wanted to change something. It wanted to be the fastest car ever. The red car needed more fuel to go faster. It found a new fuel that was tasty. The red car thought the tasty fuel would make it go faster. So, the red car drank the tasty fuel. It felt very strong and fast. The red car raced with other cars. It was winning! But then, the red car started to feel sick. The tasty fuel was not good for the car. The red car stopped and went back to its old fuel. It learned that it is better to be safe and healthy than to be the fastest.

Once upon a time there was a good queen and she had a worry. Every day she would worry about what would happen in the future. One day, the queen thought of a good idea. She decided to take a walk around her kingdom and talk to her people. She asked them what made them happy and what worried them. The good queen listened to the worries of her people and decided to do something to help them. She made sure that everyone in her kingdom had enough food, clothes and toys to play with. She also made sure that everyone had a warm place to sleep and stay safe. The more time the queen spent helping her people, the less she worried. Everyone was so happy and she was very proud of herself. The queen realized that helping others made her feel very good and her worries melted away. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big garden. In the garden, there were many pretty flowers and tall trees. The garden was a fun place for all the animals to play. One day, a graceful deer came to the garden. The deer liked to dance and jump. All the animals liked to watch her dance. They said she was the best dancer in the garden. A little bird wanted to dance too. The bird asked the deer to teach her. The deer said, "I can try, but you must insist on practicing." The bird promised to practice every day. They danced and played in the garden, and soon, the bird could dance just like the deer.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to swing and slide and run. They saw a big pole with a shiny ball on top. It was a Christmas pole. They wanted to touch the ball. "Let's go see the ball," Lily said. "OK," Ben said. They ran to the pole and reached for the ball. But the ball was very high. They could not get it. "Maybe we can climb the pole," Ben said. He tried to wrap his arms and legs around the pole. But the pole was very slippery. He slid down and fell on the grass. "Ouch!" he said. Lily tried to help him. She pulled his arm. But the ball was still too high. "Maybe we can steal the ball," Lily said. She looked around. She saw a big stick. She picked it up. "Look, I have a stick," she said. She ran to the pole and poked the ball with the stick. The ball wobbled and shook. It was very fragile. It broke and fell on the ground. It made a loud noise. "Uh-oh," Lily said. Ben looked at the ball. It was broken and sad. He felt bad. "That was not nice," he said. Lily felt bad too. She dropped the stick. "I'm sorry," she said. They heard a voice. It was the park keeper. He was angry. "Who broke the ball?" he said. Lily and Ben were scared. They ran away. They did not play in the park anymore. They learned not to steal.
