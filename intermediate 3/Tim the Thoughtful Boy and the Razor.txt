Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Thoughtful Boy and the Razor

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a thoughtful boy named Tim. He had a toy razor that he loved to play with. He would pretend to shave like his dad. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came to play. "Can I play with your razor?" Sue asked. Tim nodded and said, "Yes, you can." They took turns pretending to shave and had lots of fun. When it was time for Sue to go home, they put the razor away. They waved goodbye and Tim felt happy that he had shared his toy with his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to learn new things. One day, she wanted to learn how to dance. She had a friend named Sam who was gifted at dancing. Sam had a good rhythm when he danced. Amy asked Sam to teach her how to dance. Sam was happy to help Amy learn. They danced together every day. They laughed and had fun. One day, while they were dancing, something unexpected happened. A cat jumped into their dance area and started to dance too! The cat had a great rhythm and danced very well. Amy and Sam were surprised to see the cat dance. Amy and Sam became friends with the cat. They learned that the cat was also gifted at dancing. Now, Amy, Sam, and the cat danced together every day. They all learned new dance moves from each other. And they lived happily, dancing together, and having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby bird named Tim. Tim lived in a small cage. He liked to sing and play. One day, Tim saw a big cake outside his cage. He wanted to eat it, but he could not get out. Tim's friend, a little girl named Sue, saw the cake too. She knew that Tim wanted the cake. Sue thought of a surprise for Tim. She took the cake and put it in a box. Then, she opened the cage and let Tim out. Tim flew out of the cage and saw the box. He opened the box and found the cake. He was very happy and shared the cake with Sue. They both learned that sharing is caring and being kind to friends brings good surprises.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to school. She had a loyal dog named Max. Max loved to follow Lucy everywhere. When Lucy went to school, Max wanted to go too. But dogs cannot go to school. At school, Lucy's friend Tim said, "I want to see Max!" Lucy said, "Max is at home. Dogs cannot come to school." Tim was sad. He wanted to play with Max. After school, Lucy and Tim went to Lucy's house. They found Max playing in the mud. Max was very dirty. Lucy said, "Max, you need a wash!" Tim and Lucy washed Max with water and soap. Max was happy and clean again. Lucy, Tim, and Max played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little squirrel. The squirrel loved to study. He would study all day long. He had a friend, a big bird, who would help him study. One day, the squirrel saw an angry dog. The squirrel was scared, but he had an idea. The squirrel and the big bird made a plan. They would study together and find a way to make the angry dog happy. They studied and studied, and they found out that the angry dog loved to play with a ball. So, they got a ball and went to the dog. The squirrel and the big bird gave the ball to the angry dog. The dog was surprised and happy. He played with the ball and was not angry anymore. The squirrel, the big bird, and the dog became friends. They all played together, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a spade on the ground. Tim picked it up and started to dig in the dirt. He was very happy and liked to dig. Tim saw a bug on the ground. The bug was confused and did not know where to go. Tim wanted to help the bug, so he used the spade to make a path for the bug. The bug followed the path and found its home. Later, Tim felt cold. He started to freeze. He put the spade down and ran back inside his house. Tim's mom gave him a warm hug and a hot cup of milk. Tim was not cold anymore, and he smiled.
