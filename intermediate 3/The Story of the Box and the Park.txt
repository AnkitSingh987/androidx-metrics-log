Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Box and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong metal box. It was in a park where many kids played. The metal was shiny and all the kids liked it. They would touch it and play with it every day. But one day, a little boy tried to tear a piece of the metal off the box. He wanted to take it home with him. The other kids saw the little boy and told him, "No, don't tear the metal. We all like it and want to play with it too." The little boy felt sad and stopped trying to tear the metal. He knew it was not nice to take things that were for everyone to enjoy. From that day on, the kids in the park took turns playing with the big, strong metal box. They made sure it stayed nice and shiny for everyone to enjoy. The little boy learned that sharing and playing together was better than having the metal all to himself. And the metal box was always available for everyone to play with and have fun.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a big onion farm. Many animals lived on this farm. They were all friends and played together every day. One day, a small rabbit found a big onion in the field. He wanted to share it with his friends. The rabbit went to his friends and said, "Let's eat this onion together!" But some friends refused to eat the onion. They said, "We don't like onions. They make us cry." The rabbit felt sad but he still wanted to share. The rabbit went to his other friends and asked them to eat the onion. They agreed and ate the onion together. They all had tears in their eyes, but they were happy. The friends who refused to eat the onion saw this and felt sad. They learned that sometimes, trying new things with friends is more important than what you like or don't like. The moral of the story is: Don't refuse to try new things, especially with friends. You might find happiness in unexpected places.

Once there was a young girl with an orange dress on. She was out in the park with her mom. She saw a seat and ran over to it. She loved how colorful it was, with bright green and yellow stripes. She sat on it, feeling very excited. Next, the girl saw a little bird flying up in the sky. It was orange and she liked the color even more when she saw it in the sky. She waved at it with a happy smile on her face. Soon, the girl saw another young girl standing near her seat. She was wearing a dress just like the girl's, an orange dress. The young girl greeted her with a friendly hello. The first girl said hi back and smiled. Then the two girls talked and laughed together. Finally, the girls went off to play in the park, leaving the seat behind them. It was such a fun time!

Lily loved to paint. She had many colors and brushes and papers. They were her equipment. She liked to make pictures of flowers and animals and rainbows. She was very happy when she painted. One day, she wanted to paint a big picture on the wall. She asked her mom if she could do it. Her mom said no. She said the wall was not for painting. She said Lily could paint on paper or canvas. Lily was sad. She really wanted to paint on the wall. She waited until her mom was busy. Then she took her equipment and went to the wall. She started to paint a mighty lion. She used yellow and brown and black. She made a big mane and a long tail and sharp teeth. She was very proud of her lion. But then her mom saw her. She was very angry. She said Lily was naughty. She said she had to stop painting and clean the wall. She said she had to say sorry. Lily was scared. She did not want to make her mom angry. She did not want to clean the wall. She did not want to say sorry. She wanted to keep her lion. She started to cry. She said she was sorry. She said she liked her lion. She said she did not mean to be naughty. Her mom hugged her. She said she loved her. She said she understood. She said she liked her lion too. But she said the wall was not for painting. She said she had to clean it anyway. She said she would help her. She said they could paint another lion on paper or canvas later. Lily nodded. She wiped her tears. She took a cloth and some water. She started to clean the wall with her mom. She was still sad, but she felt better. She knew her mom was not angry anymore. She knew she could paint again. She knew she had a mighty lion in her heart.
