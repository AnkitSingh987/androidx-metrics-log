Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bug and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Tim. Tim was a delicate bug who had a special power. He could glow in the dark. At night, Tim would use his glow to help his friends find their way home. One day, Tim found a big box full of stuff. He wanted to keep it all for himself. But then, Tim thought about his friends who needed some of the stuff too. He decided to share the stuff with everyone. Tim's friends were so happy that he shared the stuff with them. They all played together and had lots of fun. Tim learned that sharing with others made him feel good inside. And every night, his glow shined even brighter, lighting up the whole forest. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can make you feel happy and bright.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park. In the park, he saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a big, red ball. Tom wanted to play with the ball too. Tom said, "Can you show me how to play with the ball?" Sue was happy to show him. They played with the ball and had lots of fun. But then, Tom had an urge to kick the ball very hard. Tom kicked the ball so hard that it flew far away. They both looked for the ball, but they could not find it. Sue was very sad and Tom felt sorry. In the end, they never found the ball. Sue was still sad and Tom was still sorry. They both went home with a bad feeling.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his friends. One day, they decided to play a game with a pole. They wanted to see who could use the pole to jump the highest. They were very competitive. Tim's friend, Tom, said, "I bet I can jump higher than you!" Tim replied, "No, I can jump higher!" They took turns using the pole to jump. They laughed and had a lot of fun. But then, Tim's little sister, Sue, came to play. She wanted to try using the pole too. Tim and Tom didn't want to let her play because they were competitive. Sue was sad and started to cry. Tim's mom came outside and saw Sue crying. She told Tim and Tom, "You should let Sue play too. It's more fun when everyone can play together." Tim and Tom thought about it and decided to let Sue join the game. They all took turns using the pole to jump. They had even more fun playing together. The moral of the story is that it's more enjoyable to include everyone and not be too competitive.

One day, a little girl named Sally was excited to go outside. She put on her boots and coat. It was wet outside, and she wanted to jump in puddles. Sally saw a big puddle and ran to it. As Sally ran, she did not see a banana peel on the ground. She stepped on it and slipped. "Oh no!" she said. Sally's boot came off when she slipped. She was sad and did not know what to do. Just then, a friendly dog came by. The dog picked up Sally's boot in its mouth and brought it to her. "Thank you, dog!" Sally said. She put her boot back on and they played together, jumping in puddles all day.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Anna and her mom were making pasta for dinner. They were having fun and laughing in the kitchen. They were so happy that they forget to turn off the stove. The pasta started to get very hot and smoke came out of the pot. Anna's mom saw the smoke and said, "Oh no! The pasta is dangerous now! We need to turn off the stove!" Anna quickly turned off the stove and they both sighed with relief. They were glad that they stopped the dangerous pasta in time. Just then, they heard a knock on the door. It was their neighbor, Mr. Brown. He smelled the pasta and wanted to make sure they were okay. Anna's mom said, "Thank you for checking on us, Mr. Brown!" They all laughed about the silly pasta adventure and decided to have dinner together. The unexpected visitor made their day even more special.
