Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Bear and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little bear named Ben. He loved to collect things. He had many toys, leaves, and rocks in his room. Ben was a very dependable bear. His friends knew they could always count on him. One day, Ben went for a walk in the forest. He wanted to collect more things for his room. He found some pretty flowers and colorful stones. Ben was very happy. He put them all in his bag and went back home. When Ben got home, his friends were waiting for him. They needed help with a big project. Ben was a dependable bear, so he put his new things away and helped his friends. They all worked together and finished the project. Everyone was happy because Ben was a good friend.

Tom and Lily went to the zoo with their mom. They saw many animals, like lions, monkeys, and birds. But their favorite was the seal pool. The seals were big and gray, and they swam and jumped in the water. "Look, mom, the seals are so funny!" Lily said, pointing at a seal that was playing with a ball. "Yes, they are, sweetie. Do you want to feed them?" mom asked. "Yes, yes, please!" Tom and Lily said together. Mom bought some fish from a man and gave them to Tom and Lily. They walked to the edge of the pool, where some seals were waiting. "Be careful, kids. Don't touch the seals, they might bite. Just throw the fish to them," mom said. Tom and Lily nodded and threw the fish to the seals. The seals caught the fish with their mouths and made happy noises. "Wow, they are so smart!" Tom said. But Lily wanted to touch a seal. She saw a seal that was close to her. It looked friendly and cute. Lily reached out her hand and touched the seal's head. "Ouch!" Lily cried. The seal bit her finger. It hurt a lot. Mom ran to Lily and took her hand. She saw blood on her finger. "Oh, no, Lily, what did you do?" mom asked. "I touched the seal, mom. I wanted to pet it," Lily said, sobbing. Mom hugged Lily and said, "I'm sorry, honey, but you can't touch the seals. They are wild animals, not pets. They don't like to be touched by strangers. You have to be careful and listen to me." Lily nodded and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I won't touch the seals again." Mom took Lily to a nurse, who cleaned and bandaged her finger. Tom followed them, feeling nervous. "Are you okay, Lily?" Tom asked. Lily smiled and said, "I'm okay, Tom. Thank you. The seal didn't mean to hurt me. It was just scared." Tom hugged Lily and said, "I'm glad you're okay, Lily. I love you." "I love you too, Tom," Lily said. Mom smiled and said, "I love you both, kids. Now, let's go see some other animals, okay?" Tom and Lily nodded and said, "Okay, mom." They left the seal pool and went to see some other animals. They had fun, but they never touched the seals again.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Max. Max loved to play with his favorite toy, a long wire. He would run and jump with the wire in his mouth. One day, Max's friend, a little bird, asked Max, "Why do you always play with that wire?" Max just gave a little shrug. He didn't know why, but he loved his wire so much. Max and the little bird played together with the wire all day. They had so much fun, and Max was always there to help his friend. The little bird knew that Max was a dependable friend, and they were happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, filthy dishwasher. It was sad because it was so dirty. Every day, it would say, "Please, clean me." One day, a little girl heard the dishwasher's demand. She wanted to help. She got a wet cloth and started to clean the dishwasher. She scrubbed and scrubbed until it was shiny and clean. Then, something unexpected happened. The dishwasher started to talk! It said, "Thank you for cleaning me. I am a magic dishwasher. Because you helped me, I will help you too." From that day on, the dishwasher would clean all the dishes by itself. The little girl and the dishwasher became best friends and lived happily ever after.
