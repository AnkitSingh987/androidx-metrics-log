Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pin

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his ball. One day, Tim saw a pin on the floor. He was scared because the pin was sharp. Tim wanted to throw the pin away so it would not hurt anyone. He picked up the pin and tried to throw it in the trash. But he missed and the pin fell back on the floor. Tim's mom came into the room and stepped on the pin. She was hurt and cried. Tim felt sad because he did not throw the pin away right. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a monkey named Max. Max loved to wear hats. One day, he found a big hat. The hat was difficult to wear. Max wanted to wear the hat to show his friends. Max went to his friend, the elephant. "Look at my hat!" said Max. The elephant said, "Your hat is too big!" Max was sad. He wanted to wear the hat, but it was too difficult. Max went home and thought. He had an idea. He cut the hat into two smaller hats. Max gave one hat to the elephant. They both wore the hats and were very happy. The difficult hat was now easy to wear.

Ben and Lily were playing in the yard when they saw dark clouds in the sky. They heard a loud noise like a drum. It was thunder. "Let's go inside, Ben. It's going to rain," Lily said. "No, I want to stay and watch the lightning. It's bright and pretty," Ben said. Lily was scared of the lightning. She did not think it was pretty. She thought it was scary and dangerous. She ran to the door and opened it. "Come on, Ben. Please come inside with me," she said. Ben did not listen. He stayed in the yard and looked at the sky. He saw a big flash of lightning. It was yellow and white. It made a zigzag shape. Ben thought it was cool. He wanted to discover more about it. He ran to the fence and climbed on it. He wanted to get closer to the lightning. He did not know that it was a bad idea. Lily saw Ben on the fence. She was very worried. She knew that the fence was made of metal. She knew that metal could attract lightning. She knew that Ben could get hurt. "Ben, get down from there! It's not safe!" she shouted. But Ben did not hear her. He was too busy looking at the lightning. He did not see another flash coming his way. He did not feel the shock that hit him. Lily screamed. She ran to Ben and pulled him off the fence. He was not moving. He was not breathing. He was not alive. Lily cried. She wished she could bring Ben back. She wished he had listened to her. She wished they had gone inside when the thunder started. She wished they had played a mild game instead of watching the lightning. She wished she had not lost her brother.

Kathy wanted to know more. She had been asking questions all day. She saw a pigeon and asked it a question: "What do you do?" The pigeon cooed an original song, but Kathy couldn't understand it. She asked another question: "What is your name?" The pigeon flew away and Kathy was sad. She asked a different bird, but it didn't answer. She asked every animal in the forest, but none of them could answer her questions. Kathy was frustrated, and she started to cry. Her tears fell like rain, soaking the ground. She cried until she had no more tears to cry. She realised that no one would ever answer her questions. Kathy went home feeling empty and alone, and she vowed never to question anything ever again.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was an independent dog who liked to go on walks all by himself. He loved to explore the park and run around with his friends. One day, Max found a little lost cat. The cat was scared and didn't know how to get home. Max wanted to help and lead the cat back to her house. They walked together through the park, across the street, and finally found the cat's home. The cat's family was so happy to see her. They thanked Max for being a good friend and helping her. From that day on, Max and the cat became best friends. They played together and learned about each other's history. Max and the cat had many fun adventures, and they were always there for each other.
