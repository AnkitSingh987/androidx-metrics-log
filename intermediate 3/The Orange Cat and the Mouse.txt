Category: Intermediate
Title: The Orange Cat and the Mouse

One day, a big orange cat went to a museum. The museum had many nice things to see. The cat was there to protect the museum. At night, a little mouse came to the museum. The mouse wanted to take a pretty thing. The orange cat saw the mouse and said, "You cannot take that. I protect the museum." The mouse said, "I do not want to take anything. I want to protect the museum too!" The orange cat was surprised. They became friends and protected the museum together.

Molly was so excited. It was her first time to go on a slide tour. She hopped in the car with her mom and dad, and they drove away. When they arrived, there were so many fun slides. It was so exciting! Molly couldn't wait to slide down the first one. Molly slid down the first slide, and it felt so wet! She slid down the next slide, and the one after that. She slides down so many different slides! After sliding down all the slides, Molly was so tired. She was very happy and wet. She had so much fun on her first slide tour!

One day, a pale boy named Tim went to his friend's house to play. They liked to watch television together. They were watching their favorite show when Tim's friend said, "I'm hungry. Let's go to the kitchen and get some food." When they left the room, Tim forgot to close the door. They were eating in the kitchen when they heard a loud noise from the living room. They ran back to see what happened. The television was on the floor! It had fallen down because the door was not closed. Tim and his friend were sad. The television was broken. Tim said, "I'm sorry I didn't close the door." His friend said, "It's okay. We can still play together without the television." They learned that it's important to close doors and that they can have fun without watching television.

Once upon a time, there was a big, proud lion named Leo. Leo lived in a beautiful forest with his friends. They liked to play games and have fun together. One day, while they were playing, a little bird came to them. The bird said, "Help! There is an emergency! My friend is stuck in a tree!" Leo and his friends went to help the bird's friend. They found the little bird stuck high up in the tree. Leo yawned, he was very tired from playing, but he knew he had to help. Leo climbed the tree and saved the little bird. The bird was so thankful to Leo and his friends. They all became good friends and continued to play together in the beautiful forest. And Leo felt very proud of himself for helping his new friends.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play in the park. They had a lot of toys, but their favorite was a big red car that they could ride together. They loved to zoom around the grass and pretend they were racers. One day, they saw a new boy in the park. He had a cool bike that had shiny wheels and a loud horn. Tom and Lily gazed at the bike and wished they had one too. They forgot about their car and ran to the boy. They asked him if they could try his bike. The boy was not very nice. He said no and laughed at them. He said their car was old and ugly and he would never trade with them. He rode away, leaving Tom and Lily sad and angry. They decided to get back at him. They waited until he parked his bike near a tree and went to play with some other kids. Then they sneaked up to the bike and pushed it hard. The bike rolled down a hill and crashed into a fence. It was a big wreck. The wheels were bent, the horn was broken, and the paint was scratched. Tom and Lily felt happy for a moment. They thought they had taught the boy a lesson. But then they heard him cry. He ran to his bike and saw the damage. He was very upset. He loved his bike and it was a gift from his grandpa. He looked around and saw Tom and Lily. He knew they had done it. He yelled at them and told them they were mean and bad. Tom and Lily felt sorry. They realized they had done a wrong thing. They had hurt the boy and his bike. They had also lost their car. They had left it near the tree and someone had taken it. They had no toys to play with now. They wished they had been nice and shared their car with the boy. Maybe he would have shared his bike with them too. They learned a hard lesson that day. They learned that being jealous and mean does not make you happy. It only makes you lose what you have and hurt others. They learned that being kind and generous is better. It makes you have friends and fun. They hoped the boy would forgive them and they could play together someday. They also hoped they could find their car again. They missed it a lot.
