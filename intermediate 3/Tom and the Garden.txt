Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Garden

Once upon a time, a wealthy man named Tom lived in a big house. He loved to decorate his house with colorful things. One day, Tom found a long spear in his garden. He thought it would be fun to use the spear to decorate his house. Tom put the spear on the wall and invited his friend Sam to see it. Sam came over and said, "Wow, Tom! Your house looks nice with the spear on the wall!" Tom was very happy that his friend liked the spear. Every day, Tom and Sam played with the spear in the garden. They pretended to be brave warriors. They had so much fun together. In the end, Tom's house was not only beautiful but also a place full of joy and laughter.

One day, a big dog named Max was in the park. He saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was not very strong, but he wanted to play with Max. Max was ready to play, but he wanted to explain something to Tim first. Max said, "Tim, I have big muscles. I can run very fast and jump very high. You need to be careful when we play." Tim did not listen to Max, because he was too excited to play. They started to play, and Max ran very fast. Tim tried to catch Max, but he fell down. Max felt bad for Tim. He knew that he should have explained better about his big muscles. Tim was sad, and Max learned that he needed to be more careful with his friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to find nuts and build his home in the tall trees. One sunny day, he found the most tasty nut he had ever seen. He was very happy and wanted to share it with his friend, a little bird named Bella. Sam went to Bella's tree and said, "Bella, I found a tasty nut! Would you like to share it with me?" Bella looked at the nut and said, "Yes, Sam! Let's eat it together!" So, they sat on a branch and shared the tasty nut. It was so yummy that they both smiled and laughed. After they ate the nut, Sam and Bella decided to help each other build their homes. Sam helped Bella find soft leaves for her nest, and Bella helped Sam find strong sticks for his home. They worked together and had fun. At the end of the day, they were both very tired but happy. They learned that sharing and helping each other made them feel good and made their friendship even stronger.

Once there was a furry cat named Sam. Sam liked to play in the garden. He loved to roll in the soft grass. One day, Sam found a big squash. He wanted to play with it. He pushed it with his paw. The squash started to roll. Sam was happy and chased it. But then, the squash whispered, "Please stop!" Sam was surprised. He did not know squashes could talk. The squash said, "I am not a toy. I am a magic squash." Sam and the magic squash became best friends.

Lily and Max were twins who liked to play together. But sometimes they disagreed about what to play. One day, their aunt came to visit them. She brought them a new toy: a big puzzle with many pieces. "Wow, thank you, auntie!" Lily and Max said. They wanted to try the new puzzle right away. But they disagreed about where to start. Lily wanted to start from the corners, and Max wanted to start from the middle. "No, Lily, the middle is better. You can see the picture better," Max said. "No, Max, the corners are better. You can make the edges first," Lily said. They argued and argued, and did not listen to each other. They pulled the puzzle pieces from each other's hands, and made a big mess. Their aunt watched them and shook her head. "Lily and Max, stop fighting. You are not being nice to each other. You need to share and cooperate. Why don't you take turns and help each other?" their aunt said. But Lily and Max did not listen to their aunt. They kept disagreeing and fighting. They did not notice that their dog, Spot, was curious about the new toy. Spot sniffed the puzzle pieces and thought they were snacks. He picked up some pieces with his mouth and ran away. "Hey, Spot, come back! That's our puzzle!" Lily and Max shouted. They stopped fighting and ran after Spot. They tried to get the puzzle pieces back, but Spot was too fast and playful. He ran around the house, and hid under the sofa, and jumped on the bed. Lily and Max chased Spot, but they could not catch him. They realized that they had made a big mistake. They had wasted their time disagreeing and fighting, and now they had lost their new toy. They felt sad and sorry. They went back to their aunt and said, "Auntie, we are sorry. We were not nice to each other. We disagreed and fought, and we did not listen to you. And now Spot has taken our puzzle, and we can't find it. Can you help us, please?" Their aunt smiled and hugged them. She said, "I'm glad you are sorry. It's okay to disagree sometimes, but you need to respect and listen to each other. And you need to take care of your toys. Don't worry, I will help you find Spot and the puzzle. But first, you need to say sorry to each other, and promise to play nicely." Lily and Max looked at each other and said, "Sorry, Lily. Sorry, Max. We promise to play nicely." They hugged each other and smiled. They learned their lesson. They thanked their aunt and followed her to find Spot and the puzzle. They hoped they could still put the puzzle together, and see the picture. They decided to start from the corners and the middle, and help each other. They agreed that the new toy was fun, but not as fun as playing together.
