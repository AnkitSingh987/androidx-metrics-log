Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Blackboard

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to go to school and learn new things. One day, he saw a big blackboard in his classroom. Tim was a little bit worried because he did not know what it was for. Tim's teacher, Miss Jane, saw that he was upset. She said, "Don't worry, Tim! The blackboard is for us to write and draw on. It will be fun!" Tim felt better, but he was still a little bit worried. He did not want to make a mistake on the big blackboard. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl named Sue walked up to the blackboard and started to draw a pretty picture. Tim was amazed! He thought that only the teacher could use the blackboard. He felt happy and not worried anymore. Tim knew he could be brave like Sue and draw on the blackboard too. So, he went up and drew a big smiley face. Everyone in the class clapped and cheered. Tim was not worried anymore, and he loved the big blackboard.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl who found a cobweb in her garden. She was curious and wanted to see what it was. She reached out to touch it when suddenly, a voice came from behind her. "No!" it said. The small girl turned around and saw a rough-looking man standing behind her. "Don't touch the cobweb," he said. "It is dangerous." The small girl was scared, so she refused to touch it. She stepped away from the cobweb and looked back at the man with a frightened look on her face. But the man just smiled at her and said, "Good. You are wise to refuse to touch something you don't know." He then turned and walked away, leaving the small girl alone with the cobweb.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an obedient dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to help people. He had many friends in the town. One day, Buddy met a new friend named Tim. Tim was a nice boy who liked to play with toy cars. One day, Tim and Buddy went to the toy store. Tim wanted to find a new toy to play with. Buddy saw a toy gun on a shelf. He thought Tim might like it. So, Buddy recommended the toy gun to Tim. Tim liked the toy gun and decided to buy it. Tim and Buddy went to the park to play with the toy gun. They had so much fun. They played all day and laughed a lot. All the other kids in the park wanted to play with Tim and Buddy too. And that's how Buddy, the obedient dog, helped Tim make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a big watch that he loved a lot. He wore it every day. One day, Tom saw a sad girl named Sue sitting by a tree. Sue was very serious and didn't want to play. Tom wanted to help Sue, so he tried to encourage her. He said, "Sue, let's play together! It will be fun!" But Sue was still serious and didn't want to play. Tom didn't give up. He showed Sue his big watch and said, "Look at my watch! We can play a game with it." Sue looked at the watch and started to smile. She liked the watch and wanted to play with Tom. They played a game called "What Time Is It?" and had lots of fun. Sue was not serious anymore, and Tom was happy he could help her. The moral of the story is to always try to encourage others when they are sad. Sometimes, all it takes is a little kindness to make someone feel better.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Lily. She loved to wrap her toys in soft cloth. One day, she wrapped her toy bear very tight. She was so happy with the result. That night, Lily put the wrapped bear under her bed. When she woke up, she saw something strange. The bear was not wrapped anymore. The cloth was on the floor. Lily was very surprised. Lily looked around and saw her cat, Max, playing with the cloth. Max had unwrapped the bear while she was sleeping. Lily laughed and hugged Max. From that day on, Lily and Max wrapped toys together. They were the best team.
