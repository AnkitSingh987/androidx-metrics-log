Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Generous Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a generous cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to share her toys with her friends. She would let them play with her ball, her mouse, and her ribbon. One day, Fluffy heard a sound. It was a small bird outside her window. The bird looked sad and lonely. Fluffy wanted to help the bird feel better. Fluffy thought about what she could do. She remembered that she owned a soft pillow. Fluffy picked up the pillow and gave it to the bird. The bird was so happy! They became good friends and played together every day.

Tom and Mia were very thirsty. They had been walking in the hot sand for a long time. They wanted to find water and shade. They saw a green spot far away. They hoped it was an oasis. "Look, Mia, maybe there is water there!" Tom said. "Let's go, Tom, maybe we can drink and rest there!" Mia said. They ran towards the green spot. But as they got closer, they saw something scary. There were big lions lying under the trees. The lions had sharp teeth and claws. They looked hungry and angry. The oasis was dangerous. "Oh no, Mia, lions! We can't go there!" Tom said. "What do we do, Tom? We need water!" Mia said. They looked around. They saw a small hill nearby. They ran to the hill. They climbed to the top. They saw a little stream of water on the other side. The stream was clear and cool. There were no lions there. "Look, Mia, water! We can drink there!" Tom said. "Let's go, Tom, we are safe there!" Mia said. They ran down the hill. They reached the stream. They drank the water. They felt better. They smiled at each other. They had found water and shade. They had escaped the lions. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily and her brother named Tom. They liked to play together in the park. One sunny day, they went to the park with their mom. In the park, they saw a sleepy cat under a big tree. Lily and Tom wanted to play with the cat, but their mom said, "Be kind and let the sleepy cat rest." So, they played with their ball instead. Later, they saw the cat wake up and play with other kids. Lily and Tom were happy they let the cat sleep. They learned that being kind to others makes everyone happy. And they played together with the cat and the other kids, having lots of fun in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a family. The family had a mom, a dad, a boy, and a girl. They lived in a big, blue house. One day, the mom said, "It's time to clean the house." The boy and the girl were lazy. They did not want to clean. They wanted to play with their toys. The dad said, "If we all help, we can clean fast." So, the family started to clean. They picked up toys, wiped the table, and swept the floor. Soon, the house was clean. The mom said, "Good job, family! Now we can play." The boy and the girl were happy. They played with their toys and had fun with their family.

Mommy was busy getting ready for summer. She was looking forward to spending time with her little boy, outside in the wild. She gathered up some toys that they could play with outside. She found a kite to fly high in the breeze, and a pail to make mud pies. Mommy and her little boy went outside to play. He helped her to prepare for a picnic. They unpacked their lunch and found a cozy spot under a tall tree. Mommy took out some brightly-colored bubbles. These were the best kind to make on a wild summer day. Her little boy laughed and blew bubbles until the sky was filled with rainbow-colored circles. They loved spending their summer days outside. Mommy and her little boy were always so happy when they were together.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who had a big truck. He loved to load his truck with many things. One day, he saw a little boy sitting by the road. "Hey, little boy! Do you want to help me load my truck?" the wealthy man asked. The little boy said yes, and they started to load the truck together. They both felt tired, so they took a nap under a big tree. While they were napping, a naughty fox came and took all the things from the truck. When the wealthy man and the little boy woke up, they saw the empty truck and were very sad. The naughty fox had taken everything and left them with nothing.
