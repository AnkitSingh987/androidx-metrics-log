Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Shoes

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to spend time outside. He put on his shoes and ran to the park. In the park, he saw a big bug. The bug was green and had wings. Tim wanted to play with the bug, but the bug was scared. "Please don't hurt me," said the bug. Tim was sad because he did not want to hurt the bug. He wanted to be friends. "I just want to play with you," said Tim. The bug thought for a moment and decided to trust Tim. They played and had fun in the park. The bug was happy that Tim did not hurt him. Tim learned that it is important to be kind to others, even if they are different. And the bug learned that not all people are scary. They became good friends and spent many days playing together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a town. The town was very organized. The people in the town liked to balance things. They balanced toys, food, and even their pets! One day, there was an emergency. A big wind came and blew everything away. The people were sad. They lost all their balanced things. They wanted their town to be organized again. So, they worked together. They picked up their toys, food, and pets. They balanced them again. The town was organized and happy once more. They had a big party to celebrate. And they always remembered to help each other in an emergency.

Once upon a time was a girl named Lucy. She was very pretty and always had fun. One day she went outdoors and saw the sun. She liked the hot weather so she thought she should play. She found a veil on the ground and decided to throw it. She threw and threw the veil up into the sky. She thought her veil was so pretty! But it did not stay in the sky for very long. Instead, the wind blew it away. Lucy started to run after it but soon she was very tired. She stopped because she couldn't run anymore. She had to go home now. The next day, Lucy went back outdoors and she saw something. It was her veil, back on the ground! She was so happy and she picked it up and put it back on. Now she wears it every day, not just on regular days but on special days too!

One day, a mechanic named Tom was working on a big car. He was very good at fixing cars. A little boy named Tim was watching Tom work. Tim was a bit frightened of the big car, but he liked to see how things worked. Tom saw that Tim was frightened and said, "Don't worry, the car won't hurt you. Would you like to help me fix it?" Tim nodded his head and Tom showed him what to do. They worked together, and the car was fixed. Just as they finished, a big noise came from the car. Tim was scared, but Tom suggested, "Let's find out what made that noise." They looked and found a little cat hiding inside the car. The cat was scared too. Tom and Tim helped the cat get out, and they all became friends. The cat was not scared anymore, and neither was Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb. One day, he saw a big tree in his yard. It was an amazing tree. Tim wanted to climb it. Tim called his friend, Sam. "Sam, come see the amazing tree," he said. Sam came and saw the tree. They both wanted to climb it. They found a cooler to stand on. The cooler would help them reach the tree. They stood on the cooler and started to climb. Tim and Sam were very high. They could see their whole yard. But suddenly, the cooler broke. Tim and Sam fell down. They were hurt and cried. The amazing tree was not so fun anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Pip. She liked to do big things. One day, Pip was in her garden when she saw something wide in the grass. She got very excited and crouched down to take a closer look. It was a big, fat toad! "Oh wow," Pip said. "It's a big, fat toad!" Pip wanted to stroke the toad but it hopped away before she could reach it. Pip was sad so she got up and went inside. Inside, Pip's mom stirred a sauce in a pot. It smelled delicious. Pip asked if she could help. "Sure," Mom said. "But be careful not to strike the spoon too hard." Pip stirred the sauce, taking care not to strike the spoon too hard. Then, she smelled something funny - it was the toad from the garden outside! Suddenly, the toad hopped into the kitchen and hopped right into the saucepot! Pip was determined to get the toad out of the sauce. She had to act fast or it would be too late. With one big strike of the spoon, Pip managed to get the toad out of the pot. Pip was relieved. She took the toad back outside, happy that she was able to help her new friend. From that day on, Pip always took care to never strike things too hard!
