Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pears

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat pears. One day, he saw a big pear tree with many pears. Tim wanted to eat all the pears, but he did not know how to get them down. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Sam did not know how to get the pears down either. They were ignorant about how to do it. They tried to shake the tree, but the pears did not fall. Tim's mom saw them and said, "You should not try to take all the pears. It is not nice." Tim and Sam felt bad. They knew they should not be greedy. Tim's mom did not punish them, but she taught them a lesson. They learned to share and be happy with what they have.

One day, a boy named Tim found a hole in his mitten. He was sad because it was his favorite mitten. He showed it to his mom and asked, "Mom, can you repair my mitten?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. I will repair your mitten, but you must be patient." Tim waited and waited for his mom to repair his mitten. While he waited, he played with his toys. Finally, his mom finished repairing the mitten. She gave it to Tim and said, "Here is your mitten. It is like new now." Tim was so happy to have his mitten back. He put it on and went outside to play. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came and took Tim's mitten. The dog ran away very fast. Tim could not catch the dog. He was very sad because he lost his mitten. Tim went back home and told his mom what happened. His mom hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. We will try to find your mitten, but it might be gone." Tim cried because he knew he might never see his favorite mitten again.

One day, a little bird was sitting on a big tree. She looked around and saw a large, white cloud up in the sky. The little bird thought the white cloud looked so soft, and she wanted to soar up to it. "I'm going to fly up there!" she said to herself. But then another bird flew by and said, "That's crazy! You'll never make it up there!" The little bird was disappointed, but she didn't give up. She was going to try to soar up to the cloud, no matter what. She spread her wings wide, and with one flap of them, she was off. The wind was blowing against her wings and she was struggling to get to the cloud, but soon enough, she made it! She looked down and saw the other bird looking up at her with a surprised look on its face. The little bird was so proud of herself for making it up to the cloud. She had overcome the challenge and proven the other bird wrong. From now on, she knew that when something seemed impossible, she could remember this moment and remind herself that anything was possible.

Once upon a time, there was a witch. She lived in a small house in the woods. The witch had a big, crazy hat. She liked to talk to the animals and play with them. One day, a little bird came to the witch's house. The bird was sad because it lost its mom. The witch wanted to help the bird. So, she whispered a magic word to the bird. The bird felt happy and started to sing. The witch and the bird looked for the bird's mom together. They asked the other animals if they saw her. Finally, they found the bird's mom near a big tree. The bird and its mom hugged and thanked the witch. They all became good friends and lived happily in the woods.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who was out playing in a park. He was walking around when he noticed a jug lying on the ground. The jug was very fragile and he wanted to pick it up, but he was scared that it may break. Suddenly he heard a voice. It was an old lady who said, "Do not pick up the jug, it is fragile!". The little boy looked up and saw that the old lady was leading a dog. But the little boy was too curious and he wanted to pick up the jug, so he said, "No, I want to see what is in the jug!". The old lady warned him once more and said, "Don't do it! You must never mess with something so fragile". But the little boy didn't listen and picked up the jug anyway. Unfortunately, the jug was so fragile that it broke and the little boy got a tiny cut on his hand. The old lady laughed and said, "You should have listened to me!". The little boy felt very sad and he cried all the way home. The end.
