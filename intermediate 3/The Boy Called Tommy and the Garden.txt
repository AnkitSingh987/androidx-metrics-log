Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Tommy and the Garden

Once upon a time there lived a little boy called Tommy. Tommy loved exploring outside. One day Tommy was playing in the garden when he saw a big red ant. Tommy wanted to touch the ant so he put out his hand and the ant bit him. It really hurt. Tommy was really surprised and he shouted: "Ouch, it's hot!" Mummy heard Tommy shout and ran out of the house to see what was wrong. She saw the ant on Tommy's hand and she knew it had bitten him. She took Tommy inside and washed the bite with cold water. That made Tommy's hand feel better. From then on Tommy was more careful when playing outside. He knew there could be lots of interesting things to see, but some of them could hurt!

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sally. She loved to whistle. One day, she went to visit her aunt. Her aunt was very good at many things. People said she was successful. When Sally got to her aunt's house, she said, "Aunt, I want to whistle like you." Her aunt smiled and said, "I will teach you, Sally." They practiced whistling together all day long. Sally tried very hard to whistle, but she could not do it. Just then, a big wind came. It blew through the house and made a whistling sound. Sally and her aunt looked at each other and laughed. "See, Sally," said her aunt, "even the wind can whistle!" They both laughed and played together, happy that they could whistle with the wind.

One day, a boy named Tim found some clay. He wanted to use the clay to make a toy. He started to make a toy cat. Tim was very happy with his clay cat. A girl named Sue saw Tim's clay cat. "Can I use your clay, too?" Sue asked. Tim said, "Yes, you can use my clay." Sue started to make a toy dog. But Sue was rude. She took all the clay to make her dog. Tim was sad because he could not make his cat. Then, something unexpected happened. The clay cat and dog came to life! The clay cat said, "Sue, you were rude to Tim. You should share the clay." Sue was sorry. She gave some clay back to Tim. Tim and Sue played with their new clay friends and learned to share.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely frog named Fred. He lived near a small pond with many friends. Fred loved to jump and play with his friends all day long. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the pond. She saw Fred and said, "Hello, frog! You are so lovely. Can you be my friend?" Fred smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to be your friend!" Lucy and Fred played together every day. They had so much fun. One day, a big bird came near the pond. The bird wanted to eat Fred! Lucy saw the bird and said, "No, bird! You cannot eat my friend!" Lucy protected Fred from the big bird. The bird flew away, and Fred was safe. Fred was so happy that Lucy protected him. They were best friends forever. They played and laughed together at the pond every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play hide and seek with her friends. One day, Lily was playing in her room and saw a big curtain. She thought it was a great place to hide. Lily went behind the curtain and waited for her friends to find her. While she was hiding, she heard a tiny voice. Lily looked down and saw a small, upset bird. The bird was lost and didn't know where to go. Lily wanted to help the bird, so she decided to take it outside. Lily and the bird walked outside and looked for the bird's family. They looked high and low, and finally, they saw the bird's family in a tree. The bird was so happy to meet its family again, and Lily was happy to help. The bird thanked Lily, and they hugged. From that day on, Lily and the bird became best friends.
