Category: Intermediate
Title: Momo the Monkey and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a playful little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to climb trees and play with his friends in nature. Every day, Momo would wake up early and go outside to have fun. One sunny day, Momo saw a big tree that he had never climbed before. He was so excited! Momo started to climb the tree, going higher and higher. He saw birds, bugs, and pretty leaves as he climbed. Momo was very happy. When Momo reached the top of the tree, he saw his friends playing below. He called out to them, and they all came to climb the tree with him. They laughed and played together in the tree until the sun went down. Then, they all climbed down and went home to rest for another fun day in nature.

Once upon a time, there was a shy soldier. He hoped to make friends, but he was too scared to talk to others. One day, he went for a walk in the woods. During his walk, he saw a big, green frog. The frog said, "Hi, shy soldier! Do you want to be my friend?" The soldier was happy and said, "Yes, I hope we can be friends!" They played and laughed all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The frog turned into a beautiful princess! She said, "Thank you, shy soldier! You were so kind to me, and now I am not a frog anymore. I hope we can stay friends forever!" And they did.

Sara and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a big box of screws that their dad gave them. They liked to pretend they were builders and make things with the screws. "Look, I made a car!" Ben said, showing Sara his screw car. He had used four screws for the wheels and one for the steering wheel. "That's nice, Ben. But I have a better idea. Let's decorate the tree with the screws!" Sara said, pointing to the dead tree in the corner. Their mom had bought it for Christmas, but it had no lights or ornaments. "Decorate the tree? How?" Ben asked, curious. "Like this!" Sara said, grabbing a handful of screws and throwing them at the tree. Some of the screws stuck to the branches, some fell to the floor, and some hit the wall. "Wow, that's fun!" Ben said, joining Sara. They threw more and more screws at the tree, laughing and shouting. "Stop! Stop!" Their mom said, coming into the living room. She saw the mess of screws and the dead tree. She was not happy. "What are you doing? You can't decorate the tree with screws! You could hurt yourselves or break something!" "But mom, we were just playing!" Sara said, pouting. "Playing is good, but not with screws. Screws are for building, not for decorating. And this tree is dead, it needs to go to the trash. Come on, help me clean up this mess. And then we can decorate a new tree with proper ornaments, okay?" Their mom said, picking up the screws. "Okay, mom. We're sorry." Sara and Ben said, helping their mom. They felt a little sad, but also excited to decorate a new tree. Maybe they could use some of their toys instead of screws.

Ben and Lily were bored. It was a gloomy day and they could not go outside to play. They looked around the house for something fun to do. "Let's make a snack!" Ben said. He ran to the kitchen and opened the fridge. He saw an avocado and took it out. "Look, Lily, a green fruit!" Lily followed him and looked at the avocado. She did not like it. She thought it was yucky. "No, Ben, I don't want that. Let's find something else." Ben did not listen. He wanted to try the avocado. He cut it in half with a knife. He saw a big seed inside. He tried to take it out, but it was slippery. He pushed harder, but the seed flew out of his hand and hit the wall. "Oops!" Ben said. He looked at the mess. There was avocado on the wall, on the floor, and on his shirt. He heard a loud noise. It was Mom. "What are you doing?" Mom asked. She saw the avocado and the seed. She was not happy. She took the knife from Ben and put it away. She got a cloth and started to clean the wall. "Sorry, Mom," Ben said. He felt bad. He did not mean to make a mess. "It's okay, Ben," Mom said. "But next time, ask me before you use a knife. And be careful with slippery things. They can make you slip and fall." Ben nodded. He learned his lesson. He gave Mom a hug and said, "I love you, Mom." "I love you too, Ben," Mom said. She smiled and gave him a kiss. She gave Lily a kiss too. She said, "How about we make a different snack? Something that you both like." Ben and Lily cheered. They followed Mom to the kitchen. They forgot about the gloomy day. They were happy.
