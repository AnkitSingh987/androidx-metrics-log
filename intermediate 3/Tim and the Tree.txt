Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small green town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his water spray toy. He would spray water on the plants, the trees, and sometimes on his friends too. Everyone in the town knew Tim and his water spray toy. One hot day, Tim saw a big tree that looked very sad. The leaves were not green anymore. Tim thought, "I should help this tree." So, he sprayed water on the tree every day for a whole year. The tree started to look happy and green again. But one day, when Tim sprayed water on the tree, it began to move! The tree was a magic tree! It said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me. I will give you a gift." The tree gave Tim a magic spray toy that could make anything green and happy. Tim learned that when you help others, good things can happen to you too.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an ordinary train. The train had a red nose and big round wheels. Every day, the train went on a long trip from one side of the town to the other. One sunny day, the train met a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to shout and play with his friends. When Tim saw the train, he was very happy. He shouted, "Hello, train! Can I ride with you today?" The ordinary train was happy to have Tim as a friend. It said, "Yes, Tim! Hop on and let's go on an adventure together!" Tim climbed onto the train, and they went on a fun trip all around the town. They saw many things like trees, flowers, and animals. Tim and the train had a great day, and they became the best of friends.

One day, a pale cat named Kitty and a dog named Doggy were playing in the yard. They were very hungry. They decide to find some food and make a meal together. Kitty and Doggy went to the kitchen. "What should we make?" asked Kitty. "I like fish," said Doggy. "I like bones," said Kitty. They decide to make a meal with fish and bones. They cooked the fish and bones. The smell was very good. They sat down to eat their meal. "Yummy!" said Kitty. "This is the best meal ever!" said Doggy. They ate and ate until they were full. Kitty and Doggy were happy and went back to play in the yard.

One day, a big cat got an invitation to a party. The cat was very happy. The cat thought it was a very important party. The cat wanted to look very nice, so it wore a big, red hat. The cat thought the hat was not stupid. At the party, the cat saw many friends. They all had fun and ate good food. The cat was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat's hat started to increase! The hat got bigger and bigger. The cat did not understand why. All the friends at the party looked at the cat's big hat. They started to laugh. The cat felt sad and thought the hat was stupid. Then, a small mouse came to the cat and said, "Don't worry, I can help you." The mouse climbed up and took off the hat. The cat was happy and thanked the mouse. The cat and the mouse became best friends, and they had fun at the party together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys. One day, Sue found a big sheet in her room. She thought it would be fun to play with the sheet. Sue had an idea. She put the sheet over her head and pretended to be a fake ghost. Sue wanted to display her ghost costume to her mom. She walked slowly to the living room where her mom was sitting. But, when Sue got there, she saw another ghost! Sue was scared, but then the other ghost took off the sheet. It was her dad! He was playing a trick on her. They all laughed and played together, being fake ghosts with their sheets.
