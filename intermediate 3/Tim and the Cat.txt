Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Cat

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a small, harmless cat sitting on the grass. Tim liked the cat and wanted to play with it. Tim walked up to the cat and whispered, "Hello, cat! Do you want to play?" The cat looked at Tim and made a soft sound. It was a happy sound, like a purr. Tim knew the cat wanted to play with him. They played together all day. Tim would throw a ball and the cat would run after it. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Bye, cat! I hope we can play again soon." The cat made the happy sound again and Tim knew they would be friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was adorable with big blue eyes and curly hair. He loved to play with his toy cars and count their numbers. He had one, two, three, four, five cars in all! One day, Tim's friend, Lily, came to play. Lily brought her doll, and they played together. Tim showed Lily his cars, and they counted the numbers together. They had so much fun, they wanted to build a big tower with blocks to support their toys. Tim and Lily worked together to build the tower. They used the blocks to support the cars and the doll. They made the tower very tall and strong. The cars and the doll were happy on top of the tower. As the sun began to set, it was time for Lily to go home. Tim and Lily hugged and said goodbye. They promised to play together again soon. Tim felt happy because he had a great day with his friend, and he knew that they could always support each other.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. They saw a high tree with a little bird in it. The bird was hungry, so Mia wanted to feed it. "Mom, can I feed the bird?" Mia asked. "Yes, but what do you want to feed it?" her mom replied. Mia thought for a moment and said, "I want to feed it some tuna!" So, her mom gave her a small piece of tuna from their lunch. Mia threw the tuna up to the bird, but something unexpected happened. The tuna did not go to the bird. Instead, it landed on a big, friendly dog that was playing nearby. The dog ate the tuna and wagged its tail. "Oops! That was not for you, dog!" Mia laughed. The dog seemed to understand and brought Mia a ball to play with. Mia and the dog played together all day, and they became best friends. From that day on, Mia always brought tuna to the park for her new friend, the dog.

One day, a little bunny named Tim was feeling gloomy. He wanted to play with his friends, but they were all busy. So, he decided to go to the garden and look for carrots. In the garden, he saw a big, orange carrot. He tried to pull it out, but it was stuck. Tim asked a nearby bird, "Can you help me get this carrot out?" The bird flew down and tried to explain that the carrot was too big for both of them to pull out. Tim and the bird tried their best to pull the carrot together, but it just wouldn't come out. They both felt very sad. Then, a big, mean cat came into the garden. The cat saw Tim and the bird and said, "I want that carrot too!" The cat tried to take the carrot from Tim and the bird. They all pulled and pulled, but still, the carrot did not come out. In the end, the cat got very angry and chased Tim and the bird away. Tim and the bird were sad that they didn't get the carrot, and they were scared of the mean cat. They went back to their homes, feeling gloomy and wishing they had never found the big, orange carrot.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big billboard. The billboard had a picture of a yummy cake. Everyone who saw the billboard wanted to eat the cake. But they could not eat the billboard. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the billboard. He wanted to eat the cake too. So, he asked his mom to make a cake just like the one in the picture. His mom made the cake, but she used a bitter fruit by mistake. When Tim ate the cake, he did not like it. It was not yummy like he thought it would be. Tim learned that things that look yummy might not always taste good. He also learned to be happy with what he had and not to want things just because they look good. The moral of the story is to not judge things by how they look, and to appreciate what we have.
