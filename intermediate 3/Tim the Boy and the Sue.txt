Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sue

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a girl named Sue playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play with Sue, so he asked her, "Can I play with you?" Sue said, "Yes, let's play catch!" They played catch and had fun. But then, Tim noticed that Sue was getting tired. He wanted to win, so he threw the ball very fast. Sue got frustrated because she couldn't catch the ball. Tim felt happy because he thought he had victory. But Sue was sad and didn't want to play anymore. She said, "I don't want to play with you. You are not nice." Tim felt bad because he made Sue sad. He didn't have a friend to play with now. Tim learned that winning is not everything and that he should be nice to his friends.

Ben and Mia liked to play in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. One day, they saw a big hole in the ground. They wondered what was inside. "Let's go and look," Ben said. He was brave and curious. "No, don't go," Mia said. She was scared and careful. "It might be dark and scary. There might be bugs or snakes or monsters." Ben did not listen to Mia. He wanted to see the hole. He ran to the hole and looked down. He saw nothing but black. He leaned over to see more. "Ben, be careful!" Mia shouted. She ran after him. But it was too late. Ben slipped and fell into the hole. He screamed and cried. Mia screamed and cried too. She could not see him or help him. She was alone and afraid. The hole was deep and scary. Ben was hurt and lost. He wished he had listened to Mia. He wished he had not gone to the hole. He wished he could go back to the park. But he could not. He was stuck in the hole. He could only imagine what would happen next.

Early one morning, Emma woke up with the sun. She saw a figure standing outside her window. It was the Healthy Eater Monster! Emma couldn't believe her eyes. She slowly opened the window and said, "Hi, who are you?" The Healthy Eater Monster said, "I am here to help you eat healthy foods. Together, we can make sure you are healthy and strong like me." Emma smiled. She couldn't wait to learn how to eat better. Every day, the Healthy Eater Monster would come to visit. They would talk about healthy snacks, fun ways to exercise, and the importance of drinking water. Emma felt so excited to learn all this new information. The Healthy Eater Monster was always around to encourage her during tough times. Soon, Emma was feeling strong and healthy, thanks to her new friend. The Healthy Eater Monster had made such a big difference in her life. From then on, Emma never stopped eating healthy.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, Spot saw a big race happening in the park. Many boys and girls were running and having fun. Spot wanted to join them and have fun too. Spot went to the race and asked a girl, "Can I play too?" The girl said, "Yes, you can try, but it's hard to win." Spot was happy and started to run with the boys and girls. They all ran very fast, but Spot ran faster. He was a very fast dog. As they ran, Spot saw that one boy was upset. He had fallen and hurt his knee. Spot stopped running and went to help the boy. He licked the boy's knee to make him feel better. The boy smiled and said, "Thank you, Spot." Then, they both finished the race together. Even though Spot did not win, he was happy that he could help his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to play with her toys, but she was always restless. She would move from one toy to another, never staying still for long. One day, Lily was playing with her toys when she noticed her finger was red and hurt. She started to worry. "Mommy, my finger hurts," she cried. Her mommy came over and looked at her finger. "Oh, don't worry, Lily. It's just a little cut. Let's put a band-aid on it." Lily felt better with the band-aid on her finger. She went back to playing with her toys, but this time, she played more slowly and carefully. She didn't want to hurt her finger again. Then, something unexpected happened. Lily found a toy she had forgotten about. It was a beautiful doll that she loved to play with. Lily learned that when she took her time and wasn't restless, she could find things that made her happy. The moral of the story is to slow down and enjoy the things around you.
