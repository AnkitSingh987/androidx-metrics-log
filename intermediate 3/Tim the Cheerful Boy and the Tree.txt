Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Cheerful Boy and the Tree

One day, a cheerful boy named Tim went to play. He saw a sack near a tree. Tim was very happy. He wanted to show the sack to his friend, Sam. So, he took the sack and went to Sam's house. "Look, Sam!" Tim said. "I found a big sack. Let's open it!" Sam was excited too. They opened the sack, and inside was a network. The boys did not know what the network was for. They decided to play with it. They played with the network all day. They threw it in the air and caught it. They had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The network got caught on a big tree. The boys tried to get it down, but they couldn't. The tree was very tall and scary. They were sad because they could not play with the network anymore. The boys went home with the empty sack, feeling sad. They never found another network to play with again.

One day, a little girl named Sally wanted to make a pizza. She asked her mom to help her. They went to the kitchen and started to make the pizza together. Sally was very excited because she loved pizza. Sally wanted to decorate the pizza with her favorite toppings. She put cheese, tomato, and pepperoni on it. Her mom helped her put the pizza in the oven. While they waited, Sally started to feel worried. She thought, "What if my pizza doesn't taste good?" When the pizza was ready, Sally and her mom took it out of the oven. It looked and smelled yummy. Sally was not worried anymore. They cut the pizza into slices and shared it with the family. Everyone loved Sally's pizza and she was very happy.

Ted and his dad had just gone to the beach. Ted's dad was so excited to take a swim in the ocean. Ted, however, felt a bit embarrassed. His dad was bald and he was not sure that he wanted anyone to see him. Suddenly, Ted's dad looked over and said, "Come on Ted, let's go for a swim!" Ted could already feel his face turning red. He tried to get out of it, but his dad was persistent. After a few minutes of splashing in the shallow water, Ted's dad noticed some people nearby pointing and laughing at him. "Why are they laughing at me?" he asked Ted, his eyes growing big. Ted realized that everyone was laughing because his dad was bald and they could clearly see his head. Ted felt even more embarrassed now and wanted to hurry out of the water. His dad, however, was not ready to give in and told Ted that there was nothing wrong in being bald and that he should be proud of his dad. Ted smiled and they stayed a while longer in the water, enjoying every second of it.

Lily and her mom were in the car. They were going to the park to play. But there was a lot of traffic on the road. Cars and trucks and buses were everywhere. They were not moving fast. They were moving very slow. Lily was bored. She wanted to get out of the car. She wanted to swing and slide and run. She asked her mom, "Mom, when will we get to the park?" Her mom looked at the traffic. She saw red lights and honking horns. She sighed. She said, "I don't know, Lily. The traffic is terrible today. Maybe we should go back home and play there." Lily did not like that idea. She shook her head. She said, "No, mom, I want to go to the park. Please, mom, can we go to the park?" Her mom smiled. She loved Lily very much. She wanted to make her happy. She said, "Okay, Lily, we will try to go to the park. But we have to be patient and wait for the traffic to clear. Can you do that?" Lily nodded. She said, "Yes, mom, I can do that. I can be patient and wait. But can we sing a song while we wait? That will make it more fun." Her mom nodded. She said, "Sure, Lily, we can sing a song. What song do you want to sing?" Lily thought for a moment. She said, "How about the wheels on the bus? That's a good song for traffic." Her mom agreed. She said, "That's a great song for traffic. Let's sing it together." And so they did. They sang the wheels on the bus go round and round, and the horn on the bus goes beep beep beep, and the people on the bus go up and down, and the wipers on the bus go swish swish swish. They sang and sang until the traffic moved and they got to the park. And then they had a lot of fun.
