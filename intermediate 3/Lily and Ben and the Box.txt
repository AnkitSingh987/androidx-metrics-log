Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Box

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the kitchen. One day, they see a big box in the kitchen. It is a new dishwasher. Mom says it can wash the dishes for them. "Can we look inside?" Lily asks. "OK, but be careful. It is not a toy," Mom says. Lily and Ben open the box. They see a shiny machine with buttons and racks. They want to touch it. "Wow, it is cold!" Ben says. He puts his hand on the machine. "It is icy!" Lily says. She feels the machine too. "Why is it icy?" Ben asks. "Because it came from a far place where it is winter. It will get warmer soon," Mom says. Lily and Ben nod. They think it is cool to have an icy dishwasher. They wonder what else it can do. They decide to help Mom put the dishes in the dishwasher. They hope it will make the dishes clean and shiny.

Once upon a time, there was a green tent in a big park. In this tent, there lived a happy flower named Lily. One day, Lily wanted to go outside and bloom in the sun. Lily met a big tree and asked, "Can I bloom under you?" The big tree said, "No, you need more sun to grow." Lily felt sad and kept looking for a place to bloom. Lily found a small bush and asked, "Can I bloom next to you?" The small bush said, "Yes, you can!" Lily was happy and started to bloom. But soon, Lily got too big and the bush had no space. The bush became sad and Lily was asked to leave. In the end, Lily could not find a place to bloom and felt very sad. The moral of the story is to think about others before we do something.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Emma. She lived in a charming house with a big chimney. Every day, Emma played in her garden and sang songs. One day, Emma went for a walk outside her garden and spotted a beautiful rainbow in the sky. She was so excited she started to clap her hands. “Wow, look at that!” she said. Suddenly, a small fairy flew out of the chimney. The fairy was wearing a twinkling dress and had lovely wings. Emma was so surprised she stopped clapping, and just stared at the little fairy. “Don’t worry child, I’m here to help you!” said the fairy. “Let’s go find the pot of gold at the end of the rainbow!” Emma was so happy she started to clap her hands again. She nodded her head and off she went with the fairy. They ran and skipped all the way until they reached the rainbow's end. At last, they saw the pot of gold. It was so shiny and dazzling! Emma was speechless. She smiled and gave the fairy a big hug. With one big clap of her hands, the two of them took the pot of gold and were on their way back home. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the sun. One day, it was very hot. Tim's mom said, "Tim, let's go inside." Tim went inside and saw a new toy. It was a tunnel! His mom said, "I want to introduce you to your new toy, Tim." Tim was very happy. He played with the tunnel all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a girl who loved diamonds. She dreamed of finding a special diamond that was so clear it would shine as bright as the stars. One day, she took a walk in the woods and she found a beautiful diamond! It was so clear that it sparkled and shone like the moon. She was so happy she jumped up and down with joy. The girl saw that the diamond was on top of a big rock, so she carefully stood on her tiptoes and reached up until she could grab it. She looked around to make sure nobody was watching, and then put the diamond in her pocket. The girl was so excited to have found such a beautiful clear diamond. She held it tight in her hand as she ran out of the woods. From that day on, every time she looked at the diamond it made her smile.
