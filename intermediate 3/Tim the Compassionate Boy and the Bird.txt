Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Compassionate Boy and the Bird

One day, a compassionate boy named Tim went for a ride in his red jeep. The sun was shining, and Tim was happy. He drove down the road, singing a fun song. As Tim drove, he saw a little bird on the road. The bird could not fly. Tim stopped his jeep and got out. He said, "Little bird, why can't you fly?" The bird said, "I hurt my wing. I can't act like other birds now." Tim wanted to help the bird. He picked up the bird and put it in his jeep. Just then, something unexpected happened. The bird began to grow bigger and bigger! It was not a real bird; it was a magic bird! The bird said, "Thank you, Tim, for being kind. Now I can act like other birds again." Tim was surprised but happy. He and the magic bird became best friends. They had many fun adventures together in the red jeep.

One day, a little bird named Tim wanted to fly. He had a big urge to see the world from up high. He asked his mom, "Can I fly now?" His mom said, "Yes, you can try." Tim flapped his wings and began to fly. He saw a big tree with a spoiled apple. He thought it was a good place to rest. He flew down and sat on the tree branch. Just then, a loud noise came from the apple. It was a tiny bug! The bug said, "Hi, I live here!" Tim was surprised but happy to make a new friend. They talked and played together in the tree.

Nicky was so excited. He was going to hug his grandmother, who he hadn't seen in weeks. He couldn't wait to feel her soft arms around him. As Nicky arrived, he noticed his grandmother looked frail. His eyes widened as he saw her weak muscles. Nicky ran up to her and hugged her tightly. "Grandma, I miss you," he said. She smiled, but Nicky could see the sadness in her eyes. "I miss you too," she said sadly. Nicky wanted to spend as much time with her as he could. He gave her a few more hugs, but then he noticed something strange. Grandma was so cold. He started to worry and asked his mum why Grandma was so cold. Mum sighed and said, "I'm sorry Nicky, but Grandma passed away." Nicky was so sad. He gave a final hug goodbye to his beloved grandmother. He felt so lost and alone.

Billy was in the park with his family. It was a beautiful day, and he saw a big slide. He asked his father to pull him up the slide. His father agreed and grabbed him tightly, pulling him up the slide. Billy reached the top of the slide and looked down, eager to have some fun. He saw a man at the bottom of the slide and wanted to be the first one to win. He took a deep breath and slid down. The man at the bottom was rude. He shoved Billy aside as he slid past. Billy was upset and ran around to get back in first place. But the man was too fast and he won. Billy was disappointed but he felt proud that he had tried his best. He was determined to win next time. He said goodbye to the man and went back to the top of the slide, ready to go again.

Once, there was a little girl who wanted to find something special. She went outside and started to look around. She noticed a broken chain in the grass and thought it might be interesting. She stopped and bent down to pick up the chain. She guessed it had been broken for a long time. She looked around, but couldn't find the other half. The little girl decided to take the broken chain home with her, because she liked it. She decided to fix it up, so she asked her mom for help. With a bit of glue, they made the little chain all nice again. The little girl was very happy! She hung it up in her room, so she could admire it every day. When her friends came over, she showed them her special chain. Everyone was very impressed with her fix-it job. And the little girl was proud of herself. She guessed it was time to look for something new to fix!
