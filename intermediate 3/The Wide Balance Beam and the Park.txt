Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wide Balance Beam and the Park

There was a wide balance beam. The beam was in the park and everyone wanted to have a turn. John saw the beam and he wanted to have a turn too. He asked his mum if he could have a go first. His mum said yes and he hopped on the beam. He balanced nicely and he was doing a great job. Then his brother Timmy came along. Timmy asked if he could have a turn. John thought he should have all the fun so he said no. Timmy didn't like this and started to push on the beam. It shook and John was about to fall. He felt scared and started to cry. Mum saw what was happening and scolded Timmy. Then she said to John: "Why don't you let Timmy have a turn? You can play with this object together". John agreed and invited Timmy to join him. They both had a great time balancing on the wide beam.

Once upon a time, there was a big engine named Tom. Tom was a very reliable engine. He always did his job well. One day, Tom met a small engine named Tim. Tim was new and did not know how to do his job yet. Tom said, "Hi Tim! I can help you. I am a reliable engine." Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" They worked together every day. But Tim was a little scared because he was new. One day, while they were working, they heard a loud noise. Tim was very scared. Tom said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will find out what it is." Tom went to look and saw a big, funny-looking machine. It was making the loud noise. Tom talked to the machine and found out it was just lost. The machine was not scary at all! Tom helped the machine find its way home. Tim was not scared anymore. They all became good friends and worked together happily.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the lake with his mom and dad. They had a fine day to play in the water. Tim saw a small kayak near the shore. He asked his mom and dad if he could try it. They said yes, but they would watch him closely. Tim got in the kayak and began to paddle. He was having so much fun! As he moved, he made a big splash with each stroke. The water felt cool on his face. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the water and landed in the kayak with Tim! Tim was scared at first, but then he saw the fish was also scared. He knew he needed to help the fish. Tim carefully picked up the fish and put it back in the water. The fish swam away, happy to be back home. Tim felt proud that he could help a new friend.

Johny was a boy. He was three years old. He liked to eat. Johny was in his living room. He saw the screen. The screen was big and brown. Johny asked his mum: "What can I eat?" Mum said: "Let's go to the kitchen. We will eat something there." Johny was happy. He ran to the kitchen. They ate a delicious dinner. Johny was full and satisfied. He smiled. He looked at the screen again and said: "That brown screen is nice."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. They had a pretty room called a chamber. Lily thought the chamber was attractive because it had nice colors and fun toys. One day, Lily was playing with her toy bear in the chamber. She was having lots of fun, but then she accidentally tore the bear's arm. Lily was sad and didn't know what to do. She went to her mom and said, "Mommy, I tore my bear's arm. Can you fix it, please?" Her mom looked at the torn bear and said, "Don't worry, Lily. We can fix your bear together." They got a needle and thread and sewed the bear's arm back on. After that, Lily was happy again, and she promised to be more careful with her toys. She hugged her mom and thanked her for fixing her bear. From that day on, Lily and her bear played happily in the attractive chamber.
