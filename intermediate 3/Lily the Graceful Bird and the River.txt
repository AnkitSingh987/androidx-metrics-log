Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Graceful Bird and the River

One day, a graceful bird named Lily was flying in the sky. She saw her friend, Tim the turtle, on the ground. Tim was sad because he wanted to send a message to his friend across the river. But he was too slow to go there. Lily wanted to help Tim. She had an idea. She told Tim to shout the message and she would fly to the other side of the river. Tim shouted his message very loud. Lily heard it and flew to the other side. When Lily reached the other side, she found Tim's friend, Sam the squirrel. She shouted Tim's message to Sam. Sam was happy and shouted a message back to Tim. Lily flew back and shouted Sam's message to Tim. Now, Tim was happy too. Lily felt good that she helped her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a popular little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the sand at the park. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw his friend, Sally. Sally said, "Let's make a big sand castle!" Tim and Sally started to build the biggest sand castle ever. They were having so much fun. Then, a big dog came and knocked down their sand castle. Tim felt embarrass. Sally started to cry. Tim's mom said, "It's time to go home now." Tim and Sally were sad because their day at the park ended bad.

Lily loved to wrap gifts for her friends. She had a big box of paper, ribbons, and stickers. She liked to make the gifts look pretty and fun. She also liked to see her friends smile when they opened them. One day, she found a piece of velvet in the box. It was soft and shiny and red. Lily thought it was very beautiful. She decided to wrap a gift for her mom with it. She found a small book that her mom liked to read. She put the book in the velvet and tied a gold ribbon around it. She also put a heart sticker on it. She ran to her mom and gave her the gift. She said, "Mom, I made this for you. I hope you like it." Her mom was very surprised and happy. She hugged Lily and thanked her. She opened the gift and saw the book. She said, "This is one of my favorite books. And this velvet is so lovely. You are very thoughtful, Lily." Lily felt cheerful. She was glad that her mom liked her gift. She said, "I love you, Mom. You are the best." Her mom said, "I love you too, Lily. You are the best." They smiled and cuddled on the couch. They read the book together and had a cozy time.

Once upon a time, there was a shy dancer named Lily. Lily loved to dance, but she was too shy to dance in front of others. She would dance alone in her room when no one was watching. One day, a friendly bird saw Lily dancing through her window. The bird flew to the window and said, "Hi Lily! I saw you dancing. You are a great dancer! Why don't you dance with others?" Lily looked at the bird and said, "I am shy. I am scared to dance in front of others." The friendly bird had an idea. "Why don't you dance with me? I will help you be brave." Lily thought about it and decided to try. The bird and Lily danced together in her room. Soon, Lily felt more brave and happy. The next day, Lily went to the park and saw other kids dancing. She took a deep breath and joined them. She was still a little shy, but she remained brave and danced with her new friends. And from that day on, Lily was known as the brave dancer who loved to dance with everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a big rug in a small room. The rug was very pretty with lots of colors. A little girl named Amy loved to play on the rug every day with her toys. One day, Amy could not find her favorite toy. She looked everywhere and felt frustrated. She did not want to lose her toy. She looked under the rug and found a secret door! Amy was very surprised. Amy opened the secret door and saw her toy inside. She was so happy! She did not lose her toy after all. From that day on, Amy knew that her rug was special and she played with her toys on it every day.
