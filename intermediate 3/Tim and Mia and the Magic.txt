Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Mia and the Magic

Tim and Mia liked to play with their toys in the garden. They had a lot of fun with their cars, dolls, balls and blocks. But they also liked to pretend they had magic powers. They would wave their hands and say funny words, and imagine they could make things happen. One day, they found a big stick in the grass. It looked like a magic wand. Tim picked it up and said, "I am a wizard! I can do anything I want!" Mia said, "No, I am a fairy! I can do better magic than you!" They started to argue and fight over the stick. They pulled and pushed and shouted. Then, something strange happened. The stick glowed and sparkled. It made a loud noise. Tim and Mia felt a shock in their hands. They let go of the stick and fell to the ground. They looked at each other and saw that they had changed. Tim had wings and a dress. Mia had a beard and a hat. They had swapped their magic powers! They were very scared and confused. They did not like their new looks. They wanted to go back to normal. They tried to use their magic, but it did not work. They did not know the right words or gestures. They started to cry and scream. Their mom heard them and came to the garden. She saw them and gasped. She was very troubled. She did not know what had happened or how to fix it. She picked up the stick and saw that it had a label. It said, "Magic Trick Wand. For ages 10 and up. Warning: Do not use on people or animals. May cause unexpected effects." She realized that it was a toy from the store, but a very dangerous one. She wished she had never bought it. She hugged Tim and Mia and said, "Don't worry, my loves. We will find a way to make you normal again. I'm sorry I gave you this bad stick. It was not real magic. It was a trick."

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jenny. They went to the park together every day. Jack wanted to show Jenny something special. “Look!” said Jack. He had a pair of scissors. “These scissors will help us make healthy food," he said. Jenny was excited. She wanted to help. She watched as Jack used the scissors to snip up some carrots. Soon, they had a lot of carrots to eat. “Let’s eat one,” said Jack. Together, they ate the carrot. It was delicious! “Let’s make more carrots,” said Jenny. And so Jack and Jenny used their scissors to make more and more carrots. By the end of the day, they had made lots of healthy and tasty carrots. As the day went on, Jack and Jenny's friendship increased and they became even closer. They continue to go to the park everyday with the scissors to make healthy food and have fun together.

Once upon a time, a family of birds were living in a huge tree. The birds were friendly and organized, so they got along very well. One day, a storm came. The weather was not nice, with wind and rain. The birds were scared and they flew away. They scattered in different directions. One of the baby birds flew too far and got lost. The baby bird was scared and tried to find its way back home. The baby bird asked a friendly rabbit, "Help me! I'm lost!" The rabbit said, "I'll help you. Follow me!" The rabbit knew how to find the big tree, so it took the baby bird back home safely. The baby bird's family and friends were relieved they were all back together. They hugged and were happy to be organized again.

One day, a nosy little boy named Tom went to the sea with his mom. He saw a big ship in the water. Tom was very excited and wanted to go on the ship. Tom's mom said, "We can't go on the ship, but we can watch it from here." Tom was a bit sad, but he still enjoyed watching the ship. The ship was very big and had many people on it. Then, Tom heard a whisper in his ear. It was a little bird that had come to say hello. The bird said, "Hi Tom, I live on that ship. It is a lot of fun!" Tom smiled and whispered back to the bird, "I wish I could go on the ship too." The bird flew away, but Tom kept watching the ship and dreaming of the fun he could have on it.
