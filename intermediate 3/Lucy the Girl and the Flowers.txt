Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Flowers

Once upon a time, in a big country, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to gather flowers and leaves in her basket. One day, while she was walking in the forest, she met a kind rabbit. "Hello, rabbit," said Lucy. "Do you want to help me gather flowers and leaves?" The rabbit was patient and nodded. "Yes, Lucy, I would love to help you." So, Lucy and the rabbit gathered flowers and leaves together. They talked and laughed while they worked. After a while, they had a big pile of flowers and leaves. They were very happy with their work. In the end, Lucy and the rabbit went back to their homes. They were tired but happy. They knew that they had made a new friend in each other. And from that day on, they always gathered flowers and leaves together.

Once upon a time, there was a zebra named Zara. Zara lived in a big forest with many friends. She liked to play and have fun all day long. She was never serious. One day, Zara saw that her friend, the bird, had a broken wing. The bird could not fly. Zara wanted to help her friend. She thought hard and had an idea. Zara would replace the bird's broken wing with a new one. Zara found a big leaf and put it on the bird's broken wing. The bird tried to fly, but the leaf was not strong enough. Zara tried again. This time, she found a strong stick and tied it to the bird's wing. The bird could fly again! Zara and the bird were very happy. They played and had fun all day. From that day on, Zara learned to be serious when her friends needed help. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily liked to play with her mom's computer. She liked to see the pictures and hear the sounds. She liked to press the buttons and make things happen. But her mom told her not to touch the computer when she was not there. She said the computer was not a toy and it could break. One day, Lily was alone in the living room. She saw the computer on the table. She wanted to play with it. She did not remember what her mom said. She climbed on the chair and reached for the computer. She pulled it to the edge of the table. She was happy. But then, the computer slipped from her hands. It fell to the floor with a loud crash. It broke into pieces. Lily was scared. She cried. Her mom came running. She saw the broken computer and Lily. She was angry and sad. She said, "Lily, what did you do? You broke my computer! You know you are not supposed to touch it! Why did you not listen to me?" Lily said, "I'm sorry, mom. I wanted to play with it. I forgot what you said." Her mom said, "Lily, you have to remember what I say. The computer is important for my work. It is not a toy. You have to be careful with things that are not yours. You have to respect other people's things. Do you understand?" Lily said, "Yes, mom. I understand. I'm sorry. I won't do it again." Her mom said, "I hope so, Lily. But now I have to buy a new computer. And that will cost a lot of money. Money that we could have used for other things. Like buying you a new dress or a sweet treat. But now we can't. Because you broke my computer." Lily felt bad. She wished she had remembered what her mom said. She wished she had not played with the computer. She learned a lesson. She learned that actions have consequences. And that she should listen to her mom.

Once, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was weak and could not fly far. One day, he saw a big land far away. He wanted to go there, but he was too weak to fly. Tim had a friend named Sam. Sam was a strong bird. Tim called Sam and asked for help. Sam said, "I will help you, but first, we need to find a way to make you strong." Tim and Sam ate good food and played games to get strong. After some days, Tim felt strong. He called Sam and said, "I am ready to fly to the big land." They both flew to the big land and had a lot of fun.
