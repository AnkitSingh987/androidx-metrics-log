Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bright Sun Was Shining and the Garden

One day, a bright sun was shining in the sky. A dog named Max was playing in the garden. He saw a big bone and decided to bury it. Max dug a hole and said, "I will hide my bone here." He put the bone in the hole and covered it with dirt. Max was very happy and proud of his work. Later, Max's friend, a cat named Lily, came to play. Max told her, "I buried a bone in the garden. If you find it, I will give you a reward!" Lily looked around and found the bone. Max was happy and gave her a treat as a reward. They both enjoyed the sunny day and played together in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Abby. She was only three and loved to go to the shop every day. She would hop, skip, and jump in the shop, picking out sweets and things she liked. One day, as she left the shop, it was empty. Abby did not understand and asked her mom, "Why is the shop empty, Mama?" Her mom said, "Come on, Abby. It will be very interesting to see what happens tomorrow." The next day, Abby and her mom went back to the shop. When Abby opened the shop door, she was surprised. The shop was full of brightly coloured balloons and toys! Abby screamed in joy with a smile on her face. The shop was no longer empty!

Once upon a time, there was a little raven. The raven was black and dirty. He lived in a big tree near a small house. The raven liked to skip and play with his friends. One day, the raven wanted to skip with a little girl who lived in the small house. The little girl saw the dirty raven and was scared. The raven felt sad because he wanted to be friends with the little girl. The next day, the dirty raven took a bath in the rain. He felt clean and happy. The little girl saw the clean raven and smiled. They both began to skip and play together. The raven and the little girl became the best of friends.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big bird on a tree. The bird has a long beak and a red head. Anna and Ben want to look at the bird. "Let's walk to the tree and see the bird," Anna says. "OK, but be careful. The bird may be scared and fly away," Ben says. They walk to the tree and look up. The bird is still there. It makes a loud noise. Anna and Ben laugh. "Hello, bird. What is your name?" Anna asks. The bird does not answer. It looks at Anna and Ben with its black eyes. "Maybe the bird does not have a brain," Ben says. "Of course it has a brain. All animals have brains. But the bird's brain is very small and fragile. It does not know how to talk like us," Anna says. "How do you know that?" Ben asks. "My mom told me. She is a vet. She helps sick animals. She knows a lot about brains," Anna says. "Wow, your mom is very smart. I want to be a vet too when I grow up," Ben says. "Me too. Then we can help many animals and birds," Anna says. They smile at each other and wave at the bird. The bird makes another loud noise and flies away. Anna and Ben run after it and have fun.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sally. Every day Sally would go to the park to play on the slide and the swings. One day she saw something shiny, glittering in the sand. Sally bent down to take a closer look, and it was a scale! Sally couldn't believe her eyes. The scale was fake and it was spraying sparkles. She excitedly ran home to show her mom what she had found. "It's pretty, isn't it mommy?" Sally said. Her mom smiled and said, "Yes it is. I wonder what will happen when you take it to the park tomorrow." The next day Sally brought the scale back to the park. When she entered the park, she came to an area filled with kids playing with spray cans. They were having so much fun, spraying on the ground and creating beautiful designs. Sally began to feel excited. She waved the fake scale in the air and shinny sparkles sprayed out from it. Everyone stopped to watch as Sally danced around, spraying the sparkles in the air. The kids all clapped and cheered. From then on, Sally's trips to the park were filled with plenty of spraying, sparkles, and fun.
