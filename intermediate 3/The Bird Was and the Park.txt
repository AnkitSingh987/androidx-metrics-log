Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird Was and the Park

One day, a little bird was in the park. The bird was modest and kind. It liked to sing and play with its friends. The bird saw a girl with pretty hair. The girl was sad and alone. The bird flew to the girl and sang a song. The girl looked up and saw the bird. She smiled and felt happy. The bird and the girl played together in the park. They laughed and ran around. Then, the girl knelt down and whispered a wish. The bird listened and nodded. Suddenly, the bird changed into a big, pretty butterfly. The girl was very surprised! The butterfly and the girl became best friends and played in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a nice aunt named Jane. Aunt Jane had a big, pink dress. Lily loved the pink dress very much. One day, Lily asked Aunt Jane if she could wear the pink dress. Aunt Jane said, "Yes, but you must be very careful with it." Lily was so happy. She put on the pink dress and went outside to play. While she was playing, something bad happened. Lily fell down and the pink dress got dirty. Lily was very sad. She did not want to tell Aunt Jane what happened. But she knew she had to be honest. Lily went to Aunt Jane and showed her the dirty dress. Aunt Jane was not mad. She said, "It's okay, Lily. We can wash it. Thank you for telling me the truth." The moral of the story is to always be honest, even when you are scared.

Once upon a time, there was a young man with a mustache. He was very sad because his mustache was not nice. It was all messy and tangled. The young man wanted to restore his mustache to make it look good again. One day, the young man met a friend. His friend said, "I can help you restore your mustache! Let's go to my house and I will fix it for you." The young man was very happy and went with his friend. At the friend's house, they tried to fix the mustache. But it did not work. The mustache became even more messy and tangled. The young man cried and said, "My mustache is worse now! I will never have a nice mustache again." And so, the young man with the mustache stayed sad and never found a way to restore his mustache.

One sunny day, Tom went to the park with his mom. He saw a big ice-cream truck. Tom felt anxious because he wanted ice-cream. His mom said, "You can choose one ice-cream." Tom looked at all the ice-creams. There were many colors and flavors. He liked red, blue, and green. Tom did not know which one to choose. He thought hard about it. At last, Tom chose the red ice-cream. It was sweet and cold. He was very happy. His mom was happy too. They sat on a bench and enjoyed the sunny day together.

One day, an elderly man sat under a big oak tree. He liked to dream about his past. He thought about when he was a little boy and played with his friends. A small boy walked up to the man and said, "Why are you here? I want to play under this tree." The elderly man said, "I am dreaming. This tree is special to me." The small boy was sad. He wanted to play, but the elderly man was in his way. The small boy thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He said, "Can I dream with you?" The elderly man smiled and said, "Yes, let's dream together." They both sat under the oak tree and dreamed. The small boy and the elderly man became friends. They learned that they could share the tree and be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, hairy dog named Max. They loved to play together outside. One day, they went for a walk near a factory. The factory made toys for kids. As they walked, Tim saw a man working in the factory. The man was very friendly. He waved at Tim and Max. Tim waved back and said, "Hi! What are you doing?" The man replied, "I am making toys for kids like you. Would you like to see?" Tim and Max went inside the factory. They saw many toys being made. There were dolls, cars, and balls. Tim and Max had a great time at the factory. The man gave Tim a toy car to keep. Tim said, "Thank you!" and waved goodbye. Tim and Max walked back home, excited to play with their new toy.
