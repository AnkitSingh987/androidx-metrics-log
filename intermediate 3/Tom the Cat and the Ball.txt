Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom was a flexible cat who liked to play all day. His best friend was a dog named Max. They lived in a big house with a nice family. One sunny day, Tom and Max were playing outside. Tom saw a ball and said, "Max, let's play with the ball!" Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, Tom! I love to play with the ball." They took turns hitting the ball with their paws. Tom would strike the ball, then Max would strike it back. Suddenly, the ball went very high in the sky. Tom and Max looked up and saw a big bird. The bird caught the ball in its beak and flew away. Tom and Max were sad, but then they saw something shiny on the ground. It was a new ball! They were so happy and played with the new ball all day long.

One day, the sun was shining very bright. A little dog named Spot was playing outside. He was running, jumping, and having lots of fun. Spot's friend, a little bird named Blue, came to play too. They played all day and began to feel tired. But they didn't want to stop playing, so they made a plan to stay up all night. That night, the moon shone in the sky. Spot and Blue were excited to stay awake. They played hide and seek, and they told each other stories. As the night went on, they started to feel very tired. Their eyes were getting heavy, but they wanted to stay up and play. Finally, the sun began to shine again. Spot and Blue were so tired that they could hardly play anymore. They had stayed up all night, just like they planned. But now, they were too tired to enjoy the new day. So, Spot and Blue decided to take a long nap. They learned that staying up all night was not as fun as they thought. Next time, they would get some sleep and be ready to play in the sunshine.

Once upon a time, there was a little purple bird named Bobby. Bobby had a hurt shoulder. He could not fly very well. Bobby was sad. One day, Bobby met a kind old owl. The owl said, "Bobby, you can improve your shoulder. You should rest and eat good food." Bobby listened to the owl and did what he said. After some days, Bobby's shoulder got better. He could fly high and fast again. Bobby was very happy. He thanked the owl and said, "You were right, I improved my shoulder by resting and eating good food." The owl smiled and said, "Always listen to good advice, Bobby." And so, Bobby learned to listen to good advice and take care of himself. He lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is to listen to good advice and take care of yourself.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a man selling popcorn. Tim wanted some, so his mom bought a big bag for him. Tim was very happy and started to eat the popcorn. While eating, Tim saw a small bird in the park. He wanted to share his popcorn with the bird. But he was foolish and threw all the popcorn at the bird at once. The bird got scared and flew away. Tim was sad because he had no more popcorn and the bird didn't get any. Tim's mom saw what happened and told him, "Next time, be gentle and kind when sharing with others." Tim learned his lesson and promised to be more careful. The next time they went to the park, Tim shared his popcorn with the birds one piece at a time. The birds were happy, and so was Tim.

John jumped up and ran around the open field. He was chasing a butterfly. Suddenly, he heard a voice. "John! Come over here!" It was his mom. John ran to his mom and asked what she wanted. "Let's go build a hut," she said. John was excited. He had never built a hut before. So, John and his mom went to the open field. John ran around, collecting some sticks and leaves for the hut. His mom found two big stones and placed them in the middle of the field. When John and his mom were finished, the hut was built. It was open so that everyone could see inside. John was very happy. "We made a hut!" he said proudly. John's mom smiled and gave him a hug. John and his mom had lots of fun playing in the hut. They ran around, laughed and imagined many adventures. It was the best day ever!
