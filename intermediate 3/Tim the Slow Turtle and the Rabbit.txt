Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Slow Turtle and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a slow turtle named Tim. Tim had a friend, a fast rabbit named Ron. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, they found some pretty pastel chalk on the ground. The chalk was pink, blue, and yellow. Tim and Ron wanted to draw with the pastel chalk, but they could not decide who would draw first. They started to quarrel, and their voices got louder and louder. They were not happy anymore. A wise old owl saw them quarrel and came to help. The owl said, "Why don't you both draw together?" Tim and Ron liked the idea. They stopped quarreling and started to draw. Tim drew slow circles, while Ron drew fast lines. They made a beautiful picture with the pastel chalk and became happy friends again.

One day, a girl named Amy was in her comfortable home. She wanted to make a pastry. Amy asked her mom, "Can I make a pastry, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can." Amy and her mom made the pastry together. They split the dough into two parts. They put jam in the middle of one part and put the other part on top. Then, they put the pastry in the oven. When the pastry was done, they took it out of the oven. Amy and her mom shared the pastry. They were happy and said, "Yum! This is the best pastry ever!" They had a fun day making and eating the pastry together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a cat named Tom and a dog named Max. They were best friends. Every day, they would wake up early and play together in the yard. One day, Tom and Max found a big, wet ball in the yard. They were very excited. "Let's play with the wet ball!" said Tom. "Yes, let's play for a minute!" agreed Max. So, they started to play with the wet ball, rolling it around and having lots of fun. Suddenly, the wet ball began to grow bigger and bigger. Tom and Max were surprised. "What is happening?" asked Tom. "I don't know," said Max. They kept playing with the ball, but it kept growing. The ball became so big that it filled the whole yard. Tom and Max couldn't play anymore. They tried to push the ball away, but it was too heavy. They were very sad. The big, wet ball had taken over their yard, and they couldn't play together anymore. And that was the bad end for Tom and Max's fun day.

Lily and Ben liked to play with blocks. They wanted to build a big castle with many towers and walls. They worked hard and helped each other. Lily was good at making towers. Ben was good at making walls. They were a dependable team. But Tom and Mia did not like their castle. They wanted to knock it down. They waited for Lily and Ben to go away. Then they ran to the castle and pushed it with their hands. The blocks fell down and made a loud noise. Tom and Mia laughed and ran away. Lily and Ben heard the noise and came back. They saw their castle was broken. They felt sad and angry. They wanted to fix their castle. But they did not have enough blocks. Some blocks were lost or broken. They did not know what to do. Then they saw a big box in the corner. It had a label that said "More blocks". Lily and Ben opened the box and found many new blocks. They were happy and surprised. They did not know who left the box for them. Maybe it was their teacher or their mom or their dad. They said thank you to whoever it was. They used the new blocks to rebuild their castle. They made it bigger and stronger than before. They added more towers and walls and even a gate. They were proud of their castle. They succeeded in making their dream come true. They hugged each other and smiled. They were a dependable team.

John was playing in the park when he saw Cindy. They were both three years old and wanted to be friends. "Hi Cindy!" John said. "Hello John!" Cindy said. John asked Cindy, "What do you have in your bag?" Cindy told him, "I have some reliable toys inside. Would you like to take a look?" John said, "Yes! That would be great." So Cindy reached into her bag and pulled out a ball, a bear and a kite. John said, "Wow! These toys look really reliable!" Cindy smiled and said, "Yeah, I promise you can trust them." John said, "Thanks for telling me about your bag and for sharing your toys." Cindy replied, "You're welcome. Let's play together now!" John and Cindy played happily in the park all day long.
