Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Rabbit and the Bear

Once upon a time, there were two best friends; a rabbit and a bear. They were always together and loved playing. One day, they decided to go exploring. They came across a tunnel. The rabbit and the bear were so excited and quickly ran inside. Suddenly, they heard a very loud noise and saw a nasty sour liquid coming from the top of the tunnel. The liquid was soaking them! The bear shouted, "Let's get out of here!" The rabbit replied, "Ok, let's go!" The two friends quickly ran out the tunnel and never went near it again. They learned their lesson and never went exploring without their umbrellas.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went on a trip with his mom and dad. They went to a big farm with many animals. Tim loved animals and was very excited. One day, Tim saw a baby bird that fell from its nest. The bird was frightened and alone. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he picked it up gently and raised it high to put it back in its nest. The bird's mommy said thank you and flew away with her baby. Tim felt happy that he helped the bird. His mom and dad were proud of him. They all had a great trip on the farm and went home with a big smile. Tim learned that it is important to help others when they are scared or in trouble.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big, red balloon tied to a bench. Tim wanted the balloon, but it was too high for him to reach. "Mom, can you help me?" he asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, I can lift you up." Before they got the balloon, a man walked up to them. He had a big smile and a hat. The man said, "I will give you a ticket to win a prize, but you have to do something silly." Tim thought it sounded fun, so he made a stupid face and got the ticket. Later, they went to the prize booth with the ticket. Tim's mom lifted him up again so he could give the ticket to the lady. She smiled and gave Tim a big, blue teddy bear. Tim was so happy! He hugged the bear and thanked his mom for helping him. They had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy crab named Bob. Bob lived in the sea and loved to play with his friends. One day, Bob found a shiny metal toy on the beach. He was very happy and wanted to show it to his friends. Bob went to his friend, Sally the fish, and said, "Look at my new toy! Isn't it pretty?" Sally looked at the metal toy and said, "Be careful, Bob. That toy might pinch you." But Bob didn't listen. He was too excited to play with his new toy. Later, as Bob was playing with the metal toy, it pinched him. "Ouch!" he cried. He remembered what Sally had told him. Bob went back to Sally and said, "You were right. The toy pinched me." Sally smiled and said, "It's okay, Bob. Next time, just listen to your friends." From that day on, Bob was not so bossy and always listened to his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat liked to go fast in the water. One day, the boat saw a big rock. A fish came to the boat to warn it. "Watch out, boat! There is a big rock," said the fish. The boat did not want to stop. It was persistent and kept going fast. The fish tried to warn the boat again. "Please, boat, be careful! The rock is very close," said the fish. The boat finally listened to the fish. It slowed down and went around the big rock. The boat and the fish were happy they did not crash. From that day on, the boat always listened to the fish when it warned about danger. They became good friends and had many fun days in the water.
