Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Jump

One day, a little girl named Lucy was very enthusiastic. She wanted to jump on the couch. But her mom said, "No, Lucy! You can't jump on the couch." Lucy tried to resist, but she really wanted to jump. So, she waited until her mom went to the kitchen. Then, she started to jump on the couch. Suddenly, she heard her mom coming back. Lucy stopped jumping and sat on the couch. Her mom knew what Lucy did, but she didn't say anything. She just gave Lucy a big hug and told her to be careful.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dark night. People were happy because they were going to see a big firework show. They all went to the park to watch the pretty lights in the sky. The man who made the fireworks was very careful. He did not want anyone to get hurt. But one firework did not go up in the sky. It went to the side and started a small fire. The fire got bigger and bigger. People were scared. A brave dog saw the fire and knew he had to help. He ran fast to escape the fire and barked loud to tell the people to move away. The people listened to the dog and went to a safe place. The firemen came and put out the fire. Everyone was safe and they thanked the brave dog for helping them. The town had a big party for the brave dog, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a red truck. The truck liked to help people. It was very good at its job. The truck made work easy for everyone. One sunny day, the truck went to a big sand hill. It needed to pour sand into a hole. The truck was very happy to do this. It poured the sand into the hole, little by little. After the hole was full, the truck went back to its home. The people were very happy with the truck. They said, "Thank you, truck!" The truck smiled and went to sleep, ready to help again tomorrow.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play together. One day, they decided to deliver some cookies to their neighbors. They put the cookies in a basket and walked around the street. They saw a big dog with a spot on his eye. He was barking and growling at them. Lily and Ben were scared and ran away. They dropped the basket and the cookies fell out. The dog stopped barking and sniffed the cookies. They smelled tasty. He ate one and liked it. He ate another and liked it more. He ate all the cookies and felt happy. Lily and Ben came back to get the basket. They saw the dog with the spot on his eye. He was wagging his tail and licking his lips. He looked friendly. Lily and Ben were surprised and curious. They went closer to the dog and petted him. He licked their hands and nuzzled their faces. He was not mean, he was just hungry. Lily and Ben felt sorry for him. They decided to share their lunch with him. They gave him some bread and cheese and water. He ate and drank and thanked them with a bark. Lily and Ben learned that sometimes, animals are not bad, they just need some food and love. They made a new friend with the dog with the spot on his eye. They named him Spot and took him home with them. They asked their parents if they could keep him. Their parents agreed and were proud of their kindness. Lily and Ben were happy and so was Spot. They delivered some cookies to their neighbors and got a dog in return.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Skip. Skip was a happy dog who loved to play outside. One day, it was wet outside because it had rained. Skip did not mind the rain. He liked to play in the wet grass. Skip's favorite toy was a bone. He would run and skip with the bone in his mouth. He liked to hide the bone and then find it again. Skip would dig a hole and put the bone in it. Then he would cover the hole with dirt. Today, Skip found his bone in the wet grass. He was so happy! He skipped and played with the bone all day. When it was time to go inside, Skip was tired but happy. He had a fun day playing with his bone in the wet grass.
