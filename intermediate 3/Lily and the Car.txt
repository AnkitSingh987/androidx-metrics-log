Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Car

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her toy car. She saw her mom wrap a box with pretty paper. Lily had an idea. She wanted to wrap her toy car too. She found a roll of film and started to wrap her car. Lily's big brother, Tom, saw her wrapping her car with the film. He said, "Lily, that film is not for wrapping. Mom will be angry." Lily did not listen. She kept wrapping her car with the film. Later, mom came into the room and saw Lily's wrapped car. She looked angry. She said, "Lily, I told you not to use the film for wrapping." Lily felt bad. She said, "I'm sorry, mom." Mom hugged her and said, "Next time, ask me before using something new." Lily promised to ask mom next time, and they unwrapped the car together.

Once upon a time, there was a big clock. The clock was deaf. It could not hear the sounds around it. The clock lived in a box. One day, a girl named Lily came to unpack the box. She opened the box and saw the big clock. The clock was very pretty. Lily was happy to see the clock. Lily put the clock on the wall. The clock could not hear, but it could still tell the time. Lily and the clock were now friends. They had fun together every day.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a perfect round ball. It loved to roll around all day long. The ball was very happy when it played in the field. One morning, at sunrise, the ball saw a little girl. The girl had a big smile on her face. She ran to the ball and gave it a gentle push. The ball began to roll and the girl laughed with joy. The girl and the ball played together every day at sunrise. They would roll and laugh in the perfect green field. They were very happy and became the best of friends.

One day, John was in a park playing on his scooter. He had learned to fly with it - as high as a bird! So he kept trying and trying. He was persistent. John's mom was watching him and said, "John, you are so persistent! You are such a brave boy!" John smiled and kept trying, until he actually flew higher than ever before. He exclaimed, "I did it!" John's mom clapped and said, "You did it! You really can fly on that scooter!" John was so happy, he grinned from ear to ear. He had done it. He was a flying scooter master!

Once there was a girl who loved to record. She was always picking up a piece, a stick or a rock, and recording how it sounded. One day while recording, she heard something a little different. It wasn't a loud sound but it was more of a scratchy noise. She followed the sound to a large rock and when she touched it, it made a rough noise. She was so excited! She recorded the sound and played it back over and over again. She was so proud of her new discovery. She went back to the same spot every day to record the noise and eventually it became her favorite piece of music.

One day, a bird named Bob wanted to soar high in the sky. He saw a twig on the ground and thought it would make his nest better. He picked it up with his beak and started to fly up, up, up. The twig was hard, but Bob was strong. As Bob flew higher, he met another bird named Sue. Sue asked, "What are you doing with that twig?" Bob said, "I want to make my nest better, so I found this hard twig." Sue smiled and said, "I have a lot of twigs in my nest. We can share them!" Just then, a big wind came and blew the twig out of Bob's beak. It flew far away. Bob and Sue were sad, but then they saw something unexpected. The twig had landed in a big pile of soft leaves. They flew down and saw that the leaves made a perfect nest. Bob and Sue were so happy. They made a new nest together with the leaves and the hard twig. Now they could soar high in the sky and always have a cozy nest to come back to. And they became the best of friends.
