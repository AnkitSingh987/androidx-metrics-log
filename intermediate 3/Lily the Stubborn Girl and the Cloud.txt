Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Stubborn Girl and the Cloud

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little girl named Lily. She did not like to eat her vegetables. One day, her mom decided to cook a big pot of soup with lots of yummy vegetables. Lily's mom said, "Lily, come see the steam from the soup. It smells so good!" Lily came to the kitchen and saw the steam coming from the pot. She thought it looked like a big, fluffy cloud. Lily's mom asked, "Do you want to try the soup?" Lily was stubborn, but she decided to try it. She took a small sip and said, "Yum! I like it!" From that day on, Lily was not stubborn about eating her vegetables anymore.

Ben and his mum were on an adventurous holiday. Along the way, they saw many new and unusual things. One day, Ben saw something that he had never seen before. It was a mask, made of golden metal, with delicate designs all over it. He asked his mum what it was and she said, "That's a magical mask. It has the power to heal." Ben couldn't believe it. He was so excited and he wanted to try it. His mum said, "You'll have to wait a bit longer Ben, but someday, you'll be able to use it. I promise". Ben was patient, but he was also curious. Finally, the time came for Ben to use the magical mask. He held it up to his face and it instantly started to make him feel better. He felt energy course through his body and he was healed completely. Ben had an amazing adventure with his magical mask. He was so glad that his mum had shown it to him and trusted him to know how to use it.

One day, a girl named Lily had a fake toy cat. She loved to play with it and tell it stories. Lily and her toy cat were best friends. One sunny day, Lily and her toy cat went to the park. They saw a boy named Tim with a big bowl. Tim looked sad. Lily asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I lost my ball in the big tree, and I can't reach it." Lily had an idea. She put her fake toy cat in the bowl. She told Tim, "Let's use the bowl to get your ball." They both held the bowl high and caught the ball. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Lily, and your toy cat!" From that day on, Lily, her toy cat, and Tim played together in the park. They had lots of fun and always helped each other. And they all became best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy car. He put the car on the stair and watched it go down. Tim was having a lot of fun! Suddenly, a big dog named Max came into the house. Max saw the car on the stair and wanted to play too. But Tim was frightened of Max. He didn't want Max to play with his car. Tim said, "No, Max! My car!" Max felt sad and walked away. Tim thought about it and didn't want Max to be sad. So, Tim put the car on the stair again and called Max. "Max, come play!" he said. Max came back, and they played together with the car on the stair. Tim was not frightened anymore. They both had fun and became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a white cat and a white dog. They were not friends. They would fight all the time. One day, they found a map. The map showed the way to a big, yummy cake. The cat and the dog decided to unite and find the cake together. They followed the map and walked for a long time. They crossed a river and climbed a hill. Finally, they found the big cake on a table. The white cat and the white dog were very hungry. They started to eat the cake without waiting for each other. They ate too fast and too much. Soon, they both felt sick. They wished they had shared the cake nicely. The bad ending was that the cat and the dog got sick from eating too much cake. The moral value of the story is that we should share and be patient with our friends.
