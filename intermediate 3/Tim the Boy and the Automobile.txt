Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Automobile

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy automobile. He would push it around the house all day long. He was very grateful for his automobile. One day, Tim saw a big box in the living room. He wanted to lift the box and see what was inside. He tried and tried, but the box was too heavy for him. Tim thought his automobile could help him lift the box. Tim put his automobile under the box and pushed it really hard. The box started to lift a little, but then it fell down. The box was too heavy, and it broke Tim's automobile. Tim was very sad and cried. He wished he had not tried to lift the big box with his automobile.

Anna liked to play with her toys in the garden. She had a doll, a ball, a car and a pipe. The pipe was heavy and hard, but Anna liked to imagine it was a magic wand. She waved it around and said funny words. One day, Anna saw a big hole in the fence. She wondered what was on the other side. She took her pipe and crawled through the hole. She saw a road with many cars and trucks. She thought it was exciting. She wanted to cross the road and see more. She did not look left and right. She did not see the big truck coming fast. She ran with her pipe in her hand. The truck driver did not see her either. He could not stop in time. There was a loud noise and a big crash. Anna and her pipe flew in the air. They landed on the ground. Anna did not move. She did not imagine anymore. She was gone. The truck driver was very sad. He wished he could have seen her. He wished he could have saved her. He wished she had not played with the pipe.

Once upon a time, there was a girl. She liked to play outside. One day she wanted to go for a ride. She put on her shoes and ran outside. The girl saw a big stream. It was thin and sparkly. She wanted to ride it. She got into a boat and started to paddle. The stream was so long and she had lots of fun. Soon it was time to go home. The girl waved goodbye to the stream and said until next time. Then she got out of the boat and ran back home. The end.

One day, a young girl named Amy was eager to play. She went to the park with her mom. At the park, she saw many kids. They were all full of youth and joy. Amy saw a big tree with pretty leaves. She had an idea. "Mom, can we spread a blanket under the tree?" she asked. Her mom said, "Yes, that is a good idea!" They spread the blanket on the grass and sat down. Amy played with her toys on the blanket. She saw a butterfly and tried to catch it. The butterfly flew away, but Amy was happy. She had a fun day at the park with her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to dig in the dirt and play with his friends. One day, while he was digging, he found a shiny metal toy. It was a little car! Spot was so excited and wanted to show his friends. Spot ran to his friend, Kitty the cat, and said, "Look, Kitty! I found a metal toy car! Do you want to play with it?" Kitty looked at the car and said, "Yes, Spot! Can you lend it to me for a little while?" Spot was happy to lend his toy to Kitty, and they played together all day long. After playing with the car, Spot and Kitty decided to share it with their other friends. They went to see Birdy the bird, and Froggy the frog. All of their friends loved the metal toy car, and they took turns playing with it. Spot was so happy that he could lend his toy to his friends and share the fun with everyone. And they all played happily ever after.
