Category: Intermediate
Title: The Large Cross and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a large cross in the middle of a big park. It was very old and its paint was fading away. The children who played in the park loved the cross, but they wished it was prettier. One sunny day, a kind painter came to the park with his paint and brushes. He saw the large cross and thought, "I can make this cross beautiful again!" So, he started to paint the cross with bright, happy colors. The children watched the painter as he painted the large cross. They clapped and cheered when he was done. The cross was now the most beautiful thing in the park. The children were so happy, and they played around the colorful cross all day long.

Once there was a little girl called Jenny. She was three years old and loved playing with her toys. One day, her mum bought her a new phone. The phone was smooth and pink and Jenny was so excited. She tried talking to the phone and picking it up, but it didn’t seem to do anything. Her mum showed her how to match the numbers on the phone. Jenny listened carefully to her mum and tried it out. She matched the numbers correctly and the phone lit up. Jenny was so happy and she hugged her mum. She picked up the phone and said “Hello?”. But there was no one on the other end. She looked at her mum and said “Mummy, it’s not working.” Her mum laughed and said “You need to push the green button to make a call.” Jenny pushed the green button and soon she was talking to her granny. They chatted for a while, talking about what they had been up to. Jenny said goodbye and thanked her mum for the new phone. Jenny was so pleased with her new phone, it was smooth and she had learned how to make a call. That night, she hugged the phone to her chest and fell asleep with a big smile on her face.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a big tree with a note on it. The note said, "Welcome to the park." Lucy was very happy and felt very alert. Lucy saw a boy named Tom playing with a ball. Tom said, "Hi Lucy, do you want to play with me?" Lucy said, "Yes, I want to play too!" They played together and had lots of fun. Suddenly, a big wind came and took the ball up into the sky. Lucy and Tom were very surprised! They looked up and saw a big bird flying with the ball. The bird dropped the ball back to them and said, "Welcome to my park! Let's all play together!" Lucy and Tom laughed and played with the big bird. They had a great day at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He liked to skip and play. He saw a man named Hunter. Hunter was a nice man who helped people feel safe. "Hi, Hunter!" Tim said. "Do you want to skip with me?" Hunter smiled and said, "Yes, I would like to skip with you." As Tim and Hunter skipped in the park, they saw a big box. It was a surprise! They opened the box and found many fun toys inside. They played with the toys and had a great time. They were happy and safe.

Jamie was a very enthusiastic three-year-old boy. He loved to play in the park with his parents. One day, when Jamie and his parents were at the park, Jamie saw something that made him very excited. He saw a big yellow train on the swings! Jamie squealed with joy and asked his mum if he could go on the train swing. His mum said yes and Jamie started to swing enthusiastically. He went higher and higher and giggled with delight. He felt like he was flying like a bird. After some time, Jamie was tired so his mum and dad helped him get off the swing. Jamie was sad that it was over and wanted to go on the train swing again. He asked his mum if they could come back another day. His mum gave him a big hug and promised that they could go back soon. Jamie felt so happy and went back home with a smile on his face. He couldn't wait to go back to the park to swing on the big yellow train again.
