Category: Intermediate
Title: The Temple and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big temple. The temple was different from all the others. It had many colors and shapes. It was a happy place where all the animals liked to play. One day, the animals wanted to have a race. They all lined up in front of the temple. There was a fast rabbit, a slow turtle, a big elephant, and a small mouse. They all wanted to see who was the fastest. The race started, and they all ran as fast as they could. The rabbit was very fast, but the turtle was slow. The elephant was big, but the mouse was small. They all tried their best, but in the end, the rabbit won the race. The animals were happy and cheered for the rabbit. They all had fun and went back to the different temple to play some more.

One day, a little boy named Tim was in the kitchen. He wanted to make cocoa. He got a cup, some cocoa, and a spoon. He put the cocoa in the cup and began to mix it with water. As he mixed, he saw something strange happen. The cocoa started to bubble and grow bigger! Tim was frightened. He didn't know what to do. He called for his mom to come and help. Mom came running into the kitchen. She saw the big, bubbly cocoa and started to laugh. "Oh, Tim!" she said, "You mixed it too fast!" Mom showed Tim how to mix the cocoa slowly and carefully. The cocoa went back to normal. They both laughed and enjoyed their cocoa together. And from that day on, Tim knew how to mix cocoa just right.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Emma. Emma liked to keep her room clean. She kept her toys in the box and her book on the shelf. One day Emma wanted to use the toilet. She ran to the toilet, but there was something wrong. The toilet was not clean. Emma was upset. So Emma called her mum for help. Emma's mum came quickly. She grabbed the cleaning supplies and mop and made bubbles in the toilet. Emma watched as her mum cleaned and scrubbed the toilet. When the toilet was finally clean, Emma was so happy. She smiled and hugged her mum. The toilet sparkled and shone. The end.

Lily and Max are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. They also like to help Mom and Dad with some tasks. Mom and Dad give them stickers when they do a good job. One day, Mom asks Lily and Max to help her fold the laundry. She shows them how to fold a shirt and a sock. She says, "Can you repeat what I do?" Lily and Max nod and try to fold their own shirts and socks. They look at Mom and smile. Mom says, "You are doing great! Now, can you repeat what I say?" She points to a shirt and says, "This is a shirt." Lily and Max point to their shirts and say, "This is a shirt." Mom points to a sock and says, "This is a sock." Lily and Max point to their socks and say, "This is a sock." Mom claps and says, "You are so smart! You can repeat words and actions. I am very proud of you." She gives them each a sticker and a hug. Lily and Max hug her back and say, "Thank you, Mom. We love you." Mom says, "I love you too, my adorable twins. You make me happy every day." She kisses them on their cheeks and says, "Now, let's finish the task and put the laundry away." Lily and Max say, "Okay, Mom. We will help you." They pick up their folded clothes and follow Mom to the closet. They are happy to help and to learn new things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and wear her fancy dress. One day, Lily got a gift from her grandma. It was a pretty doll. Lily was very happy and played with her doll all day. One day, Lily and her doll were playing outside in the mud. They had lots of fun, but her doll got very dirty. Lily knew she had to wash her doll to make her clean again. She took her doll inside and filled a big bowl with warm water and soap. Lily gently washed her doll in the soapy water. She scrubbed the dirt away and made her doll nice and clean. After the wash, Lily's doll looked like new again. Lily was happy that her doll was clean, and they played together every day. And from that day on, Lily always made sure to wash her doll after playing outside.
