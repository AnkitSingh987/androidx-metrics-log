Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She went to school every day. Sally liked her classroom a lot. It was full of colors and toys. One day, Sally's teacher, Miss Jane, made a tasty snack for the class. She told Sally and her friends, "I have a yummy treat for you today!" Sally and her friends were very happy. They could not wait to eat the tasty snack. Miss Jane put the snack in the oven to cook. But she forgot about it! The snack started to burn. Smoke came from the oven. Miss Jane quickly took it out. The snack was not tasty anymore. Sally and her friends were sad. But Miss Jane said, "Don't worry, we will make a new snack tomorrow!" Sally and her friends smiled and went back to playing in their classroom.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little bunny named Ben. He lived in a small hole in the woods. Ben had many friends, like the birds, the squirrels, and the mice. One sunny day, Ben and his friends wanted to gather nuts and berries for a big party. They all worked together to find the best nuts and berries in the woods. While they were gathering, Ben found a secret hiding spot behind a big tree. "Look what I found!" Ben said to his friends. They all came over to see the secret spot. They decided to hide some of their nuts and berries there, so they would have a surprise for later. At the party, they all had fun eating and playing games. When it was time to go home, Ben said, "Remember our secret hiding spot!" His friends nodded and smiled. They knew they could come back to the secret spot anytime they wanted. And so, Ben and his friends had many more fun days gathering nuts and berries and enjoying their secret hiding spot in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Jill. She was three years old and loved to play. One day, Jill wanted to go outside. She asked her mom, "Can I go outside now?" Her mom said, "Yes, but don't go too far. Just stay in the garden." Jill said, "Ok!" and went out. She saw the big green backyard and started to zoom around. She felt so happy and was running so fast that it felt like she was flying. Then, she saw something and stopped. It was a pass, stuck in the fence. She tried to pull it out and it was too tight. No matter what she tried, nothing fit. Jill decided to go back and ask her mom for help. When she arrived at the house, Jill said, "Mommy, can you help me? I found a pass but it won't fit in the fence." Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, honey. I can help you." She took the pass and put it into the fence. It fit perfectly. Jill smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" She zoomed off, off to play once again.

Lily liked to polish her books. She had many books in her room. She liked to make them shiny and clean. She used a soft cloth and some water. She rubbed the cloth on the books gently. One day, Lily went to the library with her mom. The library had many more books than her room. Lily wanted to polish them all. She took a cloth from her bag and started to polish a book on a shelf. But the librarian saw her and said, "No, no, no! You can't polish the books here. They are not yours. You have to be careful with them. Put the cloth away and use your eyes, not your hands." Lily felt frustrated. She did not understand why she could not polish the books. She liked to polish books. She thought she was helping. She did not want to use her eyes. She wanted to use her hands. She started to cry. Her mom heard her cry and came to her. She hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I know you love books. But the library books are for everyone to share. You can't polish them because you might damage them or make them wet. You can look at them and read them, but you have to be gentle and respectful. Do you understand?" Lily nodded. She wiped her tears and said, "I'm sorry, librarian. I won't polish the books anymore. Can I still read them?" The librarian smiled and said, "Of course you can, Lily. You are a good reader. And you can polish your own books at home as much as you like. Just don't bring the cloth to the library again, okay?" Lily agreed. She put the cloth back in her bag and picked a book to read. She was still a little frustrated, but she also felt curious and happy. She liked the library. She liked books. And she liked to polish them.
