Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play ball on the pitch. One sunny day, he was playing with his friends when a big, confused dog came running over. The big dog took Tim's ball and ran away. Tim and his friends chased the dog, but they couldn't catch him. Tim was very sad and began to complain. "Oh no! My ball is gone. What will I do now?" he cried. Just then, a kind girl named Lisa came over. She saw Tim's sad face and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told her about the big, confused dog that took his ball. Lisa thought for a moment and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I have an extra ball we can play with." Tim smiled and thanked Lisa. Together, they played on the pitch with their new ball. Tim learned that sometimes things go wrong, but with the help of friends, everything can be okay.

One day, a restless dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a big hill and thought, "I want to see the view from the top!" Spot started to walk up the hill. As he walked, he saw a small cat named Mittens. Mittens was sad because she lost her toy. Spot wanted to help Mittens. He thought, "If I find the toy, I can offer it to Mittens and make her happy." Spot looked everywhere for the toy. He looked under trees and behind rocks. Then, he saw something shiny near the top of the hill. Spot ran to the shiny thing and picked it up. It was Mittens' toy! He was so happy that he could help her. But when he turned around to give the toy to Mittens, he saw that she was now at the top of the hill. Spot was surprised! Mittens had found her own way up the hill. Spot ran up the hill to join Mittens. They played with the toy together and enjoyed the beautiful view. Spot was not restless anymore. He was happy that he could help his new friend Mittens, and they both had a great day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw with his pencil. One day, Tim felt dizzy when he woke up. He did not know why he felt dizzy. He wanted to draw, but he could not. Tim tried to find his mom to tell her he felt dizzy. But his mom was not in the house. Tim was sad and scared. He looked all around the house but could not find her. He sat on the floor and started to cry. Just then, Tim's mom came in the house. She had been outside in the garden. Tim realized he was not alone. He told his mom about feeling dizzy. His mom hugged him and gave him some water. Soon, Tim felt better and went back to drawing with his pencil.

Lily and Max were playing in the garden. They saw a big yellow flower with many seeds in the middle. Lily liked the flower. She wanted to pick it and give it to her mom. "Can I have the flower, Max?" she asked. "No, Lily, you can't," Max said. "The flower is not for us. It is for the bees and the birds. They need the seeds and the nectar." "Why do they need them, Max?" Lily wondered. Max tried to explain. "The bees and the birds help the flower grow. The bees carry the pollen from one flower to another. The birds eat the seeds and drop them in new places. Then new flowers can grow there. The flower is very important for the garden. It is also very beautiful and graceful. It follows the sun all day long. That's why it is called a sunflower." Lily listened to Max. She learned something new. She understood that the flower was not for her. She decided to leave it alone. She smiled at the sunflower and said, "Thank you for being so pretty and helpful, sunflower. I hope you have a good day." Then she and Max went to play with their ball.
