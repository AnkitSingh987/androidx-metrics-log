Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with sand. One sunny day, Tim went to the beach with his mom. He was very excited and impatient to build a big sand castle. When they got to the beach, Tim started to build his castle. He ordered his toy trucks to help him move the sand. "Trucks, help me build the castle," he said. The trucks beeped and moved the sand, just like Tim wanted. Soon, Tim's sand castle was all done. It was the biggest castle on the beach! Tim was very happy and proud. He showed his mom, and she smiled. "Good job, Tim," she said. They had a fun day at the beach, playing with the sand and the trucks.

One sunny day, a little girl named Mia and her dog, Max, went outside to play a game. They liked to play catch with a big, red ball. Mia would throw the ball and Max would run to get it. They laughed and played for a long time. As they played, Max saw a big puddle. He jumped in and got all wet and dirty. Mia said, "Max, you need a bath now!" Max did not like baths. He ran away and hid behind a tree. Mia looked for Max, but she could not find him. Suddenly, a cold wind blew and it started to rain. The rain washed Max clean. Max came out from behind the tree, all clean and happy. Mia said, "Wow, Max! The rain gave you a bath!" They both laughed and went inside to get warm and dry.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to draw with her crayons. One day, she found a big box with many crayons inside. She was very happy and wanted to open the box. Amy tried to open the box, but it was hard. She asked her friend, Tom, for help. "Tom, can you help me open this box?" Tom came over and tried to open the box too. They both pushed and pulled, but the box was still hard to open. Finally, Amy had an idea. She said, "Let's ask my big brother for help!" They went to find her big brother, and he helped them open the box. Inside were the most colorful crayons they had ever seen. Amy and Tom were so happy, they drew pictures all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, modern theater. People loved to go there and watch shows. One day, a little boy named Tim and his mom went to see a play at the theater. They were very excited. During the play, Tim saw a man in a funny costume. He pointed at the man and said, "Look, Mom! Look at the funny man!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. He is very funny. But remember to be quiet so others can watch too." After the play, the funny man came out to meet the people. Everyone was surprised! They did not know he was a real person. The funny man said, "Hello, I am Mr. Funny. I hope you liked the play." Tim said, "I did! You made me laugh." Then, Mr. Funny said, "I have a secret. I was not always funny. I used to be sad all the time. But one day, I decided to make others happy, and it made me happy too." Tim learned that making others happy can make you happy as well. From that day on, Tim tried to make others happy by being kind and helpful. He learned the moral value of spreading happiness. And every time he went to the modern theater, he would remember Mr. Funny and his important lesson.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, round basketball. He liked to play with it. Tim had a friend named Sam. Sam also liked to play with the basketball. They would bounce it and throw it to each other. Tim and Sam were a bit competitive. They wanted to see who was better at playing with the basketball. They would try to get the ball from each other and see who could throw it the farthest. One day, they thought of a new game. They decided to bury the basketball in the sand and see who could find it the fastest. They took turns hiding the basketball and finding it. They had lots of fun playing their new game with the basketball.
