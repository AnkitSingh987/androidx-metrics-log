Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Toy

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim was very sad and miserable. He did not know why his toy car would not go. He asked his mom to help him. His mom saw a cable near the toy car. She picked it up and started to explain to Tim. She said, "This cable makes the toy car go. We need to plug it in the wall." Tim listened to his mom and learned something new. Tim's mom plugged the cable in the wall. The toy car started to go. Tim was very happy. He played with his toy car all day. Tim was not miserable anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a black cat. The cat liked to play near a fence. One day, the cat saw a big dog on the other side of the fence. The dog was not nice. The cat and the dog did not like each other. The cat and the dog would fight through the fence. They would hiss and bark at each other. They wanted to see who was stronger. The cat was fast, but the dog was big. They fought every day. One day, the fence broke. The cat and the dog could now fight without the fence. They fought and fought. The dog was too strong for the cat. The cat got hurt and could not play anymore. The cat and the dog never became friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to bake a cake. She went to the kitchen and found a big pot. Sue thought the pot was harmless, so she decided to use it for baking her cake. Sue's mom came into the kitchen and saw Sue with the pot. "What are you doing, Sue?" her mom asked. "I want to bake a cake in this pot," Sue said. Her mom smiled and said, "That's a great idea, but let's use a smaller pot to make it easier." So Sue and her mom baked the cake together in a smaller pot. They had lots of fun mixing the cake and putting it in the oven. When it was done, they took it out and shared the yummy cake with the whole family. Everyone loved Sue's cake, and she was very happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was a sunny day, but there were big wet puddles from the rain the day before. Tim wore his favorite vest and boots to keep him warm. Tim saw a frog near a wet puddle. The frog was wet too. Tim wanted to hold the frog, so he reached down and picked it up. The frog was slimy and cold, but Tim loved it. Tim showed the frog to his mom, who was watching from the door. She smiled and told Tim to let the frog go back to its home. Tim put the frog down near the wet puddle and watched it hop away. Tim was happy he got to hold the frog, and he ran back inside to tell his mom all about it.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Mr. Ugly. Mr. Ugly was not pretty, but Tim loved him very much. They played together all day and night. One hot day, Mr. Ugly said, "Tim, I am so hot. I think I will melt." Tim looked at him and said, "Don't worry, Mr. Ugly. I will help you." Tim put Mr. Ugly in the fridge to make him cold again. After some time, Tim took Mr. Ugly out, and he was not melting anymore. But then, something strange happened. When Mr. Ugly came out of the fridge, he was not ugly anymore! He was very pretty. Tim was so surprised. Mr. Ugly said, "Thank you, Tim. The cold made me pretty." They played together, and there was silence in the house. Tim and Mr. Ugly were happy, and they stayed friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful man named Tom. He was very good at lifting heavy things. One day, he met a nice girl named Sue. They liked to talk and play together. Tom said to Sue, "I can lift big things because I am powerful." Sue was very happy to see Tom lift heavy things. They had a lot of fun together. One day, Tom asked Sue, "Do you want to be together forever? We can have a marriage." Sue said, "Yes, I want to be with you forever." They had a big party with their friends and family. Everyone was happy for Tom and Sue. After the marriage, Tom and Sue lived in a nice house. Tom still lifted heavy things, and Sue always cheered for him. They were very happy together and loved each other very much.
