Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had an adorable pet dog named Spot. They liked to play together and read books. Lily's favorite book was about a brave knight who saved a princess. One day, Lily and Spot found a very old book in the attic. The book was dirty and starting to rot. They wanted to read it, so they took it to their room. They opened the book and saw pictures of a scary monster. The monster was big and mean. Lily and Spot got scared, but they kept reading. The more they read, the more the monster came to life. The monster jumped out of the book and into their room. Lily and Spot tried to hide, but the monster found them. The monster was too strong, and they could not stop it. The monster took the old book and left their home. Lily and Spot were very sad and scared. They never found the old book again, and they learned to be careful with old, rotting things.

Once upon a time, there was a family. The father was a hunter and used a gun to shoot. One day, he wanted to take his kids with him on the hunting trip, but they were scared. The father felt uncomfortable and decided to leave them at home. At night, the father told them stories of his hunting trip. He told them that he used his gun to shoot a wild animal. Suddenly, the kids started crying and cried all night. The father felt a lot of regret. He wished he never took the gun to the hunting trip and he hugged his kids to make them feel safe again. The father promised never to take his gun on a hunting trip again. He had learnt an important lesson and from then on always felt uncomfortable when he had to use his gun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with red apples on it. Tim was hungry, so he picked a big, red apple from the tree. But the apple was filthy! Tim said, "Yuck! I need to clean this apple." Tim went to the water and washed the apple. He took a bite and said, "Yum! This is a good apple." As he ate, he started to feel very sleepy. Tim sat under the tree and fell asleep. While Tim was sleeping, a squirrel came up to him. The squirrel looked at the apple and said, "Hey, that's my apple!" The squirrel took the apple from Tim and ran away. Tim woke up and saw the squirrel with his apple. He laughed and said, "Silly squirrel, you can have the apple!" Then, Tim went home to tell his mom about his fun day at the park.

Tom and Sam were friends who liked to play with toys. One day, they found a big bin full of old things. They wanted to see what was inside. They climbed on the bin and opened the lid. Inside the bin, they saw many things that looked fun. There were books, clothes, toys, and more. Tom and Sam started to take out the things and throw them on the ground. They did not care if they broke them or made them dirty. They just wanted to have fun. But they did not see that one of the things in the bin was a sharp knife. When Sam reached for a toy, he cut his hand on the knife. He cried out in pain and dropped the toy. He saw blood on his hand and felt very hurt. Tom heard Sam cry and ran to him. He saw the knife and the blood and felt very scared. He tried to help Sam, but he did not know what to do. He wished they had not played with the bin. He felt very sorry and regretted what they had done. They heard a voice behind them. It was Sam's mom. She had seen them playing with the bin and came to stop them. She saw Sam's hand and was very angry and worried. She took Sam to the doctor and told Tom to go home. She said they were very naughty and careless. She said they should never play with things that are not theirs or that could hurt them. She said they should respect other people's things and be careful with their own. Tom and Sam learned a lesson that day. They realized that playing with the bin was a bad idea. They apologized to Sam's mom and to each other. They promised to never do it again. They understood that some things are not toys and that they should think before they act. They hoped that Sam's hand would heal soon and that they could still be friends.
