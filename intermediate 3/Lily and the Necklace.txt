Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Necklace

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily found a box of old jewelry in her grandma's room. She saw a necklace with a big, green stone. It looked a little dirty and disgusting, but she thought it could become pretty if she cleaned it. Lily took the necklace to her friend, Tom. She said, "Look, Tom! I found this necklace. It's disgusting now, but I want to make it pretty. Will you help me?" Tom agreed to help, and they went to find water and soap. Together, they cleaned the necklace, and it became very shiny and pretty. Lily put it on and felt like a princess. She thanked Tom for his help, and they played dress-up with the jewelry all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little square. This square was very special because it could glow. It was an adorable square, and all the kids loved to play with it. One day, a little boy named Tim found the glowing square. He was so happy and showed it to all his friends. They played with the glowing square all day long. The square made them feel happy and warm. But one day, something unexpected happened. The adorable square stopped glowing. The kids were sad and did not know what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. He gave the square a big hug, and suddenly, the square started to glow again! It turns out that the square just needed love to keep glowing. From that day on, the kids always gave the square a big hug, and it never stopped glowing again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy that he loved very much. It was a big, red ball. It was very expensive. Tim played with it every day. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was struggling to get down. Tim wanted to help the cat. He thought that his big, red ball could help. He rolled the ball to the tree and stood on it to reach the cat. Tim was careful not to hurt the cat's skin. He picked the cat up and put it on the ground. The cat was happy and ran away. Tim felt good that he could help the cat. He went home to play with his big, red ball.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Daisy. Daisy was three years old and she had lots of friends. One day Daisy and her friends decided to go on an adventure. They went outside and started to explore. The sky was bright and clear but then suddenly a loud "thunder" noise came from the sky. Daisy was scared and she held her friends hands tightly. "Don't worry Daisy," said one of Daisy's friends, "We can manage it." Daisy was surprised. She knew her friends were very cool and could handle tough situations. So the friends managed to find a tree with lots of leaves. They sat under the tree and waited for the storm to pass. When the thunder was gone, Daisy and her friends had lots of fun. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little orange cat. The cat liked to wander outside and play. One day, the cat walked far away from home. The cat saw many fun things, like trees and flowers. As the sun began to set, the sky turned orange too. The cat looked up and saw the pretty colors. But then, the cat realized it was getting dark. The cat wanted to go home, but it didn't know the way back. Just then, a little light appeared. The cat followed the light as it moved slowly. The light led the cat all the way back home. When the cat saw its house, it was very happy. The cat knew that the little light had helped it find its way home.

Tim and Sam were friends who liked to play in the woods. They saw many animals and plants there. One day, they saw a big house with a fence. They wanted to see who lived there. They climbed over the fence and walked to the house. They knocked on the door, but no one answered. They opened the door and went inside. The house was dark and gloomy. They saw many things that scared them. There were heads of animals on the walls, guns on the table, and traps on the floor. "Let's get out of here!" Tim whispered to Sam. "This is the house of a hunter. He might come back and catch us." But before they could run away, they heard a loud voice behind them. "Who are you and what are you doing in my house?" It was the hunter. He was big and angry. He had a hat on his head and a gun in his hand. "We are sorry, sir. We just wanted to see your house. We didn't mean to bother you." Sam said, trying to be polite. "Too late for that. You have bothered me a lot. You have trespassed on my property and touched my things. You are very naughty boys. I will teach you a lesson." The hunter said, pointing his gun at them. He invited them to stay for dinner, but not as guests. He wanted to cook them and eat them. Tim and Sam screamed and cried, but no one heard them. The hunter locked them in a cage and went to the kitchen. He never let them go. It was a very bad ending.
