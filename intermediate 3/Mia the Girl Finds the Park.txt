Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl Finds the Park

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia was very glad because her mom said she could have a lollipop. When they got to the park, Mia saw her friend, Tom. Mia said to Tom, "Look, I have a lollipop!" Tom was happy for Mia, but he did not have a lollipop. He asked Mia, "Can you show me where you got the lollipop?" Mia said, "Yes, I will show you." Mia and Tom went to the store with Mia's mom. Mia's mom bought a lollipop for Tom. Now, both Mia and Tom had lollipops. They were very glad and went back to the park to play.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a dog named Spot. Spot was a good dog. Tim and Spot liked to play outside. They played ball and ran around. One sunny day, Tim put a leash on Spot and went for a walk in the park. They saw other kids and dogs playing. Tim found a ball and kicked it. Spot ran to get the ball and bring it back to Tim. But this time, when Spot got the ball, something unexpected happened. The ball started to float in the air! Spot jumped up and held the ball with his teeth. Now, Spot was floating too! Tim and the other kids were very surprised. Spot and the ball floated back down to the ground. Tim took the ball and saw that it was not an ordinary ball. It was a magic ball! Tim and Spot played with the magic ball and had lots of fun. And from that day on, their walks in the park were never the same.

Once upon a time, there was a big couch. A girl named Lily liked to sit on the couch. She was a successful helper to her mom and dad. One day, Lily was sitting on the couch and saw a cat outside. The cat was very sad and had a frown on its face. Lily wanted to make the cat happy. She went outside and gave the cat a toy. The cat played with the toy, but still had a frown. Lily tried to give the cat some food, but the cat did not want it. She was sad that she could not make the cat happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat jumped on the couch and started to smile. Lily was surprised. She realized that the cat just wanted to sit on the couch with her. They sat together, and the cat was happy. Lily was happy too, because she made a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there lived a famous zebra named Zeb. Zeb was famous because he had very pretty stripes. All the kids loved to see Zeb and his stripes when they visited the zoo. One day, Zeb was very tired. He wanted to rest, but the kids kept coming to see him. Zeb found a big tree with lots of shade to rest under. He lay down and closed his eyes. While Zeb was resting, a little girl came to see him. She saw Zeb sleeping and said, "Shh, the famous zebra is resting. Let's not wake him up." All the kids were quiet and let Zeb rest. When Zeb woke up, he felt much better and thanked the little girl for letting him rest.

Once upon a time there was a bald man. He was very sad because he wished he had hair like everyone else. So he took a rake and pretended it was a comb. He used the rake to comb his head and pretended he had lots of long hair. But it didn’t work because he was still bald. He was very sad and he cried. Suddenly, a friendly wizard appeared. He said he could help the bald man. He waved his wand and sprinkled some magical dust. With a poof of smoke, the bald man suddenly had a head full of beautiful hair. He was so happy. He thanked the wizard and said goodbye. He went home, excited to show his family his new hair. From then on, he was no longer sad about being bald. He was happy because he had lovely, long hair.
