Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Book

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a book. The book was big and blue. Lucy loved to read books. She was very happy. She sat down and opened the book. She started to read it. But when she did, she felt dizzy. Lucy did not know why she felt dizzy. Lucy did not want to stop reading the book. She wanted to know what was inside. She guessed that maybe the book was magic. Lucy closed her eyes and tried to read the book again. This time, she did not feel dizzy. She was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The book started to talk! The book said, "Hello, Lucy! I am a magic book. I made you dizzy because I wanted you to guess that I am magic. Now we can be friends and read together!" Lucy was very surprised, but she was also very happy. She and the magic book became best friends and read many stories together.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She loved to wear pretty clothes. In her room, there was a big wardrobe. Inside the wardrobe, she kept all her pretty dresses. One day, Lily's friend, Sam, came to play. They played with their toys and had lots of fun. Then, Lily had an idea. "Let's play dress-up!" she said. Sam agreed, and they went to the wardrobe. They found many pretty dresses and hats. Lily took out a very pretty dress. She lay it on the bed. "I will wear this one," she said. But when she put on the dress, she found a secret note in the pocket. The note said, "Look under the bed." Lily and Sam looked under the bed and found a treasure box full of shiny, pretty things. They were so happy and played with their new treasures all day.

One day, a little girl named Anna found a magic wand. She waved it and said, "I want a big piece of candy!" Suddenly, a big piece of candy appeared in front of her. She was very happy and ate the candy. Anna showed the wand to her friend Tom. Tom was confused. "How did you do that?" he asked. Anna said, "I used this magic wand. You can try it too!" Tom took the wand and wished for a toy car. The toy car appeared, and Tom was very happy. But then, the magic wand started to vanish. Anna and Tom were sad. They wanted to play with the wand more. But they still had their candy and toy car, so they played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a goat. The goat was delicate. Tim loved his goat very much. One day, Tim and his goat went to play. They met a big boy named Sam. Sam said, "Let me play with your goat." Tim said, "No, my goat is delicate. You might hurt it." Sam was mad. Sam took the goat when Tim was not looking. The goat was scared. Sam played rough with the goat. The goat got hurt. Tim was sad.

The boy looked at the bicycle in front of him. He wanted to use it. He said to his mom, "Please let me use the bicycle, Mommy?" Mom thought for a moment and said, "Ok, as long as you are careful." The boy smiled and quickly hopped on the bicycle. He rode the bicycle around the yard. It was fun! He pedaled faster and faster. Mom watched him go, a fierce look on her face. Soon, the boy got tired and wanted to stop. He used the brakes and the bicycle came to a halt. The boy got off and grinned. Mom said to him firmly, "Remember to always use the brakes on the bicycle, ok?" The boy nodded his head and said, "Ok Mommy. I promise!"

Timmy liked to play hide and seek with his big sister, Anna. She was very good at finding him, but he was also good at hiding. He knew a secret place in the kitchen, where there was a big cupboard with a small door. He could crawl inside and be hidden from Anna. One day, they were playing hide and seek again. Anna counted to ten and said, "Ready or not, here I come!" Timmy ran to the kitchen and opened the small door. He crawled inside the cupboard and closed the door behind him. He was very quiet and still. He heard Anna's footsteps in the hall, then in the living room, then in the bedroom. He smiled. She would never find him here. But then, he heard a loud noise. It was the door of the big cupboard. Someone was opening it. Timmy was scared. He hoped it was not Anna. He hoped it was Mommy or Daddy. He peeked through a crack and saw a face. It was not Mommy or Daddy. It was a stranger. A man with a mask and a bag. He was looking for something to take. He saw Timmy. "Hey, what are you doing here?" the man said. He grabbed Timmy by the arm and pulled him out of the cupboard. Timmy screamed. He wanted Anna. He wanted Mommy. He wanted Daddy. He wanted to be hidden. "Shut up, kid!" the man said. He put his hand over Timmy's mouth. He looked around. He saw a phone on the counter. He picked it up and dialed a number. He said, "I found something in the kitchen. A kid. What should I do with him?" Timmy heard another voice on the phone. It was Anna. She said, "Let him go, or I'll call the police. I saw you from the window. I have your picture. I have your license plate. You have no choice. Surrender." The man dropped the phone. He looked at Timmy. He looked at the window. He looked at the door. He was trapped. He was scared. He let go of Timmy and said, "OK, OK, you win. I surrender. Don't call the police. Please." Timmy ran to the door. He opened it and saw Anna. She hugged him and said, "You're safe, Timmy. You're brave. I'm sorry I couldn't find you sooner. I love you." Timmy hugged her back and said, "I love you too, Anna. You're smart. You're strong. You saved me."
