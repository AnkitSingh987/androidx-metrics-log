Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mom and Her Baby and the Coat

Once upon a time there was a mom and her little baby. Mommy wanted to take the baby for a walk, so she put on a nice, warm coat and shoes and went outside. They walked until they reached a big open space where mommy could see the sky. The baby started to feel cold, so mommy said, "Let's sit here for a little while and I will serve you something nice and warm. Don't worry, it will feel cozy soon." Then mommy took out a warm cup of yummy hot cocoa and served it to the baby. The baby smiled and said, "Thank you mommy!" The baby enjoyed the cocoa and soon felt warm again. Mommy hugged baby and said, "I'm glad I could serve you something nice and warm to make you feel better." Then they started on their way again, walking and smiling in the sunshine.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Tim went to the park to discover new things. He was very alert and looked around to see what he could find. As Tim walked, he saw a big round thing on the ground. He picked it up and saw it was a football. "Wow! A football!" Tim said. He was so happy to find it. He wanted to play with his friends, so he called out, "Hey, friends! Come and play with me and my new football!" His friends came running and they were all very excited too. They played with the football all day long. They kicked it, threw it, and had so much fun. Tim and his friends were very alert, making sure no one fell or got hurt. At the end of the day, Tim and his friends were tired but happy. They had discovered a new game to play together with the football. Tim said, "Thank you, friends, for playing with me today. I had so much fun!" His friends agreed and they all went home, excited to play again soon.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a smelly pond. In the pond, there was a raft. On the raft, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was scared to swim, so he stayed on the raft. One day, a big frog named Fred came to the pond. Tom saw Fred and began to tremble. Fred saw Tom and said, "Why are you scared? I am just a frog." Tom replied, "I am scared to swim. I stay on the raft." Fred smiled and said, "I can help you learn to swim. It's fun!" Tom thought about it and decided to try. With Fred's help, Tom learned to swim. They became best friends. The smelly pond was not so bad after all. The village people were surprised to see Tom swim. They learned that even if something seems scary, it can be fun if you try. And that's how Tom and Fred taught everyone the value of facing their fears.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He was three years old. Every day he liked to read. He read in the morning, he read in the afternoon, and he read at night before bed. One day, Jack wanted to go on an adventure. He wanted to know what was east of his house. So he put on his shoes, packed his book, and started walking east. He walked and walked, until he came to an honest man's house. The man welcomed Jack and asked him why he was there. Jack said he was looking for adventure. So the honest man showed Jack around. He showed him trees, birds, and flowers. He showed Jack how to read the clouds in the sky. Jack was having a great time! The man gave Jack a big hug, and Jack waved goodbye as he began walking east again. He had found the adventure he wanted, and now he was going home. Jack read a lot on his way home, and he was very happy. He had learned many new things on his special adventure. He read even more once he was back home, and he never forgot the honest man in the east.
