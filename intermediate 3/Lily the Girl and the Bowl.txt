Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bowl

One day, a little girl named Lily went to her mom and made a request. She wanted to make a cake. Her mom said, "Okay, let's bake a cake together!" They went to the kitchen and started to get everything they needed. Lily's mom got a big pan and put it on the table. "This is the pan we will use to bake the cake," she said. Lily looked at the pan and smiled. She was excited to make a cake with her mom. They mixed all the cake stuff in a big bowl. It was hard work, but they had fun. When they were done, they put the cake mix into the pan. Lily's mom put the pan into the hot oven. They had to wait a little while for the cake to bake. Lily and her mom sat at the table and talked while they waited. "I love making cake with you, mom," Lily said. Her mom smiled and said, "I love it too, Lily. It's a special time for us." When the cake was done, they took it out of the oven and let it cool. Then they put frosting on it and shared a big piece. It was the best cake they ever made.

One day, a little girl named Lucy heard a loud noise in the kitchen. She felt dizzy from the noise. Lucy wanted to find out what was making the noise, so she walked into the kitchen. In the kitchen, Lucy saw her mom trying to fix the noisy thing. It was a big box with buttons. Lucy asked her mom, "What is that noise, mom?" Her mom said, "It's the big box, Lucy. It's broken, and I am trying to fix it." Lucy wanted to help her mom. She picked up a toy hammer and hit the big box. The noise stopped, and the big box was fixed. Lucy's mom was happy and said, "Thank you, Lucy! You helped me fix the noise!" Lucy smiled and felt proud that she could help her mom.

Sara likes to use her crayons. She has many colors, but her favorite is green. She likes to draw green things, like grass, trees, and frogs. One day, she sees a big sunflower in the garden. It is yellow and brown, with green leaves. Sara thinks it is very pretty. She asks her mom if she can use a sunflower for her drawing. Her mom says yes, and helps her cut one from the stem. Sara takes the sunflower and her crayons to the table. She puts the sunflower on a white paper. She looks at it carefully. She sees the yellow petals, the brown center, and the green leaves. She picks up her green crayon and starts to draw. She draws a big circle for the center, and many small lines for the petals. She draws a long line for the stem, and two small ovals for the leaves. She is very happy with her drawing. She shows it to her mom and says, "Look, mom, I drew a sunflower!" Her mom smiles and hugs her. She says, "You did a great job, Sara. Your sunflower is very beautiful. Do you want to hang it on the fridge?" Sara nods and says, "Yes, please!" Her mom helps her put a magnet on the back of the paper and hangs it on the fridge. Sara looks at her drawing and the sunflower. She feels proud and happy. She says, "Thank you, mom, for the sunflower. I love you!" Her mom says, "I love you too, Sara. You are my sunflower!"

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Buddy. Buddy was very stubborn. He did not like to listen to his mom. One day, Buddy found a lock on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and ran away. Buddy's mom called him, but he did not want to come back. He wanted to play with the lock. A little girl saw Buddy and wanted to help. She had a long rope. The little girl tied the rope to Buddy's collar and started to lead him back to his mom. Buddy did not want to go, but the little girl was very nice. She talked to him and made him feel happy. Buddy started to like the little girl. She led him back to his mom, and they all played together. Buddy learned that it was good to listen and not be so stubborn.
