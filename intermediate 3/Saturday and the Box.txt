Category: Intermediate
Title: Saturday and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to explore. Every Saturday morning she went on adventures around the neighborhood. This Saturday morning the little girl decided to take a different road. As the little girl made her way further down the road, it became enormous. She had never seen a road so big. Ahead of her, she saw a large box that had been dropped off on the side of the road. On the box it said, "Deliver to the tree". The little girl wanted to see what was inside, so she grabbed the box. Instantly, the box became too heavy for her to carry. She tried and tried, but she couldn't take it any further. The little girl was about to give up when she heard a voice whisper in the wind. "Do not forget, if you can deliver, you can accomplish your goal." The words gave the little girl hope. She looked up and saw that the road led straight to a tall tree. With all of her strength she carried the box as far as she could. When she reached the tree, she looked inside and to her surprise, she found an enormous, shiny red balloon. The little girl smiled and laughed with joy. She had taken on a challenge and delivered!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a red scooter that she loved to ride. One day, Lucy went to the park with her scooter. The park was empty. Lucy was happy because she could ride her scooter fast. Lucy started to push her scooter. She pushed it faster and faster. She saw a big hill and thought it would be fun to ride down. At the top of the hill, Lucy looked down and got a little scared. She knew she had to be brave. Lucy pushed her scooter down the hill. She went faster and faster. At the bottom of the hill, there was a big pile of leaves. Lucy went right into the leaves and laughed. She was so proud of herself for being brave. And that is the end of the story.

One day, a mighty dog named Zigzag went to the park to play. He saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her toy. Zigzag wanted to help her find it. Zigzag asked Lily, "Do you remember where you last saw your toy?" Lily thought hard and said, "I remember I was playing near the big tree." So, they went to the big tree to look for the toy. As they were looking, a big wind came and blew them away. They tried to hold on, but the wind was too strong. Zigzag and Lily were scared. The wind took them far away from the park, and they never found the toy. In the end, they were both very sad.

One day, a little bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a small pebble on the ground. The bird felt helpless, because the pebble was too big for it to move. A big dog saw the bird and came to help. The dog said, "Hi bird, do you need help?" The bird said, "Yes, please. Can you help me move this pebble?" The dog used its strong legs and pushed the pebble away. Then, something unexpected happened! The pebble turned into a beautiful butterfly. The butterfly said, "Thank you for helping me. I was stuck in that pebble and you set me free!" The bird, the dog, and the butterfly became best friends and played together every day.

Lila and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a hut in the park. The hut is made of wood and straw. It has a door and a window. "Look, a hut!" Lila says. She points to the hut. "Can we go inside?" "Maybe," Tom says. He is curious. He walks to the hut. He knocks on the door. "Hello, is anyone there?" No one answers. Tom opens the door. He sees a table, a chair, and a lamp. He also sees a funny hat on the table. The hat is big and red. It has feathers and bells. "Wow, a funny hat!" Tom says. He picks up the hat. He puts it on his head. He looks at Lila. "How do I look?" Lila laughs. She thinks Tom looks funny. She goes inside the hut. She sees a funny coat on the chair. The coat is long and green. It has buttons and pockets. "Wow, a funny coat!" Lila says. She puts on the coat. She looks at Tom. "How do I look?" Tom laughs. He thinks Lila looks funny. They look at each other. They giggle. They feel happy. They do not know that the hut belongs to a clown. The clown is in the park. He is making balloons for the children. He will come back to the hut soon. He will see Lila and Tom in his funny clothes. He will be surprised. He will be angry. Or maybe he will be amused. What do you think?
