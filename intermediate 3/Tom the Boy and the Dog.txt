Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to polish his toy car. He would make it shiny and clean. Tom's mom said, "Good job, Tom!" One day, Tom took his car outside. He saw a big landscape with trees and flowers. He played with his car in the grass. The car went fast up and down the hills. Tom saw a dog. The dog looked guilty. The dog had a dirty face. Tom said, "I can help!" He used his cloth to polish the dog's face. The dog was happy and clean. Tom and the dog played in the big landscape together. They had a fun day.

---

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. Spot saw a big tree and wanted to play with the sticks. He found a small stick and picked it up. Spot tried to snap the stick, but it was too hard. He tried again and again, but the stick did not snap. Spot felt embarrassed. Then, Spot saw a flame. The flame was small and warm. Spot wanted to touch the flame, but he knew it was hot. He watched the flame dance and wiggle. Spot liked the pretty colors of the flame. Spot's friend, a little bird named Blue, saw him and came to say hello. Blue saw the stick and helped Spot snap it. They played with the stick and watched the flame together. Spot and Blue were happy friends.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his toy cars. One day, while playing, he saw a big ambulance go by. Tim was very excited and decided to follow it. As Tim followed the ambulance, he saw his friend, Sue, who was upset. Tim asked her, "Sue, why are you upset?" Sue said, "I lost my toy bear and I can't find it." Tim wanted to help Sue, so they both stopped following the ambulance to look for the toy bear. As they were searching, they found the toy bear sitting on the back of the ambulance! They were both very surprised and happy. Sue got her toy bear back and Tim learned that sometimes, helping a friend is more important than following an ambulance.

---

Once upon a time, there was an ordinary house. Nothing too special happened there. But one day, something exciting did happen. A sign appeared on the door. It was a big and colourful sign, so everyone could see it. The sign said, "Welcome to the party". Everyone was very happy when they saw it! The people who lived in the house started to decorate the house for the party. They put up balloons and streamers. They even made a big cake. Everyone was excited for the party. It was a big success. Everyone had fun and enjoyed the delicious cakes. They all agreed that it was an ordinary day, until the sign appeared. It made the day so much more special.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a favorite toy, a stuffed bunny. The bunny was filthy because Lily took it everywhere with her. One day, Lily's mommy said, "Lily, your bunny is so dirty! We need to restore it and make it clean again." Lily looked at her bunny and said, "Okay, mommy." Lily and her mommy washed the bunny in warm, soapy water. They scrubbed the bunny gently until it was no longer filthy. Then, they hung it up to dry in the sun. When the bunny was dry, Lily hugged it tight and said, "Thank you, mommy, for helping me restore my bunny. Now it is clean and soft again." From that day on, Lily took better care of her bunny. She made sure it stayed clean and loved it even more.

---

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to swing and slide and run. But Lily saw a big hill and wanted to climb it. She said to Ben, "Come with me, let's go up the hill." Ben was scared. He said, "No, Lily, it's too high. We might fall and hurt ourselves. Let's stay here and play." Lily was angry. She said, "You are a baby, Ben. I'm not afraid. I'm going to climb the hill by myself." She ran away from Ben and started to climb the hill. Ben felt lonely. He watched Lily go up and up. He wanted to be with her, but he was too scared. He wished she would come back and play with him. Lily reached the top of the hill. She felt proud and happy. She looked around and saw the park and the trees and the sky. She shouted, "Look at me, Ben, I'm on the hill!" But Ben did not hear her. He was busy playing with a new friend. He met a boy named Sam, who liked to swing and slide and run. They were having fun together. Lily felt lonely. She wanted to share her joy with Ben, but he was not there. She wished he would look at her and smile. She started to climb down the hill. But the hill was steep and slippery. Lily lost her balance and fell. She rolled and rolled and bumped and scraped. She cried out, "Help, help, I'm falling!" Ben heard her. He looked up and saw Lily falling. He was scared and sorry. He ran to help her. He said, "Lily, Lily, are you okay?" Lily stopped falling. She was hurt and sad. She said, "Ben, Ben, I'm sorry. I was mean to you. I should have played with you." Ben hugged her. He said, "It's okay, Lily. I'm sorry too. I should have gone with you. I'm glad you're not hurt too much." They said sorry and hugged and made up. They went back to the park and played with Sam. They learned to be brave and kind and share. They were not lonely anymore. They were happy.
