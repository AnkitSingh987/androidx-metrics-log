Title: Andrew and David and the Woods

Once upon a time, there were two boys, Andrew and David. Andrew was march-ing through the woods. Suddenly, he felt very hot, so he stopped and sat down. David saw Andrew, so he march-ed over to him. "Hi Andrew! Why are you so hot and tired?" asked David. Andrew said, "I was in such a hurry to get here that I didn't slow down. Now I'm so sleepy!" David said, "If you keep going too fast, you won't get far in the end. Remember, it's better to take your time than to rush!" Andrew smiled and thanked David. The two boys started march-ing again, this time at a much slower pace. They made it to the end of their journey without getting tired. The moral value of this story is that it´s important to take your time and don't rush things, or else you could get tired very quickly.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a small bush with red berries. Lily was happy and asked her mom, "Can I pick some berries?" Her mom said, "Yes, but only a few." Lily picked some berries and saw a small hole near the bush. She thought it would be fun to bury the berries in the hole. So, she put the berries in the hole and covered them with dirt. She told her mom, "I buried the berries in the ground." A few days later, Lily and her mom went back to the park. They saw that the hole where Lily buried the berries had a new small bush with more red berries. Lily was very happy and her mom said, "You helped the bush grow by burying the berries. Now there are more berries for everyone to enjoy!" The moral of the story is that when we share and help others, we can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was an ordinary girl who loved to play with her friends. One day, while playing in the park, Lily found a dirty cloth on the ground. She picked it up and thought about what to do with it. Lily decided to remove the dirt from the cloth. She took it home, washed it with soap and water, and hung it up to dry. When the cloth was clean and dry, she saw that it was very pretty. It had bright colors and a beautiful pattern. Lily wanted to share the cloth with her friends, so she took it back to the park. She showed the cloth to her friends, and they all loved it. They took turns wearing it as a cape, a hat, or a scarf. They played with the cloth all day long. The moral of the story is that even ordinary things can become special when we take care of them and share them with others.

Lily and Anna were best friends. They liked to play with dolls and dress up. One day, they found a red bracelet in the park. It was shiny and pretty. "Wow, look at this!" Lily said. "Can I have it?" "No, I saw it first!" Anna said. "It's mine!" They both wanted the red bracelet. They started to pull and tug on it. They did not want to share. "Stop it, you're hurting me!" Lily cried. "Let go, it's not fair!" Anna yelled. They were so busy fighting that they did not see the big dog. The dog was hungry and curious. He saw the red bracelet and thought it was a toy. He ran towards Lily and Anna and snatched the bracelet from their hands. He ran away with it. "Hey, come back!" Lily and Anna shouted. "That's ours!" They chased the dog, but he was too fast. They lost him and the red bracelet. They felt sad and angry. They looked at each other and realized how silly they had been. They had lost their friendship over a red bracelet. They felt sorry and hugged each other. "I'm sorry, Lily. I should have let you have it." Anna said. "I'm sorry, Anna. I should have shared it with you." Lily said. They smiled and forgave each other. They decided to play with their dolls and dress up again. They did not need the red bracelet to be happy. They had each other.
