Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cave

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to race with his friends. They would race in the park, in the yard, and even in the house. Tim was fast and always won the races. One day, Tim and his friends found a cave in the woods. The cave was big and dark. They decided to race to the cave and back. Tim was excited and ready to win another race. When the race started, Tim ran as fast as he could. His friends tried to catch up, but Tim was too fast. Tim reached the cave first and then ran back to the start. He won the race and all his friends clapped. They all had fun and played in the woods until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an obedient boy who always did what his mom and dad said. One day, Tim's mom asked him to get a plate from the cupboard. As Tim opened the cupboard, he found a small, talking bird inside! The bird said, "Hello, Tim! I recommend you take the blue plate today." Tim was very surprised but listened to the bird. Tim took the blue plate to his mom. When she saw it, she smiled and said, "Oh, Tim, this was my favorite plate when I was a little girl! How did you know?" Tim just smiled and thought about his new friend, the talking bird in the cupboard.

Once upon a time, there was a giant. The giant was very popular. All the little kids liked to play with him. They would climb on his back and laugh together. The giant was always happy to play with them. One day, the kids wanted to unite and make a big surprise for the giant. They made a giant cake for him. They put candles on it and waited for the giant to come. When the giant saw the cake, he was so happy! He blew out the candles and they all ate the cake together. But then, something unexpected happened. When the giant ate the cake, he became very small! He was now the same size as the kids. They were all surprised, but they still played together. The giant liked being small and the kids liked having a new friend their size. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to collect rocks. One day, he went to the park to find more rocks for his collection. At the park, there was a big swing that Tim liked to play on. As Tim was looking for rocks, he met a girl named Lily. Lily said, "I like to collect rocks too! Can we look for rocks together?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, let's collect rocks together!" So, they started to look for rocks near the swing. Suddenly, they saw a big, dead tree next to the swing. They heard a noise coming from the tree. Tim and Lily were scared, but they went closer to the tree. To their surprise, they found a secret door in the tree! Inside the door, there were many colorful rocks. Tim and Lily were very happy and collected all the rocks to share with their friends. The dead tree was a secret rock treasure, and they had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tom. Tom had a big itch on his back. He wanted to scratch it, but his legs were too short. Tom was angry because he could not reach the itch. One day, Tom went to the park to play. He saw a big tree and had an idea. He wanted the tree to help him with his itch. Tom ran and rubbed his back on the tree. The itch went away, and Tom was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. When Tom looked at the tree, he saw a big cat stuck in the branches. The cat was scared and wanted to get down. Tom was no longer angry about his itch. He wanted to help the cat. Tom barked loud, and a nice man came to help. The man got the cat down from the tree. Tom and the cat became friends, and they played in the park together every day.
