Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Play

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the theater with her mom. They were going to see a play. Lily was very excited. She had been patient and waited for this day for a long time. Before the play started, Lily's mom said, "Lily, if you need help, just ask. And remember to be patient during the play." Lily nodded and smiled. She was ready to watch the play. As the play went on, Lily saw a boy on stage who needed help. He was trying to lift a big box, but it was too heavy. Lily remembered what her mom said about helping. She stood up and said, "I can help you!" The boy smiled, and together they lifted the box. Everyone in the theater clapped for Lily and the boy. Lily's mom was proud of her for being patient and helpful.

Lily and Lola are twins. They like to collect things. They have a big box of things they find. They have shells, rocks, feathers, buttons, and stickers. One day, they go to the park with Mom. They see a pond with ducks. They want to collect duck feathers. They run to the pond and look for feathers. "Look, Lily, I found a big feather!" Lola says. She picks up a white feather and puts it in her pocket. "Good job, Lola!" Lily says. She finds a brown feather and puts it in her pocket too. They keep looking for feathers. They see a duck with a shiny green feather on its head. They want that feather too. "Let's get that feather, Lily!" Lola says. She tries to get closer to the duck. "Be careful, Lola!" Lily says. She follows Lola. But the duck does not like them. It quacks loudly and flaps its wings. It splashes water at them. Lily and Lola get wet and scared. "Mom, help!" they cry. Mom comes and picks them up. She takes them away from the pond. "It's okay, girls. The duck was just protecting its feathers. You can't take them. They belong to the duck." Mom says. Lily and Lola feel sorry. They did not know that. They say sorry to the duck. "Sorry, duck. We won't take your feathers." they say. The duck calms down. It nods its head. Lily and Lola hug Mom. They are happy. They still have their own feathers. They go home and put them in their box. They are successful collectors. They have many things. They love to collect things. They love each other. They are happy twins.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue wanted to go on a journey with her friends. They wanted to see the big park far away from their home. Sue was very excited to go on this journey. On the way to the park, Sue tripped and fell down. She started to cry because her knee hurt. Her friends stopped and looked at her. Sue felt embarrassed because she had fallen in front of her friends. Sue's friends were very kind. They helped her stand up and made her feel better. They told her it was okay to cry and that everyone falls sometimes. Sue smiled and thanked her friends. They continued their journey to the big park, and Sue was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a slow turtle named Tim. Tim loved nature and liked to store food for his friends. One day, he went to the big tree to find nuts and fruits. While Tim was picking up nuts and fruits, he saw a big bird. The big bird was sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, big bird?" The big bird said, "I lost my baby. Can you help me find my baby?" Tim wanted to help, so they looked together. They looked and looked, but they could not find the baby bird. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim found the baby bird in his shell! The baby bird was hiding there all along. The big bird was happy and thanked Tim. Tim, the big bird, and the baby bird became best friends and played together in nature every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big war. It was dangerous and made people very sad. They needed help to feel happy again. A kind man named Tom wanted to help the town. He found a book that he could read to the people. He thought the book could make them happy and forget about the dangerous war for a little while. Tom read the book to the people every day. They started to feel better and smile more. The war ended, and the town was safe again. The people were thankful for Tom and the happy moments he gave them.
