Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lady and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a lady. She had many fragile things in her house. One day, she wanted to sort all her things. The lady called her friend, the cat. "Cat, can you help me sort my fragile things?" she asked. The cat said, "Yes, I will help you, lady." Together, they sorted all the fragile things. They were very careful. The lady was happy, and the cat was happy too. They had fun sorting fragile things. And they lived happily ever after.

---

Once upon a time, there was a big maze. In the maze, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a friend named Tom. Tom was a small cat. They liked to play in the maze. One day, Lucy and Tom got lost in the maze. Lucy said, "Tom, we have to find a clear way out." Tom meowed and nodded. They walked and walked, trying to find the way out. Finally, they saw a clear path. Lucy said, "Look, Tom, we found it!" Tom meowed happily. They walked out of the maze and went home. Lucy and Tom were very happy to be out of the maze. They played and had fun together every day.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a pen that he loved very much. One day, Tim felt very miserable because he could not find his pen. He looked everywhere for it, but it was not there. Tim saw a little bug on the floor. The bug seemed to know where the pen was. Tim thought, "Maybe if I follow the bug, it will show me where my pen is." So Tim followed the bug. The bug went under the table, behind the chair, and through the door. The bug led Tim to a big, dark hole. Tim was scared, but he wanted his pen back. He went into the hole, but he could not see anything. Tim was very sad and he could not find his pen. He tried to go back, but he could not find the way out. Tim was lost and never found his pen.

---

One day, a gentle cat named Lily found some shiny jewelry. She saw it on the floor in her house. Lily liked how it sparkled and wanted to play with it. So, she picked it up with her mouth and carried it to her favorite spot. Lily played with the jewelry for a long time. But soon, it got dirty from her paws. Lily wanted to make it shiny again. So, she used her soft tail to wipe the jewelry clean. She was very careful not to hurt it. After wiping the jewelry, it was shiny again. Lily was happy and proud of her work. She showed her clean jewelry to her friends. They all liked it and played with it together. And from that day on, Lily took care of her shiny jewelry and always kept it clean.

One day, Tom and his mom went to a big place with many people. They needed to get a passport. A passport is a book that lets you go to other places far away. Tom was very excited. Tom's mom talked to a nice lady behind a desk. The lady said, "Please attach your photo to this paper." Tom's mom put a picture of Tom on the paper. Then, Tom saw a man with a big red bag. The man looked funny, and the bag smelled spicy. Tom went to see the man. The man opened the bag and said, "Surprise! I have spicy chips for everyone." Tom was so happy. He took the chips and shared them with his mom and the nice lady. They all laughed and enjoyed the spicy chips while they finished getting Tom's passport.

Lily and Ben are playing in the kitchen. They see a big pot on the stove. They want to make some noise. "Let's clap the pot!" Lily says. "OK!" Ben says. They take the pot and clap it hard. It makes a loud bang. They clap it again and again. They laugh and have fun. But Mom is in the living room. She hears the noise. She is alert. She does not like the noise. "What are you doing?" Mom asks. She comes to the kitchen. She sees Lily and Ben with the pot. "Stop that!" Mom says. "You are making too much noise. You will break the pot. And you will hurt your hands." Lily and Ben stop clapping. They look at Mom. They are sorry. "Sorry, Mom," Lily says. "Sorry, Mom," Ben says. Mom takes the pot and puts it back on the stove. She hugs Lily and Ben. "It's OK," Mom says. "But next time, please play with something else. How about some blocks or books?" Lily and Ben nod. They go to their room. They find some blocks and books. They play quietly. They are happy.
