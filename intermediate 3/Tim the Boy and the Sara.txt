Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sara

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. It was very icy and cold, but he wanted to have fun. He saw his friend, Sara, who was trying to carry a big box of stuff. "Can you help me, Tim?" Sara asked. "This stuff is heavy." Tim wanted to help, but he didn't like the icy cold. "I don't like the cold," Tim said. "Can we play inside?" Sara was sad, but she had to accept his answer. So, they went inside to play. They had a lot of fun, but Sara still needed help with her stuff. Tim saw that Sara was still sad, so he decided to be brave. "Okay, Sara," Tim said. "I will help you with your stuff, even if it's icy cold outside." Sara was very happy, and they both went outside to carry the stuff. They were good friends and helped each other, even when it was not easy.

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with his friend, Sam. Sam was a boy who loved to play all day. One day, Sam found a big bottle with a label on it. The label had a picture of a sun. Sam wanted to see what was inside the bottle. He asked Tom to help him. Tom was very smart and knew how to open the bottle. Tom and Sam poured the bottle into a big bowl. Out came a lot of yellow paint! They were so happy to see the bright color. They painted the whole house with the yellow paint. Now, their gray house was as bright as the sun on the label. They laughed and played in their sunny house all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big swamp. In the swamp, there lived a frog named Fred. Fred was a very comfortable frog. He liked to sit on his big green leaf and watch the fish swim by. One day, Fred saw a little bird named Betty. Betty was flying very fast, but she looked tired. Fred said, "Hi Betty, why don't you come and sit with me for a while?" Betty said, "I can't, I need to get home, but I can't find my way." Fred wanted to help Betty, so he said, "I know a way to get home, but we have to delay a little. We can go slow and rest when you are tired." Betty agreed, and they started their journey together. As they went through the swamp, they saw many pretty things like big flowers and little bugs. They took their time and rested when they needed to. At last, they reached Betty's home. Betty was so happy that she hugged Fred and said, "Thank you, Fred! I couldn't have done it without you!" From that day on, Fred and Betty became the best of friends. They would always visit each other and play in the comfortable swamp. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a wide, big rabbit named Tom had a plan. He wanted to find and eat the best celery in the world. He told his friend, a small bird named Sue, about his plan. Sue liked the plan and wanted to help Tom find the best celery. Tom and Sue went on a long walk to find the best celery. They looked in the garden, the park, and even near the pond. They found many good celery, but not the best one. Sue told Tom to not give up, the best celery is out there. Finally, they found a big, green celery hidden behind a bush. It was the best celery they had ever seen! Tom and Sue were so happy that they danced and shared the celery together. Their plan had worked, and they found the best celery in the world.

Tom and Anna liked to play in the old barn. They liked to look at the animals and the hay and the tools. But they did not like the cobwebs. The cobwebs were sticky and dusty and scary. They made Tom and Anna sneeze and itch. One day, Tom and Anna found a big cobweb in the corner of the barn. It was very big and very dusty. Tom wanted to touch it, but Anna said no. She said it was yucky and they should leave it alone. But Tom did not listen. He reached out his hand and touched the cobweb. The cobweb was very sticky. It stuck to Tom's hand and his arm and his hair. Tom tried to pull it off, but it only got worse. He got more and more cobweb on him. He started to cry. He looked like a ghost. He was very pale and very sad. Anna saw Tom crying and felt sorry for him. She knew he was only curious and did not mean to make a mess. She went to him and helped him get the cobweb off. She used a stick and a cloth and some water. She was very gentle and kind. She said it was okay and she forgave him. Tom was very happy and grateful. He hugged Anna and said thank you. He said he was sorry and he would not touch the cobwebs again. Anna hugged him back and said it was fine. They smiled and laughed and went to play with the animals. They were very good friends. They liked the barn, but they did not like the cobwebs.
