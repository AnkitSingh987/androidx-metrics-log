Category: Intermediate
Title: Ducky the Duck and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky lived in a pond with his parent. They had a happy life, swimming and playing in the water. One day, it was very wet outside. The rain was falling, and the pond was full. Ducky's parent told him, "Ducky, you should stay near me today. The water is very high, and I want to keep you safe." But Ducky wanted to play and swim far away. He did not listen to his parent and went far from the pond. As Ducky swam far away, he got lost. He was scared and alone. Ducky did not know what to do. Then, he remembered what his parent said. Ducky tried to find his way back to the pond. Finally, he found his parent, who was very happy to see him. Ducky's parent provided a warm hug and said, "Ducky, always listen to me. I want to help you and keep you safe." Ducky learned his lesson. From that day on, he always listened to his parent, and they lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parent, as they want to keep you safe and happy.

One day, a little pupil named Tim went to the park. He loved to slide on the big, shiny slide. It was his most important thing to do every day. Tim's friends would play with him and they all had fun sliding together. One sunny day, Tim and his friends went to the park to slide. But when they got there, the slide was gone! They looked all around the park, but they could not find it. Tim and his friends were very sad. They didn't know what to do without the slide. Then, something unexpected happened. A big truck came into the park with a new slide. It was even bigger and shinier than the old one! Tim and his friends were so happy. They played on the new slide all day long, and it became their new important thing to do every day.

One day, a girl named Lily found a pink coat. She loved it so much. Lily wanted to give the coat to her best friend, Sue. Sue was so happy when she saw the coat. They played together all day wearing the coat. The next day, it was very cold. Lily and Sue went to the park to play. They saw a dog with no home. The dog was cold too. Lily and Sue wanted to help the dog. They thought hard and had an idea. Lily and Sue put the pink coat on the dog to keep it warm. The dog was happy and warm. But then, a big wind came. It blew the pink coat away! The dog was cold again. Lily, Sue, and the dog were all very sad. The pink coat was gone forever.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a jog in the cold park. He wore a big coat to keep warm. He saw his friend, Sue, sitting on a bench. Sue looked sad. "Hi Sue, why are you sad?" Tim asked. Sue said, "I can't think of a poem for school." Tim thought for a moment. "Let's jog! It might help you think of a poem." Sue agreed and they started to jog together. After a while, Sue stopped and smiled. "I have an idea for my poem! Thank you, Tim!" They were both happy and continued jogging in the cold park.

Once upon a time, there was a pig. The pig lived on a farm. The farm was old. The pig was very happy on the farm. He liked to play all day. One day, the pig wanted to finish a puzzle. The puzzle was big and fun. The pig worked very hard. He wanted to see the whole picture. The sun was going down, but the pig did not stop. At last, the pig finished the puzzle. But it was very late. The pig was very tired. He went to sleep. When the pig woke up, the puzzle was gone. The wind blew it away. The pig was sad. The end.
