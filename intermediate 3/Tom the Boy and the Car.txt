Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Car

One day, a boy named Tom wanted to go on a trip. He had a modern red bag to pack his things. Tom thought it was a good idea to ask his mom what to pack. "Mom, what should I pack in my red bag?" Tom asked. His mom said, "Pack your clothes, a toy, and a snack." Tom was happy with his mom's idea. Tom packed his clothes, a toy car, and a yummy apple. He was ready to go on his trip. Tom and his mom went to the park and had lots of fun. They were glad they packed everything they needed.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom loved to play the trumpet. Every day, he would go to the park and play his trumpet for all the kids. One day, while Tom was playing his trumpet, he saw a small flower. It was trying to bloom, but it was having a hard time. Tom wanted to help the flower, so he played a soft and happy tune on his trumpet. As Tom played his trumpet, the little flower began to bloom. It grew bigger and bigger until it was a big, beautiful flower. The kids in the park clapped and cheered for Tom and the flower. And from that day on, Tom and the flower were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cool cat and a big dog. They both wanted to win a race. The race was to see who was the fastest. The cool cat and the big dog were very excited to start the race. The race began and the cool cat ran very fast. The big dog tried to catch up, but the cool cat was too fast. The big dog got very tired and wanted to stop, but he did not want to surrender. He kept running even though he was tired. In the end, the cool cat won the race. The big dog was sad because he did not win. He was so tired that he fell down and got hurt. The moral of the story is to know when to surrender and not push yourself too hard.

Once upon a time there was a brave little girl called Mia. Every day Mia went for a walk in the park. On this particular day she saw something that made her shout with joy. It was a big patch of tiny green mint leaves growing in the grass. Mia ran up to the patch of mint and started to pick the little leaves. She was so happy she shouted and shouted with excitement. But then, from the bushes came a big tough mouse. It squeaked at Mia and jumped up and down. Mia was afraid of the mouse, but she also wanted to keep picking the mint leaves. So she shouted at the mouse and said, “Go away!” But the mouse kept jumping around. Mia shouted again and again until finally the mouse scurried away. Then Mia was able to pick the mint leaves without being disturbed. Mia was so delighted, she shouted with joy one last time and skipped off with her mint leaves.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. He had a loyal dog named Spot. They liked to play outside every day. They would run, jump, and have so much fun together. Tim and Spot were best friends. One day, Tim and Spot went to the park. They saw a big tree with many leaves. Tim wanted to climb the tree. Spot watched him from the ground. When Tim was high up, he felt a fall. He was scared, but Spot was there to help him. Spot barked and ran to Tim's side. That night, Tim had a dream. In his dream, he and Spot were flying in the sky. They flew over the park, the big tree, and their house. Tim felt so happy with his loyal friend. When he woke up, he hugged Spot and they went to play outside again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a sweet rabbit named Tim. Tim liked to run and play all day. One day, Tim saw a track in the ground. He was very curious about where the track would go. Tim followed the track and met a big bear. The bear said, "Hello, little rabbit. Do you want to see what I have?" Tim said, "Yes, please." The bear revealed a big, shiny box. Tim was very excited to see what was inside the box. But when the bear opened the box, there was nothing inside. Tim was sad and the bear laughed at him. Tim learned that sometimes, things are not as good as they seem. The moral of the story is to be careful when following unknown tracks and not to trust strangers easily.
