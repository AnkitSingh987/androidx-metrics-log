Category: Intermediate
Title: Jenny and Ben and the Hill

Jenny and Ben were adventurous twins who liked to explore the park. They ran and jumped and played on the swings and slides. They saw a big hill with green grass and flowers. They wanted to climb it and see what was on the other side. "Let's go, Ben!" Jenny shouted. "Maybe we can find a treasure or a castle or a dragon!" "OK, Jenny!" Ben shouted back. "But we have to be careful. Mom said not to go too far from the playground." They ran up the hill, holding hands and laughing. They felt the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They reached the top and looked around. They saw a pond with ducks and a bridge. They also saw a sign that said "Keep Out". "What's that, Jenny?" Ben asked, pointing at the sign. "I don't know, Ben. Maybe it's a secret place. Let's go and see!" Jenny said, feeling curious. "But Jenny, the sign says 'Keep Out'. That means we are not allowed to go there. Maybe it's dangerous or scary or mean." Ben said, feeling scared. "Come on, Ben. Don't be a baby. We are adventurous, remember? Maybe it's a fun place. Maybe it's where the treasure or the castle or the dragon is. Let's go and see!" Jenny said, pulling Ben's hand. They crossed the bridge and walked towards the sign. They did not see the man who was watching them from behind a tree. He had a big hat and a big beard and a big dog. He did not like children who did not follow the rules. He waited for them to come closer. He was ready to shout at them and chase them away. He did not know that Jenny and Ben were adventurous. He did not know that they had a surprise for him.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play with clay. They had many colors of clay and they made different shapes with them. They liked to feel the texture of the clay, how it was soft and smooth in their hands. One day, they decided to make a famous person with clay. Lily wanted to make a princess with a long dress and a crown. Ben wanted to make a superhero with a cape and a mask. They each took some clay and started to work on their person. But soon, they ran out of some colors. Lily needed more pink for her dress and Ben needed more blue for his cape. They looked at each other's clay and saw that they had the colors they needed. Lily reached for Ben's blue clay and Ben reached for Lily's pink clay. "Hey, that's mine!" Ben said, pulling his clay away. "No, that's mine!" Lily said, pulling her clay away. They both frowned and started to argue. They did not want to share their clay. They wanted to make their person the way they wanted. Their mom heard them and came to see what was wrong. She saw them fighting over the clay and shaking their heads. "What are you doing, Lily and Ben?" she asked. "Why are you not sharing your clay?" "Because I want to make a superhero and she wants to make a princess!" Ben said. "Because I want to make a princess and he wants to make a superhero!" Lily said. Mom smiled and said, "You know, you can both make a famous person with clay. You don't have to make the same person. You can make different people and still share your clay. You can even make a person who is both a princess and a superhero, if you want. That would be very cool and creative." Lily and Ben looked at each other and thought about what mom said. They realized that mom was right. They did not have to fight over the clay. They could share it and make different people. They could even make a person who was both a princess and a superhero, if they wanted. That would be very cool and creative. They smiled and said, "Sorry, mom. Sorry, Lily. Sorry, Ben." They hugged and said, "Let's share our clay and make famous people together." They took some clay and started to work on their person. They used different colors and shapes and made a person who was both a princess and a superhero. They gave her a pink dress and a blue cape, a crown and a mask, and a big smile. They were very proud of their person and showed it to mom. Mom was very happy and said, "Wow, that's a wonderful person you made. She is both a princess and a superhero. She is very famous and very kind. You are very smart and very nice. You learned how to share and how to be creative. I'm very proud of you, Lily and Ben." Lily and Ben were very happy and said, "Thank you, mom. We love you." They hugged mom and each other and played with their clay. They learned that sharing is caring and that being creative is fun. They had a good day.
