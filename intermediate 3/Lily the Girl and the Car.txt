Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a sweet smile and liked to help others. One sunny day, Lily went to the park with her mommy. She carried her favorite bag with her. Inside the bag, she had toys and snacks. At the park, Lily saw a boy named Timmy. Timmy was sad because he lost his toy car. Lily wanted to help Timmy feel better. She opened her bag and took out a toy car. "Here, you can have my car," Lily said. Timmy's face lit up with a big smile. Then, Lily's mommy called her. "Lily, it's time to go home!" she said. Lily waved goodbye to Timmy and started to walk away. Timmy called out, "Thank you for the car, Lily!" Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome! Pass it to someone else who needs it one day!" And with that, Lily and her mommy went home, happy that they made a new friend.

One day, a lively little pupil named Tim went to school. He loved to play and learn with his friends. Tim was a happy boy who always had a big smile on his face. In class, the teacher said, "Today, we will draw pictures of our favorite animals." Tim insisted, "I want to draw a big, strong lion!" His friend, Jane, wanted to draw a pretty butterfly. They laughed and started to draw their pictures. As they were drawing, something unexpected happened. A real lion came into the classroom! The lion was friendly and just wanted to play. Tim said, "Wow, my drawing came to life!" The lion became their new friend, and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy cross that he loved very much. One day, Tim felt sad and miserable because he had no friends to play with. Tim thought, "Maybe if I sell my toy cross, I can buy a new toy and make friends." So, Tim went to the market with his toy cross to sell it. At the market, he met a girl named Lily. Lily saw the cross and said, "I like your cross, can I buy it?" Tim said, "Yes, but I am selling it to buy a new toy and make friends." Lily smiled and said, "You don't need to sell your cross. We can be friends and play together." Tim felt happy and they played together all day. Tim and Lily became best friends, and Tim didn't need to sell his cross after all. They had lots of fun and were never miserable again.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He wanted to go to the park and play. He put on his shoes and ran outside. In the park, he saw a big zigzag slide. Tim loved slides, so he ran to it as fast as he could. At the slide, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was scared to go down the zigzag slide. Tim said, "Don't be scared, I will help you!" Sue smiled and said, "Okay, let's try together." They both climbed up the slide and got ready to go down. Tim told Sue, "When I say 'go,' we will push off and slide down." Sue nodded and held Tim's hand. Tim counted, "One, two, three, go!" They both pushed off and went down the zigzag slide. They laughed and had so much fun. After that, Tim and Sue played together all day at the park.

Tim and Mia are friends. They like to play with blocks and crayons. One day, they find a big box in the attic. Inside the box, there are many shiny things. Tim and Mia are very happy. They want to see what the shiny things are. They take out some shiny things from the box. They see a star, a heart, a moon, and a sun. They are all symbols. Tim and Mia know what symbols are. They learned them in school. They put the symbols on the floor and make a picture. "Look, Mia, I made a night sky with the star and the moon," Tim says. He is enthusiastic. He likes the night sky. "That's nice, Tim, but I like the day sky better," Mia says. She is also enthusiastic. She likes the day sky. "Look, I made a day sky with the sun and the heart." Tim and Mia look at each other's pictures. They are both very good. They smile and hug. They are happy with their shiny symbols. They decide to share them and make a bigger picture. They use the star, the heart, the moon, and the sun to make a big circle. They say it is a symbol of their friendship. They are very proud of their picture. They show it to their mom and dad. Their mom and dad are also proud of them. They say they are very clever and creative. They give them a kiss and a cookie. Tim and Mia are very happy. They love their shiny symbols and their friendship.
