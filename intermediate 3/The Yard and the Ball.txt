Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yard and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big yard. In the yard, there was a pretty, red ball. The ball was very attractive. All the kids loved to play with it. One day, a boy named Tim came to play. He saw the pretty ball and wanted to take it. But he did not ask. He just took the ball. He tried to steal it. A girl named Sue saw Tim take the ball. She said, "No, Tim! That is not nice! You can't steal the pretty ball!" Tim felt sad. He knew he did something wrong. Tim gave the ball back to Sue. They both played with the ball in the yard. They had a lot of fun. From that day on, Tim learned not to steal and to always ask before taking things.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He was three years old and was very obedient toward his mommy. One day, he was playing in the backyard when he suddenly spotted a big animal. It was a big, gray bear! Tom was so scared he started to panic. He started running, but the bear followed him. Tom shouted to his mommy that the bear was chasing him. His mommy quickly ran to his side, scaring away the bear. She hugged Tom and said, "Don't be scared. Everything is okay now, I am here." Tom was relieved to be safe again and was thankful that his mommy was there to protect him. Tom nodded and said, "Yes, Mommy. I'll be more obedient from now on."

Once upon a time, in a land full of sunshine, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day in the warm sun. One day, a little bird named Betty flew by and saw Tom. "Tom, come play with me!" Betty said. Tom looked at Betty and said, "I am too lazy to play. I want to sleep in the sunshine." Betty wanted to urge Tom to play, so she thought of a fun game. "Tom, let's play catch! I will fly, and you can try to catch me!" Tom liked the idea and got up from his nap. They played catch for a while, and Tom started to feel less lazy. He liked playing with Betty in the sunshine. They laughed and had fun together. From that day on, Tom and Betty played together every day. Tom learned that playing with friends was more fun than being lazy in the sunshine.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly goat named Mr. Goat. He lived on a small farm with his friends. Mr. Goat was very kind and loved to help others. One day, a new animal came to the farm. It was a little lamb named Lily. Lily was shy and didn't know anyone. Mr. Goat wanted to help her make friends, so he decided to introduce her to the other animals. First, they went to see the cow. "Hello, Cow," said Mr. Goat. "This is Lily the lamb. She is new here." Cow smiled and said, "Nice to meet you, Lily!" Then, they went to see the pig. "Hi, Pig," said Mr. Goat. "Meet our new friend, Lily." Pig was happy to meet her too. Lily felt much better after meeting her new friends. She was no longer shy and played with the other animals every day. Mr. Goat was glad he could help Lily, and they all lived happily on the farm.

One day, a kind pirate named Sam went to the sea. He had a big ship and a funny hat. Sam liked to find treasure and make new friends. He did not like to scare people, but sometimes he did by mistake. One sunny day, Sam saw a little island. He thought there might be treasure there. He took his ship to the island and started to look around. Sam saw a big tree and started to dig under it. He found a big box and was very happy. But when Sam opened the box, he did not find treasure. Instead, he found a little bunny! The bunny was scared at first, but Sam was kind to it. They became best friends and went on many adventures together. The bunny even got a little pirate hat too. And from that day, Sam and the bunny were known as the kindest pirate friends on the sea.
