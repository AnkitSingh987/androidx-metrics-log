Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Farmer

A brown farmer was walking across the field one day. Suddenly, something caught his eye. He stopped and looked around and saw a little crow, who was shivering and shaking all over. The farmer was filled with pity and walked over to the little bird. "What's wrong, little crow?" he asked softly. The crow told the farmer that he had been playing around and had accidentally broken the farmer's window. He felt so bad that he had done it and he was so scared that the farmer would be mad at him. The farmer heard the little crow's story and he felt so sorry for him. He knew that the crow didn't mean to do it and he wanted to forgive the little bird. So the farmer smiled and said, "I forgive you, little crow." The crow felt so relieved and so grateful to the farmer for understanding and forgiving him. He thanked the farmer and flew away. From that day forward, the farmer always remembered the little crow and the incredible power of forgiveness.

Anna was a daughter who liked to smile. She had long hair that was brown and soft. She liked to play with her dolls and her dog. She was happy most of the time. One day, Anna's mom said, "Anna, we are going to visit grandma today. Do you want to come with me?" Anna nodded and smiled. She loved her grandma. She always gave her hugs and cookies. She also had a big garden with flowers and birds. Anna and her mom got in the car and drove to grandma's house. It was a long drive, but Anna did not mind. She looked out the window and saw the trees and the clouds. She sang songs with her mom and played with her dog. When they arrived, grandma was waiting for them at the door. She smiled and said, "Hello, Anna and mom. I'm so happy to see you. Come in, come in." She hugged them and kissed them. She took them to the kitchen and gave them cookies and milk. She asked them how they were and what they did. Anna told her grandma about her dolls and her dog. She showed her grandma her long hair and how she could braid it. She asked her grandma about her flowers and her birds. She listened to her grandma's stories and laughed. She felt warm and cozy. Anna and her mom stayed with grandma for a while. They had fun and talked a lot. They also helped grandma with some chores. They watered the flowers and fed the birds. They cleaned the dishes and made the beds. They thanked grandma for everything. When it was time to go, Anna hugged her grandma and said, "I love you, grandma. You are the best. Can I come again soon?" Grandma smiled and said, "I love you too, Anna. You are a sweet daughter. You can come anytime you want." She gave her a gift and waved goodbye. Anna and her mom got in the car and drove back home. Anna was tired but happy. She had a good day. She smiled and said, "Mom, I like grandma. She is nice and kind. Can we visit her again soon?" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, Anna. We can visit her again soon. I like grandma too. She is my mom and your grandma. We are a family." Anna nodded and smiled. She felt loved and lucky. She closed her eyes and fell asleep.

One day, a little horse named Sam lived in a stable. He loved to play with his friends. They would run, jump, and have fun together. Sam was a happy horse. One day, a new horse named Max came to the stable. Max was rude. He didn't want to play with Sam and his friends. Max said, "I don't want to play with you. I am better than you." Sam felt sad. He wanted Max to be his friend. So, Sam thought of a plan. He invited Max to play a game with him and his friends. Max agreed to play. They played together and had so much fun. Max learned that it was not nice to be rude. In the end, Max became friends with Sam and they all played together happily in the stable.
