Category: Intermediate
Title: The Humble Mouse and the Bird

Once upon a time there was a humble little mouse. He lived alone in a tiny hole in the wall. He spent each day in peace and quiet, just the way he liked it. One day, the mouse was startled by a loud noise. He peered out of his hole to see what was happening. He saw his friend, a quirky little bird, marking on the wall with a twig. "What are you doing?" said the mouse. "Making marks," said the bird excitedly. The mouse thought this was a strange sight, but he didn't want to be rude. With hesitation, he said "Oh, that's very interesting!" The bird was too busy marking to notice the mouse's apprehension. He kept making more and more marks until the mouse's tiny hole was covered in markings. The mouse was horrified. Everywhere he looked was a mess. He was filled with despair. Finally, the bird flew away, leaving a trail of noise behind him. The little mouse was left in a world of his own destruction.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big blue helmet that he wore when he rode his bike. He loved his helmet because it kept his head safe. One day, Tim was worried. He could not find his helmet anywhere. He looked in his room, under his bed, and even in the yard. Tim was very sad and whispered to his mom, "I can't find my helmet." His mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I know where your helmet is." She took Tim's hand and led him to the garage. There, on a shelf, was his big blue helmet. Tim was so happy! He put on his helmet and rode his bike all day long.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went outside to play. It was very cold, so she wore a cap to keep her head warm. She saw her friend Tom playing in the snow. Lucy ran to him and said, "Hi Tom! Let's play together!" Tom looked at Lucy and said, "Your cap looks crazy!" Lucy laughed and said, "I know! My mom made it for me." They both started to make a big snowman. But soon, they began to shiver because it was so cold. Lucy and Tom decided to go inside and drink some hot cocoa to get warm. They sat by the fire and talked about their fun day. They were happy to have each other as friends and couldn't wait to play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat herbs from his garden. One day, he went to his garden to eat some herbs, but they were not ready yet. Bob was sad and hungry. He hopped to his friend Sue, the squirrel. "Sue, can I borrow some herbs from your garden?" asked Bob. Sue said, "Yes, of course, Bob! My herbs are ready. You can have some." Bob was very happy and hopped to Sue's garden. But when he got there, he saw a big, mean bird eating all the herbs! The bird looked at Bob and said, "These are my herbs now!" Bob was scared, but he had an idea. He hopped back to his own garden and got some seeds. He went to the bird and said, "If you give me and Sue some herbs, I will give you these seeds. You can grow your own herbs!" The bird thought about it and said, "Deal!" So, the bird gave Bob and Sue some herbs, and they all became friends. They helped each other grow their gardens, and they always had enough herbs to eat. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful spider named Spidey. Spidey lived in a big tree with many friends. Spidey loved to play and have fun with all his friends. One day, Spidey saw a little bird who was sad. The bird lost her mom and could not find her. Spidey wanted to help the bird. He said, "Don't worry, little bird. I hope we can find your mom." Spidey and the little bird went to look for her mom. They asked all their friends in the tree if they saw the mom. After a while, they found her. The little bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Spidey!" From that day on, Spidey and the little bird were best friends. They played together every day and always had fun. They were both very cheerful and loved to help their friends.
