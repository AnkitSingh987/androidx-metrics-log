Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fridge

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an intelligent boy who loved to learn new things. One day, Tim saw his mom frown while looking inside the fridge. He wanted to know why she was sad. Tim asked his mom, "Why are you sad?" His mom said, "There is no more food in the fridge. We need to go to the store and buy more." Tim wanted to help his mom, so he said, "Let's go to the store together!" Tim and his mom went to the store and bought lots of food. They put the food in the fridge, and Tim's mom was happy again. Tim felt good because he helped his mom. From that day on, Tim always helped his mom when she needed it.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play basketball. He loved to play with his big, bouncy basketball. He took it outside to play with his friends. Tim tried to throw the basketball into the basket. He missed the first time. He missed the second time too. But Tim was persistent. He did not give up. He tried again and again. Finally, after many tries, Tim made the basketball go into the basket. All his friends clapped and cheered. Tim was so happy that he didn't give up. He learned that being persistent helps you do better.

One day, a boy named Tim found a spark in the forest. The spark was very fair and bright. Tim was happy to find the spark. He took it home to show his mom and dad. They were happy too. But, Tim did something he would regret. He put the fair spark in his room and went to play outside. While he was playing, the spark grew bigger and bigger. Tim did not know this was happening. When Tim came back to his room, he saw that the spark had turned into a big, friendly bear! Tim was surprised and scared at first. But then, the bear gave him a big hug. Tim and the bear became best friends. They played together every day, and Tim never regretted finding the spark again.

One day, a beautiful bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a little boy under a tree. The boy was sad. The bird wanted to help him. The bird flew down and said, "Why are you sad, little boy?" The boy looked up and said, "I can't reach the apples on the tree. I want to give one to my mom." The bird wanted to support the boy. It said, "I can help you. Climb on my back, and I will fly you up to the apples." The boy climbed on the bird's back, and they flew up to the apples. The boy picked a big, red apple and said, "Thank you, beautiful bird!" The bird smiled and flew the boy back down. The boy was happy and ran home to give the apple to his mom. The bird flew away, happy that it could help.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby bunny named Daisy. Daisy loved to hop and play in the sunny meadow with her friends. One day, while playing with her friends, Daisy saw a big, pretty flower. She hopped over to the flower and sniffed it. "Ah-choo!" Daisy sneezed. Her friend, a little bird, flew over to her and asked, "Daisy, are you okay?" Daisy nodded and said, "Yes, I just sneezed because of the flower." They both laughed and continued to play in the meadow. Daisy was careful not to sniff flowers too close again, but she still had lots of fun with her friends. And every time she saw a flower, she remembered the time she sneezed and smiled.

Once upon a time there was a spider. She lived in a quiet house. One day the spider wanted to go for a walk, so she dropped down from the wall. The spider saw a flower and began to explore. She crawled up the stem and around the leaves. But then it started to rain and the spider had to find somewhere safe. Suddenly she saw an old shoe. The spider scurried inside and curled up. "What are you doing in there?" asked a friendly voice. It was a small girl with pigtails. The spider looked up shyly. "I'm just being quiet," she said. The girl smiled. "Well, you can stay here. It's nice and dry." The spider nodded gratefully and the girl smiled once more.
