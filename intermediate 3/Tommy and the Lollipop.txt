Title: Tommy and the Lollipop

Tommy loves lollipops. He likes to lick them and make his tongue green. He has a big lollipop in his hand. He is happy. But then he sees his sister Lily. She is sad. She does not have a lollipop. She looks at Tommy's lollipop and wants it. Tommy thinks for a moment. He loves his lollipop, but he loves his sister too. He decides to act nice and share his lollipop with Lily. He breaks it in half and gives one half to Lily. Lily smiles and says thank you. She licks her half of the lollipop and makes her tongue green too. She hugs Tommy and says he is the best brother. Tommy feels good. He licks his half of the lollipop and makes his tongue green too. He hugs Lily and says she is the best sister. They both enjoy their lollipops and act happy.

One day, a little dog named Max saw a flame. The flame was big and hot. Max was very curious, so he went near the flame. He saw that the flame was on a tree. Max wanted to help, so he ran to find his friend, the big bear named Ben. Max found Ben and told him about the flame. Ben was serious and said, "We must put out the flame!" They went back to the tree, but the flame was now bigger. Max and Ben tried to put out the flame by throwing dirt on it, but it did not work. Ben started to frown because they could not stop the flame. The flame got bigger and bigger. Max and Ben were very sad. They could not save the tree. The tree burned down and Max and Ben felt bad. They wished they could have stopped the flame, but it was too late.

Once upon a time, there were two friends: a bear and a rabbit. They often spent time doing activities together. One day, the bear asked the rabbit a question in a language the rabbit was unfamiliar with. The rabbit was confused and didn't know how to reply. The bear then tried to teach the rabbit the language. The rabbit was competitive and tried its best to learn. Despite its efforts, the rabbit still could not reply to the bear. The bear was very patient and continued to teach the rabbit. Through many lessons, the rabbit slowly learned how to reply in the new language. The rabbit was so happy it had learned a new language it let out a joyful cry! The bear was glad they had become friends and could now talk in the same language. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play in his backyard. One day, while playing, he saw a big stick on the ground. He picked it up and thought it would be fun to break it. Tim's friend, Sam, was watching him from his backyard. Sam felt envious because he wanted to break a stick too. He called out to Tim, "Hey Tim, can I break a stick with you?" Tim smiled and said, "Sure, Sam! Come over and we can break sticks together." So, Sam went to Tim's backyard, and they both found more sticks to break. They had so much fun breaking sticks and playing together. In the end, both Tim and Sam were happy, and they shared many more fun days in the backyard.

Once upon a time, there was a shop. This shop was full of soft things. There were soft toys, soft pillows, and soft blankets. It was a very nice shop. A girl named Lily worked in the shop. Lily liked to design new soft things. One day, she made a soft toy that looked like a cat. It was very cute. Lily put the soft cat toy in the shop for others to see. A little boy named Tim saw the soft cat toy in the shop. He loved it so much. Tim asked his mom if he could have the soft cat toy. His mom said yes. Tim was very happy. He took the soft cat toy home and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an elderly lady named Sue. She lived near a temple. Every day, she would walk to the temple to say hello to her friends. One sunny day, while Sue was walking to the temple, she saw a big bug on her arm. She was scared and said, "Oh no! A bug!" Sue wanted the bug to go away, so she gave it a little slap. The bug flew off her arm and she felt better. When Sue got to the temple, she told her friend Jane about the bug. Jane laughed and said, "Silly Sue, bugs are not so bad!" Sue smiled and they both went inside the temple to play with their other friends.
