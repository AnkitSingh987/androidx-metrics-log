Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Mom

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some food for dinner. Tim saw some green broccoli and wanted to eat it. His mom said, "Yes, we can start eating more broccoli." When they got home, Tim's mom found a broken plate on the floor. Tim's dog, Spot, had knocked it over. "Oh no!" said Tim's mom, "We need to clean this up!" While they were cleaning, they heard a noise from the kitchen. They went to see what it was. It was Spot! He was eating the broccoli! Tim laughed and said, "Spot likes broccoli too!" His mom smiled and said, "Now we all can eat broccoli together!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play with her toy bear, Ben. One sunny day, Mia and Ben went to the park. There, they found a big, green seat. Mia was very happy and said, "Ben, let's sit on the seat!" Mia and Ben sat on the seat and looked around. They saw a big dog standing near them. The dog was confused. He did not know what to do. Mia said, "Hi dog! Do you want to play with us?" The dog wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, I want to play!" So, Mia, Ben, and the dog played together all day. They had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Mia and Ben said goodbye to their new friend. The dog said, "Bye Mia and Ben! Let's play again soon!" And they all went home with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a little mosquito was flying around in a big garden. The mosquito loved to spin and dance in the air. He would spin so fast that he would get dizzy, but he liked it. In the garden, there was a big, mean bird who did not like the mosquito. The bird saw the mosquito spinning and thought it was a good time to catch him. The bird flew fast towards the dizzy mosquito, ready to grab him. But the mosquito saw the bird coming and stopped spinning. He was very dizzy, but he knew he had to fly away. The mosquito flew as fast as he could, and the bird chased him. They went round and round in the garden, but the mosquito was too quick. The bird got dizzy from all the spinning and had to stop. The mosquito was safe, and he went back to spinning and dancing in the air, happy and free.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sarah. She was three years old and very lively. One day, Sarah was very hungry and asked her mum for some food. "Mummy, I'm very hungry," said Sarah. "OK," replied her mum. "Let's get some meat." They went to the supermarket and bought a big piece of meat. Sarah was excited. After getting home, her mum put the meat in the oven and set the timer. Sarah watched the timer and as it counted down she could feel her stomach grow. At last, the timer stopped and the smell of the delicious meat filled the air. It was ready. Sarah was so sleepy that she could barely keep her eyes open while she was eating her meal. But, the meat was so good that it kept her awake. When she finished her meal, she felt so full and content. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play and laugh. One day, he found a fancy hat. He put it on and something strange happened. Tim could change into anything he wanted! Tim changed into a big cat and ran around. He had so much fun. Then he changed into a bird and flew high in the sky. Tim loved his new fancy hat. He wanted to share the fun with his friend, Lily. So, Tim went to Lily's house to show her the fancy hat. He told her a joke to make her laugh. Lily laughed and laughed. Then, Tim put the hat on Lily's head. But, instead of changing, Lily stayed the same. They tried again, but nothing happened. Tim was sad for Lily. But then, Lily said, "I don't need to change to have fun. We can play and laugh together just as we are!" And so, Tim and Lily played and laughed all day long, and they were very happy.
