Category: Intermediate
Title: The Painter and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a painter. He loved to spin around and make pretty pictures. He was very adventurous and liked to try new things. One day, the painter went to the park. He saw a big tree and had an idea. He put paint on a big paper and spun around the tree. The paint made a beautiful picture. The painter felt happy and proud of his work. He showed it to his friends, and they all loved it too. From that day on, the painter kept being adventurous and making more fun art.

Once there was a little girl named Faith. She wanted to make something special for a party she was having, so she decided to make a veil. She gathered some fabric from around the house and started to sew it together. But the more she worked, the messier it got. Soon, Faith's veil was a tangled mess. She looked at it sadly. "I wanted to make something special," she said. Just then, her mother came into the room. "What's wrong, sweetheart?" she asked. Faith showed her the veil. "I wanted to make a special veil, but I'm not very good at sewing." "It doesn't have to be perfect," her mother said. "Sometimes the messiest things can be the most beautiful!" So, Faith kept working. When she was done, it wasn't the prettiest veil ever, but it was special to Faith. Everyone at the party said it was beautiful. Faith was proud of her messy veil!

One day, a little boy named Tim was restless. He wanted to go to the store with his mom. He jumped up and down and said, "Mom, can we go to the store, please?" Mom sighed and said, "Okay, Tim. Let's go to the store." They walked to the store together, holding hands. At the store, Tim saw a big, red ball. He asked his mom, "Can I have the ball, please?" Mom said, "Yes, you can have the ball." Tim was very happy. They went home and played with the ball all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bear named Ben. Ben lived with his mom in a small house in the woods. Ben loved to bake with his mom. They would bake yummy treats like cookies and cakes. One day, Ben and his mom wanted to bake a special cake. They wanted to use honey to make the cake sweet. Ben asked his mom, "Can we bake a honey cake today?" His mom said, "Yes, let's do it!" Ben was very excited. They put on their aprons and started to bake the cake. They mixed flour, eggs, and honey in a big bowl. Ben's mom said, "Now we need to put the cake in the oven to bake." But the oven was very stubborn. It did not want to open. Ben and his mom tried to open the oven door, but it just would not open. Ben said, "Oh no, what can we do?" His mom had an idea. She said, "Let's ask our friend, Mr. Fox, for help." Ben and his mom went to Mr. Fox's house. They asked him for help to open the stubborn oven. Mr. Fox was very strong and smart. He said, "I can help you." They all went back to Ben's house, and Mr. Fox opened the oven door. Ben and his mom were very happy. They put the honey cake in the oven to bake. The honey cake baked in the oven, and soon, it was ready to eat. Ben, his mom, and Mr. Fox all sat down and enjoyed the yummy honey cake. They were all very happy, and they had a great day together.

Once upon a time, there was an ordinary beetle named Bob. Bob lived in a big tree. He had many friends, but he was sad. He wanted to do something special. One day, Bob met a wise old owl. The owl said, "I can help you. Find a big stick and snap it in two. Then, something special will happen." Bob thanked the owl and went to find a big stick. Bob found a big stick and used all his strength to snap it in two. Suddenly, a beautiful butterfly came out of the broken stick. The butterfly said, "Thank you, Bob! You set me free!" Bob felt very happy, and all his friends cheered for him. From that day on, Bob knew that even an ordinary beetle could do something special.
