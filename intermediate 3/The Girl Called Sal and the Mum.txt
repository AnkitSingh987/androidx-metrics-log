Title: The Girl Called Sal and the Mum

Once there was a little girl called Sal. Sal was only three years old and she was very naughty. She would often run around and make messes for her mum to clean up. One day, Sal's mum was really busy. She was very stressed and so Sal's naughty behaviour made her even more frustrated. She said to Sal "You need to be more patient and behave!" But Sal just kept running around and making messes. Sal's mum took a deep breath and decided to try something different. She said to Sal "If you're a lovely and patient girl, I will give you a special surprise!" Sal's eyes lit up and she said "Yes! I can be a good girl!" Now every day, Sal was much more patient, and behaved in a very lovely way. Whenever her mum caught her being naughty, she would just remind her of the special surprise. Sal soon realised how much better it felt to be patient and lovely, and so she continued to behave. The patience and love that Sal showed made her mum very proud. Of course, Sal got her special surprise and she was so excited! Sal's mum was thankful for her lovely behaviour, and happy that her daughter had learnt such an important lesson.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue lived in a small house. They loved to cook. One day, Tim and Sue wanted to make a big meal for their friend, Cat. Tim said, "Let's cook a yummy soup for Cat. She will be so happy!" Sue agreed and said, "Yes, and we need to be reliable friends and make it the best soup ever!" They started to cook the soup together. They put in carrots, peas, and corn. As they cooked, they saw a point on the wall. "What is that?" asked Sue. Suddenly, a big, friendly elephant popped out from behind the wall! The elephant said, "I smelled your soup, and I want to help you cook!" Tim, Sue, and the elephant cooked the soup together, and it was the best soup ever. Cat was so happy, and they all had a great time.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big cardboard box. It was ugly and old. Tim wanted to play with it. He thought it would be fun to make a house. He took the box to his room. In his room, Tim met his sister, Sue. Sue was not nice. She said, "I want the box. Give it to me!" Tim said, "No, I found it. It's mine!" They were mad at each other. Sue wanted to spoil Tim's fun. While they were fighting, their dog, Spot, came in. Spot saw the ugly cardboard box. He thought it was a toy. So, Spot jumped on the box and it broke. Tim and Sue were sad. Tim said to Sue, "Now no one can play with the box." Sue said, "I'm sorry, Tim." They both learned that fighting can spoil things. But it was too late. The ugly cardboard box was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim and his friend, a big brown bear named Ben. They loved to play together in the forest. One day, they found a big muddy puddle. Ben jumped right in and got filthy. Tim did not want to get dirty. "Come on, Tim! It's fun!" Ben said. But Tim disagreed, "No, I don't want to get dirty like you." Ben was sad, but he understood. Later, when the sun was going down, they went back to Tim's house. Tim's mom saw Ben and said, "Oh, Ben, you are filthy! You need a bath." After Ben took a bath, Tim gave him a big hug. They both smiled and were happy to be clean and together again.

Once upon a time, there was a steak. It lived in the oven. The oven was a warm and harmless place. One day, the steak wanted to get out of the oven. So it tried to burn itself out of the oven. Its plan worked - it started to burn itself. The steak was scared. It wanted to get back safe and sound. But the fire was too strong! The steak burned until it was all gone. The little steak was so sad and scared. It was gone without ever getting out of the oven. It was a sad, harmless ending.
