Category: Intermediate
Title: The King and the Hat

Once upon a time, in a big yellow castle, there lived a kind king. The king had a big yellow hat that he wore every day. Everyone in the kingdom loved the king and his big yellow hat. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the castle. She had a question for the king. "King, why do you always wear your big yellow hat?" she asked. The king smiled and said, "One day, you will find out, Lucy." Days went by, and the sky turned dark. Big rain clouds came, and it rained and rained. The people in the kingdom were sad and wet. They went to the king for help. The king took off his big yellow hat and held it up high. Suddenly, the hat grew and grew until it was big enough to cover the whole kingdom! The rain stopped, and the people were dry and happy again. Lucy saw what happened and understood why the king always wore his big yellow hat. It was a magic hat that could keep everyone safe and happy. And from that day on, Lucy never questioned the king's big yellow hat again.

Once upon a time, there was a big black cat. The cat liked to mix things. One day, he found a pie. The pie was in a small house. The cat went into the house. He saw a little girl. The girl was sad. The cat wanted to help the girl. He thought the pie could make her happy. So, the cat mixed the pie with some fun things. He mixed in some red, blue, and yellow. The girl saw the cat and the pie. She smiled and laughed. The cat and the girl ate the pie together. They both felt happy. The cat learned that sharing and helping others can make everyone happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked to a big park with an open space. Tim saw many kids playing and having fun. He wanted to join them and play too. As Tim played with his new friends, he saw something in the grass. He went closer to discover what it was. It was a pie! It looked very yummy. Tim was happy to find the pie and wanted to share it with his friends. Tim and his friends sat in the open space and ate the pie together. They all liked the pie and had a great time. After they finished the pie, they played more games until it was time to go home. Tim was happy he made new friends and discovered the yummy pie.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park when they saw a man with a big brush and a lot of colors. He was a painter. He was painting a picture of the trees and the sky on a big white wall. "Wow, look at that!" Tom said. "He is making a beautiful picture!" Lily nodded. She liked the colors. She wanted to see them closer. She ran towards the wall, but Tom stopped her. "Wait, Lily, don't go there!" he said. "The painter might not like it. He might get angry." Lily looked at the painter. He did not seem angry. He seemed happy. He was humming a song and smiling. He saw Lily and Tom and waved at them. "Hello, little ones!" he said. "Do you like my picture?" "Yes, we do!" Lily said. "It is very pretty!" "Thank you!" the painter said. "Do you want to help me?" Lily and Tom looked at each other. They nodded. They wanted to help. The painter gave them each a small brush and a cup of color. He showed them where to paint on the wall. He said they could paint anything they wanted. Lily and Tom were very excited. They started to paint flowers and birds and stars. They had a lot of fun. The wall became more and more colorful. The painter was very pleased. He said they were very good painters. He said they made his picture more beautiful. Lily and Tom were very happy. They thanked the painter. They gave him a hug. They said they liked painting. The painter hugged them back. He said he liked painting too. He said he liked painting with them. They looked at the wall. It was an enormous picture. It was a picture of the park and the trees and the sky. And it was a picture of Lily and Tom and the painter. And it was a picture of friendship.
