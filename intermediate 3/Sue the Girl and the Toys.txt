Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was feeling anxious one day. She wanted to do something fun to feel better. She thought of an idea. Sue wanted to act in a play with her toys. Sue got her toys and a big bowl. She put the bowl on the floor. She put her toys around the bowl. The bowl was the stage for her play. Sue was the boss of the play. She told her toys what to do. They all acted in the play. Sue's toys acted very well. They made Sue very happy. The anxious feeling went away. Sue and her toys had so much fun. They acted in the play all day long. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to bathe her toy bear. She filled a small bin with water and put her bear in it. She was very happy to see her bear getting clean. Lucy's friend, Tom, came to play. He saw the bin and asked, "What are you doing, Lucy?" Lucy said, "I am giving my bear a bath. Do you want to help?" Tom was an honest boy and said, "Yes, I want to help." They played with the bear in the bin. The water started to get dirty. Lucy and Tom did not see the big hole in the bin. The water went out of the hole and made a big mess on the floor. When Lucy's mom saw the mess, she was not happy. She told Lucy and Tom that they could not play together for one week.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to go to the zoo with his mom and dad. At the zoo, he saw many animals like big lions, tall giraffes, and funny monkeys. Tim's favorite part of the zoo was the fancy bird area, where he could see colorful birds with big feathers. One day, Tim and his family went to the zoo. They went to see the fancy birds, but there was a problem. A big wind came and made a tear in the bird's cage. The birds were scared and started to fly away. Tim and his family were sad because they loved to see the birds. Tim had an idea. He asked his mom and dad to help him fix the tear in the cage. They found some strong string and worked together to close the tear. The birds saw that their cage was safe again and flew back home. Tim and his family were happy to see the fancy birds again, and they had a great day at the zoo.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl named Tina. Tina loved to search and explore every day. Today she decided to search around her house. She began to look inside her living room but could not find anything interesting. She then went outside in the garden, but still could not find anything fun. Suddenly, Tina heard a sound coming from the backyard by the shed. She looked around the corner and saw her kitty! Tina was so excited, she shouted "nice kitty! I want to play with you!" But the Kitty stepped away from Tina and ran away. "Where is it going?" She thought. Tina started to chase the kitty but it was too fast for her. Tina was very sad, so she decided to go back to searching in the garden. She looked around and saw a friendly bunny! Her heart was so happy, she started to giggle and jump around. Tina carefully approached the bunny, and she put her foot out to give it a pet. The bunny hopped away, but Tina did not give up. She kept searching until the bunny came back again. Finally, Tina was able to give the bunny a nice pet. She was so pleased, she gave the bunny a kiss on the head, and they became the best of friends!

One day, a little boy named Tim found a pen on the floor. He picked it up and said, "Wow, a pen! I can draw with this." Tim started to draw a big sun, a house, and a tree. But the pen did not work. Tim said, "This is a stupid pen!" Tim's friend, Sue, saw him and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim showed her the pen and said, "This pen is stupid. It doesn't work." Sue took the pen and looked at it. She said, "Oh, you need to push the top to make it work." Tim pushed the top of the pen and it started to work. He was so happy. He said, "Thank you, Sue! You helped me." Tim and Sue drew pretty pictures together with the pen. They had a fun day playing and drawing.
