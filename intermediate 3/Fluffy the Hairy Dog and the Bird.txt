Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Hairy Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a very hairy dog named Fluffy. Fluffy lived on a street with many other dogs. Fluffy was very kind and always liked to help his friends. One day, Fluffy saw a little bird who was lost. The bird was sad and didn't know how to get home. Fluffy wanted to help, so he asked the bird a question. "Where do you live?" The bird said, "I live in a big tree near the park." Fluffy knew where the park was, so he decided to help the bird find its home. Fluffy and the bird walked down the street, and they asked other animals if they knew where the big tree was. Soon, they found the big tree near the park, and the bird was very happy. Fluffy felt good because he helped his new friend. The other animals saw Fluffy's kindness and wanted to be kind too. The moral of the story is that helping others can make everyone happy.

One day, a small bird was feeling anxious. It was time for her to fly, but she was scared. She looked at her parent and said, "I need help." Her parent smiled and said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will be with you. We can do it together." They went to the edge of the nest. The parent bird showed her how to flap her wings. The little bird tried and tried. She started to feel less anxious. Soon, she was flying next to her parent. They were both happy and proud.

One day, a little girl named Lily woke up and yawned. She looked at her clothes and wanted to wear her favorite dress. But, when she tried to put it on, it did not fit. Lily was sad and asked her mom for help. "Mom, my dress does not fit. What can I do?" Lily asked. Her mom looked at the dress and said, "Lily, you have grown so big! We need to get you new clothes that fit." So, Lily and her mom went to the store to find new clothes. They found a pretty dress that fit Lily just right. Lily was so happy and thanked her mom. They went home and Lily showed her dad her new dress. "Dad, look at my new dress! It fits me well!" Lily said with a big smile. Her dad hugged her and said, "You look so pretty in your new dress, Lily!" From that day on, Lily wore her new dress all the time. She was happy that her clothes fit and she could play and have fun with her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a calm human named Tom. Tom lived in a small house near a big tree. Every day, Tom would play with his dog, Sam, and they would have lots of fun together. One sunny day, Tom and Sam went to the park to play. They saw a big door in a hill. Tom and Sam entered the door and found a room full of toys and games. They were so happy to play with everything. After playing for a long time, Tom and Sam felt tired. They left the room and went back to their small house. They ate some food and took a nap. When they woke up, they told their friends about the fun room they found. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat pizza. One day, his mom said, "Tim, let's make a pizza together!" Tim was very happy. They made a big, round pizza with lots of cheese. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, the pizza is fragile." Tim tried to be careful, but he tripped and the pizza flew into the air. The pizza fell apart and the pieces scatter all over the floor. Tim was sad. Just then, their dog, Spot, came running in. Spot saw the pizza on the floor and began to eat it. Tim's mom laughed and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can make another pizza, and Spot can have this one." Tim smiled, and they all had a fun time making and eating pizza together.
