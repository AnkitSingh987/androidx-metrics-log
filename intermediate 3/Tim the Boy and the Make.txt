Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Make

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to make a perfect snack. He asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me make a snack?" Tim said. His mom said, "Yes, let's make a yummy snack together." They went to the kitchen and turned on the stove. They put a pan on the stove and put some cheese in it. The cheese started to melt. Tim was very happy to see the cheese melt. Just then, their dog, Spot, came into the kitchen. Spot saw the cheese melting and jumped up to get it. He knocked the pan off the stove! Tim's mom turned off the stove and they all laughed. They decided to make a new snack, and this time, they made sure Spot was outside.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Bob. Bob loved to play and have fun. One day, Bob met a friendly dog named Max. They became best friends. "Let's play together," said Max. Bob agreed and they played all day. Then, Max said, "I know a fun game. It's called 'supply and spell.'" Bob had never played this game before, but he was excited to try it. As they played, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and joined them. The bird said, "I can help you play the game too!" Bob and Max were surprised, but they let the bird play with them. Together, they had a great time playing "supply and spell" and made a new friend.

Once, in a big forest, there was a small squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to collect acorns. One day, he found a very big and serious acorn. He was so happy and wanted to show it to his friend, Tom the bird. Sam called out, "Tom, come look at my big, serious acorn!" Tom flew down and saw the acorn. "Wow, that is a big acorn!" Tom said. Sam and Tom played with the acorn, rolling it around. Suddenly, a big, mean fox came and saw the acorn. The fox wanted it too. The fox screamed, "Give me that acorn!" Sam and Tom were scared. They did not want to give the acorn to the mean fox. But the fox was too big and strong. He took the serious acorn away from Sam and Tom. Sam and Tom were very sad. They lost their big, serious acorn and could not play with it anymore. The bad fox ran away with the acorn, and Sam and Tom went home with nothing.

One day, while playing in the park, Jack saw a shiny, bright piece of plastic. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. The park was empty and Jack had fun running and playing. Suddenly, Jack heard a loud voice. He turned around and saw his mom. "What have you got there?" asked his mom. Jack hesitated and then showed his mom the piece of plastic. His mom looked very pleased. She said, "This is just what we need. Keep it safe in your pocket." Jack didn't know what she meant, but he tucked the plastic away into his pocket. Later at home, Jack watched as his mom used the plastic to make a cake. Jack was surprised. He asked, "Why did you need this piece of plastic?" His mom replied, "It was to protect the cake from looking rude." Jack thought this was very strange but as he took a bite of the cake, he was very pleased to see that it was delicious. He suddenly realized why his mom wanted the piece of plastic. It had been the perfect way to put the finishing touches on the cake.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Emma. She was very busy today. She had school work to finish. She had to use her pencil to write her work. Emma tried her best, but it was hard! She got tired and her hands hurt. She decided to take a break. Then she remembered she had to finish her work. Emma got back to work. She used her pencil to draw a picture. She was so proud of herself! All her hard work paid off and she finished her work. Emma was so happy. She made sure to put away her pencil. Then she ran off to find something else to do! She was so busy and smiles filled her day.
