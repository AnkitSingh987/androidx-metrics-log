Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bug and the Flower

One day, a little bug went for a walk. The bug saw a big, green leaf. The bug thought, "I want to eat that leaf!" So, the bug went to the leaf and took a bite. It was yummy! Next, the bug saw a pretty flower. The bug thought, "I want to sit on that flower!" So, the bug went to the flower and sat on it. The bug was happy. A small ant saw the bug and felt envious. The ant thought, "I want to sit on a flower too!" So, the ant went to the flower and sat next to the bug. They both smiled and enjoyed the sun.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in the park. He was very enthusiastic and loved to run around. He liked to hear the birds sing and the wind blow. While Tim was playing, he saw a cop. The cop was looking for a lost dog. The dog was big and had a loud bark. Tim wanted to help the cop find the dog, so they looked together. They searched and searched, but they could not find the dog. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim heard a meow and saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cop helped the cat get down from the tree. Tim and the cop were happy they saved the cat, even though they did not find the dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, she found a big root in the ground. She wanted to see what was at the end of the root, so she started to pull and pull. As Sue pulled the root, she found a big, sour lemon. She was very surprised! She did not think a root could grow a lemon. Sue showed her mom the sour lemon. Her mom smiled and said, "This is a special lemon. It can teach us to value what we have." Sue and her mom took the sour lemon inside. They made lemonade together. The lemonade was not sour anymore, it was sweet. Sue learned that even if something seems bad, like a sour lemon, it can still be good if you value it. From that day on, Sue always remembered to value what she had, and she lived a happy life.

John was observing a race with his friends. He was excited because it was a popular race. His friends were cheering loudly when they saw the racers arrive. John asked his friend Billy, "Why is everyone so excited?" "They love these races!", said Billy, with a big smile on his face. They could see the racers lining up for the race. John saw how fast the racers ran. He couldn't believe it! He was so excited to watch the race. The racers started their race and the crowd cheered even louder! It was so much fun to watch. Then, to everyone's surprise, the winner was John's friend Billy! He was very popular with the crowd. John was so happy for his friend. He said to Billy, "Congratulations! That was an awesome race!". Billy smiled and said, "Thanks John! I'm so excited to have won!"

Once upon a time there were two friends, Joe and Sam. Joe wanted to go outside and play, but Sam wanted to stay inside and watch TV. Joe poked Sam's arm and said, "Let's go play outside with the long stick!" Sam didn't like being poked, but he agreed. So they both went outside and Joe found the long stick. He started using it to poke at a fly. But the fly was getting mad and began to buzz loudly. And soon the fly had enough of the poking and flew away. Joe and Sam stopped playing and looked at one another. Joe said, "That was not nice of me. I should not have poked the fly." Sam nodded and said, "Yes, it would have been better to leave the fly alone." So the two friends learned a lesson: it is important to be kind to all creatures, even a small fly. It is better to use our words instead of poking to get what we want.
