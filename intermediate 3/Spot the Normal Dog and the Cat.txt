Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Normal Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a normal little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play, but today he was in a hurry. He had to find his stand where he kept his favorite toy. As Spot hurried to his stand, he saw his friend, Cat. Cat was also in a hurry. They both reached the stand at the same time. They looked and saw that there was only one toy there! Spot thought it was his toy, but Cat thought it was her toy too! Spot and Cat were both sad and confused. Then, they looked at the toy again and saw that it was not a normal toy. It was a new toy that they could both play with together! Spot and Cat were happy and played with their new toy all day long. The unexpected toy made them even better friends.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Emma wanted to mix up something wonderful. She went to the mall with her mommy to find the perfect ingredients. At the mall, Emma saw so much food. She wanted to try them all! First, she picked up a package of marshmallows. Then, she found some chocolate chips. Finally, she chose a carton of cream. When Emma and her mother got home, Emma mixed all the goodies together in a bowl. She was so successful! "Mommy, look at the wonderful mix I made", said Emma excitedly. Her mommy smiled and said, "You did a great job! Let's put it in the oven and I'll make us the best cookies ever!" Emma was so happy. She had mixed something wonderful together and soon they would get to eat it.

One day, a girl named Lily found a big, important book. She loved the book because it had many fun pictures. Lily wanted to show the book to her friend, Tom. Lily went to Tom's house and said, "Tom, look at this important book! It has many fun pictures!" Tom looked at the book and said, "Wow, Lily! That's a big book! Can I hold it?" Lily gave the book to Tom, but he did not hold it carefully. The book fell down and some pages ripped. Lily was sad because the important book suffered. She told Tom, "You did not hold the book carefully, and now it is hurt." Tom said, "I am sorry, Lily. I did not mean to hurt the book." The book could not be fixed, and Lily and Tom were sad. The moral of the story is to always be careful with things that are important to others.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a very jealous dog. He did not like it when his friend, a cat named Fluffy, got more treats than him. One day, Spot saw Fluffy get a big pick of treats from their owner, Mr. Brown. Spot wanted a big pick too. The next day, Mr. Brown said, "Spot, today you will serve me by helping me pick up sticks in the yard. If you do a good job, I will give you a big pick of treats too." Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Woof! Woof!" He was excited to help and get his treats. Spot worked hard all day picking up sticks. When he was done, Mr. Brown was very happy. He gave Spot a big pick of treats, just like he promised. Spot was not jealous of Fluffy anymore. They both enjoyed their treats and played together in the yard. From that day on, Spot learned that helping and serving others can bring good things.

Leo was a lion who lived in the jungle. He was very proud of his big mane of hair. He liked to show it off to the other animals and make them feel bad. He never wanted to bathe, because he thought water would ruin his hair. One day, he met a monkey who was bald. The monkey was very friendly and asked Leo if he wanted to play. Leo laughed and said, "No, I don't play with bald monkeys. You are ugly and silly. Go away and leave me alone." The monkey felt sad and said, "That's not nice, Leo. You should not judge others by how they look. You should be kind and share. Maybe you would have more friends if you did." Leo snorted and said, "I don't need friends. I have my hair. It is the best thing in the jungle. Now go away and don't bother me again." The monkey shrugged and left. He went to the river and bathed. He felt clean and happy. He met some other monkeys who were also bald. They played and had fun. Leo stayed in his den and admired his hair. He did not notice a big tick that was hiding in his mane. The tick bit Leo and made him sick. Leo felt itchy and sore. He scratched his hair and it started to fall out. He looked in a mirror and saw that he was bald. He felt scared and sad. He had no hair and no friends. He went to the river and saw the monkey. He was still playing with the other monkeys. They looked happy and healthy. Leo felt sorry and said, "Hello, monkey. I was wrong. You are not ugly and silly. You are nice and smart. Can I play with you?" The monkey smiled and said, "Of course, Leo. You are welcome to join us. We don't care if you have hair or not. We care if you are kind and share. Come and bathe with us. It will make you feel better." Leo nodded and said, "Thank you, monkey. I'm sorry I was mean to you. I learned a lesson. Hair is not the best thing in the jungle. Friends are." The monkey and Leo became friends. They bathed and played together. They also helped each other to get rid of the ticks. Leo was happy and grateful. He realized that being bald was not so bad after all.
