Category: Intermediate
Title: The Navy Boat and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a navy boat. The boat was very big and strong. The boat liked to complete tasks in the sea. One day, the boat saw a little green fish. The fish was very jealous of the boat. The jealous fish wanted to be big and strong too. So, the fish thought of a plan. The fish asked the boat to help it complete a task. The boat agreed to help the little green fish. But, something unexpected happened! The little green fish became a big green fish! The big green fish was now as big as the navy boat. The boat was very surprised. Now, the boat and the big green fish could complete tasks together in the sea. They were both happy and not jealous anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. The mouse lived in a small hole near a big tree. It was easy for the mouse to find food and play around the tree. One day, the mouse saw a big cat. The cat wanted to catch the mouse. The mouse was scared and ran away. The cat chased the mouse, but the mouse was fast. Then, the mouse had an idea. He ran to a tall man who was kneeling near the tree. The man saw the mouse and the cat. He thought the cat was trying to hurt the mouse. So, the man picked up the mouse and put it on his shoulder. The cat was sad and walked away. But the man did not know that the mouse and the cat were friends. They liked to play chase together. The mouse was not scared at all. The cat and the mouse laughed and went back to play near the tree. The man did not know he helped the mouse play a trick on the cat.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Jane who was very brave. She wanted to explore the world and discover all its amazing things. One day she decided to go on an adventure. She bowled through the forest, jumping over trees and bushes. She walked for hours, but the scenery was so amazing she didn't notice the time. Eventually she stopped and decided to take a break. That's when the pain started. She felt a sharp pain in her foot, so she tried to take off her shoe but it was too tight. Jane started to cry as the pain got worse. Unfortunately, as Jane bowed down to take off her shoe, she suddenly fell and broke her leg. She screamed in pain - her adventure had come to a bad end.

Once upon a time, there was a smart little fish. The fish lived in a big pond. The fish had many friends, and they all played together every day. One day, the smart fish saw something shiny. It was a hook! The smart fish knew that the hook could disturb his friends. So the fish swam up to the hook to try and make it go away. But as the smart fish got close to the hook, something unexpected happened. The hook caught the smart fish! The smart fish tried to get away, but it was too late. The hook took the smart fish away from the pond and his friends. The smart fish was very sad, and his friends missed him a lot.

Once upon a time there was a little girl with long, brown hair. She was three years old and loved to dance and giggle. One day she was walking around the park with her mommy when the sky suddenly turned gloomy. The little girl started to panic and grabbed her mommy’s hand tight. They looked around and noticed that someone had spilled wine all over the ground. That’s when the little girl started to cry. She didn’t know what to do or how to help. Her mommy hugged her tight and said, “It’s okay sweetheart, let’s just look for a way to clean up the mess.” So together they wiped up the wine using some napkins they found in their bag. Then the sky started to brighten up. The little girl was so relieved! She smiled and said a big “thank you” to her mommy for helping her. They gave each other a big, warm hug and kept on walking around the park, singing and laughing as they went.
