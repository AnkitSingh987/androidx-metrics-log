Category: Intermediate
Title: The Freezer and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a big freezer. It was a little scary, but it was nice too. Inside the freezer, there were many yummy things to eat. There were cold ice creams, tasty fruits, and sweet treats. One day, a nice man came to load the freezer with more food. He had a big box full of good things to eat. He put the food in the freezer, one by one. The freezer was very happy to have new food for everyone to eat. But then, a scary monster came to the freezer. The monster wanted to eat all the food! The nice man and the freezer were scared. They didn't want the monster to eat everything. So, the nice man and the freezer worked together to chase the scary monster away. They were brave and strong. The monster ran away and never came back. The freezer was safe and full of yummy food for everyone to enjoy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved pizza more than anything in the world. One day, his mom went to the store to buy a big pizza for dinner. Tim was very excited. While his mom was at the store, Tim thought about the pizza. He wanted to eat it all by himself. Tim was being selfish. He didn't want to share the pizza with his sister, Lily. When his mom came home, she put the pizza on the table for dinner. Tim grabbed the pizza and ran to his room. He didn't want to share with Lily. But, Tim ate too much pizza too fast. His tummy started to hurt very bad. Tim felt sad and sick. He learned that being selfish was not good.

One day, a girl named Lily found a pen. It was a gentle pen, not too big or too small. She wanted to write with it. So, she took the pen and a piece of paper. Lily started to write a story about a cat and a dog. In the story, the cat said, "Hello, dog. Do you want to play with me?" The dog said, "Yes, cat. I want to play with you, too." So, the cat and the dog played together. They ran, they jumped, and they had fun. Lily was happy with her story. She showed it to her mom and dad. They liked it too. They said, "Good job, Lily! Your story is nice." Lily felt proud and smiled. She knew she would write more stories with her gentle pen.

Once there was a girl named Jacklyn. She was 3-years-old and loved to play. One day she went to the park with her mom. As soon as Jacklyn saw the swings she said, "Mommy, swing!" Her mom said, "Okay, sweetie. But first find something to swing with." Jacklyn ran to the toy basket and took out a big club. She held it in the air and said, "Look mommy, I found a club!" Her mom said, "That's great! Now let's put it to use." They put the club on the swings and her mom pushed her very high. She felt so warm and happy as she flew up into the sky. She laughed and laughed as she swung up high. Finally, when she couldn't swing anymore she said, "Mommy, when can I swing again?" Her mom said, "Whenever you want, sweetheart. Whenever you want."

Once upon a time, there lived a gifted little girl. Her name was Daisy and she loved to play. On Daisy's birthday, her family wanted to celebrate her special day. Daisy's mom called her over and said, "Daisy, come and see what's in the attic!" "What is it?" Daisy asked, feeling excited. "Let's go and see," her mom replied. So, Daisy went up the stairs to the attic and saw a big surprise. There was a birthday cake, with ice cream and candles. "Let's celebrate your birthday," said Daisy's mom. Everybody cheered, and Daisy blew out the candles on her cake. Daisy and her family had a wonderful day celebrating in the attic. The end.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play in the kitchen. They pretend to be cooks and serve food to their toys. One day, they find a big pot in the cupboard. It is shiny and has a long handle. "Let's make soup with this pot!" Sara says. "OK, what do we need?" Ben asks. They look around and see some carrots, potatoes, onions and water. They put them all in the pot and stir with a spoon. They turn on the stove and wait for the soup to boil. "Soup is ready!" Sara says. "Let's serve it to our toys!" They take the pot and carry it to the table. But the pot is very heavy and hot. The handle is long and hard to hold. Sara and Ben lose their balance and drop the pot. The soup spills all over the floor and the toys. Sara and Ben are scared and sad. "Mommy! Daddy! Help!" they cry. Mommy and Daddy hear the noise and run to the kitchen. They see the mess and the twins. They are angry and worried. "What have you done?" Mommy asks. "We wanted to make soup and serve it to our toys!" Sara says. "But you used a big pot and turned on the stove! That is very dangerous! You could have burned yourselves or the house!" Daddy says. "We are sorry! We didn't know!" Ben says. Mommy and Daddy hug the twins and calm them down. They clean up the mess and the toys. They explain to the twins that they should never play with the stove or the pots without an adult. They should only use safe and small toys for their pretend games. "We understand, Mommy and Daddy. We won't do it again!" Sara and Ben say. They promise to be more careful and listen to their parents. They learn that playing is fun, but safety is important. They also learn that some things are too big or too hot for them to handle. They should ask for help when they need it.
