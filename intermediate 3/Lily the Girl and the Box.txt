Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big bag of dirty laundry. She wanted to send it to her grandma to help her wash it. So, Lily put the laundry in a box and went to the post office. At the post office, Lily met a nice man named Mr. Brown. Mr. Brown saw Lily's big box and asked, "What are you sending, Lily?" Lily said, "I am sending my laundry to my grandma. She will help me wash it." Mr. Brown smiled and said, "That's a good idea, but you can also learn to wash it yourself." Lily thought about it and decided to learn how to wash her laundry. She went home and asked her mom to teach her. Her mom was happy to help. They washed the laundry together, and Lily felt proud. When the laundry was dry, it was warm and clean. Lily learned that it's good to ask for help, but it's even better to learn how to do things yourself. And every time she put on her warm, clean clothes, she felt proud of the work she had done.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a small pebble. It was so pretty and shiny. Mia liked it a lot, so she decided to keep it. She put the pebble in her pocket and went to play with her friends. While playing, Mia started to feel worried. She thought she lost her pebble. She looked everywhere for it. She asked her friends to help her find the pebble. They all looked around, but they could not find it. Then, Mia's friend, Tom, found the pebble. But it was not just a pebble anymore. It had turned into a beautiful, shiny stone. Mia was so happy and surprised! She thanked Tom for finding it. Now, Mia and her friends all played together, with the pretty stone shining in the sun.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were animals. Lily was a tiger and Ben was a monkey. They ran and jumped and roared and chattered. "Look at me, I'm a unique tiger!" Lily said. "I have pink stripes and purple spots. No other tiger has that." Ben laughed. "You're silly, Lily. Tigers don't have pink stripes and purple spots. They have orange stripes and black spots. You're not a real tiger." Lily frowned. "Yes, I am. I believe I'm a tiger. And I like my colors. They make me special." Just then, they heard a loud roar from behind a bush. They turned and saw a big, furry animal with orange stripes and black spots. It was a real tiger! Lily and Ben were scared. They ran to their mom, who was sitting on a bench. "Mom, mom, there's a tiger in the park!" they cried. Mom looked at the bush and saw the tiger. She smiled. "Don't worry, kids. That's not a real tiger. That's a man in a costume. He's part of a circus that's in town. He's just playing with you." Lily and Ben felt relieved. They looked at the man in the costume. He waved at them and winked. "See, Lily, he's not a unique tiger. He's just a man," Ben said. Lily nodded. "But I still believe I'm a tiger. And I still like my colors. They make me happy."

One day, a big bear and a little bunny were walking slow in the woods. They saw many pretty flowers with different colors. The big bear saw a pattern on the ground made by the flowers. "Look, little bunny!" said the big bear, "The flowers make a nice pattern on the ground. Do you like it?" "Yes, big bear, I like it a lot!" said the little bunny with a big smile. They both care about the flowers and the pattern, so they decided to make their own pattern with the flowers. They picked up some flowers and put them on the ground. The big bear and the little bunny worked together and made a beautiful pattern. They were very happy and proud of what they made.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play hide and seek with his friends. He thought it would be fun to hide in the closet. Tim tried to open the closet door, but it was stuck. He felt a little foolish for not being able to open it. Tim tried again to open the closet door. This time, he pushed very hard. The door opened, and something unexpected happened. A big pile of toys fell out of the closet! Tim was surprised and happy at the same time. Now, Tim had a new game to play with his friends. They all helped him pick up the toys and put them back in the closet. Then, they played hide and seek. Tim found a new hiding spot, and his friends had a great time trying to find him.
