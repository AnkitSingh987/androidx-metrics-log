Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Toys

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. But then Lily saw a shiny bottle on the table. It was wine that their mom had left there. Lily wanted to see what was inside. She climbed on a chair and reached for the bottle. Ben saw her and got scared. He knew that wine was not for kids. He tried to stop her. "Stop, Lily! That is not for us! That is mom's drink!" he shouted. But Lily did not listen. She grabbed the bottle and pulled hard. The bottle was heavy and slippery. Lily struggled to hold it. She lost her balance and fell off the chair. The bottle fell too and broke on the floor. The wine spilled everywhere. It made a big mess and a loud noise. Mom heard the noise and came running. She saw Lily and Ben and the broken bottle and the wine. She was very angry and sad. "What did you do?" she asked. "That was a special wine that dad gave me for our anniversary. How could you be so rude and careless?" Lily and Ben felt very bad. They knew they had done something wrong. They were sorry and scared. "We are sorry, mom," they said. "We did not mean to break your wine. We were just curious. Please don't be mad at us." Mom sighed and hugged them. She was still angry and sad, but she loved them. She knew they were just kids and they made mistakes. "I love you, but you have to be more careful and respectful," she said. "Wine is not for kids. It can make you sick and hurt you. And you have to ask before you touch things that are not yours. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nodded. They understood. They said they were sorry again and helped mom clean up the mess. They learned their lesson and never touched wine again.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly bird named Bob. Bob liked to play and sing all day. Bob had many friends in the forest. One day, Bob saw a pretty stream. He wanted to see where it went. So, Bob started to soar high in the sky. He flew over the trees and the stream. He saw fish and frogs playing in the water. As Bob flew, he felt very happy. He sang a fun song for all his friends to hear. They all came to the stream to play with Bob. They had a great time by the water, and they were all very jolly.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to play in the sand at the beach. One sunny day, Buddy met a little girl named Lily. Lily also loved playing in the sand. "Hi, Buddy! Can you help me use this sand to make a big castle?" Lily asked. Buddy barked happily and wagged his tail. Together, they started to build the castle. Buddy dug in the sand with his paws and Lily used her hands to shape the sand into a big castle. They worked together and had lots of fun. When they finished, they stepped back to look at their big sand castle. "Wow, Buddy! We did a great job!" Lily said with a big smile. Buddy barked and wagged his tail, proud of their work. From that day on, Buddy and Lily became best friends, always playing in the sand and having fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to create things. One day, he made a boat. Tim was very happy with his boat. He wanted to show it to his friends. Tim and his friends went to the lake. They put the boat in the water. The boat started to move. But then, something unexpected happened. The boat started to grow bigger and bigger. Tim and his friends were very surprised. Tim was persistent. He wanted to find out why the boat was growing. He and his friends looked inside the boat. They found a magic stone. The stone made the boat grow. Tim and his friends had a lot of fun playing with the magic boat. They had a great day at the lake.
