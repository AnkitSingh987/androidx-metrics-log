Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a magic toy. The toy was very special. But the magic was not strong. It was cheap magic. One day, Sue wanted to prevent her cat from going outside. She used the magic toy to help her. The toy tried to stop the cat, but the magic was not strong enough. The cat went outside anyway. Sue was sad. She wanted better magic. But she learned that cheap magic is better than no magic. And she still loved her magic toy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a smooth lamp. He saw that it was very pretty. Tim wanted to show his mom the lamp. He ran to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a pretty lamp!" His mom looked at the smooth lamp and said, "Wow, Tim! That is a nice lamp. What if you shake it? Maybe something fun will happen." Tim thought that was a good idea. He held the lamp tight and started to shake it. As Tim shook the lamp, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came out of the lamp! Tim and his mom were very surprised. The dog said, "Thank you for shaking the lamp! I am a magic dog. I will be your friend and play with you." Tim was so happy, and they all played together every day.

---

Mum was tired. She had been playing with her young son all day long. He wanted to play more, but Mum said it was time for a rest. She decided to make tea. She put her things away, then went to get some water. She filled the pot and put it on the stove. While it heated up, Mum made some sandwiches. Soon, the water was hot. Mum put some tea leaves into the pot and stirred them with a spoon. Then she filled two cups with tea. Mum and her son sat at the table and enjoyed their tea and sandwiches. Mum said, "we needed a break. Tea can make us feel better". Her son smiled. He loved his young Mum's tea.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to sit in the sun and play with her toys. One day, Kitty was very hungry and wanted a meal. Kitty's mom was cooking a meal in the kitchen. She was very careful not to spill anything. Kitty sat and watched her mom cook. She could smell the yummy food and her tummy growled. When the meal was ready, Kitty and her mom sat down to eat. They were very careful not to make a mess. Kitty enjoyed her meal and felt happy. After the meal, they both cleaned up and went to play outside.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside in the warm sun. Every day, she would spin around and around in her yard, laughing and having fun. One day, Lucy went to the park with her mom. She was very careful as she played on the swings and the slide. She made sure to hold her mom's hand when they crossed the street. Lucy was a good listener and always followed the rules. As the day went on, the sun began to set. Lucy and her mom sat on a bench and watched the beautiful sunset. The sky was full of pretty colors like pink, orange, and purple. They held hands and smiled at the lovely view. It was a perfect day for Lucy and her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Bessie. Bessie was an anxious cow. She lived on a big farm with her friends. One day, Bessie saw a big, round toy in the farm yard. It was a new toy for the animals to play with. Bessie went to her friend, the pig. "Look at the big, round toy!" said Bessie. "What do we do with it?" The pig said, "We spin it!" So, Bessie and the pig went to the big, round toy and started to spin it. As they spun the toy, they laughed and had fun. Soon, all the other animals came to see what was happening. They all took turns spinning the big, round toy. Bessie was not anxious anymore. She was happy to be with her friends, spinning the toy and having fun.
