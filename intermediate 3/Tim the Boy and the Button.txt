Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Button

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, interesting box. The box had a big button on it. Tim was very curious about the box and the button. Tim decided to push the button. When he pushed it, the box opened and out came a funny-looking toy. The toy could talk and dance. Tim was very surprised and happy. But then, the toy started to push the button on the box too. Every time the toy pushed the button, a new toy came out of the box. Soon, there were too many toys in Tim's room. Tim had to push the button one more time to make all the toys go back in the box.

One day, a little girl named Mia was very hungry. She wanted to bake a cake. Mia put on her apron and went to the kitchen. She found flour, eggs, and sugar. Mia mixed them all together in a big bowl. She put the mix in the oven to bake. While Mia was waiting, her friend Tom came over. Tom saw Mia's finger had a bandage. "What happened to your finger?" Tom asked. Mia said, "I got a small cut, but it's okay now." The oven beeped and the cake was done. Mia and Tom took the cake out of the oven. They were both very hungry. Mia cut the cake into pieces and they both ate it. It was a yummy cake. Mia and Tom were happy and full.

One day, a little girl named Sue got a new pet. It was a small, obedient dog. She named her dog Max. Sue was very happy to have Max as her new friend. Sue and Max played all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Sue said, "Max, you are a good dog!" Max wagged his tail and barked, "Woof!" They had so much fun together. At night, Sue set a little bed for Max next to her bed. Max was happy to sleep near Sue. They both went to sleep with big smiles on their faces. Sue and Max were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a pet bird named Sky. Sky lived in a birdcage. Mia loved Sky very much. Every day, Mia would play with Sky and feed him. One day, Mia's mom asked her to clean Sky's birdcage. Mia was an obedient girl, so she said yes. She took the birdcage outside to clean it. Mia was very careful not to drop it. She knew that if she dropped the birdcage, Sky would be sad. While Mia was cleaning the birdcage, Sky was watching her. He saw how hard Mia worked. Sky wanted to help Mia. So, when Mia was done, Sky flew back into the birdcage. He was a good bird. Mia was happy that Sky was obedient too. They both knew how important it was to be careful and listen to their mom. The moral of the story is that being obedient and careful makes everyone happy.

One day, a little bird named Tim was very sleepy. He lived in a big tree with his family. Tim loved to fly high in the sky and play with his friends. One day, while Tim was flying, he found a flute on the ground. He picked it up with his beak and took it to his tree. Tim tried to play the flute, but it was hard for him because he was a bird. Tim's friend, a squirrel named Sam, saw Tim with the flute. Sam knew how to play the flute and taught Tim how to play it too. They played the flute together and made beautiful music. Tim was so happy that he forgot he was sleepy. They played and played until the sun went down and it was time for bed.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to celebrate her birthday. She wanted a big cake with lots of sugar on it. Her mom said, "Be careful, Lily. The cake is delicate." One day, Lily saw a big, red ball. She wanted to play with it. But the ball was close to the cake. Lily tried to get the ball, but she bumped the table. The delicate cake fell on the floor. Lily was sad and cried. Lily's mom came into the room and saw the cake on the floor. She was not happy. She said, "Lily, you did not listen. Now we can't celebrate with the cake." Lily felt bad and said sorry. But it was too late. The cake was gone, and they could not celebrate.
