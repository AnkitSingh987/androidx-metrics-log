Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Park

One day, a big, heavy cat named Tom went to the park. He saw his friend, a little mouse named Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his sauce for his yummy food. Tom wanted to help his friend. Tom said, "I will lend you my sauce. But first, we must find it in my house." They went to Tom's house and looked everywhere. They found the sauce under a big toy. Tim was very happy. Tim said, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend." They went back to the park and ate their yummy food with the sauce. From that day on, Tom and Tim always helped each other when they needed it. The moral of the story is to always help your friends when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play by the sea and watch the tide go in and out. One day, he saw a lot of disgusting trash on the beach. He felt sad and decided to do something about it. Tim asked his friend, Sally, to help him. They began to sort the trash into piles. "Look, Sally," said Tim, "we can put the paper here, and the cans there." Sally nodded and said, "That's a good idea, Tim. Let's make the beach clean and pretty again." After they sorted and picked up all the trash, the beach looked nice and clean. Tim and Sally felt happy and proud of their work. They learned that working together, they could make a big change. The moral of the story is that teamwork helps us to do great things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She loved to eat yogurt every day. One day, Sally saw a mysterious box in her room. She was very curious about the box. Sally opened the mysterious box and found a big red button inside. She pressed the button, and a voice said, "Record your wish!" So, Sally wished for more yogurt. The next day, she found a big bowl of yogurt on the table. She was so happy! But then, Sally noticed that her friends didn't have any yogurt. She felt sad for them. So, she pressed the button again and wished for yogurt for all her friends. The next day, everyone had yogurt! Sally learned that sharing with her friends made her even happier than just having yogurt for herself.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was an ordinary dog who loved to play with his ball. One day, Max was playing with his ball in the yard. He was having so much fun. But then, Max lost his ball. It went under the fence and into the woods. Max was sad. He wanted to find his ball. So, he went on an adventure to find it. He walked and walked through the woods. As Max looked for his ball, he saw a big rabbit. The rabbit was trying to escape from a mean fox. Max decided to help the rabbit. He barked and scared the fox away. The rabbit was happy and thanked Max. After helping the rabbit, Max found his ball. He was so happy! He went back home and played with his ball in the yard. Max learned that helping others can make you feel good too. And that was the end of Max's adventure.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big market. Many people went to the market to buy things. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the market with his mom. They saw many colorful things to buy. In the market, there was a harmless dog. The dog was nice and did not hurt anyone. The dog liked to move around and play with the people. Tim saw the dog and wanted to play with it too. Tim's mom told him not to play with the dog because they had to buy things. But Tim did not listen to his mom. He played with the dog, and they moved far away from his mom. Tim got lost and started to cry. A kind man found Tim and took him back to his mom. Tim's mom was happy to see him safe. She told Tim, "Always listen to your mom and dad." From that day, Tim learned to listen to his mom and dad, and they lived happily ever after.
