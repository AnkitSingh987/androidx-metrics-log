Category: Intermediate
Title: The Hidden Park and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a hidden park. In the park, there was a big tree. One day, a little boy named Tim found the park. Tim saw a sign near the tree. The sign said, "Do not climb the tree." Tim wanted to climb the tree, but he tried to resist. He played near the tree but did not climb it. One day, a little girl named Amy came to the park too. Amy saw the sign, but she did not resist. She climbed the tree. Tim was scared for Amy. He thought something bad would happen. But, when Amy climbed the tree, she found a big surprise. There was a tree house hidden in the tree! Tim and Amy became friends and played in the tree house every day. The sign was just a trick to keep the tree house a secret.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a lemon tree in his yard. One day, he saw a careless bird near the tree. The bird was trying to stand on a thin branch, but it kept falling. "Mr. Bird, why don't you stand on a bigger branch?" Tim asked. The bird looked at him and said, "Thank you, I will try." The bird moved to a bigger branch and was able to stand without falling. Tim picked a lemon from the tree and gave it to the bird. The bird was very happy. From that day on, Tim and the bird became good friends. They played together every day and took care of the lemon tree. And they always remembered to be careful and help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful cat named Lily. She lived in a small house with a big closet. Lily loved to play and hide in the closet. One day, she found a pretty hat in the closet. She put the hat on her head and looked at herself in the mirror. Lily wanted to show her friends her new hat. She went outside and saw her friend, Max the dog. Max saw Lily's hat and said, "Wow, that is a beautiful hat!" Lily felt very happy and proud. She bowed to Max to say thank you. Lily and Max played all day in the sun. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. When it was time to go home, Lily took off her beautiful hat and put it back in the closet. She knew that the hat was special and wanted to keep it safe. Every time Lily wore her beautiful hat, she would bow to her friends and they would all smile.

Lily and Ben were going on a holiday with their mom and dad. They were very happy and excited. They packed their bags with clothes, toys and books. Mom told them to wear warm coats and hats, because it was cold where they were going. On the plane, Lily and Ben sat next to a boy and a girl who looked different from them. They had dark skin and curly hair. They spoke a language that Lily and Ben did not understand. Lily and Ben were curious and wanted to talk to them, but the boy and the girl ignored them. They whispered and laughed to each other. Lily and Ben felt sad and angry. They thought the boy and the girl were rude and ignorant. They did not want to be friends with them. They told their mom and dad what happened. Mom and dad listened and smiled. They said, "Lily and Ben, you should not judge people by how they look or what they speak. They are not ignorant, they are just different. They have their own culture and history. Maybe they are shy or scared of you. Maybe they want to be friends, but they don't know how. You should try to be kind and respectful to them. You might learn something new and have fun with them. That is the best way to enjoy your holiday." Lily and Ben thought about what their mom and dad said. They felt sorry for being mean and rude. They decided to give the boy and the girl another chance. They smiled and waved at them. They said, "Hello, we are Lily and Ben. What are your names? Where are you from? Do you want to play with us?" The boy and the girl smiled and waved back. They said, "Hello, we are Ayo and Nia. We are from Nigeria. We are going to visit our grandma. Yes, we want to play with you." Lily and Ben were happy and surprised. They found out that Ayo and Nia liked the same things as them. They liked to draw, read and sing. They shared their toys and books. They taught each other some words in their languages. They laughed and had fun. Lily and Ben learned a valuable lesson. They learned that people are not ignorant, they are just different. They learned that being kind and respectful is the best way to make friends and enjoy their holiday.
