Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yellow Bird Sat on Tree and the Frog

One day, a yellow bird sat on a tree. The bird saw a green frog down by the water. The bird felt envious of the frog because it could swim. The bird wanted to swim too, but it could only fly. The bird flew down and said to the frog, "I wish I could swim like you." The frog whispered back, "I wish I could fly like you." They both felt sad because they wanted to do what the other could do. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the frog into the water. The bird quickly flew to help the frog. The bird grabbed the frog with its feet and lifted it out of the water. The frog was so happy that the bird saved it. They both learned that they were special in their own way, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his football. One sunny day, he went to the park with his mom and dad to play. While playing, Tim saw a big pond. He had an idea. He wanted to sail his football on the water like a boat. So, he threw the ball into the pond and watched it float. Just then, a loud quack came from a duck. The duck swam to the football and pushed it back to Tim. Tim laughed and clapped. He played with the duck and the football all day long.

One day, a little girl named Sue saw dark clouds in the sky. She thought it might rain soon. Sue decided to take her umbrella with her when she went outside to play. She was glad she had the umbrella, just in case. Sue met her friend Tom at the park. Tom looked at Sue's umbrella and said, "Why did you bring that?" Sue told Tom, "I think it will rain soon." Tom laughed and said, "I don't think so." As they played, the dark clouds grew bigger. Then, rain started to fall. Sue opened her umbrella and stayed dry. Tom got all wet. Sue said, "See, I told you it would rain." Tom said, "You were right, Sue. I wish I had an umbrella too." Sue was glad she had decided to bring her umbrella.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to spin around and around. She also loved to eat muffins, especially pink ones. One day, Lily's mom made her a big pink muffin. Lily was very happy and excited. Lily wanted to share her pink muffin with her best friend, Sam. So, she put the muffin in a box and went to Sam's house. On her way, she saw a little bird who looked hungry. Lily thought for a moment and decided to give the bird a small piece of her pink muffin. The little bird was very happy. As Lily continued to walk, she met more animals who looked hungry. Each time, she gave them a small piece of her pink muffin. When she finally reached Sam's house, she opened the box and saw that there was only a tiny piece of the pink muffin left. Lily felt sad that she couldn't share a big piece with Sam. But, to her surprise, Sam was very happy and proud of her. He said, "Lily, you shared your pink muffin with all the animals. That is very kind of you." They both ate the last piece of the pink muffin together and learned that sharing with others is a good thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ava. Ava liked to pray before bed. She prayed for peace and love for everyone. She was a very kind girl. One day, Ava saw a bird who was sad. The bird had no friends to play with. Ava wanted to help the bird find peace and happiness. She thought about how she could make the bird happy. Ava had an idea! She went to the park where many birds were available to play. She brought the sad bird to the park. The sad bird met new friends and started to play. The bird found peace and was happy. Ava was very happy too. She prayed that night, thanking for the bird's happiness.
