Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and make bubbles with her soap. One day, she saw her mom cooking in the kitchen. Sue wanted to help, but she was too small and felt helpless. Sue had an idea. She took her soap and made lots of bubbles in the kitchen. She thought the bubbles would help her mom cook. But, her mom said, "No, Sue, bubbles are not for cooking. They can make the food taste bad." Sue was sad, but she understood. She went to play with her toys in her room. Later, her mom called her back to the kitchen. Sue saw a big pot on the stove. Her mom said, "Be careful, Sue. The pot is hot, and it can burn you." Sue nodded and stayed away from the hot pot. She learned that she could help in other ways, like setting the table for dinner.

Once upon a time, a little rabbit was hopping in the woods. The rabbit was very clean and liked to keep his home tidy. He liked to order his toys and food in neat rows. One day, a big wind came and messed up the rabbit's home. The rabbit was sad because his clean home was now messy. He tried to order his things, but the wind made it hard. The rabbit had an idea. He asked his friend, the bird, for help. The bird flew up high and talked to the wind. The wind felt bad for making the rabbit's home messy. So, the wind blew softly and helped the rabbit put everything back in order. In the end, the rabbit's home was clean and neat again. The rabbit was happy and thanked the wind and the bird. The rabbit, the bird, and the wind became friends and played together in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a generous infant. His name was Jane and he always took care of his baby dolls. One day, Jane was watching his baby dolls from the window when a bird flew into his garden. Jane decided to give the bird some food and water, as he valued it so much. Just as Jane was about to feed the bird, a man in a car stopped in front of the house. The man came out of the car and started to speak to Jane. Man:"I have come here to take your bird away. I will give you some money in exchange." Jane was sad but he accepted the offer and the man drove away with the bird. The man never returned the money he promised and Jane was very sad. The generous infant realized how valuable the bird was to him. He had made a mistake and lost something he should have valued.

Ben and Lily went to the zoo with Mom and Dad. They saw many animals, like lions, monkeys, and birds. But Ben and Lily liked the zebras the best. They had black and white stripes and long tails. "Can we feed the zebras, Mom?" Lily asked. "Sorry, honey, we can't. The zebras have their own food. We can only look at them," Mom said. Ben and Lily looked at the zebras through the fence. They saw one zebra that was very hungry. It was trying to reach some leaves on a tall tree. But the tree was too high for the zebra. The zebra stretched its neck and legs, but it could not get the leaves. "Look, Lily, that zebra is hungry. It wants the leaves," Ben said. "I know, Ben. I wish we could help it," Lily said. They felt sorry for the hungry zebra. They wanted to give it some of their snacks. But they knew they could not. They had to follow the rules of the zoo. "Maybe the zookeeper will come and give the zebra some food," Mom said. "I hope so, Mom. The zebra looks sad," Ben said. Lily nodded. She hoped the zebra would get some food soon. She and Ben waved goodbye to the zebra and went to see the other animals. They hoped the zebra would be happy.

One day, a furry cat named Kitty was very hungry. She did not know what to eat. She walked around her home and found a big pizza on the table. Kitty was so happy! She jumped up and down and said, "Yummy pizza for me!" Kitty took a big bite of the pizza, but it tasted funny. She looked at the pizza and saw a little bug on it. "Oh no!" Kitty said. "I don't want to eat a bug!" The little bug looked at Kitty and said, "Please don't eat me! I am your friend!" Kitty was very surprised. She did not know bugs could talk. She decided to be friends with the little bug. They played together and had fun. From that day on, Kitty and the bug shared the pizza and were the best friends ever.
