Category: Intermediate
Title: Mommy and the Sun

The sun was shining brightly and it was a very nice day. Mommy and Little Girl were walking together. "Let's go to the meeting," said Mommy. Little Girl didn't like meetings, so she was scared. But Mommy gave her a big squeeze and said: "Don't worry. It will be fun." So Little Girl and Mommy went to the meeting. When they arrived, Little Girl saw a big black building. What was inside? Little Girl was scared. Mommy gave her another squeeze and said: "It's going to be ok, Little Girl. Remember, I'm here with you." Little Girl was still scared, but she felt better. Slowly, she walked inside the big black building with Mommy. And from that day, Little Girl wasn't scared of meetings anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a man. The man was scared of dirt. He did not like to get dirty at all. One day, he went for a walk and saw a big puddle. He was very scared to walk near it. The man tried to go around the puddle, but he slipped and fell in the mud. He was so scared and sad. He did not like being dirty. He ran back to his house as fast as he could. When he got home, the man knew what he had to do. He had to wash. He filled a big tub with water and soap. He washed and washed until all the dirt was gone. The man was happy and not scared anymore. He knew that if he got dirty again, he could always wash and be clean.

John was in the park with his mother, when he saw a cow. He was very excited, because he had never seen a cow before. John asked his mother, "Can I go see the cow?" She said yes, so he ran over to it. The cow was very playful. It jumped around and chased its tail in the sunshine. John thought the cow was very funny. He watched the cow for a while and then noticed that the cow had very long hair. John asked, "Can I cut some of the cow's hair?" His mother nodded and gave him some scissors. John carefully cut some of the cow’s hair and took it home to show his father. John was very proud of himself for being brave enough to cut the hair of the playful cow.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play ball. He would run and jump to catch the ball. He knew how to play very well. One day, Spot saw a big, fierce cat. The cat had big, sharp teeth. Spot wanted to play with the cat, but the cat did not want to play. Spot tried to show the cat how to play ball. He threw the ball high in the air. The ball went up and up, like a high pitch. Spot did not know the cat was not nice. The cat jumped and caught the ball. But the cat did not give the ball back. The cat ran away with the ball. Spot was very sad. He lost his ball and did not get to play. The end.

One morning, Mary and her mommy went on a ride. Mary had never seen such a beautiful landscape. Everywhere she looked there were trees and flowers. She couldn't believe how pretty it was. "Mommy," she said, "this place is so good." "Yes," her mommy replied, "it sure is." They kept riding until they reached the top of a hill. Mary was so excited that she started to jump up and down. "Look at that," her mommy said. Mary looked out over the landscape and saw a big lake. The water was so blue and the sun was shining so brightly. "It's so beautiful," Mary said. "Yes, it is," her mommy replied. "Let's take a ride around the lake and enjoy the view." So, Mary and her mommy rode around the lake. It was the best ride ever! Mary couldn't believe how lucky she was to be able to see such a beautiful landscape. When they were done, Mary said, "Mommy, this was a really good ride." Her mommy smiled and replied, "Yes, it was."
