Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Jack and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Jack. He was searching for something, and he just couldn't find it. "Where could it be?" he thought to himself. Jack asked his mom, "Mom, have you seen my toilet?" His mom replied, "No, I haven't seen it - let's go look together." Jack and his mom searched the house. They looked high and low, but still couldn't find it anywhere. Just as Jack was about to give up, his mom had an idea. "Let's look outside, Jack," she said. Jack and his mom went outside and searched around the garden and the shed. Then, in a corner of the garden, Jack noticed something. It was small and flexible - it was his toilet! Jack and his mom hugged each other in delight. Jack shouted, "I found it!" Now Jack had his toilet back, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a popular maze. Many kids loved to play in the maze. They would run, laugh, and have fun. One day, a big storm came and messed up the maze. The kids were sad. A nice man saw the sad kids. He wanted to help. He decided to restore the maze. The nice man worked hard. He fixed the broken parts and made the maze look like new again. Soon, the popular maze was ready for the kids to play in again. When the kids saw the maze, they were so happy. They ran, laughed, and had fun, just like before. The nice man smiled, knowing he had done a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a young dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and have fun. One day, Spot found a bottle of lotion. He sniffed it and it smelled nice. Spot wanted to play with the lotion. Spot used his mouth to lift the bottle of lotion. He carried it to his favorite place, the big tree. Spot wanted to share the nice smell with his friends. He wagged his tail and called his friends over. His friends, a cat and a bird, came to see what Spot had found. They all smelled the lotion and liked it too. They played together under the big tree, happy to share the nice smelling lotion. And they all had a fun day together.

Lila liked to play with her mom's bracelet. It was shiny and had many colors. She wanted to wear it all the time, but her mom said no. She said the bracelet was very old and weak. It could break easily if Lila pulled it or dropped it. One day, Lila saw the bracelet on the table. Her mom was not in the room. Lila thought, maybe I can borrow it for a little while. She took the bracelet and put it on her wrist. She felt very happy and pretty. She ran to the mirror to see how she looked. But on the way, she tripped on a toy and fell down. The bracelet flew off her wrist and hit the floor. Lila heard a loud snap. She looked and saw the bracelet was broken. Some of the beads were loose and some were missing. Lila felt very sad and scared. She knew her mom would be angry and sad too. She heard her mom coming back. She quickly hid the broken bracelet under a pillow. She hoped her mom would not notice. But her mom saw the pillow was not in its place. She lifted it and saw the bracelet. She gasped and asked, "Lila, what happened to my bracelet?" Lila started to cry. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I wanted to borrow it for a little while. But I fell down and it broke. I didn't mean to break it. Please don't be mad at me." Her mom hugged her and said, "Lila, I'm not mad at you. I'm glad you're not hurt. But you should have asked me before you took the bracelet. It was very special to me. It was a gift from my grandma. She gave it to me when I was a little girl like you. It was very old and weak. It could not handle being played with. You should be careful with other people's things. They might have a lot of meaning and value to them. Do you understand?" Lila nodded and said, "I understand, mom. I'm sorry I broke your bracelet. I won't take things without asking again. I love you, mom." Her mom said, "I love you too, Lila. And I forgive you. Maybe we can fix the bracelet together. Or we can make a new one with the beads that are left. What do you think?" Lila smiled and said, "I think that's a good idea, mom. Thank you for being so nice to me." They hugged and kissed and went to find some glue and string. They learned a lesson that day. They learned to respect other people's things and to be honest and sorry when they make a mistake. They also learned to be kind and forgiving and to make the best of a bad situation. They were happy and grateful for each other.
