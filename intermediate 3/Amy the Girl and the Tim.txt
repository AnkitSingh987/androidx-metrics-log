Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Tim

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a toy rod that she loved to play with. One day, she met a boy named Tim who had a big, colorful paper. Tim was a bossy boy who always wanted things his way. Amy showed Tim how to fold the paper to make a fun toy. But Tim didn't want to listen. He wanted to fold the paper his way, even though it didn't look as fun. Amy tried to help, but Tim didn't want her help. He just kept being bossy. In the end, Tim's paper toy didn't work because he didn't listen to Amy. He felt sad and realized that being bossy was not good. Tim learned that listening to others and working together can make things much better.

One day, a gentle cat named Tom found a big blanket. He was very happy. He wanted to show his friend, a dog named Max. Tom went to Max's house with the blanket. "Look, Max! I found a big blanket. We can play with it!" said Tom. Max was excited too. They started to play a game. They would pass the blanket to each other. Tom would throw the blanket to Max, and Max would catch it. Then Max would throw it back to Tom. They were having a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. When Max threw the blanket to Tom, it flew up into a tree. They both looked up and saw a big bird sitting on the blanket. "Oh no! How can we get our blanket back?" asked Tom. The bird heard Tom and decided to help. The bird picked up the blanket with its beak and dropped it down to Tom and Max. They were so happy and thanked the bird. Now they could play with their blanket again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim felt miserable one day because he had no friends to play with. He decided to go to the park to see if he could find someone to play with him. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sally. Sally was trying to pick up a big rope to play jump rope, but it was too heavy for her. Tim walked over to Sally and said, "Can I help you with the rope?" Sally smiled and said, "Yes, please! Thank you!" Together, they picked up the rope and started to play jump rope. As they played, more kids came to join them. Tim and Sally became good friends, and Tim was not miserable anymore. They learned that when you help others, you can make friends and be happy too. And that was the moral of the story: always be kind and helpful to others.

Mum and Jack were on a walk around the park. "Look Jack, see that caterpillar!" Jack was very excited and he ran over to where the caterpillar was. "Hello little caterpillar," said Jack politely. The caterpillar wriggled back and suddenly was gone! "Where did it go Mum?" Jack asked. Mum laughed. "It wriggled away, Jack. Let's see if we can find it." They searched under the trees and around the bushes, but they couldn't find the caterpillar. Suddenly, they spotted it camouflaged on a modern fence. Jack smiled. "We found it!" he shouted. "Let's go and say hello." The caterpillar didn't move as Jack peered closely at him. Mum smiled. "Come on Jack, we have to go home now." Jack waved goodbye to the caterpillar, and the two of them set off for home.

One day, a person named Tom wanted to spend time with his friends. He went to the park where they liked to play. Tom saw his friends playing on a big, round thing. They were having fun. Tom ran to the big, round thing and started to play with his friends. They went round and round very fast. After a while, Tom felt dizzy. He was going round too fast. Tom asked his friends to stop. When the big, round thing stopped, Tom got off. He sat down on the grass with his friends. They laughed and talked. Tom was happy to spend time with his friends, even if he felt dizzy.
