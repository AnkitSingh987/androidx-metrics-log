Category: Intermediate
Title: The Volcano and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, there was a big volcano. The volcano was very tall and lived far away. Many animals lived near the volcano. They were happy and played all day. One day, a deaf rabbit came to the volcano. The deaf rabbit could not hear, but he was very good at playing. He made friends with all the animals. They played hide and seek, and the rabbit always found the best hiding spots. One day, the rabbit played a magic trick. He said, "Watch me vanish!" All the animals watched as the rabbit disappeared. They were amazed. The rabbit came back and they all clapped. They played more games near the volcano and were always happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his uncle. They were having a lot of fun. Tim felt safe with his uncle. They played on the swings, went down the slide, and even played in the sand. Then, Tim saw a big, funny-looking bird. He pointed and said, "Look, Uncle! A funny bird!" His uncle looked and laughed. They watched the bird walk around the park, and it even came close to them. Suddenly, the bird started to talk! "Hello, Tim and Uncle!" it said. Tim and his uncle were very surprised. They didn't know birds could talk. They talked to the bird and had even more fun at the park. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye to their new friend, the talking bird.

---

One day, a cute little cat named Kitty went for a walk. She saw many fun things outside. She saw a big tree, a red ball, and a small pond. Kitty was very happy to play outside. Kitty saw a dark cave and wanted to discover what was inside. She walked slowly into the dark cave. Inside the cave, she heard a small voice say, "Who is there?" Kitty was a little scared, but she said, "I am Kitty, who are you?" The small voice said, "I am Bunny, a cute little bunny." Bunny hopped out from the dark cave. Kitty and Bunny became good friends. They played with the red ball and climbed the big tree together. Kitty was happy she discovered the dark cave and her new friend, Bunny.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a cat named Tom. One day, Tom felt sick and his back hurt. Lily wanted to help Tom, so she gave him a massage. She rubbed his back gently to make him feel better. Tom said, "Thank you, Lily. That feels nice." But then, Tom did a stupid thing. He climbed a tall tree and got stuck. Lily saw him and started to weep. She was sad and scared for her cat. Lily's mom saw her crying and asked, "Why do you weep, Lily?" Lily told her mom about Tom in the tree. Her mom helped her get Tom down. They were happy again. Tom learned not to do stupid things, and Lily learned to help her cat when he needed her.

Lily liked to read books. She had many books on her shelf in her room. She liked to look at the pictures and learn new things. Sometimes she read with her mom or dad, and sometimes she read by herself. One day, she saw a new book on her shelf. It was a big book with a red cover and a picture of a lion. She wondered what it was about. She took it off the shelf and opened it. It had many words and no pictures. She could not read the words. She felt sad and confused. She went to find her mom. She showed her the book and asked, "Mom, what is this book? I can't read it. It has no pictures." Her mom smiled and hugged her. She said, "This is a book for grown-ups. It is a story about a lion who lives in Africa. It is a fine book, but it is not for you yet. You need to learn more words before you can read it. Maybe when you are older, you can read it with me." She put the book back on the shelf and gave Lily another book. It was a small book with a blue cover and a picture of a cat. She said, "This is a book for you. It has pictures and easy words. It is a story about a cat who likes to play. I recommend this book. It is fun and funny. Do you want to read it with me?" Lily nodded and smiled. She liked cats. She said, "Yes, please. I want to read this book with you. Thank you, mom. You are the best." They sat on the couch and read the book together. Lily laughed at the cat's antics and learned some new words. She was happy and proud. She loved to read books.
