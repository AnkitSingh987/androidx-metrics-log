Category: Intermediate
Title: Remy the Rabbit and the Tunnel

One day, a little rabbit named Remy was feeling very sleepy. He yawned and rubbed his eyes. He wanted to find a cozy place to take a nap. As he hopped around, he noticed a tunnel. "This looks like a good place to sleep," Remy thought. He went inside the tunnel and saw a small mouse named Molly. Molly was also feeling sleepy. Remy asked, "Can I sleep here with you?" Molly said, "Yes, let's sleep together!" Remy and Molly curled up close and went to sleep. They had a nice, long nap in the cozy tunnel. When they woke up, they felt happy and not sleepy anymore. They played together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a man who lived all by himself. He was very lonely and he did not have a wife. He was very worried and he got very sad. Then one day, something special happened. He met a lady. She was very nice to him and she said she liked him very much. He liked her too, so he started to mention her to all his friends. He told them about how nice she was and how much he liked her. Soon he started to think about marrying her. He thought it could be a very happy life. He asked her to be his wife and she said yes! He was very happy and no longer worried. They got married, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy doll that she loved very much. The doll was her fake baby, and Lily liked to play mother with it. She would care for the doll, feed it, and put it to sleep. One day, Lily and her mother went to the park. Lily took her fake baby with her. They played in the sun, and Lily's mother pushed her on the swings. Lily held her fake baby tight so it would not fall. When it was time to go home, Lily and her mother walked back to their house. Lily made sure to care for her fake baby all the way home. When they got inside, Lily's mother gave her a hug and said, "You are a very good mother to your fake baby." Lily smiled and knew that she had learned how to care from the best mother of all - her own.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Bo. Bo liked to dig in the yard. One day, he found a shiny gem. Bo wanted to use the gem to buy treats. He was very persistent and kept digging to find more gems. Bo told his friend, a little cat named Mimi, about the gems. Mimi wanted to help Bo find more gems. They both dug and dug, looking for more shiny gems. They were very happy and worked together. But then, something unexpected happened. A big rain came and filled the hole with water. Bo and Mimi could not find any more gems. They were sad, and the hole became a big, wet mess. The gem they found was lost in the water, and they could not use it to buy treats.

Once upon a time, in a calm and quiet place, there was a big, strong man. He had big muscles and loved to help people. One day, he met a little girl who was sad. She told him that her clothes were wet and she did not know how to dry them. The big, strong man wanted to help her. He took her clothes and laid them out in the sun. They waited and watched as the sun dried the clothes. The little girl was happy to see her clothes dry and thanked the man with the big muscles. The man felt good that he had helped the little girl. He learned that being calm and helpful was important. The little girl learned that asking for help was okay. They both learned that when we help each other, we all feel happy.

Once upon a time, there was a long cat named Tom. Tom liked to store his toys in a big box. One day, Tom saw a screen in his house. He had never seen it before. He wanted to play with it. Tom's friend, Sam the dog, came over. "What is that?" asked Sam. "I don't know," said Tom. "Let's find out!" They played near the screen, but they were careful not to touch it. Later, Mom came home. She saw Tom and Sam by the screen. "You found the new TV!" she said. "Let's watch a fun show!" Tom and Sam were happy. They watched a show about a cat and a dog who were friends, just like them.
