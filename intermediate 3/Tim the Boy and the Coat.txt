Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Coat

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a cold day, so he wore a big warm coat. At the park, Tim saw a big ice cream truck. "Mom, can I eat ice cream?" he asked. His mom said, "Yes, but it is cold. Are you sure?" Tim said, "Yes, I want ice cream!" Tim went to the truck and got a big ice cream cone. He took a big bite and it was very cold. His teeth hurt and he did not like it. He looked at his mom and said, "Mom, it's too cold! I don't want it anymore." His mom said, "That's okay, Tim. Next time, we can get a warm snack when it's cold outside." Tim learned that it's better to eat warm food when it's cold and save the cold treats for hot days.

Once upon a time, a playful dog named Buddy lived in a big yard. He liked to run and jump all day. Buddy had a friend named Timmy, a little boy who lived next door. They loved to play together. One day, Buddy and Timmy were playing a game of catch. Buddy would throw the ball and Timmy would run to catch it. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball went over the fence and into the woods. Timmy said, "Oh no, Buddy! The ball is gone. What do we do?" Buddy and Timmy decided to go find the ball. They walked into the woods, but they were scared. The woods were dark and they didn't feel safe. Suddenly, they heard a noise. It was a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "Hi, I am Ben. I found your ball. Here you go!" Buddy and Timmy were so happy and thanked Ben. They went back to their yards and played catch again, feeling safe and happy. And they made a new friend, Ben the bear.

Once upon a time, there was a cute dog named Max. Max was very scared because he had to go to the doctor for an operation. He was shaking and trembling. Max's friend, a little bird named Lily, saw him and asked, "Max, why are you trembling? What's wrong?" Max told her about the operation and how scared he was. Lily had an idea. She told Max, "I will go with you to the doctor. I will hold your paw and sing to you." Max felt better with Lily by his side. At the doctor's office, they found out the operation was just a check-up! Max and Lily laughed, and they were both happy.

Once upon a time, in a green forest, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a big box of gold rocks that she loved to play with. One day, she decided to go for a walk and take her gold rocks with her. As she walked, she met a big frog. The frog said, "Lily, come play with me and give me some gold rocks." Lily thought about it and said, "Okay, but only if you promise to be nice." The frog agreed, and they played together for a while. But soon, the sky turned dark and it started to rain. Lily wanted to go home, but the frog did not want to give back her gold rocks. The frog said, "No, I want to keep them!" Lily was sad and cried as she walked back home in the rain, without her gold rocks.

Once there lived a little girl called Ella. She was only three years old and loved to explore the world around her. One day, Ella was looking out of the window and noticed a spark in the sky. The spark was so small and beautiful, it filled her with hope. She quickly ran outside and tried to catch the spark, but it was too fast for her. Suddenly, a big gray cloud appeared. It was dark and scary, so Ella ran inside and shut the door. She hid under the table and held her teddy bear tight. "Where did that spark go?" Ella asked her teddy bear. "I want to find it so bad," she added. Suddenly, the spark appeared in the room. Ella felt so happy and hugged the spark. "I will never let you go," she said. From that day, Ella never stopped hoping. Every night she looked out the window, in search of that spark. That spark had given Ella hope and showed her that everything will be okay.
