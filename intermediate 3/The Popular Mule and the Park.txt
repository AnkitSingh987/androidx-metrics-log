Category: Intermediate
Title: The Popular Mule and the Park

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a popular mule. This mule was very kind and loved to play with kids. Everyone in the town liked the mule and wanted to be his friend. One day, the mule went to the park to play. He saw many kids playing there too. They were running, jumping, and laughing. The mule was very happy to see them having fun. He joined the kids and played with them all day long. As the sun began to set, it was time for the mule to go home. The kids said goodbye and thanked the mule for playing with them. The mule smiled and walked back to his home, feeling happy and loved. He knew he would go back to the park to play with his friends again soon.

---

Lily liked to pick daisies in the park. She liked their white and yellow colors and their soft petals. She made daisy chains and put them on her head and her doll's head. She felt happy and pretty. One day, she saw a man sitting on a bench. He was bald and looked sad. He had no hair on his head and no smile on his face. Lily wondered why he was so sad. She wanted to make him happy. She walked up to him and said, "Hello, mister. Do you like daisies?" The man looked at her and said, "Daisies? I don't know. I haven't seen a daisy in a long time." Lily said, "I have many daisies. I can supply you with some. Here, take this daisy chain. It will make you look nice and feel happy." She put the daisy chain on his head and smiled. The man was surprised and touched. He said, "Thank you, little girl. You are very kind and sweet. You have made my day." He smiled and hugged her. Lily felt happy and proud. She had made a new friend and helped him feel better. She said, "You're welcome, mister. I'm glad you like daisies. They are my favorite flowers."

---

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Rex, went for a walk. They saw a big slide in the park. Tim said, "Rex, let's go down the slide!" Rex barked and wagged his tail. Tim went up the slide and waited for his turn. When it was his turn, he sat down and zipped down the slide. Rex, being a reliable dog, waited at the bottom for Tim. Tim said, "Wow, that was fun!" Tim and Rex played on the slide all day, taking turns and zipping down. They laughed and barked, having a great time together. When the sun went down, they went home, tired but happy.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot had soft fur and loved to play with his friends. One day, Spot saw a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. Spot said to the bird, "I will help you find your mom." The bird was happy and thanked Spot. They walked together, and Spot would point with his nose to show the bird where to go. They looked everywhere for the bird's mom. At last, they found the bird's mom. She was so happy to see her little one. She thanked Spot for being so helpful. The bird's wing got better, and they all played together. The moral of the story is that it is good to help others when they need it.

Tommy was a humble little boy. He often had to take the elevator to get to his favorite park. One day on his way there, he poured some juice on the floor of the elevator. He was scared and wanted to clean it up, but the elevator was so big that it felt like an impossible task. Suddenly, he saw that a janitor had heard the noise. The janitor quickly grabbed a mop and poured cleaning fluid over the spill. Tommy was so relieved as the janitor quickly mopped up the mess together. When they were done, the janitor gave Tommy a friendly pat on the head and a beaming smile. Tommy was very thankful and went to the park, full of happy thoughts.
