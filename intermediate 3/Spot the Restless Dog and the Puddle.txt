Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Restless Dog and the Puddle

One day, a restless dog named Spot wanted to play. Spot went outside and saw a big puddle. He jumped and splashed in the puddle. Spot was very happy. Spot's mom came outside and said, "Spot, you are dirty! You need to bathe." Spot did not like baths, but he knew his mom was right. He went inside to get clean. While Spot was in the bath, he saw a print on the wall. It was a picture of a cat. Spot was surprised! He did not know that his mom liked cats too. Spot said, "Mom, I did not know you like cats!" His mom smiled and said, "I like all animals, Spot. Now finish your bath and we can play with the cat next door." Spot was excited for a new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a lovely box in his room. It was red and had pretty pictures on it. Tim was very happy. He wanted to show his best friend, Lily, the lovely box. Tim went to Lily's house and knocked on the door. "Lily, come and see what I found!" he said. Lily came out and saw the lovely box. "Wow! That's a nice box, Tim!" she said. They both sat down and opened the box. Inside, they found many colorful toys. Tim and Lily decided to share the toys from the lovely box. They played all day with the toys, and they were very happy. They took turns and made sure each of them got to play with every toy. Sharing the toys made both Tim and Lily feel good, and they knew that they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there lived a bug named Bob. Bob was the bravest bug in the garden. One day, while exploring the garden, Bob came across a soft green leaf. Bob was very excited and he wanted to unite the leaf with his family of bugs. But when Bob tried to unite the leaf he found a tiny creature standing in his way. It was a small beetle! The beetle said firmly, “This leaf is mine!” Bob was determined to unite the leaf with his bug family but he didn’t want to fight. He asked the beetle to cooperate with him. The beetle thought for a moment, then smiled. “Okay,” the beetle said happily, “We can unite the leaf together.” Bob was relieved and they both carried the leaf with their little legs and united it with the bug family. They all cheered and Bob was happy that he could unite the leaf with his bug family without any fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a very creative girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and make up fun games. One day, she found a whip in her toy box. She didn't know what it was, but she thought it looked fun. Lily decided to play a game with the whip. She tried to balance it on her head and walk around the room. It was hard, but she was good at it. She walked and walked, trying not to let the whip fall off her head. Her dog, Spot, watched her and wagged his tail. After a while, Lily got tired of balancing the whip on her head. She thought of a new game. She would use the whip to play with Spot. She waved the whip in the air, and Spot jumped and tried to catch it. They played and played, and they both had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to whistle all day long. He was very happy when he whistled. One day, Bobo saw a big yellow lemon on the ground. He wanted to play with the lemon. Bobo picked up the lemon with his beak. He tried to whistle, but he could not. The lemon was too big in his beak. Bobo felt sad. He wanted to whistle and play with the lemon at the same time. He thought and thought about how to do it. Then, Bobo had an idea. He put the lemon on the ground and pushed it with his foot. Now, Bobo could whistle and play with the lemon. Bobo was very happy. He whistled and played with the lemon all day long.
