Category: Intermediate
Title: The Light Green Parrot and the Tree

Once, there was a light green parrot. The parrot could talk. It lived in a big tree. The parrot liked to play with kids. The kids liked to play with the parrot too. One day, a mom said, "I permit you to play with the parrot." The kids were happy. They ran to the tree to play with the parrot. They played catch and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The parrot said, "I am not a real parrot!" The kids were surprised. The parrot was a toy that could talk. They all laughed and played more.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely bird. This bird lived on a wire. Every day, the bird would look around and see no friends. The bird would sleep on the wire at night. One day, another bird came to the wire. The lonely bird said, "Hello, do you want to be my friend?" The new bird said, "Yes, I would like to be your friend." They played and had fun. At night, they slept on the wire together. But one day, while they were playing, the wire moved! It was not a wire, but a long, friendly snake. The snake said, "Hello, little birds. I like being your wire, but I want to play too!" Now, the lonely bird, the new bird, and the friendly snake were all friends. They played and slept together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had long hair that she loved very much. One day, Sue saw a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly and looked sad. Sue wanted to help the bird. Sue picked up the bird and took it home. She gave it food and water. The bird started to trust Sue. It would sit on her hair and sing songs. Sue's mom said the bird was not dead, but hurt. They would help the bird get better. Days went by, and the bird's wing got better. Sue knew it was time for the bird to go back to its family. She took the bird outside and it flew away. Sue was happy that the bird was not dead and could fly again. The bird would visit Sue sometimes and sit on her hair, singing songs.

One day, a little girl named Amy was very hungry. She went to the fridge to find something to eat. The fridge was big and cold. She saw a note on the fridge. Amy could not read the note. She felt frustrated. Amy went to her mom and said, "Mom, I am hungry. There is a note on the fridge, but I can't read it. Can you help me?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, let's go read it together." They went to the fridge and read the note. The note said, "There are yummy treats in the fridge for you!" Amy and her mom opened the fridge and found a big plate of cookies. They were so happy. They ate the cookies together and had a fun time.

Once upon a time, in a very cold place, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with ice. He would find icy puddles and jump on them to break the ice. One day, Tim found a very big icy puddle. He was so excited to jump on it. As Tim was about to jump, a little bird flew down and said, "Please mind the ice, Tim. It's very cold and slippery. You might fall and hurt yourself." Tim looked at the bird and said, "Thank you, little bird. I will be careful." Tim decided to be very careful and not jump on the icy puddle. Instead, he found some sticks and drew pictures on the ice. He made a picture of the little bird and showed it to her. The bird was very happy and thanked Tim for minding the ice. From that day on, Tim and the little bird became good friends. They played together every day and always remembered to mind the ice. They had lots of fun and stayed safe in the cold, icy place. And they lived happily ever after.
