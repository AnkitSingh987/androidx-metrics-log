One day, a little boy named Tim saw something strange. A hairy ghost appeared in his room. Tim was scared of the ghost. The ghost was big and had long, hairy arms. Tim wanted the ghost to go away. Tim thought of a plan. He would ask the ghost to play a game. If Tim won, the ghost would leave. The ghost agreed to play. They played a game of hide and seek. Tim hid behind a chair, and the ghost looked for him. The ghost found Tim, but Tim was fast. He ran away and hid again. The ghost could not find him this time. Tim won the game. The hairy ghost said goodbye and disappeared. Tim was happy and not scared anymore.

One sunny day, a little dog named Max was very excited. He saw a big ball and wanted to play with it. Max ran around the ball like crazy, having lots of fun. Max's friend, a bird named Lily, came to play too. She landed on Max's shoulder and said, "Let's play together!" Max was happy and wagged his tail. They played with the ball all day long. Max would push the ball with his nose and Lily would fly around it. They laughed and had a great time. At the end of the day, Max and Lily were tired but very happy. They couldn't wait to play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, Mom asked Tim to tie a tight knot on an apron for her. Tim tried his best, but he was not very good at tying knots. Mom smiled and said, "It's okay, we can try again later." The next day, Mom was using a mixer to make a cake. Tim wanted to help, so he held the mixer very tight. Mom was happy with Tim's help, and they mixed the cake together. The cake was yummy, and they shared it with their family. Tim learned that it's okay if he can't do something right away. He knew that if he kept trying, he would get better. Tim felt good about helping his mom and being a good boy. The moral of the story is to never give up and always try to help others.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom liked to follow a pen. His friend, a dog named Max, liked to watch Tom play. One day, Max asked Tom, "Why do you follow the pen?" Tom said, "I like how it moves. It is fun to chase it." Max wanted to try too. So, Tom let Max follow the pen. They both had fun and played all day. From that day on, Tom and Max played with the pen together. And they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a poor driver named Tom. He drove a big, red bus. One day, it rained very hard. The rain made big puddles on the road. Tom's bus had to drive through the puddles. Tom saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was standing by a big puddle. She was sad because her shoes were wet. Tom stopped the bus and said, "Sue, come inside the bus. You can soak your feet and make them dry." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom!" She got on the bus and soaked her feet. The poor driver was happy to help Sue. They became good friends and rode the bus together every day.
