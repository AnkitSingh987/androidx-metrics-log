Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Red Bird and the Stick

One day, a red bird named Bobo was flying in the sky. He gazed at the big trees and the green grass. Bobo was looking for his friend, a tutor named Mr. Wise. Mr. Wise was a kind owl who helped Bobo learn new things. Bobo found Mr. Wise sitting on a tree branch. "Mr. Wise, can you help me find my favorite toy?" asked Bobo. Mr. Wise nodded and said, "Let's look for clues together." They looked around and saw a broken stick on the ground. Mr. Wise told Bobo that this was a clue. As they followed the broken sticks, they saw a big dog named Ruffy. Ruffy had Bobo's favorite toy in his mouth. Bobo was scared, but Mr. Wise said, "Don't worry, I will talk to Ruffy." Mr. Wise asked Ruffy to give back the toy, but Ruffy said, "No, it's mine now!" Bobo was sad, but Mr. Wise had a plan. He told Bobo to find a bigger stick for Ruffy. Bobo found a big stick and gave it to Ruffy. Ruffy was very happy and gave back Bobo's toy. Bobo and Mr. Wise went home, happy that they had solved the problem together.

Once upon a time, in a wild land, there was a big lake. Many animals lived near the lake. One day, a little deer with a big horn came to the lake. The deer was very thirsty. He put his mouth in the water and drank. As the deer drank, a small frog jumped into the lake. "Splash!" went the water. The deer was scared and jumped back. The frog laughed and said, "I am sorry, deer. I did not mean to scare you." The deer with the big horn smiled at the frog. They played near the lake, making big splashes in the water. They had lots of fun together. From that day on, the deer and the frog were the best of friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little girl named Lily. She had a big, red ball that she loved to play with. One day, she lost her ball in the park. She felt very sad. Lily saw a kind, old man who asked, "Are you okay?" Lily nodded and said, "I lost my ball." The old man said, "Let's find it together. I will keep you safe." They looked around the park and found the big, red ball near a tree. Lily was so happy! She thanked the old man and gave him a big hug. From that day on, Lily knew she had a friend to help her feel safe and not so shy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big orange bag. She liked to pack her toys in the bag. Lily took her bag everywhere she went. One day, Lily went to the park. She saw a cocoa tree. The tree had many cocoa pods on it. Lily thought the cocoa tree was very pretty. She wanted to take a cocoa pod home. Lily picked a cocoa pod from the tree. She put the cocoa pod in her orange bag. Then, she went home. Lily showed the cocoa pod to her mom. Her mom was happy. Together, Lily and her mom made hot cocoa from the cocoa pod. They drank the hot cocoa and it was very yummy. Lily loved her adventure with the cocoa tree and her orange bag.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Lucy found a small box in her yard. "What's this?" she asked herself. Lucy wanted to hide the box, so she decided to bury it in the dirt. She dug a hole and put the box inside. "Now it's a secret," she said with a big smile. Later, Lucy's friend Tom came over to play. They were playing catch when Tom tripped and fell on the spot where the box was buried. The box popped out of the ground! Tom looked surprised and said, "Wow, what's this?" Lucy laughed and told him about her secret. They opened the box together and found colorful toy cars inside. They played with the cars, happy that they found a new treasure.
