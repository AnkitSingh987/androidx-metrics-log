Category: Intermediate
Title: The Gray Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat. The gray cat liked to play on the floor. One day, the gray cat found a ball on the floor. The gray cat played with the ball all day. The gray cat's friend, a brown dog, saw the cat playing. The brown dog wanted to play too. The gray cat did not want to share. The gray cat insisted, "No, this is my ball!" The brown dog was sad. He went away to find another toy. The gray cat kept playing with the ball on the floor. The ball rolled away and the gray cat chased it. The gray cat did not see the open door. The gray cat ran outside and got lost. The gray cat was sad and could not find his way home.

Once upon a time, there was a modern doctor named Tom. He had a big bag that he would carry with him to help people. One day, Tom got a call from a mom who said her little boy, Tim, was not feeling well. Tom went to Tim's house to see what was wrong. When he got there, he saw that Tim was very sad. Tim's toy bear was broken! The bear was very important to Tim, and he couldn't sleep without it. Tom wanted to help Tim feel better. So, he took out his big bag and found a way to fix Tim's toy bear. After the bear was fixed, Tim was so happy, and he could sleep well again. Tom was proud that he could help Tim and his toy bear.

One day, a big red bus was driving down the road. The bus was very fair to all of the people who rode it. It would stop and let them on and off whenever they needed. The bus liked to help people. One day, the bus saw a little girl who needed to bathe. She was very dirty and sad. The bus wanted to help her, but it did not know how. The bus tried to think of a way to help the little girl. It wanted to make her happy. Then, the bus had an idea. It went to the big water hose at the bus stop. The bus sprayed water all over the little girl. She was surprised, but then she started to laugh. The water cleaned her, and she was happy. The bus was happy too, because it helped the little girl.

One day, a polite cat named Kitty went to visit her friend, a dog named Buddy. They liked to play and eat snacks together. Today, they decided to make toast for their snack. Kitty put the bread in the toaster and pushed the button down. They waited for the toast to pop up. When it was ready, Buddy said, "Please pass the butter, Kitty." Kitty gave Buddy the butter and he put it on the toast. Suddenly, the door opened and the wind blew in. "Oh no, the door is open!" said Kitty. "We must shut it!" Buddy ran to the door and shut it tight. They were happy the wind was gone. They sat down and enjoyed their toast together.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to pick berries from the bushes near her house. One sunny day, she went to the bushes and saw the most beautiful, red berry she had ever seen. "Wow! This berry must be very special," she thought. Lily picked the berry and brought it home. She showed it to her mom and said, "Look, Mom, I found this pretty berry! Is it expensive?" Her mom smiled and said, "No, dear, it is not expensive, but it is still special because you found it yourself." Lily decided to keep the berry safe. She took a soft cloth and used it to cover the berry. That night, she told her dad about the berry. Her dad listened and said, "Lily, I am proud of you for taking care of the berry. It shows that you value even the little things in life." The next day, Lily shared the berry with her friends. They all enjoyed it together. From that day on, Lily learned that even small things can bring happiness, and it is important to share with others. The moral of the story is: It's not the price or size of something that matters, but the love and care we put into it, and the joy we get from sharing it with others.
