Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Fish and the Swam

One day, a little fish named Bob went for a swim. Bob liked to swim in the deep water because it was safe. He saw many other fish and they all played together. They swam fast, they swam slow, and they had a lot of fun. Bob saw a big fish and said, "Hello, big fish! Do you want to play with us?" The big fish did not say anything. He just looked at Bob and the other fish. Then, the big fish opened his mouth wide and tried to eat Bob's friends. But Bob was brave and said, "No, big fish! You must yield!" The big fish stopped and listened to Bob. He said, "I am sorry, little fish. I was hungry and did not think about being nice." Bob told the big fish that it was important to be kind and share with others. The big fish agreed and they all became friends. From that day on, the big fish learned to share and be kind to the other fish. They all swam together and played in the safe, deep water. The little fish taught the big fish that it was better to yield and be nice, than to be mean and scary. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a loud dog named Max. Max loved to bark all day and all night. He liked to run and play with his red ball. Max had a friend named Tim, who was a little boy. Tim liked to play with Max, but sometimes Max did not behave. One day, Tim and Max went to the park. Tim saw a big ice cream truck. He wanted an ice cream cone. Tim told Max to sit and wait. Max tried to behave, but he was very excited. He jumped up and down and barked very loud. Tim got his ice cream cone and started to eat it. Max saw the cone and wanted some too. He jumped up to get a lick, but he knocked the cone out of Tim's hand. Tim was sad, and Max felt bad. Max knew he needed to behave better. From that day on, Max tried his best to be a good dog and not be so loud. Tim and Max were still the best of friends.

There was a family of four, Mummy, Daddy and two brothers. They went for a walk one sunny afternoon. Little brother was holding Daddy's hand and pointing to all the animals, trees and the sky. He was very excited! Suddenly, he spotted a tall metal box. "What's that?" he asked Daddy. Daddy smiled and told him it was a dishwasher. Little brother was amazed and wanted to take a closer look. So Daddy let him walk up to it and look inside. Little brother could see it was full of different plates and bowls. Daddy said that it was a special machine which washes all the dishes so that they are nice and sparkly, clean and fine. Little brother thought it was the best thing ever! And, the family happily walked on, leaving the dishwasher behind them.

Once upon a time, there was a restless dog named Spot. Spot lived in a big lab with his friend, a kind man named Tom. Tom and Spot liked to play together every day. One sunny day, Tom and Spot went to the park to play. Spot saw a big, round ball. Spot was very excited and decided to kick the ball. He kicked it high into the sky, and it went very far. Tom clapped his hands and laughed. After a long day of playing, Spot and Tom went back to the lab. Spot was tired, but he was happy. They had so much fun kicking the ball in the park. Now, Spot could rest and dream about playing with Tom again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a big, generous bear named Ben. He loved to share his food with his friends in the forest. One sunny day, Ben found a big pile of yummy fruits. He wanted to share them with his friends. Ben's friend, a small bird named Tim, came by and saw the fruits. Tim wanted to help Ben carry the fruits to their friends. But the fruits were very heavy. Tim thought and thought. Then, Tim had an idea! He suggested that they put the fruits in a big bag and carry it together. Ben thought it was a great idea. So, Ben and Tim put the fruits in a big bag and carried it together. They shared the fruits with all their friends in the forest. Everyone was so happy and grateful for the generous bear and his smart friend, Tim. They all enjoyed the yummy fruits and had a fun day together.
