Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to collect things like rocks, leaves, and toys. One day, Tim felt sad because he had no one to play with. He decided to go for a walk to find something new to collect. As Tim walked, he saw a pizza shop. The pizza smelled so good that it made him very hungry. Tim thought, "Maybe I can collect pizza!" He went inside the shop and asked for a slice. The kind man gave him a slice of yummy pizza. Tim took the pizza home and showed it to his mom. She smiled and said, "That's a funny thing to collect, but it's also a tasty snack!" They shared the pizza and Tim was not sad anymore. From that day on, Tim collected happy memories with his mom and friends.

One day, a helpful dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a wide hole in the ground. Max wanted to jump over the hole, so he tried very hard. He ran fast and jumped high, but he fell into the hole. In the hole, Max found a big red ball. He tried to push the ball out of the hole. He pushed and pushed, but the ball was too big. Max was sad. Then, a big bird saw Max in the hole. The bird tried to help Max. The bird grabbed the red ball with its beak and pulled it out of the hole. Max was happy! The bird and Max became friends. They played with the red ball all day.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cone. He took it everywhere he went. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He brought his cone with him. While playing, he put the cone on the ground and something fun happened. A little dog came running and thought the cone was a toy too! The dog picked up the cone in his mouth and started to run. Tim laughed and chased after the dog. They played a game of catch with the cone. Tim and the dog had lots of fun together. In the end, Tim and the dog became good friends. They played with the cone every day at the park. It was no longer an ordinary cone, but a special toy that brought them lots of joy.

Once upon a time there was an oak tree which stood very high. A little girl loved playing around the tree. Every night she would go to the oak tree and talk to it. One night, the little girl said to the oak tree, "I wish I could climb you!" The oak tree replied, "I'm sorry, I'm too high to let you climb me. You'll fall." The little girl felt disappointed but she refused to give up. She told the oak tree, "I'm sure if I try, I can make it to the top!" The oak tree said nothing and the little girl went away. Every night she kept returning to the oak tree and each time she told it, "I still think I can make it to the top of you." One night, the oak tree replied, "I believe you can, if you try very hard." The little girl was so excited and she started to climb the oak tree. She worked very hard and with a lot of effort, she finally reached the top. The little girl was so proud of herself and shouted with joy. It turns out the oak tree was just waiting for her to finally refuse to give up. After that, it became the little girl's favourite spot. From that day on, the little girl and the oak tree were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a kind bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day long. He would hop in the grass, hop in the dirt, and hop with his friends. One day, he saw a big house. The house had a fireplace. Bob wanted to see the fireplace, so he hopped inside the house. He hopped around the room and saw the fireplace. It was big and warm. He thought it would be fun to hop near the fireplace, so he did. As Bob hopped closer to the fireplace, he did not see a toy on the floor. He tripped on the toy and fell into the fireplace! The fire was hot and Bob got hurt. He hopped out of the fireplace and ran away. Bob learned that hopping near fireplaces is not safe.
