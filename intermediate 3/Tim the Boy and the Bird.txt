Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a wild bird on a metal fence. The bird was trying to balance on the fence. Tim thought it was funny and wanted to talk to the bird. "Hi, bird! Can you balance well?" Tim asked. The bird looked at Tim and said, "Yes, I can balance very well. Watch me!" The bird then hopped on one leg and balanced on the metal fence. Tim was amazed. Tim tried to balance like the bird, but he could not do it. The bird saw Tim and said, "Don't worry, Tim. Keep trying and you will learn to balance too." Tim practiced every day and soon he could balance just like the wild bird on the metal fence. They became good friends and played together at the park all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to count. She would count everything she saw. One day, her mommy made a fun quiz for her. Sue was very happy. In the quiz, there were many things to count. Sue counted five red balls, three blue birds, and two yellow cats. She felt sorry for the cats because they looked sad. Sue wanted to help the cats feel better. Sue finished the quiz and gave the cats a big hug. The cats were not sorry anymore. They were happy. Sue's mommy was proud of her. Sue learned that counting and helping others can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom loved to solve problems. One day, while playing with his friends, they found a big arrow on the ground. Tom and his friends did not know where the arrow came from. They were very curious. They decided to follow the arrow to see where it would lead them. As they walked, they found more arrows on the ground. After following the arrows for a while, they reached a big tree. In the tree, they saw a cat that was stuck and scared. Tom and his friends wanted to help the cat. They worked together to solve the problem. They found a long stick and used it to help the cat down from the tree. The cat was very happy, and Tom and his friends felt proud. They had solved the problem and saved the cat.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was an amazing girl. She loved to play with her friends in the park. One day, she saw a cute cat. The cat gave Amy a wink. Amy was very happy. Amy wanted to invite her friends to meet the cat. She ran to her friends and said, "I found a cat! Let's go see it!" Her friends were excited. They all went to the park to see the cat. When they got to the park, the cat was still there. It gave them all a wink. The kids laughed and played with the cat. They had a lot of fun. The cat was their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy car that she loved to play with every day. But one day, her toy car broke. Sue was very sad and wanted to repair her toy car. Sue's dad saw her sad face and said, "Don't worry, Sue. We will fix your toy car." They went to the table and saw the car was only an inch away from being fixed. They had to put a small part back in its place. But it was difficult because the part was so tiny. Sue and her dad tried and tried to repair the toy car. They did not give up even when it was difficult. Finally, they fixed the toy car! Sue was so happy and thanked her dad. They played with the toy car together, and Sue learned that even difficult things can be done with a little help.

Sara likes to help her mom with the furniture. She likes to move the chairs and the tables and the books to make the room look nice and organized. She knows where everything goes and she likes to hear her mom say "good job, Sara!" One day, Sara hears a loud noise from the living room. She runs to see what it is. She sees a big mess on the floor. The furniture is all over the place. The chairs are upside down, the tables are broken, the books are torn. Sara is scared and sad. She wonders what happened. She hears her mom come in. She sees her mom's face. Her mom is angry and surprised. She asks Sara what she did. Sara says she did not do anything. She says she was helping with the furniture and then she heard a loud noise. She says she does not know what happened. Her mom looks around. She sees something move behind the couch. She goes to check. She finds a big dog. The dog is wagging its tail and licking its lips. The dog looks happy and guilty. Her mom realizes the dog came in from the open window and made the mess. She is not angry at Sara anymore. She is angry at the dog. She tells the dog to go away. She closes the window. She hugs Sara and says she is sorry. She says she knows Sara did not do anything. She says they will clean up the mess together. She says they will make the room look nice and organized again. She says she loves Sara. Sara smiles and says she loves her mom. She says she likes to help her mom with the furniture. She says she does not like loud noises or big dogs.
