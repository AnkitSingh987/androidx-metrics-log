Category: Intermediate
Title: The Zebra and the Lake

Once there was a zebra. He was a nice zebra, but he was very weird. Everywhere he went people would get scared of him and yell at him. He didn't understand why, but he kept walking anyway. One day, he was walking through the forest and he saw a beautiful lake. He wanted to go for a swim, but when he got to the lake the other animals were scared and started to yell at him. He was so sad he couldn't enjoy the lake. He tried to explain that he just wanted to have a nice swim, but the other animals wouldn't listen. They kept yelling and eventually chased him away from the lake. The zebra was very sad. He had never felt so alone. The zebra decided to never go back to the lake again. He was so hurt by the way the other animals had treated him. He wished the animals could have been nicer, but in the end, he had a bad ending.

One day, a little bird was very thirsty. He wanted to drink some water. So, he flew to the river to find some. When he got there, he saw a big, frustrated cat. The cat was trying to catch a fish, but he couldn't. The little bird felt bad for the cat and wanted to help. "Mr. Cat," said the little bird, "I can help you catch a fish if you promise not to eat me." The cat looked at the bird and thought about it. "Okay," he said, "I promise I won't eat you." So, the little bird flew down to the water and caught a fish for the cat. The cat was very happy and thanked the bird. The cat and the bird became friends. They played together every day and drank water from the river. They were both very happy. One day, while they were playing, a big storm came. The sky turned dark and the wind blew hard. The river started to flood, and the cat and the bird were scared. But then, the storm stopped, and the sun came out. The river became calm again, and there was peace. The cat and the bird were very happy and thankful for their friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a cow. The cow liked to swing. Every day, the cow went to the park to swing. One day, the cow went to the park, but the swing was empty. The cow was sad. Where was the swing? The cow looked around and saw a big tree with a rope. The cow went to the tree and saw that the rope was the swing! The swing was not gone, it was just in a new place. The cow was happy and played on the swing all day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was an icy cold day, but Tim wanted to make a snowman. He put on his warm coat and hat and went out to have fun. He saw his friend, Sam, who wanted to play too. While they were making the snowman, they saw a big pile of waste near their snowman. Tim and Sam were sad because the waste made their snowman look bad. They wanted to find out who did it and fix the problem. Tim and Sam asked their friends, and they found out that a big boy named Max had made the waste. They went to Max and told him it was not nice to make waste near their snowman. Max felt bad and said sorry. He helped them clean up the waste and make their snowman look nice again. In the end, Tim, Sam, and Max learned that it is important to keep their place clean and not make waste. They also learned that when you do something wrong, you should say sorry and fix it. This way, everyone can have fun together.

There was a normal boy named Tim. Tim liked to study. Every day, he would study with his books and learn new things. One day, while he was studying, his friend Sam came to play. "Tim, let's play ball!" said Sam. But Tim wanted to study more. "No, I want to study now. We can play later," Tim told Sam. Sam was sad, but he waited for Tim. Later, they played ball and had fun. Tim was happy he studied first, and Sam learned that sometimes it is good to wait. The moral of the story is to be patient and take turns.
