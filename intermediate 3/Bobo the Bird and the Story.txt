Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Bird and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high in the sky. One day, Bobo saw another bird and wanted to fly higher than him. Bobo felt competitive. "Let's see who can soar higher!" said Bobo to the other bird. They both started to fly up, up, up. Bobo's spirit was strong, and he wanted to win. But the other bird was also very good at flying. As they flew higher, the sky became dark, and it was hard to see. Bobo and the other bird did not know where they were going. They got lost and could not find their way back home. The two birds learned that being too competitive can lead to bad things. They wished they had just played together and had fun instead of trying to be better than each other. The moral of the story is that it is better to have fun and be friends than to always try to win.

One sunny day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She saw a small, lost kitten under a big tree. The kitten was sad and scared. Mia wanted to help the kitten feel better. Mia walked up to the kitten and said, "Hi, little kitten! I am Mia. Do you want to be my friend?" The kitten looked at Mia and gave a soft "meow." Mia smiled and picked up the kitten. She gave it a tight hug. The kitten felt warm and safe in Mia's arms. Mia held the kitten's paw in her palm. They walked back to Mia's house together. When they got home, Mia's mom saw the kitten and said, "Oh, what a cute kitten!" Mia told her mom all about how she found the kitten and how they became friends. Mia, her mom, and the kitten all hugged each other. They were a happy family.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Helen. Helen loved dressing up, wearing pretty clothes and playing make-believe. One day, Helen found a special pair of shoes in her wardrobe. They were made of soft leather and had shiny buttons. They looked magical. So Helen put the shoes on straight away and began to dance around her bedroom. As she danced, she spun faster and faster and soon she began to feel dizzy. Just then, Helen's mummy walked in and saw her twirling around. "Oh Helen, those shoes are so pretty!" said her mummy. "Be careful not to get too dizzy!" Helen laughed, and kept on dancing, feeling happier than ever. She loved wearing the leather shoes and she never felt so magical.

Ben and Lily wanted to play dress up. They found some old costumes in the attic. Ben put on a pirate hat and a sword. Lily wore a fairy dress and a wand. They pretended to be on a ship, sailing to a magic island. "Arr, matey, I see the island ahead!" Ben said. "There must be treasure there!" "Maybe, but we have to be careful. There might be monsters, too!" Lily said. She waved her wand and made some sparkles. They climbed down the stairs and went to the backyard. They saw a big tree that looked like a ship. They ran to it and climbed on the branches. But they did not see the dog next door. The dog was big and loud. He did not like strangers. He saw Ben and Lily and barked. He jumped over the fence and ran to the tree. "Roof, roof, roof!" The dog said. He wanted to catch them. Ben and Lily were scared. They did not know what to do. They looked for a way to escape. "Quick, jump to the swing!" Ben said. He saw a swing hanging from another branch. He grabbed his sword and swung to it. "Help, I can't reach it!" Lily said. She was too small. She held her wand and hoped for a miracle. Then, they heard a voice. It was their mom. She came out of the house and saw the dog. She was compassionate. She did not want the dog to hurt them. "Hey, boy, calm down. It's okay. They are just playing." She said. She walked to the dog and petted him. She gave him a treat and a toy. The dog stopped barking. He wagged his tail and licked her hand. "Mom, you saved us!" Ben said. He jumped off the swing and hugged her. "Mom, you are a fairy godmother!" Lily said. She flew down from the tree and kissed her. Mom smiled and hugged them. She looked at their costumes and laughed. "You two are very creative. But next time, be careful of the dog. He is not a monster, but he is not a friend, either." She took them inside and gave them some cookies and milk. They told her about their adventure. They were happy and safe. They learned a lesson. They did not need a magic island. They had a magic mom.
