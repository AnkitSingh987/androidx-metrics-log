Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Tree

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play outside in the mud. They make mud pies and mud castles. They get very dirty and messy. "Time to bathe, Lily and Ben!" Mom calls from the house. "You have to be clean for dinner. We have rice and chicken." Lily and Ben groan. They do not want to bathe. They want to play more. They run away from Mom. They hide behind a big tree. "Where are Lily and Ben?" Mom asks. She looks around. She sees their muddy footprints. She follows them to the tree. "Boo!" Lily and Ben shout. They jump out from behind the tree. They try to scare Mom. But Mom is not scared. She is angry. "Lily and Ben, that is not funny. That is dangerous. You could get hurt or lost. You have to listen to me. Now come on, let's go bathe." Mom says. Lily and Ben feel sorry. They know Mom is right. They say sorry to Mom. They hold her hand. They go to the house. They bathe in the tub. They wash off the mud. They put on clean clothes. They smell nice. They go to the kitchen. They see the rice and chicken on the table. They are hungry. They say thank you to Mom. They eat their dinner. They are happy. They hug Mom. They love Mom.

One day, two crickets were happily playing together in the sun. Then, a big gust of wind came and separated them. One of the crickets was blown far away and the other one stayed where it was. The cricket that didn't get blown away tried to find its friend. It hopped over trees and through fields, but no matter how hard it tried, it couldn't find its friend. It felt very uncomfortable and sad. The cricket kept looking for its friend until it was dark. But it was too late. The two crickets were never reunited again. They were separated forever.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a garden with many plants. One day, she found a small vine that was broken. Lily wanted to fix it. Lily went to her dad and said, "Dad, can you help me fix the small vine?" Her dad smiled and said, "Yes, let's go outside and fix it together." They took some string and went to the garden. Lily and her dad tied the broken vine with the string. They watched as the small vine grew bigger and stronger. Soon, the vine had many pretty flowers. Lily and her dad were very happy. They hugged and said, "We did it! We fixed the small vine!"

Once upon a time, there was a queen who lived in a big castle. She was very excited because today was the day of the big ball. All her friends were coming to dance and play. The queen had a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with a red ball. He would kick it with his little legs and run after it. The queen told Spot, "You can play with your ball, but be careful not to kick it too hard." Spot was playing with his ball in the garden when he kicked it very hard. The ball flew into the sky and landed in the big ballroom. All the people at the party stopped and looked at the ball. The queen walked over and picked up the ball. She smiled and said, "I knew this would happen, Spot. But that's okay. Now we can all play together!" Everyone laughed and started to kick the ball around the room. The queen, Spot, and all their friends had a fun and exciting day.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a deer. The deer was very happy and liked to play with its friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. The forest was full of fun and life. One day, an unknown big animal came to the forest. It was so big and scary that all the little animals ran away to hide. The big animal started to destroy the trees and the homes of the little animals. The deer and its friends were very sad and scared. The brave deer decided to talk to the big animal. It walked up to the big animal and said, "Please stop destroying our forest. We all want to live here and be happy." The big animal looked at the deer and felt sorry for what it had done. It promised to never destroy the forest again. The deer and its friends were happy and thanked the big animal. From that day on, they all lived together in the big green forest, and they played and laughed every day.
