Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a new ball to play with. She was very happy. Lily loved to play with her new ball on the porch. She would throw it and catch it all day. One day, a little boy named Timmy walked by. He saw Lily playing with her new ball. Timmy stared at the ball because he wanted to play too. He felt sad because he did not have a ball. Lily saw Timmy staring and felt bad. She asked Timmy if he wanted to play with her. Timmy smiled and said yes. They played with the new ball together on the porch. They had lots of fun. Soon, Lily and Timmy became good friends. They learned that sharing is good. When you share, you make others happy and you feel happy too. And that is the moral of the story: sharing makes everyone happy.

One day, Tim found a large coin in his room. He did not know where it came from. He was very happy to find it. He thought about what he could buy with the coin. He wanted to buy a big toy car. Tim went to the shop with the large coin. He showed the coin to the shop man. The shop man looked at the coin and said, "This is a special coin. You can't buy a toy car with this coin. You can buy a real car!" Tim was very surprised. Tim went to the car shop with the large coin. He showed the coin to the car man. The car man smiled and said, "Here is your car!" Tim got a real car for the large coin. He was very happy and could not believe it. He thought it was a dream, but it was true.

Once upon a time, there was a little house with a big chimney. In the house lived a noisy bird named Chirpy. Chirpy loved to sing all day long. One day, it started to rain. The rain was very heavy, and the water began to soak the chimney. Chirpy's friend, Squeaky the mouse, came to visit. "Chirpy, your chimney is wet! Let's fix it together," said Squeaky. Chirpy and Squeaky worked hard to fix the chimney and keep the water out. After they fixed the chimney, they were both very tired. "Thank you, Squeaky," said Chirpy. "You are a good friend." They learned that working together made things easier and more fun. And from that day on, Chirpy and Squeaky always helped each other when they needed it.

---

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to eat rice. One day, he found some rice in a big bowl. He was very happy and wanted to eat it all. But this rice was bitter. Tom did not like bitter rice. He wanted to surprise his mom by making the rice taste good. So, Tom went to find some sweet things to put in the rice. He found some honey and mixed it with the rice. Tom gave the rice to his mom. She was very surprised and happy. She said, "Tom, you are a good boy for trying to make the rice taste better. We should always try to make things better for others." And they all ate the rice together, and it tasted good. The moral of the story is to always try to help others and make things better for them.

One day, a girl named Lily wore her favorite skirt. It was a sunny day, but the wind was harsh. Lily wanted to play outside with her friends. She asked her mom, "Can I go out and play?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful with your skirt." Lily went outside and played with her friends. They ran, jumped, and laughed. But then, something unexpected happened. Lily fell into a big puddle of mud. Her skirt was all wet and dirty. Lily was sad and went home. Her mom saw the dirty skirt and said, "Let's wash it and dry it." They washed the skirt and hung it outside to dry. The wind blew the skirt away into the sky. Lily and her mom tried to catch it, but it was gone. Lily was very sad because she lost her favorite skirt.
