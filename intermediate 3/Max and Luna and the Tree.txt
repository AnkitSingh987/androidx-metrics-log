Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and Luna and the Tree

One day, a dog named Max went to visit his friend, a cat named Luna. Max wore a thick, blue collar that he liked a lot. Luna lived in a small house with a big tree. When Max got to Luna's house, he saw her sitting under the tree. "Hi Luna," said Max. "Do you like my thick collar?" Luna looked at Max's collar and said, "Yes, Max. It is very nice." They played together all day. They ran, jumped, and had a lot of fun. Max and Luna were happy to visit each other. They knew they were best friends.

Daniel and his dad were walking in the park when they spotted a squirrel. "Look at this squirrel, Daniel!" Dad said, pointing to the animal. "He's very fast!" Daniel was laughing and wanted to get closer. He started running to the squirrel, but it quickly ran away. Dad laughed, too, and gave him a hug. "Let's try again," Dad said. "This time, you have to be gentle and quiet, okay?" Daniel understood and nodded. He and Dad walked slowly towards the squirrel and got really close. But just as they were about to touch it, the squirrel ran away again. "What can we do now?" Dad asked. Daniel thought for a little bit and then said, "Let's rub a stick on the ground and make the squirrel come back." Dad was amazed and suggested that they try it. Daniel took a branch from the ground and started rubbing it. Suddenly, the squirrel came back. Daniel was very happy and said, "It worked!" Dad smiled and said to him, "You're very smart".

Once there was a shy little girl. She liked to move around and explore new places. One day she went to a big, wide grassy field. She saw all kinds of animals, bugs and plants. She was admiring them all when she heard a buzzing noise. It was coming from a mosquito. She was scared but curious. She watched it as it moved around in the sky and then slowly moved closer to her. She got shy again and decided to move away to a safer place. Quickly, the little girl ran to a nearby tree and climbed up. She could see the mosquito fluttering around in the sky from where she was. It was too far away to bother her. She smiled and continued to carefully explore the tree, admiring its beauty and the many wonders of nature. She was happy to have moved away from the mosquito.

Tim and Sam were friends who liked to play at the park. They liked to go on the slide, the swing, and the seesaw. One day, they saw a big box near the slide. They wondered what was inside. "Let's search the box!" Tim said. "Maybe there is something fun!" "OK!" Sam said. They ran to the box and opened it. Inside, they saw many colorful balls. They were happy and started to throw the balls at each other. But they did not see that the box had a sign on it. The sign said: "Do not touch. These balls are for the clown show." Soon, a man came to the park. He was wearing a funny hat, a red nose, and big shoes. He was the clown. He was looking for his box of balls. He saw Tim and Sam playing with them. "Hey, you stupid kids!" he shouted. "Those are my balls! Give them back!" Tim and Sam were scared. They dropped the balls and ran away. They hid behind the slide. "Sorry, mister!" they said. "We did not know. We just wanted to play." The clown was angry. He picked up his balls and put them back in the box. He closed the box and locked it. He took the box and left the park. Tim and Sam felt bad. They learned a lesson. They should not touch things that are not theirs. They should ask before they search. They should respect other people's things. They said sorry to each other and went back to play on the slide. They decided to be more careful and polite next time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was weak and often tired. One day, Tim found a letter on the ground. He picked it up and saw a picture of a big, strong man on it. Tim wanted to be like the man in the picture, so he showed the letter to his mom. "Mom, look at this letter!" said Tim. "Can I be like the man in the picture?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, but you need to eat well and exercise to get strong." The next day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw the big, strong man from the letter. The man was kneeling on the ground, looking sad. Tim went over to him and asked, "Why are you sad?" The man looked up and said, "I lost my letter with my mom's picture on it." Tim was surprised and showed the man the letter he found. The man was happy and hugged Tim. They both learned that strength comes from love and caring for others.
