Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Computer

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy computer. He liked to play with his computer every day. But one day, Tim thought his computer was boring. He wanted to make it more fun. Tim had an idea. He went to the kitchen and found a big jug of water. He thought, "If I pour water on my computer, it will be more fun!" So, Tim poured water all over his computer. But, oh no! The computer stopped working. Tim was sad. He learned that pouring water on a computer was not a good idea. From that day on, Tim played with his other toys and never thought they were boring again.

One day, a boy named Tom went for a jog in the park. Tom liked to jog because it made him feel good. As he jogged, he saw his friend, Lily, playing with a toy car. Tom stopped jogging and went to play with Lily. Lily said, "Tom, look at my toy car! It can go very fast!" Tom looked at the car and thought it was very useful. They played with the toy car and had a lot of fun. But then, the toy car hit a big rock and it broke. "Oh no!" said Lily, "My toy car is broken!" Tom wanted to help Lily. He found some glue and fixed the toy car. Lily was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom! You are very useful!" They played with the toy car and continued to have a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little lion named Leo. Leo loved to roar very loud. He thought that if he roared loud, he would win every game. He felt the victory would be his. One day, Leo met an interesting fox named Fifi. Fifi was very smart and fast. They decided to play a race game. Leo roared loud, thinking he would win. But Fifi was faster and won the race. Leo felt sad, but Fifi told him that it's not just about roaring loud. It's about trying your best and being a good friend. Leo learned that being kind and working hard is more important than just roaring loud for victory. And they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a dark room. In the room, there was a faucet. The faucet was sad because it could not spread water. It wanted to help people wash their hands, but it could not. One day, a little girl named Lucy came into the room. She wanted to wash her hands. Lucy tried to turn on the faucet, but no water came out. "Why are you not working, faucet?" she asked. The faucet wanted to tell her, but it could not talk. Lucy went to her mom and said, "Mom, the faucet in the dark room is not working." Her mom went to the room and saw the faucet. She knew what to do. She fixed the faucet so it could spread water. The faucet was so happy. Now, Lucy and her friends could wash their hands in the dark room. The faucet was no longer sad. It could spread water and help people. And everyone was happy.

One day, a little beetle went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The beetle was very rude. He did not say "please" or "thank you" when he met other bugs. The little beetle found a leaf on the tree. He wanted to wrap it around himself to stay warm. But the leaf was too high for him to reach. He asked a friendly ant for help. The ant tried to help the beetle, but the beetle was rude again. The ant did not like this and went away. Then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew the leaf down to the ground. The little beetle was happy and wrapped himself in the leaf. He learned that being rude was not good. From that day on, the beetle said "please" and "thank you" to all the bugs he met. And all the bugs liked the little beetle.

The clever cat wanted to sell something. He found a bin and started to put his things in it. He thought he could make some money if he sold it. The cat put his toys in the bin and waited for a customer. After a long wait, a person came and asked him if he wanted to buy his things. He was so happy that someone wanted to buy his things. However, when the cat went to get the things from the bin, he was shocked. The buyer had taken everything from the bin! He had been fooled! The cat was very sad because he had lost all his things. He had been very clever, but not clever enough to realise that the buyer was going to trick him. He had been sold out!
