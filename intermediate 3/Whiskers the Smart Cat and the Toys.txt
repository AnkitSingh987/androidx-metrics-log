Category: Intermediate
Title: Whiskers the Smart Cat and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Whiskers. Whiskers lived in a big house with his best friend, a little girl named Lucy. They loved to play and have fun together. One day, Lucy's mom told her they needed to go to the store on Wednesday. Lucy was excited because she loved going to the store. She asked Whiskers if he wanted to come along, and he meowed happily. On Wednesday, Lucy and Whiskers went to the store with Lucy's mom. They saw lots of yummy food and fun toys. Whiskers was very smart and helped Lucy find everything they needed. When they were done, they went home and had a big, fun dinner with all the food they got from the store.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a thin musician. He loved to play songs for everyone. He had a big, shiny trumpet that he played all day. One day, while he was playing, a little girl came up to him and said, "Mister, I like your music. Can you play more?" The musician smiled and played more songs for the little girl. They were both happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The thin musician started to gain size! He grew bigger and bigger until he could not play his trumpet anymore. The little girl looked at him and said, "Don't worry, Mister. I will help you." She ran to find help and came back with a magic wand. She waved the wand, and the musician became thin again. They both laughed and played music together, and everyone in the town danced and clapped.

One day, a zebra named Zee was walking in the mud. He got very filthy. Zee saw his friend, a bird named Bee. Bee was flying and saw Zee in the mud. "Zee, you are so filthy!" Bee said. Zee looked at his legs and saw the mud. "Oh no! I need to be clean," Zee said. Bee had an idea. "Let's go to the river!" Bee said. Zee and Bee went to the river. Zee jumped in the water and washed the mud off. He was clean again. Zee was happy and started to clap. "Thank you, Bee!" Zee said. Bee smiled and clapped too. They played by the river and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and share them with her friends. One day, she found a big sheet in her room. Lily had an idea to make something fun with the sheet. Lily called her friend Tom and said, "Come over! I have a big sheet. We can make something fun and play together." Tom was excited and ran to Lily's house. When he got there, they both looked at the sheet and thought of what they could make. Then, Lily had a great idea. They used the big sheet to make a tent. They put the sheet over a table and crawled inside. It was so much fun! They played in the tent all day long, sharing their toys and laughing together. In the end, they were happy they had the big sheet to make their fun day even better.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. One day, it was a bitter cold night. Tim was in his warm bed, but he could not sleep. He felt scared of the dark. Tim called out, "Mom, I am scared of the night!" His mom came into his room and sat next to him. She said, "Don't be scared, Tim. The night is just a time for us to sleep and rest." "But I heard someone mention a big monster in the night!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "That's just a story, Tim. There are no monsters in the night." Tim felt better after talking to his mom. He closed his eyes and went to sleep. The next morning, Tim woke up happy and not scared of the night anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. Tom loved to ride the big bus every day. One day, Tom went to the bus stop to wait for the bus. While waiting, Tom saw a small bird. The bird sang a beautiful song. Tom decided to listen to the bird. He closed his eyes and enjoyed the song. The bird's song made Tom smile. Suddenly, Tom heard the sound of the bus leaving. He opened his eyes and saw the bus driving away. Tom had missed the bus because he was listening to the bird's song! But Tom was not sad. He was happy because he had heard the beautiful song. From that day on, Tom always took time to listen to the birds and enjoy the simple things in life.
