Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bear and the Woods

Once upon a time there was a young bear. He was anxious because he was lost and alone in the woods. He had no idea how he got there or how he would find his way home. Suddenly, he heard something rustling in the bushes. He was scared, so he bowed his head and stayed still. "Who's there?" he asked in a small, scared voice. From the bushes appeared a giant grizzly bear. He said in a deep voice, "I'm the king of these woods. Who are you?" The bear was so scared he could hardly speak. He tried to explain but the words wouldn't come out. The grizzly bear growled menacingly and said, "You must be punished for trespassing in my woods!" The young bear was very anxious, but he knew he must stay brave. So he bowed his head and waited for the grizzly bear's punishment. Suddenly, the grizzly bear roared and charged at the young bear. The young bear ran and ran as fast as he could, but the grizzly bear was too fast. In a few moments, the grizzly bear had caught up to the young bear and was ready to attack. The young bear closed his eyes and braced himself, but it was too late. The grizzly bear pounced on him and the young bear was never seen again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a famous pilot. This pilot flew a big plane in the sky. He loved to fly and see the clouds. One day, the plane broke. The pilot was sad. He wanted to fix the plane so he could fly again. He looked for help from his friends. His friends came to help him fix the plane. They worked together and soon, the plane was fixed. The famous pilot was happy and flew the plane high in the sky with a big smile on his face.

One day, Sam's Mommy brought him a recorder. She showed Sam how to make sounds with it and he thought it was soooo fun! He practiced making different noises and then he made a recording of Mommy talking. He was so excited to hear his Mommy's voice coming out of the recorder. Mommy said, "Let's go pick some butter!" Sam shouted, "Yes!" as they headed out the door and down the street. On their way they laughed and talked and Sam noticed how lively the place was. At the market, they found the butter and Sam held it tight. Mommy said, "Let's go home and make a delicious treat together!" Once they got home, they used the butter to make the most delicious pancakes! They laughed and enjoyed each other's company, and Sam was so excited to hear his Mommy's voice on the recording. He thought to himself, "I'm so lucky to have such a lively Mommy to have fun with!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tim loved to play with his toys and explore the garden. One day, Tim saw a shiny bulb in the garden. He wanted to touch it, but his mom warned him, "Tim, don't touch that! It's dangerous!" Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. Later, a tiny bug came to visit Tim. The bug said, "Hi, I'm Bob. I can make that bulb safe for you!" Tim was surprised, "Wow, really?" Bob touched the bulb and it turned into a pretty flower. Tim smiled and thanked Bob. They became good friends and played together in the garden every day.

One day, a normal boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to spread his blanket under it. Tim sat on the blanket and started to play with his toys. A cop walked by and saw Tim. The cop said, "Hi, Tim! What are you doing here?" Tim said, "I am playing with my toys under the tree." The cop smiled and said, "That sounds fun! Can I play with you?" Tim said, "Yes, you can!" As they played, a big wind came and blew the blanket away. The cop and Tim were surprised! They ran after the blanket and caught it. They laughed and played some more. Tim and the cop had a fun day at the park.
