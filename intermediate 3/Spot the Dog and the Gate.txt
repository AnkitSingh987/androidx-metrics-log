Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Gate

One day, a dog named Spot wanted to get out and play. He saw a gate near his home. The gate was big and green. Spot thought he could push the gate open, but it was too heavy for him. He tried and tried, but he could not open it. Then, Spot had an idea. He found a fake bone in his toy box. He thought he could use the bone to open the gate. He put the bone under the gate and pushed with all his might. The gate moved a little, but not enough for Spot to get out. Spot was sad, but he did not give up. He asked his friend, a cat named Fluffy, to help him. Fluffy was very smart. She found a small hole near the gate. Fluffy and Spot dug the hole bigger and bigger. Soon, it was big enough for both of them to get out. Spot and Fluffy were so happy. They played and played until they were tired. Then, they went back home through the hole and closed the gate. Spot learned that with a little help from his friends, he could do anything.

Once there was a man who worked in a temple. He was a very hairy man. One day, he was cleaning the temple when he noticed a 3-year-old playing around with some of the items in the temple. He shouted to the little boy, "What are you doing here, kid? You shouldn't be here!" The little boy said, "I was just exploring the temple. I was curious to see what was inside." The man said, "You need to leave these things alone. They are important and delicate." The little boy said, "But why can't I explore the temple? I just wanna look!" The man said, "No! You must leave. This isn't a place for kids." The little boy started to cry. He didn't understand why he couldn't explore the temple. The man felt bad and said, "Okay, You can stay for a little while. But be careful and don't touch anything." The little boy was happy and thanked the man. He spent the next few hours exploring the temple, learning about the different items and their importance. He was very happy to have been given the chance to explore the temple.

One day, a cat named Tom wanted to go to a big party. Tom put on his best hat and started to walk. The sky was clear and he felt happy. On his walk, Tom saw a big tree. He thought it would be fun to climb it. Tom went up the tree and saw a bird's nest. He wanted to play with the bird, but the bird flew away. Tom felt sad and climbed back down. Tom kept walking to the party. When he got there, he saw that the door was closed. He knocked and knocked, but no one opened the door. Tom was too late. The party was over. He walked back home feeling very sad.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked in the park and saw many things. Tim gazed at the big trees and pretty flowers. He was very happy. As they walked, they found a big barrel near a bench. Tim was curious and wanted to look inside. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim!" He slowly opened the barrel and found many colorful balls inside. Tim was excited and started to play with the balls. Suddenly, a small dog came running to Tim. The dog looked fearful and sad. Tim and his mom decided to help the dog. They gave the dog some water and a ball to play with. The dog became happy and wagged its tail. Tim, his mom, and the dog played together until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a careless boy. He always left his toys all around the house. One day, Tim found a toy gun in his toy box. He was so happy and played with it all day. While playing, Tim saw a big bug on the wall. He used his toy gun to scare the bug away. But something unexpected happened. When he pointed the gun at the bug, a tiny thank you card came out of the toy gun. The bug took the card and flew away. Tim was very surprised. He never knew his toy gun could do that. From that day on, Tim was not careless anymore. He always put his toys away when he was done playing. And every time he found a bug, he used his magic toy gun to send them a thank you card. The bugs never came back, and Tim's house was always clean and bug-free.
