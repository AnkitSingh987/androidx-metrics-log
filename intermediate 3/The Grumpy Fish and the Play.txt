Category: Intermediate
Title: The Grumpy Fish and the Play

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy old fish who lived in the sea. He was always sad and did not like to play with other fish. One day, he swam close to the shore and saw a little girl building a sandcastle. "Hello, grumpy fish!" said the little girl. "Why are you so sad?" The grumpy fish looked at her and said, "I don't have any friends to play with in the sea. That's why I am sad." The little girl thought for a moment and then smiled. "I have an idea! You can be my friend, and we can play together on the shore. I can build sandcastles, and you can watch me." The grumpy fish liked the idea and decided to give it a try. Every day, he would come to the shore and watch the little girl build sandcastles. Soon, he was not grumpy anymore. He was happy because he had a friend to play with. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had very long hair. He did not like his long hair. He wanted a haircut. One day, Tim's mom took him to get a haircut. Tim was happy. He sat in the chair and the nice lady cut his hair. Tim's hair was short now. After the haircut, Tim went home. He wanted to show his friends his new haircut. Tim went outside and lay on the grass. He looked up at the sky and smiled. Tim was very happy with his new haircut.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a pretty light in the sky. It was shiny and bright. He wanted to catch it, but it was too high. He asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Tim said, "Sam, please help me catch the crazy light. It is so pretty and I want it." Sam thought it was a fun idea, so they both tried to jump and reach the light. But it was still too high. Tim had an urge to find a way to get the light. He saw a big tree and thought they could climb it. Tim and Sam climbed the tree, but the light was still too far away. They felt sad, but they didn't give up. Then, Tim's mom came out and saw them. She said, "Boys, you can't catch the light. It is the sun, and it is very far away. But you can enjoy its warmth and the pretty colors it makes in the sky." Tim and Sam understood. They could not catch the light, but they could still have fun playing under it. They were happy and played together until the sun went down.

One day, a little butterfly went for a crawl on a leaf. The butterfly saw a big, loud bee. The bee was flying fast and making a lot of noise. The butterfly was scared and hid under the leaf. The loud bee saw the butterfly and came close. The butterfly was very scared, but the bee said, "Don't be scared, I just want to be friends." The butterfly was still scared, but it slowly came out from under the leaf. The bee and the butterfly became friends. They played together and had a lot of fun. The butterfly learned that even if someone is loud and looks scary, they can still be a good friend. And the loud bee learned to be more gentle with new friends. So, both of them were happy and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there lived two friends, Joe and Jeff. Joe was a very organized person, while Jeff was not. One day, Jeff asked Joe, "How do you stay so organized? Do you have a secret?" "Yes, I do! I make an image of the things I need to do," Joe replied. Jeff was amazed. "An image? How does that help?" Joe smiled. "By picturing the tasks ahead, I'm able to plan and remember what I need to do. That's how I stay organized!" Jeff thought about it and decided it was worth a try. He asked Joe for tips, and Joe happily gave him some advice. After a little practice, Jeff was able to organize his tasks more successfully. He was so happy! From then on, he and Joe were the best of friends.
