Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Box

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some cheap toys. At the store, Tim saw a big box of plastic blocks. The box had lots of colors like red, blue, and yellow. Tim was very happy and said, "Mom, I want this one!" His mom said, "Okay, Tim. Let's add it to our cart." They put the plastic blocks in the cart and went to pay. When they got home, Tim opened the box and started to play with the blocks. He made a tall tower and a long bridge. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's dog, Spot, came running into the room. Spot was excited and bumped into the tower. The plastic blocks fell down and made a big mess. Tim was sad, but then he had an idea. He said, "Spot, let's build together!" Tim and Spot started to build a new tower, and it was even better than before.

One day, a big dog named Max found a large jar. Max did not know what was in the jar. He wanted to find out, so he took the jar to his friend, a small cat named Lily. Max asked Lily, "Do you know what is in this jar?" Lily looked at the jar and said, "No, but let's follow the map on the jar. Maybe it will show us." Max and Lily followed the map on the jar. They walked and walked until they found a big tree. They dug under the tree, and something unexpected happened! The tree started to talk! The tree said, "Thank you for finding my jar! It has magic seeds inside. Now you can grow your own talking trees!" Max and Lily were very happy. They took the jar home and grew many talking trees. They made lots of new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a square. The square liked to spin. It spun all day long. The square had a friend, a wild cat. The cat liked to watch the square spin. One day, the cat wanted to spin too. The square and the cat spun together. They spun fast and slow. They spun around and around. The cat was happy to spin with the square. Then, the cat spun too fast. The cat felt bad. The cat could not spin anymore. The square was sad. The cat went away. The square was alone. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a warm, soft toy that she loved to play with every day. One day, Lily saw a new, shiny object on the table. She wanted to play with it too. Lily's mommy saw her looking at the object and said, "No, Lily! That object is not for you to play with." But Lily didn't listen. When mommy wasn't looking, she took the object and ran to her room. In her room, Lily played with the object and her warm toy. But she was not careful. She made a big tear in her soft toy. Lily started to cry. Her mommy came in and saw the tear. She was sad too. Lily learned that she should listen to her mommy.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there were two groups of people who did not like each other. They always fought and had a big war. The people in the village were sad and suffered because of the war. One day, a little girl named Lily found a fragile bird with a hurt wing. She took the bird to her friend, Max. Max was from the other group. They talked and said, "We need to help this bird. It is suffering because of the war." Lily and Max worked together to help the fragile bird. They showed the people in the village that they could be friends, even though they were from different groups. The people saw this and decided to stop fighting. The war ended, and the village was happy again. The people learned that being friends and working together was better than fighting. The fragile bird got better and flew away, reminding everyone that they should be kind and help others.
