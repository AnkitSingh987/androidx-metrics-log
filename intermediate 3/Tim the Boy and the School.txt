Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the School

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a bit spoiled. He did not want to go to school. He liked to play all day. One day, Tim's mom said, "You must go to school, Tim." Tim did not like this. He did not want to go. He started to think. Tim thought of a plan. He said, "I will hide my school bag. Then, I cannot go to school." So, he hid his school bag under his bed. But, Tim's mom found the bag. She said, "You must go to school, Tim. It is good for you." Tim went to school. At school, he found new friends. He had fun. Now, Tim likes school.

One day, a red ball was on the ground. A boy named Tim saw the ball and wanted to play with it. But, another girl named Lily also wanted the red ball. They both reached for it at the same time. Tim and Lily looked at each other and paused. They did not know what to do. They both wanted the red ball, but they did not want to fight. So, they thought of a plan to share the ball. They decided to play a game together. Tim would roll the red ball to Lily, and Lily would roll it back to Tim. They took turns and had a lot of fun. The red ball made them happy, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a model. Everyone said she was so alert and she could always protect herself. One day, she went for a walk in the park. A bad man saw her and said, "Hey, model. I want to take you somewhere special." She was scared and said, "No! I'm not going anywhere!" But the bad man didn't care. He grabbed her arm and pulled her away. The model screamed and yelled, but no one could protect her. In the end, the bad man put her in a car and drove away. The model was never seen again. To this day, no one knows what happened to her. Everyone hopes she is OK, but they know she needs someone to protect her.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who was very stubborn. He always wanted to do things his own way. One day, his mom suggested they go to the store to buy a special package. The boy did not like this suggestion, but he decided to go anyway. When they arrived at the store, the mom showed the boy the special package. It had many things inside, like toys and sweets. The boy really wanted it and he kept on asking his mom to get it for him. But his mom said no, it was too expensive. Still, the little boy was very stubborn and he kept asking his mom. She tried to explain that they could not afford it, but he kept pushing her. Suddenly, she had an idea. She suggested they could find something similar and cheaper. This time, the little boy agreed. So they went around the store looking for the package. Finally, they found a very similar one at a much better price! The little boy was very happy and his mom was proud of him for being so stubborn!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy phone that he liked to play with all day. One day, his toy phone did not work. Tim was sad and did not know what to do. He wanted his toy phone to work so he could play with it again. Tim asked his mom for help. She looked at the toy phone and said it would be fine. She went to the kitchen and came back with a new battery. She put the new battery in the toy phone and it started to work again. Tim was very happy and said thank you to his mom. He played with the toy phone all day long. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy phone started to talk to Tim! It was a real phone all along. Tim was so surprised and excited. He talked to his new friend, the phone, and they played together happily ever after.
