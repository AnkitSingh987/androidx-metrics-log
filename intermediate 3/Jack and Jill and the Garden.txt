Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and Jill and the Garden

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jill. Jack lived in a big garden. One day, Jack and Jill wanted to play in the garden. But Jill had a problem—there was a big wall in the way. Jack said to Jill that he had an idea. The wall was made of blocks and Jack thought if they worked together, they could separate the blocks and make a hole in the wall. So, Jack and Jill got to work. After lots of pushing and pulling, the blocks separated and the friends made a hole. They were so excited and jumped through the opening. But as soon as they stepped into the garden, they saw something was not right. The garden was not the same as before—people had taken flowers, trees and even the path! The garden had changed and did not look the same. Jack and Jill were really sad. But they had an idea—they decided to use the original blocks to build the wall. That way, no one else could get into the garden and it would look like the way it did before. So, Jack and Jill worked really hard. They arranged the blocks in the same way it was before and built the wall. When they finished, they looked at the garden and it was just like before. Seeing that, Jack and Jill smiled. They had worked together and fixed the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy, a car with a zip. He loved to play with his car all day. One day, Tim saw a big red button that said "record". He thought it was a game, so he pushed the button. The button made a loud sound and a big light started to flash. Tim's mom came running and said, "That is dangerous, Tim! Don't touch the big red button again." Tim said sorry and hugged his mom. From that day on, Tim only played with his car with the zip and never touched the dangerous button again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the kitchen. One day, he saw a big box called a freezer. He did not know what it was. He was ignorant about the freezer. Tim opened the freezer and saw many cold things inside. He saw ice and yummy food. Tim took out a cold ice cream. He was so happy! Tim wanted to say thank you to the freezer. Tim gave the freezer a big kiss. His lips felt very cold and funny. He laughed and ate his ice cream. From that day on, Tim knew what a freezer was and loved it very much.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a coin on the ground. It is shiny and round. "Look, a coin!" Tom says. "Let's buy some ice cream with it." "Good idea!" Lily says. "I want chocolate ice cream. What do you want?" "I want vanilla ice cream. Come on, let's go to the shop." They run to the shop and give the coin to the man. The man smiles and gives them two cones of ice cream. "Thank you, mister!" Tom and Lily say. They lick their ice cream and enjoy the cold and sweet taste. But then, Lily makes a funny face. "Ow, ow, ow!" she says. "My ice cream is spicy!" Tom looks at her cone and sees some red flakes on it. "Oh no, Lily! You got chili ice cream! That's not chocolate, that's chili!" Lily spits out the ice cream and cries. Tom feels sorry for her and gives her his cone. "Here, Lily. You can have my ice cream. I don't mind chili ice cream. I like spicy things." Lily stops crying and smiles. She takes Tom's cone and gives him a hug. "Thank you, Tom. You are a good brother. I love you." "I love you too, Lily. Next time, we will be more careful with the coins."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Hilda. She was very brave and liked to explore. One day, she wanted to see what was beyond the big, green trees at the edge of the woods. She was about to go in when she discovered a shelter. It was a pretty shelter with a pointy roof and lots of windows. Hilda was very excited. She went up to the shelter and knocked on the door. But no one answered. She tried again, but still, no one answered. Hilda started to get worried. She didn't know who might be living in the shelter and she was all alone. She was so scared she started to cry. But soon a kind voice said, "Don't be scared. I'm a friend." It was a friendly animal who lived in the shelter. He took Hilda in and showed her around. They became fast friends and Hilda went home feeling happy and safe. She discovered that the shelter was a place of fun and adventure, filled with lots of new and pretty things to explore.
