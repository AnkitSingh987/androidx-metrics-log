Category: Intermediate
Title: The White Bird and the Song

Once upon a time, there was a little white bird. The bird loved to sing a happy song. The bird lived in a big tree. The tree was in a big forest. One day, a little boy got lost in the forest. The boy was sad and scared. He heard the white bird's song. The boy said, "Please, help me find my way home." The bird wanted to help the boy. The white bird sang a special song. The song helped save the boy. The boy followed the bird's song and found his way home. The boy was happy and said, "Thank you, white bird, for saving me with your song." The white bird was happy to help the boy.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim had a broken toy. He was very sad. He wanted to make his toy shiny again. So, he decided to polish it. He took a soft cloth and rubbed the toy gently. Tim's friend, Jane, came over to play. She saw the broken toy and said, "Oh no! Your toy is broken! Can I help you polish it?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, please!" Together, they polished the toy until it was shiny again. Then, they went to the doctor for a check-up. The doctor took an x-ray of Tim's hand. Tim was scared, but Jane held his hand. The x-ray showed that everything was fine. Tim and Jane were happy and went home to play with their shiny, polished toys.

John was 3 years old and he liked to pretend. One day he was pretending he was a pirate who dreamed of going on adventures. As he played, he thought of how deep and exciting the oceans were. He picked up his hat and said to his mom, "I'm going on an adventure, mom!" He left the room and started walking down the hallway, thinking about the depths the ocean held. John stopped, thinking of the treasure he could find. He was lost in the daydreaming until his mom called him back. "John, don't go too far. Come back soon!" She said. John smiled and nodded. He thought about the many adventures he could have. He knew that with each step he took, he'd have an exciting new journey. He was so excited, he started to run down the hallway, his pirate hat flapping behind him. He was off on his deep adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play and pretend all day long. One day, he had a fun idea. He wanted to pretend he was going to a big school called "university." Fluffy told his friends about his idea. They all thought it was a great plan. They all got ready to go to the "university." They packed their bags and put on their pretend hats. They were very excited. They did not know that something fun was about to happen. When they got to the pretend "university," they saw a big surprise. There was a huge pile of toys waiting for them! They played with the toys all day long. They had so much fun at their pretend "university." And at the end of the day, they knew that playing pretend was the best thing ever.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the gym with his mom. The gym was a big room with lots of toys and games. Tim loved playing there with his friends. While playing, Tim saw a dead bug on the floor. He did not like dead bugs, so he frowned. Tim showed the bug to his mom, and she frowned too. Tim's mom picked up the dead bug and threw it away. Tim stopped frowning and went back to play with his friends. They all had lots of fun at the gym.

One day, a little girl named Amy saw an adorable cat. The cat was lost. Amy wanted to help the cat find its home. She said, "I hope we can find your home, cat!" Amy and the cat walked west. They looked for the cat's home. They saw a big tree. Amy asked, "Is your home near the big tree, cat?" The cat did not know. They kept walking. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came and took the cat away. Amy was sad. She could not find the cat's home. The cat was gone. The end.
