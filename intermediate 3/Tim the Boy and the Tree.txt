Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tree

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He walked in the park and saw a big tree. Tim wanted to climb the tree. As he started to climb, he heard a terrible sound. It was a loud "buzz" noise. Tim was scared. He looked around and saw a big, angry bee. The bee was near a switch on the tree. Tim didn't want the bee to sting him. He thought of a plan to get the bee away from the switch. Tim picked up a stick and threw it far away. The bee saw the stick and flew after it. Tim quickly climbed the tree and hit the switch. The tree changed into a fun slide. Tim slid down with a big smile. The terrible sound was gone, and Tim played in the park all day.

One day, a little fish named Tim wanted to perform a dance. He lived in the big ocean with his friends. Tim was a very happy fish. Tim asked his friends to help him make a fake stage. They used rocks and shells to build it. Tim was so excited to perform his dance on the stage. The day came for Tim to perform. All his friends came to watch. Tim danced and everyone clapped. They had a fun day by the ocean, and Tim was the happiest fish of all.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a funny texture. Max thought it was foolish to touch the tree, but he did it anyway. He felt a shiver when he touched the tree. Max saw his friend, a cat named Luna. Luna asked, "Why did you touch the tree, Max? It made you shiver!" Max said, "I don't know. I was just curious." They both laughed and played together. Max learned not to touch things that might make him shiver. He and Luna had a fun day, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a tall cliff near the sea. The cliff was rough and hard to climb. Many animals lived near the cliff, but they never tried to climb it because they knew it was too dangerous. One day, a brave little bird named Billy decided he wanted to stand on the top of the cliff. He thought he could see the whole world from up there. So, he flew up to the top and stood on the edge. "Wow! I can see so far!" he said to himself. But then, the wind started to blow very hard. Billy tried to hold on, but he was too small and the wind was too strong. "Help me!" he cried. But no one could hear him. The wind blew Billy off the cliff and he fell into the sea. The other animals saw what happened and felt sad. They knew they should never try to stand on the dangerous cliff.

Once upon a time there was a big, busy branch. Every day people came to the busy branch to mail letters and packages. One day, a three year old came to the busy branch to mail a letter. He was excited, but the big busy branch was very overwhelming. At the busy branch, he saw big machines that took the letters and packages and he saw lots of people. He was not sure what to do! He asked a man for help and the man said he should put his letter in the big machine. The three year old smiled and looked around the busy branch. He saw that the people in the busy branch were all happy, busy with their mailings. He put his letter into the big machine with a smile and waved goodbye. The three year old was so proud. He had gone to the busy branch all by himself and mailed his letter. He had no idea that one day his letter would be delivered to his friend!

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The park was big and had many fun things to do. Tim liked to play on the swings, slide, and climb on the jungle gym. Today, there was a special event at the park. There were going to be fireworks! As the sun went down, Tim and his mom sat on a big, empty field. They looked up at the sky, waiting for the fireworks to start. Tim was very excited. He had never seen fireworks before. He asked his mom, "What do fireworks look like?" His mom said, "You'll see, they are very pretty and bright." Finally, the first firework shot up into the sky. It made a loud noise and then, boom! It burst into many colors. Tim looked in awe as more and more fireworks filled the sky. They were red, blue, green, and yellow. The empty sky was now full of beautiful lights. Tim and his mom watched and clapped, enjoying the show.
