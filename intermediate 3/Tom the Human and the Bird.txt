Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Human and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a human named Tom. Tom lived in a dry place with a lot of sand. He was a very nice man who liked to help others. One day, Tom saw a big sign. The sign said, "Help me, please." Tom wanted to find out who needed help, so he followed the sign. He walked and walked until he saw a small bird with a hurt wing. Tom took the bird home and took care of it. The bird got better and could fly again. But the bird did not want to leave Tom. It stayed with Tom and helped him too. The bird would bring Tom food and sing songs to make him happy. The twist in the story is that the bird was a magic bird. It could talk and do things that other birds could not do. The bird told Tom that it put the sign up to find a kind human to be friends with. The moral of the story is that when you help others, good things can happen to you too.

Once upon a time, there was a big hall. In the hall, there was a big party. Many kids were playing and having fun. One little girl named Lucy was very spoiled. She always wanted to play with all the toys and not share with the other kids. Lucy's mom told her, "You must share and be nice, or you will not be allowed to play with the toys." But Lucy did not listen. She took all the toys and did not let the other kids play with them. The kids got sad and told their moms and dads. The moms and dads talked to Lucy's mom. Lucy's mom was not happy. She took all the toys away from Lucy and said, "You are not allowed to play with the toys now." Lucy cried and cried, but she learned that being spoiled is not good.

Once upon a time, there was a strong ant named Andy. He loved to lift things and show off how strong he was. One day, Andy saw a big step in front of him. He wanted to climb it, but it was too high for him. He knew he needed help to get to the top. Andy asked his friend, Bee, to help him. Bee was scared, but Andy told her, "Don't worry, we can do it together!" They tried to lift each other up, but they were not strong enough. They needed more help. So, Andy and Bee asked their other friends, Cat and Dog, to join them. Together, they all tried to lift each other up. With their strong teamwork, they finally climbed the big step! They were so happy and proud of what they had done together. From that day on, they knew they could do anything if they worked together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to paint. She was very enthusiastic about it wherever she went. One day she saw some grapes on the table. She thought they were so pretty and wanted to paint them. So the little girl grabbed a paintbrush and started painting the grapes. The little girl put purple, pink, orange and yellow on the grapes. She was very proud of her work and smiled, showing her bright teeth. After she was done she showed the grapes to her mom. Her mom was amazed and said "These grapes look so beautiful. You did a great job!" The little girl was so happy that she finished painting the grapes. She felt so enthusiastic and decided she wanted to paint some more!

Once upon a time, there was a big clock in the town. It was a regular clock that told the time. All the people loved the clock. One day, the clock stopped working. The people were sad because they did not know the time. A little girl named Lucy wanted to help. She had an idea. She went to her friend Tom's house and said, "Tom, can you invite the clock fixer to come and fix the clock?" Tom said, "Yes, I can do that!" So, Tom invited the clock fixer to come to the town. The clock fixer came and fixed the big clock. The people were very happy. They said, "Thank you, Lucy and Tom, for inviting the clock fixer!" The clock started working again, and everyone knew the time. The moral of the story is that if we work together and help each other, we can fix problems.
