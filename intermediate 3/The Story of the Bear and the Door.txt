Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Door

One day, a group of animal friends were playing in the forest. They were very creative and liked to make up new games. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. It was a happy day. The group heard a loud noise coming from a little house. They walked over to see what was happening. Inside the house, they saw a big, angry bear. The bear said, "I cannot shut my door! It is stuck!" The animal friends wanted to help the bear. The creative group thought of a plan. They pushed and pushed on the door together. With a loud creak, the door finally shut. The bear was so happy and thanked the group. From that day on, the bear and the group of animal friends played together in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a shy dog named Spot. Spot loved to eat waffles. One day, he saw a big waffle on the table. He wanted to eat it, but he was too shy to ask. A kind cat named Kitty saw Spot looking at the waffle. Kitty said, "Spot, do you want the waffle?" Spot nodded his head. Kitty helped Spot get the waffle from the table. Spot ate the waffle and was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Kitty!" All the animals saw how happy Spot was and they clapped their hands. They all said, "Yay, Spot got his waffle!" Spot was not shy anymore and he was very happy to have a new friend in Kitty.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat vegetables. One day, he saw a big, green vegetable in the garden. He was very alert and wanted to eat it. Bob hopped over to his friend, Sam the squirrel. He said, "Sam, I saw a big, green vegetable in the garden. Let's go get it!" Sam was scared and said, "No, Bob, that might be a trap. We should not go." But Bob did not listen. Bob went to the garden and tried to get the vegetable. He missed it and fell into a hole. Bob was very sad and scared. He could not get out. Sam came to help, but he could not get Bob out of the hole. In the end, Bob was stuck in the hole and could not get out. He was very sad. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends when they try to help and keep you safe.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a shiny diamond in his backyard. He was so happy and wanted to show it to his best friend, Lily. Tim thought, "This diamond is so pretty, I want to keep it all to myself." He felt a little selfish, but he didn't want to share it with anyone. Tim went to Lily's house to show her the diamond. He held it tightly in his hand and said, "Look, Lily! I found a shiny diamond. But I don't want to share it. It's all mine!" Lily felt sad and said, "That's not nice, Tim. Friends should share." Tim thought about what Lily said and began to feel bad. He knew that being selfish was not good. He started to regret not wanting to share the diamond with his friend. Tim looked at Lily and said, "You're right, Lily. I'm sorry for being selfish. We can share the diamond together." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. That's what friends do." From that day on, Tim and Lily played together with the shiny diamond, and they both learned the importance of sharing and being kind to each other.

One day, a little crab was walking on the beach. He saw a big shell and wanted to eat it. The crab said, "I want to eat that shell!" But the shell was not available to eat because it was too big. A little fish heard the crab and said, "I can help you eat the shell!" The crab and the fish tried to eat the shell together. They took turns trying to bite it, but it was still too big to eat. Then, something unexpected happened. The big shell started to move! It was not just a shell, it was a turtle! The turtle said, "Please don't eat me!" The crab and the fish were surprised. They said sorry and became friends with the turtle. They all played together on the beach and had a fun day.
