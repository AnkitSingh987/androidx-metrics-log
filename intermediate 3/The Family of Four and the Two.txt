Category: Intermediate
Title: The Family of Four and the Two

Once upon a time, there was a family of four. Mama, Papa and two sweet girls like dolls. One day, Mama and Papa had to go out. They were too busy to take their two girls with them. The two girls, who were five and three years old, stayed home by themselves. The five year old said to her little sister, "Let's play a game!" The three year old girl smiled and said, "Yes!" Then the five year old said, "Let's pretend that this room is our own place. You can be the princess, and I'll be the queen!" The little girl became very excited. She wanted to dress up like a princess, so she put on a sparkly dress. Then she said, "Let's go explore our new kingdom!" The two girls laughed and ran around the room, pretending they were in a special kingdom. Soon they were having so much fun, they didn't even notice when Mama and Papa came home. Mama and Papa saw the two girls playing, and Papa said, "It looks like someone has been busy today!" Mama gave her two girls a big smile, and said, "You two had a wonderful time, didn't you?" The girls both smiled and nodded, and Mama said, "Well, let's go get some dinner now!"

Once upon a time, in a small wood, there lived a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day under the big tree in the wood. One day, a little bird named Sally landed on the tree branch above Tom. "Hello, Tom!" said Sally. "Why are you so lazy? Don't you want to play with me?" Tom yawned and said, "No, Sally. I just want to sleep. I don't like to play." Sally felt sad but she had an idea. She flew away and came back with a tiny seed. She gave it to Tom and said, "If you plant this seed, it will grow into a big tree. Then, we can play together on the tree." Tom liked the idea, so he dug a small hole and put the seed in it. He covered it with dirt and waited. Soon, the seed began to grow into a small plant. Tom was very happy to see it grow. As the days went by, the plant grew bigger and bigger. Tom was not lazy anymore. He played with Sally on the big tree every day. They had so much fun together, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little bunny named Binky. Binky loved to hop and play in the sunny meadow with his friends. One day, Binky's stomach felt funny, so he went to see his wise friend, Mr. Owl. "Mr. Owl, my stomach feels funny," said Binky. "What should I do?" Mr. Owl thought for a moment and then said, "Binky, you need to settle down and rest. That will help your stomach feel better." So, Binky found a soft spot under a big tree and lay down to rest. As he settled down, his stomach started to feel better. Binky was happy and soon he was hopping and playing with his friends again. From that day on, whenever Binky's stomach felt funny, he knew just what to do.

Once upon a time there was a graceful fairy named Sally. Sally loved to fly around and play in the clouds. One day Sally was whizzing through the sky and she sneezed! Her sneeze made the clouds go away and she could now see down to the ground below. She saw a family in their garden having dinner. Sally was so excited and wanted to join them! She flew down and carefully landed on the table. Everyone was so surprised to see the graceful fairy! The parents asked Sally, "Would you like to join us for dinner?" Sally was so happy and said, "Yes please!" Everyone laughed and Sally enjoyed a delicious dinner with the family. After dinner, Sally said goodbye and she flew back off into the sky.

Lily and Ben are best friends. They like to play with blocks and dolls and cars. They share their toys and help each other. They are very happy. One day, they find a big box in the garage. It has many colorful papers and ribbons and stickers. They want to make something with them. They ask their mom if they can use them. Mom says yes, but they have to be careful and not make a mess. Lily and Ben start to cut and glue and stick the papers. They make cards and hats and flags. They have a lot of fun. But then, they have a problem. They both want the same sticker. It is a shiny star. It is very pretty. "I saw it first!" Lily says. She tries to grab the sticker. "No, I did!" Ben says. He pulls the sticker. They tug and tug. The sticker rips. Snap! Lily and Ben are very sad. They look at the broken sticker. They look at each other. They feel sorry. "I'm sorry, Lily," Ben says. "I was mean. You can have the sticker." "I'm sorry, too, Ben," Lily says. "I was selfish. You can have the sticker." They don't know what to do. They both want to be nice. They both want to make up. Mom comes to see what they are doing. She sees the broken sticker. She sees their sad faces. She smiles. "You know what?" she says. "You don't need the sticker. You have something more important. You have each other. You have friendship. That is the best thing you can have." She gives them a big hug. Lily and Ben hug her back. They hug each other. They feel happy. They decide to make a new card. They use the broken sticker. They write "Best Friends" on it. They give it to mom. Mom loves it. She says it is the most beautiful card she ever saw. Lily and Ben are best friends. They like to play with blocks and dolls and cars. They share their toys and help each other. They are very happy.
