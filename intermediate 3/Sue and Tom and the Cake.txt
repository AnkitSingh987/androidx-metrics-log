Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue and Tom and the Cake

One day, a girl named Sue and a boy named Tom wanted to bake a sweet cake. They had an oven in their kitchen. They were very excited. Sue said, "Let's separate the eggs and mix them with sugar." But Tom wanted to mix everything together. They were not happy with each other. They decided to bake two cakes, one with separated eggs and one with mixed eggs. When the cakes were done, they tried both cakes. Sue's cake was very sweet and fluffy. Tom's cake was not as sweet and a bit heavy. They both learned that following the right steps is important. The moral of the story is: when we work together and listen to each other, we can make something better.

Once upon a time, in a distant land far away, there was a big sleepy lion. The lion would yawn all day long. He would yawn in the morning, in the afternoon, and at night. One day, the lion found a glass full of water. He was very thirsty, so he drank it all up. The water was cool and tasted good. The lion felt happy and not so sleepy anymore. The lion went to play with his friends, the monkey and the elephant. They all had fun together in the distant land. And from that day on, the lion did not yawn all day long, because he had found a new way to be happy and awake.

Once upon a time, there was a little robot made of metal. The robot had a very important job. It would blink its eyes to help people find their way in the dark. One day, the robot met a big dog. The dog did not like the robot's blinking eyes. The dog barked and growled at the robot. The robot was sad and scared. It did not want to make the dog angry, but it had to keep blinking to help the people. The robot thought of a plan. It blinked its eyes very fast, like a game. The big dog stopped barking and started to play with the robot. They became friends, and the dog even helped the robot guide people in the dark. The robot and the dog worked together, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, in a warm and sunny garden, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside with her toys. One day, Lucy found a stand with pretty flowers on it. She liked the flowers because they were colorful and smelled nice. Lucy wanted to show the stand with flowers to her mom. But when she turned around, the stand disappeared! Lucy was sad and didn't know where it went. She looked around the garden, but she couldn't find it. Then, Lucy saw her dog, Max, running towards her. Max had the stand with flowers in his mouth! He had taken it to play, but he brought it back to Lucy. She was so happy to see the stand again. Lucy thanked Max and they both went inside to show her mom the pretty flowers.

Ben and Sam are brothers. They like to draw pictures with crayons. They have a big book of paper to draw on. But they have to share the crayons and the paper. Sometimes they fight over them. One day, Ben wants to draw a picture of a lion. He takes the yellow crayon and the brown crayon. He draws a big lion with a mane and a tail. He is proud of his picture. He shows it to Sam. "Look, Sam, I made a lion. Do you like it?" Ben asks. Sam looks at the picture. He does not like it. He thinks it is ugly. He wants to draw his own picture. He takes the red crayon and the black crayon. He draws a big X over Ben's lion. He covers it with red and black lines. "Ha, ha, ha, I made your lion better. Do you like it now?" Sam says. Ben is angry. He sees what Sam did to his picture. He feels sad and mad. He wants to hit Sam. He yells at him. "Sam, you are mean! You ruined my picture! You are a bad brother!" Ben cries. Sam does not hear Ben. He is deaf. He cannot hear what Ben says. He only sees his mouth move. He thinks Ben is happy. He smiles and laughs. "Ben, you are funny! You make funny faces! You are a good brother!" Sam says. Ben does not understand Sam. He thinks Sam is mocking him. He does not know that Sam is deaf. He does not know that Sam needs help to hear. He does not know that Sam loves him. The end.
