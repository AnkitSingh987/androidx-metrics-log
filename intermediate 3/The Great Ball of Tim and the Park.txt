Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Park

Once upon a time, in a warm summer, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very enthusiastic about playing outside with his friends. He loved to run, jump, and play games. One day, Tim saw a big ball in the park. He wanted to play with it, but he didn't know how to get it. Tim asked his friend, Sam. "Can you help me get the big ball?" Sam tried, but he couldn't manage to get the ball either. They both felt sad. Then, they saw another friend, Lily, walking by. They asked her for help. Lily was very smart. She found a long stick and used it to get the ball. Tim and Sam were so happy! They all played with the ball together. The conflict was solved, and they learned that working together can solve problems. The moral value is that teamwork makes things easier.

One day, a soft cat named Mimi went for a walk. Mimi liked to walk to the east, where the sun comes up. She saw her friend, a dog named Toby. Toby was big and strong, but very kind. They liked to play and run together. "Hi Toby," said Mimi. "Do you want to walk east with me?" Toby wagged his tail and said, "Yes, Mimi, let's go!" So, they walked and played and had lots of fun. As they walked, they found a big box. Mimi and Toby looked at the box. Then, something unexpected happened. Mimi jumped in the box and disappeared! Toby was scared and called out, "Mimi, where are you?" Suddenly, Mimi jumped out of the box and said, "Here I am, Toby!" They both laughed and continued their walk to the east, happy to be together again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to learn about history. He liked to wake up early and read his history books. Tim lived in an icy cold town where the snow never stopped falling. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue also loved history. They decided to explore the icy forest to learn more. Tim said, "Let's wake up early and go find history in the forest!" Sue agreed, and they were excited for their adventure. The next day, Tim and Sue woke up early and went to the icy forest. They walked and talked about history. But then, they got lost. They were cold and scared. They could not find their way home. Tim and Sue were very sad because their adventure had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the mud. One day, he got very filthy. His clothes and hands were all dirty. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Oh no, you need to clean up!" Tim went inside the house to wash his hands. His mom helped him pour water on his hands to get them clean. The water washed away all the dirt and mud. Tim's hands were now clean and he was happy. The next day was Tim's birthday. He got a big gift from his mom and dad. Tim opened the gift and found a new toy car inside. He loved the toy car and played with it all day. Tim remembered to stay clean while playing with his new toy car, and he had a great birthday.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lazy boy named Tom. He loved to play with his kite all day long. Tom did not like to do his chores or help his mom and dad. One day, Tom's mom said, "If you don't do your chores, you will be punished." Tom didn't want to be punished, but he also didn't want to stop playing with his kite. So, he thought of a plan. Tom tied his kite to a big stick and put it in the ground. The kite flew high in the sky, and it looked like Tom was still playing with it. Then, he quickly did his chores. When his mom saw the kite in the sky, she thought Tom was still playing. She was about to punish him, but then she saw that all the chores were done. Tom's mom was very surprised and happy. From that day on, Tom was not lazy anymore and always did his chores before playing with his kite.
