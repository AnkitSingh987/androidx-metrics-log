Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Flower

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big, purple flower. The flower was as big as her hand! Lily touched the flower with her finger. It felt soft and nice. A small bug came out of the flower. The bug said, "Hi, I am Buzz. I live in this purple flower." Lily was happy to see Buzz. She said, "Hi, Buzz! I like your home. It is very pretty." Lily and Buzz became friends. They played together every day. Lily would visit the purple flower, and Buzz would come out to see her. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a small bird named Bob. Bob loved to sing. Every day, he would sing for all his friends in the forest. One day, it was very cold outside. Bob was sad because he could not sing when it was cold. Bob had an idea. He would ask the wise owl for a license to sing in the cold. The wise owl lived in a big tree. Bob went to the big tree and asked the wise owl for help. The wise owl gave Bob a special license to sing in the cold. Now, Bob could sing even when it was cold outside. All his friends were happy to hear him sing. They danced and played while Bob sang his songs. The forest was filled with joy and laughter, even on the coldest days.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to walk on the path near her house. Lily had a big, reliable dog named Max who always went with her. They were best friends. One sunny day, they walked on the path and saw a big tree. Lily wanted to climb it, but Max barked, as if he knew something. Lily didn't listen and climbed the tree. When she was up high, she saw a bird's nest with eggs. Lily was happy and wanted to show Max, but she slipped and fell. Lily was scared and hurt, so she closed her eyes and started to pray. When she opened her eyes, she saw Max had turned into a big, soft pillow! Lily had landed on Max-pillow and didn't get hurt. They went back home, and Lily thanked Max for being so reliable. From that day on, Lily always listened to Max, and they had many more adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a dad who went to the park with his little daughter. She was so excited because she had never been to the park before! When they got to the park, she started to chew on a big, round ball. The ball was yellow and it was so crunchy! The pair then went to the playground. As they touched the sand, it felt so soft and smooth underneath her feet. She put her hands in the sand and even put some of it in her mouth to chew. As they were walking around, they heard a strange noise coming from the dark corner of the park. The dad and the daughter went closer to find out what the sound was. They saw a big, black cat hiding in the shadows. It was meowing softly and chewing on a piece of rope. They could tell it was scared. The dad and the daughter reached out and touched the cat gently. The cat seemed to be so relieved that it stopped chewing the rope and purred. The dad scooped the cat up and hugged it tightly. From then on, the dad, the daughter and the black cat were the best of friends and spent many days playing together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big bird named Ollie. Ollie was an ostrich. He had lots of friends in the forest. Ollie was a very helpful bird. He liked to supply food to his friends. One day, Ollie found a spoiled apple. He did not know it was spoiled. He thought it was a good apple. Ollie wanted to give the apple to his friend, Timmy the turtle. Timmy the turtle saw the apple and knew it was not good. Timmy said, "Ollie, this apple is spoiled. We cannot eat it." Ollie was sad. He did not want to give his friend a bad apple. Ollie and Timmy went to find good food. They found yummy berries and nuts. Ollie and Timmy shared the food with all their friends. Everyone was happy. Ollie learned to check the food before he supplied it to his friends.
