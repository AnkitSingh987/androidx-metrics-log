Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Squirrel and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to play with his friends in the big forest. One day, Sam saw a distant tree with many acorns. He was very happy and wanted to get the acorns for his friends. Sam ran to the tree and saw a sign. The sign said, "Do not touch the acorns." Sam was sad, but he wanted to be good. So, he did not touch the acorns. Then, a big bird came and took the sign away. The bird said, "I put the sign there as a joke! You can have the acorns." Sam was very surprised and happy. He took the acorns to his friends, and they all had a big party.

Once upon a time, there was a poor dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his owner, who had a whip. Max did not like the whip, as it made him very sad. One day, Max went for a walk and found a magic stone. The magic stone could change things. Max wanted to change the whip, so his owner could not use it anymore. He touched the whip with the magic stone, and something unexpected happened. The whip changed into a soft toy! Max was so happy. He took the soft toy to his owner, who was surprised. The owner played with Max and the soft toy, and they had a lot of fun. From that day on, Max and his owner were happy, and the whip was gone forever.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy car. He loved to make it go fast across the floor. His mom was in the room too. She was making food for Tim to eat. "Mom, can I put the car in my mouth?" Tim asked. Mom said, "No, I do not permit that. It is not safe." Tim felt sorry for asking. But then, something unexpected happened. A little dog came into the room. The dog saw the toy car and wanted to play too. The dog picked up the car in its mouth and ran away. "Come back!" Tim yelled. The dog stopped, looked at Tim, and dropped the car. Tim was happy to have his car back. He knew not to put it in his mouth.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Mark. He had a pink toy car that he loved very much. Mark played with his pink car all day long, pushing it around the house and making fun noises. One day, Mark's mom told him not to play with his pink car near the big glass window. She said it was not safe. But Mark did not listen. He kept playing near the window, and his pink car hit the glass. The glass broke, and his mom was very sad. Mark's mom had to punish him for not listening. She put his pink car away for the whole day, and Mark could not play with it. Mark was sad too, but he learned to listen to his mom. From that day on, he played with his pink car in a safe place, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a generous boy who loved to share with his friends. One day, Tim found a funny video on his mom's phone. Tim went to his friend Sam and said, "Sam, I found a funny video. Let's watch it together!" Sam was happy and they watched the video together. They laughed and had a good time. Later, Tim saw another friend, Lily. He said, "Lily, I have a funny video. Do you want to watch it with me?" Lily said yes, and they laughed together too. Tim felt happy because he shared the video with his friends. All his friends were happy too. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can make everyone feel good.

Mia likes to make toast for breakfast. She puts bread in the toaster and waits for it to pop. She spreads butter and jam on the toast and cuts it into pieces. She likes to share her toast with her furry friend, Luna. Luna is a cat. She is black and white and has soft fur. She likes to sit on Mia's lap and purr. She also likes to eat toast, but only a little bit. She prefers to lick the butter and jam off the toast and leave the bread. One day, Mia and Luna hear a loud noise outside. They look out the window and see a big truck. The truck has a picture of a dog on it. The dog is brown and has floppy ears. It is holding a bone in its mouth. The truck says "Woof Woof Delivery". Mia and Luna are excited. They wonder what the truck is delivering. They run to the door and wait for the truck to stop. They hope it is something for them. Maybe it is a new toy, or a book, or a treat. The truck stops in front of their house. The driver gets out and opens the back door. He takes out a big box and carries it to the door. He rings the bell and smiles. He says "Hello, I have a delivery for Mia and Luna. Are you Mia and Luna?" Mia and Luna nod. They are very curious. They thank the driver and take the box inside. They open the box and see what is inside. It is a big, fluffy, furry blanket. It is pink and has stars on it. It is very soft and warm. Mia and Luna are very happy. They hug the blanket and each other. They say "Thank you, Woof Woof Delivery!" They spread the blanket on the couch and snuggle under it. They eat their toast and watch the truck drive away. They have a cozy and yummy breakfast.
