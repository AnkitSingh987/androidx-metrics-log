Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Huge Dog and the Leaves

One day, a huge dog named Max went outside. The fog was thick, and Max could not see very far. Max saw a pile of leaves and decided to scatter them. Max's friend, a small cat named Lucy, came over. "Max, be careful in the fog!" she said. Max just laughed and kept playing in the leaves. Lucy was worried, but Max did not listen. As Max scattered the leaves, he did not see the big hole in the ground. Max fell into the hole and could not get out. Lucy tried to help, but she was too small. Max was stuck in the hole, and the fog was still all around.

Once upon a time, there was an old cat. The old cat liked to play with an open box. One day, the old cat saw a big dog. The big dog wanted to play with the open box too. The old cat did not want to surrender the open box. The big dog was not happy. The big dog and the old cat started to fight for the open box. In the end, the big dog won. The old cat had to surrender the open box. The old cat was sad and walked away. The big dog played with the open box, but the old cat never got to play with it again.

Lily liked to play with her dad's office things. She liked the pens, the papers, the stapler and the calculator. She liked to pretend she was working like her dad. Sometimes, her dad let her sit on his lap and help him with his work. One day, her dad had a very important work to do. He had to complete a report for his boss. He told Lily he needed to focus and not to disturb him. Lily nodded and said she would be quiet. She took some papers and pens and went to the corner of the office. She wanted to make her own report. She drew some pictures and wrote some words. She used the stapler to put the papers together. She used the calculator to make some numbers. She was very proud of her report. She wanted to show it to her dad. She walked to her dad's desk and said, "Dad, look at my report. I completed it for you." She put her report on top of his report. Her dad looked at her report and smiled. He said, "Wow, Lily, this is a very nice report. You did a great job. Thank you for helping me." He hugged her and kissed her. He said, "But you know, this is my report. I need to send it to my boss. Your report is very pretty, but it is not what my boss wants. And you used some of my expensive papers and pens. Those are for special work only. Next time, please ask me before you use them, okay?" Lily felt sorry. She said, "I'm sorry, dad. I didn't know. I just wanted to make you happy. Can I keep my report?" Her dad said, "Of course, you can keep your report. It is yours. And you made me very happy. I love you, Lily. But now, I need to finish my report. Can you play with something else for a while?" Lily said, "Okay, dad. I love you too. I will play with something else." She took her report and went to the corner of the office. She found some toys and books. She played with them quietly. She was happy. She had completed her report. And her dad had liked it.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there lived a girl named Ann. Ann loved to get colors from the big box she had. She had red, blue, yellow, and many more colors. One sunny day, Ann went outside with her colors. She saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to color it. She took her red color and started to color the tree. The tree looked very pretty with the red color on it. Soon, her friends saw the tree and came to help. They got their own colors and started to color the tree too. They used blue, yellow, and green. The tree became the most colorful tree in the busy little town. Everyone was happy and loved the beautiful tree.
