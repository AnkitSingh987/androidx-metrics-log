Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to imagine. He would think about big things and small things. One day, Tim thought about baseball. He wanted to play with his friends. Tim was very smart. He knew how to play baseball well. He would hit the ball far and run fast. His friends liked to play with him because he was so good at the game. One sunny day, Tim and his friends played baseball in the park. They had so much fun. Tim hit the ball very far, and his friends cheered for him. They played until it was time to go home. Tim was happy because he could imagine and play baseball with his smart friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She was a restless girl who always wanted to play. One day, her mom gave her a big piece of candy. Amy was so happy to receive the candy. She played with it and showed it to her friends. Amy's friends wanted a piece of candy too, but Amy didn't want to share. Her friends were sad and went away. Amy played alone with her candy, but she started to feel lonely. As the day went on, Amy ate her candy. But she ate it too fast, and her tummy began to hurt. She cried because she was in pain and had no friends to help her. The bad ending is that Amy learned that she should have shared her candy and played with her friends.

Lily and Sam were playing outside in the garden. They liked to run, jump and chase each other. They also liked to look at the sky and see the clouds and the birds. One day, they saw something very pretty in the sky. It was a rainbow. It had many colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple. Lily and Sam pointed at the rainbow and said: "Wow! Look at that!" They wanted to see the rainbow closer. They ran towards the rainbow, but it seemed to move away from them. They ran faster and faster, but the rainbow was still distant. They wondered why they could not reach the rainbow. They saw their mom watching them from the window. They ran back to her and asked: "Mom, why can't we touch the rainbow? It is so pretty and colorful!" Mom smiled and hugged them. She said: "The rainbow is not a thing you can touch. It is a light that fills the sky when the sun and the rain meet. It is like a magic gift from nature. You can enjoy it with your eyes, but you can't catch it." Lily and Sam nodded. They understood. They thanked mom and went back to the garden. They looked at the rainbow again and felt happy. They decided to make their own rainbow with water and a hose. They sprayed water in the air and saw the colors shine. They laughed and played with their own rainbow. They had fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big brain that he liked to use. He was very good at helping people. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tight tree. The cat was sad and scared. Tim said to the cat, "Don't worry, I will help you." He thought hard with his big brain and found a way to get the cat down. Tim used a long stick to reach the cat and gently pushed it out of the tight tree. The cat was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Everyone in the town saw what Tim did and they admired him. They said, "Tim is very kind and smart. We should all be like Tim and use our brains to help others." From that day on, the people in the town learned to be kind and helpful, just like Tim. The moral of the story is to use your brain to help others and be kind.

Once upon a time, a thoughtful little boy named Tim went for a wander. He put on his gear, like his hat and shoes. He wanted to see the world outside. As Tim wandered, he saw a big tree. He thought the tree was very pretty. He saw a bird in the tree. The bird was singing a nice song. Tim smiled and kept wandering. Soon, Tim saw a little pond. He saw fish swimming in the water. They looked happy. Tim felt happy too. He went back home and told his mom and dad about his wander. They hugged him and said they were proud of their thoughtful little boy.
