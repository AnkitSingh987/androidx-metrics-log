Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Garden

Lily and Ben like to design things with their toys. They have many blocks, cars, dolls and animals. They make houses, roads, farms and zoos on the floor of their room. They have fun and talk to each other. "Look, I made a big house for my dolls," Lily says. "It has a roof, a door, a window and a garden." "That's nice, Lily," Ben says. "But I made a better house for my cars. It has a garage, a ramp, a tunnel and a bridge." They show each other their houses and play with them. They pretend to drive the cars and dress the dolls. But then, Lily has an idea. She wants to make a surprise for Ben. She takes some blocks and makes a deep hole in the floor. She covers it with a thin sheet of paper. She puts some grass and flowers on top. She thinks Ben will not see the hole. "Ben, come and see my new garden," Lily says. "It is very pretty and colorful." Ben walks over to Lily's garden. He likes the grass and the flowers. He wants to touch them. He steps on the paper. But the paper breaks and he falls into the hole. "Help, help!" Ben cries. "I'm in a deep hole!" Lily hears Ben and runs to him. She sees him in the hole. She feels sorry and scared. She did not want to hurt Ben. She wants to help him. "Ben, I'm sorry, I'm sorry," Lily says. "I made a hole to surprise you. But it was a bad surprise. I will get you out." Lily finds a rope and throws it to Ben. Ben grabs the rope and Lily pulls him out. Ben is dirty and angry. He does not like the hole. He does not like the surprise. "Lily, that was a mean thing to do," Ben says. "You could have hurt me. You made a mess of the floor. You are not a good designer." Lily feels sad and ashamed. She knows Ben is right. She made a mistake. She wants to say sorry again. She wants to make Ben happy. "Ben, I'm very sorry," Lily says. "I wanted to make you laugh. But I made you cry. I will clean the floor. I will make you a nice surprise. Please forgive me." Ben looks at Lily. He sees her tears and her smile. He remembers they are friends. He remembers they like to design things together. He decides to forgive her. "OK, Lily, I forgive you," Ben says. "But don't make any more holes. Let's make something nice together. Let's design a castle for our toys." Lily and Ben hug and smile. They forget the hole and the mess. They take their blocks and make a big castle on the floor. They put their dolls and cars inside. They have fun and talk to each other. They are happy. They are good designers.

Timmy was feeling very nervous. He had his first day of school today and he didn't know what to do. He was wearing a new vest and he looked at it in the mirror. When he arrived at school, he saw some of his friends. He was still feeling a bit nervous but he smiled at them anyway. Suddenly, his teacher told them it was time to go inside. Timmy took a deep breath and turned towards the door. He felt a bit better inside the classroom, even though he was still a bit nervous. He sat down in his chair and looked around the room. He noticed all the kids were wearing different colour vests, just like him. He was feeling better already! Suddenly, the teacher clapped her hands and announced it was time to turn their chairs around. Timmy was a bit nervous again, but he gulped and turned his chair around. Everyone else was doing the same thing. He was relieved he had done it correctly. Timmy felt much better as the day went on. He made some new friends and he even received a sticker for turning his chair around correctly. He was definitely ready for the next day of school!

It was a rainy day and Lily wanted to go outside and play. She put on her boots and her coat and asked her mom for an umbrella. "Here you go, sweetie. Be careful and don't get wet," her mom said, handing her a pink umbrella with flowers. "Thank you, mom. I love you," Lily said, opening the umbrella and running to the door. She stepped outside and saw a big puddle. She smiled and jumped into it, making a splash. She laughed and felt the raindrops on her face. She liked the sound of the rain and the smell of the wet grass. She saw a little bird on a branch, shivering and wet. She felt sorry for the bird and wanted to help. She walked over to the tree and said, "Hello, bird. Are you cold? Do you want to share my umbrella?" The bird looked at her and nodded. It flew down and landed on her shoulder. Lily held the umbrella over both of them and said, "There, now we are both warm and dry. Do you want to be my friend?" The bird chirped and nuzzled her cheek. Lily smiled and hugged the bird. She was happy to have a new friend. She said, "What is your name, bird?" The bird said, "My name is Blue. What is yours?" "My name is Lily. Nice to meet you, Blue," Lily said. They talked and played under the umbrella, until Lily's mom called her for lunch. Lily said, "Blue, I have to go now. But you can come with me if you want. Do you want to come?" Blue said, "Yes, I do. Thank you, Lily. You are very kind." Lily said, "You're welcome, Blue. You are very sweet." She removed the umbrella from her shoulder and held it in her hand. She and Blue walked back to the house, where her mom was waiting for them. Lily said, "Mom, this is Blue. He is my new friend. Can he stay for lunch?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course he can, honey. He is very cute. Come on in, both of you. I have some sandwiches and juice for you." Lily and Blue went inside and enjoyed their lunch. They were very happy and grateful. They had found each other on a rainy day.
