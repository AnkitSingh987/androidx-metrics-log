Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Song

One day, a little boy named Tim found a rough, old radio in his room. He did not know how to use it. So, he took it to his mom. "Mom, what is this?" he asked. His mom smiled and said, "This is a radio. It plays music and tells stories. Let me show you." She turned the knob and the radio made a sound. They heard a song and started to dance. After some time, the radio stopped playing music. Tim's mom sighed. "Oh no, the radio is broken," she said. Tim felt sad but his mom had an idea. "Let's fix it together," she said. They worked together and fixed the radio. When it started playing music again, they both laughed and danced. Tim was happy to have a working radio and learned something new that day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Fifi. Fifi was a very spoiled dog. She always got what she wanted. One day, Fifi was playing outside when she found a tiny flea. The flea was jumping and dancing around. "Hi there, flea!" Fifi said. "Do you want to play with me?" The flea did not answer at first. Then, it said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, Fifi!" Fifi and the flea played all day long. They ran and jumped and had so much fun. But soon, it was time for Fifi to go inside. She said goodbye to her new friend, the flea. "Goodbye, flea! I will see you tomorrow!" Fifi said. "Goodbye, Fifi!" the flea answered. And they both knew they would play again the very next day.

Lily and Max were best friends. They liked to play with toys, read books, and sing songs. One day, they found a shiny penny on the ground. They both wanted it very much. "Look, a penny!" Lily said. "It is so shiny and pretty. I want it!" "No, I want it!" Max said. "It is so amazing and cool. I saw it first!" They started to pull the penny from each other. They were not happy. They were angry and sad. Then, they heard a voice. It was their teacher, Miss Rose. She saw them fighting over the penny. "Children, what are you doing?" she asked. "Why are you fighting over a penny?" "Because it is shiny and pretty and amazing and cool!" Lily and Max said. Miss Rose smiled. She had an idea. She took the penny from them and showed them something. "Do you see this?" she asked. "This is a magic penny. It can do something very special. But only if you share it." Lily and Max looked at the penny. They were curious. They wanted to know what it could do. "What can it do?" they asked. Miss Rose whispered in their ears. "It can make you happy. But only if you give it to someone else. If you keep it for yourself, it will make you sad. Do you want to try?" Lily and Max nodded. They wanted to be happy. They did not want to be sad. Miss Rose gave the penny back to them. "Now, you can choose. Who do you want to give the penny to?" Lily and Max looked at each other. They thought for a moment. Then, they smiled. They both said at the same time, "I want to give the penny to you!" They gave the penny to each other. They hugged and said, "Thank you! You are my best friend!" They felt happy. They felt warm and good. They did not care about the penny anymore. They cared about their friendship. Miss Rose clapped her hands. She was proud of them. She said, "Well done, children! You have learned a very important lesson. Sharing is caring. And caring is magic. You have made the penny magic by sharing it. And you have made each other happy by caring for each other. That is the most amazing thing of all!"

Once upon a time, in a land of ice and snow, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small igloo with his family. One day, Tim felt very uncomfortable in his igloo because it was too small for him to play. Tim went outside and saw a big rope. He thought, "If I pull this rope, maybe something fun will happen!" So, Tim started to pull the rope with all his might. As he pulled, a big, shiny sled appeared! "Wow!" Tim shouted with joy. "A new sled to play with!" Tim called his friend, Sam. "Hey, Sam! Look at this big sled I found! Let's play together!" Sam came running and saw the sled. "That's so cool, Tim! Let's pull the sled and ride it on the snow!" They took turns pulling each other on the sled and had a fun day playing outside. And Tim didn't feel uncomfortable in his igloo anymore, because he found a fun way to play outside.
