Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he decided to go to the creek near his house. Before going, he put on his favorite clothes. He dressed in a red shirt and blue pants. Tim walked to the creek and saw many things. He saw fish swimming in the water. He saw birds in the sky. Tim was very happy. He played near the creek for a long time. As the sun started to set, Tim knew it was time to go home. He walked back to his house and told his mom and dad about his fun day at the creek. They were happy to hear about his adventure. Tim went to bed dreaming about the next time he would play at the creek.

Once upon a time, in a lively little house, there lived a girl named Lucy. She liked to play with her toys and eat yummy food. Lucy had a big pepper in her kitchen. She thought it was funny and liked to show it to her friends. One day, Lucy wanted to scare her brother, Tim. She took the big pepper and hid behind the door. When Tim walked in, Lucy jumped out and said "Boo!" with the big pepper in her hands. Tim was so scared that he ran away, yelling "Ahhhh!" Lucy laughed and laughed, and Tim came back to see what was so funny. When he saw the big pepper, he laughed too. They played with the pepper and had a fun time. From that day on, Lucy and Tim were not scared of the big pepper anymore. They knew it was just a funny, lively pepper that made them laugh.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue was deaf, but she was very good at understanding what people said with their hands. Sue had a friend named Tom. Tom and Sue liked to play together. One day, Sue and Tom went to a party. At the party, there was a game. The game was to find a veil that was hidden. Sue and Tom looked and looked for the veil. They looked under chairs, behind doors, and in boxes. Finally, Sue found the veil! But when she picked it up, she saw that it was dirty. Sue felt sad because the veil had to suffer from being dirty. Tom saw Sue was sad, so he helped her clean the veil. Sue and Tom were happy because they found the veil and made it clean again. They had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He loved to play with his marbles. One day, a new marble appeared in his toy box. It was big and shiny. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to show his new marble to his best friend, Sam. But when he went to Sam's house, Sam was not there. Tim felt sad and worried. He did not know where Sam was. He looked everywhere for Sam but could not find him. Finally, Tim found Sam at the park. They played with the new marble together. They were both very happy. Tim and Sam learned that they should always tell each other where they are going. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw an old man near his house. The old man looked sad and had a pebble in his hand. Tim went to the old man and asked, "Why are you sad?" The old man said, "I am sad because this pebble is too small." Tim wanted to help, so he looked for a bigger pebble. He found one and gave it to the old man. The old man took the pebble, but he started to complain. "This pebble is too big," he said. Tim felt sad because he could not make the old man happy. He tried to find a pebble that was just right, but he could not. The old man kept complaining, and Tim felt sadder and sadder. In the end, Tim stopped trying to help the old man and went home. He learned that he could not make everyone happy, and sometimes, it is better to let things be.
