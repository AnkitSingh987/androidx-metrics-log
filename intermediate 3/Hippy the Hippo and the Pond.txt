Category: Intermediate
Title: Hippy the Hippo and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo named Hippy. He lived in a perfect pond with his friends. They liked to play and swim all day. Hippy was happy, but he had a question. One day, Hippy asked his friend Froggy, "Why am I so big?". Froggy looked at Hippy and said, "You are a hippo, and hippos are big. That's what makes you special." Hippy smiled and felt good about being a big hippo. Hippy and Froggy played in the perfect pond every day. They splashed water and had fun. All the animals in the pond were happy. Hippy's question was answered, and he loved being a big hippo.

One day, a cool cat named Sam was walking in the park. He saw a circle on the ground. Sam was very curious about the circle. He wanted to know what it was. Sam asked his friend, the dog named Max, "What is this circle?" Max said, "I don't know. Let's ignore it and keep playing." But Sam couldn't ignore the circle. He wanted to find out more. Sam and Max asked other friends in the park about the circle. No one knew what it was. They all decided to sit inside the circle and have a fun time together. The circle became their favorite spot to play and laugh.

Once upon a time, there was a big hall. In the hall, there were many kids. They liked to play and have fun. They were very creative. They made pretty things with their hands. They drew on the walls, and they built towers with blocks. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the hall. She saw the kids playing and wanted to join them. She walked up to a boy named Tim. Tim was drinking water from a cup. Lily said, "Hi, Tim. Can I play with you?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Lily. Let's be creative together." They played with the blocks and made a big tower. They painted on the walls with bright colors. They had a lot of fun. After playing, they were thirsty. They went to drink water. Tim said, "Lily, I'm happy we played together. We made the hall even more fun." Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. We are good friends now." They drank their water, and they played some more. They were happy in the big hall together.

Once upon a time there was a yellow squash. The squash was very lonely, so it wished for a friend. Suddenly, a butterfly flew by and the squash asked, "Can you be my friend?" The butterfly did not answer, but flew away. The squash was very sad that the butterfly wouldn't be her friend. She waited a long time and eventually she became very bored. She wished for a friend even more. Suddenly, the yellow squash heard a voice. It was a bee! The bee said, "I can be your friend!" The squash was so excited and said, "Yes, please be my friend!" The bee and the squash became friends right away. They flew around together and had so much fun! They played tag and hide-and-seek and they explored the garden. The bee and the yellow squash stayed friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a young hunter. He liked to go into the woods to find food for his family. One day, he saw a big, red ball in the sky. The young hunter thought it was a big fruit. He wanted to serve the big fruit to his family. So, he tried to jump and catch it. But the big, red ball was too high. He tried and tried, but he could not get it. The young hunter was sad. Then, a kind bird saw the young hunter. The bird told him that the big, red ball was not a fruit. It was the sun. The young hunter thanked the bird and went home. He told his family the story, and they all laughed. The young hunter learned something new that day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, he wanted to complete a big jump over a pointy rock. Spot's friend, Cat, came to watch. "You can do it, Spot!" said Cat. Spot felt happy and excited. He ran, jumped, and tried his best to complete the big jump. But Spot did not jump high enough. He fell on the pointy rock and felt ashamed. "I'm sorry, Spot," said Cat. "It's okay," said Spot. "I'll try again and do my best." Spot tried again and again. Each time, he felt less ashamed and more brave. Finally, Spot completed the big jump over the pointy rock. "Yay, Spot!" cheered Cat. "You did it!" From that day on, Spot and Cat played together and always tried their best. They knew that even if they felt ashamed, they could still complete anything with hard work and friendship.
