Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Car and the Child

Once upon a time there was a small child who wanted a new vest. He was eager to wear it everywhere he went. Every morning he would ask his mom for a new vest but she would always delay it. Eventually, the child got fed up and he decided it was time to get what he wanted. One day, right before dinner, he asked his mother again. She said, "not now, sweetheart, I'm too busy to get it." The child was not happy. He shouted, "I want the vest now!" and ran outside the house. The child was so eager to get the new vest that he went out into the street and started looking around. But it was too late. A car driver couldn't see the little boy and hit him. The child was hurt and crying. His mother rushed outside and started calling for help. She was sad and angry at the same time - she wished she had just given her son the vest earlier.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily found a bottle in her yard. It was a very important bottle because it could record what people said. Lily was so happy to find this bottle. She had an idea to play a game with her friend, Tim. Lily went to Tim's house and said, "Let's play a game with my new bottle. We can talk into it, and it will record our words!" Tim was excited to play with the important bottle, too. They took turns talking into the bottle, saying things like, "I like ice cream" and "My favorite color is blue." After they finished playing, Lily and Tim listened to the bottle. They heard their words come out of the bottle, just like they had said them. They laughed and clapped their hands. They knew the important bottle was very special. They played with the bottle all day and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, in a small village, lived a wise old man. He had a magic sword that he kept close to him. The sword could talk and it would help the wise man do good things. One day, a big, mean dragon came to the village. The dragon was angry and scared everyone. The wise man knew he had to stop the dragon. So, he took his magic sword and went to the dragon. The wise man and the sword talked to the dragon. They told the dragon to be nice and not to scare the people. The dragon listened and became nice. The village was safe and everyone was happy. The wise man and his magic sword were heroes.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very proud of his new toy, a magnet. He loved to play with it all day. He would pick up little things with it and watch them stick. One day, Tim's friend, Sara, came over to play. "What is that?" she asked, pointing at the magnet. Tim wanted to explain to her what it was, but he didn't know how. So, he showed her how it worked. He picked up a little paper clip with the magnet. Sara was amazed! "Wow! How does it work?" Sara asked. Tim thought for a moment and said, "It's magic!" They both laughed and played with the magnet all day. They were very happy to have such a fun toy to share.

Bob and his friends were in the same class at school. Every day they would share ideas with each other. One day, Bob had a really original idea. He wanted everyone to come to his house for a special party. His friends all thought it was a great plan, so Bob's mum and dad agreed. They went to the store and bought lots of snacks and drinks. On the day of the party, Bob's friends brought presents and cards. They all had a lot of fun playing games and eating snacks. At the end of the day, they all had a group hug to say goodbye. They all agreed they had shared a very special day together.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to make a yummy snack. He asked his mom, "Can I make a yummy snack?" His mom said, "Yes, you can. Let's go to the kitchen." Tim and his mom went to the kitchen. They found a pan to cook the snack. Tim's mom put the pan on the stove. She said, "Be careful, Tim. The pan can get hot." Tim put some yummy food in the pan. They watched the food cook. It smelled so good! Tim's mom said, "It's time to eat!" They put the food on a plate and sat down to eat. Tim and his mom both took a bite of the yummy snack. Tim said, "This is so good, mom! I want to make more yummy snacks!" His mom smiled and said, "We can make more yummy snacks together, Tim." From that day on, Tim and his mom loved to go to the kitchen and make yummy snacks with the pan. They had lots of fun cooking and eating together.
