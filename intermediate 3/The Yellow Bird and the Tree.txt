Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yellow Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a yellow bird. The yellow bird had a friend, a red bird. They liked to play together in the big tree near the house. One day, they found a hammer under the tree. The hammer was big and heavy. The yellow bird and the red bird wanted to use the hammer to build a nest. They spread little sticks and leaves all around the tree. They used the hammer to make the nest strong. It was hard work, but they were happy. When the nest was done, the yellow bird and the red bird sat inside. They felt safe and warm. They were very proud of their new home. From that day on, the two friends lived together in the big tree with their strong nest. And they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a dancer. She was a nice dancer. She danced every day. One day, she was angry. She missed her dance. She looked for her dance. She looked up. She looked down. She looked left. She looked right. She could not find it. The dancer was sad. Then, a big twist happened! A little bird came. The bird said, "I have your dance!" The dancer was happy. She danced with the bird. The dancer was not angry anymore.

One day, a gentle cat named Sam went for a walk. Sam saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he climbed, he saw a little bird. The bird looked sad. "Hi, little bird," Sam said with a smile. "Why are you sad?" "I want to know how tall I am, but I don't have a measure," the bird replied. Sam had an idea. He called his friend, the ant. The ant had a long line of friends. Sam asked the ant and his friends to stand in a line next to the bird. The bird was as tall as five ants! The bird was happy and smiled. But then, something unexpected happened. A strong wind blew, and the tree started to shake. Sam and the bird held on tight. When the wind stopped, they saw that the tree had moved! Now, the tree was next to the bird's family. Everyone was happy and safe. The bird thanked Sam and the ants for their help. They all smiled and played together for the rest of the day.

One day, a small girl named Lily went to watch a show. At the show, many people were there to perform. Lily was very excited to see all the fun acts. She clapped her hands and laughed as each person did their tricks. Lily's favorite act was a funny man who did magic. The funny man had a big hat and pulled things from his pocket. "Look, I have a spicy pepper in my pocket!" he said. Lily giggled as the funny man pretended to eat the spicy pepper and made a funny face. After the show, Lily went home and told her mom all about the fun she had. "Mom, I want to perform like the funny man!" Lily said. Her mom smiled and said, "You can be anything you want, Lily." From that day on, Lily practiced her own tricks and made her family laugh.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a thin cat named Tim. Tim was always hungry. He loved to eat. One day, Tim saw a cupboard. He thought, "Maybe there is food in there." Tim tried to open the cupboard, but it was too high. He jumped and jumped, but he could not reach it. Tim was sad. He wanted the food. He was so hungry that he started to tremble. Then, a big dog named Max saw Tim. Max wanted to help the thin cat. Max went to the cupboard and opened it with his nose. Inside, they found a big bowl of food. Tim and Max ate the food together. They were not hungry anymore. They became good friends and lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a quiet bush near his house. Tim had an idea. He wanted to build something near the bush. He ran to get his blocks. Tim started to build a tower with his blocks. His friend, Sam, came over. Sam said, "I want to help build the tower too!" So, Tim and Sam worked together to make the tower taller and taller. As they were building, a bird flew out of the quiet bush! It was a mommy bird. She had a nest in the bush. Tim and Sam looked at each other and smiled. They decided to build a little house for the bird family next to the tower. The mommy bird was happy, and Tim and Sam had a fun day playing and building together.
