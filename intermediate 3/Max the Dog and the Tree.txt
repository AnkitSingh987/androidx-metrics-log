Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Tree

One day, a little dog named Max was very nervous. He had a big idea. He wanted to take a nap on top of the tall tree. Max told his friend, a cat named Lily. "Max, that's not a good idea," said Lily. "Dogs don't nap in trees. Cats do." But Max didn't listen. He climbed up the tree, higher and higher. He was so high up that he could see his whole yard. He felt proud and excited. Max found a big branch to nap on. As he slept, the wind started to blow. The tree shook and Max woke up. He was scared and tried to climb down, but he fell. Max got hurt and learned that he should have listened to Lily.

One day, a silly cat named Tom set out to make a sandwich. He put cheese, ham, and lettuce on the bread. Tom was so proud of his sandwich. He wanted to show his friend, a dog named Max. Tom went outside to find Max. He held the sandwich high in the air so Max could see it. Max saw the sandwich and wagged his tail. He was so happy to see Tom and the sandwich. But then, a big wind came. It blew the sandwich out of Tom's hand. The sandwich flew far away and landed in a muddy puddle. Tom and Max were very sad. They could not eat the sandwich now. It was a bad day for them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty bracelet that she loved very much. One day, she saw that her bracelet was weak and it made her sad. Lily's mom saw her sad face and asked, "What's wrong, Lily?" Lily showed her mom the weak bracelet. Her mom said, "Don't worry, we can repair it. Let's go see Mr. Fox, he can help us fix it." They went to see Mr. Fox, who was very good at fixing things. Mr. Fox looked at the weak bracelet and said, "I can repair this for you, but I need a strong string to make it strong again." Lily and her mom went to find a strong string for the bracelet. They found the strong string and gave it to Mr. Fox. He worked hard to repair the weak bracelet. When he was done, the bracelet was strong and pretty again. Lily was very happy and thanked Mr. Fox for fixing her bracelet. From that day on, Lily took good care of her bracelet and it stayed strong and pretty for a very long time.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to study. He found many things interesting. He would look at books with big pictures and learn about animals, cars, and trees. One day, Tim saw a small bug in the bathroom. He thought it was very interesting. He watched the bug walk on the wall and wondered what kind of bug it was. He wanted to study it and learn more about it. Tim showed the bug to his mom. She told him it was called an ant. Tim thought ants were very interesting. He went back to the bathroom to study the ant some more. He watched it walk and carry things. Tim was happy to learn about ants and wanted to find more interesting things to study.

Once upon a time, there was a big park. In the park was a big bench. One day, a little girl was sitting on the bench. She was 3 years old and she liked to play in the park. Suddenly, a fairy appeared! "I can grant you one wish!" the fairy said. The little girl thought for a moment. "I want to win!" she said. The fairy smiled. "Your wish is granted!" Suddenly, the little girl felt a magical power. She ran as fast as lightning! She won the race, to the surprise of everyone. Everyone cheered for the little girl. She was so happy to have won! The little girl smiled and thanked the fairy. The fairy winked at her. "You should always believe in yourself," the fairy said, before disappearing. And, with that, the little girl went back to her bench, knowing that she had won.
