Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rabbit and the Woods

Once, there was a little rabbit who lived in the woods. One day, he could feel the chills and he started to shiver in fear. He saw the scary lions coming closer and he started to run away. His heart was beating very fast. He looked around and he saw a clear stream near the waste. He quickly jumped in the stream and the lions stopped. They were too scared to come near the water. The little rabbit was so relieved, he thought how he would have been in danger if he had stayed still. He knew that it was important to be brave but also wise and know when to move away. He was so thankful to the clear stream where he found safety. The little rabbit learned a lesson that day: it was possible to be brave and still keep away from the danger.

One day, a boy named Tim had a tie. He did not like the tie. It was big and itchy. Tim was very sad. He did not want to wear the tie. He thought the tie was miserable. Tim's mom said, "You have to wear the tie. It is a special day." Tim did not want to, but he put on the tie. He was not happy. He walked to the park with his mom. He hoped the park would make him feel better. At the park, Tim saw a dog. The dog saw Tim's tie. The dog liked the tie. It jumped up and took the tie off Tim. The dog ran away with the tie. Tim was so happy! He did not have to wear the tie anymore. The dog made Tim's day better.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a rare, big ice cream cone. It could talk! The ice cream cone had a friend named Tim. Tim was a little boy who loved to listen to his ice cream cone friend. One sunny day, Tim and the ice cream cone went to the park to play. The ice cream cone said, "Tim, listen! I hear a bird singing." Tim listened and heard the bird too. They both smiled and continued to play. As they played, a little girl named Lily came to the park. She saw Tim and the talking ice cream cone. Lily said, "Wow! A talking ice cream cone is very rare!" Tim and the ice cream cone became friends with Lily, and they all played happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat carrots. One day, he saw a big, yummy carrot in the garden. But, it was in a place he was not allowed to go. Bob asked his friend, the bird, "Can you help me get the carrot? I am not allowed to go there." The bird said, "Okay, I will help you, but you must be careful." They tried to get the carrot, but it was not easy. The bird flew up high to see if anyone was watching. The clumsy bunny hopped closer to the carrot. Suddenly, a big dog came running and barking at them. They got scared and ran away. Bob and the bird hid behind a tree. They were sad they could not get the carrot. Then, the bird had an idea. "I will distract the dog, and you can grab the carrot," said the bird. Bob agreed and they tried again. The bird flew around the dog, making him chase her. The clumsy bunny hopped quickly and grabbed the carrot. They ran away together, happy they got the yummy carrot. From that day on, they shared many more adventures and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Lily. She was three years old and had long blonde hair which she loved to play with. One day, she was playing in the park and noticed something strange in the bushes. It seemed like a shirt. She was a bit scared but was also curious. She decided to take a closer look. When she reached the bushes, Lily saw that the shirt was pink and had green spots all over it. She thought it looked funny and wanted to try it on. As she took off her own shirt and put on the pink one with green spots, Lily heard a voice in the bushes which said, "That shirt looks scary on you." Lily was so scared that she screamed and ran home. When she got inside, her mum asked her what had happened. Lily said, " There was a scary voice in the bushes and it said the shirt looked scary on me." Her mum smiled and said, "I think the shirt looks very pretty on you. Don't worry - I'll protect you from the scary voice." Lily was glad that her mum was there to help her. From then on, she always felt safe and never went near the scary voice in the bushes again.
