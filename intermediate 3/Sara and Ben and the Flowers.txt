Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Flowers

Sara and Ben were twins who liked to play in the garden. One day, they saw a big, bright bulb in the ground. They wondered what it was. "Maybe it is a ball," Sara said. "Maybe it is a toy," Ben said. They decided to dig it out and see. They used their hands and a stick to make a hole. They pulled and pulled, but the bulb was very hard and heavy. "Help me, Sara," Ben said. "Help me, Ben," Sara said. They pulled together, but the bulb did not move. They got very tired and dirty. Just then, their mom came out and saw them. She was not happy. "What are you doing, Sara and Ben?" she asked. "We are trying to get this bulb, mom," they said. "That is not a bulb, that is a bow," mom said. "It is a flower that will grow in the spring. You should not dig it out, you should let it sleep in the ground until it is ready to bloom." Sara and Ben felt sorry. They did not know it was a bow. They liked flowers. "We are sorry, mom," they said. Mom hugged them and said, "It is okay, Sara and Ben. You were curious, and that is good. But you should also be careful and respectful of nature. Nature has its own time and way of doing things. Sometimes, you have to wait and see what happens." Sara and Ben nodded. They learned a lesson. They put the bow back in the hole and covered it with dirt. They said, "Good night, bow. See you in the spring." They went inside with mom and washed their hands and faces. They were very sleepy. They had a nice dream about flowers.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emily. She had a cat named Fluffy. Emily and Fluffy loved to play together. They played with toys, they played in the park, and they played with words. Emily was learning a new language, and Fluffy was learning too. They thought it was fun, not boring. One day, while they were playing in the park, a big dog came running towards them. The dog was barking loudly. Emily was scared, but Fluffy jumped in front of her. Fluffy wanted to protect Emily. Fluffy hissed at the big dog, and the dog stopped barking. Emily hugged Fluffy and said, "Thank you for protecting me!" They went home and told Emily's mom the story. Mom was proud of Fluffy for being brave. That night, Emily and Fluffy practiced their new language together. They were happy to have each other, and they knew they would always protect each other.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his dog, Max. They went to the park and saw a thick tree. Tim wanted to climb the tree, but Max wanted to chew a stick. Tim said, "Max, look at this tree! Let's climb it!" But Max kept chewing his stick. Tim tried to climb the thick tree, but it was too big for him. He looked at Max and said, "Max, can you help me climb the tree?" Max stopped chewing his stick and looked at Tim. Max barked and wagged his tail. Max found a big, thick branch and pulled it down for Tim to hold on to. Tim held the branch and climbed up the tree. He was so happy that he could see the whole park from up there. He said, "Thank you, Max! We did it together!" They played in the park all day, climbing trees and chewing sticks. When it was time to go home, Tim and Max were very tired. They walked home, hand in paw, and couldn't wait to come back to the park the next day.

Once upon a time there was a dragon. It was quiet and lived in the forest. One day, it got very thirsty. It needed something to drink. So, the dragon ventured out of the forest to find some water. On its way, the dragon saw a stream. But the water didn't look tasty. So, the dragon kept walking until it spotted a pond. The pond looked very refreshing so the dragon decided to take a drink. When the dragon finished its drink, it felt content and flew back to the forest. And from then on, the dragon visited the pond often. Whenever it was thirsty, it would take a nice, quiet drink from the pond.
