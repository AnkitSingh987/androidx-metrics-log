Category: Intermediate
Title: The White Dove and the Girl

One day, a little girl saw a white dove in the sky. She called out to it and waved her arms in the air, but the dove flew away. Suddenly, the girl heard a voice coming from behind her. She turned around to see an old man. "Why did you call out to the dove?" said the old man. The little girl answered, "I wanted it to come play with me." The old man smiled and said, "The dove will never come down if you just call out. You need to give it something to answer." The girl was confused. "What do I need to give it?" The old man looked up at the sky and said, "Do you see the white clouds in the sky?" "Yes," said the girl. "Try to give the dove one of those. If you do that, the dove may answer your call," the old man said. The little girl looked up at the sky, grabbed a handful of fluffy clouds and threw them into the air. Sure enough, the white dove flew down and landed at the girl's feet. She smiled and the dove cuddled up to her.

Once upon a time, there was a polite cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with her friend, a little girl named Lucy. They loved to play together and help each other. One day, Lucy wanted to bake a cake. She mixed all the ingredients and put the cake in the oven. Then, she went to play with Kitty. While they were playing, Lucy forgot about the cake in the oven. Kitty smelled the cake and said, "Lucy, the cake!" Lucy ran to the oven and took the cake out just in time. It was not burned. Lucy said, "Thank you, Kitty, for reminding me!" They both enjoyed the cake together and had a fun day.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to make cookies. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Yes, let's make normal cookies." Lucy and her mom put all the things they need in a big bowl. Lucy started to stir the mix. She stirred and stirred, but the mix was not normal. It was too hard to stir. Lucy was sad. Her mom said, "Don't worry, we can fix it." They added more water to the mix. Lucy stirred again. This time, the mix was normal. They made the cookies and put them in the oven. When the cookies were done, they took them out of the oven. Lucy and her mom ate the cookies. They were very happy. The cookies were yummy and normal.

Once upon a time, a bossy cat lived near a big mountain. The cat loved to match things. She would match socks, toys, and even her food. Every day, she would go out and find new things to match. One day, as the cat was walking near the mountain, she saw a big box. The box was full of things that did not match. She was very upset. The cat decided to fix the box and make everything match. As the cat started to match the things in the box, something unexpected happened. The box began to shake and move. Suddenly, the box opened up and turned into a big, friendly dog. The dog had been hiding in the box all along! The cat was surprised, but the dog just wanted to play. So, the bossy cat and the big dog became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, loud bird named Bob. Bob liked to talk a lot. One day, he saw a big barrel in the yard. Bob flew to the barrel and started to talk to it. "Hi, barrel! How are you today?" Bob asked. The barrel did not talk back. Bob felt sad. He thought maybe the barrel did not like him. He tried to talk to the barrel again, but it still did not talk back. Bob's friend, a small, quiet mouse named Tim, saw Bob talking to the barrel. Tim went to Bob and said, "Bob, the barrel cannot talk. It is not alive like us." Bob felt better knowing the barrel did not dislike him. The next day, Bob saw Tim trying to get some cheese from a high shelf. Bob used the barrel to help Tim reach the cheese. They both learned that even if something cannot talk, it can still be helpful. And they lived happily ever after.
