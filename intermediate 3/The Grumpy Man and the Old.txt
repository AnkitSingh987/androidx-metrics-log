Category: Intermediate
Title: The Grumpy Man and the Old

Once upon a time there was a grumpy old man who walked around town carrying a light. Everyone did not understand why the old man carried the light, but they were too afraid to ask him. Every morning the old man went around town and reminded everyone to be careful. He said, "Remember to always keep your light burning, because sometimes bad things can happen when you are not careful." Everyone would listen and nod, but spent their days wandering around with no light. One day he warned a group of children about walking around when it was dark. The grumpy old man shouted, "Don't forget your light!" But the children ignored him and went off into the dark. Suddenly the children heard a loud scream. They quickly ran back to the old man to find that he had been attacked by a robber! He had been seriously injured and had to be taken to the hospital. The children were very saddened to learn what had happened and they never forgot the old man's words. It was a sad reminder that if you want to stay safe then it is important to keep your light lit at all times.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to play in the deep mud. One day, he found a big stick. He wanted to split the stick to make two. Tom asked his friend, Sam, "Can you help me split this stick?" Sam said, "Yes, let's use a big rock." They found a big rock and used it to split the stick. Now, they had two sticks. Tom and Sam walked to the park. They saw a tree with a pick on it. They used their sticks to get the pick. They were happy and played with the pick all day.

Sara loves penguins. She has many toys of penguins. She likes to play with them and pretend they are her friends. One day, she goes to the zoo with her mom and dad. She is very happy to see the real penguins. "Look, mom, dad, penguins!" Sara says. She runs to the fence and watches the penguins swim and slide on the ice. They are black and white and have funny beaks. Sara thinks they are cute and funny. "Can I bathe with the penguins, mom?" Sara asks. She wants to touch the penguins and play with them in the water. "No, Sara, you can't bathe with the penguins," mom says. "They are not toys. They are rare animals. They need to be safe and happy in their home. You can look at them and learn about them, but you can't touch them." Sara feels sad. She wants to be friends with the penguins. She sees one penguin that is smaller and has a yellow spot on its head. It looks different from the other penguins. Sara thinks it is special and rare. "Mom, what is that penguin?" Sara asks. She points to the small penguin with the yellow spot. "That is a rockhopper penguin, Sara," mom says. "It is a rare kind of penguin. It lives in far away places where there are rocks and cliffs. It can hop and climb very well. It is very brave and smart." Sara likes the rockhopper penguin. She thinks it is cool and pretty. She smiles and waves to it. The rockhopper penguin looks at Sara and makes a loud noise. It sounds like it is saying hello. "Hello, rockhopper penguin," Sara says. "You are rare and I like you. Maybe one day I can bathe with you. But for now, I will just watch you and be your friend." The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play with her toys. One day, Mia's mom gave her a big sheet. Mia was so happy. She wanted to make a tent with the sheet. Mia asked her mom, "Can I use the sheet to make a tent?" Her mom said, "Yes, I permit you to use the sheet." Mia said, "Thank you, Mom!" She took the sheet and some chairs to make the tent. When the tent was ready, Mia invited her friends to play with her. They all had fun playing in the tent. They played with all the available toys. At the end of the day, Mia and her friends were very happy. They loved playing with the sheet and the toys, and they couldn't wait to do it again.
