Category: Intermediate
Title: Benny and the Grass

One day, Benny went outside to play on his lawn. Every day he enjoyed hopping, running, and twirling around the green grass. Today was different, though. Benny noticed something on the grass. It wasn't green- it was yellow and brown. He asked his mom, "Mom, why is the grass brown?" "It's because it hasn't been taken care of," replied Mom. "We need to work hard to improve it." Benny wanted to help, but he didn't know how. Then Mom gave him an idea. "Let's do some gardening! We can put some special food and water on the lawn to help it get better." So Benny grabbed a shovel and a bucket and they went to work. After a few hours, they stepped back and admired the lawn. It had changed from brown and yellow to a lush, green color. Benny was so proud of what they had done together. But before he could jump and play, Mom said, "Remember to always keep your lawn healthy! A bitter lawn won't help you play." Benny nodded and smiled. He had learned an important lesson - taking care of his lawn was just as important as having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite bottle. It was his original bottle, the one he got when he was a baby. He loved this bottle very much. One day, Tim's mom said they were going to the park. Tim was very excited. He wanted to bring his bottle with him. But when he looked for it, he could not find it. Tim was sad because he missed his bottle. Tim and his mom went to the park without the bottle. They played on the swings and the slide. Tim had a lot of fun. When they got home, Tim's mom found the original bottle under his bed. Tim was so happy to have his bottle back. He hugged it and promised never to miss it again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the sea with his mom. They saw a big, high rock. On the rock, there was a dolphin. The dolphin was sad. Tim asked the dolphin, "Why are you sad?" The dolphin said, "I want to go back to the sea, but I can't. I need to pay the sea king first." Tim and his mom helped the dolphin. They gave the sea king a big shell. The sea king was happy. He let the dolphin go back to the sea. But then, something unexpected happened. The dolphin jumped high and turned into a sea king too! He said, "Thank you, Tim and mom! Now I can be with my friend, the sea king." Tim and his mom were very happy. They waved goodbye to the two sea kings and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Missy. She liked to play with her ball. One day, she saw a note under her bed. The note was big and easy to see. Missy did not know who put the note there, but she was very excited. The note said, "Find me in the big tree." Missy thought for a moment. She remembered the big tree near her house. Missy ran to the big tree and looked up. She saw a big bird sitting on a branch. The bird had her ball! Missy tried to jump and get her ball, but it was not easy. She tried again and again. The bird saw Missy and dropped the ball. Missy was happy to have her ball back. She knew the bird was the one who left the note. From that day on, Missy and the bird were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was 3 years old and very excited to go to school. On the first day of school, Tom walked into the classroom and saw all the other kids. He was very scared. He didn't want to speak to anyone. But then, he felt something in his pocket. He was happy to find out that it was a special rock. He held it in his hand and smiled proudly. Tom felt brave enough to talk to the other kids. He showed them the rock in his pocket and told them all about it. He said it was magical and could make wishes come true. The other kids were so excited to hear this. Soon, all the kids in the classroom were speaking loudly and happily. Tom was very proud. He had faced his fear and made new friends. He knew that the special rock in his pocket had helped him along the way.
