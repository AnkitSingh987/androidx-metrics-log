Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Tim and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a small house, there were two friends, Tom and Tim. They liked to play, laugh, and share their toys. One day, Tom told a joke to Tim. Tim laughed very hard at the joke. They were very happy. Later, a poor dog came to their house. The dog was hungry and sad. Tom and Tim wanted to help the dog. They shared their food with the dog. The dog ate the food and felt better. But one day, the dog went away. Tom and Tim looked for the dog everywhere, but they could not find it. They felt very sad. They did not laugh or tell jokes anymore. The dog never came back, and Tom and Tim missed their friend.

Lily liked to draw with her crayons. She made many pictures of animals, flowers and houses. One day, she decided to draw a dragon. She used green, red and yellow crayons to make a big dragon with wings, scales and fire. She showed her picture to her mom. "Look, mom, I made a dragon!" she said proudly. "Wow, Lily, that's a very creative dragon!" her mom said. "I like how you used different colors and shapes. Where does your dragon live?" Lily thought for a moment. "My dragon lives in a cave on a mountain. He likes to fly and play with other dragons. He is very friendly and brave." "That sounds like a fun place to live. Do you think your dragon would like to come and visit us?" her mom asked. Lily smiled. "Yes, I think he would. He likes to meet new friends. Maybe he can come tomorrow and play with me and my toys." "OK, Lily, that sounds like a good idea. But remember, dragons are very big and loud. You have to be careful and gentle with them. And don't forget to share your crayons with him, OK?" "OK, mom, I will. I can't wait to see my dragon!" Lily said. She hugged her picture and put it on her wall. She hoped her dragon would come soon and make her day more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Max. Max loved to play with his ball all day. One day, Max's ball rolled under a big tree. Max went to get it but saw a pick under the tree too. Max saw a little girl named Lily. Max said, "Lily, look! I found a pick under the tree." Lily was happy and said, "Max, can we use the pick to dig in the dirt?" Max said, "Yes, let's do it!" As Max and Lily dug in the dirt, they found a big box. They never thought they would find something like this. Max said, "Lily, you mentioned digging, and now we found a big box!" They opened the box and found lots of toys. Max and Lily played with the toys all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit named Bob. Bob was an incredible rabbit. He could jump very high and run very fast. He lived in a small house in the woods with his friend, Tom the turtle. One day, Bob and Tom went to the park to play. Bob said, "Tom, let's give the ball to each other and play catch!" Tom agreed, and they started to play. They were having a lot of fun, and Bob was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down from the sky and took the ball away. Bob and Tom were very sad. They tried to get the ball back, but the bird flew away with it. They went home without their ball and the day ended badly.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Penny lived in a small house. Penny was a happy girl, but sometimes she felt troubled. She didn't like it when her friends were sad. One sunny day, Penny went to play in the park. She saw a small boy who looked troubled. He was sitting on the swings all alone. Penny walked up to the boy and asked, "Why are you sad?" The boy looked at her and said, "I lost my toy car." Penny wanted to help the boy feel better. So, she took his hand and said, "Let's find your toy car together!" The boy smiled and they started to look for the toy car. They searched high and low, under the slides and behind the trees. Finally, Penny found the toy car hidden in the bushes! The boy was so happy that he started to laugh. Penny laughed too. They played together all day long, and the boy was not troubled anymore. From that day on, Penny and the boy became best friends. They always helped each other when they were troubled. And they laughed and played together every day, making each other happy.
