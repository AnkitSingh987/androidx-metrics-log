Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Great Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a great big dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friend, a little cat named Daisy. One day, they found a pretty diamond on the ground. They looked at it and thought it was very special. Max said to Daisy, "Let's take the diamond to our friend, Mr. Owl. He will know what to do with it." So, they went to see Mr. Owl. When they showed him the diamond, Mr. Owl's eyes opened wide. "Oh, this is a very special diamond," he said. "But you must be careful not to embarrass yourself with it." Max and Daisy didn't understand, but they took the diamond back to their play area. They started to play a game with the diamond, tossing it back and forth. Then, something unexpected happened. Every time they threw the diamond, they would trip and fall. They started to laugh at each other, not realizing they were embarrassing themselves. In the end, Max and Daisy learned that the diamond made them clumsy, but they still had fun playing with it. They decided to keep the diamond as a reminder to always have fun and not worry about being embarrassed.

One day, a boy named Tom found a magazine on the floor. He thought it was a powerful book. He wanted to read it. He sat down and began to look at the pictures. Tom saw a big, strong man stepping on rocks in the magazine. Tom wanted to be powerful like the man in the magazine. He thought if he stepped on rocks like the man, he would be strong too. So, Tom went outside to find rocks to step on. He found some small rocks and started stepping on them. As Tom was stepping on the rocks, something unexpected happened. The rocks started to move! They were not rocks at all, but little turtles! Tom was surprised and stopped stepping on them. He was happy to find new friends and played with the turtles. In the end, Tom learned that being kind and gentle is more powerful than being strong.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside in the snow. One day, it was very cold, and Tim's mom told him not to go out. "Tim, don't go out, you will freeze," she said. But Tim didn't listen. Tim went outside and saw a black cat. The cat was cold too. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he picked it up. The cat didn't like it and tried to run away. "No, cat, I want to help you!" Tim said. They both started to feel very cold. Tim and the cat were freezing, and they wanted to go back inside. But the door was locked! Tim's mom had locked it because she was mad that he didn't listen. They both sat outside, cold and sad. The moral of the story is always to listen to your mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a normal boy who loved to play outside. One day, Tim saw something shiny in the sky. It was a star! The star was so bright and pretty. Tim wanted to keep the star. He asked his mom, "Mom, can I have the star?" His mom said, "Yes, but you must take good care of it." Tim was so happy. He went outside and found a nice spot to bury the star. He dug a hole and put the star inside. Then, he covered it with dirt. Every day, Tim watered the spot where he buried the star. After a week, a beautiful plant grew. It had shiny leaves that twinkled like stars. Tim knew that he had done a good job taking care of the star. He was proud and showed his mom the plant. They both smiled and knew that they had a special star plant in their yard.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was a glad girl who liked to help her mom. One day, her mom asked her to mail a letter. Mia took the letter and walked to the mailbox. On her way, she met a nice lady. The lady said, "Hi, Mia! I need some lotion for my hands. Can you help me find some?" Mia said, "Yes, I can help you!" They went to the store and found a bottle of lotion. The lady was very glad and said, "Thank you, Mia!" After helping the lady, Mia remembered the letter. She went to the mailbox and mailed it. When she got home, her mom was glad Mia had helped the lady and mailed the letter. They had a happy day together.
