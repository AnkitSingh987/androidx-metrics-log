Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird and the Bottle

Once upon a time there was a little bird who was feeling very scared. He was all alone and he had been unable to find his nest. Suddenly he heard a loud noise. He looked around and saw a bottle that was rolling off a cliff. He knew he had to help. He flew over and grabbed the bottle in his beak, and started to fly back up. But it was very hard. The bottle was too heavy for the poor little bird. He started to get tired and he thought he wouldn't be able to rescue the bottle. But then a strong wind blew and it helped the bird to fly faster. He flapped his wings as hard as he could and he was able to fly back up with the bottle. He was so successful! The little bird smiled as he placed the bottle back on the safety of the cliff. He had rescued the bottle and he felt so proud!

One day, a chubby cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little girl with ice-cream. Tom was hungry and wanted some ice-cream too. So, he went up to the girl and said, "Can I have some ice-cream?" The little girl looked at Tom and said, "Yes, you can have some ice-cream, but you have to do something for me first." Tom was happy and asked, "What do you want me to do?" The girl said, "I want you to shut the door of my house. It is open, and I cannot reach it." Tom went to the girl's house and saw the door was open. He jumped up and shut the door. But when he shut the door, something unexpected happened. The door turned into a big ice-cream cone! Tom was very surprised but also happy. He went back to the girl and said, "I shut the door, and it turned into ice-cream!" The girl laughed and said, "That is my magic door. Now we both can have ice-cream!" They both ate the ice-cream and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a warm waffle. It was very yummy. The warm waffle liked to wear a little hat. The hat was red. One day, the warm waffle went for a walk. It saw a cat. The cat was big and fluffy. The cat liked the warm waffle's hat. The cat wanted to wear the hat too. The warm waffle and the cat became friends. They played together. They took turns wearing the red hat. They were very happy. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. The cat saw a bottle on the ground. The cat said to the dog, "Look at that brilliant bottle! Let's see what's inside." The dog picked up the bottle with his mouth and shook it. They heard a sound coming from the bottle. The cat and dog were very curious. They wanted to know what was making the sound. The cat used her paw to open the bottle. When the bottle was open, a tiny mouse jumped out! The mouse said, "Thank you for letting me out of the bottle! I was stuck in there for a long time." The cat and dog were surprised to see the mouse. They all became friends and played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Peter. Peter lived in a big house with his family. One evening, Peter's family invited some people over for dinner. When the guests arrived, they brought some wine. The wine was spicy and smelled funny. Peter's mom noticed how curious he was and said, "Peter, why don't you try some of this wine?" Peter shook his head. "No, mommy. I don't like spicy stuff!" But Peter's mom just smiled and said, "Sometimes it's better to rise above what you're scared of. Give it a try, you might like it!" So Peter carefully took a small sip, and to his surprise, he liked it! The wine was sweet and tasty--not spicy like he thought. Peter was so excited that he lifted the glass up high. "Look at me, I'm rising like a brave boy!" he shouted. Everyone cheered and laughed. They were so proud of Peter for rising above his fear and trying something new. He had figured out that tasting something spicy wasn't so scary after all.
