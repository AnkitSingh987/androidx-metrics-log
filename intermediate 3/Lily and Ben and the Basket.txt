Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Basket

Lily and Ben are playing in the kitchen. They see a big basket of red tomatoes on the table. Lily likes tomatoes. She picks one up and says, "Look, Ben, this is a tomato. It is red and round and juicy." Ben nods and says, "I like tomatoes too. Can we eat them?" Lily shakes her head and says, "No, we can't eat them now. Mommy said we have to sort them first. She said some are big and some are small, and some are good and some are bad. We have to put them in different piles." Ben asks, "How do we sort them?" Lily says, "We have to look at them and touch them and smell them. If they are big and firm and smell good, they are good tomatoes. We put them in this pile. If they are small and soft and smell bad, they are bad tomatoes. We put them in that pile." Ben says, "OK, let's sort them." He picks up a tomato and looks at it. It is long and wrinkled and has a hole in it. He says, "This is a bad tomato. It is not round and it has a hole. It smells bad too." He puts it in the bad pile. Lily picks up a tomato and looks at it. It is big and smooth and has a green stem. She says, "This is a good tomato. It is round and it has a stem. It smells good too." She puts it in the good pile. They keep sorting the tomatoes until the basket is empty. They have two piles of tomatoes, one good and one bad. They are proud of their work. They call Mommy and show her what they did. Mommy smiles and says, "Well done, Lily and Ben. You sorted the tomatoes very well. You are very smart and helpful. Now you can have some good tomatoes for a snack. And we can throw away the bad tomatoes. They are not good for anything." Lily and Ben are happy. They wash their hands and eat some good tomatoes. They are red and round and juicy. They say, "Thank you, Mommy. We love tomatoes. And we love sorting too."

Once upon a time, there was an elderly cat named Navy. Navy liked to play outside, but one day it was very cold. Navy went outside and started to shiver because of the cold air. Navy saw a big, warm coat on the ground. The coat was perfect to help Navy not shiver anymore. Navy put on the coat and felt warm and happy. No more shivering for Navy! But, guess what? The coat was not just a coat. It was a magic coat! When Navy put on the coat, it made her jump very high. Navy was so surprised and had so much fun jumping high in the air. The cold day turned into a fun day, all thanks to the magic coat.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Bob. Bob loved to jump and play all day. One day, he saw a big bowl of rice. Bob was very hungry, so he jumped in the bowl to eat the rice. As Bob was eating, a serious bird named Sam came. Sam said, "Bob, why are you in my rice? That is not nice!" Bob looked at Sam and said, "I am sorry, Sam. I was hungry and I love rice." Sam thought for a moment and said, "It's okay, Bob. We can share the rice. But next time, please ask first." Bob and Sam ate the rice together and became good friends. They played and jumped every day, and always remembered to share.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the beach with her mom. Amy loved to play in the sand. She took her useful toy shovel and pail to build a big sand castle. Amy started to dig and spread the sand to make her castle. Then, she saw a little crab walking by. The crab looked at her and said, "Hello, can I help you build your sand castle?" Amy smiled and said, "Yes, you can help me. We can make the biggest sand castle together!" The crab used its claws to help spread the sand and make the castle even bigger. When they finished the sand castle, Amy and the crab were very happy. They played together all day at the beach. Amy's mom watched them and smiled. She knew they had made a great new friend.
