Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yellow Panda and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a yellow panda. The yellow panda lived in a big forest. The yellow panda had many friends. One day, the yellow panda saw a sign. The sign had a picture of a big tree. The yellow panda showed the sign to his friends. They all looked at the sign. They wanted to find the big tree. The yellow panda and his friends walked and walked. They looked for the big tree. At last, they found the big tree. The yellow panda and his friends played under the big tree. They were very happy. They played all day. The yellow panda and his friends loved the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit. The rabbit was very serious. He liked to hop and play with his friends in the forest. But the rabbit had a big dream. He wanted to sell carrots to all his friends. One day, the serious rabbit went to the market. He took all the carrots he could find. He put the carrots on a big table. Then he waited for his friends to come and buy the carrots. His friends came to the market. They saw the serious rabbit and his carrots. They were happy to buy the carrots from him. They all said, "Thank you, rabbit!" The serious rabbit was so happy that he could sell carrots to his friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big oak tree stood tall in the icy cold. A little boy named Tim walked by the oak tree. He saw a shiny red ball up in the tree. Tim wanted the red ball, so he tried to climb the oak tree. As Tim climbed, he saw a big bird. The big bird had the shiny red ball in its beak. Tim asked the bird, "Can I have the red ball, please?" The big bird looked at Tim and then dropped the ball. Tim was happy. But when the red ball fell, it bounced very high. It went up, up, up, and landed on the moon! Tim was sad, but then the big bird flew up to the moon and brought the ball back. Tim was happy again, and he and the big bird played with the red ball all day.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Spot lived in a cozy house. He had a family that loved him, and he was so happy. Spot was always excited when it was time to go out and play in the park. On one sunny day, Spot was walking in the park and he saw something shiny and yummy. He ran over, and it was a bone! He picked it up and looked around to make sure nobody was watching. Spot couldn't believe it, he owned the bone! He was so excited, he could hardly wait to go back to his family and show them. When Spot got back home he ran to show his family what he found. He proudly announced, "I own the bone!" His family cheered and helped him find the perfect spot to hide and save the yummy bone. Spot's family promised to share the bone with him once he found it again. And so, Spot was very happy and ran off to find his special bone. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a black cat sitting on the ground. Tim thought the cat was very pretty. He walked closer to the cat and said, "Hi, cat! What are you doing?" The cat looked at Tim and said, "I am thinking about how to catch a big, tasty fish for my dinner. Do you want to help me?" Tim thought it was a great idea and said, "Yes! I will help you catch a fish." So, Tim and the black cat went to the pond near their house. They saw a lot of fish swimming in the water. The cat jumped in and caught a big fish with its paws. Tim clapped his hands and said, "Good job, cat! You caught a big fish!" The black cat was very happy and thanked Tim for his help. They both went back to their homes and had a tasty dinner. From that day on, Tim and the black cat became the best of friends, and they always played together.
