Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and the Mark

Once upon a time, there were two brothers named Sam and Mark. Sam loved the outdoors and often spent days exploring the weather. Mark on the other hand loved to stay inside and play competitive board games. One day, Sam asked Mark to come outside and play with him in the sun. He said, "Come on Mark, let's go outside and mark together. We can play tag and have so much fun!" But Mark shook his head and said, "I don't want to go outside. I want to stay inside and play my board game. It's way more competitive than tag." Sam was disappointed; he liked spending time with his brother. So he thought of a way for them to still spend time together. "Let's make a compromise," he said to Mark. "I'll play one round of your board game, then you can come outside and play tag with me for one round." Mark thought it over and agreed. Soon enough, they were both playing a game together and having a lot of fun. After the game was over, Sam and Mark went outside to enjoy the sunny weather and played tag until sunset. In the end, Sam and Mark both agreed that it was a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play catch with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim found a ball with a zipper on it. He thought it would be fun to play with. "Spot, let's play!" Tim said. He picked up the ball and threw it. Spot ran fast to catch the ball. When he did, the zipper opened, and a treat fell out. Spot was so happy! Tim and Spot played catch with the zipper ball all day. Each time Tim threw the ball, a treat would fall out for Spot to eat. They had so much fun, and Spot knew that he had the best friend ever.

Once upon a time, there were two happy kittens. One was called Lola and the other was called Milo. They were the best of friends and were always up to something. One day, they were playing in the garden when they spotted a big olive tree. “Lets try to climb the olive tree,” said Lola excitedly. “Ok,” said Milo, “I’ll give it a try”. So both cats started to climb up the tree. After a few minutes, Milo had managed to reach the top of the tree. But Lola was still struggling halfway up. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. It was an old car driving towards them. The cats froze in fear. The car stopped. The driver, an old man, saw the cats in the tree. He climbed out of his car and said: “You two look very brave. Climb down and I’ll drive you home safely. I promise I’ll be very careful”. The cats were so happy that they ran back down the tree and into the car. The old man drove them home safely. The cats thanked the old man for his kindness and waved goodbye as he drove away. From that day on, they never forgot the big olive tree and their happy adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish. The fish had a name. His name was Bob. Bob had a big scale on his side. The scale was perfect and shiny. Bob was very proud of his perfect scale. One day, Bob swam in the water. He saw a new friend. The new friend was a crab. The crab had a name too. Her name was Sue. Sue saw Bob's perfect scale. Sue liked the scale. She said, "Wow! Your scale is very nice!" Bob and Sue played together. They swam and laughed. They had lots of fun. They liked being friends. Bob showed Sue his perfect scale. Sue showed Bob her strong claws. They were happy with each other. Now, Bob and Sue are best friends. They swim and play every day. They are always together. Bob's perfect scale and Sue's strong claws make them special. And they are happy to have a friend who likes them just the way they are.
