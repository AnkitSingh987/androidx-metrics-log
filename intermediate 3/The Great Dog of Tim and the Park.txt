Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Dog of Tim and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite shirt with a zipper. He loved to zip it up and down, up and down. The zipper made him so happy. One day, Tim went to the park with his mommy. They saw a funny dog. The dog liked to joke and play. Tim laughed and laughed at the dog's jokes. The dog made Tim so happy. Tim wanted to show the dog his zipper. He zipped it up and down, up and down. The dog laughed and laughed too. They played and joked all day. Tim, his zipper, and the funny dog were the best of friends.

---

One day, a girl named Sue was playing in her room. She liked to imagine she was a judge. She would look at her toys and tell them if they were good or bad. Today, she saw that her toys were all different. Sue had a big bear, a small cat, and a funny dog. She told the bear he was good because he was big and could give her hugs. She told the cat she was good too, because she was small and could sit on her lap. But when she looked at the funny dog, she didn't know what to say. Then, something unexpected happened. The funny dog started to talk! He said, "Sue, I may be different, but I can make you laugh and smile." Sue was so surprised but then she laughed. She told the funny dog that he was good too, because he made her happy. And from that day on, Sue learned that being different can be a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Bob. Bob liked to play with his toys and run in the park. One day, Bob saw a wallet on the ground. The wallet was tight and hard to open. But Bob wanted to see what was inside. Bob tried to quit looking at the wallet, but he could not. He wanted to know what was inside so bad. He tried to open the wallet with his paws, but it was too tight. Then, he tried to use his teeth, but it was still too tight. Finally, Bob got the wallet open. But, there was nothing inside. Bob was sad that he spent so much time trying to open the wallet. He missed playing with his toys and running in the park. The day was over, and Bob had a bad ending to his day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily was very pretty. All day, she would play with her toys and smile. She had a big, friendly smile that made everyone happy. One day, while Lily was playing outside, she saw a little boy. The boy was very attractive. He had a big smile too. Lily stared at the boy and said, "Hi! My name is Lily. Do you want to play with me?" The little boy stared back at Lily with a big smile and said, "Yes! My name is Tom." Lily and Tom played together all day. They laughed and smiled a lot. They became very good friends. From that day on, Lily and Tom always played together and shared their big smiles with everyone they met.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toy bunny. One day, she saw her mommy wrap a big box. Lily was very excited. She thought the box was for her. Lily asked her mommy, "Is that box for me?" Her mommy smiled and said, "You will see soon." Lily could not wait. She wanted to know what was inside the box. That night, Lily had a dream. In her dream, she saw a big toy bunny in the box. The next day, Lily's mommy said, "It's time to release what's in the box!" Lily was so happy. She opened the box and saw a big toy bunny, just like in her dream. But then, the bunny started to move! It was not a toy bunny; it was a real bunny! Lily was very surprised. Lily felt a little embarrassed because she thought it was a toy bunny. But she was also very happy to have a new friend. She named the bunny Fluffy. Lily and Fluffy played together every day, and they became the best of friends.
