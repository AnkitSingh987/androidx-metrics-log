Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bunny and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a kind bunny named Bob. Bob liked to play with his friends in the park. They would run, jump, and have lots of fun together. One day, they decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. They all wanted to win. Bob and his friends lined up to start the race. They heard "Go!" and they all ran as fast as they could. They ran and ran, and soon, Bob saw that he was in front of all his friends. He was so happy, but he also knew that his friends wanted to win too. As they got close to the finish line, Bob decided to slow down a little bit. He wanted to let his friends catch up to him. They all crossed the finish line together, and they were so happy. They all felt like they had a win. After the race, they were tired and decided to settle down under a big tree. They all agreed that it was the best race ever because they all had fun and shared the win.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tim. Tim loved to play with paper. One day, he found a big book with many papers. The book was about history. Tim liked the pictures in the book. Tim wanted to make a toy from the paper. So, he started to fold the paper. He folded it many times. At last, he made a pretty paper bird. Tim was very happy with his new toy. Tim played with his paper bird all day. He showed it to his friends. They liked the bird too. They all wanted to learn how to fold paper. Tim taught them, and they had fun making paper toys together. And that's how Tim and his friends learned about history and folding paper.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to eat treats. Every day, he would sit by the door and wait for his friend, Tim, to come home from school. Tim always had a treat for Spot in his pocket. One day, Tim came home and found Spot very alert. Spot was jumping up and down, wagging his tail. Tim knew that Spot wanted his treat. Tim reached into his pocket and pulled out a big, yummy treat for Spot. Spot was so happy, he barked and danced around Tim. Spot took the treat and ran to his favorite spot under the tree. He sat down and began to eat the treat. It was so tasty! Spot was very happy that Tim always brought him treats. And Tim was happy to see Spot so excited. They were the best of friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a mysterious restaurant with his mom. The restaurant had many big pictures on the walls. Tim saw a picture of a big cat with a hat. He was very curious about it. "Mom, what is that cat?" Tim asked. His mom did not know the answer. She told him to question the man who worked there. The man said, "That cat is our secret friend. He helps us with a very important job." Tim wanted to know more, but the man told him it was a secret. Tim was sad, but he ate his food. Suddenly, the big cat from the picture jumped out! The cat was real! Everyone in the restaurant was scared. The cat said, "Do not be scared. I am here to help." The cat told Tim that he was there to fix a big problem in the restaurant. The cat found a little mouse in the kitchen. The mouse was eating all the food. The cat caught the mouse and took it outside. Everyone was happy, and Tim got to meet the mysterious cat. The cat told Tim that he was a special friend who helped the restaurant. Tim felt happy and excited. He could not wait to tell his friends about the big cat with a hat.

One day, a little boy named Tom went to the park with his mom. They saw a big, green melon on the ground. Tom's mom said, "Look, a harmless melon! Let's open it and eat it." Tom and his mom tried to open the melon, but it was too hard. A kind man saw them and said, "I can help you open the melon." The man hit the melon, and it opened. But, something unexpected happened! Inside the melon, there were many small toys! Tom was so happy and said, "Wow! I thought we would eat the melon, but we found toys instead!" They all laughed and played with the toys at the park.
