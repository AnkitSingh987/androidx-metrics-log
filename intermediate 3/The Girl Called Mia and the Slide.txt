Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Mia and the Slide

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Mia. She was three years old and very brave. Mia loved Sunday. On Sunday she went to the park with her family. She smiled and laughed so much that it made her family happy. One Sunday Mia decided it was time to be brave and play on the big slide. It was very high. She was scared but the thought of having fun with her family made her happy. Mia thought to herself that it would be foolish to be scared. So she slowly climbed up the ladder and was on the top of the slide. She stared for a few seconds and laughed as she went down the slide. Mia had a wonderful Sunday and she learned that loving to try new things was fun. Her family had lots of fun too and were happy that Mia was brave.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a big ball near the grass. Spot was so excited! Spot ran to the ball and barked, "Woof, woof! I want to play!" The ball did not say anything, but Spot knew it was happy too. Spot and the ball played all day in the grass. They had so much fun! As the sun started to go down, Spot's mom called him, "Spot, time to come in!" Spot was tired but he enjoyed his day with the ball. He knew tomorrow he could play in the grass again. Spot was a very happy dog.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Bob. He lived in a small garage with his family. Bob had a big, round belly and loved to play all day. One day, Bob found a toy that could ring. He played with the toy and made it ring again and again. The sound was so fun and made him very happy. He wanted to show his family the new toy. Bob took the ringing toy to his family in the garage. They all played with the toy and made it ring. The sound filled the garage, making everyone laugh and smile. They played with the ringing toy until the sun went down. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly sock who wanted to balance on a knot. The sock lived in a small house with his best friend, a little shoe. They loved to play together in the garden. One sunny day, the sock saw a big tree with a knot on it. He thought, "I want to balance on that knot!" So, he asked his friend, the shoe, to help him. The shoe agreed and they went to the tree. The smelly sock climbed up the tree and tried to balance on the knot. The shoe watched and cheered him on. The sock wobbled and almost fell, but he kept trying. Finally, the sock was able to balance on the knot! The shoe clapped and the sock felt very happy. They both laughed and played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a clean doctor named Ben. He liked to help people feel better. Every day, he would go to his big office and wait for people to come see him. One day, a little girl named Lily came to see Doctor Ben. She had a toy bear with her. Lily told Doctor Ben that her bear was not feeling well. Doctor Ben looked at the bear and thought about what to do. He had to choose the best way to help the bear feel better. Doctor Ben chose to give the bear a small band-aid for its paw. He put the band-aid on the bear's paw and smiled at Lily. Lily was so happy that her bear felt better. She thanked Doctor Ben and went home with her bear. From that day on, Lily and her bear were always happy and healthy.

Once upon a time, there was a bear called Bob. Bob was frightened. One day he saw a hot thing in the grass. He wanted to take a closer look. Bob slowly walked closer to the hot thing. He was so scared he was shaking. When he reached it, he saw it was a big red hot box. Bob asked, "What is this?" Suddenly he heard a voice from the box. It said, "Bury me!" Bob was so frightened he ran away screaming. He went back to his home in the woods. Bob thought about the hot box all day. He decided he would bury it. The next morning, Bob took a shovel and dug a deep hole in the ground. He placed the hot box in the hole and buried it. When Bob finished he was so happy. From that day on, he was never frightened by the hot box again. The end.
