Category: Intermediate
Title: The Monkey and the Banana

Once upon a time, there was a monkey. He was worried because he had no money. He felt sad and alone. He kept walking and walking until he saw a woman. He asked her: "Do you have any money?" The woman smiled, pat his head and said: "Yes, I do. I can pay you, what do you want?" The monkey thought for a moment, and then said: "I'd like a banana, please." The woman smiled and took out her wallet. She payed the monkey with two bananas and some coins. The monkey thanked her and went on his way. He felt much better now that he had some money. He waved goodbye to the woman as he skipped around the corner and back to his home.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim loved to walk and play outside. One day, he found a thermometer on the ground. It was a tool to tell how hot or cold it was. Tim took the thermometer and went for a walk. He wanted to show it to his friend, Lily. When he got to Lily's house, they looked at the thermometer together. It said it was a warm day. Tim and Lily were happy to play outside with the thermometer. They walked and played until it was time to go home. Tim put the thermometer in his pocket so he could use it again later. The end.

Ben and Mia are best friends. They like to play in the park with their toys. One day, they find a big blue ball under a tree. It is shiny and bouncy. They want to play with it. "Can I have it first?" Ben asks. "No, I saw it first!" Mia says. They both grab the ball and pull. The ball pops and makes a loud noise. Ben and Mia are scared and sad. They drop the ball and cry. A dog comes running to them. It is brown and fluffy. It has a red collar with a tag that says Spot. Spot is a good and dependable dog. He likes to help people. He sees the broken ball and the crying children. He wants to make them happy. Spot picks up the ball with his mouth and brings it to Ben and Mia. He wags his tail and barks. He chews the ball and makes funny noises. Ben and Mia stop crying and look at Spot. They laugh and pet him. They think he is silly and cute. "Thank you, Spot!" Ben says. "You are a nice dog!" Mia says. Spot licks their faces and snuggles with them. He is happy too. He has made new friends. Ben and Mia share their toys with Spot. They play together in the park until it is time to go home. They hug Spot and say goodbye. They promise to come back and play with him again. Spot watches them leave and smiles. He is a happy and dependable dog.

Once, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy, and it was orange. She loved her toy very much. One day, she saw a boy named Tim. Tim had the same orange toy. Sue wanted to have both toys, but Tim did not want to give his toy away. Sue and Tim started to struggle. They both pulled the orange toy, but it did not move. They got very tired and sad. Then, a wise old woman saw them. She told them, "You can both play with the orange toy together. Sharing is better than fighting." Sue and Tim thought about it. They decided to share the orange toy and play together. They became good friends and had lots of fun. The old woman smiled as she watched them play. Sue and Tim learned that sharing is better than fighting, and they were happy.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Jane. Jane wanted to go on an adventure. She looked around for something that could help her and she found a cane. It was the perfect cane for her! Jane put the cane in her hand and it felt just right. She smiled and started off on her adventure. She used her perfect cane to help her walk through the forest. Everywhere she went she was careful not to trip. Jane was so excited to see what was around each corner! Finally, she came to a lake. Jane was amazed. She sat down, put her perfect cane to the side, and watched the sun set. Jane had the most perfect adventure.
