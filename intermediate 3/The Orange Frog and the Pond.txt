Category: Intermediate
Title: The Orange Frog and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little orange frog. The frog lived in a big pond. The frog liked to jump and play all day. One day, the frog saw a small boy. The boy had a toy. The frog wanted to play with the toy too. The boy saw the frog and smiled. He put the toy on the ground for the frog to play with. The frog and the boy played with the toy. They had a lot of fun. After they played, the boy put the frog back near the pond. The frog was happy and said, "Thank you!" The boy and the frog became good friends. They played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny and a big bull. The bull had a long, smooth horn on his head. The bunny liked to tease the bull. Every day, the bunny would hop near the bull and say, "You can't catch me!" One day, the bull was tired of the bunny's teasing. He said, "Bunny, stop teasing me! I don't like it." The bunny felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, bull. I won't tease you anymore." From that day on, the bunny and the bull became good friends. They played together in the forest and had lots of fun. They learned that being kind to each other was much better than teasing.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Emma and Peter. They liked to play together every day. One day, they went to the vegetable shop. Emma wanted to buy a big carrot, but Peter wanted a pumpkin. The shopkeeper was rude and refused to give them either. Emma got angry and refused to move. Peter had an idea. He asked the shopkeeper to give him a veg that they both wanted. Luckily, the shopkeeper agreed. He handed them both a big red pepper. Emma and Peter were happy and excited. They thanked the shopkeeper for being helpful. The shopkeeper smiled and said it was no problem. Emma and Peter held the pepper close. They laughed and enjoyed the day. They ran home and showed their pepper to their mum. She said it was time for dinner and she asked them to wash it before eating. They found out it was delicious!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a big cardboard box. Tim wanted to make a house with the box. His mom said, "I will help you, Tim. I will encourage you to make a good house." They worked together to make the house. Tim's mom was very reliable. She always helped him. They made a door and a window for the house. Tim was happy. One day, Tim heard a noise in the cardboard house. He went to look. Inside, he found a small bird! The bird had made a nest. Tim said, "Hello, bird! You can stay in my house too." The bird was happy, and they became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, rich cat named Spike. Spike liked to stretch his long legs every day. He lived in a big house with a nice family. They loved him very much. One day, Spike saw a big box in the living room. He was very curious about the box. Spike's family was getting ready for a big party. They put up pretty lights and made yummy food. Spike watched them put a big, soft thing in the box. Spike thought it was for him. He was very excited. He stretched his legs and got ready to jump in the box. At the party, lots of people came to play and eat. They all gathered around the big box. Spike stretched and jumped into the box. But, surprise! The soft thing was not for Spike. It was a big cake! Spike landed on the cake and made a big mess. Everyone laughed, and Spike was happy to be the star of the party.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. They had a big blue door that kept them safe at night. Mia loved to play with her toys and dance in the living room. One day, Mia's mom said, "Mia, I hope you will be good today. I need to go to the store. I will be back soon." Mia nodded and promised to be good. She played with her toys while her mom was gone. Mia heard a knock on the big blue door. She knew she should not open it without her mom, but she was curious. She opened the door just a little bit and saw a friendly dog. The dog wagged its tail and Mia felt safe. She let the dog in and they played together until her mom came home. When her mom saw the dog, she smiled and said, "Mia, you were good and kept the door safe. Now we have a new friend to play with." Mia, her mom, and the dog played together happily ever after.
