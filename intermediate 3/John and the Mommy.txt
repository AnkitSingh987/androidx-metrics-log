Category: Intermediate
Title: John and the Mommy

John and mommy went to the store together. They looked at mattresses. Mommy wanted to buy something comfortable but not too expensive. John saw a yellow mattress and said, "Mommy, I like that one!" Mommy was nervous because it looked a bit too expensive. So they started to discuss. John asked, "Mommy, can I have the yellow one?" Mommy said, "Sweetie, I'm not sure. We need to find something that fits in our budget." John thought for a moment, and then suggested, "What about a blue mattress?" Mommy said, "That's a good idea! We can look at the blue ones and see if one if perfect for us." So they looked at the blue mattresses together until they both agreed on one. Mommy said, "That one is just perfect! Let's buy it." John was so excited and gave Mommy a big hug. They took their new blue mattress home. Now it was time for a cozy nap.

Once upon a time, there lived a boy. He liked to whistle on the hill every day. It made him happy. One day, he went to the hill but it was so dark. He looked around and noticed something. It was a thick fog. The boy got scared and ran away. He wanted to go back home but he couldn't remember the way. He kept whistling, but it made no difference. Suddenly, he heard a voice calling to him. It was a monster. The monster said, "I heard you whistle. If you want to go home, you must give me your toy." The boy was so scared that he gave the monster his toy. The monster was happy and said, "Now you will never find your way home." The boy was sad. He was stuck on the hill forever.

Lily was excited to go to the park with her mom and dad. She loved to play on the swings, the slide and the sandbox. She also liked to see the ducks and the squirrels that lived near the pond. When they got to the park, Lily ran to the swings and asked her dad to push her. She felt the wind in her hair and the sun on her face. She laughed and shouted, "Higher, higher!" Her mom watched her from a bench and smiled. She remembered when she was a little girl and played on the same swings with her own mom and dad. She had a memory of them holding her hands and spinning her around. She felt happy and sad at the same time. After a while, Lily got tired of the swings and wanted to try the slide. She climbed up the ladder and slid down with a whoosh. She did it again and again, until she saw a big pile of leaves on the ground. She ran to it and jumped in. She felt the leaves crunch and crackle under her feet and over her head. She giggled and said, "Look, mom, look, dad, I'm a leaf monster!" Her mom and dad joined her and lay down on the leaves. They made leaf angels and threw leaves in the air. They hugged and kissed Lily and told her they loved her. Lily felt warm and cozy and said, "I love you too. This is the best day ever!"

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He was very smart and always knew the right things to do. One day, Jack decided to take a trip to the sea. He went there to rest and look around. It was a beautiful day and the sea looked so peaceful. Jack sat down on the beach and said, "Ah, this is nice." But then, he heard a big wave sound. Jack was a bit scared but then he heard a voice. The voice said, "Don't worry Jack, it's just the sea resting. The sea needs to rest too." Jack smiled and said, "That's true. We all need to rest sometimes." Jack enjoyed the rest of his day at the sea and had a wonderful time.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. He loved to play and run all day. One sunny day, Spot went to the park with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. At the park, Spot saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it, but Lily was not ready. She was talking to her friend. Spot started to complain. He barked and jumped. He was very impatient. Lily saw Spot and knew he wanted to play. She said goodbye to her friend and went to Spot. Lily picked up the ball and threw it far. Spot ran fast to catch it. He was very happy. They played until the sun went down, and then they went home.
