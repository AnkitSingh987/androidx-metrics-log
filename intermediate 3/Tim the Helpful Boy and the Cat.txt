Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Helpful Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a helpful boy named Tim. He had a big staff. Tim loved to help people with his staff. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was sad and scared. Tim wanted to help the cat. Tim tried to use his staff to get the cat down, but the tree was too tall. Tim became sad. He wanted to help the cat, but he did not know how. Then, he saw a big box. Tim had an idea. He put the box under the tree. Tim used his staff to push the cat off the tree. The cat fell into the box. The cat was happy and safe. Tim became happy too. He was helpful with his staff. Everyone was happy in the end.

Once upon a time there was a big bear. He was walking through the forest, shivering in the chilly air. Suddenly he came upon a small foot. He bent closer to take a look. “Who’s there?" he asked in a deep, big voice. “It’s me, little mouse," came the reply. “I’m shivering because it’s so cold." The big bear smiled. “Let me help you! I will carry you on my back," he offered kindly. “Thank you!" the little mouse said, still trembling from the cold. The big bear did as he promised, and they both travelled through the forest until they came to a cozy den. As soon as they were safe and warm, the little mouse stopped shivering. “Thank you for taking me away from the cold! I will never forget your kindness," the little mouse said gratefully. The big bear gave him a big, happy smile. “You’re welcome, friend. Now let’s get some rest."

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lily. She was three years old, and she was curious about everything. One day she was in the kitchen. She saw her mum making coffee. It smelled unusual, so Lily wanted to try some. Her mum said no, but Lily was determined. She asked again and her mum said if Lily could find a way to reverse the coffee, she could have some. Lily thought and thought. She wanted the coffee so badly, she was not about to give up. Then suddenly, an idea popped into her head. She said “I can turn the cup over, so the coffee’s upside down - like a reverse!” Her mum smiled and said “That’s clever! Go ahead, try it.” Lily carefully grabbed the cup and reversed it. Sure enough, the coffee poured out into the cup. She tasted it and smiled. It was so yummy! From then on, Lily and her mum made reverse coffee ever day. It was their unusual way of enjoying coffee together.

One day, a big mouse went to play outside. It was very cold, and the mouse could see his breath. He saw a big puddle and wanted to jump in it. But when he tried to jump, he could not move. The mouse was stuck! He had to call for help. "Help, help!" the mouse cried. A big dog heard the mouse and came running. "What is wrong, little mouse?" asked the dog. "I am stuck and cannot move," said the mouse. The dog looked at the puddle and saw that it was frozen. The dog had an idea. He ran and found a warm blanket. He put the blanket on the mouse and the ice around him. Soon, the ice started to melt, and the mouse was free. The mouse and the dog were so happy they could play together. From that day on, the big mouse and the big dog were best friends. They played together every day, but they always remembered to be careful around frozen puddles.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat waffles for breakfast. One day, Tim's mom made him a big, yummy waffle. Tim was very excited. As Tim ate his waffle, he saw his dad tying his shoes. Tim wanted to learn how to tie his shoes too. He asked his dad, "Can you teach me how to tie my shoes, please?" His dad smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! I will show you." Tim's dad showed him how to make a loop, wrap the other lace around, and pull it tight. Tim tried to do it, but it was hard. He did not give up. He kept trying and trying. Finally, Tim tied his shoes all by himself. Tim was so happy that he could tie his shoes like his dad. Tim's mom and dad were very proud of him. They knew that Tim could do anything he set his mind to. From that day on, Tim always tied his shoes before eating his waffle for breakfast. And they all lived happily ever after.
