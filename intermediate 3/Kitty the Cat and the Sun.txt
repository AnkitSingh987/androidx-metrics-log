Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her friends outside. One sunny day, Kitty went outside to play with her friends. While they were playing, Kitty saw something behind the laundry. She walked closer to the laundry and moved it. Kitty was happy to reveal a big, bouncy ball. She called her friends to come and see the ball. Together, they played with the big, bouncy ball all day. They laughed and had lots of fun. When the sun went down, Kitty and her friends said goodbye and went home. They couldn't wait to play together again the next day.

Once upon a time, in an ancient house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to dress up in pretty clothes. Her favorite color was yellow, so she had many yellow dresses. One day, Lily found a big yellow dress in her mom's closet. She put it on and went outside to play. She saw her friend Tom and said, "Look at my pretty yellow dress!" Tom said, "Wow, that's a big dress!" They played all day, but the dress was too long and Lily kept tripping on it. At the end of the day, Lily fell down and the yellow dress ripped. She was very sad and cried. Her mom was not happy with her for ruining the dress. Lily learned that she should not wear big dresses when playing outside.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He loved to play outside and look at the sun in the sky. One day Jack was out in his garden, looking up at the bright yellow sun. He noticed something very jolly. It was a rainbow! Jack smiled and said, "woah, that's beautiful!" He decided he wanted to get a closer look. So, Jack ran over to his swingset and began to climb. He reached up and grabbed a branch, but he couldn't reach the rainbow. Just then, Jack's mom appeared. She said, "Jack, don't worry, I'll help you see the rainbow. I'll carry you up to the top of the tree and then you can look for it." And so Jack's mom lifted him up and Jack looked up to the sky. He saw the beautiful colors of the rainbow stretch out before him. He smiled, and said, "Thanks, mom! Can we look at the sun now?" And his mom said, "Of course! Let's go and look at the sun together." So they both ran outside and looked at the castle in the sky. Jack smiled and clapped his hands, feeling so jolly and happy. He had seen the sun and his very own rainbow.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a rare bicycle. It was red and blue. He loved his bicycle very much. He rode it every day. One day, Tim wanted to paint his bicycle. He wanted to make it even more special. He got yellow paint and a big brush. Tim started to paint his bicycle. It looked so pretty and bright. Tim's friends saw his bicycle and loved it too. They all wanted to paint their bicycles like Tim. They painted their bicycles with many colors. They had so much fun. Now, everyone in the town had a pretty, painted bicycle just like Tim.

One day, a boy named Tim had a dull red wagon. He liked to play with it every day. Tim would put his toys in the wagon and pull it around the yard. One morning, Tim saw his friend Sue. She had a shiny new wagon. "Look at my new wagon!" said Sue. Tim felt sad because his wagon was old and dull. He wanted a shiny wagon too. Tim had an idea to surprise Sue. He found some paint and painted his dull red wagon to make it shiny. When he was done, he showed Sue his shiny wagon. "Wow, Tim! Your wagon is shiny now too!" Sue said. They played together with their shiny wagons, and Tim was happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to school. He saw his friend, Sue, who had a big, orange ball. Tim said, "Hi Sue! Can we play with your orange ball today?" Sue said, "Yes, Tim! Let's play a game. The one who wins will get a surprise!" They played with the orange ball all day. They threw it, kicked it, and had lots of fun. As they played, they saw a big, orange box near the school. They were very curious. Tim said, "I think the surprise is in the box!" They played harder to see who would win. At the end of the day, Tim won the game. Sue said, "Good job, Tim! Let's open the big, orange box!" They opened the box, and found many more orange balls inside. They were so happy and shared the balls with all their friends at school. The surprise was more fun than they ever thought!
