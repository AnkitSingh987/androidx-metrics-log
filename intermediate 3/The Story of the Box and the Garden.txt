Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Box and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a boy. He was playing in the garden and he found a stick. He liked the stick very much and he picked it up. He sniffed it and it smelt funny. He was very surprised! He looked around the garden and he saw a big yellow box. He wondered what was inside the box and he was so curious. He opened the box and it smelt smelly. He was so surprised! Inside the box he found many sticks, just like the one he had found before. The boy was so excited and he wanted to play with the sticks. He played all day and took some of the sticks home with him. He was so happy, he had never been so surprised before!

Ben and Lily were playing on the beach. They liked to build sand castles and dig holes. They also liked to watch the water go in and out. They knew it was called the tide. "Look, Lily, the tide is going out!" Ben said. "We can go closer to the water and find shells and crabs." "OK, Ben, but we have to be careful. Mom said not to go too far. The tide can change and come back fast." Lily said. They ran to the water and started to look for treasures. They found some shiny shells and some funny crabs. They put them in their buckets. They were having fun. But they did not notice that the tide was starting to reverse. The water was coming back in. It was getting closer and closer to their sand castles and holes. "Ben, Lily, come back!" Mom shouted. "The tide is reversing! It's important to stay safe!" Ben and Lily heard Mom and looked at the water. It was very near their sand castles and holes. They were scared. "Quick, Lily, grab your bucket and run!" Ben said. They ran back to Mom with their buckets. They were sad to leave their sand castles and holes, but they were glad to be safe. "Good job, Ben and Lily. You listened to me and came back. The tide can be dangerous sometimes. It's important to pay attention to it." Mom said. She hugged them and gave them a snack. They ate their snack and looked at their shells and crabs. They were happy. They learned something new about the tide.

Lily and Tom were best friends. They liked to play with balls and dolls and cars. One day, they found a big ball in the park. It was red and shiny and bouncy. They wanted to play with it together. But they had different opinions. Lily wanted to throw the ball high in the air and catch it. Tom wanted to roll the ball on the ground and kick it. They argued and argued. They did not want to share the ball. They did not want to listen to each other. Then, a big dog came. It saw the ball and ran towards it. It grabbed the ball with its teeth and ran away. Lily and Tom were sad and angry. They lost the ball. They blamed each other. They did not want to be friends anymore. A nice lady saw what happened. She came to Lily and Tom and said, "I'm sorry you lost your ball. It was a very nice ball. But you know, balls are not as important as friends. Friends are dependable. They help you and care for you and have fun with you. You should not fight over opinions. You should respect and compromise. That's how you keep your friends." Lily and Tom thought about what the lady said. They realized she was right. They felt sorry for being mean and stubborn. They said sorry to each other and hugged. They decided to play with something else. They did not need the ball to be happy. They had each other. The moral of the story is: Friends are more important than things. You should not let opinions ruin your friendship. You should be dependable and respectful to your friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his band of friends. They liked to run, jump, and chase each other all day long. One sunny day, Tom's friend, a little bird named Tina, flew up to him. She had a question for him. "Tom," she asked, "do you want to play with us today?" Tom was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to play with you and the band!" Together, Tom, Tina, and their friends played all day long. They had lots of fun and laughed a lot. When the sun started to set, they all waved goodbye and went to their homes. Tom was very tired, but he was happy that he had spent the day playing with his band of friends.
