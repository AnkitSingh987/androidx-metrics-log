Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mouse and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a mouse who was very small and very foolish. He liked to explore, always jumping into one adventure after the next. One day, the mouse found himself in a very dark cave. He couldn't see anything, so he decided to use his ears to figure out what was in there. After a few minutes, he heard a strange noise. "Who's there?" the mouse asked. "It's me," a voice answered. The mouse was scared, but he decided to take a closer look. To his surprise, he saw a big and friendly creature. "Don't be afraid," the creature said. "I'm here to help you. What do you need?" The mouse thought for a moment. Then, he nodded and said, "I'm lost and I need to find my way home." The creature smiled. "Well, then you've come to the right place! Come along, I'll show you the way." And so, with the help of the friendly creature, the foolish mouse found his way back home.

One night, there was a little girl who was very sleepy. She was walking in the forest and she started to slip. She slipped into a slippery and wet swamp. Suddenly, she saw a skull! It was so scary that she almost screamed but then she remembered that she was very sleepy. She gathered all her bravery and got out of the swamp. After that, she kept walking and never slipped again. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in the kitchen. He saw a faucet. The water came out fast when he turned it on. Tim liked to watch the water. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim. Don't play with the faucet too much, or it might break." But Tim did not listen. He kept turning the faucet on and off. Suddenly, the faucet broke! Water went everywhere, very fast. Tim was scared. He called his mom, "Mom, the faucet is broken! Help!" His mom came quickly and turned off the water. She was not happy, but she hugged Tim. "Next time, listen to me, okay?" Tim nodded and said, "I will, Mom."

John and his dad were walking through the park one day. John was very curious and wanted to know what was around every corner. As they were walking, John saw something moving in the bushes. "What's that dad?" asked John. "That's a snake," replied his dad. John was so excited, he wanted to get a closer look. He ran right over to the snake and tried to grab it. The snake quickly displayed its teeth and hissed at John. John was a little scared, but still curious. He asked his dad if they could take the snake home. His dad replied, "No, John. It's not safe to pick up wild animals!" John didn't listen, and he reached down to try to grab the snake. The snake bit John on the hand and ran away. John was in a lot of pain and had to go to the hospital. Moral of the story: it is not safe to be too curious around wild animals.

Sam and Lily liked to play in the woods. They found a big cave and wanted to see what was inside. They took a flashlight and some snacks and went into the cave. "Wow, look at all the rocks and bats!" Sam said. He shone the flashlight on the walls and the ceiling. "This is so cool!" Lily nodded. She saw something shiny on the ground. She picked it up and showed it to Sam. "Look, I found a yellow ring! It is so pretty!" Sam looked at the ring. He wanted it for himself. He grabbed it from Lily and ran deeper into the cave. "Ha ha, it is mine now!" he said. Lily was angry and sad. She ran after Sam and tried to get the ring back. "No, give it back! You are mean!" she said. But Sam was too fast. He ran into a dark corner of the cave and hid behind a rock. He put the ring on his finger and smiled. He felt very happy. But he did not see the big spider that was on the rock. The spider was hungry and angry. It saw Sam and his yellow ring. It thought Sam was food. It jumped on Sam and bit him. Sam screamed and dropped the ring. He felt very sick and scared. Lily heard Sam scream and ran to him. She saw the spider and the ring. She was scared too. She tried to help Sam, but the spider was too big and strong. It spread its legs and wrapped them around Sam and Lily. It dragged them into its web and ate them. The yellow ring was left on the ground. Nobody saw it again. The end.
