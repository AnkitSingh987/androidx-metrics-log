Title: Zippy the Car and the Biggie

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Zippy. Zippy liked to drive very fast. One day, Zippy was driving down the road when he saw a big truck. The truck was stuck in the mud and looked helpless. Zippy wanted to help the truck, so he drove up to him. Zippy said, "Hi, I am Zippy. I can help you get out of the mud." The truck said, "Thank you, Zippy. My name is Biggie. I have zero ideas on how to get out of here." Zippy tried to push Biggie out of the mud, but it was very hard. Zippy did not give up. He kept trying and trying until Biggie was finally out of the mud. Biggie said, "Thank you, Zippy. You are a good friend." Zippy was happy that he could help Biggie, and they both drove away together.

Once upon a time, there was a big lake. It was very far away. A boy named Tom and a girl named Sue went to the lake to play. They saw a sack near the lake. They were very happy to find it. Tom said, "Let's open the sack!" Sue agreed. They opened the sack and saw many colorful balls. They started to play with the balls near the lake. They threw the balls in the air and laughed. As they played, a big wind came. It blew the balls into the lake. Tom and Sue were very sad. They tried to get the balls back, but they could not. The balls were gone forever. Tom and Sue went home with the empty sack and a sad heart.

Once upon a time, there was a big oak tree. It was in the middle of a pretty park. The tree was very old and wise. Many little animals lived in the tree. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, a small bird wanted to be successful. He wanted to fly very high. The bird asked the big oak tree, "How can I fly high?" The oak tree said, "You must believe in yourself. Then you can fly high." The small bird listened to the oak tree. He believed in himself. He tried to fly high. He was successful! All the animals in the tree were happy for the bird. They all learned to believe in themselves too. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tim. Tim had a toy robot with big arms. He loved to play with the robot every day. One day, while playing with the robot, he met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, can I play with you?" Tim said, "Yes, you can play with my robot." Sue was happy, and they played together. They took turns making the robot move and turn. As they played, Sue said, "Wow, the robot's arms can turn!" Tim said, "Yes, they can turn all around!" They had lots of fun playing with the robot and its big arms. In the end, Tim and Sue became good friends. They played together every day, and they always shared their toys. They were both very kind to each other, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a little bird with a broken wing. The bird could not fly and was very sad. It sat on a branch and tried to whistle, but it could not. It wanted to have fun with its friends. One day, a kind girl saw the bird and said, "Oh no! Your wing is broken. I will help you." The girl took the bird home and gave it some food. The bird thought the food was disgusting, but it ate it anyway because it was hungry. The girl took care of the bird for many days. The bird's wing got better, and it learned how to whistle again. The bird was so happy. It said, "Thank you for helping me." The girl smiled and said, "You're welcome. Now you can fly and play with your friends again." The bird flew away, whistling a happy song.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived two friends, Tom and Sam. They liked to play together every day. One day, they found a big, red button in the park. They both wanted the button for themselves. Tom and Sam began to quarrel about who should keep the button. They were not careful with their words and started to hurt each other's feelings. Their faces turned red and they stopped playing together. A wise old cat saw them and said, "If you share the button, you can both enjoy it and stay friends." Tom and Sam thought about it, and decided to share the button. They learned that sharing and being careful with words can keep friends happy.
