Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wise Owl and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl. The owl had a friend, a small mouse. One day, the owl told the mouse, "I have a gift for you, but you must accept it with your foot." The mouse was very happy and excited. The next day, the mouse saw a big tree. The wise owl said, "This is where your gift is." The mouse looked up and saw a yummy nut hanging from a branch. The mouse tried to jump and get it, but it was too high. The wise owl told the mouse to use his foot to accept the gift. The mouse thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He used his foot to push a big rock under the tree. He jumped on the rock and got the nut with his foot. The mouse was very happy and thanked the wise owl for his help.

Once upon a time, there was a weak little dog. His name was Tiny. He had soft fur. Tiny loved to play with his friends. One day, Tiny saw a big toy. He wanted to destroy it. He tried to bite it, but he was too weak. His friends saw him and wanted to help. Together, they all pushed and pulled. The big toy was destroyed. Tiny and his friends were happy. They played all day and Tiny felt strong.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Tom. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, they saw a huge tree in the forest. They wanted to make a treehouse. Tim and Tom needed to borrow an axe to cut the tree. They went to their friend Sam's house. Sam was happy to help and gave them the axe. They said, "Thank you, Sam!" and went back to the huge tree. Tim and Tom used the axe to cut the tree. It took a long time, but they did it. They made a fun treehouse to play in. They were very happy and invited Sam to play with them. They all had a great time in their new treehouse.

Once upon a time, there was a big hall. The hall was very lonely because no one came to play. It wanted to become a happy place where kids could have fun. One day, a little girl named Lily found the lonely hall. She thought it would be a great place to play with her friends. So, she asked her friends to come and play with her in the hall. Soon, the hall was full of kids playing and laughing. The hall was not lonely anymore. It had become a fun place for all the kids to play. And the hall and the kids lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with a big gate. Lucy was sad because her days were boring. She wanted to find something fun to do. One day, Lucy saw a mailman at the gate. He gave Lucy a letter. The letter said, "Help others and you will be happy." Lucy thought about it and decided to help her mom and dad. First, Lucy helped her mom cook dinner. Then, she helped her dad fix the gate. After that, she mailed a letter for her grandma. Lucy felt happy because she helped others. The moral of the story is that helping others can make our days less boring and make us happy.

Lily loved to dance. She danced in the morning, in the afternoon, and at night. She danced with her dolls, her teddy bear, and her mom. She danced when she was happy, when she was sad, and when she was bored. One day, Lily found a bracelet in her mom's drawer. It was shiny and colorful, with many beads and charms. Lily thought it was the most beautiful thing she had ever seen. She put it on her wrist and felt like a princess. She ran to show her mom. "Mom, look at my bracelet! It is so pretty! Can I keep it?" she asked. Mom smiled and hugged Lily. "That bracelet is very special, Lily. It was a gift from your grandma, who is in heaven now. She gave it to me when I was a little girl, like you. She said it was unique, just like me. And you are unique, too, Lily. You are my sweet and smart and brave girl." Lily felt warm and happy. She hugged her mom back and said, "Thank you, mom. I love you. And I love grandma, too. Can I dance with her bracelet?" Mom nodded and said, "Of course, Lily. You can dance with her bracelet. I think she would like that. She loved to dance, too. Just like you." Lily and mom danced together in the living room, with the bracelet sparkling on Lily's wrist. They danced with joy, with love, and with memory. They danced with grandma, too, in their hearts.
