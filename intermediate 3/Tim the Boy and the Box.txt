Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Box

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He was very tired, so he let out a big yawn. As he yawned, he saw a ghost! The ghost was white and looked confused. Tim was scared, but he wanted to help the ghost. Tim asked the ghost, "Why are you confused?" The ghost said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." Tim decided to help the ghost look for the toy. They looked under the bed, in the closet, and behind the door. The ghost was still sad and confused because they could not find the toy. Finally, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's look in the toy box!" They looked in the toy box and found the ghost's toy! The ghost was so happy and not confused anymore. Tim and the ghost played with the toy together. After playing, they both yawned and took a nap.

One day, a cheerful girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a pretty frame on the ground. Lily picked it up and thought, "I want to find a nice picture for this frame." Lily went to her friend Tim's house. "Look what I found!" she said. "Let's draw a picture to put in the frame." Tim was happy to help. They drew a big sun and a tree with lots of leaves. Just as they finished the picture, a big wind came and blew the picture out of Lily's hand. She tried to catch it, but the wind was too strong. The picture flew up into the tree and got stuck. "Oh no!" said Tim. "What can we do?" Just then, a friendly bird saw the picture and picked it up. The bird flew down and dropped the picture right into the frame. Lily and Tim were so surprised and happy. They thanked the bird and hung the frame in their playroom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an enormous box of stamps. He loved to play with them and make pretty pictures. One day, Tim decided to show his stamps to his friend, Sally. "Look, Sally!" said Tim. "I have lots of stamps! Let's make pictures together!" Sally was very excited and they started to play. They used the stamps to make flowers, trees, and even a big sun. But then, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse came out from a hole in the wall. The mouse saw the stamps and wanted to play too. The mouse took a stamp and made a tiny picture of a house. Tim and Sally were surprised, but they laughed and clapped their hands. "Wow, Mr. Mouse! You are a great artist!" said Sally. Tim, Sally, and Mr. Mouse decided to make more pictures together. They had lots of fun and became very good friends. From that day on, they always played with the stamps and made beautiful art together.

Once upon a time, two best friends were on their way to the festival. As they walked, one of the friends stumbled and the other one tried to help her. First friend: "I stumbled. Did you see me?" Second friend: "Yes, I did. I am going to help you." First friend: "Thank you. That's so nice of you." The second friend was flexible and so she helped the first friend up and soon enough both were laughing about it. When they finally reached the festival, they saw so many wonderful things, like toy figures, bouncing balls and all sorts of treats. The two friends laughed and enjoyed everything there, grateful that the second one was so flexible and helpful. They had the best time and stayed until it was dark.

One day, a boy named Tom found a big nail. He wanted to do something original with it. He thought and thought. Then, he had a great idea. He would shoot the nail into a tree and make a swing. Tom went to his dad and said, "Dad, can you help me shoot the nail into the tree? I want to make a swing." His dad agreed and they went to the tree. As they were about to shoot the nail, a bird came out of the tree. The bird said, "Please don't shoot the nail into my tree. My family lives here." Tom and his dad were very surprised. They did not want to hurt the bird's family. So, they found another way to make the swing without shooting the nail into the tree. The bird was happy and Tom had fun on his new swing.
