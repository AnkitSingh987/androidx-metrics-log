Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cat of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big purple ball that he liked to play with. One day, he saw a small, troubled cat stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he threw his purple ball at the tree. The ball hit the branch and the cat jumped down. The cat was very happy and started to play with Tim and his purple ball. After playing, Tim saw that the cat was dirty. He went inside his house and got a cloth. He wiped the cat clean and gave it some food. The cat was not troubled anymore and stayed with Tim as his new friend. They played with the purple ball every day and were always happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat. The cat was very hungry. The cat saw a little mouse. The mouse had a pot of ink. The cat wanted to eat the mouse. The mouse said, "Please do not eat me. I will give you my ink." The cat thought about it. The cat wanted the ink, but it was still hungry. The cat said, "I will take the ink, but I am still hungry." The mouse had an idea. The mouse said, "I will find you more food. Then you will not be hungry." The cat agreed. The mouse went to find more food for the cat. The mouse gave the cat some cheese. The cat ate the cheese and was not hungry anymore. The cat and the mouse became friends. They played together and shared the ink. The cat was happy and not hungry anymore. The mouse was happy too because it had a new friend. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a modest house. In this house, lived a girl named Amy and her mom. Amy liked to design things. She would draw and color all day. One morning, Amy's mom made waffles for breakfast. Amy had an idea. She said, "Mom, can I design my waffle?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Amy. You can design your waffle." Amy got some fruits and made a face on her waffle. She used blueberries for the eyes, a strawberry for the nose, and banana slices for the mouth. Amy showed her mom the waffle face. They both laughed and enjoyed their breakfast.

Once there was a little girl who wanted to go for a walk in the forest. She asked her mother if she could borrow her boots. Her mother said "yes", and the little girl went to the forest. The little girl found a twig and picked it up. She thought it was lovely. She decided to take it home and show it to her mother. On the way home, she saw a bird and the bird said "hello". The little girl said "hello" back and waved goodbye. When she got home, her mother was so pleased to see the twig. She said "that is so lovely, thank you for bringing it inside." The little girl said "I'm glad you like it". Her mother told her she could keep the twig, and she hugged her daughter tightly. The little girl loved how her mother made her feel so special. She thanked her for letting her borrow the boots and for showing her so much love. The twig was a lovely reminder of her special day in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a red radio that she loved to listen to every day. One day, Sue heard a sad song on her radio. The song was about a girl who lost her toy. Sue thought that was not fair for the girl in the song. The next day, Sue went to the park with her radio. She played on the swings and listened to happy songs. But when it was time to go home, she could not find her radio. Sue started to cry. She remembered the sad song about the girl who lost her toy and now she lost her radio. Sue's mom saw her cry and went to help her. They looked everywhere in the park. Finally, they found the red radio under the slide. Sue was so happy and hugged her mom. From that day on, Sue always made sure to keep her radio safe and close to her. And she learned that sometimes things might not seem fair, but with a little help, they can turn out okay.
