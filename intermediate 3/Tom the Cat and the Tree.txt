Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Tree

One day, a chubby cat named Tom went for a jog. He liked to run and play in the sun. Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max was happy to see Tom and wanted to play too. Tom and Max played by the big tree. They saw a red mailbox. Tom had a blue letter. He wanted to mail it to his friend, a bird named Sue. Max helped Tom put the letter in the mailbox. Sue got the letter from Tom. She was very happy. Sue flew to the big tree to thank Tom and Max. They all played together and had a fun day. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little bird named Bluey. Bluey was a helpless bird who could not fly. He had many friends in the forest who tried to help him learn how to fly. One sunny day, Bluey met a kind squirrel named Squeaky. Squeaky saw Bluey sitting on the ground, looking sad. He asked, "Bluey, why are you sad?" Bluey said, "I can't fly, and I feel helpless." Squeaky wanted to help Bluey, so he had an idea. Squeaky told Bluey to wave his wings up and down, just like the other birds. Bluey tried to wave his wings, but he still could not fly. Squeaky thought harder and said, "Let's ask our other friends for help!" They went to their friends in the forest, and all of them tried to help Bluey wave his wings. With their help, Bluey started to slowly lift off the ground. He felt so happy and thanked his friends. From that day on, Bluey could fly and was no longer helpless.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a wide pond with ducks swimming in it. Tim loved ducks! He waved at the ducks and said, "Hi ducks! I want to play with you!" A duck came out of the water and said, "Hello Tim! My name is Ducky. I smell some gas. Can you help me find where it's coming from?" Tim nodded and they started to look around. They found a small gas can near the pond. Tim picked it up and said, "Here it is, Ducky! We found the gas!" Ducky smiled and thanked Tim. They waved goodbye to each other and Tim went back home, happy to have made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a humble little village by the sea. All the villagers were friends and always helped each other out. One day, a violin arrived in the village. Everyone was so excited. The village mayor gathered all the villagers around. "Let's cover the violin with a cloth," he said, "and take it to the park where we can have a concert!" So the village filled up the park, and the mayor removed the cloth to reveal the sparkling violin. Everyone was so excited to see it. But as soon as the mayor picked it up, something strange happened. The violin started to play by itself! A beautiful melody filled the park, and everyone was mesmerized. The mayor bowed before the piano. "Thank you for the music," he said. "It was the best concert ever!" And that was how the humble village by the sea got to hear a magical violin play.

Once upon a time, there was a mom and her daughter. They liked to play with soft toys. The daughter had many soft toys. Her favorite soft toy was a big, soft bear. The mom and daughter played with the bear every day. One day, the mom told the daughter that the bear could grow. The daughter was very happy. She wanted the bear to increase in size. The mom said they needed to give the bear a lot of hugs to make it grow. The daughter hugged the bear every day, hoping it would get bigger. After many days of hugs, the daughter woke up one morning and saw the bear had increased in size. It was now a huge, soft bear. The daughter was so happy. She knew her hugs made the bear grow. The mom and daughter played with the big, soft bear and had lots of fun.
