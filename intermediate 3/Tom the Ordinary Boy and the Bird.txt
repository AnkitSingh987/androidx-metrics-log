Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Ordinary Boy and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an ordinary boy named Tom. He liked to play outside and eat sweet treats. His favorite sweet was a mint candy. One day, he went to the store to buy more mints. On his way back, Tom saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. He put his mints in his pocket and started to climb. When he reached the top, he saw a beautiful bird. The bird looked at Tom and asked, "Can I have a mint?" Tom gave the bird a mint, and they became friends. After playing with the bird, Tom knew it was time to return home. He climbed down the tree and said goodbye to his new friend. When he got home, he told his mom about the bird and the tree. His mom was happy that Tom had a fun day. Tom ate a mint and shared one with his mom too.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree in a park. The tree was very reliable. Every day, kids would lean on the tree and play. One day, the tree said to the kids, "Let's have a party!" The kids were very happy. They said, "Yes, let's have a party!" They told all their friends about the party. They all came to the tree with food, games, and fun. At the party, the kids played games, ate yummy food, and laughed a lot. The tree was so happy to see the kids having fun. The kids said, "Thank you, tree, for the best party ever!" The tree smiled and knew it was a great day.

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was a swing. The swing was icy because it was very cold outside. A little bird wanted to play on the swing, but it was too icy for the bird to sit. One day, a nice girl named Sue saw the bird trying to sit on the icy swing. Sue had an idea. She ran home and got a warm blanket. She put the blanket on the swing so the bird could sit on it and not be cold. The little bird was so happy to sit on the warm swing. It played all day and had so much fun. But then, a big cat came to the park. The cat saw the bird and wanted to catch it. The bird was scared, but it was smart. It used the swing to go very high and escape from the cat. The cat could not catch the bird, and the bird was safe. The bird and Sue played on the swing every day and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, in a big oak tree, there lived a little squirrel named Sam. Sam was very impatient. He loved to chew on nuts all day long. One day, he found a big shiny nut under the oak tree. He was so happy, he could not wait to chew it. Sam took the nut to his friend, a wise old owl. The owl told Sam, "Wait until tomorrow, and you will find something very good." But Sam was too impatient. He wanted to chew the nut right away. So, he went back to the oak tree and started to chew the nut. As Sam chewed the nut, something unexpected happened. The nut cracked open, and out came a beautiful butterfly! The butterfly flew around Sam and the oak tree, making everything look so pretty. Sam was surprised and happy. He learned that sometimes, waiting can bring something even better.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys. One day, Sue lost her favorite toy, a small red car. She looked everywhere, but she could not find it. Sue was very sad. Sue's mom saw her crying and asked, "Sue, why are you crying?" Sue replied, "I lost my red car and I can't find it." Her mom said, "Don't worry, we will find it together. But first, let's have a little talk about taking care of our things." Sue listened to her mom's talk, which was like a small lecture. Sue's mom told her that it's important to put things away when we are done playing with them. That way, we won't lose them and we can find them easily next time. Sue thought her mom was right and promised to be more careful. Together, they searched for the red car. They found it under Sue's bed. Sue was so happy! She gave her mom a big hug and said, "Thank you, Mom! I promise to put my toys away when I'm done playing." From that day on, Sue always put her toys away after playing. She never lost anything again. The moral of the story is to always take care of our things and put them away when we are done, so we don't go crazy looking for them later.
