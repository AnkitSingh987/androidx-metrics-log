Category: Intermediate
Title: The Colorful Bird Was Flying and the Magic

One day, a colorful bird was flying in the sky. It saw a hedge on the ground. The bird wanted to sit on the hedge, so it flew down. As the bird sat on the hedge, it saw a big, cold thing. The cold thing was melting. The bird was very curious. It went closer to the cold thing. The bird touched the cold thing. Suddenly, the bird turned into a colorful ice bird! The bird was not a real bird anymore. It was now a part of the melting cold thing. The hedge was magic, and it changed the bird's life forever.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Lucy saw a small bird on the ground. The bird had a hurt wing and could not fly. Lucy wanted to help the bird. Lucy went to her friend Tom. She asked, "Can you lend me a box to help the bird?" Tom said, "Yes, I can lend you a box." Lucy took the box and went back to the bird. She put the bird in the box and took it to her mom. Lucy's mom knew how to make the bird feel better. After a few days, the bird's wing was better. The bird could fly again. Lucy and Tom let the bird go, and it flew up into the sky. They were very happy to see the bird fly. The bird did not fall anymore. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, in a tiny house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to march around her garden, eager to find new things. One sunny day, she put on her hat and went outside. As she marched, she saw a big, green grape on the ground. "Wow!" said Lily, picking it up. She looked around to see if there were more grapes. She was very eager to find more. Lily marched and marched, and she found more grapes! She picked them up and put them in her basket. When she had a lot of grapes, she went back home. Lily shared the grapes with her family, and they all enjoyed a yummy snack together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play with her amazing toy brick. She would build tall towers and small houses with her brick. Lily loved her brick very much. One day, Lily decided to play a game. She wanted to hide her brick and find it again. So, she closed her eyes and counted to ten. While she counted, she hid the brick behind a big tree in her yard. When she opened her eyes, Lily looked for her brick. She looked under the bush, near the flowers, and by the swing, but she couldn't find it. Lily felt sad and started to cry. Her big brother, Tom, saw her crying and asked what was wrong. Lily told him about her lost brick. Tom helped her look for the brick. They looked everywhere, and finally, they found it behind the tree. Lily was so happy, and she hugged her brother. From that day on, they played with the amazing brick together.

One day, a little girl named Amy went outside to play. She looked up at the sky and saw a big, white cloud. The cloud looked amazing. It was so fluffy and soft, like a giant pillow. Amy wanted to discover more about the cloud. She climbed a tall tree to get closer. As she climbed higher and higher, she felt like she could almost touch the cloud. She reached out her hand and imagined how soft the cloud would feel. When it was time to go back down, Amy told her friends about the amazing cloud she saw. They all wanted to discover the cloud too. So, they all climbed the tree together and looked at the big, white cloud. They all agreed that it was the most amazing cloud they had ever seen.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. In the tree, he found a piece of gum. Tim thought it was yummy, so he put the gum in his mouth. While Tim was chewing the gum, a small bird came to the tree. The bird looked angry. The bird wanted the gum back, because it was her gum. The bird tried to take the gum from Tim. Tim did not want to give the gum back. He looked at the angry bird and said, "No, this is my gum now!" The bird was very sad. Tim felt bad for the bird, so he gave the gum back. The bird was happy, and they became friends.
