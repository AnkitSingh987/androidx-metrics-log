Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Treasure

Lily and Ben went to the farm with Mom and Dad. They wanted to pick pumpkins for Halloween. Mom and Dad gave them each a basket and said, "You can pick any pumpkin you like, but only one. Look for a big, round, orange one." Lily and Ben ran to the pumpkin patch. They saw many pumpkins, but some were too small, some were too green, and some had holes or spots. They looked and looked, but they could not find the perfect pumpkin. Then, Lily saw something shiny in the grass. She went closer and saw a gold coin. She picked it up and showed it to Ben. "Look, Ben, I found a treasure!" she said. Ben was happy for Lily, but he was also sad. He wanted to find a treasure too. He looked around, but he could not see anything shiny. He felt like crying. Lily saw Ben's face and felt sorry for him. She remembered that Mom and Dad said they could only pick one thing. She thought for a moment, and then she had an idea. She gave the coin to Ben and said, "Here, Ben, you can have the treasure. I don't need it. I already have something better." Ben looked at Lily and smiled. He took the coin and hugged her. "Thank you, Lily, you are very kind. What do you have that is better than the treasure?" he asked. Lily pointed to a big, round, orange pumpkin that was hiding behind a scarecrow. "I have this pumpkin. It is the perfect pumpkin for Halloween. And I have you, Ben. You are my best friend. I am very grateful for you." Ben and Lily carried their pumpkin and their coin to Mom and Dad. They were very happy and proud. Mom and Dad praised them for their good choices and their good hearts. They said, "You are very lucky, Lily and Ben. You have found the best things in the pumpkin patch."

One day, a little cat named Lucy walked in the park. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Lucy climbed up the tree and sat on a branch. She looked around and saw many pretty things. But then, she saw a unique bird. The bird had many colors and looked very nice. Lucy wanted to be friends with the bird. Lucy said with a soft voice, "Hi, bird! I am Lucy. Do you want to be friends?" The bird looked at Lucy and sighed. The bird said, "I am sorry, Lucy. I am very shy. I don't have many friends." Lucy felt sad for the bird. She said, "Don't worry, bird. I can help you make friends. We can play together and have fun. You can meet my friends too." The bird smiled and said, "Thank you, Lucy. That sounds nice. I would like that." So, Lucy and the bird became friends. They played together in the park and had a lot of fun. The bird was not shy anymore, and he made many new friends. Lucy and the bird were very happy to have each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She was very proud of her big, red ball. One day, Sue met a boy named Tim. Tim had some gum in his hand. He said, "Sue, do you want some gum?" Sue said, "Yes, please!" But she did not trust Tim. She had a feeling he was not nice. So, she held her big, red ball close to her. Tim gave Sue the gum, and they played together. They had fun, but Sue still did not trust Tim. Later, Tim said, "Sue, let's play a game. Close your eyes and count to ten." Sue did as Tim said. When she opened her eyes, her big, red ball was gone! Tim had taken it. Sue was sad, but she knew she should have trusted her feeling.

Once there was a pretty little girl named Jasmine. She loved to study and she was always trying to learn new things. One day Jasmine was busy studying at her desk when her mum came into the room with a plate of warm toast. She said to Jasmine, “I made this toast for you. Why don’t you take a break from studying and have a snack?” Jasmine thought for a moment and said, “No thanks mum, I want to keep studying.” Her mum smiled and said, “That’s a very good idea. It’s nice to take a break every once in a while though. Have some toast and then go back to your studies.” Jasmine took a break, ate the toast, and then went back to studying. She knew that studying was very important but taking a break every once in a while was good too. The moral of this story is that it is important to work hard but to also take some time to relax and enjoy life.
