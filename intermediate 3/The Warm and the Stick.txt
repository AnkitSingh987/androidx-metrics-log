Category: Intermediate
Title: The Warm and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a warm, sunny day. A little girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw a long string on the ground. She picked it up and started to play with it. Lily saw a little boy named Tim. He was playing with a stick. "Hi, Tim!" said Lily. "Look at my string!" Tim looked at the string and smiled. "Wow, that's a long string!" he said. "Can I play with it too?" Lily nodded and handed the string to Tim. Together, Lily and Tim played with the string. They poked it at things, like the grass and the dirt. They even poked it at a bug, but the bug ran away. They laughed and played with the string all day long. At the end of the day, they were both very happy and tired. They went home, ready to play again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She was three years old. One day, Sally found a big knot. It was long and round, and it was just lying there by the creek. It was such a fine knot! Sally wanted to keep it for a special gift. But when she tried to pick it up, something was not right. She could feel a sharp bite on her finger! Sally started to cry. Suddenly, a friendly bird flew over. He had seen what had happened. He told Sally that the knot was actually the home of some little bugs. They had tried to bite her because she was trying to get too close to their home. The bird asked her to leave the knot alone, and let the bugs stay safe. Sally agreed. She said goodbye to the bugs and returned home. She was so happy that she had been able to help out the bugs. That was a very fine knot indeed!

One day, a little girl named Lily found a unique rock. The rock was shiny and had many colors. She was very happy and wanted to show her friends. Lily put the rock in her hand and went to find her friends. Lily found her friends, Max and Sara, playing in the park. She showed them her unique rock. Max and Sara were amazed by the rock. They wanted to play with it too. Lily said, "We can all play with the rock, but it must stay in my hand." Max and Sara agreed. They played with the rock all day. They took turns holding Lily's hand so the rock would stay with her. They had a lot of fun with their new unique rock friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very sad and miserable. He did not have any friends to play with. Every day, he would wake up, eat his breakfast, and play all by himself. One day, Tim found a big bar of soap in his bathroom. He thought of a fun idea. He could use the soap to make bubbles! Tim blew on the soap and made lots and lots of bubbles. The bubbles were so big and pretty that they floated out of the window and into the sky. Other kids saw the bubbles and wanted to play with them too. They all came to Tim's house and asked if they could join him. Tim said yes, and they all played with the bubbles together. Tim was not miserable anymore. He made many new friends, and they all learned that sharing fun things can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived with a nice family and their little girl, Lily. They all loved each other very much. One day, Fluffy and Lily went to visit Lily's uncle. Lily's uncle had a big farm with lots of animals. Fluffy and Lily played with the animals all day. They saw cows, pigs, and chickens. They were all very happy. Suddenly, they heard a small cry. It was a baby bird that had fallen from its nest. Fluffy looked at Lily and said, "We must save the bird!" Lily nodded and they gently picked up the bird. Together, they put the bird back in its nest. The bird's mommy was so happy and thanked Fluffy and Lily. Lily's uncle saw what they did and said, "You two are very brave and kind. Good job saving the bird!" Fluffy and Lily smiled, feeling proud of their good deed. From that day on, Fluffy and Lily knew that they could always help and save their friends. They had a great time at the farm and couldn't wait to visit again.
