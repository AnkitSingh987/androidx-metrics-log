Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobby the Nosy Boat and the Ship

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little boat named Bobby. Bobby lived in a harbor with other boats. He loved to measure things with his long rope. One day, Bobby saw a big ship coming to the harbor. He thought, "I want to measure that ship!" So, Bobby waited for the ship to come closer. As the ship came near, Bobby used his rope to measure it. The ship was very long! Bobby was happy that he could measure such a big ship. And from that day on, all the boats in the harbor knew Bobby was the best at measuring things.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They had a sack with some snacks and a napkin. They liked to run and slide and swing and laugh. But then they saw a big dog. The dog was barking and running towards them. Ben and Lily were scared. They grabbed their sack and ran to the bench. They hid behind the napkin. The dog came closer and closer. He sniffed the sack. He smelled the snacks. He wanted to eat them. He tried to bite the sack. But the sack was hard. He could not open it. He growled and pawed at the sack. Ben and Lily were very quiet. They hoped the dog would go away. They wished their mom would come soon. They hugged each other and waited. Then they heard a whistle. It was the dog's owner. He called the dog's name. The dog stopped growling and pawing. He looked at his owner. He wagged his tail and ran to him. Ben and Lily were happy. The dog was gone. Their sack was safe. Their mom was here. They came out from behind the napkin. They smiled and waved. They ate their snacks and shared them with their mom. They had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dirty bird. This bird loved to soar high in the sky. One day, the bird saw a yummy piece of celery on the ground. The bird wanted the celery very much. But there was a problem. A small, fast rabbit also saw the celery. The rabbit wanted the celery too. The dirty bird and the fast rabbit did not want to share the celery. They both wanted it all. The dirty bird had an idea. The bird would race the rabbit to see who could get the celery first. The bird and the rabbit ran very fast. In the end, the bird got to the celery first. But the bird saw how sad the rabbit was. The bird decided to share the celery with the rabbit. They both ate the celery and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a white doll that she loved very much. The doll was her best friend. Every day, Lily played with the white doll and talked to it. She told the doll all her secrets and dreams. One day, Lily lost her white doll. She looked everywhere for it, but she could not find it. Lily was very sad. She did not want to play or speak to anyone. She missed her doll so much. One day, Lily's mom found the white doll under the bed. She gave it to Lily and said, "Lily, always put your toys away when you are done playing with them. This will help you find them next time." Lily was so happy to have her doll back. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you, Mommy. I will put my toys away from now on." From that day on, Lily always put her toys away and never lost her white doll again. And she always remembered to speak nicely to her mom and say "thank you" when she got help.

Once upon a time, there was a lively sun who loved to shine bright in the sky. Every morning, the sun would say "Hello!" to all the people in the little town. The people would smile and wave back. One day, the sun saw a man making something called coffee. The sun asked the man, "What is coffee?" The man said, "It's a drink that wakes me up and makes me feel good." The sun wanted to help the man feel even better. From that day on, the sun shone extra bright and warm on the man's coffee. The man was very happy and said, "Thank you, sun, for making my coffee even better!" The sun smiled and continued to shine on the little town, making everyone feel good and happy.
