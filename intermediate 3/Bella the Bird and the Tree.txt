Category: Intermediate
Title: Bella the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a pretty bird named Bella. Bella loved to sing and play with her friends in the big tree. One day, Bella found a meal on the ground. It was a big, yummy worm. Bella called her friend, Timmy the squirrel, and said, "Timmy, I found a meal! Let's split it and eat it together." Timmy was very happy and came running to see the worm. They both took a piece of the worm and enjoyed their meal. After they finished eating, they played together all day long. Bella and Timmy were very happy to share their meal and have fun with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big river. It was a safe place for all the fish and frogs to play. They loved to swim and jump in the water. The river was their home, and they were very happy. One day, a big storm came. It was very loud and scary. The storm wanted to destroy the river. The fish and frogs were afraid. They did not want their home to be destroyed. But the river was strong. It fought the storm and kept the fish and frogs safe. The storm went away, and the river was still there. The fish and frogs were happy again. They knew that their river was the best and safest place to be.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to run fast. Tom was so fast that he could beat all his friends in a race. One day, Tom went to school. He was excited to show everybody how fast he could run. At school, Tom's teacher said they would have a race. Tom was so happy. He knew he would succeed and win the race. All his friends lined up to race, and Tom was ready to run fast. The race started, and Tom ran as fast as he could. But he did not see a big rock on the ground. Tom tripped on the rock and fell down. He did not succeed in the race. Tom was sad, and his friends were sad too. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a mighty boy named Tim. He had a big drum that he loved to play. Every day, Tim would go outside and play his drum for all his friends. One day, Tim and his friends went to the park to play. They played on the swings, the slide, and the see-saw. Tim brought his drum, too. He played it while his friends danced around. They had so much fun! When it was time to go home, Tim wanted to finish his drum song. He played it loudly for all to hear. His friends clapped and cheered for him. Tim felt very happy and proud. The end.

Lisa was very excited to go to her dance class. She liked to wear her pink dress and shoes and dance with her friends. She also liked her teacher, Miss Amy, who was very nice and fun. But today, Lisa felt a little scared. Miss Amy said they would learn a new dance, and Lisa did not know if she could do it. She did not want to make a mistake or fall down. She wanted to be a good dancer. "Lisa, are you ready?" Miss Amy asked, smiling. "We are going to start soon." Lisa nodded, but she felt her tummy hurt. She wished she could stay home and play with her toys. "Lisa, you look worried," Miss Amy said, gently. "Is something wrong?" Lisa shook her head, but she could not speak. She felt her throat tight. "Lisa, it's okay to be nervous," Miss Amy said, kindly. "But you don't have to be afraid. We are going to have fun and learn together. You are a smart and brave girl, and you can do anything you want. Just relax and enjoy the music. And remember, I am here to help you." Miss Amy gave Lisa a hug and a kiss. She took her hand and led her to the dance floor. Lisa felt a little better. She looked at Miss Amy and smiled. "Thank you, Miss Amy," Lisa said, softly. "You are the best teacher ever." Miss Amy smiled back. "You are very welcome, Lisa. You are the best student ever. Now, let's dance!" Lisa followed Miss Amy's steps and moved her arms and legs. She listened to the music and felt happy. She forgot about her fear and had fun. She was careful not to bump into anyone, but she also relaxed and let go. She felt like a star. Lisa loved her dance class. She was glad she came. She learned a new dance, and she made Miss Amy proud. She also made herself proud. She was a good dancer.
