Category: Intermediate
Title: Spike the Dog and the Cat

One day, a little dog named Spike went to work with his dad. Spike was a very good dog. His dad was grateful to have him. They went to a big place where they helped people. At work, Spike saw a big cat. The cat was not nice to Spike. The cat said, "You can't be here!" Spike was sad. He did not know what to do. He wanted to help his dad and make him proud. Spike went to his dad and told him about the mean cat. His dad said, "Don't worry, Spike. We can work together to make the cat nice." Spike and his dad talked to the cat. They were kind to the cat. Soon, the cat was nice to Spike too. They all worked together and were grateful for each other.

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Lily. She lived in a house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Spot. Lily loved to play with her toys upstairs in her room. One day, she had to complete a puzzle before dinner time. As Lily worked on her puzzle upstairs, she heard a strange noise. She looked around, but she didn't see anything. She went back to her puzzle, but the noise happened again. This time, she saw her dog, Spot, standing by the door. Spot wanted to play, but Lily had to complete her puzzle first. Lily decided to be kind and play with Spot for a little bit. She threw a ball, and Spot ran to get it. When Spot came back, he had a puzzle piece in his mouth! Lily was surprised. Spot helped her find the missing pieces, and they completed the puzzle together. It turned out that Spot was the one who hid the pieces all along! They had fun, and Lily was happy to have such a kind and playful dog.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy jet. The jet was very clean and nice. It had a big job to do. It had to fly high in the sky to take people to a fun place. One day, the jet met a little bird. The bird was scared to fly high. The jet said, "Don't worry, little bird. You can trust me. I will help you fly high in the sky." The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, tidy jet. I will try." The next day, the jet and the bird flew high in the sky together. The bird was not scared anymore. The bird said, "I am so happy I trusted you, tidy jet. Now, I can fly high in the sky!" The tidy jet smiled and said, "I am happy to help, little bird." From that day on, the bird and the tidy jet were best friends. They flew high in the sky together every day, helping each other and having fun. And they lived happily ever after.

In the modern living room, the mother and child sat together on the sofa. The child had a bowl of popcorn on his lap. The mother looked around the room and smiled. She asked the child if he liked it. The child smiled and nodded. Suddenly, the popcorn began to pop! The mother laughed and said, “The popcorn is still warm. Let’s stay here and enjoy it.” So, the mother and child remained on the sofa. They talked, laughed, and ate popcorn. They stayed there for the rest of the day. The mother and child had a wonderful time together. They were happy to remain in the modern living room, enjoying their popcorn.

One day, a little fox was walking in the warm sun. He was very happy. He saw a big tree and wanted to rest under it. As he sat down, he heard a voice. "Hello, little fox," said the voice. The little fox looked up and saw a big bird in the tree. "I have a question. Can you answer it?" The little fox smiled and said, "Yes, I can try to answer your question." The big bird asked, "Why is the sun so warm today?" The little fox thought for a moment and then said, "The sun is warm because it loves to make us happy and warm too." The big bird and the little fox laughed and played under the warm sun. They became good friends and answered many questions together.
