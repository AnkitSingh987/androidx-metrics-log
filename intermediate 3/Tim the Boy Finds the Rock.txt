Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Rock

One day, a boy named Tim went on a long walk with his mom. Tim had a small pocket on his shirt. He liked to put things in it. They walked and walked until they saw a big tree. Tim found a pretty rock under the tree. He showed it to his mom. "Wow, Tim! Can I see?" she asked. Tim said, "Yes, Mom." She looked at the rock and smiled. "Let's send a picture of the rock to Dad," she said. They took a picture and sent it to Dad. As they walked back home, Tim felt his pocket. The rock was gone! He was sad. But then, he heard a tiny voice. "Thank you for the rock!" It was a small bug. The bug had taken the rock to make a home. Tim was happy to help the bug and went home with a big smile.

One day, a little cat named Kitty had a meeting with her friends. They wanted to talk about a big party they were going to have. Kitty was very excited to see her friends and plan the fun party. At the meeting, Kitty's friends were talking too much and not listening. Kitty felt frustrated. She wanted everyone to work together and make the party great. So, she said, "Please, friends, let's all listen and share our ideas." Her friends heard her and stopped talking. They all took turns sharing their ideas for the party. Kitty was happy that her friends listened. They planned a great party, and everyone had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia walked on a path. The path was long and had many pretty flowers. Mia saw a pale blue butterfly. She wanted to follow the butterfly. Mia followed the butterfly to a seat under a big tree. She sat on the seat and looked at the butterfly. "Hi, butterfly!" Mia said. "You are so pretty!" The butterfly flew away, and Mia saw a little boy named Tom. Tom was sad. "Why are you sad, Tom?" Mia asked. Tom said, "I lost my toy." Mia wanted to help Tom. They looked for the toy together. They found the toy near the seat. Tom was happy, and they played together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who wanted to find an adventure. She put on her favourite dress and picked up her Teddy bear. Then, one sunny day, she decided it was time to go. "Let's march!" said the little girl. She grabbed Teddy and they went on their way. They marched east until they reached a dry and dusty field. The sun was hot and the ground was cracked. "We made it!" said the little girl, feeling tired but happy. Just then, a curious grasshopper hopped out of the long grass. He asked the little girl, "Where are you going?" The little girl was very excited to have made a new friend. She took his hand and said, "We're on an adventure! Let's march east together." So the little girl and her new grasshopper friend set off across the dry and dusty field, marching east to search for new adventures. They were never seen again, but it's said they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty little girl. She was so happy and cheerful. Every day, she would play in the garden and have a lot of fun. But one day, something changed. The girl started to worry. She didn't know why she was worried, but it made her feel uncomfortable. Mama saw that her daughter was worried and asked her about it. "What's wrong, sweetheart?" Mama asked in a mild voice. The girl slowly looked up to Mama and said, "I don't know why, but I'm feeling worried". Mama reassured her daughter that it was alright. She hugged the girl and said, "Don't worry, I'm here for you". The girl smiled and the worry slowly disappeared.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a little oyster named Ollie. Ollie had a hard shell and lived on the soft sand. The sea could be harsh sometimes, but Ollie was safe in his shell. One day, Ollie met a fish named Fin. Fin liked to chew on things. He said to Ollie, "I like to chew. Can I chew on your shell?" Ollie said, "No, please don't. My shell keeps me safe from the harsh sea." Fin listened to Ollie and did not chew on his shell. They became good friends and played in the sea together. Ollie and Fin learned to be careful in the harsh sea and had many fun days.
