Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Lazy Cat and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. He loved to sleep all day and never did any work. One day, Tom heard about a big party for all the animals in the town. To go to the party, every animal needed a ticket. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, said, "Tom, you should get a ticket for the party. It will be so much fun!" Tom was not sure. He began to worry. He thought getting a ticket might be hard work. Max told Tom, "Don't worry. I will help you get a ticket." Max and Tom went to get the ticket. They had to help clean the park to get it. Tom was lazy, but he saw Max working hard. Tom decided to work hard too. They both cleaned the park and got their tickets. At the party, Tom and Max had a great time. They danced, played games, and ate yummy food. Tom learned that working hard can bring good things. He was happy he had listened to Max and got the ticket. From that day on, Tom was not so lazy anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big hill. On top of the hill, he saw something unusual. It was a big, red ball. Tim wanted to play with the ball. Tim met a girl named Sue. "Do you see that big, red ball?" he asked. "Yes," said Sue. "Do you trust me?" Tim asked. "Yes, I trust you," said Sue. They both walked up the hill to get the ball. When they got to the top, something unexpected happened. The ball was not a ball at all! It was a big, red hat. Tim and Sue laughed. They put the hat on and rolled down the hill together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to keep his toys organized. Every day, he would put his toys in a special place, so he knew where to find them. Tim had many toys, but his favorite was a big red ball. One sunny day, Tim went to play with his big red ball. He hoped to have lots of fun. He threw the ball up and down, and kicked it all around. Tim was so happy to play with his big red ball. As he played, Tim saw a pretty image in the sky. It was a big, white cloud that looked like a bunny. Tim smiled and thought about how much fun it would be to play with a big, white bunny. He hoped that one day, he could play with a real bunny, too.

Once upon a time, there was a pig. The pig lived on a farm with many other animals. The pig was very hungry, so he went to find some food. The pig found a big tree with sour apples. He ate and ate until he was full. Then, he saw a bucket near the tree. The pig had an idea. He decided to fill the bucket with the sour apples. The pig carried the full bucket back to the farm. All the other animals saw the sour apples and wanted to try them too. They all ate the sour apples and played together. The pig was happy to share his food with his friends.

Once upon a time, a lively little girl named Rose was spending the day at the park. She saw many people playing and having fun, and she wanted to join them. When Rose noticed a group playing a game with colorful pieces, she asked if she could join. "Sure!" said one of the children and handed her a piece. Rose was so excited! The game was so much fun and Rose felt so happy. When the game was done, she saw that the other children were paying in money. She questioned them, "What is money?" The children laughed and explained that money is like a special kind of helpers that help with buying things. Rose was amazed and enjoyed learning about money. It was a wonderful day full of lively fun and learning for Rose!

One day, a happy clown came to town. He had a wide smile and wore a big red nose. He wanted to make all the children laugh and have fun. He walked around, making funny faces and doing silly tricks. The children saw him and started to laugh. The clown said, "Do you want to see a fun trick?" The children nodded their heads and said, "Yes, please!" The clown took out a big, colorful ball and started to juggle. He threw the ball high in the air and caught it with his hands. The children clapped and laughed. The clown then took out a tiny hat and put it on his head. He danced and sang a funny song. The children clapped their hands and sang along. They all had a great time with the happy clown. And when it was time for the clown to leave, they all said, "Thank you, clown, for making us laugh!"
