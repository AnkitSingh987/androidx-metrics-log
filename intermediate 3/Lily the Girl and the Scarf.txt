Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Scarf

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a red scarf. She loved her scarf very much. But she thought it was boring. She wanted to make it more fun. Lily had an idea. She got her mom's help to cut the scarf. They cut it into many small pieces. Then, they put the pieces back together. Now, the scarf had a new shape. Lily was very happy. Her scarf was not boring anymore. She wore it every day. Her friends liked her scarf too. They all played together, with Lily's fun scarf. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Amy loved cherries very much. They were her favorite fruit. One day, her mom said, "Amy, come! We have a great surprise for you!" Amy ran to her mom and saw a big cherry tree in their yard. "Wow!" said Amy. "A cherry tree! Can I eat the cherries?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can eat the cherries when they are red and ready." Every day, Amy would look at the cherry tree and watch the cherries grow. She could not wait to eat them. One sunny day, Amy saw the cherries were red and big. She called her mom and dad, "Come, come! The cherries are ready!" They all went outside and picked the cherries together. Amy ate the cherries and said, "These are the best cherries ever!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was eager to see the flowers bloom in her garden. Every day, she would run outside and look at the flowers. But one day, she could not find her shoes to go outside. Lily looked everywhere for her shoes. She looked under her bed, in the closet, and even in the kitchen. But she could not find them. Then, she remembered she left them upstairs. So, she hurried up the stairs to get her shoes. When Lily got upstairs, she found her shoes by her bed. But, there was a problem - her shoes were dirty. She knew her mom would be upset if she wore dirty shoes in the house. So, Lily decided to clean her shoes before going outside. Once her shoes were clean, Lily put them on and ran outside to see the flowers bloom.

Lily and Roxie were friends and they loved to play together. One day they decided to play a game. “Let’s play a game and test what we know,” said Roxie. “Yes, let’s do it!” said Lily. They decided to play a guessing game. Lily gave Roxie an orange message. “What’s inside?” asked Roxie. “You’ll have to guess,” said Lily. Roxie thought for a bit and then guessed, “Is it a treat?” Lily smiled. “Yes, it’s a treat! You guessed it right.” Roxie was so happy and they both hugged each other. They had a lot of fun playing the test game.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cushion. It lived in a small house with a little boy named Tim and his mom. Tim loved to play with the cushion. He liked to jump on it, sit on it, and even sleep on it. One day, Tim was playing with his friend, Sally. Sally saw the orange cushion and wanted to play with it too. But when Sally tried to take the cushion away from Tim, she pinched his hand by accident. Tim cried out, "Ouch! That hurt!" Sally said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't mean to pinch you." Tim's mom heard them and came to see what happened. She told them, "It's important to share and be careful when playing with friends. We don't want to hurt each other." Tim and Sally learned to share the orange cushion and play nicely together. They had lots of fun and became the best of friends. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with your friends, so everyone can have fun and be happy.

One day, a little car was driving down the road. It had four round tires. The car saw a weird-looking tree. It was big and had many colors. The car wanted to look at the tree again. So, the car decided to reverse and go back. As the car was reversing, it heard a loud noise. "Oh no!" said the car. One of its tires hit a big rock. The tire was now flat. The car felt sad. It couldn't move anymore. A kind truck saw the car and stopped. "What's wrong?" asked the truck. The car told the truck about the flat tire. The truck helped the car change the tire. They became friends. The car learned that it's important to be careful and watch where you're going. And the car also learned that friends can help when you're in trouble.
