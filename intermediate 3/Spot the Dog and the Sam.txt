Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Sam

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was not like other dogs. He had an ugly, big nose. Spot felt sad because the other dogs would not play with him. But Spot wanted to make friends. One day, Spot saw a new dog named Sam. Spot went up to Sam and said, "Hi, I am Spot. Do you want to play with me?" Sam looked at Spot's ugly nose but then smiled and said, "Yes, Spot! I would love to play with you." Sam and Spot had a great start to their friendship. They played all day and had fun. Spot was happy that Sam accepted him, even with his ugly nose. From that day on, Spot and Sam were best friends and always played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat grapes. One day, his mom did not allow him to eat more grapes. She said they were all gone. Tim was sad and wanted more grapes. Tim had an idea. He thought there might be some hidden grapes in the house. He started to look everywhere. Under the bed, behind the couch, and even in the toy box. But he could not find any grapes. Just when Tim was about to give up, he saw his dog, Max, playing with something. It was a big, purple ball. Tim went closer and saw that it was not a ball, but a giant grape! Tim was so happy and surprised. He shared the giant grape with Max, and they both had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a big design contest in a small town. A nice girl named Jane loved to design pretty things. She liked to draw and make beautiful things out of paper and colors. She was very excited to be in the contest. In the contest, there was a judge who would pick the best design. Jane worked very hard on her design. She made a lovely picture of a house with a big tree and flowers. She was very proud of her work. But a mean boy named Tom was jealous of Jane's design. He wanted to win the contest too. On the day of the contest, Tom did something very bad. When no one was looking, he took Jane's design and hid it. Then, he showed his own design to the judge. The judge picked Tom's design as the winner. Jane was very sad and cried. She never found her design, and Tom never told anyone what he did.

Once there was a boy. The boy liked to act. He acted all day. He was brilliant at acting. The boy had a friend. The friend liked to act too. They acted together. One day, the boy and the friend had a problem. They both wanted to act as the king. The boy said, "I want to be the king." The friend said, "No, I want to be the king." They were sad. They did not know what to do. Then, the boy had an idea. He said, "We can both be kings!" The friend liked the idea. They acted as two kings. They were happy. They acted all day. The boy and the friend were brilliant kings.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Bobo. Bobo liked to play ball with his friends. They played in the sun and had fun. One day, Bobo and his friends wanted to play a game. They made a goal with two sticks. The game was to kick the ball into the goal. Bobo tried very hard to score a goal, but it was not easy. After a while, Bobo felt tired. He sat down under a tree to relax. He watched his friends play and kick the ball. Soon, Bobo felt better and joined the game again. This time, he scored a goal! Bobo and his friends were happy and played until the sun went down.

Lily liked to look at small things with her microscope. She had a microscope from her dad, who was a scientist. She could see bugs, leaves, and hairs very big and clear. She thought it was fun and amazing. One day, she brought her microscope to school. She wanted to show her friends what she could see. She was very proud of her microscope. She offered to let them look too. She was a thoughtful girl who liked to share. But some of the kids were not nice. They did not care about the microscope. They wanted to play with other toys. They said the microscope was boring and silly. They laughed at Lily and made her feel bad. They took her microscope and threw it on the ground. It broke into pieces. Lily cried and cried. The teacher came and saw what happened. She was very angry and sad. She scolded the kids who broke the microscope. She told them they were mean and rude. She told them they had to say sorry to Lily and help her fix the microscope. She told them they had to learn to be kind and respectful. The moral of the story is: You should not make fun of other people's things. You should not break other people's things. You should be thankful when someone offers to share with you. You should be thoughtful and gentle with other people's feelings.
