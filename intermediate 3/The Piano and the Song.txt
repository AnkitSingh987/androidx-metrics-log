Category: Intermediate
Title: The Piano and the Song

Once upon a time, there was a big piano in a small room. A little girl named Lucy loved to play it. One day, she wanted to play a new song, but she had a struggle. The keys were hard to push, and the music did not sound right. Lucy felt angry. Lucy's friend, Tom, came to visit. He saw Lucy was angry and asked, "Why are you angry?" Lucy told him about the piano and the new song. Tom said, "Let's try together." They both sat at the piano and tried to play the song. At first, they still struggled. But they kept trying. Soon, the music started to sound better. Lucy and Tom played the new song together, and they were happy. They played the piano all day and did not feel angry anymore.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the shop with his mom. It was an unusual shop, with many big and small things. Tim saw a toy he liked, but his mom said, "No, not today." As they left the shop, it started to rain. Tim and his mom ran to their car, but the rain made it hard to see. They saw a big puddle and jumped over it. Tim laughed and said, "Rain is fun!" Then, something unexpected happened. A big frog jumped out of the puddle! The frog looked at Tim and said, "You are nice. I will give you one wish." Tim thought about the toy he saw in the shop. He said, "I wish for the toy." The frog smiled and the toy appeared in Tim's hands. Tim and his mom were very happy and thanked the frog. They went home and played with the toy all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little cricket. The cricket lived in a small box. A girl named Lily had the box. Lily was sometimes careless. One day, she played with the box outside. Lily wanted to see the cricket. She opened the box to look at it. But she was careless and did not hold the box well. The cricket saw the chance to jump out. It was free! Lily tried to catch the cricket, but it was too fast. The cricket was happy to be free. It jumped and played in the grass. But then, a big bird saw the cricket. The bird was hungry. The cricket did not see the bird. The bird swooped down and ate the cricket. The cricket was gone, and Lily was sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to make things with her hands. One day, she tried to make a dress for her doll. She used a needle and thread to make a stitch. But it was not easy. Lily felt frustrated. Lily's mom saw that she was sad. She said, "Don't worry, Lily. I will teach you how to make a good stitch." Lily was happy. She knew her mom could help her. They worked together to make the dress. It started to look nice. At last, the dress was done. Lily was so proud of her work. She showed it to her dad. He smiled and started to applaud. "Great job, Lily!" he said. Lily felt happy. She knew that with practice, she could do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a big mall. In the mall, there were many shops. One day, a boy named Tim went to the mall with his mom. Tim saw a very important letter on the floor. The letter needed to be mailed. Tim picked up the letter and showed it to his mom. They went to find the mail place in the mall. When they found the mail place, Tim gave the letter to the mail person. The mail person said "Thank you!" and mailed the important letter. Tim and his mom were happy they could help.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little yellow raven. The raven liked to dance. Every day, the raven would dance under the big trees. One day, while the raven was dancing, a big wind came. The wind blew the raven far away from the forest. The little raven was sad and scared. But the raven did not give up. The raven danced to feel better. As the raven danced, a kind bird saw the yellow raven. The kind bird helped the little raven find the way back to the big forest. When they got back, the yellow raven danced with joy. The kind bird and the yellow raven became best friends. From that day on, they danced together under the big trees every day.
