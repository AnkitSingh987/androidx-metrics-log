Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Thoughtful Boy and the Chicken

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim loved to create things. One day, he wanted to create a funny joke to make his friends laugh. He thought and thought about what to say. Tim went to his friend, Lily, and said, "I have a funny joke for you!" Lily was excited to hear the joke. Tim said, "Why did the chicken cross the road?" Lily asked, "Why?" Tim smiled and said, "To get to the other side!" But then, something unexpected happened. The chicken from the joke appeared! It walked right up to Tim and Lily. The chicken said, "That's not the real reason I crossed the road. I crossed the road to find my friend!" Tim and Lily were surprised but they laughed. They had never met a talking chicken before. The chicken joined them and they all became good friends. They had fun creating more jokes together.

One day, a big bear and a little dog were playing with a loop. The loop was unique because it was big and red. They liked to throw it and catch it. The bear was very good at catching the loop, but the dog was not. The bear said, "Dog, try to catch the loop with your mouth." The dog tried, but he could not catch it. The bear laughed and said, "It's okay, dog. You can try again." Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the loop up into the sky! The bear and the dog looked up and saw the loop flying away. They were very sad. A bird saw the loop and caught it with its beak. The bird flew down and gave the loop back to the bear and the dog. The bear and the dog were very happy. They said, "Thank you, bird, for giving us our unique loop back!" The bird was happy to help, and they all played together.

Mira and Ben are twins. They like to play outside. But today, the weather is bad. It is cold and wet and windy. They cannot go to the park or ride their bikes. "Mom, we are bored!" Mira says. "Can we do something fun?" Mom thinks for a moment. She smiles and says, "I have an idea. Why don't you make a unique picture with some things you find in the house?" Mira and Ben are curious. They run to their room and look for things to use. They find some paper, crayons, glue, scissors, and stickers. They also find some feathers, buttons, leaves, and shells. They start to make their picture. Mira draws a big sun with yellow crayons. She glues some feathers around it to make rays. Ben draws a blue sky and a green grass. He glues some leaves and shells to make flowers and animals. They are very happy with their picture. They show it to Mom. Mom is proud of them. She says, "Wow, what a unique picture! You are very creative. You made your own weather!" Mira and Ben smile. They say, "Thank you, Mom. We had fun. We don't mind the bad weather anymore. We can make our own weather!" Mom hugs them and says, "That's wonderful. I love you both. Now, let's hang your picture on the wall. And then, we can have some cookies and milk." Mira and Ben agree. They shut their glue and scissors and put them away. They follow Mom to the kitchen. They enjoy their cookies and milk. They are happy. They made a unique picture and a good day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lazy cat named Tom. One day, Tom saw a thick fog outside his house. He could not see much, so he stayed inside. Tom's friend, a little bird named Tim, came to visit. "Tom! Tom! Can you tell me what is outside?" chirped Tim. Tom looked out the window and said, "It is fog, Tim. It is hard to see." Tim wanted to play outside, but Tom was feeling lazy. "Let's play inside today," Tom suggested. They played with their toys and had so much fun. The fog went away, and they could see outside again. Tom and Tim were happy to be friends and play together.
