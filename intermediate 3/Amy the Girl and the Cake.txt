Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to bake with her mom in the kitchen. They would use the oven to make amazing cookies and cakes. Amy liked to watch the oven when it was hot and see the cookies grow. One day, Amy wanted to show her friends how she baked with her mom. She asked her mom if she could record them while they baked. Her mom said yes, and they started to bake. Amy held the phone and recorded her mom making the cake mix. But then, something went wrong. The oven started to make a loud noise. Amy and her mom looked at the oven and saw smoke. They were scared. Amy's mom turned off the oven and opened the door. The cake was burnt. They were sad, but they knew they could try again. They cleaned the oven and made a new cake. This time, it was perfect. Amy was happy to share the video of their amazing cake with her friends.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a squirrel on a tree. The squirrel had a big nut in its mouth. It was hungry and wanted to eat the nut. "Look, a squirrel!" Anna said. "It is so cute and fluffy!" "Can we give it some bread?" Ben asked. He had a sandwich in his backpack. "No, bread is not good for squirrels. They like nuts and seeds," Anna said. She knew a lot about animals. The squirrel saw Anna and Ben. It was curious and friendly. It jumped down from the tree and ran towards them. It wanted to say hello and maybe get some more food. "Hi, squirrel!" Anna and Ben said. They smiled and waved at the squirrel. The squirrel came closer and sniffed their backpacks. It smelled something yummy. It was Ben's sandwich. The squirrel wanted to try it. It used its sharp teeth and cut a hole in the backpack. It took out the sandwich and ran away with it. "Hey, that's my sandwich!" Ben shouted. He was angry and sad. He liked his sandwich. "Come back, squirrel!" Anna shouted. She was surprised and amused. She thought the squirrel was funny. The squirrel did not listen. It was happy and full. It had a nut and a sandwich. It climbed up another tree and ate them. It did not share with anyone. It was a greedy squirrel.

One day, a little dog named Spot had a big itch on his back. He tried to scratch it, but his paw could not reach it. Spot felt sad because the itch was very bad. He walked around the yard, hoping to find something to help him. As Spot walked, it started to rain. The rain made the ground wet. Spot saw a big tree and went under it to stay dry. Under the tree, he met a small bird. The bird saw that Spot was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, Spot?" Spot whispered to the bird, "I have a big itch on my back, and I can't reach it." The bird felt sorry for Spot and wanted to help. The bird flew up and used its beak to scratch Spot's itch. Spot felt so happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" The rain stopped, and the sun came out. Spot and the bird played together in the wet yard. They splashed in the puddles and had lots of fun. Spot was happy that he met a new friend who helped him with his itch.

One winter morning Jack was feeling very careless. He decided to fly outside and see what he could see. He looked up at the sky and saw a beautiful bird flying. The bird said, "Hello Jack! I'm flying to the other side of the world." Jack was so excited and asked the bird, "Can I come with you?" The bird agreed and the two friends flew off together. Jack and the bird flew through the sky, over the snowy fields and above the treetops. They flew higher and higher until suddenly the bird stopped. Pointing ahead, the bird said, "Look! There it is!" Jack saw a beautiful castle with a wall around it. Jack and the bird flew down to the castle and landed inside. Jack explored the castle walls, amazed at how wonderful and big it was. After a while, the bird said it was time to go. So Jack waved goodbye and they both flew away. As they flew back towards Jack's home, Jack knew he had experienced something very special and he would never be careless again.
