Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Park

Once upon a time, in a big green park, a playful dog named Max loved to roll in the grass. Max was always happy and liked to play with his friends. One sunny day, Max saw a big ball and wanted to play with it. Max ran up to the ball and said, "Hi ball, do you want to play with me?" The ball did not talk, but Max thought it said yes. Max pushed the ball with his nose and the ball began to roll. Max and the ball rolled and played together all day long. At the end of the day, Max and the ball had a race. Max ran as fast as he could and the ball rolled just as fast. Max won the race and felt very proud of his victory. Max and the ball became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy who wanted to find something special. He decided to follow a path that led to a farm. When he arrived, he saw horses, cows, and sheep. He was so excited! The boy was so happy and he ran around the farm, looking at everything. Soon he found a special barn by the horse pen. He went inside and saw a little puppy! The boy was so happy to see the puppy. He was very thoughtful and gentle around the pup. He pet the puppy and talked to him. They were best friends! The boy and the puppy followed a path around the farm and ran together all day. As the sun set, the boy thought about how special the farm was. He knew he would come back soon!

Once upon a time there was a pilot. He wanted to fly a new airplane. He had never flown one before, but he decided to try it. He asked his father for permission. His father said, "It looks like a lot of fun, but you have to be really careful. Please be safe." The pilot said, "Yes, Daddy! I will." So he hopped into the new airplane and took off. He had a lot of fun flying and soon he was a very confident pilot. But at one point he became a bit too confident and he almost crashed. The pilot remembered what his father had said to him and he thought, "I need to please my father by being safe." He regained control of the airplane and he safely flew it home. The moral of the story is that it is important to pay attention to what adults tell us. We should make sure to always keep them in mind and be responsible.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big mind. He liked to think and learn new things. One day, Tim went for a walk with his dog, Max. They walked into a big fog. Tim could not see very well. Max barked, but Tim could not see him. Tim was scared, but he knew he had to find Max. He walked into the unknown fog. He called Max's name. He heard Max bark again. Tim followed the sound of Max's bark. He walked and walked until he found Max. Tim was happy to find Max. They hugged each other. Tim and Max walked back home together. They knew they could face the unknown fog because they had each other. They were brave and their love made them strong.

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in his yard. He found a big ball and started to play with it. Spot was very happy. But then, he kicked the ball too hard and it went into the neighbor's yard. Spot felt sad and hurt because he could not get his ball back. Spot's dad saw him feeling hurt and asked, "Spot, why are you sad?" Spot said, "My ball went into the neighbor's yard, and I can't get it back." Spot's dad said, "Don't worry, Spot. I will help you get your ball back." Spot and his dad went to the neighbor's office to ask for the ball. The neighbor was very nice and said, "Sure, you can have your ball back." Spot and his dad said, "Thank you!" They went back home and Spot was happy again. Spot and his dad sat in the yard and played with the ball. They had so much fun together. Spot learned to be more careful when playing with his ball, so it wouldn't go into the neighbor's yard again. And whenever Spot felt hurt, he knew his dad would always be there to help him.
