Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Board

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy bear. The bear liked to play with a board. He would put the board on the ground and jump on it. He had so much fun. One day, a little girl saw the hairy bear. She was not scared. She walked up to the bear and said, "Hello, bear! Can I play with your board?" The bear nodded his head, and they played together. After they played, the little girl gave the big, hairy bear a hug. They became best friends, and they played with the board every day. The bear was very happy, and so was the little girl.

One day, a big angry dog named Max was in the park. Max liked to shout at other dogs and people. He was not very nice. Max had a life of being angry and shouting at others. One sunny day, Max saw a small cat named Luna. Max went up to Luna and shouted, "Go away, cat!" Luna looked at Max and said, "I am not scared of you, dog. I want to be your friend." Max was very surprised. No one had ever wanted to be his friend before. Max started to feel happy. He and Luna played in the park all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. Max learned that being nice was more fun than being angry. From that day on, Max and Luna were best friends and Max never shouted at anyone again.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Tim. Tim always made a mess when he played. One day, Tim was playing with his ball near the waste bin. He did not know that the waste bin was full. Tim hit the ball too hard and it went into the waste bin. "Oh no!" said Tim, "My ball is in the waste!" Tim's friend, a little bird named Sue, flew down to help. "Don't worry, Tim," said Sue, "I will get your ball for you." Sue picked up the ball with her beak and gave it back to Tim. Tim was very happy. He knew that he should be more careful when playing near the waste bin. From that day on, Tim was not as clumsy as before. He and Sue played together every day, and they always had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a kind fairy. The fairy lived in the garden, where she wrote beautiful stories. Every day, the fairy wrote carefully and neatly. She was always polite and said hello to the flowers. One day, the fairy invited the little three year old to play with her. The fairy gave the little one a special pen and a paper. Together, they wrote a story about a brave knight. The little one was so excited! He wrote the words, while the kind fairy corrected them. When they were done, the fairy said, “You have written a very good story. Well done!” The little one smiled and said, “Thank you! I’m so happy.” The kind fairy smiled back and said, “It was a pleasure writing with you.”

Once upon a time, there was a calm town. In this town, there was a kind doctor who could heal people. Everyone in the town loved the doctor. One day, a big missile fell from the sky. It made a loud noise and scared everyone. The people in the town did not know what to do. They were very sad and scared. The kind doctor said, "Do not worry, I will heal the town." The people felt better when they heard the doctor's words. The doctor worked hard and soon the town was calm again. The people were happy and thanked the doctor for healing their town.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a healthy boy who loved to run and play. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big red door. He was curious and wanted to see what was behind it. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim!" Tim tried to open the door, but it was locked. He could not get in. Tim's mom had an idea. She took out a jar of jam from her bag. She said, "Let's have a picnic and forget about the locked door." They sat on the grass, ate sandwiches with jam, and had a fun time together. In the end, Tim didn't mind that the door was locked. He was happy to spend time with his mom at the park.
