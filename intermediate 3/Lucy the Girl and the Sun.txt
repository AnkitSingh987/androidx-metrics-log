Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play outside. The sky was blue, and the sun was shining. As she played, she heard a distant sound. It was thunder. She was scared, but she wanted to know where the sound came from. Lucy asked her friend, Tom, "Did you hear the thunder?" Tom said, "Yes, let's find it!" They walked and walked, looking for the thunder. They found a big hill and decided to lie down and rest. They looked at the sky and saw dark clouds coming. Suddenly, a big, friendly giant appeared! The giant said, "Hello, I am making the thunder! I like to play with the clouds." Lucy and Tom were surprised but happy to meet the giant. They played together, making thunder and laughing. The thunder wasn't scary anymore, it was fun!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Will. He was three, but he looked like he was five. Will was charming and kind, and everybody loved him. One day, Will went for a walk. On the way, he saw something on the ground. It was leather. Will picked it up and held it tight. He took it home and asked his mom what it was. She said it was a special leather glove. She then said Will had to wash it. Will was confused and asked why he had to do this. His mom said, “It will look beautiful if you wash it.” So Will went and washed the glove. When he was done, he couldn't believe his eyes! The leather was so shiny and pretty. He was very happy and thanked his mom for the charm.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play with things. One day, they find a big box of things. There are balls, cars, dolls, books and more. "Wow, look at all these things!" Lila says. "Let's count them!" "Okay!" Ben says. "You count the balls, and I count the cars." They start to count. Lila counts one, two, three, four, five balls. Ben counts one, two, three, four, five, six, seven cars. "I have more!" Ben says. "I have seven cars, and you have five balls." "No, you don't!" Lila says. "You are foolish. You counted the same car twice. Look, this car and this car are the same. They are both red and have four wheels." Ben looks at the cars. He sees that Lila is right. He counted the same car twice. He feels silly. "Oops, you are right." Ben says. "I am sorry. I made a mistake. We have the same. We both have five things." Lila smiles. She is not mad at Ben. She knows that everyone makes mistakes sometimes. "It's okay." Lila says. "We are still friends. Let's count the dolls next. Maybe we have more dolls than balls and cars." "Okay!" Ben says. "Let's count the dolls. Maybe we do." They start to count the dolls. They have fun counting and playing with the things. They are happy.

Once upon a time, a big gorilla lived in a large zoo. He was very nosy and liked to watch people who came to visit him. One day, he saw a little girl with a red balloon. The gorilla wanted the red balloon, so he tried to get it. He reached out his big hand and grabbed the balloon. But, the balloon flew away and he could not catch it. The little girl was sad, but the gorilla felt bad too. The zookeeper saw what happened and had an idea. He went to the store and bought many balloons. Then, he came back to the zoo and released the balloons into the gorilla's cage. The gorilla was very happy to have so many balloons, and the little girl smiled too. The nosy gorilla learned to be careful with balloons and became friends with the little girl.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lisa. Lisa loved to swing on the swing in her garden. It made her feel happy and free! One day, Lisa's mom took her for a walk down the road. They passed a big tree with a swing hung from it. Lisa asked if she could swing, and her mom said yes. So Lisa started swinging, higher and higher until her feet were in the sky. She laughed and laughed. Suddenly, she saw something funny. What was it? Two cats chasing each other around the trunk of the tree! This made Lisa laugh even more. As Lisa walked away with her mom, she thought about what had happened. She realised that being friendly and kind was more fun than chasing each other around like the cats. Lisa knew that she would remember this lesson for a long time.
