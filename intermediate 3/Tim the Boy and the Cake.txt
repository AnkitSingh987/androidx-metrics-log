Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cake

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a fun party. At the party, there was a big box. Tim's mom said, "There is a surprise inside the box." Tim was very excited to see what was inside. Tim and his friends played crazy games at the party. They laughed and had so much fun. Tim's mom said, "It's time to open the big box!" All the kids gathered around the box. Tim opened the box and found a card. The card had a picture of a big, happy cake. Tim's mom said, "The surprise is a big cake for everyone!" All the kids were so happy and enjoyed the yummy cake.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. Sue saw a big, soft toy on a high shelf. She wanted the toy so much. "Mom, can I have that toy, please?" Sue asked. Her mom looked at the toy and said, "That is a luxury toy, Sue. It is very good, but we don't have money for it now. Maybe we can get it later." Sue was sad, but she knew her mom was right. They went back home. Sue tried to forget about the toy, but she couldn't. She thought of a plan. The next day, Sue did many good things. She cleaned her room, helped with the dishes, and was very nice to her little brother. Sue's mom was so happy. At the end of the day, Sue's mom said, "You were so good today, Sue. Let's go back to the store and get that luxury toy." They went to the store, and Sue reached high to get the toy. She was so happy and thanked her mom.

One day, there was a little girl walking in the sunshine. It was very warm and she felt very lucky to be outside. Suddenly, a strong wind blew and the girl started to shiver. She hugged her arms and ran to her house. She was cold, but she was happy that the sun still shone. When the little girl opened the door, she saw something was really special - inside there was a warm fire! She ran to the fireplace and held her hands up to the heat. It was so comfortable and cosy that she began to smile. The sun may have been hiding behind the clouds, but the little girl was still lucky. She was warm and safe in her own home, and that made her feel very happy.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw an unknown animal. It was big and had stripes. She knew it was a tiger. Lucy was not scared. She wanted to be friends with the tiger. Lucy said, "Hello, tiger. Welcome to the park. Do you want to play with me?" The tiger looked at Lucy and smiled. It was a nice tiger. It wanted to play with Lucy too. They played on the swings and the slide. They had so much fun. Lucy and the tiger became best friends. They played in the park every day. Everyone loved the kind tiger.

One day, a girl named Amy and her mom decide to go on a holiday. They pack their bags and get in the car. They drive to a fun place near the sea. Amy is very happy to play in the sand and swim in the water. While playing, Amy finds a thin stick. She shows it to her mom and says, "Look, Mom! I found a thin stick. What can we do with it?" Her mom smiles and says, "We can use it to draw in the sand, Amy. Let's make a big heart together." As they start to draw, a big wave comes and washes away their heart. Amy is sad, but her mom says, "It's okay, Amy. We can always draw another one." They draw a new heart, and this time, it stays. They learn that sometimes things go away, but we can always try again and make it better. The moral value of the story is that we should not give up when something unexpected happens. We can always try again and make things better.
