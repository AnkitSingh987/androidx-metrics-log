Category: Intermediate
Title: Ziggy the Jolly Puppy and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a jolly little puppy named Ziggy. Ziggy always played in the garden, eating flowers and chasing the butterflies. But one day, he made a mistake. He dug up a large hole right in the middle of the garden. When the family saw this, they were angry and they decided to punish Ziggy by giving him no treats. Ziggy was sad, but he knew he must obey. The next day, instead of digging, Ziggy ran around the garden in a zigzag pattern, following the butterflies. Even though Ziggy had not been allowed to have any treats, he was still happy and jolly. This story teaches us an important lesson about being honest and obeying the rules. Even if we make a mistake, we should apologise and learn from it, so that we don't get punished again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy box in a little girl's closet. The little girl, named Amy, wanted to open the box. But it was too heavy for her to move. She asked her mom for help. Amy's mom came into the room and saw the heavy box. She told Amy to relax and not worry. Together, they pushed the heavy box out of the closet. Amy was very happy and excited to see what was inside. When they opened the box, they found a big, fluffy pillow. Amy's mom said it was a special pillow to help her relax and sleep better. Amy was very happy and hugged the pillow. From that day on, she always slept with her special pillow and felt very relaxed.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to go on walks with his dog, Sam. They would walk by the big tree, the red house, and the pond. One day, they found a box. Tim did not know what was in the box. It was a mystery. Tim took the box home and showed it to his mom. "Mom, I found a box. What is inside?" he asked. His mom looked at the box and said, "I don't know, Tim. It is a fine box. Let's open it and see." Tim and his mom tried to open the box, but it was locked. They could not open it. Tim was sad. The next day, Tim's friend, Sue, came to play. Tim showed Sue the box. Sue had an idea. "Let's ask my dad. He has a key that can open the box," she said. Sue's dad came and offered to help. He used his key and opened the box. Inside the box, there were toys and candy. Tim, Sam, and Sue were happy. They shared the toys and candy and played all day. The mystery was solved, and they all had fun.

Tom and Sue like to play in the woods. They see many animals and plants. They are happy and curious. One day, they see a brown fox. The fox is fast and smart. It runs and jumps and hides. Tom and Sue want to see it better. They follow the fox. The fox leads them to a big hole. The hole is dark and deep. The fox stops and looks at them. It points with its nose to the hole. It makes a sound like "come". Tom and Sue are not afraid. They think the fox is friendly. They want to see what is in the hole. They go closer and closer. But the fox is not friendly. It is hungry and mean. It pushes Tom and Sue into the hole. They fall and scream. They cannot get out. The fox laughs and runs away. Tom and Sue are sad and scared. They cry and cry. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small bird. The bird was very envious of other birds. They had big nests with lots of twigs. The bird wanted a big nest too. So, it started to look for twigs. One day, the bird saw a big twig. It was very happy. The bird tried to lay the twig on its nest, but it was too big. The bird tried again and again, but it just could not make the twig fit. The bird became very sad. It did not know what to do. It kept trying to lay the big twig on its nest, but it never worked. The bird's nest broke and fell from the tree. The bird learned that being envious and wanting too much can lead to bad things.
