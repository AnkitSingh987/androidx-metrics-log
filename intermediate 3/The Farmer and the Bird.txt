Category: Intermediate
Title: The Farmer and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a farmer who lived in a small village. One day, he was walking around the village when he passed through the forest. While walking, he observed a big fat bird in the sky. He thought that it would be wonderful if it was his pet. So, without thinking, he tried to catch it. He said, "I must catch that bird!" But no matter how hard he tried, the bird flew away. The farmer was very stupid. He tried again and again, but the bird kept escaping. Eventually, it was getting dark so the farmer had to give up. He said to himself, "Why am I so stupid?" The moral of this story is that you should think twice before taking any action. If you think about the consequences of your actions, you won't regret them later.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small, modern house with his mom and dad. He loved to play with his toys and eat yummy snacks. One day, Tim wanted to find a toy in the cupboard. He asked his mom if he could use the cupboard to look for his toy. His mom said, "Yes, you can use the cupboard, but be careful with the doors." Tim was very happy and went to the cupboard. He opened the cupboard doors and found many toys inside. Tim played with his toys all day long. The cupboard became his favorite place in the house. And from that day on, he always remembered to use the cupboard carefully and keep it neat and tidy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a light drum in his room. He did not know where it came from. He thought it was a gift. Tim loved to play with the drum. He played it all day long. His mom and dad liked to hear him play. One day, Tim saw a group of kids playing music in the park. They had a guitar, a flute, and a big drum. Tim wanted to join them. He ran home and got his light drum. Then, he went back to the park. Tim asked the kids, "Can I join you?" The kids looked at Tim's drum and smiled. "Of course, you can join us!" they said. Tim was happy. He played his light drum with the other kids. They made beautiful music together. Everyone in the park stopped to listen. They clapped and danced to the music. Tim and his new friends played music every day in the park. They became the best music group in town. Tim's light drum was their secret. It made their music sound special. And Tim was very happy that he could join the group and share his gift with everyone.

Once upon a time there was a bad cat who didn't like parties. He always ruined them. One day, he heard about an interesting party and decided to go. When he got there, he saw all the fun things the children and adults were doing. He tried to join in, but everyone booed him away. He was very sad and he didn't know what to do. Just then, a kind girl with long hair and beautiful eyes stepped forward and said, "Please don't be sad. Come with me. I'll save you!" The girl held out her hand and the bad cat took it. The girl took the bad cat away from the party, but it was too late. The party was already ruined. No one ever saw the bad cat again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box with many fun toys. One day, he found a long tape in the toy box. He did not know what the tape was for, but he wanted to play with it. Tim took the tape and started to look around. He saw his dog, Spot. Spot was a very obedient dog. Tim had an idea! He would use the tape to make a leash for Spot. Tim tied one end of the tape to Spot's collar and held the other end in his hand. Tim and Spot went for a walk with the tape leash. Spot was happy and walked next to Tim. They looked at the trees, flowers, and birds. Tim was happy too because he found a new way to play with the tape. They walked back home and Tim put the tape back in the toy box. From that day on, Tim and Spot always used the tape to go for walks.
