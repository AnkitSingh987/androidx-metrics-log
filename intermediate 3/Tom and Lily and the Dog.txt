Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Dog

Tom and Lily were best friends. They liked to play in the park, draw pictures and share snacks. One day, they saw a big dog in the park. The dog was barking and running around. Tom and Lily were scared of the dog. "Let's go away from the dog," Lily said. "He might bite us." But Tom saw that the dog had a collar and a tag. He thought the dog was lost and needed help. He was brave and wanted to help the dog. "I will offer the dog some of my snack," Tom said. "Maybe he will be nice and stop barking." Tom took out a piece of cheese from his pocket and walked slowly to the dog. He held out his hand with the cheese and said, "Hello, dog. Do you want some cheese? Are you lost?" The dog stopped barking and sniffed the cheese. He wagged his tail and licked Tom's hand. He ate the cheese and looked happy. Tom saw the tag on the dog's collar. It said, "Max. Call this number if you find me." Tom and Lily were happy that they found Max's owner. They called the number and waited for Max's owner to come. Max's owner was very grateful and thanked Tom and Lily. He gave them a hug and a reward. Tom and Lily were proud of themselves. They had helped a lost dog and made a new friend. They had big hearts and were very brave. They went back to playing in the park, smiling and laughing.

Once upon a time there was a woman. She liked to keep unusual things. She kept so many unusual things that people thought she was strange. One day, the woman kept a little puppy. The puppy was so small that it fit in her pocket. She kept the puppy with her wherever she went. The woman took the puppy to the park and the puppy had so much fun! It chased the butterflies and played in the grass. People were amazed to see how the woman could keep such a small puppy safe and happy. Even though the puppy was only a few inches tall, the woman kept it safe and happy. The woman and the puppy were so happy together. They kept up their unusual adventures wherever they went.

Once upon a time, there was a player named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball all day. He was very healthy because he played a lot. One day, Tim saw a big hole in the ground. He wanted to prevent his ball from falling into the hole. So, he thought of a plan. Tim found a big stick and covered the hole. Now, his ball could not fall in. Tim was happy and played all day with his ball. He stayed healthy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a regular trunk. It was empty, but it wanted to be full. So each day it asked ‘What can I add?’. One day, a soft blanket was added. The trunk was so pleased that it leapt around in happiness. The next day, bright toys were added to the trunk. They rattled and rolled and filled the trunk with fun. The day after, a yummy snack was added. The trunk was so happy that it laughed and sang a song. The trunk was now full and happy. Now it could hold all the fun things it needed. The end.

One day, a little bird named Tim was flying near a big volcano. He saw a unique rock that he wanted to take a rest on. As he sat on the rock, he saw a big, red dog named Max. "Hi Max," said Tim. "Do you want to rest with me on this unique rock by the big volcano?" Max said, "Yes, I am tired. Let's rest here together." As they were resting, the big volcano started to make a loud noise. Tim and Max looked up and saw something unexpected. The volcano was not really a volcano! It was a big, red mountain that could talk! "Hello, Tim and Max," said the big, red mountain. "I am not a volcano, I am a talking mountain. I am happy to see you resting on my unique rock." Tim and Max were very surprised but happy to meet their new friend, the talking mountain. They all became good friends and had lots of fun together.
