Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Girl and the Deep

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who lived in a town. She liked to explore and look at things that were new and fun. One day, she decided to examine a deep field near the town. She was curious and wanted to find out what was inside. She walked and walked, but there was nothing to see. Suddenly, she heard someone talking. She stopped and listened carefully. It was a friendly old man who was speaking. "Hello," he said. "I am examining this deep field. Have you ever seen anything special around here?" The girl shook her head and said, "No, I haven't seen anything." The old man smiled and said, "Well, why don't you examine it too? You never know what you might find." The girl smiled, thanked the old man, and went back to exploring the deep field. She looked into every corner and found all sorts of exciting things. It was a great adventure!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a new ball. He loved to play with it. He liked to squeeze it and throw it high in the sky. One day, Tim saw a big trophy in the park. He wanted to win it. A big girl named Sally was also in the park. She wanted the trophy too. They both thought of a game. They would see who could throw the ball the highest. The one who could throw it the highest would win the trophy. Tim tried very hard. He squeezed the ball and threw it up. It went very high. Then it was Sally's turn. She also squeezed the ball and threw it high. They both looked up to see whose ball went the highest. Tim's ball went higher than Sally's. So, Tim won the big trophy. Sally was sad, but Tim said, "Let's play together next time." Sally smiled and they became friends. They played with the new ball and the trophy every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an adventurous boy who loved to play outside. One day, Tim wanted to go on an adventure. Before he went out, Tim needed to dress. He put on his favorite pants and got ready for his big adventure. As Tim was playing, he saw a big tree. He thought it would be fun to climb the tree. So, he started to climb. Tim was very happy and excited. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's pants got stuck on a branch! He tried to get free, but he couldn't. Tim was scared, but he remembered what his mom always said: "When you need help, ask for it." So, Tim called for help. His friend Sam heard him and came to help. Sam helped Tim get his pants unstuck from the tree. Tim was so thankful for his friend's help. From that day on, Tim learned that it's okay to ask for help when you need it. And that was the moral of the story: always ask for help when you need it.

Tommy was a big boy who liked to play with his cars and trucks. He had many cars and trucks of different colors and sizes. He liked to make them zoom and crash on the floor. He also liked to wear his uniform, a blue shirt and pants that his mommy gave him. He said his uniform made him look like a policeman. One day, Tommy was playing with his cars and trucks in his room, when his little sister Lily came in. Lily was a baby who liked to grab and chew things. She saw Tommy's cars and trucks and wanted to play with them too. She crawled to Tommy and reached for his red car. "No, Lily, that's mine!" Tommy shouted. He snatched the car from Lily and pushed her away. Lily started to cry. "Tommy, what are you doing?" Mommy asked. She heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw Tommy holding his car and Lily crying on the floor. "Mommy, Lily tried to take my car!" Tommy said. "She can't play with my cars and trucks, they are mine!" "Tommy, that's not nice. You have to share with your sister. She is too little to understand. She just wants to play with you. You can't be selfish and mean. You have to be kind and gentle." Mommy said. "But Mommy, she will break them! She will put them in her mouth and make them dirty! She can't play with my cars and trucks!" Tommy said. "Tommy, I understand that you love your cars and trucks, but they are just toys. They are not more important than your sister. She is your family and you have to love her and protect her. You can't hurt her or make her cry. You have to allow her to play with some of your cars and trucks. You can show her how to play with them and teach her their names and colors. You can be a good big brother and a good policeman." Mommy said. Tommy looked at Mommy and then at Lily. He saw that Lily was still crying and looked helpless. He felt sorry for her. He remembered that Mommy said he had to be a good big brother and a good policeman. He decided to listen to Mommy and share with Lily. "Okay, Mommy, I will share with Lily. I will allow her to play with some of my cars and trucks. I will show her how to play with them and teach her their names and colors. I will be a good big brother and a good policeman." Tommy said. He put down his red car and picked up his yellow truck. He gave it to Lily and said, "Here, Lily, this is a truck. It is yellow. You can play with it, but don't put it in your mouth. It is not food. It is a toy." Lily stopped crying and smiled. She took the truck and held it in her hands. She looked at Tommy and said, "Thank you, Tommy. You are a good big brother and a good policeman. I love you." Tommy smiled too. He felt happy. He hugged Lily and said, "You are welcome, Lily. You are a good little sister and a good friend. I love you too." Mommy smiled and said, "I'm proud of you, Tommy. You are a good big brother and a good policeman. You are also a good son. I love you both." She hugged Tommy and Lily and kissed them. They played with the cars and trucks together and had fun. They were a happy family.
