Category: Intermediate
Title: Blue the Fragile Bird and the Song

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a fragile little bird named Blue. Blue was very good at singing. One day, Blue found a shiny piece of something on the ground. Blue picked it up with its beak and showed it to his friend, Kitty. "Look, Kitty! I found a shiny piece!" said Blue. Kitty looked at the shiny piece and said, "Wow, Blue! That's pretty!" Blue and Kitty went to the village to show everyone the shiny piece. All the people and animals gathered around to see it. Blue sang a beautiful song, and everyone applauded. They clapped their hands and said, "Good job, Blue! You are a great singer!" Blue felt very happy and proud. He knew that even though he was fragile, he could still make others happy with his singing and his shiny piece. And from that day on, Blue and Kitty became the best of friends, and they shared many more adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing in his room when he heard his mom call him. "Tim, come to the table!" she said. Tim ran to the table and saw a big surprise. There was a huge cake on the table! Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to eat the cake, but his mom said, "Not yet, Tim. We need to wait for your dad." Tim felt frustrated because he wanted to eat the cake now. He sat at the table and waited for his dad to come home. When Tim's dad came home, he saw the cake on the table and said, "What a nice surprise!" Tim and his parents all sat down at the table and enjoyed the cake together. Tim was no longer frustrated, and they all had a great time.

One day, a clean mole named Moly was digging in the dirt. Moly liked to make new friends. Moly saw a big tree and thought, "I want to welcome a friend to my home." Moly dug and dug until he saw light. He popped out of the ground and saw a big cat. Moly said, "Hello, big cat! I am Moly the mole. I welcome you to my home." The big cat looked at Moly and said, "Thank you, Moly. But I am not a cat, I am a tiger." Moly was surprised! He did not know the big cat was a tiger. The tiger was nice and did not hurt Moly. They became good friends and played together. Moly welcomed the tiger to his home and they had fun every day.

One day, a bee named Buzz went out to find honey. Buzz flew in the dark woods. He saw a big bear. The bear was sad. "Hello, bear. Why are you sad?" asked Buzz. The bear said, "I want honey, but I can't find any." Buzz wanted to help. "I can show you where to find honey," he said. The bear was happy. They went to find honey together. Buzz showed the bear a big tree. The bear climbed the tree. But, surprise! There was no honey in the tree. It was full of sweet, yummy fruit. The bear and Buzz ate the fruit and became best friends. They learned that sometimes, the best things come when you least expect them.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there lived a good rhinoceros named Remy. Remy had a lot of friends. He liked to play and have fun with them. One day, Remy and his friends found a big bowl. They wanted to mix something yummy in it. So, they put in apples, bananas, and other fruits. They used a big stick to mix it all up. Remy and his friends had a great time eating the mix they made. They laughed and played all day. And from that day on, they loved to mix fruits and share with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars and his big board. One hot day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, let's go outside and play!" Tim looked at his mom and said, "No, I want to play with my cars and board." His mom said, "But it's hot outside, and we can play with water to cool down." Tim thought for a moment and said, "Okay, but I want to bring my board too!" So, Tim and his mom went outside with the board. They played with water and splashed each other. Tim put his board on the ground and used it as a bridge for his toy cars. He was having so much fun that he forgot how hot it was. After a while, Tim's mom asked him if he wanted to go back inside. But Tim refused, "No, mom, I want to play more!" And they continued to play and laugh in the hot sun.
