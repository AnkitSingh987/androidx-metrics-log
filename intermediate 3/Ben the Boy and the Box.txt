Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Boy and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Ben. He had a special box with him, it was a case. Every morning, Ben would open the case and examine it. Inside it he found a big surprise. One day, Ben was playing when his mom called him. She had something special to show Ben. It was a big box with a big red bow. The box was closed. Ben’s mom said, “Let’s examine the box to find out what’s inside!” So the two of them opened the box together. Inside there were lots of toys! Ben and his mom were so excited. Ben was so happy to receive such a wonderful surprise. The moral of the story is that it’s important to take the time to examine things and open them up to find out what’s inside. You may be surprised by what’s in store.

One day, a squirrel named Tom was in the park. He was looking for nuts to eat. He saw a big tree with many nuts on it. Tom was very happy. Tom gazed at the tree and saw a pale nut. He thought it was special. He climbed the tree and took the nut. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. When he showed Sam the nut, Sam was very excited too. But then, something unexpected happened. The nut started to shake and crack open. A little bird came out of the nut! Tom and Sam were very surprised. They thought it was a nut, but it was really a bird's egg. The bird became their new friend.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a modest box. The box was a mystery. Sue wanted to keep the box. She asked her friend, Tom, "Should we open the box?" Tom said, "No, let's keep the box closed. It is fun to guess what is inside." They both thought about what could be in the mystery box. Sue thought it was a toy, but Tom thought it was candy. They played with the box, but the mystery made them fight. Sue wanted to open it, but Tom did not. They stopped playing, and Sue kept the box. That night, Sue heard a noise from the box. She was scared but opened it anyway. Inside the box was a small, lost kitten. Sue was so happy and surprised! The next day, she showed Tom the kitten, and they both agreed to take care of it together. The mystery box brought them a new friend, and they were happy again.

One day, a little girl was walking to the park. Suddenly, she heard a loud sound coming from a nearby house. She heard loud cries and weeping. The little girl peeked through the window of the house. She saw a boy, who was about the same age as her. He was upset and was crying. The little girl tried to talk to the boy, but he was too upset to listen. She asked him why he was so noisy, but he just cried louder. The little girl wanted to help the crying child, so she offered him her favourite teddy bear which she had brought to the park. The boy pulled the teddy bear close and held it like a shield. But then something happened; a horrible, not-so-nice man appeared at the window. He shouted at the little girl and the weeping boy. The man took the teddy bear away, and the little girl ran away and never returned. The boy was left all alone and had nobody to comfort him. He kept on weeping and wept until morning. The little girl never learnt what happened to the boy, but she was sure the man was very mean.

One day, a big car had a boo-boo. A mechanic named Tom came to help. Tom was very good at fixing cars. He looked at the car and saw the problem. Tom said to the car, "I see you need help. I will fix you." The car was happy. Tom worked hard to make the car feel better. He observed the car and knew just what to do. It was a bitter cold day. Tom was cold, but he did not stop. He wanted to help the car. The car said, "Thank you, Tom. You are very kind." Tom smiled and kept working. Soon, the car was all better. Tom was happy he could help. The car said, "You are the best mechanic!" Tom and the car were now good friends. They played and laughed together.
