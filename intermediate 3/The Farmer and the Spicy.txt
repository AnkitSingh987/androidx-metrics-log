Category: Intermediate
Title: The Farmer and the Spicy

Once upon a time there was a farmer. On her farm there was a very spicy vegetable growing, which no one had ever seen before. The farmer decided to use the spicy vegetable to make a soup. She carefully chopped it up and added it to the boiling pot. Once the soup was finished, she offered it to her neighbours. They were brave and tried the soup. "It's too spicy!" one of the neighbours exclaimed. The soup was so spicy that none of them could eat it. The farmer was sad that no one could use the spicy vegetable. Everyone threw away the soup and the farmer felt a bit silly. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very restless. He wanted to play outside, but it was raining. His mom told him to play in the garage instead. Tim was happy because the garage had many fun things. While in the garage, Tim found a big box. He wanted to know what was inside. He tried to open it, but it was hard. He pulled and pulled until it opened. Inside, he found a big ball. Tim was very excited and started to play with it. Tim threw the ball high up in the air. But the ball hit something and made a loud noise. Tim got scared and ran to tell his mom. When they went back to the garage, they saw that the ball had broken a window. Tim was sad because he made a big mess. His mom was not happy, and Tim had to help clean up the broken glass.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dark night. All the people were in their homes. There was a man named Tom. Tom had a big truck. He needed to deliver something very special. Tom had a lot of cocoa in his truck. The cocoa was for the people in the town. They loved to drink cocoa when it was cold outside. Tom wanted to make them happy. He started to drive his truck on the dark night. Tom drove all night. He stopped at each house to deliver the cocoa. The people were so happy when they saw Tom and his truck. They thanked him for the cocoa. Tom smiled and drove away. The town was warm and happy because of Tom and his cocoa.

Once upon a time, there was a sad duck named Dolly. Dolly was sad because she had oil on her feathers. She could not play with her friends in the pond. One day, Dolly met a kind frog named Freddy. Freddy said, "I can help you remove the oil from your feathers." Dolly was happy to hear that. Freddy and Dolly tried to remove the oil by rubbing Dolly's feathers on the grass. But the oil did not come off. Dolly said, "I am still sad because the oil is still on my feathers." Freddy felt bad and said, "I am sorry, Dolly. I tried my best to help you." Dolly stayed sad and could not play with her friends in the pond. The oil stayed on her feathers, and she could not find a way to remove it.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big sand area where kids were playing. Tim ran to the sand and started to dig with his hands. He felt the thick sand between his fingers. A girl named Sue was playing in the sand too. She had a toy shovel and bucket. Sue saw Tim and walked over to him. She said, "Do you want to play with me?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes!" Sue offered her toy shovel to Tim, and they began to dig together. Tim and Sue made a big sand hill with the thick sand. They put toy cars on top of the hill and watched them roll down. They laughed and played all day. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Thank you for playing with me, Sue!" Sue smiled and said, "You're welcome! Let's play again soon!"

Once upon a time, there was a big grape. The grape lived in a field with many other grapes. They all lived on a long vine. The big grape was sad because it was not the same as the others. It was not round and small. It was big and not as available to pick. One day, a small bird came to the grape field. The bird saw the big grape and felt sad for it. The bird wanted to help the big grape feel better. The bird had an idea. It wanted to unite all the grapes in the field to make the big grape feel like it was a part of them. The bird went to all the grapes and told them about the big grape. The grapes listened and felt sad for the big grape too. They all wanted to help. So, the grapes united and became friends with the big grape. They played together and had fun. The big grape was happy now. It felt like it was a part of the grape family. The bird was happy too. It had helped the big grape feel better. The field was now full of happy grapes, all united and playing together.
