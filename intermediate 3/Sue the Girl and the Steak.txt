Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Steak

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toy kitchen. She had a fake steak that she pretended to cook for her friends. She would put the fake steak in her toy oven and wait for it to be ready. One day, Sue's mommy was cooking a real steak in the real oven. Sue watched her mommy cook the steak. But her mommy was not careful and the steak started to burn. Sue saw the smoke and knew that the steak was burning. Sue's mommy took the burnt steak out of the oven. She was sad that the steak was burnt. Sue wanted to make her mommy feel better, so she brought her fake steak from her toy kitchen. Sue's mommy smiled and thanked Sue for her sweet help. They both laughed and enjoyed playing with the fake steak together.

Lily and Ben were twins. They liked to play with their toys and books. But today, they had to stay at home. Mom was sick and Dad had to work. They had a babysitter, but she was boring. She only watched TV and did not talk to them. Lily and Ben were bored. They looked for something fun to do. They saw a big table in the living room. It had a cloth on it. They wondered what was under the cloth. "Let's play hide and seek," Lily said. "You hide under the table and I will find you." "OK," Ben said. He crawled under the table and lifted the cloth. He saw something shiny and round. It was a vase. It had flowers in it. It looked pretty. Lily counted to ten and then looked for Ben. She saw the cloth moving. She ran to the table and pulled the cloth. She saw Ben and the vase. She smiled. "I found you!" she said. But then, the vase wobbled and fell. It broke into pieces. The water and the flowers spilled on the floor. Lily and Ben gasped. They were scared. "Oh no!" Lily said. "We broke the vase. Mom and Dad will be angry. What will we do?" They looked at the mess. They did not know how to fix it. They started to cry. They were worried. The babysitter heard the noise. She came to the living room. She saw Lily and Ben and the broken vase. She frowned. "What happened here?" she asked. "Who broke the vase?" Lily and Ben looked at each other. They did not want to lie. They did not want to get in trouble. They did not know what to say. They were sorry.

Once upon a time, there was a weird letter. It was on the table. The letter was red, blue, and green. It was very pretty. The little girl, named Lily, saw the letter. Lily wanted to remove the letter. She asked her mom, "Can I take the letter?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can take it." So Lily took the letter from the table. Lily opened the letter. Inside, there was a big smiley face. It made Lily happy. She showed the weird letter to her mom. They both laughed and had a fun day.

One day, a friendly dog named Spot found a football. Spot loved to play with the football. He would run and jump and catch it in his mouth. Spot's best friend was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to paint pretty pictures with bright colors. One sunny day, Spot brought the football to Sue. "Sue, let's play!" said Spot. Sue smiled and said, "Okay, Spot! But first, I want to paint the football with my colors." Spot wagged his tail and let Sue paint the football with many colors. As they played, something unexpected happened. The colorful football started to glow! The bright colors made the football magical. Spot and Sue were very surprised. They played with the magical football all day, and it made them very happy.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Lolly. Lolly had a special case that she loved very much. She used to keep all her most reliable toys inside. Every day, Lolly used to polish the case with a soft cloth. She worked very hard and made sure it was super shiny. People saw her and they thought, 'That is one very reliable case!' Soon enough, Lolly's case became the talk of the town. Everybody wanted a case just like Lolly's. But Lolly was already very happy and proud of her reliable case. She knew that her hard work was paying off.
