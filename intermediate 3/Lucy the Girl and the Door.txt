Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Door

One day, a little girl named Lucy was very eager to play. She ran to her bedroom to find her favorite toy. But when she opened the door, she got a big surprise. Her room was full of balloons! Lucy was so happy and excited. She jumped and played with the balloons all day. Later, her mom came into the bedroom with a big smile. She told Lucy that the balloons were a surprise for her birthday. Lucy had forgotten it was her birthday! She hugged her mom and thanked her for the fun surprise. Then, they heard a loud noise from the living room. They went to see what it was. To their surprise, the balloons had moved from the bedroom to the living room! Lucy's dad was there, laughing. He had moved the balloons as a joke. Lucy, her mom, and her dad all laughed and played with the balloons together, having the best birthday ever.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tom. One day, Tom went outside to play. He wanted to discover new things in his big yard. The sky was blue and clear. Tom felt very happy. As Tom walked around, he saw a little bird. The bird said, "Hello, Tom! Do you want to be my friend?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend, little bird!" They both played and had fun. At the end of the day, Tom and the little bird went back to the small house. Tom's mom made them a yummy snack. They ate and laughed together. Tom was glad he discovered a new friend. And the little bird was happy too. They promised to play together every day under the clear, blue sky.

Lily and Sam were best friends. They liked to play in the park with their dolls and cars. One day, they saw a big map of the world on the wall of the library. They pointed at the different colors and shapes. "What is this?" Lily asked. "This is a country," Sam said. "A country is a big place where many people live. Like our home, but bigger." Lily nodded. She saw a pink country that looked like a flower. She liked it very much. "I want this country," she said. "It is so pretty and delicate." She reached out to touch the map, but Sam stopped her. "No, Lily, you can't do that. The map is not for us. It is for the library. If you touch it, you might break it or make it dirty. That is not nice." Lily was angry. She wanted the pink country. She pushed Sam away and scratched the map with her nails. She made a big hole in the pink country. Sam was shocked. He saw the hole and the torn paper. He felt sad and mad. "Lily, why did you do that? You ruined the map. You are a bad friend. I don't want to play with you anymore." He ran away, leaving Lily alone. Lily felt sorry. She realized she had done something wrong. She wished she could fix the map and make Sam happy again. She ran after Sam and found him sitting on a bench, crying. She sat next to him and hugged him. "Sam, I'm sorry. I was silly and mean. I didn't mean to hurt the map or you. Please forgive me. I still want to be your friend." Sam looked at Lily. He saw her sad face and her sorry eyes. He remembered how much fun they had together. He felt his anger melt away. He hugged Lily back and smiled. "It's okay, Lily. I forgive you. You are still my friend. But next time, don't touch things that are not yours. And don't scratch the map. It is not nice." Lily nodded. She learned her lesson. She and Sam went back to the library and told the librarian what they had done. The librarian was not happy, but she was kind. She gave them some tape and paper and helped them fix the map. She also showed them some books about the countries of the world. Lily and Sam thanked her and promised to be more careful. They learned a lot and had fun. They were happy to be friends again.
