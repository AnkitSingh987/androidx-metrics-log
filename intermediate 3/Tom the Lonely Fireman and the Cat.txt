Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Lonely Fireman and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a lonely fireman named Tom. He lived in a small house with his big red truck. Tom loved to help people, but he had no friends to play with after work. One sunny day, Tom saw a cat stuck in a tall tree. He wanted to help the cat. He put on his fireman hat, climbed the big red truck, and poured water on the tree. The cat jumped down and landed safely on the soft grass. Tom was so happy that he saved the cat. The cat was happy too and decided to stay with Tom. They became best friends and were never lonely again. Tom and the cat played together every day after work, and they lived happily ever after.

Anna liked to play in the garden. She liked to dig in the dirt and look for insects. She found worms, beetles, ants and spiders. She was very enthusiastic about insects. She wanted to learn more about them. One day, she saw a big brown thing in the corner of the garden. It looked like a log, but it was soft and wet. She went closer and touched it. It was squishy and smelly. She saw many insects crawling on it and inside it. She was curious and excited. "Mommy, mommy, come and see!" she shouted. "I found a rot log with lots of insects!" Mommy came and saw the rot log. She wrinkled her nose and said, "That's not a log, Anna. That's a rot pumpkin. Someone threw it away after Halloween. It's not good to touch it. It has germs and fungus and it can make you sick." Anna was surprised and disappointed. She didn't know that pumpkins could rot. She liked pumpkins. They were orange and round and had faces. She wanted to play with the insects, but she didn't want to get sick. "Can we take some insects home, mommy?" she asked. "I want to watch them and learn about them." Mommy thought for a moment and said, "Well, we can take some insects that are not harmful, like worms and beetles. But we have to be careful and gentle with them. And we have to put them in a jar with some dirt and leaves and holes for air. And we have to let them go after a few days. They belong in the garden, not in the house." Anna nodded and smiled. She agreed with mommy. She wanted to be kind to the insects. She and mommy picked some worms and beetles and put them in a jar. They took the jar home and put it on the windowsill. Anna watched the insects with enthusiasm. She learned a lot from them. She was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a small cabin in the woods. A clumsy bear lived in the cabin. He was big, brown, and very friendly. The bear liked to play with his friends, the birds, and the rabbits. They all had fun together in the woods. One day, the bear found a tiny seed on the ground. He dug a small hole and put the seed in it. He covered the seed with dirt and gave it some water. He was excited to see what would grow from the seed. Every day, the bear watered the seed and watched it grow. It grew into a small green plant. The plant grew bigger and bigger. The bear was happy to see the plant grow tall and strong. His friends, the birds and rabbits, came to look at the plant too. They all loved the big, green plant that grew from the tiny seed. And they all lived happily in the woods, with the tall plant and the small cabin.

Once upon a time there was a lion. Every day he would take a trip around the bright jungle. Everywhere he went he would roar to show how powerful he was. One day a little bird was in the jungle when the lion roared. The bird was scared and flew away. The lion chuckled and said, "Where are you going little bird?" The bird quickly stopped flying and replied, "I'm so scared! I thought you would eat me!" The lion laughed and said, "No need to be scared, I won't eat you! I just love to roar. I roar when I take my trip around the bright jungle." The bird felt relieved and said, "Thank you! I won't be scared anymore." The lion smiled and said, "That's good. I'm just having fun! Now let's both continue on our trips." And they both went off, the lion roaring and the bird singing.
