Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Modest Cat and the Car

One day, a modest cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big police car with its lights on. Tim felt scared but he knew the police were there to help. Tim went up to the police car and said, "Hi, I'm Tim. Can I help you?" The police smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. We accept your help. We need to find our lost dog, Max." Tim felt happy to help. He looked around and saw a long path. He thought, "Maybe Max is down this path." So, Tim and the police walked down the path together. They found Max and everyone was happy. The police thanked Tim for his help and Tim felt proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play in the snow. One day, she found a big box outside. The box was wrapped with a pretty bow. Sue was very excited and wanted to see what was inside. Sue opened the box and saw a small snowman. The snowman was not like the ones she made. It was original and had a big smile. Sue loved the snowman and played with it all day. They had so much fun in the snow. But then something unexpected happened. The sun came out and the snowman started to melt. Sue was very sad. But her mom told her that the snowman was not gone forever. When it snowed again, Sue could make a new friend. The moral of the story is that when we lose something, we can always try again and make something new.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Sunny and Adam, who liked to play in the park. It was always very peaceful there. One day, Sunny and Adam were playing tag when Sunny pinched Adam's arm. Adam started to cry and said, "That hurt!" Sunny felt so sorry that she had pinched Adam. She hugged him and said, "I'm sorry. I didn't mean to pinch you." Adam looked at Sunny and said, "It's ok. Forgiveness is like an open door. It helps us to find peace." Sunny nodded in agreement. She smiled at Adam and said, "Let’s play tag again! This time no pinching." Adam smiled and the two friends ran off to play in the peaceful park.

One day, a big, red mushroom was in the woods. It was a very selfish mushroom. It did not want to share its space with anyone. A little bug came by and asked, "Can I lean on you, mushroom?" The mushroom said, "No, you cannot lean on me. Go away!" The little bug was sad and left. Later, a big wind came and blew all the leaves and plants around. The mushroom was scared. It wished it had friends to help it. But it was too selfish, and no one wanted to be its friend. Then, the little bug came back. It saw the scared mushroom and said, "I will help you, even if you were mean to me." The little bug called its friends, and they all leaned on the mushroom. The wind did not blow the mushroom away. The mushroom was happy and said, "Thank you, little bug. I will not be selfish anymore." From that day on, the mushroom and the little bug were best friends, and they shared their space in the woods with everyone.

One day, a bird named Tim loved to sing. He sang all day long. Tim was very happy. He had a friend named Sam. Sam was a squirrel. Sam had a package. The package was big and wrapped in pretty paper. Sam said, "I found this package, but I can't open it." Tim tried to help. They pulled and pulled, but the package did not open. They felt sad. Tim had an idea. "Let's sing a song to make us feel better," he said. So they sang a happy song. As they sang, the package started to open by itself. Inside, they found a lot of spoiled food. It smelled bad! Tim and Sam learned that not all things that look nice are good. They felt happy because they still had each other and their song. The moral of the story is, don't judge a package by its cover.
