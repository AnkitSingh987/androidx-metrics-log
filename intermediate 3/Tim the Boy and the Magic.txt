Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Magic

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He looked up at the sky and saw a big bird. The bird was very pretty and had many colors. Tim wanted to talk to the bird, so he called out, "Hello, bird! Can you talk to me?" The bird looked at Tim and answered, "Yes, I can talk. I am a fake bird, and I can do many fun things. Do you want to play with me?" Tim was very surprised, but he was also happy. He had never met a fake bird that could talk before. They played all day, and Tim learned that the fake bird was magic. The bird could change its size and shape, and it could even make things appear and disappear. At the end of the day, Tim said goodbye to his new friend. He knew that he would never forget the special day when he met the magic fake bird in the sky.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big tree in the park. The tree has many green leaves and brown branches. It also has a hairy thing on the trunk. "What is that?" Tom asks, pointing at the hairy thing. "I don't know," Sue says. "Maybe it is a bug." They go closer to the tree. They want to see the hairy thing better. But when they get near, the hairy thing moves. It has two eyes, a nose, and a mouth. It is not a bug. It is a squirrel! "Hello, little squirrel," Tom says. "Do you live in the tree?" The squirrel looks at Tom and Sue. It makes a funny noise. It sounds like "chirp, chirp, chirp". Then it runs up the tree and hides in a hole. "Bye, little squirrel," Sue says. "Maybe we can play with you another day." Tom and Sue smile. They think the squirrel is cute and funny. They go to play on the swings. They are happy. They like the park and the tree and the hairy squirrel.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big saw on a bench. It was shiny and sharp and had many teeth. "Can we borrow the saw?" Lily asked Tom. "It looks fun to cut things with it." "I don't know," Tom said. "It might be dangerous. And it belongs to someone else. Maybe they need it." "Please, Tom, just for a little while," Lily begged. "We can cut some sticks and make a house. And we can put it back when we are done. No one will know." Tom was not sure, but he wanted to make Lily happy. So he said, "Okay, but be careful. And don't cut yourself or anything else." They took the saw and ran to a distant corner of the park, where there were many sticks and leaves. They started to cut the sticks and make a house. They were having fun, until they heard a loud voice. "Hey, you kids! What are you doing with my saw?" It was the owner of the saw, a big man with a red face and a hat. He was angry and he came running towards them. Lily and Tom were scared. They dropped the saw and tried to run away, but the man was faster. He grabbed them by their arms and shook them. "How dare you borrow my saw without asking?" he yelled. "Do you know how dangerous this is? You could have hurt yourself or someone else. And you ruined my work. I was using this saw to fix the bench. Now I have to start over. You are very naughty and you will pay for this." He dragged them to the bench and showed them the damage they had done. They had cut the wood and made holes and scratches. The bench looked bad and broken. Lily and Tom felt sorry and ashamed. They wished they had never borrowed the saw. They wished they had listened to Tom. They wished they could go back and undo what they had done. But it was too late. The man called their parents and told them what happened. Their parents were angry and disappointed. They scolded them and took them home. They made them apologize to the man and pay for the repair. They also grounded them and took away their toys. Lily and Tom learned a hard lesson. They learned that borrowing without asking is wrong and that playing with dangerous things is foolish. They learned that their actions have consequences and that they have to be responsible. They learned that sometimes, fun can turn into trouble.
