Title: Lizzie and the Car

Once there was a little girl called Lizzie. Lizzie went for a drive with her mum and dad in their reliable car. She loved exploring and when the car came to a stop, Lizzie couldn't wait to get out. She ran over to a tire in the grass and touched it. “What does this feel like, mum?” Lizzie asked. “It's a tire,” her mum replied. “And it feels bumpy.” Lizzie pressed her fingers harder into the tire and then tasted it. “Yuck!” she cried. “It tastes strange.” Her mum laughed and said, “That's because it's a tire, sweetie. It's not meant to be eaten!” Lizzie laughed too and ran back to the car. She had an exciting morning exploring and she knew she could rely on her mum and dad.

Lily was a chubby girl who liked to play with fire. She had a box of matches that she hid under her bed. Sometimes, when her mom and dad were not looking, she would light a match and watch it burn. She liked the bright flame and the warm feeling. One day, she found a cloth in her closet. It was a pretty cloth with flowers and birds on it. Lily thought it would look nice on her wall. She took the cloth and a match and went to her room. She pinned the cloth on the wall and lit the match. She held the match close to the cloth and smiled. But then, something bad happened. The cloth caught fire and started to burn. The fire grew bigger and bigger and made a lot of smoke. Lily was scared and dropped the match. She ran out of her room and screamed for help. Her mom and dad heard her scream and ran to her room. They saw the fire and the smoke and grabbed a bucket of water. They threw the water on the fire and put it out. They were angry and worried. They looked at Lily and said: "Lily, what did you do? You could have burned the whole house! You could have hurt yourself and us! Playing with fire is very dangerous and wrong. You should never do it again. Do you understand?" Lily nodded and started to cry. She was sorry and ashamed. She hugged her mom and dad and said: "I'm sorry, mom and dad. I was stupid and curious. I won't play with fire again. I love you." Her mom and dad hugged her back and said: "We love you too, Lily. But you have to be careful and listen to us. Fire is not a toy. It can burn and hurt. You have to respect it and stay away from it. Do you understand?" Lily nodded and said: "I understand, mom and dad. I learned my lesson. Fire is not a toy." The moral of the story is: Playing with fire is dangerous and wrong. You should respect fire and stay away from it.

Once upon a time there was a beautiful rainbow up in the sky. All of the colors were so pretty and fun to look at. One day, a big, white cloud moved over the rainbow, and the colors started to disappear. The white cloud had destroyed the rainbow. The little boy who had been looking at the rainbow was so sad, but he decided to make a new rainbow of his own. He grabbed a bunch of colors and spread them around on the ground. He made a big, colorful circle with white in the middle. It was a beautiful rainbow. Then, it started to rain. The raindrops fell all over his rainbow, and the colors washed away. But, the white stayed the same. So, the little boy smiled and ran around in the rain. He still had the white part of his rainbow, and it made him really happy.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to bake some toast. She went to the kitchen and put the bread in the oven. Lily was very excited to eat her toast. While the toast was baking, Lily saw a cat outside. The cat was very interesting. It had a big hat and a funny tail. Lily went outside to play with the cat. They had a lot of fun together. When Lily came back inside, she smelled something strange. She had forgotten about her toast! The toast was burnt and black. But the cat had an idea. It took off its big hat, and there were fresh, yummy toasts inside. Lily and the cat shared the toasts and became best friends.
