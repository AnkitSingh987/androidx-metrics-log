Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Tom and the Hat

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play in the snow. They make a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They give him a carrot nose and two coal eyes. "Look, our snowman is so big and nice!" Sara says. "Yes, he is. But he needs a friend. Let's make another one!" Tom says. They roll more snow and make a smaller snowman. They lift him up and put him next to the big one. They give him a hat and a scarf too. But they have no more carrots or coal. "What can we use for his nose and eyes?" Sara asks. "Let's look around. Maybe we can find something." Tom says. They run and look for things in the snow. They find a pine cone, a twig and some berries. They bring them back to the snowman. "Here, we can use these. The pine cone can be his nose, the twig can be his mouth and the berries can be his eyes." Tom says. They stick them on the snowman's face. He looks funny and cute. "Wow, he is so cool! He has a different nose and eyes than the big one. He is special." Sara says. "Yes, he is. And they are both our friends. Let's give them names. What do you think?" Tom says. Sara thinks for a time. She looks at the snowmen and smiles. "I know. The big one can be Icy, because he is icy and white. And the small one can be Piney, because he has a pine cone nose." Sara says. Tom likes the names. He nods and claps. "Good names! Icy and Piney. They are perfect. Let's say hello to them." Tom says. They go to the snowmen and hug them. They say hello and tell them their names. They play with them and have fun. They are happy.

Once upon a time there was a happy girl named Lucy. She loved to explore and today she decided to go on a special adventure. Lucy stepped outside and saw a big, empty field. She stepped over the grass and along the path, searching for something exciting. Suddenly, Lucy stepped into a spooky cave. She was a little scared but she took a deep breath and stepped inside. It was surprisingly light inside the cave and Lucy could see lots of bones all over the ground. She smiled and realized she had stepped into the home of lots of skeletons! Lucy carefully stepped between the bones, exploring the cave and feeling happy. She couldn't believe her luck - discovering a skeleton cave! She started to twirl and jump and soon she was completely surrounded by happy skeletons. They all jumped and waved their little bones to the tune of Lucy's song. The day flew by and soon it was time for Lucy to leave. She waved goodbye to her happy skeleton friends and stepped out of the cave, ready for her next big adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to look for things in the park. One day, he found a big stone. The stone was very special. Tim took the stone home. He was hungry, so he asked the stone for food. The stone gave him a big apple. Tim was happy and ate the apple. But he wanted more food from the stone. Tim was very hungry now. He asked the stone for more food. The stone gave him a lot of food. Tim ate and ate, but he was still hungry. He kept asking the stone for more food. In the end, Tim ate too much. He felt sick and his tummy hurt. The stone could not help him anymore. Tim was sad and wished he had not been so hungry.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk in the cold outside. He put on his big coat, hat, and gloves. Tim saw a snowman with a big smile. The snowman said, "Hi, I am Sam the Snowman!" Tim said, "Hi Sam! You are very cold. Can you turn around?" Sam the Snowman turned around and around. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. Sam the Snowman was happy to make Tim laugh. Tim thought with his brain, "Sam the Snowman is cold, so I will give him a big warm hug." Tim hugged Sam the Snowman tight. Sam said, "Thank you, Tim! I feel warm and happy now." They played in the cold all day and became best friends.
