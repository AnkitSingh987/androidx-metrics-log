Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rabbit and the Door

Once upon a time, there lived a rabbit. He was small and shy, always feeling a bit fearful. One day, while he was hopping around in the meadow, he found something strange. A tiny door had appeared from nowhere. He didn't know what it was and what it could do. He hopped up to the door and tried to open it. But it was too small, even for a rabbit. He peered inside, but all he could see was darkness. He was about to hop away when he heard a voice from inside. "Come inside! I'm sure you won't be sorry!" The rabbit was so scared, but he was also curious. He stepped into the darkness and, suddenly, he disappeared! He had gone on a magical adventure that no one else could ever know about. But when he returned, he found he was still feeling a bit fearful. That little door had taken him somewhere he would never forget.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a red plastic ball. She loved to play with it. She would throw it, kick it, and bounce it all day long. Her friend Tom came over to play with her. They were having so much fun with the ball. Sue wanted to encourage Tom to try a new game. She said, "Let's play catch!" Tom was excited to try. They stood far away from each other and started to throw the ball. But, when Sue threw the ball really hard, something unexpected happened. The red plastic ball hit a tree and broke into two pieces! Sue and Tom were very surprised. They thought the ball was strong, but it was not. They were sad, but then they had an idea. They put the two pieces together and made a new toy. Now, they could play a new game with their red plastic toy. They were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful bird. The bird had many colors on it. The bird liked to fly high in the sky. One day, the bird wanted to go to space. The bird asked a friend for help. The friend had a big balloon. The friend said, "I will attach the balloon to you. Then you can fly to space." The bird was very happy. The bird and the friend attached the balloon. The bird went up, up, up into the sky. The bird saw the beautiful space. The bird was very happy. The bird came back down and told all the friends about the beautiful space.

Ben and Lily like to play in the park. They run and jump and laugh. They see a big tree with green leaves. They want to climb the tree. They hold the tree and pull up. The tree is rough and sharp. It hurts their hands. They say ouch and let go. They look at their hands. They have red marks and scratches. They run to the fountain. They put their hands in the water. The water is cool and nice. It makes their hands feel better. They smile and splash each other. They forget about the tree. They see a duck in the pond. They want to feed the duck. They find some bread on a bench. They break the bread into pieces. They throw the pieces to the duck. The duck is happy and quacks. They are happy too. They have fun in the park. They do not climb the tree again. They like the water and the duck. They say bye to the duck and go home. They tell their mom about their day. She hugs them and gives them a snack. They are tired and happy.

One day, a large dog named Max was playing in the park. He saw a shiny trophy near a tree. Max wanted to win the trophy, so he ran to the tree. Max met a small cat named Lily under the tree. Lily said, "I want to win the trophy too!" Max and Lily decided to have a race. The winner would get the trophy. They both ran as fast as they could. Max won the race and got the trophy. Lily was sad, but Max said, "Don't be sad, Lily. We can share the trophy and relax together." Lily smiled and agreed. They both sat under the tree, relaxed, and looked at their shiny trophy. Max and Lily were happy to be friends and share their prize.
