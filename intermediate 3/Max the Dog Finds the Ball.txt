Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog Finds the Ball

One day, a dog named Max went for a jog in the park. He saw a big rubber ball on the ground. Max was very happy and wanted to play with the ball. Max ran to the rubber ball and kicked it very hard. The ball flew into the air and hit a small girl. The girl started to cry. Max felt ashamed because he didn't mean to hurt her. Max went to the girl and licked her face to say sorry. The girl stopped crying and smiled at Max. She threw the rubber ball for Max to catch. They played together and became good friends.

The animal was very unhappy. It was the most miserable animal in the whole wide world. Every day it felt sad and alone. The animal didn't know why it was feeling so bad. One day, the animal decided it would try something new. It would use its legs and walk away. The animal walked and walked. It kept walking until it saw something different. It was a beautiful meadow, with the sun shining and birds singing. The animal felt so happy, like it had never felt before. It ran and rolled in the grass. Then, it heard a voice. It was someone calling its name. When the animal looked up, it saw a very kind person. It was the same person who had helped the animal use its legs to walk away from the miserable place it came from. The animal felt so loved and grateful.

Rosie was so proud of her first toast. She made it all by herself in the kitchen. She took the bread out of the box and meticulously cut it into the perfect toast shape. She carefully placed the toast in the toaster and eagerly waited for the toaster to finish its work. As she heard the crisp sound of the toaster, she knew that her piece of toast was ready. Rosie beamed with pride as take out her piece of toast. Suddenly, she heard someone peeking behind her back. She turned around and saw her dad. "That's looks delicious, Rosie!" said her dad. "You made it all by yourself, didn't you?" Rosie nodded her head, feeling so proud that she was able to make a piece of toast all by herself. Her dad hugged her and ate the toast with her. What a wonderful day it was!

Once upon a time, there was a pilot named Tim. Tim loved to fly his big plane up in the sky. One sunny day, he saw a small house with a big table outside. He decided to land his plane near the house. When he got to the house, he saw a lady named Sue. Sue was busy making food. Tim asked, "What are you doing?" Sue said, "I am making delicious food to serve to my friends." Tim wanted to help Sue serve the delicious food. So, they put the food on the big table outside. Soon, Sue's friends came, and they all ate together. They laughed and had fun. Tim was happy he could help Sue, and they became good friends.

Once there was a baby bunny. He was very alert and loved exploring the world. One day, he decided to crawl out of his cozy bunny hole. He hopped across the grass and saw a tasty olive. He smelled it and couldn't resist! He crawled across the grass to get the olive and was so excited to enjoy it! But then he noticed there was a bird nearby, watching him. The bunny was alert and knew he had to be careful. He snuck closer to the olive and very quickly crawled away with it. He was so happy to have it that he ran back to his bunny hole and ate the tasty olive until it was all gone. The bunny felt so satisfied!

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and eat snacks. One day, they see a big plate of cookies on the table. The cookies have chocolate and nuts and sprinkles. They look yummy. "Wow, look at the cookies!" Sara says. "Can we have some, Mom?" Mom is in the kitchen. She hears Sara and Ben. She smiles and says, "Yes, you can have one cookie each. But first, you have to wash your hands and say please." Sara and Ben run to the sink. They wash their hands with soap and water. They dry them with a towel. Then they go back to the table. They say, "Please, Mom, can we have a cookie?" Mom nods and says, "Yes, you can. But be careful, they are still hot. And don't make a mess." Sara and Ben say, "Thank you, Mom." They look at the plate of cookies. They want to choose the best one. They point at different cookies and say, "I want this one. No, I want that one. No, this one is better." They see a big cookie in the middle of the plate. It has the most chocolate and nuts and sprinkles. It is the most popular cookie. They both point at it and say, "I want this one!" They look at each other. They both want the same cookie. They start to argue. They say, "It's mine. No, it's mine. Give it to me. No, you give it to me." Mom hears them. She is not happy. She says, "Stop it, Sara and Ben. You have to share. You can't have the same cookie. You have to pick another one. Or you can split it in half." Sara and Ben think about it. They don't want to split the cookie. They want the whole cookie. But they don't want to make Mom angry. They don't want to lose their cookie. They decide to share. They say, "OK, Mom. We will share. We will split it in half." Mom is happy. She says, "Good. That's very nice of you. Here, I will help you cut it. Then you can each have a piece." Mom cuts the cookie in half. She gives one piece to Sara and one piece to Ben. They say, "Thank you, Mom." They take a bite of their cookie. It is delicious. They smile and say, "Mmm, this is the best cookie ever." Mom smiles and says, "You're welcome, Sara and Ben. I'm glad you like it. And I'm proud of you for sharing. That's what good twins do."
