Category: Intermediate
Title: The Famous Cup and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a famous cup. Everywhere the cup went, it was admired and praised by all. The cup was so proud of its fame that it decided it should hang high up so that all could admire it. It decided to hang itself on the highest branch of the tallest tree in the forest. But, when the cup was a few steps away from the tree, it noticed a small mouse. The cup asked the mouse, “What are you doing here?” The mouse replied, “I am trying to reach the acorns at the top of the tree. But, I can’t reach them. Can you help me?” The cup looked at the mouse and thought for a while. Then, it said, “Follow me!” The cup hung itself on the lowest branch of the tree, and the mouse happily reached the acorns. The moral of the story is: When you are famous, use your fame to help others.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little dog named Max. Max loved to play all day long. He would play with his toys, run around, and jump in the grass. One day, Max was very tired after playing all day. He needed to rest. Max's friend, a little boy named Sam, came to visit him. Sam said, "Max, you look tired. Let's rest and stay inside the house for a while." Max agreed and they both went inside to rest. They sat on the soft rug and soon, Max felt much better. After they rested, Max and Sam decided to play a game. They wanted to see who could jump the highest. They took turns jumping, and Max was very successful. He jumped higher than Sam. They laughed and played until it was time for Sam to go home. Max was happy that he had a fun day with his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree was in a park. The park had many different trees. One day, a little bird came to the big tree. The little bird had a shiny ornament in its beak. The little bird said to the big tree, "I want to spread my wings and fly. Can you keep this ornament safe for me?" The big tree said, "Yes, I can keep it safe. Put it on one of my branches." The little bird put the ornament on a branch. It looked very pretty. The little bird spread its wings and flew away. The big tree and the ornament were very happy. They waited for the little bird to come back.

Sara loved to bake with her mom. They would mix flour, sugar, eggs and butter in a big bowl. Then they would roll out the dough and cut out shapes with cookie cutters. Sara liked to make stars, hearts and flowers. One day, Sara saw a new cookie cutter on the shelf. It was shaped like a statue of a lady with a long dress and a crown. Sara asked her mom if she could use it. Her mom said yes, but she had to be very careful. The statue cookie was delicate and could break easily. Sara was very excited. She rolled out some dough and pressed the cookie cutter on it. She lifted the statue cookie and put it on a baking tray. She made two more statue cookies and decorated them with sprinkles. Then her mom put the tray in the oven and set the timer. Sara waited for the cookies to bake. She smelled the sweet aroma and heard the timer beep. Her mom took out the tray and let it cool. Sara ran to see her statue cookies. They looked beautiful and yummy. Sara picked up one of the statue cookies and held it in her hand. She wanted to show it to her dad. But as she walked to the living room, she tripped on a toy and fell. The statue cookie broke into pieces. Sara felt sad and cried. Her mom heard her cry and came to hug her. She said it was okay. She still had two more statue cookies and they could make more later. She also said that the broken cookie still tasted good and gave her a piece. Sara smiled and ate the cookie. It was delicious. She felt better and thanked her mom. She decided to be more careful with her delicate statue cookies.
