Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy phone that she loved to play with all day. One day, Lily was playing with her toy phone in the park. She was very happy. Then, a big wind came and made her toy phone fall from her hands. Lily was frightened. She looked around and saw her toy phone on the ground. She said, "Oh no, my phone!" A kind boy named Tom saw what happened. He picked up the toy phone and gave it back to Lily. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom!" They became friends and played with the toy phone together in the park. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a man named Tim. Tim was a dependable man. He worked in an office. Every day, he would go to his office and work hard. Tim had many friends at the office. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sue, frown. Tim asked Sue, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I lost my toy." Tim wanted to help Sue find her toy. They looked all around the office. Finally, they found Sue's toy under a chair. Sue was happy again. She said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sue!" From that day on, Tim and Sue were even better friends. And they always helped each other in the office.

Once upon a time, there was a big, cold room. In this room, there was a circle on the floor. Sam, a little boy, liked to play in this room. One day, Sam saw the circle and decided to kneel down and touch it. As Sam touched the circle, something unexpected happened. The circle began to glow and talk! The circle said, "Hi, Sam! I'm a magic circle! I can make your toys come to life!" Sam was very happy and excited. Sam brought his toy car and put it in the circle. The car started to move and talk! The car and Sam played all day. Then, the car said, "Thank you, Sam, for making me alive!" Sam and the magic circle became best friends, and they had many fun days together.

Once upon a time, in a big, green park, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her red ball. One sunny day, she was playing with her ball when it rolled away. Lily ran after the ball and found a small, blue bird. The bird said, "Hi, I am Blue. Can you help me find my way home? I can't remember where I live." Lily looked at the bird with a little worry. She wanted to help, but she didn't know how. Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's look for a big tree. Your home might be there." They walked and walked until they saw a big tree. The sky was clear and blue. Blue looked up and saw his nest. He was so happy! "Thank you, Lily," said Blue. "You helped me find my home." Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome, Blue. I am happy to help." They became good friends and played together in the big, green park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little puppy named Pup. Pup loved to walk with his best friend, a boy named Tim. They walked every day and had fun playing together. One day, Pup and Tim found a big, old tree. This tree was very ancient. They had never seen a tree like this before. As they walked closer, they found a big hole in the tree. Suddenly, out of the hole, came a tiny, talking bird! The bird said, "Hello, Pup and Tim! I am a magic bird. I can make one of your wishes come true." Tim and Pup were very surprised. They had never met a talking bird before! Tim wished for a big bag of treats for Pup. The magic bird made the wish come true. Pup and Tim thanked the bird and walked back home. They shared the treats and played happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jake. Jake was a lively three year old. One morning Jake was playing in the garden when he suddenly felt a bit dizzy. He decided to go lie down in the bathroom and have a rest. He lay down of the bathroom rug and listened to the water run from the tap. Soon he drifted off to sleep. When he awoke, he was feeling much better. The sun was shining brightly and he decided to go outside and play again. As soon as he stepped out the door, he heard a lot of noise coming from the garden. He saw his friends playing and quickly ran out to join them. Jake wanted to play too, but he had one problem - he had forgotten to put on his shoes! He decided to jump in the air anyway and as he jumped up, he felt an odd sensation. His feet had left the ground, but he felt as though he were still stuck there. He tried to move but he couldn't. He had sprung himself into the air and he couldn't come back down. Jake cried out for help but no one heard him. He was stuck up there in the air and he started to feel dizzy. Eventually he fell to the ground, but unfortunately he was not able to get back up. He was too dizzy and the fall had been too hard. The other children ran for help, but it was too late for Jake
