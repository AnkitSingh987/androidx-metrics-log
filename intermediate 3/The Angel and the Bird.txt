Category: Intermediate
Title: The Angel and the Bird

Once there was a small angel. She was lovely, with sparkly wings and a sweet smile. Every day the angel would fly all around the world, seeing different sights and doing nice things for people. One day she flew to a printing shop. The shop was full of machines, whirring and whizzing as they made colorful pictures. The angel was so delighted, she decided to make her own print. She found a picture of birds flying and had the machine print it. But when the machine was done, she realized it had made a mistake. One bird was missing. The angel was very sorry. The bird had been so beautiful and now it was gone. She wanted to make it right. So she flapped her wings and made her magic. The next day, when the angel went back to the shop, there was an extra bird printed on the page. The angel spread her wings and flew away, happy that she was able to help. She was a kind, special angel, and by being sorry she was able to make something lovely.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run very fast. One day, he found a trail near his house. He asked his mom, "Can I run on the trail?" His mom said, "I permit you to run on the trail, but be careful." Tim was so happy. He put on his shoes and went to the trail. He ran fast like a rabbit. He saw pretty flowers and tall trees. He even saw a butterfly. Tim felt like he was flying in the wind. After running for a while, Tim got tired. He sat down on the grass to rest. He looked around and saw that he was lost. He started to cry. But then, he heard his mom calling his name. She found him and gave him a big hug. They walked home together, hand in hand, and Tim learned to always stay close to his mom when running on the trail.

Once there was a boy named Tom. He loved to play with his toy letters. One day, he found a big box full of letters. He was very happy. Tom took the box to his friend Sue. He said, "Look, Sue! I have a great box of letters. Let's play with them!" Sue was happy too. They started to play with the letters on the floor. Tom and Sue made words with the letters. They made the word "cat" and the word "dog". They had a lot of fun. They played with the letters all day. At the end of the day, they put the letters back in the box and went home.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sally lived in a tidy house. She loved to play with her toys and keep her room clean. One day, Sally found a bar of soap in the bathroom. She didn't know what it was, so she took it to her mom. "Mom, what is this?" Sally asked. Her mom smiled and said, "That is soap, Sally. We use it to wash our hands and stay clean." Sally liked the idea of staying clean, so she decided to wash her hands with the soap. Later, Sally's friend Timmy came over to play. They had fun playing with their toys, but Timmy started to cry because he missed his mom. Sally wanted to comfort Timmy, so she took him to the bathroom and showed him the soap. "Look, Timmy," Sally said, "This is soap. We can wash our hands and feel better." Timmy washed his hands with the soap, and he started to feel better. He smiled at Sally and said, "Thank you, Sally. The soap made me feel better." They went back to playing with their toys, and they had a great day together. Sally was happy that she could help her friend feel better by sharing her soap.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a pink cow named Daisy. Daisy was very kind and loved to help her friends. One sunny day, Daisy saw a little bird with a hurt wing. "Can you help me, please?" asked the little bird. Daisy nodded her head and said, "Yes, I will help you." Daisy used her big, soft nose to lift the little bird onto her back. They went to find the bird's mom. When they found her, the mom bird said, "Thank you, Daisy, for helping my baby." Daisy and the little bird became good friends. They played in the big green field every day. The little bird's wing got better, and they had lots of fun together. And from that day on, everyone knew that Daisy was the kindest, most helpful pink cow in the whole field.
