Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Toys

Anna and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They liked to build towers and castles with their blocks and pretend they were kings and queens. They had a lot of fun together. But outside, the sky was dark and the wind was loud. Their mom came in and said, "Kids, we have to go to the basement. There is a big storm coming. It is called a hurricane. It can tear down houses and trees. It is very scary." Anna and Ben felt afraid. They did not want to leave their toys. They did not want to go to the basement. They did not know what a hurricane was. They asked, "Mom, can we take our toys with us?" Their mom said, "No, we have to hurry. We can only take what we need. Grab your coats and your flashlights. We will be safe in the basement. Don't worry, your toys will be here when we come back." Anna and Ben did what their mom said. They put on their coats and took their flashlights. They followed their mom to the basement. They heard the wind howl and the rain pound. They hugged each other and hoped the hurricane would go away soon. The next day, the sun came out and the storm was over. Their mom said, "Let's go upstairs and see if everything is okay." Anna and Ben were curious and excited. They wanted to see their toys again. They went to the living room and gasped. Their toys were everywhere. The blocks were broken and scattered. The hurricane had torn their towers and castles apart. Anna and Ben felt sad and angry. They said, "Mom, look what the hurricane did to our toys! It ruined them!" Their mom said, "I'm sorry, kids. I know you loved your toys. But we can get new ones. The most important thing is that we are safe and together. The hurricane could not tear us apart. We are still a family. And that is not scary. That is wonderful."

Once upon a time, there was a little duck. He had a red cap on his head to keep him warm. One day, he was walking near the lake when he heard a harsh voice. He looked up and saw a mean bird, who was mad at him. The mean bird said, "Go away! You are not welcome here!" The little duck was very scared and ran away. As he went, he heard the mean bird call after him, "And don't come back!" The little duck was so sad and scared. He wanted to find a safe place to hide. He saw a small log next to the lake, so he hopped inside. The little duck curled up inside the log, and shut his eyes so tight. He kept his cap on so he could stay warm. He stayed inside the log all day, until the sun began to set. Once the sun went down, the mean bird was gone. The little duck was very happy and he came out of the log. As he was walking away, he saw the mean bird one last time. But this time the bird wasn't being so harsh. Instead, he said, "I'm sorry. Please come back again!" And so the little duck went back to the lake, never to be afraid of the mean bird ever again.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day. One day, Spot was very hungry. He went to his owner, Tim, and said, "I am hungry, can I have a treat?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "I think I have a treat for you in the refrigerator. Let's go look!" They went to the kitchen and opened the refrigerator. Inside, they found a big, yummy bone for Spot. Spot was so happy to see the bone. He knew that if he was a good dog, he would get a treat. Spot took the bone and went to his favorite spot to settle down and enjoy it. He chewed and chewed until he was full and happy. Spot learned that being a good dog always has a tasty reward.
