Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bear and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bob. Bob was a tough bear who lived in the woods. He liked to lay on the soft grass and look at the sky. One day, Bob found a dessert on the ground. It was a big, red, and sweet apple. Bob was very happy. He lay down on the grass and ate the dessert. After eating the dessert, Bob felt sleepy. He lay on the grass for a long time. When he woke up, the sky was dark. Bob went back to his home in the woods, feeling happy and full.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful town, there was a small stable. In the stable, there lived a horse named Tom and a cow named Sue. They were very good friends and they loved to play in the grass. One sunny day, while Tom and Sue were playing, they heard a cry. They looked around and saw a little bird on the ground. The bird was hurt and could not fly. Tom and Sue wanted to help the bird, so they took it to their stable. In the stable, Tom and Sue took care of the little bird. They gave it food and water. They were very kind to the bird. One day, the bird started to cry, but it was not a sad cry. The bird started to change! It grew big and strong, and turned into a beautiful unicorn! The unicorn thanked Tom and Sue for their help and kindness. They all became very good friends and played together every day in the peaceful town.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to create things. He would make new things every day. One day, he found a long strip of paper. It was a special strip because it was ancient. It was very old. Tim wanted to create something fun with the ancient strip. He thought and thought. Then, he decided to make a paper hat. Tim was very happy with his idea. He folded the strip this way and that way. Soon, the paper hat was ready. Tim wore the paper hat all day long. He showed it to his friends and they all loved it. The ancient strip had become a fun hat for Tim. And so, Tim learned that he could create something special with anything, even with an old strip of paper.

Sam and Tom were brothers who liked to play with snow. They made a big snowman in their yard, with a hat, a scarf, and a carrot nose. They also gave him a razor for a mouth, because they thought it looked funny. "Look at our snowman, he is so cool!" Sam said. "Yes, he is. But we have to be careful, the razor is sharp. Don't touch it, or you will get hurt," Tom said. They went inside to have some hot cocoa, and left the snowman outside. They did not know that the sun was coming out, and it was getting warmer. The next day, they went outside to play with the snowman again. But they saw something that made them sad. The snowman was gone! All that was left was a pile of snow, a hat, a scarf, and a carrot. And the razor, which was still shiny and sharp. "What happened to our snowman?" Sam asked. "He melted, because it was too hot. The sun made him go away," Tom said. They felt bad for their snowman, and wished they could make him again. But then they saw something that made them happy. The snow had melted into a big puddle, and they could splash and play in it. "Look, the snowman gave us a new game! We can have fun in the water!" Sam said. "Yes, we can. But we have to be careful, the razor is still there. Don't step on it, or you will get hurt," Tom said. They moved the razor away from the puddle, and put it in the trash. Then they had fun in the water, and forgot about their snowman. They did not know that the next day, it would snow again.
