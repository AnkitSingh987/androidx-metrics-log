Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He loved to take pictures with his camera. One day, he went to the park with his mom and his dog, Max. Max was a deaf dog, but he was very good at understanding hand signs. At the park, Tom saw a big tree. He wanted to take a picture of Max under the tree. Tom asked Max to sit and stay. Max did as he was told. Tom was happy and ready to take the picture. Just as Tom was about to take the picture, a squirrel ran by. Max saw the squirrel and wanted to play. Max jumped up and ran after the squirrel. Tom was surprised and he dropped his camera. The camera broke, but Tom was not sad. He laughed as he watched Max play with the squirrel. In the end, Tom, Max, and the squirrel became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim was very happy and loved to play outside. One day, Tim found a small, round thing on the ground. He liked it, so he put it in his pocket. Tim's mom told him not to put things in his pocket that he found on the ground. But Tim did not listen. He played all day with the small, round thing in his pocket. He felt it was a special thing. Later, Tim saw his mom was sad. She lost her ring. Tim felt bad and looked in his pocket. He found the small, round thing was his mom's ring. He gave it back to his mom. Tim said sorry and felt regret. His mom was happy to have her ring back. Tim learned to listen to his mom and not put things in his pocket.

One gloomy day, little Jane was walking in the park. She saw a prune on the ground and decided to pick it up. When Jane was about to put the prune in her pocket, a big voice said "That prune is mine! Give it back!" Jane looked up and saw a big man wearing a funny hat. He smiled at her and asked her to accept his prune. Jane was scared, but then she realized the man was being nice. She smiled back at him and said "okay!" Jane accepted the prune and thanked the man before running off to enjoy her snack. The big man watched Jane run away with a big smile on his face. He was happy that the little girl had accepted his prune.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty fish named Fin. Fin had a big friend named Shark. They lived in the sea and liked to play together. One day, while they were playing, they saw a twisty rope in the water. Fin and Shark wanted to play with the twisty rope. They both swam fast to get it. But, they both reached the twisty rope at the same time. They did not know who should get the twisty rope. Fin and Shark started to feel sad. Then, Fin had an idea. He said, "Let's share the twisty rope and play together!" Shark liked the idea. They both held the twisty rope and played a fun game. Fin and Shark were happy friends again. They learned that sharing is good and makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big gray elephant named Ellie. Ellie lived in a green forest with many trees. She had a long nose that she used to spread water on her back to keep cool. One day, Ellie saw smoke in the forest. The smoke made it difficult for her to see. She was scared and wanted to find her friend, the little red bird named Ruby. Ellie called out, "Ruby, where are you?" Ruby heard Ellie and flew to her. "I am here, Ellie," she said. "Let's go away from the smoke." They walked away from the smoke until they found a safe place near a big lake. They played in the water and had a fun day together. From that day on, Ellie and Ruby always stayed close to each other and took care of their beautiful forest home.
