Category: Intermediate
Title: The Park and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a big park. In the park, there was a deep pond. One sunny day, a boy named Tim and his mom went to the park. They took a soft blanket to sit on. They put the blanket under a tree, near the pond. Tim saw a duck in the pond. He said, "Mom, look! A duck!" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Be kind to the duck." Tim wanted to play with the duck. He went close to the pond, but his mom said, "Tim, be careful. The pond is deep." Tim listened to his mom. They had a fun day at the park. Tim learned that it is good to be kind and to listen to his mom. The duck was happy too. The end.

Once upon a time, there was an island with a big lake. On the lake lived a little fish named Larry. Larry was an obedient fish and he always followed the rules of the lake. One day, Larry was swimming around the lake when he spotted a big net. He peeked out from the water and saw a girl standing by the lake. The girl was throwing the net into the lake and trying to catch some fish. Larry dived back under the water to hide. But, he soon heard the girl calling out. "Where are you, Larry?" asked the girl. Larry thought the girl was talking to him. He came out of the water and swam to the girl. "I caught you!" said the girl. The girl put Larry in a bowl and brought him home with her. At home, the girl was very happy with Larry. She fed him and took care of him. Larry was happy too, because he was an obedient fish and he knew he was safe with the girl. From then on, Larry was an obedient fish and the girl and Larry were very happy on the island.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle sun who loved to laugh. Every day, the sun would shine and make the weather warm and nice for all the animals to play outside. The sun's laugh made everyone happy, and they all loved the gentle weather. One day, a big cloud came and covered the sun. The weather got cold and the animals couldn't play outside anymore. They missed the sun's gentle laugh and the warm weather. They all wished for the sun to come back and make the weather nice again. The sun heard the animals' wishes and decided to push the big cloud away. With a big laugh, the sun broke through the cloud and the gentle weather returned. The animals were so happy to feel the sun's warmth and hear its laugh again. They all played outside and enjoyed the nice weather together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to read her big book with many pages. One day, while reading her book, she saw a tiny bug on a page. The bug was fair and had big eyes. Amy was very surprised. Amy said, "Hello, little bug! What are you doing on my page?" The bug looked at Amy and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to bother you. I am just looking for some food." Amy smiled and said, "That's okay, little bug. Let's find you some food together." Amy and the bug looked around the room for some food. Suddenly, the bug saw a big, green leaf. The leaf was on a tall plant near the window. The bug was very happy and flew to the leaf. But when the bug tried to eat the leaf, it felt a pinch! The leaf was not a leaf at all, but a sneaky frog hiding on the plant. The frog laughed and said, "I almost got you, little bug!" The bug flew back to Amy, and they both laughed at the funny twist. From that day on, Amy and the bug were the best of friends.

One day, a famous boy named Tim had a big rocket. He loved to collect small rocks. Tim went to the park to find more rocks for his collection. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's rocket and said, "Wow, I like your rocket! Can I play with it?" Tim said, "Yes, but can you help me collect rocks first?" Sue agreed, and they looked for rocks together. After they collected many rocks, Tim and Sue played with the rocket. They had so much fun! The rocket went up and down, and they laughed. They became good friends and collected rocks and played with the rocket every day.
