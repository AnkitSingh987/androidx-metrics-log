Title: The Bear and the Song

Once there was a bear who wanted to be happy. He wanted to sing a song, but he could not find the right one. He went everywhere looking for the right song, but nothing was perfect. One day he went a bit further and found a fake song that he thought was perfect. As soon as he tried to sing it, he started to sneeze. His sneezes made the other animals laugh. The bear was embarrassed by his sneezes. The bear realised that he could not find the song he was looking for. He decided to just be happy with himself and be true to who he was. That was the best song he could ever find. The moral of the story is that we should all be true to ourselves. We should not try to copy other people, because what we are is good enough.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play baseball. One day, he went to the store with his mom to buy a new baseball. Tim saw a cheap baseball on the shelf. He was very happy. "Mom, can I have this baseball?" Tim asked. His mom looked at the price and said, "Yes, it's cheap. We can buy it." Tim and his mom went home. Tim played with his new baseball in the yard. He threw it high in the sky and saw it come back down. He caught it every time. Tim was having so much fun. Later, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. They both played baseball together. They took turns throwing and catching the ball. They laughed and had a great time playing with the cheap baseball. The sun began to set, and it was time for Sam to go home. Tim waved goodbye to his friend. He felt happy that he got to play baseball with his new cheap ball and his best friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to measure things. One day, Lily found a big stick. She wanted to see how long it was. So, she used her steps to measure it. Lily took one step, then two steps, then three steps. She found that the stick was three steps long. Lily was happy. She wanted to find more things to measure. She walked around and saw a rock. The rock was different from the stick. It was round and not long. Lily tried to measure the rock with her steps, but it was hard. She thought and thought. Then, she had an idea. She used the stick to measure the rock. The stick was longer than the rock. Lily learned that she could use different things to measure. The moral of the story is that we can use different ways to solve problems. If one way does not work, we can try another way.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Jane. Jane was feeling troubled and so she decided to search the wind. She wanted the wind to help her feel better. Jane went outside and looked up to the sky. She noticed the wind was blowing gently and softly. She closed her eyes and listened. When she opened her eyes, she saw her friend, Michael. He was standing right next to her. "What are you doing?" asked Michael. Jane replied, "I am searching for the wind. I need it to help me feel better." Michael said, "I can help you find it. Let's go and look together." So they both went outside, searching and searching. Soon they felt the breeze on their faces. They were listening to the sound of the wind and it was making them both happy. At the end of the day, Jane was no longer troubled. She was feeling better. The wind had helped her to feel better.

One day, a pink cat named Lucy went for a walk. She liked to walk in a zigzag way. It was a fun way to walk. Lucy saw her friend, a dog named Max. "Hi Max!" said Lucy. "Want to walk with me?" Max looked at Lucy's zigzag walk. He shook his head. "No, I refuse," said Max. "I like to walk straight." Lucy felt sad. But she kept walking in her zigzag way. Soon, she met a bunny named Bella. Bella liked to hop in zigzags too. "Hi Bella!" said Lucy. "Want to walk with me?" "Yes!" said Bella. "I love to zigzag!" So Lucy and Bella walked and hopped in zigzags together. They had a fun day. And Max saw them and smiled. He knew that everyone has their own way of walking.
