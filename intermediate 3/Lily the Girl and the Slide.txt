Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Slide

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a favorite doll named Daisy. Daisy was a bald doll with a big smile. Lily took Daisy everywhere she went. One sunny day, Lily and her mom went to the park. Lily played with Daisy on the slide. "Whee!" said Daisy, "This is fun!" Lily laughed and they went down the slide together. But then, Lily left Daisy in the sun. The sun was very hot, and Daisy began to melt. Lily saw this and quickly picked up her doll. "Oh no, Daisy! You're melting!" said Lily. She took Daisy to a shady spot under a tree. Daisy stopped melting, and they played happily ever after.

John was a mechanic. He loved to fix things. He was very tough. He would put his tools on the workbench, put on his safety glasses and start working. One day, John was having trouble fixing a car. He had been working on it for hours and he was getting tired. Suddenly, a small voice said, "Hey! Let me help!" John turned around to see a cute little three-year-old boy with a toy wrench in his hand. John smiled at him and said, "That's very kind of you, but I don't think a toy wrench can fix this car." The little boy smiled back and said, "Just watch me! I'm gonna put it together." John watched as the little boy started working on the car. He was so amazed that the boy was so tough and determined. After a few minutes, the car was fixed. John patted the little boy on the head and said, "Wow! That was amazing! I'm glad you came to help me. You're a real mechanic!" The little boy smiled and John knew he had made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat. The cat liked to move and play. One day, the cat saw a row of cups on a table. The cat thought it would be fun to move the cups. The cat jumped on the table and started to move the cups. The cat felt happy but also a little stupid. The cat knew it should not be on the table, but it was too much fun. Suddenly, the cat moved one cup too much. All the cups fell off the table and broke. The cat was sad and scared. The cat knew it had done something bad. The cat's fun day had a bad ending.

Nora was very worried. She had a meeting with her boss tomorrow and she was sure it wouldn't go well. She had been very frustrated with all of the tasks he was asking her to do. Nora's friend saw the worry on Nora's face and said, "What is wrong? Why do you look so worried?" Nora sighed and said, "I have a meeting with my boss tomorrow and I'm scared it won't go well. I'm so frustrated with all of the work he keeps giving me!" Nora's friend smiled and said, "Don't worry, I'm sure things will turn out okay". Nora nodded and said, "I hope you're right". The next day Nora went to her meeting. Her boss was very understanding and gave her kind words instead of more tasks. Nora left the meeting with a smile on her face. She wasn't worried anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside. One day, Mia saw a big rainbow in the sky. She was very happy. She wanted to show her mom and dad the rainbow. But when she tried to open the door, it was locked! Mia was worried. She didn't know what to do. Mia thought hard. She remembered that the key was under a big rock. She lifted the rock and found the key. Mia was happy again. She unlocked the door and ran inside to tell her mom and dad about the rainbow. When Mia showed her mom and dad the rainbow, they were so surprised. The rainbow was not just in the sky, it was also on the ground! The colors were everywhere. Mia, her mom, and her dad played in the colorful rainbow all day. They had so much fun, and they never locked the door again.
