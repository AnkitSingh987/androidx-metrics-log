Category: Intermediate
Title: Meg the Girl and the Sun

Once, there was a little girl named Meg. Every day, she was excited about playing with her friends. Meg was even more excited when her parents said that they would take her to the beach! When Meg arrived at the beach, she saw the bright sun and the shiny sand. She ran and jumped into the cool, clear water. Meg felt so happy! But then, she felt something wrong. Her head was starting to hurt and she felt too hot. Meg's parents took out a thermometer and checked her temperature. It was high! Meg was very tired and had to go home. At home, Meg was sad that she couldn't stay at the beach. But then, she remembered that it was important to take care of her health. She realized that it was better to stay at home and rest, instead of getting sick. Meg was excited about being at the beach but she was also excited about taking care of her health. Meg learned a valuable lesson that day and it made her feel so much better!

Once upon a time, there was a famous girl named Lily. Lily loved to fix things. She had a big box of jewelry that she liked to wear every day. One day, Lily's friend, Tim, came over to play. "Look at my pretty jewelry!" she said to Tim. Tim looked at the jewelry and smiled. But then, he saw a broken necklace. "Oh no! Your necklace is broken!" Tim said. Lily looked at the broken necklace and said, "Don't worry, I can fix it!" She took out her tools and started to fix the necklace. Tim watched her and clapped his hands. "You're so good at fixing things, Lily!" he said. Soon, the necklace was fixed, and Lily put it on. She and Tim played with the jewelry all day, and they were very happy. From that day on, Lily was known as the famous girl who could fix anything.

Anna likes to sign. She signs hello, thank you, and I love you to her mom and dad. She signs with her friends at school too. They learn new signs every day. One day, it rains a lot. Anna cannot go outside and play. She is sad. She looks out the window and sees a big rainbow. She wants to sign rainbow, but she does not know how. She asks her mom to teach her. Her mom shows her how to make an arc with her hands and move them across the sky. Anna tries to copy her. She signs rainbow, rainbow, rainbow. She is happy. She calls her friend Ben on the phone. She tells him about the rainbow and the sign. She asks him if he knows how to sign rainbow. He says no. He is jealous. He wishes he could sign rainbow too. He asks his mom to teach him. His mom does not know how to sign. She says they can look it up on the internet. They find a video that shows how to sign rainbow. Ben watches and learns. He signs rainbow, rainbow, rainbow. He is happy. He calls Anna back. He tells her he can sign rainbow now. He shows her on the phone. Anna is happy too. She says they are both smart and good signers. They sign I love you to each other. They are friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the theater with her mom. They were very excited to see a play. Lily's friend, Tim, was in the play, and she was envious. She wanted to be in a play too. As the play was about to start, the lights went off. Suddenly, a loud noise came from the stage. The actors were scared! Lily's mom said, "Oh no! What was that?" The people who worked at the theater found out that a big box had fallen on the stage. They needed someone small to help move it. Lily raised her hand and said, "I can help!" She went on stage and helped move the big box. Everyone clapped for her. After the play, Tim came to Lily and said, "You were great on stage! I wish I could be as brave as you." Now, it was Tim who was envious of Lily. They both laughed and promised to be in a play together next time.
