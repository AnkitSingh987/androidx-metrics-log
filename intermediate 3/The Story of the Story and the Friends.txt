Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Story and the Friends

Once upon a time there were two friends who wanted to take a walk. They looked around and found a pretty path that they followed. It got darker and darker, but they kept going – they were both very persistent. Finally, they heard a loud noise coming from the path. One of the friends said, “Let’s listen. What do you think it is?” The other one said, “I’m not sure, but let’s keep going.” They kept walking, but the noise got louder and louder. Suddenly, it became very clear what it was – it was a big, scary monster! The two friends quickly ran away and hid, but the monster was too fast – it chased them and caught them at the end of the path. The friend who wanted to listen said, “I told you we should have listened! Now we’re in trouble.” Sadly, the monster was too powerful, and the friends had to go home with nothing. The moral of the story is to always listen to your gut and be persistent – sometimes, it’s the only way to stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little jet. This jet was amazing. It could fly very high in the sky. It loved to play with the birds and the clouds. The jet was very happy. One day, the jet saw a big bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. The jet wanted to help. The jet had an idea. It could use its amazing speed to find help for the bird. The jet rubbed the bird's hurt wing gently to make it feel better. Then, the jet flew away to find help. The jet found a kind man who could fix the bird's wing. The man and the jet went to the bird. The man fixed the bird's wing. The bird was very happy. The jet was happy too. They all played together in the sky. The jet learned that helping others is the most amazing thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to be creative with her toys. One day, her mom said, "Lily, you can play outside, but you must eat your vegetables first." Lily did not like vegetables, but she wanted to play outside. So she thought of a creative plan. She took her vegetables and made a pretty picture on her plate. She made a tree with green beans, a sun with corn, and a house with carrots. When her mom saw the creative picture, she smiled. "That's very nice, Lily," she said. "But you still need to eat your vegetables." Lily knew her mom was right, so she ate her vegetables. After that, her mom permitted her to play outside. Lily was happy she ate her vegetables and got to play outside.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a crazy dog named Max. Max liked to run around and play all day long. One day, Max found a cane in the park. He picked it up with his mouth and started to play with it. Max was having so much fun with the cane that he didn't see Tom, the little boy who owned the cane. Tom was sad because he needed the cane to help him walk. He saw Max playing with it and asked Max to give it back. Max didn't listen and kept playing with the cane. Tom's big sister, Jane, saw what was happening and decided to help her brother. She came up with a plan to get the cane back from Max. Jane found a big, red ball and showed it to Max. Max loved balls and went crazy when he saw it. He dropped the cane and started to play with the ball instead. Jane picked up the cane and gave it back to Tom. Max almost destroyed the cane, but now Tom had it back and could walk again. Everyone was happy, and Max learned to be more careful with other people's things.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with paper. They can fold paper into many shapes. They can make hats, boats, and planes. One day, they find a big book of paper animals. They want to fold the paper animals. They look at the pictures and try to copy them. Lily folds a paper cat. She makes ears, eyes, and a tail. She is happy with her cat. She shows it to Ben. "Look, Ben, I made a cat. Do you like it?" she asks. Ben looks at the cat. He likes it, but he wants to make something else. He folds a paper dog. He makes legs, a nose, and a tail. He is happy with his dog. He shows it to Lily. "Look, Lily, I made a dog. Do you like it?" he asks. Lily looks at the dog. She likes it, but she wants to make something else. She folds a paper bird. She makes wings, a beak, and a tail. She is happy with her bird. She shows it to Ben. "Look, Ben, I made a bird. Do you like it?" she asks. Ben looks at the bird. He likes it, but he wants to make something else. He folds a paper snake. He makes a long body, a tongue, and eyes. He is happy with his snake. He shows it to Lily. "Look, Lily, I made a snake. Do you like it?" he asks. Lily looks at the snake. She does not like it. She is scared of snakes. She screams and runs away. "Help, help, a snake!" she cries. Ben laughs and chases her with his snake. He thinks it is funny. "Come back, Lily, it is only paper. It cannot bite you!" he says. Lily runs to their mom. She is alert and hears Lily's scream. She sees Ben with his snake. She is not scared of snakes. She knows it is paper. She smiles and hugs Lily. "Don't worry, Lily, it is not a real snake. It is a paper snake. Ben made it. He is very clever. You are very clever too. You both made beautiful paper animals. I am proud of you," she says. Lily and Ben hug their mom. They are happy. They show their mom their paper animals. They tell her how they folded them. They ask her to fold some with them. She agrees. They have fun folding paper animals together.
