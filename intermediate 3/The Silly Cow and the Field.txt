Category: Intermediate
Title: The Silly Cow and the Field

Once upon a time, there was a very silly cow. One day, the cow decided to enter a wheat field. The cow was so silly that he couldn't find his way out of the field. The cow was stuck in the wheat field for days. Finally, a farmer came over to the field and saw the silly cow. The farmer said to the cow, "Why did you enter the field?" The cow just stood there without saying a word. The farmer was so angry that he decided to call the police. The police came, and the farmer said, "The cow entered the wheat field, and it can't find its way out." The police took the cow away and put it in jail. The silly cow never got to see the wheat field ever again. It was sadly sent away and never seen again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an amazing red apple. He loved his apple very much. Every day, he would look at it and smile. He would say, "I love you, amazing apple!" One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's apple and said, "Wow, that's an amazing apple!" Tim replied, "Yes, it's my favorite!" They became friends and played together. While they were playing, the apple fell and broke in two. Tim and Sue were sad. But then, they had an idea. They shared the broken apple and ate it together. It was the best apple they ever had. They both said, "This broken apple is still amazing!" And they stayed friends forever.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. In the park, there was a huge statue. Tim liked to play near the statue because it was big and fun. As Tim played, a girl named Sue came up to him. "Hi, I am Sue. Do you want to play with me?" she asked. Tim felt a little embarrass because he was shy, but he said, "Yes, let's play." They played and had fun. Suddenly, the huge statue started to move! Tim and Sue were shocked. The statue was not a statue at all, it was a big friendly giant! The giant smiled and said, "Hello, kids! I was just taking a nap." Tim and Sue laughed and played with the giant all day.

Lily and Ben were on a trip with their mom and dad. They stayed in a big hotel with many rooms and a pool. One day, they wanted to go to the pool, but their mom said they had to wait. She had to do some work on her laptop. Lily and Ben were bored. They looked out the window and saw a big park with swings and slides. They wanted to go there and play. They had an idea. They put on their shoes and hats and sneaked out of the door. They hoped their mom would not see them. They ran down the hall and took the elevator. They pressed the button for the lobby. The elevator stopped and the door opened. They saw a man with a big smile and a badge. He was the hotel manager. "Hello, little ones," he said. "Where are you going?" Lily and Ben were scared. They thought he would tell their mom. They tried to escape. They ran past him and out of the hotel. They crossed the street and ran to the park. They climbed the swings and slid down the slides. They had fun. They forgot about their mom and the hotel. But the hotel manager did not forget. He was generous. He knew their mom was busy. He wanted to help. He called their mom and told her where they were. He also sent a car to pick them up. Soon, Lily and Ben saw a car with a hotel logo. They saw their mom get out. She looked angry and worried. She hugged them and scolded them. "Lily and Ben, you were very naughty," she said. "You should never leave the hotel without me. It is dangerous. You could get lost or hurt. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nodded. They felt sorry. They said sorry to their mom and to the hotel manager. They thanked him for his help. The hotel manager smiled. He gave them a big bag of candy and toys. He said they were welcome to come back to the hotel anytime. But they had to promise to stay with their mom. Lily and Ben promised. They got in the car and went back to the hotel. They learned their lesson. They decided to wait for their mom before going to the pool. They also decided to share their candy and toys with their mom. They were generous, too.
