Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Robot

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to play with his friends. They played with cars, dolls, and balls. They had a lot of fun. But one day, a new kid came to play. The new kid was different. His name was Sam. Sam had a big toy robot. Tim and his friends had never seen a toy like this before. They wanted to play with the robot too, but Sam did not want to share. Tim and his friends were very sad. They did not know what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. He asked Sam to mind his robot while they all played together. Sam agreed, and they all played a big game with the robot. They had even more fun than before. Tim and Sam became best friends, and they learned that sharing and playing together was the best way to have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy skull. He loved to play with the skull all day. One day, Tim's mom said, "You cannot play with the skull in the house. It is too big and stupid. It may break things." Tim was sad. He went outside to play with his skull. He saw his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Can I play with your skull too?" Tim said, "Yes, but we must be careful. My mom does not permit us to play with it in the house." They played with the skull all day. But then, they started to fight. Tim wanted to play with the skull alone. Sam wanted to play with it too. They both pulled on the skull. The skull broke into two pieces. Tim and Sam were very sad. They knew they had been stupid. They said sorry to each other. They promised to share and play together from now on. And they both learned that it is better to share and play together, than to fight and break things.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely family. They had a mommy, a daddy, and a little girl named Lily. Every day, they would sit together and have dinner. They were always happy and would talk about their day. One day, mommy made a big dinner. She told Lily to behave and wait for daddy to come home. Lily was very hungry, but she wanted to be a good girl. So, she sat and waited for daddy. Daddy came home very late. When he saw the dinner, he was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the food away! The family was sad and hungry. They learned that sometimes, even when you behave, things don't always go as planned.

One day, a little animal was playing in the woods. The animal was worried because it had lost its way. It did not know how to go home. So, the animal walked and walked, looking for its home. As the animal walked, it saw a big tree. The animal thought it might see its home if it went up the tree. So, it climbed and climbed until it was very high. The animal decided to peek from behind the leaves to find its home. But, something unexpected happened! Instead of seeing its home, the animal saw a big party with many other animals! The worried animal was not lost after all. It had found a surprise party that its friends made just for it! The animal was very happy and had lots of fun with its friends.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toy cars in the living room. They liked to make noises like vroom, beep, and honk as they drove them around. Tom had a red car and Lily had a blue car. They pretended they were going to the park, the store, and the zoo. "Look, Lily, I can reverse my car like this," Tom said, as he moved his car backwards. "Beep, beep, beep. Watch out!" "That's cool, Tom, but I can do this," Lily said, as she spun her car around in a circle. "Whee, whee, whee. I'm so fast!" They laughed and kept playing, but they did not notice that their mom was getting tired of their noise. She was trying to read a book on the couch, but she could not concentrate. She had a headache and she wanted some quiet. "Tom, Lily, can you please play a little quieter?" she asked them. "Mommy needs some rest." "OK, Mommy, we're sorry," Tom and Lily said. They tried to play quieter, but they still made some noise. They did not want to stop playing. Mommy put down her book and got up from the couch. She walked over to the toy box and picked up a big yellow truck. She smiled and said, "Do you know what this is?" Tom and Lily looked at her and said, "Yes, Mommy, it's a truck." Mommy nodded and said, "That's right, it's a truck. And do you know what trucks can do?" Tom and Lily shook their heads and said, "No, Mommy, what can trucks do?" Mommy said, "Trucks can make a lot of noise. Like this." She pressed a button on the truck and it made a loud roar. "Rrrrrrrrr!" Tom and Lily gasped and covered their ears. They did not like the noise. It was too loud and scary. "Mommy, stop, stop, please!" they cried. "We don't like the noise!" Mommy pressed the button again and the truck roared louder. "Rrrrrrrrr!" Tom and Lily ran to the couch and hid behind the pillows. They were scared and sorry. "Mommy, we're sorry, we're sorry!" they said. "We'll play quieter, we promise!" Mommy turned off the truck and put it back in the toy box. She walked over to the couch and hugged Tom and Lily. She said, "It's OK, my loves, I forgive you. But you have to remember that sometimes Mommy needs some quiet. Can you do that for me?" Tom and Lily nodded and said, "Yes, Mommy, we can. We love you, Mommy." Mommy said, "I love you too, Tom and Lily. Now, how about we read a nice story together?" Tom and Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Mommy, we'd like that." They snuggled up with Mommy on the couch and picked a book. They read the story and soon they fell asleep. They were tired from playing. Mommy kissed them and smiled. She was happy and proud. She closed her eyes and rested too. She was tired from roaring.
