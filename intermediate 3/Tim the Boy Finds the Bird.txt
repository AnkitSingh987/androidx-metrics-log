Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very persistent. He always asked his mom to permit him to play outside. One day, a big package arrived at their house. "Mom, can I open the package?" asked Tim. "Yes, but be careful," his mom said. Tim opened the package and found a big, colorful ball inside. He was so happy and asked his mom if he could play with it outside. While playing with the ball, something unexpected happened. The ball hit a big tree and a small bird fell down. Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. "Mom, the bird fell down!" Tim shouted. His mom came out and saw the bird. "Don't worry, Tim. We can help the bird," she said. They took care of the bird until it was strong again and flew away. From that day, Tim learned that it's important to be kind and help others when they are in need. He also learned to be more careful when playing outside.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. This rock was in a pretty landscape with trees, flowers, and a river. The rock was very big and felt ashamed because it could not move or play like the other things in the landscape. One day, a strong wind came and split the rock into two. The rock was now two smaller rocks. The two rocks were very happy because they could now move a little bit. They started to play with the trees, flowers, and the river. Then, something unexpected happened. The two rocks found out that they could join back together and become the big rock again. They were not ashamed anymore because they knew they could change and play with their friends. The big rock and the landscape lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They took a big bottle of juice to drink when they got thirsty. They played on the swings and the slide, and Tim had lots of fun. Tim saw a little girl named Sue playing with a toy. He wanted to play with it too, but Sue didn't want to share. Tim got mad and pinched Sue's arm. Sue started to cry, and Tim's mom came over to see what happened. "Tim, why did you pinch Sue?" his mom asked. Tim said, "She wouldn't share her toy with me." His mom said, "That's not nice, Tim. You should say sorry to Sue." Tim felt bad and said sorry to Sue. Sue stopped crying and they both shared the toy. Later, Tim and Sue got thirsty, so they went to drink the juice. But Tim's mom said, "This juice is disgusting! I think it's old." They threw the juice away and drank water instead. The moral of the story is to always be nice and share with others. Also, check if the juice is good before you drink it.

Anna and Ben love to go to the zoo. They like to see all the different animals. Their favorite animals are the penguins. The penguins are black and white and have funny feet. They swim in the water and slide on the ice. One day, Anna and Ben go to the zoo with their mom. They run to the penguin house. They see many penguins in the open pool. They watch them splash and play. They hear them make loud noises. "What are they doing?" Anna asks. "They are singing," mom says. "Penguins sing to each other. They have their own songs." "Can we sing with them?" Ben asks. "Sure, why not?" mom says. "Let's try to copy their sounds." Anna and Ben start to sing with the penguins. They make high and low noises. They try to match the penguins' tunes. The penguins look at them and sing back. They seem to like the children's songs. Anna and Ben have fun singing with the penguins. They feel happy and close to the animals. They thank mom for taking them to the zoo. They say goodbye to the penguins and wave. They hope to come back soon and sing with them again.
