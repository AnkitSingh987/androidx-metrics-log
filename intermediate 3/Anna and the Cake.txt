Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Cake

Anna liked to help her mom in the kitchen. She wanted to be independent and do things by herself. She learned how to serve food on a plate and carry it to the table. She was very careful not to spill or drop anything. One day, her mom made a big cake for her dad's birthday. It smelled very good and looked very pretty. Anna wanted to serve a piece of cake to her dad. She asked her mom if she could do it. "Sure, you can, Anna. But be very careful, the cake is heavy and the knife is sharp. Do you need my help?" her mom said. "No, thank you, mom. I can do it by myself. I am independent," Anna said. She took a plate, a knife and a fork. She cut a big piece of cake and put it on the plate. She held the plate with both hands and walked slowly to the living room. She had to cross a long path from the kitchen to the living room. The path had many things on it: toys, books, shoes, a ball. Anna tried to avoid them and not step on them. She did not want to trip or fall. She looked at the cake and smiled. She knew her dad would be happy. She reached the living room and saw her dad sitting on the couch. He was reading a book. He did not see her coming. She said in a loud voice, "Surprise, dad! I have a piece of cake for you. Happy birthday!" Her dad looked up and saw her. He was very surprised and very happy. He put down his book and hugged her. He said, "Thank you, Anna. You are very sweet and very independent. You made my day. I love you." He took the plate from her and ate the cake. It was very delicious. He shared some with Anna. They both enjoyed it. Anna felt proud and happy. She had served her dad a piece of cake by herself. She had crossed the path without any trouble. She had shown her mom and dad how independent she was. She was a big girl.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a curious squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to find nuts to eat. One day, Sam met another squirrel named Sally. Sally was very pretty, and Sam liked her a lot. They played together and found many nuts. One day, Sam said to Sally, "I like you. Will you marry me?" Sally said, "Yes, I will marry you. But first, you must find the biggest nut in the forest." Sam was excited and went to look for the biggest nut. Sam looked everywhere, but he could not find the biggest nut. He felt sad. Then, he met a wise old owl. The owl said, "Do not worry, Sam. The biggest nut is in the tallest tree." So, Sam climbed the tallest tree and found the biggest nut. Sam brought the biggest nut to Sally, and they were very happy. They got married and lived happily ever after in the big tree, sharing their love and lots of nuts.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One night, Tim went to sleep in his bed. He had a weird dream. In the dream, there was a big monster. The monster was scary. This was a nightmare for Tim. Tim woke up and ran to his mom. "Mom, I had a nightmare," he said. His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tim. It was just a dream. Let's go back to sleep." Tim and his mom went back to his room. His mom stayed with him until he fell asleep. Tim did not have any more nightmares that night. He had a good sleep and woke up happy.

Ben and Mia went to the park with their mom. They saw a big pond with many ducks and geese. They had some bread to feed them. "Look, mom, a boat!" Ben said, pointing to a small wooden boat that someone had left on the water. "Can we play with it?" Mom nodded and helped them get closer to the boat. They observed the boat carefully. It had a sail, a rudder, and a flag. It looked like a pirate boat. "Arrr, mateys, this is our boat now!" Mia said, pretending to be a pirate. She took the flag and waved it in the air. "Ahoy, captain, where are we sailing to?" Ben asked, joining the game. He took the rudder and steered the boat. "We are sailing to Treasure Island, where we will find gold and jewels!" Mia said, making a telescope with her hand. "But watch out for the hungry sharks!" They played with the boat for a long time, making sounds and stories. The ducks and geese watched them curiously. They also looked hungry. "Mom, can we feed the ducks and geese?" Ben asked, remembering the bread. "Of course, sweetie, let's share our bread with them," Mom said, smiling. She took the bread and broke it into pieces. She gave some to Ben and Mia and threw some to the water. The ducks and geese came closer and ate the bread happily. They quacked and honked to say thank you. Ben and Mia observed the ducks and geese. They saw how they swam, dived, and flapped their wings. They learned their names and colors. They had a lot of fun at the park with their mom, the boat, and the ducks and geese. They were not hungry anymore. They were happy.
