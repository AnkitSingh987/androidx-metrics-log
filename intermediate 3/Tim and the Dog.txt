Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his old dog, Max. They were all very happy together. Tim loved to play outside with Max, and they would run and jump all day long. One day, while Tim and Max were playing, they saw something strange in the sky. Tim looked up and recognized it was a tornado! He was scared and didn't know what to do. But Max, the old dog, knew they had to go inside the house to be safe. Max barked at Tim, "Woof! Woof! We need to go inside now!" Tim listened to Max, and they both ran inside the house. Tim's mom and dad were surprised to see them. Tim told them about the tornado, and they all hid in a safe place until it was gone. The tornado passed by, but it didn't hurt their house. They were all very lucky. After the tornado was gone, Tim, his mom, dad, and Max went outside to see if everything was okay. To their surprise, they found a big box in their yard! The tornado had brought it there. They opened the box and found lots of toys and fun things to play with. Tim and Max were so happy, and they played with their new toys all day long. The tornado was scary, but it brought them a nice surprise.

Lily and Ben were very hot. They wanted to play outside, but the sun was too bright and the air was too sticky. They went inside their house and looked for something fun to do. "Let's play with the fan!" Lily said. She liked to feel the wind on her face and hair. She turned on the fan and ran in front of it. "Whee!" Ben followed her and smiled. He also liked the fan. He grabbed a paper and held it near the fan. The paper flew away and made a noise. "Wow!" They played with the fan for a while, until they got thirsty. They went to the kitchen and looked for something to drink. They saw a pitcher of lemonade on the counter. "Let's have some lemonade!" Ben said. He poured two cups and gave one to Lily. They drank the lemonade and made a face. "Yuck!" The lemonade was very sour. It made their tongues hurt and their eyes water. They did not like the lemonade at all. "Who made this lemonade?" Lily asked. She put down her cup and wiped her mouth. "Mama did," Ben said. He also put down his cup and looked for water. "She said it was good for us." Lily and Ben did not think the lemonade was good for them. They thought it was bad for them. They wanted something sweet and cold. "Let's suggest to Mama to make some ice cream!" Lily said. She had an idea. "Maybe she will listen to us and make some ice cream for us." Ben liked the idea. He nodded and smiled. "Yes, let's suggest to Mama to make some ice cream!" They ran to find Mama and tell her their suggestion. They hoped she would say yes and make some ice cream for them. They did not want any more sour lemonade. They wanted some sweet ice cream.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a regular day at her play school. She played with her friends and had a lot of fun. But then, she fell down and felt pain in her knee. Sue started to cry. A kind teacher came over and said, "Oh no, Sue! What happened?" Sue looked at her knee and said, "I fell down and now it hurts." The teacher gave her a hug and helped her sit down. Sue gazed at her friends playing and wished she could join them. The teacher put a band-aid on her knee and said, "Now you can play again, Sue." Sue smiled and went back to play with her friends. She was happy and the pain went away.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. Lily loved to wear attractive clothes. One day, she wore her favorite dress and went to play with her friends. While playing, Lily found a secret chamber in her backyard. It was a small room with many pretty things. Lily and her friends went inside to look. But, they did not know that a naughty boy named Tom was hiding in the chamber. Tom jumped out and scared Lily and her friends. They were sad and wanted to leave the chamber. Lily was brave and told Tom to stop being mean. Tom listened and said sorry. In the end, they all played together in the chamber and had a great time. Lily's attractive dress made her feel happy and strong.
