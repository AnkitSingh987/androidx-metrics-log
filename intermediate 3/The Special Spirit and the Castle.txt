Category: Intermediate
Title: The Special Spirit and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a special spirit. It was deep in a castle, and it wanted to escape. It looked around, but the castle was too tall. The spirit was stuck and couldn’t find a way out. But then, the spirit had an idea. It thought and thought until it came up with a plan. The spirit created a ladder that was very deep. It climbed up and up until it reached the sky. As the spirit reached the top, it felt so free. With a big jump, it flew away from the castle. The spirit was so happy that it had escaped. From then on, the spirit soared deep through the sky each and every day. Now, the spirit is free to explore and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max loved to travel and find new things. One day, Max went on a long walk with his best friend, Sam. They walked and walked until they were both very tired. "Max, I'm so tired," said Sam, as they sat down under a big tree. Max wagged his tail and said, "Me too, Sam. Let's rest here for a bit." As they sat under the tree, Max saw something in the dirt. It was a big bone! Max and Sam were very happy. They played with the bone and had lots of fun. After playing, Max and Sam were tired again. They decided to go home and show their new bone to their friends. They knew they had a great day and couldn't wait for their next adventure.

One day, a big bug and a little bug found a log. They both wanted the log for their home. The big bug said, "This is my log!" The little bug said, "No, it is my log!" The big bug and the little bug started to struggle. They pushed and pulled the log. They both wanted the log because it was fancy. It had pretty colors and nice holes for them to live in. A wise old bird saw the bugs fighting. The bird said, "Stop! You can both live in the log. Share the fancy log and be friends." The big bug and the little bug stopped fighting. They both lived in the fancy log and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, good secret. Lily loved to smile and play with her friends in the park. One sunny day, Lily went to the park to share her secret with her best friend, Tom. They sat under a big tree and Lily whispered her secret in Tom's ear. Tom's eyes grew wide, and he smiled too. Just then, a funny thing happened. A little bird heard Lily's secret and started to sing it out loud. Everyone at the park heard the secret and started to smile and laugh. Lily and Tom looked at each other and laughed too. The secret was out, but it made everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a big dog named Bow. Bow loved holidays because everyone was happy, and he got many treats. But this holiday was a little bit difficult for Bow. He wanted to find a special gift for his best friend, a little cat named Mew. Bow looked and looked for the perfect gift. He looked under the big tree, he looked behind the small house, and he even looked in the tall grass. But Bow could not find the gift he wanted. He felt sad and worried that Mew would be upset. Then, something unexpected happened. Bow saw a shiny box in the park. He ran to the box and opened it. Inside, he found the most beautiful toy mouse. Bow knew Mew would love it! He took the toy mouse and gave it to Mew. Mew was so happy and surprised. They played together all day, and it turned out to be the best holiday ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a small radio. He loved to listen to the songs on the radio. It made him very happy. One day, the radio made a harsh noise. Tim was sad. He tried to fix it, but it didn't work. He asked his mom to help him. She looked at the radio and saw a part was broken. Mom took Tim to the store to buy a new part for the radio. When they got home, they fixed the radio together. But when they turned it on, it didn't play songs anymore. Instead, it talked to them! The radio told Tim and his mom that it was a magic radio. It could grant them one wish. Tim and his mom looked at each other, surprised. They didn't know what to wish for. After thinking for a while, Tim wished for a big ice cream cone. The magic radio made the ice cream cone appear. Tim and his mom laughed and enjoyed the ice cream together. They knew they would never forget their special magic radio.
