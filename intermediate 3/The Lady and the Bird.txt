Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lady and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a lady who loved to decorate. She had a big party to plan, but she was nervous. She did not know if her friends would like her decorations. She worked very hard to make her home look pretty. One day, a little bird saw the lady feeling sad. The bird wanted to help. The bird told all its friends about the lady and her party. The bird's friends wanted to help, too. They all had a secret plan. On the day of the party, the lady was still nervous. When she opened her door, she saw a big surprise. The bird and its friends had decorated her yard with beautiful flowers and leaves. The lady was so happy. Her friends loved the decorations, and everyone had a great time at the party. The lady knew she had some special new friends in the little bird and its friends.

---

One day, a little girl named Lily went to a dance class. She loved to dance and have fun. She wore her favorite pink dress and her dance shoes. In her hand, she held a note from her mom that said, "Have a great time, love Mom." At the dance class, Lily met a silly boy named Tim. Tim liked to make funny faces and be foolish. He made Lily laugh a lot. They danced and danced, and had lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew the note from Lily's hand. The note flew up, up, up into the sky. Lily and Tim looked at each other and decided to chase after it. They ran and ran, but the wind was too strong. They thought they would never catch the note. Suddenly, a kind bird saw the note and grabbed it with its beak. The bird flew down and gave the note back to Lily. "Thank you, bird!" said Lily and Tim. They were so happy to have the note back. From that day on, Lily and Tim became best friends. They promised to always help each other and to dance together, even when they felt foolish. And they always remembered the kind bird who helped them catch the note.

One day, a soft cat named Kitty went to the park. She saw her friend, a little bird named Blue. Blue had a message for Kitty. He whispered, "I found something fun! Follow me!" Kitty and Blue went deep into the park. They found a big tree with a hole in it. Blue whispered, "Look inside!" Kitty looked and saw a shiny box. She knew it was special. Kitty and Blue took the box home. They opened it and found a big, soft, bouncy ball. They played with the ball all day long. They knew the special box was a gift just for them.

---

One day, a little shrimp went to play near the sea. The water was calm and the sun was shining. The shrimp saw a big fish and wanted to be friends with it. The big fish said, "Hello, little shrimp! Let's play a game. I will give you a hint, and you must find what I am talking about." The shrimp was excited and said, "Okay, I am ready to play!" The big fish gave the first hint, "It can run fast on land, and it has a long tail." The shrimp thought about it and swam around to find the answer. After a while, the shrimp found a crab running fast on the sand. It had a long tail too! The shrimp went back to the big fish and said, "I found it! The answer is a crab!" The big fish smiled and said, "Good job, little shrimp! You are very smart!" They both laughed and played together all day in the calm sea.

One day, a cat named Tom found a spray bottle. He liked to spray water on the plants. He saw a candle on a table. Tom thought it would be fun to spray the candle too. Tom's friend, a dog named Jerry, came over to play. Jerry smelled something sour in the air. He asked Tom about the smell. Tom said he did not know, but they kept playing. Later, they saw the candle was still on the table. They remembered the sour smell from before. Tom sprayed the candle with water, and the sour smell went away. Tom and Jerry were happy and played all day.
