Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Grass

One day, a little bird named Tim was trying to work on his nest. It was on a cliff, high up in the sky. Tim was frustrated because it was hard to find sticks and grass to make the nest. Tim saw his friend, a big bear named Ben. "Ben, can you help me get sticks?" asked Tim. Ben said, "Yes, I can help you, Tim." So, they went to find sticks and grass together. But, Ben was too big and heavy for the cliff. When he tried to help Tim, the cliff broke and they both fell down. Tim and Ben were very sad that they could not finish the nest.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. The red ball was very happy. One day, the red ball saw a mattress. The mattress was soft and nice. The red ball wanted to play with the mattress. So, the red ball asked the wind to help. The wind was strong and could send the red ball to the mattress. The wind blew and sent the red ball to the mattress. When the red ball reached the mattress, they played together all day. They had so much fun. The red ball and the mattress became best friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl, her name was Sarah. She liked to play with her toys and cats. One day she was feeling very brave and wanted to show her friend Joe how good she was at making a stack. Sarah carefully stacked all of her blocks on top of each other. She was so proud of herself that she wanted to show everyone how tall her stack was. But then the stack fell over and embarrassed her in front of everybody. Sarah wanted to cry, but Joe came to the rescue. He said, "Don't worry, Sarah. It's ok to make mistakes. Here, let me show you how to make a stack that won't fall down." Joe carefully stacked all of the blocks on top of each other, creating a beautiful little tower. Sarah was amazed. She said, "Thank you! Can we play with the stack now?" Joe smiled and showed Sarah how to make towers with the blocks and then they made it even better by adding tasty little treats to the stack! The two friends played with the stack until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a nice driver named Tom. Tom drove a big bus with many seats. He loved to help people get to where they needed to go. One day, Tom saw a little girl crying on the side of the road. He stopped the bus and said, "Why are you crying?" The little girl said, "I can't find my way home." Tom wanted to improve her day, so he said, "I can help you find your home." Tom and the little girl got on the bus and drove around until they found her house. The little girl was so happy and said, "Thank you, Tom, for being so nice and helping me!" Tom smiled and drove away, happy to have improved her day.

Once upon a time, there was a big gorilla named Gogo. Gogo was very selfish. He did not like to share his toys with his friends. One day, Gogo was playing in the mud. He got very dirty. His mom said, "Gogo, you need to wash up before you can play with your toys." Gogo did not want to wash. He wanted to play with his toys now. But his mom said, "No, you have to wash first." So, Gogo went to wash. He washed his hands, his face, and his feet. When he was all clean, he came back to play with his toys. Gogo saw his friends playing with his toys. He was mad at first, but then he learned to share. Gogo was not selfish anymore, and they all played together happily.

Once upon a time, there was a busy little bee. The bee liked to fly around and settle on pretty flowers. One day, the bee found a bottle of shampoo in a garden. The bee did not know what shampoo was, but it smelled nice. The bee decided to settle on the bottle and taste the shampoo. The bee got a little bit on its nose, but it did not taste good. The bee flew away from the shampoo bottle and found a big, pretty flower. The bee was happy to settle on the flower and forget about the shampoo. The bee went back to being busy, flying from flower to flower.
