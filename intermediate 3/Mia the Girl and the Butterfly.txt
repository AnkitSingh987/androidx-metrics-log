Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside with her friends. One sunny day, she found a long hose in her yard. She thought it would be fun to play with it. Mia called her friend, Sam, and said, "Come play with me and my hose!" Sam came running over, and they started to play. They splashed water on the grass and laughed. Then, they saw a big butterfly. The butterfly was graceful as it flew around them. Mia and Sam stopped to listen to the butterfly's wings. They could hear a soft sound as it moved through the air. "Wow, it's so pretty!" Sam said. Mia agreed, and they continued to play with the hose, making sure not to scare the butterfly away. They had a fun day playing and listening to the sounds of nature.

Once there was a brother and sister. The brother was weird. He liked to eat bugs. The sister did not like bugs. She would say, "Yuck! Bugs are not food!" One day, they went to play outside. The brother saw a big bug. He wanted to eat it. The sister wanted to prevent him. She said, "No, don't eat the bug! It is not good." The brother did not listen. He ate the bug. Then, something unexpected happened. The bug turned him into a frog! The sister was scared. She said, "Oh no! What do we do?" The frog-brother found a magic bug. He ate it. He turned back into a boy. The brother said, "I will not eat bugs anymore." The sister was happy. They went home and told their mom the story.

Once upon a time, there was a smart boy named Tim. Tim had a toy automobile that he loved to play with. He would drive it all around his house and make car sounds as he played. One day, a new girl named Sue moved in next door. Sue saw Tim playing with his automobile and wanted to play too. Tim wanted to be a good friend, so he decided to share his toy with Sue. As they played together, something unexpected happened. The toy automobile started to grow bigger and bigger! Suddenly, it was big enough for Tim and Sue to ride in. They looked at each other in surprise and laughed. They took turns driving the big automobile, sharing it and having lots of fun together.

One day, a modest girl named Lucy was playing with her toy car. She loved the car very much. It was a sunny day, and she was having fun. Lucy met a boy named Tim. Tim said, "I like your car. Can I play with it?" Lucy said, "Yes, you can play with my car." They played together, and they were happy. Tim tried to make the car go fast. He pushed it hard, and the car broke. Lucy was sad. Tim said, "I am sorry. I will fix it." Tim tried to fix the car, but he didn't know how. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's reverse the car!" They pushed the car backward, and it started to work again. Lucy and Tim were surprised and happy. They played with the car, and their love for it grew even more.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty cat. The cat loved to play with a plastic ball. One day, the cat was playing with the ball and it rolled into a big puddle. The cat was sad because the ball was wet and dirty. The cat tried to get the ball, but it was too far in the puddle. The cat asked a dog to help. The dog was big and strong. The dog walked into the puddle to get the ball. The dog got the ball, but now the dog was all wet and dirty too. The cat and the dog were not happy. They were wet and dirty from the puddle. They tried to shake the water off, but they were still wet. The cat and the dog were sad because they were soaked and could not play with the ball anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She liked to play with her friends in the park. One day, Lucy found a thin, shiny paper on the ground. She picked it up and thought about what she could do with it. Lucy saw a small, round pebble on the ground. She had an idea! She decided to wrap the thin paper around the pebble to make it look pretty. She showed it to her friends, and they all thought it was very nice. They wanted to make their own pretty pebbles too. Lucy and her friends spent the day wrapping pebbles with thin paper. They made many pretty pebbles and shared them with other kids in the park. Everyone was happy, and they learned that sharing and being kind made the park a fun place to play.
