Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Jess and the Bird

Once upon a time there was a girl called Jess. Jess liked playing outside in the sunshine. One day, Jess was in the park when she saw a grape on the ground. "What is this?" Jess asked. Jess bent down to pick it up and a bird replied. "It's a grape!" Jess was excited. "Can I eat it?" She asked. "That's up to you," the bird said. "It looks fair enough to eat." "Yay!" said Jess. She took a bite and it was delicious. She said thank you to the bird and skipped away happily. The end.

Once there was a man and his wife. The man was poor. They lived in a small house. The man wanted to make his wife happy. He thought, "I will find a gift for my wife." The man went out to look for a gift. He saw a pretty flower. He thought, "My wife will love this flower." He picked the flower and started to go home. On his way, he met a talking bird. The bird said, "Give me the flower and I will give you three wishes." The man was surprised. He said, "Okay, I will give you the flower." He gave the flower to the bird. The bird said, "Now you have three wishes. What do you want?" The man thought about his poor wife. He said, "I wish we were not poor. I wish we had a big house. I wish my wife was always happy." The bird said, "Your wishes will come true. Go home and see." The man went home. He saw a big house. He saw his wife smiling. He realized the bird was right. The man and his wife lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small town where everyone had their own mailbox. In this town, lived a little girl named Sally and her best friend, a dog named Spot. They loved to play together and were always happy. One day, Sally and Spot found a big, red ball that didn't fit in their toy box. They wanted to keep it safe, so they tried to put it in their mailbox. But the ball was too big! Sally said, "Oh no, Spot! The ball doesn't fit in our mailbox. What should we do?" They decided to ask their friend, Mr. Owl, for help. Mr. Owl was very wise and always had good ideas. He told them, "Sally, Spot, you don't need to put the ball in your mailbox. You can share it with your friends in the town. That way, everyone can play with it and have fun." Sally and Spot loved the idea and shared the big, red ball with everyone in the town. All the kids and animals played together and had a great time. They learned that sharing with others can make everyone happy. And that was the moral of the story: sharing is caring.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Jim. Both were interested in nature, but they had different ideas. Jack thought they should count the leaves on a tree while Jim wanted to explore the lake. Jack said, "Let's number the leaves. That's what I like to do! We can do it quickly and then go to the lake." But Jim replied, "No way! We should stay here and explore. That's the best way to learn about nature!" The two friends argued for a while, until Jack had an idea. He said, "Okay, let's do both! We can number the leaves quickly, and then we have plenty of time to explore the lake." Jim thought this was a wonderful plan. He said, "You're right, Jack! That's the best way. We can do it fast and still learn about nature!" So they both happily went ahead, counting the leaves and then exploring the lake. They had a great time together and learned a lot about nature!

Once upon a time, there was a big body named Mr. Circle. He had many friends who were different from him. They all lived in a colorful world called Shape Land. Mr. Circle was always happy and liked to encourage his friends to have fun. One sunny day, Mr. Circle and his friends went to play at the park. They were playing hide and seek. Mr. Circle was very good at hiding because he could roll behind the trees. But something unexpected happened. While hiding, Mr. Circle found a magic wand! Mr. Circle picked up the wand and showed it to his friends. They all took turns using the magic wand. With a wave, they changed into different shapes. They encouraged each other to try new shapes and laughed as they played. But then, the wand broke! They were stuck in their new shapes. They learned to love their new different shapes and continued to encourage each other to be happy. And they all lived happily ever after in Shape Land.
