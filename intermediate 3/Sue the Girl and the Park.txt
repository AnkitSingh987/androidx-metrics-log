Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toy dog, Max, in the park. They had so much fun together. One day, Sue found a big box of powder. She said to Max, "Look, Max! I found powder. Let's play with it!" Sue and Max played with the powder, making it rise into the air like dust. They laughed and played all day long. But then, as the sun went down, Sue and Max started to feel sick. They had played with the powder too much. Sue said, "Oh no, Max! We don't feel good." They went home and told their mom. Their mom was sad and said, "Sue, you should not play with things you don't know." Sue and Max felt very bad and went to bed, hoping to feel better soon.

Once upon a time, there was a team of animals. They were good friends. They liked to play together. The team had a dog, a cat, and a bird. They were a happy team. One day, a bad fox came to their home. The fox wanted to steal their food. The fox was very sneaky. The team did not like the bad fox. The dog, the cat, and the bird worked together. They stopped the bad fox from stealing their food. The bad fox went away. The team was happy again. They played and had fun. The team was safe and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Fluffy. Fluffy was very happy because his family just moved to a new house. They had many boxes to unpack. Fluffy wanted to help, so he looked for his family's things in the boxes. In one box, Fluffy found a phone. He wanted to show his family the phone, but he did not know how to carry it. Fluffy tried to pick up the phone with his mouth, but it was too big. He tried to push it with his nose, but it was too heavy. Fluffy felt sad because he could not help his family unpack. He sat down and cried. His family saw him and came to comfort him. They told Fluffy that they loved him, even if he could not help. But Fluffy still felt sad, because he wanted to be a big help to his family.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She wanted to go on a trip with her family. Lily's mom said they needed to buy some luggage first. So they went to the store. At the store, they saw many bags. There was a big, dark bag. Lily liked the dark bag. Her mom said it was a good choice. They bought the dark luggage and went back home. Lily was very happy. She packed her toys and clothes in the dark luggage. The next day, Lily and her family went on their trip. They had a lot of fun and made good memories. And the dark luggage carried all their things safely.

One day, a calm cat named Kitty decided to travel. Kitty wanted to see a new view. She walked and walked until she found a big hill. Kitty was so happy to find a new place. Kitty climbed the big hill. She was excited to see the view from the top. But when Kitty got to the top, she saw something unexpected. There was a big pond with many fish. Kitty loved fish! Kitty wanted to catch the fish, but she was scared of the water. Then, a friendly frog jumped out of the pond. The frog said, "Don't worry, Kitty! I can help you catch the fish." Kitty and the frog became best friends, and they had a fun day at the pond.

Once upon a time there was a man who was very restless. He had been searching everywhere for the perfect fit. Every price he saw was too high. One day, he decided to go to a shop that had many things that would fit him. Without asking, he tried one of the items on and it fit perfectly. But the price of the item was too high. When the shopkeeper saw him, he said, "That looks like it fits well. How much are you willing to pay for it?" The man thought for a moment before answering. "I'm sorry but I can't afford that price." The shopkeeper frowned. "I'm afraid I can't help you then. You'll have to leave." The man left with nothing. He was so restless, but he didn't have the money to get what he wanted.
