Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Sam and the Flowers

Once, a boy named Tim had a big brother named Sam. They loved to play in their wild backyard with tall trees and pretty flowers. One day, Sam said, "Tim, we should study the wild bugs in our yard." Tim and Sam went outside and started to look at the bugs. They saw a big red bug and a small green bug. They talked to each other about the bugs they saw. Tim said, "Sam, I like the green bug more than the red bug." As they studied the bugs, they found a very big and strange bug. It was bigger than all the other bugs they saw. They were scared, but they wanted to know more about it. So, they talked to the strange bug. The strange bug said, "Hello, I am a friendly bug. I can make your wild backyard even more fun." Tim and Sam were happy. They let the friendly bug stay in their yard. The friendly bug made their wild backyard more fun with new games and pretty colors. Tim and Sam learned that even if something looks different or scary, it can still be good and helpful. They had a great time studying the bugs and playing in their wild backyard with their new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Mia. She had a loud voice and loved to sing. One day, Mia found a big red fruit in the kitchen. It was round and shiny. Mia was very happy and wanted to share her joy with others. Mia decided to record her loud singing about the big red fruit. She held a small box that could hear her voice and save it. Mia sang, "I love my big red fruit, it is so round and shiny!" She sang it over and over again, louder and louder. When she was done, Mia played her song for her friends. They all clapped and danced to the loud song about the big red fruit. Mia was so proud of her song and shared the big red fruit with everyone. They all enjoyed it and had a fun day together.

Ben and Lily were twins who shared a room. They liked to play with their toys and read books before bedtime. Sometimes, they would talk about their dreams and what they wanted to do the next day. One night, Ben woke up from a bad dream. He felt scared and sad. He saw a big monster with sharp teeth and claws chasing him in the dark. He wanted to run away, but he could not move. He screamed for help, but no one heard him. He opened his eyes and saw Lily sleeping next to him. He reached out and shook her gently. "Lily, Lily, wake up!" he whispered. Lily rubbed her eyes and looked at Ben. She saw that he was crying and shaking. "What's wrong, Ben?" she asked. "I had a nightmare," Ben said. "It was very scary. I don't want to sleep anymore." Lily hugged Ben and tried to comfort him. "It's okay, Ben. It was just a dream. It's not real. The monster can't hurt you. You're safe with me." She turned on the lamp and showed him their toys and books. "Look, we have our friends here. They will protect us. Do you want to read a story or play a game?" Ben nodded and wiped his tears. He picked up a book with colorful pictures. "Can you read this to me, Lily?" he asked. "Sure, Ben. I like this book. It's about a brave knight and a friendly dragon. They have fun adventures together," Lily said. She opened the book and started to read. Ben listened and smiled. He felt better and less afraid. He forgot about his nightmare and enjoyed the story. They read and played until they felt sleepy again. Then they turned off the lamp and cuddled under the blanket. "Good night, Ben. I love you," Lily said. "Good night, Lily. I love you too," Ben said. They closed their eyes and fell asleep. They did not have any more nightmares. They had sweet dreams instead.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was a very useful dog. He liked to help his friends. One day, he met a little cat named Luna. Luna was lost and hungry. Max wanted to help Luna. Max said, "I will lead you to some lunch." Luna was happy. She followed Max to a big house. Inside the house, there was a nice lady. The lady gave Luna some yummy lunch. Luna ate the lunch and felt much better. After lunch, Max and Luna played together. They became good friends. Max always led Luna to good places. Luna was happy to have such a useful friend. And they lived happily ever after.
