Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Robot

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She had a toy that she loved very much. The toy was a small robot with a big red button on it. Lily played with her robot every day. One day, Lily found out that if she pushed the red button, the robot would increase in size. She pushed the button and the robot grew bigger and bigger. Lily was so happy and surprised. She said to the robot, "Wow, you are so big now!" The robot replied, "Yes, Lily. Thank you for pushing my button. Now we can have even more fun together!" They played all day and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Lily pushed the button again and the robot became small. They both went to bed, excited to play again the next day.

Once upon a time there was an old truck. It was a curious truck and the people who saw it were amazed by it. One day, a boy came up to the truck to take a closer look. He was very curious and he wanted to test it. He hopped in the truck and put his hands on the wheel. He put the truck into gear and stepped on the gas. The truck started up and rumbled away. The boy was very excited! He drove the truck around for a few minutes and then parked it back in its spot. He was so proud of himself for testing the truck. When he hopped out, he had a huge smile on his face. He had tested an old truck and he had done it all by himself.

Once upon a time, there was a reindeer named Remy. Remy was a selfish reindeer. He did not like to share his toys with his friends. One day, Remy's mom told him to bathe. Remy said, "No, I don't want to bathe!" The next day, Remy saw his friends playing in the sun. They were all clean and shiny. Remy wanted to play too, but his friends said, "You are dirty, Remy. You need to bathe first!" Remy felt sad that he could not play with his friends. So, Remy went home and decided to bathe. He became clean and shiny. Then, Remy went back to play with his friends. They were happy to see him clean. Remy learned that being selfish was not good. He started to share his toys and became a better friend.

Once there was a dog called Spot. Spot was a happy dog who liked to chase the wind and spin around in circles. One day, Spot was playing in the park when he saw a leash. Spot decided to play. He started to spin around and around, faster and faster. He loved the way the leash felt in his mouth. Suddenly, Spot felt something yank him back. He had gone too far and the leash was too tight. The leash was hard and he wouldn't come loose. Spot was stuck and he was scared. He cried loudly, but no one came to help him. In the end, Spot was stuck in one place, spinning in circles and unable to move. He never got to play in the park again.

Lily was a girl who liked to part her hair in two. She had two long braids that she tied with pink ribbons. She thought she looked very pretty with her braids. One day, she went to the park with her mom and saw some other girls playing with a ball. She wanted to join them, but they did not like her braids. They said they were too mild and boring. They said she should cut them off or dye them a different color. Lily felt sad and angry. She did not want to change her braids. She liked them the way they were. She told the other girls to leave her alone. She said they were mean and rude. The other girls did not like what Lily said. They decided to teach her a lesson. They waited until Lily was not looking and grabbed one of her braids. They cut it off with scissors and threw it away. They laughed and ran away. Lily felt a sharp pain and a tug on her head. She touched her hair and saw that one of her braids was gone. She screamed and cried. She looked for her mom, but she was too far away. She felt alone and scared. The moral of the story is: You should not judge people by their appearance. You should be kind and respectful to others. You should not hurt people who are different from you. You should be proud of who you are.
