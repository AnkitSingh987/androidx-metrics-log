Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big cardboard box. She loved to play with it every day. She would hide in it, and pretend it was her special house. One day, Lily found a little cat. The cat was deaf, but it was very sweet. Lily wanted to comfort the cat, so she brought it to her cardboard house. They played together, and the cat was happy. Lily and the deaf cat became best friends. They played in the cardboard house every day. The cat felt safe and loved. And so, Lily and the cat lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a big strawberry plant in the garden. It had many red strawberries on it. Tim wanted to get the strawberries, but his hands were dirty. Tim went to his mom and asked, "Can I get the strawberries?" His mom said, "Yes, but first you need to wash your dirty hands." Tim washed his hands with soap and water. Now his hands were clean. Tim went back to the garden and picked the red strawberries. He brought them to his mom. She was happy and said, "Good job, Tim! Now we can eat the yummy strawberries together." They both ate the strawberries and smiled.

One day, a curious little girl named Sally went for a walk by the river. She saw a big box on the ground. Sally wanted to know what was in the box. She said, "I will unpack the box!" Sally opened the box and found a small toy boat inside. She was very happy. Sally said, "I will put the boat in the river and watch it go!" So, she put the boat in the water and watched it float away. As the boat floated down the river, it suddenly grew bigger and bigger. Sally was surprised! The boat stopped and a kind duck came out. The duck said, "Thank you for unpacking me, Sally! I was stuck in that box for a long time. Now we can play together!" Sally and the duck became good friends and played by the river every day.

Once upon a time, in an ancient land, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to walk and play outside. He had a big, old tree in his yard that he liked to sit under. One sunny day, Tim wanted to go for a long walk. He asked his mom if he could go. His mom said yes, but he had to be back before dark. Tim was very happy and started his walk. Tim walked and walked until he found a little dog. The dog was lost and wanted to go home. Tim knew he had to help the dog. Together, they walked back to the dog's house. The dog was so happy to be home. Tim felt good that he helped the dog and went back to his own home, just in time for dinner.

Once there was a boy named Tim. He liked to play with his ball. One day, he saw a big net near his house. Tim wanted to play with the net, but it was too high for him to reach. Tim saw a big box. He thought, "I can use this box to reach the net." So, he put the box under the net and stood on it. Now he could reach the net. Tim was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The box broke and Tim fell down. The net was not fun anymore. It was dull. Tim was sad and hurt. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. She had a big head with clean, shiny hair. One day, Anna went outside to play with her toy cars. She loved to scatter them all around the yard and watch them zoom and race. "Anna, come here!" called her mom from the door. "It's time to clean up your toys and come inside for lunch." Anna looked at her mom and nodded her head. She began to pick up her cars one by one. As she was cleaning, a friendly squirrel came by. It looked at Anna and said, "Hi, can I help you?" Anna smiled and said, "Yes, please! Can you help me pick up my cars?" The squirrel nodded its head and started to help Anna. Together, they picked up all the cars and put them away. Anna thanked the squirrel and gave it a big hug. Then, she went inside to eat lunch with her mom. The squirrel waved goodbye and ran up a tree, happy to have made a new friend.
