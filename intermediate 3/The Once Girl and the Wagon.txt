Category: Intermediate
Title: The Once Girl and the Wagon

There was once a little girl who wanted to have a pet. She begged her mom to let her buy an animal. Her mom said she could, but she had to sell something first. The little girl looked around her room, and saw her old toy wagon that she didn't play with anymore. She took it outside and put it on the sidewalk. A kind neighbor stopped to take a look. He asked her, “How much do you want for this wagon?” The little girl said, “I want to sell it for a pet. Can you give me enough to buy one?” The friendly neighbor smiled and said, “How much does it weigh?” The little girl didn’t know, so the neighbor put it on the bathroom scales. He said, “I can pay you this much for it.” The little girl was very tidy and organized her money to find the right amount. She asked her mom if it was enough, and her mom said, “Yes! Now let’s go get your pet!”

Once upon a time, there was a red coin. The coin was so special that it could burn if it got too hot. One day, the coin got too close to the sun. The sun was so strong that it made the coin burn. It glowed bright red and turned into ashes. The next day, the coin's friends were so sad. They wanted the red coin back. Suddenly, a magical bird flew down from the sky. The bird said that it could make the red coin come back. The bird gathered all of the ashes and blew them sky high. Then, it made a wish that the red coin would be back. The ashes started to sparkle and glimmer. Then, the red coin appeared out of thin air! Everyone was so happy and cheered for the magical bird. From then on, the red coin was safe and all of its friends were very happy.

One day, a smart girl named Lucy and her dog, Max, went on vacation. They went to a big, fun park. Lucy and Max played all day long. They played on the swings, the slide, and in the sand. They were very happy. At lunch time, Lucy said, "Max, can you guess what I have for us to eat?" Max barked and wagged his tail. He was very hungry. Lucy opened her bag and took out two big sandwiches, one for her and one for Max. As they were eating, a funny thing happened. A little bird flew down and took Max's sandwich! Max chased the bird, but he could not catch it. Lucy laughed and said, "Don't worry, Max. I have another sandwich for you." Max was happy again, and they finished their lunch. After lunch, they played some more. Then, they went home. They were tired but happy. They had a great vacation, even with the unexpected bird taking Max's sandwich.

Once upon a time there was an old museum. Inside the walls of the museum were lots of things to see and learn. Every day, a young boy would come to the museum. He loved to mark all the things that he found. He used a big red crayon and marked things like chairs and tables and old books. One day, he got very excited to see something he had never seen before. It was a big horse made out of wood. He was so excited, he marked it with his crayon right away. The museum curator saw what the boy did and asked him to stop. But the boy was so happy with his mark, he stuck out his tongue and ran off. The curator shook his head, but he was happy that the boy had found something so nice inside his old museum.

Once there was a girl named Sue. She was very regular - she always followed her daily routine. Today, it was time for Sue to take a test. She was very excited! She put on her outfit and ran out the door to catch the bus. The bus was almost full when she arrived but she caught the last seat. On the way to school, Sue thought of a plan. When the bus arrived at school, Sue turned to the other passengers and said, "I'm going to catch the test. Who's going to join me?" Everyone said no, but Sue didn't give up. Finally, one little girl said she would join Sue. Together, the two of them ran for the test. They were so happy when they caught it!
