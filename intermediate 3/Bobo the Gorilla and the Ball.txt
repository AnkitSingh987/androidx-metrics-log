Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Gorilla and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a gorilla named Bobo. Bobo was big and strong, but he had a broken toy. His toy was a ball. He loved to play with his ball, but now it was broken. Bobo was very sad. One day, Bobo had a plan. He wanted to fix his broken ball. He looked around the zoo and found a little boy with a new ball. Bobo thought, "If I can take the boy's ball, I can fix my broken ball." So, Bobo waited for the right time to take the ball from the little boy. When the little boy was not looking, Bobo took the ball. The little boy cried and cried. Bobo tried to fix his broken ball with the new ball, but it did not work. Now, both balls were broken, and the little boy was very sad. Bobo's plan did not work, and now everyone was unhappy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim and his mom. Tim loved to play with his toys and make noise all day long. His mom would often say, "Tim, please shut the door and play quietly." But Tim didn't like silence, so he would keep playing loudly. One day, Tim's mom brought home a big, sour lemon from the store. "Tim, I want to show you something," she said. "This lemon is very sour. If you can be quiet and shut the door, I will let you taste it." Tim was curious, so he shut the door and sat down in silence. His mom cut a small piece of the sour lemon and gave it to him. Tim tasted the lemon and made a funny face. "Wow, this is very sour!" he said. From that day on, Tim learned to shut the door and play quietly sometimes. He knew that silence could be good, just like the sour lemon. And Tim's mom was happy that her little boy was learning to be quiet and enjoy the silence too.

Anna liked to wander around the park with her mom. She saw many things that made her happy. She saw birds and flowers and dogs and swings. She liked to run and jump and play. One day, she saw something new in the park. She saw a group of people sitting on mats. They were doing yoga. They were bending and stretching and breathing. Anna was curious. She wanted to try yoga too. She left her mom and wandered over to the group. She found an empty mat and sat on it. She tried to copy what the people were doing. She lifted her arms and legs and made a bridge. She twisted her body and made a pretzel. She lay down and made a star. The people saw Anna and smiled. They thought she was cute and silly. They invited her to join them. Anna was happy. She liked yoga. She liked the people. She liked the park. She looked for her mom and waved. Her mom saw her and waved back. Her mom was proud of Anna. She thought Anna was brave and smart and fun. She walked over to the group and said hello. She asked Anna if she wanted to do yoga with them. Anna nodded. She wanted to do yoga with her mom. She wanted to do yoga with her new friends. She wanted to do yoga in the park.

Once upon a time there was an adorable bear. His name was Tom and he was very curious. One day, Tom went to the woods to explore. He was walking around when he saw something shiny and sparkly! It was a zipper! Tom had never seen a zipper before and he wanted to know what it did. He carefully touched the zipper and heard a click sound. Suddenly, the zipper opened and a bright bow popped out! Tom was so excited that he could barely contain himself. He wanted to play with the bow, so he picked it up and twirled it around. The bow was so light and fluffy that it filled Tom with happiness! Tom had a great time playing with the bow and he kept it with him forever. He loved that bow and everyone knew it. Tom was the most adorable bear in the woods with his shiny bow!
