Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cow and the Pig

Once upon a time, there was a big, red hen. She had a big bag of popcorn. The popcorn was very expensive. She wanted to share it with her friends. The hen went to her friend, the cow. The cow was laying in the grass. The hen said, "Hello, cow! I have popcorn. Do you want some?" The cow said, "Yes, please!" So, they ate the popcorn together. Next, the hen went to her friend, the pig. The pig was also laying in the mud. The hen said, "Hello, pig! I have popcorn. Do you want some?" The pig said, "Yes, please!" So, they ate the popcorn together. They were all happy and full. The end.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there was a humble girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends and learn new things. One day, Lily found a big book. The big book was about history. It had many stories from long, long ago. Lily wanted to read it, but the words were too hard for her. Lily asked her big sister for help. Her big sister said, "I will help you, but first, you must number these toys from one to ten." Lily looked at the toys and tried her best to number them. But she could only count to five. Lily felt sad and the toys were still not numbered. Then, Lily's mom came into the room. She saw Lily was sad and asked, "What is wrong, my little girl?" Lily told her mom about the big history book and the toys. Her mom smiled and said, "I will teach you how to number the toys and read the big book." Lily felt happy again. Lily and her mom spent the day learning to number the toys and read the big book together. They had so much fun! Lily was proud of herself for learning new things. And that is how the humble girl named Lily learned about history and how to number toys from one to ten.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy food to put in their refrigerator at home. Tim saw a big, red apple that he wanted to eat. His mom said, "We need to pay for it first, Tim." At the counter, Tim gave the store man some money to pay for the apple. The store man said, "That is not enough money, Tim." Tim looked sad because he really wanted the apple. His mom said, "It's only fair that we pay the right amount for the apple." Tim's mom gave the store man more money, and they paid for the apple. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" They went home and put the food in the refrigerator. Tim ate his big, red apple and it was very yummy.

Once upon a time there lived a small boy who loved to keep a flame burning. He named it Flicker, and every day he fed Flicker with sticks and twigs. One day, he decided to take Flicker with him on an adventure. The little boy and his flame journeyed through forests, valleys, and rivers. Along the way, they crossed a rough mountain. Suddenly, Flicker started to flicker and fade. The little boy said, "Don't worry Flicker, I'll keep you safe." He spent hours trying to keep the flame burning. Finally, they reached the top of the rough mountain. The little boy looked into the sky and said, "Look! We made it! And we didn't give up." The flame looked brighter and said, "Yes! I knew we could do it."

One day, a little gray cat named Luna went for a walk. The sun was up, and the sky was blue. Luna liked to play with her friends outside. Today, she wanted to find her friend, the bird named Sky. "Sky, where are you?" Luna called out. But all she heard was silence. There was no sound at all. Luna felt a little sad. She wanted to play with Sky. Just then, Luna saw a big box. It had a pretty bow on it. Luna was very curious. She went closer and saw a note. The note said, "Luna, this is for you." Luna was so happy to receive a gift. She opened the box, and there was Sky! "Surprise! I was hiding!" said Sky. Luna laughed, and they played all day long. The gray cat and the bird had a great time, and they were not sad anymore.
