Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Balloon and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big red balloon. It was a very special balloon. It could glow at night. The balloon lived in a park with lots of other balloons. But some balloons were bad. They liked to pop and scare the kids. One day, the glowing red balloon saw a bad balloon near a little girl. The bad balloon wanted to pop and make her cry. The glowing red balloon did not want the little girl to be sad. So, it floated over to the bad balloon to stop it. The glowing red balloon touched the bad balloon. The bad balloon started to glow too! The bad balloon felt happy and did not want to pop anymore. The little girl saw the glowing balloons and smiled. From that day on, the red balloon helped all the bad balloons glow and be good.

Anna loved pink. She had a pink dress, a pink hat, a pink doll and a pink bike. She wanted to wear her pink dress to the party, but her mom said no. Her mom said the dress was too fancy for the party, and she had to wear something else. "But mom, I love my pink dress!" Anna said. "It is so pretty and soft and shiny. Please, can I wear it?" "No, Anna, you can't," her mom said. "The party is at the park, and you will get your dress dirty and torn. You have to wear your jeans and your sweater. They are warm and comfy and easy to wash." Anna was sad. She did not like her jeans and her sweater. They were blue and gray and boring. She wanted to look like a princess, not like a boy. She pouted and crossed her arms. "Mom, please, please, please, can I wear my pink dress?" she begged. Her mom sighed. She knew Anna loved her pink dress, but she also knew it was not a good idea to wear it to the park. She looked at Anna's sad face and had an idea. "OK, Anna, how about this?" she said. "You can wear your pink dress for a little while at home, before we go to the party. You can twirl and dance and pretend you are a princess. But then, you have to change into your jeans and your sweater, and put your pink dress away. Do you promise?" Anna's eyes lit up. She nodded and smiled. "Yes, mom, I promise!" she said. "Thank you, thank you, thank you!" She ran to her room and put on her pink dress. She felt happy and beautiful. She came back to the living room and showed her mom. "Look, mom, look!" she said. "I am a princess!" Her mom smiled and hugged her. "You are a princess, Anna," she said. "A very sweet and smart princess. Now, let's have some fun before we go to the party. Do you want to read a story, or play a game, or sing a song?" Anna thought for a moment. She wanted to do all those things, but she also wanted to wear her pink dress for as long as possible. "Can we do all of them, mom?" she asked. Her mom laughed and nodded. "Sure, Anna, we can do all of them," she said. "But remember, you have to keep your promise. When it is time to go, you have to change into your jeans and your sweater, OK?" Anna nodded and hugged her mom. "OK, mom, I will keep my promise," she said. "But I will always love my pink dress." Her mom kissed her cheek and said, "I know, Anna, I know. And I will always love you."

One day, a loud taxi was driving down the street. The taxi was very fast and made a lot of noise. People in the town did not like the loud taxi because it scared them. One day, the loud taxi was driving too fast and it hit a big rock. The taxi was destroyed. The people in the town were sad because they needed the taxi to go to places. But then, something unexpected happened. A new, quiet taxi came to the town. The people were happy because the new taxi was not loud and scary. The new taxi drove slow and safe, and everyone liked it. The town learned that sometimes, something bad can lead to something good.
