Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Thoughtful Cat and the Track

One day, a thoughtful cat named Tom was walking on a track. He saw a little dog named Max. Max was sad because he was dirty. "Hi Max, why are you sad?" asked Tom. Max said, "I am dirty and I want to be clean." Tom had an idea. He found a spray bottle with water. He sprayed Max with the water. Max was happy and clean now. But then, something unexpected happened. The water made Max grow very big! Max and Tom were surprised. They learned that helping friends can make them grow in many ways.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived near a pretty reef with many colors. He had a lot of friends who liked to play with him. One day, they all decided to go on an adventure to find a new reef. Fin was very excited to go with them. As they swam, they saw many new things. They saw big fish and little fish. They saw plants that moved with the water. But they did not find the new reef. Some of Fin's friends wanted to give up and go back home. But Fin did not want to give up. He wanted to keep going and get to the new reef. So, Fin and his friends kept swimming. They swam up, down, left, and right. And then, they saw it! The new reef was even more pretty than their old reef. It had more colors and more places to play. Fin and his friends were very happy they did not give up. They learned that if you keep trying, you can get to where you want to go.

Once upon a time, a wealthy king lived in a castle. He liked to zoom around the castle in his fast car. He warned his people not to go too close to the car when it was zooming. One day, an adventurous three year old named Billy didn't listen to the king's warning. He ran towards the car when it was zooming. The king shouted at Billy "Don't come too close!" but he was too late. Billy was just about to touch the car when the king quickly grabbed him away. Billy smiled at the king and said "Thank you". The king smiled back and warned him once again to never come too close when the car was zooming. Billy didn't listen and a few days later, he came too close to the car again. The king shouted at Billy and warned him again at not to come too close. This time, Billy listened and thanked the king for warning him. Now, whenever the king zooms around the castle, he always checks if Billy is watching and if he is, he reminds him of the warning.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a huge toy. It was a big, green dinosaur. Tim loved his dinosaur very much. He played with it every day. One day, Tim and his dinosaur went to the park. They played in the sand. Tim had an idea. He wanted to bury the dinosaur in the sand. So, Tim dug a hole and put the dinosaur in it. He covered it with sand. The dinosaur was hiding! Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He dug in the sand to find his toy. He found the dinosaur and gave it a big hug. Tim and his dinosaur played all day. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play sport. Tim's favorite sport was ball. He had a red ball that he liked to kick and throw. One day, Tim's ball was broken. It had a big hole in it. Tim was very sad. He tried to fix the ball, but he could not. He asked his mom to help, but she could not fix it too. Tim did not want to lose his ball, but it was broken. He could not play with it anymore. The ball was thrown away. Tim was very sad and cried. That was the end of Tim's favorite red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy who liked to play. He loved to run around and laugh out loud. His mom often sat on the porch to watch him and make sure he stayed safe. One day, the boy was playing with a long string. He ran around the garden and started to swing around it. He was so careless and careless that he did not realize that the string was tangled around a bush. He kept tugging on the string. But then, suddenly, the string tightened around his neck and he could not breath. He started to scream and cry. His mom heard his cries and rushed to save him. She quickly pointed at him, and the string untangled right away. The boy was safe, but his mom was scared. She hugged him and told him to be careful. She said, "Whenever you play, always pay attention and don't do something without thinking first. Otherwise, it can be very dangerous." The boy learned his lesson, and his mom hugged him tight. They both knew that the careless and irresponsible behavior of the boy could have had a bad ending that day.
