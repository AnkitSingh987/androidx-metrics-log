Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Song

One day, a little girl named Lily found a birdcage in her room. She did not know where it came from. It seemed very odd to her. Inside the birdcage, there was a tiny bird. The bird looked sad and guilty. Lily wanted to help the bird. She opened the birdcage and the bird flew out. The bird was very happy now. It flew around the room and landed on Lily's shoulder. The bird started to sing a beautiful song. Just then, Lily's mom came into the room. She saw the bird and smiled. "Lily, you found the birdcage I hid for your birthday!" she said. "The bird is a magical friend who can sing and talk!" Lily was so surprised and happy. She hugged the bird and her mom. The bird was not guilty after all. It was a special gift for Lily.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy milk for their home. Tim liked to drink milk in the morning. The store was very noisy, with people talking and laughing. As they walked down the aisle, Tim's mom said, "Remember to grab the milk, Tim." But Tim was looking at the toys and didn't hear her mention the milk. He picked up a toy car and showed it to his mom. Suddenly, a big dog ran into the store. Everyone was surprised and scared. The dog was barking loudly, making the store even more noisy. Tim's mom said, "Stay close to me, Tim." The dog ran to the milk aisle and started to drink the milk from a carton. Tim saw this and laughed. He said, "Look, Mom! The dog likes milk too!" Tim's mom smiled and said, "Yes, he does. Now, let's get our milk and go home." In the end, they bought their milk and Tim got his toy car. They left the store, still talking about the unexpected dog who loved milk too.

One day, a little dog named Spike went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to stretch his legs. So, he went to the tree and began to stretch. As Spike was stretching, a small bird flew down and sat on a branch. The bird looked at Spike and felt confused. "Why is this dog stretching by my tree?" thought the bird. Spike saw the bird and said, "Hello, I am just stretching my legs. I like this tree." The bird smiled and said, "That's okay, you can stretch here. My name is Birdy." Spike and Birdy became friends and played together by the tree every day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy loved to scatter seeds around the forest. He would fly high and low, finding the best seeds available. He would pick them up with his beak and scatter them on the ground. One day, Billy found a big pile of seeds under a tall tree. He was so happy! He started to pick up seeds and scatter them everywhere. As he scattered the seeds, he saw his friends, the squirrels, running around and playing with the seeds too. Billy and his squirrel friends had so much fun playing with the seeds. They scattered them all over the forest, making it look very pretty. In the end, they were all tired but very happy. They knew that by scattering the seeds, they were helping the forest grow.

Once there were two friends, Jack and Sam. Jack was always fair and wanted to help people. One day he had an idea and decided to print it out. Jack said to Sam, "I want to give the earth something special. What do you think I should do?" Sam thought for a minute and then said, "Why don't you print something beautiful and give it to the world!" So Jack took a piece of paper, printed on it and put it on his front door. Then he stood back and watched. When people saw the paper, they smiled and thanked him for being so fair. The next day, the paper was gone and in its place was a bouquet of flowers. It was a sign from the world that it appreciated Jack's kind act. From then on, Jack kept printing out special things for the earth.
