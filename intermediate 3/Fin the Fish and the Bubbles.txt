Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Bubbles

One day, a little fish named Fin wanted to swim. He lived in a big pond with many other fish. The pond was very busy. Fin had many friends, but he wanted to explore. Fin saw an exit in the pond. He swam to his friend, Bubbles. "Bubbles, let's swim to the exit," said Fin. Bubbles was scared, but he wanted to go with his friend. They swam to the exit together. They saw a new world outside the pond. Fin and Bubbles were happy they went on an adventure. They swam back to their busy pond and told all their friends.

John heard sirens blaring outside of his house. He peered out the window and saw fireman driving a big red truck. He liked the fireman's uniforms - he always thought they were so cool. John ran outside and asked the fireman, "What's wrong?" The fireman replied, "There's a fire nearby. We're going to rescue some folks." John watched intently as the fireman jumped in the red truck and sped off. He ran back inside and asked his mom what happened. She said, "The firemen are going to rescue people from a fire." Then she added, "It's so brave of them." John felt excited. He knew he wanted to be a fireman one day, just like the ones he'd seen. He decided to stay up late that night and watch out the window. Sure enough, a few hours later, the firemen pulled back up to the house with the red truck. They were carrying a couple of people and looked relieved. The fire had been mild, and no one was hurt. The rescue had been a success. John jumped up and down with joy. He couldn't wait to be a fireman one day and rescue people from fires just like these brave men had done.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird. The bird was very busy. It had to find food for its baby birds. The bird would say "tweet-tweet" as it looked for food. One day, the bird saw a big cat. The cat was hungry too. The cat looked at the bird and said, "I want to eat you!" The bird was scared, but it had to be brave for its baby birds. The bird thought of a plan. The bird said to the cat, "I know where there is more food. Let me show you." The cat followed the bird. They walked and walked, but the cat did not see any food. The cat was very mad. The cat said, "You tricked me!" The cat was so mad that it ate the bird. The baby birds were sad and hungry, and they missed their mom.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely moon. It was big and round and bright. The moon lived up in the sky, far away from everyone. It was all by itself. One day, the moon saw a little star. The star was also lonely, just like the moon. They wanted to be friends, but they were separate. They could not play together. The moon was sad, and the star was sad too. Then, a kind cloud came by. The cloud saw the lonely moon and star. The cloud wanted to help them. So, the cloud moved close and pushed the moon and star together. Now, the moon and the star were friends. They were not lonely anymore. They were happy, and they shined together in the sky.

Once there was a girl named Lucy. She was very popular and had many friends. Every day she would go to the park and play. One day, she decided to go swimming. She put on her suit and hopped in the water. She loved it and stayed in for a long time. When she was done, she remembered that she had left her sack by the pool. She hopped out and ran over to it. But she noticed something strange--someone had taken it! Lucy got very angry! She looked around the park but could not find her sack. She went and told all of her friends what had happened. They all wanted to help and came up with lots of ideas, but Lucy was still sad that her sack was gone. Finally, one of her friends found it! It had been tucked away in the corner of the park. Lucy was so happy and thanked her friend. From then on, she was always sure to keep an eye on her sack while she swam.
