Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Window

Lily and Ben are twins who like to play with their toy vehicles. They have cars, trucks, buses, and trains that they zoom around the floor. Sometimes they share, but sometimes they want the same vehicle and they fight. One day, they hear a loud noise outside. They run to the window and see a big race. Many vehicles are going very fast and trying to be the first. They have bright colors and make vroom-vroom sounds. Lily and Ben are excited and want to watch. "Wow, look at that red car!" Lily says. "It is so fast and shiny. I want it!" "No, I want it!" Ben says. "It is better than your pink bus. Pink is for girls!" They start to argue and pull each other's hair. They don't notice that their mom is behind them. She is not happy. "Stop it, you two!" she says. "You are being very rude and mean. It doesn't matter what color or shape your vehicles are. They are all fun and good. And you should not fight over them. You should share and be kind. That is what makes you happy, not being competitive." She takes them away from the window and makes them sit on the couch. She gives them each a hug and a cookie. She tells them a story about how she and her brother used to play with vehicles when they were little. They learned to take turns and help each other. They had more fun that way. Lily and Ben listen and feel sorry. They say sorry to each other and hug. They decide to play with their vehicles together and not fight. They learn that being competitive is not always good. Being friendly and fair is better.

Once upon a time, there was a big, open park. In the park, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play and have fun in the park. One sunny day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. Tim saw his friends playing with a ball. He ran to them and they all played together. They laughed and played for a long time. After a while, Tim started to feel tired. He let out a big yawn. His friends were tired too. Tim's mom came to the park with a big surprise. She had ice-cream for everyone! Tim and his friends were so happy. They all sat down and enjoyed the yummy ice-cream. After eating their ice-cream, they felt awake and ready to play again. And they played in the open park until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat named Tom. Tom had a big foot that he liked to show off to his friends. One day, he went to play with his best friend, a dog named Sam. While they were playing, Tom said, "Sam, please look at my big foot." But Sam did not look at Tom's foot. Instead, Sam looked up at the sky and saw a bird flying. This made Tom sad because he wanted Sam to see his big foot. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird flew down and landed on Tom's big foot. Tom was surprised and so was Sam. They both laughed and played with the bird. From that day on, Tom, Sam, and the bird became best friends, and they all played together, forgetting about Tom's big foot.

One day, Mommy and Sally were in the living room. Mommy pointed at the fan and said to Sally, "Look, that's a fan." Sally thought for a moment, then smiled. She said, "Mommy, can I design a fan?" Mommy smiled back and said, "Sure, why not?" So Sally got her crayons and paper and started designing the fan. When she was finished, her fan looked really cool. It had green wings and a yellow body. Mommy said, "That looks great, Sally!" But then Sally saw a bug crawling on the fan. She said, "Mommy, the bug is disgusting!" But Mommy said, "That's okay, it's natural for bugs to be around." Sally said, "Okay." Mommy then put the fan on the wall and Sally was happy. She said, "Mommy, I like my fan!" Mommy smiled and said, "Me too!"
