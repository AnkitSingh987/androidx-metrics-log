Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bear

One day, a little boy named Tim found a wet toy bear in the park. The toy bear had a big hole and needed a stitch. Tim took the toy bear home to fix it. At home, Tim asked his mom for help. "Mom, I need to fix this bear. Can you help me stitch the hole?" His mom said, "Of course, Tim! Let's fix the bear together." They stitched up the hole, and the bear looked good as new. The next day, Tim went back to the park with his fixed toy bear. Suddenly, the bear started to talk! "Thank you, Tim," said the bear. Tim was very surprised but happy. They became best friends and played in the park every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big white armchair in his house. He asked his mom, "Can I stay on the armchair?" His mom said, "Yes, you can stay on the armchair, but only for a little while." Tim climbed up on the white armchair and sat down. He felt very happy and comfy. But then, something unexpected happened. The armchair started to talk! It said, "Hello, Tim! I am a magic armchair." Tim was very surprised. He asked the armchair, "What can you do?" The magic armchair said, "I can take you on a fun ride around the room!" So, Tim held on tight and the white armchair started to fly around the room. They went up and down, and side to side. Tim laughed and had a great time. When the ride was over, Tim said, "Thank you, magic armchair!" The armchair said, "You're welcome, Tim. Come back and visit me again soon!" Tim promised to visit the armchair again and went to tell his mom about his amazing adventure.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a letter on the floor. She did not know what it said. She showed the letter to her mom. Her mom read the letter and smiled. "This letter is from your grandma," she said. "She will come to visit us soon." Sue was happy. She loved her grandma. She remembered that her grandma made tasty cookies. Sue thought about the cookies and her mouth watered. She could not wait for her grandma to come. A few days later, grandma came to visit. Sue ran to hug her. Grandma smiled and said, "I have a surprise for you." She took out a box from her bag. Inside the box were the tasty cookies Sue loved. Sue was so happy. She knew her grandma would bring the cookies. They all sat down and enjoyed the tasty cookies together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a tiny, harmless bug on the ground. Tim looked at the bug and said, "Hi, little bug! What are you doing on the earth?" The bug looked up at Tim and said, "I am looking for my friend, the ant. Have you seen him?" Tim thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, I saw an ant by the big tree." So, Tim and the bug went to the big tree to look for the ant. When they found the ant, the bug was very happy. The ant looked at Tim and said, "Thank you for helping my friend find me. We can all play together now!" Tim, the bug, and the ant played all day long. They had so much fun on the earth, and Tim was happy he made new friends. From that day on, Tim knew that even the smallest, harmless bugs could be great friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his red bike. Every day, he would ride up and down the street, feeling the wind in his face. One sunny day, Tim saw a big sign that said "Wonders for Fun". He was curious, so he rode his bike to the place. When he got there, he saw many toys and games available. Tim was very happy. Tim played with the toys and games all day. He made new friends and shared the wonders with them. When the sun went down, Tim rode his bike back home, excited to tell his mom and dad about his amazing day.
