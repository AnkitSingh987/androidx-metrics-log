Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bear and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a cold winter, there was a big bear. The bear was rough and lived in a cave. The bear liked to gaze at the snow outside his cave. One day, he saw a little bird shivering in the cold. The bear felt sad for the bird and wanted to help. He let the bird come into his cave to get warm. The bird was very happy and they became friends. They played together and had fun. One day, the bird found a magic seed. The bird and the bear planted the seed outside the cave. The next day, a big tree grew! The tree was not like other trees. It had warm leaves and fruits that kept them warm all winter. The bear and the bird were surprised and happy. Now, they could enjoy the winter without being cold.

One day, a boy named Tim had a scary toy. It was a big, green monster. Tim loved to play with his scary toy every day. On Wednesday, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play at his house. Sam saw the scary toy and wanted to play with it too. Tim was not sure if he should lend his scary toy to Sam. He thought Sam might break it. But Sam asked nicely, so Tim said yes. They played with the scary toy all day and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. While they were playing, the scary toy started to talk! It said, "I am not scary, I am nice!" Tim and Sam were very surprised. They did not know that the toy could talk. From that day on, Tim and Sam played with the talking toy every Wednesday. They were happy that they shared the toy because now they had a new friend.

One day, a cop named Jim was walking in the park. He saw a boy with a big, disgusting bug in his hand. The boy was scared and wanted to send the bug away. Jim knew he had to help the boy. "Let me help you, little boy," said Jim. The boy smiled and gave the bug to Jim. Jim took the bug and walked to the big tree. He thought the bug would be happy there. But as he put the bug on the tree, the bug started to change. It was a big surprise! The disgusting bug turned into a beautiful butterfly! The boy and Jim were so happy. They watched the butterfly fly away into the sky. The boy said, "Thank you, Jim!" And from that day on, Jim knew that even disgusting things can become beautiful.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy who loved to explore and discover new things. One day, he wanted to explore an old pit in the nearby forest. He thought the pit looked unique, so he wanted to find out what secrets it could hold. The boy made his way down the grassy sides of the pit, hoping to find something special. But as he reached the bottom, it was all darkness. He decided to take a pause and try to look around. Suddenly, he heard a big noise and something big raced past him! Startled and scared, the little boy ran away. He ran as fast as he could, back up the pit and out of the forest. He never knew what he had seen and he never returned to the unique pit again.

One day, a fast rabbit and a slow turtle wanted to play catch. They both went to the park. The rabbit was very good at the game. He had a lot of speed. The turtle was not so good. He was very slow. The rabbit said, "I can catch better than you!" The turtle felt sad. He wanted to be good at catch too. He thought the rabbit was not being nice. The turtle said, "I may be slow, but I am smart. I have an idea. Let's play catch with my adorable little friend, the mouse." The rabbit agreed. The mouse was very small and quick. It was hard for the rabbit to catch the mouse. The turtle was very happy. He said, "See? You are not the best at catch now." The rabbit saw that he was not always the best. He said sorry to the turtle. They all played catch together and had a lot of fun. The rabbit learned to be nice and not to make fun of others.
