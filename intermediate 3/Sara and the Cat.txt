Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and the Cat

Sara was a little bird who liked to sing and fly. She had a nest in a big tree with her mom and dad. But Sara was not happy with her nest. She thought it was too small and too plain. She wanted to improve it and make it bigger and prettier. One day, she saw some shiny things on the ground. They were red and blue and yellow and green. Sara thought they would look nice on her nest. She flew down and picked them up with her beak. She did not see the grumpy cat who was hiding behind a bush. The cat saw Sara and jumped out. He wanted to catch her and eat her. He ran after her and tried to bite her tail. Sara was scared and flew away as fast as she could. She dropped the shiny things and they fell on the ground. The cat was angry and hissed. Sara reached her nest and told her mom and dad what happened. They hugged her and said, "We are glad you are safe, Sara. You don't need to improve your nest. It is perfect for us. We love you just the way you are." Sara smiled and said, "I love you too, mom and dad. You are right. My nest is cozy and warm. I don't need shiny things to make it better. I only need you." She snuggled with them and sang a happy song. The grumpy cat heard her and went away. He did not like happy songs.

One day, a boy named Tim found a green kite in his room. Tim was very happy. He wanted to fly the kite outside. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, can I fly my green kite today?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful and don't go too far." Tim went outside and started to fly his kite. The kite went up and up in the sky. It was so high, Tim could not believe it. He was very proud of his green kite. Then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew very hard, and the kite flew away from Tim's hand. Tim was very sad. He tried to find his green kite, but it was gone. He went back home and told his mom what happened. His mom said, "I am sorry, Tim. Next time, listen to me and don't let the kite go too high." Tim learned that he should listen to his mom and not be too proud.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Fido. Fido loved to play and eat. One day, he found a shiny thing on the ground. It was a screw. Fido thought it was a toy and wanted to play with it. Fido picked up the screw with his mouth and started to run around. He was very happy. But then, Fido tried to eat the screw. It was not good to eat. It hurt his mouth and made him feel bad. Fido's mom saw what happened and took the screw away. She told Fido not to eat things that are not food. Fido learned that it is important to be careful with what we put in our mouths. Sadly, Fido's mouth hurt for many days. The story teaches us to only eat food and not to play with things that can hurt us.

Once there was a cat, who was walking in the park. Suddenly, it heard a fierce growl. The cat shivered and ran away to hide behind a desk. “What was that?” asked the cat. The desk answered back in a deep, scary voice, “Help me. There is something fierce outside.” The cat shivered again. It saw a big, fierce bear come into the park. The bear was growling loudly. The cat was so scared, it couldn’t move. “Help me!” said the desk “Please!” But the cat was too scared to help. The bear walked closer and closer. Suddenly the bear lunged at the cat and ate it up in one bite. The desk shivered and quietly said, “That was so fierce.”

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful house. In the house, there was a big fireplace. Every day, a girl named Lily would clean the fireplace. She liked to make it look nice and shiny. One day, while Lily was cleaning the fireplace, she found a pretty stone. The stone was shiny and bright. Lily was very happy to find the stone. She put it in her pocket and kept cleaning. Later that day, Lily showed the stone to her mom. Her mom looked at the stone and smiled. She told Lily that the stone was magic. When Lily held the stone, she could make the fireplace clean itself! Lily was so surprised and happy. Now, she could play more and still have a clean fireplace.
