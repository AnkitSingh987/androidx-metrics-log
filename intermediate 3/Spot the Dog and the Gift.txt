Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a smelly dog because he liked to play in the mud. Spot's owner, a little girl named Jane, always had to give him a bath after playing outside. One day, Jane's mom asked her to deliver a gift to her friend's house. Jane said yes, but she wanted to take Spot with her. Her mom said it was okay, but only if Spot was clean. So, Jane gave Spot a bath and they went on their way. As they walked, Spot saw a big mud puddle. He wanted to play in it, but he remembered that he had to be clean to help Jane deliver the gift. So, Spot walked around the puddle and stayed clean all the way to Jane's friend's house. Jane and Spot delivered the gift, and her friend was very happy. On their way back home, Jane let Spot play in the mud puddle as a reward for being a good helper.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a black belt that he loved to wear every day. He liked to show it to his friends and they thought it was very cool. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. He saw a man working very hard to fix a broken swing. Tim went up to the man and asked, "Can I help you work?" The man smiled and said, "Sure, Tim. You can hold my tools with your black belt." Tim wrapped his black belt around his waist and put the tools in it. He helped the man work on the swing. They fixed it together, and all the kids at the park were happy to play on the swing again. The unexpected thing that happened was that Tim's black belt had a secret pocket. Inside the pocket, Tim found a shiny gold coin. He showed it to the man and they were both surprised. The man told Tim, "You can keep the coin because you helped me work today." Tim learned that helping others is a good thing to do and can bring you surprises.

Once upon a time, there was an old lady who lived in a small house. She had a pet cat named Lung. Lung was a very good cat. The old lady loved Lung very much. One day, Lung got sick. The old lady was very sad. She did not want Lung to be sick. She wanted Lung to be happy and run around again. So, the old lady decided to pray. She prayed to make Lung feel better. After the old lady prayed, Lung started to feel better. Lung was not sick anymore. The old lady was very happy. She knew her prayers helped Lung. From that day on, the old lady and Lung lived happily together.

One day, a bald man named Tom went for a walk in the park. He saw a big tree with many sticks on the ground. Tom dared to pick up a stick to play with it. He felt happy and excited. Tom found a small dog near the tree. The dog was cute and friendly. Tom dared to throw the stick for the dog to catch. The dog ran fast to get the stick and brought it back to Tom. They played together for a long time. As the sun began to set, Tom and the dog were tired but happy. They said goodbye to each other and went home. Tom put the stick by his door to remember the fun day. Every time he saw the stick, he thought of the dog and smiled.

One day, a little bunny named Lucky went to the market. Lucky loved to hop, so he hopped all the way there. The market had many things like apples, carrots, and toys. Lucky was very happy. At the market, Lucky saw a big, round ball. He wanted to play with it. Lucky hopped to the ball and gave it a big push. The ball rolled fast and hit a big pile of apples. The apples fell down and rolled everywhere. Lucky was scared. Just then, a nice man came to help. He picked up the apples and smiled at Lucky. He said, "You're a lucky bunny!" Lucky felt better. The man gave Lucky some apples and a hug. Lucky hopped all the way home, happy with his new apples and a new friend.
