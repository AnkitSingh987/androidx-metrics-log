Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little cat named Lucy. Lucy loved to play outside. One day, she saw a big, soft bed near a tree. Lucy was very careful, but she wanted to lay on the bed. While Lucy was laying on the bed, she felt a small itch on her back. She tried to scratch it, but the itch did not go away. Lucy was sad and went back to her house to tell her mom about the itch. Lucy's mom knew just what to do. She had seen a special cream in the store that could help with itches. She went to the store, bought the cream, and put it on Lucy's back. The itch went away, and Lucy was happy. She knew that her mom would always help her when she needed it.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a mailman named Tom. Tom was a little careless. Sometimes, he would miss putting the mail in the right house. The people in the town did not like this. One day, Tom went to deliver the mail. He saw a little girl named Lily sitting on the steps of her house. She was sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily said, "I miss my friend. She moved away and I have not heard from her." Tom felt bad for Lily. He wanted to help her. He looked in his bag and found a letter for Lily. It was from her friend! Tom had missed it before because he was careless. He gave it to Lily, and she was so happy. She thanked Tom and went inside to read her letter. From that day on, Tom was more careful with the mail. He did not want anyone to miss their important letters again. The people in the town were happy with Tom, and he was proud to be their mailman.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was frightened of a big dog named Rover. Every day, Rover would tease Fluffy when she tried to eat her food. One day, Fluffy had an idea. She made friends with a bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was very smart and could fly high in the sky. Fluffy asked Chirpy to help her with Rover. Chirpy had a plan. When Rover came to tease Fluffy again, Chirpy flew down and took Rover's food. Rover was sad and hungry. He said sorry to Fluffy for teasing her. Fluffy and Chirpy shared their food with Rover, and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily had a toy bear named Ted. Ted was very light and had big ears. Lily loved to play with Ted all day long. One day, Lily and Ted went to the market with Lily's mom. At the market, they saw a big scale to weigh things. Lily's mom weighed the fruits that she bought. Lily had an idea. She put Ted on the scale to see how much he weighed. Ted was very light, just like a feather. While they were at the market, Lily saw a boy who didn't have any toys. The boy looked sad. Lily thought about her toy bear, Ted. She knew that sharing is caring. So, Lily gave her light toy bear, Ted, to the sad boy. The boy's face lit up with a big smile. Lily felt happy too. From that day on, Lily learned that sharing with others can make everyone happy. And the town learned from Lily's kindness, making the world a better place for all.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big drain on the side of the road. He was curious about the drain and went closer to look. Tim was frightened because he thought something might come out of the drain. Just then, a small cat came out of the drain. The cat was scared too. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he picked it up. The cat was happy that Tim saved it from the drain. Tim took the cat home and gave it some food. The cat ate the food and started to feel better. Tim's mom said they could keep the cat. Tim was so happy that he jumped and turned around. Now, Tim and the cat are best friends.
