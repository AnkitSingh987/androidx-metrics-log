Category: Intermediate
Title: Suzie and the Boat

Suzie dreamed of becoming a great sailor one day. She lived near a harbor, and she loved to watch the boats go in and out. Every day she listened to the stories of the experienced sailors who came to dock their ships. One day, one of the sailors said something that made Suzie think: "If you want to be a successful sailor, you must have healthy dreams." Suzie was not sure what this meant, so she asked the sailor. He said, "It means that you must take care of yourself, get plenty of exercise, and make sure to stay away from bad things. If you do that, you will be bigger and stronger and be able to succeed." Suzie followed the sailors' advice. She worked hard and took care of herself, and before she knew it, she was ready to set sail! On her first voyage, Suzie set course for the harbor near her house. As soon as she arrived and docked her boat, she realized that the sailor's advice had been true. With a healthy body and healthy dreams, she had succeeded!

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very obedient. He always listened to his mom and dad. They taught him the value of being a good dog. Spot had a worry, though. He was scared of the big and loud cars on the street. One day, Spot went for a walk with his mom. They were having fun and playing in the park. Suddenly, Spot saw a big ball across the street. He loved to play with balls, so he wanted to get it. He forgot about his worry and the value of listening. Spot started to run to the ball, but then he heard his mom's voice. She said, "Stop, Spot!" He remembered to be obedient, and he stopped right away. Just then, a big and loud car went by very fast. Spot was safe because he listened to his mom. The unexpected thing was that a nice man in the park saw what happened. He gave Spot a new ball to play with. Spot learned that being obedient and listening to his mom and dad was very important. The moral value of the story is to always listen to the ones who love and care for you.

Paul and his mum were in the city one day. Paul could hear lots of traffic noises and feel the hot sunshine on his face. He looked up at the tall buildings and found them very exciting! "Look at all these tall buildings, mum!" Paul said. "Yes, they look very deep," Mum replied. Then Paul heard a loud sneeze. He looked around and saw an old man with a big hat. Paul ran up to the old man and said "Good day sir! Why did you sneeze so loudly?" The old man smiled and said: "Ah, I was too close to the traffic and it made me sneeze. Always be careful when near such loud noises!" Paul thanked the old man and ran back to his mum. He gave one last look at the deep buildings and then followed Mum home.

One day, Tom and his mom went to the park. The sun was shining and the flowers were starting to bloom. They saw a wide tree and sat under it to rest. Tom found a hammer on the ground and picked it up. "Look, Mom! I found a hammer," Tom said. His mom looked at it and smiled. "Let's see if we can find who it belongs to," she said. They walked around the park asking people if they lost a hammer. Then, they saw a big, sad bear sitting by a broken swing. The bear was trying to fix the swing but couldn't. Tom and his mom went to the bear and asked, "Is this your hammer?" The bear looked at the hammer and smiled wide. "Yes, that's my hammer! Thank you!" the bear said. Tom and his mom helped the bear fix the swing. They all became friends and played together in the park. The bear was not sad anymore and they all had a fun day.

Once, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily had a pink jug that she did not like. She would say, "I hate my pink jug!" every day. Lily's mom and dad would try to make her like the jug, but Lily still did not like it. One day, Lily's mom put a big, yummy cake in the pink jug. Lily saw the cake and thought, "Maybe the pink jug is not so bad." She took a big bite of the cake and it was so good! Lily started to like the pink jug a little bit more. The next day, Lily found a cute, little cat inside the pink jug. The cat was so nice and soft. Lily loved the cat and she loved the pink jug now too. The pink jug was not so bad after all. It brought Lily a yummy cake and a new friend.
