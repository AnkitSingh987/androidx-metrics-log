Category: Intermediate
Title: Mama and the Park

Mama was out playing in the park with her two little girls. "Look, girls," she said, pointing to a large brown pebble. "Let's play with this pebble!" The two girls jumped up and down with excitement. The older one picked up the pebble and held it in her hand. It was warm and heavy. "It's incredible!" she exclaimed. Mama smiled and took the pebble from her daughter. She placed it in the center of the park and said, "Let's see who can get the pebble first. Let's play!" The girls clapped their hands and ran towards the pebble. Mama watched them as they played. Time went by quickly, but soon it was time to go home. The pebble was still there in the park, waiting for the next child to discover it and play with it.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a rough rock. She saw an image of a big bird on the rock. Sue was very happy. She ran to her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom! I found a rock with a bird on it!" Sue said. Tom looked at the rock and smiled. "Let's go to the park and lay the rock there. Maybe the bird will come to life!" Tom said. At the park, they put the rock on the ground. They waited and waited. Then, a big bird came from the sky. The bird looked at the rock and smiled. Sue and Tom were so happy. The bird had come to life because they laid the rock in the park.

One day, a bird named Tim was in his nest. He was a weak bird and could not fly well. He saw his friend, Sam, the squirrel, on the ground. "Hi, Sam!" Tim called out. "Can you help me? It seems I am too weak to fly." Sam looked up at Tim and said, "Yes, I can help you. I will find some food to make you strong." Sam went to look for food. He found a big nut and brought it to Tim's nest. Tim ate the nut and felt stronger. He tried to fly again, but still could not fly well. Just then, a butterfly named Lily came by. She saw Tim and said, "You need to flap your wings faster to fly well." Tim tried flapping his wings faster, and he could fly! It was unexpected, but Lily's advice helped Tim. Now, Tim, Sam, and Lily played together every day.

Once there was a mommy and daddy who wanted to provide a special surprise for their three year old daughter. They decided to create a maze in their backyard. Daddy worked hard to build it with special colors and shapes. The little girl was so excited when she saw the maze! She ran around the big and crazy maze, going in and out of all of the turns. After a while, she emerged from the maze with a huge smile on her face. Every day, the little girl played in her maze and found all sorts of crazy adventures. Mommy and Daddy watched with smiles as their daughter had fun in the maze they had provided.

Once upon a time, there was a brave zebra who liked to run. Every day, he ran through the grassy fields, enjoying the fresh air. Then one day, he got a surprise. As he was running, he saw something strange in the distance. It was a lion! The zebra was scared, so he started to scream. Meanwhile, a brave little boy heard the noise from his window. He looked out and noticed the zebra running away from the lion. Without hesitating, the brave boy jumped out of his window and ran towards the zebra. When he arrived, he saw the zebra standing there trembling. The little boy shouted at the lion, "Go away, you big mean lion!" The lion was so scared, he ran away into the forest. The zebra was so happy, he jumped up and down. Then the little boy and the zebra ran back to safety. The two were now friends forever. Whenever they ran together, the little boy would shout, "Be brave like a zebra!"

Once upon a time, there was a glowing star far away in the night sky. Its light was so bright, it could be seen all around the world. One night, a little girl named Clara saw the star's light. She thought it was so beautiful, she just had to get closer. So, Clara made a wish and flew up to the star. As she got close, the star's light was very warm. She waved hello, and the star glowed even brighter in return. Clara saw that the star was a big shrimp, and it was very polite. It said, "Hello! What brings you up here?" Clara told the shrimp about how beautiful its light was and how it made her feel. The shrimp smiled, then said, "I'm so glad you're here and I will dance for you." And so, Clara and the star shrimp danced in the night sky. They twirled and spun around and around until she was too tired to go on. Then Clara thanked the star shrimp for the wonderful dance and flew back home with a smile. The glowing star watched until she was out of sight, then went back to its place in the night sky.
