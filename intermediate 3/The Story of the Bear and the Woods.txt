Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a big, dirty bear. The bear lived in the woods. He liked to play in the mud all day. One day, the bear found a stick. He wanted to write his name with the stick. He tried to write on the ground, but it was too hard. The bear saw a little girl. She had a piece of paper and a pen. The bear asked the girl if he could write his name. The girl said yes and gave the bear the paper and pen. The bear wrote his name and was very happy. The little girl and the bear became good friends and played together in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to help her mom cook. One day, they were making a tasty sauce together. Amy's job was to pour the sauce into the pot. Amy was very excited to pour the sauce. But as she poured, something unexpected happened! A little cat jumped onto the table and knocked the sauce out of Amy's hand. The sauce fell all over the floor! Amy was sad, but her mom had an idea. They made a new tasty sauce together and poured it into the pot. This time, they made sure the cat was outside. When they finished, they all sat down to eat. The sauce was even more tasty than before, and they all laughed about the silly cat.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty little boy named Jimmy. He was playing outside with his toy gun and shooting everywhere. His mum was not happy and told him, "Jimmy, no shooting!" Jimmy did not listen though and kept shooting and making loud noises. His mum was getting angry and she said, "Jimmy, if you don't stop shooting, I will take away your medal!" Jimmy knew that his medal was very special to him, so he stopped shooting. His mum was pleased and as a reward for being such a good boy, she gave him a medal. Jimmy was so happy. He went to his friends and showed them his medal. They were so impressed and told him that he was a good boy and they said, "Jimmy, you should not be naughty anymore!" Jimmy agreed and promised that he would never be naughty again. From then on, Jimmy was always a good and well behaved boy. He was so proud of his special medal!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a kite that he loved to play with. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom to fly his kite. The sky was very blue, and the wind was just right. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's kite and said, "I like your kite, can I play too?" Tim said, "Yes, we can play together!" Tim and Sue took turns flying the kite high in the sky. They were having a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind quit blowing, and the kite fell down. Tim and Sue were sad. They thought their fun was over. But then, a big dog came running over and picked up the kite in his mouth. The dog wanted to play too! Tim, Sue, and the big dog played together, running and chasing the kite on the ground. They all had a great day at the park.

Bobby and his mommy were walking in the field. Bobby was feeling a bit helpless and asked his mommy for help. "Mommy, why do we have to study?" Bobby asked. "We are studying so that when you are older, you can help yourself and others," Mommy explained. Bobby didn't understand, so Mommy tried to explain slightly better. "Studying means using your mind to figure out things. It is a way to learn about the world," said Mommy. Bobby felt a little less helpless and started playing in the field. Mommy watched with a kind smile on her face. Bobby enjoyed looking at the flowers, the grass and the birds in the sky. He thought that studying could be fun too.

Lily and Ben are playing in the garage. They have a lot of toys and books there. They like to make noise and have fun. "Look, Lily, I have a cute car!" Ben says. He shows her a red car with big wheels and a shiny horn. "It can go fast and beep!" "Wow, Ben, that is a cute car!" Lily says. She has a pink doll with long hair and a dress. "Can I play with it?" "Sure, Lily, you can play with it. And I can play with your doll!" Ben says. He gives her the car and takes the doll. "She is so pretty and soft!" They play with each other's toys for a while. They make them talk and move and laugh. They are very happy. Then they hear a loud noise. It is their mom. She is opening the garage door. She has a big smile on her face. "Hi, kids, I'm home!" she says. "What are you doing in the garage?" "We are playing, mom!" Lily and Ben say. They run to her and hug her. "We have cute toys!" "I see that, you have cute toys!" mom says. She looks at the car and the doll. "And you are sharing them, that is very nice!" She picks them up and kisses them. "You are such good kids! I love you!" "We love you too, mom!" Lily and Ben say. They clap their hands and giggle. "You are the best mom!"
