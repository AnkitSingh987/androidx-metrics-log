Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bug and the Grass

Once upon a time, in a big park, there lived a funny little bug. The bug liked to play in the green grass all day long. He had many friends in the park, and they all loved to play together. They played hide and seek, and they laughed a lot. One sunny day, the bug and his friends decided to play a new game. They wanted to see who could number the most blades of grass. They thought it would be a fun game, and so they started counting. One, two, three, they counted and counted. After a while, the bug and his friends got very tired. They realized that there were too many blades of grass to count. They decided to stop counting and just play together instead. They learned that sometimes it's better to have fun with friends than to try to do something that is too hard. The moral of the story is: It's more important to enjoy time with friends than to focus on things that are too difficult.

One day, a girl named Sally wanted to help her mom. Her mom needed to deliver a sweater to a friend. The sweater was tight and warm. Sally was happy to help. Sally and her mom walked to the friend's house. They saw a cat and a dog on the way. Sally said, "Look, Mom! A cat and a dog!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, they are cute!" When they got to the friend's house, Sally gave the sweater to the friend. The friend said, "Thank you, Sally!" Sally felt proud to help her mom. They walked back home together, happy and smiling.

Once upon a time, there was a cute kitten who loved to explore. But one day, the kitten found a dark corner to play in and didn't come out. The other animals looked and called for her, but she stayed put. They knew they had to help the kitten, so they hatched a plan. They lured her with some yummy treats and finally the kitten scrambled out of the corner. But then she ran right into the boss animal on the farm and the boss animal roared, “You must surrender!” The kitten was so scared, she wanted to hide again, but the other animals saved her. Everyone surrounded the boss animal and cheered for the kitten, until the boss animal gave up. The kitten felt safe, and so relieved. From that day on, the kitten was never scared, and everyone was happy she was safe.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing with a big round ball. They were happy and ran after the ball. The cat was fast, and the dog was strong. They took turns to push the ball. It was important to them because it was their favorite toy. Suddenly, the ball went into a small house. The cat and the dog were sad. They wanted their ball back. They tried to open the door, but it was shut. They did not know what to do. Then, a tiny mouse came out of the house. The cat and the dog were surprised. The mouse had the ball! The mouse was strong too. The cat, the dog, and the mouse played with the ball together. They were all happy and had a lot of fun. The cat and the dog learned that even small friends can do important things.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with many bananas. Tim was hungry, so he prayed, "Please let me have a banana." A nice monkey heard Tim and gave him a banana. Tim said, "Thank you, monkey!" Tim took a bite of the banana. "Oh no, this banana is sour!" Tim said. The monkey laughed and said, "I'm sorry, I gave you the wrong one. Here, try this sweet banana instead." Tim took a bite of the new banana and it was sweet. He was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The sour banana turned into a big, sweet cake! Tim, the monkey, and all their friends at the park shared the cake and had a fun day.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play outside in the park. Today, they see a big puddle of water. They want to splash in it. "Look, Ben, a puddle!" Mia says. "Let's go and splash!" "OK, Mia, but we need our boots. The water is wet and cold," Ben says. They run to their moms and ask for their boots. "Here you go, Ben. These are your blue boots. They are smooth and shiny," Ben's mom says. "Thank you, mom. I like my blue boots. They are smooth and shiny," Ben says. "Here you go, Mia. These are your pink boots. They are smooth and soft," Mia's mom says. "Thank you, mom. I like my pink boots. They are smooth and soft," Mia says. Ben and Mia put on their boots and run to the puddle. They jump and splash and laugh. They enjoy the water and the sun. They enjoy playing with each other. "Ben, you are funny. You make big splashes," Mia says. "Mia, you are funny. You make small splashes," Ben says. They splash some more and then hug. They are happy. They are friends.
