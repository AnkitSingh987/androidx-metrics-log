Category: Intermediate
Title: The House and the Door

Once upon a time, there was an old house. Every day, a small girl named Lucy walked around the house. One day, she saw a door that was shut. She wanted to open it, but her mommy stopped her. "No, Lucy," her mommy said. "It is too dangerous. We must keep it shut." Lucy was very curious. Why was the door shut? What was behind it? She wanted to know, so she asked her mommy. "Mommy, why is the door shut?" she asked her mommy. "That door is a special way," her mommy said. "It goes to a very dangerous place, so we must keep it shut to stay safe." Lucy listened carefully. She said goodbye to her mommy and went to play. She would never forget the door that was shut.

Paul was excited! It was his first time going to the park to search for treasures. His older brother had shown him how to search the ground, and he couldn't wait to start looking. Paul asked his mom if he could bring a bag to collect the treasures he found. She said yes, and he was ready to go. When Paul arrived at the park, he saw lots of other kids searching the ground. They were all so competitive! He was worried that he wouldn't find anything before they did. Paul began searching around the park. He searched the ground everywhere he could. He saw some of the other kids finding cool treasures, and he wished he could find some too. Just when he was about to give up, he finally found something. It was a small rock with a pretty flower on it. Paul was so excited about his find! He had searched so hard and it felt so great to finally get a treasure. He couldn't wait to show his mom what he had found.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce lion who lived in a big forest. The lion loved to play in the sunshine. Every day, he would run and jump with his friends. One day, the lion and his friends decided to play a new game. They wanted to part the tall grass with their paws. They took turns running and pushing the grass to make a path. The lion was very good at this game. He made the biggest path of all. His friends cheered and clapped for him. They played in the sunshine all day long, and they were all very happy.

Ben and Lily were playing with their dog Spot in the garden. They liked to throw a ball and watch Spot run after it and bring it back. Sometimes Spot would not give the ball back and they had to grab his collar and tickle him until he dropped it. They laughed and hugged Spot, who licked their faces. One day, they saw a big box in the corner of the garden. It was brown and had a lot of words on it that they could not read. They wondered what was inside. They asked their mom, who was busy in the kitchen, if they could open it. She said yes, but be careful and don't make a mess. Ben and Lily ran to the box and lifted the lid. They saw a lot of colorful things inside. There were papers, paints, brushes, stickers, scissors, glue, and more. They were very happy and excited. They decided to make something creative with the things in the box. They took out some papers and paints and started to draw and paint. They made pictures of Spot, their mom, their house, and their favorite things. They were having so much fun that they did not notice that Spot was curious too. He came to the box and sniffed it. He saw something shiny and round. It was a ball of foil. He thought it was a toy and he wanted to play with it. He jumped into the box and grabbed the ball of foil with his mouth. He ran out of the box and started to roll and chase the ball of foil. Ben and Lily heard Spot's barking and looked up. They saw him running around with the ball of foil. They thought it was funny and cute. They wanted to join him and play with him. They put down their papers and paints and ran after Spot. They called his name and tried to grab his collar. But Spot was too fast and too happy. He dodged them and ran away. They chased him around the garden, laughing and shouting. They did not see that Spot was heading towards the pond. The pond was deep and had a lot of water. Spot did not know that. He saw his reflection in the water and thought it was another dog. He wanted to play with the other dog. He ran towards the pond and jumped in. Ben and Lily saw Spot jump into the pond. They were scared and worried. They knew that Spot could not swim. They ran to the pond and saw Spot splashing and struggling in the water. He dropped the ball of foil and tried to get out. He looked at them and cried for help. Ben and Lily did not know what to do. They wanted to help Spot, but they could not swim either. They looked around and saw a big stick. They had an idea. They grabbed the stick and ran to the pond. They reached out the stick to Spot and told him to bite it. Spot saw the stick and bit it. Ben and Lily pulled the stick and Spot out of the pond. They hugged him and dried him with a towel. They were very happy and relieved. They told Spot that he was a good boy and that they loved him. Spot was happy and grateful. He licked their faces and wagged his tail. He was sorry for taking the ball of foil and jumping into the pond. He did not mean to scare them or make them sad. He just wanted to have fun. He learned his lesson. He decided to play with his own ball and stay away from the pond. He also decided to share his ball with Ben and Lily. They were his best friends and he loved them too.
