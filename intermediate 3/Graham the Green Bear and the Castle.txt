Category: Intermediate
Title: Graham the Green Bear and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a big green bear named Graham. He loved exploring and playing outside in the sunshine. One day, he decided to go to a special land with lots of green hills. He thought it would be fun to explore. As he walked up to the hill, he noticed something dull and grey. It was a giant castle hidden among the trees. With a sigh, he knew he had found something special. He also knew that he must enter the castle. Filled with curiosity, Graham opened the heavy door and stepped inside. He was amazed to find a room full of dull walls and objects. There was barely any color in this place. Suddenly, a loud voice boomed from inside the room. “It’s time for you to surrender!” it said. Graham was scared, but he was also brave. He took a deep breath and said, “I am not afraid. I surrender.” Then, a magical thing happened — the walls and objects started to sparkle and shine in a rainbow of colors. Graham smiled and hugged his teddy bear. He had done the right thing.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big hole in the ground. It looked dark and deep. "Let's open it and see what's inside," Ben said. He was curious and brave. "No, Ben, don't do that. It's dangerous. Mommy said we should not go near holes in the ground. They can have bugs or snakes or rats," Lily said. She was careful and scared. But Ben did not listen to Lily. He ran to the hole and kicked the dirt around it. He wanted to open it more and more. He did not see the sign that said "Warning: Gas Pipe". Suddenly, there was a loud boom. The hole exploded and fire came out. Ben screamed and fell down. He was hurt and burned. Lily cried and ran to him. She called for help. But it was too late. Ben was gone. The hole had taken him away. Lily was sad and alone. She wished she had stopped him. She wished he had listened to her. She wished they had played somewhere else. The hole was crazy and bad. It had ruined their fun and their lives.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, were playing in the yard. Tim had a big toy truck that he liked to load with small cups. He would fill the cups with dirt and then dump them out fast. Spot liked to watch Tim play and sometimes he would help by digging holes for the dirt. "Spot, help me load the cups on my truck," said Tim. Spot wagged his tail and started to dig a hole. Tim filled the cups with dirt and put them on the truck. They were having lots of fun. Suddenly, as Tim was loading the last cup, he saw something shiny in the dirt. "What's this?" Tim asked. He picked it up and saw it was a shiny key. Tim and Spot looked at each other, wondering what the key could open. They decided to look for whatever the key would fit in. This was the start of a new, fun adventure for Tim and Spot.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big blue sea. He had many friends, like the crab and the starfish. One day, Fin saw a shiny thing in the water. He wanted to take it, but he knew it was not his. He felt a little guilty, but he took it anyway. Later, Fin's friend, the crab, was sad because he lost his shiny thing. Fin felt regret for taking the shiny thing. He knew he should give it back to the crab. So, he swam to the crab and said, "I am sorry, I took your shiny thing. I feel guilty." The crab was happy to have his shiny thing back. He forgave Fin, and they continued to swim and play in the big blue sea. And Fin learned that it is always better to do the right thing.
