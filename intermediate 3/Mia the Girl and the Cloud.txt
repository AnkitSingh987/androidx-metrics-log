Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Cloud

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play outside. She saw her friend Tom gazing at the sky. Mia asked, "What are you looking at, Tom?" Tom said, "I see big, thick clouds up there." Mia and Tom wanted to make a picture of the clouds. They took paper and glue to make it. They put the glue on the paper and stuck cotton to it. The cotton looked like the thick clouds in the sky. When they finished, they showed their moms. Their moms were happy and said, "Great job!" Mia and Tom felt proud of their cloud picture. They gazed at it and smiled.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very anxious because he had to go on a big adventure. He knew he needed a shield to protect him. So, Tim went to find his friend, Sam. "Sam, can you help me find a shield?" Tim asked. Sam said, "Yes, I will help you, Tim." They both looked and looked until they found a big, strong shield. Tim was happy and thanked Sam. Together, Tim and Sam went on the adventure. They walked and walked until they found a big tree. The tree would not yield and let them pass. Tim used his shield and pushed the tree. The tree moved, and they could go on. Tim and Sam were very happy and continued their adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a fairy who loved to play in the garden. He was happy and never scared. One day, the fairy was picking flowers when he noticed a big, red rose. He was so excited that he jumped down to pick it. Suddenly, a big loud noise frightened him. The fairy was very scared so he ran away in tears. He looked back to see a giant monster coming right after him! The fairy cried harder and harder and said, "Please don't hurt me! I'm only a fairy!" But the monster didn't care, and it chased the fairy until it caught him. It tore the fairy into pieces, and the garden was never the same again.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a great big toy box. Lily loved her toys. One day, her toys began to quarrel. They did not want to share the toy box. Lily was sad. She wanted her toys to be happy. She thought of a plan. Lily took out a big, soft blanket. She put the blanket on the floor. Lily placed all her toys on the blanket. Now, the toys were not in the toy box. They stopped their quarrel. The toys were happy on the blanket. But then, something unexpected happened. The toys became friends and wanted to go back in the toy box together. Lily was happy to see her toys getting along. She put them back in the toy box. The toys were happy to share the great big toy box. And Lily and her toys lived happily ever after.

One day, a big dog and a small cat went to play near a pond. They both liked to splash in the water. The dog would jump in and make a big splash, while the cat would dip her paw in and make a small splash. They laughed and played together. Then, a bird flew by and saw the two friends playing. The bird wanted to join in, but she was scared of the water. She did not like the sight of it. The dog said, "Come play with us, bird!" The bird shook her head and said, "I am sorry, I am scared of the water." The dog and the cat wanted to help the bird. They thought of a plan. They found a big leaf and put it in the water. The bird stood on the leaf and they all splashed together. The bird was not scared anymore. They all played and had fun. In the end, the bird learned that it is okay to be scared, but with the help of friends, we can face our fears. The dog, the cat, and the bird became best friends and played together every day. And they all learned that when we help each other, we can do anything.
