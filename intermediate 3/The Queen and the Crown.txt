Category: Intermediate
Title: The Queen and the Crown

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a kind queen. She had a big castle and many friends. The queen loved to play with her friends in the garden. One day, the queen met a little girl named Lily. Lily asked, "Can I play with you, queen?" The queen smiled and said, "Of course, Lily! I promise we will have lots of fun." The next day, they played in the garden again. They laughed and had a great time. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the queen's crown far away. The queen and Lily looked for the crown, but they could not find it. Lily said, "Don't worry, queen. I will find your crown. I promise." The queen smiled and thanked her. Lily looked and looked for the crown. And then, she found it! The crown was on a little bird's head. The bird had thought it was a nest. Lily and the queen laughed and played with the bird. The queen was so happy to have her crown back and a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time, in a big house, there lived a little dog named Timmy. Timmy felt helpless because he could not find his favorite treat. He looked under the bed, in the kitchen, and even in the yard. Timmy was very sad. One sunny day, Timmy met a friendly cat named Kitty. Kitty said, "Why are you sad, Timmy?" Timmy replied, "I can't find my treat. I feel helpless." Kitty wanted to help Timmy and said, "Let's look for your treat together!" Timmy and Kitty searched the house again. They looked in the living room, and there it was! Timmy's treat was hiding behind the big chair. Timmy was so happy that he wanted to celebrate. "Thank you, Kitty! Let's celebrate and share my treat!" Together, they enjoyed the treat and played all day. Timmy was not helpless anymore because he had a new friend, Kitty. From that day on, Timmy and Kitty were the best of friends and always helped each other. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, he heard a sound coming from the big tree. "What is that sound?" Spot thought. Spot looked up and saw a little bird in the tree. The bird was singing a happy song. Spot said, "Hello, bird! I like your song. Can you guess what I like to play with?" The bird looked at Spot and said, "Is it a ball?" Spot jumped up and down. "Yes, you are right! I love my ball!" The bird flew down from the tree and landed next to Spot. They played with the ball together. Spot would throw the ball and the bird would fly to catch it. They laughed and played all day long. As the sun began to set, the bird said, "I have to go home now. I had so much fun playing with you, Spot!" Spot smiled and said, "I had fun too! Come back tomorrow and we can play again!" The bird flew back to the tree and Spot went home, excited for another day of fun with his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a modest girl named Lily. She had a big imagination. Lily liked to imagine all kinds of things. She imagined she was a princess or a superhero. Sometimes, she imagined she was a teacher. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom. She saw a butterfly land on her hand. She imagined that the butterfly was her friend. They played together in the park. The butterfly stayed on her hand all day. When it was time to go home, the butterfly flew away. Lily was sad, but she knew she could always imagine more friends. She waved goodbye to the butterfly with her hand. Lily was happy because she had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Jane. Jane was very busy. She liked to play with her toys and run around in the park. One day, Jane was playing with her ball when she saw a dog. The dog looked at Jane and said, "Can I come play with you?" Jane was surprised because dogs don't talk! But she said, "Yes, come play with me!" They played all day and had lots of fun. But then, the dog said, "I have to go now." Jane was sad, but the dog told her, "Don't worry, I will come back." And just like that, the dog turned into a bird and flew away. Jane knew she made a special friend and couldn't wait to play with the dog-bird again.
