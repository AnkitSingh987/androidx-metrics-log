Category: Intermediate
Title: The Queen and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a kind queen. She lived in a big castle. She loved to watch people perform songs and dances. One day, a little girl came to the castle. She wanted to perform for the queen. The girl danced and sang a pretty song. The queen was very happy. The queen was grateful for the girl's performance. She gave the girl a big hug and a yummy treat. They became good friends and had lots of fun together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They brought a big tent to play in. Tim was very excited to run and play with his friends. While Tim was running, he saw a foolish dog. The dog was barking and chasing its tail. Tim laughed and said, "Silly dog, you can't catch your tail!" The dog stopped and looked at Tim. Then, it ran to the tent and went inside. Tim followed the dog into the tent. Inside, they played a fun game of catch. The dog would run and catch the ball, then bring it back to Tim. They played for a long time and had lots of fun. Finally, it was time to go home. Tim said goodbye to his new friend, the foolish dog. "Bye dog, see you next time!" he said. Tim and his mom left the park, and Tim couldn't wait to come back and play again.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Tom. He wanted to wear a patch. He went outside his house and tried to find one. But the patch he found was too small. Tom wanted to find a bigger patch, so he kept walking. He saw a nice patch in the garden of an old lady's house! He tried to reach it, but he was too small. So he asked the old lady if he could have it. She said yes, and gave him the patch. Tom was very happy and thanked her. The patch was very big and very nice. Tom went home and tried to put the patch on his shirt. But he didn't know how. So he asked his mom for help. His mom put the patch on his shirt. Tom was so happy that he had a nice patch on his shirt. He showed it to everyone and said, 'Look what I found!'

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a noisy little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and sing songs all day. One day, Lily saw a big purple ball in her yard. She ran outside to play with it. As she played, a friendly bird flew down and said, "Hi, Lily! Can I play with you?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, bird! Let's play together!" They played with the purple ball all morning, laughing and having fun. After playing, they got hungry. Lily and the bird found some yummy berries to eat. Lily picked one up and said, "Let's taste them!" The berries were sweet and juicy. They ate until their tummies were full. Then, they went back to playing with the purple ball, happy and full of energy.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl called Lily. Lily especially loved playing dress up and liked to have fun pretending to be a princess. One day, she was playing in her garden wearing her very delicate pink hat. She was leaning against the fence, watching a butterfly fly from flower to flower. Suddenly, she heard a voice behind her. It was her mother. ‘Lily, be careful not to lean too much on the fence,’ her mother said. ‘It looks weak. What if it breaks?’ Lily looked at the fence again and saw that it was indeed delicate. Lily ran to her mother and hugged her, thankful for her warning. From then on, Lily was always careful not to lean on delicate things, so that everything could remain safe. The moral of this story is to always be careful and not to lean on delicate things.

Once upon a time, there was a hot sun in the sky. It was so hot that a little boy named Timmy stayed inside with his mom. Timmy's mom was a patient lady who always took care of him. One day, Timmy looked out the window and saw a bird trying to settle in a tree. The bird was tired from flying in the hot sun. Timmy said, "Mom, can we help the bird?" Timmy's mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can give the bird some water." They took a small bowl and filled it with water. They put the bowl under the tree for the bird. The bird drank the water and felt better. The bird was happy and settled in the tree. Timmy and his mom went back inside their house. They were happy they could help the bird on such a hot day.
