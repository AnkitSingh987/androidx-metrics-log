Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Mia and the Fish

Ben and Mia were twins who liked to fish with their dad. One day, they went to the lake with their dad and brought two poles and some worms. Dad gave one pole to Ben and one pole to Mia and said, "Be careful and have fun." Ben and Mia put worms on their hooks and threw them in the water. They waited and waited, but no fish came. Ben got bored and said, "This is no fun. I want to go home." Mia said, "No, let's keep trying. Maybe we will get a fish soon." Ben did not listen to Mia. He threw his pole on the ground and ran to the car. He said, "I don't want to fish anymore. I want to play with my toys." Dad said, "Ben, that is not nice. You should be patient and share with your sister. Fishing is not only about getting fish, it is also about spending time with your family and enjoying nature." Mia stayed with her dad and kept fishing. She was happy to be with her dad and to see the birds and the flowers. She said, "Dad, I like fishing with you. It is fun and relaxing." Dad said, "I'm glad you do, Mia. You are a good girl and a generous sister. You are not selfish like Ben." Soon, Mia felt a tug on her pole. She pulled hard and saw a big fish on her hook. She was very excited and said, "Dad, look! I got a fish! It is so big and shiny!" Dad said, "Wow, Mia, that is a great fish! You did a good job. I'm very proud of you." Mia smiled and said, "Thank you, dad. Can I share my fish with Ben? Maybe he will like fishing more if he sees my fish." Dad said, "That is very kind of you, Mia. You are a generous sister. Yes, you can share your fish with Ben. Maybe he will learn from you and be more patient and grateful next time." The moral of the story is: Be patient and generous, and you will be rewarded.

Once there was a little boy called Jimmy who loved to play with his toys. One day, Jimmy's mum asked him to help her tidy the living room. Jimmy liked helping his mum, so he decided to get to work. 'Can you help me scatter these jugs?' She asked, pointing to four jugs on the floor. Jimmy nodded eagerly and grabbed a jug. He started to carry it around the room and 'scatter' it wherever there were available spaces. As he did this, Jimmy started humming a happy tune to himself. Once Jimmy had finished, he looked around the room with pride. He had scattered the jugs very neatly and tidied the whole room. His mum smiled and hugged him. 'Thank you for helping out Jimmy. You did a great job!'

Once upon a time there was a bean. The bean was restless and didn't know what to do. Everywhere it looked and everywhere it went, there just seemed to be more and more beans. The bean decided to ask the other beans why it felt so restless. It asked, "Why am I so restless?" None of the other beans had any answers. Thankfully, a wise old bird flew by and saw the bean's situation. He flapped his wings and said, "Maybe you feel restless because you haven't done anything!" The bean was so glad to hear that. That night, it ate a yummy meal and then went off exploring in the garden. The bean was so happy and busy. It spent days flying around, chasing butterflies, sleeping in the sun and eating ripe berries. The bean was finally content and no longer restless!

One day, a bright bee went for a march. The sun was hot and the sky was blue. The bee liked to fly and look at the flowers. As the bee marched, he saw a big tree. The tree had many green leaves. The bee wanted to see what was on the tree. So, he flew up and up. He saw something he did not expect. The tree was not a tree! It was a big, green hat. A tall man was wearing the hat. The man was happy to see the bee. The bee and the man became friends. They marched together and had a fun day.
