Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball and run in the park. One day, Max was sad because his back hurt. His friend, a kind cat named Lily, saw Max and wanted to help. Lily said, "Max, I promise to make you feel better." Lily knew how to give a massage. She used her soft paws to rub Max's back gently. Max started to feel better and wagged his tail happily. Later, they found a bitter lemon on the ground. Max and Lily tried to eat it, but they didn't like the taste. They laughed and made a funny face. Max's back felt better, and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. They played in the park every day. Sue liked to comb Spot's hair to make him look nice. One day, it was very cold outside. Spot and Sue went to the park to play. They saw a big puddle. The puddle had ice on it. Spot wanted to play on the ice. Sue said, "Spot, be careful!" But Spot did not listen. He jumped on the ice and started to slide. Sue laughed and clapped her hands. Suddenly, the ice began to crack. Spot was scared. Sue shouted, "Freeze, Spot!" Spot stopped moving and looked at Sue. Sue carefully walked to the edge of the ice and grabbed Spot's collar. She pulled him off the ice, and they both were safe. They went home and Sue combed Spot's hair. They were happy and warm together.

Once upon a time, there was a big castle. Inside the castle lived a king and queen. They had a bell that would ring when someone came to visit. One day, a little boy came to the castle. He wanted to play with the bell. He pulled the rope and the bell rang very loud. The king and queen heard the bell and came out to see who was there. When the king saw the little boy, he was angry. He told the boy not to ring the bell again. The little boy was sad, but he said sorry to the king. The queen gave the boy a hug and they all became friends. From that day on, the little boy promised not to ring the bell unless someone was visiting the castle.

Once upon a time, there was a big stadium. It was a very impressive place where people played games. One day, the sun was shining brightly in the sky. A little boy named Tim went to the stadium with his mom and dad to watch a game. While they were at the stadium, Tim saw a shiny ball on the ground. He said, "Mom, look at that shiny ball!" His mom said, "Yes, Tim, it's very shiny. Let's go see it." They walked over to the shiny ball and picked it up. Just then, something unexpected happened. The shiny ball started to talk! The ball said, "Hello, I am a magic ball. I can make your wishes come true." Tim, his mom, and his dad were very surprised. They wished for a fun day at the stadium, and the magic ball made it happen. They had the best day ever.

One day, a big and impressive aeroplane flew in the sky. It was very high and fast. A little boy named Tim saw the aeroplane and wanted to fly too. He told his mom, "I want to fly like the aeroplane!" His mom smiled and said, "You can pretend to fly, Tim!" So, Tim ran around the yard with his arms out like wings, making plane sounds. He was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bug flew into Tim's mouth, and he accidentally bit it. "Yuck!" Tim said, spitting it out. He didn't want to bite the bug. But after that, Tim felt like he could really fly. He knew it was just pretend, but it made him feel even more like the impressive aeroplane in the sky.

Once there was a little boy named Peter. He was three years old and very smart! One day he took a walk with his mom to the street. He saw a big ball rolling down the street in front of them. Peter was so excited. “Mom can I roll the ball too?” asked Peter. “Yes, of course you can,” his mom answered with a smile. So Peter rolled the big ball ahead of him down the street. He was so happy and had so much fun! He rolled it faster and faster, over and over, until they were both too tired to carry on. “That was really fun,” said Peter. His mom nodded in agreement. “Yes, you were very smart with that ball! Now let’s go home and get some rest.” So they both headed home, with Peter still smiling from the fun he had rolling the big ball down the street.
