Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Castle

Sara and Ben were playing with their blocks. They wanted to build a big castle with towers and walls and a gate. They worked hard and helped each other. They put the blocks on top of each other and made shapes and patterns. "Look, our castle is almost done!" Sara said. "We just need a flag for the top." "I have a red cloth we can use," Ben said. He ran to his room and came back with the cloth. He tied it to a stick and put it on the highest tower. "There, now it looks like a real castle!" Sara and Ben were very proud of their castle. They clapped their hands and cheered. "Yay, we did it!" they said. Mom and Dad heard the noise and came to see what they were doing. They saw the castle and smiled. They were happy that Sara and Ben played nicely and shared their blocks. They also clapped their hands and praised them. "Wow, what a beautiful castle you made!" Mom said. "You are very creative and clever." "And you are very obedient and kind," Dad said. "You followed the rules and cleaned up your mess." Sara and Ben hugged Mom and Dad. They felt good and loved. They learned that building a castle was fun, but building a family was even better.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little red bird named Bob. Bob loved to fly around and sing happy songs. One day, he saw an impressive vine hanging from a tall tree. Bob had a plan to play on the vine. Bob called his friend, a small brown squirrel named Sam. "Sam, come see the vine! Let's play!" said Bob. Sam was excited and ran to the vine. They took turns swinging on the vine, laughing and having fun. After they played, Bob and Sam sat on a branch, tired but happy. They looked at the vine and smiled. "That was the best plan, Bob!" said Sam. The two friends continued to play in the forest, making more plans and having more fun adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play in his room with his toys. One day, he saw a hidden door behind his big toy box. Tim was very curious. He asked his mom, "Can I open the hidden door?" His mom did not know about the door. She looked at it and nodded. "Yes, you can open it, but be careful," she said. Tim was very excited to see what was behind the door. Tim opened the door slowly. It was dark inside. He saw a long cable on the floor. He picked up the cable and started to pull it. Suddenly, the lights in the house went off. Tim and his mom were scared. They did not know what to do. The cable was important, and they should not have touched it. Now, they had no light in their house, and they were sad.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. He loved to test things. One day, he found a grape. The grape was flexible, like a rubber ball. Tim was very happy and wanted to play with it. Tim took the grape to his friend, Sam. They played with the grape all day. They threw it, bounced it, and rolled it. The flexible grape was so much fun. But then, something bad happened. The grape broke. It was not so flexible anymore. Tim and Sam were very sad. They could not play with the grape anymore. The fun day had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his big pillow like a horse. One day, Tim saw an empty box in his room. He had an idea. He wanted to ride his pillow horse into the box and pretend it was a secret cave. As Tim rode his pillow horse towards the empty box, he met his friend, Sally. "What are you doing?" asked Sally. "I'm riding my pillow horse to the secret cave," said Tim. Sally wanted to join, so she grabbed her own pillow and they both rode their pillow horses into the empty box. Inside the box, it was dark and scary. Tim and Sally tried to be brave, but they were not happy. They heard a loud noise and both started to cry. The box fell over, and they were still sad. They never played in the empty box again.
