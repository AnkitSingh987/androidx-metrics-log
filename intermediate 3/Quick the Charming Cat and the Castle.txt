Category: Intermediate
Title: Quick the Charming Cat and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a charming little cat named Quick. Quick was very fast and loved to play. One sunny day, Quick went to the park to play with his best friend, a dog named Buddy. As they played, Quick and Buddy saw a big tower of blocks. They wanted to play with the blocks, too. Quick said, "Let's build something big with the blocks!" Buddy agreed and they started to build. They built a big castle with the blocks and were very proud of their work. Just as they finished, a strong wind blew through the park. The wind was so strong that it started to destroy their castle. Buddy said, "Oh no, our castle!" Quick had an idea. He used his speed to run around the castle very fast, creating a wind that pushed against the strong wind. The winds fought, and Quick's wind won. Their castle was saved, and Quick and Buddy were happy. They continued to play, knowing that they could face any challenge together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went with his mom to the white store. They wanted to find a toy for Tim's friend, Lily. The store was big and full of fun things to play with. Tim saw a big, white teddy bear and said, "Mom, can we get this for Lily?" His mom looked at the bear and said, "Yes, that is a nice toy for Lily." But when they went to pay, Tim's mom could not find her money. They looked and looked, but the money was lost. Tim and his mom felt sad because they could not buy the white teddy bear for Lily. They went home with no toy and had a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little boat. It had a big sail and loved to go fast on the water. One sunny day, the boat wanted to race with its friends. It knew that it needed to be very fast to win. The boat saw a big rock on the shore. It had an idea! It thought that if it could dry its sail on the rock, it would go faster. So, it went to the rock and laid its sail on it. The sun was hot, and the sail became very dry. The boat was excited to race with its friends. It put the dry sail back on and started the race. It went so fast, faster than ever before. The boat won the race, and all its friends cheered. The cheerful little boat was very happy, and it knew that the dry sail had helped it win.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very intelligent. She loved to read books and play with her toys. One day, Sue found a big, red ball in her room. She was so happy and wanted to play with it. As Sue played with the ball, it bounced high into the air. The ball hit her thumb and it hurt a little. Sue did not cry but she knew she needed to be more careful. She decided to thank her thumb for being so strong and helping her play. Sue hugged her thumb and said, "Thank you, thumb, for being so good to me." She felt better and continued to play with her big, red ball. Sue knew that even though her thumb was small, it was very important. She was grateful for her thumb and her intelligent mind that helped her learn and have fun every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a beautiful couch. The couch was very soft and cozy. It was a special couch because it could talk! One day, a little boy named Tim came into the room. He saw the beautiful couch and wanted to sit on it. He asked the couch, "Can I sit on you?" The couch replied, "Yes, you can sit on me, Tim!" Tim sat on the couch and they became good friends. They would talk and laugh together every day. The beautiful couch always replied to Tim's questions and made him very happy. And they lived happily ever after.
