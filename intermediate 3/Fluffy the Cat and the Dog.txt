Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Cat and the Dog

One day, a little cat named Fluffy wandered away from her home. She walked and walked, and soon she was lost. Fluffy felt scared and wanted to go back home. Fluffy saw a big house and went inside. In the house, there was a smelly stove. Fluffy wanted to find food, but the smell made her nose hurt. She did not like the smelly stove. A big dog named Spot lived in the house. Spot saw Fluffy and barked loud. Fluffy was scared and ran away fast. She found her way back home and was happy. From that day on, Fluffy never wandered far from home again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tim. Tim was a very impatient dog. He liked to run, jump, and play all day long. One day, Tim's mom asked him to help her load some toys into a big box. Tim wanted to play, but he knew he should help his mom. So, Tim helped his mom load the toys into the big box. It was hard work, but they did it together. After they finished, Tim's mom said, "Thank you, Tim! Now you can go play." Tim was so happy to play with his friends. After a long day of playing, Tim was very tired. He knew it was time for a nap. He found a soft, warm spot and lay down. As Tim took his nap, he dreamed of all the fun he had with his friends. When he woke up, he was ready for another day of playing and helping his mom.

One day, a nervous ostrich named Ollie went to the park. He saw a big tree with many leaves. Ollie liked the tree and wanted to play with the leaves. Ollie said to his friend, "I want to snap the leaves from the tree. Can you help me?" His friend, a small bird, said, "Yes, I can help you, Ollie!" They worked together to snap the leaves. Ollie was happy, and his friend was happy too. They played with the leaves all day long. The nervous ostrich was not nervous anymore.

One day, a gray cat went out to play. The wind was strong, and it blew the cat's red ball far away. The cat did not want to lose its ball, so it ran fast to catch it. The cat was very happy when it found the ball. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew even harder, and the cat started to fly! The cat was scared at first, but soon it found that flying was fun. It flew high and low, and even did a flip in the air. After a while, the wind stopped, and the cat fell down. But it did not get hurt. The cat landed on a soft bed of grass. The cat was very happy that it did not lose its ball and that it had a fun adventure. It ran back home to tell its friends all about it.

Once upon a time, there was a big cow named Lucky. Lucky had a friend, a little bird named Tiny. They lived on a nice farm and played all day. One day, while they were playing, Lucky started to lean on a fence. The fence broke and Lucky got scared. Tiny told Lucky not to worry. They went on an adventure to find help. They walked and walked, then they saw a man. The man was cooking a steak. Lucky and Tiny were very hungry, so they asked the man if they could have some. The man said yes, but first, they had to help him fix the fence. Lucky and Tiny agreed. They all worked together to fix the fence. When it was done, the man gave them a big piece of steak to share. Lucky and Tiny learned that helping others can make good things happen. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a big, red ball went bounce, bounce, bounce down the street. It was a sunny day, and the ball was very happy. It saw a little girl with a cookie. The cookie was big and yummy. The little girl saw the ball and said, "Hi, ball! Do you want some of my cookie?" The ball did not talk, but it bounced up and down. The little girl knew that the ball wanted some cookie. She was very generous and broke the cookie in half. She gave one half to the ball. The ball and the little girl played together all day. They shared the cookie and had lots of fun. They were very happy because they had each other and the yummy cookie. The generous little girl and the bouncing ball became best friends.
