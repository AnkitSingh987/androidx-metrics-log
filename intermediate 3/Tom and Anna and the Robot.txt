Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Anna and the Robot

Tom and Anna were friends. They liked to play with toys. One day, they found a big box of screws. They wanted to make something with them. "Let's unite the screws and make a robot!" Tom said. "Yes, that sounds lovely!" Anna said. They took the screws and tried to put them together. They used a hammer and a wrench. They made a head, a body, and two arms for the robot. "Now we need legs," Tom said. "I have an idea," Anna said. "Let's use our legs!" She took two screws and tried to poke them into her shoes. Tom did the same. They thought they looked like robot legs. "Look, we are robots now!" Anna said. "Beep boop beep!" Tom said. They walked around the room, making robot noises. They were having fun. But then, something bad happened. The screws in their shoes hurt their feet. They started to bleed. They cried out in pain. "Ow ow ow! The screws are hurting us!" Anna said. "Help help! We want to take them off!" Tom said. But they could not take them off. The screws were stuck in their shoes. They could not walk. They could not run. They could not play. They were very sad. They wished they never found the screws. They wished they never tried to make a robot. They wished they were just friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the mall with his mom. They walked around and saw many stores. Tim liked the mall because it had lots of toys and yummy food. His mom liked it too because she could buy things for their home. In one store, Tim saw a poor little dog. The dog was sad and alone. Tim asked his mom, "Can we help the dog, please?" His mom said, "Yes, let's take him home and make him happy." Before they left the mall, Tim's mom had to lock their car. Tim watched her and learned how to lock and unlock the car. He was very proud. They put the dog in the car and went home. Now, the dog was happy, and Tim had a new friend.

Alice had a hammer. She wanted to smash something, but she did not know what. She looked around but nothing around seemed special. Then suddenly, she saw something unknown. It was a rock. She grabbed the hammer and smashed the rock. Little pieces fell all around her. She smiled. She then realized the rock was not so unknown - it was full of tiny, beautiful crystals! She was excited. She put the crystals in a special box and kept them safe. Alice had something special now, and she realized how important that was!

Once there was a girl called Sally, who was three years old. She had a dream that one day she would become a wise, kind adult. One day, Sally was watching a movie with her parents when, suddenly, there was an emergency. Everyone had to run outside quickly. Sally's mom said, “Run, Sally, run!” And so Sally ran as fast as she could. She felt scared and confused and knew she needed to be brave and wise. Finally, the emergency was over and Sally and her family were safe. Sally was relieved and proud that she had been wise enough to do what her mom said. From that day on, Sally remembered her dream and promised she would try to be as wise as she could.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a furry cat named Luna. Luna loved to speak with her friends, the birds, the dogs, and the mice. They all lived happily in their little space in the town. One day, a new cat came to town. He was big and scary. Luna and her friends were afraid of him. They didn't want to speak to the new cat. They stayed in their space and didn't come out to play. But Luna was a kind cat. She thought that maybe the new cat was just lonely. So, Luna went to speak to him. She found out that he was just sad because he had no friends. Luna invited the new cat to join her and her friends in their space. Soon, the new cat became friends with everyone. They all played together and had fun in their space. The town was happy again. The moral of the story is to always be kind and speak to others, even if they seem scary at first.
