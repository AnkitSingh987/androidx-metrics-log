Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Max

One day, a little boy named Tim went to school. His teach was a nice lady named Miss Lisa. Tim was happy to learn new things. At school, Tim met a rough boy named Max. Max was not nice to Tim. He said, "You can't play with us, Tim!" Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Tim told Miss Lisa about Max. Miss Lisa said, "Max, we are nice to our friends at school." Max said, "I am sorry, Tim." They all played together and had fun. Tim and Max became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a rich king. He had a big, sharp sword. The king was very kind. One day, a small boy came to the king. He told the king a truth. The king was happy to know the truth. The king gave the boy a gift for telling the truth. The boy was very happy. They both became good friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was an old man who wanted to borrow something special for a festival. He was very old and his body was heavy, so he knew he wouldn't be able to carry it very far. The old man asked his friend if he could borrow something from him, and his friend said yes! He borrowed a bright red wagon. He was so happy and could hardly wait to take it to the festival. He arrived at the festival, with the bright red wagon in tow. Everyone was amazed at how beautiful it was and how heavy the old man was carrying it! Everyone wanted to borrow the wagon but the old man said no. He said it was his special wagon and he was never going to lend it out again. The old man enjoyed the festival so much, and brought the bright red wagon home with him. He always remembered the day he borrowed the wagon and brought it to the festival. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had a great big ball made of metal. He loved to play with his ball all day. One day, Max saw a small hole in the ground. He thought it would be fun to put his ball in the hole. But the hole was too small for the ball. Max had an idea. He would dig the hole bigger so his ball would fit. Max started to dig and dig. He began to struggle because the ground was very hard. Max was very tired, but he did not give up. He knew he could make the hole bigger for his great big ball. After a long time, Max finally made the hole big enough for his ball. He pushed the metal ball into the hole and it fit perfectly. Max was very happy and proud of himself. He had learned that even when things are hard, if you keep trying, you can do great things. And from that day on, Max and his metal ball had even more fun playing together.

Tom and Lily like to play catch with their ball. They throw the ball to each other and try not to drop it. Sometimes they run and jump to catch the ball. They have fun and laugh a lot. One day, they see a big plane in the sky. It makes a loud noise and flies very fast. They stop playing and look up. They wonder who is in the plane and where it is going. "Wow, look at that plane!" Tom says. "It is so big and shiny!" "I want to fly in a plane someday," Lily says. "It must be fun to see the clouds and the birds." "Me too," Tom says. "Maybe we can be pilots when we grow up. Pilots are the ones who fly the planes. They wear special clothes and hats and talk to other pilots on the radio." "That sounds cool," Lily says. "But I want to be a different kind of pilot. I want to fly a rocket to the moon and see the stars and the aliens." Tom thinks for a moment. He likes the idea of flying a rocket, but he also likes the idea of flying a plane. He does not know which one he wants to be more. "Maybe we can be both," he says. "We can fly planes and rockets and see many different things. We can be the best pilots ever!" Lily smiles and nods. She likes Tom's idea. She thinks they can do anything they want if they work hard and dream big. They hug each other and then go back to playing catch with their ball. They still look at the plane in the sky, but they also imagine flying their own planes and rockets. They are happy and excited. They are Tom and Lily, the future pilots.
