Category: Intermediate
Title: The Flower and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a pretty flower. The flower wanted to bloom. It lived next to a well. The well had good water. The flower was happy. One day, a new flower came. The new flower was big and strong. The first flower felt jealous. It wanted to be the best flower. The first flower tried to grow more. It drank water from the well. Soon, it started to bloom. The two flowers became friends. They both looked pretty and were happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat bread with butter. One day, he saw that there was no more butter in the house. He felt sad and asked his mom, "Mom, can we get more butter?" His mom said, "Tim, we can grow our own butter!" Tim was excited and asked, "How, mom?" His mom told him about a special light-yellow plant that could grow butter. They went outside to plant the seeds. As the days went by, Tim and his mom took care of the plant. They gave it water and made sure it got light from the sun. One day, they saw a big, mean bird trying to eat their plant! Tim shouted, "Go away, bird!" The bird flew away, and Tim saved the plant. Soon, the plant grew big and strong. It was time to get the butter! Tim and his mom picked the light-yellow butter from the plant. They were so happy to have their own butter. From that day on, they always had yummy butter for their bread.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a new toy. It had numbers on it. Tim did not know what the numbers meant. He asked his big sister, Lily, to explain. Lily said, "Tim, the numbers are for counting. One, two, three, and so on." Tim tried to count, but he got the numbers mixed up. Lily got mad and said, "No, Tim! You are not doing it right!" Tim felt sad and went to his mom. She saw the new toy and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim told her about the numbers and how Lily got mad. Mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tim. We can learn together." Mom and Tim sat down and played with the new toy. They practiced counting the numbers. Soon, Tim could count all the numbers right. He was so happy, and Lily was proud of him too.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They played in the sand and swam in the water. Tim loved the beach and was so happy. While playing in the water, Tim saw a jellyfish. He was scared and screamed, "Mom, help! Jellyfish!" His mom came quickly and said, "Don't worry, Tim. The jellyfish won't hurt you if you don't touch it." Tim listened to his mom and stayed away from the jellyfish. They continued to play and have fun. At the end of the day, Tim felt successful because he had a great day at the beach with his mom. They went home and Tim told his dad all about the jellyfish and how he didn't touch it.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a cool toy car that she loved to play with. One day, she took her car to the park to show her friends. At the park, a big dog saw the cool car and wanted to play with it too. The dog took the car in its mouth and ran away. Lily was sad and wanted her car back. Lily knew she had to hurry to get her car back from the dog. She ran fast and called for the dog to stop. Her friends helped her chase the dog. They all hurried together. Finally, the dog dropped the car and Lily got it back. She was happy and thanked her friends for helping her.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was three years old and very intelligent. Every morning, she would wake up and look for her mommy and daddy. Her mommy was a scientist and worked with networks. One day, she felt extra curious. She asked her mommy, "What is a network?" Her mommy smiled and said, "A network is a set of machines that all work together to make things smarter and faster." The little girl nodded, understanding the explanation. The next morning, when she woke up, the little girl was excited. She knew that she was becoming more and more intelligent every day, just like mommy's networks!
