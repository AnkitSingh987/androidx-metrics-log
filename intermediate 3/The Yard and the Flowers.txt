Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yard and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a big yard. In the yard, there was a Mommy and her little baby. The Mommy was going to raise the baby in the yard. Every day, Mommy would take the baby outside to play in the big yard. The baby loved running around in the grass and looking at all the big trees. One day, Mommy planted some big seeds in the big yard. She told the baby they would grow into something special. The baby was so excited to see what would grow! Every day, the baby would come outside and see if the big seeds had grown. One day, the baby noticed that the seeds had grown very tall. Mommy said the plants were called flowers and it was time to raise them up high. Mommy and the baby got some big rocks and put them around the flowers to help them stay raised up. The baby was so happy to see the flowers so tall in their big yard and showed all their friends when they visited. The baby and Mommy would always remember their first flowers. They would be sure to always love and raise them up to be as big and beautiful as possible.

Once upon a time, there was a little gray pebble. The gray pebble lived in a big park. The park had many trees and a big pond. The gray pebble had many friends. They liked to play together. One day, the gray pebble wanted to change. It did not want to be gray anymore. The gray pebble wanted to be a different color. It wanted to be like its friends. The gray pebble tried to change, but it could not. The gray pebble became very sad. It did not want to play with its friends anymore. The gray pebble stayed by the pond all alone. The gray pebble never changed. It stayed gray and sad forever.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. He loved to sleep all day and did not like to play or run. One day, Tom saw a big box with a symbol on it. The symbol was a picture of a cat just like him. Tom was curious about the box, so he decided to see if he could fit inside. He tried to push himself into the box, but he was too big. Tom thought, "If I play and run more, maybe I will become smaller and fit in the box." So, Tom started to play and run every day. He played with his ball and ran around the house. After some time, Tom became smaller and not so lazy. He tried to fit in the box again, and this time he could! Tom was happy and learned that being active is much better than being lazy. And that was the moral of the story.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a regular toy that he loved very much. It was a soft rag with a happy face on it. Tim took the rag with him everywhere he went. One day, Tim and his rag went to the park. They saw a big tree. Tim wanted to climb the tree. He said to his rag, "Let's go up the tree!" Tim and his rag climbed the tree together. They saw a big branch and sat on it. Suddenly, they saw a ball strike the tree. The ball fell to the ground. A girl named Sue came to get the ball. She looked up and saw Tim and his rag. Sue said, "Hi! Can I play with you?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes! Let's play together!" So Tim, his rag, and Sue played in the park all day. They had lots of fun and became good friends.

One day, a little girl named Ann was very excited. She put on her shoes and went outside. She wanted to meet her new friend, Tom. Tom lived in the house next door. As Ann walked to Tom's house, she saw a big exit sign by the road. She was curious and walked towards it. She saw a little lost dog under the sign. "Hello, little dog," Ann said. "Are you lost? Let's find your home." Ann and the dog walked around looking for the dog's home. They found Tom on the way. Tom was surprised to see the dog. "Ann, this is my dog, Max! I was looking for him all day!" Tom said. Ann was happy to help. The three friends played together and had fun. They learned that helping others is a good thing to do.
