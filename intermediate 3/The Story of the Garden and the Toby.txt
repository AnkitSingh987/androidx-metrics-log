Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Garden and the Toby

Today was a sunny day. There was a gentle breeze, and the birds were singing sweetly. The sky was bright blue, and it seemed like the day was going to be perfect. Toby was playing outside in the garden, running around and playing hide and seek with his friend. He had been looking forward to this all morning, and he was having the time of his life. But then, he noticed something strange; the sky started to turn dark, and it started to rain. At first, Toby thought it was going to be a small, sweet shower, but before he knew it, it had become a full-blown storm. The rain became heavier and he could hear loud thunder and lightning. Toby started to become afraid. He and his friend ran to the house to take shelter. Toby was very scared, but he was also very sad. He was so close to playing the whole day in the garden, and now, the rain ruined his plans. The storm finally passed, but Toby was very unhappy. Today, he had looked forward to sunshine and playing in the garden, but instead, he got nothing but rain.

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a little sheep. The sheep felt worried. It did not know where its mom was. The sheep looked around and saw a big tree. It thought maybe its mom was there. As the sheep went to the tree, it met a bird. The bird asked, "Why are you worried, little sheep?" The sheep said, "I can't find my mom. Can you help me?" The bird said, "Yes, let's look for her together." The sheep and the bird looked for the mom. They looked under the tree, by the pond, and on the hill. But they could not find the mom. Then, something unexpected happened. The little sheep began to suffer. It felt sick and weak. The bird tried to help, but it could not. In the end, the little sheep never found its mom and stayed sick. The bird felt sad because it could not help its new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a fast cat named Tom. Tom loved to race. He was very eager to test his speed against others. One day, he saw a small bug named Tim. Tom thought it would be easy to win a race with Tim. They started the race. Tom ran fast, but then he saw something he did not expect. Tim had tiny wings! Tim could fly! Tom was surprised, but he still tried to win the race. In the end, Tim flew over the finish line first. Tom was not sad, though. He learned that even a small bug can be fast too. Tom and Tim became good friends and raced together many times.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim had a beautiful feather. One day, Tim saw his friend, the cat, wash her fur. Tim was eager to try it too. Tim went to the water and started to wash his feather. He washed and washed until it was very clean. Tim's feather looked even more beautiful now. His friends saw how pretty it was and wanted to wash their feathers too. Soon, all the birds in the forest were washing their feathers. They learned that being clean made them feel good and look nice. The moral of the story is to always keep yourself clean and tidy.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs and stones. Lily found a shiny nail and showed it to Tom. "Look, Tom, I found a nail. It is shiny and hard. Can I keep it?" Lily asked. Tom frowned. He wanted the nail too. He thought it was his turn to find something cool. He tried to grab the nail from Lily's hand. "No, Lily, give me the nail. It is mine. You always find the best things," Tom said. Lily did not want to give the nail to Tom. She held it tight and pulled it away from Tom. They started to fight over the nail. They both got angry and sad. "Stop, stop, stop!" Mom said. She heard them fighting and came to the garden. She saw the nail and the dirt on their hands and clothes. She was not happy. "What are you doing, Lily and Tom? Why are you fighting over a nail? That is not nice. You could hurt each other or yourselves. Nails are not toys. They are sharp and dangerous. Where did you find it?" Mom asked. Lily and Tom stopped fighting. They looked at Mom and the nail. They felt sorry and ashamed. They told Mom they found it in the dirt. Mom took the nail from Lily's hand. She looked at it and saw it had a deep scratch on it. She wondered where it came from and what it was used for. She decided to throw it away in the trash. "Listen, Lily and Tom, you should not fight over things. You should share and take turns. You should also be careful what you dig in the dirt. You might find something that is not safe or that belongs to someone else. Do you understand?" Mom said. Lily and Tom nodded. They understood. They said sorry to Mom and to each other. They hugged and made up. They decided to play something else in the garden. They did not want to fight or find nails anymore. They wanted to have fun and be happy.
