Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Door

One day, Tom was feeling hungry. He went to the kitchen and opened the refrigerator. Unfortunately, all the food was gone! This made him very angry. He had a temper tantrum and screamed: "I'm so hungry! Where is the food?!" Suddenly, the doorbell rang. Tom was relieved and ran to the door. It was his Mom who had come home from work. She had a big paper bag full of groceries. Tom smiled and hugged his Mom. But then, his Mom remembered the temper tantrum. She said, "Tom, why were you so angry earlier?" Tom explained, "I couldn't find any food in the refrigerator!". Mom smiled and said, "Let's reach into the bag and see what I brought home!". Tom was so excited. He reached in the bag and pulled out two big boxes of pizza. He squealed with delight and they hugged. Tom was relieved that Mom came home at the perfect moment. He learned a valuable lesson that day that anger never solves your problems.

One day, a boy went to the barber. The boy wanted a haircut. The barber was honest and nice. The boy sat in the chair and the barber began to cut his hair. The barber said, "I will tell you a story while I cut your hair." The boy was happy to listen. The barber told a story about a big, funny dog who liked to play with a ball. The boy laughed and laughed. Then, something unexpected happened. The barber's scissors turned into a magic wand. The barber waved the wand and the big, funny dog from the story appeared! The boy and the dog played with the ball together, and they all had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a clever lady. She lived in a small house near the park. The lady had a big dog named Spot. Spot was a very good dog. One day, the lady took Spot to the park to play. They played with a ball. Spot liked to run and catch the ball. The lady would throw the ball far away, and Spot would bring it back. They were both very happy. After they played for a long time, the lady said, "Spot, kneel!" Spot did what the lady said. He was a very clever dog too. They went back to their small house and had a nice nap. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a market. At the market, people sold many things like toys, food, and clothes. Every day, lots of people went to the market to buy what they needed. One day, a slow turtle named Tim went to the market. He wanted to buy a new bow for his friend's birthday. Tim walked very slow, but he was happy because he liked looking at all the things in the market. Finally, Tim found a pretty bow for his friend. He gave the bow to his friend at the birthday party. His friend was very happy and they played all day. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a long stream. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, liked to play near the stream. They would throw sticks and watch them float away. One day, Tim saw a big, red ball near the stream. He wanted to play with it, but it was too far away. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, can we buy a long stick to get the red ball?" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, let's go to the store and buy a long stick." They went to the store and bought a long stick. Tim and Spot went back to the stream. Tim used the long stick to reach the red ball. They played with the ball all day long, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a dog named Tim. Tim was a competitive dog who loved to play with his friends. One day, Tim and his best friend, Tom the cat, were playing a game of catch. Tim said, "I'll lay the ball here, and you try to get it before me!" So, Tim laid the ball on the ground and ran to the other side of the yard. Tom, who was not as fast as Tim, tried his best to get the ball first. But Tim always got there before Tom. Tom felt sad and left the game. Tim saw Tom was sad and said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I didn't mean to make you feel bad. Let's play a new game where we can both have fun." So, Tim and Tom played a new game together. In the end, Tim learned that winning isn't everything, and being a good friend is more important.
