Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max loved to play fetch with his bone. One day, Max dug a deep hole and hid his bone in it. He was very happy with his hiding spot. The next day, Max went to the deep hole to get his bone, but it was gone! Max was sad and began to cry. Suddenly, he heard a whisper behind him. It was his friend, the cat named Lily. Lily had a big smile on her face and said, "I have a surprise for you, Max!" Max followed Lily to a big tree. There, Max saw his bone hanging from the tree! Lily had put it there as a fun surprise for Max. Max was very happy and wagged his tail. From that day on, Max and Lily played fetch with the bone together, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a brave dog named Toby. Toby liked to help people. One day, Toby found a lost toy. He wanted to return the toy to its owner. Toby went to the police. He said, "I found a toy. Can you help me find its owner?" The police were happy to help. They knew Toby was a brave dog. Together, they looked for the toy's owner. At last, they found a little girl. She was very happy to see her toy. She said, "Thank you, Toby!" Toby was proud to help and return the toy.

Minnie was a wise old mouse who often went for a walk on Long Road. One day she was walking along and noticed a stair in the middle of the road. “I wonder what that stair is for?” Minnie asked a nearby cat sitting on the fence. The cat smiled and said “That is the stair to Share Mountain. Every day, animals from all over come to Share Mountain to share their stories and experiences with each other.” Minnie was very excited. She wanted to go to Share Mountain but it was far away. She had to cross the road and climb the stair. “That looks like hard work” she thought. But then she remembered she was a wise old mouse. She knew she didn’t have to do it alone. She decided to ask a nearby bird to help her cross the road and climb the stair to Share Mountain. The bird was happy to help and soon they were at the top of the stair. Minnie was so excited. Finally, she could share all her stories and experiences with other animals.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy cereal for breakfast. Tim saw a big box with a fun picture on it. He wanted that cereal very much. "Mom, can we get this one?" Tim asked. His mom looked at the box and said, "Yes, we can get that one." The next morning, Tim was very excited to eat the new cereal. He sat at the table and his mom poured the cereal into a bowl. She also poured hot milk on the cereal to make it soft and yummy. Tim was so happy to eat his breakfast. While eating, Tim wanted to play with his ball too. He put the ball on the floor and gave it a big kick. The ball flew across the room and hit the cereal box. The box fell on the floor, and cereal went everywhere. Tim's mom said, "Oh no! We need to clean this up." Tim felt sad and helped his mom clean the mess. After cleaning, Tim learned not to play with his ball while eating.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom was lying in the sun. He was very sleepy and did not want to play. Suddenly, a little bird appeared. The bird was very happy and wanted to talk to Tom. "Hi Tom!" said the happy bird. "Do you want to play with me?" But Tom did not want to play. He was too lazy. Then, the bird saw a phone on the ground. The bird picked up the phone and showed it to Tom. Tom looked at the phone and started to feel less lazy. They played with the phone together and had a lot of fun.
