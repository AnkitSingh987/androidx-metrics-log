Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lawyer and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a lawyer. The lawyer was confused. He did not know where to go. He walked and walked, trying to find his way. One day, he saw a big tree. He sat under the tree to rest. He started to observe the birds in the tree. They were singing and flying around. The lawyer liked watching the birds. As he watched, a little girl came to the tree. She saw the confused lawyer and asked, "What are you doing?" The lawyer said, "I am watching the birds." The little girl smiled and said, "I know the way. I can help you." The lawyer was happy. They walked together, and the lawyer was not confused anymore.

Anna and Ben are brother and sister. They like to play in the garden. One day, they find a big, shiny box under a tree. They wonder what is inside. "Let's open it!" Ben says. He tries to lift the lid, but it is too heavy. He sees a lock on the side. "We need a key," he says. "Maybe we can twist it," Anna says. She puts her hand on the lock and turns it hard. She hears a click. "I did it!" she says. She lifts the lid and looks inside. "What is it?" Ben asks. He peeks over her shoulder. He sees a lot of colorful things. There are balls, dolls, cars, books, and more. They are all beautiful. "It's a treasure!" Anna says. She smiles. "We can play with it!" They take out the things and start to play. They have a lot of fun. They do not see the sign on the box. It says: "Do not open. Belongs to the witch."

Once upon a time, there was a powerful frog named Fred. Fred loved to dance and jump in the pond. One day, he saw a beautiful princess named Lily sitting by the pond. Fred hopped over to her and said, "Hi! I am Fred. I love to dance. Do you want to dance with me?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to dance with you!" They danced together and had so much fun. After they danced, Lily said, "Thank you, Fred. I have a secret. My mom told me that if I kiss a frog, something special will happen." Fred was excited and said, "You can kiss me!" When Lily kissed Fred, he turned into a handsome prince. Lily and Fred were so happy and they danced together forever.

One day, a cat named Tom went for a walk. He was very alert and saw a big puddle. Tom wanted to play in the puddle. He jumped in and started to soak his fur. It was fun, and he felt happy. Suddenly, Tom saw a big dog. The dog was not friendly. Tom was in shock. He did not know what to do. The dog started to chase Tom, and Tom ran away as fast as he could. Tom tried to hide, but the dog found him. The dog was not nice to Tom. In the end, Tom was very sad and scared. He wished he had not played in the puddle.

Once upon a time, there was a dull kitchen. In the kitchen, there was a big pot of spaghetti. Mom was cooking it for dinner. She went away to get more sauce. While she was away, a cat jumped on the table. The cat saw the spaghetti and wanted to eat it. It ate all the spaghetti from the pot. When the cat was done, it felt bad. It wanted to replace the spaghetti. The cat looked around and found some string. It put the string in the pot. When Mom came back, she did not know what happened. She put sauce on the string and called everyone to eat. When they started eating, they found out it was not spaghetti but string! They all laughed and had a fun time. The cat was happy that it made everyone laugh.

Anna likes to print pictures with her crayons. She prints flowers, animals, and stars. She prints on paper, on cardboard, and on the wall. She prints everywhere. One day, she finds a big box in the garage. She opens it and sees many things inside. She sees books, clothes, toys, and a camera. She wonders what they are for. She thinks it is a mystery. She decides to print on the things in the box. She prints on the books, on the clothes, on the toys, and on the camera. She prints her name, her face, and her favorite colors. She prints on everything. She is happy with her prints. She thinks they are pretty. She puts the things back in the box and closes it. She goes to play with her doll. Later, her mom comes home. She sees the box in the garage. She opens it and gasps. She sees the prints on the things in the box. She is angry and sad. She says, "Anna, what did you do? These are the things I was going to donate to the children who need them. You ruined them with your prints. How could you be so selfish?" Anna hears her mom and feels bad. She runs to the garage and says, "I'm sorry, mom. I didn't know. I thought it was a mystery. I wanted to print on them. I like to print." Her mom hugs her and says, "I know you like to print, Anna. But you have to be careful with other people's things. You have to ask before you print on them. You have to be respectful and helpful, not selfish and rude. Do you understand?" Anna nods and says, "I understand, mom. I'm sorry. I want to be helpful, not selfish. How can I help?" Her mom smiles and says, "You can help by cleaning the things in the box. You can use a cloth and some water to wipe off your prints. You can also help by making some new prints on some paper. You can write sorry notes and draw nice pictures for the children who will get the things in the box. You can also help by giving some of your own toys and clothes to the children who need them. You can be generous and kind, not greedy and mean. Do you want to do that?" Anna smiles and says, "Yes, mom. I want to do that. I want to be generous and kind. I want to make the children happy. I want to print for good, not for bad." Her mom kisses her and says, "I'm proud of you, Anna. You learned a valuable lesson today. You learned that printing can be fun, but also harmful. You learned that you have to be responsible and thoughtful with your prints. You learned that you have to share and care for others. You learned that printing can be a way of expressing yourself, but also a way of apologizing and making amends. You learned that printing can be a mystery, but also a solution." Anna hugs her mom and says, "Thank you, mom. I love you. I love to print. But I love to help more."
