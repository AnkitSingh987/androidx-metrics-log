Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Graceful Cat and the Dog

One day, a graceful cat named Lily went for a walk. She saw a card on the ground. The card had a picture of a big ice cube on it. Lily picked up the card and showed it to her friend, a dog named Max. "Look, Max! I found a card with an ice cube," said Lily. Max looked at the card and said, "Let's play a game. When I say 'freeze,' we must stop and not move." They started to play the game. Lily and Max ran and jumped. When Max said "freeze," they stopped. They had so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. When Max said "freeze" one more time, they both turned into real ice cubes! A little girl saw the ice cubes and picked up the card. She read the words on the card out loud: "Say 'unfreeze' to turn back." The little girl said "unfreeze," and Lily and Max turned back into a cat and a dog. They were so happy to be free and continued to play, but they never played the freeze game again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a mine where he found pretty rocks. He liked to play in the mine with his friends. One day, Tim found a tight hole in the mine. It was so small that only he could fit inside. Tim crawled into the tight hole and found a secret room. In the room, there was a big bottle. Tim was very thirsty, so he decided to drink from the bottle. As he drank, he heard a tiny voice say, "Please, don't drink all of it. I need some too." Tim looked around and saw a small bug. The bug was also thirsty. Tim felt sorry for the bug, so he shared the drink with him. The bug thanked Tim and they became friends. They both drank until they were not thirsty anymore. From that day on, Tim learned that it is important to share with others. He always shared his drinks and his pretty rocks with his friends and the bugs in the mine. Everyone was happy and they all had fun together. The moral of the story is to always share with others, because it makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog with soft fur. The dog had a toy that he loved to play with all day. One day, a little cat came to the dog and asked if he could lend the toy to her. The dog did not want to lend his toy, but the cat looked sad. So, the dog thought for a moment and decided to help the cat. He said, "I will lend you my toy, but you must give it back soon." The cat was very happy and played with the toy. But after some time, the toy became dull and not as fun. The dog saw this and felt bad for the cat. He wanted to make the cat happy again. The dog had an idea. He asked the cat to play with him and his toy together. The cat agreed, and they both had a lot of fun. From that day on, the dog and the cat became best friends and always shared their toys with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Sam. Sam was a very frightened dog. He was scared of big dogs, loud noises, and even his own shadow. One day, while Sam was walking in the park, he met a big dog named Max. Max liked to tease Sam. He would bark very loud and chase Sam around the park. "Woof, woof! I'm going to get you!" Max would say. Sam would run away, trying to find a quiet place to hide. One day, a little girl saw Max teasing Sam. She walked over and said, "Max, it's not nice to tease Sam. He's frightened." Max felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Sam. I didn't know you were scared." From that day on, Max and Sam became good friends and played together in the park.

Once there was a naughty parrot. He lived with a family and they really liked him. One day, the family invited a new friend over to play. The parrot was curious and started to talk to the new friend. The naughty parrot asked the friend, "Do you want to play?" The new friend was surprised. He said, "Wow! You can talk!" The parrot replied, "Yes, I can mind my own business too!" Everyone laughed. From then on, the naughty parrot was very popular and everyone wanted to be his friend.
