Category: Intermediate
Title: The Helpful and the River

Once upon a time there lived a helpful, little girl. She lived in a small village. Every day, she liked to go down to the river, where she could see the fish swimming in the clear water. But one day, she noticed something strange. A big row had split the river in two! The little girl knew that something had to be done. She spoke to all the other people in the village and asked them for help. Everyone was really helpful and they all started work on repairing the big row. A week later, the big row had been repaired but the little girl thought it was too weak. So, she spoke to all the other people in the village again, and they came back to make it stronger. Now, the little girl was very happy that the big row was no longer split and the river flowed freely again. Everyone was so grateful for her help and she knew that everyone was truly helpful! The End.

Lily loved to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Anna. Anna had long hair, a pink dress and a shiny crown. Lily liked to pretend that Anna was a princess and she was her friend. One day, Lily saw a picture of a girl on a magazine. The girl looked just like Anna, but she was bigger and had a smile. Lily showed the picture to her mom and asked, "Who is this, mom?" Mom looked at the picture and said, "This is a model, Lily. She is a person who wears nice clothes and poses for photos. She is very pretty, isn't she?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, she is. She is like Anna. Can I be a model too, mom?" Mom smiled and hugged Lily. She said, "You are adorable, Lily. You can be anything you want to be when you grow up. But you don't need to be a model to be beautiful. You are beautiful just the way you are." Lily realized that mom was right. She didn't need to look like Anna or the model to be happy. She was happy with herself and her dolls. She thanked mom and gave her a kiss. Then she went back to play with Anna and her other dolls. She made up stories and had fun. She was a princess and a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a tough boy named Tim. He loved to kick a ball. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue said she could do a spell. "I can make your ball jump high with my spell," Sue told Tim. Tim was excited and wanted to see the spell. Sue said a magic word, and the ball started to jump very high. Tim and Sue played with the ball all day. They had so much fun. The ball jumped higher and higher. At the end of the day, Tim thanked Sue for her spell.

One day, a little girl named Sally found a big box. She did not know what was inside. It was a mystery. Sally was very excited. She wanted to find out what was in the box. She asked her friend, Tom, to help her. "Tom, can you help me open this box?" Sally asked. Tom said, "Yes, let's settle this mystery!" They tried to open the box, but it was too hard. They needed to find a key. Sally and Tom looked everywhere for the key. They looked in the house, in the yard, and even under the bed. Finally, they found the key in the kitchen. They were very happy. They opened the box and saw many shiny things. They found rich, golden coins and pretty jewels. Sally and Tom were very happy. They decided to share their treasure with their friends. From that day on, Sally and Tom were the best of friends. They always helped each other and shared their things. They never forgot the day they solved the mystery of the big box.

Once upon a time, there was a big room. In the room, there was a desk. It was a nice desk. On the desk, there was a toy. The toy was very fit. It could run and jump. One day, a boy named Tim came into the room. He saw the toy on the desk. "Wow," said Tim. "That toy is so fit. I want to play with it." He picked up the toy and played with it. They had a lot of fun. Later, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw Tim playing with the toy. She said, "Tim, please mind the desk. It is old and can break." Tim said, "Okay, mom. I will mind the desk."
