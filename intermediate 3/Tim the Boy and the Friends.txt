Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Friends

Once upon a time, in a land of ice and snow, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small igloo with his family. They were poor, but they were happy. Tim loved to play with his friends in the snow. One day, while playing, Tim found a big box. He did not know what was inside. He asked his friends if they wanted to see. They all said yes. When they opened the box, they found many toys and warm clothes. They were so happy! Tim and his friends decided to share the toys and clothes with other poor kids in their village. They learned to accept and help each other. The village became a happy place. The kids played with their new toys and wore their warm clothes. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a famous book. This book had many pages. One day, a boy found the book. The boy was very happy. He wanted to read every page. The boy started to read the book. He read one page, then another page, and another. The boy had fun reading the book. The book had many good stories. The boy loved the stories. But, the last page had a bad story. The boy was sad. The book was not fun anymore. The boy did not want to read the book again. The book was not famous anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to paint. He had a big box of paints in many colors. One day, he decided to paint a picture of a mighty tree. As Tim started to paint, he saw a small bug in the paint box. The bug was stuck in the paint. Tim wanted to help the bug. So, he picked up the bug and cleaned it. The bug was happy to be free. The bug wanted to thank Tim. So, the bug helped Tim paint the mighty tree. They painted together and had lots of fun. In the end, the tree looked beautiful. Tim and the bug were very proud of their work. And they became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, a little mouse lived in a meadow. He always loved going on adventures and exploring new places. One day, he decided to climb a tall vine. He held tightly to the vine and he zoomed all the way up to the top. But when he looked down, he noticed that he was really high up! He was scared and he was embarrassed that he had climbed so high. He was about to slide down the vine when he heard a little voice from below. It was a baby deer, who had watched the mouse climb. The baby deer said, “Climbing is fun, but make sure you don’t climb too high next time!” The little mouse was grateful for the deer’s advice. He learned a valuable lesson - it’s important to be careful when you explore and be sure to stay safe. The little mouse then zoomed down the vine and thanked the baby deer for its kind words. From then on, the little mouse was much more careful when he explored.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw his friend, Sam, on a bike. Sam was going fast. Tim wanted to ride a bike too. He asked his mom if he could have a bike with pedals. His mom said, "Yes, but first you must write a note to say thank you." Tim wrote a note that said, "Thank you, Mom, for the bike with pedals." Then, his mom got him a bike. Tim was happy. He and Sam could ride bikes together. Tim and Sam were competitive. They wanted to see who was the best at riding bikes. They had a race. Sam said, "Let's see who can go the fastest!" Tim and Sam raced down the street. Tim was fast, but Sam was faster. They both had fun, and they played all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very comfortable in his little house. He loved to play with his ball and run around the yard. One day, he saw a big turn in the road and decided to go explore. As Spot walked down the turn, he saw a little bird with a hurt wing. Spot wanted to help the bird heal. He remembered that his mom had a special box with things to help heal at home. Spot ran back to his house as fast as he could to get the special box. Spot brought the special box to the little bird and used the things inside to help heal the bird's wing. After a while, the bird felt much better and could fly again. Spot and the bird became the best of friends, and they played together every day. Spot was so happy that he could help his new friend heal and have fun together.
