Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Cat and the Shelf

One day, a little cat named Tim went to the store. Tim wanted to help his mom fill the shelf at home. The shelf was empty and needed new things. Tim saw a big bag of dry cat food. He thought it would be perfect for the shelf. Tim asked the store man, "Can I have the big bag of dry cat food, please?" The store man smiled and said, "Of course, little cat. Here you go." Tim took the big bag and went home. When Tim got home, he filled the shelf with the dry cat food. His mom was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a great helper." Tim felt proud and happy that he could help his mom.

Lily liked balloons very much. She had a red balloon that she got from a nice man at the park. She played with it every day. She would throw it up in the air and catch it, or kick it like a ball, or make funny noises with it. One day, Lily wanted to show her balloon to her friend Tom. She took it with her to his house. Tom was happy to see Lily and her balloon. He asked if he could play with it too. Lily said yes, because she was a good friend. She did not mind sharing her balloon. Tom was very clever. He knew how to make animals with balloons. He twisted and bent Lily's balloon until it looked like a dog. He gave it to Lily and said, "Woof, woof!" Lily was amazed. She liked her balloon dog very much. She thanked Tom and gave him a hug. They played with the balloon dog for a while, until it was time for Lily to go home. Lily wanted to take her balloon dog with her, but she also wanted to be nice to Tom. She asked him if he wanted to keep it. Tom said no, because he had many balloons at home. He said Lily could have it, because it was her balloon. He did not mind giving it back to her. Lily was very happy. She said goodbye to Tom and took her balloon dog with her. She held it carefully, so it would not pop. She thought Tom was a very clever and kind friend. She hoped he would come to her house soon, so they could play with balloons again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a funny dog named Buzzy. Buzzy loved to eat. Every day, he would eat a big bowl of dog food. He would wag his tail and bark happily when it was time to eat. One day, Buzzy found a piece of paper on the ground. It was a license. He didn't know what it was, but it smelled interesting. So, Buzzy decided to eat the license. He chewed it up and swallowed it. Buzzy's friend, a little boy named Timmy, saw him eat the license. Timmy said, "Buzzy, why did you eat that license?" Buzzy just wagged his tail and barked. Timmy laughed and said, "You're a funny dog, Buzzy. Let's go play!" And they both ran off to play together, happy as can be.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy bear named Ben. Ben loved to tell the other animals what to do. One day, Ben saw a little bird named Billy trying to open a jar of jam. Ben knew he could help, but he wanted to make sure he was the one in charge. Ben walked up to Billy and said, "I can support you, but you have to listen to me." Billy looked at Ben and nodded. He knew that with Ben's help, he could open the jar of jam. Together, they worked to open the jar. Ben used his big paws to hold the jar, while Billy used his beak to twist the lid. Suddenly, the lid popped off and the jam was free! Billy was so happy that he shared the jam with Ben. They both learned that working together was better than being bossy.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. The mouse lived in a small house. One day, the mouse was very confused. He did not know where his toy was. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. Then, the mouse saw a big box. The box had his name on it. He was so happy! The mouse opened the box. Inside, he found a new toy. He had received a gift from his friend, the cat. The mouse played with his new toy all day. He was not confused anymore. He knew his friend, the cat, had given him the toy. The mouse and the cat played together and had lots of fun. They were best friends forever.
