Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and Tim and the Park

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Tim. They had been having a great time playing together in the park all morning. When noon rolled around, Jack decided to go home. Tim wasn't ready to go home yet and he started to complain. He said, "It's not fair, why do I have to go home now?". Jack tried to be understanding and told Tim he could come over to his house later that afternoon. That changed Tim's mood and he happily agreed. But when Tim arrived at Jack's house he noticed something was wrong. The house was very messy and nothing was clean. Tim started to complain again and said, "Why is your house so dirty? Your mom should do a better job of keeping it clean!". Jack was very sad hearing these words. Jack's mom was very sick, and he had been trying his best to keep the house clean. Jack told Tim he had to go home now. Tim said goodbye and went home, feeling ashamed of what he had said. He never saw Jack again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally had a pet dog named Spot. Spot was a good dog, but sometimes he did not behave. One day, Sally and Spot went to the park. They saw a big red ball. Spot wanted to play with the ball, but Sally said, "No, Spot. You must behave." Spot listened to Sally and sat down. Later, they went home and Sally's mom made dinner. The food was spicy, but Sally and Spot liked it. They were happy to be together and have a fun day. And from that day on, Spot always tried to behave.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat named Sally sailing in the sea. Sally's captain, Jack, was brave and wanted to win big. He looked out in the distance, and noticed a clear horizon. Jack knew there was something special out there, so he told his crew to prepare to sail. As they sailed, the crew could feel a great victory coming. Suddenly, a huge storm began and the boat started to rock and shake. Everyone was scared and the boat nearly tipped over! Jack yelled out, “Steady now! We can do this. Keep sailing and we’ll come out victorious.” The crew kept sailing, scared but determined. When the storm finally passed, they were greeted with a beautiful, clear sky. Jack looked out and saw land in the distance. His crew had won the battle. They sailed up to shore and arrived triumphant! Jack smiled and said, “That’s the victory we were aiming for!”

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big jar of sauce. He loved his sauce so much. It was his own sauce that his mom made for him. Tim took the sauce everywhere he went. One day, Tim went to the park. He played with his friends and had fun. But he forgot his sauce on a bench. When Tim went back to find it, the sauce was gone. Tim was very sorry and sad. A girl named Sue saw Tim and asked why he was sad. Tim told her about his lost sauce. Sue said, "Don't be sorry, Tim. I saw a dog take the sauce." Tim and Sue looked for the dog and found it. They got the sauce back. Tim was happy, and he and Sue became good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He saw a big refrigerator. It was cold and white. Tim was hungry, so he opened the refrigerator to find something to eat. Inside, there were many foods. Tim saw apples, cheese, and milk. He wanted to find the best food available. He poked the cheese and poked the milk. They did not feel good to eat. Then, he poked the apples. They felt just right. Tim took the apples out of the refrigerator. He ate one apple and it was very yummy. He was happy that he found the best food to eat. Tim's tummy was full and he felt good.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his favorite toy, a red ball. He would chew on it all day long. Spot and his best friend, a little boy named Tim, would play fetch with the red ball in the park. One sunny day, Tim and Spot went to the park to play. Tim threw the ball, and Spot ran to catch it. But when Spot found the ball, he also found a match lying on the ground. Spot picked up both the ball and the match in his mouth and brought them back to Tim. Tim saw the match and knew it was not safe for Spot to chew on. He took the match away and said, "Good job, Spot! You are a loyal friend for bringing this to me." Tim threw the red ball again, and Spot happily chased after it. From that day on, they continued to have fun playing in the park, and Spot knew he was a loyal friend to Tim.
