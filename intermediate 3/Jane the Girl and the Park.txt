Category: Intermediate
Title: Jane the Girl and the Park

One day, a girl named Jane had a doll. It was a gloomy doll with a sad face. Jane would play with the doll all day. She would say, "Don't be sad, doll. I am your friend." One day, Jane and the doll went to the park. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Jane said to the doll, "See, doll? It's a happy day. You don't have to be gloomy." But the doll still looked sad. In the park, Jane saw a boy who was crying. Jane went to the boy and said, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my toy." Jane gave the boy her gloomy doll. The boy smiled and said, "Thank you! Now I have a new friend." The doll's face changed and it was not gloomy anymore. Jane was happy that her doll found a new friend and made the boy happy too.

Once there was a little bird who wanted to fly. She was very persistent and worked hard everyday to improve her flying skills. Soon, she learned that she could fly, but she was still a bit scared. One day, a big bird saw what she was doing and decided to support her. She spread her wings and showed the little bird how to flap them. She also sprinkled a few feathers onto her back to help her stay in the air. The little bird was very thankful to the big bird and kept trying every day. Day after day, she practiced and slowly got better at flying. Soon, she was no longer scared and she could fly all around the sky with the big bird supporting her too.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Anna. She was three years old and very excited. She wanted a puppy really badly, so one day she asked her mom if she could have one. Her mom said, "I promise I’ll think about it" as she gave Anna a big hug. Anna was so happy and she couldn't wait! The next day, Anna's mom had a surprise for her. She had bought Anna a puppy! His fur was soft like cream and he was the cutest puppy ever. Anna was so excited and ran around with him. The family next door saw what had happened, and the two boys there felt envious. They wished they could have a puppy too. But Anna was very happy with hers, and she promised to play with him every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to draw pictures. One day, it was very cold outside, so Tim stayed in his warm house to draw. Tim drew a picture of a big, friendly bear. He showed his mom the picture and said, "Look, Mom! I drew a bear!" His mom smiled and said, "Wow, Tim! That's a great picture! But remember, bears need to behave well, just like you." The next day, Tim took his picture to school. He showed his friends the bear and told them, "This is my bear. He knows how to behave." His friends liked the picture and said, "We want to draw bears, too!" So, Tim and his friends all drew pictures of bears. They made sure their bears were behaving well, just like Tim's mom said. And even though it was cold outside, they had a lot of fun drawing pictures together.

It was a bright day, and the sunshine was warm on Amy's face. She ran across the grass to where her mommy was standing. "Mommy, what's that yucky smell?" Amy asked. Mommy shrugged and said, "It's disgusting, isn't it? I think that it's coming from the pond over there." Amy's face wrinkled up in a frown. She had been looking forward to playing near the pond, but now it smelled too bad. Mommy put her arm around Amy and said, "Let's go somewhere else where it doesn't smell so bad, okay? The sunshine is too nice today to stay here." Amy smiled and nodded. They walked away from the pond, toward a place where the air didn't stink.

Once upon a time, there was a big race. Many animals wanted to win. They all ran very fast. The race started at sunrise. The sky was light and pretty. The sun was happy to watch the race. In the end, the rabbit won the race. All the animals cheered. The rabbit was very happy to win. The sun smiled and said good job.
