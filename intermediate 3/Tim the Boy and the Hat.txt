Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to eat jam on bread. One day, Tim saw a big jar of jam on the table. It was easy for him to open the jar. Tim took the jam and put it on his bread. He was so happy. He started to dance and clap. He wanted everyone to applaud for the jam. But then, something unexpected happened. The jar of jam started to wiggle and shake. It was not just jam, it was a magic jam! The magic jam jumped onto Tim's bread and turned it into a big, funny hat. Tim was so surprised, but he laughed and wore the hat. Everyone around him laughed and applauded too. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy a gift for Lily's grandma. Lily wanted to find something nice and reliable that her grandma would love. At the store, Lily saw a pretty bottle of perfume. She picked it up and smelled it. "Mmm, this smells good!" Lily said. "Mom, can we get this for grandma?" Her mom nodded and smiled, "Yes, that's a great idea. Grandma will love it." When they gave the perfume to Lily's grandma, she was very happy. She hugged Lily and said, "Thank you for caring about me and finding such a lovely gift." Lily felt proud and knew that her grandma would always remember her reliable gift.

One day, a big cat named Tom went for a slow walk. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue wore a pretty apron with many colors. She looked sad and had a big frown on her face. Tom wanted to help Sue feel happy again. Tom asked, "Why do you frown, Sue?" Sue said, "I lost my toy ball. I can't find it anywhere." Tom thought he could help Sue find her toy ball. He had a good nose for finding things. Tom and Sue looked high and low for the toy ball. They looked near the trees and by the flowers. Then, Tom saw something round and colorful under a big rock. He knew it was the toy ball. Sue's frown turned into a big smile. She hugged Tom and said, "Thank you for finding my toy ball!" Tom felt happy that he could help his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was a very lucky girl. She had a big, soft mattress to sleep on every night. One day, Lucy's friend, Tommy, came over to play. "Lucy, why is your mattress so big and soft?" Tommy asked. "I am a lucky girl," Lucy said with a big smile. Tommy and Lucy played and jumped on the mattress all day. They laughed and had so much fun. As the sun started to go down, they began to feel tired. Tommy laid down on the mattress and said, "Wow, this is so comfy. I wish I had one like this." Lucy realized how lucky she was to have such a nice mattress. She decided to share it with Tommy. "You can sleep here tonight, Tommy. We can both be lucky." Tommy smiled and said, "Thank you, Lucy. You are a good friend." They both slept on the big, soft mattress that night, feeling very lucky and happy.

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a regular cat named Tom. Tom was a small, fluffy cat who liked to play with his friends. One day, Tom saw a big red ball in the park. He wanted to play with it, but he didn't know if he should. Tom thought about it and decided to ask his friends for help. He went to his friend, a little bird named Tim. Tim was very fast and could fly high in the sky. Tom asked Tim if he should play with the big red ball. Tim said, "Yes! It will be fun!" So, Tom and Tim went to the park to play with the big red ball. They had a great time playing together. They rolled the ball, kicked it, and even jumped on it. After a long day of fun, Tom and Tim were tired. They went back to their homes in the city and took a long nap. They were happy they decided to play with the big red ball.
