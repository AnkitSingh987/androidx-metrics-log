Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily and her dog, Spot. They were best friends. One day, Lily found a broken jewel in her garden. It was very pretty and shiny. Lily showed it to Spot and said, "Look what I found!" Spot wagged his tail and barked happily. He had an idea. Spot suggested that they should show the broken jewel to Lily's mom. So, they went inside the house and showed it to her. Lily's mom looked at the jewel and smiled. She said, "I think I can fix it." Lily's mom took the broken jewel and put it back together. Then, something unexpected happened. The jewel began to glow and a fairy appeared! The fairy said, "Thank you for fixing my jewel. As a reward, I will grant you one wish." Lily and Spot looked at each other and smiled. They knew just what to wish for.

One day, a cool cat named Tom found a flashlight. He liked to balance on things, like logs and rocks. With his new flashlight, he could play at night too. He was very happy. One night, Tom was out playing with his flashlight, balancing on a big log. He saw another cat, named Sam, who wanted to play too. But Sam was not good at sharing. He tried to take the flashlight from Tom. Tom did not want to give up his flashlight. They both tried to balance on the log and hold the flashlight at the same time. It was hard to do. They both fell off the log and the flashlight rolled away. Tom and Sam looked at each other and decided to be friends. They learned to take turns with the flashlight and balance together on the log. Now, they could both have fun and play at night.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a cold forest with lots of big trees. The trees were made of lumber. Tim liked to fly around the trees and play with his friends. One day, Tim was flying when something unexpected happened. He saw a big brown bear near his tree! Tim started to panic. He was very scared of the bear. He thought the bear would hurt him and his friends. But then, the bear saw Tim and smiled. The bear was not mean. He was a nice bear who just wanted to play with Tim and his friends. Tim was happy and not scared anymore. They all played together and had lots of fun in the cold forest.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy car. It was red and shiny. A man named Tom wanted to drive the car. But Tom did not know how to drive. So, he asked his friend, Sam, to teach him. Sam was a good teacher. They went to a big open space. Tom sat in the car, and Sam sat next to him. Sam started to teach Tom how to drive. Tom tried very hard to learn. He pushed the pedals and turned the wheel. The heavy car went fast and slow, left and right. But one day, Tom was not careful. He did not listen to Sam's teach. He drove the heavy car too fast. The car went off the road and hit a big tree. The car was broken, and Tom and Sam were sad. They knew they could not drive the heavy car again.

Lucas and Mommy went to the beach. Mommy had a cooler and they took it with them to the beach. Lucas was very excited to go there. When they arrived, Lucas found a bucket and some shovels on the sand. He wanted to play with them. Mommy said, "Can I borrow your cooler for a little bit?", and Lucas said, "Yes, sure!" He was happy to help. Mommy put her sandwiches and drinks in the cooler and took it away. Lucas was still playing in the sand. After some time, She came back with the cooler. She said, "Thank you for lending me the cooler. It was very fine of you." Lucas was happy to hear his mommy say that. They both smiled and went back to playing in the sand.
