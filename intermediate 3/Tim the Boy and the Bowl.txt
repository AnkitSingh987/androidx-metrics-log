Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bowl

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to sit and play with his toy cars. One day, his mom asked him to help her make a cake. She said, "Tim, can you help me put the butter in the bowl?" Tim was excited to help. Tim looked at the smooth butter and thought it looked like a fun toy. So, he took the butter and started to play with it. His mom saw him and said, "Tim, we need the butter for the cake. Please put it in the bowl." Tim felt sad because he wanted to play, but he knew he had to help his mom. Tim put the butter in the bowl and helped his mom make the cake. When the cake was done, they ate it together. It was so yummy! Tim learned that helping others can be fun and make everyone happy. And from that day on, he always helped his mom in the kitchen, and they made many more yummy cakes together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a big green ball near the slide. Tim wanted to play with it, so he picked it up. "I will test this ball and see if it is fun to play with," Tim said. While he was playing, a rude girl named Amy came up to Tim. "That's my green ball!" she said. "Give it back!" Tim was sad, but he gave the ball back to Amy. Amy took the ball and went to play with her friends. Later, Tim saw Amy and her friends playing with the green ball. It went very high in the air and got stuck in a tree! Amy and her friends could not get it down. Tim had an idea. He found a long stick and used it to get the ball out of the tree. Everybody was happy and surprised! Amy said, "Thank you, Tim! You can play with us and the green ball now." Tim smiled and they all played together.

One day, a weak girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a big melon for a snack. Lucy saw the biggest melon she had ever seen! She wanted to carry it, but it was very heavy. Lucy tried to pick up the melon, but she struggled. "Mom, can you help me?" Lucy asked. Her mom said, "Sure, but let's get a smaller melon. This one is too big for you to carry." Lucy and her mom picked a smaller melon and went home. When they got home, Lucy's dad was in the kitchen. He had a surprise for them. He had made a big fruit salad with lots of melons! Lucy and her mom laughed. They did not need to buy a melon after all. They put the small melon with the other fruit and enjoyed the yummy fruit salad together.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet girl named Lily. She liked to wear her favorite uniform every day. It was a pretty dress with lots of colors. One day, Lily started to worry. She could not find her uniform. She looked in her room, under her bed, and in her closet. But her uniform was not there. Lily felt sad. Then, her mommy came into the room. She saw Lily worry and asked what was wrong. Lily told her about the missing uniform. Mommy smiled and showed Lily that her uniform was in the laundry. It was being washed so it would be clean and pretty again. Lily felt happy and hugged her mommy.

Once upon a time, there was a shy frog named Tim. Tim lived near a big pond. He liked to splash in the water all day. One day, he saw a jar near the pond. The jar was big and empty. Tim hopped over to the jar and said, "Hello, jar! Do you want to splash with me?" The jar did not talk. Tim was sad but he had an idea. He would fill the jar with water from the pond. Tim jumped into the pond and splashed water into the jar. He did this many times. The jar was now full of water. Tim said, "Now you can splash with me, jar!" Tim hopped into the jar and splashed in the water. He was very happy. From that day on, Tim and the jar were best friends. They splashed in the water together every day. Tim was not shy anymore. He had a friend to play with and they had lots of fun.
