Title: Spot the Lonely Dog and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a lonely dog named Spot. He lived in a small house with a big yard. Spot loved to play and run in circles all day. One day, a new dog named Max moved in next door. Spot was very happy and wanted to be friends with Max. Spot said to Max, "Hi, I am Spot! I like to perform tricks and run in circles. Do you want to play with me?" Max looked at Spot and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, Spot!" Spot and Max played together all day. They ran in circles and performed tricks for each other. But then, it started to rain very hard. The rain made the yard very wet and muddy. Spot and Max kept playing, but they got very dirty. Spot's owner saw them playing in the mud and was very angry. "Spot, you made a big mess! You cannot play with Max anymore," said Spot's owner. Spot was very sad and went inside his house. He looked out the window and saw Max playing alone in the rain. Spot felt lonely again, and he wished he could play with his friend Max.

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was an amazing swing. It was a hot day, and the sun was shining bright. Many kids loved to play on this swing, and they were very happy. One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park to play on the swing. She was very excited. But, she saw that the swing was different. It was not strong like before. The sun was so hot that it made the swing start to melt. Sue did not know this, and she went to play on the amazing swing. Sue sat on the swing and started to swing back and forth. Suddenly, the swing broke! Sue fell down and began to cry. The amazing swing was gone, and the kids were very sad. The hot sun had melted the swing, and now they could not play on it anymore. The park was not fun without the amazing swing.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely train. The train was sad because it had no friends. It would display big puffs of steam as it moved on the tracks. One day, the train saw a big hill. It thought, "I will go up the hill and maybe I will find a friend there." So, the train went up the hill, puffing steam as it went. When the train reached the top, it saw another train at the bottom of the hill. The other train was also lonely and displaying steam. The two trains were happy to see each other and became best friends. They didn't feel lonely anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big pink house. In the house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy box full of toys. One day, Sue saw a thief take her toys. Sue was sad. Sue had a plan to get her toys back. She asked her friend, Tom. Tom said, "Yes, let's get your toys!" They put on their shoes and went outside to find the thief. Sue and Tom saw the thief hiding behind a tree. Sue said, "Give me my toys back!" The thief was sorry and gave her toys back. Sue and Tom were happy. They went back to the pink house and played with the toys all day.

Once upon a time there were two friends named Sarah and Joe. They were both looking forward to having a fun day together. Sarah said to Joe, “Let’s go find a cookie!” Joe replied, “That sounds yummy!” So the two of them went off in search of a cookie. The two friends soon came to a bakery and saw a big cookie on the counter. Joe was very excited and said, “I want one!” Sarah said, “Let’s get two, one for each of us.” When Sarah and Joe got the cookies, Sarah noticed that a friend of theirs was looking at them with envious eyes. So Sarah handed the friend a cookie and said, “Here you go! Now we all can have one.” Joe smiled and said, “That’s very nice of you!” The moral of the story is that it’s important to be generous and share rather than feeling envious of what others have. Appearing generous makes us better people!
