Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy bear. He was so big and strong that he thought that he could never be defeated. So he went around the woods and challenged everyone to a fight. The bear was so confident that he thought he would always win. But one day, he stumbled upon a little rabbit. The rabbit was small and weak, but it didn't give up. It used all its courage and fought the big, hairy bear. To the bear's surprise, the rabbit won the fight. The bear was so mad that he started to tear up the ground with his big claws. He was so angry that he didn't even see the victory of the rabbit. The rabbit just looked sadly at the bear and went away. The moral of this story is that sometimes the little and weak ones can find their victory against the big and strong ones. But the bad ending is that sometimes the big and strong ones just don't want to accept their defeat.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to eat fruit. Her favorite fruit was the red apple. She also liked the yellow banana and the green pear. One day, Lily's mom told her not to be impatient. She said, "Wait for the fruit to be ready. If you eat it too soon, it will not taste good." But Lily did not listen. She wanted to eat the fruit now. So, she took the fruit from the tree before it was ready. Lily took a bite of the fruit, but it was not sweet. It was sour and tasted bad. She had spoiled the fruit by not waiting. Lily was sad. She learned that being impatient was not good. Next time, she would wait for the fruit to be ready.

Once upon a time, there was a brave bear. He loved to dance and twirl around the forest. Every day, he would dance around the trees and over the hills. But one day, there was a big storm and the brave bear was scared. He hid behind a tree and waited until the storm had passed. Suddenly, he noticed the most beautiful thing he had ever seen - a match! The brave bear knew he had to find out what it was. So with a brave heart, he ventured over to the match. The bear couldn't believe his eyes when it suddenly started to dance! The brave bear was so excited he started to dance too. All around the match, he bounced and twirled. They danced together for hours until the storm had passed. From that day onwards, the brave bear and the match were the best of friends. Whenever the brave bear was scared, the match would start to dance and he knew everything was going to be okay.

Once upon a time, in a gray house, there lived a kind doctor. The doctor liked to help people heal when they felt sick. One day, a little girl came to the doctor with a hurt leg. "Ouch! My leg hurts," said the little girl. The doctor looked at her leg and saw a small cut. "Don't worry, I will help you," said the doctor. He took some oil and put it on the girl's cut. The oil was magic and would help her heal fast. After a while, the little girl felt better. "Thank you, doctor!" she said with a big smile. The doctor was happy to help her. And so, the little girl went back home, feeling much better. From that day on, she always knew that the kind doctor in the gray house could help her heal when she was sick.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog. The dog liked to play with his ball. One day, he saw a poem on the ground. The dog did not know what it was, but he liked it. The dog had a friend, a cat. The cat wanted the poem too. They both wanted the poem, so they fought. They pulled and pulled, but no one won. The dog had an idea. He would lock the poem in a box. The cat did not like this idea. The dog locked the poem in the box, and the cat was sad. The dog was sad too. They lost the poem and were not friends anymore.
