Category: Intermediate
Title: Spike the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spike. Spike liked to play and run in the park. One day, he saw a big ball and wanted to play with it. But the ball was stuck in a tree. Spike was scared because the tree was very tall. Spike wanted to solve the problem. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He found a long stick and used it to poke the ball. The ball started to move, but it didn't fall down yet. Spike tried again and again. He was not scared anymore. With one last push, the ball fell from the tree. Spike was happy that he solved the problem. Now, he could play with the big ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big pond. In the pond, there was a flexible frog named Fred. Fred liked to jump and play with his friends. They had so much fun in the pond. One day, Fred and his friends wanted to get a ball that was far from the pond. They all jumped very high and far to get the ball. When they got the ball, they saw that it was not a ball. It was a big, round rock! Fred and his friends were surprised. They did not know what to do with the big rock. Then, they had an idea. They pushed the big rock into the pond. The rock made a big splash! They all laughed and played in the water. The big rock made their pond even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim liked to walk and play outside. One day, while he was playing, he found a small door in a big tree. He opened the door and saw a chamber inside the tree. Tim went inside the chamber and saw a little frog. The frog said, "Hi! Do you want to play with me?" Tim said, "Yes, I want to play!" So, the frog and Tim played together in the chamber. After a while, Tim wanted to go home. But when he tried to open the door, it was stuck! Tim and the frog tried hard to open it, but they couldn't. They were trapped in the chamber. Tim felt sad and missed his home. The frog felt sorry for Tim, but they couldn't do anything. They stayed in the chamber, and Tim never saw his family again.

Once upon a time there was a generous princess. Every day she liked to fill up her bucket with water from a nearby river. One day, when she was nearly finished, she saw an open door. The princess was curious and wanted to know what was behind the door, so she stepped inside. It was a magical room full of treasures. The magical room was filled with sparkling jewels, and the princess was so excited. The princess gasped in awe and said, "Oh wow! I wish I could fill my bucket with these jewels." Suddenly, she heard a voice. It said, "If you give a generous gift to someone, I will fill you bucket with treasures." The princess smiled. She knew exactly what to do. She gave away her beloved horses to her neighbours and filled her bucket with magical jewels. She was very happy. The princess couldn't believe her luck. It just goes to show, it pays to be generous!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved very much. One day, Tim saw that the light in his room was getting brighter and brighter. He felt dizzy from the bright light. Tim did not know what to do. Tim's toy car wanted to help him. The toy car knew that the light was increasing and making Tim feel bad. So, the toy car decided to find a way to stop the light from getting brighter. The toy car went on a big adventure to find help. The toy car met a nice bug who knew how to fix the light. The bug told the toy car that the light was getting brighter because of a magic rock. The toy car and the bug went to find the magic rock. They found the rock and used it to make the light go back to normal. Tim was so happy that he wasn't dizzy anymore, and he thanked his toy car and the bug for their help. They all became good friends and went on more adventures together.
