Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Gentle Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a gentle cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her ball in the yard. One day, while playing, she saw a glow near the big tree. Kitty went to the tree and found a little bug. The bug was glowing! Kitty said, "Hello, little bug. Why do you glow?" The bug said, "I glow to light up the night. I am a firefly." Kitty and the firefly became friends. They played together every day. When it was time for Kitty to go home, the firefly would help light her way. Kitty was happy to have a glowing friend by her side.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl called Lisa. She loved to gaze out of her bedroom window and dream of faraway places. One day, while she was gazing, Lisa saw something glittering in the garden. She hurried outside to take a closer look. It was a beautiful golden crown! Lisa was so excited. She picked it up and put it on her head. She imagined herself as a princess and sang a song as she skipped around her garden. Suddenly, the doorbell rang. Standing at the door was a fat, old lady with a basket full of tasty cookies. She said it was her crown and she wanted it back! Lisa refused to give it to her, saying she wanted to keep it forever. The old lady got angry and started shouting. Suddenly, Lisa started crying. She said she only wanted to play. The old lady's face softened and she smiled. She offered a deal to Lisa. She said Lisa could keep the crown if she gave the lady a cookie. Lisa happily agreed. She gave the old lady a cookie and the lady gave Lisa a big hug. Lisa put the crown back on her head and went back to whom she was before - a little girl who loved to dream while she gazed out of her bedroom window.

One day, John went to the zoo with his family. When they arrived, John was so excited to see the animals. He looked around and saw lions, giraffes, zebras and elephants. John looked at the giraffes and thought they were so cool. Suddenly, he heard his mom call out. "John," she said. "Come over here and look at this sweet elephant!" John ran over and saw the elephant. He thought it was so sweet. He looked at its trunk, its big ears and its long tail. He smiled and said, "I like the zoo!" His family laughed and agreed. They all had a great day at the zoo, looking at all the animals.

Tom and Mia are playing in the park. They like to run and slide and swing. But Tom wants to play with Mia's ball. He tries to take it from her. Mia says no, it is her ball. She holds it tight. Tom gets angry. He pushes Mia to the ground. Mia falls and scrapes her knee. It hurts a lot. She cries and cries. Tom feels bad. He did not mean to hurt Mia. He kneels next to her and says sorry. Mia looks at Tom. She sees his sorry face. She stops crying. She shows him her knee. It has blood and dirt on it. Tom says ouch. He takes a tissue from his pocket and wipes her knee. He blows on it gently. Mia smiles. She says thank you. She gives Tom her ball. She says they can share it. Tom is happy. He says thank you too. He gives Mia a hug. They stand up and hold hands. They go back to play with the ball. They are friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a human named Tom. Tom was a busy man. He had a big job in a big city. Every day, he would wake up early, put on his clothes, and go to work. One day, Tom was walking to work when he saw a cat. The cat was very cute, and it made Tom smile. But then, the cat made Tom sneeze! Tom was not happy. He did not like to sneeze. Tom went to work, but he could not stop sneezing. His friends at work laughed and said, "Tom, you are sneezing a lot today!" Tom felt sad. He wanted the sneezing to stop. When Tom went home that day, he saw the cat again. The cat was sitting by his door. Tom looked at the cat and said, "Please, cat, do not make me sneeze anymore." The cat looked at Tom and walked away. The next day, Tom did not sneeze at all. He was very happy. From that day on, Tom and the cat were friends. They played together every day, and Tom never sneezed again.
