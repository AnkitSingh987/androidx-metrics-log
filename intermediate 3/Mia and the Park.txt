Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia and the Park

Once upon a time, in a lovely, quiet town, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big dog named Max. They liked to play together in the park. One day, they met a new friend, a little boy named Tim. "Hi, I'm Tim," said the little boy. "Do you want to play with me?" Mia and Max were happy to play with Tim. They played catch and hide and seek. They had lots of fun in the park. But Tim did not understand Max when he barked. "Why does Max bark?" Tim asked Mia. "He is trying to talk," Mia said. "He wants you to understand him." Tim listened carefully to Max and soon, he could understand Max too. Now, Mia, Tim, and Max play together every day. They have lots of fun in the lovely, quiet town. And they all understand each other very well.

Once there was a boy named Timmy. He wanted to get to the top of the tall tree but there wasn't a way. He was a bit scared. Then he had an idea! He would get a ladder! He went to the store and got a ladder. He carried it back home and it was heavy. He was so happy! He put the ladder against the tree and climbed all the way to the top. At the top he saw a big nest with lots of eggs. He was so surprised! Timmy wanted to touch an egg, so he reached out. But then he heard a noise. He was scared and quickly returned to the ladder and ran from the tree. He was frightened but so happy he got to see the biggest eggs he ever saw! He couldn't wait to tell his mom and dad all about his adventure.

One morning, a little girl named Lucy woke up. She was so happy because today was her birthday. She put on her pretty dress and went to the kitchen. Her mom had made her a big cake. "Happy birthday, Lucy!" her mom said. "Thank you, Mom!" Lucy said with a big smile. Lucy's friends came to her house to play. They played games, and then it was time to eat the cake. But, oh no! Lucy's dog, Max, had jumped on the table and spoiled the cake. Lucy was sad, and her friends were sad too. They didn't know what to do. Then, Lucy's mom had an idea. She turned on some music and said, "Let's all dance!" Lucy and her friends started to dance, and they were happy again. They danced and laughed all morning. Even though the cake was spoiled, they still had a great time at Lucy's birthday party.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had a big toy box, but it was empty. He wanted to fill it with fun toys to play with. Tom's mom said, "You need money to buy toys." Tom wanted to find a way to get money. One day, Tom saw a big jar on the kitchen table. The jar was full of money. Tom thought, "If I can fit my hand in the jar, I can get the money." Tom tried to put his hand in the jar, but his hand was too big. Tom was sad because he could not get the money. Tom's mom saw him trying to get the money from the jar. She said, "Tom, you can't take that money. It is not yours." Tom was sad, but he knew his mom was right. He would find another way to get money for his toys. From that day on, Tom helped his mom with chores to earn money. Soon, his empty toy box was full of fun toys to play with.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl who was very brave and fierce. She wanted to take on the biggest challenges. One day, she decided to part her way from her friends and take an adventure. She came across a big open field with an arrow in it. She got very curious and decided to get the arrow. She stood in front of the arrow and said, "I'm going to bravely part my way to get the arrow!" But suddenly, a fierce-looking dragon stepped out and said, "You can't have that arrow! I need it for my mission!" The little girl was determined to get the arrow but she was too frightened of the dragon. She tried to be brave, but soon the fears got the best of her. Then, a voice told her, "You don't need to feel scared, just be brave and you can do anything you want." The girl understood the message, took a deep courage, and finally took the arrow. She didn't give up and found the courage to bravely part her way and get what she wanted. The moral of the story is that we should never give up, even when things seem tough, because with courage and determination we can do anything we set our minds to.
