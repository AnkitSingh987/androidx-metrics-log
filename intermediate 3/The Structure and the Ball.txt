Category: Intermediate
Title: The Structure and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big structure. It was a house. In this house, there was a boy. The boy was not nice. He was spoiled. He did not remember to say "please" and "thank you." The boy liked to play with his toys all day. One day, the boy found a new toy. It was a pretty ball. He played with the ball inside the house. He did not remember the rules. His mom said, "Do not play inside with the ball." But the boy did not listen. The boy threw the ball in the house. The ball hit a glass. The glass fell down and broke. The boy's mom was sad. The boy was sad too. The ball was taken away. The boy did not have fun anymore. The end.

One day, a little bird named Tim was in his nest. He wanted to fly in the open sky, but he was scared. His mom and dad would always encourage him to try, but he was still afraid. One morning, Tim saw a big storm coming to the land. The wind was strong and the trees were shaking. Tim knew he had to fly away to be safe, but he was still scared. Tim's mom and dad told him, "You can do it! We will be with you!" They encouraged him to be brave. Tim took a deep breath, opened his wings, and jumped. He started to fly! Tim was so happy and proud. The storm passed, and Tim was safe with his family.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little rabbit named Bobo. Bobo loved to pick yummy berries. One day, he saw a strange turn in the path. He thought it would be fun to explore it. Bobo walked down the turn, and he met a big, scary wolf. The wolf had a dangerous look in his eyes. "Hello, little rabbit," said the wolf. "Where are you going?" "I am picking berries," said Bobo. "Do you want to help me?" The wolf smiled and nodded. Bobo did not know that the wolf was very hungry and wanted to eat him. As they picked berries together, the wolf suddenly grabbed Bobo. The little rabbit tried to run away, but it was too late. The dangerous wolf ate poor Bobo, and the forest was quiet once again.

One day, Tom and Sam went to the park. They saw a big rocket. The rocket was open. They wanted to go inside. Tom said, "Let's go in the rocket!" Sam said, "No, I disagree. It is too big." Tom and Sam talked about it. They decided to go in the rocket together. Inside the rocket, they found many buttons. Tom pushed a button. The rocket started to shake. They went up, up, up into the sky. They saw the stars and the moon. Tom and Sam were happy they went in the rocket. They had a fun adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a big bee. The big bee lived in a hive. The hive was in a tree. The tree was in a park. The park had many kids. One day, a boy came to the park. The boy had a book. The boy sat under the tree. The boy read the book. The big bee saw the boy read. The big bee liked the book. The big bee flew to the boy. The big bee said, "Can I read too?" The boy was scared. But then, the boy saw the big bee was nice. The boy and the big bee read the book together. The big bee learned to read. The big bee read all day. The big bee read to the hive. The hive loved the stories. The big bee and the hive were happy. And the boy and the big bee were friends.

Once upon a time there was a dependable truck. He was really special because he could fill up with fuel and drive on roads all day long. Every day he would head to the farm. At the farm he would help the animals by delivering their food. The farmer counted on the truck to make sure the animals were spoiled with plenty of yummy food. The farmer loved the truck and he took good care of him. He always made sure he had enough fuel so he could do his job. One day as the truck was coming back from the farm, he felt something different in his engine. He had run out of fuel! The farmer was so worried about the truck, he quickly drove to help him. When the farmer finally arrived the truck was so happy. The farmer filled the tank with fuel and the truck felt better. He was then able to get back to work, delivering food to the animals and helping the farmer out. The truck was dependable and without him, the farm wouldn't be the same. He was sure happy to be spoiled by the farmer's love each and every day.
