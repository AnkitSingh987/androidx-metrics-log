Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little red bird. The bird lived in a small nest under a big tree. The bird had a best friend, a tiny ant. They liked to play and sing together. One day, a big mean cat came to the tree. The cat wanted to eat the bird and the ant. The bird and the ant were very scared. They needed to find a way to stop the cat. The ant had an idea. The ant would teach the bird to fly high and fast. The bird learned to fly up, up, up in the sky. The cat could not catch the bird. Then, the bird helped the ant hide in a safe place under a rock. The cat could not find the ant. The cat was sad that it could not catch the bird and the ant. The bird and the ant felt safe again. They were happy that they helped each other. From that day on, they played and sang together under the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a busy bee. The bee loved to fly around and visit many places. One day, the bee saw a big building. It was a university. The bee decided to enter the university. Inside, there were many big people. They were learning and talking. The bee flew around and watched them. The bee thought it was fun. The bee knew it was time to go home. It left the busy university and flew back to its hive. The bee told its friends about the big building. They all wanted to visit the university too.

One day, a boy named Tim found a colorful paper. He wanted to make a jet with it. So, he started to fold the paper. He made a big and nice jet. Tim was very happy. Tim went outside to play with his jet. He threw it up in the sky. The jet flew very far. Tim ran to catch the jet, but it went too fast. He was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The jet came back to Tim. It was like magic! Tim was very surprised and happy. Now, he could play with his jet all day. The colorful jet was his new best friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play in the mud. One day, Tom got very filthy. His hands, face, and clothes were all covered in mud. Tom's mom saw him and said, "Tom, you cannot come inside the house like that! You must clean up first." Tom knew his mom would not allow him to come inside when he was so dirty. Tom went to the garden to wash his hands. There, he saw a big cauliflower. He picked it up and took it to his mom. His mom was happy to see the cauliflower. She said, "Thank you, Tom! Now we can have this for dinner." Tom cleaned up and went inside the house. That night, Tom and his mom ate the cauliflower for dinner. It was so yummy! Tom was happy he had found the cauliflower and he promised to stay clean next time he played outside.

Once upon a time, there lived a smart girl in a big house. She was very curious and she wanted to try everything. One day, her mom saw her eating something from the kitchen. Her mom said to her, "It's not a good idea to taste things you don't know. You could get a bad taste." But the little girl ignored her mom and she took out something from the fridge. It looked shiny and she tasted it. It was bitter and she did not like the taste! The next day, the little girl woke up feeling very bad. She was too sick to go to school. Her mom was sad and angry. It turned out that the shiny thing was a dangerous poison! The little girl learned a lesson that day: never taste anything without knowing what it is.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a spoon on the floor. He picked it up and saw that it was a very big spoon. Tim thought it would be easy to eat his soup with the big spoon. He was very happy. Tim showed the spoon to his friend, Sarah. "Look at my big spoon!" he said. Sarah looked at the spoon and said, "Wow, that's a very big spoon! Can I try it?" Tim said, "Yes, you can try it. But please give it back to me after you try it, okay?" Sarah said, "Okay, thank you, Tim!" Sarah tried the big spoon with her soup. But the spoon was too big, and the soup spilled all over her. She was very sad and started to cry. Tim was sad too because his big spoon made his friend cry. They both learned that sometimes, even if something seems easy, it might not be good.
