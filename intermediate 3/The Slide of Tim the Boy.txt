Category: Intermediate
Title: The Slide of Tim the Boy

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide and was very excited. He ran to the slide and started to climb up. As he got closer, he saw something on the slide. It was a big, disgusting, and smelly fruit. It had been there for a long time and had started to rot. Tim did not want to touch it, so he called his mom. His mom came and saw the yucky fruit. She used a stick to push it off the slide. Tim was so happy that his mom helped him. He went down the slide and had a lot of fun. The park was a great place to play, even with the disgusting fruit.

Once upon a time, there was a monster. The monster was big and green. He lived in a dark cave near a river. The monster wanted to be successful and have many friends. One day, the monster met a little boy. The boy was not scared. The monster said, "Hi, do you want to be my friend?" The boy said, "Yes, but first, let's play in the river and soak our feet." The monster was happy and they went to the river. The monster and the boy played in the river. They soaked their feet and had fun. But soon, it got dark. The boy was scared and wanted to go home. The monster was sad because he wanted to be successful and keep his new friend. The boy left and never came back. The monster stayed in the cave, still not successful and with no friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big factory. Many people worked in the factory. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the factory with his mom. Tim was very impatient. He did not want to wait for his mom to finish her work. Tim saw a big machine and went close to it. He wanted to touch it but his mom said, "No, Tim! It is not safe." Tim was sad and began to weep. His mom felt bad and said, "Just wait a little more, we will go home soon." But Tim did not listen. He went to the machine again when his mom was not looking. He touched the machine and it started to make a loud noise. People came running to see what happened. Tim's mom was very worried. She knew that Tim did not listen to her. The big machine broke because of Tim. The factory had to stop working. Many people lost their jobs. Tim and his mom had to leave the town. They were very sad and wept as they said goodbye to their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a soft velvet toy cat that she loved very much. Every day, she would hold her cat and play with it. It was her favorite toy. One day, Lily could not find her velvet cat. She looked under her bed, behind the sofa, and in her toy box. She started to feel frustrated because she could not find her special cat. Lily wanted to hold her cat and play with it, but she did not know where it was. Finally, Lily found her velvet cat under a big pillow. She was so happy to see her cat again. She held her cat tightly and gave it a big hug. From that day on, Lily made sure to always keep her velvet cat close so she would not lose it again.

Once upon a time, there was a little otter named Ollie. Ollie was different from other otters because he liked to add rocks to his collection. He had big rocks, small rocks, and all kinds of different rocks. Ollie loved his rocks very much. One day, Ollie was playing near the river when he found a very special rock. This rock was shiny and different from all the other rocks he had. He was so happy and couldn't wait to add it to his collection. Ollie swam back to his home as fast as he could. When Ollie got home, he found that all of his rocks were gone! Ollie was very sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a big bird flew down and said, "I took your rocks to play with, but I didn't know they were yours. I'm sorry, Ollie." The big bird gave all the rocks back to Ollie, and they became good friends. Ollie was very happy to have his rocks back, and now he had a new friend to play with too.
