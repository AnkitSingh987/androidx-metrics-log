One day, a gray cat went out to play. The cat saw a little fly. The fly was fast and fun to watch. The cat tried to catch the fly, but the fly was too quick. The cat said, "Fly, let's play!" The fly said, "Okay, but you have to be fast!" They played and played, running and jumping all day long. The cat and the fly became good friends. After a long day, the gray cat and the fly were very tired. They decided to take a nap together. The cat curled up under a tree, and the fly rested on a leaf. They slept and had sweet dreams, happy to have found a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim had a dream every night. In the dream, he was a big hero who would prevent bad things from happening. He would help people and animals who were weak. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. He wanted to help the cat, just like in his dream. He called out to the cat, "Don't worry, I'll save you!" Tim tried to climb the tree, but he was too weak. He could not get the cat down. A big wind came and blew the cat out of the tree. But the cat did not land on the ground. Instead, it fell into a river. Tim could not swim, so he could not save the cat. The cat was carried away by the water. Tim was sad because he could not prevent the bad thing from happening, just like in his dream.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max liked to tease the little cats in the park. He would run around them and bark loudly. The cats did not like this, and they would run away. Max thought this was very funny. One day, the cats had a plan. They wanted to stop Max from teasing them. They decided to have a pretend war with the dogs. The cats hid behind a big tree and waited for Max to come. When Max came, the cats jumped out and began to chase him. Max was very surprised and started to run away. As Max was running, he tripped on a stick and fell. The cats stopped chasing him and began to laugh. Max was very embarrassed. He realized that teasing others was not nice. From that day on, Max never teased the cats again, and they all became good friends.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the garden. They have a big sandbox and a slide. They also have a basket of toys. One day, they are playing with their toys. Lily has a doll and Ben has a car. They make noises and pretend to go on adventures. They are having fun. But then, Lily sees a banana on the table. She is hungry. She wants the banana. She leaves her doll and runs to the table. She grabs the banana and peels it. She takes a big bite. "Hey, that's my banana!" Ben shouts. He leaves his car and runs to the table. He tries to take the banana from Lily. They pull and tug. The banana breaks in half. They both fall on the ground. "Stop it, you two!" Mom says. She comes out of the house. She sees the mess. She is not happy. "You need to share and be tidy. Look at your toys. They are everywhere. And look at the banana. It is wasted. You need to clean up and say sorry." Lily and Ben feel bad. They know they did wrong. They say sorry to each other. They hug and make up. They pick up their toys and put them in the basket. They throw the banana peel in the bin. Mom smiles. She is proud of them. She brings them two cups of juice. "Here, you can drink this. It is good for you. And next time, ask before you take something. OK?" Lily and Ben nod. They take the cups and drink the juice. They say thank you to Mom. They are thirsty. The juice is sweet and cold. They feel better. They go back to the garden. They play with their toys again. They share and take turns. They are happy. They are tidy. They are good twins.
