Category: Intermediate
Title: Polly the Girl and the Apple

Once there was a little girl named Polly who loved to explore. One day she came across an old cushion lying on the ground in her garden. She knelt down and cautiously touched it. It felt soft, like a fluffy cloud. Polly was curious, but when she heard something crinkle inside the cushion, she paused. Then she carefully peeled it open. What she found inside filled her with excitement – a shiny red apple! She could almost taste it as she picked it up. But just as she was about to take a bite, she heard a nosy voice from behind. It was her nosy neighbor, Mrs. Ann. “What are you up to, little girl?” Polly was about to answer when Mrs. Ann gasped. She had spotted the wrinkled up newspaper inside the cushion. “Oh my! I see now why you were so pausey. This is no ordinary cushion! I planted an apple tree in my garden last summer, and it looks like one of my apples has gone missing!” Polly's heart raced as Mrs. Ann took the apple from her and placed it back in the cushion. But then the old lady smiled. “Now, doesn't this make you a lucky girl? You get to take the apple home with you!” The apple was delicious, and Polly would never forget the lucky day she found it

Once upon a time, there was a unique ball. It loved to bounce all day long. One day, it bounced far away from its home. It was lost and sad. As the ball bounced, it saw a big mill. The mill was where they made yummy things to eat. The ball went inside and met a kind man. The man saw the ball and smiled. The kind man helped the ball find its way home. The ball was happy and thanked the man. They became good friends. From that day on, the ball and the man would bounce and play together at the mill.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat sandwiches. One sunny day, he went to the park with his mom. He was very happy because his mom made him a big sandwich for lunch. At the park, Tim saw a small, frightened bird. The bird was hungry and couldn't find any food. Tim said, "Hi bird! Are you hungry? I have a sandwich!" The bird looked at Tim and nodded its head. Tim broke off a small piece of his sandwich and gave it to the bird. The bird was very happy and ate the piece of sandwich. Tim and the bird became friends. They played together in the park. Tim counted the number of times the bird flew in a circle around him. They had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said goodbye to his new friend. The bird flew away, but Tim knew they would play together again soon.

Kyle and his dad were walking on a sunny day. They stopped next to a big field full of tall grass. "Dad, what are we going to do here?" Kyle asked. "We're going to create a trail," Dad said. Kyle got excited. He loved making trails. He grabbed two sticks and started to organize the tall grass. After a few minutes, they had made a long, windy trail through the field. Dad picked up a few rocks and used them to show where the trail went. "Now let's go explore the trail," Dad said. So Kyle and Dad began walking down the trail. The sun was bright and the birds were singing. It was a beautiful day. The two of them followed the trail until it ended. They looked back at the trail they had created and both smiled. The trail was perfectly organized and they were so proud of what they had done. "Let's do this again soon," Dad said. "Yes! Let's make more trails," Kyle said.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big book in his room. He opened the book and saw many yummy pictures of food. Tim wanted to eat them all, so he told his mom, "Mom, I want to make the yummy food in this book." Tim's mom said, "Okay, let's cook together!" They started to make the yummy food from the book. They made a big cake, some cookies, and a pie. Tim was so happy to eat them all. But then, something unexpected happened. As they were eating, they heard a loud scream from the book. They looked at the book and saw that the yummy food pictures were gone! Instead, there were pictures of little food people who were scared because they were being eaten. Tim and his mom stopped eating and said, "We're sorry, little food people. We won't eat you anymore." And the little food people smiled and went back to their book world.
