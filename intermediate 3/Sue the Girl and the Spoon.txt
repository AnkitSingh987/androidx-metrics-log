Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Spoon

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big dream. She wanted to make the best soup ever. She wanted to be rich with friends who loved her soup. One day, Sue found a big pot. She put water, vegetables, and meat in the pot. She began to stir the soup. She stirred and stirred. The smell of the soup filled the air. It was a good smell. Friends came to Sue's house. They wanted to try her soup. They all took a spoon and tasted it. They loved it! Sue's dream came true. She was rich with friends who loved her soup. They all played and laughed together. And that is the end of the story.

Once upon a time, there was a generous reindeer. Every day, it ran and hopped around in the meadow. It loved to catch butterflies and chase dragonflies. One day, it saw something sparkly in the sky. It was Santa Claus! He was on his sleigh, being pulled by eight reindeer. They were all the same size, but the generous reindeer looked the biggest and strongest. Santa saw the generous reindeer and asked if it wanted to join him. The reindeer said yes, and soon it was catching air with the other reindeer, pulling Santa's sleigh. They flew together all night and had such fun! When the sun came up, the generous reindeer had become part of the magical reindeer team. It felt happy and proud for being so brave and generous.

One day, a boy named Tim found a weird raft by the lake. It was big and round. Tim liked the raft, so he pushed it into the water. He wanted to play with it. Tim jumped on the raft and started to splash in the water. The water was cool and fun. Tim laughed as he splashed more and more. The sun was shining, and Tim was happy. Soon, Tim's friend, Sue, came to the lake. She saw Tim and the weird raft. Sue wanted to play too. They both splashed and laughed on the raft. They had a great day at the lake.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a birdcage in her room. Inside the birdcage was a stubborn bird named Blue. Lucy wanted to play with Blue, but Blue wanted to remain in the birdcage. "Blue, come out and play with me," Lucy said. Blue shook his head and said, "No, I want to stay in my birdcage." Lucy was sad, but she had an idea to make Blue come out and play. Lucy went to the kitchen and got some bread. She put the bread near the birdcage and said, "Blue, I have some yummy bread for you. Come out and eat it with me." Blue looked at the bread and thought it looked tasty. But he was still stubborn and said, "No, I want to remain in my birdcage." Lucy was sad, but she did not give up. She went outside and found a pretty flower. She put the flower near the birdcage and said, "Blue, look at this pretty flower. Come out and smell it with me." Blue looked at the flower and thought it was pretty. But he was still stubborn and said, "No, I want to remain in my birdcage." Lucy was very sad, but she had one more idea. She got her favorite toy, a soft teddy bear, and put it near the birdcage. She said, "Blue, this is my favorite toy. Come out and play with it with me." Blue looked at the teddy bear and thought it looked fun. He was not so stubborn anymore and said, "Okay, Lucy, I will come out and play with you." Lucy was so happy that Blue came out of the birdcage. They played with the teddy bear, ate the bread, and smelled the flower together. From that day on, Blue and Lucy were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a figure named Bobo. Bobo was a clear toy made of glass. He lived in a big box with other toys. Every day, the toys would play and have fun together. One day, a big wind came and blew the box away. Bobo and his friends were scared. They did not know what to do. Just then, a kind bird saw them and wanted to help. The bird flew down and picked up Bobo with its beak. The bird flew high in the sky to find the box. As the bird flew, it saw a big tree. The tree had a big bow on it. The bird thought the bow was the box and flew towards it. But when it got close, it saw that it was not the box. It was a big party for the tree! The tree was very happy and invited Bobo and the bird to stay. So, they all had fun at the party and forgot about the box. In the end, Bobo and the bird made many new friends and they all lived happily together.
