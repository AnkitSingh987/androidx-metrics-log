Category: Intermediate
Title: Mark the Dog and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a big dog named Mark. Mark was a very scared dog. He was scared of the dark, loud noises, and even his own shadow. One day, he heard a little girl named Sue crying. Sue was lost and scared too. Mark was scared, but he wanted to help Sue. He said, "Don't cry, I will help you find your home." Sue stopped crying and looked at Mark. She felt a little better with Mark by her side. They walked through the village, looking for Sue's house. As they walked, the sun started to set. Mark was scared of the dark, but he knew he had to be brave for Sue. They finally found Sue's house just as the sun went down. Sue hugged Mark and said, "Thank you for helping me, even though you were scared too." Mark felt happy and proud that he could help his new friend. From that day on, Mark was a little less scared, because he knew he could be brave when it mattered.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom lived in a big house with a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat popcorn. One day, Sue made a big bowl of popcorn and sat down to watch a movie. Tom wanted to gain some popcorn too. He jumped on the couch and meowed, "Can I have some popcorn, please?" Sue looked at Tom and said, "Okay, but only a little." Tom was so happy that he stopped being grumpy. Tom and Sue shared the popcorn and watched the movie together. They laughed and had a lot of fun. From that day on, Tom was not grumpy anymore when he had popcorn with Sue. They became the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a baby. The baby wanted to play with the pretty broccoli. So mommy said, "OK, you can play with it, but be careful." The baby was so excited and she giggled as she tossed the broccoli around. She wasn't paying attention and she accidentally buried the broccoli in the garden. Mommy scolded the baby and said, "You should always listen to me! Now you won't be able to play with the broccoli anymore." The baby was sad and started to cry. She learned her lesson - it's important to always listen to mommy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a long tail that wagged when he was happy. One day, Spot went on a walk and got very dirty. His tail was covered in mud. Spot's mom saw him and said, "Spot, you need to bathe. You are very dirty." Spot did not like to bathe, but he knew his mom was right. So, he went to the big, open tub and got ready for his bath. As Spot's mom washed him, his tail started to wag. The water was warm and it felt good. Spot said, "Thank you, mom. I feel clean now." After the bath, Spot's tail was clean and he was happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big box of green blocks. Tim was very happy. He started to play with the blocks. He built a tall tower with the green blocks. Then, a girl named Sue arrived at the park. Sue saw Tim playing with the green blocks. She asked, "Can I play with the green blocks too?" Tim said, "Yes, let's play together!" So, Tim and Sue played with the green blocks and built a big house. But then, something unexpected happened. A small dog came running to the park. The dog bumped into the green block house. The green blocks fell down! Tim and Sue were sad. But the dog wagged its tail and looked sorry. Tim and Sue laughed and said, "It's okay, we can build again!" So, they all played together and built an even bigger green block house.

Once upon a time, there was a unique little ant. He lived in a small house near a big river. Every day, he would go out and look for food to bring back to his family. One day, it rained a lot. The river got bigger and bigger. Soon, it was a flood! The water came close to the little ant's house. He was scared. He needed to finish getting food before the water got to his house. The unique little ant ran fast. He found food and carried it back to his house. He finished just in time! The water stopped before it got to his house. The little ant and his family were safe and happy. They had food to eat and a dry house to live in.
