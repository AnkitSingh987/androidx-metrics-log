Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Fish and the Gem

One day, a fish named Sam wanted to swim far away. He was a thoughtful fish who liked to find gems. Sam swam in the deep blue sea, looking for a shiny gem. Soon, Sam saw a big gem on the sea floor. He wanted to take it home. But a crab named Carl was there too. Carl wanted the gem as well. They both tried to grab the gem at the same time. Sam and Carl looked at each other. They knew they could not both have the gem. Sam thought for a moment. Then he had an idea. "Let's share the gem," Sam said. Carl agreed. They both swam back to their homes, happy to share the gem with each other.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful land, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. Every day at noon, he would go to the park to play with his friends. One sunny day, Tim saw a big ball in the park. He wanted to gain a new friend to play with the ball. He walked up to a girl named Sue and asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played with the ball and had lots of fun. They laughed and played until the sun went down. From that day on, Tim and Sue became best friends. They played together every day at noon. They were happy to have gained a new friend in each other. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big house with a porch. A rich man lived in the house. He had a big garden with many pretty flowers. Every day, he sat on the porch and looked at his garden. One day, a little girl walked by the house. She saw the rich man on the porch and said, "Hello! I like your flowers. Can you show me your garden?" The rich man smiled and said, "Of course! Come with me." They walked in the garden together. The rich man showed the little girl all the flowers. They laughed and had fun. The little girl was very happy. From that day on, they became good friends and enjoyed the beautiful garden together.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a big house with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to play together all day long. One sunny day, Fluffy and Lily went outside to play. Fluffy saw a tall tree and wanted to climb it. He stretched his legs and jumped up onto the tree. Lily clapped her hands and laughed as she watched Fluffy climb higher and higher. After playing for a while, Fluffy and Lily got very thirsty. They went back inside the house to find something to drink. Lily's mom gave them a big cup of juice to share. They drank the juice together, and it made them feel happy and refreshed. Then, they went back outside to play some more.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars in the mud. One day, while playing, one of his cars got stuck in the mud. Tim was sad because his car was not clean anymore. Tim's dad saw him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can repair your car and make it clean again." Tim smiled and they went inside the house. They washed the car with water and soap, and the mud came off. The car was clean again, just like new. Tim was very happy to have his clean car back. He thanked his dad for helping him repair it. From that day on, Tim learned to take better care of his toys and to clean them after playing in the mud.

Once upon a time, there was a small boat that sailed in the blue sea. On the boat were two friends - Luke and Sara. "Let's cross the sea!" shouted Luke. "Yes!" agreed Sara. So off they sailed. Day after day, they sailed across the wide blue sea. As they sailed, Luke and Sara were filled with compassion. They helped fish swimming near the boat, they watched beautiful birds as they flew in the sky, and they said hello to every creature they met. After a few days, Luke and Sara finally reached the other side of the sea. They were so happy that they had travelled safely. The friends hugged each other and smiled. The end.
