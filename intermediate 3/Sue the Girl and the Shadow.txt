Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Shadow

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw her shadow on the ground. It looked very big and graceful. Sue said "hello" to her shadow, but it did not answer. Sue kept walking and her shadow followed her. She tried to run away from it, but it was always there. She felt sad because she wanted to play with her shadow, but it wouldn't answer her. Then, something unexpected happened. A small cat walked by and saw Sue's shadow. The cat started to play with it! Sue was happy to see that her shadow could play after all. She joined the cat and they all played together. Sue learned that her shadow could be a fun friend, even if it didn't answer her.

Tom was a very sad boy. He had lost his special notebook and he couldn't find it anywhere. He looked everywhere he could. He looked under the bed, behind the couch and in the closet. But the notebook was nowhere to be found. Tom was so sad that he started to cry. His mom could see how upset he was, so she said "Let's clean up and see if it's here". So Tom started cleaning the house. He moved the chairs and cleaned under the table. Then he checked inside his drawers and the cupboard. But the notebook was nowhere to be seen. Finally, Tom checked in the coat pocket. And there it was! His special notebook! Tom was so happy he gave his mom a big hug. Tom was not so sad anymore. He had learned that it was important to clean and take care of his things.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lonely boy named Tim. Tim had no friends, so he would play by himself every day. One day, while playing with his ball, it went into the yard of a big house. Tim was scared to go get it. Inside the big house, there was a kind nurse named Mary. Mary saw Tim looking sad and asked, "Why are you sad, little boy?" Tim said, "I lost my ball in your yard, and I am too scared to get it." Mary smiled and said, "Don't be scared, I will help you." Mary went into the yard and borrowed Tim's ball. She gave it back to him and said, "Here is your ball. Remember, it is okay to ask for help when you need it." Tim was very happy and thanked Mary. They became good friends and played together every day. Tim learned that it is important to ask for help when you need it, and he was never lonely again.

Once upon a time there lived two cats. One was called Bob and the other was called Sue. Bob was a fat cat and Sue was a skinny cat. Every morning Bob would put something in Sue's bowl. He would fill it with food! One day Sue asked Bob, “Why do you fill my bowl with food?” Bob smiled and replied, “Because I like to see you eat. It makes me happy when you're full and happy.” Sue said, “Thanks Bob. That’s so kind of you. I will always be thankful for your help.” Bob and Sue were the best of friends and Bob always filled Sue's bowl with food to make sure that she was well-fed and happy.

Once upon a time, in a big sky, there was a little star. The star was very tough. It could not break or fall. The star had a friend, a small bird. The bird liked to fly high and visit the star every day. One day, the bird saw a big tree with a fruit that looked like the star. The bird thought, "I want the star to taste this fruit." So, the bird took the fruit and flew to the star. The star was happy to see the fruit and wanted to taste it too. The bird gave the fruit to the star, and the star tasted it. The fruit was sweet and yummy. The star said, "Thank you, bird, for the yummy fruit!" The bird smiled and said, "You're welcome, tough star!" They were very happy and stayed friends forever.
