Category: Intermediate
Title: Emma the Girl and the Balloon

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma had a special journal. She loved to fill the journal with her drawings. Every day, she would draw something new. One sunny day, Emma went to the fair with her mom. They saw many fun things. Emma saw a big, round balloon. She asked her mom, "Can I have the balloon, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can." Emma was very happy. She took her balloon and her journal to a bench. She started to draw the fair in her journal. She drew the big wheel, the yummy ice cream, and her pretty balloon. Emma loved her day at the fair and her journal was full of happy memories.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat loved to sail on the water. The water was gentle and the boat was happy. One day, the boat saw a big wake in the water. The boat was scared, but it wanted to sail across the wake. So, the boat went very slow and gentle. The boat sailed over the wake and made it to the other side. The boat was so happy and proud. Now, the boat knew it could sail over any wake, as long as it was gentle and slow. And the boat lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. One day, Sue had a big idea. She wanted to celebrate the night with Tom. Sue said to Tom, "Let's have a fun night party!" Tom meowed happily, and they started to plan. They put up pretty lights and set out yummy food. They invited all their friends, like the dog, the bird, and the rabbit. As the night began, everyone came to the party. They played fun games, ate good food, and danced to happy songs. Sue and Tom were so happy to celebrate the night with their friends. And when the night was over, they knew they had the best party ever.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and find bugs and stones. They had a big bucket to put their treasures in. They were very happy. But then they heard a loud noise. It was their mom. She was angry. She saw that they were dirty and that they had made a mess. She said, "Lily and Ben, come inside right now! You need to take a bath and clean up. And don't touch anything with your dirty hands!" Lily and Ben were scared. They did not want to go inside. They did not want to take a bath. They wanted to keep playing. They ran to the shed and hid inside. They locked the door with a key. They thought they were safe. But their mom followed them. She knocked on the door. She said, "Lily and Ben, open the door! This is not a game. You need to listen to me. I am your mom and I love you. But you also need to obey me and respect our home. Please, unlock the door and come out." Lily and Ben felt sorry. They knew their mom was right. They had been naughty and rude. They did not want to make their mom worry or sad. They found the key and unlocked the door. They came out and hugged their mom. They said, "We are sorry, mom. We love you too. We will take a bath and clean up. We will not hide from you again." Their mom smiled. She said, "Thank you, Lily and Ben. I am glad you are sorry. I forgive you. But remember, playing is fun, but you also need to be responsible and careful. Dirt is not bad, but you also need to be clean and healthy. And hiding is not smart, but you also need to be honest and brave." Lily and Ben nodded. They learned their lesson. They went inside with their mom. They took a bath and cleaned up. They put their treasures in a safe place. They were happy again.
