Category: Intermediate
Title: The Potato and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a little potato. The potato was round and brown, and it lived in the ground. It was a harmless potato, and it had many friends. One day, the potato decided it was time to settle down and grow big. It found a nice spot in the garden, next to a tall tree. The tree gave the potato a little shade, and the potato was happy. As the days went by, the potato grew bigger and bigger. It made friends with the other plants in the garden. They all played together and enjoyed the warm sun. The potato was very happy to have settled down in such a nice place. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there were two friends, a dog and a frog. Every day, the dog would feed the frog a yummy treat. One day, the frog asked the dog for a drum. The dog was surprised but agreed to get the frog his own drum. The next day, the dog brought the frog an original drum. The frog was so excited! He placed the drum on the ground and began to play. He tapped his hands and feet on the drum and sang a happy song. The dog then came flying to see the frog's amazing performance. She smiled and said: "That was wonderful! You play the drum so well!" The frog smiled proudly and thanked the dog for getting him the drum. They hugged and laughed joyfully. From that day onwards, they both would perform and feed each other yummy treats.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a clean tube in his toy box. He did not know what it was for, but he liked it a lot. Tim took the tube with him everywhere he went. He gazed at it all day, trying to think of what he could do with it. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his tube. He saw a big hill and had an idea. He put the tube on the hill and sat down inside it. Tim gazed at the bottom of the hill, excited for his new game. Tim pushed himself and started to roll down the hill in his clean tube. He went faster and faster, laughing and having fun. When he reached the bottom, he was so happy. Tim knew that the tube was special and he would have many more fun days with it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to climb. He would climb trees, rocks, and even his bed. One day, Tom found an old suit in his dad's closet. The suit was big and brown. Tom put on the old suit and went outside to play. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he climbed, he met a squirrel. The squirrel looked at Tom and said, "Why are you wearing that old suit?" Tom smiled and said, "I found it in my dad's closet. I like it!" The squirrel laughed and said, "It's too big for you. But it's funny!" Tom and the squirrel became friends. They played and climbed trees together all day long. At the end of the day, Tom went home and took off the old suit. He put it back in his dad's closet. Tom was happy he had found the suit and made a new friend. He couldn't wait to play with the squirrel again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a kind dog named Buddy. Buddy was very compassionate. He loved to play with his ball and help his friends. One day, Buddy was playing with his ball when he saw a little bird in trouble. The bird was stuck in a tree, and it could not fly away. Buddy wanted to rescue the bird. He thought of a goal to get the bird out of the tree. Buddy tried to jump high to reach the bird, but he could not jump high enough. He did not give up. He found a long stick and used it to help the bird. Buddy used the stick to push the bird gently. The bird became free and could fly again. The bird was very happy that Buddy rescued it. Buddy was happy too. He knew that being compassionate and helping his friends was important.
