Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and the Door

Sara was playing with her dolls in her room. She liked to make them talk and have tea parties. She heard a knock on the door. It was her mom. "Hi, sweetie. I have something for you," her mom said. She gave Sara a big hug and a small envelope. "What is it, mom?" Sara asked. "It's a note from your grandma. She sent it to you because she loves you and misses you. Do you want to open it?" her mom said. Sara nodded and tore the envelope. She pulled out a piece of paper with some words and a picture. She could not read the words, but she recognized the picture. It was a drawing of her and her grandma holding hands and smiling. "Look, mom! It's me and grandma!" Sara exclaimed. "Yes, it is. Do you like it?" her mom said. "I love it! It's so pretty. Can I hang it on my wall?" Sara said. "Of course you can. Grandma would be happy to see that. She also wrote you a nice message. Do you want me to read it to you?" her mom said. "Yes, please," Sara said. Her mom read the note aloud. It said: "Dear Sara, I hope you are having fun and being good. I miss you very much and I can't wait to see you again. You are my special granddaughter and I am very proud of you. I made this picture for you to surprise you and make you smile. I hope you like it. I love you with all my heart. Love, Grandma." Sara felt warm and happy inside. She hugged the note and then hugged her mom. "Thank you, mom. Thank you, grandma. I love you both. I am very grateful for this note. It is the best surprise ever," Sara said.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a clumsy gorilla named Gogo. Gogo loved to play and have fun with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Gogo and his friends decided to play a new game. They wanted to see who could turn around the fastest. They all stood in a circle and started to spin. Gogo tried to turn fast like his friends, but he was so clumsy that he fell down. His friends helped him up and they all laughed together. Gogo's friends knew he was clumsy, but they loved him just the way he was. They showed Gogo how to turn slowly and carefully. With practice, Gogo got better at turning. Soon, he could turn almost as fast as his friends. They all played together, happy and full of love for each other. And Gogo learned that it's okay to be clumsy, as long as you have good friends by your side.

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Tony. Tony was three years old. He was in the park with his mum and dad. Tony and his parents were having a picnic on the grass. Suddenly, Tony saw a white sock lying on the ground. He picked it up and ran over to show his mum. Tony's mum said, "Where did you find this sock, Tony?" Tony said, "I found it in the park, mum." His mum looked guilty and said, "I think this sock belongs to your dad. He must have left it here." Tony laughed and said, "Oh Dad, you are so silly!" His Dad laughed too and said, "Yes, I am very guilty! I must have left it here when we were in the park. Let's go on our walk now." So Tony, Mum and Dad continued their walk in the park. They had a lot of fun and Tony never forgot the guilty sock!

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play and run all day long. He also loved to tell jokes to his friends. His favorite joke was about a sour lemon. Max would say, "Why did the lemon stop rolling? Because it ran out of juice!" All his friends would laugh and laugh. One sunny day, Max was very tired from playing. He decided to take a nap under a big tree. He lay down on the soft grass and closed his eyes. As he slept, he dreamed about telling his sour lemon joke to all his friends. When Max woke up from his nap, he saw his friends gathered around the tree. They had been waiting for him to wake up so they could play together. Max was so happy to see them. He jumped up and told his sour lemon joke to everyone. All his friends laughed and they played together until the sun went down. And that night, Max went to bed with a big smile on his face, excited for another day of fun and laughter.
