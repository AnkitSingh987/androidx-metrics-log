Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the River

One day, a big dog named Max went to play near the river. He saw a small cat named Luna. Luna was scared of the water because of a big flood that happened before. Max wanted to help Luna not be scared. Max said, "Luna, let's play near the water. I will be with you, so you don't need to be scared." Luna was still scared, but she wanted to be brave. So, she went with Max to play near the water. As they played, Max saw a big stick in the water. He jumped in to get it. When he came back, Luna was gone! Max felt ashamed that he left Luna alone. He looked and looked for her. Then, he saw Luna up in a tree! She had climbed up to get away from the water. Max was happy that Luna was safe. They went home and promised to play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat yummy food. His favorite yummy food was cookies. He liked to eat cookies with his mommy and daddy. One day, it started to get dark outside. Tim was scared of the dark. He asked his mommy to send his daddy to get some cookies. Tim thought if he had some yummy cookies, he wouldn't be scared of the dark anymore. Daddy went to the store and got the cookies. When he came back, Tim, mommy, and daddy all ate the yummy cookies together. Tim was not scared of the dark anymore. They all smiled and had a good time.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty dog named Spot. Spot loved to dig holes in the yard. One day, Spot dug a big hole and found a ball. He was very happy. Spot's mom saw the big hole and the dirt on his skin. She knew he had been naughty. She said, "Spot, you must be punished for making a mess." Spot felt sad because he didn't want to be punished. Spot's mom made him stay inside the house for one day. Spot learned his lesson and promised to be a good dog. From that day on, he never dug holes in the yard again. And he and his mom lived happily ever after.

There was a boy named Tom. He was miserable because he could not say a word. Every day, he tried to talk, but no words came out. One day, Tom found a magic pair of scissors. The scissors could cut out words. Tom cut the word "hello" from a book. He put the word in his mouth and he could say it! Now, Tom was not miserable anymore. He cut more words and ate them. He could talk to his friends and family. They were all happy and played together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a gentle cat sitting by a tree. Tim wanted to be friends with the cat, so he walked close to it. But the cat was scared and ran away. Tim felt sad. Tim saw his friend Sue and told her, "I want to be friends with the cat, but it ran away." Sue said, "You need to be slow and quiet, so the cat won't be scared." They saw the cat again. This time, Tim and Sue walked very slow and quiet. They got close to the cat. The cat did not run away. It let Tim and Sue pet it. They were all friends now.

Once upon a time, there was a big box. Inside the box there was something special. It was a pillow! The pillow was smooth and fluffy. The little girl loved the pillow so much. She wanted to keep the pillow safe and sound, so she decided to store it in her bedroom. Every morning when she woke up, the girl always gave the pillow a big hug. One day, she even decided to take the pillow with her wherever she went! On a hot day, the girl brought her pillow to the beach. She laid it on the sand and it felt so smooth. The girl spent the day playing games and swimming in the sea, but she never forgot to give her pillow a big hug whenever she passed by. In the end, the girl took her pillow back home and stored it in her bedroom, safe and sound.
