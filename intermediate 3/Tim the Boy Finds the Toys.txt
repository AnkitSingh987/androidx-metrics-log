Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to be organized. He always put his toys away and kept his room clean. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, it's time to bring you for a haircut." At the haircut place, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue did not like to be organized. Her hair was messy and her clothes were not neat. Tim said, "Hi Sue, I am getting a haircut. You should get one too, so you can be organized like me." Sue did not like what Tim said. She got angry and pushed Tim. Tim fell and got hurt. Tim's mom came to help him. The bad ending made Tim and his mom sad. The moral of the story is to be kind to others and not to judge them by how they look.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to dream at night. In his dreams, he would play with his friends and have fun. One day, Tim had a dream about a big green ball. The green ball was very big and very fun to play with. Tim and his friends played with the green ball all day long. They were so happy. But Tim was sometimes careless. He did not look where he was going when he played. He bumped into a tree and the green ball flew away. Tim and his friends were sad. They looked for the green ball, but they could not find it. Tim learned to be more careful when he played.

Peter was a 3 year old boy who lived in a small house. One day he wanted to go on an adventure. He looked around his house for something interesting. He noticed a bookshelf in the corner of his room. He walked over and tried to move it, but it was too heavy. He squatted down and started to whistle. Suddenly, a small, magical door opened in the bookshelf! Peter was very excited. He peeped through the door and saw large trees and bright flowers. He wanted to explore, but he was nervous because it looked a bit difficult. At that moment, his mum walked in the room. "Peter, what are you doing?" asked his mum. Peter explained what he'd found and asked if he could go on an adventure. After some thought, his mum said yes. She even agreed to come with him. Peter smiled and was so happy. Together, he and his mum ventured through the small door and enjoyed a wonderful adventure.

One day, a sleepy cat named Tom was taking a nap under a big tree. He liked to sleep a lot because it made him feel good. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. It was a very nice day. Suddenly, Tom smelled smoke. He opened his eyes and saw a small fire near his tree. Tom knew he had to rush and get help. He ran fast to find his friend, a dog named Max. Max was big and strong, and Tom knew he could help put out the fire. Tom found Max and told him about the fire. They both rushed back to the tree, but when they got there, the fire was gone. Instead, they found a little girl with a red bucket. She had seen the fire too and had put it out with water from her bucket. Tom and Max were very happy and thanked the little girl. The unexpected help from the little girl saved their tree, and they all became good friends.

One sunny day, a little girl named Amy went to play in her backyard. She saw a calm, pretty cat sitting on the fence. Amy wanted to pet the cat, but it was too high for her to reach. She felt sad. Amy had an idea. She found a big box and put it under the fence. She stood on the box to get closer to the cat. But when she tried to pet the cat, she slipped and fell off the box. She began to cry. Amy's mom heard her cry and came outside. She helped Amy up and gave her a hug. Then, the calm cat jumped down from the fence and walked over to Amy. The cat let Amy pet it, and they became friends. Amy was happy again.
