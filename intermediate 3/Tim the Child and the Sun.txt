Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Child and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a nice child named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Tim found something on the ground. It was a big ice cream cone. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to eat the ice cream, but he saw a sad girl named Sue. Sue was crying because she lost her ice cream. Tim thought for a moment and decided to do something nice. He gave Sue the ice cream cone he found. Sue was very happy and stopped crying. But as they talked, the sun was very hot and the ice cream started to melt. Tim and Sue saw the melting ice cream and laughed. They decided to share the melted ice cream and became good friends. The moral of the story is that being nice to others can bring you happiness and friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a big chest in the middle of the room. It was made of wood and was very open. The little girl wanted to know what was inside it. She approached the chest and knocked on it. "Hello, is anyone in there?" When no one replied, the little girl decided to take a look inside. As she opened the chest, a beautiful doll appeared. The little girl was so excited! She sat down and looked at the doll. After a few minutes, she told a story to the doll. "Once upon a time, there was a little boy who always liked to lie. He lied to everyone, even his mother and father". The little girl suddenly heard a voice. "No, I never lie!" It was the doll! The little girl was amazed. "You can talk!" she exclaimed. "Yes, I can," the doll replied. "From now on, I won't let anyone lie. I will help you tell the truth." The little girl was full of joy. She hugged the doll and thanked her. From that day on, she and the doll were best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. But his mom said, "No, it's too high." Tim was sad, but he saw something else. He pointed at the soil and said, "Look, mom! A big, heavy rock!" His mom looked at the heavy rock and said, "Let's move it together." They pushed and pushed, but the rock did not move. Then, something unexpected happened. The heavy rock started to wiggle! Tim and his mom stepped back as the heavy rock wiggled more and more. Then, the rock cracked open! Inside the rock, there was a small, friendly looking creature. It was a baby turtle! Tim and his mom were so surprised. They decided to take the baby turtle home and give it a nice place to live. Tim was happy because he made a new friend. The heavy rock was not a rock after all, but a turtle egg!

Once upon a time, in a big town, there was a mechanic named Tom. He was a very modern mechanic. He loved to fix cars and trucks. He had a big garage with many tools. One day, Tom was working on a car when he heard a loud noise. It made him jump. "What was that?" he thought. He looked around and saw a big cat. The cat had knocked over a box. Tom laughed. "Silly cat, you scare me!" he said. The cat became Tom's friend. They played together in the garage. The cat liked to watch Tom fix cars. Tom liked having a friend. They were happy and the garage was a fun place to be.

Once upon a time, there was a little shrimp. The shrimp lived in a big sea. The shrimp was very proud of how fast it could swim. One day, the shrimp met a big fish. The big fish wanted to eat the shrimp. The shrimp was scared but had an idea. The shrimp said, "I will feed you if you don't eat me." The big fish agreed. The shrimp found some tasty food for the big fish. The big fish was happy and did not eat the shrimp. But something unexpected happened. The tasty food made the big fish grow smaller! Now, the big fish was smaller than the shrimp. The shrimp was very surprised but happy. The shrimp and the small fish became best friends and swam together in the big sea.
