Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mop and the People

One day, a mop lived in a big house. The mop was very good at cleaning. It would supply clean floors for the people in the house. The mop liked to make the floors shine. One hot day, the mop was working hard. It was cleaning the kitchen floor. Suddenly, the mop started to dance! The mop was dancing and cleaning at the same time. The people in the house were very surprised. The people laughed and clapped. They had never seen a dancing mop before. The mop was happy to make the people smile. From that day on, the mop would dance and supply clean floors for the people in the big house.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous tree. In the tree, there was a big knot. A little boy, Tom, saw the knot and wanted to remove it. Tom asked his friend, Sue, "How can we remove the knot?" Sue said, "We can use a big stick to hit it." So, they found a big stick and started to hit the knot. After hitting the knot many times, the enormous tree started to shake. They didn't know that the tree was not strong. Suddenly, the tree fell down on their playhouse. The playhouse broke and they were very sad. They learned not to remove knots from big trees.

One day, a little puppy went for a walk. The puppy seemed very happy. He liked to jump and play in the sun. The puppy saw a big red ball and wanted to play with it. The puppy ran to the ball and started to play. But then, a big girl came and took the ball away. The puppy felt sad and sorry. He did not know what to do. But then, something unexpected happened. The big girl came back with a new ball just for the puppy! The big girl was not mean, she just wanted to play with the puppy too. Now, the puppy and the big girl played together and had a lot of fun. The puppy was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red umbrella. It liked to play outside with the kids. One day, it rained a lot. The umbrella felt very happy because it could help the kids stay dry. The kids took the umbrella outside to play in the rain. They jumped in puddles and laughed. But one little boy did a stupid thing. He tried to bathe in a big puddle with the umbrella. The umbrella was not happy, but it still kept the boy dry. After playing in the rain, the kids went back inside. They put the umbrella away to dry. The umbrella was happy it could help the kids have fun in the rain. The kids learned not to bathe in puddles and to always use the umbrella when it rained.

One day, a colorful bird named Bob went to find a herb. He wanted to make his nest smell nice. He flew around and saw a big tree with many green leaves. Bob saw a little girl named Sue near the tree. Sue was rocking on a blue swing. Bob flew down and said, "Hi, Sue! Can you help me find a herb for my nest?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Bob! I know where to find herbs." Sue and Bob walked to a garden with many flowers. They found the herb that smelled nice. Bob said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue said, "You're welcome, Bob! Have fun with your herb!" Bob took the herb to his nest. His home now smelled very nice. He was happy he met Sue and they became good friends.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to act. They had a big box of clothes and toys that they used to pretend to be different people and animals. One day, they found a wool hat and scarf in the box. They decided to act like sheep. "Baa, baa," said Tom, putting on the hat and scarf. "I am a sheep. I eat grass and have wool." "Baa, baa," said Lily, putting on another hat and scarf. "I am a sheep too. I follow you and have wool." They ran around the room, making sheep noises and bumping into things. They had a lot of fun. They did not see their cat, Max, watching them from the sofa. Max was a big, black cat who liked to hunt. He saw the twins acting like sheep and thought they were his prey. He licked his lips and got ready to pounce. He waited for the right moment, when they were not looking. Suddenly, he jumped on Tom and bit his neck. Tom screamed and fell to the floor. Lily saw what happened and ran to help him. She tried to pull Max off, but he was too strong and scratched her face. She cried and called for their mom. Mom heard the noise and came to the room. She saw the cat attacking the twins and shouted at him. She grabbed him and threw him outside. She ran to the twins and hugged them. She saw that they were bleeding and hurt. She called for dad and the doctor. The doctor came and took the twins to the hospital. He said they needed stitches and medicine. He said they were lucky to be alive. He said they should not act like sheep again. He said the cat was dangerous and should not come back. The twins were very sad and scared. They did not want to act anymore. They did not want to see the cat anymore. They did not want to wear wool anymore. They wished they had never found the hat and scarf. They wished they had played something else. They wished they had been more careful. They wished they had been more sleepy.
