Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Park of Tim and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a brilliant toy. It was made of rubber. He loved his toy very much. Tim would play with his toy all day long. One day, Tim took his toy to the park. He played with it under the big tree. He was very happy. But then, a big wind came. It blew Tim's toy away. Tim could not find his toy. He started to cry. Tim looked for his toy all day. He asked his friends if they saw it. But no one could help him. The sun went down and it was time to go home. Tim walked home, still crying. He never found his brilliant rubber toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little red house. The house had a big door with a lock. The lock was there to keep the house safe when the family went away. The family loved their little red house very much. One day, a funny man with a big nose came to the house. He saw that the door was locked. He wanted to go inside, but he could not. The funny man with the big nose looked around to find another way in. He saw a window, but it was not available for him to climb through. The family came home and saw the funny man with the big nose. They asked him what he was doing. The funny man said he just wanted to see the little red house. The family opened the door and let him in. They all had a fun time together in the little red house.

---

One day, a boy named Tim found a big book called a dictionary. He felt helpless because he could not read the big words in it. He wanted to learn from the dictionary, but he did not know how. Tim's friend, Sue, arrived at his house to play. She saw the dictionary and said, "I can help you read it!" Tim was happy and they sat down to read together. Sue showed Tim how to find words he did not know and they learned new words together. One day, Tim and Sue had a race to find a word in the dictionary. Sue found it first and Tim was sad. Sue said, "It's okay, we can try again!" They kept playing and learning, and soon Tim was not helpless with the dictionary anymore. They became better friends and had fun learning together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a clever fox. The fox was very good at finding food. One day, the fox saw a big tree with a twist in it. In the twist, there was a shiny thing. The fox wanted the shiny thing very much. The shiny thing was the spirit of the forest. The spirit was very kind and liked to help the animals. The fox tried to get the shiny thing, but it was too high up. The fox jumped and jumped, but could not reach it. A little bird saw the fox and asked, "Why are you jumping?" The fox said, "I want the shiny thing in the tree." The bird was very small, but it had a big heart. It wanted to help the fox. The bird flew up and got the shiny thing for the fox. The fox and the bird became good friends. The spirit was happy to see them help each other and live happily in the forest.

One day, a man wanted to drive to the store. He wanted to buy a pie. The store was wide and had many things. The man was happy to go there. When the man got to the store, he saw a big, wide pie. It was the biggest pie he had ever seen! He was so excited to take it home. But when he picked it up, something unexpected happened. The pie started to wiggle! The pie wiggled and wiggled, and then it jumped! The man was very surprised. The pie was not a pie at all. It was a big, wide hat! The man laughed and laughed. He took the hat home and wore it all day.
