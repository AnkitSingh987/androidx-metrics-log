Category: Intermediate
Title: Finn the Fish and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Finn. Finn loved to swim in the cool pond near his home. Every day, he would swim with his friends and they would play games together. One day, while Finn was swimming, he saw a new fish in the pond. The new fish was shy and didn't join the others. Finn swam over to the new fish and said, "Hi, I'm Finn. Do you want to swim with us?" The new fish smiled and said, "Yes, thank you. My name is Lily." Finn and Lily became good friends. They swam together in the cool pond every day. Sometimes, Finn's mom would allow them to have a picnic by the pond. They would eat yummy snacks and laugh together. Finn, Lily, and their friends had lots of fun swimming and playing in the cool pond.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in an ordinary house with his mom, dad, and dog, Spot. One day, Tim found a faucet in his backyard. It was not like the other faucets in his house. Tim was very curious about this faucet. Tim decided to turn on the faucet. Water came out and started to fill a big hole in the ground. Tim played with the water and had lots of fun. But soon, the hole was full, and the water began to reveal a secret door in the ground. Tim was excited to see what was behind the door. Tim opened the door and went inside. It was very dark and scary. Tim wanted to go back home, but the door closed behind him. He was lost and afraid. Tim called for his mom, dad, and Spot, but they could not hear him. Tim was stuck in the secret room, and he never found his way back home.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went on an unusual tour with her mom. They went to a farm where they saw many animals. Lucy was very happy to see the animals. At the farm, Lucy saw a little duck who could not swim. The duck was sad. Lucy wanted to help the duck. She asked her mom, "Mom, can we help the duck?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's try to help the duck." Lucy and her mom managed to find a big, round thing that the duck could sit on. They put the duck on the thing and pushed it into the water. The duck was now able to swim with the other ducks. The duck was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lucy!" In the end, the unusual tour became a happy day for everyone.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a rhinoceros. He was very tidy. He liked to keep his home clean. All the other animals liked how clean he was. One day, a big storm came. The wind blew leaves and dirt into the rhinoceros' home. The rhinoceros began to worry. He did not like his home to be messy. He tried to clean up, but the wind blew more and more leaves and dirt in. The rhinoceros became very sad. He could not make his home tidy again. The other animals saw the rhinoceros worry and tried to help. But the storm was too strong. The rhinoceros' home stayed messy, and he stayed sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little cloud. It lived high up in the sky. The shy cloud liked to hide behind the big clouds. It did not want anyone to see it. One sunny day, the shy cloud wanted to play with the other clouds. It tried to slip closer to them. But the wind blew the shy cloud far away. The shy cloud felt sad. Then, a kind bird saw the shy cloud. The bird wanted to help the shy cloud. It asked the other clouds to play with the shy cloud. The shy cloud was so happy. It was not shy anymore. They all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tom. Tom had a bad dream one night. He woke up and felt scared. The nightmare had a big monster in it. Tom did not like the monster. Tom went to his mom and dad's room. He asked, "Can I sleep with you? I had a bad dream." Mom and dad said, "Yes, come in." Tom felt safe and happy. But when Tom got in bed, he saw the monster from his bad dream! He was scared again. But then, the monster said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I'm not a bad monster. I just wanted to be friends." Tom was surprised. He said, "Okay, let's be friends." They all slept well and had good dreams.
