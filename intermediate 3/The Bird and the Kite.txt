Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird and the Kite

Once upon a time there was a bird who lived on a tall tree near the sun rise. Every morning, the bird would top of the tree and bow to thank the sunrise for its beauty. One morning, something strange caught the bird's eye. In the distance, the bird could see something thin, shimmering in the morning light. The bird had never seen anything like it before and quickly flew down to take a closer look. The bird approached the thin thing and tapped it lightly with its beak. Suddenly, the thin thing opened up and the bird jumped back in surprise. "Hello!" said the thin thing. "Don't be afraid. I'm just a kite looking for a friend." The bird smiled and bowed to the kite. "I'm your friend!" it said. "Let's fly together!" And so, the bird and the kite flew side by side, and they both watched the beautiful sunrise every morning.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy car with a buckle. The buckle was on the car's seat. Tim loved to play with his toy car every day. He would buckle the toy car's seat and pretend the car was driving fast. One day, Tim's toy car was feeling poor. It was sad and did not want to play. Tim tried to buckle the seat, but the car refused. The car did not want to drive fast anymore. Tim was sad too. He wanted his toy car to be happy and play with him. Tim decided to make the car feel better. He gave the car a bath and cleaned it up. He even gave it a new paint job. The toy car started to feel better. It was not poor anymore. The car let Tim buckle the seat, and they played together again. They drove fast and had lots of fun. Tim and his toy car were happy once more.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the woods. She saw a tiny light far away. She felt curious and wanted to see what it was. Lily walked closer and closer to the light. As she got near, she heard a soft whisper. "I'm hungry," said the whisper. Lily looked around and saw a tiny, hungry bug. The bug was making the light! Lily was surprised because she didn't know bugs could talk and make light. Lily wanted to help the hungry bug. She found a leaf with some food on it and gave it to the bug. The bug ate the food and felt happy. Then, the bug's light grew bigger and brighter. Suddenly, the bug turned into a beautiful fairy! The fairy thanked Lily for helping her and gave her a wish. Lily wished for a new friend, and the fairy became her best friend forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in an ordinary house with her mom and dad. Every morning, Amy's mom made her toast for breakfast. Amy loved her toast very much. One day, Amy woke up and went to the kitchen. Her mom was not there. Amy began to wonder where her mom was. She looked around the house and found her mom in the garden. Amy asked her mom for toast. Her mom said, "I am busy. Can you make toast by yourself today?" Amy was scared, but she wanted toast. So, she went to the kitchen and tried to make toast. At first, it was hard, but then she did it! Amy was very happy and proud. She ran to her mom and showed her the toast. Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Amy! You can do things by yourself if you try." From that day on, Amy learned that she could do many things if she tried. She did not need to always ask for help. Sometimes, she could do it all by herself. And that made Amy feel very special.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play at the park. Her favorite thing to play on was the swing. She would swing high up in the sky and feel like she could touch the clouds. One sunny day, Amy went to the park with her mom. She saw a big, flexible branch on the ground. She thought it would be fun to play with. Amy picked it up and tried to split it in half, but it was too strong. Amy had an idea. She asked her mom to help her tie the branch to the swing. Now, the swing was even more fun! It was like a swing and a game all in one. Amy and her friends played on the new swing all day long, and they were very happy.
