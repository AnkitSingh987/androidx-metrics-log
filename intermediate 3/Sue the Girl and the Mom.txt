Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Mom

One day, a little girl named Sue was very excited. She saw her mom putting on makeup. Sue wanted to try it too. She thought it would be fun. The next day, when mom was not looking, Sue went to her mom's room. She found the makeup and started to put it on her face. She put on red lipstick, blue eyeshadow, and pink blush. Sue was having so much fun. Later, mom came into the room and saw Sue with makeup all over her face. Sue felt ashamed. She knew she should not have touched mom's makeup. Mom smiled and said, "It's okay, Sue. Next time, just ask me, and we can do makeup together." Sue was happy and learned to ask before using things that were not hers.

One day, a small ant named Tim wanted to become big and strong. He saw a line of ants on a march to find food. Tim thought, "If I join the march, I will become big and strong too!" As Tim joined the march, he met another small ant named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, I want to become big and strong too!" Tim was happy to have a friend who wanted the same thing. They marched together, talking and laughing. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Tim and Sue away from the march. They landed in a place with lots of food! They ate and ate, and soon they became big and strong. Tim and Sue were happy that the big wind brought them to this new place. They knew they could always find their way back to the march, but for now, they enjoyed their time together, eating and growing stronger.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Dave and Jake. Jake wanted to lead, so he asked Dave if they could go get some food. Dave said yes and they skipped happily down to the kitchen. There was lots of food there, but it was all very strange! There was green jelly, purple mashed potatoes and pink spaghetti! Dave and Jake stopped and looked at each other. They laughed and thought it was funny. "What should we do?" Dave asked Jake. Jake thought for a moment. "Let's try some of it!" he said. So they both tried a bit of the green jelly and a bit of the purple mashed potatoes. It was delicious! With a full belly, they ran off back to the garden to play. And that is the tale of how Dave and Jake went on an adventure to find some funny food!

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy a toy for Tim's friend's birthday. Tim saw a plastic toy car. It was red and shiny. He said, "Mom, can we get this car? It is very pretty." His mom said, "Yes, let's buy this one." On the way home, Tim's mom told him that the plastic car was not the original toy she wanted to buy. She said, "I wanted to buy a big teddy bear, but the car is nice too." Tim thought about the teddy bear and said, "Maybe next time we can buy the teddy bear." At the birthday party, Tim gave his friend the plastic car. His friend was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Tim! This is the best gift ever!" Tim was happy that his friend liked the car. He knew that the original toy his mom wanted to buy would have been good too, but the car was perfect for his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a blue car. The blue car loved to race. One day, the blue car wanted to win a big race. So, the blue car went very fast. All the other cars wanted to win too. During the race, something unexpected happened. The blue car saw a big massage chair on the side of the road. The blue car wanted to try the massage chair. It stopped racing and sat in the chair. The blue car felt very good and relaxed. While the blue car was enjoying the massage, the other cars raced by. But then, the massage chair started to move! It was a magic massage chair! The chair raced faster than all the other cars. The blue car and the massage chair crossed the finish line first. The blue car won the race with the help of the magic massage chair.
