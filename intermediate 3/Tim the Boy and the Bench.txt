Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bench

One day, a little boy named Tim found a small piece of metal on the ground. He picked it up and decided to take it home. As he walked, he stepped on a big rock and fell down. Tim felt angry because his knee hurt. Tim saw a girl named Sue sitting on a bench. She saw Tim fall and asked, "Are you okay?" Tim showed her his knee and said, "I fell and now I'm angry." Sue looked at the metal in Tim's hand and said, "What is that?" Tim told Sue, "I found this metal and I want to take it home." Sue smiled and said, "Let's go together. We can make a fun game with the metal." Tim and Sue stepped carefully as they walked home, and they were not angry anymore. They played a fun game with the metal and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box with many toys. He liked to play with his toys every day. One day, Tim received a new toy. It was a big, red ball with a hook on it. Tim was very happy to get this new toy. In the park, Tim saw an elderly lady. She had a little dog. The little dog liked to play with the ball too. But the dog did not want to give the ball back to Tim. The dog ran away with the ball. Tim was sad because he wanted to play with his new toy. Tim had an idea. He asked the elderly lady to help him. The lady called the dog to come back. The dog listened to the lady and came back with the ball. Tim said "thank you" to the lady and the dog. Now, Tim could play with his new toy again.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Tim went for a walk. He saw a wide tree and wanted to play near it. Tim ran around the tree and barked happily. While playing, Tim found a small book on the ground. It was a passport! It had pretty pictures inside. Tim picked it up with his mouth and started to look for the owner. The wind began to blow, and Tim started to shiver. He looked around and saw a girl crying. Tim ran to her with the passport in his mouth. The girl was so happy to see her passport, and she hugged Tim. They became good friends and played together near the wide tree, keeping each other warm and not shivering anymore.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to find a book to read. She went to her bookshelf where she kept all of her favorite books. Lily loved her bookshelf because it was always reliable and had many fun books for her to read. Lily saw a book about a bunny who liked to skip. The bunny would skip in the grass, skip by the pond, and skip with his friends. Lily thought the bunny was very happy and fun. She liked the way the bunny would skip and wanted to try it too. Lily took the book to her mom and asked her to read it. As her mom read the story, Lily tried to skip just like the bunny. She skipped in the living room, she skipped in the kitchen, and she even skipped outside. Lily had so much fun skipping like the bunny in the book. She was very happy that her reliable bookshelf had given her another great story to enjoy.

Lily liked to design things. She had many crayons and papers. She made pictures of flowers, stars, and animals. She gave them to her mom and dad. They smiled and said they were pretty. One day, Lily wanted to design something new. She saw a big white wall in the living room. She thought it was boring. She wanted to make it colorful. She took her crayons and climbed on a chair. She started to draw on the wall. She made a long rainbow, a big sun, and a happy face. She was very proud of her design. She called her mom and dad to see it. But they did not smile. They looked angry and sad. They said she was naughty and she had to stop. They said she had to clean the wall. They took away her crayons and papers. They sent her to her room. Lily felt very sorry and sad. She did not understand why they were mad. She thought they would like her design. She wanted to make them smile. She cried and cried in her room. She wished she had not drawn on the wall. She wished she had her crayons and papers back. She wished she could say sorry and hug them. But they did not come. She was alone.
