Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Furry Bird and the River

One day, a furry bird named Bob wanted to relax. He flew to a big tree and sat on a branch. He looked at his wing and saw it was dirty. Bob's friend, a small bug named Tim, saw him. Tim said, "Hi, Bob! Why are you sad?" Bob said, "My wing is dirty. I want to be clean." Tim had an idea. He told Bob, "Let's go to the river. The water will make your wing clean." So, Bob and Tim went to the river. Bob put his wing in the water and it became clean. Now, Bob was happy and could relax with his friend Tim.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big, soft blanket that she loved very much. The blanket was her best friend. It was a mysterious blanket because it could change colors when she was happy or sad. One day, Lucy heard a loud scream coming from outside. She was very scared, but she took her blanket and went to see what was wrong. She saw a small cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared too, and it was screaming for help. Lucy wanted to help the cat. She tried to think of how her blanket could help. Then, she had an idea! She held her blanket up high, and the cat jumped onto it. The cat was safe now, and it was very happy. Lucy felt proud that she could help the cat with her mysterious blanket. The cat and Lucy became good friends. They played together every day, and the cat loved the blanket too. The moral of the story is that when we help others, we make new friends and feel good about ourselves.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a red dress that she loved very much. One day, her mom said they were going to a party. Lucy was very happy. Lucy's mom showed her a pretty veil to wear with her red dress. The veil was like a soft, thin cloth. Lucy put it on her head and looked in the mirror. She felt like a princess. At the party, Lucy met a boy named Tim. He had a red hat. Tim said, "Let's play hide and seek." Lucy thought it was a good idea. She hid under a big table with her veil covering her face. Tim found her and they both laughed. They played all day and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red truck. The truck had a sack in it. The truck was very creative and could do many things. It could go fast, it could go slow, it could even talk to people. The truck loved to help people and make them happy. One day, the truck was driving down the road with its sack. It saw a little boy who was sad. The truck stopped and asked the boy, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my toy. I cannot find it anywhere." The truck wanted to help the boy and make him happy. The truck thought very hard and had a creative idea. It opened the sack and said, "Look inside!" The boy looked inside the sack and found his toy! He was so happy and surprised. The truck had picked up the toy by accident, but it was a good accident. The boy thanked the truck, and they became best friends. The truck and the boy went on many fun adventures together.

One day, a girl named Lily wore her favorite skirt. It was a pretty skirt with many colors. She was very excite to wear it to play outside. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Lily knew it was going to be a fun day. Lily went to the park with her mom. They played on the swings, the slide, and the sandbox. Lily's skirt was flying in the wind as she played. She was so happy and excite to be outside. Her mom watched her and smiled. After playing, Lily and her mom sat down for a snack. They had dry cookies and juice. Lily's skirt was a little dirty from playing, but she didn't care. She had so much fun and was very excite to go back to the park again soon.
