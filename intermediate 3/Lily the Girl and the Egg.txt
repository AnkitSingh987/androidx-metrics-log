Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Egg

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to collect pretty things in her bag. One day, she found a fragile egg on the ground. The egg was very delicate, so she had to be careful. Lily put the fragile egg in her bag and took it home. She showed it to her mom and dad. They told her that the egg belonged to a bird and that she should put it back where she found it. Lily went back to the place she found the egg and saw a sad bird looking for her egg. Lily gave the egg back to the bird, and the bird was very happy. Lily learned that it is important to be kind and not take things that do not belong to her.

One day, a little cat named Luna went for a walk. She liked to wander around and see new things. Luna was a very curious cat. She walked and walked until she saw a big store. It was a grocery store. Luna thought it would be fun to go inside. Luna went into the grocery store and saw lots of food. She saw apples, bananas, and yummy treats. Luna was so excited, she started to run and play. She knocked over a big pile of cans. They fell down and made a loud noise. A nice man who worked at the store came over. He was not happy. Luna felt sorry for making a mess. She wanted to help the nice man. So, Luna picked up the cans with her little paws. The nice man saw Luna helping and smiled. He was not mad anymore. Luna was happy to help and make the nice man smile. Then, Luna went back home, feeling good about her day at the grocery store.

One day, Tom and Sue were playing outside. They found some heavy clay. Tom said, "Let's make something with this clay!" Sue thought for a moment and said, "I recommend we make a big, funny face!" They both laughed and started to make the face. They made two round balls for the eyes and a long, silly smile. They also made a big, round nose. When they were done, they showed their funny face to Mom. Mom smiled and said, "Good job! I love your funny face!" Tom and Sue were very happy and played with their heavy clay face all day.

Tom is a restless boy. He likes to see new things and play with them. He does not like to sit still and listen to Mom or Dad. One day, Tom sees a shiny thing on the table. It is a watch. It has a round face and a long band. It makes a tick-tock sound. Tom wants to see how it works. He takes the watch and runs to his room. Mom sees that the watch is gone. She is angry. The watch is hers. It is a gift from Dad. She loves the watch. She looks for Tom and the watch. She finds Tom in his room. He has taken the watch apart. There are many small things on the floor. They are the parts of the watch. Tom is trying to put them back together. He does not know how. Mom is very angry. She yells at Tom. She tells him that he is naughty and that he broke the watch. She takes the watch and the parts. She tells Tom to stay in his room. Tom is sad. He did not mean to break the watch. He just wanted to see how it works. He cries in his room. He wishes he had not been so restless.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate butterfly named Betty. Betty loved to add colors to her wings. She had a big race with her friend, Timmy the turtle. Betty wanted to win and feel the victory. She thought of a plan to make her faster. Before the race, Betty found pretty flowers. She added their colors to her wings. The colors made her feel strong and fast. Timmy saw Betty's wings and knew she was ready for the race. He felt a little scared but wanted to try his best. The race started, and Betty flew fast with her colorful wings. Timmy walked slow but did not give up. In the end, Betty won the race and felt the victory. She was happy but also proud of Timmy for trying his best. They were both winners in their hearts.
