Category: Intermediate
Title: Momo the Nosy Monkey and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to climb trees and play with his friends in the forest. One day, Momo saw a big pipe near the river where he and his friends liked to swim. Momo called his friend, Lulu the bird, "Lulu, come see this big pipe!" Lulu flew down and looked at the pipe. "What do you think it is, Momo?" she asked. Momo didn't know, but he wanted to find out. He decided to climb on the pipe and see what was on the other side. As Momo climbed the pipe, he saw a big bear named Bubu. Bubu was eating honey from a beehive. Momo was curious and wanted to try some honey too. But Bubu said, "Momo, you should not take things that are not yours. It is not nice." Momo felt sad and knew that Bubu was right. He said, "I am sorry, Bubu. I will not take the honey." Momo learned that it is important to ask before taking things that belong to others. He went back to his friends and told them the story, so they could learn the lesson too. From that day on, Momo and his friends always asked before taking anything that was not theirs. They played happily in the forest, and everyone was kind to each other. The moral of the story is to always ask before taking something that belongs to someone else.

One day, a little girl named Sally found a new box in her room. Inside the box, there were many things to decorate. Sally was very happy. She wanted to make her room pretty. Sally's friend, Tim, came over to help. They put up pretty pictures and shiny stars. Then, Sally saw a small flame on a candle. She thought it was pretty too. She said, "Tim, let's put the flame in the room to make it more pretty!" Tim was not sure, but he wanted to help Sally. They put the candle with the flame in the room. At first, it looked nice. But then, the flame got bigger and bigger. It caught the pretty pictures on fire. Sally and Tim were scared. They ran out of the room and called for help. Sally's mom came and put out the fire. But the room was not pretty anymore. It was all black and burnt. Sally and Tim were very sad. They learned that playing with a flame was not a good idea.

Ben and Mia love to play soccer in the park. They have a big ball and a small goal. They take turns to kick the ball and try to score. Sometimes they miss and the ball goes far away. Then they run after it and bring it back. One day, they are playing soccer and having fun. Ben kicks the ball very hard and it flies over the goal. Mia says, "Wow, that was a good kick!" They run to get the ball, but they cannot find it. They look behind the trees, under the bench, in the bushes, but the ball is not there. They feel sad and confused. Where did the ball go? They see a man walking his dog near the park. They ask him, "Have you seen our ball? It is big and red and has white stars on it. We kicked it over the goal and it vanished." The man shakes his head and says, "No, I have not seen your ball. Maybe someone took it or it rolled away. I'm sorry, kids." Ben and Mia feel angry and upset. They think someone was mean and stole their ball. They want their ball back. They love their ball. They start to cry and hug each other. They feel ashamed that they lost their ball. Then they hear a voice behind them. It is their friend Sam. He is holding their ball and smiling. He says, "Hey, Ben and Mia, I found your ball. It was on the other side of the park, near the fence. It bounced off the pitch and landed there. I saw it and brought it to you. Here you go." Ben and Mia are happy and surprised. They thank Sam and take their ball. They are glad that their ball did not vanish. They are glad that Sam is their friend. They wipe their tears and smile. They say, "Let's play soccer again. And this time, let's be careful not to kick the ball too hard." They laugh and run to the goal. They play soccer and have fun.
