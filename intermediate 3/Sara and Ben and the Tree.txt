Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Tree

Sara and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. They liked to swing, slide and run. But their favorite thing was to climb the big tree in the corner. The tree was very old and very tall. It had many branches and leaves. Sara and Ben thought the tree was impressive. One day, they decided to climb to the very top of the tree. They held hands and helped each other. They were careful not to slip or fall. They felt brave and happy. When they reached the top, they could see the whole park and the sky. They smiled and hugged. "Wow, this is amazing!" Sara said. "I know, right? We are so high!" Ben said. They looked at each other and felt a warm feeling in their chests. They did not know what it was, but they liked it. They leaned closer and kissed. It was their first kiss. They felt a tingling in their lips and cheeks. They giggled and blushed. The tree saw everything and smiled. It was happy for Sara and Ben. It had seen many children play and grow under its shade. It had seen many kisses and hugs. It knew that Sara and Ben were special. It wished them well and gave them a gentle breeze. Sara and Ben thanked the tree and climbed down. They held hands and ran to their parents. They did not tell anyone about their kiss. It was their secret. They knew they would always remember it and the tree.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a charming fox. The fox liked to do many things. He liked to run, jump, and play with his friends. One day, the fox met a new friend, a little bird. The fox said, "Hello, bird! Do you want to play with me?" The bird said, "Yes! I want to play too!" So, the fox and the bird played all day long. They ran, jumped, and had lots of fun. But the fox was not a good friend. He wanted to eat the bird. The bird did not know this. The fox said, "Bird, come closer. I have a secret." The bird came closer, and the fox ate the bird. The other animals in the forest saw what the fox did. They were sad and did not want to play with the fox anymore. The charming fox was all alone. The moral of the story is: always be a good friend and do not hurt others.

One day, a lively cat named Tom wanted to bake a cake. He found a big bowl and put in flour, sugar, and eggs. He mixed it all up and put the cake mix in a pan. Tom was very happy and excited. Tom put the cake in the oven and waited. He wanted to make the cake cold, so he opened the fridge. Inside the fridge, he found a big surprise! There was already a cake in there! It was a yummy cake with pink frosting and sprinkles. Tom didn't know what to do. He took the cake from the fridge and put it on the table. Then, the oven beeped. Tom's cake was done! He took it out and saw that his cake was blue. Now, Tom had two cakes! He called his friends to come and eat the cakes with him. They all had a fun and tasty day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She liked to play in the park. One day, she saw a shiny crystal on the ground. Lucy was glad to find it. She picked it up and put it in her pocket. The next day, Lucy went back to the park. She took out the crystal and showed it to her friend, Tom. They both kneeled to look at it closely. Suddenly, the crystal started to glow. Lucy and Tom were surprised. Then, a tiny fairy appeared from the glowing crystal. The fairy told Lucy and Tom that she was lost and needed help to find her way home. Lucy and Tom were happy to help the fairy. They followed the fairy's directions and found her home. The fairy was so thankful, and as a reward, she turned the crystal into a magic toy that could change into anything they wanted. Lucy and Tom were very glad and played with the magic toy every day.
