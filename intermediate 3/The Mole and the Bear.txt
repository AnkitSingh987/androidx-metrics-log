Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mole and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little mole. The mole lived in a warm hole in the ground. The mole loved to dig and play all day. One day, the mole met a big bear. "Hello, mole," said the bear. "Do you want to play with me?" The mole was happy and said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" They played together all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The bear finished digging a hole and found a big treasure chest. They opened it and saw lots of shiny stones. The mole and the bear were very happy and shared the treasure with their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Max. Max liked to tease his friend, a big dog named Buddy. Max would jump on Buddy and run away very fast. Buddy did not like when Max teased him. It made Buddy's heart sad. One day, Max was careless and got stuck in a tree. Max was scared and cried for help. Buddy heard Max and came to see what was wrong. Max looked down at Buddy and said, "Please help me, I am stuck!" Buddy felt bad for Max, even though Max teased him. Buddy stood on his back legs and reached up to help Max. Max jumped down and landed on Buddy's back. Max said, "Thank you, Buddy!" From that day on, Max did not tease Buddy anymore. They became best friends and their hearts were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a weak tree in a backyard. The tree had a big problem. It started to rot. The tree was very sad and did not know what to do. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the tree and said, "Why are you so sad, tree?" The tree told Tim that it was weak and starting to rot. Tim thought for a moment and said, "I will help you, tree!" Tim went to his dad and told him about the weak and rotting tree. His dad said, "We need to take care of our backyard and help the tree get better." So, Tim and his dad worked together to fix the tree. They cut off the rotting part and gave the tree water and love. After some time, the tree became strong and healthy again. The tree was very happy and thanked Tim and his dad. The moral of the story is: when we take care of our backyard and help others, we can make things better and everyone can be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl with an uncle. He was always working very hard. One day, he was sitting at his desk, sorting through a pile of things. The little girl asked her uncle, "What are you doing?" He answered, "I'm sorting my things. I'm putting them all in order. It's a very hard job." The little girl asked to help, so her uncle said, "Yes, of course. Here, you can sort this small pile into three piles. See if you can do it!" So, the little girl began to sort the things. She carefully made three piles of things. Her uncle was very proud. "Well done!", he said. The little girl smiled, happy that she could help her uncle.

Once upon a time, in a hot land, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very thirsty. He looked for water, but he could not find any. One day, Tim saw a green place far away. He thought it was an oasis. He walked and walked, but he could not reach it. Tim was sad and tired. A nice bird saw Tim and recognized he needed help. The bird flew to the oasis and brought back water for Tim. They became good friends. From that day on, they helped each other and were never thirsty again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a brilliant red car. This car loved to speed up and down the street. It would go very fast, making all the kids in the town laugh and cheer. One sunny day, the red car was speeding down the street when it saw a little blue car. The blue car was sad because it could not go as fast as the red car. The red car wanted to help the blue car feel happy. So, the red car slowed down and let the blue car speed up. Together, they raced down the street, going fast and having fun. The kids in the town clapped and cheered for both cars. The blue car was now happy, and the red car felt good for helping its new friend. They became best friends and raced together every day, making the town a happier place.
