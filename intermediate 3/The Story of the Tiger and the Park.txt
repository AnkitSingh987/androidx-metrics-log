Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Tiger and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was colorful and lived in the city. She liked playing in the park and running around. One day, when she was playing in the park, she saw a tiger. She was surprised and a little scared. The tiger looked at her and said, "Come here, little girl. I am friendly." The little girl was still worried, but she stepped closer. The tiger said, "Do you want to become a tiger with me?" The little girl was amazed and said, "Yes! I want to become a tiger." The little girl and the tiger danced and had fun in the park. Then, the little girl started to change. She had tiger stripes and a tail. She had become a colorful tiger! The little girl and the tiger ran around the park together and had lots of fun. They were the best friends.

Sammy was excited. Today was special. His two parents supplied a cool surprise. He and his friends hopped on the bus and headed to the park. As they approached the park, Sammy saw a big tree. The tree was really tall and looked really cool. He couldn't wait to find out what his parents had supplied. Sammy and his friends ran towards the tree. When they got closer, Sammy saw something shiny near it. He gasped. His parents supplied a kite! Sammy clapped with joy. He and his friends flew the kite together. The cool winds made it go high in the sky. Sammy was so happy he had such cool parents!

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a big blueberry bush. Mia thought the blueberries looked amazing. She wanted to eat them, but she was not tall enough to reach the blueberries. Mia saw a boy named Tom at the park. Tom was tall. Mia asked Tom to help her get the blueberries. Tom said, "I will help you, but I want some blueberries too." Mia agreed. Tom snapped the blueberries off the bush for Mia. They both ate the blueberries and they were very happy. But then, there were no more blueberries left on the bush. Mia and Tom were sad, but they learned to share and became friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a box of powder. She did not know what it was, but she wanted to play with it. She took the powder and threw it in the air. The powder went everywhere! Lily was happy to see the powder fly, but she knew she made a mess. Lily started to feel regret. She knew she should not have played with the powder. She was frightened that her mom would be mad at her. She tried to clean up the mess, but the powder was everywhere. Lily's mom came into the room and saw the mess. She was not mad. She told Lily that it was okay to make mistakes. They cleaned up the powder together. Lily learned not to play with things she did not know.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent dog named Spot. Spot loved to help people. One day, while Spot was walking in the park, he saw a small boy named Timmy. Timmy was sad because he lost his toy. Spot asked, "Why are you sad, Timmy?" Timmy replied, "I lost my toy and I don't know where it is." Spot wanted to help Timmy find his toy. They looked everywhere in the park. They looked under the trees, near the swings, and by the pond. But they could not find the toy. Then, Spot had a great idea. He knew how to print a new toy for Timmy. Spot went to the big printer and printed a new toy for Timmy. The toy was hot when it came out of the printer, so they waited for it to cool down. Timmy was so happy and thanked Spot for his help. They played together in the park with the new toy and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. He would kick it all day long. One day, Tim saw his mom drinking something brown. He asked her, "What is that?" His mom said, "This is coffee, but it's not for kids." Tim wanted to try it, but his mom said no. Tim was sad, so his mom had an idea. She made him a fake coffee with warm milk and a little bit of chocolate. Tim was so happy with his fake coffee. He held it carefully and went outside to play. He kicked his ball high and far. While he was playing, his friend Sue came over. Tim shared his fake coffee with Sue, and they both laughed and played together.
