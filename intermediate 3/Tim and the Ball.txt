Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big red ball stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to play with the ball, so he tried to pull it down. A wise old man was giving a lecture to some kids nearby. He saw Tim trying to pull the ball and said, "Be careful, Tim! You must think and try a better way." Tim thought for a moment and found a long stick. He used the stick to gently push the ball out of the tree. The ball fell down, and Tim caught it. He thanked the wise old man for his help. The other kids clapped for Tim and learned that it's important to think before doing something. From that day on, Tim and the other kids always tried to think before they acted, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to read a book. He sat on the floor and opened his favorite one. It had many pictures of tasty food like apples, cake, and pizza. Tim loved to look at the pictures and imagine how good they tasted. Then, Tim's friend, Sue, came to his house. Sue said, "Let's watch a movie!" Tim was excited because he liked movies too. They picked a movie about a big, funny dog. They sat on the sofa, ready to watch and laugh. But, something unexpected happened! The big, funny dog in the movie jumped out of the screen! He was real now, not just in the movie. Tim and Sue were so surprised. The dog said, "I am hungry! Do you have any tasty food?" Tim showed the dog his book with the pictures of tasty food. The dog laughed, and they all read the book together.

Jake took a peek inside his bag. He saw a pale white rabbit. He was so excited to have a new pet. "Hello there," he said to the rabbit. "I'm Jake, what's your name?" The rabbit hopped out of the bag and scurried away. Poor Jake was so sad. Jake chased the rabbit all around the house, but he couldn't catch him. Suddenly, Jake stopped. He heard a loud crash from down the street. Jake ran outside to see what happened. He found the rabbit lying in the road, hit by a car. He knew it was too late. His new pet was gone. Jake was heartbroken. He picked up the bag that held the pale white rabbit and slowly walked home.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there lived a cat named Tom. Tom loved to perform tricks for his friends. He could jump high and run fast. One day, Tom and his friends were playing outside. Suddenly, the wind started to blow very hard. The trees were shaking, and the sky turned dark. It was a hurricane! Tom and his friends were scared. They ran to find a safe place to hide. As they hid, the wind stopped blowing. The sun came out, and everything was quiet. But when they looked around, they saw that the hurricane had left a big surprise. It had blown a big, colorful circus tent into their town! Tom and his friends went inside and found that they could now perform their tricks for everyone in the town. The hurricane had brought them a new place to play and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a modest boy named Tim. He liked to try new things. One day, he wanted to watch a movie. He asked his mom, "Mom, can we watch a movie?" His mom said, "Yes, let's try a new movie today." They went to the store and found a movie about a big red ball. Tim was happy and said, "I want to watch this one!" They went home and watched the movie together. Tim loved the movie and laughed a lot. He tried to jump like the big red ball in the movie. Tim and his mom had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Chloe. She was three years old. Chloe was so excited when she went to the store with her mum. Chloe saw a pink suit hanging on the wall. She asked her mum "Can I have it?" Mum replied "Yes, of course you can! I will buy it for you." Chloe was very happy and she received the pink suit. Back home, Chloe put on her new suit. She looked so cute in it. Mum said "You look so beautiful in that pink suit!" Chloe smiled and said "Thank you, mum!"
