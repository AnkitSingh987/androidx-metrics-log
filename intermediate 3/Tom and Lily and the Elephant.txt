Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Elephant

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play together. One day, they go to a large place with many animals. It is a zoo. "Look, Tom, a lion!" Lily says. She points to a big, yellow animal with a long mane. The lion roars and shakes its head. "Wow, it is loud!" Tom says. He watches the lion with wide eyes. He thinks it is scary and cool. They walk to another place. They see a tall, gray animal with a long nose. It is an elephant. The elephant sprays water from its nose and flaps its ears. "Can we touch it, Lily?" Tom asks. He wants to feel the elephant's skin. "Maybe, but we have to be careful," Lily says. She watches the elephant with curiosity. She thinks it is funny and smart. They go to a fence. They see a sign that says "Do not feed the animals". They see a man with a bucket of food. He gives some food to the elephant. The elephant takes the food with its nose and puts it in its mouth. "Hello, kids, do you like the elephant?" the man asks. He smiles and waves at them. "Yes, we do!" Tom and Lily say. They smile and wave back. "Would you like to touch it?" the man asks. He holds out his hand. "Yes, please!" Tom and Lily say. They take his hand and follow him. They touch the elephant's skin. It is rough and wrinkly. The elephant makes a noise. It sounds like a trumpet. Tom and Lily laugh. They think it is fun and amazing. They thank the man and say goodbye to the elephant. They watch it for a while. Then they go to see more animals. They have a great day at the zoo.

Once upon a time, there was a serious cat named Tom. Tom liked to surprise his friends. One day, he had a plan to surprise his best friend, a dog named Max. Tom found some oil in the kitchen. He put the oil on the floor, right in front of the door. He knew Max would come in and slip on the oil. Tom hid behind the chair, waiting for Max. Max came in and saw the oil on the floor. He did not want to slip, so he walked around it. Tom was very sad that his surprise did not work. But Max saw Tom and gave him a big hug. They laughed and played together all day long.

Jimmy was feeling naughty. He had to eat his sandwich in the kitchen but instead he decided to run away. He ran out the door and ran till he reached the park. He played on the swings and slides and even built a sandcastle with the sandy beach. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise and saw someone running towards him. It was his mom! She was running to get him and when she arrived, she was very angry. She asked Jimmy why he had been so naughty and run away. Jimmy was very embarrassed and he knew he had to explain why. He quietly told his mom that he was too hungry and didn't want to eat his sandwich in the kitchen. He wanted to play instead. His mom smiled and shook her head. She said that he could have lots of fun but he also had to receive his sandwich. Jimmy smiled as he received his sandwich from his mom and thanked her for understanding. He was glad she was so understanding and he went back to the park to enjoy the rest of this day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a white cat named Snow. Snow loved to play with her toy weapon, a small soft sword. Every day, Snow would run and jump with her sword, pretending to be a brave hero. One day, while Snow was playing, she saw a big dog named Max. Max wanted to play too, but Snow was scared. Max was much bigger than her, and she thought he might take her toy weapon. So, Snow decided to run away and hide. But as Snow ran, something unexpected happened. Max started to yell, "Stop!" Snow stopped running and looked back. Max was not trying to take her weapon, he just wanted to give her a toy he found. It was a big, soft shield that matched her sword. Snow felt happy and they became best friends, playing together with their new toys every day.
