Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Gray Dog and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little gray dog named Max. Max was an adventurous dog who liked to play outside. One day, Max went to the park to improve his running skills. While Max was running, he saw a big gray rock. Max thought the rock could talk! He asked the rock, "Can you talk?" To Max's surprise, the rock said, "Yes, I can talk!" Max was so happy to have a new friend. They played together and had so much fun. But then, Max found out that the rock was not a rock at all! It was a turtle who was also gray. Max and the turtle became best friends and had many more adventurous days together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to get a haircut. Tim was scared of haircuts. His mom said, "Don't worry, it will be quick." She gave him a fake smile to make him feel better. Tim knew it was fake, but he held his mom's hand and went inside. In the haircut place, Tim saw a big chair. He sat down and the haircut man put a cape on him. The haircut man said, "I will be very gentle." Tim closed his eyes and felt the haircut man rub his head. It felt nice and Tim started to relax. When the haircut was done, Tim looked in the mirror. He saw his new haircut and smiled. The haircut man gave Tim a lollipop. Tim's mom said, "See, it wasn't so bad!" Tim was happy and not scared of haircuts anymore.

Tommy and his mommy were having fun in the park. It was a beautiful day and Tommy was excited to play! He was quickly drawn to the sandbox. He saw a bucket full of bricks and he couldn't wait to get started. His mommy said, "Tommy, why don't you show me how you can build a tall tower with these bricks?" Tommy was happy. He began to stack up the bricks, one on top of the other. He worked hard and soon he had a big tower in front of him. "Wow!" said his mommy. "That tower is so tall and the bricks are stacked so tight! You did a wonderful job. Well done, Tommy!" Tommy smiled and beamed with pride. He was so happy that his mommy was pleased. They stayed in the park for a while longer, playing, laughing and having fun!

One day, a shy cat named Kitty wanted to play with her friends. She saw them playing with a ball near the big tree. Kitty felt scared to ask if she could play too. She wanted to quit being shy, but she didn't know how. Kitty's mom saw her and said, "Kitty, why don't you go play with your friends? I know you can do it. Let's do a test. Try to say 'hi' to them and ask if you can play too." Kitty took a deep breath and walked to her friends. She said, "Hi, can I play too?" Her friends smiled and said, "Of course, Kitty! We are happy you asked." Kitty felt good that she passed the test and wasn't shy anymore.

Once there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was very excited because it was her birthday and her mom said they could decorate the house. Jane was so happy she couldn't contain it. Jane and her mom hung festive balloons, streamers, and some of Jane's favorite pictures. She ran around the house, admiring all the bright colors. She was so excited she started to giggle. After a few hours, it was time for the cake. Jane's mom lit the four candles and Jane took a deep breath of oxygen. Then she made a wish, blew out the candles, and the family sang happy birthday to her. Jane was so confused. She didn't understand what all the decorations and cake were for, until her mom explained it was her birthday! Jane clapped her hands and cheered. She was now the most decorated girl in the house. The end.

One day, a girl named Mia wanted to mail a letter to her friend, Tom. She drew a big, happy sun on the letter. Mia put the letter in a blue box outside her house. She felt excited. Mia saw a bird with harsh, loud sounds. The bird moved with a lot of motion. It flew up and down, and left and right. Mia said, "Hello, bird! Why are you so loud?" The bird said, "I am loud because I am happy! I like to fly with motion in the sky. I saw you mail a letter. That is fun too!" Mia laughed and said, "Yes, mailing letters is fun. I like to draw pictures for my friends." The bird and Mia became friends. They played together and had fun. Mia mailed more letters and the bird sang happy songs. They both loved their new friendship.
