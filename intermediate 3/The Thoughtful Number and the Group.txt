Category: Intermediate
Title: The Thoughtful Number and the Group

Once there was a thoughtful number. He wanted to add himself to a group of numbers, so he asked all the other numbers if he could join them. The numbers said no, so the number was very sad. He kept asking the numbers, but they always said no. One day, he had an idea. He thought that maybe if he could add himself to the group, they would say yes. He started to add himself to the group. But when he finished adding himself, the numbers were very angry. They shouted at him and said he could not be part of the group. The number was so sad. He knew that he was not allowed to be part of the group and he couldn't do anything to change it. He went away, feeling very lonely, and he never had another chance to join the group of numbers.

One day, a lovely girl named Sue went to play outside. She saw a big pile of snow. Sue had an idea. She wanted to make an igloo. Sue started to make snow blocks. Sue's friend, Tim, came to help. They made many snow blocks. They built the igloo together. They were very happy. But Tim had a secret. He had a snowball behind his back. When the igloo was done, Sue went inside. She did not know about the snowball. Tim threw the snowball at the igloo. The igloo did not break. It was very strong. Sue came out of the igloo. She saw the snowball on the ground. She knew what Tim did. But Sue did not get mad. Sue laughed. She made a snowball too. They had a fun snowball fight. The igloo was their safe place.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big blue balloon in the sky. It is very pretty. "Look, Anna, a blue balloon!" Ben says. "Wow, it is so pretty! I want it!" Anna says. They run after the balloon. They want to catch it. But the balloon is too high. They cannot reach it. "Maybe we can ask someone to help us," Ben says. They see a man with a ladder. He is fixing a light. They go to him. "Excuse me, sir, can you help us?" Anna says. "What do you need, little ones?" the man says. "We want the blue balloon in the sky. It is very pretty. Can you get it for us?" Ben says. The man looks at the balloon. He smiles. "Sure, I can try. But you have to be careful. The balloon might pop," he says. He puts his ladder under the balloon. He climbs up. He reaches for the balloon. He grabs it. He comes down. "Here you go, kids. The blue balloon is yours," he says. He gives the balloon to Anna and Ben. They are very happy. They thank the man. "Thank you, sir, you are very nice!" Anna says. "Yes, thank you, sir, you are very kind!" Ben says. They hug the balloon. They play with it. They are very careful. They do not want the balloon to pop. They have a lot of fun. They are glad that the blue balloon happened to be in the sky. It is very pretty.

Once upon a time there was a small girl called Alice. She was three years old. Every day she liked to go for a walk in the park. One day, while she was on her walk, she noticed a big doggy. She was a bit scared but she wasn’t sure why. She decided to stay and watch the doggy from a safe distance. The doggy was feeling tired. He lay on the ground and started to take a nap. Alice noticed this and thought it was strange. She approached the doggy carefully and saw that he was fast asleep. She was not scared any more. When the doggy awoke, he wagged his tail and jumped up and down with joy. Alice was happy that the doggy was so friendly. The moral of the story is that it is important to not be too scared when you see something strange. It can often be a pleasant surprise!
