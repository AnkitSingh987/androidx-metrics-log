Category: Intermediate
Title: The Stack of Rings and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small room, there was a big stack of rings. The rings were all different sizes and colors. One day, a little girl named Amy came into the room. She saw the stack and wanted to play with the rings. Amy picked up the rings and started to ring them together. As she played, a small angry bird flew into the room. The bird saw the stack of rings and wanted them too. The bird said, "Give me the rings, or I will be very angry!" Amy did not want to give the rings to the bird, so they both became very angry. Amy thought for a moment and then said, "Why don't we share the rings? We can take turns and play together." The angry bird thought about it and agreed. They played with the rings, taking turns and having fun. In the end, Amy and the bird became good friends. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can turn angry moments into happy ones.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to watch the sunset every day. One day, Lily met a boy named Tim. Tim was sometimes rude. But Lily wanted to be friends with him. Lily said to Tim, "Please come with me to see the sunset. It is very pretty." Tim thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, I will come with you." So, Lily and Tim went to see the sunset together. As they watched the sunset, Tim learned to be nice. He said, "Thank you, Lily, for showing me the sunset. It is very pretty. I will not be rude anymore." Lily was happy that she made a new friend and that Tim was nice now. They watched the sunset together every day and were friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a tall cat. The tall cat was very sad. The tall cat had a small box of ink. The tall cat wanted to play with the ink, but the ink was very bad. One day, the tall cat saw a small dog. The small dog was happy. The small dog said, "Let's play!" The tall cat was not happy. The tall cat said, "No, I have bad ink. I can't play." The small dog wanted to help. The small dog said, "I will take the bad ink away." The tall cat yawned and gave the small dog the ink. The small dog tried to take the ink away, but the ink was very bad. The ink made the small dog very sad too. Now, the tall cat and the small dog were both sad. They could not play because of the bad ink. They could not find a way to be happy again. The tall cat and the small dog stayed sad and could not play ever again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a charming little dog named Copper. Copper loved to play with his friends, run in the park, and eat tasty treats. One sunny day, Copper and his friends were playing with a big red ball. "Wow, Copper, you are so fast!" said his friend, Bunny. "I wish I could run like you!" Copper smiled and said, "Thanks, Bunny! Just keep trying, and you'll get faster too!" Suddenly, while they were playing, a big bird flew down and took the red ball away. Copper and his friends were sad and surprised. They didn't know what to do. Copper had an idea. He said, "Don't worry, friends! I will get our ball back!" So, Copper ran as fast as he could and jumped up to catch the bird. To everyone's surprise, Copper could fly! Copper flew high in the sky and got the red ball back from the bird. His friends were so happy and cheered for him. From that day on, Copper was not just a charming little dog who loved to eat treats, but also a hero who could fly.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a good pencil that he loved to draw with. One day, he could not find his good pencil. Tim was sad and did not know what to do. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. Then, Tim's friend, Sally, came to play. She saw Tim was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told her about his lost good pencil. Sally wanted to help Tim find his pencil. They looked and looked, but still could not find it. Finally, Sally had an idea. She said, "I will bring my good pencil from home, and we can share it." Tim was happy, and they both played and drew with Sally's good pencil. They had lots of fun, and Tim was not sad anymore.
