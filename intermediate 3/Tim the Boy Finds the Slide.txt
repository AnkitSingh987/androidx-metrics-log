Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Slide

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big board near the slide. Tim thought it would be fun to play with the board. His mom said, "That is fine, but be careful." Tim started to play with the board. He put it on the slide and sat on it. He went down the slide very fast. Tim was happy and laughed. He did it again and again. But then, something unexpected happened. The board broke in half! Tim was scared and screamed. His mom came running to him. She saw the broken board and said, "It's okay, Tim. You are fine. Let's go home and fix the board." Tim smiled, and they went home to fix the board together.

Once there was an attractive girl called Alice. She wanted to be a flute player and so she asked her mommy to take her to the park with her flute. When they arrived, Alice played her flute. The music was beautiful and it made the birds sing a different kind of song. Everyone in the park was so happy to hear it. Alice played for a long time and she made many new friends. She smiled and laughed with them and it was a very happy day. When she was done playing, Alice and her mommy packed up the flute and went home. Alice had a lot of fun at the park playing her flute!

One day, a little bird was in a hanger. The bird did not know how to escape. The bird was ignorant. It did not know what to do. It was sad and wanted to go outside. A kind boy saw the bird. He wanted to help the bird escape. The boy opened the hanger door. He said, "Come on, bird! You can go now!" But the bird was still scared. The boy had an idea. He found some bread. He put the bread outside the hanger. The bird saw the bread. It was happy. The bird flew out of the hanger to eat the bread. The bird escaped! The boy and the bird were both very happy.

One Sunday, a little girl named Mia and her mom went to the park. They saw an incredible big tree. Mia wanted to reach the high branch with the pretty leaves. "Mom, can you help me reach the branch?" asked Mia. Her mom tried to help her. They stretched their arms up high, but they still could not reach it. Just then, a friendly tall man saw them. He walked over and said, "I can help you reach the branch." He reached up and picked a pretty leaf for Mia. "Thank you!" said Mia and her mom. They all smiled and enjoyed their Sunday at the park.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lot of mist. The mist made it hard for people to see. They felt miserable because they could not play outside. The sun was hiding, and the sky was gray. One day, a kind girl named Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's make the mist go away!" She asked her friends to help her. They all agreed, and they started to think of a plan. They wanted to make the town happy again. Lily and her friends decided to sing a happy song. They believed that their happy song would make the mist go away. They sang and danced in a circle. Slowly, the mist started to disappear. The sun came out, and everyone was happy again. The town was no longer miserable, and they all thanked Lily and her friends for their help.

Once upon a time, there was a shy girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily loved to eat prunes, but she was too shy to ask her mom for them. One sunny day, Lily saw a big box of prunes on the table. She was very happy and wanted to eat them all. But she remembered her mom told her to always split the prunes with her friend, Tim. Lily went to find Tim, and they both sat down to eat the prunes. They were so excited to eat them, but then something unexpected happened. When they opened the box, there were no prunes inside! Instead, there were lots of colorful balls to play with. Lily and Tim were surprised, but they laughed and played with the balls all day. They forgot about the prunes and had lots of fun together. In the end, Lily was not shy anymore, and she made a new friend in Tim.
