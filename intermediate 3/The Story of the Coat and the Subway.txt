Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Coat and the Subway

Once upon a time there was a little girl who wanted to get to her grandpa's house quickly. She asked, "Can I take the subway?". Her mom said, "Yes, if it's ok with daddy", so the little girl asked her dad, "Daddy can I take the subway?" Her dad said, "Yes, but be sure to take a comfortable seat and enjoy your ride". So the little girl put on her coat, grabbed her bag and went to the subway station. She asked the station master, "Can I take the subway to my grandpa's house?" The station master said, "Yes! Just be sure to request the right stop when you get there." The little girl thanked the station master and got on the subway. She enjoyed the ride and when she finally arrived at her grandpa's house, she requested the stop and got off. Her grandpa was waiting outside to give her a big hug!

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived an intelligent girl named Lily. She had a funny pet, a potato. The potato had a big smile and liked to play with Lily. One day, Lily and the potato played a game. They ran and jumped around the house. But then, the potato said, "I quit! I am tired!" Lily laughed and gave the potato a hug. After that, Lily and the potato took a nap. When they woke up, they played more games. They were happy and had lots of fun. And they never quit being best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy sack. He loved to play with his toy sack every day. One day, Tim saw a sharp rock outside. He thought it was pretty and wanted to keep it. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, I found a sharp rock. Can I keep it in my sack?" His mom looked at the rock and said, "Okay, but be careful. The rock might make the sack weak." Tim played with his sack and his new sharp rock. He was very happy. But after a while, the sack started to get weak. Tim didn't notice it. He played and played until the weak sack broke. All his toys fell out. Tim was sad. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, my sack is broken. What can I do?" His mom said, "I told you the sharp rock might make the sack weak. Let's fix it together." So, Tim and his mom fixed the sack. Tim learned to be careful with sharp things. He never put the sharp rock in his sack again. Tim and his mom were happy, and they played with the toys together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Mark. Mark was a fearful boy. He was scared of many things. One day, Mark went to the park to play. While playing, Mark saw a big shadow. Mark was very scared. He did not know what it was. But then, a nice man came to explain. The man said, "Do not be scared, Mark. It is just a tree's shadow." Mark felt better. He was not scared of the shadow anymore. But then, something unexpected happened. The shadow started to move! Mark and the man were very surprised. It was not a tree's shadow at all. It was a big, friendly dog! The dog just wanted to play with Mark. Mark was not fearful anymore. He played with the dog and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog with a big nose. He liked to smell everything. One day, he found a pile of unknown toys. The dog was very happy. He wanted to take the toys to his home. So, he started to load the toys on his back. He carried them one by one. It was hard work, but the little dog did not give up. Finally, all the toys were at his home. The little dog played with the toys all day. He was very tired but happy. His big nose helped him find the unknown toys, and he had a lot of fun.
