Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Slide

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a quiet day, and Tim was very excited to play. He saw a big board near the slide and wanted to climb it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I climb the board, please?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim, but be careful." Tim was so happy and started to climb the board. It was not too hard, but he felt like a big boy. After Tim climbed the board, he slid down the slide with a big smile on his face. He looked at his mom and said, "I did it!" His mom clapped and said, "Good job, Tim!" They played at the park all day, and Tim felt very proud.

Once upon a time, there was a white dog named Spot. Spot lived on a big ranch with his family. They loved to play games together every day. Spot's favorite game was to bury his toys in the yard. He would dig and dig until he found the perfect spot to hide them. One day, Spot's family told him they were going on a trip. They would be gone for a few days. Spot was sad, but he knew he could still have fun. He thought about the toys he had buried and decided to find them all while his family was away. Spot spent the whole day digging and finding his toys. He found his red ball, his blue bone, and his green squeaky toy. But there was one toy he could not find. It was his yellow rubber duck. Spot looked and looked but could not find it. The next day, Spot heard a car. His family was back from their trip! They saw Spot and all the toys he had found. They were so happy and proud of him. They helped him look for the yellow rubber duck, and they found it together in the last spot they looked. From that day on, Spot and his family played together on the ranch. They buried toys and found them again. They had lots of fun, and Spot was always the best at finding the hidden toys.

Once upon a time, there was a small furry robot. It had shiny claws and big eyes. The robot wanted to join the fun, so it asked around to see if anyone wanted to play. Jamie heard the robot and said, "Yes, join us! I'm making a big sandcastle." The robot hopped up and down with delight and ran over to Jamie. "We can make it even bigger!" The robot said. Jamie and the robot worked together to make the biggest sandcastle ever. When they were finished, they were very proud. Jamie asked, "What do you want to do now?" The robot thought and then said, "Let's go to the park and swing on the swings." So, Jamie and the furry robot went to the park. They had lots of fun swinging and laughing. They joined hands and spun around until they both got so dizzy that they had to lay down and rest. When the day was over, Jamie said to the robot, "Thank you for joining me today. I had such a good time." The robot smiled and nodded. "Me too! Can we do it again?" Jamie smiled and said, "Yes, of course!" And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily liked books. She liked to look at the pictures and hear the stories. She had many books in her room, but her favorite book was orange. It had a big lion on the cover and funny animals inside. One day, Lily wanted to read her orange book, but she could not find it. She looked under her bed, in her closet, and on her shelf, but the book was not there. She felt sad and asked her mom, "Mom, where is my orange book?" Mom smiled and said, "I have a surprise for you, Lily. Come with me to the living room." Lily followed her mom, hoping to see her book. In the living room, Lily saw a big box with a bow on it. Mom said, "This is for you, Lily. Happy birthday!" Lily was happy. It was her birthday and she had forgotten. She opened the box and saw many things: a doll, a puzzle, a ball, and a hat. But she did not see her book. She looked at her mom and said, "Thank you, Mom, but where is my orange book?" Mom winked and said, "Look behind the couch, Lily. There is one more thing for you." Lily ran to the couch and looked behind it. She saw her orange book, but it was not alone. Next to it was a small, furry, orange kitten. The kitten meowed and licked Lily's hand. Lily was very happy. She picked up the book and the kitten and hugged them. She said, "Thank you, Mom, this is the best birthday ever! I love my orange book and my orange cat!" Mom hugged Lily and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I knew you liked orange things. I saw you reading your book every day, so I thought you would like a friend who looks like it. I hope you will take good care of your cat and your book." Lily nodded and said, "I will, Mom. I will read my book to my cat and play with him. What is his name?" Mom said, "You can name him whatever you want, Lily. It's your cat." Lily thought for a moment and said, "I will name him Leo, like the lion in my book. He is brave and strong and orange, like Leo." Mom smiled and said, "That's a good name, Lily. I'm sure Leo will love you and your book. Now, let's have some cake and celebrate your birthday." Lily, Mom, and Leo went to the kitchen and had cake and ice cream. Lily was very happy. She had her orange book, her orange cat, and her mom. She thought it was the best day ever.
