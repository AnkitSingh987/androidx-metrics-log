Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Boat

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big navy boat on the water. He gazed at the boat for a long time. Tim loved boats and wanted to see it up close. Tim walked to the water and saw a man next to the boat. The man was eating something that looked disgusting. Tim asked, "What are you eating?" The man said, "It's my lunch. Do you want some?" Tim said, "No, thank you." Tim went closer to the boat, but he fell into the water. The water was cold and dirty. Tim was scared and cried for help. The man with the disgusting lunch tried to help, but he couldn't swim. Tim stayed in the water, cold and sad.

Once upon a time, there was a big piano in a small room. The piano was very soft to touch. A little girl named Amy liked to play the piano every day. She loved the soft music it made. One day, Amy's friend, Ben, came over to play. Amy said to Ben, "I can play the piano, and it makes soft music!" Ben was excited and wanted to try too. But when Ben played the piano, he pressed the keys too hard. The music was loud and not soft. Amy told Ben, "You need to settle down and play softly." Ben listened to Amy and tried again. This time, he played the piano softly. The soft music made them both happy. From that day on, Ben learned to settle down and play the piano softly. Amy and Ben played the piano together, making beautiful soft music. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends and learn from them.

One day, a little boy found a magazine in the park. He looked at the pictures and saw a man being rude. The boy did not like the rude man. He wanted to hide the magazine so no one else would see the rude man. The boy thought hard. He decided to bury the magazine. He dug a hole with his hands in the soft dirt. The boy put the magazine in the hole and covered it with dirt. Now, no one could see the rude man in the magazine. Later, the boy told his mom about the magazine and the rude man. His mom was proud that he hid the magazine. She told him that it was good to keep rude things away. The boy felt happy and knew he did the right thing.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play on her porch with her toys. She had a big red ball, a yellow duck, and a blue car. She played with them every day, but she was always slow and careful so they wouldn't break. One day, Lily's mom told her that they were going to get a new pet. Lily was very excited and couldn't wait to play with the pet on her porch. Her mom said, "Be slow and careful with the new pet, just like you are with your toys." The next day, they brought home a small, soft kitten. Lily was so happy! She took the kitten to the porch to play with her toys. But the kitten was not slow and careful like Lily. It jumped on the big red ball and it rolled away. Then, it knocked over the yellow duck and chased the blue car. Lily started to weep because her toys were not safe. Lily's mom came to the porch and saw her weeping. She hugged Lily and said, "Don't worry, we will teach the kitten to be slow and careful like you." They worked together to teach the kitten how to play nicely with the toys. Soon, Lily, the kitten, and the toys were all happy on the porch, playing slow and careful games together. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tom wanted to sail his toy boat. He went to the pond near his house. The boat had a big strip on it. The strip was red and blue. Tom was so happy to play with his boat. Tom put his boat on the water. At first, the boat went slow. But then, the wind blew hard. The boat went very fast! Tom ran to keep up with his boat. He did not want to lose it. Then, something unexpected happened. A big duck came to the pond. The duck saw Tom's boat. The duck thought the strip was food. The duck picked up the boat with its beak! Tom was scared. But the duck was nice. It gave the boat back to Tom. Tom said thank you to the duck. From that day on, Tom and the duck became good friends.
