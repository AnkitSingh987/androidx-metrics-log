Category: Intermediate
Title: Poppy and the Slide

Poppy the pig was very hungry. He went on a long walk in search of something to eat. He looked around but couldn't find anything. He decided to have a hop, skip and jump. Poppy skipped and hopped along the grassy field, until he heard a bell ringing. He stopped to look and saw a little slide in a nearby playground. He had never seen a slide before and he got excited to try it. Poppy slowly started to climb up the steps to the slide. He got to the top and slowly slid down. He felt the wind in his face as he jabbed down. Poppy felt so happy. He soon realized that he still hadn't found anything to eat. He quickly ran off to look for food. After a while, Poppy was very tired and thought he would never find something to eat. Suddenly, he turned the corner and saw a picnic blanket with lots of yummy food. He was so happy! He immediately started to eat. Moral of the Story: Poppy had a lot of fun playing in the playground, but eventually he had to leave and continue his search for food. Sometimes it is important to remember why we are doing something, and not let ourselves get distracted.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and make a big, messy room. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, it's time to clean up your messy room. Let's do some work together." Tim didn't want to clean up, but he knew he had to help his mom. So, he started to pick up his toys and put them away. As he was working, his best friend, Sam, came to the door. "Hi, Tim! Can I join you?" asked Sam. "Sure, Sam! We can work together to clean my messy room," said Tim. They laughed and had fun as they picked up the toys. Soon, the room was clean and they could play together. Tim learned that work can be fun when you join with a friend.

One day, Tom and Sue were playing in the house. They wanted to relax, but it was hard. They heard a loud sound from the cupboard. They looked at each other and were scared. Tom said, "What is in the cupboard?" Sue said, "I don't know, let's open it." They opened the cupboard and saw a thick book. The book was making the loud sound. Tom and Sue were not scared now. They took the thick book and sat down. They looked at the pictures and read the words. They were happy and could relax now.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to dress up in pretty clothes. One day, she got an invitation to a party. She was very excited and wanted to wear her best dress. At the party, Mia met a boy named Tim. Tim's clothes were not as nice as Mia's. He wore a shirt with a small tear and shoes with a scuff. Mia said, "Your clothes look terrible, Tim!" Tim felt sad and didn't want to play with Mia anymore. Mia's mom saw what happened and told her, "Mia, it's not nice to say mean things about someone's clothes. What matters is being kind and having fun at the party." Mia felt bad for what she said. She went to Tim and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. Let's play together and have fun at the party." After that, Mia and Tim played together and had a great time. Mia learned that it's not what you wear, but how you treat others that makes a good friend. And that was the best party lesson of all.

One day, a little sheep named Fluffy went for a walk. Fluffy wanted to be famous. He thought if he could do something big, everyone would know his name. He saw a big hill and thought, "I will climb to the top!" As Fluffy climbed, he met a nice bird. The bird said, "I will help you, Fluffy!" The bird wanted to encourage Fluffy. They climbed up the hill together. Fluffy felt happy and strong with his new friend. When they got to the top, they saw a big surprise. There was a big party for Fluffy! All his sheep friends were there. They said, "We want to make you famous, Fluffy!" The bird had told them about Fluffy's big climb. Now, Fluffy was the most famous sheep in the world, and he had a great party with all his friends.
