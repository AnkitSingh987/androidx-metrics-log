Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Man and the Cane

Once upon a time, there was a man named Tom. Tom was very good at making things. One day, he wanted to create something new. He thought and thought about what to make. Then, he had a great idea. He would create a cane. Tom worked hard to make the cane. He used wood and paint to make it look nice. He was very happy with his work. The cane was strong and pretty. Tom knew it would help people walk better. Tom showed his cane to his friends. They all liked it very much. They told Tom he was successful. Tom felt proud of his work. He knew he had made something good. And from that day, Tom and his cane helped many people in the town.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to her mom. She wanted to explain her new style. She put on a funny hat, a big shirt, and small shoes. "Look, Mom!" Amy said. "I have a new style!" Her mom looked at her and smiled. "Oh, Amy, you look so funny! But, I think your shoes are too small. Don't they hurt?" Mom asked. Amy thought for a moment and said, "Yes, they do hurt a little. But I like my new style." Just then, Amy's cat, Fluffy, jumped into the room. Fluffy was wearing Amy's big shoes! Amy and her mom laughed. "Fluffy has a new style too!" Amy said. Now, Amy and Fluffy both had funny styles and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely big boat. The boat had a big anchor. One day, the boat went to a pretty island. On the island, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily saw the boat and said, "Wow, what a lovely boat!" She went on the boat and met the captain. The captain was nice and said, "Hello, Lily! Do you like my boat?" Lily and the captain became friends. They played on the boat and had fun. But one day, the sun was very hot. It was so hot that it started to burn the boat. The captain and Lily were scared. The captain said, "Lily, let's put the anchor down and go to the island." They put the anchor down and swam to the island. They were safe. The boat was a little burnt, but it was still lovely. Lily and the captain were happy. They played on the island and had fun. And they were always careful when it was very hot.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to chew on his toys all day long. He had a big body and a waggy tail. One sunny day, Buddy went to the park with his friend, a little boy named Tim. They ran and played together. Buddy would chew on sticks while Tim threw them for him to catch. As they played, Tim told Buddy how smart he was for being so good at catching the sticks. Buddy felt very happy and proud of his intelligent body. They played until the sun went down, and then they went home, tired but happy.

One day a little boy was walking in the park. He saw a weapon on the ground. He was very scared and wanted to shut his eyes. But he was brave and looked at the weapon carefully. He saw that it was fragile and he knew he should not touch it. He looked around and saw a nice lady walking her dog. He ran to her and asked her to shut the weapon away. She understood and took the weapon. She put it in a safe place and thanked the little boy for being so brave. The little boy smiled, happy that he had done the right thing.

One day, a dog named Max found a big bone in the park. Max was very happy. He took the bone to his comfortable bed at home. Max wanted to design a special place to keep his bone safe. Max asked his friend, Cat, for help. "Cat, can you help me design a place for my bone?" Cat said, "Yes, Max. Let's make a nice box for it." They found a box and painted it with bright colors. Max put his bone inside the box. Every day, Max would take the bone out of the box and play with it. Then, he would put it back in the box and sleep in his comfortable bed. Max and Cat were very proud of their design. They knew the bone was safe and Max was happy.
