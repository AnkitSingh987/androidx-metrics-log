Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a big, soft paw. He liked to play in the park with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to run and jump and laugh together. One sunny day, Spot and Lily were playing in the park when a butterfly landed on Spot's paw. Spot was very glad and said, "Look, Lily! A butterfly on my paw!" Lily knelt down to see the butterfly up close. She smiled and said, "Wow, Spot! It's so pretty!" The butterfly flew away, and Spot and Lily continued to play. They were happy to have seen the pretty butterfly. They played until the sun went down, and then they went home. Spot and Lily were very glad to have each other as friends.

---

One day a little girl named Sally was playing in her backyard. She was exploring the small patch of wild grass and trees. As Sally was looking around she heard a rustling noise. Peeking through the grass and trees, she saw a pretty yellow bird. Sally was so excited and exclaimed, "Oh look! A wild yellow bird!" The bird looked at Sally and replied, "Hello Sally! Would you like to come and play with me?" Sally happily said yes and the two of them had a great time. The bird brought Sally some supplies to make a house. She gathered a few sticks, some leaves and some pretty flowers. She carefully supplied the birdhouse with all of these things. The house was done and the two of them were happy. The wild yellow bird put one final touch - a bright yellow feather - on top of the house. "Your birdhouse is so pretty, Sally," said the bird. Sally smiled and said, "I'm so glad you like it." And they both lived happily ever after.

One day, a tired cat named Tom was walking in the park. He saw a little bird sitting on a tree. The bird seemed very sad. Tom wanted to know why the bird was sad, so he asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The little bird said, "I am sad because I am tired of flying all my life. I want to try something new." Tom thought for a moment and then said, "Why don't you try walking with me? It's fun and not tiring." So, the little bird hopped down from the tree and walked with Tom. They walked and walked until they were both tired. Then, they sat under a big tree to rest. The little bird was happy because he tried something new. Tom was happy because he made a new friend. From that day on, they spent a lot of time together, enjoying life and trying new things.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a heavy blanket in his room. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sue. Tim picked up the blanket and walked to Sue's house. On the way, he saw a big puddle. He tried to walk around it, but he slipped and fell. The heavy blanket got all wet and dirty. Tim was sad and said, "Oh no! My blanket is all dirty. What will Sue say?" He picked up the blanket and continued to walk to Sue's house. When he got there, he knocked on the door. Sue opened the door and saw Tim holding the dirty, heavy blanket. Sue looked at the blanket and said, "Don't worry, Tim! We can clean it together." They went inside and washed the blanket. It took a long time, but they had fun. After the blanket was clean and dry, they put it on the floor and sat on it. They talked and laughed, happy that they had fixed the problem together.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had an unusual toy. It was a big, red ball with glue on it. Tim liked to play with his ball all day. One day, Tim took his ball to the park. He wanted to push the ball and see how far it would go. So, he pushed the ball very hard. The ball rolled and rolled until it hit a tree. When the ball hit the tree, the glue on the ball stuck to the tree. Tim tried to pull the ball off, but it was too sticky. Tim was sad. Then, a nice girl named Sue came and helped Tim. They both pushed the ball together, and it came off the tree. Tim and Sue laughed and played with the ball for the rest of the day.
