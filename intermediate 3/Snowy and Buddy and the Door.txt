Category: Intermediate
Title: Snowy and Buddy and the Door

One day, a white cat named Snowy went to visit her friend, a brown dog named Buddy. Snowy walked to Buddy's house and knocked on his door. Buddy opened the door and said, "Welcome, Snowy! Come in!" Snowy and Buddy played with toys and shared yummy treats. They laughed and had a lot of fun. Buddy showed Snowy his favorite toy, a big red ball. Snowy showed Buddy her favorite toy, a small blue mouse. After playing, Snowy and Buddy sat down and talked. Snowy said, "I like our visit. We should do it more." Buddy agreed, "Yes, Snowy. I like when you visit, too. We are good friends." They smiled and continued to play until it was time for Snowy to go home.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a thermometer. He did not know what it was. He showed it to his mom. "Mom, what is this?" he asked. His mom said, "Tim, that is a thermometer. It is useful. It tells us if it is hot or cold." Tim wanted to try it. He went outside with the thermometer. It was a sunny day. The thermometer said it was hot. Tim was happy to know that. Later, it was night. Tim took the thermometer outside again. This time, it said it was cold. Tim told his mom, "Mom, the thermometer is useful. It told me it is cold now." His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. The thermometer is very useful. Now we know when to wear warm clothes and when to wear cool clothes." Tim was happy to have a useful thermometer.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Lily. She had a big, round body and loved to play with her friends. One day, she went to the park to play with her best friend, Tim. When Lily got to the park, she saw Tim playing with a big, red ball. "Can I borrow your ball, Tim?" Lily asked. Tim smiled and said, "Sure! Let's play together!" They took turns kicking the ball and laughing. After playing for a while, they got tired and sat under a tree. They talked and laughed, feeling happy with their big, round bodies. The sun was shining, and they had lots of fun playing together. And that's how Lily and Tim spent their happy day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to design pretty things. One day, she made a big, red bow. Lily was very proud of her bow. Lily wore her bow to the park. While playing, she met a new friend, Tim. Tim saw the bow and thought it was a butterfly. He tried to catch it, but Lily said, "No, no, Tim! It's just a bow." Tim felt silly, but then he had an idea. He took the bow and put it on a tree. Now, it looked like a real butterfly! Lily and Tim laughed and played with their new "butterfly" friend. They were both proud of their fun day at the park.

One day, two friends, Jake and Peter, were playing in the park. It was a sunny, hot day and they were very excited. Suddenly, they heard a noise. It was a loud, screeching sound. "That sound is coming from over there," said Peter, pointing to an old car. They went to the car and saw that one of the tires had parted from the rim and was rolling away. "Oh no," exclaimed Jake. "We must get it back!" They set off after the tire, but it was too fast. No matter how hard they ran, they couldn't catch it. The tire rolled further and further away until it was out of sight. "We lost it," said Peter sadly. Both friends knew they wouldn't be able to find the tire and they sadly went home. It was a hot, sad day.

One day, a little girl named Sally found a dry paper on the floor. She picked it up and saw a picture of a big tree with a cat on it. Sally wanted to help the cat, so she blinked her eyes and started to think. Sally had a friend named Tim. She ran to Tim and showed him the paper. Tim looked at the paper and blinked his eyes too. He wanted to help the cat as well. They both went to the big tree they saw in the paper. When they got there, the cat was still in the tree. Sally and Tim tried to reach the cat, but they were too small. The cat was scared and didn't know what to do. Sally and Tim blinked their eyes and thought very hard. They had an idea! They found a long stick and used it to help the cat get down. The cat was happy and so were Sally and Tim. They saved the day and made a new friend.
