Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Tim

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She saw her mommy's iron on the table. She thought it looked boring. Lily wanted to find something fun to do. So, she went to look for her friend, Tim. Lily found Tim in his room. She said, "Tim, let's play hide and seek!" Tim agreed, and they started to play. Lily hid behind the big chair, and Tim tried to find her. He walked around the room and said, "Where is Lily?" Lily could not help but peek out from behind the chair. Tim saw her and shouted, "I found you!" Just then, the boring iron started to move! It jumped off the table and started to dance. Lily and Tim were so surprised. They laughed and danced with the iron. It was not boring anymore. From that day on, they had lots of fun playing with the dancing iron.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big, green friend named Broccoli. They liked to play together in the park. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. Tim thought Broccoli was very fine. One sunny day, Tim and Broccoli went to play at the park. But when they got there, the gate was shut. Tim was sad, and Broccoli was sad too. They wanted to find a way to open the gate so they could play. Tim and Broccoli tried to push the gate, but it did not open. They tried to pull the gate, but it still did not open. Then, Tim had an idea. He found a big stick and used it to lift the gate's lock. The gate opened, and Tim and Broccoli were so happy. They went inside and played all day, having a fine time together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who hated sunglasses. She thought that sunglasses made people look silly. One day, she was at the playground with her friends. One of her friends was wearing a pair of sunglasses. The little girl made mean things about the sunglasses. Her friend was very sad. She was honest and told the little girl that she did not like how she was acting. The little girl was surprised to know that she hurt her friend's feelings. The little girl had a lesson to learn. She learned that it is not nice to talk mean things about others. Even if something is not your favorite, you should still be nice to someone who likes it. She learned to be honest and respectful to everyone. From then on, the little girl was very kind and she stopped hating sunglasses.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird. The blue bird could speak. It lived in a tree near a cheap shop. One day, the blue bird saw a cat. The cat was big and hungry. The cat wanted to eat the blue bird. The blue bird was scared. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat started to speak too! The cat said, "I don't want to eat you, blue bird. I want to be your friend." The blue bird was happy. They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to play outside. She saw a big tree with lots of leaves. Sue wanted to climb the tree. She put her foot on a branch and went up, up, up. She was very high and could see her house. Sue saw a twist in the tree. She wanted to see what was there. She moved closer and saw a little bird. The bird looked worried. Sue asked the bird, "Why are you worried?" The bird said, "I lost my mom. I am scared." Sue wanted to help the bird. She said, "Do not worry. We will find your mom." They looked around and saw a big bird on a branch. The little bird was happy. "That is my mom!" Sue and the little bird went to the big bird. The big bird said, "Thank you for helping my baby." Sue was happy. She knew everything was fine.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. They saw many animals like lions, monkeys, and elephants. Tim loved the zoo. At the zoo, there was a man who could print pictures of the animals. Tim wanted a picture of a funny monkey. The man said, "Here you go, Tim!" and printed a picture for him. When Tim looked at the picture, he saw that it was not a monkey, but a big elephant! Tim felt embarrassed. He said, "Mom, this is not the monkey!" His mom laughed and said, "It's okay, Tim. Let's go back and ask for the right picture." They went back, and the man printed the correct picture. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you!"
