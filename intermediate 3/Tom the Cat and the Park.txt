Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Park

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a friendly cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his ball. One day, Tom saw a big frame in the park. The frame was very big and pretty. Tom wanted to play with the frame. Tom asked his friend, a dog named Sam, to come and play with him. They played a game called "pass". Tom would pass the ball to Sam and then Sam would pass it back to Tom. They had so much fun playing near the big frame. But then, a big wind came and blew the frame away. The frame fell on the ball and broke it. Tom and Sam were sad because they could not play "pass" anymore. They went home and did not play again that day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. It was a quiet day. Tim liked to play at the park. He found a small box under a tree. The box had a stamp on it. The stamp had a picture of a dog. Tim opened the box. Inside, he saw a toy dog. The toy dog looked like the dog on the stamp. Tim was very happy. He played with the toy dog at the park. Then, something unexpected happened. The toy dog started to move! It was not a toy dog after all. It was a real dog! Tim was very surprised. The dog wagged its tail and barked. Tim and the dog played together at the park all day. They became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big oak tree. The oak tree was very old and strong. It was in a beautiful forest. Many animals liked to play near the oak tree. One sunny day, a little rabbit named Lily was playing near the oak tree. She saw a brilliant butterfly. Lily wanted to catch the butterfly, so she ran after it. But the butterfly was fast and flew away. Lily felt sad. Then, a wise old owl came down from the oak tree. The owl said, "Don't be sad, Lily. The butterfly will come back if you wait." So, Lily decided to delay and sit near the oak tree. After a while, the brilliant butterfly came back, and Lily was happy again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a rich player named Tom. Tom was very good at playing ball. He loved to play with his friends every day. Tom was also very kind and liked to share his toys with his friends. One sunny day, Tom and his friends went to play ball in the park. They saw a big tree with a swing hanging from it. They all took turns playing on the swing. Tom thought of an idea. He said, "Let's hang our ball on the tree and see who can hit it the most times!" All the friends liked the idea and started to play. They had lots of fun taking turns hitting the ball hanging from the tree. After playing for a while, they got tired and sat down to rest. They talked about how much fun they had and how they liked to share their toys with each other. The moral of the story is that sharing with friends makes everyone happy. Tom was a rich player, but he didn't keep all his toys for himself. He shared them with his friends, and they all had a great time together.

Once upon a time, there were two friendly friends who loved to play together. Every day, they played with their stuff and had lots of fun. One day, the two friends started to quarrel. They argued about who had the most stuff and wouldn't stop! Finally, the two friends stopped arguing and agreed to share their stuff. They were both happy and went back to playing together. They played all day long and had lots of fun. They were still friendly even though they had quarreled. They learned an important lesson - it’s best to share and be kind!

Once upon a time, there was a painter. He loved to paint all day. He painted the sun, the sky, and the trees. But he had a secret. He kept something hidden. The painter had a magic brush. This brush was very special. It could make his paintings come to life. He kept it hidden so no one could find it. He used it only when he was alone. One day, a little girl saw the painter use the magic brush. She promised to keep the secret hidden too. They became good friends. They painted together and made their paintings come to life. They were very happy and kept the magic brush secret safe.
