Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rhinoceros Living and the Toys

Once upon a time there was a young rhinoceros living in the jungle. He was very excited because he had some new toys that he hadn't seen before. One day, he decided to examine them and see what they do. He looked closely at each one and touched them gently. Suddenly he heard a noise behind him! The rhinoceros turned around and saw a little monkey watching him. The monkey said to him, "Hello! What are you doing with all those new toys? Can I help you examine them?" The rhinoceros smiled and said, "Yes, let's examine my new toys together!" So the two of them examined each toy, moving them around and discovering what each one did. It was so much fun! After they were finished, the rhinoceros thanked the monkey for helping him examine his new toys. They said goodbye and the rhinoceros went back to playing with his toys.

One day, Tom saw a tall bookcase in his room. The bookcase was big and full of many books. Tom loved to read books, but he could not reach the top. He wanted to read a book from the top of the bookcase. Tom had an idea. Tom went to the kitchen and found a chair. He put the chair by the bookcase. But the chair was not tall enough. He looked around and saw a big box. Tom put the box on the chair. Now, he could reach the top of the bookcase. He was very happy. Tom climbed up and got the book. But when he got down, he saw a big mess. The box and chair were dirty. Tom knew he had to clean it up. He got a cloth and wiped the chair and the box. Now, everything was clean again. Tom was proud of himself and read his new book with a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to design things. He had a big box of toys in the garage. The garage was filthy, with dirt and dust everywhere. One day, Tim went to the garage to play. He wanted to design a new toy with his blocks. But when he opened the box, the blocks were gone! Tim was very sad and didn't know what to do. Then, Tim saw a trail of blocks on the floor. He followed the trail and found his dog, Max. Max had taken the blocks to make a surprise for Tim. Max had designed a big tower with the blocks! Tim was so happy, and they played together all day.

Lily likes to play with her toys. She has a red car, a yellow ball, and a black plane. She likes to make the plane fly in the air and say "vroom vroom". She thinks the plane is very fast and cool. One day, Lily sees a big plane in the sky. It is white and blue and has a lot of windows. She wants to go on the big plane and see what is inside. She asks her mom if they can go on the big plane. Her mom says no, they have to stay home and do some work. Lily is sad and angry. She does not want to do work. She wants to go on the big plane. She thinks of a plan. She waits until her mom is busy on the phone. She grabs her black plane and runs outside. She sees the big plane in the sky again. She thinks it is coming closer. She runs to the road and waves her black plane. She hopes the big plane will see her and stop. But the big plane does not see her. It is too high and too fast. It does not stop. It goes over her head and makes a loud noise. Lily does not hear the noise of the car behind her. She does not see the lights or the horn. She only sees the big plane. She does not move. She does not feel the pain. She only feels the black plane in her hand. The black plane is broken. Lily is broken too.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house near a big hill. The man and his wife were very happy together. They liked to play and laugh all day. One day, it was very hot outside. The sun was shining, and everything was warm. The man and his wife decided to go for a walk. They went up the big hill to see what they could find. When they got to the top, they saw a big ice block. They were very surprised! The ice block started to melt because of the sun. The man and his wife were frightened. They did not know what to do. They held hands and ran down the hill, back to their house. They were safe and happy again. They learned that even when they were frightened, they could be brave together.
