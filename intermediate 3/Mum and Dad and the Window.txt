Category: Intermediate
Title: Mum and Dad and the Window

Mum and Dad were enjoying a peaceful afternoon. They were discussing what type of pet they should get for their daughter. Suddenly, she came running in, with a huge grin on her face. She mentioned there was something unusual outside their house. “What do you mean, darling?” asked Dad. The little girl eagerly said, “Right outside the window, there's a big, ordinary head!” Mum and Dad looked out of the window and sure enough, there was a big, ordinary head, just floating in the air! They were all quite baffled by the strange sight. “How on Earth did it get there?” asked Mum. The little girl shrugged, “I don't know. But I like it! Can I keep it?” Mum and Dad both laughed and agreed that the strange head could stay. The little girl was delighted and thanked her parents. From that day forward, the family enjoyed having their very own, ordinary head!

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw a lot of candy on the shelf. He wanted to eat some, but his mom said, "No, Tim. We have to pay for it first." Tim felt sad, but he listened to his mom. As they walked to the front of the store, Tim saw a light door. He thought there might be more candy behind the door. He opened it and saw a big room with many colors. A kind man was there, and he said, "Hi, Tim! Do you like candy?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, I do!" The kind man warned Tim, "You can have some candy, but only take one piece." Tim picked a big red candy and said, "Thank you!" He went back to his mom and showed her the candy. His mom was surprised and asked, "Where did you find that, Tim?" Tim told her about the light door and the kind man. They went back to the door, but it was gone! The candy was still in Tim's hand. They both laughed and went home, happy about their fun day at the store.

One day, a girl named Mia and her dog, Max, went to play in the woods. They liked to gather sticks and leaves. They found a small cabin. Mia said, "Look, Max! A cabin!" Inside the cabin, they saw a table with a big cake. Mia and Max wanted to eat the cake. But first, they had to find the owner of the cabin. Mia called out, "Hello? Is anyone here?" A fast rabbit named Remy came out of a room. Remy said, "Hi! I made the cake. We can all eat it together." Mia, Max, and Remy ate the cake and played all day. They became good friends and had lots of fun in the little cabin.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow pig. The pig lived on a farm with many friends. The pig liked to play all day. One day, the pig met a girl. The girl wanted to ride the pig. The pig said yes, and they had fun. They rode around the farm together, laughing and playing. After they were done, the girl said thank you to the yellow pig. They became best friends. From that day on, they played and rode together every day. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful girl named Amy. She loved to wear hats. One day, she had to choose a hat to wear to a party. She had many hats in her room. There were big hats, small hats, and even funny hats. At the party, Amy wore her favorite hat. It was a big, red hat. All her friends liked her hat. They played games and ate cake. They were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Amy's hat away! Amy was sad, but her friends were there to help. They looked everywhere for the hat. Then, they saw a bird in a tree. The bird was wearing Amy's hat! The bird liked the hat too. So, Amy let the bird keep the hat. She went home and chose a new hat to wear. Now, Amy and the bird were both happy with their hats.
