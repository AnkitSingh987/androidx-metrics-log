Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Phone

One day, a little girl named Mia found a rare toy phone in her room. She was very happy to play with it. She showed it to her mom, dad, and her baby brother, Tom. They all liked the rare toy phone. Mia played with the phone all day. She pretended to talk to her friends, her grandma, and even her dog, Max. Max looked at the phone and gave a big yawn. He was sleepy from playing all day. At night, Mia took the phone to bed with her. She yawned and hugged the phone tight. She knew she would have more fun with her rare toy phone the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a huge dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his bone. One sunny day, Buddy was playing in the mud with his bone. He got very dirty. Buddy's mom saw him and said, "Buddy, you need to wash your paws and your bone. They are very dirty." Buddy nodded and went to the water to wash. He washed his paws and his bone. When he was done, his mom said, "Good job, Buddy! Now you are clean and can play again." Buddy wagged his tail and ran to play with his clean bone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small hut with his mom and dad. One day, Tim felt very hungry. He told his mom, "Mom, I am hungry. Can I have some food, please?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. I will make you a sandwich. But you must hurry and eat it, we have to go to the park soon." Tim ate his sandwich very fast. He was excited to go to the park and play. When they got to the park, Tim saw a big, red ball. He ran to the ball and started to play. He was having so much fun. Suddenly, a little dog came running up to Tim. The dog looked hungry too. The dog grabbed Tim's ball and ran away with it. Tim was sad, but he had an idea. He told his mom, "Mom, let's give the dog some food. Maybe he will give my ball back." His mom agreed, and they gave the dog a sandwich. The dog ate the sandwich and gave the ball back to Tim. They all played together and became good friends. And from that day on, Tim always shared his food with his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a blueberry. This blueberry was very special because it was independent. It did not need help from anyone. It liked to do things on its own. One day, the blueberry decided it wanted to write. It picked up a pen and started to write. The blueberry wrote about its day, its friends, and all the fun things it did. The blueberry was very happy. It liked to write and be independent. Every day, the blueberry would write more and more. And that is the story of the independent blueberry who loved to write.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very independent boy. He liked to do things by himself. One day, he found a toy that could shoot. He was so excited to play with it. Tim took the toy outside and started to shoot at the sky. He made a wish while shooting. He wished for a new friend to play with. As he played, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was also independent and liked to play alone. Tim and Sue became friends and played with the toy together. They both made wishes and shot at the sky. They were happy to be independent but also have a friend to share their fun with. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a shiny arrow in his yard. He picked it up and showed it to his friend, Sally. "Look what I found!" Tim said. Sally looked at the arrow and said, "Wow, it's so shiny!" Tim and Sally decided to play with the arrow. They took turns throwing it in the air and watching it shine in the sun. Then, something unexpected happened. When Tim threw the arrow, it flew very far and landed in a big tree. They could not get it back. Tim and Sally were sad, but they had a reliable dog named Spot. Spot was good at getting things. They asked Spot, "Can you get our shiny arrow?" Spot barked and ran to the tree. He jumped high and got the arrow. Tim and Sally were so happy. They thanked Spot and continued to play with their shiny arrow.
