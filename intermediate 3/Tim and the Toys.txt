Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Toys

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to watch his favorite cartoon. He asked his mom, "Mom, can I watch my cartoon now?" His mom said, "Yes, but first you need to sort your toys." Tim didn't want to sort his toys. He thought it was a terrible job. He wanted to watch his cartoon right away. So, he made a plan to trick his mom. He hid his toys under his bed and told his mom he had sorted them. His mom believed him and let him watch his cartoon. While Tim was watching his cartoon, his mom went to his room to check. She saw the toys under his bed and was very sad. She turned off the TV and said, "Tim, you didn't sort your toys. You lied to me. Now you cannot watch your cartoon today." Tim felt bad and started to cry. The moral of the story is: always tell the truth and do your work before play.

A young boy was feeling helpless. He wanted to go somewhere special but did not know where to go. His father had an idea. He asked, "Why don't we visit the library?" The young boy was so excited! He had been to the library many times before with his father but this time was going to be extra special. They drove to the library and went inside. There were books everywhere! The young boy was so amazed by the rows and rows of books that he did not know what to do first. His father said, "Let's explore and find some interesting books." So they looked around the library and talked about the books they liked. After looking around for a while, they each found a book that they wanted to read. The young boy felt so happy. He thought to himself... visiting the library was a great idea!

One day, a boy named Tim found a modern bottle. The bottle was shiny and new. Tim was very happy to find the bottle. He showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a bottle!" said Tim. His mom smiled and said, "That's a nice bottle, Tim." Tim took the bottle to his room. He wanted to play with it. He put the bottle on the floor and watched it. Suddenly, the bottle started to grow! It began to increase in size. Tim was very surprised. "Wow!" he said. "The bottle is getting bigger!" Tim called his mom to see the big bottle. When his mom saw the bottle, she was amazed. "How did this happen, Tim?" she asked. Tim said, "I don't know, Mom. It just started to grow!" They both looked at the big, modern bottle in Tim's room, and they were very happy to have such a special bottle.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with puzzles. One day, he found a big puzzle with a picture of a fierce lion on it. Tim was very excited to play with the puzzle. Tim started to take the puzzle pieces and put them together. His friend, Sam, came to visit. Sam saw the puzzle and said, "Wow, that lion looks fierce! Can I help you?" Tim said, "Yes, please! Let's take the pieces and put the lion together." They worked and worked, and the lion started to look more and more fierce. Finally, Tim and Sam finished the puzzle. They were very proud of their fierce lion. They showed the puzzle to their moms and dads, who were also very proud. Tim and Sam played with the puzzle all day long, and they had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter. The hunter liked to find tasty food. One day, he went into the woods to look for something to eat. In the woods, the hunter met a big bear. The bear said, "What do you do here?" The hunter said, "I look for tasty food." The bear said, "I have tasty food. Come with me." The hunter went with the bear to a big cave. Inside the cave, there was a lot of tasty food. The hunter was very happy. But then, the bear closed the cave. The hunter could not get out. The hunter was sad because he could not go home.
