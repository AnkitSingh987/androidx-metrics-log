Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Sue and the Toys

Tom and Sue like to play in the kitchen. They open and close the cabinets and look for snacks. One day, they find a big jar of cookies in a high cabinet. They want to eat the cookies, but they are too small to reach the jar. "Let's get a chair and climb up," Tom says. He runs to the living room and gets a chair. He speeds back to the kitchen and puts the chair near the cabinet. Sue climbs on the chair and tries to grab the jar. "Be careful, Sue," Tom says. "The jar is heavy and the chair is wobbly." Sue does not listen. She pulls the jar with both hands and loses her balance. The jar falls on the floor and breaks. Cookies and glass fly everywhere. Sue falls off the chair and hits her head. She does not move or make a sound. Tom is scared. He thinks Sue is dead. He cries and shouts for help. Mom and Dad hear him and run to the kitchen. They see the mess and Sue on the floor. They are shocked and worried. "Tom, what happened?" Mom asks. "We wanted cookies," Tom says. "Sue fell and broke the jar. Is she dead?" "No, she is not dead," Dad says. He picks up Sue and checks her head. "She is just knocked out. We need to take her to the doctor. She might have a bump or a cut." Mom hugs Tom and tries to calm him down. She tells him that Sue will be okay and that they love him. She also tells him that he should not play with the cabinets or the jars. They are not toys. They are dangerous. Tom nods and says he is sorry. He hopes Sue will wake up soon and be his friend again. He also hopes there will be some cookies left for them.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to play in the grass. Tim was a stubborn boy who liked to joke. He would always try to make Spot laugh. They loved to run and play together. "Spot, why did the grass go to the doctor? Because it was feeling green!" Tim joked. Spot just looked at Tim and wagged his tail. They kept playing and having fun. As they played, they found a big hole in the grass. Tim was very surprised. "What is this?" he asked. Spot barked and jumped into the hole. Tim was scared, but he jumped in too. They landed in a fun world full of candy and toys. Tim and Spot played all day and had the best time. They never knew that there was a magical world under the grass.

Once upon a time, a rocket stood in the sky. It was thick, like a tree. The rocket was very still and no one moved around it. Everyone stared at the rocket in the same spot, day after day. One day, a small boy named Larry noticed something was different. Larry asked, "What's happening?" Everyone said nothing would happen, but Larry knew something was about to happen. Suddenly, the rocket started to move. It moved so fast that Larry could hardly keep his eyes on it. It went higher and higher until it was out of sight. Larry was amazed and excited. He couldn't wait to see what would happen next. The next day, Larry saw a spaceship land in the same spot where the rocket stood. He couldn't believe his eyes. People came out of the spaceship and everyone cheered! Larry was so happy that he wanted to jump with joy. He had been right - the rocket had taken people on an amazing journey.

Once upon a time, there was an ugly monster who wanted to scare everyone. He roared and stomped around, trying to make people afraid. One day, a brave knight came by and saw the monster. He wanted to protect everyone from the monster, so he reached down from his horse and picked up a little shield. He raised it up high and faced the monster. The monster was so scared that he ran away! Everyone was very thankful for the brave knight, and thanked him for protecting them. The knight smiled and waved goodbye as he rode away. Everyone was thankful that he had a shield!
