Category: Intermediate
Title: Sarah and the Mirror

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. She was so proud of herself and always wanted to be the best at everything she did. One day Sarah decided she wanted to style her hair. She got out a brush, a scrunchie, and some ribbons and happily sat down in front of a mirror. She started to brush her hair but when it was so sticky, she became frustrated and started to cry. Her mom came over to her and said, “Don’t worry, Sarah. Why don’t you try rubbing the brush instead?” So the little girl really tried hard and started to rub the brush through her hair. To her surprise, her hair started to look really nice. She smiled proudly and started braiding her hair with the ribbons and scrunchie. Although it was a lot of hard work, Sarah was so proud of herself for doing such a great job styling her hair. From then on, she always remembered to rub her brush before styling her hair.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play on the beach. One day, she found a big, pretty shell. It was a rich, shiny color. Lily was very happy. Lily saw a little crab that needed help. The crab was stuck in a hole. Lily used the big shell to dig the sand. The crab was free and he was happy. Lily and the crab became good friends. Every day, Lily and the crab played on the beach. They helped each other find more pretty shells. They had a lot of fun together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Tiny. Tiny loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, while playing with his friends, he found a toy sword. Tiny thought it was so much fun to play with the sword. Later that day, Tiny met another dog named Fluffy. Fluffy saw the sword and wanted to play with it too. But Tiny did not want to share. They started to quarrel about the toy sword. "It's mine!" said Tiny. "No, I want it!" said Fluffy. A wise old cat saw Tiny and Fluffy fighting. She came over and said, "You two should not quarrel. It's better to share and play together." Tiny and Fluffy thought about it and agreed. They decided to share the toy sword and became best friends. From that day on, they always played together and shared their toys.

Sam and Lily liked to play with boats. They had many boats of different colors and sizes. They took their boats to the harbor, where the big boats and the water were. They wanted to make their boats sail on the water. "Look, Lily, my boat is blue and fast. It can go far away," Sam said. He put his boat on the water and pushed it with his hand. "My boat is red and pretty. It has a flag and a sail. It can go wherever it wants," Lily said. She put her boat on the water and blew on the sail. Sam and Lily watched their boats move on the water. They were happy and excited. They did not see the big wave that came from the big boat. The big wave hit their boats and made them flip over and sink. "Sam, Lily, come here. Your boats are gone. The wave took them away," their mom said. She was on the shore and saw what happened. Sam and Lily ran to their mom. They were sad and angry. They wanted their boats back. "Mom, can we get our boats back? Can we go in the water and find them?" Sam asked. "No, Sam, you can't. The water is deep and cold and dangerous. You have to stay on the shore. Maybe your boats will come back later," their mom said. "But mom, we love our boats. We don't want to lose them. We want to play with them," Lily said. "I know, Lily, but you have to be patient. Sometimes things happen that we can't control. You have to ignore the bad things and think of the good things. You still have each other and me and more boats at home. Maybe we can make new boats or buy new boats. Would you like that?" their mom said. Sam and Lily nodded. They hugged their mom and each other. They were still sad, but they felt a little better. They decided to ignore the wave and the water and the harbor. They decided to think of their mom and their boats and their fun. They decided to be patient and hopeful. Maybe their boats would come back. Maybe they would get new boats. Maybe they would play with boats again.
