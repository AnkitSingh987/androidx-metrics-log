Category: Intermediate
Title: The Beautiful Crystal and the River

Once upon a time there was a beautiful crystal. It sparkled and glowed in the sun. Every day it would spend its time in the river, where it would splash around and have fun. One day there was a thunderstorm, and the crystal was scared. It hid in a deep cave and waited. But when it came out, it was so unhappy. It had become very spoiled. The crystal's friends saw how sad it was, so they decided to do something special. They brought out the biggest bucket they could find, and filled it up with cool, fresh river water. They made a special place for the crystal, and put it in the middle of the bucket. As soon as it felt the cool water, the crystal smiled and began to splash around again. It splashed so much that the water splashed out of the bucket and ran down the river bank. Suddenly, the crystal began to cry. Its tears sparkled in the sunlight, and all its friends knew that it was happy again.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, attractive rock in his yard. The rock was shiny and smooth. Tim liked the rock very much. He picked it up with his hand and took it home. In his room, Tim put the rock on his bed. At night, he heard a noise. It went "boo!" Tim was scared. He looked around and saw the rock. The rock was making the noise. It wanted to play. Tim was not scared anymore. He played with the rock. They had fun. They were friends. The rock did not scare Tim anymore. They were happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a red car. The car was very calm. It liked to keep going on the road. One day, the car went for a drive. As the car drove, it saw a big tree. The tree was in the way! The car did not know what to do. It tried to keep calm. The car thought and thought. Then, the car had an idea. It went around the tree! The car was happy. It could keep going. And the car stayed calm. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a famous park. In the park, there was a big bench. Every day, kids would come to the park to play and sit on the bench. One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw the big bench and wanted to sit on it. But when she got close, she saw that the bench was dirty. Someone had spilled juice on it and it was all sticky. The bench was spoiled. Amy was sad but she had an idea. She went home and asked her mom for a cloth and water. She came back to the park and cleaned the bench. Now, the bench was clean and not spoiled anymore. All the kids were happy and they played together on the famous bench.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Fin. Fin was a successful fish, and he had many friends. One day, Fin found a shiny scale on the ground. He thought it was very pretty, so he picked it up. Fin wanted to show his friends the shiny scale. He put it in a safe place, under a rock, and went to find his friends. When he came back with his friends, the shiny scale was gone! Fin was sad, and his friends helped him look for the scale. As they were looking, a small bird flew down from the sky. The bird had the shiny scale in its mouth! The bird had seen the scale and thought it was a pretty thing for its nest. But when the bird saw how sad Fin was, it gave the scale back to him. Fin and his friends were so happy, and they all lay down to rest. The bird became their new friend, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big hammer. He liked the hammer very much. He took it everywhere he went. Tim's mom told him, "Be alert and protect the hammer, it is very special." One morning, Tim saw a big, scary bug in his room. He knew he had to protect his toys from the bug. He remembered what his mom said about being alert and protecting the hammer. Tim picked up the hammer and said, "I will save my toys!" Tim hit the bug with the hammer. The bug went away, and his toys were safe. Tim was happy that he was alert and protected his hammer. His mom was proud of him too. From that day on, Tim knew he could always protect his toys with his special hammer.
