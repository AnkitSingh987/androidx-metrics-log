Category: Intermediate
Title: Jojo and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a brave giraffe called Jojo who was eager to go on a search. Jojo was feeling very proud, so she told her friends. “I’m going on a search. Who wants to join me?” The other animals were scared, so they decided to stay put. But Jojo was determined, so she set off alone. As she ventured deeper and deeper into the forest, she came across a big rock. Suddenly, a voice came from behind it. It was a squirrel, and he had an important question for Jojo. “Do you want to discuss something?” he said. Jojo thought for a moment, then replied, “Yes, I would like that.” So they began to discuss the best way to go on a search together. Eventually, the pair decided to work as a team. They set off, exploring and searching all around the forest. Jojo felt so proud when they eventually found what they were looking for. The pair became great friends, and Jojo knew she would always be proud of their search.

Once upon a time, there was a small bug. The bug loved to crawl on the ground. One day, while crawling, it found a shiny jewel. The bug was very happy and thought the jewel was beautiful. The bug wanted to show the powerful lion the jewel. So, the bug crawled to the lion. The lion was big and strong. The bug showed the lion the shiny jewel. The lion liked the jewel too. The bug and the lion became good friends. They played together every day. The bug would crawl around and the lion would watch. They were happy to have found the shiny jewel and to have each other as friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside, especially when it would rain. She would put on her rain boots and dance in the puddles. One day, it rained a lot. Sue was very happy. Sue's friend, Tom, came over to play. He saw Sue playing in the puddles and said, "Sue, look at all the rain! How can we measure how much rain there is?" Sue thought for a moment and said, "We can use a cup to catch the rain and see how full it gets!" They put a cup outside and watched as it filled with rain. After a while, they saw that the cup was full. "Wow!" said Tom, "That's a lot of rain! We are rich with rain today!" Sue agreed, and they continued to play in the puddles, happy with their rainy day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, angry lion. He was so angry he wanted to roar, so that's what he did. He roared really loud and it made everyone run away. The lion was still angry, so he started to walk down the street. Suddenly, he heard a crash and saw a little girl standing next to a broken glass. She was crying. The lion asked the little girl, “Why are you sad?” The little girl said, “My glass broke!” The lion then said, “Don't worry - I can help you.” The lion used his big paw and blew as hard as he could. Suddenly, the glass pieces jumped back together and the little girl was happy. The lion smiled and said, “Now let's make sure you don't break it again.” The little girl hugged the lion, and they both lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim found a fake square toy. It was red and soft. He liked it a lot. He played with it all day. He threw it, and it bounced back to him. It was so much fun. Then, Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue was sad. She did not have a toy to play with. Tim wanted to help Sue. He thought about the fun he had with the fake square toy. Tim went to Sue and said, "Here, you can have my toy." He gave the fake square toy to Sue. Sue was happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim!" Now, Tim and Sue played together. They threw the fake square toy to each other. They laughed and had a great time. They were happy to share the toy and be friends.
