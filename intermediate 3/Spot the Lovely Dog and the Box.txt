Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Lovely Dog and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a lovely dog named Spot. Spot liked to help his family prepare for the day. He would bring them their shoes and wag his tail. Spot learned many words from his family. One day, Spot saw a big box in the living room. He was very curious. He wanted to know what was in the box. He went to his family and barked the word "box". They laughed and told him to wait. The next day, the family opened the box. Spot was so excited! But when they opened it, there was another lovely dog inside! Her name was Daisy. Spot was surprised, but he was happy to have a new friend. They played together and helped the family prepare for the day. And they all lived happily ever after.

One sunny day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a pretty flower and wanted to show her mom. Mia pointed at the flower and said, "Look, Mom! Pretty flower!" As they looked at the pretty flower, Mia saw a tiny ant on the ground. The ant was carrying a small leaf on its back. Mia pointed at the ant and said, "Look, Mom! Tiny ant!" Mia's mom smiled and said, "Yes, that's a hardworking ant!" Mia watched the ant walk away with the leaf. She was happy to see the pretty flower and the tiny ant at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named True. True was restless and wanted to do something fun. She thought about what to do and had an idea. She decided to bake a cake for her family. True went to the kitchen and found all the things she needed. She mixed the cake stuff in a big bowl. She was excited and said, "This will be the best cake ever!" True put the cake in the oven and waited for it to bake. After a while, the cake was ready. True took it out of the oven and let it cool down. Her family came into the kitchen and saw the cake. They all said, "Wow, True! This looks yummy!" True was happy and proud of her cake. They all ate the cake together and had a great time.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. Tim was deaf, but he could still enjoy seeing all the animals. His mom encouraged him to look at all the different animals and learn their names. At the zoo, they saw a big lion. The lion roared loudly, but Tim could not hear it. His mom said, "That is a lion, Tim. It is very strong and has a loud roar." Tim smiled and nodded. He liked the lion a lot. After they saw many animals, they went to the gift shop. Tim saw a toy lion and wanted it. His mom bought it for him, and he was very happy. The trip to the zoo was a great day for Tim and his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot loved to wear sunglasses. One day, he went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining, and he looked very cool in his sunglasses. At the park, Spot met a new friend. The new friend was a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy wanted to welcome Spot to her park. She had never seen a dog wearing sunglasses before. They played together all day, having fun in the sun. But then, something unexpected happened. Spot's sunglasses fell off his face! Fluffy picked them up and put them on. Everyone was surprised to see a cat wearing sunglasses. Spot and Fluffy laughed and played together even more. They were happy to share the sunglasses and be best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old fox named Mr. Fox. He lived in the woods with his best friend, a small bird named Tweet. They loved to play games and have fun together. One day, Mr. Fox and Tweet found a big trap in the woods. They knew it was not safe. Mr. Fox said, "Tweet, we need to help our friends stay away from this trap. Let's make a sign to warn them." Tweet agreed, "Yes, Mr. Fox, that's a great idea!" Mr. Fox and Tweet went to find things to make the sign. They found some big paper and a black pen. Mr. Fox told Tweet, "I will print the words on the sign, and you can help me put it up." Mr. Fox printed the words "Stay away! Big trap!" on the paper. They worked together to put the sign near the trap. The next day, their friends in the woods saw the sign and stayed away from the trap. Everyone was safe and happy. Mr. Fox and Tweet were proud of their good work. They knew they had done something important to help their friends. And from that day on, all the animals in the woods knew that Mr. Fox and Tweet were wise and helpful friends.
