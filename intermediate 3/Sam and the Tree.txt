Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a kind squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to eat peanuts. One day, Sam found a big peanut under a tree. He was very happy. Sam wanted to eat the peanut, but he saw his friend, a little bird named Tim, who was hungry too. Sam thought it would be nice to share the peanut with Tim. So, Sam asked Tim to join him and eat the peanut together. They both enjoyed the peanut and were very happy. Sam and Tim learned that sharing with friends is a good thing to do. From that day on, they always shared their food and played together. And they lived happily in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was not very good at organizing his toys. One day, he made a big mess in his room. His mom was not happy. She said, "Tim, you need to organize your toys and clean your room!" Tim tried to organize his toys, but he felt stupid. He did not know where to put them. He started to cry. His big sister, Jane, heard him and came to help. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you organize your toys." With Jane's help, Tim organized his toys. They put the cars in one box and the dolls in another. After a year, Tim became very good at organizing his toys. His mom was happy, and Tim was proud of himself. He was not stupid anymore.

Once upon a time, two friends were eager to go on an adventure. They wanted to explore something new. They decided to go to the space. So they packed their things and off they went. Once they arrived, they saw a big hill. They decided to climb it. When they got to the top, they found a big hole. In the hole was a box. Inside the box were lots of special things. The two friends were so excited. They were eager to know what was inside the box. But before they could open it, they had to bury it somewhere. So they quickly found a nice safe place to bury the box. After it was buried, they each said a wish that the box will be safe. And with that, the two friends went back home, eager to go on their next adventure.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to go outside. He put on his hat, and he put on his shoes. Tim was ready to play in the sun. Tim saw a harmless bug on his skin. He said, "Hello, bug! Why are you on my skin?" The bug said, "I am just going for a walk." Tim smiled and let the bug go. Tim played all day with his ball. He had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said goodbye to the bug. He went inside and told his mom about the harmless bug on his skin.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to create a fence around his play area. He thought it would be fun to have a special place just for him and his toys. Tim found some sticks and started to build the fence. It was a little difficult, but he did not give up. He asked his mom for help, and together they made the fence strong and tall. Now, Tim and his toys have a safe and happy place to play behind the fence. They play there every day, and Tim is proud of the fence he created with his mom's help.

One day, a nosy little girl named Poppy went for a walk. She saw a cat with a big, red bow. The cat was trying to tie the bow, but it was not easy. Poppy said, "Mr. Cat, can I help you tie your bow?" The cat nodded and said, "Yes, please, Poppy. I want to look nice for my friend's party." Poppy tied the bow for the cat. The cat was very happy and said, "Thank you, Poppy! You are a good friend." They walked to the party together and had a fun time.
