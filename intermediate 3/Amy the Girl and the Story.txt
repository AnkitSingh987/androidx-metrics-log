Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a bad dream, called a nightmare. In the nightmare, there was a rude monster. The monster would always say mean things to Amy. She was very sad. One day, Amy met a nice cat. The cat asked, "Why are you sad?" Amy told the cat about the rude monster in her nightmare. The cat said, "I will help you. You need to reply to the monster and tell him to go away." That night, Amy had the nightmare again. The rude monster came and said mean things. This time, Amy was brave. She replied to the monster, "Go away! I don't like you!" But the monster did not go away. Instead, the monster got even bigger and scarier. Amy was very scared. The next day, Amy told the cat what happened. The cat was sad too. They learned that sometimes, even if you are brave and reply to the bad things, they might not go away. The moral of the story is that we can't always make the bad things go away, but we can try to be strong and face them.

One day, a dog named Spot wanted to go to the park. Spot put on his fake dog hat so he could play with his friends. He was very excited to see them. At the park, Spot saw his friend, Max. Max said, "Hi Spot! I like your hat. Can I try it on?" Spot said, "Sure, Max!" and gave him the hat. When Max put on the hat, something unexpected happened. Max turned into a big, blue ball! Spot was very surprised. He didn't know what to do. Then, he had an idea. Spot took off the hat from Max and he turned back into a dog. They both laughed and played in the park without the hat. They learned that sometimes, it's better to just be yourself.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a brave little bird. The bird lived in a pine tree. The bird loved to joke with his friends. One day, the bird met a big cat. The bird said, "Let's joke and play!" The cat smiled and said, "Okay, let's play." But the cat was not nice. The cat wanted to eat the brave little bird. The bird did not see the danger. They joked and played, and then the cat ate the bird. The end.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the river with her mom. She loved to play near the water. Lucy saw a naughty duck splashing in the river. The duck was having so much fun! Lucy said to her mom, "Can I play with the duck in the river?" Her mom said, "No, Lucy. The river is too big for you. You can play near the water, but not in it." Lucy really wanted to play with the duck. She felt an urge to go in the water. But she knew her mom was right. So, she played near the water and talked to the duck. The duck was happy to have a friend. They had a fun day by the river.

Once upon a time, there was a regular dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play with his ball. He had many friends in the park. One sunny day, Spot had a big job to complete. He had to find his ball that he lost. He looked under the tree, behind the big rock, and in the tall grass. Spot was sad because he could not find his ball. Then, a little bird saw Spot and wanted to help. The bird flew high up in the sky and saw the ball near the pond. Spot and the bird went to the pond and found the ball. Spot was so happy to complete his job, and he thanked the bird. From that day on, Spot and the bird were best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little chicken. This chicken was interesting because it could sign with its wings. It lived on a farm with other animals. One day, the chicken wanted to play with the other animals. It signed to the cow, the pig, and the sheep. But the other animals did not understand the chicken's signs. They just looked at the chicken and went away. The chicken felt sad and alone. It decided to leave the farm to find a friend who could understand its signs. It walked and walked but could not find anyone. The chicken became lost and could not find its way back home. The chicken was never seen again. The moral of the story is that we should be patient and try to understand others, even if they are different from us.
