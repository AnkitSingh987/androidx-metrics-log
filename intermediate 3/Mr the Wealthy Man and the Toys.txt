Category: Intermediate
Title: Mr the Wealthy Man and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Mr. Big. He had a big house with many toys. He liked to cover his toys with a soft blanket to keep them clean. One day, he met a little girl named Sue. Sue asked, "Can I touch your toys, please?" Mr. Big said, "Yes, but be gentle, and remember to cover them when you're done." Sue was very happy, and she played with the toys all day long. As the sun went down, Sue covered the toys with the soft blanket, just like Mr. Big asked. They became good friends, and Sue visited Mr. Big's house to play with the toys every day. They both had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big bedroom with lots of toys. Tim loved his bedroom very much. One day, Tim went to visit his grandma. He was happy to see her, but he started to miss his bedroom. Tim's friend, Sam, came to visit him at grandma's house. Sam told Tim about his new toy car. Tim felt envious because he wanted a new toy car too. He wished he could go back to his bedroom and play with his toys. At the end of the day, Tim's mom came to pick him up. Tim was happy to go home. When he got back to his bedroom, he saw a surprise. His mom had bought him a new toy car! Tim was no longer envious of Sam. He played with his new toy car and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big motor. It was a bad motor, but it could keep going and going. One day, the motor went out to play with its friends. While they were playing, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down and picked up the motor. The motor was so scared! The bird took the motor high up in the sky. But then, the bird saw that the motor was not a bad motor anymore. It was a good motor now. The bird was so happy! It took the motor back down to the ground. The motor and the bird became best friends and played together every day. And the motor kept being good forever.

Once upon a time, there was a small, weak bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree near a pretty lawn. Tim had many friends who played with him every day. One sunny day, Tim met a big cat. The cat said, "Hello, little bird. I am hungry. Can you help me find food?" Tim wanted to help, so he said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's find food together." They looked around the lawn for food to feed the big cat. After a while, they found some food. Tim was happy to help the big cat. But the big cat was not a nice friend. The cat said, "Thank you for the food, little bird. But I am still hungry." And then, the big cat ate Tim. The cat was full, but Tim was gone. Tim's friends missed him, and they were sad that he was gone.

Lily and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They had a big bowl, a spoon, some flour, and some water. They poured the water into the bowl and started to stir. "Look, Ben, we are making cake!" Lily said. "Yum, yum, cake!" Ben said. "Can we add some sugar?" Lily nodded. She reached for the sugar jar on the counter. But she was too small and she knocked over a candle. The candle fell on the floor and lit a napkin on fire. "Oh no, fire!" Lily screamed. "Fire, fire!" Ben echoed. They ran out of the kitchen and looked for their mom. She was in the living room, reading a book. She heard their cries and saw the smoke. She dropped the book and ran to the kitchen. She grabbed a wet towel and threw it on the fire. She put out the fire and turned off the stove. She hugged Lily and Ben and checked if they were hurt. They were not hurt, but they were scared and sorry. "I'm sorry, Mom, we were playing and we made a mess and a fire," Lily said. "I'm sorry, Mom, we were not careful and we were naughty," Ben said. Mom sighed. She was angry and relieved. She was angry because they could have burned the house and themselves. She was relieved because they were safe and the fire was small. She looked at the bowl of flour and water and the sugar jar. She looked at the book she was reading. It was a book about a wealthy fireman who saved people and animals from fires. She smiled a little. "It's okay, my loves, I'm glad you are okay. But you should never play with fire or candles. They are very dangerous. And you should always ask me before you use the kitchen. Do you understand?" she said. "Yes, Mom, we understand," Lily and Ben said. "Good. Now, how about we clean up this mess and then we read this book together? It's a story about a fireman. Do you like firemen?" she said. "Yes, Mom, we like firemen!" Lily and Ben said. They helped Mom clean up the kitchen and then they cuddled on the couch. Mom read them the story about the wealthy fireman who had a big truck and a cute dog. They liked the story and they learned a lot. They decided they wanted to be firemen when they grew up. But they also decided they would never play with fire again.
