Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a pair of scissors that belonged to her mom. Sally liked to use the scissors to cut pretty shapes out of thick paper. One day, Sally saw a big, thick tree. She thought, "I will cut a shape from the tree with my scissors!" But the scissors were too small for the thick tree. Sally felt sad. Sally's mom saw her and said, "Don't worry, Sally. The scissors belong in the house for cutting paper, not outside for cutting trees." Sally smiled and went back inside to cut more pretty shapes from thick paper.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man. He had a big zipper on his jacket. One day, he went for a walk in the park. He saw a little girl playing with her ball. The ball rolled to the bald man. The little girl asked, "Can I have my ball back?" The bald man did not permit her to have the ball. He said, "No, this is my ball now." The little girl was very sad. She went to her mom and told her what happened. Her mom was not happy. The bald man went home with the ball. He tried to play with it, but it was not fun. The zipper on his jacket broke. He was cold and sad. He wished he had given the ball back to the little girl.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot hurt his paw and needed a bandage. His mom put a perfect bandage on his paw. Spot felt better and went outside to play. While Spot was playing, a big bird came and tried to take his bandage. Spot did not want to lose his bandage, so he tried to escape. He ran very fast and hid behind a big tree. The big bird flew away, but then something unexpected happened. Spot saw that the big bird had dropped a beautiful toy. Spot picked up the toy and took it home. He showed his mom and they played together. Spot was very happy, and he knew that he was safe with his perfect bandage.

One sunny day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia loved the park because she could play on the swings and slides. Today, the park was very busy with many kids playing and laughing. Mia saw a little boy named Tom. Tom was eating a snack. Mia walked up to Tom and said, "Hi! I'm Mia. What are you eating?" Tom replied, "Hi Mia! I'm eating a cookie. Do you want to share?" Mia nodded her head and Tom broke the cookie in half. Mia took a bite and said, "Yum! Thank you, Tom!" After playing together, Mia and Tom got thirsty. Mia's mom had a surprise for them. She had brought a small picnic with tea and more cookies! They all sat down and had a fun tea time together. Mia and Tom laughed and talked while they enjoyed their tea and cookies. The busy park became a wonderful place for new friends to share a special moment.

Once upon a time, in a small town, winter came. Snow fell and it was very cold. A curious little girl named Lily wanted to play outside. She put on her coat, hat, and gloves. Lily loved to make snowmen and snow angels. Lily saw her friend Tom. Tom was sad because his hands were cold. Lily said, "Tom, you need gloves." Tom insisted, "No, I don't need gloves. I'm fine." Lily knew that Tom needed gloves to keep his hands warm. Lily went home and found an extra pair of gloves. She gave them to Tom. Tom put on the gloves and smiled. Now, they could play in the snow together. They made a big snowman and had so much fun.

John was very nervous. His parents told him they were going to send him to a fashion show. He had never been to one before. When he arrived he saw a lot of people wearing fancy clothes. He didn't look like them, his clothes were very plain. John went up to one of the fashion designers and asked for help. The designer gave him a special shirt and some shoes. But when it came time to pay, John didn't have enough money. He started to cry. The designer got angry. She shouted at John and said that he couldn't have the clothes. She told him to leave the show. John was so upset that he ran out of the building. John never returned to the fashion show. He was too scared. He was too nervous to ever go back.
