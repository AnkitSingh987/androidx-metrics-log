Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Dog

One day, a boy named Tim went on a tour with his mom. They wanted to see all the fun things in the town. Tim felt very safe with his mom. On the tour, they saw a big red bus. The bus had a number on it. The number was "5". Tim said, "Mom, look! The bus has a number 5!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. That's a big red bus with a number 5." As they walked, they saw a big dog. The dog looked mad. Tim's mom said, "Stay close, Tim. We need to be safe." But Tim did not listen. He went to pet the dog. The dog bit Tim's hand. Tim cried and his mom was sad. The tour was not fun anymore.

One day, an old man was walking around the park. He saw a little girl sitting on a swing, picking the flowers that were growing in the grass. The old man was not pleased and he said to her, "That's against the law!". He pointed to the sign that said "Do not pick flowers." The little girl didn't understand the old man. She looked up at him with big blue eyes and said, "What does law mean?". The old man tried to explain, but the little girl didn't understand. The old man was getting angry now. He wanted her to understand that picking the flowers was wrong. But then he remembered his granddaughter and how she liked to pick wildflowers from the garden too. He softened his face and said, "It's ok little girl, but it's against the law." The little girl nodded. She thanked the old man for understanding and carefully put the flowers back into the ground. She made sure she didn't pick any more flowers and ran off to play. The old man smiled to himself and said, "That's better." He watched the little girl run around in the park and he was glad he could help.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to fly high in the sky. One day, while Tim was flying, he saw something yummy on the ground. It was a waffle! Tim was very hungry, so he flew down to eat it. As Tim started to eat the waffle, he met a big, harsh bear named Bob. Bob said, "That's my waffle!" Tim was scared but said, "Can we share the waffle? I am very hungry." Bob thought about it and said, "Okay, we can share." So, Tim and Bob ate the waffle together. They became good friends and played all day long. From that day on, they always shared their food and had many fun adventures together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and look for treasure. One day, Spot met a big cat named Whiskers. Whiskers said, "I know where to find treasure, but you must yield and let me lead the way." Spot wanted the treasure, so he let Whiskers lead the way. They walked and walked until they saw a big tree. Whiskers said, "The treasure is here!" Spot was very happy and started to dig under the tree. But as Spot dug, the tree started to shake. The tree fell down with a loud crash, and Spot was very sad. He did not find the treasure, and the big tree was gone. Spot learned that he should not yield to someone he just met, even if they seem friendly.

Once there was a boy named Jack. He was very curious and wanted to explore the world. One day, he decided to go for a walk. Everywhere he went, he noticed lots of weird things. He saw a bird with three legs and a tree that was purple! As Jack was walking, he saw a stand. He had never seen anything like it before. He walked up to the stand and saw that it was filled with strange foods. He reached out to grab something, but all of a sudden, the stand disappeared! Jack was scared and didn't know what to do. Then, he heard a voice that said, "Jack, the stand will end when you are ready to go home." Jack thought this was very weird, but he was relieved that the stand wasn't scary after all. So, he said goodbye to the stand and went on his way. Jack never forgot his weird and wonderful adventure. He learned that the world is a strange place, but sometimes it's filled with exciting and unexpected surprises.
