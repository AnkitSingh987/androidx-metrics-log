Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Lily

Once upon a time, there was a small, peaceful town. In this town, there was a nice shelter where animals lived. The shelter was a happy place for them. Many kids and their families would come to visit and play with the animals. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the shelter with her mom. Lily loved animals and wanted to bring one home. She saw a cute dog and said, "Mom, I admire this dog so much! Can we take him home?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can adopt him." Lily was very happy. She named the dog Buddy. They played together every day and had a lot of fun. Buddy was so glad to have a new home, and Lily was so glad to have a new friend. The town stayed peaceful, and everyone lived happily ever after.

---

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play together. One day, they visit the park with their moms. The park has a big flag. The flag is red, white and blue. It has stars and stripes. "Look, Ben, the flag is flying!" Anna says. "Yes, it is. Do you know what the flag means?" Ben asks. Anna shakes her head. She does not know. "The flag is a sign of our country. Our country is called America. We live in America. The flag shows that we are loyal to America. Loyal means we love and respect our country," Ben explains. Anna nods. She thinks that is nice. "Can we be loyal to other things too?" she wonders. "Sure, we can. We can be loyal to our families, our friends, our pets, our toys, anything we care about," Ben says. Anna smiles. She has an idea. "Let's make our own flags. We can draw what we are loyal to on them. Then we can wave them in the park," she suggests. Ben likes the idea. He agrees. They find some paper and crayons. They draw their flags. Anna draws her mom, her dad, her baby brother, her dog and her doll. Ben draws his mom, his dad, his sister, his cat and his bike. They are proud of their flags. They run to the park. They wave their flags. They shout, "We are loyal!" The moms see them. They laugh. They hug them. They are happy. The end.

Once upon a time, in a friendly forest, there lived a small rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to play games with his friends. One day, Remy and his friends decided to play a new game called "Find the Leaf." In this game, one friend hid a big leaf and the others had to find it. Remy was very good at finding things, but sometimes he got lost. This time, Remy tried very hard to find the leaf but he could not. He felt sad. Remy's friends saw that he was sad and came to help him. Together, they looked for the leaf and found it! Remy was so happy that he hugged all his friends. They all learned that when they help each other, they can succeed. The moral of the story is: working together makes everything easier and more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had a terrible itch on his back. He tried to scratch it, but he could not reach it. Max felt sad and did not know what to do. He just gave a little shrug. One day, Max saw a cat playing with a long cut string. Max had an idea! He asked the cat, "Can you help me with my itch?" The cat said, "Yes, I can help you." So, the cat used the cut string to scratch Max's back. Max felt so happy and the terrible itch was gone! Max and the cat became good friends. They played together every day. Max never had to shrug about his itch again. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim found a fancy arrow in his yard. He showed it to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "I recommend we find out where it came from!" They went to ask their wise friend, Mr. Owl. Mr. Owl said, "This arrow belongs to the king. You should take it back to him." Tim and Sam went to the king's big house. When they got there, the king was sad. He said, "I lost my fancy arrow. I can't find it anywhere." Tim showed the king the arrow he found. The king was happy to see his arrow. But then, something unexpected happened. The king said, "This is not my arrow. My arrow has a red tip. This one has a blue tip." Tim and Sam were confused. They didn't know where the fancy arrow came from. They decided to keep looking for the owner of the arrow. And the king kept looking for his red-tipped arrow too.
