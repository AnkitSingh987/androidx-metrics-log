Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Max. Max was always hungry. One day, he went for a walk with his friend, a big dog named Sam. They walked and played in the park. Max saw a pretty collar on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Sam, can I borrow this collar?" Sam said, "Yes, you can borrow it, but we need to find who it belongs to." They looked for the owner of the collar. They asked a cat, a bird, and a squirrel. Finally, they found a sad little dog named Lucy. She was the owner of the collar. Max gave the collar back to Lucy. She was very happy and said, "Thank you, Max and Sam!" Max and Sam were very proud. They went home and had a big meal because they were very hungry. They had a good sleep and had fun dreams about their adventure in the park.

Anna and Ben are brother and sister. They like to play outside when the sun is shining. They have a big ball, a red bike and a yellow kite. They share their toys and have fun. One day, the sky is gray and it starts to rain. Anna and Ben are sad. They cannot play outside. They have to stay inside with Mom and Dad. They look out the window and see the raindrops falling. Mom says, "Don't be sad, my loves. The rain is good for the flowers and the trees. And soon the sun will shine again and make a rainbow in the sky." Dad says, "Why don't you play with your books and your puzzles? You can learn new words and colors and shapes. And you can make up stories and draw pictures." Anna and Ben nod. They go to their room and find their books and puzzles. They sit on the floor and start to play. They read and talk and laugh. They learn and create and imagine. They are glad. They have each other. They are brother and sister. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with dough. She would make funny shapes, like a cat or a car. One day, Lucy saw her mom with a small box. It was a record box. Lucy was very curious. Lucy asked her mom, "What is that?" Her mom said, "This is a record box. It can save your voice when you talk." Lucy thought it was fun. She wanted to try it with her dough. So, she put the dough near the record box and said, "Hello, dough!" Later, Lucy's mom went to the store. Lucy was upset because she wanted to go too. She had an idea. She took the record box and hid it under her bed. She thought her mom would take her to the store if the record box was gone. But when her mom came back, she was very upset. She looked everywhere for the record box. Lucy felt bad and told her mom, "I hid the record box under my bed." Her mom was not happy. She said, "Lucy, that was not nice. Now you cannot play with the record box or the dough for a week." Lucy was very sad. She learned that hiding things from others can make them upset. She promised never to do it again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was always worried. He worried about everything. One day, Tim saw his dad reading a newspaper. Tim didn't know what a newspaper was, so he felt ignorant. Tim asked his dad, "Dad, what is that?" His dad smiled and said, "This is a newspaper, Tim. It tells us what is happening in the world. But don't worry about it, you are too young to understand." Tim felt better after talking to his dad. He learned that it was okay not to know everything. The next day, Tim saw a girl who didn't know how to tie her shoes. He remembered what his dad said and told the girl, "Don't worry, we can learn together." So, Tim and the girl learned how to tie their shoes. They were happy and stopped worrying. They knew that it was okay to ask for help and learn new things. The moral of the story is: Don't worry if you don't know something, just ask and learn.
