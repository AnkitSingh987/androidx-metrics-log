Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mighty Lion and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a mighty lion. It liked to have tea with his friend the rabbit. But one day, the lion started to worry. He couldn't find his cup of tea anywhere. "Oh no!", he said. He looked for his cup everywhere. He asked the rabbit for help. The rabbit hopped around, searching high and low. Finally, he found a cup of tea under a rock. The lion was so happy he roared with joy! He picked up the cup and enjoyed the tea with his friend. From then on, the lion never worried about his tea again.

---

One day, a smart cat named Tom went to the station. He wanted to get on a train to go see his friend. At the station, he saw a big red button. It said "push" on it. Tom was curious and wanted to push the button. As Tom was about to push the button, a small dog named Tim saw him. Tim said, "No, Tom! Don't push the button! It will make the train stop and everyone will be late." Tom didn't listen and pushed the button anyway. The train stopped and everyone was sad and angry. Tom felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I should have listened to you." Tim forgave Tom and they both helped to fix the train. The train started moving again and everyone was happy. Tom learned to listen to his friends and not to do things without thinking about what could happen.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Max. Sue loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, she got very filthy from playing all day. Sue wanted to go inside her house to eat lunch. But when she tried to open the door, she noticed the knob was gone! Sue was very sad and scared. She did not know how she would get inside her house without the knob. Then, Max the dog came to help. Max had taken the knob to play with it. He thought it was a toy! Max brought the knob back to Sue, and she put it back on the door. Sue was so happy and hugged Max. They went inside to eat lunch together, and Sue learned to always keep an eye on Max when he played with new toys.

---

One day, a big boy named Tom found a hammer. He liked the hammer very much. Tom did not want to share the hammer with his friends. He was being selfish. Tom's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the hammer and said, "Can I play with the hammer too?" Tom said, "No, it is mine. You can't play with it." Sam was sad and sat on the ground. A wise old man saw them. He said, "Tom, sharing is good. If you share, you will have more fun." Tom thought about it and decided to share the hammer with Sam. They both played and had lots of fun together. The moral of the story is that sharing is good and being selfish is not nice.

One day, a little girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw her shadow on the ground and said, "Hi, shadow! Do you want to play with me?" The shadow did not say anything, but it followed her everywhere she went. Lily was happy to have her own shadow as a friend. They played all day, but when the sun started to go down, Lily's shadow got longer and longer. Lily felt sad and asked, "Shadow, why are you getting so big?" The shadow still did not say anything. Lily started to cry because she thought her shadow was leaving her. Just then, a little boy named Tom came to play with Lily. He saw her crying and asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily told him about her shadow getting big and she thought it was leaving her. Tom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Lily. It's just the sun going down. Your shadow will be back to play with you tomorrow." Lily stopped crying and played with Tom until it was time to go home. She knew her shadow would be back to play with her again the next day.
