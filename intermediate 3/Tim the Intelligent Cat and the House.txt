Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Intelligent Cat and the House

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent cat named Tim. He lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Lily. One day, they saw a lot of dust in their house. Lily said, "Tim, we need to remove the dust. It is not good for us." Tim thought for a moment and said, "You are right, Lily. Let's clean the house together." So, they started to clean the house. They removed the dust from the floor, the tables, and the chairs. The house looked nice and clean. Later that day, Lily's mom came home and saw the clean house. She was very happy and said, "Well done, Lily and Tim! You both did a great job!" Lily and Tim learned that working together and being intelligent can make things better. And they lived happily ever after.

One sunny day, a red ball and a bubble went to play in the park. The red ball loved to bounce high, and the bubble liked to float in the sky. They were very good friends. "Let's play a game," said the red ball to the bubble. "I will try to resist bouncing, and you try to resist floating. Whoever does it longer wins!" The bubble agreed, and they started the game. The red ball tried very hard to resist bouncing, but it was so hard for him. The bubble tried not to float, but the wind blew, and it went up in the sky. The red ball and the bubble laughed together because they could not resist doing what they loved. In the end, they decided to play together, bouncing and floating, enjoying the sunny day at the park. And they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, ugly toy. Tim did not like the toy because it was ugly. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim took his ugly toy with him. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to see if his ugly toy could fly. Tim threw the toy up in the air. The ugly toy did not fly. The toy started to fall from the tree. Just then, a girl named Sue saw the ugly toy falling. Sue caught the toy before it hit the ground. Sue smiled at Tim and said, "I like your toy!" Tim smiled back at Sue. Tim and Sue played with the ugly toy and became good friends. From that day on, Tim liked his ugly toy because it helped him find a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved motorcycles. One day, he saw a big, high hill. He wanted to take his toy motorcycle to the top. Tim started to walk up the hill. It was a long way to the top. When he got there, he was very tired. But he was also happy. He put his toy motorcycle down and looked at the view. Suddenly, a big wind came. It took Tim's motorcycle and blew it high into the sky! Tim was sad, but then something unexpected happened. A big, friendly bird caught the motorcycle in its beak and brought it back to Tim. He was so happy! Now, Tim and the bird were friends, and they played with the motorcycle together on the hill.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to eat sausages for breakfast. One morning, their mom cooked some sausages and put them on a plate. She told them to share the sausages and be modest. Tom and Lily each took a sausage and started to eat. But Tom wanted more. He saw that there was one sausage left on the plate. He reached for it and said, "I want this sausage. It is mine." Lily looked at him and said, "No, Tom. That is not fair. You already have one sausage. You should share with me. Mom said to be modest." Tom did not listen to Lily. He grabbed the sausage and put it in his mouth. He thought he was clever and happy. But he was wrong. The sausage was too hot for him. He burned his tongue and his lips. He spat out the sausage and cried. He felt pain and regret. Lily saw that Tom was hurt. She felt sorry for him. She took the sausage and broke it in half. She gave one half to Tom and said, "Here, Tom. You can have this half. I will have the other half. We can share and be modest." Tom took the half sausage and said, "Thank you, Lily. You are kind and generous. I am sorry for being greedy and rude. I learned my lesson. I will share and be modest from now on." Mom heard their words and smiled. She hugged them and said, "I am proud of you, Tom and Lily. You are good twins. You know how to answer and how to be modest. You also know how to eat sausages. They are tasty, but you have to be careful. They can be hot and hurt you." Tom and Lily nodded and said, "Yes, mom. We understand. We love you and we love sausages." They finished their breakfast and went to play. They were happy and wise.
