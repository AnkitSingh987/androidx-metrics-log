Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Blanket

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very happy because she was going on vacation with her family. They were going to the beach. Sue liked the beach because it was easy to play in the sand and water. On their way, Sue's mom said, "We need to get some food for our trip." They stopped at a little store. Sue's dad bought some apples, bananas, and sandwiches. Sue said, "Thank you, Dad!" At the beach, Sue made a big sandcastle with her brother. They played in the water too. Sue's mom and dad sat on a blanket and watched them. They all had a fun day at the beach. At the end of the day, Sue said, "Thank you for the fun vacation!"

Once upon a time, there was a big hospital. It was very empty. No one was inside. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the hospital with her mom. They wanted to make the hospital full of friends. Lily had a magic wand. She would wave it, and something fun would happen. She waved the wand at the hospital. Suddenly, lots of toys and balloons appeared. The empty hospital was now full of colors and fun. Lily and her mom went inside the hospital. They saw many kids playing and laughing. Everyone was happy. Lily waved her magic wand one more time. A big cake appeared, and they all had a big party. The empty hospital was now a happy place for everyone.

Ben likes to play with his ball. He likes to roll it on the floor and catch it. He likes to roll it to his mom and dad and make them smile. He likes to roll it to his dog and make him chase it. One day, Ben rolls his ball too hard. It goes past his mom and dad and past his dog. It goes into the kitchen and hits the dishwasher. The dishwasher makes a loud noise and starts to shake. Ben hears water and soap coming out of the dishwasher. He sees bubbles and foam on the floor. Ben is nervous. He thinks he broke the dishwasher. He thinks his mom and dad will be angry. He thinks his dog will slip on the bubbles and foam. He runs to the kitchen and tries to stop the dishwasher. He pushes the buttons and pulls the door. He cries and says sorry. His mom and dad hear the noise and come to the kitchen. They see Ben and the dishwasher and the bubbles and foam. They are not angry. They are surprised and worried. They hug Ben and tell him it is okay. They turn off the water and unplug the dishwasher. They call someone to fix it. They clean up the bubbles and foam with towels and a mop. They dry the floor and the dishes. They play with Ben and his ball. They tell him to be careful next time. They tell him they love him. Ben is happy. He says he loves them too. He says he is sorry for the dishwasher. He says he will roll his ball softly. He rolls his ball to his dog and makes him chase it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to balance on one foot. One day, Amy found a thick envelope on the ground. She picked it up and looked inside. Amy saw a picture of a cat in the envelope. She wanted to show her friend, Tim. She ran to Tim's house and knocked on the door. Tim opened the door and said, "Hi, Amy! What do you have?" Amy showed Tim the thick envelope and the picture of the cat. "Look what I found, Tim! A cat picture in this envelope!" Tim looked at the picture and smiled. "Wow, that's a nice cat!" Amy and Tim played together, taking turns to balance on one foot while holding the envelope. They laughed and had lots of fun with the cat picture. And that's how Amy and Tim spent their day, sharing their special find with each other.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing. They found a big bowl. The cat said, "I want to turn the bowl." The dog said, "No, I want to turn the bowl." They both wanted to turn the bowl, but they could not agree. The cat and the dog saw a long stick. They thought the long stick could help them turn the bowl. They both pulled the stick to turn the bowl. But the stick broke, and they still could not turn the bowl. Suddenly, a big bird came and took the bowl away. The cat and the dog were sad. They should have worked together to turn the bowl. Now, they had no bowl to play with.
