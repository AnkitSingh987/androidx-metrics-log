Category: Intermediate
Title: Mom and Dad and the Spoiled

One day, Mom and Dad took their spoiled 3 year old to the furniture store. He had never been before, and he was so excited! He ran around, pointing and giggling at every big and little thing he saw. He climbed on the chairs, and stuck his head under the tables, and examined the lamps and the rugs. Finally, Mom and Dad helped him pick out a new bed for his room. He was so excited about it that he partied all the way home! He danced and sang and hugged his parents. That night, his new bed was set up and he had a big happy sleep in it. From then on, he was never spoiled at the furniture store again!

Once upon a time, there was a big, expensive castle. In the castle, there was a king and a queen. They had a little boy named Jack. Jack liked to play with his toys and use his toy shovel to dig in the sand. One day, Jack went outside to play. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she did not have any toys. Jack wanted to help Sue, so he said, "Do you want to play with me?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, please!" So, Jack and Sue played together. They used Jack's toy shovel to build a sand castle. They had a lot of fun and became good friends. From that day on, Jack and Sue played together every day. They learned that sharing and helping others can make them happy. And the big, expensive castle was filled with joy and laughter.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little girl named Lily. She had a pretty red scarf that she loved to wear. One day, she decided to go outside and play in the snow. Before she went out, she asked her mom to help her wrap the scarf around her neck. Lily's mom wrapped the scarf snug and warm. Now, Lily was ready to play! Lily had so much fun in the snow. She made a snowman and gave him her red scarf to wear. Then, she went inside to have hot cocoa. The snowman looked very tidy with Lily's scarf, and they both had a great day.

Sara and Ben are playing in the garden. They have a big box of cards with pictures of animals on them. They want to match the cards that have the same animal. "Look, I have a card with a bee!" Sara says, holding up a yellow and black card. "Me too!" Ben says, showing his card. "Bees are adorable. They make honey and buzz around the flowers." They put their cards together and smile. They have made a match. "Let's find more matches!" Sara says, picking another card. "What animal is this?" She shows Ben a card with a brown and white animal with horns and a long tail. "That is a goat," Ben says. "Goats are funny. They eat grass and make noises like 'baa'." "Do you have a goat card?" Sara asks. Ben looks through his cards. He finds one with a goat, but it is different from Sara's. His goat is black and white, and has a beard. "No, I don't have a match," he says. "My goat is not the same as your goat." Sara and Ben look at their cards. They see that some animals have different colors or shapes, even if they are the same kind of animal. "That's okay," Sara says. "We can still learn about the animals. Maybe we can find other ways to match them." "Like what?" Ben asks. "Like, we can match them by their sounds, or their homes, or their food," Sara says. "That sounds fun!" Ben says. "Let's try it!" They keep playing with their cards, matching the animals by different things. They learn a lot and have a lot of fun. They love playing with their cards and with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny who loved to hop. One day, while hopping in the forest, he found a big red ball. The ball was incredible! It was shiny and bright. The little bunny wanted to play with the ball, so he gave it a big push. The ball started to roll and it went faster and faster. But suddenly, the ball hit a rock and made a loud noise. "Pop!" The ball was not a ball, but a big bag of gas! The gas made a funny sound as it came out of the bag. The bunny was scared at first, but then he started to laugh. He hopped all the way home, excited to tell his friends about the incredible gas ball he found in the forest. And from that day on, the little bunny and his friends loved to play with the gas ball, and they all had lots of fun.
