Category: Intermediate
Title: John and the Dress

John was three years old and he always liked to get dressed up in the morning. Today he was excited because he was going to wear his favourite green dress. He grabbed it from the row of clothes and jumped up and down on the spot. "How are you feeling today John?" said his mum, who was also getting dressed in the room. "I'm so excited to wear my green dress," said John with a smile. John's mum gave him a hug and smiled. Then, all of a sudden, his nosy little sister came into the room. "Can I wear the green dress too?" she asked eagerly. John and his mum laughed. "No, it's my special dress," said John. His sister stuck out her tongue, but then ran off to find something else to wear. John then finished getting dressed and went off to school. He was always so happy when he was wearing his favourite green dress.

Once upon a time, there was a black pick. It was a special pick and one day, a little girl wanted to peek at it. She snuck over to where the pick was and carefully peeped inside. In the pick she could see sparkles and glitter. The little girl couldn't believe her eyes! She quickly put her hand into the pick and pulled out a big blue ball. She was so excited and couldn't wait to show it to her friends! She ran away with it, playing with it for the rest of the day. Then the little girl carefully placed the black pick back in its spot. The end.

One day, a grumpy dog named Max went for a jog. Max liked to jog in the park. He saw a big tree and a small screw on the ground. Max picked up the screw with his mouth. He jogged to his friend, a happy cat named Lily. Max showed Lily the screw. Lily and Max played with the screw. They were not grumpy anymore. They had fun and went home. Max and Lily liked to jog and find things together.

Once upon a time, there was a rare goose. This goose was very special. It could vanish and then come back. All the other geese were amazed. One day, a big mean bird came to the pond. The bird wanted to take the rare goose away. The other geese were scared. They did not want their friend to go. The rare goose knew what to do. It vanished! The big mean bird was confused. It looked around, but could not find the rare goose. The mean bird flew away. The rare goose came back, and all the geese were happy. They knew their friend was safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tyler. He had just turned three and was very curious about the world. He liked to explore and go on adventures. One day, Tyler saw a cabinet that was in the corner of his grandma's house. He was very curious about what was inside, so he just had to take a look. He opened the drawer and had a peek. "What do you see in there?" his grandma asked. "I think it's a bed," Tyler guessed. "Yes, that's right. It is indeed a bed," his grandma said, with a smile. Tyler kept looking around, until he noticed something in the corner. It looked really filthy. "Grandma, what is that?" Tyler asked, pointing to the corner. His grandma looked and said, "I think it's a mouse!" Tyler was so surprised he just had to take a closer look. Sure enough, it was a mouse. He couldn't believe it. He had guessed right.

One day, a big hairy dog named Max went for a walk. He wore a tie around his neck. Max liked to look nice. He saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked Max. Tim said, "I have a big exam at school today, but I don't know how to tie my shoes." Max wanted to help his new friend. Max had an idea. He used his tie to show Tim how to make a knot. Tim tried it on his shoes. It worked! Tim was happy and hugged Max. "Thank you, Max!" he said. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took away Tim's exam papers. Max ran fast and caught the papers with his teeth. Tim was so happy that Max saved his exam. From that day, Max and Tim were best friends. They played together and helped each other. They knew that they could do anything when they were together.
