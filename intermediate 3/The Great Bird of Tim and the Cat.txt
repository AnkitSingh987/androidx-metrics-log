Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Bird of Tim and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a gray cat named Fluffy. They liked to play together in the yard. One day, while they were playing, Tim and Fluffy heard a noise. They stopped and listened. It was a small bird, singing in a tree. Tim and Fluffy went to see the bird. The bird was happy to meet them. They played together all day, and then went home. Tim, Fluffy, and the bird were now best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had soft fur. He liked to run and play all day. One day, Spot saw a big tree. He saw a dead branch on the tree. Spot did not know it was dead. He thought it was a good place to hide his toy. Spot ran to the tree. He put his toy on the dead branch. But the branch fell down! Spot was scared. Spot looked at the branch. He saw that it was dead. He learned not to put his toys on dead branches. From that day, Spot played safe and had lots of fun.

---

One day, a pale cat named Lily wanted to invite her friends to her house. She wanted them to see the pretty view from her big window. Lily told her friends, "Come to my house, I have a surprise for you!" Her friends were very excited. The next day, Lily's friends came to her house. They played games and had fun. Lily said, "Wait, I have a surprise for you all!" She took them to the big window. They saw a beautiful view of the sun going down. The sky was full of colors. Lily's friends were very happy. They thanked her for inviting them to her house. The beautiful view was a great surprise. They all went home with big smiles on their faces. Lily was very happy too, because she could share the pretty view with her friends.

---

Lily loved penguins. She had many penguin toys and books. She wanted to see real penguins. Her mom said they could go to the zoo. Lily was very happy. At the zoo, Lily saw many animals. She saw lions, monkeys, elephants, and giraffes. But she only wanted to see penguins. She asked her mom to take her to the penguin house. Her mom said yes. They walked to the penguin house. It was cold and dark inside. Lily saw many penguins swimming and sliding on the ice. She wanted to touch them. She saw a sign that said "Do not feed or touch the penguins". She did not read the sign. She did not know what it meant. She saw a penguin that was bigger and blacker than the others. It had a yellow beak and yellow eyes. It looked scary. Lily thought it was the boss of the penguins. She wanted to be friends with it. She chose to go closer to it. She reached out her hand to touch it. The penguin did not like Lily. It did not want to be touched. It opened its beak and bit Lily's hand. Lily screamed. Her mom ran to her and pulled her away. The penguin hissed and flapped its wings. Lily was hurt and scared. She cried. Her mom hugged her and said sorry. She said Lily should not touch the penguins. She said they were wild animals and could be dangerous. Lily learned her lesson. She said sorry to the penguin and to her mom. She said she would never touch the penguins again. She said she still loved penguins, but only from far away.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a gloomy day, but Tim didn't care. He loved playing on the swings and slides. As they walked to the park, Tim's mom gave him a piece of chocolate. He was very happy and ate it right away. At the park, Tim saw a little girl named Sue. She was sitting alone and looked sad. Tim went up to Sue and asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I lost my chocolate and now I have none." Tim felt bad for her and decided to help her find her chocolate. They looked all around the park, but they couldn't find the chocolate. Tim didn't want to disturb his mom, so he didn't tell her about Sue's lost chocolate. Instead, he had an idea. Tim took out another piece of chocolate from his pocket and gave it to Sue. She was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They played together on the swings and slides until it was time to go home. Even though it was a gloomy day, Tim and Sue had a lot of fun. They became good friends and promised to play together again soon.
