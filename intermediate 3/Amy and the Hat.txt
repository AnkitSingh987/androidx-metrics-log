Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy and the Hat

Once there was a little girl, who was only three years old. Her name was Amy. She loved exploring the world. One day, while she was out exploring, she saw something very interesting. It was a tall, fancy hat, with a big feather sticking out of the top. Amy was very curious, so she went up to the hat and asked her mother about it. "Mommy, what is this hat?" she asked. Her mother smiled and explained. "This is a fancy hat, Amy. It's called a top hat and people wear it when they want to look special." Amy was surprised, but before she could say anything else, her mother said, "Let's try it on, shall we?" Amy giggled with delight as her mother placed the hat on her head. She jumped around and laughed as the feather tickled her nose. Her mother smiled and said, "You look very fancy with your top hat, Amy! Now, let's go and show everyone your new look!"

One day, a gifted boy named Tom went for a walk. He liked to find new things. Tom saw a big tree with a red ball on the left side. He wanted to play with the ball. Tom picked up the ball and saw a little girl. The little girl looked sad. Tom asked her, "Is this your ball?" The girl said, "Yes, it is. I lost it when I was playing." Tom gave the ball back to the girl. She was very happy. She said, "Thank you for finding my ball." Tom and the girl played with the ball for a long time. They had fun and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo. He lived in a pond with his friends. The hippo was worried. He wanted to protect his friends from danger. One day, a mean crocodile came near the pond. The hippo knew that crocodile was not nice. The hippo wanted to keep his friends safe. So, he talked to the crocodile. He asked the crocodile to leave and not hurt anyone. The crocodile listened to the hippo. The crocodile left the pond and did not come back. The hippo's friends were very happy. They thanked the hippo for protecting them. The moral of the story is to always help and protect your friends when they need you. Friends are important, and we should take care of them.

One day, a smooth cat named Kitty and a big dog named Doggo went for a walk. They saw a horn on the ground. Kitty said, "What is this?" Doggo said, "It is a horn. Let's keep it!" They picked up the horn and walked to the park. They met a small bird named Birdy. Birdy said, "Hello! What do you have?" Kitty said, "We found a horn. We will keep it." Birdy wanted to play with the horn too. So, Kitty, Doggo, and Birdy played with the horn all day. They had a lot of fun. They were very happy to keep the horn and play together.

Once upon a time, there was a magic cat named Fluffy. Fluffy could remove things with a wave of his tail. He lived in a big house with a little girl named Sue. Sue loved Fluffy very much. One day, Sue felt helpless because she lost her toy. She looked everywhere but could not find it. She was sad and asked Fluffy for help. Fluffy wanted to help his friend Sue. Fluffy used his magic tail and removed all the things in the room. He found the toy under the bed. Sue was happy and hugged Fluffy. They played together all day. Sue was not helpless anymore because she had her magic friend, Fluffy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog with soft fur. The dog liked to play with his friend, a small bird. They lived in a big yard with a tree. The tree was their safe place to play. One day, the bird said, "Let's play a game. I will fold my wings and you fold your fur. Then we can hide from each other." The dog wagged his tail and said, "Yes, let's play!" They took turns hiding and finding each other. The dog folded his fur and hid under the tree. The bird folded her wings and hid in the grass. They laughed and played until the sun went down. Then, they went back to their safe tree, happy and tired.
