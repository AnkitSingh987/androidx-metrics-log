Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Helpful Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a helpful bird named Bob. Bob lived in a big tree with his friends. One day, Bob found a long wire in the tree. He did not know what it was for, but he thought it was fun to play with. Bob and his friends liked to jump on the wire and clap their wings. They played on the wire all day and had lots of fun. But one day, something unexpected happened. When Bob jumped on the wire, it moved and made a loud noise. The noise was so loud that all the birds flew away in fear. But then, they saw a big door open in the tree. Inside the door, there were many toys and treats for the birds. The wire was a secret door opener! Bob and his friends clapped their wings in joy and played with their new toys all day long.

Once there were two friends, Jack and Jilly. They were very excited because they were going to the restaurant for lunch. As they entered the restaurant, Jack asked Jilly, "What do you want to eat?" "I want a perfect hamburger," Jilly replied. So Jack tied his shoelaces and then went up to the counter to order two perfect hamburgers. He brought them back to the table and showed them to Jilly. Jilly was so excited she wanted to tie her own laces so she could eat her hamburger like Jack. Jack showed Jilly the special way he ties his laces and she tried to copy him. After several attempts, she learned to do it perfectly! Jack and Jilly enjoyed their perfect hamburgers together and had a very happy lunch.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. He lived in a big pond with many other fish. One day, a new fish came to the pond. His name was Rude Ray. Rude Ray was not nice to the other fish. He would swim fast and splash water on them. The other fish did not like Rude Ray. They wanted him to be nice. One day, Fin had an idea. He made a big sign that said, "Be nice, Rude Ray." Fin showed the sign to all the other fish. They all liked it and clapped their fins. Fin swam to Rude Ray and showed him the sign. Rude Ray looked at the sign and said, "I am sorry, I will be nice." From that day on, Rude Ray was nice to all the other fish, and they all lived happily in the big pond.

One day, a dog and a cat saw a big tree. The tree had a ladder on it. The dog said, "I want to climb the ladder!" The cat said, "No, I want to climb the ladder!" They started to fight. They did not see the bird on the tree. The bird watched the dog and the cat fight. The bird said, "Stop fighting! I have an easy way to help you both climb the ladder." The dog and the cat stopped fighting. They looked at the bird and said, "What is the easy way?" The bird said, "You can both climb the ladder, one after the other." The dog and the cat thought this was a good idea. They stopped fighting and took turns climbing the ladder. When they reached the top, they found a big, yummy cake. They shared the cake and became friends. The bird smiled, happy to see them getting along.

Once upon a time, there was a little green frog named Bob. Bob lived near a small bridge over a tiny stream. He loved to jump and play with his friends all day long. One day, Bob saw a big red ball near the bridge. He wanted to play with the ball, but he was too scared to cross the bridge. His friends laughed at him, and Bob felt embarrassed. Bob blinked his big eyes and thought, "I can do it. I will cross the bridge and play with the ball." So, Bob took a deep breath and hopped onto the bridge. He went slowly and carefully. When he reached the other side, he felt so happy and proud of himself. Bob played with the big red ball and had lots of fun. His friends saw how brave he was and stopped laughing at him. They all crossed the bridge and played together. The moral of the story is that you should never be afraid to try new things, even if they seem scary at first.
