Category: Intermediate
Title: People and the Festival

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, colorful festival. People were very happy. They loved to go to the festival every year. One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to travel to the festival. She asked her mom, "Can we go to the festival today?" Her mom said, "Yes, but first we must do our work." Lucy and her mom worked hard all day. They knew the festival would be fun. When they finished their work, they got ready to go. At the colorful festival, Lucy and her mom had a great time. They played games, ate yummy food, and saw many pretty things. They were so happy they went to the festival after working hard. And they knew they would come back next year for more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a regular toy, a microscope. He loved to look at small things with it. One day, Tim took his microscope to the park. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue wanted to play with Tim's microscope. But when Sue tried to take it, she made Tim slip and fall. Tim was sad and angry. He did not want to share his microscope with Sue. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the microscope up into the sky. Tim and Sue were both very sad. They decided to work together to find the microscope. They looked all around the park, and finally found it in a tree. Tim and Sue became friends and shared the microscope, playing together happily.

---

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. They see many flowers in the park. Lily likes the yellow ones. Tom likes the red ones. They want to pick some flowers for their moms. They run to the flowers. They pick some with their hands. They are careful not to hurt the flowers. They put the flowers in their pockets. They are happy. They have great flowers for their moms. They go to the swings. They swing high and low. They laugh and sing. They see a big bee. The bee is looking for flowers. The bee sees the flowers in Tom's pocket. The bee flies to Tom. Tom is scared. He does not like bees. Lily sees the bee. She wants to help Tom. She picks up a stick. She swings the stick at the bee. She hits the bee. The bee falls to the ground. Tom is happy. Lily is brave. She saved him from the bee. He gives her a hug. He says thank you. He gives her a flower from his pocket. She gives him a flower from her pocket. They put the flowers on their heads. They look funny. They laugh and smile. They have great friends. They have great fun. They have great flowers. They have great moms. They go home. They give the flowers to their moms. Their moms are happy. They hug them and kiss them. They say they love them. They have great kids. They have a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a tall dancer. She loved to dance all day. She danced in the sun and danced in the rain. Everyone liked to watch her dance. One day, a little car came to the town. It could zoom very fast. The car saw the tall dancer and wanted to dance too. So, the car zoomed around the dancer as she danced. The tall dancer and the car became good friends. They danced and zoomed together every day. The people in the town loved to watch them. And they all lived happily ever after.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy food for dinner. Tim's mom got a cart to put the food in. The cart was normal, with four wheels and a place to put the food. Tim saw his friend, Sally, in the store. Sally said, "Hi, Tim! My mom and I are buying food too." Tim said, "Hi, Sally! What are you getting?" Sally said, "We got apples and bread. What do you recommend we get next?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "I like bananas. You should get bananas too!" Tim and Sally's moms finished shopping and they all went home. Tim and his mom made dinner with the food from the store. Tim was happy he could help Sally by recommending bananas. He knew it was a normal day, but it was still fun to see his friend and help her.
