Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Healthy Boy and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a healthy boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. Tom's mom would always make sure he ate good food to stay strong. One sunny day, Tom's mom said, "Tom, I need you to send a letter to Grandma. Can you do that for me?" Tom nodded and took the letter from his mom. He put on his shoes and went outside. As Tom walked to Grandma's house, he saw his friends playing. "Hey Tom, come play with us!" they shouted. Tom smiled and said, "I have to send this letter to Grandma first, then I can play." So, Tom kept walking until he reached Grandma's house. Grandma was so happy to get the letter and gave Tom a big hug. After that, Tom went back to play with his friends, feeling proud of his helpful task.

Lily and Ben were twins who loved to sing and dance. They wanted to perform in front of many people and make them happy. One day, their mom and dad took them to a big hotel where there was a talent show for kids. Lily and Ben were very excited and practiced their song and dance in their room. Before the show, they met a girl named Mia who was also going to perform. Mia was very nice and friendly and said she liked their song and dance. She asked if they could be friends and play together. Lily and Ben said yes and followed Mia to the playroom. There, they saw many toys and games and had fun with Mia. But then, they heard an announcement that the talent show was about to start. Lily and Ben wanted to go back to their room and get ready, but Mia said they still had time and asked them to stay and play. Lily and Ben felt torn. They liked Mia, but they also wanted to perform. They looked at each other and remembered their promise to their mom and dad to do their best. They told Mia they had to go and thanked her for being nice. Mia looked sad and said they were leaving her alone. She tried to make them feel guilty and said they were not loyal friends. Lily and Ben felt sorry for Mia, but they knew they had to do the right thing. They said goodbye and ran to their room. They made it just in time and put on their costumes and grabbed their microphones. They went on stage and sang and danced with all their heart. The audience clapped and cheered and smiled. Lily and Ben felt happy and proud. They saw their mom and dad in the front row and waved at them. Their mom and dad waved back and said they loved them. After the show, they met Mia again. She said she was sorry for being mean and asked if they could still be friends. Lily and Ben said yes and hugged her. They said they understood she was lonely and scared, but they also had to keep their promise. They said they could still play and have fun, but they also had to respect each other's dreams. Mia nodded and smiled. They all became loyal friends and learned to support each other.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who played a guitar. She got very angry when something bad happened. One day she dropped her guitar and it broke. She was so mad that she started to cry. But then she stopped and thought. Even if something bad happened, she could still forgive. With a big hug, she hugged her guitar and forgave it - even though it was broken. Her friends were so amazed that she could forgive something bad. They asked her how she could do that. "Well," she said, "it's just that forgiving can make things feel better and it's the nice thing to do." Since then, the little girl was always ready to forgive. Even when things were bad, she knew that forgiving made things feel better. She felt happy and free when she forgave.

One day, a big van came to the town. It was red and shiny. A man got out of the van. He had a weak dog with him. The dog was sad and tired. The man said, "I need help. Can anyone keep my dog for a day? I will come back tomorrow." A little girl named Amy heard the man. She wanted to help the weak dog. Amy took the dog home. She gave it food and water. The dog was happy. They played all day. The next day, the man came back. He said, "Thank you, Amy, for keeping my dog." The weak dog was now strong and happy.
