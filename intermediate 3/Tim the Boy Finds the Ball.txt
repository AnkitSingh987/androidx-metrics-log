Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Ball

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a big ball stuck in a tree. He wanted to get the ball down, but his arms were not strong. Tim felt sad because he needed more muscle to reach the ball. He tried to jump high, but his legs felt tight. "I wonder how I can get the ball down," Tim thought. He looked around the park and saw a girl named Sue. Sue was tall and strong. Tim asked Sue for help. Sue said, "I can help you!" She walked to the tree and reached up. With her strong arms, she got the ball down. Tim was so happy! He thanked Sue and they played with the ball together. They became good friends and had lots of fun at the park.

One day, a girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a big box on the step of her house. It was a big surprise! She wanted to know what was inside. She asked her mom, "Can I open it?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can." Lucy opened the box and revealed a very modern toy. It was a robot! She was so happy. She played with it all day. But then, her friend Tom came over. Tom wanted to play with the robot too. Lucy did not want to share. They both got very sad. Lucy's mom saw them and said, "You both can play with the robot together." Lucy and Tom shared the robot and played all day. They learned that sharing is fun and they became best friends.

Lily liked to listen to the radio. She liked the songs and the stories. She wanted to be on the radio too. She had a clever idea. She asked her mom for a record. A record was a round thing that made sounds when you put it on a machine. Mom gave Lily a record. It was an old one that she did not want anymore. Lily took the record and a pen. She made marks on the record with the pen. She made a face, a star, and her name. She was very proud of her record. She went to the machine that played the records. She put her record on it. She turned it on. She waited for her sounds to come out. But nothing happened. The record only made a scratchy noise. Lily was sad. She did not understand why her record did not work. Mom came and saw what Lily had done. She explained to Lily that the pen had ruined the record. The record had tiny grooves that made the sounds. The pen had made the grooves go away. Lily felt sorry. She did not mean to break the record. Mom hugged Lily and said it was okay. She said she had another clever idea. She took Lily to her room. She showed her a toy that looked like a radio. It had a button that said record. Mom said Lily could use the toy to make her own sounds. She could sing, tell stories, or make noises. She could press the button and the toy would save her sounds. Then she could play them back and listen to them. Lily was happy. She thanked Mom and tried the toy. She had fun making her own radio show. She felt like a star.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in the kitchen. She saw her mother cooking something on the stove. Lily was curious and asked, "Mother, what are you making?" Mother smiled and said, "I am making a yummy cake for us to eat. But be careful, the stove is hot and it can burn you." Lily wanted to help her mother, so she tried to put some of the cake mix into the bowl. But oh no! Lily dropped the whole box of mix into the bowl. The cake mix went everywhere! The cake was spoiled. Mother gasped and looked at the mess. She thought for a moment, and then she laughed. "Well, Lily, we made a mess, but we can clean it up and try again. This time, we can make an even better cake together." Lily and her mother cleaned the kitchen and made a new cake. It was the best cake they ever had. And Lily learned to be more careful in the kitchen.
