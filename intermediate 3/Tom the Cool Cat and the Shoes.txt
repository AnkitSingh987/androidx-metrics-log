Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cool Cat and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a cool cat named Tom. Tom loved to wear his red shoes every day. He would walk around the town, showing off his shoes to everyone he met. One day, Tom met a pretty cat named Sue. Sue also had beautiful shoes. Tom thought Sue was very nice. They liked to play together and show each other their shoes. They became best friends. After some time, Tom and Sue decided to marry. They had a big party with all their friends. Everyone wore their best shoes to celebrate. Tom and Sue were so happy to be together, and they lived happily ever after with their cool shoes.

Once upon a time, there was a big dark room. In the room, there was a box. In the box, there was a pretty wrap. The wrap was red and blue. One day, a girl named Sue went into the dark room. She saw the box and said, "What is in this box?" She opened the box and found the pretty wrap. Sue took the wrap and put it on. Sue's friend, Tim, came into the room. He saw Sue in the pretty wrap and said, "You look nice!" Sue said, "Thank you, Tim! I found this wrap in the box." Tim looked in the box and saw a bottle. The bottle had wine in it. Tim said, "We can have a party with this wine!" Sue and Tim took the wrap and the wine. They left the dark room and had a fun party with their friends. Everyone loved the pretty wrap and the wine. They all had a great time.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a blue object in her toy box. She did not know what it was. She picked it up and showed it to her mom. "Mom, what is this?" she asked. Her mom looked at the object and smiled. "That is a stick to stir, Lily. It is used to mix things together," her mom said. Lily wanted to try it out. She went to the kitchen and found a big bowl. She put some water and food colors in the bowl. She used the blue stick to stir the water. As she stirred, something unexpected happened. The water started to bubble and change colors. Lily was surprised and called her mom. "Mom, the water is changing!" she said. Her mom came to see and laughed. "Lily, you found a magic stick! When you stir with it, it makes things change colors!" They both laughed and played with the magic stick, stirring and watching the colors change.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a bush. The bush had many yummy berries. All the animals loved the bush because it would provide them with food. One day, a selfish bird came to the bush. The bird wanted all the berries for itself. It did not want to share with the other animals. So, the bird tried to eat all the berries very fast. The other animals saw the selfish bird and became sad. They wanted to eat the berries too. They all came together and talked to the bird. They told the bird that sharing is good and being selfish is not nice. The bird understood and started to share the berries with everyone. From that day on, all the animals in the forest were happy and shared with each other.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a dirty figure in her room. She wanted to make it clean, so she asked her mom for help. "Mom, can you help me clean this figure?" she said. Her mom looked at it and said, "Sure, let's clean it together." They went to the sink and started to wash the figure. But, Lily's brother Sam saw them and wanted to play with the figure too. "I want to play with it!" he said. Lily didn't want to share and said, "No, I found it first!" This made Sam sad and he started to cry. Mom knew what to do. She said, "Why don't we all play with the figure together after it's clean? We can have a great time!" Lily and Sam liked this idea. They cleaned the figure and then played with it all day. They had so much fun and were happy to share.
