Category: Intermediate
Title: Jane the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She loved to play with her toys and her dog, Max. One day, Jane found her mom's perfume on the table. She thought it would be fun to put the perfume on Max. Jane sprayed the perfume on Max, and Max did not like it. He ran around the house, trying to get away from the smell. Jane felt regret for putting the perfume on Max. She knew it was not a good idea. She found a towel and tried to rub the perfume off Max. Max was happy when the smell was gone. Jane promised she would not put perfume on Max again. She learned her lesson and played with Max and her toys, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play in her garden. One day, she found an empty pot. She had an idea! "I will plant a seed and grow a beautiful flower," she thought. Lily asked her mom, "Please, can I have some earth for my pot?" Her mom said, "Yes, of course!" So, Lily filled the empty pot with earth. Then, she put a seed in the earth and watered it every day. Soon, a little green plant started to grow. Lily was so happy! She took care of the plant and watched it grow bigger and bigger. One day, a pretty flower bloomed. Lily smiled and thanked the earth for helping her grow her beautiful flower.

Once there was a big dog named Max who lived in a big country. Max had a little brother named Sam. Max was jealous of Sam because Sam got more treats from their mom. Max wanted to be the best dog in the family. One day, Max thought of a plan to make Sam look bad. Max took a toy from Sam's room and hid it in the yard. Max told their mom that Sam lost the toy. Mom was sad and said she would punish Sam. But, Sam knew where Max hid the toy. Sam went to the yard and found the toy. Sam showed the toy to their mom. Mom saw that Max was trying to trick Sam, so she punished Max instead. Max learned that being jealous and mean was not good. He said sorry to Sam and they played together happily.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with many beds. The beds had soft mattresses on them. The room was very organized. In this room, two friends, Tom and Sue, lived together. One day, Tom and Sue were talking. Tom said, "I like the blue mattress best." But Sue said, "No, I like the red mattress best." They did not agree. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the mattresses away. Tom and Sue were sad. They looked at each other and said, "Now we have no mattresses to disagree about." But then, they saw a big, new mattress. It was both blue and red. They were happy and agreed that they both liked this new mattress. Tom and Sue learned that it was better to share and agree than to disagree.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy bell. On Tuesday, Tim was ready to play with his friends. He took his toy bell with him. Tim and his friends played a game. They took turns to ring the bell. When the bell rang, they would run and hide. The one who rang the bell had to find the others. Tim rang the bell and his friends ran to hide. But then, something unexpected happened. The bell would not stop ringing! Tim and his friends were scared. They all came out of their hiding spots and helped Tim. They learned that if they work together, they can fix anything. The moral of the story is that teamwork makes things better.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy rock. The rock liked to sit by the window and watch the birds fly by. One day, a little bird flew up to the window and saw the rock. "Hello, rock!" said the little bird. "Why do you sit by the window all day?" The rock replied, "I like to watch the birds and feel the sun on me. It makes me happy." The little bird thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea! I can help you see more of the world. I can push you around, and we can have fun together." The rock liked the idea, so the little bird started to push the heavy rock. It was hard work, but the little bird was strong. Together, they rocked and rolled all around the yard. They saw the trees, the flowers, and the other animals playing. The rock and the little bird became best friends, and they had many fun days together in the sun.
