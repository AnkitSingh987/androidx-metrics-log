Category: Intermediate
Title: The Temple and the Spicy

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a big temple. The temple was very pretty. All the people in the village loved the temple. One day, a little boy named Tim found a spicy pepper. He wanted to share it with his friends. But his friends did not like spicy food. Tim was sad. He thought, "What should I do with this spicy pepper?" Tim had an idea. He decided to bury the spicy pepper near the temple. He dug a small hole and put the pepper in it. Then, he covered the hole with dirt. After some days, a beautiful plant grew in that place. The plant had more spicy peppers. The people in the village saw the plant and were happy. They learned that even if they did not like something, it could still be good for others. The village started to grow more spicy pepper plants, and they shared them with other villages. Everyone learned to share and care for each other. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tim and Sam were playing with their toys in the backyard. They had a lot of machines, like cars, planes, and robots. They liked to make them zoom and crash and fight. "Look at my robot, it is so cool!" Tim said. "It can shoot lasers and fly and smash your car!" "No way, my car is cooler!" Sam said. "It can go fast and jump and dodge your lasers!" They made their machines battle each other, making loud noises and pretending to be hurt. They had fun, but they also wanted to win. "Ha, I got you, your robot is broken!" Tim said, grabbing Sam's robot. "Hey, give it back, that's not fair!" Sam said, pulling Tim's car. They started to fight, pulling and pushing and yelling. They were not having fun anymore, they were angry and sad. Their mom heard them and came to the backyard. She saw them fighting and was not happy. "Boys, what are you doing?" she asked. "Why are you fighting over your toys?" "They are mine, he took them!" Tim said. "No, they are mine, he broke them!" Sam said. Mom shook her head. "You are both wrong. You are not sharing and being kind. You are hurting each other and your toys. You need to stop and say sorry." Tim and Sam looked at each other. They knew mom was right. They felt bad for fighting. They did not want to lose their toys or their friend. They let go of their machines and said sorry. "I'm sorry, Tim, I should not have taken your robot." Sam said. "I'm sorry, Sam, I should not have broken your car." Tim said. They hugged and smiled. They gave each other their machines back. "Can we play together again?" Tim asked. "Sure, let's play together." Sam said. They picked up their machines and played nicely. They made them zoom and crash and fight, but they also helped and shared and laughed. They had more fun than before. Mom watched them and smiled. She was proud of them. She said, "That's better, boys. You learned a good lesson today. Fighting is not cool, but surrendering and making up is."

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had a big cart that they could fill with their dolls, cars, books and blocks. Sometimes they would display their toys on the floor and pretend they were in a shop or a museum. One day, they decided to display their toys outside in the garden. They pushed their cart along the path and found a nice spot under a tree. They took out their toys and arranged them on the grass. They made signs with crayons and paper to show what their toys were. "Look, this is my dinosaur. He is big and green and has sharp teeth," Tom said, holding up a plastic toy. "And this is my princess. She is pretty and pink and has a crown," Lily said, showing a doll. They invited their mom and dad to come and see their display. Mom and dad were very proud of their twins and praised their creativity and imagination. "Wow, what a wonderful display! You have so many different toys and you made them look so nice," mom said. "Yes, you are very clever and talented. Can we buy some of your toys?" dad asked, playing along. Tom and Lily smiled and nodded. They pretended to sell their toys to their parents, who gave them hugs and kisses as payment. They had a lot of fun playing with their display, until they heard a loud bark. It was their dog, Spot, who had been sleeping in his kennel. He was restless and wanted to join the game. He ran towards the cart and jumped on it, making it tip over. The toys fell out and scattered on the ground. Spot wagged his tail and licked the twins' faces. "Spot, no! You messed up our display!" Tom and Lily cried. "Bad dog! Go away!" they shouted. But Spot did not understand. He thought they were playing with him. He picked up a toy in his mouth and ran away, hoping they would chase him. Tom and Lily were angry and sad. They had worked hard on their display and now it was ruined. They looked at their mom and dad, who tried to comfort them. "It's okay, sweeties. Spot didn't mean to spoil your display. He just wanted to play with you. He loves you very much," mom said. "Maybe you can make another display later, or share some of your toys with Spot. He likes to play with you, too," dad said. Tom and Lily thought about it. They loved Spot, too, even when he was naughty. They decided to forgive him and play with him. They picked up their toys and put them back in the cart. They called Spot and threw him a ball. He came running and dropped the toy he had taken. They all laughed and played together in the garden. They were happy again.
