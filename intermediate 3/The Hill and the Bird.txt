Title: The Hill and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. On the hill lived a bird who loved to sing. The bird had a weird song. It sang every day. One day, a cat heard the bird's song. The cat wanted to catch the bird. It climbed the hill and found the bird. The bird saw the cat and stopped singing. The cat got close to the bird, but then something unexpected happened. The cat started to sing the bird's weird song too! They both sang together on the hill. From that day on, the cat and the bird were friends and sang their weird song together.

Once upon a time there was a girl. She was very small and her name was Mary. Mary loved to play with her toys. One day, Mary wanted a structure to play with. She asked her mum, "Mummy, can I have a structure?" Mum smiled and said, "Yes, you can have a structure. But first you need to go outside to find one." Mary smiled and ran off outside. She found a pale structure in her backyard and she was so excited. She quickly ran back inside and showed Mum the structure. Mum smiled and said, "That's a pale structure! You can keep it and play with it." Mary was so happy. She said "thank you" to Mum and ran off to play with her structure. She was so happy that she had a new structure to play with and she wanted to play with it all day. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big round ball. It was very thick and strong. The ball liked to roll around and play with its friends in the park. One day, the ball met a new friend. The friend was a long and thin ruler. The ruler could measure things. The ball wanted to learn how to measure too. The ruler taught the ball to measure. They measured a lot of things together. They measured a big tree, a small rock, and even a thick book. The ball was very happy to learn something new. The ball and the ruler became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big guard dog named Max. He was very strong and always kept the house safe. Max loved to bark and play with his little friend, a boy named Tim. They would run and jump together in the yard. One day, Tim saw a terrible bug. It was big and scary. Tim was afraid of the bug, so he asked Max to help him. Max barked at the bug to make it go away. But the bug did not go away. Instead, the bug started to increase and grow bigger and bigger. Max tried to guard Tim from the big terrible bug, but the bug was too strong. It pushed Max away and went after Tim. Tim ran away as fast as he could, but the bug caught him. The bug took Tim away and Max could not save him. And so, the story had a bad ending, with Tim and Max never playing together again.
