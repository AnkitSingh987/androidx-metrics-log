Category: Intermediate
Title: John and the Beautiful

John was feeling very glad. It was a beautiful day outside, and he loved feeling the warm sunshine on his face. He was also very excited, because he had a special surprise. He had been planning it for weeks, and it was time to try it out. John mixed together pieces of paper, crayons and glue. He wanted to make something special. Soon, he had it. It was the most beautiful art project he had ever seen. He couldn't wait to show it to his friends. But then the clouds moved in, and it began to rain. John was so sad. He could feel the rain washing away his beautiful creation, and he knew it was ruined. He sighed and went inside, no longer feeling so glad. John's special surprise had been ruined. It was a hard lesson to learn, but it had been foreshadowed by the darkening clouds. From now on he would be more careful when planning his art projects, and hopefully his next surprise will have a better ending.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a toy screen. He loved to play with it all day. One day, he went to the park with his mom. It was a quiet, sunny day. Tim took his toy screen with him. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. She was playing with a ball. Sue said, "Hi, Tim! Want to play with my ball?" Tim said, "Yes, let's play!" So, they played together. Tim put his toy screen down on the grass. While they were playing, Tim kicked the ball too hard. It went up high in the sky. Sue and Tim looked up and saw the ball drop. The ball landed on Tim's toy screen with a big thud. The screen broke into pieces. Tim was sad. He said, "My screen is broken. What do I do now?" Sue felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I will help you fix it." They tried to put the pieces back together, but it was too hard. Tim's mom came over and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will get you a new screen." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, mom."

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and have tea parties with her dolls. One sunny day, she set up a tea party in her room. As she poured tea into the tiny cups, she noticed her teddy bear, Mr. Snuggles, was not at the party. Sue looked around her room and found Mr. Snuggles sitting on her bed. She picked him up and said, "Mr. Snuggles, it's time for our tea party!" Sue placed Mr. Snuggles next to her other dolls at the tea party. She poured tea for everyone and they all had a fun time. Sue loved her tea parties with her dolls and Mr. Snuggles. And when the sun went down, Sue hugged her toys and went to bed, excited for the next day's tea party.

John was a brave little boy. He was out for a walk with his mom, when they came across a big building. John stared in amazement. "What is that, Mommy?" He asked. "That's a factory", his mom replied. John couldn't take his eyes off the towering building. He wondered what was inside. "Can we go inside?" he asked. "No, it's not safe for little boys like you," his mom said. "But you can look from the outside." John looked up in awe, and wondered what the factory made. He imagined the brave robots at work inside. John smiled and said, "Let's go home now. I feel brave just looking at the factory." His mom smiled back and said, "Yes, let's go home. You are very brave, John!"

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fancy party. All the people in the town went to the party. They wore pretty clothes and ate yummy food. At the party, there was a big chair. It was the best seat in the town. A little girl named Lily went to the party with her mom and dad. She saw the big chair and asked her mom, "Can I sit in the big seat?" Her mom said, "Yes, but only for a little bit." Lily sat in the big seat and felt very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The big seat started to move! It was not just a seat, but also a magic ride. It took Lily high in the sky, above the town. She saw her house, the park, and the school from the sky. Lily was scared at first, but then she loved the magic ride. When it was time to go back, the magic seat took her to the party. She told her mom and dad about the ride. They were surprised but happy that Lily had fun. From that day on, the people in the town knew about the magic seat. It became their favorite thing in the town. And everyone lived happily ever after.
