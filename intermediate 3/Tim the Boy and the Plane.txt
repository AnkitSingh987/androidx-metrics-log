Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Plane

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch planes fly in the sky. One day, he saw a big plane flying very high. He wanted to ride on the plane. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can I ride on the plane?" His mom said, "I recommend we go to the airport and see if we can ride on a plane." Tim was very happy. At the airport, Tim and his mom found a nice man who worked with the planes. The man said, "Yes, you can ride on the plane today!" Tim and his mom got on the plane and had a fun time. They saw the clouds and the birds from the sky. Tim was very happy that he got to ride on the plane.

One day, a little cat named Kitty went for a walk. She saw a big, ugly bug. The bug said, "Hi, Kitty! Let's be friends!" Kitty was scared, but she said, "Okay, let's be friends." Kitty and the bug walked until they found a big open space. They sat down to relax. The bug said, "I like to play in the open space. What do you like to do, Kitty?" Kitty said, "I like to play too!" They played and laughed together in the open space. Soon, Kitty was not scared of the ugly bug anymore. They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a boy named Tom was in his room. He wanted to add stickers to his ceiling. He thought it would be fun to look at them when he went to bed. His mom said, "Tom, you can't reach the ceiling. You are too small." Tom felt sorry that he couldn't do it. He asked his big sister, Lily, to help him. Lily said, "Okay, Tom. But you have to give me one of your toys." Tom agreed, and they began to add stickers to the ceiling. As they were adding stickers, they heard a noise. The noise was coming from the closet. Tom and Lily opened the closet, and a big pile of toys fell out. The toys were hiding in the closet! Now, Tom didn't have to give Lily one of his toys. They were both happy and kept adding stickers to the ceiling.

One day, a big dog named Max was ready to hear a story. Max sat down next to his friend, a little cat named Lily. Lily opened the book and started to read the story out loud. In the story, there was a funny bunny who loved to sneeze. Every time the bunny would sneeze, all the other animals would laugh. The bunny would say, "I can't help it! I just have to sneeze!" The animals would laugh more and say, "Silly bunny, always sneezing!" One day, the bunny sneezed so hard that something unexpected happened. The sneeze was so big that it blew all the leaves off the trees! The other animals were surprised and stopped laughing. They said, "Wow, bunny, that was a big sneeze! We didn't know you could do that!" Max and Lily looked at each other and laughed. They liked the story's twist and how the bunny's sneeze was so big. Max said, "I want to sneeze like the bunny!" Lily giggled and said, "Maybe one day, Max, but for now let's just enjoy the story."

Jill likes to eat celery. She likes the green and the crunch. She likes to dip it in cheese. She has a big bunch of celery in the fridge. One day, Jill sees a bunny in the garden. The bunny is white and fluffy. It has long ears and a pink nose. Jill thinks the bunny is lovely. She wants to be friends with the bunny. She goes to the fridge and takes some celery. She breaks it into small pieces. She goes to the garden and puts the celery on the grass. She hopes the bunny will come and eat it. The bunny sees the celery. It hops over and sniffs it. It likes the smell and the taste. It nibbles on the celery. Jill smiles and watches the bunny. She says hello and tries to pet it. But the bunny is scared. It hears a loud noise. It is a dog. The dog is big and brown. It has sharp teeth and a long tail. It runs to the garden and barks at the bunny. It wants to catch the bunny. The bunny is in danger. It drops the celery and runs away. It looks for a safe place to hide. Jill is worried. She loves the bunny. She wants to save the bunny. She picks up the celery and throws it at the dog. The dog is surprised. It stops and looks at the celery. It sniffs it and bites it. It does not like the celery. It spits it out and makes a face. Jill uses this chance to grab the bunny. She holds it in her arms and runs to the house. She closes the door and locks it. The dog cannot get in. The bunny is safe. Jill hugs the bunny and kisses it. She gives it more celery and some water. She makes a bed for it in a box. She says she will take care of it. The bunny is happy. It licks Jill and cuddles with her. Jill and the bunny are friends. They both like celery. They both think the other is lovely.
