Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fish and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little fish. The little fish lived in a big pond. The little fish liked to swim fast. He was a fast fish. He loved to play with his friends in the water. One day, the fast fish saw a big ball. The ball was near the pond. The fast fish wanted to play with the ball. He swam fast to the ball. He wanted to kick the ball like his friends on land. The fast fish tried to kick the ball. He used his tail to kick it. The ball went up in the air. The fast fish was happy. He kicked the ball again and again. The other fish saw him and came to play too. All the fish kicked the ball with their tails. They had a lot of fun. They played all day long. The fast fish was happy that he could kick the ball like his friends on land. At the end of the day, the fast fish and his friends swam back to their homes in the pond. They were tired but happy. They knew they could play with the ball again tomorrow. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the shop with his mom. He was a little fearful because he had never been to this shop before. As they walked in, Tim held his mom's hand tight. In the shop, Tim saw a big, fluffy toy. He asked his mom, "Can I have the toy, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can have it." Tim was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog walked into the shop. It seemed to be lost. Tim was scared at first, but then he saw that the dog was nice. The dog and Tim became friends, and they all went home together.

Once upon a time, there was a happy square. The square loved to play with his friends, the circle and the triangle. They were always enthusiastic and had lots of fun together. One day, the square saw a big ball. He wanted to play with it, so he asked his friends, "Please, can we play with the big ball?" The circle and the triangle agreed, but they were a little scared. The square didn't notice and was very excited. As they played with the big ball, it started to roll faster and faster. The circle and the triangle tried to tell the square to stop, but he didn't listen. Suddenly, the big ball hit a tree and broke into pieces. The square was very sad and his friends were upset. They all learned that they should be more careful when playing with new things.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy dog named Max. Max liked to sleep and eat all day. He did not like to play or run. Max had a red ball that he loved, but he did not play with it much. The ball belonged to Max, and he kept it near his bed. One day, Max smelled a funny gas. It made him feel very awake! Max was not lazy anymore. He wanted to play with his red ball. So, Max picked up the ball and began to run and play. He was having so much fun, he did not want to stop. But then, the gas went away. Max started to feel lazy again. He stopped playing and went back to his bed. Max was sad that he was lazy again, but then he saw his red ball. He realized that even without the gas, he could still have fun playing. Max decided to play with his ball more, and he was not so lazy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his family. One day, Buddy's family said, "We are going on a vacation. We will sail on a big boat!" Buddy was very excited. On the vacation, Buddy met another dog named Daisy. They played and ran on the big boat. One day, Daisy said, "Buddy, let's play a game. We must find the best stick on the boat!" Buddy nodded, and they started to look. Buddy found a big stick and showed it to Daisy. Daisy found a small stick and showed it to Buddy. They both laughed and played with the sticks. But then, the boat started to rock. Buddy's family called him, "Buddy, come here! Stay close!" Buddy remembered he was a loyal dog and went to his family. Daisy went to her family too. The boat was safe, and everyone was happy. Buddy and Daisy learned that being loyal to their family is important. They played together for the rest of the vacation, but always stayed close to their families. And they all lived happily ever after.
