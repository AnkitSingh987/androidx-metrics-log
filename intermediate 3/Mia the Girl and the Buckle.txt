Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Buckle

One day, a little girl named Mia found a buckle on the floor. She picked it up and saw that it was shiny. Mia wanted to find the owner of the buckle, so she asked her mom for help. Her mom was very compassionate and said, "Let's go find who this buckle belongs to." Mia and her mom went outside and walked around. They saw a boy named Tim who was sad. Mia asked, "Tim, did you lose a buckle?" Tim's eyes lit up, and he said, "Yes, that's my buckle! Thank you for finding it!" Mia gave the buckle back to Tim, and he was so happy. They all went to the park to play together. Mia learned that it's good to be compassionate and help others.

One day, a big truck was driving down the road. The sky was dark with big clouds. The truck driver, named Tom, was happy because he liked to drive in the rain. Suddenly, it started to rain very hard. The truck was getting a big soak from the rain. Tom said, "I like the rain, but this is too much!" He had to stop the truck because he could not see the road. Then, Tom saw a little girl with a big umbrella. She was walking in the rain too. The girl came to Tom and said, "Hi, I can help you!" She shared her big umbrella with Tom so he could see the road. The rain stopped, and the sun came out. Tom said, "Thank you, little girl!" They both smiled and waved goodbye as Tom drove the truck away.

One day, a big bear named Ben was very tired. He went to his soft bed to sleep. But, his friend, a little bird named Lily, came to play. She did not want to disturb Ben, but she wanted to give him a hug. So, she flew close to him and said, "Ben, can I give you a hug?" Ben opened his eyes and said, "Yes, Lily. I like hugs." Lily hugged Ben and they both smiled. Then, they heard a noise outside. They went to see what it was. They saw a big box near the trees. It was a surprise! They opened the box and found a lot of toys inside. Ben and Lily were very happy. They played with the toys all day. In the end, they were both tired again. They went back to Ben's soft bed and slept together, happy with their new toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had a job to serve food to his friends. He loved to make them happy with tasty treats. One day, Max got a message from his friend, Cat. The message said there was a terrible storm coming. Max was scared, but he knew he had to warn his friends. Max ran to all his friends and told them about the terrible storm. They all went to a safe place and waited. The storm came and went, but everyone was safe. Max was happy he could help his friends.

Once upon a time there was a chubby little boy named John. One day, he and his parents went to a restaurant. On the menu, John saw something really scary. It had big sharp teeth and funny claws and it kept staring at him. Every time John went to the restaurant, he would see that same scary thing on the menu. One time, John's Mom told him to try it and he was so scared. When the food came, much to his surprise, it looked like nothing more than a big piece of delicious fried chicken. He was relieved to find out that the scary thing on the menu was not as scary as he'd thought! The moral of the story is that something can look scary, but it may not be so bad after all. Even if things don't look quite how we expect them to, it's always important to gain understanding and relate to each other with an open mind.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to watch TV. Every day after school, he would sit down and watch his favorite show. One day, Tim's mom said he could not watch TV until he finished his dinner. Tim was sad, but he knew he had to eat. He sat down at the table and took a bite of his food. It was rough and not yummy. Tim did not want to eat it, but he really wanted to watch TV. So, he took a deep breath and ate all his food as fast as he could. When Tim finished his dinner, he ran to the TV. But, something unexpected happened. The TV would not turn on! Tim was very sad and confused. Just then, his mom came in with a big smile. She had a surprise for him - a new TV! Tim was so happy, and they watched his favorite show together.
