Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy motorcycle that he loved to play with. He would ride it all around his house and make loud "vroom vroom" sounds. Tim's mommy would always recommend him to play outside, but he was too scared to take his motorcycle out. One sunny day, Tim decided to be brave and take his motorcycle outside. He played and played, and soon he got hungry. Tim saw a tasty-looking snack on the ground. It was a shiny red apple. He picked it up and took a big bite. But, oh no! The apple was not an apple, it was a sneaky red ball! Tim was surprised and a little sad. He missed his tasty snack. Just then, a kind squirrel came by with a real apple in its little paws. The squirrel saw Tim's sad face and gave him the apple. Tim was so happy! He thanked the squirrel and shared the apple with his new friend. From that day on, Tim and the squirrel played outside together, and Tim was never scared to take his motorcycle out again.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to tell all the other animals what to do. One day, Tom saw a big envelope on the ground. He wanted to bring it to his friend, the dog named Sam. Tom told Sam, "Bring the envelope to me, and then we can open it together." Sam did not like how bossy Tom was, but he wanted to be a good friend. So, Sam picked up the envelope and brought it to Tom. When they opened the envelope, they found a beautiful picture inside. It was a picture of all the animals playing together and being happy. Tom and Sam learned that being kind and working together is much better than being bossy. And from that day on, Tom was a much nicer friend to everyone.

One day, a big dog and a small cat were playing in the park. The dog wanted to play catch, but the cat found it difficult. The dog threw a ball in the air and said, "Catch the ball, cat!" The cat tried to ignore the dog's motion, but the dog kept throwing the ball. The cat said, "I don't want to play catch. It is too hard for me." The dog felt sad and stopped playing catch. He said, "I'm sorry, cat. Let's find a game we both like." They played hide and seek together and had a lot of fun. From that day on, they always played games that both of them enjoyed.

Once upon a time, there was a rich family who lived in a big house. They had a nice backyard with lots of yummy things growing in it. One day it began to rain. It rained and rained and the rain wouldn't stop. Days passed and the rain kept pouring. Before long, the whole backyard was turned into a giant puddle of water! The family was so upset and tried to find a way to make the water go away, but nothing worked. The water just kept coming back. The family understood that their rich backyard was gone forever and they were sad, but they made the best of it and found ways to play and have fun in the big puddle. Still, no one could forget that the rain had ruined their backyard and made them poor. The end.

Anna likes to collect things. She has a box full of shiny stones, feathers, buttons and shells. She likes to look at them and make patterns with them. Sometimes she shows them to her friends and they trade. One day, Anna finds a new thing to collect. It is a pale eye. It is not a real eye, but a glass eye. It is round and smooth and has a blue iris and a black pupil. Anna thinks it is pretty and puts it in her pocket. She runs home to show her mom. But her mom is not happy. She says the eye belongs to someone else. She says it is not nice to take things that are not yours. She says Anna has to give it back. Anna feels sad and sorry. She does not want to hurt anyone. She asks her mom who the eye belongs to. Her mom says it belongs to Mr. Lee, the old man who lives next door. He lost his eye in a war and wears a glass eye instead. Anna and her mom go to Mr. Lee's house. Anna knocks on the door and says hello. She gives him the eye and says sorry. Mr. Lee smiles and says thank you. He says he is not angry. He says he is glad Anna likes his eye. He says he has another eye that he does not use. He says he will give it to Anna as a gift. Anna is surprised and happy. She says thank you and hugs Mr. Lee. She says she will take good care of his eye. She says she will put it in her box with her other things. She says she will always remember him. Mr. Lee says he is happy too. He says he likes Anna. He says she has a good heart. He says he will always remember her. He says he hopes she will come and visit him again. He says he has more stories to tell her. He says he has more things to show her. He says he has more things to collect.
