Category: Intermediate
Title: Print the Dog and the Cat

One day, a big dog named Print went for a walk. He saw a little cat with a whip. The cat was not nice. Print wanted the whip, but the cat said no. Print felt sorry and asked again. The cat got mad and hit Print with the whip. Print cried and ran away. He was very sad. Print tried to take the whip one more time, but the cat hit him again. Print got hurt and went home. He was not happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy farmer. He worked all day on his farm. He had many animals like cows, pigs, and chickens. One day, he wanted to finish his work early. He was very tired. The grumpy farmer saw a big, shiny egg near the chicken house. He thought it was a chicken egg. He was happy and took the egg to his house. He wanted to eat it when his work was done. But when he finished his work and went back to his house, the egg was gone! Instead, there was a baby dragon. The dragon looked at the farmer and smiled. The grumpy farmer was not grumpy anymore. He was happy to have a new friend. They lived together and had fun on the farm.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Tim. Tim loved to eat cheese. One day, he found a big piece of cheese in the kitchen. But there was a big, terrible cat named Tom who lived in the house too. Tom did not like Tim. Tim wanted the cheese, but he was scared of Tom. Tim's friend, Sam, came to help. Sam said, "I will remind Tom about a toy. You can get the cheese then." So, Sam went to Tom and talked about a toy. But Tom did not care about the toy. Tim tried to get the cheese, but Tom saw him. Tom ran after Tim very fast. Tim was so scared. He could not get the cheese. Tom caught Tim, and it was a bad ending for Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue. They both had a big toy box. In the toy box, there was a needle. The needle was very special to them. They used it to fix their toys when they broke. One day, Tim wanted to use the needle to fix his teddy bear. But Sue wanted to use it too, to fix her doll. They both wanted the needle at the same time. This made them very sad. They didn't know how to be fair. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's share the needle! I will use it first to fix my teddy bear, and then you can use it to fix your doll." Sue thought this was a great idea. So, Tim fixed his teddy bear, and then he gave the needle to Sue. She fixed her doll with a big smile on her face. In the end, Tim and Sue learned how to share and be fair with each other. They were happy to play together with their fixed toys. And from that day on, they always shared their toys and played together nicely.

One day, a big red ball and a small blue ball went on an adventure. They wanted to find the tallest elevator in the town. They bounced and bounced all the way to the big building with the elevator. When they got there, they saw a big door. The big red ball said, "Is this the elevator?" The small blue ball said, "I think so!" They bounced inside and the door closed. The elevator went up, up, up, very high. At the top, the door opened. They bounced out and saw something strange. The floor was icy and slippery. The big red ball said, "This is not what I expected!" The small blue ball laughed and said, "Me neither, but it's fun!" They slid and bounced on the icy floor, having a great time on their unexpected adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl named Lucy. She loved to play and hold her toys. One day, she saw some big furniture in her house. Lucy wanted to be brave and climb on it. As she climbed, she held on tight to the furniture. She was so happy and excited! But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the furniture over. Lucy was very scared and sad. She didn't know what to do. The furniture was broken and Lucy couldn't play on it anymore. The brave little girl learned that it's not always good to climb on big things.
