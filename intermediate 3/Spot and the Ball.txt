Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to move around and play with his ball. One day, he saw his friend, the cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was sitting on a comfortable chair, looking happy. "Hi, Fluffy!" said Spot. "Do you want to move and play with my ball?" Fluffy looked at Spot and said, "No, thank you, Spot. I am very comfortable here on my chair. But I can watch you play." Spot was a little sad, but he still wanted to play. So he moved and moved, running and jumping with his ball. He had a lot of fun. After a while, Spot was tired but happy. He had success in playing with his ball all by himself. He went back to Fluffy, who was still sitting on the comfortable chair. "You did a good job, Spot!" said Fluffy. "That was a big success!" Spot smiled and lay down next to Fluffy. They both felt happy and comfortable, enjoying the rest of the day together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat toast for breakfast. One morning, she made some toast and put it on a plate. She was very excited to eat it. Suddenly, the toast vanished! Lily was scared and said, "Oh no! Where did my toast go?" Her big brother Tom came into the room and saw Lily looking sad. He asked, "What's wrong, Lily?" Lily told him, "My toast is gone! I am sad and scared." Tom thought for a moment and then said, "Don't worry, Lily. I will help you find your toast." They looked everywhere in the house, and finally found the toast under the table. It turns out their dog, Max, took it when Lily wasn't looking. Lily was happy to have her toast back and said, "Thank you, Tom!" The moral of the story is to always look after your things and ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, Sam the Cat found the most wonderful thing in the world: a ball of yarn! He was so excited that he couldn't keep it to himself. He decided to serve it to his three friends, Daisy the Dog, Anna the Mouse, and Ben the Bunny. He ran over to Daisy and said, "Look what I found! A beautiful ball of yarn!" But Daisy shook her head and said, "No, Sam, that yarn looks very ugly." Next, Sam served the yarn to Anna and Ben. Anna's eyes lit up and she said, "Oh Sam, this yarn is so soft and silky! I think it looks very pretty!" Ben nodded and said, "Yes, I think it looks just perfect!" Sam smiled and said, "Good! I'm glad you both like it!" The three friends then shared the yarn with each other and had lots of fun, until the sun went down.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He had a whistle with him. He liked to blow the whistle and hear the sound it made. Tim saw a big tree with sweet, red apples on it. He wanted to taste one. Tim asked a girl named Sue, "Can you help me get an apple?" Sue said, "Yes, I can climb the tree and get one for you." So Sue climbed the tree and got an apple for Tim. He was very happy. When Tim took a bite of the apple, he heard a funny sound. It was not the whistle. It was a bird hiding in the apple! Tim and Sue laughed. They did not know a bird could be in an apple. The bird flew away, and they played with the whistle and ate apples together.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play in the wild woods near his home. He liked to find new things like pretty rocks, shiny bugs, and soft leaves. One day, Tim met another youth, Sue, who was playing in the woods too. Sue was sad because she could not find her toy bear. Tim decided to help Sue look for her bear. They looked under rocks, behind trees, and in the tall grass. Finally, they found the toy bear near a big tree. Tim and Sue were very happy. They played together in the wild woods and became good friends. The moral of the story is that helping others can make you happy and bring you new friends.
