Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Sam and the Lake

Tim and Sam were brothers who liked to play in the lake. They had a kayak that their dad gave them. They took turns to paddle the kayak and have fun. One day, Tim wanted to paddle the kayak for a long time. He did not want to share with Sam. Sam was angry and asked Tim to let him have a turn. Tim said no and kicked Sam away. Sam fell into the water and got wet. Sam was sad and scared. He cried for help. Tim felt bad and realized he was mean to his brother. He paddled the kayak to Sam and helped him get in. He said sorry and hugged Sam. Sam forgave Tim and thanked him. They paddled the kayak together and shared. They were happy and had more fun. The moral of the story is: Sharing is caring. Kicking is not nice.

One day, Molly was out with her parents in the wild. All around her were different animals, like rabbits and squirrels. All the colours of the animals were so brilliant that Molly clapped her hands together in delight. Suddenly, there was a ringing sound. Molly looked and saw a beautiful, bright yellow bird sitting in a tree. It was a very special kind of bird and it had a unique and loud ring. Molly and her parents watched in amazement. Molly wanted to keep the bird near her, so she asked her parents if she could have it. They said yes, so Molly picked up the bird gently and hugged it. From that day on, the bird was Molly’s pet. It reminded her of the wild, and its ringing was a beautiful sound.

Once upon a time there lived a rabbit called Henry. He was a very fast and speedy bunny who loved to run and jump around the garden. One day Henry saw a big circle in the garden. He wondered what it was. He hopped around the circle, intrigued. Suddenly, Henry noticed a gloomy atmosphere surrounding the circle. He gasped in surprise and hopped closer to investigate. All of a sudden, a magical voice called to Henry from the middle of the circle. Startled, he stopped. "What are you doing here? This is my circle," the voice said. Henry knew it was very rude to speed around somebody else's circle. "Oh, I'm sorry," he apologized. "I didn't know this was your circle." The voice laughed, "It's okay. You can play here if you like, just don't speed around too much!" From then on, Henry enjoyed playing in the circle. They became good friends and Henry always remembered to be respectful and not speed around too much. The end.

Ben was a big boy. He had a new baby sister, Lily. He wanted to help Mom and Dad with Lily. He liked to look at her in the crib. She was very small and cute. One day, Mom said, "Ben, can you help me wrap a present for Lily? It's her birthday tomorrow." Ben nodded. He liked to wrap presents. He followed Mom to the living room. Mom gave him a big, soft blanket. It was pink and had flowers on it. "Can you fold the blanket like this?" Mom showed Ben how to fold the blanket in half, then in half again. Ben tried to copy Mom. He folded the blanket, but it was hard. The blanket was too big for him. He dropped it on the floor. He felt sad. Mom smiled. She said, "It's okay, Ben. You can try again. Here, let me help you." She picked up the blanket and helped Ben fold it. Then she said, "Now, can you put the blanket in this box?" She gave Ben a small, white box. Ben took the box and put the blanket inside. He was happy. Mom said, "Good job, Ben! You wrapped a present for Lily. She will love it. Do you want to give it to her?" Ben nodded. He wanted to give the present to Lily. He picked up the box and followed Mom to the crib. Lily was sleeping. She looked very peaceful. Ben said, "Shh, Mom. Lily is sleeping. We have to be quiet." He tiptoed to the crib and put the box next to Lily. He said, "Happy birthday, Lily. This is a present from me. It's a blanket. You can use it when you are cold. I hope you like it." Lily opened her eyes. She saw the box. She was confused. She did not know what it was. She reached for it. She touched it. It felt hard. She pushed it. It fell off the crib. It made a loud noise. Lily started to cry. Ben was shocked. He did not mean to make Lily cry. He said, "Oh no, Lily. I'm sorry. I didn't mean to scare you. Please don't cry. I love you." He tried to hug Lily, but Mom stopped him. Mom said, "Ben, it's okay. Lily is just surprised. She doesn't understand what a present is. She will learn soon. You are a very sweet brother. You did a very nice thing for Lily. Thank you for helping me wrap the present. Come on, let's go and get some cake. We can celebrate Lily's birthday together." She picked up Ben and kissed him. She picked up the box and the blanket. She said, "Don't worry, Lily. We'll be back soon. We'll open the present together. You'll see. It's a very beautiful blanket. You'll love it." She smiled at Lily and kissed her. She took Ben and the present to the kitchen. Lily stopped crying. She looked at the crib. She was still confused. She wondered what a present was. She closed her eyes and fell asleep.
