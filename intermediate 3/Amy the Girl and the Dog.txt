Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play with her toy, Zigzag. Zigzag was a funny, wiggly toy that Amy would hold in her hands. Amy and Zigzag were best friends. One day, Amy and Zigzag went to the park. They saw a big dog. The dog looked dangerous. Amy was scared. She held Zigzag tight in her hands. Zigzag said, "Don't be scared, Amy. I will help you." Amy and Zigzag made a plan. They walked in a zigzag path, so the big dog could not catch them. The dog tried to follow them, but he could not. Amy and Zigzag were safe. They went home and played together all day.

One day, a bird with modest wings lived in a cage. The bird liked to sing. A girl named Sue loved the bird. She would listen and record the bird's songs. Sue and the bird were happy. One day, Sue said, "Bird, I want to show you something." Sue opened the cage. The bird flew out of the cage. The bird was scared, but Sue said, "Don't worry, I am here." The bird flew around and saw a big tree. The bird sang a new song. Sue recorded the new song. Then, something unexpected happened. The tree talked! The tree said, "Thank you, bird, for your beautiful song. I have been lonely, but your song made me happy." The bird, Sue, and the tree became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn monkey. He lived in a big tree. The monkey liked to play tricks on his friends. One day, he thought of a new trick. The monkey saw a big, round fruit. He thought it would be funny to throw it at his friends. He picked it up and threw it at his friend, the bird. But, something unexpected happened. The big, round fruit did not hit the bird. Instead, it fell down and broke open. The monkey looked at the broken fruit and realized it was not a fruit at all. It was a ball! Inside the ball, there were many colorful balls. The monkey and his friends had so much fun playing with the colorful balls. The stubborn monkey learned that it is better to share and play with friends than to play tricks on them.

Once upon a time, there was a playful boy named Tim. Tim liked to collect things. He had a big box full of toys and rocks. One day, Tim went to the park to find new things to collect. While at the park, Tim saw a long snake. The snake was green and had pretty spots. Tim thought the snake would be a great thing to collect. He picked up the snake and put it in his box. Tim took the snake home and showed it to his mom. His mom was scared and said, "No, Tim! The snake is not a toy." The snake bit Tim's hand. Tim cried and the snake went away. Tim learned not to collect snakes anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to take a nap under a big tree. One day, while Kitty was napping, a distant sound woke her up. Kitty saw a line of ants and decided to follow them. She asked the ants, "Where are you going?" The ants said, "We are going to a big party with lots of food. You can come too, but don't eat all the food!" Kitty followed the ants to the party. There, she saw lots of food and started to eat. She forgot what the ants told her. Soon, all the food was gone. The ants were sad and mad at Kitty. They told her to leave and never come back. Kitty felt bad and walked away, sad that she had no friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside and examine the world around her. One day, Lily saw dark clouds in the sky. She knew the weather was going to change soon. Lily went inside her house and told her mom, "Mommy, the weather is changing. I think it will rain soon." Her mom smiled and said, "Thank you for telling me, Lily. Let's make some yummy cookies while we wait for the rain to come." Lily and her mom made yummy cookies together. The smell of the cookies filled the house. As they finished baking, they heard the pitter-patter of raindrops on the window. Lily and her mom sat by the window, enjoying their cookies and watching the rain fall outside.
