Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a cool, green forest, there lived a big, friendly bear. The bear liked to walk and play with his friends, the birds and the squirrels. They all loved the cool forest and its noises. One day, the bear heard a new noise. It was a little girl, singing a pretty song. The bear walked to the girl and said, "Hi, I like your song. It is a nice noise." The girl smiled and said, "Thank you, bear. I like your big, soft fur." The bear and the girl became friends, and they played together in the cool forest. The girl taught the bear how to bow, and they danced to the sounds of the forest. The birds and the squirrels joined in, and they all had fun together. The bear and the girl were happy, and they played in the cool forest every day.

John and his mommy were on a walk in the park. John was excited to see people gathering around the fountain, so they went to see what was happening. John saw that there were two ladies in the middle of the fountain. One of them was wearing a beautiful veil and was smiling. The lady in the white veil was very shy and didn't say very much. The other lady in the colorful veil was very kind. John's mommy leaned down and asked him if he wanted to go closer. John nodded and they walked over. The lady in the white veil smiled at him and touched his shoulder with her gentle hand. John thanked her for the kind gesture. Then, the lady with the colorful veil said to him that it was special gathering because it was a celebration of joy! Everyone smiled and was happy. John thought it was so special that he asked if he could wear one of the veils. The ladies smiled, and gave him the white veil. John was so shy, but he was happy, and all the people around cheered for him. John and his mommy gathered around the ladies to take photos and enjoy the moment. At the end of the day, John thanked the ladies for the special gathering and he was sure he had made a new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a farmer in a field. The farmer was sad because he had no wheat. Tim had a bag of lucky wheat seeds. He wanted to help the farmer. Tim said, "I have lucky wheat seeds. I will set them in your field. They will grow big and strong." The farmer was happy and thanked Tim. Together, they set the lucky wheat seeds in the field. After some time, the wheat grew big and tall. The farmer was very happy. He shared the wheat with Tim and his family. They all learned that helping others can make you feel lucky too.

One day, a little girl named Anna wanted to dress up. She put on a high hat and a long, pretty dress. Anna felt very happy in her fancy clothes. She wanted to show her friend, Sam, her new look. So, she went outside to find him. Anna saw Sam by a big tube. The tube was high and long. Sam said, "Anna, look at this cool tube! Let's play here." Anna and Sam had a lot of fun playing by the tube. They laughed and talked all day. Then, something unexpected happened. A small dog came out of the tube! The dog was wearing a high hat like Anna's. Anna and Sam were very surprised. The dog said, "I like your hat, Anna! Can I play with you?" Anna and Sam laughed and said, "Yes, let's all play together!" And they had even more fun with their new dog friend.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia saw a swing and ran to it. She was very happy. Mia's mom said, "Be careful, Mia! Hold tight!" Mia sat on the swing and her mom pushed her. Mia felt like she was flying. She laughed and said, "Mom, look at me! I'm so high!" Her mom smiled and kept pushing her. After a while, Mia's mom said, "Okay, Mia, it's time to release the swing and go home." Mia let go of the swing and it stopped moving. They both walked home, holding hands, talking about their fun day at the park.
