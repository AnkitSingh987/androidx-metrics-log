Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cable

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy a cable for their TV. There were many cables to choose from. Tim saw a long cable and a short cable. His mom said they needed a regular cable, not too long and not too short. Tim looked around and found a regular cable. It was just the right size! He showed it to his mom, and she smiled. She said, "Good job, Tim! That's the perfect cable for our TV." Tim felt happy that he could help his mom. They bought the regular cable and went home. Tim's mom put the cable on the TV, and it worked great! They watched their favorite show together. Tim was proud that he could choose the right cable for their TV.

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and laugh. But then they heard a buzz. A mosquito was flying near them. It wanted to bite them and make them itch. "Go away, mosquito!" Anna shouted. She waved her arms and tried to hit it. But the mosquito was too fast and too small. It dodged her and flew to Ben. "Ow!" Ben cried. He felt a sting on his leg. The mosquito had bitten him. He rubbed his leg and frowned. It was red and sore. "Stop, mosquito! Stop!" Ben yelled. He also waved his arms and tried to hit it. But the mosquito was too clever and too sneaky. It flew away from him and back to Anna. Anna and Ben felt helpless. They did not know how to get rid of the mosquito. They wished they had a net or a spray or something to scare it away. Then they saw their mom coming out of the house. She had a big smile and a glass of juice for them. "Mom, mom, help us!" Anna and Ben said. They ran to her and hugged her. They told her about the mosquito and showed her their bites. "Shh, shh, it's okay," mom said. She kissed their bites and gave them the juice. She also had a secret weapon. She had a fan. She turned it on and pointed it at the garden. The fan made a strong wind. The wind blew the mosquito away. It could not fly against it. It had to leave Anna and Ben alone. "Bye-bye, mosquito!" Anna and Ben said. They smiled and drank their juice. They were happy and safe with their mom and her fan. They did not have to worry about the mosquito anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was three years old, with bright, bouncy blonde curls. One day, Lily decided to make some waffles for breakfast. She grabbed all the ingredients she needed and, with her mom's help, started to mix it together. When the batter was ready, she put it into the waffle maker. Then she waited and watched as they started to cook. She was very excited to finally have her waffle. But then, something unexpected happened. Lily noticed that her waffle was very thick. She realized it was too thick to eat. She began to get upset. Just then, her mom walked in. She saw Lily crying and asked what was wrong. Lily explained the situation and her mom smiled reassuringly. “I know how to fix this!” her mom said. She added a little bit more batter to the waffle maker to make the waffles thinner. After a few minutes, the waffles were ready. Lily was so excited to eat her waffle that was now the perfect thickness. She laughed and gave her mom a big hug. From that day on, Lily never forgot to add just the right amount of batter to make the perfect waffles.

Michael was very happy. He was about to unpack his new purple toy. He could not wait to see what he had. He ran up to his mum and said "Mum! I have a feeling my toy is going to be special!" His mum smiled and replied "I'm sure your toy will be special! Why don't you unpack it and see what it is?" Michael carefully took the box and sat on the floor. He undid the ribbon and opened the lid. Excitement ran through him as he reached in and pulled out his new purple toy. It was a puppy! Michael's eyes lit up as he looked at his new toy. He hugged it and said "This is the best toy ever! I love it so much!" Mum smiled and said "Well, I'm glad you like it. Now let's get your puppy unpacked and ready to play!" Michael quickly helped unpack the toy and soon he was ready to start playing with it. It was the best day ever!
