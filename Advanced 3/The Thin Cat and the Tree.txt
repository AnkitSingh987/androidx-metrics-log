Title: The Thin Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat. The cat was purple. Its name was Kitty. One day, Kitty went for a walk. Kitty saw a big tree. Kitty wanted to climb the tree. But, Kitty was scared. Kitty tried to climb the tree. Kitty saw a bird. The bird was purple too. The bird said, "My name is Birdy." Kitty and Birdy were friends. They played together. The tree was not scary anymore.

One day, a boy named Tim found an unusual box. The box had a big button on it. Tim pushed the button and out came a print. The print had a picture of a cat on it. Tim showed the print to his mom. "Mom, look at this print I found!" said Tim. His mom looked at the print and said, "That's a nice print, Tim. But we can improve it by coloring it." So Tim and his mom colored the cat on the print. They used red, blue, and yellow. When they were done, the cat looked very pretty. They put the print on the fridge. The next day, Tim went to the fridge to get a snack. He saw the print and noticed something unexpected. The cat on the print was gone! In its place was a real cat, with the same red, blue, and yellow colors. The cat jumped off the fridge and said, "Thank you for improving my picture. Now I am a real cat!" Tim and his mom were very surprised, but happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little black cat named Zigzag. Zigzag loved to play outside. One sunny day, Zigzag went to the park to play with her friends. In the park, Zigzag saw her friend, a big dog named Woof. Woof was running around and having fun. Zigzag called out, "Woof, come play with me!" Woof heard Zigzag and ran over to her. They played together, chasing each other and laughing. After a while, they got tired and sat under a tree. Zigzag and Woof talked about their day. Zigzag said, "I had so much fun playing with you today, Woof." Woof wagged his tail and said, "Me too, Zigzag. Let's play again tomorrow!" They smiled and went home, excited for more fun days at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a swan named Sally. Sally was a very intelligent swan. She loved to learn new things every day. One day, Sally met a friendly frog named Fred. Fred said, "Hi Sally! I can teach you how to jump high like me!" Sally was excited and said, "Yes, please! I want to learn!" Fred taught Sally how to jump high. Sally was very happy. She learned to jump high like a frog. Sally and Fred played and jumped all day long. They became best friends forever.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to cook. She went to the kitchen and saw a big, thick book. The book had many fun things to cook. Lily picked a cake to make. She was happy to cook. When she was cooking, Lily saw a surf outside her window. She wanted to play with the surf. She took her cake and went outside. She put the cake on the surf and started to play. The cake was on the surf, and it went up and down. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird saw the cake on the surf. The bird wanted the cake too. The bird flew down and took the cake from the surf. Lily was sad, but then she saw the bird was happy. Lily thought, "I can cook another cake." She went back inside and cooked a new cake. This time, she shared it with her friends and the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer who had a melon patch which was full of melons. He used to go there every day to pick some melons and take them to the market. One day, the farmer met a little boy who wanted to pick some melons. The farmer agreed, but made him promise not to wander away too much. However, the little boy was so excited that he forgot the farmer's warning and went too deep into the field. Suddenly, he saw some dangerous animals running around. He realized it was too dangerous and he had to run away. He ran back to the farmer and told him what he had seen. The farmer said, "See, I told you! You must always keep your promises and remember not to go too far." The little boy promised to the farmer he would always remember his warning. From then on, the little boy enjoyed picking melons with the farmer in a safe place.
