Title: Max the Dog and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play outside in the sun. One day, it was very cold and Max went outside to play. He started to shiver because it was so cold. Max did not like the cold but he wanted to play. Max saw a big tree and wanted to hide behind it. He ran to the tree and took a big breath with his lung. Max felt embarrassed because he was shivering so much. He wanted to be brave and not shiver in the cold. Max decided to go back inside where it was warm. He stopped shivering and felt happy again. Max learned that it is okay to be embarrassed sometimes and that it is better to play outside when it is warm.

Once upon a time, there was a cute mommy and a sweet daddy. They had a cute little girl. The mommy and daddy loved the little girl very much and trusted her. One day, the mommy and daddy decided to let the little girl enter an adorable painting contest. They told her to do her best and that it did not matter if she won or not. The little girl was very excited. The day of the contest arrived and the little girl was very excited. Everyone admired her cute painting. But when it came time to announce the winners, the little girl did not win. She was very disappointed. The mommy and daddy told her that it didn't matter if she won or not. They hugged her and said they still loved her and trusted her even though she didn't win. The little girl had learned an important lesson that day - she learns that trust and love are more valuable than winning.

Once upon a time, there was a light flower named Lily. Lily lived near the ocean. Every day, she saw big waves and felt the cool wind. Lily loved living by the ocean. One day, a small boy named Tim came to play near the ocean. He saw Lily and said, "Hello, pretty flower! I like how you bloom." Lily felt very happy to hear this. Tim came to visit Lily every day. One day, Tim brought a new friend, a tiny fish named Fin. Fin was from the ocean. He said, "Hi, Lily! The ocean is my home." Lily, Tim, and Fin played together every day. They were the best of friends and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Abbie. She lived in a house with her mom, dad and her little sister who was called Lily. One day, Abbie was playing in her garden and she saw something sparkly in the soil. She started to dig to see what it was and she found something shiny and round. Abbie asked her mom what it was and she said it was a penny. Abbie was so excited and she ran off to show her sister the penny. Lily was so amazed by the penny she asked if she could have it. Abbie thought for a moment and then she said "Yes, but you must promise to be honest with me." Lily agreed and said she would be honest. Abbie gave Lily the penny and they both imagined what they could buy with it. "Let's start a lemonade stand," suggested Lily. Abbie thought this was a great idea and ran off to get glasses, lemons and sugar. Lily shared her honest opinion on how the lemonade tasted, while Abbie shared her's. Later that day, Abbie and Lily sat in their garden with a jar filled with money from their lemonade stand. They looked at each other, smiled and promised to start a new adventure together every week.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a smooth stone near a hedge. He picked it up and smiled. The hedge was green and tall. Tim liked to walk near the hedge. Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was playing with a pile of stones. Tim said, "Hi Sue! Can I play too?" Sue said, "Yes, Tim! Let's add stones to my pile." Tim and Sue added stones to the pile. They made the pile very big. The stones were smooth and fun to touch. They laughed and played near the hedge. Soon, it was time to go home. Tim and Sue said, "Bye! Let's play again soon." They left the pile of smooth stones near the hedge. Tim and Sue had a fun day playing together.
