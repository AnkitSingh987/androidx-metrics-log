Title: Sarah and the Rabbit

Once upon a time there was a harmless owl called Sarah. She was curious and brave and she wanted to explore the world. Every day when the sun came up, Sarah would fly around and look for something new. One day she flew across to a big tree and saw a safe. She was excited and flew around it to take a look. "Hello," said a voice. Startled, Sarah looked down and saw a little rabbit. "Do you want to play?" asked the rabbit. Sarah nodded excitedly and hopped closer. The rabbit gave Sarah a big kiss and said, "This safe is for you now. You can keep your things safe in it!" She thanked the rabbit, gave him a kiss and then hugged her safe. She knew that the safe would keep her things harmless. From that day on, Sarah never felt scared or alone. She was happy to explore the world and she always had a safe place to keep her things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to draw with her pastel colors. Every day, she would look at the sky and draw what she saw. The sky was her favorite thing to draw because it was so big and pretty. One day, Lily met a dependable dog named Max. Max was a very good dog. He would always be by Lily's side when she drew pictures. They became best friends. Max would look at the sky with Lily and help her pick the right pastel colors for her drawings. Together, they drew many beautiful pictures of the sky. They would look at the clouds and the sun, and use pastel colors to make them come alive on paper. Lily and Max were very happy, and they knew they would always have each other to share their love for the sky and pastel colors. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy weapon, a sword. He loved to play with his sword all day. One day, Tim saw a big, spoiled fruit on the ground. He thought it would be fun to spread the fruit using his sword. Tim said to his friend, "Watch me spread the spoiled fruit with my weapon!" His friend watched as Tim swung the sword at the fruit. The fruit splattered everywhere, and Tim laughed. But then, Tim's mom came outside. She saw the mess and said, "Oh no, Tim! You shouldn't have spread the spoiled fruit." Tim felt bad and knew he had to clean up the mess. He learned not to play with spoiled things and to be more careful with his toy weapon.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small stream. The water in the stream was very clear and cold. All the animals in the forest loved to drink from the stream. One day, a huge bear came to the stream. He wanted to drink water, but the stream was very small. The bear asked the stream, "Can you permit me to drink water?" The stream said, "Yes, you can drink my water, but be careful not to make me dirty." The next day, the bear came back with a big cup. He was very thirsty, and he wanted to drink a lot of water. But when he put the cup in the stream, the water became dirty. The other animals in the forest were very sad because they could not drink the water. The bear felt bad for making the stream dirty, so he cleaned the water for the other animals. From that day on, the bear always made sure to keep the stream clean. The animals in the forest were happy, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, Penny the cat went for a walk. She saw her friends playing in the park. They were running and jumping. Penny wanted to join them and have fun too. She ran to her friends and they all played together. As they played, a big ball rolled towards them. Penny and her friends were confused. They did not know where the ball came from. They looked around and saw a little boy crying. He wanted his ball back but was too scared to ask. Penny had an idea. She picked up the ball with her mouth and walked to the little boy. She gave the ball back to him. The boy was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Penny!" Then, the little boy joined Penny and her friends, and they all played together in the park.
