Title: The Flea and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little flea. The flea lived on a big dog. The dog was nice, but sometimes it was difficult for the flea to understand the dog. One day, the dog went for a walk. The flea jumped and played on the dog's back. The dog saw a cat and wanted to play, but the cat did not understand the dog. The cat was scared and ran away. The flea felt sad for the dog. It wanted to help the dog make friends. The flea talked to the dog and told it to be gentle with the cat. The dog listened and tried again. This time, the cat understood the dog, and they became friends. The flea was happy, and they all played together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a kind nurse named Lily. She loved to help people feel better. Lily also loved flowers, especially yellow ones. Every day, she would look at her garden and wait for the flowers to bloom. One day, a little boy named Timmy came to see Nurse Lily. He was sad because he had a boo-boo on his knee. Nurse Lily put a yellow band-aid on Timmy's knee and told him a secret. She said, "When my yellow flowers bloom, something magical will happen." Timmy went home and waited for the flowers to bloom. He looked at his yellow band-aid and thought about what Nurse Lily said. Days went by, and finally, the yellow flowers in Lily's garden bloomed. Timmy ran to see Nurse Lily right away. When Timmy arrived, Nurse Lily was smiling. She said, "The magical thing happened! My yellow flowers made me happy, and I want to share that happiness with you." She gave Timmy a bouquet of yellow flowers, and his boo-boo felt better. From that day on, every time Timmy saw yellow flowers, he thought of Nurse Lily and her magical garden. And just like the flowers, their friendship bloomed and grew stronger.

Once upon a time, there was a little fragile bird named Timmy. Timmy lived in a small nest on a big tree. He loved to fly around and play with his friends all day. One day, the sun went down and it was dark outside. Timmy was scared of the dark. He didn't know how to go back to his nest. He saw a kind squirrel named Sally. Sally said, "Don't worry, Timmy. I will help you get home." Sally took Timmy's wing and they walked together. They saw other animals who were happy to welcome Timmy and Sally. They said, "Hello!" and "Good night!" to them. Soon, Timmy and Sally found the big tree with the small nest. Timmy was so happy to be back home. He thanked Sally for helping him in the dark. They became best friends and played together every day. From that day on, Timmy was never scared of the dark again because he knew he had friends to help him.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a fast dog named Speed. Speed loved to run and catch balls. He was very light on his feet. One day, Speed saw a big cat. The cat was up in a tree. Speed wanted to catch the cat, but he could not climb. Speed had an idea. He found a ball and threw it at the tree. The ball hit the tree and made a loud sound. The cat looked down and saw Speed. The cat did not like the sound, so it jumped from the tree and ran away. Speed ran after the cat. They ran and ran, but Speed could not catch the cat. Then, Speed saw a big net. He had an idea. He threw the ball into the net. The cat saw the ball and jumped into the net to catch it. Speed ran to the net and caught the cat. They both laughed and played with the ball together. From that day on, Speed and the cat were friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sara. She loved to play dress-up with her toy crown. One day, Sara got a letter in the mail. The letter said there was a big dress-up party, and Sara was very excited. She knew she had to get ready for the party with her pretty crown. Sara put on her favorite dress and her shiny crown. Her mom helped her get ready. Sara looked in the mirror and smiled. She knew she was going to have a great time at the party. When she was all ready, Sara and her mom went to the big dress-up party. At the party, there were lots of kids wearing fun costumes. Sara saw a boy with a cape, a girl with wings, and even a dog with a hat. Then, the party had a big surprise. Everyone who wore a crown got a special prize! Sara was so happy that she wore her crown. She had a great time at the party, and she knew her crown was very special.
