Title: The Clean Glass and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a clean glass. It was so clean and clear that it would shine in the sun. The glass lived on a table in a big room. Every day, the sun would come in and make the glass shine. The glass was very happy. One day, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into the room. The bird saw the shine from the clean glass and thought it was water. The bird flew down to the glass to take a drink. But, when the bird got close, it saw that the shine was not water, but the clean glass. The bird was sad but then it had an idea. The bird flew outside and found a big leaf. It put the leaf on the glass and now, the glass did not shine anymore. The bird was happy because it could now see that the glass was not water. The clean glass learned that sometimes, it is good not to shine too much. From that day on, the glass and the bird became best friends.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see many things in the park. They see birds, flowers, trees and people. One day, they see a man with a camera. The man is taking pictures of the birds. Lily and Ben are curious. They want to see the camera. They go to the man and say hello. The man is friendly. He says hello back. He shows them his camera. He says the camera can make the birds look big and clear. Lily and Ben are amazed. They ask the man if they can try the camera. The man says yes. He helps them hold the camera and press the button. They take pictures of the birds, the flowers, the trees and each other. The man says they are good at taking pictures. He says they are healthy and happy. He says he will print the pictures and give them to them later. Lily and Ben say thank you. They say goodbye to the man and the birds. They go back to play in the park. They are happy. They have a new friend and a new hobby.

Once upon a time, there was a panda named Pandy. Pandy was a very big panda who lived in the green woods. One day, Pandy felt very uncomfortable because he was very dirty. He had been playing in the mud with his friends. His fur was all brown and sticky. Pandy knew he needed to bathe to feel better. So, he went to the river near his home. The water was cold and clear. Pandy jumped in and started to wash away the mud. He splashed and played in the water, making sure to clean every part of his fur. As Pandy was bathing, his friend, a little bird, came to visit him. The bird said, "Pandy, you look so clean and happy now!" Pandy smiled and replied, "Yes, I feel much better after my bath. Thank you for noticing, little bird!" And they both played by the river for the rest of the day.

One day, Emma and her brother Aaron decided to have a picnic in the woods. As they laid down the blanket for their picnic, Aaron noticed something poking out from beneath a bush. It was a small, humble sock. Aaron picked up the sock. He excitedly showed it to Emma and asked what it was. Emma thought for a moment and then replied, "It's a sock. Let's discuss what we should do with it." The siblings looked around, and after a while they decided it was best to take the sock home and wash it. Emma suggested they put the sock on a tree branch so they could find it on their way back. Aaron agreed and they both tied the sock to a branch. The two then enjoyed their picnic together. When they finished, they started the journey back home, and they spotted the sock still hanging on its branch. Aaron happily took the sock home. Back at home, they discussed the best way to take care of the sock. Emma suggested they put the sock into the washing machine, and Aaron agreed. After the sock was clean, Aaron and Emma talked about who should get the sock. Finally, they decided it would be best for whoever needed it most. They agreed to keep the sock handy in case a humble friend might need it.
