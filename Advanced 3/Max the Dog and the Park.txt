Title: Max the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play and run in the park. One day, Max saw a big cat. The cat had a yummy treat. Max wanted the treat too. Max went to the cat and said, "Can I have a treat, please?" The cat looked at Max and said, "You speak very polite. I will give you a treat." Max was happy to get the treat. He said, "Thank you, cat!" They both played and had fun. Max learned that being polite is good. And they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Ann. She was three years old and loved playing outside. One day, Ann was playing in the garden when she noticed something strange. The sky was turning an odd colour and the air began to feel different. Suddenly, a tornado appeared in the sky. It was spinning in circles and Ann felt scared. She shouted, “Mommy! Help me!” Her mom ran out and said, “Don’t worry, Ann. We’ll stay organized and split into different rooms. That way, we won’t get hurt!” While the tornado whirled and blew, Ann and her mom stayed safely in the house, split into two different rooms. Ann was proud that they were so organized. After a while, the tornado stopped spinning and flew away. The sky turned blue again and Ann was relieved. She gave her mom a big hug and said, “Thanks for helping me stay safe!”

The frog was hopping around the empty pond. It hopped and hopped until the frog started to glow. The frog was so excited! The frog said to its friend, "Look how I'm glowing! I'm glowing, I'm glowing!" The other frog replied, "That's amazing! What do you think is happening?" The glowing frog said, "I think the sun is making me glow." The other frog said, "Yes, that must be it! You look beautiful!" The glowing frog was so happy. It hopped around the empty pond with its friend, glowing and smiling. The sun shone brightly and the two frogs had the best time.

Josh was a 3 year old who loved playing with his pet dog, Spot. Josh and Spot were the best of friends. One day, Josh took Spot for a walk. Josh put a pretty collar on Spot and put the leash on it. Spot wanted to go and explore the neighbourhood. Josh said, "Spot, you must remain with me. We will have more fun if we stay together!" Spot did not want to, so he kept pulling the leash. Josh was getting frustrated, but he kept tugging at the leash gently and kept Spot with him. Josh could see that Spot in his pretty collar was enjoying the neighbourhood. Josh said, "Spot, you must remain with me. I know you want to explore, but staying together will make us both happy." Spot understood, and stayed close to Josh. Josh was happy that Spot had listened to him and they both enjoyed their walk. They also met some of Spot's friends and enjoyed playing together. Josh and Spot remained together, and had fun all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Kate. She was only three years old and loved playing with her friends. One day, Kate and her friends decided to go to the nearby cornfield for some fun. The field was plentiful and the children played for hours, running and hiding amongst the tall, green stalks. But when a gust of wind blew, the corn shook and made a rattling noise. Kate and her friends were startled and found it very funny. Kate then had an idea - she suggested they play a game of kick the corn! Everyone was excited and the game began. Everyone had a turn and it was lots of fun. But then the game got a little bit too rough. Kate kicked a corn really hard, upsetting her friends. She had been too rude and they all started to cry. The day ended sadly. Kate and her friends went home feeling embarrassed and sad. Kate had learned an important lesson - to always be kind and never be too rude!
