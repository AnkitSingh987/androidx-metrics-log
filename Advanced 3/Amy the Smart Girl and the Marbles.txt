Title: Amy the Smart Girl and the Marbles

Once upon a time, in a small town, lived a smart girl named Amy. She loved to play with her colorful marbles. She had a big bag full of them. She would roll them, spin them, and make them race each other. One sunny day, Amy decided to make a fun game. She took a big piece of paper and started to fold it. She folded it once, twice, and then three times. Soon, she had a small paper house with little rooms inside. Amy put her marbles in the paper house. She pretended that the marbles were a family living in the house. They had a mommy marble, a daddy marble, and baby marbles too. They played together, ate together, and slept in their cozy paper beds. Amy had so much fun playing with her marbles in the paper house. And the marbles were happy to have such a smart friend like Amy.

Once upon a time, there was a small rocket. It was smooth and shiny. The rocket wanted to stretch and go up high in the sky. It liked to watch the birds fly and wanted to be like them. One day, the rocket met a big bird. The bird said, "I can help you stretch and go up high like me." The rocket was very happy. The big bird told the rocket to follow him to a tall tree. The rocket followed the big bird to the tall tree. The big bird said, "Now, stretch as much as you can and jump off the tree." The rocket was scared but wanted to be like the big bird. So, it stretched and jumped off the tree. But the rocket could not fly like the big bird. It fell down and broke into pieces. The big bird felt sad for the rocket. The rocket should have known it was not a bird and could not fly.

Once there was a dog named Ben. Ben loved to eat jelly and he was always very excited whenever there was some to eat. Ben found an original jar of jelly all by himself one day. He was so excited that he didn't wait for anyone before he started eating it. Then, he started to suffer. He had a sore stomach because he ate the whole jar too quickly. He had only eaten a little bit of the jelly, but it was enough to make him feel very sick. He was so sad that he couldn't enjoy his original jar of jelly. Ben decided he should be more careful next time and wait for someone to share the jelly with him. That way he wouldn't suffer anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful girl named Sue. Sue liked to help her mom and dad in the house. One day, her mom asked her to find a number in the kitchen. Sue was very excited to help. Sue looked and looked, but she could not find the number. Then, she saw the big refrigerator. She opened it, and there it was! The number was on a big, round cake. Sue was so happy to find it. Sue took the cake with the number out of the refrigerator. She brought it to her mom, who was very proud of her helpful girl. They all ate the cake together, and Sue felt good because she had helped her mom find the number.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, round stomach. He liked to eat a lot. One day, he saw a crazy cat running around. The cat was tearing up paper and making a mess. Tim thought it was funny and laughed. Tim said to the cat, "Hey, crazy cat! Why are you tearing the paper?" The cat stopped and looked at Tim. It said, "I'm looking for a magic treasure map. I heard it was hidden in one of these papers." Tim was surprised. He didn't know cats could talk! He wanted to help the cat find the treasure. So, they looked through all the torn papers together. Suddenly, they found a map with a big "X" on it. They knew it was the treasure map! They followed the map to a big tree. The cat climbed up the tree and found a box hidden in the branches. It brought the box down and they opened it together. Inside the box, there were lots of yummy treats for Tim and the cat to share. They both ate until their stomachs were full and became the best of friends.
