Title: Tim the Child and the Dog

One day, a child named Tim went to the park. He liked to sing songs. He sang a song about a big, red ball. Tim was happy when he sang. A scared dog heard Tim sing. The dog ran to Tim and said, "Please help me. I lost my toy bone." Tim wanted to help the scared dog. They looked for the toy bone together. They found the toy bone under a big tree. The scared dog was happy now. Tim and the dog played with the toy bone. Then, they sang the big red ball song together. They were not scared anymore. They were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little angel. This angel lived in a big, rich house. The house had many rooms, and the angel liked to play in all of them. One day, the angel found a pretty toy. The toy was broken, and the angel wanted to fix it. So, the angel tried to attach the pieces back together. The angel worked very hard, and soon, the toy was all fixed. The angel was very happy to have fixed the toy. The angel played with the toy all day long. The toy and the angel became best friends, and they lived happily ever after in the big, rich house.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a broken toy on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Mom, can I get this fixed?" His mom looked at the toy and said, "This is a fly toy. Let's go home and try to fix it." When they got home, Tim and his mom worked together to fix the broken fly toy. They glued the wings back on and waited for it to dry. Once it was dry, Tim took the fly toy outside to play. He threw the fly toy up in the air and watched it fly around. Tim was very happy that he and his mom fixed the broken toy. They played with the fly toy in the park until the sun went down, and then they went home to rest.

Michael was a very stubborn boy. He was 3 years old and always wanted to do things his own way. One day, he was walking in the woods. Suddenly he heard a voice. It said, "Come closer! I have something to give you." He hesitated for a moment, but decided to approach the voice. When he got closer, he saw it was an owl perched on a tree. The owl said, "I have something special for you, Michael. Here is a wisdom for you to remember: never be too stubborn to try something new." Michael was very surprised. He said, "Thank you for your wisdom." The owl nodded and then flew away. Michael couldn't forget what the owl said. He remembered it whenever he was tempted to be too stubborn. He was very thankful he had heard the owl's wisdom.

Once upon a time, a troubled crane was walking in a meadow. It was feeling very scared and alone. Suddenly, the crane saw something flying up high in the sky and it knew it had to follow. The crane spread its wings and flew higher and higher, and soon it could see a sack in the horizon. The sack was as big as a mountain, so the crane started to worry about how it would ever reach it. But the crane kept flying and eventually it made it to the sack and rested there. The crane was happy and it decided to stay, but little did it know that the sack was very troubled too - the sack was filled with trouble. The crane was not happy, but it was determined to stay and help fix the sack. So the crane started to work and gradually the trouble was going away. Every night, the crane would spread its wings and take flight, repairing and patching up the sack until eventually it was back to being strong and right. The crane was proud of its job and it was very happy to have helped the troubled sack. It was able to spread its wings and soar higher and higher than ever before.
