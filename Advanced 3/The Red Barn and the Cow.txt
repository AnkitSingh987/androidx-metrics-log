Title: The Red Barn and the Cow

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. In the barn, there were many fat animals. There were fat cows, fat pigs, and fat chickens. They all lived happily in the barn. Every day, the kind farmer would come to fill their food bowls with yummy food. One sunny day, the fat cow said to the fat pig, "I am so full! I cannot eat more food!" The fat pig laughed and said, "Me too! The farmer always fills our bowls with so much food!" Just then, they heard a loud noise. The barn door opened, and a big, strange animal walked in. The fat animals had never seen this animal before. It had long legs, a long neck, and a big round body. The fat cow, fat pig, and fat chickens were very surprised. The strange animal said, "Hello, I am a fat horse! I was lost, but I found your barn. Can I stay here with you?" The fat animals looked at each other, and then the fat cow said, "Yes, you can stay with us. We have lots of food, and the farmer will fill your bowl too!" From that day on, the fat horse lived with the fat animals in the big red barn. They all ate together and played together. The farmer filled their bowls with food, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a happy boy named Tim. Tim loved to enjoy his day by riding the subway with his mom. They would go up and down, all around the town. Tim would look out the window and see cars, trees, and people. He was so happy! One day, Tim and his mom went on the subway again. But this time, they saw a sad girl named Sue. Sue was crying because she lost her toy. Tim wanted to help Sue, so he looked all around the subway. Then, he found the toy under a seat! Sue was happy now, and they all became friends. The next day, Tim, Sue, and their moms went to the subway again. But this time, something unexpected happened. The subway stopped moving and the lights went out. Everyone was scared. Tim and Sue held hands and tried to be brave. They learned that it's important to help each other during hard times. But, the subway didn't start moving again, and they all had to walk home. They were sad but learned a big lesson about being there for each other.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy girl named Jane. Jane had a beautiful dress that she loved. Jane's mom asked her if she wanted to go out and play but she said no. Instead, Jane wanted to stay home and include her dress in lots of fun activities. Jane and her mom had a chat about it. Jane's mom said, "But you won't have anyone to play with!" But Jane was determined. Jane said, "I'll find something to do with just my dress!" Jane's mom smiled and said, "Okay, I'll get some other things you can use to include your dress and you can have lots of fun!" Jane was so excited. When Jane went outside, she was able to include her dress in many activities. She played dress-up with her teddy bear, she made a dress for her cat out of a pillowcase, and she even made a dress for her dolly out of curtains! Hours went by and by the end of the day, Jane had had lots of fun, and she was no longer grumpy. Jane thanked her mom, who smiled and said, "I'm glad you had fun. Making up games to include your dress is a great way to have fun!"

Once upon a time, a little spider named Sam was very tired. He had been working all day to make a big web. Sam wanted to see how much his web could weigh. He called his friend, a small ant named Amy, to help him. "Hey Amy, can you help me see how much my web can weigh?" Sam asked. Amy thought it was a fun idea and agreed to help. They decided to find some things to put on the web to see if it could hold them. First, they put a leaf on the web. The web held the leaf just fine. Then they put a stick on the web. The web still held strong. Sam and Amy were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big gust of wind came and blew the leaf and stick off the web. The wind was so strong that it blew Sam and Amy away too. They held on to each other and landed in a soft pile of leaves. They were safe but very surprised. From that day on, Sam and Amy learned that sometimes, unexpected things happen. They still had fun trying to weigh the web, but they also learned to be more careful. And even though Sam was tired, he was happy to have a friend like Amy to help him.
