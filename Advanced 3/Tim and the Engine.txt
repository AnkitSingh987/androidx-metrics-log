Title: Tim and the Engine

One day, Tim and his dad went to the park. They saw a big red engine. Tim noticed that the engine looked weak. It was not shiny and new like the other engines. Tim asked his dad, "Daddy, why is the engine weak?" His dad said, "It is old and needs help to be strong again." Tim and his dad decided to help the weak engine. They cleaned it and painted it to make it look new. The engine was very happy and became strong again. Now, Tim and his dad visit the engine every week to make sure it stays strong and happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a modest cat named Tom. Tom was a very simple cat who loved to play with his friends. One day, Tom met a new friend, a little bird named Bella. Bella and Tom played together in the park. They had so much fun that they didn't want to stop. Tom said, "Bella, I am happy we are friends." Bella replied, "Me too, Tom." They laughed and played until the sun went down. One day, Bella told Tom she didn't like cats. Tom was sad and asked, "Why, Bella?" Bella said, "I heard cats are mean." Tom thought for a moment and said, "Not all cats are mean. I am a nice cat." Bella thought about it and decided to reverse her opinion. "You are right, Tom. You are a nice cat." From that day on, Tom and Bella became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a penguin. This penguin was very attractive. It had a shiny coat and a cute face. The penguin liked to go for walks. One day, the penguin decided to go to the park. It walked and walked until it found a nice place to play. The penguin saw other animals at the park too. They all wanted to be friends with the attractive penguin. The penguin had a fun day at the park. It played with its new friends and then went home. The penguin was happy to have had such a great day. And from then on, the penguin always went to the park to play with its friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gloomy day. The sky was very gray, and the rain was falling. A little girl named Lily wanted to go outside and play but she couldn't because of the rain. She felt sad and looked out the window, hoping the rain would stop soon. Later that day, the rain stopped and the sky became clear. Lily went outside to play. She saw something shiny on the ground. It was a little star! The star was lost and wanted to go back to the sky. Lily wanted to help the star, so she picked it up and held it in her hand. Lily had an idea. She climbed up a big hill with the star. When they got to the top, Lily threw the star up into the sky as hard as she could. The star flew up into the air and twinkled brightly. The star was so happy to be back in the sky, and Lily was happy to have helped her new friend. They waved goodbye and promised to meet again someday.

Once upon a time there was a mommy, a daddy and their little girl, Rosie. Rosie was three years old and loved to have a tea-party. One day, she wanted to celebrate. She asked her mommy if she could tie a knot in a ribbon to hang from the ceiling. Mommy said yes and gave her a special ribbon. Rosie was so excited that she clapped her hands and jumped around. Rosie tried to tie the knot, but she was too small and it was too difficult. She quickly got frustrated and started to cry. Daddy hugged Rosie and said, “Sweetheart, instead of getting frustrated it is better to take a break and try to do something small that you can do.” Rosie felt better and decided to try and make a paper heart. Rosie taped one end of the ribbon to the ceiling. She then danced around the room and held the other end of the ribbon in her hand. When she spun around too fast, she felt a bit dizzy, but still managed to tie a big knot in the ribbon. Mommy and Daddy clapped for Rosie and celebrated with her. The moral of the story is to not give up when something is too hard. Take a break and do something else, then try again!
