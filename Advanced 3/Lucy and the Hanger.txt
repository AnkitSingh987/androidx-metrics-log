Title: Lucy and the Hanger

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy had a red hanger. She loved her hanger so much. She put her pretty dress on it. One day, the hanger was sad. Lucy did not understand why. Lucy was anxious to help her hanger. She asked her mommy and daddy for help. They told her to give the hanger a hug. Lucy hugged her hanger tight. The hanger felt better. From that day on, Lucy and her hanger were very happy. They played together every day. The hanger was never sad again. Lucy understood that her hanger needed love, and they were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, a little car named Cara lived in a small town. Cara was a fearful car, always scared to go far. One day, her friend Tom the truck said, "Cara, let's go on a trip together!" Cara was scared but wanted to try. Before they left, they needed fuel. Cara and Tom went to the fuel place. They filled their bellies with fuel. Cara felt better and less scared. As they drove, Tom said, "Cara, let's measure how far we go." Cara liked that idea. They measured the road and went very far. In the end, Cara was not scared anymore. They both had a fun trip and went home happy.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Spot. Spot was a very friendly dog. He loved to play with his ball and run in the park. One day, Spot saw a new friend. It was a little bird named Tweet. Spot wanted to welcome Tweet to his park. Spot said, "Hi Tweet! Welcome to my park! Do you want to play with me?" Tweet said, "Yes, Spot! I would love to play with you!" So, Spot and Tweet played together all day. They ran, jumped, and had lots of fun. Before they went home, Spot made a promise to Tweet. He said, "Tweet, I promise to be your friend forever. We will always play together in this park." Tweet was very happy. From that day on, Spot and Tweet were the best of friends. They always kept their promise to play together and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a small island in the middle of the sea. On the island, there were many weird animals. They were all friends and liked to play together. One day, the animals decided to make a big surprise for their friend, Bird. They wanted to make her happy. So, they all worked together to build a big, beautiful nest for Bird to live in. When the nest was finished, they called Bird to come see it. Bird was so surprised and happy when she saw the nest. She thanked her friends for the wonderful gift. From that day on, all the animals on the island lived happily together.

Tom and Sam were friends who liked to play in the woods. They found a hut made of sticks and leaves. They decided to make it their secret place. One day, they saw a big truck with shiny wheels and loud noises. It stopped near the hut and a man came out. He had a big camera and a hat. "Hello, boys," he said. "I am a reporter. I want to watch your hut and take pictures. It is very special and interesting. Can I come in?" Tom and Sam were envious of the man and his truck. They wanted to have a camera and a hat too. They nodded and let him in. The man watched the hut and took many pictures. He asked Tom and Sam questions about how they made it and what they did there. He smiled and praised them. Tom and Sam felt proud and happy. They forgot about their envy and showed the man their toys and drawings. They told him stories and jokes. The man thanked them and gave them each a candy. He said goodbye and drove away. Tom and Sam waved and ate their candy. They looked at each other and smiled. They learned that their hut was special and interesting, and that they did not need to be envious of others. They were happy with what they had and with their friendship. They went back to their hut and played.
