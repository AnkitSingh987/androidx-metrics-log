Title: Ben and Mia and the Ship

Ben and Mia liked to play in the big cupboard in the kitchen. They pretended it was a ship and they were sailors. They had a flag, a map and a telescope. They also had a toy parrot that said funny things. One day, they decided to sail to a faraway island. They closed the cupboard door and made noises like the wind and the waves. They looked at the map and the telescope and said, "Ahoy, matey! Land ho!" But they did not know that their grandpa was in the kitchen too. He was deaf and he could not hear them. He wanted to make some tea and he opened the cupboard door. He was surprised to see Ben and Mia inside. "Hello, my little sailors," he said. "What are you doing in there?" Ben and Mia were scared. They thought he was a pirate who wanted to steal their ship. They shouted, "Shiver me timbers! It's the enemy! Quick, hide the treasure!" They grabbed the flag, the map and the telescope and ran out of the cupboard. They left the toy parrot behind. Grandpa laughed and picked up the parrot. He said, "Don't worry, I'm not a pirate. I'm your grandpa. Do you want some tea?" The parrot said, "Polly wants a cracker!" Grandpa smiled and gave the parrot a cracker. He said, "You're a clever bird. Maybe you can teach me some sailor words."

One day, a boy named Tim went to the gym. The gym was important to Tim because he liked to run and jump there. He was happy to play with his friends. While playing, Tim saw a big red button. He thought it would be fun to press it. When he pressed the button, something unexpected happened. The gym started to increase in size! It became so big that Tim and his friends could play even more games. But then, the gym stopped increasing. Tim and his friends were scared that they would be in trouble. Just when they thought they would be caught, a nice gym teacher came in. She told them that the button was a secret surprise for everyone. The gym was now bigger and better for all the kids to play in. Tim and his friends were happy and knew that the gym was even more important now.

Once upon a time, in a small stable, there lived a happy horse named Daisy. Daisy loved her home because it was perfect for her. Every day, she would wake up early and play with her friends. One day, Daisy woke up and saw a big box in the middle of the stable. She was very surprised. She called her friends to come and see the box. They all gathered around, wondering what could be inside. Slowly, Daisy opened the box. Inside, they found a little kitten! The kitten was scared, but Daisy and her friends were kind. They all became the best of friends and played together every day. The kitten had found a new perfect home in the stable with Daisy and her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy who loved to imagine. He would think about big cars, fast planes, and rich houses. He liked to play with his toy gear all day long. One sunny day, he took his toy gear outside to play. While he was playing, he saw a big, rich house. He thought, "I want to see that house!" So he walked closer to the house, still playing with his gear. As he got closer, he saw a big, mean dog. The dog saw the boy and his toy gear. The dog wanted the gear, so it ran fast to the boy. The boy was scared and tried to run away. But the dog was too fast and took the boy's toy gear. The boy was very sad and went home without his favorite toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little car. This car was very attractive. It was red and shiny like a big apple. The car was made of steel. It was strong and could go very fast. The car loved to speed down the road. One day, the car met a big truck. The truck was also made of steel, but it was not very attractive. The truck was slow and could not speed like the car. The car wanted to help the truck become fast and attractive too. So, the car and the truck went to a special place where they could get cleaned and painted. The truck got a new coat of paint and started to look more attractive. Then, they practiced speeding together. Soon, the truck could speed too. The car and the truck became best friends and sped down the road together, happy and attractive.
