Title: The Cricket and the Store

Once upon a time there was a cricket. He was very restless. He wanted to store the sunshine. He asked the little girl, "Can you help me store the sunshine?" The little girl smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you!" So, the cricket and the little girl looked around for a place to store the sunshine. Finally, they found an empty jar. The cricket hopped into the jar and said "Store it up, store it up!" The little girl carefully put the lid on the jar and the sunshine was stored away. The cricket was so happy that he was able to store the sunshine. He said to the little girl, "Thank you for helping me!" The little girl smiled and said, "You're welcome, Cricket!"

Once upon a time, there was a shy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, he lost his ball in the tall grass. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. Spot's friend, Bird, saw a package fall from the sky. Bird called Spot to come see. Inside the package, there was a new ball! Spot was so happy. He played with the new ball all day. When the sun went down, Spot saw something in the grass. It was his old ball! Spot picked it up and returned it to his house. Now, Spot had two balls to play with. Spot and Bird played together every day, and they were very happy.

One day, a girl named Amy put on her smart dress. She was very happy. She and her mom were going to a restaurant for lunch. Amy's mom said, "We need to zip your dress first." At the restaurant, they saw a big dog. The dog was very nice. It sat next to them. Amy and her mom talked to the dog. "Hello, dog!" said Amy. "Do you want some food?" Then, something unexpected happened. The dog stood up on two legs. It took off a mask, and it was Amy's dad! He had been hiding as the dog all along. He said, "Surprise! I wanted to make you both smile today." Amy and her mom laughed and laughed. They had a great time at the restaurant with dad.

Trevor was walking through the woods, looking for pine cones. He liked collecting them, and he thought it was fun! Suddenly, he saw something up ahead. It looked like a big mound of pine cones! He was so excited, he ran towards them. When he arrived, Trevor gasped. There were hundreds of pine cones, of all shapes and sizes! He couldn't believe it! Trevor started to sort through the pile, grouping the cones together. After a while, he looked up and saw a large, modest pine tree. He realised it must have been the tree the cones had come from! Trevor smiled and thanked the tree for the wonderful gift. Suddenly, a voice broke the silence. A friendly squirrel had been watching Trevor and asked, "What are you doing?" Trevor beamed and told the squirrel proudly, "I'm sorting pine cones!"

One day, a zebra named Zeb was very sleepy. He had a book that he liked to read before bed. Zeb's friend, a bird named Blue, would help him read the big words. They read the book every night, and it made Zeb very happy. One day, Zeb could not find his book. He looked and looked, but it was gone. Zeb was very sad and did not want to sleep without reading his book. Blue saw how sad Zeb was and had an idea to make him happy again. Blue told Zeb to close his eyes and listen. Blue started to tell the story from the book. Zeb was happy to hear the story, but then something unexpected happened. Blue changed the story! In this new story, Zeb was a hero who saved other animals. Zeb loved the new story even more than the book. From that day on, Zeb and Blue made up new stories every night before bed.

One day, a little dog felt anxious. He was shy and did not like to play with other dogs. Yesterday, he tried to play with a big dog and it made him feel embarrassed. The big dog laughed at him because he was small and scared. The little dog wanted to make friends, but he did not know how. He saw a small cat playing alone. The little dog thought, "Maybe the cat will be my friend." So, he went to the cat and asked, "Will you be my friend?" The small cat was happy to have a friend. They played together all day. The little dog was not anxious or embarrassed anymore. He was happy to have a new friend who did not laugh at him. They became best friends and played together every day.
