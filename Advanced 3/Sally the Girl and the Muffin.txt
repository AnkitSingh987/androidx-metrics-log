Title: Sally the Girl and the Muffin

Once there was a little girl named Sally. One day, Sally wanted to dance. She put on her favorite pretty dress, and ran around her house. She twirled, spun, and hopped around. She laughed and felt so happy! Then, Sally went to the kitchen. She looked around and couldn't believe her eyes. There were big muffins on the counter. They were pink and looked delicious! Sally was so excited and she grabbed one. With a big bite and a smile, Sally ate the muffin. But then, Sally noticed something. Her mom's face was serious. Sally thought she had done something wrong. She put down the muffin and became very quiet. She didn't want to dance anymore. But then, Sally's mom smiled. She said, "I'm so proud of you for dancing and being so happy. Here, why don't you have another muffin?" Sally smiled, and they both laughed. She knew that everything was okay. So Sally danced and ate muffins, and was so happy!

Once there was a little girl called Lily. She was only 3 years old. One day, Lily went for a walk in the park. As she walked, she watched the sky. It was a beautiful colour - a deep blue. Suddenly, the sky started to glow. Lily's face lit up with amazement. The sky was turning a beautiful orange. Lily stared at it in awe. Then, the sky started to turn a bright pink. Lily was so excited - she had never seen colours like this before. The sky started to turn cold, and the colours were fading. Lily wanted to stay and keep watching, but it was getting late. She said goodbye to the sky, then went home to bed. In her dreams, Lily could still see the sky glowing in all its beautiful colours.

Once upon a time, there was a funny bunny. The funny bunny liked to move fast. He hopped and jumped all day long. He had a big smile on his face. The funny bunny was very happy. One day, the funny bunny found a puzzle. The puzzle had many colors. The funny bunny wanted to put the puzzle together. So, he sat down and started to move the pieces. He moved them here and there. The funny bunny was having fun. After some time, the funny bunny finished the puzzle. He was so proud of himself. The puzzle had a picture of a big, happy sun. The funny bunny smiled and hopped away. He knew he could do anything if he tried.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box that was always open. One day, he found a small piece of coal in his toy box. Tim was very curious about the coal. Tim went to his mom and asked, "Mom, what is this black thing?" His mom said, "That is coal, Tim. It is very dirty. You should close your toy box so it stays clean." Tim didn't listen to his mom and left the toy box open. Later that day, Tim played with the coal and his toys. The coal made his toys very dirty. Tim's mom saw the dirty toys and was very sad. She said, "Tim, I told you to close your toy box. Now your toys are dirty, and we cannot clean them." Tim felt bad for not listening to his mom. The toys stayed dirty, and Tim learned to always close his toy box.

One day, a dog named Max went to search for his favorite toy. It was a red ball. Max looked under the bed, behind the door, and in his toy box. But he could not find the red ball anywhere. Max felt sad. Max's friend, a little girl named Lily, saw him looking sad. She asked, "Max, why are you sad?" Max barked and showed her that he could not find his red ball. Lily said, "Don't worry, Max. I will help you search for your red ball." They looked everywhere in the house. Then, Lily found the red ball. It was hiding behind Max's heel! Max was so happy. He jumped and barked, "Thank you, Lily!" But when Max tried to play with the ball, it did not bounce. The ball was dead. Max and Lily looked at each other, feeling surprised. Lily had an idea. She said, "Max, let's go to the store and buy a new red ball!" Max wagged his tail and barked happily. They went to the store and bought a new red ball that bounced very high. Max and Lily played with the new red ball all day long, and they were both very happy.
