Title: Lucy the Kitten and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten named Lucy. Lucy loved to play with her ball and run around the house. One day, Lucy saw a big pool of water. She wanted to swim and have fun. Lucy jumped into the pool, but the water was heavy. She could not swim! She felt scared and cried for help. A big fish heard her cry and came to the pool. The fish saw Lucy and wanted to help her. The big fish swam to Lucy and said, "Hold on to my tail." Lucy held on tight and the fish pulled her out of the water. Lucy was safe and happy. The fish and Lucy became best friends. They played together every day, and Lucy never jumped into the heavy water again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to prepare food for his friends. One day, he wanted to make a great luxury meal for them. He asked his friend, Sam, "Do you want to help me make a great meal?" Sam said, "Yes, I want to help you!" Tim and Sam worked hard to make the food. They made a big cake, some tasty soup, and some yummy sandwiches. They felt very happy. But when they were done, they saw that the food was not good. The cake was too big, the soup was too hot, and the sandwiches were too small. They were sad because they could not have the great luxury meal they wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a huge dog named Max. Max was very smart, so his family got him a tutor. The tutor was a nice lady who helped Max learn new things. Max and the tutor had fun playing and learning together. One day, Max saw a big ball. He wanted the ball very much, so he demanded it from the tutor. The tutor said, "No, Max. That ball is not yours." But Max was not happy. He wanted the ball and did not listen to the tutor. Max tried to get the ball, but he was too big and clumsy. He knocked over a lot of things and made a big mess. The tutor was very sad, and Max's family was angry. They told Max he could not have the tutor anymore. Max was sad too, because he lost his friend and did not get the ball.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red bird. The bird had a long neck. One day, the bird saw a naughty cat. The cat was trying to take a toy from a little boy. The bird did not like this. The bird hoped to help the little boy. The bird flew down and tried to tell the cat to stop. But, the cat did not listen. The bird had an idea. The bird used its long neck to grab the toy from the cat. The cat was surprised! Then, something unexpected happened. The cat said sorry to the bird and the little boy. The cat did not want to be naughty anymore. The bird, the cat, and the little boy became friends. They all played with the toy together and had fun.

Once, there was a little girl called Mary. Mary was small and liked to play outside. One day, when the sun was at its highest, which is at noon, Mary decided to go and visit her friend, Lily. She picked a bunch of flowers and ran to her house. When she reached the door, she knocked on it, but no one answered. "Maybe Lily isn't at home," Mary thought. Then she noticed something strange. The window was very small, but a big cat fit through the opening. "How did he fit through the window?" Mary wondered. Just then, Lily opened the door. "Oh, Mary, come in, I was just tidying up," she said. Mary asked Lily about the big cat in her window. "That's my neighbour's cat, Tom. Tom's been coming in through my window to make a mess of my things," she said. Mary was amazed. "But how can he fit?" Mary asked. "It's simple: if you have a really little hole, even a big cat can fit," said Lily. Mary quickly understood the moral lesson: no matter how little something is, it can cause a lot of trouble if you don't pay attention. From that day on, Mary stayed more alert and never let anything in through the window without first making sure it was safe.
