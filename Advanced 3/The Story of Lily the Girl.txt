Title: The Story of Lily the Girl

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to make things with her hands. One day, she made a toy for her best friend, Max. Lily wanted to give the toy to Max, so she went to visit him. On her way, she saw a big dog. Lily was worried. She didn't know if the dog was nice or not. She hid behind a tree and waited for the dog to go away. After the dog left, Lily continued her visit to Max's house. When she got there, she gave Max the toy she made. Max was so happy! They played with the toy all day. Lily was not worried anymore. They had a great day together.

Once there was a boy with dark hair. His dad always said that the minerals in the boy’s hair gave him super powers. The boy never believed it – until one special day. It was a dark night one night, when the boy was walking home. Suddenly, he saw something out of the corner of his eye! It was a tiny mineral! It was glowing brightly. The boy touched the mineral and he knew right away that he had been given a very special power. The power to kiss things and make them disappear. The boy was so excited! He ran to his dad and told him the news. Daddy smiled, and he said, “Well, my son, it looks like you’ve discovered your super power. Now, be sure to use it for good!” The boy thought about all the things he could do with his new power. He decided he would start his journey of discovery by kissing the stars that shone bright in the dark night sky. He closed his eyes, made a wish, and then he did it – he kissed the stars! The stars glittered and sparkled, as if they knew he had just released their magic. The boy had done it – thanks to the mysterious mineral!

Jill was a three year old living with her family. She loved exploring and learning new things. One sunny day, she was playing with her best friend Ethan outside. They were running around, laughing and having fun. Suddenly, Jill picked up a stick from the ground and touched an electric fence with it. She felt a sharp shock! Ethan quickly looked at her and said: "Jill, why did you do that? You should never touch things without knowing what they are!". Jill was confused but she learnt an important lesson. She now knew to always be careful and to ask questions before touching something new. Ethan was feeling lively and shared a warm smile with her. Then, he said: “Let's go play a different game – one that won't shock us!" Jill smiled back and they ran off to have more fun, but this time with safety in mind:-)

One day, a girl named Lily found her mom's makeup. She saw many colors, but she liked the green one most. She wanted to do something fun with it. Lily had an idea. She called her friends to help her. They wanted to organize a green makeup party. They all came to her house and put on the green makeup. They looked very funny! They played games and had a lot of fun. Their moms and dads laughed when they saw them. They took pictures of their green faces. The green makeup party was a big hit!

One day, a boy named Tim had an exam at school. He was upset because he did not want to take the exam. He told his mom, "Mom, I am upset. I don't want to go to school today." His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tim. You will do well in your exam." At school, Tim's teacher gave him a big paper with many questions. Tim looked at the paper and felt more upset. His friend, Sam, saw Tim and said, "Don't be upset, Tim. We can do this together." Tim smiled and felt better. After the exam, Tim went home and told his mom about his day. His mom was happy that Tim felt better. She gave Tim a gift to make him even happier. Tim opened the gift and saw a toy car. The car was wrapped in a big, shiny paper. Tim loved the car and played with it all day.
