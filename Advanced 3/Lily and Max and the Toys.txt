Title: Lily and Max and the Toys

Lily and Max were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a purple dragon that could make sounds and move its wings. They named it Fluffy and took turns to hug it and talk to it. One day, their mom told them to behave and share Fluffy, because she had to go to the store and buy some food. She said she would be back soon and gave them a kiss. Lily and Max said okay and promised to be good. But as soon as mom left, they started to fight over Fluffy. Lily wanted to play with it first, but Max said it was his turn. They pulled and pushed and yelled at each other, and Fluffy got scared and angry. It roared and bit Lily's hand, making her cry. Max laughed and grabbed Fluffy, but Fluffy bit his ear, making him scream. Mom heard the noise and came back quickly. She saw Lily and Max crying and bleeding, and Fluffy lying on the floor, broken and silent. She was very sad and angry. She took Lily and Max to the doctor and threw Fluffy in the trash. She said they were naughty and selfish, and they would not get any new toys for a long time. Lily and Max felt sorry and wished they had behaved and shared Fluffy. They missed their purple dragon and hoped it would come back in their dreams. But it never did.

Tom and Sue liked to play in the park. They liked to dig in the sand and look for shiny things. Sometimes they found coins or buttons or rocks. They called them minerals. They wanted to find the best minerals ever. One day, they saw a big gray rock in the sand. It was very heavy and hard. They thought it was a special mineral. They tried to lift it, but it was too heavy. They asked a big boy to help them. He said he would, but he wanted to see the mineral first. He looked at the rock and laughed. He said it was not a mineral. He said it was a piece of concrete. He said they were silly and did not know anything. He said he would not help them. He walked away, still laughing. Tom and Sue felt very bad. They felt embarrassed. They did not like the big boy. They did not like the gray rock. They wanted to find a real mineral. They decided to dig somewhere else. They dug and dug until they found something shiny and colorful. It was a necklace with many beads. It was very pretty and sparkly. They thought it was a wonderful mineral. They were very happy. They forgot about the big boy and the gray rock. They put the necklace around Sue's neck and ran to show their mom. They felt proud and smart. They liked the necklace. They liked each other. They liked to play in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big lily. The lily was in a deep pond. Many fish and frogs lived in the pond. The lily was very pretty. It was the most beautiful lily in the pond. One day, a little girl came to the pond. She saw the lily and liked it a lot. She wanted to lift the lily out of the pond. The girl tried and tried, but the lily was too deep in the water. The girl was sad and sat down by the pond. Then, something unexpected happened. A friendly frog jumped out of the water and onto the lily. The frog helped the girl lift the lily out of the water. The girl was so happy! She thanked the frog and took the lily home. The frog and the girl became best friends. They played together every day by the pond.

Once upon a time there was a little mouse who wanted to weigh himself. He found a big rock and placed it on the ground. He then stood on one end and tested to see if the rock would balance on one side. As the mouse weighed himself, a mosquito buzzed by. The little mouse was scared and quickly ran back to his hole. He told his mom that the mosquito was dangerous. His mom said, "That's why it's important to be aware of your surroundings before you act." The little mouse learned a big lesson that day. It's important to watch around and make sure it is a safe environment before doing something that could be dangerous.
