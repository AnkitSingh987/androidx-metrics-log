Title: Lily and Tom and the Toys

Lily and Tom are playing with their toys in the living room. Lily has a doll and Tom has a car. They are having fun making noises and stories with their toys. Suddenly, Tom sneezes loudly. He covers his nose with his hand. Lily looks at him and asks, "Are you sick, Tom?" Tom shakes his head. "No, I'm not sick. I just sneezed. Sometimes I sneeze when there is dust or fur or something in the air." Lily nods. She remembers that her mom told her that sneezing is a way to get rid of things that bother your nose. She says, "Maybe there is dust or fur or something in the air here. Maybe we should clean our toys." Tom agrees. He says, "Yes, maybe we should. Cleaning our toys is a good idea. Maybe we will find something interesting." They put their toys in a basket and carry it to the kitchen. They ask their mom for a cloth and some water. They start to wipe their toys with the cloth and the water. As they clean their toys, they find some interesting things. They find a button, a feather, a coin, and a sticker. They show each other their findings and wonder where they came from. They decide to keep their interesting things in a small box. They finish cleaning their toys and put them back in the living room. They are happy that they cleaned their toys and found some interesting things. They also hope that they won't sneeze anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a fragile bird named Whistler. Whistler loved to whistle all day long. She had a pretty nest on a big tree. One sunny day, Whistler met a friendly worm. The worm lived in the soft soil under the tree. They said "hi" to each other. Whistler asked, "Can you whistle like me?" The worm smiled and said, "No, I cannot whistle, but I can wiggle in the soil." Whistler and the worm became good friends. They played together every day. Whistler would whistle happy songs, and the worm would wiggle and dance in the soil. They had lots of fun and made many more friends in the village. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big box of crayons. They liked to design pretty pictures together. They were very useful to the little girl who owned them. She loved to draw with them every day. One day, the crayons began to argue about who was the best color. This made them very unhappy. They did not want to work together anymore. The little girl was sad because her crayons were not making nice pictures anymore. They were having a color war. The little girl had an idea. She took out a big piece of paper and started to draw. She used every crayon in the box. The crayons saw how beautiful the picture was when they all worked together. They stopped their war and became friends again. The little girl was happy, and the crayons went back to being useful and creating beautiful designs together.

Once upon a time, there was a cold day. On that cold day, two friends, Andy and Joy, were playing together. They had a great idea - to make a whip to play with. They found a stick and used a spray to make it sticky. Then they wound some string around the stick. Soon, the whip was ready! Andy and Joy had so much fun playing with the whip. They were running around and whipping each other with the whip. The whip flew back and forth between them. But eventually the day got colder, so they decided to go home. But they never forgot their fun time with the whip.

Lily was a princess who lived in a big palace. She liked to play with her dolls and her dog, Max. One day, she heard a loud noise outside. She ran to the window and saw a big truck with many boxes. "What is that?" she asked Max. Max barked and wagged his tail. He wanted to go and see. "Come, Max, let's go and find out," Lily said. She put on her shoes and her coat and ran down the stairs. She met her father, the king, in the hall. He was holding a paper and looked confused. "Hello, Lily. Do you know what this is?" he asked, showing her the paper. Lily looked at the paper. It had many words and numbers that she did not understand. "No, Daddy, I don't know. What is it?" she asked. "It is a bill. It says we have to pay a lot of money for the truck and the boxes. But I don't know what they are for. I did not order them," he said. "Maybe they are a surprise for us," Lily said. "Can we open them and see?" The king smiled and nodded. He took Lily and Max to the truck and opened one of the boxes. Inside, there were many books. "Books!" Lily exclaimed. "I love books! Can I have one, Daddy?" "Of course, my dear. You can have as many as you like. Maybe they are not such a bad surprise after all," he said. He opened another box and found more books. And another, and another. All the boxes had books. "Wow, Daddy, we have so many books! We can read them all and learn many things," Lily said. "Yes, we can, Lily. But I still wonder who sent them to us. And why they sent so many. And how we are going to pay for them," he said. He looked at the paper again and scratched his head. He was still confused.
