Title: Sue the Girl and the Lollipop

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big lollipop. It was very pretty and colorful. She was so happy! Sue wanted to eat the lollipop, but it was too big for her. So, she decided to imagine eating it instead. Sue closed her eyes and imagined the sweet taste of the lollipop. She could see herself eating it in a dark room. But then, something unexpected happened! The lollipop in her imagination started to glow! It was not dark anymore. The room was now filled with bright colors from the glowing lollipop. Sue opened her eyes and saw that the lollipop in her hand was now smaller. It was just the right size for her to eat! She was so surprised and happy. Sue learned that sometimes, when you imagine something, it can come true. And from that day on, Sue always believed in the power of her imagination.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to draw. They had many crayons of different colors. One day, they decided to have a race. Who could draw the best picture in five minutes? "Ready, set, go!" Lily said, and they both grabbed their crayons and paper. Lily chose a red crayon and started to draw a big heart. Ben chose a blue crayon and started to draw a fast car. They drew very quickly, trying to make their pictures look nice. They did not look at each other's paper. They wanted to surprise each other. But then, something bad happened. Lily's red crayon broke in half. She gasped and looked for another red crayon. But there was none. All the other crayons were ordinary colors, like yellow and green and brown. Lily did not like those colors. She wanted a red crayon. "Ben, can I have your red crayon, please?" Lily asked, looking at Ben's paper. She saw that he had a red crayon, but he was using it to draw the car's wheels. "No, you can't. It's mine. I need it for my car," Ben said, holding the crayon tightly. He did not want to share it with Lily. He wanted to win the race. Lily felt sad and angry. She thought Ben was being mean. She did not want to be his friend anymore. She threw down her paper and ran away, crying. Ben felt sorry and confused. He did not mean to hurt Lily's feelings. He just wanted to finish his car. He looked at his paper and realized that his car was not very good. It was just a blue rectangle with red circles. He looked at Lily's paper and saw that her heart was very pretty. It was big and red and had a smiley face. He wished he had given her his red crayon. He picked up his paper and ran after Lily. He wanted to say sorry and make up with her. He wanted to be her friend again. He hoped she would forgive him.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Tom. Tom loved to go to the park and play with his friends. One day, while they played, one of his friends dared Tom to climb the highest tree. Tom hesitated but he was brave so he decided to take up the challenge. Tom started to climb the tree but it wasn't easy. His feet slipped and the branches scratched his hands. When he reached the top, he felt so proud! He was about to shout for joy when his eyes caught a purple butterfly. It was so beautiful! Tom quickly realized that he had learned an important lesson. It is not always a good idea to do something even when your friends dare you. It can be dangerous and you may get hurt. So it's always important to think twice and use your own judgement before you do something.

One day, a big parade was in town. All the kids were happy. They wanted to see the big parade. There were big cars, people in funny clothes, and music. It was a fun day for all. A little boy named Mark was at the parade with his mom. He was so excited! He saw a hidden spot behind a big tree. He said to his mom, "Mom, can I go to that spot?" His mom said, "Yes, but don't go too far, Mark." Mark went to the hidden spot. He could see the parade very well. But then, something unexpected happened! A big dog came out from behind the tree. The dog was part of the parade. The dog said, "Woof! Hi, Mark! Do you want to join the parade with me?" Mark was so surprised! He said, "Yes, I want to join the parade!" Mark and the big dog walked in the parade together. They had so much fun. The people in the parade and the people watching all clapped and cheered for Mark and the big dog. It was the best day ever for Mark.
