Title: Tom the Boy and the Park

One day, a boy named Tom went outside to play. The sun was shining and it was warm. Tom saw a big missile in the park. He did not know what it was, but he thought it looked fun. So, he decided to skip near it and play with it. Tom met a girl named Sue. Sue saw the missile too. She said, "Tom, let's skip around the missile!" They both started to skip and laugh. They were having a lot of fun. But, the missile started to make a loud noise. Tom and Sue stopped skipping and looked at it. They did not know that the missile was not a toy. The missile went up in the sky and made a big boom. Tom and Sue were very scared and ran away. The park was not safe anymore.

George was an obedient boy. He liked to do what his Mum told him to do. One day, his Mum said "George, shut the door" so he did. He put the key in the lock and turned it until it was shut. After lunch, George's Mum made him a sandwich. It was delicious! George loved eating sandwiches. He ate it in two bites and asked for more. That afternoon, George wanted to play with his toy car but remembered he had to be obedient. So he shut the box and went to fetch his books. He started writing and had a great time. He was proud that he was being obedient.

Once upon a time, there was a large dog named Spot. Spot loved to listen to his human, Sam, talk. One day, Sam got a new jacket with a zipper. Spot saw the zipper and thought it was a toy. He tried to play with it, but it got stuck. Sam heard the noise and came to see what happened. Spot looked sad because he did not want to make Sam upset. Sam smiled and helped Spot get the zipper unstuck. They both laughed and played together. From that day on, Spot learned to listen carefully when Sam talked about new things. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He wanted to yield with his friends, but he was too scared. He felt ashamed. He walked slowly until he reached the landscape. The landscape was full of flowers, trees and green hills. He was amazed by its beauty. The little boy was too scared to yield. He saw his friends playing but he stayed alone in the landscape. Until finally his courage grew and he ventured forward! He took a few steps, then a few more and he suddenly found himself yielding with his friends! The little boy was so happy and no longer felt ashamed. He urged his friends to join him and they all yielded in the beautiful landscape until the sun went down. The little boy was no longer scared, he felt excited and confident. From then on, he always yielded with his friends in the landscape, feeling brave and happy each time.

Once upon a time, in a faraway land, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because he was going to visit his friend, Sam. Sam lived in a big house near the woods. Tim had never been to the woods before, so he asked his mom if he could go. His mom said yes, but she told him to be careful. Tim and Sam played all day long in the woods. They had so much fun running, jumping, and laughing. But then, they saw a big, dark cave. Tim was scared, but Sam wanted to go inside. Tim remembered his mom's words and requested Sam not to go in. But Sam didn't listen and went inside the cave. As they went deeper into the cave, it got darker and darker. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. It was a big, scary monster! Tim and Sam were so scared that they ran out of the cave as fast as they could. They didn't stop running until they reached Sam's house. From that day on, Tim and Sam never went near the cave again. They learned that sometimes, it's better to listen to the advice of others, especially when they are trying to keep you safe.
