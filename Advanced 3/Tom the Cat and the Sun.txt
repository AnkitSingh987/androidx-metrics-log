Title: Tom the Cat and the Sun

One day, a little cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a perfect, big hedge. The hedge was green and tall. Tom wanted to see what was behind the hedge. As Tom got closer to the hedge, he saw something move. He blinked his eyes to see better. Behind the hedge, there was a small, cute bunny. The bunny blinked back at Tom. Tom and the bunny became friends. They played together by the perfect hedge all day. When the sun went down, they said goodbye. Tom went home, happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a lab. The lab was very tidy. In the lab, there was a big, fun mat. The mat was soft and colorful. One day, a little frog came to the lab. The frog saw the mat and wanted to jump on it. The frog jumped high, and then jumped low. The frog was so happy and had lots of fun. Soon, other animals came to the lab. They saw the frog and the mat. They all wanted to jump too. So, they took turns jumping on the mat. They all had a great time in the tidy lab.

Once upon a time, there was a busy robot named Robby. Robby liked to count. He counted everything he saw. One day, Robby went for a walk in the park. In the park, Robby saw many things to count. He counted big trees and little trees. He counted red flowers and yellow flowers. He counted dogs and cats playing. Robby was very happy counting everything. Then, Robby met a little girl named Lily. Lily saw Robby counting and wanted to learn too. Robby taught Lily how to count. They counted together and became good friends. From that day on, Robby and Lily counted everything they saw, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten named Fluffy. Fluffy was playing with her toy mouse. She was very happy. One day, Fluffy saw an angry dog. The dog wanted to take her toy mouse away. Fluffy got scared and hid under a big chair. The angry dog tried to reach Fluffy, but he was too big. Fluffy was brave and decided to snap at the dog. The dog got scared and ran away. Fluffy was safe and could play with her toy mouse again.

Ben and Lily liked to play with their toy automobiles. They had many different kinds, big and small, red and blue, fast and slow. They made roads and bridges with blocks and pretended to drive around. One day, Ben's favorite automobile broke. He was very sad. He tried to fix it, but it did not work. He showed it to Lily and said, "Look, my automobile is broken. It has a big crack and a wheel is missing. What can I do?" Lily thought for a moment. She had an idea. She said, "Maybe we can heal your automobile. We can use some glue and a sticker and a new wheel. Do you want to try?" Ben nodded. He liked Lily's idea. They went to find the things they needed. They found some glue and a sticker with a star on it. They also found a wheel that belonged to another toy. They worked together to heal Ben's automobile. They put glue on the crack and stuck the sticker on it. They put the wheel on the axle and spun it. It worked! Ben was very happy. He hugged Lily and said, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend. You helped me heal my automobile. Now it looks even better than before. It has a star and a wild wheel. Let's play with it." Lily smiled. She was happy too. She said, "You're welcome, Ben. I'm glad you like it. Your automobile is very cool. Let's play with it. We can have a race." They took their automobiles and raced on the roads and bridges. They had a lot of fun. They were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue. They were friends and liked to play together every day. Tim and Sue were happy and had a lot of fun. One day, Tim and Sue decided they wanted to improve their playtime. They wanted to pretend they were in a marriage. So, they dressed up in big clothes and said nice things to each other. But Tim felt embarrassed because his clothes were too big and funny. Even though Tim was embarrassed, Sue told him that it was okay. They played and laughed all day, and their friendship grew even more. They learned that improving their playtime and trying new things made them better friends. And they lived happily ever after.
