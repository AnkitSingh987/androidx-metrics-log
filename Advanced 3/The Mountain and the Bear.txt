Title: The Mountain and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a big mountain. It was a dull mountain, not very pretty. A little boy named Tim lived near the mountain. He liked to play and listen to the birds sing. One day, Tim went to the mountain. He heard a voice say, "Hello, Tim!" Tim was surprised. He did not know who was talking. He looked around and saw a big, friendly bear. "Hi, bear!" said Tim. They became friends and played all day. Then, the bear showed Tim a secret. The dull mountain was not dull at all. The bear pushed a big rock, and a door opened. Inside, the mountain was full of pretty colors and shiny rocks. Tim and the bear were happy and played in the colorful mountain every day.

One day, a poor little boy named Tim found a basketball. He was so happy because he loved to play. He took the basketball to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Tim and his friends played a fun game. They threw the basketball into a hoop. They laughed and had a good time. But then, something unexpected happened. The basketball got stuck in a tree! Tim and his friends tried to get the basketball down, but they could not reach it. Tim started to weep because he was sad. His friends felt sad too. Just when they thought the basketball was lost, a big bird flew by. The big bird saw the basketball in the tree and wanted to help. It used its beak to get the basketball out of the tree. Tim and his friends were so surprised and happy! They thanked the big bird and played more basketball. They all had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a messy room. In the room, there was a big switch on the wall. A little girl named Amy lived in the house. She always wanted to know what the switch did. One day, Amy's friend Tom came to play. They played with toys and had a lot of fun. Then, Amy showed Tom the switch. "Let's send something to the switch," she said. Tom nodded, and they both threw a toy at the switch. The switch turned on! Suddenly, the messy room started to clean itself! Toys went back into their boxes, and the floor became shiny. Amy and Tom were very surprised. They laughed and clapped as they watched the room become clean. From that day on, they knew that the switch was magical.

Once upon a time, there was a reindeer named Bob. He was a light reindeer with a big heart. Bob always helped his friends. Today, he was going to prepare for a big party. Bob and his friends worked very hard. They got balloons, made cake, and played games. Everyone was happy and excited for the party. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the balloons away. Bob and his friends were sad. They thought the party was ruined. But then, Bob had an idea. He told his friends to hold hands and jump. Together, they jumped high into the sky. They caught the balloons and saved the party. Everyone was happy again. Bob and his friends learned that when they work together, they can do anything. And they all had the best party ever.

Once there was a little girl called Rebecca and she was three years old. She was standing outside the stable, looking at the animals. She wanted to get closer to the animals, so she kneeled down in front of them. Suddenly, an old man appeared. He wasn't nice, he was mean and he shouted at Rebecca. He said, "What are you doing here, you stupid little girl?" Rebecca was scared and she started to cry. The old man said, "If you want to go in the stable, you must find the key to the door." Rebecca tried to find the key all around the stable, but she didn't find it. She started to cry again. The old man said, "If you don't find the key, you can never enter the stable again. What a stupid mistake you've made." Then he walked away. Rebecca was all alone at the stable now and she was very sad. She never did find the key and she couldn't enter the stable again. She cried and cried until she fell asleep.
