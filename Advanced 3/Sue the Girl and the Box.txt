Title: Sue the Girl and the Box

One day, a little girl named Sue found a long box. She opened the box and saw a muffin inside. Sue thought it was a yummy muffin. She wanted to eat it but not now. So, she decided to hide the muffin. Sue went to her room and put the muffin under her bed. She thought it was a good hiding place. Later, her friend Tom came to play. They played with toys and had fun. Then, Sue remembered the muffin. Sue said to Tom, "I have a yummy muffin. Do you want to eat it with me?" Tom said, "Yes, I like muffins!" Sue looked under her bed, but the muffin was not there. They looked everywhere in the room. Finally, they found the muffin behind a big toy. Sue and Tom were happy. They sat down and ate the yummy muffin together. They laughed and played more after eating the muffin. It was a fun day.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, named Amy and Jack. They were best buddies and loved to play together. One day, they were running around in a meadow and laughing. Amy jumped and shouted, "I'm the fastest! I'm gonna win the race!" Jack giggled and chased after her. Around and around they went, and Amy kept thinking that she was gonna win. But then Jack did a sudden spin and made Amy dizzy. He ran ahead and laughed all the way across the finish line. "I won!" Jack said, beaming. Amy was panting and dizzy, but she was also smiling. "That was so much fun! Let's race again!" she said. The two of them laughed as they lined up to race one more time.

Once upon a time, there was a unique bird named Spike. Spike was very special because he could heal others. He lived in a big tree with his friends. One day, Spike found a hurt bug. He wanted to help the bug, so he used his heal power. But something unexpected happened! Instead of healing the bug, Spike turned it into a big, pretty flower. Spike was sad and confused. He didn't know why his heal power didn't work. He went to his friend, the wise owl, for help. The wise owl told Spike that his power changed because he became more unique. Spike was happy to have a new, special power. He went back to the big tree and turned more bugs into pretty flowers. All his friends loved the new flowers, and they all played together in the colorful garden.

Once upon a time there was a black pick. It was small and so shiny, the sun shone off it brightly. One day a little girl named Lucy saw the pick. She wanted to pick it up and take it with her. But it was so very high, she couldn't reach. Lucy wanted the pick so badly that she began to hope. She hoped and hoped and hoped, until suddenly the pick started to move. The pick moved slowly down and got close enough that Lucy could reach it. With a smile so bright and happy, she grabbed the pick and held it tight. Lucy knew that her hope had made it possible. She was happy to have the pretty black pick in her hands and she never let it go.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a soft blanket. He was so excited because it was very cozy. He wanted to share his new blanket with his friends, so he invited them to his house. They all sat on the soft blanket and watched a film on TV. They laughed and talked while they watched the film. "Wow, this blanket is so soft!" said Tim's friend, Sam. "I wish I had one like it." "I like sharing my soft blanket with you," Tim replied. "It makes me happy to see you all enjoy it." After the film, they played games and ate snacks on the soft blanket. They had a great time together. When it was time for Tim's friends to go home, they all thanked him for sharing his soft blanket and the fun film. The moral of the story is that sharing can make you and others happy.
