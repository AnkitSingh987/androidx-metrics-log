Title: Tim the Boy and the Exam

One day, a boy named Tim had a big exam at school. He was not happy because exams are dull. Tim's mom told him, "You must go and do your best." So, Tim went to school with a sad face. At school, Tim's teacher gave him a paper with many questions. Tim looked at the paper and saw something strange. All the questions were about loading trucks with toys. Tim loved trucks and toys! He started to feel happy. Tim's friend, Sam, looked at Tim and said, "This is not dull at all!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, it is fun!" They both finished the exam and went outside to play. The exam was not dull after all, and Tim learned that sometimes, unexpected things can happen.

Lilly was so excited, because it was her birthday! Her mum had promised her a special birthday cake. Mum carefully brought the cake out from the kitchen. It was a lovely cake with pretty pink and white decorations. Lilly's eyes lit up as she said "wow, it looks delicious!" Mum smiled and said "it sure does, go ahead and blow out the candle!" Lilly carefully blew out the candle and suddenly the cake began to move. Lilly and her mum looked at each other in surprise. Suddenly the cake revealed a beautiful surprise - a shining gold necklace with a tiny heart! Lily gasped in surprise, and her mum said "it's for you, my lovely Lilly". Lilly was so happy and hugged her mum. She said thank you and put the necklace on. It was a lovely reminder of a very special birthday - one she would never forget!

Once upon a time, in a land far away, lived a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot. They loved to play and explore the woods near their home. One sunny day, they found a map. The map showed where a treasure was hidden. "Let's go find the treasure," Tim said to Spot. They walked and walked until they found a big, old tree. The map said the treasure was under the tree. Tim and Spot dug and dug. Then, they found a big box. Inside the box was the treasure. It was full of brilliant, shiny stones. Tim and Spot were very happy. They went home to discuss what to do with the treasure. "We can share the treasure with our friends," Tim told Spot. And so, they did. Everyone was happy and had a big party to celebrate.

Tom and Anna like to play with their toys in the big box. They have cars, dolls, balls and books. But sometimes they fight over the toys and make a lot of noise. Mom and Dad do not like that. One day, Mom and Dad have to go out for a while. They tell Tom and Anna to be good and not to fight. They also tell them not to touch the lock on the door. The lock is for keeping bad people out. Tom and Anna say yes and wave goodbye. But when Mom and Dad are gone, Tom and Anna start to fight again. Tom wants the red car and Anna wants the blue doll. They pull and push and yell. They do not hear the lock click. The wind has blown the door shut and locked them in. Tom and Anna stop fighting and look at the door. They try to open it, but it does not move. They try to call Mom and Dad, but they do not answer. They start to cry and hug each other. They are scared and sorry. After a while, they hear a knock on the door. It is Mom and Dad. They have come back and found the door locked. They have a key and they turn it. The lock opens and the door opens. Tom and Anna run to Mom and Dad and hug them. They say they are sorry and they love them. Mom and Dad hug them back and say they love them too. They are not angry, but they are worried. They tell Tom and Anna to never touch the lock again. Tom and Anna nod and say yes. They are grateful that Mom and Dad are back and that they are safe. They promise to share their toys and not to fight. They go back to the big box and play happily.
