Title: Mary and the Park

Once a little girl named Mary was outside in the park. She wanted to play on the swing but became frustrated when it started to move too slow. She was angry and started stomping her feet. Her mother noticed and said, "Mary, why are you so upset?" Mary said, "I'm mad because it was taking so long!" Her mother smiled and said, "Delaying is not always bad. Sometimes taking a moment to pause can help us see things with a soft eye." Mary thought about it for a bit and she slowly understood. She looked up at the swing, seeing it with a softer eye and with fewer distractions. Suddenly she felt a lot better about the slow pace. This story teaches us that it is important to take a moment to pause and be patient. We can look at things with a softer eye and often times this will help us gain a new perspective on the situation. Taking some time to delay can help us gain new understanding and knowledge.

One day, a boy and a girl went to play outside. They saw a big hill and wanted to go up. The boy said, "Let's climb the hill!" The girl was scared, but the boy urged her to try. They started to climb the hill. On their way, they found something disgusting. It was a big pile of mud. The boy and the girl laughed and played with the mud. They got dirty, but they had fun. At last, they reached the top of the hill. They were very happy. They looked down and saw their home. The boy and girl held hands and ran down the hill. They went back home to tell their mom and dad about their fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a nice dog named Bob. Bob loved to eat steak. Every day, he would dream about eating a big steak. One day, Bob found a big steak on the ground. He was very happy. But then, Bob saw a smaller dog who was very hungry. The small dog looked at the steak and then looked at Bob. Bob thought about his dream and how much he loved steak, but he also thought about the hungry dog. Bob decided to share the steak with the smaller dog. They both enjoyed the steak together. After they ate, the small dog was very thankful and they became friends. The moral of the story is to share what you have with others, and you will make friends and be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big nation. The people in the nation were very proud. They worked together to make their land the best. One day, the nation had a big party. They wanted to cover the whole land with pretty colors. The people were very excited. They painted their houses, their cars, and their streets with bright, happy colors. The proud nation looked beautiful with all the colors. Everyone was happy and had fun at the party. They danced and played all day. The people in the nation knew that they were the best when they worked together.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a raven. The raven had many friends who would visit him. One day, a little girl named Lily came to visit the raven. Lily was a happy girl, but she was also very impatient. She wanted to play with the raven right away. The raven liked Lily, so they played together. They ran and jumped and had so much fun. As the sun went down, it was time for Lily to go home. The raven was sad, but he knew Lily would visit again. And every time she did, they would play and laugh together.

Once upon a time, there was a gray mill. The mill was big and old. It was in a small town. People used the mill to make flour from wheat. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the mill. He saw a man working there. Tim asked, "What are you doing?" The man said, "I am making flour. I will explain how." Tim was excited to learn. The man showed Tim how the mill worked. But Tim did not listen well. He wanted to play with the big mill. The man said, "No, Tim. It is not safe." Tim did not listen. He played with the mill and got hurt. The man was sad because he could not help Tim.
