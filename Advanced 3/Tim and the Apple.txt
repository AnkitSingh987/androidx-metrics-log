Title: Tim and the Apple

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He was very hungry. He wanted to find something delicious to eat. He opened the refrigerator and saw many yummy things. There were apples, cheese, and juice inside. As Tim reached for an apple, he heard a voice. It said, "Hello, Tim!" Tim was very surprised. He looked around, but he did not see anyone. He asked, "Who is speaking?" The voice said, "It's me, the refrigerator!" Tim could not believe it. The refrigerator could speak! Tim and the refrigerator talked for a little while. They talked about food and how to keep it cold. Then, the refrigerator told Tim a secret. It said, "If you say the magic word, I can make your food even more delicious!" Tim was very excited. He asked, "What is the magic word?" The refrigerator told him, "It's 'please'." Tim said, "Please make my apple more delicious!" And just like that, the apple became the most delicious apple Tim had ever tasted.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to create. Every day, she would use her imagination to build wonderful things out of blocks and paper and paint. One day, she decided to make something special. With lots of colorful paper and glue, she created a judge puppet. The puppet had a long nose, bright eyes and a smile. She was so proud of her creation. But the puppet wasn't perfect. It was a bit clumsy and it kept falling down. The little girl wanted to make it perfect, so she tried to make some adjustments. But, no matter how hard she tried, it just kept wobbling about. Finally, she decided that it was best to leave it as it was. Even though it was a bit wobbly, the little girl still thought it was the most special creation of all.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and make mud pies. They found a big pot and filled it with mud and leaves. They pretended it was a soup for the fairies. "Let's give some to Mommy," Lily said. "She will love our soup." Ben nodded. He picked up the pot and followed Lily to the house. But the pot was very heavy and Ben was very small. He tripped on a stone and fell down. The pot flew out of his hands and landed on Mommy's head. Mommy was resting on a chair. She had a headache and wanted some quiet. She was not happy when the pot hit her head. She screamed and jumped up. Mud and leaves went everywhere. "What are you doing?" Mommy asked. "Why did you throw a pot at me?" "We didn't throw it, Mommy," Lily said. "We made a soup for you. But Ben fell and the pot flew." Mommy looked at Lily and Ben. They were covered in mud and had silly smiles. She wanted to be angry, but she couldn't. She saw the pot and the soup and laughed. "You are very silly, but very sweet," Mommy said. "But next time, please don't make soup with a pot. And please don't hit me with it." She hugged Lily and Ben and took them to the bathroom. She washed them and the pot and the soup. Then she gave them some cookies and milk and let them rest on the couch. She rested with them and kissed their heads. She still had a headache, but she also had a happy heart.

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there lived a king. The king had a tight hat that he loved to wear. One day, the king went for a walk in the forest. In the forest, the king saw a strange rock. The rock started to glow! The king was very surprised. He said, "Wow! What a pretty glow!" A little squirrel came and saw the glowing rock too. The squirrel said, "Hello, king! This rock is magic. It makes your hat glow too!" The king put his tight hat on the rock, and it began to glow. The king and the squirrel became good friends. They played in the forest and had fun with the glowing rock and hat. And they lived happily ever after.
