Title: The Girl Called Lola and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl called Lola. She was three years old and very curious. One day, Lola was walking around her house when she noticed something unusual. On her bedroom floor there was a tiny creature. It was a little baby mouse! Lola ran to get her Mum who had a very compassionate look on her face when she saw the mouse. Mum gently picked up the mouse and said, “Oh my! This little one must have lost its way!” Lola's Mum sensed that the mouse was scared and she wanted to help. She urged Lola to make a warm and cosy home for the mouse from a shoebox. Together, with Lola's help, they filled the shoebox with plenty of food and cotton wool for the mouse to make a bed. Afterwards, Lola's mum smiled. She was glad that the mouse was somewhere safe and urged Lola to take good care of the mouse. Lola smiled back with happiness. She was glad that she had been able to help with her kind and compassionate actions.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a famous baker. She loved to bake yummy treats for everyone. One day, she wanted to make something new and tasty. She went to a big tree and found lots of red berries. She picked the berries and took them to her kitchen. She wanted to bake a berry cake for her friends. She mixed the berries with flour and sugar. She put it in the oven and let it bake. When it was done, she shared the berry cake with her friends. They all loved it and said it was the best cake ever. The famous baker was very happy.

Once there was a bear that wanted to get home. He was in a hurry and he couldn't wait to see his mom. He started walking away, but he wasn't sure what way to go. Then he thought of a bright idea. He looked up and saw a friendly bird flying in the sky. He called out "Excuse me, can you help me find my way home?" The bird stopped and looked carefully around. "Yes, I can help", he said in an honest voice. He showed the bear the right way to go and said, "Hurry now, you'll be home soon". The bear ran off in the direction the bird pointed. As he ran the wind ruffled his fur. He was so happy to be going home. The bear was almost home when he came to a crossroads. He looked around but he couldn't remember which way to go. Then he saw the friendly bird again and he hurried up to him. The bear asked the bird, "Which way is my home?". The bird pointed in one direction and said, "Hurry this way, you'll be there soon!" The bear thanked the bird for his help and then he ran off in the direction the bird had pointed. Finally the bear was home. He gave his mom a big hug and thanked the honest bird for helping him find his way home.

One day, a little girl named Lily was ready to play. She put on her shoes and went outside. Lily loved to play in the mud. She made mud pies and got very dirty. When it was time to go inside, Lily's mom saw the mess. "You must wipe your feet," she said. Lily wiped her feet, but she was still dirty. Her mom was not happy. "You need a bath," she said. Lily got in the bath and played with her toys. Then, something unexpected happened. A little frog jumped into the bath with her! Lily was scared at first, but then she laughed. The frog was just as dirty as she was. They both got clean and became friends. The frog showed Lily that love can come from unexpected places.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She was very active and always up for an adventure. One day, as she was play-wrestling with her teddy bear, she accidentally fell and scraped her knee on the floor. She started to cry, but then she felt a hand on her shoulder. "Don't cry," said her mom, "I can make it better. I have something that can make the hurt go away." Jane looked up, curious. Her mom pulled out a flexible bandage and showed it to her. "This," said her mom, "Will help, and make it better." Jane watched as her mom slowly and gently worked the bandage around her knee. "Do you feel better?" asked her mom. Jane smiled and nodded. "Good," said her mom. Jane never forgot how the flexible bandage had made her feel better.
