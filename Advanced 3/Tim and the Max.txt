Title: Tim and the Max

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, tough dog named Max. They liked to play together in the yard. One day, Tim saw his mom making a sandwich. She spread some yummy stuff on the bread. Tim wanted to make a sandwich too. He asked his mom, "Can I make a sandwich for Max?" His mom said, "Sure, but you need to know the price of the things you use." Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, I will learn the price." Tim and Max went to the store with mom. They saw bread, cheese, and the yummy stuff. Tim asked, "What is the price of these?" Mom said, "The bread is two coins, the cheese is three coins, and the yummy stuff is four coins." Tim counted his coins and bought everything he needed. Back at home, Tim spread the yummy stuff on the bread and added cheese. He gave the sandwich to Max. Max wagged his tail and ate the sandwich very fast. Tim was happy and said, "Now I know the price of making a sandwich for my tough friend, Max!" And they played together in the yard all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobo. Bobo liked to dive in the sky. One day, Bobo saw a big green cactus. The cactus was patient and did not move. Bobo wanted to dive near the cactus. But the cactus had sharp points. The sharp points could hurt Bobo. Bobo was sad. He wanted to play with the cactus. Bobo thought hard. He had an idea. Bobo found a soft ball. He gave the ball to the cactus. Now, the cactus had the ball, and Bobo could dive near the cactus. Bobo and the cactus were happy. They played together every day.

Billy was a very generous little boy who often shared his toys with his friends. His favorite thing to do was to fly his kite in the park. He was so amazed by the bright colors that flew so high and gracefully in the wind. One day, Billy's friends asked him if he wanted to go flying kites together. Billy was so excited that he went running with them. However, when they arrived, the wind was so strong that it destroyed several of their kites. Billy was very sad. He didn't know how to fix the kites that were broken. His friends were so disappointed and started to leave. Then, he saw a tear in one of his friends' eyes. He felt so helpless, that he offered to buy him a new kite. His friends were thankful and accepted his generous offer. The next day, however, something terrible happened. Billy was playing with his new kite when it got tangled in some power lines nearby. He was so scared that he didn't know what to do and he started to cry. Soon enough, people who heard him screaming for help came to rescue him. Unfortunately, by the time they arrived it was too late. Billy suffered from electric shock and passed away.

Once upon a time there was a happy little girl. She loved to help people, by giving them kind smiles and offering to share her toys. One night, the moon was smiling down on the girl and she waved to it. She wanted to help the moon so she was very polite and said hello. The moon felt so special that it sang back to the girl. The girl was overjoyed and danced around. She asked the moon for help because she wanted to go to the moon one day. The moon was very nice and showed the girl how to look up into the sky and imagine being on it. The girl was so excited and imagined floating on the moon with its gentle light surrounding her. She thanked the moon and waved goodbye. The little girl now remembers the night that the moon helped her out and she is always very polite and kind to it. She knows that the moon is always watching over her and looking out for her.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play hockey with his friends in the park. They would run and laugh, having a wild time together. Tim was always happy when he played hockey. One day, it rained a lot. The ground was wet and muddy. Tim and his friends still wanted to play hockey. They put on their boots and went to the park. They were excited to play in the mud. Tim was running fast with the hockey stick. But then, he felt his feet slip in the mud. He fell down and got all dirty. His friends laughed, and Tim laughed too. They all slipped and got dirty, but they had so much fun playing hockey in the mud.
