Title: The Lion and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion had a very loud roar. He lived in a jungle with many trees and animals. The lion was friends with a little bird. The bird liked to fly high in the sky. One day, the bird found a shiny jewel. The jewel was red and pretty. The bird wanted to give the jewel to the lion. The bird flew down to the lion and showed him the jewel. The lion was very happy and gave a big roar. The lion and the bird played with the jewel all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. Soon, they both became tired. The lion and the bird lay down under a tree. They looked at the pretty jewel and smiled. Then, they closed their eyes and went to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a famous truck. This truck was very big and strong. It was the most famous truck in the town. Everyone knew it and loved it. One day, the famous truck went on a trip. It went through the town and saw many things. The truck was very excited to see all the people, houses, and trees. It was a fun trip for the truck. At the end of the day, the famous truck went back home. It was very tired but happy. The truck had a lot of fun on its trip and was excited to go on another one soon. And all the people in the town were happy to see the famous truck too.

One day, a delicate butterfly went to visit her friend, the ant. The ant lived in a tiny house on a hill. They liked to play and talk together. The butterfly was excited to see her friend and share a new game she learned. As they started to play, they felt the ground shake. It was an earthquake! The butterfly and the ant were scared. They held each other tight and hoped it would stop soon. The earthquake made a big mess, but they were safe. After the earthquake, they saw something unexpected. The shaking had made a beautiful flower grow in the ant's yard. The butterfly and the ant were happy to see the pretty flower. They forgot about their fear and played together near the flower all day.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Ella. Ella was very attractive and very kind. One day, when it was almost noon, Ella was walking in the park. While she was walking, she noticed a young boy walking towards her. She smiled at him and they said hello. The boy's name was Luke and he asked Ella if she wanted to marry him. Ella was so surprised. At first, she did not know how to respond. But then she started to really like him and said yes. They walked together to the park pond and Ella and Luke joined hands and said, "I do." Just as they said, "I do," the clock hit noon. Ella and Luke were now married! They skipped together and smiled, fully in love. They knew that they would have a happy life together and love each other forever.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. He liked to sleep all day and play all night. One day, Tom was hungry and wanted to eat something yummy. Tom went to the kitchen and saw a big bowl of sauce. He thought it would be tasty. Tom decided to eat the sauce with his favorite food, fish. He put the sauce on the fish and started to eat. After eating, Tom felt happy and full. He went back to his cozy bed and took a long nap. When he woke up, he played with his toys and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tom decided to eat sauce with his fish every day.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a pretty flower. The flower was red and calm. Lily wanted to take the flower home. Lily asked her mom, "Can I take the flower home?" Her mom said, "No, we cannot take the flower. It is not ours." Lily was sad but she did not tell her mom. Lily went back to the park when her mom was not looking. She pulled the flower from the ground. The flower was not calm anymore. It was sad and hurt. Lily took the flower home, but it did not live.
