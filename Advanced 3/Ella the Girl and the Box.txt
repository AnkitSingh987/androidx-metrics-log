Title: Ella the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Ella. Ella was only 3 years old but she was very brave. She loved exploring and she was always curious about the world. One day, Ella decided to explore her attic. She climbed up the stairs to the attic and opened the door. It was very dark and uncomfortable. Ella slowly walked in and soon found a big, shiny box. Inside the box she found a long pole. Ella carefully picked up the pole and held it in her hands. She was determined to try and balance it on her head! Ella carefully positioned the pole on her head and stepped forward, trying to balance it. After a few tries she was able to stand still and balance the pole perfectly. Ella was so happy that she could do it! “Look, Daddy! I can balance the pole!" shouted Ella. Her dad smiled and proudly said: "That's amazing, Ella. You are so brave!" Ella was so proud of herself. She kept balancing the pole until it was time to go to bed. From that day on, Ella was always confident in herself and she loved exploring the attic.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his basketball. One sunny day, Tom went outside to play with his basketball. He had so much fun bouncing it up and down. As Tom played, he saw a big dog named Max. Max wanted to play with Tom's basketball too. Tom tried to resist, but Max was too fast. Max took the basketball and ran away. Tom was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. Max came back with the basketball and a new ball. Max wanted to be friends with Tom and play together. Tom was happy, and they played with the basketballs all day long. They became the best of friends.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play outside. They hope to see snow soon. They want to make a snowman and snowballs. One morning, they wake up and look out the window. They see white everywhere. It is snowing! They are very happy. They put on their coats, hats, gloves and boots. They run outside with their mom. "Look, mom, snow!" Lily says. "Yes, it is snowing a lot. It is very high," mom says. "Can we make a snowman, mom?" Ben asks. "Of course, you can. But be careful, it is cold," mom says. Lily and Ben find a good spot to make a snowman. They roll big balls of snow and stack them on top of each other. They find a carrot, a scarf, a hat and some buttons to decorate their snowman. They give him a smile and a name. "His name is Bob," Lily says. "Hi, Bob. You are our friend," Ben says. They hug their snowman and take a picture with him. Then they make some snowballs and have a snowball fight. They laugh and have fun. They hope the snow will stay for a long time. They love snow.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little pine tree. The little pine tree was very happy. It liked to dance and play with the other trees. The other trees were bigger and older, but they loved the little pine tree. One day, the little pine tree said, "Let's play a game. The one who behaves the best wins!" The other trees agreed and they all started to behave nicely. They stood tall and let the sun shine on them. They let the wind blow through their leaves. The little pine tree was the happiest of all. It behaved so well that it won the game. The other trees clapped and said, "Well done, little pine tree! You are the best!" The little pine tree danced and played even more. And they all lived happily ever after.

Connor and Timmy were best friends. One day Connor asked Timmy if he wanted to go for a swing in the park. Timmy was very excited and said yes. When they arrived in the park Connor took out a ticket from his pocket and gave it to Timmy. "This ticket will let us play on the swings," Connor said. Timmy was so grateful and thanked his friend. He said, "Thank you Connor, you're so kind." Connor smiled and said, "You're welcome Timmy! Now let's go and have some fun!" The boys happily went to the swings. They laughed and shouted with joy as they swung back and forth. They had the best time in the park.
