Title: Sunny the Yellow Bird and the Dish

Once upon a time, there was a yellow bird named Sunny. Sunny was very good at balance. One day, Sunny found a dish on the ground. The dish was also yellow, just like Sunny. Sunny wanted to balance the dish on his head. He tried and tried, but the dish kept falling down. Finally, Sunny could balance the dish on his head. Sunny was very happy and showed his friends. But then, something unexpected happened. The dish started to wiggle and jump! It was not a dish at all, it was a yellow bug. The yellow bug was just hiding and looked like a dish. Sunny and his friends laughed and played with the yellow bug. They all became good friends.

One day, a little infant was playing in the park. He liked to play with his ball and run around. But then, his ball vanished! The infant was very sad and sorry that he lost his ball. He looked all around the park to find his ball. He looked by the swings, the slide, and the sandbox. But he could not find it anywhere. The infant felt more and more sorry that he could not find his ball. Just when he was about to give up, a big dog came running up to him with the ball in its mouth. The dog had found the ball and brought it back to the infant! The infant was so happy and not sorry anymore. He played with the dog and his ball for the rest of the day.

Molly was a curious three year old. She wanted to know why her grandma couldn't hear. One day, Molly asked her grandma, "Why are you deaf?" Her grandma smiled at Molly. She told her that when she was younger, she had been in an accident and hurt her leg. After she had been in the hospital for a while, the doctors told her she was deaf. Molly was very surprised. She couldn't believe she hadn't heard about this before. "Do you still have a sore leg?" Molly asked. Grandma nodded and smiled. She told Molly that her leg still hurt sometimes, but she was used to it. Molly was filled with wonder. She couldn't believe all the things she was learning. She decided to ask her grandma more questions the next time they were together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the dirt. One day, while he was digging, he found a shiny mineral. The mineral was very fine and pretty. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, look what I found!" His mom looked at the fine mineral and said, "Wow, Tim! That's very pretty. Let's wipe it clean so we can see it better." Tim and his mom wiped the mineral until it was shiny and clean. Tim was so happy with his fine mineral. He showed it to all his friends and they all thought it was pretty too. From that day on, Tim always kept his special mineral with him wherever he went.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor. He was different from other sailors. He liked to print pictures of boats. He had many boats on his wall. One day, the sailor went to the sea. He saw a big boat. It was different too. It had many colors. The sailor wanted to print a picture of the big boat. The sailor took a picture of the big boat. He went home and printed it. He put the picture on his wall. Now, the sailor was very happy. He had a new, different boat on his wall.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ball. The ball lived in a cooler. The cooler was not old, it was modern. The ball wanted to see the world. So, the ball tried to escape from the cooler. One day, the ball found a small hole in the cooler. The ball squeezed through the hole. The ball was so happy to escape! The ball rolled and rolled in the grass. The ball saw flowers, trees, and a big blue sky. But soon, the ball missed its cooler. The ball wanted to go home. The ball rolled back to the cooler. The cooler was happy to see the ball. The ball was happy to be home. The ball still dreamed of the world, but now it knew that the cooler was its home.
