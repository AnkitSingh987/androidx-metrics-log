Title: Lily the Unique Girl and the Drawing

Once upon a time, there was a unique girl named Lily. She loved to dance and play with her friends. One sunny day, she found some chalk on the ground. Lily picked up the chalk and started to draw on the sidewalk. She drew a big circle, a small circle, and a line. Her friends came over and saw her drawing. They wanted to join in too. Together, they drew a big, colorful picture on the sidewalk. Then, they all started to dance around the picture, laughing and having fun. They danced until the sun went down, and they were all very happy.

Once, there was a small circle. It travelled around and around, bringing happiness and joy. One day, the small circle stopped at a house and a young girl ran outside with her hands outstretched. "Oh! A small circle!" she said with a big smile. "Can I keep it?" The small circle smiled. It just wanted to give the young girl joy. The small circle rolled away from the house and then suddenly it stopped as it heard a loud scream coming from the house. The small circle had seen signs of sadness and heard shouts of anger in the house before. It had known that if it stayed, it would give the young girl only sorrow in the end. Yet, it still chose to stay with hopes of bringing joy. The small circle felt helpless and scared. But it could not turn away now. It had to face the sorrow it had brought to the home. When it came back, the little girl was crying and the house was filled with sorrow. The small circle sadly gave the young girl one last hug and then it rolled away, hoping the girl would soon find happiness again.

Once upon a time, there was an unusual curtain. It seemed to hang down so low. Every day, the boy wanted to lift it. He tried and tried, but nothing ever changed. One day, the boy got a little bit too brave. He reached out and lifted the curtain. Underneath, he found a secret room. He was so excited! But the room seemed strange and dark. The boy knew he shouldn’t go in. But he decided to take a peek anyway. Inside was something very unusual. What could it be? As the boy stepped closer, he realized it was a monster! It was huge and wild. The monster laughed a loud, scary laugh and the boy started to run. But it was too late. The monster chased him out of the secret room. The boy would never forget the day he lifted the curtain. He knew it was a bad idea!

One day, a boy named Tim found a broken kite in the park. It was an incredible kite with many colors. Tim wanted to fly the kite, but it needed repair. He asked his dad, "Can we fix the kite?" His dad said, "Yes, we can try." They worked together to fix the kite. They used tape and string to make it strong again. Tim was excited to fly the kite now that it was fixed. The next day, Tim went to the park with his dad and the repaired kite. They started to fly the kite high in the sky. Suddenly, a big bird came and took the kite away! Tim was sad, but his dad said, "Don't worry, we can make a new kite together." They went home and made an even more incredible kite. Tim learned that sometimes, unexpected things happen, but we can always find a way to make it better.

Once upon a time, there was a king. The king was very sad and miserable. He did not like his big, heavy crown. It made his head hurt. One day, a little girl came to see the king. She had a pretty flower in her hand. The king looked at the flower and smiled. The little girl had an idea. The little girl took the flower and attach it to the king's crown. The king's face lit up with a big smile. He was not miserable anymore. From that day on, the king wore the flower on his crown and was the happiest king in the world.
