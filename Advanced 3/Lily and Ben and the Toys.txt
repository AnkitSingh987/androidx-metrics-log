Title: Lily and Ben and the Toys

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They had a lot of toys and they liked to make noise. They did not listen to Mom when she told them to tidy up. They just wanted to have fun. They saw a big hole in the ground. They wondered what was inside. They decided to bury some of their toys in the hole. They thought it was a good game. They threw their cars, dolls, balls and books in the hole. They covered them with dirt and leaves. Mom came out and saw the messy garden. She was very angry. She asked them where their toys were. They pointed to the hole. Mom was shocked. She told them to dig out their toys. She said they were very naughty and wasteful. She said they should take care of their things and not ruin them. Lily and Ben felt sorry. They realized they had made a big mistake. They worked hard to dig out their toys. They were dirty and broken. They cried and said they were sorry. Mom hugged them and said she forgave them. She gave them a clean blouse to wear. She said they should learn from their mistake and be more responsible and respectful. Lily and Ben nodded. They said they would not bury their toys again. They said they would tidy up their garden and share their toys with others. They thanked Mom for her love and kindness. They learned a valuable lesson that day.

Once upon a time, there was a modern scarf. It was made of the softest wool, so soft that it felt like a cloud. Everybody wanted the modern scarf. One day, a little girl named Jenny wanted to wear the scarf. But, before she could reach it, it flew away right out of her hands! The modern scarf flew right out the window and flew away into the sky. Jenny didn't know how to prevent the scarf from flying away. The scarf flew and flew until it was too far away for Jenny to see. But Jenny didn't give up. With all her might, she ran and ran until she could barely breathe. Eventually, she reached the scarf and caught it before it flew away forever. She was so happy she hugged the scarf tightly in her arms. Now, she wears the modern scarf all the time to prevent it from flying away!

One cold winter day, Jack and Lily were walking to the park. Jack shouted with excitement, "Look, Lily! A big pole in the middle of the park!". When they reached the pole, Jack touched the pole slowly and felt many tiny bumps. He asked Lily, "What is this?" Lily replied quietly, "It's a birdhouse! See how still it is?". As Jack and Lily watched, they saw a small bird inside. Suddenly the bird spread out its wings and flew off in to the sky. Just then, the wind started to blow, and snowflakes began to fall all around them. Jack and Lily got so excited that they started to jump and spin around, playing in the snow. Suddenly, they both stopped and noticed that everything around them had begun to freeze. All the trees became quiet and still, and Jack and Lily's smiles vanished. Jack asked, "Lily, why is everything freezing?". Lily said, "It's wintertime, Jack. That's why everything freezes!". Jack and Lily hugged each other tight and then ran back home, happy to be in a warm place.

One day, a green frog named Zip hopped near a pond. Zip loved to play and jump all day long. He had many friends who lived in the pond too. One sunny day, Zip met a big fish. The fish said, "Zip, I want to play with you, but you have to surrender and let me win." Zip thought about it and said, "Okay, we can play, but we must take turns to win." As they played, a big bird flew down and tried to catch the fish. Zip jumped high and scared the bird away. The fish was so happy and said, "Thank you, Zip! You saved me!" From that day on, Zip and the fish played together and took turns winning. They became best friends and always looked out for each other. The pond was a happy place for everyone.
