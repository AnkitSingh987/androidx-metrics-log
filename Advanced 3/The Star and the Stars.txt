Title: The Star and the Stars

Once upon a time, there was a cute little star. The star lived in the sky with many other stars. Every night, the stars would come out and play together. One night, the cute little star wanted to find a friend who would match with it. It looked at all the other stars in the sky. Some were big, some were small, and some were different colors. The cute little star felt sad because it could not find a friend to match with. Then, the cute little star saw another star that was just like it. They were both the same size and color. The two stars became best friends and played together every night. They were so happy to have found a friend that matched with them. And they lived happily ever after.

Once there was a big gem. It was so big and so pretty. Everyone wanted to have it. But two brothers wanted it most. So the brothers got into a fight to see who could get the gem. The older brother was very smart and he had a plan. He was going to lie about who was the strongest and try to win the gem. He was very creative and made up a story about how he was the strongest one and could get the gem. But the younger brother also wanted to win the gem. He was also very smart. He thought of something very creative. He said, "I have a plan. Let's both try to get the gem and whoever can lift it the highest will win it." The older brother was angry, but he agreed. They both tried to lift the gem, but it was too heavy for them. In the end the gem stayed where it was and neither brother won it. The brothers were upset and angry, but there was nothing they could do. And that's the end of the story.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a toy cat. The cat was very naughty. Tom would always support his cat when it did naughty things. One day, Tom and his cat found a big box. The box had a symbol on it. The symbol was a star. Tom and his cat wanted to see what was inside the box. They opened the box and saw many colorful balls. Tom and his cat played with the balls. They threw the balls in the air and laughed. But then, something unexpected happened. The balls turned into big, yummy ice creams! Tom and his cat were very happy. They ate the ice creams and forgot about being naughty. From that day on, they were always good and never did naughty things again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red armchair in the middle of the room. A cat and a dog lived in the house. They both liked to sit on the armchair. But they were not careful. They would jump on it and make a mess. One day, the cat and the dog had a fight. They both wanted to sit on the armchair at the same time. They yelled and pushed each other. They were not happy. The armchair started to look bad because they were not careful. The mom saw the mess and was not happy. She told the cat and the dog to display good behavior. She showed them how to be careful and share the armchair. The cat and dog listened to the mom. They sat on the armchair together, being careful not to make a mess. They were happy, and the armchair looked nice again.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful chair. The chair was red and very soft. The chair lived in a big room with other chairs and tables. One day, the beautiful chair wanted to move. It wanted to see the world outside the big room. So, the chair moved very slowly towards the door. It went step by step, very carefully. The beautiful chair finally reached the door. It saw the sun, the grass, and the trees. The chair was very happy that it moved. Now, the beautiful chair could enjoy the outside world with its new friends.
