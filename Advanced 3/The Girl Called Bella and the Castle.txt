Title: The Girl Called Bella and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a young girl called Bella. Bella loved to go to the beach. One day, Bella went to the beach and started to build a castle with the sand. Suddenly, she heard some laughter. She looked around and saw two boys poking each other. They were both very young. Bella got angry. She stopped building her castle and stood up. She shouted, "Stop poking each other!" The two boys stopped, but one of them stuck his tongue out at Bella and started poking her. Bella was really angry now. She started to cry. Suddenly, an old man came and stood in front of her. He was holding a long stick and he said, "Stop poking the young girl!" The boys looked scared and they ran away. The old man helped Bella to stand up. He stayed with her until she stopped crying. From then on, Bella learnt to always stand up for herself, no matter how young she was.

Timmy was an adventurous boy who liked to explore new things. One day, he saw a big automobile parked outside his house. It was shiny and red and had four round wheels. Timmy wanted to see what was inside the automobile, so he opened the door and climbed in. The automobile had many buttons and levers and a big wheel. Timmy wondered what they did, so he started to push and pull and turn them. He heard a loud noise and felt the automobile move. He was scared and tried to stop, but he did not know how. The automobile went faster and faster and bumped into other automobiles and trees and houses. People were angry and shouted at Timmy. They called the police and the firemen to help. The police and the firemen came and stopped the automobile. They took Timmy out and asked him why he did that. Timmy was sorry and said he was just curious. He said he did not mean to hurt anyone or anything. He said he wanted to thank the police and the firemen for saving him. But the police and the firemen were not happy. They said Timmy was naughty and dangerous. They said he broke the law and caused a lot of damage. They said he had to go with them and face the consequences. They said he had to pay for what he did. They took Timmy away and left him alone in a dark and cold room. Timmy was sad and scared and wished he never saw the automobile. He wished he stayed at home and played with his toys. He wished he listened to his mom and dad. He learned a hard lesson that day. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a row of thick trees. In the trees, there was a big bell. The bell would ring every day. All the animals in the forest liked to hear the bell ring. One day, the bell stopped ringing. The animals were sad. They all went to the row of thick trees to see what was wrong. They saw that the bell was stuck in a tree. A small bird flew up to the bell. The bird pushed the bell with its beak. The bell started to ring again. The animals were happy to hear the bell ring. They all danced and played in the row of thick trees.

One day, a little girl named Sue saw a green sweater in a store. She really liked it. "I want the green sweater, Mom!" she said. Her mom looked at the sweater and smiled. She said, "Okay, we can buy the green sweater." The next day, Sue wore her green sweater to the park. She saw her friend, Tim. Tim looked at Sue's sweater and said, "I want a green sweater too!" Sue and Tim played in the park all day, and they had lots of fun. The next day, Sue and Tim went to the store with Tim's mom. They found a green sweater for Tim. But when they tried to buy it, something unexpected happened. The green sweater talked! It said, "I don't want to be bought. I want to stay with my sweater friends!" Sue, Tim, and their moms were very surprised. They decided to let the green sweater stay at the store with its friends. Sue and Tim still had fun playing together, even without matching green sweaters.
