Title: The Wild Whale and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a wild whale in the big blue sea. He wanted to go on a big adventure, so he decided to pass through the ocean. As he passed through the sea, he saw fish and coral and seaweed. He bumped into a school of small fish and they startled him! He passed so close to a giant rock that he could have touched it. The wild whale kept swimming, passing under the moon and stars in the night sky. He passed schools of dolphins, and they seemed to be playing tag with eachother. When he saw the beach, the whale was so excited. He could rest and play in the sand and sleep in the sun! He passed the time relaxing, until he was ready to go back home. So the wild whale swam back through the big blue sea, with so many memories of his adventure. He was so happy to be back home, safe and sound.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to collect things. She would find pretty rocks, soft leaves, and even small sticks. One day, Sue wanted to do something creative with her collection. She went to the big tree in her yard and saw a long root coming out of the ground. Sue had an idea. She would use the root to make a path for her collection. She put the rocks, leaves, and sticks along the root. Sue was very happy with her creative path. She showed her mom, dad, and little brother. They all liked it too. Sue felt proud of her work and knew that she could make more fun things with her collection.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little ice cream cone named Melty. Melty lived in a zoo with many animal friends. Melty loved to play with the animals, but he was always careful not to stay in the sun too long, or else he would melt. One sunny day, Melty and his friend, Ellie the elephant, were playing near the water. Ellie said, "Melty, come play in the water with me!" Melty was scared and said, "No, Ellie, I can't. I will melt if I get too hot." Ellie understood and decided to play in the shade with Melty instead. They had a lot of fun together. At the end of the day, Melty was happy that he didn't melt, and he was grateful for his caring friend, Ellie. And they lived happily ever after in their cute little zoo.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely prince. He lived in a big castle with his mom and dad. The prince had a lot of friends, but he liked to play alone too. One day, the prince went to his room to play. He heard a strange noise. He wanted to find out what it was. The prince started to peek around the room. He looked under the bed, behind the door, and in the closet. Then, the prince saw something move under his toy box. He was scared but wanted to see what it was. He moved the toy box and found a tiny kitten! The kitten was not a prince, but it was lovely too. The prince and the kitten became best friends and played together all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a careful driver named Tom. Tom loved to drive his big, red car. He drove it all around the town, helping people get to where they needed to go. One day, Tom decided he wanted to learn something new. He wanted to learn how to drive a big, yellow bus! Tom thought it would be fun to help even more people get to where they needed to go. Tom went to bus school and learned how to be a careful bus driver. He was very good at it. Soon, Tom was driving the big, yellow bus all around the town. Everyone was happy because Tom was a careful driver who helped them get to where they needed to go. And Tom was happy because he learned something new.

One day, a little bunny named Tim was hopping in the forest. He was looking for a carrot to eat. Tim was a calm bunny, but sometimes he would get scared. As Tim hopped along, he saw a big carrot in the ground. He tried to pull it out, but it was stuck. "Help me, please," Tim asked his friend, a bird named Sam. Sam flew down and helped Tim pull the carrot. Just then, they heard a loud noise. Tim and Sam were scared. "What was that?" Tim asked. Sam looked around and saw it was just a squirrel playing with some leaves. "It's okay, Tim," Sam said. "It's just a squirrel." Tim and Sam felt calm again. They shared the big carrot and continued to play in the forest, happy and unafraid.
