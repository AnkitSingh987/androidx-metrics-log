Title: Mummy and Daddy and the Jack

Mummy and Daddy were walking with their son, Jack, one day. It was a very hot day and Jack's tummy felt funny. Daddy took Jack to the doctor. At the doctor's office, Daddy said to Jack, "The doctor is going to give you some medicine." The doctor said, "There may be a little hurt but it will feel better soon!" Jack was scared and started to cry. But the doctor was friendly and said, "Don't worry! It's just an ordinary shot." Daddy held Jack tight as the doctor gave him the shot. Jack said, "I don't like this medicine!" But the doctor said, "It's ok. It will help your tummy feel better soon". Jack was brave and bravely took the shot. Daddy said, "I'm proud of you, Jack! You are brave like a lion!". Then the doctor said, "Now let's go home! You'll feel better soon". And sure enough, Jack felt a lot better after taking the medicine.

Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Timmy. Timmy was a happy baby who loved to play with his toys and laugh with his mommy and daddy. But one day, Timmy felt sad because his favorite toy was lost. Timmy's mommy and daddy looked all over the house to find the toy. They looked under the bed, behind the sofa, and even in the kitchen. But they could not find the toy anywhere. Timmy was very sad and began to cry. Mommy and daddy said, "Let's take a nap, Timmy." So, Timmy took a nap with his mommy and daddy. When they woke up, Timmy's mommy found the toy under Timmy's pillow. Timmy was so happy to see his toy again! He hugged his toy, and his mommy and daddy, and they all played together. And Timmy was not sad anymore.

One day, a tall girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a new sweater for Lily. Lily saw a pretty red sweater and said, "Mom, I like this one!" Her mom said, "Let's measure you to see if it fits." Lily stood still while her mom used a tape to measure her. After measuring, her mom said, "This sweater is the right size for you, Lily!" They were both happy and went to pay for the sweater. When they got home, Lily put on her new red sweater. But something unexpected happened! The sweater started to grow and grow! It became so big that it covered Lily's whole body. Lily said, "Mom, help! My sweater is too big!" Her mom laughed and said, "Don't worry, Lily. It's a magic sweater. It will go back to the right size soon." And just like that, the sweater went back to the perfect size for Lily. They both laughed and enjoyed the magic sweater.

One day, a little elephant named Ella was playing with her friends. They were all having a lot of fun. But Ella was tired and wanted to nap. So, she said to her friends, "I am going to take a nap. I will play with you later." Ella went to find a nice place to nap. She saw a big tree and thought, "This is a good place for a nap." She was very careful when she lay down, so she would not hurt her trunk. She put her trunk on the soft grass and closed her eyes. While Ella was napping, a little bird came by. The bird saw Ella's trunk and said, "Hello, what is that?" Ella woke up and said, "This is my trunk. I am an elephant." The bird smiled and said, "Nice to meet you, Ella. I am a bird." Ella smiled back and they became good friends. After that, they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ann. She loved to play with her toys and read books. One day, she found a big book in her room. She did not know what it was. She asked her mom, "What is this big book?" Her mom said, "That is a dictionary. It has many words and their meanings. It can help you learn new words." Ann was very happy. She wanted to learn new words. Ann's mom suggested, "Why don't you read the dictionary every day and learn a new word?" Ann liked the idea. Every day, she would open the big dictionary and learn a new word. Soon, she knew many words and could talk to her friends better. Ann was very happy with her big dictionary.
