Title: The Long Hotel and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a long hotel. In the hotel, there was a cat and a dog. The cat and the dog did not like each other. They would often strike each other and run away. One day, a little boy came to the hotel. He saw the cat and the dog fighting. The boy said, "Stop! You should be friends!" The cat and the dog looked at the boy and stopped fighting. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat and the dog started to talk! They said, "Thank you, little boy. We will be friends now." The boy, the cat, and the dog all played together and had fun in the long hotel. From that day on, the cat and the dog were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a small castle. It was in the middle of a big field. The castle had a king, a queen, and a little prince. One day, a big, mean giant came to the field. He saw the small castle and wanted to destroy it. The king, queen, and little prince were scared. They had an idea! They gave the giant a big cake. The giant ate the cake and became happy. He did not want to destroy the small castle anymore. The king, queen, and little prince were happy too. They all became friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a great big bear named Bobo. Bobo lived in a big forest with many trees. One day, while Bobo was walking, he found a shiny gold rock. He was very happy and wanted to show his friend, Lulu the rabbit. Bobo went to Lulu's home and said, "Lulu, look what I found! A gold rock!" Lulu looked at the gold rock and said, "Wow, Bobo! That's great! But, you should hide it. Someone might want to take it from you." Bobo thought about it and decided to hide the gold rock in a hole near his home. A few days later, a sneaky fox named Foxy came to the forest. Foxy heard about the gold rock and wanted it for himself. Foxy went to Bobo and asked, "Bobo, can I have your gold rock?" Bobo remembered what Lulu said and refused, "No, Foxy. It is mine. You cannot have it." Foxy was sad but decided to leave the forest. Bobo and Lulu were happy that they kept the gold rock safe. From that day on, they knew that they could always trust each other and keep their things safe. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a mean comet. It had fierce eyes and it would move through the sky, scowling at the world below. One day a brave little girl called Molly decided to make the comet smile. She waved her arms in the air and shouted "hello!" But the mean comet just frowned and flew away. Molly decided to try again. She took a big deep breath and sang a beautiful song. The comet watched her and listened carefully. Then it smiled! Soon it was laughing loudly. Molly was so happy. From then on the comet always grinned when it flew over the little girl's house. Molly would wave and the comet would wave back. Everyone was happy.

Once, there was a little boat that wanted to sail. It was a sunny day, and the water was calm. The little boat was excited to go on an adventure. As the little boat began to sail, it met a big fish with shiny scales. The fish was sad because it tasted something bitter in the water. The little boat asked the fish, "Why are you sad, big fish?" The big fish said, "I am sad because the water tastes bitter, and I don't like it." The little boat wanted to help the big fish. So, it sailed far away to find sweet water for the big fish. The little boat sailed and sailed until it found a place with sweet water. It brought the sweet water back to the big fish. The big fish was happy and thanked the little boat. They became good friends, and the little boat and the big fish sailed together every day.
