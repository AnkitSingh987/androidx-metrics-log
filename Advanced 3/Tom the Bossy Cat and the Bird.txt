Title: Tom the Bossy Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to polish his shiny bowl every day. He would rub it until it was very clean and bright. Tom was very proud of his bowl. One day, Tom saw a little bird outside. The bird was dancing and jumping with a lot of motion. Tom wanted to join the bird and dance too. So, he went outside and started to dance with the bird. As they danced, Tom forgot about being bossy. He was having so much fun with his new friend. They danced all day long. From that day on, Tom learned to be kind and play with his friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a chubby bunny was hopping near a hill. He saw a hole in the hill and wanted to see what was inside. He poked his nose into the hole, but something poked him back! The chubby bunny was scared and jumped back. He looked around and saw a little mouse. The mouse was mad because the bunny poked his home. The bunny said sorry to the mouse, but the mouse was still mad. The bunny had an idea. He found some yummy food for the mouse and gave it to him. The mouse was happy now and they became friends. The chubby bunny and the mouse played together near the hill every day.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable cat. The cat loved to sleep all day. It would sleep on a big, soft bed. The cat was very happy. One day, the cat saw a big bin. The bin was open. The cat thought it would be fun to sleep in the bin. So, the cat jumped into the bin and went to sleep. While the cat was sleeping, a big wind came. The wind blew the bin lid closed. The cat could not get out. The adorable cat stayed in the bin and was very sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to create things with her hands. One day, she made a big, round ball with clay. She was very happy with her creation. Sue wanted to show her mom the ball she made. But on her way to find her mom, she dropped the ball. The ball fell on the floor and got a big crack. Sue was very sad. Sue's mom saw the cracked ball and gave her a big hug. She told Sue they could fix it together. They worked together and fixed the crack. Sue was grateful for her mom's help. They both smiled and played with the ball.

Once upon a time, there was a family that was different. They were unique compared to all other families. One morning, Dad picked up a newspaper from the sidewalk. His son watched and said, "Dad, what's that?" Dad said, "It's a newspaper. I trust it to tell me useful things." The family decided to go for a drive. Dad said to the son, "Let's trust the newspaper to tell us where to go." They followed the directions in the newspaper and drove for many miles. In the end, the family arrived at a beautiful park. They had never seen this park before. Dad smiled at his son. He said, "I'm glad we trusted the newspaper!" The family had a wonderful day at the park. They laughed and played together. The son thought to himself that it was nice to trust the newspaper. He had never experienced a day like this before and he knew it would stay in his memory forever.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She wanted to make a card for her friend's birthday. She was very excited to make the best card ever. Lily wanted to be successful in making her friend happy. Lily knew she needed to measure the paper to make the card the right size. She found a ruler and measured the paper. She cut it out and folded it to make the card. Lily drew a big cake and wrote "Happy Birthday" on the front. She gave the card to her friend at the birthday party. Her friend loved the card and gave Lily a big hug. Lily felt very happy and successful because she made her friend smile. The end.
