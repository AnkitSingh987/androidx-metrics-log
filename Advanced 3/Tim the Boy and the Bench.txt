Title: Tim the Boy and the Bench

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat sandwiches. One day, his mom made him a big, cold sandwich for lunch. Tim was very happy. Tim went to the park to play and eat his sandwich. He saw a sad girl named Sue sitting on a bench. She had no food and was hungry. Tim thought for a moment. He remembered his mom told him to always mind others and help when he could. Tim walked to Sue and shared his cold sandwich with her. Sue smiled and they became friends. They played together all day at the park. Tim learned that sharing and minding others made him feel happy and good inside.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Mark. Mark loved to watch movies. His favorite movie was about a big, red ball that could talk. The ball was very useful to the people in the movie because it helped them find their way home. One day, Mark wanted to make his own movie. He asked his mom and dad for help. They said yes and made a movie with Mark and his toys. Mark was very happy and proud of his movie. Mark showed his movie to his friends. They all liked it very much. They thought Mark's movie was useful because it made them laugh and feel happy. Mark felt like a big movie star and had lots of fun with his friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a famous pizza place. Everyone loved to go there and eat pizza. The pizza maker, Tom, was very happy because he made the best pizza. One day, a big cat came to the pizza place. The cat wanted to eat all the pizza. Tom wanted to prevent the cat from eating the pizza. He tried to hide the pizza, but the cat found it. Tom was sad because the cat ate all the pizza. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat started to make pizza! The cat made even better pizza than Tom. Everyone was happy and loved the cat's pizza. Tom and the cat became friends and made famous pizza together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive tree in a pretty forest. The tree had many friends, like birds, bugs, and animals. One day, a little bird named Timmy came to visit the tree. Timmy was very small, but he could sing beautiful songs. "Hello, big tree!" said Timmy. "I enjoy singing songs in the mist. Do you want to hear one?" The tree was happy to listen. Timmy began to sing a special song about the sun coming out after the mist. The tree and all its friends loved the song. The next day, the forest was full of mist. All the friends of the tree remembered Timmy's song. They knew the sun would come out soon. And just like Timmy sang, the sun came out and made the forest bright and warm. The tree and all its friends were so happy. They knew Timmy's song was magic.

One day, a beautiful bird was flying in the sky. It loved to fly in zigzag lines. The bird was very happy. The bird saw a big tree and wanted to look at it. It flew down to the tree. But when it got close, a mean cat was there. The cat wanted to catch the bird. The bird was scared and did not know what to do. The bird had an idea. It flew up high and started to fly in zigzag lines again. The cat tried to jump and catch it, but it could not. The cat was too slow, and the bird was too fast. The bird was safe and happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally who loved to play with her toy phone. One day, while playing, her toy phone began to ring. Sally was surprised because it never rang before. She picked it up and said, "Hello?" On the other end of the phone, a tiny voice said, "Hi Sally! I am an onion, and I need your help!" Sally was confused but wanted to help the onion. She asked, "What can I do to help you, onion?" The onion said, "I am embarrassed because I cannot find my onion friends. Can you help me find them, please?" Sally agreed to help the onion and went outside to look for the other onions. She searched high and low but could not find them anywhere. Then, Sally remembered her toy phone and decided to call the onion back. The onion answered and said, "Thank you for trying, Sally. But I have a secret to tell you. I am not really an onion. I am your toy phone!" Sally was shocked and laughed. The toy phone had played a funny trick on her. From that day on, Sally and her toy phone became the best of friends, and they always had fun playing together.
