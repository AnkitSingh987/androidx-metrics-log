Title: Sue the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to fold paper and play sports. She also loved to eat tasty food. One sunny day, Sue went to the park to play with her friends. While they were playing, Sue saw a big tasty cake on a table. She was very hungry and wanted to eat it. But then, something unexpected happened. The cake started to talk! The cake said, "Hello Sue, I am a magic cake. If you fold me, I will turn into a sport ball." Sue was surprised, but she wanted to see if it was true. So she carefully folded the tasty cake. Just like the cake said, it turned into a sport ball. Sue and her friends played with the magic ball all day long. They had so much fun, and Sue learned that sometimes, unexpected things can be the best surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat. His name was Clyde. Every day after lunch, Clyde liked to take a walk. On this day, he was very excited because it was a special day – it was Match Day! Clyde thought that Match Day meant his mommy was going to take him for another walk and he couldn't wait! As soon as his mommy opened the door, he hurried out and started running down the street, meowing excitedly. When Clyde reached the park, he saw many different cats playing with each other. Some were big and some were small. He saw his friend Simon and quickly ran up to him. As he approached, he asked, "Is it Match Day?" Simon chuckled and said, "Yes, it is! Let's go play a game of match!" Clyde was delighted and together they ran off to find the perfect match. Soon they found one, which was a perfect fit for both of them. And they had such a great time playing together. Later that day, Clyde returned home full of joy and excitement, looking forward to the next Match Day.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow cat named Sunny. Sunny lived in a house with a big kitchen. The kitchen had a big fridge, a stove, and a table. Sunny loved to play in the kitchen. One day, Sunny's friend, a small dog named Buddy, came over to play. They played hide and seek in the kitchen. Buddy hid under the table and Sunny looked for him. When Sunny found Buddy, Buddy said, "You found me! Let's play again!" Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. They both started to panic. Sunny asked, "What was that?" Buddy said, "I don't know. Let's go see!" They went to see what made the noise. It was just a pot that fell on the floor. They both laughed and went back to playing in the kitchen.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the farm. They liked to feed the cows, chase the chickens and pet the sheep. But they did not like the pig. The pig was grumpy and dirty and always made loud noises. One day, Lily and Tom found a big apple under a tree. They both wanted to eat it, but they did not want to share it. They started to quarrel and pull the apple from each other. "Give me the apple, it is mine!" Lily said. "No, it is mine, I saw it first!" Tom said. They did not notice the pig who was watching them from behind a fence. The pig was hungry and wanted the apple too. He saw his chance and ran towards them. He snatched the apple from their hands and ran away. Lily and Tom were shocked and angry. They looked at each other and felt sorry. They realized they had been silly and selfish. They had lost their apple and their fun because of their quarrel. They decided to make up and be friends again. They hugged and said sorry. They went to look for another apple to share. They also learned to be kind to the pig and gave him some corn. The pig was happy and stopped being grumpy. He even let them stroke his nose. Lily and Tom smiled and played happily in the farm. They learned that sharing is caring and that being nice to others makes everyone happy.
