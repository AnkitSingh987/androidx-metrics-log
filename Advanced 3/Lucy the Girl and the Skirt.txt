Title: Lucy the Girl and the Skirt

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved her pretty skirt. She wore it every day. She thought she looked very nice in it. One day, Lucy met a big dog. The dog was very intelligent. Lucy asked the dog, "Do you like my skirt?" The dog did not say anything. He just shrugged. Lucy smiled and said, "It's okay. I still like my skirt and I think you're a smart dog." They became good friends and played together every day. And Lucy always wore her pretty skirt.

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. Spot was a normal dog who liked to chew on things. He walked and walked, and soon he saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a long neck. Spot was surprised. "Hello," said the long neck. "I am Dino, the dinosaur." Spot was happy to meet Dino. They talked and played together. Spot showed Dino how he could chew on a stick. Then, something unexpected happened. Dino tried to chew on a stick too, but he couldn't. His teeth were not good for chewing sticks. Dino was sad. "Don't worry, Dino," said Spot. "We can find something else for you to chew." They looked around and found some soft leaves. Dino tried to chew the leaves and he liked it. Spot and Dino were happy and played together all day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. She was very modest and liked to bake. One day, she decided to try baking a cake. She got out her ingredients and all the equipment she would need and started baking. When the cake was almost done, Sarah's mom came by and said: "That looks amazing, Sarah, you are such a great baker!" Sarah gave her mom a big wink. Mom said, "Such a wink can only mean one thing - you are preparing a surprise for me!" Sarah shook her head and laughed. Mom continued, "Let me guess - it's a cake?" Sarah smiled and nodded. "You are a clever girl," Mom said as they enjoyed the cake together. Sarah felt very proud and happy. She knew that in her modest way, she could make something special.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who really liked to sleep. Every day he loved to sleep for long hours. One day he thought he would try something different and he decided to take a nap in a coal mine. The coal mine was very dark and scary but he was brave and so he decided to go ahead. He soon found the perfect spot and fell asleep. But little did he know, there was a giant monster sneaking around in the coal mine! The monster was searching for someone to eat. When it saw the little boy sleeping it decided to eat him. The little boy woke up with a start and saw the monster. He was so scared that he started to cry. But then he had a brilliant idea. He quickly closed his eyes and pretended to be asleep. The monster got very confused and decided to leave. The little boy was so relieved and he knew that sleep had saved him this time. And ever since then, the little boy was very careful to always sleep in an easy and safe place.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy he loved a lot. He played with the toy every day. One day, Tim left his toy outside in the rain. The toy got wet, and its skin started to peel off. Tim felt guilty for leaving his toy outside. The next day, Tim took the toy to his friend, Sue. Sue tried to fix the toy's skin, but she couldn't. Tim started to weep because his toy was still broken. Sue felt sad too, and they both wept together. Tim and Sue tried to find a new skin for the toy, but they couldn't find one. The toy stayed broken, and it made Tim and Sue very sad. They both learned that they should take better care of their toys.
