Title: Sue the Girl and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue lived on a big farm with her mom and dad. They grew lots of wheat in their field. One day, her mom asked her to help gather the wheat. "Sue, can you help me gather the wheat today?" her mom asked. Sue thought gathering wheat was boring, but she wanted to help her mom. So, they went to the field and started to gather the wheat. As they were gathering, Sue saw something shiny in the wheat. "Mom, look!" she said, pointing at the shiny thing. Sue's mom looked and saw the shiny thing too. They walked closer and found a big, shiny egg! They had never seen an egg like this before. Sue and her mom took the egg home and showed it to her dad. They all wondered what could be inside the egg. The next day, the egg cracked open and a baby dragon came out! The dragon was friendly and became Sue's new friend. Now, gathering wheat was not boring anymore, because Sue had her dragon friend to help her.

Once upon a time, in a small room, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big eraser that was her favorite toy. One day, Lily felt very tired and wanted to take a nap. Before she went to sleep, she put her eraser on the chair. But when she lay down, her bed was uncomfortable. She tried to sleep, but she couldn't. She thought, "Maybe my eraser can help me feel better." So, Lily took her eraser and put it under her pillow. Then, she lay down again. "Ah, this feels much better!" she said. Now her bed was not uncomfortable anymore. She closed her eyes and took a nice, long nap with her eraser under her pillow. And they both had a happy and cozy sleep.

Lily and Sam were friends who liked to play in the park. They had a lot of toys, but they did not like to share them. One day, they brought their toys to the park and put them on a bench. They played with their own toys and did not let anyone else touch them. A boy named Max came to the park with his mom. He saw the toys on the bench and wanted to play with them. He asked Lily and Sam if he could join them, but they said no. They said the toys were theirs and they did not share. Max felt sad and went to play on the swings. Lily and Sam kept playing with their toys, but they soon got bored. They looked around and saw that the other kids were having fun on the slides, the seesaw, and the sandbox. They wanted to join them, but they did not want to leave their toys on the bench. They were afraid that someone might take them or break them. They decided to take their toys with them, but they realized that they had too many. They could not carry them all by themselves. They needed help, but they did not have any friends. They looked at Max, who was still on the swings. He looked happy and kind. They wondered if he would help them. They went to Max and said they were sorry for being mean. They asked him if he wanted to play with them and their toys. Max smiled and said yes. He helped them carry their toys to the sandbox, where they built castles and bridges. They shared their toys and had fun. They learned that sharing was good and made them happy. They became friends and played together every day.

Max had an incredible Mother. Every day, Max, his Mother, and the rest of the family would gather together in the garden. Max loved it when the family gathered, because he got to play with his cousins. They would sing, dance and play all day. One day, Max noticed that his Mother had a special talent. She could make beautiful flower crowns from the flowers in the garden. She was so incredibly talented! Max asked his Mother, "Can I make one too?" His Mother smiled and said, "Of course you can, Max! Pick some flowers and I'll help you." So, Max and his Mother went to pick the flowers. Working together, the created the most incredible flower crown Max had ever seen. He was so proud. From that day, Max and his Mother made many more flower crowns together, gathering together every day in the garden.
