Title: Lily the Girl and the Leaves

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big tree with colorful leaves. Lily wanted to decorate her head with the leaves. She picked up some leaves and put them on her head. "Look, Mommy!" Lily said. "I have a colorful head now!" Her mommy smiled and said, "Yes, you do! It looks very pretty." Lily played in the park all day with her colorful head. She showed her friends, and they decorated their heads too. They all had so much fun with their colorful heads and played until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a prince named Tom. He lived in a big castle with his mom and dad. The prince was a good boy, but sometimes he was a bit harsh when he talked to others. He had a big party coming up, and he wanted everything to be perfect. One day, the prince told his helper, "I want you to serve the best food at my party." The helper nodded and said, "Yes, prince Tom, I will do that." The prince smiled, but he didn't say thank you. The helper felt a little sad. At the party, everyone was happy and having fun. The helper served the best food, just like the prince asked. But then, the prince saw a big cake. He didn't know the helper made it just for him. He said, "Who made this cake? I don't like it." The helper felt very sad and went away. Later, the prince found out that the helper made the cake. He felt bad for being harsh. He went to the helper and said, "I am sorry for being mean. Thank you for the cake." The helper smiled and forgave the prince. From that day on, prince Tom learned to be kind and say thank you. Everyone lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely cow named Daisy. Daisy lived on a farm with her friends. She had a big, red barn to sleep in and lots of green grass to eat. One day, Daisy decided to take a walk around the farm. She saw her friend, the horse, eating some hay. Daisy said "hello" and kept walking. Next, she saw the chickens playing in the yard. Daisy thought they looked like they were having fun. At the end of her walk, Daisy found a big, shiny pond. She took a long drink of the cool water. Daisy felt happy and refreshed. She walked back to her barn, ready for a nice nap. And Daisy had a lovely day on the farm.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Ella. She was only three years old and was full of life. Every day, Ella went to the stage at the park with her friend Marco. Marco was a lot older than Ella, but the two of them were always playing together. One day, Ella and Marco were running around the stage when Ella noticed something. She called out to Marco, "Look Marco! There's a warm hug on the stage!" Marco ran over and Ella showed him a big warm hug shaped like a circle. Marco exclaimed, "That's amazing, Ella! What shall we name it?" Ella thought for a while and then said, "How about we call it 'Grandma's Warm Hug'? Grandma always gives the best hugs." Marco smiled and said, "I think Grandma's Warm Hug is the perfect name!". From that day forward, Ella and Marco always made sure to give Grandma's Warm Hug a big smooch before they left the stage.

One day, a big dog named Max found a little brush in his yard. Max liked the brush because it was soft and nice. He thought, "I will protect this brush because it is my friend." Max met a small cat named Lily. Lily saw the brush and said, "Max, can I play with the brush too?" Max said, "No, Lily. I must protect my brush. It is difficult for me to share." Lily was sad and walked away. Later, Max was playing with the brush near the road. He did not see a car coming. The car hit the brush and it broke. Max was very sad. He wished he had shared the brush with Lily.
