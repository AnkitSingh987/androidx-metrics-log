Title: Sue the Girl and the Song

Once upon a time there was a girl named Sue. She was very adorable and loved to have parties. Today she was having a special birthday party and she had invited all her friends. Everyone was very excited! At the party, Sue's friends sang her a special song and gave her lots of gifts. When it was time to open the last gift, Sue was very surprised. Inside was a cute little puppy! He was so adorable. Sue was so happy to have her little puppy and named him Spot. Sue accepted Spot and he was now part of her family. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little girl named Daisy. She loved to keep her room clean and her toys in order. Daisy had a best friend named Tom. Tom was not as tidy as Daisy, but they played well together. One day, Daisy wanted to borrow Tom's toy car. She asked Tom, "Can I borrow your car, please?" Tom said yes, and they played together all day. They had so much fun with the toy car, taking turns and laughing. At the end of the day, Daisy gave the toy car back to Tom. She said, "Thank you for letting me borrow your car." Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Daisy." They both learned that sharing and borrowing can be fun and make their friendship even better.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to build things. They used blocks, sticks, stones, and anything they could find. They made houses, cars, castles, and rockets. They had fun and shared their toys. One day, a new boy came to their street. His name was Sam. He had unusual eyes. One was blue and one was brown. He also had a big scar on his arm. He looked different from Lily and Ben. Lily and Ben saw Sam walking with his mom. They wanted to say hello, but they felt shy. They thought Sam might not like them. They thought he might not want to play with them. They hid behind their blocks and watched him. Sam saw Lily and Ben's blocks. He liked to build things too. He wanted to join them, but he felt shy. He thought Lily and Ben might not like him. He thought they might not want to play with him. He smiled and waved at them. Lily and Ben saw Sam's smile and wave. They felt happy. They realized Sam was friendly. They realized he wanted to play with them. They smiled and waved back. They invited him to their block pile. They said, "Welcome, Sam. Do you want to build with us?" Sam felt happy. He said, "Yes, please. Thank you, Lily and Ben. Do you want to see my scar? It's from a dog bite. It doesn't hurt anymore." Lily and Ben said, "Yes, please. We want to see your scar. It's cool. We like your eyes too. They're unusual. They're beautiful." Sam, Lily, and Ben became friends. They built things together. They had fun and shared their toys. They learned to like each other's differences. They learned to be kind and welcoming.

One day, two friendly friends were outside playing. They were enjoying the sunshine, and having lots of fun. Suddenly, one of the friends saw something in the distance. It was a bright light, shining from behind a hill. The friends were curious, so they decided to explore and see what it was. When they arrived, they saw a beautiful sight: a brightly shining star twinkling in the night sky. They were both amazed. One of them wanted to stay and watch the star, while the other wanted to go. This created a conflict between the two friends. In the end, the two friends found a way to come up with a solution. They both agreed that they could take turns watching the star. This way, they could both share the wonderful sight and find a way to be friends. After that, the two friends had many more adventures and enjoyed their time together in the friendly sunshine.

Milly was an inquisitive 3-year-old. She loved spending time exploring things around her. One day, she found a mysterious photo on the ground. She picked it up and took it to her parents. "Mama, Papa. What's this?" she asked curiously, holding up the photo. Milly's parents weren't sure. They examined the photo and noticed it seemed quite old. Suddenly, her father spoke. "Let's spend some time trying to figure it out," he said. Milly was ecstatic! She loved mysteries and couldn't wait to solve it. The family spent the whole day asking around and exploring the area, but they couldn't find any clues. In the end, the mysterious photo remained a mystery. Milly smiled. She had loved spending the day with her parents trying to solve the mystery. That made it the best day ever.
