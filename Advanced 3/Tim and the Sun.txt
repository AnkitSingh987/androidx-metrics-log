Title: Tim and the Sun

One day, Tim was excited. He had a plan to draw a big sun. He took his paper and crayons. He started to draw. He drew a big circle and rays. The sun was very pretty. Then, Tim wanted to draw a house. But he made a mistake. He needed an eraser. He looked for the eraser. He found it under the bed. He was happy. He erased the mistake and drew the house again. But, the eraser was magic! The house came to life! Tim was surprised. He learned that it's okay to make mistakes. Sometimes, mistakes can be fun and special.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, soft carpet in his room. He loved to play on it with his toys. One day, his mommy told him, "Tim, if you don't clean your room, you will be punished." Tim didn't want to be punished, so he started to clean his room. But while cleaning, he found a small, hidden door under the carpet. Tim was very curious and opened the door. He saw a bright light and went inside. In the hidden room, Tim found a magic toy box. The toys were alive and they could talk! One toy said, "Hello, Tim! We were waiting for you. You found us, so you won't be punished. Let's play together!" Tim was so happy and played with his new toy friends all day. When he went back to his room, it was clean and his mommy was proud of him.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy kid. He loved to explore and go on adventures. One day, he decided to go searching around his house. He looked high and low in all the rooms, but he couldn't find anything exciting. Then he decided to look outside. He hopped and skipped around his garden, peering under bushes and behind trees. Suddenly, he found a big, hairy caterpillar. It was the most amazing thing he had ever seen! The caterpillar had big, furry feet and horns on its head. The kid was so excited that he ran back into the house to show his mom. He showed her the hairy caterpillar and she told him he had found a rare and special insect. She put the caterpillar in a jar and put it in the window so the kid could keep an eye on it. The kid was so happy that he now had a special friend to take care of. They would play hide and seek every day, and the kid would find other exciting creatures in the garden. Life was so much fun when you could go on so many adventures!

Jill and Jack were very bored. They had been playing inside for hours, and didn't know what to do. Suddenly, Jack had an idea. "Let's go to the playground and go on the slide!" he exclaimed. Jill smiled. She loved slides. They quickly put on their shoes and ran outside to the playground. As they arrived, they could hear the other kids playing and laughing. But the best bit was the big red slide! Jack ran to the top and Jill followed him. As they sat at the top, they both smiled. They started to speak to each other. "Let's race to the bottom!" Jack said. Jill giggled. She knew it would be fun. They took two deep breaths and pushed off down the slide. Jack was the first one off the slide, but Jill wasn't far behind! She had won the race against her boring day!

Once upon a time, there was a hairy woman. She lived in a small house. The woman had a cat. The cat was her best friend. They played together every day. One day, the woman and the cat disagreed. The woman wanted to play with a ball, but the cat wanted to play with a toy mouse. They both wanted to play, but they could not agree on what to play with. The woman and the cat were sad. They did not want to play alone. They tried to play with the ball and the toy mouse at the same time, but it did not work. They were still sad. The woman and the cat stopped playing together. They were not friends anymore. The cat went away and did not come back. The woman was very sad and missed her cat. This story has a bad ending because the woman and the cat could not agree and lost their friendship.
