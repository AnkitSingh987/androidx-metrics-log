Title: Fin the Restless Fish and the Gilly

One day, a restless little fish named Fin was swimming in a small pool. Fin did not like to stay still. He always wanted to swim fast and play with his friends. The other fish in the pool would often tell Fin, "You need to rest sometimes, or you will get tired." One day, a big storm came, and the pool started to get very full. The water was moving fast, and all the fish were scared. Fin's best friend, Gilly, was having a hard time swimming. Fin swam up to Gilly and said, "I will support you, Gilly. We will swim together." Fin and Gilly swam side by side, helping each other in the strong water. The other fish saw how Fin was supporting Gilly and started to help each other too. The storm went away, and the water in the pool became calm again. After the storm, all the fish were very tired. Fin realized that sometimes it is good to rest. He told Gilly, "I will try to rest more, so I can be strong when my friends need help." Gilly smiled and said, "That's a good idea, Fin. We all need to rest and support each other." From that day on, Fin learned to balance his restless energy with rest. He became a better friend and a stronger fish, always ready to support his friends when they needed him. And the pool was a happier place for all the fish.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with clay. He would put the clay on the table and make fun shapes with it. One sunny day, Tim made a big clay ball. He put a smiley face on it and showed it to his mom. She was very happy and clapped her hands. Tim felt proud and played with his clay ball all day. His friends came over and they all laughed and had fun with the clay. They put the clay in their hands and made more shapes. It was a jolly day for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a big couch in the living room. It was a nice place to sit and play. One day, Mom said she would add a new pillow to the couch. The little boy, Tim, was very excited to see the new pillow. That night, Tim had a dream. In the dream, he saw a big, scared cat. The cat was frightened because it could not find a nice place to sleep. Tim wanted to help the cat, but he didn't know how. The next day, Mom added the new pillow to the couch. Tim saw the pillow and remembered his dream. He knew what to do. He put the new pillow on the couch and called the cat. The cat jumped on the couch and laid on the new pillow. The cat was not frightened anymore. Tim was happy that he could help the cat. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once there was a little girl and she had the brightest smile. Everywhere she followed the happy lady with a kind voice and loving eyes. The lady showed her the world and taught her about all the wonderful things it could bring. One day, the little girl and the happy lady went for a walk in a big park. The sun shined brightly as the lady told the little girl about the colourful flowers and sweet birds in the sky. She even saw a big tree with leaves that rustled in the wind. The little girl laughed and decided to follow the lady even further. They went past the pond, where they could see the fishes swimming around, and through the meadow filled with bright yellow dandelions. All of a sudden, the lady leaned down and picked up a shiny penny. She showed it to the little girl and said it was a special surprise just for her! The little girl's eyes sparkled as she hugged the lady and thanked her. The bright day was coming to an end, and they headed home while the little girl clung onto her new lucky penny. She whispered her thanks to the kind lady and vowed to continue to follow her loving lead.
