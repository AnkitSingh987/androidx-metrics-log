Title: Susie and David and the Park

Once upon a time, there were two children, Susie and David, who were best friends. They liked to play together and had lots of fun. One day, Susie and David were walking in the park, when they saw a beautiful picture hanging up on a tree. It was colourful and sparkled in the sun. Excitedly, they ran over and Susie grabbed it. But, David said, "No! That's not yours." Susie was angry and said, "It is too!" She started to yell and David got scared. But then, something strange happened. All of a sudden, the picture started to melt in Susie's hands! It was soft and hot, and had a bitter smell. Susie was sad and started to cry. She looked at David and said, "I'm sorry. I was wrong. I won't do it again." David smiled and wiped away her tears. He said, "That's okay. Let's go and find something else to play with." And so, Susie and David had fun together again.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy lady. She lived in a big house with many pets. One day, she saw a small bug crawl on her floor. "Oh no!" the lady said. "You are in trouble, little bug. You cannot stay in my house." The bug looked at the lady and said, "Please, let me stay. I will not cause any trouble. I will be your friend." The wealthy lady thought about it. She decided to let the bug stay. They became good friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a kind elderly man. He had a big red car. He loved to drive his car around the town. One day, his car stopped working. It needed new oil. The elderly man went to the store. He bought a big jug of oil. He wanted to replace the old oil in his car. He brought the oil home and put it in his car. His car was happy to have new oil. The elderly man and his big red car went for a drive. They drove around the town and waved to their friends. Everyone was happy to see the man and his car. And the car was happy to have new oil.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy car. He would push it all around the house. One day, he decided to play near the garage. Tom was tired, but he wanted to keep playing. He pushed the toy car harder and harder. The car went faster and faster. It went into the garage. Tom was not allowed to go in the garage, but he wanted his toy car back. Tom went into the garage to get his toy car. He was scared, but he wanted to play more. When he got the car, he heard a loud noise. The garage door closed. Tom was stuck inside. He cried and cried, but nobody heard him. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old man who lived in a small village. His name was Grandpa Harry. One day, Grandpa Harry and his three year old grandson decided to go for a walk in the nearby forest. As they were walking, Grandpa Harry said to his grandchild: "Remember, always be wise and never quit, no matter how hard things may get." The little boy nodded, not really understanding but knowing that it was important. They reached a big lake and took off their suits. Grandpa Harry smiled, remembering the days when he was a child and had done the same. He said to his grandson: "Life can be hard, and sometimes it's easy to quit. But always remember, things can be hard but if you stay strong, you will be rewarded one day." The little boy hugged Grandpa Harry and smiled at his wise words, before they jumped into the lake to have some fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little church in a small town. People would go there to pray and sing. One day, an unknown man came to the town. He was very nice and would help everyone. The unknown man saw that the church was very old and needed help. He decided to fix the church. The man and the people of the town worked together to make the church beautiful again. They painted the walls and fixed the broken things. One day, it started to rain very hard. The rain would pour down and not stop. The people were sad because they thought the church would get wet and dirty again. But the unknown man had a big idea. He made a big umbrella to cover the church. The rain could not touch the church, and it stayed dry and clean. The people were so happy and thanked the unknown man. The moral of the story is that helping others can bring happiness to everyone.
