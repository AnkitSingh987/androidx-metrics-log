Title: Tom and the Car

One day, Tom and his mom went for a drive. They saw many things on the road. They saw a big red truck and a small blue car. Tom was very happy to see all the things outside. As they drove, Tom saw a bulb on the ground. It was a pretty yellow bulb. Tom wanted to take it home and show it to his dad. His mom stopped the car and picked up the bulb for Tom. When they got home, Tom showed the bulb to his dad. But the bulb did not work. Tom was upset. His dad said, "Don't worry, we can get a new bulb." They went to the store and bought a new bulb. Tom was happy again.

One time, Sandy the bear and his best friend, Henry the rabbit, were in the woods. They were collecting leaves to bake in the oven. Sandy was a very clean bear and was disgusted with the amount of dirty leaves they found. He said to Henry, "These leaves are filthy! We should not bake them." But Henry disagreed. He said, "Let's keep looking. We might find some cleaner leaves." So they kept looking around the woods, finding more and more leaves. Finally, they had enough leaves to bake something special. Sandy still wasn't happy. He thought they were all too dirty. "It's no use" said Sandy. "We can't bake these filthy leaves!" But Henry just smiled. "Don't worry" he said. He put the leaves in the oven and set the timer. After a few minutes, something amazing happened. The oven doors opened and out came a delicious smelling apple pie! Sandy was so thrilled that he forgot all about the dirty leaves. They both laughed in happiness, and feasted on the pie. It was the best day ever!

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sally was walking near a little shop. She saw a sign and it said: "Delicious food!" Sally felt hungry and decided to enter the shop. When she was inside, Sally saw a delicious-looking sandwich. She wanted to eat it, but she also noticed that she had no money. Sally decided to talk to the shopkeeper. She said to her: "Please, can I have one of those sandwiches?" The shopkeeper asked her: "Do you have any money for it?" Sally shook her head and said: "No, I don't have money." The shopkeeper smiled and said: "You can work here to pay for your order. If you help me feed my hungry customers, you can have your sandwich afterward." Sally smiled and accepted the offer. She worked very hard, and when she was finished she happily sat down to enjoy the delicious sandwich. The moral of the story is: If you help others, you will get something in return.

Once upon a time, in a big, green field, there was a friendly flower. The flower was red and yellow. It was very pretty. The flower had a friend, a little boy. The little boy's name was Tim. One day, Tim came to the field to see his friend, the flower. "Hello, flower!" said Tim. "Do you know a fun game we can play?" The flower did not know, but it thought for a moment. Then, it had an idea. "Let's play hide and seek!" said the flower. Tim started to count. "One, two, three..." While Tim was counting, the flower did something unexpected. It changed its color to green! When Tim opened his eyes, he could not find the flower. "Where are you, flower?" Tim called out. The flower giggled and turned back to red and yellow. "Here I am!" it said. Tim laughed and they played all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little spoon. The spoon lived in a big kitchen with many friends. There were plates, cups, forks, and knives. They all liked to play together. One day, the spoon wanted to have a loud party. So, the spoon asked its friends, "Do you want to be loud with me?" The cups and plates said, "Yes, let's be loud!" They all jumped and danced. They made a lot of noise. The forks and knives clanked together. The plates and cups clapped. The spoon sang a fun song. They were all very loud and happy.
