Title: The Girl Called Amy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Amy. She was three years old and very weak. Amy wanted to marry her special friend Sam, so she had an idea to wrap the rings around the tree in the garden. Amy asked Sam, “Will you marry me?” Sam replied, “I want to marry you too, but my mummy said I can’t do it.” Amy was very sad, but she knew she had to find a way to make her mummy understand. She decided she had to show Sam’s mummy how special their friendship was, so she wrapped the rings around the tree in the garden. The next day, when Sam’s mummy saw the rings, she understood why Sam and Amy wanted to get married. She said, “Yes, you can marry, but wait until you’re both older!” Amy and Sam were so happy that they hugged each other and laughed. They knew they could marry when they were ready. The end.

One day, Timmy and his dog, Spot, were walking through the park. Spot was very happy. He was running and playing and sniffing everything he could find. Timmy smiled as he watched his dog, until he spotted a bicycle. He couldn't believe his eyes! Timmy wanted the bicycle so badly that he asked his mom if he could have it. Mom said she would think about it. Timmy was so excited that he ran off to play with Spot. Now he just had to wait. The next day, Timmy and Spot returned to the park. This time, mom had agreed to let Timmy have the bicycle! He pulled it along the path, so happy to finally have it. Then he remembered something. Timmy had learned a new word recently: "calm". He took a deep breath and focused on how to ride calmly and carefully. Finally, after he had learned the basics, Timmy hopped on the bicycle and pedaled as fast as he could. Spot ran alongside, barking happily as they both enjoyed the newfound freedom of the open path. Timmy was so proud that he had been able to pull it off. He couldn't wait to show everyone how well he could ride his new bicycle!

Once upon a time, there was an adorable penguin named Pippin. Pippin lived in a cold place with lots of snow and ice. He had many friends and liked to play with them all day. One day, Pippin and his friends were playing a fun game of tag. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. As they played, Pippin started to feel very tired. He stopped and let out a big yawn. "I need to rest," Pippin said to his friends. Pippin sat down on the ice to take a nap. While he was sleeping, something unexpected happened. The ice under Pippin began to crack! His friends saw this and quickly woke him up. "Pippin, the ice is breaking!" they shouted. Pippin opened his eyes and jumped off the cracking ice just in time. He was safe, and his friends were so happy. They all hugged and continued to play, but this time, they played away from the cracking ice.

Anna likes grapes. She likes to eat them and play with them. She sees a big bunch of grapes on the table. She wants to take some grapes. She climbs on a chair and reaches for the grapes. But she is too small. She cannot get the grapes. She is sad. She sees her big brother Tom. He is tall. He can get the grapes. She asks him for help. She says, "Tom, please give me some grapes. I like grapes." Tom is gentle. He loves his little sister. He smiles and says, "OK, Anna. I will give you some grapes. But you have to share with me. And you have to be careful. Do not drop the grapes. They will make a mess." He picks some grapes and gives them to Anna. She is happy. She says, "Thank you, Tom. You are nice. I will share with you. And I will be careful. I will not drop the grapes." They sit on the floor and eat the grapes. They are sweet and juicy. They enjoy the grapes. They are happy.
