Title: Max the Dog and the Lucy

Once upon a time, there was a brave dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Lucy. They loved to play in their big yard. One day, they saw a big, old tree. It was very tall and looked like it was going to fall down. Lucy said, "Max, we should stay away from that tree. It looks like it could rot and fall." Max and Lucy played in the yard every day, but they stayed away from the big, old tree. They knew it was not safe. The days went by, and the tree started to look worse. Max and Lucy knew they had to do something. One day, Max had a great idea. He told Lucy, "We can tell my friend, Mr. Beaver, to help us. He can chew the tree down, so it won't fall on our house." Lucy thought it was a good idea. They went to Mr. Beaver and asked him for help. Mr. Beaver was happy to help Max and Lucy. Mr. Beaver came to their house and started to chew on the tree. It took him a whole day, but he finally made the tree fall down safely away from their house. Max and Lucy were very happy. They knew they had been brave and smart to stay away from the tree and ask for help. From that day on, Max, Lucy, and Mr. Beaver played together in the yard. They were the best of friends, and they knew they could always count on each other to keep their house and yard safe.

Sam and Mia were at the zoo with their mom. They saw many animals, like lions, monkeys, and penguins. But Sam's favorite animal was the gorilla. He wanted to see the big, hairy gorilla up close. "Mom, can we go to the gorilla house?" Sam asked. "Sure, honey, let's go," mom said. They walked to the gorilla house, where they saw a big glass window. Behind the window, there was a family of gorillas. There was a daddy gorilla, a mommy gorilla, and a baby gorilla. The baby gorilla was playing with a ball, and the daddy gorilla was watching him. The mommy gorilla was lying on a pile of hay, yawning. "Look, mom, the mommy gorilla is sleepy," Mia said. "Maybe she had a busy day," mom said. Sam pressed his nose against the glass. He wanted to touch the gorilla. He thought the gorilla was soft and cuddly. He wished he could be friends with the gorilla. "Hi, gorilla," Sam said. "Do you want to play with me?" The mommy gorilla heard Sam's voice. She opened her eyes and looked at him. She did not like Sam. She thought Sam was too loud and too close. She was jealous of her baby. She did not want anyone to take her baby away. She growled at Sam. "Roar!" the mommy gorilla said. Sam was scared. He jumped back from the glass. He did not want to play with the gorilla anymore. He ran to his mom and hugged her. "Mom, the gorilla is mean," Sam said. "It's okay, Sam, the gorilla was just protecting her baby," mom said. "She loves her baby very much, just like I love you and Mia." Sam felt better. He understood the gorilla. He still liked the gorilla, but he did not want to bother her. He looked at the baby gorilla, who was still playing with the ball. He smiled at the baby gorilla. "Bye, gorilla," Sam said. "Have fun with your ball."

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat wanted to go to a port. The boat was very proud. It knew it was the best boat. One day, the boat saw another boat. The other boat was lost. The proud boat wanted to help. It said, "I will show you the way to the port." The lost boat was happy. They went to the port together. The lost boat said, "Thank you, proud boat. I recognize you are a good friend." The proud boat learned that being helpful is important. It was not just a big, proud boat anymore. It was now a big, proud, and helpful boat. And they all lived happily ever after.
