A young girl named Emma was playing in her garden when she came across a lumber pile. Little did she know, the lumber pile was home to a small deaf animal. Emma began to play in the lumber and soon realised that something was living inside. She began to talk to it, calling out to it, 'Hello? Are you in there?' But there was no response. 'Maybe it's a deaf animal', she thought to herself. So Emma made sure to keep her distance. She didn't want to scare or hurt the animal in any way. But she wanted to protect it. She began to bring the animal food and water each day. She also left bits of lumber around the area so the animal could make itself a home. The animal was thankful. One day, it even got close enough to Emma that she was able to pet its soft fur. And Emma continued to protect the lumber pile and the deaf animal, never forgetting its home.

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk. It was getting dark outside. She saw a big tree and sat under it. Lily felt a little sleepy and let out a big yawn. A little bird flew down and said, "Hi, Lily! What is that on your head?" Lily had a veil on her head. She told the bird, "It is a veil. My mom gave it to me. I like it a lot!" Just then, a strong wind blew. The wind took Lily's veil and it flew far away. Lily and the bird looked at each other, very surprised. They did not know what to do. The bird said, "Don't worry, Lily. I will help you find your veil." So, they went on an adventure. They looked high and low, and after a while, they found the veil stuck in a bush. Lily was so happy to have her veil back. She thanked the bird for helping her. They became good friends and played together until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, in a cold forest, there lived a small rabbit named Remy. Remy was a very good rabbit. He always listened to his mom and dad. He had many friends in the forest. One day, Remy and his friends wanted to play a game. The game was to see who could jump the highest. Remy was small, but he believed he could win. His friends were bigger, but Remy did not give up. He tried very hard and jumped very high. Remy won the game! His friends clapped for him. Remy felt happy. This was a victory for him. The moral of the story is to always believe in yourself. Even if you are small or things are hard, you can still win. Just like Remy, always try your best and never give up.

One day, a fast octopus named Ollie lived in the sea. Ollie loved to swim and play with his friends. He was very good at swimming and could go very fast. All his friends liked to watch him swim. One day, Ollie found a big ball. He picked it up and showed it to his friends. They all wanted to play with the ball. Ollie said, "Let's play catch!" So, they started to throw the ball to each other. Ollie was so excited that he swam very fast to catch the ball. But then, something unexpected happened! Ollie was swimming so fast that he did not see a big rock. He hit the rock and drop the ball. The ball started to float away. Ollie's friends all swam fast to help him. They all pushed the big rock together and Ollie was free. Then, they all swam fast to catch the ball before it floated away. They got the ball back and were very happy. From that day on, Ollie and his friends always played together and helped each other. They learned that playing together was more fun than playing alone. And Ollie learned to be more careful when he swam fast.
