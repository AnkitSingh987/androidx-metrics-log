Title: Tim the Boy and the Drum

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys all day. His favorite toy was a big, red ball. One day, Tim was playing with his ball when he saw a box outside his house. Inside the box, Tim found a drum. It was a gift. Tim was so happy to receive a drum! He played with it all day, making loud noises. His mom and dad could hear him play the drum from far away. But one day, Tim was careless. He left his drum outside in the rain. The drum got all wet and it didn't make any noise anymore. Tim was sad. He learned to take better care of his toys. And from that day on, he made sure to keep his toys safe and dry.

Sara was a very obedient girl. She had a big, comfy pillow. Every day Sara would pretend that her pillow was a magical horse. She would jump on it and ride it through the house. One day, Sara's Mommy asked her what she was doing. Sara told her, “I’m pretending my pillow is a horse and I am riding it!” Sara’s Mommy smiled and said, “That sounds like lots of fun.” So every day, Sara pretended to ride her magical horse around the house. When it was time to take a nap, she would cuddle up with her beloved pillow, because she knew it was always her special, magical horse.

Once upon a time, there was a restless little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day long. One day, Spot saw a big cushion in the living room. It was so soft and fluffy! Spot jumped on the cushion and started to play. Spot's friend, a little cat named Whiskers, walked into the room. Whiskers saw Spot playing on the cushion and said, "Spot, you should not jump on the cushion. It is for comfort, not for play." Spot stopped jumping and looked at Whiskers, feeling a little sad. Whiskers walked over to Spot and said, "It's okay, Spot. We can still have fun together without jumping on the cushion." They decided to play a game of tag instead. After playing, both friends were tired, so they lay down on the cushion to rest. Spot now understood the cushion was for comfort, and he learned to treat things with care.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play and run around the town. One day, Tom saw a big building called the temple. The temple was very pretty and had many colors. Tom was very excite to go inside and see what was in the temple. Inside the temple, Tom met a small mouse named Jerry. Jerry was very nice and wanted to be friends with Tom. They started to play and run around the temple. Tom was very happy to have a new friend. They laughed and played all day long. Tom said to Jerry, "I am so happy we are friends!" Jerry smiled and said, "Me too! Let's play together every day!" Tom and Jerry became the best of friends and played together at the temple every day. They were always happy and excite to see each other. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily was in the kitchen with her mom. Her mom was making toast for breakfast. Lily liked her toast with butter and jam. The smell of the toast made Lily's tummy rumble. Lily's mom said, "Lily, I have to leave the room for a moment. Can you stay calm and wait for the toast?" Lily nodded her head and said, "Yes, mom. I can wait." While her mom was gone, Lily heard a noise. It was her cat, Max. Max jumped on the table and tried to eat the toast. Lily said, "No, Max! That is my toast!" Max looked at Lily and jumped down from the table. Lily's mom came back and saw that Lily had saved the toast. She was proud of her little girl. They sat down together and enjoyed their breakfast.
