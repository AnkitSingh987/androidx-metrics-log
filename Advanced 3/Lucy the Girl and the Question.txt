Title: Lucy the Girl and the Question

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside in the open. One day, she saw a big post near her house. It had a question on it. Lucy wanted to answer the question, but she was too small to reach the post. Lucy asked her friend Tom for help. Tom was taller than Lucy. But Tom was scared to answer the question on the post. He thought it was a trick. Lucy and Tom had a fight. They were both sad. Then, Lucy's mom came to help. She lifted Lucy up so she could answer the question on the post. The question was, "What is your favorite color?" Lucy said, "My favorite color is pink!" Tom saw that it was not a trick, and he answered too. His favorite color was blue. They both were happy and stopped fighting. They played together in the open again, and they were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue lived on a farm with her mom, dad, and big brother. They had a big field full of corn. Sue loved to play in the corn field and feel the comfortable wind on her face. One day, Sue's mom told her that they would receive a visit from their neighbor, Mr. Brown. He was an old man who lived alone and needed help. Sue's mom asked her to take some corn to Mr. Brown. Sue put the corn in a basket and went to Mr. Brown's house. When she arrived, Mr. Brown was very happy to see her. He thanked Sue for the corn and they sat down to eat it together. Sue felt good because she helped someone. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, she saw a dirty bag near her house. She did not know what was inside, but she wanted to find out. Sue picked up the dirty bag and brought it inside her house. Sue opened the dirty bag and found lots of oats inside. She was very happy because she loved to eat oats. Sue put the oats in a big bowl and went to tell her mom. She knew that her mom could make something yummy with them. Sue's mom looked at the oats and smiled. She knew just what to make. Sue and her mom worked together to make a big, yummy oat cookie. When it was done, they shared the cookie with Sue's friends. Everyone loved the cookie, and they all said thank you to Sue for bringing the oats. The dirty bag had a nice surprise inside after all.

One day, a girl named Lily went to visit her friend Tom. They wanted to play and have fun. Lily had a big, loud laugh that made everyone happy. Tom had a new picture book that he wanted to show Lily. They sat down to look at the pretty pictures together. As they looked at the picture book, a big dog named Max entered the room. Max saw the book and wanted to play too. He jumped on the book and ripped a page. Tom was sad and said, "Max, you broke my book!" Lily thought for a moment and said, "Don't worry, Tom. We can fix it!" They found some tape and put the ripped page back together. Tom was happy again, and they continued to look at the picture book. Max sat quietly, and they all enjoyed the rest of the day together.

Once upon a time, there was a baby mouse named Tuesday. She loved playing in the backyard of her home. One day, Tuesday decided to explore the house. She had heard stories of the big humans that lived inside it, but she had never seen them before. As Tuesday snuck around the house, she noticed something nice in the kitchen. It was a big block of cheese! Tuesday quickly snatched the cheese and was ready to escape. Suddenly, a loud noise came from upstairs. "Oh no!" said Tuesday. She knew it was the big humans she had heard stories about. But it was too late, they had heard Tuesday and were coming down the stairs! Quickly, Tuesday turned around and ran as fast as she could. She couldn't believe it when she was able to escape out of the house and back into the safety of the yard. Tuesday was very proud of herself. Even though the humans almost caught her, she managed to escape with her stolen prize: the big block of nice cheese.
