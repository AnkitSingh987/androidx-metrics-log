Title: The Statue and the Park

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was a statue. The statue was of a big man. He looked very bossy. The kids liked to play near the statue every day. One sunny day, a little girl wanted to climb the statue. But, the bossy statue seemed to say "No!" The little girl was sad. She asked her friends to help her climb the statue. They all tried, but the statue seemed to refuse. The kids were not happy. They decided to ask their moms and dads for help. The moms and dads came and saw the bossy statue. They told the kids that it was not safe to climb. The kids understood and played near the statue, but never tried to climb it again.

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was a green basketball. The basketball was very happy because kids loved to play with it. One sunny day, two friends, Tom and Tim, found the green basketball. They both wanted to play with it. But they did not want to share. They started to quarrel. "Why don't we play together?" said a wise old man who was watching them. "You can both have fun with the green basketball if you play together." Tom and Tim thought about it and agreed. They stopped fighting and played with the green basketball together. They had so much fun and became even better friends. The moral of the story is that sharing and playing together is more fun than fighting over things.

Tom and Lily were playing with marbles in the garden. They liked to roll them on the grass and see who could get them closer to the big rock. They had many marbles of different colors and sizes. "Look, Lily, I have a blue marble with white spots. It is very pretty," Tom said, holding up his marble. "I have a red marble with yellow stripes. It is very fast," Lily said, showing her marble. They took turns rolling their marbles and counting the steps to the rock. Tom was winning by one step. "Last round, Lily. Hurry up and roll your marble," Tom said. Lily nodded and threw her marble with all her might. It flew over the grass and hit the rock with a loud clang. Then it bounced back and rolled into the road. "Oh no, my marble!" Lily cried. She ran after it, forgetting to look both ways. Tom saw a big car coming fast on the road. He shouted, "Lily, stop! Car!" But it was too late. The car hit Lily and her marble. There was a loud screech and a thud. Lily and her marble did not move. Tom was very sad and scared. He wished he had not told Lily to hurry. He wished he had been more obedient and stayed away from the road. He wished he could have his sister and her marble back.

One day, a boy named Tom went to play in his room. He saw his big wardrobe and wanted to look inside. The wardrobe was very rough, but Tom didn't care. He opened the door and saw many clothes. Tom wanted to wear his dad's big coat. He tried to reach it, but it was too high. So, he found a small chair and stood on it. As he grabbed the coat, his foot began to slip. "Oh no!" said Tom, as he fell to the floor. His mom heard the noise and came running. "Are you okay, Tom?" she asked. Tom said, "Yes, I just slipped. I wanted to wear dad's coat." Mom smiled and said, "Be careful next time. Let me help you." She put the coat on Tom and they both laughed.

Once upon a time there was a brave girl named Nancy. She wanted to go on an exciting journey, so off she went. She moved through valleys, up mountains, and over rivers. As she moved, she saw lots of lovely things. A bright rainbow, a cute hedgehog, and twinkling stars. Every night, she camped in a different spot and looked up at the sky. It was so beautiful! After a while, Nancy reached her destination. It was a big city far away, and she had made her whole journey by herself. It was a lovely place with lots of people and colourful buildings. Nancy felt so brave and proud. She had used her determination, courage and love for adventure to make an incredible journey. She had fun and experienced lots of lovely things along the way!
