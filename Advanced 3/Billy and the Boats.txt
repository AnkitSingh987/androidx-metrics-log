Title: Billy and the Boats

Billy had been watching the boats sailing in the sea all day. The boats were getting higher and higher until finally they were so high that Billy could not see them anymore. He was starting to get worried that he would never see the boats ever again, but then he noticed something. He saw a big bright light from the horizon and he knew what it was - the boats were coming back! Joyfully, Billy ran out to the water's edge and stood there, looking up to the sky and waited. Then after a while, the boats returned - they were higher than they'd ever been before! Billy was so happy he was jumping and clapping with joy! He knew that although it had seemed like these boats had gone away forever, they had actually come back bigger and better than ever before!

One day, a driver named Tom was in his car. He liked to speed very fast. He had a friend named Lily. Lily did not like to speed. She liked to go slow and be safe. One day, Tom and Lily went to buy spicy food. They liked spicy food a lot. "Tom, please do not speed today," said Lily. "We can go slow and be safe." Tom said, "Okay, Lily, I will not speed today." But Tom forgot his promise. He started to speed very fast. Suddenly, a big dog ran in front of the car. Tom hit the brakes and the car stopped just in time. The spicy food flew out of the car and landed on the dog. The dog liked the spicy food and became their friend. Tom learned not to speed and to be safe.

Once upon a time, there was a regular boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his plastic toys. Every day, he played with his toys and had fun. One day, while playing, Tom saw his plastic toy car stop on its own. He was confused. Why did the car stop? He tried to push the car, but it would not move. Then, something unexpected happened. The plastic toy car started to talk! It said, "Hi Tom, I am your toy car!" Tom was very surprised. From that day on, Tom and his talking toy car became best friends and had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a hidden bridge in the woods. It was behind many trees and bushes. The bridge was very old and no one used it anymore. One day, a little boy named Tim found the bridge. He wanted to test if it was safe to walk on. He put one foot on the bridge and then the other. The bridge did not break, so he walked across it. On the other side of the bridge, Tim found a beautiful garden with flowers and butterflies. He played there all day and had so much fun. When it was time to go home, he crossed the hidden bridge again and went back to his house. Tim couldn't wait to tell his friends about the hidden bridge and the garden he found.

Sara and Ben like to play in the park. They run and jump and laugh. They see a big tree with brown leaves. The tree is dry and sad. "Look, Sara, the tree is not happy," Ben says. "Why is the tree dry?" "I don't know, Ben. Maybe it needs water," Sara says. "Let's help the tree." They find a bucket and a tap. They fill the bucket with water. They carry the bucket to the tree. They pour the water on the tree. "Here, tree, drink some water," Sara says. "We want you to be happy." The tree feels the water on its roots. The tree feels a little better. The tree says thank you. "Thank you, Sara and Ben. You are very kind. You make me happy," the tree says. Sara and Ben smile. They hug the tree. They are happy too. They play some more in the park. They are friends with the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Bob. Bob was not very nice. He did not have many friends because he did not like to share. Bob always wanted more things for himself. One day, a nice boy named Tim came to visit Bob. Tim wanted to be friends with Bob. Tim asked Bob if he wanted to play with him. But Bob said no because he did not want to share his toys. Tim was sad and left Bob's house. Bob was alone and started to feel lonely. He realized that being mean and not sharing was not good. But it was too late, and Tim did not want to visit Bob again. The bad ending of the story is that Bob stayed alone and had no friends. The moral value of the story is that we should always be kind and share with others, or we might end up lonely like Bob.
