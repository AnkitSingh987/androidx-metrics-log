Title: The Gorilla Was Walking and the Bird

One day, a big gorilla was walking in the forest. He looked troubled. He did not know where to find food. He walked and walked, but he could not find anything to eat. He felt very sad. Then, he saw a small bird. The bird saw the gorilla and asked, "Why are you sad?" The gorilla said, "I am hungry, and I cannot find food." The bird thought for a moment and then said, "I will help you find food if you promise to be my friend." The gorilla shrugged and said, "Okay, I will be your friend." The bird led the gorilla to a big tree with lots of fruit. The gorilla was happy and ate a lot. Then, something unexpected happened. The gorilla started to fly! He had become a flying gorilla! Now, the gorilla could find food easily, and he and the bird became best friends. The moral of the story is that when you help others, good things can happen to you too.

Once upon a time there was a beautiful princess. She was walking in the forest when she started to slip on the leaves. She couldn't keep her balance and slowly fell to the ground. Suddenly, a friendly bear came to help her. He said to the princess, "You must be careful when you walk in the forest. You must take slow steps!" The princess thanked the bear and said, "Thank you for looking after me. I will be more careful next time." The bear waved goodbye and the princess continued her journey, but this time walking slowly and carefully.

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat yummy food. One day, Tim went to the store with his mom to buy some yummy food. At the store, Tim saw a big cake. He wanted to eat the cake, but his mom said no. Tim was very sad. He wanted the cake so much that he tried to take it. But, when he touched the cake, it fell on the floor. Tim's mom was not happy. Tim knew he had to fix the mess. He found a cloth and started to wipe the floor. His mom saw him trying to clean up and smiled. She knew Tim was sorry. They bought a small cake to share and went home. Tim learned that it is important to listen to his mom and not to take things without asking.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom was walking down the street. He saw a police car go by. The police car had loud noises and bright lights. Tom thought it was fun, so he decided to follow the police car. As Tom followed the police car, he met a dog named Max. Max said, "Hi Tom, what are you doing?" Tom replied, "I am following the police car. It is fun! Do you want to come with me?" Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, let's go!" Tom and Max followed the police car and saw it stop at a house. The police helped a lady who had lost her keys. When the lady found her keys, she thanked the police. Tom and Max were happy they followed the police car and saw the good things the police do. They went home and told all their friends about their fun day.

One day, a very hungry cat named Tom went to an office. He was looking for food. In the office, he saw a big box. The box was closed. Tom was very excited because he thought there might be food inside the box. Tom asked a dog named Max, "Can you help me open the box? I am very hungry." Max said, "Sure, let's open the box together." They both pushed the box and it opened. But there was no food inside. Instead, there were toys for kids. Tom and Max were sad because they were still hungry. But then, they saw a man come into the office with a big bag of food. The man shared his food with Tom and Max. They were very happy and not hungry anymore. The moral of the story is: when we help each other, good things can happen. Even if we don't get what we want right away, we might get something even better later.
