Title: Lily the Girl and the Wolf

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, messy room with toys all over the floor. One night, Lily went to bed and had a dream. In her dream, she met a friendly wolf. The wolf said, "Hello, Lily! Your room is very messy. Can I help you clean it up?" Lily was happy and said, "Yes, please!" Together, they picked up all the toys and made the room neat and tidy. The wolf said, "Good job, Lily! Now your room is nice and clean." Lily thanked the wolf and woke up. She saw her messy room and remembered her dream. So, she started to clean her room just like she did with the wolf in her dream.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the mud. He would jump and splash, making a big mess. One day, Tim got an idea. He wanted to record himself playing in the mud. Tim asked his mom for a camera. She said yes, but told him not to get it dirty. Tim was very careful. He put the camera on a chair and pressed the red button. Now, the camera was recording him playing in the mud. Tim had so much fun, but then he slipped. He fell right into the mud. The mud went everywhere, even on the camera. Tim felt guilty because he made the camera dirty. He told his mom what happened. She was not mad. She helped him clean the camera and they watched the video together. They laughed and had a great time.

One day, a giant stood in a field. He was huge and his arms were spread wide. He was very enthusiastic. Suddenly, a tiny 3 year old child walked up to him and stopped. She looked up at the giant and smiled. The giant was surprised to see such a small child. The giant said: "Hello young one! Why have you come to see me?" The little girl replied: "I heard you were very enthusiastic, so I wanted to meet you!" The giant smiled and said: "That's very kind of you. Would you like to stand on my shoulders?" The little girl was so excited. She said: "Yes please!" The giant carefully lifted her up and she stood on top of his shoulders. From there, she could see far and wide. She laughed and smiled with joy. The giant said: "What do you think?" The little girl said: "It's amazing! Thank you so much!" The giant smiled and said: "You're welcome. It was my pleasure." The little girl thanked the giant and skipped away, happy and full of joy. The giant waved goodbye, feeling just as happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a nosy cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to talk and discuss things with her friends. One day, Kitty saw her friend, Bunny, wearing a pretty dress. "Wow, Bunny! I like your dress," said Kitty. "Let's discuss fashion!" Bunny smiled and they talked about pretty dresses and hats. But Kitty was nosy and wanted to know where Bunny got her dress. Bunny said, "I got it from a secret shop." Kitty wanted to know the secret, but Bunny did not tell her. This made Kitty sad, but she kept asking Bunny. One day, Kitty followed Bunny to the secret shop. She saw that the shop was full of pretty things. But when she tried to go in, the shop owner said, "You are too nosy, Kitty! You cannot come in." Kitty felt bad and went home. She learned that being nosy was not good. From that day on, she stopped being nosy and her friends liked her even more.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Bill and James. Bill was a strong battery and James was an understanding man. One day they were having a picnic, when James asked "What can you do with your strong battery?" Bill replied "I can power lots of things! I'm really strong." James was confused. "But how do you know if you are strong enough? I don't understand," said James. Bill replied "I tried powering different things and I could do it without any problems, so that's how I know I'm strong." James smiled. He now understood how Bill knew he was strong. The two friends laughed and enjoyed the rest of their picnic.
