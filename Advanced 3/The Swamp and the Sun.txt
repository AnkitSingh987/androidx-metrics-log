Title: The Swamp and the Sun

One day Emily was in her backyard when she saw a swamp. She had never seen a swamp before and she was very excited. She looked out over the swamp and it was so lively. The sun was shining and she could see butterflies whizzing through the air. Emily wanted to get a closer look so she decided to go stand in it. She hopped and skipped over to the edge of the swamp. When she got there, she heard a voice. It said, "You can stand in here if you want, but be careful! The ground is wet and it can get slippery." Emily remembered the voice's words and slowly stepped into the swamp. It felt strange standing in the swamp. She stood there, taking in all the sights. The swamp was alive with the chirping of birds and the buzzing of bees. She felt free and happy as she explored. When it was time to go home, Emily slowly stepped out of the swamp and thanked the voice for its warning. She smiled, understanding that the swamp is a safe and lively place.

One day, a poor cat was very hungry. It walked and walked to find some food. It saw a big house with a big tree. In the tree, it saw a pretty bird. The cat thought, "I can eat the bird!" The cat said, "Hello, bird! I am so hungry. Can you help me find some food?" The bird looked at the cat and said, "Yes, I can help you. I saw a shiny fork in the grass. Let's go find it together." The cat and the bird walked to the grass. They found the shiny fork. The bird said, "Now, I will reveal a secret. Under this fork, there is a big yummy cake." The cat was very happy and excited. But when they lifted the fork, there was no cake. Instead, there was a big, mean dog! The dog chased the poor cat and the bird away. They were so sad and still very hungry. The cat learned that sometimes, things are not what they seem.

One day, a sun named Sunny loved to shine very bright. Sunny was a very hot sun. He made all the plants and animals feel warm and happy. Sunny had a friend named Sink, who was a big bowl in a kitchen. Sink liked to hold water for people to wash their hands and dishes. Sunny said to Sink, "I like to shine and make everything warm. What do you like to do, Sink?" Sink replied, "I like to hold water for people to wash things. We both help people in different ways!" One hot day, Sunny was shining so bright that Sink's water became very hot too. A little girl named Lily came to wash her hands. She touched the hot water and said, "Ouch! The water is too hot!" Sink felt sad that he could not help Lily. Sunny saw that Sink was sad and decided to help. He hid behind a cloud to make the day less hot. Soon, the water in Sink became cool again. Lily came back and washed her hands with a smile. Sink was happy to help Lily, and Sunny was happy to help his friend Sink. They both knew that they could work together to make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely rocket. It sat on the ground and looked up at the sky. It wanted to fly high and see the stars. It was very sad because it could not fly. One day, a little boy found the lonely rocket. He wanted to help it fly. The boy tried to fix the rocket, but he did not know how. He thought that if he gave the rocket a lot of candy, it would be happy and fly. The boy gave the rocket lots and lots of candy. The rocket ate all the candy, but it still could not fly. The candy did not help the rocket. Instead, it made the rocket very sick. The candy spoiled the rocket, and it could never fly. The lonely rocket stayed on the ground, and the boy was sad.
