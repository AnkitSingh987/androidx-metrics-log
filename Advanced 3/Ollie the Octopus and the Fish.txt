Title: Ollie the Octopus and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little octopus named Ollie. Ollie was very shy and embarrassed to talk to his friends. He had a problem: he could not chew his food well. One day, Ollie met a friendly fish named Finn. Finn said, "Hi, Ollie! Why are you sad?" Ollie told Finn about his problem. Finn wanted to help Ollie learn to chew. Finn and Ollie practiced chewing together. They tried with soft food and hard food. Ollie got better at chewing. He was not embarrassed anymore. Ollie and Finn became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little sheep named Wooly. Wooly had lots of soft wool on his body. He loved to play with his friends in the green grass. One day, Wooly saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it. Wooly was very alert and saw the ball move fast. He had to rush to catch it. Wooly and his friends ran and played with the red ball. They were so happy. After a long day, they were tired but had lots of fun. They went back to their warm home and slept well.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. They brought a big blanket to sit on. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Lucy felt very happy. Lucy saw a boy named Tim playing with a ball. The ball rolled onto the blanket. Tim asked, "Can you explain how to fold this blanket?" Lucy said, "Yes, I can help you!" They both took the corners and pulled the blanket together. Lucy was strong and so was Tim. They folded the blanket neatly. Tim said, "Thank you, Lucy!" They both smiled and played with the ball together. The sun began to set, and it was time to go home. Lucy and Tim said goodbye and went back to their homes, excited to play together again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a big family of bears. They lived in a cozy cave in the woods. One day, Mama Bear decided to take her cubs on a visit to their Grandma Bear's house. They were all very excited and got ready for the trip. On their way to Grandma Bear's house, the cubs saw a big river. They wanted to play in the water. Mama Bear said, "No, we must not separate. We need to stay together." But the cubs did not listen. They ran to the river and started to play. Mama Bear tried to get the cubs out of the water, but they were having too much fun. They did not want to leave. Suddenly, a big wave came and separated the cubs from Mama Bear. The cubs got scared and cried for help. Mama Bear tried to save them, but it was too late. The cubs were swept away by the water, and the family never got to visit Grandma Bear's house.

Jill was a wild 3 year old. She always liked to explore new places and try new things. One day, she saw a big ear on the ground. She picked it up and was so excited. "I will take this home," Jill said. Jill went to show her mother what she had found. But when her mother saw the ear, she said, "Put it back where you found it, Jill! It doesn't belong to you." Jill was angry and replied, "No! I want to keep it!" But her mother said, "No! You must remove it." Jill was very angry and did not want to listen. She shouted back at her mother, "No! It is mine!" But then her mother said sternly, "Jill, you must remove the ear. Now!" Jill understood that she had to do what her mother said. With a heavy heart, she removed the ear from her pocket and put it back on the ground. With one last look, she sighed and went inside.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Jacques. Every day Jacques’s mom would give him a seat in the kitchen for dinner. Jacques liked his seat, because it was the most reliable seat in the house. At dinnertime, Jacques’s mom would serve him lots of his favorite foods. Jacques loved to eat broccoli the best - he could always count on his mom for a big plate of broccoli beside him. Jacques was so happy eating his broccoli each day. He knew it was a reliable treat that would make him feel full and happy. He showed his appreciation to his mom by taking an extra big bite every day. The end.
