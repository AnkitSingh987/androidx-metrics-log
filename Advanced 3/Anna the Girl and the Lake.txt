Title: Anna the Girl and the Lake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. She loved to explore her neighborhood and adventure around. One day, Anna wanted to play outside. Her mommy was in the kitchen and said, "Anna, take your umbrella with you. It's gonna rain later." Anna picked up the umbrella and went outside to play. Soon it started to rain. Anna held the umbrella to keep herself dry. As Anna was walking, she heard her mommy calling her from the door. Anna's mommy said, "Don't go too close to the lake Anna, it's dangerous." Anna said, "Ok mommy. I will stay away from the lake." Anna kept playing until the storm was over. As she walked home, she was feeling grateful for her mommy warning her to stay away from the lake. Anna is always happy to listen to her mommy's advice. The end.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was three years old and loved to pick things. One day the little girl went on a walk. She saw a beautiful landscape. It had long, green grass and colorful flowers. She wanted to pick some of the flowers to show her mom. But all the flowers were way too high. She kept walking until she saw a tree. It had big, long branches. She knew she could pick the flowers if she found a way to get up there. So she ran back home and grabbed a tall ladder. She brought it back to the tree and climbed up. She picked lots of yellow, purple and pink flowers. The little girl was so excited. She climbed back down and ran home to show her mom. The mom was so happy and proud. She put the flowers in a vase and put it on the kitchen table. The little girl smiled with pride. She had picked the most beautiful flowers ever!

Once upon a time there was a kind old lady. She was very generous and liked to help everyone. One cold winter day she was walking in the park when she saw a little bunny. The bunny had lost a button from his coat. The kind old lady had lots of buttons and searched her pocket until she found one that would fit. She kneeled down and was very careful to sew the button back on with a sharp needle. The little bunny was so happy to have his button back and thanked the kind old lady. The old lady smiled and gave the bunny a great big hug. The bunny ran off happily and the generous old lady watched him go with a warm heart.

Tom and Anna are twins. They like to play with their toys in the morning. They have many toys, but their favorite is a big red car. The car is expensive. Their dad bought it for them on their birthday. One morning, Tom and Anna wake up early. They want to play with the car. They go to the living room, where the car is. But they see something else. There is a big box on the floor. It has a bow and a card. The card says: "To Tom and Anna, from Grandma and Grandpa. We love you!" Tom and Anna are curious. They want to see what is in the box. They look at each other and nod. They open the box carefully. Inside, they find a big teddy bear. The teddy bear is soft and brown. It has a blue scarf and a smile. Tom and Anna are happy. They hug the teddy bear. They say: "Thank you, Grandma and Grandpa! We love you too!" They put the teddy bear next to the car. They decide to play with both toys. They have fun in the morning. They are good twins.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside, even when it rained. One day, it rained very hard, but Tim still wanted to play. Tim saw his friend Sam and said, "Let's play in the rain!" Sam agreed, and they played in the light rain. But soon, they started to fight about who could jump in the biggest puddle. They pushed and yelled at each other. Tim's mom saw the fight and said, "Stop fighting! You should share and have fun together." Tim and Sam felt sad and said sorry to each other. They decided to play and share the puddles. They had a lot of fun and became best friends. The moral of the story is to share and be kind to your friends. Playing together is more fun than fighting.
