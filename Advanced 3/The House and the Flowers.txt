Title: The House and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a pretty house. The house had a nice porch. On the porch, there were many attractive flowers. The flowers were red, blue, and yellow. They made the house look happy. One day, a girl named Lucy came to the house. She wanted to play a game. Lucy thought of a number. She said, "I am thinking of a number. Can you guess it?" The flowers on the porch tried to guess the number. They said, "Is it one? Is it two? Is it three?" Lucy smiled and said, "Yes! The number is three!" The flowers on the porch were happy. They danced in the wind. Lucy played with the flowers all day. They had lots of fun together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a factory. Inside the factory, there were many workers. One day, one of the workers slipped. Unfortunately, the worker fell into a machine and was not available anymore. All the other workers started to cry and were very sad. They couldn't repair the machine, which meant the factory had to close. It was a bad ending for all the workers.

One day, a big ball went for a roll in the park. The wind was strong and pushed the ball fast. The ball met a small, ignorant bug who did not know about the wind. "Hi, bug!" said the ball. "Do you want to roll with me?" The bug looked at the ball and said, "Yes, but I don't know how to roll." The ball told the bug, "Just let the wind push you, and you will roll too." So, the bug and the ball rolled together in the park. The wind pushed them fast and they had a lot of fun. The bug was happy to learn how to roll with the help of the ball and the wind.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with crayons and paper. They make pictures of animals, flowers and cars. They speak to each other and laugh. One day, a new boy comes to their class. His name is Sam. He has a big bag of crayons. He does not speak to anyone. He sits alone and makes pictures. Tom and Mia are curious. They want to see his pictures. They go to his table and say hello. Sam does not look at them. He keeps making pictures. Tom and Mia see his pictures. They are very different. They are not of animals, flowers or cars. They are of unknown things. Things with many colors, shapes and lines. Things that look like stars, monsters and machines. Tom and Mia are surprised. They do not understand his pictures. They ask him what they are. Sam does not answer. He does not speak. He only makes more pictures. Tom and Mia are sad. They want to be friends with Sam. They want to play with his crayons and learn about his pictures. They want him to speak to them and laugh. But Sam does not want to. He only wants to make pictures of unknown things.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day and gaze at the sky. He watched the birds fly and the clouds move. One day, Tom saw a big dog named Judge. Judge was always busy and never lazy. Judge liked to run and play with his friends. Tom wanted to be like Judge, but he was too lazy to try. Tom watched Judge and his friends play. He decided to try to play with them. Tom got up and ran with Judge. They had a lot of fun, and Tom learned that it was good to not be lazy.

Little Mary was eagerly awaiting evening. She was gathering sour apples from the apple tree in the backyard, so that she could make a delicious pie. She heard a loud voice behind her and jumped. "What are you up to?" her grandpa asked. "Making a pie for morning," Mary replied. Grandpa smiled and joined her in picking the apples. After a while, the two were finished, and their basket was full. "Let's take these to mom and see if she needs help making the crust," Grandpa said. Mary nodded and followedGrandpa inside. Mom thanked them for the apples and handed Grandpa a rolling pin. "I need you to roll out the dough until it's thin. Then we can put in the apples," Mom said. Grandpa carefully rolled the pin back and forth, until the dough began to yield. "Looks ready!" he said happily. Mom and Mary quickly added the sour apples, and before they knew it, they had a beautiful apple pie ready. Mary couldn't stop smiling - she couldn't wait to enjoy their tasty evening treat!
