Title: Mia the Girl and the Story

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to eat pears. One day, she went to the market and saw a brilliant, big pear. It was so shiny and yummy. Mia wanted to eat it right away. Mia asked the man at the market, "Can I have the pear, please?" The man said, "Yes, but you must promise to share it with your friends." Mia nodded her head and took the pear home. But she did not want to share it. She wanted to eat it all by herself. Mia hid the pear under her bed. When she went to get it later, the pear was gone. It had vanished! She looked everywhere, but could not find it. Mia was very sad. She wished she had shared the pear with her friends. The moral of the story is: Always share with others, or you may lose what you have.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very friendly and liked to talk to everyone she met. One day Sue met a deaf woman. Sue had never met someone who was deaf before. She was curious but didn't know how to talk to the woman. Then Sue had an idea. She went and grabbed a piece of paper and some crayons. She drew an image of a smiling face and showed it to the woman. The woman smiled and Sue knew that she had gotten her message across. The woman started to draw an image of a tree. She was trying to lend Sue the image of how peace and quiet she felt beneath a tree. Sue got the message and smiled. She felt like she and the woman were now friends.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet boy named Tim. He loved to play with his basketball. Every day, he would take his ball and play outside. He was very happy. One day, Tim could not find his basketball. He looked in his room, but it was not there. He looked in the yard, but it was not there either. Tim was sad. Then, he remembered that he had put the basketball in the shed. Tim went to the shed and used a key to unlock the door. Inside, he found his basketball. Tim was happy again and played with his basketball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a poor girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily loved to play dress up, but she only had a few old clothes in her wardrobe. One sunny day, Lily went outside to play. She found a big box near her house. Lily was curious and opened the box. Inside, she saw a beautiful dress that she had never seen before. She was so happy and put the dress on right away. But something unexpected happened. As soon as Lily wore the dress, she turned into a pretty butterfly! She flew high in the sky and played with her new butterfly friends. After a while, Lily decided to go back home. She took off the dress and turned back into a girl. From that day on, Lily knew that her wardrobe had a magical dress that could make her dreams come true.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy pig named Pinky. Pinky lived in a big, pretty house on a farm. She loved to play in the mud all day long. One sunny day, Pinky was playing in her favorite mud puddle. She liked to poke the mud with her pink snout. It was so much fun to watch the mud wiggle and jiggle. As Pinky played, she saw her friend, Ducky, walking by. Ducky wanted to play in the mud too. So, they both poked the mud and laughed. They had a great day playing together in the mud.

Once upon a time, there was a big, clumsy guard. He was very nice, but he always dropped things. His job was to show people the way in a big castle. One day, a little girl came to the castle. She wanted to see the pretty flowers in the garden. The clumsy guard said, "I will show you the flowers. Follow me!" They walked together, but the guard tripped on a rock. "Oops!" he said. The little girl laughed and helped him up. They finally found the pretty flowers. The little girl said, "Thank you, Mr. Guard!" The clumsy guard smiled and said, "You're welcome!" They became good friends and played in the garden every day.
