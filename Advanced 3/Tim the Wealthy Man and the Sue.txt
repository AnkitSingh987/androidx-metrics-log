Title: Tim the Wealthy Man and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tim. Tim loved to take a walk every day. One sunny day, while Tim was walking, he saw a little girl named Sue. "Hi, Sue!" said Tim. "Do you want to walk with me?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, I would like that!" As they walked, they saw a new family moving into a house. Tim and Sue went to welcome them. The family was very happy to meet their new friends. "Welcome to our home!" said the mom. "We are so glad to meet you!" Tim and Sue played with the family's kids and had a fun day. They all became good friends and had many more walks together.

Once upon a time, there was a purple cat named Luna. Luna was very smelly because she did not like to use water to clean herself. She lived in a small house with her friend, a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim said to Luna, "Luna, you are smelly. Let's use water and soap to make you clean and nice." Luna did not want to, but she saw that Tim was sad. So, she agreed to try it. Tim helped Luna use water and soap to clean herself. After, Luna was not smelly anymore. She felt happy and Tim was happy too. They played together all day. From that day on, Luna learned to use water and soap to stay clean. The moral of the story is to always listen to your friends because they want what is best for you.

Tom is a dog. He likes to play with his human, Anna. Anna is jolly. She always smiles and hugs Tom. She also teaches Tom new things. Tom likes to learn. One day, Anna takes Tom to the park. There are many other dogs and humans there. Tom sees a big ball. He runs to get it. He wants to play with Anna. But another dog gets the ball first. He is bigger and faster than Tom. He growls at Tom. He does not want to share. Tom is scared. He barks at the other dog. Anna hears Tom. She runs to him. She says, "No, no, bad dog! Leave Tom alone. He is my friend. You have to be nice." She takes the ball from the other dog. She gives it to Tom. She says, "Good boy, Tom. You are brave. I am proud of you." Tom wags his tail. He is happy. He licks Anna's face. He says, "Thank you, Anna. You are the best human. I love you." He learns that Anna will always protect him. He also learns to share with other dogs. He makes new friends. He has fun. He is jolly.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play outside. She was very independent and loved to explore. One day, she met a new friend named Tom. Tom said, "Amy, do you want to play a game? We can play freeze tag!" Amy smiled and said, "Yes, I love that game!" They played freeze tag all day long. When Tom would tag Amy, she would freeze like ice. When Amy tagged Tom, he would freeze too. They laughed and had so much fun. Amy and Tom became best friends. They played together every day. Their love for each other grew as they played and laughed. They were always happy when they were together. And so, Amy and Tom stayed best friends forever. They learned that playing together and sharing love made them both very happy. They continued to be independent and explore the world, but now they did it together.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Abby and Benny. They were always playing together and having a lot of fun. But one day, Abby was feeling very selfish. She was looking at an old silver coin that she found on the ground and didn't want to share it with her best friend. Benny said to her, "Abby, please share the silver coin." But Abby just shook her head no. She was really wishing to keep it all to herself. That night, Abby had a dream. She dreamt that Benny was really angry at her for being so selfish. She kept hearing his voice saying, "Abby, you need to behave better". The next day, Abby met Benny again and remembered her dream. She decided she would not be selfish anymore and decided to share the silver coin with her best friend. When Benny saw the coin he was so surprised, he said, "Thank you Abby!" The two friends hugged each other and laughed. They both knew that behaving better was always the right thing to do.
