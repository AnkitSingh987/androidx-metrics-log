Title: Lily and the Ladder

Lily was a little girl who liked to play in her room. She had many toys and books and a big bed with a pink blanket. She also had a ladder that went up to a small loft where she could hide and pretend. One night, Lily was not sleepy. She wanted to stay up and read her favorite book about fairies. She climbed the ladder with her book and a flashlight and went to the loft. She felt happy and cozy there. But after a while, she heard a loud noise outside. It sounded like thunder and rain. Lily was scared of storms. She wanted to go back to her bed and hug her teddy bear. She felt anxious and alone. She tried to climb down the ladder, but it was dark and slippery. She was afraid she would fall and hurt herself. She wished her mom or dad would come and help her. She started to cry. Then, she saw a light in the door. It was her mom. She heard her voice saying, "Lily, are you okay? I heard you crying. What are you doing up there?" Lily felt relieved and happy. She said, "Mom, I'm sorry. I was reading and I got scared of the storm. Can you help me down?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, sweetie. I'm here for you. Come on, let's go to your bed. I'll read you a story and sing you a lullaby. You'll feel better soon." She climbed up the ladder and hugged Lily. She helped her down and tucked her in her bed. She read her a story and sang her a lullaby. Lily felt calm and safe. She closed her eyes and fell asleep.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to eat oranges. One day, she saw a big orange tree in her yard. She was very happy and excited. She knew something fun was going to happen. Lucy wanted to pick the oranges, but they were too high up. So, she asked her big brother Tom for help. Tom got a long stick and helped Lucy get the oranges. They picked many oranges and put them in a big basket. Lucy and Tom took the oranges inside their house. They wanted to finish eating all the oranges. They ate and ate until their tummies were full. They were very happy they got all the oranges from the tree. The fun thing that Lucy knew would happen had happened.

Once upon a time, there was a little flea named Fifi. Fifi lived in an empty jar. She was very sad and lonely because she had no friends to play with. One day, a kind girl named Lily found Fifi in the jar. Lily said, "Oh, little flea, why are you so sad?" Fifi replied, "I'm sad because I'm all alone in this empty jar." Lily felt sorry for Fifi and wanted to care for her. So, she took Fifi home with her. Lily and Fifi played all day long. They had so much fun together. But one day, Fifi got very hungry. Fifi said, "Lily, I'm so hungry. Can you find me some food?" Lily looked around and saw a big dog. She thought the dog might have some food for Fifi. Lily put Fifi on the dog, hoping she could find some food there. But the dog felt Fifi on him and started to scratch. The dog scratched and scratched until Fifi fell off. Fifi was so scared that she ran away, never to be seen again. Lily was sad because her new friend was gone.

Once upon a time there was a farmer, and he had a leak in the roof of his barn. The farmer was worried, because the leak was getting bigger and bigger each day. The farmer asked some of his friends for help. His friends suggested that he get a gentle patch to fix the leak. The farmer went shopping and found the perfect gentle patch. He took it back and put it in place over the leak. When the farmer came back to check on the leak a week later, he was very sad. The patch was no longer there and the leak was bigger than ever. The farmer's friends came over to help, but sadly, nothing they did seemed to work. The leak had become too big, and the barn was in danger of being destroyed. The moral of the story is that it's important to take action at the earliest opportunity if something is going wrong, or else the problem could become too big to fix.
