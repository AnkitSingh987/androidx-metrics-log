Title: The Story of the Waiter and the Spaghetti

Once there was a big boy. He dreamed of eating lots of spaghetti. One day, he went to a restaurant and asked for some spaghetti. The waiter said, "Sure, kid". The big boy was so happy. He thought the waiter was nice and he trusted him. The waiter brought the spaghetti. It looked a bit boring, but the big boy was so excited to eat it, he just gobbled it up! When he finished, he asked the waiter for more. The waiter chuckled and said, "No, that's all for you". But then the big boy felt really sick. The spaghetti was bad and he knew he shouldn't have trusted the waiter. He never ate spaghetti again.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to help his dad. His dad was looking for his license. Tim felt proud that he could help his dad find it. They looked everywhere in the house. Tim asked his dad, "What does the license look like?" His dad said, "It is a small card with my name and picture on it." Tim and his dad looked under the bed, behind the couch, and in the kitchen. Finally, Tim found the license in his dad's coat pocket. He gave it to his dad and said, "Here it is!" His dad was very happy and proud of Tim. They both smiled and hugged each other. From that day, Tim always loved to help his dad with anything he needed.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to take things from her friends. One day, she took a toy from her friend, Doggy. Doggy was sad and cried. Kitty did not care and went to her bed. That night, something unexpected happened. Kitty's bed began to shake and move! It took Kitty to a faraway land. In this land, there were many sad animals. They told Kitty that someone took their toys too. Kitty felt bad and wanted to help. She found a big box with all the toys she took. Kitty gave the toys back to the sad animals. They were happy again! Kitty learned that taking things from others is not nice. From that day on, she never took anything without asking.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a creative boy named Tom. He liked to play with his toy lumber. He would build big towers and houses with the lumber. One day, Tom had a new idea. He said, "I will imagine a new game with my lumber." Tom started to play his new game. He built a big wall with the lumber. He said to his friend, "Look at my big wall. Can you jump over it?" His friend tried to jump, but he could not. He said, "No, I can't. It's too big." Just then, a small bird flew by. The bird saw the wall and said, "Why did you build this big wall?" Tom said, "I wanted to imagine a new game." The bird said, "Why don't you try to imagine a game where everyone can play together?" Tom thought about it and said, "That's a good idea!" So, Tom and his friend took down the wall. They used the lumber to build a big, fun playground. All the kids in the village came to play. They had so much fun and were very happy. Tom learned that it was better to imagine games where everyone could play and have fun together.
