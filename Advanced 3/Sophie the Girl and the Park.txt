Title: Sophie the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Sophie was walking in the park. She found a very happy hippo! She smiled and said "Hello!" The hippo said hello back and asked Sophie if she wanted to go on an adventure. Sophie was so excited and agreed. They went to the playground and met a lot of new friends. They had a blast racing, playing games, and laughing a lot. At one point, Sophie felt a bit embarrassed because she didn't know how to play the game. The hippo saw her feeling shy and quickly ran over to help. He encouraged her, and soon she was playing with the others again. After the adventure, Sophie thanked the hippo for helping to make her feel better. She said, "I'm so glad I met you today". The hippo smiled and said, "Remember that you can do anything if you have courage". The moral of this story is that with a little courage, you can do anything you set your mind to. Don't be embarrassed to try something new!

Lily wanted to create a surprise for her mom's birthday. She asked her dad to help her. They went to the kitchen and looked for things to make a cake. "What do we need, dad?" Lily asked. "We need flour, eggs, milk, sugar, butter, and chocolate," dad said. "Can you help me find them?" Lily nodded and searched the cupboards and the fridge. She found everything except the chocolate. She remembered that mom had a secret stash of chocolate in her bedroom. She ran to get it. But mom was in her bedroom, reading a book. She saw Lily sneak in and grab the chocolate. "Lily, what are you doing with my chocolate?" mom asked. Lily froze. She did not want to ruin the surprise. She thought fast. "I... I want to share it with you, mom," Lily said. "It's your birthday, and I love you." Mom smiled and hugged Lily. She knew what Lily was up to. She decided to play along. "That's very sweet of you, Lily," mom said. "But you know what? I have a better idea. Why don't we go to the kitchen and make a cake together? I think dad is waiting for us." Lily agreed. She was happy that mom was not angry. She hoped that the cake would be tasty. They went to the kitchen and joined dad. They mixed the ingredients and poured them into a pan. They put the pan in the oven and waited. They smelled the cake baking. It smelled good. When the cake was done, they took it out and let it cool. They decorated it with frosting and sprinkles. They put candles on top. They sang happy birthday to mom and blew out the candles. They cut the cake and ate it. It was tasty. Lily was proud of her surprise. She hugged mom and dad. "Happy birthday, mom," Lily said. "I love you." "I love you too, Lily," mom said. "And I love your cake. It's the best birthday ever."

Once upon a time there was a deaf prune. His name was Peter Prune. He lived in a tiny garden all by himself. One day, a little girl called Bethany came to the garden. When Bethany saw Peter Prune, she shouted "Hello!" But Peter couldn't hear her. Peter was very sad. Suddenly, Bethany had an idea. She got a big bag, and filled it with leaves. She tied it up, and shook it around. When she put it next to Peter, he could hear the rustling. His deafness was gone! Peter was so happy he jumped up and down. He thanked Bethany with a big hug. Then, they started to play with the bag. They used it to sack the leaves, and to make a big pile of prunes in the garden. Bethany and Peter had a fun day. They played until it was dark. Before Bethany left, she gave Peter one final hug and said, "Thank you". Then she went home to bed. And from that day on, Peter never forgot his special friend, Bethany. He was never deaf again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a favorite color. She loved the color blue. She had a blue dress, a blue ball, and even a blue room. Mia also liked warm hugs from her mom and dad. One day, Mia went to a store with her mom to order a blue cake for her birthday. Mia said, "Mom, can we order a big blue cake?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mia. We can order a big blue cake just for you." When they got home, Mia's dad gave her a warm hug. "I can't wait for my birthday," she said. "I will have a big blue cake!" Her dad laughed and said, "Yes, Mia, your cake will be very blue and very yummy." On Mia's birthday, she got her big blue cake. It was so pretty and yummy. Mia's friends came to her party, and they all had a great time. They played with the blue ball and ate the blue cake. Mia felt very happy and loved.
