Title: The Goat and the Log

Once upon a time, there was a little goat. This goat loved to play all day. He had a lot of fun running and jumping in the field. One day, he saw a big log and had a crazy idea. The goat wanted to balance on the log. He put one foot on the log and then another. He started to wobble, but he did not fall. The goat was so happy, he danced on the log. Soon, all the other goats came to watch. They clapped and cheered for the little goat. The little goat was so proud of himself. He had so much fun balancing on the log, and he made all his friends smile.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He was hungry. He saw the big refrigerator. It was dark in the room. Tim wanted to find food. He opened the refrigerator door. The light was on inside. He saw many things to eat. Tim heard a voice say, "Hello!" He looked around. No one was there. He looked in the refrigerator. He saw a talking apple! The apple said, "I am your friend. Let's play!" Tim was very surprised. Tim and the apple played all day. They had so much fun. But then, the apple said, "I am tired." Tim put the apple back in the refrigerator. The next day, Tim opened the refrigerator again. The apple was gone! Tim was sad. But then, he saw a talking orange. The orange said, "Hello, I am your new friend!" Tim was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy called John. John had a magic hat. He was so excited to try it on and he gathered it up from his room. Then, he put it on his head. Suddenly, he felt an anxious feeling in his chest. He didn't know why. He took his hat off and looked outside. Everything was very foggy and a mist was gathering in the air. John felt scared and he said to himself, "What is happening?" Suddenly, a friendly voice answered, "Don't worry John, the mist is just a magical fog that usually only appears around this time of the year." John was still a bit anxious, but he knew he was safe. He and the friendly voice talked until the mist cleared. John gathered up his hat and went off to play, feeling happy and safe.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her yard. She liked to pretend she was a princess. She put on a pretty dress and a shiny crown. Lily saw a path in her yard. She thought it was a secret path to a special place. Lily walked on the path and met a sweet bunny. The bunny said, "Hello, Princess! I am lost. Can you help me find my way home?" Lily said, "Yes, I will help you, Bunny." They walked together on the path, looking for the bunny's home. As they walked, they met a big mean bear. The bear said, "This is my path! You can't walk here!" Lily was scared, but she had an idea. She said, "Mr. Bear, let's pretend this path is for everyone. Then we can all be friends." The bear thought about it and then smiled. "Okay, Princess. Let's all be friends." The bear, Lily, and the sweet bunny walked together on the path, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived an incredible bird named Billy. Billy was very happy because he could fly high in the sky. One day, while Billy was flying, he saw a little girl named Lucy sitting under a tree. Lucy looked sad, so Billy flew down to her. "Why are you sad?" asked Billy. Lucy looked up and said, "I have a question. How do I fly like you?" Billy thought for a moment and said, "I can try to help you." Billy tried to help Lucy fly. They jumped and flapped their arms, but Lucy could not fly. Then, Lucy had an idea. "Let's jump from a small hill!" she said. They climbed the small hill and Lucy tried to jump. But she began to fall down the hill instead. Billy quickly flew down and caught her before she hit the ground. "Thank you, Billy," said Lucy, smiling. "Even though I can't fly, I'm happy to have a friend like you." From that day on, Billy and Lucy became best friends and played together in the forest every day.
