Title: The Dirty Horse and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a dirty horse. He lived on a farm with his friends. The dirty horse loved to play in the mud with the pigs. They had so much fun getting dirty together. One day, the dirty horse saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with it. He ran up to the ball and gave it a big slap. The ball went high into the sky. The dirty horse was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball came down and hit a bird. The bird was not hurt, but it was very mad. The bird flew down and slapped the dirty horse on the nose. The horse was surprised, but he learned not to slap things too hard. From that day on, the dirty horse played gently with his friends on the farm.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Today, it was raining hard outside. Tim looked out the window and saw the rain falling on the ground. He wanted to play outside, but he could not because of the rain. Tim's mom saw him looking sad. She said, "Tim, do you want to play a game inside with me?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Mom! Let's play a game." They played a fun game together, and Tim forgot about the rain outside. The rain stopped later, and the sun came out. Tim's mom said, "Tim, you can go outside and play now." Tim was happy and ran outside to play in the sun. He knew that even when it rained, there was always fun to be had inside with his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a sister named Emma. Emma was very gifted. She could run fast, jump high, and sing pretty songs. Lily wanted to be like Emma, but it was hard. One day, Lily tried to run as fast as Emma. Lily ran and ran, but she could not catch up. Lily felt sad and wanted to quit. But Emma said, "Don't quit, Lily! I will help you. We can do it together." The next day, Emma and Lily practiced running together. They ran every day, and Lily got better and better. Soon, Lily could run just as fast as Emma. They were both very happy. And Lily knew that she should never quit.

Once upon a time, there was a little green frog. The frog lived in a big pond with his family. One day, the frog saw a big bird fly near the pond. The bird was looking for food. The frog was scared and hid under a big leaf. The bird saw the frog and wanted to catch him. The frog knew a magic spell to make the bird go away. The frog screamed the spell as loud as he could. The spell was "Go away, big bird!" The bird heard the spell and flew away very fast. The little green frog was happy that the spell worked. He went back to his family and told them about the big bird. They were all happy that the frog was safe. From that day on, the little green frog knew that he could use his magic spell to protect his family.

Once upon a time in a far away land there lived a hungry bear. Every day, he looked around for food but could never find enough to satisfy him. One day, the bear decided to hide inside a cabinet. He felt safe and warm inside the cabinet. But soon the bear started to feel hungry again and wondered how he was going to get more food. Suddenly, the bear heard a voice saying, "If you want food, look for it outside the cabinet." The bear followed this advice and used his senses to search for food. He found plenty of food outside the cabinet and he was soon full and happy. The moral of the story is that you should never hide away from the problems in your life. Instead, you should use your senses to find solutions and succeed.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big structure to play on. Tim was so happy! He ran to the structure and started to climb up. As Tim was playing, he saw a girl named Lily. Lily had a delicious apple. Tim wanted the apple, but Lily didn't want to share. "No, it's my apple!" said Lily. Tim felt sad. Then, Tim saw another apple on a tree. He tried to reach it, but he couldn't. Suddenly, the apple fell from the tree! Tim picked it up and took a big bite. It was so delicious! Tim and Lily both had apples now, and they played happily together on the structure.
