Title: The Bug and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was a little bug. He was walking in the woods and it was very sad. He was all alone and he was looking for someone to be his friend. Suddenly, he saw a butterfly! He was so excited and he ran up to it. The butterfly smiled at him and he asked if they could be friends. The butterfly thought for a second and then she said that she would love to be friends with him. The little bug was so happy and he asked what they could do together. The butterfly said that they should go to the pond. The little bug was so excited and he agreed. But then the little bug noticed something. A big, mean bee was coming after them! The little bug was scared but the butterfly decided that they should keep running. So they raced to the pond until they were safe. The little bug and the butterfly were very happy that they were able to get away from the bee. From then on, they were the best of friends!

Once upon a time, there was a curious little dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play. One day, he saw a big shelter in the woods. Spot wanted to see what was inside. Spot went close to the shelter and heard a small voice. "Hello, who is there?" asked the voice. Spot said, "I am Spot, a curious dog. Can I come in?" The voice said, "Yes, come in, but be careful." Spot went inside the shelter and met a little bird named Tweet. They became friends and played together. Tweet told Spot that the shelter was not safe. Spot remembered the warning and felt scared. They decided to leave the shelter. As they left, the shelter fell down with a loud crash. Spot and Tweet were safe outside. They were happy they listened to the warning. Spot and Tweet went back to play in the woods, and they stayed close to each other.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big hill. The hill was a volcano. Tim was very excited. He wanted to see the volcano up close. Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue lived near the volcano. Tim asked Sue, "Can we go see the volcano?" Sue nodded and said, "Yes, we can go together!" They walked to the volcano. Tim tried to climb it, but it was too hard. He felt frustrated. Sue saw Tim and said, "Don't worry, we can try again another day." Tim smiled and they went home.

One day, a smart girl named Sue went to the subway with her mom. They had to go to the other side of town. Sue was very excited because she had never been on a subway before. She looked at the big train and said, "Mom, I want to ride the subway all day!" While they were on the subway, Sue saw a big dog. The dog's owner was a tall man. The dog looked at Sue and said, "I want a treat!" Sue was surprised that the dog could talk. She told her mom, but her mom did not believe her. So, Sue demanded, "Mom, please give the dog a treat!" Sue's mom gave the dog a treat, and the dog said, "Thank you!" Then, the dog turned into a beautiful fairy. The fairy told Sue that she was very smart and kind. She said that because Sue was so nice to the dog, she would grant her one wish. Sue wished to ride the subway all day with her mom, and the fairy made it come true.

Once upon a time, there was a big party. Everyone was happy. Dad had a camera. The camera was impressive. It could take many pictures. Dad wanted to provide memories for everyone. At the party, a funny clown made everyone laugh. The kids played games. They ate cake too. Dad took pictures with his camera. But then, something unexpected happened. The clown took off his big red nose. It was not a clown! It was Grandpa! Everyone was surprised. Grandpa had a big smile. He wanted to make the party fun. Dad took a picture of Grandpa with the camera. The party was very special.
