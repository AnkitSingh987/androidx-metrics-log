Title: Lucy and Jack and the Park

Once upon a time there were two friends called Lucy and Jack. They liked to play together in the park everyday. One day, Lucy asked Jack, "Can I borrow your eye?" Jack said, "Yes, but you must take care of it." Lucy took the eye in her hand and smiled. The eye was very soft. She then said to Jack, "I'll permit you to have it back to when I'm done." Jack nodded. Then, Lucy and Jack had lots of fun playing in the park with the eye. They pretended to be pirates and sailed away on a big adventure. When they finished playing, Lucy gave the eye back to Jack. 'Thank you for lending me your eye," she said. Jack smiled and said, "You're welcome!"

One day, a boy named Tim had a dream. In his dream, he saw a big avocado. The avocado could talk! It said, "Hello, Tim! I am a magic avocado." Tim and the avocado played and had fun. But soon, the avocado became angry. "Why are you angry?" asked Tim. The avocado said, "You must share me with others or I will be sad and angry." The next day, Tim found a real avocado. He thought it was the magic one from his dream. So, he shared it with his friends. But, it was not magic. It was just a normal avocado. His friends got angry too, because they did not like avocados. In the end, Tim learned that not everything in dreams is real. And he also learned that sharing is good, but we should share things that others like too.

Once upon a time, there was a nice house. In the house, there was a door. The door was always closed. One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to open the door. She was very brave. When Lucy opened the door, she saw a ghost. The ghost was not scary. The ghost was nice and friendly. The ghost said "Hi" to Lucy. Lucy said "Hi" back to the ghost. They became friends. Lucy and the nice ghost played together. They had so much fun. They played with toys and laughed. Lucy was happy she opened the door. Now, she had a new friend, the nice ghost. They lived happily ever after.

One day, a wet crocodile was walking near a big river. He saw a small boy playing with a ball. The ball was very bouncy and fun. The crocodile wanted to play too, but he was too shy to ask the boy. The crocodile said to himself, "I will just watch the boy play." But then, the ball slipped out of the boy's hands and rolled towards the crocodile. The boy ran to get it and saw the crocodile. He was scared and said, "Please don't hurt me, Mr. Crocodile!" The crocodile smiled and said, "I won't hurt you. I just want to play with your ball too." The boy was surprised and said, "Okay, let's play together!" They played with the ball all day, and the boy learned that the crocodile was not mean, just shy. The crocodile was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Fred. He was an adventurous rabbit and always loved exploring. One day, while he was poking around the woods, Fred found a mysterious hole. "What is this?" he said to himself. He poked his head inside the hole and saw that it was lined with white snow! "Wow! It's winter in here," he thought. He decided to explore further and kept poking around. Suddenly, he was swept away in a swirling snow storm! He tried to shake it off, but he couldn't. He was stuck. Eventually, after hours and hours of struggling, Fred realized he would never make it out. Even when winter ended and the snow disappeared, he was still trapped in the snow cave. He was never seen again. Moral of the story: even poking around in places that look safe can be dangerous!

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to travel and see new things. They have a club for adventurous kids. They call it the Travel Club. One day, they find a big map in the attic. The map has many colors and pictures. They see a picture of a mountain with snow. They want to go there. They pack their bags with warm clothes, snacks and a camera. They tell their parents they are going to the park. But they are not. They are going to the bus station. They buy two tickets to the mountain. They get on the bus and sit by the window. They see many things on the way. They see farms, rivers, bridges and towns. They are happy and excited. They get off the bus at the mountain. They see the snow and the trees. They feel the cold air. They put on their hats and gloves. They start to walk up the hill. They want to see the top. They have a lot of fun. They make snowballs and snowmen. They take pictures and laugh. They are the best Travel Club ever.
