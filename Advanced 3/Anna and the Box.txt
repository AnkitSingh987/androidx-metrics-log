Title: Anna and the Box

Anna liked to play with her dolls in her room. She had many dolls with different dresses and hair. She gave them names and made them talk to each other. She was happy when she played with her dolls. But sometimes, Anna heard a scary noise from the closet. It was a loud bang, like something falling or hitting the door. Anna was afraid of the noise. She thought there was a monster in the closet. She ran to her mom and dad and told them about the noise. Mom and dad came to Anna's room and opened the closet. They looked inside and saw what made the noise. It was a big box of books that fell from the shelf. There was no monster in the closet, only books. Mom and dad smiled and hugged Anna. They told her not to mind the noise. It was just a box of books, not a monster. They said they would fix the shelf and put the books back. They told Anna to be calm and not to be afraid. Anna felt better after mom and dad explained the noise. She thanked them and went back to play with her dolls. She was not scared of the closet anymore. She was happy again.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw a small bug. It was a mosquito. Tim did not recognize it. He wanted to be friends with the mosquito. Tim said, "Hi, bug! Want to play with me?" The mosquito felt embarrassed. It did not want to play with Tim. It wanted to fly away. But it did not want to hurt Tim's feelings. So, the mosquito said, "Okay, Tim. We can play together." As they played, something unexpected happened. The mosquito started to change. It grew bigger and bigger. Suddenly, the mosquito turned into a beautiful butterfly. Tim was so surprised! The butterfly said, "Thank you, Tim, for being my friend. I was once a mosquito, but your kindness changed me into a butterfly." Tim and the butterfly played together all day long. They had so much fun. And from that day on, Tim knew that being kind could make a big difference in the world.

Once upon a time, there was a big ocean. In the ocean, there was a fish named Fin. Fin was a patient fish who liked to play with his friends. One day, he met a new friend named Spike. Spike was a small fish with a long nose that he used to poke things. Fin and Spike were playing near the shore. Fin said, "Spike, let's race to the big rock!" Spike agreed and they started to swim fast. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wave came and pushed them onto the sand. Fin and Spike were scared. They looked at each other and said, "We need to get back to the ocean!" They worked together to wiggle and flop back into the water. They were so happy to be back in the ocean, and they promised to be more careful when playing near the shore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a rod that was very fine. He loved to play with his rod every day. One day, he saw a girl named Sue who was sad. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tim wanted to help Sue find her toy. Tim and Sue looked everywhere for the lost toy. They looked under the big tree, behind the small house, and in the tall grass. They could not find Sue's toy. Tim felt sad too, but he knew he had to keep looking to make Sue happy again. As they walked near the river, Tim saw something shiny in the water. He used his fine rod to reach it. He pulled it out and saw that it was Sue's toy! Sue was so happy to have her toy back. She said, "Thank you, Tim! Your fine rod belongs to a great friend like you." From that day on, Tim and Sue were the best of friends and played together with their toys every day.

Sara and Ben are playing with blocks in the living room. They are building a big tower with different colors. Sara likes to make patterns with the blocks, but Ben likes to mix them up. "Look, Sara, I made a rainbow!" Ben says, showing his tower to Sara. "No, Ben, that is not a rainbow. A rainbow has red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple. You have pink, brown, black, and white. That is not a rainbow." Sara says, ignoring Ben's tower. "But I like these colors. They are pretty." Ben says, feeling sad. Sara does not listen to Ben. She keeps making her own tower with the right colors. She wants to make it very tall and clean. She does not want any other colors to mess it up. Ben tries to talk to Sara again, but she does not answer. He feels lonely and bored. He decides to do something else. He looks around the living room and sees a big ball. He likes balls. He picks up the ball and throws it in the air. He catches it and throws it again. He has fun. But then, the ball hits Sara's tower. The tower falls down and the blocks scatter everywhere. Sara is very angry. She screams at Ben. "Ben, you are so bad! You broke my tower! You ruined my blocks! Go away!" Sara says, crying. Ben is very sorry. He did not mean to break Sara's tower. He tries to say sorry, but Sara does not listen. She pushes him away and picks up the blocks. She tries to fix her tower, but it is not the same. Ben feels very sad. He wishes Sara would forgive him. He wishes they could play together again. He does not like to ignore Sara, or to be ignored by Sara. He loves Sara. She is his sister.
