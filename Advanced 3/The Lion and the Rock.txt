Title: The Lion and the Rock

Once, there was a big lion. He was very dizzy. He did not know why. The lion lived in a big forest with many trees. One day, the lion saw a shiny rock. The rock was very pretty. He wanted to make it even more pretty. So, he decided to polish the shiny rock. But when he started to polish the rock, something strange happened. The shiny rock began to talk! The lion was very surprised. The rock said, "Thank you for making me shiny! I am a magic rock. Now, I will make you not dizzy anymore." And just like that, the lion was not dizzy anymore. The lion and the magic rock became very good friends.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make food for their dolls and teddy bears. They had plastic plates, cups, spoons and forks. They also had some pretend food, like bread, cheese, eggs and fruit. "Today we are going to serve a very expensive meal," Lily said. "We need to make it look nice and tasty." "OK, what do we need?" Ben asked. "We need some bread and cheese for the starter, some eggs and ham for the main course, and some fruit for the dessert," Lily said. "And we need to cut them with our fingers." "Why do we need to cut them with our fingers?" Ben asked. "Because we don't have a knife, silly," Lily said. "And it's more fun." They started to cut the pretend food with their fingers and put them on the plates. They arranged them in different shapes and colors. They were very careful not to hurt their fingers. "Look, I made a smiley face with the cheese," Ben said. "And I made a flower with the fruit," Lily said. They were very proud of their work. They carried the plates to the table where their dolls and teddy bears were waiting. They put the plates in front of them and said: "Bon appetit! This is a very expensive meal. We hope you like it." They pretended to feed their toys and make them say "yum" and "thank you". They had a lot of fun playing with their toy kitchen.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog. He was playing in the park when he noticed a bright bulb on the ground! He was so excited and quickly started to dig it out with his paws. He was almost finished when he heard a voice calling, "Hey, that's my bulb!" He looked up and saw a grumpy old man standing there. The dog was so scared that he ran away. The old man chased after him, shouting loudly. Suddenly, the bulb began to glow and disappeared in a flash! The man stopped in his tracks and gasped. He turned to the dog and said, "That's what you get for trying to take my bulb!" The old man was so angry he grabbed the dog and threw him out of the park. The poor dog ran away, not understanding what had just happened. He never saw the bulb again.

One day, a little girl named Anna was playing in her room. She heard a sound from the kitchen. It was her mom cooking something spicy. Anna wanted to see what her mom was making. She walked to the kitchen and saw her mom cooking a big pot of soup. Anna asked, "Mom, what are you making?" Her mom replied, "I am making spicy soup, but be careful, it is very hot." Anna wanted to taste the soup, but she knew that if she did it without asking, her mom would punish her. But Anna couldn't help herself, so she tried to taste the soup when her mom wasn't looking. Suddenly, the pot started to shake and make a loud sound. Anna was scared and cried, "Mom, help!" Her mom turned around and saw the pot shaking. She quickly took the lid off and out came a friendly talking bird! The bird said, "Thank you for letting me out! I was stuck in that pot!" Anna and her mom were very surprised, but they were happy to have a new friend.
