Title: Lucy the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a small, delicate cactus. She loved her cactus very much. She would water it and take care of it every day. One day, Lucy's mom said she would teach her how to plant a new cactus. Lucy was very excited. She got her little shovel and her mom gave her a new cactus seed. As they started to dig a hole for the new cactus seed, something unexpected happened. A tiny, talking bird flew out of the hole! The bird said, "Hi, Lucy! I can teach you how to make your cactus grow big and strong." Lucy and her mom were very surprised. They listened to the little bird, and he taught them how to take care of their cactus. Soon, the cactus grew big and strong, just like the little bird said. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a bright red motorcycle. It loved to go fast and make loud noises. The motorcycle had many friends, but its best friend was a little car. One day, the motorcycle and the car had a quarrel. They were arguing about who was faster. The motorcycle said, "I am faster!" The car said, "No, I am faster!" They were both very angry. They decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. The race started, and they went very fast. But then, they saw a little squirrel in the road. They both stopped quickly so they would not hit the squirrel. The motorcycle and the car looked at each other and realized that being fast was not the most important thing. They decided to be friends again and not quarrel anymore. The moral of the story is that it is better to be kind and caring than to be the fastest or the best.

One day, a little caterpillar went for a walk. It was an icy cold day, but the caterpillar was very brave. He wanted to find a warm place to sleep. As he walked, he saw a big tree and thought it would be a good place to rest. The caterpillar climbed the tree and met a friendly bird. The bird said, "Hello, little caterpillar! What are you doing in this icy tree?" The caterpillar said, "I am cold and looking for a warm place to sleep." The bird wanted to help, so he pointed to a cozy nest in the tree. "You can sleep in my nest," said the bird. But then, a mean squirrel came and said, "No, that is my nest!" The bird and the caterpillar were sad. They did not want to fight with the squirrel. So, they went to find another warm place. The bird pointed to a small hole in the tree. The caterpillar went inside and found it was very warm. The bird and the caterpillar were happy, and they became good friends.

Ben and Lily are twins. They like to play in the park with their mom and dad. They swing on the swings, slide on the slides, and run on the grass. They have a lot of fun. But after a while, they start to feel tired. Their legs are sore, their eyes are heavy, and their mouths are dry. They tell their mom and dad they want to go home. "Okay, sweeties, let's go home," mom says. "But be careful on the way. Hold our hands and watch where you go." Ben and Lily nod. They hold their mom and dad's hands and walk to the car. They see a big dog, a red truck, and a yellow flower on the way. They point and say their names. When they get to the car, they climb into their seats and buckle their belts. Mom and dad do the same. They drive home, listening to a song on the radio. Ben and Lily are very sleepy. They close their eyes and dream. They dream of the park, the swings, the slides, and the grass. They dream of the dog, the truck, and the flower. They dream of their mom and dad, who love them very much. They are happy. They are safe. They are tired. They are dreaming.
