Title: Clara and the Rainbow

Once there was a little girl called Clara. She was three years old and liked to play in the garden. One day, she was picking fruit from the tree and found an orange. When she squeezed it gently, juice came out and she smiled. Suddenly, a rainbow appeared in the sky and she was amazed. She pointed to the sky and said to her mum, "Look! What is that?". Her mum said, "That is a rainbow. It's very lucky to see one." Clara smiled again and said, "Let's have some of the fruit for lunch." They went back inside and ate the warm oranges with honey. Clara was so happy and ate two of them. She kept smiling and said, "Yummy!" At the end of the day, Clara looked up at the rainbow and said, "Thank you for a very lucky day." Then she waved goodbye to the rainbow and went back inside to dream of more happy days.

One day, a baby was studying a big book. The baby wanted to learn many things. A kind, compassionate dog saw the baby and wanted to help. The dog said, "Baby, let me help you study. I know many things." The baby smiled and said, "Yes, please help me, dog." They studied together and became good friends. One day, while they were studying, a big wind came and blew the book away. They were sad, but they learned that they didn't need the book to be happy. They had each other, and that was the best thing to learn.

Once upon a time, there was a band of three friends. They liked to play music together. They had a big drum, a small guitar, and a shiny flute. They loved to make people happy with their songs. One day, they wanted to surprise their friend Bee. Bee was feeling uncomfortable because she lost her favorite toy. The band thought their music could make Bee feel better. So, they hid behind a big tree near Bee's house. When Bee came out of her house, the band started to play their music. They played a happy song with the drum, the guitar, and the flute. Bee heard the music and looked around to find where it was coming from. The band jumped out from behind the tree and yelled, "Surprise!" Bee was so happy to see her friends playing music just for her. The music made her feel better, and she forgot about her lost toy. They all danced and played together, and Bee felt happy again.

Lily and Sam like to create things with paper and glue. They cut and fold and stick paper to make animals and cars and houses. One day, they find a shiny penny on the floor. They wonder what they can create with it. "Look, Sam, a penny!" Lily says. "It is round and brown and has a face on it." "Wow, Lily, a penny!" Sam says. "Maybe we can use it to make something new." They think and think. Then they have an idea. They get some white paper and a scissors. They cut out a big circle and a small circle. They glue the small circle on the big circle. Then they glue the penny on the small circle. They make two eyes and a mouth with a marker. "Look, Sam, we made a snowman!" Lily says. "The penny is his nose. He is white and cute and has a smile." "Look, Lily, we made a snowman!" Sam says. "The paper is his body. He is white and happy and has a face." They are very proud of their snowman. They put him on the table and show him to their mom. Mom smiles and hugs them. "You are very creative, Lily and Sam!" Mom says. "You made a snowman with a penny. He is white and beautiful and has a sparkle."

Once upon a time, there was a big onion. The onion had a friend, a small boy named Tim. Tim and the onion liked to play in the yard. They felt safe and happy when they were together. One day, Tim asked the onion a question. He said, "Onion, do you have a family?" The onion did not know, so they decided to look for the onion's family together. As they looked around, they found a big surprise! The onion's family was not a group of onions, but a bunch of flowers! The onion was not an onion after all, but a flower bulb. The flower bulb was very happy to find its real family, and Tim was happy too. They played together in the yard, feeling safe and loved.
