Title: The Mouse and the Elephant

Once upon a time, there was a mouse who was very helpless. He wanted to marry, but he didn't know who he should marry. He was very lonely and didn't know what to do. One day he was looking for food when he found a very big mushroom. He was so excited that he decided to marry the mushroom! He asked her, "Will you marry me?" The mushroom laughed and said, "No, silly mouse, I can't marry you! Mushrooms can't marry!" The poor mouse felt very helpless. He was so sad that he had to cry. Suddenly, he heard a voice from nearby. He looked up and saw an elephant. The elephant smiled gently and said, "Don't worry, little mouse. I will marry you and be your partner." The mouse was so happy and he jumped up and down. From that day on, the mouse and elephant were married and were very happy together!

Once upon a time, there were two brothers named Jack and John. They used to play in the park every day. One day, the brothers decided to take a walk and explore the park. They came across an underground garden with lots of colourful flowers. Jack was very interested in the garden, but John was scared. He wanted to go back home. Jack said, "Oh look! It is so pretty down here. I want to remember it forever." John said, "It is hot down here and I am scared. I want to go home. Let's remember to never come down here again." Jack remembered these words, and he never came back to the underground garden with his brother. From this the brothers learnt a valuable lesson: even if something looks pretty, it's dangerous to explore something you don't know.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly little lizard. He lived in a big green tree. He loved to play with his friends, the bird and the squirrel. One day, the lizard saw a beautiful butterfly. The lizard wanted to change and be like the butterfly. He wished very hard and, suddenly, he changed into a butterfly too! Now, the jolly lizard could fly with his friends in the sky. They all played together and had so much fun. The jolly lizard was very happy with his change. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little cat. The cat had a bell. The cat liked the bell a lot. The cat's mom wanted to attach the bell to the cat's neck. The cat was happy about that. One day, the cat went for a walk. The cat's bell made a nice sound. The cat liked the sound. The cat walked to the park. The cat played with a ball. The cat had a lot of fun. The cat went back home. The cat's mom was happy. The cat's bell helped the mom find the cat. The cat and the mom were very happy together. They both loved the cute bell.

John woke up and saw his mom standing by the window. She trembled as she looked down at the small creek. John asked his mom why she was so scared. His mom told him that she was scared because the creek was too weak. She said that the creek wouldn't be able to keep them safe from the storm. John asked his mom why the storm was coming and she explained that a huge storm was on its way. The sky was getting darker and the wind was shaking trees. John was scared and started to tremble. He asked his mom if they were in danger. His mom told him that she would do her best to keep them safe. She hugged him tightly and promised to protect him. After that, she went outside to prepare for the storm. John stayed inside the house and feared the worst. Soon enough, the storm came and John heard thunder and hail. He trembled as the storm got worse and he couldn't help but feel helpless. Thankfully, his mom was right, the weak creek kept them safe and the storm eventually passed. John realized that his mom always knows best.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a toy bear named Bobo. Bobo was her best friend. They played together every day. Lucy always said, "Bobo is mine." One day, Lucy went to the park with her mom. She took Bobo with her. They played on the swings and slides. But when it was time to go home, Lucy could not find Bobo. She felt very sad. Lucy and her mom looked everywhere in the park. Then, they saw a boy holding Bobo. The boy looked guilty. Lucy said, "Bobo belongs to me." The boy gave Bobo back to Lucy. She was so happy. Lucy, Bobo, and her mom went home and played together.
