Title: Lila and the Tree

Lila loved to draw fairies. She used her crayons to make them in many colors. She gave them wings, dresses, and crowns. She drew them on paper, on the wall, and on her bed. She wanted to show everyone her fairies. One day, she had a big idea. She asked her mom for some tape and scissors. She cut out her fairies from the paper. She taped them to sticks. She made a fairy display. She was very proud of her work. She took her fairy display outside. She wanted to find a good place to put it. She saw a pale tree with no leaves. She thought it looked sad. She decided to make it happy. She stuck her fairies on the tree. She made it look like a fairy home. She ran to get her mom and dad. She wanted them to see her fairy display. She said, "Look, look, I made a fairy tree! Do you like it?" Her mom and dad smiled. They said, "Wow, Lila, you are very creative. Your fairy display is beautiful. We love it." Lila was very happy. She hugged them and said, "Thank you. I love you. And I love fairies."

Johnny and Amy were best friends. They were playing together outside in the park. Suddenly, Johnny saw something shiny near the bench. He ran over and realized it was a toy car. He wanted it more than anything! He looked around and no one was watching, so he decided to steal it quietly. He hid it in his pocket and smiled. Amy heard a strange sound and realized that Johnny had taken the car. She was very angry and shouted at Johnny for stealing. Johnny tried to explain, but Amy was too mad to listen. Suddenly, a policeman came and asked them for the toy car. Although Amy was mad at Johnny, she told the policeman a lie to protect him. She said that she had taken it. The policeman looked tired, so he decided to let them go. He said that stealing was bad and they should never do it again. Johnny and Amy agreed and they looked at each other with a big smile. From that day on, they promised to never steal again and to always tell the truth.

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to play with her toy mixer. She loved to pretend to make yummy treats for her friends. Before she started, Mia asked her mom, "Can I dress up to play?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can wear your chef hat and apron." Mia put on her hat and apron and felt like a real chef. She was so happy! Mia started to play with her mixer. She pretended to mix cake batter and cookies. Suddenly, the mixer started to spin very fast. It made Mia feel dizzy! She didn't know what to do. Just then, her big brother Tom came into the room. He saw the mixer spinning fast and making Mia dizzy. Tom quickly turned off the mixer and said, "Don't worry, Mia. I will fix it for you." Mia was so thankful for her brother's help. They fixed the mixer together and continued to play. From that day on, Mia and Tom had lots of fun making pretend treats for their friends and family.

Once upon a time, there was a big office. Inside the office, there were many people who liked to paint. They painted all day long. They painted on the walls, on the tables, and even on the floor. The office was very colorful. One day, a new person came to the office. His name was Bob. Bob saw all the paintings and thought they were fake. He didn't believe that people could paint so well. But the people in the office wanted to show Bob that they could paint. So, they gave Bob some paint and a brush. They showed him how to paint. Bob tried to paint, but it was hard. He painted a little bit every day. Soon, Bob could paint very well. He was happy and proud. Now, Bob knew that the paintings in the office were not fake.
