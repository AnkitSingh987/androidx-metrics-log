Title: The Rich Man and the Diamond

Once upon a time, there was a rich man who had a lot of money. His name was Fred. He loved to show off all his riches. He had fancy clothes and expensive jewellery. One day he saw a special cut of diamond in a shop. He wanted to buy it, but it was very expensive. He decided to take it anyways. When he came home, he showed all his family the shiny, sparkly diamond. Everyone was impressed. He was so proud, and started to call himself a different name. "I'm now the 'Diamond King'," he claimed. But then, something bad happened. He accidentally cut his finger on the diamond, and it hurt! He dropped it on the floor and burst into tears. Every member of his family went over to him, and hugged him. This made him feel a lot better, and he stopped crying. In the end, he was still rich and still had lots of money, but now he had also learnt a very important lesson - to be careful and not take any risks with his money.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty was very heavy and loved to take a nap all day. One day, while Kitty was napping under a tree, a tiny mouse came to play near her. The mouse did not see the heavy Kitty sleeping there. The mouse played near Kitty's tail and accidentally woke her up. Kitty was not happy and wanted to catch the mouse. But the mouse was very fast and tried to escape. Kitty tried to catch the mouse, but she was too heavy to run fast. The mouse ran and hid in a small hole in the ground. Kitty felt sad and tired, so she went back to take a nap under the tree. When she woke up, she saw the mouse again. But this time, the mouse brought her a big piece of cheese. Kitty and the mouse became friends and shared the cheese. The heavy Kitty learned that being nice is better than being mean, and she and the mouse played together every day.

Sara was a flexible girl. She could bend her legs and arms in many ways. She liked to do yoga with her mom and dad. They had a big mat and some books with pictures of poses. Sara could do the poses better than her mom and dad. Yesterday, Sara had a new friend come to her house. His name was Ben. He liked to play with cars and trucks. Sara wanted to show him her yoga books. She said, "Welcome to my house, Ben. Do you want to do yoga with me?" Ben looked at the books and the mat. He did not know what yoga was. He said, "No, thank you. I want to play with cars and trucks. Do you have any?" Sara was sad. She liked cars and trucks, but she liked yoga more. She wanted Ben to like yoga too. She said, "Please, Ben. Yoga is fun. You can bend and stretch and breathe. It is good for your body and mind. Look, I can do this pose. It is called the bridge." Sara lay on her back and lifted her hips and chest. She made a curve with her body. Ben looked at her. He was curious. He said, "Wow, Sara. You are very flexible. How do you do that?" Sara smiled. She said, "It is easy. You just have to practice. Come, I will show you. You can do it too." Sara helped Ben to do the bridge pose. He felt his back and legs stretch. He said, "This feels good. And it is fun. Can you show me more poses?" Sara was happy. She said, "Yes, of course. There are many poses. We can do the cat, the dog, the tree, the snake, and more. Come, let's do yoga together." Sara and Ben did yoga for a long time. They had fun and learned new things. They became good friends. Sara was glad that she welcomed Ben to her house. She learned that he was flexible too.

Once upon a time, in a lively forest, there was a little fox. The fox had soft, red skin. Every day, the fox would set out to play with his friends. One day, the fox saw a big, dark hole. He was scared but curious. The fox thought, "Maybe I can find something fun in the hole." So, he set off to see what was inside. The fox went into the hole. It was very dark and cold. The fox heard a loud noise. He was very scared. Then, a big bear came out of the shadows. The bear was angry that the fox was in his home. The little fox tried to run away, but it was too late. The big bear caught the fox, and the lively forest was quiet again.
