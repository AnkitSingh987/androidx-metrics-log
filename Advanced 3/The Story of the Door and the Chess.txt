Title: The Story of the Door and the Chess

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She had a brother and everyday they played chess together. The chess pieces were bright and yellow. One day, the brother said to his sister, “Let’s play chess!” The girl smiled and said, “Yes!” They both sat down and started playing. The brother suddenly said, “I want to be a king!” The girl said, “Okay,” and shut the door. She wanted the game to be special. After a while, they looked around the room and started laughing. Looking at the yellow chess pieces they laughed together. It was so much fun!

One day, Tom and Sam went to the park. Tom wore a red cap. Sam wore a blue cap. They wanted to jog. Tom said, "Let's race!" Sam said, "Yes!" They ran fast. Tom was competitive. Sam was competitive too. They both wanted to win. They jogged and jogged. The wind blew their caps off. They laughed. In the end, Tom won. Sam said, "Good job!" They found their caps on the ground. They went home happy. They had fun jogging at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big object in the park. It was so big that all the kids wanted to play with it. One day, a little boy named Timmy went to the park with his mom. Timmy was very excited to play with the big object. "Mom, can I play with the big object?" Timmy asked. "Yes, Timmy, but you must behave and be careful," said his mom. So, Timmy went to play with the big object, and he was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The big object started to move! All the kids were surprised. "Oh no!" said Timmy. "What is happening?" The big object was not just an object, it was a big friendly dog! The dog had been sleeping under a blanket, and now it was awake. The kids were scared at first, but then they saw that the dog was nice. They all played together and had a great day at the park.

One day, a big red bus was driving down the street. It was an amazing bus with many happy kids inside. They were all going to the park to play. On the way to the park, the bus hit a big bump. The bus went up and then down very fast. The kids inside were scared. Then, something unexpected happened. A big tear appeared on the side of the bus. The driver stopped the bus and looked at the tear. He did not know what to do. Just then, a friendly dog came running to the bus. The dog took the tear in its mouth and started to pull. The tear became smaller and smaller until it was all gone. The kids cheered and thanked the dog. They continued to the park and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Tim who was very thoughtful. One day he was playing in the garden when he noticed something strange on the ground - coal. He bent down to examine it and found that it looked soft and squishy. He wondered what it was so he decided to give it a try and he put it in his mouth. He started to chew and much to his surprise it was actually very tasty! He enjoyed the taste and kept on chewing until he ate it all up. He was very happy that he'd had an unexpected treat and he was even more thoughtful than before.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there lived a big gorilla named Gogo. Gogo was very filthy because he loved to play in the mud all day. He had so much fun jumping and rolling around in the mud. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the zoo with her mom. She saw Gogo and said, "Mommy, look at that dirty gorilla! Can we help clean him?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, let's ask the zookeeper if we can help." The zookeeper gave Lily and her mom a big brush to replace the old one. They started to scrub Gogo gently. Gogo liked the tickling feeling and laughed. After they finished, Gogo was clean and shiny. "Thank you for helping me," said Gogo to Lily and her mom. "Now I feel so much better!" Lily and her mom were happy that they could help Gogo. They waved goodbye to their new friend and went home, knowing that they made a difference in Gogo's life.
