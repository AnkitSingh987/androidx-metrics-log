Title: Mia the Girl and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to dance and listen to jazz music. She was very excited when she danced. One day, Mia went to the park to dance and listen to jazz. She saw a big rock and decided to balance on it. Mia was very good at balancing and she danced on the rock. But then, Mia lost her balance and fell down. She was sad and hurt. The jazz music stopped, and Mia went home with a bad ending to her day.

Once upon a time, there was a busy little squirrel named Sammy. Sammy loved to find peanuts and hide them in his home. One day, Sammy found a big peanut under a tree. He was very happy and wanted to take it home. On his way home, Sammy saw a big bird. The big bird wanted the peanut too. Sammy did not want to give the bird his peanut. He paused and thought about what to do. He decided to run very fast with the peanut to his home. Sammy ran and ran, and the big bird chased him. Sammy was scared, but he did not stop. He got to his home and put the peanut inside. The big bird could not get the peanut now. Sammy was safe, and he was very proud of himself.

Once upon a time there was a little girl with fair skin and bright eyes. She was very curious and one day she decided to explore the beach. As she was walking along the sand, she spotted a big oyster. Suddenly the oyster started to move and a voice came out of it. It said: "I am a magical oyster from the sea. I can grant you any wish, just say it out loud". The little girl was so excited. She said: "I wish to be the most beautiful girl in the world!" The oyster replied: "Your wish is granted!" and it started to turn. But then the little girl felt an evil force taking over her. Her beauty started to fade away. She started to cry. When she looked in the mirror, she could see that her beautiful fair skin had turned dark and ugly. The little girl was so sad and she realized that she had been tricked by the oyster. She realized that some wishes are too dangerous to be granted.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks and make things. One day, they find a big box of blocks with different shapes and colors. They are very happy and excited. "Look, Lily, I can make a house with these blocks!" Ben says. He attaches some blocks together and makes a wall. "Wow, Ben, your house is beautiful!" Lily says. She attaches some blocks too and makes a roof. "Can I help you with your house?" "Yes, Lily, you can. We can make the house bigger and better!" Ben says. They attach more blocks and make a door, a window, and a chimney. They are very proud of their house. They go inside and pretend to live there. They have fun and laugh. "Ben, you are a good friend. I like to play with you," Lily says. "Lily, you are a good friend too. I like to play with you too," Ben says. They hug and smile. They are happy. They have a beautiful house and a beautiful friendship.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She was bored because all her toys were boring. She wanted to find something new and fun to play with. So, she went to her daddy's room to look for something. In her daddy's room, she saw a big box. Lily opened the box and examined all the things inside. She found a funny hat, a big ball, and a colorful book. She was very excited and ran to show her daddy what she found. Lily said, "Daddy, look at these fun things I found in your room!" Her daddy smiled and said, "I remember those! Let's play together." They played with the funny hat, the big ball, and read the colorful book. Lily was not bored anymore because she had fun playing with her daddy.
