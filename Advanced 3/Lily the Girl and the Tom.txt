Title: Lily the Girl and the Tom

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a diary where she wrote all her secrets. Lily loved to nap after playing with her friends. One day, while Lily was taking a nap, her brother Tom found her diary. He felt guilty, but he still read her secrets. When Lily woke up, she saw Tom with her diary and was very sad. Lily told her mom about what Tom did. Her mom was not happy with Tom. Tom felt very bad and said sorry to Lily, but Lily was still sad. The bad ending made Tom learn that he should not read others' secrets.

One day, a little boy named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a piece of candy high up in the branches. Tom wanted the candy, so he tried to reach it. He jumped and jumped, but he could not reach the candy. Tom saw a tall girl named Sue. He asked her, "Can you help me reach the candy?" Sue said, "Yes, I will help you." Sue reached up high, but she could not reach the candy either. They both felt sad. It started to get cold and icy outside. Tom and Sue were still trying to reach the candy. They did not see the icy ground and they both slipped. Tom and Sue fell down and got hurt. They did not get the candy and had to go home with their bumps and bruises.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She liked to put her toys in a zigzag line. One day, Lily found an interesting rock. The rock was shiny and smooth. She put the rock with her toys in the zigzag line. The next day, Lily saw that the interesting rock was gone. She looked everywhere, but she could not find it. She was very sad. Then, she saw a big, beautiful butterfly in her room. The butterfly was the same color as the interesting rock. Lily watched the butterfly fly to her toys. It touched the zigzag line with its wings. Then, something unexpected happened. All her toys started to move and dance! Lily was so surprised and happy. The butterfly flew to Lily and landed on her hand. It was the interesting rock that turned into a butterfly! Now, Lily and the butterfly played together every day. They had lots of fun making her toys dance in a zigzag line. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to play with her mom's makeup. She saw all the pretty colors and thought it would be fun. She decided to put some on her face. She put on red lipstick, blue eyeshadow, and pink blush. She looked in the mirror and smiled. Lucy's mom saw her and was upset. She told Lucy that makeup is not for little kids. Lucy felt sad and started to cry. Her mom hugged her and said it was okay, but she needed to wash her face. Together, they went to the bathroom and washed off the makeup. Lucy's mom told her that when she is older, she can wear makeup too. Lucy felt better and decided to play with her toys instead. She knew that one day, she could wear makeup just like her mom.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a nice lady named Sue. Sue loved to welcome people to her home. One day, a man came to her door. He said, "Hello, I am very hungry. Can you please help me?" Sue felt bad for the helpless man, so she welcomed him in. She gave him food to eat. But she did not know that the man was a thief. The thief saw a big, shiny box. He wanted to take it. He said, "I need to go to the bathroom." Sue showed him the way. When Sue was not looking, the thief took the shiny box and ran away. Sue came back and saw the empty spot. She knew something was wrong. Later, Sue's friend came to visit. Sue told her friend about the thief. Her friend said, "I saw a man with a shiny box! Let's go find him!" They found the thief and got the shiny box back. Sue learned to be more careful when welcoming people into her home.
