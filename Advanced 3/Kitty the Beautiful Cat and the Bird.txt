Title: Kitty the Beautiful Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to sleep all day long. She would find a warm, cozy spot and take long naps. One day, Kitty heard a voice by the exit. It was a little bird named Birdy. Birdy said, "Kitty, come play with me outside!" Kitty opened her eyes and looked at the exit. She thought it would be fun to play with Birdy. Kitty and Birdy played all day long. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. When the sun began to set, Kitty said, "Birdy, I am tired. I want to sleep now." Birdy replied, "Okay, Kitty. Let's go back inside and sleep." Kitty and Birdy went back inside and found a cozy spot to sleep. They closed their eyes and slept happily together. From that day on, Kitty and Birdy became the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sarah. Sarah was a very adventurous girl and she was always looking for something fun to do. One day, Sarah was walking in the park with her mom. As she was walking, she noticed a giant puddle on the ground. "Mommy, look! A puddle!" shouted Sarah. "That's cool, Sarah. But be careful. You don't want to step in it and get your feet wet," said her mom. Sarah smiled and skipped away from her mom. She couldn't wait to go and explore the big puddle! She ran around the puddle and she looked in it. Then she did something crazy. She picked up a stick and started to cut the puddle into a smaller size. Her mom watched her curiously, wondering what she was up to. Finally, Sarah finished cutting the puddle and ran away, giggling and laughing. She came back to her mom, who chuckled when she saw Sarah's finished work - a beautiful smiley face in the puddle!

Once upon a time, there was a very rude cat named Tom. He liked to play tricks on everyone in the town. One day, he saw a little bird gazing at the sky. Tom thought it would be fun to scare the bird. Tom snuck up behind the bird and yelled, "Earthquake!" The little bird was so scared that it flew away. Tom laughed and laughed. He thought he was so funny. But then, something unexpected happened. The ground started to shake. There really was an earthquake! Tom was so scared that he ran and hid under a big tree. The little bird saw Tom and felt bad for him. It flew down and sat next to him. They both waited for the earthquake to stop. After that day, Tom became a nicer cat and never played rude tricks again.

Once upon a time, there was a playful bee named Buzz. Buzz lived in a big tree with his bee friends. They all loved to fly and play in the sun. One day, it started to rain. The bees had to stay inside their tree home. Buzz was sad because he wanted to play outside. He looked out of the tree and saw a little girl named Lily. "Hi, Bee," Lily said. "Why are you sad?" "I want to play outside, but it is raining," Buzz replied. Lily thought for a moment and then said, "Let's play inside your tree home!" Buzz was happy and invited Lily inside. They played and laughed together until the rain stopped. When the sun came out, Buzz and Lily went outside and played in the flowers. They became the best of friends and played together every day.

Anna was a wealthy girl who had many toys and clothes. She liked to play with her dolls and dress them up. But she did not like to share with her friends. She was selfish and mean. One day, her friend Lily came over to play. Lily saw a beautiful doll with long hair and a pink dress. She asked Anna if she could play with it. "No, you can't!" Anna shouted. "This is my doll and only I can play with it. Go away!" Lily felt sad and hurt. She did not understand why Anna was so rude. She wanted to be friends with Anna, but Anna did not care about her feelings. Anna's mother heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw Anna holding the doll and Lily crying. "Anna, what are you doing?" she asked. "Why are you being so unkind to Lily?" "Because this is my doll and I don't want to share!" Anna said. "Anna, that is not how you treat your friends. Sharing is good and makes others happy. Being selfish is bad and makes others sad. You have a heart, don't you? How would you feel if Lily did the same to you?" Anna did not answer. She looked down and felt ashamed. "Anna, you need to learn a lesson. You are going to punish yourself for being selfish. You are going to give your doll to Lily and say sorry. And you are not going to play with any of your toys for the rest of the day. Do you understand?" Anna nodded. She did not like the idea of giving her doll to Lily and not playing with her toys. But she knew she had done wrong and had to make up for it. She walked over to Lily and handed her the doll. She looked at her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was mean to you. You can play with my doll. Do you want to be friends?" Lily smiled and hugged Anna. She said, "Thank you, Anna. I forgive you. You are very nice. Yes, I want to be friends." Anna felt a warm feeling in her heart. She realized that sharing was better than being selfish. She learned a valuable lesson that day. She and Lily became good friends and played happily together.
