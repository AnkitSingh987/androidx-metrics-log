Title: The White Bunny and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a white bunny. The bunny liked to have picnics. One sunny day, the bunny decided to have a picnic with his friends. The bunny and his friends went to the park. They found a nice spot under a big tree. The bunny used a soft blanket for everyone to sit on. They all sat down and started to eat yummy food. After the picnic, the white bunny and his friends played fun games. They laughed and had a great time. When the sun went down, they all said goodbye and went home. The white bunny couldn't wait for the next picnic.

Once upon a time, there was a man named Tom. Tom was a regular man who liked to design things. He had a big drawer full of colors and tools to help him create new things. One day, Tom met a little girl named Sue. Sue saw Tom's drawer and said, "Wow! You have so many colors. Can you show me how to design?" Tom smiled and said, "Of course, Sue! Let's design something fun together." They sat down near the drawer and started to create a beautiful picture of a big, bright sun and a happy, green tree. As they finished their picture, Sue looked at Tom and said, "Thank you, Tom! Designing with you was so much fun!" Tom felt happy and said, "You're welcome, Sue! I loved designing with you too." From that day on, Tom and Sue became best friends. They spent many days together, designing new things and filling the drawer with more colors and tools. And they lived happily ever after.

Sara loves dolphins. She has a big book with many pictures of dolphins. She likes to look at the book and learn their names and colors. She wishes she could see a real dolphin and play with it. One day, Sara goes to the beach with her mom and dad. They bring a bucket and a shovel and some sand toys. Sara makes a big sandcastle with a moat and a tower. She is very proud of her sandcastle. She looks at the water and wonders if there are any dolphins there. She sees something shiny and gray jump out of the water. It is a dolphin! Sara is very happy and excited. She runs to the water and waves at the dolphin. The dolphin sees her and makes a loud noise. It sounds like "eeek eeek eeek". Sara thinks the dolphin is saying hello. "Hello, dolphin!" Sara says. "Do you want to play with me? I have a sandcastle and some toys. You can come and see them." The dolphin nods and swims closer to the shore. Sara runs back to her sandcastle and brings a toy boat. She puts it in the water and pushes it towards the dolphin. The dolphin nudges the boat with its nose and makes it spin. Sara laughs and claps her hands. The dolphin makes the "eeek eeek eeek" noise again. Sara thinks the dolphin is happy. Sara and the dolphin play with the boat for a while. Then Sara brings a toy fish and throws it in the water. The dolphin catches the fish and tosses it back to Sara. They play catch with the fish. Sara and the dolphin have a lot of fun. Sara's mom and dad watch them and smile. They are glad that Sara has made a new friend. They think the dolphin is very smart and friendly. They wonder why the dolphin is alone and not with other dolphins. "Are you lonely, dolphin?" Sara asks. "Where are your family and friends?" The dolphin makes a sad noise. It sounds like "aww aww aww". Sara thinks the dolphin is sad. She hugs the dolphin and says, "Don't be sad, dolphin. I am your friend. You can come and play with me anytime. I will always be here for you." The dolphin hugs Sara back and makes a happy noise. It sounds like "yee yee yee". Sara thinks the dolphin is grateful. She kisses the dolphin and says, "I love you, dolphin. You are the best." The dolphin kisses Sara and says, "I love you, Sara. You are the best."
