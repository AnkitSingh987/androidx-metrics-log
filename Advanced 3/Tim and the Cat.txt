Title: Tim and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he did not have any friends. One day, he decided to go to the park to find a friend. At the park, Tim saw a warm and soft cat. The cat looked at Tim and said, "Hi, I am Cat. Do you want to be my friend?" Tim was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend!" They played together all day. As they played, they found a magic stick. The stick had power to make things big or small. Tim and Cat had a lot of fun making things big and small. They became best friends and had many more fun days at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Jake. He was three years old and loved to explore. One day, he asked his mum if he could go on an adventure and explore different places. Mum said, “Yes, as long as you stay calm and stay in the places I allow you to.” “Okay,” said Jake. He packed a bag with some snacks and a map and set off. He explored a meadow, a stream and a pond, and then came to a small cave. It looked dark, but he was brave. “Mum, can I go inside?” he asked. Mum thought for a moment, and then said, “Yes, but only if you remain calm.” So, Jake went inside the cave. It was cool and damp, with a few rocks and stones. He explored each corner and soon found a very special place. It was his own secret spot — a little area which was all his own. “Mum, look!” he said proudly. “This is mine!” Mum smiled and allowed Jake to keep exploring his special spot. Jake felt happy and calm in his little area, and returned there many more times. He had found his own special place — one that only he was allowed to explore.

Once upon a time, there was a big board. The board was in a park where kids played. One day, a boy named Tom came to the park. He saw the board and wanted to play with it. Tom was not careful and he split the board in half. He felt guilty because he broke the board. Tom wanted to fix the board so the kids could play again. He went to his dad and asked for help. His dad was kind and said, "Don't worry, we will fix the board together." They took some glue and put the two halves of the board back together. The next day, Tom went back to the park. The board was now fixed and the kids could play again. Tom was happy that he fixed the board and he learned to be more careful. The kids in the park thanked Tom and his dad for fixing the board. And they all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time a little fish called Tom lived in the sea. Tom lived in a hidden reef with many other fish. One day, Tom wanted to explore the underwater world around him. He asked his friend, "What's out there?" His friend answered, "There are lots of places you can swim, but be careful. You must always protect yourself." Tom nodded, and set off on his journey. He swam through some coral and could see fish, crabs and even coral sharks swimming around. He went to the edge of the hidden reef, and there he saw a beautiful island. He wanted to explore, but then he remembered his friend's advice. He knew he must protect himself, so he decided to stay at the hidden reef. Tom was a little sad, but he enjoyed watching all the creatures swim around him. One day, he met a friendly shark who helped him explore the world around him. Together they swam around, discovering new creatures and places. From then on Tom always protected himself, and enjoyed exploring the hidden reef with his new shark friend.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called George who lived on a farm. One day, he noticed something strange in the sky. He asked his mother, "Mama, what's that in the sky?" His mother smiled and said, "It's going to be an easy day George, because I am going to sell my arm at the market." The little boy was confused and he asked, "What is an arm?" His mother said, "An arm is something that helps people move. I am selling my arm so I can buy the food that we need." The morning was busy and George went to the market with his mother. He saw her selling her arm and he was sad and scared. He asked her, "Mama, why did you have to sell your arm? Can't we just get the food another way?" "It's ok George," she said. "This was the easiest way to get food." George's mother returned home with enough food to feed the family. She knew that selling her arm paid off and it was all thanks to her hard work and determination.
