Title: Lily the Flower and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little flower named Lily. Lily was a nervous flower. She lived in a big garden with many other flowers. One day, a new flower named Daisy came to the garden. Daisy was very big and strong. Lily was scared and said, "I don't know if I fit in with the other flowers." Daisy smiled and said, "Don't be nervous, Lily. You are a beautiful flower and you fit in just right." Lily felt better and soon became friends with Daisy. They played and laughed together in the garden. From that day on, Lily was not nervous anymore. She knew she fit in with all her friends in the garden.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily lived in a small house. One day, she went to the garden to play. She saw a big, red strawberry. Lily wanted to eat the strawberry. Lily looked around to see if anyone was watching. She felt a little guilty, but she picked the strawberry and ate it. It was so yummy! She smiled and her tummy was happy. Later, her mommy came to the garden. She saw the missing strawberry and asked Lily if she ate it. Lily felt guilty and said, "Yes, mommy, I ate the strawberry." Mommy hugged Lily and said, "It's okay, but next time, please ask before you eat."

Once upon a time there was a little rat who lived in a big drum. He loved to bang on the walls of his drum and make lots of noise. But one day, one of the walls of the drum broke, and the rat saw a way to escape. At first, the rat was scared. He didn't know the outside world, and he hardly knew what was out there. But he was brave, and he decided to try to escape. The rat quickly found out that escaping was not easy. Everywhere he went he came into difficult situations and had to use his wit and intelligence to get past them. He didn't give up, and eventually he was successful and found himself free in the outside world. The moral of the story is that if you have courage and don't give up, even difficult situations can be overcome.

Once upon a time, there was a dog with an orange collar. The dog liked to play in the park with his friends. They would run, jump, and bark all day long. One day, the dog saw a big ball in the park. He pointed at the ball with his nose. His friends saw the ball too. They all wanted to play with the big ball. The dog with the orange collar ran to the ball first. He pushed the ball with his nose and it rolled very fast. His friends chased the ball and they all laughed. They played with the big ball until the sun went down. Then, they went home, tired but happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big pink bird. The bird was very graceful. It would fly high in the sky and look down at the world below. One day, the bird saw a little girl. The girl was sad. She had lost her toy. The pink bird wanted to help. It flew down and looked for the toy. The bird found the toy under a bush. It picked up the toy with its beak. The bird flew back to the girl. But, surprise! The toy had turned into a beautiful pink flower. The girl was happy. She thanked the graceful bird and they became friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. The sun was shining, and the ground was very dry. He saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. While he sat there, he heard a strange sound. He looked around and saw a little ghost. "Hello," said the ghost. "My name is Boo. Do you want to play with me?" Tim was not afraid. He nodded and said, "Yes, I want to play with you, Boo!" They played all day, running and jumping. Then, they saw a big cat. The cat was trying to fight with a small dog. Tim and Boo didn't want them to fight. So, they went to the cat and the dog and said, "Stop fighting! Let's all play together." The cat and the dog stopped fighting. They all played together until the sun went down. Tim, Boo, the cat, and the dog became good friends. They promised to play together again soon.
