Title: Dee and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a small, independent bird named Dee. Dee liked to design her own nest. She would find sticks, leaves, and other things to make her nest pretty and strong. One day, Dee found a big, shiny rock. She thought it would be perfect for her nest. But the rock had a lot of weight. Dee tried very hard to carry it, but she could not do it alone. A kind squirrel saw Dee and wanted to help. Together, they carried the rock to Dee's nest. The squirrel helped Dee design her nest with the rock. When they were done, the nest was the prettiest one in the forest. The unexpected thing that happened was that Dee and the squirrel became best friends. They learned that working together made things easier and more fun. The moral of the story is that we can do more when we help each other, even if we want to be independent.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue went to the train station with her mom. They were going to visit her grandma. Sue was very excited. She knew this trip was important. She held her mom's hand tightly as they walked. At the station, Sue saw a big train. It was so big that it made her blink her eyes. She looked at the train and blinked again. She could not believe how big it was. Suddenly, the train started to move. Sue and her mom were not on it yet! But then, something unexpected happened. A small train came out from behind the big train. Sue and her mom got on the small train. They laughed and smiled. They did not need the big train after all. The small train took them to grandma's house, and they had a great time.

Beth was excited. She was going to the store with her mom. The store was filled with all sorts of exciting things. When they got to the store, Beth and her mom spent a long time looking at all of the things to buy. After they had picked out some things, Beth was allowed to pick out a toy. She chose a bright yellow car that made a beep noise. On the way home, Beth couldn't stop playing with her new car. When they finally got to their house, Beth's mom said it was time to put her car away. Beth was sad, but put it in her room. The next day, Beth and her mom went back to the store. When they arrived, Beth was surprised to see that the store was empty! Her mom explained that everything had been taken back to the store. Beth was upset because she had wanted to buy something else. But, just then, a truck arrived with all of the things from the store. Beth was so excited! Her mom said that the store had returned everything, so Beth could get something else. She quickly chose another toy, and ran back home with it. Beth had returned to the store, and now it was full again!"

Once upon a time, there was a busy little ant named Tim. He had many friends, and they all worked together. They helped each other carry food and build their home. Tim was always happy to help his friends, but sometimes he would think too much about what he wanted to do. One day, while Tim was carrying a big leaf, he tripped and hurt his knee. It was very sore, and Tim couldn't walk. His friends saw him and came to help. They all tried to think of a way to make Tim's knee feel better. They put a soft leaf on it and told him to rest. Tim didn't like to rest. He wanted to be busy and help his friends. But he knew he had to let his knee get better. While he was resting, something unexpected happened. Tim learned that he could still be helpful by thinking of new ways for his friends to work together. He became very good at this, and soon his friends were working better than ever. In the end, Tim's knee got better, and he went back to being busy with his friends. But he never forgot the lesson he learned. Sometimes, it's good to stop and think, so you can find new ways to help others. And that's the story of Tim, the busy little ant who learned the value of thinking and helping his friends in new ways.
