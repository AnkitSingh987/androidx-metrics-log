Title: The Story of the Bear and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy bear. The bear lived in a small house in the woods. In the house, there was a big wardrobe. The bear loved to relax next to the wardrobe every day. One day, the bear went for a walk. While he was away, a little girl came into the house. She saw the wardrobe and wanted to play. She opened the wardrobe and found pretty dresses inside. The girl tried on the dresses and danced around. The hairy bear came back home and saw the girl. He was very angry. The bear did not want anyone to touch his wardrobe. He roared loudly, and the girl got scared. She ran away from the house, crying. The bear never saw the girl again, and he could not relax anymore.

Lily likes to play with animals. She has a cat, a dog and a fish. She loves them all. She gives them food and water and hugs. She likes to lift them and hold them. One day, Lily sees a new animal in the yard. It is big and brown and has a long nose. Lily thinks it is a funny animal. She wants to play with it. She runs to the animal and says hello. The animal is not happy. It does not like Lily. It is an angry animal. It is a bear. It growls and shows its teeth. It lifts its paw and hits Lily. Lily falls down and cries. She is hurt. Lily's mom hears Lily cry. She runs to the yard and sees the bear. She is scared. She yells and throws a stick at the bear. The bear runs away. Lily's mom picks up Lily and hugs her. She sees blood on Lily's face and arm. She is sad. She calls for help. She takes Lily to the car and drives to the doctor. The doctor looks at Lily and says she is not fine. She has bad cuts and bites. She needs stitches and medicine. She has to stay in the hospital for many days. Lily is in pain. She misses her cat, her dog and her fish. She wishes she never played with the bear.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little bunny named Binky. Binky was a harmless bunny who loved to play with his friends. One day, he went for a walk in nature with his best friend, a tiny bird named Chirpy. As they walked, Binky and Chirpy talked and laughed. "I love the pretty flowers," said Chirpy. "Me too," said Binky. They saw lots of trees, flowers, and bugs. They had so much fun! But then, Binky and Chirpy realized they did not know the way back home. They had forgotten the path they took. "Oh no, what do we do?" asked Binky. Chirpy thought for a moment and said, "Let's ask the big wise owl for help." So, Binky and Chirpy went to find the wise owl. They asked him for help, and he showed them the way back home. Binky and Chirpy were very happy and thanked the wise owl. They learned to never forget the path when they played in nature again. And they lived happily ever after.

There was a infant who lived in the forest with her parents. Every day, her parents would take her to find amazing things. One day, they went to the river and the infant could see a big fish swimming in the river. The infant thought it was so amazing that she wanted to show everyone. She asked her parents, “Can I go tell people about the fish?” Her parents said, “Yes, you can go show them.” So the infant went and told the other people in the forest about the fish. They were so amazed. The people asked her, “Where did you see the amazing fish?” The infant replied and said, “I saw it at the river.” But the people got angry and so they chased the infant away. They said, “How dare you tell people about this amazing fish? You can never come back here again!” And so the infant had to go away and she never saw the amazing fish again.
