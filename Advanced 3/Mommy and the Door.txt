Title: Mommy and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a good mommy and her good baby. Mommy was very happy and they would play together every day. One day, Mommy got a lock. She wanted to show the baby how to use it. She put the lock on the door and said: "Now, baby, turn this way and end the lock." The baby tried to turn it and said: "It's stuck!" Mommy grabbed the lock and said: "Let me help." She quickly turned it the other way and the lock clicked. Mommy said: "That's it! Now it's good and the door is locked." The mommy and baby laughed and hugged. They had fun playing with the lock together. Mommy said: "Now let's go find something else to do!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was worried because he lost his favorite towel. He looked for it everywhere in his house. He could not find it. Tim was very sad. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw Tim was worried. Sam asked, "Why are you worried, Tim?" Tim told Sam about the lost towel. Sam wanted to help. Sam said, "I recommend we look for it together." Tim liked the idea. They looked in the house again. As they looked, they found a big box in the corner. They opened the box and saw many towels. Tim's favorite towel was there too! But, there was a twist. The towels were all the same color as Tim's towel. Tim was happy but confused. He did not know which one was his towel. Sam laughed, "Tim, I got you a surprise! I made more towels like yours so you never lose it again!" Tim was so happy and thanked Sam. They played with the new towels all day.

Anna and Ben liked to play in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. One day, they saw a big box under a tree. It was a mystery. What was inside the box? They walked to the box and looked at it. It was brown and had a red ribbon. They tried to open it, but it was locked. They heard a noise from inside. It was a growl. They were frightened. They ran away from the box. A man came to the park. He had a key. He opened the box. He smiled. He took out a puppy. It was his birthday present. He hugged the puppy and thanked his wife. He saw Anna and Ben hiding behind a bench. He waved at them and said, "Do you want to meet my puppy? He is very friendly and cute." Anna and Ben came out of their hiding place. They were curious. They walked to the man and the puppy. The puppy licked their faces and wagged his tail. They smiled. They petted the puppy and played with him. They were not frightened anymore. They liked the puppy. They liked the mystery.

Once upon a time, a blue bird named Bobby lived in a big tree. Bobby loved to fly high in the sky and play with his friends. One day, he found a shiny silver coin on the ground. Bobby showed the silver coin to his friend, Timmy the squirrel. Timmy said, "Wow! That's pretty! But be careful not to destroy it." Bobby and Timmy decided to keep the silver coin safe in a small hole in their tree. They played together every day, and the silver coin stayed safe. The blue bird and the squirrel were very happy and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Sam. Sam was very hungry. One day, Sam went to his backyard. He found some boxes and he decided to build something with them. Sam worked very hard and he built a big bed. He put a mattress on the bed. The mattress was soft and Sam felt so happy. Sam laid down the mattress and he laid down too. The bed felt so comfortable, he wasn't hungry anymore. He closed his eyes and he fell asleep. The next morning, Sam woke up and he felt better. He knew his bed was so comfortable that he wasn't hungry anymore. He thanked the mattress for helping him. Sam and his mattress were friends. Every day, they would play together and Sam wouldn't be hungry anymore.
