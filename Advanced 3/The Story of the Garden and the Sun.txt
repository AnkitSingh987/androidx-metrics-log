Title: The Story of the Garden and the Sun

Once there was a little girl, who planted a tiny seed in her garden. She looked after it carefully, watering it every day. One day, when she came out she saw that the seed had grown into a huge sunflower and it had one beautiful, yellow petal. The girl was so grateful that she had something so lovely in her garden. But soon she noticed that the petal was shrinking and getting smaller and smaller. The girl was confused: what could be making the petal shrink? She thought very hard and eventually realized that when the sun was shining it was shrinking the petal. When the sun went down, the petal grew back to its original size. The girl was happy and amazed by this sight. She realized the power the sun had and how special her garden was. She was grateful that she had discovered this.

One day, a happy girl named Sue went for a walk. She found a big tube in the park. Sue looked at the tube and said, "Wow! What a big tube! I want to play with it!" Sue picked up the tube and walked to her friend Tim's house. She knocked on the door. Tim opened the door and saw Sue holding the tube. "Hi Sue! What do you have?" Tim asked. Sue replied, "I found a big tube in the park. Let's play with it!" Sue and Tim played with the tube all day. They rolled it, jumped over it, and even crawled through it. They had so much fun playing together. At the end of the day, Sue and Tim were very tired but very happy. They said goodbye to each other and Sue walked back home with her big tube.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She was so excited to explore the world around her. Everywhere Sarah went, she always rubbed things with her hands to get a better sight. One day, she discovered something new. No matter how much she rubbed the rock, nothing happened. But Sarah had a feeling that something would happen if only she kept rubbing. Finally, while Sarah was rubbing the rock, it split in half. At first, Sarah was confused. But then she realized that inside the rock was something new and amazing - a sparkling diamond! She couldn't believe her sight! Sarah had discovered something truly special and she couldn't wait to show her family and friends. From that day on, she would never forget the wonderful feeling she experienced when her hard work of rubbing the rock paid off.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to watch his favorite show on television. He asked his mom, "Can I watch TV now?" His mom said, "Yes, but only for a little while." Tim was very stubborn. He wanted to watch TV all day. He didn't want to stop and play outside with his friends. His mom told him, "Tim, you need to go outside and play. It's a sunny day." But Tim didn't want to listen. Finally, his mom said, "If you don't go outside to play, I will turn off the TV." Tim knew his mom was serious. So, he turned off the television and went outside to play with his friends. After playing all day, Tim was happy he listened to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little star. This star was very crazy. It liked to dance and sing all day long. It was a very happy star. One day, the star saw a fruit on the ground. The fruit was not good. It was rot. The star felt sad for the fruit. It wanted to help the fruit. The star talked to the fruit. It told the fruit to be happy and dance like the star. The fruit tried to dance, but it was still rot. The star learned that it cannot fix everything. But being happy and kind can make others feel better too.

Anna and Ben went to the park with Mom. They saw a big pond with many ducks and swans. Anna liked the swans. They had long necks and white feathers. Ben liked the ducks. They had yellow beaks and brown feathers. Mom gave Anna and Ben some bread to feed the animals. Anna and Ben broke the bread into small pieces and threw them into the water. The ducks and swans came closer and ate the bread. They made noises like quack and honk. Anna and Ben laughed and clapped. Then Mom said it was time to go. She took Anna and Ben to a small shop near the pond. The shop had ice cream and candy. Mom said they could have a treat for being good. Anna and Ben were happy. They chose their favorite flavors of ice cream. Anna picked strawberry and Ben picked chocolate. Mom paid the shopkeeper some coins and thanked him. The shopkeeper smiled and gave them spoons. Anna and Ben licked their ice cream. It was cold and yummy. They shared some with Mom. Mom said it was delicious. They walked back to the car, holding hands and enjoying their treat. They saw the ducks and swans again. They waved goodbye to them. The ducks and swans waved back with their wings. Anna and Ben said they had a fun day at the park. Mom said she loved them very much.
