Title: Lily and the Sun

Once upon a time, a delicate pony named Lily lived on a farm. Lily was very pretty and loved to play with her friends in the sun. She had a shiny mane and a soft tail that swayed when she ran. One day, Lily saw an apple on the ground. It was not a good apple. It was old and had started to rot. Lily's friend, a small bird named Tim, said, "Don't eat that apple, Lily. It's bad for you!" Lily listened to Tim and left the apple alone. They played together all day, running and laughing in the warm sun. At the end of the day, they were tired but happy. They knew they had made the right choice to not eat the bad apple.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack who wanted to find a way to show how he felt about the world. He came across an attractive thermometer and wanted to introduce it to his friends. Jack was so excited to show everyone the thermometer, but when he tried to show them it didn't work. His friends were disappointed and started to leave. This made Jack very sad, but he refused to give up. Jack worked hard to figure out why the thermometer wasn't working and eventually he discovered that it needed some energy to power it. So he added a little battery and the thermometer started to work. All of Jack's friends were so glad and thanked Jack for teaching them such an important lesson. The moral of the story is that even when things seem hard, working together and trying again can solve the problem.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Lisa. She was three years old and loved to explore. One day she was on an adventure in the woods and she found something very special. It was a surprise! Lisa was so excited that she said "Wow! What is it?" She couldn’t wait to see what the surprise was. When she opened it, she found a little toy puppy. She was so happy! She hugged it and said "I love you puppy!". But then she noticed something was wrong. The puppy was not moving. Lisa was very sorry and started to cry. Then suddenly the puppy woke up! He started to wag his tail and lick Lisa’s face. She was so happy! Lisa hugged him and said " I am so glad I found you, surprise!" The puppy and Lisa became best friends. They played together every day and went on many adventures. And that's how Lisa found her surprise - a best friend!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He was a nervous boy. Tim had a dog, and his dog was named Max. They lived in a small house near the woods. One day, Tim and Max went for a walk in the woods. They found a big tree with a hole in it. Inside the hole, there was a bottle of wine. Tim said to Max, "Look, Max, we found wine! Let's save it for later." As they walked back home, a big, mean bear saw them. The bear wanted the wine. The bear said, "Give me the wine, or I will eat you!" Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. Tim and Max ran away from the bear, but they were not fast enough. The bear caught them and took the wine. Tim and Max were sad. They went back home without the wine, and they never found any more wine again.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in the sand at the beach. She had a big towel to sit on. She was worried because she could not find her friend, Tim. Lily wanted to play with Tim, but she did not know where he was. Lily saw a big sand hill and thought Tim might be behind it. She walked to the sand hill and found a boy sitting on a towel. "Are you Tim?" she asked. The boy looked up and said, "No, I am Sam. But I can help you find Tim." Lily was happy to have help. Sam and Lily looked everywhere for Tim. Finally, they found Tim sitting on his towel under a tree. Lily was so happy to see her friend. They all played together and had a fun day at the beach. The sun was setting and it was time to go home. Lily learned that sometimes things do not go as planned, but with the help of new friends, everything can turn out okay.
