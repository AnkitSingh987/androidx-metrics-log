Title: The Story of the Park and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a big, dark park. In the park, there was a pretty fountain. Many people would go to the park to look at the fountain. They would admire it and say, "Wow, what a nice fountain!" One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. They walked and played near the fountain. Lucy loved the water and would laugh as it splashed. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. The water in the fountain turned into candy! All the people in the park were so surprised. They ran to the fountain and started to eat the candy. Lucy and her mom joined them, and everyone had a great time. They all admired the magical fountain even more now. Later, it was found out that a kind candy maker had put magic into the fountain to make people happy. The dark park became a happy place where people would come to eat candy and play. And the pretty fountain was admired by everyone who visited.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. The toy car had a special power. It could lock its wheels and go very slow. One day, Tim took his toy car outside to play. He pushed the car and made it go slow by locking the wheels. He liked to watch the car go slow because it made him feel like he had a magic power. As Tim played with his car, a little girl named Sue came to play with him. They both had fun with the toy car, taking turns locking the wheels and making it go slow. They laughed and played all day, enjoying the special power of the toy car. And they became good friends, all because of the little toy car that could lock its wheels and go slow.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Mary. She loved to explore and was always looking for new and exciting places to play. One day she found a creek near her home and she was so excited. She raced home to grab a bucket and some friends and went to the creek and played in the water. Mary noticed the water had an orange tint to it. She wondered why it was that color and went to ask her dad. He said, "the orange in the water comes from the factories upstream. They use too much of the chemicals and it pollutes the creek." Mary felt bad for the creek and decided to help. She told her friends and they all worked together to help keep the creek clean. They would pick up the trash and make sure the factories were following the rules. Mary’s dad was very proud of her and said, "you have served the creek well by helping to keep it clean and healthy." The moral of the story is; when you help take care of the environment you are helping to serve your community.

One day, a little girl named Olive went to the park with her mom. Olive loved the park because she could play on the swings and slides. Olive's mom said, "Olive, I will bring a yummy snack for us to eat." At the park, Olive played and played. After a while, she was very hungry. Olive's mom said, "Olive, it is time for our yummy snack!" Olive was so excited to eat the snack her mom had brought. But when Olive's mom opened the bag, she found a big, funny-looking fruit. "Oh no!" she said. "I brought the wrong snack!" Olive looked at the fruit and said, "Let's try it, Mom!" They both took a bite and found out it was very yummy. They laughed and enjoyed their new snack at the park.

One day, a gifted deer was in the woods. The deer could do a special thing. It could vanish and then come back. The deer had a friend, a little bird. The bird said, "Can you show me how you vanish?" The deer said, "Yes, watch me." And the deer vanished. The bird was very surprised. Then, the deer came back. The bird said, "Wow, I want to try!" The deer said, "I can help you." The deer touched the bird with its nose, and the bird vanished too! They played hide and seek all day, and had lots of fun.
