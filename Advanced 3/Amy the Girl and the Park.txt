Title: Amy the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy had a toy cell that she loved to play with. It was a fake cell, but she didn't care. She would talk to her toy friends on the cell all day long. One day, Amy went to the park with her mom. She took her toy cell with her to talk to her toy friends while she played. But when it was time to go home, Amy forget her toy cell at the park. She didn't notice it was gone until she got home. Amy was very sad and told her mom about the lost cell. Her mom said they would go back to the park the next day to find it. The next day, they went to the park and looked everywhere for the toy cell. Finally, they found it under a slide. Amy was so happy to have her toy cell back and promised never to forget it again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very impatient. He wanted to learn how to ride a bike, but he was scared. His mom said, "Tim, I will help you. We can do it together." For a week, Tim and his mom practiced riding the bike. Every day, his mom would encourage him. She would say, "You can do it, Tim. Keep trying!" Tim would try and try, but he would fall down. He would get back up and try again. One day, Tim was riding his bike and he did not fall. He was so happy. He said, "Mom, I did it! I can ride my bike!" His mom smiled and said, "I knew you could do it, Tim. I am so proud of you!" Tim felt proud too. He knew that with a little help and encouragement, he could do anything.

Once upon a time there lived a poor mouse. The mouse wanted to find somewhere to hide from all the cats. He found a mill and thought it would be a safe place. He entered the mill and was cheerful when he realized it was a nice and warm place. But, soon the mouse realized that the mill was full of dangerous things and his happiness disappeared. He was scared and decided to leave immediately. As he was running out of the mill, a big cat appeared. The mouse was extremely frightened, he tried his best to hide, but it was too late. The cat quickly captured him. The moral of the story is: Don't be too quick to decide something without first understanding the situation.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. They would run, jump, and have fun together. One day, they played in the mud and got very dirty. Tim's mom said it was time for a shower. Tim did not like showers, but he knew he had to get clean. He got in the shower and washed off all the mud. When he was done, he felt much better. After the shower, Tim's mom told him that it is important to value being clean. Tim thought about it and agreed. From that day on, Tim always took showers when he got dirty. He learned to value staying clean and healthy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Alex. He was three years old and he liked to explore outside. One day, he was out in the garden and he saw something in the dirt that looked very special. He picked it up and shouted, "Look! I found something!” His mom came running and said, “What is it?” Alex pointed to the small object in his hands and said, “I think it’s jewelry!” His mom took it and said, “Yes, it looks like jewelry. It must be very important if it was just lying there. We should keep it safe in my closet, so nobody can lose it.” So Alex and his mom put the jewelry in the closet and made sure it was locked. Alex was so happy to have found an important jewelry!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, Tim's mom asked him to get a knife to cut some fruit. Tim was very happy to help. He found the knife and gave it to his mom. She started to cut the fruit for them to eat. As they ate the fruit, Tim noticed that the bowl was getting empty. He wanted to eat more fruit, so he asked his mom to increase the amount of fruit in the bowl. His mom agreed and went to the fridge to get more fruit. But when she opened the fridge, she saw that all the fruit was gone! Tim's mom was very surprised. She didn't know what had happened. Then, Tim remembered that he had seen his dog, Max, in the kitchen earlier. They looked around and found Max hiding under the table with the missing fruit. Max had taken the fruit when they weren't looking! Tim and his mom laughed, and they all shared the fruit together.
