Title: Tommy and the Bird

Tommy was playing outside with his favourite rag. Dangerous animals didn't live nearby, so he felt safe and happy. He looked around and yelled, "Where are you?" A harmless small blue bird perched on the rim of a pot in the garden, which made Tommy smile. He shook the rag at the bird and yelled, "Fly away!" The bird flew away, as if it was afraid. Tommy was now alone with his rag, so he tossed it up in the air and yelled, "Catch!" Tommy caught his rag again, and then he laughed and yelled, "Yay!" He felt so free and happy that he started dancing around with his rag. Suddenly, a loud noise came from his house. His mom had found him and she was yelling for him. Tommy knew he had to go back, so he picked up his rag and ran towards his mother. She hugged him and said, "Tommy, there are some dangerous things out here, so stay close and be safe." He nodded and said, "Yes, Mommy," and then he looked back at his rag, which had made his adventure so harmless and so much fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big world. In this world, there were two friends, a cat and a dog. The cat was small and the dog was big. They liked to play together every day. One day, the cat said, "I want to find the end of the world." The dog thought it was a fun idea. So, they both started to walk. They walked and walked until it was clear that they were far from home. At last, they saw a big tree. The cat said, "This is the end of the world!" They were happy and sat under the tree. They talked and played until it was time to go back home. They had a great day and were very tired.

One day a little girl was playing in the park. She was singing a charming poem as she ran around, enjoying her day. Suddenly, a new person appeared in the park. He was a kind old man with a friendly face. The little girl stopped singing and said hello to the friendly old man. He smiled and said "Hello young one, what is that charming poem you were singing"? The little girl answered "It's a poem about the beauty of the park. It goes like this: 'In the park the flowers appear, in the park the birds sing so clear.'" The old man smiled and said "That is a very nice poem. I wish I could have heard the whole thing." The little girl said "I can sing you the whole poem if you like." The old man nodded and said "Please, that would be lovely." So the little girl began to sing the charming poem about the park again for the old man. When she finished, the old man was smiling and clapping. The little girl was happy and thanked him for listening. They said goodbye and the old man walked away. The little girl returned to her playing, singing her charming poem again.

"Run, Sammy! Run!" yelled Sammy's mom from the porch of their house. Sammy giggled and ran as fast as his chubby legs could carry him. His curls bounced as he ran around the backyard, stopping every now and then to take in the attractive summer sights. Finally, Sammy came back to his mom and said, "I'm done running, Mommy!" His mom smiled and said, "I'm so proud of my Sammy, you are so fast and strong!" Sammy smiled, blushing in the admiration. Sammy's mom reached down and gave him a sweet kiss on his neck. "You make me so happy," said Sammy's mom. Sammy smiled even wider, feeling very content. "Let's go inside now, okay? And get you something cool to drink!" Sammy nodded and ran back inside, ready for some juice!

Once upon a time, there was a robot. The robot was deaf, but he was very happy. He had a big ball that he liked to roll all day. One day, the robot saw a friend, a little cat. The cat was sad because it lost its ball. The robot wanted to help the cat. He took his own ball and rolled it to the cat. The cat was very happy and they played together. As they played, more friends came to join. There was a dog, a bird, and a rabbit. They all played with the robot and the cat. They learned that sharing makes everyone happy. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.
