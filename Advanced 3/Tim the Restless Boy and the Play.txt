Title: Tim the Restless Boy and the Play

Once upon a time, there was a restless boy named Tim. He did not like to sit still. He liked to run and play all day. Tim did not care about math. He thought it was boring. One day, his mom told him he had to do his math work. Tim did not want to do it. He wanted to play outside with his friends. But his mom said, "You must do your math work first." Tim was not happy. He tried to do his math work, but he was too restless. He could not focus on the numbers. So, he decided to go outside and play anyway. While Tim played, his math work sat on the table. His mom found it and saw that it was not done. She was very sad and angry. When Tim came back inside, his mom told him he could not play with his friends for a whole week. Tim cried because he did not listen to his mom and now he could not play.

Once upon a time, there was a tall girl. She had a box of pastels that she liked to mark with. Every day she decorated the walls of her house with pretty pictures. One day, she took her pastels outside and made a big mark on the sidewalk. The pictures she drew were very nice. Everyone who saw they said "Wow!" and clapped for the tall girl and her pastels. The tall girl smiled proudly and kept marking.

Once upon a time, there was a little shrimp. The shrimp lived in a filthy pond. It did not like the dirty water. The shrimp wanted a clean home. One day, a kind girl came to the pond. She saw the filthy water and the shrimp. The girl wanted to help the shrimp. She had a plan to replace the dirty water with clean water. The shrimp was happy. The girl started to replace the water. The shrimp was excited. But then, something unexpected happened. The clean water made the shrimp grow big! It was not a shrimp anymore, it was a big fish. The girl and the fish became best friends. They played together every day.

Sam was a young boy who loved exploring. One day, he went into the woods looking for a new place to discover. As Sam went deeper into the trees, he noticed a large old house at the end of a path. He stopped and heard a noise coming from inside. Sam was a little scared but he bravely walked closer. Peeking through the window, he saw a ghost stealing jewelry. The ghost had a fake smile and Sam knew he had to do something to stop it. He went around the back of the house and saw the ghost coming out with more and more jewelry. Sam knew he had to act fast. He started running towards the ghost and shouted! The ghost got scared and dropped all of the jewelry. Sam quickly gathered it back up and the ghost ran away. Sam was relieved. He knew he had stop the ghost from stealing more things. He smiled and went back home.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big fake rock in his yard. He thought it was very funny. He decided to keep the rock and show it to his friends. Tim took the rock to his friend Sue's house. They played with the rock and laughed. Then, something unexpected happened. The fake rock opened up and a small toy came out. Tim and Sue were in shock. They did not know the rock had a toy inside. Tim and Sue played with the toy and had lots of fun. They were happy that the fake rock had a surprise inside. From that day on, they always looked for more fake rocks, hoping to find more surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy computer that he loved to play with every day. One day, while playing with his computer, it broke into two parts. Tim felt very sorry and sad. Tim went to his mom and showed her the broken computer. His mom saw the two parts and felt sorry for Tim too. She told him not to worry and that they would try to fix it together. They sat down and carefully put the two parts back together. With a little bit of love and patience, the computer was fixed! Tim was so happy and thanked his mom. From that day on, Tim took extra care of his computer and always remembered the day his mom helped him fix it.
