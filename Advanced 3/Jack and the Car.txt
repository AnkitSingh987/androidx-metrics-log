Title: Jack and the Car

A small boy named Jack was playing in the garden one day. Playing with his toy car, he noticed his neighbour in her garden. Jack smiled and said hello, and his neighbour waved back. “Can I borrow your weight?” asked Jack. “What do you want it for?” asked his neighbour. “I need it to hold my toy car still. It keeps sliding away!” His neighbour thought it was a good idea and agreed. She got the weight and handed it to Jack. “It’s a bit smelly,” said Jack, scrunching up his nose. “Well, it was in the garage,” said his neighbour with a laugh. Jack took the weight and thanked her. Now he could play with his car without it sliding away!

Once there was a little pig who was walking through the meadow. Suddenly, he got an alert feeling. He stopped and looked around. He noticed a bin poking out of the ground. He went closer to investigate. The bin had holes in it. He poked his nose inside and sniffed. He heard some strange noises coming from it. He jumped back in alarm. “What’s in there?” the little pig wondered. He was brave, so he poked his nose in a little bit further. Then he heard something that sounded like a voice. “Help me!” The little pig was startled. He had to figure out what was in the bin. He poked around some more. Suddenly, a little fox poked its head out of the bin. “Thank you for helping me! I was stuck in the bin and I couldn't get out!” said the fox. The little pig was relieved and glad that he had been so alert. He had saved the fox from being trapped in the bin.

Once upon a time, in a land of green trees, there lived a little bird. The bird loved to perform songs for his friends. One day, the bird saw a big judge sitting under a tree. The judge was watching all the animals play. The bird wanted to perform for the judge, but he was scared. His friends told him, "Don't be scared, just do your best." So, the bird flew to the tree and started to sing. The judge listened to the bird's song, but he did not like it. The judge told the bird, "You are not good at singing. Go away." The bird was very sad and flew away. He stopped performing for his friends. The other animals missed the bird's songs and they were sad too. The moral of the story is to always be kind with your words, because they can hurt others.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a playful cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with the family's laundry. One day, Kitty decided to gather all the laundry and make a big pile. Kitty went around the house, picking up shirts, pants, and socks. She put them all in a big pile in the living room. Then, she jumped and played in the pile. She was having so much fun! Mom came into the room and saw the big mess. She said, "Kitty, why did you make a mess with the laundry?" Kitty just looked at her and continued to play. Mom was not happy. She said, "Kitty, you must clean up this mess right now!" But Kitty did not want to clean up. She just wanted to play. Because Kitty did not clean up the mess, she got in trouble. Mom took away her favorite toy and Kitty had to watch as the laundry was put away. Kitty was sad and learned her lesson.

Once upon a time there was a magical river. It was full of incredible creatures and different coloured flags. One day, a little girl went to visit the river with her family. She was amazed! She said to her family, "Wow! This is incredible!" They all started to splash in the river, and soon they were having the best time ever. All the creatures were laughing and jumping around too. The little girl waved a beautiful pink flag and shouted, "Let's go explore!". Her family jumped in and splashed around, looking for new and exciting things. They had an incredible adventure and the little girl never forgot the amazing magical river she had visited. She even kept the flag as a reminder!
