Title: Mia the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to play in her garden. In the garden, there were many ordinary flowers. But Mia wanted a big sunflower. One day, Mia asked her mommy, "Mommy, can I have a sunflower, please?" Her mommy said, "Of course, Mia. Let's plant a sunflower seed together." So they planted a seed in the garden. Every day, Mia watered the seed and waited. Soon, a tiny sprout came out of the ground. Mia was so happy! The sprout grew and grew. It became a big, beautiful sunflower. Mia and her mommy loved the sunflower very much.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane loved to play and make things with her hands. One day, she found a big box of junk in her room. It had lots of things like paper, old toys, and sticks. Jane was very happy and thought, "I can make something fun with this!" Jane started to cut the paper into fun shapes. She cut circles, squares, and even hearts. As she was cutting, her mom came in and said, "Be careful with the scissors, Jane. They can be bitter if you don't use them right." Jane nodded and kept cutting carefully. The next day, Jane showed her mom the beautiful things she made from the junk. Her mom was so proud of her. She said, "You did a great job, Jane! You were careful with the scissors and made something amazing from the junk." Jane smiled, happy that she had listened to her mom and made something special.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park. He had a big, soft pillow with him. He liked to play with his pillow. In the park, he met a girl named Sue. Sue was very patient and kind. Tom said, "Sue, let's play with my pillow!" Sue said, "Yes, let's go!" They took turns throwing the pillow in the air and catching it. They were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the pillow away. Tom and Sue ran after the pillow, but they could not catch it. The pillow went far, far away. Tom was very sad. He lost his pillow. Sue said, "I'm sorry, Tom." They went home without the pillow. It was a bad day for Tom and Sue. They did not know that the wind would take the pillow away.

Once upon a time, there was an otter named John. John was very fat. He loved to play in the water, but he found it hard to swim. One day, John saw a beautiful boat zooming past him. John was so excited and wanted to go in the boat. So, he quickly jumped in and started waving his arms and legs like paddles. Just then, John's parents came to help him. They said: "John, you cannot zoom in the boat. You need to learn to swim properly first before you can get in a boat." John was very sad and apologized to his parents for not listening. After that, John practiced and practiced to learn how to swim. Eventually, with the help of his parents, John became so good at swimming that he could swim faster than the boat could zoom! The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and practice to achieve your goals.

Once upon a time, there was a weak little fly. The fly wanted to paint a picture, but it was hard for him to hold the brush. One day, the fly met a big, strong ant. The ant saw the fly was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, little fly?" The fly said, "I want to paint, but I am too weak to hold the brush." The ant wanted to help the fly. So, the ant held the brush and the fly told the ant what to paint. Together, they painted a beautiful picture. The fly was happy now, and they became good friends. They painted more pictures together, and they had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to make a sandwich. She took out bread, jam, and a knife. Sue was ready to make her mighty sandwich. As she began to stir the jam, she heard a tiny voice. "Stop!" it said. Sue looked around and saw a tiny ant. The ant was talking to her! This was unexpected. The ant said, "Please don't use that jam. My family lives inside." Sue was surprised, but she wanted to help the ants. So, Sue and the ant found a new jar of jam. They all became friends and enjoyed the mighty sandwich together.
