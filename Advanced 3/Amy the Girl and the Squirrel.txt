Title: Amy the Girl and the Squirrel

One day, a small girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. They saw a big tree with roots coming out of the ground. Amy wanted to sit on the root, but it was dirty. Her mom said, "Let's polish the root to make it clean." Amy and her mom polished the root until it was shiny and clean. Then, they sat on it and ate their lunch. They had tasty sandwiches and shared them with a squirrel. The squirrel said, "Thank you for the tasty food!" As they were eating, a big wind came and blew their bag of food away. Amy was sad, but the squirrel had an idea. He ran to his tree and brought back nuts and berries for Amy and her mom. They all shared the food and had a great day. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with others, because you never know when you might need help too.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very adventurous. He loved to play and have fun. One day, Tim saw a big slide in the park. He wanted to play on the slide. Before Tim went on the slide, he decided to study it. He looked at the slide from top to bottom. He saw that it was very tall and very fast. But Tim was not scared. He wanted to try the slide. Tim climbed up the slide and sat at the top. He was ready to go down. But when he slid down the slide, he went too fast. Tim fell off the slide and got hurt. He cried, and his mom came to help him. Tim learned that sometimes being adventurous can lead to a bad ending.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Daisy and Daisy Duck. Daisy was the lazy one, lying in the sun all day, while Daisy Duck was always on the hunt for adventure. One day, Daisy Duck saw something very mysterious. It was a tiny inch worm, slowly crawling along the grass. Suddenly, the inch worm vanished into thin air! Daisy Duck looked around with her big eyes, trying to find the creature. She called out, "Yoo-hoo, where have you gone?" Just then, a loud voice came from behind them. "Did you call for me?" asked the inch worm. Daisy and Daisy Duck were so surprised. How did the inch worm reappear? Daisy asked her lazy friend, "How did it vanish and then come back again?" The inch worm replied, "As all inch worms can, I can vanish and reappear wherever and whenever I please. I am in full control of my magic. Now, if you'll excuse me, I must be on my way." With that, the inch worm vanished again. Daisy and Daisy Duck stayed still, trying to take in what just happened. Daisy smiled at her best friend, and they both agreed that today had been a very exciting but lazy day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and baby brother. Jane loved to play with her toys, but her room was very messy. Her mom told her to clean it up, but Jane did not want to. She let out a big sigh. One day, Jane's mom said, "No more playing until your room is clean." Jane was sad, but she knew she had to clean her room. She went upstairs and saw her filthy room. There were toys and clothes everywhere. Jane started to pick up her toys and put them away. As Jane cleaned, she found a toy she had lost a long time ago. She was so happy! Jane realized that cleaning her room was not so bad after all. When she was done, her mom was proud of her. Now, Jane could play with her toys in a clean room.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside in the mud. One day, she played so much that her feet got very dirty. Sue knew it was important to have clean feet, so she went inside her house to clean them. Sue filled a big bowl with warm water and put her dirty feet in it. She used soap and a soft cloth to clean her feet. The water turned brown from all the mud! Sue was happy to see her feet becoming clean again. After her feet were clean, Sue put on her favorite socks and shoes. She felt good knowing her feet were clean and healthy. Sue promised herself that she would always remember how important it was to clean her feet after playing outside. And from that day on, she always did.
