Title: Tim the Boy and the Game

Once upon a time, in a small, tidy room, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to think about new games to play with his friends. One day, Tim thought of a great game that he knew would be a big success. Tim called all his friends to come over and play his new game. They all gathered in his tidy room and listened as Tim explained the rules. They were all very excited to play, and they knew that it would be a lot of fun. As they played the game, everyone had a great time. They laughed and smiled, and Tim's game was a big success. In the end, they all helped Tim clean up and keep his room tidy. And from that day on, Tim and his friends played his game every chance they could.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird. The bird liked to fly high in the sky. One day, the bird saw a lot of fog. The bird could not see very well. The bird was scared to fly in the fog. A small bug saw the bird. The bug wanted to help the bird. The bug said, "I will encourage you to fly in the fog." The bird felt happy. The bird and the bug became good friends. The bird and the bug flew together in the fog. They were having fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The fog went away very fast. The bird could see the sky again. The bird was not scared anymore. The bird and the bug were very happy. They played together all day long.

Once there was a little girl who loved to look up at the sky. Every day she would notice sunshine and beautiful clouds. She would also make up stories about what the clouds looked like. Today the little girl noticed that the sky was grey and dark. She started to worry that it would rain and she wouldn't be able to play outside. But then she noticed a beautiful white cloud in the far corner of the sky. She smiled and said, "I'll make that my special cloud!" The little girl wanted to make sure the cloud stayed special, so she decided to clean it. She grabbed a cloth and a bucket of soapy water and set off to clean the cloud. She climbed up a ladder, reaching the cloud with a sponge and the soapy water. As she started cleaning the cloud, she noticed that each stroke of the sponge made the cloud a little more beautiful and bright. When she was finished cleaning, she stepped back and marveled at her work - the cloud was sparkling white! She then settled herself into the grass and looked up at her beautiful cloud and sighed with delight.

Lily was curious about the big red truck that stopped near her house. She saw some men in yellow clothes and helmets get out of the truck. They were firemen. They had a long hose and a ladder. They ran to the house next door, where smoke was coming out of the window. Lily wanted to see what was happening, so she followed them. She gazed at the firemen as they sprayed water on the fire. She heard them shout and saw them break the door. She wondered if they were saving someone inside. "Hey, little girl, you shouldn't be here. It's dangerous," one of the firemen said to her. He picked her up and carried her back to her house. He knocked on the door and gave her to her mom. "Thank you, fireman. I'm sorry, she was just curious," her mom said. "It's okay, ma'am. Just keep her away from the fire. We'll take care of it," the fireman said. He smiled at Lily and gave her a sticker with a fireman badge on it. Lily smiled back and said, "Thank you, fireman. You are brave and kind. I want to be a fireman when I grow up."

Molly was so excited! Today she was getting ready to go on a big trip. She ran around her bedroom, collecting her things and packing them into her suitcase. Molly's mom came into the room and said, “I think you’re all set! Is there anything else you want to bring?” Molly smiled and nodded, “Yes! I want to bring peace!” Molly's mom smiled too, “I think that’s a great idea. You can bring a deep peace with you wherever you go.” Molly nodded, feeling the deep peace inside her heart. She finished packing her bags and soon she was ready to go. Molly was so excited to start her big trip and to bring peace with her wherever she went.
