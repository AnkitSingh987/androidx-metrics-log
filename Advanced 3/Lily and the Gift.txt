Title: Lily and the Gift

One day, a little girl named Lily was very excited. It was her birthday and she knew she would get a gift. She jumped up and down, waiting for her mom and dad to give her the gift. Mom and Dad came into the room with a big box. Lily opened it and saw a bike! It was a beautiful bike, but it had a flat tire. Lily was sad. But Dad said, "Don't worry, we will repair it!" Dad fixed the tire and Lily was happy again. She rode her bike all day, laughing and smiling. The gift made her very excited and she loved her new bike.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Emma. Emma loved fruit very much. She loved apples, bananas, and grapes. She thought that fruit was attractive because they were colorful and yummy. One day, Emma went to the market with her mom. She saw a big basket of fruit. She wanted some, so she said to her mom, "Mom, can I request some fruit, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Emma. You can have some fruit, but you must share it with your friends." Emma was very happy. She took the fruit to her friends and they all enjoyed it together. They had a fun time eating the fruit and playing games. Emma learned that sharing is important and makes everyone happy. The moral of the story is to always share with others, because it brings happiness to everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a big palace. In the palace lived a king and a queen. They had a little dog named Spot. Spot was very fast. One day, Spot was playing outside. He was running fast and chasing a ball. The king and the queen were watching him. Suddenly, Spot disappeared behind a big tree. The king said, "Oh no! Where did Spot go?" The queen said, "Let's find him fast!" They looked for Spot near the tree. Then, they saw him. Spot was hiding and wagging his tail. They all laughed and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl who wanted to explore the forest. She entered the long forest, but soon she got lost. It was getting dark, and the girl was so scared. Suddenly, she heard a voice behind her. "Hi! What are you doing here?" the voice asked. The girl spun around to see a tall man. She didn’t answer him. "You seem lost. Maybe I can help?" the man said. The girl still didn’t answer, so the man grabbed her arm. The man took the girl deeper into the forest, until they reached a cabin. He pushed her inside, and locked the door. The girl was never seen again.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Dave and Wendy. Dave was a jolly boy and Wendy was a jolly girl. One day, Dave and Wendy decided to play a game. Wendy said, "We'll both draw on a piece of paper, but you have to draw a circle and I have to draw a line. Whoever's drawing meets first, wins!" Dave and Wendy both agreed, took a piece of paper and drew their mark. Dave was very excited and started to draw quickly. Wendy was very patient and was taking her time. Suddenly, Dave's mark met Wendy's mark. Dave screamed out of excitement, "I won! I won!" Wendy, who was a bit sad, replied, "That's not fair. You got to the mark first because you drew faster". Dave gave Wendy a hug with a big smile and said, "You can play again, and I'll draw slower". The two jolly friends continued to draw, having lots of fun. When they were done, they hugged and said goodbye with big smiles.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was a very independent girl. She liked to do things all by herself. One day, she went for a walk in the woods. While walking, Sue saw a big skull. The skull was very old and had been there for a long time. Sue was not scared, but she was very curious about the skull. As Sue got closer to the skull, she stumbled on a rock. She fell down but did not cry. Sue got up, dusted herself off, and continued her walk. She was a brave and independent little girl.
