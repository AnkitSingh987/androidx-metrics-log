Title: Lily and Tom and the Bear

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to play in the woods. They had a big basket of food for a picnic. They walked and walked until they found a nice spot under a tree. They spread a blanket and opened the basket. "Look, Tom, I have a charming bear for you!" Lily said, holding a stuffed toy. "He is soft and brown and has a red bow." "Thank you, Lily, he is very nice!" Tom said, hugging the bear. "I have a surprise for you too!" He reached into the basket and pulled out a book. "It is a story about a girl and a bear. Do you want to hear it?" "Yes, please!" Lily said, sitting next to Tom. They opened the book and looked at the pictures. Tom began to read in a low voice. "Once upon a time, there was a girl who lived in a cottage with her grandma. One day, she went to visit her grandma, who was sick. She wore a red cloak and carried a basket of food. But on the way, she met a big, bad bear who wanted to eat her. He asked her where she was going, and she told him. He said he would go ahead and wait for her at her grandma's house. He ran and ran, and got there before her. He knocked on the door and pretended to be her grandma. He said, 'Come in, my dear, I have been waiting for you.' The girl opened the door and saw the bear in the bed. He had put on her grandma's nightgown and cap. He said, 'What a charming girl you are! Come closer, so I can see you better.' The girl was scared, but she tried to be polite. She said, 'What big ears you have, grandma!' The bear said, 'The better to hear you with, my dear.' She said, 'What big eyes you have, grandma!' The bear said, 'The better to see you with, my dear.' She said, 'What big teeth you have, grandma!' The bear said, 'The better to eat you with, my dear!' And he jumped out of the bed and tried to catch her. But the girl was quick and smart. She ran to the door and shouted, 'Help, help, there is a bear in the house!' A hunter who was passing by heard her shout and came to the rescue. He shot the bear with his gun and saved the girl. He took her to her real grandma, who was very happy to see her. They had a nice picnic and thanked the hunter for his bravery. And they all lived happily ever after." Lily and Tom clapped their hands and smiled. They liked the story very much. They ate their food and played with their bear. They had a wonderful day in the woods. They did not know that a real bear was watching them from behind a bush. He was hungry and curious. He waited until they left, and then he came out and sniffed the basket. He found some crumbs and a piece of cake. He ate them and licked his lips. He thought they were very tasty. He wished he had met the twins before the hunter. He thought they would have been charming too.

One day, a little otter was swimming in a lake. She was very alert and always aware of what was happening around her. She knew there were creatures in the lake that she should resist. Suddenly, she heard a loud splash. It was coming from the other side of the lake. The otter knew this could be trouble. She quickly swam to the other side, being very alert so she would not get into any danger. When she arrived, she saw a big fish swimming around. The otter knew she had to resist the fish. She stayed still and waited. The fish did not know the otter was there. He kept swimming around, not noticing her. The otter was happy she was able to resist the fish. She stayed still until the fish swam away. Then the otter happily swam off.

Once upon a time, there was a kind king who lived in a big castle. The king was very busy every day, doing lots of work to make his people happy. He cared for them and made sure they had food and a place to live. One day, the king found a big, beautiful apple in his garden. He wanted to share the apple with his people. So, he took a knife and split the apple into many small pieces. He gave one piece to each person in his kingdom. The people were very happy and thanked the king. They learned that sharing with others can make everyone happy. The moral of the story is that it is good to share with others, even if you have a little, because it can bring happiness to everyone.
