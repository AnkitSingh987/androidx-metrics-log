Title: Mary the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mary. Mary liked to play in the park. Mary was playing on the swings one day when she noticed a man trying to find something. She went over to him and asked what he was looking for. The man said he had lost something very important. Mary asked if she could help, and he said yes. So they started searching together. Finally, they spotted the lost object. It was a wallet! The man thanked Mary and promised to reward her. As they walked back, the man reminded Mary to be honest. He said that honesty was like a special order that could never be lost. Mary learned a new valuable lesson that day: always be honest in everything you do!

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to play with his red ball. One day, Max saw a big box. He thought it would be fun to fill the box with balls. Max asked his friend, a little cat named Sam, "Can you help me fill the box with balls?" Sam said, "Yes, I will help you!" They both started to fill the box with balls. They filled it up to the tip. The box was so full, it looked like it would fall. Max and Sam played with the balls in the box. They had so much fun! But then, the box tipped over. All the balls rolled out. Max and Sam laughed and laughed. They knew they would have to fill the box again. But they didn't mind, because they had fun playing together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gifted cat named Tom. Tom loved to catch things, like balls and mice. One day, Tom saw a big pizza on a table. It looked so good, and Tom wanted to try it. Tom had a plan. He would jump up and catch the pizza with his paws. He tried and tried, but he couldn't reach it. Tom felt sad. Then, he saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was nice and wanted to help Tom. Tim picked up the pizza and gave it to Tom. Tom was so happy to have the pizza. He shared it with Tim. They both ate the pizza and had a fun time. The moral of the story is that when we help each other, we can all be happy.

Once upon a time in a small town, there was a boy named Tom. He had an ancient spade that he found in his grandpa's shed. Tom liked to dig in the dirt with his spade. One sunny day, Tom saw a big hill of dirt. He wanted to dig a hole with his spade. As Tom dug, he found a small box. It was very unexpected! Tom was excited to see what was inside the box. When Tom opened the box, he found a toy soldier. The toy soldier could talk! The soldier said, "I surrender!" Tom was very surprised. He and the toy soldier became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to write on a big paper. She looked for a good place to write. She saw a carpet and thought it was a good place. Sue did not know that her brother, Tom, was hiding under the carpet. He wanted to play a game with her. Sue started to write on the paper. She did not see that the pen was making marks on the carpet too. Tom felt the pen on the carpet and thought it was a bug. He jumped out from under the carpet and said, "You have a stupid bug on the carpet!" Sue was very surprised to see Tom. She did not know that he was under the carpet. They both looked at the carpet and saw the marks from the pen. They laughed and said it was a funny game. Now, they always remember to check under the carpet before they write.

Once upon a time, there was a family who loved to roll down the hiills. They were graceful as they rolled and had so much fun. One day a new family moved in with them and they decided to invite them to join them. However, the new family refused to join them and said their own family was too big for rolling. The old family was sad about this and so kept on rolling by themselves. Then, one day, the new family came back and wanted to join in the fun. The old family was happy about this and so allowed them to join them. They all had a wonderful time rolling down the hill and everyone felt happy afterwards. This teaches us an important lesson - that it is important to always be kind and gracious, even to strangers. Everyone should be welcomed with a spacious heart and an open mind. This is the best way to have fun and family together.
