Title: Tom and the Motorcycle

Once upon a time, there was a small, warm town. In this town, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy motorcycle. He loved his motorcycle very much. Every day, he would play with it and make it go fast. One day, while Tom was playing with his motorcycle, it started to grow. The motorcycle got bigger and bigger. Tom was very surprised. He did not know that his toy could increase in size like that. Now, his toy motorcycle was as big as a real motorcycle! Tom was so happy to have a big motorcycle. He thought he could ride it now. But when he tried to ride it, the motorcycle turned back into a small toy. Tom was sad, but he still loved his toy motorcycle. He knew that even if it was small, it was still special.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a graceful bunny named Lily. She loved to hop and play all day long. One day, she saw a shiny thing on the ground. It was a license. Lily did not know what it was, but she thought it was very pretty. Lily wanted to show her friends, so she decided to borrow the license for a while. She hopped to her friend Bear's house. When she showed Bear the license, he looked very surprised. "This is my license!" he said. "I have been looking for it everywhere!" Lily felt bad for taking the license without asking. She said sorry to Bear and gave it back. Bear was so happy to have his license back that he gave Lily a big hug. From that day on, Lily learned to always ask before borrowing things, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a soft leaf. The soft leaf was green and liked to dance in the wind. The leaf had many friends in the tree, like birds and bugs. One day, a little girl came to the tree. She saw the soft leaf and asked a question. "Why do you dance, leaf?" The soft leaf smiled and said, "I dance because it makes me happy." The little girl liked the soft leaf. She would come to the tree every day and ask more questions. The soft leaf and the little girl became good friends. They were happy and loved to dance together.

Once upon a time, a family was going on a trip. They needed to pack their bags. Mom, Dad, and their little girl, Lily, all had things to put in the bags. They put in clothes, toys, and food. As they were packing, Lily saw a big black bag. She asked, "What is in the big black bag, Mom?" Mom said, "That is a surprise for our trip, Lily." Lily was very excited about the surprise. On the trip, the family was having fun. They played games and ate snacks. Then, they opened the big black bag. Inside the bag was a new puppy! Lily was so happy and said, "I love our new puppy! Thank you, Mom and Dad!" The family had a great trip with their new puppy.

Once there was a little girl. She was very modest and she loved to play in the kitchen. One day while she was playing, she decided to cook a new dish. She got all the ingredients and started to mix them together. Suddenly, she started to sneeze. She sneezed three times in a row. Her mum heard her sneezing and came running. “What’s the matter dear? Are you ok?” her mum asked with concern. The little girl felt embarrassed. “I sneezed three times”, she said shyly. “Ah, that’s alright sweetheart. Let’s finish this special dish you’re making”, her mum said with a smile. The little girl and her mum finished the dish and waited for it to be cooked. The dish was ready soon and it smelled delicious. The little girl felt so proud of her modest creation and couldn’t wait to taste it. And that’s how the little girl’s first dish was made - all thanks to her sneezes.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was frightened of the dark. One night, he heard a noise outside his room. He slowly opened the door to see what it was. He saw a toy gun on the floor. Tim picked up the toy gun and said, "I am not frightened anymore." He walked around the house with the toy gun, feeling brave. He checked every room to make sure there were no monsters hiding. His mom saw him and asked, "What are you doing, Tim?" Tim replied, "I am not frightened of the dark anymore. I have my toy gun to protect me." His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim. Now, let's go to bed." Tim went to bed feeling safe and happy with his toy gun by his side.
