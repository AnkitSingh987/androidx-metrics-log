Title: The Incredible Elephant and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was an incredible elephant. The elephant was big and strong. One day, the elephant met a little mouse. The mouse was sad and started to weep. "Why are you weeping?" the elephant asked the mouse. The mouse said, "I lost my way home, and I'm very scared." The elephant wanted to help the mouse find its home. They walked and walked, but they could not find the mouse's home. The sky got dark, and it was time for the elephant to go home. The elephant said, "I'm sorry, little mouse. I must go home now." The mouse was still sad and wept more. The elephant went home, and the mouse stayed lost. The story has a bad ending because the mouse never found its home.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big sack in his yard. He did not know what was inside, so he opened it. Inside the sack, there were many cool, shiny stones. Tim was very happy to find the stones. Tim wanted to share the stones with his friends. He ran to his friend Sue's house. "Sue, look what I found!" Tim said. Sue looked at the cool stones and smiled. "Wow, those are pretty!" she said. Tim and Sue decided to scatter the stones all over the yard to make it look nice. They threw the stones here and there. When they were done, the yard looked very pretty with the cool, shiny stones scattered all around. Tim and Sue were very happy with their work.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird named Peanut. Peanut loved to sing all day. One day, Peanut met a big blue bird. The blue bird said, "Peanut, your singing is too loud. Please quit." Peanut felt sad and stopped singing. He flew away to find a quiet place. As he flew, he saw a big tree with many red apples. Peanut loved apples. He landed on a branch and picked an apple to eat. Just then, a little green bird flew to Peanut. The green bird said, "Peanut, I heard you stopped singing. I love your singing. Please sing with me." Peanut was very happy. He began to sing with the green bird. They sang and ate apples together, and they became best friends. From that day on, Peanut never quit singing again.

Once upon a time, there was a little baby. Her name was Crystal. Crystal was very excited because today was her first day of kindergarten! She was going to make lots of new friends. She walked through the school door and was in awe. Everything was so beautiful! The floor was polished and the walls were painted in a lovely pink color. She saw plenty of pictures hung on the wall of animals, smiling children and sunny days. "Can you see the lovely pictures on the wall, Crystal?" asked her teacher, Ms. Winnie. Crystal nodded and smiled. "Did you mention the bird in the corner of the room?" Ms. Winnie asked excitedly. "'It's so lovely!" Crystal walked closer and noticed a beautiful bird with bright feathers sitting on a branch in the corner of the room. "Yes, it is so lovely!" replied Crystal, happily. Ms. Winnie smiled at Crystal. "Yes, it is. Now let's go meet your new classmates!" Crystal smiled and felt so happy, she was going to have lots of fun in her new kindergarten. She felt so much love in the room. Her new adventure was beginning!

One day, a little boy named Tim found a package. Tim was confused. He did not know what was in the package. He asked his mom, "Mom, can I open the package?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. You can open the package." Tim opened the package and found a toy car inside. He was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Mom!" Tim wanted to use the toy car. He put the car on the floor and pushed it. The car went fast. Tim laughed and played with the car all day. He was not confused anymore.

One day, a pretty girl named Lily went to play in a big maze. She wanted to find the way out. As she walked, she saw a little boy. The boy had a frown on his face. "Hi, I am Lily. Why do you frown?" asked Lily. The little boy said, "I am lost. I can't find my way out of the maze." Lily smiled and said, "Let's find the way out together." Lily and the boy walked through the maze. They turned left and right. After a while, they found the way out. The boy's frown turned into a big smile. They both laughed and played outside the maze. And they became good friends.
