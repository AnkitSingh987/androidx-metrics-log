Title: Tom the Boy and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside all day. He liked the sun, the trees, and the soft grass. He had many friends who liked to play with him too. One day, the sun was very hot and the wind was harsh. Tom and his friends wanted to gain more fun, so they decided to wait until the evening to play. They knew the sun would be less hot and the wind would be less harsh then. In the evening, Tom and his friends went outside to play. The sun was not too hot anymore, and the wind was not too harsh. They played games, laughed, and had a lot of fun. Tom was very happy that they waited for the evening to play, and they all had a great time together.

Once upon a time, there was a big turkey. The turkey had a terrible problem. It could not find any food to eat. The turkey was very sad and hungry. One day, the turkey met a kind squirrel. The squirrel had a big nut. The squirrel said, "Let's split the nut and share it!" The turkey was very happy. They used a big rock to split the nut into two parts. After they ate the nut, the turkey felt much better. The terrible problem was gone. The turkey and the squirrel became good friends. They played and shared food every day. They were very happy.

Once upon a time there were two twin sisters who lived in an ancient forest. The name of the first sister was Mia and the second sister was called Ella. One day Mia and Ella wanted to explore the forest and see what it included. "Let's go and see what the forest includes!" Mia said to Ella excitedly. So the sisters started their adventure and walked further into the forest. As they walked, Mia and Ella saw butterflies, birds, squirrels, and all sorts of other animals. They even saw an ancient tree that was so tall, it almost touched the sky! Ella pointed at the majestic tree and said, "Wow, that tree is so ancient!" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, let's explore more of the forest to see what else we can find." So, the twin sisters continued on their forest adventure and the forest included surprises around every corner.

One day, a boy and his dog went to the beach. The boy had a red pail and a yellow shovel. The dog had a smelly, wet nose. They wanted to play in the sand and the water. The boy started to dig a hole. He wanted to bury something in the sand. The dog found a big stick and brought it to the boy. The boy put the stick in the hole and covered it with sand. They were both very happy. Suddenly, a big wave came and washed the stick out of the sand. The boy and the dog were surprised. They thought the stick was gone forever. But then, the wave brought back a shiny, new toy car. The boy and the dog played with the car all day long. They had so much fun at the beach.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim went to a big stadium with his mom. The stadium was very big and had deep steps. Tim was very excited to see a game there. "Mom, I can't wait to see the game!" said Tim. His mom smiled and said, "Me too, Tim. But don't forget to hold my hand so we don't get lost." As they walked to their seats, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and took Tim's hat. Tim was very sad and said, "Oh no, my hat! I love that hat." His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will find it later." After the game, they looked for Tim's hat. They found it near a big tree. But guess what? The big bird had put a shiny star sticker on the hat. Tim was so happy, he said, "Wow, the bird made my hat even better!" And from that day on, Tim never forgot his special day at the stadium.
