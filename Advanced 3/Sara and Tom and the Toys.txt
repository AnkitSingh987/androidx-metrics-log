Title: Sara and Tom and the Toys

Sara and Tom are twins. They like to play with toys and books. But they do not like to share. They often fight over their things. One day, Sara finds a telephone in the closet. It is big and red and has buttons. Sara thinks it is fun to press the buttons and hear the sounds. She pretends to talk to her friends. Tom sees Sara with the telephone. He wants to play with it too. He runs to Sara and tries to grab the telephone. Sara holds it tight and says, "No, it is mine! Go away!" Tom gets angry and says, "No, it is mine! You are mean!" He pulls the telephone hard and makes Sara fall. Sara cries and says, "You are bad! I hate you!" Mom hears the noise and comes to the closet. She sees Sara and Tom fighting over the telephone. She is not happy. She takes the telephone away and says, "Stop it, both of you! This is not a toy. This is a real telephone. You could break it or call someone by mistake. You have to share and be nice to each other. Do you understand?" Sara and Tom nod and say, "Yes, Mom." They are sorry for fighting. They hug and say, "I love you." Mom smiles and says, "I love you too. Now, go and play with something else. And be careful with the telephone." She puts the telephone back in the closet and locks the door. Sara and Tom go and find some other toys and books. They play together and do not fight. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a cop named Ben. Ben was a very nice cop. He liked to help people and keep them safe. One day, it was very hot outside. Ben was walking down the street when he saw something strange. Ben found a magic bottle. He opened the bottle and a little man came out. The little man said, "I can make things shrink!" Ben thought that was very cool. He asked the little man to show him how it worked. The little man pointed at a big ball. He said some magic words and the ball started to shrink! It got smaller and smaller until it was tiny. Ben was so happy. He thanked the little man and they played with the tiny ball all day.

One day, a pale little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a sad tree on the earth. The tree was not happy because it was thirsty. Lily wanted to help the tree. Lily said, "Tree, why are you sad?" The tree said, "I am thirsty, and I need water." Lily thought she could help the tree by giving it water. Lily went to get a big cup of water. She brought it back to the tree and poured the water on the earth near the tree. The tree drank the water and started to feel better. The tree was happy, and it said, "Thank you, Lily, for your help."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, while playing, he found something disgusting. It was a slimy, smelly worm. Tim didn't like the worm, but he knew it lived underground. Tim had an idea. He would put the worm back underground where it belonged. So, he dug a hole and put the worm in it. Then, he covered the hole with dirt. Tim was happy that the worm was back where it should be. But, something unexpected happened. The next day, Tim went outside to play and saw the hole he dug was now filled with beautiful flowers. The disgusting worm had turned into a lovely surprise. Tim learned that sometimes, even things that seem yucky can bring happiness.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty vase in a big house. The vase had many beautiful flowers in it. The flowers smelled nice, but the water in the vase was sour. The people in the house did not like the sour smell. One day, a small boy named Tim saw the vase. Tim wanted to rescue the flowers from the sour water. He asked his mommy for help. Mommy said, "Yes, we can rescue the flowers together." Tim and his mommy took the flowers out of the vase. They threw away the sour water and put clean water in the vase. Then, they put the flowers back in the vase. The house smelled nice again. Tim was happy that he could rescue the flowers.
