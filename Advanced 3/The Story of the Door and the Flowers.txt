Title: The Story of the Door and the Flowers

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, modern house. In this house, lived a happy family. The family loved to decorate their home with pretty things. They had a big sign on the door that said "Welcome". One day, the family decided to make new signs to decorate their house. They used many colors and drew animals, flowers, and stars. The little girl in the family made a sign with a big, red heart on it. The family put up their new signs all around the house. The house looked even more beautiful with the colorful signs. The neighbors saw the house and smiled. The family was very happy with their pretty, modern home.

Once upon a time, there was a slow turtle named Tim. Tim had a little brain, but he was very kind. He liked to help his friends in the forest. One day, Tim met a sad rabbit named Rob. "Why are you sad, Rob?" asked Tim. Rob said, "I lost my carrot. I don't know where it is." Tim wanted to help Rob, so he said, "Let's look for your carrot together." They looked and looked, but they could not find the carrot. Then, Tim had an idea. "I will replace your lost carrot with a new one," he said. Rob was happy and thanked Tim. They went to find a new carrot together. In the end, Tim and Rob found a big, tasty carrot. They shared the carrot and became best friends. The moral of the story is: when you help others, you make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. The ball loved to roll all day long. It would roll up the hill and down the hill. One sunny day, the ball was rolling very fast down the hill. It was feeling very happy and alert. As the ball rolled down, it saw a big bowl of soup on a table. The soup was hot and smelled yummy. The ball was hungry and wanted to eat the soup. But the ball didn't know how to eat soup! It only knew how to roll. Just then, a kind bird saw the ball and the soup. The bird had an idea. The bird picked up the ball and dropped it into the soup. Splash! The ball was now in the soup! The ball felt warm and happy in the soup. And that is how the ball learned to eat soup.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat. The cat liked to play with a tool. The tool was a ball. The cat played with the ball all day. One day, the cat wanted to improve. The cat wanted to jump high and run fast. The cat thought, "If I am not fat, I can jump and run better." So, the cat played with the ball more and more. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball got bigger and bigger. The cat was surprised. The cat played with the big ball, and the cat got thin. The cat could jump high and run fast. The cat was happy. The moral of the story is: play and have fun to improve and be happy.

Anna was a little girl who liked to ask many questions. She wanted to learn about everything in the world. She asked her mom, her dad, her teacher, and her friends. But sometimes they did not know the answers, or they were too busy, or they did not like her questions. One day, Anna found a big book in the library. It had a picture of an owl on the cover. She opened the book and saw many words and pictures inside. She could not read all the words, but she liked the pictures. She saw animals, plants, stars, and people. She wondered what the book was about. She took the book to the librarian and asked, "What is this book?" The librarian smiled and said, "This is a book of wisdom. It tells you many things about the world and how to live well. But it is not easy to understand. You have to test yourself and think hard to learn from it." Anna was curious and excited. She said, "Can I borrow this book?" The librarian nodded and said, "Yes, you can. But be careful. This book is very old and fat. It is heavy and fragile. You have to treat it with respect and care." Anna thanked the librarian and carried the book to her home. She was eager to test herself and learn from the book of wisdom. She did not know that the book had a secret. The owl on the cover was not just a picture. It was a magic owl that could talk and watch. And it was waiting for the right person to open the book and listen to its voice.
