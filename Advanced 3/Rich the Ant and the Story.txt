Title: Rich the Ant and the Story

Once upon a time, there was an ant named Rich. Rich could not resist the smell of a delicious pie baking in the oven. One day he made a decision to enter the oven and have a taste of the pie. He was sure that he would be able to skilfully approach the pie without getting hurt. Rich crawled inside the oven and sure enough the pie was very delicious. It was so delicious that Rich decided to stay in the oven a bit longer without realizing that the oven was still on! Suddenly the oven started to heat up and Rich was in serious danger. He tried to crawl out of the oven, but it was too late. He got burned and died miserable death. The moral of this story is that it is important to resist temptations as they can lead to serious consequences.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to spin around in circles. One day, Max found a big barrel in the park. He barked at it and said, "Why are you here, barrel?" The barrel was very sad and felt miserable. It said, "No one wants to play with me. I am too big and round." Max wanted to help the barrel. He had an idea! Max said, "I know! We can spin together and have fun!" Max and the barrel started to spin around and around. They spun so fast, they felt like they were flying! The barrel was not miserable anymore. It was very happy. From that day on, Max and the barrel were the best of friends. They played and spun together every day. They showed everyone that no matter how big or small, everyone can have fun and be happy.

One day, a boy named Tom went to play outside. The ground was wet from the rain that fell. He saw a big puddle and jumped in it. Splash! The water went all around. Tom laughed and jumped again. Tom saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad. She did not have a toy to play with. Tom wanted to help Sue. He said, "I will supply you a toy, Sue." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom." Tom went to his house and got a ball. He gave the ball to Sue. They played with the ball on the wet ground. They had a lot of fun. Now, Sue was happy. Tom and Sue became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a quiet town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to laugh and play with his friends. They took turns on the slide and swings at the park. Tim was a happy boy, and he always shared his toys with his friends. One day, Tim found a big red ball in his yard. He was so excited and wanted to show his friends. But this time, he did not want to share. He thought the ball was too special. So, he played alone, while his friends watched. After a while, Tim's friends went away to play together. Tim felt sad and lonely. Then, something unexpected happened. The big red ball popped! Tim realized that playing alone was not fun. He learned that it was better to share and have fun with his friends. From that day on, Tim always shared his toys and took turns with his friends. And they all laughed and played happily together.

One day, Larry the Lion went for a walk in the jungle. He was feeling very lonely. Suddenly, he heard some rustling in the trees. He stopped and looked up. There he saw a little bird trying to attach to a branch. 'What are you doing up there?' asked Larry. The little bird smiled and replied, 'I am trying to fly away to the nearest land. It's very far away and I am feeling too lonely here. Can you help me? Larry chuckled and said 'Of course I can! I will help you attach to the branch so that you can fly away'. The little bird thanked Larry as she flew away over the horizon. Larry smiled. He was no longer lonely as he continued his walk through the jungle.
