Title: The Story of the Tree and the Cop

One day, a cop was walking down the street. He was a bit careless and did not see the big hole in the ground. He fell in and got stuck. He tried to get out but he could not. He started to yell for help. A little boy named Tim heard the cop. He ran to the hole and saw the cop inside. "Don't worry, cop! I will help you," Tim said. The cop was happy that Tim was there to help him. Tim thought of a plan to get the cop out of the hole. Tim went to a nearby store and ordered a long, strong rope. He tied one end of the rope to a tree and threw the other end into the hole. The cop grabbed the rope and Tim pulled him out. The cop was very thankful to Tim for saving him. From that day on, the cop was never careless again.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to clean her soft fur. Every day, she would sit under a big tree and clean herself. One day, while Fluffy was cleaning, a little boy named Tim walked by. He saw Fluffy and wanted to be friends. Tim said, "Hi, cat! What is your name?" Fluffy looked up and said, "My name is Fluffy. Nice to meet you, Tim!" Tim and Fluffy played together every day. They had fun running, jumping, and laughing. Fluffy was always clean and happy. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom had a big axe. He used his axe to cut wood for the village. One day, Tom found a magic hat. When he put the hat on his head, it made him change. Tom was not bald anymore! He had long, pretty hair. He was very happy. Tom showed his new hair to the people in the village. They were all very surprised. They liked Tom's magic hat. Tom and his big axe were now famous in the village. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely machine. It lived in a big room with no friends. The machine was sad because it wanted to play and have fun with others. One day, a nice man came to the room. He saw the lonely machine and had an idea. He brought a big box with many balls inside. The man told the machine, "You can fill yourself with these balls and share them with others. Then you will have friends and won't be lonely anymore." The machine filled itself with the balls and went outside. It found some kids playing in the park. The machine shared the balls with the kids, and they all played together. The lonely machine was not lonely anymore. It was happy because it had friends and could have fun with them.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sally. She was wearing a pink bathrobe on a bright and sunny day. She was running and dancing in the garden when it started to rain. Sally didn't want to get wet so she ran quickly inside. Once inside she realized how wet she was. She took off her bathrobe and hung it up on a chair. She tried her best to get it dry but it just kept getting wetter and wetter. Suddenly, her mom came in and saw Sally standing in her little pink underwear. Her mom smiled and said, "Sally, why don't you put on your bathrobe? That will help to keep you warm and dry." Sally nodded her head, put on her bathrobe and ran off to her room. She realized how much better she felt when she was wearing the bathrobe. She was warm and cozy, and the rain couldn't touch her.

One day, a little boy named Tom went for a walk in the wood. He wanted to meet new friends. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tom was very happy. In the wood, Tom saw a big tree. The tree had a lot of expensive-looking toys on it. Tom wanted to play with them, but he knew he should not take things that were not his. He felt sad. Just then, a friendly squirrel came by. The squirrel saw that Tom was sad and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tom told the squirrel about the expensive toys. The squirrel said, "Let's play together instead!" So, Tom and the squirrel played all day in the wood. Tom was happy again because he had met a new friend.
