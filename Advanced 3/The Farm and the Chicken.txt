Title: The Farm and the Chicken

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On this farm, there were many animals and a big field of corn. The animals loved to eat the corn, but the farmer did not like it when they did. One day, a little chicken saw the open gate to the corn field. The chicken was very hungry, so it went inside to eat some corn. The farmer saw the chicken eating the corn and was not happy. The farmer decided to punish the chicken. He said, "You cannot eat the corn anymore." The chicken was sad, but it learned its lesson. From then on, the chicken and the other animals did not eat the corn without the farmer's permission.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit. The rabbit liked to skip. He skipped all day long. One day, the rabbit went to a big forest. The forest was a fun place to skip. In the forest, the rabbit met a big, scary bear. The bear was not nice. The rabbit was scared, but he wanted to be brave. The rabbit thought, "I will not let the scary bear stop me from skipping." So, the rabbit skipped up to the scary bear. He said, "Hello, bear! I am a rabbit who likes to skip. Can we be friends?" The bear looked at the rabbit and smiled. The bear was not scary anymore. They became friends and skipped in the forest together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Daisy. Daisy was a fragile cow, not very big and strong like the other cows. She lived on a farm with many animals, like pigs, chickens, and horses. One day, Daisy saw two birds in a quarrel. They were fighting over a shiny thing on the ground. Daisy wanted to help them, but she didn't know what to do. She thought and thought, and then she had an idea. Daisy walked over to the birds and said, "Please don't quarrel. You can both share the shiny thing." The birds looked at Daisy and then at each other. They decided to stop fighting and share the shiny thing. Daisy was happy that she could help, and the birds were happy too. From that day on, Daisy and the birds were the best of friends.

One day, a young boy named Tim went with his mom to see the big train. The train was very long and made a loud sound. Tim was very excited to see the train. He held his mom's hand tight as they walked closer to the train. "Mom, can we ride the train?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can ride the train today." Tim was very happy. They bought tickets and waited for the train to stop. When the train stopped, Tim and his mom got on. They found seats and sat down. The train started to move. It went faster and faster. Tim looked out the window and saw the sun rise. It was a big, bright ball in the sky. Tim and his mom had a fun day riding the train.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable tutor named Tom. Tom was very good at helping kids learn. He was always there when they needed him. Tom loved to turn the pages of books and teach new things. One day, Tom went to the park to meet a new kid. He was excited to help them learn. But when he got there, he saw a big surprise. Instead of a kid, there was a little green frog waiting for him. The frog looked up at Tom and said, "I need your help to learn too!" Tom was shocked but decided to help the frog. They sat on a rock and Tom began to teach the frog. The frog was very smart and learned quickly. As they turned the pages of the book, Tom realized that the frog was magical. The frog thanked Tom for being a dependable tutor and turned him into a magical tutor. From that day on, Tom could help any kid or animal learn and everyone lived happily ever after.
