Title: Kani the Girl and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Kani. Kani had a friend named Tim. One sunny day, Kani and Tim were playing together. Kani asked Tim, “Where are we going today?” Tim replied, “Let’s go on an adventure!” Kani was so excited. She asked, “What do we need?” Tim smiled and said, “We need honest fuel to remember.” So, Kani and Tim went to the store and bought a can of honest fuel. Then, they jumped on their bike and took off on an adventure. They rode through the forest and through fields of bright yellow flowers. Along the way, they stopped to remember their adventure. Every time they remembered where they had been, a new story was born. Kani and Tim had so much fun that day. They created new memories and never forgot how much fun they had.

Three friends, Jake, Sam and Suzy, were playing together in the park. They were arguing and quarrelling over who should go on the pink slide first. Nobody wanted to give in and the three of them got heated. Suddenly, Jake had an idea! He said that the three of them should race and whoever reached the slide first would get the pink slide! Sam and Suzy agreed, cheering for the challenge. All three of them ran as fast as they could but after a few seconds it was clear that Suzy was going to win. She smiled happily and jumped onto the pink slide with a whoop of joy. The victory felt sweet to Suzy and she knew it wasn't just because she was the fastest. She realised that it also felt good to come to an amicable agreement like this so her and her friends could play together. From that day onwards, the three friends always tried to use fun ways to resolve their quarrels. Pink slides made their disagreements a little bit sweeter.

Once upon a time, there was an owl. The owl was very smart. It knew many things. The owl liked to fly high in the sky. When it flew, it would soar up, up, up. One day, the owl saw a little mouse. The mouse was lost and sad. The intelligent owl wanted to help the mouse. So, the owl soared down to the ground. The owl talked to the mouse. It said, "Don't be sad. I will help you find your home." The owl and the mouse became friends. They played and laughed together. The owl soared high and found the mouse's home. They were both very happy.

Once upon a time there was a famous girl called Lucy. She was really happy and had lots of friends. One day, Lucy and her friends were playing in the park. She found a sparkly rock and wanted to take it home. But when she tried to pick it up, it wouldn't move. So Lucy started crying. "What's wrong?" her friends asked her. Lucy said, "I want the sparkly rock but it won't come off the ground. I'm sad." Her friends were very sad to see Lucy so unhappy. So they suggested that they use a stick to help her get it. They all grabbed sticks and tried to free the rock from the ground. After many tries, they finally managed to do it! Lucy was so happy and thanked her friends. She put the sparkly rock on the floor of her bedroom so she could look at it every day. And that is why Lucy was so famous for finding the sparkly rock.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her adorable dog, Max. They liked to play with a big ball. Lucy saw a big pile of junk in the park. She thought it was funny and told her mom, "Look at all that junk!" Her mom said, "Be careful, Lucy. You don't know what's in there." But Lucy didn't listen. She wanted to play near the junk. She kicked the ball, and it went right into the pile of junk. Max ran after the ball and started to dig in the junk. Suddenly, a small kitten jumped out of the junk pile! Lucy was so surprised. She felt a little embarrass because she didn't listen to her mom. The kitten was scared, but Max was nice and helped the kitten. Lucy said, "I'm sorry, little kitten. I didn't know you were in the junk." Lucy's mom smiled and said, "It's okay, Lucy. Now you know to be careful around junk." They took the kitten home and named her Daisy. Lucy, Max, and Daisy became best friends, and they all played together every day.
