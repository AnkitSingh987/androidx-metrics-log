Title: The Tall Tower and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a tall tower. A gentle, little girl named Mia lived near the tower. One day, Mia saw a tiny cat stuck on the top of the tower. The cat was scared and wanted to come down. Mia had a magic wand that could make things shrink. She said, "I will use my wand to help the cat." She waved her wand at the tower and said, "Shrink, tower, shrink!" The tower started to get smaller and smaller. As the tower became small, the cat came down safely. The cat said, "Thank you, Mia, for helping me!" Mia was happy that she could help her new friend. They played together and had lots of fun. The end.

Lily likes to have models of animals. She has a model of a lion, a model of a giraffe, and a model of a monkey. She puts them on her bed and plays with them every day. One day, she hears a knock on the door. She runs to open it and sees her friend Tom. Tom has a big box in his hands. He says, "Hi, Lily. I have a surprise for you. Do you want to see?" Lily nods and smiles. She invites Tom to come in and sit on her bed. She moves her models to make room for him. Tom opens the box and shows her what is inside. It is a model of an elephant. It is gray and has a long trunk and big ears. It is bigger than all of Lily's models. Lily gasps and claps her hands. She says, "Wow, Tom. It is so nice. Thank you. Where did you get it?" Tom says, "I got it from my grandma. She went to a far place and saw real elephants. She brought this model for me. But I know you love animals, so I want to give it to you. You can have it." Lily hugs Tom and says, "You are a good friend, Tom. I am very happy. Can we play with it?" Tom nods and says, "Yes, we can. Let's make a zoo with all your models. We can pretend they are alive and make noises." Lily and Tom have fun playing with the models. They make the elephant trumpet, the lion roar, the giraffe munch, and the monkey chatter. They are very alert and listen to each other's sounds. They laugh and enjoy their time together.

There was a smart lion. He had a piano in his house. Every day he would sit and play the piano, while roaring out loud. Other animals in the jungle heard him play and thought he was great, so they started roaring with him. One day, a hunter heard the roaring, and he came closer to investigate. He saw the lion playing his piano and decided to take him away. The lion was too scared to roar anymore. The moral of this story is that it is important to be careful and not get too loud. Even though the lion was smart and had a great talent, it was not smart enough to be quiet and he ended up in danger.

One day, a little cat named Kitty felt different. She did not want to play with her toys. She just wanted to sleep. Kitty yawned and stretched her body. She looked out the window and saw her friend, Doggy, playing outside. Kitty went outside and saw Doggy jumping and running around. Doggy saw Kitty and said, "Hi Kitty! Do you want to play?" Kitty yawned again and said, "I am too sleepy to play. I feel different today." Doggy thought for a moment and said, "Kitty, try to move like me! Maybe it will make you feel better." Kitty watched Doggy's motion and tried to jump and run like him. After a while, Kitty started to feel better. She was not sleepy anymore. Kitty and Doggy played together all day. They ran, jumped, and chased each other. By the end of the day, Kitty was very happy she tried the different motion. She learned that trying new things can make you feel better. As the sun went down, Kitty and Doggy said goodbye. They were both tired from playing all day. Kitty yawned and went inside her house to sleep. She knew she would have sweet dreams about her fun day with Doggy.
