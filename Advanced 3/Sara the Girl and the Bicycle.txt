Title: Sara the Girl and the Bicycle

One day, a little girl named Sara saw a bicycle. It was red and shiny. She wanted to learn to ride it. But she was scared. She asked her big brother, Tom, for help. Tom said, "Don't worry, Sara. I will help you learn to ride the bicycle. First, put on your thick helmet to keep your head safe." Sara put on her thick helmet. She sat on the bicycle and held the handlebars tight. Tom held the bicycle and walked beside her. Sara started to pedal. The bicycle moved slowly. "Look, Sara! You are learning to ride the bicycle!" Tom said with a big smile. Sara smiled too. She felt happy and proud. She kept pedaling, and Tom let go of the bicycle. Sara was riding the bicycle all by herself! She yelled, "I did it, Tom! I learned to ride the bicycle!" Tom clapped and cheered for her. They both had a fun day riding bicycles together.

Once upon a time there was a small boy. He was so excited. He wanted to explore what was in the square. It looked empty and he wanted to know why. So the little boy got closer and closer to the square. He used his fingers to poke the edges of the square. But inside the square was something unexpected. Suddenly, the little boy felt a sharp pain in his finger. It was so sharp he began to cry. When he looked at his finger, he saw that there was a bee on it. The bee had stung him. Soon the pain spread all over his arm and he was in agony. He was so sad, he had to go home. The little boy learnt a very important lesson that day. Never poke something that looks empty - you never know what comes next!

Once upon a time, there was a smart prince who lived in a big castle. He liked to play with his toys and run in the garden. One day, he saw a new friend in the garden. The friend was a little bird with colorful feathers. "Hi, bird!" said the prince. The bird stared at him with its small eyes. The prince stared back, and they both smiled. They played together and had lots of fun. The smart prince and the bird became best friends. They played in the garden every day. The prince was very happy to have a new friend who loved to stare and play with him. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he found a big chest in his yard. He was very happy and wanted to open it. Tim tried to open the chest, but it was hard. He felt a little bitter because he could not open it. He stumbled and fell, but he did not give up. He tried again and again, and finally, the chest opened. Inside the chest, Tim found many toys and games. He was very happy and shared them with his friends. The moral of the story is to never give up and always share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious restaurant. It was a big place with many yummy foods. The people who went there always felt happy. One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the mysterious restaurant with her mommy and daddy. They all felt very hungry. They sat at a big table and looked at the menu. Lucy saw a picture of a yummy cake and wanted to eat it. Lucy, her mommy, and her daddy ate their food and felt very full. Then, they ate the yummy cake. They all felt very happy. The mysterious restaurant was a fun place for Lucy and her family to eat.

Jill and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. But they also made a big mess. There were toys, books, and dust everywhere. Mom came in and saw the mess. She was not happy. She told Jill and Ben to clean up their toys and dust the furniture. She said they had to do it before dinner or they would not get any dessert. Jill and Ben did not want to clean up. They wanted to keep playing. They thought of a plan. They found a magic wand in their toy box. They waved it at their toys and said, "Shrink, shrink, shrink!" They hoped their toys would become so small that Mom would not see them. But something went wrong. The magic wand did not shrink their toys. It shrunk Jill and Ben! They became as small as ants. They looked around and saw their toys and dust were still big. They were scared. They did not know how to get big again. They heard Mom coming back. They tried to call her, but she did not hear them. They saw her feet walking towards them. They ran and hid under the sofa. They hoped Mom would find the magic wand and make them big again. They wished they had listened to Mom and cleaned up their mess.
