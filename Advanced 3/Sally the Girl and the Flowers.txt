Title: Sally the Girl and the Flowers

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the park. She saw a row of amazing, colorful flowers. Sally wanted to get a closer look at the flowers. As Sally walked closer, she stumbled on a rock. "Oh no!" she said. A friendly squirrel saw her fall and came to help her. "Are you okay?" asked the squirrel. "Yes, thank you," Sally replied. She stood up and they looked at the flowers together. The squirrel told Sally about all the different flowers in the row. They had a fun time and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Tim found a spear in the woods. It was a big stick with a pointy end. Tim thought it was very cool. Tim showed the spear to his friends. They all thought it was cool too. They decided to play a game with the spear. They took turns throwing it at a tree to see who could make it stick. While they were playing, a big wind came. It blew the spear high into the sky. Tim and his friends watched as the spear went up and up. Then, it seemed to vanish! They could not see it anymore. Tim and his friends were sad that the spear was gone. But they knew they could find more fun things to play with. They went back to playing and having fun, even though the spear had vanished.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Mandy. She used to help her mom in the kitchen a lot. Her mom was always very pleased with her help. One day, Mandy was in the kitchen, stirring something in a pot. Her mom said, "Mandy, can you smell something?" Mandy sniffed the air and could smell something delicious. "Yes, Mom, it smells like beef," she said. Mandy's mom said, "Yes, it's beef. We are going to serve it for dinner tonight. Can you help me put it on the table?" Mandy was so excited to help. She quickly set the table and put the beef in the middle. Her mom was so proud of her. She said with a smile, "Mandy you are so clever. Thank you for helping me serve dinner to everyone."

One day, a little alligator was very excited. He wanted to play with his friends. But he was not like other alligators. He had a magic power. He could change into other animals. The excited alligator went to the park. He saw his friend, the bunny. The alligator changed into a bunny too. They hopped and played together. They were very happy. Later, the alligator changed back. He said goodbye to the bunny. He went home to his mommy and daddy. He told them all about his fun day. They were happy he had a good time.

John was in trouble. He had lost his home in a big flood. All around him he could see destruction and there were lots of people who had also lost their homes. He had nothing except his family. He felt as if he didn't belong. John and his family had to move to a new place. It was not very nice or even comfortable. It was a modest shack where they all had to live. John felt so unhappy, nothing felt like it belonged to him. One day, Brady, a kind neighbour, invited John over. Brady had a beautiful house, it had plenty of room for John and his family. John asked Brady if they could stay but Brady said no. He said 'I'm sorry, John, but you don't belong here'. John felt so sad. He had lost his home, he didn't belong anywhere. He was so unhappy, he was all alone in the world.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the museum with his mom. They saw many things. Tim liked the big dinosaur bones. He felt rich seeing all the cool things in the museum. Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was skipping near the dinosaur bones. Tim wanted to skip too. He asked his mom, "Can I skip like Sue?" His mom said, "Yes, you can skip, but be careful." Tim was happy. He started to skip with Sue. They laughed and had fun. In the end, Tim and Sue became friends. They played and skipped all day at the museum. Tim's mom was happy that Tim had a fun day. They went home with a big smile.
