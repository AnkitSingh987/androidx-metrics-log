Title: The Great Volcano and the Backwards

Once upon a time there was a great volcano. It stood tall and proud and nobody knew what was inside. One day, something amazing happened. The volcano began to reverse! It went backwards and backwards until it was gone. It was so cool! Everyone cheered and clapped at the great volcano's disappearing act. Nobody knew how or why the volcano reversed, but it was the talk of the town. Everyone wanted to know what happened and why the volcano went away so fast. Little by little, people began to piece together the clues. They figured out the volcano reversed because of the big wind that blew that day. The wind was so strong it caused the volcano to move backwards until it disappeared! The people celebrated. They were so excited that their great volcano was gone! From then on, they called that day the day the volcano reversed.

Once upon a time, there was a pink brick. The pink brick had a friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to talk to the pink brick every day. They were very good friends. One day, Sue took the pink brick to the park to play. They talked and laughed as they played on the swings and the slide. Then, Sue and the pink brick saw a big dog. The big dog wanted to play too. Sue threw the pink brick for the big dog to catch. The big dog ran very fast and jumped high to catch the pink brick. But the big dog was too strong. The pink brick broke into many pieces. Sue was very sad. She could not talk to the pink brick anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play near the road. One day, a big, fierce cat came by. The cat was not nice to Spot. Spot did not like the cat. Spot said to the cat, "Go away! I don't want to play with you!" But the cat did not listen. The cat stayed near the road and made loud noises. Spot could hear the cat from far away. A nice girl named Lily saw Spot and the cat. She came to help. Lily said to the cat, "You must leave Spot alone!" The cat listened to Lily and went away. Spot was happy and thanked Lily. From that day, Spot and Lily became best friends and played together near the road.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobby. Bobby lived near an oasis, a place with water and trees. Bobby loved the clear water at the oasis. He would drink from it every day. One day, Bobby saw a hungry baby bird near the oasis. The baby bird looked sad and weak. Bobby wanted to help the baby bird. He decided to feed the baby bird some food. Bobby flew to the baby bird and said, "Don't worry, I will feed you." Bobby found some yummy worms and fed them to the baby bird. The baby bird ate the worms and felt much better. After eating, the baby bird said, "Thank you, Bobby, for feeding me. You are a good friend." Bobby and the baby bird became best friends, and they played together at the oasis every day. They were happy and loved their life near the clear water.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy duck. The duck liked to dress up in pretty clothes. One day, the duck went to the park to play. At the park, the duck met a frog. The frog said, "You look silly in those clothes!" The duck felt sad, but said, "I like to dress up." The next day, the duck went back to the park. The frog was there too. The frog wore a big hat. The duck was surprised. The frog said, "I was wrong. Dressing up is fun!" They played together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock in a big garden. The rock was very happy because it had many friends. One day, a pretty little daisy came to the garden. The daisy wanted to find a good spot to settle and grow. "Hello, smooth rock!" said the daisy. "Can I settle next to you and be your friend?" The smooth rock was very happy and said, "Yes, little daisy! I would love to be your friend." The daisy settled next to the smooth rock, and they became best friends. They always helped each other. The smooth rock gave the daisy shade when it was too hot, and the daisy made the rock look even prettier with her bright colors. One day, a big wind came and tried to blow the daisy away. But the smooth rock held the daisy tight and did not let her go. The daisy was very thankful to her friend, the smooth rock. The moral of the story is that friends should always help each other and be there for one another.
