Title: Max the Mule and the Box

One day, a mule named Max was walking down the road. Max was a fearful mule and was scared of many things. He was always careful not to get hurt or step on anything bad. Max had a friend named Sam, who was a small bug. Sam liked to pinch Max every now and then, just for fun. As Max and Sam were walking, they saw a big box in the middle of the road. Max was scared, but Sam said, "Do not be scared, Max. Let's go see what is inside the box." Max was still scared, but he trusted his friend Sam. So, they went closer to the box. When they were near the box, Sam pinched Max to make him feel brave. Max took a deep breath and opened the box. Inside the box, they found lots of colorful balloons! Max and Sam were so happy and started to play with the balloons. They learned that sometimes, when you face your fears, you can find something fun and unexpected.

One day, a bald man went for a walk. He saw a banana on the ground. The banana was yellow and yummy. The bald man picked up the banana and smiled. As he walked, he saw a little girl. The girl was sad. The bald man wanted to help her. He thought that maybe the banana would make her happy. So, he passed the banana to the girl. The girl took the banana and smiled. She was not sad anymore. The bald man was happy too. They both walked together, eating the banana and laughing. They had a fun day.

Once upon a time there was a little bird. He wanted to go up high but there was a problem. He couldn't reach the ladder. He was so shy and didn't know what to do. Suddenly, a monkey appeared. "Let me help you!" the monkey said and grabbed the ladder. He handed it to the little bird. The little bird was so excited. He flew up the ladder quickly! He was high in the sky! The little bird turned to the monkey and said, "Thank you!" The monkey smiled, "You're welcome!" The little bird enjoyed his time high in the sky and he thanked the monkey again and again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to bake cookies with her mom. One day, while they were baking, a thief came into their house. The thief was not nice, and he wanted to take their cookies. Lily was very scared, but she was also intelligent. She whispered to her mom, "Let's hide the cookies, so the thief can't find them." Her mom nodded, and they quickly hid the cookies under the table. The thief looked around, trying to find the cookies. He couldn't see them, so he asked Lily, "Where are the cookies?" Lily didn't want to tell him, but the thief was angry. He grabbed the cookies from under the table and ran away. Lily and her mom were very sad. They had worked hard to bake the cookies, but now they were gone. Even though Lily was intelligent, she could not stop the thief from taking their cookies. The town learned about the thief, and everyone was more careful, but Lily and her mom never saw their cookies again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She had a warm yellow mitten on one hand. The mitten was so comforting and cozy. One day, the girl went outside to play in the snow. She wanted to make a snowman, so she started to twist the snow together. As she twisted, all of a sudden, her mitten got stuck! The girl tried to untwist her mitten from the snow, but she couldn't. She tugged and yanked and pulled, but the mitten wouldn't come off. She was getting uncomfortable. She started to cry, she was so sad. Then a friendly snowman came to her rescue! He used his big round hands to untwist her mitten from the snow and soon she was free again. The girl thanked the snowman for helping her, and she hugged the mitten, feeling so happy and relieved.
