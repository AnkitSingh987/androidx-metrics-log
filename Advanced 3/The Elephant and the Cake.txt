Title: The Elephant and the Cake

One day, a big elephant went to the park. He saw a small boy eating a yummy cake. The elephant wanted some cake too. He went to the boy and said, "Give me cake!" The boy was scared and gave the elephant his cake. The elephant took a big bite of the cake. But the cake tasted disgusting! The elephant did not like it. He made a funny face and spit out the cake. The boy laughed at the elephant. The elephant felt bad because he demanded the cake from the boy. Then, the boy had an idea. He gave the elephant a banana. The elephant took a bite and it was delicious! The elephant and the boy became friends. They played together in the park all day. The elephant learned not to demand things from others and to be kind.

Once there was a little girl named Jane. She was very excited because it was her first day of preschool. She put on her warm coat and then went with her mom to the school. When she got there, she was a little bit scared. She felt embarrassed because she didn't know anyone. She wanted to hide, so she just sat in the corner. Jane was feeling very shy, so she stayed in the corner and didn't talk to anyone. She was embarrassed and didn't want to make any friends. Then suddenly, she heard her name. One of the teachers was looking for her. She had a kind smile on her face. Jane got up from her seat, felt a little bit less embarrassed, and went to join the other kids. At the end of the day, she was happy and having a lot of fun. She hugged the teacher goodbye and made sure to give her warm coat a big hug before going home.

One day, a boy named Tim found a pair of sunglasses in the park. He put them on and felt very cool. He walked around showing off his new sunglasses to everyone. But then, he saw another boy, Max, who looked sad. "Hey Max, why do you feel sad?" asked Tim. "I lost my sunglasses," Max said, crying. Tim felt ashamed because he knew the sunglasses he found belonged to Max. But he didn't want to give them back, so he said nothing. Later, Tim and Max played near a big tree. Tim's sunglasses fell off his face and broke. Now, Tim felt very sad, too. He wished he had given the sunglasses back to Max when he had the chance.

It was a sunny day and Steve and his Dad were in the stadium. They were there to watch a football match. Steve was excited. Dad: "Steve, can you sort out the healthy snacks for the match?" Steve looked around and found some apples, carrots and biscuits. He sorted them into a pile on the stadium floor. Dad: "Looks good Steve, you are very organised! Now let's watch the match". Steve and his Dad sat down to watch the match. As the match went on, Steve munched on his healthy snacks and cheered for the team. The match ended with a win for the home team, and Steve and his Dad were smiling. The day was even better with some healthy snacks!

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Pat and Sam. They were very excited as they made a big promise to each other to have a messy day. Their plan included going to the beach, making a big sandcastle and having lots of top-notch fun. They even had a big picnic with colourful foods! When they arrived at the beach, it was so messy! There was sand everywhere and, in no time, their clothing and faces were completely covered in it. That did not stop them though! They ran around and made the biggest sandcastle ever, filled with decorations and flags. They even made a promise to return to the beach the next day. It ended up being a magical day, full of laughs and memories. They included lots of fun and even more promises.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Bulb. Bulb was not a normal friend. Bulb was a talking light bulb! Tim and Bulb would talk and play all day. One day, Tim and Bulb went to the park. They saw a big tree with yummy red apples. Tim and Bulb wanted to eat the apples. They tried to get the apples, but the tree was too tall. So, they asked a tall man to help them. The tall man got the apples for Tim and Bulb. Tim and Bulb said "thank you" to the tall man. They sat under the tree and ate the yummy apples. They talked and laughed all day. Tim and Bulb were very happy. They knew they would be best friends forever.
