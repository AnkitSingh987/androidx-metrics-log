Title: The Boy and Girl and the Boat

One day, a boy and a girl went to the shore. They wanted to play in the sand and water. The sun was high in the sky, and it was a warm day. The boy said, "I want to own a big boat one day." The girl agreed, "Yes, that would be fun! We could sail far away." As they played, they found a big shell. They picked it up, and a little crab came out! The crab said, "I can help you own a boat if you let me go." The boy and girl were surprised but said, "Okay, little crab." They let the crab go, and it ran into the water. Soon, a big boat came to the shore. The boy and girl were so happy! They now owned a boat and could sail far away, just like they wanted.

Once upon a time there was a wise old cherry tree. It increased a lot every year and grew very tall. One day a bird flew by with a cherry in its beak. It dropped the cherry on the ground and it rolled right next to the wise old cherry tree. The tree looked down at the cherry with its branches and smiled. The tree was so wise that it knew what to do. It started to make the cherry grow. It sent out its roots, which got bigger and bigger, until they touched the little cherry. Then the cherry began to increase. It grew bigger and bigger, until it was big enough to pick! The little bird flew back happily with a cherry from the wise old cherry tree, and the tree was happy too. The tree knew that wisdom can help you increase the joy in your life.

Jimmy wanted to see what was living in the hive near his house. So he carefully snuck up to the hive and peeked inside. It was buzzing with a ton of bees! They seemed friendly, so Jimmy decided to look around the bee hive more. The bee hive was big and the bees were so small. Jimmy decided to tell his parents about the hive. He told them he could see the buzzing bees and that he thought the hive was a reliable home for them. His parents were impressed and decided to keep an eye on the hive. Jimmy was very proud and he peeked into the hive almost every day. But he never disturbed the bees, knowing that their home was reliable.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue went to school. In her classroom, there was a big blackboard. Sue loved to draw on the blackboard with her friends. One day, Sue saw a different color chalk. She was very excited to use it. Sue asked her friend Tom, "Can I use the different color chalk?" Tom said, "I don't know, maybe we should ask the teacher first." But Sue was too excited to wait. She took the chalk and started to draw on the blackboard. Sue drew a big sun with the different color chalk. It was very pretty. But then, the teacher came back into the classroom. The teacher looked at the blackboard and saw the big sun. She was not happy. The teacher said, "Sue, you should not have used the different color chalk without asking me first." Sue felt very sad because she knew she did something wrong. The teacher told Sue she could not play with her friends at recess. Sue learned that she should always ask before using something new.

One day, a lazy cat named Olive was playing outside. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Olive tried and tried, but she was too lazy to go up. Then, Olive saw a spray bottle near the tree. She had an idea. Olive picked up the spray bottle and began to spray the tree. As she sprayed, the tree started to grow bigger and bigger. Olive was so happy. Now, she could climb the big tree easily. But something unexpected happened. The tree was so big that it touched the sky! Olive got scared and decided not to climb the tree. She learned that being lazy can sometimes lead to big problems.
