Title: Daisy the Gentle Horse and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a gentle horse named Daisy. Daisy lived on a farm with many animals. She had a friend named Ben, who was a little boy. One day, Ben found a whip on the farm. He didn't know what it was, so he asked Daisy, "What is this?" Daisy said, "That is a whip. It can hurt, so please be careful." Ben wanted to play with the whip, but he didn't want to hurt Daisy or any of the other animals. So, he covered the whip with a soft cloth. Now, it was safe to play with. Ben and Daisy had fun playing together, and no one got hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to study. She liked to look at books and learn new things. Sue was very adventurous. She liked to go outside and play with her friends. One day, Sue found a big box of salt. She did not know what to do with it. She thought, "Maybe I can use this salt in a fun way!" Sue took the salt and went to the park. She saw her friends and said, "Let's play with this salt!" Sue and her friends played with the salt. They made shapes and drew pictures on the ground. But soon, it started to rain. The rain washed away all the salt pictures. Sue and her friends were sad. They thought their fun was over. But then, Sue had an idea. She said, "Let's study the rain and see what happens to the salt!" They watched as the rain made the salt disappear. Sue and her friends learned that rain can wash away things like salt. The moral of the story is that even when something unexpected happens, you can still learn from it and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big truck. The truck had to load many things. It went to a house to load some boxes. The truck was very strong and could carry a lot. In the house, there was a family. The family was having dinner. They had yummy food on the table. They had soup, bread, and fruit. They all sat together and enjoyed their dinner. After dinner, it was dark outside. The moon and stars were in the sky. The family went to bed and slept well. The big truck finished loading the boxes and drove away. The family and the truck were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Max. Max always seemed to trip and fall over his own feet. But he was a happy dog and loved to play with his friends. One day, Max and his friends had a race. Max wanted to win, but he was scared because he was clumsy. His friends cheered for him and said, "You can do it, Max!" Max felt brave and started to run as fast as he could. Max ran and ran, but he tripped and fell many times. But he did not give up. He got up and kept running. At the end, Max won the race! His friends cheered and clapped for him. Max felt so happy and proud. It was a big victory for Max, and he learned that even a clumsy dog can win if they try their best.
