Title: Lily the Girl and the Dog

One day, a girl named Lily went to mail a letter. She put the letter in a big blue box. It was a dark night, and she could not see very well. Lily was a little scared, but she knew she had to mail the letter. As she walked home, she saw a bright light. It was an angel! The angel said, "Hi Lily, do not be scared. I am here to help you." Lily was happy to see the angel and asked, "Can you help me get home? It is very dark." The angel smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you." But then, something unexpected happened. The angel turned into a big, friendly dog! The dog said, "I am still here to help you, Lily." They walked home together, and Lily was not scared anymore. The dog stayed with Lily and her family, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old cat named Mr. Whiskers. He lived in a small house near a big tree. Mr. Whiskers had a toy sword made of wood. He liked to play with it every day. One sunny day, Mr. Whiskers met a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy saw the sword and wanted to try it too. Chirpy said, "Mr. Whiskers, can I try your sword, please?" Mr. Whiskers said, "Yes, Chirpy, but be careful. It's not a real sword, just a toy." Chirpy took the sword and tried to fly with it. But the sword was too big for Chirpy. Mr. Whiskers saw Chirpy was having a hard time. He said, "Chirpy, let me help you." Mr. Whiskers held the sword while Chirpy flew around it. They played together all day and had lots of fun. When the sun went down, Chirpy said, "Thank you, Mr. Whiskers, for letting me try your sword. You are a wise and kind friend." Mr. Whiskers smiled and said, "You're welcome, Chirpy. I'm happy we could play together." From that day on, Mr. Whiskers and Chirpy became best friends. They played with the sword and had many fun adventures together. They learned that sharing and helping each other made their days more fun and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a funny volcano. The funny volcano could talk and laugh. One day, a little boy named Tim came to visit the funny volcano. "Hello, funny volcano!" said Tim. "Can I drink some water from the river near you?" The funny volcano laughed and said, "Yes, Tim, you can drink the water, but be careful not to drink too much!" Tim was having fun, but he did not listen to the funny volcano's warning. He drank and drank and drank from the river. The funny volcano started to look worried. As Tim drank more and more water, he began to feel sick. The funny volcano said, "I told you not to drink too much, Tim!" But it was too late. Tim felt very sick, and he could not play with the funny volcano anymore. The funny volcano was sad, and Tim learned a lesson about listening to his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Tim. Tim loved to jump high. He would jump all day long. He liked to jump over rocks, logs, and even his friends. One day, Tim saw a pretty coat hanging on a tree. The coat was red and blue. Tim wanted the coat, but it was too high for him to reach. He tried to jump, but he could not jump high enough. Tim asked his friend, the big bird, for help. The big bird flew up high and got the coat for Tim. Tim was so happy. He put on the coat and showed it to all his friends. They all clapped and cheered as Tim jumped high in his new coat.

Lily liked to play with her doll, Anna. Anna had a pretty dress made of velvet. It was soft and smooth and shiny. Lily liked to touch it and pretend Anna was a princess. One day, Lily saw a pair of scissors on the table. They were shiny too, and had sharp edges. Lily wanted to make Anna's dress more pretty. She took the scissors and cut some shapes out of the velvet. She made a heart, a star, and a flower. "Look, Anna, you have a new dress!" Lily said. "You are the most pretty princess ever!" But then, Lily's mom came into the room. She saw the scissors and the velvet pieces on the floor. She saw Anna's dress with holes in it. She was very angry. "Lily, what have you done?" she shouted. "You ruined your doll's dress! And you could have hurt yourself with the scissors! They are very sharp and dangerous! You are a very naughty girl!" She took the scissors and the doll away from Lily. She said Lily had to go to her room and stay there. She said Lily could not play with Anna anymore. Lily cried and cried. She was very sorry. She loved Anna and did not want to hurt her. She did not mean to be naughty. She just wanted to make Anna more pretty. But now she had no doll, no scissors, and no velvet. She had nothing. She felt very sad and lonely. She wished she had never seen the scissors. She wished she had left Anna's dress ordinary.
