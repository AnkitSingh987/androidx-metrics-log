Title: Sally the Cheerful Girl and the Ball

One day, a cheerful girl named Sally went to the museum with her mom. They saw big dinosaurs, pretty rocks, and old things. Sally liked the museum a lot. She had a red ball that she took everywhere. She liked to bounce it and catch it. In the museum, Sally saw a big dinosaur bone. She wanted to play with her ball near it. Her mom said, "Be careful, Sally!" But when Sally threw the ball, it hit the dinosaur bone and it started to fall. Sally was scared. Just then, a nice museum man caught the bone before it could drop. He smiled at Sally and said, "You found a secret door!" The dinosaur bone was really a handle to open a hidden room. Inside, there were more fun things for kids to play with. Sally and her mom had a great day at the museum.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to play in the water with his friends. They would splash and have fun all day long. One sunny day, Ducky saw a little fish who looked sad. Ducky asked the fish, "Why are you sad?" The little fish just shrugged because he didn't know. Ducky wanted to help the little fish feel better. Ducky thought and thought. Then, he had an idea! He asked the little fish to play with him in the water. The little fish smiled and they played together all day. From that day on, Ducky and the little fish were the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing with her toys. She saw her mom come in with a bottle of shampoo. "Lucy, you need to take a bath," her mom said. Lucy felt frightened because she didn't like baths. But her mom told her not to worry. In the bath, Lucy played with her rubber duck. She started to feel less frightened. Her mom put shampoo in her hair and washed it. Lucy saw that the shampoo made bubbles, and she started to laugh. After the bath, Lucy was happy and clean. She wasn't frightened of the shampoo anymore. She told her mom, "Next time, I won't be scared of the bath." Her mom smiled and hugged her. From that day on, Lucy loved taking baths and playing with the bubbles.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a very intelligent boy named Tim. Tim loved to learn new things and play with his friends. One day, Tim found a toy pistol in his backyard. He was very excited and wanted to show his friends. Tim went to his friend, Sam, and said, "Look what I found! It's a toy pistol!" Sam was also excited to see the toy pistol. They decided to play a game. They would raise their hands and pretend to shoot the toy pistol in the air. As they played, other kids in the village saw them and wanted to join. Soon, all the children were playing together, raising their hands and pretending to shoot the toy pistol. Everyone was laughing and having fun. At the end of the day, Tim and Sam were very happy. They had made new friends and had a great time playing with the toy pistol. They learned that sharing and playing together can bring joy to everyone. And Tim's intelligent mind thought of more fun games they could play with their friends in the future.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Tom. Tom had many things but he was not happy. One day, he went for a walk and saw a rag on the ground. He picked it up and said, "What a dirty rag!" As Tom walked, he met a girl named Sue. Sue was not rich but she was happy. Tom asked, "Why are you happy?" Sue said, "I am happy because I have my family and friends. I don't need many things." Tom thought about what Sue said. He wanted to be happy too. So, Tom gave away all his things to people who needed them. But he still felt sad. Tom did not know that being happy takes time and love, not just giving things away. The bad ending is that Tom never found happiness. The moral value is that happiness comes from love and friendship, not just from having many things.
