Title: Amy the Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to help her mom with the laundry. One day, Amy's mom asked her to repeat what she said. "Take the wet clothes outside and hang them up to dry," Amy's mom said. Amy repeated, "Take wet clothes outside and hang up." Amy took the wet laundry and went outside. It was a sunny day. She hung up the wet clothes on the line. The sun was shining, and the clothes would be dry soon. But, Amy saw dark clouds coming. She thought it might rain soon. Amy ran back inside to tell her mom about the clouds. But her mom was busy and did not listen. The rain came and the clothes got wet again. Amy felt sad because she tried to help, but the clothes were still wet. The laundry had to be done all over again.

One day, a little duck named Ducky wanted to bathe in the warm pond. He loved to play with his soap in the water. He liked how the soap made bubbles. Ducky asked his friend, Bunny, to come and play too. "Come play with me, Bunny! We can bathe and use my soap!" said Ducky. But Bunny did not want to go in the water. Bunny was scared because he could not swim. "I can't swim, Ducky! I don't want to go in the water," said Bunny. Ducky felt sad that his friend was scared. He wanted Bunny to have fun too. Ducky had an idea. He found a big, flat rock for Bunny to sit on. The rock was in the water, but not too deep. Bunny could sit on the rock and play with the soap, but not be scared. Bunny was happy and they both played in the warm pond, making bubbles with the soap.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved peanuts. One day, Sue found a big peanut. She was so happy! She wanted to show her friend Tom. Sue went to Tom's house and said, "Tom, look at my big peanut!" Tom looked at the peanut and said, "Oh no, Sue, that is a terrible peanut!" Sue was sad. She didn't know why Tom said her peanut was terrible. Just then, the big peanut opened up. Inside, there was a tiny, friendly squirrel! The squirrel had been hiding in the peanut. Sue and Tom were so surprised! They laughed and played with the squirrel. From that day on, Sue, Tom, and the squirrel were the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a big nation full of people. Each day they missed something sweet. Everyone wondered what it could be. One day a little girl named Jane had an idea. She said, "Let's bake a cake!" Everyone agreed and together they made a delicious cake with lots of sweet frosting. When the cake was finished, everyone smiled and hugged one another. They were so happy that they could share something so sweet together. Jane said, "We must always remember to never miss the chance to do something nice for each other." Everyone nodded in agreement. Jane and her friends enjoyed the sweet cake, and the nation continued to share sweetness with one another each day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim felt gloomy one day. He did not want to play with his toys. He sat by the window and looked outside. He saw an orange on the table. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, why do I feel gloomy?" His mom smiled and said, "Sometimes, we just feel that way. But playing with friends can help." So, Tim took the orange and went outside. He saw his friend, Lily. Tim wondered if Lily would play with him. He asked, "Lily, will you play with me?" Lily said, "Yes! Let's play catch with the orange." They played catch with the orange. The sun shined, and the birds sang. Tim did not feel gloomy anymore. He felt happy because he had a friend to play with. And from that day on, Tim and Lily played together every day.
