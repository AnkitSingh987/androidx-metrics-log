Title: The Poor Man and the Write

Once upon a time, there was a poor man who wanted to change his life. He thought that if he could write, he would be able to do it. His parents didn't have enough money for him to go to school and learn how to write, so he asked a friend for help. His friend taught him how to write and soon the poor man had a stack of papers with his writing on them. One day, the poor man saw a sign that said he should apply for a job. He was so excited because he knew he could do it, since he could write his name on the application. He sent in his application and was soon given the job. Now the poor man is not poor anymore. He works hard and can take care of himself and his family. Every day, he is thankful he had a friend to teach him how to write his name and make his dreams come true!

One day, a dog named Spot felt lonely. He wanted to go outside and play. Spot saw his leash and had an idea. Spot tried to put the leash on by himself, but it was hard. He pulled and pulled, but the leash would not go on. Spot felt sad and mad. Finally, Spot got the leash on, but it was not right. He went outside to play but got stuck. Spot could not get free, and no one came to help him. He stayed lonely and stuck outside.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Olive. Olive was very impatient. She wanted everything now, now, now! One day, Olive went to the park with her mom. At the park, she saw a big slide. Olive wanted to go down the slide, but she was scared. Olive's mom said, "You can do it, Olive! Just try and you will manage it." Olive took a deep breath and climbed up the steps. She sat at the top of the slide, ready to go down. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew by and dropped a shiny coin right in Olive's lap! Olive was so excited! She forgot about being impatient and scared. She held the coin tight and slid down the slide, laughing all the way. When she reached the bottom, she showed her mom the shiny coin. Olive's mom smiled and said, "See, Olive? When you are patient and brave, good things happen." From that day on, Olive learned to be more patient and brave. She knew that if she could manage the big slide, she could do anything. And she always kept the shiny coin to remind her of the day she learned this important lesson.

One day, a big snake was in the forest. The snake was very serious. He did not like to play. All the animals were scared of him. A little bird saw the snake and said, "Mr. Snake, can you please play with me?" The snake looked at the bird and said, "I don't play. I am very serious." The bird was sad, but he had an idea. He asked the snake, "Can you please help me find my lost toy? I need your help." The snake thought for a moment and said, "Okay, I will help you." The snake and the bird looked for the toy together. They found it under a big tree. The bird was very happy and thanked the snake. The snake smiled and said, "You're welcome." The other animals saw this and were not scared of the snake anymore. The moral of the story is that even serious people can be kind and helpful when asked.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to draw pictures with her crayons. One day, she drew a very pretty picture of her family. Sue was very happy with her picture and wanted to show it to her mommy. Sue went to find her mommy, but she was in a hurry. She didn't see her toy car on the floor. Sue stepped on the toy car and dropped her picture. The picture flew in the air and landed in a big puddle of water. Sue was very upset. Sue's mommy heard her cry and came to see what was wrong. Sue showed her the wet picture and told her what happened. Mommy hugged Sue and said, "It's okay. We can make a new picture together." Sue smiled and felt better. They sat down and drew a new picture, even more beautiful than the first one.
