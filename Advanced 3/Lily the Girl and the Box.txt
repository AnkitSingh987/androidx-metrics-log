Title: Lily the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to use pastels to draw pretty pictures. One day, she found a big box of dry pastels in her room. She was so happy! Lily started to draw a big, bright sun with the yellow pastel. Then, she used the blue pastel to draw the sky. She added green grass and a big red flower with the other pastels. She was having so much fun! When Lily was done, she showed her mom and dad her pretty picture. They loved it and put it on the fridge for everyone to see. Lily felt so proud of her art and she couldn't wait to use her dry pastels again to make more fun pictures.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a little bunny. The bunny liked to play on the grass. One day, the bunny saw a big bird. The bird liked to display its big wings. The little bunny felt frightened by the big bird. The bunny wanted to run away, but its legs were too small. The big bird flew down to the grass where the bunny was playing. The frightened bunny tried to hide, but the big bird found it. The big bird picked up the little bunny with its big wings. The little bunny was very scared and could not escape. The big bird took the bunny away, and they were never seen again.

One day, a boy named Tim had a toy arrow. He liked to play with his arrow a lot. He would throw it in the air and watch it fly. Tim was very happy with his arrow. One day, Tim played too hard with his arrow. The arrow hit a big rock and it broke. Tim was very sad. He looked at his broken arrow and felt frustrated. He didn't know what to do. Tim's mom saw him feeling sad. She came to help him. She took the broken arrow and fixed it with some tape. Tim was happy again. He thanked his mom and played with his arrow, but this time, he was more careful.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a dull flower. This flower felt sad because it could not bloom like the other flowers. The other flowers were bright and pretty, but the dull flower was not. One day, a kind spirit saw the sad, dull flower. The spirit wanted to help the flower bloom and be happy. The spirit talked to the flower and told it to be strong and believe in itself. The dull flower listened to the spirit and started to feel better. It tried very hard to bloom. Slowly, the dull flower began to change. It started to bloom and became a beautiful, bright flower. Now, the flower was happy, all thanks to the kind spirit.

One day, a little bee named Buzz was flying around. Buzz was a good bee. He liked to use his wings to help his friends. Buzz would fly from flower to flower, talking to all the bugs he met. One sunny day, Buzz saw a big red ball. He thought it was a flower! Buzz flew to the ball and tried to talk to it. But the ball did not talk back. Buzz was sad. He did not understand why the ball did not want to be his friend. Then, a little girl named Lily came running to the ball. She picked it up and saw Buzz. Lily said, "Hi, Buzz! This is not a flower, it's my ball!" Buzz was surprised. He did not know that balls could look like flowers. Buzz and Lily became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a large train went on a long trip. Many people and animals were on the train. The train went through big hills and green fields. The sun was shining bright, and everyone was happy. At night, the train stopped to rest. All the people and animals went to sleep. A little girl named Lily had a dream. In her dream, she saw a big, beautiful rainbow. The rainbow made her feel happy and excited. The next day, the train started moving again. As they went through the hills and fields, Lily looked out the window. To her surprise, she saw the big, beautiful rainbow from her dream! Everyone on the train looked and smiled. They all felt happy and excited, just like Lily did in her dream.
