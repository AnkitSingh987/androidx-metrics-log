Title: The Farmer and the Box

Once upon a time there was a farmer who had a big mailbox. He lived in a house surrounded by corn fields. Every day the farmer put letters in his mailbox. One day, the farmer got a big surprise. In the mailbox was a big box full of corn! The farmer quickly opened the box and the corn spilled out all over the ground. It was so messy! He couldn't believe all the corn he had received in the mail. The farmer was very happy. He could make a lot of food with it. The farmer put the corn in his kitchen and he made the most delicious dishes. He shared them with the people in the village and they all enjoyed the meals he made. The farmer was very thankful for the corn he got in the mail!

Once upon a time, there was a big garage. In the garage, there was a fancy car. The car was red and shiny. The car had a big door that could open and close. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to hold the fancy car's door. He asked his mom if he could. His mom said, "No, Tim. You are too little." But Tim did not listen to his mom. When she was not looking, he went to the garage. Tim tried to hold the fancy car's door. But the door was too big for him. It was very heavy. The door fell on Tim's hand. Tim cried out loud. His mom came running to help him. Tim's hand was hurt, and he was very sad. He wished he had listened to his mom.

Once upon a time there was a net. It was perfect, just the right size and shape. One day, the net started to shrink. It got smaller and smaller until it was so tiny it was almost invisible! But then it started to grow bigger and bigger. Until it was even bigger than before. The net was perfect again! All the bugs and animals could jump through it and play. They had so much fun with the perfect net. They ran around and laughed and had a wonderful time. Then it was time to go. The animals and bugs said goodbye to their perfect net and thanked it for such a great game. They waved goodbye and went on their way. The net stayed perfect and just the right size. It waited and watched for the next time the animals and bugs would come to play.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wise old owl named Wisdom. Wisdom was a very successful owl. He had many friends who loved to hear his stories. One day, Wisdom found a big apple on the ground. But the apple had started to rot. Wisdom wanted to teach his friends about the rotting apple. Wisdom called his friends to come and see the apple. He told them how the apple rotted and why it was not good to eat. His friends listened and learned. They were happy to have a wise and successful friend like Wisdom. And they all lived happily ever after.

Molly and her dad drove to the beach. Molly was so excited. She looked out the window and saw the warm ocean and the sandy beach. She could also see some boats. When they arrived, Molly and her dad hopped out of the car. Molly looked around and saw people swimming in the ocean and building sandcastles. Molly and her dad went to a stand that was selling oysters on the beach. Molly looked at them and said, "Yummy!" Dad smiled and said, "Let's get some!" He opened his wallet and paid for two delicious oysters. Molly smiled and said,"Yay!" They sat on the beach and opened the oysters. They were so yummy! They enjoyed the ocean breeze and the yummy oysters. They had a wonderful day at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Daisy. Daisy lived on a big farm with many friends. She liked to play, eat, and rest under the big tree. One day, Daisy felt very uncomfortable. She did not know why. Daisy went to her friend, a bird named Pete. "Pete, I feel bad. What can I do?" Daisy asked. Pete thought for a moment and said, "Daisy, you need to rest. Go to the big tree and rest there." Daisy went to the big tree, but she saw something she did not expect. There was a big hole in the tree! Daisy looked inside and found a little kitten. The kitten was lost and scared. Daisy knew she had to help. So, she let the kitten rest with her under the tree. Soon, Daisy felt better, and the kitten had a new friend.
