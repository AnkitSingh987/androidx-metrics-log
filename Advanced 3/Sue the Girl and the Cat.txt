Title: Sue the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a soft toy cat that she loved very much. One day, Sue and her cat went to the park to play. In the park, they met a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy car with a special power. It could part into two cars. Sue and Tom became friends and they played together. Sue said, "I like your car. It's very cool." Tom said, "Thank you. I like your cat. It's very soft." Sue and Tom played with their toys until the sun went down. They had so much fun together. From that day on, Sue and Tom would meet at the park and play with their toys. They were very happy friends.

Once upon a time, in a small open house, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to decorate her room with pretty things. One day, she found a big blueberry in her garden. She was very happy and wanted to show it to her friend, Tim. Lily went to Tim's house and said, "Tim, look at my big blueberry! I want to decorate my room with it. Can you help me?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Lily! Let's make your room pretty with the big blueberry." So, Lily and Tim went to her open house and put the big blueberry in the middle of her room. They decorated around it with other pretty things. They had a fun day together. When they finished, Lily's room looked very pretty with the big blueberry in the middle. They were both very happy and proud of their work.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to polish her shoes. She would make them shine so bright that you could see your face in them. One day, while she was polishing her shoes, her friend Tom came to visit her. "Hi Lily," said Tom. "What are you doing?" "I am polishing my shoes," Lily replied. "I want them to be very shiny for my sister's marriage." Tom knew that a marriage was a serious event. He wanted to help Lily make her shoes shine. So, he picked up a cloth and started to polish his own shoes too. Together, they polished and polished until their shoes were the shiniest in the town. On the day of the marriage, Lily and Tom wore their shiny shoes. Everyone at the marriage noticed how bright their shoes were. They all wanted to know how they made them so shiny. Lily and Tom just smiled and said, "We polished them together!" And from that day on, Lily and Tom became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty cat named Lily. She liked to play with water. One day, Lily found a spray bottle in the yard. She thought it would be fun to spray water on her friends. Lily went to her friend, Dog, and said, "Hey Dog, look at my new toy!" Dog came closer to see the spray bottle. Lily sprayed water on Dog's face. Dog was surprised and said, "Lily, you are naughty! Don't do that again!" Lily laughed and ran away to find her next friend. She saw Bird sitting on a tree branch. Lily sprayed water on Bird. Bird got wet and flew away, saying, "Lily, you are so naughty!" Then, something unexpected happened. Lily tried to spray water on her next friend, Frog, but the spray bottle was empty. Frog saw the empty bottle and said, "Lily, let me show you something fun!" Frog took Lily to the pond and taught her how to make big splashes with her paws. Lily had so much fun splashing with Frog that she forgot about the spray bottle. From that day on, Lily learned that it's more fun to play nice with her friends than to be naughty. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very brave boy who loved to play outside. One day, Tim was playing in the mud with his toy truck. He got very dirty and knew it was time to take a shower. Tim went inside his house and asked his mommy to help him take a shower. His mommy turned on the water and made sure it was not too hot or too cold. Tim stepped into the shower and started to wash away all the dirt from his fun day of playing. When Tim was all clean, he put on his pajamas and went to his room. He looked at his toy truck and smiled. He knew he had a great day because he was a brave boy who wasn't afraid to get dirty. And he knew that taking a shower would always make him clean again.
