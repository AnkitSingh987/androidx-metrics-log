Title: The Girl Called Bella and the Butterfly

Once there was a girl called Bella. She loved playing in the garden and one day, when she was outside she saw something appear. It was a monster! She was scared and ran away, but the monster kept following her. "Help!" she cried out. Suddenly, someone touched Bella's shoulder. It was Bella's mom. "Don't worry, Bella," mom said. "It's okay, the monster won't hurt you." Bella was still scared, but she was also very curious. She asked mom, "What is the monster?" Mom smiled. "It's called a butterfly," she said. "Can I touch it?" Bella asked, excitedly. Mom nodded and showed Bella how to be gentle and enthusiastic with the butterfly. Bella carefully touched the butterfly and it flew away. Bella was no longer scared. She smiled and ran back outside to play.

Once upon a time, there was a big shark. The shark was very sad and miserable. He swam alone in the deep blue sea. He had no friends to play with. One day, the shark saw a little fish stuck in a net. The fish was scared and crying for help. The shark wanted to save the little fish. He swam fast to the net and used his sharp teeth to break it. The little fish was free and very happy. He thanked the shark for saving him. From that day on, the shark and the little fish were best friends. They played together every day, and the shark was not miserable anymore.

One day, Tom wanted to organize his toys. He had a big grocery bag full of soft teddy bears. He loved them very much. Tom put all the teddy bears in a line on his bed. Tom's mom came into the room. She saw the grocery bag and the teddy bears. She said, "Good job, Tom! You organized your toys so well!" Tom smiled and felt happy. Later, Tom played with his soft teddy bears. He pretended they were at a grocery store. They picked out food and put it in their carts. Tom had a fun day with his organized toys.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to look for bugs and flowers. Lily saw a big spider on a leaf. She was not afraid of spiders. She thought they were graceful and clever. "Look, Tom, a spider!" she said. "It has eight legs and a shiny back. Do you want to see?" Tom did not like spiders. He thought they were scary and icky. He did not want to see the spider. He wanted to make it go away. "No, Lily, I don't want to see!" he said. "Spiders are bad. I will throw a stone at it!" He picked up a small stone and threw it at the spider. But he did not throw it well. He missed the spider and hit Lily's hand. Lily cried out in pain. The spider ran away. "Tom, why did you do that?" she said. "You hurt me and scared the spider. You are mean!" Tom felt sorry. He did not mean to hurt Lily or scare the spider. He just did not like them. "I'm sorry, Lily," he said. "I was afraid of the spider. I did not know how to throw the stone. Can you forgive me?" Lily looked at Tom. She saw that he was sorry. She was still hurt and angry, but she was also kind. She decided to forgive him. "OK, Tom, I forgive you," she said. "But don't throw stones at spiders or me again. They are graceful and I am your friend." Tom nodded. He hugged Lily and said thank you. He learned a lesson that day. He learned to respect spiders and Lily. He also learned to throw better.

Once upon a time there was a nice lady who liked to supply things to people. She had lots of things in her big bag. One day, she felt it was time to teach people a lesson so she went to the park and put a very tight law in the middle. Nobody was allowed to take anything from the bag. Lots of people came, but nobody could take anything. They all got very frustrated, but the lady just smiled and said, "Be patient. You never know what might happen!" So, everyone waited and then, on the last day, the lady said there as a surprise! She asked everyone to go around her bag and to everyone's surprise, each person was able to get something from the bag. Everyone clapped and cheered! The law had worked; the lady had been able to supply everyone with the things they needed, even after it had seemed impossible!
