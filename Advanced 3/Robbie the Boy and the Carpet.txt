Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a friendly ghost named Boo. Boo loved to play with the kids in the park. One day, while playing, Boo's toy broke. A little girl named Lily saw Boo was sad and said, "Don't worry, Boo. We can replace the toy." Boo and Lily went to the toy store to find a new toy. They looked at many toys, but Boo could not find one he liked. Then, Lily saw a toy that looked just like Boo's broken toy. She showed it to Boo and said, "Look, Boo! This is just like your old toy. We can replace it with this one." Boo was so happy that he hugged Lily. But when he hugged her, something unexpected happened. Boo turned into a real little boy! Now, Boo and Lily could play together all the time. They became best friends and had many fun adventures in their quiet little town.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big red bike in his yard. The bike had a shiny pedal. Tim wanted to ride the bike, but he did not know how. He asked his mom, "How do I ride the bike?" His mom said, "You have to push the pedal with your foot." Tim tried to push the pedal, but he was too small. He felt angry. Tim had an idea. He took a marker and put a mark on the pedal. Then, he asked his big sister to help him. She saw the mark and knew where to push. Tim was happy and they rode the bike together.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl who loved to go to the mill. Every day the little girl would go to the mill, play in the wheat fields and run around in the open spaces. But one day, the miller got angry at the little girl because she had been too careless. The miller was worried that she would gain too much from the mill and cause him to lose his harvest. The little girl was very sad and she kept away from the mill for a long time. She watched from afar as the miller busily worked in the fields. She wished she could go to the mill again and have fun like she used to. One day, she decided to try and make amends with the miller. She went to the mill and asked if she could help him work in the fields. She worked carefully and used her skills to gain only the necessary things while leaving the rest of the harvest untouched. The miller was very pleased and allowed her to come back to the mill to play again. From then on, the little girl was careful when she was playing in the fields, making sure not to take too much. The miller thanked the little girl for her hard work and they became friends again. The little girl was very happy, for she had managed to make peace with the miller and now she could go to the mill to have fun again.
