Title: The Ranch and the Cow

Once upon a time, there was a ranch. On this ranch, there were many cows. The cows were smooth and soft. One day, it was very hot. The sun was so hot that it could melt ice cream. A little boy named Tim lived on the ranch. He saw that the cows were very hot. He said to his mom, "Mom, the cows are hot! Can we give them ice cream?" His mom said, "No, Tim. Cows don't eat ice cream." Tim was sad, but he had an idea. He went to the store and bought a big fan. He put the fan near the cows. The cows were happy and not hot anymore. Tim was happy too. Then, something unexpected happened. A cow said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim was very surprised. He did not know that cows could talk. The cow said, "We can talk, but only when we are cool and not hot. You helped us, so now we can talk to you." From that day on, Tim and the cows were best friends. They talked and played every day. The ranch was a happy place. And the cows never felt hot again, because Tim always kept them cool.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a huge blueberry tree. Many animals lived close to the tree because they loved to eat the sweet blueberries. One day, a little girl named Lily found the tree while she was playing. Lily saw a squirrel with a blueberry in its mouth. The squirrel ran up the tree and put the blueberry inside a small hole. Lily was curious and wanted to know what was in the hole. She climbed the tree and found many blueberries hidden inside. Lily had an idea. She decided to release all the blueberries from the hole. She thought the animals would be happy to see more blueberries. But when she let the blueberries out, something unexpected happened. The huge blueberry tree started to grow bigger and bigger! The animals were surprised and excited. Now, they had more blueberries to eat and a bigger tree to live in. And Lily became friends with all the animals in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a girl and her family. They were very happy as they lived in a nice home with furniture and lots of things. One day, the girl wanted to show her family how good she was at balancing things, so she decided to take her mom's special vase. She put it on her head and tried to balance it with her hands. But the vase was very different to the things she had balanced before and it tipped over and smashed onto the floor. The girl cried when she saw her mom's special vase in pieces. Her family was so sad over the vase that they all went to bed without dinner. The end.

Sara and Tom were going to visit their grandma. They packed their bags with toys, books and clothes. They were very excited to see her and her big house. When they arrived, grandma hugged them and gave them cookies. She said, "Welcome, my dear ones. Come, let me show you your room. You can unpack your bags there." She led them upstairs to a cozy room with two beds and a window. Sara and Tom followed her and put their bags on the floor. They started to unpack their things and put them on the shelves and drawers. Sara took out her favorite doll, Lily, and hugged her. She said, "Lily, you will like it here. Grandma has a lot of fancy things. She has a big piano, a shiny mirror and a fluffy rug. Maybe she will let us play with them." Tom took out his toy car, Max, and zoomed it around the room. He said, "Max, you will like it here too. Grandma has a big yard, a swing and a slide. Maybe she will let us ride them." But then they heard a loud bark from downstairs. They looked out the window and saw a big dog running towards the house. It had black fur, sharp teeth and a red collar. It looked angry and mean. Sara and Tom felt scared. They said, "Who is that? Is that grandma's dog?" Grandma came into the room and smiled. She said, "Don't worry, my dears. That is Judge. He is my dog. He is very friendly and gentle. He just likes to greet people. Come, let me introduce you to him. He will be your friend too." She took their hands and led them downstairs. Sara and Tom hoped that grandma was right. They hoped that Judge would not bite them or their toys. They hoped that he would like them and their things. They hoped that they would have a fun and safe visit.
