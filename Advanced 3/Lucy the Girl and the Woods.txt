Title: Lucy the Girl and the Woods

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk in the woods. She saw a small pine tree. The pine tree had a sweet smell. Lucy liked the smell very much. Lucy had a plan. She wanted to take the sweet pine smell home. She asked the pine tree, "Can I take your sweet smell home?" The pine tree said, "Yes, but you must be gentle." Lucy was very gentle. She took a small branch from the pine tree. She thanked the pine tree and went back home. When Lucy got home, she put the branch in her room. Now her room smelled sweet like the pine tree. Lucy was very happy with her plan.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. She loved to watch movies with her best friend, Ben. They watched a movie every day after playing outside. One day, Emma had a new movie that she wanted to watch. But Ben wanted to watch his favorite movie again. Emma was persistent and kept asking Ben to watch her movie. Ben said, "Okay, let's share and watch both movies." First, they watched Ben's favorite movie. Emma liked it too. Then, they watched Emma's new movie. Ben loved the new movie. They were happy to share their movies and had lots of fun together. From that day on, they always shared their movies and played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a slow caterpillar named Catty. Catty liked to crawl on the ground and eat leaves. One day, Catty met a big, strong ant named Andy. "Hi, Catty!" said Andy. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, I do!" said Catty. "What do you like to do?" "I like to lift things," said Andy. "I can lift things that are much bigger than me!" Catty and Andy played together. Andy showed Catty how to lift a big leaf. Catty tried to lift it, too. It was hard, but with Andy's help, they did it together. They had fun and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to go to the restaurant with his family. It made him feel good. His favorite thing to eat was pizza. He would always share his pizza with his sister, Jane. Sharing made them both feel happy. One day, Tim and his family went to the restaurant. They were all very hungry. Tim was excited to eat pizza. But when they got there, the restaurant was closed. Tim felt sad. His family decided to go to the park instead. At the park, Tim and Jane found a big, yummy cake. They shared the cake with everyone at the park. All the people felt good and happy because Tim and Jane shared with them. In the end, Tim learned that sharing can make others feel good, even when things don't go as planned.

Once there was a small girl named Daisy. She wanted to make something special. So she grabbed some colored markers and drew a pattern on a piece of paper. Then Daisy held the paper above a candle. She watched as the flame made the pattern on her paper burn. As Daisy watched, she started to feel dizzy. She tried to take a step back, but the dizziness kept getting stronger. Finally, Daisy decided to take a break. Daisy's paper burned up, leaving a pile of ashes. But Daisy was still happy because she decided to make another pattern the next day!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived on a street with many friends. They all loved to play games together. One day, they decided they were ready to have a big race. The race started at one end of the street and went all the way to the other end. Tim and his friends lined up, excited to see who would win. They ran as fast as they could, laughing and cheering each other on. In the end, it was Tim who won the race. He was so happy and proud. His friends clapped and cheered for him. They all had so much fun that they decided to race again the next day. And so, the street was always filled with happy friends and fun races.
