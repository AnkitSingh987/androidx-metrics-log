Title: Mia and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia lived in a small village. One day, Mia received a surprise in the mail. She was very excited and opened it up quickly. Inside she found a beautiful butterfly. Mia had never seen a butterfly like this before. The butterfly was very polite and stayed close to Mia wherever she went. Mia loved having a special friend like the butterfly. She named it Smiley and treated it like family. One day, Mia and Smiley went to the park to fly a kite. While they flew the kite, suddenly Smiley flew away with the wind. Mia tried to reach it, but it was too late. Mia was so sad and felt lost without her friend. She kept looking up at the sky, hoping to see Smiley flying back. She kept hoping, until one day Smiley returned back to Mia. Mia was very happy to see her friend and they flew the kite together like nothing had changed. From that day on, Mia always remembered to be more polite and loving, just like her butterfly friend Smiley.

One day, little Charlotte was worried about something. She had always been spoilt, but this time something seemed very wrong. She heard her parents talking about a crane outside the house. Her mum noticed Charlotte's worried face and asked her, "What's wrong?". Charlotte replied, "I don't want the crane to take our home away". Her mum laughed and said, "Don't worry! The crane isn't here to take our home away. We're getting a crane to help build something new!" Charlotte looked up and smiled, "Will the crane build us a new house?" Her mum laughed again and said, "No sweetheart, it's going to help build a playground for you and your friends!" Charlotte smiled again and said, "Yay, that sounds fun!" And with that, Charlotte wasn't worried anymore about the crane outside her house. She was happy that it was helping build something new, and she couldn't wait to play in the new playground!

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She loved to wander in the woods near her house. She liked looking at the trees, flowers, and bugs. One of her favorite trees was a big pine tree. It was very tall and had lots of green needles. As Mia walked, she saw an unknown animal by the pine tree. It had big eyes and a fluffy tail. Mia said, "Hi, who are you?" The animal looked at her and said, "Hi, I am a squirrel. My name is Sam." Mia was surprised. She did not know squirrels could talk! Mia and Sam became friends. They played under the pine tree every day. One day, Sam showed Mia a secret door in the tree. They went inside and found a beautiful room full of shiny stones and soft beds. Mia and Sam had so much fun in their secret place. They knew they would be friends forever.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. They have many colorful toys, like cars, dolls, balls and blocks. They also have a big switch on the wall that turns on and off the light. One day, Anna and Ben are playing with their toys when their mom calls them for lunch. They run to the kitchen, but they forget to turn off the switch. The light stays on in their room. After lunch, mom says, "Anna and Ben, did you turn off the switch in your room? You should not waste electricity. It is bad for the earth and for our money." Anna and Ben look at each other. They worry. They did not turn off the switch. They say, "Sorry, mom. We forgot. We will go and turn it off now." They run back to their room, but they see something strange. Their colorful toys are not on the floor anymore. They are on the bed, on the shelves, on the window. They are all mixed up and messy. They also see a note on the door. It says, "I am the Switch Fairy. I came to your room and saw that you left the switch on. I did not like that. So I moved all your toys around. If you want them back, you have to turn off the switch and say sorry to the earth." Anna and Ben are shocked. They did not know there was a Switch Fairy. They feel bad for leaving the switch on. They quickly turn off the switch and say, "Sorry, earth. We will not waste electricity anymore. We will be more careful with the switch." Then, they hear a voice. It is the Switch Fairy. She says, "Thank you, Anna and Ben. You learned your lesson. I will put your toys back where they belong. But remember, always turn off the switch when you leave your room. It is good for the earth and for your mom." Anna and Ben say, "Thank you, Switch Fairy. We will remember. We are sorry for making you angry." The Switch Fairy smiles and says, "You are welcome. I am not angry anymore. I am happy that you are good children. Have fun with your toys, but be responsible with the switch." Then, the Switch Fairy flies away. Anna and Ben hug each other. They are happy that their toys are back. They are also happy that they did the right thing. They promise to always turn off the switch when they leave their room. They also promise to love the earth and their mom. They are good children.
