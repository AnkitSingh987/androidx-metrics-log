Title: Tim the Boy and the Pond

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big pond with ducks. The ducks were adorable. Tim liked to watch the ducks swim and play in the water. Tim wanted to play with the ducks. He ran to the pond and started to splash in the water. The ducks were happy and splashed back. Tim's mom laughed and clapped her hands. Suddenly, Tim saw something in the water. It was not a duck. It was a saw! Tim was scared, but his mom picked up the saw. She laughed and said, "This is not a scary saw, it's a toy saw!" Tim was happy and played with the toy saw and the ducks.

One sunny morning, Ben and his mother were walking a long way. Suddenly, Ben saw a big, tall lady. She was wearing a colourful dress and lots of jewels. "Mummy, who is that lady?", asked Ben. Mummy explained, “That's the dancer. She will be returning soon.” Ben was curious. “What will she return with?” “She will return with something very important,” replied mummy. Ben was so excited to learn what the dancer would return with. He waited patiently, until the dancer returned with a big box of sweets! Ben was delighted, and thanked the dancer. After, he happily shared the treats with his mummy. It was an important day for Ben and his mummy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived with his mother in a small house. They were very happy together. Tim loved to play with his toys and his mother loved to watch him play. One day, Tim and his mother went to the store. Tim saw a big, red ball that he wanted to play with. His mother said they needed to pay for the ball first. Tim was upset because he did not want to wait. His mother told him that it was important to pay for things before taking them home. Tim listened to his mother and they paid for the big, red ball. When they got home, Tim played with his new ball all day. His mother was happy because Tim had learned to be patient and do the right thing. They had a fun day playing together and Tim was not upset anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a puppy. He was very polite, and he always said "please" and "thank you". He had a red mitten with white polka dots. Everyday, the puppy liked to kick the mitten around the yard. He would chase it, kick it, and giggle. Even the birds and the squirrels liked to watch him play. One day, the puppy decided to kick the mitten down the street. It went farther and farther away. The puppy was sad and disappointed. He wanted to play with his mitten again. Luckily, the puppy's friend noticed that the mitten was missing. She ran down the street to look for it. She found it and carried it back to its owner. The puppy was so happy and he thanked his friend with lots of hugs. The puppy and his friend had a wonderful time kicking the mitten back and forth. They laughed and smiled and had fun. The puppy was happy to have his mitten back, and he was even more thankful to have such a nice friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Matt. He was three years old and very healthy. Every day he would put on his shoes and go outside to play. One day he was playing in his backyard when he saw a colorful kite in the sky. "Mommy, look at the kite!" he screamed. His mom smiled at him and said, "Why don't you go fly it?" Matt was so excited that he couldn't wait. He ran over to the kite and grabbed the string. Soon, the kite was high in the sky, just like a bird. Matt spent the rest of the day chasing after the kite and playing in the sun. After playing all day, he got very tired. "It's time to take a nap," his mom said. Matt smiled, snuggled up to his mom, and went quickly to sleep.
