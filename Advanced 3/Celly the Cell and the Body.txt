Title: Celly the Cell and the Body

Once upon a time, there was a little cell named Celly. Celly lived in a big body with many other cells. They all had jobs to do. Celly's job was to help keep the body clean. One day, Celly saw a dirty spot in the body. The dirty spot was making the body feel bad. Celly knew it was time to help. So, Celly called all the other cells to come and see the dirty spot. Together, the cells worked hard to clean the dirty spot. They pushed and pulled until the dirty spot was gone. The body felt better, and all the cells were happy. Celly knew that when they all helped, they could make the body clean and healthy.

Once upon a time, there was an old onion. She was very lonely and sad, because no one was around to love her. One day, a young boy saw the old onion and decided to take her home. He smiled and said, “I love you onion!” The old onion was so happy. She hugged the little boy and started to cry. The little boy tried to be brave and comforted the onion. He said, “Don’t cry, onion. I will always love you.” The onion was so thankful and they were happy friends ever after. They would play together and the little boy would tell onion how much he loved her. The old onion was never lonely again. The little boy showed her that love can make anything better.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with a swing. Tim wanted to play on the swing. He ran to the swing and sat down. Tim's mommy gave him a push. Tim's feet went up in the air. His knees bent as he swung back and forth. He felt like he was flying. Tim was very happy. After playing on the swing, Tim was hungry. His mommy had a yummy snack for him. It was a big, red apple. Tim took a bite and smiled. He loved playing at the park and eating yummy snacks.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named May. She had a magic toy that could shrink. May loved to play with it every day. But one day, her little brother, Tim, found the toy and started to play with it too. May started to worry because her toy was very special to her. She felt guilty for not keeping her toy safe. So, May decided to take it back from Tim. She ran to her brother and grabbed the toy. But, she didn't know that Tim had already made the toy shrink very, very small. May tried to make her toy big again, but she couldn't. She felt sad and worried because her toy was now too small to play with. May cried and wished she had kept her toy safe. And from that day on, she never found a way to make her toy big again. The end.

Once upon a time, in a warm and sunny land, there was a big tree. A little bird named Timmy lived in the tree. One day, Timmy wanted to fly high in the sky. He flapped his wings and up he went. As Timmy flew, the wind started to blow. The wind was strong, and it pushed Timmy around. Timmy was scared. He saw a big rock and decided to sit on it. He sat on the rock and watched the wind blow the leaves on the trees. After a while, the wind stopped. Timmy learned that sometimes it's good to sit and wait when things are hard. He flew back to his tree, happy and safe. The moral of the story is to be patient and wait for the right time to do things.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly bug. The ugly bug lived on a ranch. The ranch had many animals like cows, horses, and pigs. The ugly bug was not happy because all the animals did not like him. They thought he was too ugly to be their friend. One day, the ugly bug met a little girl named Sue. Sue was not scared of the ugly bug. She said, "Hello, ugly bug! Can I borrow you for a day?" The ugly bug was surprised and said, "Yes, you can borrow me." Sue took the ugly bug to her house. She showed him her room and her toys. The ugly bug was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The ugly bug started to change. He became a beautiful butterfly! Sue and the butterfly went back to the ranch. The animals saw the beautiful butterfly and said, "Wow, you are so pretty! We want to be your friend now." The butterfly said, "I was the ugly bug you did not like. I am still the same inside, just different outside. We should be friends with everyone, no matter how they look." From that day on, all the animals on the ranch learned to be friends with everyone, no matter how they looked. And they all lived happily ever after.
