Title: Sarah the Girl and the Garden

Once there was a little girl named Sarah. She found a unique armchair in her grandmother's garden. Sarah wanted to sit in it and so she climbed onto the armchair. But when Sarah was sat in the armchair, she suddenly heard a loud screaming noise! Sarah was so scared and tried to get away quickly. She jumped off the armchair and ran back into the house. But something was wrong. Sarah couldn't move her arm anymore. She had broken one of her arms while she tried to escape the armchair! Sarah had to go to the hospital and was very sad that she had broken her arm. She knew she would never be able to sit on the armchair again. It was a unique armchair, but the bad ending meant Sarah never got to find out how unique it really was.

Jim was walking through the woods one day. He saw a big tiger leaning against a tree. Its tail was moving from side to side, like it was upset. Jim approached the tiger, with caution, and asked softly, "Are you alright?". The tiger just glared and continued to sway back and forth. Jim carefully stepped back and noticed a small creature peering out from behind a bush. It was a mouse. Jim said, "Are you the one making the tiger upset?". The mouse nodded his little head. Jim bent down and said, "Don't worry, I'm here to help". The mouse nodded again and pointed to the tiger. With a brave heart, Jim took a step closer and helped the mouse up onto the tiger's back. The tiger growled but remained still. Suddenly, the mouse started purring through its little whiskers, and the tiger leaned its head down against the mouse. The mouse was purring louder, and the tiger seemed content. Jim smiled, pleased that he was able to help the two friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play in the park with her friends. Lily and her friends liked to look at the pretty flowers that would bloom in the spring. One day, an elderly lady named Mrs. Rose walked up to Lily and her friends. Mrs. Rose was very old and wise. She asked the children, "Do you know how these flowers bloom so beautifully?" Lily and her friends shook their heads. They did not know. Mrs. Rose smiled and said, "The flowers bloom because of the love and care from the youth like you. When you play and laugh near the flowers, they grow strong and happy." From that day on, Lily and her friends took extra care of the flowers in the park. They watered them and made sure they got enough sun. The flowers bloomed even more beautifully than before, and Lily knew it was because of their love and care.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Yet. Yet loved to play in the water. One sunny day, Yet went to the park and saw a big puddle. He was so excited! He jumped in the puddle to soak and splash. But then, Yet got worried. He thought, "What if I get lost?" He looked around and saw a big tree. He walked to the big tree and waited for his friend, the little bird, to come and help him. Soon, the little bird came and saw Yet by the tree. The little bird said, "Don't be worried, Yet. I will help you find your way home." They walked together, and soon, Yet was back home, safe and sound.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a big oak tree. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to play near the oak tree. Tim threw a ball for Max to catch, and they both had lots of fun. As they played, a small brown squirrel came down from the oak tree. It stared at Tim and Max, and Max stared back. The squirrel looked a little guilty. Tim asked the squirrel, "Why do you look guilty?" The squirrel did not say anything. Suddenly, the squirrel ran back up the oak tree and came back down with a big bag of nuts. The squirrel gave the bag of nuts to Tim and Max. It turns out the squirrel felt guilty because it had taken the nuts from another squirrel's home. Tim, Max, and the squirrel all shared the nuts and had a great time under the oak tree.
