Title: Kim the Girl and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Kim. She wanted to record something special. So she went to her room and grabbed a plate and a pencil. She was so excited to make something special that she didn't care how pale the plate was. She began to record something special on the plate with the pencil, but then she heard a noise downstairs. She was so scared to go and check what it was that she quickly put the plate with her special recordings on it outside the door. Kim opened the door and noticed that her parents were there. Her dad asked her why she was recording something, but she had no idea what to say. So she just stood there, pale and scared. Her dad was so angry that he threw the plate with the recordings away. Even though Kim was sad, she knew that he was just trying to keep her safe. The moral of the story is that it is important to have courage and talk to family when something bad is happening.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to play with his ball. One day, Tom went to the park to play. He saw a big pitch with green grass. He was very happy. Tom kicked his ball on the pitch. But then, something weird happened. The ball started to change colors. It turned red, then blue, then green. Tom was surprised and a little scared. Tom tried to catch the ball, but it started to disappear. First, it became see-through, then it was gone. Tom was sad. He looked for his ball everywhere. Suddenly, the ball came back. It was normal again. Tom was happy and played with his ball all day.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to complete a puzzle. It was not an ordinary puzzle. It had a picture of a flute on it. Lily liked the flute because it made pretty sounds. Lily started to put the puzzle pieces together. She found the pieces for the flute first. Then, she put the other pieces in place. The puzzle was almost complete. Finally, Lily finished the puzzle. She was very happy. She showed her mom and dad the complete puzzle with the flute. They clapped and said, "Good job, Lily!"

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Joey. He was three years old. One day, Joey was really excited because he was going to meet some new friends. He put on his special hat and waited by the window, watching for them to arrive. Suddenly he heard a knock at the door and he ran to answer it. He opened the door to find two little monkeys standing outside. Joey smiled at them and said, "Hi, I'm Joey! What are your names?" The monkeys replied, "We are Max and Sam! Would you like to play with us? Joey was so excited. He said, "Yes!" So the three of them played games in the garden until it was time for the monkeys to go home. Joey was sad to see them go, but he knew he'd see them soon. He waved goodbye and watched them go, waiting not so long until they'd come back to play with him again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Blue. Blue loved to sing. Every day, Blue would fly from his nest and sing to all his friends. Blue's friends liked to listen to him, and they all thought he was important. One day, Blue met a new friend named Red. Red was a small squirrel. Blue said, "Hello, Red! Do you want to hear me sing?" Red nodded and smiled. Blue sang a beautiful song, and Red clapped his hands. They became good friends. Blue and Red played together every day. They would visit each other's homes, and Blue would always sing a song for Red. Red began to feel important too, because he had a special friend who could sing. And they lived happily ever after.

Once, there was a girl named Amy. Amy loved to paint. She would paint all day with her friend, Tom. Tom painted well too. They would paint and play together. One day, Amy had to take an exam. She was sad because she could not paint with Tom. Tom was jealous because he wanted to take the exam too. Amy said, "Don't worry, Tom! We can paint after the exam." Amy went to take the exam. When she came back, she saw that Tom had painted a big surprise for her. He painted a fun party with all their friends. Amy was so happy and said, "Thank you, Tom! I didn't know you could paint a party!" They both laughed and played with their friends at the painted party. They were not jealous or sad anymore. They knew that they could always paint and have fun together, even after exams.
