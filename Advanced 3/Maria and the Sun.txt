Title: Maria and the Sun

Maria was so excited when she saw the pony for the first time. It was so big and strong and its shiny fur sparkled in the sun. She wanted to learn how to ride it and have all sorts of fun. She asked her parents if she could keep the pony and they said she could if she promised to take care of it. Maria happily agreed, she was so happy to have her very own pony and take it out for rides. Things were going well and Maria and her pony became the best of friends. One day they went out for a ride and Maria was feeling extra brave. She asked her pony to do some crazy tricks, like standing on its hooves, and the pony felt just as excited to show off its skills. When Maria saw how well the pony had performed, she was filled with joy. She knew then that this was the start of a beautiful friendship.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Emma went to the park with her mom. They saw a big tree with lots of fruit. Emma saw a pear on the ground and picked it up. She looked at the pear and said, "Mom, can I eat this pear?" Her mom said, "Let's examine it first, sweetie." They looked at the pear together. It was a sad, squishy pear. Emma's mom said, "This pear is too sad to eat. We should find a happy pear instead." Emma put the sad pear back under the tree and they looked for a happy pear. They found a happy, round pear high up in the tree. Emma's mom picked it and gave it to Emma. Emma ate the happy pear and said, "This is the best pear ever!" The moral of the story is, always examine things before you use them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a photo of her and her dog, Max. They liked to play together. One day, Lucy wanted to show Max the photo. She held it up and said, "Max, look! It's us!" Max looked at the photo and nodded his head. He was happy to see himself with Lucy. They decided to go outside and play. They ran around and played catch. Max was very fast, but Lucy was slow. She couldn't catch him. Lucy's mommy came outside and saw them playing. She smiled and said, "Lucy, you and Max are having so much fun!" Lucy nodded and said, "Yes, mommy! We love to play together." They played until the sun went down and then went inside to eat dinner.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved her bookshelf. She had many books on it. She liked to read the books every day. One day, Lily was upset. She could not find her favorite book. Lily looked all around her room. She looked under her bed. She looked in her toy box. But she could not find her book. Lily was very sad. She thought her book was gone forever. Then, something unexpected happened. Lily's cat, named Whiskers, walked into her room. Whiskers had Lily's book in his mouth! Whiskers had been hiding the book under the sofa. Lily was so happy. She hugged Whiskers and thanked him. Now, Lily and Whiskers read the book together every day.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a big tree with a hole in it. Lucy felt uncomfortable, but she wanted to see what was inside the hole. She walked closer and looked inside. It was very dark and she couldn't see anything. Suddenly, a small bird flew out of the hole. "Boo!" said the bird. Lucy was scared and jumped back. "I'm sorry," said the bird. "I didn't mean to scare you. My name is Timmy, and this is my home." Lucy felt better and smiled. "Hi, Timmy! I'm Lucy. Your home is very nice." Timmy and Lucy became friends and played together all day. When it was time for Lucy to go back to her home, she said goodbye to Timmy. They promised to meet again and play together under the big tree.
