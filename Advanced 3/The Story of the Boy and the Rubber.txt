Title: The Story of the Boy and the Rubber

Once upon a time there was a little boy. He was very intelligent, always eager to learn. One day he got a new rubber. It was red and very bouncy. He was so excited to try it out. Carefully, he examined the rubber. He gave it an extra squeeze. Suddenly, he heard a voice. "Hey there, little boy" the rubber said. The boy was so surprised. He couldn't believe it. "Who are you?" The boy asked. "My name is Rubber," said the rubber. The boy was so amazed. He examined the rubber closer, amazed at how intelligent it was. The two of them talked and laughed together for hours. The boy was so happy to have found a new friend. The boy and Rubber became the best of friends. They had lots of adventures together, and the boy was never bored.

One day, a big, ugly bird had a job to do. He had to deliver a bottle of lotion to a little girl named Sue. The ugly bird was very good at his job, but he was not happy. He did not like being called ugly. As the ugly bird flew to Sue's house, he saw a little boy named Tim. "Why are you so ugly?" Tim asked the bird. The bird was sad but said, "I don't know, but I have a job to do. I must deliver this lotion to Sue." When the ugly bird got to Sue's house, he gave her the lotion. Sue was very kind. "Thank you, bird! You are not ugly. You are special," she said. The bird was happy and surprised. No one had ever called him special before. Just then, something unexpected happened. The bird put some lotion on his feathers, and he turned into a beautiful bird! Sue and the bird were both amazed. From that day on, the bird was never called ugly again. He was known as the special bird who delivered lotion and brought happiness to everyone he met.

Once upon a time, there was a big pink building. It was very pretty. The sun would shine on it every day. People liked to look at the pink building because it was so nice. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the pink building. She wanted to go inside and see what was there. Lily walked up to the door and pushed it open. Inside, she saw many fun things to play with. There were toys, games, and even a big slide. Lily played and played all day long. But then, it started to get dark outside. The sun stopped shining on the pink building. Lily did not want to leave, but she knew she had to go home. She walked to the door, but it was locked. Lily was scared and sad. She cried and cried, but no one could hear her. The pink building was not fun anymore.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play and watch films. One day, they go to the cinema with Lily's mum. They want to see a good film with funny animals. Lily's mum buys them popcorn and juice. They find their seats and wait for the film to start. Lily and Tom are very excited. They see a big screen and many lights. The film begins. It is about a dog and a cat who are lost in the city. They have to find their way home and make new friends. The dog and the cat do silly things and talk in funny voices. Lily and Tom laugh a lot. They think the film is very good. But then, something bad happens. The dog and the cat get chased by a mean man who wants to catch them. He has a big net and a loud voice. He runs after them and tries to grab them. Lily and Tom are scared. They hold each other's hands and hope the dog and the cat will be okay. "Will they escape?" Lily asks her mum. "Don't worry, sweetie. Just watch and see," Lily's mum says. She knows the film has a happy ending. She has seen it before. She smiles and hugs Lily and Tom. She likes to see them happy. The film ends. The dog and the cat find their home and their family. They are safe and happy. The mean man gets caught by the police. He is sad and angry. Lily and Tom clap and cheer. They think the film is very good. They leave the cinema and thank Lily's mum. They say goodbye and promise to play again soon. They are still laughing and talking about the film. They have had a good day.
