Title: Tim the Boy and the Moon

One day, a little boy named Tim saw an impressive big moon in the night sky. He was so happy and wanted to show it to his friend, Sue. Tim ran to Sue's house and knocked on her door. Sue opened the door and said, "Hi Tim! What is going on?" Tim said, "Come see the big moon! It is so big and bright!" Sue came outside and looked up at the sky with Tim. They both smiled and were very happy to see the big moon. But then, something unexpected happened. The big moon started to change colors! It turned red, then blue, then green! Tim and Sue were so surprised. They had never seen a moon like this before. They knew that this night was very special and they would always remember the impressive, colorful moon.

Once upon a time, there was a wise cow. This cow was very smart and could do many things. One day, the cow found a big box. The big box could print pretty pictures. The wise cow wanted to make her friends happy. So, she printed many pictures of flowers, trees, and animals. She gave the pictures to her friends, and they all loved them. They said, "Thank you, wise cow!" The wise cow felt happy that she could make her friends smile. She knew that sharing and being kind were important. And from that day on, the wise cow and her friends had many fun days together, with the pretty pictures she printed.

Once upon a time there lived a kind and gentle prince. The prince had a very important rule to live by, that was to always trust. This rule had kept the prince safe ever since the day he was born. One day the prince met a bald man who seemed very sad. The prince asked why he was sad, and the bald man replied, “I have been tricked by my friend and now I don’t know who to trust.” The prince said, “If you believe in me, I will show you to trust again.” The bald man looked into the prince’s eyes and said, “Thank you, kind prince. I will trust you.” From then on the bald man trusted the prince. They both were happy, because the prince had taught the bald man the most important lesson of all. That lesson was to trust.

Mandy and her mommy were walking in the park. Mandy saw a fierce dog. The dog barked. Mandy got very scared and ran back to her mommy. "Mandy, don't be scared," said Mommy. "I'm scared," said Mandy. "It's OK, the dog can't get us. We are safe," said Mommy. Mommy and Mandy went back home. They closed the door and window so the fierce dog couldn't get in. "Mommy, I'm not scared now," said Mandy. "That's good, you are safe and sound now," said Mommy. Mandy smiled.

Once upon a time, there was a gray fish named Splash. Splash loved to swim around in the big blue sea. One day, Splash decided that he wanted to win a game. He swam up to the surface and he saw a huge waterfall. He decided to jump into the waterfall and challenge himself to a game. The waterfall was strong and fast, but Splash was brave and determined. He jumped over the rocks and dived under the cold streams of water. Splash was getting tired but he kept going. Finally Splash reached the edge of the waterfall. Splash was so happy that he had won the game. He jumped through the last stream of water and landed in a big pool of water at the bottom. He had done it! He had won! As he was celebrating his victory, he suddenly realized that the pool of water he had ended up in was incredibly familiar. He had been to this place before and suddenly he knew why - it was the same pool he had been swimming in when he started. How had Splash managed to splash back to the place he started? He had outsmarted the waterfall! Splash was so proud of himself, and he knew that he'd remember this game for a very long time.
