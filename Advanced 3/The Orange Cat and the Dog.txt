Title: The Orange Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little orange cat. The cat liked to play with a long cable. The cable was very fun for the cat. The cat would jump and run with the cable every day. One day, the cat saw a big dog. The big dog was playing with a ball. The cat wanted to join the dog and play with the ball too. The cat asked the dog, "Can I join you and play with the ball?" The big dog said, "Yes, you can join me. Let's play together!" So, the orange cat and the big dog played with the ball and the cable. They had a lot of fun and became good friends.

Tim took the wet money to his mom. She helped him dry it. Then, they went to the store together. Tim bought a new toy with the money he found. He played with it all day and was very happy.
