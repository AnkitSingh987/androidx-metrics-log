Title: Mia and the Tree

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the forest. She saw many trees and animals. Mia loved to play in the forest. One day, Mia saw a dead tree. It was very big and old. She felt sad for the tree. But Mia had an idea to make the tree happy again. She wanted to surprise the tree. Mia picked some pretty flowers and put them near the dead tree. She also brought her friends, the birds, to sing a happy song. The tree looked beautiful and alive again. The tree was very surprised and happy. Mia and her friends had a fun day in the forest.

One day, a bossy deer was walking in the woods. He liked to call out to the other animals and tell them what to do. He thought he was the best deer in the whole forest. "Hey, Rabbit!" the bossy deer called to a little rabbit. "You need to hop faster! You're too slow!" The rabbit looked sad but hopped away quickly. Then, the bossy deer saw a squirrel. "You, Squirrel! Climb that tree and find more nuts!" he called. The squirrel didn't like being told what to do, but he climbed the tree anyway. A wise old owl was watching the bossy deer from a tree. The owl called out, "Deer, why are you so bossy? The other animals don't like it." The deer looked surprised. He didn't know that his bossy ways were making the other animals unhappy. From that day on, the bossy deer tried to be kinder and listen to the other animals. He learned that being bossy wasn't the best way to make friends. And soon, the deer had many friends in the forest who liked him just the way he was.

Once upon a time there was a little squirrel. The squirrel was always looking for food and one day it found a rare acorn. It was so excited and it wanted to share the acorn with its friends. So it called out loud: "Come here everyone, I've found a rare acorn." All the other squirrels came to see the acorn and they were so happy. They had never seen a rare acorn before. They all took turns eating the acorn and were very happy. The little squirrel was very pleased to have found the rare acorn and to share it with its friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little pupil named Tim. Tim liked to go to school. He had many friends to play with. They liked to run, jump, and laugh together. Tim's school was a fun place to be. One day, Tim's mom said they were going to a fancy party. Tim was very excited. He put on his best clothes and shiny shoes. Tim's mom held his hand as they walked to the party. At the party, there were many people. Tim saw his friends from school. They played games and ate yummy food. Tim had a great time at the fancy party. When it was time to go home, Tim hugged his friends and said goodbye. Tim couldn't wait to tell everyone at school about the fancy party.

Once upon a time, there was an ugly bird named Bobo. He lived under a big tree. Bobo was not happy because he could not rise high like other birds. One day, Bobo met a kind bug named Buzzy. Buzzy wanted to help Bobo rise high in the sky. They became good friends and played together under the big tree. Buzzy had an idea to help Bobo rise. He asked all his bug friends to help. They all worked together and made a big, strong net. Bobo sat on the net and the bugs lifted him up. Bobo was so happy to rise high in the sky. From that day, Bobo and Buzzy were always together, and they played happily under the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo. The hippo loved to play in the water all day. He had many friends who played with him. One day, the hippo saw a bird flying and he wanted to learn to fly too. The hippo asked his friends to help him. His friends thought and thought. Then, they had an idea. They told the hippo to jump and spin very fast. The hippo jumped and spun, but he got dizzy. He fell down and laughed. The hippo tried many times to learn to fly, but he always got dizzy. His friends laughed and played with him. They told him it was okay, because hippos are great at swimming. The hippo smiled and knew he was the best swimmer.
