Title: The Cold Day and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a cold day. On this day, the sun did not shine bright. People in the town could not see the sun in the sky. They were sad because they liked the sun's warm light. A little girl named Amy wanted to bring the sun back. She put on her warm coat and went outside. She looked up at the sky and called out, "Sun, please come back! We miss your shine!" To her surprise, the sun heard her and began to shine a little. Amy was so happy! She ran around the town, telling everyone to look up at the sky. The people saw the sun's shine and felt its warmth. They were all very happy and thanked Amy for bringing the sun back. From that day on, the town was never without the sun's light and warmth again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Hop. Hop loved to hop all day long. One day, while hopping, Hop found a big shiny thing. It was made of steel. Hop did not know what it was, but it looked fun. Hop wanted to play with the steel thing, but it was very heavy. Hop asked his friends, the bird and the squirrel, to help him. They all tried to push and pull the steel thing, but it was too hard to move. They knew it must be expensive because it was so big and shiny. Hop and his friends decided to leave the expensive steel thing alone and go back to playing in the forest. They all hopped, flew, and ran around, having lots of fun. And they knew that even though the steel thing was big and shiny, they were happy just playing together.

Once upon a time there was an amazing engine. It was a very special engine because it could bake delicious treats for everyone. One day, an old man noticed the engine and said with a smile, "That's an amazing engine. Can it bake for me too?" The engine answered back, "Yes, of course! I can bake you something delicious." So the old man said, "Can you bake me three cakes?" The engine then got to work and soon had three amazing cakes ready. The old man was very happy and thanked the engine for the treat. The engine responded, "You're very welcome. I'm glad you enjoyed it!" And this is how the amazing engine made everyone happy by baking delicious treats.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a group of animals. In this group, there was a fast rabbit, a big bear, and a small mouse. They were friends and liked to play together. One day, they wanted to test who was the best at running. The rabbit was very impatient and wanted to start right away. The bear and the mouse agreed, and they got ready to race. The race began, and the rabbit ran very fast. The bear tried to catch up, but he was too big and slow. The mouse was even slower, but he did not give up. The rabbit was so sure he would win that he stopped to rest. While the rabbit was resting, the mouse kept running. He passed the rabbit and kept going. The rabbit woke up and saw the mouse running ahead. He tried to catch up, but it was too late. The mouse won the race, and the rabbit learned that being impatient is not always good.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to read books. One day, she found a big book in her room. She was very happy and started to read it. As she read, she saw a small pin on the floor. It was very sharp. Lucy didn't want anyone to get hurt, so she picked it up and put it in her pocket. Later, Lucy's mom came into her room. "What are you doing, Lucy?" she asked. Lucy said, "I am reading a big book, and I found a pin. I put it away so no one gets hurt." Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Lucy! You are very careful and kind." They both sat down and read the big book together.
