Title: Lily the Fairy and the Forest

Once upon a time there was a little fairy named Lily. She lived in a wild forest and loved to play all day. One day, Lily was flying around the forest and resting on the branches of the trees when a small child appeared. "Hello fairy," said the child, his eyes wide with wonder. Lily smiled. "Hello! Would you like to come and play with me?" The little boy nodded. The two of them played in the wild forest: hiding behind trees, swinging on vines, and chasing each other around. After several hours of play, Lily and the boy both grew tired. "Let's rest," said Lily. So they found a soft patch of grass, and lay down together. They were both quickly asleep, dreaming of all of their magical adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who lived in a birdcage. The birdcage was in a big room with a high ceiling. The bird liked to bounce and play all day long. One day, the bird saw a ball outside the birdcage. It was a big, red, bouncy ball. The bird wanted to bounce the ball high in the air. The bird tried to get the ball, but it could not reach it. Then, a nice girl came into the room. She saw the bird and the ball. She opened the birdcage and let the bird out. The bird was so happy! It jumped on the ball and bounced it high in the air. The bird and the girl played and bounced the ball all day long. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple giant. He lived in a huge cave near a little town. The giant was very nice, but he was also very shy. One day, the giant went to the town to make some new friends. He saw kids playing and wanted to join them. But when he walked up to them, he tripped on a rock and fell down. The giant felt very embarrassed. He quickly got up and ran back to his cave. The kids felt bad for the giant and wanted to make him feel better. So, they went to his cave with some pretty flowers. The giant saw the kids and smiled. They all played together and had lots of fun. The purple giant was happy and not embarrassed anymore.

One day, a large frog was hopping near a pond. He saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was playing with her ball near the pond. The large frog hopped closer to her and said, "Hello! I am a large frog. Can you guess my name?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "Is your name Fred?" The frog laughed and said, "No, but that is close! My name is Frank." As they were talking, the ball rolled into the pond. Lily was sad because she could not get her ball back. But then, something unexpected happened. Frank the frog jumped into the pond and got the ball for Lily. She was so happy and said, "Thank you, Frank!" From that day on, Lily and Frank were the best of friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. The large frog had become Lily's best friend, and they never ran out of games to play.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play and eat cream. One day, Fluffy found a bowl of cream in the kitchen. Fluffy was very happy and started to eat the cream. As Fluffy was eating the cream, a small bug flew by. The bug went close to Fluffy's nose, and Fluffy sneezed. The sneeze was so big that the cream went flying all over the kitchen! Fluffy felt sad because the cream was gone. But then, Fluffy's friend, a little dog named Spot, came to help. Spot licked up the cream and they both laughed. They played together all day and had lots of fun. From that day on, Fluffy and Spot were the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a grumpy boy named Jack. He went on a journey with his family to a far away hotel. When they arrived, Jack was so excited he wanted to kick something! So he went outside and kicked a rock. His family laughed and cheered him on! On the next day, they all got ready to visit the beach. But Jack wanted to just stay in the hotel room. His family was not happy, so they decided that if he stayed in the hotel, he would have to be extra good. Jack did his best all day, but he was still grumpy. He was especially grumpy when his brother and sister came in after playing all day at the beach, bragging about their adventures. Jack was so mad, he couldn't take it anymore. So he did the only thing he knew he could do. He kicked the wall! His family was surprised and not happy. But after a few minutes, Jack's grumpiness was gone and everyone was happy again! They all laughed and decided to have a fun night at the hotel!
