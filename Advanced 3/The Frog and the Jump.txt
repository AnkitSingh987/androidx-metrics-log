Title: The Frog and the Jump

Once upon a time, there was a frog. The frog was famous. Everyone knew the frog. One day, the frog went to a big place. The big place had many things to see. The frog wanted to display his jump. He jumped high and far. People clapped and cheered for the frog. The frog was happy. He jumped and displayed his jump every day. The famous frog and his jump made everyone smile.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a wild, happy fox. The fox loved to run and spin around the trees. One day, while spinning, the fox found a big, soft brush on the ground. The fox picked it up and started to use it to clean its fur. As the fox was spinning and brushing, a small bird saw the fox and wanted to play. The bird asked the fox, "Can I play with you and spin too?" The fox said, "Yes, but first, let's use the brush to clean your feathers." The bird agreed, and they both took turns brushing each other's fur and feathers. After they were all clean, the fox and the bird spun around the trees together. They had so much fun and became best friends. From that day on, they always helped each other and shared their toys. The forest animals saw their friendship and learned that sharing and helping others made everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big pot. Inside the pot, there were many vegetables. All the vegetables were friends. They lived in the pot together. They liked to play games and have fun. One day, the vegetables decided to have a race. They were very competitive. They wanted to see who was the fastest. The carrot, the potato, and the tomato all lined up to start the race. The race began, and the vegetables started to run. They ran and ran. The carrot was fast, but the potato was faster. The tomato tried to catch up, but he was too slow. In the end, the potato won the race. After the race, the vegetables were tired. They went back to the pot. They all began to stir and mix together. They were happy to be friends and have fun. They knew that it was not about winning, but about having a good time together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that he loved a lot. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. They played with the toy together, but then they started to fight. Tim was upset. Tim said, "I don't want to play with you anymore. I quit!" Sam was upset too, but he left Tim's house. Later, Tim wanted to play with his toy again, but he could not find it. He looked and looked, but the toy was gone. Tim was very upset and cried. He missed his toy and his friend, Sam. Tim's mom told him, "When you fight with your friends and quit, you may lose more than just a friend." Tim learned that it is better to share and be nice to his friends, so he does not lose them or his toys. But it was too late, the toy was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree in the park. The tree had many pretty leaves. The tree liked to display its leaves for all to see. All the other trees were jealous of the big tree. One day, a small tree said to the big tree, "I am not happy. I want to display pretty leaves too." The big tree felt sad for the small tree. There was a problem, and the big tree wanted to help. The big tree had an idea. It told the small tree, "Let's share some leaves. Then, you can display pretty leaves too." The small tree was very happy. They shared the leaves, and both trees displayed pretty leaves. All the other trees in the park were not jealous anymore. They all lived happily together.
