Title: Tim the Boy and the Tree

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, brilliant tree. Tim wanted to dig up a root from the tree, but his mom said no. He was sad. Tim asked his mom, "Please, can I have just one root?" His mom said, "No, you cannot. It is not allowed." Tim was very upset. Tim did not listen to his mom. He dug up a root when she was not looking. Suddenly, the tree fell down! Tim was scared. The tree had a big crash and broke the park bench. Tim knew he should have listened to his mom.

Once upon a time, there lived a little dog called Buster. Buster was a very happy pup and loved to play in the park. Every day, his owner Lesley would take him for a walk with a leash and Buster would have so much fun. He loved the fresh air and the feeling of happiness it brought. One day, Lesley noticed that Buster was not as clean as usual. She asked him why he was so dirty and Buster said he had been digging and running in the park. Lesley said to Buster that she valued cleanliness and wanted him to stay clean. But Buster did not listen. The next day, Lesley decided to take a longer walk with Buster, so they could go further than the park. But they had gone too far and Buster got so excited, he ran off without his leash. Lesley was so worried and shouted for Buster to come back. But he had disappeared. Lesley was so sad and never saw Buster again. The park had been his home, but now he was gone. She learnt that she should have valued cleanliness more and never let him off his leash.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to design things. One day, she wanted to make a pond in her backyard. She was very excited. Amy used a thin stick to draw her pond on the ground. She made it big and round. Then, her dad helped her dig a hole for the pond. They put water in the hole and it became a real pond. Amy loved her pond so much. She would sit by the pond and watch the fish swim. Her pond was the best thing she ever designed. She was very happy, and her pond made her backyard a fun place to be.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside with her friends. One day, she found a small box with a lock on it. Lily was very curious about what was inside the box. Lily asked her friends if they could guess what was inside. Her friend Tom said, "Maybe it's a toy!" Her friend Sue said, "I think it's candy!" But nobody knew for sure, because the box was locked. Lily took the locked box home to her mom. Her mom found a key and opened the lock. Inside the box, there was a pretty necklace. Lily was so happy and wore the necklace every day. And they all guessed what was inside the box just for fun.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to eat peanuts. One day, Sam found a big peanut under a tree. He was very happy. Sam saw his friend, Tim the bird, and said, "Look, Tim! I found a big peanut!" Tim looked at the peanut and said, "Wow, that is big! Let's open it!" So, Sam and Tim tried to strike the peanut on a rock to open it. But, when they hit the peanut, something unexpected happened. The peanut broke open, and a tiny fairy flew out! The fairy said, "Thank you for setting me free! Now, I will grant you one wish." Sam and Tim looked at each other, surprised and happy. Sam said, "We wish for more peanuts!" The fairy waved her wand, and suddenly, peanuts rained down from the sky. Sam and Tim laughed and ate peanuts all day long. They were very happy and knew they had a special friend in the tiny fairy.
