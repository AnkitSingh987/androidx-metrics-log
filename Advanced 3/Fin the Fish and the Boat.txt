Title: Fin the Fish and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in the big ocean with his family. He loved to swim and play with his friends all day long. One day, Fin saw a big boat. The people on the boat wanted to take fish from the ocean. Fin felt frustrated because he didn't want his friends to be taken away. He had to think of a plan to save them. Fin swam very fast to tell his friends about the boat. All the fish decided to swim away and hide behind some rocks. The people on the boat couldn't find any fish to take. They felt sad and decided to go back home. The fish were safe, and they all swam back to their families. The little fish learned that they could work together to keep each other safe.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Annie. She was three years old. She loved playing with her toys, but when the sun went down, she got anxious. One day, the little girl was in her room playing with her dolls. They were very cute, with different coloured clothes on. Annie wanted to look pretty like them, so she decided to dress up just like them. She found two skirts, a shirt and some shoes that she hadn't seen before. She put them all on and looked in the mirror. She felt so happy and excited. But when Annie stepped outside, she was surprised to see everyone wearing ashtrays. She was so embarrassed that she ran back inside and took off her clothes. She quickly put them away in her cupboard and never wore them again. Annie was relieved that she hadn't been the only one wearing something different. Though she was still a little anxious, she was glad to know that she wasn't the only one who liked to stand out.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty cat named Kitty. She had soft fur and big eyes. Kitty liked to play in the park with her friends. They would run, jump, and enjoy the sun. One day, Kitty's mom told her that if she helped clean the house, she would get a reward. Kitty was excited and started to help. She picked up her toys and put them away. She also helped her mom with the dishes. When they were done, Kitty's mom gave her the reward. It was a big, shiny ball. Kitty was very happy. She took her new ball to the park and played with her friends. They all enjoyed playing with the ball, and Kitty felt good about helping her mom.

Bob and Sue went out for a walk one sunny day. Sue saw something that made her stop and point. She said, "Look, Bob, it's a butterfly!" Bob smiled and walked over to have a closer look. The butterfly was a bit shy; it didn't want to come close to them. Bob and Sue were very gentle and they spoke softly, but it wouldn't come closer. They thought it might fly away, so Bob said "Let's not complain, we don't want to make it feel scared." Sue had an idea. She slowly walked away, being very quiet and still. The butterfly followed her with its big eyes. It was so beautiful! Bob and Sue kept very still and the butterfly flew around their heads. Then it left them alone and flew away. Bob and Sue waved goodbye to the butterfly and went on their way, feeling happy.

Once there was a little girl who was three years old. Her name was Emma. She had lots of stuff—toys, blankets, books, and games. She was very successful at playing with her stuff. One day, Emma was playing with her stuff. Suddenly, she couldn’t find her favourite bunny. She looked everywhere, but it was gone! “Mommy! Where’s my bunny?” Emma cried. Mommy smiled and said, “Don’t worry, Emma. We’ll find it.” But no matter how hard Emma looked, the bunny was gone. Emma started to cry. All her other stuff was no fun without her bunny. Mommy hugged Emma and said, “Don’t cry, Emma. We’ll find something else Fun to do.” Mommy and Emma looked through all the other stuff until Emma found a book she loved. She read and read and soon she had forgotten all about her lost bunny. Emma was so successful at reading and playing with her other stuff that she forgot all about her missing bunny. Finally, she was happy again!
