Title: The Large King and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a large king. He lived in a big castle with many friends. The king was very nice and everyone liked him. One day, a small boy took the king's toy without asking. The king was sad and his friends were mad. The small boy felt bad for what he did. The small boy went to the king and said, "I am sorry, king." The king looked at the boy and said, "I forgive you." They became friends and played with the toy together. Everyone was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Buggy. Buggy was a special bug because he could glow. Every night, Buggy would light up and play with his friends. They loved to watch Buggy glow, and it made them all very happy. One day, Buggy was playing near the ground with his friends. He saw a big rock and wanted to show off his glowing trick. Buggy started to glow, but he was not looking where he was going. He tripped on the rock and fell down. Buggy felt embarrassed because all his friends saw him fall. Later that night, Buggy's friends came to him and said they wanted to play again. Buggy was scared to glow because he did not want to fall again. But his friends told him it was okay, and they would help him. Buggy started to glow, and his friends held his hand as they played. Buggy was happy and not embarrassed anymore because he knew his friends were there to help him.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small blue bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly and play with his friends. One day, while they were playing near the river, they saw a big pile of mud. Bobo and his friends were curious about the mud. They decided to learn more about the mud. They jumped into the mud and started to play. They splashed and rolled around, having lots of fun. Bobo and his friends learned that mud was soft, wet, and squishy. They liked the way it felt on their feathers. After playing in the mud all day, they decided to go back to their nests. But they were all dirty and covered in mud. So, they went to the river to wash off. They splashed and cleaned themselves until their feathers were shiny and clean again. Bobo and his friends learned that playing in the mud was fun, but they also learned the importance of staying clean. They all flew back to their nests, happy and ready for a good night's sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to gaze at the pretty pictures on her wall. She had a picture of a cat, a dog, and a big tree. Sue liked to look at them every day. One day, Sue was upset. Her favorite picture of the cat was gone. She looked everywhere for it. Sue looked under her bed, behind the door, and even in her toy box. But she could not find the picture. Sue's mom saw her upset and wanted to help. She looked at the wall and saw the cat picture had fallen down. Sue's mom picked it up and put it back on the wall. Sue was happy again and gazed at her favorite cat picture with a big smile.

Once there was a little girl called Sophie who loved to jump. Every day she would jump around the garden with the birds and the butterflies. One day, she found something very special in the grass. It was a red lollipop! She picked it up, held it tight and began to jump up and down. She was so excited and she kept jumping, higher and higher. Finally, she was able to single-handily bite the lollipop in half. She was very pleased with herself. Sophie kept jumping and eating the lollipop, enjoying the sweet taste. She was so persistent that she finished the whole lollipop before the day was done. She was satisfied and decided to jump back home, tired but happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box full of toys that he liked to play with. One day, Tim wanted to see how big his toy box was, so he tried to measure it. He used a long string to see how long the box was. But Tim's little sister, Sue, wanted to play with the string too. Tim and Sue started to disagree about who should get to play with the string. They both pulled on the string, but it did not break. Tim did not want to hurt Sue, so he decided to share the string with her. They both used the string to measure the toy box together. After they measured the toy box, Tim and Sue decided to play with their toys. They played all day and had lots of fun. They learned that sharing and working together was better than disagreeing. And they both lived happily ever after.
