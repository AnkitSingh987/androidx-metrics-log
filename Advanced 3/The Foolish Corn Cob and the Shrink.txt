Title: The Foolish Corn Cob and the Shrink

Once upon a time, there was a foolish corn cob. He was always getting into trouble and was always in a hurry to get things done. One day, he decided he wanted to shrink. So off he went to find a way to do it. He searched high and low. He asked everyone he met, but no one seemed to know how to shrink. Frustrated, he finally decided to try something different. He closed his eyes and wished really hard that he could shrink. Suddenly, he felt himself getting smaller and smaller. He opened his eyes and he was so tiny that he could hide between two stalks of corn! He was so excited that he jumped up and down. From that day on, the foolish corn cob was very careful to wish only for things he could have.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a tall skeleton. The tall skeleton lived in a big house. He was sad because he had no friends. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the big house. Lily saw the tall skeleton and said, "Hi, Mr. Skeleton! Do you want to be my friend?" The skeleton was very happy and said, "Yes, I would love to be your friend, Lily!" They played together and had lots of fun. One day, while they were playing, they saw a ball stuck in a tree. The tall skeleton said, "I can help!" He used his long arms to grab the ball from the tree. Lily was so happy and said, "Thank you, Mr. Skeleton!" From that day on, they were the best of friends. The people in the town saw how kind the tall skeleton was and started to like him. They learned that even if someone looks different, they can still be a good friend. And so, the tall skeleton and Lily taught everyone the importance of being kind and not judging others by their appearance.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big nose. He liked his nose because it made him feel strong. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. He wanted to help the cat. Tim tried to climb the tree. He tried and tried, but he could not get up. His strong nose was not helping him climb. Tim was sad. He wanted to help the cat, but he did not know how. Then, Tim had an idea. He found a long stick and tried to reach the cat with it. The cat grabbed the stick with its paws and Tim pulled it down. The cat was safe and Tim was happy. His strong nose did not help him climb, but his big heart and trying hard saved the cat.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a powerful lion. He was very big and strong. All the animals in the forest were scared of him. One day, the lion was very hungry and went to look for food. While walking, he met a little rabbit. The rabbit was scared, but she wanted to be friends with the lion. So, she said, "Hello, Mr. Lion! I have some food. Would you like me to offer you some?" The lion looked at the rabbit and said, "Yes, please! I am very hungry." The rabbit gave the lion some food. The lion ate the food and felt very happy. After that, they became good friends. The lion was not scary anymore, and all the animals in the forest were happy. They all played together and had lots of fun.

Max and his mom were playing together outside their house. They had lots of fun jumping around and running back and forth. His mom said, "Let's go to the park!" Max was so excited. He loved going to the park - it was his favorite place in the whole world. When they got to the park, Max could not wait to explore. He ran around looking at everything. Suddenly, he saw a tall rope bridge in the distance. He had never seen it before! Without thinking, he ran towards the bridge. But his mom called out, "Max! Be careful!" She didn't want him to be too careless. Max stopped and thought about it. He decided that it was too dangerous and he wanted to be safe. So he didn't cross the bridge and played in a different area instead. When it was time to go home, Max and his mom drove home. As they were driving, Max noticed another park. It looked just like the one he was in earlier. He asked his mom, "Can this be the park with the rope bridge?" His mom nodded. Max said, "Maybe next time I will be more careful and cross it." His mom smiled at him.
