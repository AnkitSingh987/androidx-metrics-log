Title: Bobby and the Track

Bobby was so excited! His mom had taken him to the park, and he saw a track there. The track seemed to go on and on, and Bobby wanted to have a go at running on it. He asked his mom if he could run around the track and she said yes. Bobby set off running, faster and faster. He laughed as the wind blew through his hair and the sun shone on his face. But then, Bobby started to feel very tired. He wanted to keep going, but his legs felt like lead and he couldn't move them. After a few more steps, Bobby tripped and fell. He had pushed himself too far, and he was too tired to get back up. His mom rushed over, and from then on she watched closely to make sure Bobby wasn't running too fast. This was the moral of the story: it's okay to have fun, but you have to watch out and not overdo it.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived an adventurous little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside and explore new places. One sunny day, she went to the park to find new friends to play with. At the park, she met a boy named Tom who was crying. Lucy asked, "Why do you cry, Tom?" Tom said, "I lost my toy and I don't know how to find it." Lucy wanted to help Tom, so they went on an adventure together to find his toy. As they walked, Lucy found a magic book. It had a spell inside to help find lost things. Lucy read the spell out loud and, suddenly, Tom's toy appeared! Tom was so happy, he stopped crying. They both played together for the rest of the day, and Lucy and Tom became best friends.

Tommy loved his trumpet. It was black and shiny and made loud noises. He liked to play it every day, even when his mom said it was time for a nap. He would insist on playing his trumpet, and his mom would get mad. One day, Tommy saw a big dog outside his window. The dog was black and furry and had a long tail. Tommy wanted to play with the dog, so he ran outside with his trumpet. He blew his trumpet at the dog, hoping it would like the noise. But the dog did not like the noise. The dog barked and growled and showed its teeth. Tommy got scared and dropped his trumpet. The dog ran away with the trumpet in its mouth. Tommy cried and cried. He wanted his trumpet back. His mom heard him crying and came outside. She saw the dog running away with the trumpet. She hugged Tommy and said she was sorry. She said they would get a new trumpet, but Tommy had to be careful with it. She said he could not play it when it was time for a nap, or when there were dogs around. Tommy nodded and said he understood. He still missed his trumpet, but he hoped the dog would have fun with it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was only 3 years old and she loved to explore the beach. One day, when she was playing on the beach, she found something strange. It was a small, grey object - an oyster! She had never seen one before and wanted to try eating it. Lucy picked up the oyster with her little fingers and put it in her mouth. But it didn't taste very nice. Lucy made a face and said, "Yucky! This oyster tastes terrible!" She spat the oyster out onto the beach. Suddenly, a rude voice said, "Hey, that's my oyster!" It was an old woman. She had been watching Lucy from a nearby rock. The woman said, "That's very rude! You shouldn't eat things without asking first. Next time, ask if you can have something before you eat it." Lucy felt embarrassed and apologised to the woman. She promised never to be rude again. In the end, she learned an important lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Ben. Ben was a very important bear. He had a job to protect a class of little animals. Every day, the little animals went to class to learn and play. One day, a small bird came to the class. The bird said, "I want to learn and play too!" Ben smiled and said, "You can join us, little bird. I will protect you and your new friends." The little bird was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened! A big wind came and blew the little bird away. The little animals were scared. Ben knew he had to help. He ran and ran, and finally caught the little bird. Ben brought the bird back to the class. All the little animals cheered. They knew Ben would always protect them.
