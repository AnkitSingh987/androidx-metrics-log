Title: Lily and the Magic

One day, a little girl named Lily had a bad dream, which we call a nightmare. She saw a big, scary monster in her sleep. When she woke up, she was very scared and cried. Her mom heard her and came to her room very fast. Lily told her mom about the nightmare. Her mom said, "Don't worry, I have a magic bag that can help you." Her mom took out a small bag and started to fill it with happy things like toys, flowers, and candy. She told Lily that this magic bag would keep her safe at night and chase away the bad dreams. That night, Lily went to sleep with the magic bag under her pillow. She felt good and alert, knowing that the bag was there to protect her. Lily did not have any more nightmares, and she slept well every night. She was very thankful for her mom's magic bag, and they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room with toys. In this room, there was a big toy elephant named Elly. Elly liked to weigh things on her toy scale. She weighed her toy cars, toy blocks, and even her toy friends. One day, Elly's friend, a toy monkey named Momo, came to play. Momo climbed up a big tower of blocks. "Look, Elly! I can see the whole room from here!" Momo said. Elly looked up and saw Momo high up on the block tower. Suddenly, Momo slipped and had a big fall. The blocks fell down too. Elly ran to help Momo. "Are you okay, Momo?" Elly asked. Momo was a little sad, but not hurt. "I'm okay, Elly. Thank you for helping me," Momo said. Together, they made the room tidy again and played with their toys happily.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a big garden. In the garden, he grew many things. One day, he saw a weak celery plant. It was not happy. Tim asked the celery, "Why are you weak?" The celery said, "I need more water and sun. Then I can grow big and strong." Tim wanted to help the celery. He gave it more water and moved it to a sunny spot. But the other plants in the garden were not happy. They said, "We need water and sun too!" Tim thought hard. He wanted to make all the plants happy. So, he made a plan. He gave each plant its own water and sun. Soon, all the plants were big and strong, and the weak celery was happy too. And they all lived happily in Tim's garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little seed. It wanted to grow big and strong. It wanted to be independent and have its own opinion. The little seed lived in a garden with many other seeds. One day, a big bird came to the garden. The bird wanted to eat the seeds. The little seed was scared. It did not want to be eaten. It wanted to grow and be independent. So, the little seed asked the other seeds for help. They all had different opinions on what to do. Some seeds said to hide, others said to fight. The little seed thought hard. It made its own independent opinion. The little seed decided to grow right away. It pushed and pushed, and soon it was a strong little plant. The bird saw the plant and did not want to eat it anymore. The other seeds saw how the little seed grew and became independent. They learned that having their own opinion and working together can help them face problems. The garden was now a happy place, full of strong, independent plants. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play a sport. He asked his friend, Sam, to play with him. Sam said, "Yes, let's be careful and have fun!" They played and played until they found a magic ball. The ball began to shrink them! Tim and Sam were now very small. They were scared and didn't know what to do. A big bird saw them and wanted to help. The bird said, "Don't be sad, I can help you grow big again." The bird used its magic to make Tim and Sam big again. They were so happy and thanked the bird. They went back to playing their sport, being careful with the magic ball.
