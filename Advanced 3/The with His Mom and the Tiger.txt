Title: The with His Mom and the Tiger

Once upon a time, in a big forest, a little tiger lived with his mom. The little tiger loved to play and run around. One day, his mom told him, "You need to protect yourself from the cold." The next day, the little tiger went out to play. He saw a big tree with many leaves. He thought, "This tree can protect me from the cold." So, he played near the tree all day. As the sun went down, it got very cold. The little tiger remembered what his mom said. He went under the big tree and used the leaves to keep warm. The tree protected him from the cold, just like his mom said. The little tiger was happy and safe.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little mushroom. The mushroom was very thoughtful. It liked to think about many things. One day, a tiny bug came to the mushroom. The bug said, "Can I trust you?" The mushroom said, "Yes, you can trust me." So, the bug told the mushroom a big secret. The next day, something unexpected happened. The mushroom started to grow very big. It was now the biggest mushroom in the forest. The bug was surprised and said, "Wow, you are so big now!" The mushroom smiled and said, "I am big because you trusted me with your secret. Thank you for trusting me." The bug was happy and they became best friends. And from that day, they always trusted each other.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a red toy. It was a top that could spin. Lily liked the red top a lot. She wanted to play a game with her friends. She ran to find her friends Tom and Sue. Lily showed Tom and Sue the red top. She said, "Let's play a game. We can spin the top and see who can make it spin the longest." Tom and Sue liked the game idea. They all took turns spinning the red top. Tom spun the red top first. It spun and spun, but then it stopped. Sue tried next. Her spin was better, but not the best. Finally, it was Lily's turn. She spun the red top as hard as she could. The red top spun and spun, and it did not stop. Lily won the game! Tom and Sue clapped and they all laughed. They played the spinning game all day long.

Once upon a time, in a land full of sunshine, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside with her friends. They would run, jump, and laugh under the warm sun. Lily always felt happy when she was outside in the sunshine. One day, the sunshine went away. The sky was full of dark clouds and it started to rain. Lily and her friends felt nervous. They did not like the rain, and they missed the sunshine. They wanted to save the sunshine, but they did not know how. Lily had an idea. She asked her friends to help her find a magic flower that could bring back the sunshine. They searched high and low, and finally found the magic flower. When Lily touched the flower, the rain stopped, and the sunshine came back. Everyone was happy again. Lily and her friends learned that they could save the sunshine by working together.

Once upon a time, there was a big wardrobe in a little girl's room. The little girl's name was Amy. She loved to play hide and seek with her friends. One day, Amy and her friend, Tim, decided to play inside the house. Amy said, "Tim, you count, and I will hide!" Tim started to count loudly. Amy looked for a good hiding spot and saw the wardrobe. She thought it would be the best place to hide. So, she opened the door and went inside. Tim finished counting and started looking for Amy. He looked under the bed, behind the curtains, and in the closet. But he could not find Amy anywhere. Amy, inside the wardrobe, was waiting for Tim to reveal her hiding spot. She heard a loud noise and got scared. The wardrobe door closed, and Amy could not open it. Tim searched and searched but could not find Amy. He began to cry and called for help. Amy's mom and dad came running. They looked everywhere, but they could not find Amy either. The wardrobe door was stuck, and nobody knew that Amy was inside. The game had a bad ending, and everyone was very sad.
