Title: Lily the Girl and the Blanket

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to watch a movie. She asked her mom, "Can we watch a movie, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can watch a movie after lunch." Lily was so happy. She ate her lunch very fast. After lunch, her mom put on a soft, warm blanket on the couch. They sat down and Lily's mom turned on the movie. It was about a funny bunny who liked to shrug. In the movie, the funny bunny would shrug when he did not know what to do. Lily laughed and said, "Look, Mom! The bunny is shrugging like this!" She shrugged her shoulders too. Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, he is very funny." They watched the movie until the end. Lily loved the funny bunny and his soft, shrugging dance. When the movie was over, Lily gave her mom a big hug and said, "Thank you for the movie, Mom!"

Once upon a time, in a small, noisy house, there lived a little boy named Tim and his toy bunny, Fluffy. They were best friends and played together every day. One day, Tim found a leak in the kitchen. He saw water coming out from under the sink. Tim was worried and called his mommy. "Mommy, there is a leak in the kitchen," said Tim. His mommy came and saw the water. She told Tim, "Don't worry, I will fix the leak. You go and play with Fluffy." Tim went back to his room to play with Fluffy, but Fluffy was not there. Fluffy had vanished! Tim looked everywhere for Fluffy. He was very sad. He asked his mommy, "Have you seen Fluffy?" Mommy said, "I haven't seen Fluffy, but maybe if you help me clean the water from the leak, Fluffy will come back." Tim helped his mommy clean the water from the leak. When they were done, Fluffy appeared back in Tim's room. Tim was so happy to see Fluffy, and he learned that when you help others, good things happen. From that day on, Tim always helped his mommy and daddy, and he and Fluffy stayed best friends forever.

One day, a big bear named Bob and a small rabbit named Lily were playing near the ice. They were having lots of fun, laughing and running around. As they played, they saw a big pile of ice. Lily looked at Bob and said, "Let's not disturb the ice." But Bob didn't listen. He wanted to play with the ice too. So, he jumped on the pile of ice and it broke into many pieces. Lily looked at Bob and said, "Oh no, you disturbed the ice!" Bob felt embarrassed. He knew he should have listened to Lily. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't mean to disturb the ice." Lily looked at Bob and smiled. She said, "It's okay, Bob. We can still have fun." So, they continued to play and have fun near the ice. Bob learned to listen to his friends and not to disturb things that should be left alone. And they both had a great day playing together.

Joey and his mom were going on a picnic. Joey was so excited and couldn't wait to get there. He was carrying a basket with some snacks and a match. When they got to the park, Joey ran off to explore. His mom called after him, "Joey, don't forget to clean up after yourself!" Joey just waved and kept running. He found a really nice spot under a big green tree and sat down to eat his snack. As he was eating he saw some of the wrapper and the match he was carrying fall on the ground. He needed to listen to his mom and clean it up. But instead, he just walked away. When he got back to his mom, she looked him right in the eye and said, "Did you remember to clean up after yourself?" Joey knew he should have listened and felt bad. "It's important to clean up after yourself," his mom said, "Not only is it the right thing to do, but it helps keep our parks looking nice and green." Joey hugged his mom, understanding the importance of doing the right thing. From then on, Joey always remembered to clean up after himself.
