Title: Tim the Boy and the Ice

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a plan. He wanted to play with ice. So, he went to the big ice box in the kitchen. He opened the ice box and took out some ice. Tim played with the ice on the table. But the ice made him feel uncomfortable. His hands were so cold! Tim did not like the cold feeling. He put the ice back in the ice box. Next time, Tim had a new plan. He would play with ice, but he would wear gloves. Tim was happy with his new plan. He knew that he could play with ice and not feel uncomfortable anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big forest. In the forest, there was a long trail. Many animals lived near the trail. One day, a kind, generous rabbit named Remy was hopping on the trail. Remy liked to help his friends. One day, Remy saw his friend, a small bird, in trouble. The bird's nest was about to fall from the tree. Remy wanted to prevent the nest from falling. He thought of a plan. He asked his friend, the strong elephant, to help. The elephant used his long nose to hold the nest. But then, something unexpected happened. The elephant sneezed! The nest flew up in the air. All the animals gasped. But the sneeze made the nest land back in the tree, safe and sound. The bird was happy, and all the animals cheered. Remy and the elephant saved the day, and the forest was peaceful again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to mix things in her big bowl. One day, she found a tool in the kitchen. The tool was broken, but Lily didn't know that. Lily wanted to make a cake for her mommy. She put flour, sugar, and eggs in the big bowl. Then, she tried to use the broken tool to mix everything. But the tool didn't work. Lily tried and tried, but the cake mix stayed lumpy. Lily started to cry. She didn't know what to do. Her mommy came into the kitchen and saw the mess. Mommy was sad, too. They couldn't make the cake. The broken tool made everything bad. Lily and her mommy went to bed, feeling very sad.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a wise old owl. The wise owl had a nice shelter high up in a tree. One day, a little bird came to the wise owl. The little bird was sad because he had no shelter. The wise owl and the little bird began to discuss how they could find a shelter for the little bird. They looked around the forest, but they could not find a good place for the little bird to live. The little bird was very worried and the wise owl wanted to help him. Finally, the wise owl had an idea. He asked the little bird to live with him in his shelter. The little bird was very happy and thanked the wise owl. They both lived together in the shelter and became the best of friends. And they always helped each other when they needed it.

One day, a duck named Ducky went for a walk. Ducky saw a big pond and thought it was amazing. He wanted to move close to the pond and make new friends. Ducky was very happy. Ducky met a frog named Froggy. Froggy said, "Hi, Ducky! Do you want to play with me?" Ducky said, "Yes, I want to play with you, Froggy!" So, Ducky and Froggy played near the amazing pond. While they were playing, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the pond and said, "Hello, I am Fishy!" Ducky and Froggy were surprised. They did not know fish could talk. Fishy said, "I can talk and I want to be your friend!" Now, Ducky, Froggy, and Fishy are best friends and they play near the amazing pond every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had an ordinary bowl that she loved very much. She used it every day to eat her food. One day, her friend Tim came over to play. They played with blocks, dolls, and cars. Then, it was time to eat. Sally brought out her ordinary bowl, but Tim wanted to use it too. They both wanted the bowl and started to fight over it. Sally's mom saw the fight and had an idea. She went to the big printer in the house. She printed a new bowl for Tim. It was just like Sally's ordinary bowl. Now, Sally and Tim each had a bowl. They stopped fighting and ate their food happily. From that day on, they shared everything and played together all the time.
