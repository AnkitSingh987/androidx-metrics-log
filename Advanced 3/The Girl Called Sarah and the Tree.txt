Title: The Girl Called Sarah and the Tree

Once upon a time there was a girl called Sarah. She was very persistent. One day, Sarah wanted to get a big red apple from a tree. She looked at the tree and it was very high up. Sarah thought for a while and then she got an idea. She found a ladder and put it against the tree. She climbed the ladder and asked the tree for an apple. The tree said no. Sarah was very persistent. She asked the tree again and again. Finally, the tree gave her a big red apple. Sarah was happy and climbed down the ladder with the apple. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful flower. Every day the flower grew bigger and brighter. One day, the flower began to bloom. It spread its petals high and wide and was so colourful. The little flower looked around and saw a special journal on the ground. Excited, it floated over to the journal and opened it. Inside, it found an alert bird sitting on a branch. The bird looked up and smiled. The bird asked if the flower wanted to join him on a journey. The flower replied with a shy nod, so the bird hopped onto the flower's petals and they set off. Together they visited forests, meadows and cities. Wherever they went, the flower bloomed with the most beautiful colours. They found all kinds of fun things. Before the sun went down, the bird thanked the flower for their adventure and flew away. The flower went back home and opened its journal. Inside the journal, the flower wrote all the wonderful things that happened during their journey. What a magical day!

Once upon a time, there was a big house. In the house, there was an attic. The attic was up high, and it was dark. But, it was a fun place to be. One day, a little girl named Mia went into the attic. She saw something furry in the corner. It was hiding, but Mia wanted to see what it was. She walked closer to the furry thing. Mia found out that the furry thing was a cat. The cat was scared, but Mia was nice. She gave the cat some food, and they became friends. Mia and the cat liked to be in the attic together. They had lots of fun, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a brown dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball and run around the yard. One sunny day, Max's friend, a girl named Lily, came to visit. "Hi Max!" said Lily. "I have a new toy for you!" She showed Max a shiny ball. Max was very excited and wanted to play with it. But first, Lily wanted to polish the ball to make it even shinier. Max watched as Lily carefully polished the ball until it sparkled in the sun. After playing with the shiny ball, Max and Lily went inside the house. Lily's mom had a bottle of sweet-smelling perfume on the table. Max sniffed the perfume and sneezed. "Achoo!" Max said. Lily laughed and said, "Silly Max, perfume is for smelling, not for sneezing!" Max and Lily spent the whole day playing and having fun. They loved their new shiny ball and the sweet-smelling perfume. As the sun began to set, they hugged each other and said goodbye. Max couldn't wait for the next time Lily would come to play.

Lily and Max were twins who liked to play outside. One night, they saw the moon in the sky. It was big and bright and round. "Look, Lily, the moon is smiling at us!" Max said. "Hi, moon!" Lily said. "You are very pretty. Can we be your friends?" The moon did not answer, but it seemed to wink at them. "Maybe the moon is shy," Max said. "We should be polite and say good night." "Good night, moon," Lily and Max said together. "We hope to see you again soon." They went inside and got ready for bed. Their mom tucked them in and kissed them. "Did you have fun outside?" she asked. "Yes, mom, we saw the moon and talked to it," Lily said. "We were polite and said good night," Max said. "That's very nice of you," mom said. "The moon is a good friend to have. Do you remember the song I taught you about the moon?" "Yes, mom, we remember," Lily and Max said. They sang the song softly with their mom. It went like this: I see the moon and the moon sees me God bless the moon and God bless me I love the moon and the moon loves me Good night, moon, and good night, me They finished the song and closed their eyes. They dreamed of the moon and its smile.
