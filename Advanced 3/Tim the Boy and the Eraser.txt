Title: Tim the Boy and the Eraser

One day, a boy named Tim found a big eraser. It was not like other erasers. It was shiny and clean. Tim wanted to show his friend, Sue. So, he put the eraser in his bag and went to her house. When Tim got to Sue's house, they sat in her room. Tim said, "Look what I found!" He took the eraser out of his bag. Sue looked at the eraser and said, "Wow, it is so shiny! Did you polish it?" Tim nodded and said, "Yes, I did. I used a soft cloth to make it shiny." They both smiled and played with the eraser. They liked how it felt in their hands. It was mild and nice to touch. Then, Tim and Sue used the eraser to clean their drawings. It worked very well. They were happy to have found the big, shiny eraser. They knew it was special, and they promised to take good care of it. They would polish it after each use, so it stayed shiny and clean forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a brother named Sam. Tim and Sam loved to play together. They always had so much fun. One day, Tim and Sam found a big box of colorful toys. They were very excited to play with them. The toys were red, blue, yellow, and green. They played with the toys all day long. At the end of the day, Tim and Sam were very happy. They loved their new colorful toys. They could not wait to play with them again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man. He loved his lawn and it was always shining in the sun. One day, he noticed that something was wrong because the grass wasn't shining like it usually did. He knew something had gone wrong. The bald man walked around his lawn and then he saw it. A big dark spot on the grass. When he looked closer, he saw that someone had spilled something which had made the grass not shine. The bald man was very angry. He didn't know who had made the mess, but he knew he had to clean it up. He was determined to make his lawn shine again. The bald man worked hard and he eventually managed to get the mess cleaned up. He was relieved when the sun shone on his lawn again because it was shining and beautiful. From then on, he was careful to keep his lawn clean so that it would always shine.

One day, a big, heavy truck came to deliver a big box to a small house. Inside the house, there lived a nice girl named Lily and her mom. They were very excited to see what was inside the box. Lily's mom said, "Let's open the box and see what's inside!" When they opened the box, they saw a pretty red candle with a picture of a little flame on it. Lily and her mom were happy because they loved candles. That night, Lily and her mom lit the candle. But something unexpected happened! Instead of a small flame, a big, friendly talking flame appeared! The flame said, "Hello, I am here to help you with anything you need!" Lily and her mom were surprised, but they were happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she saw something shiny in a tree. She thought it was a diamond. Lily wanted the diamond very much. Lily tried to reach the diamond, but she was too small. She thought of a plan. She went to her room and got a chair. Lily brought the chair to the tree and stood on it. She reached up, but the diamond was still too high. The next day, Lily found a big stick. She used the stick to reach the diamond. This time, she could touch it. With a big smile, Lily pulled the diamond down. It was not a diamond, but a shiny toy. Lily was happy to have a new toy to play with.
