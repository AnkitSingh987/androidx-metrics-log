Title: Rosie and Jody and the Story

Once upon a time there were two friends, Rosie and Jody. They loved playing together and one day they decided to make ice cream. They took some milk and cream and mixed it together. Then they added some yummy fruits. Jody had a funny idea to sprinkle some sugar on it. Finally they put the mixture in the freezer to make ice cream. When it was ready, the friends started eating it. But then something funny happened to the ice cream - it started melting. Rosie started getting very angry at Jody. She said, "You should have used more style when you put the sugar in! Now look what happened." Jody felt really bad but didn't say anything. The moral of the story is that it's important to be careful of something when you are with a friend. Taking care and having style in what you do will help make things work out better.

Once upon a time, there was a little elephant. The elephant had a trunk that was very tight. The trunk included a lot of things: apples, oranges, and a big rock. One day, the elephant wanted to go for a walk. But the trunk was so tight that he couldn't take a single step! The elephant asked his friend, a little lion, for help. "What can I do? My trunk is too tight," the elephant complained. The lion thought for a moment. "I know," he said. "Let me help you open it." So the lion pushed and pulled and eventually the trunk opened. "Thank you, my friend," the elephant said gratefully. The elephant was now free to go for a walk. He was so happy he could finally explore the world!

Once upon a time, there was a big cat. The cat liked to stare at things. One day, the cat saw a man with a bottle. The bottle had wine in it. The cat stared at the bottle. The man saw the cat and smiled. He said, "This wine is strong. It is not for cats." The cat did not understand the words, but it kept staring at the bottle. The man laughed and put the bottle away. Later, the cat found a toy. The toy was strong too, just like the wine. The cat played with the toy and forgot about the bottle. The cat was happy, and the man was happy too. They both had a good day.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a toy car. The car could go forward and reverse. Tim loved to play with his toy car. He had a friend named Sue. Sue was a nice girl, but she was troubled because she did not have a toy car like Tim. One day, Sue said, "Tim, can I play with your toy car?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Yes, you can play with my toy car." Sue was very happy. They played together and had lots of fun. While they were playing, the toy car got stuck in a hole. Tim and Sue were sad. They tried to get the car out, but it would not move. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's use the reverse to get the car out!" They pushed the car in reverse, and it came out of the hole. Tim and Sue were happy again. They learned that when you share and help each other, you can solve problems together.

Once upon a time there was a very brave fish. He was swimming in the ocean and felt very independent. The fish saw a reef nearby and decided to explore it. But as he got closer, he noticed something unusual. He saw a net stuck in some rocks! The fish said to himself, "That net looks like it could hurt some of the animals living here. I must support them!" He swam around and around the reef, pushing the net away. But it seemed like it wouldn't budge. Suddenly, he heard some soft voices. "Thank you friend!" they said, "We don't know how to get this net off without you!" The fish smiled. He had done something unusual, but he was glad he had been able to support the reef. And from then on, the fish made sure to look after the reef whenever he swam by.
