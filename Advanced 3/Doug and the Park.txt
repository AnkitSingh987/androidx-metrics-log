Title: Doug and the Park

Doug was a cute three year old who loved being outside. One day he wanted to go on a march with his parents. His parents were very excited for the march. "Let's go Doug!" his mom said as she took his hand and they headed outside. Doug and his parents walked and walked until they reached the park. Doug's eyes widened in surprise. There were lots of colorful firework in the sky! "Wow!" Doug exclaimed. His father warned him to be careful. "We don’t want you to get too close, Doug. Fireworks are beautiful but can be dangerous," he said. Doug nodded, understanding the importance of being careful. He took one step closer to see the fireworks better and suddenly felt scared. He wanted to march away, so he started running back to his parents. When he reached them, he told them all about how he had been brave and marched away when he felt scared. His parents beamed with pride and hugged him. Doug was so glad he had been brave and listened to his father. After that, his parents watched the fireworks with him, but they made sure to stay back far enough that they were all safe.

Once upon a time there was a pick. The pick was very mysterious. No one knew where it had come from. Every day the mysterious pick changed colour. First it was purple, then it was yellow, then it was green. One day a magical creature appeared. It was called a 'permit'. This permit allowed the pick to do something special. The pick was now able to give the people something they wanted. The people in the village were so excited. They had never seen anything like this before. They allowed the pick to give them what they wanted. The pick granted everyone their wishes. Everyone was so happy, they laughed and cheered. The mysterious pick and the permit had made everyone in the village very happy. They all thanked the magical creature for granting them their wishes.

Tom and Sue were friends who liked to play with a big ball. They would bounce the ball to each other and catch it. They had a lot of fun with the ball. One day, they saw a shiny box in the park. They ran to the box and opened it. Inside, they found many things that sparkled and glittered. They were very happy. They thought they had found a treasure. "Wow, look at all these things!" Tom said. "Let's take them home and keep them!" "No, Tom, we can't do that," Sue said. "These things belong to someone else. We have to be polite and leave them here." "But Sue, they are so pretty and shiny! We can share them and play with them!" Tom said. He did not want to be polite. He wanted to keep the treasure. He grabbed the box and ran away from Sue. Sue was sad and angry. She shouted at Tom to stop and come back. But Tom did not listen. He ran faster and faster with the box. He did not see the big hole in the ground. He tripped and fell into the hole. The box flew out of his hands and broke. The shiny things scattered everywhere. Tom hurt his head and his leg. He cried and cried. Sue heard Tom's cry and ran to the hole. She saw Tom lying in the hole, bleeding and crying. She felt sorry for him. She tried to help him out of the hole. "Tom, are you okay?" Sue asked. "I'm sorry you fell. But you should not have taken the treasure. It was not yours." "I know, Sue, I know," Tom said. "I was wrong. I was greedy and rude. I should have listened to you. You are a good friend. Can you forgive me?" Sue nodded and hugged Tom. She forgave him. But she was still sad. They had lost their ball and their treasure. And Tom had to go to the hospital. The moral of the story is: Be polite and share with others. Do not take what is not yours. Or you might lose what you have and get hurt.

One day, a group of animals were playing in the wild. They were having a lot of fun. They were running, jumping, and laughing together. The group had a big race planned, and they were all very excited. But then, the big bear said, "Wait! We must delay the race. I lost my toy and need to find it." The other animals were sad and asked, "Why do we have to delay the race?" The big bear replied, "I cannot have fun without my toy. Please help me find it." The group of animals decided to help the big bear find his toy. They looked high and low, under rocks, and in the trees. Finally, the little rabbit found the toy and gave it to the big bear. The big bear was so happy and said, "Thank you, friends! Now we can have our race." The group of animals had their race, and they all had a great time together.
