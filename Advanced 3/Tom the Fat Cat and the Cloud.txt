Title: Tom the Fat Cat and the Cloud

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play outside under the big, blue sky. One day, he saw a white cloud up in the sky. The cloud was fluffy and looked like a big ball. Tom wanted to touch the cloud, so he started to climb a tall tree. He went up, up, up the tree. But then, his mom saw him and said, "Stop, Tom! You cannot touch the cloud. It is too high!" Tom stopped and looked down. He was very high up in the tree. His mom helped him get down from the tree. Tom was sad that he could not touch the cloud. But his mom told him that he could still look at the cloud and imagine what it feels like. Tom smiled and played under the cloud all day, happy to watch it float by.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lucy. She was three years old, and she loved purple things. One day, while exploring in the garden, Lucy found a special bean. It was a purple bean! She put it in her pocket and skipped indoors to find something to do. Lucy decided to plant the purple bean in a pot. She watered it carefully every day. Soon, the bean began to grow. Lucy was so happy she danced around her kitchen singing. When the bean grew to be quite big, Lucy knew it was time to harvest the purple bean. She carefully took it out of the pot and brought it to the kitchen table. But Lucy was so excited, she started jumping and running around the kitchen. Her mom and dad had to remind Lucy to calm down and behave. After taking a few deep breaths, Lucy carefully grabbed the purple bean and went outside to plant it in the garden, just like she had done in the pot. The purple bean grew into a beautiful purple flower. Every time Lucy looked out of the window, she saw the purple flower and smiled - it was a reminder for her to always behave.

Once upon a time, there was a little squirrel who loved to sing. He would hop from tree to tree, singing at the top of his lungs. One day the little squirrel was going from branch to branch, when he stumbled upon an old nut in the middle of a leaf. He heard someone say, “That’s a hot nut!” The squirrel was curious, so he chirped, “What’s a hot nut?” The voice said, “It’s a nut that tastes really good when it’s hot! Would you like to try it?” The squirrel smiled, and said, “Yes, please!” The voice popped the hot nut in the squirrel’s mouth. It tasted so good that the squirrel started to sing. He hopped around the tree, singing and nibbling on the nut. The squirrel was so happy that he decided to share his hot nut with all the animals in the forest. He started singing, and soon all the creatures joined in, singing and dancing around the tree. When the sun set, the animals went their own ways. The squirrel was still singing, as he hopped off into the night.

Once upon a time, there was a race. Many cars were in the race. All the cars were fast and loud. One car was sorry. It was not fast. It was not loud. The sorry car wanted to win the race. The race began. All the cars went fast. The sorry car went slow. The sorry car was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The sorry car found a new way to go. It could reverse! The sorry car went reverse in the race. It went faster and faster. The other cars did not know how to reverse. They could only go forward. The sorry car went so fast in reverse, it passed all the other cars. At the end of the race, the sorry car won! It was not sorry anymore. All the other cars were happy for the sorry car. They wanted to learn how to reverse too. Now, all the cars could go forward and reverse in the race.
