Title: Sara and the Grandma

Sara and her grandma were going to the zoo. Sara was very happy. She wanted to see the lions and the monkeys and the elephants. Grandma had a ticket for Sara and a ticket for herself. She put them in her purse. They took the bus to the zoo. Sara looked out the window and saw many cars and shops and people. She asked grandma many questions. Grandma smiled and answered her. She was very kind. When they got to the zoo, they got off the bus. Grandma opened her purse to get the tickets. But she could not find them. She looked and looked, but they were not there. She felt sad and worried. She had forgotten the tickets on the bus. Sara saw that grandma was upset. She hugged her and said, "Don't be sad, grandma. It's okay. We can go back to the bus stop and look for the tickets. Maybe they are still there. Or we can do something else. I just want to be with you." Grandma felt better. She thanked Sara for being so sweet and understanding. She said, "You are a very good girl, Sara. You are right. We can try to find the tickets or we can do something else. The most important thing is that we are together. I love you very much." They held hands and walked back to the bus stop. They asked the bus driver if he had seen the tickets. He said he had. He had kept them safe for them. He gave them back to grandma. Grandma and Sara were very happy. They thanked the bus driver and went to the zoo. They had a wonderful time. The moral of the story is: Don't be angry or sad when someone forgets something. Be kind and patient. Sometimes things can be fixed. And always remember that love is more important than anything.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful little ant. The ant lived in a tiny hole in the ground. It was a cozy and safe home for the ant. One day, the ant went out to find food. The helpful ant found a big cookie. It was so big that the ant could not carry it alone. The ant saw a friend, a little bee, and said, "Will you help me take this cookie to my hole? We can eat it together." The little bee agreed, and they both worked together to carry the cookie to the ant's hole. They were happy to share the food and eat it together. The moral of the story is that when you are helpful and work together, you can achieve great things.

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there lived a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends, but sometimes they would ignore him. Spot felt sad when they did that. One day, Spot was playing with his ball near a big tree when he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was crying because she hurt her heel. Spot wanted to help, so he went to Sue and said, "Don't cry, I will help you." Sue smiled at Spot and said, "Thank you, Spot." Together, they walked slowly back to Sue's house. Spot did not ignore Sue, and Sue did not ignore Spot. From that day on, they were the best of friends, and they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big box of original toys. In her box, she had a small eraser. This eraser was her favorite toy. It was pink and soft, and it looked like a cute bunny. One day, Sue took her eraser to school. She showed it to her friends, and they all liked it too. They played with the eraser during playtime. Sue loved her eraser so much that she gave it a big kiss. After school, Sue took her eraser back home. She put it in her box with her other toys. Every day, she would take it out and play with it. And every night, she would give it a kiss before going to bed. Sue and her eraser were very happy together.
