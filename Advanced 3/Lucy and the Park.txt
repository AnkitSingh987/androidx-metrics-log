Title: Lucy and the Park

A little girl named Lucy was playing in the park when she saw a refrigerator lying in the grass. She was excited and ran to it. When she arrived, she put her hands on it and gave it a push. To her surprise, it fit perfectly through the park gate. So, Lucy decided to take it home. She pushed the refrigerator all the way to her house. When she arrived, her mom and dad were surprised to see what Lucy had found! “What is this?” asked her dad. “It’s a cool box,” said Lucy. Her mom and dad started to panic, but Lucy was excited to show them the cool refrigerator. She showed her parents how it could fit into their kitchen. They were so impressed with her find, that they decided to keep the refrigerator. From that day, Lucy and her parents were happy to have a working refrigerator in their kitchen. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little fly. This fly loved to step and dance all day long. The fly lived in a big, green garden with many friends. One sunny day, the fly was stepping and dancing when it saw a big spider web. The fly thought it would be fun to step and dance on the web, but it did not know that the web was the home of a big spider. The spider saw the fly and wanted to catch it. Just as the spider was about to catch the lively little fly, a strong wind blew and broke the web. The fly was free and flew away. The fly learned that it should not step and dance in places it does not know. The spider learned that it should not try to catch the lively little fly. From that day on, the fly and the spider became good friends and played together in the big, green garden.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ball. The big, red ball loved to play with paint. One sunny day, the big, red ball went outside to paint a picture. The big, red ball painted a big, colorful rainbow. The big, red ball was very happy. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. The big, red ball saw a big, blue ball rolling towards it. The big, blue ball also loved to paint. The big, red ball and the big, blue ball decided to paint together. They painted a big, beautiful picture of a big, happy sun. The big, red ball and the big, blue ball became best friends, and they loved to paint together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She loved to march around the house and pretend she was a soldier. One day, Jane heard singing and strumming coming from the garden. She peered out the window and saw a person with a guitar singing and playing. Jane was so curious, she decided to march outside and meet the person. When Jane arrived, she saw that the person was an old man. He had an icy look in his eyes and he seemed very sad. Jane said, “Hello. What’s your name?” The old man smiled at Jane and said, “My name is Robert, and I was just playing my guitar. Would you like me to play a song for you?” Jane said, “Yes, please!” So Robert started to play a beautiful song on his guitar and Jane marched around the garden in time with the music. She smiled and sang along, and eventually the ice melted away from Robert’s eyes. Jane was happy to have met Robert, and she waved goodbye as she marched back home. That night, Jane laid in bed and felt peaceful, listening to the memory of Robert’s icy guitar song.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toys in the living room. One day, they see a big bottle of lotion on the table. It is cheap lotion that their mom uses for her dry skin. "Look, Lily, lotion!" Tom says. He grabs the bottle and opens the cap. "What is lotion, Tom?" Lily asks. She is curious. "Lotion is good for your skin. It makes it soft and smooth. Watch me!" Tom says. He squeezes the bottle and starts to rub lotion all over his face and arms. Lily laughs. She thinks Tom looks funny with lotion on his face. She wants to try too. She takes the bottle from Tom and starts to put lotion on her hair and legs. "Stop, Lily, stop!" Tom says. He is angry. He wants the lotion back. "You are wasting the lotion! Mom will be mad!" "No, Tom, no!" Lily says. She is happy. She likes the lotion. "You had enough lotion! It is my turn!" Tom and Lily start to fight over the bottle. They pull and push and shout. The lotion spills on the floor and the couch and the rug. They make a big mess. Mom hears the noise and comes to the living room. She sees Tom and Lily covered in lotion and the living room in a mess. She is shocked and angry. "Tom! Lily! What have you done?" Mom says. She takes the bottle from them and puts it away. "This is cheap lotion, not toy lotion! You are not supposed to play with it! You are supposed to use it only a little bit!" Tom and Lily feel sorry. They did not know they were doing something bad. They say sorry to Mom. "Sorry, Mom," Tom says. "Sorry, Mom," Lily says. Mom sighs. She loves Tom and Lily, but they are very naughty sometimes. She takes them to the bathroom and starts to wash the lotion off them. She hopes they will learn their lesson.
