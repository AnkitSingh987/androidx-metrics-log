Title: The Boy Called John and the Orange

Once there was a little boy called John. John was very lonely. He didn't have any friends and he was sad all the time. He loved oranges, so one day he picked an orange from a tree and he was so happy. He held the orange close, and it made him feel better. He looked around, trying to find someone to share his orange with. But all he saw were strangers. Suddenly, he saw another little boy. John was so surprised! The other boy smiled at John and asked if he could have some of the orange. John was so happy that he had found a friend. He gave some of the orange to the other boy and they both ate it together. They talked and played until it was dark. John was so happy. “Thank you for being my friend,” he said. The other boy said it was okay, and smiled. “Let's forgive and forget,” he said. John understood. He hugged his new friend and now he was never lonely again. He knew he had been forgiven and that was the best gift he had ever received.

Once upon a time, there was a group of five friends. They liked to play in the park every day. One day, they saw a big, dull-colored dog. The dog looked sad and lonely. "Let's play with the dog," said one friend. So, they went to the dog and started to play. They threw a ball, and the dog ran to get it. They were all having fun. But then, the dog did something bad. It tried to bite one of the friends. The friend screamed, "Ouch! The dog bit me!" The other friends got scared. They all decided to stay away from the dog. They didn't want to get bitten, too. The dog was sad, but it knew that it shouldn't bite. The friends went back to playing, but they were more careful around dogs now. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little helicopter was feeling gloomy. It was sad because it was dirty and needed a wash. It asked its friend, the big truck, "Can you help me wash?" The big truck said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's wash you now." So they went to the water and started to wash the little helicopter. The helicopter started to feel happy again. But then, a mean car came and splashed mud all over the helicopter. The helicopter was dirty and sad again. The big truck tried to help, but it was too late. The mean car had made the little helicopter very dirty. The helicopter tried to fly, but it was too heavy with all the mud. It fell down and broke. The big truck was sad and angry at the mean car. But there was nothing they could do. The little helicopter could not fly anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to run and play in the park with his friends. One day, he saw a small bird with a hurt wing. Max wanted to help the bird, so he hurried to find a bandage. Max looked everywhere for a bandage. He looked in the trees, the grass, and even in the pond. But he could not find a bandage. Max felt sad because he wanted to help the bird very much. In the end, Max could not find a bandage for the bird. The bird's wing stayed hurt, and it could not fly with its friends. Max felt very sad that he could not help the bird. The bird had to stay on the ground, and Max went home with a heavy heart.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. Tim was very excited to play in the sand and water. He was also very hungry, so his mom gave him a sandwich to eat while they sat by the water. As Tim ate his sandwich, he saw a jellyfish in the water. The jellyfish was also hungry. Tim leaned over to his mom and said, "Mommy, can I give the jellyfish some of my sandwich?" His mom smiled and said, "No, Tim. Jellyfish don't eat sandwiches. They eat tiny things in the water." Tim watched the jellyfish swim away, and he finished his sandwich. He was happy to play at the beach and see the hungry jellyfish.
