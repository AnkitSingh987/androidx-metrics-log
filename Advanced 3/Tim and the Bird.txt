Title: Tim and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a seat under a big tree. Tim felt tired, so he sat on the seat to rest. He looked around and saw many pretty flowers and birds. Suddenly, Tim saw a small bird stuck in a trap. The bird was sad and scared. Tim felt bad for the bird and wanted to help. He opened the trap and the bird was free. The bird was very happy and flew away. Tim felt good for helping the bird, but he also felt a little ashamed. He knew that traps could hurt animals. Tim decided to tell his friends not to use traps anymore. From that day on, Tim and his friends promised to be kind to all animals.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a happy boy, but he had a big worry. Every night, he had a bad dream called a nightmare. It made him feel hurt inside. One day, Tim told his mom about his worry. He said, "Mommy, I have a nightmare every night and it makes me feel hurt." His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you chase the nightmare away." That night, Tim's mom gave him a special teddy bear. She said, "This teddy bear will help you feel safe and not hurt. It will chase your nightmare away." Tim held the teddy bear tight and went to sleep. The teddy bear made Tim feel safe, and he did not have any more nightmares. He was not worried anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to help her daddy in the kitchen. One day, daddy was making a cake for mommy's birthday. Daddy said, "Lily, can you please pour the milk into the bowl?" Lily was very careful when she poured the milk. She did not want to spill it. "Good job, Lily!" said daddy with a big smile. When the cake was done, Lily and daddy put candles on the cake. They sang "Happy Birthday" to mommy. Mommy was so happy to see the cake that Lily helped make. They all ate the cake and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a wild soldier. He lived in a big forest. He liked to play with the animals and the trees. The wild soldier was very strong and brave. One day, the wild soldier went to the town. He wanted to buy some food. He saw a big store with lots of yummy things. He picked up some apples and some bread. Then, he went to the person at the front of the store to pay. But the wild soldier did not have any money. The person at the front of the store got very mad. The wild soldier was sad and he had to leave the store without any food. He walked back to the big forest, feeling very hungry and sad.

One day, a smelly dog named Fido went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to pee on it. But when he lifted his leg, he slipped and fell into a hole. Fido felt embarrassed. In the hole, Fido found a small cell. Inside the cell, there was a little bird. The bird said, "Hello, I am Chirpy. I am stuck in this cell. Can you help me get out?" Fido wanted to help Chirpy. Fido and Chirpy worked together to open the cell. They pushed and pushed until the door opened. Chirpy was free and thanked Fido. They both climbed out of the hole and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a great toy named Mr. Bear. Amy and Mr. Bear loved to play together. They would play all day and have lots of fun. One day, Amy was very sad. She had lost her favorite top. She looked everywhere, but she could not find it. Mr. Bear saw that Amy was sad, so he wanted to comfort her. He gave her a big hug and said, "Don't worry, Amy. We will find your top." Amy and Mr. Bear looked all around the house. They looked under the bed, behind the couch, and even in the kitchen. Then, they finally found the top in the toy box. Amy was so happy! She hugged Mr. Bear and said, "Thank you for helping me find my top." From that day on, Amy and Mr. Bear were even better friends. They knew that they could always help each other and have a great time together. And they lived happily ever after.
