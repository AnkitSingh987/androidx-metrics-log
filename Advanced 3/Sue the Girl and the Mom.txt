Title: Sue the Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to bake with her mom. One day, they decided to make a cake. They wanted it to be a perfect cake. Sue and her mom mixed the cake and put it in the oven. They waited and waited. When the cake was done, they took it out to cool. Sue was so excited to see the cake. It looked perfect. Sue and her mom put icing on the cake. They added pretty colors and fun shapes. It was time to eat the cake. They cut the cake and took a bite. It was so yummy. Sue was happy. The cake was perfect and the day had a happy end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big book with many pages. He loved to read the book every day. One day, he found a page that made him nervous. On the page, there was a picture of a tall tree. Tim was scared of tall trees. The next day, Tim went to the park with his mom. In the park, Tim saw a tall tree just like the one on the page. He was nervous, but he wanted to be brave. Tim's mom said, "It's okay, I will stand with you." Tim and his mom stood by the tall tree. Tim looked up and saw a cute squirrel in the tree. The squirrel was eating a nut. Tim smiled and was not nervous anymore. He was happy he stood by the tree with his mom. From that day on, Tim was not scared of tall trees anymore. He liked to stand by them and watch the squirrels. The page in his book did not make him nervous anymore. Tim learned that sometimes, things are not as scary as they seem.

Tommy was very excited to go to the park with his dad. As they walked along the path, Tommy spotted something very big and purple in the distance. "What is that, Daddy?" Tommy asked curiously. "That's a giant and he's a very friendly giant. He likes to show children new and exciting things," his dad replied. Tommy and his dad continued on their way and Tommy kept glancing back at the giant in the distance, hoping they'd cross paths. Later that day, Tommy and dad were walking back when they saw the giant again. "Daddy, will we miss the giant?" Tommy asked with reluctance. "Not today, Tommy. We'll always have the memory of the giant in our hearts," his dad replied with a smile. Tommy realized that although the giant was far away, just the thought of him was enough to bring happiness to his heart. That moment, Tommy also learned that sometimes happy memories are all we need to feel content.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Jane. Jane was three years old and loved to play in the backyard. One day when she was playing, she found a box. She was so excited and ran to show it to her mommy. She yelled "Look Mommy!" Her mommy smiled and said what a sweet surprise the box was. Jane opened the box to find a sweet toy inside. She was so happy she started to yell with joy. Jane played with the toy in the backyard for hours. She was so excited to have a new toy that she never wanted it to end.

One day, a little girl named Jane saw a pretty, yellow flower in her yard. She liked it so much, she wanted to make more flowers grow. Jane asked her mom, "Can we make more yellow flowers grow in our yard?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can! We need to plant seeds and water them. It will take a week for them to grow." Jane was excited. She and her mom planted seeds in the yard and watered them. They waited a week, and then something unexpected happened. Instead of yellow flowers, big, green plants started to grow. They were not pretty flowers, but Jane and her mom were surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. Even though they did not get yellow flowers, Jane and her mom had fun watching the big, green plants grow. They learned that sometimes, unexpected things can happen, and that's okay.
