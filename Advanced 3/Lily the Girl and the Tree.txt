Title: Lily the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the woods. She saw a big house. A witch lived in the house. Lily was not scared of the witch. She knew the witch was nice. The witch said, "Hello, Lily! Do you want a tasty treat?" Lily said, "Yes, please!" The witch gave Lily a big, tasty cookie. Lily loved the cookie. She said, "Thank you, witch!" and bowed to her. Later, Lily went back to the woods. She saw a big tree. The tree had a note. The note said, "Bow to the tree, and you will see something fun!" Lily remembered to bow, like she did for the witch. She bowed to the tree. The tree opened a secret door. Lily went inside and found a fun playroom. She played all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a big wood, there lived a small bird named Timmy. Timmy was a careful bird. One day, Timmy met a big dog named Max. They became good friends. Timmy told Max, "You need to be careful in the wood. There are big rocks and you might fall." Max said, "Thank you, Timmy. I will be careful." One day, Max saw a big rock, but he forgot to be careful. He fell down and got hurt. Timmy heard Max call for help. Timmy flew to Max as fast as he could. Timmy said, "I told you to be careful, Max." Max said, "You were right, Timmy. I should have been careful." Timmy helped Max get up, and they walked home together. Max promised to always be careful in the wood from that day on.

Once there was a girl. She went to the store and saw a shelf full of toys. She was so excited that she wanted to grab them all. But then she saw one toy that she really liked. The girl asked the shopkeeper if she could have the toy. The shopkeeper smiled and offered it to the girl. She was so happy and took the toy. The shopkeeper was pleased and he told her to take two more toys if she wanted. The girl thanked him and chose two more toys. The shopkeeper was very calm and said it was ok. She put the toys in her bag and walked out of the shop. She was so happy that she had three new toys to play with.

Once upon a time, in a small wooden house, there lived two sisters, Mina and Lily. Everyday, their mother would ask them to help in the kitchen. Today was different, though. Their mother asked them to fetch some oil from the grocer's. Mina and Lily decided to go, but they did not know how to buy the oil. On their way, Mina said to Lily, “Let’s ask the grocer. He will know what to do.” At the grocer’s store, they asked the owner. The grocer said, “Wait here. I will get the oil for you.” The owner brought a bottle of oil and asked if they could pay him. Mina and Lily did not have any money, so they looked embarrassed. The grocer smiled and said, “Don’t worry. Tell your mother that I said this is a special gift from me.” Mina and Lily thanked the grocer and ran back home. When they reached home, they told their mother about the gift. Their mother smiled and said, “You girls did a great job! See, I told you that you are able to do anything if you put your mind to it.” Mina and Lily were so excited and happy!

Henry was a very intelligent three-year-old boy. He loved being in the kitchen helping his mom bake cookies and cakes. He was the best helper ever! One day in the kitchen, Henry found a mysterious box. His mom said it was a special surprise for him. Henry wanted to open the box and see what was inside. But his mom said he first needed to give it a kiss. Henry put his lips to the box and made a big smooching sound. Suddenly, the box opened to reveal a stuffed toy cat! The cat had a big smile on its face and Henry was so happy. The surprise was intelligent - it responded to Henry's kisses! Every time he kissed his new toy, it made purring sounds. Henry and the toy cat were best friends. They would spend hours and hours in the kitchen together.
