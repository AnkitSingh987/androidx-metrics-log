Title: Sam and Ella and the Toys

Once upon a time, there were two friends: Sam and Ella. They were playing together in the park when suddenly a big flood came and ruined their toys. They were so sad! Sam looked at Ella and said, "Ella, it's not fair. Our toys are ruined!" But Ella had a very patient smile and said, "It's ok Sam, things can be fixed. Let's forgive the flood and find something else to play with." Sam thought for a minute and then he smiled too. Together they looked around the park and found some leaves, sticks and flowers to use as new toys. They were happy as they enjoyed their new game. Soon they forgot about the flood. The friends were so kind and patient that they taught us that even when bad things happen, we can always find a way to make things better.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a book that he loved very much. One day, Tim left his book outside in the rain. The book got wet and began to rot. Tim felt helpless because he didn't know what to do. Tim's mom saw him looking sad and asked, "Tim, what's wrong?" Tim told her about his book and how it was rotting. His mom said, "Don't worry, we can fix it together." So, Tim and his mom worked hard to dry the book and make it better. In the end, Tim's book was not perfect, but he was happy because he learned that asking for help can fix things that seem helpless. The moral of the story is: don't be afraid to ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dependable armchair. It was very cozy and soft. The armchair lived in a small house with a nice family. The family loved to sit on the armchair and read books together. One day, it rained a lot outside. The family's clothes got very wet. So, they put their clothes on the armchair to dry. The armchair was happy to help the family. But then, something unexpected happened! The armchair started to move and talk. It said, "Hello, family! I am a magic armchair!" The family was very surprised. The magic armchair helped the family with many things, like drying their clothes and cleaning the house. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Wednesday. Wednesday was a mild cat who liked to play with her friends. She had a soft, gray fur and big, green eyes. One day, Wednesday went outside to play. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. But as she climbed, she fell and hurt her paw. Wednesday started to suffer and cry. Her friends heard her cry and came to help her. They took her to her mom, who hugged her and made her feel better. Wednesday learned to be careful when climbing trees from that day on.

Once upon a time, there was a big office. In the office, many people worked together. They all liked their office because it was interesting. There were many colors and fun things to see. One day, a little cat came into the office. The cat was very curious. It walked around the office and looked at all the interesting things. The cat saw a tall chair and decided to lean on it. The chair was very big, so the cat had to stretch to lean on it. The people in the office saw the cat and smiled. They liked the cat and wanted it to stay. The cat liked the office too, and it stayed with the people. They all had fun together in the interesting office with the little cat that liked to lean on things.

Once upon a time, there was an old gate. It was in a big yard. A boy named Tim lived in the house with the yard. Tim had a dog named Max. They liked to play ball in the yard. One day, Tim and Max were playing ball. Max ran to the old gate. He raised his paw and hit the ball. The ball went over the gate! Tim and Max looked at each other. They wanted to get the ball back. They went to the old gate and opened it. They saw a big, green field! It was not there before. Tim and Max were surprised. They went in to find their ball. They played in the big, green field all day. The old gate had a magic field behind it. Tim and Max were very happy.
