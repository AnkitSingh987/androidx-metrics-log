Title: The Group of Friends and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big group of friends. They decided that they wanted to go on an adventure. So they decided to follow a leader. The leader said, “Let’s go this way. It’s hot outside, but I know a cool spot.” The group nodded and followed her. The leader took them to a big open field. It was really hot, but there was a tall tree in the corner. The leader said, “Let’s cool off here. We can relax in the shade.” The group thanked the leader for leading them to the cool spot. They all sat in the shade and laughed together. It was one of the best days ever. The group was so glad that they had followed their leader on their adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to make things. One day, he got the urge to make a big tower with his toy blocks. Tim started to build the tower. He put one block on top of another. He made the tower taller and taller. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Wow, Tim! You are making a big tower!" Tim felt happy and continued to build. He added more and more blocks. But then, the tower started to shake. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim! The tower might fall!" But Tim did not stop. He wanted the tower to be the tallest. Suddenly, the tower fell down with a big crash. Tim felt sad, but his mom said, "It's okay, Tim. You can always make another tower." Tim smiled and started to build again. This time, he would make the tower even better.

There was once a great girl who wanted to act in a play. Whenever she tried, she was very shy and made mistakes. One day her teacher gave her a cube and told her to practice with it. "Every time you act with the cube, you’ll be less and less shy. Then you can perform in the play!" said the teacher. The girl took the cube home and practiced with it everywhere she went. She acted and pretended, and the more she used the cube, the better she felt. The day of the play finally came. The girl paced nervously, and then she remembered the cube. Holding it tightly, she took a deep breath and took the stage. Her performance was great! Everyone clapped and cheered and the girl smiled with pride. "See," said her teacher. "I told you the cube would help!"

Colin was feeling very dull. He had been staring at numbers all day. He was learning to count and the numbers on the page of his book were making him tired. When his mom came home, she had lots of groceries. She asked Colin if he wanted to come to the grocery store with her. Colin instantly felt better and said yes. At the grocery store, Colin was in awe of all the colors and smells and shapes. He counted the different types of cereal, the containers of cookies and the bags of chips. His mom laughed as he ran around counting everything in sight. When they were ready to go home, Colin was exhausted but also excited. He had counted and tugged on so many things! He loved going to the grocery store and he couldn't wait to go back.

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman named Tom. He loved to catch fish in the big blue sea. One day, he saw a big fish with a shiny tail. Tom thought, "I can imagine how happy I will be if I catch that fish!" Tom tried to catch the big fish. But the fish was very smart. The fish swam away fast. Tom did not give up. He tried again and again. He was very tired, but he kept trying. Then, Tom saw something in the water. It was a small, disgusting fish. The fish was dirty and not pretty like the big fish. But the little fish looked sad. Tom felt bad for the little fish. He decided to help the little fish instead of catching the big fish. Tom cleaned the little fish and let it go back to the sea. The little fish was very happy. It thanked Tom and swam away with a big smile. Tom felt happy too. He learned that helping others is more important than getting what you want. And that was the best catch of the day.
