Title: The Rich Man and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a rich man. He had a big egg. He loved to watch the egg spin. One day, he met a little boy. The little boy looked at the egg and said, "Wow! Can I see it spin?" The rich man smiled and said, "Yes, you can." He held the egg in his hand and made it spin. The little boy clapped his hands and laughed. "Spin, spin, spin!" he shouted. The rich man and the little boy played with the spinning egg all day. As the sun went down, the little boy said, "Thank you for letting me play with your egg." The rich man smiled and gave the egg to the little boy. "Now, you can spin it whenever you want," he said. The little boy hugged the rich man and ran home to show his mom the spinning egg. They all lived happily ever after.

It was a beautiful spring day in the valley and John and his mother were out exploring together. As they walked they talked and soon they noticed a majestic maze perched atop a hill. It was a graceful structure that seemed to welcome them. John's mother asked him, "John, do you want to borrow the maze?" John was excited and nodded enthusiastically. So they made their way up the hill and soon were standing in front of the maze. John was amazed by the big walls and thought it was so fun to explore. His mother was watching him with a fond smile. "Remember to stay on the path, my love," she said. John agreed, and then he started imagining all the adventures he could have in the maze. He felt a sense of anticipation as he wondered what he would discover inside.

Once upon a time, there was a high tree in a big farm. Under the tree, there was a lot of corn. Every day, the animals came to eat the corn. One day, a small bird saw the corn and said, "Wow! Look at all the corn! Let's eat!" The other animals heard the bird and came to eat the corn too. They were all very happy. As they ate the corn, the animals started to dance and sing. The bird sang so well that the other animals began to applaud. They clapped their hands and paws and said, "Good job, bird! We like your song!" The bird felt very proud and happy. From that day on, the animals ate corn, sang, and danced under the high tree every day. They all lived happily ever after.

One day, a man and his wife went for a walk. They saw a big black dog. The wife said, "I don't like that dog. He looks mean." The man said, "Don't worry, I will keep you safe." As they walked, the black dog followed them. The man tried to snap his fingers to scare the dog away, but the dog did not go. The wife said, "I am scared. Let's go home." They ran home, but the black dog was still behind them. When they got home, the man tried to snap his fingers again, but the dog did not leave. The wife said, "We need help." But no one came to help them. The black dog stayed at their home, and they were scared all the time. The man and his wife were not happy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little house. In this house lived a mommy, a daddy, and a little girl named Lily. They all loved to eat yummy food, and Lily loved to help cook. One day, mommy was cooking dinner and Lily wanted to help. Mommy said, "Lily, you can help me split the peas for dinner." Lily was very happy and started to split the peas. While they were cooking, mommy told Lily, "After we eat, you can help me put the dishes in the dishwasher." Lily was excited to help. When dinner was over, Lily remembered what mommy said. She helped mommy put the dishes in the dishwasher. As they closed the dishwasher, mommy said, "Thank you, Lily, for being such a big help today." Lily smiled and felt proud of herself.
