Title: Sue the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a new toy. It was a big, red ball. Sue loved to play with her ball outside in the sun. One day, Sue got sick. Her mom gave her medicine to help her feel better. Sue did not like the taste of the medicine, but she knew it would help her get well. After a few days, Sue felt better. She went outside to play with her ball again. While she was playing, her foot began to slip on the wet grass. Sue was careful and did not fall. She was happy to be playing with her new ball again.

Once upon a time, there was a kitty called Milly. She was very nice and sweet. Every morning, Milly would wake up in her cozy bed. She was very happy and always meowed in the mornings when she woke up. Today, however, Milly had a different problem. Her paw hurt her badly. She tried to meow, but it hurt too much. So, Milly's mum came up to her. She said, "Oh my, Milly. What has happened? Does it hurt?" Milly just nodded sadly. Her mum stroked her fur and asked, "What do you need, Milly? Do you need a hug?" Milly shook her head no. Instead, she meowed again. Her mum knew exactly what to do. She went to the kitchen and came back with a jar of cream. She took out a bit of the cream and rubbed it gently on Milly's paw. The cream felt cool and nice and Milly was happy again. She meowed a few times in relief and thanked her mum for helping her out. Since then, she always kept the cream by her bed, just in case.

Once upon a time, there was a big wall. A sad boy named Tim lived near the wall. He had no friends to play with. Every day, he would sit by the wall and feel very sad. One day, Tim saw a big bug near the wall. The bug wanted to fight. Tim was scared, but he knew he had to be brave. So, Tim and the bug started to fight. They pushed and pulled each other. Then, something unexpected happened. As they fought, they started to laugh and have fun. The fight turned into a fun game. Tim and the bug became friends. They played by the wall every day, and Tim was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy chicken. The hairy chicken lived on a farm. The farm had many animals. The other animals did not like the hairy chicken. One day, the animals started to tease the hairy chicken. They laughed at the chicken's hair. The hairy chicken felt sad. The hairy chicken wanted to be friends with the other animals. The hairy chicken tried to play with the other animals. But they kept teasing the hairy chicken. The hairy chicken ran away from the farm. The hairy chicken was lost and alone. The hairy chicken never found its way back to the farm.

One day, a little boy named Tim was given a task by his mom. She asked him to pick up all his toys and put them away. Tim was happy to help his mom and started picking up his toys one by one. As Tim was picking up his toys, he saw his friend, Tom, outside. Tom called to Tim, "Hey Tim, come play with me!" Tim wanted to play, but he knew he had a task to do. He told Tom, "I have to finish my task first, then I can play." While Tim was putting away his toys, he tripped on a toy car and started to fall. Tim felt embarrassed as he fell to the floor. But he got up, brushed himself off, and continued to put his toys away. After Tim finished his task, he went outside to play with Tom. They played together and had lots of fun. Tim was glad he finished his task before playing, and he knew that his mom was proud of him.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow taxi. The taxi liked to hurry all day. It would go fast on the road, taking people where they wanted to go. One day, the taxi met a big, angry dog. The dog did not like the taxi. It barked and barked at the taxi. The taxi was scared and did not know what to do. The taxi decided to hurry away from the angry dog. It went very fast, and soon the dog was far away. The taxi was happy again, and went back to helping people. And the taxi and the people lived happily ever after.
