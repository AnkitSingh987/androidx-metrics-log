Title: The Beautiful Bird Was Flying and the Squirrel

One day, a beautiful bird was flying near a big tree. In the tree, there was a trunk. The bird saw the trunk and wanted to look inside. The bird was very curious. As the bird looked inside the trunk, it found a shiny thing. It was a record! The bird picked up the record with its beak and showed it to a squirrel nearby. The squirrel said, "Wow! That is a nice find! Let's play it and see what it sounds like." They put the record on a player they found in the trunk. The beautiful music started to play. The bird and the squirrel danced and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The music changed and made everyone stop dancing. The animals heard a voice from the record say, "Hello, friends! I am a magic record. I will grant you one wish." The bird and the squirrel looked at each other in surprise. They thought hard about their wish. Finally, they wished for a big party with all their friends. The magic record made their wish come true. All the animals in the forest came to the party and danced to the beautiful music. The bird and the squirrel were very happy.

Joey was out in the garden, when he noticed something strange. A pumpkin was dancing around in circles, spinning across the grass. Joey was amazed and just had to take a closer look. He walked over to the dancing pumpkin, and noticed something else; it was deaf! Joey didn't know how a deaf pumpkin knew how to dance, and he wanted to know more. He called out to the dancing pumpkin, but it didn't stop. It just kept dancing around and around. Joey tried again and louder this time, but nothing still. He thought for a minute about how he could get the deaf pumpkin to stop dancing. With his fingers, he started to make little dances in the dirt. To his surprise, the deaf pumpkin stopped dancing and looked his way. Joey asked the deaf pumpkin why it was dancing, and the pumpkin answered. "Dancing is my way of speaking. It's how I communicate, how I share my feelings. It's how I'm heard and seen." Joey was so excited to know that he could communicate with the deaf pumpkin. He asked it to tell him even more, and together they shared stories as they danced in the garden.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and make things with paper and tape. On Wednesday, they have a big idea. They want to wrap their toys and give them to each other as presents. They find some paper and tape in the craft box. They pick their favorite toys and start to wrap them. Sara wraps her doll in a pink paper. Ben wraps his car in a blue paper. They are very careful and use a lot of tape. When they are done, they hide their presents under the bed. They wait for their mom to come home. They want to surprise her with their presents. Mom comes home and sees the twins smiling. She asks them what they are up to. They say, "We have a surprise for you, Mom. Come and see!" They take her to their room and show her their presents. They say, "We wrapped our toys for each other. We want to share and be nice. Can we open them now, Mom?" Mom hugs them and says, "You are very sweet and thoughtful, my darlings. Of course you can open them. But first, let me take a picture of you and your presents. You did a great job wrapping them, even if the paper is a bit thin and torn." Sara and Ben are happy. They open their presents and hug each other. They say, "Thank you, thank you, thank you!" They play with their new toys and share them with each other. Mom watches them and smiles. She thinks, "This is the best Wednesday ever."

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, Spot found a big box of paint. Spot had an idea. He wanted to attach his ball to a stick. He put the paint on the ball and then rolled it on the ground. The ball left pretty colors behind it. Spot was very happy with his new toy. He played with it all day long. His friends saw him and wanted to play too. So, they all played with the paint and made beautiful pictures on the ground. The end.
