Title: Lily the Girl and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Lily found a notebook. It was a brilliant notebook with many colors. Lily loved to draw, so she was very happy. She wanted to show her friends the notebook, so they could draw together. Lily went to her friends and showed them the notebook. They were excited and started to draw. They drew a big sun, a blue sky, and green grass. Lily encouraged her friends to draw more and more. They had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The drawings in the notebook came to life! The big sun, the blue sky, and the green grass were now real. Lily and her friends were surprised and happy. They played in their new world all day, and they knew that their brilliant notebook was magical.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an unusual factory. This factory made toys for kids. One day, a young girl named Lily went to the factory with her mom. Inside the factory, Lily saw a big, red ball. She wanted to play with it. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, can I play with the ball, please?" Her mom said, "No, Lily. We cannot play in the factory." Lily felt sad but she listened to her mom. They walked around the factory and saw many toys. Then, Lily saw a little door. She opened it and went inside. It was dark and scary. Lily started to cry. Her mom heard her cry and found her. She said, "Lily, you should not go into places you do not know. It is not safe." They left the factory and went home. Lily did not get a new toy and she was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy bear. He lived deep in the woods in a warm, comfy den. He ate and drank, slept and snuggled, but he never ever went out and played. One day, the bear's friend, a fox, came to visit. She said, "Come on, bear! Come out and play!" But the bear just shook his head and said, "No, I don't feel like it." The fox looked sad, so the bear said, "I'm sorry. But I'm just so lazy!" The fox laughed and said, "Well, you better stay lazy then — but one day, your laziness will cause you pain!" The bear shrugged, not understanding what she meant. The fox said goodbye and sadly walked away. The days passed and the bear stayed lazier and lazier. He didn't listen to the fox, and he still never went out and played. One day, he finally decided to go for a walk. But as he stepped outside, a pain appeared in his leg — one that he couldn't ignore. The bear realized that the fox was right — his laziness had caused him pain. So he stopped being so lazy, and he learned to be active. The moral of the story is if you stay lazy, you might end up with pain — so get up and get moving!

Anna likes to print pictures with her crayons. She prints flowers and trees and animals on big sheets of paper. She prints a picture for her mom and dad and puts it on the fridge. She is very proud of her prints. One day, Anna wants to print a picture for her friend Ben, who is sick at home. She prints a big sun and a rainbow and a happy face. She writes "Get well soon, Ben" with her letters. She puts the picture in an envelope and asks her mom to take her to Ben's house. When they get to Ben's house, Anna rings the doorbell and waits. Ben's mom opens the door and smiles. She says, "Hello, Anna. What a nice surprise. Do you have something for Ben?" Anna nods and gives her the envelope. She says, "I print a picture for Ben. I hope he likes it." Ben's mom thanks Anna and invites her and her mom inside. She says, "Ben is in his room. He is feeling a little better today, but he is still tired and sad. He would love to see you." Anna and her mom follow Ben's mom to his room. Anna sees Ben lying on his bed, with a blanket and a teddy bear. He looks pale and bored. He sees Anna and his eyes light up. He says, "Hi, Anna. I'm so happy you came. What do you have for me?" Anna gives him the picture and says, "I print this for you. It is a sun and a rainbow and a happy face. I want you to be happy and well." Ben opens the picture and smiles. He says, "Wow, Anna. This is beautiful. Thank you so much. You are very compassionate. You are my best friend." Anna hugs Ben and says, "You are welcome, Ben. You are my best friend too. I hope you get better soon. I miss you at school." Ben hugs Anna back and says, "I miss you too, Anna. I hope I can come back soon. You make me feel better." Anna and Ben talk and laugh and play for a while, until it is time for Anna and her mom to go. They say goodbye and promise to see each other again. Anna feels happy and proud. She thinks, "I like to print pictures. I like to make people happy. I like to be compassionate."
