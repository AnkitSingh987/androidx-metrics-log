Title: The Story of the Door and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a small, red door with a shiny knob. Behind the door lived a funny, little man who loved to joke. Every day, he would open the door and make the children laugh with his silly jokes. One day, the little man found his shiny knob was filthy. He didn't like that at all! So, he got a soft cloth and some soap. He rubbed and scrubbed the knob until it was shiny and clean again. The next day, the little man opened the door and told the children a new joke. They laughed and clapped their hands. They were happy to see the shiny knob and the funny, little man. And they all played and laughed together in the sun.

Billy was very excited about painting. He gathered all of his supplies including the new painting set he just got for his birthday. When he found the perfect place to paint outside, an empty field, he was so happy he ran to it. "Can I look for an insect?" Billy asked his mom, eagerly. "Of course you can sweetie," replied his mom. Billy spent hours searching for a cool insect. He had never seen an insect as special as the one he finally discovered and carefully placed it in a container. He ran back to his painting place, excited to start his masterpiece. He spread the paint all over the paper. Slowly he created a painting of the special insect he found. He couldn't wait to show his mom. By the time he showed her, the painting was complete. His mom was impressed and could see how he had been thinking of the insect he found. Billy was proud of his work and was so excited to have painted something so special in the empty field.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who loved to sign. Every day he would go out to the park where he had seen a really pretty grill. He was so excited to try it out. He had been watching the older kids in the park, and they all looked like they had so much fun grilling. The little boy kept asking his parents if he could grill, but they wouldn't let him. So, he thought of a way to prove them wrong. He decided to sign up for a grilling class! He was so excited. The little boy was doing so well in the grilling class, but just as he was about to finish the last lesson he had a terrible accident. A lit piece of coal went flying, and it burnt his arm. He was in terrible pain and he couldn't move. The last thing he saw was the pretty grill. The bad ending of this story is that the little boy was so badly injured from the burnt coal that he never got to grill again. He was forever reminded of what could have been, and all he had to show for it was a scar on his arm.

Lily was a thoughtful girl who liked to make gifts for her friends. She had a big calendar on her wall, where she wrote the names of her friends and their birthdays. She wanted to make them happy on their special days. One day, she saw a new name on her calendar. It was Ben, who had just moved to her street. She did not know him, but she wanted to be his friend. She thought of what she could make for him. She decided to draw a picture of his house and his family, and write "Welcome" on it. She took her crayons and paper, and went to his house. She knocked on the door, and a woman opened it. She smiled at Lily and said, "Hello, who are you?" Lily said, "I am Lily, I live on this street. I came to introduce myself and give you a gift." She showed her the picture she had drawn. The woman said, "Oh, how thoughtful of you, Lily! Thank you so much! This is a beautiful picture. Come in, I want you to meet Ben. He is in his room, playing with his toys." Lily followed the woman to Ben's room. She saw a boy with brown hair and blue eyes, sitting on the floor with a train set. He looked up and saw Lily and his mom. The woman said, "Ben, this is Lily. She lives on our street. She made you a picture of our house and our family. Isn't that nice?" Ben said, "Wow, thank you, Lily! That is very nice of you. Do you want to play with me?" Lily said, "Yes, I do. I like trains. Can I see yours?" Ben said, "Sure, come on. I have a lot of them. You can be the conductor." Lily and Ben smiled at each other, and started to play. They had a lot of fun, and became good friends. Lily was happy that she had introduced herself to Ben, and Ben was happy that he had a new friend on his street. They both looked at the calendar, and saw that their birthdays were only a few days apart. They decided to celebrate them together, and make more gifts for each other.
