Title: Mia the Girl and the Flowers

One day, a little girl named Mia went outside to play. She saw a big sunflower in her yard. Mia was very happy and began to skip around the sunflower. She wanted to show the sunflower to her friend, Tom. Mia called Tom and said, "Come see the big sunflower in my yard!" Tom was fearful because he did not like big flowers. But he came to see the sunflower because Mia was his friend. When Tom saw the sunflower, he got very scared. He ran away and did not stop. Mia was sad because her friend was scared of the sunflower. The sunflower did not look happy too. Mia's day did not end well because her friend was not there to play with her.

There was a spoiled little boy who lived in a large house with his mommy and daddy. Every day, the mommy and daddy would give him fun things like toys and candy. One day, the little boy asked his mommy why they had so many stamps. His mommy replied, “Well, sweetheart, stamps are used to mail letters to people.” The little boy thought it was fun to have all of the stamps, so he asked his mommy if he could mail a letter. His mommy agreed and gave him some paper, an envelope, and a stamp. The little boy wrote a letter to his best friend and put the stamp on the envelope. He ran outside to the mailbox and placed the letter inside. Then he came back inside and waited to see if there would be a reply. The little boy was so excited when he received a reply letter from his friend! He jumped with joy as he opened and read it. The little boy was excited to learn that his friend had also sent a stamp with the letter. The little boy was so happy that he ran back to his mommy and showed her the stamp. His mommy was very pleased and gave him a hug. From that day on, the little boy sent letters to his friends, and always received a reply with a stamp inside.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird lived in a big tree. One day, the bird met a rude ant. The ant said, "You can't carry anything! You're too small!" The bird was sad, but it wanted to show the ant that it could carry things too. So, the bird found a small leaf. It picked up the leaf with its beak and carried it to the top of the tree. The bird was very happy and proud. The rude ant saw the bird carrying the leaf and said, "Okay, you can carry a leaf. But can you carry a big stick?" The bird tried and tried, but it could not carry the big stick. The bird felt sad again. But then, a kind squirrel came and said, "Don't worry, little bird. We can carry the big stick together!" The bird and the squirrel carried the big stick together, and they were very happy. The rude ant learned that being kind is better than being rude.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On this farm, there were many animals. There was a big cow, a little pig, and a graceful bird. They were all good friends. They liked to play and talk with each other. One day, the little pig said to the big cow, "Let's play a game. We can shout and see who is the loudest." The big cow agreed, and they started to shout. The graceful bird watched them and smiled. As they were shouting, a big wind came and blew the little pig away. The big cow and the graceful bird were very scared. They shouted for help, and the farmer came running. The farmer found the little pig and brought him back to the farm. The big cow, the little pig, and the graceful bird were happy to be together again. They learned that shouting can be fun, but it can also be dangerous. The moral of the story is to be careful when playing games and to always look out for each other.
