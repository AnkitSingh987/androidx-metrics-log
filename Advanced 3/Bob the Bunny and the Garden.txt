Title: Bob the Bunny and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a bunny named Bob. Bob loved to take naps. Every day, he would take naps in the same pattern – 20 minutes in the morning, a 20-minute break in the afternoon, and a 30-minute nap before bed. One day, Bob was feeling extra tired and decided to take a longer nap. He curled up on the grass and dozed off. Suddenly, Bob woke up to a strange noise. He got up and peeked out of the grass. To his surprise, he saw a rare creature hopping around the garden. It had big ears, a big nose and huge eyes. Bob had never seen a creature like this before. He was about to take a step closer to get a better look, when he heard the creature say, "Can I take a nap with you?" Bob grinned and lied down beside the creature. They both dozed off in the warm sun. Bob had the best nap of his life!

Once upon a time, a little girl was outside playing. She wanted to explore and find something fun. She wandered down to the park and she heard a sound. It sounded like a rhythm. She looked around but she couldn't see where the sound was coming from. She felt a little frightened but she put on a brave face and kept searching. Suddenly, she heard a voice! It said, “Do you like the rhythm I’m wearing?" She looked around and saw a tall, frightening man. He was wearing a bright red hat and carrying a drum set. The little girl shook her head and said no. She was still feeling frightened. The man smiled and said, "Well, if you ever get brave enough, it's lots of fun to wear a rhythm too!" He quickly packed up his drum set and walked away. The little girl was happy that he had gone away and she kept exploring the park. She realised that she could still hear the rhythm, although it was getting quieter. She smiled, feeling brave enough to wear a rhythm as she danced around the park.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Anna. Anna was three years old and liked to play with her toys. One day, Anna was playing with her toys when she found some scissors. Anna's mum had told her not to touch the scissors, but she forgot. Suddenly, Anna felt something sharp cut her arm. She looked down and saw that her arm was bleeding. Anna was so scared that she started to panic. Anna's mum heard the noise and ran into the room. She was very frightened to see that Anna had the big black scissors in her hands. Anna's mum quickly took the scissors away and made sure Anna was ok. Despite the panic, Anna was still smiling. She knew that now she would never forget not to touch the scissors!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to run very fast. He would race with his friends and always win. Tom was a fast runner. One day, Tom found a bottle under a big tree. He did not understand what was inside the bottle. He shook it and heard a sound. Tom was very curious. Tom opened the bottle and out came a tiny bird. The bird said, "Thank you for setting me free!" Tom and the bird became good friends. They played together every day, and Tom learned to understand the bird's songs.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He heard a strange sound coming from the toilet. He was fearful, but he wanted to know what the sound was. He went to the toilet to listen. As he listened, the sound got louder and louder. Tim was scared, but he wanted to find out what was making the noise. He opened the toilet door and saw a big, angry monster. The monster was making the loud sound. The monster saw Tim and started to chase him. Tim ran away as fast as he could, but the monster caught him. The monster took Tim back to the toilet and they were never seen again.
