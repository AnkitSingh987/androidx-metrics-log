Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They brought a big, soft towel to sit on. Tim liked to play with his toys in the sand. He built a big sand castle. Tim was very happy. But then, a noisy bird flew down and landed on Tim's sand castle. The bird squawked loudly and broke the castle. Tim was sad and asked the bird, "Please, don't break my castle." The bird looked at Tim and flew away. Tim's mom saw what happened and had an idea. She took the towel and made a big, soft sand bed for the noisy bird. The bird came back and saw the soft bed. It was so happy that it stopped squawking. Tim and the bird became friends, and they all played together on the beach.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy new clothes for Lily. Lily was excited because she loved pretty clothes. Mom said, "Lily, you can pick one dress." At the store, Lily saw a bossy girl named Jane. Jane told Lily, "You should get the pink dress, it is the best!" Lily didn't like when people were bossy. She wanted to choose her own dress. Lily looked at all the clothes and picked a pretty blue dress. She showed it to mom. Mom said, "Good choice, Lily! I like it too." They bought the blue dress and went home. Lily was happy because she didn't let Jane tell her what to do. She knew she could make her own choices.

Once upon a time, there was a cat. This cat was very boring. All day, the cat would just sit and sleep. One day, the cat decided to go for a walk. While walking, the cat saw a big, red ball. The cat decided to play with the ball. The cat hit the ball and it went up into a tree. The cat was sad, but then something unexpected happened. A bird came and got the ball from the tree. The bird gave the ball back to the cat. The cat and the bird became friends. They played with the ball together all day. The cat was not boring anymore.

One day, a girl named Sue went for a walk. She liked to look at things while she walked. On her walk, she saw something shiny on the ground. It was a box. Sue picked up the box and opened it. Inside the box, there was jewelry. The jewelry was heavy and pretty. Sue wanted to show her mom the heavy jewelry. She walked back home with the box. The box was very heavy, but Sue did not stop. She walked and walked until she got home. Sue was so happy to show her mom the pretty jewelry. Sue's mom looked at the heavy jewelry and smiled. She said, "Sue, you found something very special on your walk today." Sue and her mom put the jewelry on and danced around the room. They had a fun day playing with the pretty, heavy jewelry that Sue found on her walk.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her blocks. She would try to make a big stack with them. But, sometimes, the blocks would fall down. Amy would feel uncomfortable when this happened. One sunny day, Amy was trying to make a stack again. She managed to put one block on top of another. Then, she put another block on top. The stack was getting very big! Amy was very happy. But, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew in through the window. The bird landed on the stack of blocks! The blocks did not fall down. The bird and Amy looked at each other. They both smiled. From that day on, Amy and the little bird played together and made the biggest stacks ever.

Once upon a time, there was a small ant named Andy. Andy was a very generous ant. He loved to share his food with his friends. One day, he found a big cookie. He was very happy and wanted to share it with everyone. Andy thought it would be fun to decorate the cookie. He invited his friends, the bees and the ladybugs, to help. They all worked together to make the cookie look pretty. They used tiny flowers, leaves, and even small pieces of fruit. As they were decorating, a big bird came over. The bird was hungry and wanted the cookie. Andy and his friends were scared. But then, the bird said, "I just want to help you decorate the cookie." So, they all worked together and shared the cookie in the end. Everyone was happy and full.
