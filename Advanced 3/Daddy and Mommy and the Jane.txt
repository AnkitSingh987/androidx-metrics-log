Title: Daddy and Mommy and the Jane

Daddy and Mommy took Jane to the university. She was very excited to go! It was a big building with lots of people. They met with a woman at the university, who told them that Jane was very smart. She said Jane could join the university when she was older. Daddy and Mommy were proud of Jane and hugged her. On the way home, Jane asked Daddy what the university was like. Daddy smiled and said, "It's very dull right now, Jane, but when you're older it will be different. You'll be able to care about things at the university that you think are important." Jane felt important and proud of herself. She couldn't wait to go to university and start caring about things!

One day, a big cat named Tom and a little mouse named Tim were playing in a room. They saw a smooth file on the table. Tim said, "Tom, come see this smooth file!" Tom jumped up on the table and looked at the file. "Wow, this file is very smooth," said Tom. They played with the file, rubbing their paws on it and having fun. Suddenly, the door opened and in came a big dog named Max. Max saw the cat and mouse playing with the file and wanted to join them. "Can I play with the smooth file too?" asked Max. Tom and Tim were surprised! They did not know that Max wanted to play with them. They all played together with the smooth file and became good friends.

Timmy loved to watch cartoons on TV. He liked the funny animals and the bright colors. He watched cartoons every morning before school. But one day, his mom said he could not watch cartoons. She said he had to do his homework first. Timmy was very upset. He did not want to do his homework. He wanted to watch cartoons. He said to his mom, "No, no, no! I want to watch cartoons! Please, please, please!" His mom said, "No, Timmy. You have to do your homework. It is important. You can watch cartoons later, after you finish your homework." Timmy did not listen. He ran to the living room and turned on the TV. He saw his favorite cartoon. He smiled and sat on the couch. He forgot about his homework. But then, his mom came to the living room. She saw Timmy watching cartoons. She was very angry. She said, "Timmy, what are you doing? You did not do your homework. You disobeyed me. That is very bad. You have to turn off the TV now." She took the remote and turned off the TV. Timmy cried. He said, "No, no, no! I want to watch cartoons! You are mean! You are mean!" His mom said, "Timmy, stop crying. You have to do your homework. You have to learn. You have to follow the rules. If you do your homework, I will let you watch cartoons later. But you have to do your homework first. Do you understand?" Timmy nodded. He wiped his tears. He said, "I understand. I will do my homework. I am sorry, mom. I love you." His mom said, "I love you too, Timmy. I am not mean. I want you to be smart and happy. Now, go to your room and do your homework. I will check on you later." Timmy went to his room and did his homework. He wrote his letters and numbers. He drew his pictures. He did his best. He finished his homework. He felt proud. He showed his homework to his mom. His mom said, "Good job, Timmy. You did your homework. I am proud of you. You are a good boy. Now, you can watch cartoons. But only for a little while. Then, you have to get ready for school." Timmy said, "Thank you, mom. You are a good mom. I like to watch cartoons. But I also like to do my homework. Homework is fun. Homework makes me smart." His mom smiled and hugged him. She said, "You are welcome, Timmy. You are a smart boy. I love you very much. Now, let's go watch cartoons together." They went to the living room and turned on the TV. They saw the funny animals and the bright colors. They laughed and enjoyed the cartoons. Timmy was happy. His mom was happy. They restored their peace.
