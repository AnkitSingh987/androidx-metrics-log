Title: Tom and the Rock

One day, a big, heavy rock was in the way. Tom and Tim wanted to move it. They tried very hard to push it. But it did not move. They were sad. Tom had an idea. They could dig a hole and go underground. They tried to dig with their hands. It was not easy. They kept trying. After a while, they found a big, shiny box underground. They were very surprised! They opened the box and found many toys inside. Tom and Tim were very happy. They played with the toys all day. The heavy rock was not a problem anymore.

Once upon a time there was man who had a jolly job. He drove to work every day in his car. It was a lot of fun and he always smiled when he drove. One day, he was stuck in traffic, so he asked his passengers what games they wanted to play. They all wanted to play the same game. They had to guess what object was going to come along next. They saw a tractor, a police car, and even a bus. Then, they spotted a fire truck. Everyone waved to the firefighters, and they waved back. The whole car cheered when there was a police horse trotting down the street. The man's passengers were so happy that they laughed and clapped. The man's jolly job was finished and he drove home. But before he went inside, he made sure to wave goodbye to the firefighters and their lovely horse friend.

Nina was a little girl who loved playing outside. Every day, as soon as she woke up, she ran outside to explore. Today, Nina was about to get a special treat. She was going to the park to play! Nina was told to bring her spray bottle with her. She didn't know what the spray bottle was for, but she followed the instructions and brought it with her. When they got to the park, Nina saw a thin nurse. The nurse had a big smile on her face and was spraying something into the air. Nina realized it was her spray bottle that the nurse was using! Nina asked the nurse, "What are you spraying?" The nurse replied, "It's special water that makes people happy." Nina was so excited. The nurse then said, "Would you like to help me spray?" Nina was so happy to help. She sprayed the special water around, and soon the park was filled with smiling faces. Everyone was having a wonderful time. Nina had never been so excited. She finally understood what the spray bottle she had brought with her was all about-- it was a way to make other people happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely house. The house had a big window. A little bird lived near the window. One day, the bird saw a cat. The cat wanted to disturb the bird. The bird was scared of the cat. The bird flew away from the window. The cat could not catch the bird. The bird was safe and happy. The lonely house was quiet again. And the cat went away, too.

Once upon a time, in a small brown house, there lived a little boy named Tim and his pet cat, Silver. Tim loved to play with Silver all day long. They would run, jump, and hide together. Silver was Tim's best friend. One sunny day, Tim and Silver were playing in the backyard. Silver saw a big butterfly and wanted to catch it. The butterfly flew higher and higher, and Silver tried to jump and slap it with her paw. "Be careful, Silver!" Tim shouted. But Silver didn't listen and kept trying to slap the butterfly. Finally, Silver jumped so high that she got stuck in a tree! Tim looked up and saw his friend, scared and meowing. "Don't worry, Silver! I'll help you!" Tim said. He found a long stick and used it to help Silver climb down from the tree. They were both happy and went back to playing in the sun. And from that day on, Silver never tried to slap a butterfly again.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly dog named Spot. Spot loved to play all day with his friends. After playing, he would take a nap under a big tree. One day, Spot saw a big box in the park. He wanted to remove the lid and see what was inside. But when he tried, he was too tired from playing. So, Spot took a nap near the box. While Spot was napping, a tiny mouse came along. The mouse was very strong and could remove the lid. Inside the box was a big cake! The mouse shared the cake with Spot when he woke up. They became best friends and had a jolly time together.
