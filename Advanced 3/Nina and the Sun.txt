Title: Nina and the Sun

Nina was feeling a bit down. She had always wanted to go on a holiday but she was feeling too scared to try it. She looked out the window and saw that the sky was grey and it was raining. "Don't worry," said her mum. "It will get better. Let's go and play inside. We can listen to music, play some games and have some fun!" Nina smiled but felt a little bit better. She spent the day playing with her mum, singing and dancing. And then, suddenly, the rain stopped and the sun shone through the windows. Nina's mum smiled. "It looks like a good day for a holiday!" she said. Nina's eyes lit up. She smiled and said excitedly, "Let's go!" Her mum nodded. "Let's go. I'll encourage you the entire way. You can do it, no matter how ugly it gets!" As they drove, Nina was surprised to see how the sky changed from grey to blue and the trees became greener. Soon enough, they reached the beach and were surrounded by beautiful views. Nina felt a wave of courage come over her. A holiday wasn't so scary after all! She smiled and thanked her mum for encouraging her. The two of them spent the day having fun in the sun, building sand castles and swimming in the sea.

Once there was a little boy who was always very tired. He liked to take his hat off and print the colours of the velvet onto a piece of paper. One day, while he was printing, he heard his mom calling him. She said, "Come on! Let's go! It's time for you to go to bed." The little boy was tired, but he was excited to finish his printing. He looked up, smiled and asked, "Can I finish? Please?" Mom smiled and said "Yes. But hurry, we need to go soon!" The little boy finished his printing and carefully put his hat back on. He handed his mom the velvet paper and said, "Look! I printed my favourite colour!" Mom smiled and said, "That's beautiful! Now come on, let's go to bed!" The little boy was tired but happy that he had finished his printing project. He skipped to his bedroom with a big smile on his face!

One day, a peaceful cat named Tim and a dog named Sam were playing a game. They wanted to see who could jump the highest. "I will win!" said Sam. "No, I will win!" said Tim. They took turns jumping, but they could not tell who jumped higher. Then, a big bird flew by and saw them playing. The bird said, "I can help you see who will win. I will watch from the sky." Tim and Sam liked this idea. They jumped again, and the bird watched from above. "Tim wins!" said the bird. Sam was sad but said, "Good job, Tim." As they were talking, a bottle of shampoo fell from the sky. It hit the ground and made bubbles everywhere. Tim, Sam, and the bird started to play with the bubbles. They forgot about the jumping game and had lots of fun together. The bird said, "We all win when we play together and have fun."

Once upon a time, there was a selfish little duck who lived in a pond. Every morning, the duck would go swimming around the pond to examine what was inside. One day, the duck found some yummy food and wanted it all for himself. He didn't want to share with the other animals in the pond! So, the selfish duck kept all the food for himself and ate it up. He didn't care that the other animals were very hungry. But then, something strange happened. All the animals crowded around the duck and said, "Why are you so selfish?". The duck had to explain why he kept all the food for himself. The duck was very ashamed and felt guilty. He decided that from now on, he would always share food with the other animals in the pond. The duck and the other animals became the best of friends and every day they would examine the pond together, looking for yummy food to share.
