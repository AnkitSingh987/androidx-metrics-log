Title: Nappy the Napkin and the Table

Once upon a time, there was a small napkin named Nappy. Nappy was very useful. He helped clean up messes at the table. One day, Nappy was on a table with many other napkins. Nappy did not know where he belonged. A little girl named Sarah came to the table. She saw Nappy and said, "You belong with me, Nappy." Sarah picked up Nappy and took him to her table. They sat down together to eat lunch. Sarah spilled some juice on the table. Nappy said, "I can help!" Nappy cleaned up the juice. Sarah smiled and said, "Thank you, Nappy! You are very useful." From that day on, Nappy and Sarah were best friends, and Nappy always knew where he belonged.

Once upon a time, there was a happy girl named Lalu. She loved to sing. Every day, Lalu would sing with her friends in the park. One day, they were singing when suddenly they spotted a mysterious box. What could be inside it? Lalu's friends were too scared to open it, so they ran away. But Lalu was not scared. She was very brave and she wanted to solve the mystery. She tucked her hair behind her ears and slowly opened the box. Suddenly, she was met with a loud burst of laughter! Inside the box was a big, happy clown. Lalu and the clown shared a big hug and started to laugh and sing together. The mystery was solved and Lalu was overjoyed. She invited the clown for a snack and continued to sing and laugh together. Lalu and the clown had a great day!

Once upon a time, there was a brave dog named Max. Max loved to run and play on the road near his house. One day, Max saw a big box on the side of the road. He wanted to see what was inside. Max knew how to unlock the box. He used his nose and paws to open it. Inside, he found a big, shiny ball. Max was so happy! He took the ball and started to play with it on the road. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the ball away from Max. He tried to catch it, but the ball went far away. Max was sad because he lost his new toy. The brave dog learned that it is not always good to unlock boxes on the road.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big festival. All the people came to have fun. They were open and happy. They played games, ate yummy food, and danced. One day, a little girl named Sally wanted to improve her dancing. She practiced and practiced. She danced all day and all night. She got better and better. Sally was so excited to dance at the festival. At the festival, Sally started to dance. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew everyone's hats away! All the people stopped to catch their hats. But Sally kept dancing. The wind saw Sally dancing and stopped. It liked her dance so much that it stopped blowing the hats. Everyone was so happy and thanked Sally for her dance. From that day on, Sally's dance made the wind and the people happy. And the festival was always fun.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the garden. They had a lot of fun with their cars, dolls, and blocks. But they also liked to make new things with their rubber bands. They could stretch them, twist them, and link them together. One day, they found a big white ball in the grass. It was soft and bouncy, and they wanted to play with it. But they did not know who it belonged to. They looked around, but they did not see anyone. "Maybe we can keep it," said Tom. "It is so cool and fun." "No, we can't," said Lily. "That is not nice. Someone must be looking for it. We have to find the owner and give it back." They decided to ask their mom for help. She was in the kitchen, making cookies. They showed her the white ball and told her what they found. "That is a very nice ball," said mom. "But it is not yours. It belongs to the boy next door. He loves to play soccer, but he lost his ball yesterday. He was very sad and asked me if I had seen it." Tom and Lily felt sorry for the boy. They wanted to make him happy again. They took the white ball and ran to his house. They knocked on the door and waited. The boy opened the door and saw them. He was surprised and happy. He recognized his ball and thanked them. "Wow, you found my ball! Thank you so much! You are very kind and honest. Do you want to play soccer with me?" Tom and Lily nodded and smiled. They liked the boy and his ball. They went to the garden and played soccer together. They had a lot of fun and became friends. Mom watched them from the window and smiled. She was proud of Tom and Lily. They had solved a problem and learned a lesson. They had done the right thing and shared their joy.
