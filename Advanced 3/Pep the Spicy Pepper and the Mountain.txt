Title: Pep the Spicy Pepper and the Mountain

Once upon a time, there was a spicy pepper named Pep. Pep loved to dance and join his friends in fun games. One day, Pep and his friends were playing in the park when something unexpected happened. All of a sudden, the ground began to shake. It was an earthquake! Pep and his friends were scared, but they held hands and stayed close. The earthquake stopped, and they looked around to see what had changed. To their surprise, the earthquake had made a big pile of dirt. Pep had an idea. He asked his friends to help him make a big mountain with the dirt. They all joined together and built a huge mountain. They climbed to the top and looked at the view. The earthquake had brought them closer, and they were happy to have each other.

Once there was a girl who loved cones. She wanted to wrap a cone in something flexible, so she went to look for the perfect material. First, she tried wrapping the cone in a blanket. It was a little too thick. Then she tried covering it with yarn. The yarn was nice and colorful, but it was too soft. Finally, the girl found some fabric that was just right. It was strong but also flexible. She carefully wrapped the cone in the fabric, so it was all tucked in safe and snug. The girl was happy with her new cone creation. She could squeeze it, shape it and even play with it. She loved her flexible cone and took it with her wherever she went!

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a blue door. He had a pet cat named Spot. One day, Tom heard a noise from the kitchen. He went to see what it was. He saw water coming out of the faucet. Tom tried to turn off the faucet, but it would not stop. He did not know what to do. Tom called his friend, Sue, for help. Sue came to his house to answer his call. She looked at the faucet and found a way to stop the water. Tom was very happy. He thanked Sue for her help, and they had a fun day together with Spot.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Daisy. She had a furry friend called Fluffy who was always by her side. One day, they decided to go on a special adventure. As they started walking, they found an old box outside the house. Daisy said, “I wonder what’s inside? Let’s twist the box and find out!”. So Daisy and Fluffy found themselves twisting the box and eventually, it opened. Inside was a hairy spider, its eight legs wriggling as they looked on in surprise. Daisy looked at the spider and said “Thank you, Mr Spider, for showing us what was inside the box. We didn’t know what surprises it held.” Fluffy agreed, adding “Yes, we should always be careful before we twist something. We don’t know what we’ll find”. And with this, the two friends continued on their adventure, learning a valuable lesson about being careful in life.

One day, a little boy named Tom saw a big vase in his house. He did not like the vase. He said, "I hate this vase. It is bad." Tom's mom heard him and said, "Tom, we do not say 'hate'. We say 'do not like'." Tom said, "Okay, Mom. I do not like this bad vase." One day, Tom was playing with his ball in the house. He threw the ball and it hit the bad vase. The vase fell and broke. Tom was scared. But then, something unexpected happened. Inside the vase, Tom found a small toy car. He loved toy cars. He said, "I like this car. I am happy the bad vase is gone." Tom's mom came in and saw the broken vase. She was not happy. But she saw the toy car and said, "Tom, you found the car I hid for your birthday. It is a surprise gift. But please, do not play ball in the house again." Tom said, "I am sorry, Mom. I will not play ball in the house. I am happy I found the car, but I will be more careful next time."
