Title: Lisa and the Door

One day, Lisa and her Daddy went to the store. Daddy had to get some items for their house. He took something off the shelf and looked it over. Lisa asked, "What is it, Daddy?" He smiled. "It's a doorbell. When we get home, we can put it up." Lisa was very excited. She ran to the cash register and waited impatiently. Suddenly, the doorbell rang. Lisa was surprised. The doorbell was ringing even though it wasn't in the house yet. "Why is it ringing, Daddy?" "That must mean someone is here," he said. He opened the door, and there was Lisa's friend, Bill. He was holding a note. "Hi Bill!" said Lisa. Bill handed the note to Daddy, who thanked him and opened it. Inside was a message from Lisa's Mommy. With a smile, Daddy handed the note to Lisa. "Your Mommy wants you to come home right away." Lisa nodded and smiled. She was eager to get home to see what their new doorbell looked like.

Timmy was a little mouse who liked to drink milk. He lived in a big house with his family and many other mice. Sometimes, he would sneak into the kitchen at night and look for a bowl of milk to drink. One night, he found a bowl of milk on the table. He climbed up a chair and reached for the bowl. He smelled the milk and it was delicious. He licked his lips and started to drink. "Hello, little mouse," said a voice behind him. Timmy turned around and saw a big cat. The cat had yellow eyes and sharp teeth. He looked hungry and angry. Timmy was scared and wanted to run away. "Please don't eat me, Mr. Cat," Timmy said. "I just wanted some milk. It was delicious." "Well, I want some milk too," the cat said. "And I want some mouse. You look delicious." The cat jumped on the table and chased Timmy. Timmy ran as fast as he could. He dodged the cat's paws and ran to the edge of the table. He saw a window and jumped out. He landed on a tree branch and climbed down. He ran back to his family and told them what happened. They hugged him and said he was brave. They gave him some cheese and water. Timmy was happy and safe. He learned a lesson. He decided not to drink milk from the table again. He drank water from the sink instead. It was not as delicious, but it was safer.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and baby brother. Lily's room was upstairs, and she loved to play with her toys there. She always had a big smile on her face when she played. One day, Lily's friend, Tim, came to her house to play. Tim saw Lily's toys and felt envious. He wanted to have toys like Lily's, too. Lily noticed Tim's face and asked him what was wrong. Tim told her that he wished he had toys like hers. Lily thought for a moment and then decided to share her toys with Tim. They played together and had lots of fun. Tim's envious feeling went away, and he had a big smile on his face, too. They both learned that sharing can make everyone happy. And from that day on, Lily and Tim always shared their toys and had fun together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Rose. Rose was only three years old, but she already loved her books. Every day, she would pick up a book and learn something new. One day, Rose found a very big book. It was a dictionary. She opened it to look at all the words, and there were so many! Rose could not understand them, but she still liked to look at them. Then, Rose found a yummy cookie. She wanted to eat the cookie right away, but something inside her told her to finish the dictionary first. So, Rose decided to read a few pages and then have the yummy cookie. When Rose finished the dictionary, she was very proud of herself. She had learned something new, and she was even able to spell some of the words. She knew that if she put her mind to it, she could do anything. The moral of this story is that learning is important. When you learn something new, you can do great things. And if you work hard and stay focused, you will be able to end with a yummy reward!
