Title: The Graceful Bird and the Bugs

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird. The bird could fly high in the sky. One day, the bird flew very far away from home. The bird started to feel tired. It needed fuel to keep flying. The bird saw some yummy bugs on the ground. The bird went down to eat the bugs. As the bird ate the bugs, it started to stumble. The bird was not a bird anymore! It turned into a funny bunny. The bunny hopped away, happy with its new life.

Once upon a time, there was a big bookshelf in a room. The bookshelf had many books on it. One day, a little boy named Tim came into the room. He saw the bookshelf and wanted to find a good book to read. Tim's mom came into the room and saw Tim looking at the bookshelf. She mentioned, "Tim, there is a funny book on the bottom shelf. It will make you laugh!" Tim looked at the bottom shelf and found the funny book. He started to read it and laughed a lot. As Tim was laughing, his sister Lily came into the room. She saw Tim reading the funny book and wanted to read it too. But when she tried to take the book from Tim, she accidentally knocked over some other books from the bookshelf. Lily felt embarrassed and said, "I'm sorry, Tim." Tim smiled and said, "It's okay, Lily. We can read the funny book together." And they both sat down and read the funny book, laughing together.

One day, a cat and a dog were walking by a rail. They saw a big, red, spicy pepper on the ground. The cat and dog looked at each other and wanted to eat it. But they did not know if they should. The cat and the dog started to discuss. The cat said, "I like spicy. I want to eat it." The dog said, "I do not like spicy. It is too hot for me." They talked and talked, but they could not agree. Finally, a bird came and took the spicy pepper away. The cat and dog were sad, but they learned that they should not fight over things. They walked away from the rail, still friends, and went to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a big oak tree in a park. It was very old and strong. One sunny day, a fast rabbit named Remy ran by the oak tree. He saw a small bird named Benny sitting on a branch. Benny looked sad. "Hi Benny, why are you sad?" asked Remy. Benny said, "I can't fly fast like my friends. They all went away and left me here." Remy thought for a moment and said, "Don't worry, Benny. I can help you find your friends." Remy and Benny went on a long walk to find Benny's friends. They looked in the park, by the pond, and even in the tall grass. At last, they found Benny's friends playing near the big oak tree. Benny was so happy that he could fly and play with his friends again. Remy felt good for helping his new friend. In the end, they all played together near the big oak tree. They had lots of fun and became best friends forever.

Candy was walking down the street when she noticed a store. It had lots of treats inside and she wanted to go in. But when she got to the door it was locked. She was so disappointed! Just then, a little girl approached Candy. She said that she was the store's owner and asked Candy why she wanted to go in. Candy told her that she wanted some treats. The store's owner told Candy that the store was only open to people who were competitive. She said that Candy must do something to prove she was competitive and then she would lead her inside. But what could Candy do? Candy thought for a moment and then smiled. She had an idea! She raced around the block and when she got back she was beaming. She had run the fastest race of her life and the store's owner was very impressed. The store owner told Candy that she was very competitive and then she opened the store and let her in. Candy was so excited. She was able to pick all the treats she wanted and it was the best day ever!
