Title: The Story of the Ball and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a child. The child liked to play with his toys. One day, he found an unknown toy in his room. It was a small, red ball. The child played with the unknown ball all day. He threw it, kicked it, and rolled it. After playing, the child was very dirty. His hands were full of dirt. So, he went to the bathroom to wipe his hands clean. But, as he wiped his hands, the unknown red ball started to grow bigger and bigger. It grew so big that it filled the whole room. The child tried to stop it, but he could not. The big red ball pushed him out of the house. The child was very sad and scared because he could not go back to his home.

Once upon a time, there was a white duck. The white duck lived near a big pond. The white duck liked to swim and play all day. One day, the white duck saw a big stick. The white duck wanted to play with the stick. The white duck tried to snap the stick with its beak. But the stick was too big and strong. The white duck tried and tried to snap the stick. The white duck was sad because it could not snap the stick. Then, the big stick fell on the white duck. The white duck could not swim and play anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a pretty lake. A tall tree stood near the lake. One day, a little bird wanted to drink water from the lake. The bird was very patient and waited for a long time. As the bird flew down to the lake, it saw a big fish. The fish said, "Hi, bird! Can you twist and dance for me?" The bird was happy and said, "Yes, I can!" So, the bird twisted and danced for the fish. The fish clapped and said, "Thank you, bird! You are a good friend." The bird smiled and drank water from the lake. The bird and the fish became best friends. They played together near the lake every day.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle statue that liked to lead all the other statues in the garden. Every day, the gentle statue would gently guide all the other statues around the garden. It was always a fun time, and the other statues always listened to what the gentle statue said. One day, the gentle statue decided to take all the other statues on an adventure. The gentle statue lead them up the hill, through the trees and past the river. The other statues had so much fun! They felt so happy going on an adventure with the gentle statue. At last, the gentle statue led the other statues to the top of the hill. From the top of the hill they could see the beautiful forest with all its twinkling lights. The other statues were so thankful for the gentleness of the gentle statue, so they decided to give it a big hug. Then, they all went back down the hill together. From that day on, the gentle statue always lead all the other statues on fun adventures. They were all so happy to follow the gentleness of the gentle statue.

Once upon a time, there was a big dishwasher in a small kitchen. The dishwasher was very uncomfortable because it was always full of dirty dishes. It wanted to be clean and shiny, but it was always full of yucky food. One day, a little girl named Sally came into the kitchen. She saw the dishwasher and said, "Oh no! The dishwasher is so unhappy! I will help it." Sally started to take out the dirty dishes and spread soap on them. She then put the dishes back in the dishwasher and turned it on. After the dishwasher was done, Sally opened it and saw all the clean dishes. The dishwasher was now very happy and comfortable. Sally said, "You're welcome, dishwasher! I will help you stay clean and happy." And from that day on, Sally always made sure the dishwasher was clean and comfortable.
