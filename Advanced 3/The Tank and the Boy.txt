Title: The Tank and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a big tank. It had no name. The tank was alone and wanted a friend. One day, a small boy came to see the tank. The boy was happy and wanted to play with the tank. The boy said, "Hi tank, do you have a name?" The tank said, "No, I don't have a name. Can you give me one?" The boy thought for a moment and said, "I will name you Tanky." Tanky was very happy to have a name. The boy and Tanky played all day. They had so much fun. Tanky was the best friend the boy could have. They played whenever the boy was available. And so, the boy and Tanky were always together. They played and laughed every day. Tanky was never alone again, and the boy was happy to have a friend named Tanky.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy some pasta for dinner. Lily saw the pasta and it was orange! She was very happy and said, "Look, mom! Orange pasta!" Her mom smiled and put the pasta in the cart. Later, at home, Lily helped her mom part the pasta into two pots. One pot was for them and the other pot was for their friends who were coming over. Lily was excited to share the orange pasta with her friends. When their friends came over, they all sat down to eat. But when they opened the pots, the pasta was not orange anymore! It was white! Everyone was surprised. Lily's mom said, "Oh, the orange color must have come off when we cooked it!" They all laughed and enjoyed their dinner together.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Tommy who went to bed feeling heavy. He was worried about his nightmares. Just as Tommy lay down to sleep, he heard a noise. It was a loud, low rumble. Tommy was scared so he hid under his blanket. Suddenly, there was a voice. It said, "Don’t be scared, Tommy. I’m here to catch your nightmares!” Tommy was amazed. He sat up slowly and saw a tall figure standing in the corner of his room. It was wearing a bright red cape and had a large sack slung over its shoulder. Tommy said, "Will you really catch my nightmares?” The figure answered, "Yes! My sack is full of magical dust to help you sleep peacefully!” Tommy was relieved, so he lay down and closed his eyes. And when he woke up the next morning, his nightmares were gone!

One day, a big, strong, and dependable dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a ball and wanted to play. Max picked up the ball and ran to find a friend. Max found a cat named Luna. They decided to play a game to see who could leave the ball the farthest. Luna went first, and she threw the ball far. Max went next, and he threw the ball even farther. Max was so happy, he did a little dance. Max and Luna played more games, and they both had fun. At the end of the day, Max had the most wins. Luna said, "Max, you are very good at games." Max and Luna became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. Under the tree, two friends, Cat and Dog, liked to play. One day, Cat found a pretty cloth. It was soft and had many colors. Cat was very happy and decided to spread the cloth on the ground to sit on. Dog saw the cloth and wanted it too. Dog felt jealous of Cat's pretty cloth. Dog thought, "Why does Cat have the cloth and not me?" So, Dog went to Cat and said, "I want the cloth too!" Cat did not want to share the cloth with Dog. They started to fight. They pulled and tugged on the cloth, each wanting it for themselves. They did not know how to share and be kind to each other. In the end, the cloth ripped apart. Now, neither Cat nor Dog could have the pretty cloth. They both felt sad and angry. They had lost the cloth and their friendship because they did not know how to share.
