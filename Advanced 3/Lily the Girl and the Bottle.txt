Title: Lily the Girl and the Bottle

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to dance. She danced all day long, and she was very happy. One day, Lily saw a pretty bottle on the ground. The bottle had a word on it: "poison." Lily did not know what "poison" meant, but she thought the bottle was pretty. Lily felt anxious because she did not know what to do with the bottle. She decided to show it to her mom. Her mom saw the bottle and said, "Oh no, Lily! This is not good. Poison can make us very sick." Lily felt scared, but her mom took the bottle and put it away in a safe place. After the bottle was gone, Lily felt happy again. She went outside to dance with her friends. They danced and played all day long. Lily was glad that her mom had helped her, and she knew she would always be careful with things she did not understand.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very sad and miserable. He did not have any friends to play with. One day, he found a package near his house. He was very excited and wanted to see what was inside the package. Tim took the package and went to his room. He lay on his bed and opened the package. Inside the package, he found a big toy car. Tim was very happy and started to play with the toy car. But the toy car was not a good toy. It was a bad toy car. The toy car broke Tim's other toys and made a big mess. Tim's mom saw the mess and was very angry. She took the toy car away and Tim was sad again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily who had many toys. She loved her toys, but she was a spoiled girl. She did not like to share her things with her friends. Lily's mom always told her, "If you don't share, you will be punished." One day, Lily's friend, Tom, came over to play. They had fun playing with the toys. But when Tom wanted to play with Lily's favorite toy, she said, "No, it's mine!" Tom was sad and went home. Lily's mom saw what happened and said, "Lily, you did not share. Now you will be punished." Lily's mom took away her favorite toy for a day. Lily was sad, but she learned her lesson. The next time Tom came over, Lily shared her favorite toy with him, and they had a lot of fun.

Mommy was very excited. She had a special treat for her three year old daughter. It was a big bag of sugar. The little girl's eyes lit up with excitement and she jumped up and down. Mommy said, "Do you want to try some of the sugar?" The little girl nodded her head and said, "Yes, please!", and then she ran over to Mommy. Mommy was very patient and carefully took out a handful of sugar, and then gave it to her daughter. The little girl put it into her mouth, and the taste of the sugar was like nothing she had ever experienced before. She feel in love! She licked the sugar off her fingertips and then said, "Can I have more please?" Mommy smiled and said, "Yes, of course! But be careful and do not eat too much. We do not want to get sick." The little girl nodded and happily munched away. The sugar was wonderfully exciting and she could not wait for her next sugar adventure!

One day, a little bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a wet hanger on a tree. The bird wanted to return the hanger to its home. But a big cat was under the tree. The cat wanted to catch the bird. The bird was scared but wanted to help. It flew down to get the wet hanger. The cat jumped to catch the bird, but the bird was fast. It took the hanger and flew away. The bird returned the wet hanger to its home. The cat was sad but the bird was happy. The bird helped and stayed safe. Everyone was happy in the end.
