Title: The Ancient Castle and the Door

Once upon a time there was an ancient castle. It stood proudly in the middle of a large green field. One day a little girl with long curly hair came to the castle. She stood at the large wooden door and shouted "Do you welcome me?". Suddenly the big heavy door opened and out came an old man with a long white beard. He said "Young one, I gladly welcome you. Please come inside. The girl smiled and stepped into the castle. She was amazed at how old and different it looked. Everything looked so ancient! The old man said "Welcome to my home! I am the King of this castle. What's your name, young one?". The girl said "My name is Emily". The King said "Well, Emily, it is a pleasure to meet you. The King and Emily talked for a while and then Emily said "Thank you for your kind welcome, King! I must go now". The King bid her farewell and Emily returned home with a smile on her face. The end.

One day, a boy named Tom found a rough football in his yard. He wanted to play with it, but it was too big for him. So, he asked his friend, Sam, to help him measure it. "Sam, can you help me measure this football?" Tom said. Sam agreed, and they tried to measure the football together. They found out that it was too big for them to play with. Tom and Sam were sad because they could not play with the rough football. They tried to kick it, but it was too heavy. They tried to throw it, but it was too big. As they were playing, a big dog came and took the football away. Tom and Sam were very sad, and they could not play with the football anymore. The dog ran away, and they never saw the football again.

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She lived in a big castle near the sea. The princess liked to sail on her boat. One day, she wanted to sail to a far island. On the way to the island, she saw an ugly fish. The fish was not nice. It wanted to stop the princess from sailing. The princess was scared, but she did not want to give up. The princess thought of a plan. She found a big net and caught the ugly fish. Then, she sailed to the island. The princess was happy and safe. She played on the island all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sally. Sally loved to mix things together and make them different. One day, Sally went to her Mom and asked if she could make something. Her Mom smiled and agreed. She gave Sally some ingredients to mix. Sally grabbed a bowl and started mixing the ingredients. She was very careful not to make a mess. She mixed and mixed until the ingredients became one thing. Sally looked up at her Mom and said, "I made this! It's special!" Her Mom smiled. "It sure is! It looks wonderful. I'm proud of you." Sally smiled back. From then on, she kept mixing things, careful to make new and wonderful things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She was three years old and loved playing with her toys. One day, Sarah saw a blue drum in the toy store. She was so excited, she knew she had to have it. She asked her mom to buy it for her. "It's so pretty mommy, please can I have it?" asked Sarah. "Yes, of course," replied mommy, and she bought the blue drum for Sarah. When Sarah got home, she picked up the drum, and started pushing it around the floor. The drum made a nice thumping sound as Sarah pushed it. She laughed with glee as it made different noises. Sarah was so pleased with her new toy, she played with it all day. She pushed it from the living room, to the kitchen, and even up the stairs! Everywhere Sarah went, the blue drum followed. She had lots of fun making happy, noise with her drum. The end.
