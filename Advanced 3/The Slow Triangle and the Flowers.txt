Title: The Slow Triangle and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a slow triangle. The slow triangle liked to walk and look at the flowers. One day, the slow triangle met a fast circle. The fast circle liked to run and play games. The slow triangle and fast circle became friends. They liked to be with each other. The slow triangle liked how the fast circle could run, and the fast circle liked how the slow triangle could see the pretty flowers. One day, the slow triangle and fast circle decided to marry. They were very happy together. They had a big party with all their friends. The slow triangle and fast circle lived happily ever after, running and looking at flowers together.

Once upon a time, there was a big mountain. The mountain was very warm. Many animals lived on the mountain. They were all friends. They liked to play together. One day, they wanted to play a new game. They asked the mountain, "Mountain, let us play a new game!" The mountain said, "Okay, let's play hide and seek." So, the animals started to play hide and seek. The animals looked for a good place to hide. They found a big hole in the mountain. They all went inside to hide. But, surprise! The hole was a door to a big, warm room. The mountain was a house! The mountain was happy to let the animals live in the warm room. Now, they all lived and played together in the warm mountain-house.

Once upon a time, in a hot, sandy land, there was a beautiful oasis. The oasis had a big pool of water, many green trees, and lots of sweet fruit. In this oasis, there lived a generous bird who loved to share her fruit with all her animal friends. One day, the bird was flying high in the sky when she saw her friend, the little mouse. The little mouse was very hot and tired. The generous bird wanted to help her friend, so she decided to bring him to the oasis. She picked up the little mouse in her beak and flew him to the big pool of water. When they got to the oasis, the little mouse was so happy. He drank water from the big pool and began to soak his tired feet. The bird gave him sweet fruit to eat. As they were enjoying their time at the oasis, something unexpected happened. The water in the big pool started to bubble and shake. Suddenly, a big fish jumped out of the water! The fish was very friendly and wanted to play with the bird and the mouse. They all laughed and played together for the rest of the day, and the oasis became an even happier place for all the animals to share.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to search for his toy vehicle. It was not a regular vehicle - it was a big, red fire truck. Tim loved to play with his fire truck all day long. As he searched, he asked his friend, Sam. "Sam, have you seen my fire truck?" Sam shook his head and said, "No, Tim. I have not seen it. Let's search together." So, Tim and Sam looked under the bed, behind the door, and in the toy box. Finally, they found the fire truck in the yard under a big tree. Tim and Sam were very happy. They played with the fire truck all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a yummy triangle cake. Three friends, Cat, Dog, and Bird, saw the cake. They all wanted to eat it. But, there was only one piece of cake. Cat, Dog, and Bird started to quarrel. They all said, "I want the cake!" They got very mad at each other. They did not know what to do. Then, a wise old Owl came. Owl said, "Stop the quarrel! Share the cake!" The friends listened to Owl. They cut the yummy triangle cake into three parts. Cat, Dog, and Bird ate the cake together. They were happy and friends again.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent cat named Kitty. Kitty had her own little drawer in the house. She loved to sleep and play in her drawer. One day, Kitty found her drawer was not empty. There was a toy in it. Kitty was not happy. She did not like the toy in her drawer. She tried to take the toy out, but it was too big. Kitty felt sad and did not know what to do. She wanted her drawer to be empty again. Kitty thought and thought. Then, she had an idea. She pushed the toy with her head and paws. It took some time, but the toy finally came out of the drawer. Kitty was so happy! She jumped back into her drawer and took a nice, long nap.
