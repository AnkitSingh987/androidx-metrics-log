Title: The Fancy Diamond and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a fancy diamond that could spin. It lived in a big forest with many friends. One day, the diamond met a little bird who was very sad. "Why are you sad?" asked the spinning diamond. The little bird said, "I lost my way home and I don't know where my family is." The fancy diamond wanted to help the little bird. It had an idea. It said, "I will spin and shine so bright that your family will see us and come here." The diamond began to spin faster and faster. It shined so bright that it lit up the whole forest. Soon enough, the little bird's family saw the bright light and flew towards it. They were so happy to find their lost little one. The little bird thanked the fancy diamond for its help. From that day on, they became the best of friends and the little bird's family always knew where to find them, thanks to the spinning diamond.

One day, a cute dog was in the park. He saw a towel on the ground. He went to the towel and sat on it. A little boy came to the dog and said, "Hi dog, is this your towel?" The dog didn't know what to say. He just looked at the boy. The boy tried to take the towel, but the dog didn't want to let it go. They both pulled on the towel, but the dog was stronger. Then, something bad happened. The towel tore in half. The boy was sad and the dog was sad too. The cute dog and the little boy didn't get to play with the towel anymore.

The sun was in the sky, morning light was shining on the fields. Sam was very excited today because he was going to the market. He had a wide cart with him and he filled it with all his treasures. He wanted to buy something special. He walked past the farmer's house and whispered to himself, "I wonder what I'll find?" He wished he could find something bigger and better than anything he had before. When Sam reached the market he saw it had many wide carts with things laid out for sale. He went around trying to find the best treasure for himself. After looking around for a long time he found the perfect item. It was a bright red cart that was just the right size for him. Sam was so excited to show his parents what he had found. He pulled out the cart and showed them. His parents were happy for him and said, "You have done well". The next day, Sam took his cart out and it was perfect for him. He filled it with his treasures and smiled at his parents. He knew this was the best treasure he had ever found.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She loved to play outside with her friends. They liked to run, jump, and laugh in the warm sun. One day, Lily and her friends were playing near a big tree. They saw a pretty sunset in the sky. The sky was pink, orange, and yellow. They were very happy to see it. Lily had a hat on her head. She wanted to see the sunset better. So, she removed her hat. She looked up at the pretty sunset and smiled. Her friends did the same. They all enjoyed the pretty sunset together, and they played until it was time to go home.

Sally was so careless. She let go of her toy and it started to spin. It whirled around and around. Sally turned her head and saw a pizza on the counter. She was so excited and ran over. She reached up but it was too far away. She tried to grab some of it with her fingertips, but it was too hard. She decided to go faster. The toy started to spin faster and faster. The pizza still wasn't close enough. Little by little, Sally inched closer and closer. But it was too late. Her toy suddenly flew off the table and hit the pizza! Cheese, sauce and topping went everywhere! Sally felt so sad. Even though she had been so careless, she didn't mean to break the pizza. Tears filled her eyes and she ran off to her room, vowing never to be careless again.
