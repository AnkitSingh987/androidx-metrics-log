Title: Tom the Boy and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had an ancient box that he found in his room. He liked to shake the box and listen to the sound it made. Inside the box, there was a penny that his mom gave him. It was a special penny. One day, Tom met a girl named Sue. Sue saw the box and said, "What is that?" Tom replied, "It's my ancient box with a penny inside. I like to shake it." Sue asked, "Can I try?" Tom gave the box to Sue, and she shook it too. They both laughed and played with the box. Later, a big wind came and blew the box out of Sue's hand. The box fell on the ground and broke. The penny rolled away and was lost. Tom and Sue were very sad. They tried to find the penny, but they could not. The ancient box and the special penny were gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little figure named Tim. Tim was a very organized figure. He liked to keep all his toys in the right place. He had a big box for his cars, a small box for his blocks, and a medium box for his dolls. One day, Tim's friend, Sally, came to play. Sally was not organized like Tim. She took out all the toys and made a big mess. Tim did not like this. He wanted his toys to be in the right place. Tim decided to teach Sally how to be organized. He showed her how to put the toys back in the right boxes. Sally watched Tim and learned from him. Soon, Sally was very good at being organized too. They played together and had a lot of fun. And from that day on, Sally always helped Tim keep their toys in the right place.

Once upon a time there lived a youth called Jack. He was very brave and strong, and he liked to shoot with his bow and arrow. One day he found a field full of targets, and he decided to try to shoot them. When he lifted his bow, he managed to hit all of the targets with impressive accuracy. He was so pleased with himself. Just then, some other children came along. They had never seen anyone shoot targets like that before and were amazed at Jack's skills. One spoke up, saying: "Wow, that was really great! Can you teach us too?" Jack smiled and said: "Sure, I'd be happy to help. Come on, let's do it together!" So together they all shot the targets, until they had all hit their mark. It was very impressive! They all laughed together and felt so proud of themselves. The end.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Joel and Emily. They were both 3 years old. One day Joel said to Emily, 'Let's have a competition - the first one to win gets a special surprise!' Emily excitedly shouted, 'Yes! Let's do it!' They both started running, racing across the garden. Emily was winning, but then Joel tripped and Emily stopped to help him. 'Thank you, Emily!' Joel said. Emily smiled and said, 'I knew that it was more important to be kind than to win.' Joel and Emily hugged and took a memory of that day with them forever. The surprise that they both won was something unknown and totally magical.

The sun was out. It was a warm day. The birds were singing. Timmy's mommy got a big orange cane out of the closet. It was big enough for Timmy too! Mommy said, "Let's go to the lake and soak the cane". So Timmy and Mommy carried the orange cane to the lake. When they got there, it was so pretty! The water was blue and there were little fish swimming around. Mommy said, "Let's soak the cane!" So Timmy and Mommy put the orange cane into the lake. It floated on top of the water. They watched the cane bob up and down in the water. It was so much fun! After a while Mommy said, "We should go home now." So Timmy and Mommy picked up the orange cane and took it back home. They had so much fun just soaking the cane in the lake!
