Title: Molly and the Eraser

Molly was feeling anxious. She was missing her eraser. She didn't know where it had gone. Molly asked her mom, "Mommy, have you seen my eraser?" Her mom replied, "No, Molly I haven't seen it. Let's look for it together." They both looked around the house, but couldn't find it. Molly started to cry. "I really miss my eraser!" Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, Molly. We will find it." Molly smiled and said, "OK!" They continued searching, and suddenly Molly saw it under the sofa. Molly shouted, "Mommy, I found it!" Her mom said, "That's great, Molly! I'm so happy." Molly hugged the eraser and said, "Me too. I'm so happy to have my eraser back."

Once upon a time, in a big house, there lived a wealthy man. He had a shiny dragon toy that he loved a lot. Every day, he would polish the dragon to keep it clean and bright. One day, a little girl came to his house. She saw the shiny dragon and wanted to play with it. The wealthy man said, "No, you can't play with my dragon. It's too special." The little girl was sad, but she had an idea. She found a dirty old toy and started to polish it. The toy became shiny and beautiful. The wealthy man saw this and was surprised. The wealthy man learned that sharing is good. He let the little girl play with his shiny dragon, and they became friends. They polished their toys together and shared them with others. And they all lived happily ever after.

Sara and Tom are playing with their toys in the living room. Sara has a doll and Tom has a car. They are having fun and making noises. "Look, my doll can dance!" Sara says. She moves her doll's legs and arms and makes her spin. "Wow, that's nice!" Tom says. He applauds for Sara's doll. "Can you do that too?" Sara nods and stands up. She tries to copy her doll's moves and dance. She feels happy and proud. "Yay, Sara!" Tom cheers. He applauds louder and louder. Mom hears the noise and comes to see what they are doing. She smiles and joins them. "What a great show!" Mom says. "You are both very talented!" She hugs Sara and Tom and gives them a kiss. She also has a surprise for them. "Look what I have for you!" Mom says. She shows them a small box. She opens it and inside there is a shiny diamond. "It's a necklace for Sara and a ring for Tom!" Mom says. "They are very special and beautiful, just like you!" Sara and Tom gasp and clap their hands. They love the diamond gifts. They thank Mom and put them on. They feel very happy and lucky. They hug Mom and each other. They continue to play and dance and applaud.

One day, a big white bird had to deliver a gift to a far away nation. The bird was happy and excited to go on this trip. The gift was very special, and it would make many people happy. As the white bird flew over the big blue sea, something unexpected happened. The bird saw a very big fish jump out of the water. The big fish wanted to say hello to the bird. The bird was scared at first, but then they became friends. The big fish and the white bird played together in the sea. After playing, the white bird remembered the gift it had to deliver. The bird looked everywhere, but the gift was gone! The big fish felt sorry and wanted to help. They both looked for the gift and finally found it floating in the sea. The white bird thanked the big fish and continued its journey to deliver the gift to the far away nation.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a bicycle that he loved to ride. One day, it was very cold outside, but Tim still wanted to ride his bicycle. As Tim was riding his bicycle, he saw his friend, Sam. Sam was sad because he didn't have a bicycle. Tim thought for a moment and then mentioned to Sam, "Why don't we share my bicycle? We can take turns riding it." Sam smiled and they started to take turns riding the bicycle. They had so much fun together! The cold didn't bother them because they were happy and having a good time. In the end, Tim and Sam learned that sharing can make everyone happy.
