Title: Tim the Boy and the Toys

One day, a little boy named Tim found a dirty switch in his room. He didn't know what it did, so he called his mom. "Mom, what does this switch do?" he asked. His mom came into his room and looked at the switch. "Oh, Tim! That switch turns on the light in your closet. But it's very dirty. We should clean it," she said. They got a cloth and wiped the dirty switch until it was clean. Now, when Tim wanted to find his toys in the closet, he could use the clean switch to turn on the light. He was happy that he called his mom to help him. And his mom was happy that Tim learned something new. They both smiled and played with the toys together.

Once upon a time, there was a green house. It was very unusual! Inside the house lived a family: two mommies, a daddy and a little girl. One day, the family decided to settle the green house. That meant moving the big furniture and putting away all the toys and books. Together, the family worked hard to get the house ready. Everyone was very pleased with how the green house looked. It was so neat and tidy! Everyone felt happy and settled. The family enjoyed living in their green house and it was very unusual. They spent many happy days playing and having fun together. Everyone was sad when it was time to move and say goodbye to their green house.

Once upon a time, there was a big castle. A king and queen lived in the castle. They had a little boy. The little boy liked to play with his toy car. One day, the little boy found a toy he did not know. It was unknown to him. He asked his mom and dad, "Whose toy is this? Does it belong to me?" The king and queen did not know. They asked everyone in the castle, but no one knew. The little boy decided to find out who the toy belonged to. He asked his friends and the animals. Finally, he found a little girl who had lost her toy. The toy belonged to her. The little boy gave the toy back to the little girl. They became good friends and played together in the big castle.

Once upon a time, there was a nice man with a big mustache. He lived in a small house near a pretty park. Every day, he would walk and say hello to everyone he met. People liked him because he was always kind and helpful. One sunny day, the nice man with the big mustache saw a little girl crying in the park. He walked over and asked, "Why are you crying, little one?" The little girl looked up and said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." The nice man with the big mustache felt sad for her and said, "Don't worry, I will help you find your toy." They both looked around the park and wondered where the toy could be. After a while, they found the toy under a tree. The little girl was so happy and said, "Thank you, nice man with the big mustache!" He smiled and said, "You're welcome! Remember to always help others when they need it." The little girl nodded and they both went on their way. From that day on, the little girl always tried to be helpful and kind to others, just like the nice man with the big mustache. And everyone in the park lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it and to be kind to everyone you meet.

Once upon a time there was a chubby blue bear. He lived in a big forest and he loved to slide. Every day he would slide up and down the hill. He loved the way the air rushed past him when he went down. One day, he decided to go on a special adventure. He found a big slippery slide and thought it would be the perfect adventure. He gave it a try and loved it! He slid up and down the slide over and over again. At the end of the day, he was so tired but so happy. He had had so much fun. He looked at all the fun he had had and he smiled. He decided he wanted to come back and slide again and again!
