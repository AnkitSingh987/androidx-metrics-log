Title: Lily the Girl and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a shy little girl named Lily. She had a magic candle that could disappear. Lily loved her candle very much. One day, Lily was playing with her candle. She made it disappear and then come back. She was very happy. But then, she heard a loud noise. It scared her, and the candle disappeared again. Lily looked for her candle everywhere. She was very sad. But then, she remembered that she could make it come back. She closed her eyes and said, "Please, come back!" The magic candle came back, and Lily was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a big red engine. The engine had a job to do. It had to go fast and far to help people. But the engine was very impatient. It wanted to go now! One day, the engine saw a big door. The door was close. The engine wanted the door to open. It pushed and pushed, but the door did not move. The engine was sad and did not know what to do. Then, a small blue engine came. The blue engine was very smart. It knew how to open the door. The blue engine said, "Wait, I can help!" The blue engine pushed a button, and the door opened. The big red engine was happy and said, "Thank you!" The two engines went through the door, and they worked together to help people.

One day, a big cat found a telephone. The cat did not know what it was. The cat saw a little mouse playing near the telephone. The cat wanted to catch the mouse. The mouse saw the cat and ran away. The cat urged the mouse to come back. The mouse said, "No, you will catch me!" The cat said, "I will not catch you if you help me with this thing." The mouse came back and looked at the telephone. The mouse said, "This is a telephone. You can talk to other animals with it." The cat was happy and they both played with the telephone together. The cat did not catch the mouse, and they became friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim was sad. He saw his friends play a new game, but he did not know how to play yet. He sat on the grass and watched them. Tim's big sister, Sue, came to him and said, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told her he did not know how to play the new game. Sue smiled and said, "I will help you. Let's try it together." Sue encouraged Tim to play the game with her. They played together, and Tim started to learn. He was not sad anymore. He was happy to play with his friends, and they all had fun.
