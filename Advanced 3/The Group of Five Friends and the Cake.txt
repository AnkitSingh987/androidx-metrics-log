Title: The Group of Five Friends and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a group of five friends. They loved to play together. But one day, they found a big, yummy cake. They all wanted to eat the cake, but they did not know how to split it. One friend, a big, selfish boy, said, "I want the whole cake for myself!" The others were sad and did not like that idea. They all wanted a piece of the cake too. So, they decided to find a way to split the cake. Just when they were about to cut the cake, a big wind came and blew the cake away! The friends were very surprised. They laughed and realized that they should not be selfish. They decided to go and find another cake and make sure to split it nicely this time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a purple dress that she loved to wear. One day, Lucy went to her class. She was very happy. In the class, there were many kids, and they all liked to play together. Lucy saw her friend Tom. Tom said, "Look, I have a big box!" They were both excited. Lucy thought there could be something fun inside the box. She said, "Let's open it!" Tom and Lucy opened the box, and inside was a big, purple ball. They knew they would have lots of fun with the ball. As they played with the ball, it went up, up, up in the air. Suddenly, it got stuck in a tree. Lucy and Tom looked at each other and screamed, "Oh no!" They tried to get the ball down, but it was too high. Then, their teacher came and helped them. She got the ball down from the tree, and they were all happy. Lucy and Tom said, "Thank you!" They played with the purple ball for the rest of the day, and had lots of fun together.

One day, a little girl named Lily was looking at her calendar. She was confused because she did not know what day it was. She asked her mom, "Mom, what day is it today?" Her mom said, "Today is Monday, dear. We can improve your calendar by putting a big star on today's date." So, they put a big, shiny star on the calendar to show that it was Monday. The next day, Lily woke up and saw that the calendar had two stars on it! She was very surprised and confused. "Mom, why are there two stars on the calendar?" she asked. Her mom laughed and said, "Oh, your little brother must have put another star on the calendar. Let's fix it and put a big heart on today's date instead." From then on, Lily and her mom always used fun shapes to mark the days on the calendar, and Lily was never confused again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a funny man. This funny man could create special things. One day, he wanted to create winter in the town. The funny man started to dance and sing. He clapped his hands and stomped his feet. Snow began to fall from the sky. The town was now full of snow. The people were happy to see the winter. The children played in the snow. They made snowballs and threw them at each other. They laughed and had a lot of fun. But soon, the snow became too much. The snow was too high and too cold. The people were not happy anymore. The funny man tried to stop the winter. He danced and sang again. But the snow did not stop. The town was covered in snow and the people were very cold. In the end, the funny man could not stop the winter. The town stayed cold and snowy. The people were sad and the funny man was sad too.

Once upon a time, there lived a young girl named Anna. She was only three years old, but she was brave and full of imagination. One day, Anna went to the nearby park with her parents. As she wandered around, she noticed a mysterious club in the distance. Anna asked her mom and dad if she could go over and take a closer look. "Be careful," her mom said. "That place looks dead." Anna laughed and waved goodbye as she ran over to the club. When she arrived, she saw the door was ajar. Anna pushed it open and stepped inside. The place was dark and empty. Anna wasn't sure what she had found, but she knew it was an adventure waiting to happen. She explored the various rooms and corners, but she couldn't find anything exciting. So she decided it was time to go. But when she got to the front door, she realized it was closed. She tried to open it, but it wouldn't budge. Anna was stuck! Suddenly, a voice came from behind her. "Do you need help, little one?" It was an old man who had come in from outside. The man opened the door for Anna and said, "This place is a secret club. It's only open to those who have courage and imagination. I think you have both in abundance." Anna thanked the man and ran back to her parents
