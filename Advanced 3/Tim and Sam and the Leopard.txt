Title: Tim and Sam and the Leopard

Tim and Sam were brothers who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big truck with a cage on it. Inside the cage, there was a big cat with spots. It was a leopard. "Wow, look at that!" Tim said. "Can we go closer?" "OK, but be careful," Sam said. "Leopards are wild and can bite." They walked slowly to the truck and peeked at the leopard. The leopard looked at them and growled. It was not happy. It wanted to get out of the cage. Suddenly, the truck driver came back. He had a key in his hand. He opened the cage to give the leopard some food. But he did not see Tim and Sam. The leopard saw its chance. It jumped out of the cage and ran towards the boys. It was fast and angry. Tim and Sam were scared. They did not know what to do. They panicked. They screamed and ran away. "Help, help, a leopard is chasing us!" they shouted. Some people in the park heard them and saw the leopard. They were also scared. They called the police and the zoo. The police and the zoo came quickly. They had nets and guns. They caught the leopard and put it back in the cage. They thanked the truck driver for bringing the leopard to the zoo. They scolded him for being careless. They also talked to Tim and Sam. They told them they were brave and lucky. They told them not to go near wild animals again. They told them to be more careful. Tim and Sam nodded. They were sorry and relieved. They hugged each other and their mom. They learned a lesson. They never played near leopards again.

Once upon a time, there was a polite lion named Leo. He lived in a big forest with many friends. Leo liked to help his friends and provide them with things they needed. One day, a small bird named Bella lost her way. She was scared and hungry. Leo saw Bella and wanted to help her. He provided her with some food and showed her the way back to her nest. Bella was very happy and thanked Leo for his help. From that day on, they became best friends. All the animals in the forest loved Leo, the polite lion, who always helped them when they needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a beetle. He was very flexible and could move his body in all directions. He had a lot of adventures and loved to explore and do new things. One day, he encountered a wise old lizard and asked him for advice. The lizard told the beetle: "If you stay flexible and do your best, you can do anything!" The beetle smiled, thanked the wise lizard and continued his journey. The beetle explored a lot and enjoyed the peace and quiet of his adventures. Everywhere he went, he tried to stay flexible and do his best. One day, he came across a mud puddle in the middle of a clearing. He hopped in and soon found himself stuck in the mud! He tried to move and wriggled his body in all directions, but the harder he tried, the deeper he sunk. Suddenly, the wise words of the old lizard came back to him. He remembered that if he stayed flexible, he would be able to get out. He concentrated on staying flexible and allowing his body to move, and after a few minutes, he was able to pull himself out from the mud puddle! The beetle felt relieved, and grateful for the wise words of the lizard. From that day forward, he remembered the lesson he had learnt. No matter how stuck he felt, if he stayed flexible and did his best, he could reach any goal.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his blanket. He loved his blanket very much. It was not difficult for him to have fun with it. He would wear it like a cape and pretend to be a superhero. Tim's friend, Sally, came over to play. She saw Tim's blanket and said, "Can I play with your blanket too?" Tim said, "Yes, but we have to share it." They both played with the blanket, but they could not hear their moms calling them for dinner. As they played, the blanket got caught on a tree branch and ripped. Tim and Sally were very sad. They knew they could not fix it. They both went home for dinner and told their moms about the ripped blanket. Their moms were not happy and said they should have been more careful. Now, Tim did not have his favorite blanket anymore.
