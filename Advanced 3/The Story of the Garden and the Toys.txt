Title: The Story of the Garden and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a grandma and a little girl. The little girl was only three years old. Every day, the little girl would visit her grandma to play. She loved to play with old, rusty toys in her grandma’s garden. One day, the grandma warned, “Be careful, little one. Take small, careful steps around here.” The little girl listened to her grandma and did as she said. She stepped slowly and lightly around the garden. But then the little girl stepped on something sharp. It gave her a shock. “Ouch!” she cried. Her grandma smiled down at her and said, “I told you before to take small, careful steps around here. That is the lesson for today.” The little girl learned that day that being careful can help us to stay safe. Taking small, careful steps is always a good idea!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived with her mom and dad in a light blue house. Mia loved to help her mom and dad with chores. One day, her mom asked her to go to the market with her. At the market, Mia and her mom saw many things. They saw red apples, big bananas, and round oranges. Mia's mom said, "Mia, can you help me pick some apples?" Mia was happy to help. She picked the best apples she could find. After they got the apples, Mia and her mom went to pay. The man at the market said, "You have nice apples! Good job, Mia!" Mia felt proud and thanked the man. They went back home and had a yummy fruit salad for lunch. Mia was happy she could help her mom at the market.

Once upon a time, there was a little brown dog. The dog liked to run and play. One day, he saw a big sign in the park. The dog could not read, but he saw a picture of a dog on the sign. He felt happy and wanted to play near the sign. The little brown dog started to run around the sign. He saw a big tree and decided to wonder what was behind it. The dog found a small hole in the ground. He thought it would be fun to dig and see what was inside the hole. The dog dug and dug, but he did not find anything. He was very sad. The little brown dog did not know that the sign said, "Do not dig here." The dog got very dirty and tired. He went home, but his family was not happy. They had to give the dog a bath and clean the mess he made. The little brown dog learned not to dig near signs anymore.

Billy was walking down the street with his daddy. He was so happy, because his daddy was bringing him an extra special surprise. "Look Daddy, what's in the bag?" asked Billy. "It's a surprise! You'll find out soon!" replied his dad. They approached a fat man and Billy's dad said "Excuse me, could you help us?" "Sure I can!" replied the fat man. "What can I do?" "I was wondering if you could do me a favor and bring this bag inside that house over there." "No problem," smiled the fat man. He grabbed the bag from Billy's daddy and headed to the house. A few minutes later, the fat man returned with the bag looking happy. He handed the bag back to Billy's dad and said goodbye. Billy was so excited, he couldn't wait to see what was in the bag. His dad smiled and said "Go ahead Billy, open the bag." Billy opened the bag, and inside he found a big, beautiful toy train set! He was so excited, he gave his dad a giant hug. They thanked the fat man for helping them, and then happily went home to play with their new toy train set.

Once upon a time there was a little pig. Her name was Penny. Penny was very sorry, because she had lost her favorite banana. She wanted to find it, but she didn't know where to look. Then one day, Penny had an idea. She decided to go to the market to see if her banana was there. She looked all around until she finally found it! She was so excited and ran to get it. But just before she could take her banana, the storekeeper stopped her. He said he couldn't let her take it. He said that he wanted to prevent other people from stealing the bananas. Penny was sad, but she knew the storekeeper was right. She said "I'm sorry," and she walked away. But later that day she went back to the market and bought a new banana. This time she was very careful to not lose it!
