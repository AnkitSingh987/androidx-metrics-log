Title: Let and the Dog

One day, a funny cat and a funny dog were playing near a fence. The cat whispered to the dog, "Let's play hide and seek!" The dog said, "Yes, I love that game!" The cat closed her eyes and started to count. The dog ran and hid behind the fence. The cat counted to ten and said, "Ready or not, here I come!" She looked everywhere but could not find the dog. She was very confused. As the cat was looking for the dog, she heard a whisper behind her. "Boo!" It was the dog! He had jumped over the fence to surprise the cat. They both laughed and played the game again. They had a great time and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a brave boy named John. He had a dream of finding a beautiful island and so one day, he decided to go. He put on his hat, grabbed his bucket and skipped happily to the beach. John could see an island far away, so he skipped across the waves, feeling proud. Suddenly, the waves were getting bigger and bigger, until they were too big for John to cross. John was so sad that he couldn’t get to the island, until he saw a sailboat. He thought this was his chance and quickly hopped on board. He was so excited to get to the island that he forgot about being careful. But the boat was going too fast, and it capsized. John fell into the cold water. He tried to swim, but it was too difficult. Sadly, John was never able to reach the island. He was so proud, but he was never able to make his dream come true.

One day, a small, fragile bug named Tim went for a walk. Tim liked to spin around in the grass. He would spin so fast that he would get dizzy. Tim was happy when he spun. One day, Tim met a big bug named Tom. Tom said, "Hi Tim! I like to spin too! Let's spin together for a week!" Tim thought this was a great idea. They spun and spun, and they were both very happy. But one day, while they were spinning, something unexpected happened. They spun so fast that they turned into beautiful butterflies! Tim and Tom were surprised and happy. They flew away together, still spinning and laughing.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Hannah who was 3 years old. She had a baby sister named Ellie. One day, Hannah asked her mother, "What is a baby?" Her mother smiled and explained, "A baby is someone who is small, sleeps a lot and needs help to stay safe. It is important that we take care of babies, including your sister." Hannah thought for a moment and replied, "But other people don't have babies." Her mother nodded and explained, "That's true. But we should always remember to be kind to everyone, especially those who may just be starting out in their lives. You can help by being extra kind to your sister, because she is sleepy a lot and needs lots of love and extra special care." Hannah smiled and said, "Yes, I can do that!" Morale: It is important to always be kind and look out for those who may need help.

Once upon a time, there was a boy who loved to draw. He had a big box of crayons. His favorite crayon was red. He used it all the time. It was easy for him to make pretty pictures with his red crayon. One day, he wanted to draw a big sun. He took his red crayon and started to draw. But the crayon broke! The boy was very sad. He tried to fix it, but it was not easy. He asked his mom for help. She tried to fix the crayon, but it was too small. The boy was very sad. He could not draw with his red crayon anymore. The boy learned that sometimes things break, and it's not always easy to fix them.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She was very creative and loved to make things with her toys. She had a big box full of memories, like her favorite doll and a pretty hat. Mia's friend Tom came over to play. They were having fun when Tom leaned on the box of memories and it fell over. All of Mia's memories spilled out on the floor. Mia was sad and said, "Tom, you made a big mess!" Tom felt bad and wanted to fix it. He had an idea. He said, "Mia, let's make a new memory! We can use your toys to make a fun game." Mia liked the idea and they started to play. As they played, they found a toy they had never seen before. It was a magic wand. They waved it and all the memories went back into the box. Mia and Tom were so happy. They learned that even when things go wrong, they can still make new memories together.
