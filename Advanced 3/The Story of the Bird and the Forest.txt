Title: The Story of the Bird and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small, yellow bird. This bird had many animal friends. One day, the yellow bird saw a big, scary animal coming near the forest. The yellow bird flew to her animal friends and said, "I must warn you! Big, scary animal is coming!" The other animals were scared and asked, "What can we do?" The yellow bird had an idea. "Let's hide in our homes and be very quiet," she said. All the animals hid in their homes, just like the yellow bird said. The big, scary animal came into the forest, but could not find any animals to bother. It left the forest, sad and alone. The yellow bird and her friends were safe and happy. They thanked the yellow bird for her help and they all played together in the forest.

Once upon a time there was a grumpy old man. Every day he woke up early in the morning, and went about his routine. But one day the old man realised he had lost his calendar. He was very grumpy and couldn't remember what he was supposed to do that day. He asked his young neighbour, “Do you know where my calendar went?” The young neighbour thought carefully, before saying, “Maybe it's important to stick to your routine, it helps us to remember things.” The old man thought about this for a moment before he understood. He thanked the young neighbour and went back to his house. When the old man arrived home, he saw his calendar on the kitchen table. He had forgotten it was the day his favourite neighbour was coming over to visit. From that day onward, the old man was much happier and never lost the calendar again. Moral of the story: it's important to stick to a routine, it helps us to remember things.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little boat. It wanted to sail in the sky. The boat was very sad because it could not fly like a bird. One day, a kind bird saw the boat and said, "I can help you sail in the sky!" The boat was very happy. The bird picked up the boat with its beak and flew up to the ceiling of a big room. The boat said, "Wow! I am sailing in the sky! Thank you, bird!" The bird smiled and said, "You're welcome, cute boat! I am happy to help you." The boat and the bird became very good friends. They played together every day. And the cute little boat was never sad again.

One day, a bossy cat named Tom found a big eraser. He wanted to wrap it as a gift for his friend, a dog named Max. "I will give this to Max," Tom said. Tom found some paper and started to wrap the eraser. As he was wrapping it, a little bird came and said, "What are you doing, Tom?" Tom said, "I am wrapping a gift for Max." The bird wanted to help, but Tom was bossy and said, "No, I can do it myself." When Tom finished wrapping the eraser, he went to Max's house. Max was very happy to see Tom and the gift. But when Max opened the gift, there was a big surprise! The eraser was gone, and inside the wrap was a toy car. Tom and Max were both very happy with the unexpected gift, and they played with the toy car all day long.

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play outside in the spring, when the sun was warm and the flowers were pretty. They liked to skip on the grass, and look for bugs and birds. One day, they saw a big hill near their house. It looked very high and impressive. Lily and Ben wanted to climb it and see what was on the other side. "Let's go, Lily!" Ben said. "It will be fun!" "I don't know, Ben," Lily said. "It looks scary. What if we fall?" "Don't be afraid, Lily," Ben said. "We can do it. We are strong and brave. Come on, follow me!" Ben started to skip up the hill, but Lily stayed behind. She was not sure if she wanted to go. She watched Ben go higher and higher, until he was almost at the top. "Look, Lily, I made it!" Ben shouted. "It's amazing up here! You have to see it!" Lily felt happy for Ben, but also sad for herself. She wished she had gone with him. She decided to try to skip up the hill too. She took a deep breath and started to move. But she was too late. The hill was steep and slippery, and Lily lost her balance. She fell down and hurt her knee. She cried out loud. Ben heard her cry and ran down to help her. He saw her knee bleeding and felt sorry for her. "Are you okay, Lily?" he asked. "I'm sorry, I should have waited for you." "It's okay, Ben," Lily said. "I'm sorry, I should have gone with you. I was scared, but I wanted to see the hill too." Ben hugged Lily and said, "It's okay, Lily. We are still best friends. Next time, we can go together. And we can skip slower and safer. The hill will still be there." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Ben. You are a good friend. And you are very impressive. You skipped all the way to the top!" They both laughed and walked back to their house, holding hands. They learned that skipping is fun, but it is better to do it with a friend, and to be careful and not too fast. They also learned that being brave is good, but being kind is better. They were happy and proud of themselves.
