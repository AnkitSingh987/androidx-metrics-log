Title: Tim the Boy and the Sam

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play ball on the pitch. One day, a new kid named Sam came to play. Sam was very good at playing ball, and Tim felt jealous. Tim said to his friend, "I promise I will play better than Sam." His friend smiled and said, "You can do it, Tim!" The next day, Tim practiced very hard. But then, something unexpected happened. Sam came to Tim and said, "I saw you play. You are good. Can we play together?" Tim was surprised. He thought Sam would be mean, but Sam was nice. They played together and became best friends. And they promised to always help each other get better at playing ball.

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing in her room. She saw her mom was sad and frustrated. Her mom was trying to clean the floor with a mop, but the mop was not working well. Sue wanted to help her mom feel better. Sue thought of a plan. She went to the kitchen and found a new mop. She took the new mop to her mom and said, "Mom, I will help you!" Her mom was very happy and they started to clean the floor together. While they were cleaning, they heard a loud noise. It was a big, funny cat that came in the house! The cat saw the old mop and started to play with it. The cat was very good at cleaning the floor with the old mop. Sue, her mom, and the funny cat cleaned the floor together and had lots of fun. The mom was not frustrated anymore, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, a rhinoceros and a lion. The lion and the rhinoceros were very different, the lion had a mane and the rhinoceros had horns, but they would always have lots of fun together. One day they decided to go on a trip to explore the jungle. But while they were exploring they got lost and they couldn't find their way back. They both got very scared and started to argue. The lion said, “It’s all your fault that we’re lost. Do something to help us find our way back!” The rhinoceros said, “No, it’s not my fault. You’re the one who said to go here in the first place!” The lion was so angry that he roared and chased after the rhinoceros. But just as the lion was about to strike the rhinoceros, he stopped and said, “I’m sorry. Please forgive me.” The rhinoceros hugged the lion and said, “I forgive you, let’s never argue again. Let’s find our way home together as best friends.” So the lion and the rhinoceros worked together, and eventually they found their way back. From then on they never argued

One day, a mild cat named Tom went out to play. Tom took his camera with him. He wanted to take pictures of his friends. He saw his friend, Sam the dog, and said, "Hi Sam! Let's play and take pictures." Tom and Sam played and took many pictures. They saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tom said, "Let's settle down and take a picture by the big tree." But Sam was scared of the big tree. He didn't want to climb it. Tom could see that Sam was scared. He had an idea. He said, "Sam, we don't have to climb the big tree. We can just take a picture next to it." Sam was happy with Tom's idea. They took a picture next to the big tree and had a great day playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a baby. He was very cheerful. Everywhere he went, he touched everything. He touched a chair, a ball, a toy train, and even a flower. Everywhere he went he touched something with his little hands. He was so cheerful that it made everyone around him smile. One day, he went outside to the garden. He touched the flowers and the leaves and smiled. He touched the grass with his little feet and laughed. It felt so soft and ticklish! He was so cheerful that he began to dance. He jumped around and laughed. The baby climbed up a tree. He touched each branch as he went higher and higher. Suddenly, he slipped and was about to fall. But then something magical happened. A giant hand came out of nowhere and caught him. It was the kind fairies. They took him in their hands and flew away with him. What became of the baby? Well, he had the most magical adventure of his life. He went to places he'd never seen before. He laughed and giggled and touched everything he encountered. Everywhere he went had a cheerful feeling about it. Eventually, the fairies brought him home, safe and sound.
