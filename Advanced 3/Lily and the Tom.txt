Title: Lily and the Tom

Lily liked to play hide and seek with her big brother Tom. She was very good at finding hiding spots that he could not see. One day, she had a new idea. She saw a big blanket on the couch. She thought, I can hide under the blanket and Tom will not find me. She ran to the couch and crawled under the blanket. She made herself very small and quiet. She heard Tom count to ten and say, ready or not, here I come. She smiled and waited for him to look for her. Tom looked everywhere for Lily. He looked under the bed, behind the curtains, in the closet, but he could not find her. He started to feel frustrated and confused. Where could she be? He thought. He went back to the living room and saw the couch. He did not notice the blanket. He thought, maybe she is behind the couch. He walked to the couch and looked behind it. He saw nothing. He scratched his head and sighed. He was about to give up when he heard a giggle. He turned around and saw Lily's face appear from under the blanket. She said, boo! Tom jumped and screamed. He was so surprised and scared. He dropped to the floor and covered his eyes. Lily laughed and laughed. She thought, I got him good. He looked so crazy. She got out of the couch and hugged him. She said, I win, I win. Tom smiled and hugged her back. He said, you win, you win. You are the best hider ever. They both laughed and played some more.

Once upon a time, there was a group of yellow chickens on a farm. Every morning they would run around, looking for things to eat and play. One day, they found something even better - a basket filled with delicious grass and seeds. The chickens were so excited! They took some of the grass and seeds back to the barn to share with their friends. The chickens started to prepare the food and made a big, tasty feast. The chickens were all so happy, they decided to have a party. They danced around and sang cheerful songs all day. Suddenly, one of the chickens noticed something in the corner of the barn. It was a giant yellow egg! The chicken ran to get the others and said, “Let’s prepare the egg! We can share it with the rest of the group!” The chickens worked quickly and soon had the egg ready for their friends. Everyone lined up and sang a happy song as they savored the delicious meal. The yellow chickens enjoyed their party and danced until the sun went down. They ended the day with happy bellies and smiles on their faces!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom did not like to play with other cats. He liked to be alone. One day, Tom saw a line of ants. The ants were in a file, walking one after the other. They were carrying food to their home. Tom was curious and decided to follow the ants. As Tom followed the ants, he saw them work together and help each other. This made Tom feel happy. He learned that being with friends and helping each other was fun. From that day on, Tom was not grumpy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little dolphin named Dolly. She lived in the big blue sea with her family. Dolly was a happy dolphin who loved to play and swim with her friends. One day, while Dolly was playing, her friend Sam the Fish said, "Hey Dolly, I found a big shell! Let's go see it!" They swam to the shell, but when they got there, they saw a dead crab. Dolly and Sam were sad, but then something unexpected happened. The crab started to move! It wasn't dead after all! The crab said, "Hello, I'm Carl the Crab! I was just playing a trick. I can disappear and reappear whenever I want!" Dolly and Sam were surprised and happy that the crab was alive. They all became friends and played together in the sea.
