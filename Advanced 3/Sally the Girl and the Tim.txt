Title: Sally the Girl and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sally. Sally had a big pair of scissors. She loved to cut paper with them. One day, Sally saw a boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the scissors. "Why are you scared, Tim?" asked Sally. Tim said, "I am scared because the scissors are sharp. They might hurt me." Sally wanted to make Tim feel better. She thought of a fun game. "Let's play pretend," she said. "We can pretend to marry the scissors to a paper doll." Tim thought this was a good idea. They made a paper doll and had a pretend wedding for the doll and the scissors. Tim wasn't scared anymore. Sally and Tim became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a large tree. Under the tree, there was a present. The present was very pretty. It had a big red bow on it. A little boy named Tim saw the present. He wanted to know who it was for. Tim asked the tree, "Who is this present for?" The tree did not answer. Tim asked the birds, "Who is this present for?" The birds did not answer. Tim asked the wind, "Who is this present for?" The wind did not answer. Tim was sad. Then, a big, friendly dog came. The dog said, "The present is for you, Tim!" Tim was very happy. He opened the present. Inside was a toy car. Tim played with the toy car and the dog all day. They had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy cat. The cat lived in a small house with a big tree. The tree had many branches. The cat liked to play on the tree. One day, the cat saw a big branch. The cat wanted to twist and play on the branch. The cat jumped and twisted on the branch. The cat was happy. But then, the branch broke. The cat fell down. The cat was sad and hurt. The cat could not play on the tree anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a mouse. He was walking around looking for food when he noticed something. It was a big piece of cheese. He was so excited that he ran over and started to eat it. But then he heard a loud voice. It was coming from a big, scary cat. The cat was heading right towards him! The mouse was so ashamed that he ran away quickly. He tried to find somewhere to hide, but it was too late. The cat was close behind him. The mouse was so scared that he tripped over and fell. The cat was very angry and scooped up the mouse. The cat carried the mouse away, far away from the cheese. The mouse was never seen again. He was so embarrassed and ashamed that he never wanted to go back, ever again.

Once there was a keen little boy. He wanted to try new sports. So one day he put on his special clothes and ran outside. He saw some kids playing a game with a ball! He was so eager to join in. He ran over and asked to join in. The other kids looked at him and said “you too small to play with us”. But he didn’t let that stop him. He ran forward and grabbed the ball. Then he snapped it up in the air. Everyone was so surprised at how well he played. So the other kids let him play with them. He had lots of fun playing and running for the ball. He was so happy to be part of the game. He even made some new friends. As the afternoon went by, everyone smiled and laughed as they had a wonderful time playing sports together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Tina who loved to explore the outdoors. One day, Tina's mommy and daddy suggested they go on a camping trip together. Tina was so excited! The next day, they loaded up their car and drove to the campground. Daddy took care of setting up the tent while mommy scouted out a good firewood spot. Tina went exploring along the trails and soon she came across a beautiful butterfly. "Look, Mommy! It's so slow and pretty," Tina said. "Yes, it sure is," Mommy replied. Tina stood watching the butterfly until it flapped its wings and flew - straight into her hands! Holding the butterfly gently, Tina said "Bye bye, little butterfly. I'll let you go now," and she opened her hands and released the butterfly. The butterfly flew away, flapping its wings slowly into the sky until it was out of sight. Tina smiled and said "Bye bye butterfly!" The end.
