Title: Sam and the Car

Sam liked to play with his cars. He had many cars of different colors and sizes. He liked to make them go fast on the floor and make loud noises. He also liked to match his cars with the same color wheels. He had red wheels, blue wheels, yellow wheels and green wheels. One day, he found a new car in his toy box. It was black and shiny and had four big wheels. Sam was very happy. He wanted to match the new car with black wheels, but he could not find any. He looked everywhere in his toy box, but there were only red, blue, yellow and green wheels. He was sad. He decided to try the other wheels on the new car. He put a red wheel on the front left, a blue wheel on the front right, a yellow wheel on the back left and a green wheel on the back right. He looked at the car and smiled. He thought it looked funny and colorful. He made the car go fast on the floor and made loud noises. But then, something bad happened. The wheels did not fit well on the new car. They were too loose and wobbly. As Sam made the car go fast, the wheels fell off one by one. The car crashed on the floor and made a sour noise. Sam was very sad. He picked up the car and the wheels and put them back in his toy box. He wished he had black wheels for his new car. The next day, his mom came home with a surprise for him. She gave him a big hug and a small box. She said it was a gift for being a good boy. Sam opened the box and saw four black wheels. He was very happy. He ran to his toy box and took out his new car. He put the black wheels on the new car and they fit perfectly. He looked at the car and smiled. He made the car go fast on the floor and made loud noises. He thanked his mom and gave her a big hug. He was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a small, tight family who lived in a little house. In the house, they had a television. The family loved to watch fun shows together. One day, the sun was shining, and Mom said, "Let's go to the park!" But little Timmy wanted to remain at home and watch his favorite show on the television. "Please, Mom, can I stay and watch?" he asked. Mom thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, Timmy, you can remain here and watch your show. But when it is over, you must come to the park with us." Timmy was happy and hugged his mom tight. At the park, the family played games and had a great time. When Timmy's show was over, he went to the park and joined them. They all had fun together and then went home to eat a yummy dinner. From that day on, the family made sure to spend time both watching television and playing outside. They learned that it was important to have a balance between the two. And they remained a happy, tight family.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his own toys and run around in the yard. One day, Spot's family decided to go to the mall. Spot was very excited because he had never been to the mall before. When they got to the mall, Spot saw many people and other dogs. He was happy to see so many new faces. Spot's family went to different stores and Spot got to see many new things. He even got to pick out his own toy at the pet store. At the end of the day, Spot and his family went home. Spot was tired but very happy with his new toy. He played with it all night and couldn't wait to go back to the charming mall again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch movies with his family. One day, they decided to watch a new movie together. Tim was very excited. As they started the movie, Tim began to feel frightened. There were big, scary monsters in the movie. Tim hid behind his mom and dad, but he still wanted to complete the movie with his family. Finally, the movie ended and the scary monsters went away. Tim was happy that he completed the movie with his family. They all hugged and laughed, and Tim was not frightened anymore. They all went to bed and had sweet dreams.
