Title: Lily the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big head with lots of curly hair. She loved to wear pretty things on her head. Every day, she would pick a new headband or hat to wear. One day, Lily saw a small, helpless bird outside her window. The bird had lost its way and could not find its family. Lily wanted to help the bird, so she put on her favorite hat and went outside. Lily took the bird in her hands and looked for its family. She walked and walked, and finally, she found the bird's family. The bird was so happy, and Lily felt good for helping. That night, she wore her favorite hat to bed, feeling like a true helper.

Tom and Lily are playing in the park. They see a big orange ball on the grass. They run to the ball and touch it. "Wow, this ball is very soft and bouncy!" Tom says. "Can we play with it?" Lily asks. They look around and do not see anyone. They decide to play with the ball. They throw it, catch it, and kick it. They have a lot of fun. A man comes to the park. He observes Tom and Lily playing with his ball. He is not happy. He walks to them and says: "Hey, that is my ball! Give it back to me!" Tom and Lily are scared. They do not know the man. They drop the ball and run away. The man picks up his ball. He looks at it and sees that it is dirty and scratched. He is angry. He says: "This is a special ball. It is made of rubber. It is very expensive. You should not play with other people's things!" He puts the ball in his bag and leaves the park. He does not see Tom and Lily hiding behind a tree. They are sad and sorry. They wish they had asked before playing with the ball. They learn a lesson.

Lily liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Anna. Anna had a blue dress and long hair. Lily took Anna everywhere. She talked to Anna and hugged her. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom and Anna. She saw a big slide and wanted to try it. She left Anna on a bench and ran to the slide. She had fun sliding down and up again. She forgot about Anna. When it was time to go home, Lily looked for Anna. But Anna was not on the bench. Lily felt sad and scared. She cried and asked her mom where Anna was. Her mom said they had to look for Anna. They asked the other kids and the park keeper, but no one saw Anna. Lily wished she had a better memory. She wished she remembered where she put Anna. She felt like her heart was empty without Anna. She hoped Anna was not lost or lonely. Then, she saw a boy holding Anna. He was walking to the exit with his dad. Lily ran to him and said, "That's my doll! That's Anna! Please give her back!" The boy looked surprised and said, "Sorry, I thought she was mine. I have a doll like her at home. Here, you can have her." He handed Anna to Lily. Lily was so happy. She hugged Anna and thanked the boy. She said sorry for forgetting her. She told her mom she would improve. She would never leave Anna alone again. She would always remember where she put her. She smiled and kissed Anna. She felt her heart was full again.

Once upon a time, in a small modern house, there lived a kind cat named Mimi. Mimi loved to play with her toy mouse, and she would squeeze it every day. She was very happy and had lots of fun. One sunny day, Mimi was playing with her toy mouse when she saw a big red ball outside. She wanted to play with the ball, but she got into trouble. Mimi was too small to push the ball, and it rolled on top of her toy mouse. Mimi was sad and did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. A friendly dog named Max came to help Mimi. Max was very strong and could push the big red ball away. Mimi was happy and thanked Max. They became best friends, and they played together every day. Mimi learned that even when you are in trouble, friends can help you.
