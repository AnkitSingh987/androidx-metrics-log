Title: The Grumpy Uncle and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy uncle. He lived in a small house near the park. He had a big garden with many flowers and trees. He loved his garden very much. One day, a little girl came to play in the park. She saw the pretty flowers in the grumpy uncle's garden. She wanted to pick some flowers to take home. But the grumpy uncle said, "No, you cannot pick my flowers. They will spoil if you take them." The little girl was sad, but she did not want to spoil the flowers. She went back to the park to play. The grumpy uncle watched her playing and felt bad. He thought, "Maybe I can give her some flowers, so she will be happy." So, the grumpy uncle picked some flowers from his garden and took them to the little girl. He said, "Here are some flowers for you. Please be careful and do not spoil them." The little girl was so happy, and she said, "Thank you, uncle!" From that day on, the grumpy uncle was not grumpy anymore. He and the little girl became good friends. They played in the park and took care of the flowers together. And the flowers never spoiled.

Once upon a time, there was a polite cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her friends, a dog named Doggy and a bird named Birdy. They always had fun together, but they wanted to find a way to unite and play a new game. One day, Kitty had an idea. "Let's play a game with a measure!" she said. Doggy and Birdy agreed, and they found a small stick to use as their measure. They decided to play a game where they would take turns jumping over the stick. "Okay, I'll go first," said Doggy. He jumped over the stick and landed on the other side. Birdy went next, flying just a little bit to get over the stick. Finally, it was Kitty's turn. She jumped very high and landed softly on the other side. All three friends were happy that they found a new way to unite and play together. They continued playing their new game with the measure, laughing and enjoying their time. And from that day on, they always remembered to be polite and include each other in their games.

Once upon a time, there was a normal garden. It had pretty flowers and green grass. The sun shined on the garden every day, and the rain made the plants grow big and strong. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the garden. She saw a tiny bug on a leaf. The bug said, "Hi! My name is Buzz. I can perform a dance for you." Lily was happy to see the bug dance. Buzz danced on the leaf, and Lily clapped her hands. They had fun in the normal garden. When it was time to go home, Lily said goodbye to Buzz. She knew she would come back to the garden to see her new friend again.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who lived in a house near the beach. Every day he loved to go to the beach and play in the sand. Yesterday was especially special because he got to play with his best friend. They had lots of fun until it started to rain, so they decided to go inside. But when the little boy wanted to leave, he discovered that the door was dry! His little friend said to him, "Let's go through the window!" But when the little boy opened the window, he discovered that the beach had been destroyed by the rain! He was so upset! He wanted to cry, but he was too shocked to do so. Then his little friend said, "Come on, let's go home." The little boy sadly said, "Yes, let's go home." So they both left the wet beach and went home. The little boy was so upset that he couldn't stop thinking about what had happened yesterday.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big toy bear that she loved very much. The bear was big and soft. Lily liked to hold the bear on her shoulder when she walked around her house. One day, Lily's friend Tom came to visit her. Tom saw the bear and said, "I want to play with the bear too!" Lily thought it was difficult to share her bear, but she let Tom hold the bear on his shoulder. Tom and Lily played with the bear all day. But when it was time for Tom to go home, he did not want to give the bear back to Lily. Tom took the bear with him and Lily was very sad. She cried and cried, but the bear did not come back.
