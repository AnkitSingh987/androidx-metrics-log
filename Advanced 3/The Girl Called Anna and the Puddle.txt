Title: The Girl Called Anna and the Puddle

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Anna. Anna felt very troubled. She was sad and lonely. One day, Anna decided to take a stroll near the park. As she walked, she felt the warm sun on her face and saw the bright blue sky - and it made her feel a little bit better. Suddenly she stopped. Just ahead, on the other side of the park, was a giant puddle. Anna had an idea! She went over to the puddle and put one foot in. She squeezed her eyes shut and sighed. It felt so nice to soak her hot feet in the cool water. But then, Anna heard a noise. Someone had seen her. It was a park ranger. He said, "Young lady! You must leave this puddle alone. If you stay here any longer, you will get in trouble." Anna was scared, so she quickly left the puddle and ran home. Once upon a time, there was a young musician. He liked to play his drum all day. He made happy sounds and everyone liked to listen. One day, a little bird came to the musician. The bird said, "Please don't disturb my sleep. I am very tired." The musician felt sad. He did not want to make the bird unhappy. The next day, the young musician played his drum softly. The little bird slept and did not feel disturbed. When the bird woke up, it sang a pretty song. The musician and the bird became good friends. They played music together and everyone loved to listen.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play all day long. He was a very persistent dog. He never wanted to stop playing. One day, Spot saw a big ball in the park. He wanted to play with it, but another dog was already playing with the ball. The other dog did not want to share. Spot felt sad. Spot did not stop trying to play with the ball. He was very persistent. Finally, the other dog saw how much Spot wanted to play and decided to share the ball. They played together and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had many toys, but his most favorite toy was his slide. He loved to play on his slide all day, and he was very happy. Tim's slide was big and sometimes difficult to climb, but he never gave up trying. One day, Tim saw a small bird near his slide. The bird was sad because it couldn't fly up to its nest. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he thought of a plan. He knew his slide could help the bird, but he didn't know how yet. The next day, Tim tried to help the bird by putting his slide close to the tree. The bird hopped on the slide and used it to get to its nest. The bird was so happy that it sang a beautiful song for Tim. The slide was difficult to move, but Tim was happy that he could help his new friend. And from that day on, Tim and the bird played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a happy boy named Tom. Tom was full of energy and always loved exploring. One morning Tom decided to take a walk in the woods near his house. He came across a beautiful lake. In the middle of the lake was a small island. Tom wanted to complete his adventure by reaching the island. He asked his dad, "Can I go to the island, Dad?" "It's too long a way for you, it's too deep for you to walk," Dad said. Tom was disappointed, but he did not give up. He asked his mom, "Mom, can I use a boat to get to the island?" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tom, you can use a boat. You can use mine." Tom was so excited. He jumped into the boat and set sail. After a long time of sailing he finally arrived at the island and felt happy to have completed his journey.
