Title: Vera and the Garden

One day, Vera was walking around her garden with Mum. They saw a bright blue bird singing in the trees. Vera was so happy and smiled and looked up at the sky. But then suddenly, Mum said it was time to go to the airport. Vera sighed, feeling sad. But then Mum gave her a big hug and said it would be alright. As Vera and Mum went to the airport, Vera saw lots of amazing things. There were planes taking off and there were cool cars everywhere. At the airport, Mum smiled at Vera and said goodbye. Vera felt a little bit better now as Mum had given her a gentle kiss on her forehead. Vera waved goodbye to her mum as she flew away. Vera knew it was time to go back home.

Once upon a time, in a warm and sunny land, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside and explore nature. One day, while she was playing, she saw a big tree with lots of pretty leaves. She wanted to stretch her arms and touch the leaves. As Lily tried to reach the leaves, a mean and grumpy dog came near her. The dog did not like that Lily was near the tree. He barked loudly and scared Lily. She was sad and didn't know what to do. Lily ran back to her house and told her mom about the mean dog. Her mom hugged her and said they could not play near that tree anymore. Lily was sad that she could not touch the pretty leaves and play in nature. The mean dog had won, and Lily could not enjoy her warm, sunny days outside like before.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle boy named Tim. Tim loved to look at things with his microscope. One day, he found a small, round thing in the yard. He picked it up and took it inside. Tim said, "I will look at this thing with my microscope." He put the thing under the microscope and began to rub it. As he rubbed, the thing started to grow bigger and bigger. Tim was very surprised. The thing grew so big that it turned into a friendly giant. The giant said, "Thank you for rubbing me, Tim. I was stuck as a small thing for a long time." Tim and the giant became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had amazing skin that was soft and smooth. Lily loved to play outside and choose her favorite toy to play with every day. One day, Lily met a new friend named Sam. Sam asked, "What toy will you choose to play with today?" Lily thought and said, "I will choose my big, red ball. We can play catch together." Lily and Sam had so much fun playing catch with the big, red ball. They laughed and ran around the yard. After playing, they sat down on the grass, feeling happy and tired. Lily's amazing skin glowed in the sun, and Sam said, "Your skin is so nice, just like a baby's." They both smiled and knew they would be friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Penny. She had a big dog named Max. Penny loved to feed Max every day. She thought it was a useful thing to do. Max was always happy when Penny fed him. One day, Penny and Max went to the park. They saw a little boy with a red ball. The boy said, "Hi, my name is Tom. Do you want to play with me?" Penny and Max said yes. They played with the ball and had lots of fun. Later, the ball rolled away and got stuck in a tree. Tom was sad. Penny had an idea. She said, "Max, do you remember when I feed you? You can be useful now!" Max jumped up and got the ball out of the tree. Tom was happy again. Penny, Max, and Tom played until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a very important job. He had to squeeze the alarm when there was trouble. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. He knew he had to help. So, he ran and squeezed the alarm. The loud noise told everyone there was a problem. Soon, a big, strong man came to help. He climbed the tree and saved the cat. Everyone was happy and thanked Tim for squeezing the alarm. Tim felt proud of his important job.
