Title: The Girl Called Cindy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Cindy. Cindy was very generous, sharing all of her toys with others. One day, her mum said that it was time to get ready for bed. Cindy hopped into her pjs, brushed her teeth and ran to her bed. As she was getting into bed, she accidently hit her knee on the bedpost. She couldn't help but cry out in pain. Mum came rushing into the room and comforted Cindy, whispering that everything was alright. Cindy quickly stopped crying and thanked her mum. Mum then stroked Cindy's hair and said, "You know, you are so generous; I'm so proud of you." Cindy smiled and gave her a big hug. She was very thankful for her mum's kind words. "Thank you Mum!" she said. Mum smiled and tucked Cindy into bed, giving her a goodnight kiss. Cindy soon fell asleep, content and happy.

Once there was an elephant. He wanted to escape his cage. He was very tight and fit so he squeezed out of the bars. He was so happy and excited to have escaped. He then looked around and noticed he was in a big city. He thought, "What have I done?!" He started to wonder how to get back to the jungle where he belonged. He noticed a little girl passing by and he asked her, "Where am I?" She looked surprised and said, "You are in the city and you can't go back to the jungle now." The elephant felt so sad and he began to regret ever leaving his cage. He knew he would never be able to escape the city. He would never be able to go back to the jungle. He had made a mistake and there was no way he could ever undo it. The little girl felt so sorry for the elephant. She wished she could help him, but there was nothing she could do. The poor elephant was stuck in the city and he would never get back to the jungle. He would regret his escape forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly frog. He lived in a small pond. The frog was not happy because he was in trouble. He could not find any food to eat. One day, a little girl came to the pond. She saw the ugly frog and felt sad for him. She wanted to save the frog from trouble. The little girl had an idea. She went to her home and brought back some food for the frog. She gave the food to the frog, and he ate it all up. The ugly frog was so happy that he was saved from trouble. From that day on, the little girl and the ugly frog became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a kind tutor who liked to serve food to his friends. One day, he made a big cake for them. He felt very happy and could not wait for them to come. When his friends came, they saw the cake and wanted to eat it. But one friend was not nice. He wanted the whole cake for himself. The other friends did not like this and they started to fight. The tutor felt sad and ashamed. He did not want his friends to fight. But the fight got bigger and bigger. In the end, the cake fell on the floor and broke. The friends went home, still fighting. The kind tutor was left all alone, with no cake and no friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fancy bird. This bird loved to soar high in the sky. It would fly up and down, making all the kids happy. One day, the bird saw a police car. The police were helping a lost dog. The fancy bird wanted to help too. So, it soared down and landed near the police. The bird and the police worked together. They found the dog's home and the happy family. The fancy bird soared high again, and everyone waved goodbye. The bird and the police were friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom loved to watch the screen all day. He would sit in front of the screen and watch his favorite shows. One day, Tom saw a small mouse named Jerry. Jerry wanted to watch the screen too. But Tom did not want to share. So, Tom tried to steal Jerry's cheese to make him go away. Jerry said, "No, Tom! That's not nice!" Jerry was sad, but he had an idea. He asked Tom, "Can we watch the screen together and share the cheese?" Tom thought about it and said, "Okay, Jerry. We can share." So, Tom and Jerry watched the screen together and shared the cheese. They became good friends and learned that sharing is better than stealing. And they lived happily ever after.
