Title: Fred the Green Frog and the Turtle

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Fred. He lived in a small pond. Fred had a friend, a fish named Fin. They liked to play together all day. One day, Fred found a pill near the pond. He did not know what it was. He showed it to Fin. Fin said, "We should give it to Mom. She will know what to do." But Fred wanted to keep it. He thought it was special. Fred and Fin were not happy. They did not agree. Then, a wise old turtle came by. The turtle said, "Value your friendship more than the pill." Fred and Fin knew the turtle was right. They gave the pill to Mom and played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a pet cat named Fluffy. They loved to play and have fun together. One day, Amy found a big, sour lemon in the kitchen. She wanted to play a game with Fluffy using the lemon. Amy decided to raise the lemon high in the air and throw it so Fluffy could chase it. But Fluffy did not like the sour smell of the lemon. Fluffy made a funny face and ran away. Amy was sad because Fluffy did not want to play with the lemon. Amy's mom saw her feeling sad and had an idea. She gave Amy a soft, colorful ball to play with Fluffy. Amy raised the ball and threw it, and Fluffy happily chased it. They played together all day long, and Amy was happy again. From that day on, Amy and Fluffy always played with the ball instead of the sour lemon.

Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big puddle of water. Lily wants to jump in it, but Ben says no. "That water is smelly. It has dirt and bugs in it. We should not go near it," Ben says. Lily does not listen. She runs to the puddle and jumps in it. She splashes water all over herself and Ben. Ben is angry. He kicks the water back at Lily. "Stop it, Lily! You are making me wet and smelly too!" Ben shouts. Lily laughs. She thinks it is fun. She kicks more water at Ben. Ben kicks more water at Lily. They are both wet and smelly and happy. A dog comes to the park. He sees Lily and Ben playing in the puddle. He likes water. He runs to the puddle and joins them. He barks and wags his tail. He kicks water with his paws. He licks Lily and Ben's faces. Lily and Ben are surprised. They stop kicking water. They look at the dog. He is cute and friendly. They pet him and hug him. "Hello, dog. Do you want to play with us?" Lily asks. The dog barks yes. He is happy too. He has found new friends. Lily and Ben have found a new friend too. They forget about the smelly water. They play with the dog in the park.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a big gorilla. The gorilla was upset because he had no friends to play with. One day, he saw a little bird pass by. The bird was very happy and singing a sweet song. The gorilla asked the bird, "Why are you so happy?" The bird said, "I am happy because I help others. When I pass by, I give them a happy song to make them feel better." The gorilla thought this was a good idea. So, he decided to help others too. The next day, the gorilla passed by a small rabbit who was lost. The gorilla helped the rabbit find its way home. The rabbit was very happy and thanked the gorilla. The gorilla felt good about helping the rabbit. From that day on, the gorilla was not upset anymore. He made many friends by helping others. The moral of the story is: when you help others, you will feel happy and make friends too.

One day, a little insect was walking near a big pond. The insect was fearful because it could not swim. It saw other insects playing in the water and wanted to join them. The fearful insect thought about how to get close to the water without falling in. As the insect walked closer to the pond, it saw a big rock. The insect had an idea. It could stand on the rock and watch the other insects play. The fearful insect climbed on the rock and looked at the water. It felt happy to see the other insects splash and have fun. Suddenly, a big frog jumped into the pond and made a big splash. The water came up and touched the fearful insect's feet. The insect was surprised, but it did not fall into the water. It laughed and started to splash water with its feet too. The insect was not fearful anymore and had fun with the other insects.
