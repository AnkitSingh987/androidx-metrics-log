Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was very enthusiastic about exploring the world. One day, she decided to go for a walk in the woods. As she was walking, she saw something shiny in the grass. She kneeled down to take a closer look and discovered it was a whistle! She happily picked it up and blew it loudly. Suddenly, a bird flew out from the trees and landed on her shoulder. It was an enthusiastic bird and it enjoyed hearing the whistle so much that it stayed with the little girl all day. The two of them played in the woods together, the bird singing and the little girl playing her whistle. At the end of the day, they both felt so happy they were bouncing with enthusiasm!

Jack was very excited when his mommy gave him a special camera. He quickly put the camera around his neck and he ran around the house. He took pictures of everything he could see. "I'm going to make lots of pictures," said Jack. His mommy smiled and said, "Yes, that's a very healthy thing to do. Just remember not to take pictures of other people unless they allow you to." Jack nodded and ran outside. He started to take pictures of birds and flowers and the sky. But then he saw something he wanted to take a picture of. It was a person sitting in a chair. He wanted to take their picture, but he remembered what his mommy said. He asked them if he could take their picture and the person said, "No." Jack was very disappointed, but he respected their wishes. He didn't take the picture and he went home with an empty camera.

One day, a boy named Tom got a new watch. It was not a boring watch. It had many colors and made fun sounds. Tom loved his watch and wore it all the time. Tom liked to play outside. One day, he saw a cat climb a tree. The cat was scared and could not come down. Tom wanted to help the cat. He thought his watch could prevent the cat from being scared. Tom showed his watch to the cat. The cat saw the colors and heard the fun sounds. The cat was not scared anymore. It climbed down the tree and ran to Tom. Tom was happy he could help the cat with his fun watch. They played together and became good friends.

Sue was so happy. They all had a fancy tea party together.
