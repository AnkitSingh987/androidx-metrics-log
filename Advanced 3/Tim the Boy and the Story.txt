Title: Tim the Boy and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch videos on his mommy's phone. One day, Tim saw a new video that he didn't understand. He felt ignorant because he didn't know what it was about. He asked his mommy to explain it to him, but she was busy. Tim started to cry because he felt left out. His big sister, Sara, saw him crying and asked what was wrong. Tim told her about the video and how he didn't understand it. Sara sat down with Tim and watched the video with him. She explained what it was about in a way that Tim could understand. Tim stopped crying and thanked Sara for helping him. He learned that it's okay to ask for help when you don't understand something. The moral of the story is that it's important to ask for help and not to be sad when we don't know something. We can always learn from others and grow together.

The sun was shining on a beautiful forest. David and his mom were walking when they came across an old abandoned boat. "What's that mommy?" David asked, pointing to the boat. "That's a boat, honey," his mom said, "Let's take a closer look!" They walked up to the boat and saw a big pile of gears inside it. "What are these for, mommy?" David asked. "They're to make the boat work," his mom said, "We can put it back together and explore the river!" So they went to work, and soon the boat was ready to go. As the boat started to rise, the sky grew darker. In the distance, a storm was coming. "Mommy, I'm scared," David said. But it was too late. The storm was already upon them. The waves were high and the wind was fierce. The boat was no match for the strength of the storm and soon it was sinking. "Hold on tight, David!" his mom shouted. But it was no use. The boat was no match for the force of the storm and soon it was gone, leaving only the broken gears of the beautiful boat behind.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lucky toy car that he loved to play with. Every day, he would go to the park with his mommy and sit on a bench to play. One day, while playing on the bench with his lucky car, Tim saw a little girl crying. He asked her why she was sad. The little girl said that she lost her toy doll. Tim wanted to help, so he told the girl to repeat after him, "I will find my toy." They said it together many times. As they repeated the words, they looked around the park. Suddenly, Tim saw the toy doll under the bench! The little girl was so happy, and they both learned that when you help others, good things happen to you too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ella. She loved to dance and play with her friends. One sunny day, Ella and her friends went to the park to dance and have fun. While they were dancing, Ella saw a big hole in the ground. She was curious and went to look at it. Suddenly, she fell into the hole! Ella was scared and called for help. Her friends heard her and ran to get her mom. Ella's mom came and pulled her out of the hole. Ella was so grateful to her mom and her friends for helping her. She learned that it is important to be careful and not go near big holes. And she also learned that friends and family are always there to help when you need them.

"Mum, what's finish?" Mum smiled at her three-year-old daughter. "It means to complete something. Like when you finish a puzzle." The little girl thought for a moment. "Do you know lots of words, mum?" "Yes, I know lots of words in different languages. Lots of people around the world speak different languages." The little girl smiled. "So, when they finish all their words, they can't speak anymore?" "No! All languages are alive and never finish. So, no matter how many words you learn, you'll always have something new to learn." The little girl giggled, then yawned. "Okay mum, I'm getting tired so I'm gonna finish learning my words now." Mum smiled. "Goodnight sweetheart. Sleep tight, and don't worry, dead language won't come to get you!"
