Title: Mommy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a Mommy and her little girl. The girl wanted to go to the park and play. So, Mommy helped her put on her pants. Then, they went outside. The girl was so excited to play in the park. She ran up the hill, and it was very high. She could see for miles. She laughed and laughed. But soon, it was time for them to go. Mommy called for the girl, and she had to return. So, Mommy and the girl went back home. The girl took off her high pants and put them away. Then, she ran to her bedroom to play. The end.

Joe was a 3 year old boy who enjoyed exploring the outdoors. One day he was climbing a tall tree when he fell out of it and landed on the ground. He was very scared and hurt, but he was lucky that a compassionate woman was nearby. She ran up to him and comforted him, giving him a hug to make him feel better. The next day the woman came to Joe's house and told him and his family that she had a surprise for him. She brought Joe a brand new mattress! Feeling very excited, Joe helped her put the mattress on his bed. He was thrilled to have such a big, comfortable place to sleep. Once the mattress was complete, Joe thanked the kind woman for the surprise. He was so happy to have an amazing new bed to sleep on. Joe was glad that the compassionate woman had cared enough to bring him the special gift. From then on, Joe was excited each night to fall asleep on his new mattress.

Once, there was a wide fence. It was so tall and it seemed like it was made of forever. But, one day, something special happened; something that would change the landscape forever. The sun shone down and revealed a small door at the bottom of the wide fence. It was as if the fence was saying "Come, come and explore the world on the other side!". Two children saw the door and, with a lot of courage, they opened it. When they opened it, they saw a magical land. A land of trees, flowers and animals like they had never seen before. It was so beautiful! The children explored the magical land every day and saw things that amazed them. And, as the days passed, the wide fence became something different for them - a friend. And, whenever they needed a moment of peace, they knew that the wide fence was there to keep them safe.

One day, a boy named Tim found an open box. In the box, he saw a big guitar. Tim was very happy. He wanted to play the guitar all day. But his friend, Sam, wanted to play with the guitar too. They both wanted the guitar, but there was only one. Tim and Sam did not know what to do. They both wanted to play with the guitar, but they did not want to be mad at each other. They thought and thought. Then, they had an idea. They could take turns playing the guitar! Tim would play first, then Sam would play. In the end, Tim and Sam were both happy. They played the guitar and had a lot of fun. They learned that sharing is good. They played all day and became better friends.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to make a ball with dough. He tried very hard to make the ball. He did not succeed at first. He felt sad. Then, his friend Sally came to play. Sally was not selfish. She wanted to help Tim. They tried to make the dough ball together. They worked and worked. At last, Tim and Sally made the dough ball. They were happy. They succeeded! They played with the dough ball all day. They learned that it is good to help friends.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the winter. He would run and jump in the snow. Spot had a best friend named Tim. Tim was a little boy. They would play together all day. One day, Spot and Tim found a big pile of leaves. They decided to scatter the leaves and jump in them. It was so much fun! They laughed and played for hours. But then, they saw something hiding under the leaves. They moved closer to see what it was. To their surprise, they found a little kitten! The kitten was cold and scared. Spot and Tim decided to take the kitten home and keep it warm. They named the kitten Fluffy. From that day on, Spot, Tim, and Fluffy became the best of friends. They played together every day, no matter if it was winter or summer. And they always remembered the day they found Fluffy in the leaves.
