Title: The Fountain and the Duck

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was a pretty fountain. It was a sunny day, and a little girl named Mia went to play near the fountain. Mia saw a duck swimming in the fountain. The duck looked confused, and Mia wanted to help it. Mia called her friend Ben to help the confused duck. They thought and thought about how to help the duck. Then, they had an idea! They would make a ramp for the duck to get out of the fountain. Mia and Ben found some wood and started to make the ramp. They worked hard and did not stop until they finished the ramp. The confused duck saw the ramp and slowly walked up it. The duck was so happy to be out of the fountain! Mia and Ben felt good because they helped the duck. The moral of the story is: when we work together and finish what we start, we can help others.

Once there was a girl and a boy. They were very cheerful and loved to play together. One day the girl picked up a cloth. She lifted it up and kissed it. She was so happy with what she did. But then, the boy came over. He was not so cheerful when he saw the girl kissing the cloth. He got very angry and took the cloth away from her. The girl was upset and cried. The boy felt bad but he didn't give the cloth back. The end.

Once upon a time there was a brilliant farm. It was filled with animals, vegetables and other fun things. One day, a little duckling went out for a nice walk. He noticed some hay hanging from one of the farm's trees and decided to investigate. Suddenly, the duckling heard a voice yell out, "Hey! Be careful what you hang on that tree!" He looked around, and saw a friendly farmer robot nearby. "What do you mean?" the little duckling asked. The robot explained that the hay was meant to feed the animals on the farm, and it was important to keep it where it belonged. The little duckling thanked the robot for the advice, and decided to treat the farm with respect. The robot smiled and said, "That's brilliant, my friend!"

One day, a thoughtful bear named Ben went for a walk in the woods. He saw a big tree with honey inside. Ben was very happy, because he loved honey. He wanted to eat the honey, but he did not know how to get it out of the tree. Ben tried to slap the tree to make the honey come out. He slapped it once, then twice, but the honey did not come out. Then, something unexpected happened. A small bee flew out of the tree and landed on Ben's nose. Ben was scared of the bee, but the bee said, "Don't be scared! I can help you get the honey out of the tree." Ben was surprised that the bee could talk, but he was happy for the help. The bee went back to the tree and brought out some honey for Ben. They both ate the honey together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a young bird named Billy. Billy lived in an open tree with his family. They were very happy. One day, Billy's mom went to find food to feed her family. She flew high and low, looking for tasty bugs and worms. Billy and his brothers and sisters waited in the open tree, excited to eat. Finally, Mom came back with lots of food. She started to feed Billy and his brothers and sisters. They ate and ate until their tummies were full. They all felt very happy and loved.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw the big blue sky and asked his mom, "Mom, can you explain why the sky is so big?" Mom said, "The sky is big because it covers the whole world. It is like a big blanket that keeps us safe." Tim looked at a dead flower on the ground and said, "Mom, why is this flower dead?" Mom explained, "The flower is dead because it did not get enough water and sun. But don't worry, new flowers will grow soon." Tim was happy to learn new things from his mom. They spent the whole day playing outside under the big blue sky. And when they saw a new flower growing, they made sure to give it water and lots of love.
