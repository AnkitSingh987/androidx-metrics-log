Title: Lily and the Blanket

Lily liked to help her mom sew. She watched how mom put the needle and thread through the cloth and made pretty things. Lily wanted to try too. She saw a needle on the table and reached for it. "Ow!" Lily cried. The needle was sharp and it hurt her finger. She saw a drop of red on her skin. She was scared and ran to mom. Mom saw Lily's finger and hugged her. She put a band-aid on it and kissed it. She told Lily that needles are not for playing. They are only for sewing with care. She said she was sorry that Lily got hurt. Lily felt better. She said she was sorry too. She learned her lesson. She did not want to touch needles anymore. She wanted to cuddle with mom and watch her sew. Mom smiled and gave her a soft blanket. Lily felt warm and peaceful. She loved her mom and her mom loved her. They were happy.

Anna liked to design things. She had many crayons and papers and stickers. She made pictures of flowers and animals and houses. She also liked to design clothes for her dolls. She cut and sewed and glued fabrics and ribbons and buttons. One day, Anna wanted to design something new. She asked her mom, "Can I design something underground?" Her mom looked surprised. "Underground? What do you mean?" Anna said, "I want to make a secret place under the ground. Like a cave or a tunnel. Where I can hide and play and have fun." Her mom smiled. "That sounds interesting. But how will you do that?" Anna thought for a moment. She said, "I can use a big cardboard box and some blankets and pillows. And I can draw on the box with my crayons. And I can put some toys and books inside. And I can make a door with a flap. And I can put the box under the table. That will be my underground place." Her mom nodded. "That sounds like a good plan. Do you need any help?" Anna shook her head. "No, thank you. I can do it by myself. I like to design things." Her mom said, "Okay, then. Have fun, my little designer. But remember to come out for lunch." Anna said, "Okay, mom. I will." She hugged her mom and ran to get her materials. She was excited to design her underground place. She thought it would be the best place ever. She did not care if it was not normal. She liked to be different.

One day, Mary and her family took a drive to the farm. Mary was excited. She hopped out of the car and admired the farm. Everything was so big! She was happy to see the animals and the trees. At the farm, Mary saw lots of animals. There were cows and sheep and chickens. The barn was very special, it had a lot of dirty straw in it. Mary thought the chickens were so funny. They were making lots of noise and running around. Mary and her family kept walking around the farm. They saw a big pond with ducks swimming in it. But then, they saw something else. Mary's mom said "Oh my! Look at all this filth!" Mary looked closer and saw a lot of mud and dirt. It was very messy! Mary's mom said they had to be careful not to get dirty. Mary and her family had a nice time at the farm. Mary admired all the animals and the big pond with the ducks swimming in it. They were careful not to get too dirty, but it was a lot of fun. Mary would never forget her special trip to the farm!

Once there was a boy named Tim who loved to go by train. One day, he was at the station waiting for his train to arrive. He saw the train approaching in the distance and heard its loud whistle. Tim was very excited and started running to join his train. Then he heard someone calling him, it was his mother. She said, "Tim, wait a minute! If you don't look out for the tracks, you might not get on the train safely and you can get hurt." Tim stopped running and looked carefully. He saw the tracks were very clear and he was able to step onto the train without any problems. Tim's mother hugged him and said, "When you go to catch a train and take risks, always use your common sense and be careful. Have a safe journey." Tim thanked his mother and waved goodbye. He learned an important lesson that day - never take risks if the danger is not clear.
