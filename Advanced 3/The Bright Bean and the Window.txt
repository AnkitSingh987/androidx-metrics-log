Title: The Bright Bean and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a bright bean. The bean liked to clap with its hands. Every day, it clapped and clapped. One day, the bean heard noises outside its window. Peeking through the window, the bean saw other beans clapping too. The bean easily hopped over to the other beans. When the bean joined, everyone started clapping faster and faster. The beans held hands and spun around in circles. As they laughed and clapped, the sun shined brighter and brighter. The day was so much fun! When it was time to leave, all the beans clapped one last time as they waved goodbye.

Once upon a time there was a beautiful duck who loved to play in the pond. He always made sure to be extra careful because the pond was very smooth. One day, the duck was playing in the pond and he saw a little boy. The duck was so excited to have a new friend. He asked the boy to come play with him, but the boy said, "No, I can't get wet". The duck was really sad and flew away. He flew to a tree, sat down and thought to himself, "If only the boy would love to get wet like I do!". The moral of the story is that it's ok to try new things, even if it means getting a little wet. Everyone needs to be brave and have fun. Love trying new things!

Lily and Max were playing with their blocks. They wanted to build the tallest tower ever. They stacked and stacked the blocks, but they did not agree on how to do it. "No, Max, you have to put the red block here!" Lily said. "But I like the blue block better!" Max said. They started to argue and pull the blocks from each other. Their tower wobbled and fell down with a loud crash. The blocks scattered all over the floor. They both felt sad and angry. They did not want to play with each other anymore. Their mom came into the room and saw the mess. She asked them what happened. "We were trying to build the tallest tower ever, but we could not agree on how to do it!" Lily said. "We both wanted to have the victory, but we only made a big mess!" Max said. Their mom smiled and hugged them. She said, "I know you both want to have fun and do your best, but sometimes you have to settle and share. How about you try to build the tower together, and take turns choosing the blocks? That way, you can both have the victory and be happy." Lily and Max thought about it. They decided to give it a try. They said sorry to each other and picked up the blocks. They worked together and built a new tower. It was not the tallest tower ever, but it was very pretty and strong. They were proud of their tower and of themselves. They gave each other a high five and a hug. They said, "We did it! We settled and shared and had the victory!" Their mom clapped and cheered for them. She said, "You are both very smart and kind. I am so proud of you!" They smiled and thanked their mom. They played with their tower and had a lot of fun. They learned that settling and sharing can make them happy and have the victory.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Sara. She was 3 years old and loved going to the beach with her family. The beach was so much fun and Sara always loved feeling the sand between her toes. One day, Sara's mum and dad showed her something amazing. In the sand at the beach, there was a live starfish. Sara's parents were so excited and wanted to show Sara how amazing it was. Sara didn't understand why they were so excited, as she couldn't even see the starfish. She looked down and was very ignorant, she didn't see the beauty that her parents saw. "Look!" said Sara's dad, as he pointed to the starfish. "It is so special to find a starfish on the beach." Sara still didn't understand and just looked around, confused. "Don't be ignorant, Sara," said her mum. "Look at the starfish, isn't it beautiful?" Sara peered down, and suddenly she saw it! She was so excited, and could now understand why her parents were so amazed. Sara beamed and said, "Wow! It's so pretty!" She waved excitedly at the starfish and then went off exploring, feeling very lucky to have seen the starfish.
