Title: The Bull and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a big bull. The bull lived on a farm. The farm had many animals. The bull was not nice. He was harsh. He would push the other animals. One day, the farmer saw the bull being harsh. The farmer did not like it. He wanted to send the bull away. The farmer talked to the bull. He said, "If you do not be nice, I will send you away." The bull did not want to go away. He wanted to stay on the farm. So, the bull tried to be nice. He stopped being harsh. The other animals were happy. The bull was happy too. The farm was a nice place to live.

Once there was a little girl, who was only three years old. She had a lot of worries, because she felt different to the other children. She always thought she was a bit unusual. One day, the little girl went to school and the teacher decided to give her a test. She was very worried, but finally the test was over. She expected to have done a great job, but the result was terrible! The little girl felt even more worried and asked the teacher why she did so badly. The teacher looked at her and said: "Maybe you are just not quite talented enough". The little girl went home feeling sad and worrying about her future. She thought she was always going to be different and unusual. She was scared of what the future held for her and had to accept that sometimes things don't turn out how you want them to.

Once upon a time, there was a big, rough rock. This rock could talk! It had a secret, the truth about the forest. One day, a small boy named Tim came to the forest. He saw the rock and said, "Hello, rock! Can you tell me a story?" The rock replied, "Yes, I can. I store the truth about the forest. Long ago, the trees and animals played together. They were all friends. But, a big storm came and scared them. Now, they hide and don't play." Tim wanted to help. He went to the trees and animals. He said, "Rock told me the truth. You were friends once. Let's be friends again and play." The trees and animals listened to Tim. They remembered the good times. They became friends again and played together. The forest was happy, and the rough rock smiled, knowing it shared the truth it had stored.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. The mouse was very enthusiastic. He liked to run and jump all day. One day, the mouse saw a big red ball. The ball made the mouse excite. The mouse played with the big red ball. He pushed it and chased it. The ball rolled and bounced. The mouse was having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The big red ball popped! The mouse was sad. But then, he saw lots of little balls inside the big red ball. The mouse was happy again. He played with the little balls all day. The mouse was excited and loved the surprise.

Once there was a grandpa who was very old. He was happy and liked to read old novels. He had one special novel that he always kept near and dear to him. One day, a naughty bunny came up to the grandpa. The bunny had glittering eyes and asked grandpa: “Can I pay for your novel? Please, please!” Grandpa said no and tried to push the bunny away. The bunny got angry and started to hop up and down in frustration. Grandpa was worried but he kept saying no. He told the bunny he cherished the old novel more than anything. The bunny wasn’t happy but eventually left grandpa alone. Grandpa was relieved and breathed a sigh of relief. He realized how important his old novel was to him. He cherished it even more and promised to always keep it close.

Once upon a time, there was a big red bucket. It was a cheap bucket, but it was very strong. The bucket lived in a small house with a boy named Tim. One day, Tim and the bucket went to the park. They were playing in the sand. Tim used the bucket to make a big sand castle. But then, a mean dog came and knocked down the sand castle. The bucket was sad, and Tim was mad. Tim did not want to leave the park. He wanted to make another sand castle with the bucket. So, they tried again. This time, the sand castle was even bigger. The mean dog came back, but Tim said, "No! Don't break our sand castle!" The dog listened and left them alone. Tim and the bucket were very happy. They played in the park all day, and the mean dog never came back. In the end, Tim and the bucket went home, ready to make more sand castles another day.
