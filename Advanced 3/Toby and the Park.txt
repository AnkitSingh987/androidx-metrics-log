Title: Toby and the Park

Toby wanted to race his friends at the park. He saw a large and attractive tent that he wanted to race past. As Toby was about to start running, one of his friends told him he couldn't run past the tent. Toby was confused and asked why. His friend said that Toby's older sister had asked them all not to touch any of the tents. Toby was disappointed and started to cry. He wanted to race his friends and the tent was too attractive to ignore! One of his friends had an idea. He suggested that Toby could race around the tent instead. Toby smiled and was happy with the suggestion. He raced around the tent, cheering loudly. Everyone cheered him on and Toby was glad he was still able to race.

Once upon a time, in a land full of colors, there was a busy little prince. He loved to run, jump, and play all day. The prince had many friends, like the kind dog, the fast cat, and the big tree. One sunny day, the prince was playing with his friend, the kind dog. The prince said, "Let's play hide and seek!" The dog wagged his tail and agreed. The prince closed his eyes and counted, "One, two, three, four, five!" The dog ran and hid behind the big tree. The prince opened his eyes and started to look for the dog. He looked under the small rock, but the dog was not there. Then, he looked behind the red flowers, but the dog was not there either. The prince felt sad because he could not find his friend. Suddenly, the fast cat came running and said, "I can help you find the dog!" The prince smiled and said, "Thank you, cat!" The cat climbed the big tree and pointed to the dog hiding behind it. The prince laughed and said, "I found you, dog!" The dog came out from behind the tree, wagging his tail, and they all played together happily ever after.

Mabel was very excited. Today, her family was going on a huge journey. After breakfast, Mabel quickly dressed in her favourite yellow dress and waited for the family to unpack. Then, the news came. Mabel's father said that the family was going to the zoo today! Mabel couldn't believe it. She shrieked with delight and ran around the house. She began to ask her mother a million questions. "What kind of animals will we see?" she asked. Her mother smiled and said that they would find out soon. Mabel was so excited and curious. She couldn't bear to wait any longer. She wanted to see what kinds of animals were at the zoo. Finally, the family arrived. Mabel watched her father unpack the car. She was the first one to run out of the car and into the zoo! Mabel looked around in awe. There were so many huge and beautiful animals. She spotted lions, monkeys and even elephants! Mabel couldn't believe it and ran around the zoo, excited to explore it. It was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to dance. Lily would dance all day and all night. One day, she found a broken toy. The toy had a sign that said "repair me". Lily wanted to help the toy. Lily looked around for someone who knew how to repair. She asked her mom, but her mom was busy. She asked her dad, but her dad was not available. So, Lily decided to try and fix the toy herself. She worked very hard and finally, the toy was fixed. The toy was a magic toy. It could dance too! Lily and the toy danced together. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy turned into a real boy! The boy said "Thank you, Lily, for fixing me. I was a boy who was turned into a toy. Now, we can dance together forever!" And they did.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, hard rock. He wanted to show his friend, Sue. Tim walked to Sue's house with the rock in his hands. When he got there, he said, "Look, Sue! I found a big, hard rock!" Sue looked at the rock and said, "Wow, that's a big rock! But I found something too!" Sue showed Tim a small plant. It was a True plant. Tim was amazed. Tim and Sue started to argue about whose find was better. Tim said, "My rock is better because it's big and hard!" Sue said, "No, my True plant is better because it will grow and yield food!" They couldn't agree, so they decided to ask their friend, Ben. Ben listened to both of them and thought for a moment. Then he said, "Why don't you both put your finds together? The rock can protect the True plant, and the plant can yield food for both of you!" Tim and Sue smiled and agreed. They put the rock and the True plant together and watched as the plant grew and yielded food. They learned that working together is better than fighting.
