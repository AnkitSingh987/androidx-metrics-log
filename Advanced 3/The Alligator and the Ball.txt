Title: The Alligator and the Ball

One day, a big alligator went for a walk. He saw a small boy with a red ball. The boy was sad because his ball was not shiny. The alligator wanted to help the boy, so he thought of an easy way to make the ball shiny. The alligator decided to polish the ball for the boy. He used his big tail to rub the ball. The ball became shiny and bright. The boy was very happy and said thank you to the alligator. But, the alligator did not know that the ball was magic. When the ball became shiny, it made the alligator small! Now, the alligator was the same size as the boy. They both laughed and played with the red ball together. The boy and the small alligator became very good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was very independent. He liked to play all by himself. One day, Tom found a needle on the floor. He did not know what it was, but he thought it was a toy. Tom saw a little mouse named Jerry. He wanted to tease Jerry with the needle. Tom ran to Jerry with the needle in his paw. Jerry saw the needle and got scared. He did not want to play with Tom and the needle. Tom felt sad that Jerry did not want to play. He put the needle away and found a ball. Tom and Jerry played with the ball together. They had lots of fun. Tom learned that it is better to play with nice toys and not tease his friends.

Once, there was a bear who liked to hide. He loved the sunshine, but his days were often grey and cloudy. So he decided to hide even more. The bear hid in deep crevices, behind trees and even in hollow logs. But even when he did find sunshine, it was often too bitter for his taste. One cloudy day, the bear decided to try something different. He would stay out of his hiding places and wait to see what would happen. Soon he saw a warm, yellow light coming down from the sky. He realized it was the sunshine and he felt so happy! He moved closer and closer until the warmth of the sunshine filled his entire body. The bear laughed and smiled and the sunshine felt so nice. He knew that even when it was cloudy, he could always find sunshine if he just waited. The bear was never bitter again; he remembered how wonderful the sunshine was and looked forward to spending more time with it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a slow eater. Every day, he would sit at the table and eat his food very slowly. His mom and dad would always finish their food before him. One sunny day, Tim was eating his lunch outside. He had a yummy sandwich and some fruit. As he ate slowly, a hungry bird flew down and took a piece of his food. Tim was surprised but kept eating. The bird came back, and this time it brought a friend. They both took pieces of Tim's food. Tim knew he had to finish his food faster. So, he ate a little faster, and the birds couldn't take any more of his food. Tim was happy that he finished his food and the birds didn't get it all.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to paint. One day, he found a big zip bag. Inside the bag, there were many paint colors. He was very happy. Tim wanted to paint a big tree. He started to paint, but the wind was harsh. It blew his paint away. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He painted and painted until the tree was done. Suddenly, the tree began to shake. The paint on the tree came to life! The painted tree grew arms and legs. It was not a nice tree. It chased Tim away. Tim ran and ran, but the tree caught him. The tree painted Tim, and he could not move. The end.
