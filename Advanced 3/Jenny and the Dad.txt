Title: Jenny and the Dad

Once upon a time there were a mom, a dad and their three year old daughter. Little Jenny loved exploring the backyard of her house, and one day she came across a slimy tube. She was about to pick it up when her mom said, "No Jenny, leave it there." But Jenny was persistent. She wanted to see what was inside the tube. Finally, her dad came over, got down on one knee, and whispered to her, "Jenny, do you know why I'm telling you not to pick up that tube? Well, because we must take care of the things in nature. Everything has a purpose and if we take something from its habitat, that would be very wrong." Jenny was sure she was going to get in trouble for disobeying but after hearing her dad's words, she learned a valuable lesson. From then on, she made sure to take care of nature by leaving the things she found alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite toy, a big, soft bear named Ben. Tim took Ben everywhere he went. One day, while playing outside, Tim noticed a small patch on Ben's arm. The patch was scary to Tim because he thought it meant Ben was hurt. Tim ran to his mom and showed her the scary patch. Mom looked at the patch and smiled. She told Tim not to worry. The patch was just a part of Ben that made him special. She said that everyone has something that makes them different, and that's what makes them special too. From that day on, Tim was not scared of the patch. He learned that being different is not scary, but special. Tim and Ben played happily ever after, and Tim always remembered the lesson his mom taught him. The moral of the story is that we should not be scared of what makes us different, but instead, we should celebrate what makes us special.

Lily and Tom were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They were very happy. Then they saw a penguin. The penguin was black and white and had a funny beak. It was walking on the snow. Lily and Tom were surprised. They had never seen a penguin before. "Hello, penguin!" Lily said. "Where do you come from?" The penguin did not answer. It looked at them with its small eyes. It was very mysterious. "Maybe it is lost," Tom said. "Maybe it came from the zoo." "Or maybe it is a joke," Lily said. "Maybe someone put it here to make us laugh." They decided to touch the penguin. They wanted to see if it was real or not. They walked towards the penguin slowly. But the penguin did not like that. It did not want to be touched. It opened its beak and made a loud noise. It sounded like a horn. It scared Lily and Tom. They ran away from the penguin. They hid behind the snowman. They were very afraid. The penguin looked at them. It was not a joke. It was a real penguin. It was not happy. It wanted to go back to its home. It walked away from the snowman. It saw a big truck. The truck had a sign that said "Zoo". The penguin ran to the truck. It jumped inside. It hoped to find its friends. Lily and Tom watched the truck drive away. They saw the penguin wave at them. They waved back. They were not afraid anymore. They were curious. They wondered how the penguin got here. They wondered where it was going. They wondered if they would ever see it again. They decided to go home and tell their mom and dad. They had a very interesting story to tell. They had met a mysterious penguin.

Once upon a time, on a charming evening, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a big, round ball in the sky. It was the moon. Lucy wanted to touch the moon, so she tried to reach it with her hand. But it was too far away. Lucy saw a long stick on the ground. She had an idea. She picked up the stick and thought, "I can poke the moon with this stick." She held the stick high up in the air and tried to poke the moon. But still, it was too far away. Just then, Lucy's mom came outside. She saw Lucy trying to poke the moon with the stick. She smiled and said, "Lucy, the moon is very far away. We can't touch it, but we can enjoy its beauty every evening." Lucy put down the stick and looked at the moon with her mom. They both smiled and enjoyed the charming evening together.
