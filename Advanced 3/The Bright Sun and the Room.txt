Title: The Bright Sun and the Room

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. A little girl named Lily wanted to sleep, but the sun was too bright for her. She had an idea to make her room dark so she could sleep. Lily found a big jug in the kitchen. She filled the jug with water and carried it to her room. She thought if she poured the water on the floor, it would make the room dark. But when she poured the water, it did not make the room dark. It only made the floor wet. Lily was sad that her plan did not work. She asked her mom for help. Her mom closed the curtains to make the room dark. Now, the room was dark enough for Lily to sleep. She was happy and slept well.

Once upon a time, there was an old man. He loved polishing things. He polished rocks, dolls and even toys! Every day, he would sit outside his house and get to work. One day, he found something very special. It was a fancy mark. He knew he had to polish it. So, he grabbed some cloth and started to shine it up. When he was finished polishing, something amazing happened! The fancy mark began to sparkle and shine. The old man was so happy. He shouted, "Wow! It looks so nice!" A little girl was walking by and she saw the old man. She stopped and asked, "What are you doing? That looks so pretty!" The old man smiled and said, "I am polishing this special mark. When I am done, it will be even more beautiful!" The little girl gasped in awe. She could not believe that the old man was making something so fancy. A few days later, the old man finished the mark. It was so beautiful that people came from miles around to see it. The old man was very proud. He knew that the hard work he put into polishing really paid off!

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play and run outside. One day, Max saw a big cat in the yard. The cat was not like any cat Max had seen before. It was big and had a long tail. Max said to the cat, "Who are you? I have not seen you before." The cat looked down and said, "I am Tom. I am new here and I feel ashamed because I have no friends." Max felt sad for Tom and wanted to help him feel safe and happy. Max and Tom started to play together in the yard. They ran and jumped and had so much fun. Soon, other animals saw them playing and wanted to join. They all became friends and played together every day. Tom felt safe and was not ashamed anymore. Everyone was happy and had a great time.

Sammy was a 3 year old boy who loved school. He always looked forward to the morning when his mommy would drive him there and he could play with his friends. One day, Sammy arrived at school to find that a bully was picking on his classmates. He watched as the bully played his mean jokes and made everyone miserable. Sammy didn't like seeing his friends upset, so he decided to do something about it. He marched up to the bully and said, "Stop! You can't treat people like this!" The bully tried to push Sammy away, but Sammy was brave and wouldn't back down. The bully finally gave in and left the school in shame. Everyone else was so glad that Sammy stood up to the bully. From that day on, everyone knew that Sammy was a mark of courage at the school. Moral of the story: You can stand up to bullies by being brave and showing courage.

Once upon a time, there was a wide garden. In the garden, there were many radishes. One radish was very special. It was big and red. One sunny day, a little girl came to the garden. She saw the big red radish. She wanted to pick it. She tried to pull the radish out, but it was very hard. She leaned and leaned, but the radish would not come out. Then, a friendly rabbit came to help. The rabbit and the girl leaned together. With a big pull, the radish came out of the ground. The girl and the rabbit were happy. They shared the big red radish and became best friends.
