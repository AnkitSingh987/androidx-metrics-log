Title: The Man with Mustache and the Toys

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there lived a man with a big mustache. His mustache was so big that everyone in the town knew who he was. He was a very nice man who liked to help people. One day, a big storm came to the town. The storm was very strong and broke many things. The people in the town were sad because their homes and toys were broken. The man with the big mustache wanted to help them. The man with the big mustache had a special power. He could restore things that were broken. He went to each house and used his power to fix everything. He made the people in the town happy again. They all said "thank you" to the man with the big mustache, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there lived a smelly prince. The prince had a big ball that he liked to pass to his friends. They would play every day and have lots of fun. One day, the smelly prince went to the park to pass the ball with his friends. They laughed and played all day long. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. It was a very happy day. At the end of the day, the smelly prince went back to his castle. He took a bath and became a clean prince. He was very tired but also very happy. The prince slept well that night, dreaming of more fun days passing the ball with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old man who loved to build. Every day, he would build a big tower with his blocks. The tower was very tall and pretty. The wise old man was very proud of his tower. One sunny day, a little boy came to see the wise old man. The little boy said, "Can I help you build the tower?" The wise old man was happy and said, "Yes, you can help me. Let's build the tower together." As they built the tower, it got taller and taller. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew by and knocked the tower down. The wise old man and the little boy were sad, but they did not give up. They started to build the tower again, and this time, they made it even bigger and stronger. And they became very good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little sheep named Fluffy. Fluffy was very healthy and strong. He loved to play with his friends in the big field. One day, Fluffy's friend, Bunny, asked him to promise to play with him the next day. Fluffy said, "I promise!" The next day, Fluffy went to the big field to play with Bunny. They played hide and seek, and Fluffy was very good at hiding. Bunny looked and looked, but he could not find Fluffy. Fluffy was hiding behind a big rock, and he was very quiet. But then, a big, bad wolf saw Fluffy hiding behind the rock. The wolf was very hungry and wanted to eat the healthy little sheep. Fluffy saw the wolf and got very scared. He tried to run away, but the wolf was too fast. The big, bad wolf caught Fluffy, and Fluffy was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy baby fox. One foggy day, she was walking in the woods looking for food. All of a sudden, she heard a voice. "Hello, small fox," the voice said. The fox replied, "Who said that?" "It was me, the big bear!" replied the bear. The fox was scared. She thought that the bear was going to try to eat her. "Don't worry, I won't hurt you," said the bear. "Can you help me find my dinner?" The fox thought for a moment, and then replied, "Sure. Follow me!" So the fox and the bear walked together through the fog in search of dinner. They looked high and low, but nothing seemed to be around. Just when they thought all hope was lost, the fox spotted some food in the distance. The bear and the fox both cheered. As they sat down to have dinner, the bear said to the fox, "Thank you for helping me out today, you're a true friend." The fox smiled and replied, "You're welcome."
