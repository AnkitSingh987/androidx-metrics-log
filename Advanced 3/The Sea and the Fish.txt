Title: The Sea and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big sea. In the sea, there was a twisty fish. The twisty fish was troubled. He wanted to play with the other fish, but they were scared of his twist. One day, the twisty fish met a little crab. The crab was not scared of the twisty fish. They played together in the sea. They swam and twisted around each other. The twisty fish was not troubled anymore. Soon, more sea friends came to play. They saw the twisty fish and the crab having fun. They joined the twist and swam in the sea. The twisty fish was happy. He had many friends, and the sea was full of laughter and fun.

Tommy was walking through the forests with his mommy. His mommy was looking for something. He asked, "What are you looking for, Mommy?" She said, "I'm looking for a branch." Tommy was intrigued, so he started to look around too. After a few moments, Tommy spotted a branch and shouted, "Mommy, I found it!" His mommy clapped her hands and replied, "That's a good job, Tommy!" Tommy was so excited! He picked up the branch, but it was really heavy! His mommy said, "It looks like it might rain. We should probably go home." As they walked back home, Tommy dragged the branch along. He was very sleepy, so his mommy carried the branch for him. When they got back home, his mommy said, "Let's put this branch next to the fireplace." Tommy was excited, but he was also very sleepy. His mommy tucked him into bed and Tommy soon fell fast asleep. The next morning, Tommy woke up feeling refreshed. He saw the branch his mommy found the night before. He asked his mommy, "What are we going to do with the branch?" She said, "We are going to chop it up and use it for firewood to stay warm this winter." Tommy was so excited! He smiled, knowing that he and his mommy had found something really special.

One day, a little frog named Tim wanted to spring very high. He saw a big tree and thought, "If I can spring to the top, I will be the best frog ever!" Tim tried and tried, but he could not spring high enough. Then, he saw a shiny medal hanging from a branch. Tim wanted the medal so much. He thought, "If I get the medal, I will be very glad!" So, Tim tried again. He took a deep breath and sprang with all his might. This time, Tim sprang so high that he touched the medal. He was so glad! All the other frogs cheered for him. Tim felt very proud and happy. From that day on, Tim wore the medal and showed everyone how high he could spring.

Once upon a time, there was a long, long snake named Sam. Sam was made of rubber and lived in a big jungle. He liked to play with his friends, a bird named Bob and a monkey named Max. One sunny day, Sam, Bob, and Max were playing catch with a ball. Sam threw the ball too hard and it hit Max's head. Max was sad and cried. Sam felt bad and said, "I am sorry, Max. I didn't mean to hurt you." Max looked at Sam and said, "I forgive you, Sam. We all make mistakes." Then, they all hugged and played together again. They had lots of fun and were happy to be friends. From that day on, Sam, Bob, and Max played together every day. They learned to be careful and always said sorry when they made a mistake. They were the best of friends and lived happily in the big jungle.

Sandy and Burt were walking along the beach. They were looking for pretty shells. Suddenly, Burt spotted one. He bent down and picked it up. He held it up to his eye and looked inside. He was so excited he started to shout! But then, something happened. They heard a loud noise. Sandy started to panic. Burt told her not to worry because the noise was just a mild thunderstorm in the distance. He held her close and gave her a hug. Then, the thunder stopped and all was quiet again. Sandy was relieved. She looked down and saw the pretty shell in Burt's hands. She smiled and was no longer afraid.
