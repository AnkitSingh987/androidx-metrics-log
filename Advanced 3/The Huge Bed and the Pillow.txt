Title: The Huge Bed and the Pillow

Once upon a time, there was a huge bed in a small room. The bed was so big that it took up most of the space. A little girl named Sue loved to play on the huge bed. She would jump, roll, and sleep on it. It was her favorite place to be. One day, Sue was playing on the huge bed when she heard a sound. It was a soft, funny noise. She stopped jumping and listened. The sound came again, and Sue was curious. She looked under the bed, but saw nothing. She looked behind the bed, but still saw nothing. Sue climbed back on the huge bed and heard the sound again. This time, she looked under the pillow and found a small, talking toy. The toy was making the funny noise! Sue laughed and played with the toy all day. The huge bed had a new friend for her, and Sue loved it even more.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Sue felt a big sneeze coming. She knew it was important to cover her mouth when she sneezed, so she used her palm to cover it. After Sue sneezed, she went to wash her hands with soap and water. She knew that keeping her hands clean was very important. Sue's friends saw her wash her hands and they wanted to do the same. They all went to wash their hands together. Sue and her friends learned that it is important to cover your mouth when you sneeze and to wash your hands after. They stayed healthy and happy by doing this. The moral of the story is to always cover your mouth when you sneeze and wash your hands to stay healthy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a restaurant. The restaurant was very nice and people liked to go there to eat. The people who worked there were very reliable. They always did a good job and made everyone happy. One day, the restaurant was very messy. The workers needed to organize it. They put all the plates and cups in the right place. They cleaned the tables and the floor. They made sure everything looked good for the people who came to eat. When the people came to the restaurant, they saw how clean and organized it was. They were very happy. They enjoyed their food and had a great time. The reliable workers were proud of their hard work. The restaurant was the best place to eat in town.

Once upon a time, a little red car named Zoomy loved to zoom around the town. Zoomy was a stubborn car and did not like to stop. One day, Zoomy saw a big rake in the road. Zoomy's friend, a blue car named Beepy, saw the rake too. "Zoomy, we must stop!" said Beepy. "The rake is in the way!" But stubborn Zoomy did not want to stop. Zoomy wanted to keep zooming. So, Zoomy zoomed faster and faster towards the rake. As Zoomy got closer to the rake, Beepy yelled, "Zoomy, please stop!" But Zoomy did not listen. Zoomy hit the rake very hard. Zoomy's wheels broke and Zoomy could not zoom anymore. Beepy felt sad for Zoomy. Zoomy learned that sometimes it is important to stop and listen to friends. But now, it was too late for Zoomy to zoom again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue. They lived in a small house with their mom and dad. One day, it was very cold outside, so their mom made them some hot cocoa to keep them warm. Tim and Sue both loved hot cocoa a lot. But there was a problem. There was only one cup of cocoa, and they both wanted it. Tim said, "I want the cocoa!" Sue said, "No, I want the cocoa!" They both looked fierce and did not want to share the cocoa. Their mom saw them fighting and said, "You need to share the cocoa." Tim and Sue looked at each other and thought about it. They decided to share the cocoa. Tim took a sip, then Sue took a sip. They smiled and felt happy. From that day on, they always shared and never fought over cocoa again.
