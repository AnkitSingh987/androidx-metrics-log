Title: Tim and the Tree

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was a hot and dry day. Tim saw a big tree and thought, "I will sit under the tree." He could imagine having fun with his friends there. Soon, a mosquito came by. The mosquito said, "Hello, Tim! I am a nice mosquito. I will not bite you." Tim was surprised. He did not know that mosquitoes could talk. They played together all day. The mosquito showed Tim how to fly high in the sky. Tim was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The mosquito said, "Tim, I have to tell you something. I am not really a mosquito. I am a fairy!" Tim could not believe it. The fairy gave Tim a big hug and said, "Thank you for being my friend." They played together every day after that.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some food for dinner. Tim saw a big, round squash. He asked his mom, "Can we buy the squash?" His mom said, "Yes, it is cheap. We can make a good meal with it." At home, Tim helped his mom to fill a big pot with water. They put the squash in the pot to cook it. Tim was very excited. He couldn't wait to eat the squash for dinner. His mom said, "We will have a yummy surprise later." When the squash was cooked, Tim's mom cut it open. Inside, there were many seeds. Tim's mom said, "We can plant these seeds and grow more squash!" Tim was happy. He knew that they would have lots of squash to eat in the future. In the end, Tim and his mom enjoyed their cheap squash dinner. They saved the seeds to plant in their garden. Tim learned that good things can come from small surprises.

Once upon a time, there were two kids named John and Lisa. They were best friends and spent most of their days playing together. One day, John was very excited because he had been given a special present. He realized it was his birthday, but his friend Lisa had not brought him a present. He was very frustrated. But suddenly, Lisa opened up her pocket and pulled out a present for John. John was very excited and thanked Lisa for the present. But then John realized that the present Lisa gave him was her favorite stuffed toy! He was so frustrated and sad. John sadly said goodbye to Lisa and left to go home. His birthday did not turn out as he had hoped. The End.

One day, a girl named Lily went to see an opera with her mom. The opera had people singing and dancing on a big stage. Lily liked the music and the pretty dresses the people wore. She felt proud of the singers for doing such a good job. At the opera, Lily's mom had a little machine to record the music. She pressed a button and the machine made the music stay inside it, so they could listen to it later. Lily thought it was like magic. After the opera, Lily and her mom went home. They played the music from the machine for Lily's dad. He said, "Wow, the opera was so good! I wish I could have been there too." Lily smiled and said, "Don't worry, Dad. We can listen to the opera together anytime we want with mom's magic machine!"

One day, a furry cat went out to play. The cat saw a hose in the yard. The cat said, "I will set the hose on the grass." The cat set the hose on the grass and turned on the water. A dog saw the cat and said, "Can I play with the hose too?" The cat said, "Yes, you can play with the hose." The dog and the cat played with the hose and had a lot of fun. But then, the water made a big mess. The yard was very wet and muddy. The dog and the cat got dirty. The mom saw the mess and said, "You made a big mess! You can't play with the hose anymore." The dog and the cat were sad because they could not play with the hose anymore.
