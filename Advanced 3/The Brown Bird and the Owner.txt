Title: The Brown Bird and the Owner

Once upon a time, there was a brown bird. It belonged to no one and loved to soar in the sky. It was so happy, flying up high. Whenever it flew, its brown feathers sparkled. One day, it was soaring high in the sky when it saw something strange. It was another brown bird, but this one had an owner. Its owner held a string attached to the bird, and it followed along like an obedient puppy. The free bird watched in awe as the owned bird flew with its owner. The free bird wanted an owner too, but it was scared of being tied down. It was used to the feeling of freedom and soaring in the sky. It stayed away from the owned bird and continued to soar on its own. But sometimes, it would watch from afar, wishing it had an owner too.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted a cookie. She saw the cookie jar on the high shelf. Lucy tried to touch the jar, but she was too small. Lucy had a plan. She went slow and got a chair. She put the chair by the shelf. Lucy climbed up and touched the cookie jar. She opened the jar and took a cookie. She was very happy. Lucy climbed down and ate her cookie. It was yummy!

Mommy and Jakey went to the store together. Jakey was having so much fun! Mommy bought some steak. When they got home, Mommy and Jakey got ready to share the steak. Mommy cut it into slices. Jakey saw that it was a strong steak. Mommy and Jakey had some of the steak at the table together. They both said the steak was yummy. Mommy asked Jakey if he wanted to share the last slice of steak with his stuffed bear, George. Jakey thought it was a great idea, so they put the last slice of steak on the table for George. Mommy and Jakey smiled at each other when they finished sharing the steak. They were happy and full.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big helmet. It was red and shiny. He wanted to wear it and play with it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I have the helmet, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim put on the helmet and went outside. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because her kite was stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to help Sue. He thought the helmet would make him strong and dependable. Tim tried to climb the tree to get the kite. He observed the branches and found a good one. He pulled on the branch and the kite came down. Sue was so happy! She thanked Tim and they played together. Later, Tim's mom saw him playing with Sue. She smiled and said, "I knew the helmet would help you be a good friend." Tim felt proud and knew that he could always count on his helmet to help him be dependable.

Once upon a time, there was a nice sun who loved to shine. Every day, the sun would shine on a small school. In the school, there were many little pupils. They liked to play and learn. One day, a new pupil came to the school. She was shy and did not know anyone. The nice sun saw her and wanted to help. The sun shined brightly and said, "Hello, new pupil! I am the sun, and I like to shine. You will make friends here, and you will be happy." The new pupil looked up and saw the nice sun shining. She smiled and said, "Thank you, sun. You are very nice. I will try to make friends and be happy too." The sun smiled back and kept shining. The new pupil played with the other pupils, and they all became friends. They learned and had fun together. The nice sun shined on them every day, making them happy. And they all lived happily ever after.

John wanted to try something new. He watched as the elderly man spun his body around and around. It looked so fun that he couldn't wait to give it a try. So he spread out his arms and legs and started to spin. He felt the wind all around him. Soon, John was spinning faster and faster. He closed his eyes and laughed as he spun round and round. He felt like he was soaring through the sky. He didn't want the feeling to ever end. When he felt like he couldn't spin any more, John stopped. He opened his eyes and looked around. The elderly man was still spinning, calmly, gracefully. He was amazed that the man could spin for so long. John decided he wanted to spin like the elderly man. He spread his arms and legs and began to twirl around and around. This time, he was careful to spin slowly and calmly. After a few moments, he was spinning just like the elderly man. John ended his spinning and opened his eyes. He felt great. He said hello to the elderly man, thanked him for showing him how to spin, and walked away, proud of his twirling achievement.
