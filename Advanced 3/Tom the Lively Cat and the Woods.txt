Title: Tom the Lively Cat and the Woods

One day, a lively cat named Tom wanted to go on an adventure. He heard about a big maze in the woods. Tom was very excited to go and play in the maze. Tom went to the maze and started to walk inside. It was very big and had many paths. Tom was having fun and he went left and right, up and down. He laughed and jumped as he tried to find his way. But soon, Tom got lost in the maze. He tried to find his way out, but he could not. He started to feel sad and scared. Tom called for help, but nobody could hear him. The lively cat was now all alone in the big maze.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a blackboard. The blackboard was very big and very black. A little boy named Timmy went to the big room every day to learn and play with his friends. One day, Timmy saw a brilliant light in the room. The light was so bright and shiny. It made Timmy very happy. He wanted to show his friends the shiny light. So, he took his friends to the big room with the blackboard. But when they got there, the light was gone. Timmy was sad. He wanted to find the light again. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. Timmy started to suffer because he wanted the light to come back. Then, Timmy had an idea. He took a piece of chalk and drew a sun on the blackboard. The sun was big and round, just like the brilliant light. Timmy and his friends were happy again. They played and laughed in the big room with the blackboard, and they knew that the brilliant light was always with them.

Anna and Ben like to play in the park. They run and jump and slide and swing. They have fun with their friends and their toys. But sometimes they get tired and want to rest. One day, they see a big tree in the park. It has many green leaves and a thick trunk. They think it looks like a good place to sit and relax. They run to the tree and find a shady spot under the branches. They sit down and lean on the trunk. They feel a soft fur on their backs. They turn around and see a big cat. It is black and white and has yellow eyes. It is sleeping on the tree. It does not see them or hear them. It is a very lazy cat. Anna and Ben smile and pet the cat. The cat purrs and opens one eye. It looks at them and closes the eye again. It does not mind them. It likes to be warm and cozy. Anna and Ben stay with the cat for a while. They talk and giggle and make up stories. They are happy and calm. They like the park and the tree and the lazy cat. They think they have found a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a mom and her daughter. They wanted to travel to a far away place. The daughter was very patient, and she waited for the day they could go. One day, a big mean wolf came to their home. The wolf said, "You cannot travel! I will not let you." The mom and daughter were sad, but they did not give up. The daughter had an idea. She said, "Let's wait until the wolf is asleep, and then we can travel." They waited and waited. When the wolf fell asleep, they left their home. The mom and daughter traveled far away and found a new, happy place to live.

Once there was a dependable olive tree. It used to give fruit to the animals that lived in the woods around it. But one day, a big dry spell came. All the animals were getting thirsty, especially the little rabbit who loved olives. The rabbit considered the olive tree's fruit to be the most dependable source of water during the dry spell. He asked the olive tree to please give him a piece of fruit. The olive tree said that it was too dry, and it was unable to produce any olives. The rabbit felt sad. He asked the olive tree if there was anything he could do to help make it not so dry. The olive tree said, yes, there is one thing that would help. The rabbit asked what it was, and the olive tree said, "If you can find some water for me to drink, then I can make olives for you and all the other animals to eat." The rabbit was so excited. He searched and searched until he finally found a stream with some water. He brought it back to the olive tree and helped the tree to drink the water. Pretty soon, the tree started to produce olives again! The other animals were so happy and relieved. From then on, the olive tree became known as the most dependable tree in the woods. The rabbit always remembered how important it was to help each other, even during a big dry spell.
