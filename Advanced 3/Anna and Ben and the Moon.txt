Title: Anna and Ben and the Moon

Anna and Ben were playing in the yard after dinner. They liked to look at the sky and see the stars and the moon. The moon was big and bright and round. Sometimes it was white, sometimes it was yellow, sometimes it was orange. "Look, Ben, the moon is purple today!" Anna said, pointing at the sky. Ben looked up and saw the purple moon. He was surprised and curious. He wondered why the moon was purple. "Why is the moon purple, Anna?" he asked. Anna shrugged. She did not know why the moon was purple. She made up a story. "Maybe the moon is purple because it is happy. Maybe it likes to change colors and play with us. Maybe it wants to be our friend," she said. Ben liked Anna's story. He smiled and nodded. He wanted to be the moon's friend too. "Can we talk to the moon, Anna?" he asked. Anna thought for a moment. She had an idea. "Maybe we can, Ben. Maybe if we say hello to the moon, it will say hello back. Maybe if we ask the moon to yield, it will yield. Let's try," she said. Anna and Ben stood up and waved at the purple moon. They shouted together. "Hello, moon! We like you! You are pretty and purple! Will you yield to us? Will you be our friend?" They waited for the moon to answer. They hoped the moon would yield. They hoped the moon would be their friend.

Once there was a kangaroo who lived in the Outback. He was a strong and fast kangaroo and loved to explore the desert. One day, the kangaroo hopped away from home and ended up in a strange land. He was scared, as he had never seen anything like it before. The kangaroo tried to find his way back home but he just could not find the way. Suddenly, a friendly wizard appeared and saw that the kangaroo was lost. The wizard waved his wand and sent the kangaroo back home. But the wand was broken and some of the magic didn't work. When the kangaroo reached the Outback, he found that his legs were a bit wobbly and he couldn't move properly. The wizard had sent him home but unfortunately, he was a bit broken. The kangaroo needed help and luckily his family were nearby. They fixed his legs and hugged him warmly. Everyone was happy and the kangaroo hopped home, safe and sound.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big room with many toys. One day, her mom told her to pack her toys in a box. Lucy was a good girl, so she started to pack her toys. As Lucy was packing, she found a clean sock behind her toy box. She was happy to find it, because it was her favorite sock. She put the sock on her foot and went back to packing her toys. When Lucy finished packing, she saw a tip of a crayon under her bed. Lucy picked up the crayon and put it in the box with her toys. Now, her room was clean and tidy. Lucy's mom was very proud of her, and they had a fun day playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play near the sea. One day, he saw a big black boat on the water. He thought it was so cool and wanted to get a closer look. Tim's mom saw him near the water and called out, "Tim, I warn you, don't go too close to the sea! It can be dangerous." Tim stopped and listened to his mom. He knew she was trying to keep him safe. Later that day, Tim and his mom sat on the sand and watched the black boat sail away. They talked about the boat and had a fun day together at the beach. Tim was happy he listened to his mom's warning and stayed safe.

Once upon a time, there was a fast rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to run and play in the big green field. One day, Remy heard about a big event that would happen soon. This event was a race for all the animals. Remy was very excite to join the race. On the day of the race, all the animals came to the big green field. Remy saw his friends, like the slow turtle and the happy bird. The race started and Remy ran very fast. He was sure he would win. But then, something unexpected happened. Remy saw a little bug in trouble. The little bug was stuck in a hole and could not get out. Remy stopped running and helped the little bug. The other animals passed Remy, but he did not care. After Remy saved the little bug, he ran fast again. But the slow turtle won the race. Remy was not sad because he did a good thing. The moral of the story is that being kind and helping others is more important than winning. Remy learned that it is good to be fast, but it is better to be a good friend. And that made Remy very happy.
