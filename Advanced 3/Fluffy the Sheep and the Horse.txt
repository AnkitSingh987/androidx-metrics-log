Title: Fluffy the Sheep and the Horse

Once upon a time, there was a little sheep named Fluffy. Fluffy had a lot of soft wool. One day, Fluffy felt very miserable. She was sad because her wool was too long and heavy. Fluffy asked her friend, the horse, "Can you help me? My wool is too long." The horse said, "I can grab some scissors and we can cut your wool." Fluffy was happy and said, "Thank you, friend!" The horse grabbed the scissors and cut Fluffy's wool. The wool fell to the ground. Fluffy felt much better. She was not miserable anymore. Fluffy and the horse played together all day long. They were very happy friends.

Once upon a time, in a humble little house, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and include them in all her games. She had a big sheet that she used to make a tent for her toys to play in. One day, Sue decided to make the biggest tent ever. She took her sheet and asked her mom to help her. Together, they made a big tent that covered the whole room. Sue was very happy and excited. Sue invited all her toys to play in the tent. They played games and had a lot of fun. The toys were happy to be included in Sue's big tent. At the end of the day, Sue and her toys went to sleep in their humble tent, dreaming of more fun days to come.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a sister named Rose. They lived in a small house with their mom and dad. Lily and Rose loved to play together in their old, brown treehouse. One day, Lily and Rose had a big fight. Lily wanted to play with her sister's teddy bear, but Rose did not want to share. Lily was very sad and ran away to the treehouse. Rose felt bad and wanted to make her sister happy again. Rose thought about how to fix the problem. She remembered a promise she made a long time ago. Rose had promised always to be nice to her sister. So, she took her teddy bear and went to the treehouse. Rose said sorry to Lily and gave her the teddy bear to play with. Lily was happy again, and they both promised to be nice to each other. From that day on, Lily and Rose played happily together in their old treehouse.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy whistle that he loved to play with. One day, Tim was feeling naughty. He took his whistle and went to play outside. In the park, Tim saw a girl named Lily. Lily was folding a paper to make a pretty bird. Tim thought it would be fun to blow his whistle very loudly. So, he went close to Lily and blew the whistle. "Toot! Toot!" It scared Lily, and she dropped her paper bird. She was sad and said, "Why did you do that?" Tim felt bad for making Lily sad. He said, "I'm sorry, I was being naughty. Can I help you fold a new bird?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, please." They sat together, and Tim helped Lily fold a new paper bird. They became good friends and played together every day. Tim learned that it was better to be kind than to be naughty.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little mouse named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with a sink. One day, Tim wanted to make a cake, but he did not have a big bowl. Tim went to his friend, the kind rabbit named Sam. "Sam, can I borrow your big bowl, please?" asked Tim. Sam said, "Yes, you can borrow my bowl, but please bring it back when you are done." Tim baked a tasty cake with the big bowl. When he finished, he remembered to bring the bowl back to Sam. Sam was very happy and said, "Thank you for bringing my bowl back, Tim." They both enjoyed a piece of cake together. The moral of the story is: always return what you borrow from others.
