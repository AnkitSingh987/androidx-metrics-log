Title: Lila and Tom and the Stone

Lila and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to look for stones and leaves and flowers. Lila found a beautiful stone. It was shiny and smooth and had many colors. She showed it to Tom. "Look, Tom, I found a beautiful stone!" she said. Tom looked at the stone. He wanted it for himself. He tried to take it from Lila. "Give me the stone, Lila. It is mine!" he said. Lila refused to give him the stone. She held it tight in her hand. "No, Tom, it is not yours. I found it first. It is mine!" she said. Tom got angry. He pushed Lila and made her fall. Lila dropped the stone and started to cry. "Tom, that is not nice! You hurt Lila!" said a voice. It was their teacher, Miss Jane. She saw what happened and came to help. She picked up Lila and the stone and hugged them. "Are you okay, Lila?" she asked. Lila nodded. She still cried a little. "Tom, you have to say sorry to Lila. You cannot take things that are not yours. You have to share and be kind." said Miss Jane. Tom felt sorry. He knew he did wrong. He went to Lila and said sorry. "I am sorry, Lila. I was mean. Can we be friends again?" he asked. Lila smiled. She forgave Tom. She showed him the stone again. "Look, Tom, it is a beautiful stone. Do you want to play with it?" she asked. Tom nodded. He thanked Lila. They played with the stone together. They made a necklace with it and some string. They were happy. Miss Jane was proud of them. She gave them a hug and a cookie. They had a good day.

Once upon a time there was a sheep who was very gifted. She had the ability to grab anything and everything with her long, soft ears. Every day she would grab apples, carrots, even flowers and bring them to her friends who couldn't reach them. One day, while she was out in the meadow all by herself, she noticed something shiny in the grass. She grabbed it with her ears and brought it back to her house. Inside her home she saw that the shiny thing was a string and so she decided to grab it and start to play. The sheep enjoyed playing with the string so much that she decided to go out and grab more strings. She was delighted when she realised that the strings turned into a wonderful rope when she put them all together. The gifted sheep then used the rope to knit a beautiful blanket for her friends. Everyone who saw the blanket was amazed and thanked the sheep for her hard work. She then grabbed lots of other strings and yarn to make more blankets for the people in the village and they were forever thankful to the gifted sheep.

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Tim. He wanted to soar high in the sky. Tim was a little scared, but his friend, Sam the squirrel, told him, "You can do it, Tim!" One day, Tim heard a tap on his door. It was Sam. Sam said, "Let's go to the park and play!" Tim was excited, but he knew that there was a fierce dog at the park. Tim was scared of the dog, but he wanted to play with Sam. At the park, Tim saw the fierce dog. He was scared, but he remembered what Sam said. Tim took a deep breath and started to soar high in the sky. The fierce dog could not reach him. Tim was so happy that he could play with Sam and be safe. From that day on, Tim loved to soar high and play with his friend Sam.

Once upon a time there was a big family. They all liked each other very much and often had fun together. But sometimes the family would disagree about what to do. One year, the family wanted to do something impressive. They had an idea that everyone wanted to do together but couldn't agree on what it should be. After a lot of talking, the family decided to go to the beach. At the beach, everyone was filled with excitement. The sun was shining, the waves were crashing and the sand was so soft. Everyone was having so much fun that they couldn't disagree anymore! They played games, splashed around in the water, and ate yummy snacks. The day was so enjoyable and impressive that the whole family vowed to go back to the beach together every year. They had realized that even when they disagreed, they could still figure out ways to have fun together!
