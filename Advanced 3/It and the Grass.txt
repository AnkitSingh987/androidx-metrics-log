Title: It and the Grass

It was a sunny day and Johnny wanted to go wandering. He went outside and looked up at the sky. He could see the high clouds sailing past. Johnny looked around and he saw a big field of grass with no end in sight. He started walking, looking around at all the plants and birds. After a few minutes, he heard a voice calling out behind him. It was his dad. "Johnny, where are you going?" his dad asked. Johnny stopped and said, "I'm just wandering. I'm looking for something." His dad smiled. "Well, why don't you wander around for a year and see what you find?" Johnny thought about it for a minute and then he smiled. "OK! I'll wander for a year and see what I find!" And so Johnny went wandering for a whole year, exploring all sorts of new places, seeing new things and learning lots of interesting things. The year sure was high with adventure and fun! At the end of the year, when Johnny returned home, he had lots of stories to tell. He was sure that he had found something special.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. His favorite toy was a big, red, fake helmet. He wore the helmet all day and pretended to be a brave hero. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play with his friends. They wanted to play a game of hide and seek. Tim wore his red helmet and started to count. His friends ran to find good hiding spots. Tim found his friends hiding behind trees and in bushes. After the game, they were all tired and hungry. Tim's mom made them sandwiches. Tim helped her fold the napkins. They all sat down, ate their sandwiches, and laughed together. Tim felt happy with his red helmet and his friends.

Once there was a little bug who loved to crawl. Every day he crawled around in the grass looking for snacks. Today he crawled faster than ever! He crawled and crawled until he found the biggest snack of all. It was a delicious cookie! He grabbed the cookie and started to eat it. He ate it so fast that he didn't even have time to digest it. He crawled away with a smile on his face, feeling happy after his snack.

Once upon a time, there was a little kid named Tim. Tim had a white toy he loved so much. The toy was a fluffy bunny. Tim took the bunny everywhere he went. One day, Tim and his bunny went to the park. They played on the swings and the slide. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He asked his bunny, "Will you support me while I climb the tree?" The bunny said, "Yes, I will support you, Tim." So, Tim put the bunny in his pocket and started to climb the tree. He felt brave with his bunny by his side. They climbed higher and higher until they reached the top. From the top of the tree, Tim and his bunny could see the whole park. They saw kids playing and dogs running. Tim and his bunny felt happy. They climbed down the tree and went home. Tim knew his bunny would always support him.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very excited because his mom said he could help her make cookies. Tim loved cookies and he loved helping his mom. Tim's mom said, "Tim, can you please open the cabinet and get the bowl?" Tim was happy that his mom let him do this. He opened the cabinet and got the bowl. Then, they put all the things they needed into the bowl and mixed them up. After they made the cookie dough, Tim's mom let him put the cookies on the pan. Tim said, "Thank you, Mom, for letting me help you!" They both smiled and put the cookies in the oven. Soon, the house was filled with the smell of yummy cookies. Tim and his mom enjoyed eating the cookies they made together.

Once upon a time, in a jolly little house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily liked to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Lily's mom asked her to fold some napkins. Lily was happy to help. Lily folded the napkins into fun shapes. She made a dog, a cat, and a bird. Lily's mom smiled and put the napkins on the table. Then, Lily's mom asked her to get some food from the refrigerator. Lily opened the big refrigerator and found yummy food inside. She took out apples, cheese, and bread. Lily and her mom made a tasty meal together. They sat down at the table with the fun napkins and enjoyed their food. They were both very happy.
