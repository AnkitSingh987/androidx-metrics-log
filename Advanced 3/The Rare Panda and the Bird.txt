Title: The Rare Panda and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a rare panda. One morning, the panda woke up very early. He was very hungry. He wanted to find some food to eat. The panda walked and walked until he met a little bird. The bird said, "Hello, panda! What are you doing?" The panda said, "I am hungry. I want to find some food." The bird said, "I know a place with lots of food. Follow me!" So, the panda followed the bird. They walked and walked until they found a big tree with lots of food. The panda was very happy. But then, a big, mean bear came. The bear was angry and said, "This is my food! Go away!" The panda was sad and walked away. He was still hungry and did not find any food to eat. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very independent and liked to do things by herself. One day, Lily wanted to try something new. She opened the trunk that was in her house, and found so many exciting things inside! She saw colorful clothes, sparkly jewelry and toys, but the thing that caught her eye was a big book. Lily was so curious and excited, she opened the book and started to read it. She read and read, and soon found out all the book's secrets. The book taught her how to yield all sorts of things, and Lily was amazed. She could yield music from her old toy piano and ride a bike with no help. She became even more independent and stronger! Lily was very proud of how far she had come. She put the book back in the trunk with a smile, never forgetting the wonderful things it had taught her.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside in the rain. She liked the wet grass and the wet leaves. She thought it was fun to splash in the puddles. One day, Sue saw her mom pour water from a big cup into a small cup. Sue thought it was a cool style. She wanted to try it too. So, she got her toy cups and went outside to play in the rain. Sue found a big puddle and filled her big toy cup with wet rainwater. Then, she poured the water from the big cup into the small cup, just like her mom did. Sue was happy with her new style of playing. She played in the rain all day, pouring water from one cup to another.

Once upon a time, there was a little bridge over a cold river. Many animals lived near the bridge. They were happy and played together all day long. One day, a big storm came and made the river very cold and fast. A small mouse tried to cross the bridge, but she was scared. She did not want to suffer in the cold water if she fell. A kind rabbit saw the mouse and decided to help her. The rabbit held the mouse's hand and walked with her across the bridge. They both made it safely to the other side. The other animals saw this and learned that helping each other was a good thing. They all became better friends and helped each other when they were in trouble. The moral of the story is that we should always help others when they need it, and we will be happy and safe together.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Pete. He was three years old and loved playing in the garden with his best friend Jake. One day Pete and Jake were playing when the sky got really dark. "It's getting late," said Jake. "Time to go in." "No," said Pete. "Let's count words first!" So the two little boys sat down in the warm grass and started counting words. They counted one, two and then three. Pete looked up, excited. "We can do this forever!" he said. But Jake shook his head. "Wait a minute," he said. "Do you know why we're counting words?" Pete didn't know. "It's so we can learn to read," said Jake. "It's called reading practice. Once we can count words, we can start to read them. Then books will come to life!" Pete smiled. He couldn't wait to start reading. As the two friends went into the house, they knew they had discovered a very special way to make books even warmer.
