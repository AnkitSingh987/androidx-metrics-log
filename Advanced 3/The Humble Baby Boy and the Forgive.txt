Title: The Humble Baby Boy and the Forgive

Once upon a time, there was a humble baby boy who lived in a small crib. The crib was cozy and warm, the perfect place for the little boy to sleep. Every day the boy's parents came to his crib to check on him and tell him stories. They would always remind the little boy to be kind and forgive others, even when they make mistakes. One day, the little boy was very angry because a friend had said something mean to him. He refused to forgive the friend. However, his parents reminded him again that he should forgive. The little boy thought his parents were wrong and refused to forgive. He ended up being very lonely and sad. Eventually, the little boy realized how important it was to forgive. When he finally did, he found that life was much happier and his friends were much kinder. From then on, the little boy always tried to be humble and forgive. And all was good in the crib.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a hairy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toys and run around in the yard. One day, Spot found a card on the floor. It was a pretty card with a picture of a cat on it. Spot picked it up in his mouth and wagged his tail. Spot ran to his friend, a little girl named Sue. "Look, Spot found a card!" said Sue, clapping her hands. "Let's play with it!" Sue and Spot played a fun game with the card. They took turns hiding it and finding it again. They laughed and had a great time. But then, Spot got too excited. He jumped on the card and it broke in half. "Oh no!" said Sue. "The card is broken! What do we do?" Spot felt sad, but then he had an idea. He ran to get some tape. Sue and Spot worked together to fix the card. When it was all done, they hugged and promised to be more careful next time. And they continued playing with their fixed card, happy and full of love.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, lived a cat and a dog. The cat was always cleaning and making things look nice. The dog was big and strong, but sometimes he would tremble when he heard loud noises. One day, the cat said to the dog, "I am going out for some time. Please keep the house tidy while I am gone." The dog nodded and promised to do his best. While the cat was away, the dog heard a loud bang outside. He began to tremble and knocked over a table, making a big mess. The cat came back and saw the mess. "Oh no!" cried the cat. "You did not keep the house tidy!" The cat was very sad, and the dog felt bad for making a mess.

One day, a small, harmless bug was walking on a wall. The bug was very happy. It liked to walk up and down the wall all day. The sun was shining, and the bug was having fun. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew the bug off the wall. The bug was scared. It did not know what to do. It called out, "Help! I need someone to save me!" A little girl heard the bug and came to help. The little girl saw the bug on the ground. She knew the bug was harmless and needed help. She carefully picked up the bug and put it back on the wall. The bug was so happy to be saved. It said, "Thank you for saving me!" The little girl smiled and watched the bug walk up and down the wall again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to dress in her favorite clothes. Every day, she would put on her pretty dress and go outside to play. One day, Lily went out to play in the big landscape. There were many trees, flowers, and animals. She saw a bunny hopping and a bird singing. Lily was very happy to be outside in the fresh air. Lily got hungry, so she went home to eat. Her mommy made her a yummy sandwich. Lily thought it was the best sandwich ever. After eating, she went back outside to play some more. She played all day long in the beautiful landscape, and she had a great time.
