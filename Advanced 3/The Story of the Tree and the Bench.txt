Title: The Story of the Tree and the Bench

Once upon a time, there was a big park with an enormous tree. Under the tree, there was a little bench where kids could sit and play. One sunny day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue went to the park to play. Tim and Sue wanted to build a big tower with their toy blocks. They sat on the bench and started to add blocks, one on top of the other. The tower grew taller and taller as they added more blocks. They were so happy and proud of their big tower. Suddenly, a strong wind blew and knocked down their tower. The blocks fell all around the bench. Tim and Sue were sad, but they did not give up. They picked up the blocks and started to add them again. This time, they built an even bigger tower. And they lived happily ever after, playing and adding blocks under the enormous tree.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a great bird. The bird had big wings that could spread wide. One day, a small cloud came to the forest. The cloud was sad because it could not spread rain on the plants. The great bird saw the sad cloud and wanted to help. The bird spread its wings and gave the cloud a big hug. The hug made the cloud very happy. The cloud started to spread rain on the plants. The plants grew big and tall because of the rain. The animals in the forest were happy too. The great bird and the cloud became good friends. They learned that helping each other made the forest a better place. And that was the moral of the story: helping others can make everyone happy.

Once there was a brilliant hammer. It was so brilliant, it worked really hard. One day it saw a bunch of toys that it wanted to help out. So it looked around and spotted a pile of logs. It quit what it was doing, grabbed the pile of logs and started pounding them together. All the toys watched it work and cheered. The hammer was so tired that it quit after a long day of work. All the toys said thank you and waved goodbye, but the hammer was already dreaming of what it would build tomorrow. The next day, the hammer was back and ready to work again. It picked up its hammer and started pounding the logs into shape. All the toys watched in amazement as the hammer was able to make brilliant pieces of furniture with ease. Finally, the hammer had finished its work. All the toys clapped and cheered. The hammer had done a brilliant job and deserved a rest. "Thank you for all your hard work hammer," one of the toys said. The hammer smiled, waved goodbye and quit for the day.

Once upon a time there was a mummy, daddy and baby. Mummy and daddy wanted to take a walk, but it was raining and the sky was dull. Mummy said, "Let's quit this walk and stay home". Daddy said, "No, let's go for a walk; the rain will soon stop". So mummy, daddy and baby went for a walk. But the rain kept on falling and the ground got more and more wet. Pretty soon there was a flood. Mummy said, "Uh oh, quit this walk now!" Daddy said, "Yes, let's go home now!" So they all quit the walk and went home. Inside they were safe and warm. The End.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a fierce lion. The lion had a weapon, a big sharp claw. He used his weapon to scare all the little animals in the forest. The lion thought it was fun to see them run away. One day, a small bird saw the lion scaring her friends. The bird felt sad and began to weep. The lion saw the bird weeping and felt bad. He asked the bird why she was weeping. The bird told the lion that his fierce actions and weapon made her friends scared and sad. The lion thought about it and decided to change. He stopped scaring the little animals and became their friend instead. The lion learned that being kind is better than being fierce. And all the animals lived happily and played together.
