Title: The Dragon and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a small dragon. He lived in a big cave. All the other dragons were big and strong. The small dragon felt sad because he was not big like them. One day, the small dragon met a kind girl. The girl saw that the dragon was sad. She wanted to help him. The girl gave the small dragon a kiss on his nose. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. The small dragon grew big and strong, just like the other dragons! The small dragon was very happy. He learned that being kind and loving can make you strong, too. The girl and the small dragon became best friends. They played together every day, and they both learned that love and kindness can make you big and strong.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient toy. It was old and had a cord. The toy could move when you pulled the cord. It was a little car. The car lived in a big toy box with many other toys. The toys loved to play together. One day, the little car wanted to go on a big adventure. It asked its friend, the toy dog, to come along. They waited until the night when the boy who owned them was asleep. Then, they pulled the cord and the little car began to move. The toy dog jumped on the car and they went out of the toy box. As they moved around the room, they saw something unexpected. A big, shiny toy robot was standing near the door. The robot said, "I can help you on your adventure." The little car and the toy dog were happy. They thought the robot would make their adventure more fun. But then, the robot did something surprising. It picked up the little car and the toy dog and put them back in the toy box. The robot said, "It's not safe to go on adventures at night." The little car and the toy dog were sad, but they knew the robot was right. They decided to wait and have their adventure another day.

One day, a dog named Spot wanted to go for a walk. He had a long leash, but he could not find it. Spot asked his friend, Cat, "Can you lend me your leash?" Cat said, "Yes, I can lend you my leash. But it is not long like yours. It is short." Spot was happy and said, "Thank you, Cat!" They went for a walk together. Spot liked the short leash too. They saw many things like trees, flowers, and other animals. They had a fun day. When they went back home, Spot found his long leash. He was happy but remembered that he had fun with the short leash too. Spot thanked Cat again for lending him the leash. They became best friends and went for walks together every day.

One day, a cat and a dog made a plan. They wanted to play a trick on their friend, a big bear. They thought it would be fun to make a disgusting cake. They put mud, bugs, and yucky stuff in it. They laughed as they made the cake. The cat said, "Bear will think this is so funny!" When the cake was done, they took it to bear's house. They knocked on the door and said, "We have a cake for you, Bear! We made it just for you!" Bear was very happy and thanked them. Bear took a big bite of the disgusting cake. But, to their surprise, Bear loved it! He said, "This is the best cake I have ever had!" The cat and the dog looked at each other in shock. Their plan did not work as they thought, but they were happy that Bear liked the cake. They all laughed and played together for the rest of the day.

Billy likes to watch. He watches everything around him. Today, he found something really exciting. He saw a minute. It was so small and original. He wanted it for himself. All day, he tried to get the minute but it kept moving away from him. He tried to follow it, but it was too quick! Suddenly, his little brother came over with a big smile. He was holding the minute. Billy was so angry with his brother, but he was too small to do anything about it. Billy yelled and tried to snatch the minute, but his brother was too fast and jumped away. Billy tried to catch him but he just couldn't reach him. His brother was having too much fun playing with the minute! Billy eventually gave up the chase and went back to watching. He watched his brother playing with the original minute, and waited for his chance to take it back.
