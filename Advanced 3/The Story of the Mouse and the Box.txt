Title: The Story of the Mouse and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy diamond. It was in a box. The box was in a house. A small mouse lived in the house too. The mouse liked the diamond. The mouse wanted the diamond to escape with him. One day, the mouse made a plan. He pushed the box near the door. The door was open, so the mouse could run away with the diamond. The mouse was happy. He thought his plan would work. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came! The wind blew the door shut. The mouse could not escape with the diamond. The mouse was sad. But the wind also blew the box open. Inside the box was not just one diamond, but many diamonds! The mouse was happy again. He could not escape with the diamonds, but now he had many shiny friends to play with.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Jack. He loved to look up in the sky and see the big jets. He was always so excited to see the jets soaring up in the sky, going very fast. One day, Jack decided to take a walk to a quiet place where he could really enjoy looking at the jets. He walked and walked until he came to a lake, which was very still and quiet. Jack sat down by the lake and suddenly he could see a jet in the sky far away. He watched it as it flew closer and closer. As it got closer, Jack noticed it was getting very quiet in the sky. Before long, the jet was flying directly over him, so close to him that he could almost touch it. Jack smiled as he watched the jet race off into the distance. He felt very happy that he had got to see such a beautiful jet, flying so quietly through the sky.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little fairy. She had a magic wand. The fairy liked to help others. She lived in a small house made of leaves. One day, a little boy came into the forest. He was lost and scared. He saw the fairy's house, but he did not know what it was. It was unknown to him. He went closer to look at it. The fairy saw the boy and came out of her house. She said, "Don't be scared. I am a good fairy. I can help you." The boy looked at her and started to believe her. The fairy waved her magic wand, and the boy was not lost anymore. He was happy and thanked the fairy. They became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big folder. The big folder was very good at holding papers. It lived in a small room with other folders. One day, a little girl named Sue found the big folder. Sue said, "Wow, you are a big folder! Can you hold my drawings?" The big folder was happy to help. It opened up and Sue put her drawings inside. The big folder kept the drawings safe and flat. Sue liked to draw a lot. She drew every day. The big folder remained with her always, holding her drawings. Sue and the big folder became best friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a charming girl named Lily. Lily loved to read. She had many books with pretty pictures. Her favorite book was about a big, red coat. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, it's cold outside. Put on your coat and let's go for a walk." Lily put on her big, red coat and went outside with her mom. They walked and walked until they saw a little dog. The dog was cold too. Lily said, "Mom, can we help the dog?" Her mom nodded. They took the dog home and gave it a warm coat. The dog was happy. They all sat down and Lily read her favorite book about the big, red coat to the dog. The dog listened and wagged its tail. They all had a fun day together.

Once upon a time there lived a little boy called Tim. Every day he woke up feeling happy and excited, because he knew he was going to have a new adventure. One day Tim was playing in his garden and he saw a big box lying there. He opened the box and inside he found a big red ball, it was the most beautiful thing he had ever seen! "I must have it!", said Tim. He hugged the ball tightly. Suddenly, he heard a voice. It was his father. "How are you, Tim?", he asked. "I'm playing with this ball, Daddy!", replied Tim. "It's very important that you complete whatever you set out to do today, with love." said his father. Tim nodded and smiled. Tim played with the ball for many hours and before long, he had completed all his adventures for the day. He hugged the ball again and thought to himself, 'this is the most important thing in the world - I love it!'
