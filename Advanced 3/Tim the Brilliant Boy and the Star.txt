Title: Tim the Brilliant Boy and the Star

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant little boy named Tim. He loved to look at the stars in the sky. One night, Tim saw a new star. It was very bright and very big. He thought it might be a new planet. Tim was very excited. Tim went to his friend, Jane. He whispered to her, "I found a new planet!" Jane was excited too. They wanted to tell everyone about the new planet. They whispered to their friends, and their friends whispered to more friends. Soon, everyone knew about the new planet. But then something unexpected happened. Tim's dad, who knew a lot about stars, told them the truth. It was not a new planet. It was a big, bright star that was going to explode soon. Tim and his friends were sad that it was not a planet, but they were still happy to know about the special star. They all watched the sky together, waiting for the brilliant star to explode.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a lamp that she loved very much. The lamp was her best friend. It was red, and it made her room bright at night. One day, Amy's mom gave her a bitter lemon. Amy did not like the taste. She said, "Mom, I don't like this lemon. It's too bitter." Her mom said, "Okay, we won't eat bitter lemons anymore." That night, Amy told her lamp about the bitter lemon. She said, "Lamp, I had a bitter lemon today. I did not like it." The lamp listened to her and made her feel better. From that day on, Amy and her lamp always talked about their days before bedtime. They were very happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a big red truck. The truck had oil inside. The truck loved to share the oil with his friends. His friends were a small blue car and a fast green bike. They were always alert and ready to play. One day, the truck said, "Let's share my oil today!" The small blue car and fast green bike were happy. They took the oil and said, "Thank you, big red truck!" They all played together all day long. But they did not see the big hole in the road. The small blue car and fast green bike fell into the hole. They could not get out. The big red truck tried to help, but he also fell into the hole. They all stayed in the hole and could not play anymore. The moral of the story is to always be alert and look where you are going.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He wanted to measure his bed. Tim was very excited to do this. He wanted to see how big his bed was. Tim was a bit careless. He did not know how to measure the right way. Tim tried to use his hands, his feet, and even his toys to measure his bed. But he could not do it right. Then, Tim's mom came in. She saw Tim trying to measure his bed. She smiled and gave Tim a ruler. Tim and his mom measured the bed together. They found out how big the bed was. Tim was very happy and thanked his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. His name was Sam. Sam was 3 years old and he loved to kick. Every day, he would head outside and kick a ball with his neighbor. Today, Sam noticed something different about his neighbor. He looked much more reliable than usual. Sam was curious. “Why do you look so reliable today, neighbor?” Sam asked. The neighbor smiled. “I've just been chosen to help at the big event. I'm very proud!” he replied. Sam was so excited. He ran to kick his ball and shouted “Congratulations! You'll do a great job!” The neighbor smiled and thanked Sam. Everyone in the neighborhood was proud of him. From then on, his neighbor was even more reliable than before.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was excited to celebrate her birthday. Lily's mom and dad were busy getting all the stuff ready for her big day. One day, Lily found a big box in her room. She was confused. What could be inside? She tried to open it, but it was locked. Lily's mom and dad saw her and smiled. They said, "Lily, be patient. You will see what's inside on your birthday." Finally, Lily's birthday came. She was so happy to celebrate with her friends. They played games, ate cake, and had lots of fun. Her mom and dad told her it was time to open the big box. Lily opened the box and found a beautiful new bike inside. She was so happy! All this time, the big box was a surprise for her birthday. Lily's mom and dad had planned it all along. They all went outside to ride the bike, and Lily had the best birthday ever.
