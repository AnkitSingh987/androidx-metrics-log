Title: Molly the Mule and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a mule named Molly. Molly was eager to make new friends. She lived on a farm with other animals like cows, pigs, and chickens. One day, Molly saw a new animal on the farm. She did not know what it was. She went to the cow and asked, "Who is that?" The cow said, "I don't know. Go question the pig." So, Molly went to the pig and asked the same question. The pig said, "I don't know either. Go question the chicken." Molly went to the chicken and asked the same question again. The chicken said, "That is a dog. His name is Spot." Molly was excited to meet Spot, but Spot did not want to be friends with Molly. He barked at her and scared her away. Molly was sad. One day, a big storm came to the farm. All the animals were scared. But Spot was brave. He helped Molly find a safe place to hide from the storm. Molly was surprised that Spot helped her. After the storm, Molly and Spot became good friends. They learned that even though they were different, they could still be friends.

Once upon a time, in a wide, wide house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toys, but her room was full of dust. One day, her mom told her to clean her room. Sue didn't want to, but her mom was very stern. She said, "Sue, you must clean your room today!" Sue started to clean her room. She found a special toy under her bed. It was a magic wand! Sue waved the wand and said, "I demand all the dust to go away!" The dust began to move and soon it was gone. Sue's room was now clean. Later that day, Sue's mom came into her room. She was so happy to see it clean. Sue showed her mom the magic wand. Her mom smiled and said, "Sue, you did a great job cleaning your room. The magic wand is a special toy, but remember, you must always clean your room yourself." Sue nodded and hugged her mom. From that day on, Sue always made sure her room was clean.

Once upon a time there was a bee. The bee was looking for a place to live and to provide for her young. She flew around the meadow until she found the perfect spot; a sweet smelling hive tucked away in a corner. The bee was so happy. She went inside and immediately started to do her job as a bee. She gathered sweet nectar from flowers and made honey to provide a comfy home for her little bee family. The bee worked hard all day long and soon the hive was buzzing with the sound of her many new children. She provided them with her delicious honey and taught them the important skill of collecting nectar. The bee and her family enjoyed a quiet and sweet life in the hive, buzzing away happily together. And they lived happily ever after!

Once upon a time, there was a funny van that could talk. The van had big, round wheels and a bright, happy face. It loved to drive around and play with the children in the park. One sunny day, the funny van met a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to build a big tower with his toy blocks. The van wanted to help Tim, so it opened its doors and let Tim add more blocks from inside the van. Together, they built the biggest and most colorful tower ever. Tim was so happy, and the funny van was proud to help. From that day on, Tim and the funny van were the best of friends, always playing and having fun together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily wanted to travel to a fancy place. She put on her favorite dress and her big hat. She was ready for her big trip. Lily walked to the park and saw a big tree. In the tree, she found a pear. The pear was big and green. Lily picked the pear and put it in her bag. On her way back home, Lily met a squirrel. The squirrel was hungry. Lily shared the pear with the squirrel. They ate the pear together and became friends. The end.
