Title: The Bird Was and the Frog

One day, a little bird was in a thick tree. The bird wanted to sing a song. The bird did not know any songs. The bird felt sad. The bird saw a big frog. The frog knew many songs. The frog said, "I can help you. Let's try to sing a song together." The bird was happy and said, "Yes, please." The bird and the frog tried to sing a song. They sang loud and soft. They sang high and low. Soon, the bird could sing a beautiful song. The bird and the frog were very happy. They sang songs all day in the thick tree.

Once upon a time, there was a very patient man. He was determined to win a race. The race was to carry a big weight. Every day, the patient man practiced by picking up the heavy weight and walking around. One day, another man saw him practice and he was impatient. He wanted to win the race, so he asked the patient man if he could try. But the patient man said no. The impatient man was angry. He snuck back to the patient man's house. He took a big knife and cut the weight. He was so happy that he carried it away. But when it was time for the race, the patient man won! That's because he was so strong and patient, even though he was carrying a light weight. The impatient man felt so silly.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. Lucy felt lucky because it was a sunny day. At the park, Lucy saw a big tree with something yellow on it. She was very curious and wanted to see what it was. Lucy asked her mom, "What is that yellow thing on the tree?" Her mom smiled and said, "That is a lemon, Lucy. We need lemons to make lemonade." Lucy was excited and wanted to make lemonade when they got home. When they went back home, Lucy and her mom picked lemons from the tree. They squeezed the lemons and made yummy lemonade. Lucy was so happy and felt lucky to have a lemon tree in her yard. She shared the lemonade with her friends and they all loved it.

Tommy loved to play with his toy dragon. He named it Spike and he liked to imagine that it was a real dragon that could fly and breathe fire. He would make Spike roar and flap his wings and chase his other toys around the room. One day, Tommy's mom brought home a surprise for him. It was a big box with a bow on it. "What is it, Mommy?" Tommy asked, excited. "It's a gift for you, Tommy. Open it and see," Mommy said, smiling. Tommy tore off the bow and the paper and opened the box. Inside was a huge stuffed dragon, much bigger than Spike. It was green and chubby and had shiny scales and a long tail. Tommy gasped and hugged the dragon. "Wow, Mommy, thank you! It's a giant Spike!" Mommy laughed and said, "You're welcome, Tommy. I thought you would like it. You can play with it and imagine that it's a real dragon, too." Tommy was very happy. He put the giant Spike next to his bed and played with it every day. He imagined that they were flying over mountains and forests and having adventures. He still loved his toy Spike, but he liked the giant Spike more. He thought it was more fun and more real. But one night, something strange happened. Tommy woke up and heard a loud roar. He opened his eyes and saw the giant Spike moving and glowing in the dark. He was scared and confused. He reached for his toy Spike, but it was gone. He looked around and saw that his other toys were gone, too. He saw the giant Spike's mouth open and fire coming out. He realized that the giant Spike was not a stuffed dragon, but a real dragon. And he was hungry. Tommy screamed and tried to run, but it was too late. The giant Spike snapped his jaws and swallowed Tommy whole. The end.
