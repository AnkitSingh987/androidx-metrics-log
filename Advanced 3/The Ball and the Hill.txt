Title: The Ball and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a big ball that loved to roll. The ball was always ready to play. One day, the ball rolled into the woods and met a new friend. The new friend was an axe. The axe was very nice, but he could not roll like the ball. The ball and the axe wanted to play together, but they did not know how. The ball had an idea. "Let's roll down the hill together!" said the ball. The axe was not sure, but he wanted to try. So, the ball and the axe went to the top of the hill. As they started to roll down the hill, something unexpected happened. The axe began to roll too! The axe was so happy that he could roll with his new friend. They both rolled down the hill, laughing and having fun. From that day on, the ball and the axe were best friends, and they played together every day.

One day, a colorful bird named Billy was flying around. Billy saw a big tree with a small hole. He thought, "This could be a good home for me!" So, he went inside the hole and found it very cozy. Billy met a friendly squirrel named Sally who lived in the tree too. Sally said, "Hi, I'm Sally. I recommend you make your home here too. It's very nice and we can be friends!" Billy was happy and said, "Yes, I will make my home here!" Billy and Sally became best friends. They played games, shared food, and had lots of fun in their colorful tree home. They were both very happy and enjoyed their simple life together.

Once upon a time, in a busy valley, there lived a big red car. The car loved to drive up and down the valley. One sunny day, the car met a little blue car. "Hi, I am the big red car. Do you want to play with me?" asked the big red car. "Yes, I want to play," said the little blue car. They played all day, driving up and down the valley. They had lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The little blue car got stuck in a big hole. The big red car tried to help by using reverse. It pulled the little blue car out of the hole. They were both happy and continued to play. At the end of the day, the big red car and the little blue car became best friends. They played together in the busy valley every day, and they always helped each other when they needed it.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a wander in her yard. She saw a small lizard near a big rock. Lily was happy to see the lizard and wanted to be its friend. "Hi, lizard! I am Lily. Do you want to play with me?" she asked. The lizard looked at her and nodded its head. They played together all day and had lots of fun. Lily felt successful at making a new friend. But then, something unexpected happened. The lizard started to grow big and tall! It was not a lizard at all, but a friendly dragon! Lily was not scared, and the dragon gave her a ride in the sky. They both had a great time and became best friends forever.

Jordan was so excited! He had been patiently waiting for his dad to give him the rare ash. His dad had told him it was a special gift that would help him to make things clean. At last the day arrived and Jordan was very eager to try it. His dad showed him how to use it and gave him instructions to make sure he handled it with care. "Now Jordan, you must always remember to use it for good. It's very powerful and that's why it's so rare," said his dad. Jordan smiled and gave his dad a hug. He was so happy that his dad shared this special gift with him. Jordan went to his room and began to use the ash. He was amazed at how it made everything so clean quickly! Now Jordan's room was the neatest room in the entire house.
