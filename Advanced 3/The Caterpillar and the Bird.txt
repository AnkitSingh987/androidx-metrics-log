Title: The Caterpillar and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little caterpillar. The caterpillar was very hungry. It saw a big, heavy leaf and wanted to eat it. The caterpillar was very excited. The caterpillar started to eat the leaf. It ate and ate and ate. But the leaf was so heavy, it made the caterpillar's tummy hurt. The caterpillar felt sad. Then, a big bird saw the caterpillar. The bird wanted to eat the caterpillar. The caterpillar was scared and started to scream. The bird came closer and closer. The caterpillar tried to hide, but the bird found it. The bird ate the caterpillar, and the caterpillar was gone.

One day, a furry cat went out to play. The cat saw a chain on the ground. The cat played with the chain and had a lot of fun. A little boy saw the cat and said, "Hi, cat! Can I play with you?" The cat said, "Yes, come play with me and my chain!" They played and had a great time. Then, the cat and the boy found a big door. The boy said, "Let's open it!" But when they opened the door, a big wind came and shut it fast. The boy and the cat were scared, but then they saw a big room full of toys. They played all day and had even more fun.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. It was very cold and icy. He put on his big coat and ran to his friend, Sam. Sam had a toy gun that he wanted to show Tim. Sam said, "Look, Tim, I have a new toy gun! Let's play cops and robbers!" Tim was excited, so they began to play. They ran around, pretending to catch bad guys. While they were playing, Tim found a big box. It was locked, but Tim had a key. He used the key to unlock the box. Inside the box, they found a big, warm blanket. Tim and Sam were very happy because they were cold from the icy weather. They wrapped themselves in the blanket and continued to play, now feeling warm and cozy.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Tom and Lucy. Tom was a curious little boy who wanted to try new things. One day he said to Lucy, “I want to learn about the world. Can you help me?” Lucy replied, “Yes, of course I can! I know a modern way to learn.” Tom was very excited. “What is it?” he asked. “It’s called a computer,” said Lucy. “It can show you many things. You can use it to learn about different places, animals and plants. I think it will be lots of fun.” Tom’s eyes lit up. “Let’s do it!” he said. So the two of them went to the library to use the computer. The modern computer showed them many amazing things, and Tom learned a lot. He was so happy and smiled happily at Lucy. “That was great!” he said. “Yes,” said Lucy. “Learning is really fun!”

Once upon a time, there was a yellow duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to play games with his friends. He was very competitive and always wanted to win. One day, Ducky and his friends decided to play hide and seek. Ducky was very good at hiding, so he thought he would win for sure. He found a big tree to hide behind, and waited for his friends to find him. Little did he know, his friends had a surprise planned for him. Ducky's friends found all the other hiding friends first. Then, they all quietly went to the big tree where Ducky was hiding. They jumped out and yelled, "Surprise!" Ducky was so surprised that he fell back into a soft pile of leaves. Everyone laughed and had fun. Even though Ducky didn't win the game, he learned that sometimes the best part of playing is the fun and surprises along the way.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a busy doctor. The doctor helped people feel better. The doctor had a big bag with all the things he needed. One day, a girl named Lily felt sick. She was in bed with a red face. Her mom called the busy doctor for help. The doctor came to Lily's house very fast. The doctor looked at Lily and put a cold cloth on her head. Then, he gave her some medicine to drink. Lily started to feel better. The busy doctor smiled and went to help more people. Everyone loved the busy doctor.
