Title: Victoria and the Fish

Once upon a time, Victoria the mermaid was living the best life in the deep blue sea. Every night she would swim around and soak up all the magical sights around her. One day, Victoria ran into a little fish called Stitch. Stitch told Victoria he was lost and needed help finding his way home. Without hesitation, Victoria said she would help him and Stitch was so relieved. Victoria said, "Let's go, Stitch!" They both began to swim and soon enough, Victoria found Stitch's home. Stitch cheered and thanked Victoria for her help. As a way to show her appreciation, Stitch spoiled Victoria with something special. Stitch opened a shell and inside was the most beautiful crystal Victoria had ever seen. Victoria was delighted and thanked Stitch for the amazing gift. She said, "I will never forget this! Thank you so much!" Stitch smiled and said goodbye to Victoria as she swam away, happy and excited to show off her new crystal.

Once upon a time, there was a small, modern town. In this town, there was a girl named Lucy. Lucy liked to play outside in the nice weather. But one day, the weather was not nice. The sky was dark and the wind was strong. Lucy frowned. She wanted to play outside. Her mom said, "Not today, Lucy. The weather is not good for playing." Lucy did not like this. She wanted to play with her friends. But she stayed inside and played with her toys. Later, Lucy saw the sun come out. She smiled and went outside to play. But the weather changed again. The wind blew harder and the rain started. Lucy got very wet and cold. She was sad and ran back inside. The bad weather had won this time.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tim. Tim was very tired, so he lay down to take a nap. He curled up in his soft, warm bed and closed his eyes. Suddenly, Tim heard a loud noise. It was the telephone! The sound made Tim jump and shake. He walked over to the telephone and picked it up with his mouth. "Hello?" said a voice on the other end. It was Tim's friend, the cat named Sam. "I want to play!" said Sam. Tim was still tired, but he wanted to play with his friend. So, Tim shook his tail and said, "Okay, let's play!" They played all day long and had lots of fun. When the sun went down, Tim and Sam were both very tired. They went back to their homes and took a long, cozy nap.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little mouse with a long tail. The little mouse loved to eat tasty cheese. One sunny day, the little mouse found a big piece of cheese under a tree. "Yum! This cheese looks so tasty," said the little mouse. He picked up the cheese with his tail and carried it to his small house. The little mouse was so happy. He wanted to eat the tasty cheese all day. But, something unexpected happened. The sun was so hot that the cheese started to melt. The little mouse was sad, but then a friendly bird flew by. The bird saw the melting cheese and said, "Don't be sad, little mouse. Let's share the tasty cheese!" The little mouse agreed, and they both ate the melted cheese. It was still very tasty. From that day on, the little mouse and the bird became best friends. They always shared their food and played together in the big green forest.

Once upon a time, there was a busy little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toys and count her colorful tapes. One day, she was in her room counting her tapes when her friend, Tim, came over. "Hi Amy!" said Tim. "What are you doing?" "Hi Tim! I'm counting my tapes. I have red, blue, and yellow ones. Do you want to help me?" Tim was happy to help Amy count her tapes. They sat on the floor and started counting together. "One, two, three," they counted. They had so much fun counting the tapes and talking about their favorite colors. After they finished counting, Amy and Tim decided to use the tapes to make a big picture on the floor. They used the red tape to make a big heart, the blue tape to make a big star, and the yellow tape to make a big sun. They were very proud of their picture and showed it to their moms. The moms were very proud of them too.
