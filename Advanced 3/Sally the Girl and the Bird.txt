Title: Sally the Girl and the Bird

One day, a little girl named Sally was very excited. She was going to the park with her mom. On the way, she saw a big tree. She wanted to poke the tree with a stick. Her mom said, "Be careful, Sally. Don't poke too hard." Sally poked the tree and a little bird flew out. The bird landed on Sally's head. It liked her hair. Sally laughed and said, "Hello, bird! Do you like my hair?" The bird chirped and flew away. Sally was happy. At the park, Sally saw a boy who had no hair. He looked sad. Sally went to the boy and said, "Don't be sad. Look, I can poke my hair!" The boy laughed and they played together. Sally learned that sharing her fun with others made her even more excited.

Once upon a time, there was a soft, fluffy bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to play outside in the sun. His favorite day was Sunday because he could play all day long. One Sunday, Bobo saw a big, red ball in the yard. He wanted to play with it, but he knew it belonged to his friend, Tommy. Bobo thought, "If I take the ball, I might get punished." But he wanted to play with it so much that he took it anyway. After playing with the ball for a while, Tommy came outside and saw Bobo with his ball. Tommy was not happy and told Bobo that he should not take things without asking. Bobo felt sad and said sorry to Tommy. They both decided to share the ball and play together. From that day on, Bobo always asked before using someone else's things, so he would not get punished again.

Tommy was walking to town. He had wanted to go to the park to play, but he was not allowed by himself. His parents had said he needed to stay home, so he was stuck walking around during sunny days. He walked with his eyes down, feeling helpless without his friends and parents. Until, he heard laughter coming from across the street. He looked up and saw a group of children playing in the park. They were so full of life and energy! Tommy decided to join them, so he crossed the street to get closer. He timidly asked them if he could play, but they just looked at each other and laughed. He was feeling more and more helpless, but he kept on asking them. Finally, one of the kids nodded and said, "Sure! You can join us!" Tommy smiled and the group headed to the swing sets. Tommy felt a huge sense of relief, he was no longer alone, and he felt safe and happy in the park. He was even walking around with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a healthy boy who loved to run and play outside. One sunny day, Tim went on a trip to the park with his mom. They were very excited to play together. At the park, Tim saw a squirrel and started to chase it. But, he tripped on a rock and fell down. "Ouch!" Tim cried. His mom ran to him and saw that he had a small cut on his knee. "Don't worry, Tim. It will heal soon," she said. Tim's mom put a band-aid on his knee and gave him a hug. "Now you can play again, but be careful," she told him. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" They played at the park all day, and Tim's knee started to heal. They had a great trip and went home happy.

Once there was a brilliant radish. This radish loved to move. Every day it would hop and bounce and jump around. It was so excited to explore everything! One day, the radish saw a pond. It looked so inviting, the water was very clear and still. The radish waved goodbye to the other plants and jumped right in! It swam around, loving the feeling of the cool water on its roots. Soon, the radish found a little fish swimming around. They looked at each other and said hello. The fish and the radish became fast friends and they decided to explore together. They darted around the pond and had so much fun. Finally, after a long day of swimming, the radish and the fish said goodbye, and the radish hopped out of the pond. As it walked back to its home, it knew that it had met a brilliant new friend.
