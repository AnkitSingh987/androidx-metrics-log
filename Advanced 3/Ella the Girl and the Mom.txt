Title: Ella the Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ella. She was eager to make something special for her mom. Ella thought and thought about what to make. Then, she had a great idea! She would make a pretty bag for her mom to carry things in. Ella found some soft cloth and cut it into two big pieces. She put the pieces together and sewed them with a needle and thread. She made a handle for the bag too. Ella was very happy with her work. Ella put the bag in a box and wrapped it with a big bow. She gave it to her mom with a big smile. Her mom was so happy and gave Ella a big hug. From that day on, Ella's mom used the bag all the time. Ella felt proud that she could make something so special for her mom.

One day, on a Wednesday, a smooth, big ball was rolling down a hill. It was going very fast. A little girl named Lily was playing near the hill. She saw the ball and wanted to stop it. She ran after it, but the ball was too fast for her. Lily's friend, Tom, saw her running. He wanted to help her. They both tried to stop the ball together. They jumped in front of the ball, but the ball just kept going. They were so tired. Then, a little bird saw them and wanted to help too. The bird flew up high and dropped a small stick in front of the ball. The ball hit the stick and stopped. Lily, Tom, and the bird were very happy. They didn't know that a small stick could stop a big ball. They all became good friends and played together every Wednesday.

One day, a hungry little ant named Andy went for a march. He wanted to find food to take back to his family. On his way, he found a big theater. He could hear music and laughter coming from inside. Andy got curious and wanted to see what was happening. As Andy entered the theater, he saw a big stage with many bugs dancing and singing. He met a kind ladybug named Lucy. Andy said, "I am hungry and looking for food. Can you help me?" Lucy smiled and said, "We are having a big party after the show. You can join us and eat with us." Andy watched the show and enjoyed it very much. After the show, he joined the party and ate a lot of food. Andy thanked Lucy and shared the food with his family. They were all very happy. Andy learned that being kind and asking for help can bring good things. The moral of the story is: Don't be afraid to ask for help, and always be kind to others.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little dog named Max. Max did not like the cold. When it was cold, Max would struggle to go outside. He would shake and shiver, but he did not want to wear his warm coat. Max's mom would try to put the coat on him, but Max would run away. One day, it was very cold outside. Max's mom said, "Max, you must wear your coat today!" Max did not want to wear the coat, but he wanted to go outside and play. Max struggled to put on the coat, but he did it. He was still a stubborn dog, but he knew he needed to wear the coat to play outside. When Max went outside, he saw his friend, a cat named Luna. Luna was wearing a warm coat too. Max and Luna played in the cold, but they did not feel cold because they wore their coats. Max learned that sometimes it is good to listen to his mom, even if he is a stubborn dog.

Once upon a time there lived an old chubby mouse. He lived a long and happy life but one day something strange happened. He took a deep breath and gasped for oxygen. All the other animals noticed his big breaths and wondered why he was breathing so hard. The mouse knew he was running out of oxygen and had a plan to save himself. He snuck into the nearby village and found a small red balloon. He tied it to his back and jumped into the air. As he flew he caught a breeze and soared into the sky. The mouse was so happy that he began to laugh. But soon he realized his balloon was shrinking. He looked down to see the chubby mouse was beginning to plummet. With a loud pop, the balloon burst which sent the mouse crashing to the ground. But luckily, the mouse had enough oxygen left in his balloon to save him! He landed safely back on the ground and everyone around cheered. The mouse learned a valuable lesson that day and was able to live another long and happy life.
