Title: The Story of the Hill and the Story

Once upon a time, there were two friends. They liked to play together very much. But one day, one of them was being very foolish. He was running around and jumping off a big hill. His friend said, "No! That's not a good idea!" But the foolish friend kept going. Suddenly, he fell and bumped his head. His friend was scared and sent for the ambulance. The ambulance came quickly and took the foolish friend to the hospital. The doctors made him better and he was very happy. The moral of the story is, don't be foolish! Listen to your friends and stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a toy box with a lock on it. Amy loved her toys very much. One day, she met a new friend named Ben. Ben was very flexible and could bend his body in many ways. "Look, Amy," said Ben, "I can touch my toes!" Amy was amazed and wanted to learn how to be flexible too. She thought maybe there was a toy in her toy box that could help her. The next day, Amy and Ben played together. Amy remembered the lock on her toy box. She knew there was a special toy inside that could help her be flexible like Ben. "Wait here," she told Ben, "I have a surprise for you!" Amy opened the toy box and found a stretchy toy snake inside. She showed it to Ben and said, "Look, this snake is flexible too! Let's play with it and learn how to be flexible together!" They played with the snake and practiced bending their bodies. Soon, Amy and Ben were both very flexible and they loved playing together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was a happy bird who loved to fly and collect things for his nest. His nest was in a big tree, where he could see the whole world. One day, Tim saw a beautiful purple flower. He thought it would be perfect for his nest. So, he flew down and picked the purple flower with his beak. Tim was very excited to take it back to his nest. When Tim put the purple flower in his nest, it made his home look so pretty. He was very proud of his collection. From that day on, Tim continued to collect more things to make his nest the most beautiful in the whole world. And he lived happily ever after.

Amy was playing in the garden one day when she saw something cold. She felt curious and went to have a closer look. She saw that it was a rub. It was a shiny silver rub with a handle. Amy wanted to take it home but it was heavy and she couldn't lift it. She tried tugging and pulling but it wouldn't move. She tried pushing it, but it was still too heavy. She was getting frustrated and started to cry. Just then, her mum came out and saw her crying. She put her arm around Amy and said, "I can help you with this, darling." She then bent down and picked up the rub with ease. She explained that the rub was too heavy for Amy to carry. Amy smiled as she watched her mum carry the rub into the house. She was so glad that her mum could help. From then on, Amy made sure to ask for help when something was too hard to do alone.

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Tom. Tom had a bright red hat. He liked to help people every day. One day, Tom saw a cat in a tree. The cat was scared. Tom wanted to help the cat. He got a long ladder to attach to the tree. He climbed up the ladder to save the cat. Tom reached the cat and held it tight. He climbed down the ladder with the cat. The cat was happy to be safe. Tom felt good for helping the cat. Everyone cheered for the brave fireman.

Once upon a time, there was a small house. In the house lived a kind family. There was a mommy, a daddy, and a little girl. The little girl's name was Lucy. They were very happy. One day, mommy had a baby. The baby was an infant. Lucy was so excited to welcome her new baby brother. She wanted to help mommy take care of him. Lucy touched her baby brother's skin. It was smooth and soft. She loved to hold him and give him hugs. Lucy, her mommy, daddy, and baby brother lived happily in their small house. They loved each other very much.
