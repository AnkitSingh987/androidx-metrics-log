Title: The Red House and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big red house. In the house, there was a big room with a fireplace. A little boy named Tom lived in the house. Tom loved to play near the fireplace. He liked to watch the fire dance and listen to the wood crack. One day, Tom was playing near the fireplace when he heard a noise. It was a loud noise that made him jump. Tom was scared. He looked around to see what made the noise. He saw a little red bird sitting on the floor. The bird looked scared too. Tom wanted to help the bird. He opened the window, and the bird flew out. But as the bird flew away, it dropped a shiny red stone. Tom picked up the stone and looked at it. It was very pretty. He showed the stone to his mom and dad. They were very happy and surprised. The red stone was magic! It made the fireplace always warm and safe. Tom and his family lived happily ever after with the magic red stone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a very special pen. This pen was the only original pen in the whole world. It could draw any picture Amy wanted. Amy loved her pen very much. One day, Amy's friend, Tom, came to play. He saw the original pen and thought it was very nice. He asked Amy if he could use the pen. Amy was scared that Tom might lose her pen. But Tom made a promise to Amy. He said, "I promise, I will not lose your pen. I will give it back when we are done." Amy let Tom use her original pen. They drew many fun pictures together. Tom kept his promise and gave the pen back to Amy when they were done. Amy was happy that she shared her pen with her friend. They had a great day and played together all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty turkey. This turkey liked to pull things. He pulled on the dog's tail. He pulled on the cat's whiskers. He even pulled on the little girl's hair. Everyone was sad because of the naughty turkey. One day, the naughty turkey saw a big red ball. He wanted to pull it. The little girl said, "No, turkey, don't pull the ball!" But the naughty turkey did not listen. He ran and pulled the ball very hard. The big red ball went up, up, up in the air. It hit a tree and got stuck. The little girl cried. The dog and the cat were sad too. The naughty turkey looked at them and then at the ball. He knew he did a bad thing. But now, the big red ball was gone forever. And everyone was unhappy because of the naughty turkey.

One day, a strong boy named Tom went for a walk. He saw a small house with a door. The door had a lock. Tom was curious and wanted to see what was inside. He tried to open the lock, but he could not. Tom went to his friend, Sue, and asked for help. Sue said, "I can help you open the lock, but we must be careful." They went back to the small house and Sue opened the lock. When the door opened, they saw a small flame inside. Tom and Sue were scared of the flame, but they knew they had to put it out. They found some water and threw it on the flame. The flame went away, and they were happy. They learned that it is important to be careful and not to open things that are locked. The moral of the story is that sometimes things are locked for a reason, and we should be careful not to disturb them.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who wanted to build a special mail box. She got lots of boxes, glue and tape and began to work. She was very careful and made sure all the boxes were stuck together really well. The mail box was very delicate and fragile. She decorated it with lots of pretty colours and sparkles. When it was finished she was so proud of it. The girl went to the post office and put her special box on the shelf. From then on, every day she would go to check if there was some mail for her. She was always so excited to open the letters and find surprises inside. And that's how the little girl built her very own special mail box!
