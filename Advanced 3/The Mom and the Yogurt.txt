Title: The Mom and the Yogurt

Once there was a mom who had three little kids. She took them to the playground where they could play and have fun. One day they all decided to go to the ice cream shop. The mom picked up three different flavors of yogurt. The first yogurt was red and it tasted sweet like strawberries. The second yogurt was blue and it had a yummy flavor like blueberry. The third yogurt was yellow and it had an ordinary flavor. The little kids tasted all three flavors and started to play. Then the mom realized that her little ones liked the ordinary flavor the best! They ate every last bit of their yogurt and then went back to the playground. They all had a great day filled with fun, laughter and delicious yogurt!

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there lived a spoiled hippo named Happy. Happy loved to play all day long, but he did not like to wait for anything. He always wanted things right away. One sunny day, Happy and his friends were playing a game. They had to wait their turn to play. Happy did not like this. He wanted to play now. So, he tried to delay the game by hiding the ball. He thought this would make his friends let him play first. But something unexpected happened. Happy's friends found the ball and they were not happy with him. They told Happy that he needed to learn to wait his turn. Happy felt bad for what he did and said sorry to his friends. From that day on, Happy learned to wait his turn and not be a spoiled hippo anymore. And they all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Tim. Tim was very sad and miserable. He did not like his life as a bug. He wanted to be something else. One day, Tim saw a big tree. He thought, "If I can snap a branch, maybe I will be happy." So, Tim tried to snap a small branch. He tried and tried, but he was too little. A kind bird saw Tim and asked, "Why are you so sad?" Tim told the bird about his life and how he wanted to snap a branch. The kind bird helped Tim snap the branch. They both laughed and played together. Tim learned that even though he was a bug, he could still have fun and be happy. And from that day on, Tim was not miserable anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a cat named Kit. Kit loved to stretch in the sun every day. One day, Kit saw a big tree with a toy at the top. Kit had an idea to get the toy. Kit tried to climb the tree but it was difficult. Kit's friend, a bird named Sky, saw him try. Sky wanted to help Kit. Sky flew up and got the toy for Kit. Kit was happy and said "thank you" to Sky. The two friends played with the toy together. They learned that when things are difficult, it is good to help each other. And when friends help each other, they can do anything.

One day, a kid named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, smooth slide that he wanted to play on. But there was a mean dog in front of the slide. Tim was sad and scared. Tim thought of a plan. He found a ball and threw it far away. The mean dog ran after the ball. Now, Tim could go to the slide. He was happy and excited. Tim climbed up and sat at the top of the smooth slide. As he started to slide down, he saw the mean dog coming back. But, the dog was not mean anymore. The dog was wagging its tail and wanted to play. Tim and the dog became friends and played together at the park.

Once upon a time, there lived a mighty little bug. He had two big feet, perfect for crawling, and loved exploring the swamp nearby his home. Every day the little bug would inch ever closer to the heart of the swamp, growing more and more curious about what it held. One day, the little bug was floating across the calm water when he heard a loud rumbling sound. Growing scared, he started to turn around and go home. But before he could, he saw two eyes staring straight at him. It was a great monster living in the center of the swamp. He looked so big and mighty compared to the little bug, but the little bug wasn't scared. Thinking fast, it waved hello, and the monster waved back. The little bug was surprised, and decided to pay a few visits to the monster. On their visits the little bug learned so much; they shared stories, laughed and sang songs. Eventually, the little bug realized how important it was to be brave when meeting new things. So the next time the little bug ventured into the swamp, he had no fear. He knew that when faced with something new, the courage inside of him would never fail!
