Title: Lily and Sam and the Ball

Lily and Sam were playing in the living room. They liked to look at the big tree with many lights and shiny things. Mommy said they were ornaments and they were very pretty. "Can I touch one?" Sam asked Lily. He saw a red ball that looked like a candy. "No, no, no!" Lily said. "Mommy said we can't touch them. They can break and hurt us." "But I want to see it closer," Sam said. He reached for the red ball. Lily tried to stop him, but it was too late. Sam pulled the red ball and it fell to the floor. It broke into many pieces with a loud noise. "Oh no!" Lily and Sam said together. They were scared and sad. They heard Mommy coming. "What happened here?" Mommy asked. She saw the broken ornament and the children's faces. "We're sorry, Mommy," Lily and Sam said. "We didn't mean to break it." Mommy sighed. She was not happy, but she was not angry either. She knew they were curious and didn't know better. "It's okay, my loves," Mommy said. She hugged them and kissed them. "I'm glad you're not hurt. But you have to be careful with the ornaments. They are not toys. They are for looking, not touching." Lily and Sam nodded. They said they were sorry again. Mommy smiled and said she loved them. She helped them clean up the mess and put a new ornament on the tree. It was a friendly star that sparkled. Lily and Sam liked it. They learned their lesson and never touched the ornaments again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Penny. Penny loved to collect things and make neat stacks with her stuff. One day, Penny found a very special box outside. She opened it and found lots of colourful stones inside. She was so excited! Penny carefully collected the stones and prepared a stack of them in the box. Suddenly, a monster appeared! He was very rude and he grabbed the box away from Penny. Penny shouted at him and told him to give it back. The monster was still very rude and he wouldn't listen to Penny. He kept the box and he ran away with it. Penny was so mad! She tried to chase the monster but she was too slow. Then, she had an idea. Penny went back to her house and prepared a big stack of her own things. She made it look like the box full of stones. The monster came back and he was so confused. He grabbed the stack thinking it was his box, and he ran away! Penny cheered with joy! She had saved her box, and the monster was never seen again.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous jellyfish. It lived in the deep blue sea with many fish friends. The jellyfish loved to swim and play with them all day long. One day, the jellyfish saw that the sea was not clean. There was trash everywhere. The fish friends were sad and did not want to swim and play anymore. The enormous jellyfish wanted to help make the sea clean again. So, the jellyfish and the fish friends worked together to clean the sea. They picked up all the trash and put it away. After a long day, the sea was clean again. The enormous jellyfish and the fish friends were happy. They swam and played in the clean sea all day long.

One day, a little cat named Kitty was playing outside. She saw big, gray clouds in the sky. Kitty felt a drop of water on her head. It started to pour! She ran under a tree to hide from the rain. While Kitty was under the tree, she saw a bright lightning in the sky. It made a loud noise! Kitty was scared and wanted to go home. She ran as fast as she could to her house. When Kitty got home, her fur was all wet. She felt ashamed that she was scared of the lightning. Her mom gave her a warm hug and told her it was okay to be scared. They sat inside and watched the rain together.
