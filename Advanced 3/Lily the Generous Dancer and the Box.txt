Title: Lily the Generous Dancer and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a generous dancer named Lily. She loved to dance and play with her friends. One day, Lily found a big box in her room. She thought, "I can put my toys in this box." Later, Lily went to the park with her friends. She told them, "I found a big box. I will put my toys in it." Her friends were excited and wanted to see the box too. They all went to Lily's house to look at the box. When they got to Lily's house, they saw the big box. Lily and her friends put all their toys in the box. They played and danced together all day. At the end of the day, they found out that the big box was a magic box. All the toys they put inside became new and shiny. Lily and her friends were very happy and played with their new toys every day.

Once upon a time, three friends were excitedly preparing to start a race. One was a bear, one was a cat and one was a bee. The three friends lined up at the start line. The bear was eager to win the race. “I’m going to be the first one to cross the finish line,” he declared. The cat and bee laughed. “We’ll see about that!” the cat said with enthusiasm. The race began and the three friends sprinted as fast as they could. As the bear got closer and closer to the finish line, the cat and bee shouted for him to surrender. The bear kept running and despite the cat and bee’s enthusiasm, he crossed the finish line first. He had won the race! The cat and bee hugged the bear. “Good job, you won the race!” they said with a smile.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog. The dog liked to poke things with his nose. One day, the dog went for a walk to the west. He saw a big, hard rock. The dog thought it would be fun to poke the rock. When he poked the rock, something unexpected happened. The rock started to move! The dog was very surprised. He poked the rock again, and it moved some more. The dog was happy that he found a new friend to play with. But then, the rock changed. It turned into a big, friendly bear! The bear had been sleeping, and the dog's poke woke him up. The dog and the bear became best friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Mia. She liked to travel around the world and see different places. One day Mia decided to make a film of her travels. She took her camera and started planning her journey. Mia went everywhere, including the beach and the mountains. Everywhere she looked, she saw something new and exciting. On the last day of her trip, Mia was packing her suitcase and the house was very messy. Her mum saw this and said, "Mia, please tidy up before you go." Mia smiled and said, "Yes mum, I will". Then she finished packing her suitcase and was ready to go. Mia had a wonderful time taking photos and filming her trip. When she got back home, she was very excited to share her film with her family. Everyone loved it and were amazed at all the places Mia had visited. Mia was happy that she could make such a wonderful film, but she enjoyed her trips even more!

Sam was out on a beach with his mom, looking for seashells. Sam noticed a weird looking rock on the shore next to them. "Mom, what's that?" Sam asked, pointing at it. "That's a reef," his Mom smiled. Sam remembered learning about reefs in his science class. "So, a reef is a special kind of rock?" His Mom nodded. "That's right. Reefs are very spicy, so keep your distance." Sam chuckled, then remembered what he had been doing before he noticed the reef. He started looking for shells again. He found a pretty one and smiled. "Mom, look what I found!" Sam said. He held the shell up and showed it to her. "That's beautiful," his Mom said. Sam smiled, then ran down the beach to look for more shells.
