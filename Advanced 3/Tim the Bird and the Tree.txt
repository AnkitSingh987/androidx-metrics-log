Title: Tim the Bird and the Tree

One day, a small bird named Tim was flying in the sky. He saw a big tree with many red berries on it. He thought, "I want to eat those berries. They look very yummy!" He flew down to the tree to eat the berries. When Tim was eating the berries, a big cat named Max was watching him. Max thought, "I want to catch that small bird." Max hid behind the tree and waited for something to happen. Tim saw Max hiding and thought, "I need to be careful." He said to Max, "You can't catch me!" Tim flew up high in the sky. Max tried to jump and catch him, but he was too slow. Tim was safe and happy. Tim flew away to find more berries. He knew that he had to be careful and watch out for cats like Max. But he also knew that he was too fast for them. Tim was a smart bird, and he always stayed safe.

One day, Mandy was very scared. She heard the thunder outside and she was really scared. Mandy's mommy came over to her and said: "It's okay Mandy, there's nothing to be afraid of. Just trust me". Mandy felt better because she trusted her mommy. They hugged for a long time and it was so warm. Mandy felt safe and then the thunder stopped. Mandy's mommy said: "See, it's all gone now. You can trust me, it'll be okay". Mandy smiled and said: "Thank you mommy, I feel much better now". Mandy and her mommy hugged again and it was so warm. Mandy knew that she could always trust her mommy, no matter what.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl called Milly who lived in a far away, distant village. Every Sunday, she liked to sing in the sunshine. One day, when Milly was walking down the village street, she heard another voice singing. She stopped to listen. The voice sounded old, but it was still beautiful. Milly followed the sound until she reached a big house. At the bottom of its garden, she found a very old lady. The lady smiled at Milly and said: "Hello little one, why are you here?" Milly said: "I heard your voice and I wanted to see who was singing." The old lady smiled again and said: "It's me, I love to sing! Words come to life when I sing them and make me feel better. Join me if you like." Milly beamed and joined the old lady in song. As they sang together, Milly forgot all about the distant village and felt happy and connected.

Once there was a girl who was very delicate. She had a beautiful comb that she loved because it made her red hair look so nice. Every day she would run her fingers through it, feeling the smoothness and life of the handle. One day she was brushing her hair, when the comb got so hot it started to burn! She dropped it in pain and ran away, afraid of the heat. But later that night, she heard a soft knock on her window. She opened it to find a man who had come with a new, delicate comb. He said, "I thought you might need this. It is specially made and won't get too hot." The girl was so happy. Every day she would use the new comb and it never got hot. The man had been so kind, and she could always feel the delicate care he put into making the comb. From that day forward, the girl remembered to be gentle with the delicate things in life.

Once there was a mule named Max. He loved to eat spicy food. One day, he found a big red pepper in the field. He was very happy and wanted to eat it right away. Max said to his friend, the cow, "Look at this spicy pepper I found! I want to eat it!" The cow said, "Be careful, Max. It might be too spicy for you." But Max didn't listen. He took a big bite of the red pepper. Soon, Max started to suffer. His mouth was very hot and he didn't like it. He ran to the river to drink some water. The water helped and he felt better. Max learned that it's good to listen to friends. He knew that next time he would be more careful with spicy food.
