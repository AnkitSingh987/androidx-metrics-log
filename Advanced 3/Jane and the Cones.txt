Title: Jane and the Cones

Once upon a time, there was a helpful little girl called Jane. She wanted to do something nice for her friends so she decided to make ice cream cones. So Jane went outside and gathered some cones. She was very helpful and quick. Then she went to get some ice cream, sugar and sprinkles. She mixed them together and put them into the cones. When she was ready, she went to her friends house. They were all so excited to see the cones. Jane's friend said, "Thank you for being so helpful Jane". Jane smiled and said, "you're welcome. Let's all strike our cones to make a toast". Everyone did as Jane said and they all enjoyed their ice cream cones. It made Jane super happy to see her friends so happy.

Tim and Anna are twins. They go to school every day. They wear the same uniform: a blue shirt and a red skirt or pants. They like their uniform. It makes them look neat and smart. One day, their mom says they can choose a new uniform. She shows them a catalog with many colors and styles. Tim and Anna are excited. They look at the catalog and point at what they like. "I want a green shirt and a yellow skirt!" Anna says. "I want a purple shirt and a black pants!" Tim says. Their mom smiles. She says, "Those are very bright and fun colors. But are they polite for school?" Tim and Anna are confused. They do not know what polite means. They think it means nice or good. "Yes, they are polite!" they say together. Their mom shakes her head. She says, "Polite means you follow the rules and respect others. The school has a rule that you wear blue and red. If you wear different colors, you might make the teacher and the other kids unhappy. They might think you are rude or silly." Tim and Anna do not want to make anyone unhappy. They do not want to be rude or silly. They want to be polite. Their mom suggests, "Why don't you choose a different uniform for the weekend? You can wear any color you like on Saturday and Sunday. But for school, you wear blue and red. Is that okay?" Tim and Anna think about it. They like the idea of having a weekend uniform. They nod and say, "Okay, mom. That sounds good." Their mom hugs them. She says, "You are very smart and kind twins. I am proud of you."

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a shiny jewel on the ground. It was red and sparkled in the sun. Lily and Ben both wanted the jewel, so they started to fight over it. "Give me the jewel, it is mine!" Lily shouted. "No, it is mine, I saw it first!" Ben yelled. They pulled and tugged at the jewel, but it was too hard to break. They got angry and sad, and forgot to have fun. A lady who was walking her dog saw them and came over. She smiled and said, "Hello, children. Why are you fighting over this jewel?" Lily and Ben stopped and looked at her. They felt ashamed and said, "We both want it, it is so pretty." The lady nodded and said, "I understand, but do you know what this jewel is? It is not a real jewel, it is a candy. You can eat it, but only if you share it." She took the jewel and broke it in half. She gave one half to Lily and one half to Ben. They looked at the candy and saw that it was dry and hard. It did not taste very good, but they were polite and said, "Thank you, lady." The lady said, "You are welcome, children. Next time, remember to be kind and generous. Sharing is better than fighting, and real jewels are not always what they seem." Lily and Ben nodded and said, "We are sorry, lady. We will share and be nice to each other." They hugged and said sorry to each other. Then they threw away the candy and went to play on the swings. They learned their lesson and had a happy day.
