Title: Tilly and the Mountain

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Tilly and Jack. They were both very restless and loved to play together in the fields. One day, Tilly had an idea. She wanted to find a great truth. But how could they do that? Then Jack had an idea. He said, "Let's go on an adventure and explore the world for the great truth!" Tilly was so excited about this that she jumped up and down. So they set out on their adventure. They visited every corner of the world together and asked many people, but no one could give them the great truth. But they kept searching and searching. Finally they reached the top of a tall mountain. There, they asked a wise old man. He said, "The truth can only be found when you search inside your heart." So Tilly and Jack sat still and quiet, and searched inside their hearts until they found the great truth they had been looking for. Then they smiled and hugged each other, happy that they had found the truth.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a big, pink eraser. It was a very good eraser. Lily liked to draw with her eraser. It was always reliable and could fix any mistake. One day, Lily went to the park. She took her eraser with her. She drew pictures in the sand with her eraser. She drew a cat, a dog, and a big tree. Lily was very happy. But then, it was time to leave the park. Lily went home and forgot her eraser at the park. She was sad. The next day, she went back to the park. She found her eraser in the sand. Lily was happy again. She never left her eraser again.

Once upon a time, there was an old tree. The tree was very old and had started to rot. One day, a little girl named Lily was playing near the tree. She saw a shiny diamond stuck in the tree. Lily tried to get the diamond out, but it was stuck very tight. A little squirrel came by and saw Lily. The squirrel said, "What are you doing?" Lily said, "I'm trying to get this shiny diamond out of the old tree." The squirrel wanted to help Lily. They both tried to get the diamond out of the tree. With a little push and pull, the diamond came out. Lily was happy and thanked the squirrel. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to draw pictures on paper. One day, she drew a big, scary monster. It had sharp teeth and big eyes. Amy showed her mom the picture and said, "Look, Mom! I made a scary monster!" Amy's mom smiled and said, "That's a great picture, Amy! But be careful not to spoil it." Amy nodded and went to her room to put the picture away. But as she walked, she tripped and fell. The paper with the scary monster flew out of her hands and landed on the floor. Suddenly, the scary monster came to life! It jumped off the paper and started to dance around the room. Amy was scared at first, but then she saw the monster was not mean. It just wanted to play. So, Amy and the scary monster played together all day. They had so much fun, and Amy learned that even scary monsters can be good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big building. Inside the building, many people worked and played. One day, a little girl named Mia came to the building with her mom. Mia was a very careful girl. She always held her mom's hand when they walked together. Today, Mia saw a big red ball in the building. She wanted to play with it. Mia asked her mom, "Can I play with the ball, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Mia was so happy. She ran to get the ball. But then, a big boy named Tom came and took the ball away. He said, "This is my ball!" Mia was sad and she wanted the ball back. She told Tom, "Give me the ball, please!" Tom did not want to give the ball back. But Mia was brave. She demanded, "Give me the ball now!" Tom saw that Mia was strong, so he gave her the ball. Mia was happy and played with the ball. She was careful not to hurt anyone. In the end, Mia and Tom became friends and played together.
