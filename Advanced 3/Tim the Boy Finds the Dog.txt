Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Dog

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big bed that he loved to sleep in every night. He also had a small dog named Max. Max was very fair and kind to Tim. They played together every day. One day, Tim decided to tease Max. He would take Max's toy and run away with it. Max did not like this game. He felt sad and did not want to play anymore. Tim saw that Max was sad and stopped teasing him. Tim learned that it was not nice to tease his friends. He said sorry to Max and they played a new game together. They had lots of fun and were happy again. From that day on, Tim was always fair and kind to Max. They became the best of friends and had many more happy days together.

Once upon a time, in a busy little farm, there lived a cow named Bessie. Bessie was a very happy cow. She had many friends like the dog, cat, and chicken. They all played together in the farm. One day, the farmer mentioned that he needed to go away for a short time. He told the animals to take care of the farm. Bessie and her friends were nervous, but they said they would do their best. While the farmer was gone, a big wind came and blew the farm gate open. Bessie and her friends were scared. They knew they had to close the gate to keep the farm safe. They all worked together, pushing and pulling until the gate was closed. They were tired but happy that they had saved the farm. When the farmer came back, he was proud of his busy little helpers.

Once upon a time, there was a big head named Bob. Bob was very creative. He loved to draw, paint, and make things. One day, he had a great idea. He wanted to unite all his friends and make something special together. Bob told his friends, "Let's make a big, beautiful picture!" All his friends were excited. They got paper, crayons, and paint. Bob knew this would be fun, but he had a secret plan too. They all started to draw and paint together. Bob's plan was to make the picture even more special. He got some glue and put it on the paper. Then, he put all the little pictures together. Now, the big picture was done. Bob showed the big picture to his friends. They were so happy! They didn't know Bob's secret plan, but it made their picture even better. The friends were united and had lots of fun being creative together. From that day on, Bob and his friends always worked together. They made many beautiful things. And they learned that when they unite, they can do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat had a big anchor. The boat liked to keep the anchor with it all the time. The anchor made the boat feel safe. One day, the boat felt uncomfortable. It wanted to play with the other boats. But the anchor was too heavy. The boat could not move fast. The other boats went away. The boat was sad. It wanted to keep the anchor and play with the other boats. The boat had an idea. It asked a friend for help. The friend helped the boat lift the anchor. Now, the boat was not uncomfortable. It could play with the other boats and keep the anchor too. The boat was happy.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a nosy squirrel. This squirrel loved to search for nuts. He would run up and down the tree all day, looking for the best nuts to eat. One sunny day, the nosy squirrel saw a big, shiny nut on the ground. He ran down the tree and picked up the nut. He was very happy, because he had never seen such a big nut before. But, the nosy squirrel did not want to share his big nut with his friends. So, he searched for a hiding place. He looked under a rock, behind a bush, and even inside a log. At last, he found the perfect spot and hid his nut. Later, the nosy squirrel's friends came to play. They saw him hiding the nut and asked if they could share. The nosy squirrel thought about it and decided to share his big nut with his friends. From that day on, the nosy squirrel and his friends searched for nuts together. They had lots of fun and learned that sharing made them all very happy.
