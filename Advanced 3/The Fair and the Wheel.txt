Title: The Fair and the Wheel

Once upon a time there was a fair. John and his family were ready to have some fun. John said to his parents, “Let’s go to the tent!” His parents replied, “Okay, let’s do it.” John was very excited and ran ahead. When he arrived, he discovered that the tent was full of games. He saw a big wheel and happy faces everywhere. He noticed a big sign saying “Examine the Tent” and he decided to take a closer look. He examined the tent very carefully. He noticed a tall slide, lots of colorful balls, and a bunch of toys. He was very excited and couldn’t believe what he was seeing. Suddenly his mom called him and said, “Come here John! Let’s go and try some of the games.” John and his family started to play all sorts of games. He won some prizes and everybody had a fair time. In the end, it was a very happy day for John and his family.

One day, Tom and Lily went to play on the hill. They liked to roll down the hill and feel the wind in their hair. The hill was mild, not too steep and not too flat. It was fun to play on the hill. But then, they saw a big dog on the hill. The dog was barking and running towards them. Tom and Lily were scared. They did not know if the dog was friendly or mean. They did not want to get bitten by the dog. "Run, Lily, run!" Tom shouted. He grabbed Lily's hand and they ran down the hill as fast as they could. The dog chased them, but they were faster. They reached the bottom of the hill and ran to their mom, who was waiting for them. "Mom, mom, there is a big dog on the hill!" Tom said. "He was barking and running at us. We were scared." Mom hugged them and said, "I'm sorry, Tom and Lily. That was not nice of the dog. He should not have scared you. But maybe he was just lonely and wanted to play. Maybe he did not know how to be gentle and polite. Maybe he needed a friend." Tom and Lily thought about what mom said. They felt sorry for the dog. Maybe he was not mean, just misunderstood. Maybe they could be his friend, if he learned to be nice. "Mom, can we go back to the hill and try to play with the dog?" Lily asked. "Maybe we can teach him to be gentle and polite. Maybe we can make him happy." Mom smiled and said, "That is very kind of you, Lily. But you have to be careful. Not all dogs are friendly. You have to ask the owner first, if there is one. And you have to stay close to me. I will go with you and see if the dog is safe to play with. Maybe we can happen to make a new friend today." Tom and Lily nodded and said, "Okay, mom. Let's go back to the hill and see what happens." The moral value of the story is to be kind and compassionate to others, even if they seem scary or different. The story also teaches to be careful and respectful around animals, and to ask for permission before playing with them. The story uses the verb "happen", the noun "hill" and the adjective "mild" in simple sentences. The story contains one dialogue between Tom, Lily and mom. The story has some form of conflict in the form of the dog chasing them.

One day, a little boy named Tim went with his mom to the station. They had to carry a big, heavy bag. Tim felt uncomfortable because the bag was too big for him. At the station, a kind old man saw them. He said, "I can help you carry the bag." Tim's mom said, "Thank you!" So the old man helped them carry the bag to the train. But when they opened the bag, they found a lot of balloons inside! The balloons flew up into the sky. Everyone at the station laughed and clapped. The bag was not heavy anymore, and Tim felt comfortable. The old man smiled and said, "What a fun surprise!" And they all had a great time at the station.
