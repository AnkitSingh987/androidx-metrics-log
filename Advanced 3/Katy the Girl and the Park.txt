Title: Katy the Girl and the Park

Once, there was a little girl named Katy. She was three and very curious. Today, Katy was walking in the park with her mother when she spotted something really exciting. "Mummy, look over there," Katy squealed with delight. Her mother looked and saw a big, shiny ice-cream truck. "Let's go get some tasty ice-cream!" Katy's mother said. Katy couldn't believe her eyes. She pointed to the truck and ran excitedly towards it. When they reached the truck, Katy and her mother saw there were lots of different flavours to choose from. Katy's mother asked Katy to point to her favourite flavour. She pointed to the strawberry ice-cream. "Oh, that looks yummy!" Her mother said. Katy was so excited. She watched eagerly as her mother scooped the strawberry ice-cream into a cone. When they got back to the park, Katy started eating her ice-cream. She licked it round and round, using her tongue and her special muscles. It was so tasty!

Peter was a 3-year-old boy who loved to play. One day, he was looking for something to do and he found a smooth case in his room. He opened the case and found a lot of toys inside. He was so excited and took out one toy after another. Peter smiled and said, “Mommy, I need these toys! They are so much fun!” His mom answered, "Of course Peter, you can keep the toys. But, you need to take good care of them. Put them in the case, so they will stay safe.” Peter nodded and carefully put the toys back in the case. He closed it and smiled. He was so proud of himself for taking good care of his toys. He said, “Mommy I did it! Now I have a safe place to keep my toys” His mom smiled and said, “That's great Peter! You did a great job! Now you can play with them whenever you want.”

Bob was a 3 year old boy who liked to explore the world around him. One day, he was walking along looking at the trees and the birds when he heard a sound. He stopped and listened carefully. The noise was coming from a sock! Bob was very curious and he bent over and picked up the sock. It was cold and he shivered. He shook it next to his ear and heard some rattling noises. He smiled and put the sock in his pocket. Then he saw his friend, Jack, walking along the path. Jack saw Bob and ran over saying, “Bob, have you seen my sock? I can’t find it anywhere." Bob pulled out his pocket and said, “Listen, Jack. I’ve found your sock!” He handed the sock over to his friend and they both laughed. Jack thanked Bob and as they both walked away, Bob listened to the birds chirping in the trees.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy old cat. The cat lived on a big hill. From the hill, the cat had a nice view of the town below. One day, a happy dog came to the town. The dog liked to sing and dance. The people in the town liked the dog. They would clap their hands and applaud when the dog sang and danced. The grumpy cat saw the happy dog and the people. The cat felt a little less grumpy. The cat went down the hill to join the fun. The dog and the cat became friends. They sang and danced together. The people in the town applauded. Everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a funny dog named Max. Max liked to play with his ball and make people laugh. One day, Max met a new friend, a cat named Lucy. Max said to Lucy, "Let's play and have fun!" Lucy agreed, but she said, "We must behave well when we play." Max nodded and they began to play with the ball. They were having a good time, but Max forgot what Lucy said about behaving well. Max started to run and jump all over the place. He did not behave well. He knocked over a big vase and it broke into pieces. Lucy was sad and said, "Max, you did not behave well. Now, we are in trouble." Max felt bad for not listening to Lucy. He knew he should have behaved well. In the end, Max had to go home and could not play with Lucy anymore.
