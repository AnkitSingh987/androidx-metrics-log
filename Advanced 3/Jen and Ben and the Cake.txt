Title: Jen and Ben and the Cake

Jen and Ben are twins. They like to play and have fun. One day, they see a big basket of strawberries in the kitchen. They love strawberries. They want to eat them all. But Mom says, "No, you cannot eat them all. They are for the cake. I need them for the cake. You can have one each, and then you have to wait." Jen and Ben are sad. They want more strawberries. They think of a plan. They are creative. They sneak into the kitchen when Mom is not looking. They take some strawberries from the basket. They hide them under their shirts. They run to their room. They are happy. They have more strawberries. They eat them fast. They are sweet and juicy. They smile and giggle. But Mom sees them. She sees the red stains on their shirts. She sees the empty spaces in the basket. She is angry. She says, "Jen and Ben, you are naughty. You took the strawberries. You did not listen to me. You spoiled the cake. You have to say sorry. And you have to help me make a new cake." Jen and Ben are sorry. They say sorry to Mom. They help her make a new cake. They learn to share and to follow the rules. They still love strawberries. But they love Mom more.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a selfish cat named Tom. Tom was very good at singing. He loved to sing opera all day long. His friends, the birds, did not like his singing. They wanted to sing with Tom, but he never let them. One day, Tom found a big, shiny bell. He thought it would be fun to attach the bell to his tail. He believed it would make his opera singing even better. So, he tied the bell to his tail and started to sing. The bell rang loudly every time Tom moved. As Tom sang, something unexpected happened. His bird friends heard the bell and flew over to see what was happening. They saw Tom with the bell attached to his tail and thought it was a game. They started to sing along with Tom, and the sound of their voices and the bell made beautiful music. Tom realized that he was wrong to be selfish. He learned that when he shared his opera singing with his friends, they could all make beautiful music together. From that day on, Tom and his bird friends sang opera together, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Sam. Sam loved to eat sausages. One day, Sam found a big sausage on the ground. He was very happy and wanted to eat it. But the big sausage was broken. Sam was sad. He wanted to fix the sausage. So, Sam went to his friend, a little cat named Lily. Lily was good at fixing things. Sam asked Lily, "Can you help me fix my big sausage?" Lily said, "Yes, I can help you." So, Lily and Sam worked together to fix the big sausage. They used a soft cloth and some glue. Soon, the big sausage was fixed. Sam was very happy. He thanked Lily for helping him. Sam and Lily sat down and shared the big sausage. They ate it all up. It was very yummy. From that day on, Sam and Lily were the best of friends. They played together every day and always helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little bunny named Bobo. Bobo had a favorite blanket that he took everywhere. One day, Bobo saw a small hole in his blanket. He was very sad and wanted to fix it. Bobo went to his mommy and asked for help. Mommy said, "We can fix it with a stitch, Bobo!" Bobo was happy and wanted to learn how to stitch. Mommy taught Bobo how to make a stitch, and together, they fixed the hole in the blanket. The moral of the story is that when we want to fix something, we can ask for help and learn new things. Just like Bobo, we can be brave and try to learn how to do things we have never done before.
