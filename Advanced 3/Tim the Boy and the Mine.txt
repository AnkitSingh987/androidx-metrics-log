Title: Tim the Boy and the Mine

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy mine that he loved to play with. One day, he saw a mild dog in his yard. Tim wanted the dog to be his friend, so he tried to make the dog accept his toy mine. But the dog did not want to play with the mine. The dog just looked at Tim and walked away. Tim was sad because the dog did not accept his toy mine. He sat down and cried. The mild dog never came back to play with Tim and his toy mine. Tim was sad and lonely, and he never found a friend to play with his toy mine.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the park. She saw her friend Tom. Tom was sad. Sally asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "I did not watch the lecture. I feel guilty." Sally had an idea. "Let's find the lecture and watch it," she said. Tom smiled. They looked and looked for the lecture. They saw a big tree. They saw a big rock. They did not see the lecture. Then, something unexpected happened. A squirrel ran by with a paper. Sally and Tom chased the squirrel. The squirrel dropped the paper. It was the lecture! Tom felt happy, not guilty. Sally and Tom watched the lecture together. They learned a lot and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Tom. Tom had a big house with many rooms. In one room, he had a big calendar on the wall. The calendar had many colors and pictures of animals. One day, Tom wanted to put a new picture on the calendar. He found a pretty picture of a cat. He took the old picture off the calendar and put the new picture on it. The cat was very cute, and Tom liked it a lot. Tom showed the new calendar to his friend, Sue. Sue thought the cat was cute too. They both liked the calendar very much. They looked at the calendar every day and smiled. They were very happy with the new picture.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside in her big yard. One day, she saw a pretty bird. The bird had deep blue feathers and a long tail. Mia wanted to show the bird to her mom. Mia ran inside her house and told her mom about the bird. Her mom gave her a shiny ornament to show the bird. The ornament was round and sparkly, like a little sun. Mia was very happy and went back outside to find the bird. Mia found the bird near a tree. She showed the bird the ornament. The bird liked the ornament and started to sing a sweet song. Mia danced and laughed with the bird. They played together all day, and Mia knew she had made a new friend.

Once there was a boy named Will. He lived on the corner of his street and each day he looked out of his window waiting for a taxi to take him to school. He believed that one day the taxi would be there, but it never was. One day, Will was feeling brave and decided to go outside and find the taxi himself. He searched, but he couldn't find it. He was getting tired and he started to cry, but he kept looking. Eventually, he found a soft, fuzzy taxi but it wasn't what he had been expecting. It was not a real taxi, just a toy! Will was so disappointed that he stopped believing that a real taxi would ever come. He stayed outside, playing with the toy taxi until it was too dark to see anything. He had to go inside and sadly he realised that he would never see a real taxi. He had lost his chance and he would never ride in a real taxi.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went outside to play in the cold. The cold was bitter, but Tim wore a big coat to keep warm. He saw a big snowman in the park. Tim wanted to take a picture with the snowman. Tim asked the snowman, "Can I take a picture with you?" The snowman did not talk but smiled big. Tim took a picture with the snowman. They both looked happy in the picture. After taking the picture, Tim went back to his house. He showed the picture to his mom. His mom said, "Wow, Tim! That's a great picture with the snowman!" Tim smiled and felt warm, even though it was cold outside.
