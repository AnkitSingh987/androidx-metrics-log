Title: The Bell and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there was a big bell. The bell was very special. It could help people when they were in trouble. All they had to do was raise their hand and ring the bell. One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the forest. He saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to climb. But when he climbed up, he got stuck. Tim was scared and remembered the big bell. He raised his hand and rang the bell for help. A big, strong bear heard the bell and came to help Tim. But the bear was not nice. He took Tim away and did not help him get down from the tree. Tim was very sad and wished he had not climbed the big tree. The moral of the story is to be careful when you play and not to trust everyone who comes to help you.

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo. The hippo was very restless. He could not sit still. He would walk, jump, and run all day. The other animals were not happy. They wanted the hippo to be calm. One day, the hippo thought, "I need to find a way to be less restless." He saw a wise old owl in a tree. The hippo asked the owl for help. The owl told the hippo to try sitting by the river and watch the water flow. The hippo did what the owl said. He sat by the river and watched the water. He felt calm and peaceful. From that day on, the hippo would go to the river when he felt restless. He learned to be calm and still. The other animals were happy, too. They all played together and had fun. The moral of the story is to find a way to be calm when you feel restless.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy girl named Lily. She loved to play in her classroom with her friends. One day, Lily saw a big box in the classroom. "Let's push the box together," said Lily to her friends. They all pushed the box and found a lot of toys inside. They were so happy and started to play with the toys. But Lily wanted to play with all the toys. She said, "I want to play with all of them first!" Her friends did not like that. They said, "Lily, you are being bossy. We should all share the toys." Lily realized she was wrong and said sorry to her friends. They all played together and had a great time in the classroom.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Suzie. She wanted to be a scientist when she grew up, so she decided to study different things. One day, she found a nail and wanted to learn about it, so she decided to study it. She studied the color and shape of the nail and found it was dark and sharp. Then she studied it carefully and found out it was very fragile. She was surprised and said, “What a fragile nail!” Her mother, who was watching nearby, said, “Honey, it’s important to always study something before you touch it. Some things can be very fragile.” Suzie nodded, understanding the lesson. She put the nail back in its spot and continued to study other things.

Sara and her mom were out for a walk when suddenly it started to snow. Sara was amazed and she shouted, “Mommy, it’s snowing! Look!” Her mom smiled and said, “Yes, Sara. Isn’t it beautiful?” The snowflakes were light and fluffy, and they fell around her like feathers. Sara thought it was the most magical thing ever. She opened her mouth to catch a snowflake and felt it freeze on her tongue. But then something strange happened. As Sara looked up at the sky, she noticed a strange dull image stuck in the air. It was hard and frozen like an ice cube and it didn’t move at all. She asked, “Mommy, what is that?” Her mom smiled and said, “That’s an icicle, Sara. It’s formed when the snowflakes freeze and turn into ice.” Sara was fascinated. She felt like she had just seen a secret image that only she could see. She smiled and looked around, hoping to find more icicles.
