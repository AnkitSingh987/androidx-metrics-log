Title: Everyday and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a girl who had a pet bunny. Everyday she made sure to look after her pet carefully and protect him. The bunny always followed the girl, hopping along at her side. One day, when the bunny and the girl were out walking in the park, they came across a tall, polite dog. The bunny was a bit scared, so the girl made sure to stay close and protect him. The big dog asked, "Hi there! Can I be your friend?" The girl smiled and gave a polite nod, and the bunny hopped closer to her side. The big dog said,"That's okay, I understand. I just want you to know that you and your bunny are safe with me. I'll always protect you!" The bunny hopped closer and the girl smiled with relief. She was glad to have such a kind, polite friend to protect her and her pet bunny. The end.

John was a curious 3 year old. He was curious about many things, including words. He asked his parents many questions about words. One day, John asked his parents what a dictionary was. His parents replied that it was a book that contained many words. John was excited to receive a dictionary. John eagerly waited for the day he would receive the dictionary. Finally, one day, he received a package. He couldn't wait to open it and find his dictionary. When he opened the package, he was surprised to find a book full of tiny words. He was a little frustrated because he couldn't tell what the words meant. John's parents explained to him that he had to look up the words in the dictionary to find out what they meant. John was disappointed, but he decided to give it a try. He looked up his first word, "frustrated". After he read the definition, he smiled. He understood what frustrated meant. John was pleased with his new dictionary and excited to learn more words.

One day, a young cat named Tiny went for a walk. She liked to walk to the rhythm of her own steps. As she walked, she saw a big door. It was close to her favorite tree. Tiny had never seen this door before. Tiny met a small mouse named Tim. Tim said, "Don't go close to the door, Tiny! It's not safe." But Tiny did not listen. She wanted to see what was behind the door. So, she went close to the door and opened it. Inside the door, there was a big, mean dog. The dog saw Tiny and chased her. Tiny ran away as fast as she could, but the dog was too fast. The dog caught Tiny, and she was never seen again. Tim was sad because he lost his friend. He wished Tiny had listened to him.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dove. The dove had big, strong wings. It could fly very high in the sky. The dove had many friends in the forest. They liked to play with the dove. One day, the dove saw a little girl. The girl was sad. She had lost her toy. The dove wanted to help her. So, the dove said, "I will take you to find your toy." The dove took the girl on its back. They flew high in the sky. They looked for the toy. They found the toy near a big tree. The girl was happy. She said, "Thank you, mighty dove!" The dove and the girl became good friends. They played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor named Tom. He lived in a modern house near the sea. Tom loved to laugh and play with his friends. He had a big boat that he would sail on the water. Every day, he would go out on his boat and have fun. One sunny day, Tom went out on his boat to sail. He saw a big fish jump out of the water. Tom laughed and clapped his hands. He wanted to show his friends the big fish. But then something unexpected happened. The big fish started to talk! It said, "Hello, sailor Tom! I am a magic fish!" Tom was very surprised. The magic fish told Tom that it could grant him one wish. Tom thought for a moment and wished for a never-ending supply of ice cream. The magic fish laughed and made Tom's wish come true. From that day on, Tom and his friends enjoyed ice cream every day and had lots of fun together.
