Title: Timon and the Park

Timon and his dad were in the park. Timon ran towards the big pit and was happy. He pointed to it and said "Race, Daddy!" His dad smiled and nodded. Timon started to run around the pit. It was big but he didn't get tired. He kept running, faster and faster. His dad laughed and started to race with him. They ran around and around, until Timon started to go slow. His dad sighed and bent over to pick him up. Timon smiled. He was so tired from the race. He hugged his dad and said he wanted to do it again. Back at home, Timon talked about his race for the rest of the day. He was so proud of himself for running so quickly around the pit.

Once upon a time, in a small hut, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to fold paper into fun shapes. One day, she wanted to make a big paper boat. She was very careful, making sure each fold was just right. Mia's friend, Tom, came over to play. "Look, Tom," said Mia, "I made a big paper boat!" Tom was excited and wanted to make one too. Mia taught Tom how to fold the paper carefully. They both made big paper boats. They went to the pond to sail their boats. Suddenly, a strong wind blew Mia's boat far away. Mia was sad, but Tom said, "Don't worry, Mia. We can make another one together!" They went back to the hut and made a new boat. This time, they were even more careful. The moral of the story is that when something unexpected happens, friends can help make things better.

Once upon a time, there was a serious vehicle. It was a big truck that liked to help people. The truck was always busy, and its wheels went round and round. One day, the truck saw a glow in the sky. It was a beautiful star that shined very bright. The truck wanted to see the star up close, so it drove and drove, trying to get closer to the glow. As the truck got closer, the star talked to the truck. The star said, "Thank you for being so serious and helping people." The truck smiled and went back to help more people. The star's glow made the truck very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Joe. He was three years old and loved collecting all sorts of things. One day, his mommy told him to take a walk and see what he could collect. So Joe excitedly went off and soon spotted an old celery plant. He quickly ran over, dug up a pile of celery, and put it in a basket. Joe went home happy with his celery collection. He showed it to his mommy and asked her what they could do with it. "Let's make soup together!" his mommy replied. Joe was thrilled and started to help his mommy cut up the celery. They were soon done making a very delicious celery soup. Yum!

Once upon a time, a little dog named Max had a dream. He wanted to go far away and play with his friends. But Max needed a special thing called a "license" to go out and play. One day, Max found a rough, old license on the ground. He thought, "This must be mine!" So he took the license and went to play with his friends. They played and played, and Max was so happy. But suddenly, Max's mom came running to him. She said, "Max, that is not your license! That is a license for a big car!" Max was surprised, but he gave the license back to his mom. Then, his mom gave him his very own license, and Max could go play with his friends every day.

One day, a big billboard lay in the park. It was very boring. It had no fun pictures or words. The kids did not like it. They wanted to play, but the billboard was in their way. A girl named Sara had an idea. She told her friends, "Let's make the billboard fun!" They all agreed. They got paint and brushes. They painted the billboard with bright colors. In the end, the boring billboard was pretty and fun. The kids were happy. They played in the park, and the colorful billboard made them smile. The conflict was gone, and everyone had a great time.
