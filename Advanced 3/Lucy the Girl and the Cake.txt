Title: Lucy the Girl and the Cake

One day, a little girl named Lucy woke up early. She went to the kitchen to find something to eat. She saw the big freezer and thought, "I want something cold!" Inside the freezer, Lucy found a big box. It was so important to her mommy. Lucy took the box out and opened it. She saw a big, cold cake! Lucy was so happy and started to eat the cake. It was so yummy! Just then, Lucy's mommy came into the kitchen. She saw Lucy eating the cake and was surprised! "Oh no, Lucy! That cake was for your birthday party!" Lucy felt sad, but her mommy hugged her and said, "It's okay, we can make a new cake together." So they did, and Lucy learned that sharing and telling the truth are important.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lamp in his room that he loved very much. The lamp was his friend, and it helped him see at night. One day, Tim was playing with his ball in the house. He was not careful, and he hit the lamp. The lamp fell and broke. Tim felt terrible because he had destroyed his friend, the lamp. He was sad and cried. Tim's mom came to see what happened. She saw the broken lamp and hugged Tim. She told him that it was important to be careful when playing inside the house. Tim learned his lesson and promised to be more careful. He understood that being careful can keep the things we love safe.

Once there was a brown doll. Her name was Daisy and she was all alone. One day, Daisy decided to go for a walk. She zipped through the meadow and felt the fresh breeze on her face. Suddenly, she heard a voice. It was a little boy saying, "I'm so lonely. Can you be my friend?" Daisy smiled and said, "Of course. I'm Daisy. What's your name?" The little boy replied, "My name is Jack. Would you like to come over to my house?" Daisy nodded her head and said, "Yes, I would love to!" So, Daisy and Jack zipped all the way to Jack's house. There, Daisy and Jack began to have lots of fun together. They played games and laughed all day long. Daisy was the happiest doll in the world that day!

Once upon a time, in a peaceful forest, there lived a little bird. The bird loved to sing all day long. It would sit on a log and sing its favorite songs. The other animals in the forest liked to listen to the bird's sweet voice. One day, a big storm came to the forest. The wind blew hard, and the rain fell down. The little bird was scared and hid under the log. It did not sing its songs that day. The other animals missed the bird's singing and felt sad. After the storm, the sun came out, and the forest was peaceful again. The little bird came out from under the log and started to sing. The other animals were happy to hear the bird's songs again. They all danced and played together in the beautiful, peaceful forest.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her toys and be silly. One day, she decided to play with her toy vegetables. She took them out of her toy box and put them all around her room. Amy's mom came into the room and saw the toy vegetables everywhere. She told Amy to kneel down and pick up her toys. Amy did what her mom said and started to pick up her toy vegetables. As Amy picked up her toys, she found a very silly vegetable. It was a carrot with a funny face on it. Amy laughed and showed it to her mom. They both laughed and had fun playing with the silly carrot. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a small island. On the island, there was a poor boy named Tom. Tom had a big paper. He wanted to fold it into a boat. One day, Tom and his friend Sam had a fight. Sam did not want to help Tom fold the boat. Tom was sad and angry. He tried to fold the boat by himself, but it was hard. Tom's mom saw the boys fighting. She told them, "Friends should help each other." Tom and Sam said sorry to each other. They worked together to fold the boat. The boat was big and strong. They learned that when friends help each other, they can do big things.
