Title: Sara and Ben and the Toys

Sara and Ben were twins. They liked to do everything together. They liked to play with their toys, read their books, and sing their songs. They were very happy. But today was a special day. It was their birthday. They were turning four years old. They were very excited. Their mom and dad had planned a big party for them. They had invited their friends, baked a cake, and bought some presents. They wanted to celebrate with their family and friends. But there was a problem. It was raining very hard. The sky was dark and the wind was loud. Sara and Ben looked out the window and felt sad. They could not go outside and play. They could not have their party in the garden. They wondered if their friends would come. "Don't worry, my darlings," their mom said. "We can still have a charming party inside. We have plenty of games and balloons and snacks. We can make our own fun. And I'm sure your friends will be here soon. They love you very much." Sara and Ben smiled. They hugged their mom and said thank you. They decided to be brave and cheerful. They helped their mom decorate the living room with streamers and banners. They put on their party hats and blew their whistles. They sang their favorite songs and danced around. Soon, they heard a knock on the door. It was their friends. They had come with their parents, umbrellas, and gifts. They were wet and cold, but they were happy to see Sara and Ben. They said hello and happy birthday. They came inside and joined the party. Sara and Ben were overjoyed. They welcomed their friends and shared their toys. They played pin the tail on the donkey, musical chairs, and hide and seek. They ate cake and ice cream and opened their presents. They laughed and clapped and cheered. They had a wonderful time. They forgot about the rain and the wind. They felt warm and cozy in their home. They felt loved and grateful for their family and friends. They had the best birthday ever. They celebrated with all their hearts.

Once there was a little girl called Abi. She was three and liked to play with her dolls. One day, Abi got very sick. She felt very miserable and her mom knew she needed medicine. Abi's mom took her to the hospital and the doctor gave Abi some medicine to take. Abi had to take the medicine every day and do her best to get better. She was also told to study her sickness and learn how to take care of herself. Abi was a very good student and worked hard. Slowly, Abi started feeling better. She stopped being so miserable and began to play and laugh with her dolls again. She thanked the doctor for his medicine and vowed to keep studying her illness so she would never feel as sick again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to play in the park. She would climb up and down the monkey bars and swing on the swings. One day when she was playing she found a big, long rope. She excitedly grabbed it and started to swing it around. The little girl's mom saw her playing and called out to her. "What are you doing?" she asked. The little girl said, "I found this rope! Can I keep it and play with it?" Her mom replied, "Yes, but be careful. If the rope is too tight, it might break!" The little girl nodded her head, and the mom smiled. Now, the little girl had something new to play with. She wrapped the rope around the monkey bars, then shook it as hard as she could. She laughed and smiled as the rope swung around, happy to have something new to play with. The girl's mom watched her play and smiled. She was glad her daughter was having so much fun. She reminded the girl to not make the rope too tight, in case it might break. The little girl remembered her mom's words and was more careful with the rope. She made sure it wasn't too tight, so she could keep playing and swinging!
