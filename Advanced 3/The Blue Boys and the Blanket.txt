Title: The Blue Boys and the Blanket

Once upon a time, there was a blue boys who loved yogurt. Every day he would ask for yogurt for breakfast and play with it. He would put it on his head, he would make sculptures out of it. One time he decided he would stay in his playroom and eat all the yogurt he wanted. He stayed in there all day, eating yogurt and playing with it. He made a blue yogurt blanket to stay warm and eat yogurt. He even imagined a little blue yogurt world, with a blue yogurt tree and a blue yogurt river. At night he was still in his playroom and he was feeling very sleepy. He slept in his blue yogurt blanket, until morning. The next morning, he was still in his playroom and he quickly ate some yogurt for breakfast. He had so much fun the day before that he decided he wanted to stay in his playroom for one more day and keep eating yogurt.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a miserable boy because he could not walk. He tried and tried, but he could not succeed. One day, Tim met a big, friendly dog. The dog could walk, and Tim wanted to walk too. The dog said, "Tim, I can help you walk. Just hold on to my back and try to take steps." Tim held on to the dog's back and tried to walk. They walked around the yard, but Tim still could not walk by himself. Tim and the dog tried every day to help Tim walk. They wanted to succeed, but it was very hard. One day, Tim got very sad and said, "I will never walk by myself." The dog felt sad too. In the end, Tim never learned to walk. He stayed miserable and the dog was sad too. They never succeeded, but they still stayed friends.

The sun was shining in the sky. It felt so warm and healthy. A little girl named Sarah was playing outside in the sunshine. Suddenly, she saw a group of butterflies. All of them were different colors and sizes. She wanted to catch them all. But the butterflies were too fast for Sarah. She ran after them for a long time, but eventually she had to surrender. She stopped running and watched them flutter away. She was so sad she couldn't catch them, but the sun was still shining in the sky. Sarah felt grateful that the sun was still shining. It made her feel healthy and happy. She smiled at the sun, and it seemed like the sun smiled back. Sarah then decided to sit in the sunshine and enjoy its healthy warmth.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was a little frog. The frog was very old. He had many friends, but he was not happy. He wanted to taste something new, something different. One day, the old frog saw a big, red fruit on a tree. He wanted to taste it, but it was too high for him to reach. He asked his friends, the fish and the turtle, for help. They all worked together and helped the old frog to reach the fruit. When the frog tasted the fruit, he was so happy. It was the best thing he had ever tasted. He shared the fruit with his friends, and they all enjoyed it. The old frog learned that when you work together with your friends, you can do anything. And that was the moral of the story: always help your friends and work together.

Once upon a time, there was a big blackboard in a small room. One day, a boy named Tom came into the room. Tom saw the blackboard and wanted to draw on it. He picked up a piece of chalk and started to draw a big sun. Suddenly, Tom's friend, Sally, came into the room. She saw Tom drawing on the blackboard and said, "Tom, you should not draw on that blackboard. It is not yours." Tom felt guilty and stopped drawing. He looked at Sally and said, "I am sorry, I did not know." Just then, they heard a noise coming from the corner of the room. They went to see what it was. They found a big, old apple that was starting to rot. Tom and Sally looked at each other, surprised. They did not expect to find a rotting apple in the room. Sally had an idea. She picked up the apple and said, "Let's go outside and throw this away. Then we can play together." Tom agreed, and they left the room, forgetting all about the blackboard. From that day on, Tom and Sally always played together and never felt guilty again. They learned that it is important to share and take care of things, even if they are not yours.
