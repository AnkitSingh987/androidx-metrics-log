Title: Lucy and the Swings

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy had a charming blue folder. She loved her folder very much. She put all her pretty drawings in it. Lucy took her folder everywhere she went. One day, Lucy went to the park with her mom. They played on the swings and had a fun time. But when they were going home, Lucy started to worry. She could not find her blue folder. "Mom, where is my folder?" Lucy asked. Her mom said, "I don't know, let's go back to the park and look for it." They went back to the park and looked everywhere. Lucy was very worried. Then, they saw a little boy holding the charming blue folder. Lucy said, "That's my folder!" The little boy said, "I found it on the swing. I'm sorry, I didn't know it was yours." He gave the folder back to Lucy. She was so happy and thanked the little boy. Lucy and her mom went home with the charming blue folder, and Lucy never worried about losing it again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jimmy. He was three years old and he loved to explore. One day, he was walking in the park when he noticed a mysterious door. He walked up to the door and tried to open it, but it was locked. He wanted to know what was inside the door. He decided to take the spray out of his pocket and spray it all over the door. Suddenly, the door opened up and Jimmy was surprised! He quickly walked inside and saw something he had never seen before. It was a magical world full of colourful creatures and plants. He heard a noise and suddenly he saw a tiger walking towards him. Jimmy was scared and ran back out of the mysterious door, but the tiger had him cornered. Jimmy froze in fear, but then he remembered the spray in his pocket. He quickly pulled out the spray and sprayed it at the tiger. It worked! The tiger ran away and Jimmy ran back home. From that moment on, Jimmy knew that he should never go near a mysterious door again!

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Tom. Tom liked to balance on the fence in the garden. One day, Tom saw a big bag of mail by the door. Tom wanted to help. He tried to take the mail to the people in the house. He put the mail in his mouth and tried to balance on the fence. But the mail was too big and heavy for Tom. Tom fell off the fence and dropped the mail in the mud. The people came out and said, "Oh no, our mail is dirty!" Tom felt sad that he could not help with the mail. The people had to clean their mail, and Tom went back to the garden.

Lily liked to play the piano. She learned from her mom, who was very good at it. Lily liked to make music with her fingers and hear the sounds. Sometimes she sang along, too. But Lily was lonely. She had no friends to play with. Her mom was busy with work and her dad was far away. Lily wished she had someone to share her music with. One day, a new family moved in next door. They had a boy named Max, who was Lily's age. Max saw Lily playing the piano through the window and knocked on the door. "Hi, I'm Max. Can I play with you?" he asked. Lily smiled and nodded. She opened the door and invited Max in. She showed him the piano and taught him some notes. Max liked the piano, too. He tried to copy Lily and make music. They played and laughed and sang together. Lily was happy. She had a friend to play with. She thanked Max for coming over. "Thank you for playing with me. You are a good friend," she said. Max hugged Lily and thanked her, too. "Thank you for teaching me the piano. You are a good friend, too," he said. They decided to play the piano every day. Lily was not lonely anymore. She had Max and the piano.
