Title: Max the Dependable Dog and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Max. Max liked to eat minerals that he found in the ground. One day, Max found a big, shiny mineral. He was very happy. Max wanted to keep his shiny mineral safe, so he put it in a little box. He shut the box tight, so no one could take it. Max carried the box everywhere he went. He loved his shiny mineral very much. One day, Max met a little girl named Lisa. Lisa was sad because she lost her toy. Max wanted to help Lisa. He gave her his shiny mineral from the box. Lisa was very happy, and they became best friends. Max knew that being a good friend was more important than keeping the shiny mineral all to himself.

One day, a boy named Tim was very eager to play with his new toy, a computer. He had been waiting for a long time to get it. His mom said, "Tim, you can play with the computer after you eat your lunch." Tim ate his lunch very fast. He was so excited to play with the computer. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim. The computer is not a toy. You must learn how to use it right." Tim went to his room and turned on the computer. He saw many things on the screen. He tried to touch the screen, but nothing happened. Tim felt sad. He did not know how to use the computer. Then, his big sister, Jane, came into the room. She saw Tim was sad. Jane said, "I can help you, Tim. Let's learn how to use the computer together." Tim smiled and felt happy. Tim and Jane learned how to use the computer. They played games, drew pictures, and had lots of fun. Tim was glad that he had his big sister to help him. Now, he knew how to use the computer right.

Lily and Ben wanted to make a snowman. They put on their warm coats, hats, gloves and boots. They took a spade and a carrot from the shed. They ran outside to the garden, where the snow was white and soft. They rolled a big ball of snow for the body, and a smaller one for the head. They used the spade to make arms and legs. They put the carrot for the nose, and two stones for the eyes. They found a scarf and a hat to dress the snowman. They were very happy with their snowman. He looked friendly and funny. "Hello, Mr. Snowman!" Lily said. "Do you like your new home?" "Yes, I do!" Ben said, pretending to be the snowman. "Thank you for making me!" Lily and Ben laughed and hugged the snowman. They felt very comfortable and warm, even though it was cold outside. But they did not see the sun coming out from behind the clouds. The sun was bright and hot. It started to melt the snow. The snowman began to shiver and drip. "Oh no!" Lily said. "Mr. Snowman is melting!" "We have to save him!" Ben said. "Let's move him to the shade!" They tried to push the snowman, but he was too heavy and wet. They could not move him. They watched as the snowman became smaller and smaller, until he was gone. Lily and Ben felt very sad. They cried and hugged each other. "Goodbye, Mr. Snowman!" they said. "We will miss you!" They went back to the house, where their mom gave them hot chocolate and cookies. They told her about their snowman, and how he melted. She hugged them and said they could make another one, when it snowed again. Lily and Ben smiled and felt better. They knew they would always remember their snowman, and the fun they had with him.

One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big, yellow aeroplane in the sky. He was very happy and excited. He said, "Mom, look at the yellow aeroplane! It is so big and pretty!" His mom looked up and smiled. She said, "Yes, Tim, it is a very nice aeroplane. Let's observe it as it flies by." They both watched the yellow aeroplane fly high in the sky. It made a soft noise as it went by. As they watched, a little bird flew near the aeroplane. The bird was also yellow. Tim laughed and said, "Mom, the bird is the same color as the aeroplane!" His mom agreed and they both enjoyed watching the yellow bird and the yellow aeroplane together.
