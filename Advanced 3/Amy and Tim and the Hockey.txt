Title: Amy and Tim and the Hockey

Once upon a time, there was a family with two kids – Amy and Tim. Amy was spoiled and Tim was always nice. One day, Amy wanted a hockey. Her parents said they could supply it to her, but Tim told them he wanted one too. His parents replied they wouldn't supply two. Amy started to cry and Tim felt sad too. The parents said they would only supply one hockey, but they promised Amy and Tim they could have a game of hockey. The parents bought a hockey and set up a game. Everyone had a really good time. Amy shared her hockey with Tim and they had fun playing together. In the end, all the kids were happy.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. One was an elephant, the other a gorilla. They were the best of friends and did everything together. One day, they heard a strange noise outside. When they looked out, they saw a helicopter! "Let's go and see it!" said the elephant. But the gorilla was worried. He had never seen a helicopter before. "What if it wants to fight?" said the gorilla. "Don't worry" said the elephant. "I'll protect you." The two friends walked outside. The helicopter was hovering in the sky. "See, it's just a helicopter" said the elephant. The two friends looked up in awe. Suddenly the helicopter flew away and they laughed. "It was nothing to be worried about" said the elephant. The two friends hugged and laughed. They were so happy that nothing had happened. And they lived happily ever after!

Tom and Lily were brother and sister. They liked to play in the river near their house. One day, they found a big raft made of wood and rope. They wanted to use it to float on the water. "Let me go on the raft first!" Tom said. He was older and bigger than Lily. "No, I want to go on the raft first!" Lily said. She was younger and smaller than Tom. They began to quarrel. They pushed and pulled the raft. They shouted and cried. They were very angry with each other. "Stop it, you two!" their mother said. She came to the river and saw them fighting. "You have to share the raft. You can take turns. Or you can go on the raft together." Tom and Lily looked at each other. They felt sorry for being mean. They said sorry to each other and to their mother. "Can we go on the raft together?" Tom asked. "Yes, we can!" Lily said. They smiled and hugged. They climbed on the raft and held hands. They floated on the water and had fun. They were happy again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a pretty rose in the garden. She wanted to pick it up, but it was too high for her to reach. Lily felt sad and wanted to surrender. But then, she saw a friendly bird. The bird saw Lily was sad and wanted to help. The bird flew up and picked the rose for her. Lily was so happy! She thanked the bird and they became friends. They played together all day long. But then, something unexpected happened. The rose turned into a spicy candy! Lily and the bird were surprised. They laughed and shared the candy. They had a fun day and a new story to tell their friends.

Lily was excited to ride in the van with her mom and dad. They were going to the park to play and have a picnic. Lily liked the park. It had swings and slides and ducks. She put on her hat and shoes and ran to the van. Mom and dad got in the front seats and buckled up. Lily got in the back seat and buckled up too. She looked out the window and saw the trees and houses and cars. She sang a song with mom and dad. They were happy. But then something bad happened. A big truck came out of nowhere and hit the van. The van spun and crashed and made a loud noise. Lily felt scared and hurt. She cried and screamed. Mom and dad cried and screamed too. The truck driver got out of his truck and ran to the van. He was sorry and sad. He called for help. Soon sirens and lights came. People in uniforms came and took Lily and mom and dad out of the van. They put them on stretchers and in ambulances. Lily saw blood and broken glass and smoke. She wanted to go to the park. She wanted to play and have a picnic. She wanted to ride in the van with mom and dad. But she couldn't. The van was gone. The park was gone. The picnic was gone. Everything was gone. Lily was not excited anymore. She was sad.
