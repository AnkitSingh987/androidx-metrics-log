Title: Mum and the Sunday

Mum was in the kitchen on Sunday morning getting ready for a special morning. Timmy asked Mum: "Mum what are you doing?". Mum replied "I'm making breakfast, Timmy. I'm getting ready for a special Sunday". Timmy was very excited and told Mum: "I'm ready, too!". So they both sat at the table and had a lovely breakfast. After that they spent most of the day playing games, running around and exploring outside. They had so much fun and were very happy. Sunday was a special day for them both and they were very happy to be together.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Dolly. She loved to dance. One day, she went to the pond to play with her friends. The pond was very big and had many fish in it. Dolly saw her friend, a frog named Freddy. "Hi, Freddy!" Dolly said. "Do you want to dance with me?" Freddy looked at Dolly and smiled. "Yes, Dolly. I love to dance too!" They danced and danced by the pond. They were so happy. Then, they saw something shiny in the water. It was an expensive ring. Dolly and Freddy looked at each other and smiled. They knew they had found something very special. Dolly and Freddy showed the ring to their friends at the pond. Everyone was very excited. They all danced and played, happy to have found something so special. And from that day on, they always remembered their special dance by the pond.

Once there was a young boy named Bob. He was 3 years old and he loved going on adventures! One day after dinner, Bob was feeling very sleepy and decided to take a nap. 'Let's find a nice spot to take a nap', he said. So Bob went outside and saw a path by the river. It looked so inviting! But as he stepped onto the path, he noticed it was dirty. He was scared and stopped. Then a friendly frog hopped up to him and said, 'You don't have to worry! All you have to do is hop on the path, and stay on the path, until it's clean'. So Bob took a deep breath and hopped on the path. He went up, down, and around and the dirt started to disappear. Finally, when the path was clean, Bob felt relieved and hopped off. He found a nice grassy spot, lay down, and fell asleep. When he woke up a few hours later, he was feeling refreshed and happy!

Once upon a time, there was a funny little dog named Spot. Spot had a big, round nose and loved to play all day. Spot's best friend was a girl named Sue. Sue and Spot would always enjoy their time together. One sunny day, Sue found a nail on the ground. She thought it was very interesting. Sue showed the nail to Spot and said, "We will need this later." Spot wagged his tail, excited to see what Sue had planned. Later that day, Sue and Spot found a broken fence. Sue remembered the nail she found earlier. She used the nail to fix the fence, and it was as good as new. Spot and Sue were very happy. They played by the fence all day and enjoyed their fun adventure.

Once upon a time, in a big palace, there lived a fat king. He had many friends, but his best friend was a little cat. They liked to play together all day long. One day, the fat king and the cat were playing with a ball. The ball rolled far away and lay under a big tree. The cat said, "I will get the ball for you, my friend." The king was happy and waited for the cat to come back. The cat got the ball and brought it back to the king. They continued to play and had a lot of fun. The fat king learned that it was important to help his friends. He became a better king and made his people happy too. The moral of the story is to always help your friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends in the park. One day, Lily took her friend's toy without asking. She felt ashamed for what she did. Lily knew she needed to say sorry to her friend. She felt regret for taking the toy. So, she went to her friend's house and gave the toy back. She said, "I am sorry for taking your toy. I should have asked you first." Her friend smiled and said, "Thank you for giving it back. I forgive you." They hugged each other, and peace came back to their friendship. From that day on, Lily promised herself that she would always ask before taking something that was not hers.
