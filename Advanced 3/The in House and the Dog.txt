Title: The in House and the Dog

Once upon a time, a big fierce dog lived in a small house. The dog did not like the kids who lived next door. Every week, the kids would play outside and make a lot of noise. The dog would bark at them and growl. The kids were scared of the dog. One day, the kids were playing with a ball. They kicked the ball too hard, and it went into the dog's yard. One of the kids said, "I don't want to get the ball. The dog will bite me!" The other kid said, "I hate that dog. He is so mean!" The kids tried to get the ball back, but the dog was too fierce. They were scared, so they ran away. The dog barked and growled at them. The kids were sad because they could not play with their ball anymore. The next day, the kids told their mom about the dog. Their mom said, "We can't do anything about the dog. He lives there, and we live here." The kids were sad and did not play outside for a long time. In the end, the kids never got their ball back. They were too scared of the fierce dog. They did not play outside as much, and they always felt sad when they saw the dog.

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was a big statue. The statue was a bossy man who liked to hold a stick. The children would play near the statue every day. One sunny day, a little girl named Sally was playing near the statue. She saw a pretty bird sitting on the bossy man's head. Sally said, "Hello, bird! Do you like sitting on the bossy man's head?" The bird replied, "Hello, Sally! I do like it here. But, guess what? This bossy man can talk!" Sally was very surprised. She didn't know statues could talk. The bossy man's statue started to move and said, "Hello, Sally! I am not just a statue. I am a magic statue!" Sally was so happy to meet a magic statue. They became good friends and played in the park together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, round stone. It was a very serious stone, with a face on it. Tim thought the face looked like it had a lot of life in it. He decided to take the stone home with him. When he got home, Tim showed the stone to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a special stone!" he said. His mom smiled and said, "That's a very nice stone, Tim. What are you going to do with it?" Tim thought for a moment and then said, "I'm going to rub it and see if something happens!" So, Tim rubbed the stone with his little hands. Suddenly, the stone's face began to move and talk! It said, "Thank you for rubbing me, Tim. I was very sleepy, but now I am awake!" Tim and the stone became good friends. They played and talked every day. The stone told Tim many stories about its life. And Tim was always happy to rub the stone when it needed to wake up.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She was very careful as she walked. She liked to see all the things around her. She saw a big tree, a red ball, and a cute dog. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. As she walked, she saw a shower. It was outside and all alone. Amy thought it was funny to see a shower outside. She said, "Hello, shower! What are you doing here?" The shower said, "Hi, Amy! I am here for a surprise." Amy was excited to see what the surprise was. The shower said, "Stand back and be careful." Then, the shower turned on and water came out. The water made a big, beautiful rainbow. Amy laughed and clapped her hands. She had never seen a shower make a rainbow before. It was a very special day for Amy.

Once there lived a brave young bear called Sandy. Sandy was always looking to help out other animals in the forest. One day, Sandy found an old deer in the forest. The deer told Sandy that his stomach hurt, so Sandy decided to help. Sandy knew the only way to help the deer was to find some rare water that he heard about in the forest. He decided to kneel down and pray to the forest spirits for help. The forest spirits came and told Sandy that the water he was looking for was in a nearby lake. Sandy quickly ran to the lake, filled his bucket with the rare water and brought it back to the deer. The deer was soon feeling better after drinking the rare water. Sandy was happy to see the deer well again. From this experience, Sandy learned the important lesson that with faith and courage, anything is possible.
