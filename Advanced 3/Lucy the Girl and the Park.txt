Title: Lucy the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to travel with her mom and dad. One day, they went on a trip to the big park. Lucy was so happy! At the park, Lucy saw a stubborn little boy who would not share his toy. Lucy walked up to him and said, "Please, can I play with your toy too?" The little boy looked at her and bit his lip. He thought about it for a moment, and then he said, "Okay, you can play with my toy." Lucy was very happy, and they played together for a long time. Later, Lucy's mom called her to go home. Lucy said goodbye to her new friend, and they promised to play together again. Lucy's mom and dad were proud of her for making a new friend and for being so kind. They knew that their little girl would always be a good friend to others.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Tom and Tim. They loved to play near the river. One hot day, they found a shiny pebble near the water. Both friends wanted the pebble for themselves. Tom and Tim started to quarrel about who should keep the pebble. They got very loud and angry. Their faces turned red, and they forgot they were friends. They did not want to share the pebble. A wise old owl saw the two friends quarreling. The owl told Tom and Tim, "If you share the pebble, you both can be happy and play together." The friends listened to the owl and decided to share the pebble. They stopped quarreling and played happily together. The moral of the story is that sharing is better than fighting.

Lily liked to play with her toy mouse. She named it Mimi and gave it a pink bow. She would pick it up and hug it and pretend it was her friend. She took it everywhere with her, even to bed. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom and dad. She brought Mimi with her and put it in her pocket. She wanted to show it to the other kids. She saw a big slide and wanted to try it. She ran to the slide and climbed up the ladder. But when she reached the top, she felt something was wrong. She reached into her pocket and gasped. Mimi was gone! She looked down and saw her toy mouse on the ground, near the slide. She felt very anxious. What if someone took it or stepped on it? She wanted to go down and get it, but she was scared of the slide. It was too high and too fast. She started to cry and called for her mom and dad. They heard her and came to help her. They saw Mimi on the ground and understood what happened. They told Lily not to worry. They said they would get Mimi for her and that she was brave for trying the slide. They helped her go down the slide and hugged her. They picked up Mimi and gave it back to Lily. She was so happy and relieved. She thanked her mom and dad and hugged Mimi. She said she would never lose it again. She learned to be more careful with her toy mouse and to enjoy the slide.

One morning, Peter woke up and went outside. Outside, he saw a furry penny on the ground. "Wow!" shouted Peter, "What a shiny penny!" So Peter decided to pick it up. Suddenly, a voice said, "That penny is mine!" Peter looked up and saw a little bird. The bird continued, "Do you mind throwing me my penny, please?" So Peter said "Yes!" And he threw the penny back to the bird. The bird caught the penny and said, "Thank you!" Peter looked at the bird and smiled. "You're welcome," he said. Suddenly, something brightly coloured and furry flew down from the sky and landed in Peter's hands. It was a fuzzy toy! Peter was so happy. He had received a furry toy! "I don't have a penny," said the bird, "but this toy is my thank you gift for giving me back my penny". "Thank you!" shouted Peter, "This furry toy is very special to me!".
