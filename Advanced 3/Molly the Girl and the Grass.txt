Title: Molly the Girl and the Grass

Once there was a girl named Molly. She was only 3 years old. One sunny day Molly wanted to go outside and jump in the grass. When she got outside, she saw something shiny on the ground. It was a needle! Molly had never seen a needle before and she was scared. Molly was confused and didn't know what to do. Then, her big brother came to help her. He was very helpful. He picked up the needle very carefully and explained to Molly that there can be many dangerous things outside and that she should always be careful. Molly learned her lesson. The next day, Molly was back outside and wanted to jump in the grass. But then she remembered the needle she had seen. She stopped and looked around carefully to make sure that everything was safe before she jumped. She was glad she listened to her big brother. Molly learned that being careful and paying attention always helps. She was smart to use her newfound lesson and stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly comet. It was flying through the sky. It was not like the other comets. It was sad because it was smelly. No one wanted to be near it. One day, the smelly comet saw a big star. The star was acting funny. It was jumping and dancing in the sky. The smelly comet wanted to be friends with the star. The comet thought, "Maybe the star will not care that I am smelly." The smelly comet went to the big star. The star was happy to see the comet. They played and danced together. The star did not care that the comet was smelly. They had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The big star started to smell too! The smelly comet was surprised. The comet asked, "Why do you smell now?" The big star said, "I was acting funny because I was trying to hide my smell." The smelly comet and the big star were both smelly. But they were happy because they had each other. They danced and played together in the sky. They were the best of friends. And they learned that it was okay to be smelly, as long as they had each other.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to jog every day. One day, he went on a long journey to find a big park. On his journey, Spot met a big, happy cat. The cat said, "Come play with me." Spot and the cat played and ran all day. They had so much fun together. But then, something unexpected happened. The big, happy cat was really a little girl dressed up like a cat! She said, "Surprise! I wanted to play with you, Spot." Spot was very surprised, but he was happy to have a new friend to play and jog with. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily loves to sign. She knows how to say many things with her hands. She can sign her name, her colors, her animals, and her feelings. She likes to teach her friends how to sign too. Today is Lily's birthday. She is very happy. She is having a party with her friends. She has a cake, balloons, and presents. She wants to sign "thank you" to everyone who comes. She sees her friend Ben. He has a big smile. He gives her a present. It is a book. Lily loves books. She signs "thank you" to Ben. He signs "you're welcome" back. He learned how to sign from Lily. They are both enthusiastic. They go to the table. They see the cake. It has candles. Lily signs "happy birthday" to herself. She blows out the candles. She makes a wish. She wishes for more books and more friends who can sign. She cuts the cake. She gives a piece to Ben. He gives a piece to Lily. They eat the cake. It is yummy. They sign "delicious" to each other. They are both happy. They hug. They are best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a happy dog named Spot. Spot had a long tail that wagged when he was excited. He loved to play with his friends, the cat and the bird, in the big yard. One sunny day, Spot, the cat, and the bird decided to have a race. They all lined up at the start and got ready to zoom. The bird flapped its wings, the cat stretched its legs, and Spot wagged his tail. When the race began, they all zoomed as fast as they could. The bird flew high, the cat ran low, and Spot's tail wagged as he ran. In the end, they all crossed the finish line together. They were so happy, they all wagged their tails and zoomed around the yard some more.
