Title: Lily the Polite Girl and the Sock

Once upon a time, there was a polite girl named Lily. She had a funny sock that she loved to wear. The sock had a big smile on it, and it always made Lily laugh. One day, Lily put on her funny sock and went to play with her friends. When her friends saw the sock, they started to laugh too. They all thought the sock was very funny. Lily and her friends played all day, laughing and having fun. The funny sock made everyone happy. And from that day on, Lily always wore her funny sock when she played with her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with a red door. One day, his mommy told him to be careful with a fragile cup she just bought. Tim promised to be careful. Tim wanted to play with his friends outside. He opened the red door and saw a new friend. The new friend asked, "Can I play with you?" Tim said, "Yes, I accept you as my friend." They played and had fun together. But Tim forgot about the fragile cup. He bumped into it, and the cup broke. Tim felt sad. His mommy said, "Remember to be careful with fragile things." Tim learned to be more careful and to always remember what his mommy tells him.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the shore with his mom. He saw a big crab near the water. The crab was red and had big claws. Tim wanted to poke the crab to see what would happen. Tim walked closer to the crab and poked it with a stick. The crab was very angry. It tried to pinch Tim with its claws. Tim's mom saw the crab and told him to be careful. She said, "Don't poke the crab, or it will hurt you!" But Tim didn't listen. He poked the crab again. This time, the crab pinched Tim's finger. It hurt a lot! Tim cried and his mom came to help him. She gave him a sour candy to make him feel better, but it didn't work. Tim's finger hurt all day long. He was sad that he didn't listen to his mom. Now, Tim knew that poking crabs was not a good idea. The day at the shore was not fun anymore.

Once upon a time, there were two adorable puppies. They ran and played each day in the big yard. One day, they found a row of sticks in the grass. The sticks were so exciting and fun to play with! The puppies chased after the sticks and twisted them in all directions. Suddenly, one of the sticks broke into two. The puppies were very sad, for the stick was now ruined. The puppies realized how important it is to be gentle with things, so everything stays in perfect condition. The two puppies decided to leave the sticks alone and play with something else. They agreed they would always be careful when they play, so nothing gets ruined ever again. The puppies were happy again and went off to find something else adorable to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her toy bin. One day, Amy's mom told her a story about a princess who was going to marry a prince. Amy really liked the story and wanted to play it with her toys. Amy took her favorite doll, Lucy, and said, "Lucy, do you want to marry my toy bin?" Lucy said, "Yes, I want to marry the bin, but I am worried. What if the bin does not like me?" Amy hugged Lucy and said, "Don't be worried, Lucy. The bin will like you, I promise." Amy put Lucy next to the bin and said, "Now you are married!" Lucy and the bin were happy. Amy played with them all day long. At the end of the day, Amy put Lucy and the bin next to each other and said, "Good night, Lucy and bin. I hope you have sweet dreams."

Once upon a time, there was a snake named Sam. Sam was a dependable snake. He liked to help others. One day, Sam met a bird named Betty. Betty was sad. She lost her food. "Betty, I will provide you food," said Sam. "I am a dependable snake, and I want to help you." Betty was happy. She thanked Sam. Sam and Betty went to find food together. Sam and Betty found some yummy worms. They shared the worms. Betty was not sad anymore. She had a new dependable friend, Sam the snake. They played together every day. And they lived happily ever after.
