Title: Anna and the Ball

Anna was playing in the garden with her new ball. She liked to throw it high and catch it again. She saw a spider on a flower. The spider was small and black and had eight legs. "Hello, spider," Anna said. "Do you want to play with me?" The spider did not answer. It was busy spinning a web. Anna watched the spider make a circle of silk. She thought it was pretty. "Can I touch your web?" Anna asked. The spider shook its head. It did not want Anna to break its web. It had worked hard to make it. "Please, just a little," Anna said. She reached out her hand. The spider got scared. It jumped off the flower and landed on Anna's ball. The ball was round and bouncy. The spider felt it move under him. "Whoa!" the spider said. He did not like the ball. He wanted to get off. Anna saw the spider on her ball. She thought it was funny. She laughed and picked up the ball. "Look, spider, you can spring with me!" Anna said. She threw the ball up and down. The spider bounced with the ball. He felt dizzy and sick. "Stop, stop, please stop!" the spider said. He wanted to get off. Anna heard the spider. She felt sorry for him. She stopped throwing the ball and put it down. The spider crawled off the ball and ran away. "Bye, spider," Anna said. "Sorry I scared you. Maybe we can play another day." The spider did not answer. He was glad to be away from the ball. He looked for another flower to make a new web. He hoped Anna would not find him again.

Once upon a time, there was a magical diamond. It was so special and shiny that everyone wanted to keep it safe and protect it from harm. One day, an ordinary little girl called Sarah saw the diamond. She had never seen anything so beautiful before. Sarah wanted to keep it for herself, so she made a plan. She asked her dad for help. "Daddy, can you help me protect the diamond?" she asked. Her dad smiled and said, "Yes, my darling. I will protect the diamond for you." So Sarah and her dad kept the diamond safe. They made sure that it was never out of their sight, so that it would stay safe forever. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. Jack liked to help his mom and dad around the house. One day, his mom asked him to bury her old napkin in the garden. Jack was very patient, so he happily agreed. He got a shovel and carefully dug a hole in the garden. He was very careful not to hit any worms or bugs! When he was done, he carefully put the old napkin in the hole and filled it up with dirt. When he was finished, he patted the ground down nice and flat. Next he put a few colorful rocks around the area so his mom and dad could always find the napkin. After that, Jack felt proud of his work and he was ready for a nap!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a nosy little girl named Lily. She loved to wake up early and go to her class. Lily liked to learn new things and play with her friends. One day, Lily woke up very early. She got ready for class and was excited to see her friends. She put on her shoes, grabbed her bag, and ran to the door. Lily's mom said, "Wait, Lily! Don't forget your lunch!" Lily took her lunch and went to class. In the class, Lily saw a new girl sitting alone. Being nosy, she went to talk to her. The new girl said her name was Rosie. Lily and Rosie became friends and played together all day. From that day on, they always woke up excited to go to class and be with each other.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a kind dog named Buddy. Buddy was a very good dog who loved to help others. One day, Buddy saw a little girl named Lily who was sad because she lost her toy. Buddy wanted to help Lily find her toy. Buddy thought about where the toy could be. He had an idea and suggested to Lily, "Let's look in the park!" Lily agreed and they went to the park together. They searched high and low, and finally, they found the toy under a big tree. Lily was so happy and thanked Buddy for his help. After finding the toy, Buddy and Lily played together in the park. They took turns on the slide and swings, always making sure to follow the order of who went first. They had so much fun and became the best of friends. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others when they need it, because it can lead to wonderful friendships.
