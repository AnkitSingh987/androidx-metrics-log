Title: The Hairy Bear Living and the Woods

Once upon a time there was a hairy bear living in the woods. Every day he went out to the forest to find food. One day while the bear was walking he saw a waste basket with some yummy treats in it. He was so excited that he nodded his head and quickly began to grab them. Suddenly a voice spoke up. "Hey! That's mine!" The bear looked up and saw a small child. He felt a bit shy and he quickly nodded his head before he ran away. The child smiled and said, "Thank you, hairy bear!" The bear nodded his head and kept running until he was back in the safety of the woods. From then on he only went out to search for food when the child wasn't around!

Once upon a time there was a playful little girl named Daisy. She had a pet puppy named Lucky. Daisy always played with Lucky outside, and they had lots of fun together. One day, Daisy saw a friendly creature. She couldn't quite tell what it was, so she asked her mom. "Mommy, what is that?" Daisy said. Her mom smiled and said, "That is a squirrel. He looks alert, and he is offering you something with his palm." Daisy was so excited. She slowly stepped closer to the friendly squirrel, and looked at the object in his palm. It was a piece of candy. The squirrel said, "Take my candy and share it with your puppy. I'm sure you both will like it!" Daisy smiled and said, "Thank you, squirrel!" Then she took the candy and gave it to her puppy, Lucky. Lucky wagged his tail, and the two friends enjoyed their special treat.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red button in a small room. People said that this button was very important. It could protect everyone in the town. One day, a little boy found the room with the big, red button. He thought it looked fun to play with. He did not know that it was not a toy. The little boy was about to push the button, but a wise, old man saw him. The old man knew that pushing the button would be a stupid thing to do. The wise, old man ran to the little boy and told him, "Don't push the button! It can protect us all, but only if we don't touch it." The little boy listened to the old man and did not push the button. The town was safe, and the little boy learned a lesson about listening to wise, old people.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Poppy. She lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. Poppy loved to play with her toys and go outside in the sun. One day, Poppy saw a big box in the living room. She wanted to carry it outside to play, but it was not easy. Poppy tried and tried, but she could not move the box. She was sad and sat down on the floor. Then, her daddy came into the room and saw Poppy sitting on the floor. He asked her what was wrong. Poppy told him about the big box and how she could not carry it outside. Her daddy smiled and said, "Don't worry, Poppy. I will help you." Together, they carried the box outside, and it was easy. Poppy was very happy, and they played with the box all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a princess who loved to spin. She would spin around and around all day long in her big room. One day, she met a dependable friend named Tom. Tom was a nice boy who always helped her when she needed it. One day, the princess and Tom were playing in the garden. They were spinning around and around. Suddenly, they saw a big, scary dog. The dog barked at them, and they were scared. The princess said, "Tom, what do we do?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "Let's spin really fast and maybe the dog will get dizzy and go away." So, the princess and Tom started to spin as fast as they could. The dog watched them and started to spin too. The dog spun so fast that it got dizzy and fell down. The princess and Tom were surprised that their plan worked. They laughed and continued to spin and play in the garden. The big, scary dog got up and walked away, not bothering them anymore. The princess and Tom were happy that they could keep playing and spinning without being scared. They knew they could depend on each other to solve any problem that came their way.
