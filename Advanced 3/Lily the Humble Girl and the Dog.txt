Title: Lily the Humble Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a humble girl named Lily. She had a little diary where she wrote all her thoughts. One day, she had to pack her things and go to her grandma's house. On her way to grandma's house, she met a big dog. The dog wanted to take her diary. Lily was scared, but she did not want the dog to have her diary. She thought of a plan to keep her diary safe. Lily found a stick and threw it far away. The dog ran to get the stick. While the dog was gone, Lily quickly packed her diary in her bag. She ran to her grandma's house and told her about the big dog. Grandma hugged her and said, "You are brave, Lily." They sat together, and Lily read her diary to grandma. They were happy and safe.

Once upon a time there was a big drum that sat atop a hill. Everyone in the village was scared of the drum because, when it rumbled, the ground would tremble and the animals would run away. One day, a brave three year old heard the drum and thought he could go and play on it. Even though his mommy and daddy said not to, he was very curious and so he decided to sneak off to play. The three year old went up the hill, with every step the ground trembled beneath him. He was so scared, but he wanted to play and explore so he kept going. When he reached the drum, he touched it and the ground shook more than before. He was so scared and ran away as quickly as he could. He never returned, and from that day forward the village was much safer from the rumble of the big drum. The moral of the story is that it is important to listen to mommy and daddy and follow instructions so that you stay safe.

Jimmy rushed home from the stadium. He had just been to the football game with his family, and he was so excited to tell them all about it. “What did you do at the stadium?” asked Mommy. “We saw a football game!” said Jimmy. “It was so much fun!” Mommy smiled. “What did you eat there?” “Yummy hot dogs!” Jimmy said. “They were the best!” Mommy laughed. “I’m glad you had a good time. Now let’s hurry and have dinner.” So Jimmy rushed to the table and tucked into some delicious lasagna. He couldn’t wait to tell his friends about everything he had seen and done at the stadium!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to joke a lot. He had a job at a farm. At the farm, he took care of a rare, big bird. The bird was very special. One day, Tim said to the bird, "Why did the chicken cross the road?" The bird looked at him and laughed. Tim was very surprised. He did not know that the bird could talk. "I can talk, too!" said the bird. Tim and the bird became good friends. They joked and talked all day long. One day, the bird told Tim a secret. The bird could make a rare, magic egg. The egg could make anyone laugh. Tim and the bird shared the egg with their friends, and everyone was happy.

The day was dark and gloomy. Little Anna was so scared, she kept trembling. She hugged her favorite teddy bear close to her chest. Her mom saw her trembling and tried to encourage her. "It's alright honey, don't worry," said her mom with a smile. Little Anna felt a bit better and followed her mom to school. But as soon as she stepped inside the school, things turned ugly. A group of kids were saying mean things to her. Anna started to cry and her mom tried to encourage her again. "Don't worry Anna, you can be strong," said her mom. But it was too late. Anna was too scared and she ran out of school. Her mom was worried, but she knew she had to let her go. Little Anna kept running until she tripped over an ugly rock. She hurt her knee and cried uncontrolably. Her mom ran to her and tried to encourage her one last time. "It's going to be alright Anna, don't worry," said her mom. But still, the bad ending was inevitable. Anna never returned to school again.
