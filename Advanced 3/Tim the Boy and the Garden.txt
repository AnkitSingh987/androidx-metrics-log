Title: Tim the Boy and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a fearful boy who was scared to do many things. One day, his mom asked him to help her water the plants in their garden. Tim was worried, but he wanted to help his mom. Tim went to the faucet to get water for the plants. He was scared to turn the faucet on, but his mom told him it was okay. She allowed him to turn the faucet on and off by himself. Tim felt happy and proud that he could do it. After they watered the plants, Tim was not so fearful anymore. He learned that trying new things can be fun and not so scary. The moral of the story is: don't be afraid to try new things because they might be fun!

Once upon a time, there was a normal dolphin named Dolly. Dolly loved to study the sea. She liked to learn about all the fish and plants in the water. One day, Dolly met a friendly fish named Finn. Finn said, "Hi, Dolly! What are you doing?" Dolly replied, "I am studying the sea. I like to learn about everything in the water. Do you want to study with me?" Finn was happy and said, "Yes, I want to study with you!" Dolly and Finn swam together, studying the sea. They saw big fish, small fish, and pretty plants. They had lots of fun learning about their home. And from that day on, Dolly and Finn were the best of friends, studying the sea and having fun together.

Anna and Ben are playing in the garden. They have a big bucket of water and some cups. They want to soak each other with water. "Ben, I will soak you first!" Anna says. She fills her cup with water and runs towards Ben. "No, I will soak you first!" Ben says. He fills his cup with water and runs towards Anna. They splash each other with water and laugh. They are wet and happy. But then they see Mom coming. She is holding a big towel and a basket of fruit. "Anna, Ben, come here!" Mom says. "You are soaking wet! You need to dry off and have a snack." Anna and Ben run to Mom. She wraps them in the towel and gives them each a banana. "Thank you, Mom!" Anna and Ben say. Mom smiles and peels a banana for herself. She holds it in her palm and takes a bite. "Be careful, Mom!" Anna says. "There is a monkey behind you!" Anna and Ben point to the tree. There is a monkey swinging from a branch. It is looking at Mom's banana. Mom turns around and sees the monkey. She is surprised and drops her banana. The monkey jumps down and grabs the banana. It runs away with a squeak. "Hey, that's my banana!" Mom says. Anna and Ben giggle. They think the monkey is funny. "Mom, can we have another banana?" Anna and Ben ask. "Sure, you can have another banana," Mom says. "But this time, hold it tight and don't let the monkey take it." Anna and Ben nod. They take another banana and hold it tight. They eat their bananas and watch the monkey. They are careful and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a big armchair. The armchair was a calm and quiet place. A little girl named Lily liked to sit in the armchair and read her books. One day, Lily found a small bow on the floor. She picked it up and put it on her armchair. She thought it was pretty and it made her armchair look nice. She went to bed and was excited to sit in her armchair the next day. The next day, Lily woke up and ran to her armchair. She saw that the bow was gone! She looked all around the room, but she could not find it. Then, she saw her cat, Mittens, playing with the bow. Lily laughed and said, "Mittens, you took my bow!" She took the bow from Mittens and put it back on her armchair. Now, Lily and Mittens had a fun game to play together.
