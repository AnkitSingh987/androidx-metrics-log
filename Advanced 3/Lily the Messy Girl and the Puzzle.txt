Title: Lily the Messy Girl and the Puzzle

Once upon a time, there was a messy girl named Lily. She liked to play in the mud and make a big mess. One day, Lily wanted to complete a puzzle with her mom. They sat down and started to put the pieces together. As they were working on the puzzle, something unexpected happened. A big wind came through the window and blew all the puzzle pieces away! Lily and her mom were sad, but they decided to look for the pieces outside. While looking for the puzzle pieces, they found a big, shiny treasure box. They opened the box and saw that it was full of new, clean toys! Lily and her mom were so happy. They played with the new toys and forgot all about the messy puzzle. The wind had brought them a fun surprise!

Once upon a time, two children were playing together. Jack was four years old, and Sandy was three. They were playing pass with a ball in the garden. They were both laughing and having lots of fun. Jack said to Sandy, "Let's play pass with the mail instead of the ball!" Sandy was very excited and shouted, "Yes!" So Jack and Sandy passed the mail, back and forth to each other. They were both so lively, running and laughing and having an amazing time. Suddenly, Jack noticed that the mail was torn. He said to Sandy, "You should be more careful when you pass things, Sandy. See how torn it is now?" Sandy nodded and said, "Yes, I will be more careful. We should always be careful when we pass things, since we need to take care of what we have." Jack and Sandy hugged each other and continued to have a great time. They learned a valuable lesson that day - Always Not Pass things carelessly!

One day, a little duck named Ducky went to the pond. He loved to splash in the water. It was a sunny day and the water felt so good. Ducky saw his friend, Froggy, sitting on a rock. "Hi, Froggy!" Ducky said. "Come splash with me!" Froggy jumped into the water with a big splash. They had so much fun playing together. They swam and splashed all day long. Then, something incredible happened. The water in the pond started to change color. It turned into a rainbow! Ducky and Froggy were so surprised. They had never seen a rainbow pond before. They decided to stay and play in the incredible rainbow water. They knew it was a special day they would never forget.

Once upon a time, there was a little red bird. The bird lived in a big tree with many branches. The bird loved to fly from branch to branch, singing happy songs. One day, the bird found a big red fruit on a branch. The fruit was so sweet and yummy. The bird loved the fruit so much, it did not want to share with anyone. The bird wanted to keep the fruit all to itself. But, the bird did not eat the fruit fast enough. The fruit started to spoil. It turned brown and yucky. The bird was sad. It learned that it is better to share with friends, so nothing gets spoiled.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play and sing together. One day, Anna's mom says, "We are going to the opera today. Do you want to come, Anna?" Anna is happy. She likes the opera. She says, "Yes, mom. I want to come." Ben hears Anna and her mom. He feels jealous. He likes the opera too, but his mom and dad are busy. He says, "Anna, can I come with you to the opera?" Anna's mom says, "I'm sorry, Ben. We only have two tickets. Maybe another time." Anna and her mom go to the opera. They see people in nice clothes. They hear beautiful music. They clap and smile. Anna thinks the opera is fun. Ben stays at home. He is sad and angry. He does not want to play or sing. He thinks the opera is not fair. Later, Anna and her mom come back. They see Ben sitting on the porch. Anna says, "Hi, Ben. We had a good time at the opera. Do you want to hear about it?" Ben says, "No, I don't. I don't like the opera. I don't like you." Anna is surprised and hurt. She says, "Ben, why are you so mean? I thought you were my friend." Ben says, "You are not my friend. You left me alone. You did not share the opera with me." Anna's mom says, "Ben, I understand that you are jealous, but that is not a reason to be rude. Anna did not do anything wrong. She wanted to share the opera with you, but we could not take you. Maybe next time, we can all go together. Would you like that?" Ben thinks for a moment. He feels sorry for being mean. He says, "Yes, I would like that. I'm sorry, Anna. I'm sorry, mom. I was jealous, but I still like the opera. And I still like you." Anna smiles and hugs Ben. She says, "It's okay, Ben. I forgive you. You are still my friend. And I still like you too." Anna and Ben go inside. They play and sing together. They are happy again.
