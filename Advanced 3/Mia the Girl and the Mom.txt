Title: Mia the Girl and the Mom

One day, a little girl named Mia went to a big store with her mom. They were looking for new furniture for their home. Mia saw a big white chair that she liked a lot. She said, "Mom, look at that white chair! Can we get it, please?" Her mom looked at the chair and said, "Yes, let's listen to what the store man says about it." So they went to find the store man. When they found him, he was talking to another family. Mia and her mom waited and listened. The store man said, "This white chair is very nice and soft. It's perfect for your home." Mia's mom liked what she heard. She bought the white chair for their home. When they got home, Mia's mom put the new white chair in their living room. Mia was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Mom! I love our new white chair." Mia and her mom sat on the chair and read stories together. They loved their new furniture.

Sally was three and loved visiting the library. Every morning, her mum would take her and today was no different. When Sally arrived, she was excited. She looked around amongst all the books, trying to decide which one to borrow. “Look!” She exclaimed, “There’s this one!”. She pointed to a book. “I’m sorry, sweetheart,” replied her mum. “That one’s not available today.” Undeterred, Sally kept exploring and eventually found another book. “I like this one,” she said and ran up to the librarian to borrow it. The librarian was very friendly and glad to help Sally. After checking it was available for loan, she handed it to her and gave her a big smile. Sally and her mum left the library with the book in hand. Both of them couldn’t wait to read it!

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a rare tiger. This tiger was not like other tigers. It had blue and white stripes. The rare tiger was very special. One day, the rare tiger was playing with his friends. They were playing a game called "strike the tree." All the animals took turns to hit the tree. The rare tiger was very good at the game. He could strike the tree very hard. In the end, the rare tiger won the game. All his friends clapped and cheered for him. They were very happy for the rare tiger. They all had a fun day playing in the forest.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a big tree with lots of red berries. She wanted to pick some, but she noticed a small bird on a branch. The bird was sad because it could not find any food. Lucy thought it was rude to take the berries all for herself, so she decided to share with the bird. Lucy picked some berries and gave them to the bird. The bird was very happy and ate them all up. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to grow bigger and bigger. Lucy was very surprised and a little scared. But then, the big bird smiled at Lucy and said, "Thank you for sharing the berries with me. I am a magic bird, and because you were not rude and shared, I will give you a special gift." The bird touched Lucy with its wing, and suddenly she could fly! Lucy and the magic bird became best friends and had many fun adventures together.

One day, a big dog and a small cat lived near a long pole. They did not like each other. They would fight every day. The dog would bark and the cat would hiss. One sunny day, the dog was very careless. He ran too fast and hit the pole. The dog cried and the cat laughed. The dog felt sad and hurt. The cat saw the dog was sad. She stopped laughing and went to help the dog. They stopped fighting and became friends. They played near the pole and had fun together.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Elle and Max. Elle was three and Max was four. They were playing together in the park. "Let's play hide and seek!" Elle said to Max. Max nodded and said, "I'll hide, you can come find me." So Elle began to run around the park, looking for Max. Suddenly, she saw some money. It was incredible! Elle was so happy and she wanted to pick it up. But Elle remembered what her mommy had said. She remembered that it was wrong to take money that wasn't hers. So Elle didn't choose to run after the money. Instead, she ran to find Max. Elle found Max hiding by the slide and they ran around the park and laughed together. At the end of the day, Elle knew she had made the right decision. She knew it felt better to play with friends than to take someone else's money. That evening, Elle told her mommy what had happened, and her mommy smiled. The moral of the story is that it is better to do the right thing, even when it's hard. True friends are more valuable than money.
