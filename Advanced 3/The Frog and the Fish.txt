Title: The Frog and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little frog. The frog lived in a pond. The frog loved to cook. One day, the frog met a big fish. The fish was hungry. The frog said, "I can cook for you." The fish said, "Yes, please!" The frog cooked a spicy bug soup. The fish tasted it and said, "Yum! Thank you, frog!" They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a mysterious girl called Molly. Molly lived high in the sky above the earth. One day Molly had a big idea. She wanted to give everything on earth a special name. Molly started to find names for the trees, the clouds and the rivers. The earth began to fill with beautiful names. Then she thought of something even bigger - to name every single creature living on the earth. Molly excitedly began to give names to the bears, the cats and the birds. She was having so much fun. But then a terrible storm broke out and the earth began to shake. Molly was very scared. In the chaos Molly forgot all the names she had just created. Molly felt very sad. Then she realized that she could use her mysterious powers to create new names for the creatures. She started to think of new words and give the creatures a new name. Everyone was so happy and Molly learnt an important lesson. No matter what happens, names never have to end.

Once upon a time there were two best friends: Anna and John. They lived near each other and often used to visit each other's houses. One day, John said to Anna, "Let's go do something special. I want to spoil you!" Anna thought for a moment and then said, "Let's go visit the cheap candy store!" Anna knew that John always liked candy. So, Anna and John got ready and went to the cheap candy store. They were amazed when they saw all the colorful candy and small toys. They both wanted to buy something, so they each got a small toy and lots of candy. They enjoyed their visit to the cheap candy store very much.

Jack wanted to get a trophy. He went to the store and he saw a beautiful trophy. It was very shiny and had a pretty design on it. He asked his mom if he could get it. His mom said yes and Jack was so happy. He paid for the trophy with his own money that he had been saving. When he got home, he put it in his room carefully. He looked at it every day and smiled. He was proud of his beautiful trophy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He wanted to complete his drawing of a big tree. He took his crayons and paper and sat under the tree. It was a fun day. Soon, a big dog came near Tim. The dog was a threat. Tim was scared. He said, "Go away, dog!" The dog did not go away. Tim's friend, Sam, saw the dog and came to help. Sam said, "Shoo, dog!" The dog went away. Tim was happy. Tim and Sam sat under the tree. They drew pictures together. Tim did not think the day was boring anymore. They had a great time and went home with their drawings.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They climbed a big hill. When they got to the top, Tim saw a beautiful view. He saw trees, flowers, and a river. Tim said, "Mom, look at the pretty view!" His mom looked and smiled. She was very compassionate and always listened to Tim. She replied, "Yes, Tim, it is very pretty. I am happy we came here today." Then, they saw a little girl crying. Tim asked her, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I lost my toy." Tim wanted to help. He looked around and found the toy under a bush. The little girl was so happy. She replied, "Thank you, Tim!" They all played together and had a great day.

Jack was in his grandparents' house looking for something. He had to find it quick! Grandma said it was important. He had already checked all the usual spots: the sofa, the cupboard and the wardrobe. But he still hadn't found it. He thought to himself, he had to search the ceiling! So Jack searched high and low, looking up and all around the ceiling. "Ah ha!" he said. There it was, tucked away in a corner, held in place with little wooden blocks. It was a cheap plastic toy. Jack was so happy, he let out a huge cheer. Grandma came in to find out what the noise was. She saw Jack holding the toy in the air and she smiled kindly. "I am glad that you found it my dear. I knew you would," she said. Jack was surprised at how Grandma seemed to know about the toy but he shrugged and just smiled. Grandma went to make dinner and Jack went to play with his toy. He never knew about how Grandma had put the blocks up there in the corner of the ceiling to keep the toy safe from being found too easily!
