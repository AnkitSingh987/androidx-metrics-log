Title: The Story of the Rock and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big, round, ugly rock. It sat in a park where kids would come and play. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the rock and said, "Ew, that's an ugly rock!" His friend, Sue, came over and looked at the rock too. She said, "Yes, it is ugly, but it's also round and smooth. Let's sit on it and talk." So, they sat on the ugly rock and talked about their day. As they sat, a tiny bug came out from under the rock. Tim and Sue watched as it moved around. Tim said, "That bug is ugly too, just like the rock." Sue smiled and said, "But it's still a part of our world, and we should be kind to it." Tim thought about what Sue said and decided she was right. From that day on, Tim learned to be kind to all things, even if they were ugly. And the big, round, ugly rock became their favorite place to sit and talk.

Sammy was very excited. It was his birthday and he was getting a special present. His parents said it was something he had always wanted, but he didn't know what it could be. When he opened it, he found a fan! This fan was the most special fan that anyone could get; it even had sparkles. Sammy was delighted and excited to show it to his friends. When his friends saw the fan, they all got very jealous. They hadn't gotten something as special as Sammy and couldn't believe it. Their jealousy was soon replaced with admiration, though. They had all heard about the fan and were eager to try it. They wanted to feel how warm and cool it was. They all wanted to be like Sammy and have the special fan. Sammy smiled at his friends. He had always believed that he would have this fan someday and it had finally come true. His birthday was the best day ever!

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. He loved to nap all day long. One sunny day, Tom woke up and saw a little bird outside his window. The bird was singing a happy song. "Hi, bird!" said Tom. "What is your name?" The bird replied, "My name is Bob. I was sent by my friends to sing for you." Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, Bob. Your song is very nice." After that, Tom and Bob became good friends. They played together every day. Tom was not so sleepy anymore, and they were both very happy.

Timmy was very excite. Today was his birthday and he knew his mother had a big surprise for him. He jumped out of bed and ran to the living room, where he saw a big box with a bow on it. "Happy birthday, Timmy!" his mother said, hugging him. "Do you want to open your present?" "Yes, yes, yes!" Timmy said, tearing off the paper. Inside the box, he found a soft and fluffy teddy bear. He loved it and hugged it tight. "Thank you, mommy! He is so cute!" "You're welcome, sweetie. He is your new friend. You can name him whatever you want," his mother said, smiling. Timmy thought for a moment. He looked at the teddy bear's brown fur and black nose. He had an idea. "I will name him Lazy," he said. "Lazy? Why Lazy?" his mother asked, curious. "Because he looks like he likes to sleep a lot, like me," Timmy said, giggling. "And because he is not a busy bear, like daddy." His mother laughed. "That's a funny name, Timmy. I like it. Lazy is a lucky bear to have you as his owner. Now, do you want some cake and ice cream?" "Yes, please!" Timmy said, holding Lazy in his arms. He followed his mother to the kitchen, where a yummy chocolate cake was waiting for him. He was very happy. It was the best birthday ever.

John was a very active 3 year old. He loved to dance and nothing made him happier than hopping, twirling and spinning around. One day, his mom gave him a big present - it was a big map of the world. John was so excited by the map that he immediately started dancing around it. John's brother Billy saw the map, looked at John and frowned. "That's so unfair, I want a map too," Billy said. John could see that Billy was jealous of him. "You can use mine," John said. Billy couldn't believe it. He smiled and started dancing around the map with John. They twirled and hopped and spun for hours, until it was time for bed. They settled in for the night, dreaming of all the places they wanted to go to on their big map.
