Title: The Body and the Toys

Once there was a body. It was small and belonged to an anxious girl named Abbie. She was always worried about things, and she had a lot of toys and things to sort. Every day she would take out two toys and sort them. Is this the car? Is this the doll? It usually took Abbie a long time to decide what toy went in what pile. One day, Abbie's mom came over and saw her sorting the toys. "That's a lot to do, Abbie," she said. Abbie nodded sadly, because she was so anxious it made sorting the toys hard. "I have an idea," her mom said. "Let's take one of these toys, and you can put it over here without sorting." So Abbie and her mom picked one toy and put it in a special place. Then Abbie was no longer anxious and was able to sort all her toys without worry. She even had time to play with them too. And every day after that, she made sure to put one toy in the special place so she wouldn't get anxious while sorting.

Once upon a time, there was a fast rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Remy saw his friend, Tim the turtle, trying to supply food for his family. Tim was very slow, so it was hard for him to get food. Remy had an idea. He said, "Tim, I can help you! I am fast, and I can get food for you. You can watch me run and learn how to be fast too!" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, please help me, Remy!" So, Remy ran fast and supplied food for Tim's family. Tim watched Remy run and tried to learn how to be fast. They all ate and played together. Remy and Tim became best friends and helped each other every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to stretch her arms high up in the sky. She would pretend she was a tall tree, reaching for the sun. One day, Sue found a folder on the ground. The folder was weak and old, but it had pretty pictures on it. Sue took the folder to her mom. Her mom opened the folder and saw a big map inside. The map showed a secret path to a fun park. Sue and her mom were so happy! They decided to follow the map and find the park. As they walked, Sue kept stretching her arms like a tree. They finally reached the end of the path, but instead of a fun park, there was a big lake. Sue and her mom were surprised, but they laughed. They had a fun day playing by the lake, and Sue kept stretching her arms, pretending to be a tree by the water.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. She saw a red jacket that she really wanted. She asked her mom, "Can I have the red jacket, please?" Her mom said, "We will see, let's look at the price." At the store, another little girl named Emma also wanted the red jacket. Emma and Lily both wanted the same jacket, but there was only one left. They both looked at each other, not happy. Lily's mom saw that the red jacket was on sale. She told Lily, "You can have the red jacket, but you need to share with Emma." Lily did not want to share, but she knew it was the right thing to do. Lily and Emma both received the red jacket. They took turns wearing it and became good friends. They learned that sharing can be fun and make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a black bike that he loved very much. He rode his bike every day, using the pedals to go fast or slow. One day, Tim saw a lot of pretty flowers in a field. He decided to gather some flowers for his mom. As Tim was picking the flowers, a little girl named Sue came by. Sue was sad because she did not have a bike. Tim wanted to help Sue, so he let her ride his black bike. Sue was very happy, and she used the pedals to go fast and slow, just like Tim. After playing together, Tim and Sue became good friends. They gathered more flowers for their moms and took turns riding the black bike. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can make you both happy and create new friendships.
