Title: Hoppy the Frog and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a frog named Hoppy. Hoppy was quick and loved to jump around. He lived in a warm pond by himself. One day, Hoppy decided to take a nap. As he napped, a big bird came down and took him away from his pond. Hoppy was so scared he wanted to go home. The bird asked, "Where do you want to go?" Hoppy cried and said, "I want to keep warm in my pond." The bird said, "OK, I'll take you back home." So the bird flew Hoppy back to his pond. Hoppy promised to himself to be quick and stay away from birds!

One day, a little girl named Lily went to school. She was very happy because she had a new fit dress to wear. Lily's school was a big, fun place where she played with her friends and learned new things. At school, Lily and her friends were playing when they saw smoke. "Oh no! What is that?" asked Lily's friend, Tom. "I don't know, but it smells like something is burning," said Lily. They went to tell their teacher, Miss Jane, about the smoke. Miss Jane said, "Thank you for telling me, kids. Let's all go outside and be safe." All the children went outside, and the firefighters came to put out the fire. The school was safe, and Lily and her friends were happy. They learned that day how important it is to be careful and tell an adult when something is wrong.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a fan. It was a fancy fan with pretty colors. Sue loved her fan very much. She used it all the time to keep cool in the hot sun. One day, Sue went to the park to play. She brought her fancy fan with her. Sue played with her friends and had lots of fun. But she forgot her fan on a bench. Sue did not remember her fan when it was time to go home. Sue came back to the park the next day to find her fancy fan. But it was gone. Sue was very sad. She looked everywhere, but she could not find her fan. Then, she saw a big bird in the sky. The bird had her fancy fan in its beak. The bird flew far away with Sue's fan, and she never saw it again.

One day, Sam went to a store with his Dad. Sam was very excited, but he was also curious to know what surprise his Dad had in store for him. Suddenly, Dad stopped in front of a velvet box. Sam couldn't control his excitement, he couldn't even imagine what was inside. But Dad would not answer his queries, he just smiled and opened the box to introduce his surprise - a toy car! Sam was so delighted and he ran around the store with the car making all kinds of engine sounds. But then suddenly Dad realized that Sam was leaving the store without him. He tried to call Sam back, but Sam was too stubborn to listen and kept playing with the car. Finally, Dad was able to get Sam back in the store. He was happy to be reunited with Dad, and he promised to never be so stubborn again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, expensive tent. It was very pretty. A family wanted to go camping in the woods. They took the tent with them. The tent was very heavy. The family complained, "This tent is too heavy!" But they still brought it to the woods. In the woods, they put up the tent. It was very big and nice. They were happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A strong wind came and blew the tent away. The family was sad. They had to sleep under the stars. They did not complain anymore. They learned their lesson. The moral of the story is: do not complain about what you have, because you might lose it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very bored. He did not want to play with his toys. He did not want to watch television. Tim wanted to do something fun. Tim's mom saw that he was bored. She said, "Tim, why don't you go outside and play with your friends?" Tim thought that was a good idea. He put on his shoes and went outside. Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue was playing with a ball. Tim asked, "Can I play too?" Sue said, "Yes, let's play catch!" They played catch for a long time. Then, they got very thirsty. Tim and Sue went inside to drink some water. After they drank water, they were not thirsty anymore. They went back outside to play some more. Tim was not bored anymore.
