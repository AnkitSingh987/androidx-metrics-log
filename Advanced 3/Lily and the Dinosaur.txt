Title: Lily and the Dinosaur

Lily likes to show her toys to her friends. She has many toys, but her favorite is a big dinosaur. She says it is a history toy, because dinosaurs lived a long time ago. She knows their names and sounds, and likes to pretend they are wild and scary. One day, she brings her dinosaur to school. She wants to show it to her teacher and her classmates. She puts it on her desk and waits for the show time. She is very excited and proud of her toy. But when the show time comes, she sees that another boy has the same dinosaur as her. His name is Ben, and he also likes history toys. He says his dinosaur is better than hers, because it can roar and move its tail. He makes it roar at Lily's dinosaur, and says it is more wild and scary. Lily feels sad and angry. She thinks Ben is mean and rude. She does not want to share her toy with him. She grabs her dinosaur and hugs it. She says it is her dinosaur, and it is the best dinosaur in the world. The teacher sees them and comes to help. She says they should not fight over toys, because toys are for fun and learning. She says they can both show their dinosaurs, and tell the class what they know about them. She says they can also play together, and make their dinosaurs be friends. Lily and Ben listen to the teacher. They think for a moment, and then nod. They decide to show their dinosaurs, and share their history facts. They also decide to play together, and make their dinosaurs roar and move together. They find out they have a lot in common, and they become friends. They learn that toys are more fun when they are shared.

Once upon a time, in a far away land, there lived a big, selfish frog. This frog loved to grab all the toys and not share with others. All the little animals in the land were sad because they wanted to play with the toys too. One day, a small bird came to the land. The bird saw the selfish frog and said, "Why don't you share your toys with the other animals? It is not nice to grab all the toys for yourself." The frog just laughed and said, "I like having all the toys. They are mine!" But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the toys away! The selfish frog was very sad and started to cry. The small bird saw this and said, "Don't worry, we can all work together to find the toys and share them." The frog agreed, and all the animals in the land helped to find the toys. In the end, the selfish frog learned to share and play with others. The animals in the land were happy and became friends with the frog. They all played together, and the land was a happy place for everyone.

Lily and Tom were playing with their toys in the living room. They liked to pretend they were pirates and explorers, and they had a big map and a toy phone. They heard a loud noise outside. It was a storm. The wind was blowing hard and the rain was falling fast. "Let's call Mommy and Daddy and tell them we are safe," Lily said. She picked up the toy phone and pressed a button. It made a ring sound. She waited for someone to answer, but no one did. "Maybe they are busy," Tom said. "Maybe they are driving home in the storm." Lily tried again. She pressed the button and the phone rang. But still no one answered. She started to feel scared. She hugged Tom and said, "I hope they are okay. I hope they come home soon." Tom hugged her back and said, "Don't worry, Lily. They will come home. They love us. They always come home." They heard another loud noise. It was the door. It opened and Mommy and Daddy came in. They were wet and tired, but they smiled when they saw Lily and Tom. "Hi, sweeties. We are home. We are sorry we didn't answer the phone. The storm was so harsh, it made the real phone stop working. But we are here now. We are safe. And we are so happy to see you." Lily and Tom ran to them and gave them a big hug. They felt warm and happy. They told them about their game and their toy phone. Mommy and Daddy laughed and said, "You are so clever and brave. You are our little pirates and explorers. And we are so proud of you."
