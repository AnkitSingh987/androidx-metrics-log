Title: Tom and Lily and the Box

One day, Tom and Lily wanted to play pirates. They put on hats and scarves and found a big box. They said the box was their ship and they sailed on the sea. They pretended to look for treasure and fight other pirates. "Ahoy, matey!" Tom said. "Do you see any gold?" "Arrr, not yet," Lily said. "But I see a big island. Maybe there is treasure there." They jumped out of the box and ran to the island. It was really a pile of pillows and blankets. They dug and dug and found a shiny necklace. "Wow, we are rich!" Tom said. "This is the best treasure ever!" They put the necklace around Lily's neck and danced and sang. They were very happy. But then, they heard a loud noise. It was their mom. She came into the room and saw the mess. "What are you doing?" she asked. "It's time for bed. You have to sleep now." Tom and Lily looked at each other. They did not want to sleep. They wanted to play more. "But mom, we are pirates," Tom said. "Pirates don't sleep. They have adventures." Mom smiled. She knew they had fun. But she also knew they were tired. "Pirates do sleep, sometimes," she said. "They need to rest and dream. And you are very brave pirates. You can sleep anywhere, even on a ship." She helped them make a tent with the blankets and pillows. She gave them each a hug and a kiss. "Good night, my brave pirates," she said. "I love you." Tom and Lily crawled into the tent. They snuggled with each other and closed their eyes. They felt cozy and safe. They still had the necklace. They smiled and whispered, "Good night, matey." They fell asleep and dreamed of more pirate adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Rachel. She had a wonderful secret. She was so excited, she just had to share it. “Mummy, I have something amazing to show you!” She exclaimed. Rachel’s mum smiled at her little girl and asked her to unpack her surprise. Rachel jumped up with glee, ran over to her bedroom and disappeared for a few minutes. When Rachel returned, she was carrying a large box. She began to unpack it, and suddenly the room was filled with a wonderful smell. Rachel had made a delicious cake and iced it with her favourite colours. “What a wonderful surprise!” Rachel’s mum said. “Wherever did you learn to bake such a delicious cake, you clever girl?” Rachel’s face lit up with a big smile. “I have a great baking teacher!” she said. Rachel’s mum was filled with wonder. But suddenly, the room got very quiet. Rachel looked sad and her face was miserable. “What’s wrong, sweetie?” Rachel’s mum asked. Rachel sighed. “My baking teacher is at home, but I can’t see her or hug her, because she lives far away.”

Once upon a time in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a terrible problem. His copper toy was stuck in a twist. He tried to pull it but it would not come out. One day, a big dog came to Tim's house. The dog saw the copper toy in the twist. The dog barked and jumped. Tim was scared. He thought the dog would take his toy away. But the dog did not take the toy. The dog pushed the twist with his nose. The twist opened, and the copper toy was free! Tim was so happy. He hugged the big dog and they became best friends. The terrible problem was gone, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Momo. Momo loved cheese more than anything. He thought cheese was so tasty. One day, Momo saw a big piece of cheese in a house. He wanted to join the other mice and eat the cheese. Momo and his friends went into the house. They saw the cheese on a table. Momo and his friends were very happy. They climbed on the table and started to eat the tasty cheese. They ate and ate until they were full. But then, a big cat came into the room. The cat saw the mice and wanted to catch them. Momo and his friends tried to run away, but they were too full from eating the cheese. The cat caught Momo and his friends. They were very sad. The moral of the story is not to be greedy. If Momo and his friends had not eaten so much cheese, they could have run away from the cat.
