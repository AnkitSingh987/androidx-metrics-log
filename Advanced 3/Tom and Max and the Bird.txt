Title: Tom and Max and the Bird

One day, a cat named Tom and a dog named Max were playing in the park. They saw a big bottle of ink. Tom said, "Let's play with the ink!" Max was scared, but he could not resist. They opened the ink bottle and started to play. They made many funny pictures on the ground. Suddenly, a spicy smell came from the bottle. Max said, "What is that spicy smell? I don't like it!" A big bird flew down from the sky. The bird said, "That is my spicy ink! I use it to make my bird art!" Tom and Max were surprised. They said sorry to the bird. The bird was nice and let them keep playing with the spicy ink. From that day on, they all made art together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was an open field. In it, there were lots of animals. A little rabbit hopped out of the field, and said, "Hi, everyone! It's so nice to be out in the open! Let's play!" The other animals all agreed and came out of the field. They began to play a game, and had lots of fun. But there was one animal who wasn't joining in. It was a little beaver. The rabbit asked, "Why aren't you playing with us, little beaver?" The beaver replied, "I don't know how to play the game." The rabbit said, "It's easy! Just keep repeating the same thing we do, and you'll catch on. Come on, join us!" The beaver smiled, and hopped into the game. He repeated the same actions as the other animals, and soon learned how to play. The beaver had lots of fun, and the animals all laughed and cheered. They played until sunset, and then said goodbye. The rabbit said, "Good bye, little beaver! Come back soon!" The beaver nodded, and hopped away. The other animals said goodbye too, and watched him until he disappeared in the field. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a curious little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. She saw a big jar of candy and wanted some. Her mom said, "Lily, if you are good, I will provide you with some candy." As they walked around the store, Lily was very good. She did not touch anything and stayed close to her mom. When they got to the checkout, Lily's mom said, "You were very good, so I will provide you with some candy." Lily was very happy. But when they opened the jar of candy at home, something unexpected happened. Instead of candy, there were little toys inside! Lily was surprised, but she thought the toys were fun too. She said, "Thank you for the toys, Mom!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to imagine big adventures. One day, Tim found a map in his room. The map was very important because it showed the way to a big treasure. Tim decided to follow the map. He walked and walked until he saw a big tree. The map said the treasure was under the tree. Tim was very excited. He started to dig and dig. After a while, he found a big box. Tim was so happy! But when Tim opened the box, he found that it was empty. The treasure was gone! Tim was very sad. He learned that not everything we imagine comes true. Sometimes, we need to be careful and think before we start an adventure.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to a parade with her mom. Mia loved parades. She liked the big balloons and the fun music. Mia held her mom's hand so she would not get lost. At the parade, Mia saw a big dog. The dog looked careless and happy. Mia wanted to talk to the dog. She asked her mom, "Can I talk to the dog?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Mia went to the dog and said, "Hi, dog!" The dog looked at her and said, "Hi, Mia!" Mia was very surprised. Dogs do not talk! But this dog did. Mia and the dog talked and had fun at the parade.
