Title: The in Calm Pond and the Fish

Once upon a time, a little fish lived in a calm pond. The little fish loved to swim and play with his friends all day. One day, the little fish saw a big, yummy bug near the water. He wanted to eat the bug, but it was too far away. The little fish had an idea. He saw a long stick near the pond. He thought, "If I strike the bug with the stick, it will fall into the water, and I can eat it." So, the little fish asked his friend, the frog, to help him. The frog jumped out of the water and struck the bug with the stick. But, something unexpected happened. When the frog struck the bug, it jumped and landed on the frog's head! The little fish and the frog laughed and laughed. They learned that even if things don't go as planned, you can still have fun and be happy with your friends. And that was the most important thing of all.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Jane. Jane wanted to catch a butterfly one day. She ran around outside and finally saw one flittering in the garden. She was so excited to catch it and said, “Oh, I see one! I will get it!” Jane crept slowly closer and outstretched her hand. Just then, the butterfly flew away. Jane was sad but then remembered something. Her daddy had told her that butterflies don’t like to be touched, so she had to be careful and watch the butterfly instead. Jane watched intently but the butterfly kept flitting away. Jane began to feel nervous as she was scared she would never catch it. Just then, Jane had an idea. She placed her hand very carefully near the butterfly and started to whisper something. Then, the butterfly landed softly in her palm. Jane was so happy. “See, Dad! I got it!” she said, proudly.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful girl named Sue. She loved to play with her friends and do math. She could count to ten and add two and two. Sue was very happy. One day, while Sue was playing with her friends, she found a magic wand. She waved it around and said, "Disappear!" All of a sudden, her friends disappeared! Sue was sad and scared. Sue knew she had to use her math skills to bring her friends back. She counted to three and waved the wand again. "One, two, three!" she said. And just like that, her friends reappeared! Sue and her friends were so happy. They played together and Sue knew she had a powerful gift with her math.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl and she loved to imagine. One day, she imagined a big pole in the sky. She looked up at the pole and imagined all the adventures that she could have with it. The pole was so tall and shiny. She could climb as high as she wanted and explore the unknown! She was so excited that all she wanted to do was play with the pole. But then, her mommy called out to her. She had to be obedient and help her mommy with the housework. No matter how much she wanted to, the pole would have to wait. The girl was disappointed, but she was obedient and she helped her mommy. When she finished the housework, she went back to the pole and imagined all the great things she could do with it. The girl never stopped dreaming about her pole and its adventures!

One day, a lovely bird named Blue went to play with his friend, a little fly. The fly was very good at balance. They liked to play together in the big park. Blue said to the fly, "Let's play a game! You balance on my beak and we will see how long you can stay without falling." The fly was happy and said, "Okay, let's try!" Blue and the fly played their game. The fly balanced on Blue's beak and did not fall. They laughed and had fun. Then, they went to find more friends to play with. They had a very nice day.
