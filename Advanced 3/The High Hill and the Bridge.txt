Title: The High Hill and the Bridge

Once upon a time there was a high hill. At the top of the hill was a tall castle. There were many bright windows and lots of pretty flags waving in the wind. One day, the king wanted to build a bridge to the top of the hill. He told the workers to include a cable for the bridge. The cable was long and strong, and it took lots of time to install. Finally, the cable was finished and the bridge was ready. It was very high and sparkly in the sunshine. Everyone cheered and clapped. The king and his family were so proud of their new bridge. Every day they used it to get to the top of the hill. In no time at all, a great many people crossed the bridge. They walked, skipped, and ran across the bridge and said hello to each other. The cable was part of a very special bridge for everyone to use and enjoy.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the woods. She saw many trees, birds, and pretty flowers. Mia liked to explore and find new things. As she walked, Mia stumbled on a large rock. She looked down and saw something shiny. It was a diamond! Mia was very excited and happy to find the diamond. Mia took the large diamond home to show her mom and dad. They were so proud of her. They put the diamond in a special box. Mia and her family always remembered the fun day in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was very small and she had bright blue eyes. Her name was Emma and she was three years old. One day, Emma was playing in the garden when she spotted something really special. It was a start. It was brilliant! Emma loved it and she decided to make it her very own. Emma decided to pick it up and take it inside so she could keep it safe. But when she went to pick it up, something happened. She suddenly heard a voice behind her. “No Emma, don’t pick it up!” said the voice. It was her mom. Emma's mom explained that the start was a butterfly, and that it was something very special. She said that they should leave it alone. Emma looked into her mom's eyes and smiled. She knew that her mom loved her and only wanted the best for her. Emma knew she would never take the start away, but she still loved it. And so she left it where it was and went back inside.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a pretty flower. The flower was about to bloom. A little rat lived near the flower. The rat liked the rich smell of the flower. One day, the rat said to the flower, "You are so pretty! I like how you smell." The flower smiled and said, "Thank you! I will bloom soon and look even more pretty." The next day, a big wind came and blew the flower away. The rat was sad. He did not know what to do. But then, he saw a small seed where the flower was. The rat knew what to do. He planted the seed and watered it every day. Soon, a new flower grew. It bloomed and was even more pretty than the first one. The rat and the new flower became best friends. They learned that when something bad happens, good things can come from it if you work hard and don't give up.

Once there was a little lamb named Lucy. She was very happy and loved to run and play. One day all of her friends were playing outside and Lucy wanted to join in. But she was too little and couldn’t keep up. She was getting sad and didn’t know what to do. Her mama saw her and said, “How about I make you something special?” Lucy was so excited and put on a big smile. “Yes please!” Mama grabbed some wool and started to make something. She worked hard for days, braiding the wool and sewing it together. Finally, she finished and handed something small to Lucy. It was a little car with wheels and was just the right size for Lucy. Lucy said, “Wow, thank you Mama!” She couldn’t wait to play with her new toy. She jumped in and started to drive around the yard, zooming and spinning around. All of her friends stopped playing and came over to watch. Lucy was so proud of her new car and the other animals wanted one too. Mama laughed and said, “Where did that hard work get me?” But she was happy to see how much fun Lucy was having. Lucy zoomed around all day and when night fell she tucked her car away until the next day.
