Title: Kitty the Cat and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play in the big yard. One day, Kitty saw something new in the yard. It was a big box with a hole. Kitty was curious and wanted to see what was inside. Kitty asked her friend, the bird, "What is that?" The bird replied, "Be careful, Kitty! That is a trap! Don't go near it!" Kitty looked at the trap and felt a little angry. She didn't like traps in her yard. Kitty had an idea to reveal what was inside. She found a long stick and poked the trap with it. The trap door opened and out came a little mouse. The mouse was happy to be free. Kitty and the mouse became friends and played together in the yard. They promised to always help each other stay away from traps.

Once upon a time, in a gloomy forest, there lived a bear named Bobo. One day, Bobo decided to go for a walk. He saw his friend, Bunny, sitting under a tree. Bunny looked sad. Bobo asked, "Why are you sad, Bunny?" Bunny said, "My back hurts. I need a massage to feel better." Bobo wanted to help his friend feel better, so he said, "I will find someone to give you a massage." Bobo walked and walked until he found a wise old owl. The owl knew how to give massages. Bobo asked the owl, "Will you help Bunny? She needs a massage." The owl agreed to help Bunny. Bobo and the owl went back to Bunny. The owl gave Bunny a massage, and Bunny felt much better. Bunny was happy and thanked Bobo and the owl. They all played and laughed together in the gloomy forest.

Once upon a time, there was a big aeroplane. The aeroplane was very happy. It loved to fly high in the sky. One day, the aeroplane wanted to do something fun. It thought, "I will decorate myself!" The happy aeroplane put on pretty colors and shiny things. It looked so nice. All the people on the ground looked up and said, "Wow! Look at that pretty aeroplane!" The aeroplane felt very proud and happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The shiny things started to fall off! They fell down, down, down to the ground. The people were surprised. They picked up the shiny things and made a big, pretty picture on the ground. The aeroplane looked down and saw the picture. It was a picture of the happy aeroplane! The aeroplane felt even happier now. It knew that even when things go wrong, something good can still happen.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They found a perfect spot to stay and play. Tim loved to run and jump on the soft green grass. He saw a big tree and wanted to play hide and seek with his mom. "Mom, let's play hide and seek!" said Tim. "You hide first, and I will find you!" His mom agreed and went to hide behind the big tree. Tim closed his eyes and started to count. When he opened his eyes, he was ready to find his mom. As Tim looked for his mom, a big dog came out from behind the tree. The dog wanted to play too. The dog barked, and it scared Tim. "Mom, help!" cried Tim. His mom came out from her hiding spot and hugged him. "Don't be scared, Tim. The dog just wants to play with us too." Tim smiled and they all played together. The big dog became a new friend, and it was a perfect day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved pizza very much. One day, Tim's mom made a big pizza for dinner. Tim was very happy. Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the pizza and felt envious. He wanted some pizza too. While Tim was not looking, Sam took a big piece of pizza and ate it quickly. When Tim saw that a piece of pizza was gone, he was sad and asked Sam if he took it. Sam said sorry and asked Tim to forgive him. Tim was a good friend and forgave Sam. They both shared the rest of the pizza and played happily together.
