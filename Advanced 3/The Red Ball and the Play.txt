Title: The Red Ball and the Play

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. It had a small handle. Two friends, Tom and Tim, found the ball in a distant park. They both wanted to play with it. They did not want to share. Tom and Tim began to quarrel. They pulled the ball from each other. The handle of the ball broke. They both felt sad. The ball rolled away and fell in a big hole. They could not play with it anymore. The friends learned a lesson. They should have shared the ball. Now, they have no ball to play with. They walked home with sad faces.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a big, safe house with her best friend, a little girl named Sue. They loved to play together every day. One sunny day, Kitty and Sue went to the park. In the park, they met a sad, little bird. The bird was lost and hungry. Kitty wanted to help the bird, so she offered the bird some of her cat food. The bird ate the food and felt much better. Then, Kitty and Sue took the bird back to their safe house. They all became good friends and played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a hot and sandy land, there was a pretty oasis. The oasis had tall trees, green grass, and a big pond. Many animals came to the oasis to drink water and rest. One day, a tired bunny came to the oasis. He was very thirsty and hungry. The bunny saw a yummy carrot growing near the water. He wanted to eat the carrot, so he cut it with his sharp teeth. After the bunny cut and ate the carrot, he felt much better. He was not tired anymore. He played with his friends at the oasis and had lots of fun. Then, they all took a nap under the tall trees. And they lived happily ever after.

Little Sally was planning a party and was very excited. She asked her mom, "Mommy, can I add my best friends to the party?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, of course!" So, Sally invited her very best friends and they all came to the party. Everyone was so friendly and they had lots of fun! They played games, laughed, and ate cake. At the end of the party, Sally's mom asked, "Sally, did you have a good time?" Sally nodded and smiled. She said, "I had the best time, and my party was especially great because all my friends were so friendly."

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play with dolls. They had many dolls, but their favorite was Anna, a famous singer doll who could sing and dance. They always took turns to play with Anna, but sometimes they fought over her. One day, Tom wanted to play with Anna, but Lily said it was her turn. Tom got angry and tried to grab Anna from Lily's hands. Lily held on tight and said, "No, Tom, it's my turn! You had Anna yesterday!" Tom and Lily pulled and pulled, and Anna's dress ripped. They both gasped and looked at Anna. She had a big hole in her dress, and a pin was sticking out of it. The pin had been inside the dress to make Anna sing, but now it was broken. Anna tried to sing, but she only made a loud noise. She sounded like a frog. Tom and Lily were shocked and sad. They loved Anna's voice, but now she couldn't sing anymore. They both started to cry and scream. "You broke Anna! You broke Anna!" they shouted at each other. They blamed each other for ripping Anna's dress and breaking her pin. Their mom heard them and came to see what was wrong. She saw Anna's dress and pin, and she was very angry. She took Anna away from them and said, "You two have been very naughty! You ruined your favorite doll! You can't play with her anymore! Go to your rooms and think about what you did!" Tom and Lily felt very sorry and ashamed. They wished they had not fought over Anna. They wished they had shared her nicely. They wished they could hear her sing again. But it was too late. Anna was gone, and they had no one to play with. They went to their rooms and cried. They had a very bad day.
