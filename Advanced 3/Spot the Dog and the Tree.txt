Title: Spot the Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a hot land, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very hot and wanted to find some shade. He looked around and saw a big tree with lots of shade. Spot ran to the tree to cool down. Under the tree, Spot met a small cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was also hot and feeling helpless. Spot said, "Let's find something cold to melt in our mouths." Fluffy agreed, and they went on a search for something cold. They found a small ice cream shop and went inside. Spot and Fluffy got big ice cream cones to melt in their mouths. But when they went back to the tree, the shade was gone. The sun had moved, and the tree's shade was now far away. Spot and Fluffy felt helpless and sad, as they sat in the hot sun with their melting ice cream cones.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. He liked to roar very loud. All the animals in the forest were scared of him. The lion was always restless. He would run and jump all day. One Sunday, the lion was very tired. He wanted to rest, but he could not. He was too restless. The lion saw a little rabbit. The rabbit was calm and quiet. The lion asked the rabbit, "Why are you so calm on Sunday?" The rabbit said, "Sunday is a day to rest and be with friends. It is good to be calm and enjoy the day." The lion thought about it and decided to try it. He sat down and did not roar. He spent the day with the rabbit and other animals. The lion learned that it is good to be calm and enjoy time with friends. He did not roar so much anymore. The forest was a happier place. And the lion was not so restless. The moral of the story is: it is good to take time to rest and be with friends.

Once there was a dry penguin. He was happily walking near the shoreline when he decided to strike a small rock. He hit it so hard, it flew out of his way and into the water. The penguin looked at where the rock had gone and saw something new. In the water was a small boat, with a person in it. The penguin thought the person in the boat was so nice, so he waved. The person in the boat waved back and said hello. Then, the penguin swam to the boat and climbed in. The person was so happy to see the penguin that he gave it a funny toy. The penguin was thrilled! He played with the toy for hours and kept it safe, even when he was walking around the shoreline. The penguin would never forget that day. He had made a new friend and found a special toy. The dry penguin was happy indeed.

Jim was about to have a lot of fun. He put on his most attractive outfit and ran outside. He wanted to pretend to be something he had never been before. When Jim got outside he saw his friends playing together. He said, “Hi everyone! What are we pretending to be today?” His friends giggled and said, “Let's pretend to be whales! We can all make funny sounds and move like whales.” Jim thought this sounded like a lot of fun. He put his hands on his stomach and started to “breathe” like a whale. His friends laughed and joined in. All of a sudden, Jim heard a loud noise. He stopped what he was doing and looked up. In the sky he saw a huge whale flying by! Everyone gasped. Jim knew his whale pretending had paid off.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a bathrobe. He loved his bathrobe very much. It was soft and warm. Tim wore it every day after his bath. One day, Tim's mom had to mail a letter. She was angry because she could not find her stamp. Tim wanted to help his mom. He took off his bathrobe and put the letter inside it. He thought the bathrobe would keep the letter safe. But when Tim went outside to mail the letter, a big wind came. The wind took the bathrobe and the letter away. Tim was sad and his mom was still angry. They never found the bathrobe or the letter again. The end.
