Title: Jenny the Jolly Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a jolly little girl named Jenny. She liked to run around and play outside all the time. One day she went to the park with her mommy and wanted to go for a jog. Mommy said "Not today darling, maybe next week". Jenny didn't want to wait so she requested a jog right away. Mommy smiled and said "Ok, if you are jolly enough, then let's go for a jog". Jenny got so excited she started bouncing all around the park. They went for a nice jog, and afterwards, Jenny was so happy and out of breath. She ran back to Mommy and said "That was so much fun!". Mommy laughed and said "It sure was. How about we jog again next week?". Jenny nodded and gave her Mommy a big hug. She knew that if she was jolly enough, Mommy always found a way to make her request happen!

Anna liked to help Mommy in the kitchen. She liked to order the plates and cups and put them in the dishwasher. She felt like a big girl when she did that. One day, Anna was ordering the dishes after lunch. She saw a shiny knife on the counter. She wanted to put it in the dishwasher too. She reached for the knife, but it was very sharp. It cut her finger and made it bleed. Anna cried out loud. It hurt a lot. Mommy heard Anna's cry and ran to the kitchen. She saw the knife and the blood and hugged Anna. She said, "Oh, sweetie, you should never touch knives. They are very dangerous. They can hurt you badly." She took Anna to the sink and washed her finger. She put a band-aid on it and kissed it. She said, "There, there, it will be okay. You are very brave. But please, don't order the knives anymore. Let Mommy do that, okay?" Anna nodded and said, "Okay, Mommy. I'm sorry. I just wanted to help." She hugged Mommy and said, "I love you, Mommy." Mommy smiled and said, "I love you too, Anna. You are a very good helper. But sometimes you need to be careful. Now, how about we order some cookies and milk for a treat?"

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big sea. One day, he saw an empty bottle floating in the water. Fin was curious and wanted to join the bottle on its journey. As Fin swam closer, he saw a note inside the bottle. He used his tail to push the bottle to the shore, where he could read the note. The note said, "Help me find my way home. Love, Star." Fin decided to help Star find her way home. He swam and swam, asking all the sea animals if they knew where Star lived. Suddenly, he met a big whale who knew where Star was. The whale said, "I will take you to Star." The whale took Fin to a place where the sea met the sky. There, Fin saw Star. But Star was not a fish or an animal, she was a real star in the sky! Fin was so surprised. Star thanked Fin for helping her find her way back to the sky. From that day on, Fin and Star became best friends, even if they lived in different worlds.

One day, Kelly was playing in the yard when something appeared. She ran to tell Daddy. "Look, Daddy! Something purple appeared in the yard!" she shouted. Daddy walked to the yard and bent down. In the grass was a big purple flower. "That's a beautiful flower, Kelly," Daddy said. He touched the petals of the flower, and it looked wonderful. Kelly was amazed. She couldn't believe how something so purple and pretty could just appear! Daddy smiled, then he took a picture of the flower with his phone. He said that they could remember the flower forever. Kelly was very happy. From then on, Kelly would always look for new purple things to appear in the yard.

One day, a swan named Lily lived by a lake. She liked to play with her friend, Tom the frog. They had lots of fun in the sun. One hot day, Tom said, "Let's spray water to cool off!" They took turns spraying water at each other. Lily would flap her wings and make a big splash. Tom would jump high and make a small splash. They laughed and played all day. Later, they found a spicy red fruit. They did not know what it was, but they wanted to try it. They remembered the water game and had an idea. Lily and Tom sprayed water on the fruit to make it cool. They ate the fruit and it was not spicy anymore. They were happy and played more games by the lake.
