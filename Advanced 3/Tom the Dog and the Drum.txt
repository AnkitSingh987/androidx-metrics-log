Title: Tom the Dog and the Drum

One day, a big dog named Tom found a drum in the park. He wanted to play with it but he did not know how. He saw a small cat named Lily and asked her, "Do you know how to play the drum?" Lily said, "Yes, I do! I can help you. But you have to promise not to lie to me." Tom agreed and they started to play the drum together. They made loud and fun sounds. They were very happy. But one day, Tom wanted to play the drum alone. He told Lily, "I lost the drum. I don't know where it is." Lily was sad but she believed Tom. Later, she found the drum hidden behind a tree. Tom was playing it alone. Lily felt hurt and said, "Tom, you lied to me. Friends should not lie to each other." Tom felt bad and said sorry to Lily. They promised to always be honest and alert to each other's feelings. They played the drum together again and were happy friends. The moral of the story is to always be honest and kind to your friends.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a wise old owl. He had big round eyes and lived in a tall tree. Every day, he would watch the other animals play and have fun. One day, a little squirrel came to the wise owl and asked, "Can you play with me?" The owl looked at the squirrel and said, "I am too old to play, but I can teach you something new." The squirrel was happy and listened to the wise owl. The next day, a mean fox tried to take the squirrel's nuts. The wise owl told the squirrel to ignore the fox and hide the nuts. The squirrel did as the owl said, and the fox went away. The squirrel was happy and thanked the wise owl for his help. From that day on, the squirrel and the owl became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small cabin, there lived a normal family. There was a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Lily. They were happy in their cabin. They played games and had fun. One day, Lily found a big egg. She didn't know where it came from. Lily decided to lay the egg in her room. She was very excited to see what would come out of the egg. Days went by, and the egg started to crack. A little creature came out. It was not a normal animal. It was big and scary. The creature was very hungry. It ate all the food in the cabin. Lily and her family were sad. They had no more food. The big, scary creature was still hungry. It looked at Lily, her mom, and her dad. The creature ate them too. Now, the cabin was empty. The big, scary creature left to find more food. The normal family and their happy cabin were gone.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious big cat. The cat had a very long tail. One day, he met a small bird. The bird was scared, so the cat said, "I will protect you, little bird." The cat and the bird became good friends. They played together every day. The bird would fly high in the sky, and the cat would chase the bird with his long tail. They were very happy. One day, a big wind came and blew the bird away. The cat was sad, but he knew what to do. He used his long tail to catch the bird and protect him from the wind. The bird was very thankful, and they became even better friends. The moral of the story is to always help and protect your friends, no matter what happens.

Once upon a time there was a family who wanted to visit the city. So one day they packed up the car and drove to the city. The traffic was very noisy, so their small child was scared. Mum said, “Don’t worry baby, we just have to drive slowly and safely.” But the child was still afraid of the traffic. Dad thought of a good idea and said, “Let’s park the car and walk!” Mum and Dad held hands with the child and guided them through the noisy traffic. They were surprised to see how many people, cars, buses and trucks there were. Eventually, they safely reached the other side and continued on their visit. The family was so excited to be in the city and they enjoy every minute of their visit. They saw tall buildings, parks and tasted many delicious treats. At the end of the day, they all agreed, the visit to the city had been very fun!
