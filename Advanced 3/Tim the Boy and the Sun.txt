Title: Tim the Boy and the Sun

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. He had a big, soft towel to sit on. The sun was hot, and the sand was warm. Tim felt happy. Tim saw a big, blue sea. He wanted to swim, but he didn't dare. He felt uncomfortable. His mom said, "It's okay, Tim. I will be with you." So, Tim and his mom went into the water. They splashed and played. Tim felt brave. Then, something unexpected happened. The big, soft towel flew away! The wind took it up, up, up. Tim and his mom looked at each other and laughed. They didn't need the towel anymore. They had fun in the water and felt happy.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy judge who was always in a bad mood. Every day he worked at the court and always made people feel bad. One day, he was in a courtroom and he said something mean to a little girl. All of a sudden, something strange happened. The judge started to freeze! He stayed like that, standing still, for a full minute. Everyone in the courtroom was very surprised. But the little girl started to smile. The judge suddenly came back to life and he felt really embarrassed. Now, he wasn't so grumpy anymore. He still worked at the court but he was much nicer now. The little girl was happy and told him "you don't have to be mean - be nice to everyone!" The judge understood and started to be kinder to everyone he met. He even said nice things to the little girl every time she saw him. From then on, he always tried to be kind and friendly. He could still be grumpy sometimes, but not nearly as often as before.

Once upon a time, there was a big, bald man named Tom. He was very strong and liked to lift heavy things. One day, Tom went to the park to play. At the park, Tom met a funny little boy named Sam. Sam liked to tell jokes. Sam said, "Why did the chicken cross the road?" Tom smiled and asked, "Why?" Sam giggled, "To get to the other side!" Tom laughed and they became friends. As they played, a big wind blew Sam's hat away. Tom tried to lift Sam up to get it, but he couldn't! It was a surprise! Sam had a secret. He was a magic boy and he was too heavy for Tom to lift. Sam laughed and said, "It's okay, I can use my magic to get my hat back!" With a wave of his hand, Sam's hat flew back to him. Tom was amazed and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, we are going to grandma's house." Tim was happy because he loved his grandma. They got ready and went to the car. When they arrived at grandma's house, Tim saw that grandma was confused. She was looking for her coffee. Tim asked, "Grandma, what's wrong?" Grandma said, "I can't find my coffee, Tim." Tim wanted to help, so he started to look for the coffee, too. Tim found the coffee in the kitchen. He gave it to grandma and she was very happy. Tim felt good because he helped his grandma. The moral of the story is: always help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a little leopard. His spots were unusual. They were shaped like stars. The little leopard lived in a big forest with his family. One day, the little leopard was playing with his friends. They were playing a game called "pinch and run." The leopard would pinch his friends, and they would laugh and run away. They all had fun playing together. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came, and it blew the little leopard far away from his friends. The little leopard was scared, but he was brave. He walked and walked, and he found a new forest with new friends. The new friends loved his unusual spots and played "pinch and run" with him too. And the little leopard was happy.
