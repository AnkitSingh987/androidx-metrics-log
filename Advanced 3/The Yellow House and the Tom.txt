Title: The Yellow House and the Tom

Once upon a time, there was a yellow house. In this house, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom did not like skeletons. He would say, "I hate skeletons!" One day, Tom found a little skeleton in his room. He was scared and ran away. But the little skeleton did not want to scare Tom. It wanted to be friends with him. So, the little skeleton followed Tom. Tom saw the little skeleton again and stopped running. He noticed that the skeleton was not scary, but friendly. They played together and had fun. From that day on, Tom did not hate skeletons anymore. He and the little skeleton became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a robot. The robot lived in a distant land. The robot was big and strong. One day, the robot met a little boy. The boy did not know what a robot was. The robot wanted to explain. The robot said, "I am a robot. I can help you." The boy and the robot became friends. They played together every day. The robot helped the boy with many things. They were very happy.

Bob was a young boy who liked to perform. Every day, he would get out on the front porch and do a dance or a song with all his heart. His parents thought it was delightful. One day, Bob's parents took him to a special room. It was different than any other room he had been in before. In the middle of the room was a stage. Bob had never seen a stage before, and he thought it was exciting. As Bob's parents watched, he began to perform. He showed the audience some of his dance moves, and they were very graceful. Everyone clapped and cheered after each one of Bob's performances. Bob was so proud that he had performed so gracefully. He was sure that he could do it again!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lucky suit that he loved to wear. It was blue and had a big yellow star on it. Tim felt so happy when he wore his lucky suit. One day, Tim went to sleep in his lucky suit. When he woke up, he saw a big surprise. His lucky suit had turned into a magic suit! Tim could fly in the sky with his magic suit. He felt like a superhero. Tim flew around his house and saw his friends playing outside. They all looked up and saw Tim flying. They were so happy! Tim felt very lucky to have his magic suit and his good friends. They played together all day, and Tim knew that his lucky suit made it a special day.

Lily and Ben are friends who like to play in the park. One day, they see a big hole in the fence. They wonder what is behind it. Lily suggests they go and look. Ben is scared. He says there might be monsters or bad people. Lily says she is brave and curious. She wants to see new things. They crawl through the hole and find a dark tunnel. They hear a loud noise and see a bright light. It is a subway train. It goes very fast and makes a lot of wind. Lily and Ben are amazed and excited. They want to ride the train. They see a sign that says "tickets". They do not have any money. They do not know what to do. A kind lady sees them and smiles. She is compassionate. She asks them where they are from and where they want to go. She says she has an extra ticket and she can take them with her. She says the train is fun and safe. She says she will bring them back to the park later. Lily and Ben are happy and grateful. They thank the lady and follow her. They get on the train and sit next to her. They see many different places and people through the window. They learn new words and facts. They have a wonderful adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a regular boy named Tim. Tim liked to ride his bike with his friends. They would pedal fast and go up and down the street. Tim's bike was red and shiny. One day, Tim saw a tear in his bike tire. He was sad because he could not ride his bike with his friends. Tim showed his dad the tear in the tire. His dad said, "Don't worry, we will fix it." Tim's dad got a new tire and put it on the bike. Tim was happy again. He got on his bike and pedaled fast with his friends. They rode up and down the street, laughing and having fun. And they all lived happily ever after.
