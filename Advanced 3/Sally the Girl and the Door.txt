Title: Sally the Girl and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally was 3 years old and she loved to play and pretend. One day, Sally noticed that there was a door that she had never seen before in her house. She was curious and decided to open the door. But when she opened the door, there was nothing inside. Sally wondered why was there a door but nothing else. She asked her mom, but her mom just said to Sally it was ok and just to ignore the door. But Sally couldn't do that. One evening, Sally decided to pretend she was playing a game. She wanted to see if she could find an escape. So Sally went to the door in her house and opened it. This time when she opened the door, there was a path in front of her! Sally wanted to explore, but she was weak and tired, so she had to go to bed. But she liked the idea of escaping so she decided to try it again the next day. When Sally woke up the next morning, she was so excited to explore. She opened the door and this time there was a big surprise: the path was leading to a secret forest with lots of fun things to see! Sally was so glad that she pretended to find the escape!

---

Mommy was cooking dinner while little Sally played with her toy figures. Suddenly, Sally looked up and said, "Mommy, I want to go to the zoo!" Mommy stopped cooking and said, "That's a great idea! Let's go!" So Sally and Mommy got their coats and added some brown hats for the cold weather. They got into the car and went to the zoo. When they arrived, Sally was so excited she almost forgot to turn off the car. They opened the car doors and stepped out. Sally couldn't wait to see the animals, so she ran ahead. Mommy followed Sally to the zoo and watched as she stopped at each exhibit. She added some food to the ducks and sang a song to the monkeys. Sally was so happy that her eyes sparkled in the sun. Mommy and Sally left the zoo a little while later, but Sally was still talking about the animals they saw. She couldn't wait to go back and add more food to the ducks.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Lily. Lily loved to play with her friends in the park. One day, she saw a big rock near the pond. The rock was so big that it could weigh more than Lily and her friends. Lily had an intelligent idea. She wanted to show her friends how strong she was by moving the rock. She tried and tried, but the rock did not move. Lily's friends saw her trying and came to help. They all pushed the rock together, and it moved a little bit. They were so happy! The moral of the story is that sometimes we need help from our friends to do hard things. Even if we are intelligent like Lily, we can't always do everything by ourselves. It's good to work together and help each other.

One day, a thin cat named Tim went for a walk. He walked to the west, where the sun sets. He saw many fun things, like birds and bugs. As Tim walked, he met a big dog named Max. Max said, "Hi, I like to number my toys. I have five toys. How many toys do you have?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "I have three toys. I like to play with them all day." Max and Tim became good friends. They played with their toys together in the west, where the sun was warm and bright. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet bee named Buzz. Buzz lived in a big tree with his bee friends. They all worked together to make honey. One day, Buzz met a little girl named Lily. She was sad because she lost her toy. Buzz said, "Don't worry, Lily. I will help you find your toy." They looked around the big tree, but they could not find the toy. They felt sad and tired. Lily sat down and started to pray. She prayed for help to find her toy. After she prayed, a big wind came and blew the leaves. There, under the leaves, was Lily's toy! She was so happy! She thanked Buzz and gave him a big hug. Buzz and Lily became best friends and played together every day.
