Title: Emma the Girl and the Bird

One day, a little girl named Emma went for a walk. She saw a bird in a tree. The bird was enormous! It was the biggest bird Emma had ever seen. The bird looked down at her and said, "Hi! My name is Buddy." Emma was so happy to meet Buddy. She asked, "Buddy, can you read?" Buddy shook his head and said, "No, I can't read. Can you teach me?" Emma smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you learn to read." They sat down under the tree with a big book. Emma read the words to Buddy, and he listened carefully. They read the book together, and Buddy started to learn the words. Soon, Buddy could read the book all by himself. Emma and Buddy were very happy. They had lots of fun reading together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer who had a green hat. He lived on a farm with many animals. The farmer loved to play with his animals and take care of them. One day, the farmer met a nice lady. The lady liked the farmer's green hat. They talked and laughed together. The lady said, "I like your green hat, farmer!" The farmer smiled and said, "Thank you, I like it too!" After some time, the farmer and the lady decided to marry. They had a big party on the farm with all the animals. Everyone was happy and had lots of fun. The farmer and the lady lived happily ever after, taking care of their animals and wearing their green hats.

Once upon a time, there was a smart boy named Tim. He had a toy phone. He loved to play with his phone all day. One day, Tim saw his dad cook. His dad used fire to cook the food. Tim thought the fire was pretty. Tim said to his dad, "Can I play with the fire?" His dad said, "No, Tim. Fire can burn and hurt you. You must stay away from it." But Tim did not listen to his dad. When his dad left the room, Tim took his toy phone and went near the fire. He wanted his phone to be warm. But the fire was too hot. The toy phone started to burn. Tim was scared and cried. His dad came back and saw the burnt toy phone. He was sad and said, "I told you to stay away from the fire. Now your toy phone is gone." Tim learned his lesson, but it was too late. His toy phone was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a modest girl named Lily. She loved to wear a veil on her head. One day, she wanted to mail a letter to her friend, Tom. Lily went to the mail box with her letter. When she opened the box, she saw a big frog inside! The frog was wearing a veil like Lily. She was very surprised. Lily and the frog became good friends. They both liked to wear veils and mail letters. The frog was not a real frog, but a prince! One day, he turned back into a prince and they lived happily ever after.

Once there were two twins named Jack and Jill. They were healthy and strong and always happy to play together. But one day, Jack had an idea. He wanted to write something special. So he got some paper and pencils and he sat down to write. He did not know what to write but he decided to make a story about his twin. He worked hard and wrote slowly. He was so excited when he finished. He talked to his twin and they decided to read it out loud together. As they read, they laughed and smiled and they felt very happy. When they finished, they knew they had something special. They decided to keep writing stories together and to share them with all their friends. They became very good at writing stories and soon everyone was reading the stories of Jack and Jill. As Jack and Jill grew older, their stories got better and better but they kept the same twist of being healthy, strong and always happy to write together.
