Title: Sunny the Yellow Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a yellow cat named Sunny. Sunny liked to play games. One day, Sunny saw a file on the floor. She did not know what it was, so she decided to play a guessing game with her friend, Tim the dog. Sunny said to Tim, "Guess what I found!" Tim looked at the file and said, "I don't know, what is it?" Sunny laughed and said, "It's a file! Let's play with it!" So, they played with the file, throwing it in the air and catching it. But then, the file broke. Sunny and Tim were sad. They knew they should not have played with something they did not know. And now, they could not play with the file anymore. The end.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the library with her mom. Amy loved the library. It was full of books and fun things to do. She liked to fill her bag with books to read at home. Today, she wanted to find an unusual book. At the library, Amy found a big, red book. It was unusual because it had no words. "Mom, look at this book!" Amy said. Her mom looked at the book and said, "That is an unusual book, Amy. Let's take it home and see what it is about." When they got home, Amy opened the big, red book. Suddenly, a big, friendly dog jumped out of the book! Amy and her mom were very surprised. The dog wagged its tail and played with Amy. They had a lot of fun. From that day on, Amy knew that the library was a magical place where anything could happen.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cricket. He lived in a big green garden with many friends. The cricket loved to race with his friends every day. They would run and jump and have lots of fun. One day, the cricket had an idea. He wanted to race with the biggest friend he had, a giant turtle. The turtle was very slow, but he was wise and kind. All the friends came to watch the race. They were excited to see who would win. The race began, and the cricket jumped very fast. The turtle walked very slow. The cricket was sure he would win. But then, something unexpected happened. The cricket saw a friend in trouble. A little ant was stuck in a hole. The cricket stopped the race to help the ant. The turtle kept walking slow and steady. The cricket saved the ant and then continued the race. But when he reached the finish line, the turtle was already there. The cricket lost the race, but he was still happy. He learned that helping friends is more important than winning. All the friends cheered for the smart cricket and the wise turtle. They knew that kindness and friendship were the real winners.

Billy was in the garden playing with his friends. They were running and laughing, having lots of fun. Suddenly they heard a loud noise and looked up to see a fire engine rushing down the street. The fire engine was bright red and made lots of noise. It had a big hose on the back. The children ran towards the fire engine and watched as it pulled up outside the house on the corner. A man got out and rushed up the garden path with the hose trailing behind him. The children felt guilty - they knew they had made the noise that made the fire engine come. They ran away quickly and hid behind the bushes until the fire engine left. They were relieved when it drove away. Billy and his friends went back to playing, but he was sad that he had made the noise that caused so much trouble.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was in a classroom with his friends. They were all learning and having fun. Tim felt happy in his classroom. One day, Tim did something foolish. He took a toy from his friend, Sarah, without asking. Sarah was sad and cried. Tim's teacher saw what happened and said, "Tim, you should not take things without asking. That is not nice." Tim felt bad and regret what he did. He said, "I am sorry, Sarah. I will not do it again." Sarah smiled and they played together happily. Tim learned to be kind and not to do foolish things.
