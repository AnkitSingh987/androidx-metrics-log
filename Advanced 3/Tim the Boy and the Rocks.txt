Title: Tim the Boy and the Rocks

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play on his porch. His porch was a fun place to be. One sunny day, Tim saw some rocks on the porch. He thought it would be easy to count them. So, he started to number the rocks. He counted one, two, three, four, and five rocks. Tim was very happy. He picked up the rocks and put them in his pocket. Then, he went inside to show his mom. His mom was proud of him for counting the rocks. They had a fun day playing with the rocks together.

Once upon a time, there was a big black dog named Max. Max loved to eat jam on his bread. One day, Max saw a beautiful flower start to bloom in his garden. He was so happy to see the pretty flower. Max wanted to share his jam with his friend, a little bird named Sam. So, Max called out, "Sam, come and eat jam with me!" Sam flew down and they both enjoyed the sweet jam together. But then, a naughty cat came into the garden. The cat wanted to eat Sam! Max barked loudly, "No, cat! You can't eat my friend!" The cat got scared and ran away. Max and Sam were safe, and they continued to eat their jam and watch the flower bloom.

Once upon a time there was a crocodile who wanted to use something very fast. After thinking for a while, he decided to use his legs to get around quicker. So, he started running and running, faster and faster. The crocodile was getting tired, when he spotted a man fishing in the river. He thought that the man would be able to help him out so he swam right over to him. “Hello” said the crocodile. “Can you please help me? I want to use something very fast.” The man smiled and said, “You can use my boat! It’s very fast and will take you wherever you want to go. All you need to do is climb in and I’ll take you on a ride.” The crocodile was so excited! He quickly hopped in the boat and the man started rowing. Soon the crocodile was speeding across the water, faster than he could ever imagine! He felt like he was the fastest crocodile in the world. He was so happy he screamed with joy!

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She lived in a big castle. The princess had a dull dress. She did not like her dull dress. She wanted a pretty dress. One day, the princess went for a walk. She saw a big, pretty flower. The flower was not dull. The princess picked the flower and put it on her dress. Now, the princess was happy. Her dress was not dull anymore. She said, "Thank you, pretty flower!" The princess went back to the castle. She showed everyone her pretty dress. Everyone liked the princess's pretty dress. They said, "Thank you, princess!" The princess was very happy. She played with her friends all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty king. He had a big castle and many people lived there. One day, the king wanted to number all his people. He asked his helper, "Can you help me count everyone?" The helper said, "Yes, I can help you." They started to count everyone in the castle. They counted 1, 2, 3, 4, and so on. They counted and counted until they had a big number. But then, there was a war. Another king wanted the mighty king's castle. The two kings fought with their people. They fought day and night. The war was very loud and scary. In the end, the mighty king lost the war. The other king took over the castle. The mighty king and his people were very sad. They had to leave their home and find a new place to live. The mighty king learned that counting his people was not as important as keeping them safe.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Maya. She was three years old and very curious. One morning, Maya's mommy asked her if she wanted to go for a walk. Of course, Maya said yes! They walked for a long time, until Maya saw something. It was a box, made of wood and with a shiny pink ribbon dangling from the top. Maya asked if she could open the box and her mom said yes. When Maya opened the box, her eyes lit up. Inside was something magical! It was a clean, white feather. Maya held it up and said, "Mom, look! I receive a magic feather!" Her mommy hugged her and said, "That's amazing, sweetheart! Take care of it." Maya held the feather in her hand, feeling very proud and happy. From that day on, wherever she went, Maya would bring her special, magical feather.
