Title: The Story of the Blanket and the Newspaper

Once there was a little girl who was very tired. She wanted to take a nap, so she went and got a newspaper. Then she lay down on the floor and used the newspaper as a blanket. It was so flexible that she was able to make it into a nice shape and it helped her get nice and cozy. She quickly fell asleep and had a lovely nap. It was so peaceful that she stayed asleep for a long time. When she finally woke up, she felt refreshed and ready for the day. The newspaper had done its job – the little girl had had a wonderful snooze.

Crowds of people were standing around on a hill, waiting in anticipation for the moment when the sun set. Paul and his mother were there as well. Paul was so excited to see the sunset that he was trembling. "Look at the sky, Paul!" his mother said. "The colours of the sunset are so beautiful!" Paul's eyes lit up as he looked at the colourful sky. He couldn't stop looking at all the hues of orange and yellow. After a few moments, he asked, "What happens after the sunset?" "When the sun sets," his mother replied, "The stars come out and it becomes dark. But don't worry, the stars will be so pretty." Paul nodded and watched as the sun disappeared behind the horizon. As the night sky grew darker, he noticed a bright star shimmering against the blackness. Paul smiled, amazed and excited at the beauty of the night sky. He felt awed and the moment suddenly made him feel so small and helpless. His mother noticed the look on his face and softly said, "Don't worry, Paul. That star is so far away, it will never hurt you." Paul felt safe again, knowing that his mother was there to protect him. Then, he looked up into the night sky, taking in its beauty with a hard gaze.

Once upon a time, there was a small bug named Tim. Tim had a horn on his head. He lived in a big garden with his friends. They played together all day long. Tim liked to run, jump, and laugh. One day, Tim saw a big bug with a big horn. The big bug wanted to fight Tim. Tim was scared, but he knew he had to be brave. He looked at his small horn and took a deep breath. Tim and the big bug started to fight. They pushed each other with their horns. Tim was small, but he was fast and smart. In the end, Tim won the fight. The big bug said sorry and they became friends. From that day on, they all played together in the big garden, happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a big swamp. In the swamp, there were many animals. One day, a little frog named Tim hopped around. Tim was a healthy frog. Tim saw a pretty flower. He wanted to examine it. He hopped closer to the flower. He looked at it very carefully. The flower was very pretty. As Tim hopped back to his home, he saw his friend, a big fish named Sam. They played together in the swamp. They were happy and had a lot of fun. The swamp was a nice home for Tim and Sam.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cow named Bessie. Bessie lived on a farm with other animals. They all liked to play together. One day, Bessie thought she would surprise her friends with a gift. Bessie went to her friend, the pig, and said, "I have a surprise for you!" The pig was very excited and asked, "What is it?" Bessie gave the pig some yummy beef to eat. The pig was very happy and said, "Thank you, Bessie!" But then, something bad happened. The pig got very sick from eating the beef. Bessie was sad and didn't know what to do. All the other animals were sad too. Bessie learned that her surprise was not a good one, and she felt sorry for the pig.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful town, there was a big stand. On the stand, there was a cat named Tom. Tom loved to stretch every day. He would stretch up, down, left, and right. Tom was a very happy cat. One day, Tom went to stretch on his stand, but it was not there. Tom was sad. He looked around the town, but he could not find his stand. As he looked, he saw a big box. Tom was curious and wanted to see what was inside the box. Tom went to the box and opened it. Inside, he found a new stand! It was bigger and better than his old stand. Tom was so happy and surprised. He jumped on the new stand and began to stretch. Now, Tom could stretch even more and be the happiest cat in the cheerful town.
