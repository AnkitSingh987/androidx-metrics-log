Title: Tom and Sue and the Cake

One day, Tom and Sue wanted to have a party. They liked the color blue. So, they decided to make a blue party. They had blue balloons, blue cake, and wore blue clothes. Tom tried to blow up the big blue balloons. He blew and blew. "I can't do it," Tom said. Sue tried too. She blew and blew. "I can do it!" Sue said. The big blue balloon got bigger and bigger. At the party, all their friends came. They all wore blue. They played games and ate the blue cake. "This is the best blue party ever!" said their friend, Sam. Everyone laughed and had fun at the blue party.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play with her toy blocks. One day, she wanted to attach her blocks to make a big tower. She was very excited to build it. Mia started to build her tower on one side of the room. She was very careful to make it clear where the blocks went. She did not want her tower to fall down. Mia's mom watched her play and smiled. As Mia was attaching the last block, something unexpected happened. A small bird flew in through the open window and knocked over her tower! Mia was sad, but then she laughed. She knew she could build another tower, and maybe the bird would help her next time.

Jim was a small boy. He had an enormous teddy bear. One day he was playing with his bear and he made a big cut on it. He was sad and started to cry. His mummy saw what happened and came to help him. She took some tissue and wiped away the tears. Then she took some tape and carefully covered the cut. Jim smiled. Mummy said, "Now the cut is gone, don't worry anymore." She hugged Jim and he felt very happy. That night he held the teddy bear and went to sleep. The next day, he and his enormous teddy bear set off on an adventure. They were ready to explore the world!

Once upon a time, there was a big hall. The hall was very boring. It had no colors or fun things to play with. One day, a little boy named Tim went inside the hall. He wanted to make the hall fun. Tim had an idea. He found a ball and decided to strike it. The ball bounced off the walls of the hall. The sound made the hall less boring. Tim laughed and played with the ball. Soon, other kids came to the hall. They saw Tim playing with the ball. The kids joined Tim and they all played together. The once boring hall became a fun place for everyone. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a miserable little ant. He had an unusual problem, he was always being covered up. He was so unhappy, he decided to go and find help from a butterfly. The ant traveled and eventually he stumbled upon the butterfly. He asked her, "Will you please help me? I am always getting covered up and I feel miserable". The butterfly looked sympathetic. She said, "I can help, but first you must fly with me". The ant was scared, but he was so miserable he agreed. So the ant and the butterfly flew up high, until they could find a place free from being covered up. It was so beautiful and he was so happy not to be miserable anymore. The ant thanked the butterfly and they said their goodbyes. The butterfly waved as the ant flew back home, to never be covered up again.

One day, a busy cat named Tim was walking in the kitchen. He saw a napkin on the floor. Tim wanted to play with the napkin, but he did not see the water near it. As Tim ran to the napkin, he slipped on the water. He fell down and it hurt. Tim was sad and the napkin was still far away. A nice dog named Sam saw Tim. Sam wanted to help Tim. He picked up the napkin with his mouth and gave it to Tim. Tim was happy and they played together.
