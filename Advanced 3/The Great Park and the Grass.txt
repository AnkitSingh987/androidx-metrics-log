Title: The Great Park and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a great park. In this park, many animals lived and played. One day, a little girl named Sally went to the park with her mom. In the park, Sally saw a small animal. It was a bunny. The bunny was hopping and playing in the grass. Sally said to her mom, "Look, mom! I see a bunny!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Sally, it's a cute little bunny." Sally and her mom walked more in the park. They saw more animals. They saw birds, squirrels, and even a deer. Sally loved the park and the animals. She had a great day with her mom.

Once upon a time, in a big valley, there lived a happy dog named Spot. Spot loved to perform tricks for his friends. He could jump high, roll over, and even dance on his back legs. One sunny day, Spot was playing with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They were having fun in the valley, running and laughing. Suddenly, Spot saw a big, red ball. He felt a little guilty because it was not his ball, but he wanted to play with it. Spot took the ball and showed it to Lily. Lily asked, "Spot, is this your ball?" Spot shook his head and said, "No, but I want to perform a trick with it." Lily thought for a moment and said, "Okay, but we should find the owner after your trick." Spot performed a great trick with the ball. He jumped high in the air, flipped, and caught the ball in his mouth. Lily clapped and cheered. After the trick, they found the owner of the ball and gave it back. The owner was happy, and Spot felt good for doing the right thing.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little cell. The little cell was small and helpless. It lived with its family in a tree. They were all very happy. One day, a fire started in the forest. The fire started to burn the trees. The little cell and its family were scared. They did not know what to do. The fire was getting closer and closer to their tree. Then, a big rain came. The rain put out the fire and stopped the trees from burning. The little cell and its family were safe. They were very happy and thankful for the rain that saved them.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat named Bobby. Bobby was excited to go on a big trip to the port. He had to measure how much water he needed for the trip. "Are you ready, Bobby?" asked his friend, Sally the Seagull. "Yes, I am," said Bobby happily. On the way to the port, Bobby saw other boats who were not excited. They didn't measure their water and now they were stuck. They had to wait for help. Bobby felt sad for them. When Bobby reached the port, he was very happy. He had enough water, because he measured it before the trip. His friend, Sally, was proud of him. The moral of the story is to always be prepared and plan ahead. This way, you can have a safe and happy trip, just like Bobby the boat.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little butterfly. The butterfly was sad because it could not fly well. It wanted to find a friend who would trust it and help it. One day, the butterfly met a kind bird. The bird saw the butterfly was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, butterfly?" The butterfly said, "I am poor and cannot fly well. I need a friend who will trust me and help me." The bird wanted to help the butterfly. They had a problem, though. A big, mean cat was trying to catch them both. The butterfly was scared, but the bird said, "Trust me, butterfly. I will help you fly and keep you safe from the cat." The butterfly trusted the bird, and they worked together to fly away from the cat. They became best friends, and the butterfly was not sad anymore. They lived happily, knowing they could trust each other.
