Title: Amy and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a pretty house, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her toys and run around in her big yard. She was always happy and loved to share her toys with her friends. One day, Amy found a little box in her room. It was closed, and she wanted to see what was inside. So, she tried to open it. She pulled and pulled, and finally, the box opened! Inside the box, there was a tiny bird. The bird was scared and wanted to escape. Amy knew that the bird needed to be free. She took the bird outside and opened her hand. The bird was happy to escape and flew away into the sky. Amy watched the bird fly away, and she felt happy that she could help the bird. From that day on, Amy always remembered to be kind and help others when they needed it.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to watch a movie. She asked her mom, "Mom, can we watch a movie today?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can watch a movie after you finish your lunch." Lucy ate her lunch very fast. It was easy for her because she was excited about the movie. She managed to finish her lunch quickly. Her mom was happy and said, "Good job, Lucy!" They sat down to watch the movie together. Lucy was very happy. The movie was fun and they laughed a lot. After the movie, Lucy said, "Thank you, Mom. I love watching movies with you."

One day, a big dog named Max was very proud of his lawn. He liked to run, jump, and play on it. The sun was bright and the grass was green. Max was happy. Max saw his friend, a little cat named Luna. Luna said, "Max, your lawn is so pretty! Can I play on it too?" Max said, "Yes, Luna, come and play with me!" They played and had fun together. Then, something bad happened. A big rain came and made a big mess on the lawn. The grass turned brown and the flowers died. Max and Luna were sad. They could not play on the lawn anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a very curious 3-year-old girl named Sarah. Everywhere Sarah went she was trying to learn new things. One day, Sarah was in the living room and saw a strange object. “Mommy, what is that?” Sarah asked. “That, Sarah, is a telephone,” Mommy explained. “It is a very useful tool we use to talk to family and friends that live far away. Let me show you how it works.” Mommy took the telephone, which was made of thick black plastic, and showed Sarah how to use it. She explained that Sarah had to press the buttons on the telephone to talk to someone. Sarah took the telephone and started to press the buttons. Soon she heard a voice on the other end and was so excited. Mommy made sure to explain how to use the telephone safely and that Sarah should not talk to strangers. Sarah had so much fun learning about the telephone and couldn't wait to show her new friend!

Once upon a time, there was a wide river. On one side of the river, there was a little house. In the house, there lived a boy named Tim and his mom. They were very happy. One day, Tim saw a big iron boat on the river. He wanted to go on the boat. Tim asked his mom, "Can I go on the boat, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim was so happy. He started to rush to the boat. He ran fast, like a race car. Tim got on the boat and looked around. It was fun. The boat took Tim across the wide river. He saw fish and birds. Tim waved to his mom from the boat. His mom waved back. They both smiled. When the boat ride was over, Tim rushed back to his mom. They hugged each other. Tim said, "Thank you, Mom, for letting me go on the boat!" They went home and had a great day.
