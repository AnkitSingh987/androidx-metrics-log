Title: Sarah the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She loved to walk with her mom and dad. Every day, they walked to a big house called the opera. The opera had many people singing and dancing. Sarah liked to watch them. One day, Sarah saw a light bird at the opera. The bird was very light and pretty. It could sing and dance too. Sarah wanted to be friends with the bird. She walked to the bird and said, "Hello, bird! Let's be friends." The bird was happy to be friends with Sarah. They walked and danced together at the opera. Sarah and the bird became best friends. They walked to the opera every day and had lots of fun. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy bird named Bob. Bob lived near the sea, where a big yacht was. One day, Bob met a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play near the yacht. Bob said, "Hi Tim, do you want to play with me?" Tim said, "Yes, but I have to go home soon. Can we play for a little bit?" Bob said, "Sure, but we must not delay or you will be late." They played near the yacht, but they were having so much fun that they forgot the time. The sky became dark, and it was time for Tim to go home. But they had delayed too long. Tim tried to run home, but he got lost in the dark. He was very scared and sad. Bob tried to help, but he could not find the way either. Tim learned that he should not delay when it is time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was three years old and loved to go shopping with her mom. One day, Jane and her mom went to the store to buy a circle. When they got to the store, the bad man said, “I don’t have any circles for you to buy.” Jane was very disappointed, but then her mom said, “It’s okay, sweetie. Let’s look around and see if we can find something else.” So they searched around the store, but they couldn’t find a circle anywhere. Jane started to get sad, but then she saw a big, round plate that was the same shape as a circle! “Mommy, can I buy this plate?” Jane asked. Her mom smiled and said, “Yes, that’s a good idea!” So Jane and her mom bought the plate, and even though it wasn’t a circle, Jane was happy because she still got to shop with her mom!

Once upon a time, there was a delicate little bird named Blue. Blue loved to sail in the sky. One day, Blue saw a big, dark cloud coming. Blue knew he had to hurry home before the rain started. As Blue hurried, the wind blew hard. It was not easy for him to fly. Blue saw his friend, Bunny, on the ground. Bunny was also trying to hurry home. Blue and Bunny decided to help each other. They knew they could do it together. Blue and Bunny hurried as fast as they could. They reached their homes just in time. The rain started, but they were safe and dry. Blue and Bunny were happy they could count on each other. They knew that friends are always there to help in times of need.

Once upon a time, there was a big pink cat named Fluffy. Fluffy had a best friend named Spot. Spot was a small black dog. They liked to play together every day. One day, Fluffy and Spot wanted to celebrate their friendship. They decided to have a party. They invited all their friends to come and play with them. Everyone was very excited. At the party, they played games, ate yummy treats, and danced. They all had a lot of fun. Fluffy and Spot were very happy to celebrate with their friends. And from that day on, they knew they would be friends forever.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to an opera with her mom. The opera was very organized. There were many people singing and dancing on the stage. Amy liked the pretty dresses and the happy songs. During the opera, Amy saw a small cat. The cat was napping under a chair. Amy walked to the cat and said, "Hi, cat! Why are you napping here?" The cat opened its eyes and said, "I like the music. It makes me feel sleepy and happy." Amy sat down next to the cat and they listened to the opera together. The cat started to nap again, and Amy felt sleepy too. They both took a nice nap at the opera, and when they woke up, they clapped and cheered for the singers and dancers.
