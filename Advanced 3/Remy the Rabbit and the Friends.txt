Title: Remy the Rabbit and the Friends

Once upon a time, in a big wood, there was a small rabbit named Remy. Remy liked to hop and play all day with his friends. But sometimes, Remy would get lost and cry. One day, Remy and his friends were playing hide and seek. Remy found a slow, big snail to hide behind. He thought it was the best hiding spot. But when it was time to go home, Remy was lost. He started to cry. Remy's friends heard him cry and came to find him. They saw the slow, big snail and knew Remy must be near. They found Remy and hugged him. Remy was so happy to see his friends. They all went home together, and Remy never cried in the big wood again.

One day, a kind girl named Sara went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a toy for Sara's little brother, Tim. When they got to the store, they saw many toys, but they all had a high price. Sara's mom said, "We need to find a toy with a low price." Sara looked around and saw a nice toy car. She picked it up and saw that it had a low price. She was very happy and showed it to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Sara! This is a great toy for Tim." As they were leaving the store, Sara saw a little boy who was sad. He wanted a toy, but his mom said they didn't have enough money. Sara felt bad for the boy, so she went to her mom and asked if they could help. Her mom said yes, and they bought a toy for the boy too. The boy was so happy that he yelled, "Thank you, Sara!" Sara felt good because she was kind and helped someone else.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a fancy eraser. The eraser was big, soft, and pink. Tim liked to use the eraser when he drew pictures. One day, Tim's mom said, "Let's prepare a cake for your dad's birthday!" Tim was happy and wanted to help. He wanted to draw a picture of the cake first. So, he took out his fancy eraser and some paper. As Tim started to draw, his fancy eraser began to talk! "Hello, I'm Ernie the Eraser!" it said. Tim was very surprised. He did not know that erasers could talk. Ernie helped Tim draw the best cake ever. Tim showed his mom the drawing. She was happy, too. They made the cake together. When Tim's dad came home, he was so surprised by the cake. He loved it! And Tim's fancy eraser, Ernie, became his best friend.

Once upon a time there was a fisherman who was very proud. He would go out fishing every day and often came back with big hauls. One day, the proud fisherman fell asleep and drifted away out at sea. As he slept, the waves rocked him back and forth until the boat started to sink. All the proud fisherman's things drifted away. The proud fisherman woke up and found himself on a far away island. "Where is my boat?" he shouted. A fish heard him and said, "I know where it is, follow me!" The fish led the fisherman to the edge of the island where the boat was stuck in the mud. The proud fisherman thanked the fish and they said goodbye. The fisherman worked hard to get the boat off the mud and soon made it back to shore safe. He was tired but so proud. From that day on, the fisherman always took a nap before setting sail.

Once upon a time there were two best friends named Kevin and Adam. They were both very small and they both loved each other a lot. One day, they decided to go to the park to play. While they were walking, they noticed a thin mineral on the ground. It was very special and sparkly. They both wanted the mineral, but they knew that only one of them could have it. So they made a promise. The one who was faster would get the mineral. Adam was quicker than Kevin and he took the mineral. But that didn't make Kevin sad. Adam remembered their promise and he gave the mineral to him. They were both very happy. Kevin and Adam gave each other a big hug and went back to the park to play. And they kept the thin mineral forever.
