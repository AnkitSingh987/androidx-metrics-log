Title: The Story of the Rock and the Temple

Once upon a time, there was a big, hard rock near a temple. Many people liked to visit the temple to celebrate happy times. They would dance, sing, and eat yummy food with their family and friends. One day, a little boy went to the temple with his mom and dad. They wanted to celebrate his birthday. The boy saw the hard rock and thought it looked fun to climb. So, he asked his mom and dad if he could try. They said yes, but to be careful. The boy climbed the hard rock slowly and carefully. When he got to the top, he could see the whole temple! He felt so happy and proud. His mom and dad clapped and cheered for him. They all celebrated together and had a great day at the temple.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to run and play in the grass. He liked to hear the birds sing. Buddy was very comfortable in his home. One day, Buddy saw a big, black cloud in the sky. He did not know what it was. But he could hear a loud noise coming from it. The noise scared Buddy, but he did not know why. The big, black cloud got closer and closer. The loud noise got louder and louder. Buddy was not comfortable anymore. He ran away from the grass and his home. The big, black cloud came and took away Buddy's home. Buddy was sad and scared.

Sara liked to design things. She had many crayons and papers and stickers. She made pictures of flowers and animals and houses. She was very good at design. One day, Sara saw a big bulb in the garage. It was shiny and round and had a wire. She wondered what it was for. She was very nosy. She took the bulb and some crayons and papers and stickers. She wanted to design something with the bulb. She went to her room and put the bulb on her table. She drew a face on the bulb with a crayon. She gave it eyes and a nose and a mouth. She stuck some stickers on the bulb to make hair and ears. She made a hat for the bulb with paper. She was very happy with her design. She showed her design to her mom and dad. They smiled and said it was very nice. They told her the bulb was for a lamp. They said they would help her put the bulb in the lamp. They said the bulb would make light when they turned on the lamp. Sara was very excited. She wanted to see her design with light. She went with her mom and dad to the living room. They put the bulb in the lamp and turned it on. The bulb made light and the face on the bulb glowed. Sara clapped and laughed. She said she liked her design. She said she liked the bulb. She said she liked to be nosy.

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant. The elephant was sad because he was alone. One day, he met a nice girl elephant. They liked to play together. They played in the sun and in the icy water. The two elephants became best friends. They wanted to be together forever. So, they decided to marry each other. They were very happy. All the other animals came to see the elephant wedding. After they married, the elephants were not sad anymore. They lived happily ever after. They played in the sun and the icy water every day. And all the animals were happy for them too.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a sweet girl named Lily. Lily had a best friend named Ben. They liked to play and share toys with each other. One day, Ben came to Lily and asked, "Can you lend me your red ball?" Lily said, "Yes, you can have it. But you have to give it back when you are done." Ben was happy and took the red ball to play. After a while, Lily's mom gave her a task. She said, "Lily, please put your toys away." Lily started to put her toys away, but she could not find her red ball. She remembered that she lent it to Ben. Lily went to Ben's house to get her red ball back. But when she got there, she saw that the red ball was broken. Ben had not taken good care of it. Lily was very sad and cried. From that day on, Lily did not lend her toys to Ben anymore. The end.
