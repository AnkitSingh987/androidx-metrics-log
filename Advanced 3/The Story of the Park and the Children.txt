Title: The Story of the Park and the Children

Once upon a time, there was a park full of happy children. They loved to play in the park and have fun. They all behaved nicely, too. One day, something different happened. A new child arrived at the park. He was very shy. He didn't want to play with the other children. He stayed in the corner and was quiet. The other children were curious. They wanted to talk to the new child. But he was too shy to talk to them. They decided to behave nicely. They asked him if he wanted to join in the fun. Slowly, the new child started to smile. He finally had the courage to play with the other children. Soon they all were laughing and running around together. The park was filled with fun and joy. The children had learnt to look beyond their differences and be nice to one another.

---

One day, a graceful cat named Lily was walking in the woods. She saw a small pill on the ground. The pill started to glow. Lily was very surprised. Lily said, "Hello, glowing pill! Why are you here?" The pill replied, "I am here to help you be a good friend to others." Lily was happy and wanted to learn. Lily took the glowing pill to her friend Tom, the dog. Tom was sad because he lost his toy. Lily shared her toy with Tom. Tom said, "Thank you, Lily! You are a good friend." The glowing pill smiled. The moral value of the story is to be a good friend and share with others.

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there was a queen. The queen was very busy. She had many things to do. But she liked to jog every day. It made her feel good. One day, the queen went for a jog in the park. She saw a little cat. The cat was sad. The queen stopped and asked, "Why are you sad, little cat?" The cat said, "I lost my toy." The queen wanted to help the cat, so they looked for the toy together. They found the toy near a big tree. The cat was very happy. The queen felt good because she helped the cat. The queen and the cat became good friends. They jogged together every day. The queen learned that helping others makes you feel happy. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a driver. He was very jolly, and he loved to drive his car. Every day, he put on his driving clothes and drove around the city. One day, the driver was about to start his car when he heard a knock. He opened the door and saw a little monkey. The little monkey asked the driver if he could part with him. The driver thought it was a great idea, so he agreed and let the monkey into his car. The driver and the little monkey drove off together. They drove down a long winding road and arrived at a beautiful park. The driver and the little monkey got down from the car, and the driver took out a picnic basket. They sat in the grass and ate the food, then they played a game of tag. At the end of the day, the driver drove the little monkey back to his home. The little monkey said goodbye to the jolly driver, thanking him for the wonderful day they spent together. The driver smiled and waved goodbye and they drove off in different directions.

One day, a small dog was lost. He could not recognize where he was. The dog was anxious. He wanted to find his owner. He walked and walked, looking for his owner. The dog saw a big tree. He thought maybe his owner was there. But the owner was not there. The dog was sad. He walked more, hoping to find his owner soon. Then, the dog saw a girl. The girl knew the dog. She said, "I know your owner!" The dog was happy. The girl took the dog to his owner. The dog and his owner were so happy to see each other. They hugged and played. The dog was not lost anymore.
