Title: Bob and the Emily

There was a popular barber called Bob. Every day he enjoyed going to work and meeting his customers. One sunny afternoon a three year old girl called Emily and her mom walked in. Emily’s mom said to Bob, “Hi, my daughter would like to have her hair washed and brushed please.” Bob smiled and said “Sure! Follow me.” They both went to Bob’s corner and he showed Emily a special stool and a basin of warm water. Emily was a bit scared but Bob said, “Don’t worry! I’m going to help you bathe your hair. It will be fun.” After a few minutes Emily was enjoying the warm water and bubbles. Bob washed her hair with shampoo and combed it gently with a big brush. When he was done, Emily’s hair looked shiny and soft. Bob quickly blew dry her hair and gave her a big hug. Emily’s mom thanked Bob for the good job, and said “You sure are a popular barber around here.” Emily smiled and said “Yeah! I love coming here.”

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Anna. She was very clever and always liked to play with puzzles and mazes. One day, she found a special maze and wanted to try it out. Anna was clumsy and kept making mistakes. Her fingers shook and she kept getting stuck. After a while she started to feel frustrated. But then, Anna remembered something her mum said. She used her finger to gently touch the maze and then feel where she should go next. She took her time and was careful. In the end, Anna managed to complete the maze. She felt tired but happy. She had learnt a very important lesson – it is okay to take your time to get things right.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. One day, Tim's car went under his bed, and it was very dark under there. Tim felt scared to reach under the bed, but he really wanted his car back. He asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me get my car? It's under the bed and it's dark," said Tim. His mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I have a light!" Mom turned on the light and reached under the bed. She urged Tim to look with her. "See, there's nothing to be scared of," she said. Tim saw his car and felt brave. He reached under the bed and grabbed his car. He was so happy to have it back. From that day on, Tim wasn't scared of the dark anymore. He knew that with a little light and some help from his mom, he could be brave and find anything he needed. And his mom always remembered to say his name when she urged him to be brave.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to bake with her mom. Sue was always careful when she baked. She liked to mix things in a big bowl. One day, she wanted to bake a cake all by herself. Sue asked her mom if she could use the computer to find a cake recipe. Her mom said yes. Sue was very excited. She looked at the computer and found a good recipe. She wrote down all the things she needed to make the cake. The next day, Sue got all the things she needed to bake the cake. She was very careful to follow the recipe. She mixed everything in the big bowl. She put the cake in the oven to bake. Sue was so happy that she could bake a cake all by herself. When the cake was done, Sue took it out of the oven. She was very careful not to touch the hot oven. She let the cake cool down. Then, she put frosting on the cake. It looked so yummy! Sue's mom came home and saw the cake. She was so proud of Sue for baking the cake all by herself. They ate the cake together, and it was the best cake ever. Sue knew that being careful and using the computer to find the recipe had helped her bake a great cake.
