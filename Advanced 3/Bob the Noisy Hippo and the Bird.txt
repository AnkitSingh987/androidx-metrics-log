Title: Bob the Noisy Hippo and the Bird

One day, a noisy hippo named Bob lived by a big river. He loved to splash and play all day. The sun was hot and the water felt nice. Bob was very happy. One day, a little bird came to the river. "Hi, hippo! Can you help me?" the bird asked. "I need to cut a big leaf for my nest, but my beak is too small." Bob wanted to help his new friend, so he used his big teeth to cut the leaf for the bird. "Thank you, hippo!" the bird said, and flew away. The next day, the bird came back with more friends. They all needed help to cut leaves. Bob was happy to help them all. But then, something unexpected happened. The birds started to sing a beautiful song just for Bob! The noisy hippo loved the song so much, he started to dance. From that day on, Bob and the birds became best friends, and they all lived happily by the big river.

One day, a fat squirrel named Sam was looking for nuts in the park. He saw a little nut on the ground and picked it up. Sam was very happy to find the nut because it would support his family. As Sam was walking home, he met another squirrel named Tim. Tim said, "Hi Sam, I see you found a nut. Can I have it, please? My family is very hungry." Sam thought for a moment and then gave the nut to Tim. "Here you go, Tim. I hope it helps your family." Just then, a big wind came and blew many nuts from the trees. Sam and Tim saw the nuts falling and were very surprised. They both ran to pick up the nuts and shared them with each other. Now, both their families had enough nuts to eat. Sam and Tim were very happy and became good friends.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big box that they could turn into anything. Sometimes it was a castle, sometimes it was a spaceship, sometimes it was a stage. They liked to pretend and have fun. One day, they decided to make the box into a stage and put on a show for their parents. They found some hats and scarves and instruments and got ready. Lily wanted to sing a song that she learned at school. It was a song about jazz, a kind of music that made her happy. Ben wanted to sing a different song, one that he heard on the radio. It was a song about rock, a kind of music that made him excited. They started to quarrel about which song to sing. Lily said that jazz was better because it was smooth and cool. Ben said that rock was better because it was loud and fast. They both wanted to sing their own song and not listen to the other. They raised their voices and pulled the instruments. The box started to shake and fall apart. Their parents heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. They saw the box in pieces and the children angry and sad. They asked them what happened and why they were fighting. Lily and Ben told them about their songs and how they could not agree. Their parents smiled and hugged them. They said that both songs were good and that they could sing them together. They said that music was a way to share and enjoy, not to quarrel and destroy. They said that they were proud of their children for being modest and creative, but that they also needed to be respectful and kind. They helped them fix the box and set up the stage again. They gave them some tips on how to sing in harmony and how to take turns. They also brought some snacks and drinks for the show. Lily and Ben apologized to each other and thanked their parents. They decided to sing both songs, one after the other, and then a third song that they made up together. It was a song about friendship, a kind of feeling that made them happy and excited. They sang and played and had fun. Their parents clapped and cheered and joined them. It was a great show.
