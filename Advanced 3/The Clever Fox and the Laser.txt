Title: The Clever Fox and the Laser

Once there was a clever fox. He loved to play tricks on everyone. One day, he saw a laser! He wanted to touch it, so he did. It made a funny noise and a bright light! He wanted to play a game with the laser, so he pointed it at the trees. When he touched it again, the trees lit up in different colours! Everyone was so surprised! The fox was so excited to be playing with the laser, he showed off his cleverness to all his friends. They all wanted to touch it too, so the fox let them. Soon everyone was laughing and having fun with the laser. The fox was so proud of his clever idea. He had made everyone happy that day, just with a little laser!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy cat named Mr. Whiskers. They loved to play together every day. Lily had a smooth, shiny rock that she liked to hold. It made her feel happy. One day, Lily found a bottle of perfume in her mom's room. It smelled really nice. She sprayed some on Mr. Whiskers, but he did not like it at all. He tried to fight the smell by rolling around on the floor. Lily felt bad for making Mr. Whiskers upset. She gave him a big hug and washed him in the sink. After he was all clean, they played together with the smooth rock. They were both happy again, and Lily knew she would not use perfume on Mr. Whiskers ever again.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little girl named Mia. She loved to go outside and raise her arm up high in the air. She would wave it with a big exciting smile on her face. One day, her mother noticed her doing this to a passerby. She asked Mia what she was doing. Mia explained, "I'm waving to show everyone how happy I am! I'm so gifted". Her mother smiled and said, "That's very sweet, Mia. You should keep waving to show love and happiness to everyone you meet". Mia nodded in agreement and ran out to continue to raise her arm up high and wave to everyone. She was so happy that her movements made everyone smile too. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty skirt. The skirt was old, like something ancient. She loved her skirt very much. One sunny day, Sue went outside to play. The sun was shining bright. Sue met her friend, Tom. Tom saw Sue's skirt and said, "Wow, Sue! Your skirt is so pretty!" Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom! My skirt is very old, but I love it." They played together all day under the shining sun. Sue's ancient skirt sparkled, and they had lots of fun.

One day, a little boy called Johnny was walking with his mom in the garden. They walked for a while and then stopped. Johnny saw a cobweb stretching between two trees. He went closer to look at it. He was fascinated by the webs’ different shapes. "Mom, look! Can I stretch it?", he asked. His mom nodded. Johnny carefully grabbed the cobweb between two fingers and carefully began to stretch it. He noticed that it was becoming bigger and bigger. Suddenly, something nasty happened. Something grabbed Johnny's hand and stung him. It was a bee! Johnny screamed in pain and ran away. The cobweb was also broken. Johnny learnt his lesson: never stretch cobwebs.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a dull book. It sat on a shelf all day long. No one wanted to read it. The book felt sad. One day, a little girl named Lily saw the dull book. She thought, "Maybe the book is not so bad. I will read it." Lily took the book and started to read. She found out that the book was not dull after all! It was a fun story about a cat who loved to eat. In the story, the cat would eat and eat all day long. It would eat big fish, small birds, and even bugs. One day, the cat found a special treat. It was a magic fish! The magic fish told the cat, "If you do not eat me, I will give you a wish." The cat thought about it and said, "Okay, I will not eat you. I wish for more food to eat every day." The magic fish granted the wish, and the cat lived happily ever after with lots of food to eat. Lily loved the story and shared it with her friends. The dull book was now a favorite for everyone.
