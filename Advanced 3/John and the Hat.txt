Title: John and the Hat

The sun shone brightly in the sky. Today was going to be a special day for John. He had a brand new outfit to wear and he couldn't wait to show his friends. He put on a blue shirt, green shorts and a pair of ordinary shoes. John was so excited that he ran outside to the stable. His friends were already there, wearing their new clothes too. They were oohing and ahhing at each others outfits, when suddenly one of them asked, "John why don't you have a hat to wear?" John thought for a moment and replied. "Well, my mother said I don't need to wear a hat because it's not an ordinary day." The rest of his friends laughed and the conversation changed but John kept his thoughts on his special day to himself. He knew his mother was right and even though it wasn't ordinary, it would still be a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Max. Max liked to wear a bathrobe after he took a bath. One day, Max wanted to weigh his bathrobe. Max put the bathrobe on a big toy scale. The scale said the bathrobe was not heavy. Max was happy because he liked his light bathrobe. Max wore his bathrobe and played with his friends. His friends liked Max's bathrobe too. They all had fun and played all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Buddy. Buddy was a very intelligent dog. Buddy had a big friend named Sam. Sam worked in an office. Buddy liked to go to the office with Sam. One day, Buddy wanted to go to the office with Sam. But Sam said, "No, Buddy, you cannot come today." Buddy was sad. He did not want to stay home alone. Buddy refused to stay home. He wanted to go to the office with Sam. So, Buddy followed Sam to the office. When Sam saw Buddy, he said, "Buddy, you are a smart dog, but you must stay home today." Buddy understood and went back home. Later, Sam came home and played with Buddy. They were happy together.

Once upon a time there lived a very selfish boy. His name was Tim. He loved picnics. So one day, he asked his mom if they could have a picnic. His mom said that sounded like a nice idea. Tim decided to get to work and he designed a picnic that was very special. He got ready a big bowl of strawberries, some sandwiches and lots of apple juice. When it was ready, he went outside and started to set up the picnic. But Tim was feeling very selfish. He didn't want to share his picnic with anyone else. So he decided to keep it for himself and his mom. Just then, his best friend Jack rode by on his bicycle. He stopped and asked what Tim was doing. Tim reluctantly told him he was having a picnic, but he was keeping it for himself and his mom. Jack was a bit surprised that Tim was so selfish. He said, "But why don't you share the picnic with me too. That way we can all have fun!" Tim thought about it and he realized Jack was right. He smiled and said, "OK, let's have a picnic together!"

Mommy was so proud of Ann, her three year old daughter. Ann had been helping mama clean their house all morning long. She took great care to dust the shelves, sweep the floor, and make everything sparkle. Mommy saw how hard Ann was working and said, "Ann, you are doing a great job! I am so proud of you!" Ann smiled and kept cleaning. But then Ann started to feel different. Her lungs started to hurt and she started coughing. Mama said, "Ann, you have been working hard. You should take a break. Sit and rest for a while." But Ann kept cleaning. She was determined to finish the job. Finally, Ann's lungs gave out and she couldn't clean anymore. She was too tired, and too sick. Mommy saw that Ann was in pain, and she was no longer proud. Mama wrapped Ann in a blanket and said, "It's alright, dear. We still have a lot to do, but you must take a break. You need to take care of yourself."
