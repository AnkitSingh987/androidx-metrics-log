Title: Max the Dog and the Stick

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a dry stick on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth. Max was very happy with his new stick. He wagged his tail and ran around. Max saw a big cat named Tom. Tom was shivering because it was cold outside. Max wanted to help Tom feel warm. He said, "Tom, let's play with my stick! We can run and jump, and you will not shiver." Tom liked the idea. He said, "Yes, Max! Let's play together." They played with the dry stick. They ran and jumped. Soon, Tom was not shivering anymore. He was warm and happy. Max and Tom played until the sun went down. They were tired but had fun. They said goodbye and went home. Max kept his dry stick as a memory of the fun day he had with Tom.

One day, Tom went to a new place. He wanted to make new friends. He saw a girl and wanted to say hi. Tom said, "Hello! My name is Tom. What is your name?" The girl did not understand Tom's words. She spoke a different language. Tom felt sad. He wanted to be friends with the girl. He saw a sour lemon on the ground. He picked it up and gave it to the girl. The girl smiled and said "thank you" in her language. Tom was happy that she liked the sour lemon. Then, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird could speak both languages! The bird said, "I can help you talk to each other." The bird introduced Tom and the girl. They became good friends and played together all day. The bird helped them talk and have fun.

One day, a little boy named Tom went to the park. He saw a big bike with pedals. He wanted to ride the bike. But his mom warned him, "Be careful, Tom. The bike is too big for you." Tom did not listen. He climbed on the bike and started to pedal. He went very fast. "Look, Mom! I can do it!" he said. But then, something unexpected happened. A little dog ran in front of the bike. Tom tried to stop, but he fell off the bike. He was scared, but he was okay. The little dog came over and licked Tom's face. Tom laughed and said, "I should have listened to my mom."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a daughter who loved to play and imagine. She would dream of being a tough superhero, saving the day. One sunny day, Lily put on her superhero cape and went outside to play. She imagined she was flying high in the sky, looking for people who needed help. She felt strong and tough, just like her superhero friends. As she played, she saw a small kitten stuck in a tree. Lily knew this was her chance to be a tough superhero. She climbed the tree and saved the kitten. The kitten was so happy and thanked Lily. Lily felt proud and knew that even a little daughter could be a tough superhero.

Once upon a time there was a monkey. He was very lonely, as he was the only one of his kind in the forest. He was always looking for friends to play with. One day, he saw something shiny and bright coming his way. It was a magical bird and it had all kind of colours on its feathers. The bird was carrying a big, impressive banana. The monkey was so happy that he started dancing around. He eagerly asked the bird if he could have the banana. The bird said yes, and as it flew away, it told him to share it with somebody else. The monkey was so excited that he forgot what the bird said and quickly started eating the banana. As he was eating it, he noticed the other animals in the forest. He realised that he should have shared the banana with them. At the end of the day, the monkey learned that it is always better to share what you have with others rather than keeping it all for yourself. He never forgot this lesson and he shared his happy moments with other animals in the forest.
