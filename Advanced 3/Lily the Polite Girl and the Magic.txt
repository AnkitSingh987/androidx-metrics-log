Title: Lily the Polite Girl and the Magic

One day, a polite girl named Lily found a magic wardrobe. She was very happy. Lily wanted to see what was inside the wardrobe. She opened the door and saw many pretty clothes. She wanted to try them on. Lily put on a dress from the wardrobe. The dress was very big. She wished the dress would shrink so it would fit her. Suddenly, the dress started to shrink! Lily was very surprised. She thought the wardrobe was magic. Lily called her friend Tom to come and see the magic wardrobe. Tom tried on a big hat. He said, "I wish this hat would shrink." The hat started to shrink too! They were both very happy. They played with the magic wardrobe all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The magic wardrobe started to shrink! It became smaller and smaller. Lily and Tom were very sad. They could not play with the magic wardrobe anymore. The magic wardrobe was too small to use.

Lily and Daddy were playing in the park. They liked to slide down the big hill on a cardboard box. It was fun and fast. Lily felt the wind in her hair and the sun on her face. "Look, Daddy, I can go faster than you!" Lily said, as she pushed the box with her feet. "Wow, you are very impressive, Lily!" Daddy said, smiling. "But be careful, the grass is wet and slippery." Lily did not listen. She wanted to show Daddy how brave she was. She slid down the hill again, but this time she went too fast. She could not stop the box. She slipped off the edge and landed in a puddle. "Splash!" Lily was wet and muddy. She felt cold and scared. She started to cry. "Oh, no, Lily, are you okay?" Daddy said, as he ran to her. He picked her up and hugged her. He checked her for boo-boos. "You are very lucky, Lily. You did not hurt yourself. But you are very dirty and wet. We need to go home and change." "I'm sorry, Daddy. I was silly. I should have listened to you." Lily said, sniffling. "It's okay, Lily. I know you were having fun. But sometimes you have to be careful and follow the rules. They are there to keep you safe." Daddy said, wiping her tears. "I love you, Daddy." Lily said, cuddling him. "I love you too, Lily." Daddy said, kissing her. "You are still my impressive little girl."

James looked proudly at the sand at the beach. "This sand is incredible," he said to his mom. His mom smiled. "I'm so proud of you, James." she said. "You worked really hard to get here, and now you get to enjoy the sand." James smiled. He liked the sand, and he wanted to take some home. So he filled up a small bag with sand and showed it to his mom. "I want to take it home," he said. His mom shook her head and said, "No, that's not allowed. But don't worry, you can receive some nice souvenirs at our house instead." James was excited and proudly carried the souvenirs back home. He couldn't wait to show them to his friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Mary. Mary was very adventurous. One day Mary was outside playing in her garden and saw a hose. She asked her mum what it was and said, "Mum, what is that hose?" Her mum explained that the hose was for watering the plants. She also said, "If you are feeling adventurous, you can use it to spray your friends too!" Mary was very excited and so her mum recommended that they can invite Mary's friends round to play a game with the hose. Mary was thrilled and couldn't wait to share the adventure with her friends. When her friends arrived, they all had fun spraying each other with the hose. Mary's mum smiled as she watched her daughter having so much fun with her friends. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a dancer named Lily. Lily loved to dance all day long. She was very good at dancing. One day, Lily wanted to design a new dance just for her friends. Lily was alert and looked at her friends while they played. She saw her friend Tom jump high, her friend Sue run fast, and her friend Max spin around. Lily thought, "I will put all these moves in my new dance." Lily worked hard to make the dance. She showed her friends the new dance she designed. They all loved it! They jumped high like Tom, ran fast like Sue, and spun around like Max. Lily was happy that she made a fun dance for her friends.
