Title: Billy the Boy and the River

One day, a boy named Billy was walking near the river. He saw a baby duck swimming in the water and he stopped to watch it. He watched as the duck passed him by. Then, Billy noticed a strip of white fabric floating in the water. It was flowing past him very quickly. He thought it looked special and exciting. But he was scared to touch it. Suddenly, a big, dark bird landed on the strip of fabric. It spread its wings and looked like it was protecting it. Billy got even more scared. He ran away from the river and hid behind a tree. Then, he heard a voice. It said, "Don't be scared, Billy! This strip belongs to me! I collect these special strips and now I've found another one!" Billy was relieved. He was happy to know that the strip was safe. He smiled and went home.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a bossy girl named Lily. She liked to tell everyone what to do. All the kids would listen to her because she was very smart. Lily had a big brain. One sunny day, Lily and her friends were playing in the park. They played hide and seek. Lily told her friends, "You must remain here while I count." She counted to ten, and then she looked for her friends. She found them all, except for one boy named Tim. Lily looked and looked for Tim, but she could not find him. She was very surprised. "How could this happen?" she thought. Then, she saw a note on the ground. It said, "I found a secret spot!" Lily was amazed. She had never seen this spot before. She followed the note to the secret spot and found Tim hiding there. Lily realized that sometimes, even bossy people with big brains can learn from others. From that day on, Lily listened to her friends more and was not so bossy. And they all played happily together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a popular bus. This bus had a nice staff who made everyone happy. The bus went from one stop to another, picking up people and taking them to fun places. One day, a little girl named Lily got on the bus with her mom. The bus staff said, "Hi Lily! Welcome to our bus!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you!" She was very excited to ride the popular bus with her mom. As the bus went from stop to stop, Lily and her mom had a great time. They saw many fun things, like a park and a big tree. When it was time to go home, Lily said to the bus staff, "I had so much fun today! Thank you for taking us around!" The staff smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily! We're happy you had fun. Come back soon!" And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was very enthusiastic. Every day she would come running out her door, excited to begin her day. Her mom was in the kitchen making something special. She was forming dough and hummed a happy tune as she worked. When the little girl found out what mom was making, she leapt with joy! Mom said, "The dough will be ready soon. Let's sit down and wait, okay?" The little girl was so enthusiastic she could hardly sit still. She hopped up and down and told mom, "I can't wait!" Mom looked at her with a twinkle in her eye. She said, "I think that when the dough is ready, you'll be the most enthusiastic one of all." The little girl wondered what she meant, but soon the dough was ready and the little girl was so excited she thought she might burst. She saw that mom had made delicious cookies for all to enjoy. The little girl clapped with glee, as she was the most enthusiastic of them all!

Once upon a time, there was a big tree in the west. The tree was very dry. One day, a kind bird came to the tree. The bird wanted to help the tree. So, the bird flew to a big lake and filled her beak with water. The bird flew back to the dry tree and gave the water to the tree. The tree started to feel better. The bird did this many times. Each time, the tree felt better and better. The tree was not dry anymore. One day, the bird found a shiny stone near the lake. The bird took the stone to the tree. The stone was magic! The tree and the bird became friends. The tree could now make water by itself. The bird did not have to fly back and forth anymore. The moral of the story is: when you help others, good things can happen to you too.
