Title: The Farmer and the Duck

Once upon a time, there was a farmer who had a lovely farm. The farmer had many animals. He had cows, pigs, chickens, and a big, big horse. Every day, the farmer would feed the animals. He loved to take care of them. One day, the farmer went to feed the animals, and he saw a little, lost duck. The duck said, "Quack, quack, I am lost. Can you help me?" The farmer said, "Of course, little duck, I will help you find your family." The farmer and the duck went to look for the duck's family. They looked and looked, but they could not find the duck's family. The farmer felt sad for the duck. Then, something unexpected happened. The other animals on the farm started to quack like ducks too! They wanted the lost duck to feel at home. The duck was so happy and said, "Thank you, friends!" The farmer smiled and knew that the duck had found a new family on his lovely farm. The farmer fed all the animals, and they lived happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max liked to protect his family. He was very good at it. Max had a friend named Sam. Sam was a small bird who liked to sing. One day, Sam found an enormous flute. The flute was much bigger than Sam! Sam wanted to play the flute, but it was too big for him. Max saw Sam trying to play the enormous flute. Max wanted to help Sam. Max had an idea. He used his strong legs to push the flute. Now, Sam could play the flute. Sam was very happy. Together, Max and Sam played music with the enormous flute. Max protected Sam while he played. Their family loved the music they made. Max and Sam were very happy. They played music every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little town, there lived a kind dog named Spot. Spot had a big heel that he loved to play with. Every day, Spot would run and jump with his heel. One sunny day, Spot met a new friend named Fluffy. Fluffy was a small, happy cat. They played together all day long. Spot showed Fluffy his heel and they had so much fun. "Let's play a game," said Spot. "We can see who can get the heel first. If you get it, I will surrender and let you keep it." "Okay!" said Fluffy, excited to play the game. They both ran and jumped, trying to get the heel. In the end, Fluffy got the heel first. "I surrender!" laughed Spot. "You are very good at this game." Spot and Fluffy played together every day after that. They were the best of friends and always had a cheerful time.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round pumpkin. It grew in a garden with lots of other healthy plants. The pumpkin was very happy because it was growing bigger and bigger every day. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the garden. She saw the big pumpkin and said, "Wow! What a big pumpkin! I want to take it home and make a yummy pie." The pumpkin was scared, but it knew that Lily was a kind girl. Lily took the pumpkin home and started to make the pie. As she was making the pie, she noticed that the pumpkin was sad. So, Lily decided to restore the pumpkin back to the garden. She said, "Don't worry, pumpkin. I will take you back to your home." The pumpkin was very happy to be back in the garden with its friends. Lily learned that it is important to care for others and make them happy. And from that day on, Lily and the pumpkin became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Mr. Brown. He lived in a big house with lots of rooms. One day, Mr. Brown decided to learn math. So, he went to the town to find a teacher. In the town, he met a kind lady named Miss Green. She was a math teacher. Mr. Brown asked her, "Can you teach me math?" Miss Green said, "Yes, I can help you." They went back to Mr. Brown's big house to learn. They would sit at a table and do math together. Mr. Brown learned to count, add, and take away. He was so happy to learn math with Miss Green's help. As they learned math, they became good friends. They would sit and talk about many things. Mr. Brown loved having a friend to learn with. And they lived happily ever after.
