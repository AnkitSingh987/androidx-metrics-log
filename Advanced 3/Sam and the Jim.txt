Title: Sam and the Jim

One day, Sam walked into the classroom. His friend Jim was already sitting at the back. Sam was jealous. He had hoped to be the first to arrive. “What are you doing, Jim?” Sam asked. “I'm studying,” Jim said. “Studying? What is that?” Sam asked. “It's when I read a poem and try to remember it,” Jim said. Sam thought that was cool! So, every day, Sam and Jim studied a poem together. Sam was very happy to learn with Jim, and not so jealous anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. It liked to sail on the water. One day, the boat met a small candle. The candle was sad because it could not sail. The boat had an idea. It said, "I can help you sail!" The candle was very happy. The boat put the candle on its back and they started to sail together. As they sailed, they saw a fake fish. The fake fish was just a toy. The boat and the candle laughed and played with the toy fish. They had so much fun sailing together. And they were best friends forever.

One day, a lovely cat named Kitty went to play outside. She saw a small box and wanted to examine it. Inside the box, she found some medicine. Kitty did not know what it was, so she took it to her friend, Doggy. "Doggy, look what I found!" said Kitty. "What is it?" Doggy looked at the medicine and said, "I don't know, but let's ask Wise Owl. He knows many things." They went to Wise Owl's tree and showed him the medicine. Wise Owl took a long look and said, "This is special medicine that can make you run very fast! But be careful, it only lasts for a short time." Kitty and Doggy wanted to try the medicine, so they each took a little bit. Suddenly, they started running very fast! They raced around the yard and laughed as they zoomed by. But then, something unexpected happened. They ran so fast that they ended up in a new place they had never seen before! Kitty and Doggy were amazed by the new place, but they knew they had to find their way back home. They asked the animals they met for help and soon found their way back to their lovely home. They were happy to be back, but they also knew they had a great adventure because of the special medicine.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to find new things. One day, Spot went for a walk in the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to find out what was behind it. Spot walked around the tree and found a small cat. The cat was sad and said, "I can't find my way home." Spot wanted to help, so he said, "I will stay with you and help you find your home." The cat was very happy and thanked Spot for being so polite. Together, Spot and the cat looked for the cat's home. They asked other animals in the park if they knew where the cat lived. Finally, a bird told them where the cat's home was. Spot and the cat went to the house, and the cat's family was so happy to see her. They thanked Spot for helping and invited him to stay for dinner. Spot and the cat became best friends, and they always remembered to be polite and kind to each other.

Once upon a time, in a small kitchen, there was a chef named Tom. Tom liked to cook yummy food for his friends. One day, he decided to have a big dinner and invite all his friends. Before the dinner, Tom needed to organize his menu. He thought about what his friends liked to eat. He chose pizza, chicken, and ice cream. Tom knew these were yummy foods that his friends would love. On the day of the dinner, Tom's friends came over. They were so happy to see the yummy food on the menu. They ate the pizza, chicken, and ice cream, and had a great time. Tom was happy that he organized a fun dinner for his friends.
