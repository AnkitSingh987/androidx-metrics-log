Title: The Bulb and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a pretty bulb. The pretty bulb lived in a big garden with many flowers. The pretty bulb liked to talk to the flowers every day. One day, the pretty bulb saw a little girl. The little girl was sad. The pretty bulb wanted to help. So, the pretty bulb started to talk to the little girl. They talked about the sun, the sky, and the pretty flowers in the garden. The little girl started to smile. She was happy now. The pretty bulb was happy too. They became good friends. Every day, the little girl came to the garden to talk to the pretty bulb. They talked and played together, and they were both very happy.

Once there was a child who loved to create. She built blocks, she drew pictures, and she even crafted log cabins out of small sticks in her backyard. One day the child got very excited because she had an idea. She gathered some leaves, wood pieces, twigs, and feathers, and created a special log. She worked very hard and when she was done she smiled because she had made something really fine. The child admired her creation, which she named Loggy, and she spent much of her time carrying Loggy around the house or carrying it while she played in the garden. She created so many wonderful things with Loggy that her parents felt their hearts fill with joy at the sight of it. The child's family knew that for as long as Loggy was around, the house would be filled with lots of fun and happiness.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to learn new things. One day, he saw a big wreck in the park. The wreck was old and broken. Tim was very curious about it. "Mom, can we go see the wreck?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, let's go see it, but be careful." As they walked closer to the wreck, Tim saw a small bird. The bird was honest and told Tim, "I live in the wreck. It is my home." Tim was surprised and wanted to learn more about the bird's home. Tim and the bird became friends. They played and learned together. Tim learned about the bird's home in the wreck and how to be an honest friend. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was very obedient. She always listened to her mommy and daddy. One day, Lily's mommy asked her to wear a pretty dress. Lily went to her room to find the dress. In her room, there was a hanger with the pretty dress. But, when Lily tried to take the dress off the hanger, something unexpected happened. The hanger started to talk! It said, "Wait, Lily! I can help you wear your dress." Lily was surprised, but she let the hanger help her. The hanger was very good at helping Lily wear her dress. After that day, the hanger always helped Lily wear her clothes. And they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat spaghetti. One day, she saw a big, thick plate of spaghetti on the table. She was very happy and wanted to eat it all by herself. But then, her friend Tom came to play. Tom was hungry too. Sue did not want to share her spaghetti. Tom was sad and did not want to play anymore. Sue thought about it and decided to offer some spaghetti to Tom. Sue and Tom shared the thick spaghetti and had a great time playing together. They both learned that sharing makes everyone happy. And that was the day Sue and Tom became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a jolly little town, there lived a happy boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends and share his toys. One day, Tim went to his friend, Sue, to borrow her ball. "Can I borrow your ball, Sue?" Tim asked. Sue looked at him and said, "Yes, but you must give it back tomorrow." While they were playing, a big boy named Rob came and took the ball. He said, "This is my ball now!" Tim and Sue were sad and scared. They didn't know what to do. Rob was a threat to their fun. Tim thought for a moment and said, "Let's ask Rob to play with us. Maybe he will give the ball back if we are nice to him." So, they went to Rob and asked, "Do you want to play with us?" Rob looked surprised, but then he smiled. "Yes, I would like to play with you," he said. They all played together and had a great time. At the end of the day, Rob gave the ball back to Sue. They all became good friends and learned that sharing and being kind can solve problems.
