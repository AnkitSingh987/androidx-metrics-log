Title: The Bug and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a small bug. The bug liked to crawl. It crawled on the grass, the dirt, and the leaves. One day, the bug saw an image on a big rock. The image looked like a big, happy face. The small bug wanted to crawl up to see the happy face. The small bug crawled up the rock. It was hard work, but the bug did not give up. When the bug reached the happy face, it smiled. The small bug was happy it could crawl up and see the image.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk with her mom. They walked and walked until they saw a big tower. Lily liked the tower because it was very tall. Lily wanted to observe the tower more, so she asked her mom if they could go closer. They walked up to the tower and saw that it was made of fake bricks. It was not a real tower, but a toy one. Lily and her mom laughed and played with the fake tower. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, they went home and told everyone about the big, fake tower they found.

Once upon a time there was a wise old man called Bob. Bob had a very special knife. Wherever Bob went, the knife went with him. One day Bob was walking along the road when he heard a voice calling, "Hey Bob! Can I play?" It was a little girl called Mary. "Sorry Mary," said Bob, "I have to quit playing now. I have to get going and the knife must come with me." Mary understood, "Well okay Bob. Maybe I will see you another time and you can play with me then." Bob said goodbye to Mary and kept walking with his knife. He was wise enough to know when to quit and stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna who loved to laugh and smile. One day, Anna's mommy and daddy decided to take her to the theater. Anna was very excited! She put on her prettiest dress and they drove off together. At the theater, Anna was amazed! Everywhere she looked, she saw colorful lights and happy people. She smiled wide, excited to see the show. Before the show started, Anna's mommy gave her something special; a big, delicious cookie. Anna bit into it and smiled even wider -- it tasted so good! Suddenly, a loud voice boomed throughout the theater. "Ladies and Gentlemen," the voice said cheerily, "Welcome to the show!" Anna and her family all smiled. Then, the show began!

Once upon a time, there was a soldier. He was very organized. Every day, he would look at his things and make sure they were neat. One day, he went for a walk in the park. As the soldier was walking, he saw a big box. He went to look at it. Inside the box, there were many toys. The soldier was very surprised. He did not know where the toys came from. The soldier decided to take the toys home. He organized them in a neat line. Then, something unexpected happened. The toys started to move and talk! The soldier and the toys became good friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to swing and slide and run. But they also liked to look for rocks. They thought rocks were pretty and fun. One day, they saw a big pile of waste near the fence. It was dirty and smelly and had many things in it. Lily and Ben wanted to see if there were any rocks in the waste. They ran to the pile and started to dig. "Look, I found a shiny rock!" Lily said, holding a metal can. "It is round and smooth and has a picture on it." "Let me see!" Ben said, grabbing the can. "No, this is not a rock. This is waste. It is bad and sharp and can hurt you." He threw the can back into the pile. But he did not see that the can hit a glass bottle. The bottle broke and made a loud noise. Lily and Ben were scared. They heard a dog bark. They turned around and saw a big, angry dog running towards them. It had black fur and yellow teeth and red eyes. It wanted to bite them. Lily and Ben were very nervous. They did not know what to do. They tried to run away, but the dog was faster. It caught them and bit them hard. Lily and Ben screamed and cried. They were hurt and bleeding. They wished they had not gone to the waste pile. They wished they had stayed on the swing and slide and run. They wished they had listened to their mom and dad. But it was too late. The dog did not let them go. The dog was hungry and mean. It was a bad ending.
