Title: The Bear and Bear and the Rock

One day, a big bear and a small bear went to the woods. They wanted to see who could weigh more. The big bear thought it was a good idea. The small bear did not know what "weigh" meant, but he did not want to be ignorant. So, he agreed to the idea. The big bear said, "Let's find a big rock to see who weighs more." They found a big rock and the big bear stood on it. The rock did not move. The small bear stood on the rock, and it moved a little. The big bear laughed, "I am heavier than you!" But then, something unexpected happened. The rock was not a rock, but a big, angry turtle! The turtle was mad that the bears stood on him. He chased the bears out of the woods. The big bear and the small bear were scared and ran away fast. They never went back to the woods again.

One day, there was a dog named Patch who was feeling very sick. He had been feeling bad for weeks. Patch's family hoped that he would get better. They took him to the veterinarian. The veterinarian was very modest and kind. The veterinarian checked Patch carefully and sadly shook his head. He said that Patch's sickness was too advanced for him to help. Patch's family was very sad. The veterinarian said there was nothing more he could do. Patch's family had to say goodbye to Patch that day. They all hugged him and wished him well. As Patch passed away, his family hoped he was at peace now.

One day, a tiny bug named Bob found a tidy, round thing in his home. He did not know what it was, but it had a spark on it. Bob wanted to show it to his best friend, Lily the butterfly. Bob went to Lily and said, "Look what I found, Lily! It has a spark!" Lily looked at the tidy, round thing and said, "I don't know what it is, but it's nice. Let's spin it and see what happens!" When they tried to spin the round thing, it made a funny sound. They both laughed. But then, another bug named Tom came and saw the round thing with the spark. Tom wanted to take it away from Bob and Lily. "Give it to me!" said Tom. "No, we found it first!" said Bob. Lily said, "Let's all play with it together!" Tom agreed, and they all took turns spinning the tidy, round thing with the spark. They all played happily, and the spark on the round thing made them laugh. They learned that it was more fun to share and play together.

Once upon a time, there was a penguin who was very crazy. He did all kinds of silly things! One day the penguin had an idea. He wanted to listen to what people were saying. So he hid and listened very carefully. First he heard a little girl laughing. The penguin wanted to laugh too, so he started hopping around in a funny way. Everyone around him started laughing. Suddenly, the penguin heard a dog barking. He started making a loud noise back at the dog. It was so silly! Everyone laughed even more. The penguin heard a baby crying. He tried to cheer it up by singing a song. It worked and the baby stopped crying. Everyone smiled and clapped. The penguin had fun listening to all the sounds. He was so happy that he started dancing. Everyone clapped and laughed. The penguin had a great time being so crazy!

Spencer was walking to the park, when he noticed something sweet. He looked around and saw a sandwich! Spencer was surprised. He knew it was for him, because it had his name on it. He took the sandwich and bit into it. It tasted so good! He started to dance around in surprise. He really liked the sandwich because it was so sweet. After Spencer finished eating the sandwich, he started playing in the park. He ran and laughed and felt so full of joy. He was so surprised by this sweet sandwich! Spencer had a lot of fun that day, and he was grateful to whoever made him the sweet sandwich. He will never forget his surprise!
