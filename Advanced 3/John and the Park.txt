Title: John and the Park

John was a gentle 3-year-old who wanted to learn new words. So, his parents got him a special dictionary. The dictionary was so big John had to part it in half to be able to carry it. John spent hours sitting on the floor flipping through his dictionary, learning the new words. It was his favorite thing to do. He loved being able to understand things that other kids couldn't. One day, John's Mom and Dad had a surprise for him. They were going to the park. Excitedly, John grabbed his dictionary and followed his parents outside. When they arrived at the park, they went over to the swings. John knew what was coming even before his parents said it. With a smile, he opened up his dictionary. As he rode the swing, he practiced his words. John had been learning all day with his dictionary and he was proud to show off all the new words he had learned. Everyone at the park was in awe at how much John had learned! John felt so proud of himself and knew his dictionary played an important part in his success.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big, unknown toy. It was new to him. Tim wanted to play with the toy. Tim walked up to the toy and grabbed it. He felt happy. He pushed the start button on the toy. The toy began to move and make fun noises. Tim laughed and played with the toy for a long time. After a while, Tim's mom called him. It was time to go home. Tim stopped playing with the toy and said goodbye. He knew he could come back to the park and play with the unknown toy again another day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very honest boy. He always told the truth to his mom and dad. Tim had a club with his friends, and they would meet under a big tree in his backyard. One sunny day, Tim and his friends were playing in their club. They were having so much fun. They played games, ate snacks, and laughed a lot. Suddenly, they heard a noise coming from behind the tree. Tim and his friends decided to peek behind the tree to see what was making the noise. To their surprise, they found a little lost puppy. The puppy was very cute and looked scared. Tim knew that the honest thing to do was to find the puppy's home. Tim and his friends worked together to find the puppy's family. They asked people in their neighborhood if they knew the puppy. Finally, they found the puppy's family, and they were so happy to have their puppy back. Tim felt good about being honest and helping the puppy. His friends were proud of him too. They knew that their club was special because they always did the right thing. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a purple bee. The bee lived in a big tree. The bee was very happy. One day, the purple bee wanted to play. The bee saw a flower. The bee went to the flower and shoot out some honey. The honey was sweet and sticky. The purple bee played all day. The sun went down and the bee was tired. The bee went back to the big tree and went to sleep. The purple bee had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a fancy dress that she loved to wear. One day, Sally and her mom went to a big store to buy some things. While they were in the store, Sally saw a big door. She asked her mom, "What is that?" Her mom said, "That is the exit. It is where we go to leave the store." Sally thought about the exit and wanted to go there. Sally's mom saw that she was looking at the exit. She said, "Sally, let's finish shopping first." Sally agreed and they kept shopping. When they were done, her mom suggested, "Now we can go to the exit and go home." Sally was happy to go through the exit, holding her mom's hand in her fancy dress.
