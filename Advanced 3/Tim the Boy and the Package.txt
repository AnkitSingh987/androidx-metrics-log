Title: Tim the Boy and the Package

One day, a little boy named Tim found a package near his house. He was very happy and excited. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, I found a package! Can I open it?" His mom looked at him and said, "No, Tim. That package is not yours. You should not open it." Tim felt sad but he was also a bit naughty. When his mom was not looking, he took the package to his room and opened it. Inside the package, there were lots of colorful candies. Tim ate some of them and they tasted very sweet. He then hid the package under his bed. Later that day, Tim's mom found the opened package and the candies. She was very angry and said, "Tim, you were very naughty! You should not have opened the package and eaten the candies. They are not yours!" Tim started to weep because he knew he did something wrong. Tim's mom told him that he had to give the package back to the person who lost it. They went to their neighbor's house and found out that the package belonged to them. Tim had to say sorry to the neighbor and give back the candies he did not eat. The neighbor was very sad because the candies were for their child's birthday party. Tim felt very bad and knew that he should never be naughty again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a good girl who always tried to behave. One day, Lily's mom was in the kitchen, and she was using a knife to cut some fruits. Lily saw the knife and thought it was shiny and pretty. She wanted to touch it, but her mom said, "No, Lily! That knife is sharp and can hurt you. You must behave and not touch it." Lily listened to her mom and did not touch the knife. Later that day, it was very hot outside, and Lily's mom made some lemonade. She poured it into a glass and gave it to Lily. But when Lily took a sip, she was surprised! The lemonade was not cold like she expected, it was hot! Lily's mom had mixed up the hot water with the cold water by mistake. They both laughed, and Lily's mom made some cold lemonade for them to enjoy together.

One day, Jack was walking through the park. He was feeling very lonely. Suddenly, he saw a glove on the ground. He bent down to pick it up and as he did, a strong gust of wind blew it away. Jack chased the glove but it continued to fall further and further away. Finally, Jack stopped and called out, "Wait! Come back!" But the glove didn't answer. Jack was about to give up when he heard a voice from the sky. He looked up and saw a bird soaring high above. The bird said, "Don't worry, I'll get it for you!" Jack watched in amazement as the bird caught the glove in its beak and flew back down to him. Jack smiled as the bird dropped the glove into his hands. He said, "Thank you!" The bird smiled back and flew away, leaving Jack feeling less lonely. He continued his walk with the glove in hand.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a shy little girl named Lily. She was so shy that she didn't talk to anyone. Today, she saw a tiny ant trying to carry a big leaf. The ant was having a hard time, but it didn't give up. Lily went closer to the ant and said, "Hi, little ant. You are small, but you carry that big leaf. I am shy, but I want to be brave like you." The ant looked up at Lily and said, "Hi, Lily. You can be brave too. Just try and never give up." Lily decided to be brave like the ant. She started talking to other kids and made many friends. She learned that it's okay to be shy, but it's also important to be brave and try new things. And just like the ant, Lily never gave up.
