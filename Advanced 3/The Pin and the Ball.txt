Title: The Pin and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a pin. The pin was very sad because it was always separate from the other pins. One day, the pin saw a big, red ball. The pin wanted to be friends with the ball, so it went up to the ball and said, "Hi! Can we be friends?" The big, red ball looked at the pin and said, "Sure! We can be friends. But first, you must help me with a task. I need to become successful by winning the race with the other balls." The pin wanted to help its new friend, so it thought of a plan. The pin would hide in the grass near the race track. When the other balls came near, the pin would jump out and stop them. That way, the big, red ball could win the race and be successful. The day of the race came, and the pin hid in the grass. The race started, and the big, red ball began to roll. The other balls came closer and closer. The pin jumped out to stop them, but something unexpected happened. Instead of stopping the other balls, the pin popped the big, red ball! The big, red ball lost the race, and the pin was very sad. It had tried to help its friend, but instead, it had made things worse. The pin went back to being separate from the other pins, and it never tried to help anyone again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad and miserable. He wanted to play outside, but it was raining. So, Tim stayed inside his house and tried to find something fun to do. Tim found a nail on the floor. He picked it up and looked at it. He thought, "I can use this nail to make a picture on the wall." Tim started to tap the nail on the wall, making a little noise. He was having fun and forgot about the rain outside. But the noise from the nail began to disturb his mom, who was taking a nap. She woke up and saw Tim playing with the nail. She said, "Tim, please do not disturb me with that noise." Tim felt sorry and stopped. His mom gave him a big hug and they played a quiet game together. Tim was not miserable anymore.

Lily was a tall girl who liked to go to the zoo with her mom. She loved to see all the different animals, but her favorite was the otter. She liked how the otter swam and played in the water, and how it had a shiny brown coat and a long tail. One day, Lily saw a new otter in the zoo. It was smaller than the other otters, and it looked scared and lonely. Lily felt sorry for the new otter, and wanted to be its friend. She asked her mom if she could tell the new otter a story. Her mom said yes, and helped Lily climb up on a bench near the otter's pool. Lily looked at the new otter and smiled. She said, "Hello, otter. My name is Lily. I have a story for you. Do you want to hear it?" The new otter looked at Lily with curiosity. It swam closer to the bench and nodded its head. Lily was happy that the otter wanted to listen. She said, "Once upon a time, there was a tall girl who liked to go to the zoo. She saw a new otter who was scared and lonely. She told the otter a story, and they became friends. The end." The new otter liked Lily's story. It clapped its paws and made a happy noise. It jumped out of the water and gave Lily a wet hug. Lily giggled and hugged the otter back. She said, "You are a good otter. I like you. Do you want to hear another story?" The new otter nodded its head again. Lily smiled and said, "Okay, let me tell you another story. This one is about a big bear who liked to eat honey..."

Once upon a time there was a lady. She was doing her work, but she wasn't very happy. She frowned a lot and her work was getting very dirty. Then one day a little girl came along and watched the lady working. She was only 3 years old and she was curious. She asked, why do you frown? The lady answered, because my work is so dirty. I want it to be clean, but it never seems to work out. So the little girl offered to help and the lady was very happy. Together, the two of them cleaned the lady's work and soon it was shining. The lady smiled and the little girl was very proud. The lady thanked the little girl for her help and she hugged her. From then on, the lady always smiled when she was doing her work.
