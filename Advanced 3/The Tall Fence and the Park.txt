Title: The Tall Fence and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a tall fence in a park. All the kids wanted to play on it but no one dared to jump over it. Johnny was different, though. He was always brave. One day, he decided he was brave enough to jump over the fence. He looked around to make sure all the other kids were watching. Then, he took a deep breath and jumped. He made it! Johnny was very proud of himself, and the other kids clapped for him. Johnny jumped again and again, until he wasn't scared of the fence anymore. All the other kids were brave too, and they all jumped the fence together. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo named Hippy. Hippy was different from other hippos because he had a blue spot on his head. One day, Hippy met a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was flying around and saw Hippy's blue spot. Chirpy asked, "Why do you have a blue spot, Hippy?" Hippy said, "I don't know, but I believe it makes me special." Chirpy and Hippy became good friends. They played together every day, and Hippy felt happy with his blue spot. One day, a mean crocodile saw Hippy and Chirpy playing. The crocodile did not like that Hippy was different. The crocodile said, "I don't believe you should be friends with Chirpy. You are not the same." Hippy was sad, but he did not listen to the crocodile. The crocodile was angry and wanted to hurt Hippy. So, when Hippy was not looking, the crocodile bit Hippy's leg. Hippy cried out in pain, and Chirpy flew away in fear. Hippy was hurt and could not play anymore. The crocodile swam away, leaving Hippy all alone.

Dan and his wife May like to play games. They have a big box of cards with pictures on them. Sometimes they play a game where one of them picks a card and the other one has to guess what it is. One day, Dan picked a card and showed it to May. It had a picture of a yellow fruit with a peel. "Can you guess what this is?" he asked. May looked at the card and smiled. "I know what this is. It is a banana. I like bananas. They are sweet and soft." Dan nodded. "You are right. It is a banana. You are very good at this game. Do you want to pick a card now?" May nodded. She reached into the box and pulled out a card. It had a picture of a red flower with many petals. "Can you guess what this is?" she asked. Dan looked at the card and frowned. He did not know what it was. He had never seen a flower like that before. "I don't know what this is. It is very pretty, but I don't know its name." May giggled. "It is a rose. It is a special flower that people give to someone they love. I love you, so I will give you this card." Dan smiled. He felt happy and warm. He hugged May and thanked her. "You are very generous. You always give me nice things. I love you too, so I will give you a kiss." They kissed and hugged and played some more. They had fun with their cards and with each other. They were happy and they loved each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big dragon. The dragon lived in an icy cave. The dragon liked to play with his friend, the bell. One day, the dragon went to the bell to play. He wanted to make the bell ring. The dragon hit the bell, but it did not ring. The dragon was sad. Then, a small bird came to help. The bird flew up and hit the bell. The bell rang loud and clear. The dragon was happy. The bird and the dragon became best friends. They played and made the bell ring every day. The dragon learned that even small friends can do big things.

Once there was a boy named Jimmy. He was only three years old, but he was very brave and had a heart full of love. He wanted to help his mommy around the house, so he asked if he could use the mop. His mommy smiled and said yes. She showed Jimmy how to use the mop, and how it would help her keep the floors clean. Jimmy was so excited to help. He was a very successful mop master! After Jimmy was done mopping, his daddy came home from work. Daddy was so proud of Jimmy's hard work and helpfulness. He supported him and told him how proud he was. Jimmy smiled and thanked his dad. He was so happy to receive his dad's support. He knew that he had done a very successful job. And he was full of pride too - he was becoming a great helper!
