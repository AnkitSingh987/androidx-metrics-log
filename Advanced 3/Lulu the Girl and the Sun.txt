Title: Lulu the Girl and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Lulu was in her backyard. All of a sudden, she heard an amazing noise. She looked up in the sky and saw a beautiful lightning bolt. Lulu was so excited that she started to nod her head. The lightning was moving so fast, it was like it was chasing something. Suddenly, it started to rain. Lulu's mom came out of the house and told her it was time to come inside. Lulu wanted to stay outside and see the lightning. Her mom smiled and nodded her head. So, Lulu stayed outside in the rain, watching the beautiful lightning that was flashing and moving so fast across the sky. She was so excited, she even clapped her hands. After a while, the lightning and the rain stopped, and the sun came out. Lulu waved goodbye to the lightning and nodded her head. Then, she ran inside with a big smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was fearful of the dark. Every night, she would pray to feel safe. Her mom would hug her tight and say, "Don't worry, Lily. I'm here." One day, Lily saw a small kitten. The kitten was also fearful. It opened its mouth and said, "Meow." Lily wanted to help the kitten feel safe, just like her mom did for her. So, she brought the kitten home. Lily told her mom about the kitten. Her mom smiled and said, "Let's pray for the kitten to feel safe too." They prayed together, and the kitten felt happy. From that day on, Lily and the kitten were never fearful of the dark again.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing outside. She saw a big red apple on the ground. "Yummy!" thought Lucy. She picked up the apple and took a big bite. But when she bit the apple, it tasted disgusting! "Yuck!" said Lucy. She didn't want to eat it anymore. Lucy's friend Tom came over and saw the apple. "Can I have a bite?" he asked. Lucy didn't want Tom to eat the disgusting apple, so she said, "No, don't bite it! It's yucky!" Tom listened to Lucy and didn't bite the apple. Just then, a funny little squirrel came running up to them. It picked up the apple and took a bite. The squirrel didn't think it was disgusting at all! In fact, it loved the apple. The squirrel looked at Lucy and Tom and said, "Thank you for the yummy apple!" Lucy and Tom were very surprised. They didn't know squirrels could talk! They laughed and played with the squirrel until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Speedy. Speedy was a very different car because he could change colors. He loved to go very fast and play with his friends. One day, Speedy went to the park and saw a soft blanket on the grass. He thought it was the perfect place to rest after speeding around all day. So, he stopped and lay down on the blanket. While Speedy was resting, his friends came to the park too. They saw the different colored car on the blanket and knew it was their friend Speedy. They all lay down on the blanket with him and took a nice nap. When they woke up, they played and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very foolish boy. He liked to play and jump all day. One day, Tim fell down and hurt his leg. His mom took him to the hospital. At the hospital, the doctor said, "Tim, you need to be careful when you play." Tim felt sad and said, "I will be careful, doctor." The doctor put a bandage on Tim's leg. That night, Tim and his mom prayed. They said, "Please help Tim be careful when he plays." Tim learned to not be foolish. He played carefully and did not get hurt again. The moral of the story is to always be careful when you play.

Once upon a time, there was a girl. She had black hair and liked to play hockey. One day, she came to the hockey field, but the field was empty. She was sad and started to weep. She wanted to play hockey with her friends. Her mom saw her crying and asked her why. The girl said she was sad because there was no one to play hockey with her. The mom took her daughter's hand and said "Let's go. I will take you to a special place where we can play hockey." The girl was excited and they went to a new hockey field. There she found many players and friends ready to play. The girl was so happy that she started to laugh and the tears stopped coming. The girl and her mom played hockey together. They had lots of fun and even made some new friends. From that day on, they played hockey together every day and they were never sad again.
