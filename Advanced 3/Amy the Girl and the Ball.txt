Title: Amy the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play outside with her ball. One day, while playing, her ball went far away. She saw a harmless stranger near her ball. The stranger had a big smile and seemed nice. Amy went to the stranger and said, "Please, can you give me my ball back?" The stranger said, "Sure, but first, come with me to get some candy." Amy thought about it and said, "Okay, I like candy." Amy went with the stranger and they walked far away from her home. They never found any candy. Amy was lost and could not find her way back. She was very sad and scared. The moral of the story is to never go with a stranger, even if they seem harmless.

Once upon a time, there was a rare, big, red ball. It was so big that it could split into two smaller balls. The ball had a band around it, which made it look very pretty. One day, the big red ball met a small blue ball. The small blue ball said, "Hi, big red ball! I like your pretty band. Can you split and play with me?" The big red ball was very happy and said, "Yes, I can split and play with you!" So, the big red ball split into two smaller balls, and they all played together. They rolled around and had lots of fun. The small blue ball was very happy to have new friends, and they all played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to travel. One day, he decided to go to a new town. The town was far away, so the walk was difficult. But Spot was brave and did not give up. When Spot got to the town, he saw a big cat. The cat looked at Spot and said, "Hello, little dog. What is your name?" Spot replied, "My name is Spot. I traveled a long way to see this town." The cat smiled and said, "My name is Whiskers. I can show you around if you want." Spot and Whiskers walked around the town together. They saw many fun things like a park, a big red ball, and a yummy ice cream shop. Spot and Whiskers became good friends. They played together all day. When it was time for Spot to go home, he said, "Thank you, Whiskers. I had a great day." Whiskers replied, "You're welcome, Spot. Come back soon." Spot promised he would and went back to his home, happy to have a new friend in the town.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Emma. She was only three years old, but she was very nosy. She was always looking for something new to do and explore. One day, she went for a walk in the park. She saw a treat and went over to examine it. She had never seen anything like it before. She picked it up and gave it a sniff. It smelled sweet and delicious. Emma wanted to try the treat, but she knew she shouldn't eat something she found in the park. She put the treat back down and kept walking. Soon she noticed another nosy kid who was also examining the treat. She went over to the kid to make sure they were okay. They were having a great time eating and examining the treat. Emma watched with a smile as the other kid examined the treat. Even though she was nosy, she wanted to make sure the other kid was safe. After a few more minutes, Emma said goodbye and went home.

Once, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toys. One day, Tim saw an ashtray on the table. He did not know what it was. He thought it was a toy. He picked it up and started to play with it. Tim's mom saw him playing with the ashtray. She said, "Tim, put that down. It is not a toy." Tim did not want to leave the ashtray. He was persistent. He kept playing with it. Then, something unexpected happened. The ashtray started to move! It was not an ashtray at all. It was a little bug hiding inside. The bug came out and said, "Hello, Tim." Tim was very surprised. He and the bug became good friends.
