Title: Tom the Busy Veterinarian and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a busy veterinarian named Tom. Tom liked to help animals all day long. He had a big room where he kept the animals safe. He always made sure to lock the door so they would not run away. One day, Tom had to go away for a little bit. He asked his friend, Sam, to watch the animals for him. Sam said yes and promised to keep the animals safe. Tom told Sam to lock the door so the animals would not run away. Sam had fun with the animals, but he forgot to lock the door. The animals ran away and got lost. Tom came back and was very sad. He looked for the animals, but he could not find them. The animals were gone, and the story had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because he got a new television in his room. He loved to watch his favorite shows and play fun games on it. His mom and dad were happy to see him so excited. One day, Tim saw a game on television where people had to match shapes and colors. He thought it was so much fun and wanted to play too. Tim asked his mom to help him find things around the house to match. They found socks, cups, and toys that were the same color or shape. Tim was very good at matching and his mom was proud of him. After playing the matching game, Tim learned that it is important to share and help others. He decided to share his toys with his little sister and help her match things too. Tim's mom and dad were very happy to see him being kind and helpful. Tim learned that sharing and helping others made him feel good inside, and that it was more fun than just watching television.

The door opens and a nice lady smiles at them. "Hello, children. Do you want to go to the library?" she asks. Lily and Tom nod. They like the library. There are many books and toys and games. And it is warm and cozy. "Come on, then. Hop in. I will take you there," the lady says. Lily and Tom are happy. They run to the bus and get in. They sit together and look out the window. They see the cold and gray outside, but they do not mind. They are going to the library. They are going to have fun.
