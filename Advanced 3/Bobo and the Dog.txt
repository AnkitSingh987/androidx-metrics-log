Title: Bobo and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Bobo. Bobo liked to play with his ball all day. One day, Bobo saw a shiny coin on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and carried it to his human friend, Tim. Tim was very happy to see the coin. He decided to borrow it and buy a treat for Bobo. So, they went to the store together. At the store, they saw a big jar of treats. Tim gave the coin to the store man and got a treat for Bobo. But the treat was not a normal treat! When Bobo ate it, something unexpected happened. Bobo started to talk like a human! Now, Bobo and Tim could talk to each other. They became the best friends and had many fun adventures together. And they always remembered the special coin that made it all happen.

Once there was a brave bear who was trying to find food in a dark forest. He searched for a long time but couldn't find anything. Just then he saw a big armchair with some food on it. He was so happy that he quickly ran to the armchair. The bear was about to take the food when he saw a big, mean lion. The bear was terrified and tried to run away but the lion growled and said, "If you want the food, you must answer my riddle." The brave bear knew he had no choice and he had to reply. The lion asked, "What has a face but no eyes?". The bear thought and thought but he was running out of time. So he grabbed the food and ran back into the forest. The lion was so angry that he chased the bear. The bear ran fast but the lion was faster and he caught the bear. The bad lion ate the bear and the brave bear met his end.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle princess named Lily. She was going to marry a prince named Jack. Lily wanted a big diamond for her marry day. On the marry day, Lily and Jack were very happy. But, a big twist happened! A big bird came and took the diamond away. Lily and Jack were sad. They did not know what to do. Lily and Jack went to find the diamond. After a long walk, they found the big bird. The big bird was gentle too! The bird gave the diamond back. Lily, Jack, and the big bird became friends. They had a fun marry day.

Lisa and her mom went to the store. Lisa saw an amazing balloon and asked her mom if she could have it. "Oh, a big, colorful balloon," her mom said, "that's a very generous idea. Unfortunately, we can't afford it." Lisa began to struggle and said, "Can we try, Mommy? Please!" Lisa's mom smiled and said, "Okay sweetie, we'll try." They both went to the shopkeeper. Her mom was a bit shy but Lisa shouted with all her might. "One balloon please!" The shopkeeper smiled and said, "What color of balloon would you like?" Lisa's eyes sparkled and she pointed to a pretty green one. The shopkeeper seemed very generous and didn't charge them anything. Lisa and her mom thanked the shopkeeper and went out. Lisa proudly held her balloon in her hands and smiled. "I love it, mommy!" said Lisa, "thank you for getting me this wonderful balloon." Her mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, sweetie - you did a great job of struggling for your balloon." Lisa grinned and hugged her mom, and they continued their journey. Lisa held her balloon up and watched as it floated in the air, feeling very proud of what she had achieved.

Once upon a time, there was a hero. The hero was very fat. He liked to eat a lot. He lived in a small house in a big forest. The hero had many friends who lived in the forest too. One day, the hero saw a big tree. He wanted to climb the tree to see the whole forest. He tried to climb, but he was too fat. His friends, the birds, saw him trying. They wanted to help the hero. The birds came to the hero and said, "We will help you climb!" They took a big rope and tied it around the hero. Then, the birds flew up and pulled the rope. The hero went up, up, up into the tree. From the top of the tree, the hero saw the whole forest. He was very happy.
