Title: Bella the Girl and the Bow

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Bella. Bella was only three years old, but she was brave and strong. One day, Bella went to the kitchen to get a snack. When she opened the freezer, she found a tight bow! It was so pretty - it seemed to be made of silver. Bella was so excited. Bella picked up the tight bow and ran to show her mummy. She shouted "Mummy, Mummy, look at the pretty bow I found in the freezer!" Mummy looked at the pretty silver bow in Bella's hands and said, "That bow looks lovely, Bella! Where did you find it?" "I found it in the freezer!" Bella said with a big smile. "Isn't it gorgeous?" "Yes, it is," said Mummy. "It must have been special and someone put it away in the freezer to keep it safe. Now, why don't you put the bow in your room so you can always keep it safe?" So Bella took the tight bow to her room, where she could look at it every day. From that day on, the tight bow had a special place in Bella's heart.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived an adventurous leopard. He loved to play and run all day. The leopard had lots of friends, like birds, monkeys, and rabbits. One day, the adventurous leopard wanted to play a new game. He asked his friends, "Please, come play with me." All his friends were excited and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played hide-and-seek, and the leopard was very good at finding his friends. The birds flew high, the monkeys climbed trees, and the rabbits hid in the grass. But the adventurous leopard found them all. They all had a fun day and said "please" and "thank you" to each other. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy jet. He would zoom it around his room and pretend to fly high in the sky. One day, Tim's mom taught him how to fold paper to make a paper jet. Tim was so happy! He folded the paper just like his mom showed him. Soon, he had a paper jet to play with too. Tim and his toy jet went outside to play with the paper jet. "Ready, set, go!" said Tim as he threw the paper jet into the air. The paper jet flew far and high, just like the toy jet. Tim was so proud of his paper jet, and he played with it all day long.

Once upon a time, in a modest little house, there was a girl named Lily. She liked to help her mom with the laundry. One sunny day, while they were hanging the clothes outside, a small bug came to play. The bug said, "Hi, I'm Buzz! Can I play with you?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Buzz! You can help us with the laundry!" Buzz was happy and started to help. They had a lot of fun together. But then, Buzz felt hungry. He saw a leaf and decided to bite it. "Yum, this is tasty!" he said. Lily and her mom laughed. They finished the laundry, and Buzz went back to his tree. They all became good friends and played together every day.

Once there was a little girl. She was wearing a pink skirt, and she was very excited. She ran out to the garden and sat down beside a big puddle. The puddle was filled with water, and it was very sparkly. She took off her shoes and stuck her feet in the water. The water was cold but it felt nice. She was so happy that she started to splash around. Every time she moved the water made more and more sparkles. She played and splashed until she was out of breath. But then she became a bit anxious. She looked around and could not see her mommy. Where had she gone? She sat still in the puddle and looked around anxiously, but she still could not see her. However, a few moments later she heard a voice calling her. It was her mummy, and she had been watching the whole time! They hugged each other and went inside. The little girl was still wearing her wet skirt, but she was so happy she did not mind.
