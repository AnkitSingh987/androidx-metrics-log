Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy matches. He liked to do tricks with them. But sometimes, Tim was careless with his matches. One day, Tim was playing with his matches near the big tree. He was so happy that he did not see the dry leaves on the ground. Tim dropped a match on the leaves. The leaves started to burn and the big tree was in danger! Tim's mom saw the fire and ran to help. She put out the fire with water. Tim was very sad and sorry. His mom told him not to be careless with matches. Tim learned his lesson and promised to be more careful. The big tree was safe, and everyone was happy. The moral of the story is to always be careful and not to play with dangerous things like matches.

One day, a group of animals decided to march to the big pole in the park. The animals were a dog, a cat, a bird, and a deaf rabbit. The rabbit could not hear, but he could see and hop very well. They all wanted to play together at the pole. When they got to the pole, the dog said, "Let's play tag!" The cat said, "Yes, I like tag!" The bird said, "Me too!" The deaf rabbit could not hear them, but he saw them running and hopping, so he joined in the fun. They all played tag around the pole and had a great time. After a while, they got tired and sat down to rest. The bird said, "That was fun!" The dog said, "Yes, I love playing with you all!" The cat said, "We should do this again!" The deaf rabbit could not hear them, but he could see their happy faces. He knew they all had a good time playing together at the big pole.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a big red balloon. She was so happy. She played with the balloon all day. She did not want to play with her boring toys anymore. Amy's friend, Tim, came over to play. Tim saw the red balloon and said, "Wow! That is a big balloon! Can I play with it too?" Amy said, "Yes, we can play together." They played with the balloon all day. They threw it up in the air and caught it. But then, Tim accidentally let the balloon drop. It hit the ground and made a loud noise. They were scared, but then they laughed. They picked up the balloon and played with it some more.

One day, a happy dog named Max was playing on the lawn. He was enthusiastic and loved to run and jump. Max was having so much fun playing with his ball. Then, a new friend named Tim came to play. Tim was a little cat who lived next door. Tim said, "Max, can I play with you?" Max was happy to have a new friend, so he said, "Yes, I accept! Let's play together!" As they were playing, something unexpected happened. A big bird swooped down and took Max's ball! Max and Tim were surprised. They looked at each other and decided to work together to get the ball back. They chased the bird and got the ball back. Max and Tim became best friends and played on the lawn every day.

Once upon a time there was a small village near the mountains. In this village there lived a boy, Jeremy. Jeremy was very brave and he liked to explore the forest near his village. One day, Jeremy went for a walk in the forest and he heard a loud noise coming from the mountain. He got scared, but he bravely decided to go near the mountain. To his surprise he found a giant dragon! The dragon was so big and so scary that Jeremy was afraid. But he didn't give up. He closed his eyes and started to pray. He asked for help and he said that if the dragon would leave his village alone he would be very happy. Suddenly, the dragon went away and left the village alone. Jeremy was so happy, because he learned that with faith and bravery he can solve any problem. Since then Jeremy did not fear anything, because he knew that when everything seemed lost, he could always pray to the heavens for help.
