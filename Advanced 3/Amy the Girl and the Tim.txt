Title: Amy the Girl and the Tim

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big, ugly tree in her yard. Amy was scared of the tree, but she wanted to play near it. One sunny day, Amy's friend, Tim, came over. They went outside to play. Tim saw the ugly tree and said, "I dare you to touch it!" Amy was scared, but she wanted to show Tim she was brave. So, she slowly walked up to the tree and touched it. As soon as Amy touched the tree, a big lollipop fell from it! She picked it up and showed it to Tim. They were both very surprised and happy. They shared the lollipop and played near the ugly tree all day. From that day on, Amy wasn't scared of the tree anymore.

One day, Tom and Sam went to play outside. It was a clear day with a big blue sky. They found a bow and some arrows. They took turns shooting the arrows at a tree. Tom saw a thermometer on the tree. He said, "Look, Sam! The tree has a thermometer!" Sam looked and said, "Wow! The tree must be sick!" They decided to help the tree feel better. Suddenly, a bird came and took the thermometer. It was not a sick tree after all! The bird said, "Thank you for finding my thermometer!" Tom and Sam were happy they could help the bird. They played with the bow and arrows until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was an honest boy named Tim. Tim loved to sort his toys. One day, he found a toy motorcycle in the park. He was very happy. Tim took the motorcycle home. His friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the motorcycle and said, "Wow, that is a nice toy! Where did you get it?" Tim told him the truth. "I found it in the park. I want to find the owner." Tim and Sam went to the park. They asked other kids, "Is this your motorcycle?" At last, they found the owner, a little girl named Lily. Lily was happy to see her toy again. She said, "Thank you for being honest and giving my toy back." The moral of the story is to always be honest and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to read her big book with many pages. She was always ready to read a new page each day. One day, Lily went to the store with her mom. She saw a toy she really wanted. Her mom said, "Lily, you need to pay for the toy if you want it." So, Lily gave the man some money and he gave her the toy. Lily was so happy and ready to play with her new toy. She went home and showed it to her dad. He was proud of her for paying for it all by herself. And after playing with her toy, Lily read a new page in her big book before bedtime.

There was a family of four who went out to eat. When they arrived at the restaurant, they were all feeling a bit restless. Mama and Papa handed the kids a menu and asked them what they wanted. The three year old, James, looked at the menu and said, "Ice cream please!" Mama and Papa laughed and reminded him that the menu said food and not desserts. Mama asked James, "What kind of food do you want? Hamburgers? Fries? Pizza?" James said he wanted burgers, so Papa called the waiter over to take their orders. After the waiter left, Papa started to talk about his day. He told a funny story that made everyone laugh. Mama quieted the family down when the food came and everyone began to eat. James ate his burger and fries with a smile. He enjoyed the food, but he still had one thing on his mind – ice cream!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl and she loved to chew. She chewed on everything she found - it was her favorite thing to do! She found some lovely yarn in the cupboard and she tucked it in her pocket. Then she went off and found a nice spot where she could have a chew. The girl chewed and chewed on the yarn and it tasted so healthy and delicious. She enjoyed her time so much that she made sure to put the yarn back in the cupboard afterwards. The little girl was so happy she chewed all the yarn, it really made her feel good. She was full and content and smiled, knowing that she was being very healthy!
