Title: Lily and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a lucky girl because she had a big bookshelf in her room. Every day, she would pick a book from the bookshelf and enjoy reading it with her mom. One sunny day, Lily and her mom picked a book about a happy cat. They sat down and read the book together. Lily enjoyed the story very much. She liked the happy cat and how it played with its friends. The next day, Lily picked another book from her bookshelf. This time, it was about a funny dog. Lily and her mom read the book and laughed. They had a lot of fun. Lily felt lucky to have so many books to enjoy.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She loved to play with her violin. One day, Lily saw a sad boy named Tom sitting under a tree. Lily wanted to make Tom happy. Lily went to Tom and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tom said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." Lily wanted to help Tom, so she thought of an idea. She took out her violin and started to play a happy song. While playing the violin, Lily said, "Let this song remind you of happy times." Tom began to smile and feel better. They both looked for Tom's toy and found it behind the tree. Tom was very happy and thanked Lily. The moral of the story is that we can use our talents to help others and make them happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a soft, red velvet toy named Mr. Bear. Lucy and Mr. Bear were very happy together. They played all day in the sun, and at night, they slept together in her bed. One day, while playing, Lucy saw a big dog. The dog seemed very nice and friendly. "Hi dog, do you want to play with me and Mr. Bear?" Lucy asked. The dog wagged its tail and barked, "Woof, woof!" Lucy thought the dog was happy to play with them. Lucy and the dog played with Mr. Bear. But then, the dog grabbed Mr. Bear in its mouth and ran away. Lucy tried to catch the dog, but it was too fast. She cried and cried, as she could not find Mr. Bear again. Lucy was very sad, and she missed her velvet friend.

Once upon a time, in a pretty garden, there lived a little bee. The bee was very happy. It liked to fly from flower to flower, getting sweet nectar. One day, the bee met a sack. The sack was big and brown. The bee asked the sack, "What are you doing here?" The sack said, "I am waiting for someone to fill me with yummy things." The bee had an idea. It flew to all the pretty flowers and got lots of sweet nectar. Then, the bee gave the nectar to the sack. The sack was very happy. Now, the sack and the bee were friends. They spent every day together in the pretty garden. And they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a clever girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and eat yummy food. One day, her mom gave her an onion to play with. Lily thought it was a funny toy. Lily played with the onion all day. She rolled it, tossed it, and hid it. But then, the onion disappeared! Lily looked everywhere. She looked under the bed, behind the chair, and even in her toy box. Finally, Lily found the onion in the garden. It was hiding under a big green leaf. She was so happy to find her funny toy. She ran back inside to show her mom. Lily and her mom laughed and played with the onion together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a thoughtful boy. He liked to watch videos on his mom's phone. His favorite video had a big, red ball that bounced up and down. One day, Tim's big sister, Sue, saw him watching the video. Sue liked to tease Tim. She took the phone and hid it behind her back. Tim was sad and asked for the phone back. Sue saw that Tim was sad, so she gave the phone back. Tim was happy again. He showed Sue the video with the big, red ball. They both laughed and watched the video together. From that day on, Sue was more thoughtful and did not tease Tim anymore.
