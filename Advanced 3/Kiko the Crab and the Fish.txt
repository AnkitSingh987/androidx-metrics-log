Title: Kiko the Crab and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little crab named Kiko. Kiko lived on the shore with his family. One day, Kiko felt confused. He did not know where his family went. Kiko saw a big fish named Fifi. He asked Fifi, "Do you know where my family is?" Fifi said, "I saw them go that way." She pointed with her fin. Kiko thanked Fifi and went that way. As Kiko walked along the shore, he found his family. They were playing in the sand. Kiko was happy and not confused anymore. He joined his family and they all played together on the shore.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree with his family. Every day, he would fly around and sing happy songs. One day, Tim saw a big crack in the tree. He was worried and wanted to fix it. Tim thought if he could find something to separate the crack, the tree would be safe again. Tim found a stick and pushed it into the crack. Suddenly, the tree started to shake! Tim was scared, but then something unexpected happened. The tree opened up like a door, and inside was a secret room full of toys and treats! Tim was so happy and couldn't wait to share it with his family.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor. He had a big boat. He liked to sail on the water. But the sailor was rude. He would ignore other people. One day, the sailor saw a small fish. The fish said, "Hello!" But the sailor ignored the fish. The fish was sad. The sailor kept sailing. Then, something unexpected happened. The small fish grew very big. The big fish swam fast and pushed the boat. The sailor was scared. He said, "I am sorry for being rude. Please be my friend." The big fish smiled and they became friends. From that day, the sailor was always nice to everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with a big, red ball in a big square yard. He would run and jump and have lots of fun. One day, Spot's ball went outside the yard. Spot tried to get the ball, but the gate was locked. Spot did not know how to open the lock, so he sat and looked at the ball. Then, a nice girl named Lucy saw Spot. She saw the ball and the locked gate. Lucy knew how to open the lock. She opened it and got the ball for Spot. Spot was very happy and wagged his tail. They played together in the square yard all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small cabin, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to learn and play. One day, Tim saw a big, dangerous snake near the cabin. Tim was scared, but he wanted to be brave. Tim said to the snake, "Go away! You are dangerous!" But the snake did not listen. The snake came closer to Tim. Tim was very scared, but he did not run away. The dangerous snake bit Tim. Tim cried and felt very bad. He learned that he should have run away from the dangerous snake. The bad ending made Tim sad, and he knew to be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. She loved to dress up in pretty clothes. One day, she saw a big, spicy pepper on the side of the road. She picked it up and showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look what I found, Tom!" said Anna. "It's a big, spicy pepper!" Tom looked at the pepper and said, "Be careful, Anna. Spicy peppers can make your mouth hurt." Anna didn't listen to Tom. She took a big bite of the pepper. Her mouth felt very hot! "Oh no!" she cried. "My mouth is on fire!" Tom brought her some water to help cool her mouth down. After that, Anna learned to always listen to her friends when they give her good advice. She knew that they cared about her and wanted to help her. And she never ate a spicy pepper again.
