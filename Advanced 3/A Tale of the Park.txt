Title: A Tale of the Park

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl. She always had a lovely dress on and she smiled a lot. One day, the girl was walking in the park, admiring the flowers. Suddenly, a big man appeared. He said to the girl, “I like your dress! Give it to me!” The girl tried to be brave. She said, “No! This dress is lovely, and I'm keeping it.” But the man wouldn't listen. He grabbed the dress and ran away. The girl was very sad. She cried and said, “Why did he take my dress? It was so lovely!” The girl was never able to wear that dress again. She was very sad and never smiled again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to get letters from her grandma. One day, Lucy got a letter that made her feel fearful. It was big and had a scary picture on it. Lucy showed the letter to her mom. Her mom said, "Don't worry, Lucy. It's just a letter from your grandma with a funny picture. She loves you and wants to make you laugh." Lucy felt better and opened the letter. Inside the letter, there was a funny story from her grandma. Lucy laughed and felt happy. She loved getting letters from her grandma, even if they looked scary at first.

Once upon a time there was a smart mouse named Sam. He liked to travel everywhere. One day, he took a journey to explore the world. Sam came to a big lake full of water. He saw a fish swimming and called out, "Hello, Fishy!" Fishy stopped swimming and said, "Hello, Sam! Why are you here?" Sam smiled and said, "I am here to explore the lake and find new friends. Do you want to come with me?" Fishy grinned and said, "Yes, I would love to!" So, the two new friends swam around the lake and played together. They splashed and laughed and had a lot of fun. Some other animals came to see their fun and joined them too! Sam and Fishy enjoyed travelling together around the lake, and made a lot of new friends. They were so smart and had a wonderful time!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy bunny that she loved very much. One day, Sue saw that her bunny had a big hole in it. Sue started to cry. Sue's mom heard her cry and came to see what was wrong. "Why are you crying, Sue?" her mom asked. Sue showed her mom the hole in her bunny. "Don't worry," said her mom, "I can fix it with a stitch." Sue's mom took a needle and thread and sewed a stitch to close the hole in the bunny. While she was sewing, she found something disgusting inside the bunny. It was a big, yucky bug! Sue's mom took the bug out and threw it away. When the bunny was all fixed, Sue stopped crying. She hugged her bunny tight and said, "Thank you, Mom!" From that day on, Sue and her bunny were very happy, and they never saw the disgusting bug again.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a cute little bird named Bobo. Bobo had many friends in the tree, but he was the smallest of them all. He loved to play with his friends, but he also loved to learn. One day, Bobo decided he wanted to learn math. Bobo asked his big sister, Lulu, to teach him math. Lulu was very smart and knew a lot about math. She taught Bobo how to count and add. Bobo was so happy to learn math. He practiced counting and adding every day with Lulu. One sunny day, Bobo and Lulu decided to fly high in the sky. They flew over the tree and saw all their friends below. Bobo was so happy that he could fly and learn math with his big sister. He knew that even though he was the smallest bird in the tree, he could do big things, like fly and learn math. And that made Bobo very happy.
