Title: Tim the Boy and the Cap

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red cap that he loved very much. He wore the cap every day. One day, Tim lost his cap. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. Tim was very sad. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to help him find the cap. They looked high and low, but the cap was not there. Tim and Sam went to a shop to buy a new cap. They saw a red cap just like the old one. But the cap was hard to get. They had to pay a lot of money for it. Tim and Sam worked hard to get the money. They did chores for their moms and dads. They cleaned the house and washed the car. At last, they had enough money to pay for the cap. Tim and Sam went back to the shop and bought the red cap. Tim was so happy to have his red cap again. They learned that working hard and helping each other can solve problems.

Jonny was running around the garden, enjoying the sun. His mum yelled from the kitchen, "It's time to go to school, Jonny". Jonny ran inside and grabbed his blue vest, put it on and joined his mum. On their way to school, Jonny was spinning around and around until his was dizzy. His mum laughed, "Oh Jonny, you look so silly!" Suddenly, a group of children came around the corner. Jonny's mum said, "Come on Jonny, join the children!" Jonny smiled and followed the group to school. He felt special wearing his blue vest! When they reached school, the children all went into their classrooms. Jonny stood by his mum, feeling a bit dizzy from all the spinning. Then, his teacher said, "Come join us Jonny". Jonny happily joined the class, enjoying his very first day of school.

Once upon a time there was a nosy fox. He was always on the lookout for stuff. One day he went on a big adventure to find stuff. He searched and searched all day, but had no luck. Just as he was about to give up, he saw a big box. He opened the box and inside was lots of stuff he had been looking for! He was so excited. Just then he heard a voice. It was a rabbit. The rabbit asked: "Why are you so nosy? What are you looking for?" The fox answered: "I'm looking for stuff. Today I'm very lucky to find some!" The rabbit said: "That's great! You must be the luckiest fox in the world!"

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Jack and Betsy. They wanted to have fun so they decided to go to the park. When they arrived, they saw a big tree and Jack said, "Let's swing!" Betsy thought it was a great idea and they started swinging. They laughed and laughed while they swung higher and higher. Suddenly, a thick mist appeared in the sky. They had never seen this before so they knew something bad was going to happen. Jack and Betsy shouted, "Let's get out of here!" and they ran to the playground. But the mist was too strong and they kept running until they were exhausted. After a while, they stopped to catch their breath and saw a delicious looking apple tree nearby. Jack and Betsy were so thirsty and hungry that without thinking, they started eating the apples. But the apples were magic apples and when they finished them all, something bad happened. They started to grow younger and before long, they were only 3 years old again. Jack and Betsy cried and cried, wishing they had never taken those delicious apples. They were never able to get back to their adult life again.

Once upon a time, there was a dolphin named Dolly. Dolly was a very good swimmer. She loved to play with her friends in the water. One day, Dolly saw something floating in the water. It was big and not moving. Dolly was curious and went closer to see what it was. As Dolly got closer, she saw that it was a big fish. The fish was not swimming. It was dead. Dolly felt sad for the fish. She wanted to help. Dolly had an idea. She would measure the fish to see how big it was. Then, she would tell her friends to help her move the fish. Dolly swam next to the fish and measured it with her long nose. It was very big! She swam back to her friends and told them about the fish. They all went to the fish and helped Dolly move it. Together, they pushed the fish to a safe place. Dolly and her friends were happy they could help. They swam and played together for the rest of the day.
