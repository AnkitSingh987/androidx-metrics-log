Title: The Mighty Aeroplane and the Sara

Once there was a mighty aeroplane. It zipped through the sky and soared higher than the clouds. Two children, Joe and Sara, watched it fly past them in awe. "Wow!" exclaimed Joe. "Can we be like that aeroplane, Sara?" "Yes," smiled Sara. "Let's pray that one day our dreams can take us places too." So the two children prayed for the mighty aeroplane to carry them where ever their dreams wanted to go. They imagined all sorts of incredible places the aeroplane could take them and smiled in delight. The next day, Joe and Sara went back to the same spot and looked up at the sky. Suddenly, there it was! The same mighty aeroplane that had flown past them the day before. Joe and Sara waved and smiled. They knew that with a little bit of faith and prayers, their dreams could take them anywhere.

Once there was a gifted avocado. It had more seeds inside than any other avocado in the world. Every day it spread its happiness to everyone around it. It was so special that one day the birds noticed it. They started pecking at the avocado. It was so delighted that it wanted to share its joy so it spread its seed everywhere. The birds found the seeds and took them back to their nests to feed the little babies. As the days went by, many more birds visited the avocado, and soon it had no more seeds to share. But the help of the birds, the avocado’s gift was spread out all around the world. Everywhere people looked they could find happiness brought by the special avocado’s seed.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little dog named Buddy. Buddy lived with his family in a small house. They loved to play and have fun together. One day, Buddy wanted to find a big stick for his family to play with. He looked everywhere but could not find one. Then he saw a big tree with a stick high up. Buddy thought, "I will succeed and get that stick for my family." Buddy jumped and jumped, but he could not reach the stick. His family saw him trying and said, "Buddy, you are so clever! We can help you get the stick!" They all worked together, and finally, they got the stick. Buddy and his family were so happy. They played with the big stick all day long. Buddy knew that with his family, they could succeed in anything they tried.

One day, a big, red car went for a drive. The car saw a rainbow in the sky. The rainbow was very pretty with many colors. The car wanted to go to the end of the rainbow. The car was driving too fast and being careless. It did not look where it was going. It bumped into a blue car. The blue car said, "Hey, be careful! You should not drive so fast." The big, red car said, "I am sorry, blue car. I will drive slower and be more careful." The two cars drove together to find the end of the rainbow. They were happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a new red car. It had four wheels and a shiny handle. The car liked to drive fast on the road. It went vroom, vroom, vroom! One day, the car met a big blue truck. The truck had a big handle too. They became friends and drove together on the road. They had so much fun, going beep, beep, beep! After a long day, the new red car and the big blue truck drove home. They were happy and tired. Their handles sparkled in the sun as they said, "See you soon, friend!"

Lily liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Anna. Anna had long hair and a pink dress. Lily liked to comb Anna's hair and change her dress. She had a box of clothes for Anna. One day, Lily found a roll of tape in her dad's drawer. She wondered what it was for. She decided to use it for Anna. She cut some tape and stuck it on Anna's hair. She made Anna a new hairstyle. She thought it looked nice. But when she tried to take the tape off, it was very hard. The tape pulled Anna's hair and made it messy. Lily felt sad. She had ruined Anna's hair. She wanted to fix it, but she didn't know how. She ran to her mom and showed her Anna. Mom saw the tape and the hair. She understood what happened. She hugged Lily and said, "Don't worry, sweetie. We can fix Anna's hair. We just need some scissors and a comb." Mom took Lily and Anna to the bathroom. She carefully cut the tape and the hair. She combed Anna's hair and made it smooth. She said, "Look, Lily. Anna has a new hairstyle. She has a short bob. It suits her. She looks fierce." Lily looked at Anna. She saw that Anna's hair was different, but still pretty. She smiled and said, "Thank you, mom. You are right. Anna looks fierce. I like her new hairstyle. I will be more careful with tape next time." She hugged Anna and mom. She was happy.
