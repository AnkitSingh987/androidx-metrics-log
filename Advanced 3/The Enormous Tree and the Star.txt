Title: The Enormous Tree and the Star

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was an enormous tree. The tree had many pretty ornaments hanging on it. The people in the village loved the tree. They liked to look at the ornaments and play under the tree. One day, a little girl named Lucy found a new ornament on the tree. It was a shiny star. She called her friend Tom and said, "Look, Tom! I found a new ornament on our tree!" Tom looked at the shiny star and said, "Wow, it's so pretty!" As they were looking at the star, they saw a sign near the tree. The sign said, "Please do not take the ornaments from the tree." Lucy and Tom thought about taking the shiny star, but they remembered the sign. They knew it was important to follow the rules. Lucy and Tom decided to leave the shiny star on the tree for everyone to enjoy. They learned that sharing is good and makes everyone happy. The village people were happy, too, because they could all enjoy the pretty ornaments on the enormous tree together. And the tree stood tall, making the village a happier place.

Once upon a time, there was a polite prince who lived in a big castle. He loved to play with his friends, the animals in the forest. One sunny day, the prince was playing with a little rabbit. The rabbit was sad because he lost his toy ball. The polite prince wanted to help the rabbit find the toy ball. They looked under the trees, behind the bushes, and by the river. The prince promised the rabbit that they would find the toy ball. They searched and searched, but they could not find it. At last, they found the toy ball in a hole under a big tree. The rabbit was so happy! The prince and the rabbit played with the toy ball all day long. The rabbit thanked the polite prince for keeping his promise. They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a little fish named Fin was very excite. He wanted to swim in the big sea with his friends. He told his mom, "I want to go swim with my friends, please!" His mom said, "You can go, but you must be serious and safe. The big sea can be scary." Fin promised to be serious and listen to his mom. Fin and his friends swam and played in the big sea. They had so much fun. They saw other fish and pretty plants. When it was time to go home, Fin thanked his mom for letting him swim in the big sea. He was a serious and safe fish.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit. This rabbit was very popular. All the other animals loved to play with him. One day, the rabbit found a big piece of paper. He wanted to make a toy with it. He tried to fold the paper, but it was hard. The rabbit asked his friends for help. The squirrel, the bird, and the frog all tried to fold the paper. But they could not do it. They started to feel sad. The rabbit really wanted the toy, and his friends wanted to help him. Then, a wise old owl came by. She saw the rabbit and his friends trying to fold the paper. The owl knew how to fold the paper into a toy. She showed them how to do it. The rabbit and his friends were very happy. They all played with the new toy together. The rabbit was thankful for his friends and the wise old owl.

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there lived a cat named Tom. Tom was a very helpful cat. Every Tuesday, he would serve food to his friends. He loved to see them happy. One Tuesday, Tom was very busy. He had to serve food to all his friends, but he had no help. Tom was sad, but he tried his best. He served food to his friends one by one. But Tom was so tired, he forgot to look where he was going. He tripped and fell. All the food went on the ground. Tom's friends were sad, and Tom was sad too. They all went home hungry.
