Title: Tim the Dragon and the Ball

One day, a little dragon named Tim was playing outside. He loved to roll around in the mud. He would get all dirty and disgusting, but he didn't care. It was so much fun! Tim's mom would always tell him to stay clean, but he never listened. One sunny day, while Tim was rolling in the mud, he saw a big, shiny ball. He thought it was a toy, so he rolled it around with his nose. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball cracked open, and a little baby dragon came out! Tim was so surprised. The baby dragon was not disgusting like Tim. It was clean and shiny. Tim decided to take care of the baby dragon and teach it how to roll in the mud. But the baby dragon didn't like the mud. It liked to stay clean, just like Tim's mom. So, Tim learned that it was okay to be clean sometimes, too. They all played together, and Tim was happy to have a new friend.

Once there were two boys playing in the garden. One boy saw a big paper falling down from the sky. The boy was so happy. He shouted to his friend "Look there is a paper!" His friend came and they both started running to catch the paper. When they reached, they noticed that the paper was torn into two pieces. They both looked sad. Then the first boy said, "Let's fix this paper and make it like before." They both tried and after a lot of hard work, they finally fixed the paper. The boys were so happy to see it. Then the first boy said, "We learnt an important lesson today. It is always better to fix things that are broken, then to buy a new one. It is also important to help each other and work together." The two boys smiled happily. They had a great time fixing the paper.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a stubborn little boy named Tim. He loved to play games with his friends. One day, they decided to play a game called "Truth or Dare." In this game, Tim and his friends took turns asking each other to tell a truth or do a dare. When it was Tim's turn, he always chose to do a dare. He was very brave and liked to show his friends how strong he was. One day, Tim's friend dared him to climb a big tree. Tim was scared, but he did not want to tell the truth. So, he climbed the tree. His friends cheered for him. When Tim came back down, he felt happy and proud. From that day on, Tim learned that it was okay to be scared sometimes, and he could still be brave and strong.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. The girl loved making all kinds of things. One day, the girl found a old cabinet in the corner of her room. She got a bright idea. She was going to fold papers and make something special inside the cabinet. So, she got all kinds of paper, big and small, and started folding. She made a lot of shapes; a duck, a snake, and a boat. When the girl was done, she put all of the shapes inside the cabinet. Then, she smiled and looked around. She was so proud of her work and felt very thoughtful. She was sure her friends would love it! And they did! Every time they visited they looked in the cabinet, and they were surprised each time. The little girl was the happiest she had ever been, and she knew she would make more fun things in the cabinet. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play and eat. One day, Max found a sausage on the floor. It was brown and long. Max thought, "This sausage looks yummy!" So, he started to chew on it. Max's friend, a little bird named Lily, saw Max chewing on the sausage. Lily flew down to Max and said, "Max, that sausage is disgusting! Don't eat it!" But Max did not listen to Lily. He kept chewing on the disgusting sausage. After a while, Max felt sick. His tummy hurt, and he didn't want to play anymore. "I should have listened to Lily," Max thought. Next time, Max will only eat food that is not disgusting, and he will listen to his friends.
