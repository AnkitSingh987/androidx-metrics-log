Title: Mimi the Mouse and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved cheese more than anything. One day, she found a big piece of cheese in the kitchen. Mimi was very happy and wanted to share it with her friend, Billy the cat. Mimi said, "Billy, I found a big cheese. Let's decorate it with flowers and share it." Billy liked the idea, so they both decorated the cheese with pretty flowers. Then, they sat down to eat the cheese together. But when Mimi and Billy took a bite, they both said, "Yuck! This cheese is disgusting!" They didn't know that the cheese had gone bad. Mimi and Billy felt sick and had to go to bed. They were sad that their special cheese treat had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a little dog named Toby. Toby liked to play with his friends. But sometimes, Toby was very bossy. He would tell his friends what to do and tease them if they did not listen to him. One day, Toby's friends decided to plan a surprise for him. They wanted to show him that being bossy was not nice. They all went to the park and hid behind the trees. When Toby came to play, he could not find his friends. He felt sad and lonely. After a while, Toby's friends jumped out from behind the trees and yelled, "Surprise!" Toby was so happy to see them. He realized that he should not be bossy and tease his friends. From that day on, Toby was kind and always played nicely with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a bunny. She was very kind and always said hello to her friends. One day, she was hopping through the forest and saw something strange. It appeared to be a big, orange carrot! She hopped over to it and noticed it was big and yummy. She couldn't believe what she had found! The bunny was so excited and didn't want to eat it all by herself. She decided to bring it back to her friends. She hopped along, carrying the big carrot in her paws. When she got to her friends, they were so happy to see her. She showed them the carrot and they were so amazed. They all thanked her for being so kind. Together they ate the carrot and had a great time. At the end of the day, the bunny was very happy. She had found a big, yummy carrot and shared it with her friends. She truly was a very kind bunny!

Lily and Ben were friends who lived in a big house with a big garden. They liked to play outside, but today it was raining. They looked out of the window and saw the rain falling on the flowers and the grass. "I have an idea!" Lily said. "Let's make a tent with some blankets and pillows. We can pretend we are camping in the forest." "That sounds fun!" Ben said. "But where will we get the blankets and pillows?" Lily and Ben ran to their rooms and took some blankets and pillows from their beds. They were very wealthy, so they had many blankets and pillows. They brought them to the living room and used the sofa and some chairs to make a tent. They crawled inside and made themselves cozy. They pretended they were camping in the forest. They heard the rain on the roof of the tent and imagined it was the sound of the wind and the birds. They told each other stories and jokes and laughed. They had a lot of fun. They were happy they had each other and their big house and their big garden. They did not mind the rain. They knew it would stop soon and they could play outside again. They thanked God for their blessings and hugged each other. They were the best of friends.

Anna loved to play her violin. She played it every day after school. She played songs that made her happy, sad, or excited. She played for her family, her friends, and her cat. She wanted to be a great violinist when she grew up. But Anna did not like to nap. She thought naps were boring and a waste of time. She wanted to play more violin, or read books, or draw pictures. She did not listen to her mom, who told her that naps were good for her. She said that naps helped her grow, learn, and rest. One day, Anna had a big recital at school. She was going to play her violin in front of everyone. She was very nervous and excited. She practiced a lot the night before, and did not nap at all. She thought she would be fine. But she was not fine. She was very tired and sleepy. She could not focus on her music. She made many mistakes. She felt uncomfortable and sad. She wished she had napped. She saw her mom in the audience, who looked worried and sad too. After the recital, Anna ran to her mom and hugged her. She said she was sorry for not napping. She said she learned her lesson. She said she would nap from now on. Her mom smiled and kissed her. She said she was proud of her for trying her best. She said she loved her no matter what. She said they would go home and nap together. Anna nodded and smiled. She felt better. She still loved her violin, but she also loved her nap.
