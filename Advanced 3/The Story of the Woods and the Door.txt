Title: The Story of the Woods and the Door

Once upon a time, there were two friends who loved to jog together. Every morning, they would put on their joggers and jog through the woods, past a magical cabin. One day, the friends wanted a break and so they stopped their jog and went to the cabin. Inside was a large feast, with the most delicious foods they had ever tasted. The friends were very excited! One of the friends was a bit selfish, so without asking the other he started to eat all the food. His friend was so mad and asked him to give him some, but he refused. Suddenly, the door to the cabin slammed shut and they couldn't get out! No matter how hard they tried they were stuck. They never got to jog together again and their friendship was ruined.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane who liked to stare into the rain. Jane liked to stand outside and let the rain fall on her. One day as she was staring, it started to rain harder and harder. Jane was getting wet, but she still kept looking up. As the rain came down it was so strong that it felt like needles! Then, suddenly, Jane heard a voice. "Jane, come inside!" It was her mom. Jane was so surprised she yelled back, "But Mom, I love the rain!" Her mom smiled and said, "I know, but it's too strong for you. Come inside now." So Jane slowly started walking inside and turning around, she saw that the rain was still strong and she smiled. She loved watching the rain, even if it was too strong for her.

Lily liked to play in the park with her friends. They liked to run, swing, slide and climb. One day, they saw something shiny under a bush. They went to look at it. It was a soldier. He was made of metal and had a hat and a gun. He looked very old and dirty. Lily picked him up and showed him to her friends. They wondered who he was and where he came from. They decided to take him to the big hill. They thought he would like to see the sky and the birds. They ran to the hill and put him on the grass. They sat around him and talked to him. They asked him his name and his story. But he did not answer. He was very quiet. Lily felt sad. She thought the soldier was lonely and missed his home. She wished she could make him happy. She gave him a hug and a kiss. She said, "Don't worry, soldier. We are your friends. We will take care of you. You are not alone." The soldier felt warm and loved. He smiled a little. He was glad he had met Lily and her friends. He felt less sad. He whispered, "Thank you, Lily. You are very kind. You are my friend."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad and miserable. He wanted to be strong and have big muscles like his big brother, Ben. One day, Tim asked Ben, "How do I get big muscles like you?" Ben smiled and said, "You have to exercise and eat healthy food." So, Tim did what Ben said. He exercised every day and ate lots of fruits and vegetables. After some time, Tim started to see his muscles grow. He was very happy. One day, his family saw how strong he had become. They all clapped and applauded for him. Tim felt proud and was not miserable anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a famous fox. He was so famous that everyone knew him and wanted to be his friend. One day, the fox found a special suit. He put on the suit and wanted to show it off to everyone. The fox was so excited that he forgot that he had to watch out for the bad wolf. He ran around the woods showing the suit off to everyone. As the fox continued on his way, the wolf got closer and closer. The fox was getting close to his home, when he felt the wolf's breath on his fur. He was so scared and knew he had to do something. Suddenly, he remembered his special suit! He quickly put on the suit and the wolf ran away. The fox was safe and quickly understood the value of the suit. He remembered that it was not only a way to show off, but it also was able to protect him. Finally, he had not only found a way to be famous with his special suit, but also to keep himself safe. The fox learned an important lesson of always being prepared, even when showing off his suit.
