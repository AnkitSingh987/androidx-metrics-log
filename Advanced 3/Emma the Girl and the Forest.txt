Title: Emma the Girl and the Forest

Once upon a time there was a girl named Emma. She was a brave girl who liked to explore. One day she found a shield in a nearby forest. It was very interesting - it was blue and sparkly. Emma thought it would be fun to take it with her. Emma asked her daddy if she could keep the shield. Her daddy said, "If you stay safe, you can keep the shield." Emma smiled, happy that she could keep the shield. She took the shield with her everywhere. She stayed safe during each of her adventures. She even used the shield to help protect her friends. Everyone was so impressed with the shield that they all wanted one too! Emma's daddy was very proud of her. He said, "You have stayed safe and have been so responsible. You can keep the shield forever." Emma was so excited. She hugged her daddy and thanked him for the shield.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to dress up and make herself look pretty. Every day, Lucy would go to her room and try on her different outfits. One day she was getting ready to go out, she was about to put on a dress she had never worn before. As she opened her wardrobe, something caught her eye. It was an old picture tucked away in the back corner. Lucy felt curious about the picture and was determined to find out more. She grabbed it and went to show it to her mum. “Mum, what is this picture?” Lucy asked. “It was painted a long time ago,” her mum explained. “It is a dark picture of me and your grandpa when we were young.” Lucy smiled. “I love the picture,” she said. “Can I put it up in my room?” “Of course you can,” her mum said. So Lucy quickly dashed off and hung the picture on her wall. She stood back and admired it as she quickly put on her dress, ready to go out and explore. The rest of the day, Lucy looked at the dark picture and smiled. She was glad that she had found it and was happy to have it hanging in her room.

Lila and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to look for insects under the rocks and in the grass. They found ants, beetles, spiders and worms. They put them in a jar with some leaves and water. "Look, this one is very small and shiny!" Lila said, pointing to a green insect with wings. "Wow, it is pretty! Can I hold it?" Ben asked. "Okay, but be careful. It is very fragile. You have to promise not to hurt it or let it fly away." Lila said. "I promise, I promise!" Ben said. He took the insect gently in his hand and looked at it closely. It tickled his palm and he giggled. Suddenly, they heard their mom calling them from the house. "Kids, it's time for lunch! Come inside and wash your hands!" "Okay, mom, we're coming!" Lila and Ben shouted. They ran to the house, but Ben forgot to put the insect back in the jar. He still had it in his hand. "Ben, what do you have there?" Mom asked, seeing the green insect. "It's a small and shiny insect. We found it in the garden. It's very pretty." Ben said. "It is, but you can't keep it. It belongs to the garden. It needs to be free and find its friends and food. You have to let it go." Mom said. "But I promised Lila not to let it fly away." Ben said. "You can keep your promise and let it go. You can tell Lila that you did the right thing. She will understand. Come on, let's go back to the garden and say goodbye to the insect." Mom said. Ben nodded. He felt sad, but he knew Mom was right. He followed her to the garden and opened his hand. The insect flew away and landed on a flower. "Bye, bye, insect. Thank you for being my friend for a little while." Ben said. He waved to the insect and smiled. He hoped it would be happy and safe. He ran to Lila and told her what happened. She hugged him and said he was a good brother. They went inside and had lunch. They still liked to look for insects, but they always let them go. They promised.
