Title: Sue the Girl and the Door

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to do something fun. She thought it would be perfect to play with her ball inside the house. Sue bounced the ball up and down. But, she did not see the door that was closed. Sue threw the ball and it hit the door very hard. The door opened and her dog, Max, ran out. Sue was sad and worried. She knew she had to find Max and bring him back home. Sue went outside and called for Max. After a while, she found him near a big tree. She was so happy to see him. Sue and Max went back home, and she promised to be more careful when playing inside the house. From that day, Sue played with her ball outside, and everything was perfect again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very independent and loved to do things by herself. One day, she decided to decorate her room all by herself. She found some paper, glue, and colors. Lily started to make a big mess in her room. She glued paper on the walls and colored everywhere. Her mom came in and said, "Oh, Lily! What a shock! What are you doing?" Lily smiled and said, "I'm decorating, Mom!" Mom laughed and said, "Let's clean up and decorate together." Lily agreed, and they worked together to make the room beautiful. In the end, Lily learned that it's okay to ask for help and that decorating is more fun with someone you love.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma loved to eat waffles for breakfast. One day, she was very tired but she still wanted to eat her waffle. Emma said to her mom, "Mommy, I am tired. Can I eat my waffle in bed?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Emma. You can eat your waffle in bed today." Emma was so happy. She took her waffle to her bed and started to eat it. Her little dog, Max, came to her bed too. Max looked at the waffle and wanted some. Emma gave Max a small piece of her waffle. Then, she gave Max a big kiss on his nose. Max wagged his tail and they both enjoyed the waffle together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ancient tree in a park. This tree was very old and wise. Every day, the sun would shine on the tree, making it feel warm and happy. Many people would come to the park to see this special tree. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the park to do some work. He needed to draw a picture of the ancient tree for his school. As Tim was drawing, he heard a soft voice. The voice said, "Hello, little boy." Tim looked around but saw no one. Then, Tim realized that the voice was coming from the ancient tree! The tree could talk! The tree told Tim that it had a secret. The secret was that every time the sun shined on the tree, it could talk to people. Tim was very surprised and happy. He told all his friends about the talking tree. From that day on, Tim and the ancient tree became best friends.

Once there was a girl who found a poppy. She was so excited and decided to throw it. She threw it as high as she could, so high it almost touched the sky. But all of a sudden, a big and mean bird came, grabbed the poppy and flew away. The girl was sad and started to cry. Her mom saw her crying and asked her what happened. The girl told her about the poppy and how themean bird had taken it away. Her mom tried to cheer her up. She said: don’t worry honey, there are much more fun things to do in life that don’t involve poppies. But the girl was too sad and nothing her mom said seemed to help her. She stayed bored and so very sad that she couldn’t enjoy any of the fun activities. The girl never found her poppy again and everthing stayed boring forever.
