Title: Ella the Elephant and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Ella. Ella lived in a safe place with her family. She loved to play with her friends and had a lot of fun. One day, Ella saw a little bird who was sad. The bird had lost its way and could not find its home. Ella wanted to help the bird, so she said, "Do not be sad, little bird. I will help you find your home." Ella and the bird went on a long walk, hoping to find the bird's home. They asked other animals for help, but no one knew where the bird's home was. Ella and the bird started to feel tired, but they did not give up. Finally, they found the bird's home. The bird's family was very happy and said, "Thank you, Ella, for helping our little bird." Ella felt happy too. She learned that even though she was big and strong, she could still be kind and help others. The moral of the story is that you can always help someone, no matter how big or small you are.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. He was very careful when he walked. He held his mom's hand tight. At the store, they saw a big line. They had to wait to buy their things. Tim was not happy. He wanted to go home and play. Tim asked his mom a question. "Mom, why do we have to wait in this line?" His mom said, "We have to wait our turn, Tim. We have to follow the order." Tim did not like the order. He wanted to find a way to make the line go faster. Then, Tim had an idea. He said to his mom, "I will go to the front of the line and ask if we can go next." His mom said no, but Tim did not listen. He ran to the front and asked the man there, "Can we go next?" The man was kind and said, "Of course, little one." Everyone in the line was surprised. They did not expect the man to let Tim go next. They started to ask if they could go next too. Soon, the line was gone. Everyone was happy and thanked Tim for his brave question. Tim's mom was proud of him for helping. They went home and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lucy. One day, Lucy was wandering around in the forest when she saw something strange. In the middle of the forest, there was an enormous pastry with a bright, shining glaze. Intrigued, Lucy approached it, wondering what was special about this pastry. When all of a sudden, the pastry started to shrink before her eyes! Lucy was shocked, but she still couldn't resist the temptation to take a bite. She gingerly reached in and, as soon as her fingers touched the pastry, the shrinking stopped. She asked the pastry, "Why were you shrinking?" The pastry replied, "I wanted to find someone to eat me! I was so hungry, but I was too big to be eaten!" Lucy smiled and happily took a bite out of the tasty pastry. She finished the last crumb, feeling content as the sun slowly started to set. Lucy waved goodbye to the pastry as she turned around to go back home, feeling happy and filled with joy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very enthusiastic and loved to solve problems. One day, Tim saw a big ashtray on the table. He wanted to find out what it was for. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, what is this for?" His mom replied, "That is an ashtray. It is for putting out fire from grown-up things." Tim was curious and wanted to solve a problem with the ashtray. He thought and thought, and then he had an idea. Tim took the ashtray outside and filled it with water. He brought it back inside and showed his mom. "Look, Mom! I solved a problem. Now the ashtray can be a bowl for the dog to drink water." His mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! You are very smart." And they all lived happily ever after.
