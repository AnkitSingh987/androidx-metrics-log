Title: Lily and Ben and the Giraffe

Lily and Ben were very happy. They were going on a safari with their mom and dad. They wanted to see many animals and learn new things. They got in a big car with big wheels. The car drove on a dirt road. They saw trees and grass and flowers. They saw birds and monkeys and zebras. Lily and Ben asked many questions. They wanted to know everything. "Mom, why are zebras black and white?" Lily asked. "Because they can hide from lions in the shadows," Mom said. "Dad, why are monkeys so noisy?" Ben asked. "Because they talk to each other and warn each other of danger," Dad said. Lily and Ben learned a lot. They were very curious and smart. They came to a big lake. They saw a green animal with a long neck and spots. It was a giraffe. It was drinking water. Lily and Ben wanted to see it closer. "Can we go out of the car, Mom?" Lily asked. "No, honey, it's not safe. The giraffe might get scared and run away. Or there might be other animals that are not friendly," Mom said. "But we want to see the giraffe, Dad," Ben said. "Sorry, kids, we have to stay in the car. The car is our safe place. We can watch the giraffe from here," Dad said. Lily and Ben were not happy. They wanted to touch the giraffe and feed it. They did not listen to their mom and dad. They opened the door and got out of the car. They ran towards the giraffe. The giraffe saw them and got scared. It lifted its head and made a loud noise. It ran away. Lily and Ben were sad. They did not see the giraffe anymore. But they did not know that the giraffe was not the only animal in the lake. There was a big green animal with sharp teeth and a long tail. It was a crocodile. It was hiding under the water. It saw Lily and Ben and got hungry. It opened its mouth and swam towards them. Lily and Ben did not see the crocodile. They were too busy looking for the giraffe. They did not hear their mom and dad shouting at them to come back. They did not hear the crocodile splashing in the water. They did not hear anything until it was too late. The crocodile grabbed Lily and Ben and dragged them under the water. They screamed and cried. They were very scared and hurt. They wished they had listened to their mom and dad. They wished they had stayed in the car. The end. Moral value: Listen to your parents. They know what is best for you. Foreshadowing or setup and payoff: The mom and dad explain why the animals have different colors and sounds to help them survive. The giraffe is green and has spots to blend in with the trees. The crocodile is green and hides under the water. The kids ignore the warnings of their parents and go out of the car. They lose the giraffe and find the crocodile. The colors and sounds of the animals are important for the plot and the theme.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat fruit. One day, she found an interesting fruit in her garden. It was big and red. She had never seen this fruit before. Lily went to her mom and asked, "Mom, what is this interesting fruit?" Her mom said, "It's called an apple. It's very yummy." Lily did not understand what an apple was, but she was excited to try it. Lily took a big bite of the apple. It was sweet and juicy. She shared the apple with her friends. They all loved the new fruit. Lily learned that trying new things can be fun and yummy. She understood that it's good to share with friends. And they all lived happily ever after, eating apples and sharing with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends. One day, they all went to the park to play. They saw a long line of ants on the ground. The ants were very little and busy. Tim and his friends thought it would be fun to make the ants go away. Tim started to step on the ants. His friends saw him do it and they all started to do it too. They were all having fun, but they didn't know that they were being mean to the ants. The ants were not happy and felt embarrassed because Tim and his friends were not being nice. Then, something unexpected happened. The ants decided to work together and they climbed up on Tim and his friends. The ants tickled them, and they all started to laugh. They couldn't stop laughing and they felt embarrassed too. In the end, Tim and his friends learned that it's not nice to make fun of others or hurt them. They said sorry to the ants and promised to be kind from that day on.
