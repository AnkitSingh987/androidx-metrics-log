Title: Lucy and the Toys

Once upon a time, a playful cat named Lucy loved to design things. She would make pretty wraps for her toys. Her best friend was a little mouse named Tim. They played together every day. One day, Lucy had an idea. She wanted to make a big wrap for a surprise. Tim was very excited. They worked together to design the best wrap ever. They knew something fun was going to happen. Finally, the big day came. Lucy and Tim put the big wrap on a box. Inside the box, they found lots of new toys to play with. They were so happy that their hard work made their playtime even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big sack. He liked to put toys in the sack. He hoped to fill the sack with more toys. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue had a toy he did not have. Tim felt jealous. He wanted that toy too. He hoped Sue would share with him. Sue saw Tim looking at her toy. She did not want him to be jealous. So, she gave Tim the toy. Tim was happy. He put the toy in his sack. Now they could play together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to read. Every day, he would read books under a big tree. Tom was very proud of how much he could read. One day, Tom woke up very early to read. It was still dark outside. He went to the tree and sat down. Soon, the sun began to rise. The sunrise was big and bright. Tom was happy to see it. As Tom read his book, something unexpected happened. The big tree started to talk! The tree said, "I am so proud of you, Tom. You read so much and I love to listen." Tom was very surprised but happy. Now, Tom and the tree would read and talk every day at sunrise.

Once upon a time, there was a lily in a garden. The lily was very interesting. Every day, animals came to see it and guess what color it was. One day, a little rabbit came hopping by. He wanted to take a guess. He said, "I think the lily is yellow." The bird that was sitting nearby said, "No, it looks like it could be pink." The spider said, "No, I think it is purple." But the bee said, "I think it looks blue!" The lily was curious, so it said to the animals, "Guess again!" The animals were happy to do so. After a while, they all decided that the lily was a beautiful rainbow color. They were all very happy. The end!

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a big lion. The lion liked to roar very loud. One day, he found a shiny rock. The shiny rock was a diamond. The lion was very happy to have the diamond. In the forest, there was an elderly rabbit. The rabbit saw the lion with the diamond. The rabbit wanted to see the diamond too. The rabbit hopped over to the lion and asked, "Can I see your diamond?" The lion roared and said, "Yes, you can see my diamond." The elderly rabbit looked at the diamond and smiled. The rabbit and the lion became good friends. They played in the forest with the diamond and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, a healthy dog named Spot wanted to visit his friend, a cat named Fluffy. Spot liked to play and run, and he wanted to show Fluffy how to be healthy too. So, Spot started his visit to Fluffy's house. On the way, Spot saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it, but he knew dogs can't do that. Spot tried and tried, but he could not succeed. He felt sad and sat under the tree. Then, something unexpected happened. A kind squirrel came down from the tree. The squirrel helped Spot by showing him a new way to reach Fluffy's house. Spot was happy and thanked the squirrel. In the end, Spot and Fluffy played together and had a fun day.
