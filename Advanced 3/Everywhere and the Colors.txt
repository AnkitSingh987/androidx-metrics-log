Title: Everywhere and the Colors

Once upon a time there was a girl who loved colors. Everywhere she went, she looked for the prettiest colors. One day she took her favorite colors and split them in two - one for her, and one for her friend. She was so excited. But when the girl brought the colors to her friend, she was very spoiled. She refused to take her half of the colors. So the girl decided to keep them all for herself. She wore the colors in her hair and smiled. The girl was so happy with her colors. Everywhere she went, everyone admired them. She wore them every day with pride. Even though she was spoiled, she still enjoyed the beauty of her color.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big hall. The hall was empty and quiet. No one went inside because they did not know what to do in the hall. One day, a little girl named Anna walked by the hall. She saw how empty it was and wanted to help make it a fun place. Anna had an idea. She went to her friend Tom and said, "Let's help make the empty hall a fun place to play!" Tom agreed, and they went to the hall together. They brought toys, games, and colorful decorations. They worked together to make the hall a fun place for everyone. Soon, other kids in the town heard about the fun hall. They came to play with Anna and Tom. The empty hall was now filled with laughter and fun. All the kids were happy that Anna and Tom helped make the hall a great place to play.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a polite bird named Lily. Lily loved to sing her song every morning. All the animals in the town would wake up and listen to her sweet voice. One day, a big cat named Tom came to the town. Tom was not so nice. He wanted to prevent Lily from singing her song. So, he tried to catch her. The animals in the town did not like this. They wanted to hear Lily's song every day. The animals worked together to stop Tom. They made a big net and caught him. Then, they told him to be nice and let Lily sing her song. Tom learned to be polite and let Lily sing. From that day on, the town was happy and everyone enjoyed Lily's song.

Lily and Ben were bored. They had played with all their toys and read all their books. They wanted to do something new and fun. They saw a big calendar on the wall. It had many pictures of different places and animals. They liked to look at the pictures and learn their names. "Let's play with the calendar!" Lily said. "We can pretend we are going to those places and see those animals." "Okay!" Ben said. "But how do we play with the calendar? It is too big and too high." Lily had an idea. She found a chair and climbed on it. She took the calendar off the wall and brought it to the floor. She opened it and spread it out. "Look, Ben! Now we have a big map!" Lily said. "We can roll on it and pretend we are traveling." Ben liked the idea. He joined Lily on the floor. They rolled on the calendar and made noises like the animals they saw. They had a lot of fun. But they did not know that the calendar was very expensive. It was a gift from their grandma, who had bought it from a special shop. She had written a note on the back, saying how much she loved them and hoped they would enjoy the calendar. When their mom came home, she saw the calendar on the floor. It was torn and wrinkled and dirty. She was very angry and sad. "Lily and Ben, what have you done?" she asked. "This was a very expensive calendar from grandma. She wanted you to learn from it, not to ruin it. How could you be so careless?" Lily and Ben felt very bad. They did not know the calendar was expensive. They did not want to make their mom and grandma unhappy. They said they were sorry and hugged their mom. "We are sorry, mom. We did not know. We just wanted to play and have fun. We love the calendar and the pictures. We love you and grandma. Please don't be mad at us." they said. Their mom sighed and hugged them back. She knew they did not mean to harm the calendar. She knew they were just curious and playful. She loved them very much. She said, "I know you are sorry. And I know you love the calendar and us. But you have to be more careful with things that are not yours. And you have to ask before you take something off the wall. The calendar was a special gift from grandma. She will be very sad if she sees it like this. Maybe we can fix it somehow. Or maybe we can buy a new one. But you have to promise me you will not do this again. Okay?" Lily and Ben nodded and promised. They said they would help their mom fix the calendar or buy a new one. They said they would call their grandma and tell her they were sorry and they loved her. Their mom smiled and kissed them. She said, "Okay. I forgive you. And I'm sure grandma will too. But now it's time for dinner. Let's go to the kitchen and wash our hands. And maybe we can look at the calendar later and learn some new words. How does that sound?" Lily and Ben agreed. They followed their mom to the kitchen. They still felt bad about the calendar, but they also felt happy that their mom was not mad at them anymore. They hoped their grandma would feel the same. They decided they would be more careful with their things from now on. And they decided they would still play with the calendar, but in a different way.
