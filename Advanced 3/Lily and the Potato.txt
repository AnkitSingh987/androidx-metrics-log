Title: Lily and the Potato

Lily was a young girl who liked to help her mom in the kitchen. She liked to wash the potatoes and put them in a big pot. Mom said they would make yummy soup for dinner. One day, Lily was washing the potatoes when she saw a small one with a funny shape. It looked like a bunny with long ears and a nose. Lily thought it was very cute and wanted to keep it. She put it in her pocket and finished washing the other potatoes. Lily forgot about the potato in her pocket and went to play with her toys. She ran and jumped and rolled on the floor. She did not notice that the potato fell out of her pocket and rolled under the sofa. When mom came to the kitchen, she counted the potatoes and saw that one was missing. She asked Lily where the last potato was. Lily remembered the bunny potato and ran to her room. She looked in her pocket and saw that it was empty. She felt sad and said, "I lose the potato, mom. I lose the bunny potato." Mom hugged Lily and said, "It's okay, sweetie. We can look for the potato together. Maybe it is hiding somewhere." They searched the house and found the potato under the sofa. Lily was happy and said, "I find the potato, mom. I find the bunny potato." Mom smiled and said, "That's great, honey. But we have to cook the potato for the soup. How about we make a bunny face with some carrots and peas on top of the potato? Then you can eat the bunny potato and it will make you strong and healthy." Lily liked that idea and nodded. She helped mom make the soup and enjoyed eating the bunny potato. She said, "Thank you, mom. The soup is yummy and the bunny potato is funny." Mom said, "You're welcome, darling. I love you very much." They hugged and kissed and had a cozy dinner.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little girl named Mia. One day, Mia found a magic stone. The stone was shiny and pretty. Mia liked it a lot. Mia showed the magic stone to her friend, Tom. Tom said, "Wow! I want one too!" Tom was envious of Mia's magic stone. That night, Mia and Tom sat under the stars. Mia yawned and said, "I'm sleepy." As Mia slept, she had a dream. In her dream, she and Tom found more magic stones. They shared the stones with all their friends. Everyone was happy and no one was envious anymore. The next day, Mia told Tom about her dream. They both smiled and played together, happy with their magic stone.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. The sky was gray and the wind was blowing. Tim saw a big cloud in the sky. He thought it looked like a tornado. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, I see a tornado!" His mom looked at the sky and said, "It's not a tornado, Tim. But we should go inside because it might rain soon." They went inside and watched the gray clouds from the window. Tim hoped the rain would not touch his toys outside. Later, the wind started to blow harder. Tim and his mom heard a loud noise. They saw a real tornado outside their window! They were scared, but they knew what to do. They went to their safe place in the house and waited for the tornado to pass. When the tornado was gone, they went outside. Tim's toys were all over the place, but they were safe. Tim was happy that he and his mom were safe too. He learned that even if the clouds look like a tornado, it is always good to be prepared for one.

Once upon a time, there was a strong dog named Max. Max lived in a house with a big, wide yard. He loved to run and play in the yard all day long. Max was very happy. One day, a small cat came into Max's yard. The cat wanted to play with Max, but Max did not want to play with the cat. He chose to ignore the cat. The cat felt sad and left the yard. The next day, Max was playing in the yard when he saw a big, scary dog coming. Max was afraid and needed help. He looked for the cat, but the cat was not there. The scary dog chased Max away from his house. Max lost his home and his wide yard. The moral of the story is to be kind to others, so they will be there to help you when you need them.
