Title: Amy and the Mirror

One sunny day, a little girl named Amy went to play outside. She saw a mild hill with soft grass. Amy wanted to play a game. She took her small mirror and decided to bury it in the soft dirt at the bottom of the hill. As she dug a hole, her friend Tom came over. "What are you doing?" he asked. Amy said, "I am going to hide my mirror in the ground. Do you want to help me?" Tom agreed, and they both buried the mirror together. After they buried the mirror, they played a game of finding it. They took turns hiding and finding the mirror. They laughed and had fun all day long. At the end of the day, they went home tired but happy.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man who loved to play golf. One day, he went to a big store and bought a new golf set. He was very happy and excited to play with his new toy. The next day, the rich man went to the park to unpack his golf set. He took out the golf sticks and the small ball. He was ready to play. Just then, a little dog came running by and took the ball in its mouth. The rich man tried to get the ball back from the dog, but the dog was too fast. It was a twist he didn't expect. The little dog ran away with the ball, and the rich man had to run after it. They ran and ran until they reached a big tree. Under the tree, the rich man found many more balls that the little dog had taken. The dog liked to collect balls from people who played golf. The rich man laughed and played with the dog. From that day on, they became best friends and played golf together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a big bag. He was ready to go to the park. Tim put his toys in the bag. He was very happy. At the park, Tim saw a twisty slide. He loved the twisty slide. He played on the twisty slide all day. He went up and down, and laughed a lot. When it was time to go home, Tim took his bag. He put his toys back inside. He said bye to the twisty slide. Tim and his bag went home, ready for more fun tomorrow.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a wild cauliflower. It was a very big cauliflower. The wild cauliflower could talk and it had many friends. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the garden. He could hear the wild cauliflower talking to its friends. Tim was very surprised. He asked the cauliflower, "How can you talk?" The cauliflower said, "I am a wild cauliflower and I am special." Tim wanted to take the cauliflower home, but the cauliflower said, "No, please let me stay with my friends." Tim thought about it and decided to leave the cauliflower in the garden. He came back every day to talk and play with the cauliflower and its friends. The moral of the story is to be kind to others and respect their wishes.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. He lived in a small house with his friend, a little mouse named Jerry. Tom liked to tease Jerry, but they were still good friends. One day, they went to the garden to find some food to eat. They saw a tiny plant. It was an herb. Tom and Jerry decided to take the herb back to their house. They thought it would make their food taste good. They put the herb in a pot and gave it water and sun. The next day, they woke up and saw something unexpected. The herb had grown into an enormous plant! It was so big that it filled their whole house. Tom and Jerry were very surprised. They did not know what to do with the enormous herb. They thought and thought. Then, they had an idea. They would share the herb with all of their friends in the garden. So, they cut the enormous herb into many pieces and gave them to their friends. Everyone was so happy. Tom and Jerry learned that sharing can make everyone happy, and they stopped teasing each other.
