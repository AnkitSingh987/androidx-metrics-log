Title: The Story of the Box and the Bell

Once upon a time, there was a big, empty box. The box was in a room full of lumber. One day, a small cat named Mimi found the box. Mimi liked the box and decided to play in it. Mimi jumped in the box and heard a loud ring. She looked around and saw a small bell. Mimi played with the bell, making it ring more. She was very happy. Soon, Mimi's friends came to play too. They all played with the bell and the lumber. They made a fun fort with the empty box and the lumber. They played together all day, ringing the bell and having fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went on a journey with his mom. They went to the big shop to buy a toy. Tim was very happy because he loved toys. At the shop, Tim saw a big, red ball. He said, "Mom, can we buy this ball?" His mom said, "Sorry, Tim, we can only buy one toy today." Tim was sad but he kept looking for a toy. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl named Sue came up to Tim and gave him a toy car. She said, "I have two of these cars, you can have one." Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Sue!" Tim's mom was happy too, and they went home with a new toy without buying one.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a watch. The watch was big and shiny. Tim loved his watch. He wore it all day and all night. One day, Tim was walking in the park. He saw a big tree with red and green fruit. The fruit looked good, but Tim did not know if he could eat it. Tim wanted to try the fruit. He picked one and took a bite. It was bitter! Tim did not like the taste. He tried to yield the fruit, but it was stuck in his hand. Tim was sad and scared. He did not know what to do. He thought the fruit would never come off his hand. Just then, a kind squirrel came to help Tim. The squirrel took the fruit from Tim's hand. It was not stuck anymore! Tim was so happy. The squirrel told Tim that he could trade his shiny watch for a sweet fruit. Tim thought about it and decided to trade his watch. The squirrel gave Tim a sweet fruit, and Tim gave the squirrel his watch. They both were very happy with their new things. And from that day on, Tim and the squirrel were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful university. Everyone wanted to go there to learn powerful things. Every day, the university would rise up and look proud and tall. All the students were so happy they could go! They studied hard and the university got even more powerful. All the people in the town were proud. Every day, the university became even more special. One day, two kids saw the powerful university. They wanted it to be theirs forever. They asked the university if they could join, but it said no. The kids were so sad! But then the university said that if they worked hard, they could rise to the top and become powerful just like the university was. So the kids worked hard and studied a lot. Finally, after lots of hard work, the two kids rose to the top and became just as powerful as the university. Everyone was so proud of them!

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was sitting on her porch. She had a fragile toy in her hand and was rubbing it gently. The little girl's mom walked over and asked, "What are you doing, honey?" The little girl smiled and said, "I'm rubbing it." Her mom smiled and said, "That's very nice! Make sure it stays safe, though. It looks fragile." The little girl nodded, then went back to rubbing her toy. She looked out over the porch and watched the birds flying around. She kept rubbing and looking until it was time to go inside. The little girl hugged her toy and ran inside the house. She knew that with a little bit of care, her fragile toy would stay safe.
