Title: The Wealthy Mouse and the Cheese

Once upon a time there was a wealthy mouse. This mouse had a lot of cheese in its house. Every day, the mouse would take out some cheese and smile. One day, the mouse noticed a girl standing outside its house. The girl was also smiling while looking at the mouse's cheese. The mouse wanted to share its cheese with the girl, so it left a big piece of cheese outside the house. When the girl saw the cheese, she smiled even bigger. She thanked the mouse and started eating the cheese. The mouse had never seen someone enjoy its cheese so much, so it smiled and kept watching. The girl finished the cheese, thanked the mouse and left. From that day on, the mouse kept leaving cheese outside so that anyone passing by could share in its cheese and smile.

Lila was a little girl who liked to play outside. She had a big yard with many flowers and trees. She liked to look for bugs and animals in the grass. One day, she found a cricket. It was green and shiny, and it made a loud noise with its wings. Lila was happy. She wanted to keep the cricket as a pet. She made a small house for it with a box and some leaves. She gave it some water and some bread. She named it Chirpy. She lay down next to the box and watched Chirpy. He was very busy. He ate and drank and jumped and sang. Lila's mom called her for dinner. She told Lila to wash her hands and come inside. Lila did not want to leave Chirpy. She asked her mom if she could bring him inside. Her mom said no. She said crickets belong outside, not inside. She said Chirpy would be happier in the yard. Lila was sad. She did not want to say goodbye to Chirpy. She gave him a hug and a kiss. She told him he was a sweet cricket and she loved him. She thanked him for being her friend. She put him back in the grass and covered him with some leaves. She hoped he would be safe and warm. Lila went inside and ate her dinner. She told her mom and dad about Chirpy. They smiled and listened. They said Lila was a kind and gentle girl. They said they were proud of her. Lila felt better. She knew Chirpy was still out there, and maybe she would see him again. She went to bed and dreamed of crickets.

Once there was a puppy. The puppy was young and wanted to go for a walk. He took off running and it seemed like he would never stop. The puppy kept running until he came across a river. He was not sure if he should cross. It was deep and the water was rough. But the puppy was brave and wanted to explore. The puppy looked around and saw a bridge leading across the river. He put one paw out then the other and slowly walked across the bridge to the other side. On the other side, the puppy was so excited by his adventure! He had seen something new and it was a lot of fun. He was happy he had been brave and taken the lead.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Jane. Jane had a weak arm, but she loved to play outside. One sunny day, Jane went to the park with her mom. She carried a basket full of toys. At the park, Jane met a boy named Tim. Tim saw Jane's basket and said, "Can I play with your toys?" Jane said, "Yes, but be careful. My basket is weak and can tear." They played together, laughing and having fun. But then, Tim accidentally tore the weak basket. Jane was sad, but Tim said, "I'm sorry, Jane. I will help you fix it." They worked together to fix the basket, and it was as good as new. From that day on, Jane and Tim became best friends. They always played together and took care of each other's things. And they always remembered to be careful with their weak basket.
