Title: The Tall Tree and the Sandwich

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. In the tree, there was a yummy sandwich. A little boy and a little girl saw the sandwich. They both wanted the sandwich very much. The little boy tried to grab the sandwich first. But he was not tall enough. The little girl tried to grab the sandwich too. She was not tall enough either. The boy and girl were both sad. Then, they had an idea. The boy stood on his toes and the girl stood on his back. Now, they were tall enough. The girl grabbed the sandwich. They were both happy and shared the sandwich. They ate the sandwich together and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a garden where she grew many things. One day, she found a big radish in her garden. It was red and round. Sue was very happy. The sun was shining brightly that day. The radish seemed to shine too. Sue showed her radish to her friend, Tom. Tom looked at the radish and felt envious. He wished he had a radish like that in his garden. Sue saw that Tom was envious and had an idea. She said, "Let's plant more radishes together in your garden!" Tom agreed, and they worked together to plant more radishes. Soon, both their gardens were full of big, shining radishes, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time there was a littly boy named Sam. He was three years old, and he loved to explore. One day he went outside and peeked into an old shop. Inside he saw a pile of books about history. He was so curious! Sam went inside and asked the shop keeper, "What are these?" The shop keeper smiled at Sam. "They are books about our history, Sam. History is very important!" Sam smiled. "Can I please peek in them?" The shop keeper nodded. Sam eagerly opened the books, and peeking in each one he found out all sorts of interesting things about the world. Sam was so pleased to have discovered these books. He thanked the shop keeper and said goodbye. History had become an important part of Sam's life!

Frank was walking in the park one day and saw a beautiful fountain and stopped to look at it. The fountain was sparkling in the sun and the water was so calm. Frank said to himself, "I wish I could stay here forever." Just then a little bird flew above him and said, "You can stay here if you mind the fountain." Frank was quite surprised and looked around, but there was nobody else around. He said to the bird, "Who are you talking to?" The bird replied, "I'm talking to you. You just need to mind the fountain to stay here." Frank asked, "What do you mean when you say 'mind the fountain'?" The bird said, "You need to be kind and gentle and look after it. See it everyday, make sure the water is clean, and ensure that it stays calm. You must also protect it from all harm. If you do that, you can stay here and enjoy the fountain forever." Frank thanked the bird, and promised to mind the fountain forever. He was so happy and peaceful each day, knowing he would always have the fountain to enjoy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his white toy car. It was a model of a big car that his dad had. Tom would take his toy car everywhere he went. One day, Tom's mom was making a cake in the kitchen. She asked Tom to help her. Tom was very happy to help. He took a big spoon and started to stir the cake mix. He stirred and stirred until it was all mixed up. While Tom was stirring, he had an idea. He took his white model car and put it on the table. He stirred the cake mix around the car, making a road for the car to drive on. Tom's mom smiled and they both had fun playing with the car and making the cake.
