Title: Amy the Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She went to the mall with her mom. They saw many shops and played fun games. Amy loved the mall. One day, Amy saw a dark room in the mall. She wanted to examine it. Her mom said, "No, Amy. It's not safe." But Amy did not listen. She went inside the dark room when her mom was not looking. In the dark room, Amy got lost. She cried and cried. Her mom looked for her but could not find her. The dark room was too big and scary. In the end, Amy learned to always listen to her mom.

Manny was a clever boy. He was always thinking of something new and wanted to try things out. One day, he found an old tie in his daddy's drawer. He asked his dad why it was in the drawer. His dad said, "Do you want to find out? It's a surprise!" Manny liked surprises and nodded his head excitedly. So his dad told him to go to his room, close the door and tie the tie around his waist. Manny was so excited, he couldn't wait to find out what happened next. After Manny tied the tie, he heard a strange sound. He opened the door and heard his dad say, "Look, Manny, your tie has increased in size! I think it's time for you to learn how to tie a real tie." Manny was so happy to have learned a new skill. His dad's clever plan had paid off and Manny had a new tie!

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. She had a pet cat named Max. Sue and Max loved to play catch with a ball. One day, they went to the park to play. While at the park, Sue saw a boy named Tim. Tim was saying an ugly word. Sue did not like ugly words. She went to Tim and said, "Please do not say that ugly word. It is not nice." Tim looked sad and said, "I am sorry, Sue. I will not say the ugly word again." Just then, a big wind came and blew the ugly word away. Sue, Max, and Tim were happy. They all played catch together and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time there lived a unique family. Every day, a daddy, a mommy, and their 3 year old daughter, Rachel, went for a walk around the mill. Every time, Rachel asked her parents to wear her a special dress she loved. One day, Mommy and Daddy said, “Rachel, today is special. Let’s all wear something special.” Rachel was very excited. She quickly put on her favorite dress, made of colorful flowers. She opened the door, ready to go on the family walk with Mommy and Daddy. When they arrived to the mill, they saw something unexpected. Rachel’s favorite dress was the same as the miller’s daughter who was working! Rachel was so surprised. She thought the dress was so special and unique, she thought that nobody else would have the same kind. Rachel was so happy to see that the miller’s daughter had the same dress on. They started talking, each wearing their unique dress. Mommy and Daddy smiled, as they watched Rachel chatting with the miller’s daughter.

Once upon a time, there was a huge cat named Tom. Tom was very hungry, so he went to the kitchen to find some food. He saw a table with a big cake on it. Tom's eyes got big and he said, "Yummy! I want to eat that cake!" Tom jumped on the table and started to spread the cake with his paws. He spread the cake all over the table, making a big mess. Tom was having so much fun, he didn't see his friend, little dog Jerry, come into the kitchen. Jerry saw the mess and said, "Tom, what are you doing? You are making a big mess!" Tom looked at Jerry and said, "I am eating the cake, but it is so big and I wanted to spread it first." Jerry laughed and said, "Tom, you are very silly! Let's clean up the mess and share the cake." So Tom and Jerry cleaned up the mess and shared the cake. They were both very happy and full. From that day on, they always shared their food and had lots of fun together. And Tom learned to spread the cake more carefully.
