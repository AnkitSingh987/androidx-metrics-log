Title: Sally and the Balloon

One day, Sally and her little brother, Brad, were playing in their backyard. Brad said, "Look, I'm flying!" He spread his arms out like an airplane, ran around the yard, and pretended to fly away. Sally laughed and went to find her dad. When she found him, Sally asked, "Dad, can we fly like a real plane?" Her dad smiled, "No, honey, I'm sorry. That might be a bit too scary for you two." Then he added, "But I know something that would be even more fun!" Sally and Brad remained curious as her dad drove them to a nearby field. When they got there, they both saw a hot air balloon! They couldn't believe it, and hurried over to it. Brad grabbed Sally's arm, his eyes wide and excited. But when a gust of wind blew, and the balloon started to shake, Sally and Brad stepped back. It was a bit scary, after all. Just then their dad came to their side, and said "Let's get inside, and it'll be alright." Sally and Brad laughed, and hopped into the basket. As the balloon rose higher, their dad pointed down and said, "See? Flying isn't so scary after all!"

Once upon a time, there was a ball that lived on a street. The ball was red and it was troubled. It didn't have any friends to play with. The ball was sad and lonely. One day, a boy came to the street. The boy saw the ball and wanted to play. The boy started to kick the ball up and down the street. The ball was happy to be playing with the boy. But the boy kicked the ball too hard. The ball went very far away and got lost. The boy looked for the ball, but he could not find it. The ball was all alone again and even more troubled than before.

Once upon a time, there was a happy girl named Amy. Amy loved to bake with her mom. One day, her mom told her a secret. "We will bake a big cake for dad," she said. Amy was very excited because her dad was in the navy and was coming home soon. Amy and her mom baked the cake. It was a big, yummy cake. Amy could not wait for her dad to see it. She knew it would make him happy too. They put the cake in a safe place, so it would be a surprise when dad came home. Finally, the day came when dad was coming home. Amy and her mom went to meet him. Dad was so happy to see them. When they got home, Amy showed dad the cake they baked. Dad was very happy and proud of his little girl. They all ate the cake together and had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a big green garden, there was a tiny insect. The insect was very sorry because it had no friends. It wanted to play but all the other insects were too busy. One day, the insect met a big, friendly frog. The insect said, "Will you be my friend?" The frog looked at the insect and said, "Yes, I will accept you as my friend. I am sorry you are lonely." From that day on, the insect and the frog played together in the garden. They were very happy and never felt sorry again. And all the other insects saw how much fun they were having, and they wanted to be friends too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived with her mom, dad, and her dog, Max. Lily liked to play with Max and help her mom and dad around the house. One day, Lily's mom asked her to help put the dishes in the dishwasher. Lily was a bit scared, but her mom encouraged her. She said, "You can do it, Lily! You are a great helper." Lily felt happy and started to put the dishes in the dishwasher. Lily and her mom finished putting all the dishes in the dishwasher. Lily's mom gave her a big hug and said, "Thank you, Lily! You did a great job." Lily felt proud and happy that she could help her mom. From that day on, Lily always helped with the dishwasher and other things around the house.
