Title: The Clean Room and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a clean room. In the room, there was a light. The light was hanging from the ceiling. It was a very pretty light. The room was always bright and happy. One day, a little girl named Amy came into the room. She saw the light and said, "Wow! What a pretty light!" The light heard Amy and said, "Thank you, Amy! I like to hang here and make the room bright." Amy liked to play in the clean room with the light. They would talk and laugh together. The light would shine on Amy's toys, and they would have so much fun. The light and Amy became very good friends. The end.

Sara was very cold. She wanted to go outside and play with her friends, but she did not have a warm jacket. She looked in her closet and saw a big jacket that was her brother's. She thought maybe she could wear it and be warm. She put on the jacket and zipped it up. It was very big and heavy. She felt something in the pocket. She put her hand inside and felt something soft and wet. She pulled it out and saw it was a half-eaten sandwich. It was green and smelly. It was disgusting! Sara screamed and threw the sandwich on the floor. She took off the jacket and ran to her mom. She told her what happened and showed her the sandwich. Her mom was angry and said, "That's your brother's jacket. He always fills his pockets with food and forgets about it. He is very messy and wasteful. He needs to learn to clean up after himself." She gave Sara a hug and said, "Don't worry, sweetie. I have a jacket for you. It's clean and warm and fits you well. You can wear it and go outside and have fun." She took Sara to her room and gave her a pink jacket with flowers on it. Sara liked it very much. She thanked her mom and put on the jacket. She felt cozy and happy. She went outside and joined her friends. They played in the snow and made a snowman. Sara had a lot of fun and forgot about the disgusting sandwich. She was glad she had a nice jacket and a nice mom.

Once upon a time, there was a big truck. The truck was very quiet. It drove down the street without making any noise. People would stop and admire the truck as it went by. One day, the quiet truck met a little boy named Tim. Tim loved the truck and said, "Wow! I admire your truck. It's so quiet and big!" The truck was happy to hear this and replied, "Thank you, Tim! I am happy to be quiet and big." Tim and the quiet truck became good friends. They would play together every day. Tim would ride in the truck and they would go on fun adventures. Everyone in the town loved the quiet truck and admired it very much. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a very envious bear. He saw that the other animals were always having fun and he thought it wasn't fair. So, he decided to make the fun his own. He went out and found a very big lock and he put it over the entrance of his den. That way, he could play and rest in his den without any of the other animals interrupting him. He was very proud of himself and he felt safe, happy and content. But one day, the other animals were looking in through the entrance and they were feeling envious of what he had. They whacked the lock with a stick and it fell off the entrance. The bear was angry and he knew he had to find another way to keep the other animals out. He decided he would rest in his den during the day when the other animals were out playing. That way, he could have fun in his den without the animals knowing. And the bear lived happily ever after - with his lock still intact.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Spot. Spot liked to examine things. He would look at them very closely. One day, he found something new. It was a treat. Spot picked up the treat with his mouth. He was very happy. He wanted to show his best friend, a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was sleeping in the sun. Spot ran to Fluffy and showed her the treat. Fluffy woke up and saw the treat. She was happy too. They decided to share the treat. Spot broke it into two pieces. They ate the treat together and were very happy. After that, they played all day in the sun.
