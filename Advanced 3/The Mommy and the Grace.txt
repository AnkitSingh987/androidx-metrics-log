Title: The Mommy and the Grace

Once there was a mommy, daddy, and a little girl. She was only three and her name was Grace. Grace was always happy, but today she was sad. Grace was feeling sour. She looked around and saw her mommy giving her daddy a hug. She wanted a hug, too. Grace went to hug her mommy, but it was too late. Mommy was already gone. Then she saw her daddy picking something off the wall. It was a hook! Grace ran over to her daddy and hugged him tight. Daddy smiled and said, "It's ok, Grace. Everything is going to be just fine." Grace was so happy. She hugged her daddy even tighter and they stayed like that until Grace stopped feeling so sour.

John was out playing in the garden. He saw a raft in the garden pond. He wanted to play with it, but he was scared that it might tip over. Suddenly, the raft began to fall. John reached out to catch it, but he was too late. The raft fell into the pond with a splash. John stuck his arm into the bitter water to try to grab the raft. It was so cold that he wanted to jump away. He tried again, this time gripping the raft tightly. With a big tug, he got the raft out of the pond. John was so happy, he shouted "Yay!" John couldn't wait to go on the raft. So, he put it into the water and climbed on. It wobbled at first but soon he was paddling away. He went around the pond and when he got back, he shouted for his mum who was watching from the other side. "Look at me, Mum! I'm on the raft!" He shouted. Mum smiled and clapped. She said, "Well done, John. You did a great job rescuing the raft from the pond!" John smiled and paddled away, feeling very proud of himself.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, blue drawer in her room. Lily liked to put her toys in the drawer. She had a lot of toys, but she did not like to share them. Lily was a little bit selfish. One day, Lily's friend, Tom, came over to play. Tom saw the big, blue drawer and asked Lily if she could bring out some toys to play with. Lily did not want to share her toys, but she did not want her friend to be sad. So, she opened the drawer and brought out some toys for them to play with. As they played, Lily learned that sharing her toys made her and Tom happy. She was not selfish anymore. From that day on, Lily always brought out her toys from the big, blue drawer to share with her friends. They all had fun playing together, and Lily was a good friend.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a weird little bird. The bird was weird because it could vanish and reappear. One day, the bird flew far to the east where it met a big elephant. "Hello, big elephant!" said the weird little bird. "Did you know that I can vanish?" "Wow! Show me!" said the big elephant. So, the weird little bird vanished and reappeared in front of the big elephant. The big elephant was amazed and wanted to learn how to vanish too. The weird little bird taught the big elephant how to vanish and reappear. Together, they went back to the village and showed their friends how they could vanish and reappear. All the animals in the village were amazed and wanted to learn too. The weird little bird and the big elephant taught everyone how to vanish and reappear. The moral of the story is to share your special talents with others, because it can bring happiness and fun to everyone around you.

Mama and Papa went to the store to buy food. Mama and Papa saw a lot of people in the store, but nobody was happy. Then, Mama and Papa heard two people arguing. Mama said, "It looks like a fight." Papa nodded. The two people arguing were very angry. They shouted and waved their arms. Papa said, "Let's go. We don't want to be here." So, Mama and Papa quickly left the store. On the way home, Mama said, "I'm glad that no one got hurt." Papa nodded again.
