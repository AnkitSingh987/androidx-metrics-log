Title: Tim and the Cup

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big empty fountain. Tim asked, "Mom, why is the fountain empty?" Mom said, "The fountain is empty because it needs to be fixed. The water will come back soon." Tim wanted to help keep the fountain full. He ran to the nearby pond and filled a small cup with water. He brought the cup to the fountain and poured it in. Tim did this many times, but the fountain stayed empty. Just then, a kind man came to the park. He saw Tim trying to fill the fountain. The man said, "I have a surprise for you." He pressed a button on a remote. Suddenly, the fountain was full of water! Tim was so happy. He clapped his hands and said, "Thank you!" The man smiled and said, "You're welcome. Now we can all enjoy the fountain together."

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play dress up. He had a big box of costumes. One day, he found a new costume. It was a big, red cape. Tim believed he could be a superhero with this cape. Tim put on the cape and ran outside. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue was deaf, but she could talk with her hands. Tim wanted to show Sue his cape. He said, "Look at my costume!" Sue looked at the cape and smiled. She talked with her hands, saying, "It's a nice cape!" Tim and Sue played together all day. They both believed they were superheroes. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they went home. Tim took off his cape and put it back in the costume box. He knew he could be a superhero again another day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Bella. She was a very organized girl, but her room was always disorganized. One day her mom saw the mess and asked her to organize her room. But Bella wanted to play and said no. "I don't want to organize," Bella said. "But if you don't, then you can't play," said her mom. Bella was sad but she started organizing her room. It was hard work, but eventually she was finished. She found a lot of colorful toys she had forgotten. Now that she had organized everything, it was easier to pick out what she wanted to play with. When Bella was done she was very happy. Her mom hugged her and said that it's always a good idea to organize things. "Organizing not only makes it easier to find what you need," said her mom, "It also teaches us to be responsible for our belongings." The moral of the story is that organizing can be fun and scents, and it also teaches us to be responsible and to keep a tidy space.

One day, a tall man named Tom went for a walk. He wanted to find a new path in the woods. He took his camera to record what he saw. Tom loved to walk and see new things. As he walked, Tom met a little girl named Sue. Sue said, "I know a fun path, but it's hard to find." Tom asked, "Can you show me?" Sue smiled and said, "Follow me!" They walked and walked until they found the hidden path. Tom and Sue walked on the path and saw many pretty things. Tom recorded everything with his camera. At the end of the path, they found a big surprise. It was a beautiful waterfall! Tom and Sue were very happy they found the secret path.

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey who was very hungry. He had looked everywhere but he could not find any food. He was sad and felt defeated. Then, the monkey looked up and saw a flame in the sky. He wanted to reach it, so he decided to climb the tallest tree. He started to climb and climb, but he was so small and weak. But then, something amazing happened. The monkey was able to reach the flame and it turned out to be a bright, juicy fruit. He was overjoyed and so excited! The little monkey ate the fruit and it was so sweet and delicious. He felt so happy and was no longer hungry. The little monkey went back down the tree and decided he would come back to the flame every day to find more food. He was thankful and always knew he could find something tasty at the flame.
