Title: Lily the Girl and the Tried

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. One day, she found a pretty box in her room. She wanted to see if her toy car would fit in the box. She tried and tried, but the car was too big. Lily felt frustrated. Lily's mom saw her trying and said, "Wait, let's try something else." She brought a smaller toy car for Lily. Lily tried to put the small car in the box. This time, the car fit perfectly. Lily was very happy. Lily played with the box and the small car all day. She learned that sometimes she needs to wait and try something else when she feels frustrated. And from that day on, Lily always remembered her mom's advice.

Once upon a time, there was a soft, green ball. The ball lived in a big park with many trees. It loved to play with the children who came to the park. One sunny day, a boy named Tim came to the park. He saw the soft, green ball and wanted to play with it. He picked it up and said, "Hello, ball! Let's play together!" The ball was very happy. Tim threw the ball high in the sky. It went up, up, up and then came down, down, down. Tim would strike the ball with his hand and it would fly far away. The soft, green ball loved playing with Tim. They played all day and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. One night, he heard a noise in his room. He felt a tremble in his legs. Tim saw a little mouse by his bed. The mouse said, "Hello, Tim! I am Momo the mouse. I am looking for my steel toy. It is cool and shiny. Can you help me find it?" Tim and Momo looked under the bed. They found the cool steel toy. Momo was happy. Tim was not scared anymore. They became good friends and played together every night.

Once upon a time, in a cute little house, there lived a cat named Tom and a dog named Max. They were best friends and loved to play together in the yard. One sunny day, they were playing hide and seek when it was time for lunch. Tom's mom called out, "Tom, Max, come inside for food!" They ran into the house and sat at the table. Tom's mom gave them yummy food to eat. Tom had a big bowl of milk and Max had a big bone. After they finished eating, Tom said, "Max, let's stay inside and play with my toys." Max nodded and wagged his tail. They played with Tom's toy cars and had lots of fun. When it was time for bed, they both fell asleep, dreaming of their next adventure.

Tommy was a curious three-year-old. He was always looking around for something new and exciting. One day, he was gazing out the window of his house when he saw an adventurous man. The man was walking around the neighborhood, looking out at the sky. Tommy was intrigued. He couldn't take his eyes off of the man. He watched him carefully and began to imagine all the places he could go and what kinds of things he could do if he was that adventurous. The next day, Tommy started to build an airplane out of cardboard and scraps he found around the house. After an hour of hard work, the plane was complete. He ran outside and climbed into the cockpit. With a huge grin on his face, Tommy took off. He flew over the neighborhood, waving to the man below. The man waved back and smiled. When Tommy landed, the man ran up and introduced himself. His name was Paul and he was a pilot. He offered to teach Tommy how to fly a real airplane. Tommy was overjoyed. He had always dreamed of being an adventurous pilot and here was his chance. He spent the next few months learning all he could from the pilot and eventually started taking flying lessons. It was only a few short months later that Tommy was soaring the skies in his very own airplane. He was living out his dream and he had the adventurous man to thank for it.
