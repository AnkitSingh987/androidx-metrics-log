Title: Ben the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Ben. Ben liked to play in the park. One day, Ben saw a big ball. The ball had a pretty patch on it. Ben wanted to play with the ball. He thought, "I will borrow this ball." Ben was very impatient. He did not wait to find the owner of the ball. He took the ball and ran away. Ben played with the ball all day long. He had so much fun. But he did not know that the ball was not his. At the end of the day, a sad girl came to the park. She was looking for her ball. The girl saw Ben with her ball. She was very upset. Ben knew he did something wrong. The girl took her ball back and left the park. Ben felt bad and was all alone. He wished he had waited and not taken the ball.

One day, two children, Tommy and Emma, were walking in the woods. Tommy was feeling a bit tired, and a big yawn escaped his mouth. He was amazed by the mist that surrounded him, making everything look mysterious. Emma was brave and adventurous, so she started to explore. She stepped forward into the mist, hoping to find something special. She walked for a few minutes until the mist started to clear. Suddenly, Emma found a big tree with a hollow in the centre. Inside the hollow, she found a shiny, golden coin. Emma was so excited and couldn't believe her luck. She ran back to Tommy, who was still yawning. After a while, Tommy was ready to explore with Emma. The two children went on an adventurous journey together, exploring the misty woods. And of course, the golden coin was their special lucky charm. That way they always stay safe!

Once upon a time, there was a green frog who lived in a busy pond. The pond was full of fish, ducks, and other animals. The green frog liked to jump and play with his friends. But sometimes, he would complain when it was too loud. One day, the green frog met a new friend in the pond. The new friend was a tiny fish. The fish was very busy swimming around and playing with the other fish. The green frog wanted to play with the tiny fish, but he could not swim as fast. The green frog started to complain. He said, "It is too busy in this pond! I can't play with my new friend." The tiny fish heard the frog and decided to help. They found a quiet spot in the pond where they could play together without all the noise. The green frog and the tiny fish played all day. They had a lot of fun and did not complain anymore. They were happy to be friends and to have found a quiet place to play in the busy pond. And from that day on, they played together every day, and the green frog never complained again.

Once upon a time, there was a lively bunny. The bunny loved to play in the forest. The forest was full of big trees and pretty flowers. The bunny had many friends in the forest. They would hop, run, and play all day long. One day, while playing, the bunny saw a big, shiny rock. The rock was very pretty. The bunny wanted to show the rock to his friends. He picked up the rock and started to hop back to his friends. But as he hopped, the rock made him disappear! The bunny was scared, but he did not drop the rock. He knew he had to find his friends. He hopped and hopped, but he was still gone. His friends looked for him, but they could not see him. They were very sad. Then, the bunny had an idea. He dropped the shiny rock and he came back! His friends saw him and they were so happy. They all hugged and played together again. The bunny knew that he should not pick up the shiny rock again. They all played in the forest and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a funny crocodile. He liked to help his friends. One day, he saw a bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. The funny crocodile wanted to help the bird. The crocodile had an idea. He let the bird sit on his back. Then, he swam very fast. The bird felt like it was flying! The bird was happy. The funny crocodile was happy too. They became good friends. One day, the bird saw a big fish in the water. The fish wanted to eat the crocodile! The bird had a plan. It used its beak to tickle the fish. The fish laughed so hard, it swam away. The bird helped the funny crocodile. They were a great team.
