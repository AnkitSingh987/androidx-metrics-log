Title: Billy and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a naughty little boy called Billy. Billy loved playing outside, but he didn't like going to the hairdresser for a haircut. One day, Billy's mum said to him "Billy, it's time for your haircut. Put your shoes on and come with me". But Billy didn't want to go, so he started to cry. Suddenly, Billy's dad had an idea. He said "Billy, why don't you write a letter to the hairdresser and tell him what kind of haircut you want? Then you can take the letter with you when we go to the hairdresser". Billy liked this idea and he quickly wrote a letter. When they arrived, the hairdresser read the letter and gave Billy the perfect haircut. Billy was so happy and he thought it was nice to write a letter. The end!

One day, a group of friends went on a safari. They saw many animals like lions, elephants, and monkeys. They were very happy and excited. They wanted to sing a song for the animals. So, they started to sing a fun and loud song. As they sang, they saw a messy spot on the ground. They did not know what it was. They went closer to look at it. They thought it was mud, but it was not. It was a big pile of paint that the monkeys had spilled. The monkeys heard the friends singing and came to see them. The monkeys were excited and started to throw the paint at the friends. The friends laughed and joined the monkeys. They all played with the paint and had a very fun and messy day. In the end, the safari turned into a big paint party with the animals.

One day, Jane was playing in her garden. All of a sudden she sneezed. Her Dad heard it and came to see what was wrong. Dad: "Are you okay, Jane? Why did you sneeze?" Jane: "My nose tickled!" Dad: "Oh dear, I think you should go inside and rest." Jane: "But I've got lots of time to play!" Dad: "I know you want to play, but I want you to be reliable and listen to me. You need to go inside." So, Jane reluctantly went inside, feeling sad. Dad followed her and carried her up to her bedroom. He tucked her into bed and gave her a big hug. Jane smiled and drifted off to sleep. Thanks to Dad's reliable advice, Jane soon felt better and was soon back in the garden, playing and sneezing happily.

One day, a little girl named Lily and her daddy went to the park. They saw a big, filthy puddle. Lily wanted to jump in it, but daddy said "No, it's too dirty!" Lily felt sad, but daddy encouraged her to play on the swings instead. While they were playing, a dog came running to the park. The dog was very happy and ran to the filthy puddle. The dog jumped in the puddle and got all wet and dirty. Lily and daddy laughed at the funny dog. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog shook all the water and dirt off, and it was clean! Lily and daddy were so surprised. Daddy said, "Wow, I didn't know that could happen!" They all played together in the park, and Lily learned that sometimes, fun surprises can happen.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the river with his mom. They saw a raft near the water. Tim's mom said, "Tim, I want to introduce you to this raft. It can help us cross the river." Tim and his mom got on the raft. They were happy and laughed as they floated on the water. The sun was warm and the birds sang songs. As they moved along the river, they saw a big fish jump out of the water. The fish said, "Hello, little boy and mom. I am a talking fish!" Tim and his mom were very surprised. They didn't know fish could talk. The talking fish swam with them until they reached the other side of the river. Tim and his mom thanked the fish for the fun adventure and waved goodbye as they walked back home.
