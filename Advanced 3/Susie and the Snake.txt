Title: Susie and the Snake

Once upon a time, a little girl named Susie was walking in the woods. She was feeling brave so she decided to explore. As she took each step, she searched for interesting treasures. Suddenly, she saw something colourful and slithering near her feet. She stopped in her tracks and said, "What is that?" It was a snake! She stepped back and felt scared. The snake hissed then slithered away. Susie took a deep breath and stepped carefully towards the snake. Even though she was scared, she wanted to make sure the snake was okay. When Susie was only a few steps away, the snake stopped and flicked its tongue. She held her breath, not knowing what to do next. Then, out of the blue, the snake started speaking! "Don't worry Susie, I mean you no harm. I'm just looking for food. I'm a good snake". Susie smiled, relieved. She stepped closer and glanced down at the snake. "You're a very good snake", she said and held out a leaf for it to eat. The snake eagerly accepted the gift and thanked Susie before slithering away. From then on, Susie and the friendly snake had a very good friendship. The End

Once there was a mother bird who was building a nest. She said to her little baby bird that she had to go to find food. The little bird said, "Wait, I want to come with you!" But the mother said, "No darling, stay here and wait for me." A few hours later, the mother bird returned with a tasty snack for the little bird, who had been waiting patiently in the nest. The little bird was so excited that it started to open the snack with its beak, when suddenly the mother bird got an idea. She said, "Let's play a game!" The mother bird said, "Try to open the snack without using your beak!" The little bird tried, but it just couldn't get the snack open. After a few more tries, the little bird was getting frustrated and said, "I can't do it!" The mother bird said, "It's ok, don't be sorry. I'll help you open it. That's what mothers are for." She let the little bird get the snack open with her beak, and the little bird was very happy. The mother bird said, "See, I told you that being patient pays off! Now you can enjoy this delicious snack." The little bird thanked his mother and they both went back to the nest to enjoy the tasty treat together.

Once upon a time, in a land of big hills and pretty flowers, there was a grumpy skull named Mr. Bones. Mr. Bones lived in a small, dark cave. He did not like the sun or the happy animals that played outside. One day, a little bird named Chirpy came to the cave. Chirpy said, "Mr. Bones, come march with me outside!" Mr. Bones replied, "No, I am grumpy and I like my cave." Chirpy did not give up. He said, "Please, Mr. Bones. It is fun outside!" Chirpy took Mr. Bones by the hand, and they started to march. They marched up the big hills and through the pretty flowers. Mr. Bones felt the warm sun on his face and saw the happy animals playing. As they marched, Mr. Bones started to smile. At the end of the day, Mr. Bones said, "Thank you, Chirpy, for showing me how fun it is to march outside." Chirpy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Mr. Bones. I am glad you are not grumpy anymore." From that day on, Mr. Bones and Chirpy were the best of friends, and they marched together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very proud because it was his birthday. He was so happy and excited. Tim's mom and dad had a big surprise for him. They did not tell Tim what it was. Tim could not wait to find out. He jumped up and down with a big smile on his face. When it was time for the surprise, Tim's mom and dad showed him a big cake with candles. Tim was so proud and happy. He blew out the candles and everyone clapped. Tim had a great birthday and he would always remember it.
