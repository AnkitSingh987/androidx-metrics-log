Title: Tim the Boy and the Dolphin

One day, a boy named Tim went to the sea with his mom. They wanted to see the water and the fish. Tim was very happy. He hoped to see a playful dolphin in the water. As they walked near the water, Tim and his mom observed a big rock. Tim's mom said, "Remember this rock, Tim. It will help us find our way back." Tim nodded, and they kept walking. After a while, they saw a playful dolphin in the water. Tim was so excited! He and his mom watched the dolphin jump and splash. When it was time to go home, they found the big rock again. It helped them find their way back, just like mom said.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tommy. Tommy was only three years old, and he loved playing outside. One day, Tommy was out in the yard and he heard a strange sound. Thinking it might be fun, Tommy went to see what was making the noise. When he arrived, he saw a very unusual event. Tommy was amazed to see that a group of animals were marching in a line around the yard. There were birds, cats, and even little mice. They were all walking in a line together and it was quite amazing. Tommy couldn't believe his eyes. He went up to one of the birds and asked, "What are you doing?" The bird replied, "We are marching around the yard. It's an unusual thing to do, but it's lots of fun!" Tommy was delighted. He decided to join the march, and he was soon marching around the yard with all the animals. Every day, Tommy and the animals would march around the yard, and it became a very special event for them. The End.

One day, a cute little ball was in a room. It liked to roll all around the room. It rolled on the floor and it rolled on the bed. The ball was very happy when it rolled. One day, the ball met a little toy car. The toy car said, "Hi, I like to roll too! Can we roll together?" The ball said, "Yes! Let's roll and have fun!" So, the cute ball and the toy car rolled all around the room. They rolled under the table and they rolled on the chair. They laughed and played together. And they became the best friends who loved to roll in the room.

Once upon a time, there was a happy song. It was sung by a bird far away. It was a nice song, but nobody knew what it said. One day, a small child heard the song. She kept listening to it and soon she knew the words. She began to sing it! Everyone thought it was a funny song. The child's parents wanted to know the song too. So the child taught them. They all sang the song together and had lots of fun. Soon, the neighbours heard the song. They all partied and sang together. Everyone was singing the same song. It was fantastic! The song spread to other places. More people partied and sang the song. Eventually, the happy song was known all over the place. Nobody knew where it had come from. It was an unknown song!

Sam was super excited. It was his first day of school! He nervously put on his backpack and zipped it closed. "Ready?" His Dad asked him. Sam nodded. "Let's go!" On their way to school, Dad reminded him, "Remember, follow the rules and practice your ABCs." Sam arrived at school feeling scared and embarrassed. Everyone seemed so big and loud compared to him. Finally, it was time for recess. Sam noticed a group playing tag and zipped over to join them. He proudly chased his classmates and was having a lot of fun. Suddenly, the school's warning bell rang. Sam had lost track of time. He was the last one to go back inside. He was ashamed for not following the rules. Dad had reminded him about the rules and he still broke them. Sam resolved to not let it happen again and he found that as long as he was careful, he could have lots of fun at school.
