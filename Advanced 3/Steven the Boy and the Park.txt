Title: Steven the Boy and the Park

Once upon a time there was a small boy named Steven who went to the park with his dad. Whilst running around, he felt an itch on his arm. He rubbed his arm and his dad asked him what was wrong. Steven replied "I have an itch". With a smile, his dad replied "Maybe a friendly caterpillar is visiting you". Steven was curious and started to search around, but he couldn't find any caterpillar. Suddenly, he noticed a small furry caterpillar on his arm. He investigated and realised that the caterpillar was what had made him itch. He looked up to his dad and showed him the caterpillar. His dad smiled and then Steven noticed the small caterpillar had become bigger, it was now a butterfly! Steven was amazed; he didn't realise that caterpillars could turn into butterflies. He gently butterfly flew away into the sky. Steven watched it fly away and realised that life can bring so many surprises. He held his dad's hand and smiled as they walked away from the park.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Jane. They were very close and loved spending time together. One day, Jack said to Jane, "Let's go on a picnic!" Jane was really excited. So Jack packed a bag with some sandwiches, fruit, and drinks. He zipped up the bag neatly and they set off to the park. When they arrived, they spread out the picnic blanket on the grass and enjoyed all the delicious food. Suddenly, Jane saw another family enjoying their picnic not far away. She became jealous and said "I don't want to stay any more". Jack was disappointed, but he packed up their bag and they went home. Back at home, Jack comforted Jane by telling her, "We'll have lots more picnics together in the future". Jane smiled and cheered up.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He had a regular folder that he loved very much. One day, he went outside to gather leaves for his folder. He found many pretty leaves to put inside. Tom was very happy with his leaves. He went to show his mom all the leaves he gathered. But when he opened his folder, something unexpected happened. Instead of leaves, there were colorful drawings inside. Tom was very surprised! He didn't know how the drawings got there. His mom smiled and said, "I saw you gather leaves, so I drew pictures of them for you." Tom was so happy to have both the leaves and the drawings in his folder.

Mia and Leo were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with many leaves. They wanted to climb the tree and see what was on top. "Let's go, Mia!" Leo said. "Maybe we can find a prize in the tree." "OK, Leo!" Mia said. "But be careful. The tree is very old and ancient. It might break." They climbed the tree slowly and carefully. They reached the top and looked around. They saw many birds and squirrels. They also saw a shiny thing hanging from a branch. "Look, Mia! A prize!" Leo said. He reached for the shiny thing. It was a necklace with a blue stone. "Wow, Leo! It's so pretty!" Mia said. "Where did it come from?" "I don't know, Mia. Maybe someone left it here long ago. Maybe it's magic." Leo said. He put the necklace around his neck. He felt happy and proud. Suddenly, they heard a loud crack. The branch they were sitting on broke. They started to fall. "Help, Mia!" Leo said. "We're going to drop!" "Help, Leo!" Mia said. "Hold on to me!" They grabbed each other and screamed. They dropped to the ground. They landed on a pile of leaves. They were not hurt, but they were scared. They looked at each other and hugged. They were glad they were OK. "Are you OK, Mia?" Leo said. "I'm OK, Leo." Mia said. "Are you OK?" "I'm OK, Mia." Leo said. "But look, I lost the prize." They looked at the tree. The necklace was gone. It had fallen off Leo's neck when they dropped. They could not see it anywhere. "Maybe it was not a prize, Leo." Mia said. "Maybe it was a curse. Maybe the tree did not like us taking it." "Maybe you're right, Mia." Leo said. "Maybe we should leave it alone. Maybe it belongs to the tree." They got up and dusted themselves off. They decided to go back to their parents. They learned a lesson. They would not climb the ancient tree again. They would not take things that were not theirs. They would be happy with what they had.
