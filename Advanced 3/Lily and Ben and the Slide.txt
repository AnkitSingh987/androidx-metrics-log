Title: Lily and Ben and the Slide

Lily and Ben liked to play in the park. They liked to slide, swing, and run. One day, they saw a big hole in the ground. They wanted to see what was inside. They went near the hole and looked down. It was dark and deep. They heard a noise. It sounded like a growl. They got scared. "Maybe there is a monster underground," Lily said. "Maybe it is a big, heavy, scary monster," Ben said. They wanted to run away, but they also wanted to be brave. They had an idea. "Let's find something to fit in the hole and block it," Lily said. "Good idea," Ben said. They looked around and saw a big rock. It was heavy, but they worked together to push it. They rolled it to the hole and pushed it in. The rock fit in the hole. The noise stopped. They felt happy and proud. They high-fived each other and ran back to the slide. They had fun and forgot about the monster underground.

There once lived a fat rabbit. Every day, the rabbit liked to collect things: stones, flowers, leaves and even apples. He would spend hours looking for the perfect items to add to his collection. One day, the rabbit was so focused on finding new things that he didn’t notice something new had arrived. “Hello there,” said a small voice. Startled, the rabbit looked around and saw a little girl standing by the tree. “What do you want?” asked the rabbit. The little girl smiled. “I want to collect together!” she said. The rabbit considered this. He thought it would be nice to have company while collecting things, so he agreed. Together, the rabbit and the little girl found many interesting items for their collections. They spent so much time together that soon they became best friends. The rabbit was very happy. He loved having a friend who shared his passion for collecting. And the little girl was happy too. Every day she came to collect with her fat rabbit friend.

Once upon a time, there was a silly farmer. He lived on a farm with many animals. He had cows, pigs, and chickens. One day, the silly farmer made a promise to his animals. He said, "I promise to take good care of you." But the silly farmer did not keep his promise. He forgot to feed the animals. The cows were hungry, the pigs were hungry, and the chickens were hungry too. The silly farmer was too busy playing games and having fun. He did not think about the animals. In the end, the animals were very sad and hungry. They had to leave the farm to find food. The silly farmer was all alone on his farm. He cried because he missed his animals. He learned that he should always keep his promises, but it was too late.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named True. True lived in a small house with a big, open yard. One day, True saw a cat in the yard. The cat was dirty and needed to wash. True said, "Hello, cat! You are dirty. Let me help you wash." The cat looked at True and said, "No, I don't want to wash. I like being dirty." True tried to help the cat wash, but the cat ran away. True chased the cat all around the open yard. They ran and ran, but True could not catch the cat. At last, True stopped to rest. The cat climbed a tall tree and looked down at True. True wanted to help the cat, but the cat didn't want help. The cat stayed dirty and never came down from the tree. True was sad that the cat didn't want to be clean. The cat stayed in the tree, and True went back to his small house. The cat and True never became friends, and the cat stayed dirty forever.

One day, a little girl named Lily had a funny dream. In her dream, she found a magic box. The magic box could talk! It said, "Hi Lily, I can make your toys come to life!" Lily was so happy. But, there was a problem. Lily's toys were not nice. They did not want to play with her. They made a big mess in her room. Lily was sad. She asked the magic box, "Can you make my toys nice?" The magic box said, "I will try." The magic box tried very hard. It used all its magic to make the toys nice. And it worked! The toys became nice and played with Lily. They had so much fun together. Lily was happy again. When she woke up from her dream, she smiled and hugged her toys.
