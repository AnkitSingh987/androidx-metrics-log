Title: Fin the Fish and the New

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big tank with his fish friends. One day, Fin saw a new fish in the tank. The new fish was very original. It had bright colors and big eyes. Fin swam to the new fish and said, "Hi! My name is Fin. What's your name?" The new fish smiled and said, "Hello, Fin! I'm Bubbles. I can jump very high!" Fin was excited to see Bubbles jump. Bubbles swam to the top of the tank and jumped high in the water. Fin and his friends clapped their fins and cheered. They were very happy to have a new friend who could do something special. From that day on, Fin and Bubbles played and jumped in the tank together, having lots of fun.

One day, on a Wednesday, Tom and Sue were playing in the park. They found a big, hard rock. Tom wanted to take it home, but Sue wanted it too. "Let's share the rock," said Sue. Tom did not want to share. He wanted the rock all to himself. This made Sue sad. Tom saw Sue's sad face and thought about it. He decided to share the rock with her. "Okay, we can share the rock," said Tom. Sue was happy, and they played with the rock together. They learned that sharing is more fun than keeping things all to themselves.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lucy. One day, Lucy found a big box in her room. She was very excited to unpack the box. Inside the box, she found a shiny diamond. Lucy thought it was the prettiest thing she had ever seen. Lucy showed the diamond to her friend, Tom. Tom said, "Wow, that is a big diamond! But it looks uncomfortable to hold." Lucy agreed but she loved the diamond so much, she did not want to let it go. As they played with the diamond, it started to get very hot. Lucy and Tom felt uncomfortable holding it. They tried to put the diamond back in the box, but it was too late. The diamond became too hot and burned their hands. They both cried and wished they had never unpacked the box.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a fragile toy hose. He loved to play with it in the garden. One day, he took his hose outside to water the plants. As Tim played, he saw a small cat lying under a tree. The cat was very tired. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he used his fragile hose to give the cat some water. The cat drank the water and felt better. Tim and the cat became good friends. They played together every day. Tim used his hose to water the plants, and the cat would lie in the warm sun. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was very angry. He did not like it when other animals touched his toys. One day, he saw a little mouse named Jerry playing with his toys. Tom did not want Jerry to touch his toys. Tom went to Jerry and said, "Do not touch my toys! I order you to go away!" Jerry was scared of the angry cat, so he ran away very fast. Tom felt better now that Jerry was not touching his toys. The next day, Tom saw Jerry again. This time, Jerry was not touching Tom's toys. Instead, he was playing with his own toys. Tom was not angry anymore. He went to Jerry and said, "Let's play together." From that day on, Tom and Jerry became good friends and played together with their toys.

Once upon a time, there was a poor dog named Buddy. Buddy was dirty and needed a bath. He didn't like to bathe, but he knew he had to. One day, Buddy's friend, a cat named Kitty, came to visit. Kitty said, "Buddy, you need a bath. You're very dirty!" Buddy agreed, but he wanted to watch a video first. Kitty found a fun video for them to watch together. They laughed and had a good time. After the video, it was time for Buddy's bath. Kitty helped Buddy get into the water. But something unexpected happened! The water was magic and it turned Buddy into a clean, fluffy bunny! Buddy and Kitty were surprised, but they liked the change. Now, they could play together and Buddy didn't have to worry about getting dirty again.
