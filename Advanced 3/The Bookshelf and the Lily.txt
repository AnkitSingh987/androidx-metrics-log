Title: The Bookshelf and the Lily

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a bookshelf. The bookshelf was in a gloomy room. It was not happy. It wanted to be in a bright room with many friends. One day, a little girl named Lily came into the room. She saw the sad bookshelf and wanted to help. Lily thought hard. She had an idea! She took all the books from the bookshelf and put them on the floor. Then, she painted the bookshelf with many colors. The bookshelf became very pretty. But, Lily did not put the books back on the bookshelf. She forgot. The next day, Lily's mom came into the room. She saw the books on the floor and was not happy. She told Lily to clean up the mess. Lily felt regret. She knew she should have put the books back on the bookshelf. Lily learned that when you help someone, you must finish the job. Now, the bookshelf was in a bright room with many friends. It was happy. Lily learned to always finish what she started. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a poor dog named Max wanted to cook. He was very hungry. Max did not know how to cook, but he wanted to try. He went to the kitchen and saw a big pot. Max put some food in the pot. He was very excited. Max was cooking the food when he heard a loud noise. It was a big cat. The cat wanted to eat Max's food too. Max did not want to share his food. He was very sad. The cat was mean and did not want to go away. Then, something unexpected happened. Max saw a small exit in the kitchen. He had an idea. Max ran to the exit and called the cat to follow him. The cat followed Max out of the exit. Max quickly went back inside and closed the door. The cat was outside, and Max was inside with his food. Max was very happy that he could cook and eat his food all by himself.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Mark. Mark always loved to spray silly things with a water spray bottle. One day, Mark decided to spray his dad's alarm. He thought it would be fun. Mark was scared when he heard the loud alarms going off in the house. It was really crazy and noisy. He started to cry. His mom came running into the room and saw Mark. She asked him why the alarms were going off. Mark told her that he had sprayed the alarm with his water bottle. His mom looked at Mark seriously and told him, “Mark, you should never spray things that you don't know how to control. You could have caused a lot of trouble!” Mark understood. From then on, he made sure to think before he sprayed something. He learnt an important lesson that day - always be smart before you do something crazy!

Once upon a time, there was a man named Dan and a woman named Sue. Dan and Sue were married, and they did their best to take care of their family. One day, Dan was out looking for a job. He asked many people, and showed them what he could do. Eventually, Dan found a job at a smelly factory. He was very excited to get the job, so he could make money and help his family. When Dan went home to tell Sue, she was happy. However, she was also worried because she heard that the factory was smelly. "We'll make it work," she said, "If you like the job then you should do it." So Dan took the job and he worked hard every day. Despite the smell of the place, Dan managed to keep a smile on his face. He worked to provide for his family and life was good. Dan and Sue were happy.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Sam. He was three years old, and he loved to play in the snow. One chilly day, he quickly put on his mitten and ran outside to build a snowman. He scooped up a big pile of snow and rolled it into a ball. He did this two more times for the snowman's body. But when it came time to make the head, Sam couldn't find his other mitten. He searched his pockets but he couldn't find it. He was so frustrated he kept saying "stupid mitten!" He looked everywhere, but it was nowhere to be seen. Just then, Sam heard a voice. It was his mom, speaking from his warm living room. She said "try the drawer, Sam - you know where it is." Sam opened the drawer, and he saw his mitten inside. He said "thank you!", and then he repeated the same words - thank you, thank you - over and over again. Then he put on the mitten and continued making his snowman. In the end, it was the biggest, most beautiful snowman he had ever seen!
