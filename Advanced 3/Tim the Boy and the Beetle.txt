Title: Tim the Boy and the Beetle

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to study. He saw a harmless beetle on the ground. The beetle was very small and had a shiny back. Tim thought the beetle was very pretty. Tim wanted to show his friend, Sam, the beetle. He picked up the beetle and went to Sam's house. "Look at this beetle, Sam!" said Tim. Sam looked at the beetle and got scared. "No, Tim! I don't like bugs!" said Sam. Tim didn't listen to Sam and put the beetle on Sam's hand. Sam was so scared that he hit the beetle and it fell to the ground. The beetle didn't move anymore. Tim was sad and Sam felt bad. They learned that they should listen to each other and be careful with small creatures.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big book of math. Lucy thought math was amazing. Every day, she would sit and do her math. She would wonder what new things she would learn. One sunny day, Lucy went to the park with her math book. She sat under a big tree and started to do her math. As she was doing her math, something unexpected happened. A little squirrel came down from the tree and took her math book! Lucy was very surprised. She did not know what to do. The squirrel ran away with her math book. Lucy ran after the squirrel. She wanted her math book back. The squirrel ran up another tree. Lucy looked up and saw a big nest. The squirrel put her math book in the nest. Lucy wondered why the squirrel took her book. Then, she saw baby squirrels in the nest. They were looking at her math book. Lucy smiled. She realized that the squirrel wanted to learn math too. She thought it was amazing that even squirrels liked math. Lucy decided to share her math book with the squirrel family. They all sat under the tree and did math together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Ryan. He loved to wear his boots. Everywhere he went, he wanted to wear them. One day, he wanted to go to the park. He grabbed his boots and ran to find his mom and dad. He called out, “Mom! Dad! Can I wear my boots to the park?” His parents smiled and nodded. Ryan was so pleased. He shouted for joy as he put on his boots. Once his boots were on, Ryan ran outside. He raced around and jumped over puddles in joy. Then, something sweet caught his eye. It was a candy stand! Without hesitation, Ryan ran to the stand and demanded a lollipop. He smiled from ear to ear when he got it! As Ryan sucked on his lollipop, he danced around in his boots. He was very happy.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Pete and Pam. Pete was always very curious, while Pam was a bit more conservative. One day, they decided to explore the nearby woods. As they walked, they came across a pond. They stood at the edge and looked into the clear waters. They noticed a small fish swimming close to the surface, so Pete decided to pick it up. As he reached for the fish, it slipped through his fingers, started to splash around, and finally settled in another part of the pond. Then Pam had an idea. She said "Let's stop and think before we act, instead of being ignorant and just doing whatever we want. If we're more careful and think more, we can avoid making mistakes". Pete put his thumb up in agreement and they both smiled. From then on, they reminded each other to stop and think before they acted. When they made mistakes, they corrected themselves, learned from them and moved on. Moral of the story: A little bit of thought can help us avoid making mistakes and help us settle the right way.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play hockey with his friends. They would play hockey every day after school. Tim had a gray hockey stick that he liked very much. One sunny day, Tim and his friends were playing hockey in the park. Tim hit the ball very hard with his gray hockey stick. The ball flew high into the sky and landed in a small fire that someone had made. The ball started to burn and turned black. Tim was sad that his ball burned, but his friends cheered him up. They found another ball to play with and continued their hockey game. Tim promised to be more careful next time and they all had lots of fun.
