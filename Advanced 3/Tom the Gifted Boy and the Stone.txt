Title: Tom the Gifted Boy and the Stone

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gifted boy named Tom. He loved to laugh and play near the creek with his friends. One day, he saw something shiny in the water. He thought it was a special thing and wanted to find out what it was. The next day, Tom and his friends went to the creek again. They played and laughed all day. Tom remembered the shiny thing and told his friends about it. They all wanted to find it, so they looked and looked, but they could not find it that day. On the third day, Tom and his friends went back to the creek. They played and laughed some more. Then, Tom saw the shiny thing again! He reached into the water and pulled it out. It was a beautiful, shiny stone. Tom and his friends were so happy that they found the special thing. They knew the creek was a magical place and they would always remember the fun times they had there.

One day, John went to visit the original alligator. He wanted to see the alligator as he had never seen one before. When he arrived, he saw the alligator sunbathing in the sun. John said, "Hello alligator!" But the alligator didn't say anything back. It just stared at him. John felt very scared. He didn't know what to do. Suddenly, the alligator jumped up and started running towards him. It was very fast. John was so scared that he started to cry. Just then, John's mom came to visit. She saw the alligator running and quickly picked up John. She started running away from the alligator. The alligator chased them until they made it safely back home. John felt relieved and thanked his mom for saving him. His mom said, "It's ok, John. Let's not visit any original alligators again!"

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play hide and seek. One day, they decided to play in their dad's office, where he kept a lot of papers and books and things. They thought it would be fun to find new hiding spots and surprise each other. Ben went to hide first, and he saw a big jar on a shelf. He climbed on a chair and reached for the jar. He was curious what was inside. He opened the lid and peeked. He saw a lot of shiny coins and green papers. He knew they were money, and he wanted some. He thought it would be easy to take some and buy candy and toys. He put some money in his pocket and closed the jar. Lily counted to ten and went to look for Ben. She looked under the desk, behind the curtains, and in the closet. She could not find him. She wondered where he was. She saw the chair by the shelf and thought maybe he was hiding there. She climbed on the chair and saw the jar. She opened the lid and peeked. She saw a lot of money, too. She was surprised and happy. She thought it would be easy to share some with Ben and buy candy and toys. She put some money in her pocket and closed the jar. Ben and Lily came out of their hiding spots and met in the middle of the office. They smiled at each other and said, "I found you!" They hugged and showed each other their pockets. They both had money. They both felt guilty and scared. They heard their dad coming up the stairs. They did not know what to do. They knew they had done something bad. They wished they had not peeked.

A boy named Billy wanted a flute. He had never played one before, but he really wanted one. Every day, Billy asked his mom for a flute, but his mom said no. One day, Billy saw a thin flute in the store. He asked his mom if he could have it, but she said no. Billy was so sad that he didn't get the flute. That night, Billy had a dream. In his dream, Billy saw a flute that was so thin, he could carry it anywhere. When he woke up, Billy smiled. He knew that his mom wouldn't say no to a flute that thin. So the next day, Billy asked his mom if he could have the thin flute. And this time, his mom said yes! Billy was so happy. At last, he had the flute he wanted so badly.
