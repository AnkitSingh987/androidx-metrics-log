Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Lily. She liked to play with her toys and her pet cat, Max. One sunny day, Lily found a long string in her room. She thought it was fun to play with. So, she tied one end of the string to Max's tail and the other end to her toy doll. She pretended that Max and the doll were getting married. Max and the doll walked down the hallway, with the string between them. Lily was very happy to see Max and the doll together. She clapped her hands and laughed. After the game, she gave Max a big hug and put the doll back on her bed. Lily knew that being kind and having fun made her and her friends happy.

Once there was a little girl called Mia. She was only three years old and she wanted a bicycle very much. So, one day Mia's mum and dad asked her to choose a bicycle in the shop. She looked around and saw three different bicycles - one was red, one was blue and one was yellow. Mia chose the red one with sparkly wheels. Mia was very excited and she wanted to ride her new red bicycle as soon as possible. But when she tried to ride it, Mia just couldn't do it. Every time she tried, she would fall over and get grumpy. Mia didn't understand why the bicycle was so difficult to ride. Then one day, before she tried it again, Mia's mum said to her, "Mia, riding a bicycle is difficult. You have to practice it a lot before you can do it." Mia listened to her mum and kept practicing riding her bicycle. Soon enough, she was riding it just like the other kids. Mia learned a very important lesson - with practice and hard work, anything can be achieved.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there lived a little mole. The mole had a big problem. He had a bad leg. He could not play with his friends. He was sad. One day, the mole met a kind bird. The bird saw the mole's bad leg. The bird said, "Do not be sad. I will help you. Let us pray together." The mole and the bird closed their eyes and prayed. After they prayed, the mole felt better. His bad leg was not so bad anymore. He could play with his friends again. The mole thanked the bird. They became good friends and played together every day.

Lily liked to play with her pillow. She pretended it was a big and soft animal that could talk to her. She named it Fluffy and gave it a voice. "Hello, Fluffy," Lily said one day. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, Lily," Fluffy said. "You are my best friend. What do you want to play?" Lily thought for a moment. She had an idea. "Let's play famous," she said. "We can pretend we are on TV and everyone likes us." "OK, Lily," Fluffy said. "How do we play famous?" Lily smiled. She got up and put on a hat and a scarf. She also found a pair of sunglasses and a necklace. She looked at herself in the mirror. She felt very fancy. "I am a famous singer," she said. "And you are my famous pillow. We sing songs together and make people happy." She picked up Fluffy and held him close. She started to sing a song she made up. It was about flowers and rainbows and stars. She sang very loud and very happy. Fluffy sang along with her. He made funny noises and words. He also sang very loud and very happy. Lily and Fluffy had a lot of fun playing famous. They did not care if anyone heard them or saw them. They only cared about each other. They were the best friends in the world.

Once upon a time, in a small farm, there was a grumpy mule named Momo. Momo did not like to play with the other animals. He just wanted to eat and sleep all day. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily came to visit the farm. She saw Momo and said, "Hello, Momo! Do you want to play with me?" Momo just looked at her and made a grumpy face. Lily did not give up. She walked up to Momo and gave him a big hug. Momo felt the warm hug and something changed inside him. He was not grumpy anymore. He started to play with Lily and the other animals on the farm. They all had a fun day together. From that day on, Momo was never grumpy again. All he needed was a hug from a friend.
