Title: Lucy and Max and the Duck

Once upon a time there were two friends, Lucy and Max. Lucy had a plastic toy duck that she loved and would take everywhere with her. One day, Max asked if he could play with it. "Yes," Lucy said happily. Max smiled and thanked her. Then he started playing with the duck and soon he was having lots of fun. As Max was playing, he accidentally dropped the duck into the mud. He was sad and quickly ran to Lucy. But when Lucy saw what had happened to her toy duck, she was still glad. "It's alright," she said. "Things get dirty sometimes, but that doesn't mean that you can't wash them up and make them look good again." Max was surprised. He cleaned up the duck and they both smiled. The moral of the story is that even when things go wrong, you should still be glad. With the right attitude and a little bit of work, you can often turn a bad situation into something good.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the market with his mom. They needed to buy a big, round squash for dinner. Tim liked to help his mom, so he held her hand as they walked to the market. At the market, Tim saw a famous dog. The dog could do tricks like jump and roll. Tim said, "Mom, look at the dog! He is so famous!" His mom said, "Yes, Tim, that dog is very good at tricks." Tim and his mom bought the squash and went home. They cooked the squash for dinner. Tim was happy that he saw the famous dog and helped his mom at the market.

Once upon a time, in a big valley, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia had a heavy ball that she liked to play with. Every day, she would kick the ball and run after it, laughing and having fun. One sunny day, while Mia was playing with her ball, she saw a dog. The dog was chewing on a bone. Mia walked up to the dog and said, "Hi, dog! Do you want to play with me and my ball?" The dog wagged its tail and barked, "Yes, Mia! I would love to play with you and your ball!" So Mia kicked the heavy ball, and the dog ran after it. They played together all day in the valley, having lots of fun. As the sun began to set, Mia and the dog were tired but happy. They sat down under a big tree and shared a snack. Mia gave the dog a new bone to chew, and they became the best of friends. From that day on, they played together in the valley every day, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was an ugly bug. The ugly bug had big wings and could fly. It liked to fly around the big tree in the park. One sunny day, the ugly bug flew very fast. While flying, the ugly bug saw a big mouth. The mouth belonged to a happy frog. The frog liked to eat bugs. The ugly bug was scared of the big mouth, so it flew away quickly. The ugly bug flew to a safe place near the flowers. It met a nice butterfly. The butterfly was pretty and could also fly. The ugly bug and the pretty butterfly became friends. They flew together and had lots of fun.

Once there was a little pig called Curly. He was so excited as today he had been invited to the amazing slide. All he wanted to do was just slide and slide! He was so happy. On the way to the slide Curly noticed a large plate of meat. He asked his mum, "Can I have some of that delicious meat?". His mum said no and explained if he had some he wouldn't be able to enjoy the slide. So, Curly kept walking and reached the slide. He looked up, saw the amazing slide and quickly hopped on. He just wanted to slide and slide! Curly was having so much fun on the slide, he didn't notice the plate of meat sitting next to the slide. He spent lots of time having the time of his life sliding up and down. The hours passed and Curly suddenly noticed it was dark outside. He suddenly remembered he hadn't had dinner yet. He was so hungry. But sadly, it was too late. Curly had spent all day on the slide and his mum had already gone home. He would have to wait until morning to have dinner.
