Title: Lily the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a friendly girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sun. Her favorite thing to do was to stand in her garden and look at the pretty flowers. One day, Lily saw a big red strawberry growing in her garden. She was so happy! She wanted to eat the strawberry, but it was too high for her to reach. Lily tried to jump, but she still could not get the strawberry. Just then, a friendly bird flew down and picked the strawberry for Lily. But instead of giving it to her, the bird ate the strawberry! Lily was sad, but then the bird did something unexpected. It dropped a tiny seed into Lily's hand. She planted the seed, and soon, many strawberries grew in her garden. Lily and the bird became good friends, and they shared the strawberries together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue was deaf, but she knew a special language. This language was not like talking. It was with her hands. Sue loved to talk with her hands. One day, Sue went to the park. She saw a little bird. The bird was cold. Sue wanted to help the bird. She found a soft thing to cover the bird. The bird was not cold anymore. Sue and the bird became friends. They played in the park every day. Sue talked to the bird with her hands. The bird learned Sue's special language. They were very happy together.

One day, a big black dog and a small cat met at the park. The dog had a red ball, and the cat had a blue toy mouse. They wanted to play with each other's toys, so they decided to make a trade. "Can you help me trade my red ball for your blue toy mouse?" asked the big black dog. The small cat said, "Yes, I can help you trade." As they were trading their toys, a big wind came and blew the red ball and blue toy mouse away. They both ran to get their toys back, but the wind was too strong. They tried and tried, but they could not catch their toys. In the end, the big black dog and the small cat were sad because they lost their toys. They learned that sometimes, it is better to be happy with what you have and not make trades.

Nola was walking in the park, enjoying the shade of the trees. She spotted a butterfly and smiled at its cheerful colours. Nola started chasing it and soon they were playing together. Nola: "Come let's count the spots!" The butterfly nodded and they both started counting. They counted up to ten, before the butterfly flew away. Nola waved goodbye and smiled with excitement. Nola: "Let's do it again tomorrow!" The next day, Nola was back in the park enjoying the shade of the trees. She spotted the same butterfly and ran towards it, with a cheerful face. Nola: "Look! It's you! Let's count the spots again!" The butterfly nodded and they started counting, laughing and talking as they did. When they reached ten, the butterfly flew away and Nola waved him goodbye. Nola: "See you soon!" And with that, Nola continued on her way, still smiling with joy and feeling refreshed by the shade of the trees.

One day, a little squirrel named Tim went out to find some nuts. The sun was just starting to rise in the sky, making everything bright and warm. Tim was very happy and excited to start his day. As Tim was looking for nuts, he heard a loud noise. "What was that?" he asked his friend, the bird. The bird said, "I don't know, but be careful!" Tim felt a little scared but kept looking for nuts, hoping the loud noise would go away. Finally, Tim found a big pile of nuts under a tree. He was so happy! But then, the loud noise came back. This time, it was a big dog barking! The dog wanted to play with Tim, but Tim was scared. He remembered what the bird said and quickly ran up the tree. The dog couldn't reach him there. Tim was safe and could enjoy his nuts. He learned that sometimes, it's good to listen to your friends.
