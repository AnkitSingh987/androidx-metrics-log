Title: Lily the Girl and the Tim

One day, a girl named Lily put on her perfect red dress. She went with her mom to the hospital to visit her sick friend, Tim. Lily was very happy to see Tim and wanted to make him feel better. At the hospital, Lily saw a big, blue ball. She picked it up and said, "Tim, let's play with this ball! It will be fun!" Tim smiled and said, "Okay, Lily!" They played with the ball in the hospital room, laughing and having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball hit a big red button on the wall. The room started to shake, and the lights went out. Lily and Tim were scared. The hospital was not the same anymore. They didn't know what to do. In the end, they could not play anymore, and the day was not perfect.

Nina was a polite three-year-old with a heart as light as ash. Every day, when her papa came home from work, she would run to him with her arms wide open and hug him tight. She loved to feel his warm arms wrap around her in the tightest of hugs. One day, Nina ran to hug her papa, but he stayed still with his arms crossed. “What’s wrong, Papa?” asked Nina sweetly. “Oh my sweet Nina, I’m too tired for a hug today,” he said with a sad smile. Nina was so polite that she knew she shouldn’t push it. She just smiled and said, “That’s ok Papa. I’ll hug you extra tight tomorrow.” Her papa smiled and said, “I’d like that!” That night, they snuggled together in bed and talked about their day. Nina was determined to hug Papa extra hard the next day. Finally, when Papa arrived home, Nina ran to him and hugged him as tight as she could. Papa hugged her back and smiled. “Thank you for the extra special hug, Nina,” he said. Nina giggled and said, “It was my pleasure, Papa.”

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play with trains. They have a big box of trains and tracks. They make a long subway in their room. The subway has many cars and stops. One day, Ben and Mia have a fight. They do not want to share their trains. They say mean words to each other. They break their subway in two. Ben takes his trains to one side of the room. Mia takes her trains to the other side. They do not talk or look at each other. Mom comes to see what is wrong. She sees the broken subway and the sad faces. She asks Ben and Mia why they are not playing together. They tell her they are mad at each other. Mom says that is not nice. She says friends should unite, not fight. She says they have more fun when they share and help each other. Ben and Mia think about what Mom says. They feel sorry for being mean. They say sorry to each other. They hug and smile. They decide to unite their trains and tracks again. They make a new subway that is bigger and better than before. They have fun playing with their subway. They are happy friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with a long string. The string was his favorite toy. He would run and jump, trying to catch the string. Fluffy was very happy. One day, Fluffy was playing with the string, and it disappeared! Fluffy looked everywhere for the string. He looked under the bed, behind the door, and even in his food bowl. But the string was gone. Fluffy was very sad and sorry. Fluffy's friend, a little dog named Spot, saw that Fluffy was sad. Spot wanted to help Fluffy find the string. They looked everywhere together. Finally, they found the string in a big box. Fluffy was so happy and thanked Spot for helping him. They played with the string together, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there lived a curious little girl called Liza. She was always asking questions, trying to make sense of the world around her. One day, she struck up a conversation with an old man at the local market. He was selling all sorts of mysterious things, and when Liza asked what the most mysterious thing he had for sale was, he replied: "Wisdom." Liza asked him where she could buy wisdom. The old man replied, "You can't buy wisdom, little one. Wisdom must be earned and found." Liza thought about this for a moment, and then asked, "So how do I find it?" The old man smiled and said, "Come back anytime, and I will tell you. For now, just keep your eyes, ears, and heart open to the mysteries around you!" Liza thanked the old man and went off in search of wisdom.
