Title: The Prince and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a prince. The prince was very sad because his days were dull. He wanted to go on an adventure. He wanted to rescue someone. One day, the prince saw a cat stuck in a tree. He thought, "I will rescue the cat!" So, the prince climbed the tree. He got the cat and climbed down. The cat was happy, and the prince was happy too. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat turned into a princess! She said, "Thank you for rescuing me, prince!" The prince and the princess became friends. They had many fun adventures together. The prince's days were not dull anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a camera that he loved to use. One day, he went to the park with his friend, Sue. They wanted to take pictures of the pretty flowers and big trees. As they walked, Tim saw a rude dog. The dog barked loudly at them. Tim said, "Sue, let's take a picture of the rude dog!" But when he looked for his camera, he realized he forgot it at home. "Oh no, I forgot my camera!" Tim said sadly. Just then, a kind cat came up to them. The cat had Tim's camera in her mouth! The cat had found the camera and brought it to Tim. Tim and Sue were very happy. They took pictures of the cat, the rude dog, and the pretty park. And they never forgot the kind cat who saved the day.

One day, a girl named Lily went to a big building with her mom. Inside the building, they saw a pink elevator. Lily loved the color pink, so she was very happy. She wanted to go up in the elevator with her mom. When they got inside the pink elevator, Lily saw a man who was writing on a paper. She asked the man what he was writing. The man said he was writing a letter to his friend. Lily thought it was fun to write letters. Lily and her mom went up in the pink elevator. They got out and did their work. When they were done, they went back to the pink elevator to go down. Lily said she wanted to write a letter too. Her mom said they could write one when they got home. Lily was excited to write a letter and ride the pink elevator again.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a girl named Anna. She had a big red cushion that she loved very much. Anna would hug her cushion every night before she went to sleep. One day, Anna's mom said, "Anna, I want to teach you how to make your bed." Anna was excited. She wanted to learn how to make her bed look nice and tidy like her mom's. Anna's mom showed her how to put the sheets on, and how to fold the blanket. Then, she showed Anna how to put the big red cushion on top. "Now your bed looks very tidy!" her mom said with a smile. Anna was so happy. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you for teaching me, Mommy!" From that day on, Anna made her bed every morning and put her big red cushion on top. She was very proud of her tidy bed.

One day, a gentle rabbit named Bob was very hungry. He wanted to make a salad. He hopped to the garden to find some yummy leaves. But when he got there, he saw a big bear named Tom. Tom said, "This is my garden. You can't take any leaves!" Bob was sad but he had an idea. "Can I work for you and help you in the garden? Then maybe I can have some leaves for my salad." Tom thought for a moment and said, "Okay, you can work for me. If you do a good job, you can have some leaves." Bob worked very hard in the garden. He pulled out the bad plants and watered the good ones. Tom was happy with Bob's work. "You did a good job, Bob. You can have some leaves for your salad," said Tom. Bob was very happy. He made a big salad and shared it with Tom. They became good friends and worked together in the garden every day.
