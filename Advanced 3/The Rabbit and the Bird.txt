Title: The Rabbit and the Bird

Once upon a time there was a rabbit. The rabbit had a box. He wanted to open it, but it was locked. He pulled and he shook the box, but nothing happened. He was very sad. But then a fairy came along. She waved her wand and said some magic words. The box opened, and inside was a beautiful bird. The bird flapped its wings and flew away. The rabbit was so happy. He smiled and he shouted. He ran after the bird, but it was too fast. Then he had an idea. He asked the fairy for help. The fairy waved her wand and said some more magic words. The bird turned around and flew back to the rabbit. It landed on the rabbit’s head and he released it. It flew away, free and easy. The rabbit was very happy. He smiled and waved goodbye to the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy car that she loved very much. One day, Sue's toy car was broken. She was very sad. Sue's mom said, "I know how to fix your car. We need a magic mint to make it all better." Sue knew she had to find the magic mint to rescue her toy car. Sue looked everywhere for the magic mint. She looked under her bed, in her toy box, and even in the garden. At last, she found the magic mint under a big tree. Sue took the magic mint to her mom. Her mom used the mint to fix the broken toy car. Sue was so happy to have her car back. She played with her car all day long, and from that day on, she always knew that the magic mint could rescue anything that was broken.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to draw with her color pencils. One day, she found a very important pencil. This pencil could make things vanish. Lily showed her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom! This pencil can make things vanish!" she said. Tom didn't believe her. "Show me," he said. Lily drew a big circle around a toy car, and it vanished! Tom was amazed. Lily and Tom played with the pencil, making things vanish. But they didn't think about what would happen when they made too many things vanish. Soon, their toys, books, and even their clothes were gone. "Oh no! What did we do?" cried Lily. Tom was very sad too. They had lost all their things because they didn't think before they acted. The moral of the story is: always think before you do something, or you might lose what is important to you.

One day, a kid went outside to play. They were so careless and ran everywhere. They ran down the street, around the trees and even into their neighbor's garden. They were having so much fun! But then, the kid stumbled and fell! They started to cry and their neighbor came out to see what was wrong. They saw the kid on the ground and helped them up. The neighbor asked the kid if they were OK and the kid nodded. The neighbor told the kid to be careful next time and that running was not allowed in the garden. The kid nodded again and thanked the neighbor before running off again. The kid was a bit more careful this time, but they still had lots of fun. Before they knew it, it was time to go inside. The kid said goodbye to their neighbor and ran inside. What a fun day!

Mama was in the kitchen cooking with her apron on. She was being careful not to make a mess. Her three year old daughter walked up to her and asked, "Mama, what are you doing?" Mama leaned over and said, "I'm cooking. I'm making you something special!" The daughter was so excited and she clapped her hands together. She said, "Yay! What are you making?" Mama smiled and leaned down to give her a hug. She said, "I'm making a surprise. Now remember to be careful around the kitchen so you don't get hurt. OK?" The daughter nodded and said, "OK Mama! I'll be careful!" She leaned over and watched what Mama was cooking, smiling the whole time.
