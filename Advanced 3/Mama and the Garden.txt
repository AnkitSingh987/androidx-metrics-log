Title: Mama and the Garden

Mama was cooking in the kitchen. Every now and again, she lifted her hand to her head and gave it a little scratch. "Oh, this is a terrible itch!" she said. "I must have gotten it from sitting too long in the hot garden". Suddenly, a loud creak came from the door. In ran a small bundle of energy. It was a 3 year old girl with bright eyes, it was Alice! She scurried over to Mama and said "What's wrong, Mama?" "I have an awful itch" said Mama. "Let me scatter it" said Alice. She smiled and grabbed her hands together. Suddenly, with a wave of her hands, she scattered the itch. Mama felt a wave of relief was over her. She gave Alice a big hug, and then said “Thank you Alice! The itch is gone". Alice beamed "Glad I could help Mama!" She scurried away, still feeling hot from the summer sunshine.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. She was three years old and lived with her mum. One day, Sarah wanted a special treat, so her mum allowed her to choose something to eat for herself. Sarah found a delicious looking plate of cookies on the kitchen table. She was very excited and reached out to grab one. "No, no, no," her mum said in a gentle voice. "Those are far too naughty for you. We can't allow that." Sarah hung her head in disappointment and trudged back to the table. She was almost in tears until her mum said, "I've got an idea. How about you have an apple instead? It's a healthy, yummy treat and it's not bad like those cookies." Sarah lit up and smiled. She ran to the fridge, pulled out an apple and gave it the biggest bite. It was delicious! Sarah was so happy she had been allowed to have such a special treat.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little yacht. The yacht lived in a big blue sea. It liked to float and play with the fish. One day, the quiet yacht saw a big storm coming. The wind blew hard and the waves got big. The yacht was scared. It wanted to find a safe place. The yacht started to move fast. It went to a small bay with trees and sand. The storm went away and the quiet yacht was happy again. It stayed in the bay, playing with the fish and enjoying the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Catty. Catty loved to dress up in soft clothes because she liked the texture. One day, Catty saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her favorite dress. Catty wanted to help Lily feel better. Catty said to Lily, "Why are you sad?" Lily said, "I lost my dress. It was my favorite one." Catty knew she could help. She ran home and found a dress with the same soft texture that she liked. Catty took the dress to Lily and said, "Here, try this dress. It is soft and nice." Lily tried the dress on and smiled. She said, "Thank you, Catty! I love this dress. It feels so soft." Catty was happy that she could help Lily. They became best friends, and they learned that sharing can make others happy. And that was the best feeling of all.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was very excited. She went to the store with her mom to buy a sausage. The sausage was for their dinner that night. Lucy could not wait to eat it! On the way home, Lucy saw her friend Tim. Tim had a big dog named Max. Max liked to play and jump. Lucy said, "Hi Tim! Look at the sausage we got for dinner!" Tim said, "Wow! Max loves sausage too!" Just then, Max saw the sausage and tried to take it from Lucy. But Tim was quick and held Max back. "No, Max!" Tim said. He wanted to prevent Max from eating Lucy's sausage. Lucy was happy that Tim stopped Max. She thanked Tim and went home with her mom. At dinner, Lucy and her family enjoyed the sausage. Lucy was still excited and told her family about Max trying to take it. They all laughed and had a good time.
