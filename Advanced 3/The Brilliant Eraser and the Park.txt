Lily was sad because she lost her toy. Max wanted to help. He said, "Don't worry, I will find your toy and keep it safe." Max wagged his tail and used his magic. Soon, he found Lily's toy under a bush. "Here is your toy!" said Max. Lily was so happy and thanked Max for his help. They became best friends and played together every day. From that day on, Max helped all his friends keep their things safe. Everyone loved Max and his magic tail. And Max and Lily lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a big, bossy cat named Tom found a soft, velvet pillow in the park. He loved the pillow so much that he decided to take it home. Tom carried the pillow in his mouth, feeling very proud. On his way home, Tom met a little, sad bird named Sue. Sue had lost her nest in the park and had nowhere to sleep. She saw Tom's velvet pillow and thought it looked very cozy. Sue asked Tom if she could share the pillow, but Tom said no. He wanted the pillow all to himself. As the sun began to set, Tom felt bad for Sue. He thought about how nice it would be to have a friend to share the pillow with. So, Tom went back to the park and found Sue. He told her they could share the velvet pillow. They both snuggled on the pillow and became the best of friends. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can make you happier and help you make new friends.
