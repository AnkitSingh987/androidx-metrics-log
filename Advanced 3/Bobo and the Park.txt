Title: Bobo and the Park

One day, a big, honest dog named Bobo heard a loud noise. The noise came from the park. Bobo wanted to find out what the noise was, so he started to hurry. As Bobo hurried to the park, he saw his friend, a small cat named Mimi. Mimi heard the noise too. They both hurried together to find the noise. When they got to the park, they saw a big party. There were kids, balloons, and cake. The noise was just happy kids playing. Bobo and Mimi were happy too. They joined the party and played with the kids all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a magic pen. The pen was mighty. It could talk to her. Mia loved her pen very much. One day, Mia and her pen went to the park. They saw a big tree. The pen said, "Let's draw a pretty picture on the tree!" Mia was happy. She used the pen to draw a big sun, a green tree, and a blue bird. The pen was very mighty. It made the picture come to life! The sun shined bright, the tree danced, and the bird sang a sweet song. Mia and her pen played all day in the park. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, Mia said, "Thank you, mighty pen, for the best day ever!"

One day there were two best friends, Sam and Susan, who had the same teacher. The teacher's name was Mrs. Smith and she told her classes to listen to her and to mind their manners. Sam and Susan were pupils in her class and they liked their teacher, but sometimes the lessons were very boring. One day, Mrs. Smith came in with a big box and told the class that there was a surprise inside! Sam and Susan were so excited to see what was in the box and Mrs. Smith said that if they listened to her and minded their behaviour, she would show them what was inside. Sam and Susan promised to listen and they tried so hard to focus and learn, but sometimes they were too curious to stay still. Then, one day, Mrs. Smith opened the box and let out two puppies! Sam and Susan were so happy, they couldn't believe it! They could now play with the puppies and Mrs. Smith said that if they minded their promise she would keep the puppies in the class everyday. Sam and Susan kept their promise and the puppies stayed. They played with them everyday and their lessons with Mrs. Smith were never boring again!

Once upon a time there was a popular present. Everyone was talking about it and wanted to get it. But this present was very special and only a few lucky people got it. One day, a little girl named Lilly asked her mum if she could have the popular present. Her Mum said yes and Lilly was so excited! When Lilly got her own present, she couldn't wait to do something fun with it. She played with it all day and it made her very happy. Lilly's friends soon heard about her present and they all wanted one too. All of Lilly's friends shared the present and they all did something fun with it. Lilly was so glad that she got the popular present, and she and her friends had lots of fun with it!

Once upon a time, in a wide place with lots of trees and flowers, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to study bugs and play with her dog, Max. They had lots of fun together, running and laughing. One day, Lily and Max met a talking bird named Chirpy. Chirpy said, "Hello, Lily and Max! I know a place where you can find lots of bugs to study." Lily was very happy and asked, "Where is this place, Chirpy?" Chirpy said, "Follow me, and I will show you." They followed Chirpy to a big tree. But when they got there, they found out that the bugs were really tiny fairies. The fairies said, "We will help you study, Lily, but you must be kind to us." Lily promised to be kind, and they all became good friends.
