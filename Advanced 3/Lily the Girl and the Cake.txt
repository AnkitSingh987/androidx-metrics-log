Title: Lily the Girl and the Cake

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to bake a cake. She asked her mom, "Can we bake a cake, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's bake a cake together!" They mixed flour, sugar, and eggs in a big bowl. Then, they put the cake in the oven. While they waited, Lily's mom said, "Let's make ice-cream to eat with our cake!" Lily was very happy and said, "Yes, I love ice-cream!" When the cake and ice-cream were ready, Lily and her mom sat down to eat. Lily took a big bite and said, "This is so yummy! Thank you, mom." Her mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I am grateful we can bake and eat together."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim did not like to hear stories. He would say, "I hate stories!" One day, his mom told him a new story. This story was about a big, dangerous lion. In the story, the dangerous lion lived in a big forest. Many animals were scared of the lion. They did not want to be near him. The lion was very sad because he had no friends. He did not want to be dangerous. One day, a small rabbit came to the lion. The rabbit said, "I am not scared of you. I want to be your friend." The lion was very happy. They played together and became best friends. The other animals saw this and were not scared anymore. Tim liked this story. He learned that even if something seems dangerous, it can still be good. Tim did not hate stories anymore. He wanted to hear more stories about the lion and his friends.

One day, a mighty big cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little dog named Tim crying under a tree. Tom asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim said, "I lost my toy and now I am all alone." Tom wanted to help and comfort Tim. He gave Tim a big hug and a rub on his head. They started looking for the toy together. They looked high and low, but they could not find it. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew a lot of leaves in the air. When the leaves fell down, there was the toy! Tim was so happy and thanked Tom. From that day on, Tom and Tim were best friends.

Mary and her brother were walking in the woods one day. Mary spotted an acorn that was resting on a soft patch of grass. She said to her brother, “Can we pick it up?” Her brother nodded his head yes and they both reached down. Once they had both grabbed the acorn, they had a big argument. Mary wanted to keep the acorn, while her brother wanted to use it to wash his hands in the river. Mary stamped her feet, yelling “no!” Her brother held the acorn tightly and said, “Please, little sister. I need to wash my hands.” Mary thought for a moment. She realized that her brother was right and so she gave the acorn to him. She said, “OK, take it and go wash your hands.” Her brother smiled and ran off to the river. Once her brother had walked away, Mary found another acorn. This one was even softer than the first one. She smiled and put it in her pocket, so she could keep it forever.

Once upon a time there was a small aeroplane. It flew in the sky and looked very pretty. The aeroplane wanted to count. He counted the clouds in the sky. One, two, three, four clouds! He was so happy he could count. The aeroplane flew over the house. He wanted to count the windows. One, two, windows! The aeroplane was very pleased. He flew over the park next. He wanted to count the trees. One, two, three, four trees! The aeroplane was so excited. The small aeroplane was so proud of himself for being able to count. He flew away, counting all the way home.

Timothy was a very small person. Every day he went to the park. One day, he was walking and something caught his eye. It was a very big rock. He touched it and it felt sour. "Eww, this rock feels sour," Timothy said. His mum said, "That's because it's starting to rot. Rocks can rot just like food can." "But rocks don't eat food," said Timothy. His mum laughed. "No, but over time, the rain and sun can make them rotten and sour." Timothy was very surprised. He touched the rock again, and it still felt sour. "Wow," he said. His mum smiled and said, "Look around, you can see a lot of sour things." Timothy looked around and saw that a lot of things in the park were beginning to rot. It was amazing to him that rocks could rot too.
