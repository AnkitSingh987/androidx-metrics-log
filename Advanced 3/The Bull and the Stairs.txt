Title: The Bull and the Stairs

Once upon a time there was a bull. He was foolish and often got into trouble. One day, the bull saw that everyone was celebrating. He wanted to join in too. The bull ran around in circles, kicking up dust. He made loud noises and jumped around playfully. Everyone laughed at his silly antics. The bull was so happy that he wanted to celebrate too. He ran to the kitchen and started eating all the food. Everyone cheered for him. The bull even learned some new tricks. He started spinning around and running up and down stairs. The bull was so silly that everyone laughed and celebrated even more. The bull was thrilled to be part of the celebration. He had a great time doing silly things and making everyone laugh. Everyone celebrated with the bull until the sun went down.

Lily was going to a wedding with her mom and dad. She wore a pretty dress and a hat. She was very excited to see the bride and the groom. Mom said they were her aunt and uncle. At the church, Lily saw the bride. She had a long white veil on her head. It looked like a cloud. Lily wanted to touch it. She walked up to the bride and said, "Hello, auntie. Can I touch your veil?" The bride smiled and bent down. She said, "Yes, you can, Lily. But be very careful. It is very delicate and special. It is a gift from your uncle." Lily nodded and reached out her hand. She touched the veil softly. It felt like silk. She said, "Wow, it is so pretty and soft. I like it." The bride said, "Thank you, Lily. You are very sweet and polite. Do you want to see my flowers? They are roses. They smell nice." Lily said, "Yes, please. I love flowers. Can I smell them?" The bride said, "Of course. But be careful not to prick yourself. They have thorns. They are sharp." Lily said, "OK. I will be careful." She smelled the roses. They smelled like perfume. She said, "They are so nice. Thank you, auntie. You are very beautiful." The bride said, "You are very welcome, Lily. You are very beautiful, too. Come, let's go to the altar. The groom is waiting for us. He is your uncle. He loves you very much." Lily said, "OK. Let's go. I love him, too." She took the bride's hand and walked with her. She was happy. She liked weddings.

Once upon a time, there was a kind and compassionate girl named Amy. Amy had a big box of matches. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. While playing, Amy met a boy named Tim. Tim wanted to play with Amy's matches. But Amy knew it was not safe. She tried to resist and told Tim, "No, we cannot play with matches. They can hurt us." Tim was upset and tried to take the matches from Amy. But Amy was brave and stood her ground. She said, "We must be careful and not play with things that can harm us." In the end, Tim understood and agreed with Amy. They decided to play with a ball instead. The moral of the story is to always be compassionate and resist things that can cause harm.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who had lots of lumber. He wanted to build a big house with it. He needed help to sort the lumber into small and big pieces. So, he asked his friend, Tom, to come and help him. Tom came to help the wealthy man. They started to sort the lumber together. The wealthy man said, "Tom, please put the small pieces here and the big pieces there." Tom nodded and they worked together. But, Tom was not careful. He dropped a big piece of lumber on the wealthy man's foot. The wealthy man cried, "Ouch! My foot!" He was very sad and angry. The big house was not built, and the wealthy man's foot hurt a lot. This was a bad ending for the wealthy man and Tom.

One day, a little kangaroo went to the park. He liked to jump and play there. The park was big and had many fun things to do. The little kangaroo was very happy. While playing, the kangaroo saw something on the ground. It was brown and looked disgusting. The kangaroo did not know what it was. He thought maybe it was food. The kangaroo ate the disgusting thing. It tasted very bad. The little kangaroo felt sick. He went home and did not play anymore. The kangaroo learned not to eat things from the ground.
