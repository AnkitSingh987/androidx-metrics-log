Title: Everywhere and the Monkey

Once, there was a middle-aged dragon who was feeling a bit bored. Everywhere he went, he saw the same things and did the same things. He wanted something new and exciting. His best friend, an adventurous monkey, recommended that he try to climb a new scale. The dragon was thrilled at the idea, so he decided to give it a go! He was nervous at first, but soon he realized how fun and exciting climbing the new scale was! As he reached the top, he admired the beautiful view and the sense of accomplishment he felt. The dragon thanked his adventurous monkey friend for the wonderful recommendation. He realized that trying something new can be rewarding and fun and he vowed to follow his friend's advice more often.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little dinosaur. The dinosaur was sad and gloomy because he had no friends to play with. He walked through the forest, looking for someone to be his friend. One day, the dinosaur saw a small bird sitting on a tree. The dinosaur said, "Hi, bird! Do you want to play with me?" The bird looked at the dinosaur and said, "Yes, let's play!" They played a game where they poked sticks into the ground to make funny shapes. As they played, the dinosaur started to feel happy. He was not gloomy anymore because he had a new friend. The dinosaur and the bird played together every day, and they became best friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play dress up with her toys. One day, she found a big piece of cardboard in her room. Sue had a fun idea. Sue used the cardboard to make a fierce mask for her teddy bear. She put the mask on the bear and they played together all day. Sue and her teddy bear had a great time being fierce and strong. But then, it was time for bed. That night, Sue had a dream. In her dream, she and her teddy bear went on a big adventure. They wore their fierce masks and saved the day. When Sue woke up, she was happy. She knew that her cardboard mask made her and her teddy bear brave.

Ben and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a big clock that made funny noises and a snowman that they had made from clay. They liked to pretend that the clock was a monster and the snowman was their friend. "Roar! I am the clock monster and I will eat you!" Ben said, making his voice scary. He moved the clock closer to the snowman. "No, no, please don't eat me!" Lily said, making her voice sad. She hugged the snowman and tried to protect it. "Too late! I am hungry and you are tasty!" Ben said, and touched the snowman with the clock. The snowman started to melt because the clock was warm. "Hey, stop it! You are ruining our snowman!" Lily said, and pushed the clock away. She saw that the snowman had lost its nose and eyes and was very soft. "I'm sorry, Lily. I was just playing. I didn't mean to melt our snowman." Ben said, and looked at the snowman. He felt bad for making Lily sad. "It's okay, Ben. We can make another snowman. But we have to be careful with the clock. It is hot and it can hurt us." Lily said, and smiled at Ben. She was not angry anymore. They put the clock on the table and took some more clay. They started to make a new snowman, but this time they gave it a hat and a scarf to keep it warm. They also looked at the window and saw that it was getting dark outside. "Look, Ben. It is dark now. We have to go to bed soon." Lily said, and pointed at the window. "Okay, Lily. But can we keep our snowman in our room? I don't want it to melt again." Ben said, and picked up the snowman. "Sure, Ben. We can keep our snowman in our room. But we have to be quiet and not wake up Mom and Dad." Lily said, and took the clock. They tiptoed to their room and put the snowman and the clock on their nightstand. They said good night to their toys and crawled into their beds. They closed their eyes and fell asleep, dreaming of snow and monsters.
