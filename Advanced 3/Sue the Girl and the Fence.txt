Title: Sue the Girl and the Fence

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived on a ranch with her mom and dad. Sue liked to play with her dog, Spot, and her cat, Fluffy. One day, Sue and her friends went to play near the big tree. They heard a loud noise. It was a big truck! The big truck made the ground shake. Sue, Spot, and Fluffy got scared. They ran back to the ranch, but the truck followed them. The truck was too close and hit the fence. The fence broke and the animals ran away. Sue, her mom, and her dad were very sad because they lost their animals.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to design things. He would draw and color all day. One day, Tom saw a filthy faucet in his house. It was very old and dirty. Tom wanted to make it pretty again. Tom told his mom, "Mom, can I design a new faucet?" His mom said, "Yes, Tom, you can try." So, Tom got his paper and crayons. He drew a beautiful faucet with bright colors. Tom showed his mom the design. She was very happy. Tom's mom took the design to a store. They made a new faucet just like Tom's drawing. They put the new faucet in their house. The old, filthy faucet was gone. Now, they had a pretty faucet that Tom designed. Tom and his mom were very proud.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant little star. This star had many friends on a big ball called a planet. They loved to play and have fun together. But one day, two of the friends had a quarrel. They were not happy with each other, and the little star was sad. The little star wanted to help its friends. It thought, "I will make them happy again." So, the little star shined its light very bright. The friends saw the light and it made them smile. They forgot about their quarrel and started to play again. The brilliant little star was so happy to see its friends playing together. The friends on the planet were happy too. They knew that the little star was watching over them. From that day on, they promised to be kind to each other and never quarrel again.

Once upon a time, there was a small rabbit who had a hurt foot. The rabbit couldn't play anymore because it was too painful to take a step. So the rabbit decided to take a nap. While the rabbit was napping, a mouse came and noticed the hurt foot. "What happened to your foot?' the mouse asked. "I hurt it very badly" the rabbit replied sadly. The mouse knew exactly what to do, he ran off and came back with a bandage. With the mouse's help, the rabbit put on the bandage and felt much better. After the bandage was on, the rabbit and the mouse had some time to play together, even though the rabbit was still weak. Through this the rabbit and the mouse became great friends. The moral of the story is that even when we feel weak, there is always something we can do to help other people - like staring a friend in need!

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to dive deep in the water. One day, while diving, he found a fake, shiny rock. Fin was very happy and wanted to show it to his friend, Sam the seahorse. Fin swam to Sam and said, "Look, I found a shiny rock!" Sam looked at the rock and said, "That's not real, it's a fake rock." Fin was sad but thought maybe he could find a real shiny rock. The next day, Fin went diving again. He swam deeper and deeper, and then he saw it - a real shiny rock! He picked it up with his little finger and swam back up to show Sam. When Fin showed Sam the real shiny rock, Sam was very happy for him. They laughed and played together, and Fin learned that sometimes you have to dive deep to find something real and special.
