Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was only three years old, but she was very adventurous. One day she decided to go on a journey. She walked through the woods and came across a small gas station. Lily went up to the owner and said cheerfully, "Hello! Can I have please have some gas?" The owner smiled and chuckled. "Well, little one, you're a bit too small to be buying gas. But I can give you some!" He gave her a small can of gas and some instructions. Lily thanked him and set off on her journey again. After walking for a while, she came across a tiny shed in the middle of the forest. She went inside the shed and found the most beautiful thing! It was a small toy car that seemed to be powered by the gas that the man had given her. Lily was so excited. She thanked the man in her head and continued her journey, pushing the toy car along with her.

Once upon a time there was a tall ant. He was very busy looking for food. He was busy rubbing his back legs together as he looked around for something to eat. He didn't find anything yet, but he kept looking. Then the ant heard a noise coming from a nearby tree. He hopped up onto the trunk and rubbed his antenna together. He saw a little caterpillar eating a leaf and he knew he was hungry too. The ant got closer and closer until he was right beside the caterpillar. He slowly reached out a leg and carefully rubbed the caterpillar's back. The caterpillar paused, looked over and smiled. The ant smiled back and asked if the caterpillar had any food to share. The caterpillar was so happy to share and so were both the ant and the caterpillar. Together they enjoyed their snack. The ant thanked the caterpillar and hopped away. He was so happy to have found food and he couldn't wait to tell all his friends. The end.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Ivy who wanted to be mighty. She wanted to be brave, strong and build value in the world. One day, Ivy was walking in the park when she found a big, round ball. It was rolling back and forth and Ivy got very excited. She ran to pick it up and said, "This must be a mighty ball!" Ivy started to roll the ball around with glee. Everywhere she went people stopped and watched. She even rolled it up a big hill without stopping. Everyone was so amazed by her mightiness as she kept rolling the ball with all her strength. She was so proud of herself and the value she had created. Soon a young boy appeared, and he declared, "I want to roll the ball too!" Ivy smiled and handed him the ball. They rolled the ball together around the park, laughing and having a great time. The end.

Lily liked to eat waffles for breakfast. She liked to put butter and honey on them. She also liked to help her mom make them. She would pour the mix into the waffle maker and wait for the beep. One morning, Lily wanted to surprise her mom with waffles. She got up early and went to the kitchen. She found the mix and the waffle maker. She poured some mix into the waffle maker and closed it. She did not wait for the beep. She poured more mix and more mix. The waffle maker was full of mix. It started to spill out and make a big mess. Lily did not know what to do. She ran to her mom's room and woke her up. "Mom, mom, I made waffles for you!" she said. "But they are very messy. Please come and see!" Her mom got up and followed Lily to the kitchen. She saw the waffle maker and the mix all over the counter and the floor. She was not happy. "Lily, what did you do?" she asked. "You made a big mess. You should not use the waffle maker by yourself. You could get hurt. And you wasted a lot of mix. Now we have no waffles for breakfast." Lily felt bad. She did not mean to make a mess. She wanted to make her mom happy. She started to cry. "I'm sorry, mom. I wanted to make you waffles. I love you, mom." Her mom hugged her and wiped her tears. "I love you too, Lily. It's okay. You tried to do something nice. But next time, please ask me first. And let's clean up this mess together. Maybe we can have some toast and jam instead."
