Title: Anna and Ben and the Bench

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a pair of sunglasses on a bench. They are shiny and pink. "Wow, look at these sunglasses!" Anna says. "They are so pretty. I want to wear them." "No, I want to wear them!" Ben says. "They are so cool. Give them to me." Anna and Ben start to pull the sunglasses. They do not want to share. They are being naughty. "Stop it, you two!" Mom says. She sees them fighting. She is angry. "Those sunglasses are not yours. They belong to someone else. You have to give them back." Anna and Ben feel sorry. They do not want to make Mom angry. They do not want to be naughty. They put the sunglasses back on the bench. "Sorry, Mom," Anna says. "Sorry, Mom," Ben says. Mom hugs them. She is happy. She says, "It's okay. I know you like the sunglasses. But you have to be nice and honest. Maybe we can buy you some sunglasses of your own. How about that?" Anna and Ben smile. They say, "Yes, please!" They go to the shop. They find some sunglasses that they like. Anna picks a pair of purple sunglasses. Ben picks a pair of blue sunglasses. They wear them and feel happy. They do not fight anymore. They are good twins.

Once upon a time, there was a little fox. The fox lived in a big forest. The forest had many trees and flowers. One day, the fox saw an interesting flower. The flower was big and pretty. The fox liked the flower very much. The fox watched the flower bloom. The flower grew bigger and bigger. The fox was very happy to see the flower bloom. The fox danced around the flower and played. Every day, the fox visited the interesting flower. The fox and the flower became best friends. The fox was happy, and the flower was happy too. They played together in the big forest forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She liked to play with her toys and have fun. One day, she found a big, expensive box in her room. She was very excited to see what was inside the box. Inside the box, Lucy found a shiny stone. She decided to rub the stone to see if something would happen. As she rubbed the stone, a lot of ash came out of it. The ash was gray and soft, and Lucy liked to touch it. Lucy played with the stone and ash all day long. She made shapes with the ash and had a great time. At the end of the day, she put the stone back in the expensive box and went to sleep, dreaming of more fun days to come.

Once upon a time, there was a little dolphin who lived in the big sea. The dolphin was upset because it could not escape from a big net. It wanted to swim and play with its friends. One day, a big fish saw the upset dolphin. The big fish wanted to help the dolphin escape. The big fish swam very fast and made a big hole in the net. The dolphin was so happy it could now swim and play with its friends again. But then, something unexpected happened. The big fish was really a magic fish! The magic fish turned the dolphin into a big bird. Now the dolphin could not only swim in the sea but also fly in the sky. The dolphin was not upset anymore and loved its new life as a bird.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a poor girl named Lily. She loved to draw with her pastel crayons. One day, Lily was drawing a big, happy sun when her yellow pastel broke. "Oh no!" said Lily, "My pastel is broken. How can I fix it?" She tried to put the pieces together, but it didn't work. Lily's friend, Tim, saw her trying to fix the pastel. He said, "Lily, don't worry. I can help you." Tim took some tape and put it around the broken pastel. Now, the pastel was fixed. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Together, they finished drawing the big, happy sun. And from that day on, they always helped each other when something was broken.
