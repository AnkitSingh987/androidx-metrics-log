Title: Sue the Helpful Girl and the Cake

One day, a helpful girl named Sue wanted to bake a cake. She put on her apron and went to the kitchen. Sue asked her mom, "Can you help me bake a cake?" Her mom said, "Yes, I can help you." Sue and her mom got all the things they needed to make the cake. They mixed everything in a big bowl. Then, they put the mix in a round pan. Sue's mom put the pan in the hot oven to bake. After some time, the cake was ready. Sue's mom took the cake out of the hot oven. They let it cool down. Then, they put icing on the cake. Sue and her mom were happy. They shared the cake with everyone in the family.

Once there was a little girl named Rose. Rose was three years old and she loved going outside to play. One day, Rose went out for a walk. When she came upon a field, she discovered something disgusting. In the middle of the field was an enormous insect! Rose cried, "Oh no! That's so awful!" Just then, her mom came running over to her. She put her arm around Rose and said, "It's alright, Sweetie. Don't worry, it won't hurt you." But Rose was still afraid of the disgusting insect. She kept on crying until her mom picked her up and carried her away from the field. Once they were a safe distance away, Rose finally stopped crying. Her mom smiled and hugged her, and they went home happy.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy loved cake more than anything else. One day, she heard that a new cake shop would arrive in her town. She was very happy and could not wait to try the yummy cake. The day the cake shop arrived, Amy and her friends went there. They saw many different cakes. There were big cakes and small cakes, round cakes and square cakes. Amy chose a beautiful cake with pink and white frosting. She was so excited to eat it. After eating the delicious cake, Amy and her friends felt very happy. They played in the park and laughed together. The peaceful town was filled with joy and laughter because of the new cake shop. And from that day on, Amy and her friends always had a good time eating cake together.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Lucy. Lucy loved to dance to jazz music. She would move her tail and paws to the beat. Her friends, a dog and a bird, liked to watch her dance. They thought she was the best dancer they had ever seen. One day, Lucy and her friends heard about a big party happening in the park. They were so excited to go and celebrate. They knew there would be music and dancing. Lucy was very happy and couldn't wait to show everyone her jazz dance moves. When they got to the party, Lucy started to dance to the jazz music. Her friends clapped and cheered for her. Everyone at the party watched Lucy dance and thought she was amazing. Lucy was so happy to share her dancing with her friends and celebrate with them. They all had a great time at the party and danced the night away.

Once upon a time, in a small town, winter came. Snow fell from the sky and covered the ground. It was very cold. The people in the town wore warm clothes and played in the snow. They made snowmen and had fun. One day, a little girl named Lily did not understand why her snowman was upset. She asked her mom, "Why is my snowman upset?" Her mom said, "Snowmen can't be upset, dear. They are just made of snow." Lily thought and thought. She wanted to make her snowman happy. She found a big, warm hat and put it on her snowman's head. She said, "Now you are not cold, Mr. Snowman." The snowman looked happy now, and Lily was happy too. They played in the snow all winter long.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient tap. One day a little girl went to visit it. The girl squatted down and looked at the old tap. She put out her hand and pressed it with her fingertips. “Tap, tap,” she said. Then she looked up at the sky and thought to herself, “What can this old tap do? It looks so old and tired.” Suddenly, an funny old man popped up. “The ancient tap, young lady?” he said. The girl nodded. “Well, I just think it might be able to give us water one day,” said the old man. “Really?” said the girl. “Yes, indeed,” said the old man. “It all depends on what you believe.” The girl smiled and thought to herself, “This ancient tap could be amazing one day.” She thanked the old man and then she skipped away, feeling very excited.
