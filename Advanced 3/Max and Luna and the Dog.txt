Title: Max and Luna and the Dog

One day, a dog named Max went for a walk with his friend, a cat named Luna. They walked past lots of traffic, with big cars and loud noises. Luna was scared, but Max said, "It's fine, I am here with you." As they walked, Max found a big bone. He was so happy! But, Luna wanted the bone too. They both wanted to keep the bone. Max said, "Let's bury the bone and come back later to see who can find it first." They dug a hole and put the bone in it. Then, they covered the hole with dirt. They went home and waited for the next day. The next morning, they went back to the spot where they buried the bone. They both started digging, and dirt flew everywhere. Finally, Max found the bone. Luna was sad, but Max said, "We can share the bone. It's fine. We are friends." Luna was happy, and they both played with the bone together. They learned that sharing was more fun than fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a big, enormous tree. It was so big that it touched the sky. Two little birds lived in the tree. One day, the birds had a quarrel. "Why is your waist so big?" asked the first bird. "Because I eat a lot," said the second bird. "Well, I will eat more too, and then my waist will be big like yours!" said the first bird. The first bird started to eat a lot, and soon, its waist became big. The two birds were happy, and they stopped their quarrel. They knew that their big waists were a sign of being strong and healthy. And they lived happily ever after in their enormous tree.

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a baby. The baby was very curious and always wanted to explore. The mommy warned the baby, "You must behave or else something bad will happen!" But the baby would not listen. One night, the baby tried to reach the mysterious moon. Suddenly he heard mommy's voice again, "I said you must behave!" But it was too late. The baby tumbled out of his crib and just barely scraped his knee. He was sad for a few moments and then he had an idea. Very carefully, he crawled back into bed and waited. Soon, he felt something magical brushing his cheek - a shiny silver light! He looked out the window and saw a mysterious moon. The baby smiled and repeated mommy's words softly, "I'm sorry I didn't behave. I will listen next time." He snuggled up close to his blanket and looked at the moon. He knew he had been lucky and he would always try to be good.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing with her toys. She was very happy. But then, she saw a big glass on the table. It was close to the edge. She felt anxious. Mia called her mommy, "Mommy, the glass is close to the edge! It might fall!" Her mommy came and moved the glass away from the edge. She said, "Thank you, Mia. You are a good helper." But then, Mia's little brother, Tim, came running. He bumped into the table and the glass fell. It broke on the floor. Tim started to cry. Mia felt sad too. Mommy said, "It's okay. We can clean it up." Mia and her mommy cleaned the broken glass together. Tim stopped crying. They all hugged and felt happy again.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Sally and Adam. They were walking in the park near their homes one day when they found a strange door. It was made of wood and was painted blue. Sally wanted to open it, but Adam said it was foolish. He explained that it was an underground door and it was not safe to open. But Sally was determined and opened it. They went through the door and found a room with a large pile of paper. Sally said, “We can fold this paper into pretty shapes. It will be so much fun!” Adam thought it was a good idea, so they got to work folding the paper. They ended up making a beautiful paper airplane. Sally and Adam had a lot of fun and learned a valuable lesson about being careful and not being too foolish.
