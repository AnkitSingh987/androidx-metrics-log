Title: The Fierce Tornado and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a fierce tornado. It was big and strong. It went around the town, making a lot of mess. People were scared and stayed inside their homes. One day, the tornado saw a little boy. The boy was not scared. He walked up to the tornado and said, "Please stop making a mess. My friends and I want to play outside." The tornado felt bad and stopped spinning. The boy and the tornado became friends. The boy and the tornado played together every day. They had so much fun. But one day, the boy had to move away. The tornado was sad. It wanted to end the fun times with a big surprise. So, the tornado cleaned up all the mess it made in the town. The people were happy, and they said thank you to the tornado. The tornado knew it was time to say goodbye to the boy. They hugged, and the tornado went away. The town was safe and clean again, and the boy never forgot his special friend, the fierce tornado.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a powerful bird named Bob. Bob loved to soar high in the sky. Every day, he would fly and play with his friends. One day, while Bob was flying, he saw a big, round squash on the ground. He wanted to show the squash to his friends. So, Bob tried to pick it up with his beak. But, the squash was too heavy for him. Bob did not give up. He called his friends to help him. Together, they all tried to lift the squash. With their powerful wings, they made the squash soar high in the sky. They were so happy that they could do it together. From that day on, they played with the squash and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy dog named Spot. One day, Tim and Spot went to the park to play. While they were playing, they found a smelly sock. Tim picked it up and said, "Ew, this is smelly!" Tim wanted to show his friend, Sally, the smelly sock. He thought it was funny. So, he asked his mom to help him send an image of the sock to Sally. His mom took a picture of the smelly sock with her phone and sent it to Sally's mom. Later, Sally saw the image of the smelly sock and laughed. She said, "That's a very smelly sock, Tim!" Tim and Sally had fun talking about the smelly sock. They were happy that they could share funny things with each other, even when they were not together.

Once upon a time, in a little stable, there lived an incredible horse named Star. Star was big and strong, and he loved to run and play. Every day, he would race with his friends in the big, green field. One day, Star and his friends found many colorful balls in the field. They decided to have some fun and sort the balls by their colors. Star was very good at sorting, and he helped his friends put the red balls with the red ones, the blue balls with the blue ones, and the yellow balls with the yellow ones. When they finished sorting the balls, the stable looked so pretty with all the colorful balls. Star and his friends were very happy. They played and laughed together all day long. And from that day on, the incredible Star and his friends had even more fun in their beautiful stable.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful land, there was a little hut. In the hut lived a kind man named Tom and his small cat, Mimi. They were very happy together. One day, Tom found a magic rock. He showed it to Mimi and said, "Look, Mimi! This rock can make things shrink!" Mimi looked at the rock and felt scared. Tom wanted to try the rock, so he pointed it at a big tree. The tree became very small. Tom thought it was fun, but Mimi did not like it. She said, "Tom, please stop. It is not good." But Tom did not listen. He pointed the rock at more things and made them shrink. Soon, the peaceful land was not peaceful anymore. Everything was very small and the people were sad. Tom felt bad and tried to make things big again, but he could not. The magic rock was gone. Tom and Mimi went back to their hut, but they were not happy anymore.
