Title: The Tidy Room and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room. In the room, there was a calendar on the wall. The calendar had pretty pictures on it. A little girl named Lucy lived in this room. She loved her room and took good care of it. One day, Lucy's friend, Tom, came to play. They played with toys and had a lot of fun. But soon, the room was not tidy anymore. Toys were everywhere. Lucy looked at her calendar and saw it was time to clean up. Lucy said to Tom, "Let's care for my room and make it tidy again." Tom agreed and they picked up all the toys. They put them back where they belonged. When they were done, the room was tidy again. Lucy and Tom looked at the calendar and smiled. They were happy to have a clean room to play in.

Once upon a time, there was a warm, cozy nest with a big, happy bird family. The mommy bird had a beautiful feather that was very special to her. She always kept it close. One day, the mommy bird said to the baby birds, "I have to go get food. Do not touch my special feather while I am gone." The baby birds nodded and promised not to touch the feather. But when mommy bird left, the baby birds could not resist. They took the feather out to play. They did not know that playing with the feather would spoil it. They played and played until the feather was dirty and broken. When mommy bird came back, she saw the spoiled feather and was very sad. The baby birds felt bad and said, "We are sorry, mommy. We should have listened to you." Mommy bird hugged her babies and said, "It's okay. We can find a new special feather together." From that day on, the baby birds learned to listen to their mommy and not to spoil things that were special. They found a new feather and took good care of it, and the bird family lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to play outside. It was very cold and she started to shiver. Sue saw a big, ugly bug. The bug was shivering too. She felt sad for the bug and wanted to help it. Sue said to the bug, "You look cold. Let's make you some clothes so you won't shiver." The bug was happy and said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue took some leaves and made a tiny dress for the bug. She put it on the bug and they both smiled. Now the bug was warm and not shivering anymore. Sue and the bug played together all day. They showed their friends the new fashion dress for the bug. Everyone thought it was a great idea. The ugly bug was now warm and happy with Sue.

Once upon a time, there was a famous car named Speedy. Speedy was a very fast car. Everyone in the town loved Speedy because he could speed down the road faster than any other car. One day, Speedy went to a class with other cars to learn new things. The class was very fun. They learned how to go slow, how to go fast, and how to be safe on the road. Speedy liked the class and made many new friends. After the class, Speedy and his new friends went to the park. They played together and had lots of fun. They raced each other and Speedy won because he was the fastest car. Everyone cheered for Speedy, and they all had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play dress up. She had a big blue dress that she liked the most. The blue dress was so pretty and made her feel like a princess. One day, Sue decided to act like a princess while wearing her blue dress. She put on the dress and went outside to play. She walked around her yard, waving to the flowers and the birds. Sue felt so happy and special. Soon, her friends saw her in the blue dress and wanted to play too. They all put on their own pretty dresses and acted like princesses together. They had a fun tea party and danced in the sun. Sue and her friends had a great day, and they all loved playing dress up and acting like princesses in their pretty dresses.
