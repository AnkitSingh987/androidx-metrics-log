Title: Sally the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sally. She was only three years old and loved to play with her friends in the park. One day, Sally was out playing in the park with her friends when she stumbled upon a shiny red cap on the ground. She asked her friends, "What is this?" Rebecca said, “It looks like a cap. Maybe it is lucky.” Sally pointed at the cap and said, “I want to keep it!” Rebecca smiled and said, “If you think it is lucky, then you should keep it.” So Sally picked up the shiny red cap and put it on her head. It fit perfectly! She smiled, feeling very lucky to have found such a special cap in the park. From then on, Sally always wore her new red luck cap. She was sure it would bring her good luck!

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lena. She was very brave and adventurous. One day, Lena wanted to go swimming in a lake near her house. Lena went to the lake and stepped in. But the water was too deep! "Help me!" Lena screamed. Suddenly, a kind old man appeared. He said, "Don't be helpless, little one. I can help you. I will hold your hand, and we can complete the swim together." Lena was so relieved. With the old man's help, she was able to swim in the lake. Lena was so happy that she jumped out of the water and hugged the old man. It was only then that Lena realized that the old man was actually a magical being. He had been watching over her and foreshadowing the complete swim in the lake.

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there was a little squirrel named Squeaky. Squeaky loved to eat oats. Every day, he would find oats and eat them all up. One day, Squeaky met a small bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was sad and said, "I am hungry and I cannot find any food." Squeaky wanted to help Chirpy, so he said, "I can share my oats with you." Chirpy and Squeaky ate the oats together. Chirpy was happy and said, "Thank you, Squeaky! You are a good friend." But then, they heard a loud noise. It was a big bear named Grumpy. Grumpy was not happy. He started to complain, "I am so hungry and there are no oats left for me." Squeaky and Chirpy felt bad for Grumpy. They decided to help him find more oats. They all looked and looked. Finally, they found a big pile of oats. Grumpy ate the oats and felt much better. Now, Squeaky, Chirpy, and Grumpy were all happy. They became good friends and always shared their food with each other. And they never complained again.

One day, a long cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little bird named Tim. Tom said, "Hi, Tim!" and gave him a wink. Tim was happy to see Tom and they played together. Tom and Tim found a big box. They did not know what was inside. They wanted to send the box to their friend, Sue the dog. They thought Sue would like the surprise. When Sue got the box, she opened it and out jumped a funny frog! The frog said, "Boo!" and Sue laughed. The frog was a new friend for them all. The long cat, the little bird, and the dog played with the frog and they all had a great day.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in her garden. She saw a red thing on the ground. She did not recognize it at first. Then, she looked closer and saw it was a strawberry. Lily was happy and picked it up. Lily saw her friend, Tim, who looked troubled. Tim was sad because he could not find any strawberries. Lily wanted to help her friend. She showed him the strawberry she found and told him to look closer at the ground. Tim and Lily looked at the ground together. Soon, they found more strawberries. Tim was not troubled anymore. They picked all the strawberries and shared them. Tim and Lily were very happy and ate the strawberries together.
