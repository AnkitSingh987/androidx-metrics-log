Title: The King and the Crown

Once upon a time, there was a King. He had a very special Crown, which he wore at every occasion. He was very proud of it. Everywhere he went, people would bow and say "Hello King!" and he would rub his Crown and feel very happy. One day, a wicked wizard appeared in the Kingdom. He was so clever, everyone started paying attention to him instead of the King. He was so popular! The people began to bow and say 'Hello Wizard!" everytime he passed. The King was not happy and was feeling jealous of all the attention the wizard was getting. The wizard was much cleverer and seemed to have all the answers to the people's problems. The King was so jealous, he took off his Crown and threw it away! That night, he had a terrible dream. In his dream, the wizard was wearing the Crown! And the people were all bowing to him instead of the King! The King awoke in a fright and realised how wrong it was to be so jealous. He ran to find his Crown, and when he put it back on, he knew it was a reminder to be humble, and not to be jealous of other people's success. That is why it's important to show respect for everyone, even the popular and the clever!

Once upon a time, there were two friends, David and Genie. David always rushed around, wanting to do a million things in a day. His nosy friend Genie, was always asking him to slow down and take a break. One day, David was walking quickly along the main street when he noticed a big billboard with Genie's face plastered all over it. David stopped in his tracks and exclaimed, "Genie! What are you doing there?" Genie laughed and replied, "I'm running for mayor! I'm hoping to help make our town the best place to live." But David was a bit angry. He said, "How could you do this without telling me? You always rush around and you're nosy too. You never have time for me!" Genie was surprised of David's reaction and tried to explain, "David, I'm sorry I didn't tell you my plan in advance. I was excited and just wanted to take a chance. Friends can still hang out, no matter how busy life gets. That's why we are best friends." David suddenly realized that Genie was only trying to help the town and agreed to still be friends. From then on, the two friends decided to slow down and enjoy the moment, no matter what.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom and his little brother, Tim. They lived in a house with their mom and dad. Tom did not like to accept when Tim wanted to play with him. Tom would say, "No, Tim. You are too little!" One day, Tom and Tim went to the park. The sun was hot and harsh. Tim wanted to play on the slide, but Tom said, "No, Tim. I am going to play on the swings." Tim was sad, but he went to play on the slide by himself. As Tom played on the swings, he saw Tim on the slide. But Tim was stuck and scared! Tom knew he had to help his brother. He ran to the slide and said, "I am sorry, Tim. I should have played with you." But it was too late. Tim fell from the slide and got hurt. Tom felt bad and wished he had accepted to play with Tim from the start.

One day, a huge wolf went for a walk in the woods. He heard a whistle sound. The sound was coming from a little bird. The bird was sad because it lost its family. The huge wolf wanted to help the little bird. They looked for the bird's family together. But the bird's whistle made the huge wolf feel bad. The sound hurt his ears. The huge wolf tried to stop the bird from whistling. They started to fight. The bird kept whistling, and the wolf got very mad. The bird flew away, and the wolf was all alone. The bird never found its family, and the wolf stayed sad in the woods.
