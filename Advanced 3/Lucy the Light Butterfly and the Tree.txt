Title: Lucy the Light Butterfly and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a light butterfly named Lucy. Lucy loved to fly in the sun and play with her friends. One day, in the month of march, Lucy and her friends went to the big tree. As they played near the tree, Lucy saw a little bug stuck in a spider's web. The little bug was sad and scared. Lucy wanted to be a hero and save the little bug. So, she flew to the web and tried to help the bug. But, surprise! The little bug was not stuck at all. It was a tricky spider in disguise. The spider wanted to catch Lucy and her friends. But Lucy was smart and fast. She flew away with her friends, and they were all safe. They learned to be careful and watch out for tricky spiders.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man. He had a little pony. The pony was brown and liked to play. The bald man liked to cook. He cooked yummy food every day. One day, the bald man wanted to cook a big meal. He asked his pony to help him. The pony was happy to help. They went to the store to buy food. They bought apples, carrots, and potatoes. They went home and started to cook. The bald man and the pony cooked a big pot of soup. They put the apples, carrots, and potatoes in the pot. The soup was very yummy. The bald man and the pony ate the soup and smiled. They had a great day cooking together.

Once upon a time, there was a fast bunny named Bob. Bob liked to run and play in the big, green field. One day, Bob found a shelter in the woods. The shelter was made of sticks and leaves. Bob thought it was a nice place to rest. Inside the shelter, Bob met a little bird named Tina. Tina was very friendly. "Hi, Bob!" said Tina. "I made this shelter. Do you like it?" Bob said, "Yes, it's very nice! You did a great job!" Tina was happy to hear that. One day, as they were playing, they heard a loud noise. A big tree was falling towards their shelter! Bob and Tina were scared. But then, something unexpected happened. A strong wind blew the tree away from their shelter. It was saved! Bob and Tina were very thankful and continued to play and display their friendship in the shelter every day.

Once upon a time, there was a brave child named Joe who lived on a beach. One day, the sky turned dark and the winds began to blow hard. Joe knew it was a hurricane. “I’m scared,” Joe said as he hid inside his house. Joe’s dad looked outside and said, “Don’t worry, Joe. We’re safe here.” Suddenly, a large wave shot across the beach. Joe was scared, but he wanted to be brave. He grabbed his dad’s hand and said, “Let’s go outside!” Joe and his dad went outside, and they watched the huge waves shooting across the beach. Joe and his dad stood in the wind, brave and strong. The hurricane eventually passed by and the sun came back out. Joe smiled and said, “That was fun!”

Once there was a little girl named Anne. She was only three years old, but she was very curious. One day she wanted to explore the neighbourhood and went for a walk. She was wearing a bright and purple dress and felt proud of it. Anne came across an old lady's house and noticed her garden was full of beautiful flowers. She thought it would be a nice idea to pick one. She chose a purple flower and quickly picked it. Anne suddenly heard a noise. She looked around, but it was too late. The old lady had seen her! She explained that the flowers in her garden were special and that picking one had made her very sad. Anne knew she had made a mistake. She apologised, gave the flower back and promised never to do it again. The old lady was very pleased and offered Anne a meal as a sign of forgiveness. Anne learned a valuable lesson that day - it's never wise to take something which doesn't belong to you. Losing something may seem like a small thing, but it can cause someone else to lose a lot.
