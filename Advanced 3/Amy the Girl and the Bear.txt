Title: Amy the Girl and the Bear

One day, a little girl named Amy went to her room to play. She had a lot of toys, but her favorite toy was a big, soft bear. She loved to hug the bear every day. Amy's little brother, Tim, saw her hugging the bear and wanted to play too. Amy did not want to share her bear with Tim, so she hid it under her bed. Tim looked sad and walked away. Amy felt guilty because she did not share her favorite toy with her brother. She wanted to make him happy again. Amy went to find Tim and saw him in the living room. He was playing with a new toy, a big, green dinosaur! Amy was surprised and happy for Tim. She ran to him and gave him a big hug. They both played together, sharing their toys and laughing.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big red van outside his house. Tim was a thoughtful boy, always looking and thinking about things around him. He liked to watch the cars and vans go by his house. Tim saw a man get out of the van. The man said, "Hi Tim! I have a magic trick to show you. Watch this toy car." Tim watched as the man held the toy car in his hand. With a wave, the car seemed to vanish! Tim was amazed and asked, "Where did it go?" The man smiled and said, "It's magic, Tim! I made the car vanish. Do you want to try?" Tim nodded, excited to learn the magic trick. The man showed Tim how to wave his hand and say the magic words. Tim tried it, and the toy car appeared again. Tim and the man played with the magic trick for a while. Tim's mom called him inside for lunch. The man waved goodbye and got back in his big red van. Tim watched as the van drove away, still thinking about the magic trick he had learned.

Once upon a time, there was a big table. It was a weird table because it had funny legs. The table lived in a house with a nice family. One day, the family wanted to give the table a gift. They found a big, soft cloth to wrap around the table. The table was very happy with its new cloth. The family and the table had a big party. They laughed, played, and ate yummy food. The table was happy to be wrapped in its new cloth and be with its family. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree with his family. One day, Tim wanted to print a picture of his home, the big tree. He asked his friend, the squirrel, for help. "Can you help me print a picture of our tree?" Tim asked. The squirrel nodded and helped Tim print the picture. They used leaves and berries to make the picture pretty. When they finished, Tim was very happy with their work. But while they were busy, a cat saw them and wanted to catch Tim. Tim saw the cat and knew he had to escape. He quickly flew away and hid in the tree. The cat tried to climb the tree but couldn't reach him. Tim was safe. Tim thanked the squirrel for helping him print the picture and escape from the cat. They both learned that working together and being brave can help you face any danger. And from that day on, Tim and the squirrel were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toys and talk to her friends. One day, she saw a telephone on the table. She wanted to touch it, but her mom said, "No, Amy, that's naughty." Amy really wanted to touch the telephone, so she waited for her mom to go into another room. When her mom left, she reached up and touched the telephone. It was very big and made a funny noise. She felt naughty, but she liked it. Later, her mom came back into the room and saw Amy with the telephone. She said, "Amy, I told you not to touch the telephone. That's naughty." Amy felt sad and said sorry to her mom. Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, just remember to listen next time." Amy promised to be a good girl and not touch the telephone again.
