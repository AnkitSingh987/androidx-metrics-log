Title: Lily and the Mom

Lily liked to help her mom at the office. She would bring her papers, pens and snacks. She wanted to support her mom, who worked hard every day. Sometimes, her mom would let her draw on the whiteboard or play with the phone. One day, Lily saw a big red button on the wall. It looked shiny and fun. She wondered what it did. She asked her mom, but her mom was busy talking to someone on the phone. She said, "Not now, Lily. That button is very important. Don't touch it, okay?" Lily nodded, but she was curious. She waited until her mom was not looking. She climbed on a chair and reached for the button. She pressed it with her finger. She heard a loud siren and saw flashing lights. She felt scared and excited. Her mom turned around and saw what Lily had done. She gasped and dropped the phone. She ran to Lily and pulled her off the chair. She said, "Lily, what have you done? That button is the fire alarm! You can't press it unless there is a real fire! Now everyone has to leave the office and the firemen will come!" Lily started to cry. She did not know she had done something bad. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I just wanted to see what it did. I wanted to help you." Her mom hugged her and said, "I know, Lily. But you have to listen to me when I tell you something. You can't touch things that are not yours. You have to be careful and follow the rules. You can't help me if you cause trouble." They left the office with the other people. They saw the firemen arrive with their trucks and hoses. They heard the sirens and saw the smoke. Lily felt ashamed and sad. She had made a big mistake. She had not supported her mom. She had made her work harder. She wished she had never seen the button.

Once upon a time, in a big nation, there was a dependable tree. The tree was very big and strong. Many people and animals liked the tree because it gave them shade and a place to play. One day, a little boy named Tim was playing near the tree. He loved to climb and swing from its branches. Suddenly, the tree started to shake. Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. He held on tight to the tree. The shaking stopped, and Tim looked around. To his surprise, the tree had turned into a giant swing set! The dependable tree had become a fun place for all the kids in the nation to play. Everyone was so happy and excited. And from that day on, the tree was known as the magical shaking tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty had a big dream. She wanted to go on a fun trip to the park. Kitty thought about the park every day and night. She dreamed of playing with the birds and the bugs. One sunny day, Kitty saw a little red wagon with a handle. She jumped in the wagon and waited. Soon, a friendly dog named Spot came by. Spot saw Kitty in the wagon and asked, "Do you want to go to the park?" Kitty said, "Yes, please!" Spot grabbed the handle and pulled the wagon. They went to the park together. Kitty and Spot played with the birds and the bugs. They had a great time. Kitty's dream came true. From that day on, Kitty and Spot were best friends. They went to the park every day to play and have fun.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was three years old and he loved to play outside. One day, Tom was playing in his garden when he heard a funny noise coming from the fence. When Tom went to look, he saw a little bird hopping around and chirping. Tom was a bit scared so he asked the bird “Who are you?” The bird looked at Tom and answered “I am a bird and I am here to talk to you about love.” Tom was really curious, so he asked the bird “What is love?” The bird looked at Tom with patient eyes and said “Love is a special feeling. It’s like when someone is very kind to you, or when you care deeply about something.” Tom thought about this for a moment and said “Oh, that sounds nice. Does everyone have love?” The bird smiled and said “Yes everyone has love, it is inside all of us.” Tom was happy to know that everyone could have love and thanked the bird for answering his questions. Tom and the bird said goodbye and Tom went back inside, feeling a little bit wiser.
