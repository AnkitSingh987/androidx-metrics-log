Title: Lily and Ben Find the Toys

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park after school. One day, they found a big sack near the slide. They opened it and saw many toys inside. "Wow, look at all these toys!" Lily said. "Let's take them home and play with them." "But maybe they belong to someone else," Ben said. "Maybe they are lost or forgotten. We should not take what is not ours." Lily did not listen to Ben. She wanted the toys. She said, "No, they are ours now. We found them. Come on, help me carry the sack." Ben felt bad. He did not want to hurt Lily, but he did not want to do something wrong. He said, "No, Lily, I don't want to. It is not fair. Let's leave the sack here and go home. Maybe the owner will come back and find it." Lily got angry. She said, "Fine, then I will take the sack by myself. You are not my friend anymore. Go away!" She grabbed the sack and ran away. Ben was sad. He watched Lily go. He hoped she would change her mind. But Lily did not change her mind. She took the sack to her house and hid it in her room. She played with the toys all by herself. She did not share them with anyone. She did not talk to Ben anymore. She thought she was happy, but she was not. She was lonely and greedy. One evening, she looked out of her window and saw the sunset. It was very pretty. It had many colors. She remembered how she and Ben used to watch the sunset together and say how sweet it was. She missed Ben. She realized she had made a mistake. She felt sorry. She decided to take the sack back to the park and look for Ben. She hoped he would forgive her. She hoped they could be friends again. She hoped they could watch the sunset together and say how sweet it was. The moral of the story is: Do not be selfish and take what is not yours. Be honest and kind. Friendship is more important than toys.

Once upon a time there was a brave pirate. He sailed the deep, deep sea looking for adventure. One day whilst sailing, he heard a strange noise. He paused to listen closer and what he heard made him very excited. He had heard the sound of a treasure chest being opened! The pirate steered his boat towards the noise and soon enough he came upon a small island. On the island there was a big chest full of treasure! The pirate was so happy he wanted to shout. But he paused, because he heard something else. He heard the loud voices of other pirates! It seemed that he wasn't the only one to have heard the alluring call of treasure. Without a moment of hesitation, the pirate raced up the hill and got to the chest first. He opened it and inside was all sorts of jewels and gold. He had found the treasure! The pirate was very excited and he gave a loud shout of joy. He gathered all of the jewels and gold and put it in his boat, never to be seen again. He sailed off into the deep, deep sea with a smile on his face. And that is the story of the brave pirate and his treasure.

Once upon a time there was a humble man who loved to swim. Every day he would go to the lake and jump in the water, feeling the coolness of the waves. He felt so free and alive. One day however, the man felt like something was wrong. He looked out at the lake and noticed the water was dry. He became so sad and confused and said to himself, "How can the lake be dry?". He asked the birds flying over and all they said was, "We don't know". He decided he would look for help and travelled to the nearest village. He asked the people if they knew why the lake was dry and they said, "No, we don't know". The man was so sad as he realised he could never swim again and left the village. Sadly, he never found the answer to the mystery of the dry lake and never had the chance to swim again.
