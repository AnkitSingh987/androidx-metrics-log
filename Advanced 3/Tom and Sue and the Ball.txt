Title: Tom and Sue and the Ball

One day, Tom and Sue went to a big park to play. They wanted to pick a good spot to have a fun game. Tom saw a fair spot under a big tree. "Let's play here!" said Tom. Sue looked at the spot and said, "Yes, this spot is fair. But we need a ball to play." Tom looked sad. He forgot to bring a ball. They sat under the tree, hoping to find a ball. As they sat, a nice boy came by with a ball. He saw Tom and Sue looking sad. "Do you want to play with my ball?" he asked. Tom and Sue smiled and said, "Yes, please!" They all played together at the fair spot under the big tree, and they had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat that loved to sail. One day, the little boat met a slow, old turtle. The turtle was sad because he could not find any cherries to eat. Cherries were his favorite food. The little boat said, "Don't be sad, slow turtle. I can help you find some cherries." So, the little boat and the slow turtle went on a journey to find cherries. They sailed across the water and found a big cherry tree on an island. The slow turtle was so happy! He said, "Thank you, little boat, for helping me find the cherries." They ate cherries together and became best friends. From that day on, they sailed and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a kind frog named Freddy. Freddy lived in a swamp with his friends. They all liked to play and have fun together. One day, Freddy decided to organize a big party for all his friends. He invited the turtles, the ducks, and the fish to come and play games. Everyone was very excited to go to Freddy's party. When the day of the party came, all of Freddy's friends came to the swamp. They played games, ate yummy food, and had a great time. Freddy was happy to see his friends having fun. They all thanked Freddy for organizing the best party ever.

One day, a brave little soldier was walking in the forest. Suddenly, a huge wind came and pushed him over. He felt so guilty for letting the wind push him over. He tried to stand up and keep walking. Just then, an old man appeared from behind a tree. He said to the soldier, "Why do you feel guilty? We can all be pushed over sometimes - it doesn't mean anything." The soldier smiled. He appreciated the old man's advice. He said, "Thank you. I'm feeling better now." But then another strong wind came and pushed the soldier down again. This time he couldn't get up. All his strenght was gone. The old man looked worried. He said, "Little soldier, I think it's time for you to go home." The little soldier sadly nodded. He knew his adventure in the forest had come to an end. He slowly turned and walked away. The old man watched as the little soldier walked away. His heart felt heavy and he felt guilty - he knew he should have done something more to help. But it was too late. The little soldier was gone forever.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tom. Tom had a toy crab that he loved to play with. The crab was ugly, but Tom liked it a lot. He would pinch the crab's claws and make it dance. One day, Tom saw two bugs having a fight in the garden. The bugs were pinching each other with their tiny legs. Tom wanted to help, so he took his ugly crab and put it between the bugs to stop the fight. The bugs saw the ugly crab and stopped fighting. They were scared of the crab's big pinchers. Tom was happy that the fight was over. He played with his crab and the bugs in the garden all day long.

Once upon a time there was an incredible lady who needed to go shopping. She was running late and didn't have enough time to supply all the things she wanted. The lady went to the store, but unfortunately there wasn't enough of what she needed. She needed to find more so she decided to go to another store. When she arrived she saw there were plenty of things she needed. Suddenly, when she was gathering the things, strange noises began to appear. She became scared and started to run away. She quickly got in her car and drove off, but she didn't know that someone else was in the car too. When she got home, the lady was shocked to find the car was full of robbers. They had tricked her and taken all the things she had gathered! The incredible lady was very sad and angry. The bad ending was that the lady lost all the things she wanted to supply.
