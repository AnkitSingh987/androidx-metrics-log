Title: Tim the Bunny and the Park

One day, a little bunny named Tim went to the park. Tim loved to hop and play with his friends. Today, he wanted to have a fun picnic with them. So, he made a big sandwich with yummy things inside. At the park, Tim saw his friend, a silly duck named Lucy. Lucy liked to make funny faces and quack really loud. Tim and Lucy sat down to eat the big sandwich together. They laughed and shared the yummy sandwich. After they ate, Tim and Lucy played a game. They hopped around the park, trying to hop higher than the other. They were so happy and tired after playing. They said "bye" to each other and went home.

One day, Tim went to his friend Tom's house. Tim saw a big fireplace in the living room. He felt cold and requested Tom to light the fireplace. Tom said, "Okay, let's do it together." They put wood in the fireplace and lit it. The fire was warm and nice. But then, something unexpected happened. The fire grew bigger and bigger. Tim and Tom got upset and scared. Tom's mom came in and saw the big fire. She was very upset too. She tried to put out the fire, but it was too late. The fire had spread all over the house. Tim, Tom, and Tom's mom had to leave their home. They were all very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a mechanic named Tom. Tom was a serious man who liked to fix cars. One day, while he was working, a little girl named Sally came to his shop. "Hi, Tom!" said Sally. "Can you provide help for my toy car? It doesn't go anymore." Tom looked at the toy car and saw that it needed a new wheel. So, he found a small wheel and put it on the toy car. Sally was very happy and thanked Tom. As she started to leave, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the toy car up into a tree! Sally was sad, but Tom had an idea. Tom climbed the tree and got the toy car back for Sally. She was so happy and said, "Thank you, Tom! You are the best mechanic ever!" And they both laughed and played with the toy car.

Once upon a time, there was a big, interesting engine. It was very dirty and needed a wash. The engine lived in a small town with many friends. One day, a little girl named Amy saw the dirty engine and decided to help. Amy called her friend, Ben, and said, "Let's wash the interesting engine. It is very dirty." Ben agreed and they both went to the engine with water and soap. They started to wash the engine, scrubbing it with the soap and water. As they washed the engine, it started to look shiny and new. The interesting engine was very happy and said, "Thank you, Amy and Ben, for making me clean again!" Amy and Ben smiled and said, "You're welcome, engine! We're glad we could help." And from that day on, the interesting engine was always clean and shiny, thanks to Amy and Ben.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Tara and Jake. They were playing in Tara's backyard one day and found a strange box. Inside the box was an exam. Jake wasn't sure of what it was, so he asked Tara. "It's an exam," said Tara. "I've seen them before." The two of them got very excited, they decided to try it together. They worked on it for a while and when they finished, they were very happy. But suddenly, the exam vanished! Tara and Jake looked everywhere, but it was nowhere to be found. They tried to be flexible and find it again, but it didn't appear. Tara and Jake were very sad and never found the exam again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a toy box in her room. This toy box was her chamber. She loved to play with her toys and keep them in her chamber. One day, her mom told her, "Sue, you have too many toys. They don't all fit in the chamber." Sue was sad. She did not want to give away any of her toys. She thought they were all good. She had an idea. She said, "Mom, I will find a way to make them all fit in the chamber." The next day, Sue tried to put all her toys in the chamber. She put the big toys at the bottom and the small toys on top. But they still did not fit. Sue was very sad. She sat down and thought about what to do. Then, Sue had a great idea. She took out all the toys and put them back in the chamber one by one. This time, she put the toys in a special way. She put the toys that were the same shape together. When she was done, all the toys fit in the chamber. Sue was very happy. Her mom was proud of her. She said, "Sue, you did a good job. Now all your toys fit in the chamber." Sue smiled and played with her toys all day.
