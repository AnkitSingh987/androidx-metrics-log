Title: Amy the Girl and the Man

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy who loved to march. Every day she would go out in her front yard and march around and around. One day as she was marching, she spotted something on the ground. It was a beautiful marble! She picked it up and examined it. Then, she put it into her pocket and kept on marching. Amy marched and marched until she came across a rude man. Amy looked up at the man and asked, "What are you doing here?" The man scowled and shouted, "That's none of your business! Now, go away!" Aghast, Amy quickly marched away. She was so glad to get away from the rude man. She ran straight to her Mom, and showed her the marble. Her Mom smiled, and said, "Oh what a beautiful marble! Where did you get it?" Amy explained, "I got it while I was marching in the front yard!" Amy and her Mom smiled and hugged each other. All's well that ends well!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite cloth. It was a big, soft cloth with many colors. Tim loved to play with it and use it as a cape when he pretended to be a superhero. One gloomy day, Tim was playing with his cloth. He wanted to share it with his best friend, Sam. So, Tim decided to split the cloth into two pieces. Now, both Tim and Sam could have a piece of the special cloth. Tim and Sam played with their pieces of cloth all day. They were so happy to share the soft, colorful cloth. Even though the day was gloomy, Tim and Sam had lots of fun together. And that made the gloomy day feel bright and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to hang from the trees and play with his friends. They would swing from one tree to another, laughing and having fun. One day, Momo heard about a big event in the jungle. All the animals were going to be there. Momo was very excited and couldn't wait to go. He put on his favorite hat and started to swing from tree to tree, heading to the event. When Momo got there, he saw all his friends and many other animals. They were all having fun, playing games and eating yummy fruits. Momo joined them and had the best time ever. He played with his friends, ate lots of fruits, and even made some new friends. At the end of the day, Momo was very happy and couldn't wait for the next big event in the jungle.

Once upon a time, in a dark sea, there lived an octopus. The octopus had many friends. They liked to play together. One day, the octopus wanted to play a new game. The octopus said to his friends, "Let's play hide and seek in the dark sea." But his friends did not want to play. They said, "No, we disagree. The dark sea is not safe." The octopus was sad. He wanted to play in the dark sea. So, he went alone. He did not listen to his friends. He swam deeper and deeper into the dark sea. As the octopus was hiding, a big shark came. The shark saw the octopus and ate him. The octopus should have listened to his friends. The dark sea was not safe.

Once upon a time, a playful dog named Spot lived in a small house. Spot loved to play all day long. One day, Spot saw a big pizza on the table. He wanted to eat it, but it was too high for him to reach. Spot tried to jump up to get the pizza, but he couldn't. He said, "I want the pizza, but I struggle to reach it." Spot's friend, a cat named Whiskers, heard him and came to help. Whiskers was good at climbing and jumped on the table. Whiskers pushed the pizza to the edge of the table so Spot could reach it. But, the pizza fell on the floor! Spot and Whiskers looked at the pizza and laughed. They said, "We didn't expect that!" They ate the pizza together and had a fun day.
