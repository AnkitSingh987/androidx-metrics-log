Title: Claudia and the Box

Once upon a time there was a brave girl. Her name was Claudia. She wanted to save the world! One day while playing outside, she saw a metal box. She opened the box and found some fireworks inside. Claudia knew the fireworks were not safe, so she placed them in the river. Claudia watched as the fireworks bobbed up and down in the peaceful river. She was proud she had saved the day. "Good work, Claudia," said her mom. "I'm so proud of you." Claudia smiled and thought to herself, "Maybe I can save the world!"

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Speedy. Speedy was an amazing car. He could speed very fast. One day, Speedy met a big truck named Biggie. They became friends. Speedy said to Biggie, "I wish I could speed as fast as a rocket!" Biggie laughed and said, "You are already very fast, Speedy!" One sunny day, Speedy and Biggie went to race. Speedy wanted to show how fast he could speed. They started the race. Speedy went very fast. Biggie tried to keep up, but Speedy was too fast. As Speedy raced faster and faster, he did not see a big rock on the road. Speedy hit the rock and broke. Biggie stopped to help, but it was too late. Speedy could not be fixed. From that day on, Biggie always remembered to be careful when going fast.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there was a snake. The snake was not nice. He was a bad snake. He liked to scare all the little animals who lived in the forest. The little animals did not like the bad snake. One day, the bad snake saw a little bunny. The bunny was very scared of the snake. The snake wanted to scare the bunny more. But a big bird saw the snake and the bunny. The big bird wanted to help the little bunny. The big bird told the little bunny to stay near her. The little bunny stayed with the big bird. The bad snake tried to scare them, but the big bird was not scared. The big bird told the bad snake to go away. The bad snake went away. The little bunny was happy and said thank you to the big bird. From that day, the little bunny and the big bird were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to draw with her crayons. She had a big box of crayons with many colors. One day, Sue and her family were going on a trip. Sue was very excited. Sue's mom told her to pack her things for the trip. Sue packed her clothes, her toys, and her crayons. She did not want to forget her crayons because she wanted to draw during the trip. Sue put everything in her bag and was ready to go. On the trip, Sue's family went to a big park. Sue saw a big dog and felt scared. Her mom told her not to worry because the dog was friendly. Sue took out her crayons and drew a picture of the dog. The dog wagged its tail and Sue was not scared anymore. Sue had a fun trip with her family and her crayons.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. The tree had many branches, and many leaves. One day, a little bird gazed at the tree. The bird wanted to build a nest in the tree. The bird found a chain near the tree. The chain was shiny and strong. The bird picked up the chain and put it in the tree. The bird used the chain to make a nest. Every day, the bird would sit in the nest and gaze at the sky. The bird was very happy in the tall tree. The tree and the bird became good friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a King who wore a very big crown. Every day, he would make promises to his people, and they loved him. One day, he decided to go on a journey and asked his people to stay and be loyal to him. The King had gone for many days, when suddenly a new man came to the kingdom and asked for the King's crown. Everyone was very scared and did not know what to do. They asked the man to go away, but he refused. Then, one little boy in the kingdom, who was brave, said that he would meet the man and try to talk to him. He went and talked with the man, who said that he just wanted the crown, and would leave if he was given it. The boy thought a moment, then said that if the man promised to always be loyal to the King and surrender to the King's authority, then he could keep the crown. The man agreed, and the boy gave him the crown. The King later returned, and the people were so relieved to see him! They were so proud of the boy too, for teaching the man to surrender to the King and not take the crown by force. It is important to remember that when someone is too tight with something, it is important to surrender and respect the authority.
