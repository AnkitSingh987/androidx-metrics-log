Title: The Ball and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small ball that could bounce very high. One day, the ball wanted to bounce higher than ever before. So, it went to a tall tree and started to bounce. As the ball bounced higher and higher, it saw a wise judge who was a big owl. The judge owl looked at the ball and said, "Little ball, it is dangerous to bounce so high. You might get hurt or lost." But the ball did not listen and kept trying to bounce higher. Finally, the ball bounced so high that it got stuck in the tree. It was scared and called for help. The judge owl heard the ball and came to help. The owl used its strong wings to push the ball out of the tree. The ball was free and learned that it was important to listen to wise friends. From that day on, the ball never tried to bounce too high and always remembered to be careful.

One sunny day, a fast car went for a drive. The car loved the nice weather. It sped down the road, feeling grateful for the warm sun. As the car drove, it saw a big tree. The tree said, "Hello, fast car! I like your speed!" The car said, "Thank you, big tree! I like the weather today!" But then, something unexpected happened. The sky turned dark, and rain started to fall. The fast car had to slow down. It was sad because it could not speed anymore. The big tree saw the car and said, "Don't worry, fast car. You can come under my branches to stay dry." The fast car was grateful for the big tree's help. They became good friends, and the car learned that even when the weather changes, there's always something to be happy about.

One sunny day, a bunny was hopping around the meadow. She was having so much fun! Suddenly, she saw a snake slithering in the grass, and she was very scared. Hoping to escape, the bunny hopped away quickly. The snake was feeling very hungry. He felt his stomach grumbling. He noticed the bunny hopping away and decided to follow her. His long body slowly moved closer to the bunny with every hop she took. The bunny was getting tired and the snake was getting closer. Suddenly, out of nowhere, he opened his wide mouth and bit her. The bunny screamed with pain. Her furry skin came off from her body and she felt helpless. The ignorant snake didn't care that he had caused pain to another creature. He simply opened his wide mouth one more time and ate the bunny in one bite. The end.

Dave and his dad were walking down the street. Dave was pushing his dad's cart, with a lot of groceries in it. Suddenly, Dave stepped on something sharp. He looked down and saw a newspaper. Dave's dad told him that the newspaper was really old and should have been thrown away. But when Dave saw the newspaper he was really excited. He wanted to keep it. "No, no, no," his dad said. "It's too old and too stupid." Dave begged and pleaded but his dad said no. Then finally, his dad had an idea. "Okay, I have an idea," he said. "Let's put the newspaper in the cart and you can use it for drawing. Can you do that?" Dave's eyes lit up. He nodded his head and put the newspaper in the cart. From that day on, Dave always used the newspaper to draw funny pictures.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big box of blocks. He was very happy. Tim liked to play with the blocks. He made tall towers and long roads. The blocks were noisy when they fell down. Tim liked the sound. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. They played with the blocks together. They made a big city with the blocks. It was very noisy when the blocks fell down. They both laughed. Sam said, "I like to play with blocks!" Tim spoke, "Me too! Let's play more!" They played with the blocks all day. They were very happy and had a lot of fun.
