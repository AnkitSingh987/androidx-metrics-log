Title: Mike and the Paint

One day, Mike went on a trip. He brought some paint with him. When Mike got to his destination, he was ready to paint. He saw a big, blank wall outside and he said to himself, “I should paint that wall!” So Mike grabbed his paintbrush and started to paint. He painted for a long time and soon the wall was covered in bright colors. When he was done, Mike took a step back and smiled. His work looked normal, but it was also special because it was made by Mike. Mike felt proud of his work. He said to himself, “I did it!”

Once there was a boy named Tim. He was only three years old. One day, his parents invited an elderly horse to come and visit them. Tim was amazed at the sight of the old horse. He asked his parents, "Can I pet the horse?" His parents answered, "Yes, but make sure you go slow and be gentle with him." Tim went up to the horse and very carefully petted the horse's nose. The horse nuzzled Tim's hand and made a soft whinny. Tim was so excited. The next day, Tim invited other kids to come and meet the horse. Together they all fed the horse apples and carrots and played hide-and-seek around the barn. The horse was so happy. Tim and the horse became the best of friends.

Sara was very frustrated because she couldn't touch her cup of tea. It was kept high up on a shelf and she couldn't reach it. She wanted very much to drink the tea because it smelled so good and it looked so yummy. Sara thought for a few moments. She decided to ask her big sister for help. She ran to her sister and said, "Please can you help me touch the tea?" Her sister smiled and said, "Sure, little one." She got a chair, stood on it, and brought down the cup of tea. She placed it on the table and said to Sara, "Now you can touch it." Sara was very excited. She gave her big sister a hug and then touched the cup of tea. She held it in her two hands and smiled. At last she could touch her cup of tea! She drank it and said, "Thank you, sister, it was so delicious!"

Once upon a time, there was a wise cat named Tom. Tom got an invitation to a big party. He was very excited to go. Tom put on his best hat and went to the party. At the party, he saw many friends. They danced and played games. They all had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. Tom saw a big cake, and he wanted to take a piece. But when he took a piece, the cake jumped! It was not a cake, but a big, funny hat. All the friends laughed and played with the funny hat. The party was even more fun than before.

One day, a little girl named Mia saw a big volcano. She wanted to remove the rocks from the volcano. She thought, "Maybe I will find something tasty under the rocks!" Mia asked her friend Tom, "Do you want to help me remove the rocks from the volcano?" Tom said, "Yes, let's do it!" They worked hard to remove the rocks. After a while, they found a hidden cave. Inside the cave, they found tasty fruits. Mia and Tom were so happy! They shared the fruits with their friends. The volcano had a nice surprise for them after all.

Once upon a time, there was a big field. In the field, there were many incredible animals. They all played together every day. One day, a little bunny went to the field. The bunny wanted to play with the incredible animals. The bunny saw a big elephant and asked, "Can I play with you?" The elephant said, "Yes, come play with us!" So the bunny and the elephant played together in the field. They had lots of fun. They became very good friends. And the bunny learned that if you ask nicely, you can make incredible friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a mysterious storm. The storm was very big and loud. It made the people in the village scared. They did not know what to do. One day, a wise old man came to the village. He told the people, "I can help you with the storm. Let's measure how big it is." The people were happy and said, "Yes, please help us!" The old man and the people worked together to measure the storm. After they measured the storm, they found out it was not as big as they thought. The old man said, "See, the storm is not so scary now. You just needed to learn about it." The people thanked the old man and learned that sometimes things are not as scary as they seem. The moral of the story is to not be afraid of things you don't understand, and to ask for help when you need it.
