Title: Lily and the Apple

Once upon a time, a girl named Lily went to her yoga class. She loved yoga. It made her feel good and happy. Lily liked her yoga teacher, who was an old lady. The old lady was very nice and taught Lily how to bend and stretch. One day, after yoga class, the old lady gave Lily a big red apple. She said, "This apple will taste very good. But you must not eat it in your house. Eat it outside, under the big tree." Lily said thank you and took the apple home. Lily was excited to taste the apple. She forgot what the old lady said and took a big bite inside her house. Suddenly, the house started to shake. The walls and roof fell down. Lily was very sad. She remembered what the old lady said, but it was too late. Her house was gone.

Once upon a time there was a fisherman. He was very strange and weird. He was often seen cutting along the shoreline of the beach. One day, a 3 year old child saw the strange man cutting by the shore and asked her mother, "What is that man doing mommy?" The mother replied, "He is cutting down some trees." The little girl said, "Why does he have to cut the trees down?" The mother answered, "To make sure that nobody gets hurt in the forest. That's why it's important to be careful and kind when we are in the forest." The little girl nodded, understanding the moral value of being careful and kind in the outdoors. From that day onward, the little girl was always careful and kind when she was out in the forest. She was grateful to the strange fisherman for helping her understand why it's important to be careful around nature.

Once upon a time, there was a brown bear called Bob. Bob loved to keep himself clean and neat, but there was this one cobweb in the corner of his cave. Every day, it kept growing bigger and bigger and Bob wanted to get rid of it. Bob asked his friends for help. He said, "can someone please help me get rid of this cobweb?" Everyone looked at Bob and then shook their heads. It was just too big! Just then, little rabbit hopped nearby. He said to Bob, "I have an idea. Why don't you blow the cobweb away?" Bob tried and tried but the cobweb wouldn't budge. Then a voice said, "Keep trying, I'm sure you can do it!" Bob looked up and saw a curious-looking squirrel. He said to Bob, "Be persistent. Don't give up!" So Bob kept trying and after a few more attempts, the cobweb flew away! Bob thanked his friends for all the help but he was the one who kept trying until he achieved success.

Once upon a time, in a large house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy box. One day, he found a talking doll inside the box. The doll had a smile on its face and was wearing a blue dress. "Hello, Tim!" said the doll. "Do you want to play with me?" Tim was happy and nodded his head. They played together all day long. They ran from one side of the house to the other, laughing and having fun. As the sun went down, Tim and the doll were tired. They sat on the floor next to the toy box. "Thank you for playing with me," said the doll. "You are my new best friend." Tim smiled and nodded. They put the toys back in the box and went to bed, excited for more fun the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play and discover new things in his backyard. One sunny day, he found something very strange. It was a skull! Tim was scared at first, but he looked closer and saw that it was fake. It was just a toy. Tim showed the fake skull to his friends. They all thought it was cool and wanted to play with it. They played games and had fun with the fake skull. After a while, one of Tim's friends, Sam, wanted to keep the skull for himself. He took it from Tim and ran away. Tim was very sad, but he knew that fighting was not right. He decided to talk to Sam and tell him how he felt. Sam listened and understood. He gave the fake skull back to Tim and they continued to play together. The moral of the story is to always use words to solve problems instead of fighting.
