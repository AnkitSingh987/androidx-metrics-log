Title: The Pair of Sunglasses and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a pair of sunglasses. They were very glad. They liked to hang on a tree. Every day, the sun would shine, and the sunglasses would help people see better. One day, a little boy came to the tree. He saw the sunglasses hanging there. He wanted to wear them. So, he took them off the tree and put them on his face. The sunglasses were very glad to help the little boy see better. But then, a big wind came. It blew the sunglasses off the little boy's face. They flew far away and got lost. The little boy was sad. The sunglasses were not glad anymore. They could not hang on the tree and help people see better. And that is the end of the story.

One day, a human named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big, soft bed in the middle of the park. Tom was very tired, so he lay down on the soft bed. He felt happy and cozy. Just then, a bird flew down and landed on the bed. The bird said, "This is my bed, human. Why are you on it?" Tom was surprised. He did not know birds could talk. "I am sorry," said Tom. "I did not know this was your bed." The bird looked at Tom and thought for a moment. Then, the bird said, "You can stay on my bed, but you must help me find food." Tom agreed to help the bird. They worked together to find food for the bird and became good friends. In the end, Tom and the bird shared the soft bed and had a great nap in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a unique little bird named Will. Will was not like the other birds. He had many colors on his wings, and he could sing very pretty songs. One day, Will met a big friendly bear. The bear said, "Will, your songs make me happy. Can you promise to sing for me every day?" Will nodded his head and said, "I promise, Mr. Bear. I will sing for you every day." So, every day, Will sang his pretty songs for the big friendly bear. They became the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a furry pet named Fluffy. Fluffy was a small, happy dog. He loved to play with his best friend, a little boy named Timmy. One day, Timmy found a seed in the yard. He showed it to Fluffy and said, "Let's grow this seed!" So, they dug a hole and put the seed in the ground. Every day, Timmy and Fluffy watered the seed and waited for it to grow. Soon, a tiny green plant came out of the ground. Timmy and Fluffy were so excited! They kept watering the plant and it grew bigger and bigger. One day, the plant had a pretty flower on it. Timmy looked at Fluffy and said, "We did it, Fluffy! We grew a flower!" Fluffy wagged his tail and barked happily. Timmy and his furry pet Fluffy loved their flower and took care of it every day. They knew that if they worked together, they could make anything grow.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. The rock was very far away. It was in a distant place where no one could see it. The rock was not happy because it was all alone. One day, a soft scarf fell from the sky. The scarf was very pretty and colorful. The rock liked the scarf very much. The scarf wrapped around the rock and made it feel warm and cozy. Now, the rock and the scarf were friends. They stayed in the distant place together. The rock was not alone anymore. The rock and the scarf were happy. They played and laughed every day.

Molly was very excited as she got dressed for the day. She pulled on her bright red dress and put on her shoes that sparkled in the sun. She couldn't wait to go outside and have some fun. Molly's mum had a surprise for her - a pot of paint. Molly was so confused; she had never painted before. With a little help from her mum, she learned to dip her brush in the paint and carefully colour on the paper. She felt so proud to see her colourful wonky shapes on the page. When Molly had finished painting, Mum took her to the garden and together they put some paint on a big canvas. Molly used the paint to create a bright rainbow and big blue clouds. Then, the rain started to fall and the colours began to run together to make a beautiful, colourful mess. When Molly and her mum went inside, they were both covered in paint. Molly giggled at the funny sight and she was glad they had all that fun. Mum helped Molly take off all the paint so that she looked clean and neat. Molly was so excited to wear her new dress that night. And although she was a little confused about why it had paint on it, she was still very happy. Now she could have fun with her family having dressed for the occasion.
