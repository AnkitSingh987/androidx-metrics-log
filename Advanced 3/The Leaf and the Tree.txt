Title: The Leaf and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a small leaf. The leaf lived on a big tree. The tree was in a pretty forest. The leaf loved to dance in the wind. The wind was sometimes harsh, but the leaf was strong. One day, the leaf was tired. It needed to rest. So, the leaf lay down on a soft bed of grass. The leaf closed its eyes and began to rest. It was very quiet in the forest. Then, something unexpected happened. A big gust of wind came and lifted the leaf into the air. The leaf was scared at first but then it started to enjoy the ride. The leaf flew high in the sky and saw many new things. At the end of the day, the leaf landed back on its tree, happy for the adventure it had.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her favorite toy, a red tie. She would run and jump with the tie in her mouth all day long. One day, Kitty's tie got very dirty. It was filthy! Kitty was sad because she did not want to play with a dirty tie. Kitty's best friend, a dog named Buddy, saw her looking sad. Buddy asked, "Why are you sad, Kitty?" Kitty told Buddy about her filthy tie. Buddy had an idea. He knew that Kitty's mom had a special key that could unlock the door to the room with soap and water. They could clean the tie! Buddy and Kitty looked for the key. They found it under a big chair. Kitty used the key to unlock the door. Inside, they found soap and water. They washed the filthy tie until it was clean and bright red again. Now, Kitty was happy and could play with her favorite tie. Buddy and Kitty played together and had lots of fun. And they knew that if the tie ever got dirty again, they could unlock the door and clean it.

Once upon a time, there was a big, shiny diamond. It was in the forest. A little girl found the diamond. She was very happy. She wanted to keep it. The little girl was selfish. She did not want to share the diamond. She hid it in her room. She did not let her friends see it. One day, there was a big show. People were clapping and having fun. The little girl brought the diamond to the show. Everyone saw the diamond and clapped. The little girl learned to share and was not selfish anymore.

Once upon a time, in a hidden place, there was a pretty girl named Lily. Lily loved to sing songs. Her songs made everyone happy. One day, she met a nice boy named Tom. They liked to play and sing together. Tom asked Lily to marry him. Lily said yes, and they were very happy. On the day of the marry, everyone came to see Lily and Tom. They wore pretty clothes and sang songs. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Lily away. Tom was sad and everyone was worried. Tom went to find Lily. He walked and walked. He heard a song. It was Lily's song! Tom followed the song and found Lily. She was hidden in a tree, safe from the wind. Tom helped Lily down from the tree. They went back to the marry and sang their song together. Everyone was happy again.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing in her room. She saw a big wardrobe in the corner. It was very old, and she had never looked inside. She was ready to explore. She opened the door and saw many colorful clothes. Lucy put on a pretty dress from the wardrobe and started to dance around. She heard a tiny voice say, "Hello! Will you play with me?" She looked down and saw a small mouse wearing a tiny hat. Lucy smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" As they played, Lucy saw the mouse bite a piece of cheese. She laughed and shared her snack with the mouse. They played and ate until it was time for bed. Lucy put the dress back in the wardrobe and said goodnight to her new friend.
