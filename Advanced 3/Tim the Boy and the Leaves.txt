Title: Tim the Boy and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tim. Tim loved to collect things. He collected rocks, sticks, and leaves. One day, he found a new thing to collect. It was a game called chess. Tim took the chess game home. He played with the chess pieces. There were big ones and small ones. Tim liked the horse piece best. He played with the chess game every day. Tim wanted to share his chess game with his friends. So, he took it to school. All the kids played chess together. They had lots of fun. Tim was happy because he could share his collection with everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a small bear. He wanted to look fashionable, so he went shopping in the city. He saw lots of high fashion, but none of it was quite right for him. Just then, the bear heard a loud noise. It was coming from the tallest building in the city! He ran to the top and saw a giant shaking it. He was so big and his shaking was so strong that the building was about to fall over! The small bear was so scared that he held onto the building as tight as he could. He shook and shook, hoping that he could make it stop. But nothing could stop the giant, so the building fell over. The bear was crushed under the rubble. The bear never got to wear his new high fashion clothes.

One day, a big gorilla named Gogo lived in the forest. Gogo was very harmless. He liked to play with all the little animals in the forest. They all liked Gogo too. One sunny day, Gogo and his friends were playing near the river. A little bird named Bibi came to Gogo and said, "Gogo, my nest fell from the tree, and I can't get it back up. Can you help me, please?" Gogo smiled and said, "Yes, Bibi, I will help you." Gogo picked up the nest and put it back in the tree. Bibi was very happy. But then, a little mouse named Mimi came to Gogo and started to complain. "Gogo, you are too big and loud when you play. You scare me," said Mimi. Gogo felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Mimi. I will try to be more careful when we play." From that day on, Gogo was more careful when he played with his friends. They all had lots of fun together, and nobody was scared of the big, harmless gorilla.

Once upon a time there were two special twins. They both liked to watch the sun rise every morning. Each day they would get up early and take a seat on a boulder. They could see a special view of the sun that no one else could. One day the twins noticed something unique. Instead of just seeing one sun come up in the morning, they saw two. They both looked alike but it was clear they were twins. The twins knew it was special to watch two suns come up in the morning. They both knew they should enjoy the view while they could. But what would happen after the next day? The next morning came and the twins returned to their spot. They couldn't believe what they were seeing: two moons! The two moons looked the same, but still had something unique about them. This was a sight very few people ever seen. From then on, each sunrise the twins could watch two moons come up in the sky. They had something very unique and special to share that no one else had.

Once upon a time, in a big universe, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. Buddy was a good friend to a little girl named Jane. They played all day and had lots of fun. One day, Jane felt sad. She lost her toy and could not find it. Buddy saw that Jane was sad and wanted to help. "Woof, woof!" said Buddy, as he looked around for the toy. Jane smiled at Buddy and said, "Thank you, Buddy. Let's find my toy together." Buddy and Jane looked everywhere for the toy. They looked under the bed, in the closet, and behind the couch. Finally, they found the toy under a big tree in the yard. Jane was so happy and hugged Buddy. Buddy comforted Jane and made her feel better. From that day on, Buddy and Jane knew they would always be there for each other. They were the best of friends in the whole universe. And they lived happily ever after.
