Title: Tom the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with a cord. One day, Tom saw a big ball. The ball was round and fun. Tom wanted to play with the ball. Tom went to the ball and said, "Hi, ball! Can I play with you?" The ball said, "Yes, Tom! Let's play together!" Tom was very happy. They played all day. As they played, Tom used his paw to strike the ball. The ball rolled and hit the cord. The cord swung back and forth. Tom and the ball laughed and played until the sun went down. They were very happy friends.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Alex. He was 3 years old and he loved playing outside in the garden. One evening, while playing in the garden, Alex heard something in the bushes. It sounded quite strange. He stood still and listened. Suddenly, out from the darkness hopped a small, brown bunny. Alex had never seen him from before. "Hi there" the little bunny said. "I was just looking for a pin in the bushes". Alex was a little disturbed by the bunny, but he was also a little curious. "What do you need a pin for?" he asked the bunny. The little bunny held up a ball of wool and said: "I want to hold the wool together, but I need a pin to do it". Alex was now feeling less disturbed. He had seen his Mommy using pins when she sewed. So he reached down and picked up a pin from the ground. He handed it to the bunny who smiled and thanked him. Then he hopped away back into the dark. Alex smiled and went back to playing in the garden.

Once upon a time, in a great big forest, there lived a wild bunny. The wild bunny liked to hop and play all day long. One day, the wild bunny found a little lost bird. The bird was sad and scared, so the wild bunny decided to care for the little bird. The wild bunny and the little bird became very good friends. They played together, and the wild bunny cared for the little bird every day. The little bird was very happy with the wild bunny, and they had lots of fun in the great big forest. But one day, something unexpected happened. The little bird started to grow very big! The wild bunny was surprised and didn't know what was happening. Then, the little bird turned into a great big bird! It turns out, the little bird was a baby eagle! The wild bunny and the great big eagle stayed friends, and they played and cared for each other in the great big forest.

Jane and her mom were going on an adventure. Jane was feeling excited. Everywhere she looked there was something new to explore. Out the window, she saw a big, light blue refrigerator that seemed to be waiting for them. Jane and her mom kept walking towards the refrigerator. As they got closer, Jane started to feel nervous. She had never seen anything like it before. What could it be used for? When Jane and her mom reached the refrigerator, Jane's mom opened the door. Inside, Jane saw all kinds of yummy food. She was so excited that she started jumping up and down. Her mom laughed and said, "That's the refrigerator. It keeps food cold so it stays fresh." Jane smiled and felt relieved. Now she knew what the refrigerator was for.

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny day, a little dog went out to play. He liked to run and jump in the park. The little dog was very happy. While playing, the little dog found a red ball. He did not know who the ball belonged to. He wanted to find the owner of the ball. The little dog picked up the ball and started to look for the owner. The little dog showed the red ball to many people in the park. At last, he found a little girl who was looking for her ball. She was the owner! The little girl was very happy and thanked the little dog. They played with the red ball together and had a lot of fun.
