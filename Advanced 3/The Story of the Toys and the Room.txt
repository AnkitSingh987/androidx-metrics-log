Title: The Story of the Toys and the Room

Once there was a young girl who was always very careful. One day she decided it was time to clean her room. She started by picking up her toys and putting them all away in their boxes. She was very careful to make sure her room was neat. Then she took her broom and swept all the dirt and dust out of her room. Finally she took a damp cloth and wiped all of the surfaces in her room until they were nice and clean. When she was finished, the young girl stood back and admired her clean room. She had done a great job and was very proud of herself. The young girl had been so careful that her room sparkled like new!

One day, a kind girl named Sue went to the park to play. She saw a big board near the swings. Sue walked up to the board and looked at it. The board had many colors on it. It was very pretty. Sue saw a boy named Tim near the board. Tim looked sad. Sue asked him, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to draw on the board, but I don't have any colors." Sue wanted to help Tim. She had some colors in her bag. She took them out and gave them to Tim. Tim was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Sue! You are very kind." Together, Sue and Tim drew on the board. They made a big sun, a tree, and a house. They had a lot of fun playing at the park with the board. Sue and Tim became good friends. They learned that being kind and helping each other can make the day more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a unique pistol. The pistol could fly! It floated around like a balloon and went wherever it wanted. Everywhere the pistol went, people would say, "Wow! What is that thing flying up there?" The fly pistol liked to go on adventures. One day, it flew over a pond and saw some frogs jumping around. It watched the frogs for a while and then kept flying. Later, the pistol flew over a forest and saw a bunch of birds. They were singing and having a grand old time! The pistol stayed for a bit and then flew away. Finally, the fly pistol arrived back home. The next day, it flew off again to explore the world. It was so much fun discovering new places and having adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a fat zebra. The zebra was always very dirty and wanted to look nice. One day, he decided to take a bath. He stepped into the water and it felt very nice. He washed his head, his body and even his long tail. He scrubbed until he felt very clean. After he was washed, he felt so much better! His stripes were nice and shiny and he was very happy. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny. He wanted to have lots of fun, so he hopped around the forest. Every time he hopped, his shadow followed behind him. The bunny hopped over to a big tree and stopped. He was feeling rather ashamed because his shadow looked so big. He didn't like having this big, black shadow follow him around. But then he had an idea. He hopped higher and higher, higher than any other bunny had ever hopped before. On his way up, he hopped up to the sun and asked for its help. The sun smiled down on the little bunny and warmth filled the air. Soon, his shadow shrank until it was very small. The bunny hopped back down to the ground, feeling very proud. He no longer felt ashamed of his shadow! From then on, he happily hopped around the forest.

One day, a young boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with lots of leaves. Tim wanted to climb the tree, but he was too small. He tried and tried, but it was a struggle. "Mom, can you help me climb the tree?" Tim asked. His mom said, "You are still too little, Tim. Let's play on the swings instead." While they were playing on the swings, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the leaves off the tree. Now, Tim could see a ladder behind the leaves! Tim and his mom went to the ladder. Tim climbed up and up, and he could see the whole park from the top. He was so happy that he could finally climb the tree, even if it was a little different than he thought.
