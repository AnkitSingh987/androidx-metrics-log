Title: Amy the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to ride the subway with her mom. They would go on fun trips to the park and the zoo. One day, Amy and her mom were waiting for the subway. Amy's mom said, "Be careful, Amy. We must protect each other near the subway tracks." Amy listened and held her mom's hand tight. When they got on the subway, Amy saw a little bird. The bird was scared and flew around the subway car. Amy said, "Mom, let's protect the bird and help it get out." They were very careful and helped the bird find its way out. Everyone on the subway clapped and cheered. Amy and her mom smiled, happy to have helped the little bird.

Sara and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were pirates and look for treasure. Sara had a big hat and a sword. Ben had a map and a spyglass. "Look, Sara, I found a clue!" Ben said, pointing to a rock with a mark on it. "The treasure is under the big tree!" They ran to the tree and started to dig with their hands. They were eager to see what was inside. They hoped it was gold and jewels and candy. But as they dug deeper, they heard a hiss. A snake was hiding under the dirt. It was angry and scared. It showed its teeth and coiled its body. "Ah, a snake!" Sara screamed. She dropped her sword and ran away. Ben was scared too, but he did not want to leave the treasure. He picked up Sara's sword and waved it at the snake. "Go away, snake! This is our treasure!" Ben shouted. "Or I will make you surrender!" The snake did not like the sword. It did not want to fight. It slithered away to find a new home. Ben was brave and happy. He dug some more and found a box. He opened it and saw shiny coins and beads and chocolates. "Sara, come back! I scared the snake away! Look what I found!" Ben called. Sara came back slowly. She was still afraid of the snake. But when she saw the box, she smiled. She hugged Ben and said sorry for running away. "It's okay, Sara. You are my sister and my pirate mate. We can share the treasure." Ben said. They ate some chocolates and put the coins and beads in their pockets. They decided to bury the box again and make a new map. They had fun playing pirates and looking for treasure. They did not see the snake again.

Little Dora was always so excited for Sunday. Every Sunday she woke up early and put on her prettiest dress. This Sunday was extra special - it was the day that her daddy was coming home from work. He hadn’t been home for weeks now, and Dora was so excited. She couldn’t help but smile as she put on her prettiest dress, with the little blue bow that reminded her of her daddy. As the day went on, Dora began to get impatient. She wanted to see her father so badly! She kept busy by tidying up the house and playing with her little toy car - anything to pass the time. Finally, the door opened and her daddy stepped in. “Daddy!” she shouted in excitement, running to him and hugging his legs. She had missed him so much! Dora smiled at her daddy, glad that he was home. He smiled back, reminding her that every Sunday should be special and that he would never be too away for too long.

One day, a duck was walking in the park. Suddenly, he saw something wonderful. It was a shiny gold coin just lying there on the ground. The duck was so happy that he said, "Lucky me! I'm going to pick up the coin and take it home". But a fat cat appeared from behind a tree and said, "I want that gold coin". The duck quickly answered, "No way! It's mine". The cat started to get angry and said, "Give me the coin or else I'm going to catch you". The duck was scared, but he was also very smart. He said, "Let's make a deal. I'll give you half of the coin, if you promise to not prevent me from picking up the other half". The cat thought about it and then smiled. "Ok, deal". The duck picked up the lucky coin and ran back to his home.
