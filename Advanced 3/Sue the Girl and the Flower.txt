Title: Sue the Girl and the Flower

One day, a girl named Sue put on her pretty skirt. She wanted to play outside with her friends. Sue was very happy because it was a sunny day. While playing, Sue smelled something spicy in the air. It made her nose tickle. She tried to hold it in, but she could not. Sue let out a big sneeze! When she sneezed, she saw something unexpected. A tiny fairy appeared in front of her! The fairy said, "Thank you for sneezing me out of the spicy flower!" Sue was so surprised. She did not know that fairies lived in spicy flowers. The fairy and Sue became good friends and played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Suzy. One day, when Suzy was playing in her room, she saw her bookshelf glowing. She looked up in amazement and asked her mum, "Why is it glowing, mummy?" Her mum hugged her and said, "Because it's very nice and you should always be nice too, sweetheart." Suzy smiled and said, "Okay, mummy". The next day, Suzy was about to go to school when her mum called her for a surprise. When Suzy got to the front door, there was a beautiful bookshelf, glowing in the morning light. Suzy was so happy and asked her mum, "Why is this bookshelf glowing too?" Her mum smiled and said, "Because you were nice to everyone, Suzy. Being nice is like a glow that is always with you." Suzy nodded and said, "I understand, mummy." Since then, Suzy has followed her mum's advice and tries to be nice to everybody. Whenever she was kind and helpful to people, a warm glow would come into her heart. The moral of the story is that being nice and kind is like a magical glow that surrounds you.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to mix things in her big bowl. One day, she decided to make a special cake. She wanted it to be a surprise for her mom and dad. Mia thought about what she could put in the cake to make it special. She looked around the kitchen and found a bitter lemon. Mia thought the bitter lemon would be a fun surprise in the cake. She mixed the cake and put the lemon inside. Then, she put the cake in the oven to bake. While the cake was baking, Mia felt the skin on the lemon. It was bumpy and yellow. She thought about how the lemon would taste in the cake. When the cake was ready, Mia took it out of the oven. She was so excited to show her mom and dad. They all sat down to eat the cake together. When her mom and dad took a bite, they made a funny face. They tasted the bitter lemon inside the cake. Mia laughed and said, "Surprise!" Her mom and dad laughed too. They told Mia that it was a very special cake, and they loved it because she made it for them.

Once upon a time, there was a big black ship. It sailed on the blue sea. On the ship, there was a kind captain and his crew. They were good friends and they all trust each other. One sunny day, the ship found a small island. The captain and his crew went to the island. They saw pretty flowers and tall trees. They played and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came from the sky. The bird was not black, but it was very big. The bird took the captain and flew away. The crew was scared, but they trust the captain. They knew he would find a way to come back. And that's what happened! The captain made friends with the bird and it brought him back to the ship. The crew was happy and they all sailed away together with their new big bird friend.

Once upon a time, there was a humble dove. The dove lived in a big tree. One day, the dove was flying high in the sky. The dove saw a little boy. The boy was sad. The dove flew down and asked the boy, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my ball. It went over the fence." The dove wanted to help the boy. So, the dove flew over the fence to find the ball. As the dove passed the fence, it saw a big dog. The dog had the ball! The dove was scared, but it wanted to help the boy. The dove talked to the dog. The dog was nice! The dog gave the ball to the dove. The dove took the ball back to the boy. The boy was happy! The boy said, "Thank you, dove!" The dove was happy too. They both learned that even when you are scared, you can still help others.
