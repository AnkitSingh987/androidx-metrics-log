Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in his yard. One day, it was very gloomy. The sky was gray and sad. But Tim still wanted to play. Tim found a purple ball in his yard. He loved the color purple. He wanted to play a game. Tim decided to bury the purple ball in the dirt. He dug a small hole and put the ball inside. Then, he covered the ball with dirt. Later, the sun came out and the gloomy day was gone. Tim went back to the yard to find his purple ball. He dug in the dirt where he buried it. He found the ball and was very happy. Tim played with the purple ball all day long.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Aaron and Becca. Every morning they would meet at the beach and look for waves to surf. Becca had grown tired of Aaron's behavior. He always took the best waves, even if it meant being selfish. Becca felt miserable and unimportant when they were together. "My friend Becca," said Aaron, "why don't you surf too?" Becca sadly replied, "Because you're always taking the best waves and leaving me with nothing." Aaron felt ashamed and knew he had to change his behavior. The next morning Aaron let Becca pick the first wave. He helped her paddle and gave her plenty of space to surf the wave. Becca's whole mood changed. She was no longer miserable and felt important again. From that day forward, Aaron and Becca always took turns surfing. They made sure to always behave in a respectful way towards each other. The moral of the story is that it's important to be thoughtful and polite to your friends!

Lily and her mum went to the park. It was a sunny day and it was very clear. When they arrived, Lily was so excited when she saw the parrots. She ran up to the colourful birds and watched them flying around. "Let's enjoy them!" said her mum. Lily jumped up and down, happy to be so close to the parrots. She watched them curiously, as they flew from branch to branch. She couldn't believe they were so close. Suddenly, one parrot came down to Lily and sat on her shoulder. Lily was so surprised, she laughed and smiled. She loved the parrot's colourful feathers against her skin. Mum laughed too. "Let's enjoy this moment," she said. Lily was so happy to be so close to the parrot. She enjoyed this special moment for a long time. When it was time to leave, she waved goodbye to the parrot. Lily knew she would never forget this special day with the parrot.

Once upon a time, there was a cool cat named Tom. Tom had a big red ball that he loved to play with. One day, Tom could not find his ball. He looked everywhere but could not find it. Tom was very sad. Tom asked his friend, Sam the dog, if he had seen the ball. Sam said, "I have not seen it, but I know a magic word that can help you find it." Tom was very excited and asked Sam to tell him the magic word. Sam whispered the word in Tom's ear. Tom said the magic word and started to rub his paws together. Suddenly, the big red ball appeared in front of him. Tom was so happy that he jumped and danced around the ball. He thanked Sam for helping him and they played together with the ball all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big pile of clay. Tim wanted to gather the clay to make a toy. He called his friend, Sam, to come and help him. Sam said, "Yes, I will help you gather clay." As they were gathering clay, they saw a fierce dog. The dog was big and loud. Tim and Sam were scared. They ran away from the dog and hid behind a tree. The dog came closer and closer. But then, something unexpected happened. The fierce dog started to wag its tail and play with the clay. Tim and Sam looked at each other and smiled. They said, "The dog just wants to play with us and the clay!" So, they all played together and had a fun day.
