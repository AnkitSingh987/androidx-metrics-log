Title: The Potato and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big potato. It lived in a garden with many other potatoes. But this potato was not happy. It felt bad all the time. One day, a kind bird saw the bad potato. The bird wanted to help. It told the potato, "You should rest. Rest will make you feel better." So, the potato listened to the bird and took a rest. After a long rest, the potato felt better. It was not a bad potato anymore. It was a happy potato. The kind bird was happy too. They became good friends and played together in the garden. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a messy dog named Spot. Spot liked to play in the mud all day. One day, Spot heard a sound coming from a big hole. It was a little cat crying for help. Spot wanted to rescue the cat, so he tried to jump into the hole. But Spot was too messy and slipped on the mud. He fell into the hole too. Now, both the cat and Spot were stuck in the hole. They tried to call for help, but no one heard them. They were very sad. Spot and the cat stayed in the hole for a long time. They never got out. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big, old tree. The tree had many roots. One little root was scared of the dark. Every night, the root would tremble. One day, a small bug came to the root. The bug said, "Why do you tremble?" The root said, "I am scared of the dark." The bug said, "I can help you. Let's be friends." The bug and the root became friends. They played together every day. When it was dark, the bug would stay with the root. The root was not scared anymore. They were happy and never alone again.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Lily. Lily loved to collect things. She collected all kinds of things like rocks, leaves, and pretty flowers. One day, Lily went outside to find more things for her collection. She found a shiny rock, a soft leaf, and a big, pretty flower. She put them all in her bag and went back to her house. When Lily got home, she put all her new things on her bed. She looked at her collection and felt very happy. She knew she was a clever girl for finding such nice things. And every night, she would look at her collection before going to sleep, dreaming of more things to collect.

Once upon a time, there was a strong dog named Max. Max loved his blanket very much. He would sleep on it every night. One day, Max could not find his blanket. He was very sad. Max started to search for his blanket. He looked in the big room. He looked in the small room. He looked under the table. But he could not find his blanket. Max was very sad. Then, Max saw his friend, the cat named Luna. Luna said, "I saw your blanket outside!" Max went outside and found his blanket under a tree. Max was very happy. He thanked Luna and took his blanket back to his bed. Now, Max could sleep happily again.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with thick branches. Tim saw a butterfly net on the ground and picked it up. He wanted to catch butterflies with the net. Tim ran around the park with the net, but he could not catch any butterflies. He got mad and started to demand that the butterflies come to him. The butterflies did not like this and flew away. Tim's mom told him that he should be nice and patient. Tim tried being nice and patient. He said "please" and "thank you" to the butterflies. Then, something amazing happened. The butterflies came to him and let him catch them in the net. Tim learned that being nice and patient is better than demanding things. And that was the moral of the story.
