Title: Mae the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Mae. Mae was very curious. One day she was walking in the garden and she saw a wall. She was very surprised. She wondered what was on the other side of the wall. Mae decided to find out what was behind the wall. She started to move some dirt. She was so happy when she saw something shiny. She started to dig and soon she had dug deep enough to find a box. Mae was curious so she opened the box. Inside was a messy jumble of things. Mae liked all the shiny things in the box and decided to bury it. She carefully mixed the dirt andpushed it back into the wall. Mae ran off to play. She would come back soon to dig up the box and find out what was inside.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to tell all the other animals what to do. One day, Ducky went to the pond to play with his friends. The pond was full of water, and all the animals loved to splash and swim in it. Ducky decided to spread his wings and fly around the pond. He wanted to show his friends how big and strong he was. As he flew, he saw his friends playing in the water below. He called out to them, "Look at me, I am the biggest and strongest duck in the pond!" His friends looked up and saw Ducky flying high in the sky. They clapped their wings and cheered for him. Even though Ducky was bossy, they still liked to play with him. The sun began to set, and it was time for all the animals to go home. Ducky landed back in the water and said goodbye to his friends. He promised to be a little less bossy the next time they played together. As the stars came out, Ducky went home, happy to have spent a fun day with his friends at the pond.

One day, a big cat and a little dog were playing near a wet pond. They were having lots of fun. The big cat said, "I can jump very high!" The little dog said, "I can run very fast!" Then, they saw a big fish in the wet pond. The big fish jumped out of the water and gave the big cat a little slap on the face. The big cat was very surprised. "Why did you do that?" asked the big cat. The big fish laughed and said, "I just wanted to play, too!" The big cat, little dog, and big fish all played together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a weird lawyer. He wore a big hat and had a long nose. He liked to show people his funny dance. One day, he met a little girl named Amy. "Hello, Amy," said the weird lawyer. "Do you want to see my funny dance?" "Yes, please!" said Amy, clapping her hands. The weird lawyer began to dance. He moved his arms and legs in a silly way. Amy laughed and clapped. She thought it was the best dance she had ever seen. The weird lawyer was happy to make Amy smile. From that day on, they became good friends. They played and danced together every day, and everyone loved the weird lawyer's funny dance.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. A little boy named Tom lived on the farm. He loved to play outside all day. One day, Tom saw a big cauliflower in the garden. He thought it was fair and pretty. He wanted to show it to his mom. Tom's mom told him that a fair was coming to town. She said, "If you take care of the cauliflower, it will grow bigger and we can take it to the fair." Tom was very happy. He watered the cauliflower every day and waited for the fair to arrive. Finally, the fair arrived. Tom and his mom took the big cauliflower to the fair. Many people saw the cauliflower and said it was the biggest one they had ever seen. Tom was very proud of his cauliflower. At the end of the day, Tom won a prize for the biggest cauliflower at the fair. He was so happy that he took care of it and it grew so big.
