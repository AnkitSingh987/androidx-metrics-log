Title: John and the Bowl

John was very excited because today was the day he was going to help Mummy make purple cupcakes. He watched as Mummy carefully cracked two eggs into a big bowl, and then poured in some milk. She took a fork, and then stirred and mixed the contents until it was a thick purple mixture. "John," Mummy said. "Remember how to help me?" John thought for a minute, then remembered. "I pour!" he said, pointing at the purple mixture. Mummy nodded. "That's right. You can help me by pouring the purple mixture into the baking tin." Excitedly, John grabbed the bowl and carefully poured the purple mixture into the baking tin. When he was finished, Mummy smiled proudly. "Look, John. You have made some delicious purple cupcakes."

Once upon a time, there was a brave little lion named Luke. He lived with his family in a big, green forest. Every day, the family would play together, eat lunch and have lots of fun. One day, Luke's family went to explore a dark cave. When they entered, Luke was scared and wanted to go home. He was brave though and decided to follow them. As they wandered through the cave, they were suddenly stopped by a mean dragon. The dragon started to threaten them and Luke's family was scared. But then, Luke stepped forward and said he would protect them. He said he wouldn't let the dragon hurt them. The dragon was so surprised that it left right away. Luke's family was so proud of him! They hugged him and told him how brave he was, and how much they loved him. Luke felt so happy that he had been able to protect them. He had resisted the dragon, and love had won!

Once upon a time, there was a smart little girl named Amy. She loved to eat toast for breakfast every day. Her mom would make it just right, with some butter on top. One night, Amy had a dream. In her dream, she saw a big, friendly bear. The bear also loved to eat toast. They sat down at a table and ate toast together. They laughed and talked as they ate their toast. When Amy woke up, she told her mom about her dream. Her mom smiled and made some toast for them to share. They both felt happy and loved, eating their toast just like Amy and the bear in her dream.

Mummy was very excited. It was Tuesday and she was off to take Patcy to a special place. Patcy jumped with joy and said "Where are we going Mummy?". Mummy smiled and said "we are going to the park". Patcy's eyes widened and she nodded with joy. When they arrived at the park, Patcy was full of wonder. She could see slides, swings and lots of colourful toys. She looked around, confused and asked Mummy "What can I play with?". Mummy pointed at the slide and said "Why don't you start with the slide?". Patcy nodded and giggled. She ran to the slide and started sliding down. The afternoon flew by and Patcy had enjoyed every minute. She looked up at Mummy and said "Can I come here every Tuesday?". Mummy smiled and nodded. Patcy smiled and hugged Mummy. Now she knew that every Tuesday would be just as exciting as this one.

Lily likes to play with her toy car. She makes it go zoom, zoom, zoom on the floor. She pretends it is a race car and she is the driver. She has a lot of fun. But sometimes, Lily has to pause. Pause means to stop for a little bit. Lily pauses when her mom calls her for lunch. She pauses when she has to go potty. She pauses when she hears a loud noise outside. One day, Lily pauses when she sees something bright in the sky. Bright means to shine a lot. Lily looks up and sees a big, yellow ball. It is the sun. Lily thinks the sun is pretty. She wonders what it is like to touch it. Lily wants to go to the sun. She thinks maybe her toy car can take her there. She puts her toy car on the window sill. She climbs on a chair. She reaches for her toy car. She says, "Start, car, start!" But the car does not start. It does not move. It does not make any sound. Lily is sad. She cannot go to the sun. She gets down from the chair. She picks up her toy car. She hugs it. She says, "I love you, car. You are still my favorite." Lily goes back to the floor. She plays with her toy car again. She makes it go zoom, zoom, zoom. She pretends it is a race car and she is the driver. She has a lot of fun. She does not pause anymore. She smiles.
