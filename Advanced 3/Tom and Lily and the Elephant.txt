Title: Tom and Lily and the Elephant

Tom and Lily went to the zoo with Mom and Dad. They saw many animals, big and small, loud and quiet, furry and scaly. Tom liked the lions and the tigers, and Lily liked the monkeys and the birds. They came to a cage with a strange animal. It had a long nose, big ears, and a gray skin. It was eating some hay. Tom asked, "What is that animal, Mom?" Mom said, "That is an elephant, Tom. It is very big and strong, and it can use its nose to pick up things and spray water." Tom wanted to see the elephant's nose up close. He went closer to the cage and reached out his hand. He poked the elephant's nose with his finger. The elephant felt the poke and lifted its nose. It looked at Tom with its small eyes. It was confused. Why did this little human poke its nose? It did not like it. It made a loud noise and sprayed some water at Tom. Tom was wet and scared. He ran back to Mom and Dad. Lily laughed. She said, "That was silly, Tom. You should not poke the elephant's nose. It does not like it." Mom and Dad hugged Tom and dried him with a towel. They said, "It's okay, Tom. The elephant was just surprised. You should be careful and respectful with the animals. They have feelings too." Tom nodded. He said, "I'm sorry, elephant. I did not mean to make you angry. You have a very cool nose." The elephant heard Tom and calmed down. It waved its nose at Tom and made a soft noise. It was not angry anymore. It was curious. Tom and Lily smiled. They waved back at the elephant and said, "Bye, elephant. You are very cool too."

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a pale girl named Lily. She loved to play the piano. Every day, she would sit and make pretty music for her family. One sunny day, Lily wanted to gather flowers for her mom. She went outside to the garden. She saw red, blue, and yellow flowers. She picked some of each color and made a nice bunch. Lily came back inside and put the flowers on the piano. Then, she played a happy song. Her mom smiled and hugged her. They all enjoyed the pretty flowers and sweet music together.

Sara and Ben were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big leather ball that their dad gave them. They liked to kick the ball, throw the ball, and roll the ball. One day, their mom said, "It's time for your nap, Sara and Ben. You need to rest and grow." Sara and Ben did not want to nap. They wanted to play with their leather ball. They said, "No, mom, we are not tired. We want to play more." Their mom was polite and patient. She said, "I know you love your ball, but you also need to sleep. How about this? You can take your ball with you to your bed, and I will read you a story. Then you can nap with your ball." Sara and Ben thought about it. They liked stories, and they liked their ball. They agreed to go to their bed with their ball. Their mom read them a story about a brave lion who also liked to nap. Sara and Ben listened to the story and cuddled with their ball. Soon, they fell asleep. Their mom smiled and kissed them. She was proud of them for being polite and listening to her. She knew they would wake up happy and ready to play again. She also knew they learned a lesson: napping is good for you and your ball.

Once upon a time, there was a kind teacher named Miss Lily. She wanted to design a beautiful classroom for her students. She thought about using different colors and fun things to make the children happy. Miss Lily went to the store and bought different colored paints. She painted the walls of the classroom with blue, green, yellow, and red. She also found many fun toys and games for the children to play with. The next day, the children came to the new classroom. They were so excited to see the different colors and play with the new toys. They thanked Miss Lily for designing such a fun classroom. And they all had a great day learning and playing together.
