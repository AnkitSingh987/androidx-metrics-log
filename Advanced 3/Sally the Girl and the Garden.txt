Title: Sally the Girl and the Garden

One day, a little girl named Sally went for a walk. She saw a pretty rose in a garden. The rose was red and attractive. Sally wanted to touch the rose, but she was scared. She did not dare to touch it because it had sharp points. Sally saw a friendly bee near the rose. The bee said, "Hello, I can help you. I can get the rose for you." Sally was happy and said, "Thank you, bee. I like the rose, but I am scared of the sharp points." The bee went to the rose and took it for Sally. The bee was careful not to touch the sharp points. Sally was very happy and said, "Thank you, bee. You are very nice." The bee and Sally became good friends and played in the garden all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a toy cat named Luna. Luna was very dry and needed a massage. Amy wanted to help Luna. Amy tried to reach for her mommy's lotion. It was too high for her. Amy climbed on a chair to reach it. She got the lotion and gave Luna a nice massage. After the massage, Luna felt much better. Amy was happy that she could help her toy cat. They played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She loved to learn new things. Her dad said, "Lucy, one day you can go to a big school called a university." Lucy was very excited. One day, Lucy and her friend Tom were playing. Tom said, "I want to go to the university too, but it is very expensive." Lucy thought for a moment and then she had an idea. "Why don't we ask our moms and dads to help us save money?" she said. Tom liked the idea and they both went to talk to their parents. Lucy and Tom's parents were happy to help. They all worked together to save money for the university. The kids did small jobs to help too. After a long time, they had saved enough money for Lucy and Tom to go to the university. They were so happy and thankful for their parents' help.

Once upon a time, there were two friends: a rabbit and a pig. Every morning, the rabbit would go to the pond to wash and then go to the pig's house for lunch. The pig would make a special mug ready for the rabbit and the mug was very impressive. One day, when the rabbit came over to the pig's house, he saw that the special mug was not there. He asked the pig, "Where is the special mug? Are you going to make it today?" and the pig said, "No, I forgot to make it." The rabbit was sad and told the pig, "If you forget something, we can't be friends." The pig quickly took action, ran to the pond, and washed the special mug for the rabbit. The rabbit was very happy about the pig's effort and he smiled. The lesson here is that friendship is only strengthened when one puts effort into it.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl called Daisy. Daisy wanted to play in the garden. She asked her mum if she could and her mum said yes, she could. Daisy ran outside with a smile on her face. The garden was full of flowers. She loved the smell and the pretty colours. When she went to the bottom of the garden, she found a jelly. It was so big and squishy. She thought it looked tasty. Daisy asked her mum if she could eat the jelly. Her mum said no, she couldn't. Daisy asked why not and her mum said it was scary. She said it was important not to eat things if they look scary. Daisy agreed and went back to playing in the garden. The end.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk with his boy, Tim. Tim put a leash on Max to keep him safe. They walked down the street and saw many things. They saw big trees, pretty flowers, and other kids playing. As they walked, they saw a strange rock. The rock had a soft glow. Tim and Max looked at the rock, and Tim thought it was really cool. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. When they got home, Tim showed the glowing rock to his mom. His mom said, "That's a stupid rock. It's just a toy." But Tim and Max loved the glowing rock. They played with it all day and had lots of fun.
