Title: The Boy Called John and the Park

Once upon a time there was a little boy called John. One day, he went to the park with his dad. When he got there, he noticed something good. He saw a big wood. He ran to it and started to hug it. His dad said, "John, that is so nice of you!" John was so happy and grateful. They went to the the park again. And again, John noticed the wood. Each time he went, he gave the wood a hug and his dad said, "John, that's so nice of you!" One day, John asked his dad, "Can we take some of the wood home." His dad said, "Yes, John, we can do that." So they took some of the wood home and John said, "I am so grateful for the wood." John used the wood to build a friends. He said, "That's the best thing I have ever built!" His dad was so proud and said, "John, you did a great job!" John was happy and grateful. From that day on, John made sure to notice the wood every time he went to the park. He was always so grateful to have the wood in his life.

But it was locked and she couldn't open it. She wondered what the mystery was inside. Lillie tried asking her friends for help, but none of them could figure it out. She was sad and disappointed. Just then, an older boy came along. He had a friendly smile and he said kindly, "Come, Lillie. Let us unite and open this box." "Really?" asked Lillie, not quite sure. "Yes, really," said the boy. "Together, we can do it!" So the two of them worked together and soon, they had the box open. Inside, they found a magical, harmless surprise! It was truly something special. The End.

Once upon a time there was a magical crystal that was very unique and special. It held many secrets that no one had ever seen before. One day, two children, Jim and Tina, were walking along a path when they noticed the crystal in the distance. They stopped and stared in amazement. "Wow," said Jim. "That looks amazing. Let's go and check it out!" So the two children ran towards the crystal and as they got closer, they realised that something special was about to happen. Suddenly, the crystal began to move and a voice said, "if you want to release the secrets of the crystal, you must give a special hug to it." Jim and Tina looked at each other and smiled, before giving the crystal a big hug. As the hug ended, a beautiful light shone from the crystal and all of its secrets were released. Jim and Tina gasped in surprise and delight as they realised how truly unique the crystal was! They stayed there until the sun began to set, before they had to leave and go home. But they never forgot the magical crystal and its unique secrets.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy drum. Tim would slap the drum with his hands to make a rhythm. He would play it all day long, making fun sounds. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. Sue saw Tim's filthy hands from playing in the dirt. "Ew, your hands are dirty!" she said. Tim looked at his hands and said, "Oops! I will wash them." After washing his hands, Tim and Sue played the drum together. They took turns slapping it, making a fun rhythm. They laughed and danced to the beat. They had a great time playing with the drum, and from that day on, Tim always washed his hands before playing.
