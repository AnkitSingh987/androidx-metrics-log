Title: Ben and Lily and the Toys

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play with their toys. But they did not like to clean up their toys when they were done. They left them all over the floor, on the bed, and on the table. Their room was very messy. One day, their mom gave them each a penny. She said, "This is a special penny. If you put it under your pillow at night, it will grow and become bigger. But you have to do something good first. You have to clean up your room and make it nice and tidy." Ben and Lily were excited. They wanted to see their pennies grow. They ran to their room and started to pick up their toys. They put them in the toy box, on the shelves, and in the drawers. They made their bed and dusted the table. They worked hard and fast. When they were done, they looked at their room. It was not messy anymore. It was clean and neat. They felt happy and proud. They said to each other, "We did a good job. Now our pennies will grow." They put their pennies under their pillows and went to sleep. The next morning, they woke up and checked their pillows. They were surprised and happy. Their pennies had grown and become bigger. They were now quarters. They ran to their mom and showed her their quarters. She smiled and hugged them. She said, "I'm so proud of you. You did a good thing. You cleaned up your room and made it nice and tidy. And you learned a lesson. When you do something good, good things happen to you." Ben and Lily nodded and smiled. They learned their lesson. They decided to keep their room clean and neat from then on. And they also decided to save their quarters for something special.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an amazing toy automobile. He loved to play with it all day long. He would push it around the room and make it go fast. One day, Tim's amazing automobile got lost. He looked for it everywhere but could not find it. Tim was very sad. He told his mom, "Mom, I can't find my automobile." His mom said, "Let's pray and ask for help to find it." So, Tim and his mom closed their eyes and prayed. After they prayed, Tim looked under his bed and found his amazing automobile! He was so happy. Tim said, "Thank you, Mom, for helping me pray. I found my automobile!" They both smiled and played with the automobile together.

The little boy was walking in the park, looking for something special. He had a memory of when he visited the park with his mother. That had been a very happy time. He wanted to find something he could treasure as a special memory of his own. Suddenly, he spotted something that glinted at him in a nearby tree. He ran over to take a closer look and saw that it was a shiny silver coin. He was so proud when he managed to climb up and pick it up. He gasped in amazement at his precious find. Suddenly, he felt a hand on his shoulder and heard a voice say, "Well done! I knew you could do it!" He spun around to see his mother's smiling face. His mother had always been proud of him and encouraged him to do his best. It looked like she had known he would find the coin before he did. She gave him a big hug and said, "I'm so glad you have this special memory."

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy cars in the garden. They liked to make noises and pretend they were racing. Lily had a red car and Ben had a blue car. They were having fun. But then they saw some smoke coming from the house. It was black and smelly. They heard a loud beep-beep-beep. They were scared. "Mama! Papa!" they shouted. They ran to the house. They saw Mama and Papa in the kitchen. Mama was holding a pan with a twisty cloth. Papa was opening the window. "What happened?" Lily asked. "I was making some eggs for lunch, but I forgot to watch them. They burned and made smoke. The twisty cloth is to stop the fire. The beep-beep-beep is the alarm. It tells us to be careful," Mama explained. "Are you okay?" Ben asked. "Yes, we are okay. But we are very tired. We need to rest and clean up. Can you help us?" Papa asked. Lily and Ben nodded. They felt sorry for Mama and Papa. They wanted to help. They took their toy cars and put them away. They helped Mama and Papa to wash the dishes and wipe the table. They gave them hugs and kisses. They were good children. They made Mama and Papa happy.
