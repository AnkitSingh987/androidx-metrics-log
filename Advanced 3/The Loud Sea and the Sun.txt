Title: The Loud Sea and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a loud sea. On it, there was a fish called Fred. Fred liked to pinch. He pinched the waves, he pinched the rocks and he pinched the water. One day, Fred decided to pinch the sun. He said to the sun, "I'm gonna pinch you!" But the sun was way too high and Fred couldn't reach it. Suddenly, Fred had an idea. He swam and he swam until he saw a tall tree. He climbed up the tree until he reached the top. He pinched the sun and the sky lit up with colors. Everyone on the sea was amazed. Fred was so happy he pinched the sun. He celebrated by pinching the waves one more time and swimming back to his home. He told everyone what he had done and everyone cheered. From then on, Fred was known as the pincher of the loud sea!

Once upon a time, there was a brave lion called Freddy. He was a noble lion who springed through the forest searching for food during the day. One day, Freddy found a tight apple tree that had juicy, red apples on it. With a single spring, Freddy hopped onto the tree and munched away at delicious apples. Just then, a little mouse scurried from the bushes and asked, “What are you doing up there, Freddy?” Freddy the lion replied, “I found this delicious apple tree and I am eating the apples. Do you want some?” “Yes please!” squeaked the little mouse excitedly. So Freddy the brave lion reach down and carefully offered the little mouse a tight, juicy apple before continuing to eat as much as he liked from the tree. The brave lion and the small mouse had a wonderful snack together before they went their separate ways.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play upstairs in her room. One day, she found a big, empty box. She thought it would be fun to fill the box with her toys. Lily started to put her toys in the box. She put her teddy bear, her toy car, and her doll in the box. The box began to weigh more and more. Lily was happy that her toys had a new home. When the box was full, Lily tried to carry it downstairs. But it was too heavy for her. So, she asked her big brother, Tom, for help. Tom helped Lily carry the heavy box downstairs. They put the box in the living room and opened it. Lily and Tom played with the toys all day long.

One day, a jolly tornado came to town. It saw a little girl named Sally. Sally was not scared of the tornado. She wanted to talk to it. "Hi, tornado!" Sally said. "Do you want to play with me?" The tornado was happy to hear Sally talk to it. It said, "Yes, I would love to play with you!" Sally and the jolly tornado played all day. They ran and jumped and had a lot of fun. The tornado was so happy to have a friend to talk to. Sally was happy too. At the end of the day, the tornado had to go. Sally said, "Bye, tornado! Come back soon!" The tornado smiled and said, "I will, Sally. I had a great time!" And so, the jolly tornado left the town. Sally knew that her new friend would come back to play and talk with her again one day.

One day, a gentle cat and a dog went for a walk. They saw a rail near a big tree. The cat said, "Let's celebrate on the rail!" The dog nodded and wagged his tail. They jumped on the rail and started to dance. The cat sang, "We celebrate, we celebrate, we dance all day!" The dog barked and danced too. They were very happy. But then, the rail broke. The cat and the dog fell down. They cried and went home. Their fun day was over.

There was a big piano in the room. A little girl named Amy wanted to play it. She felt miserable because she did not know how to play the piano. One day, a nice lady came to Amy's house. The lady said, "I can teach you to play the piano. We will add new notes every day." Amy felt happy and said, "Yes, please teach me!" Each day, Amy and the lady played the piano together. They added more notes and songs. Soon, Amy could play many songs. She was not miserable anymore. She loved to play the piano and share her music with others.
