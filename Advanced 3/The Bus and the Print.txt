Title: The Bus and the Print

Once upon a time, there was a bus. It was a big bus, and it was cold. Soon, the bus was driving to a new place. It was very exciting! Along the way, the bus slowed down and stopped. Everyone stepped off the bus. The people on the bus went inside a big building. On an old machine, they could print pictures. They could pick any picture they wanted to print. The kids were very excited to print a picture. Everyone chose a picture and printed it. When they were finished printing, the bus was ready! Everyone hopped back inside the bus and waved goodbye to the building. Then, the bus drove away and drove off to the next new place.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little jellyfish named Jilly. She lived in the big blue sea with her fish friends. Jilly loved to play, but she was too shy to join in the fun games. One sunny day, Jilly saw her friends splashing in the water. They were having so much fun! Jilly wanted to join, but she was scared. She hid behind a big rock and watched them play. Then, a friendly fish named Sam swam up to Jilly. Sam said, "Hi Jilly! Do you want to come and splash with us?" Jilly was still shy, but she nodded her head. Sam held her hand and they swam together. Jilly and Sam joined the other fish and started to splash. Jilly felt so happy! She was not shy anymore. From that day on, Jilly and her friends splashed and played together in the big blue sea. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a messy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the mud and dig in the dirt. One day, while playing, Spot found a pretty shell. He picked it up in his mouth and showed it to his friend, a little bird named Tweet. "Look, Tweet! I found a shell!" said Spot. Tweet looked at the shell and smiled. "That's a nice shell, Spot. Let's follow the path and see if we find more shells!" said Tweet. So, Spot and Tweet followed the path. They found more shells and had lots of fun. They also found a big, messy puddle to play in. Spot and Tweet splashed and laughed all day. They were very happy with their messy adventure and the pretty shells they found.

One sunny day, a little bird sat on a branch. The bird was happy because the weather was mild. It liked to sing and play with its friends. A small girl named Lily saw the bird. She said, "Hi, bird! You look happy. Can we play together?" The bird nodded and sang a pretty song for Lily. They played and laughed all day long. As the sun went down, Lily gave the bird a big hug. She said, "Thank you, bird, for being my friend today. I hope we can play again soon." The bird sang one last song and flew away, looking forward to the next time they would meet.

Once there was a little frog who lived in a pond. All the other frogs had a long, slippery tail and could swim quickly. But the little frog had a short, stubby tail! He wished and wished for a long tail so he could swim with the other frogs. One day, the little frog decided he was going to keep trying, he was so very persistent! He gathered up all his courage and he tried to get a longer tail. He yelled and shouted, he hopped and jumped, but he just couldn't seem to get a longer tail. Then, he sneezed! He tried again and again and the more he sneezed - the longer his tail seemed to get! The other frogs were so surprised! But the little frog was determined, eventually he had a long, slippery tail like all the other frogs. The moral of the story is: if you keep trying and don't give up, you can reach your goals. No matter what it is, if you are persistent and keep trying you can succeed!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a lucky boy. He had a big, fun family. They loved to play games and laugh together. One day, Tim's family went to see a firework show. Tim was very excited. He had never seen fireworks before. His family told him, "You will believe how pretty they are!" Tim could not wait to see them. When the fireworks started, Tim was so happy. The fireworks went "boom" and "bang" in the sky. They were very pretty. Tim felt very lucky to have his family and to see the fireworks. He knew he would always believe in the magic of fireworks.
