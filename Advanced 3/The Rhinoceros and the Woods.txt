Title: The Rhinoceros and the Woods

Once upon a time there was a rhinoceros. He lived in a forest and was very happy. One day, he was walking around the woods and he saw something very hard in front of him. He looked closer, and it was a diamond! He was very excited and he wanted to keep it for himself. He quickly grabbed the diamond, but just then it vanished! He was very sad, he thought he would get to keep the diamond. The rhinoceros soon realized that he did not need the diamond to be happy. Even without it, he still was happy because of all the things around him. He decided to stay in the woods, so he wouldn't forget what had happened. The moral of the story is that material things cannot make us really happy.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient finger. One day, it appeared in front of two 3-year old children. The children were shock-struck and looked at each other. “What is this?” asked one of them. “I don’t know,” the other said. “It’s so ancient.” Suddenly, the finger began to move. The two kids jumped in surprise. “Don’t be scared,” said the finger. “I’m here to show you something.” The two kids looked at each other and then nodded. The finger then pointed to a nearby temple. “There is a great secret there,” said the finger. “Let’s go explore and see what’s inside!” The kids followed the finger, excited to learn what the secret was. After they arrived, they were surprise to find a big treasure. “This is amazing!” they both shouted. The finger smiled and said, “This is the end of your adventure. Take the treasure with you!” The kids said goodbye to the ancient finger and took the treasure home.

One day, a big cow with a horn was in a field. She was a spoiled cow who did not like to share. She liked to eat all the grass and flowers by herself. She did not let the other cows eat with her. A little bird saw the big cow and said, "Why do you not share with the other cows? They are hungry too." The big cow did not listen. She kept eating and eating. The next day, the big cow did not wake up. The little bird flew to her and said, "Wake up! Wake up! Something is wrong!" The big cow opened her eyes and saw that her horn was gone! She was sad and did not know what to do. The little bird said, "I will help you find your horn if you promise to share with the other cows." The big cow agreed. They looked and looked until they found the horn. The big cow put her horn back on and shared with the other cows. She was not spoiled anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a bright red train. The train loved to go fast on the tracks. One day, the train met a little boy named Tim. Tim was very happy to see the train. "Hi, train!" said Tim. "Can you help me unpack my toys?" The train was happy to help. They unpacked all of Tim's toys together. The train and Tim became good friends. The train and Tim played with the toys every day. They learned that when you help others, you make new friends. And that's how the bright red train and Tim had lots of fun and learned a good lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a huge piano in a big room. It was so big that it could make the whole room shake when someone played it. A little boy named Tim loved to listen to the music from the huge piano. Every day, he would sit and listen to the beautiful sounds. One day, Tim saw a big shadow on the wall. He felt scared, but he did not know what it was. He kept listening to the piano, but the shadow grew bigger and bigger. The music from the piano started to make the room shake more and more. Tim tried to run away from the shaking room, but he was too slow. The huge piano fell over and made a very loud noise. Tim was very sad and scared because the beautiful music was gone. The big shadow was the huge piano falling, and now the room was quiet and still.
