Title: The Girl Called Lucy and the Slide

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She loved to play outside in the winter. One day it was very icy and she wanted to make some slides. So she went outside and found some plastic. She tried to really hard to make a slide but the plastic was not sliding. Then Lucy started to get mad and threw the icy plastic away. She was sad and felt like she will never be able to make a slide. She started to cry, but then she heard a voice. It was her mom. She said, “Lucy, if you work hard and stay positive, you can do it!” So Lucy worked and worked until finally, the icy plastic began to slide! Lucy was so proud and happy. She had a huge smile on her face and said, “I did it!” She spent the rest of the day having fun and sliding on her icy slide.

Once upon a time there was a big nation in a far away place. One day, two birds came to the nation. One of the birds said to the other, "Let's start a fun adventure!" The other bird agreed and they started their journey. As they flew over the land, they saw a giant mountain and lots of animals running around. The birds were so amazed. Next, they saw a big lake and duck swimming around. One of the ducks said, "I'm so glad you came to visit us. Let's start a race around the lake!" The birds smiled and picked the fastest bird to race with the duck. They began the race and the birds cheered on their side. After a few minutes, the duck won the race. Everyone was very proud. The birds thanked the duck before they flew away. They knew they had shared a special moment with the nation. The two birds shared many more adventures, but they never forgot the big nation they had visited.

One day, a bright sun was in the sky. A boy named Tim saw a trumpet on the ground. He picked it up and wanted to play it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I play the trumpet?" His mom said, "Yes, I will allow you to play the trumpet, but only outside." Tim went outside and played the trumpet. It was loud and fun. He saw a dog come to him. The dog liked the trumpet too. Tim played the trumpet for the dog. The dog danced and wagged its tail. They had a great time. Tim was happy he found the trumpet and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a jolly little town, there was a beautiful sunset. The sky was full of colors like red, orange, and pink. All the people in the town loved to watch the sunset together. One day, a new friend came to the town. His name was Tim. Tim wanted to see the sunset too. The people in the town were happy to have a new friend. They all recommend that Tim should watch the sunset with them. But Tim did not listen to his new friends. He wanted to watch the sunset all by himself. He walked far away from the town to see the colors in the sky. When the sun went down, it was very dark. Tim was all alone and he could not find his way back to the town. He was sad and scared. And that is how the story ends.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Jack and Joe. They were walking in the park together when they encountered a small bird. Jack looked at the bird and noticed that it had a very filthy eye. Jack said to Joe, "Let's offer the bird some help." Joe agreed and they both carefully approached the bird. Jack said to the bird, "We can help you. Can we offer you a better place to live?" The bird looked at them with its one good eye and replied, "Yes, please!" So, Jack and Joe offered to take the bird to a cleaner and safer place. They took the bird to the forest and gave it a huge tree to stay in. The bird happily thanked Jack and Joe and flew away, no longer living in a filthy place. Jack and Joe smiled, happy that they had offered their help.
