Title: Lily and the Bird

Lily liked to watch the motorcycles zoom by on the road. She thought they were fast and loud and cool. She wanted to ride one someday, but her mom said they were too dangerous for little girls. One day, she saw a motorcycle parked outside the store. It was shiny and red and had a big helmet on the seat. Lily ran over to touch it, but a man came out of the store and saw her. "Hey, what are you doing?" he asked. He looked angry and scary. Lily was scared and ran back to her mom. She started to cry and tell her what happened. Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lily. You shouldn't touch things that don't belong to you. That's not nice or polite. The man was probably worried that you would hurt his motorcycle or yourself. Next time, you can ask him if you can look at it, but don't touch it without permission." Lily nodded and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I just wanted to see the motorcycle. It's so pretty and graceful. It looks like a bird flying." Her mom smiled and said, "I know, honey. Motorcycles are very graceful, but they are also very powerful and dangerous. You have to be careful and respectful around them. Maybe when you are older, you can learn how to ride one safely. But for now, you can enjoy watching them from a distance." Lily agreed and said, "Okay, mom. Thank you for telling me. I love you." Her mom said, "I love you too, Lily. Now let's go home and have some cookies."

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was called Annie. Annie was very scared one day when she saw a diary on the floor. She did not know what it was. All of a sudden a big gust of wind came through the room and scattered the pages of the diary all over the floor. Annie was frightened and ran away screaming. Later she found out that the diary belonged to her mother. It was full of pictures of Annie when she was a baby and stories about all the fun things they did together. Annie was so happy that she went to her mother and gave her a big hug. Annie and her mother decided that if Annie ever felt frightened again, she would look at the diary and think about all the lovely times they had shared.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim found a key. The key was from an unknown place. Tim did not know what the key could open. He was very curious. He showed the key to his friends, and they all wanted to find out where the key came from. One day, Tim and his friends went on a walk. They wanted to unite and find the secret door that the key could open. They looked everywhere in the park, but they could not find any door. They were tired and sad. Just as they were about to give up, a big wind blew the key from Tim's hand. The key flew into a bush. Tim and his friends went to the bush to get the key back. They found a small door hidden inside the bush! The key fit perfectly, and the door opened. Behind the door, they found a secret playground just for them. They were so happy and played together all day long. The key had brought them to a magical place they never expected.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, while playing, the ball rolled near a dangerous road. Spot's friend, a little girl named Lily, saw the ball near the dangerous road. She knew it was not safe for Spot to get it by himself. Lily said, "Spot, don't go! It's dangerous!" Spot barked and waited. Lily looked around and saw a long stick. She used the stick to rescue the ball from the dangerous road. Spot wagged his tail happily. He was glad that Lily saved his ball. After that, Lily broke a piece of her cookie and gave it to Spot as a treat. They both sat under a tree and shared the cookie. Spot and Lily were happy that they could play safely together again.
