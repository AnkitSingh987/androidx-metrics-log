Title: Tim the Gifted Boy and the Sam

Once upon a time, there was a gifted boy named Tim. Tim liked to organize his toys and his pennies. One day, he found a penny on the ground. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. Tim walked to his friend, Sam, and said, "Look, I found a penny!" Sam was sad because he did not have any pennies. Tim thought for a moment and said, "You can have my penny, Sam!" Sam was very happy, and they played together all day. Tim learned that sharing is good and makes friends happy. And from that day on, Tim and Sam were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tall sausage. He wanted to pass by the forest. He wiggled and hopped, and hopped and wiggled. He passed the trees and the flowers. He saw the butterflies and the bees. He passed a pond, a rock, and some sand. He felt a bit tired, so he stopped to rest. Suddenly, a big bird flew past him. It was so tall, it almost touched the sky! The sausage was so amazed, he wanted to follow the bird. He hopped and wiggled some more, but he just couldn't keep up. The bird was too fast and too far. So, the sausage just kept walking until he passed the forest. He was so happy he didn't give up, and he knew he could make it. He was proud of himself!

Once upon a time, there was a unique alligator. This alligator was not like the others. It had many colors on its skin. It was very pretty. One day, the alligator went to the park to play. It wanted to display its colors to all the other animals. The alligator was very happy and proud. All the animals in the park liked the unique alligator. They played with it all day. The alligator was very happy. It had many new friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dancer named Lily. She loved to dance and was always eager to learn new moves. One day, her mom told her they were going to pick apples at the farm. Lily was excited because she had never been to a farm before. At the farm, Lily and her mom started to pick apples. Lily was having so much fun that she started to dance around the trees. She met a little boy named Tom, who was also picking apples. Tom saw Lily dancing and said, "I want to dance too! Can you show me how?" Lily smiled and said, "Of course! Let's dance together." As Lily and Tom danced around the farm, they found a secret door behind a big tree. They opened the door and found a room full of dancers! The dancers were practicing for a big show. Lily and Tom were surprised and happy. They joined the dancers and had the best time of their lives. When it was time to go home, Lily and Tom knew they had found a magical place. They promised to come back and dance with their new friends again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat meat. One day, her mom made a big plate of meat for dinner. Sue was very happy. But when Sue took a bite, the meat was very harsh. Sue did not like the taste. She started to weep. Her mom saw her weeping and asked, "Sue, why are you weeping?" Sue said, "The meat is harsh, Mom, I don't like it." Her mom then told her a story about kids who had no food to eat. They were very sad and hungry. Sue listened and felt bad for them. She stopped weeping and ate the harsh meat. Sue learned that it is good to be thankful for the food we have.

Once upon a time there was a boy. He had a red rag. He liked to push the rag around. He pushed the rag across the floor. He pushed the rag up the stairs. He even pushed the rag out the door. It was fun, but it made him feel a bit uncomfortable. His Mommy said no more pushing the rag. So the boy had to find something else to do. He decided to go find a kitty and pet it instead. He knew the kitty wouldn't make him feel uncomfortable. He found a kitty, and they had lots of fun together!
