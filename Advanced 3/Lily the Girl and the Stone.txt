Title: Lily the Girl and the Stone

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk with her daddy. They walked through the ordinary park near their house. Lily loved to play in the park with her daddy. They played on the swings and the slide. They had so much fun together. As they were walking, Lily saw something shiny on the ground. She pointed at it and said, "Daddy, look!" Her daddy bent down and picked it up. He discovered it was a pretty, shiny stone. "Wow, Lily, this is a special stone!" he said. Lily took the stone from her daddy and smiled. "I want to keep it," she said. Her daddy agreed, and they continued their walk in the park. The shiny stone made their ordinary day feel extra special.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to school. She was excited because today was math day. Sue loved math and wanted to learn more. Her teacher, Mr. Brown, was very kind and had a colorful room. During math time, Mr. Brown said, "Okay kids, let's count from one to ten." Sue was happy and started counting, "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!" But then, something unexpected happened. Sue's friend, Tom, said, "Sue, you miss nine!" Sue was surprised. "Oh no, I miss nine?" she asked. Tom nodded and said, "Yes, you said eight and then ten." Sue felt sad, but Mr. Brown told her, "It's okay, Sue. Let's try again." So Sue tried again and this time, she didn't miss nine. Everyone clapped and Sue felt happy.

One day, a little girl named Ann was playing in her room. She had a toy box with many toys. She wanted to find her favorite toy, a small bunny. Ann looked and looked, but she could not find the bunny. It was hidden under her bed. She felt sad. Ann's mommy came into the room and saw her sad face. "What's wrong, Ann?" she asked. Ann told her mommy that she could not find her bunny. Her mommy said, "Let's look together." They looked in the toy box, under the bed, and behind the books. Then, Ann realized her bunny was hiding behind the green broccoli toy! Ann was very happy to find her bunny. She said, "Thank you, mommy!" But then, her mommy saw the broccoli toy and had an idea. "Ann, why don't we eat some real broccoli for dinner tonight?" Ann did not like broccoli. She made a face and said, "No, mommy. I don't want to eat broccoli." Her mommy smiled and said, "If you try the broccoli, I will give you a surprise after dinner." Ann thought about it and agreed. At dinner, she ate her broccoli and found out it was not so bad. After dinner, her mommy gave her a new toy - a big, soft bunny! Ann was very happy and hugged her mommy. She learned that trying new things can be good.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved candy very much. One day, he saw a big candy store and wanted to go inside. Tim asked his mom, "Can we go in? Please let me get some candy." His mom said yes, and they went into the candy store. Tim was so happy to see all the candy. He picked some red candy, blue candy, and yellow candy. When they got home, Tim ate some of the red candy. But it tasted terrible! He did not like it at all. Tim learned that not all candy is good. He decided to share the other candy with his friends and hoped they would like it more than he did.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, he went for a walk in the icy cold park. As he walked, he saw a big tree. Under the tree, he met a small, lost dog. The dog was shivering from the cold. "Hi, dog! Are you lost?" Tim asked. The dog barked and wagged its tail. Tim knew he had to help the dog find its home. So, they walked together, looking for the dog's house. After some time, they found the dog's house. The dog's owner was very happy to see her dog again. She said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping my dog come home!" She gave Tim a reward: a big, warm hug and a yummy cookie. Tim and the dog were happy, and they became good friends.
