Title: John and the Balloon

John and his sister, Jane, were very troubled. They both felt sad and alone. But then one day, their mom brought home a special balloon for them. It had a friendly smiley face on it, and special colors. John and Jane hugged the balloon and it made them feel better. John and Jane went outside to play with the balloon. They chased it around the yard, laughing and giggling. Suddenly, the balloon began to drift away! John and Jane started to worry, so they ran and ran until they lost sight of the balloon. John and Jane were very sad. But then their mom came outside to comfort them. She hugged them both and told them that they were loved. She said even when they don't see it, she and their balloon are always with them. John and Jane felt better and smiled. They knew that everything was going to be alright.

One day, a big cat and a small dog were playing in the park. The cat had a mug with her. She liked to show her mug to everyone because it was very pretty. The dog was poor and did not have a mug. The cat said, "Look at my pretty mug, I like to show it to everyone!" The small dog was sad because he did not have a pretty mug to show. He looked at the big cat and said, "I wish I had a pretty mug to show too." The big cat thought about it and had an idea. She said, "Let's go find you a pretty mug too!" The big cat and the small dog went to look for a pretty mug for the dog. They looked high and low, but they could not find a mug. Then, they saw a very old lady who needed help. She was trying to carry a heavy bag. The big cat and the small dog helped the old lady carry her bag. The old lady was so happy that she gave the small dog a pretty mug as a thank you. The small dog was so happy to have a pretty mug to show. The big cat and the small dog showed their mugs to everyone in the park. They learned that helping others can bring good things to you. And they played happily with their pretty mugs ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a beautiful landscape with many trees, flowers, and animals. Lucy felt happy to be outside in the sun. As she walked, she saw a poor, sad bird sitting on a branch. Lucy asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird replied, "I can't count how many friends I have, and it makes me feel lonely." Lucy wanted to help the bird, so she said, "I can count your friends for you!" The bird felt better and called for his friends. One by one, the bird's friends came, and Lucy counted them. "One, two, three, four, five!" she said. The bird was happy to know he had five friends. He thanked Lucy and they all played together in the beautiful landscape.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom was a very big cat who loved to eat. One day, Tom had a bad dream, a nightmare. In his dream, he was so big that he could not fit through the door to get his food. The next day, Tom was very scared. He did not want to be too big. So, he decided to eat less and play more. He played with his toys and ran around the house. Tom was having so much fun playing. One day, while playing, Tom knocked over a plant. The plant fell and made a big mess. Tom felt very bad and embarrassed. But then, something unexpected happened. Tom's owner came and saw the mess. Instead of being mad, she laughed and hugged Tom. She was happy that Tom was playing and not just eating all day.

Lily loved to look at the lovely butterflies in the garden. She liked their bright colors and how they flew from flower to flower. She wanted to have a butterfly of her own, but she knew she could not catch them. One day, she saw a big butterfly with blue and yellow wings. It was the most beautiful butterfly she had ever seen. She followed it with her eyes until it landed on a pink rose. She tiptoed closer and reached out her hand. "Hello, butterfly," she whispered. "Can I borrow you for a little while? I just want to hold you and look at you. I will be very gentle and I will give you back." The butterfly did not answer. It did not move. Lily thought it was sleeping. She carefully picked it up and held it in her palm. She smiled and felt happy. But then, the butterfly woke up. It was scared and confused. It did not like to be borrowed. It wanted to fly away. It flapped its wings and tried to escape. "Ow!" Lily cried. The butterfly had scratched her finger with its sharp legs. She dropped it and it flew away. She felt sad and sorry. She ran to her mom and showed her the scratch. Her mom hugged her and put a band-aid on it. "It's okay, Lily," her mom said. "Butterflies are not for borrowing. They are for watching and admiring. They are free and happy in the garden. Next time, just say hello and let them be." Lily nodded. She learned her lesson. She still loved butterflies, but she never tried to borrow them again. She watched them from a distance and enjoyed their lovely colors and movements. She was happy, too.
