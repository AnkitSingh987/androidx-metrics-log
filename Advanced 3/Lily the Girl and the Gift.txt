Title: Lily the Girl and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends and share her toys. One day, she found a big envelope on the ground. She opened it and saw a lot of pretty pictures inside. Lily thought they were very attractive. Lily wanted to show the pictures to her friends. But her mom insisted that she should find the owner of the envelope first. Lily was not happy about it, but she listened to her mom. They went around asking people if they lost an envelope with pretty pictures. Finally, they found the owner of the envelope. It was a kind old lady who was very happy to see her pictures again. The old lady thanked Lily and her mom and gave Lily a special gift. The gift was a beautiful toy that Lily had never seen before. The moral of the story is to always do the right thing, even if it is not what you want to do. Sometimes, when you help others, you get something nice in return.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Bob and Sue. One day, Bob said to Sue, "I have a joke for you." Sue was excited, so she said, "Tell me!" Bob revealed the joke to Sue. They both laughed and laughed. Suddenly, Bob heard someone coming. He was scared and whispered to Sue, "Hide!" Sue hid just in time and Bob pretended nothing was happening. The door opened and in came Mr. Smith. He asked, "What are you two up to?" Bob said, "Oh, nothing!" Mr. Smith smiled and said, "I think I heard laughter. What's the joke?" Bob was scared, but he told Mr. Smith the joke. Mr. Smith laughed too and said, "That was a good joke!" Sue giggled and then they all laughed together. And that is the story of Bob and Sue's joke!

Tom likes to help his mom in the kitchen. He likes to polish the spoons and forks with a napkin. He thinks it is important to make them shiny and clean. Mom says he is a good helper. One day, Tom finds a big spoon in the drawer. It is very dirty and dull. Tom wants to polish it with his napkin. He rubs and rubs, but the spoon does not shine. He wonders what is wrong with it. He shows the spoon to his mom. Mom smiles and says, "That is not a spoon, Tom. That is a ladle. We use it to scoop soup. It is made of wood, not metal. You cannot polish wood with a napkin. But you can wash it with soap and water." Tom learns a new word: ladle. He also learns that different things need different ways to clean them. He is happy to help his mom wash the ladle. He still likes to polish the spoons and forks, but he also likes to learn new things. Mom says he is a smart helper.

Once upon a time, there was a humble cow named Bessie. Bessie lived on a farm with her friends. She liked to eat grass and play in the sun. One day, Bessie saw a little flower starting to bloom. She went to her friend, the horse, and said, "Look at the pretty flower! It is starting to bloom." The horse looked at the flower and smiled. They both liked to see the flower grow. But then, something unexpected happened. The flower grew bigger and bigger, and soon it was taller than Bessie and the horse. They were very surprised! The flower had become a big tree with many pretty flowers on it. Bessie and the horse were happy to have a new friend, the big flower tree. They played under the tree every day and enjoyed its shade.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a red wolf. The red wolf was not nice. He liked to get things that were not his. One day, a little girl was walking in the forest. She saw the red wolf and said, "Hello, red wolf. What do you want?" The red wolf looked at her and said, "I want to get your food." The little girl did not want to give her food to the red wolf, but she was scared. She said, "Okay, you can get my food." The red wolf took the food and ran away. The little girl was sad and hungry. She went home and told her mom about the red wolf. Her mom said, "Next time, do not talk to the red wolf. He is not nice." The little girl learned her lesson, but she still felt sad because the red wolf got her food.
