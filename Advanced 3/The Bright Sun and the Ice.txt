Title: The Bright Sun and the Ice

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It was a hot day, and all the kids wanted to eat ice. They went to the ice shop to buy some. The ice shop had a big sign with a picture of ice on it. The kids were very happy when they saw the sign. The kids went inside the ice shop and asked for ice. The ice man gave them ice in many colors. There was red ice, blue ice, and green ice. The kids said, "Thank you!" and started to eat their ice. It was cold and sweet, and they loved it. After they finished eating their ice, the kids went outside to play. They ran and jumped under the bright sun. They were very happy because they had ice to eat and friends to play with. The end.

One day, a white dog named Spot went for a jog in the park. He loved to run and play with his friends. As he was jogging, he saw a little girl named Lily sitting on a bench. She looked very sad. "Hi Lily, why are you sad?" asked Spot. Lily said, "I miss my friend, she moved away and now I have no one to play with." Spot felt bad for Lily and wanted to help her feel better. So, Spot and Lily played together in the park. They ran, they laughed, and they had lots of fun. After a while, Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Spot. I am happy now. I learned that even when we miss someone, we can still find new friends to play with." From that day on, Spot and Lily became best friends. They always played together and had lots of fun. The moral of the story is that even when we miss someone, we can still find happiness and make new friends.

Once upon a time there was a generous fish named Leo. Every day in the warm summertime, he would lay in the sun, dreaming about the adventures the day would bring. One day, he decided to go for a swim. Leo went out deeper and deeper until he could no longer see the shore. He saw some smaller fish, and asked them where he was. The fish said “You have swam too far, now you must find a way back.” Leo thought he could make it back, but he was wrong. He tried and tried but he just kept swimming farther away. He asked the smaller fish for help again, but they just watched. Finally, they said “We can’t help you, you are too generous!” And Leo was never seen again.

Jimmy and his Daddy were in the garage. Daddy was looking at their motor. "This motor needs to be fixed," Daddy said. Jimmy looked at the motor. "Can I help?" he asked his Daddy. "Not today Jimmy. But you can watch," Daddy answered. Jimmy didn't like that. He wanted to help too. "Why can't I help?" he asked. Daddy spoke in a calm voice. "It's too complicated. You need to be a big boy to do this." Jimmy was angry. He crossed his arms. "Big boys can do more," Jimmy said. Daddy smiled. "That's true," he said. "But being a big boy also means speaking calmly and respecting your elders." Jimmy thought about it for a moment. Then he smiled. "OK. I can be a big boy," he said. Daddy gave Jimmy a big hug. "I'm proud of you," Daddy said. Jimmy hugged Daddy back. He felt proud too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ruby. She was so excited to go with her mom to buy her very special Christmas present; a big, colorful, candy cane. Ruby jumped and clapped with joy when she saw it. It was a strong cane and made with the most delicious candy. She could almost taste the sweetness in her mouth. On their way to the car, it began to snow. When Ruby was in the car, the candy cane began to melt from the heat of the seat. "Mom," said Ruby sadly, "My candy cane is melting away." Mama smiled, then said, "Don't worry Ruby. We can do something special to save it." Mama grabbed the candy cane and put it in a cold place. When she came back, the candy cane was still colorful and strong. Ruby smiled and said, "Thank you, mom!" They both hugged and enjoyed the sweet taste of the candy cane. Everyone lived happily ever after.
