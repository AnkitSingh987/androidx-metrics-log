Title: The Proud Volcano and the Grass

Once, there was a proud volcano. It was very tall and strong, towering over the grass around it. Every day, the volcano would stand proud and tall. But then one day, something unusual happened. Suddenly, the volcano started to shake and drop pieces of rock onto the ground. The proud volcano had become a rumbling one! It was very exciting, and the other animals around feared it. But then, something else happened. Out of the top of the proud volcano came a loud BOOM! Out of the volcano erupted a huge burst of hot lava. It was a spectacular sight, with orange and red flames exploding into the air. The animals around were amazed. They had never seen anything like it before. The proud volcano had shown them something amazing. They watched in awe as the hot lava slowly dropped back down into the volcano.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. They saw a big tire on the grass. It was round and black and had a hole in the middle. "Let's play with the tire!" Tom said. "We can roll it and jump through it." "Okay!" Lily said. "That sounds fun." They took turns rolling the tire and jumping through it. They laughed and had a good time. But then, something bad happened. Tom rolled the tire too hard and it went out of the park. It rolled down the street and towards a busy road. "Oh no!" Tom and Lily said. "The tire is going to the road! There are cars and trucks and buses there. It is not safe!" They ran after the tire, but they were too slow. The tire was faster. It was about to cross the road when a man saw it. He was wearing a blue uniform and a hat. He was a police officer. "Stop!" the police officer said. He ran to the tire and grabbed it. He lifted it up and moved it away from the road. He saved the tire and the people in the cars. "Thank you, sir!" Tom and Lily said. They were happy and relieved. They ran to the police officer and hugged him. "You're welcome, kids," the police officer said. "But you should be more careful. Playing with a tire is not a good idea. It can be dangerous. You could get hurt or cause an accident. Next time, play with something else." Tom and Lily nodded. They felt embarrassed. They did not mean to be naughty. They just wanted to have fun. "Sorry, sir," they said. "We won't play with the tire again. We will play with our ball or our dolls or our books." "That's good," the police officer said. He smiled and gave them the tire. "Here, you can keep the tire. But only use it as a seat or a table or a decoration. Don't roll it or jump through it. Understand?" "Yes, sir," Tom and Lily said. They understood. They thanked the police officer again and went back to the park. They put the tire under a tree and sat on it. They played with their ball and their dolls and their books. They were happy and safe. They learned their lesson.

One day, a bright sun shone in the sky. A little boy named Tim went for a walk. He found a pretty place with water and trees. It was an oasis. Tim saw a big tree with yummy fruits. He wanted to eat them. A bird in the tree said, "Don't spoil the fruits, Tim. They are for everyone." Tim was sad, but he knew the bird was right. He walked away from the tree. But then, he heard a loud noise. It was a big, mean bear! The bear wanted to spoil all the fruits. Tim was scared, but he had an idea. He talked to the bird again. "Let's work together to stop the bear," Tim said. The bird agreed, and they made a plan. The bird flew around the bear, making it dizzy. Tim found a big net and threw it on the bear. The bear was caught! The oasis was safe, and everyone could share the fruits. Tim and the bird became best friends. They learned that working together is better than spoiling things for others.
