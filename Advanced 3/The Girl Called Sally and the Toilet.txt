Title: The Girl Called Sally and the Toilet

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Sally. Sally wanted to go to the toilet. She entered the toilet and it felt really big. Sally was a bit scared. But then a magical fairy appeared! The fairy had shiny wings and a magical wand. She told Sally that she was very flexible, and that the toilet wasn't so scary. The fairy waved her wand, and suddenly the toilet walls moved! They moved around Sally and the fairy, creating a cozy place for them both to sit inside. Sally and her new friend the fairy played in the toilet for ages. They danced and sang and imagined all sorts of magical things. Sally was so happy, and when it was time to leave the toilet, she thanked the fairy for coming and for making the toilet so flexible!

One day, a sweet cat named Kitty was playing in the park. She saw a big red ball and wanted to play with it. But, a big dog named Doggy also wanted the ball. They both looked at the ball, not sure what to do. Kitty had an idea. She ran home and found a plate. She put the plate on the ground and waited. Doggy saw the plate and was curious. He walked over to the plate and sniffed it. Kitty knew this was her chance. Kitty quickly ran back to the park and grabbed the ball. She let the ball go, and it rolled away. Doggy saw the ball roll away and decided to chase it. Kitty had won! She was so happy that she had found a way to release the ball and play with it.

One day, there was a clever little girl called Maria. She was very excited, because she was going to make pancakes. Her mum opened the cupboard and took out a big pan. Maria was even more excited. She said to her mum, “I help too?” Her mum smiled and said: “Yes, of course. You can pour the pancake mix into the pan!” So Maria carefully poured the mix into the pan. Then she watched as the mix began to cook. As the pancakes got ready, Maria realised that she did something clever. She said with glee, “Me made pancakes!” Her mum smiled and said: “Yes, you did! Let’s eat them now!” So they ate the delicious pancakes, and Maria was very excited. She had made something yummy, all by herself.

Once upon a time there was a small girl named Lily. She liked to play in her garden in the summertime. One day, Lily was playing in the garden when it got very cold. She looked up, and the sky was grey. Suddenly, the wind blew and it started to snow. Lily was so excited. She ran around the garden and looked at all the trees. They were covered in frost and all the leaves were frozen. Each tree looked like a giant ice lolly! Lily went over to the smallest tree and hugged it. It felt so cold. When she pulled away, she looked closely and saw a little bird sitting in a branch. The bird had frozen too! Lily was sad to see the tiny bird all by itself. She carefully placed it on the ground. Then she ran inside, got a warm blanket, and wrapped it around the tree so the bird would stay warm. When she came back outside, the bird had thawed and flew away! Lily was so glad the little bird was free.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia loved to play on the swings. She would swing high in the sky and feel the wind blow her hair. Today, the swings were wet because it rained in the morning. Mia's mom said, "Be careful, Mia. The swings are wet." Mia did not listen to her mom. She wanted to swing so badly. So, she sat on the wet swing and started to swing back and forth. Her pants got wet, and she felt cold. Mia's mom saw her and said, "Mia, I told you the truth. The swings are wet." Mia kept swinging, even though she was cold and wet. Suddenly, she slipped off the swing and fell on the ground. She started to cry because she got hurt. Mia's mom came to help her and said, "Mia, you should have listened to me. Now you are hurt and wet." Mia's day at the park ended with her feeling sad, wet, and hurt. She learned that it is important to listen to her mom and the truth. Next time, Mia will wait for the swings to be dry before playing on them.
