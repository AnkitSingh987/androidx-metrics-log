Title: Happy the Horse and the Friends

Once upon a time, there was a horse named Happy. Happy was a very available horse. He always wanted to play with his friends. One sunny day, Happy and his friends decided to race. They all ran as fast as they could. Happy wanted to win the race so he ran and ran. Happy was very fast. He passed all his friends and crossed the finish line first. Happy was so happy that he won the race. All his friends cheered for him. They all had a great day.

Lily and her mom went to the airport. They wanted to see Lily's dad. He was away for a long time. He worked on big planes that helped people. Lily and her mom had a big bag. They put it on a belt. The belt moved the bag to a big room. Lily and her mom showed their tickets to a friendly lady. The lady smiled and gave them stickers. The stickers had numbers and letters. Lily and her mom walked to a gate. The gate had the same numbers and letters as their stickers. They waited for a long time. Lily saw many planes outside. They were big and shiny. Some had wings that moved. Some had words and pictures. Lily liked the plane that had a rainbow. Lily heard a voice. The voice said their plane was ready. Lily and her mom got in a line. They gave their stickers to another friendly lady. The lady said hello and pointed to a door. Lily and her mom walked through the door. They went inside the plane. The plane had many seats and windows. Lily and her mom found their seats. They buckled their belts. Lily looked out the window. She saw the rainbow plane next to them. She waved to it. The rainbow plane waved back. The plane started to move. It went faster and faster. Lily felt a bump. The plane lifted off the ground. It rose higher and higher. Lily saw the airport get smaller and smaller. She saw the clouds get closer and closer. She was flying. She was happy. She was going to see her dad.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky lived near a small pond with his family. The pond was their home, and they loved to swim and play in it every day. One day, Ducky was playing near the pond. He was being careless and not watching where he was going. He did not recognize that he was getting too close to the edge of the pond. Suddenly, Ducky slipped and fell into the water. He was scared because he did not know how to swim yet. Ducky's mom saw him fall and quickly swam over to help him. She told Ducky that he needed to be more careful when playing near the pond. Ducky learned his lesson and promised to be more careful in the future. From that day on, Ducky always made sure to watch where he was going and never played too close to the edge of the pond again. The moral of the story is to always be careful and pay attention to your surroundings.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a very bossy dog. He liked to tell all the other animals what to do. One day, Max saw a little cat named Lily. Lily was trying to take a nap under a big tree. Max went up to Lily and said, "You can't nap here! This is my spot!" Lily was very sad, but she didn't want to make Max angry. So, she tried to find another place to nap. But Max followed her and said, "You can't nap there either!" Finally, a wise old owl saw what was happening. The owl said to Max, "You should be nice to others and let them nap where they want. If you are kind to others, they will be kind to you too." Max thought about what the owl said and decided to try being a nice dog. Max let Lily nap under the big tree, and they became good friends. From that day on, Max was not a bossy dog anymore. He learned that being kind and sharing with others made everyone happy. And that's how Max learned the importance of being a good friend.
