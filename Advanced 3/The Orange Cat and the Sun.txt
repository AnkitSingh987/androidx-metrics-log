Title: The Orange Cat and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cat. The cat liked to play with a little girl. The little girl's name was Lily. Lily and the cat were best friends. They liked to play in the sun all day. One day, Lily went to school. In the school, there was a big blackboard. Lily liked to draw on the blackboard. She drew a picture of her big orange cat. The cat was not at school, but Lily wanted her cat to be with her. When Lily came home, she told her cat about the blackboard. Lily wanted to show her cat the blackboard. She put her cat on a seat in her room. The cat sat on the seat and looked at the blackboard. Lily drew a picture of them playing together. The cat liked the picture. They were happy.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a small bird. The small bird was building a nest. It wanted to complete the nest soon. The small bird was very happy. One day, a big wind came. The small bird was scared. The nest was not complete yet. The small bird held on tight to the nest. The wind blew and blew, but the small bird did not let go. The wind stopped, and the small bird was safe. It worked hard to complete the nest. Soon the nest was done. The small bird was not scared anymore. It was happy in its new nest.

Once upon a time, there was a warm little house near a mill. In the house lived a nice family with a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and look at the big mill outside her window. One day, Sue went outside to play near the mill. She saw a small cat sitting on a seat by the mill. The cat looked sad and cold. Sue walked up to the cat and said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to come to my warm house?" The cat meowed and jumped off the seat. Sue picked up the cat and they went inside the warm house. Sue's mom gave the cat some milk and it started to purr. Sue smiled and said, "You can stay with us, cat. We will be your family." From that day on, Sue and the cat played together near the mill every day. They were very happy and loved their warm little house. And the cat always had a special seat by the window, where it could watch the big mill with Sue.

Once upon a time, there was a boy and a girl who were friends. The boy had a tree and the girl had a plant. The boy and the girl took good care of their trees and plants, watering them every day and giving them sunshine and love. But then one day the boy became very ignorant and instead of taking care of his tree, he forgot about it. The girl, however, never forgot about the plant and kept on taking care of it. As time passed, the plant grew bigger and beautifully. But the tree of the boy withered away and became smaller. The boy and girl soon realised how valuable it is to take care of something and how ignorant it is to forget about it. So they ran to the tree and started taking care of it again. With their love, care and attention the tree started to grow again and became bigger and more beautiful than ever. The girl and the boy lived happily ever after, learning the valuable lesson that it's important to take care of things and not be ignorant.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to drink milk every day. One day, Kitty's friend, Bunny, told her that drinking too much milk is dangerous. Kitty did not agree with Bunny. She said, "I love milk! It's not dangerous." So, Kitty drank a lot of milk that day. But, something strange happened. Kitty's tummy started to hurt a lot. Kitty went to her mom and told her about her tummy pain. Her mom said, "Bunny was right. Drinking too much milk can be dangerous. You have to be careful." Kitty learned her lesson and didn't drink too much milk again. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends because they might know something you don't.
