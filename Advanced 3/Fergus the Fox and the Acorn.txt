After the bear was gone, Fergus emerged from the bush and let out a huge sigh of relief. That was when he realized - the bear had taken his acorn! Fergus was very sad and he started to panic again. Once upon a time, there was a lion who lived in a distant land. The lion was brave and kind, but he was also very lonely. Every day, the lion would roar in the hopes that someone would hear. One day, a small child came across the lion's roar. She was walking far away from her home when she heard the lion's distant roar. The child was very frightened, but she was also curious. She quietly stepped closer to the lion, and she smiled when she saw the picture of a big lion standing before her. The lion was so surprised to see the little girl that he didn't know what to do. He roared again, but the little girl just laughed. The little girl and the lion became great friends. They would play and explore the distant land together. The little girl would take pictures of all the new things they saw, and the lion would roar with excitement. The little girl's family was so happy to see her friendship with the lion and they were amazed at the pictures she took. The little girl and the lion were the best of friends, and they were never lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his friend, a little boy named Tim. They played all day long and had lots of fun. One day, Tim's mom made yummy muffins. Tim wanted to share a muffin with Tom. He promised Tom that he would give him a muffin if Tom could find his toy car. Tom was very happy and started to look for the toy car. Tom looked under the bed, behind the door, and in the toy box. Finally, he found the toy car under the big chair. Tim was so happy that he gave Tom the muffin as he promised. Tom and Tim ate their muffins together and played with the toy car all day long. They were the best of friends, and they always kept their promises to each other.

It was black and loud and angry. It ran after Lila and Tom. They were scared. They tried to run away, but the dog was faster. It bit Lila's leg and made her cry. It scratched Tom's face and made him bleed. Lila and Tom fell down. They felt dizzy and hurt. They wanted their mommies and daddies. They wanted to go home. They wanted to have peace. But the dog did not stop. It growled and barked and jumped on them. It was a bad dog. It was a bad day. It was a bad ending.
