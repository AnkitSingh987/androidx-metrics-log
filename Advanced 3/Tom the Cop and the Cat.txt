Title: Tom the Cop and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a cop named Tom. Tom was a very flexible cop. He could bend and twist in many ways. One day, Tom had a plan to help the town. Tom started his plan. He walked around the town, helping people. He helped a cat get down from a tree. He helped a boy find his lost toy. Tom was very happy to help. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Tom up into the air. Tom was scared, but he remembered that he was flexible. He twisted and turned in the air and landed safely on the ground. The people in the town were surprised and cheered for Tom. From that day on, Tom was known as the flying cop who always had a plan to help others.

Once upon a time there was a tall house. The roof was made of glass and the walls were so thin it was like tissue. Inside the house were two toys, a big doll and a small soldier. The big doll was named Mabel. Mabel loved to pour tea. She made it each day and shared it with her friends. The small soldier was named Kevin. He lived in a box near Mabel. Kevin loved to fight wars. He fought with sticks and swords, and was brave and strong. One day, Mabel and Kevin had a big fight. The two were so angry that the glass roof started to shake and the fragile walls cracked. Mabel tried her best to pour tea and make peace, but nothing worked. The fight continued until there was nothing left to fight for. Mabel and Kevin were both very sad and tired. Mabel knew that she needed to pour extra love and tea to help Kevin feel better. Finally, after a few days, Mabel and Kevin became friends again. They hugged each other tightly and promised never to fight again. The walls mended and the glass roof grew strong. Mabel and Kevin were happy once more and they enjoyed pouring tea together.

Once upon a time, there was a queen who loved to chew. She was very enthusiastic about her chewing. The queen lived in a big castle with lots of friends. One day, a little girl came to visit the queen. The little girl said, "Hello, I am here to see the queen." The queen came out to meet the little girl. She was chewing on a big, red apple. The little girl asked, "Why do you like to chew so much?" The queen smiled and said, "Chewing makes me happy, and I like to share my happiness with everyone." The queen had a big party, and everyone was invited. At the party, there were lots of things to chew on. There were apples, carrots, and even chewy candy. The little girl had a great time at the party, and she learned to be enthusiastic about chewing too. As the party went on, the queen whispered to the little girl, "I have a surprise for you." The little girl was excited and couldn't wait to see the surprise. She knew it was going to be something fun and chewy. At the end of the party, the queen gave the little girl a big box. Inside the box was a big, chewy toy. The little girl was so happy and said, "Thank you, queen, for the fun party and the chewy toy!" They both laughed and continued to chew on their favorite things.

One day, a lively mouse went for a walk. The mouse saw a big cat. The cat did not recognize the mouse. The mouse was happy, but the cat looked mad. The mouse walked fast, but the cat walked faster. The cat wanted to catch the mouse. The mouse was scared. The cat and the mouse ran and ran. At the end, the cat caught the mouse. The mouse was sad. The lively mouse was not lively anymore. The cat was happy, but the mouse had a bad ending.

There was once an old man who was very foolish. He liked to pinch things and one day he pinched the door. The door wasn't happy about it and it shut very hard on the man's fingers. The old man was so surprised that he started to cry. He had never been so foolish before! Another day, he decided to try not to pinch things. But he found it so hard not to be foolish. So he decided to pinch the door again. He pinched the door but this time it didn't shut on his fingers. He was very relieved to have avoided being hurt. The old man learned his lesson not to be so foolish. He made sure to never pinch the door ever again!
