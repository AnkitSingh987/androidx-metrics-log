Title: The Bird of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He had a big toy chest full of fun things to play with. Tim liked to watch his toys as he took them out of the chest. Tim heard a knock on the door. He opened it and saw a tiny bird. The bird said, "Hello, Tim! Can I come in? It's very cold and bitter outside." Tim said, "Yes, come in and play with me!" The bird flew into the room and went straight to the toy chest. The bird started to sing a magical song. Suddenly, all the toys came to life! Tim was so surprised and happy. He and the bird played with the toys all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy called Jake. Jake was only 3 years old and loved lollipops. Everyday, he would beg his mom for a lollipop. One day, his mom said no and Jake started to complain. He said that it wasn't fair and he wanted a cool lollipop. Jake's mom got angry and said, "No more complaining or you won't get anything!" But Jake kept complaining and his mom got really mad. She said, "That's it! No lollipop for you!" Jake felt really sad and started to cry. Now he regrets complaining too much for a lollipop. The moral of the story is that complaining too much can get you in trouble. From now on, Jake only asks nicely for what he wants.

One day, Jack was walking down the street when he met a skull. The skull looked very proud. "Hello!" he said to the skull. The skull didn't say anything. Jack tried again. "Can I be your friend?" The skull's eyes lit up and it said, "Yes!" Jack smiled and was very excited. Jack decided to show the skull around town. They went to the park and the beach, and even the movie theater. Everywhere they went, Jack was proud to have the skull as his friend. At the end of the day, Jack said goodbye to the skull and waved as it walked away. He couldn't wait until the next time they'd meet.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there was a man who liked to drive his car. He was a bit ignorant and didn't know the way to many places. One day, he wanted to go to the park to play. He started to drive, but he didn't know the way. He asked a little girl for help. The little girl said, "Go straight, then turn left, and you will find the park." The man thanked her and drove the way she told him. At last, he found the park and was very happy. He played all day and made new friends. The man learned that asking for help was good, and he was no longer ignorant about the way to the park.

A young boy named James stood on the hill looking towards the horizon. He observed the nation below. It was once busy, with people walking about and laughing together. But then something strange happened; the nation all of a sudden became very quiet and dead. James thought it strange. He had never seen anything like it before. He asked his mom what had happened, but she didn't know. Everyone in the nation just suddenly seemed to be gone. As James observed the nation, he noticed some movement. People had started to return, but they were different. He watched as they spoke in a strange mixture of languages and all began to live peacefully together. He was amazed and couldn't help but smile. When he returned home, James could tell something had changed. He could feel the happiness in everyone's hearts and he was glad something so wonderful had happened to his nation.

One day, a green automobile was in the park. A boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, were playing near the automobile. They were having a lot of fun. "Spot, let's search for a big ball!" said Tim. They looked and looked, but they could not find the ball. Then, they heard a loud noise. It came from the green automobile. They were scared, but they wanted to find the ball. They went close to the automobile. The green automobile opened its door. Out came a big, friendly robot! The robot said, "I have your ball, Tim and Spot." It gave the ball to them. Tim and Spot were very happy. They played with the robot and had more fun than ever before.
