Title: Sunday and the Car

Once upon a Sunday, there was a little boy. He was so excited to play outside, he jumped up and down with happiness. He found his favourite toy car and he grabbed it in his hand. He pushed it around the garden, making car noises as he drive it around on the grass. His mum looked at him from the doorstep and said with a smile, "What are you doing my darling? You look so excited!" The little boy looked up and grinned, "I'm pushing my car around! I love playing with it on Sundays." His mum laughed, "That's great, my love. Have fun." The little boy smiled and pushed his car around until the sun started to set. At the end of the day, he was so happy and excited to have enjoyed his Sunday.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a fun fair with his family. There were lots of games to play, and Tim wanted to win a prize. He saw a big, shiny ball as a prize and wanted it very much. Tim tried many games, but they were boring. He did not win any prize. Then, he noticed a new game. It was a ball toss game. He wanted to try it. Tim threw the ball and it went into the hole. He won the game! Tim got the big, shiny ball as his prize. He was very happy and showed it to his family. They all cheered and had a great day at the fun fair.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to skip and play outside. One day, it was gloomy and she felt sad because she could not skip outside. Lily decided to stay inside her house and find something fun to do. Lily found her toys and decided to put them in order. She put her dolls, cars, and blocks in nice, neat lines. Suddenly, she heard a soft noise from her toy box. To her surprise, a tiny, talking bird flew out of the box! The bird said, "I am a magic bird. I can make the gloomy day bright and sunny for you." Lily was so happy and asked the bird to make the day sunny so she could skip outside. The bird sang a special song and the gloomy day turned into a bright, sunny day. Lily thanked the magic bird and went outside to skip and play. The bird flew away, but Lily knew that she would always remember the day the magic bird made her gloomy day bright and sunny.

Once upon a time, in a big nation, there was an enormous crab. He lived near the sea and loved to play with his friends. He was a nice crab, but sometimes he would pinch with his big claws. One day, the enormous crab was playing with his friends when he saw a big rock. He thought it was a new friend, so he went to say hello. He pinched the big rock to play, but something unexpected happened. The big rock moved! The big rock was not a rock at all. It was a big turtle! The turtle said, "Ouch! Why did you pinch me?" The enormous crab said sorry and told the turtle he thought he was a big rock. The turtle laughed and they became best friends. From that day on, the enormous crab and the big turtle played together every day, and the crab never pinched anyone again.

One day, a long cat named Tom found a big box. The box was shut very tight. Tom wanted to open the box and see what was inside. He tried to push the box with his strong paws, but it did not open. Tom asked his friend, a small bird named Sue, for help. "Sue, can you help me open this shut box?" Tom said. Sue flew down and used her sharp beak to open the box. When the box opened, there was a lot of ash inside. Tom and Sue did not know what to do with the ash. They decided to take the ash to the big tree in the park. They put the ash under the tree and played together all day.
