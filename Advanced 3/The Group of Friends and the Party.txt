Title: The Group of Friends and the Party

Once upon a time there was a group of friends who liked to celebrate together. On one special day, the friends went to a special party. Everyone was having fun, but something strange happened. The party felt hot and some of the friends got tired. One of they said to the others, "We need oxygen!" The others agreed and began to look for oxygen. Unfortunately, one of the friends was very rude and made a mess of the party. He knocked balloons over and yelled at the others. None of the friends wanted to celebrate anymore, and they all left the party. At first, the friends were angry. But then they decided they all should give the rude friend a second chance. Together they went back to the party and surprised the rude friend. They all hugged him and the party ended on a happy note.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to win a trophy. He saw a sign that said, "Lemon Race Today!" So, Tim went to the race. At the race, there were many kids. They all wanted to win the big trophy. The race was to see who could eat a sour lemon the fastest. Tim was scared, but he wanted to try. Tim's friend, Sue, was there too. Sue said, "I don't like sour things, but I want to try too!" They both got ready to eat the sour lemon. The race began, and all the kids started to eat the sour lemons. Tim and Sue made funny faces because the lemons were very sour. But they kept eating. In the end, Tim won the race! He got the big trophy and was very happy. Sue was happy for Tim too. They both had fun at the sour lemon race.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom loved to nap all day. One day, Tom saw a big mixer in the kitchen. The mixer was very fun and it made a loud sound. Tom wanted to play with the mixer, but he did not know how. He found a long string and tried to tie it to the mixer. He pulled the string very hard. Suddenly, the mixer started to spin very fast. The mixer spun so fast that it made a big cake! Tom was so surprised and happy. He shared the cake with his friends. From that day on, Tom was not just a sleepy cat, but also a great baker.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Jack. He was very nice and polite. He liked to play outside in the sunshine and always do the right thing. One day, Jack's Mom told him to get a pot from the pantry. He quickly got the pot and brought it to his Mom. With a smile, his Mom asked Jack to write his name on the pot with a marker. Jack did as he was told, and he was very proud of his accomplishment. The following day, Jack's Mom called him into the kitchen. She showed him his pot, which had a plant growing out of it! Jack was so happy and proud that he had written his name on it. He realized that it was the pot that he had written on the day before. Look at the wonderful things that can happen when you are polite and follow your mother's instructions, Jack thought to himself. He continued to take good care of the pot and the plant, so that it would keep growing.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On this farm, there were many animals. There was a cow named Daisy. Daisy was a very nice cow. She had a friend named Benny. Benny was a harmless bird. One day, Daisy and Benny were playing near the fence. Daisy saw a big piece of beef on the ground. She wanted to eat it. But the beef was stuck to a tear in the fence. Daisy asked Benny, "Can you help me get the beef?" Benny said, "Yes, I can help you." Benny flew up to the fence and used his beak to tear the beef free. Then, Daisy could eat the beef. Daisy was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Benny!" After that, Daisy and Benny played some more. They had lots of fun on the farm. They were the best of friends, and they always helped each other. And they lived happily ever after.
