Title: Mandy and Joe and the Park

Mandy and Joe were best friends. Every day, they went to the same park, ate lunch together and played together until the night. But sometimes, Joe would get tired and want to go home. Mandy always recommended that they stay just a little bit longer. "It's not even dark yet," she'd say. "Let's play until the moon comes out!" Joe shook his head. "No, Mandy," he said. He pointed up at the pale sky. "It's already time. Come on, let's go home." Mandy crossed her arms. "But why do we have to go home now? We can stay longer!" Joe held out his hand. "Look, it's already getting late," he said. He smiled kindly. "Besides, I promise we'll come back tomorrow, okay?" Mandy sighed. She knew that Joe was right. They both stood up and walked back home together. No matter what, Mandy always knew she could rely on her best friend, Joe.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play in the garden. They had a big tree house that their dad made for them. They had many toys and books in the tree house, but their favorite thing was a big box of costumes. They liked to dress up as different animals and pretend to have adventures. One day, they decided to be rats. They put on gray clothes and masks with whiskers and tails. They crawled on the floor and made squeaky noises. They looked for cheese and nuts in the kitchen. They scared their mom and made her laugh. "Be careful, you little rats," their mom said. "Don't go near the shed. There might be a real rat there, and he won't like you." Lily and Ben nodded, but they were curious. They wanted to see the real rat. They thought he might be their friend. They sneaked out of the house and ran to the shed. They opened the door and peeked inside. It was dark and dusty. There were many things piled up in the shed. They saw a bike, a lawn mower, a toolbox, and a pile of old clothes. They also saw a hole in the wall. They heard a rustle and a sniff. "Hello?" Lily said in a small voice. "Is anyone there?" A big, black rat came out of the hole. He had sharp teeth and red eyes. He looked angry and hungry. He saw Lily and Ben and growled. "Who are you?" he said. "What are you doing in my home?" Lily and Ben were scared. They realized they had made a mistake. They were not rats. They were children. They had no cheese or nuts to offer. They had nothing to say. They turned around and ran out of the shed. They slammed the door behind them. They ran back to the house and climbed up to the tree house. They took off their costumes and hugged each other. "That was incredible," Ben said. "We almost got eaten by a real rat." "Let's never do that again," Lily said. "Let's be something else next time." They agreed. They looked at the box of costumes and picked out new ones. They decided to be birds. They put on colorful feathers and beaks. They flew around the garden and sang. They were happy and safe. They had learned their lesson.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful town, there was a big red ambulance. The ambulance loved to help people. It would go fast, with its lights on and its siren loud, to make sure everyone was safe. One day, a little boy named Timmy saw the ambulance. He thought it was fun and exciting. He wanted to dare his friend, Tommy, to play a game. The game was to pretend they were in the ambulance, and they would run around making loud siren noises. Timmy and Tommy played the game all day. They ran very fast, making loud noises like the ambulance. But they did not see the big rock in their way. They ran right into it, and both boys fell down, hurting themselves. The real ambulance came to help Timmy and Tommy. The boys were sad and scared. They learned that it was not a good idea to play games and dare each other without thinking about what could happen. From that day on, they promised to be more careful when playing games.
