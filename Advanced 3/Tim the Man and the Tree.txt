Title: Tim the Man and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a kind old man named Tim. Tim walked with a cane because he was old and his legs were weak. He had a big heart and liked to help people. One sunny day, Tim saw a small, dead tree in the park. The tree needed help to grow big and strong. Tim went home and got a bucket of water. He carried the water to the tree with his cane. Tim insisted on helping the tree every day. He gave the tree water and love. After some time, the tree started to grow. It became big and strong. Tim and the tree were very happy. They spent many days together in the park, and everyone loved the big, strong tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved ice-cream more than anything. One day, he wanted to own his ice-cream shop. He asked his mom to help him make a shop in the yard. The next day, Tim's mom helped him build a small ice-cream shop. Tim was so happy! He had many ice-creams to sell. His friends came to buy ice-cream from him. But then, something unexpected happened. An angry dog came to the shop. The angry dog scared Tim and his friends. But then, the dog saw the ice-cream. The dog licked the ice-cream and became very happy. The dog was not angry anymore. Tim and his friends laughed and shared ice-cream with the dog. Tim's ice-cream shop became famous, and everyone loved it.

One day, a pretty girl named Lily was playing with her toys. She had a lot of fun with her dolls and cars. Lily also had a big bottle of glue. She loved to make things stick together. Lily had an idea. She wanted to make a pretty picture for her mom. She took her glue and started to stick her toys on a big paper. She put a doll next to a car and a ball next to a bear. It looked very nice. Lily was very happy with her picture. But then, something unexpected happened. Lily's mom came into the room and saw the toys stuck on the paper. She was not happy. She told Lily that she should not use her toys like that. Lily felt sad. She learned that it is important to take care of her things and not to waste them. From that day on, Lily used her glue only for paper and made her mom many pretty pictures.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit called Sarah. She was very messy and loved making a mess. She never tidied her toys away and always left them scattered around her room. One day, Sarah was playing outside and she saw a bow. She picked it up and started playing with it. Sarah's mum called out, "Sarah, what are you doing with that bow? Put it back down and come here". Sarah bowed to her mum and said, "Sorry mum, I just love bows! Can I keep it please?". Her mum smiled and said, "Yes, you can keep it. But you need to clean up these messes of yours!" Sarah bowed again and said, "Ok, mum. I'll do it!" Sarah knew that from now on, she'd always have to tidy up her toys before she started playing with something new. She kept the bow with her and hugged it tightly. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a brown bear. He lived in a big forest. The bear loved to sing songs. Every day, he would walk through the forest and sing a happy song. One day, the bear met a little bird. The bird was sad. The bear wanted to help the bird. So, he sang a happy song to the bird. The bird listened to the song and started to feel happy too. The bird and the bear became best friends. They hugged each other and played together every day. They sang happy songs and walked through the forest. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily found an ordinary envelope on the floor. It was white and had her name on it. She was very curious about what was inside. She asked her mom, "Can I open this envelope?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can open it, but be careful." Lily carefully opened the envelope and found a note inside. The note said, "If you lie down on your bed, close your eyes, and count to ten, you will find a surprise in your room." Lily was very excited and ran to her room. She lay down on her bed, closed her eyes, and counted to ten very slowly. When she opened her eyes, she saw a big mess in her room. All her toys were on the floor, and her books were everywhere. Lily was sad and started to cry. She didn't know what to do. Her mom came into the room and saw the mess. She said, "Lily, you have to clean up this mess right now." Lily had to spend the whole day cleaning up her room, and she didn't get any surprise. The note in the envelope was not true, and it made Lily very unhappy.
