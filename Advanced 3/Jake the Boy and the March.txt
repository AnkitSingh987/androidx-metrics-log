Title: Jake the Boy and the March

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jake. Jake was three years old and he liked to march around his house. Every day, Jake would march up and down the hallway, and he loved to make a lot of noise. One year, Jake decided to march all around the world. He marched across the oceans and climbed the highest mountains. Everywhere he went, people would smile, wave and cheer. He would march all day long, until he was too tired to go on. Finally, after a whole year of marching, Jake made it back home again. Jake had never been so happy. He had seen so many different places and made so many new friends. He was the noisiest three year old anyone had ever seen! And to this day, Jake still loves to march around his house. He still sings his happy marching songs and enjoys the journey of life.

Once upon a time, there was a fast car. The car liked to speed down the road. The car was very proud of its expensive look and fashion. It thought it was the best car in the town. One day, the car saw a slow bike. The car laughed at the bike and said, "You are so slow! I am much better than you!" The bike felt sad, but it did not say anything. The car sped away, leaving the bike behind. Later, the car and the bike had a race. The car was winning, but it saw a big hole in the road. The car was going too fast and could not stop. It fell into the hole. The bike was going slow, so it could stop and go around the hole. The bike won the race. The car learned that being fast and expensive is not always the best. It is important to be careful and kind to others.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a cat named Tom. They loved to play together. One day, they saw something interesting in the park. It was a big, colorful ball. Mia said to Tom, "Do you want to play with the ball?" Tom meowed and nodded his head. They ran to the ball and started to play. They kicked the ball and chased after it. They had lots of fun. After a while, Mia and Tom were very tired. They sat under a tree to have a nap. The tree was big and gave them lots of shade. They closed their eyes and slept. When they woke up, they played with the ball again. Mia and Tom had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to design things like houses, cars, and clothes. One day, she decided to design a big, warm coat. She wanted to make the coat because it was very cold outside. Lily was worried that her friends would be cold too. So, she worked hard to design coats for all her friends. She used soft and warm cloth to make the coats. She also used bright colors because they made her happy. After she finished, Lily gave the warm coats to her friends. They were all very happy and not cold anymore. They played outside in the snow, and no one was worried about being cold. Lily was happy she could help her friends stay warm.

One day, a little girl named Lisa went for a walk. She saw a stream with water. She felt comfortable sitting by the stream. She liked to watch the water move. A frog came by and said, "Ribbit! Please don't disturb the water. We live here." Lisa was surprised to hear the frog talk. She said, "Okay, I won't disturb the water. I just like to watch it." Then, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the stream! The fish said, "Thank you for not disturbing the water. We can be friends now." Lisa, the frog, and the fish became good friends and played together by the stream.

"I'm going to imagine!", yelled Davey enthusiastically. He was a little 3 year old boy with a big imagination. He closed his eyes and imagined he was a bird, flying high above the clouds. Davey started to move his arms up and down, feeling the wind on his face. He felt so free and happy. Suddenly, Davey heard a loud noise and opened his eyes. He saw that a big tree had fallen and was now laying on the ground. "Oh no!", said Davey sadly. He noticed that the fallen tree had broken the wings of a little bird. The bird had stopped moving. Davey felt very sad. He wished that he had wings so he could fly and help the bird. But he just had his two little arms, so he couldn't do anything. Davey walked up to the bird to see if it was okay. He carefully touched it and sadly said: "He's gone now". Then Davey started to quietly cry. The end.
