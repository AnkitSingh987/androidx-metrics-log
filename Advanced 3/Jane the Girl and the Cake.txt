Title: Jane the Girl and the Cake

One day, a little girl named Jane was playing in her room. She had a loyal dog named Max. Jane and Max loved to play with dough. They would make fun things like stars and animals. Today, they were going to study how to make a big cake with the dough. As they played, they made a big mess. Dough was everywhere! Jane and Max were so busy, they didn't hear Mom coming. When Mom saw the mess, she was not happy. She told Jane to clean up and go to bed. Jane was sad, but she knew she had to listen to Mom. Max helped her clean up the dough. That night, as Jane slept, something unexpected happened. Max sneaked into the kitchen and made a big cake with the dough they had cleaned up. He wanted to make Jane happy. In the morning, Jane woke up and saw the big cake. She was so surprised! She knew Max had made it for her. Jane and Max laughed and ate the cake together. They were the best friends ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. She loved to play outside. One day, she saw a big stick on the ground. Emma had an idea. She wanted to balance on the stick. Emma put the stick on the ground and tried to walk on it. At first, it was not easy. She fell off many times. But she did not give up. She tried and tried until she found the right way to balance on the stick. One day, her friend Lily came to play. Lily saw Emma balancing on the stick. "Wow, Emma! How do you do that?" Lily asked. Emma smiled and said, "It was not easy at first, but I found a way to balance. I can show you how to do it too!" And so, Emma helped Lily learn to balance on the stick, and they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to tell everyone what to do. Tom had many friends, but sometimes they did not like how bossy he was. One day, Tom decided he wanted to gain more friends. On Tuesday, Tom went to the park. He saw a little dog named Max. Tom tried to be nice and not bossy. They played with a ball and ran around. Tom and Max had a lot of fun together. Tom was happy to make a new friend. After that day, Tom tried not to be bossy anymore. He learned that being nice helped him gain more friends. Tom, Max, and all their friends played together every Tuesday. They were all very happy.

Once upon a time there was a puppy. The puppy was very small and had big brown eyes. One day the puppy saw a big bow lying on the ground. It had bright colours and looked very nice. He ran towards it and started to play with it. Just then, the owner walked past. He saw the puppy playing with his bow and was so glad. The owner picked up the puppy and the bow and hugged them both. He said "You can play with my bow anytime!" The puppy smiled and the owner petted him gently. From then on, the puppy was always happy when he saw the bow. Every day he'd take it out and play. The owner was always glad to watch him playing. The puppy and the owner were very happy together. They would take long walks, play entirely games and cuddle on the sofa. The puppy loved his new owner and they were as happy as they could be.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in the big ocean with his family. He loved to play with his friends and swim all day long. One day, while Fin was playing with his friends, they saw something strange in the water. They were ignorant, and they didn't know what it was. They decided to swim closer to see what it was. As they got closer, they saw it was a big bag filled with pretty, shiny things. They wanted to play with the shiny things, so they all worked together to open the bag. When they opened the bag, the shiny things began to scatter all around them. They were so excited and started to play with them. But then, something unexpected happened. The shiny things started to change colors and float up to the surface of the ocean. The little fish were amazed and watched as the shiny things turned into beautiful, colorful balloons. They had never seen anything like it before, and they all swam happily together under the ocean, watching the balloons float away.
