Title: The Story of the Sheep and the Wolf

Once upon a time, in a big field, there were many sheep. They were lively and liked to play all day. One day, a boy named Tom came to gather the sheep. He wanted to take them to a new field to eat. Tom said, "Sheep, come with me. We will go to a new field." The sheep were happy to follow Tom. They walked and walked until they reached the new field. The new field had a lot of yummy grass for the sheep to eat. But the new field was not safe. A big, mean wolf lived there. The wolf saw the lively sheep and wanted to eat them. The sheep were scared and ran away. Tom tried to save the sheep, but the wolf was too fast. The wolf ate some of the sheep and the others were lost. Tom was very sad and went home alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to bake with her mom. They made many yummy treats like cookies, cakes, and pies. One day, Amy wanted to make something very special for her dad's birthday. She decided to bake a fancy cake. Amy and her mom put on their aprons and started to bake the cake. They mixed flour, sugar, and eggs in a big bowl. Then they poured the mix into a cake pan. They put the pan in the oven and set the alarm. The alarm would tell them when the cake was ready. While they waited for the alarm, Amy and her mom made the frosting. They used blue and green colors to make it look fancy. When the alarm rang, they took the cake out of the oven. It smelled so good! They let the cake cool, then put the fancy frosting on top. Amy's dad loved his fancy birthday cake, and they all enjoyed eating it together.

One day, a small cat named Kitty found a big frame in the park. She thought it was a fun toy to play with. Kitty picked up the frame and took it to her friend, Doggy. "Look, Doggy! I found a frame!" said Kitty. Doggy looked at the frame and said, "Wow, that's a big frame! Let's play with it!" As they played with the frame, something unexpected happened. When Kitty jumped through the frame, she changed into a big cat! Doggy was so surprised. "Kitty, you are so big now!" said Doggy. Kitty and Doggy took turns jumping through the frame, changing from small to big and big to small. They laughed and played all day. They knew they had found a very special frame that made their day full of fun and surprises.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a good boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toys and his computer. He played games on the computer every day. One day, Tom's mom told him to organize his toys and his computer. She wanted him to make his room look nice and clean. Tom did not like to clean, but he wanted to be a good boy. Tom put all his toys in a big box. Then, he put his computer on a small table. His room looked very nice and clean. Tom's mom was happy and gave him a big hug. Tom felt good for organizing his room.

Billy was feeling very sad because he had lost his favorite yellow belt. He had worn it every day and now that it was gone, he felt so sad. His mommy saw how sad he was and she tried to comfort him. She said that she would help him find it. So they set off on an adventure to find the belt. Everywhere they went, they asked if anyone had seen a yellow belt but nobody had. Billy was getting more and more upset as he didn't know if he would find it. Finally, his mommy had an idea, she said that she knew someone who might have seen it. Billy was so happy, and soon enough, they had found the belt! Billy was overjoyed and put the belt on, feeling better at last. His mommy hugged him and comforted him. She said that she was so glad that they had found it and she was happy that his favorite belt was back around his waist. Billy smiled and hugged his mommy knowing everything was alright again.
