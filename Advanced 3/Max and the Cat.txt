Title: Max and the Cat

One day, a little dog named Max heard some fun music. It was jazz! He could not resist and started to dance. Max danced and danced. He was so happy. Max saw his friend, Cat. Cat was safe on a big tree. Max called out, "Cat, come dance with me!" Cat looked down and saw Max dancing. Cat wanted to have fun too. So, Cat climbed down from the tree. Cat and Max danced together to the jazz music. They danced and laughed. They were very happy. They knew they were safe and having a great time.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small mushroom. The mushroom was confused because it did not know where it was. It wanted to find its family. One day, a little boy came to the forest. He saw the confused mushroom and wanted to help. The boy decided to take the mushroom to find its family. They walked and walked, but they could not find the mushroom family. As they walked, it started to get dark. The little boy and the mushroom were very tired. They could not find the mushroom family and they were lost. The boy and the mushroom stayed in the dark forest, sad and alone. They never found the mushroom family and they never got out of the forest.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Lily. She was very enthusiastic about her new toy. It was a big, red ball. She loved to play with it every day. She would throw it, bounce it, and kick it. Lily and her ball were best friends. One day, while playing with her ball in the park, Lily saw a big cut on a tree. She was curious about the cut and wanted to know how it got there. So, she decided to ask her mom. Her mom told her that the cut was made by a big, sharp thing called an axe. Lily didn't like the idea of a big, sharp thing hurting her friend, the tree. The next day, Lily went to the park again to play with her ball. She was still thinking about the cut on the tree. While playing, she accidentally kicked her ball too hard, and it went flying into the air. When it came down, it hit the tree with the cut. The ball split in half! Lily was very sad. She cried and cried because her best friend, the ball, was broken. And that's how the story ends, with Lily feeling sad and missing her best friend, the red ball.

Once there was a little girl called Daisy who was very stubborn. She loved to choose things for herself, no matter what anyone said. One day, Daisy had a special wish. She wanted a puppy more than anything in the world. She told her Dad, but he said she couldn't have one. Daisy was very disappointed, but she didn't give up. For weeks, Daisy kept asking her Dad if she could have a puppy. No matter how many times he said no, Daisy was still determined. She was very stubborn and wouldn't take no for an answer. Finally, Daisy's Dad said yes! Daisy was so happy. She couldn't wait to choose the puppy of her dreams. She went to the pet store and picked out the most beautiful pup. From that day on, Daisy and her new puppy were the best of friends. Daisy was so glad that she had stuck to her wish and been so stubborn.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a job. Her job was to collect pretty stones from the park. She was very excited to do her job every day. One sunny day, Lucy went to the park to collect stones. She found many pretty stones that day. Suddenly, she saw a big, shiny stone. She was so excited! But when she picked it up, the stone started to talk! The talking stone said, "Hello, Lucy! I am a magic stone. I can help you find more pretty stones if you take me with you." Lucy was surprised and excited. She took the magic stone with her, and they found many pretty stones together. They had a lot of fun, and Lucy loved her job even more.
