Title: The Poor Man and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a poor man who lived in a small hut. He had a big tree in his yard. One day, he decided to cut the tree to make more space. As he started to cut the tree, he heard a tiny voice say, "Please don't cut me!" The man looked around and saw a little bug on the tree. The bug said, "I live here with my family. If you cut the tree, we will have no home." The poor man felt sad for the bug and decided not to cut the tree. Just then, the tree began to shake and something unexpected happened. The tree turned into a big, beautiful house! The bug said, "Thank you for not cutting the tree. I am a magic bug and now you have a new home." The poor man was so happy and thanked the bug. From that day on, they all lived happily together in the big, beautiful house.

One day, a big clumsy cat named Tom went outside. The weather was nice and sunny. Tom liked to play in the sun. He saw a little mouse named Jerry. Tom wanted to catch Jerry. Tom demanded that Jerry come to him. But Jerry was fast and smart. Jerry ran away from Tom. Tom tried to catch Jerry, but he was too clumsy. He fell down and got very dirty. Tom was sad and tired. He thought he would never catch Jerry. But then, something unexpected happened. Jerry came back to help Tom. Jerry was not scared of Tom anymore. They became friends and played together in the nice weather.

Once upon a time, there was a big, scary pumpkin. It lived in a small garden near a little house. The pumpkin had a big smile and two eyes. It was not a bad pumpkin, but it looked scary. One day, a little girl named Amy came to the garden. She saw the scary pumpkin and said, "Why are you so scary? I want to be your friend." The pumpkin did not talk, but it was happy that Amy wanted to be its friend. Amy had an idea. She went to her house and found a pretty dress. She put the dress on the pumpkin. "Now you are not scary, you are pretty!" Amy said. The pumpkin was very happy. Amy and the pumpkin became best friends, and they played together every day.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. Today they see a big slide. They want to try it. "Look, Ben, a slide!" Anna says. "Let's go!" They run to the slide. Anna goes first. She sits on the slide and slides down. She laughs. It is fun. "Wow, Anna, that looks fun!" Ben says. "Can I go next?" "Sure, Ben, it is your turn!" Anna says. She moves away from the slide. Ben sits on the slide and slides down. He smiles. It is fun. They slide again and again. They have fun. The slide is long. They go fast. They watch other kids play. They see swings, a seesaw, and a sandbox. They want to try them too. "Come on, Anna, let's play more!" Ben says. "OK, Ben, let's go!" Anna says. They run to the swings. They swing high. They feel the wind. They have fun. They watch the birds fly. They see the sun shine. They are happy.

Little Max loved to play with his figures. He had all sorts of animals, farmers and knights that he would set up in a line, pretending they were having a race. One day, as Max was playing, he heard a loud voice. It demanded, “Come over here!” He looked around him, but Max could not see anyone. Then he saw a tiny little figure with wings, no bigger than Max’s hand. Even in the bright sunshine, this figure glowed with a soft light. Max carefully went over to the figure. As he got closer, he could hear the figure demanding, “I need your help!” Max nodded his head in agreement, and the figure handed him a shiny stone. “Take this and put it on this tree over there,” the figure said, pointing. Max took the stone and did exactly as demanded. He carefully placed the stone on the branch of the tree. Suddenly, a beautiful sound filled the air; a sound like a thousand birds singing together at once. Max smiled and said “Thank you!” to the little figure. The figure smiled back, and then flew away. Max watched until he could no longer see the figure, and then happily returned to playing with his figures.
