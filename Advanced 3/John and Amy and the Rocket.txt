Title: John and Amy and the Rocket

John and Amy were good friends. They loved to play together every day. One day, they decided to play a game. John said, “I have a great idea. Let’s play a game.” Amy smiled. “What kind of game?” John had a sneaky smile. “We will poke this rocket and see where it flies.” Amy was excited. “Poke it? That sounds fun! Let’s do it!” So, John and Amy began to poke the rocket. They poked it harder and harder until it flew up in the sky, higher and higher. They watched it until it disappeared in the clouds. John and Amy were impressed by the rocket’s flight. It flew so high! But, suddenly, the rocket shot out of the sky and came back down to the ground. John and Amy ran up to the rocket and saw that it was damaged. Amy was sad. She said, “We shouldn’t have poked the rocket so hard.” John smiled and said, “Don’t worry. I have a plan to fix the rocket and make it even more impressive.”

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play with her friends, but sometimes she was a bit selfish. One day, while playing with her friends, she saw some ash on the ground. Amy said, "Look, I found ash! I can use it to improve my drawings!" Her friends wanted to use the ash too, but Amy didn't want to share. "No, it's mine!" she said. But then, something unexpected happened. A small bird flew down and took the ash from Amy's hand. The bird said, "You should learn to share with your friends." Amy was surprised, but she understood. From that day on, she tried to be less selfish and shared with her friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Polly. Polly was three years old and she loved to go outside and explore the big world around her. One day while she was out walking, she came to a street. Polly carefully balanced her way down the street, welcoming each new challenge as she stepped from one foot to the other. Suddenly, a loud car startled her, and she stumbled. "Oh no!" shouted Polly, as she fell onto the street. A kind man walked over to help her. "Are you alright?" he asked in a gentle, patient voice. "Yes," said Polly. She was a bit scared, but the man's kind demeanor made her feel better. The man offered to help Polly get back on her feet and Polly said yes. She held on to him as he balanced her back to safety. Unfortunately, the man was not as kind as he seemed. As soon as they reached the other side of the street, he grabbed Polly and ran away. Polly cried and screamed but no one was around to save her. She was never seen again.

Sara and Tom are twins. They like to play with toys. One day, they see a new toy in the shop. It is a telephone. It has buttons and sounds. Sara and Tom want to buy the telephone. They ask their mom. "Mom, can we buy the telephone?" they say. Mom looks at the price. It is too much. "No, we cannot buy the telephone. It is too expensive. We have other toys at home," she says. Sara and Tom are sad. They go home with mom. They play with their old toys. But they still think about the telephone. They have an idea. They can save their money. They can do chores and help mom. They can buy the telephone later. They start to save their money. They clean their room. They help with the dishes. They feed the cat. They put their money in a jar. They count their money every day. They are happy. After a few weeks, they have enough money. They go to the shop with mom. They see the telephone. It is still there. They give their money to the shopkeeper. He gives them the telephone. They are very happy. They go home with mom and the telephone. They play with the telephone. They pretend to call each other. They make funny sounds. They laugh. They thank mom for letting them buy the telephone. Mom hugs them. She is proud of them. She says, "You are good children. You worked hard and saved your money. You bought the telephone by yourselves. You are safe and happy. I love you."
