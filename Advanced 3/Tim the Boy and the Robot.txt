Title: Tim the Boy and the Robot

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big piece of cardboard. He wanted to make something fun with it. He thought and thought, and then he decided to make a robot. Tim was very excited to start. Tim got some tape to attach the cardboard pieces together. He made a big robot body, two long robot arms, and two wide robot legs. When he was done, Tim looked at his robot and said, "Hello, Mr. Robot! My name is Tim." But the robot didn't say anything back. Tim felt a little sad, but he didn't know that the robot was just a toy and couldn't talk. Tim's mom saw him looking sad and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim said, "My robot won't talk to me." His mom smiled and said, "That's because it's made of cardboard, Tim. It's not a real robot, but it's a very nice toy you made." Tim didn't know that before, but now he understood. He wasn't ignorant anymore. Tim smiled, hugged his cardboard robot, and played with it all day long.

Sara and Ben were twins who liked to play pretend. One day, they decided to be mail carriers and deliver letters to their stuffed animals. They took some paper and crayons and wrote some messages. Sara wrote "I love you" to her bunny, and Ben wrote "You are fierce" to his lion. They put their letters in envelopes and stuck some mints on them as stamps. Mints were their favorite candy, and they thought they looked like shiny coins. They put their envelopes in a bag and went around the room, giving them to their toys. When they came to the lion, Ben said, "Here is your mail, Mr. Lion. You are fierce and brave." He handed him the envelope and smiled. But the lion did not smile back. He roared and grabbed the envelope with his teeth. He tore it open and ate the mint. Then he looked at Ben and licked his lips. "Mr. Lion, what are you doing?" Ben asked, scared. "I am hungry, and you are my mail, too," the lion said, and pounced on Ben. Sara saw what was happening and ran to help her brother. She grabbed a pillow and threw it at the lion. "Leave him alone, you mean lion!" she shouted. The pillow hit the lion on the head and made him dizzy. He let go of Ben and fell on the floor. Sara and Ben hugged each other and ran away from the lion. They decided to play something else and never mail anything to the lion again. They also hid their mints from him, just in case.

Once every year, Sally's family went on a vacation. "Where are we going this year?" Sally asked her mom. "We're going to the beach!" Her mom replied. Sally was so excited. She couldn't wait to get there and see all the pretty sights. On their way there, they saw all sorts of things, including a flower garden, a river, and some friendly animals. When they arrived, the beach was even more beautiful than Sally had imagined. The sand felt soft against her toes and the water was cool and refreshing. She played in the sand and made sand castles. It was the best day of her vacation. At the end of the day, Sally felt very sleepy. She said goodnight and went to bed. She felt so thankful for such a nice vacation, it had been a truly pretty day!

Once upon a time there was a fat puppy. The fat puppy was owned by a small girl called Jo. Jo loved the puppy and they played together every day. One day when Jo was playing with the puppy, she asked the puppy if it wanted to go for a walk in the park. The puppy was very excited and started to bounce around the room. Just then, Jo's mother came into the room and saw the puppy. She said no to Jo and told her that the fat puppy was not allowed to go to the park. Jo and the puppy were very sad. Jo asked her mother why she couldn't take her puppy for a walk. Her mother explained that the park was for people not for pets. Jo thought for a moment, then she had a great idea. She asked her mother if she could take the puppy for a walk on the sidewalk instead. Her mother agreed and Jo and the fat puppy were very happy. They went for a long, happy walk on the sidewalk.
