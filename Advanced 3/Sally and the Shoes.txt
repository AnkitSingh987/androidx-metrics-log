Title: Sally and the Shoes

Today, Sally wanted to be extra special. She asked her mum to help her polish her shoes. Her mum smiled and said, "Of course!" As her mum polished her shoes, Sally watched the shadow of the brush on the ground. When the shoes were done, Sally was so happy. She said, "Thankyou, Mummy! They look so sweet!" Sally's mum hugged her and said, "You look perfect now. Let's go out and show the world your shiny shoes!" Sally's eyes lit up, and she took her mum's hand. As they walked outside, the sun shone down on Sally's polished shoes. She smiled, as she enjoyed the sweet feeling of looking extra special.

Once upon a time, there was an honest dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his family. Max liked to count things. He would number his toys, the trees in the yard, and even the stars in the sky. One day, Max found a big, soft bed. It was a luxury bed, just for dogs. Max was very happy. He jumped on the bed and played with his toys. Max thought, "This is the best day ever!" Max wanted to share his new bed with his friends. He invited all his friends to his house. They all played on the luxury bed and had a great time. Max and his friends were happy and tired. They all took a nap on the big, soft bed. Max was an honest and good friend to everyone.

Mama and Papa took their three year old daughter, Daisy, to the park. They walked together until they reached a bridge. Mama told Daisy to be careful as they crossed the bridge. Daisy listened to her Mama carefully. As they crossed Daisy saw a little duck swimming in the water. Daisy asked Mama, "Mama, what's that?" Mama told Daisy, "That's a duck, darling." Daisy watched the duck for a few moments and then said, "Mama, it's so helpful!" Mama smiled and said, "Yes, darling, it is helpful." The three of them kept walking until Daisy got tired and wanted a rest. Papa stopped and said, "Why don't we have a rest here by the bridge?" Daisy listened as Mama and Papa discussed. Finally, Mama and Papa agreed. They all sat down to enjoy the view. After a short break, Daisy was ready to go again. Mama, Papa and Daisy continued their walk until they reached the park and enjoyed their day together. Daisy was so thankful that Mama and Papa were helpful.

Once upon a time, there was a big palace. In the palace, there was a king. The king had a big chair. The chair was very comfortable. The king liked to sit on the chair. He would value his chair a lot. One day, the king was not in the palace. A little dog came. The dog saw the chair. The dog jumped on the chair. The dog liked the chair too. The dog felt very comfortable. The king came back to the palace. He saw the dog on his chair. The king was not angry. He smiled at the dog. The king and the dog became friends. They shared the chair. The king learned to value the dog too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her cat, Tippy. Every night, before she went to bed, Sue would lock the door to keep her family safe. One night, Sue heard a noise outside her window. She looked out and saw a bird that looked different from the others. The bird said, "Hi, Sue! I am lost. Can you help me find my way home?" Sue wanted to help the bird, so she asked her mom and dad if it was okay. They said yes, but they told her to be careful. Sue unlocked the door and went outside with the bird. They looked around and soon found the bird's family. The bird said, "Thank you, Sue!" and flew away with its family. Sue went back home, feeling happy that she helped the bird. She locked the door again and went to bed, knowing that she had made a new friend that night.
