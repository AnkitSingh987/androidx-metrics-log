Title: Sam and Lucy and the Elephant

Once upon a time there were two friends, Sam and Lucy. They decided to travel together to see the world. Sam had an eraser in his pocket. On their journey, they encountered a naughty elephant. The elephant started to cause trouble and Sam dropped the eraser on the ground. The naughty elephant swallowed the eraser, making Sam very sad. Lucy knew she had to help her friend. She boldly stepped up to the naughty elephant, spoke very gently and said to him, "Please give back what does not belong to you." Surprisingly, the naughty elephant listened! The elephant gave back the eraser and Sam was so happy. The moral of the story is this: even when facing a big problem, be nice and polite, and you may just get the solution you need.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who lived in the forest. She was very peaceful and happy. One day, she wanted to go for a walk, so she grabbed her bottle and set off. On her walk, she saw so many different things. But then, she started to get tired. She forgot which way she had come from. She didn't know where she was anymore. The little girl started to worry, but then she heard the birds singing in the trees. She knew she was still surrounded by the forest. She followed the singing of the birds and soon enough, she came across her bottle. She was happy to find it again and soon enough, she found the path that led her home. She went home feeling peaceful, knowing she hadn't been lost forever.

Once upon a time, there was a wide hurricane. It was so strong that it was pulling everything in its path. The wind was blowing so hard that the trees and houses were shaking. The leaves in the trees were being ripped off and flying through the air. The hurricane was so wide that it was hard to miss. Everyone was scared and they tried to get away from the hurricane as quickly as they could. Everyone was worried that the hurricane was going to pull their homes down! The people in town tried to be brave, but the hurricane was just too strong. They ran and hid, but not before the hurricane had pulled down all the trees and knocked over all the houses. Everyone was very scared. The hurricane lasted all night, but eventually it passed and the sun came out. Everyone was so happy that the hurricane was gone, and they worked together to pick up the mess it had left behind. They started to rebuild their homes and put the leaves back on the trees. By morning, the town was back to looking just like it had before. Everyone was so relieved and thankful that the hurricane was gone. They knew that in wide storms like these, it's always important to stay safe!

Once upon a time, there was a small puppy named Tim. Tim liked to play all day with his ball. He was very comfortable in his warm home. One day, Tim's ball went far away. He looked for the ball, but he could not find it. Then, he heard a voice say, "Is this your ball?" It was a big dog named Max. Tim didn't expect to meet a new friend that day. Max said, "You should remain with me and we can play together." Tim was happy and played with Max all day. They became best friends and lived happily ever after.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play soccer in the park. Tom has new gear for soccer. He has a red shirt, black shorts, and blue shoes. He thinks he looks cool. "Look at my gear, Mia!" Tom says. "Do you like it?" Mia looks at Tom. She does not like his gear. She thinks it does not fit. His shirt is too big, his shorts are too small, and his shoes are too tight. She thinks he looks funny. "Your gear does not fit, Tom," Mia says. "Your shirt is like a dress, your shorts are like underwear, and your shoes are like socks. You look silly." Tom feels bad. He feels embarrass. He does not want to play soccer anymore. He wants to go home and change his gear. "Why are you mean, Mia?" Tom says. "I like my gear. It is new and cool." Mia feels sorry. She does not want to hurt Tom. She wants to be his friend. She thinks of a way to make him feel better. "I am sorry, Tom," Mia says. "I was joking. Your gear is fine. It does not matter how it fits. It matters how you play. You are a good soccer player. Do you want to play with me?" Tom smiles. He feels happy. He does not care about his gear anymore. He cares about his friend. He wants to play with her. "Okay, Mia," Tom says. "Let's play soccer. But next time, can you be nice?" Mia nods. She hugs Tom. They run to the field. They play soccer. They have fun. They are friends.
