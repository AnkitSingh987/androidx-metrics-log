Title: Amy the Girl and the Rock

One day, a little girl named Amy went to gather rocks outside. She loved to find all kinds of rocks and put them in her bag. She was very persistent in her search for the best rocks in the universe. As she was looking, she saw a big, shiny rock. "Wow! This must be the best rock in the universe!" Amy said. She picked up the rock and put it in her bag. But something unexpected happened. The rock started to talk! "Hello, Amy," said the rock. "I am a magic rock from the universe. You are so persistent in your search for rocks that I will give you one wish." Amy thought for a moment and said, "I wish for more friends to play with." The rock nodded and suddenly, all the rocks in her bag turned into new friends for Amy to play with. They all had fun together, and Amy was very happy.

Once upon a time there was an orange nurse. She came to help the family with the baby. The baby liked to play a game called hide. The nurse was so good at the game. The nurse would hide and the baby would try to find her. Sometimes it would take a long time. Sometimes the orange nurse would even move to a different place when the baby wasn't looking. But the baby always found her in the end. They would laugh and hug and the baby would say, "You're so good at hide!" The orange nurse smiled and said, "It's fun to play!" The baby and the orange nurse played hide for a long time. They both had a great time. So the baby and the orange nurse became very good friends.

One day, a big, sleepy dog lay in the sun. He was very happy. But then, he felt an itch on his back. He tried to scratch it, but he could not reach. He felt sad. A little bird saw the dog and flew down. "Why are you sad?" asked the bird. The dog said, "I have an itch and I can't reach it." The bird wanted to help. "I can help you," said the bird. The bird flew to the dog's back and started to scratch the itch. The dog was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The itch moved! The bird was surprised. The dog and the bird laughed. They played together and became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a land full of unique animals, there was a wise old owl named Wisdom. Wisdom loved to stretch his wings and fly high in the sky. Every day, he would stretch his wings and fly to talk to his friends. One day, Wisdom met a little mouse who wanted to learn how to stretch and be unique like him. The wise old owl thought about it and decided to teach the mouse. They practiced stretching and being unique together. In the end, the mouse learned how to stretch and be unique. He was so happy, and all the other animals loved him too. Wisdom was proud of his new friend, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big, impressive bird named Bobby wanted to deliver a message. He had a very important meeting with his friends, the animals. He flew to the tree where the meeting was going to be. All of his friends were there: the cat, the dog, the cow, and the duck. Bobby said, "Hello, friends! I have a message for our meeting." The cat, the dog, the cow, and the duck listened carefully. They all wanted to hear what Bobby had to say. Bobby told them, "We need to help each other and be kind. We can all do something special to make our home better." The cat, the dog, the cow, and the duck agreed. They all promised to do their best to help each other and be good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy box full of toys. In the box, she had a favorite toy. It was a colorful cube. Lily loved to play with her cube every day. One day, Lily could not find her cube. She looked in her toy box, but it was not there. She felt very sad. She wanted to play with her cube, but she could not find it. Lily asked her mom for help. Lily's mom helped her look for the cube. They looked high, and they looked low. Then, they saw the cube on a shelf. It was too high for Lily to reach. Her mom picked her up, and Lily reached for the cube. She was so happy! Lily and her mom played with the cube together, and they had a fun day.
