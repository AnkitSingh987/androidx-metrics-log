Title: Anna and the Dictionary

Anna was a jolly 3 year old girl who loved to laugh and play. She didn't know many words, but she loved to play pretend. One day Anna decided to make a dictionary. She went around the neighbourhood and asked her friends to help her. Once everyone had gathered, Anna said, "Let's make a dictionary! I heard that words are fun, so we can all make one together." Her friends laughed, "What do we need for that?" Anna smiled and said, "All we need to make a dictionary is paper, pens and imagination!" Everybody was excited and everyone set to work. They wrote down all their words and drew pictures to go along with them. After many hours of hard work, the dictionary was finally complete. Everybody cheered, shouted and laughed with joy. The dictionary was filled with fun and jolly words that would bring lots of smiles. Though Anna couldn't read the words yet, she was thrilled to have her very own dictionary. She knew that soon enough she could learn to read them, and this made her very happy.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside in the snow. They make snowballs and snowmen and snow forts. They have a lot of fun. One day, Anna sees a red sled in Ben's yard. She wants to try it. She asks Ben, "Can I borrow your sled?" Ben says, "No, you can't. It is my sled. I got it for my birthday. It is very fast and tough. You might break it or lose it." Anna feels sad. She says, "Please, Ben. I will be careful. I will give it back to you. I just want to slide down the hill once." Ben thinks for a moment. He likes Anna. He does not want to make her sad. He says, "Okay, Anna. You can borrow my sled. But only for a little while. And you have to share it with me." Anna smiles. She says, "Thank you, Ben. You are a good friend. I will share it with you. Come on, let's go to the hill." They take the sled to the hill. They take turns sliding down. They laugh and scream. They have a lot of fun. They are happy. They share the sled. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there were two little friends named Holly and Sara. Holly liked to settle on the blanket and Sara liked to do yoga. She was a bendy yoga teacher. One day, they decided to explore somewhere new. Off they went and settled in a little meadow. The sun was out and it was so warm. Everywhere they looked there were wildflowers and little animals. Suddenly, they heard music. A yoga instructor was practising nearby. Sara asked the instructor if Holly could join in too. So, Holly and the yoga instructor settled down. Holly tried her best to copy the movements and the instructor was very impressed. When the lesson was finished, the instructor praised Holly for trying her very best. She had become a little yoga master. Holly and Sara smiled and hugged. They had had a wonderful day!

One day, a gifted cat named Tom went for a walk. He liked to look at the trees, the grass, and the sky. He saw a hedge and went near it. He heard a sound and wanted to find out what it was. As Tom stared at the hedge, a big, friendly dog named Max came out. Tom was scared at first, but Max just wanted to play. They played together in the sun, running and jumping. They were very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. Tom saw a big box near the hedge. Tom and Max looked at the box and saw a note. The note said, "For Tom and Max." They opened the box and found toys and treats! They played with the toys and ate the treats. They were very surprised and happy.

Jonny was 3 years old and loved to kick objects. Today, he was in his kitchen and he saw a cupboard. He got very excited and he ran to it. He put his foot up, ready to kick it. "That looks cool," he said. Jonny's mother heard him and came over. She said "It's not nice to kick the cupboard, Jonny. It's not a toy." Jonny looked at the cupboard again and he liked it, but he knew not to kick it. Instead, he looked around the kitchen and he spotted a ball on the floor. He ran to it, and said, "I can kick this!" And so Jonny did, he kicked the ball and laughed when it rolled across the kitchen. His mother smiled and told him that he was very cool.
