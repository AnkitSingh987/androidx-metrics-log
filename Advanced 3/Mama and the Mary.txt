Title: Mama and the Mary

Mama said it was time for bed. Mary was tired, so she went to her room. She hopped on her bed and snuggled into her favourite cushion. She closed her eyes and was so close to falling asleep, then she heard a noise. When Mary opened her eyes, she saw that a monster was in her room. Mary was scared, so she refused to get out of bed. The monster got angry and started to roar. He wanted Mary to leave her room, but she wouldn't move. Suddenly, the monster grabbed Mary in its big hands. The monster said she wouldn't be able to stay in her room, but Mary was determined to refuse! She held onto her cushion, ready to fight. After a long time, the monster gave up. He left the room and Mary was relieved. But when she got out of bed, she noticed that her room was not normal. Everything was a mess! Her cushion was ripped and her room was ruined. Mary realised that she couldn't refuse the monsters forever. Even though she tried to fight, things had still gone really badly. Mary knew she needed to be more careful in future.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little house, there lived a girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to cook with her mom. One sunny day, they decided to make a yummy cake. Lucy's mom said, "Lucy, can you please pick a pan for our cake?" Lucy looked at all the pans and picked a big, round one. She thought it was perfect for their cake. Together, Lucy and her mom mixed the cake batter. They poured it into the pan Lucy picked. They put the pan in the oven and waited. When the cake was done, they took it out and let it cool. Lucy and her mom decorated the cake with sweet frosting and colorful sprinkles. They were very happy with their cake. They shared it with their family, and everyone loved it. From that day on, Lucy always picked the pan when they baked a cake. And their cheerful little house was filled with love, laughter, and lots of yummy cakes.

Tom and Lily are playing with a big box. They want to make a car out of it. They find some wheels, a steering wheel and a horn. They also find some screws and a screwdriver. "Can you help me, Lily?" Tom asks. He holds a screw and the screwdriver. "I want to put this wheel on the box." Lily nods. She likes to help Tom. She takes the screw and the screwdriver. She puts the screw in a hole on the box. She turns the screwdriver round and round. The screw goes in the box. The wheel is fixed. "Good job, Lily!" Tom says. He gives her a hug. "You are very useful. Now we have one wheel. We need three more." They work together. They put the other wheels on the box. They also put the steering wheel and the horn on the box. They make a car out of the box. "Look, we made a car!" Lily says. She is very happy. She gets in the box. She holds the steering wheel. She honks the horn. "Beep beep!" she says. Tom gets in the box too. He sits next to Lily. He smiles. "Let's go for a ride!" he says. They pretend to drive the car. They have fun. They are happy. They are friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his elderly grandma. They went to the park to play. Tim was very happy, but his grandma had a frown on her face. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, grandma?" Grandma said, "I lost my pocket with my special key inside. I can't find it anywhere." Tim wanted to help his grandma, so they started to look for the pocket together. As they looked around the park, a big dog came running to them. The dog had something in its mouth. It was grandma's pocket! The dog dropped the pocket at their feet and wagged its tail. Tim and his grandma were very surprised and happy. The frown on grandma's face turned into a big smile. They thanked the dog and went home, feeling lucky that something unexpected had happened.
