Title: The Story of the Ball and the Puddle

Once upon a time, in a big, green park, there was a small, red ball. The ball was sad because it was all alone. It wanted to play with the kids, but it was stuck in a wet puddle. One sunny day, a nice girl named Lily appeared. She saw the wet, red ball sitting in the puddle. Lily wanted to help the ball, so she looked for something to get it out. She found a long cord under a tree. Lily used the cord to pull the wet ball out of the puddle. The red ball was so happy to be out of the puddle! Lily and the ball played all day in the big, green park. They had so much fun, and the red ball was never sad again.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty butterfly. She had many friends, but she was a bit selfish. She loved to fly and play all day, but she did not like to share her toys. One day, while the butterfly was playing, a big wind came. The wind took her favorite toy away. The butterfly was very sad. She asked her friends to help her find her toy. But her friends did not want to help her because she was selfish and did not share her toys with them. The butterfly knew she had to change. She promised her friends that she would share her toys and be kind. Her friends saw that she was trying to be better and they decided to help her. Together, they found the toy and the butterfly shared it with her friends. From that day on, the butterfly was not selfish anymore and they all played happily together.

One day, a boy named Tim had a guitar. He loved to play it all day. Tim's guitar made him very happy. It was a big guitar with many colors. Tim liked to make songs with his guitar. One day, it was very wet outside. It rained a lot. Tim wanted to play his guitar outside, but he knew it was not good for his guitar. The rain could destroy it. So, he played inside and looked out the window. After the rain stopped, Tim went outside with his guitar. He played a happy song for the sun. The sun dried up all the wet. Tim and his guitar were very happy. They played songs together all day.

One week, Mommy and Daddy said that it was time to tidy up the house. Everything was very messy. Mommy told Goose to start in his bedroom. Goose tried to peek around the corner of his room. He noticed toys and books lying all over the place. So, Goose got to work picking everything up and putting it in the right places. He discovered a box in the corner of the room that had lots of old toys in it. He decided to peek inside and see what was there. He was surprised to see how much stuff was inside - cars, stuffed animals, and a game he liked to play! After a long week, Goose and Mommy had tidied up not only Goose’s bedroom, but the entire house. It was much neater now and Goose was proud to show off what he had done!

Once upon a time, there was a big hotel. In the hotel, there was a fun game. The game was for everyone. People wanted to win the game. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the hotel. Tim wanted to play the game. He wanted to win, too. But Tim felt a little guilty. He thought he was too small to win the game. But Tim played the game anyway. He tried his best. And guess what? Tim won the game! He was so happy. Everyone at the hotel clapped for Tim. And Tim did not feel guilty anymore, because he knew that anyone can win if they try their best.

Once upon a time, there was a big hall where people would work. In this hall, they made yummy treats for everyone. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the hall with his mom. "Mom, can we see them work?" asked Tim. His mom said, "Yes, we can watch them make the yummy treats." They stood near a window and watched the people work. They made cookies, cakes, and pies. Tim's eyes grew wide with excitement. After watching them work, Tim and his mom went to the store next to the hall. They bought some of the yummy treats they saw being made. Tim was so happy as he ate his cookie. "This is the best day ever!" he said with a big smile.
