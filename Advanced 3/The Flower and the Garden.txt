Title: The Flower and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little flower. The flower was troubled because it could not bloom. It wanted to be a big, pretty flower like all the others around it. The little flower felt sad and alone. One day, a kind infant came to the garden with her mom. The infant saw the troubled flower and said, "Why are you sad, little flower?" The flower replied, "I cannot bloom like the others. I am not pretty." The infant smiled and touched the flower gently. She said, "Do not worry, little flower. You will bloom soon, and you will be very pretty." The next day, the little flower started to bloom. It was so happy and thankful to the infant. From that day on, the flower was never troubled again.

Once upon a time, there was a clean peach. It was sitting on a table in the sun. The peach was very round and sweet. Everyone wanted to eat the peach. One day a boy came along and picked up the peach. He wanted to wrap the peach for later. So, he wrapped it in some paper. He went home and put it in the fridge. He waited anxiously for later when he could eat the special peach. When the time came, he opened the fridge and the peach was still clean. He took it out and ate it - it was delicious! The end.

One day, Ben and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig holes and fill them with stones, leaves and bugs. They had a lot of fun making their own little worlds. But then, Ben saw something shiny in Lily's hole. He was very nosy and wanted to see what it was. He reached out his hand and grabbed it. It was a necklace that Lily had found in her mom's drawer. It was very pretty and sparkly. "Hey, that's mine!" Lily shouted. "Give it back!" "No, it's mine now!" Ben said. "I found it in your hole!" They started to pull and tug on the necklace. They were both very angry and did not want to share. They did not hear their mom calling them for lunch. Suddenly, the necklace broke. The beads flew everywhere. Ben and Lily gasped. They had ruined the necklace. They felt very bad and scared. They looked at each other with sad eyes. They did not want to fight anymore. They wanted to be friends again. "I'm sorry, Lily," Ben said. "I should not have taken your necklace. It was very mean of me." "I'm sorry, too, Ben," Lily said. "I should not have yelled at you. It was very rude of me." They hugged each other and said, "I forgive you." They picked up the beads and ran to their mom. They told her what had happened and asked her to fix the necklace. Their mom was not happy, but she was glad that they had said sorry and forgiven each other. She hugged them and said, "I love you, my nosy little diggers."

Once upon a time, there were two children named Jack and Jill. They both wanted to create something special with their clay. They knew it would take a lot of patience. Jack said, "Jill, let's start by making some animal figures. I think I can make an elephant!" Jill smiled and said, "That's a great idea! I'm going to pinch the clay and make a rabbit!" So, Jack and Jill started crafting their figures with the clay. They were patient as they pinched and rolled it. When they were finished, they had made a beautiful elephant and rabbit. Jack and Jill beamed proudly at their creations. "I'm so happy with what we made!" said Jill excitedly. Jack smiled and said, "Me too! I think this is going to be our best project ever!"

Billy and his dad went to the forest. It was very wide, with tall trees everywhere. His dad said, "Let's ride your bike, Billy!" So, he got on and started pedaling away. Suddenly, as he was riding, he saw a big flame ahead! He was about to go closer, but his dad said, "No, Billy. That's too dangerous." But Billy really wanted to see it, so he rode closer and closer. When he got there, the flame was really big. Billy asked, "What is it Dad?" His dad said, "That's a campfire, Billy. People made it so they can stay warm." Billy smiled and said, "Let's ride closer! I want to feel the heat!" His dad said, "Okay, just don't get too close." So they rode closer and closer until they were right next to the flame. Billy felt the warmth and he smiled. He said, "Thanks, Dad!" His Dad smiled and said, "You're welcome, son." Then, they went home.
