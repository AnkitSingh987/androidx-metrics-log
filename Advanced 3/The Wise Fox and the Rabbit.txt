Title: The Wise Fox and the Rabbit

Once there was a wise fox. He was very clever and had great wisdom. He lived in a deep forest. One day, a naughty rabbit came along. He was very ignorant and didn't know anything. He saw the fox and decided to go up to him. Rabbit asked, "Can I taste your wisdom?" Fox replied, "No! You are too stupid. You must go away." But Rabbit wouldn't listen and kept on asking to taste Fox's wisdom. After a while, Fox got very angry. He shouted, "No!" Then, he chased Rabbit away and sent him far away from the forest. Rabbit was very scared and he never came back. Fox was sad, he wanted to share his wisdom, but too many ignorant people in the world made him afraid.

Once upon a time there was a fierce lion. He was the biggest and strongest lion around. He wanted everyone to know it too. One day, he looked around and saw a small number. It was tiny and so he thought it would be easy to complete. The little number had other ideas. He challenged the lion to a race. "I am so fast! I bet I can beat you!" the number proclaimed. The lion scoffed. "There is no way a small number like you can beat me." But the number was determined. "Just try me!" The lion hesitated, but then agreed. He started to run as fast as he could. The number followed, but he was much slower. The lion started to gloat as he raced ahead. But then something unexpected happened. The number began to get larger and faster. By the time the lion heard the finish line, the number had become so big and so strong that it crossed first! The lion was stunned. He had been defeated by the fierce little number. From then on, he never looked down on anyone, no matter how small they were.

One day, a clever fox went for a walk. He wanted to meet new friends. In the woods, he met a big, wise owl. The owl was very old and knew many things. The fox liked the owl very much. "Hi, owl! I want to learn from you," said the fox. "You have much wisdom." The owl smiled and said, "I will teach you, clever fox. But first, you must do something for me." The fox was excited and asked, "What do you want me to do?" The owl said, "Bring me a big, red apple from the top of the hill." The fox ran fast to the hill and saw the apple tree. But the apples were not red, they were green! The fox felt sad and thought, "What can I do?" Then, he had a clever idea. He took a green apple and painted it red. He ran back to the owl with the red apple. The owl smiled and said, "You are very clever, fox. Now, I will teach you my wisdom." And they became good friends.

One day, a big crocodile went to the lake. He saw a group of animals. They were going to perform a show. The crocodile wanted to join them. He said, "Can I be in the show too?" The animals looked at the crocodile. They were scared. The crocodile was big and had sharp teeth. But they did not want to be rude. So, they said, "Yes, you can be in the show." The crocodile was happy. He practiced with the animals. They danced and sang together. The show was very fun. All the animals and the crocodile were friends now. They were not scared of the crocodile anymore. The crocodile was not rude, and they all had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a polite cat named Tom. Tom loved to spin around and play with his friends. One day, Tom found a big case in the park. He was very excited and wanted to open it. Tom asked his friends to help him open the case. They all tried to open it by spinning it, but it did not work. They were sad but did not give up. They tried to spin the case faster and faster, but it still did not open. Finally, they spun the case so fast that it flew up into the sky. The case went higher and higher until they could not see it anymore. Tom and his friends were very sad because they could not open the case. They went home feeling sad, and never found out what was inside the case.
