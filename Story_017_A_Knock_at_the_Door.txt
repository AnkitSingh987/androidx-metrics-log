Title: A Knock at the Door
Category: Beginner

It is Wednesday evening. The clock shows seven o'clock. 
Mary is in her living room. She is a young woman. She lives in a small apartment. She is alone today. 
She is sitting on the sofa. She is watching television. She is watching a funny movie. She is laughing. 
Her cat, Fluffy, is sleeping on her lap. Fluffy is a white cat. She is very soft. 

The apartment is warm and cozy. Outside, it is dark and a little cold. 
Suddenly, Mary hears a noise. 
Knock, knock, knock. 
The noise comes from the front door. Someone is knocking at the door. 

Mary stops laughing. She mutes the television. The room is quiet. 
Knock, knock, knock. 
There it is again. It is a loud knock. 
Fluffy wakes up. She jumps down from Mary's lap. She walks to the door. She sniffs the air under the door. 

"Who can it be?" Mary thinks. 
She is not expecting anyone. Her friends did not say they were coming. Her mother lives in another city. She does not know who is at the door. 
She feels a little nervous. But she also feels curious. 
She stands up from the sofa. She walks to the front door. 
"Who is it?" she asks in a loud voice. 
There is no answer. 

Knock, knock, knock. 
The person knocks again. 
Mary looks through the small hole in the door. It is a peephole. She can see outside. 
She looks, and she sees a face. It is a familiar face. It is a smiling face. 
It is her sister, Anna! 

Mary is very surprised. Anna lives far away. She studies at a university in another town. 
Mary quickly unlocks the door. She opens the door wide. 
"Anna!" Mary says loudly. 
"Surprise!" Anna shouts. She opens her arms. 

Mary hugs her sister. She is very happy. 
"What are you doing here?" Mary asks. "You are supposed to be at university."
"We have a short holiday," Anna explains. "I wanted to surprise you. I took the bus this afternoon."
"That is wonderful!" Mary says. "Come in, come in. It is cold outside."

Anna steps into the apartment. She is carrying a big blue bag. The bag looks heavy. 
"Put your bag on the floor," Mary says. 
Anna puts the bag down. She takes off her winter coat. She takes off her scarf. 
"It is good to be here," Anna says. She looks around the living room. "Your apartment looks nice."
"Thank you," Mary says. 

Fluffy the cat walks to Anna. She rubs against Anna's legs. 'Meow,' she says. 
"Hello, Fluffy," Anna says. She bends down. She strokes the cat's white fur. Fluffy purrs happily. 

"Are you hungry?" Mary asks her sister. "Did you eat dinner on the bus?"
"No, I didn't," Anna says. "I am very hungry. I could eat a horse!"
Mary laughs. "I don't have a horse. But I have some chicken and rice. I cooked it an hour ago. It is still warm."
"That sounds perfect," Anna says. 

They go to the kitchen. Mary takes two plates. She puts chicken and rice on the plates. She puts the plates on the small kitchen table. 
"Sit down," she says. 
Anna sits down. She starts to eat. 
"This is delicious," Anna says with her mouth full. "Thank you, Mary."

Mary sits next to her. She is not hungry, but she likes watching her sister eat. 
"How is university?" Mary asks. 
"It is good. It is very busy," Anna says. "I have many classes. I have a lot of homework. But I like my teachers."
"That is good to hear," Mary says. 
"And how is your job?" Anna asks. 
"My job is fine," Mary answers. "It is the same as always. I work from nine to five."

They talk for a long time. They talk about their parents. They talk about their friends. They laugh a lot. 
Anna finishes her dinner. She helps Mary wash the dishes. 
Then they go back to the living room. They sit on the sofa. Fluffy jumps up and sits between them. 
"I am so glad you knocked on my door today," Mary says. 
"Me too," Anna says. She yawns. She is tired from the long bus trip. 
"You can sleep in my bed tonight," Mary says. "I will sleep on the sofa."
"No, you take the bed," Anna says. "I am smaller. The sofa is fine for me."
"Okay," Mary agrees. 

It is a happy evening. A surprise knock at the door is not always scary. Sometimes, it brings the best surprises. It brings family, food, and laughter. Mary is happy she opened the door.
