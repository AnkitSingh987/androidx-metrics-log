Title: A Day in Bed
Category: Beginner

It is Wednesday morning. Outside, the sun is shining, and children are walking to school. But inside the house, it is very quiet. Sam is not going to school today. He is sick. He must stay in bed. 

Sam wakes up late. It is ten o'clock. He looks at the ceiling of his bedroom. His head does not hurt as much as yesterday, but he still feels very weak. He is not hot anymore, but his body is tired. 
His mother opens the bedroom door. She carries a tray with a bowl and a glass. 
"Good morning, sleepyhead," she says with a smile. "How do you feel today?"
"I feel a little better," Sam answers. "My head is okay, but I am very tired."

"That is normal," his mother says. "Your body is fighting the infection. You need to stay in bed all day and rest. I brought you some chicken soup."
She puts the tray on Sam's lap. The soup is hot and smells delicious. It has small pieces of chicken, carrots, and soft noodles. 
Sam sits up slowly. He takes the spoon and eats the soup. The warm liquid feels very good on his sore throat. It is easy to swallow. He eats the whole bowl. 
"That was very good," he says. "Thank you."

"Now, it is time for your medicine," she says. 
She pours the thick pink liquid into the small measuring cup. Sam drinks it quickly. He does not like the fake strawberry taste, but he knows he needs it. He drinks a large glass of water to wash the taste away. 

His mother takes the empty tray. "I have to do some work downstairs," she says. "Try to sleep some more. Or you can read a book."
She leaves the room and closes the door. 

Sam is alone in his bedroom. At first, it is nice. He does not have to do math. He does not have to run in gym class. He is warm under his blankets. 
He closes his eyes and tries to sleep. But he is not sleepy anymore. He slept for a very long time in the night. 

He opens his eyes. He looks around the room. He looks at his posters on the wall. He looks at his clothes on the chair. 
After one hour, Sam feels something new. He feels bored. 
Being sick is boring. 

He sits up. He reaches to his bedside table and picks up a book. It is a story about a young detective who solves mysteries. He opens the book to chapter one. He starts to read. 
The story is interesting. The young detective is looking for a missing dog. Sam reads for an hour. He forgets that he is sick. He forgets that he is bored. He is busy solving the mystery in his head. 

But soon, his eyes feel tired. Reading makes his head ache a little bit again. He puts the book down. 
He turns on the small radio on his desk. He listens to some quiet music. He watches the dust dancing in the sunlight coming through the window. 

At one o'clock, his mother brings him a sandwich and some juice. 
"Are you very bored?" she asks him. 
"Yes, a little bit," Sam admits. "I want to go outside and play."
"I know," she says kindly. "But if you rest today, you will be strong tomorrow. If you try to play today, you will be sick for a longer time."
Sam knows she is right. He eats his sandwich slowly. 

The afternoon is very long. Sam listens to the radio. He sleeps for a short time. He looks out the window and watches the birds in the garden. 
He thinks about his friends at school. He wonders what they are learning in science class. He wonders who won the football game at recess. 

Finally, the sun goes down. His father comes home from work. He comes upstairs to visit Sam. 
"Hello, champion," his father says. "Are you feeling better?"
"Yes, Dad. But I am very bored of this bed," Sam says. 
His father laughs. "That is a good sign. When you are bored, it means you are getting better. Only sick people want to stay in bed all day."

His father sits on the edge of the bed, and they talk for a while. Then, it is time for dinner, another dose of pink medicine, and sleep. 
Sam closes his eyes. He hopes tomorrow will be different. He is tired of resting. He is ready to be healthy again.
