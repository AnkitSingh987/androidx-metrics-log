Title: A Small House
Category: Beginner

This is a story about a family. The family is the Miller family. There is a father. There is a mother. There is a young boy. His name is Tommy. There is a young girl. Her name is Lucy. 
The Miller family lives in a house. It is not a big house. It is a very small house. 
The small house is white. It has a red roof. It has a brown door. It has four windows. 

In the front of the house, there is a small garden. There is green grass in the garden. There are yellow flowers. There are red flowers. Mrs. Miller loves the flowers. She waters the flowers every morning. 
There is a small path from the street to the brown door. The path is made of stones. 

Let's go inside the house. 
You open the brown door. You step inside. You are in the living room. 
The living room is small. There is a green sofa. There is a small television. There is a small coffee table in front of the sofa. There is a rug on the floor. The rug is blue and soft. 
The family likes the living room. In the evening, they sit on the green sofa. They watch television together. They talk about their day. It is very cozy. 

Next to the living room is the kitchen. The kitchen is very small. There is a small stove for cooking. There is a small fridge for food. There is a small sink for washing dishes. 
In the middle of the kitchen, there is a round table. There are four chairs around the table. The family eats all their meals at this table. They eat breakfast here. They eat lunch here. They eat dinner here. 
Sometimes it is difficult to cook in the small kitchen. If Mr. Miller is cooking, Mrs. Miller cannot come in. There is no space for two people. But they do not mind. They work together. 

There is a small bathroom in the house. It is at the end of a short hall. The bathroom is very clean. There is a shower, a toilet, and a small sink. There is a mirror on the wall. There are white towels on a shelf. 

Where does the family sleep? 
There are two bedrooms in the small house. 
One bedroom is for the parents. It is Mr. and Mrs. Miller's room. It is a very small room. There is only a bed and a closet for clothes. The bed fills almost the whole room. But it is very comfortable. 

The other bedroom is for the children. Tommy and Lucy share this room. 
It is a small room for two children. There are two small beds. Tommy's bed is by the wall. Lucy's bed is by the window. 
Between the beds, there is a small desk. Tommy and Lucy do their homework at this desk. They take turns. First Tommy uses the desk. Then Lucy uses the desk. 
They have a toy box in the corner. They must keep their toys in the box. If the toys are on the floor, there is no space to walk. 

Sometimes, Tommy wants a big room. 
"I want a big room," Tommy says. "I want a big bed. I want space to run."
"I know, Tommy," his father says. "Our house is small. Your room is small."
"Can we buy a big house?" Lucy asks. "With big rooms?"
Her mother smiles. "A big house is very expensive," she says. "We cannot buy a big house right now. But our small house is very nice."

"Why is it nice?" Tommy asks. 
"Because it is warm," his father says. "In winter, a small house is easy to keep warm. A big house is cold."
"And it is easy to clean," his mother says. "I can clean this house very fast. A big house takes a long time to clean."
"And we are always close to each other," his father says. "I can hear you laugh. We can talk easily from room to room."

Tommy and Lucy think about this. 
"It is true," Lucy says. "I like to hear Mother singing in the kitchen."
"And I like our garden," Tommy says. "The flowers are pretty."
"Yes," Mrs. Miller says. "We have everything we need. We have a place to sleep. We have a place to eat. We have each other."

The small white house is not a palace. It does not have big empty rooms. It does not have a swimming pool. 
But it is full of love. It is full of laughter. It is a happy home for the Miller family. They love their small house very much.
