Title: Getting Ready for Work
Category: Beginner

It is morning. The sun is up. The alarm clock rings. It is six o'clock. 
Mr. Brown opens his eyes. He is a tall man. He is a father. He is a husband. He is also a bank manager. Today is Thursday. He must go to work. 

He sits up in bed. He yawns. He stretches his long arms. He gets out of bed. His wife, Mrs. Brown, is still sleeping. He walks quietly. He leaves the bedroom. 
He goes straight to the bathroom. He takes off his pajamas. He turns on the shower. The water is hot. There is a lot of steam. He steps into the shower. The hot water feels good on his skin. It wakes him up. 
He uses soap. The soap smells like lemons. He washes his hair with shampoo. He rinses his hair. He turns off the water. He steps out of the shower. 
He takes a large, white towel. He dries his body. He dries his hair. 

Now, he looks in the mirror. He needs to shave. He has a little beard on his face. He takes his shaving cream. He puts the white foam on his face. He takes his razor. He carefully shaves his face. Up and down, up and down. Now his face is smooth. He washes his face with cold water. 

Mr. Brown goes to his dressing room. He needs to choose his clothes for work. He wears a suit to the bank. 
He opens his closet. He has many suits. He has a black suit, a blue suit, and a grey suit. Today, he chooses the blue suit. It is a very nice suit. 
He takes a white shirt. The shirt is clean and ironed. He puts on the shirt. He buttons it. 
He takes his blue trousers. He puts them on. He puts on a brown leather belt. 

Now he needs a tie. He has many ties. He looks at them. He chooses a red tie with small white dots. He stands in front of the mirror. He ties the red tie around his neck. He fixes the collar of his shirt. 
He puts on his blue jacket. He looks in the mirror again. He looks very professional. He is almost ready. 
He sits on a chair. He puts on his brown socks. He puts on his brown leather shoes. The shoes are very shiny. He ties the laces. 

He walks to the kitchen. The kitchen is empty. The house is quiet. 
He makes some coffee. He puts a coffee pod in the machine. He presses a button. The machine makes a loud noise. Hot, dark coffee pours into his white cup. It smells strong and rich. 
He does not eat a big breakfast. He just eats a piece of toast with butter. He stands in the kitchen. He drinks his coffee. He eats his toast. He thinks about his day at the bank. He has a meeting at ten o'clock. He has a lot of work to do. 

He washes his coffee cup. He leaves it on the sink. 
Now he must find his things. He needs his briefcase. His briefcase is brown leather. He carries his papers in it. 
He goes to the living room. The briefcase is on the sofa. He opens it. He checks inside. His important papers are there. His pens are there. He closes the briefcase. 

He needs his wallet. He feels his back pocket. His wallet is there. It has his money and his cards. 
He needs his phone. He looks on the kitchen counter. There is his phone. It is a black smartphone. He puts it in his front pocket. 
He needs his car keys. Where are the car keys? He looks on the table near the door. The keys are not there. He looks on the kitchen counter. The keys are not there. 
"Oh, where are they?" he mutters to himself. 

He thinks for a minute. Ah! He remembers. He wore his grey jacket yesterday. The keys are in the pocket of the grey jacket!
He walks back to his closet. He finds the grey jacket. He puts his hand in the pocket. Yes! He feels the cold metal. He pulls out the keys. He smiles. 

He walks to the front door. He puts on his watch. It is a silver watch. The time is seven o'clock. It is time to leave. 
He opens the front door. He steps outside. The morning air is cool and fresh. 
He walks to his car. It is a black car. He uses the key to unlock the door. Beep, beep. 
He opens the car door. He puts his brown briefcase on the passenger seat. He sits in the driver's seat. He closes the door. 
He puts the key in the ignition. He starts the engine. The engine roars. 
Mr. Brown is fully dressed. He has his briefcase. He has his coffee in his stomach. He is ready for work. He drives down the street, on his way to the bank.
