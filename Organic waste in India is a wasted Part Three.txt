Title: Organic waste in India is a wasted Part Three

Organic waste in India is a wasted opportunity

What it lacks is a unified view of the resource and a clear line of accountability from generation to productive use. The waste problem will not be solved by constructing more facilities in isolation. It will be solved by measuring the resource, protecting segregation throughout the chain, selecting locally appropriate technologies and rewarding verified recovery.

For a nation seeking to become a developed economy by 2047, continuing to dump, burn and neglect such a valuable resource is more than an environmental failure. It is a wasted economic opportunity. Archana Vaidya is an Executive Council member of Sustainable Development Forum Himachal Pradesh. Shashi Kad is the CEO of Sage sustainability, an impact-focused sustainability advisory firm based in Bengaluru.
