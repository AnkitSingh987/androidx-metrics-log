Title: Making the Invitations
Category: Beginner

The next afternoon, when Mia came home from school, she went straight to her bedroom. The plan for her fifteenth birthday was set. Her father had successfully booked the 'Mystery Mansion' escape room for next Saturday at two o'clock in the afternoon. Now, Mia had to invite her five best friends. 

She sat at her desk and opened her laptop. She could easily send a quick text message to everyone, but she thought that was a bit boring. An escape room party was a special event, so it needed special invitations. 

"I am going to make them look like secret spy documents," Mia decided. 
She opened a graphic design program on her computer. She chose a background that looked like old, yellow paper. She selected a font that looked like it came from an old-fashioned typewriter. 

At the top of the page, she typed: 'TOP SECRET MISSION'. 
She smiled. This was going to look very cool. 
Below the title, she began to write the details of the party. 
*Agent Name:* (She left a blank space here to write her friends' names by hand later.)
*Your presence is requested for a highly important mission. You have been selected because of your excellent puzzle-solving skills.*
*Mission Objective: Escape the Mystery Mansion before time runs out.*
*Date:* Saturday, November 15th. 
*Time:* 1:30 PM (We must arrive early for the mission briefing). 
*Location:* The Escape Center, 45 Main Street. 

Mia read over the text. It sounded mysterious and exciting. But she realized she forgot some important practical information. She added a few more lines at the bottom. 
*After the mission, we will celebrate our survival with pizza and cake at my house.*
*Please RSVP by this Thursday so I can confirm the team numbers.*

"RSVP," Mia muttered to herself. She remembered her teacher explaining that it was a French abbreviation that meant 'please reply'. It was important to know exactly who was coming so her parents could buy enough pizza. 

She printed five copies of the invitation on her color printer. The old yellow paper effect looked fantastic. She took a black pen and carefully wrote the names of her five best friends in the blank spaces: Chloe, Maya, Leo, Noah, and Zoe. 

Mia wanted the invitations to look authentic, so she folded each piece of paper into three sections. She put them into five brown envelopes. Instead of licking the envelopes to seal them, she used a red wax stick and a small metal stamp she had bought at a craft fair last year. She melted the red wax over the edge of the envelope and pressed the metal stamp into it. 
When the wax cooled, it looked like an official, sealed document from a secret government agency. 

"Perfect," Mia said, admiring her work. She was very proud of her creativity. 

Her mother knocked on the bedroom door and walked in. 
"How are the invitations coming along, Mia?" she asked. 
Mia handed her one of the brown envelopes with the red wax seal. 
"Wow, this is incredibly impressive," her mother said, carefully opening the envelope to read the paper inside. "Top Secret Mission? I love it. Your friends are going to be very excited when they receive this."

"I hope so," Mia said, putting the other four envelopes into her school bag. "I am going to give them out tomorrow morning before the first class. I just hope everyone is free on Saturday."
"I am sure they will make time for your fifteenth birthday," her mother reassured her. "Especially when the invitation looks this good. Now, we need to think about the decorations and the cake. The escape room is only the first half of the party. We have a lot of work to do at home!"

Mia nodded. Planning a party was a lot of responsibility, but she was enjoying every minute of it. She zipped her school bag closed, knowing that tomorrow, the secret mission would officially begin.
