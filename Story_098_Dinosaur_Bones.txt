Title: Dinosaur Bones
Category: Beginner

Emma and Sarah stood frozen at the entrance of the 'Giants of the Past' room. In the very center of the massive room, standing on a wooden platform, was a complete dinosaur skeleton. It was a Tyrannosaurus Rex, and it was terrifyingly huge. 

"Look at the size of it," Sarah whispered. Her eyes were as wide as saucers. 
The skeleton was made of thick, brown bones. Its back legs were incredibly thick and powerful, like the trunks of large trees. Its front arms were very short and small. But the most frightening part was the head. The skull was enormous, and the mouth was open wide, showing rows of long, sharp teeth. 

"I am glad they are not alive anymore," Emma said, staring at the sharp teeth. "It could eat a whole car in one bite."
"Me too," Sarah agreed, holding her notebook tightly against her chest. 

Their mothers walked up behind them. 
"It is very impressive, isn't it?" Emma's mother said. "Why don't you girls read the information board? That is what you are here for."

Emma and Sarah walked closer to the wooden platform. There was a large white sign with black text next to the dinosaur's foot. Emma took out her black pen and opened her blue notebook. 

"Okay, let's take notes," Emma said, suddenly feeling very professional. "You read it, Sarah, and I will write."
Sarah leaned close to the sign and began to read aloud. 
"The Tyrannosaurus Rex lived about sixty-five million years ago," she read slowly. "It was one of the largest meat-eating dinosaurs that ever lived on Earth."
Emma wrote quickly in her notebook. 'T-Rex. 65 million years ago. Meat-eater. Very large.'

"What else does it say?" Emma asked. 
"It says it was twelve meters long and weighed up to eight thousand kilograms," Sarah continued. "And look at this fact! Its teeth were thirty centimeters long, and they were shaped like bananas."
"Banana-shaped teeth," Emma muttered, writing the strange fact down. "That is a very good detail for our report. Mr. Harrison will love that."

They spent the next hour walking around the large room. They saw many different kinds of dinosaur bones. They saw a Triceratops skeleton with three huge horns on its face and a large bony frill around its neck. Sarah tried to draw a quick picture of the Triceratops skull in her notebook, carefully shading the three horns. 

They saw a Stegosaurus skeleton with large, flat bony plates running all down its back and sharp spikes on the end of its tail. 
"It used the spikes on its tail to fight off predators, like the T-Rex," Sarah read from another sign. 
"Write that down," Emma said, scribbling furiously. "Defense mechanism."

They also saw a massive Brachiosaurus leg bone. Just the leg bone alone was taller than Emma's mother. It was hard to imagine an animal that big actually walking on the Earth. 

"Did you get enough information?" Sarah's mother asked, checking her watch. 
Emma looked at her notebook. She had filled three entire pages with facts, dates, and descriptions. 
"I think so," Emma said proudly. "We have enough facts to write ten pages."
"We only need three pages," Sarah reminded her with a laugh. 

"Well, you still have the space room to see," Emma's mother suggested. "Do you want to write your report about dinosaurs, or do you want to learn about the stars first?"
Emma and Sarah looked at the giant T-Rex skeleton one last time. It was amazing, but they wanted to see everything before they made their final choice. 
"Let's go to the top floor," Emma decided. "Let's go to space."
They put their notebooks in their bags and headed for the grand marble staircase, leaving the ancient, dusty bones behind.
